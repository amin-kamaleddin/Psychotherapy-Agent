# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and non-judgmental warmth throughout, including safety screening and normalization of setbacks. Affirmations are somewhat generic and occasionally protocol-like, limiting personalization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but brief, often followed quickly by advice. Jordan's deeper emotional experiences (shame, fear of failure) are rarely explored in depth before moving to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus progressing from agenda-setting to coping, a concrete action (rental call), and a specific 9:30 AM plan. Minor deduction for jumping to solutions before fully exploring the presenting hopelessness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative elements include menu-based agenda setting, permission-seeking, and joint role-play. However, the session leans advice-heavy with long prescriptive blocks and uneven power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices and respects Jordan's hesitancy, with his preferences clearly shaping the session. Could better affirm Jordan's own ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies primarily on providing information and solutions rather than eliciting Jordan's own motivations and ideas. Little use of open questions about his reasons, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks for preferences and offers choices, but expertise is positioned with the clinician. Limited inquiry into Jordan's own knowledge or what worked for him previously.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Builds on Jordan's spontaneous change talk ('I'd like to not feel stuck') and the role-play generated confidence talk. Rarely explicitly evokes change talk through open questions about his own hopes or reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers light reframing ('that doesn't mean you've failed') and redirects to 'one tiny daily win,' but sustain talk is often skipped over. No double-sided reflections pairing pessimism with Jordan's own change talk.
