# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and warmth with validating statements like 'That sounds not foolish, but wise.' Affirmations are frequent but occasionally feel formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, accurately naming the double bind of fear. Missed opportunities for deeper reflection on grief and loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains focus on target behaviors and progresses logically to a concrete plan. Some drift around the demand for more tests; a closing summary would strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way partnership with co-created plans ('Maybe we write it together'). Power-sharing is exemplary.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent support of choice, honoring the client's condition to keep testing on the table. Client designed her own coping steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn almost entirely from the client through Socratic questions, leading to self-generated insights and needs. Exemplary evocation of meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the client as the expert on her own body; every plan element came from her. Explicit requests for her input throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation of change talk, including 'I'd like to not live like that' and concrete self-generated plans. Client's own motivations were consistently reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without reinforcement and reframed skillfully with double-sided reflections. Occasionally re-engaged directly, but never argued with.
