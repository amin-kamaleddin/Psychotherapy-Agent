# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with genuine affirmations of Marco's effort and capability. Praise was somewhat formulaic and achievement-focused rather than conveying deeper unconditional regard for Marco beyond his progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening that captured Marco's insight, frustration, and disappointment. Reflections were often brief and quickly paired with multiple questions, and deeper emotional layers were touched on rather than fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (juice consumption and weight loss) with no topic drift. Every turn moved purposefully from insight to concrete action, commitment, and scheduled accountability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with option menus, preference checks at each decision point, and a co-constructed plan. Some rapid-fire multiple-choice menus meant the counselor set the agenda of options rather than truly open co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offered choice among options and checked acceptability, with Marco retaining full ownership of the plan. Fell short of a 5 because Marco's ownership wasn't explicitly reinforced in words and the check-in structure was presented fully formed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew motivation largely from Marco, eliciting his analysis, preferred strategies, confidence, and success definitions, and his change talk flourished. Sometimes overtaken by a solution-focused menu-offering style that provided ideas rather than consistently eliciting Marco's own first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treated Marco as the expert, offering options and asking how best to support him, with his own ideas shaping the plan substantially. Some choices and structures, like the five-item check-in template, were framed without as much negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk through concrete action questions, confidence scaling, and success definitions, yielding strong responses from Marco. Did not deeply amplify or reflect the change talk itself, such as elaborating on why confidence was a 10.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged Marco's love of OJ neutrally and explored it briefly, then pivoted quickly toward solutions. Did not use double-sided reflections strategically to resolve ambivalence, and elaborating on his attachment to juice risked reinforcing sustain talk.
