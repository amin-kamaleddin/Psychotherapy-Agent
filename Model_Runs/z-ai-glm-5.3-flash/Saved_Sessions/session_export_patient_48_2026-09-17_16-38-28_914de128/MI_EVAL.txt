# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful tone with effective reframing ('that doesn't make you lazy') and supportive regard. Affirmations lean plan-focused ('that's a solid plan') rather than individualized, person-centered praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captures Farid's ambivalence ('stuck between wanting to move and being wiped out') and his confidence shifts. Reflections sometimes stay shallow and pivot quickly to problem-solving rather than exploring his self-doubt and discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on increasing physical activity throughout — every exchange ties to the concrete plan (6:45 walk, 5-minute fallback, Saturday stroll, 3pm crash marker). Session progresses logically with no drift off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions are genuinely co-constructed ('we'll tweak it') with Farid shaping timing, rewards, and backups. The counselor sometimes offers menus of pre-formed solutions, keeping the exchange slightly less than fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy strongly reinforced: 'I'm here to support your choice,' no-pressure weekend options, and full acceptance when Farid rejected the alarm-across-the-room idea. Farid clearly owns his mantra, reward, and contingency plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from Farid — his own reasons, solutions, confidence rating, and mantra. Occasionally supplied ideas before exhausting Farid's own, and the structured rhythm substituted for deeper exploration of meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Farid positioned as expert on his own life, and his contributions (coffee reward, alarm label, Saturday stroll) substantially shape the final plan. Front-loading options occasionally diluted power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire, need, and commitment language — confidence ruler moved Farid from 6 to 7, and having him voice the plan and mantra generated strong statements ('I can survive a week of this'). Counselor repeatedly reflected and built on Farid's own change ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk reframed effectively (e.g., 'lazy' recast as circumstance) and doubt acknowledged briefly before pivoting to solutions. Some worries (weekend derailment, 'excuses') were met with quick problem-solving rather than strategic double-sided reflection.
