# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard from opening affirmations through normalizing ambivalence. Years of avoidance met with compassion, never judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections throughout ('the hope and the fear sitting right next to each other'). Hana repeatedly confirms understanding by building on the reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on exam avoidance progressing logically from readiness to a specific commitment (Saturday practice test). Every response moves toward change without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; counselor asks rather than tells and lets Hana define the smallest step and future agenda. The plan is entirely co-constructed from her insights.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and pace consistently reinforced ('you don't have to resolve that today,' 'at your pace, no audience'). Explicitly rejects an expert role and celebrates Hana's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within the client via the successful presentation memory, her reasons for change, and commitment language. Counselor explicitly returns ownership ('that came from you, not from me').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client ('you know yourself best'). Hana's own ideas drive the entire plan, demonstrating her contributions shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation of rich change talk ('I want to try,' 'anxiety doesn't get to decide anymore. I do'), which is then highlighted and reflected back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk met with double-sided reflections that validate without amplifying, followed by pivots toward change. A few reflections briefly restate the fear without an immediate pivot, but sustain talk is never reinforced.
