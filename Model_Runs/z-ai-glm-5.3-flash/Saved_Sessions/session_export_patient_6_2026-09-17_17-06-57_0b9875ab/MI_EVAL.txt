# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Alexei's experience and affirming his efforts with a warm tone. Falls short of 5 due to somewhat formulaic warmth and limited explicit unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening accurately captured Alexei's perspective, including his minimization and values connection. Reflections were sometimes thin, followed by advice-giving, and missed emotional layers like fear of the cop incident and worry about his mom.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus maintained on alcohol use consistently tied to Alexei's soccer, college, and scholarship goals. Every turn advanced toward concrete change, culminating in a clear, client-generated action plan with a specific time and place.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with menus rather than directives, role-play invitations, and client-shaped plans. Falls short of 5 because the counselor still tended to pre-package options rather than co-create them from scratch.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client choice with repeated option-offering for lines, scripts, timing, and strategy. No advice was imposed, and Alexei's refusals (e.g., skipping rehearsal, rejecting texting) were respected without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from the client's own values, producing strong change talk that grew from defensiveness to commitment. At times filled space with advice and scripts rather than continuing to elicit deeper reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine inclusion of Alexei's input existed, but the counselor frequently slipped into an expert/advisor stance with pre-made scripts and step lists. Power-sharing was uneven since the counselor often supplied answers before consulting the client's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk tied to the client's values, with strong statements about soccer goals and commitment by session end. Evocation was somewhat passive, lacking active deepening tools like confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk, but also missed chances for double-sided reflections pairing Alexei's minimization with his stated goals. Redirects were inconsistent rather than skillful pivots, and some focus remained on obstacles.
