# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates warm, unconditional positive regard throughout, explicitly separating Amira's worth from her outcomes ("I'm not keeping score here"; "Awkward is more than welcome here"). No judgment or disapproval appears even when Amira describes relapse fears and self-disappointment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflective listening that captures emotional nuance, including the precise double-sided reflection of Amira's ambivalence. Amira repeatedly confirms the accuracy of the reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains good focus on the target behavior and builds momentum toward a concrete step and return commitment. However, the opening exchanges stay in broad exploration of helplessness, and the session's most productive turn (the drawing memory) emerged from the client rather than counselor-guided direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed: tasks are framed as joint ("build on together"), and the action plan (sketchbook tonight, report back) is entirely Amira-generated. Amira's self-knowledge about pacing directly shapes the therapeutic contract.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces Amira's ownership consistently ("your experiment, on your terms," "I'd rather you steer than me guess") and turns her request not to be pressured into a mutual agreement. Amira explicitly credits this non-coercive stance as why she opened up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight and motivation originates from Amira herself, elicited through open questions about what could change and what feels possible. Her closing insight ("everything always felt too big") is clear evidence of motivation evoked, not instilled.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Amira as the expert ("You know yourself best") and solicits her feedback on the session, which substantially shapes the therapeutic contract. Her contributions visibly drive the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk, moving Amira from regret to desire to a concrete commitment (looking for the sketchbook tonight and reporting back). Each increment is reflected and amplified without rushing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with validating reflections rather than arguing, then pivots toward change. Lost points because the session's opening lingered in validating hopelessness before pivoting, extending the sustain talk longer than necessary.
