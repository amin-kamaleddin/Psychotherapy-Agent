# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with ambivalence normalized and unconditional regard conveyed at closing. Affirmations focused more on the plan than on Hana's personal strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections captured Hana's ambivalence well, though 'that makes sense' became formulaic and her fear of repeated failure was not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation, advancing steadily from rulers to a concrete, actionable Monday plan without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana's own contributions substantially shaped the plan, and she said it 'feels like mine.' However, the checklist and option menus were largely clinician-proposed, giving parts an expert-driven structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was regularly affirmed with choices offered and the plan built on Hana's own time, reward, and follow-up preference. The clinician-generated checklist and slight urging ('trust yourself') kept it from a 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ruler questions effectively drew out Hana's own reasons and coping ideas, with confidence rising from 3 to 7. Menus were sometimes offered before eliciting her ideas, and emerging change talk was not elaborated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana's input was central — her delay strategy, time, reward, gum, and check-in all came from her. Some advice-giving lapses tilted expertise toward the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance and confidence rulers skillfully elicited change talk, with confidence rising from 3 to 7. Moments like 'this was easier than I expected' passed without deepening.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk (de-stressing, 'things look fine now,' no promises after the week) was acknowledged neutrally but not strategically softened — no double-sided reflections or reframing back toward change.
