# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with good affirmations of Elise's choices. Would benefit from more explicit affirmations of her personal strengths and courage, as warmth was somewhat brief and functional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early, accurately capturing Elise's core ambivalence. Reflections became sparse later, missing the deeper emotional layer such as her fear of judgment from Dana and the belonging concern.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with every exchange advancing toward a concrete, personalized plan. No topic drift, closing with a well-structured commitment and check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong co-construction of the plan, including asking what Elise wanted from the check-in and honoring her scheduling preference. Structure was largely counselor-driven (tips, menus, scenarios), tempering full partnership equality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choice and honored Elise's preferences, including her own script and check-in time. Slightly limited by frequent advice-giving and counselor-generated option menus that subtly steered rather than purely elicited.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effective elicitation via scaling questions and rehearsing her own words, producing strong change talk. Much of the session involved the counselor supplying ideas and scripts rather than drawing solutions from Elise, whose creativity emerged only late.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently solicited Elise's input, and her contributions visibly shaped the plan. However, much of the content originated from the counselor, positioning them somewhat as the idea source.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Excellent use of importance/confidence rulers tracking confidence from 4 to 8, plus rehearsal of her own line and a Monday commitment that elicited mobilizing change talk. Limited by heavy reliance on counselor-generated options rather than open evocation of Elise's own reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Briefly acknowledged worries and reframed potential failure supportively ('Monday is a test, not the verdict'). Responses tended to jump to problem-solving rather than rolling with ambivalence through reflection first.
