# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, but affirmations target the plan content rather than Malik himself — no direct affirmation of his honesty, willingness, or self-generated lines.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate summaries and acknowledgment of Malik's annoyance, but reflections are largely informational restatements and miss deeper feelings like being distrusted or controlled, often doubling as transitions into advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays well-focused, moving logically from exploration to a concrete, time-limited plan and rehearsal, though the declared target (cannabis use) receives little direct attention or discrepancy work.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Malik shapes the plan by rewriting the script and choosing options, but the session becomes clinician-generated choices with Malik picking among them, with advice given before he asked for solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-giving and acceptance of Malik's boundary on smoking without pressure; willingness to try felt more like negotiated compliance than internally owned choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** After the second turn, nearly all content (truce components, scripts, tone tips) comes from the counselor; Malik's own reasons for change are never explored in depth and discrepancy with his values is undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing via inviting Malik's preferences and letting him adapt the script, but the overall expert-driven stance solicits his expertise only after the intervention is designed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicited commitment and preparation talk ('I could try the text thing,' 'Two weeks') and his own reasons, but missed chances to deepen dissatisfaction ('it does get old') or use confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk was repeatedly reflected and legitimized ('keeping your weekend smoking is reasonable') without double-sided reflections or gentle discrepancy development, letting 'that part's staying the same' stand unchallenged.
