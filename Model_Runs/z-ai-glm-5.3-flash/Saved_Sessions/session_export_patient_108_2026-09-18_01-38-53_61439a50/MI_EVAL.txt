# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong affirmation of autonomy throughout; person's stated choice to plan the talk and fridge note was accepted without pushing. Reflections consistently conveyed respect and understanding.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent reflective listening, e.g., rephrasing 'discomfort isn't damage' and 'backpedalling' with warmth. Person's statements reflected feeling understood (e.g., 'That's it exactly').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Both clinician and person had clear, shared sense of purpose: preparing to hold the boundary with Dave. Direction shifted responsively (rehearsal) per the person's request.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Person generated all solutions (script, date, fridge note) with clinician as guide. Clear partnership, e.g., 'planning it together would help' led directly to joint practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used open questions to elicit the person's own motivations ('what would make it doable?'). Affirmed strengths ('catching your backpedal twice'). Advice was offered only with permission-seeking tone. No directiveness despite person asking for help, asking permission to push practice further ('Want to push it one step further?'), and framing plan around person's own words ('my words, my date, my fridge note').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was strongly evoked and person verbally affirmed change ('I'm ready. Genuinely ready, not just telling you what you want to hear'). Follow-up open questions elicited coping plan ('I'll remember right here... I did it once... so I can do it again').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative tone with shared decision-making ('would it help to plan together?'). Strong mutuality; minor deduction for clinician occasionally leading the pacing of the rehearsal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Numerous questions drew out commitment and plan ('what will remind you...?'), yielding sustained talk. Person self-labeled strengths; clinician reinforced. Slight limitation: some affirmations slightly shifted focus from person's talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ambivalence ('every part of me wants to say... stay another month') was met with reflection and reframing ('guilt can sit in the same room as doing the right thing') rather than confrontation. Helped person verbalize counter-backpedal strategies organically.
