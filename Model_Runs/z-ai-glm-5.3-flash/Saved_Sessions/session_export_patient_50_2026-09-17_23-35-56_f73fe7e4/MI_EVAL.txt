# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes shame (e.g., 'no judgment here — reaching out took real courage'). Embarrassing admissions are met with affirmation rather than judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections throughout, including the pivotal client-derived reframe 'you're avoiding the feeling, not the test,' which produced a genuine shift ('something clicked').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, unwavering focus on the target behavior (taking the exam) from start to finish, with every question advancing the agenda toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian interaction ('help me figure this out with you'), with plans co-constructed entirely from client-generated ideas and no unsolicited expert advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership are consistently reinforced ('You don't have to believe it will work yet'), and no plan element is prescribed — even the 'rule' was the client's idea.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation, evidence, and plans are all drawn from within the client, with evocative questions surfacing deep reasons ('what has kept you coming back each time?').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client expertise is explicitly and repeatedly centered ('You're the expert on your anxiety'), and every plan element originated from her contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Rich change talk evoked and deepened throughout ('I want my life back,' 'I'm tired of it winning'), selectively reinforced and built into the client's own plan. Newly noticed excitement emerged from evocative exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('this is different,' 'panic might still win') is acknowledged neutrally and redirected to change, though sometimes without maximally strategic double-sided reflections amplifying the change side.
