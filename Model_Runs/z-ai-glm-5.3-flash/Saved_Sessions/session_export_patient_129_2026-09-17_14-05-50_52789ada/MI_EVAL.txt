# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful stance, validating Tyler's irritation about being forced to attend. However, affirmations of Tyler's own strengths are sparse and somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections, but emotional disclosures about mom's yelling, his social life, and fear of losing face go unreflected. Rapid problem-solving crowds out reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clear, concrete focus with actionable steps, but drifts from substance use toward pure conflict negotiation. The referral concern is never revisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Role-play is interactive with check-ins, but the pattern is largely counselor-prescribes, client-performs. Tyler adopts the counselor's wording rather than co-creating his own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices, respects preferences, keeps the plan confidential, and lets Tyler set his acceptable outcome. Falls short due to heavy-handed, per-turn script delivery.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is supplied via scripts rather than elicited; almost no open questions about Tyler's own values, goals, or concerns about use. His self-motivated moment is left unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks for input on agenda and permission before role-play, but the expertise stance dominates with scripts and checklists. The overall direction remains counselor-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged ('I won't use at home or on school nights'), but arose from persuasion rather than deliberate evocation. Tyler's own reasons for moderating use were never explored or reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about mom, friends, and 'normal' use is bypassed rather than addressed. No double-sided reflections, and ambivalence about use is never surfaced.
