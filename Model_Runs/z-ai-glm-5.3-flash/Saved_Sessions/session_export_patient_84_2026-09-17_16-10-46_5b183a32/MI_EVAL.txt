# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout; validates Malik's annoyance and affirms his efforts. Slightly formulaic at times, with occasional pushing of options when Malik hesitated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured Malik's core dilemma between peace at home and peer identity. Deeper emotional issues, like the trust rupture with his mother, were passed over in favor of moving to options.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained consistent focus on concrete steps, progressing coherently to a specific commitment ('I'll try it this week'). Focus drifted somewhat from substance use itself to mother-son conflict management.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative structure: asked permission, offered menus, and incorporated Malik's edits into the plan. Counselor dominated idea generation early, and collaboration deepened only after Malik asserted himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Malik's choice with option menus and genuine decision points (text vs. in-person, declining role-play). Options were always pre-framed by the counselor with mild steering, limiting full ownership reinforcement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked some evocative questions and elicited Malik's motivations, but quickly supplied scripts and content, doing much of the motivational work for him. Intrinsic reasons about the use itself and the trust issue remained underdeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Malik's input and asked permission, but frequently supplied the actual content and lines, with Malik's contributions shaping the session only after he reclaimed them ('I'd say it my way').

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some genuine change talk ('maybe she'd ease up,' 'I'll try it this week'). Much of it was generated by the counselor's proposed scripts, and key statements weren't deepened with exploratory follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing, avoiding reinforcement of resistance. However, double-sided reflections and strategic pivoting were rare, and option menus were repeatedly re-offered after pushback rather than rolling with resistance.
