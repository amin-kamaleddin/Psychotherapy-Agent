# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with consistent validation and affirmations of progress. However, praise occasionally felt formulaic rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both sides of Kevin's ambivalence. Deeper emotional exploration of his embarrassment and identity tied to footy and mates was missing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing risky drinking with a progressive arc from exploration to a concrete plan. Gently redirected off-focus moments and closed with a follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving — Kevin's own ideas (meal first, $60 cash, Dave's nudge) formed the core of the plan. Power balance was notably equal throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Kevin's ownership; rejected suggestions were accepted without pressure. Kevin set his own limit and terms, with the counselor adapting freely.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocative questions, confidence ruler, and values-based 'pride' question elicited authentic change talk. Some turns relied on counselor-supplied option lists rather than open evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly solicited Kevin's input and let his choices drive the plan. Occasionally stacked multiple suggestions/questions at once, drifting toward an advice-listing expert stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of change language including the confidence ruler and values-based questions. The confidence question came late, and desire/ability talk could have been evoked earlier.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and redirected via double-sided reflections. A few counselor suggestions briefly invited more sustain talk (e.g., the mocked soda option).
