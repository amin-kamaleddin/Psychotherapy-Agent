# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unconditional positive regard despite Richard's hostile, externally-referred start, validating his position without judgment. Honored his conditions (e.g., privacy from his wife) and affirmed his competence genuinely.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and deep, capturing the punitive nag-stress-smoke cycle and skillfully linking doubt with aspirations. Richard's 'Finally someone gets it' confirms reflections landed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on smoking change throughout, using marital material as a bridge rather than a detour. Each exchange built progressively from pre-contemplation to a self-generated one-month action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as a true partnership — every decision point (routine, timeframe, support) was handed to Richard, and the emerging plan was entirely his. The clinician's role was limited to process guidance, exactly as MI calls for.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never advised, pressured, or prescribed; consistently asked for Richard's terms and honored his stated conditions. Reinforced his self-directed change style and amplified his own slip-recovery framework rather than replacing it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically drew out Richard's own reasons (retirement dreams, grandkids, identity as someone who does hard things) rather than supplying them. Produced spontaneous planning language and conditional commitment entirely from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Richard as the expert on his own life, and all plan elements originated from him. Richard's 'Never been asked that before' confirms the partnership was experienced as meaningful.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Transformed sustain talk into change talk at every turn — evoking elaboration from 'I'm not going anywhere' and confidence from past mastery. Elicited commitment-adjacent language: a self-generated plan with success criteria and anticipated obstacles.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk, using double-sided reflections and reframing toward competence. Deducted because sustain talk was sometimes reflected back at length before pivoting, giving some air to the resistant narrative.
