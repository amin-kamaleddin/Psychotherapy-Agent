# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with frequent, specific affirmations honoring both sides of Kevin's ambivalence. However, some affirmations are tied to change-oriented content rather than unconditional positive regard for Kevin as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is consistently accurate and often complex, tracking Kevin's social fears well. Kevin's shame about the public embarrassment was glossed over with forward-moving questions rather than explored emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on drinking in relation to the ankle injury throughout, with each turn advancing the arc toward a concrete, client-owned plan. Direction without pressure while remaining client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal problem-solving with open, invitational questions and no expert pronouncements. The plan emerged entirely from Kevin, who even noted the collaborative stance was landing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No pressure or prescriptive advice; the plan, motivation, and permission to be imperfect were all framed as Kevin's own. Autonomy was actively celebrated rather than merely acknowledged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every substantive turn ended with a question eliciting Kevin's own reasoning, and no reasons for change were supplied by the clinician. Kevin's motivation, plan, and barriers were all articulated in his own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kevin was repeatedly positioned as the expert, and his contributions demonstrably shaped the session — the plan, pocket strategy, and success criteria all came from him. No unsolicited advice or expert role assumptions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session's standout strength — Kevin's change talk deepened progressively from 'maybe knowing when to stop' to 'I want more nights I actually remember.' Change talk was systematically evoked, reflected, and converted into a client-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated rather than argued with, with textbook double-sided reflections and consistent pivots toward change. A couple of sustain statements received reflections that slightly extended rather than pivoted, but sustain talk was never reinforced.
