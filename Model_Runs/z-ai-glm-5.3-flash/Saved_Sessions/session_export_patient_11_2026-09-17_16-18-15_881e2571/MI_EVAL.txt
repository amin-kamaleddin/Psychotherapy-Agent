# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating Amira's adjustment difficulty and supporting her choice to defer nutrition work. However, warmth was somewhat formulaic, with limited individualized affirmation of her strengths beyond task-related praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some accurate reflections but kept them brief and often immediately followed by multiple questions or planning. Did not deeply explore emotional layers such as feeling like 'a stranger' in her new neighborhood or the discouragement underlying the salsa incident.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on increasing physical activity from start to finish, steering away from nutrition tangents and moving consistently toward a concrete plan. Lost a point because direction was achieved through counselor-driven planning rather than guiding Amira to clarify her own goals, and question-stacking diluted focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Included collaborative elements such as permission-seeking and option menus, but the balance tipped toward counselor-led planning with specific times, steps, and check-in protocols proposed in rapid succession. Amira mostly ratified the counselor's plan rather than co-creating it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed choices as Amira's and supported her decision to postpone nutrition work, with her explicitly concluding 'it's mine.' However, the detailed prescriptive plan and contingent praise structure slightly shifted ownership toward the counselor's system rather than her intrinsic capacity for self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used some effective evocative moves like confidence scaling and future-oriented questions, drawing out Amira's own reasons (mood, pride, capability). Evocation was intermittent, with many turns spent recapping the plan rather than developing her rich change talk into deeper commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission before offering options and invited Amira to choose, but frequently assumed the expert role by generating the entire plan (alarm time, shoe placement, route scouting, if-then script). The session's structure and content were largely counselor-driven rather than shaped substantially by her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked good evocative questions including confidence scaling and 'what would raise your confidence from 7 to 8?' However, failed to consistently reflect or amplify Amira's strong change talk, letting statements like 'That little boost is what I'm chasing' pass without reinforcement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Appropriately avoided dwelling on Amira's mild sustain talk and reframed briefly without amplifying doubts. Could have been stronger with double-sided reflections, but given minimal sustain talk, the approach was adequate.
