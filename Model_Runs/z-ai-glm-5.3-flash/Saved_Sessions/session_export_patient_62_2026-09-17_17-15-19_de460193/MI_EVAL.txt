### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, non-judgmental stance with effective affirmations ('You're cautious but willing to try'). Warmth occasionally feels formulaic, and discouragement/setbacks are not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures Jordan's weariness and frustration. Reflections sometimes pivot to advice rather than deepening exploration of fear of failure and emotional toll.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on securing stable employment, moving clearly from exploration to a concrete, time-bound plan with timeline and follow-up protocol.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine early collaboration, but the second half tilts toward advisor-client as the counselor delivers templates, scripts, and checklists with Jordan in a largely reactive role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is explicitly supported ('You're in charge of what we work on') and Jordan's preferences are respected. However, unsolicited structured advice somewhat constrains his space to shape his own approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence scaling question effectively surfaced change talk, but most momentum came from the counselor's ideas rather than Jordan's own motivations, values, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing at the start, but the session shifts toward an expert-driven stance with rapid-fire solutions, positioning the counselor as problem-solver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Spontaneous change talk is well noticed and reinforced, and the confidence question moved Jordan from 2 to 5. Evocation is limited by the quick shift to problem-solving and advice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('nothing changes,' 'roads that dead-end') is neither amplified nor actively softened; responses acknowledge it briefly then redirect to practical options rather than using double-sided reflections or reframing.
