# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, even when Tariq challenged the counselor's motives; however, few explicit affirmations of his strengths (four clean months, care for family) limited depth of positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of surface feelings were offered, but significant vulnerable disclosures about his father and mother were bypassed in favor of moving to the practical task.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained consistent focus on the presenting concern and drove toward a concrete outcome with a clear backup plan; however, direction stayed at the immediate crisis rather than connecting to broader rehabilitation goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was genuinely interactive, with Tariq's edits shaping the note and script in real time, though the counselor did most of the drafting labor rather than eliciting Tariq's own words first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tariq exercised meaningful choice at every step (tone, wording, delivery, rehearsal), and the counselor explicitly praised his self-directed stance with no pressure or paternalism.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied on offering solutions and drafted content rather than drawing out the client's own motivations; Tariq's motivations (family connection, release, reputation) surfaced but were never explored with open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided an authoritarian stance and offered choices, but took the expert role in drafting content and never explicitly acknowledged Tariq as the expert on his own situation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged spontaneously ('I'm done letting officers get under my skin') but was met with logistics rather than being developed; no open questions explored why staying clean matters or his release goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was briefly acknowledged without amplification, but there was little active redirecting—no double-sided reflections or reframing of the officer conflict as a chance to practice the composure Tariq himself described.
