# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with affirmations of Wiremu's efforts ('that's hard but really brave'). Affirmations are brief and formulaic, limiting depth of acceptance around shame-laden disclosures.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Opens with reflective phrases but rarely uses reflective listening afterward. Emotionally rich disclosures (shame, the sting of urine screens, loyalty to Tristan) are repeatedly met with bullet-pointed advice rather than reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, goal-directed agenda (safety check, action plan, parenting course, role-play) focused on alcohol, violence accountability, and reunification. Some drift into logistics and the housing ambivalence is left untied to target behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Co-creates the text draft and role-play with genuine two-way exchange in the latter half. The first half is dominated by unilateral expert advice, with Wiremu initiating most collaborative moves himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and honors Wiremu's refusal of a course search. However, advice-giving is heavily prescriptive and the suggested housing move does not honor his stated ambivalence, leaving the change process largely clinician-scripted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Wiremu's powerful motivations (tamariki, whānau, cultural reconnection) are stated spontaneously but never explored or deepened. The clinician supplies reasons and solutions rather than eliciting the client's own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks Wiremu's preference and tailors a draft text at his request. The overall stance remains expert-driven, with long prescriptive lists and little inquiry into Wiremu's own ideas about what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges spontaneous change talk and invites focus, but does not strategically deepen it. Commits like 'I'll ring my GP this week' are not amplified or linked to Wiremu's values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk, but never actively explores Wiremu's repeated ambivalence about moving with whānau or discomfort with urine tests. Double-sided reflections are absent and the ambivalence remains unresolved.
