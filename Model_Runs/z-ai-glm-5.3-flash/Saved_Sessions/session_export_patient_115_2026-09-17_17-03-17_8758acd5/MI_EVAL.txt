# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout—Samir's minimization and gum rejection were affirmed rather than judged. Affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic, missing deeper, individualized recognition of his identity as a father and hard worker.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Samir's exhaustion, the importance of his cigarette wind-down, and his football motivation. Deeper emotional reflections of his shame and family skepticism ('she's heard promises before') were underused.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on reducing smoking tied to asthma control; every exchange folded into a fully specified concrete plan with no drift. Session closed with specific start day, ritual, support, reminder, and check-in timing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—the plan was co-built at every step from Samir's own choices and wording, with the counselor acting as a resource rather than an authority. Samir left owning the plan fully.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was emphasized repeatedly, and Samir's modifications (coffee, text preference, reminder wording, declining a script) were all honored. Support was somewhat menu-based rather than emphasizing full ownership expansively.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Value-based and scaling questions elicited Samir's core motivation (football with his son) and concrete commitments, moving confidence from 6 to 7.5. Some responses slid into pre-packaged advice rather than letting Samir generate strategies himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samir was treated as the expert on his life, with genuine power-sharing in shaping the plan. At times the counselor drove the agenda with rapid multi-option menus rather than open exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of change talk early—'playing football with my boy'—with confidence scaling producing concrete commitments. Reinforcement of Samir's elaborated change statements relied more on summaries than direct evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about the wind-down cigarette and past failures was not reinforced, but was also not actively softened with double-sided reflections or deeper exploration. Redirects were brisk rather than strategically gentle.
