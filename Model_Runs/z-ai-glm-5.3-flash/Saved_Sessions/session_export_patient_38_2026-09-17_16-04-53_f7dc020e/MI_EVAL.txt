# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental; normalized her conflict about emergency contraception and never criticized her inconsistency. However, explicit affirmations of Elena's strengths (e.g., 'you handled a scary situation') were sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening throughout, accurately capturing her relief, shaken feelings, and 3am anxiety. Missed reflecting her pivotal statement 'I don't want to feel that powerless again,' limiting deeper emotional mirroring.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session drifted into anxiety-management territory while repeatedly deprioritizing the referred target (contraception). Forward movement was Elena-generated only ('maybe next time'), with little counselor seeding of links toward future readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative process — choice menus, plan adapted to Elena's feedback (code word, shortened sticky note, added 'buy test'). Somewhat counselor-drafted, presenting ready-made options rather than brainstorming jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session's strongest domain: consistently affirmed Elena's ownership ('I'm here to support your choices'), validated her pacing, and never pressured or imposed. All plan steps were selected by Elena herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena produced real intrinsic change talk, but most counselor questions were process/preference questions rather than strategic evocative ones about values, confidence, or vision. Key change talk moments were summarized rather than developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena was positioned as the expert and her ideas genuinely shaped the session (3am plan, code word, sticky note). Could improve with explicit acknowledgment of her expertise and more co-editing of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged ('powerless,' 'that scare was enough') but passed without reflection or evocative follow-up, e.g., 'What would it take to feel powerful again?'. Her self-aware admission about not being as careful was met with a general summary rather than amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well without amplifying it — accepted her avoidance while gently reframing EC as not 'admitting I need it.' Lacked double-sided reflections tying sustain talk to her change talk to actively pivot toward change.
