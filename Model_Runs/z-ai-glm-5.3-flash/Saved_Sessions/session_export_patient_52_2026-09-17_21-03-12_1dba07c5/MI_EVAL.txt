# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard and warmth from start to finish, with affirmations of Irina's strength and honesty and no judgment even when she initially minimizes her drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including double-sided reflections capturing both the benefits of drinking and her guilt, and a poignant reflection of 'breathing room instead of survival.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus progressing logically from exploring ambivalence to a specific goal, supports, and a concrete commitment (texting the neighbor today), with each question building momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: the counselor seeks Irina's read, uses the confidence ruler interactively, and lets Irina's own idea (texting before leaving) drive the action step, which they then operationalize together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice-giving or pressure; all plans emerged from Irina, with affirmations reinforcing her agency and explicit normalization of her limits and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from within via open questions and the confidence ruler, eliciting Irina's own reasons—her son, her mother's example, and her vision of Saturdays.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Irina is explicitly positioned as the expert, and her ideas (reduction plan, tea/show strategies, social worker, texting commitment) substantially shaped and drove the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A key evocative question elicited the commitment to one drink instead of two, reinforced with affirmations, a confidence ruler, and future-oriented questions producing rich change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
