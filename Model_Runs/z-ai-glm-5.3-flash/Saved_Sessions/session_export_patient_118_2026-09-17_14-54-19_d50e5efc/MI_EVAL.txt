# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, never judging or shaming despite Seamus's defensiveness. Strong evidence of felt acceptance: 'You're the first one didn't make me feel like a criminal for lighting up.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections used consistently, capturing his wariness, priorities, and begrudging openness. Reflections are sometimes formulaic ('You're clear...' repeated) and miss opportunities to explore emotional undercurrents like fear of failure and shame from years of lecturing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus, honoring his dental goal while following his momentum into smoking without topic drift. Session ends with a fully specified, achievable plan: dental booking, the 12th start date, trigger-specific tools, and a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-constructed — Seamus chose the start date, tracking method, and partner script wording, with input sought before acting. Falls short of top marks because options were delivered as a pre-packaged expert list rather than built jointly from his own knowledge, and some turns feel checklist-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary and explicit autonomy support from start to finish ('No push—your choice,' 'only if you want'), including accepting his 'no promises' stance without pressure. This approach visibly disarmed his reactance and converted resistance into agreement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operates on the principle that motivation lives in the client — notably by not lecturing, which elicited his own reasons (chewing, money, partner peace). Relies heavily on informational menus rather than evocative questions (e.g., importance/confidence rulers) to draw out his motivations directly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Seamus as decision-maker ('No push—your choice,' 'Which would you like me to do first?'), and his contributions demonstrably shaped the session. Slips into expert list-giving mode when presenting options rather than first asking what he already knows or has considered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk by respecting his pace, letting him generate his own rationale ('No point getting the teeth sorted and then wrecking them') and commit concretely to dates and actions. Could deepen and amplify commitment statements with targeted questions like 'What makes this time different?' rather than moving straight to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk, consistently pivoting toward change without arguing (e.g., acknowledging past patch failures then redirecting to 'Which of those sounds at all doable?'). Some reflections restate sustain talk verbatim without reframing; more double-sided reflections would improve this.
