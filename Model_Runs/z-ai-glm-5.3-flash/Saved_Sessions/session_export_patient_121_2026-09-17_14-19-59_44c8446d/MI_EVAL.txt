# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations ('smart choice', 'trying still takes courage') and normalized setbacks. However, warmth is somewhat formulaic and Sofia's defensiveness about years of blame isn't met with deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections about protecting herself, but responses quickly pivot to advice-giving. The fear of betrayal and years of hurt are never deeply reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, focused progression from coping strategies to a concrete micro-plan with rehearsal and follow-up. Direction is maintained largely through advisor-style momentum rather than collaborative agenda guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia selects strategies, class, and opener, and her ideas are incorporated. However, the pattern is largely expert-delivers-options-client-picks, making it feel more like consultation than equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are repeatedly offered, 'no promises' is accepted without pushback, and non-commitment is permitted. Still, prescriptive micro-instructions (playlist now, timer, reward) steer within a directive frame.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Virtually no open questions explore Sofia's own motivations, values, or reasons for change. Her hope about reconnection emerged spontaneously but was met with logistics and instructions rather than development.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor assumes the expert role with rapid-fire option lists, scripts, and step-by-step instructions. Little effort is made to draw out Sofia's own expertise or let her solutions shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk ('I can do ten minutes', 'headphones might actually work') was consolidated into a plan with rehearsal, but it emerged spontaneously rather than through skillful evocation. No exploration of importance or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is tolerated with brief reframes and normalizing, but no double-sided reflections are used. When Sofia threatens to quit if the plan fails, the response is generic validation rather than a strategic pivot toward her own desire for change.
