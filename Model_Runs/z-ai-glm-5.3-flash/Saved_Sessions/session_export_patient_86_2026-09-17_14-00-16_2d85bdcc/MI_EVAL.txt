# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and respectful of Marco's anti-lecture stance, with no disapproval of smoking. However, warmth is minimal and there are no affirmations of his honesty, autonomy, or commitment to follow through.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; Marco's emotions, ambivalence, and meaningful statements (e.g., 'my mouth, my habits') go unreflected. The counselor jumps to information and task lists rather than seeking to understand his perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Consistent practical focus on dental hygiene behaviors with concrete plans and schedules. However, the smoking cessation target is deliberately set aside and never revisited, even when Marco opens the door.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Advice is dispensed rather than negotiated—unsolicited information blocks and fixed plans without asking Marco's input. The counselor also repeatedly withdraws agency ('I can't book for you') rather than co-creating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco's choices are explicitly respected ('no lectures,' 'respect your choices'). Yet support is passive: no affirmations of his capacity for self-direction, and rigid refusals to assist with booking read as unhelpful rather than choice-promoting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no open questions are asked, and Marco's own motivations emerge spontaneously despite, not because of, the counselor. His reasons, hopes, and the wife's influence are never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely informational, with expertise treated as residing with the clinician despite Marco's protests that he knows the basics. Agency is withdrawn near the end, undermining partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marco offers multiple change-adjacent statements ('I'll give the week a go,' 'we'll leave that door open') that are never reflected, affirmed, or deepened. 'We'll leave that door open' is a significant missed opportunity, met with booking logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, and the smoking topic is avoided via explicit agreement, which respects Marco but constitutes avoidance rather than skilled softening. No double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to convert his openness into change language.
