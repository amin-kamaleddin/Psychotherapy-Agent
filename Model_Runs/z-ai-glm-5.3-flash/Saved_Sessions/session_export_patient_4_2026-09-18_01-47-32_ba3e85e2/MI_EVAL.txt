# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard was maintained throughout, including validation of the patient's testing demands and conditions rather than protest. The client's spontaneous statement — 'Thank you for actually listening, not just telling me it's stress' — provides in-session behavioral evidence of felt acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate, consistent, and reached emotional depth, including double-sided reflections that held ambivalence ('two truths side by side') and insights the client immediately confirmed. Minor gap: the self-worth dimension of 'I just feel small' was not explicitly mirrored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session held a coherent behavioral-activation target with a clear arc from eliciting what helps to securing commitment ('Monday morning, I start'). Testings agendas were folded in as a parallel track without derailing or suppressing them.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Partnership was explicit and consequential: the client generated the walking idea, safety parameters, success criteria, and contract terms, while the counselor added no content she hadn't first supplied. 'How would you like us to work together from here?' capped this power-sharing stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership was reinforced systematically ('yours, on your terms,' 'you don't have to choose between the two') with escape clauses accepted without negotiation. Choice was structurally embedded, and the contract was negotiated entirely on the client's conditions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A textbook evocation sequence elicited progressively stronger client change talk — D/A/R moving to commitment — with success criteria drawn from the client rather than imposed. The counselor amplified her strongest statements in her own metaphor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The 'expertise resides in the client' stance was explicit, repeated, and demonstrably shaped the session downstream. The plan ('Deal?') was literally the client's construction on her terms.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was evoked skillfully, nearly all originating from the client, moving from 'Maybe... walking more?' to 'Monday morning, I start.' Client-defined success criteria (sleep, morning dread, Gulnara's observation) were elicited rather than suggested.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with and was effectively met with empathic acknowledgment plus pivots toward change. Re-voicing the sustain agenda ('the questions for Dr. Brown stay yours to ask') granted the scan demand repeated airtime, and the fear 'What if it's something serious?' was answered with logistics rather than gently explored — defensible, but strict coding reflects loud, unresolved sustain talk at the close.
