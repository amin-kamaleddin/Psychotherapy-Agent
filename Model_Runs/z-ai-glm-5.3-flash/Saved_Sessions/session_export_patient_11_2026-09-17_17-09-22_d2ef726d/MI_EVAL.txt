# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with no labeling or disapproval. However, affirmations were somewhat formulaic and praise-heavy rather than conveying deep unconditional regard independent of the client's compliant engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening was limited; vulnerable disclosures (mirror self-criticism, fear of repeating past failures, a 'loud inner critic') were repeatedly bypassed in favor of planning questions. Understanding was adequate but shallow, with emotional nuance often missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on increasing physical activity from start to finish, with every exchange progressing toward a concrete, measurable plan. The counselor maintained focus even when nutrition came up, building coherently to defined success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira's answers shaped the plan, but the counselor dominated with advice-giving, tips, and stacked questions (2-4 per turn) that steered rather than co-created. More back-and-forth exploration with the client generating options first was needed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent choice-based questions and optional advice framing supported the client's agency. However, pre-packaged solutions (exercise menus, specific phrases, tracking formats) subtly constrained options, with minimal explicit reinforcement of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and asking the client to define success drew out some good material, but motivation was largely assumed rather than evoked. 'Why' and elaboration questions were scarce; the counselor supplied reasons instead of drawing them from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice-based language was used, but the counselor's suggestions substantially drove the plan's content (exercises, tracking ideas, self-compassion phrases). Amira was rarely invited to generate her own solutions or positioned as the expert on her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira generated abundant change talk herself, which the counselor acknowledged but did not actively deepen. No elaboration questions or evocative prompts amplified her reasons; session time went to logistics rather than reinforcing motivation language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not reinforce resistance or dwell on barriers, which was appropriate. However, sustain-talk moments (fear of routines slipping) were met with logistics rather than skillful double-sided reflections connecting her concerns to her motivation.
