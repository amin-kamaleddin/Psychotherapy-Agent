# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, explicitly deferring judgment ('that's your call entirely') and reframing drinking as self-care; client's closing thanks confirm the nonjudgmental tone landed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, accurate reflections that go beneath surface content, e.g., 'losing ownership of your own story' and the double-sided reflection on wanting a clearer night while fearing grief from friends.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on alcohol tied to the injury, moving from exploration to values, consequences, and a concrete experiment with defined success criteria—no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Work framed as joint ('let's think it through together'), options offered neutrally, and the plan's content (two-beer ceiling, success criteria) came from the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice consistently reinforced, honoring 'I'm not promising anything big' and framing the plan as Matteo's self-designed experiment where a negative outcome is also informative.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Key insights originate with the client ('breaking a rule I didn't know existed'), drawn out by open questions and mirrored back rather than supplied by the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client expertise explicitly located from the start and repeatedly returned to ('I'll turn it back to you, the expert'), with Matteo's own ideas substantially shaping the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic evocative questions generated strong desire and commitment language ('I remember the whole night,' 'that'd feel like my choice'), with each change statement reflected and reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimizations and humor-as-deflection redirected with curiosity and validation, though occasionally met with neutral rather than actively softening or double-sided reflections.
