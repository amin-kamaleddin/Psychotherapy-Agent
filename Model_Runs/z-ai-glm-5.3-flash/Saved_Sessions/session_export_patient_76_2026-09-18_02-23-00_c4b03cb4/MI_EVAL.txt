# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The trainee establishes unconditional positive regard from the start and maintains it even when self-harm is disclosed. Cutting is validated as a coping attempt rather than judged, with no disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and sustained, including double-sided reflections and reflections capturing both emotional and bodily experience. Reflections build progressively on Liora's disclosures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The trainee maintains clear focus on self-harm as the target behavior with a logical session arc and a concrete potential direction (the classmate). Growth area: consolidate progress with a summary or collaboratively name a specific next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed, with the trainee asking rather than telling and incorporating Liora's own words into reflections. Key content (the friend) emerges from the client, not the trainee.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly and consistently reinforced through permission-seeking and framing possibilities as questions rather than directives. No pressure or controlling language appears; Liora's 'maybe' is left in her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The trainee consistently elicits Liora's own reasons and capacities, drawing out hope, values, and a tentative relational resource. Growth area: use a second, differently framed attempt when Liora says 'I don't know' and explore past successes and strengths more.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Liora is explicitly positioned as the expert on her own experience, and the trainee asks her to generate solutions rather than supplying them. Client contributions genuinely shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The trainee skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk from a precontemplative client, eliciting desires like 'to not be this tired anymore' and a tentative 'maybe.' Growth area: deepen commitment language through scaling, confidence exploration, or an explicit next step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently not reinforced; strategic questions pivot toward costs of cutting and toward alternatives. Growth area: some reflections restate sustain talk before pivoting, which slightly amplifies it.
