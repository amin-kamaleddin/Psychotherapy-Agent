# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgment, actively countering shame around family history of alcoholism and affirming Mei's work. Affirmations occasionally feel slightly formulaic, and normalization of setbacks could be more explicit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including the opening double reflection on ambivalence and the insightful reframe of Mei's self-criticism about willpower. The counselor consistently captures both content and emotional core.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on drinking and contraceptive reliability with good forward momentum toward a concrete first step. The opening stayed somewhat broad, and the contraceptive thread developed somewhat passively rather than being linked explicitly earlier.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative session in which every plan element was generated or selected by Mei in response to open invitations. The counselor positioned Mei's real-world action as the centerpiece, functioning as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Mei's ownership was consistently reinforced, with no imposed advice and validation of her preference for support rather than surveillance. The closing message that the plan isn't about being flawless further protected her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight originated from Mei, with the counselor eliciting and amplifying her reasoning rather than supplying motivation. Mei ended with self-generated commitment and hope, demonstrating evoked motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Mei was consistently positioned as the expert, with her contributions directly shaping the session's trajectory. The entire intervention plan originated from her with only light counselor scaffolding.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation and reinforcement of change talk, with Mei's own words strategically highlighted ('listen to what you just said'). Mei's commitment escalated to a fully self-designed behavioral plan and a concrete first step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoided dwelling on sustain talk and effectively reframed doubts toward planning. However, Mei's fears of being seen as fragile or 'the family pattern' were validated without double-sided reflections, and low confidence was acknowledged rather than actively strengthened.
