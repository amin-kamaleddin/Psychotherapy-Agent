# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with no labeling or shaming, even amid defensiveness. Validated Brandon's framing ('it's not a crime') while affirming his character and maintaining respect throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captured both sides of his ambivalence ('part of you values that unwind...'). Reflections built on his exact words — the brunch sting, the 'you earned it' voice, the pride obstacle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on weekend drinking with progressive momentum: imagining change → strategies → accountability → sister conversation → concrete plan → coping plan → readiness confirmation. Advanced without rushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange between equals; the plan was co-constructed entirely from Brandon's own material (running, sister, Dave's pacing). His 'thanks for not just lecturing me' confirms the non-hierarchical stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision originated from Brandon; counselor reinforced ownership ('it's my call, my reason', 'a plan that's yours'). No advice or pressure appeared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from within — the accountability insight, the dignity-preserving framing, and his own coping self-talk all came from Brandon's mouth. All change reasons were client-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook partnership statement ('I don't have the answer for that — you do') that shaped the entire session's direction. Closing question let Brandon resolve his own loose end.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic evocative questions escalated Brandon's change talk from tentative ('maybe not going as hard') to concrete planning (Saturday run, sister text, 8pm craving plan). Explicitly named his commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with neutral or double-sided reflections and pivots toward change reasons, avoiding amplification. Minor gap: a few minimization statements (e.g., 'I'm not promising I'll never overdo it') could have received more active reframing.
