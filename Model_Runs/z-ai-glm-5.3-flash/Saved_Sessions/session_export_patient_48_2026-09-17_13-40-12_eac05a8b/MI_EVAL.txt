# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with gentle normalization ('Be kind to yourself'), but warmth is generic and individualized affirmations of Farid's strengths are missing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal; feelings of self-doubt, guilt, and fatigue were skipped in favor of prescriptive steps, and acknowledgments were superficial rather than complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on physical activity and converged on a concrete, realistic plan, though pushing toward a plan early was premature for a contemplation-stage client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction was predominantly counselor-driven with pre-formatted plans, templates, and checklists; genuine two-way co-creation of strategies was largely absent.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional choices were offered and Farid's decline of an extra reminder was accepted, but the overall style was directive and prescriptive ('nonnegotiable') with little emphasis on his ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor rarely drew out Farid's own motivations or reasons for change; his hope and desire to avoid guilt surfaced spontaneously but were never elicited or amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed an expert role with numbered directives and dictated checklists, rarely inviting Farid's ideas before offering solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Farid generated change talk spontaneously ('hopeful,' 'Tuesday, I'll try to commit'), but the counselor did not evoke, reflect, or deepen it; credit for keeping momentum with the reminder question.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about laziness, failure, and excuses was mostly skipped rather than strategically softened; no double-sided reflections or affirmations countered his self-criticism.
