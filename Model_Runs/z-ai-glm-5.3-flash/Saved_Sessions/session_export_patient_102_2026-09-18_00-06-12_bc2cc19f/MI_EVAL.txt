# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard despite a context inviting moralizing; explicit affirmations and 'I'm not here to tell you it's wrong' convey warm acceptance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captures Milan's self-perception and emotional undercurrents, but reflections sometimes stay strategic rather than deepening the fear beneath his minimization (e.g., the cousin's accident).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive focus from framing the concern to a concrete self-generated plan, with each exchange strategically advancing resolution of the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative, with joint agenda-setting ('you can decide where we start') and a plan emerging entirely from Milan's own reasoning—his shift from resistance to co-construction confirms genuine partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly and consistently supported ('you get to decide what fits for your life'); the counselor validates a self-selected middle path and never prescribes or pressures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary evocation with a defensive, precontemplative client—values (basketball, parents' trust) were drawn from Milan himself, letting the discrepancy do the motivational work in his own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Milan is repeatedly positioned as the expert, and his contributions demonstrably shape the session's focus and final plan—textbook power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic elicitation via the client's own risk criteria and values produced strong client-generated change talk culminating in commitment ('the driving one. That feels like me'), which the counselor consistently reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Resistance is defused effectively without disputing ('I'm not here to argue with that'), though the opening reflection echoed sustain talk at some length before redirecting, and minimizations could have been pivoted from slightly faster.
