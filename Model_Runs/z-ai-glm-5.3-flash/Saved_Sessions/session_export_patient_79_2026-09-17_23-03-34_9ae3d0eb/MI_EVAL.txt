# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, validating Luis's frustrations and affirming his efforts (e.g., 'The fridge list was your idea, not your doctor's') without ever criticizing his sarcasm or resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate, including double-sided reflections ('you're already finding ways to make this work... and at the same time, some days there's just no room for a checklist'). Luis consistently built on reflections rather than pushing back.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on asthma self-management, progressing from problem exploration to concrete action (glovebox plan, telling his daughter) without drift or imposed direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative, with Luis's own ideas (fridge list, glovebox, telling his daughter) shaping its entire direction. The counselor never prescribed or assumed the expert stance, and honored Luis's agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly reinforced Luis's ownership ('I'm not going to add anything to your plate,' 'on your terms, no system forced on you') and offered genuine choice, which Luis accepted ('just see how it goes my way').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every motivation came from Luis himself — the highway scare, his daughter, regaining control — with the counselor skillfully deepening his own reasons. Luis moved from sarcastic resistance to explicit commitment ('that one's happening') in one session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positioned Luis as the expert ('You're the expert on your own life') and every solution originated from him. The counselor's influence worked entirely through reflecting and amplifying Luis's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and deepened, notably pausing on the truck idea ('That's not nothing; that's you designing a solution') and using future-oriented questioning ('What would it feel like next Tuesday morning?'). This elicited strong commitment language and personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('hasn't killed me yet,' 'too much for me right now') was consistently redirected without argument or correction, pivoting instead to Luis's own solutions and limiting scope to prevent resistance.
