# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment about the client's secrecy; specific affirmations and unconditional validation ('I'll be here next session either way') feel genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, naming the emotional core (e.g., fear of being seen as 'fragile now') and echoing the client's own metaphors; reflections consistently articulate what the client almost says.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on the target behavior of peer disclosure, advancing from ambivalence to a concrete plan (Saturday, movies, casual framing) with follow-up; pacing is strategic, not merely exploratory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session unfolds between equal partners, with the client's ideas (Marisol, timing, casual approach) shaping the plan and no expert stance taken; the client explicitly confirms feeling heard and self-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with repeated affirmations of ownership and no advice or persuasion; the client's statement that 'this actually feels like my choice' is strong behavioral evidence of effectiveness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key motivation originates from the client (asthma friend observation, Marisol, the insight that not-telling isn't keeping her safe); evocative questions draw out reasons, ability, and spontaneous commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client's contributions genuinely determine the working plan because the counselor elicited rather than supplied them; affirmations credit her own problem-solving and explicitly locate authority in the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk, moving the client from ambivalence to concrete planning and amplifying her own change language; the session culminates in strong commitment ('Okay. Saturday. Movies, popcorn, and just telling her.')

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced and is often met with effective double-sided reflections and reframing of freezing as acceptable; only falls short because some fear moments ('what if she goes quiet') were explored openly rather than met with active strategic reframing toward confidence.
