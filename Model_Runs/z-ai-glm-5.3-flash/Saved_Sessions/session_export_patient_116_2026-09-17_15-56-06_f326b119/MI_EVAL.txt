# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance maintained throughout, with validation of resistance and humor. However, warmth is formulaic ('I hear you' preambles) with few personalized affirmations of strengths or autonomy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections reliably captured factual content but stayed at a surface level and were repetitive. The emotional layer — discomfort with pills reminding him 'something's wrong' and fear of hospitalization — was never deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on medication adherence with a clear path from resistance to a concrete micro-step (pill by the coffee at breakfast). Some drift occurred with an overly broad risk list and asking Samir for his diagnosis.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions were framed as shared, with options co-created around Samir's routine and open invitations to redirect. The balance tilted, though, during long advice/information blocks in which Samir stayed a passive consumer.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support — choice was reinforced from start to finish, with menus of options and pressure-free acceptance of refusal. Samir even remarked, 'You're giving me a choice now? That's new,' showing the stance was palpable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions elicited the hospitalization comment, a genuine hook for motivation. But rationale and solutions were mostly clinician-supplied, with no exploration of Samir's values, scaling questions, or development of his own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samir was repeatedly positioned as decision-maker and asked for input before suggestions were offered. However, advice-heavy exchanges meant his contributions shaped the session somewhat mechanically rather than substantively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Real kernels of change talk emerged (exploring 'less annoying' options, breakfast pairing, 'I can manage that much'). These were not deepened or reinforced — no amplifying reflections, scaling, or elaboration, and hedges ('no promises') went unaddressed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Abundant sustain talk was mirrored back neutrally rather than strategically softened — no double-sided reflections or pivots toward change. The risk-information block arguably amplified the negatives without helping resolve ambivalence.
