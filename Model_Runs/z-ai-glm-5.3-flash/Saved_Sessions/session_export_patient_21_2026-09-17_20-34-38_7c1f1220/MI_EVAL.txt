# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental regard with affirmations like 'that's wisdom, not cheating' and respect for the client's pace and limits throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, accurate simple and complex reflections captured emotional nuance, confirmed by the client's increasingly open elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity, building progressively from barriers to a concrete first step and meaningful success markers without tangents or drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session unfolds as genuine joint exploration, with the plan co-constructed entirely from the client's own routines and the follow-up framed collaboratively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No directives or pressure appear anywhere; the client explicitly thanks the counselor for not pushing a big overhaul, and choice/self-direction are woven into every exchange.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn almost entirely from within the client through open questions and values-linking rather than persuasion, culminating in 'this actually feels like something I can do.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was repeatedly placed with the client ('you know your rhythms better than I do'), and her contributions demonstrably shaped the entire plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled open questions elicited rich desire and reason talk (patience, hikes with daughter, grandkids), and the client's own 'it doesn't sound impossible' and 'I actually feel okay about trying that' show successfully cultivated change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced and was consistently pivoted using double-sided reflections, though some reflections restated sustain talk at moderate length and skepticism about sleep benefits could have been reframed more actively.
