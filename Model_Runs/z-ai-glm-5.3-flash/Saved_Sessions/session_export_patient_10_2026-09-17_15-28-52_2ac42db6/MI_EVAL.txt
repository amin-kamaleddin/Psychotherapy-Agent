# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout, with explicit affirmation of the client's commitment, validation of her fear of disappointment, and specific positive feedback after the role-play.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections captured her ambivalence, but reflections became less frequent during problem-solving and emotional themes were acknowledged once without being deepened or revisited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on securing backup childcare to enable employment; the session built progressively from options to script to role-play to a concrete Thursday plan with contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong equal-partner dynamic with help offered contingent on her preference, comfort checks before role-play, and Amina's choices directly shaping the session, including the script revision she requested.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were consistently offered and Amina set the pace, but the problem-solving phase was advice-heavy with counselor-supplied menus and scripts rather than evoking her own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited confidence (her '10' rating), her own plan, and strong commitment language, though deeper personal reasons for working and strategies for overcoming doubt went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina was repeatedly positioned as the decision-maker and her choices shaped the session, though early option-listing was somewhat counselor-directed rather than drawn from her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully channeled momentum via scaling questions and choice points, eliciting strong change talk; could have deepened further by inviting elaboration on why working matters to her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about barriers and fear of failure was acknowledged and pivoted to solutions, but double-sided reflections pairing it with her confidence and desire were underused to resolve ambivalence directly.
