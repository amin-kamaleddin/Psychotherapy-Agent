# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental tone with affirmations of partial progress. However, affirmations are somewhat generic and relapse is not explicitly normalized, preventing a higher score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections but increasingly sparse as the session shifts to lists and instructions. Samir's fear of failure and dread of hour six receive only brief acknowledgment without deeper exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on target behaviors from opening question to a concrete, time-bound Thursday test shift. Every exchange advances the behavioral focus without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Samir's input shapes the plan, but the counselor drives the session with menus, scripts, and plan structure. True co-construction between equals is only partially achieved.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent choice-offering and respect for Samir's pacing ('don't go planning my whole life'). Little explicit emphasis on his ownership of the change process, and plan construction is somewhat prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and confidence elicitation draw out Samir's own motivations (his chest, his wife). Deeper values behind change are not explored and motivations aren't consistently amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Periodic invitations for Samir's input, but the session is largely expert-driven with counselor-generated options. Samir mostly approves or tweaks rather than co-creates.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good use of open questions and scaling to elicit strong change talk ('part of me wants this to work'). Hopeful statements are not consistently reflected or amplified when they emerge.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ambivalence is acknowledged without judgment, but sustain talk about failure is only passively validated. No strategic reframing or double-sided reflection to resolve ambivalence.
