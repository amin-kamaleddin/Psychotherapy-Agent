# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a polite, nonjudgmental tone with appropriate safety screening. However, affirmations are procedural and formulaic, missing chances to affirm Andrei's strengths (e.g., past smoking cessation, willingness to consider change).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; responses are largely educational and directive. Significant change talk is not reflected, and minimizing statements receive generic placating rather than accurate exploration of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor keeps a clear, consistent focus on hypertension, medication adherence, and alcohol reduction without drift. However, direction is maintained largely by pushing a predetermined agenda rather than collaboratively exploring the core issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely counselor-led, with plans and goals presented as finished products while Andrei acts as a compliant recipient. Limited true power-sharing occurs despite Andrei picking some specifics.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers superficial menu choices (alarm options, exercise timing), providing some support for choice. But the substance of the plan is imposed, and Andrei's autonomy statements (e.g., not quitting) are not explicitly honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is supplied through facts and plans rather than drawn from the client. The counselor never asks about Andrei's own reasons, concerns, or confidence, and emerging change talk goes undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, delivering recommendations and a pre-packaged plan. Andrei's contributions are incorporated passively rather than shaping the session's substance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerges from the counselor's structured plan rather than skillful evocation. No open questions explore Andrei's reasons, importance, or confidence, and change statements are never deepened or affirmed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with a lecture of guidelines and health facts rather than double-sided reflections, risking entrenchment. The generic 'That makes sense' fails to genuinely defuse resistance before moving to persuasion.
