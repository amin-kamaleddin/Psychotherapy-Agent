# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains consistent respect and nonjudgment, affirming Kevin's self-insight and never criticizing his drinking. However, there is no explicit normalization of Kevin's embarrassment or shame about the injury, and warmth remains somewhat restrained.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captures Kevin's ambivalence and the stress-drinking link, including subtle reflections like 'the dumb stuff happening afterward.' Reflections occasionally skip emotional depth, leaving Kevin's embarrassment and fear of social exclusion unaddressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on drinking and its consequences, moving from vision to planning to high-risk coping without drifting off-topic. Each counselor turn advances the change process with clear momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed, with 'figuring out together' language and every step building on Kevin's own ideas (gym history, Marcus, his coping options). The counselor asks rather than prescribes, and Kevin's answers determine the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kevin's ownership is consistently reinforced: the 'if anything' opening, 'not because I said so,' and explicit attribution of the plan to Kevin ('that's your plan'). No directive advice or pressure appears, and his choice to keep going out with friends is accepted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor's core strategy is evocation, asking what has worked before, what would feel doable, and how Kevin would know it's sticking. Kevin's motivation visibly grows from guarded ('maybe?') to committed ('that's the big one,' 'I'll text the guy'), demonstrating motivation drawn from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located in Kevin: 'you know your life and your social world better than anyone' and 'everything in that list came from you.' The gym plan, Thursday coping strategy, and stress focus all emerged from Kevin's own words, amplified and returned to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation throughout, including the miracle-type question, the 'biggest win' question that produced Kevin's deepest commitment language, and the Thursday coping scenario. Kevin's final plan — texting Marcus before work 'so I can't chicken out' — is strong mobilizing change talk directly cultivated by the counselor.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor never argues with sustain talk, instead reframing it by separating 'the good part and the painful part' and accepting Kevin's desire to keep socializing within the plan. The redirection is mostly implicit, however, and more explicit double-sided reflections could better pivot his ambivalence.
