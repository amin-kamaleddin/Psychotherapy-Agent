# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental normalization of Naomi's fears and doubts throughout. Regard is genuine but somewhat formulaic, with affirmations focused mostly on her competence rather than her as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of Naomi's experience, including her physical sensations, practical reasoning, and ambivalence. Reflections stay mostly content-level with little exploration of deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on managing social anxiety during shoots, with every turn advancing a concrete behavioral plan. Redirects tangents back to the plan without losing warmth.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructs real plan elements with Naomi, including her own shortened if-then sentence and homework tied to her Saturday job. However, the overall structure and strategies originate from the counselor, so the partnership is not fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and seeks permission, accepting Naomi's preferences without pressure. Occasionally steers toward preferred options, keeping this from a 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some of Naomi's own motivation through confidence ratings, her own if-then wording, and asking what practice feels doable. The session remains largely counselor-driven with prescriptions rather than open exploration of her values and hopes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Naomi's input on choices, but the expert stance dominates—the counselor supplies strategies and structure while Naomi mostly selects from menu items. Her expertise from years of managing shoots goes unacknowledged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi produces meaningful change talk, and the counselor capitalizes on it via the confidence ruler (1 to 4/10). However, the counselor rarely deepens change talk with open follow-ups and often moves quickly to prescribing next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoids dwelling on sustain talk, offering brief normalization and redirection to action. Sometimes relies on quick reassurance rather than strategic double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
