# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout — validating even Kenji's fears and self-deprecating remarks ('not weird at all,' affirming his pride). Individualized affirmations like 'You know yourself best' communicate deep positive regard rather than formulaic warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, opening with a complex reflection capturing both sides of Kenji's ambivalence. Reflections track his emerging insights precisely, including his sister's worry and the form's lasting surprise.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on alcohol reduction, progressing logically from awareness to tracking to sustainability to defining week-one success. Every response advances the arc without topic drift, and even exploratory questions deepen commitment to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership of equals — the psychiatrist never prescribes, and the final plan is entirely Kenji-designed (app placement, 7pm reminder, backup rule, 'record, don't judge'). The clinician serves as a curious collaborator building on the client's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are framed as Kenji's own and his pacing is respected (doctor first, sister later). Autonomy support is mostly implicit in question framing, with fewer explicit ownership statements than expected for a 5, and evaluative enthusiasm ('I love that...') subtly steers at times.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice or reasons for change are supplied; every question draws out Kenji's own motivations — the form surprise, his sister, his pride, his money-discipline strengths. Client-owned change talk escalates visibly, culminating in his emergent commitment ('This feels... different, somehow').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located in the client ('You're the expert on your own life,' 'You'd know better than I would'). Kenji's contributions genuinely shape the session — tracking, app strategy, and sharing sequence all emerge from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically sequenced evocative questions ('What would make week one feel like a win?') elicit progressively stronger change talk. Change talk is reflected back to deepen it rather than passing over it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubts are generally acknowledged briefly then pivoted toward client strengths, and avoidance is reframed toward new awareness. Some sustain talk is reflected back with substantial airtime without an immediate pivot, which keeps this from a 5.
