# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with genuine, individualized affirmations that countered Timur's self-critical labels. His ambivalence, hesitations, and need for belonging were all normalized and honored without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captured both surface content and deeper emotional meaning (belonging, identity, shame). Reflections powerfully mirrored Timur's own language, such as 'knowing the cost and feeling it every day are different things.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on the pro-social change goal and progressed deliberately from ambivalence exploration through barrier-solving to concrete commitment. Each exchange built logically toward action without rushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was fully co-constructed from Timur's own ideas, with no expert-driven advice-giving at any point. The counselor's reflections, affirmations, and open questions shaped rather than dictated the direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly reinforced throughout ('you get to decide how this goes'), and Timur's reluctance to commit out loud was validated rather than challenged. This non-pressuring stance paradoxically strengthened his commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight and plan element came from Timur himself, with skillful questions evoking desire, ability, and commitment talk. The session culminated in his self-generated commitment to tell Fiona tonight and attend the centre this week.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently positioned Timur as the expert on his own life, drawing out his solutions rather than offering advice. The phone-call plan, the 'just sit and listen' condition, and going with Fiona all originated from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked, reflected, and amplified each time it appeared, with commitment language explicitly highlighted. The session directly produced mobilizing change talk and a concrete commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was briefly acknowledged then reframed toward change, never reinforced or dwelt upon. Effective reframes included countering 'I'm lazy' with the 22-day example and reducing the centre barrier to a single no-pressure visit.
