# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance with frequent affirmations, but praise occasionally felt formulaic and cheerleading rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured barriers and ambivalence well, but the counselor rarely explored the deeper emotional layer of discouragement and lost identity as 'a guy who used to work out.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, focused attention on re-establishing exercise throughout, with logical momentum toward a concrete, escalating plan and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tone was cooperative, but the counselor drove the agenda with multi-question turns and supplied the solution architecture, limiting true co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Dante's choice and control over the plan, though autonomy was somewhat undermined by providing a pre-set solution menu and leading questions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions successfully elicited change talk, but much of the motivational content (options, cue, backup plan, self-message) was counselor-provided rather than drawn from the client, with limited exploration of deeper values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative language and choice were present, but most solutions were counselor-generated, with little acknowledgment that the client holds the expertise; Dante largely adopted rather than co-created the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and the 'what might raise that to a 7?' prompt effectively moved Dante from 5–6 to 7 confidence and increased change talk, though some success relied on heavy counselor suggestion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Briefly reflected barriers without dwelling on them and pivoted to actionable reframes; the score is limited by occasionally inviting barrier talk directly, though mitigated by effective follow-up reframes.
