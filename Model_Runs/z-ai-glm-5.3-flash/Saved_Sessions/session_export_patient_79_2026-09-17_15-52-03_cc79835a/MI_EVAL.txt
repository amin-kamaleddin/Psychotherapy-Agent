# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout without arguing against Luis's sarcasm or defensiveness, with consistent validation. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deep, individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected both the practical burden and emotional tone ('your doctor's push feels intrusive and you've got a lot on your plate'). Reflections stayed at content-summary level without deeper exploration of underlying fear or feeling overwhelmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior, moving steadily from frustrations to options to a concrete plan, confidence check, technique, and doctor script. Minor drift into logistics and caveats; tighter summarizing and linking to broader values would raise this.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked permission before advising, offered choices, and the final plan was genuinely co-constructed from Luis's ideas. Pre-drafted options and scripts sometimes made Luis more of a selector than a co-creator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client ownership with 'no pressure, just possibilities,' let Luis design his own cue and success criteria, and accepted his refusal of follow-up without pressure. Exemplary, skillful autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The confidence ruler and 'what would count as actually helped?' elicited genuine change talk and Luis's own success criteria. Missed opportunities to evoke deeper importance talk linking the inhaler test to Luis's larger health values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed client expertise by asking permission and letting Luis shape the cue plan, doctor script, and one-week test. Some pre-packaged options and lukewarm affirmations kept this from the highest rating.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited commitment-adjacent language ('if that happened even once, I'd probably keep carrying it'; 'That'd be proof') through the confidence ruler and success-criteria questions. Did not deepen commitment language further, e.g., exploring 'I don't wanna come back' as motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with substantial sustain talk and sometimes reframed gently, but occasionally echoed sustain talk ('not a huge life change,' 'you're in charge') without pivoting toward change. Little use of double-sided reflection or strategic redirection.
