# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment, shaming, or labeling. Affirmations like 'That's valid' and 'not failure' validate the client's needs without disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captures her ambivalence and stressors well. Reflections are occasionally brief and could go deeper into the emotional core, especially the guilt about time with her son.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on alcohol and stress coping from the opening onward. Guiding toward concrete steps (specific nights, confidence ratings, fallbacks, follow-up) without foreclosing exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal partnership with options offered rather than prescriptions and client input sought at every step. The plan was fully co-constructed from Irina's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership is consistently reinforced via open menu choices and permission to bend the plan. No persuasive or coercive language; all commitments came from Irina.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation elicited from within through open and scaling questions, with no lecturing or persuading. Strong client reasons for change ('he deserves me at my best') emerged in response to evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise located within the client, whose ideas (tea ritual, seltzer placement, neighbor Anna) shaped the plan. Irina explicitly recognized this: 'I made the plan myself.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk consistently evoked and reinforced through strategic questions and confidence scaling. Irina volunteered commitments ('one drink instead of two,' 'maybe a six now') arising from her own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally met with neutral or reframing reflections that pivot to her own solutions. A few reflections briefly mirrored sustain talk without immediately pairing change-side content.
