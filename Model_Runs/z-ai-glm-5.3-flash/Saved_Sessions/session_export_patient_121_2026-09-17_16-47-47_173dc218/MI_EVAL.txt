# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone with consistent validation of Sofia's perspective. Some affirmations feel formulaic and ambivalence is not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are brief and often quickly followed by questions or option menus. Sofia's key vulnerable moment about not having an adult believe her received only a general response rather than a deep empathic reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on attending class and building a concrete plan that built incrementally. Direction occasionally came at the cost of exploring broader concerns, and the counselor sometimes steered toward her own agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's input shaped the plan (choosing the exit plan, before-school timing, her own backup plan), but the pattern was largely counselor-proposes-options, Sofia-picks. Ready-made scripts created an uneven power balance rather than joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was emphasized repeatedly and Sofia made her own decisions throughout. The volume of prescriptive scripts and instructions subtly narrowed her sense of authoring the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and confidence rulers effectively drew out Sofia's own motivations and hopes, producing genuine change talk. Evocation was sometimes channeled toward the counselor's predetermined plan rather than open exploration of her values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided an authoritarian stance but repeatedly supplied pre-packaged scripts, numbered lists, and checklists, positioning the counselor as the plan designer. Sofia's contributions were incorporated but expertise resided mostly with the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation of change talk via rulers and open questions, with reinforcement of key statements like 'Awkward beats skipping.' Fell short of a 5 by somewhat leading Sofia toward the counselor's preferred plan rather than exploring her own reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk (blaming Maya, 'it's on her, not me') was acknowledged but not actively softened or reframed toward agency. More double-sided reflections and strategic redirection toward controllable actions were needed.
