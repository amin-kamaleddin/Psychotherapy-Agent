# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tone is nonjudgmental but formulaic; emotionally significant disclosures (jar-opening win, not spiraling) were never affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening nearly absent; counselor jumped to advice instead of reflecting fears of failure, self-compassion milestones, and other emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus maintained on the physical activity target; never drifted, and nutrition appropriately kept out of scope.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is one-directional: plans, checklists, and templates delivered rather than co-created; client's own ideas came from her initiative, not joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice offered (pick times, choose strategies), but within a prescriptive structure the counselor designed unilaterally.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation supplied by counselor rather than drawn from client; her stated readiness and benefits were never explored or leveraged.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Expert-driven stance throughout: unsolicited plans and instruction lists with little acknowledgment of the client's expertise over her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Client generated abundant change talk spontaneously, but counselor never elicited, elaborated, or reinforced it (no open questions, affirmations, or scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Barriers (couch, decision fatigue, fear of fizzling) were met with fix-it lists instead of double-sided reflections or exploration of ambivalence.
