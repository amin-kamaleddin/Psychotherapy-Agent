# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and affirmed Elena's small steps without judgment, including respecting her privacy when she declined to share her zip code.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captured the emotional core of her panic and relief. Points off because some reflections were generic and the counselor pivoted to options rather than deepening the emotional exploration Elena requested.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on contraception while flexibly following Elena's preference to process feelings first, with a logical session progression. Minor drift occurred when offering anxiety-management tools, pulling slightly off the primary focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance, co-creating the 'if-this-happens' plan and jointly scheduling next steps. Points off because psychoeducation was somewhat expert-delivered rather than first eliciting Elena's existing knowledge.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: offered menus of options, asked permission, honored boundaries, and affirmed her right to wait. Elena explicitly stated the non-pushy approach prevented her from shutting down.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within, eliciting Elena's own reasons and self-generated conclusions. Points off because multiple-choice menus occasionally did the eliciting work for her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Elena as the expert and consistently followed her lead, with her contributions substantially shaping the session's direction and outcomes.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked strong change talk, including 'I don't want to count on luck twice' and 'I'm not saying never anymore,' culminating in a commitment to discuss the pill next visit. Could have deepened commitment language further, e.g., with confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk non-confrontationally, accepting her hesitancy without arguing and using elaboration questions to surface inconsistencies. Minimal active reframing beyond neutral acceptance.
