# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; defensive and resistant statements were validated rather than corrected, with frequent affirmations like 'That's not daft at all.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and emotionally attuned, including nuanced reflections on resentment and the psychological function of drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on target behaviors with deliberate progression from ambivalence to a concrete plan, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal partnership; the plan was co-created, and the counselor's ideas never overrode the patient's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership actively reinforced; choice was explicitly offered and the patient's pace and preferences (e.g., deferring finger-pricking) were respected without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor systematically drew out the patient's own reasons, solutions, and metrics, resulting in internally generated commitment ('Deal's a deal, even with meself').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within the patient, who chose the target behavior, generated swap ideas, and designed the plan, shifting from defensive to fully engaged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and deepened with open questions and reinforcing reflections, moving the patient from 'what should I do first?' to self-authored commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged with double-sided reflections and gently redirected without argument; only minor limitation was occasionally elaborating sustain talk (e.g., the pint's rewards) slightly before pivoting.
