# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth and unconditional positive regard are consistent throughout, with specific, genuine affirmations and no judgment of the client's weight history or culturally valued food. Nothing labels, disapproves, or conditions regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally deep, e.g., the opening double-sided reflection and the capture of food as love, culture, and belonging. Each reflection builds directly on what the client just revealed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session progresses logically from past patterns to concrete first steps and future identity, with questions advancing rather than circling. It falls short only because the target behavior remains somewhat broad rather than sharply defined.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed entirely from the client's own materials, with summaries organizing her agenda and future collaboration explicitly invited. The client's feedback ('a plan I can actually live with') confirms the joint nature of the work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice, persuasion, or pressure is used; every step is elicited from the client's own reasoning and her choice about weight goals is left fully hers. The client explicitly thanks the clinician for 'not handing me another list of rules.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client through open questions, with no clinician-supplied reasons or solutions. She ends by articulating identity-level change talk in her own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located in the client ('You know your life and your patterns better than anyone'), and the session's direction visibly follows her answers. Her closing remark confirms she felt the power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully elicit rich client-generated motivation, which is then reinforced with reflection and affirmation before deepening. The closing question consolidates commitment language ('I want to hold onto that feeling').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and folded into double-sided reflections or reframed as self-knowledge. A few scale-focused moments rest without an explicit pivot back toward change language, keeping this just below mastery.
