# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with frequent genuine affirmations ('That takes real initiative'). Childcare barriers treated as legitimate problems, not personal failings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently capture both content and emotion, and the patient builds on them. The final reflection on her shift from avoiding favors to designing a fair arrangement shows deep understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays focused on the target behavior of attending interviews via securing childcare, advancing step-by-step to concrete plans and timeline. No drift; every exchange moves toward actionable change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True partnership—every plan element originates from the patient, with the clinician co-exploring rather than prescribing. Pacing is responsive and patient-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All choices are genuinely the patient's; the clinician asks rather than tells and reinforces ownership at closing. Patient's final statement reflects self-generated commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn out from the patient rather than supplied—her confidence rating, her own insights, plan, and timeline. She ends fully convinced by her own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located with the patient ('You know your schedule and your kids better than anyone'), and all solutions come from her. Her contributions visibly shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and confidence rulers evoke rich change talk ('This week. I'm saying it out loud now'). Confidence in the job is named and amplified as a strength.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubts are reflected without amplification, handled via double-sided reflection and quick pivots to change-focused questions. Only minor room to explicitly affirm and reframe the doubt rather than move past it.
