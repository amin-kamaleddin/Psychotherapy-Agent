### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally strong, accurate reflective listening, including skillful double-sided reflections of the client's values. One inaccurate mind-read about stress/midterms briefly broke attunement, though recovery was quick.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior despite a deflecting client, with each exchange building logical momentum toward a concrete weekend commitment. No aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collegial interaction with plans co-constructed from the client's own ideas, never imposing an agenda. Falls short of full co-creation since the client's contributions didn't substantially redirect the session's path and there was no explicit check-in on agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice consistently reinforced through option-offering rather than prescribing, with no persuasion or 'should' language. The client's increasing ownership is direct evidence autonomy was genuinely supported.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation methodically drawn from within via questions inviting the client's own reasons, culminating in unprompted intrinsic motivation statements. The client confirmed the meeting got him thinking rather than feeling lectured.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly attributed to the client, with all plans built exclusively from his own generated solutions. The client's surprise at not being lectured confirms the partnership landed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk systematically elicited and deepened through masterful reframes and progressively commitment-oriented questions. The client's arc from refusing to change to wanting to try demonstrates highly effective cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely avoided amplifying sustain talk by pivoting to future-oriented questions and selectively reflecting change-relevant content. Sustain talk was occasionally reflected back fully, and the 'turning 21' rationalization was never leveraged into discrepancy.
