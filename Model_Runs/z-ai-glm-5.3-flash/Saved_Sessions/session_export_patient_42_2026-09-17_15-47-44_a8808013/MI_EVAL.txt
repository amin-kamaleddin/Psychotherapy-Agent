# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and never judges the smoking or social attachment. Client explicitly confirms feeling accepted: 'Thanks for not making me feel stupid.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate ('balancing health worries and fear of losing social connection') but brief, and deeper emotional nuances (mother's lung trouble, loneliness living alone) are named but not explored before moving to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking cessation from opening question through a concrete, client-chosen action plan. Session progresses logically from exploration to values to planning to relapse preparation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan builds on the client's own ideas and permission is sought before suggesting, but some turns (the four-strategy 'simple plan,' late-session scripts and craving tactics) are more advice-heavy than fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is consistently affirmed and the client selects the day, substitute, reward, phrase, and reminder. No coercive or pressuring language appears.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out values, ruler responses, and client-generated solutions, with confidence rising from 4 to 8. Some coping strategies and scripts were counselor-supplied rather than evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positions the client as the expert, who generates the coffee idea, comeback line, and reward. Occasional supplying of menu options and scripts slightly dilutes client-driven expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation, including 'What would make change worth it?' and ruler-based momentum reinforcement (4→8). Some change talk (mom's lung trouble) was not deepened before pivoting to past attempts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and skillfully reframed ('keeping the social part while changing your smoking'; 'slipping once isn't failure; it's information'). Early sustain talk is sometimes restated alongside change talk rather than actively pivoted.
