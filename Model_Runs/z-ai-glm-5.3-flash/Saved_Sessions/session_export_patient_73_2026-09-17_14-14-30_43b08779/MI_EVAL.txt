# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and offers affirmations, but warmth is brief and generic, with little individualized regard for Linda's courage in self-referring.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal and superficial; emotional disclosures like fear of repeated failure and grief over losing smoking social rituals go unreflected as responses pivot to information delivery.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps consistent focus on smoking cessation and produces a concrete plan (doctor visit, gum experiment, follow-up), though direction is achieved through prescriptive agenda-setting that outpaced the patient's stated readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Power-sharing is uneven, with pre-packaged scripts, checklists, and texts dominating the interaction; there is no agenda-setting and little joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some choices (text options, deferring the quit date) but frequently assigns behaviors and deadlines rather than eliciting the patient's own choices, never explicitly handing ownership back to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The approach is primarily informational, importing solutions rather than drawing out the patient's motivations and ideas; her spontaneous change talk and self-generated suggestions are left undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, writing the plan and scripts while Linda's expertise and contributions go uncredited, making the flow provider-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited only once via a readiness ruler whose answer ('maybe a five') is never explored, and spontaneous change talk ('I can do two weeks', 'for my health and the money') passes unamplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Never argues against change or reinforces sustain talk, but Linda's ambivalence ('I'd be the odd one out on breaks') is skipped rather than addressed with double-sided reflections.
