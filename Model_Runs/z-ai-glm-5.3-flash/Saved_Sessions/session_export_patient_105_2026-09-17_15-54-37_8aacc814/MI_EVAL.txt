# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard; Naomi's embarrassment was normalized and her hesitation respected throughout, with no judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of her triggers, feelings, and preferences were frequent, but they compressed facts rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning like fear of judgment or loss of past ease.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on speaking up at shoots; the session progressed from exploration to a concrete, timed plan with rehearsal and backups, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi's contributions directly shaped the plan, though several turns stacked 3-4 questions at once, subtly shifting flow toward a counselor-led agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was reinforced at nearly every turn with permission-seeking, multiple options, and respect for Naomi's pacing; her ownership was consistently affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn largely from Naomi's own reasons, phrases, and coping ideas via rulers and open questions, but evocation of broader values and long-term vision remained limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi was framed as the expert with frequent choices and plans built from her own words, though quick multi-question blocks occasionally nudged rather than purely followed her lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance/confidence rulers (confidence rose from 3 to 7.5), past-success questions, and commitment requests produced strong commitment language; missed chances to deepen discrepancy kept it from a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk (breathing 'useless,' hiding behind the camera) was not reinforced but also largely bypassed without strategic softening — few double-sided reflections or explicit exploration of her avoidance default.
