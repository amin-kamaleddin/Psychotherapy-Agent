# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains consistent warmth and nonjudgment throughout, helping Sofia open up progressively. However, affirmations follow a somewhat formulaic pattern ('You're the expert here') and ambivalence is not normalized at its emotional depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate complex reflections capturing Sofia's ambivalence and deep perspective-taking on family expectations. Some vulnerable emotional moments (e.g., 'That's kind of sad to say out loud') are met with reflection but quickly pivoted to questions rather than fully sat with.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains exceptional focus on the target behavior while progressing logically from coping alternatives through values to a concrete, time-bound plan. Every question advances the exploration without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is genuinely two-sided, with no advice-giving or lecturing, and Sofia's answers determine the session path. It falls short of 5 because the rhythm is consistently question-reflection-question, with slightly less overt back-and-forth negotiation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is explicit and consistent—the psychiatrist never prescribes, and every plan element originates from Sofia herself. Her closing statement ('It doesn't sound impossible when I say it like that') reflects genuine self-directed decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out motivation from within, letting Sofia generate her own reasons for change through her own words. Sofia moves from pre-contemplative minimization to volitional planning entirely through evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly conveys that expertise resides in the client, and Sofia's own contributions shape the session substance. However, the 'expert' framing is repeated as a set phrase rather than demonstrated through more varied power-sharing moves.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session's greatest strength—skillful questions evoked Sofia's desire, reasons, need, and commitment language. Her arc from 'not a big deal' to 'I guess this is kind of a real plan now' directly reflects this mastery.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally acknowledged briefly with neutral reflection then redirected toward change, including skillful double-sided reflections on inclusion. Some openings (e.g., 'I don't really see what the problem is') were passed over with questions rather than strategic reframing.
