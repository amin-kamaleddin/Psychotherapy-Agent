# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with affirmations of Laura's own ideas and emphasis on self-compassion, though affirmations occasionally feel generic and the warmth is somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens well and responds accurately to the values surprise, but reflections are sparse; Laura's emotionally rich material (guilt spiral, perfectionism, work exhaustion) is never explicitly reflected or validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior throughout with zero topic drift — every turn moves toward a concrete plan with a specific trigger, video, and scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed to a degree (Laura picks her trigger, video, and check-in), but the counselor supplies nearly all plan content in a rapid tip-dump style and repeats identical checklists verbatim across multiple turns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Laura chooses her trigger time, video, and check-in time, but the counselor immediately proposes a specific prescriptive plan rather than eliciting her ideas first, tipping the balance toward counselor-driven planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor relies on tips, if-then plans, and checklists rather than drawing out Laura's own motivations; the guilt spiral and perfectionism pattern she reveals are never explored via open questions, giving the session a persuasive/educational rather than evocative feel.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine client expertise is honored (her solutions, her timing), but the stance is undermined by rapid-fire prescriptive advice — defining domains, prescribing sequences, alarms, and clothing — with the same checklist repeated nearly verbatim four times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor successfully elicits commitment language and concrete change talk (readiness 7, 'I want this', 'I'll commit to that one'), but fails to deepen or reinforce Laura's strongest change statements with affirmations or follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively defuses Laura's guilt-based sustain pattern by channeling toward achievable plans and framing lapses as 'data, not failure' with a lowered bar ('5 minutes counts', 'touch the mat').
