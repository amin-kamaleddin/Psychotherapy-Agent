# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently respected Maria's time constraints, skepticism, and defensive stance without judgment, using warm affirmations at the end. Early warmth was somewhat brief and transactional, offering less explicit validation of her emotional experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Maria's ambivalence and fear of being dismissed, such as reflecting her value of control. Empathy sometimes shifted quickly into information-giving, and deeper emotional undercurrents of distrust were touched but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a clear focus on inhaler adherence despite Maria's resistance, moving consistently toward a concrete outcome. Maria left with a specific behavioral commitment to make an appointment with a documented plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions were co-constructed, Maria's own threshold and conditions were incorporated into the plan, and the counselor asked permission before adding elements. Some exchanges had a slight expert-driven lean when the counselor supplied content quickly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was a standout strength, with repeated choices, a time-limited trial framing, and explicit exit criteria honoring Maria's own conditions. Maria's closing line 'Don't tell my doctor I said that' reflected a self-authored decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evocation was uneven — the counselor frequently supplied the medication facts, question list, and script rather than eliciting them from Maria. The most evocative moments came late with the role-play and outcome question, while early sessions focused on information and logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor positioned Maria as the decision-maker and actively incorporated her contributions into the final plan. At times the counselor took the lead in structuring content before Maria fully articulated her own goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively evoked and captured change talk, with Maria moving from 'I'm not promising anything' to 'I'll actually make that appointment.' Early change talk evocation was limited as the first exchanges focused more on information than Maria's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification, and brief corrective information was paired with pivots to Maria's agency, visibly softening her resistance over the session. Double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk were not consistently used.
