### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with explicit normalization of Lukas's mixed feelings and validation of his bind. Falls short of 5 as praise occasionally feels slightly effusive or formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections, including sophisticated double-sided and meaning-level reflections (e.g., the homesickness connection). Falls short of 5 because some reflections merely restate his words and the shame/fear of exposure could be explored more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on methamphetamine use with logical progression to a concrete plan (weekday abstinence, talking to Kai, the Danish crowd). Falls short of 5 because the final exchange leaves Lukas's central obstacle unaddressed, without a summary or consolidation of the plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interview unfolds between equal partners with questions rather than directives and a plan co-constructed from Lukas's own ideas. Falls short of 5 because the psychiatrist occasionally leads the agenda slightly, e.g., presuming a replacement-focused direction rather than checking what Lukas wants to explore.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly reinforced from the opening line through the close, with no advice, persuasion, or coercion anywhere. The plan is entirely Lukas's own, and his self-defined pacing—including keeping weekend use 'for now'—is accepted without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from within the client, with every key insight (weekday plan, Theo, homesickness link, acting) originating with Lukas. Falls short of 5 because confidence in the plan is never explicitly explored and the expressed doubt at the close goes unaddressed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly and explicitly located in the client, and his contributions demonstrably shape the session. Theo becomes the model, and the weekday plan and Danish footy crowd emerge entirely from Lukas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through open questions tied to Lukas's own expertise, eliciting concrete commitment language and self-generated solutions. Falls short of 5 because responses to change talk sometimes move quickly to the next question rather than deepening the commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never argued with, but reflections sometimes repeat sustain content at length without strategic pivoting. No double-sided reflections pairing sustain with change talk, and the final 'Sunday morning Lukas' doubt is left hanging.
