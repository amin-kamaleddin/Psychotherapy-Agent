# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Nabil's anger throughout, but explicit affirmations of his strengths (willingness to role-play, insight about apologizing) were sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured both content and stakes, including reputation fears. However, reflections relied on repetitive 'I hear you' openers and deeper emotional layers (shame, strain at home) were never fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, logical focus throughout — from venting to concrete coping and rehearsal for Tuesday's appointment, with no aimless drift and explicit tracking of the client's stated priority.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine back-and-forth with real incorporation of the client's corrections and choices (15-minute limit, pacing of escalation), but the counselor supplied most of the substantive content, keeping power-sharing uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and respected Nabil's pacing ('one step at a time'), though rapid-fire menus and immediate offers to draft things sometimes pressured quick decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client motivation was drawn out (choosing options, evaluating his rehearsal), but the session was largely advice-driven, with no open questions exploring Nabil's values, hopes, or discrepancies between his anger and family/professional identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm tone but frequent slips into an expert/advisor role — drafting letters, scripts, and escalation steps without first eliciting the client's own ideas about what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Structured choices and skill rehearsal successfully evoked change talk and self-efficacy ('that's actually pretty good,' 'I could try it Tuesday'). Weakened by reliance on offering options rather than open questions drawing out the client's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about the system was consistently validated then redirected toward actionable change without arguing. Effective double-sided move, though pivots to menu options were sometimes abrupt, cutting short the client's own resolution of ambivalence.
