# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful tone throughout with no judgment, though affirmations center on the plan rather than Amira herself, missing chances for deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Decent reflective content exists, but emotional material like feeling 'off-balance' and low was bypassed in favor of logistics, keeping emotional understanding surface-level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on increasing physical activity; every exchange ties back to concrete steps with no topic drift, steadily progressing toward a defined action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way planning incorporating Amira's schedule, equipment, and ideas, though the counselor at times supplied the routine, template, and strategy menu rather than fully eliciting solutions from her.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers meaningful choices and positions Amira as decision-maker, but occasionally leans persuasive ('Love this') and imposed 'no bargaining' language rather than having her fully own it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and confidence ratings early, but shifted quickly to counselor-provided content and never explored why the change deeply matters or connected it to her core values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites input and co-developed plans, but frequently slips into an expert advice-giving role with ready-made routines, strategy lists, and templates rather than drawing solutions from Amira.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira generated abundant spontaneous change talk and confidence ratings were asked, but the counselor rarely deepened it with evocative follow-ups, acknowledging rather than amplifying it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handled mild sustain talk reasonably via normalization and safety nets like the fallback plan, but missed chances to reframe her fear of one skip becoming three or pivot to her own coping successes.
