# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard shown throughout, from non-judgmental acknowledgment of the court mandate to consistent affirmations like 'You talked yourself into it, not me.' Warmth is genuine and never moralizing about the shoplifting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captures ambivalence and emotional nuance, e.g., the double-sided reflection 'Both of those are true.' Reflections occasionally pivot quickly to questions, and deeper feelings like shame and fear of being 'the lame one' are only lightly explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on shoplifting and prevention across every exchange, from the mall exception to the concrete final question. The session shows progressive momentum from ambivalence to a specific plan with no aimless drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor works as an equal partner, eliciting Jordan's input before offering anything and co-developing the check-in plan. A 4 rather than 5 because the counselor contributes none of their own expertise, making the exchange slightly one-directional rather than a true pooling of knowledge.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported from validating the mandate wasn't Jordan's choice to reinforcing 'That's you doing the deciding, not me.' Choices are consistently offered back, and Jordan's shift to 'I'm actually talking myself into this' reflects successful support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from within — the exception story, the phone goal, pride in accomplishment — with no arguing for change. Jordan's closing self-statements are the hallmark of excellent evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in Jordan ('you know your world better than I do'), and all key plan elements (envelope, cover script, phone goal) originate from him. Exemplary partnership behavior throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked through exception-finding ('Have there been moments where you managed to hold back?') and amplified ('That's exactly how change starts'). Jordan's language strengthens over time to concrete planning, clear evidence of effective cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never amplified and is reframed toward change, e.g., acknowledging friends' teasing as 'a real cost' then redirecting to sticking with saving. A more strategic pivoting away from repeated peer-pressure discussion could further minimize sustain talk.
