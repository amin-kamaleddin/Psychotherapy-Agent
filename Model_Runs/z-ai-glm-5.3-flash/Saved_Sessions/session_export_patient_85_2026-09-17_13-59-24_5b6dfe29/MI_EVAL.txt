# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with validation of frustration, but affirmations are sparse and formulaic, delivered mostly at the start of advice-heavy turns rather than expressing genuine individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One good early reflection captured Marco's discouragement accurately, but reflections largely disappear afterward; his pride, attachment to OJ, and growing optimism go unreflected as the counselor pivots quickly to information-giving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on juice consumption and moves toward concrete action with no drift, though direction is achieved through directive structure and checklists rather than collaborative goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration through occasional choice points and preference solicitation, but most turns are monologue-style instruction blocks with Marco relegated to approving the counselor's agenda rather than true two-way problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menus of options acknowledge choice, but advice density is high with prescriptive scripts and schedules, and there are no explicit autonomy-affirming statements reinforcing Marco as the author of his own change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marco's strong change talk emerged spontaneously, but the counselor makes almost no effort to elicit motivation — no open questions about reasons for change, values, or what success would mean to him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor frequently assumes the expert role, prescribing word-for-word scripts, weighing schedules, and templates; Marco's own ideas drive the session but are never explicitly credited or built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco generates substantial change talk himself, but the counselor does little to deliberately evoke or deepen it — no scaling questions on importance, no reinforcement of commitment statements, and no exploration of why this time feels different.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco's sustain talk about loving OJ and finding dilution hard is acknowledged but bypassed rather than softened; there are no double-sided reflections, and his own slip-tolerant reframe goes unreinforced.
