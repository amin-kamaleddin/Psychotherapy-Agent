# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental stance with normalization of ambivalence and affirmation of progress. Falls short of 5 due to somewhat formulaic, repeated affirmations ('22 days') and limited individualized unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early, accurately reflecting Timur's core struggles and values. Some emotionally charged moments (fear of disbelief, shame) were handled with quick problem-solving rather than deeper reflection of feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on a stable, crime-free life, converging on a concrete, time-bound plan (Thursday porch conversation, weekly check-ins, one-page plan with verifiable steps). Every turn ties back to the change goal with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine two-way shaping, with Timur's edits (tea-on-the-porch wording) adopted into the plan. Short of 5 because much of the raw material (scripts, plan structure, options) originated from the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent offering of choices with genuine acceptance of Timur's decisions and revisions. Later turns became prescriptive—supplying scripts, breathing technique, and plan structure—reducing Timur's ownership of the 'how'.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Timur's own motivations ('not yet' rule, porch mornings, commitment statements). Later in the session increasingly supplied answers and lists rather than continuing to evoke his hopes and capabilities.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Timur as decision-maker and incorporated his contributions into the plan. Tilted toward expert-driven problem-solving by supplying scripts, the three-item structure, and proof options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, culminating in strong commitment language ('Thursday. I'll actually do this.'). Short of 5 because some change talk was counselor-scripted, and opportunities to deepen commitment (confidence scaling) were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally normalized doubts ('ambivalence, not failure') and redirected toward concrete steps rather than arguing. Occasionally engaged doubt content directly with reassurance lists instead of redirecting to Timur's own agency and successes.
