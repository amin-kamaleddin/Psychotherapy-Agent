# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental stance with frequent affirmations, but some praise feels formulaic and lacks deep individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captures mixed feelings and validates ambivalence, though reflections often stay at content level and miss deeper emotional/identity meaning (e.g., the social role of drinking).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on sustaining smoking cessation and moderating drinking, progressing logically to a concrete test plan, though the opening is diffuse and some exchanges circle over plan details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan is genuinely co-created with the client selecting and modifying options, but the counselor supplies pre-packaged menus, retaining a slight expert tilt rather than equal idea generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: genuine choice offered at every decision point, and the client's counter-conditions (no pre-reminder, celebrations exempt) honored without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out self-efficacy talk (confidence ruler, coping strategies), but evocation centers on plan mechanics rather than the client's deeper values or reasons for moderating drinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treats the client as the expert and incorporates all his contributions into the plan, but delivers a pre-packaged strategy list expert-style rather than brainstorming his ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes commitment and strong self-efficacy language via choice questions and the confidence ruler, though change talk about reasons/desire to change drinking itself is less developed than plan logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk without arguing and redirects to small flexible steps, but fully accommodates the 'celebrations exempt' framing without gently examining it or helping resolve ambivalence about drinking.
