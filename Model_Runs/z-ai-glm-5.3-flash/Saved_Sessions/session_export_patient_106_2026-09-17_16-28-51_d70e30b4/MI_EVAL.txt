# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard with no judgment or lecturing. Client explicitly confirms feeling unjudged ('came in expecting to be told off').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflections tracking the client's meaning and emotions, including ambivalence and resistance (e.g., the after-bed cigarette). Responds attuned to the drawing disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus and ends with a concrete, dated plan. At times presents multiple options/questions in rapid lists, giving a checklist-like pacing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan is genuinely co-constructed; client's conditions shape every element. Counselor checks agreement at each step and respects her decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client sets her own terms—start date, trial length, retained cigarette, slip protocol. No pressure, coercion, or implicit conditionality; trial framed as no-strings experimentation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client's own reasons for change (the drawing, thirdhand smoke concerns). Rarely deepens change talk once it appears—the drawing moment deserved slower exploration of discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client's expertise drives the session; counselor adapts to her input and defers medical specifics. Repeatedly asks for her input and adjusts accordingly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicit commitment language ('Monday. I'll try it') and her intrinsic reason for change. Could have deepened change talk further before moving to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Does not argue with sustain talk; incorporates the retained cigarette as harm reduction. The 'counting down to day fifteen' risk remains unaddressed for after the trial.
