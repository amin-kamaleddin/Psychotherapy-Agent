# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout; no disapproval of avoidance. However, warmth is somewhat formulaic and avoidance is not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but empathy is thin relative to disclosures. Missed opportunities to reflect her harsh self-criticism and fear about wanting to go.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on the target behavior; every turn advances toward a concrete, measurable plan (Thursday 12:30, 15 minutes, Beverly Hills Library).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration in finalizing details, but the counselor generated nearly all content while the client mostly selected from offered options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice repeatedly affirmed and non-attendance framed as useful information, but options were counselor-defined rather than client-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was imported through menu questions rather than drawn out. The client's reasons, values, and emerging hope went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks for input and co-constructs a plan, but repeatedly assumes the expert role with scripts, techniques, and venue supplied by the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged naturally but was not reinforced or deepened; the hopeful moment passed without exploration and evocative questions were absent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without amplification and a fallback plan reduces shame, but reflections mirror ambivalence without double-sided reframing toward her desire to try.
