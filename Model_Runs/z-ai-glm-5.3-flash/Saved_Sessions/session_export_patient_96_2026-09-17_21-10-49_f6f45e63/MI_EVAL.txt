# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, treating the piercing as legitimate rather than a problem. Warm, individualized affirmations were used with no judgment or disapproval despite the client's dismissive stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were consistently accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both surface concerns and deeper needs (e.g., respect rather than lectures). Reflections deepened as the client opened up, demonstrating excellent reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear, progressive focus throughout: from balancing enjoyment vs. risk, to a checklist, to a concrete first step and follow-up conversation. Each response advanced momentum without feeling coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was fully collaborative, with the counselor consulting the client before every plan element and incorporating her preferences. The client clearly experienced the plan as her own ('Nobody made me do any of this').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively fostered with explicit validation of the client's choices ('That's still my choice though') and a plan framed as emerging from her own ideas and timeline. The closing emphasized that 'nothing got taken from you.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within the client — the counselor offered zero persuasive arguments, with every rationale coming from Marisol herself. Open questions consistently elicited her own reasons, hopes, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in the client ('You're the expert on your mouth'), and the plan was substantially shaped by her contributions. Her ownership of the plan was repeatedly affirmed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk from a pre-contemplative client, amplifying 'everyone wins' and asking targeted evocative questions. The client generated strong self-motivating statements and committed to a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently avoided and effectively reframed (e.g., converting 'if something started hurting' into information-seeking motivation). A few moments (e.g., 'appointments are a pain') could have been met with more strategic double-sided reflections, making this a 4 rather than a 5.
