# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with specific affirmations ('a fair test you've designed for yourself') and unconditional acceptance at the close ('a win either way'). Skepticism was honored without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently went beyond surface content to underlying meaning, accurately capturing ambivalence and the 'exhale' insight. The client confirmed accuracy repeatedly ('never separated the two before').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus and progressed from exploration to a concrete, measurable plan. Direction was somewhat patient-led, with the evening-ritual exploration briefly drifting from the quantified experiment before reconnecting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed; the counselor asked rather than told, and the plan emerged from the client's own 'training experiment' idea. The client's contrast with his feared 'lecture' is direct evidence of full collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly supported ('You don't have to promise anything here; this is yours to shape') with genuine choices offered and no pressure or prescribing. The client's 'thanks for not pushing' confirms he experienced authentic choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight and plan element came from the client via skillful open questions. His self-generated coping ideas and success criteria demonstrate motivation drawn from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert from the opening line, with the experiment entirely the client's design—his metrics, tracker, and notes app. His closing comment ('planning my own training block') shows power-sharing produced genuine ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk, from early questions eliciting the client's own plan to generating specific success criteria and implementation language. The client's trajectory from 'I don't know if it's affecting much' to 'I'll actually do it' is strong evidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk using double-sided reflections and neutral reframing ('a fair test'). Minor deduction for occasionally restating sustain talk (e.g., 'weekends as normal') without always actively pivoting.
