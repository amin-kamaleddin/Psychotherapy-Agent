# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently validated the client without judgment, including immediately defusing resistance by validating his frustration about the 45-minute wait. Warmth and unconditional positive regard were maintained even when the client was dismissive or skeptical.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate throughout, capturing both content and the client's efficiency-driven worldview (e.g., 'the problem isn't knowledge, it's time'). Complex reflections consistently made the client feel understood, even anticipated humorously.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, progressive focus on weight/lifestyle change tied to blood pressure, moving logically from ambivalence to a concrete, measurable plan. Even playful exchanges never derailed the agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as equal partners, explicitly framing the relationship as partnership and negotiating the review format collaboratively. The 'Deal?' exchange treated the client's claims (punctuality) as legitimate.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client ownership with 'on your terms' framing and no imposed advice. Every plan element (parking, apples and nuts, numbers-first review) was generated or ratified by the client, and his boundaries were honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from within by eliciting the client's own reasons ('fewer tablets at 47', the stairs) rather than lecturing. The client talked himself into meal prep unprompted and closed reflecting on his own transformation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert on his own life and schedule, with client contributions genuinely shaping the session. Textbook power sharing where his ideas steered each next step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked escalating change talk ('I could commit' → 'I'm not ruling it out' → 'a plan I might actually follow') and consistently highlighted it. Open questions let the client generate his own reasons and plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Pivoted away from sustain talk without dismissing it, reframing 'no time' and 'food is just fuel' into solution-focused territory. Barriers were converted into planning material, and sustain talk diminished across the session.
