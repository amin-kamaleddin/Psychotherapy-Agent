# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard with no judgment throughout. Affirmations are genuine, individualized, and validate discouragement without pathologizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and emotionally attuned, including complex and two-sided reflections. Reflections consistently land and the patient builds on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on rehab engagement and physical activity with no topic drift. Every exchange moves progressively toward specifying steps and concrete commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is fully co-constructed from the patient's own ideas, with the counselor never prescribing. Patient's increasing elaboration shows his contributions drove the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are offered at every decision point, and the closing 'whichever way it goes' removes failure framing. Self-advocacy is reframed as taking charge of his own recovery.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key insights—pacing, splitting walks, the note, 'slow forward is still forward'—originate from the patient. Questions are strategically sequenced to deepen and strengthen his own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client, and his contributions (walks, note, speaking up) shaped the intervention. The patient confirms he experienced the expertise as his own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently evoke rich client change language ('slow forward is still forward,' 'I'd like to give it a real shot'), which is then reinforced and deepened. A clear arc from sustain talk to commitment-level change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk about pain and fear of regression is acknowledged briefly, then skillfully pivoted toward agency and solutions. Sustain talk progressively decreases by session's end.
