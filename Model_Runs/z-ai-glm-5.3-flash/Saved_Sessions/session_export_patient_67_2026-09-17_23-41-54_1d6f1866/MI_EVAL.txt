# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard; validated ambivalence ('the drinking helps take the edge off—that makes sense') without judgment, even when the patient minimized his drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including complex reflections capturing both sides of ambivalence and precise naming of emotional nuance ('that ache of for once').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on evening drinking and alternative coping throughout; every question advanced toward a concrete plan with a defined accountability check.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals with inclusive language ('how could we plan for it together') and jointly shaped decisions; the accountability check-in was co-constructed at the patient's suggestion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Advice never imposed; options were returned to the client, and the plan (walking, Wednesdays, skipping the Tuesday shop, the fridge note) was entirely self-authored with the counselor only scaffolding.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within via questions probing meaning and values, eliciting the patient's own insight ('actually feel better, not just not feel') and his own safeguards; commitment language was fully self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient positioned as the expert — all key ideas came from him and were explicitly credited ('your answer is your own'), visibly shaping the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and deepening of change talk; questions about why walking and what success would mean produced commitment language ('I'm actually gonna try to have good news'), which was reinforced by reflection.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used double-sided reflections to hold ambivalence without arguing or dwelling; minor room to improve, as sustain talk about tiredness and the beer's ease was occasionally restated at length before redirecting.
