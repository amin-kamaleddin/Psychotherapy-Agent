# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard throughout, with genuine affirmations and non-judgmental reception of Sofia's defensiveness and even dismissive remarks. Ambivalence was normalized ('Both make sense') without any judgment of her avoidance behaviors.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including complex double-sided reflections capturing both sustain and change elements of ambivalence. Sofia's own words were echoed effectively, and the emotional pain of isolation was validated rather than glossed over.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on school avoidance and peer isolation with no topic drift. Each exchange built progressively from exploration toward concrete next steps while remaining patient-paced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent invitational language and co-construction of the plan from Sofia's own ideas. Sofia explicitly noted this wasn't 'another adult lecturing me,' confirming the collaborative stance was palpable to her.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary framing of every step as Sofia's choice ('on your terms,' 'keeps you in control'), with no advice imposed. All plan elements were client-generated, and her pacing was honored without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the client—her own reasons, solutions, and coping resources—rather than supplied. This produced powerful self-generated insight ('the scared part isn't actually keeping me safe. It's just keeping me lonely').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sofia was explicitly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert on her and this friendship'), and her contributions directly shaped the plan. Her closing comment that she 'didn't expect to actually figure stuff out today' confirms the partnership was successful.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions skillfully evoked change talk that escalated from 'maybe if one of my friends started talking to me again' to a concrete plan (text this weekend, better attendance). Each piece of change talk was reflected and amplified to consolidate momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided dwelling on barriers, using double-sided reflections to pivot toward hope while validating realistic concerns. Sustain talk about the friend's history of 'lying a lot' was acknowledged but not always explicitly pivoted from.
