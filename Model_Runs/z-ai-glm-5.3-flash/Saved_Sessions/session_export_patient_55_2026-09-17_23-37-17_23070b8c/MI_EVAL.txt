# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong unconditional regard and frequent, specific affirmations throughout. Falls slightly short of 5 as affirmations occasionally feel gently corrective and warmth remains somewhat measured.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and consistently advances the conversation, correctly identifying the emotional core (anxiety about parents/IB) the client only implied.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear implicit focus with strategic progression from rapport to concrete strategy, but the target behavior is never explicitly named and no closing summary of next steps or follow-up plan is offered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals — the expert stance is explicitly de-legitimized and every step is offered as an invitation. The final summary is co-constructed from the client's own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced from the opening line and sustained throughout with no advice-giving, pressure, or coercive language — notable given the non-voluntary referral context. Ownership is returned to the client at the close.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client's own values and ideas, with zero counselor-provided reasons for change. A precontemplative client ended with her own concrete plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The intervention content (the Mia plan, the two-drink limit) originates entirely from the client, and the counselor explicitly signals that client expertise exceeds the screening's. Power-sharing is genuine rather than token.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic open questions produced genuine change talk ('I drank more than I meant to,' 'maybe deciding before I go how much') and a concrete plan, which were then reinforced with a change-oriented summary. Textbook evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and skillfully redirected via a strong double-sided reflection. However, the minimization ('it's not a regular problem') is left somewhat unchallenged at the end, leaving ambivalence only partially resolved.
