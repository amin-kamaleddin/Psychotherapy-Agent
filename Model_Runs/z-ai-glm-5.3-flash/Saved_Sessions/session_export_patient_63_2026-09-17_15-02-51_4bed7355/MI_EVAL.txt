# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, opening with 'I'm here to listen without judgment.' Affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captured Jordan's ambivalence and social fears. Sometimes pivoted to solutions too quickly, missing deeper exploration of guilt and self-loathing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unwavering focus on reducing shoplifting and building concrete alternatives, guiding the session to a specific plan with timing, wording, and contingencies. Focus never drifted.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally worked as an equal partner and co-constructed the plan from Jordan's ideas. Tended to present solution menus rather than brainstorming jointly, and Jordan's rehearsal resistance was not negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Jordan's choice and avoided coercion despite the court-mandated context. Persistently re-offered rehearsal after Jordan declined twice, subtly pressuring him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Jordan's own reasons and plans via open questions and scaling, ending with self-generated commitment. Occasionally supplied reasons and solutions himself, shifting motivational work to the clinician.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shared some power by letting Jordan pick options and generate his own ideas, but often adopted an expert stance with pre-written texts and scripts. Repeatedly pushed rehearsal despite Jordan explicitly saying it felt awkward.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evoked and reinforced change talk through open questions and confidence scaling, including skillfully asking what would raise his 5-6 to a 7. Sometimes answered his own questions by supplying content rather than letting Jordan generate motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk with neutral reflections but rarely used double-sided reflections or pivoted back to Jordan's reasons for change. Did not address Jordan's deflection ('I'll deal with that if it happens').
