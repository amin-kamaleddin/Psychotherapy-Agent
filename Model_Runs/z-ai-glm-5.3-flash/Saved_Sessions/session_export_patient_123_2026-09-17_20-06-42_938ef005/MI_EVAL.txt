# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, proactively normalizing the screening and responding without judgment ('Of course it's okay', 'There's no script here'). Affirmations are genuine and specific, with no hint of disapproval or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and nuanced, including a strong double-sided reflection about discomfort being worth the cost. However, reflections sometimes stay at content level rather than exploring deeper emotional undercurrents, such as the loneliness behind being 'the boring one'.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a thoughtful graduated arc from Sofia's social world to values and finally the alcohol question. Alcohol is named late in the session, and the counselor could have more explicitly checked in on her reaction to the referral, which she raised twice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction feels like joint exploration with the counselor soliciting Sofia's views before offering reflections. The counselor drives the session structure unilaterally and no plan or next step was co-created at the end.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Sofia's ownership ('I'm not grading your answers', 'That's your own strategy') with no advice-giving or pressure. Missing explicit emphasis that Sofia decides what, if anything, to change, and no invitation for her to set the agenda for next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Sofia's own values and protective reasons through open questions about friendships and goals. Brief dismissive answers ('things are fine right now') were accepted without deeper probing, and no commitment or confidence language was explicitly elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Sofia as the expert on her own life ('You know your world better than anyone'). Sofia shapes the content but not the direction, as the counselor retains control of the agenda without explicitly negotiating it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes strong change language ('I don't really see it fitting in anywhere', 'I have goals and drinking a lot doesn't really help with any of them'). Sofia's motivation was largely pre-existing, and the counselor could have deepened it with commitment-strength questions rather than moving on quickly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles minimize-and-dismiss responses without arguing, pivoting instead to Sofia's own experience and holding ambivalence with a validating double-sided reflection. Few deliberate pivots were required and no explicit reframing converted her 'annoyance' language into change-oriented language.
