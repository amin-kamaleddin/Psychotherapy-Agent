# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, judgment-free opening with consistent validation of Yasmin's feelings. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and repetitive ('thoughtful, doable plan'), lacking individualized depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures both content and emotion, including Yasmin's ambivalence about the gym and weight. Reflections are occasionally repetitive and deeper emotional pain (years of failure, 11pm spiraling) is acknowledged only lightly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains crystal-clear focus on shifting from scale-dependent patterns to sustainable gym habits. Every exchange moves the concrete plan forward, ending with a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan is genuinely co-constructed, with Yasmin generating the sticky note, sister text, and follow-up idea. The psychiatrist occasionally steers with 'Would you like...?' constructions and delivers the self-soothe script pre-formed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is repeatedly reinforced, and Yasmin's gradual approach to weighing is accepted without pushback. Some questions feel like guided multiple-choice with pre-packaged option menus rather than open autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled evocation via the vision question, confidence ruler, and future-orientation question drew out strong commitment language ('proof I can do this differently'). The psychiatrist sometimes answers for Yasmin and planning dominates the middle section, reducing evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Yasmin's expertise is positioned as central and her contributions genuinely shape the session. The psychiatrist sometimes supplies option menus rather than drawing solutions fully from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective questions ('What would feeling good look like?', confidence scaling) elicited clear change language. The psychiatrist sometimes jumps to planning and doesn't explicitly reflect change statements back to strengthen them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and gently reframed, but responses move quickly past it into planning. Fear of weight gain and 'slack off' shame are never directly explored with double-sided reflections.
