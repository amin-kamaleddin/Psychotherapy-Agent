# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental of Diego's harm-reduction approach, with genuine warmth ('I respect that you know your own pace'). Affirmations occasionally feel formulaic and focus more on competence than on Diego as a whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including accurate double-sided reflections and precise captures of Diego's core concerns ('safety net, not a supervisor'). Diego's fear of his housemate finding him dead was acknowledged but not explored emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical focus on heroin use and harm reduction, progressing from ambivalence to a concrete, achievable plan. Deeper emotional material (fear of overdose, family wounds) surfaced but was not integrated into the direction of change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine partnership throughout, with the plan co-constructed around Diego's own ideas and resources. One didactic block of psychoeducation (safer injecting) preceded eliciting his current practices, and the rhetorical 'we' was occasionally unchecked.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly hands control to Diego throughout ('What else feels like part of this week's plan, or is that enough for now?'), and affirms his self-directed pacing. Information is offered without pressure; no coercive or directive language appears.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully draws motivation from Diego rather than supplying it, eliciting powerful reasons for change, 10/10 confidence, and self-generated solutions. Even the psychoeducation ends in evocative questions that produce commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently locates expertise within Diego ('you've already shown you're a step ahead'), and his contributions genuinely shape the session's direction. Power-sharing is authentic and impactful.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertly evokes and reinforces change talk through open questions about desired direction and first small steps, yielding strong commitment ('I'll go this week. Seriously.'). Diego's own change language is repeatedly reflected and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well through double-sided reflections and respectful reframes without amplifying it. The escape function of heroin—which sustains use—is acknowledged but not deeply resolved.
