That last part hits hard—money shouldn't be what decides whether you get sick. And good news: most needle exchanges don't charge anything, they actually want you taking as many as you need, and some places deliver or have mobile vans that come closer. So cost doesn't have to be the barrier. We can look up where the nearest one is and what their hours are. Same with those alcohol wipes—usually free at the same place. Would it help if we figured out the transport piece too, since it's across town? Sometimes that's the real obstacle hiding underneath.
