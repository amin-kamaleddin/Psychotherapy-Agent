# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with no disapproval, even when the client describes repeated avoidance. Avoidance and freezing up are normalized with unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture both the behavioral loop and emotional layer ('nervous but ready'). However, the client's core self-doubt ('why start?') and fear of failure receive only brief acknowledgment rather than deeper exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on one target behavior, building logically from small step to confidence scaling, if-then planning, accountability script, and text timing. No topic drift; every exchange advances momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan and text message are genuinely co-constructed, with the client's edits and preferences (Thursday timing, roommate, snack break, wording) determining the final plan. Power-sharing is real rather than token.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is woven throughout (text options, session length, timing), with permission asked before suggestions and no pressure. Client's ownership of the final plan reflects strong autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within via confidence scaling and open questions, with the client articulating her own reasons and strategies. Deeper exploration of values and motivations is somewhat thin, pivoting quickly to behavioral planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently positions the client as the expert and co-constructs the plan and script. Score falls short of 5 due to rapidly proposed, densely stacked options that occasionally limit the client's free-form input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained evocation of commitment language with repeated confidence scaling; the client's confidence rises from 4-5 to 7.5 across the session with explicit change statements. The counselor tracks and affirms the rising confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated briefly and pivoted toward problem-solving, with self-doubt reframed as self-awareness. Occasionally responses are neutral rather than actively double-sided when past failed attempts arise.
