# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of Hana's choices and courage; no criticism of her avoidance. Praise is somewhat formulaic and lacks deep normalization of her struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections show decent perspective-taking, but the session shifts to action-planning with few complex reflections. Vulnerable statements like 'It's still scary' go largely unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior with specific, measurable steps and timelines. Hana leaves with a concrete plan, and the focus never drifts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice menus and incorporation of Hana's preferences are present, but the counselor sets most terms and supplies scripts and rules. Hana mostly selects from counselor-generated options rather than co-creating the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine options are offered and Hana's modifications are accepted without pressure. Autonomy is somewhat undermined by the prescriptive structure and heavily scaffolded decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and past-success questions elicited Hana's own reasons and confidence, but these moments are shallow and quickly redirected to planning. Her intrinsic motivations are rarely developed in depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing through choice menus, but the session is heavily counselor-structured with expert-led framing. Hana's ideas shape the plan, yet her expertise does not substantially steer the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective scaling questions drew a nice progression of readiness (4→6→7) and elicited her own reasons and success vision. Change talk could have been deepened with elaborative follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and is briefly validated, but there are no double-sided reflections or ambivalence exploration. The strategy sidesteps fear of failure rather than resolving it.
