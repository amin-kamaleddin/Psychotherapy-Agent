# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with compassionate handling of slips. However, affirmations feel somewhat formulaic and praise focuses on performance rather than the person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Content reflections are accurate ('lunch felt easier, work stress is the main trigger'), but emotional nuance is under-explored. Samuel's self-doubt and self-criticism after slips are acknowledged only superficially.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains unwavering focus on the target behavior, from journal review to a concrete, measurable plan with follow-up. Every counselor turn advances the agenda with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is largely between equals, with Samuel's tweaks adopted verbatim and plans co-constructed. However, the counselor frequently sets the agenda and presents pre-formatted plans unilaterally.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is consistently offered and Samuel's modifications were accepted without resistance. Still, frequent confidence-scaling borders on repetitive steering, and options are sometimes pre-packaged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samuel generated his own strategy, goal, self-efficacy statement, and support ideas through counselor questions. Evocation relies heavily on the 0–10 confidence scale rather than varied probes (importance, values).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samuel's own ideas substantially shaped the final plan (gum backup, plan tweak, reminder preference). Yet the counselor sometimes supplies ready-made menus and scripted plans rather than letting Samuel generate them first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation and consolidation of change talk via strategy questions and confidence scaling, ending with strong self-directed motivation. Reliance on a narrow technique repertoire limits the depth of evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled constructively with forward-looking questions and a slip-recovery reframe. However, ambivalence is never explicitly reflected, e.g., with double-sided reflections.
