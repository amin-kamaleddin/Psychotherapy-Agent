# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently respectful, warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing Daniela's anxiety and validating her difficulties without disapproval. Affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic, with limited deeper exploration of her fear of stigma.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening, including a strong double-sided reflection capturing Daniela's ambivalence about disclosure. Empathy was often brief, pivoting quickly to problem-solving rather than deeply exploring the emotional core of her fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on peer disclosure and progressively narrowed toward a concrete plan (one friend, specific time, contingency planning). Occasionally crowded the conversation with stacked questions, slightly diffusing the guiding thread.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session functioned as joint problem-solving with permission-seeking, role choices, and a plan built on Daniela's preferences. The counselor generated most of the actual content (scripts, replies), keeping power-sharing slightly uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was consistently reinforced through offering options, asking preferences, and honoring Daniela's pacing, who ended owning the plan entirely. At times the counselor directed the practice agenda and supplied scripts with implicit pressure rather than eliciting her own wording first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions successfully evoked Daniela's own reasons and commitment language. However, a substantial portion of the session involved advice-giving (scripts, replies, grounding techniques) rather than drawing ideas from the client, and her own generated ideas were never explored or built upon.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Daniela as the expert through permission-seeking, offering choices, and letting her shape the plan. The counselor supplied most of the actual wording and strategies and occasionally asked multiple questions at once.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully used importance/confidence rulers that elicited Daniela's own desire and a measurable confidence increase from 4 to 5, along with clear commitment language. Missed opportunities to deepen change talk by exploring why importance was a 6 or what would raise confidence further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and not reinforced, with one effective double-sided reflection. Little active softening occurred, as fears like 'chickening out' were met with practical suggestions rather than strategic exploration to resolve ambivalence.
