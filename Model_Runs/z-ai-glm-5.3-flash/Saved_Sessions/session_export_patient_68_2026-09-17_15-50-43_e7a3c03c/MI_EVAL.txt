# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with unconditional positive regard despite the client's defensiveness. Affirmations were somewhat generic, with limited individualized validation of his embarrassment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured Kevin's ambivalence and defensiveness well. Reflections often bundled feelings into summaries rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning, especially around his father's heart condition.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on the target behavior with purposeful progression from exploration to a concrete, behaviorally specified plan. No topic drift; every exchange moved the session forward logically.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership with Kevin's fingerprints on every element of the plan — strategies, excuse lines, supporter, and check-in time were all self-generated or co-constructed. Full collaborative power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Kevin's choice, asked permission, and let him select strategies on his terms. Some options and suggested lines were clinician-generated, with less explicit reinforcement of his ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked Kevin's own reasons for change via readiness and confidence rulers and cost questions. Moved somewhat quickly to offering solutions and didn't deepen the emotionally potent family health history.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Kevin as the expert, asking permission and letting him shape the plan. The counselor offered a fairly complete menu of strategies rather than first drawing out Kevin's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of readiness/confidence rulers and cost questions elicited meaningful change talk and commitment language. Missed opportunities to deepen early change talk and relied heavily on scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Never reinforced sustain talk and used double-sided reflections, but sustain talk was often bypassed rather than strategically softened. Restating drinking's benefits in summaries somewhat re-amplified it.
