# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard established explicitly in the opening ('whatever you decide here, you're worthy of care and respect'), with consistent normalization of ambivalence and no judgment about smoking throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emotionally accurate, progressive reflections (e.g., 'carrying this worry... while also lighting another cigarette') that surface deeper material; Rosa repeatedly confirms feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on smoking cessation moving from ambivalence to a concrete plan, start date, stumble plan, and closing task, with summaries consolidating the arc.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing: every plan element originated from Rosa, and she experiences the relationship as co-created ('we agreed on that, you and I').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Rosa's ownership of choice with zero imposed advice; explicitly names her self-generated change as 'the best kind of change.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation — questions like 'What's been keeping that hope alive?' drew out deeply personal reasons for change, and Rosa produced all motivations herself ('I'm talking myself into it').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise located firmly within Rosa; her surprise ('Did I just plan this?') directly evidences that her contributions shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes, reflects, and reinforces change talk anchored to Rosa's values (grandkids, stairs, daughter), ending with strong commitment language ('I think I'm actually doing this. Monday, one cigarette less.').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged then gently redirected with normalizing reframes ('a stumble could just be a stumble, not a verdict'), visibly softening across the session.
