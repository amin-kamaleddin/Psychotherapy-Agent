# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows basic respect and avoids judgment, but warmth is superficial and generic (e.g., 'nice choice'). Never deeply validates Sofia's unease about the referral beyond brief reassurance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal, staying at surface content without reflecting Sofia's feelings. Her anxious question about the screening and repeated minimizations ('it's not a huge deal — my grades are fine') are pivoted past with practical advice rather than explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Most serious failure: the alcohol-related screening referral is never addressed, and Sofia's direct question ('was there a reason I got called here?') is deflected inaccurately. The session drifts to generic study tips instead of the clinically relevant concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction follows an expert-recipient pattern with Sofia passively complying ('sure... that's fine', 'that was... fine'). Option-picking is superficial; her ideas are never invited and no agenda is co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Relative strength: Sofia is repeatedly offered genuine choices (topic, strategy, session length) and her decisions are respected. However, real autonomy is undercut by the prescriptive tip barrage and the inaccurate explanation of the referral, which denied her informed choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Motivation is never drawn from within the client; the approach is fundamentally advice-driven. No open questions explore Sofia's values, concerns, or the screening results that triggered the referral.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor positions himself as the advice dispenser, repeatedly re-delivering tip lists even after Sofia agreed to a plan. Her own expertise (breaks, outdoor activity) is noted once but not built upon, and the misrepresentation of the referral undermines honest partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No skillful evocation of change talk; the counselor jumps straight to strategy menus. Sofia's mild 'I could try putting it somewhere else' arose spontaneously and was never developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Positively, sustain talk is never amplified. However, Sofia's minimizations are neither reinforced nor actively softened — no double-sided reflections; the counselor simply moves past them, which is neutral rather than skillful.
