# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations and unconditional regard ('data, not judgment'). Warmth was brief, with quick movement to problem-solving that limited deeper acceptance of Kevin's embarrassment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Kevin's ambivalence and social pressures. Reflections were somewhat formulaic and could have explored the emotional weight of his self-criticism more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on reducing risky drinking, with each turn advancing toward a concrete, testable plan and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Work was genuinely co-constructed, with Kevin's ideas forming the plan's core. Rapid-fire option lists occasionally tilted the counselor toward advice-giving rather than full power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly and consistently reinforced, with Kevin's limits accepted without pushback and ownership protected throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin's own reasons, words, and solutions drove the plan, and his confidence rose from 6 to 7. Counselor sometimes supplied scripts and option menus that slightly reduced reliance on Kevin's resources.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin's input shaped the plan, including his alarm wording and refusal lines. Occasionally presented option menus rather than fully open-ended co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled use of confidence scaling, concrete commitments, and success criteria substantially increased Kevin's change talk. Less time explored Kevin's deeper personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated and reframed (e.g., keeping social life while reducing risk) without argument. Occasional joint focus on barriers without pivoting back to Kevin's capability.
