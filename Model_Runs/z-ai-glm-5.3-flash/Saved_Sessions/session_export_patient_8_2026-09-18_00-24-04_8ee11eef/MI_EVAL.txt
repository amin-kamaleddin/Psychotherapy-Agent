# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard ('There's real courage in what you just did'; 'That's not weird—that's you knowing your own needs') and validates both sides of ambivalence. No judgment or disapproval anywhere in the session, with warmth that feels genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including nuanced double-sided reflections about the wobbly and trying parts. However, when Amina disclosed harsh self-criticism ('god, you're so weak'), the counselor reflected it accurately but moved quickly to reframing rather than exploring the emotional weight of her inner harshness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains consistent focus on reducing social media use and its impact on sleep and coursework, skillfully linking exchanges back to these concerns ('that connects straight to the sleep you said has been garbage'). Each turn progressively advances toward change, from imagining a first step to specifying the experiment and defining success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership between equals—asks rather than tells, gives Amina choice about agenda, and defers to her on what 'success' and 'fair' mean. Positions himself as a curious companion rather than an expert.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces ownership and choice ('not something imposed,' 'your experiment, not a test you can fail') and honors her decision to keep the experiment private. Never pressures, persuades, or directs, and frames setbacks as information rather than failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every plan element (lecture choice, sticky note, ten-minute check window, definition of success) came from Amina in response to evocative questions. Never supplies reasons for change or advice, instead drawing out her own reasoning and reflecting her growth back to her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fosters power sharing so Amina's contributions shape the entire session—the plan is entirely her creation, elicited through questions. Explicitly asks her to define success on her own terms.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic open questions and amplifying reflections evoke a clear progression from 'every idea feels impossible' to 'mostly ready' and 'I'll actually try it.' Explicitly highlights and reinforces Amina's own commitment language throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Redirects effectively and normalizes anxiety without amplifying doubt, honoring ambivalence with double-sided reflection. Opportunities for deeper work were limited given mild sustain talk, and there was no deliberate pairing of sustain talk with prior change talk (e.g., linking her social connection needs with her grades concern) to resolve ambivalence more strategically.
