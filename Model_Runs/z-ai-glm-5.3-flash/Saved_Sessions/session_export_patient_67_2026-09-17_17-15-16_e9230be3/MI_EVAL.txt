### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with welcoming statements and validation of Kenji's ambivalence. However, affirmations are brief and formulaic rather than individualized to his specific strengths and insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Kenji's ambivalence and the functional role of alcohol (e.g., the 'off switch'). Reflections rarely touched the emotional layer beneath his hesitancy, such as loneliness or anxiety about being labeled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction throughout, progressing logically from exploration to a concrete, testable experiment with follow-up. Direction was firm without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was largely co-constructed from Kenji's own ideas, and the counselor drafted the text at his request. Some turns slid into protocol/checklist mode with pre-formatted options rather than fully shared brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly and explicitly supported Kenji's choices, accepting his rejection of options and his boundary-setting about Wednesday. Confidence scaling reinforced ownership throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used open questions and confidence rulers to elicit Kenji's own motivation, moving him from 'maybe nothing' to 'I can do one week.' Evocation was sometimes crowded out by logistics and information-gathering, limiting deeper exploration of values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Honored Kenji's expertise by letting him choose options and shape the plan's parameters. Occasionally slipped into a consultant mode with dense, batched practical steps that imposed an agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evoked change talk through open questions and confidence rulers, drawing tentative commitment from Kenji. Questions were often batched, diluting the chance to deepen key moments of change talk or explore underlying values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk neutrally and redirected toward exploration without reinforcing reasons against change. Relied on repeated invitations rather than skillful double-sided reflections or strategic reframing.
