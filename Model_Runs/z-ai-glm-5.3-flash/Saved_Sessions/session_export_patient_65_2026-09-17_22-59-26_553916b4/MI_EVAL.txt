# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental, normalizing responses throughout (e.g., to procrastination and court-ordered embarrassment), with affirmations of honesty. Julian's guardedness visibly softened, suggesting the regard was genuinely received.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and feeling, including skillful double-sided reflections ('part fear, part wanting it done'). Practical barriers were validated as real concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on securing a service placement, with each exchange advancing the agenda and no drift. Session ended with a specific, time-bound next step (texting the case worker today).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction felt like two equals problem-solving, with Julian's ideas integrated into the plan. Deduction for occasionally moving the agenda forward quickly, limiting Julian's space to redirect or raise concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership reinforced repeatedly ('Which of those feels most doable to you?'), with options offered rather than directives. Julian generated and selected his own follow-through strategy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions consistently elicited Julian's own reasons, capacity, and confidence, producing self-motivational statements and anticipated relief. Especially effective given Julian's guarded, minimally verbal presentation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Julian was positioned as the expert, and the plan genuinely originated from him. Deduction for occasionally supplying content himself (e.g., scripting the text, listing options), slightly diluting power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation and deepening of change talk — Julian moved from 'I guess' passivity to articulating his own reasons, plan, and anticipated relief. A clear developmental shift across the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently not amplified, and textbook double-sided reflections held it alongside change talk. Deduction for occasionally engaging avoidance talk directly, giving it some airtime instead of pivoting faster.
