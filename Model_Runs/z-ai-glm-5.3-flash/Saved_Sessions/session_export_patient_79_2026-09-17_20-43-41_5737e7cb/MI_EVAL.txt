# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with affirmations like 'you're the expert on your own life' and normalizing the client's skepticism. Accepted Luis's terms—including the 'bad mood' disclaimer—without any guilt-tripping.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured both content and meaning (simplicity, independence, forgetting as the real hurdle). Deeper emotional material—years of feeling criticized and feelings about his father—was touched but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained tight focus on asthma self-management from opening to closing ('One month, one call'), with each turn building toward a concrete plan. No drift off target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was fully co-constructed, with Luis shaping every element and the counselor explicitly framing it as 'your plan to shape.' Power-sharing was genuine—Luis tested it ('You'd actually let me pick?') and the counselor followed through.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was actively reinforced by offering options for follow-up and accepting Luis's rejection of fixed appointments. Explicitly framed the plan as 'you keep steering' with 'no guilt trip.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within—asking what would work, what felt doable, and what would make the inhaler routine stick. Luis's own reasons (independence, simplicity) drove the plan, and the counselor credited motivation back to him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently placed with the client, with the counselor contributing only questions and reflections. All substantive plan content (triggers, coffee anchor, pre-exertion puffs) came from Luis.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk through open questions about workable routines, prompting strong commitment language ('I can try that for a month,' 'I'll call'). Affirmations amplified and tied the change talk back to Luis's own competence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk by reflecting simplicity and Luis's preferences rather than his doubts, and gently deflected nagging concerns. Some sustain talk ('I'm not converted,' 'dropped them by February') was met with affirmation rather than dissolved through double-sided reflection.
