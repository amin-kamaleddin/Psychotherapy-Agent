# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard; met Marek's defensiveness with validation and genuine affirmations rather than judgment. Affirmations were somewhat sparse and could more often validate his worth independent of progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: precise complex reflections capturing both surface content and deeper meaning, including the beer-friends entanglement and underlying fear. Accurately named ambivalence without pathologizing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use with a coherent trajectory from minimization to concrete experimentation. Let extended dwelling on 'is it real yet?' occur without strategic summarization to consolidate progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint inquiry with curiosity-framed questions and explicit power-sharing; Marek repeatedly shaped the session's content and co-constructed ideas. No expert-driven stance appeared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: 'that's your call entirely,' experimental framing ('not a promise, just let's see'), and accepting Marek's right to not have it figured out. Never argued, persuaded, or imposed a goal.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Marek's own experience (work project, experiment ideas) rather than counselor arguments. Did not deepen the 'someday' thread with importance or confidence exploration toward resolution.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly located expertise in Marek ('You're the expert on your own life') and attributed all experimental ideas back to him. Direction was shaped through questions rather than prescriptions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk ('middle spot,' the non-alcoholic beer experiment) and reflected its evolution from 'unimaginable' to concrete ideas. Did not move toward commitment language or consolidate with a summary.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoided amplifying sustain talk, using double-sided reflections and a productive reframe of the caution-vs-excuse question. Occasionally let sustain talk (grandpa/genetics, 'wait until I get sick') stand unexplored longer than ideal.
