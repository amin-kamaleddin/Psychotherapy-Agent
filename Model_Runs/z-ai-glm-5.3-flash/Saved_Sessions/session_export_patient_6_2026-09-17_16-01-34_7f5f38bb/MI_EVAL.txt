# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirmed the client's insight and choices without scolding. Affirmation was somewhat formulaic, with little explicit normalization of adolescent experimentation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and validated key concerns like the scholarship fear. However, they stayed at the content level and missed deeper emotional material, such as feeling stuck or pressure to fit in.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and its link to soccer/scholarship goals throughout. The session progressed logically from exploration to rehearsal to a concrete commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Included brainstorming invitations and role-play at the client's request, but the power balance tilted toward the clinician supplying most material. A more equal partnership would elicit the client's own strategies first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered choice menus and avoided coercion, respecting the client's pace. The content of the skills and next-step agenda was largely clinician-directed rather than client-designed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of rulers and evocative questions drew out the client's own motivations ('Soccer is my whole plan'). At times, the counselor filled space with advice instead of continuing to elicit the client's ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for the client's input but frequently shifted into an expert/advice-giving mode with scripted refusal lines and exit plans. The client's own contributions, though strong, were underused.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance/confidence rulers elicited strong change talk ('I don't wanna become addicted'). Spontaneous change talk was not always deepened or linked back to commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled minimization without arguing, acknowledging his view and redirecting to goals. The pivot was effective but quick, without double-sided reflections naming the ambivalence.
