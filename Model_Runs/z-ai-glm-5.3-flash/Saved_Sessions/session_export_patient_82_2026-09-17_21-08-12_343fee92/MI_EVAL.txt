# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard from opening to close, with no judgment of the client's experimentation. Strong affirmations ('That's not luck, that's you') and normalization of imperfection.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including the complex reflection naming the belonging-vs-protection ambivalence and the 'one-off' narrative. Reflections landed so precisely the client asked 'Are you a mind reader or something?'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use in peer contexts while honoring the client's pace. Session progressed logically to a concrete plan and scheduled follow-up without being pushy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Equal-partner dynamic throughout, with the follow-up plan co-constructed at the client's suggestion. Counselor idea-giving was minimal and served only to draw out the client's own thinking.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decision-making was consistently handed to the client ('which one feels most doable for you?'), and all commitments were self-generated. No pressure, warnings, or directives at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every strategy (teammate ally, phone note, lock screen, exit plan) came from the client himself. Eliciting questions drew out the client's own solutions and self-motivators—evocation at its best.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit power-sharing ('You know these friends and these situations way better than I do') with the plan authored entirely by the client. Counselor role was to elicit and affirm rather than prescribe.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire, reasons, and commitment language ('I know which part of me I want to win', 'I'll text Marcus tomorrow'). Amplified Lukas's own strategy generation rather than supplying solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk, using double-sided reflections to soften ambivalence effectively. Occasionally restated sustain talk in reflections without immediately pivoting back toward change.
