# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with genuine affirmations ('That was really strong—calm, clear') and slips framed as learning rather than failure. Falls short of 5 because affirmations are somewhat brief and formulaic, with limited deeper individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures Elise's core ambivalence between quitting and fear of loneliness. However, reflections sometimes serve as brief transitions into advice, and opportunities to explore the emotional depth of her loneliness and fear of social rejection are missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, clear focus on smoking cessation with a concrete, dated action step (smoke-free Friday beer garden outing). Every turn ties back to the plan with no topic drift, progressively building toward a specific commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative—asks before advising, uses Elise's own words, and co-constructs the plan with her ideas incorporated. Falls short of 5 because the counselor often supplies the option menu rather than eliciting Elise's own solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice ('Would you like to brainstorm...?', 'Which of those feels easiest?') and positions Elise as decision-maker. However, frequent multi-option menus steer toward counselor-prepared solutions, with limited explicit reinforcement that she owns the outcome regardless of Friday's result.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation through rulers and rehearsing Elise's own words until she articulates her self-generated reason ('my reason, not someone else's rules'). Not a 5 because the counselor sometimes fills space with tactics rather than deepening her emerging motivations for connection without cigarettes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elise's choices are invited and her contributions shape some content, but the bulk of each turn is counselor-generated advice (scripts, tactics, a pre-written text). The flow is largely expert-driven rather than eliciting her ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance and confidence rulers with follow-up questions, and change talk visibly grows from 'a 5' and 'maybe' to 'I actually feel... sort of ready.' Not a 5 because her self-generated motivation ('my reason, not someone else's rules') is not deeply elaborated before moving to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced and is initially reframed well, but later responses shift toward practical tactics and scripts. Fears of rejection, peer pressure, and past relapse are answered with techniques rather than rolled with or reframed toward her own resolve.
