# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations of Kieran's choices and no criticism. Warmth was somewhat brief and practical rather than deep, limiting explicit validation of him as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections like 'just another box to check' accurately captured Kieran's resigned, monotonous experience. However, reflections stayed at the content level without exploring the deeper emotional underlayer of hopelessness and hurt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The target behavior of engagement with mandated activities was never clearly named or pursued; the session drifted into coaching a coping script. No agenda setting or linking of the script work to any larger change goal occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invited Kieran's picks among options, partially collaborative, but supplied all content unilaterally while Kieran passively selected. There was no joint problem-solving or invitation for his own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support: opening with 'You're in charge of what we talk about,' consistently offering genuine choices with permission to decline, and no pressure toward disclosure or commitment. Kieran explicitly noted the relief of being asked for less.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Beyond one early evocative question and a closing inquiry, the counselor did not draw motivation from within Kieran or explore what matters to him. Resources and answers were supplied by the counselor rather than evoked from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offering menu options and asking what felt okay conveyed that Kieran's input mattered, but the interaction was overly structured with the counselor supplying all content. Kieran's contributions never substantially reshaped the session, and his expertise was never explicitly acknowledged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** One question elicited a mild preference statement, and a faint glimmer of change talk ('I'd probably actually show up') was reflected but never amplified. There was no deliberate evocation of desire, ability, reasons, or commitment toward any change goal.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoided amplifying resistance by reflecting sustain talk neutrally and pivoting to practical help. However, restatements of sustain talk lacked double-sided reflections, and the session ended accepting 'Same as always' without gently challenging the resignation.
