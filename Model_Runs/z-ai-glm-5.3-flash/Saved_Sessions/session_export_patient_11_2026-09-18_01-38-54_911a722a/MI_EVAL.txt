# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful tone throughout with unconditional positive regard; past quitting is framed neutrally as learning rather than failure. No judgment or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are generally precise, capturing key values like 'feeling capable in my own body again.' The late 'flat and heavy' reflection was inaccurate to Amira's consistently hopeful state, one significant empathic drift.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every exchange stays focused on increasing physical activity, matching Amira's stated target, and progresses logically toward a concrete plan. The nutrition topic is acknowledged without drifting off-course.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership with open questions, co-constructed plan, and negotiated check-in structure. The 'flat and heavy' interpretation was imposed rather than checked, briefly disrupting the equal-partner feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership is explicitly and repeatedly reinforced ('because it came from you,' 'finding your own way there'), with no advice imposed. Amira defines both the plan and her own criteria for success.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently elicited rather than prescribed, drawing out Amira's own solutions and rich change talk. However, evocation relies on a modest question repertoire, and one prompt built on a premise the client never voiced.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Amira is positioned as the expert on her own life, and her contributions ('low-hanging fruit,' backup plan, no report card) demonstrably shape the session. Full, genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful scaling question and reflections elicit ability, desire, and commitment language, which are deepened and reinforced. Not a 5 due to limited evocative variety and one evocation resting on the inaccurate 'flat feeling' premise.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never amplified—past failure is reframed as learning, and barriers immediately pivot to problem-solving. The 'chore' to 'something I'm giving myself' shift converts resistance into approach language.
