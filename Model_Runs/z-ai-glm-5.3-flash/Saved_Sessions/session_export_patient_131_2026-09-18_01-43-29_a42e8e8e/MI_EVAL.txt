# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently affirmed Wiremu's autonomy, framing decisions as his own ('that reframe feels like it belongs to you') and honoring his cultural context with kōrero and whānau-centered language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections repeatedly captured both sides of his ambivalence and its emotional weight, e.g., 'The scary part and the committed part can sit together.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Each question built toward commitment — from the six-month vision to concrete plans (weekend kōrero, ring-first rule) — with no meandering or irrelevant detours.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation was fully collaborative; the clinician invited Wiremu's expertise ('what do you reckon...') and he authored all plans and rules himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Wiremu's choice was central throughout: 'choosing them, not being sent there' was highlighted and reinforced as his own design of his safety net and terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong change talk was evoked via open questions about meaning and future ('what would it mean... walking out of that conversation?'), drawing out 'I'm doing this' and his identity as the dad his kids deserve.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician followed Wiremu's lead, affirming his reframes and allowing him to develop each plan in his own words rather than imposing direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was abundant and progressively strengthened — from ambivalence to commitment ('I'm doing this', 'write it down — hold me to it') — with the clinician selectively reflecting and consolidating it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (pride, fear of losing independence, doubt) was acknowledged and reprocessed rather than confronted, helping Wiremu resolve it into commitment himself.
