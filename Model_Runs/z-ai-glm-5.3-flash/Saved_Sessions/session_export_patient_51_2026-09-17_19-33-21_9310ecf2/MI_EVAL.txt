# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful tone with genuine affirmations throughout and no judgment. However, affirmations were somewhat generic and frequently repeated near the end, diluting their individualized depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate opening reflection and validation of Ingrid's emotional undercurrent. Missed opportunities to explore the meaning behind her habit of letting health concerns slide, and reflections occasionally shifted into affirmations rather than deepened understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on medication safety despite time pressure, with a concrete plan and appropriate redirection of the citalopram duration question to the GP. Repeated goodbyes added some unfocused length to the close.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative — Ingrid's preference for the pharmacy flag shaped the plan, and the final summary echoed her own words. Direct information-giving about the drug interaction limited full co-construction, though power-sharing was strong for a brief encounter.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout domain — choices were explicitly framed as Ingrid's, her ownership was reinforced, and no pressure was applied. Only the repetitive farewells slightly dragged the close.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A few good open questions drew out Ingrid's priorities, but motivation was largely supplied by the clinician rather than elicited. No exploration of her own reasons, confidence, or what sustaining change would take.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Ingrid as the expert on her own life and let her contributions drive the plan. The direct drug-interaction answer was appropriate, though checking her existing knowledge first would have strengthened the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was well captured and amplified when it appeared, including reframing her commitment as a real change. However, no proactive evocation of deeper change talk — no exploration of reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled minimal sustain talk well, accommodating the time constraint without indulging avoidance and reframing her 'later pile' pattern as notable change. Limited opportunity to demonstrate more strategic softening.
