# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains nonjudgmental respect for Daniela's financial struggles, distrust of family, and smoking/drinking. However, warmth is minimal and affirmations of her strengths are rare, with only one clear affirmation in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections are accurate but largely disappear after the opening turns. Emotionally loaded disclosures about shame, fear of rejection, and fear of seeming unreliable are skipped over rather than named or validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays well-focused and produces concrete progress: the neighbor text was drafted and rehearsed, and Early Head Start was identified with next steps. Minor drift occurs when smoking/drinking is dispatched with tips rather than woven into a shared agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is noticeably unequal, with the counselor providing pre-written texts, scripts, checklists, and directives. Genuine collaboration occurs only when Daniela pushes her own agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menus and choices are occasionally offered, and Daniela selects her preferred path. Autonomy is undercut, however, by prescriptive instructions, copy-paste scripts, and 'try this' directives with no explicit statement that decisions are hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is treated as residing in the counselor's advice rather than the client. Daniela's own motivational insights are never reflected, affirmed, or developed into deeper commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly occupies the expert role, drafting texts and directing next steps while claiming the structuring role throughout. Daniela's best ideas are incorporated only in a lukewarm, script-editing way.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela generates meaningful change talk herself, but the counselor fails to reinforce or deepen any of it, responding instead with rapid-fire advice. No open questions elicit desire, ability, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced or argued with, but ambivalence is consistently neutralized with practical solutions rather than acknowledged. The significant sustain talk around stress-driven smoking is bypassed entirely.
