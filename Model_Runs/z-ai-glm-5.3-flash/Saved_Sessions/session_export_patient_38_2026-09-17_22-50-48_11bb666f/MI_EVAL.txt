# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental ('That's not silly at all,' 'no embarrassment needed'). Immediately respected Elena's 'no pushing' request without defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured ambivalence, though reflections sometimes shifted quickly into questions. Deeper emotional content (fear of failure, family dynamics) was acknowledged but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on contraception decision-making with logical progression from ambivalence to concrete steps. A brief information-giving moment about the pill interrupted the evocative flow, and a closing summary of the plan was missed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently worked as equal partners ('we can figure out together what fits your life'). Ideas genuinely originated from Elena and the counselor built on her contributions rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support—explicitly honored 'no pushing' and 'you get to set the pace completely.' Information was offered without persuasion, and choice was framed as belonging to Elena throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Elena's own motivations and vision of control, leading to strong client-generated reasons for change. One instance of proactive information-giving (fertility myth) briefly shifted from evoking to telling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly conveyed that expertise resides in Elena ('You know your situation better than anyone'). Elena's contributions demonstrably shaped the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk, which grew progressively stronger from vague relief to a concrete plan. Elena committed to specific steps (website tonight, visiting the campus clinic).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoided amplifying sustain talk by affirming autonomy and small steps. Sustain talk was neutralized rather than actively reframed with double-sided reflections.
