# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; minimization met with curiosity, and affirmations are genuine and specific.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong accurate reflections, including double-sided ones, but reflections occasionally stayed at the surface rather than exploring the deeper emotional layer (shame, fear of letting others down).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on drinking that progresses from the injury incident to ambivalence to a concrete two-week trial with defined success markers.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The exchange is genuinely between equals, with co-constructed decisions and summaries used to check accuracy rather than direct.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kevin sets his own limits, timeline, and success criteria; the counselor validates his choice to start small with no pressure or agenda imposition.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every key insight and motivation came from Kevin himself, with evocative questions drawing out the session's most powerful change talk about regaining control.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kevin is repeatedly and explicitly positioned as the expert, and every plan element originated from him, with his contributions substantially shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions tied to Kevin's motivations deepened his change talk visibly across the session, from 'maybe' statements to 'that's the deal I'm making with myself.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally held alongside change talk with effective double-sided reflections, though a few questions briefly invited status-quo talk, handled strategically and recovered well.
