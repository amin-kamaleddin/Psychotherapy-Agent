# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with warm normalization of Sofia's honesty and low-key preferences. However, affirmations are somewhat generic and formulaic rather than individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflections are brief factual parroting rather than emotionally attuned. Sofia's minimization, mild defensiveness, and anxiety about school follow-up are never explored or reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays loosely tied to alcohol screening and harm reduction but has no clear collaborative agenda or behavioral target. It drifts from screening to generic safety tips to school-reporting logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session is counselor-driven, with rapid multi-question turns, tip lists, and pre-written scripts while Sofia mostly complies. 'You're in charge' statements are not matched by genuine shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A relative strength — the counselor repeatedly affirms choice ('You're in charge') and offers optional tips and menus. Falls short because autonomy is expressed through menu-offering rather than reinforcing Sofia's capacity to own the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Motivation is never elicited from the client — the counselor asks nothing about Sofia's values, goals, or reasons for caution. Instead it delivers unsolicited advice and scripts even after she says she doesn't need them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Superficial power-sharing via repeated 'You're in charge' statements and A/B/C menus. Sofia's actual input has limited influence on the session's direction, and expertise is counselor-supplied rather than drawn from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Virtually no effort to evoke change talk — no questions about Sofia's reasons, values, or hopes for change. Sofia produces no language favoring change beyond passive acceptance of a pre-written text.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's repeated minimization ('it's just a normal teen thing,' 'I don't think I need them') is neither reinforced nor strategically redirected. The counselor sidesteps ambivalence by moving straight to harm-reduction tips instead of using double-sided reflections.
