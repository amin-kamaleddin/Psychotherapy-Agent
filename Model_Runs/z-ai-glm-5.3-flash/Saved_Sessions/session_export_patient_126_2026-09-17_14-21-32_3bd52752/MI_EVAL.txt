# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Polite and nonjudgmental throughout with warm framing ('no shame if it feels rubbish'), but no affirmations of Thomas's strengths such as not smoking around his child or his willingness to try the experiment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate ('the cigarette is your ritual and rare peace'), but empathy stayed shallow — the meaning of his 'five minutes of peace' and his growing irritation at repetitive recaps were never reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking and the stepping-out ritual, but progress came through repetition rather than movement, and the alcohol concern Thomas raised ('before you start on the drink') was never addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asking permission and offering menu options showed collaboration, but the plan was delivered as a finished package of tips rather than co-created, and the client's jokes about being talked at signaled an uneven balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices were offered and imperfection was normalized, but relentlessly repeating the plan after the client repeatedly signaled he had heard it felt controlling and dismissive of his preference for brevity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was rarely drawn from the client — the importance ruler answer of '2' went unexplored, and no confidence, values, or client-generated reasons for change were elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-asking and offering choices demonstrated partnership, but prescriptive 'quick tips' bypassed the client's expertise about what the cigarette ritual actually provided.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk was grudging and counselor-prompted; the ruler response was never deepened, and Thomas's commitment and its condition ('if it's rubbish, I'm going back to my usual') were not elaborated or reflected.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** No amplification of sustain talk and occasional reframing, but statements like 'there's nothing to cut back from' and 'stretching sounds silly' were bypassed rather than acknowledged with double-sided reflections.
