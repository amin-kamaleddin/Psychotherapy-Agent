# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout; Evelyn's minimization and skepticism are never met with disapproval, and her pub routine and limits are consistently validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture her ambivalence, but emotional nuances (fear around the fall, mixed feelings about her son's concern) go unexplored, with quick shifts to information-giving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every exchange ties back to alcohol use, progressing logically from exploration to a concrete, measurable plan (Tue/Thu dry days, diary, med review, refusal role-play) with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is fully co-constructed around Evelyn's constraints, and her edits (revised doctor's note wording) visibly shape the session — a hallmark of true partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Permission is sought at each step, options rather than directives are offered, and Evelyn's limits (keeping pub days) are immediately validated rather than pushed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions successfully draw out Evelyn's own reasons and plan terms, but psychoeducation supplies some reasons for change, and confidence/importance is never elicited (e.g., no scaling questions or exploration of 'partly for him').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and preference-asking position Evelyn's expertise centrally, though some content (scripts, templates, med info) was delivered in large ready-made chunks with limited client input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Targeted questions elicit genuine change talk, which is reinforced by co-developing a concrete plan, but the counselor rarely reflects change talk back to deepen it and doesn't use confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with validating double-sided reflections rather than argument, though some psychoeducation was somewhat didactic and a few reflections restated her minimization without a change-side counterweight.
