# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental toward a defensively presenting teen, thanking her for honesty and framing the session around her own priorities. Affirmations were present but brief, keeping it just below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate summaries and reflections captured Anika's perspective, including validation of her social concerns. Reflections were somewhat formulaic and didn't deeply explore emotional undercurrents like stress, family conflict, or the friendship.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on vaping and basketball eligibility and efficiently built a concrete, time-bound plan (text tonight, backup lines, if-then coping). Every turn advanced the plan without being pushy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaboration throughout: repeated permission-seeking, menu-based options, and full accommodation of Anika's edits and refusals. The plan genuinely emerged from her own contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently supported via permission-seeking, menus, acceptance of refusals, and letting her keep her harm-reduction stance. Could have more explicitly reinforced her ownership of the change process itself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Anika's own motivations through open questions, confidence scaling, and plan co-construction. Slightly limited by occasional reliance on counselor-generated suggestions rather than pure evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Anika as the expert: her text wording was adopted verbatim and her self-identified alternatives became the coping plan. Slightly limited because most specific options were clinician-generated rather than drawn from her first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence ruler effectively evoked and deepened change talk, tracking commitment from 7 to 9 with reinforced reasons for change. Missed evocative 'why a 7 and not a 3' exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument, using double-sided reflections and pivots to her reasons for change. Could have more actively reframed her 'it's not a big deal' belief beyond the eligibility frame.
