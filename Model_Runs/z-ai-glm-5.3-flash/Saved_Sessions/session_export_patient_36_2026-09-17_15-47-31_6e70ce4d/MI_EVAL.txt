# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicated consistent respect and warmth with no judgment. However, affirmations were somewhat formulaic and missed deeper individualized validation of Elena's emotional breakthrough about self-priority.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but largely content summaries rather than emotional understanding. Emotionally significant moments ('keeping promises to myself,' 'allowed to make myself a priority') were only briefly acknowledged before pivoting to logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained crystal-clear focus on protecting Sunday mornings throughout, with every turn advancing toward a concrete commitment. There was no drift, and follow-up review was explicitly arranged.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was largely a joint effort, with Elena shaping key plan elements like the drawer cue and tracking markers. However, rapid multi-question barrages and pre-packaged materials tilted the exchange toward counselor-led tool delivery.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena was consistently framed as the decision-maker and confirmed ownership of the plan. Autonomy was partially undermined by the counselor supplying ready-made scripts and templates rather than eliciting Elena's own wording.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions effectively elicited Elena's own motivations, and her confidence rose from 7 to 8. Deeper values statements about self-worth emerged but were not explored or amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Input was sought and choices offered, but the session became increasingly tools-driven, with the counselor supplying most of the concrete scaffolding. This gave a semi-expert feel rather than full power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance/confidence rulers and vision questions successfully elicited strong change talk, including Elena's 'I want to keep promises to myself.' Deeper commitment language and late-session self-respect insights were left underdeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anticipatory obstacles like 'just this once' urges were treated as problem-solving material, keeping focus on solutions. Repeatedly asking about barriers in successive turns, however, invited unnecessary elaboration of potential obstacles.
