# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard ('no judgment here... exactly as you are today') with genuine affirmations and validation throughout. No judgment, labeling, or disapproval appears anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including an insightful reframe about chaotic mornings that the client explicitly confirmed ('I never thought of it that way'). Reflections capture both facts and the client's emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on medication adherence from opening to close, moving strategically from barriers to a concrete, client-owned plan (coffee-anchor system, shopping list, two-week follow-up). Momentum is guided without pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaves as a genuine equal partner, co-constructing decisions about check-in timing, success criteria, and support involvement. Actively seeks the client's own framing of success rather than imposing clinical standards.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces client ownership ('You're the expert on your own life', 'adjusting counts as winning too') and accepts the client's modest commitment style. All solutions are client-generated, and the client recognized this: 'it feels like mine.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertly draws out the client's own motivations and solutions through open questions and the confidence ruler rather than supplying reasons for change. Elicits rich change talk and self-generated insight ('I can't fix what I'm pretending isn't happening').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions the client as the expert, with session solutions (coffee anchor, husband's involvement, check-in timing) substantially shaped by client contributions. Questions build on client expertise rather than dispensing advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk using open questions, the confidence ruler, and follow-ups on success criteria. Strategically reflects and amplifies client momentum ('You've just done something important here').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, validating the client's caution ('That caution makes sense') and immediately redirecting toward change. Sustain talk is never argued against but reframed as wisdom.
