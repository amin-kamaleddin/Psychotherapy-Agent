# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with validation of Kevin's embarrassment and social priorities. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured Kevin's ambivalence and social stakes. Emotional weight of self-deprecation and fear of being seen as 'a bore' could have been reflected more deeply before moving to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on the target behavior with a concrete next step for Friday; every turn built logically toward a specific, testable plan. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent collaborative framing with decisions co-constructed; Kevin selected strategies and added his own tweak (cash only). Option lists occasionally led rather than followed, making the power balance slightly uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect for Kevin's choices and modest goals; Kevin explicitly noted this felt different from 'being told to stop everything.' Suggestions were sometimes delivered in ways that assumed uptake.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin's own ideas (pacing, ankle excuse, cash only) shaped the plan and confidence scaling raised his confidence from 5–6 to 7.5. Motivation stayed tactically focused rather than exploring Kevin's deeper values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin was repeatedly positioned as the decision-maker, and his expertise visibly shaped the session. A dense stream of pre-packaged suggestions at times tipped toward advice-giving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully built on Kevin's change talk ('a bit more careful') and used confidence scaling that measurably moved him toward commitment. Evocation was behaviorally focused rather than values-focused.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged respectfully but not consistently reframed toward change. Double-sided reflections were used only once; more active pivoting toward Kevin's stated reasons for change is needed.
