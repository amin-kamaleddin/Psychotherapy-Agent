# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, validating Maria's skepticism as smart self-advocacy rather than labeling it non-compliance. Responded to her vulnerable disclosure about her daughter with genuine respect and affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and precise, capturing both surface statements and underlying meaning. The reflection on her daughter's fear as possibly her strongest motivation demonstrated deep understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on inhaler adherence despite Maria's disengagement attempts, progressing from resistance to a concrete plan. The final reflection consolidated the session arc effectively.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely egalitarian, with information offered as a menu and the plan co-constructed from Maria's own words. Even admitting potential knowledge limits leveled the power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Maria's decision-making authority and honored her stated conditions without pushing for more commitment. Framed her clearly as the expert on her own life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn almost entirely from Maria herself through skillful open questions about what mattered to her. Her self-generated plan and unprompted disclosure about her daughter confirmed motivation resided with her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Maria as the expert throughout, asking permission before providing information and adopting her stated preferences and terms. Her own three questions became the action plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk, with Maria progressing from skepticism to booking a doctor's appointment and possibly trying the inhaler. Amplified change talk through affirmations rather than pushing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally reframed skepticism as protective self-advocacy and used double-sided reflections well. However, fairly extensive engagement with side-effect content kept some session airtime on reasons against the inhaler rather than consistently pivoting back to change.
