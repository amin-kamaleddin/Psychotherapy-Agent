# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgment throughout; no lecturing or disapproval, explicitly noted by the client who expected a lecture but felt accepted instead. Fears are normalized and missing log entries are framed without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflections capture Rosa's ambivalence, identity concerns, and desire for choice. Reflections are sometimes functional rather than emotionally deep, leaving the emotional weight of her smoking identity and fear of self-disappointment underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation from start to finish, with every turn advancing a concrete goal. The session ends with a specific structured plan rather than drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decisions are jointly negotiated—tracking method, template content, reminder timing, and accountability style all emerged from dialogue. Rosa experiences the session as genuinely shared work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is reinforced through permission-seeking, offering choices, and naming Rosa's ownership of her pace. Her closing comment that this feels chosen rather than forced confirms effectiveness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the client, eliciting her own reasons and self-generated commitment. A 5 would require deeper elicitation and amplification of her core values and expressed hope near the end.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Rosa is treated as the expert, with her contributions substantially shaping the session and plan. The plan is genuinely co-constructed and even enhanced by the client herself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful open questions evoke client-owned commitment that grows across the session, culminating in Rosa picturing a life with fewer cigarettes. Strong change statements, like her doctor-inspired motivation and hope, could have been deepened further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and typically reframed toward agency. Some reflections simply restate the sustain talk without an active pivot toward change.
