# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with consistent affirmations and validation of ambivalence. However, affirmations are brief and formulaic, missing explicit affirmation of Camila's specific personal strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that accurately captures her experience, including weekday creep and weekend anxiety. Reflections occasionally serve as transitions into question menus, and the loneliness/fear of 'looking boring' wasn't explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction with each exchange moving purposefully toward goals. The session progresses logically from importance to confidence to concrete commitments without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan is visibly co-constructed, with Camila selecting, modifying, and rejecting components. The repetitive menu format keeps the clinician in an agenda-setting role rather than fully shared brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports her right to decide, honors her rejections, and reinforces her ownership throughout. Camila's closing realization that she doesn't have to quit everything reflects genuine experience of self-directed choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits Camila's own reasons, strategies, and hope rather than persuading. Rising confidence from 6 to 7.5 is direct evidence of motivation successfully evoked from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Camila as the expert, with her contributions genuinely shaping the plan. Somewhat menu-driven and clinician-paced, limiting her influence on session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk via importance/confidence rulers, her own solutions, and her own refusal line. Tracked her confidence progression (6 to 7.5) alongside spontaneous change talk like 'this feels more doable than I thought.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with accurate reflections and pivots toward actionable options without reinforcing barriers. Redirections are somewhat rapid; barriers could be deepened with double-sided reflections linking back to her values.
