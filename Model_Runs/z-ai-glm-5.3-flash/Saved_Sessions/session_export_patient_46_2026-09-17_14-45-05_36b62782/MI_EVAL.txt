# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance, validating Ethan's annoyance and avoiding any criticism of his drinking. Few explicit affirmations of Ethan beyond 'Choosing small steps shows you care' keep it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections repeatedly capture Ethan's perspective, including peer-pressure worries ('worried about looking like a wimp — that makes sense'). Reflections are sometimes brief and repetitive, and deeper emotional content is touched but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on Ethan's drinking throughout, culminating in a concrete plan ('three-then-soda') and confidence scaling. No drift off-task, though the pre-set focus on cutting back slightly narrows the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor seeks permission, checks understanding, invites Ethan's picks, and co-creates a plan with active role-play. Power-sharing is uneven in the advice sections, where Ethan mostly reacts to counselor-supplied ideas rather than co-generating them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is consistently offered ('What would you like from this conversation?', 'Which feels easiest?'), and Ethan's decisions shape the session. The advice-heavy menu structure constrains options and there is no explicit statement that the choice and pace of change belong entirely to Ethan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence ruler and trade-off questions elicit genuine change talk ('tired of feeling like garbage on Saturday mornings'). However, motivation is often drawn toward the counselor's predefined solutions, with key evocative questions about Ethan's own values left unasked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and preference questions are present, but the middle session is dominated by counselor-generated advice lists, placing Ethan in a reactive role. When asked what ideas he had in mind, the counselor immediately supplies the full list rather than exploring Ethan's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Trade-off questions, confidence scaling, and concrete planning evoke strong change talk and a specific weekend commitment, with confidence rising from 5 to 6. A 5 would require deeper exploration of Ethan's own reasons and values rather than guiding him toward the counselor's strategy menu.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally acknowledged briefly and met with double-sided reflections that pivot toward change. The extensive harm-reduction advice menu implicitly accepts continued drinking without deeper exploration, and missed chances to explicitly develop discrepancy keep this from a 5.
