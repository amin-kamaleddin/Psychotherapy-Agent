# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental stance with genuine affirmations of the client's insight and confidence. Missed opportunities to normalize struggle or setbacks would have deepened unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate complex reflections capturing both content and emotional nuance, e.g., reflecting the plan to outsmart the couch and the shift from 'movement slipped away' to a concrete YMCA timeline.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on building a sustainable exercise routine, with each question building progressively toward a concrete three-part action plan without tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange between equals where the client's priorities (location over amenities, YMCA, step tracker) visibly shaped the plan. No expert-driven steering.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the client's ownership—framing the plan as her choice, not a should—with no advice, directives, or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from within, eliciting the client's own values (energy, strength, confidence) which she articulated herself as her intrinsic 'motivation system.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert on what will keep her coming back; all ideas originated from her and the counselor built on them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently evoked and deepened commitment language, reinforced with affirmations and follow-ups, culminating in the client's concrete plan (visit YMCA this week, take a walk, pack the bag).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers (couch, rain, sales pressure) were acknowledged but never amplified, reframed constructively into planning strategies. Occasional double-sided reflections could have added texture, though ambivalence was minimal.
