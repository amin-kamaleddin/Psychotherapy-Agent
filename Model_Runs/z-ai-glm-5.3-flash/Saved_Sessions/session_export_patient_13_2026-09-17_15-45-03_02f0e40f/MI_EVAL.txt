# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with normalization of fear ('a shaky voice is totally normal') and affirmation of courage after the call. Affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic, with limited explicit unconditional regard for her long-standing avoidance pattern.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected Anara's core ambivalence early on, prompting deeper disclosure about fear of confirmation. Reflections were somewhat sparse, and emotional moments (e.g., the baby-related fear) were occasionally bypassed in favor of practical next steps.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus on the target behavior from the first question through a clear, progressive sequence: barriers → what a check involves → question list → day selection → script → accountability → actual call. The session culminated in a booked appointment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership: Anara modified the script, chose the gentle text, proposed sending it immediately, and requested the live call — the counselor adapted flexibly to every adjustment. The successful call was a shared achievement she described in first person ('I did it').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice at every juncture ('your choice,' 'which helps most'), and did not press when Anara said 'I'm not promising anything.' Her sense of ownership was evident in her self-directed decision to call immediately.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Anara's own motivation — her fear, her chosen day (Thursday), and her request for accountability. Relied somewhat heavily on providing tools and information rather than exploring her deeper intrinsic reasons for change (e.g., the baby-related fear).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Anara's expertise as central, letting her select next steps (script version, sister text, grounding, role-play) so her contributions genuinely shaped the session. Provided substantial informational content at points in a somewhat directive way, though always with a choice attached.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk, culminating in Anara naming a day, requesting accountability, role-playing, and making the actual call. Could have deepened her own stated reasons for change, such as what getting checked would mean for her and her baby.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk ('I'll probably talk myself out of it') without reinforcing it, and used anticipated barriers constructively to build the accountability plan. Missed opportunities to more actively reframe her self-sabotage pattern, but avoided arguing against avoidance.
