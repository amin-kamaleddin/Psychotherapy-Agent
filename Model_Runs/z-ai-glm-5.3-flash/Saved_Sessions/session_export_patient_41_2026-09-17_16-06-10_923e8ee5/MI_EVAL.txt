# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with affirmations for Elira's bravery and normalization of her nervousness. However, affirmations are somewhat generic, and explicit unconditional regard is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capturing both cognitive and somatic experiences, such as the chest tightness driving escape. However, reflections occasionally repeat Elira's words, and the emotional depth of her self-criticism and fear of judgment is not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on avoidance and follow-through from the first utterance, with every exchange building toward a concrete target. The session progresses logically from exploration to behavioral rehearsal to commitment with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session feels largely collaborative, with input sought before each step and genuine interactive role-play. However, the counselor generates nearly all ideas and scripts, positioning Elira more as a chooser than a co-creator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support with choices offered at every juncture, and Elira's pacing preferences consistently honored without pressure. She exercised autonomy by choosing options and deferring the text send.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation elicited through role-play and choice questions, with Elira discovering her own capability. However, heavy reliance on menu-based questions rather than open exploration of Elira's values, hopes, or confidence, with minimal elicitation of her own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, and her contributions demonstrably shape the session, with the counselor accommodating each request. However, the counselor often supplies all options and scripts, limiting Elira's role as the expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation and reinforcement of change talk, with Elira moving from skepticism to voluntarily committing to send the text after practice. Missed opportunities to deepen change talk through evocative questions or confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged but not reinforced, with the counselor pivoting toward actionable steps and reframing avoidance as understandable. A double-sided reflection when Elira expressed skepticism would have resolved ambivalence more explicitly.
