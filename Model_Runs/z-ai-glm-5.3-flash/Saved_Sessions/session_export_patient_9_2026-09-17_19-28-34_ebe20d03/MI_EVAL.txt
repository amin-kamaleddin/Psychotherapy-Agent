# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and non-judgmental; normalizes self-doubt and offers specific, individualized affirmations (e.g., 'You're the one constant your son has'). Closing attribution of change to the client reflects deep unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex, accurate reflections capture Amina's dual burdens and subtle fears (e.g., not making dad the 'bad guy,' son 'noticing more than I realized'). Her repeated deepening of self-exploration shows she feels genuinely understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on improving co-parenting for her son, narrowing steadily to one calm phone call about school. Every reflection and question advances the arc from exploration to concrete commitment without drifting into venting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a genuine dialogue of equals — nearly all reflections and open questions, no unsolicited advice. Amina's own ideas (pre-call plan, debrief) shape the intervention, and she explicitly notices the difference ('we actually made a plan').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No pressure, persuasion, or directives; Amina chooses the day, topic, and strategies herself, with the psychiatrist only inviting. Closing line 'That's you doing this, Amina. Not me — you' exemplifies ownership transfer.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation, insight, and planning are drawn from Amina via evocative questions (e.g., the concert memory, 'what would help you feel ready?'). Her self-generated commitment and mantra ('one call, one topic, stay calm') are definitive evidence of expert evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Amina, whose contributions (concert example, one-topic rule, Tuesday choice) drive the session. The psychiatrist builds the entire plan around her ideas, crediting her as the engine of change.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions ('What would it look like if it went the way you'd want?') elicit strong desire and commitment language, reinforced through strategic summaries. Amina moves from contemplation to a specific, self-generated plan (Tuesday call, one topic, debrief).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective pivots toward agency and values without amplifying sustain talk, but missed chances to use double-sided reflections; Amina's doubts ('his dad is stubborn as ever,' 'I'll talk myself out of it') receive only light acknowledgment rather than active reframing.
