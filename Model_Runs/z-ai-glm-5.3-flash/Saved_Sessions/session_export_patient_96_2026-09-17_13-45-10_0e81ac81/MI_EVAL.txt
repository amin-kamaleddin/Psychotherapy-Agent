# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance, validating Marisol's choices and annoyance without criticizing the piercing. The repeated crisis disclaimer in early turns felt formulaic and slightly misattuned to the low-risk context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate but surface-level reflections ('that sounds frustrating and intrusive'). Little exploration of what the piercing means to her identity or autonomy, with a quick pivot to information-giving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps consistent focus on the referral concern and progresses logically through steps, scripts, role-play, and monitoring. Direction was largely supplied by the counselor rather than collaboratively set.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marisol shaped her own script and chose options, showing some partnership. However, the middle session was dominated by expert checklists and instructions delivered without first eliciting her ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength: repeatedly affirms choice, never pressures removal, offers format options, and accepts her boundaries without pushback. Client exits retaining full ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely draws out Marisol's own motivations or values; no open questions about what matters to her and no importance/confidence exploration. Motivation emerged incidentally rather than being evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Honors client expertise in places (script drafting based on her edits, role-play, open closing questions). The agenda-setting question bundled options without a truly collaborative choice, and expert advice dominated the middle session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged organically and was supported with practical alternatives. But the counselor never deliberately evoked change talk nor reflected or amplified her tentative commitments ('I'll watch that').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing against the piercing, a strength given her resistance. But the dentist role-play emphasized reasons against change and invited sustain talk, with no strategic softening (double-sided reflections, autonomy pivots).
