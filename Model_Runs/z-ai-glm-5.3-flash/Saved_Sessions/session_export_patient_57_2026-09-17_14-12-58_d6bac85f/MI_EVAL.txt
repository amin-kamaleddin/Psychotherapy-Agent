# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, non-judgmental tone is present but brief, quickly giving way to advice. No meaningful affirmations of Jamal's honesty or courage in disclosing peer drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; emotional disclosures like fear of social rejection and worry about looking superior are skipped over in favor of tactic lists. Responses are predominantly transactional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing weekend alcohol use and navigating peer pressure, building toward rehearsal for Saturday. Focus is narrowly tactical rather than collaboratively established, preventing a 5.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session is largely counselor-led, with the counselor supplying strategies, scripts, and playing Deon in rehearsals. Jamal's ideas get incorporated but decisions are presented as counselor-designed menus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some options are offered and Jamal declines role-play initially without penalty, but the counselor pushes repeatedly and scripts lines/texts rather than fostering ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor rarely draws out Jamal's own motivations or values, moving straight to technique. Intrinsic motivators like his athletic identity and fear of judgment are never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, delivering rapid prescriptive advice and even drafting texts and scripting lines. Jamal shapes some content, but expertise is positioned in the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges mostly from Jamal himself and the rehearsal, with some reinforcement of successes. Little deliberate evocation of his own reasons or exploration of importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about peer rejection and minimization is met with more tactics, implicitly validating barriers. No double-sided reflections or effort to resolve ambivalence.
