# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard with repeated affirmations of Sofia's courage and expertise. Affirmations occasionally feel formulaic, and the shame around the drinking disclosure deserved deeper, more explicit positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally attuned complex reflections throughout, capturing ambivalence, fear, and pride. The psychiatrist caught the pivotal shift in 'so do I,' reflecting meaning rather than just content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, progressive focus on reducing drinking via reshaping her social life, building step by step to a concrete commitment and follow-up plan. Purposeful questions advanced the change process without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative in spirit with explicit invitations to co-create, and every plan element came from dialogue. However, the agenda and pacing were largely therapist-structured rather than jointly negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decision-making was consistently handed to Sofia, with no advice-giving, persuasion, or pressure anywhere in the session. Even the follow-up was framed as her idea, and all next steps were credited as her own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from Sofia rather than supplied—she articulated her own reasons, success criteria, coping strategies, and self-worth statements. Textbook evocation at a high level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly positioned Sofia as the expert, deftly returned her request for advice, and limited contributions to reflecting and amplifying. Exemplary power sharing shaped the entire session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions systematically elicited Sofia's motivations and plans, with selective reinforcement amplifying emerging change talk. Her commitment language strengthened across the session, culminating in a concrete behavioral step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fears and social loss were acknowledged without amplification, with effective double-sided reflections redirecting toward Sofia's own solutions. Sustain talk was occasionally reflected at length without an explicit pivot, and some fears weren't reframed toward her hopes.
