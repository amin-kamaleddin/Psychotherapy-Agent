# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard: affirmations were specific, limits were accepted ('If you don't reply... that's okay'), and no judgment appeared even when Amira tested the counselor. Warmth was calibrated to her request to avoid being 'too cheerful.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured Amira's ambivalence and hopelessness accurately, with summaries reflecting her key statements. Reflections sometimes restated content rather than deepening emotional meaning—opportunities to explore the pain of 'no one was around to catch me' and her abandonment worry were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on one small, concrete goal tied to Amira's stated desire, with every turn advancing the plan (timing, memory cue, fallback, 9am check-in). No aimless drift, while remaining sensitive to her pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a partner—brainstorming was offered, choices negotiated, and check-in logistics co-created with Amira's input shaping the plan. The counselor at times supplied substantial structure (options, script, fallbacks) before inviting her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice ownership was consistently reinforced, and 'not promising anything' was explicitly honored rather than pushed toward stronger commitment. No pressure, persuasion, or control language appeared anywhere in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amira's own desire talk, self-generated fallback idea, and spontaneous hope ('I feel a little less hopeless') emerged from evocative questioning rather than persuasion. Motivation was largely evoked via standardized rulers and structured questions rather than open exploration of her values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amira was repeatedly positioned as the expert, and her preferences (short, plain, not cheerful) directly shaped the plan. The somewhat prescriptive script and structured options slightly reduced full power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers evoked measurable progression (confidence 3→4) plus Amira's own desire talk, goal, and commitment language ('I'll try the breathing thing tomorrow. Once.'). Evocation was somewhat structured/formulaic, and emerging change talk was not always deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement and redirected toward small actionable change, with autonomy-supportive responses to her distrust. Summaries sometimes simply mirrored sustain talk without actively reframing it toward her demonstrated strengths.
