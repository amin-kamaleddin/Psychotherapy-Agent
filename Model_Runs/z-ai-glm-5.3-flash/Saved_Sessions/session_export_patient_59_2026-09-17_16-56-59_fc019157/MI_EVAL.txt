# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance despite Jamal's minimization, with validating phrases like 'I hear you' and 'That makes sense.' Acceptance occasionally felt mechanical, and explicit affirmations of his strengths were limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected Jamal's compliance-driven worldview, e.g., 'your real concern is avoiding another bust.' Reflections stayed at the content level rather than exploring deeper emotions like his relationship with his mom or fear of consequences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on the target behavior and program compliance throughout, with the session never wandering. Focus drifted somewhat toward program logistics rather than the marijuana use itself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission and offered choices, but generated the checklist and PO text unilaterally rather than co-constructing them with Jamal's own language and ideas. The session frequently fell into counselor-as-provider mode.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and honored Jamal's decisions without pressure or moralizing, even when he stated he wouldn't stop smoking. Would have been stronger with more emphasis on Jamal's ownership of the plan and affirming his capacity to self-direct.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The importance/confidence scaling and questions about existing strategies elicited some genuine change talk. However, motivations like his mom and senior year were not followed up on, and much of the session relied on counselor-supplied content rather than elicited material.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Jamal's input at several points, but also provided substantial expert content including a detailed checklist, a pre-written text, and prescriptive options. Missed chances to position Jamal as the expert in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicited commitment-like and need talk through the 0-10 scale and questions about doable steps. Failed to strategically deepen or reinforce this change talk, revisit the importance/confidence ratings, or explore Jamal's personal reasons beyond compliance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not argue with or reinforce sustain talk, instead acknowledging it without endorsement and pivoting to practical risk-reduction steps. A double-sided reflection connecting his goals to the behavior could have softened sustain talk more actively.
