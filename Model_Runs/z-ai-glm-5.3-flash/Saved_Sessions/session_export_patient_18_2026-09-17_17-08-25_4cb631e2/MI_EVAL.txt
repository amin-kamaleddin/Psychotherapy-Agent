# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations like 'Nice job, Laura' and respect for her lower-risk status. However, affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked individualized depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections (e.g., 'pressure is harder when it's people you look up to'), but reflections were sparse and quickly pivoted to advice, without exploring Laura's fear of seeming 'lame' in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on avoiding underage drinking and building refusal skills, progressing logically from importance/confidence ratings to skills rehearsal and a concrete action plan for Saturday.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration—Laura selected preferred lines and identified Priya as support—but the counselor largely supplied strategies and structure, with Laura reacting rather than co-creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offered choices and honored Laura's preferences (talking over role-play). Pre-drafted scripts and frequent advice-giving somewhat reduced the sense that solutions were fully hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and prompts elicited Laura's motivations (swimming, school) early on, but the session shifted to counselor-provided answers rather than consistently drawing solutions from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor quickly assumed an expert role, delivering refusal lines, strategies, and a drafted text. Laura's input shaped little of the session direction despite occasional choice questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions effectively moved Laura from 4 importance to 8 confidence, and prompts elicited commitment language ('I'll start with telling Priya'). Relied on structured techniques rather than open exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('it just hasn't been a big deal yet') was acknowledged without amplification but not strategically softened with double-sided reflections; the topic was simply moved past quickly.
