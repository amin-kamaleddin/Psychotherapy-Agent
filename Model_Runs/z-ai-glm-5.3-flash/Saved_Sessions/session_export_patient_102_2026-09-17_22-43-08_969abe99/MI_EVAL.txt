# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated warmth and unconditional positive regard; never judged or labeled the client. Affirmed disclosures without punishing them and accepted the client's refusal to quit without disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were frequent, accurate, and captured emotional meaning beyond surface content, including ambivalence and subtle self-criticism. Client's closing 'thanks for not preaching at me' confirms he felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unwavering focus on the target behavior, with every exchange advancing toward a concrete, client-owned monitoring plan. No drift; each question built progressively on the client's stated values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly between equal partners; the final plan emerged entirely from the client's ideas. Client's shift from guarded resistance to 'Deal?' demonstrates power-sharing was genuine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy actively reinforced throughout, explicitly preserving the client's right not to change and declining to push abstinence. Framed tracking and follow-up as client-owned choices rather than obligations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within the client via open questions, never lectured or supplied reasons for change. Client articulated his own warning signs, values, and self-initiated plan, confirming internally generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert on his own life; the plan was entirely client-generated. Summary language directly honored the client's expertise and reversed the referring adults' framing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk with strategic forward-looking questions. Amplified initial change talk into strong values-based commitment around sports, graduation, and holding the wheel.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing or reinforcing sustain talk and effectively redirected it, but occasionally restated sustain talk content (e.g., 'blown out of proportion') without an immediate double-sided pivot. More deliberate double-sided reflections would strengthen redirection.
