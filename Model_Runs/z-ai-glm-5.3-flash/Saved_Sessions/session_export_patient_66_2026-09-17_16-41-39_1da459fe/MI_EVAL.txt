# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance, explicitly committing to stay nonjudgmental if he smoked and validating his self-directed low-bar approach. Deducted because warmth occasionally felt formulaic, with recurring canned phrases like 'I hear your realism.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflections early, especially 'You're valuing that private off switch — the headspace and pause — more than the cigarette.' Deducted because later reflections became repetitive plan summaries without exploring deeper emotions (social pressure from coworkers, fear of being lectured).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent, unwavering focus on the target behavior from start to finish, advancing to a highly specific plan (break times, substitutes, scripts, Monday start, Thursday check-in). Handled deflection about the coping card gracefully with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently sought permission, offered options rather than prescriptions, and co-constructed a plan shaped by Kareem's choices (times, substitutes, check-in). Deducted because some options felt pre-packaged (lists of substitutes and scripted replies) rather than drawn first from the client's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support with explicit statements like 'You're in charge here' and 'I respect that you're running it on your terms,' while honoring his request not to overreact to smoking. Kareem's own closing ('I'm doing this because it's my idea, sort of') confirms successful ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic motivation through exploring what breaks give him, the readiness ruler, and naming anticipated obstacles, drawing out genuine reasons and self-efficacy. Deducted because evocation was front-loaded, shifting to logistics in the second half rather than continuing to evoke reasons for continuing beyond the week.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Kareem as the expert ('You're in charge here'), accepting his rejections without pushback and letting his input shape the plan. Deducted because offering pre-made menus and scripts tilted slightly toward the counselor's agenda rather than drawing plans primarily from his ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully pivoted from sustain talk by exploring what breaks provide, evoking meaningful change language ('I don't see why I couldn't try swapping a couple of breaks a week') and moving him from readiness 2–3 to a concrete experiment. Deducted for not deepening confidence talk or explicitly amplifying the emergent 'maybe I keep going.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk, meeting 'I like smoking' with neutral, reframing reflections that sidestepped argument. Deducted for occasionally echoing sustain elements without double-sided reflections to tip the balance toward change or validating his attachment while pivoting.
