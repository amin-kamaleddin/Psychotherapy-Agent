# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout with personalized affirmations and no moralizing about vaping. Kept from a 5 by somewhat formulaic 'you're the expert' affirmations and a lack of affirmation of her worth independent of performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and often captures the behavior's function (e.g., 'the vape is the one thing that's yours'). Emotional material (parental resentment, post-vaping guilt) is acknowledged but immediately converted into the next question rather than sat with.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focus on vaping tightens progressively each exchange — from protected time, to a concrete plan, to crisis response, to a same-night commitment — with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist contributes only reflections and questions while every load-bearing plan element (Thursday nights, Sarah, code word, tonight-text) originates with the client. No directives, corrections, or agenda overrides at any point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly respected ('no right answer,' 'your own way entirely') with no pressure or persuasion. Minor steering appears when 'Maybe' is restated as decided and the code word option is pre-formed rather than left open.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason for change is drawn from the client through evocative questions, amplified reflections, and values-linking; the counselor never supplies a rationale. The arc from 'Maybe. I'd have to see' to 'I can do that tonight' demonstrates ideal evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located in the client, both verbally and behaviorally — the entire plan is built from Anika's contributions. Inquiry is framed with humility and no advice or psychoeducation is offered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation with a well-executed confidence ruler including the 'what would nudge that number up?' follow-up. Change talk is amplified verbatim, linked to values, and moves measurably from tentative to committed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled with effective pivots and reframes, never with persuasion or advice. Slight deductions for an early reflection that briefly romanticizes the vaping and for re-invoking the resistant 'voice' after the client had already diminished it.
