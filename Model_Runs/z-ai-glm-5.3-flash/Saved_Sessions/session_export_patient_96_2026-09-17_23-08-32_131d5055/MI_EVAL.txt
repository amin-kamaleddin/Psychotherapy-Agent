# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with warm, individualized affirmations ('you're the expert on your own face and your own life') and no judgment of the piercing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and her pragmatic stance, but occasionally stayed at summary level; the emotional hint about feeling 'ganged up on' was affirmed but not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained focus and built toward concrete commitments, but the target behavior drifted from the dentist's original concern (piercing effects) to a hygiene-management frame without explicitly reconnecting to the referral issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong joint framing ('figure out together') with every step consulted; the final plan was co-constructed from the client's own ideas, and she explicitly noted the session felt different from usual lectures.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: explicit statements that decisions belong to the client, no pressure toward removal, and the door left open on her own terms ('then it's on me').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nearly all substantive content came from the client herself (mouthwash plan, 'real, visible stuff' threshold); missed opportunities to connect habits to deeper values around identity and self-expression.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary power-sharing: permission-seeking before offering direction, client expertise repeatedly affirmed, and her 'facts, no lecture' preference visibly shaped the session's course.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked commitment and action talk from a pre-contemplative client (mouthwash this week, gum check at next cleaning); could have extended the hypothetical exploration further for a top score.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk with double-sided reflections without arguing, but sometimes simply validated it rather than strategically reframing; the target behavior also remained somewhat ambiguous.
