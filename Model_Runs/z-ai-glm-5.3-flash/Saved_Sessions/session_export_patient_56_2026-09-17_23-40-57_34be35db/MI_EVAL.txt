# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, genuine affirmations with no judgment; the client explicitly noted feeling heard. Exemplary unconditional positive regard, including normalization of self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and emotionally attuned, e.g., 'deep love... and maybe an ache too,' capturing meaning beneath surface content. Every reflection deepened rather than repeated the client's words.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus from value-to-action gap through a concrete commitment (Saturday announcement, coffee maker note) with no topic drift. Direction was maintained without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work was framed as joint from the start, with questions rather than assertions and the client's ideas driving content. The client's closing feedback confirmed an equal working alliance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership were actively reinforced; no advice was given, and all strategies were client-generated and credited as such. Follow-up was left entirely in the client's hands.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open evocative questions consistently drew out the client's own motivations and vision, with explicit attribution of change to the client. Motivation was clearly evoked from within, not instilled.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client was positioned as the expert ('you know yourself best'), and every plan element originated from her material. The client confirmed experiencing genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and amplification of commitment and confidence talk ('a promise, not a plan,' 'I really think I can'). Session shows a clear arc from values to concrete self-directed action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well with double-sided reflections and reframing, though occasionally echoed before pivoting. Minor stylistic point; sustain talk was minimal in this low-ambivalence client.
