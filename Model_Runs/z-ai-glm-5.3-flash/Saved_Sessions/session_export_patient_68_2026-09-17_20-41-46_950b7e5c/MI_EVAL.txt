# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, explicitly disavowing labels ('I'm not here to slap a label on you') and affirming Kevin authentically even when he deflects. Warmth feels genuine and individualized throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate, including the double-sided opening reflection and the pivotal 'autopilot' reflection that visibly moved Kevin. Kevin repeatedly elaborates and discovers new insights, a strong indicator of felt understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focus remains steady on drinking in the context of the injury, with every exchange tied back to the target behavior. The session culminates in a concrete, client-chosen plan for Saturday.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor asks permission before offering ideas and co-constructs the plan entirely from Kevin's own material (football, Coke round, Saturday). Decisions about focus, steps, and timing are negotiated rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kevin is explicitly positioned as decision-maker ('you get to set the terms here'), with both the choice of intervention and timing left entirely to him. His 'I'm not promising anything' is accepted without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn out through strategic open questions that elicit Kevin's own reasons for change, such as 'how did you handle it?' surfacing football as a coping skill. His self-generated insights ('hearing myself say it... seems a bit backwards') demonstrate successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor positions Kevin as the expert and credits his insights as his own ('you just talked yourself out of a worry'; 'that's you doing the work, not me'). The autopilot framing, Coke experiment, and Saturday plan all originate from Kevin.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk escalates from cautious openness ('maybe it's worth hearing what you've got to say') to specific commitments ('Saturday. One round. Paying attention.'), reinforced selectively and consolidated with summaries. The closing question elicited strong commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('bad luck,' 'I'm not quitting or anything') is acknowledged without reinforcement and repeatedly pivoted toward Kevin's own reasons for change. A double-sided reflection on friends' teasing let Kevin talk himself out of the worry via Dave's sober January. No arguing or confronting anywhere.
