# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with frequent affirmations ('that was brave') and normalization of anxiety. Praise is sometimes performance-oriented rather than conveying unconditional regard independent of progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing both physical experience and professional stakes (e.g., reflecting her hot, spaced-out feelings alongside freelance pressures). Some reflections stay at content level, missing chances to deepen into the emotional meaning of her fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains crystal-clear focus on managing social anxiety at photography shoots, with every exchange tying back to the target goal of Saturday's wedding. Each response ends with a next-step question, keeping steady momentum without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ideas flow both directions — the clinician's breathing script paired with Naomi's gear-camouflage insight incorporated verbatim. The plan was co-built step by step, and the role-play format was adapted to her request to see a model first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is consistently foregrounded via option menus ('Which of these feels most doable?') rather than prescriptions. Naomi's selections drove the plan, and her 'no' to the reset break was accepted and worked around without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn consistently from within: rulers elicited her own confidence ratings, which she raised herself, and she generated her own coping insights ('my shoulders actually feel a bit lower'). The clinician rarely argues for change; Naomi discovers and voices it herself, ending at a confidence of 8.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi's contributions visibly shaped the session — her gear-disguise idea became the script, and her mid-ceremony worry set the troubleshooting agenda. Her expertise is honored, though mostly implicitly rather than explicitly acknowledged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation through readiness/confidence rulers that moved Naomi from 5 to 8, and open questions that elicited her own solutions (booking smaller events, grounding). Her change language grew organically ('I want it to be different, I really do') and was consistently reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I kind of never stick to things') was acknowledged without reinforcement and often reframed constructively, such as turning hiding behind the camera into the gear-disguise technique. However, worries were sometimes met with solution lists rather than double-sided reflections to soften ambivalence.
