# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, unconditional positive regard throughout with frequent specific affirmations ('That's not a small shift, Mai'). Mai felt never judged, even when admitting nervousness about asking questions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capturing Mai's core concerns and empowerment shift, but sometimes brief. Early anxiety and mistrust from poor doctor communication could have received deeper emotional exploration before moving to education.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on statin adherence with each exchange advancing a concrete element (timing, grapefruit, monitoring, blood tests). No topic drift, ending with a complete plan summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely between equals—co-constructing solutions and framing monitoring as mutual ('we can watch for together'). Mai's ideas shaped the session, and she noted she didn't feel rushed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy actively reinforced with offered choices ('call this week or wait?') and explicit honoring of Mai's decisions ('That was your decision to make'). Never pressured or prescribed her conclusions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within, eliciting Mai's own articulation ('nobody's going to advocate for my health but me'). Change talk flourished: 'I'll call them this week,' 'I want to be the one driving now.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within the client ('You know your life and body better than anyone'). Mai's ideas—notes app, driver's seat metaphor—were picked up, honored, and built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation via open questions ('What do you think changed for you today?') yielding deep self-motivating statements. Each change talk instance was reinforced with affirmations and deepening questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mild sustain talk (medication uncertainty, muscle worries) was acknowledged respectfully and reoriented to Mai's agency without dwelling. No deliberate double-sided reflections were used to actively resolve ambivalence.
