# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard is consistent, and ambivalence is normalized well. Affirmations are somewhat generic and could be more individualized to Camila's specific strengths (self-awareness, immediate follow-through).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures both sides of Camila's ambivalence accurately. Emotional layers (loneliness, fear of social rejection, self-criticism) are not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focus on alcohol reduction is clear and consistent from opening to concrete commitments (sober Tuesday, decline line, Dani's check-in). Each turn advances toward a specific, measurable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plans are genuinely co-constructed — Camila picks the decline line, activities, support person, and reminder wording. The counselor repeatedly checks preferences and adapts to her pacing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ownership is consistently reinforced ('we'll go at your pace'), and Camila explicitly internalized her choice ('more just... my choice'). Slips are framed as information, never pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within via scaling and open prompts, producing Camila's own reasons and rationale. At times evocation gives way to counselor-supplied tactics and scripts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila's expertise is honored — her choices shape the session, and her preference to defer weekend planning is respected. Occasional advice-dense turns lead more than follow.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions effectively elicit importance (8/10) and growing confidence (5 to 6), and Camila articulates her own commitment language. Missed chances to explore deeper reasons behind her motivation and to affirm change talk explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without amplification, with reframes like 'slips as info, not failure.' Some responses pivot to tactic lists rather than strategic double-sided reflections, and weekend worries go unresolved.
