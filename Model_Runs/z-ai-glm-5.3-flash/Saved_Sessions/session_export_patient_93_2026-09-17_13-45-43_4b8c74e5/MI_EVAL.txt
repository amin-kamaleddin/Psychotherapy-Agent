# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, but warmth is minimal and formulaic, with almost no affirmations of Margaret's honesty or her own practical ideas.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on, but they are thin and formulaic ('I hear you...'), and emotional content such as her pride, social attachment to the pub, and humor is repeatedly missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing alcohol-related risk with no topic drift, but focus is maintained by pushing content forward rather than through strategic summarizing and goal clarification with the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Margaret shapes plan details (dry days, tally, text edits), but the counselor hands over a complete plan, texts, and templates with little joint problem-solving—more client sign-off than equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices repeatedly and accepts her refusal to commit to the six-hour rule without pushing; falls short because choices are structured by counselor-made menus rather than open ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is largely supplied from outside the client—the counselor delivers the persuasive health content itself rather than eliciting Margaret's own concerns, values, confidence, or the discrepancy between her independence and her drinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices inviting her input, but the overall posture is directive with pre-packaged plans, drafted texts, and templates; her contributions shape details but not the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Margaret produced meaningful change talk, much prompted by the health-risk information, but the counselor rarely reinforces or deepens it—no affirmations, elaboration questions, or confidence exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Does not argue with or amplify her minimization, pivoting quickly to options without pushing abstinence; loses a point for never using double-sided reflections to acknowledge and gently reframe it.
