# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout: affirms the client's expertise, validates her courage, and explicitly permits ambivalence ('that worry about the weight goal? It's allowed to come along for the ride'). No judgment, even toward her attachment to weight loss.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and emotional undercurrent, e.g., 'I hear the honesty in still wanting to lose weight while knowing the old cycle has to end.' Reflections consistently hold her ambivalence with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive arc from failed strict dieting toward sustainable, enjoyment-based behaviors: what failed, what worked, concrete first steps, progress markers, and a self-monitoring strategy. Each exchange advances toward commitment without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True equal-partner interaction: questions genuinely hand over the agenda, and the plan is co-developed entirely from the client's own material. Her ideas (gym enjoyment, phone notes) shape the session outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced—'you don't have to resolve them today,' 'a way that fits your life.' No advice imposed; all decisions and framing originate with the client, on her own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation elicited entirely from the client via strategic open questions drawing out her own evidence, values, and commitment. She ends with self-articulated success markers and a self-designed plan—hallmarks of expert evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise repeatedly located in the client: 'You're the expert on your own life,' 'That's your expertise talking.' Her contributions visibly drive the session direction and the resulting plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk ('What might work better this time?') and amplifies it ('There's real determination in...'), moving the client from contemplation to commitment language like 'I could try it this week.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and paired with change-focused reframes, e.g., 'weight still in the picture but no longer in charge.' The counselor never argues against the weight goal, defusing rather than amplifying it.
