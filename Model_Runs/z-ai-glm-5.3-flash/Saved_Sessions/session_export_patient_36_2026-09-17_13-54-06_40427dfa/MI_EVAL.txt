# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and consistently nonjudgmental tone, including acceptance of Elena's doubts about the technique. However, warmth is somewhat formulaic and tied to task compliance rather than unconditional regard independent of performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections, but responses to emotionally loaded material (family guilt with her sister, 'Family is family') shift to scripts and logistics rather than reflecting the emotional weight. Empathy is present but shallow and inconsistent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, consistent focus on boundary-setting and countering 'putting yourself last.' Every element — values, if-then plans, scripts, role-plays — ties directly to that goal with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine back-and-forth with Elena's input shaping the session, including co-negotiated role-play. Held at 4 because the counselor's default remains information delivery rather than joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regular choice offered ('Want to pick one script?', 'stronger pressure or family example?') and Elena's decisions honored. Undercut by prescriptive advice given before asking Elena's own preferences or ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation resides largely in the counselor's toolkits rather than being drawn out from Elena; evocative questions ('What do you think would work?', 'How confident are you?') are largely absent. Elena's motivation was pre-existing and self-generated, not cultivated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor frequently assumes the expert role with prescriptive tip lists and scripted lines. Elena's contributions shape content, but her own ideas are rarely solicited before solutions are provided — collaborative in tone, expert-driven in structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena arrived with strong self-motivated change talk, and the counselor builds on it through commitment rehearsal. However, little active evocation or deepening of change talk through deliberate open questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Elena's doubt about the technique feeling 'silly' without dwelling on it, consistently pivoting toward action. Limited opportunity to demonstrate skillful softening, and the doubt was never explicitly acknowledged and reframed.
