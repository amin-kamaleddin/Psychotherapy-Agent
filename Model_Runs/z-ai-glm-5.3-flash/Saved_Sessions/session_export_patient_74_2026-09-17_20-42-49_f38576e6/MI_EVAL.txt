# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard was shown throughout—opening with an affirmation, respecting hesitancy, and treating minimal commitment as enough. No trace of disapproval or pressure was present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and feeling (e.g., the candy-stick image, holding ambivalence, naming the fear of failure). Linda repeatedly confirmed them: 'Yeah, exactly. That's it.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focus stayed on smoking with each exchange advancing the work—from ambivalence to strengths, problem-solving, readiness, and concrete next steps. No drift; every question tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative—ideas originated from Linda and the plan was co-developed from her own suggestions (talking to her husband, self-monitoring). The counselor checked in rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was consistently affirmed ('I'm not here to push you,' 'Whatever you decide—and whenever—is completely up to you'), including validating her right to decline. This aligned well with Linda's own insight about digging her heels in when cornered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within—her pride from the prior attempt, her tea/dishes ideas, her reframe away from 'quitting'—all elicited, not supplied. The counselor explicitly named this: 'That's your wisdom, not mine.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in the client ('You know yourself best, Linda'), and her own ideas substantially shaped the session's direction and plan. Her contributions clearly drove the outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and deepened at every turn, from her pride in the past attempt to concrete self-generated steps (tea, dishes, husband conversation). A readiness ruler moved her from zero to a self-reported 3.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I still enjoy my cigarettes,' 'I'm not promising anything') was met with acceptance and gentle pivots toward her own motivations, never argued with. Her past 'failure' was reframed as learning without reinforcing reasons against change.
