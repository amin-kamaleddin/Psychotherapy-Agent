# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with slips framed compassionately as 'data, not failure.' Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and the warmth occasionally read as coaching rather than deep individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Claudia's ambivalence well and she honored her request to ask about mood first. Reflections remained somewhat surface-level, with the emotional core of her shame and self-criticism never deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction with no topic drift. Every exchange advanced momentum toward the concrete goal, coping strategies, and the Tuesday check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were genuinely co-constructed with Claudia's ideas forming the backbone. However, the detailed prescriptive script and repeated checklist-style closing questions gave parts of the exchange a coaching flavor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly affirmed Claudia's ownership ('I'll support whatever you decide'), and she freely modified the plan and set her own check-in terms. Slips framed as information reinforced her agency without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and future-oriented questions effectively elicited Claudia's own reasons and strengthened change talk. Score fell short of 5 because the psychiatrist sometimes suggested solutions rather than consistently evoking her ideas first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Claudia was consistently positioned as the expert and her tweaks genuinely shaped the plan. Points deducted for times the psychiatrist supplied detailed scripts and instructions, sliding toward an expert/coach role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance/confidence rulers moved Claudia's confidence from 5-6 to 7-7.5 and elicited sustained change language. The psychiatrist sometimes supplied specifics rather than having Claudia generate them fully herself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A skillful double-sided reflection addressed sustain talk without amplifying resistance. However, the 'what you appreciate about drinking' question directly invited sustain talk, and there was no deliberate pivot or reframing once it emerged.
