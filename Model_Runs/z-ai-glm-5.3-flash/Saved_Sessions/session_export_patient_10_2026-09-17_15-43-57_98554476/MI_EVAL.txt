# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, supportive tone with regular affirmations ('nice work,' 'you clearly know your value') and no judgment about the missed interview. Falls short of 5 because warmth is brief and at times formulaic, with no explicit normalization of the emotional setback.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections are accurate ('disappointed but still motivated'), but many turns skip reflection and jump straight to advice or questions. Amina's discouragement and discomfort with depending on others are acknowledged only superficially and never explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on employment readiness despite childcare barriers with zero topic drift. Every exchange ties back to concrete progress, ending with a specific time-blocked commitment (Wednesday 1:00) and a follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asks permission before offering content and lets Amina's choices steer the plan (resume first, then rehearsal); her tweaks are incorporated and affirmed. Uneven during the checklist/template phase, where the counselor supplies solutions rather than co-creating them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames choices as Amina's and respects her decision to write the message to her mum herself. Advice is offered conditionally, though the dense checklist/template/bullets bursts are counselor-driven with little explicit reinforcement of her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence scaling question elicited rich change talk ('It sits about at a 10'), and open invitations drew out her own plans. However, much content was counselor-supplied rather than elicited, and deeper motivation (what a job means for her family) was never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and asks for Amina's preferences, but frequently slips into expert-delivery mode with a checklist, template, and pre-written bullets. The counselor drives agenda and content generation rather than drawing expertise from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effective evocation via the confidence scaling question and inviting Amina to select her own next steps, prompting strong commitment language. Missed opportunities to deepen change talk — pivoting to problem-solving right after her '10' confidence rating instead of reflecting and elaborating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responds to sustain talk ('I don't like depending on people') with brief validation and quick redirects toward action, never reinforcing barriers. Loses a point for not using double-sided reflections pairing barriers with Amina's stated strengths to resolve ambivalence more strategically.
