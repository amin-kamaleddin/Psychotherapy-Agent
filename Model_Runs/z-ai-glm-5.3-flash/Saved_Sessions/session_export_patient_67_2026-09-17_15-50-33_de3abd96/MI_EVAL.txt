# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with frequent affirmations ('Good thinking,' 'Nice work') and no pressure or labeling. However, warmth is somewhat transactional, and Kenji's emotional vulnerabilities (wasted Sundays, stress) are never explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections early on (drink tally surprise, ambivalence, unwinding vs. rough weekdays), but reflective listening largely disappears in the second half as the counselor shifts to rapid-fire questions and logistics. Emotional content like money mattering more than health warnings goes unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on alcohol reduction from start to finish, culminating in a highly specific plan (Mon/Wed alcohol-free, fizzy water, podcast, walk backup, 6:30pm alarm, money tracker). No topic drift; all logistics serve the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kenji chooses weeknights, alternatives, alarm wording, and edits suggestions, showing genuine co-construction. But the counselor supplies much of the planning content (alternatives list, tracker design, breathing protocol), tilting toward counselor-led problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength: explicit permission-seeking, experimental framing ('one week, just an experiment'), option menus, and immediate acceptance of Kenji's modifications. His closing remark that the plan feels 'less like a lecture and more like an experiment' confirms a non-controlling stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation drawn effectively from Kenji via rulers, open questions, and his own solutions (supermarket skip, fridge swap) including the powerful 'money talks louder than health warnings' insight. Weakened by counselor generating more content than evoking during planning and not deepening his values beyond the practical.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership signals: asking permission, offering rather than imposing ideas, and instantly adopting Kenji's Monday/Wednesday switch. Falls short of 5 due to stacked multi-question turns and counselor-driven pacing during logistics, leaving Kenji somewhat passive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled use of open questions, importance/confidence rulers, and excellent follow-up ('What would need to happen for importance to move up?') producing concrete change talk and commitment (7/10). Missed the chance to reinforce Kenji's spontaneous gratitude and self-insight.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('not saying I need to quit,' 'still only a 4') is never confronted; the counselor acknowledges it neutrally and pivots to small doable steps with experimental framing. Occasional mirroring of sustain talk in summaries without double-sided reflections keeps it from a 5.
