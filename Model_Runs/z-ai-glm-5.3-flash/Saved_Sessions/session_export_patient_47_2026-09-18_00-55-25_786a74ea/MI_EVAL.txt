# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout; Evelyn's minimization is never met with disapproval. Affirmations are somewhat repetitive and lack fully individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections capture both her values and health concerns. Reflections stay at the content level, missing deeper emotion around loneliness and fear of falling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, unwavering focus moving purposefully from exploration to ambivalence to concrete action planning. Excellent progression to a self-authored plan within one session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tone of equals exploring together with co-constructed follow-up. Collaboration is mainly through questioning rather than joint problem-solving, and requested guideline data was never shared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary — change explicitly framed as Evelyn's choice with no pressure or directives. Evelyn's own words confirm the stance landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivations come from Evelyn's own mouth via open-ended elicitation. Her final ownership of the change process is the strongest outcome of skilled evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Every plan element originates from Evelyn, reflecting strong power-sharing. Achieved more by restraint than active facilitation of agenda-shaping.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength — change talk is consistently elicited, reflected, and amplified. Evelyn progresses from denial to a full self-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk handled well with double-sided reflections and no arguing. Some moments where minimization could have been redirected more decisively.
