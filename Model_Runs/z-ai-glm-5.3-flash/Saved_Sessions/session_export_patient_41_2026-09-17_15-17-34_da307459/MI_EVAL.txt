# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout with curiosity about avoidance; affirmations are somewhat brief and formulaic, rarely affirming Elira's deeper qualities like courage or persistence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture both emotional and behavioral layers, including the relief-then-guilt cycle; occasionally pivots quickly to option-offering rather than fully validating emotional pain, such as her sadness about limited follow-through.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on anxiety-driven avoidance from problem exploration through a fully specified plan (desk, sticky note, timer, check-in), showing excellent goal-directed momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Work is framed as joint and decisions are clearly shared, with Elira choosing interventions and follow-up format; the counselor sometimes retains agenda control through structured menus and moves faster than a fully co-paced conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is woven throughout and ownership of change rests with Elira, whose confidence rose from 4-5 to 6 through her own choices; her boundaries, like not practicing in session, were honored without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from Elira—her own ingredients, coping phrase, and implementation details; some counselor suggestions led the content and her deeper values for change were never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira is consistently positioned as the expert and her choices directly shape the session; the repeated A-or-B menu format occasionally constrains her contributions to pre-set choices rather than open co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits her own plan, uses confidence ratings that produced movement from 4-5 to 6, and evokes self-generated coping language; deeper exploration of her values and reasons for change would have anchored the change talk more personally.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated and reframed toward past successes and forward-looking choices, treating panic-driven success as a usable strength; some redirects are abrupt, e.g., her fear of a friend seeing her fail goes unacknowledged.
