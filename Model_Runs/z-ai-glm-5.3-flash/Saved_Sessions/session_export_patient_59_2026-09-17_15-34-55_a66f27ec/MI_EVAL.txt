# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout despite Jamal's dismissiveness and impatience. However, affirmations were formulaic and lacked deeper individualized recognition of Jamal as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate surface-level reflections, but emotionally rich disclosures about family pressure and perceived unfairness were repeatedly skipped in favor of task logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on legal compliance matching Jamal's stated goal, with concrete next steps throughout. However, the marijuana use itself—the referral target—was never addressed, and there was no closing summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered option menus and check-ins, but the pattern was predominantly counselor-provides, Jamal-accepts. Plans were counselor-generated rather than co-constructed from Jamal's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and never pressured Jamal to change his views, respecting his control over tasks and decisions. Fell short of full marks because Jamal's ownership of the larger change process was never reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was framed around external consequences rather than elicited from Jamal's own values and goals. He offered self-motivational content unprompted, but the counselor never explored or expanded it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked preference questions and offered choices, but the session became advice-heavy with checklists and scripts. Jamal's expertise and motivations did not substantially shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal produced a key change statement ('I'm not trying to get caught up again either') that went unreflected and undeepened. No evocative questions or scaling were used to develop personal motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify or reinforce sustain talk, which is a strength. However, minimization and comparisons went largely unaddressed, and 'that would feel invasive' actually validated sustain talk without pivoting toward change.
