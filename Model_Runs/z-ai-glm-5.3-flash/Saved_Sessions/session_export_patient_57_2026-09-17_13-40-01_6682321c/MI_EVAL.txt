# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained a neutral, non-judgmental tone throughout, but warmth was generic and formulaic with few specific affirmations of Jamal's courage, strengths, or insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections captured Jamal's social fears, but most responses quickly pivoted to tool delivery, leaving emotional disclosures minimally reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on refusing alcohol at team parties with concrete planning throughout, though direction followed the coach's agenda rather than Jamal's own values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session functioned largely as expert consultation with the counselor dispensing scripts, drafts, and strategies while Jamal selected from pre-packaged options; genuine two-way problem-solving was rare.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered menus and framed refusal as a choice, but the interaction was dominated by prescriptive advice with an implied preferred outcome, and the decision was never substantively framed as Jamal's own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was treated as something to equip rather than elicit; the counselor never asked why change mattered to Jamal or how confident he felt, and the plan was largely counselor-supplied.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menu-style questions invited some input, but the overall stance was expert-driven, with pre-packaged scripts dominating and Jamal's contributions treated as material to refine rather than authoritative expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged and was noticed, but the counselor rarely reflected, amplified, or deliberately evoked it, so change talk developed mostly by accident.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced but was largely skipped over rather than reflectively handled, and repeated rehearsal of pushback scenarios kept attention on arguments against change.
