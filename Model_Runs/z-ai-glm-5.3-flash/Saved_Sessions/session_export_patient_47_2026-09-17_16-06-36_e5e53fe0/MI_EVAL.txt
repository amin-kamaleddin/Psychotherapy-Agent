# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, honoring Evelyn's wish to inform her son herself. The opening felt mildly accusatory by leading with the questionnaire result and son's worry, and affirmations were somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening capturing her ambivalence, e.g., the 'two things tugging at you' reflection. Reflections stayed at the surface and could have explored deeper emotions like her fear about the fall or feelings about her son's concern.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use and produced a concrete, testable plan with a follow-up. Early questioning was generic and some exchanges circled confidence ratings rather than advancing the change agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership in which Evelyn selected and modified options and co-constructed the plan, noting approvingly she wasn't told to give up pub nights. Occasional suggestion-heavy turns slightly unbalanced the exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices consistently framed as Evelyn's to make, with her conditions (pub nights, telling her son herself) respected. The counselor sometimes offered prescriptive suggestion lists rather than eliciting her own strategies or explicitly affirming her ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ruler questions drew out some of Evelyn's own reasons, but motivation was often seeded externally through counselor-supplied tactics and rationales. Reliance on repeated scaling questions rather than open exploration of her values limited depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evelyn's input genuinely shaped the plan — the half-pour idea, lemonade choice, and timing of telling her son. The counselor occasionally slipped into an advisor role, offering tactic lists before Evelyn had explored her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance and confidence rulers effectively elicited change talk, with confidence rising from 6 to 8. Change talk was rarely reinforced or deepened with reflections before moving to tactics, and elicitation became repetitive.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Double-sided reflections acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it, but it was often left unaddressed, notably her closing 'seven drinks a week was never a problem.' Repeated confidence ratings kept returning to ambivalence rather than pivoting firmly toward change.
