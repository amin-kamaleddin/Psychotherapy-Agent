# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout; no judgment about phone use or initial compliance-based motivation. Genuine affirmations and normalization of slips ('neither one means you've failed') communicated full acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that validated the function of the problem behavior ('the phone is doing something real for you'). Reflections consistently landed, with the patient confirming them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on the target behavior from start to finish—barrier identification through logistics, slip planning, success criteria, and follow-up. Every turn advanced the plan while remaining client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals; the plan was co-developed with the patient contributing the substance. Patient shaped session direction and the final summary framed it as 'you've built the whole thing.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support: 'it'd be my call, not hers' was affirmed, permission-style questions used, and the patient's tweak honored. Slightly short of ideal because the counselor could have more explicitly affirmed his right to adjust the plan during anticipated difficult moments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within rather than supplied; no advice or persuasion imposed. Questioning shifted the patient from external pressure to intrinsic ownership ('that'd be for me') and self-initiated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the patient as the expert ('You know your mornings better than anyone'), with every solution originating from him. The patient directly confirmed experiencing genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk via open questions and highlighting commitment language. Amplified intrinsic motivation until the patient self-initiated commitment and even requested his own follow-up accountability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (external pressure, resistance to imposed rules, phone as wind-down) was reflected neutrally then pivoted toward ownership and problem-solving. No sustain talk was reinforced; each instance became a springboard.
