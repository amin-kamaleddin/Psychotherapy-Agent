# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, non-judgmental throughout with repeated affirmations of the client's self-knowledge. However, some affirmations feel formulaic, and setbacks are not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections show good attunement to the client's competing needs. Emotional layers (e.g., discouragement about past bailouts) are not fully explored, and reflections sometimes pivot quickly to questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on re-establishing exercise with each exchange advancing a concrete, value-linked plan. No topic drift, converging on specific goals and follow-up accountability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine egalitarian interaction; the follow-up plan is co-created and every strategy originates from the client. Client explicitly confirms the collaboration helped her realize she already knew what she needed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional support for choice: 'on your own terms,' protecting the 'just looking' boundary, and offering the client options. Client confirms she did not feel pressured.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the client rather than supplied, with open-ended questions eliciting her own reasons and solutions. Counselor deliberately highlights the self-generated nature of her commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is genuinely located in the client, who authored the shoes-by-the-door plan, the 'just looking' script, and goal pacing. Counselor consistently credits the client as the plan's author.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation of change talk culminating in explicit commitment language ('I want this to actually stick this time'). Commitment statements are named and reinforced by the counselor.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers are acknowledged without amplification and redirected to client-generated solutions. However, deliberate techniques like double-sided reflections or strategic reframing are largely absent.
