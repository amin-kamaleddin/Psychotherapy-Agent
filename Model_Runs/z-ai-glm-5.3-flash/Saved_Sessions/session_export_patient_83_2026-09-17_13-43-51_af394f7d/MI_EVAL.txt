# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with explicit validation of the patient's anxiety; however, affirmations of the patient are sparse and generic, limiting expressions of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some early perspective-taking, but the session is dominated by information delivery with few true reflections; vulnerable disclosures like almost skipping the pill and her distrust ('nobody told me anything') were not explored or mirrored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on understanding and safely starting the statin, with no aimless drift; though achieved through information delivery rather than collaborative goal-setting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative gestures like asking permission to draft a script exist, but the interaction is counselor-led with limited agenda negotiation; the patient's priorities are not used to shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice offered (optional script, med review), but the session is largely directive with instructions and pre-written scripts; patient ownership of decisions is not explicitly reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost entirely information-giving with no open questions exploring the patient's own motivations, values, or confidence; her shift to readiness resulted from education, not elicitation of intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional choice points show some collaboration, but the overall stance is expert-driven; the patient's own knowledge about her routines is not actively drawn upon, and partnership language is largely absent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient generates commitment language ('I feel ready to start this now'), but this arises naturally from education rather than deliberate evocation; missed opportunity to deepen commitment when she disclosed nearly skipping the medication.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk (skepticism, near-decision to skip the pill) is not reinforced, but it is bypassed rather than acknowledged; a double-sided reflection could have been used to resolve ambivalence.
