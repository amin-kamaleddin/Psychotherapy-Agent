# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment despite the client's minimization, and immediate respect for his confidentiality request. The client's closing thanks ('for not lecturing me') confirms he experienced genuine acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captured both sides of ambivalence and validated his feeling blindsided. Reflections were sometimes practical rather than emotionally deep, missing chances to explore the shame in 'never told anyone' or the fear about bloodborne risk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on meth use and infection risk, returning to actionable steps even when the client drifted. Session ended with a specific, committed plan (kit ordered tonight, supplies before the weekend, clinic lookup).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner, co-constructing a plan shaped entirely by the client's preferences and offering expertise only at his request. Collaboration sometimes relied on counselor-generated option menus rather than inviting the client's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully honored the client's boundary that weekend use 'stays mine' and never pushed abstinence or pressured the testing decision. Choices were consistently framed as the client's own, reflected in his 'one step at a time' ending.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited genuine motivation from within, including self-discovery ('surprised I'm even considering this'). Relied heavily on structured option menus rather than open evocative questions about values, which limited depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treated the client's input as influential, with the plan shaped by his choices and explicit deference to his expertise. At times defaulted to menu-driven offerings rather than inviting the client to generate his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked substantial, genuine change talk and a concrete commitment ('I'll order the kit tonight') from a defensive client. Did not systematically deepen change talk through elaboration or confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled frequent sustain talk skillfully without arguing or reinforcing it, pivoting toward achievable harm-reduction steps. Occasionally paraphrased sustain talk back without consistently re-anchoring to the client's stated reasons for change.
