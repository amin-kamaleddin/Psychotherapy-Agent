# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, including normalizing hedging, missed days, and past failures. No judgment even when Marcus revealed his history of quitting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including subtle cues like the hedging and hopeful change talk. Reflections were generative, prompting elaboration and new insights from Marcus.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on blood sugar monitoring with each turn advancing a concrete outcome, culminating in a specific measurable plan. The session never wandered off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative approach with the counselor asking rather than telling, and Marcus's answers directly shaping the plan. Counselor expertise offered only on request.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Marcus's ownership and choice, including repositioning understanding readings as self-management rather than following orders. No advice was imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every turn contained evocative questions drawing out Marcus's own reasons and ideas, who generated all substantive content himself. Motivation was elicited rather than installed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marcus explicitly positioned as the expert, with his ideas genuinely shaping the session's plan. Active power-sharing at the highest level.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful use of selective reflection of change talk followed by open questions inviting Marcus to design his own plan, producing sustained client-generated commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally pivoted toward change rather than dwelt upon, and guilt material was well reframed. Minor growth area: more deliberate double-sided reflections could occasionally be used.
