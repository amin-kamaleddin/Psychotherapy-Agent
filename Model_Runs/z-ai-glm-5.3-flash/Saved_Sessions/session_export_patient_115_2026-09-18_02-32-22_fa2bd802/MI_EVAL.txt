# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental stance throughout, with individualized affirmations tied to Samir's own actions and progress. Sensitive material like fear and minimization is met with normalization and consistent positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and feeling (e.g., 'the fear and the hope tangled together'). Samir's repeated elaboration and deepening insight show the reflections landed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on the target behavior is maintained across every turn, with each response steering back after tangents. The conversation progresses steadily toward change with no aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely two-way; Samir's contributions (seeds, breathing, the birthday quit date, involving passengers) visibly shape the plan. The offer to 'figure it out together' is a textbook collaborative move.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change is framed as Samir's choice on his own terms, with advice offered as options and handed back to him to adapt. The psychiatrist never pressures or imposes a quit date, letting Samir arrive at it himself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently elicited from within via open questions about Samir's own reasons, capacity, and plans. Information is given only in direct response to his request for 'the how,' after which he generates his own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly positions Samir as the expert on his own life, and Samir even directs the agenda at points. The psychiatrist follows his lead, the hallmark of strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is systematically evoked and deepened, progressing from ambivalence to a concrete quit date and action plan. The mirror question amplifies commitment language, and the closing summary strategically assembles Samir's own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and redirected toward change, notably reframing the slip question as 'protecting this quit date.' The psychiatrist avoids amplifying minimization and uses doubt as material for commitment.
