# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and defused Erik's defensiveness warmly, explicitly removing pressure. Affirmations of his strengths were somewhat sparse and generic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Erik's core frustration about control and independence. Reflections were sometimes brief, pivoting quickly to questions rather than exploring deeper emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on concrete targets and progressed logically from ambivalence to a plan with follow-up. Momentum occasionally diluted by listing many options at once.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered choices at nearly every turn, checked in on fit, and let Erik set the agenda. Much of the substantive content was counselor-generated rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A clear strength — repeatedly framed choices as Erik's and built the plan on his terms. Fell short of full marks by supplying solutions and scripts rather than reinforcing his self-directed capacity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked some good open questions and elicited Erik's core value of control, but frequently answered questions with expert information instead of turning them back. Little exploration of deeper reasons for change or confidence talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Erik's input and respected his expertise, but much of the session shifted into advice-giving with lists and scripts. Erik's contributions shaped the content mainly through choosing among counselor-generated options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik produced meaningful change talk and the counselor capitalized on some of it with doable steps. However, change talk was rarely reflected back, deepened, or consolidated with confidence/importance questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk by reflecting control themes instead. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections, and Erik's 'not promising anything' was not strategically reframed.
