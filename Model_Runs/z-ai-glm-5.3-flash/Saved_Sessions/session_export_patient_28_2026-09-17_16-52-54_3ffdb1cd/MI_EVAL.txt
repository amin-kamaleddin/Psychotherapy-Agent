# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone with normalizing of worries about flaking. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and focused on the plan rather than deeper, individualized affirmation of Dante.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured Dante's ambivalence and fatigue. Did not deeply explore the emotional weight of past failed attempts or the loss of his former fit identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained tight focus on re-establishing exercise from opening through closing. Every exchange advanced the plan from importance and confidence through to a fully operationalized plan with accountability check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine co-construction: Dante supplied the timing, backup, reminder text, and check-in design. 'We'll tweak the plan together' and his positive closing response reflect true power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions consistently framed as Dante's, avoiding directives and honoring his 'one walk first' hesitancy. Option menus occasionally channeled rather than fully opened the choice space.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions drew intrinsic motivation and Dante's own solutions effectively. Missed opportunity to develop the emotional core of his past-athlete identity and post-exercise well-being.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Dante as the expert, explicitly inviting him to shape the process and check-in. Tendency to offer menu options rather than openly soliciting his own ideas at points kept this from a 5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully used rulers, confidence probing, and planning questions to escalate commitment language ('I can do one walk,' 'I can actually picture doing it'). Deepened change talk into a self-generated, specific plan with mobilizing questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without dwelling, and an if-then plan effectively converted it into a change strategy. Occasional reflections restated barriers without immediate reframing.
