# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently non-judgmental and respectful, even through repeated refusals. However, warmth felt somewhat formulaic, with limited explicit affirmation of Rowan's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of irritation were present but brief and often paired with immediate advice-giving. Deeper emotional material (e.g., Rowan's fear-laden 'maybe that's just luck?') went unexplored, and growing ambivalence was missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed anchored to the target behavior and moved from venting toward concrete outcomes (drafted messages, a possible first charge). Mid-session direction oscillated between venting and plan-offering without a clear strategic throughline.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaboration was largely performative early on, with the counselor supplying scripts and plans Rowan repeatedly declined. Genuine co-construction emerged only at the end when Rowan's own words shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was repeatedly affirmed and refusals were accepted without pressure, culminating in Rowan's own framing ('Not because anyone made me'). However, a rapid-fire menu of suggestions early on felt like subtle pushing before readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Motivation was largely informed into the client through didactic explanation rather than drawn out via open questions about Rowan's own values and concerns. The pivotal realization about emergency alerts came from the counselor's explanation, not elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invited preferences but often occupied the expert role, providing information and scripts without first asking what Rowan knew or wanted. Rowan himself noted 'nobody's actually told me straight,' and his contributions shaped the session only late.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk emerged ('maybe I'd charge it tonight just to test it out') but appeared accidental rather than strategically evoked. When change talk surfaced, the counselor pivoted to message drafts instead of deepening it with reflections or questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued against and received some reframing, but the counselor responded to it with premature plan and script offers that Rowan rejected three times. No double-sided reflections paired reservations with emerging concerns.
