# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, even when Jamal tested boundaries or minimized. Affirmations of autonomy and refusal to lecture sustained respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and frequent, capturing both sides of ambivalence and using Jamal's own language. Skepticism and perspective were consistently validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained clear focus on marijuana use framed through Jamal's value of basketball performance, progressing steadily from rapport to a concrete, time-limited experiment without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was genuinely collaborative, with the plan emerging entirely from Jamal's answers and the counselor adapting to his informal register. Decisions were co-constructed with deference to his knowledge.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was exemplary for a mandated client, immediately framing topics as Jamal's choice and accepting his limits without pushback. The plan was fully self-selected and affirmed as such.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason for change came from Jamal himself—stamina, guarding Marco, 'either way I'll know something.' The counselor never argued for change, only amplified his emerging motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly positioned Jamal as the expert, and his contributions genuinely shaped the session's direction. Challenges were met by returning ownership to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully evoked change talk from a precontemplative client, drawing out commitment language, a self-generated plan, and an experimental framing. Reinforcement of his ownership was strong.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally redirected effectively without argument, and Jamal's language shifted toward change by session end. A slight deduction because the counselor briefly voiced the sustain side in a double-sided reflection ('it's just pickup, it's not that serious'), giving brief airtime to the status quo.
