# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard, explicitly normalizing potential slips ('there is no failing here with me. If Saturday-me wins a night, that's information, not failure') and validating fears without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and incorporate the client's exact language, capturing both sides of ambivalence and the emotional weight of relapse fears ('living in that waiting mode sounds exhausting').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on drinking and contraceptive reliability with strategic, sequenced questions that build visible momentum toward a concrete plan, without the session ever wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals; every strategy originates from the client and the closing plan is co-constructed, with the counselor following Mei's lead at each step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely offers choice ('picking one weekend... or writing down that picture first') and explicitly returns control to the client, with no persuading or directive advice anywhere in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key motivations—career goals, breaking the family cycle, past exam success, and concrete strategies—emerge from the client's own words rather than being supplied, with no arguing for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions the client as the expert ('you're the expert on what belongs there'), and every intervention strategy originates from her, which the counselor names and honors.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through strategic open questions, drawing out Mei's own reasons, strategies, and rising confidence (four to six), then affirms and reflects each change talk statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced and is met with double-sided reflections reweighting toward success evidence; growth area is occasionally affirming the legitimacy of doubts before redirecting.
