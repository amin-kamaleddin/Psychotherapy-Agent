# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming. The counselor explicitly refuses to label ('that's yours to figure out') and Derrick confirms feeling respected ('thanks for not lecturing me').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both surface content and deeper meaning (e.g., 'my pride's expensive,' the social cost of sobriety). Derrick repeatedly builds on reflections rather than correcting them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on drinking, driving, and safeguards, progressing strategically from brainstorming to concrete plans, implementation, and accountability. No aimless wandering despite a conversational tone.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All strategies originate from the client and the accountability plan is jointly constructed. Derrick endorses the plan as his own ('that's a plan. First one I've had in a while').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy strongly affirmed: no pressure to accept a label, options framed as choices, and Derrick's expertise deferred to throughout. Closing gratitude confirms he experienced genuine choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and strategies are drawn almost entirely from the client via open questions (co-sign story, 'pride's expensive'). No counselor-supplied reasons for change; the session ends in a client-generated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor explicitly positions Derrick as the expert ('You know your world better than I do'). Every actionable idea — cab money, rubber band, glove box papers, texting a cousin — came from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and developed, with the counselor highlighting the shift from 'if I need' to 'I need to step back.' Session concludes with a commitment statement and an accountability check-in.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not argued with and is redirected toward controllable actions. Brief exploration of barriers ('what might get in the way') risked dwelling on obstacles, keeping this from a 5.
