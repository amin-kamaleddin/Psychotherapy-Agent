# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with honest answers about confidentiality. However, warmth is thin and transactional; vulnerable disclosures like the blackout went unacknowledged, and few genuine affirmations were offered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Responses were dominated by information delivery rather than reflections. Emotionally significant moments—the scary blackout and fear of parental discovery—were met with facts rather than reflections of the underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session spent most of its time on confidentiality logistics rather than the drinking behavior. When alcohol came up, it jumped rapidly to risk questionnaires and harm-reduction prescriptions without building motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Largely expert-to-patient interaction; Marcus's contributions were treated as data rather than shaping the session. Joint agenda-setting and co-constructed planning were absent, with summaries announced rather than checked.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menu options were offered consistently and no coercive pressure to abstain was applied. However, the two-drink limit was prescribed without permission or eliciting Marcus's own ideas, provoking predictable resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was supplied via risk lists rather than drawn from Marcus's own values and concerns. His organically emerging change talk went unexploited, with no evocative questions about what matters to him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor occupied the expert role with didactic lectures and unilateral next steps. Marcus's expertise about his social scene was engaged only as assessment data, and menu choices were formulaic rather than genuinely shaping direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged ('maybe I don't need to match how much everyone else drinks'), and the mapping exercise elicited the blackout disclosure. But these moments received no reflection, affirmation, or deepening.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk was absorbed neutrally, then met with risk lectures, creating a debate dynamic where Marcus defended his drinking. No double-sided reflections or strategic shifts away from sustain talk were used.
