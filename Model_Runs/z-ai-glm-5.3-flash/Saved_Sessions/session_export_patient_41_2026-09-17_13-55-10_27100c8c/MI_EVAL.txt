# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm and nonjudgmental affirmations are present, but they feel formulaic and the counselor jumped into problem-solving before fully receiving Elira's vulnerable disclosure about anxiety and avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; repeated emotional disclosures about self-distrust, fear of failure, and hiding setbacks were met with instructions rather than reflections or exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, consistent focus on reducing avoidance and building follow-through with concrete behavioral steps; only limitation is that task-pushing sometimes overshadowed building sustainable self-trust.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine input is invited and Elira's choices shape the plan, but the counselor frames most turns as prescriptions and supplies content, leaving the power balance uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices are offered at several points, but detailed instructions and contingency plans are imposed before Elira asks, leaving little room for her to generate her own ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation, techniques, and success criteria largely come from the counselor; Elira's own insights and confidence are never deliberately drawn out or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, dictating structure, pace, and even content, while Elira's expertise about her own patterns is never treated as the primary source of guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elira spontaneously generates meaningful change talk and self-efficacy statements, but the counselor passes over these without strategic deepening or open questions to evoke more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, which is a strength, but it is never actively softened—no double-sided reflections, reframing, or exploration of her relapse fears and avoidance pattern.
