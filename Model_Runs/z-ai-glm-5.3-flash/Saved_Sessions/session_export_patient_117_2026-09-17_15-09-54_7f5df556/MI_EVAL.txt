# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and non-judgmental throughout with frequent, specific affirmations. Praise is somewhat formulaic and achievement-focused rather than conveying deep unconditional regard regardless of outcome.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflects content and validates effort, but reflections are largely summaries and praise. Misses the emotional weight of Samuel's doubts, such as fear of failing in front of Rico and the vulnerability of resisting urges alone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on tobacco reduction from start to finish, moving logically from journal review to trigger identification to a concrete, time-bound commitment. No topic drift; direction is exemplary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely an equal partnership, with plans built from Samuel's journal data and preferences and suggestions offered with permission. Occasionally dominates with expert-framed option lists and rapid multi-question turns that tilt the power balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames actions as Samuel's choices, who sets his own goal, timeline, and script. Sometimes stacks multiple questions and options per turn, which feels subtly guiding rather than purely choice-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Samuel's own reasons, coping ideas, confidence logic, and self-talk rather than lecturing. Sometimes embeds answers in questions, reducing space for Samuel to generate his own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Samuel as the expert, and his contributions (gum on tray, 'it passes' label, walk with Rico) shape the plan and are incorporated into summaries. Frequent pre-structured option lists and dense summaries somewhat steer the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk via open questions and confidence rulers used twice effectively, with confidence rising from 5 to 7. Could deepen change talk with elaboration questions (e.g., 'Why does five a day matter to you?') instead of moving quickly to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Never reinforces sustain talk, but largely bypasses doubts (the ten minutes feeling like forever, slipping up before Rico) rather than actively softening them with double-sided reflections or reframing. Jumps to problem-solving; only gently addresses anticipated setbacks at the very end.
