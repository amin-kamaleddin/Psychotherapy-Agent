# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with genuine, specific affirmations; the patient's gratitude for not being lectured is strong evidence. Falls short of 5 only because some closing affirmations became formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate complex reflections capturing ambivalence, identity, and fear of failure. Limited mainly by repetitive word-echoing rather than deeper exploration of the emotional core.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on declining alcohol in peer settings, progressing through a coherent arc from reasons to strategies to success markers and follow-up, achieved via client-centered questions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuinely joint endeavor — every decision posed as a question and co-constructed summary language. The patient's own words confirm equal footing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit, sustained permission and choice language, with all strategies attributed to the client. The patient's statement that it's 'mine, not something I'm being forced to do' is direct evidence it landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation elicited from within the client via open, values-linked questions. The patient realizing he 'figured out my own reasons' is the strongest evidence of complete evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every plan element originated from the patient, and the psychiatrist repeatedly credited him ('That's you doing the work, not me'). Textbook power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked, selectively highlighted, and reinforced, yielding dense self-generated commitment and reasons. Fully client-owned.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk consistently met with gentle reframes and normalization without reinforcing ambivalence. Short of 5 because redirection is implicit rather than strategic double-sided reflection.
