# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout; validates Ryan's annoyance and normalizes peer pressure. Missed chances to explicitly affirm his honesty and willingness to attend a mandated session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional feeling reflections but rapid pivots to advice lists dominate. Deeper ambivalence and the school-vs-drinking value conflict Ryan himself raised were never explored with complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, logical focus on avoiding alcohol-related trouble, progressing from tactics to plans to confidence rating. Weakness: quickly narrowed to harm reduction without exploring Ryan's drinking itself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ryan's choices shaped the plan (Marcus, 'bounce'), but the counselor dominated with rapid-fire advice menus. Ryan mostly selected from offered options rather than co-creating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and accepted Ryan's rejections without pressure. Would be stronger with explicit affirmation of Ryan's ownership and capacity for change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session was largely advice-driven, with the counselor supplying reasons, tactics, and scripts. Ryan's motivators (grades, degree, probation) surfaced spontaneously with minimal exploration; the only evocative question (0-10 confidence) came very late.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for Ryan's input at decision points, but the overall pattern was expert-driven with pre-packaged tips and plans. Ryan's contributions shaped the plan but he was rarely positioned as the expert on his own situations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ryan generated meaningful change talk (school matters, 'I want to do this right'), but largely self-initiated rather than evoked. The confidence scale question was good, but change talk was not deepened or reflected when it appeared.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ryan's sustain talk ('bad luck,' 'everyone drinks') was mostly ignored via topic shifts rather than strategically reframed. Neutral handling avoided damage but missed chances to resolve ambivalence with double-sided reflections.
