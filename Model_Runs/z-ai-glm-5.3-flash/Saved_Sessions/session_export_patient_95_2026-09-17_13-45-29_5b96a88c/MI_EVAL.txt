# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic respect and repeated thanks ('Thank you for telling me'), but Maria perceives conditional regard due to the checklist-like safety questioning; her suspicion that the counselor is 'just doing their job' is never addressed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections ('drowning image makes sense') land well, but repetitive re-asking of answered safety questions signals poor listening, and Maria's explicit request to explore her pain goes unmet.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays firmly focused on safety and yields a concrete outcome (pharmacy take-back at 11:00 AM with reminder). The tight direction is appropriate for an actively suicidal client, though it crowds out emotional exploration Maria requested.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some input is solicited and plans co-created (reminder, pharmacy), but the counselor's safety agenda dominates and Maria's critique of transactional care is never explored; a shared plan never fully develops.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices rather than demands, and honors Maria's refusals (sister message, grounding exercises). Points deducted for repetitive safety questions that subtly communicate distrust of her autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation elicited (niece, 'two percent' hope), but mostly by passive space-holding rather than active evocation; little open questioning probes her reasons to live or what 'hurt less' would look like.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Options offered and refusals honored, but the checklist-driven risk questioning functions as expert gatekeeping rather than power-sharing; Maria calls this out twice without acknowledgment or adjustment.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently reflects and affirms Maria's own reasons for change (showing up, pharmacy plan, niece, 'two percent'). Missed opportunities to deepen the 'two percent' statement or explore what a different life would look like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk (appropriate), but few deliberate reframes tie her doubts to change talk; one statement verges on reassurance/persuasion rather than softening through her own reasoning.
