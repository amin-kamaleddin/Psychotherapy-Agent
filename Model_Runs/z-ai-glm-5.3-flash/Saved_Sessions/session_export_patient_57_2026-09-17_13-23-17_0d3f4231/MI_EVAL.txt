# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, non-judgmental tone, but warmth is minimal and transactional. Missed opportunities to affirm Jamal's honesty and self-awareness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; the counselor responds with questions and advice rather than reflections. Meaningful disclosures about belonging and fear of being 'the team snitch' are never reflected back or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and team compliance throughout, moving logically from incident to prevention plan. However, direction follows the counselor's agenda rather than collaboratively established goals, and the deeper party-attendance issue is deferred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction resembles directive coaching rather than collaboration between equals, with bullet lists and scripts dominating every turn. Jamal's expertise in team dynamics is treated as a filter for pre-made solutions rather than the foundation of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some genuine choice, and Jamal's edits meaningfully shape the final script. However, solutions are framed prescriptively and his ambivalence about promises isn't explored with respect for his pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is supplied externally via consequence-avoidance rather than elicited from within. Jamal's values, athletic identity, and own reasons for change are never explored; his genuine self-reflection is met with counselor-generated menus instead of curiosity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Despite offering surface-level choices, the stance is expert-driven—the counselor supplies scripts and canned refusal lines while Jamal does the real work of adapting them. His knowledge of team culture is never acknowledged as the key expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no deliberate evocation of change talk; the counselor never asks about Jamal's desires, reasons, or how drinking fits with his goals. Change talk emerges accidentally through brainstorming and is never reinforced or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal's extensive sustain talk (peer belonging, fear of exclusion, 'snitch' concerns) is met with logistics problem-solving rather than ambivalence work. No double-sided reflections, reframes, or pivots toward his own motivations are used.
