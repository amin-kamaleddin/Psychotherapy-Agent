# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating defensiveness and accepting Marek's limits without pressure. Affirmations were sparse and pragmatic, keeping warmth from being deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections captured frustration and ambivalence, but reflections were brief and sparse. Opportunities to explore the emotional meaning of Marek's social identity ('beer with the guys') were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and secured a concrete, low-pressure next step with a tracking template and follow-up plan. The focus stayed on assessment/tracking rather than connecting to Marek's values and reasons for change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was framed as joint work with menu-based options and accommodation of Marek's negotiated scope. Psychoeducation and template delivery were somewhat one-directional, with limited genuine co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear strength — repeated emphasis on Marek's choice ('no pressure to change yet,' 'realistic next steps only if you want'). His refusal to commit to drinking less was accepted without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was largely presumed to come from information delivery rather than elicited from Marek. Almost no open questions drew out his own values, reasons, or what the risk data meant to him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Deferring to Marek's choices and asking permission were done well, but expertise was delivered didactically. Marek's skepticism and social context did not meaningfully shape the session content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Structured choices successfully elicited modest change talk ('now I've got something to think about'). This change talk was never deepened or reinforced with evocative questions or exploration of his conditional commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was tolerated without arguing or reinforcing, and the 'health habit' reframe was effective. No double-sided reflections or strategic pivots were used to actively soften it.
