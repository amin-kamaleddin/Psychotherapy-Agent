# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with affirmations ('talking here already takes courage') and a direct response to Amira's fear of abandonment ('I won't give up on you'). Score limited by formulaic, repetitive affirmations and safety scripts that reduce warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate emotional reflections ('worn out and alone') and strong attunement to shame sensitivity by honoring the 'how did it feel' framing. Later shifts to task-oriented instructions crowd out deeper exploration of guilt and worthlessness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus moving from vague helplessness to a concrete, actionable plan (7:30 shower, 2 minutes outside, 8:00 check-in), plus appropriate suicide risk assessment. Direction was achieved through heavy instruction rather than collaborative refinement, and drug use was addressed only indirectly.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-constructed with Amira choosing the step, timing, and check-in format. Uneven in places due to long counselor-generated instruction blocks with minimal back-and-forth.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong choice framing ('Which of those, if any, feels doable?'), removing pass/fail framing, and optional advice. Advice-giving blocks were dense and directive, and Amira's tentative responses were met with reassurance rather than affirming her full ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Drew out some client motivation ('I can do that much,' 'maybe this is a start'), but did not explore Amira's reasons for change, values, or hopes. Counselor energy went to strategies and safety information rather than evoking intrinsic motivation, critical in early contemplation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited Amira's expertise through option menus, check-in question selection, and 'Anything else you want me to keep in mind?' — incorporating her input fully. Limited by expert drift into lengthy prescriptive advice without first seeking her ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced small commitments ('I can do that much,' specific time, check-in agreement). Did not deepen emerging change talk or explore Amira's own reasons, values, and hopes for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing against hopelessness, gently reframed minimal engagement as effort, and normalized predicted failure with low-stakes framing. Did not use double-sided reflections linking sustain talk to change talk or strategically address the worthlessness theme.
