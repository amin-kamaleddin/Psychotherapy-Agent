# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with explicit acceptance statements ('Whatever you choose is okay'). Individualized affirmations of Jordan's character came only late in the session, limiting warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Jordan's ambivalence well, especially around his grandmother. Frequent stacked questions diluted reflective listening and limited deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the flu vaccination from start to finish, progressing logically from ambivalence to a concrete, actionable plan with no aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Joint exploration through options and co-developed planning, with Jordan's ideas shaping the plan. Counselor sometimes offered packaged solutions (scripts, logistics) without first eliciting Jordan's own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with repeated explicit affirmation of choice and permission-based information sharing. Jordan's final decision was clearly self-generated, and his face-saving framing was accepted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent elicitation of Jordan's own motivations via the grandparent focus and importance ruler, producing a visible shift from 'don't expect me to change my mind' to 'I'll make it happen.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jordan's choice of the family topic drove the session, and permission was sought before advising. Turns bundling multiple questions and expert-driven closing advice slightly limited power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful and consistent evocation of change talk, from preparatory ('maybe getting the shot for them isn't the craziest idea') to commitment language ('I'll make it happen'), with selective reinforcement of these statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged with neutral reflections and resistance avoided by pivoting to family protection. Some summaries echoed sustain talk verbatim without double-sided reflections pairing it with change talk.
