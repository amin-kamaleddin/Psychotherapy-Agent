# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently validates and normalizes Naomi's disclosures and fears without judgment, though warmth at times feels formulaic with limited explicit unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early complex reflection on her performance anxiety and good perspective-taking throughout, but reflections occasionally read as paraphrase checklists rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains tight focus on managing shoot anxiety with no drift; each turn advances a concrete plan with specific rehearsal schedule, first trial, measurable win, and follow-up time.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was largely co-constructed with genuine joint activities, but strategies arrived as a pre-packaged menu rather than brainstormed together and equality in problem-solving was not explicit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered meaningful choices and framed advice as optional, though strategy introduction sometimes preceded full permission and guidance was somewhat directive in structure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used an importance ruler and small-win questions to elicit some motivation, but much content was counselor-generated and Naomi's own reasons, values, and confidence were never deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi's choices shaped the plan, but the counselor retained an expert posture, delivered numbered recommendations, and asked permission only after advice was already presented.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked clear change talk via the importance ruler and small-win framing, but missed opportunities to deepen and amplify her hopeful statements when they emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk but responses were passive, acknowledging doubts neutrally rather than using double-sided reflections or pivoting back to her stated motivation.
