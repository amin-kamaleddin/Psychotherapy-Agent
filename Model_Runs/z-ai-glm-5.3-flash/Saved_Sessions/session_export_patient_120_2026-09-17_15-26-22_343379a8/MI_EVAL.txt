# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalized Sofia's fears, but offered few individualized affirmations of her strengths or maternal commitment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate but became formulaic and repeated verbatim, and deeper emotional content—identity loss and self-criticism—went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on stopping alcohol during pregnancy with information, planning, and timely role-play rehearsal, though core ambivalence was left unexplored and no summary or consolidation occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia shaped the rehearsal and the counselor checked in, but solutions and scripts were largely delivered in list form rather than co-created from Sofia's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent permission-seeking and genuine choice in lines and rehearsal agenda, though option menus constrained solutions and pressured motivation ('I don't really have any other choice') was never reframed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No open questions were used to evoke Sofia's reasons for change or confidence; motivation surfaced only spontaneously from the client while the counselor supplied information and strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and choice-offering respected Sofia's expertise, but the flow remained expert-driven with scripted lines and option lists; her direct question 'What would you do?' was answered rather than turned back to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia spontaneously produced strong change talk about maternal responsibility, but the counselor missed these cues—no reflections, affirmations, or elaboration requests—and focused on refusal tactics instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without judgment and not amplified, but no double-sided reflections linked it to Sofia's change talk, and the counselor engaged sustain themes via workaround options rather than pivoting to her motivations.
