# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental regard ('You have nothing to apologize for,' 'I believe you'). The client's comment that no one has ever asked for her ideas before highlights how accepted she feels.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, deep reflections throughout, capturing both surface content and underlying meaning (demands, choice, the internal critic), e.g., 'the cat doesn't care about the list.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on safer coping with a concrete target (demand-free moments), culminating in a specific step: five minutes on the bench. Occasional drift into exploratory nuance slightly slows the momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal — 'as we talk together,' 'what ideas do you have' — with all ideas originating from the client and framed as joint discovery rather than prescription.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice repeatedly affirmed ('no promise needed here,' permission to leave when ready). The client's realization 'at least it's my choice then' evidences effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicit authentic client-generated motivations and solutions; the client even notes no one has asked for her ideas before. A few questions nudge toward pre-framed directions rather than purely open evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client ('You're the expert on your own life'), and her contributions directly shape the session direction — a hallmark of power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk ('Maybe it's something, I could try,' the bench and 'My call' ideas). Falls slightly short of consistently drawing out commitment and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Neither argues with nor amplifies sustain talk, accepting doubts and pivoting ('And yet you noticed something...'). Occasional echoing of sustain talk does give it slight airtime.
