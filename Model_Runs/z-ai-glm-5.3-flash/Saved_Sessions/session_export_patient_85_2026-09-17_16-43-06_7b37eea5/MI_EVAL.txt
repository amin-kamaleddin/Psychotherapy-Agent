# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated warmth and unconditional positive regard with frequent affirmations and no judgment about the weight regain. However, affirmations became somewhat formulaic and repetitive, lacking deeper individualized regard around Marco's sense of being 'tricked' and his shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections were made, but reflections were sparse compared to questions and the session shifted quickly to action planning. Marco's emotional material—feeling tricked, fear of quitting, past self-blame—was acknowledged only briefly rather than deeply explored with complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained an exceptionally clear focus on reducing fruit juice from the opening question through the end. Every exchange tied back to the juice-reduction plan with concrete forward movement and no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The tone was largely collaborative, with the plan co-constructed across turns and Marco refining and adding his own ideas. At times the counselor drove the agenda with bundled advice blocks rather than consistently negotiating each step as an equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor regularly offered choices and never pressured or shamed, allowing Marco to freely adjust and build on suggestions. Several suggestions were delivered in a directive manner (providing a full script, specific tips) without first asking permission, which tempered Marco's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The core strategy was evocative—open questions, past success elicitation, confidence scaling, and future success indicators drew motivation from Marco rather than lecturing. The score falls short of 5 because the counselor supplemented evocation with supplied content and did not fully develop the meaning behind Marco's confidence rating of 10 or his pride.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were good partnership elements, such as offering choices and asking what would help rather than prescribing. However, the counselor frequently slipped into an expert/advice-giving role, with the counselor's suggestions often coming first and shaping the plan more than Marco's contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor actively evoked and reinforced change talk, drawing out strong commitment language including a confidence rating of 10 and concrete commitments. The score falls short of 5 because the counselor did not consistently deepen change talk after it emerged, moving straight to barriers rather than elaborating Marco's confidence statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not amplify sustain talk, which is a strength. However, moments of sustain talk—Marco's love of OJ and concern about cold turkey making him cranky—were passed over without double-sided reflections or skillful pivoting, amounting to neutral avoidance rather than active softening.
