**Comments:** Warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with nonjudgmental affirmations ('that kind of honesty about yourself isn't small'). Sofia explicitly noted the absence of lecturing, though closing affirmations edged slightly toward cheerleading.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections, including a well-crafted double-sided reflection and reflections on deeper emotional meaning (fear of failure, need for connection, identity concerns).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on marijuana use and scaling back, with progressive steps toward a concrete change plan (Marisol this week, sister on Sunday).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership — Sofia's ideas shaped the entire plan, and the counselor credited her language in summaries. Client herself noted the non-hierarchical tone.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy actively reinforced: 'your plan, on your terms,' genuine choice about session direction, and success reframed away from pass/fail to Sofia's own standard.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation — motivation drawn entirely from Sofia's own realizations, most notably transforming 'I'll think about it' into self-generated commitment ('I'm doing it').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor consistently positioned Sofia as the expert ('You know yourself best'), followed her contributions rather than directing, and checked in on her agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocative questions elicited pivotal change talk ('we don't always need it to have a good time'), which the counselor highlighted and deepened, culminating in strong commitment language.

**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, pivoting from minimization and fear of failure toward possibility and past successes. Slight deduction for the early double-sided reflection giving airtime to use benefits, though strategically defensible.
