# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with openers that put Naomi at ease ('I'm glad you're here') and affirmations of her ideas and progress. Affirmations are somewhat sparse and formulaic, with little explicit unconditional positive regard beyond her ideas.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that accurately summarizes content and captures ambivalence well ('prep feels doable, practice shoots feel scary'). Reflections tend to stay at the content level, missing deeper emotional themes like shame, fear of failure, and self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains unwavering focus on managing shoot anxiety while building a concrete, progressive action plan (prep tools, break, cue, scaling, rehearsal, backup, check-in). Every turn advances the agenda with no topic drift, all tied to Naomi's stated goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is genuinely joint, with the plan negotiated item by item using Naomi's input (break timing, vibrate-only timer). The dynamic often follows a propose-and-approve pattern rather than fully equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support via permission-seeking and choice-offering ('Would it be alright...?', 'Would you prefer...?') while honoring Naomi's pace. Options are typically counselor-generated lists rather than Naomi's own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from Naomi—her goal statement, her insights ('a ladder instead of one big scary thing'), and her rising confidence all originate from her. Falls short of deeper exploration of her intrinsic values and why overcoming this matters beyond symptom relief.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Naomi's input as central, adopting her preferences and suggestions as the plan's core ('equipment check' excuse, break timing). Several steps are presented as pre-packaged menus for her to pick from rather than co-constructed from her own words.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evokes and reinforces change talk through goal questions, confidence scaling (6 to 7), and rehearsal; Naomi's language strengthens across the session. Could deepen change talk further by exploring what success would mean to her beyond symptom relief.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi voices substantial sustain talk (fear of messing up, not sticking with things, 'too simple to work'), which the counselor acknowledges without reinforcing. Responses are mostly neutral rather than strategically softening—missing double-sided reflections and affirmations to redirect toward change.
