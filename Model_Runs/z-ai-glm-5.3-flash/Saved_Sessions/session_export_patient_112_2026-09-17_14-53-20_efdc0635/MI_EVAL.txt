# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with brief affirmations ('well done'), though affirmations are somewhat generic rather than personalized to Rosa's specific strengths like her self-awareness or grandbabies motivation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture Rosa's ambivalence about weight, money, and identity, but deeper emotional content (shame, grandbabies, identity loss) is acknowledged without being explored with emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus maintained throughout with rulers, a concrete action plan, and a strong closing summary, though the early identity discussion is left unresolved before pivoting to practical planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely equal interaction with Rosa's own ideas (sister card, casual phrasing, walking) directly shaping the final plan, though list-style delivery of interventions creates brief moments of uneven power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames choices as Rosa's — asking permission before advice, offering options, respecting her pacing ('Don't push me too fast') — though the NRT reassurance subtly persuades toward a tool she distrusted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and forward-looking questions evoke self-generated solutions and growing confidence (3 to 6), but the grandbabies motivation passes without exploration and some content comes via counselor-supplied scripts rather than fully evoked client language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing with repeated 'which feels most doable' questions and genuine client input shaping the session, slightly undercut by a mid-session shift into list-form expert-driven education.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance/confidence rulers and planning questions elicits strong commitment language ('I put the gum by the coffee maker tonight'), though deeper change talk (grandbabies, explicit commitment) could be more deliberately developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk but responds to fears (weight gain, NRT distrust, 'trading one addiction') with reassurance and information rather than double-sided reflections, missing key opportunities to resolve ambivalence.
