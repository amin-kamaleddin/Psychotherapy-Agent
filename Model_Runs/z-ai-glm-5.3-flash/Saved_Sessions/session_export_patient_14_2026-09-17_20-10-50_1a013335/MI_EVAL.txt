# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, affirming Andrei's identity as a 'numbers man', respecting his time concerns, and welcoming his scepticism without judgment. Even irritable boundaries ('I'm not promising miracles') are accepted and reframed as openness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate, including complex and double-sided reflections that capture Andrei's ambivalence and deepest emotions (the father's stroke, 'it sits there'). The counselor consistently uses Andrei's own framing and language, showing deep understanding of how he thinks.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focus remains consistently on weight, blood pressure, and lifestyle while accommodating Andrei's time constraints. Every exchange advances toward a concrete, self-determined plan, culminating in the March appointment with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a genuine equal partnership—Andrei's business-planning metaphors (contingency, ROI, spreadsheets, deadlines) shape the entire intervention. Andrei explicitly notes 'that's the smartest thing you've said' and 'nobody's asked me that before,' confirming the collaborative approach worked.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All plan elements (stairs, tracking, March deadline) are generated and chosen by Andrei, with no advice imposed. The counselor explicitly states the plan is 'yours, shaped by your life, not mine,' and even the definition of success is Andrei's own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor never lectures, instead drawing out Andrei's own motivations (father's stroke, doctor's frowning, proving he can finish something for himself) and his own solutions. The shift from 'I haven't got time' to 'I'll be here in March' shows visibly growing motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Andrei, who is repeatedly asked for his own ideas and explicitly credited ('You know your tax season better than anyone'). Andrei's contributions visibly drive the session direction from start to finish.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation draws powerful motivation ('not heading the same direction he did') and self-reflective change talk ('proving I can finish something for myself'). Each change talk utterance is affirmed and built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally handled well through double-sided reflections pairing scepticism with hope, and effective redirection. However, some sceptical statements ('I'm not promising miracles') are acknowledged but not always actively reframed toward change—mostly passed over and re-anchored on motivation, which works but is not maximally strategic.
