# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with affirmations of strengths ('stubbornness and honesty') and no criticism despite Amira's self-deprecation. Unconditional regard could occasionally be more explicit during her darkest moments of self-blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate complex reflections capturing meaning beneath the surface (e.g., reflecting the 'why bother' question as risk in hoping again). Some reflections flow immediately into questions, preventing them from fully landing; deeper emotional exploration of the pain beneath self-blame would strengthen this.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful trajectory from ambivalence about connection to a concrete behavioral step, coping planning, strengths, and future visualization. The focus stays tightly on Amira's stated concerns; drug use is addressed indirectly but strategically via reconnection as leverage for recovery.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative session in which Amira's ideas shape the direction — her suggestion to text her sister becomes the centerpiece and her self-identified strengths become the coping strategy. No expert-driven steering or agenda imposition.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership: options are offered ('remembering why you're doing this, giving yourself permission to wait, or something else entirely') and Amira's choices are validated. The plan is entirely self-generated with no prescriptive advice or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn out rather than supplied — questions about strengths, meaning, and the sister's perspective elicit Amira's own 'why' and commitment. She moves from 'why bother' to 'tonight, then. I'll send it' through her own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located within the client ('That's you drawing on your own wisdom'), and Amira's contributions substantially drive the session. A model of equal partnership and power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is systematically elicited and deepened — questions about small tests, strengths, and meaning produce plans and commitment language ('I'm allowed to try again'). The counselor names and reinforces change talk and connects it to strengths and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('why bother,' 'she's only calling out of guilt') is met with empathic reflection followed by strategic pivots toward agency without invalidating hopelessness. Dwelling on reasons against change is consistently avoided.
