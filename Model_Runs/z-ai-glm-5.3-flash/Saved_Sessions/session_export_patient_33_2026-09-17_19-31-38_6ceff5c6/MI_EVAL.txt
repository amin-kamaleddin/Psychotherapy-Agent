# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Diego's self-knowledge and courage, and explicitly honored his choice not to quit without judgment or pressure. Especially impactful given he arrived expecting to be lectured.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and deeper meaning (e.g., the 'pause button' reflection and the deeper layer of not caring when low).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear focus on harm reduction from the first question, building purposefully from problem identification to concrete action steps. Client-driven plan emerged without steering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Diego's ideas shaping every element including the script, sequencing, and next-session agenda. Suggestions were tentative and negotiable.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy repeatedly reinforced: 'that's completely your call,' offering choices rather than directives, and letting Diego set the pace on deeper issues. Diego's ownership of the plan confirms the tone landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from Diego throughout via open questions, eliciting strong change talk (10/10 readiness, self-generated script and plan). No reasons for change were supplied by the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located within the client, and Diego's contributions substantially determined the session's direction. Counselor additions were framed as light, optional suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and reinforced, producing Diego's 10/10 readiness, concrete commitments, and self-generated plans. Counselor deepened it by having Diego rehearse the conversation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was mostly redirected effectively toward harm reduction rather than amplified. However, some sustain talk (the escape function) passed without strategic pairing via double-sided reflections.
