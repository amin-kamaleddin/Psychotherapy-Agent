# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment even when the patient admits enjoying smoking. Affirmations were genuine and individualized, reframing self-critical labels using her own values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captured both sides of ambivalence and attributed insights to the patient. The counselor picked up subtle emotional cues and mirrored her own language ('Lighter'). 

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on smoking with progressive movement toward change: ambivalence, stress link, replacement walk, Friday plan, Mia, jar, success criteria. No drift; every exchange served the target behavior while remaining client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner, offering options neutrally rather than prescribing and building each question on the patient's last utterance. No direct advice; direction emerged from dialogue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently affirmed ('in your own time', 'Not forever. Just Friday') and ownership repeatedly reinforced. Patient explicitly named the non-pushing approach as key and internalized the plan as her own idea.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason for change came from the patient's own mouth and was reflected and amplified. No persuasive arguments; the counselor elicited her values, supports, confidence markers, and pride.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located with the client, and all plan elements (walk, jar, Mia, success criteria) originated from her. Her closing remark — 'my idea, which I suppose it is' — confirms power sharing was fully achieved.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk at every turn and explicitly named and reinforced it ('that's you talking yourself into something'). Her language trajectory from ambivalence to 'possible' and concrete planning shows masterful cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubts were neither ignored nor amplified; double-sided reflections and pivots to strengths were used effectively. The one limitation: the patient's favourite Friday-night cigarette at the close went unaddressed, missing a brief chance to evoke confidence about that bridge.
