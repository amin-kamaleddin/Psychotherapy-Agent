# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, including reframing Marco's regain as 'missing information' rather than failure and affirming his worth. No judgment or disapproval at any point.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, accurate reflective listening throughout, including complex reflections capturing ambivalence and emotional meaning (e.g., 'running this thing instead of the weight running me'). Understanding goes well beyond surface content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on the juice consumption and weight goal with no topic drift. Every turn advances momentum, from obstacle identification to concrete commitment and relapse prevention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor behaves as an equal partner, co-constructing the session flow from Marco's own ideas with inclusive, curious questions. Educational contributions build on rather than replace Marco's expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change consistently framed as optional ('if anything'), with strategies originating entirely from Marco and explicit ownership language ('you're the one bringing it to her tonight'). The open invitation to return further supports choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within Marco via open questions eliciting his own reasons, confidence ('A 10'), values, and self-generated coping strategies. Change talk is developed and reflected at a high skill level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client ('You're the one who knows your daily routines best'), and Marco's own contributions substantially shape the session direction. All strategies originate from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked through open questions and amplified via reflections highlighting resolve and pride. Marco's commitments ('A 10', talking with his wife tonight) are consistently reflected and strengthened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I love that OJ') is acknowledged rather than amplified, with double-sided reflections and normalization. Minor growth area: a few instances quote the sustain language back rather than pivoting more actively away from it.
