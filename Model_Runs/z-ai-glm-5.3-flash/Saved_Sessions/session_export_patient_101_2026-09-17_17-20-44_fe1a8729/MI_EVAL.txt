# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard; client explicitly stated she felt unjudged about her drinking. Self-protective thinking was validated as reasonable, wise, and brave.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early reflective listening that deepened the client's core concern. Later sessions became advice-heavy, leaving emotional themes (shame, family pain, fear of telling friends) acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on the alcohol-contraception link and progressed to a concrete action plan. The drinking behavior itself (drink limits) was deferred without interim planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely two-way interaction with menus, choice questions, and co-developed scripts that the client shaped. However, the counselor supplied most ideas rather than drawing solutions from the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client made her own choices throughout, including readiness rating, first step, timeline, and declining extra prep help. Some advice (booking within 1-2 weeks) was fairly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong motivation emerged but was largely spontaneous rather than skillfully evoked. The counselor shifted to providing information and scripts rather than probing the client's reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor repeatedly offered options and honored the client's choices, additions, and timeline. Choices were mostly between counselor-generated options rather than eliciting the client's own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Substantial spontaneous change talk occurred but was not systematically reinforced or deepened. No evocative questions, confidence rulers, or explicit eliciting of commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification, and the 'permission to drink' worry was normalized. Little strategic softening—no double-sided reflections pivoting sustain talk to change talk, and extensive problem-solving on each barrier.
