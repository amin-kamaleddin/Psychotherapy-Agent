# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance despite Dylan's dismissiveness and blame externalization, with no labeling or moralizing. However, warmth remained somewhat procedural and missed opportunities to affirm Dylan's honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Dylan's practical concerns about his record and social life. However, reflections stayed at the content/strategy level and missed emotional undercurrents like his resentment and instrumental cooperation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifted toward legal/strategic damage control (statement drafting, rehearsing answers) rather than the target behavior itself. Dylan's underage drinking was never discussed beyond how to phrase it in a statement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interaction was polite and option-driven, but the counselor drafted the statement, provided all rehearsal answers, and set the process. Genuine co-construction did not occur, leaving collaboration partial rather than between equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, honored Dylan's decisions, and applied no pressure or coercion. However, autonomy support stayed at the logistical level, with the counselor supplying scripts whenever Dylan hesitated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Efforts elicited facts and compliance rather than motivation; no exploration of Dylan's values, goals, or discrepancies occurred. His motivation remained entirely external, a core MI failure with a pre-contemplative client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offering menus of options superficially shared power, but the counselor quickly assumed the expert role with templates and scripts. No effort was made to draw out Dylan's own expertise about his situation or values.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk was nearly absent, and the counselor never asked about Dylan's own reasons to reconsider drinking or future goals. Dylan's only movement was procedural compliance, not motivation for behavior change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Dylan produced heavy, sustained sustain talk ('it's just college', 'playing the game to win the game') that was neither reinforced nor actively softened. The counselor largely moved past it with logistics, leaving the anti-change position fully intact.
