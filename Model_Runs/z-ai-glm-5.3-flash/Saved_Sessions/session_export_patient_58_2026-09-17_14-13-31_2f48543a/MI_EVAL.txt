# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone ('Totally fair,' 'No judgment'), but offers no individualized affirmations and relies on formulaic closers like 'You've got this.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; Jamal's meaningful disclosures (e.g., late nights being 'the only time that's actually mine') are met with advice lists rather than reflections. No complex reflections or exploration of his frustration and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays firmly focused on tardiness with concrete steps and follow-up planning. However, direction is maintained through prescriptive guidance rather than connecting the behavior to Jamal's own values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is largely counselor-as-expert with pre-packaged solutions and imposed structures. Jamal's own ideas rarely shape the plan beyond one concession (built-in own time).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Choice is offered superficially but the counselor readily takes control when Jamal deflects ('pick them for me'). His statement 'I don't know if all that stuff is necessary' is bypassed rather than honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Almost no effort to elicit Jamal's own motivations, hopes, or values. His key disclosure ('what do I get back?') is answered with a scheduling tweak instead of curiosity, and reasons for change are supplied rather than drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor repeatedly assumes the expert role with bullet-pointed intervention lists in nearly every turn. Jamal's contributions minimally shape session content; when he hands over choice, the counselor takes over rather than returning it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal offers strong unprompted change talk ('I want to at least try,' 'I stop dreading mornings') that is never reflected or amplified. Each piece is met with logistical instructions instead of evocative follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not punished or amplified and is briefly acknowledged, but it is simply overridden with more advice. No double-sided reflections are used, and the core compliance-vs-autonomy ambivalence is never explored.
