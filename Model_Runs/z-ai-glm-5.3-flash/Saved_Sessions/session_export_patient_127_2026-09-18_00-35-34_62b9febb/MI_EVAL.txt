# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard — reframes 'lazy' as 'a standard' and 'pride,' never judges the criminal history or ambivalence, and normalizes uncertainty ('nobody here needs you sure'). Even the client's testy closer ('Don't get used to me being this agreeable') is met without defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and feeling, e.g., 'That 10am hour on the porch — feeling okay, then one text and the old life is right there.' The client repeatedly confirms accuracy ('I like hearing it put like that').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains unwavering focus on building a pro-social life and progresses logically from exploring meaningful work to a concrete, time-bound plan and a relapse strategy. Every question advances change without drifting or lecturing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange between equals — the clinician consults rather than directs, and the client's ideas shape the agenda, plan, and call script. No unsolicited advice is offered at any point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively reinforced: 'It's your call, your timing, your words' and 'nobody here needs you sure.' Every plan element is generated and owned by the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expert evocation — every motivational insight (the numbers being on his side, the porch being what he's fighting for) is spoken by the client, not the clinician. The clinician draws out the client's own wisdom through well-targeted questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client ('You know your world better than anyone'), and the plan, list, and accountability strategy all come from Timur. The clinician only asks questions that let the client's wisdom drive the content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook deepening of change talk — from 'not promising anything' to commitment language ('Not this time. No — not this time.') and a self-generated concrete plan. Questions like 'What kind of work has ever made you feel capable like that?' skillfully elicit the client's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled very well through affirming reframe ('lazy' becomes 'a standard'), but sustain talk still receives substantial airtime and some reflections on the pull of the old life give it additional airtime. The clinician pivots effectively every time, but this residual airtime prevents a 5.
