### Acceptance

**Comments:** Reflections are consistently accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing nuances like the drinking/low-mood cycle and her worry that 'the pull won't always feel reasonable in the moment.' Echoes her own phrases ('the wine will still be there tomorrow') to deepen understanding.

### Direction

### Collaboration

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every turn ends with an evocative open question that draws out her own reasons, confidence, and plans—she articulates her motivation, self-talk scripts, and success markers herself rather than being persuaded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Claudia as the expert from the opening line and actively defers expertise ('Your sense of where to start matters most here'); the entire final plan is client-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk with forward-looking questions about future feelings, priorities, and success markers, then reflects and reinforces each instance (e.g., 'better the next morning,' 'I'd call that a win').
