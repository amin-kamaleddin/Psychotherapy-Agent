# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance, normalizing fear and accepting her limits (e.g., 'a very short first call is allowed'). Falls short of 5 because affirmations are few and warmth occasionally reads as formulaic, with her final self-criticism left unaddressed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections consistently capture her ambivalence between wanting answers and fearing they'll make it 'real.' Depth is limited by brief reflections clustered with option menus and little exploration of what her fear concretely means to her.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on seeking medical evaluation, building from tracking and breathing to a scripted call to a written-message plan. There is no topic drift, and even grounding is framed as serving her ability to act.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is framed as joint problem-solving, and Anara's edits substantially shape the plan. However, the counselor tends to supply pre-built menus and checklists rather than co-constructing content from her own knowledge.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is consistently reinforced: 'support whatever you choose,' honors her 'not committing yet,' and pivots without pressure when she declines a real call today. Her own words ('I'm holding you to that') show the choice-supportive environment landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anara supplies her own reasons, script wording, and pace, showing motivation is elicited rather than imposed. The counselor sometimes provides scripts and symptom lists before exploring what she already knows and rarely asks about deeper motivations (e.g., her child).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Expertise and choice are repeatedly located with Anara ('You're in charge'), and her script rewrite is validated and used. Not a 5 because the counselor still generates most concrete options rather than drawing them out of her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation moves Anara from 'maybe doable' to weekly tracking, an authentic self-edited script, and a full role-played scheduling call. Some reliance on structured option menus rather than open evocative questions about her own reasons keeps it from a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and redirected toward manageable options without arguing. The counselor misses an affirming reframe of her 'coward's way out' self-criticism as the session ends.
