# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirmed David's boundaries and choices without judgment, even reframing potential failure warmly ('that still counts as three days fewer than before').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections captured both emotional and social dimensions, such as the social value of smoke breaks and David's shame about the cough and pattern of empty promises.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session progressively narrowed to a concrete plan (4mg gum, one smoke-free break daily, two-week follow-up), though it never lightly revisited the evening boundary as a future possibility and some practical detail discussions drifted slightly from the motivational core.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision point was offered back to David, who noted 'you didn't lecture me' — the session ran as a genuine exchange between equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly reinforced throughout ('that's entirely yours to decide'), with information offered without pressure and the plan fully owned by the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from David's own finances, partner's worry, and past success rather than supplied by the counselor, resulting in spontaneous commitment and realistic self-knowledge.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly positioned David as the expert, and all key plan elements (one-break-a-day, telling the lads, gum choice, check-in) originated from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions drew out David's own reasons, confidence, and goals, producing strong client-generated commitment ('maybe I'd start with just one break a day') and even spontaneous openness about evenings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged once as legitimate and redirected toward daytime change, but the evening boundary was re-confirmed multiple times, briefly re-focusing attention on the status quo.
