# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, including non-alarmist handling of the underage drinking disclosure. Lacks explicit affirmations of Anya's strengths, and the warm tone sometimes reads as procedural.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections, but the session is dominated by information delivery over reflective listening. Key emotional moments (feeling singled out, fear of her mom's reaction) were met with psychoeducation rather than emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus across study stress and alcohol risk, with a coherent agenda and concrete deliverables. Minor drift into general psychoeducation (binge thresholds, brain development) not tied back to her stated goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine two-way moments exist (menu choices, role-play, co-developed friend text), but the default mode is unilateral provision of counselor-generated plans. Existing client resources (code word, Marta's role) were not used to co-construct solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choice and frames plans as optional, with no pressure or moralizing. Deduction for advice frequently delivered before being requested.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is largely supplied by the counselor through expert information rather than elicited from Anya. Rarely used evocative open questions or scaling, and her change talk emerged incidentally from counselor-proposed menus rather than her own values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partially shares expertise via choices and adapting scripts at her request, but often slips into an expert-teaching stance with unrequested information dumps. Anya's existing expertise (her group's drink-watching practices) went unacknowledged in the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Commitment language did emerge ('I can see myself doing,' 'I'll write it down tonight') via the plan offer and role-play, but it was partly counselor-generated and never deepened or summarized. No open questions eliciting her own reasons for caution or confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk was repeatedly engaged and amplified with risk lectures rather than reframed toward change. When Anya anticipated being told 'it's all bad,' the counselor delivered exactly that instead of exploring ambivalence or using double-sided reflection.
