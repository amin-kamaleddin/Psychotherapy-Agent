# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard; Jordan's skepticism is met without defensiveness, and his hesitant engagement is affirmed throughout, including during the safety check.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and summaries captured Jordan's content and emotion, though reflections were sometimes brief or folded into advice. Deeper emotional themes (loneliness, the pain of fifty rejections) could have been reflected more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus from start to finish, yielding a concrete if-then plan with backup strategies, self-ratings, and follow-up. Each turn advanced the plan while staying tied to Jordan's stated goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way interaction: the counselor asked before advising, co-constructed the plan around Jordan's parameters, and had Jordan generate his own coping statement. Jordan was treated as a capable partner throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was strongly reinforced — options were offered rather than prescribed, Jordan's limits were respected, and his ownership was consistently affirmed. Jordan's growing agency ('First time in a while I've left somewhere with something') reflects this.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilfully drew motivation from within via confidence rulers, barrier questions, and eliciting Jordan's own change reasoning. Fell short of 5 as the counselor sometimes supplied techniques and rescue scripts rather than evoking them, and probed less into Jordan's deeper values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jordan was repeatedly positioned as the expert, and his own ideas (phone barrier, 10-minute listings scroll) genuinely shaped the plan. Fell just short of 5 because early option menus somewhat pre-structured the agenda instead of openly inviting Jordan's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and reinforced — confidence ratings moved Jordan from implied zero to 6.5, and he generated his own reasons and self-compassion line. Jordan progressed from vague wishing to concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged without reinforcement and often reframed into small, reversible steps. However, extensive backup plans slightly reduced strategic focus on resolving ambivalence, and no explicit double-sided reflections were used.
