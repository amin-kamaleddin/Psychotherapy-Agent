# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with individualized affirmations like 'That takes real courage' and normalization of ambivalence without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections captured both surface content and deeper meaning (e.g., 'asking feels like failing, and handing over a feed feels like losing your place with her'), repeatedly confirmed by the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus moving from the presenting concern to a concrete, time-bound plan, with every response advancing the agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with joint language and co-constructed decisions; the plan emerged from the client's own ideas rather than being prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership through genuine choice points, permission before offering information, and explicit attribution of hope to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within — the client generated her own reasons, plan, and commitment, which the counselor explicitly named: 'it came from you, not me.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client was treated as the expert on her own life, and her choices (survival plan first, pumping before bed) substantially shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions skillfully evoked desire and commitment language ('I'll ask him tonight before I talk myself out of it'), which was reinforced and consolidated into a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged empathically without reinforcement and redirected effectively, though it was sometimes reflected at length before pivoting; briefer double-sided reflections would tighten the session.
