# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental handling of an irritable client with good validation of frustration and affirmation of directness, but warmth felt somewhat formulaic and the vulnerable stress-eating disclosure went unaffirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of the core barrier (nonstop billable-minute day) and ambivalence, but empathy stayed practical and checklist-like, missing the emotional meaning of stress eating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus throughout, steadily moving from vague ambivalence to a concrete plan with confidence scaling, a backup plan, and a structured follow-up with agreed success metrics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with genuine client input shaping the plan, but the counselor tended to propose frameworks and solutions rather than eliciting ideas from the client first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, explicit support of choice: permission-seeking, menus of options, client-selected follow-up format and agenda, and framing potential failure as information to troubleshoot rather than defeat.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Confidence scaling and open questions elicited real change talk, but the counselor frequently supplied solutions rather than evoking them and never explored the client's significant stress-eating 'pressure valve' disclosure.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing — Andrei chose behaviors, reminders, and the follow-up structure — but collaboration was somewhat menu-driven, with the counselor proposing options rather than drawing ideas from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out and built on change talk (commitments, confidence scaling, 'what would make it more likely'), though could have amplified commitment statements more deliberately before problem-solving.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used double-sided reflections well and never argued with time objections, but occasionally accepted sustain talk neutrally without active reframing, and rapid solution-offering sometimes cut ambivalence exploration short.
