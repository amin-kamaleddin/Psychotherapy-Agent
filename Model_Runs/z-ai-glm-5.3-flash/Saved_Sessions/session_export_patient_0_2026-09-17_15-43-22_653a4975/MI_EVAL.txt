# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with no labeling or disapproval; however, affirmations were brief and generic, lacking individualized warmth or explicit normalization of shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured mixed feelings and worries, though reflections were somewhat formulaic and deeper emotional exploration of fear about the infection and social pressure was limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on meth use and health risks with logical progression to a concrete commitment, though the session narrowed to harm-reduction logistics rather than exploring deeper ambivalence about use overall.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and genuine incorporation of the patient's own ideas produced a co-constructed plan, but the counselor drove the menu of options and protocol-driven questions reduced fluid sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong explicit autonomy support with choices offered and refusals accepted, though rapid-fire option lists and leading questions pushed gently toward the counselor's preferred agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early evocation drew out the patient's own concerns, but the counselor increasingly supplied content rather than eliciting it, and the confidence ruler wasn't followed with deep exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some permission-seeking and patient input shaped the plan, but the counselor repeatedly shifted into expert-advice mode with pre-selected menus rather than genuinely co-creating options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effective evocation produced commitments, but change talk tapered into practical logistics and was never deepened or reinforced with reflections of the patient's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was strategically acknowledged without dwelling, though double-sided reflections to actively resolve ambivalence were not used.
