# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates consistent respect and warmth, normalizing anxiety and validating Amina's frustration about childcare. Falls short of 5 because affirmations are brief and generic, missing chances to affirm deeper strengths like her selectivity about jobs.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some accurate reflections, but reflective listening is thin relative to the amount of advice given. Emotionally loaded statements (fear of seeming desperate, 'scary in a good way') were pivoted to task-oriented suggestions rather than explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains exceptionally clear focus on overcoming childcare barriers to job-seeking throughout, with every exchange advancing a concrete thread. Closes loops consistently with confirmed next steps and no drift from the employment goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans are genuinely built from Amina's preferences and edits shape the final output. Falls short of 5 because the counselor initiates most agenda items and the overall direction, with Amina shaping details rather than direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames choices as Amina's and respects her decisions when she declines suggestions. Some items (numbered menus, day-by-day plans) are presented with an implicit preferred answer that steers her toward the counselor's structure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicits meaningful motivation (her standards, confidence, and rationale for timing follow-up). However, heavy reliance on providing solutions, scripts, and schedules means much of the session's content originates from the counselor rather than being drawn out of Amina.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adopts Amina's contributions and builds on her stated priorities, but frequently slips into expert-prescriptive mode with numbered lists and pre-written scripts. When Amina asks for guidance, the counselor supplies solutions rather than drawing out her own knowledge.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively structures questions so Amina generates her own change talk, including desire, ability, and high confidence, and evokes her report of completed action. Falls short of 5 by not deliberately deepening or reflecting this change talk back to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and redirects toward action, which is adequate but often abrupt. The 'spiral' comment deserved empathic normalization and a strengths-based reframe rather than a quick jump back to logistics.
