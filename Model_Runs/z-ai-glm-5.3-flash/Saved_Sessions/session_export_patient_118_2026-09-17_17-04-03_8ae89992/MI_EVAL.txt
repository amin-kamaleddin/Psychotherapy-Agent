# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance, repeatedly honoring resistance and affirming autonomy. Occasional procedural warmth; individualized affirmations were rare.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of frustration, weariness, and the 'warning light' metaphor, but often verbatim rather than deepening emotional meaning. Missed chances to explore his father's tooth loss and weariness with being lectured.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on the dental target and tied smoking to oral health outcomes, ending each exchange with a concrete next step. Multi-part stacked questions and lengthy information blocks slightly diluted momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked permission, offered options, and co-constructed next steps with Seamus's ideas shaping the session. Some information-heavy lectures reduced back-and-forth, and agendas were sometimes set before checking with him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: quitting framed as optional, choices offered at every step, and the smoking agenda handed entirely to Seamus, which directly produced his commitment to raise it himself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Seamus's own values (keeping teeth vs. dentures), eliciting genuine change talk. Elicited motivation was not actively deepened through confidence or readiness exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed expertise resided with Seamus via choice menus and deference to his agenda. Information delivery was sometimes dense and expert-flavored, with less acknowledgment of his own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully linked quitting to Seamus's stated goal of keeping his teeth, producing unprompted change talk ('lands a bit closer to home'). Change talk was not deepened with strategic follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently avoided arguing or lecturing, acknowledging and pivoting when Seamus deflected the smoking topic. His resistance visibly softened across the session.
