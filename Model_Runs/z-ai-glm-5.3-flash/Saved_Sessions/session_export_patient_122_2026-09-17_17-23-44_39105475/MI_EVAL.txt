# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, including honoring Sofia's request to normalize setbacks rather than offer praise. Phrases like 'I'll hold you to kindness, not perfection' demonstrate deep, nonjudgmental acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects Sofia's core concerns and key insights, with the tailored 'it still counts' phrase showing she was truly heard. Reflections are occasionally brief, and opportunities to explore the emotional weight of past diet failures and self-criticism more deeply were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, unwavering focus on the target behavior and progressively guides toward a specific, concrete action without topic drift. Each turn builds logically from general desire to a well-defined plan and follow-up structure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan emerges almost entirely from Sofia's own ideas, with the counselor offering structure and negotiated decisions ('Deal?'). Her closing statement that this feels more doable than past expert-driven approaches directly confirms the collaborative dynamic succeeded.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sofia chooses nearly every element of the plan, and the counselor explicitly commits to offering problem-solving only if wanted. Directive or persuasive language is entirely absent, powerfully reinforcing self-directed change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence scaling questions effectively drew out Sofia's own reasons and solutions, and the counselor never lectures about exercise benefits. Frequent close-ended planning questions sometimes extract logistics rather than deeper motivational meaning, leaving room to explore why feeling lighter and more energetic matters to her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is consistently treated as the expert on her own life, and her contributions directly shape the plan, including respecting her declining the text-message idea. Some question sequences are delivered as rapid-fire structured checklists, slightly constraining her space to direct the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully converts Sofia's initial change talk into concrete, client-generated commitments, with confidence scaling eliciting her own shift from 5 to 7. Her closing statements — 'this feels more doable than anything I've tried before' and 'I can do this' — demonstrate strong self-generated change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirects sustain talk without amplifying it, prompting Sofia's own reframe of the all-or-nothing pattern and her client-generated insight about why nothing stuck before. Could have more explicitly affirmed the legitimacy of her past failures before moving to solutions.
