# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Marcus with no judgment or lecturing, even when he disclosed drinking tactics. Addressed his need for respect by honoring his fear of labels and promising no lectures or calls home.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections frequently captured meaning, including double-sided reflections and the subtle 'small but' in minimizations. Falls short of a 5 because emotional exploration stayed somewhat cognitive rather than sitting with deeper feelings like shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use at parties throughout, with a coherent arc from exploring patterns to consolidating a plan. Related concerns were always tethered back to the target behavior without drift or lecturing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative exchange where Marcus's ideas were incorporated and built upon rather than corrected. The safety boundary was negotiated responsively, and the patient noted the session 'wasn't as bad as I thought.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every plan element originated from Marcus, with explicit reinforcement of his ownership ('on your terms'). The counselor even supported his request for a fallback option rather than demanding commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew out Marcus's own motivations rather than supplying reasons, yielding self-generated insights about Danny's opinion and pre-deciding his answer. Produced commitment-adjacent language from a precontemplative adolescent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Marcus as the expert ('you know your life better than I do'), and all session outcomes came from his contributions. No agenda was imposed despite the power differential with a teen.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: strategic, values-based questions led Marcus to generate all key change talk himself, from 'knowing my limit' to a concrete plan to talk to Danny. Each instance was affirmed and deepened with follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing with sustain talk and used double-sided reflections to pair it with change content. Not a 5 because sustain talk was occasionally re-voiced without an immediate pivot and some minimizations stood unexplored.
