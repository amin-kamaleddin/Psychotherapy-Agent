# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with specific, individualized affirmations. Past failures were reframed without judgment, and no disapproval or conditional regard appeared.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, complex reflections that captured both surface content and emotional core, including the salsa jar moment and subtle 'like before' reference. Metaphors were mirrored and consistently confirmed accurate by the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on increasing physical activity with progressive momentum from goal identification to setback planning and follow-up review. When nutrition surfaced, the counselor supported the client's own sequencing without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power was genuinely shared — the client's 'domino' metaphor and both-answers suggestion directly shaped the session and follow-up plan. The counselor consistently credited the plan as hers.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was reinforced at every step, including offering options for the next review and explicitly removing pressure ('whether it's three walks or one'). Nothing was prescribed or imposed; ownership was emphasized throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation was elicited from within the client through strategic questions about success criteria, past attempts, and hopes for her notes. No unsolicited advice was given, and the client's closing hopefulness confirmed the motivation was genuinely hers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located within the client ('You know yourself best here'), and every plan element originated from her. The counselor positioned himself as a curious companion rather than an expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoked desire, ability, and commitment language ('three walks that happen without negotiating with myself,' 'walking tonight'). Asking the client to define success and reflect on her future self amplified change talk effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled masterfully — nutrition hesitancy and past failures were acknowledged and reframed rather than reinforced. The harsh inner voice was guided toward a self-compassionate response generated by the client herself.
