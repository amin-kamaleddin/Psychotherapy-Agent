# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, validating even resistant or irritable statements without disapproval. No traces of judgment or labeling appear anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate, capturing both content and feeling, with complex reflections that consistently landed and paved the way for deeper disclosures. The reflections demonstrated strong emotional attunement to Jonathan's ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, progressive focus on diabetes self-management with no topic drift, strategically building toward one concrete target behavior. Each exchange advanced momentum from broad overwhelm to a specific, achievable commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was fully collaborative with no direct advice; all ideas were co-constructed and the entire action plan originated from Jonathan. The closing framing 'let me ask as a partner, not someone with a list' crystallized the equal footing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was actively reinforced at every juncture ('take it or leave it'), with external pressure from wife and doctor explicitly set aside. Jonathan's closing words — 'That's mine' — confirm the internalized ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited almost entirely from within the client, most notably producing his self-owned reason ('feeling less like garbage by mid-afternoon — that's for me'). Questions about future vision and slip recovery drew out Jonathan's own reasoning throughout.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positioned Jonathan as the expert from the opening line and maintained this stance throughout. Every strategy in the session came from Jonathan himself, demonstrating genuine belief in client wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully and progressively evoked — from a first tentative idea, through readiness scaling and autonomous motivation, to a vivid future projection. Jonathan's change talk escalated from vague to committed and owned ('That's mine').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and non-reinforced, then redirected via skillful double-sided reflections that honored ambivalence while pivoting toward change. Forgetting was reframed as realistic planning, and sustain talk was transformed into resilience planning.
