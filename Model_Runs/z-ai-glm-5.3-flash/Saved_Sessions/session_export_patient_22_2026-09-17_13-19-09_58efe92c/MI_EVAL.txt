# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and respectful tone with validation of her peer-pressure worry, but warmth is shallow and key strengths (e.g., her insight about wanting someone to like 'actual me') go unaffirmed; the safety script feels mechanical.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; vulnerable moments (feeling 'weirdly more possible,' needing a drink to loosen up on dates) were met with tip lists rather than reflections, and her ambivalence and social fears were never explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus throughout: every exchange ties back to reducing alcohol use, and the session ends with a specific plan and a Monday check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-created — she chose the days, set her own limit, and reworded the group text to fit her style — but frequent unsolicited tip lists create an advice-heavy power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices, respects her preference for reduction over abstinence, and frames slips as 'info, not failure,' though advice is sometimes given before permission and ownership of her capacity to change is underlined only at the very end.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is supplied via tips rather than drawn out — almost no open questions about her reasons or values, and her self-generated insights were met with logistics instead of amplification.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sought her input on plan elements ('Which days feel doable?', 'Want any of these more casual or firm?'), but frequently assumed the expert role with prescriptive lists rather than asking what she already knew or wanted.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Built on her spontaneous change talk to elicit concrete commitments (Mon/Wed alcohol-free, three-drink limit), but never deepened it with evocative questions about importance, confidence, or her reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk and respected her choice not to stop altogether, but missed chances to use double-sided reflections to skillfully reframe her ambivalence.
