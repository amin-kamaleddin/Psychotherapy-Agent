# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently honors Marco's autonomy, explicitly stating 'No promises needed' and 'You're leading this.' His resistance to pressure is acknowledged without judgment or confrontation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are deep and accurate, capturing ambivalence ('caught between the damage is done and you're here anyway') and identity themes ('smoking woven into who you are'). The clinician also validates emotional disclosures like the partner's anticipated tears with warmth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician skillfully steers toward change talk by highlighting 'taste your food' and the shared meals, anchoring the conversation to Marco's own values without imposing an agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is clearly collaborative, with repeated invitations ('from where you stand'), and offers tailored to Marco's explicit requests — facts, peer stories, and respecting his terms about talking with his partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marco's pacing and decisions are consistently reinforced ('Not next week... but you're the only one who knows when it's real'). His autonomy is explicitly emphasized ('You're leading this, and that's exactly how it should be').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician draws out Marco's own motivations rather than supplying them — from taste, to the table, to his partner's hopes. Marco's 'stronger, somehow' insight emerges entirely from evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician frames the work as a partnership on Marco's terms ('offering that partnership on your terms matters'). Marco himself notes 'you're not nagging, I'll give you that,' reflecting a strong working alliance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is nurtured carefully and sequentially — from small observations, to curiosity, to open questions, to the partner conversation. Each response builds naturally on Marco's own stated possibilities.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('why fix what isn't broken,' 'not tossing the pack tomorrow') is met with acceptance and gently contrasted with change talk rather than argued against. One instance of a slightly leading reframing ('I caught something small') works well but approaches steering.
