# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with affirmations like 'Liking the gym is a real strength' and 'This is brave and wise.' Never labeled the dieting history as failure, and honored her continued weight-loss desire as understandable ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured both content and emotion ('I hear how exhausting the diet cycle has been'). However, deeper emotional material—the scale as 'report card,' the critical parking-lot voice—was reflected at summary level rather than explored or named emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on replacing unsustainable dieting with enjoyable movement and self-trust, with every exchange funneling toward a concrete, collaboratively defined action. No topic drift; the plan was tied back to Yasmin's own values and well summarized at closure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal footing: the plan was co-constructed and every client modification incorporated (casual text wording, gym over walk, pep note choice). Yasmin's remark that she expected to 'fight you' over another diet but 'this feels different' directly evidences the collaborative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy actively reinforced through choices rather than directives, and her pushback against receiving another program ('picking what's fun is supposed to be the point') was respected without resistance. The closing plan was built entirely from the client's own words and preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions reliably drew out client motivation, and the plan's content (enjoyment, sister connection, self-trust) came from Yasmin herself. Occasionally counselor-supplied content (drafted text, slip plan, breathing reset) preceded eliciting the client's own ideas first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Yasmin was positioned as the expert, with choices offered and her refusal of a structured gym plan respected; her contributions substantially shaped the plan. Falls short of 5 because example plans and drafts occasionally led the agenda slightly ahead of her own articulation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and strategic confidence scaling effectively evoked strong change talk ('I want something more sustainable,' 'Thursday, I'm going anyway. Nerves and all.'). However, emerging change talk like 'maybe I'd believe this time is different' was not deepened before moving to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (the scale as report card, 'this won't work for you either') was consistently acknowledged neutrally without reinforcement, with emphasis kept on client-generated reasons for change. The persistent weight-loss longing was respectfully left unaddressed rather than actively reframed.
