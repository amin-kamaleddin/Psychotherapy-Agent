# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard is communicated consistently, with specific, individualized affirmations and no judgment anywhere in the session. The psychiatrist actively defuses shame with the reframe 'That's not boring—that's protecting things that matter to you.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and feeling, and consistently framing questions with reflections. The closing summary skillfully captures Mei's internal arc from self-doubt to building support.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus and progressive momentum on reducing alcohol use, advancing through exploration, strategy, and commitment. However, the second target—contraceptive reliability—is linked early but silently dropped from the final plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction functions as a genuine partnership of equals, with each turn built directly on Mei's words and the final plan assembled entirely from her material. The psychiatrist explicitly defers to her relational expertise ('You know your friendships better than anyone').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Control is explicitly handed to Mei from the opening line and never reclaimed, with no persuasive or directive language at any point. Every strategy question frames the decision as hers, and her self-generated contingency plan is accepted without modification.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No reasons for change are supplied by the clinician; all motivation, insights, and strategies are elicited through skillful open questions. Commitment is evoked through graduated questions culminating in Mei's self-generated concrete plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located within the client, and every solution in the final plan originated from Mei. The psychiatrist offers zero advice across the session, and Mei's contributions demonstrably shape the session's direction and outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is systematically evoked across desire, ability, reasons, and commitment, with every psychiatrist turn containing an evocative open question. Change talk is reinforced when it appears and mobilized into a concrete, client-owned plan. A minor opportunity to re-scale the confidence ruler explicitly was missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled with textbook technique: acknowledged briefly via double-sided reflections, then pivoted toward change. It is never reinforced or argued with, and the session's language steadily shifts from doubt toward agency.
