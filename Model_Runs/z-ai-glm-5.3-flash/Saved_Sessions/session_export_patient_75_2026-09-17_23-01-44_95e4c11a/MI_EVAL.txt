# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with Linda's fears and self-directed research treated as legitimate. Affirmations were occasionally formulaic and unconditional regard was not fully extended to moments of doubt or inconsistency.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful reflections, including accurate double-sided reflections capturing Linda's ambivalence. Emotional core—fear of dependence at 32, shame about inconsistency, dread of dismissal—was acknowledged but not deeply explored at the feeling level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on asthma management and inhaler adherence from open to close, with each exchange building toward a concrete client-generated plan. Summaries consolidated progress with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way interaction with every plan element originating from Linda and built upon by the counselor. Repeated check-ins ('what do you think?') ensured her ideas drove the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership consistently reinforced, and the counselor declined the expert role rather than giving a generic medical answer. No persuasive or coercive language appeared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client via open questions that elicited her own reasons, confidence, and commitment language. Linda's key insights were evoked rather than supplied.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located in the client ('You know your routine better than anyone'), and her contributions demonstrably shaped the plan. The counselor asked rather than told and deferred medical questions to a collaborative process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions evoked and reinforced abundant change talk, from 'I can do that tonight' to articulating her own reasons for getting real answers. Mirrored back her motivations and plans effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about side effects and dependence was acknowledged and reframed with effective double-sided reflections and redirection. Some instances were met with neutral acknowledgment rather than active strategic softening.
