# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental opening and consistent affirmations ('Thank you for trusting me,' 'Nice thinking'). Affirmations were somewhat generic and could have connected more to Lukas's identity as a disciplined honors student and athlete.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately captured core ambivalence and peer-pressure anxiety with concise reflections. At times reflections compressed emotional content — the identity-level worry ('whether that's... me') was acknowledged but never explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and progressed smoothly through exploration, values, planning, scaling, and rehearsal. Early reflections occasionally circled back to ambivalence, and a mid-session summary could have consolidated direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed session: Lukas chose the fake-drink strategy, crafted the refusal line, set role-play parameters, and self-evaluated. The counselor acted as coach and practice partner rather than authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and asked permission ('What would you like to change?', 'Want to try rehearsing?'). Deducted for drifting toward suggesting when offering strategy menus and sample lines rather than purely eliciting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from within via values questions, confidence scaling, and role-play, letting Lukas generate his own insights ('I don't have to win the argument, I just have to not crumble'). Confidence rose from 5 to 8 through his own work.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned Lukas as the expert by asking which strategies felt most doable and letting his ideas shape the plan. Twice offered pre-packaged option menus before eliciting his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent use of open questions and confidence scaling, moving Lukas from a 5 to 'an eight now, for real.' Change talk was clearly strengthened and tracked across the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk ('not like I'm an alcoholic') and used double-sided reflections honoring both sides. Occasionally restated sustain talk in summaries without an explicit pivot toward change language.
