# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respect is maintained but warmth is thin — no affirmations of Lars's honesty or courage, and his admission of drinking and hiding Fridays from Donna gets met with logistics rather than normalization or positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent — responses are dominated by advice and bullet points. Lars's emotional core (shame about deceiving Donna, fear of ridicule, boredom) is repeatedly skipped over.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains tight, logical focus on the highest-risk behavior — Friday nights, drinking, and mates — moving from trigger identification to rehearsal to a support plan. Loses a point for being narrowly behavioral and not connecting the plan to Lars's broader values and goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is uneven: the counselor generates problems, solutions, scripts, and texts while Lars selects and complies. Lars's own ideas are absorbed into checklists rather than genuinely built upon, with no joint agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some choice within counselor-generated menus, but the overall stance is controlling — Lars must execute a dictated plan rather than own the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Solutions and motivations are supplied prescriptively rather than drawn out; there are no open questions about Lars's reasons for change. His motivation surfaces despite the counselor's style, not because of it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumes the expert role, delivering prescriptive lists in nearly every turn and ending by dictating tasks. Lars's expertise on his mates and street is never acknowledged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lars offers meaningful change talk himself (avoiding prison, staying out of trouble with Donna), but the counselor does nothing to evoke or deepen it, and even skips the strongest moment ('Friday feels less scary') to jump to a checklist.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lars's sustain talk (boredom, fear of mickey-taking, worry the plan won't work) receives no double-sided reflections, reframes, or strategic pivots — it is mostly ignored rather than skillfully softened.
