# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; Elena explicitly noted she expected a lecture but felt heard instead. Affirmations were genuine and never judgmental.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were generally accurate and helped Elena feel understood, but were sometimes brief and formulaic, with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotional content like her shame or fear of conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on vaccine decision-making and preparing for the pediatrician visit, with a logical session progression. Occasional drift into listing resources without tying each step back to Elena's stated goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena's contributions visibly shaped the session, including the role-play scenario incorporating her sister and defensiveness. The informational middle section was somewhat counselor-directed rather than negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear strength: choices were offered at nearly every juncture, no pressure toward vaccination, and her pacing request to take 'one vaccine at a time' was honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good evocative opening questions elicited Elena's core values, but the session then shifted to information provision. Missed chances to deepen motivation, e.g., when Elena spoke about protecting her pregnant sister.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-asking supported partnership, but the middle session was heavy on information delivery, positioning the counselor as the expert. The script and role-play were counselor-proposed rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena produced meaningful change talk, but much emerged from information provision rather than deliberate evocation. Commitment statements like 'maybe I can actually do this' were not amplified or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without arguing and gently reframed, but there were no double-sided reflections. Elena's key doubt ('I had chickenpox and I'm fine') was answered with facts rather than reflective softening.
