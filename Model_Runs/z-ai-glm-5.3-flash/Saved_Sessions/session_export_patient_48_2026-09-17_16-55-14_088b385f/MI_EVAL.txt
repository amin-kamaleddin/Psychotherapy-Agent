# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with regular affirmations ('Nice work, Farid') and acceptance of Farid's self-defined success threshold. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and repeated nearly verbatim, limiting depth of individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening that captures both content and emotional undertones, e.g., normalizing fear of failure. Reflections lean practical rather than emotional — the discouragement and shame behind 'it fizzled out' went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on physical activity with a logical progression to a fully specified behavioral plan. Turns are dense with stacked questions (up to three at once), and the 'energy' focus was quietly folded back into exercise without clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly frames the session as joint work ('What would feel most helpful for us to focus on together?') and the plan was genuinely co-constructed. The counselor still supplies most specific tactics (timer, shoes, stretch names), keeping interactions from being fully equal-partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are consistently framed as Farid's, and his plan revisions and success definition are accepted without correction. Offering paired pre-selected options and repeatedly re-asking for commitments edges toward steering rather than pure ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and open questions effectively elicit Farid's own motivation, with confidence rising from 5–6 to 8. Missed a prime evocation moment — Farid's 'kind of not dreading it' remark went unexplored, so intrinsic motivation was never deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Expertise is clearly located with Farid, whose contributions (frequency, cue, fallback) substantially shaped the plan. Pre-formatted solution menus and stacked suggestions subtly prescribe rather than fully eliciting Farid's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence scaling repeatedly asked produced progressively stronger commitment language (5–6 → 7 → 8) and 'I'll give it a real shot.' Rarely amplifies change talk when it emerges — 'kind of not dreading it' received no follow-up — making evocation somewhat procedural.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently reframed ('not laziness, just a drained schedule') and redirected into barrier planning. Reframes are brief and lack double-sided reflections to leverage ambivalence.
