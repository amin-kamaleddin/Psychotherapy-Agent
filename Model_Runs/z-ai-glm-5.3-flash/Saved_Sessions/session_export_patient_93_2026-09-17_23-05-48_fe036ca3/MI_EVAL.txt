# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, with no judgment even when Margaret minimizes or resists. Affirmations like 'That distinction is wise, not silly' celebrate her self-generated insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening, with complex reflections capturing both content and meaning ('more boredom than need... while at the pub you'd feel everyone's watching'). Emotional nuances like 'pride and peculiarity' are also skillfully reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong strategic momentum toward a concrete, client-owned plan with defined success criteria and follow-up. Slight deduction because the broader referral concerns (the fall, overall intake) are not revisited in the final plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as a genuine partnership of equals, with all decisions emerging from Margaret's own reasoning and success criteria co-constructed through questions. Power-sharing is complete throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy repeatedly reinforced ('that's yours to change on your own terms,' 'Sundays are yours to decide') with choices offered rather than goals imposed. Margaret's refusal to commit to pub changes is explicitly honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn almost entirely from within — Margaret generates her own reasons, strategies, and coping tools without any lecturing. She closes feeling 'more set up for it having talked it through.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located with the client ('You're the expert on your own life'), and every substantive idea originates from Margaret. The counselor's questions draw out her solutions rather than offering advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and amplified, evolving from ambivalence to concrete commitment ('one empty teacup beside me and nothing opened'). The counselor quotes and reinforces her motivation language while eliciting desire, ability, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged and pivoted away from without argument, using double-sided reflections ('I hear two things, and both matter'). Pub attachments are validated as legitimate while attention returns to changeable behavior.
