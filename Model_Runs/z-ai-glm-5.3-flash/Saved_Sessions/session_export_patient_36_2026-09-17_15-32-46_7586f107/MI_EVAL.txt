# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and non-judgmental throughout, with affirmations of Elena's progress and values. However, praise remained somewhat procedural and checklist-driven rather than offering deeper, individualized positive regard for Elena as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate, but empathy stayed surface-level throughout. The client's most emotionally meaningful statement about years of people-pleasing was glossed over in favor of moving to logistics like reminder times and check-in formats.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on Elena's stated goal with no topic drift, moving efficiently from exploration to a concrete plan. A 5 was withheld because direction came through fast structured prompting rather than strategic consolidation of Elena's own goals and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was largely joint, with Elena's preferences directly shaping the final plan and summaries confirmed. However, the counselor proposed nearly all ideas first, leaving Elena mostly selecting from options rather than genuinely co-creating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-offering with no pressure or coercion, and Elena's decisions shaped the plan. The heavy menu-format felt more like guided choosing within counselor-defined options than open ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence ruler and questions about what helped were evocative, but most content was counselor-supplied rather than drawn from Elena. Her powerful values statement about years of self-sacrifice was not explored to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor regularly solicited Elena's input, showing some power-sharing. However, the style resembled a service provider offering menus rather than explicitly acknowledging Elena as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence ruler successfully elicited genuine commitment language, but this rich change talk was not deepened or reflected back. Few open questions targeted desire, ability, or reasons for change; much momentum came from Elena herself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena offered almost no sustain talk, and the counselor avoided fear-based framing while keeping focus on strengths and solutions. A higher score was not warranted as there was no genuine ambivalence to skillfully pivot.
