# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated warmth and unconditional positive regard with genuine, non-formulaic affirmations. Never expressed disapproval of the client's hesitation, and the client explicitly noted feeling unrushed compared to prior experiences.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflections that captured both surface content and emotional core, including sophisticated reflections of ambivalence and identity loss. The client explicitly confirmed feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with each exchange progressing the agenda toward a concrete next step. Skillfully distilled progress at the close without becoming directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance with the plan co-constructed from the client's own ideas. Occasionally elaborately polished summaries painted a picture for the client rather than letting him fully build it himself, slightly tempering pure partnership equality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support—repeatedly reinforced client ownership, accepted non-commitment without pressure, and fully de-shamed potential non-adherence ('park or no park, it all counts'). No coercive or persuasive language appeared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice was given; open questions consistently drew out the client's own reasons, abilities, and desire for change. The client's comment 'you let me talk my way into it' is the hallmark of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed that expertise resided within the client, soliciting his own strategic thinking and decision-making. The concrete step originated entirely from the client, and the closing explicitly credited him with his own progress.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk through targeted evocative questions and reflections that amplified the client's own change language. Resulted in movement from sustain talk to a self-identified first step within one session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well by normalizing ambivalence and reframing toward the positive without arguing. A few reflections restated limitations in vivid language, briefly amplifying focus on barriers before pivoting back to change.
