# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently respectful, non-judgmental tone with supportive affirmations throughout. However, affirmations are somewhat generic rather than deeply personalized, with little explicit normalization of Jamal's ambivalence or past failed attempts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections are accurate, but the counselor frequently stacks multiple questions in a single turn, crowding out space for deeper reflection. Emotional nuances like boredom at midnight and fear of quitting after one bad day are acknowledged but not deeply explored with complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains an exceptionally clear and consistent focus on the target behavior throughout, with every turn advancing the behavioral chain from problem identification through planning, troubleshooting, accountability, and setback management. There is no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed, with Jamal choosing the phone location, podcast strategy, accountability partner, and reward. Some unevenness arises from rapid multi-question turns and pre-packaged option lists that occasionally set the agenda pace rather than letting Jamal's contributions drive transitions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly affirms Jamal's freedom to choose and accepts his rejections of suggested options without pushback. A few turns rely on tightly structured option menus rather than open-ended invitations, with limited emphasis on Jamal's ownership of successes and setbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from Jamal via confidence rulers and open questions, and he articulates his own reasons for change. However, the counselor does not consistently deepen the meaning behind his motivation, leaving the intrinsic 'for me' drive underexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys that expertise resides with Jamal by soliciting his input on every plan element, and his ideas substantially shaped the plan. Some checklist-driven turns with rapid-fire option menus slightly undermine the sense that Jamal steers the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evokes change talk using the confidence ruler effectively and concrete plan-building, with Jamal's confidence rising from 6 to 8 and stronger commitment over time. Evocation leans on the same ruler technique, and opportunities to deepen desire/need talk were not pursued.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and occasionally redirects effectively, but responses are often problem-solving rather than strategic MI responses. There are no double-sided reflections or reframing, as seen when Jamal's 'plans that died by Wednesday' doubt was met with praise rather than an invitation to resolve it.
