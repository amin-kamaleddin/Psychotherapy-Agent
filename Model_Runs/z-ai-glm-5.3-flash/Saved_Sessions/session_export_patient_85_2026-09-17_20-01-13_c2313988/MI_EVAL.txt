# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, from affirming Marco's return ('tells me your health matters to you') to the non-judgmental closing ('bring me the truth, and we'll work with it'). There is no labeling, disapproval, or conditional acceptance anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and deep, with double-sided reflections capturing both Marco's efforts and his ambivalence about the juice. The counselor also reflects deeper meanings, such as framing 'I can do it again' as remembered confidence rather than hoping.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains unwavering focus on juice reduction and weight management, moving progressively from identifying the obstacle through planning to concrete first steps and a check-in. No drift off-topic occurs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work is explicitly framed as joint ('let me ask you as a partner in this,' 'let's think it through together'), and Marco's own ideas shape the final plan. Agenda negotiation is clear with genuine options offered to the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marco's ownership is consistently reinforced ('What feels like the right first step for you this week'), and the counselor never prescribes, explicitly crediting him: 'You identified the juice, you designed the steps, you set the timeline.' The closing supports self-direction even in relapse by normalizing honesty over perfection.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight and strategy originates from Marco—the obstacle, the gradual taper, the delay strategy—drawn out through questions connecting his past successes to the new goal. The counselor consistently draws motivation from within rather than supplying it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marco is explicitly positioned as the expert and doer of the work ('that's you doing the work, not me handing you answers'). When asked to be walked through hard moments, the counselor turns it back collaboratively so Marco's own experience shapes the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk, amplifying Marco's 'I can do it again' and eliciting future success markers (scale, clothes fit, habit shift). His confidence rating of 10 is deliberately highlighted and connected to his track record.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally acknowledged and redirected effectively, but the counselor briefly re-voices it prominently ('you love that orange juice'), keeping some focus on the barrier. The pivot to change is quick but could be tightened.
