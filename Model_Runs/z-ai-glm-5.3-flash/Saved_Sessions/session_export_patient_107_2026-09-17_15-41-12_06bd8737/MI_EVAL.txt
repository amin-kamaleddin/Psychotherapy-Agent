# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, but affirmations of the client were sparse and his perspective that coffee genuinely helps him function was not explicitly validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened with an accurate summary reflection, but responses shifted quickly into checklists and logistics; emotional content and the client's identity investment in coffee were never deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stayed tightly focused on coffee consumption with a concrete tracking plan and scheduled check-in, though direction emphasized data-collection logistics over the motivational core of ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as joint and negotiated the check-in schedule and logging method, but the counselor frequently supplied structure (templates, option lists) in a somewhat prescriptive way.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy language and offering of menu options rather than prescribing, though some recommendations (BP tips, symptom warnings) were delivered as instructions without checking preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked some eliciting questions that drew out curiosity, but never actively evoked or expanded motivation—the discrepancy between coffee's benefits and health concerns was summarized but not developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly treated the client as the decision-maker and honored his limits, but much of the session consisted of the counselor supplying options rather than eliciting the client's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-adjacent talk (curiosity, openness to shifting cup timing) but did not deepen it with reflections or confidence/importance questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and used neutral reframing, but rarely double-sided reflected or paired the client's status-quo statements with his own health concerns to build discrepancy.
