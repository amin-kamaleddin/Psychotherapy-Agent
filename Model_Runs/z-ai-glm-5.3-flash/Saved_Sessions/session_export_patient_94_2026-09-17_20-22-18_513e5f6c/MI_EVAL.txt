# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard, even in the face of initial defensiveness. Resistance was validated and reframed without judgment or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, emotionally attuned reflections that consistently captured both content and the fear beneath Maria's intellectualized skepticism. Maria repeatedly confirmed accuracy by elaborating and opening up.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on inhaler adherence throughout, with each question advancing the change process while remaining patient-centered. Momentum built progressively from information needs to a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook high-level collaboration—the counselor positioned Maria as the expert and co-constructed the plan entirely from her ideas. Asked rather than told, with Maria's contributions visibly shaping each exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering reinforcement of Maria's ownership, with the plan explicitly attributed to her ('That's your plan, not mine'). No pressure to commit; change was framed as her informed choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within the client—no lecturing or arguing for adherence. Maria's own reasons for change (family, exhaustion with 2 a.m. debates, fear of a bad attack) emerged and were skillfully amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Active power sharing with explicit acknowledgment of Maria's agency ('You know your schedule and body better than anyone'). The session's direction was visibly co-created from her stated needs.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions elicited progressively stronger change talk, moving Maria from pre-contemplation to a self-generated plan. Each instance of change talk was reinforced without imposing the counselor's agenda.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Double-sided reflections and validation of skepticism were used effectively to defuse sustain talk. However, some extended monologues about steroid fears and medical distrust received lengthy validation without strategic summarization or pivoting.
