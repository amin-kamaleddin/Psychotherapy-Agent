# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unconditional positive regard throughout with no judgment despite Daniel's initial minimization. Affirmations were specific and genuine, and the client explicitly thanked the counselor for not lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening, accurately reflecting surprise at the 18 drinks/week figure and ambivalence about quitting. Reflections occasionally summarized rather than fully explored emotional nuance, such as the two-year Boston pursuit and social pressure from his buddy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol in service of the marathon goal, with every turn tying back to the target behavior. The session progressed logically from exploration to evocation to a concrete commitment and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked permission before advising and co-constructed the plan from Daniel's own ideas. Some turns were multi-question and suggestion-heavy (e.g., listing multiple coping strategies at once), briefly shifting toward supplying solutions rather than fully eliciting them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Daniel's ownership with language like 'what change, if any, would you be willing to try?' and offered options as menus rather than directives. Accepted his refusal of extra homework without pushing and framed outcomes as his to discover.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from Daniel himself via questions about what mattered most, confidence, and preferred strategies, eliciting his own reasons and commitment language. The self-generated discrepancy between his health identity and drinking was his own realization, exactly what MI evocation aims for.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Daniel as the expert by asking permission, deferring to his preferences, and letting him choose his own social strategy and tracking method. The plan came almost entirely from him, which the counselor affirmed and built on rather than prescribing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully deepened change talk, moving Daniel from 'maybe skipping beers before long runs?' to a concrete plan, with confidence rising from 7 to 8. By the end, Daniel spontaneously generated further change talk about addressing weekend drinking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Quickly pivoted from minimization to reflecting health values, and effectively reframed slips as 'data, not failure.' However, some turns restated Daniel's minimization content before redirecting, which briefly amplified sustain language instead of using double-sided reflections more strategically.
