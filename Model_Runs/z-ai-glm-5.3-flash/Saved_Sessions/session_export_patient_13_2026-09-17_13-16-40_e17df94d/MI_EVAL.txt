# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently validates Anara's feelings without judgment (e.g., 'that sounds really distressing,' 'avoidance is normal') and normalizes her fear. Warmth is somewhat formulaic and brisk ('quick checklist'), slightly undercutting unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some accurate reflections ('It makes sense you'd feel scared'), but empathy is often shallow and quickly pivots to advice and task lists. Deep emotional material — her mother's health scares, shame over past cancellations — is met with reassurance rather than exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains an exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior of seeking medical care, with every exchange advancing it. No topic drift; each response ties back to reducing avoidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The doctor message was genuinely co-constructed based on Anara's feedback, and her ideas shaped the plan. However, the counselor drives most problem-solving — supplying templates, lab lists, and scripts before Anara articulates her needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Honors Anara's preferences on tone and format, but frequently prescribes specific actions with imperative language ('hit send within 20 minutes,' 'text your sister now'). The dense checklists create mild pressure toward a single predetermined path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely draws out Anara's own motivations or meanings; responses consist mainly of advice, scripts, and checklists. Key opportunities — her mother's health history, self-hatred after cancellations — were bypassed instead of explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly asks for input and incorporated Anara's contributions (softer tone, nervousness line). However, the counselor often assumes the expert role with unprompted symptom checklists, lab names, and pre-written scripts rather than soliciting her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively channels Anara's building motivation into a concrete commitment ('Wednesday. I said it.'). The momentum often came from the counselor's plan, though, rather than deliberately evoking and deepening her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not amplify sustain talk and gently counters it, but responds to her key sustain talk ('if I don't go, nothing's wrong yet') with psychoeducational rebuttal and reassurance. Lacks double-sided reflections that honor the sustain talk while pivoting toward change.
