# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, including affirming 'that's not a fluke' and respecting Isabella's 'no promises' stance without judgment. Warmth feels genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and nuanced, capturing both content and emotional undertones like ambivalence, shame, and hope. Notably precise reflections such as 'that voice offers an easier day' and 'going back feels like losing that freedom.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on community engagement, progressing logically from feeling stuck to internal dialogue to past success to a concrete self-generated step. Ends with a specific, achievable weekly commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with no unsolicited advice; solutions emerge jointly from Isabella's own history and ideas. Consistent use of 'together' framing and open questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice consistently reinforced: no hurry, honoring no promises, and endorsing the smaller step ('that's a start'). Every plan is Isabella's own with no pressure or prescription.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client: her own reasons ('less useless,' 'lighter'), her own past coping strategies, and her own solutions. Open questions effectively elicit capabilities and desires.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located within the client ('you're the one who knows those moments best'). Isabella's contributions—the fridge idea, the one-time rule—directly shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and amplified, progressing from 'yes, I guess' to concrete self-generated plans (looking it up, the fridge note). Counselor explicitly highlights Isabella's own hopeful language ('that maybe is carrying real hope in it').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but never reinforced, with effective double-sided reflections ('both of those are true right now'). Counselor consistently pivots toward the part of Isabella that wants change without arguing against reservations.
