# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgment, with validating reflections. Lacks explicit affirmations of Sofia's strengths, such as her courage in practicing despite calling it 'cringe'.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including complex reflections like 'Skipping class protects you but also feels isolating.' Deeper emotional content (being blamed since fifth grade, hallway humiliation, fear of screenshots) is acknowledged but not explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on peer conflict and avoidance, moving steadily toward a concrete plan for Monday. Some directional energy comes from the counselor's own agenda rather than continuous co-steering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine two-way exchange — Sofia shaped the plan (her own line, sitting near the friend instead of texting). Frequent advice-giving and tip lists create moments of uneven power, and agendas are often counselor-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia's choices are repeatedly reinforced and honored throughout, with explicit outs offered every time. Pre-packaged scripts and advice somewhat undercut the message that solutions should come from her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation emerged from Sofia herself, but the approach relies heavily on menus and skill-dispensing rather than evocative questions. Key explorations (why avoiding drama matters, hopes for school life, confidence for Monday) are absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's input is invited and respected, but the counselor often slips into an expert role with technique lists and drafts. Sofia's existing expertise is acknowledged but not systematically leveraged to co-create strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of choices and rehearsal generated meaningful change talk, including self-generated insight ('maybe the rumors run out of material'). Change talk could be deepened with confidence questions rather than relying on menus.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk is tolerated without reinforcement, and brief reframes appear occasionally. Double-sided reflections and strategic pivots are rare; responses often move quickly to scripts rather than softening fears.
