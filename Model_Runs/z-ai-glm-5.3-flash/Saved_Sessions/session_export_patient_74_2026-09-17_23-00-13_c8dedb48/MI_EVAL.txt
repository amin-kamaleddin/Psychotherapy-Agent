# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard communicated consistently, including welcoming Linda back unchanged and normalizing her fear of failure as 'a real concern, not a weakness.' No judgment or disapproval despite clear sustain talk.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, complex double-sided reflections captured both sides of Linda's ambivalence and subtle emotional nuances, such as the tenderness of her daughter's comment. Reflections consistently landed and Linda built on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking throughout, with questions building progressively from values to feasibility to a concrete, Linda-owned experiment. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals — Linda's own ideas (phone tally, cutting down first) shaped the session's direction, and the plan and success criteria were entirely her own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: 'you don't have to have any plans today,' 'at your pace, on your terms,' explicitly welcoming her back unchanged. Linda recognized this: 'this feels less like a fight.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn almost entirely from within the client; no advice or information supplied. Linda generated the entire plan and her own definition of success.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed that expertise resides within Linda ('What would you need to learn or know about yourself?'). Her 'not all-or-nothing' idea became the session's centerpiece — active, not token, power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked through hypotheticals, values exploration, and selective reinforcement of emergent language. Linda progressed to commitment-adjacent statements like 'I won't know until I look' and 'ask me next week if it was worth it.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, briefly acknowledging it within double-sided reflections before pivoting toward change. Early reflections occasionally restated her enjoyment of smoking at length, giving some air to sustain content, though always balanced and never reinforced.
