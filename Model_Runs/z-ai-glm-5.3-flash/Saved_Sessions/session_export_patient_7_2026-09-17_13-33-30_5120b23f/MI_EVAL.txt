# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance despite resistance, apologizing when the safety question felt off and honoring Amina's rejection of exercises. Misses opportunities to specifically affirm her strengths, such as her honesty in disclosing the fight with her mom.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers accurate emotional reflections once Amina opens up, such as naming the unfairness and exhaustion of being blamed and having private matters shared. Earlier reflections are thinner, and empathy moments are often converted back into menu options rather than sitting with the emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately prioritizes engagement and reaches the target area of family conflict, but there is no consistent thread. The session drifts through mood ratings, exercises, and silences without summaries or linking of disclosures to an emerging focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and successfully follows Amina's lead into the venting option, but the collaboration is uneven. Amina explicitly voices that constant option-choosing feels coercive, and the counselor responds with yet another menu rather than genuinely renegotiating the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A clear strength, repeatedly emphasizing choice, dropping rejected exercises, and tolerating silence or 'stop.' However, the constant three-option menus create a subtle pressure of choice rather than genuine ownership, and unstructured space was underused.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Shows little effort to elicit motivation from within Amina, with no open questions about what matters to her or how things might be different at home. Motivation-related content emerges only through venting and is not seized upon to evoke her own hopes or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids an expert stance and defers to Amina, but the relentless menu-style questions place the burden of structuring the session on a resistant teen, which she experienced as pressure. Does not genuinely draw on Amina's expertise about her own situation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is almost no deliberate evocation of change talk; questions are logistical menus rather than explorations of Amina's desires, reasons, or ability to change her situation at home. No effort is made to draw out what she might want to be different with her mom or at home.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce Amina's sustain talk about being unfairly blamed, responding with neutral validating reflections. However, no strategic softening occurs — no double-sided reflections or pivots toward what she wants different — leaving the ambivalence unaddressed.
