# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, but the counselor repeatedly recapped the plan despite Maria's clear signals to end, undermining attunement to her needs.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some decent early reflections, but reflections stayed superficial and missed deeper emotional material like distrust of the medical system and her daughter's concern; failed to recognize her escalating impatience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on inhaler adherence and produced a concrete time-bound commitment efficiently, though the plan was rushed and repetitive recaps added no directional value.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice points existed, but the plan, menu of strategies, and follow-up structure were largely clinician-supplied; Maria's own ideas (stairs test) weren't integrated collaboratively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The reversible 7-day trial and honoring Maria's conditions supported choice, but the plan's content was largely clinician-determined and her ownership wasn't explicitly affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Weakest domain: heavy reliance on information-giving and a pre-formed plan; Maria's intrinsic motivations (daughter's concern, scaling answers, curiosity about breathing) went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some inclusive language was used, but the agenda and plan were clinician-driven; Maria's expertise was acknowledged but not substantively used to shape the intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and a secured commitment were strengths, but Maria's excellent change talk (stairs test, importance of 7) went largely unaffirmed or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with curiosity and accurate safety information rather than argument, and the plan was framed as small and reversible, though fears were never explicitly validated.
