# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor was consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Sofia's frustration and affirming her coping efforts. However, warmth was somewhat procedural, with little explicit normalization of her anger or unconditional positive regard beyond the presenting problem.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both content and emotion, such as Sofia's exhaustion with being blamed and her fear about the friend. However, reflections often served as transitions to advice, and deeper feelings of hurt and isolation beneath her defensive exterior went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained excellent focus on peer conflict, school avoidance, and reconnection throughout, with no aimless drift. The session progressed coherently from venting to a specific, client-endorsed action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was largely a joint effort, with permission-seeking and Sofia's edits shaping the final plan. However, the pattern was often counselor-proposes-options and Sofia-picks, limiting genuinely mutual brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered choices and respected Sofia's autonomy, including her refusal to practice aloud and her script edits. However, frequent advice-giving and option menus shifted the session slightly toward expert problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited, including Sofia's desire for the drama to stop and her own plan phrasing. However, the counselor relied heavily on supplying solutions rather than drawing out Sofia's own reasons, confidence, and ideas, missing key evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia's contributions—her script edits, headphones idea, and neutral line—became the core of the plan, showing her expertise shaped the session. Still, the counselor often supplied the menu of options first rather than eliciting Sofia's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively captured and built on Sofia's natural change talk, leading to meaningful commitment language like 'I could actually send it' and 'I'll try it.' Missed opportunities to deepen this with questions about confidence and importance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's blame narrative and school-avoidance threats were occasionally acknowledged but never strategically softened with double-sided reflections or linked to her own goals. Her externalizing of blame remained intact at session end.
