# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, validating Elias's embarrassment and honoring his self-imposed limits without pressure. Closed by respecting his pacing and deferring the leader message to a later session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurately reflected nuanced mixed feelings, including grief and bittersweet joy, and captured meaning behind his words. Elias's own remarks ('Nobody's really asked me that before,' 'You just listened') confirm deep empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every exchange moved consistently toward rejoining the group, from barrier exploration to rehearsal and concrete first steps. Session ended with a specific, actionable plan (one song, Marta, exit seat).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a partner by offering choice menus and following Elias's preferences, including his chosen 'drummer' line. However, many scripts and plans were counselor-supplied rather than co-created from Elias's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed choices as Elias's own ('Which would you like to try?') and honored his boundaries without pushback. Elias explicitly noted he expected a lecture and didn't get one, confirming the autonomy-supportive tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited meaningful change talk by exploring values around music and connection. But specific solutions and scripts were often supplied rather than evoked, and confidence/importance was never explicitly assessed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias's contributions genuinely shaped the session, with his preferences adopted for the 'drummer' line and practicing with Marta. Falls short of 5 because several complete scripts and logistics plans were presented rather than co-developed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully amplified Elias's change talk, leading to self-motivational statements like rehearsing the 'seated drummer' line and 'Maybe it's time they remembered how.' Momentum sometimes came from counselor-supplied scripts rather than Elias's own reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk, reframing 'what's the point' and 'I'd feel like a decoration' toward strengths and small steps. However, relied more on offering solutions than double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
