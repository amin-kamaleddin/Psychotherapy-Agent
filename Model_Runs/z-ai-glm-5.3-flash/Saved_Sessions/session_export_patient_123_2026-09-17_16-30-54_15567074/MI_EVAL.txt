# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, affirming Sofia's strengths and thanking her for honesty. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and Sofia's early reluctance about being 'sent' by the screening is only briefly acknowledged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but stay at surface level, summarizing facts rather than feelings. Sofia's mild defensiveness about the screening and her 'escape' comment go unexplored, leaving understanding factual rather than emotionally attuned.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor keeps questions tied to the alcohol context, but the session's first half wanders through generic background fact-gathering, leaving Sofia confused about the screening's purpose. The session ends with generic tips rather than a focused behavioral goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing exists (asking permission, offering to wrap up), but the agenda is counselor-driven with stacked questions and expert-style tip delivery. Sofia's own ideas, like the code text, are not credited or built upon as shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor regularly offers choice, asks permission, and affirms Sofia's existing strategies rather than imposing change. The didactic tip delivery and steering toward brief intervention content keep autonomy acknowledged but not deeply reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and expertise are rarely drawn from the client; the counselor lists protective factors and prescribes refusal scripts and the code-text strategy instead of asking what has helped Sofia so far. Evocative questions are posed but never followed up.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional permission-seeking and topic choice reflect some power sharing, but the session is largely counselor-driven with rapid-fire questions and prescriptive tip-giving. Sofia's contributions are not woven back in as her own resource.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Efforts to evoke change talk are minimal, with almost no questions about Sofia's own motivations or values. Nascent self-protective language ('I usually just don't', 'I might tell my friends about that') is not reflected or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk is mild and the counselor handles it neutrally, neither reinforcing nor actively redirecting it. Mirroring sustain-leaning statements without a double-sided reflection or pivot toward her protective motivations misses an opportunity for strategic softening.
