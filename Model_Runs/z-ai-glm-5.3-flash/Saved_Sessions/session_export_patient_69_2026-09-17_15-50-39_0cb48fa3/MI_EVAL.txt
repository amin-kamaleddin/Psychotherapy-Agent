# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Kieran's frustration throughout. However, affirmations were limited and somewhat generic; more individualized recognition of his honesty and resilience would raise this to a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Early reflections of frustration and weariness were accurate and helped Kieran open up. Deeper emotional moments like 'nobody's asked' and 'Weird actually caring' went unexplored, limiting emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on a concrete target moved the session from apathy to a behavioral commitment within a short timeframe. Early menu options were somewhat scattered before the focus crystallized.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kieran's choices shaped the session content and plans were co-negotiated. However, rapid provision of scripts and example lines tilted toward an expert, advice-giving stance at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered optional menus and explicit choice at every turn, accepting Kieran's resistance without pushback. Exemplary autonomy support throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some preferences and hopes were elicited, but much of the session's momentum came from counselor-supplied scripts and solutions. Rarely drew out Kieran's own reasons, vision, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Kieran as the decision-maker through preference-based questions. Moving quickly into advice and script-writing slightly undercut the message that expertise resides in Kieran.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully converted sustain talk into change talk, culminating in commitment language like 'I'll send it.' More systematic evocation of Kieran's own reasons and confidence would reach a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged neutrally but not strategically engaged. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections pairing hopelessness with Kieran's emerging willingness.
