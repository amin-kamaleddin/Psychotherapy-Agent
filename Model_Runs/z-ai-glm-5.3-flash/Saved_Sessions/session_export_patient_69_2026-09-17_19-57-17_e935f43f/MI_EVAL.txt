# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, treating Kieran's apathy as understandable rather than pathological, with no judgment or disapproval even during dismissive moments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, consistent reflective listening captured both content and emotional undertones (sadness, weariness, fear of disappointment) despite the client's terse presentation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong focus on moving toward meaningful activity and a concrete first step. Minor deduction for the final question about 'what gets in the way,' which invited obstacle-focused talk rather than consolidating commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor behaved as an equal partner, asking permission before proposing tasks and letting every advance originate from Kieran's own ideas while acting as a thinking partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and repeated support for Kieran's choice ('I'm not here to add to the list of people telling you what to do'), with ambivalence accepted without pressure and no coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from the client, with Kieran's own words carrying momentum from 'Yeah, whatever' to a named desire and a concrete possible step.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located within Kieran, and his contributions visibly shaped the session from naming a trade interest to generating a caseworker script.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and developed change talk, moving Kieran from buried desire ('something with my hands') to a concrete possible step (looking up courses), amplified strategically.

**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally acknowledged briefly and non-judgmentally before pivoting toward change. The final question risked amplifying obstacles at a delicate moment, and Kieran's closing pessimism ('it'd just prove me right') was not addressed.
