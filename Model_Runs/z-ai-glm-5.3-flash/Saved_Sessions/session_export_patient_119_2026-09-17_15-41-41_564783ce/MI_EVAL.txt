# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental framing and normalization of slips throughout; the client explicitly said she didn't feel judged. No shaming even when admitting to dismissing reminders.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections of ambivalence (e.g., 'juggling work and kids... sounds exhausting'). Fell short of full marks because the fear tied to her aunt's blindness was never explored at an emotional level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent evocation of the client's own motivation via open-ended questions and scaling, with confidence rising from six to seven in-session. Score capped because the counselor offered option menus rather than letting the client generate more solutions herself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was accepted without argument and reframed as 'information,' with double-sided reflections defusing all-or-nothing thinking. Not a 5 because the counselor never explicitly validated the sustain talk itself (e.g., that 'feeling fine' makes prioritizing hard).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client generated her own plan (pill by toothbrush, spare in bag, husband's support), with counselor input framed as optional. She explicitly recognized the partnership: 'you helped me find something that actually fits my life.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on medication adherence from barriers to specific implementation, slip planning, and a measurable check-in. No topic drift and skillful guidance toward commitment without pressure.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All suggestions framed as optional choices; the client selected her own strategies, backup plan, and check-in metric. The counselor explicitly protected against perfectionism and reinforced ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evoking questions and confidence scaling yielded strong change talk ('I could try it this week,' confidence 6→7). A 5 withheld because options were offered somewhat quickly instead of drawing more solutions from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk met with nonjudgmental acceptance, reframing slips as information, and double-sided reflections. Not a 5 because sustain talk was never explicitly affirmed or validated before pivoting to change.
