# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor communicated unconditional positive regard throughout, explicitly removing conditions for success ('whatever happens by Friday, you're welcome here either way'). This directly disarmed Rafael's fear of having 'homework I can fail.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflections captured both sides of ambivalence and deeper meaning, notably the 'high bar' reflection that unlocked Rafael's shame cycle. The counselor also reflected the emotional layer around quiet shame and his wife's support.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained unwavering focus on re-engaging physical activity, moving from barriers to a concrete, specific plan with built-in flexibility and a Friday follow-up. Even emotional explorations served the behavioral goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session operated as a genuine partnership between equals, with the plan, contingency plans, and check-in details all coming from Rafael. The counselor's expertise was offered only as perspective, never directive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively reinforced with statements like 'you set this goal yourself' and 'there's nothing you owe me and nothing you can fail here.' Rafael's shift from feeling obligated to genuinely wanting change demonstrates effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor systematically drew all key content — the plan, obstacles, solutions, and meaning of success — from Rafael through client-directed questions. Affirmations consistently pointed back to Rafael's own insights and self-knowledge.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly located expertise within the client ('You know your life and schedule better than anyone') and credited Rafael with authorship of every plan element. Rafael's closing comment ('I've got an actual plan I made myself') confirms the power-sharing landed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully evoked and deepened change talk, strategically amplifying Rafael's pivotal shift to 'I feel like doing it, not just like I should.' Rafael's arc from 'nothing would stick' to 'maybe I'll actually have good news this time' reflects effective cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently met with double-sided reflections and reframing toward agency without dismissal — e.g., validating the fear that 'nothing would stick' while reconnecting to past success. Exploring the perfectionism behind 'it barely counts' transformed sustain talk into change talk.
