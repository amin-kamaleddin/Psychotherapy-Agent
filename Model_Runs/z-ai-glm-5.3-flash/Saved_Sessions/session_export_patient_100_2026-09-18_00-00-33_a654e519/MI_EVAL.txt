# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor validates Maya's lack of choice, explicitly reassures her she's not in trouble, and offers genuine, individualized affirmations without any judgment even during minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture both surface content and underlying values like fitting in, autonomy, and avoiding consequences. Includes a skillful double-sided reflection on self-check-in versus avoiding the topic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains steady focus on alcohol use with a logical motivational arc toward a concrete self-monitoring plan. However, the early portion lingers on minimization, and the session ends without an explicit summary or confirmation of next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as a genuine exchange between equals, with Maya's ideas (support person, Sunday check-in) driving the plan entirely. The counselor builds on each contribution rather than steering.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is reinforced from the opening ('you get to decide what we talk about') and every option is framed as Maya's choice on her own terms. No advice, pressure, or directives are given at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within using open, evocative questions about tipping points, protective strategies, and confidence. Maya's own values and self-generated commitment language emerge, remarkable with a guarded precontemplative teen.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located in Maya ('You know yourself and your friends best'), and every question invites her own thinking. Her contributions demonstrably shape the session, including the origin of the self check-in idea.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and develops change talk through targeted questions, progressing Maya from minimal ambivalence to genuine commitment ('I could probably actually do that'). The counselor consistently catches and amplifies even small change signals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective double-sided reflections acknowledge sustain talk while pivoting toward change. Some sustain talk is re-reflected at length before redirecting, slightly risking reinforcement of minimization.
