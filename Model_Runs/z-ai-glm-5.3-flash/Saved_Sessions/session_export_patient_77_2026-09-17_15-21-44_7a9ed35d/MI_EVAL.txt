# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, directly countering guilt ('You are absolutely allowed to eat breakfast'). Warmth is somewhat procedural, missing deeper affirmation of the client's persistence and courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening capturing frustration, ambivalence, and relief. However, deeper loneliness and emotional eating are acknowledged but not fully explored, with quick pivots to action planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains consistent focus on eating behaviors, moving from frustration to a specific actionable plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans built from client's resources with menu-style options, but information-giving turns are expert monologues that slightly tilt the power balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent choice reinforcement with no pressure; client selects her own changes and ownership is explicitly affirmed ('You are absolutely allowed to do this').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation drawn from the client, but much momentum comes from clinician-supplied rationale and option menus. Deeper evocation of values, hopes, and confidence is missing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong permission-seeking and shared decision-making, though advice-heavy turns with dense expert content dominate, and client ideas rarely reshape direction beyond picking from menus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk ('maybe overnight oats?', 'probably the hunger check') and hope ('maybe this is the thing that's been holding me back'). Missed chances to deepen commitment with confidence scaling or summarizing her reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk consistently met with normalization and reframing, redirecting helplessness toward understanding. Loneliness-related sustain talk is deflected toward snack planning rather than resolved.
