# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms Amina's honesty and contributions without judgment. Some affirmations were slightly effusive rather than matched by deeper validation of her underlying fears (e.g., fear of forever, fear of sleeping without the phone).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were consistently accurate and emotionally attuned, precisely naming Amina's ambivalence and guilt spiral. Amina repeatedly confirmed being understood ('Yeah, exactly — you get it').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained unwavering focus on the target behavior, with every exchange building purposefully toward a concrete behavioral experiment with success criteria and accountability. No topic drift occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was genuinely collaborative, with Amina's ideas (fair test, flatmate, podcast) directly shaping the plan. She explicitly experienced the plan as her own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was reinforced at every decision point ('your rules, your measures', 'on your terms'), with failure explicitly framed as permissible. The plan was entirely self-generated with no pressure or prescriptive advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation, including the powerful value-based change talk about actually 'doing my degree, not just surviving it.' A few responses supplied content before Amina generated it herself, and her lingering self-doubt ('I still don't know if I can actually change') was left unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly located expertise in the client and explicitly credited her agency throughout. Slightly deducted for an early framing ('gently released the autopilot') that shaped the direction more than full power-sharing would.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of deep, value-based change talk and strengthening of commitment by reflecting Amina's plan in her own words. Some change talk emerged through leading framings that did part of the client's work for her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently validated briefly, then redirected toward change without being amplified. Reframes like 'that's data, not failure' turned ambivalence into useful information.
