# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with affirmations and a client who felt accepted. However, affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deeper individualized acknowledgment of his feelings about being nagged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections ('your phone is your downtime'), but question barrages dominated and emotional themes like autonomy and frustration about nagging received only surface acknowledgment before pivoting to strategies.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on punctuality throughout, progressively narrowing from exploration to one concrete step, a three-night experiment, and a defined follow-up with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely egalitarian, with Jamal's ideas genuinely shaping the plan. Misses a 5 due to stacked multi-question turns and pre-packaged expert options that made the flow counselor-paced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated permission-seeking and explicit choices left Jamal in charge of the plan and follow-up. Multi-question barrages and menu-style advice added subtle pressure, limiting depth of client-owned exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and self-generated planning worked well, with confidence rising from 5 to 7.5. But intrinsic motivation was left unexplored—motivations remained largely external ('get off my back').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jamal was treated as the expert, co-creating the entire plan and having his choices respected. Advice menus occasionally tipped toward the expert role, and his deeper drivers weren't fully mined.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective scaling questions and evocation of Jamal's own plan, with confidence rising and self-generated change statements. Missed opportunities to amplify change talk with reflections or explore intrinsic benefits.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk wasn't reinforced, but barriers like boredom and friend pushback were met with practical fixes rather than double-sided reflections that resolve ambivalence or reframe external motivation.
