# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard established from the opening line ('no judgment here') and maintained throughout, with consistent warmth and affirmation of the client's wisdom. No judgmental, labeling, or disapproving language appears anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and emotional meaning, e.g., the salsa jar reflection and the deep reflection connecting 'proud I kept a promise to myself' to trusting herself again. No inaccurate or superficial reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear trajectory toward a concrete behavioral commitment: exploring what worked → designing a ten-minute morning loop → success markers → confidence → relapse prevention → commitment to try this week. The wish to defer nutrition is honored without drifting off-target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully co-constructed interaction with genuinely open questions building on the client's own statements; the entire plan and follow-up format originate from her. The clinician serves as a thinking partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision is framed as the client's call ('one thing at a time is completely your call'), with advice offered rather than prescribed ('one more piece, if you want it'). No directive or pressuring language appears.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivational elements — reasons, confidence rating, self-talk plan, relapse strategy — are elicited from the client rather than supplied, drawing on intrinsic motivations like strength, mood, and self-trust. Her closing statement 'I actually feel kind of hopeful' evidences evoked, not installed, motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is explicitly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert on your own life here') and her contributions genuinely shape the session. Agenda-setting power is handed to her at the close, exemplifying power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked via open questions, scaling (7–8/10 confidence with elaborated reasons), and the self-talk prompt, then consistently reflected back and amplified. The client produces rich commitment language ('I want better mood and energy — that walk is how it starts').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (past quitting patterns, anticipated excuses) is reframed into resilience planning rather than reinforced, e.g., 'You've turned what used to be a spiral into just one morning.' It is consistently redirected toward agency and change.
