# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone with validation of Evelyn's independence and minimizing without disapproval. Some praise felt formulaic and ambivalence wasn't explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured her framing of the fall, her skepticism, and her ambivalence. Reflections stayed at content level and missed deeper emotional meaning, such as the grief and sleep struggles since her husband's passing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use, moving smoothly from her perspective to psychoeducation to a concrete experiment with monitoring, safety planning, and follow-up. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed with Evelyn choosing nights, strategies, and scripts, and the counselor checked for additions at the end. The early psychoeducation segment was delivered somewhat expert-driven before exploring her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Evelyn's ownership: permission-seeking before sharing information, offering choices, and framing the plan as her own experiment. Her autonomy was never threatened.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew motivation from within via open questions, confidence scaling, and her own reasons (sleep, clarity, her son), including self-generated consideration of cutting the seventh drink. Depth of development was limited, relying mostly on the confidence question without eliciting importance or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treated Evelyn as the expert on her life, inviting her to choose strategies and co-create the plan. The initial information-giving was somewhat lecture-like before fully exploring her understanding.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk via open questions, confidence scaling, and concrete experiment planning, yielding meaningful commitments from Evelyn. Missed opportunities to reflect and deepen change talk when it emerged (e.g., her 3 a.m. insight) or elicit importance alongside confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk skillfully with agreement-based reflections and pivots to her values without arguing or confronting. Some minimization (e.g., 'not agreeing I have a problem') passed without a double-sided reflection that might have tipped the balance.
