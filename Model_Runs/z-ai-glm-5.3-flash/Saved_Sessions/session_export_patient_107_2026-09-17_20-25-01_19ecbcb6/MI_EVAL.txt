# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, including validation of sustain talk like the 'non-negotiable' morning cup. No labeling of coffee as a problem or expression of disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections capturing both content and emotional meaning, e.g., 'jittery AND scattered is the worst of both worlds.' Reflections tracked shifts such as 'vague worry floating around' becoming 'just seven days with a few rules.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, progressive focus on afternoon coffee consumption with no drift. Each exchange advanced from ambivalence toward a concrete experiment, success markers, and contingency plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals; all plan elements came from the client, with the counselor as a thinking partner. The client's comment 'I kind of talked myself into something that makes sense for me' exemplifies the collaborative spirit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced throughout: 'your experiment, your timeline, your rules,' and explicit permission built in for a rough Wednesday. No persuasive, coercive, or directive language appeared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited entirely from the client — his reasons, success criteria, and solutions — with zero unsolicited advice. Powerful self-persuasion resulted: 'I kind of talked myself into something.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within the client ('You know yourself better than anyone'), and every plan element (Monday start, water swap, Wednesday check-in) originated from him. The client substantially shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions ('What would make the end of that week feel like a win?') consistently drew out strong change talk and a fully articulated commitment plan. The client progressed from tentative 'maybes' to a concrete self-designed plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never challenged and was elegantly contained via the accept-and-pivot technique (protecting the morning while targeting afternoons). Not a 5 only because some reflections could have been more explicitly double-sided.
