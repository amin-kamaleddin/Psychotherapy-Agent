# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental, supportive stance with warm affirmations, but praise felt somewhat formulaic; deeper individualized affirmations of Amina's resilience were needed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured her frustration and strengths, but reflections stayed surface-level and repetitive, missing the emotional weight of repeated missed opportunities and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear, consistent focus on resolving childcare barriers to attending interviews, progressing logically to a concrete action plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The work was framed jointly and decisions were co-constructed through Amina's choices, though collaboration dipped when the counselor offered pre-packaged solutions rather than eliciting her ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered menus and reinforced Amina's ownership of the plan, but expert-style solution lists at times subtly constrained her self-directed problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina's motivation emerged largely on her own and was reinforced, but the counselor relied on logistics-based choice questions rather than systematically evoking her values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invited choice but often assumed the expert role with drafts, scripts, and option lists before exploring Amina's own ideas, making power-sharing uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently invited and amplified change talk, reflecting her 10/10 confidence and eliciting commitment language, but missed chances to deepen desire/need talk and elicit formal commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Briefly acknowledged worries about cost, paperwork, and being a hassle, but pivoted quickly to reassurance and solutions instead of using double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
