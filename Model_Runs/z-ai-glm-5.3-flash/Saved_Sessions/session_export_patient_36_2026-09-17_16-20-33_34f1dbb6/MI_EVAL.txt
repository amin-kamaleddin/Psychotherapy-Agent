# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained consistently warm, respectful, and supportive regard throughout, affirming Elena's efforts and emotional experiences without any judgment or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and value-attuned (e.g., framing the weekend boundary as an act of self-respect), but tended to affirm content rather than deepening into emotional nuance beneath, such as her fear of caving or history of overcommitting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained an exceptionally clear focus on protecting weekends and holding the boundary with the principal, with every exchange building momentum toward a concrete plan without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was genuinely two-way, with the psychiatrist offering expertise while Elena's ideas shaped every decision, including the co-arranged role-play, reflecting full power-sharing between equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforced Elena's ownership by asking permission, offering options rather than prescribing, and letting her choices (e.g., the warm/firm script) drive the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully drew out Elena's intrinsic motivation and coping strategies through open questions and confidence scaling, though some turns leaned toward providing solution lists rather than consistently eliciting her own ideas first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were repeatedly framed as Elena's, and her contributions clearly shaped the session, though some turns presented menu options in a slightly advice-list format rather than fully co-creating them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence scaling and rehearsal skillfully evoked strong commitment language ('I'm at a nine now,' 'calendar block tonight — promise'), though deeper exploration of the meaning of success or future self-image could have strengthened change talk further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** When minimal sustain talk appeared (urge to cave, usually caving before trying), the psychiatrist did not amplify it and effectively pivoted to coping plans, though redirections were somewhat incidental given the low level of sustain talk.
