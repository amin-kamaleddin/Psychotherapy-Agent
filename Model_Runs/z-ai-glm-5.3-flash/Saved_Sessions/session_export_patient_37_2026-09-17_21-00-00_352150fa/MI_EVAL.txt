# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, with genuine individualized affirmations and no judgment of the patient's hesitancy. Her concerns were normalized throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including a double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence and reflections of her core safety concern and values. Deep self-disclosure from the patient indicated she felt fully understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focus remained on the vaccine decision, with each exchange moving progressively toward a concrete plan. The session concluded with the patient stating intent to proceed with vaccination.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit partnership language and co-construction of every step; the patient's ideas shaped the session's course. Power-sharing was genuine throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor reinforced ownership at every turn, asked permission before informing, and honored the patient's choices. The final decision was entirely her own, including her self-verification step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the patient rather than lectured; her core insight about protecting her daughter was self-generated. Textbook evocation culminating in a self-generated decision.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient was positioned as the expert, and her preferences shaped the information path, action plan, and pacing. Permission was sought before providing data.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoked desire and commitment language, which the counselor reinforced with affirmations. Strong change talk emerged, including stated intent to vaccinate.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement and softened progressively, with pivots toward values and curiosity. Occasionally it was explored at length rather than actively redirected, though this deepened trust.
