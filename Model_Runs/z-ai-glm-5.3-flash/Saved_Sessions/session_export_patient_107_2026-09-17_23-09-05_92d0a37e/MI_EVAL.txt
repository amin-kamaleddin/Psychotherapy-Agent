# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental opening and unconditional regard throughout. Falls just short of top marks as some affirmations feel slightly formulaic and ambivalence normalization is generic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate double-sided reflections and nuanced complex reflections. Misses top marks because reflections occasionally stay cognitive and miss the emotional layer (e.g., mild health anxiety).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays firmly focused on coffee consumption with no drift, progressively moving toward a concrete, actionable plan. Consistent and purposeful.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange between equals, with the plan co-constructed entirely from the client's ideas (trim rather than quit, 2:30 walk, water bottle, alarm). Near-exemplary.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong emphasis on client ownership ('that came entirely from you') with no prescriptive language. Slightly leading closing questions about planning specifics keep this from a 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the client, who generates his own plan and troubleshooting. Minor deduction for supplying one answer ('the 3pm cup is the most automatic one').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician actively positions the client as the expert and builds on his hard-won insights. A few pre-packaged multi-option prompts slightly shape rather than follow the client's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful strategic questions evoked substantial client commitment language ('I could give it a shot,' planning, anticipated satisfaction). Slightly weaker early evocation before finding traction.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but never reinforced; double-sided reflections and effective pivots ('how much is habit, and how much is serving you?') redirect it skillfully. Reframes past failure as self-awareness. Exemplary.
