# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard maintained throughout, even when Viktor is defensive or minimizing. Affirmations are genuine and specific, with no labeling, disapproval, or confrontation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and emotionally deep, capturing both content and feeling beneath Viktor's words. Viktor builds on rather than corrects reflections, evidencing strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior while remaining patient-led, with every exchange moving incrementally toward commitment. Session closes with a concrete behavioral plan and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative — Viktor supplied all content (plan, timeline, strategies) while the clinician contributed the MI process. Viktor's closing comment ('you let me figure out my own deal') confirms the collaborative stance was felt.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively reinforced ('if you were the one deciding,' 'on your terms') with no advice, persuasion, or pressure. Viktor explicitly validated the approach and moved from external to self-endorsed motivation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason, plan, and commitment came from Viktor's mouth, elicited through skilled open questions rather than supplied by the clinician. A precontemplative referral evolved into intrinsic motivation — the hallmark of masterful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly positioned Viktor as the expert, and the entire plan was his own invention prompted by open questions. Textbook power-sharing with the client's contributions driving the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was systematically evoked and deepened through values-based open questions, progressing from 'maybe that's worth something' to 'lock it in before I talk myself out of anything.' Reasons and confidence were consistently reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plentiful sustain talk was handled masterfully — never argued with, always pivoted toward change via double-sided reflections and autonomy-respecting questions. Sustain talk was even reframed as an asset and dissolved into commitment by session's end.
