# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance that Erik explicitly received ('thanks for not going on at me'), though affirmations were sparse and his past dry weeks went unaffirmed as a strength.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made adequate reflective statements and summaries but relied heavily on question-and-advice mode, missing the emotional layer of feeling nagged and his pride in past self-discipline.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept excellent focus on alcohol reduction throughout, moving smoothly from assessment to a concrete goal, relapse plan, and follow-up review without any drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine power-sharing (Erik picked his days, rejected tracking), but the counselor often fell into an expert role with pre-packaged bullet-point advice that Erik mostly just accepted.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly honored Erik's choices on goals, days, and methods and framed slips as information rather than failure, though autonomy support was sometimes bundled with prescriptive advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on information and structured plans rather than drawing out Erik's own motivations; his reasons surfaced spontaneously but were never deliberately elicited or explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik's contributions shaped the plan, but the counselor's advice-heavy style and rapid assessment batteries kept the interaction more counselor-directed than truly co-constructed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik generated genuine change talk that was crystallized into a concrete plan, but this emerged spontaneously rather than through deliberate evocation or deepening questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and redirected productively, but never used double-sided reflections or reframing to work with Erik's ambivalence.
