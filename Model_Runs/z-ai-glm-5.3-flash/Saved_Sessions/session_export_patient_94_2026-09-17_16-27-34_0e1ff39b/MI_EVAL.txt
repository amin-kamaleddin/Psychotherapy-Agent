# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone despite Maria's hostility and refusals, conveying unconditional regard. Kept from 5 by warmth that was brief and task-focused, with limited individualized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected Maria's perspective and the nighttime episode, showing attentive listening. Reflections were mostly factual, however, leaving the emotional fear of the episode and her husband's worry underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on asthma management and inhaler adherence throughout, with every turn advancing the target behavior despite Maria's rushing and deflection. Excellent focus with a highly resistant client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-created practical solutions (purse inhaler, reminder, PCP sentence, fact sheet) with Maria's ideas shaping the plan. Some educational content was delivered in expert mode rather than eliciting her knowledge, though responsive to her request for facts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and emphasized Maria's ownership, accepting her refusal of the daily inhaler without pressure. Maria left feeling in control, with her confidentiality explicitly respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maria's motivation emerged partly through her own reflections, but much of her shift resulted from counselor-provided facts rather than evocation. No exploration of her values, reasons for health, or confidence, and no deepening of change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Maria as expert and decision-maker, adapting immediately when she rejected role-play and preferred a card. Slightly limited by counselor-driven information delivery rather than co-discovery.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively created conditions for change talk, which emerged ('it scared me,' 'I probably should've used it,' 'Maybe I'll ask my doctor about that spacer') and was reinforced. Commitment language was not deeply amplified, and no confidence/importance scaling was used.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responded to skepticism with acceptance and redirected toward Maria's own next steps rather than arguing, converting sustain talk into engagement. Minor point: two turns of information delivery slightly extended focus on her concerns.
