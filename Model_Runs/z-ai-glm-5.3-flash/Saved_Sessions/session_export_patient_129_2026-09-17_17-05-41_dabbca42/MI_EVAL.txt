# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful with a resistant adolescent, with transparent confidentiality and frequent affirmations. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deeper affirmation of Tyler as a person beyond his plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captured Tyler's emotional core and the function of his peer group. Deeper emotions — feeling judged, resentment toward his mother, vulnerability behind fear of being seen as a 'loser' — were not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior throughout, with a logical progression from engagement to a concrete, measurable plan. Kept the session on track even when Tyler tried to disengage.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was largely co-constructed, with Tyler choosing the target step, hiding spot, script, and backup person. The prescriptive option list and repeated role-play pressure briefly shifted the dynamic toward coach-and-client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was regularly affirmed through permission-seeking, choice menus, and accepting Tyler's limits. The counselor persisted with role-play after Tyler twice declined, and the initial recommendations were somewhat prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Tyler's own values (basketball, avoiding his cousin's fate) via open questions and scaling. Moved too quickly to supplying solutions rather than having Tyler elaborate the discrepancy between his goals and weekend use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tyler was positioned as the expert and his contributions genuinely shaped the plan. The counselor slipped into expert mode by supplying options before Tyler generated ideas and pushed role-play twice after refusal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk through open questions, scaling, and future-oriented exploration, yielding genuine change talk and a self-generated plan with confidence rated 8. Missed opportunities to deepen, e.g., elaborating the coach's 'sluggish' comment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided arguing, pivoting to realistic harm reduction and using reflections that acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it. Occasionally restated sustain talk verbatim and underused double-sided reflections to tip the balance toward change.
