# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone despite Ryan's minimizing statements, validating his competing priorities and normalizing frustration. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and task-focused rather than conveying deep unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captures Ryan's core ambivalence, including a standout catch of the 'driving later' excuse. Emotional dimensions—shame about the incident and fear of social rejection—were acknowledged but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior with every counselor turn advancing toward a concrete change plan. The session progresses logically from exploration to a specific, testable commitment with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative—Ryan's feedback reshaped the plan and strategies were offered as options. Falls short because several key ideas (NA beer, alarms, text script) originated with the counselor rather than being co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Ryan's ownership through menus, options, and offers rather than prescriptions, with no coercive language. Confidence rises autonomously (5 to 8) as Ryan builds his own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation through repeated confidence rulers and obstacle-planning questions that elicit Ryan's own reasons. Relies heavily on strategy-logistics questions rather than deeper evocation of values discrepancy beyond trouble avoidance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ryan is consistently positioned as decision-maker, and his choices substantially shaped the final plan. Slightly short of 5 because the counselor sometimes supplied ideas first rather than drawing them primarily from Ryan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and develops change talk via confidence rulers (5→6→8), commitment questions, and implementation prompts, even leveraging a live text-sending moment. A 5 would require more systematic evocation of Ryan's own reasons and values language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk without confrontation, reframing drinking's social rewards as something to preserve while protecting goals. Absence of double-sided reflections missed opportunities to more strategically soften ambivalence.
