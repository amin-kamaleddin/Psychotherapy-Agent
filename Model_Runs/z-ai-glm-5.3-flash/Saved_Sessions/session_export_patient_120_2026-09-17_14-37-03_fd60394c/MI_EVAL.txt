# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes Sofia's fears, but never affirms her strengths (e.g., cutting back, maternal motivation) or conveys explicit unconditional positive regard, and early responses carry a directive, problem-fixing edge.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; Sofia's guilt, fear about the baby, identity grief, and in-session anxiety are repeatedly bypassed in favor of reassurance, information, and advice lists.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session keeps a clear, consistent focus on alcohol abstinence during pregnancy and builds concrete refusal skills and weekend planning, though the direction advances toward practical solutions rather than resolving ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial collaboration — permission is asked before role-plays and Sofia shapes parts of the session — but the counselor's dense prescriptive advice monologues dominate and Sofia's ideas are not credited or woven back in.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Process-level choices (role-play, drafting texts) respect Sofia's agency, but the content stance is controlling, with prescriptive statements like 'the safest choice now is to stop alcohol completely' rather than emphasizing her control over her change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is supplied from outside rather than drawn from Sofia; her powerful spontaneous motivations (protecting her child, loving the non-drinking parts of friendship) are never elicited, deepened, or summarized despite a highly evocable client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor largely occupies the expert role with repeated multi-point advice lists, while Sofia's own good ideas (soda shot joke, Dani text, three-step plan) are not highlighted or built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia offers strong spontaneous change talk ('I wanna be responsible for this thing. I don't wanna let my kid down') but the counselor never evokes, reflects, or deepens it, and at times speaks the change rationale for her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's ambivalence ('part of me still wants to live that out,' 'ask me again on a Saturday night') is mostly bypassed with advice; there are no double-sided reflections, and the counselor indirectly reinforces sustain talk by over-focusing on peer-pressure scripts.
