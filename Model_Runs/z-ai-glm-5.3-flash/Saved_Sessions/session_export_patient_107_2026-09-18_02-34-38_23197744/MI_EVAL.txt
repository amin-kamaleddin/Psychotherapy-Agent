# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with genuine affirmations and clear acceptance of the client's non-negotiable morning cups. Slightly formulaic warmth in places and limited explicit normalization of setbacks keep it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate, capturing both sides of ambivalence and reflecting emotional subtext, not just content, throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on evening coffee and progresses logically from exploration to a concrete plan. Minor loss for not explicitly checking or naming the session agenda with the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is fully co-constructed, with the client designing the experiment, timeline, and metrics while the clinician provides only framing and reflective scaffolding. Decisions are genuinely negotiated, including the accountability arrangement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is reinforced throughout — the client's boundaries are honored, and every decision is client-generated. The clinician positions himself as a mirror rather than a director.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from within, eliciting client-generated solutions, success criteria, and a shift from dread to curiosity. Falls just short of a 5 due to lighter evocation of confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client, and all major ideas originate from Nathaniel. The 'hold up the mirror' framing is a textbook partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and deepened at every turn, with the client progressing from 'not promising anything' to commitment language and even requesting accountability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged via double-sided reflections without judgment, and boundaries are honored rather than challenged. Occasional reflections repeat the client's negatives without immediately pivoting, keeping it from a 5.
