# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental, with genuine specific affirmations; explicitly accepts the client's warning and honors her defenses. Falls short of a 5 in that regard could be more explicit about her worth independent of progress or change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections throughout, such as 'that gap is where you got stuck,' with validation without minimizing. Despair and shame are reflected cognitively but not explored deeply at the level of feeling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains disciplined focus on safety and a crisis strategy, gently redirecting each drift toward hopelessness. Session ends with a specific, co-constructed, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership with 'together' language and every plan component coming from Maria herself. Her surprise ('is that how this works?') followed by co-authoring shows real power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames even crisis planning as a choice, explicitly grants her right to resist, and accepts her refusal to promise without pressure. All plan elements originate from the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never supplies reasons for change; all motivation drawn from the client through skillful questions, culminating in her spontaneous 'I want to try.' Motivation was demonstrably elicited and expanded from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Maria's expertise as central ('You know yourself better than anyone') and credits her with the clinical work. Her own contributions substantively drove the intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout domain—skillful evocation and reinforcement of change talk at every turn, including the powerful rehearsal question. Visible in Maria's own shift to 'I... want to try. That's new.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged accurately and briefly without reinforcement, with a textbook double-sided opening reflection and reframing of the 'almost' as data. Misses a 5 in occasionally mirroring hopelessness without transforming it and not strategically reframing suicidal ideation toward reasons for living.
