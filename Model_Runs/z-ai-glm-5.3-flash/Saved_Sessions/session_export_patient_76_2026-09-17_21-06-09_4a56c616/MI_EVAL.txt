# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment toward a client presenting with self-harm and hopelessness. Specific, genuine affirmations validate even her ambivalence and fear of hope.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing somatic buildup, protective skepticism, and internal obstacles. The client's shift from flat 'I don't know' to detailed plan design demonstrates she felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with each response advancing toward concrete change. The session culminates in a specific, client-owned action plan with a defined timeframe.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is fully co-constructed, with the client's ideas (urge ratings, honesty rule, coping card, medium-bad start) shaping its direction. The counselor repeatedly invites input ('What would help most on that card itself?').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support via the 'experiment rather than a promise' framing, explicitly freeing her from coercion and framing failure as data. The client owns the plan, honesty rule, and timeline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is elicited entirely from the client—her own criteria, her 'why,' her obstacle plan, and her timeline. Her transformation from 'I don't know' to 'Tonight, before I can talk myself out of it' is powerful evidence of effective evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positions Liora as the expert and judge, and her contributions substantively shaped the session. The experiment design, card, honesty rule, and timeline all came from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation and reinforcement of change talk, progressing from sustain talk to commitment language. Open questions draw out the client's own ideas, which are then reflected back and deepened toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without amplification and skillfully reframed ('what hasn't been strong enough yet?'). Doubt about the ice was neutralized by reframing testing as an experiment, removing the risk of failure.
