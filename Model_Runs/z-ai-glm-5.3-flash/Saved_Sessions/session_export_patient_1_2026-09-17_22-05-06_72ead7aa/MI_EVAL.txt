# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard — never judges Ahmed's minimization, validates the dust theory, and responds to dismissal with curiosity. No disapproval, labeling, or judgment appears anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and keeps pace with Ahmed's evolving perspective, capturing both sides of his ambivalence. Reflections also track emotionally significant content, including the daughter disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking behavior while remaining patient-centered, briefly acknowledging deflections before returning to the topic. The session progresses steadily from cause-exploration to testing to planning without forcing the agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is entirely Ahmed's creation, with the counselor acting as a thinking partner rather than delivering advice. Ahmed's contributions genuinely shape the session, showing true co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Ahmed's ownership ('this is your call and your health,' 'two weeks is yours to take or leave') and never presses beyond what he offers. No persuasive or coercive language is present.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within Ahmed — questions elicit deeply personal reasons including pride, peace at home, non-dependence, and his daughter. Ahmed moves from precontemplation to a self-designed change experiment within one session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Ahmed as the expert ('you're the scientist of your own health') and builds every question on his own words. Power sharing is at the highest level, with all key plan elements originating from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through open questions that produce Ahmed's own plan, then deepens it to elicit self-motivators (money, wife's peace, daughter, self-image). Change talk is named, reinforced, and expanded into implementation detail.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argues with sustain talk and uses effective double-sided reflections to pivot toward change. Falls short of 5 because Ahmed's rationalizations ('they're harmless') remain largely intact and are occasionally simply mirrored without an active pivot.
