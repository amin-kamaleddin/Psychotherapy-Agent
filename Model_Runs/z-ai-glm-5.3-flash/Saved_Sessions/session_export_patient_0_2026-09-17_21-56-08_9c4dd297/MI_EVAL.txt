# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated unconditional positive regard throughout, never judging the meth use and affirming the client's disclosure ('I really appreciate you trusting me with that'). The client's growing openness and trust reflect the safety created.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, complex double-sided reflections captured the client's ambivalence and emotional undertones precisely. The client repeatedly validated reflections by expanding on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior while moving the session progressively from contemplation toward a concrete plan. No topic drift; every question tied back to the client's stated values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated fully as an equal partner, inviting the client to lead with his answers shaping each turn. The resulting commitment was genuinely self-generated rather than compliance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the client's ownership ('you're the one who gets to make that call') with no advice, pressure, or directives. All plans originated from the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew out motivation entirely from within the client, including his significant realization about what the 'fun' really was. The client's own language drove every motivational shift.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly located expertise within the client, and the plan was entirely his creation with the counselor mirroring and organizing rather than supplying content. Client ideas visibly drove the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically evoked change talk through targeted open questions and reinforced it ('not going as hard is a real change'), while highlighting client agency. The client progressed from cautious ambivalence to concrete commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Met minimizations with neutral or double-sided reflections and pivoted toward the client's own change signals. Some balanced reflections occasionally dwelled on the sustain side; a stronger pivot toward change talk could accelerate resolution.
