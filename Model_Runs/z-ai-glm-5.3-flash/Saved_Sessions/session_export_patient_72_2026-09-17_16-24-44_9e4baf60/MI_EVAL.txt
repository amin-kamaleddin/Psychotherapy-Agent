# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently warm, non-judgmental stance with natural affirmations ('Nice work,' 'You handled that really well'). Leila's preferences and 'no skipping, just sliding' rule were treated as fully legitimate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening that captured both her goals and barriers, plus proactive anticipation of her sales anxiety. Reflections occasionally summarized rather than deepened the emotional meaning of her past near-signing experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained exceptionally clear focus on re-establishing exercise, with every exchange advancing the concrete plan (Saturday 10 AM tour, 15-minute walk, fallbacks, accountability). Ended with specific, measurable commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed—Leila proposed times, backups, and accountability, and the counselor adopted her preferred shorter script. The bidirectional role-play exemplifies full collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced autonomy through choice-offering, permission-based advice, and rehearsing assertiveness against sales pressure. Fostering her ownership of the 'I'm not signing today' stance was especially strong.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Leila's own motivation via rulers, success questions, and rehearsal. Could have more explicitly reflected and amplified her spontaneous change talk ('I feel more confident when I'm active').},

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Leila as decision-maker ('What would you like to focus on today?', 'Which would help most now?') and offered expertise only after acceptance. Offers were sometimes framed as menus rather than open invitations, slightly constraining her contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited strong change talk through importance/confidence rulers and planning questions, producing concrete commitment language ('Saturday 10 AM, I'm ready'). Could have more deliberately reflected her change talk back to deepen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers (distraction, sales pressure, weather) were acknowledged briefly and redirected into solutions and her own coping strategies (mall backup, scripts). The acknowledgement could at times be more explicitly validating before pivoting.
