# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, from the opening 'I'm not here to judge' to closing affirmations. Affirmations were genuine, specific, and tied to Isabella's own values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including the double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence. The psychiatrist tracked subtle emotional shifts, not just content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use during pregnancy while moving purposefully from ambivalence to concrete action planning. Every exchange advanced toward the change goal without steering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—asked permission before each action and co-constructed the plan with Isabella shaping every practical detail. Her comment 'thanks for not treating me like a problem to fix' confirms the equal-partner experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was exemplary and explicit: 'you're the one in charge of your decisions here' and consistently reinforcing her ownership. The closing ('That was you') attributed change entirely to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argued for change; motivation was drawn from within via open questions about her values. Isabella's self-generated reasons (good parenting, avoiding preventable harm) drove the change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Isabella as the expert, and her contributions substantively shaped the session's direction. The counselor followed her lead rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk with questions like 'What made this feel possible to say out loud right now?' Her commitment language ('I've got a plan') flowed directly from these evocations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never reinforced sustain talk; used neutral double-sided reflections and pivoted back to her values and choice without debate. No dwelling on reasons to keep drinking.
