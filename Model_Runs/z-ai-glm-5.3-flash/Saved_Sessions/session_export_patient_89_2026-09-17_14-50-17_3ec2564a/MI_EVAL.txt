# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations throughout, but praise is somewhat formulaic and focused on the plan rather than the person. The discouragement of repeated failure ('I don't even know why I bother anymore') is not explicitly normalized or validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early, accurately capturing Marcus's frustration and weariness. Reflections become more procedural later, focusing on logistics rather than the deeper emotional meaning of years of yo-yo dieting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained consistent focus on physical activity throughout, building a concrete escalating plan (walk type, cue, calendar block, backup, dancing, check-in). Every exchange tied back to the target behavior with strategic, purposeful questions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with menu-style options and permission-seeking, exemplified by the joint 7pm check-in plan. However, rapid-fire idea lists (four walk-timing options, five supports in one turn) occasionally shifted toward advice-giving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices consistently framed as Marcus's, with all plans self-generated or client-selected. No directive or coercive language appeared, reinforcing ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from the client through eliciting reasons, confidence ratings, and intrinsic enjoyment (walking, dancing, family). Occasional direct provision of solutions and rationale, rather than consistently drawing all content from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treated Marcus as the expert, offering options and asking preferences. Multiple stacked questions and suggestions at times tipped toward counselor steering.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk via confidence ratings (7→9), meaningful-win exploration, and the dancing idea. Marcus responded with strong commitment language: 'I'm actually kind of excited' and 'I'll start dancing right away.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers without amplifying them and pivoted effectively, including the 'short loop > nothing' reframe of all-or-nothing thinking. Brief loss for repeatedly re-eliciting obstacles, though always paired with backup planning.
