# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone with genuine affirmations ('That's your own wisdom talking') and validation of discouragement. Withheld a 5 because some affirmations feel slightly formulaic and the fear of 'making a fuss' was not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, accurately capturing ambivalence, relief patterns, and shame ('that stings a little, but it's true'). Missed some moments to reflect deeper emotional layers like grief over lost function and the fear beneath 'I get nervous asking for things.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on increasing rehab engagement via advocating for pool exercises and planning to speak up at the next visit. The session builds coherently from exploration to concrete commitment with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Therapist acts as an equal partner, positioning Mateo's ideas as the engine of the plan. Withheld a 5 only because the structure is fairly question-driven along the therapist's arc rather than freely shaped by the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced through agenda choice, permission-seeking, and framing decisions as Mateo's. The closing 'holding the wheel again' confirms the client's restored sense of agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn out from Mateo at every turn—exception moments, reasons for change, values, and confidence—without any advice given. Culminates in his self-authored definition of persistence and renewed self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise repeatedly located within the client, with every plan element originating from Mateo. Textbook power sharing; the client's contributions genuinely shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk evoked systematically and skillfully, from exception questions ('What was different then?') to consolidating reflections ('what does it feel like, hearing yourself say that out loud?'). The therapist deepens rather than merely elicits, culminating in a self-authored plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without amplification, with quick pivots to coping and resilience planning. Withheld a 5 because double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk were not used, and some sustain talk was met with forward-looking questions rather than explicit reframes.
