# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, but warmth is formulaic and affirmations are generic (e.g., 'reaching out is huge') rather than individualized to Nadine's strengths as a mother.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; emotionally rich disclosures—3am loneliness, guilt, comparison to her mother—were met with facts and fix-it responses rather than complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on sleep deprivation, help-seeking, and normalization, progressing clearly toward concrete action steps (Wednesday bottle, pediatrician call, sister as backup).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The role-play rehearsal was genuinely interactive, but much of the session involved the counselor delivering pre-written scripts and lists of advice rather than co-constructing solutions with Nadine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One effective moment of genuine choice ('what would help most right now?'), but heavy reliance on verbatim scripts and supplied rebuttals implies the counselor knows the right way to communicate rather than trusting Nadine's own voice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation came largely from Nadine's own cooperative disposition; the counselor supplied answers and scripts rather than eliciting her reasons, confidence, hopes, or her own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor consistently assumed the expert role, directing the flow and supplying scripts verbatim, with little explicit recognition that Nadine knows her relationships and limits best.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nadine generated the change talk herself and the counselor supported it through rehearsal and specificity questions, but there was no deliberate evocation—no confidence rulers or deepening of her commitment after successful practice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about guilt and doing it all alone was acknowledged but not strategically softened; responses normalized rather than used double-sided reflections to pivot toward her values as a capable mother.
