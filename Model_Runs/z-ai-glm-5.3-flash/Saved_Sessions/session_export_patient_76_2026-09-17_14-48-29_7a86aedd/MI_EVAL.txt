# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance toward a self-harming client, with warm affirmations and direct safety questions asked without blame. Warmth was somewhat brief and formulaic, with missed opportunities to affirm Liora's courage in attending despite a coerced referral.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured Liora's exhaustion, ambivalence, and protectiveness about her coping tool. Reflections were sometimes brief, with a tendency to move quickly to problem-solving rather than deepening emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on self-harm and coping throughout—safety screening, trigger exploration, a concrete harm-reduction experiment, a written if-then plan, and arranged follow-up. The session never drifted aimlessly.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative, with plans co-created and Liora editing her own reminder wording. However, brainstorming and plan drafting were at times counselor-generated, leaving Liora in a selecting role rather than generating ideas herself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by asking permission, offering choices, and respecting Liora's refusals without pressure. Her expressions of felt choice ('I can try it once,' 'No promises') were welcomed rather than challenged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Drew out some client motivation, including Liora's closing statement 'Not cutting. Something else,' but motivation was largely channeled toward counselor-proposed strategy menus. Missed key opportunities to explore Liora's own values, hopes, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Liora as the expert on her own life, offering menus of options and adapting the plan to her preferences. Some plan drafting was counselor-led, with Liora passively agreeing at points.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked incremental change talk, moving Liora from 'nothing else really works' to committing to try the ice experiment before the next session. Experimental framing lowered the bar for commitment, though deeper exploration of Liora's own reasons for change was not pursued.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not argue with sustain talk, instead reflecting it neutrally and pivoting to an acceptable compromise (top shelf rather than handing blades over). Adapted skillfully when Liora resisted the roommate suggestion.
