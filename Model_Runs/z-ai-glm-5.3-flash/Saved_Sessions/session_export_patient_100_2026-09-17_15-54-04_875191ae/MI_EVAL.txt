# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, normalizing Maya's ambivalence and accepting her guarded disclosures. Falls short of 5 due to somewhat formulaic warmth and limited explicit affirmation of her strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, accurate reflective listening that captures Maya's guardedness and desire for a discreet change. Occasionally summary-style rather than reaching deeper emotional meaning such as fear of social exclusion or embarrassment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus from vague school concerns to a concrete self-selected experiment, with logical progression each turn without forcing it.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with permission-seeking, menu options, and a co-constructed plan. Falls short of 5 because counselor-generated tactic lists sometimes replace eliciting Maya's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice consistently offered and Maya's tentative commitment accepted without pressure. Menus occasionally constrained her sense of choice ('all sound kind of lame'), and explicit ownership statements were absent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from Maya's own words and reasoning, with minimal direct argument for change. Inconsistent at times due to counselor-delivered advice menus and lack of exploration of deeper values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maya positioned as the expert, with her ideas incorporated into the plan. Counselor still drives the agenda structure through generated lists rather than fully shared direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk via pros/cons and the confidence ruler, producing Maya's own commitments. Could deepen change talk with follow-up questions rather than moving quickly to tactics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged briefly and redirected with double-sided reflections that validate and pivot toward change. The pros/cons exercise gave substantial airtime to reasons for drinking, slightly amplifying sustain talk.
