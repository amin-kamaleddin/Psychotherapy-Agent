# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and nonjudgmental throughout, tolerating Anya's sass and earning her 'thanks for not making this preachy.' Affirmations were limited ('Nice choice,' 'strong, specific plan'), keeping it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening ('It sounds like you're not sure this is a problem...'). Reflections stayed mostly summary-level, missing the emotional weight of the embarrassing party night and secrecy from her mum.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session had a clear arc from engagement to a concrete plan with follow-up. Weakened by fully deferring the alcohol concern—the original referral reason—to next week without explicitly linking it to the stress plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine co-construction ('we can tweak it together'), with Anya's preferences shaping the plan. The counselor supplied most strategy content rather than eliciting Anya's own ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary: consistent menus of choices, permission-seeking, acceptance of deferring the party night, and nonjudgmental framing of setbacks ('we'll tweak it together'). Anya's ownership was reinforced throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation drawn out via the confidence ruler and 'which sounds easiest?', but most strategies were counselor-supplied. Anya's richest self-motivational statement about alcohol ('it does bug me a bit') was left unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anya was treated as the expert, choosing strategies, timings, and specifics (kitchen counter, 7–8pm). Short of 5 because the counselor drove the agenda options rather than first asking what Anya thought would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and reinforcement of commitment talk ('I'll do it tonight,' 'I'm setting that alarm the second I get home'). Change talk around alcohol itself was not pursued, deferring the party night discussion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I don't really think I have a problem') was not reinforced, but no double-sided reflections paired her minimization with her own expressed discomfort. The drinking concern was deferred rather than softened.
