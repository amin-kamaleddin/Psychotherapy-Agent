# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, never judging Jamal's habits or motivation and normalizing slips ('one slip doesn't kill the whole thing'). Warmth feels genuine and individualized throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are consistently accurate and deep, capturing themes like monitoring feeling 'worse than being late itself' and the shift to internal ownership, with each reflection validated by Jamal's expanding responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on punctuality, moving methodically from problem to causes to a concrete first step and relapse planning, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently frames the work as joint ('let's think ahead,' 'which of those do you think') and Jamal's ideas shape the plan. Affirmations occasionally felt slightly leading, gently guiding rather than purely sharing power.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively fostered through phrases like 'on your own terms' and choice-oriented questions, and Jamal's shift to internal motivation ('it's my call instead of theirs') is direct evidence this succeeded.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor never lectures or supplies reasons for change; all motivation is drawn from Jamal himself, culminating in his own commitment and relapse-recovery language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Jamal, with the plan repeatedly attributed to him ('a plan you designed on your own terms') and every strategy originating from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful questions like 'What would handling this on your own terms actually look like?' elicit deep intrinsic change statements, which the counselor then strategically mirrors and amplifies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about nagging and mornings is not reinforced and is reframed toward autonomy, though some sustain talk about external pressure is occasionally elaborated rather than actively pivoted away.
