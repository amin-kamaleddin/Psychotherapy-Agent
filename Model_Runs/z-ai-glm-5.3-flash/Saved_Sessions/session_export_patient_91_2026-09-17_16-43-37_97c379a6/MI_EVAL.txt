# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with frequent affirmations of Marcus's efforts. Affirmations were somewhat generic and missed the emotional weight of his fear-of-failure disclosures.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good initial empathic framing, but reflective listening was sparse amid heavy questioning and planning. Emotionally loaded statements about fear of failure went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus with clear progressive momentum from worry identification to a concrete plan and follow-up. Every exchange advanced the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was largely co-constructed based on Marcus's preferences, and resources were shared responsively. Counselor occasionally supplied ready-made solutions (wife script, video list) before exploring his own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Marcus's ownership through menus, permission-seeking, and honoring his boundaries. His growing self-directedness ('That I can do') reflects effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited motivation through choice-based questions, with Marcus generating his own change talk. Deeper exploration of personal values and why managing diabetes matters was absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marcus remained the decision-maker, with his preferences shaping the plan and his judgment affirmed. Some turns leaned toward advising by quickly offering scripts and options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively structured the session so Marcus voiced his own motivation, culminating in confident commitment language. Could have deepened change talk by reflecting it back or asking confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframed slips as non-failure and support as encouragement rather than policing, effectively softening hesitancy. Brief normalizations occasionally glossed over ambivalence rather than using double-sided reflections.
