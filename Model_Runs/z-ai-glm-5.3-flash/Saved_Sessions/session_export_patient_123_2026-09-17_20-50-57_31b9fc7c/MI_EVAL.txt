# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and warmth, using non-judgmental statements like "You're not in trouble here" and "You don't have anything to prove to me." Sofia's guarded responses were met with normalization throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were generally accurate and captured Sofia's ambivalence well, such as summarizing her friendship values and uncertainty about college pressure. However, reflections occasionally stayed at surface content rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session opened with significant drift into general background, and the alcohol screening result was never explicitly named or discussed despite Sofia's cue that she wasn't sure what to say about it. While the conversation eventually touched on future party scenarios, the target behavior of alcohol use remained implicit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor asked rather than told, invited Sofia's perspective, and built on her answers in a mutual, respectful way. However, there was no explicit agenda-setting or joint negotiation of session focus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforced Sofia's ownership and choice, framing her decisions as hers without any persuasion, pressure, or directive advice. Statements like "You're the expert on your own life" exemplified this.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from Sofia herself through questions about her best coping tools and past moments of staying true to herself, eliciting her own resources and reasons. Evocation focused on general self-efficacy rather than being explicitly tied to alcohol-related motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positioned Sofia as the expert on her own life, and her contributions directly shaped the session direction. Her mention of college freedom led to exploration of future autonomy, demonstrating genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully elicited concrete change talk about protective factors (the bonfire story, her tools for college) and affirmed and deepened it. Further development of explicit commitment language would be needed for a higher score.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk was met with non-defensive, dual-sided reflections that legitimized uncertainty without amplifying it, then pivoted to her strengths and past successes. The sustain talk was never reinforced or dwelt upon.
