# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with respectful reflections of Daniela's fears. Affirmations were sparse; more consistent individualized positive regard would raise this score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captured her core conflict so well the patient said 'you just said it better than I did.' Occasional stacked double questions diluted attunement and created a quiz-like feel.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question and reflection tied back to the disclosure decision with no topic drift. The session progressed logically from ambivalence toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely responsive two-way exchange building on the patient's material, with the plan emerging from her. Collaboration relied heavily on questions with little explicit 'we' language or agenda negotiation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Disclosure was consistently framed as optional with no pressure or directive advice at any point. The patient set her own pace and defined what help she wanted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew on the patient's own evidence (Maya's history, her gut instinct) to produce genuine self-persuasion. A few turns, like 'Notice that?', edged toward leading her conclusions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned Daniela as the expert through repeated perspective-seeking questions, and her contributions shaped the session. Power sharing was expressed through questioning rather than explicit acknowledgment of her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and highlighted change talk, moving Daniela to 'maybe I actually do it. Like soon.' A deeper exploration of confidence and a concrete commitment step would push this toward a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used double-sided reflections and the patient's own counter-evidence to soften catastrophic thinking. At times restated the fear side of ambivalence before redirecting, briefly reinforcing sustain talk.
