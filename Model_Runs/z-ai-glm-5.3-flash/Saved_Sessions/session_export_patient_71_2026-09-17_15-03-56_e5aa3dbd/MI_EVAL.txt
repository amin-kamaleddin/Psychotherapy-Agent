# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental, with the patient explicitly noting she felt safe; affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and feeling, but missed opportunities to explore the emotional weight of Laura's self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior throughout, with no topic drift, culminating in a specific, measurable plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong joint agenda-setting and co-constructed planning incorporating Laura's preferences, though occasional rapid-fire questions created a checklist-like rhythm.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Laura's ownership and used permission framing for advice, but suggested strategies rather than eliciting her own solutions first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivation, vision, and commitment language, but did not deeply connect the change to Laura's broader value system.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely incorporated Laura's feedback into the check-in structure and positioned her as the expert, though some structured exchanges reduced felt mutuality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked strong commitment language, ability statements, and rising confidence ratings, but moved quickly to logistics rather than exploring deeper reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk but handled it more by avoidance than active softening, lacking double-sided reflections and direct ambivalence work.
