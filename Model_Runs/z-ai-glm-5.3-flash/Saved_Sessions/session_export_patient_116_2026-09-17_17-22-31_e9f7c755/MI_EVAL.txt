# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone, responding to Samir's humor and guardedness with calm curiosity and regard. Affirmations were somewhat generic and rarely affirmed Samir's personal strengths, keeping this below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening that accurately captured Samir's identity concerns and family dynamic, helping him move from deflection to engagement. Occasionally fell into interrogation-like multi-question barrages rather than deeper emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on medication adherence from opening to close, gently redirecting deflection without losing momentum. The session progressed logically from exploration through ambivalence to a concrete, reviewable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed from Samir's ideas, and decisions were negotiated collaboratively. However, question barrages and pre-formatted option menus sometimes positioned the counselor as plan designer with Samir selecting from her menu rather than generating his own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly and repeatedly offered choice throughout, honored Samir's 'no promises' without pressure, and affirmed his ownership ('You chose this — that's important'). Samir's closing statement — 'it's mine, not the family committee's project' — demonstrated successfully fostered autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within, eliciting Samir's own reasons, problem-solving, and confidence, with change talk growing visibly across the session. Evocation was occasionally undercut by pre-generated solution menus rather than prompting Samir to brainstorm first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Samir as the expert, and his contributions (work dosing, coffee pairing) became the entire plan. Moments of expert stance — the rapid-fire option menu and 'pop quiz' follow-up Samir humorously noted — prevented a 5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked Samir's own motivation, with change talk growing from 'it's not really my thing' to 'I can try it this week' and confidence rising from 6 to 7.5. The counselor sometimes supplied answers herself, partially undercutting evocation of his own solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it, never argued with resistance, and pivoted strategically toward change. Occasionally mirrored sustain language back extensively without using double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
