# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout; validates skepticism and affirms efforts without pressure. Affirmations are somewhat generic, lacking deep, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is consistently accurate, capturing ambivalence and minimization well. Deeper emotional material (father's cardiac death, fear of an invisible condition) goes unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with well-paced progression from manageable step to concrete decision and follow-up plan. No topic drift; strong directional guiding.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-sided interaction; patient's own ideas incorporated into the evolving plan. Much of the content remains clinician-sequenced, with limited patient shaping of the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices consistently framed as the patient's own, with emphasis that the plan came from him. Lacks explicit acknowledgment of the patient's freedom to choose or abandon the plan without judgment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions systematically draw out the patient's own reasons, past resources, and decision point rather than imposing external ones. Motivation is clearly treated as residing within the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Patient treated as the expert on his own life; the plan emerges entirely from his contributions. Ownership of the agenda is not explicitly handed over, keeping power-sharing strong but not maximal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful sequence of open questions steadily builds change talk from vague interest to concrete commitment and planning language. Consolidated effectively with a summary and forward-looking question.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is reflected neutrally and redirected without argument. No explicit double-sided reflections or deliberate reframes transforming sustain talk into change talk.
