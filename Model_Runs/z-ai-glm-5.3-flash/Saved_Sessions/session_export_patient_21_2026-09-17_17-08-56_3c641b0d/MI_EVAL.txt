# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with consistent affirmations of Camila's strengths and normalization of setbacks, though affirmations were somewhat brief and not deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured Camila's ambivalence well, but reflections were brief and the emotional weight of her discouragement after missed days went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on increasing physical activity throughout, with every exchange advancing toward a concrete, measurable plan including contingency planning, confidence scaling, and a check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were co-constructed with Camila's ideas shaping the plan, but the counselor sometimes offered pre-packaged option menus and counselor-driven summaries rather than jointly generating ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Camila's ownership of choices without pressure or persuasion; her closing comment 'you didn't push me' confirms genuine autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong practical evocation via confidence scaling and doability questions eliciting Camila's own reasoning, but deeper values and reasons for change were not probed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila's expertise was consistently honored and she shaped the plan, though pre-packaged option menus sometimes limited her generative contribution.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation and reinforcement of change talk, producing commitment language ('I actually feel ready for this'), though early change talk was not deeply explored or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and reframed as problem-solving, but double-sided reflections linking obstacles to Camila's reasons for change were underused.
