# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and non-judgment, framing even staying at five drinks as a thoughtful choice. Warm, respectful tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent complex reflections capture both content and emotional nuance, including her independence, social attachment, and ambivalence. No misreadings of the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol reduction with purposeful progression from exploration to a specific plan, barriers, start date, and follow-up. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—the plan and success criteria are entirely co-constructed from Margaret's own ideas. The clinician offers no prescriptions, acting as a curious partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy actively reinforced ('you're doing the deciding here,' 'completely your call'), with no pressure toward abstinence. Even the fallback option is honored as legitimate.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own reasons and solutions, but some motivation leans on external anchors (son, screening form) that could be developed into intrinsic values. No confidence/importance exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located in the client, who generates the entire plan, her pub response, and success criteria. The clinician affirms and builds on her contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk well evoked, producing specific plan talk and success criteria plus a start date. Falls short of 5 as commitment language is never deepened further (e.g., confidence scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without reinforcement via double-sided reflections and skillful redirection. A couple of reflections briefly restated sustain content before pivoting.
