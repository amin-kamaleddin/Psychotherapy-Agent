# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance and validates Anya's concerns throughout. Affirmations are brief and the counselor quickly pivots to information delivery rather than offering richer, individualized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captures Anya's perspective and social anxieties, e.g., worries about seeming 'boring.' Reflections are somewhat formulaic and emotional nuances are acknowledged briefly rather than explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol attitudes and refusal skills tied to Anya's goals, with minimal topic drift. Focus is sometimes counselor-led through menus of topics rather than jointly developed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choice points and engages in requested role-play, but frequently delivers information blocks with limited two-way shaping. Anya mostly selects from counselor-generated options rather than co-creating the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice from the outset and never pressures Anya to change, offering menus throughout. Choice is expressed through option-listing rather than reinforcing Anya's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some client motivation around goals and doable strategies, prompting Anya to articulate her own reasons. Relies heavily on persuasion through education rather than systematically developing her own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Positions itself as a supportive resource but often slips into an expert stance with bulleted facts and scripts. Anya's own ideas are briefly affirmed but not substantively built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerges and commitment language is evoked when asking which strategies feel easiest. The counselor misses opportunities to reflect and amplify Anya's motivational statements, jumping instead to strategy lists.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce Anya's sustain talk, which is good. However, sustain talk ('one night won't hurt,' 'my friends manage fine') is not actively softened with double-sided reflections or strategic pivots—responses either defend risk information or redirect to tips.
