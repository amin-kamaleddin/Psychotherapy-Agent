# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with validating responses to Sofia's awkwardness and self-deprecation. Affirmations occasionally feel slightly formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including a skillful double-sided reflection holding Sofia's ambivalence. Reflections sometimes stay surface-level and miss the emotional weight beneath her flat, dismissive tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully redirected toward a concrete, client-endorsed target of sleep during exam stress. The pivot from the alcohol referral to sleep was never explicitly acknowledged or tied back to the screening purpose.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint exploration between equals, with all directions emerging from Sofia's own answers. The 'design the screening yourself' technique was an outstanding collaborative move.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoided directives, letting Sofia generate her own solutions. Lacked explicit autonomy-affirming statements reinforcing Sofia's ownership and choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nearly every turn drew out Sofia's own perspectives and reasons for change, moving her from precontemplation to self-generated motivation. Confidence and ability talk was not explicitly developed, and evocation targeted sleep rather than the referral behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary power-sharing, repeatedly framing Sofia as the expert on her own life ('You know your own life better than any screening does'). Never assumed the expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire statements about better sleep from a client with no initial change talk, then deepened them. Commitment and confidence language was never explicitly explored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled minimization neutrally without reinforcing it, and used a well-constructed double-sided reflection pairing doubt with hope. Some later sustain talk received less active softening.
