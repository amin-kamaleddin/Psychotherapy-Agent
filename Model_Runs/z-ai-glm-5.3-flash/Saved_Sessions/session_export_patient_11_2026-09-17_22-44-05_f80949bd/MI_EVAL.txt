# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard were consistently conveyed, including explicitly validating her deferral of nutrition changes and committing to a no-judgment check-in. No judgment or disapproval appeared anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and consistent, capturing both content and meaning (e.g., the jar incident, her three motivators, and her self-compassion plan). Reflections built on each client contribution effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained unwavering focus on increasing physical activity, with each response advancing toward a concrete, measurable plan. Even the nutrition topic was set aside without derailing focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative, with the check-in structure co-constructed and decisions negotiated rather than prescribed. The client's preferences directly shaped the final plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership was actively reinforced throughout, with the goal number, plan, and contingency strategy all generated or chosen by Amira. The counselor explicitly framed the change as hers rather than directing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the client through open evocative questions rather than supplied by the counselor. Every core motivation (strength, confidence, mood, energy) was elicited from Amira and developed through reflections and affirmations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located within the client, and the walking plan, 'some movement counts' rule, and three-walk target all came from Amira. The counselor reflected her own words as the foundation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoked rich change talk ('I definitely want that back,' 'three is the win'), which was reinforced by reflecting it back verbatim. Client change language was abundant and developed throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not dwelt on; the nutrition deferral was affirmed without amplifying resistance, and all-or-nothing thinking was reframed into the client's own solution. Missed-day talk was redirected toward her self-compassion plan.
