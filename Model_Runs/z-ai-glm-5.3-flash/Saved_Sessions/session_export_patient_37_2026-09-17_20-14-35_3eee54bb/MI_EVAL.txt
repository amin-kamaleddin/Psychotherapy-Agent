# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with no criticism of Elena's hesitancy or internet sources. Even the sister-in-law was validated, and Elena's shift from defensive to proud reflects genuine felt acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections, including a powerful reframe linking fear of reactions to guilt over choosing. This reframe produced Elena's 'I never thought about it like that before' and the session's turning point.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the vaccine decision, moving deliberately from clarifying ambivalence to a concrete, self-authored question list. Direction stayed on process without pressuring a specific vaccine outcome.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing: agenda items came from Elena, and the counselor checked in rather than assuming. Elena's shift from 'ready to just say no and leave' to co-building the list shows the collaboration was real.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced explicitly: confidence 'in whatever decision you make,' genuine choices about next steps, and no persuasion toward vaccination. The decision framework was built entirely around Elena's own criteria.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn out almost entirely through open questions, with nearly all insights (unprotected window, side-by-side numbers, pride in doing it properly) originating from Elena herself. The final exchange demonstrates change talk evoked from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena was repeatedly positioned as the expert, and her contributions genuinely shaped the session. Her insight about the unprotected window emerged from her own reasoning and was credited back to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoked Elena's own reasons and needs, and her change talk was consistently reflected and affirmed. The session ended with her generating concrete, self-authored next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally acknowledged briefly and pivoted toward exploration, without arguing or reinforcing fears. However, some early reflections restated sustain content at length and lacked double-sided reflections to tilt the balance toward change.
