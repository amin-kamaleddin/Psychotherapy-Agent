# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, with consistent affirmations like 'That kind of self-awareness is a real strength' and 'Listen to you — brave as a habit.' No judgment, labeling, or disapproval of the patient's history of staying silent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently captured both content and emotional nuance, including the cost of silence ('bugged you for days') and the meaning of 'I can do that even scared.' Patient repeatedly confirmed accuracy by expanding on reflections ('That's exactly it').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the staff meeting as the target behavior, progressing logically from values to concrete wording, coping strategies, and follow-through. Session ended with a well-developed, self-generated forward plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction between clear equals, with 'I'm curious' and 'here's what I'm wondering' framing rather than directives. Patient's answers visibly shaped each subsequent question and the entire plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient's ownership reinforced throughout ('in your own words,' 'however it goes'), with no advice imposed and every strategy originating from the patient. Her closing 'I've got what I need' reflects successful autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All substantive ideas (notepad script, self-talk, Rosa comparison, journaling, target sentence) came from the patient, drawn out via open questions. Strong, self-generated change talk like 'brave as a habit' and 'I keep my own word now' was evoked and amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient explicitly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert on your own life,' 'That's your expertise at work, not mine'). The entire plan was her creation and her contributions directed the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoked rich desire, commitment, and ability language ('I don't want to hide anymore,' 'brave as a habit'). Change talk was then reinforced through affirmations and linkage to her values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk in the session limited opportunities to demonstrate strategic softening; the one instance of fear was handled well with a double-sided reflection. Growth area: sustain this quality of evocation and double-sided reflection with more actively ambivalent clients.
