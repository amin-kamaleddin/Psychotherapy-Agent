# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently accepts the patient's autonomy ('You know your mouth better than anyone') and affirms her competence as a 21-year-old adult. There is no judgment or pressure regarding keeping the piercing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections demonstrate deep understanding of the patient's core concern — being treated as an adult, not lectured. The psychiatrist accurately reflects both content and the feelings beneath it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions and reflections consistently steer toward the patient's own motivations for monitoring oral health and engaging in follow-up care. The patient ends the session feeling 'weirdly better about the next appointment.'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The exchange is highly collaborative, with the patient generating all ideas (dentist monitoring, phone note, script for the appointment, plan for the next visit). The psychiatrist never imposes solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient retains full decision-making authority throughout ('my call either way'), which the psychiatrist explicitly reinforces ('with you making the final call'). The patient's choices about the piercing are honored without challenge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes change talk rather than providing it, asking what the patient values, what signs she'd watch for, and what plan fits her. The patient articulates her own reasons and strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open-ended questions invite the patient to lead ('What would you want to keep an eye on?', 'What options would you want to have?'). The tone is an equal partnership rather than expert-to-patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully draws out commitment language ('I'll ask her next time,' 'I'll write that script down') and strengthens it through reflections. Statements like 'that's me taking charge of this in your own way' reinforce self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('not now,' 'not saying never,' 'no obsessing') is met with acceptance and reflection rather than confrontation, while gently linking it to hope ('you're not shutting the door'). No discord develops during the session.
