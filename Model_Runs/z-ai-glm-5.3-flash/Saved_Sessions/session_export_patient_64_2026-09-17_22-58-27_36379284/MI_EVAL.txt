### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard — "nothing about this is a test. You're in charge." No disapproval of hesitancy; stubbornness was normalized rather than criticized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections, e.g., "It annoyed you, and I get why..." and the double-sided reflection on not needing it personally while wanting to protect grandparents. Captured identity-level meaning ("the grandson who protects his grandma").

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Steady focus on the target behavior throughout; each exchange advanced from ambivalence to a concrete plan (walk-in pharmacy after the gym) without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Plan was co-constructed using Jordan's own ideas (gym, walk-in, Sundays); counselor invited input on follow-up support rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** "You're in charge of what we explore," "You don't have to decide anything right now," reminder framed as "a nudge, not nagging." No pressure or persuasion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client via open questions; elicited Jordan's values (family protection, independence, no worry) and client-generated commitment ("I think I'm actually gonna do it").

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** "It's your plan, in your words, on your timeline"; "let's make this yours." Expertise explicitly positioned within the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Strategic open questions evoked "maybe I should get it, for them" and eventual commitment. Counselor highlighted and amplified change talk ("I notice the 'for them'"; "those are powerful words") and deepened it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without reinforcement; double-sided reflections ("I hear both things clearly... Those can both be true") and non-confrontational reframing of stubbornness. Never argued with minimization.
