# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates respect and unconditional positive regard, normalizing ambivalence and de-shaming disclosure. Occasional affirmations feel somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, with complex reflections capturing the emotional core of ambivalence. Client repeatedly confirms accuracy ('Yeah, exactly,' 'that's exactly it').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, progressive focus on alcohol reduction from concerns to a concrete first step. No topic drift; even tangents tie back to whether she needs alcohol to be herself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint exploration between equals, with the client proposing strategies and arriving at her own insights. Summaries reflect collaboration rather than authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly and repeatedly reinforced ('this is your call,' 'on your terms'), honoring her rejection of abstinence. No directive or pressuring language appears.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits intrinsic motivations—connection, self-respect, reclaiming Sundays—rather than supplying reasons for change. Helping the client voice her 'quiet knowing' powerfully shifted her toward commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located in the client, with all strategies originating from her and the counselor amplifying rather than prescribing. Session direction follows the client's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through open questions, progressing naturally from contemplation to a concrete commitment. Reinforces and deepens change statements effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying sustain talk, using double-sided reflections that pivot toward values. Occasionally restates barrier language ('the boring one') longer than necessary before the change-oriented frame.
