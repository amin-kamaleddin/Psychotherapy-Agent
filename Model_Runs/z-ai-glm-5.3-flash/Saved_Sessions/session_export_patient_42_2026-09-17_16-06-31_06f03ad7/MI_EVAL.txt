# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations and validation of ambivalence. Occasional praise feels slightly formulaic, keeping it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening capturing Elise's core dilemma and triggers. Moved quickly to solutions rather than deeply exploring the emotional pain of her social exclusion from the failed quit attempt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on smoking cessation from first utterance to last, with each turn advancing the change agenda. Session progresses logically from ambivalence exploration to concrete plan, backup, support, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-created plan from Elise's own ideas (coffee substitution, sister as support, her own wording). Elise's closing comment confirms the non-authoritarian, shared approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice and accepts when Elise declines a suggested tool. Some advice is delivered prescriptively (gum instructions, text drafts, reminder-setting), edging toward directive guidance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of confidence ruler, open questions, and eliciting Elise's own solutions and motivations. Counselor supplied considerable content (refusal lines, text drafts, tools) rather than consistently eliciting it from Elise.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly treats Elise as the expert, with her ideas substantially shaping the plan. Falls short of 5 due to reliance on option menus and somewhat directive scaffolding (drafting texts, prescribing check-in times).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elise's language shifts from sustain talk to commitment ('I'm doing this. Tuesday') with confidence rising from 6 to 6.5. Evocation relies somewhat on structured planning rather than drawing out deeper personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and skillfully reframed rather than argued against, preserving Elise's valued social connection. Endorsing the 'cutting back, not quitting forever' framing slightly softens the ultimate change goal.
