### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, including in response to gambling, minimization, and defensiveness. Affirmations are specific and genuine, with unconditional positive regard sustained even amid talk of deception and risky behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflective listening, including double-sided reflections that capture ambivalence precisely and name unspoken emotional undercurrents. Reflections consistently land, with Anya building on them to indicate genuine understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on gambling and its consequences with each exchange progressing purposefully toward change. Guides without dragging, with no topic drift across the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint exploration, asks rather than tells, and builds each response on Anya's answers. Acts as a thinking partner rather than an authority, with no expert pronouncements at any point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never advises, persuades, or prescribes; all ideas and strategies are elicited from Anya, and even the pace of change is left to her. Her closing realization that 'the text's the bit I control' demonstrates internalized autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates on the premise that motivation resides within the client, using open questions to evoke reasons, values, emotions, and vision of success. Develops what emerges, deepening the 'Egypt money' line into a full change identity and client-articulated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise within Anya and credits her with authorship of the change. All strategies originate from the client through questions crafted so her contributions shape the session, exemplifying top-level partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evokes and deepens change talk, with Anya's language progressing from vague ideas to a concrete plan and commitment. The signature intervention is developing her own change identity line ('Not tonight, that's my Egypt money') and consolidating it.
