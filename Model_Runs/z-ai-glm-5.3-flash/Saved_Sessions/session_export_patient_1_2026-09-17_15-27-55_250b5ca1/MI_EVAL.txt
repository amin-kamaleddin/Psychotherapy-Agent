# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Ahmed's skepticism and the value of his social ritual. Affirmations are brief and somewhat transactional, with acceptance of his doubts not fully deepened.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is generally accurate, capturing Ahmed's surprise and minimization without arguing. Reflections occasionally skim surface emotion and rely on formulaic openers, missing deeper exploration of the social meaning of cigar use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior throughout, with every turn advancing the agenda from assessment to a concrete negotiated plan. No topic drift; session progresses logically to specific goals and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint and consistently incorporates Ahmed's preferences, phrasings, and humor into the plan. Collaboration is diluted by pre-packaged option lists and a slightly didactic psychoeducation turn.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames choices as Ahmed's own and honors his self-directed, partial commitment without pressure. Rapid, uninvited advice menus subtly crowd out his own solution generation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and follow-up questions are genuinely evocative, and Ahmed supplied his own plan elements. However, much content (tips, refusal line alternatives) was counselor-supplied, and his importance rating never shifted, leaving motivation largely compliance-based.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ahmed's input genuinely shaped the plan, but the counselor frequently slips into expert/advice-dispensing mode with prescriptive tip lists and intermittent permission-seeking.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of importance/confidence rulers and strategic follow-up questions; confidence rose from denial to 7 with Ahmed generating his own change plan. Early desire and reasons for change were not deeply developed while importance stayed low.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but softening is largely passive, with double-sided reflections appearing late. Some counselor turns invite more sustain talk without strategic reframing.
