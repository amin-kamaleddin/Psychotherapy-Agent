# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with validating statements like 'You're right to want clear reasons and safety.' Affirmations remain brief and formulaic, lacking deeper individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections ('That sounds frustrating'), but responses are heavily informational and checklist-driven. Emotional core — feeling dismissed, the 'mystery pill' resentment — is never deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, unwavering focus on understanding and adhering to the statin from first exchange to closing summary, with a structured recap and follow-up offer.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and co-drafted the doctor message, but much follows an advise-then-ask lecture pattern. Mai had to push back twice before receiving direct engagement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated genuine option-giving, no pressure, and Mai's decisions affirmed as her own. Falls short because some option-lists steer toward drafting questions with little explicit affirmation of her ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opening question and 'which feels most helpful?' invite perspective, but the counselor rarely drew out or expanded Mai's intrinsic motivations — no confidence/importance exploration or development of her stated values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Solicits input and collaborates on the doctor message, but the expert lecture format positions the counselor as knowledge holder, rarely acknowledging Mai as the expert on her own health.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mai generated strong change talk spontaneously ('I'll switch to orange juice,' 'I'll call Dr. Ferris straight away'), but the counselor made little active effort to evoke, deepen, or consolidate it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify skepticism and gently redirected, but no double-sided reflections or deliberate pivots back to Mai's own motivations; sustain talk was absorbed by shifting into education mode.
