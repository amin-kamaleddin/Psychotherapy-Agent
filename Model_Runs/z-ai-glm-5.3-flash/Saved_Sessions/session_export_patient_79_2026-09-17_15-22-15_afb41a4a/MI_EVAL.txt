# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with a resistant client, validating dismissive comments rather than correcting them. Affirmations were somewhat generic rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured both content and meaning behind the client's defensiveness. Reflections occasionally prioritized moving toward the plan over exploring the emotions beneath his frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on asthma self-management throughout, systematically narrowing from broad frustration to a concrete plan, script, and rehearsal. No topic drift even when handling tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was genuinely co-constructed, with plans and scripts refined to the client's stated preferences and a role-play controlled by the client. No expert-driven or authoritarian moves were observed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent support for client choice, with explicit affirmations of expertise and menu options rather than prescriptions. The counselor accepted Luis's own terms for follow-up without override.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently drew out the client's own motivations and reasons rather than lecturing. Deeper personal values and confidence/importance scaling were not explored, keeping motivation at a practical level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert, repeatedly checking preferences and adjusting the plan to his feedback. The role-play reversal substantially handed power to the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited change talk through open questions, producing commitments like 'I'll try it' and 'I'll use the rescue thing more.' Missed opportunities to deepen change talk via importance/confidence exploration kept this from a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying resistance, redirecting with forward-looking questions. Some reflections restated sustain talk without double-sided pivots, and distrust of the doctor was acknowledged but not resolved.
