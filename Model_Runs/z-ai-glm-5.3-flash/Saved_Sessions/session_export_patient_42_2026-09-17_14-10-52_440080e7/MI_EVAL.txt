# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, normalizing ambivalence and preempting self-blame ('don't beat yourself up'). Affirmations were brief and lacked individualized depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early on, accurately capturing Elise's ambivalence and summarizing her pros/cons. Missed reflecting her deeper fear of not being 'interesting enough' to socialize without a cigarette.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus on smoking cessation with no topic drift, moving logically from exploration to a concrete commitment (texting Dana). Each step advanced the target with helpful summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely a joint enterprise — Elise's ratings, fears, and choice of Dana shaped the plan. However, Plans A/B and backup strategies arrived fully counselor-generated rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every intervention was offered as a choice, with consistent option-framing ('pick one,' 'Want me to draft...?'). Success was framed on Elise's terms and slips were normalized without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Much of the content (lists, plans, scripts) was counselor-supplied rather than elicited. Few open questions probed her deeper motivations — the daughter/jar motive was never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Elise as the expert in selections and choices, with her input revising the plan. Still, the actual content was mostly counselor-generated, so her expertise was consulted but not fully harnessed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited clear commitment language ('I'll try it this week with Dana... one coffee break, I can do that'). Missed chances to deepen or affirm change talk when it emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled fears of exclusion well with concrete reframes and an 'if-I-smoke' backup framing to prevent catastrophizing. Pivoted quickly without fully validating the legitimacy of her social-loss fears.
