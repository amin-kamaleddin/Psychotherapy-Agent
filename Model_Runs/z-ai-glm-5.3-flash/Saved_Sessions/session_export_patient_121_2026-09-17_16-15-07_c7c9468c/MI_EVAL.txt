# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance, validates Sofia's reactions, and respects her preferences around praise. Repeated safety check-ins become somewhat mechanical rather than individually attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections are accurate and validating, but empathic reflection largely disappears during skills rehearsal. Sofia's disclosure about Maya and her vulnerability about 'cringe' praise are not explored emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on responding to peer accusations without escalation, with each step building momentum. Minor drift from repeated safety questions and the Maya topic is deferred without follow-through.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint role-plays with responsive adjustment at Sofia's request ('make this one actually pushy'). Counselor tends to deliver pre-packaged advice blocks rather than eliciting Sofia's own solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, respects Sofia's 'no promises' stance, and honors confidentiality. Pre-packaged option menus subtly steer toward the counselor's framework with little affirmation of Sofia's ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strongest motivational moments ('kind of empowering', 'maybe controlling my part') emerge from the exercises rather than deliberate evocation. Sofia's own reasons, hopes, and strategies — especially regarding Maya — are not drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Sofia's choices and honors her preferences, but the session's middle becomes directive with dense menus of scripts and templates. Sofia chooses among options rather than co-creating them; her own ideas receive minimal integration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through behavioral rehearsal, producing empowering commitment language from Sofia. Change talk is not consistently deepened, and her hope about reconnecting with Maya goes unexplored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not argue against Sofia's blame of peers and teachers, but also rarely actively redirects it — the 'snitching' complaint goes unacknowledged. Sofia's self-generated pivot toward controlling her own part is left unexploited rather than guided with double-sided reflections.
