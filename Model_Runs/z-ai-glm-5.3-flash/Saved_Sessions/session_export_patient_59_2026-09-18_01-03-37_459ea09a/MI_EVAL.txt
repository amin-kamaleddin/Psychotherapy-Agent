# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental despite the dismissive, resistant opening, with no lecturing or moralizing. Affirmations at times feel formulaic and warmth around the social pull of his crew could be more explicit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, with complex reflections that accurately capture content and stakes. Missed a significant emotional disclosure about Jamal's mother working too hard, and reflections are somewhat instrumentally geared toward reframing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus and discipline — the session advances a coherent arc from values through concrete strategies to consolidation with no topic drift. Each question moves progressively forward, landing on a client-generated take-away.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction reads as two equals problem-solving, with Jamal's ideas visibly shaping the session. The rhythm is somewhat asymmetrical, with loaded leading questions that steer content and direction rather than allowing open agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support for a mandated client, from the opening framing through repeated 'that's your call' statements. All strategies come from Jamal with zero persuasion, warnings, or directive advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never supplies reasons for change — every motivation is drawn from Jamal through open, values-focused questions. The sustained question–reflection–affirmation pattern produces authentic self-persuasion, confirmed by his final statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Jamal as the expert and builds each question directly on his prior statements. The ending 'where'd that kid come from?' praise slips slightly into a clinician-directed framing that Jamal deflects.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicits and deepens change talk through open questions tied to Jamal's own values, yielding strong self-generated statements like 'better to look soft for five minutes than sit in front of a judge for months.' Each change talk moment is affirmed and developed rather than passed over.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without arguing or amplifying it, rolling with resistance and pivoting to planning. Briefly explores rather than redirects away from the ambivalence behind 'I'm not gonna be perfect with it' would have strengthened this.
