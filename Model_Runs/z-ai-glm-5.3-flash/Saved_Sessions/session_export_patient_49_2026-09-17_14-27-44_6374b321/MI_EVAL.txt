# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with Hana's ambivalence treated as legitimate. However, affirmations are generic and formulaic rather than individualized to her strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but shallow and often rushed into questions or advice. Emotional nuances (grandmother's gum disease, fear of existing damage, relief at non-lecturing) are rarely explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation leading to a concrete, client-chosen plan with negotiated follow-up. Slightly diluted by repetitive closing summaries that add little direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine two-way exchange early (readiness ruler, menu of options, client-generated experiment), but later turns shift toward prescriptive, counselor-dominated instruction rather than building on Hana's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong option-giving, respect for Hana's choices (delay length, start date, check-in timing), and reinforcement of self-kindness. Weakened by repetitive re-prescribing of strategies once the plan was set.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler successfully elicited Hana's own reasons and she left more motivated. But evocation is superficial — values, social meaning, and confidence were left unexplored in favor of supplying advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and choice menus convey some power-sharing, but later turns drift into expert-driven advice-giving without crediting Hana's own solutions and expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler evoked change talk and Hana generated her own plan, but the counselor misses chances to deepen it — no confidence questions, no exploring how success would feel, and little amplification of her self-motivating statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged neutrally without reinforcement, but responses lack strategic double-sided reflections or reframes. Hana's self-motivating statements about slips and not waiting for obvious damage go unamplified.
