# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and non-judgmental, with affirmations and respect for confidentiality. Warmth is practical rather than deeply personalized, keeping it below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Erik's ambivalence and validated his wind-down drinking. Missed opportunities to deepen emotional content around his father's illness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on target behaviors throughout, tying every turn to concrete steps and a time-bound follow-up plan with scheduled check-ins.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was largely co-constructed with genuine power-sharing, but the counselor drove option generation early with pre-packaged food and drink suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Erik's choices were consistently honored and the counselor repeatedly asked what felt most doable. Could be stronger with explicit emphasis that the plan belongs to Erik.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited, but the counselor often answered questions with advice rather than exploring Erik's own ideas. Internal reasons for change were surfaced but not deeply amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik's input was invited and adapted to, but the counselor frequently offered pre-packaged solutions rather than drawing strategies primarily from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik volunteered meaningful change talk and evocative questions were asked, but the counselor did not consistently deepen it with rulers, elaboration, or exploration of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged neutrally without argument, but double-sided reflections linking sustain to change talk were missing and the counselor moved quickly to logistics rather than resolving ambivalence.
