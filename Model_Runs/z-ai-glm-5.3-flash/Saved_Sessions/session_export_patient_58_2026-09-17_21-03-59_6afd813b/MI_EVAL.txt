# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opens with strong unconditional regard ('you matter here, not just the tardiness') and closes with affirmation of honesty, but presence wavers mid-session where several clinician turns drift into first-person, patient-like content, reducing consistent affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows accurate reflective listening that captures Jamal's frustration and the night-vs-morning struggle. Reflections sometimes lean toward reframing change rather than fully exploring the emotional weight of feeling surveilled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on punctuality throughout, with well-sequenced questions moving from problem to plan to obstacles to relapse planning. Some questions circle the snooze/sticky-note theme without consolidating into a summary of the agreed plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive questioning and lets Jamal's own plan shape the session, but collaboration stays implicit—no co-created summary or shared agenda-setting—and mid-session role drift undermines the equal-partner exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength: change is repeatedly framed as Jamal's choice ('your own way of making this happen,' 'What's your gut tell you'), and Jamal's pacing ('one step at a time') is accepted without pushback. No pressure or directives appear.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws intrinsic motivation (graduation, self-respect, mom's 'quiet look') through evocative questions about Wednesday's test. However, evoked reasons aren't consistently deepened, and confidence ratings or explicit commitment language are never elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Jamal as the expert on his own life and hands decision power back to him at the close. Partnership is somewhat weakened mid-session as the counselor's contributions taper, giving Jamal little to share power over.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilfully highlights intrinsic motivation ('you said part of this is for you') and evokes commitment, drawing strong change talk ('I'm done being late. For me this time'). Spontaneous change talk like graduation and self-respect deserved more reinforcement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Doesn't reinforce sustain talk but also never actively softens it—Jamal's fears of proving he can't do it and the 'one bad morning equals failure' belief are left unmet by double-sided reflections or strategic reframes.
