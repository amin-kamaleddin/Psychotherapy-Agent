# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicated consistent respect and validated Mai's desire to understand before starting, with no judgment of her skepticism. Affirmations were somewhat generic and lacked individualized warmth or explicit normalization of her initial doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Included some accurate reflections, but leaned heavily on information delivery over reflective listening. Mai's emotional shifts — frustration, worry, annoyance, and relief — went largely unacknowledged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed firmly focused on understanding and adhering to the new statin with no aimless drift. Direction was maintained through content delivery rather than deliberate goal-focused progression from ambivalence to commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint, asked permission before advising, and genuinely incorporated Mai's ideas. Occasionally reverted to advice-dispensing with lengthy didactic lists, slightly unbalancing the partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent autonomy support throughout — opened with supporting her choice and consistently offered options rather than directives. Respected all of Mai's choices (texting vs. rehearsing, sending vs. waiting) without any pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opening question was a good evocative start, but subsequent turns were dominated by education and scripts rather than open questions drawing out Mai's reasons, values, or confidence. Never explicitly explored her ambivalence about preventive medication at 34.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly asked for Mai's input, conveying that choices were hers. However, the interaction was heavily counselor-led with unsolicited information blocks, and Mai's own expertise was incorporated only when she volunteered it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mai articulated a concrete plan with clear commitment statements ('I'll send that text today,' 'I'll switch to orange juice'). However, this talk emerged from her own problem-solving rather than intentional evocation, and opportunities to deepen it (confidence rulers, values exploration) were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced, and resistance was acknowledged without amplification while pivoting to actionable steps. Largely bypassed sustain talk via information-giving rather than skillfully softening it with double-sided reflections.
