# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with no criticism, but affirmations are somewhat generic rather than deeply individualized. No explicit unconditional regard offered when Anisa voices repeated fears of failing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early on, accurately capturing Anisa's ambivalence. Reflections thin out mid-session, and key emotional content (fear of failure, the leper metaphor, her daughter watching her) is never explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation from opening to a concrete plan. Every turn ties to the target behavior with no topic drift, progressing steadily toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames work jointly and co-constructs the plan through iterative choices, building on Anisa's ideas like the kettle note. Anisa primarily selects from counselor-provided menus rather than the direction emerging from her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices, lets Anisa set her own win criteria, and respects her rejection of the quitline. Option menus are counselor-generated, and pre-scripted lines for her daughter edge toward directing her relationship rather than eliciting her own words.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation and Anisa generates her own solutions when given space. However, much of the session supplies strategies and scripts rather than drawing out her own reasons and strengths, with key moments of spontaneous motivation passed over.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites client input with questions like 'Which feels most helpful?' but much of the session is counselor-driven content delivery. Anisa shapes details but the overall structure and expertise come from the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk and commitment language ('I've got something to hold onto'), but several opportunities are under-developed. The 6/10 confidence rating was accepted with general affirmation rather than exploring what would move her to a 7 or 8.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledge sustain talk with a good double-sided reflection early on, but later validates failure fears without pivoting to strengths. The repeated 'slips are normal' framing and backup-plan scripts risk dwelling on relapse imagery rather than building resolve.
