# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm,  respectful,  nonjudgmental throughout despite defensiveness and minimization.  Explicit unconditional regard ('no judgment either way')  and an affirming closing summary.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate,  complex reflections capturing both ambivalence and lived experience,  e.g.,  license/independence and fear of being 'the outcast sipping water.' Consistently integrates his history of isolation and prior sobriety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Laser focus on drinking/driving with clear forward momentum,  moving from reasons to a concrete,  time-bound first-week plan with zero topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed;  every plan element originates from Derrick and is negotiated rather than prescribed,  with tentative ideas developed into a plan he calls 'mine.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices consistently offered ('which feels more doable',  'anything else,  or is this enough?'),  with no advice imposed.  The plan is explicitly attributed to Derrick and ownership of setbacks is protected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from Derrick himself:  his reasons,  strategies,  risk analysis,  confidence rating,  and obstacle plans all came from his own mouth.  Textbook evocative techniques (open questions,  scaling,  'what would make it higher?').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Derrick as the expert ('You're the expert on your life'),  and his contributions demonstrably shaped the entire session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk systematically evoked via open questions,  confidence scaling,  and 'what would make it one point higher?'  Affirmations reinforce Derrick's own language rather than clinician-supplied reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('it's just beer',  outcast fear)  acknowledged and pivoted via double-sided reflections without argument.  Isolation is even reframed as a resource drawing on prior learning.
