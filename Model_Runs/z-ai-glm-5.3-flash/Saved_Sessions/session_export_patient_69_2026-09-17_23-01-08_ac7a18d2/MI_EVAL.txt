# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, countering self-deprecation ('Not stupid at all') and honoring heavy disclosures. Warmth felt genuine and individualized, never formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, deepening complex reflections throughout, capturing ambivalence ('part of you does want your time to count') and subtle emotional shifts (noted increased vocal energy around bike repair). Reflections went well beyond parroting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session progressed deliberately from disengagement to a concrete plan while remaining client-defined. Each exchange served as a stepping stone toward a self-generated next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative — permission was sought, agenda was shaped by the client, and the final plan came entirely from Kieran's own ideas. His choices demonstrably steered the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was central from the opening (choosing vs. being told) and the counselor named the client's own insight that self-chosen activity is the key. No pressure applied when the client hedged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from the client via open questions ('what would make time spent feel less pointless?'). Client moved from 'Yeah, whatever' to self-generated values, self-efficacy, and a concrete commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client repeatedly positioned as the expert, and the positioning had real impact — his contributions shaped the session and the plan. Client's reaction ('like I'm worth planning for') confirms genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary sequencing of evoking, amplifying, and securing commitment talk, culminating in the client's own plan ('Tools by the door'). Values ('clear problem, clear finish') were elicited and connected to action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged, validated as honesty, and reframed toward possibility ('that's seeing the exit') without dismissing the client's perspective. Hedges were met with 'both are true' and immediate redirection to planning.
