# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with resistance met by validation rather than pressure. No judgment or disapproval appears anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate, including complex double-sided reflections that capture ambivalence without distortion. Reflections are checked against Elena's responses and confirmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays focused on contraception and moves progressively toward information-gathering and follow-up, aided by summaries. However, Elena's avoidance slows momentum and no explicit focus statement about the contraception decision is ever named.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: permission is sought before offering information, and Elena's responses visibly shape the agenda. Decisions are co-constructed with information shared partner-style rather than as recommendations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary emphasis on Elena's ownership ('You're in charge of this conversation,' 'no pressure to have an answer'). Information is offered only with permission and framed as choice-neutral, and her refusal to book anything is respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from Elena herself via evocative questions, yielding her own reasons for change (avoiding the scare, feeling less in the dark). By session's end she articulates her own follow-up plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located within Elena ('Your idea, not mine'), and her preferences directly drive the session's direction. Power-sharing is genuinely enacted, not merely stated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully elicit and reinforce change talk, culminating in 'Never feeling that scare again... that was way worse than a moody week.' Elena ultimately proposes her own follow-up, showing well-developed change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and redirected toward change, with good double-sided reflections. Warmly validating sustain talk at times slightly extends focus on the status quo rather than pivoting more actively toward change.
