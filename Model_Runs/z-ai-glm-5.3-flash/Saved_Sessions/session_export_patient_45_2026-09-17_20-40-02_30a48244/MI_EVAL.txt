# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validating Erik's sense of loss and affirming his competence without judgment. No labeling or moralizing about drinking despite clear opportunities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing the loss-of-control theme so precisely that Erik said 'Nobody's put it like that before.' Both content and feeling were reflected throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on alcohol reduction tied to diabetes, progressing steadily to a concrete plan with monitoring and a start date. No topic drift; every exchange advanced the goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal interaction: the counselor positioned themselves as someone to 'work alongside' Erik and drew on his expertise. The final plan was entirely co-constructed from his own words ('running a tighter ship').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was central throughout, repeatedly returning decisions to Erik and celebrating his ownership. His closing statement—'it's my fight, not something being done to me'—confirms perceived choice was successfully fostered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from Erik himself via open questions, eliciting his own reasons and solutions. His self-generated plan and insight about building his own plan exemplify successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Erik as the expert on his life and deflected his request for direction back to his own judgment. His contributions visibly shaped the entire plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk, with Erik progressing from 'where do you think I should start?' to a committed start date. Commitment language was affirmed each time it appeared.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was mostly acknowledged neutrally or reframed, using Erik's own insight to pivot toward change. Occasionally re-voiced (e.g., 'two pints don't feel like enough'), though strategically in service of planning.
