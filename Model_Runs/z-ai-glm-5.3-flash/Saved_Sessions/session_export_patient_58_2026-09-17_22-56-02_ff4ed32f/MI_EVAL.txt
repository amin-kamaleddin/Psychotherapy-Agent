### Acceptance

**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout — judgment-free framing, normalized slips, and immediate counter to Jamal's self-criticism ('That doesn't sound dumb'). Jamal's closing ('not as much of a lecture as I expected') confirms he felt accepted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate, capturing both surface content and underlying meaning (e.g., the internal/external perception gap and the 'nobody expects me to be fast' insight). Jamal's responses consistently confirmed he felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on punctuality and its driver (late-night phone use), moving logically from exploration to a concrete commitment ('I can start tonight') with a defined follow-up agenda. No drift off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully egalitarian — the counselor asked rather than told, invited Jamal's perspective on every decision, and framed follow-up as shared work. The plan's content came entirely from Jamal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit choice offered at every decision point ('the note, the picturing, or something else entirely') with no persuasion or advice-giving. Closing affirmed Jamal's ownership: 'you're the one who'll be making this happen at 1am.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within, with skillful deepening of the shift from extrinsic ('people off my back') to intrinsic ('feeling decent — that's the part I actually want'). Jamal's self-generated plan and ownership statements evidence successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client ('You're the expert on your own mornings'), with all strategies originating from Jamal and credited back to him. The closing summary reinforced that the plan 'is yours.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and affirmations elicited concrete planning language, and the counselor repeatedly amplified intrinsic motivation ('That's real motivation coming from you'). Jamal's deepest change talk ('for me — not just cause everyone's watching') directly resulted.

**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally redirected well toward internal reasons without dwelling on external pressure.
