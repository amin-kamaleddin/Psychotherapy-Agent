# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing ambivalence and honoring Elena's stylistic requests. Warmth felt somewhat formulaic, with the affirmation 'You're clearly protecting the life you're building' repeated nearly verbatim.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captured both content and emotional nuance (e.g., 'relieved — and exhausted from that week of waiting'). Some reflections pivoted quickly to menu-style questions rather than deepening emotional material like the self-blame or not telling her mom.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on contraception following the pregnancy scare, culminating in a concrete action step. Direction was slightly diluted by initially permitting full deferral and the absence of closing summarizing statements.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was co-constructed — options chosen by Elena, the message drafted to her specifications, and permission asked before info-sharing. The dense expert-style information list briefly shifted power away from the equal-partner frame.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model autonomy support: no pressure at any point, explicit 'no commitment' framing, and Elena set her own deadline and backup plan. The counselor affirmed her ownership without adding conditions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Elena herself, eliciting her reasons ('that scare was enough... Never again') and commitment language without arguing for change. Motivation was elicited mostly through logistics-focused questions rather than deeper exploration of values or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena's contributions shaped the plan — her side-effect questions became the clinic list and her preferences defined the message draft. Repeated closed three-option menus slightly constrained her agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk, culminating in a concrete commitment ('texting by Friday... No — I will'). Key moments like 'Never again' were not deepened with further exploration of her reasons or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced but was only partially redirected, and the counselor at times validated the status quo ('Condoms are fine if you make consistency the goal'). No double-sided reflections paired her ambivalence with her stated motivations.
