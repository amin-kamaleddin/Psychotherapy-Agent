#### **Score (1–5):** ☑1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☐5 — **3/5**
**Comments:** Indirectly evoked change talk via concrete options and prioritizing questions. Marcus produced meaningful change talk ("Something's gotta give," "I'll do the real call this week"). Missed chances to deepen it — no reflection on "Something's gotta give," no confidence scaling, no exploration of reasons for change, commitment never consolidated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☐5 — **3/5**
**Comments:** Avoided arguing or chasing the dog topic — appropriate restraint. Agreed to focus on "what I can handle," respecting pacing. But ambivalence was sidestepped rather than engaged; no double-sided reflections or gentle reframing to help resolve it.

---

**Overall Impression:** Warm, respectful, and pragmatically effective in producing one concrete commitment (Thursday billing call with script and calming technique). Strengths: autonomy support and acceptance. Key areas for growth: deepen empathy through complex reflections, use open questions and scaling to evoke client motivation, engage the dog-related ambivalence with double-sided reflections, and re-anchor the plan to asthma management as the primary clinical target.
