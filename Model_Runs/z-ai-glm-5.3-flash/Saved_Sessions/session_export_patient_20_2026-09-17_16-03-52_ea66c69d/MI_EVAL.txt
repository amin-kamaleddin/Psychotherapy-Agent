# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and respect, with affirmations like 'that takes grit' and explicit non-judgment. However, the warmth sometimes feels formulaic, with limited normalization of Brandon's ambivalence and defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but often brief restatements of the plan rather than deeper explorations of meaning. Little reflection of the emotional layer beneath Brandon's minimization—discomfort with family criticism, fear of labels, or pride in self-control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use from the start, with every exchange tied to the alcohol-free Wednesday plan and progressing logically to a concrete commitment and Thursday 9am debrief. Excellent directional structure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed—Brandon chose strategies, the check-in time, and the text tone. Multiple stacked questions and suggestions in single turns slightly tilt pacing toward the counselor's agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is regularly affirmed ('You're in charge,' 'whatever you choose'), with options offered rather than prescriptions and Brandon's one-night boundary accepted. Occasionally long menus of suggestions subtly steer rather than purely elicit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence rulers, open planning questions, and eliciting Brandon's own coping ideas drew motivation from within, including intrinsic reasons like avoiding feeling 'stupid sneaking a drink.' Deeper values-based evocation of long-term reasons for change was largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Brandon is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, and his contributions genuinely shaped the plan (rejected gym, chose the show and text style). Rapid-fire multiple questions at times feel scripted rather than truly co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of confidence scaling and concrete planning questions evoked genuine change talk, including Brandon volunteering strategies and raising other nights to address. Relied heavily on action-oriented questions rather than evoking deeper personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('everyone's blowing this out of proportion') was acknowledged briefly and redirected without argument, with skepticism reframed as normal. Double-sided reflections would have strengthened this, but sustain talk was never amplified.
