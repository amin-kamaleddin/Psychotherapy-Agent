# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard communicated consistently ('this door stays open no matter what'; imperfection framed as 'how real change works'). No judgment about smoking, past failures, or ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections throughout, including the opening double-sided reflection on ambivalence. Deeply nuanced understanding of the fear of the first week and the wish for the daughter to notice without announcement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus from exploring ambivalence to evoking reasons to concrete planning, without drift. Purposeful, patient-paced guidance toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal partnership: 'shall we sketch out... together, at your pace?' Anisa's own ideas fully shape the plan, and she herself confirms the collaborative feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of choice and ownership, including pacing at Anisa's request and explicit reassurance that failure doesn't end the relationship. No imposed advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within — personal reasons like surprising her daughter and 'getting my life back' emerge through open questions. Even a past coping resource (the tea) was rediscovered via evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client ('you're the expert here'), with her knowledge of triggers and supports driving the plan. Strong power-sharing throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions elicit rich change talk ('doable, not easy'), which is then amplified and attributed to the client ('this voice... is yours, not something I've talked you into'). Textbook-level cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and gently redirected toward agency ('even with that fear, you're already problem-solving'). Double-sided reflections keep both sides honored.
