# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout: affirms persistence, normalizes struggle without judgment, and honors the weight loss wish while warmly closing. Respect is consistent and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally deep, including reframing self-blame into 'maybe the diet failed me,' which the client explicitly confirmed. Captures both surface content and underlying fears with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on sustainable, non-diet eating, progressing logically from past failures to values to a concrete first step. Each exchange advances the goal without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session proceeds between equal partners, with the plan emerging entirely from the client (Egyptian recipes, lentil soup) and setbacks framed as 'information for us to look at together.' No expert-driven directives appear.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is consistently reinforced ('you get to let that unfold at your pace,' 'no failing here'), with the client fully in charge of the plan. Falls short of a 5 only because ownership of outcomes could be affirmed more explicitly during moments of doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within the client via open questions that elicited pivotal insights (the gym is 'mine,' the vision of peace around food). The counselor names and amplifies the client's own reasons for change rather than supplying them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions expertise within the client ('You know your life and routines better than anyone,' 'it came from you'). The client's contributions directly shaped the session direction and plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk through targeted questions about sustainability and ownership, reflecting client-generated statements back and securing a concrete commitment (lentil soup this week). Client produced strong self-directed motivation ('maybe easy is the point').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (weight loss wish, fear of spiraling) is acknowledged without reinforcement and gently reframed using double-sided reflections. Point withheld because sustain talk is sometimes acknowledged at length rather than actively pivoted.
