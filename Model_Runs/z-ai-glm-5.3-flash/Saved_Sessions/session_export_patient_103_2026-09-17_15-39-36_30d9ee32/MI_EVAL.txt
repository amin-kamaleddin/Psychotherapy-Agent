# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout despite the client's initial hostility, with no labeling or defensiveness. Accepted rejection of options gracefully.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, reframing reflections captured the client's core grievance ('fed up, not angry') and ambivalence. Reflections occasionally stayed summary-level rather than exploring deeper emotions like fear of job loss or feeling walked over.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained focus on anger, appointment delays, and job impact, converging on a concrete commitment (check-in stamp). Initial broad questions and scattered option-ling briefly diluted focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed choices as joint decisions and respected the client's preferences on handling his boss. Some early suggestions were counselor-generated rather than drawn from the client's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice ('support whatever choice feels right'), accepted partial commitment, and never pressured when options were declined. Client set the pace and scope of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited genuine client-generated change talk through open choices and affirmation. Relied heavily on option menus rather than exploring deeper values and meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly deferred to the client's expertise and accepted his corrections gracefully. Early session leaned toward offering solutions rather than co-creating from the client's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reinforced client-initiated change talk (stamp, phone call) and anchored it to his own goals. Evocation relied mostly on option menus rather than value exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and pivoted toward change without pushing. Minor issue: offered multiple problem-solving options before full rapport, briefly prolonging barrier talk.
