# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and nonjudgmental throughout, repeatedly affirming Isabella's honesty and small steps while normalizing her hesitancy. To reach 5, affirm her worth independent of follow-through given her repeated 'I can't guarantee I'll follow through.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered accurate reflections capturing both content and feeling, such as the fear of being the outsider. Deeper emotional layers (shame about repeated inaction) were only lightly explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on community engagement and moved progressively toward a concrete first step. Falls short of 5 due to a somewhat checklist-driven process not explicitly tied to Isabella's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration—checked in and incorporated Isabella's picks—but the counselor proposed most content, with Isabella mainly agreeing or selecting. Ideas were not co-generated as equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was consistently offered and non-commitment was met with support rather than pressure. Rapid-fire option menus occasionally controlled the pace rather than letting Isabella own the direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence scale and preference questions drew out some of Isabella's own reasoning, but frequent advice-giving and pre-packaged solutions undercut evocation. Missing were open questions about her hopes, prior successes, and reasons connection matters.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for Isabella's preferences and checked in regularly, but leaned into the expert role by supplying all ideas for her to choose from. Little recognition that Isabella holds expertise about her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited modest change talk through the confidence scale and concrete preference questions. Change talk was not deepened with strategic responses like exploring past successes or elaborating her motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify or dwell on Isabella's frequent sustain talk, appropriately pivoting toward next steps. However, softening relied on ignoring it rather than double-sided reflections or reframing.
