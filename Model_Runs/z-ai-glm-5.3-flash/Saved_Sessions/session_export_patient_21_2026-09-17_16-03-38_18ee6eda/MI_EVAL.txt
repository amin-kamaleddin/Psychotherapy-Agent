# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental, respectful throughout, but affirmations are somewhat formulaic and repeated rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but mostly surface-level restatements of content, missing emotional undercurrents such as fear of the all-or-nothing pattern and past burnout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains laser focus on increasing physical activity, progressing logically from readiness through barriers to a concrete plan, implementation details, and contingency planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highly collaborative — asks permission, offers menus, and incorporates the client's modifications — but the counselor tends to drive the structure with lengthy, prescriptive recaps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces client ownership with choices, shrunken plans, backup options, and a self-compassion framing; the client explicitly reports feeling unpressured.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong evocation early via rulers, but the counselor drifts into advice/teaching mode in the second half, supplying alarm instructions and scripted phrasing rather than continuing to elicit the client's ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions the client as decision-maker and her contributions substantially shaped the plan, though options were largely counselor-generated rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of readiness and confidence rulers plus follow-up questions elicited strong client-generated change talk; deeper exploration of the client's own values would elevate this further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and obstacles were proactively addressed, but concerns were met with logistics rather than reframes or double-sided reflections linked to her values.
