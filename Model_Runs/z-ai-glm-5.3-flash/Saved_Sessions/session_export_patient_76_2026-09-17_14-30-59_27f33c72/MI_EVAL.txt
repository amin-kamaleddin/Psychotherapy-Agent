# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance, thanking Liora for her honesty and accepting her refusals without criticism. Affirmations, however, were somewhat generic rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were occasional accurate reflections of exhaustion and skepticism, but reflective listening was limited. The emotional weight of her hopelessness and the powerful 'quiet after cutting' metaphor were never deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on self-harm and safety, with the behavioral experiment giving concrete momentum. Points are deducted for early broad questioning and direction achieved through structured task-giving rather than collaborative goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Liora selected strategies, named warning signs, and the plan was built from her choices, but the counselor supplied most content via pre-formatted templates and scripts. Power-sharing was real but limited, feeling more like guided instruction than joint construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice-offering was consistent and refusals (locking blades, texting Dana) were accepted gracefully without pressure. Falls short of 5 due to formulaic, instructive safety messaging and little affirmation of Liora's ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Liora's own motivations and reasons for change were rarely elicited; most change talk emerged incidentally from the experiment. There were no questions about her values, hopes, or how cutting fits her life, reflecting a tools-over-motivation approach.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some partnership was shown through menu-based choices and incorporating her picks, but the overall style remained expert-driven. Liora selected from pre-built options rather than her expertise shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged ('text her something small', 'cold water plus the walk', urge dropping to 2) and was reinforced, but this occurred through structuring a behavioral experiment rather than deliberately evoking and deepening her own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not argue with or reinforce sustain talk, but strategic softening was minimal. Liora's ambivalence ('I still don't fully believe it'll be enough') received only light acknowledgment rather than being used to build discrepancy.
