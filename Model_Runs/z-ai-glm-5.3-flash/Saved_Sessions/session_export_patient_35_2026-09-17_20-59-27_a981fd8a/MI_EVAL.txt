# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard, especially with the client's defensive minimization. The 'information, not a verdict' reframe directly counters her self-labeling as a failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflections capture both sides of the client's ambivalence and her core fear of failure. Complex reflections appear consistently throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking throughout, with each exchange advancing toward goals and a concrete next step. The closing question about evaluating Thursday provides natural follow-up structure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully co-created plans using plural language ('what if we planned for the stumbles'). The counselor accommodates the client's push-back ('don't get ahead of me'), demonstrating true power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client's pace and boundaries are explicitly honored, with no advice-giving or pressure. The commitment was kept deliberately small at the client's request.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major change idea originates with the client, elicited via values exploration, future projection, and the readiness ruler. The client articulates her own reasons and generates her own follow-through logic.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located within the client ('that's you doing this, not me'). The plan is entirely co-created from her own ideas, exemplifying top-level partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent use of open questions, future projection, elaboration, and a readiness ruler that elicited the client's own reasons for change. Change talk escalates from vague thoughts to specific commitments (the card Thursday, the walk).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally redirected effectively, with skillful reframing of relapse planning. A few reflections briefly restated sustain talk content (the stress, the switch-off feeling) without an explicit pivot toward change.
