### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing core experiences like the evening cigarette as her 'exhale.' Reflections consistently preceded and framed questions, demonstrating genuine understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking cessation with steady strategic momentum from importance to planning to future orientation. No topic drift; summaries consolidated progress and pointed to next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership with every strategy in the final plan originating from the patient. The psychiatrist's role was limited to eliciting, reflecting, and affirming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently declined to prescribe and explicitly located ownership with the patient ('that's yours, not mine'). When she declined a quit date, the psychiatrist honored her reasoning without pushing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated from the premise that motivation resides within the client, eliciting her 'why,' self-defined success metrics, and self-attributed strengths. The prompt 'What is it about you that got you those two weeks?' produced powerful identity-level change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively located expertise within the patient, crediting her for her own strategies and insights. Questions consistently drew out her knowledge, and her contributions demonstrably shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding evocation of change talk through strategic questions about importance, past success, and self-defined wins. Commitment language emerged organically and was reinforced with affirmations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, acknowledging it briefly and reframing toward values and change. Minor deduction for some reflections that restated sustain talk at moderate length, though always validation-oriented.
