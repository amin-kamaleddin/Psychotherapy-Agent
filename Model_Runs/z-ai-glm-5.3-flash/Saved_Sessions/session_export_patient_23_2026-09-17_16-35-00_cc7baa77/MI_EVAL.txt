# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with effective reframing of slips as informative rather than failure. Affirmations felt somewhat formulaic and lacked individualized recognition of Claudia's strengths and insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both sides of Claudia's ambivalence, including her desire to protect her mood while keeping a soothing ritual. Emotional depth was limited, with loneliness and depression-related flatness only acknowledged at the surface level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained unwavering focus on reducing evening alcohol use, progressing logically from exploring ambivalence to a concrete, actionable plan with a scheduled check-in. Even when addressing barriers, every exchange tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was largely co-constructed, with Claudia's tweaks validated and incorporated. However, several turns bundled multiple questions and pre-formed strategy lists, tipping the exchange toward a counselor-led agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered choices rather than directives and accepted Claudia's rejection of goals without pressure. Claudia explicitly confirmed feeling unpressured, noting the plan felt like something she could actually do.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The importance ruler skillfully elicited Claudia's core motivation, but evocation faded once planning began. Most strategies and structures came from the counselor rather than being drawn out of the client, and confidence scaling or questions about her hopes were never asked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** While preferences were sought at key moments, much of the session consisted of counselor-supplied strategy lists and templates. Claudia's own ideas shaped the plan, but the overall posture leaned toward the counselor providing solutions first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The importance ruler and follow-up questions elicited strong change talk, which was then reinforced by tying the plan to mood tracking. Missed opportunities to scale confidence or deliberately reflect change statements kept this from a higher score.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never debated; the counselor acknowledged concerns lightly and pivoted to Claudia's own stated goal. Lacked deliberate reframing of sustain talk into change talk.
