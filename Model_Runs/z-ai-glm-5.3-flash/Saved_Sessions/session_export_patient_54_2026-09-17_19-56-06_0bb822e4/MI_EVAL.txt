### Acceptance

**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing the client's tension and frustration rather than judging her drinking. Warm, individualized affirmations reinforce her identity as an independent, questioning person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflective listening, including complex double-sided reflections capturing both sides of ambivalence. Reflects core values and memorable client language ('the baby's window is only nine months') while adding meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use during pregnancy with no topic drift, progressing logically from exploration to concrete commitment. Guides strategically (evidence review, future vision, first steps this week) while remaining client-paced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly proposes a partnership ('What if we looked at this together?') and lives it—brochure plan, husband conversation, and wedding strategies all co-constructed. Client ideas visibly shape every step of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support ideally matched to this client—affirms decisional ownership, permits dissent, and reframes autonomy as an asset ('your autonomy working for you, not against you'). Uses permissive language and avoids all pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every motivation, plan, and commitment emerges from the client herself, systematically drawn out through skilled questions. The future-visualization question elicited powerful change talk that was then developed into concrete commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions the client as the expert on her own life, and her contributions substantively shape the session's direction. Explicitly attributes the plan to the client ('notice it came from you'), demonstrating genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces desire, ability, and commitment talk through strategic questions, from the future vision to the first step this week. Each affirmation highlights the client's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argues with or amplifies sustain talk, instead using double-sided reflections and inviting skepticism ('full permission to question it'). Honors the legitimate values behind resistance and redirects toward the client's own priorities.
