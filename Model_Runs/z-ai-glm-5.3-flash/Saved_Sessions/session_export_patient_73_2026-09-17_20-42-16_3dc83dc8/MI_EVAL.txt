# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, affirming courage and explicitly decoupling setbacks from judgment ('rough starts are allowed here'). No hint of disapproval or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing ambivalence ('tug-of-war') and the core social challenge. Linda confirms feeling understood: 'Thank you for not lecturing me.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on cessation while progressing from motivation to concrete planning, ending with booked follow-up and specific next steps. Every response advances toward change without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint enterprise — 'we sort it together,' co-constructed decisions and summaries built on Linda's words. Client's closing gratitude confirms the collaborative feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice consistently honored: permission-seeking, permission for imperfection, and decisions attributed to Linda ('only you could make for yourself'). All commitments are self-generated with no pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice-giving or lecturing; open questions draw out her own reasons, insights, and strategies. By session's end she articulates her own motivation, confidence, and self-authored plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Linda as the expert on her own patterns and invites her ideas, which visibly shape the plan. The entire plan (patch, support, week-at-a-time, Margaret, kettle) comes from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk via open questions about motivation, past successes, and vision. Linda's language strengthens from 'maybe someday' to booking a session, buying the patch, and planning to tell Margaret.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and handled with double-sided reflections. Scored 4 only because barriers (patio fears, teasing) were sometimes explored at length before redirecting, slightly extending focus on the status quo.
