# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with genuine affirmations and no pressure against sustain talk. Slips were normalized as 'info, not failure,' though affirmations were occasionally formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured ambivalence and practical concerns but stayed formulaic and surface-level. The emotional layer — Hana's fear of feeling 'left out' with smoking friends — was never explicitly reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on smoking cessation with a concrete action plan from start to finish. However, rapid-fire stacked questions (four in the opening turn) scattered focus and made the pace advice-forward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was largely co-constructed, with Hana choosing swaps, drafting the friend text, and shaping the final plan. Minor power imbalance from counselor-generated 'quick tips' lists that weren't always preceded by eliciting her ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: consistent permission-seeking, option menus, respecting Hana's refusal of NRT, and accepting her stated limits without pressure. Slips framed as 'info, not failure' reinforced ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was effectively evoked via scaling questions and future-oriented prompts, with Hana generating her own reasons and solutions and confidence rising from 6 to 7. Capped at 4 because frequent counselor-generated tips lists partially substituted for elicited content.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana was positioned as the expert on her own life, and her contributions visibly changed the session direction (her message draft became the final plan). The repeated 'quick tips' bursts show the counselor still stepping into advisor mode.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of scaling and menu choices produced multiple commitment statements and raised confidence from 6 to 7. Early stacked questions limited depth of initial change talk cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and reframed toward what Hana could do, without dwelling on barriers or reinforcing reasons against change. Slips were reframed as 'info, not failure,' preventing self-defeating talk.
