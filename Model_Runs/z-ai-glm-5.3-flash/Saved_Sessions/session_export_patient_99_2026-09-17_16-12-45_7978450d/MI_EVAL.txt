# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and non-judgmental with unconditional positive regard ('thanks for coming in,' 'you're making good sense,' 'Nice — that sounds like a realistic, win-focused plan'), even when Matteo minimizes his drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture Matteo's perspective well, but they sometimes stay summative rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning, such as the social anxiety of being 'the weird one with water' or belonging concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, progressive focus on reducing Wednesday drinking throughout — from exploration through rulers, an option menu, a concrete plan, safety resources, and a scheduled two-week follow-up, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed: Matteo's own ideas (alternating drinks, budget, roommate support, no pregaming, eating first) form the core, with the counselor offering a menu rather than prescriptions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported via permission-seeking ('Would it be okay to explore that together?'), offering choices, and letting Matteo set the drink cap, follow-up time, and check-in agenda without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and a decisional balance effectively evoke Matteo's own reasons and strategies, with commitment language emerging ('I'll give the three-drink thing a real shot'), but deeper intrinsic motivation — his personal 'why' beyond the ankle-as-excuse — could have been explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Matteo's expertise is consistently centered and his contributions shape the plan, though the counselor proposes the option menu and plan structure before eliciting Matteo's own ideas about what might work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance and confidence rulers plus the pros/cons exercise elicit self-motivating statements and rising commitment (importance 3–4 to confidence 7), though deeper exploration of the ratings could have strengthened change talk further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced; the counselor reflects neutrally and pivots without arguing, but uses few double-sided reflections and handles peer-pressure concerns practically rather than reframing the underlying ambivalence.
