### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with no criticism of Andrei's minimizing or nonadherence. Affirmations remain generic ('solid plan') and rarely connect to his specific strengths or values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several accurate content-level reflections (readings not urgent, meds didn't help). Misses deeper emotional meaning, such as Andrei's passivity ('I usually just nod along'), his wife's motivating role, and his 'not promising anything' ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays consistently on target behavior with a clear progression from exploration to a concrete, well-sequenced plan. The alcohol component — the key risk factor — gets minimal follow-up, with 'maybe cutting back a beer or two' left undeveloped.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Andrei's preferences shape practical details (Evolv model, phone notes, Thursday 7pm), but the counselor frequently delivers dense advice blocks and pre-packaged plans. Little inquiry into Andrei's own knowledge or prior experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice language is frequent ('Which seems most doable', 'What day and time work best') and Andrei's med refusal is accepted without argument. Choices are often constrained to counselor-generated menus, and his 'not promising anything' about drinking isn't met with explicit respect.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions effectively elicited Andrei's own reasons ('my wife would probably like that', 'I'd rather sort it now'). Much of the session consists of the counselor supplying content rather than drawing out and deepening his motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine power-sharing (coffee machine placement, script on his phone), but the counselor often slips into an expert/advice-dispensing role with unprompted recommendations. Andrei is rarely framed as the expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective scaling and the 'what would move importance from 4 toward 6 or 8' prompt evoked concrete commitments (order cuff tonight, log readings, follow-up Thursday). Confidence rose from 7 to 'seven, maybe eight' and drinking change talk emerged, though reasons for change remain unexplored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged neutrally without arguing, which is good. It is not strategically softened — no double-sided reflections, no reframing of the meds grievance, and 'the beers were never the issue' goes unaddressed at the close.
