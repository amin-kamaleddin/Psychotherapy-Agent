# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations ('That's wonderful — congrats on that shift') and normalizes Elena's adrenaline response. However, warmth is brief and somewhat formulaic, with little individualized affirmation of her deeper efforts or emotional courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections are present ('Offering an alternative really softens the no'), but the psychiatrist rarely reflects Elena's feelings or meanings. Her fear and history of caving under pressure go unexplored, as responses quickly pivot to teaching scripts rather than deepening understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains exceptionally clear focus on boundary-setting aligned with self-respect, with every response advancing the agenda. There is no drift; even the brief safety check-in is handled efficiently and the session returns immediately to the goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative elements — role-play is interactive and Elena's suggestions (alternative offers, Priya as backup) get incorporated. But power-sharing is uneven, with the counselor functioning primarily as an instructor and Elena mostly rehearsing counselor-crafted content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers some choices (which scenario to rehearse, framing techniques as options), but the overall style is prescriptive. Elena's own formulations are quickly 'tightened' into counselor-crafted versions, with little reinforcement that she owns the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor relies heavily on providing scripts and techniques rather than eliciting Elena's own motivations and resources. Her strongest self-motivators (self-respect, not caving under pressure) emerge spontaneously but are never mined through open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-style questions precede exercises, but the session is largely expert-driven, with the counselor supplying nearly all content. Elena's own ideas are acknowledged but not allowed to substantially shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena generates substantial change talk ('I'm trying to make choices that line up with self-respect'), and rehearsal creates opportunities for it. However, the counselor does not deliberately evoke or reinforce it, using generic affirmations instead of targeted evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena shows minimal sustain talk, and the counselor does not reinforce status quo language, responding to doubts with practical tools and encouragement. Guilt and fear of pushback — her core obstacles — are acknowledged only briefly rather than actively reframed.
