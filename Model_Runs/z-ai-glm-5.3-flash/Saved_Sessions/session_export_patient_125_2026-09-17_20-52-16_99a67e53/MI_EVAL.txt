# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated unconditional positive regard with specific, identity-focused affirmations and genuine warmth. No judgment about the infraction or blame toward staff; frustration was normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including double-sided reflections honoring ambivalence. Summaries captured both emotional pain and underlying values, which the client confirmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus and ended each exchange with a forward-moving question, from validation to a concrete first step. A few moments lingered on validating unfairness rather than accelerating toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully equal partnership; ideas offered only as invitations and client contributions shaped the entire plan. Counselor built on each client idea and respected his insider knowledge of the facility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client ownership ('What feels most doable to you?') and highlighted self-agency without pressure or advice. The plan was entirely the client's own construction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key motivations — family, release, mother's pride, becoming 'a different version of me' — came from the client via evocative questions. No lecturing; reasons and commitment were drawn out, culminating in a self-generated plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as expert on his environment ('You know how things run here better than I do'). The entire action plan — letter, Ms. Alvarez, rehearsed script — originated from client input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened commitment language ('I don't wanna mess up my release') through open questions about values and next steps. Reflected and amplified the client's own language rather than supplying it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk (blame, unfairness) without reinforcing it, then reframed toward agency and values. Sustain talk diminished markedly as the session progressed.
