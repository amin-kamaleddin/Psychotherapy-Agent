# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with explicit honoring of the patient's wish to avoid a forced forever quit. Affirmations remained somewhat generic, missing chances to name her specific strengths like her insight about FOMO.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured FOMO and loneliness as drivers of drinking. Deeper emotional exploration of loneliness and fear of abandonment was limited, with reflections sometimes giving way quickly to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent sustained focus on reducing weekday and Friday drinking; every exchange tied back to the concrete plan (driving line, Marisol check-in, alarm). Session closed with a sequenced plan and natural bridge to follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with permission-seeking and role-play the patient requested; her choices shaped the plan. Some exchanges defaulted to counselor-generated option menus rather than eliciting her own ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent explicit support of choice, with every strategy selected, rated, and refined by the patient. No pressure toward abstinence, and her rising confidence and self-authored plan demonstrated genuine ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of confidence scaling and future-visioning drew out strong self-motivating statements ('Saying it out loud makes it real'). Evocation leaned toward confidence/action talk rather than desire, need, and values-based change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The patient clearly drove plan content and was repeatedly asked to select and rate strategies. Counselor occasionally slipped into expert mode with bulleted option lists before her ideas were fully generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent evocation including repeated confidence scaling (5→6→7→8) and forward-looking questions strengthened change talk across the session. Change talk was not always deeply reflected on or developed before moving to the next strategy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (fear of exclusion, 'I'm not an alcoholic') was acknowledged without reinforcement and pivoted toward change. No double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk were used to strengthen strategic softening.
