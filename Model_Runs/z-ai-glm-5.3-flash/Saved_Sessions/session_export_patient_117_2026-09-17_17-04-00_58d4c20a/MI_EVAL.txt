# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and affirming, honoring Samuel's preferences on wording and acknowledgment style. Few explicit affirmations of intrinsic worth beyond achievements limited the depth of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected Samuel's values, triggers, and accountability concerns. Reflections were sometimes brief and summative, moving to problem-solving rather than exploring his fear that 'the urge wins' more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained tight focus on smoking reduction, progressing logically from journal review to a concrete, measurable plan. Occasional drift into logistical details (text wording, follow-up timing) consumed session space.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan and check-in protocol, with many elements (walk, text check-in, no-fanfare) coming from Samuel. The initial plan was counselor-generated with Samuel in a selection rather than generation role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary reinforcement of Samuel's ownership and choice, deferring to his preferences on format, wording, and timing throughout. Advice was offered as options with no pressuring or controlling language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited strong intrinsic motivations (daughter, chest tightness) and used confidence rulers effectively. However, the counselor frequently supplied solutions (gum, breathing) rather than first evoking Samuel's own ideas, missing that gum had already failed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Gave Samuel real influence over check-in format and wording, but often shifted into an expert role early with pre-packaged options. Structured menus positioned Samuel as choosing among counselor-generated ideas rather than generating his own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully connected goals to values and used confidence scaling twice, deepening commitment from 6 to 8. Sometimes answered his own evocative questions and did not always deepen change talk once it emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized slips ('part of the process, not failure') without arguing against doubt. Did not use double-sided reflections and occasionally skipped past ambivalence rather than acknowledging it.
