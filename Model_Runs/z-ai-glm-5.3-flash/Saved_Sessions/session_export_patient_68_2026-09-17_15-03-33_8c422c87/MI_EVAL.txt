# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with explicit non-judgment framing, normalization of ambivalence, and affirmations ('nice work,' 'That's a solid setup'). Minimization and joking were met with acceptance rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections captured both sides of ambivalence throughout. However, emotional layers like Kevin's opening embarrassment and fear of social judgment were acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on drinking behavior flowing logically from exploration to rulers, options, commitment, and rehearsal. Each exchange tightened a concrete plan (cash limit, water between drinks, no rounds, scripted refusals, prebooked cab).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: Kevin's ideas (cash limit, skipping rounds) shaped each next step, and the rehearsal exercise was a joint enterprise. Kevin's active elaborative participation reflected true collaborative energy rather than compliance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategies were offered as a menu for Kevin to choose from, with no pressure or directives. Kevin's ambivalence and self-made rules were accepted and validated, reinforcing his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from Kevin via rulers, 'why that number?', and eliciting his own reasons and risk predictions. However, the counselor supplied many strategy lists and scripted lines prescriptively rather than fully eliciting them from Kevin.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise clearly resided with Kevin, who selected and shaped his own strategies. His comment 'I came in thinking this'd be a lecture. It wasn't' is direct evidence of experienced power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance/confidence rulers with follow-up questions, strategy evaluation, and rehearsal. Kevin's confidence rose from 6 to 7 and his language shifted to clear commitment ('I could actually try this,' 'I'll report back').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly with neutral/double-sided reflections and pivoted toward change without amplification. Some reflections repeated the benefits of drinking verbatim, and building around 'keep the social stuff' was less actively pivoting than ideal.
