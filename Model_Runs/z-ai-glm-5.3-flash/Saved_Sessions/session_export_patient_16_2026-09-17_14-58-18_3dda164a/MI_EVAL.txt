# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental toward a defensive adolescent, with a strong affirming opening. However, warmth and affirmations of Anika's strengths are sparse and somewhat generic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections of Anika's perspective are accurate, including her worry about basketball and FOMO. Reflections tend toward cognitive restatement rather than deeper emotional reflection of ambivalence, peer loyalty, or family stress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior from agenda-setting through closing, progressing logically from values to confidence to triggers to a concrete plan. No drift off-topic, even at wrap-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes permission-seeking and choice among options, with Anika specifying her own reminder texts. But the interaction resembles a checklist, with the counselor generating the if-then templates, stash idea, and exit lines while Anika mostly ratifies them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers menus, honors Anika's rejection of role-play, and accepts seasonal rather than lifelong abstinence. However, the counselor controls nearly all option content and never explicitly affirms that Anika owns the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses confidence rulers with skilled follow-up and elicited genuine change talk ('I play better when I'm not high'). Evocation is somewhat mechanical and front-loaded, shifting to supplying content later in the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and lets Anika choose strategies, but supplies nearly all plan content herself. Anika's own expertise and resources are not leveraged as the source of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk and used the skilled question 'What would help raise that by one point?', with confidence rising from 7 to 8. Strongest change statements were not deepened or reflected back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Repeatedly endorsed sustain talk, affirming 'no need to quit forever' and the detection-avoidance framing, and even suggested stashing the pen. Never gently challenged 'I don't think it's going to hurt me' or developed discrepancy around harm minimization.
