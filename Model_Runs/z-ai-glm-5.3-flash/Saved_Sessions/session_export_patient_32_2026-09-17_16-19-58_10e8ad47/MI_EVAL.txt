# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and respectful without judgment, affirming Derrick's capacity for change. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked explicit normalization of his ambivalence and loneliness struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with a well-crafted double-sided reflection capturing his license goals versus fear of isolation. Rarely reflected the deeper emotional core (shame, grief, fear of being an outcast), moving quickly to action-oriented questions instead.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained laser focus on drinking and driving/DUI prevention throughout every exchange. Session built clear momentum from importance → confidence → concrete plan → commitment → social support.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong joint problem-solving with iterative building on Derrick's ideas and asking rather than prescribing. Occasionally stacked multiple suggestions, and Derrick's closing comment hinted the approach felt like skillful steering at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed choices as Derrick's own, letting him pick options and own the final plan. Occasional edges toward persuasion and praise-then-suggestion slightly limited full client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every strategy originated from Derrick himself, with scaling questions tracking importance and confidence from 6 to 7. His comment that this was 'the most planning I've done about my drinking ever' demonstrates successful evocation of intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely incorporated Derrick's suggestions and accepted his corrections, positioning him as the expert. Rapid multi-question turns occasionally limited how much his contributions steered the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evoked change talk through vision questions, importance/confidence scaling, and deepening Derrick's own strategies and commitments. His language progressively shifted toward change with specific, timed commitments secured.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with minimizations ('just beer,' 'bad luck') and used double-sided reflections to honor sustain talk while amplifying change reasons. Some early reflections repeated his minimization framing, and deeper sustain-talk roots (peer belonging, fear of outcast status) were redirected rather than resolved.
