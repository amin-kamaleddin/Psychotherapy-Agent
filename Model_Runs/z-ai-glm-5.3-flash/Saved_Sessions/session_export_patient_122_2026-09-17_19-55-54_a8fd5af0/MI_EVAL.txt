# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental positive regard with genuine affirmations ('You've figured out harder things than this,' 'That's your insight, not mine'). No labeling or criticism even when the client describes emotional eating and self-critical spirals.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflective listening capturing both content and emotional nuance (the couch trap, self-trust, the guilt-spiral). The client repeatedly builds on the reflections, confirming their accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior — building a sustainable movement routine — while staying connected to the client's values. Each response advances toward a concrete plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functions between genuine equals with no unsolicited advice; decisions are fully co-constructed and summaries hand the material back to the client. Minor moment at the end ('Could that work, you think?') could have been turned back to her own judgment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and choice, never prescribing, persuading, or pressuring. Even accountability planning is framed around what she would want, explicitly locating control within her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation emerges from the client through vision questions, values exploration, and a well-timed confidence ruler; no reasons for change are supplied by the counselor. She ends with strengthened commitment language and a concrete plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is actively located in the client ('You know your life best'), and every plan element originates from her with explicit crediting ('the plan has your fingerprints all over it'). Power sharing meets the top criterion.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation produced rich change talk ('maybe I'd start believing myself again') via open questions, vision exploration, and a confidence ruler that moved her from six to a conditional seven. Textbook MI strategy with measurable commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying sustain talk, briefly acknowledging the couch/stress pattern before pivoting to her problem-solving. Occasionally sustain talk (couch, stress, quitting patterns) is restated fairly explicitly rather than compressed into minimal acknowledgment before redirection.
