# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, honoring Dante's change talk and normalizing imperfection without judgment about his inactivity or failed attempts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflections, most notably reframing the goal from 'the gym' to getting back the dad underneath the exhaustion, which the client immediately confirmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, progressive focus on physical activity, moving from exploring ambivalence through evoking values to a specific behavior, contingency planning, and defining success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative, with the plan co-constructed entirely from Dante's contributions and pivoting to a kid-centered approach based on his own disclosures.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Dante's ownership, letting him define success and attributing the shift to him ('I didn't put it there; you did'), with no directives imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicited motivation from within the client—all motivation (his son, feeling like himself, 'bigger than being tired') came from Dante's own words, supported by skilled use of a confidence ruler.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert through language like 'let's put our heads together like partners' and 'That's your plan, your words,' while deferring to his own resources.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk, with the client's language escalating from desire ('I want that guy back') to commitment ('I'll tell him tonight,' 'I'm ready for Thursday') and a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently pivoted away from sustain talk without dismissing it, reframing barriers toward the client's persistent motivation and inviting collaborative problem-solving so Dante generated his own solutions.
