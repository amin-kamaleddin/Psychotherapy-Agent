# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with worries and missteps consistently normalized. No labeling or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early on that captured both emotion and underlying values. Deeper emotional moments, like 'that's not the mother I want to be,' were skipped over in favor of skill-building.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, concrete focus on the Thursday bedtime conversation, with every exchange tied to that goal. No topic drift; the session built steadily toward a rehearsed script and plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent joint framing with genuinely interactive role-play that the client's feedback directly shaped. The session ended by collaboratively identifying her remaining concern.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and let the client select options, with clear ownership evident in her commitments. Advice was somewhat dense, occasionally edging toward directing rather than purely supporting her own problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew on the client's love for her son ('for him' as a grounding cue) and used open questions to elicit her reasons and resources. Sometimes supplied solutions rather than asking what she thought would work.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned the client as decision-maker, and her contributions substantially shaped the session. Occasionally offered menus of pre-formulated options rather than explicitly inviting her own ideas or naming her as the family expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used confidence scaling effectively, tracking progress from 4 to 7, and elicited commitment language around Thursday. Slight deduction for supplying options rather than letting the client generate her own steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not dwell on sustain talk about the father, instead pivoting toward agency and preparation via the pause-script and role-play. A double-sided reflection validating her fear while highlighting readiness would have strengthened this.
