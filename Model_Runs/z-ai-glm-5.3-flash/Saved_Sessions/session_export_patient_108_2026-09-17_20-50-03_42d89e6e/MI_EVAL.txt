# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout; normalizes Oliver's conflict and reframes his self-criticism without labeling. Falls short of top marks as warmth is occasionally generic and there is little explicit affirmation of his courage or honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures Oliver's ambivalence, including the companion dynamic and loss of space. Some reflections stay at surface content without exploring deeper emotions of guilt, fear, or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains focus on the target behavior — asserting the boundary and asking the flatmate to leave — progressing coherently to a concrete, dated plan. Some turns drift into mirroring rather than advancing toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works with Oliver's own metaphors and ideas (e.g., 'proper handover,' practical kindness) rather than prescribing solutions, making the session feel jointly constructed. Sometimes echoes rather than co-creates and does not explicitly negotiate the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Lets Oliver generate his own script, timeline, and supports, reinforcing ownership through open questions. Few explicit statements affirm his freedom to choose or that the decision is entirely his.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions successfully drew out Oliver's own reasons and commitments. However, evocation is inconsistent — motivation often emerged unprompted, and confidence and values were not systematically explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Oliver as the expert, letting him shape the plan and set the date. Several turns simply paraphrase rather than add collaborative substance, with minimal acknowledgment of Oliver's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evoked strong commitment language ('Monday, I'll talk to him Monday') with a well-timed question about kindness guiding the conversation. However, much change talk was self-generated by Oliver and was not consistently reinforced or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Gently reframes guilt into 'kindness leading the conversation' without amplifying sustain talk. Softening is passive — sustain talk is sometimes restated without pivoting toward change, and double-sided reflections are underused.
