# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard maintained throughout ('whatever you decide, it's okay,' 'no promises needed here'). Falls short of 5 due to slightly formulaic affirmations and an opening reflection that misread Linda's initial hesitation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including effective double-sided reflections and the evocative question about her daughter watching. Some emotional content (e.g., the 'stomach drops' hypocrisy moment) was passed over without explicit reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on smoking and parenting values with forward momentum toward a concrete behavioral experiment. Missing an intermediate summary tying threads together before the close.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing; Linda's contributions visibly shaped the session and she noted the absence of lecturing. Occasionally the clinician's framing pushed an interpretation rather than co-constructing, and the final plan was summarized by the clinician rather than jointly negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced: 'whatever you decide, it's okay,' 'at your pace,' client-generated plan, and acceptance of partial outcomes ('both count'). No persuasive or directive language anywhere.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All change content — values, conditions, plan — was elicited from Linda's own words rather than supplied. Confidence talk was underdeveloped ('I'm not sure I can' was explored as conditions rather than self-efficacy).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook power-sharing: asks permission, defers judgment, and positions expertise within the client ('You know yourself better than anyone here'). Linda's own statements ('I'm not saying never') became pivotal session moments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation, including strategically highlighting 'I'm not saying never' and eliciting commitment-adjacent language ('I'd want her to see me trying'). Missed opportunities to deepen spontaneous change talk (e.g., 'they always would win') and no explicit confidence exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Never confronts sustain talk harshly and pivots well at times ('I'm not saying never'), but often restates sustain talk without tilting toward change, and Linda's rationalizations (smoking outside, 'some moms do worse') were never reframed.
