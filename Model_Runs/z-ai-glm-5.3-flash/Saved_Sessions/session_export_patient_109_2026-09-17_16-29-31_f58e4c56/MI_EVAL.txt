# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, non-judgmental, and adapted to Rafael's directness; affirmations were somewhat repetitive and occasionally felt formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected time and energy barriers and Rafael's insight about scheduling, but stayed surface-level and did not explore the emotional weight of his two years of guilt and self-criticism, disclosed only at the end.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus with no topic drift; every exchange advanced from barriers to a concrete scheduled plan with contingencies, accountability, and reward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed plan — Rafael refined the text wording, set his own backup, reward, and check-in protocol, and reported feeling 'a little lighter,' confirming a joint effort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Rafael's choice, accepting his rejections and refusal to commit beyond this week; occasional rapid-fire prescriptive prompts slightly reduced the sense of open choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and 'raise it by one point' questions drew out Rafael's own solutions and moved his confidence from 7 toward 8; sometimes filled space with preset menus and rarely explored the deeper personal meaning of fitness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rafael's expertise shaped the plan, and he retained ownership of key decisions; supplying ready-made scripts and preset options occasionally pre-empted his own solution-generating.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled use of importance/confidence rulers and the 'raise it by one point' question yielded Rafael's own ideas and spontaneous plans for a second day; missed chances to elicit elaborative change talk about why fitness matters personally.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('it's not really a workout,' 'that's what usually kills these plans') passed largely unaddressed with only brief normalization; the most effective reframe (10 minutes as success) came from Rafael himself rather than strategic counselor reflection.
