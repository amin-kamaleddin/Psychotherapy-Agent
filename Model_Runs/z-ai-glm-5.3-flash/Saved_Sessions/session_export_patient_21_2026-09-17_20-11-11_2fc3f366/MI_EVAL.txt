# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, with warm affirmations like 'That's not small; that's smart' and 'You built it.' Camila's ambivalence and fears are treated as legitimate rather than problems to correct.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses highly accurate complex reflections capturing both sides of her ambivalence, e.g., real things pulling at her versus the careful voice saying don't overdo it. Explicitly validates both perspectives.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains consistent focus on increasing physical activity with each question logically progressing the agenda. The session moves steadily from exploration to a concrete, client-owned plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully egalitarian — the entire plan (calendar checkmarks, husband's support, ten-minute rule) originates from Camila. The counselor's role is facilitative rather than directive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively reinforces autonomy: 'there's no pressure to have it all figured out' and 'What feels like the right next step for you to try first?' Camila designs her own plan and explicitly names feeling free ('the missing day is allowed. That feels freeing').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within via evocative questions like 'What might it mean to you?' Camila articulates her own reasons for change — pride, better sleep, avoiding her mother's path — which the counselor reflects and amplifies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Camila as the expert on her own body and days. Every plan element comes from her, with the counselor amplifying rather than directing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes rich client-generated motivation, producing statements like 'Maybe pacing myself is worth a try' and 'I'm choosing to keep moving, on my own terms.' Reinforces change talk by reflecting it back and deepening it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk without dismissing it, using brief neutral acknowledgments followed by redirection toward client-generated solutions. Sustain talk is never reinforced or dwelled upon.
