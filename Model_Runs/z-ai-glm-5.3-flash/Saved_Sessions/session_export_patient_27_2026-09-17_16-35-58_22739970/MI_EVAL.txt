# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; Dani's secrecy was never judged and her humor was met with warmth. Normalizing statements like 'practicing is smart and really normal' helped Dani feel safe and accepted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captured Dani's core fear of being seen differently. Reflections occasionally stayed at the content level and missed deeper exploration of her fear of being treated as fragile.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained crystal-clear focus on peer disclosure, progressing logically from barriers through confidant selection, rehearsal, timing, and backup plans. Guided steadily toward Dani's goal while honoring her pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint and Dani genuinely co-created her words and plan. However, the counselor sometimes supplied content (scripts, opener, breathing cue) rather than drawing it fully from Dani.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered menus of options and immediately accepted Dani's decisions, including declining further rehearsal. Framed the plan as hers and ended with optional follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely elicited motivation from within—Dani's confidant criteria, framing, timing, and confidence rating all came from her. Points deducted because core scripts and coping strategies were supplied rather than evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently sought Dani's input and preferences, and her contributions genuinely shaped the session (her wording and choices were incorporated). Some scripts were counselor-supplied, keeping it just short of full power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk via the confidence ruler, eliciting Dani's own wording, and affirming commitment language. Dani's change talk grew from tentative ('Maybe I could make it short') to firm ('I can do this').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without amplifying it, validating her fears before pivoting toward action. The 'I've got it' backup line practically softened her fear of being overprotected, though dwell time on fears was slightly long.
