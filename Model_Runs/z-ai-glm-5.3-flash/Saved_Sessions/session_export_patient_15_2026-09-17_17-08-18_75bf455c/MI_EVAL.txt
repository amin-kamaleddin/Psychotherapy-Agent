# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with repeated affirmations of the plan, though praise is somewhat formulaic and focused on compliance rather than individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on, but most responses pivot quickly to advice and logistics; feelings behind minimization and fatigue barriers go unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on BP management throughout, with every exchange tied to a concrete plan (walks, monitor, alcohol test, scheduled follow-up) and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician asks for input at points, but advice blocks dominate and the client mostly gives brief compliant answers; genuine two-way problem-solving appears mainly in the client-generated beer test.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine choices are offered, but heavy advice-giving and prescriptive tips undercut ownership, and the client's compliant responses suggest he is following the clinician's lead.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Only one values question and one readiness ruler; the session quickly shifts to dispensing solutions rather than drawing out the client's own reasons, ideas, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client's choices shaped several elements (walking, beer test, Thursday call), but the psychiatrist repeatedly assumes the expert role and agenda-setting is largely clinician-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk was elicited (wife motivation, readiness of 4, commitment to start tonight, beer test), but opportunities to deepen it—elaborating on values and confidence—were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified and the client's own beer experiment was skillfully enabled, but no double-sided reflections were used and the family-history defense got no targeted response.
