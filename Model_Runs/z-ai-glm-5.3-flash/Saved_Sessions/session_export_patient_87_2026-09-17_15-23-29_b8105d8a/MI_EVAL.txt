# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance maintained throughout with warm affirmations. However, validations were somewhat brief and the emotional weight of Marco's repeated 'failures' received limited explicit acknowledgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening identified Marco's triggers and feelings of being stuck. Reflections remained mostly cognitive/strategic and could go deeper into the discouragement and shame he hinted at.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear, consistent focus and progressed logically from trigger identification to a concrete plan and follow-up. Each exchange advanced the change goal without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative interaction with co-constructed plans and role-play. The psychiatrist occasionally slipped into an instructive mode by supplying multiple ready-made scripts rather than eliciting Marco's phrasing first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was consistently reinforced through menu-based options and open-ended invitations ('which feels most doable', 'what would feel fair?'). Marco's decisions about wording, timing, and follow-up were entirely his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was effectively drawn from Marco via scaling questions and open questions, producing his own plan and insight about perfectionism. Deeper values (kids, longevity) and stronger commitment language could have been elicited rather than focusing on logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing evidenced by offering options and incorporating Marco's own ideas like the compromise script and perfectionism insight. Occasionally the psychiatrist's scripts dominated over Marco's own wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation and reinforcement of change talk, with confidence rising from six to eight and commitment language emerging ('I'll try it tonight'). Opportunities to deepen change talk around values were somewhat under-explored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about barriers was acknowledged and redirected toward concrete, doable steps with coping plans. Barriers were revisited repeatedly, keeping some focus on obstacles, though always paired with solutions.
