# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone maintained, but sparse warmth and no explicit affirmations of Anika's honesty or strengths (e.g., her self-awareness about playing better sober).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; key emotional disclosures like stress triggers, fear of slips, and confidentiality worries receive checklists and advice rather than deep reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, consistent focus on the target behavior (THC vaping) and its connection to basketball, with only brief drift; however, the agenda is checklist-driven rather than following the patient's evolving priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is markedly unequal, dominated by counselor-drafted numbered plans and scripts; Anika's contributions are absorbed only briefly before returning to pre-set agendas, and her confidentiality question goes unaddressed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Periodic choices are offered and respected, but a prescriptive, advice-heavy style and directive language (e.g., 'Pick a goal and I'll draft it') undermine explicit support for Anika's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation emerges from the client despite, not because of, counselor technique; few open questions, no probing of her 7/10 confidence, and no use of her own words to build intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role with numbered plans and pre-formulated options, with little acknowledgment that Anika holds the expertise about her own life and triggers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong change talk emerges largely spontaneously from Anika rather than through skillful evocation, and the counselor fails to reinforce or deepen it (e.g., no exploration of 'zero is smarter for my game' or the 7/10 confidence).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is neither amplified nor argued against, but active softening techniques (double-sided reflections, linking to her values) are absent, and direct information-giving risks counter-argument.
