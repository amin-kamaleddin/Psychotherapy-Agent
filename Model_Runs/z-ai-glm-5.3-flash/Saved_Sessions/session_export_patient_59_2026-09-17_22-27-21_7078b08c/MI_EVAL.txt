# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, even when Jamal was dismissive or resistant. Validations were genuine and tailored to his values, never pathologizing his peer group.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captured Jamal's frustration with the system, attachment to his friends, and ambivalence about group leadership. Jamal progressively opened up, evidence the reflections landed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear arc from precontemplation to a self-authored action plan ('feel it, say nah, keep it moving') with no aimless drift. Every question built strategically toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used inclusive, curiosity-driven language and adapted when Jamal pushed back rather than pushing harder. The final plan was fully co-constructed from Jamal's own experiences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session explicitly framed as Jamal's choice, with repeated disclaimers of imposing ('I'm not here to trash your crew'). His ending commitment felt self-chosen rather than coerced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight originated from Jamal - the 'solid vs. parent' framing, his internal warning signal, and his exit plan. Exceptional evocation given his precontemplative, resistant presentation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly deferred to Jamal's expertise ('you know them way better than I do') and credited every insight as his own. Power sharing was genuine and visibly shaped the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions evoked notable change talk ('that's just being solid, not being the parent') and a concrete self-generated plan. Change talk was consistently reflected and reinforced with attribution to the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with double-sided reflections rather than argument, acknowledging it while pivoting toward change. Validation of unfairness was paired with gentle redirection to what Jamal could control.
