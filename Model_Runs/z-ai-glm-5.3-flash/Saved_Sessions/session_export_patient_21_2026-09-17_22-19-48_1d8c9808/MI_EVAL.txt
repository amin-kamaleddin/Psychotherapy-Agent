# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout; sustain talk about past failures is normalized rather than criticized. The client explicitly notes feeling accepted ('Makes me feel like this is actually my plan').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections capture ambivalence and emotional nuance precisely ('Hopeful with butterflies,' 'a gentle kind of accountability'). Reflections deepen the conversation rather than parrot it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with no topic drift. Each exchange builds progressively from exploring hope to a concrete, achievable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; the psychiatrist invites input at every turn and genuinely incorporates it, even designing the follow-up structure around the client's preference. The client experiences the relationship as egalitarian.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice is consistently reinforced, with all ideas originating from the client and no advice imposed. The client confirms: 'I'm doing this for me, not just because my doctor said so.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight and motivation is client-generated, from the 'start small' strategy to being around for the grandkids. The psychiatrist never lectures or supplies reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is explicitly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert on your own life,' 'you're the one steering'). The plan, check-in structure, and focus were all client-generated and credited as such.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully elicit rich change talk, which is strategically reflected back and reinforced ('I hear real ownership in your voice'). Culminates in concrete commitments: two short walks and a conversation with her partner.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never amplified, and the double-sided reflection on swimming ambivalence is a highlight. However, sustain talk was occasionally acknowledged without an explicit strategic pivot or affirmation of her capacity to overcome it.
