# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with genuine, individualized affirmations and no judgment. Warmth is especially evident in the closing reflection about the ache and steadiness of Elena's self-kindness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally deep, tracking both content and feeling (e.g., 'I'm telling myself I'm worth the effort,' 'the plan bends, it doesn't break'). Emotional texture is consistently captured and named.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus from balance to cooking evenings, concrete planning, flexibility, and generalization. Each turn advances momentum without feeling pushed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: agenda emerges jointly, questions rather than directives are used throughout, and summaries weave Elena's language into shared understanding. Elena drives the content while the counselor shapes process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena's ownership is consistently reinforced ('you'd know best,' 'that momentum is coming from you'). No advice is imposed; every plan element originates from her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within Elena through open questions ('what would balance look like through your eyes?'). Key insights — worth the effort, plan bends not breaks, self-trust — are all self-generated and reflected back.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located within Elena, whose contributions visibly shape the session direction. The cooking goal, schedule, and flexibility plan all come from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and explicitly highlighted ('less like wishing and more like deciding'). Elena's language grows stronger each turn, culminating in 'I want to keep this going... it feels like mine.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Obstacles are acknowledged accurately without amplification and pivoted to Elena's own solutions. Note: very little sustain talk was present, so skill in handling stronger resistance remains untested.
