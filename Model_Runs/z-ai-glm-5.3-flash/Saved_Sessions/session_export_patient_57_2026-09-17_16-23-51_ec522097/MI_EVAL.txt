# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental, and explicit confidentiality assurances build trust with this reluctant teen. However, praise is somewhat generic and repetitive ('Nice work'), and normalization of peer pressure is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture Jamal's core ambivalence between team belonging and protecting his season. Reflections occasionally stay at content level, missing deeper emotional themes like loneliness and vulnerability about plans falling apart.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior (alcohol at team parties) with strategically sequenced questions building toward a concrete, fully specified action plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine teamwork in which Jamal authored nearly every element of the plan—signal, refusal lines, text draft, backup plan. His surprise that the session wasn't a lecture confirms the collaborative experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames actions as Jamal's choices ('Which one feels more like you?'), yielding strong client ownership. Some turns pack prescriptive checklists of tips that slightly dilute full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful elicitation of motivation from within via readiness/confidence rulers and client-generated plans. Confidence rose from 5 to 8 within one session, with spontaneous self-motivational statements throughout.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client expertise is central—Jamal drafted the text, chose his own lines, and built the plan from his ideas. Occasional multi-option advice lists lean toward prescribing rather than pure power-sharing, though delivered with permission-seeking.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional use of rulers, forward-looking questions, rehearsal, and commitment language producing rich change talk. Confidence progressing from 5 to 8 in one session demonstrates outstanding evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally responds to sustain talk with neutral double-sided reflections and pivots to what matters most. Some sustain talk (fear of exposure, party chaos) passes without explicit affirmation-and-redirect.
