# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with warm, culturally attuned affirmations. Sustain talk was validated rather than judged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex, accurate reflections captured both sides of ambivalence and the emotional core of disclosures. Every reflection deepened the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear strategic focus on drinking with logical session progression, but the violence/aggression target behavior was only addressed indirectly. Minor conversational drift around the pride discussion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully two-way; each intervention built on Luka's prior answers and his contributions shaped the session's direction. No expert-driven steering.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decision-making was repeatedly and explicitly handed back to Luka, with no pressure toward abstinence. His partial-abstinence stance was respected throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent use of open questions to elicit success stories, desire, and anticipated pride, with change talk then developed and strengthened. Luka ended with his own reasons, plan, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Luka was positioned as the expert and the entire relapse-prevention plan originated from him. No advice or directives were offered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook use of looking forward, evoking gains, querying autonomy, and confidence imagery. Strongest change statements were selectively reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Double-sided reflections effectively pivoted sustain talk toward change and ambivalence was redirected well. One exchange dwelt slightly long on the 'giving something up' loss frame before pivoting.
