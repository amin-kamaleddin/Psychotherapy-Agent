### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard: opened with 'no judgment,' respected Aisha's alcohol boundary, and normalized slips without harsh judgment throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflections capturing her evening pattern, protector role, and minimization ('compared to people at work'), though the emotional weight of her aunt's cancer death was acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained tight focus on evening smoking reduction, progressing purposefully from exploration to a specific goal (max 3), strategies, Monday start, tracking, contingency planning, and a defined follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Aisha selecting strategies, tweaking it, and negotiating the follow-up format. Some option menus felt counselor-generated before her ideas were invited, creating slightly uneven idea generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were offered at every step; her refusals of alcohol and NRT were respected, and she ended owning the plan entirely, even planning to raise alcohol herself next visit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within via rulers, open questions, and her self-generated reasons, mantra ('Three is enough, my son needs me here'), and client-initiated accountability check-in.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Aisha was repeatedly positioned as the expert and her contributions substantially shaped the plan. Occasional pre-packaged option menus slightly limited the fully collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled use of importance/confidence rulers and open questions grew her change talk from 'maybe just... less' to a concrete plan, self-authored mantra, and accountability commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
