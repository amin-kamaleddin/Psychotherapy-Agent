# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Lars's literacy struggles and affirming his strengths. However, affirmations were brief and sometimes formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captured ambivalence and shame effectively. Deeper emotional layers — the 'everyone can do it except me' feeling and the Friday-night conflict with mates — went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the concrete goal of completing the mechanics course form, narrowing from vague worries to a specific time-bound plan (7pm start, fridge, timer, 6:45 check-in). Excellent directional skill.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Lars's own ideas shaping key elements. The counselor sometimes presented ready-made solutions first and sought agreement second rather than eliciting ideas beforehand.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and honored Lars's preferences throughout. The prescriptive 'quick tricks' list slightly narrowed perceived options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Lars's own reasons and solutions, culminating in his articulation of deeper motivators (career, Donna, being a different dad). Some motivation relied on counselor-supplied strategies rather than pure evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supportive, but the counselor occasionally slid into an expert/advice-giving role, delivering the 'quick tricks' list without first asking Lars's ideas. His own contributions did shape the plan, but solutions were often packaged rather than co-discovered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified commitment language via interest questions and confidence scaling (5→8). Moving more slowly to logistics to deepen spontaneous change talk would strengthen this.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responded to sustain talk with validation and quick pivots to workable alternatives, and reframed barriers as goal-protection. Lacked deliberate double-sided reflections to more strategically resolve ambivalence.
