# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard was maintained even during minimization and deflection, with no labeling, shaming, or disapproval. The clinician validated the client's attachment to the ritual and affirmed his agency throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was strong and accurate, capturing both rationalizations and emotional undercurrents like the 'ten minutes of peace.' Reflections stayed largely cognitive, and the deeper fear beneath Ahmed's resistance was named once but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained unwavering focus on cigar use and oral health from first question to closing summary, progressing logically from risk perception to a concrete plan. The interviewer honored the request for information and used it as a bridge back to the change goal with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Agenda-setting was openly negotiated and the plan was co-constructed entirely from Ahmed's own ideas. Summaries integrated the client's words, and his job-site analogy showed genuine ownership of the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was actively reinforced at every decision point, with conditional, self-paced change explicitly supported. Ahmed's surprise at receiving his own plan rather than a lecture confirms the autonomy-supportive stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited from within rather than imposed, with questions drawing out Ahmed's own reasons and confidence. His shift from skepticism to commitment was generated through his own reasoning, demonstrating masterful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located within the client, with Ahmed interpreting his own situation and the plan explicitly attributed to him. The client's own statement — 'it's my plan, not something forced on me' — directly evidences successful power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked via a pivotal hypothetical, then progressively developed into a self-generated plan and commitment language. Ahmed's self-motivational summary statements reflect successful, cumulative evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and skillfully pivoted without reinforcement. Occasionally sustain talk was re-reflected at length (buddy example, 'not agreeing to anything') before redirection, giving resistance slightly more airtime than optimal.
