### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental regard throughout; Irina's fear of being labeled a bad mom was validated and explicitly reframed as strength.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections, including a skilled double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence and naming her guilt and CPS fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on alcohol use and stress coping built steadily toward a concrete, trackable two-week experiment with a follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was fully co-constructed — Irina proposed the childcare referral, the one-drink test, tracking, and involving her mother.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice affirmed at every step, including asking permission before providing information and honoring her refusal to make promises.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn out from Irina herself, with the psychiatrist consistently attributing insights to her rather than supplying them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert; her contributions shaped the agenda and plan, and she noted 'you didn't lecture me... you just listened.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic open questions evoked progressively stronger change talk, which was reflected back and amplified, leading to her self-generated experiment and commitment.
