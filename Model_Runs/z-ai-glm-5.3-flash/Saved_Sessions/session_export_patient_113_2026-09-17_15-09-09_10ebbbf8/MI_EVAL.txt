# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained consistent respect and non-judgment despite blunt resistance, normalizing Rowan's frustration and affirming his choices. Warmth was somewhat brief and formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected Rowan's irritation, time pressures, and ambivalence, including holding his uncertainty about risk. Reflections were occasionally brief and moved quickly to options rather than deepening the emotional experience of feeling controlled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior from the second turn onward, progressing logically from exploring annoyance to a concrete, measurable plan. The session never wandered and ended with well-defined logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative: the counselor checked in before each step and incorporated Rowan's schedule, alarm, note format, and check-in preferences. The initial 3-day experiment structure was counselor-proposed rather than jointly generated, though Rowan shaped all its details.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: control was explicitly handed to Rowan repeatedly ('you're in charge,' 'your call,' 'stop anytime'), with genuine menu options offered. The final plan was built entirely on his terms and routines.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Rowan rather than lecturing, eliciting his own criteria for continuing or stopping. The structured experiment framing supplied some motivation, and deeper exploration of his values (work, independence) was touched but not developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Rowan as the expert, and his contributions substantially shaped the plan (breakfast timing, phone alarm, morning check-in). The core plan structure was still supplied by the counselor with limited explicit acknowledgment of his health expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and built on change talk, with Rowan moving from refusal to 'I'll do the three days' in his own words. The strategic question about what he'd notice elicited meaningful commitment tied to alerts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it, using double-sided reflections and validating rather than arguing against rationalizations. A few turns briefly repeated sustain content (e.g., 'keep not charging and accept the risk'), keeping a door open to status quo talk.
