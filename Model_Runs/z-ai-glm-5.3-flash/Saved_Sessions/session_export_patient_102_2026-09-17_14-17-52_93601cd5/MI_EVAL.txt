# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone without moralizing despite the referral context. However, affirmations were generic and rarely highlighted Milan's specific strengths or honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate, but empathy faded as the session became rapid-fire advice-giving. Milan's feelings about being judged and reported were never deeply explored or revisited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifted from the core target (under-aged binge drinking) into gossip management and refusal scripts. The drinking behavior itself was never squarely explored in terms of frequency, context, or Milan's ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration existed — permission-seeking and Milan shaping the refusal line. But the counselor generated nearly all content while Milan merely selected or tweaked it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and avoided coercion, but the heavy stream of unsolicited advice implied the counselor held the answers. Milan's disclaimers about not quitting were never affirmed as his right to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was supplied via rationales, tips, and scripts rather than drawn from Milan. His exam and playoff priorities — natural intrinsic motivators — were converted into an excuse rather than explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor quickly assumed the expert role with menus of options and pre-drafted scripts. Choosing from menus is not co-creation, and the plan was counselor-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no deliberate evocation of Milan's own reasons for change. His faint change language ('I'll give it a shot next party') went unreinforced and was buried under more scripted lines.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was frequent and persistent but was met mostly with advice-giving rather than double-sided reflections. Ambivalence was never worked with directly through his competing concerns like exams and playoffs.
