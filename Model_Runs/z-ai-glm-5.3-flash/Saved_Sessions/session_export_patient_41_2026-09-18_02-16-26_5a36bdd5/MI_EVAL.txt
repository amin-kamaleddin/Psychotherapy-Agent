# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard precisely when the patient is most self-critical, reframing 'sounds bad, huh?' without generic reassurance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reflects and validates the patient's experience, e.g., 'That push-pull must be exhausting' and echoing 'ten minutes— that feels less scary.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on follow-through and avoidance, guiding each exchange toward concrete, patient-generated steps without pulling off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites the patient's own answers ('what do you think...?') and affirms self-generated solutions rather than offering advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly reinforces the patient's ownership, e.g., 'That's you finding your own answer, not borrowing mine' and 'designing a system that works with who you are.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws out the patient's own change talk and insights—the 'too small to fail' idea, the permission note phrasing—all generated by the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shared decision-making throughout; the plan (ten minutes, roommate, note) is co-developed at the patient's pace with her words.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilfully highlights and elaborates change talk ('I could probably manage that') and closes with the patient verbalizing hope and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Non-judgmentally reframes self-critical and avoidant statements ('usually' not 'always') so sustain talk is met with regard, not confrontation.
