# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance throughout, especially around slips and financial constraints ('Notice, not judge, and try again'). Affirmations are brief and procedural, lacking deeper individualized statements of regard for her as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captures her stress and strain, e.g., the exhaustion of missed work and class. Reflections stay at surface-level stress, with less exploration of deeper emotions around family distrust and fear of failing again.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, coherent focus moving from childcare to smoking/alcohol reduction with concrete steps and clearly summarized weekly goals. Every exchange advances target behaviors without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent check-ins and co-construction; Daniela's ideas substantially shaped the plan (meal trade, texting, delay strategy). Collaboration is occasionally undermined by rapid expert-style delivery of scripts and checklists.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers structured choices and reinforces Daniela's ownership of the plan. Falls short when the counselor supplies prescriptive plans (timers, alarms, sticky notes) rather than supporting her self-generated approaches.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open questions drew out her own reasons and plans, but much of the session's substance comes from counselor suggestions. No exploration of her deeper 'why,' no confidence/importance rulers, and little values development.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and offers choices, but the session increasingly shifts into advice-driven mode with scripts, document lists, and pre-made plans. Client contributions shape the plan, yet the overall direction feels expert-supplied.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits and reinforces change talk, prompting Daniela to articulate desires and plans (Head Start, meal trade, 'little win for the day'). Missing deeper commitment language and confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids amplifying barriers, pivoting from cost concerns to solutions and reframing fear of failure with 'Notice, not judge.' Some sustain talk is passed over quickly rather than actively reflected and reframed.
