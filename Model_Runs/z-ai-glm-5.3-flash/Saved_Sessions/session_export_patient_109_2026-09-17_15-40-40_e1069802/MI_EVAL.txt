# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; consistent affirmations and normalization of ambivalence without impatience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, integrated reflections captured both surface content and deeper meaning (tiredness, awkwardness, fear of letting himself down).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight, purposeful focus on returning to exercise; every turn advanced the agenda with forward-moving questions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Patient's contributions genuinely shaped the plan, but the counselor occasionally supplied content (e.g., detailed workout template) rather than co-creating it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was consistently preserved ('if any'), refusals were honored without pushing, and ownership was reinforced by framing showing up as success.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation of the patient's own reasons and coping ideas, though occasionally supplying options rather than drawing out his own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Patient's answers determined plan specifics, but the counselor sometimes assumed the expert role with a prescribed workout design.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation — scaling questions, doable-step choices, and success definition escalated commitment from 'maybe' to 'I can do this Tuesday.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and quickly pivoted to confidence-building, though more active reframing of past failures (e.g., as persistence) would strengthen this.
