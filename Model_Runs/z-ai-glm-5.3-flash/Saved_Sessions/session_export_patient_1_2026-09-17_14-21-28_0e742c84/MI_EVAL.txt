# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and never critical despite Ahmed's dismissiveness, but warmth is minimal; responses are transactional checklists with little genuine positive regard or affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; responses are question batteries and advice lists, and Ahmed's dust theory and sense of being judged are never genuinely explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on mini-cigar use and oral health with a concrete, testable plan, though focus is held somewhat rigidly at the expense of responsiveness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The plan is clinician-designed and delivered pre-formed, then recapped verbatim multiple times despite Ahmed's explicit protests, showing little adjustment to the client as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The two-week test frame and cautious reintroduction option respect Ahmed's terms, but communication is mostly directive and prescriptive with clinician-designed menus rather than client-generated choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies on information-giving and persuasion rather than drawing out Ahmed's own motivations; virtually no open questions exploring his values, reasons, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently assumes the expert role, diagnosing and prescribing while Ahmed's expertise and contributions are acknowledged only superficially; the plan originates almost entirely from the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The test-stop frame successfully elicited commitment language ('no cigars at all, I promise'), but these openings are never deepened through reflection, affirmation, or amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and facts/reframes replace argument, but softening is mostly informational rather than strategic, with repeated safety recaps feeling corrective rather than collaborative.
