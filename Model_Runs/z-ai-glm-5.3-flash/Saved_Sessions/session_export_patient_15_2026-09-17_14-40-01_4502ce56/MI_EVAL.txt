# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, respecting Andrei's perspective even when he minimizes his drinking. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and lack individualized depth, such as his prior success quitting smoking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capturing Andrei's minimizing and data-driven curiosity. Emotional layers (wife's concern, brother dynamics, habit attachment) are only superficially acknowledged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on hypertension and alcohol reduction with no topic drift. Every exchange ties back to the target, moving from ambivalence to a concrete, measurable plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Andrei choosing readings, involving his wife, and designing the Friday exception. Counselor's preset options and frequent recaps somewhat limit full power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and accepted Andrei's refusals and modifications without pushback. Sometimes answered own questions by supplying menus rather than trusting Andrei to generate ideas first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions elicited genuine change talk, but much content (trial idea, tips, wife script, confidence menu) was counselor-supplied. Little exploration of Andrei's own values, reasons, or hopes beyond the numbers experiment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission before advising, but the expertise balance tips toward the counselor, who proposes most steps, plans, and even scripts for the wife. Andrei could be more explicitly positioned as the expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance and confidence rulers elicited a self-designed experiment and client-owned confidence boosters. Slightly limited by the counselor offering most solutions rather than consistently drawing them out.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was reflected neutrally rather than argued with, and the self-data experiment effectively reframed the discussion. Minor deduction for psychoeducation that engaged somewhat directly with the minimization.
