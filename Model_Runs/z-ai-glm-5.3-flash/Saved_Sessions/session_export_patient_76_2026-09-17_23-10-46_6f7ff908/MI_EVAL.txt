# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental regard ('you couldn't let me down either way,' 'I'm not going anywhere') affirms Liora's worth independent of outcomes. Falls short of 5 because some affirmations feel somewhat formulaic and don't fully engage the deeper shame about letting others down.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately mirrors her dissociative experience and ambivalence ('part of you protecting yourself... part of you willing to find out'). Limited reflection of deeper emotional pain — guilt and shame about her roommates and doctor — which is touched only late in the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on finding an alternative coping strategy, moving logically from warning signs to a concrete ice experiment and self-monitoring plan. Focus drifts briefly at times, and a safety plan for when ice is insufficient is never addressed despite self-harm severity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is genuinely between equals: options are offered rather than prescriptions, and every step (ice, self-tracking) is co-constructed from Liora's own words. The counselor consistently hands the agenda back to her, letting her knowledge drive the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is repeatedly reinforced: 'I'm not promising anything' is honored with 'you couldn't let me down either way,' and choices are offered without pressure. Even failure is reframed as 'just information, not a letdown,' leaving full ownership with the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within — open questions evoke her own insight that she's 'chasing the interruption' and that cutting 'costs more afterward.' Falls short of 5 because hope and deeper reasons for change beyond curiosity are not explored further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Liora is explicitly positioned as the expert ('You know that experience from the inside in a way I never will'), and her contributions demonstrably shape the session. The ice experiment and tracking plan originate from her, and the counselor builds directly on each.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully develops emergent change talk, highlighting her shift from 'nothing else has been tried' to 'ice is already in my freezer' and moving her toward planning language. Misses a 5 because commitment and confidence language is not consistently deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is neither argued with nor reinforced; instead it's reframed as openness and pivoted toward her own words that cutting 'costs more afterward.' Double-sided reflections soften ambivalence, but sustain talk is mostly tolerated rather than actively and repeatedly redirected.
