# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates frustration rather than confronting. Affirmations are somewhat generic ('Smart picks,' 'Solid plan') and lack individualized recognition of Ryan's strengths and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening capturing the social pressure and pacing struggles. Reflections lean cognitive/strategic, missing deeper emotional validation of FOMO and social anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus and progresses logically from incident to a concrete, time-bound plan with built-in follow-up. Direction is exemplary without becoming coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative — every recommendation offered with permission and Ryan's choices drive all decisions. Ryan's closing comment that the plan 'feels like mine' provides direct evidence of successful collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan consistently framed as Ryan's choice, and his rejection of techniques is accepted without pushback. Middle sections rely on dense clinician-supplied option lists with a subtle expert flavor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ryan's own motivations (scholarship, grades, avoiding conduct meetings) are elicited rather than imposed, with rulers drawing out internal resources. Readiness/confidence rose from 5 to 8.5, demonstrating highly effective evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ryan is treated as the expert on his own life, co-authoring strategies and the roommate text. Score held at 4 due to mid-session shifts into structured advice-giving that momentarily shifted authority away from Ryan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent evocation of change talk using rulers and follow-up questions that produced measurable movement. Ryan delivers strong self-generated commitment language by session end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (minimizing, FOMO) is acknowledged without reinforcement and redirected toward solutions. However, sustain talk is never explicitly linked to Ryan's values to build discrepancy, leaving some ambivalence unaddressed.
