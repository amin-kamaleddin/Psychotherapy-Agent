# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently shows unconditional positive regard, acknowledging Luis's reluctance without judgment ('Thanks for coming in, even if it wasn't exactly your idea') and adapting tone at his request.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both sides of Luis's ambivalence (e.g., a full life vs. reading up on asthma), and Luis's repeated 'Yeah, I get what you're saying' confirms he feels understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session moves logically from ambivalence to a concrete commitment (morning inhaler), with every exchange tied to the target behavior and self-defined success criteria (the hill).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interview operates between equals, with Luis's lived expertise (warehouse, coaching, the hill) supplying the session content and shaping the final plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively fostered throughout — the plan is explicitly 'yours,' 'your call,' and the counselor accepts Luis's limited commitment ('one thing, nothing else') without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from the client — fear of 'playing with fire,' pride in commitment, coaching the kids — culminating in self-generated commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly locates expertise in Luis and hands him genuine authority ('If you were in charge of designing your own plan...'), with his answers driving the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and strategically reinforced, progressing from 'playing with fire' to an explicit commitment: 'one inhaler, mornings, my call, for the kids.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sarcasm and minimization are met with reflections and redirection rather than argument, with sustain talk briefly acknowledged and used as a doorway to change talk.
