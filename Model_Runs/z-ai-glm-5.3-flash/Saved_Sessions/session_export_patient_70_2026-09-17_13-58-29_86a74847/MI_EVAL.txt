# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Neutral, non-judgmental tone throughout, but warmth is formulaic ('Nice — you've got this', 'well done') with no individualized affirmation of Lars's courage in disclosing his conviction or literacy struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; emotional disclosures (fear of his record, literacy embarrassment, shame) were met with checklists and drafts rather than reflections of his feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on Friday-night offending risk, progressing logically from gym booking to refusal texts to the polytech application with no aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Cooperative work on booking and form-filling, but the counselor supplies content and Lars mainly approves; his own expertise shaped only the final drafts rather than the overall approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and honors Lars's decisions, but options are constrained to counselor-built menus rather than open exploration or emphasizing his ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Style is predominantly advice-giving; Lars's motivations (Donna, fatherhood, cars, fear of prison) surfaced spontaneously but were never explored or amplified through open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing ('Which of those feels doable?'), but the structure is expert-driven with Lars choosing from pre-made options rather than co-creating solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lars volunteered strong change talk ('I don't wanna end up back in prison') but the counselor briefly affirmed without eliciting, deepening, or reflecting it back to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and is occasionally redirected, but there is no strategic softening — statements like 'I'm not cutting the mates off' received no double-sided reflections or reframes, just a move to action steps.
