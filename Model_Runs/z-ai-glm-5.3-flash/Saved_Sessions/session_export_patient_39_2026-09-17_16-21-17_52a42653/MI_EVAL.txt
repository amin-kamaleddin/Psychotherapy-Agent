# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout with specific, genuine affirmations. Normalized discouragement and adapted to the client's vision and memory limitations (large-letter checklist).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections including the complex reflection on the 'half of you' identity concern. Occasionally moved to problem-solving rather than lingering in the emotional pain of feared failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on increasing physical activity within medical limits, from barriers through rulers to a concrete written plan. No topic drift; even the group discussion was tied back to the exercise plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every element of the plan was co-constructed through menus of options the client selected and personalized. The client's explicit contrast with 'doctor's orders' confirmed equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options without pressure and respected the client's 'no' (declining rehearsal, declining the Marta line). The client's closing that the plan 'feels like my own' demonstrates successful autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the client's own values, solutions, words, and success criteria using rulers elicitation. The arc from ambivalence to 'I feel like maybe I can do this' evidences effective evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client authored his own script, cue, and definition of a win, with the psychiatrist explicitly deferring to his judgment. The client's 'Feels like my own somehow' confirms power-sharing succeeded.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance and confidence rulers plus the 'boost it by one point' question produced progressively stronger change talk and commitment. Confidence rose from 5 to 8 with client-generated plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged without reinforcement and redirected toward change, with the 'half a man' concern gently reframed via belonging. Occasionally pivoted to planning past pain-related discouragement a bit quickly.
