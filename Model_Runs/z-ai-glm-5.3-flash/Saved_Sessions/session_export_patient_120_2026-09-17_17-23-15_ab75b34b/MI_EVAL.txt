# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates respect and nonjudgment toward Sofia's disclosure without shaming; however, affirmations of her strengths are sparse and the emotional weight of her self-blame is not deeply validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some accurate reflections, but responds to emotionally rich disclosures (fear of loneliness, losing her identity, her mother's judgment) with advice lists and reassurance rather than deep reflections of the feelings underneath.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior throughout, with every exchange advancing a concrete behavioral sequence from refusal practice to disclosure planning, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Sofia's preferences and adjusts plans, but the counselor acts as the expert supplying scripts and strategies, making Sofia a selector rather than a co-creator of solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames choices as Sofia's and respects her edits, but the pervasive menu-offering style subtly steers her toward counselor-preselected solutions rather than eliciting her own ideas first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some of Sofia's strongest motivation emerges in her own words, but most session momentum is counselor-supplied; Sofia's direct question about choosing between friends and her baby was answered rather than turned back to elicit her own reasoning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly asks for Sofia's input on options, but supplies nearly all content herself; notably, Sofia's own text draft was stronger and more personal, yet the counselor replaced rather than built on it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens with a good evocative question that elicits change talk ('I don't want to let my kid down'), but misses chances to deepen or consolidate it—no scaling, reflection of determination, or summarizing of Sofia's own commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk reasonably well with reassurance and reframing, but does not use double-sided reflections to pair ambivalence or link Sofia's fears back to her own stated motivations.
