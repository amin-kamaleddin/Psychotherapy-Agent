# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with good validation of frustration. However, affirmations were sparse and missed opportunities to affirm Tariq's months of good compliance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One solid empathic reflection early on, but reflective listening largely disappeared afterward. Key emotional disclosures — his absent father, fear of seeming 'soft,' anxiety about staff — went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed well focused on the phone privilege, the call to his mother, and finishing strong until release. Focus was strong but maintained through task planning rather than connecting to values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements like offering choices and joint role-plays, but the counselor drafted documents unilaterally and delivered checklists. Interaction resembled coaching through a prepared plan rather than equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some choice in format, but quickly became prescriptive with exact scripts and breathing ratios. Tariq's agency was respected in form but not substantively fostered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all content came from the counselor, with open-ended evocative questions almost absent. Tariq's motivations emerged despite, not because of, the counselor's technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor repeatedly assumed the expert role, providing scripts verbatim and dictating delivery tips. The pattern was advice-giving rather than drawing out Tariq's own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Tariq spontaneously offered rich change talk about finishing strong and reuniting with his mother, but it was never reflected, affirmed, or deepened. No open questions evoked reasons for change or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately did not amplify or reinforce sustain talk about the officer, but also did not actively redirect it. No double-sided reflections or reframing toward Tariq's own goals; sustain talk was simply left unaddressed.
