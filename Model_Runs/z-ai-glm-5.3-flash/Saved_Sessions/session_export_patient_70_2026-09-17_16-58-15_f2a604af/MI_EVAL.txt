# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and warm, affirming honesty and reframing help-seeking as smart strategy. However, warmth is brief and instrumental, with moments of shame receiving quick reassurance rather than deeper acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; the counselor repeatedly jumps to solutions rather than mirroring feelings. Key emotional moments (shame about literacy, fear of rejection) were reassured instead of explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior of re-entry and employment throughout. Every exchange advances a concrete pathway with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative language and choices appear, but the actual work is largely counselor-generated. Lars remains a consumer of the plan rather than co-author.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, with Lars's decisions shaping the session. Falls short of 5 because advice is heavily counselor-generated and the closing worry was not handed back to problem-solve.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Beyond a strong opening question, the counselor provides content rather than drawing out Lars's own reasons and solutions. His motivations (Donna, fatherhood, pride in his skill) were never evoked or built upon.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power sharing through offering choices, but the counselor frequently assumes the expert role. Lars's own contributions were incorporated only glancingly rather than as the plan's foundation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong opening question evoked substantial change talk ('I don't wanna end up back inside'). However, change talk is rarely deepened or affirmed, mostly pivoting to task logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not amplified, and barriers are acknowledged without dwelling. But no strategic double-sided reflections were used, and the final relapse-risk question was left unaddressed.
