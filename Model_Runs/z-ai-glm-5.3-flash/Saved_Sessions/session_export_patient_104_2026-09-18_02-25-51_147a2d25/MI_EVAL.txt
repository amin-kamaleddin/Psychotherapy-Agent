# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with individualized warmth; normalizes doubt and separates exhaustion from failure. Client's shift from 'I'm failing' to 'asking is coping' reflects absorption of the nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both surface content and underlying meaning, including the shame dynamic around asking for help. Reflections consistently land, with the client building on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on infant sleep, exhaustion, and self-blame, progressing to a concrete plan via a well-executed focusing question. Slight redundancy in re-eliciting positive signs after the client had already affirmed them.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with every step co-constructed through open questions and reframes offered as invitations. Client's ideas drive each transition, moving from 'nobody's told me straight' to generating her own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No directives; the plan (Saturday handoff, sticky note) is entirely the client's design, with choice repeatedly returned to her. The clinician affirms her capacity for self-direction throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from within via evocative questions, with the client's language progressing from desire to ability to commitment and activation. No lecturing; the full desire-to-action arc is client-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise-resides-in-client stance is exemplary, with the clinician returning the client's own observations as evidence and positioning her as the knower. Session direction originates entirely from her answers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful strategic questions elicit desire, ability, planning, and self-prediction language, reinforced by reflection rather than supplied advice. Client moves to specific self-generated implementation ('Saturday, you do the ten o'clock feeding').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and skillfully redirected, including a textbook double-sided reflection and reframing asking as competence. Minor limitation: the mom-comparison narrative is addressed mainly via redirection rather than neutralized explicitly.
