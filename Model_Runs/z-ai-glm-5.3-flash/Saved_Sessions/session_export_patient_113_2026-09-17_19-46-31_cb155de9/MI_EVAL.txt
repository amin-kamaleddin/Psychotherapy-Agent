# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains unconditional positive regard throughout, even when the client admits intentional avoidance. Actively counters self-blame with affirmations like 'You're not lazy or careless; you're managing a lot.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both surface content ('I'm just busy') and deeper meaning (the device as a reminder of illness).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, focused trajectory on the single target behavior from start to finish, with each exchange advancing toward the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan is built jointly out of Rowan's own habits and ideas, with the clinician serving as prompt rather than advisor. Client's closing thanks for 'not just lecturing me' confirms an egalitarian experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice is consistently reinforced, including permission to revise the plan and self-granted grace for missed nights. No pressure or directive advice is present.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from within the client — his own insight, solutions, and success criteria. Clinician's role is limited to reflecting, affirming, and asking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise in the client, and all substantive ideas come from Rowan himself. Power-sharing is cemented by crediting the client: 'though honestly, you did the work.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evokes and reinforces client-generated change talk through open questions, a confidence ruler, and success definition. Client's trajectory ('maybe that'd work' → 'I'll do it') demonstrates progressive change talk development.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk without dismissing it, reframing busyness into the underlying emotional barrier. Rarely uses explicit double-sided reflections, and some pivots are quicker than ideal.
