# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, with warm nonjudgmental affirmations like 'nothing about that sounds lame to me.' Slip-ups are framed neutrally as information, not failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both content and emotional nuance, mirroring Tyler's own phrases like 'disappear from my own head.' Reflections go beyond paraphrase to underlying meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight, clear focus progressing from discrepancy through triggers, signal question, confidence rating, backup plans, and a concrete first step. No aimless drift; each exchange advances the agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals; the counselor explicitly names the partnership and co-constructs the plan from Tyler's ideas. Summaries serve Tyler's emerging conclusions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership are consistently reinforced ('this plan is entirely yours'); no directives imposed. Alternatives are left entirely to Tyler's selection.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from Tyler via open questions, with the counselor's stance that answers reside in the client. Tyler articulates the core discrepancy and generates the full plan himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in Tyler ('You're the expert on your social life'), and all plan elements originate from Tyler. The counselor amplifies rather than replaces his contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language, including a confidence ruler and reinforcement of Tyler's own words. Tyler responds with strong change talk ('I could literally text him tonight').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Double-sided reflections honor sustain talk without amplifying it, pivoting toward change. No sustained attention is given to reasons for the status quo.
