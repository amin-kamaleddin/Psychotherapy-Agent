# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout—normalizes the client's dismissiveness ('Not stupid at all') and closes with 'you don't ever have to prove anything here.' No judgment or disapproval despite the client's resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently capture underlying meaning, e.g., reframing the morning request as 'a basic need for breathing room' and the walk as 'what the treadmill never does.' Client progressively opens up, validating the accuracy of the listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, purposeful focus moving from what matters to the client toward a concrete commitment (Tuesday walk). Progression is collaborative rather than coercive, with no aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice given; every decision (walk, Tuesday) came from the client, with the counselor structuring and reflecting. Client's closing 'thanks for not making it another thing I had to prove' confirms the egalitarian tone.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly reinforces ownership ('your idea, your day, nobody else's schedule') and honors the client's right to define the outcome ('you get to decide what it means'). Never prescribes or pressures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All content of the plan (quiet, breathing room, walk, Tuesday) was elicited from the client via open and choice questions, never supplied by the counselor. Textbook evocation with a precontemplative client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly locates expertise with the client ('You know your schedule better than anyone'; 'That was all you'). Session direction and plan were fully client-owned.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk at each step, culminating in client-generated commitment language ('I could do it... Could do Tuesday'; 'I'll walk'). Affirmations amplify the client's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('feels pointless to try,' 'probably just go back to the same routine') is briefly acknowledged then redirected via double-sided reflections and pivots to problem-solving. Never amplified.
