# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone, validating Anara's pace regarding the doctor visit and her choice to keep information from her sister. Warmth is genuine but could be deepened with more explicit affirmations of her strengths to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening, accurately naming Anara's core emotions including fear, uncertainty, and the heaviness of her dread. Some reflections lean toward summarizing rather than exploring the deeper emotional meaning of her avoidance, fatigue, and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintains good focus on the target concerns and converges on a concrete, actionable plan (walk at 2:30 with backup, breathing practice, check-in). Some drift into logistical detail, and the medical evaluation — arguably the more critical target — is deferred without a bridge plan or roadmap to eventually address it.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behaves as an equal partner, co-constructing the walking plan and offering to help with the phone note rather than prescribing; Anara's ideas directly shaped the final plan. Collaboration is slightly limited by the counselor's tendency to present pre-packaged option menus rather than jointly generating ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support — consistently and explicitly frames all decisions as Anara's own ('It's completely up to you,' 'You decide who to tell'), with all plans offered as options to accept, reject, or modify. Anara explicitly credited the non-pushy approach as the reason she could commit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out Anara's own motivations and meaning-making, with the question about what she'd like to notice after the walk producing her deepest statement about the dread. Does less to evoke her own reasons for eventually seeking medical care, and some motivation-focused moments are answered with logistical options instead of deeper exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Anara as the expert, and her contributions clearly shaped the session — the plan came entirely from her input. The counselor sometimes provides fairly dense lists of options that slightly predetermine the menu of choices rather than open-endedly drawing from Anara's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes and reinforces change talk, eliciting commitment language ('it's small enough that I can't really talk myself out of it') and concrete action ('Tomorrow at 2:30. I'll try'). There is less deliberate deepening of change talk about the medical visit itself, where Anara remains pre-contemplative, and missed opportunities to further amplify her self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying reasons against change, responding to avoidance and overwhelm with brief empathic reflections and pivots to her chosen small step. Some responses subtly re-introduce sustain talk themes, and the doctor-avoidance is acknowledged but not actively reframed with double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence about medical care.
