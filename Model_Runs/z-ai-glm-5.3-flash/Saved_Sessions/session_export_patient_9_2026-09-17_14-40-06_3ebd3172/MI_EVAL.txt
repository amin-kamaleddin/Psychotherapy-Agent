# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout with affirmations of Amina's steps. Loses a point for somewhat formulaic, repeated affirmations lacking deeper individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections, but empathy thins once the counselor shifts to solution mode. Key emotional material—shame about asking for help, fear of her ex's reaction—went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept firm focus and moved logically from broad worries to a concrete, achievable plan with scaling and commitment. Slightly narrow in that the central co-parenting conflict was deferred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power sharing via preference questions, but session content was largely counselor-generated with pre-built routines, scripts, and texts. Amina selected from the counselor's menu rather than co-creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choice and honored Amina's pacing without pressure. However, options were pre-constructed by the counselor and pace was counselor-driven, limiting genuine ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Weak evocation: the counselor supplied ideas, routines, and drafted texts rather than drawing them out. Amina's own solutions and expertise about her son emerged despite, not because of, counselor prompts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked Amina's preferences, but the counselor quickly assumed the expert role with three consecutive rounds of prescriptive bullet lists and templates. Resulted in lukewarm rather than active power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used good evocation techniques (open questions, confidence scaling), and Amina generated meaningful change talk. However, her strongest commitment statements ('he's worth me pushing through') went unreflected and unamplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk but rarely actively softened it—no double-sided reflections or exploration of underlying shame. Sustain talk about her ex and self-doubt was tolerated rather than strategically resolved.
