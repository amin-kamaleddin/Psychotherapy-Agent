# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, normalizing defensiveness and honoring Kevin's confidentiality concern. However, warmth was somewhat formulaic, missing personalized affirmations of Kevin's honesty and self-insight.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early empathic statements acknowledged frustration and defensiveness adequately. As the session progressed, reflections gave way to advice-giving, and key emotional moments (embarrassment, fear of mockery) were under-explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on alcohol use and risk reduction, progressing from assessment to concrete, self-selected goals. Direction sometimes manifested as agenda-driven task lists rather than client-led momentum, with ankle-care detail briefly diluting focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking, option-offering, and co-selecting weeknights showed collaborative elements, but the clinician largely generated the plans, rules, and scripts while Kevin merely adjusted them. True collaboration would have Kevin generating more ideas himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices and menus were offered consistently, and Kevin's boundaries (one week, rejecting water-alternation as a chore) were respected. Strengthening would involve more explicit reinforcement of Kevin's ownership and less prescriptive scripting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin voiced genuine motivation ('it wasn't just the one night', willingness to try the week), but the clinician relied on multiple-choice menus and pre-packaged advice rather than open questions to develop his own reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offering choices and asking permission conveyed some partnership, but the session frequently became expert-driven with dense lists of tips, scripts, and tracking formats. The clinician positioned themselves as the knowledge source rather than drawing out Kevin's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Space was created for change talk, and Kevin produced meaningful commitments (no shots, two alcohol-free nights, tracking, texting Danny). However, much emerged through structured menus rather than open evocative questioning about Kevin's own hopes and vision of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk like 'that's how I unwind' was met with neutral validation and choices rather than argument. However, extensive harm-reduction and medical advice shifted focus away from exploring ambivalence, and sustain talk was never strategically reframed toward change.
