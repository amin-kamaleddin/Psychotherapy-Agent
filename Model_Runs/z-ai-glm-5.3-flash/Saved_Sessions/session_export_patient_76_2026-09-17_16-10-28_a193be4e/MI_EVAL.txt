# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental toward Liora's self-harm, accepting her ambivalence and refusals without pressure. Warmth is somewhat formulaic and generic rather than individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections capture her exhaustion and the function of cutting, but reflections are sparse and bundled with question stacks. Key emotional moments like 'Then it's quiet' and shame about hiding from roommates go unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, appropriate focus on self-harm throughout: risk assessment, urge exploration, coping alternatives, and a concrete safety plan with follow-up. Occasionally tips into checklist-driven rapid-fire questions that crowd out client-led exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some co-construction exists (Dana, low-key text, reminders), but the plan's substance is largely clinician-drafted with Liora passively assenting. Little genuine negotiation of agenda or integration of her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy regularly affirmed ('You're in charge here'), options offered rather than directives, and refusals accepted without pressure. Held at 4 because the plan is largely counselor-authored and closing safety scripting feels directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session is advisory rather than evocative: coping options are provided rather than elicited, with almost no questions about Liora's values, past coping, or hopes. Her one genuine resource (Dana) is not built on through evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partnership gestures exist ('You're in charge here'), but the counselor repeatedly defaults to an expert stance with pre-drafted plans and options. Liora's passivity is not countered with genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Faint commitment threads ('I can try, I guess') are left unreflected and undeepened, with no exploration of reasons for change or hopes. The session shifts quickly to advice and plan construction instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is tolerated neutrally but not strategically softened. No double-sided reflections pairing 'cutting is the only thing that helps' with her exhaustion and implicit wish to stop.
