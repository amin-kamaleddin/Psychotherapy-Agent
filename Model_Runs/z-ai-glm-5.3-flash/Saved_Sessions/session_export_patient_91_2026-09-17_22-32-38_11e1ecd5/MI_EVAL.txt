# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no judgment, normalizing ambivalence and validating fears. Client explicitly noted he expected 'another lecture' and instead felt respected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are generally accurate and complex, but one notable misreflection ('evening walks' instead of blood sugar checking) broke empathic accuracy. Some reflective phrasing is also occasionally formulaic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays well focused on diabetes self-management and progresses logically to a concrete plan. The 'evening walks' misstatement briefly diverted focus, and earlier exploration circled somewhat before converging on the plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative, with the plan co-constructed entirely from the client's own suggestions and the counselor positioned as a thinking partner. Client's statement that 'the plan came out of my own mouth' confirms the equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces client choice and ownership through permissive framing and optional offerings. Client's closing statement—'it's mine, you know?'—directly evidences that autonomy support landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn almost entirely from the client, with no lecturing; questions elicit his self-efficacy story and future visualization. This produced strong client-generated motivation and a self-declared plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly locates expertise within the client ('You're the expert on your own life'), and all plan elements originated from Marcus. Closing question reinforces that the change is the client's own work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk through open questions, drawing out rich client-generated commitment language. This culminated in concrete commitments including the nurse walkthrough and a follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally validates sustain talk briefly then pivots toward change, as with the fear of not following through. A few reflections restated the client's doubts, modestly amplifying sustain language rather than pivoting away.
