# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental responses validated the patient's fears and demand to be taken seriously. Slight room to deepen emotional exploration of loneliness during her husband's absence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and validating, e.g., acknowledging the invalidation of being told 'everything is fine.' Could have explored emotions like loneliness more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the GI complaints and the stress-symptom connection throughout. Every question advanced the agenda, ending with a concrete commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary equal-partner work: the plan (breathing experiment, diary, continued testing) emerged from the patient's own ideas. Co-construction converted directly into client-owned action.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice and never pressured or advised. All next steps were self-generated, and her condition to keep seeking tests was fully accepted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire session elicited the client's own motivations, moving her from defensive denial to 'maybe the stress keeps it going' and 'I'll start tonight.' Patient and organic, never persuasive.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned the patient as the expert, and her ideas shaped the session direction. Could share power over the agenda itself more explicitly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questions drew rich change language, including a self-initiated commitment. Could deepen confidence talk further, e.g., scaling confidence or exploring past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed (e.g., 'the scientist in you') without ever being amplified or argued against. A few more explicit bridges from sustain to change language would strengthen this.
