# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, notably avoiding the lecture the client expected. However, warmth was somewhat procedural, with no explicit affirmations of Marisol's strengths as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but shallow, quickly shifting to information delivery and option menus. Missed opportunities to explore her irritation at perceived judgment, pride in autonomy, or the ambivalence hinted at near the end.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on the piercing and oral health, ending with a concrete plan. However, direction was anchored to harm reduction without clarifying whether re-evaluating removal should be part of the broader goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor behaved as an equal partner, co-developing the blunt clinician line and building the plan from the client's statements. Short of a 5 because health information was supplied unilaterally rather than drawing on what the client already knew.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support: explicit statements of client control, opt-in menus, and graceful acceptance of refusals. The client's surprise at not being lectured demonstrated the non-controlling stance was genuinely experienced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some self-generated commitments emerged, but the counselor relied heavily on information-giving and option menus rather than open questions. For a pre-contemplative client, missing the chance to develop ambivalence was a significant lost opportunity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly positioned the client's contributions as shaping the session and avoided the expert role on the removal decision. Not a 5 because the counselor still dominated content delivery rather than actively drawing out the client's own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully cultivated client-generated commitments around harm reduction (fidget ring, monthly checks, dental follow-up). However, never explored her own reasons for caring about her teeth or whether any motivation regarding the piercing itself existed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoided reinforcing sustain talk, accepting 'I'm not taking it out' while pivoting to changeable behaviors. Fell short of a 5 due to occasionally mirroring sustain talk without double-sided reflections balancing her stated concern for her teeth.
