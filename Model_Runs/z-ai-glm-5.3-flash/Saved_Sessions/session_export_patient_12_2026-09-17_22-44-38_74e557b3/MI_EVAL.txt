### Empathy

**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections captured emotional nuance, e.g., 'assuming the worst feels like armor.' Amira's elaboration and new insight ('I never connected those two things') confirm feeling deeply understood.

### Direction

**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus moving from helplessness to identifying support, examining barriers, and a concrete possible step (texting Lucia). Each exchange built strategically without dragging the client faster than she consented to.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: ideas were co-constructed and the check-in plan was negotiated at Amira's request. No expert-driven directives appeared at any point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was reinforced at every decision point, with ownership repeatedly returned to the client. The follow-up plan was actively restructured around her stated preferences ('no direct asking, and "I didn't" is completely okay').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every meaningful step originated from Amira's own words, evolving from 'I don't know' to 'I'm choosing to try.' Evocative questioning produced a genuine self-generated insight about Lucia's role in her self-perception.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Amira was repeatedly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert on your own life'), and her contributions demonstrably shaped the session. The psychiatrist consistently attributed insights to her rather than claiming them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and amplified, evolving from regret ('I shouldn't have touched that stuff') to choice language ('I'm choosing to try') and self-directed reasoning. Each piece was strategically deepened into concrete goals and insight.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement and typically paired with pivots toward her own resources, using effective double-sided reflections. Some reflections gave substantial airtime to hopelessness language, and the broken-promises talk was not always explicitly reframed toward her capacity to keep commitments to herself.
