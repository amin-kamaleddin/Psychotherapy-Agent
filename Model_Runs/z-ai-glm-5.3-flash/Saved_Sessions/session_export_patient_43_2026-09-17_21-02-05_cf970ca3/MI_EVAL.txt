# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations like 'I hear some real resilience in you' and 'Stubborn's not always a bad thing.' No judgment of smoking, defensiveness, or ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, deep reflections capturing exhaustion, guardedness, and the pivotal emotional shift toward his daughter. Reflections consistently landed and moved the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tightly focused on smoking and health, with a clear arc from ambivalence to specific commitments (morning cigarette, Saturday park visit) and a follow-up check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: Eric proposed the cigarette swap and park visit himself, and the closing accountability agreement was co-constructed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ownership consistently reinforced ('that's your own voice coming through,' 'One small step, yours, not theirs'). Eric shifted from 'everyone else's agenda' to 'part of me wants to see if I can do this.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation elicited from within via open questions ('What part of it bugs you most?'), drawing out Eric's own reasons—his daughter, self-image, and pride—rather than supplying them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise resided with the client; questions like 'What do you think might help right now?' enabled Eric's realization that change needed to feel like his own idea, which he acknowledged at the end.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk; the pivotal reframe about keeping up with his daughter in a month led directly to Eric's concrete plan, which was then reinforced step by step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('nothing too bad,' 'not quitting tomorrow') was neither argued with nor reinforced, with effective pivots to personal motivation. A deliberate double-sided reflection early on could have resolved ambivalence faster.
