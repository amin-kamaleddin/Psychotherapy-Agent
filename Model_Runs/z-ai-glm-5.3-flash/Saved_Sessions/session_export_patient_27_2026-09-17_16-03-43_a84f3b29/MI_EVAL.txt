# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and validation without judgment; affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing emotional and practical dimensions, but missed a chance to reflect the vulnerable moment when Daniela said the reply about 'why didn't you tell me before' stung.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on peer disclosure and self-management, culminating in a concrete plan; minor drift into drafting multiple hypothetical scripts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniela's preferences shaped script version, timing, and method, and she affirmed the shared problem-solving; the expert-driven scripting content slightly weakened collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices offered at every step with permission-based advice; frequent provision of ready-made scripts undercut Daniela's ownership of her own words.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Readiness ruler and open questions elicited some client motivation, but much content was counselor-supplied; missed opportunities to explore Daniela's values (storytelling identity, trust in friendship) and deepen her 'first step' statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission before advising, but the counselor's pre-written scripts and prepared replies dominated the 'how'; having Daniela draft her own words first would improve power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation of change talk with readiness rising from 0 to 4–5 and out-loud rehearsal; limited by often providing language rather than letting Daniela generate her own.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated fears without amplifying them and redirected toward manageable steps; some exchanges dwelt on feared questions, slightly expanding sustain talk.
