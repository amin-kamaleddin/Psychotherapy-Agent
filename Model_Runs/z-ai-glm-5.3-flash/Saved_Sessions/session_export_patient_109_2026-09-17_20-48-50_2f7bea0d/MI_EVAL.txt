# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing Rafael's struggle and reframing embarrassment about his wife's help as a strength. 'No failing here, just learning' reinforces a consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and captures meaning beneath the surface, e.g., 'admitting all those tomorrows didn't count,' which Rafael confirms with 'that's it exactly.' Ambivalence is skillfully reflected as double-sided.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains consistent focus on returning to exercise with no topic drift, culminating in a concrete plan (Tuesday/Thursday mornings, bags packed, wife informed, two-week follow-up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—Rafael's ideas shape the direction of the session and the plan emerges entirely from his input. The closing 'Deal' reflects shared decision-making rather than a prescribed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Rafael's ownership ('It's your call') with no advice imposed. The wife's involvement is explicitly framed as Rafael's own idea.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions draw out Rafael's own reasons and abilities rather than supplying motivation. He generates the entire solution himself, with change talk escalating to concrete commitment and anticipation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Rafael as the expert ('You know yourself better than I do') and actively attributes the plan to him. Rafael confirms the counselor 'didn't lecture me or hand me some workout plan.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk, affirming Rafael's self-generated solutions and highlighting his ownership. Change talk escalates from 'maybe doable?' to a concrete commitment and 'I'm kind of looking forward to it a little.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally acknowledges sustain talk without amplifying it, reframing fear and discouragement effectively. Minor deduction only because some reflections briefly repeat Rafael's discouragement ('watching tomorrow keep not happening') before pivoting, though the pivot always follows quickly.
