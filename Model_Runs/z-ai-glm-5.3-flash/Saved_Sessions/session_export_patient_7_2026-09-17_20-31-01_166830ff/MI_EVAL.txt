### Acceptance

**Comments:** Counselor consistently shows unconditional positive regard toward a highly resistant adolescent, responding to 'it's not any of your business' without defensiveness and normalizing her feelings. Warmth feels genuine, tolerates boundaries, and honors her pace ('No point required.').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing surface content and deeper feelings ('walking on eggshells', 'nothing I do is ever enough'). Accepts corrections without defensiveness and tracks her shift from defensiveness to disclosure in real time.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains steady, appropriate focus on engagement, home conflict, and Amina's own feelings, skillfully redirecting blame. The final pivot to emotional disclosure slightly shifts focus away from consolidating the 'breathing room' theme she had opened.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session unfolds as a genuine exchange of equals — no prescribing or expert stance, with all breakthrough moments emerging from Amina's own words. Tentative offerings ('I wonder if...') disarm her resistance effectively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Amina's ownership and choice ('this space is yours', 'No point required'), never pressuring past her stated boundaries. Zero coercive or controlling language throughout, remarkable given her initial resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Amina's own desires ('to actually breathe', 'treated like a person') rather than supplying reasons for change. Does not develop change talk into confidence or commitment language, though this is appropriate for engagement.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Skillfully evokes Amina's own desire language ('I just want to not feel on edge... like I could actually breathe') through strategic questions. Does not deepen into commitment or confidence talk, though pacing is fitting for an engagement-stage session.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Uses effective double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk without reinforcing it, then pivots to her strengths. Occasionally restates the negative framing before pivoting, slightly extending focus on hopelessness.
