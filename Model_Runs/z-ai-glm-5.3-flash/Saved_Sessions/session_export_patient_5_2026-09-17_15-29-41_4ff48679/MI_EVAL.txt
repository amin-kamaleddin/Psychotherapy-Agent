# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with repeated affirmations ('You handled that well,' 'that's responsible and brave'). Falls short of a 5 due to somewhat formulaic warmth with limited individualized emotional attunement beyond the safety crisis.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate but brief reflections of the client's state ('You're understandably shaken'). Empathy is repetitive and shallow, without deeper exploration of her sense of betrayal or her identity as someone who likes to be informed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering, purposeful focus on the medication interaction and immediate safety, with every turn advancing a concrete agenda. Client leaves with a clear, rehearsed action plan and no session drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers tailored scripts, role-play, and checklists with permission-seeking, but the interaction is fundamentally expert-driven. Alessia's own expertise shapes logistics but not the substance of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames next steps as the client's choice ('Which would help you most right now?'). Autonomy is limited by urgent safety framing and by Alessia selecting from counselor-offered menus rather than shaping her own path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions are largely supplied by the counselor rather than drawn from the client. The opening evocative question is abandoned and her self-efficacy statements are affirmed but never elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers options inviting input, but the expertise stance dominates with dense information, directives, and checklists. Minimal elicitation of Alessia's own knowledge or ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no deliberate effort to evoke the client's own language for change. Her spontaneous change-oriented statements are neither reflected, amplified, nor deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced—her hesitation is met with appropriate guidance. However, no strategic softening via double-sided reflections or reframing of ambivalence occurs.
