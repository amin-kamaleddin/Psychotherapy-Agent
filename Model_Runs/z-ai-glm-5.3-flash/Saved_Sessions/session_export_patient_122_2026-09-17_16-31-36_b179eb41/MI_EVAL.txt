# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with genuine affirmations throughout. However, warmth occasionally feels formulaic, with limited explicit normalization of Sofia's discouragement and past failed diets.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture Sofia's emotional experience well. Reflections could go deeper into her history of self-disappointment and the loneliness of relocation, rather than shifting toward the behavioral plan.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary focus on a specific target behavior (morning 10-minute park walk) with concrete planning at every step. No meaningful topic drift occurred across the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Cooperative interaction, but the counselor tends to propose full solution packages that Sofia ratifies rather than co-constructs. Power-sharing is partial; options should be generated jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-framing and explicit ownership from Sofia ('it's mine. Nobody's forcing it on me'). Dense menus of suggestions can feel subtly steering, and ownership of potential setbacks isn't explicitly affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Confidence scaling and evocative questions elicited genuine motivation, but much motivational content is counselor-provided. Missed opportunities to explore Sofia's own reasons and values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's input is invited and her choices honored, but the counselor occupies the expert role, supplying most ideas while Sofia merely selects among them. Client-generated contributions shape the plan only modestly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation produced strong change language ('I want to try this,' 'tomorrow feels possible'). Score limited because the counselor sometimes supplies the change content himself rather than consistently drawing ideas from Sofia.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without amplification, and the fallback reframe effectively counters all-or-nothing thinking. Redirection to solutions sometimes comes too quickly, before ambivalence is fully explored.
