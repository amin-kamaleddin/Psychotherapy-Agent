# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and unconditional positive regard despite Marco's sarcasm and resistance, validating his autonomy from the start without judgment or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening captured Marco's attachment to smoking and fatigue with nagging, but occasionally stayed at the surface without exploring the emotional meaning beneath his fatalism after surgery and cancer.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking throughout, steering past deflections toward a specific, measurable behavioral plan with confidence ratings and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely worked as equals with Marco's own ideas shaping the plan, though the counselor sometimes moved faster than him by supplying the options menu and structures he simply went along with.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support—repeatedly reinforced Marco's ownership, asked permission before advising, honored his refusals, and framed slips as information rather than failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out change language, confidence ratings, and success criteria, but never elicited Marco's personal reasons for change or values—the 'why' of change remained unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned Marco as the expert with permission-asking and choice-framing, but the counselor-supplied menu of options and Marco's 'slippery' comment suggested some agenda-driving beneath the collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk from a precontemplative start, yielding a concrete plan and commitment language, though some options were counselor-generated and the importance dimension was skipped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided arguing with sustain talk and pivoted to low-stakes experiments, but double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk were underused.
