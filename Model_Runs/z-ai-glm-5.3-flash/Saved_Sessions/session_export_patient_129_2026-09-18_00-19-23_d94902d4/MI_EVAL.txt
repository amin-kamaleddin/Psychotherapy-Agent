# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout ('You're not broken'); genuine, individualized affirmations with no labeling or moralizing despite the client's resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections consistently capture underlying emotion, and Tyler's progressive openness from one-word resistance to self-disclosure shows he felt deeply understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Purposeful, logical progression from engagement toward concrete possibilities. A point is deducted because substance use itself is never directly named; focus remains on the mother relationship.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Agenda is jointly negotiated from the start, and Tyler's contributions shape every exchange; the tone is two equals problem-solving rather than expert-to-patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every step is framed as Tyler's choice ('make this useful on your terms'); no advice is imposed and the plan is entirely his creation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivations (less stress, no exhausting lying, normalcy at home) emerge entirely from Tyler's own reasoning; no persuasion or education about risks.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly and genuinely located in Tyler ('You're the expert here'), and his own ideas authentically drive the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful questioning elicits progressively stronger change language, from 'maybe I wouldn't have to sneak around' to Tyler spontaneously strategizing a concrete approach.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ambivalence is handled well via double-sided reflections, but the mother-blame narrative is engaged somewhat longer than necessary before pivoting toward Tyler's own agency.
