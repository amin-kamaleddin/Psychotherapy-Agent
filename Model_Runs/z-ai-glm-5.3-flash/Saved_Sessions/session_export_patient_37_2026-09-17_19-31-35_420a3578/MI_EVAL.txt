# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard; validates hesitancy as understandable and affirms Elena as a caring, careful parent. Minor note: affirmations occasionally follow a slightly formulaic pattern.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate, complex double-sided reflections capturing Elena's emotional core. Slightly celebratory tone in a few closing lines kept this from a perfect score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every turn maintains clear focus on resolving vaccine hesitancy, advancing steadily from ambivalence to a concrete, scheduled plan with no aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed session—Elena's ideas (question list, sister's support, sequencing) shaped the plan and were built upon rather than overridden.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly returns decision-making power to Elena, never prescribes a vaccination decision, and explicitly attributes motivation to her. Open choice between next steps at the end is textbook autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from Elena rather than supplied; open questions elicited progressively stronger change language, with no arguing or lecturing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power sharing with Elena's conditions honored and woven into the plan; occasionally the clinician framed the narrative without checking summary accuracy with her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled, consistent evocation of change talk—Elena moved from 'I do want to protect her' to 'I actually kind of want to get it done. For her.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged but never reinforced, consistently reframed toward shared protective values and action. Pivots occasionally relied on the same reframing device.
