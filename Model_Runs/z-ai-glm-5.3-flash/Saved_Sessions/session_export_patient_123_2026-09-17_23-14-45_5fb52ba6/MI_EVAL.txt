# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout; Sofia's dismissive answers are met with affirmation rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflective listening that captures content and some feeling, but reflections stay at surface level and rarely probe the emotional meaning beneath Sofia's 'everything is fine' posture.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Critical failure: alcohol — the reason for referral — is never mentioned; the session drifts aimlessly through friends, homework, and hobbies with no focus or convergence on the screening result.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian, ask-not-tell tone with Sofia's perspective invited throughout; limited only by the absence of a jointly negotiated agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ownership is consistently affirmed with no pressure or directives, but autonomy is respected passively — the counselor never offers Sofia a real choice about the clinical issue at hand.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's values and coping strengths are elicited and reflected, but motivation for change is never explored because the change target is never introduced.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames Sofia as the expert with no advice or expert pronouncements; however, the session follows her tangents rather than co-constructing an agenda, so power-sharing stays passive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No change talk about drinking is ever evoked since the target behavior is unnamed, and questions like 'what do you hope stays the same?' actively elicit status-quo language instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's sustained status-quo statements go unredirected, and some counselor questions reinforce sustain talk; no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots appear.
