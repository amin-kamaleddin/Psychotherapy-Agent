# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with potential 'failure' scenarios framed as wins ('both are wins,' 'I'll celebrate whatever happens with you'). Supportive stance reinforced through offers to troubleshoot together and reassurance that she doesn't have to do this alone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening, accurately reflecting Elena's plan and values ('that effort to actually practice your values is real self-respect'). However, her significant emotional disclosure about letting someone care for her — rather than always caring for others — was affirmed but not explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on the target behavior (values-based evening boundaries) from first turn to last, with zero drift. Every exchange builds progressively toward an actionable commitment: identifying tensions, choosing nights, troubleshooting barriers, and arranging check-ins.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong equal partnership in co-constructing check-in logistics and explicitly soliciting Elena's input at every decision point. Slight deduction because the plan structure is largely driven by the counselor's structured questioning, with some options counselor-generated rather than client-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Elena's ownership by offering choices rather than imposing them ('do you want that, or do you prefer to handle it yourself?'). Elena clearly internalizes autonomy, stating 'The sister text is my job, not yours' and 'No more excuses.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Elena's own reasons, strategies, and confidence ratings, producing genuine self-authored change talk. Deduction because the counselor sometimes provides ideas and backup options rather than purely evoking, and heavy closed questioning at times constrains elaboration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Elena as the expert, asking her preferences on nights, check-in format, and reminder content, with her answers substantially shaping the plan. Slight deduction for rapid-fire multi-question barrages that lead rather than follow, and several counselor-suggested options (note by the door, alarm, shoes by closet).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation of change talk, including a skillfully used confidence scaling question that moved Elena from a 7 to an 8. Elena's flourishing self-authored statements ('I feel really good,' 'No more excuses,' 'my evening belongs to me') demonstrate successful cultivation and reinforcement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was minimal given Elena's action-stage presentation; the counselor did not amplify resistance and briefly acknowledged obstacles only to elicit Elena's own coping strategies. Minor deduction for introducing potential barriers ('What might pull you back to grading?') somewhat readily, though framed as planning rather than dwelling.
