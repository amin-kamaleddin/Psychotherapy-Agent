# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys warmth and unconditional positive regard ('It takes real honesty to say, I value this but it's not sticking,' 'That's your own wisdom, not mine'). Rafael's self-doubt and past failure are met with acceptance and normalization, never judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, with complex reflections capturing both sides of ambivalence and distilling deeper meaning ('the true test isn't attendance at all — it's how you treat yourself after a miss'). Summaries precisely reflect Rafael's values and goals.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on returning to exercise, guiding logically from exploring a workable routine to a concrete first step (bag in the car, Tuesday visit) to relapse-proofing. Each question builds on the last without wandering off-target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions are open and invitational ('What would help you?'), and Rafael's answers shape the agenda. The plan is fully co-constructed from his own words, with summaries serving his ideas back for confirmation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Rafael's ownership and expertise ('You know your schedule and your life better than anyone'). No advice is imposed; all ideas originate from him and his capacity for self-direction is affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from within — eliciting Rafael's values (energy, patience, thirty years with his wife) and his own coping strategies. His intrinsic motivation emerges from his own speech rather than counselor persuasion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Rafael is explicitly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert here,' 'It came from you'). His contributions demonstrably shape the session, with the plan elements all originating from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertly evokes and reinforces change talk, highlighting 'doing something has to beat doing nothing' as Rafael's own wisdom and reflecting his commitment language. Change talk escalates progressively to 'I feel kind of ready? Like this is actually doable for once.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally redirects sustain talk effectively via double-sided reflections and reframing the all-or-nothing pattern toward self-permission. Occasionally mirrors sustain language ('an hour feels like the only thing that counts') before pivoting, though recovery is always quick.
