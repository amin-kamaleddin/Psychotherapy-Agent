# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic respect is maintained and Sofia notices she isn't being blamed, but affirmations are shallow and formulaic. Her guardedness and years of feeling unfairly blamed are never explicitly validated or normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; vulnerable disclosures like her fear of being ignored and her recognition of avoidance as 'hiding' are met with scripts and checklists rather than reflections. Feelings are occasionally named but not sustained or accurately explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on school avoidance and converges on a concrete, achievable plan (text the friend, attend Thursday, boundary and exit lines). Focus is well maintained, though progress is driven more by prescriptive tasks than by guiding Sofia's own goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's choices shape the final plan and the counselor tweaks materials on request, but responses read as expert deliverables (menus, scripts, checklists). She is never asked what she thinks would work or whether the plan feels right.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** No coercion, and offering options respects choice, but the prescriptive style undercuts ownership—fully worded scripts, checklists, and re-offering breathing after Sofia declined grounding. Phrases affirming her control of the plan are absent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all scripts, strategies, and solutions come from the counselor; there are virtually no evocative open questions. Sofia's own insights ('it's just hiding,' 'I can actually try this') are never drawn out or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menus and choice points let Sofia steer, and her contributions shape the plan, but the counselor repeatedly slips into an expert stance. Her expertise about her social world is accepted but never explicitly honored as valuable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor responds well to Sofia's spontaneous change talk (fixing the friendship, 'I think I can actually try this') but never deliberately evokes it with open questions or confidence rulers. Change talk emerged spontaneously or from menus, not skillful evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk isn't amplified, and redirecting from confrontation options was effective. However, ambivalence (fear of being ignored) is sidestepped rather than reframed with double-sided reflections.
