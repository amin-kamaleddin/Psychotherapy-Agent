# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveyed warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard, validating Elena's suspicion of drug companies and reframing her fears as evidence of good parenting without any judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections captured Elena's conflict, fears, and emotional shifts ('I can hear that shift in your voice'), and Elena consistently built on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on vaccine hesitancy, progressing logically from information needs to criteria for trustworthy sources to a concrete plan to review databases together.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong use of we-language ('we could look together') and co-construction of decisions based on Elena's own stated criteria, such as starting with independent oversight.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine choices were offered throughout, including format, pace, and order of information, with no pressure or persuasion; Elena controlled the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently elicited Elena's own motivations and solutions (e.g., what would make oversight 'feel real'); a slight deduction because some substantive education was counselor-supplied rather than purely evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located in the client ('you're the expert on what reassures you'), and the question about what would tell her the system is solid visibly shifted Elena's agency.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong change talk was evoked and reinforced ('It's not that I don't want to protect her. I do.'), though some movement followed counselor-provided education rather than being purely client-generated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and skillfully reframed as care, though the counselor occasionally restated sustain themes like her worry about buried information.
