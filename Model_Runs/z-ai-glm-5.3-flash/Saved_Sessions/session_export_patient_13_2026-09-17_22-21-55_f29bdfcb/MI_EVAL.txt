# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard conveyed throughout; client explicitly noted 'Nobody's made me feel stupid for waiting this long.' Counselor never criticized avoidance and repeatedly affirmed her honesty and self-knowledge.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured both sides of ambivalence and deep emotional content (e.g., fear of 'failing at even this'). Client built directly on reflections, indicating precise understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on the target behavior and moved from vague overwhelm to a concrete, time-bound plan. Minor limitation: near the end the counselor re-asked an already-answered question, creating a small circular moment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor asked permission, co-created next steps, and invited the client to define follow-up structure. The plan was fully shaped by her ideas, with the counselor positioned as support rather than director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor repeatedly reinforced client ownership ('at your pace,' 'it stays on your terms') and offered choices even about follow-up. No pressure present; client's 'it's my scary, not something being done to me' evidences effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within — the client generated the plan, self-compassion strategies, and hope statements herself. Counselor explicitly named this: 'That's hope coming from you, not from me.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor treated the client as expert on her own life, and her contributions (card idea, texting sister, timing) visibly shaped the session. Power sharing was active and genuine.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited, not supplied — progressing from vague awareness to 'Tonight I text her.' Counselor highlighted client-generated ideas and amplified her agency throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally redirected well using double-sided reflections pairing barriers with emerging change talk. Minor limitation: sustain talk was sometimes reflected at length before redirection rather than strategically minimized.
