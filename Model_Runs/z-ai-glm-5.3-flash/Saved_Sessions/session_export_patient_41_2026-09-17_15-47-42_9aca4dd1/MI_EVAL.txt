# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with normalizing statements throughout. Affirmations remain somewhat generic rather than individualized to Elira's specific efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate simple reflections early on, but emotional disclosures (chest-tightness, self-criticism, fear of not repeating) receive only brief acknowledgment before pivoting to task instructions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior; every turn ties back to a concrete behavioral plan, ending with a fully specified, time-linked action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan is genuinely co-constructed with Elira's ideas incorporated at each revision. However, long multi-part directives at times resemble expert prescription more than mutual exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently affirmed and Elira chooses among options with modifications accepted. Dense, rapid strategy suggestions can subtly pressure her toward the counselor's framing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effective confidence scaling and barrier questions, but much of the session delivers solutions rather than eliciting them, and Elira's own values and reasons for change are never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira's contributions shape the plan and permission is explicitly sought before offering strategies. The counselor still drives most of the problem-solving content rather than drawing solutions primarily from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira commits to concrete steps (6:30 alarm, 6:15 phone move, roommate check-in), supported by the scaling question. Missed opportunity to amplify change talk like 'That's more than I usually do.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubt is briefly acknowledged and never argued with, then redirected to problem-solving. Redirections feel abrupt, lacking double-sided reflections to help resolve ambivalence.
