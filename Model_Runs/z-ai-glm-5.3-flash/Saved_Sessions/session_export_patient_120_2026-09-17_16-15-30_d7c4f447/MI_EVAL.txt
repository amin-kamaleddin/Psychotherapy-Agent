# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and nonjudgmental throughout, effectively normalizing guilt around a high-risk topic. However, praise occasionally feels formulaic, and Sofia's fear of having already harmed the baby was not addressed with full emotional attunement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurately reflected key content like weekend triggers and the desire for support without a spotlight. But emotional core material — her 'stomach drop' terror about first-trimester exposure and identity grief — was acknowledged only briefly rather than explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use during pregnancy from initial assessment through weekend-specific planning and rehearsal. No topic drift; every exchange advanced toward Saturday's challenge.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine back-and-forth existed, with Sofia's preferences and wording visibly shaping the plan. However, collaboration mostly meant ratifying counselor-generated options rather than starting from Sofia's own problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Periodically affirmed choice and asked permission before advancing. Yet multiple-choice menus, prescribed step-by-step plans, and detailed scripts shifted control toward the counselor, with Sofia's best moments coming from her own initiative.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Beyond the initial importance question and readiness ruler, the counselor supplied reasons, scripts, and rationale rather than drawing them from Sofia. Rich motivational material ('please be okay, please be okay,' 'I want to feel ready, not just hopeful') went unreflected and unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumed the expert role, delivering pre-packaged solutions, numbered plans, and scripted options. Sofia's own strong ideas (drink-holding trick, exit timing, self-drafted text) were overlaid rather than built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good evocative opening questions and a readiness ruler drew out initial change talk, later reinforced. However, the session then shifted to counselor-generated plans, missing opportunities to deepen change talk like exploring what getting through Saturday sober would mean to her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified and ambivalence was briefly acknowledged without dwelling. However, fears like being 'the pregnant girl who can't party anymore' were met with validation and logistics rather than strategic reframing toward her deeper value of protecting the baby.
