# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, including when Kareem opened by refusing to quit, and skillfully normalized slips as 'not game over.' Held back from a 5 by occasionally formulaic warmth and moving quickly to advice early on.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Kareem's ambivalence well, such as his enjoyment of breaks versus policy frustration. However, the opening multi-question block assumed unvoiced feelings, and his fear of failure was answered factually rather than reflected empathically.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on workplace smoking cessation throughout, tying every turn back to the gum plan, tracking, or slip handling, and ending with a concrete plan. Only minor deductions for the scattered opening turn and technical logistics that displaced motivation exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered menu-based choices and co-constructed the final plan from Kareem's preferences (gum, phone tracking, Friday 2pm). Fell short of 5 because early advice-giving was unilateral and the tracking format was largely counselor-designed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support throughout: Kareem chose the method, tracking format, check-in timing, and tone of feedback, and his deliberately limited commitment was accepted without pressure. Consistently reinforced his ownership ('you're in control') and protected autonomy in the slip discussion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions elicited ratings and his own reasons, but evocation remained superficial: the family/home motivation and fear of failure were not deepened, importance was not probed, and the two-week commitment was largely counselor-negotiated rather than evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for Kareem's input and incorporated his preferences into the plan, but frequently positioned expertise with the clinician through front-loaded instructions. His question 'am I wasting your time?' hinted at a mild expert-client dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions successfully evoked change talk, including spontaneous mentions of possibly addressing home smoking and his daughter's concerns later. However, the pivotal daughter moment drew only a generic affirmation rather than exploration, and no commitment language was explicitly elicited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I like smoking') was met with neutral acknowledgment rather than reinforcement, and the slip-decoupling response effectively softened anticipated failure talk. Kept from a 5 by early heavy advice-giving and missed opportunities for double-sided reflection when sustain talk persisted.
