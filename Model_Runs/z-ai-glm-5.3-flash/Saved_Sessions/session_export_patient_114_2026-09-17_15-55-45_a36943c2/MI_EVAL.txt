# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with frequent affirmations throughout the session. However, affirmations were somewhat generic and formulaic rather than individualized to specific moments of vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that accurately captured Ryan's predicament, including balancing fun with protecting his future. Missed opportunities to more deeply explore the fear of relapse and the emotional weight of being the 'buzzkill' among friends.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was actively evoked through the confidence ruler, values exploration, and rehearsal, with Ryan producing notable motivation statements. However, key moments of self-efficacy (e.g., 'talking this through makes it feel more doable') were not amplified or deepened.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative session where Ryan's own language, choices, and feedback demonstrably shaped the final plan. The rehearsal and text drafting were true co-creations between equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ryan was consistently positioned as the expert, freely declined further rehearsal without pressure, and his choices drove the plan. Some turns presented prescriptive option menus rather than consistently reinforcing his ownership during moments of doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Ryan rather than lecturing, eliciting strengths, strategies, and commitment language. Could have explored underlying values (why education matters, long-term vision) more deeply before pivoting to tactics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership with genuine power-sharing — Ryan chose the buddy strategy, drafted the text himself, and picked the rehearsal. The counselor occasionally led by presenting menus of options rather than drawing solutions primarily from Ryan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Active use of evocative techniques (confidence ruler, 'what would raise confidence by one point', rehearsal) produced clear change talk from Ryan. Change talk was sometimes left unamplified rather than deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoided arguing with or reinforcing Ryan's minimizations, which is good. However, redirects were largely neutral with no double-sided reflections and missed opportunities to build discrepancy between minimizations ('everyone drinks more than me') and Ryan's stated value of education.
