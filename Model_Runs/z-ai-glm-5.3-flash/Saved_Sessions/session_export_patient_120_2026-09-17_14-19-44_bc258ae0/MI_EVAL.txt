# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgment, countering Sofia's shame ('You're not messed up') and earning her explicit gratitude. Warmth felt somewhat formulaic and affirmations were less individualized than ideal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A few accurate reflections appeared (grieving her old life), but most emotional content—fear, social anxiety, identity grief—was met with advice and scripts rather than deep reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol abstinence during pregnancy, progressing coherently from exploration to role-play to a concrete weekend plan with check-ins. Direction came through task focus rather than values-based goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction was largely counselor-directed with prescriptive advice lists, scripts, and plans; Sofia's own ideas were adopted only when she proposed them, and her input was rarely solicited before solutions were offered.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor affirmed Sofia's choices, offered options rather than directives, and let her pick the text and check-in schedule. However, advice menus subtly structured her choices and ownership of change was not consistently emphasized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The approach was primarily educational and skill-focused; Sofia's strongest motivation arose unprompted, with few evocative questions, no scaling, and little development of her baby-centered motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed the expert role, generating options and plans with minimal inquiry into Sofia's own ideas; her expertise was drawn upon only late and occasionally.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged spontaneously ('I wanna be responsible for this thing') but was rarely evoked, reflected, or deepened with targeted questions; role-play supported change only indirectly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and ambivalence was briefly normalized, but softening was passive—no double-sided reflections were used, and fears about friends and pressure were answered with problem-solving rather than ambivalence-resolving reflections.
