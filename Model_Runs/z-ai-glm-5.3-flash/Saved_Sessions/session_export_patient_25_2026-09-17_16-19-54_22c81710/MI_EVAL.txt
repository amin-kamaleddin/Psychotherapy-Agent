### Empathy

**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture Daniel's ambivalence (importance 4–5, confidence 7) and the social pressure dynamic. Misses deeper emotional exploration of the wife's concern and fear of social judgment, moving quickly to problem-solving instead.

**Comments:** Sustains clear focus on alcohol moderation at social events with no drift. Every exchange advances the plan—limit setting, pacing, refusal lines, role-play—ending with a concrete next step for Saturday's barbecue.

**Comments:** Plan is co-constructed from Daniel's own smoking cessation success; counselor role-plays Rick at his request and adapts to his self-identified people-pleasing weakness. Daniel's ideas (the daughter line, softened firm phrasing) substantially shape the session.

**Comments:** Regularly offers choice ('Which one sounds most like you?', 'Want to rehearse...?') and Daniel selects his own limit and lines. Occasionally steers with expert-suggested scripts and numbers (e.g., 2–3) and doesn't explicitly affirm Daniel's ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within via scaling questions and anchoring strategies to Daniel's prior success. He arrives at his own insights about people-pleasing, and confidence measurably rises from 7 to 8.5, ending with 'I feel ready.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly client-centered—incorporates Daniel's contributions as the anchor of the plan and lets him design the escalation cascade. At times slightly expert-driven by providing pre-written refusal lines and specific numbers before Daniel generates them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation using importance/confidence scaling, strategy choice questions, and commitment rehearsal. Daniel's change talk grows visibly from 'I could try that' to an 8.5 confidence rating and 'I feel ready.'

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about peer pressure and late-night family is acknowledged and reframed as solvable (refusal line, holding a seltzer). Counselor sometimes elaborates obstacles by scripting Rick's pushback lines and doesn't always pivot explicitly back to Daniel's reasons for change.
