# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently validates the client's silence, normalizes her protectiveness, and honors her boundaries without judgment or frustration, even under sustained resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, progressively deeper reflections (e.g., capturing how her mother's talking leaves less room for her) that directly enabled her first disclosures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Loose focus is defensible at the engagement stage, but no target or focus was named, and the 'less tight' question went unanswered and unrevisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way once the client tested him, with honest self-disclosure and shared agenda-setting; the early phase remained structurally asymmetric with repeated invitations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary control-transference for a mandated client—explicitly offering silence as an option, letting her set the pace, and transparently stating confidentiality limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out voluntary disclosures with low-pressure questions, but did not develop the emerging change talk (e.g., the 'tight/can't breathe' metaphor) into desire or need statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly locates expertise in the client ('you're the expert on your own life') and lets her contributions genuinely shape the session's direction and pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriate for engagement stage, but ripe change-adjacent moments like 'I can't breathe right' were left unexplored rather than deepened into the client's own desire for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk by reflecting it accurately and affirming her choices, though pivots are sometimes pure acceptance without a subtle bridge toward engagement.
