# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, even amid heavy sustain talk. Caution is normalized and reframed as strength with no judgment anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately capture content and underlying affect, and Jordan confirms their accuracy. Reflections consistently go beneath surface statements to meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on work stress, financial strain, and helplessness, building progressively toward a concrete commitment. Each exchange builds toward the action plan while remaining patient-paced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly defers to Jordan's expertise, and Jordan's ideas genuinely shape the session's course. The closing exchange is co-constructed and explicitly shared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice is explicitly and repeatedly affirmed ('if you choose to', 'it's yours'), with no advice imposed. Even the client's caution is honored as reasonable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Jordan's own reasoning, language, and insights via strategic evocative questions. Commitment language emerges organically and is amplified by affirmations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located with the client ('you know your workplace better than I do'), and the plan originates entirely from Jordan. Power sharing is at the highest level.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open, client-anchored questions skillfully escalate change talk from vague desire to commitment ('I'll ask... that's one flop, not my whole story'). Emerging change talk is reinforced and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Heavy sustain talk is never reinforced; double-sided reflections pair it with change talk while reframing outcomes. Sustain talk is softened without dismissal.
