# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, with affirmations and normalization of Margaret's defensiveness. Affirmations were somewhat generic and ambivalence/slips were not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured both sides of her ambivalence, including the fall's significance. Reflections rarely reached the deeper emotional layer (grief, loneliness, fear of injury).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus on alcohol-related fall risk throughout, with each turn advancing the agenda. Ended with a specific, testable plan for Thursday's pub night.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way; every decision point was a joint choice and Margaret's ideas steered the plan. The final plan was fully co-constructed — a model of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect for choice with permission-seeking and menus of options framed as her selection. Advice sometimes came as dense counselor-generated lists, diluting power-sharing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn almost entirely from Margaret via open questions, the confidence ruler, and barrier exploration. Her solutions and escalating change talk were direct products of skillful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Margaret was repeatedly positioned as the expert, and her ideas substantially shaped the session. Advice was sometimes offered quickly after permission, slightly weakening power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation produced escalating change talk and concrete commitments (tenner rule, hidden wine, refusal line). Confidence rose from 7 to 8, with change talk consistently reflected and built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged with neutral, non-reinforcing reflections and gentle pivots. Relied mostly on double-sided reflections rather than strategic reframes to actively diminish minimization.
