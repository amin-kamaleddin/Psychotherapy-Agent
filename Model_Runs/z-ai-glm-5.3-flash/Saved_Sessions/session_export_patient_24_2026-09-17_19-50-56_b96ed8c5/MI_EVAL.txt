# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard, validating Daniel's skepticism and honoring his 'fair warning' about wanting the beers back without any disapproval. Affirmations were specific and genuine ('That's wisdom, not weakness').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and emotionally attuned, with double-sided reflections capturing both sides of Daniel's ambivalence throughout. The clinician even anticipated his self-testing and validated the difficulty of examining a never-questioned behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained disciplined focus on alcohol use as the target while staying patient-centered, with each exchange advancing the agenda toward concrete planning. It ended with a specific, actionable plan and scheduled follow-up without any pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed from Daniel's own ideas, with the clinician reflecting, organizing, and affirming rather than prescribing. Daniel's comment about leaving with 'homework I actually sort of want to do' evidences the effectiveness of the collaborative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently reinforced Daniel's ownership with phrases like 'it's your call to make' and 'your data, your call,' and every plan element originated from the client. Advice was never imposed, and the follow-up was offered contingent on Daniel's agreement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key motivation emerged from Daniel's own words — his performance curiosity, 'I like winning,' and his spontaneous out-loud commitment. The clinician drew out the marathon/health discrepancy without stating it for Daniel, developing change talk from vague curiosity to commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned Daniel as the expert, exemplified by returning his 'would that even count?' question back to him rather than supplying an answer. Every commitment in the final plan came from Daniel, explicitly affirmed with 'You built this.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** This was the session's standout strength, skillfully evoking change talk at every turn and developing it progressively from vague curiosity to out-loud commitment. Daniel's own language ('I'd have to actually try it to find out') was highlighted and amplified effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Heavy sustain talk was acknowledged and validated without being reinforced, with each instance reflected accurately before pivoting toward Daniel's emerging change language. His self-doubt ('am I weaseling out?') was masterfully reframed as evidence of commitment — 'designing their own plan.'
