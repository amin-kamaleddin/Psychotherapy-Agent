# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and affirmations tied to Maria's own words, with skepticism never judged. Falls short of 5 due to some formulaic or slightly performative warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing ambivalence, fear, and frustration accurately. Sometimes moved to questions or planning before fully exploring emotional weight (e.g., friend's hoarse voice, prior dismissals).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear trajectory from vision-eliciting through barriers to a concrete, measurable, time-bound trial plan with defined check-ins. Even the steroid discussion was tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every decision point was asked rather than told, and the entire plan came from Maria's ideas. The relationship was framed as a mutual agreement between equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria set the terms of her commitment, chose her monitoring methods, and designed her own habit system. Mild steering appeared in post-commitment questions that functioned more to cement the plan than offer genuine choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All motivation statements came from the client, consistently elicited and amplified. The extended steroid information, though responsive to her explicit demand, was clinician-supplied persuasive content rather than elicited motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maria's contributions genuinely shaped the session and became the plan, with the clinician acting as facilitator, not architect. Her final 'you ended up leading' reflects true power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary progression of change talk from desire through ability to firm commitment, with each statement reflected or affirmed. Skilled evocation from a highly resistant, precontemplative client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged, reframed as legitimate caution, and redirected toward problem-solving without reinforcement. Direct medical information provision was a justified but real departure from ideal MI practice.
