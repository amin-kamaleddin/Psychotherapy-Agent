# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental with frequent affirmations and normalized setbacks. Affirmations are somewhat generic ('That's great') rather than individualized to Samuel's emotional experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that accurately captures Samuel's pride, surprise, and ambivalence. Reflections are brief and immediately stacked with multiple questions, limiting deeper exploration of stress-driven smoking and social pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight, purposeful focus on smoking reduction with every turn advancing a concrete plan: confidence rulers, refusal rehearsal, Marco as support, journaling, and a scheduled follow-up. Session ends with a specific, time-bound commitment and realistic relapse framing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely an interaction between equals; the plan is genuinely co-constructed and shaped by Samuel's own ideas. Mild expert-slant appears when the counselor supplies ready-made text wording and journal prompts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support — options offered as menus at every decision point, and Samuel's choices ('leave the text as is') are honored without pushback. Samuel self-generated the plan, sent the text on his own initiative, and reframed setbacks himself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from Samuel's own reasons, strategies, and words rather than persuasion. Confidence ruler movement from 6 to 7 and self-generated hope ('Maybe I can actually do this tomorrow') are strong evidence of effective evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicit partnership framing from the opening line ('You're the expert on your life') with consistent asking rather than telling. Occasionally question-heavy and slightly scripted, with some steering toward counselor-proposed wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation of change talk through targeted questions, confidence rulers, and behavioral rehearsal that Samuel practices successfully. Confidence rising from 6 to 7 and Samuel's abundant commitment language directly evidence effective cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally pivots from doubts to solutions and planning rather than amplifying sustain talk. Occasionally invites barrier discussion directly, eliciting some sustain talk, though it is productively resolved by Samuel himself.
