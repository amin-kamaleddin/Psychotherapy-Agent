# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains nonjudgmental tone and briefly validates Dylan's frustration, but warmth is formulaic and opportunities to affirm his strengths (grades, job, honesty) were missed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offers brief empathetic statements but quickly pivots to advice and checklists; fails to reflect the core conflict between Dylan's social life and the conduct consequences, with no complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session drifts from the referral target (underage drinking and accountability) toward hearing preparation and legal strategy; the target behavior is never explicitly explored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Flow is counselor-as-consultant delivering bullet points, with plans supplied rather than co-constructed; Dylan's 'Then what?' question was a missed opening for collaborative agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers genuine choice points and avoids pressuring Dylan to admit wrongdoing, but the advisory stance steers him toward a predetermined hearing-prep path without exploring what he wants to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Motivation and ideas come almost entirely from the counselor via tactics and scripts; Dylan's values, goals, and reasons for change are never elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor repeatedly positions itself as the expert providing checklists and scripts; Dylan's own expertise about his life and context is never recognized, resembling legal coaching more than partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Makes essentially no effort to evoke the client's own reasons for change; Dylan's spontaneous admissions ('I probably had a drink') and future-oriented question ('Then what?') were pivotal missed opportunities.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does not overtly reinforce Dylan's sustain talk, but also never redirects it or uses double-sided reflections—instead joining his externalizing of blame ('totally reasonable to want it dismissed').
