### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental regard throughout; her boundary ('don't tell me to take it out') was respected, and her commitment to the piercing was reframed as a strength rather than a problem.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently captured both content and meaning, e.g., distinguishing concrete evidence from vague warnings. The client's closing thanks confirms she felt deeply understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a coherent focus progressing toward concrete change steps. Agenda-setting was implicit via question sequencing rather than explicitly named collaboratively at the outset.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every substantive element of the plan originated from the client in response to open questions; the counselor positioned her as the expert on her own mouth and life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was affirmed from the first utterance ('that's completely your right') through the close; even the plan was framed with 'if any,' signaling no assumed commitment. Client reported feeling genuine choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation — monitoring criteria, baseline-photo plan, early-catch logic — came from the client's own mouth, elicited via strategic open questions. The counselor never supplied reasons or argued for removal.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly attributed expertise and the final plan to the client ('you did the work here'), and her contributions genuinely shaped the session's direction and outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully amplified the client's first hints of change talk ('maybe I'd look into it') into a specific commitment ('I'll ask for real examples at my next appointment'), a self-generated warning-sign list, and a monitoring plan — all client-originated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with or amplified; each instance was briefly acknowledged and reframed toward agency ('That's not backing down from your choice; that's making it stronger') without evoking reactance.
