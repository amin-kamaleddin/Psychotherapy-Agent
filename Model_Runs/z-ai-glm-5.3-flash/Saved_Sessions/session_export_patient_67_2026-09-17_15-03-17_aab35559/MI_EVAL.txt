# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental, normalizing Kenji's uncertainty; however, affirmations of his personal strengths were limited and somewhat formulaic, preventing a deeper level of positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately captured ambivalence ('You sound torn...') and validated anxiety, but reflections stayed brief and practical, leaving deeper emotional themes like loneliness and shame unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction with no drift, moving from assessment to personalized feedback to concrete steps and a time-bound follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sought permission before giving information, co-created tracking plans and message drafts, and let Kenji drive decisions; the dense risk-limits feedback block was the main shortfall from full negotiation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary support: 'You're in charge of what we focus on,' repeated choice-offering, framing cutting back as a non-all-or-nothing option, and accepting Kenji's limited commitment without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Kenji's own reasons, insights, and curiosity through open questions and a confidence scale; deeper exploration of his values and hopes beyond the practical plan would have elevated it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Kenji as the expert, with his own ideas (seltzer swap, Daisuke text) shaping the session; some informational exchanges leaned away from full mutuality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions like 'would you like to talk about next steps?' and the confidence scale elicited genuine change talk ('maybe... cutting back some?'); missed chances to deepen commitment language and reinforce his stated reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well with double-sided reflections and a pivot to feasible change ('not all-or-nothing') without amplifying resistance; the pivot was effective but not consistently strategic across all moments.
