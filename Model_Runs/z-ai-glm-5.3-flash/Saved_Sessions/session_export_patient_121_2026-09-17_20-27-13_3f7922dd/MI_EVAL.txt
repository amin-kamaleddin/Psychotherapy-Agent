# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with validation of Sofia's self-protectiveness ('being careful makes sense when you've been burned'). Missed opportunities to affirm her self-respect at defensive moments, such as 'I'm not gonna chase her down and look desperate.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured both content and underlying emotion ('the loneliness is real, but so is the risk'), and Sofia responded with increased openness ('that's actually kind of a nice way to put it').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on school conflict, isolation, and reconnection, progressing logically to a concrete first step. Withheld a 5 because there was no closing summary and the school avoidance referral concern was never explicitly addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner, asking rather than telling and building on Sofia's own ideas. Slightly undercut by moments of role confusion where the counselor supplied the client's side of the dialogue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed decisions as Sofia's ('You know her, and you know what feels safe — what's your instinct?') with no pressure or prescribing. However, twice supplied potential actions on her behalf ('I guess... I'd text her first?'), diluting her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from within via evocative questions, and Sofia's own reasoning drove the plan. Undermined at two points where the counselor voiced change talk and answers Sofia should have generated herself — a meaningful MI technique error.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Sofia as the expert ('You know the situation and the people better than anyone'), and her testing-the-friend idea became the working plan. Deducted because the counselor twice spoke her part for her, momentarily usurping her role as source of solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited genuine change talk ('I guess I could try the lunch thing') through evocative questions and highlighting her own openness. Significant flaw: the counselor voiced change talk on Sofia's behalf twice, weakening authentic evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Neither argued with nor amplified sustain talk, instead using skillful double-sided reflections pairing it with change-relevant material. Fell short of 5 when some sustain talk ('don't want to get my hopes up and look stupid') was met with reassurance rather than strategic redirection.
