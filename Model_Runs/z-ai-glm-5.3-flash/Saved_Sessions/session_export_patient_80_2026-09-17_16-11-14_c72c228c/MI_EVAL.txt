# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental tone with frequent affirmations tied to Luka's choices; however, affirmations remain brief and task-focused rather than conveying deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but rich emotional content (pride, partner's wishes, vulnerability) goes largely unreflected as the counselor pivots quickly to advice and logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior (alcohol-related anger); every exchange advances the concrete plan with a scheduled follow-up and no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent permission-seeking and Luka's preferences shape wording and tools, but the plan's architecture originates with the counselor, leaving Luka more a ratifier than co-author.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated affirmations of choice and accommodation of Luka's modifications; lessened by prescriptive menus and directive elements like the safety plan and drink placement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client motivation elicited via the small-change question and confidence ruler, but the counselor supplies many rationales; the 5/10 confidence rating and Luka's own reasons for change go unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine partnership elements like offering choices and asking permission, but the expert-driven structure of prescriptive lists and scripts limits Luka's input on plan design.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of a small-change question and confidence ruler eliciting commitment language; missed opportunities to deepen change talk by exploring the confidence rating or linking to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about drinking and pride is acknowledged briefly but tolerated rather than actively softened; no double-sided reflections or discrepancy development.
