# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and warmth, explicitly countering Farid's self-label of laziness. Fell short of top marks because affirmations occasionally felt formulaic and normalization of slips could be more explicit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including a standout capture of the 'heavy sameness' and guilt beneath the fatigue complaint. Some reflections trended toward reframing/cheerleading rather than staying with the client's emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity, with each response advancing momentum toward a concrete, measurable plan with a contingency. Even emotional content like 'stuck' is tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit joint framing ('let's put our heads together') with permission-style open questions and shared understanding checked at the close. Power is genuinely equal, with Farid positioned as the decision-maker.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are consistently left with Farid, with the counselor turning his request for validation back to his own judgment. The plan is entirely client-generated with no imposed advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions consistently draw out Farid's own reasons, values, and strategies, producing strong self-persuasion. Reliance on forward-looking hypotheticals rather than exploring deeper values history or past successes kept this from a 5.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly and explicitly located in Farid, and his ideas genuinely shaped the entire plan (lunch walks, trainers, calendar, Bilal). This is the hallmark of top-level partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire, evaluation, and commitment talk through questions like 'what would be different at the end of a week where you'd done it?' Missing explicit importance/confidence scaling and deliberate deepening kept this from a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently redirected without amplification, with barriers acknowledged but not belabored. At times self-doubt content was lightly re-voiced rather than pivoting fully toward change.
