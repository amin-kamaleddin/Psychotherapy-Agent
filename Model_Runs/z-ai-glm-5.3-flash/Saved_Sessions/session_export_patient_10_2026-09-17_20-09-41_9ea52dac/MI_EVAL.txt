# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout; missed interview reframed as 'my kids came first' and setbacks normalized with no judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections captured deep emotional nuance, naming the core fear of a clear path being 'snatched away,' which the client confirmed exactly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear, progressive focus on resolving the after-school childcare barrier, moving from vision to concrete action without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Work framed as joint puzzling; all content and decisions originated from Amina while the counselor supplied structure and credited her ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership consistently reinforced ('what feels most solvable to you,' 'you know how you work best'); the counselor never prescribed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within through open questions; Amina named her own reasons for change (independence, her daughter's question, stubbornness).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Amina positioned as the expert throughout; the plan was entirely hers, with the counselor mirroring and organizing her contributions back to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and deepened; Amina escalated from wanting to work to committing ('I'll call this week') and connecting action to identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged briefly then reframed as logistics rather than ability, consistently pivoting toward solutions the client generated herself.
