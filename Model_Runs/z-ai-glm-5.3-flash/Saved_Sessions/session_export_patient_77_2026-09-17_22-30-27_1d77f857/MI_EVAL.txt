# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment even when hopelessness was expressed. Normalized discouragement ('Feeling hopeless after that makes total sense') and validated her needs without labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, accurate reflective listening repeatedly captured the emotional core (loneliness, feeling invisible), confirmed by the patient ('Yeah... exactly. That's it.'). Also accurately reflected her shift from joke-making to sincerity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on weight-loss concerns, with each question advancing momentum toward a concrete next step. The exploration of loneliness was skillfully tied back to sustaining change, with no aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the encounter as equal partners and solicited Liyana's own definition of progress. All key ideas emerged from the patient via open questions, with the counselor affirming her expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy ('I don't have an agenda for you') and prompted the patient to own her step ('that would be mine'). No directives given; every plan originated from the client in her own timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited the patient's own reasons, hope, and self-efficacy through evocative questions rather than advice. No counselor-supplied reasons for change, a marker of top-level evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly framed the session as one between equals and followed through behaviorally, opening with an invitation for Liyana's agenda. All key ideas (Aina, dropping the joke) came from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently evoked strong client-generated change talk ('Maybe I could actually tell her for real,' 'I think I could actually do that') and deliberately reflected the commitment language to reinforce it. Produced a concrete, self-generated first step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally acknowledged sustain talk briefly and pivoted toward change without dismissing it. Minor deductions for reflecting plateau struggles and comparisons at length before redirecting, and for not using explicit double-sided reflections.
