# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard ('feeling this way doesn't mean anything is wrong with who you are'), with warmth and no judgment despite Samir's deflecting humor and resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, meaning-level reflections throughout, e.g., reframing 'like being lectured by my mother' as 'the stakes are being made to feel enormous' and noticing the shift from 'it's just pills' to 'maybe I pay attention.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on medication adherence with progressive movement from ambivalence to a concrete two-week experiment, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong equal-partner stance: co-constructed the self-monitoring plan using Samir's own resources (sister, phone alarms); Samir confirmed 'you actually asked what I think instead of telling me.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Samir's ownership ('thinking about this in your own way, on your own terms'), framing non-commitment as acceptable and letting the plan be entirely his.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from the client — his own reasons (energy, mornings, family), experiment design, and success criteria; no reasons were supplied by the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Samir as the expert ('You know your routines, your body, your family better than any doctor does'), and his contributions clearly shaped the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and developed change talk through open questions ('what would be the first small sign'), moving Samir to concrete commitments around mornings, energy, and a two-week self-monitoring experiment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed rather than argued with (e.g., 'seeing is believing for you'), but occasional full reflections of sustain talk before pivoting meant slightly less strategic softening than ideal — double-sided reflections could help.
