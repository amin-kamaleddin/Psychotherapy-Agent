# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates respect and non-judgment, affirming Elira's plan and normalizing struggles. However, warmth occasionally feels formulaic, with repeated templated phrases like 'I'm proud you built something.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections capture the anxiety-avoidance loop, but reflections are sparse relative to questions. The emotional core of Elira's freeze response, fear of failure, and 'already behind' shame is never explored with depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior of studying despite anxiety, tying every turn back to the concrete plan. The direction is sometimes overly narrow and mechanical, circling the same checklist details in the final third.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor proposes co-constructing a plan and genuinely incorporates Elira's own ideas (pillow cue, water bottle, naming anxiety). Frequent bundling of multiple questions and suggestions into single turns shifts the interaction toward a guided checklist rather than balanced dialogue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is explicitly framed throughout, and Elira sets her own check-in timing, which the counselor honors. The counselor often presets options and offers advice-laden micro-instructions rather than letting Elira generate her own solutions first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions elicit some motivation from within, but much of the session's content is counselor-supplied rather than drawn from Elira. Her own reasons for why following through matters to her are never explored, so motivation is reinforced more than genuinely evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elira's contributions shape the plan and she is offered choices, but the counselor retains the expert role in generating strategies and structures the agenda through rapid multi-question turns. Elira is not explicitly acknowledged as the expert on her own anxiety.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively evokes and reinforces change talk through scaling questions and invitations to verbalize her plan, with Elira's change talk growing across the session. Deeper evocation of her own reasons and values for change is missing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but it recurs repeatedly ('why bother,' 'I'll flake') without strategic softening or double-sided reflections. The core barrier of 'already behind, so why bother' goes entirely unaddressed.
