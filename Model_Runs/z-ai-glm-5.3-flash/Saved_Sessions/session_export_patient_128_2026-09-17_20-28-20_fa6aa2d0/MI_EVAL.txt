# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with specific genuine affirmations ('really honest self-observation'). Tyler's minimization was met with a double-sided reflection rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and meaning. Notably reframed ambivalence into the core insight that fun comes from friends, not drinks.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on alcohol use and the referral target, with each exchange building toward a concrete plan. No aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—the plan evolved entirely from Tyler's own ideas, with his input solicited before each summary. His 'I don't knows' were met with curiosity rather than direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tyler chose which strategies to try, and the closing framed next steps as 'something small to see how it goes.' Counselor never prescribed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation emerged from Tyler; the counselor never argued for change. Questions like 'What would a win look like that night?' elicited his values and a self-generated plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in Tyler ('You know your world better than anyone'). All plan elements—cash limit, separate driving, later arrival—came from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk, yielding commitment language ('I don't want a repeat of last time') and a vivid future vision. Each piece was reinforced by reflection and built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally softened sustain talk well via double-sided reflections, but the session ended just as the most potent sustain talk (FOMO, fear of being seen as boring) was opened, leaving it unresolved. A confidence/readiness scaling question would have consolidated the close.
