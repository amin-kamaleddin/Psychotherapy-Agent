# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and respectful with no judgment about her inactivity or dislikes, but affirmations were generic ('great,' 'excellent') rather than individualized strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections captured her motivation and frustration well, but the session quickly shifted to logistics, leaving emotional themes like body confidence and fear of judgment in yoga unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on re-establishing activity with concrete commitments (YMCA Thursday, Saturday walk, Friday 10 check-in), though some exchanges drifted into logistics minutiae.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were built around Leila's preferences and shaped jointly, but the counselor tended to deliver advice packages for approval rather than co-creating each element.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were regularly framed as Leila's own with permission-seeking and option-phrasing, though some guidance edged toward prescription ('simple rule...') and her own judgment was rarely affirmed explicitly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good early evocative questions and a confidence scaling query, but evocation was front-loaded; later the counselor supplied checklists and rules instead of drawing out her values and anticipated success.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission was sought and input invited, but the counselor assumed the expert role with detailed scripts and checklists — Leila twice declined offered material, signaling over-supply.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Leila volunteered rich change talk and the counselor used scaling and commitment questions, but change talk was acknowledged rather than deepened with elaborate reflections or importance probes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced and barriers were briefly reframed, but extensive obstacle elaboration occupied significant airtime without double-sided reflections to pivot back toward motivation.
