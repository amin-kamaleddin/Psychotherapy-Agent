# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently showed unconditional positive regard, affirming Marisol's honesty, autonomy, and expertise ('You're the expert on your own life here') with no judgment despite her dismissive stance. Warmth at the close reinforced acceptance without requiring commitment to piercing removal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured her perspective well ('the wait and see approach makes sense when you can't see gum changes happening'). Reflections occasionally leaned strategic rather than emotional, with little exploration of the piercing's identity or emotional significance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained clear focus on the dentist's oral health concern with no topic drift, progressing from understanding her stance to a concrete monitoring plan. The session landed on a specific, actionable next step tied directly to the referral concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was genuine two-way problem-solving—the monitoring plan was co-constructed with Marisol supplying her own solutions ('keep the ring, get actual proof, decide from there'). Her explicit gratitude ('Appreciate you actually hearing me out') confirms she experienced true partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support framed everything as Marisol's choice ('a plan that's completely yours,' 'on your own terms') with no persuasive pressure toward removal. The counselor positioned her as the decision-maker with better information, which she explicitly appreciated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently drew motivation from within, eliciting her own reasons for monitoring and even prosocial talk about informing friends. However, evocation stopped short of deeper values exploration and never amplified or tested commitment beyond the monitoring compromise.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positioned expertise within the client and consistently invited her input on next steps. The resulting plan was entirely hers, visibly built from her contributions, and her closing gratitude confirmed genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation from a pre-contemplative client yielded her own concerns ('I don't want to lose teeth obviously') and a proactive plan ('I'd be okay with getting it checked more often'). Change talk was limited to monitoring rather than the piercing itself, and commitment language was never deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoided arguing with strong sustain talk, reflecting it neutrally and pivoting toward information-gathering and self-protection. However, some reflections verbally echoed sustain talk ('Keep the ring... both matter to you here') in ways that reinforced the status quo.
