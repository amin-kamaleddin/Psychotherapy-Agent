# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing the client's coping and removing shame (e.g., 'I hear zero embarrassment needed'). The client noted the session was 'way less preachy than I expected.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, meaning-level reflections (e.g., capturing the 'ugh' as uncertainty about double-practice nights). Client responses like 'That feels honest' confirmed being understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with no topic drift, progressing logically from vague stress to a concrete, testable plan with confidence rating and follow-up scheduled.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session exemplified equal-partner collaboration, with the client driving all decisions—target behavior, accountability partner, environmental changes, and follow-up timing. The counselor's role was purely facilitative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan elements were entirely client-generated, with the counselor explicitly crediting ownership ('that's your doing, not mine') and framing honesty about slips without judgment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within—the client articulated her own reasons and measures rather than receiving lectures. Voluntarily extending her goal timeline was the strongest evidence of internalized motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary power sharing—the video-game plan, success metrics, pen-relocation strategy, and check-in timing all originated from the client, with the counselor consistently crediting the client's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk through highlighting the client's own words, success measures, and confidence scaling. The client generated sustained commitment, even spontaneously extending the plan beyond basketball season.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally pivoted away from sustain talk with strategic reflections toward solutions. The 'what might get in the way' question briefly invited obstacle talk, though it was skillfully redirected as preparation-focused planning.
