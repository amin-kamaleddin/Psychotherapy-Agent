# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained a neutral, nonjudgmental stance throughout, but the session felt transactional, dominated by form logistics with no affirmations or warm regard. The response to Kieran's disclosure of feeling flat was minimal, offering no warmth or normalization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some accurate reflections, notably capturing Kieran's exhaustion and going-through-the-motions experience. However, empathy was inconsistent and at times mechanical ('Okay — I hear you' repeated), and a significant disclosure went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session lacked therapeutic focus, consumed mostly by paperwork logistics rather than the target behavior. It ended with an unresolved quick check-in and no summary, forward movement, or plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Menus offered were so procedural they didn't constitute genuine power-sharing about the therapeutic work. No joint agenda was set and the interaction felt more like a clerk processing a client than partners exploring a concern.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor's relative strength: repeatedly emphasized choice with no pressure or coercion, honoring Kieran's preferences. Limited by the fact that autonomy offered related mostly to trivial procedural decisions rather than meaningful therapeutic choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was not meaningfully elicited; the one evocative question about what feels draining was not followed up. The poignant disclosure 'I stopped thinking about it' went completely unexplored, with no questions about values or hopes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Choices were superficially offered, but the counselor functioned largely as a form facilitator rather than a partner. Kieran's meaningful contributions were not built upon or used to shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Efforts to evoke change talk were rare and weak, with no change talk received or deepened. The opening provided by 'I stopped thinking about it' was not pursued, and no questions about desires, reasons, or needs for change were asked.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was appropriately not amplified, but there was no active softening — no double-sided reflections or strategic reframing. Some reflections risked consolidating sustain talk rather than redirecting it.
