# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, non-judgmental tone throughout, even in response to self-criticism and fear of failure. Falls short of a 5 due to somewhat formulaic affirmations and limited explicit normalization of past failures or unconditional regard toward her harsh inner critic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures Sofia's exhaustion, frustration, and worry about failing (e.g., 'That sounds really exhausting...'). Reflections were sometimes brief and quickly pivoted to questions or advice, missing opportunities to deepen understanding of her shame and self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on a concrete target behavior — every exchange advanced a specific, realistic plan (three 2-minute walks, defined success, barrier planning, reminder cues). No drift, with a fully specified behavioral commitment by session's end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Friendly tone and repeated check-ins ('Which third day feels best?') create surface collaboration, but key ideas (reset walks, if-then format, compassion phrases, supports) were counselor-generated. The dynamic was more counselor-proposes/client-approves than genuine co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some choices (days, wording options), but also a directive streak: 'non-negotiable' framing, supplied scripts, and suggested alarm wording. Sofia's autonomy would be better supported by eliciting her own ideas before offering recommendations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some good evocative questions drew out values and her own plan elements, and having her rehearse the plan aloud was well done. However, the counselor frequently answered their own questions with advice menus, shifting the motivational work from client to counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for Sofia's input and let her select among options, but most session content originated from the counselor. Sofia's 'I never thought about it that way' signaled she was receiving ideas rather than co-generating them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively invited change language ('What one small change would feel most doable?'), and Sofia produced strong change talk ending in hope ('it feels... possible'). Reinforced and had her rehearse the plan, though sometimes supplied content rather than consistently drawing solutions from her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce Sofia's doubts and used some helpful reframing ('Small, consistent actions beat big, brittle goals'). However, lacked deliberate double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk, and often bypassed ambivalence quickly to move into problem-solving.
