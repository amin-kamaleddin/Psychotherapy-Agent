### Acceptance

**Comments:** Communicates consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, affirming Camila's insights and normalizing her ambivalence and fears. No judgment or confrontation about drinking, even when she minimizes it.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captures both sides of her ambivalence and subtle meaning. Reflections consistently deepen the conversation rather than merely paraphrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
