# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with Elias explicitly stating he felt accepted. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and repetitive, lacking fully individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Elias's physical limits and ambivalence well, with good perspective-taking. Deeper emotional content (fear of decline, the 'why bother' feeling) was acknowledged but not fully explored, and reflections sometimes blended into advice-seeking questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on increasing activity from opening question to a fully specified plan (Thursday 9 AM, park route, shoes by door, follow-up). Every turn advanced the target behavior without drift, with regular plan summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Elias contributing route, timing, and support details. Some option menus subtly steered rather than open-endedly invited Elias's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary reinforcement of client choice — every decision offered as a preference, and Elias's rejection of reminders and wife-scripting was respected without pushback. Elias's preferences shaped the entire plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within via open questions, confidence scaling, and inviting Elias to define success. Deeper values (health, dignity, connection to wife) were not explored, keeping evocation somewhat surface-level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor deferred to Elias's expertise about his body and routines, and checked preferences before offering ideas. Some suggestions were presented in a menu-driven, slightly expert fashion rather than fully elicited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation produced concrete commitments ('ten minutes might be doable,' 'I'll go. That much I can promise'). Missed opportunities to deepen commitment by exploring why the change matters to him.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The 'bench counts as success' reframe effectively softened Elias's all-or-nothing thinking, with commitment to reinforce it at follow-up. Some responses to sustain talk were brief acknowledgments rather than strategic pivots.
