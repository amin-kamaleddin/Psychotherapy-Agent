# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental; validates Ethan's reluctance and harm-reduction goal. Missed opportunities for explicit affirmations of strengths keep it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries that capture Ethan's ambivalence well. Reflections remain brief and rarely explore emotional depth around belonging and parental surveillance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing risky drinking from opening to closing. Logical session progression ties every exchange back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong shared decision-making with permission-seeking and Ethan freely editing the plan. Counselor still dominates content delivery with expert-supplied strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Everything framed as optional with consistent permission-asking and acceptance of client rejections. Some advice menus feel prescriptive and successes aren't explicitly credited to Ethan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong opening and confidence ruler draw out Ethan's own solutions, which he largely generates himself. Mid-session shifts to informing with counselor-supplied strategies and scripts rather than evoking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ethan's input genuinely shapes the plan, and his decisions are honored throughout. Heavy reliance on counselor-supplied menus of options rather than evoking his ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Substantial change talk emerges via open prompts and confidence ratings. Counselor misses chances to reflect, deepen, and consolidate Ethan's commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but occupies substantial airtime without strategic softening. Few double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk or highlighting discrepancy.
