# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated respect and nonjudgment, normalizing Liyana's struggle without criticizing her snacking. However, explicit affirmations of her courage and honesty—especially around vulnerable disclosures—were largely absent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured her frustration and the emotional nature of her snacking. When she disclosed loneliness and having no close friends, the counselor pivoted to problem-solving rather than sitting with the painful emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus, progressively narrowing from snacking to evening triggers to one concrete plan for tonight. Every exchange advanced toward a specific, testable goal with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was largely collaborative, with joint pattern-spotting and integration of her ideas into the plan. However, the option-menu format sometimes presented pre-formed solutions rather than truly co-generating them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforced Liyana's ownership through genuine choices and permission-seeking before questions and suggestions. No coercive language appeared, and the plan was built entirely from her selected options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The 0-10 scales effectively drew out her own importance and rising confidence (4 to 7), and her personal solutions were elicited rather than imposed. Some evocation relied on structured tools rather than open-ended exploration of deeper values and loneliness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Liyana was repeatedly positioned as the decision-maker, and her own ideas shaped the plan. The frequent suggestion of scripts and A/B menus edged somewhat toward expert-driven problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked via scaling questions, and her confidence rose from 4 to 7 across the session. Strong change statements like 'at least I wouldn't be staring at the kitchen' went unreflected and unreinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged without amplification, and self-blame was gently reframed toward curiosity. Double-sided reflections honoring both sides of her ambivalence could have been used more.
