# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with affirmations for Naomi's efforts and warm responses to her self-disclosure. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic ('That makes sense') and lack individualized depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; responses are dominated by psychoeducation, symptom checklists, and strategy lists. Naomi's expressions of shame and fear ('the problem's me') are met with correction and reassurance rather than reflections of the emotion underneath.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistent focus on Naomi's work anxiety and progresses logically from assessment to skills to a concrete planned exposure. However, the agenda is counselor-driven toward skill delivery rather than checked against Naomi's own stated priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session functions largely as advice delivery, with Naomi's role limited to accepting or adapting counselor-generated plans. Even collaborative moments, like the role-play, are counselor-directed rather than jointly shaped.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and lets Naomi choose her starting strategy, showing some respect for choice. However, the interaction is advice-dense and prescriptive, with no statements reinforcing Naomi's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all strategies and motivation come from the counselor's toolkit rather than being drawn out of Naomi. Her emerging self-motivation ('I did spend money to be here') and self-generated insights are left unexplored, with almost no open questions about her values or ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, delivering toolkits, symptom lists, and step-by-step plans with minimal elicitation of Naomi's own ideas. Her question about whether her starting point is 'reasonable' reveals she perceives the counselor as the authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The change talk in the session is generated spontaneously by Naomi, not deliberately evoked, deepened, or reflected by the counselor. Opportunities to amplify her hope at the end are met with a plan recap instead of consolidating change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** While the counselor doesn't reinforce sustain talk, Naomi's doubt and catastrophizing are met with reassurance and fact-based reframes rather than double-sided reflections. Sustain talk is effectively buried under additional content rather than resolved collaboratively.
