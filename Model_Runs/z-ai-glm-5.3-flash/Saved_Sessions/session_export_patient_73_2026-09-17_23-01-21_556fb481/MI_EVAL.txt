# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, affirming Linda's efforts ('That's not someone who's given up') and never judging her failures. Warmth and respect are maintained throughout with no disapproving language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captures both content and emotion, including the opening complex double-sided reflection and the deeper emotional layer ('Proud — that feeling has been a long time coming'). Linda repeatedly validates the accuracy of the reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on smoking cessation and progressively narrows to a concrete behavioral target with a follow-up plan. Momentum is guided without pressure, ending with a specific weekly commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work is framed as joint ('walking alongside you,' 'figure out that first hour of the day together'), and the plan is co-constructed from Linda's ideas. The counselor responds flexibly to her request for accountability and negotiates follow-up explicitly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is repeatedly reinforced ('on your terms,' 'a plan you're actually choosing'), and all strategies originated from Linda. The counselor honors her pacing and never prescribes solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within Linda via evocative questions about past successes, anticipated pride, and coping strategies. This elicited strong desire, ability, and commitment language and a self-generated plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Linda is explicitly positioned as the expert ('You know your life better than anyone'), and all first-step options came from her. Power-sharing is evident in her choices substantially shaping the session plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and amplified through highlighting Linda's own statements ('That's you noticing something working'), evocative questions, and reflecting hope language ('That's hope talking'). This produced commitment language and a concrete first step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and pivoted toward change, with hopelessness effectively reframed. However, the social identity tied to smoking and fear of telling friends could have been explored or reframed more deeply, and some double-sided reflections repeated rather than progressively resolved ambivalence.
