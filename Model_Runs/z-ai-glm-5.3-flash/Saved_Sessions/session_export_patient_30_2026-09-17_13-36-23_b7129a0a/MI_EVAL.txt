# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with genuine affirmations of David's self-advocacy. However, the repeated scripted safety questions felt formulaic and detracted from individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections (e.g., worry given his dad's history), but key emotional openings about aging fears and his father's MI were missed in favor of lists and psychoeducation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on medication adherence with a logical progression to concrete questions and a follow-up plan. Slightly weakened by content delivery and repeated safety questions briefly derailing focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some menu-style options and a well-received co-produced note, but the session was dominated by counselor-constructed lists and instructions rather than joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Options presented honestly without pressure, and David's choices ('I'm not promising anything') were accepted without pushback. Deducted because the plan was largely counselor-constructed rather than David-chosen.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to draw out David's own motivations; his spontaneous reasons (family history, wife's encouragement) went unexplored. The counselor relied on informing and task-giving rather than evoking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing via preference questions and a usable note, but the session was mostly counselor-driven, with David's expertise (work, wife, family history) shaping it less than the counselor's agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged and was affirmed, but the counselor failed to strategically deepen it with open questions, notably skipping the pivotal reflection about his father.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** No arguing with sustain talk, and reframing information about rare side effects and time-limited trials softened resistance. However, softening relied on information-giving rather than skillful MI techniques like double-sided reflections.
