# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains respect and normalizes Maya's concerns without judgment, but warmth is limited and the tone is transactional. Little affirmation of her strengths beyond the opening, and her cynicism is bypassed in favor of problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections capture Maya's unfairness narrative well, but reflections remain shallow and quickly pivot to question batteries or option menus. Deeper emotional content—shame, the coach's disappointment, feeling watched—is left unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the party incident and its aftermath, following Maya's agenda appropriately. However, it narrows to crisis-management logistics while the underlying behavior—alcohol use—is never directly addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly presents numbered menus and ready-made scripts, positioning Maya as a recipient of services rather than an equal partner. Maya's edits are adopted, but she is never invited to generate her own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and respects Maya's edits and her refusal to overpromise, which supports some autonomy. But options are always counselor-generated, with directive advice dominating over reinforcement of her ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is treated as residing in the counselor's scripts rather than within Maya; her goals and values are never explored. Glimmers of her own motivation are passed over rather than developed into change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes an expert role, supplying scripts, tips, and step-by-step instructions while Maya chooses among pre-made options. There is little recognition that Maya holds expertise about her own school and social world.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no effort is made to elicit Maya's own motivations or language for change; the session is dominated by scripts and strategies. Her self-generated change language ('I made a mistake,' 'I'm not saying I'm gonna do it again') goes unreinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk ('I just want people to drop it,' 'there's gonna be parties, that's just how it is') is largely ignored or bypassed. No double-sided reflections or gentle pivots toward her values are used to soften it.
