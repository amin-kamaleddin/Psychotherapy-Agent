# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and affirming, honoring Aisha's limits including her alcohol refusal; however, warmth is somewhat formulaic and ambivalence isn't deeply normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captures her downtime needs and ambivalence, but deeper emotional content—fear of cancer, guilt about her son—is summarized rather than explored with emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on evening smoking reduction throughout, culminating in a specific, measurable goal ('Three in the evenings. That's my number.') without drifting despite the alcohol detour.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions are framed as joint and Aisha's answers shape the plan, though rapid-fire option menus occasionally feel more like a structured protocol than free-flowing partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support throughout: opens by handing control to the client, repeatedly offers choices, frames setbacks as non-failure, and never pressures on alcohol.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is clearly drawn from within—Aisha articulates her own reasons, sets her goal, and self-assesses confidence—though her powerful motherhood motivation could have been evoked and expanded further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inclusive language and client-generated planning are present, but question-stacking and heavy option menus create a guided, structured feel that dilutes genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and confidence scales evoke strong change talk which the counselor affirms and builds on, but the most powerful motivation (her son) is not consistently deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing, but responses to minimization and alcohol deflection amount to acquiescence rather than strategic double-sided reflections linking her values to the minimized behavior.
