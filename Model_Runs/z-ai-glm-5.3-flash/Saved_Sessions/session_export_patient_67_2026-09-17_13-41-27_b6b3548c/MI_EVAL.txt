# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and neutral with one early affirmation, but warmth is sparse and opportunities to affirm Kenji's honesty and ambivalence are missed. Regard is basic rather than consistently individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; question barrages and informational responses replace reflections. Kenji's fear about liver numbers and Sunday shakiness goes unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, focused structure on alcohol reduction with concrete steps and a defined review point. Direction sometimes tips into premature goal-setting given Kenji's readiness of 4.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is largely counselor-led with pre-formatted plans and advice delivered before exploring Kenji's perspective. He shapes minor details but major decisions are presented as settled.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Menu-style choices are offered, but the overall posture is directive with prescriptive plans overriding Kenji's stated pacing. His ownership of the decision is never explicitly reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is informed into Kenji (risks, liver numbers) rather than drawn out of him. His own reasons, values, and curiosity are left unexplored amid heavy reliance on education and scripts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor acts as expert delivering templates, scripts, and plans prescriptively. Kenji's choices are accommodated but the agenda and plan structure remain counselor-defined.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Kenji spontaneously offers meaningful change talk, but none of it is deepened with affirmations or evocative questions. The readiness ruler follow-up ('Why a 4?') is notably missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Kenji's sustain talk ('forget it, drink like normal,' weekends being hard) is left unaddressed. No double-sided reflections or reframing of his ambivalence are used.
