# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout, with unconditional positive regard ('no failure—just data,' 'your realism is a strength'). No criticism even when the client admits relapse risks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflections early ('caught between wanting connection and noticing real costs'), but some turns move quickly to planning and advice, leaving deeper feelings like anxiety about disconnecting less explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on reducing evening phone use to improve sleep, with every exchange tied to the concrete three-night experiment. No topic drift across the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Joint problem-solving with client-chosen supports, wind-down, and check-in system. Falls short of 5 because early turns were counselor-heavy, proposing the experiment structure before co-constructing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy respected ('no promises,' optional check-in, 'no failure—just data'), though prescriptive menus (scripted texts, specific wind-downs) slightly reduced full client ownership of choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of readiness rulers and open questions elicited the client's own success criteria and growing commitment ('maybe a fourth night'). Evocation was front-loaded, with later turns focused on logistics rather than deepening intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client's ideas (flatmate accountability, check-in system, text wording) shaped the plan and her agency was respected. The opening barrage of four questions and counselor-generated wind-down menus diluted pure power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Readiness rulers moved the client from 'maybe a 4' to 'a weak 5' to 'maybe a fourth night,' showing effective evocation. Sometimes the counselor supplied the plan herself rather than letting Amina fully generate her own steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('it's how I stay in touch,' 'that's my relaxation') was acknowledged rather than argued against, and 'no failure—just data' softened relapse fear. Some direct problem-solving around barriers kept focus on them rather than pivoting to change reasons.
