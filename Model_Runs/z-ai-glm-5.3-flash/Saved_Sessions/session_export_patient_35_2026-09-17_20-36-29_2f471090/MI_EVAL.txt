# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with specific, genuine affirmations that reframe Elena from 'bad mom' to someone fighting for her kid. No judgment or disapproval anywhere, even when Elena discloses smoking and minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including excellent double-sided reflections of ambivalence and emotional nuance. Skillfully mirrors Elena's fear of judgment and even her surprise at her own insight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking while linking it to Elena's stated concern about her son's ear infections, moving progressively from ambivalence to a concrete plan. Each exchange builds momentum without abandoning the client's pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: offers genuine choices, proposes joint action, and builds Elena's ideas (porch breaks, evidence appointment) into the plan. No expert-driven advice-giving at any point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly and repeatedly reinforces Elena's ownership ('this is your pace,' 'nothing happens here that you don't choose') and celebrates her self-defined success criteria. Affirms 'no forever promises' rather than pressuring commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from Elena rather than supplying it—her porch-break idea, success criteria, and insight about needing alone time all came from her. Names and reinforces these self-generated insights rather than taking credit.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively positions Elena as the expert ('You're the expert on what feels like a real break'), and her contributions directly shape the treatment plan. Power-sharing is genuine and consequential.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk, moving Elena from 'I've thought about quitting' to a full commitment stated in her own words. Reflects and amplifies change talk each time it appears, including a textbook commitment-strengthening technique at the close.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk without dismissing it, using double-sided reflections and legitimizing concerns before redirecting to Elena's own 'I could try' statements. Elena's language steadily shifts toward change.
