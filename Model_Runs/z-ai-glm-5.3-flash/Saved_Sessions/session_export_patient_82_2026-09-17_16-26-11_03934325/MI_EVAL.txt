# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with clear confidentiality limits and honoring Lukas's request to keep this from his coach. Warmth is somewhat functional, with few explicit affirmations of his courage in disclosing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections about belonging vs. goals, but brief and often followed by advice. Deeper emotional content — fear of being 'the weird one,' worry about seeming dramatic, minimization — is missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and builds a concrete plan (refusal lines, exit text, ally recruitment). Slightly narrow, focusing on tactics without connecting to Lukas's underlying values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Lukas co-creates plan elements (exit text, drink strategy) and declines rehearsal, but the overall structure is expert-driven. He mostly selects from the counselor's menu rather than generating ideas together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes Lukas's choice, with confidentiality reassurance and acceptance of his preference for a single check-in. Weakened by advice menus that steer toward pre-selected tactics.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is largely supplied by the counselor via reasons, scripts, and justifications rather than evoked from Lukas. Good evocative material ('foggy for two days,' fear of losing his spot) is generated but never developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly defers to Lukas's preferences and lets him shape the plan, but also takes an expert role with pre-packaged scripts and leads the agenda with rapid-fire suggestions. The counselor's framework dominates the session structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early forward-looking questions elicited genuine change talk, and rehearsal produced commitment-adjacent language. However, self-motivated statements are acknowledged but not deepened with confidence or commitment questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced but is largely bypassed rather than strategically softened. No double-sided reflections or efforts to resolve ambivalence about friendships and judgment.
