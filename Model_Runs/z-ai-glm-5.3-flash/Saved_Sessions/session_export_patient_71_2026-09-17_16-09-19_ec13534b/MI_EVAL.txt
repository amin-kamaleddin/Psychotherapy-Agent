# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations like 'Love this' and 'That insight is huge.' No judgmental language even when Laura expressed doubts or lower confidence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured Laura's ambivalence and perfectionist self-talk accurately. Reflections occasionally read as logistical summaries, and emotional topics like why self-love is deprioritized were lightly touched before pivoting to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus from values clarification through concrete commitment, with each exchange building momentum toward action. Drift toward painting and enjoyment was deliberately guided back to realistic weekly scheduling.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Laura generated most ideas (cue design, text wording, hike logistics) with shared decision-making. Slight deduction for the scripted, repeated 'I can walk through the handout' offer and occasionally offering to draft things Laura already did well.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Laura's ownership through menus of options, explicit permission-seeking, and acceptance of her choice to defer the volunteering reflection. Validated her desire to build her own structure rather than over-rely on Dana.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and open questions effectively drew out Laura's own reasons and insights. Occasional deduction for supplying solutions (reframes, alarm labels) rather than letting Laura generate them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Laura was positioned as the expert, with her contributions substantially shaping the plan. The repeated offer to draft messages slightly tipped toward doing for the client, though her own drafts were affirmed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful scaling questions moved Laura's confidence from 6 to 8 and elicited her own motivations and commitment language. Concrete commitment consolidation included the Dana text, 8:30 cue, and Saturday hike.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective double-sided reflections and reframing of the 5-minute fallback as a 'legitimate reset' softened perfectionist sustain talk. Occasionally restated barriers rather than always pivoting away.
