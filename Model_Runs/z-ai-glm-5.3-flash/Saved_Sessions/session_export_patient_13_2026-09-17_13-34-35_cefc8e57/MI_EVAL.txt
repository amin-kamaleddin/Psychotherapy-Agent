# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations and explicit gratitude for openness; however, repetitive verbatim safety screening felt mechanical rather than attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections showed genuine perspective-taking on Anara's fear and overwhelm, but reflections were often brief and quickly followed by advice lists, limiting deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintained good focus, narrowing from diffuse distress to a concrete micro-plan; some drift into generic advice, but the thread to avoided medical care was preserved.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were offered and the plan was genuinely co-constructed with Anara editing her own message; collaboration was diluted by solution menus delivered before exploring her ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong pacing support and honoring the request to defer the doctor discussion, though the counselor repeatedly re-introduced doctor options despite her wish to wait.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open-ended questioning allowed Anara to name her fears, but the counselor predominantly supplied scripts and tips rather than drawing out her motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anara's contributions shaped the plan and agenda, but frequent slips into an expert role with pre-packaged lists positioned the counselor as advice-giver rather than partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anara spontaneously offered meaningful change talk that was acknowledged but not actively deepened with evocative questions, scaling, or values exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument, but the counselor kept returning to doctor suggestions despite deferral requests and used no double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
