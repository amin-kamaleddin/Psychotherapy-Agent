# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout—pre-pregnancy drinking reframed as 'not carelessness, it was not knowing,' and no judgment or disapproval appears anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections track both content and feeling, and Sofia repeatedly confirms being understood, feeling 'a little less alone with it.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Steady focus guides Sofia from ambivalence to concrete plans and commitment, ending with specific actionable next steps and a follow-up visit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative ('let's figure this out together'), with openly negotiated agenda and a plan co-constructed from Sofia's ideas and the psychiatrist's structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced at nearly every turn with options rather than directives and explicit permission framing ('Your call. We go at your pace.'), which Sofia herself recognizes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation emerges from Sofia herself—identity-level questions elicit her own reasons and her strongest commitment language, 'I need to for my kid.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sofia is explicitly positioned as the expert on her own life, and her ideas substantially drive each subsequent exchange—power sharing at the highest level.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation of success memories and explicit highlighting of Sofia's own change language, producing rich client-generated commitment talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced and is consistently reframed and redirected toward actionable change; score is 4 only because fears and doubts appropriately occupy considerable airtime.
