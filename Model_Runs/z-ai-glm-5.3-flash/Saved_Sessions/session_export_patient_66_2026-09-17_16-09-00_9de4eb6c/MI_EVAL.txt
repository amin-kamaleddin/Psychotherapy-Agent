# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, accepting Kareem's resistance and ambivalence without confrontation. Few personalized affirmations of his strengths and self-awareness kept this from a higher score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections, but the emotional meaning of the 'earned' break and concerns about irritability and his daughter were glossed over. Later turns were largely psychoeducation with minimal reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking throughout with a concrete plan and scheduled follow-up. The middle section drifted into product-education at the expense of motivational progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Kareem proposing the slip log and coffee workaround and setting the follow-up. The NRT sections tipped toward lecture rather than shared discussion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy language with Kareem making every substantive choice and 'no promises' accepted without pressure. Pre-packaged menus and unsolicited instructions sometimes crowded his decision space.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good evocative questions early, but motivation-giving gave way to information-giving as the session progressed. Kareem's most committed statements emerged incidentally rather than through deliberate evocation and reinforcement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kareem's input shaped logistics and choices, but much of the session was instructional expert delivery. His own expertise about his habits was underused.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effective early evocation drew out genuine change talk. Key commitment statements like 'I don't see why I couldn't give this a proper go' were met with logistics rather than reinforcement or deepening.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged neutrally without arguing, but few double-sided reflections paired it with change talk. Some reflections amplified sustain talk, and redirections jumped to solutions rather than helping Kareem resolve ambivalence himself.
