# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unconditional positive regard with explicit non-judgment and repeated affirmations of the client's wisdom throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and captured meaning, though some stayed at summary level; emotional nuances like bracing for pushback were only lightly acknowledged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on phone-free family time; every exchange built progressively toward concrete action with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client's ideas drove the plan; solutions were co-created and the client's modifications were integrated as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership was consistently reinforced, with options framed as optional and her pacing preferences affirmed; no pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out strong client motivations and self-efficacy, but occasionally supplied ready-made options rather than evoking ideas, and didn't explore the deeper 'why' behind the value.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked permission and invited client choice, with her contributions clearly shaping the session; slightly tempered by offering multiple pre-made options rather than drawing ideas primarily from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evoked change language through scaling and commitment questions, yielding strong client change talk; could have deepened by reflecting and amplifying the change talk more before moving to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged worries about follow-through without amplifying them, pivoting to solutions; a brief validation of the concern missed the chance to reframe it as realistic planning.
