# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard even when the client was hostile about the waiting. Playfully honored his irritability ('complain about the waiting room all you like') without any judgment or defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, e.g., reflecting the puzzle of 'when' alongside the client's knowledge of the theory. Missed opportunities to deepen emotional reflection beneath the guarded, business-like exterior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unwavering focus on the target behavior despite deflection and irritation. Each response moved forward, ending with a specific, measurable commitment and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine exchange between equals—the client's own professional framing (deadlines, variance reports, invoices) became the entire structure of the intervention. The client explicitly recognized the fit: 'my kind of conversation.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy strongly supported—no promises demanded, plan entirely self-generated, and the client's refusal to promise was met with respect rather than pressure. Would be a 5 with more frequent explicit affirmations of the client's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from the client rather than supplied—no lecturing on diet or exercise. Every key insight (smallest viable change, weekly review, variance framing) came from the client through skilled questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in the client ('that's not me designing your plan; that's you'), and the plan emerged from his own accounting framework. His contributions visibly shaped every element of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was systematically evoked and amplified—the 'What would you tell a client?' reframe elicited a concrete plan, which was deepened and summarized in the client's own words. Desire, ability, and commitment statements were consistently reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and elegantly reframed, including reversing 'I haven't got time' into 'you've just made some.' At times sustain talk was simply accepted rather than actively worked with, though this showed appropriate respect for his self-knowledge.
