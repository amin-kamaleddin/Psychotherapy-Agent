# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, warm, nonjudgmental stance, validating David's skepticism as normal rather than dismissing it. Warmth is occasionally formulaic and past failed quit attempts are not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures both sides of David's ambivalence and functional aspects of smoking. Reflections sometimes stay at content level, missing deeper emotional material like fear of failure and worry about his wife's judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus from triggers to a concrete commitment to medication options, with each turn advancing toward cessation without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is consistently between equal partners, with information handed back for David's evaluation and plans co-constructed from his own ideas. The counselor answers questions while preserving decision-making authority with David.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, consistent support for David's choice, asking what matters most to him and declining to name a 'best' medication option. No pressuring or directives appear anywhere in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within, with David supplying his own reasons and commitment language. Evocation becomes secondary to psychoeducation in the final exchanges, and confidence beyond the first week is under-explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positions David as the expert and his contributions genuinely shape the session agenda. Power-sharing is active and consistent, not perfunctory.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent evocation of change talk, culminating in David's self-motivated commitment ('porch coffee, no cigarette'). The counselor skillfully consolidates commitment language using David's own words.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement, using double-sided reflections that hold ambivalence while gently orienting toward change. Some reflections restate barriers without fully resolving ambivalence.
