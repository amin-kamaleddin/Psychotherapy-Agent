# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, non-judgmental tone with some warmth ('Be kind to yourself'), but offered no explicit affirmations of Dante's strengths or past discipline, and warmth remained generic rather than individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Brief acknowledgments like 'you're juggling a lot' appeared, but reflections were minimal and quickly replaced by advice lists; deeper feelings such as fear of failing again were left unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on increasing physical activity and ended with a specific, achievable walking plan, though the focus was imposed via advice and closing repetitions were redundant.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor functioned largely as an advice dispenser after the opening, presenting expert recommendations rather than jointly problem-solving Dante's stated barriers or seeking his input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One meaningful choice ('Which of those feels most doable?') supported autonomy, but the overall style was directive with verbatim checklist instructions repeated across multiple turns.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Questions were logistical rather than evocative; there was little exploration of Dante's values, hopes, or confidence, and his motivation emerged entirely from himself without counselor elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor adopted an expert stance, offering prescriptive solutions before exploring Dante's own ideas, and responded to Dante's walk suggestion with another checklist instead of building on it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante generated meaningful change talk ('Tonight I can try... maybe even good'), but the counselor never evoked or deepened it with reflections, affirmations, or open questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was briefly normalized ('next week trap') but not strategically engaged; there was no double-sided reflection using Dante's own change-oriented words to resolve ambivalence.
