# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, honoring Eric's defensive stance and affirming his openness. Warmth occasionally feels formulaic, with limited explicit normalizing of his ambivalence or deep personal regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections capture the functional and social role of smoking in Eric's life. Reflections sometimes serve as transitions to option menus rather than deeper emotional exploration of the cough fear or wife's worry.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on smoking, progressing from ambivalence to a specific, measurable behavioral experiment. Momentum sometimes stalls in repeated menu-offering loops before Eric selects an option.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is largely between equals, with the plan substantially built from Eric's own suggestions and conditions validated. Frequent counselor-provided option menus slightly tilt the power balance away from eliciting Eric's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Eric's ownership — accepting his 'experiment, not commitment' framing, framing results as 'info, not a verdict,' and asking permission before exploring. No pressuring or controlling language is present.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers successfully elicit self-motivating statements, and Eric articulates his own criterion for change. However, much of the session relies on counselor-generated proposals, missing rich hooks like what his wife's worry means to him or his 'prove them wrong' framing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Eric is repeatedly positioned as the expert, and the experiment plan was substantially co-created from his own words. Frequent menu options keep the counselor in a proposal-generating role that crowds Eric's own idea generation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Importance/confidence rulers and a concrete commitment evoke notable change talk from Eric. But opportunities to deepen it — e.g., his admission that the cough 'bugs me most' — are passed over rather than amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is reflected without reinforcement and occasionally reframed. However, several responses restate sustain talk at length, and double-sided reflections pairing it with Eric's own concerns (cost, cough, wife's worry) are underused.
