### Acceptance

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good complex reflections capture Derrick's perspective on minimization and isolation fears, but the counselor sometimes pivots quickly to options rather than lingering with emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on DUI prevention and managing social drinking, with every exchange (rulers, plans, role-plays) advancing the target and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative: role-play scenarios chosen by Derrick, refusal scripts co-developed, and his contributions directly shape the intervention with expertise treated as shared.

### Autonomy Support

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic cultivation of change talk via repeated importance/confidence rulers with tracked upward movement (4 to 7-8), reinforcement of Derrick's own plan language, and role-play producing high-quality client change talk.
