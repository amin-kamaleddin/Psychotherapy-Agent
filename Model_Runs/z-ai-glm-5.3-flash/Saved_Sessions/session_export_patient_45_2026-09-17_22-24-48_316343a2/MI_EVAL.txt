# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Disarmed defensiveness immediately ('I'm not here to give you a row'), offered frequent genuine affirmations, and remained non-judgmental even during Erik's minimizing and defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and transformative; the 'hope more than certainty' reframe prompted Erik to say 'that's it exactly,' and emotional undercurrents were consistently captured.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol, diet, and diabetes management with purposeful progression toward a concrete, client-owned goal and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative interaction with inclusive language ('checking in together works for me'), with the plan co-constructed from Erik's own proposals and credited to him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently returned responsibility to Erik, affirmed his choices as his own ('cutting down rather than stopping is your idea'), normalized partial success, and appropriately deferred medical efficacy questions to the doctor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation was drawn from within the client—the pivotal open question unlocked Erik's internal reasons, and every reason, plan, and commitment came from Erik himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Erik as the expert on his own life, and his contributions (cutting down, Gary's water trick, Friday strategy, check-in) substantially shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled open questions ('what do YOU make of it all?') elicited strong client-generated change talk, which was amplified and highlighted throughout Erik's progression from 'why rock the boat' to a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally acknowledged briefly and redirected via effective double-sided reflections, though it was occasionally given more airtime than necessary.
