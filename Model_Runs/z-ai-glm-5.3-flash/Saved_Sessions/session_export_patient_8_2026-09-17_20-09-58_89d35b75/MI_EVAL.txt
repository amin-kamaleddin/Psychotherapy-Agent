#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ 5 ☐ 4 ☐ 3 ☐ 2 ☐ 1
**Comments:** Reinforced client ownership and choice throughout; honored refusal to quit entirely; framed the week as a no-failure experiment. No persuasive or controlling language; all decisions were explicitly the client's.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ 5 ☐ 4 ☐ 3 ☐ 2 ☐ 1
**Comments:** Motivation elicited from within—open questions, confidence ruler, and planning prompts. All key insights originated from the client (scrolling worsens anxiety; roommate accountability; one bad night ≠ failure). Counselor role was reflecting, affirming, and amplifying.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ 5 ☐ 4 ☐ 3 ☐ 2 ☐ 1
**Comments:** Expertise clearly located within the client ("You know your routine best"; "a whole week designed by you"). Every plan element (charger, book, roommate witness, sticky note) came from the client, shaping session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ 5 ☐ 4 ☐ 3 ☐ 2 ☐ 1
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation: "what do you think might make that time feel easier to get through?", "which one feels most doable for a first try this week?", confidence ruler. Resulted in strong commitment language: "Tonight. The phone's going across the room"; "Six, maybe"; "I'm actually going to do it."

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐ 5 ☑ 4 ☐ 3 ☐ 2 ☐ 1
**Comments:** Mostly avoided amplifying sustain talk; responded neutrally to "I'm not saying I'm quitting anything" and affirmed her boundary ("you're not becoming a hermit"). However, double-sided reflections were not always used to resolve ambivalence explicitly—initial sustain talk (boredom, fear of missing the group chat) was acknowledged but not strategically reframed toward discrepancy.

---

**Overall Global Ratings Score:** **4.8/5**

**Summary:** An exemplary MI session—warm, collaborative, and autonomy-supportive, with unwavering direction and nearly all motivation and plan content evoked from the client. Particular strengths included the confidence ruler, the experiment framing, and affirming self-generated insights, yielding strong client commitment language. Area for growth: deepen emotional exploration of underlying anxiety and fear of failure using more complex and double-sided reflections.
