# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with frequent genuine affirmations and respect for the patient's peer concerns. Affirmations occasionally feel formulaic and repetitive, and ambivalence is not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections capture both sides of ambivalence and elicit deeper material. Reflections stay largely at the content level, with underlying emotions like fear of exclusion and FOMO not deeply validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on alcohol use tied organically to the ankle injury, with every exchange building toward a concrete plan. No drift into unrelated territory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The final plan is built entirely from the patient's own ideas (daytime football, roommate accountability, tracking), merely organized by the clinician's questions. A genuine partnership of equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No pressure or prescribing; outcomes framed as the patient's own experiment with failure accepted as informative. The 'proof for yourself' framing powerfully reinforces client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question draws out the patient's own motivations rather than providing education, with explicit distinction of client-sourced from counselor-sourced reasons. Payoff visible in the patient's strongest commitment language about figuring it out on his own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within the client ('that's you finding your own reason, not mine') with decision-making authority explicitly handed over. The session arc from 'Okay' to a self-designed plan reflects exemplary power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: change talk is skillfully evoked and strategically reinforced, progressing from 'maybe once wouldn't be terrible' to concrete commitments like texting Marcus tonight. Desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language all elicited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged and paired with change talk in double-sided reflections without arguing against it. Some sustain talk, notably the 'sacred' Wednesday routine, was left unaddressed.
