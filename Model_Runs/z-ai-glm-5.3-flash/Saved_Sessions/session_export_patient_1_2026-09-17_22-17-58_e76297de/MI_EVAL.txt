# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Affirmations were specific and genuine (e.g., acknowledging his health choices and self-awareness), with no judgment or labeling of the smoking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections captured ambivalence well, but emotional reflection was occasionally lighter when Ahmed made vulnerable admissions ('That's the hard one').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on the cigar behavior throughout, with each exchange advancing toward resolution and even dust discussions tied back to isolating the cigar variable.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was fully co-constructed ('a plan you can own'), with mutual terms negotiated for the next visit and the client's 'show me' standard adopted as the working framework.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice or pressure was imposed; the counselor used permission-style questions and a hypothetical, letting Ahmed arrive at his own graduated plan (stop inhaling first, cut back).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every turn was an evocative question drawing out client-generated motivation, with no lecturing about tobacco risks — perfectly fitting the client's stated preferences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions consistently positioned Ahmed as the expert, and his contributions substantially shaped the session's direction — the hallmark of full partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic open questions elicited change talk ('a mask... I could try,' 'that I might try'), moving Ahmed from 5% minimization to commitment-adjacent language, though key change statements were not deepened before closing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never amplified, instead met with neutral reflection and skillful pivots, though some reflections restated sustain talk without pairing it with change-side material.
