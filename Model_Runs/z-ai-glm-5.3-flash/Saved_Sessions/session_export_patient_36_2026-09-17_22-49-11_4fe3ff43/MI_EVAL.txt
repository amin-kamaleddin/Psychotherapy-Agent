# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates warmth and unconditional positive regard, affirming Elena's self-generated insights and explicitly framing the next session as judgment-free. No labeling or conditional regard appears anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, with double-sided reflections capturing ambivalence accurately. However, reflections often restate content rather than deepen emotional nuance—for example, the self-doubt beneath 'or am I kidding myself?' went unaddressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus moving logically from momentum to a specific skill, practice plan, concrete first step, and reflection plan. Each turn advances toward the goal and culminates in a confirmed action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is genuinely between equal partners—the counselor invites Elena to set the agenda, and the plan was co-constructed from her ideas. Elena explicitly notes that the collaborative approach 'takes the pressure off.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision is framed as Elena's, from choosing the starting point to shaping the check-in format, with no prescriptive advice. The counselor repeatedly reinforces her capacity for self-direction and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is elicited almost entirely from Elena through open questions that draw out her own reasons, strategies, and values. Insights are consistently attributed back to her ('That's wisdom you generated yourself, not something I gave you').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor excels at positioning Elena as the expert, and her contributions substantially shaped the session—the entire plan originated from her. Ownership is reinforced throughout, including in the closing summary.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor actively evokes and reinforces Elena's strong change talk, which she produces and the counselor reflects back. More explicit evocation of confidence/ability language and deeper exploration of commitment strength would be needed for a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The minimal sustain talk was handled effectively by reframing doubt as skill-building and affirming careful thinking. The approach was slightly conservative, at times restating harder aspects rather than using more strategic double-sided reflections.
