# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout with affirmations of Elena's strengths. Falls short of 5 because affirmations are somewhat generic rather than individualized to her specific insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, accurate reflective listening and careful summaries track Elena's content well. Reflections remain largely content-level rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning, especially around her self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on the target behavior with each turn systematically building toward a concrete, doable action plan (cue, start date, rehearsal, reward, follow-up). No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan is genuinely co-constructed with Elena selecting and refining every element. Repeated option-menu formats and counselor-supplied tips create moments of uneven power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are consistently offered and Elena owns every decision, with no pressure or coercion. Occasional steering (pre-packaged boosters, the final 'small tip') edges toward prescribing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Confidence rulers and barrier questions elicit some motivation, but most questions are logistical and multiple-choice. Never explores why the change matters or how it connects to Elena's values and identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena's input visibly shapes the plan, but pre-packaged menus of options position the counselor as the idea source rather than inviting Elena to generate her own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Confidence rulers and planning questions produced moderate change talk, but Elena's strongest change talk was spontaneous. Missed key opportunities to deepen her insight that self-kindness 'matters more than the pause itself.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk was handled skillfully, with kind reframing pre-empting self-criticism and setback talk incorporated constructively. The skill was rarely tested and no double-sided reflections were demonstrated.
