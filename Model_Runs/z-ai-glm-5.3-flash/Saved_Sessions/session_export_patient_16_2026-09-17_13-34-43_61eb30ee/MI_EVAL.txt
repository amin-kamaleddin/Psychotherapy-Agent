# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with validation of Anika's frustration and no labeling despite defensiveness. However, affirmations are sparse and positive regard is somewhat brief and formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and validation appear early, but the session quickly becomes advice-delivery with almost no reflective listening. Emotional cues like fear of being benched and 'That part freaked me out' go unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, coherent focus on vaping and protecting Anika's team status, progressing logically through script, urge plan, and Sarah conversation. At times direction follows the counselor's agenda and spreads across too many behavior domains.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is largely counselor-led, with scripts, plans, and step lists handed down rather than co-developed. Anika's best contributions arose from her own initiative, and her push-back ('Whoa, that's a lot at once') signaled a counselor-driven pace.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine choice-offering and acceptance of Anika's season-only commitment, but much of the session used 'should'-style directives, including re-proposing distancing from Sarah after Anika rejected it. Her stated ambivalence about post-season use went unaffirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor's core mode is advice-giving rather than evoking the client's own motivations. Anika's insights ('I play better when I'm not high') surfaced spontaneously but were met with more information instead of deepening questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed an expert role, delivering prescriptive plans wholesale rather than inviting Anika to shape them. Anika explicitly pushed back that the plan was too much at once, indicating plans outpaced her readiness.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged mostly from Anika herself rather than deliberate counselor evocation. The counselor missed chances to deepen commitment language like 'seven out of ten, maybe even eight' by responding with another list.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not reinforce or amplify sustain talk and occasionally pivoted toward change, but also made no strategic effort to soften it. Statements about relaxation and fun with Sarah were simply skipped rather than addressed with double-sided reflections.
