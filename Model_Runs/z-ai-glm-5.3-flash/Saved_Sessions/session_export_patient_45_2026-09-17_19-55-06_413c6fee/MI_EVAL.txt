### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and layered, capturing both surface content and underlying meaning (e.g., "I'm the same person I've always been; I feel fine"). Erik's responses confirmed he felt deeply understood, with reflections consistently moving the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on diet and alcohol reduction with a logical progression from ambivalence exploration to concrete commitments. Pacing was deliberately unhurried, though goals could have been consolidated into a summary earlier.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work was explicitly framed as joint ("Let's figure it out together"), with the food plan co-constructed from Erik's lived experience. Erik proposed the follow-up himself, demonstrating a fully equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively fostered by consistently locating expertise and choice with Erik, even redirecting his requests for directives back to his own judgment. Erik's closing self-driven commitment demonstrates internalized ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key turn drew motivation out of Erik rather than supplying it, producing all categories of change talk (desire, ability, reasons, commitment). Erik shifted from external compliance ("what should I do?") to self-generated motivation ("I've talked myself into it").

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positioned Erik as the expert on his own life and resisted the expert trap when asked for direct advice. Erik's own contributions substantially shaped the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions (e.g., "if you were the one in charge here..." ) elicited sustained change talk across desire, ability, reasons, and commitment. The session culminated in a concrete, self-driven commitment with a follow-up plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently met with reflection and skillful double-sided reflections pairing it with change talk rather than argument. Minor deduction for occasionally re-echoing sustain content (the after-work pints) without pivoting.
