# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout ('Mad? Not even a little... That counts', 'this was always your step, not mine'). No judgmental or disapproving language anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including subtle language shifts ('yeah' vs 'I guess'). Reflections captured both surface content and underlying feelings like anxiety and the safety of drawing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on community engagement across every exchange with no topic drift. The session progressed logically to a concrete, time-bound step (one online search before next session).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned between equal partners: the plan was co-constructed from Isabella's own words and the counselor submitted ideas tentatively for approval. Client's 'Thanks for not pushing' confirms the non-hierarchical feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit choice support throughout: 'Too much, too little, or about right?', 'no promises past that', and 'whatever you found or didn't'. The counselor actively reduced external pressure while affirming ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn almost entirely from the client—art, the small class, online-first, and the 'one search' framing all originated with Isabella. No advice-giving or persuasion appeared.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor positioned Isabella as the expert ('You know yourself best') and credited her insights as her own wisdom. The counselor followed her pace rather than directing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk via open questions, amplified commitment language ('yeah' vs 'I guess'), and consolidated a client-generated plan. Isabella's trajectory to 'I'll try. One search' shows effective cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk about self-distrust and past failures was acknowledged via double-sided reflections without amplification, then pivoted toward change. Her honesty was affirmed rather than dwelling on failures.
