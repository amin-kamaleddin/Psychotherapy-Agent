# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout with affirmations of Linda's insights and legitimate steroid fears. Affirmations were somewhat generic and less individualized to her deeper emotional experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections, including a double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence between dependence fears and dog walking. Missed opportunities to deepen reflections on her shame about technique and midnight-googling anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on inhaler technique and adherence from opening to closing plan, with every turn tied to the target behavior. Logical progression through barriers → education → practice → concrete, achievable commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently framed work jointly, checked preferences, and co-constructed the plan, including collaborative role-play. Information-heavy turns without first eliciting Linda's existing knowledge reflected an uneven power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered menu-based choices and avoided coercion, with the counselor accepting Linda's stated boundaries without pushing. Repeatedly re-offering the same options sometimes felt slightly pressuring rather than fully open.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew motivation largely from Linda herself, with the 'walk the dog' goal as a recurring anchor and techniques like confidence scaling. Evocation was periodically displaced by information-giving, and deeper motivations weren't fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Incorporated Linda's contributions but repeatedly slipped into an expert/educator role with extended information delivery. Stacked multi-question turns pressured rather than fostered power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation via importance/confidence scaling drove Linda's confidence from 4 to 9 across the session. She generated her own change talk and concrete commitments ('the first time it feels like the difficulty has an actual plan attached to it').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without amplifying it and used education (preventable side effects, lowest effective dose) to reduce fears. Residual doubt ('those haven't gone away') was not explicitly reframed toward change talk.
