# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validating fear of self-disappointment and normalizing the patient's honest hedging. No judgment or disapproval appears anywhere, even when the patient expresses shame or worry.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, capturing both content and emotional undercurrent (e.g., 'doable until I think about the day of'). Double-sided reflections skillfully hold ambivalence without judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains unwavering focus on the target behavior, with each exchange building logically toward a concrete, fully specified behavioral plan (text today, one hour, exit option, accountability). Guides without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates fully between equal partners—all intervention content originates from the patient, with the counselor's role exclusively reflective and evocative. Frames accountability as a mutual promise between them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces client ownership ('readiness came from you, not from waiting') and offers choices rather than directives. No persuasive or controlling language appears.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every pivotal insight and commitment is the patient's own, drawn out through strategic open questions targeting desire, ability, reasons, and commitment. Skillfully develops her self-corrected confidence ('I've got this. Probably.').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise within the client ('That's your expertise talking, not mine') and adds no clinical content of her own. The patient's ideas substantially shaped the entire intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through selective reflection and strategic questions, culminating in spontaneous commitment language and a self-generated plan. The readiness question deepened commitment effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely avoids amplifying sustain talk, acknowledging doubts briefly with empathy before redirecting to solution-focused questions. Minor point withheld as some reflections briefly restate doubt language ('chickening out') before pivoting.
