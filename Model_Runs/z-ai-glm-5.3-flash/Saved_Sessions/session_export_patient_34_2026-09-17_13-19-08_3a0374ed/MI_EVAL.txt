# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a nonjudgmental, neutral stance toward Dylan, though warmth and affirmations of him as a person are largely absent, making the tone feel transactional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; Dylan's expressions of frustration and feeling unfairly singled out are repeatedly bypassed in favor of logistics and script drafting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** While structured, the session focuses on hearing preparation rather than the referral concern of underage drinking, which is never explored as a clinical issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Collaboration is limited to Dylan choosing between counselor-generated options; all ideas and plans are supplied by the counselor, resembling a consultant-client relationship.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some menu-based choices are offered, but the counselor strongly steers content with control-oriented advice and pre-drafted scripts, revising only when Dylan pushes back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There are virtually no open questions eliciting Dylan's own values, motivations, or concerns about drinking; the session is didactic throughout.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes an expert/advisor role from the outset, unilaterally defining the problem, goal, and plan, with only superficial choice points for Dylan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No effort is made to evoke change talk about the drinking behavior; Dylan's admission of 'a drink or two' is used only for statement crafting, not exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The session actively amplifies sustain talk by coaching minimization and 'do not recall' strategies, reinforcing avoidance of accountability rather than developing discrepancy.
