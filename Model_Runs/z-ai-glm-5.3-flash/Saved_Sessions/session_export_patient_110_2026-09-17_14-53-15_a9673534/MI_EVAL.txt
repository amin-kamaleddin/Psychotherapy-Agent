# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental tone throughout with affirmations like 'solid, doable first step.' However, affirmations were brief and formulaic, and past failed attempts were never explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, often complex reflections such as 'You're torn—want to get healthy...' and the statin-fear reflection. Lacked deeper emotional exploration of Richard's fear-of-failure pattern that he himself surfaced.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on medication adherence and activity/diet change with no drift; every turn advanced a concrete element of the plan, ending in a fully specified behavioral plan with check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: ideas flowed bidirectionally (Richard's walking timing and wife's role, counselor's rain backup and alarms), and the final plan was genuinely co-constructed with concrete help like written pharmacist questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Richard's ownership over the behavior, nights, reward, and check-in format, with options offered without pressure. Richard's closing comments confirm he experienced genuine choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Rulers and confidence-escalation questions elicited genuinely client-generated motivation, including Richard's insight that 'one miss doesn't count against me' and his values-based retirement reason.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed work as shared ('let's figure something out together') and Richard's ideas shaped the plan. Some turns were densely packed with counselor-generated options, slightly tilting idea generation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk throughout—rulers, 'what would raise confidence from 7 to 8,' missed-night handling, and closure questions drawing commitment ('I'll start Monday').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (minimization, statin fears) was acknowledged then promptly redirected to problem-solving without reinforcement. Only briefly restated reasons against change in early reflections before pivoting.
