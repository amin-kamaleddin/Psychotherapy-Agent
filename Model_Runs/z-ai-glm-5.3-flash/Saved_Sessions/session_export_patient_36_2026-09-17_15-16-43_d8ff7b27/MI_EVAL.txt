# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard with specific, genuine affirmations and no judgment of Elena's guilt or people-pleasing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, concise reflections that named guilt as the core barrier, though reflections tended to summarize content rather than deepen the emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on saying no/boundaries, guiding from exploration to rehearsal and a concrete commitment with follow-up planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with permission-seeking, co-constructed role-play, joint plan tweaking, and Elena's own success shaping the intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices consistently reinforced: Elena selected strategies, defaults, and plan details, with options offered rather than imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out Elena's motivations, successes, and self-generated rule, though some strategies and the low-cost rule were supplied rather than evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena's expertise was central to the session, but presenting the two strategies and rule leaned somewhat on the counselor's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of open questions, confidence rulers, and reinforcement of desire statements grew Elena's confidence from 8 to 9; change talk could have been reflected and amplified more explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk, none amplified; when the guilt voice emerged, the counselor responded supportively and pivoted toward Elena's success.
