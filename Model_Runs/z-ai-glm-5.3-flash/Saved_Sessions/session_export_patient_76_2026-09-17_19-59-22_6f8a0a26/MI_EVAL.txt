# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard is communicated explicitly and consistently from start to finish. Even Liora's sustain talk and apologies are met with warmth and nonjudgment, with no labeling, pathologizing, or alarm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and deep, capturing both somatic and emotional content (e.g., 'your body holds what your words can't'). The reframe of cutting as seeking quiet was confirmed by the patient with 'Yeah. Exactly that.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on self-harm as coping and moves purposefully from understanding to a concrete alternative strategy. However, the intermediate stretch (showers-insufficient debate and 'what got you through a 9') circled briefly, and a closing summary was missing to consolidate the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Collaboration is explicit and repeated ('as partners here,' 'if we put our heads together'). The final plan is fully co-constructed, with the note's content, placement, and support person all coming from Liora while the psychiatrist shaped the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Liora's ownership of choices is consistently reinforced ('What feels doable? Or is there something you'd adjust first?'), and her conditional commitment was accepted without pressure. There is no persuasive or coercive framing — she selected the strategy, location, and contact person herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited Liora's own reasons, exceptions, and plan, evoking notable hope from a hopeless client. However, confidence language and belief in her ability to follow through were not directly explored despite the conditional commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Liora as the expert on her own experience ('You know your 2am self better than anyone'), which directly shaped the session. Client contributions were incorporated throughout, with insight explicitly framed as belonging to Liora.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled open questions and future-oriented prompts produced genuine commitments ('I could write it this week') and concrete planning. Falls short of a 5 because the hedged language ('can't promise I'll use it') wasn't further explored, e.g., via confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with and was consistently reframed (skepticism as self-protection, movie night as evidence quiet is possible), with effective double-sided reflections. Some sustain talk cycles (showers insufficient at a nine) were acknowledged and redirected rather than strategically resolved.
