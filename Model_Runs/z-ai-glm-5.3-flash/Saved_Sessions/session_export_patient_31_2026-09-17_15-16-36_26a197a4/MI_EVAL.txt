# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout; reassured 'I won't judge' and reframed slips as data. Affirmations of David's strengths were somewhat sparse and pragmatic rather than personally warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured David's ambivalence and practical framing. However, emotional content like 'That stung a bit' about the money was not reflected before moving to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on a concrete target: daytime NRT trial with tracking and a scheduled Thursday follow-up. All topics were tied back to the smoking plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was co-constructed and David's preferences were adopted (gum first, one daily entry). Several turns were dense one-way information delivery that shifted into expert-educator mode.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced David's ownership: menus of options, 'It's completely up to you,' and honoring his evening-smoking boundary. Slips framed as data directly countered his all-or-nothing fear.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David's motivations (BP, work mood, partner, money) emerged spontaneously and were reflected but not actively probed or deepened. Evocation was passive rather than skillfully elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** David was repeatedly positioned as decision-maker and the final plan came directly from his preferences. Dense information blocks limited his influence over content at times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David generated change talk largely on his own and it was occasionally reflected back. Little deliberate evocation of desire, ability, or confidence language; the counselor rode momentum rather than amplifying it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument and his evening boundary was honored. Reflections were sometimes simple acceptances rather than strategic double-sided reflections linking sustain talk to his change reasons.
