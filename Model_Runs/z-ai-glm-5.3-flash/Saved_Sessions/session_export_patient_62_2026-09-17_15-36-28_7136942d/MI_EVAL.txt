# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, opening without judgment, normalizing Jordan's coping, and handling deflection and refusals gracefully. Honors requests to keep things low-stakes with no drama or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captures Jordan's exhaustion and self-protective avoidance of hope. However, the counselor sometimes pivots to problem-solving too quickly, leaving shame and grief insufficiently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus, gently steering from global hopelessness to one concrete target: a single creditor call with alarms, script, and set time. Every exchange moved the process forward without derailing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint effort — Jordan's pushback materially shaped the plan around his constraints and preferences. Loses a point because scripts and alarm texts were largely counselor-drafted with limited invitation for Jordan to edit them in his own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms autonomy ('your call, your pace,' 'I won't check up') and accepts refusals of accountability and rehearsal without pressure. Jordan's closing reflects internalized ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from Jordan rather than lecturing, eliciting his own hopes and reasons through open questions. His dramatic shift from hopelessness to self-generated commitment ('maybe I won't') demonstrates strong evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treats Jordan as the expert, offering choices at every step and accepting his decisions and pushback. Not a 5 because the early four-option menu and pre-packaged script were counselor-generated rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and progressively builds change talk, from 'paying a bill on time' to 'maybe I won't chicken out.' Each piece of change talk was reflected and developed without pushing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying sustain talk, reframing hopelessness as self-protection and meeting doubts with reflection before pivoting to possibilities. Falls slightly short because early reflections restated helpless framing without double-sided reflections linking to Jordan's own stated wishes.
