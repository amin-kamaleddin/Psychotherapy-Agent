# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental despite Rowan's dismissiveness, with affirmations of his honesty. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and did not deeply validate the emotion behind his irritation or self-distrust.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured Rowan's ambivalence well ('skeptical but willing to try'). Missed opportunities to deepen reflections on his self-doubt and minimization before moving to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior throughout, redirecting digressions without losing focus. Session closed with a clear, specific, mutually understood behavioral plan including timing, placement, backup, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-constructed, integrating Rowan's constraints and input at every step. Structured question sequences felt somewhat protocol-like, and 'Can I go now?' hinted pacing occasionally felt counselor-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent reinforcement of Rowan's ownership ('it's your call'), with a menu of options he selected and modified. Non-adherence was framed as information, and boundaries (no texts) were immediately accommodated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open and scaling questions effectively elicited Rowan's own change statements and solutions, like labeling the alarm. Rarely explored deeper reasons, values, or what 'feeling fine' means to him beyond logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rowan's expertise genuinely shaped the plan (mornings, no texts, alarm labeling). Rapid-fire structured questioning occasionally felt expert-scripted, triggering his irritation about 'making a big production.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and confidence scaling skillfully drew out change talk ('I'll dig it out tonight'), with the ladder technique getting Rowan to generate his own tweak. Some tentative change talk ('maybe') could have been amplified more, and his own 'why' was never explicitly invited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification, using double-sided reflections and pivoting toward change without arguing. Occasionally repeated sustain talk in reflections without an immediate pivot.
