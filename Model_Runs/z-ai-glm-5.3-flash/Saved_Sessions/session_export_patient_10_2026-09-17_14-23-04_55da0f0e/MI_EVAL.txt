# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing the setback and affirming the plan. Falls short of 5 due to generic reassurance ('You've got this') rather than deeper individualized affirmation of Amina's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; advice lists follow brief empathy statements and cut off exploration. Emotional disclosures — fear of no callbacks, morning hopelessness — go unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tight, efficient focus on employment barriers with concrete steps each turn. Falls short because the path is counselor-driven and doesn't deepen commitment through the client's own change talk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A fix-it stance dominates: option lists, drafted messages, and scripts delivered without co-creation. Amina did most of the collaborative work herself despite the counselor's approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Choices offered are menu selections within counselor-defined frameworks; the mode is predominantly prescriptive. Her direct bid for autonomy ('That part I can control, right?') went unacknowledged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** After one initial open question, the counselor supplies all content rather than drawing out Amina's abundant self-generated motivation. Missed opportunities to explore her '10' confidence and her own coping wisdom.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumes the expert role with pre-packaged solutions, answering Amina's own question for her. Power balance is clearly counselor-as-expert, client-as-recipient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is almost entirely client-generated and never explored or deepened. Her question about conveying reliability without defensiveness was a rich evocative opening that the counselor answered instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about never getting callbacks is left unaddressed rather than strategically softened. No double-sided reflections pairing her doubt with her confidence; ambivalence remains unresolved.
