# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens warmly and validates Tyler's resistance appropriately, but acceptance is inconsistent — the punitive mother role-play isn't adequately processed afterward, and Tyler's strengths (honesty, negotiation skills, his B in chemistry) go unaffirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections like 'feeling embarrassed and treated like a kid would piss anyone off' show decent perspective-taking, but they're shallow and formulaic, serving mainly as transitions to advice or scripts rather than deepening emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session's focus was entirely defined by Tyler's 'she's the problem' framing; substance use — the referral concern — was never explored despite disclosure, and no collaborative agenda was set.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Checks in frequently and avoids authoritarianism, but collaboration is shallow: the psychiatrist supplies scripts and strategies rather than co-creating them, and Tyler's contributions are only minimally incorporated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers genuine choices and explicitly permits refusal, but ready-made solutions subtly undermine Tyler's ownership — his best self-directed problem-solving moment goes under-acknowledged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No open questions draw out Tyler's values, hopes, or concerns about use; the answers implicitly come from the clinician. Motivation-talk that emerged ('let me prove it') was spontaneous, never evoked or reinforced — a critical gap for a precontemplative adolescent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The stance is that of a communication coach supplying answers rather than a partner drawing out the client's solutions; Tyler's insightful 'why would I be honest when this is what happens?' is never explored.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is largely absent and never actively cultivated. Tyler's spontaneous commitment language during the script practice ('give me a chance to prove I'm not some screw-up') goes unreflected and unreinforced; no evocative questions are asked.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk ('it's not a thing,' 'I did nothing wrong') is repeatedly validated and amplified, reinforcing the 'not a problem' frame without double-sided reflections or any pivot toward discrepancy.
