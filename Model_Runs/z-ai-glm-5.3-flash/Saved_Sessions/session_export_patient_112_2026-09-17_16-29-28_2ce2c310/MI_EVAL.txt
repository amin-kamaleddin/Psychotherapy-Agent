# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently respectful and supportive tone, validating Rosa's fears and normalizing setbacks. Limited explicit affirmation of her personal strengths, such as her insight about the reminder card.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures her ambivalence and repeated acknowledgment of her fears. Reflections occasionally lean toward planning rather than deeper emotional exploration of her mother's illness or smoking identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation, progressing logically from ambivalence to a concrete experiment and prep steps. Every exchange ties back to the target behavior without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is fully co-constructed, with Rosa choosing the start day, delay, strategies, and accountability; the psychiatrist supplied structure only after being invited. The back-and-forth shaping of the if-then safety net exemplifies equal-partner problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is reinforced throughout, from the opening format menu to explicit permission to protect the coffee cigarette. Rosa retains full ownership of her plan, and 'no promises beyond that' is accepted without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is effectively drawn from Rosa herself, including her mother's heart disease and her self-generated commitment card idea. Could deepen desire/ability talk further, such as exploring why her confidence is a five or six, before moving to advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rosa is repeatedly positioned as the expert, and her own ideas substantially shaped the plan. Somewhat limited by moments where the psychiatrist offers structured menus and tips rather than drawing ideas from Rosa first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation of change language through the confidence scaling question and doability checks, drawing out commitments like 'Monday it is' and 'doesn't feel impossible.' Could have explored her reasons for change and confidence rating more deeply before moving to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about weight gain, failure, and crankiness is acknowledged without reinforcement and consistently reframed, such as treating the safety-net cigarette as progress. The weight-gain discussion leans on information-giving rather than pivoting to Rosa's own coping ideas.
