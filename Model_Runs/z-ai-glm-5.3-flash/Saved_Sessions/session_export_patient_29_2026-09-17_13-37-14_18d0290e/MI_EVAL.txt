# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with strong normalization of slips, but affirmations are somewhat generic and lack individualized recognition of David's specific strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening at key moments, including a strong closing summary, but emotional moments (fear of wife's judgment, work dread, history of failure) receive logistical solutions rather than explicit emotional reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, unwavering focus on smoking cessation from start to finish, with every exchange tied back to quitting and a logical progression toward concrete commitments and a follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration—David's edits shape the scripts and plan—but the counselor issues dense directive plans before exploring David's own ideas, functioning more as expert consultation with client feedback than a meeting of equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicit choice points are offered and honored at key decisions, though the surrounding prescriptive communication style makes the session feel counselor-directed despite the verbal affirmation of choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session relies almost entirely on information, tactics, and scripts rather than open evocative questions; David's motivations (money, trust, work) surface on their own but are never systematically elicited or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David's contributions are incorporated into scripts and follow-up, but the counselor repeatedly assumes the expert role with dense advice blocks, making the structure advice-giving with client tweaks rather than genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor builds on David's existing motivation, eliciting commitment language and self-generated plans, but never uses classic MI evocative strategies to deepen change talk—much momentum comes from David's preparation-stage readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk with coping strategies and a powerful lapse-vs-failure reframe, though double-sided reflections acknowledging stress concerns while linking to motivation are absent.
