# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with affirmations of Daniela's storytelling strength. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic, and the counselor moved quickly to options rather than fully accepting her expressed ambivalence about readiness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate but often superficial reflections (e.g., acknowledging that saying it out loud 'can feel permanent'). Missed opportunities to reflect deeper emotional themes like her fear of being seen differently, with reflections frequently followed by advice lists.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on diabetes disclosure from the opening statement through a concrete plan (Friday lunch with Marisol, one-liner, text fallback). Every turn tied back to disclosure skills, rehearsal, or planning with steady momentum toward an achievable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was largely co-created, with the role-play negotiated and the plan built from Daniela's own Friday lunch idea. Early turns tilted toward counselor-supplied content (scripts, reply lines, strategies) with limited inquiry into her own ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered menus and respected Daniela's pacing, including her 'just talking, not for real yet' boundary. Would be a 5 with more explicit reinforcement that the decision and success are entirely hers, especially around her fear of chickening out.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation drawn from the client, who articulated her own reasons and fears. However, the heavy provision of ready-made scripts and strategies positioned the counselor as the source of solutions rather than eliciting Daniela's own language and ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniela's contributions consistently shaped the session — she chose the story-style script, selected Marisol, drove the role-play, and picked the texting fallback. Early content-heavy turns positioned the counselor as the source of solutions rather than fully drawing out her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively structured opportunities for change talk, and Daniela produced meaningful commitment statements ('Friday lunch, just Marisol,' 'this is kind of a big deal for me') that were reflected and built upon. Evocation was often menu-driven rather than open-ended, rarely asking her to elaborate her own reasons or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged neutrally without reinforcement, which is appropriate. But there was little strategic softening — no double-sided reflections pairing her doubts with her own change talk, and the counselor pivoted to strategy lists instead of helping resolve ambivalence.
