# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental tone with consistent framing of tracking as information-gathering rather than judgment. Affirmations were repetitive and formulaic, missing chances to affirm Kenji's vulnerable disclosures (loneliness, self-insight).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening capturing ambivalence, sleep concerns, and the significance of living alone. Some poignant disclosures ('me and my thoughts', 'hopefully it's not as bad... but probably is') went unreflected emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on alcohol reduction throughout, progressing logically from awareness to a concrete plan (tracking, voice memos, Wednesday check-in). No topic drift; even the closing invitation served the tracking goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative tone with joint planning and Kenji co-authoring the check-in structure. The tracking script and memory strategies were largely clinician-generated with Kenji choosing among them rather than co-creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision point offered genuine options (tracking vs. pros/cons, template vs. check-in, reminder vs. alarm), and Kenji's selections shaped the plan. No pressure toward any outcome; advice framed as optional.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Kenji's motivations (sleep, Mondays at work) and produced genuine insight about habit vs. craving. Later session drifted toward logistics of tracking rather than deepening Kenji's reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Kenji as the expert with menu options and agenda-shaping invitations. Some guidance remained clinician-prescribed, and Kenji's self-knowledge about drinking to avoid loneliness wasn't fully leveraged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled questions evoked Kenji's own motivations and self-efficacy ('I can do a week', 'maybe it's not just the job'). Key change talk moments ('maybe seeing it will... do something') were acknowledged but not amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without endorsement and sometimes reframed toward change, but no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots back to Kenji's change statements. 'Counting it doesn't stop it' received only brief reframing.
