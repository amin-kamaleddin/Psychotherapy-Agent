# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains basic respect and a nonjudgmental tone, but warmth is minimal and generic; no affirmations of Ryan's honesty about drinking 8-9 drinks or his self-doubt about 'talking the talk.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; the counselor jumps to option lists instead of reflecting feelings. Key disclosures about ambivalence and fear of the hearing went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifted from the drinking behavior to hearing preparation, which became the dominant focus. No exploration of drinking patterns, consequences, or goals occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Largely expert-driven: the counselor proposes, drafts, and instructs while Ryan reacts and edits. Agenda and solutions originated almost entirely from the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and avoids coercion, but also heavily prescribes specific behaviors (drink caps, exit times) and steers Ryan toward preferred framings; ownership of change is rarely reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is assumed to reside in the counselor's advice rather than elicited from Ryan. Spontaneous change talk ('how much I actually wanna change') was not explored or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumes the expert role with bullet-point lists and drafted statements, never asking what Ryan thinks would work despite his noting he'd already tried control strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk came spontaneously from Ryan; the counselor did little to evoke or deepen it. Effort went into rehearsing the hearing statement rather than exploring motivation about drinking itself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I'm not some alcoholic,' 'that's not changing') was ignored or accommodated. The counselor co-authored minimization language ('one-time mistake'), reinforcing Ryan's framing of heavy ongoing partying as isolated.
