# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, specific affirmations throughout, never judgmental of guilt or exhaustion. Some affirmations edge toward cheerleading ('That's everything I hoped you'd hear') and the warmth occasionally feels slightly performed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is a consistent strength, accurately tracking ambivalence, subtle emotional shifts, and Nadine's own redefinitions of success. Mirrors the client's exact language throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear, purposeful focus on exhaustion and infant sleep concerns with no drift. Each exchange builds toward a concrete next step and definition of success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way exchange with expert content offered as shared information. The client's contributions visibly steer the session and she confirms ownership of the conclusions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is consistently emphasized and nothing is prescribed, with psychoeducation offered and checked. Closing questions lean slightly toward shaping the agenda, and there is no explicit acknowledgment that Nadine retains full choice over follow-through.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every practical solution originates from Nadine herself, with questions consistently designed to elicit rather than instruct. Motivation is drawn almost entirely from within the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly positions Nadine as the expert, and all plan elements originate with or are shaped by her. Agenda control is explicitly handed to the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evokes and builds Nadine's own change language, from eliciting a concrete plan to drawing strong commitment talk ('Tomorrow morning'). The trajectory from hopelessness to a self-generated, owned plan is textbook.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged but not amplified, with guilt used as a pivot point. Effective redirection throughout, but no explicit normalization of ambivalence or double-sided reflections to actively resolve it.
