# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout—every disclosure met with warmth and normalization, never judgment. Lukas's identity was affirmed without lecturing, and the closing summary explicitly protected him from anticipated judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing Lukas's dual anxieties before he fully articulated them. Reflections consistently went beyond content to underlying meaning of belonging, identity, and self-trust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on alcohol use and peer pressure through a logical MI arc: exploring options, building self-efficacy, concrete planning, and commitment. Each reflection and question moved purposefully toward a specific, measurable commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—the entire plan originated from Lukas, with the clinician co-constructing rather than prescribing. The session closing as a joint 'deal' demonstrated true power sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently reinforced that change was Lukas's choice, offering no directives or persuasive pressure. Even the accountability format was negotiated collaboratively on Lukas's terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every intervention was designed to elicit Lukas's own motivations, strategies, and values. His closing realization about starting small demonstrated motivation fully elicited from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located with the client, whose contributions visibly shaped the entire session. The client-generated accountability request was adopted verbatim, defining meaningful power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The standout domain—change talk was skillfully evoked and deepened at every turn, with Lukas ending in strong commitment language ('Deal,' 'I can do that'). Client-generated solutions were repeatedly elicited and amplified with affirmation.
