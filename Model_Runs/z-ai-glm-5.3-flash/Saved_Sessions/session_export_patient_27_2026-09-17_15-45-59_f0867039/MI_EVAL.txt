# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with affirmations like 'Not crazy at all.' Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and few explicitly individualized statements of unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, accurate reflections captured Daniela's ambivalence well (e.g., 'You're juggling hope and fear'). Reflections tended to be brief summaries followed by menu options, sometimes missing deeper emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on disclosing diabetes to peers, progressing logically from ambivalence to a concrete next step (a drafted text). Each turn advanced toward the target behavior without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine partnership in co-drafting texts and incorporating Daniela's edits into the final script. Collaboration often took the form of counselor-generated options for her to select rather than eliciting her own solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support: choice reinforced in nearly every turn, explicit permission for partial disclosure, and affirmation of her timing ('waiting until you feel ready is smart'). No pressure to disclose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open questions drew out Daniela's fears and desires, but the psychiatrist frequently shifted to providing scripts, replies, and advice. Strategic evocation of her own reasons, values, and confidence (e.g., 'What would telling Maya mean for you?') was largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly asked for Daniela's preferences, but heavy reliance on multiple-choice menus kept decision-making power structurally with the counselor. Little explicit acknowledgment that Daniela is the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk emerged (practicing the sentence, planning to text 'maybe this weekend') but mostly via practical menus rather than strategic MI techniques. Little deliberate elaboration of Daniela's own motivations or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk (fear of it becoming 'real') was acknowledged with neutral paraphrases but not actively softened. No double-sided reflections pairing fear of rejection with the exhaustion of hiding.
