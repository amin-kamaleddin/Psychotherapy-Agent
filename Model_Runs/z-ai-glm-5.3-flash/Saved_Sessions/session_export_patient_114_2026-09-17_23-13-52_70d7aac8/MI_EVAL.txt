# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with specific genuine affirmations and an explicit 'not to grade you' framing that reinforces unconditional positive regard despite the conduct-referral context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and layered, capturing the core conflicts (belonging vs. consequences, caring costing fun). Reflections consistently reach the emotional core and set up the next question effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session progresses purposefully from exploring the incident to a concrete action plan and follow-up. Occasionally lets the social-belonging exploration run long without strategic summaries to consolidate momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely collaborative: questions and decisions are co-constructed, the check-in is negotiated, and Ryan ends steering his own change process ('I'll come back with real answers, not plans').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly and repeatedly reinforced — Ryan's plan is entirely his own ('my own rules') — with the check-in framed as an invitation and no directive pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation: open questions elicit Ryan's own values-discrepancy, reasons, and self-defined success markers with zero counselor-supplied arguments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ryan is consistently positioned as the expert, and his contributions (e.g., the Marco example, the drink plan) directly shape the session. Counselor expertise is offered only tentatively as an invitation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and reinforced, drawing out commitment language ('I'll do it,' 'this feels doable') and explicit highlighting of Ryan's shift from hoping to expecting.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated without amplification, then pivoted via double-sided reflections and reframing. The counselor elicits the Marco example, letting Ryan dismantle his own 'boring without being drunk' story.
