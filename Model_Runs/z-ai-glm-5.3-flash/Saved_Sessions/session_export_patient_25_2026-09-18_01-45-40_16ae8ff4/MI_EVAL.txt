# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout, with frequent genuine affirmations. Some praise is tied to outcomes rather than unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing ambivalence and tracing causal connections precisely. The clinician consistently mirrors and deepens Daniel's meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Focus on alcohol is maintained early but drifts in the latter third with the knee/work-stress tangent and a redundant repeated question. The patient, not the clinician, ends up re-anchoring the conversation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions are co-constructed and the patient's ideas drive the plan. The clinician occasionally steers by selecting which patient statements to elevate.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every plan element is chosen by Daniel, with options offered rather than prescribed and no pressure or controlling language. Contingency planning is invited without imposition.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the patient rather than supplied, with no clinician advice offered. Daniel's own reasons for change — pride, fatherhood, identity — are elicited and amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel is positioned as the expert and his contributions visibly shape the session. Some reflections occasionally pre-frame his answers rather than leaving the agenda fully to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked through targeted open questions and then reflected back to consolidate it. Daniel produces rich, specific commitments as a result.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without amplification and gently pivoted toward change. Some doubts are affirmed as self-awareness rather than actively resolved.
