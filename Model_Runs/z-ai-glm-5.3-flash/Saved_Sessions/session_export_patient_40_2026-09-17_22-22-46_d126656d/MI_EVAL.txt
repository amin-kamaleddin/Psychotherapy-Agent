# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with affirmations like 'staying clean through the hard days counts as strength too.' Warmth occasionally feels formulaic, and relapse/shame normalization is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing ambivalence and complex perspective-taking, though one reflection introduced content Elias hadn't mentioned. Emotional nuance around the oldest son's distrust was acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session stayed tethered to recovery but drifted, circling back to time with kids in a way the patient himself noticed. The Thursday commitment was never consolidated into a concrete plan summary, and one reflection redirected off the patient's actual thread.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint exploration with patient contributions visibly shaping each turn and no expert stance. Somewhat limited by repetitive question format and occasional steering via the misaligned reflection.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, high-level autonomy support: no advice or pressure anywhere, and every plan element originated from the patient. Counselor repeatedly deferred to his judgment and validated his own reframing of strength.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Excellent at drawing out patient motivations, including the strength question that elicited novel insight ('Nobody's asked me that before'). Missed opportunities to consolidate elicited motivation into a closing summary.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the patient as the expert, with every question inviting his own answers and the entire plan client-generated. Explicitly credits the patient ('it came from you'), reflecting high-level power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evoked and reinforced change language, leading to strong commitment talk ('Thursday. I'll say it before I talk myself out of it'). Opportunities to deepen commitment (scaling, summarizing collected change talk) were not fully capitalized on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and handled well via double-sided reflections and gentle reframes. Some reflections restated sustain talk without an immediate pivot, and reframes were occasionally premature.
