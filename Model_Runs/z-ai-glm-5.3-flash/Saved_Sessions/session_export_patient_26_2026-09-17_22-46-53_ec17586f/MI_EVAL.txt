# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout; the client explicitly noted she feared judgment but 'you didn't do that.' The counselor validated ambivalence and affirmed her resourcefulness without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, emotionally attuned reflections capturing both content and feeling (e.g., 'unreliable childcare is where things unravel'). Reflections were precise, non-repetitive, and advanced the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on childcare stress and smoking/alcohol with steady momentum toward concrete change, ending with a client-owned next step. Each exchange built strategically on the last.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully co-constructed session; every question invited the client's expertise and information (Head Start) was offered only when requested. The counselor built on her metaphors rather than substituting his own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership ('at your own pace,' 'it's your voice answering your doubt, not mine'). No advice was imposed; all strategies came from Daniela.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within via open questions ('What do you think it would take...?'), eliciting her own reasons and solutions. By session's end she articulated her own hope.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client was explicitly positioned as the expert ('You know your life better than anyone'). Every plan element originated from her, with the counselor reflecting and amplifying.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, repeated evocation of change talk, including the 'one reliable day a week' question that elicited rich self-motivational statements, which were then reinforced by reflecting her hope and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers were acknowledged without dwelling and skillfully reframed toward planning rather than willpower. Minor growth area: moments of doubt were echoed back before being strategically paired with confidence talk.
