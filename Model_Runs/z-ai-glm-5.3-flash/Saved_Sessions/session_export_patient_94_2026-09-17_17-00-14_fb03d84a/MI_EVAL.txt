# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, affirming Maria's practicality and adopting her 'not anti-medication, anti-guessing' framing into the plan. Affirmations stayed somewhat transactional and focused on her plan rather than deeper affirmations of her as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened with an accurate reflection of Maria's rush, skepticism, and self-reliance. However, deeper emotional underpinnings—her distrust of feeling unheard by her PCP and her fear of harm—were never explicitly reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on inhaler adherence, moving efficiently to a concrete, time-limited behavioral trial with tracking and follow-up. Direction relied heavily on rapid information delivery and checklist-style recaps rather than MI-consistent guiding.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Egalitarian tone established early, with the final plan co-constructed from Maria's own solutions (coffee maker cue, phone call follow-up). The plan was sometimes presented as a finished package ('Sound doable?') rather than genuinely negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices for timing, reminders, and follow-up, and honored Maria's limits without pressure. The two-week test was counselor-proposed rather than generated with Maria, and repeated safety instructions carried a slightly directive tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good early evocative questions drew out Maria's concerns and her stair-climbing wheeze. Her motivation was evoked but never deepened—the 'maybe I'll keep going' moment was not explored and her own reasons for change were never voiced or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly handed decisions to Maria and incorporated her contributions into the plan, ending with an open invitation. The counselor still dominated with structured recaps and didactic safety information rather than letting Maria's expertise drive content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The low-barrier two-week test successfully evoked commitment language ('before work, I guess,' 'maybe I'll keep going') and commitment-oriented choices. Missed opportunities to deepen these moments or explicitly explore her confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced, and targeted psychoeducation (spacer, rinse, low-dose risks) addressed her side-effect fears. However, no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots were used—sustain talk was met mostly with information-giving and heavy recaps.
