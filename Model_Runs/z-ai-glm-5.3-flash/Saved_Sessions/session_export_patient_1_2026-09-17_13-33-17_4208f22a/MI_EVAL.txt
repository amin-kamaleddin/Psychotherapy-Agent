# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and avoids criticizing Ahmed's minimization, but warmth is sparse and affirmations feel formulaic rather than conveying deep positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal, relying on token acknowledgments ('I hear you') instead of complex reflections. Missed opportunities to explore the cigar's personal meaning and the wife's family history kept understanding shallow.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays firmly anchored to the target behavior, tying cigar use to dental findings and concrete follow-up plans. It falls short of a 5 because direction trends toward harm-reduction and monitoring rather than change the client owns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative moments exist (joint pros/cons, role-play, Ahmed shaping his own wording), but the counselor frequently defaults to strategy lists and pre-written scripts. Ahmed's 'lot of fuss' comment suggests the plan wasn't fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers real choices and respects Ahmed's stated limits, avoiding demands for cessation. However, heavy information-giving and script provision edge toward steering, with little explicit reinforcement of Ahmed's own expertise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is treated as something to inform rather than elicit, with extensive risk education and supplied scripts. Personal change levers — his kids, pride in checkups, his wife's uncle — went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial power-sharing (offering choices, inviting practice), but the interaction is largely expert-driven with limited curiosity about Ahmed's ideas. Ahmed's contributions are incorporated but not substantially built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is evoked rarely and weakly; budding openings (acting if the dentist finds something, no smoking with drinks) go unfollowed. Ahmed's shifts emerged despite, not because of, skillful evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly mirrors and validates sustain talk without strategic double-sided reflections that pivot toward change. Ahmed's minimization is never reframed to gently develop discrepancy, leaving his anti-change position intact.
