# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard toward a guarded, mandated client — normalizing avoidance and affirming efforts without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflections of ambivalence and self-protection, though reflections occasionally stayed at content level and missed deeper emotional layers like shame and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus on employment as the change target, progressing from vague hopes to a rehearsed pitch, contact, and next-session checkpoint without railroading.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Mateo setting parameters and generating key wording revisions; inclusive language like 'figuring out the words together' pervaded the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly reinforced ('your pace, your call'; 'you're the one steering this'), with no pressure when Mateo withheld a call commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from the client — the pivotal question about the 'real Mateo' elicited his own strengths, pitch, plan, and timeline. The strongest MI skill demonstrated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Mateo was positioned as the expert in charge, with progress credited to him; the client directly confirmed it landed ('Appreciate you not talking down to me').

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent selective reflection, amplification, and discrepancy work moved Mateo from 'why bother' to a commitment to practice and call soon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
