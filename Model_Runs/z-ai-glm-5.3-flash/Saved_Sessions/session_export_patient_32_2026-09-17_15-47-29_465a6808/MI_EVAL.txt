# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, avoiding labels despite Derrick's minimizing language. Warmth was somewhat procedural and checklist-like, lacking deeper individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Derrick's core ambivalence between belonging and independence. Reflections were often brief and pivoted quickly to option lists, missing opportunities to explore the isolation, lost relationship, and shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on DUI prevention and license reinstatement from opening to closing plan. The session progressed logically from motivation to a concrete, scheduled plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with Derrick refining the script, choosing supports, and adjusting the alarm time. Frequent structured option menus tilted ideation toward the counselor, keeping power-sharing strong but not fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed choices as Derrick's, offered advice as optional menus, and accepted his modifications immediately. Relied on counselor-generated option lists rather than reinforcing his full ownership of the decision to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from Derrick via scaling questions, culminating in his unprompted admission of personal responsibility. Sometimes supplied options before exploring his own ideas, and emotional sources of motivation were evoked only lightly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Derrick's own solutions substantially shaped the final plan, demonstrating genuine power-sharing. Pre-packaged option menus slightly constrained his contribution space.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk through the license question and confidence scaling, producing Derrick's self-corrected plan and admission of choice. Could have reflected and deepened the closing self-admission of responsibility more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoided arguing with minimization, instead reflecting underlying values and transforming sustain talk into collaborative plan tweaks. Lacked explicit reframing or double-sided reflections to actively resolve ambivalence around drinking itself.
