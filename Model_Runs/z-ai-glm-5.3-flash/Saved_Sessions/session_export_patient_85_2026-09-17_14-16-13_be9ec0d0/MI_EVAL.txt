# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations like 'data, not failure' and destigmatizing reframes. Warmth was somewhat formulaic and repetitive in later messages, lacking individualized expressions of positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Brief validating reflections were present, but the counselor moved quickly into education and problem-solving. Deeper emotional experiences (pride, shock, self-blame) were rarely reflected with emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on the dietary/juice target throughout, moving coherently from exploration to a concrete plan with follow-up. Minor drift into generic health metrics (sleep, mood tracking) slightly broadened the focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative moments (inviting choice, agreeing to role-play), but the interaction often resembled an expert consultation with instructional blocks delivered without negotiating agenda. Marco's ideas did shape the plan, preventing a lower score.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional choice-based language ('Want to pick one change?') but frequent prescribing of specific scripts, templates, and plans subtly steered the client. Marco's autonomy statements ('I know myself') were not explicitly affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on providing information rather than eliciting the client's own motivations and solutions. Marco's motivations and 10/10 confidence emerged spontaneously and were not drawn out through evocative or scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited input and engaged in requested role-play, but the overall stance was expert-driven with prescriptive advice and limited exploration of Marco's own ideas. The power balance tilted toward the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marco generated substantial change talk on his own, but the counselor rarely reflected or amplified it. His 'A 10' confidence rating was completely unexplored — a significant missed opportunity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('boy, do I love that OJ') was briefly acknowledged but not actively softened with double-sided reflections or exploration of the attachment. Shifting to calorie education incidentally redirected focus but was not a deliberate MI technique.
