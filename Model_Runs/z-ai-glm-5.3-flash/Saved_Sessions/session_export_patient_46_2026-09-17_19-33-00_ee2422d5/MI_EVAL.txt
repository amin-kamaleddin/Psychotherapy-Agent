# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental with a mildly defensive teen, normalizing the situation from the start and never lecturing. Slightly scripted affirmations and limited explicit affirmation of Ethan as a person keep it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, with skilled double-sided reflections capturing Ethan's ambivalence. Emotional layers — embarrassment over the puking video and fear of being called 'boring' — were acknowledged but never deeply explored at the feeling level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question funnels toward the target behavior, and drifting conversations are gently steered back to practical strategy. The session moves in a clear arc from minimizing to a concrete plan with a specific first step and success metric.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way, with Ethan's ideas driving the plan and the therapist asking rather than telling. Structure is somewhat asymmetric, with little explicit invitation for Ethan to shape the session agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: ownership is repeatedly affirmed, control is explicitly handed back at the close, and Ethan's modest self-defined goals are supported rather than imposing abstinence. Developmentally attuned for an adolescent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All of Ethan's reasons for change were elicited through open questions rather than supplied, and confidence talk was skillfully evoked ('I actually think I could pull that off'). His closing commitment to return emerged organically from this stance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly and genuinely located in the client, with Ethan's own contributions (soccer nights, drink-in-hand) forming the core of the plan. Textbook partnership with an adolescent population.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The standout strength of the session — change talk was consistently evoked and amplified, escalating from 'maybe not getting so wasted' to 'I could pull that off' and a commitment to return.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally not dwelt on, with skillful pivots toward what is doable rather than arguing. On a couple of occasions ('some nights more fun') sustain talk passed without a reflection or pivot.
