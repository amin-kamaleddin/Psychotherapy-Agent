# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, bookending the session with support for Rosa's choices. However, affirmations were generic ('Nice work,' 'That sounds wise') rather than specific to Rosa's individual strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening captured her ambivalence and fear of failure. Deeper emotional content—embarrassment from the past failed attempt, her mother's health—went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on smoking cessation from opening to closing, with every exchange tied to building a concrete, specific action plan. The session progressed coherently from ambivalence to committed next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was largely co-constructed, with Rosa actively shaping each element. The counselor tended to propose solutions first and have Rosa select, rather than generating ideas jointly from equal footing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Rosa's choice and respected her pacing, including accepting her hesitancy about the patch. Ready-made menus and scripts somewhat constrained space for her own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out authentic change talk via open questions and the confidence ruler ('nervous but kind of ready'). Often filled silence with option lists rather than probing her own reasons and hopes more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Rosa's input and incorporated her ideas into the plan, but frequently slid into an expert role with pre-packaged menus and drafted texts/scripts. Rosa's own expertise (e.g., the Saturday practice idea) could have been drawn out more.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and the confidence ruler effectively evoked meaningful change language. Sometimes answered own questions by immediately supplying options instead of letting Rosa generate reasons first.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk received substantial airtime without consistent strategic pivoting; double-sided reflections linking fears back to change talk were rare. Extensive option lists anchored the conversation in problem details rather than resolving ambivalence.
