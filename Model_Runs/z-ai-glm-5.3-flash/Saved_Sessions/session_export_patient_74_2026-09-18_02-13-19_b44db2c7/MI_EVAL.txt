# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, unconditional positive regard throughout, explicitly validating Linda's right to keep smoking while honoring her concerns. Active affirmation ('that matters,' 'that's yours, Linda') goes beyond basic respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflections that consistently elicit deeper material, including double-sided reflections and reflections of deeper meaning. Precise mirroring of Linda's emotional language lands every time.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking with a progressive arc from ambivalence to a concrete, client-owned plan. Deflections are honored while gently continuing to move forward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals—every forward step originates from Linda's own ideas and is credited back to her. She leaves with a plan she designed herself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support: choice explicitly affirmed at multiple points with no imposed advice, and Linda designed her own plan. Confirmed by her comment, 'Thanks for not lecturing me.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from Linda's own values as a mother and role model rather than external persuasion. Her own words chart the change ('I'm thinking about it more than I was an hour ago').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Linda repeatedly positioned as the expert, with questions inviting her own ideas rather than imposing the clinician's. Her contributions demonstrably shaped the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout skill in evoking change talk through open, values-based questions, then reinforcing it with reflections and discrepancy. Momentum culminates in Linda's self-generated commitment to a weekend experiment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk handled masterfully—briefly and neutrally acknowledged without argument, then pivoted toward her own concerns. It progressively diminishes across the session, evidence of effective softening.
