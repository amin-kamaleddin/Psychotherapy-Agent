# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with multiple affirmations of Mei's courage, but affirmations are somewhat formulaic and quickly paired with problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of core concerns and ambivalence, though brief summaries followed by option lists miss deeper emotional material like shame and fear of becoming like her father.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking and improving contraceptive reliability, with concrete, time-bound steps tied to target behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with drafts offered for Mei to adapt and her contributions shaping the plan, though the counselor often supplies the full option menus, shifting generative burden away from equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Mei's ownership through choices rather than directives, respecting her pacing, and accepting her decisions; she leaves having authored her own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good opening elicitation and scaling questions, but motivation increasingly supplied through option menus and scripts rather than drawn out from Mei, especially in the second half.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Mei as the expert and lets her choices shape the plan, though pre-packaged option menus and scripts mean substantial generative work comes from the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mei volunteered strong change talk, but the counselor did little to deliberately deepen it, e.g., exploring why importance was 8 or what would raise confidence from 4.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and responded with normalization and practical reframing, though some responses jumped to solutions rather than using double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
