# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and affirming throughout with no judgment or disapproval. However, the warmth was somewhat formulaic and failed to address Sofia's confusion about the session's purpose.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used accurate surface reflections and good summaries of Sofia's routine, but stayed at a factual level and missed emotional nuance. Most critically, Sofia's expressed confusion about the meeting's purpose was never reflected or addressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The most serious failure: the referral was for a positive alcohol risk screening, yet alcohol was never mentioned. The session had focus, but on the wrong target (generic wellness/walking), and Sofia noticed the mismatch without the counselor redirecting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructed a concrete plan with Sofia choosing the cue, timing, and days, with the confidence rating used collaboratively. Points off because collaboration built a plan Sofia called 'overkill' rather than setting an agenda aligned with the referral.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and lack of pressure, and Sofia's refusals were accepted without pushback. Slightly diminished because autonomy was offered around an off-target behavior rather than the actual decision at hand.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited Sofia's preferences and confidence ratings, but applied to a trivial counselor-imposed goal rather than her motivations about alcohol use. Sofia remained passive and her perspective on why she was there was never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Sofia as the expert, with her contributions shaping the plan. However, the partnership operated within the counselor's agenda, and Sofia's direct question about the session's purpose went unaddressed, undermining genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did elicit modest change talk (commitment to walks, confidence rising from 7 to 8, specific days), but it was misdirected—focused on walks rather than alcohol. No change talk related to the target behavior was ever evoked.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia expressed repeated status-quo language, which the counselor occasionally accepted rather than strategically exploring. The alcohol-related ambivalence that should have been the focus was never engaged at all.
