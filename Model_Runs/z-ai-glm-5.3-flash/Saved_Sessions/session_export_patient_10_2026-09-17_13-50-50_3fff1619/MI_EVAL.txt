# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with effective normalization ('Missing one interview doesn't make you unreliable—life happens'), though closing lines became formulaic and the crisis disclaimer felt disconnected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened with an accurate empathic reflection, but reflective listening largely disappeared after the first exchanges, leaving key emotional disclosures (embarrassment, fear of rejection, awkwardness) unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on resolving the missed interview and childcare barriers with no drift, though the focus was so task-oriented that emotional barriers were neglected.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine two-way exchange shaped the drafts (tone, text format, neighbor choice), but the counselor typically delivered complete solutions and the session closed with monologue-style instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission before offering content and invited Amina to choose options, but then supplied fully written scripts and prescriptive checklists that undercut client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was mostly counselor-supplied through advice and scripting; Amina's self-efficacy went unprompted and undeveloped, and by the end she was signaling the counselor had 'said it enough times.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Positioned as a helper and shared some decision-making, but functioned largely as an advice-giver with fully drafted messages and step-by-step checklists rather than drawing on Amina's own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Selected and affirmed some of Amina's spontaneous change talk and produced a commitment, but missed chances to deepen it (e.g., exploring why her confidence was 8 not 10) and never affirmed her self-efficacy as a strength.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk and offered a helpful reframe of the missed interview, but bypassed Amina's worries and avoidance ('I just keep putting it off') rather than working with them strategically.
