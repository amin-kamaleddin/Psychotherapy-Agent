# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone with no labeling of heavy phone use; ambivalence normalized and slips framed without judgment ('restart-after-slip approach shows self-compassion').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Amina's ambivalence about connection and sleep. However, the emotional core of her FOMO and self-criticism ('It's embarrassing, honestly') was acknowledged only briefly rather than explored deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent, undeviating focus on evening phone use, with every turn building a concrete, actionable plan from rulers through implementation, slip recovery, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is largely co-constructed, with Amina selecting and adapting strategies that the psychiatrist incorporates. Some turns present the counselor's ideas first rather than soliciting her ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the plan as Amina's choice, with options presented as selections and the check-in offered rather than imposed. Some prescriptive advice bundles slightly reduced her sense of authorship.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out her own reasons and solutions (alertness ratings, prewritten text, roommate accountability) through evocative questioning. A few moments relied on counselor-supplied options, and underlying values were not deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Amina as the expert, with options offered as a menu and her contributions substantially shaping the plan. Some suggestion-heavy turns edged toward expert-driven advice-giving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully uses importance/confidence rulers, commitment questions, and tiny-action prompts, moving Amina's confidence from 3 to 7–8. Some emerging change talk (e.g., her recognizing all-or-nothing thinking) was not further deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and uses reframes and experiment framing to lower resistance, but engages at length with barrier logistics (friend reactions, stress scrolling) without consistent pivoting back to change language.
