# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental; the patient's boundary around quitting was honored without pressure. Warmth was somewhat formulaic and affirmation of the patient as a person was limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurately reflected surface goals and resistance, but reflections stayed at the content level and were repetitive. Vulnerable moments (staining, grandkids, failed attempts) were missed and never deepened.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifted entirely into dental logistics, and the target behavior was explicitly kept off the table with no forward movement toward smoking change. The appearance concern could have been bridged to a next step on the referral goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early questions invited the patient's priorities, but the session shifted to a didactic mode with counselor-proposed plans that the client merely ratified. Decisions were not co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was consistently reinforced ('I'll follow your lead,' 'no rush,' 'reach out when you're ready'). One deduct for pressing the script after the patient initially declined it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The patient spontaneously voiced motivation, but the counselor asked no open questions about reasons, importance, or confidence for change. Change talk was left unexplored and the approach was informational rather than evocative.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inclusive language and agenda-following were present, but the counselor remained the provider of answers (options, costs, scripts) rather than drawing on the client's own expertise. The script was offered despite the client saying no.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A safe, non-lecturing stance allowed natural change talk to emerge ('the staining bothers me more than I let on,' 'I'd have to really want to'). However, the counselor never evoked, reflected, or deepened these statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was accepted without debate or reinforcement, and the session redirected to the patient's chosen agenda. No strategic pivot or amplification followed when change talk emerged.
