# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance despite the court-mandated context, but affirmations are sparse and formulaic, with limited normalization of slips.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that captures Jordan's dual struggle, but deeper emotional content (guilt, shame, fear of being seen as soft) is touched but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior throughout, progressing logically from triggers to refusal rehearsal to relapse planning with a clear, actionable outcome and no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative role-play and co-constructed plans, but the counselor occasionally slides into advice-dispensing mode (e.g., the top-down numbered relapse plan).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames choices as Jordan's and accepts hesitations without pressure, but tends to move quickly into prescriptive option lists rather than reinforcing Jordan's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation is elicited from within, but the counselor's default is providing solutions and menus rather than drawing out Jordan's own ideas and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely positions Jordan as the expert whose choices shape the session plan, though the counselor often generates the menu of options himself rather than eliciting Jordan's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges and is occasionally reinforced, but much is spontaneous; targeted evocative questions and confidence rulers are largely absent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without amplification, but reflections lack strategic pivots or double-sided reflections toward Jordan's own change reasons, defaulting instead to problem-solving.
