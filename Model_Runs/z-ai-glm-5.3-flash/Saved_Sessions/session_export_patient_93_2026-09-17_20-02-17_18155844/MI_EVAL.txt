# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; patient spontaneously expressed feeling unjudged ('Thank you for not treating me like some old lush'). Relapse framed as 'information, not failure' with no judgment or disapproval anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including the double-sided opening reflection and precise distillation of values ('independence is the heart of it'). The counselor also warmly reflected the mother-son relationship dynamic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol intake from open to close, with every question and reflection tied to the target behavior. Progressed logically from ambivalence exploration to a concrete plan with one-month follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor proposed co-writing the plan together and positioned herself as one of several supports. Questions were genuinely open rather than leading, with interaction between equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of patient ownership with no imposed advice or pressure toward abstinence. Framed lapses as 'information, not failure' and credited the plan entirely to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within via open questions ('What matters most to you?'), and the patient explicitly noted being allowed to find her own way. Textbook evocation confirmed by the patient's self-generated plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client ('That's your expertise talking, not mine'), with the plan substantially shaped by the patient's own ideas. Power sharing was exemplary.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and reinforced, culminating in the patient's own plan (no home wine, pub limits, calendar tracking). Patient's closing remarks confirmed she had 'talked myself into a plan I might actually follow.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimizing was met with affirmation and values pivots rather than argument, and sustain talk was appropriately acknowledged without amplification. Minor deduction because a few reflections restated minimizing language ('daft, but sensible').
