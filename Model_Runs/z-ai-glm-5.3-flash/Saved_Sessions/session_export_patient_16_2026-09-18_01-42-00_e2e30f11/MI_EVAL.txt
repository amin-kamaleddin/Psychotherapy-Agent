# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout, with strong affirmations and explicit acceptance ('I'm not here to take that away from you'). No labeling or moralizing despite the sensitive context of adolescent substance use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, accurate complex reflections that capture meaning and emotion, consistently prompting the client to elaborate and gain new insights. Reflections reframed ambivalence with precision and attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on vaping from start to finish, moving progressively through evocation, planning, and commitment with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership of equals; the counselor positioned herself as curious rather than authoritative, and every plan element was co-constructed with the client as author.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: explicit disclaimers of control, offered choice at every decision point, and no prescriptions or pressure. All plan elements were client-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out the client's intrinsic motivators (athletic identity, friendship, competitiveness) rather than imposing reasons to change. The session culminated in self-initiated commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located within the client; the counselor repeatedly attributed ownership of ideas and connected the plan to the client's existing strengths and identity.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation and reinforcement of change talk using open questions, a confidence ruler, and amplification of the client's own ideas. Abundant client change talk and explicit commitment emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and redirected with skill, never argued against. The pivot from vaping as 'her moment' to client-generated alternatives was beautifully executed.
