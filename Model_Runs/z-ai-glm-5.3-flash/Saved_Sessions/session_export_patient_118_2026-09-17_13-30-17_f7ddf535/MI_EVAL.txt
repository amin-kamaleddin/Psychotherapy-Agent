# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, never arguing or labeling when Seamus pushed back on smoking talk. However, warmth felt transactional, with repetitive formulaic closing offers and little individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequately honored the client's agenda ('we'll stick to the teeth'), but never reflected emotional content like his weariness, defensiveness, or hints of ambivalence. The session stayed informational rather than emotionally attuned.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor entirely abandoned the target behavior (smoking) after one pushback and never returned to it, even when Seamus voiced readiness language. Repeated identical closing offers created a circular, aimless ending.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor solicited the client's agenda and offered options, but the session became largely one-way information delivery. The counselor acted more as an advisor than an equal partner co-creating understanding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest domain: autonomy was consistently reinforced ('Pick one now or tell you're done'; 'I respect your choice about smoking'). Seamus directly validated this: 'Thanks for not pushing it — genuinely.' No coercive language appeared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No attempt was made to elicit Seamus's own motivations, values, or reasons for change about smoking. Even his spontaneous 'when I'm ready' remark went unexplored; the session relied on information delivery instead.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing through choices and agenda questions, but the counselor slipped into expert mode with cost lists and pre-packaged questions. Seamus's own insight about readiness was not built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Seamus's spontaneous opening ('I know I'll have to deal with the smoking at some point') was a prime missed opportunity that passed without affirmation or exploration. No questions about importance or confidence in quitting were ever posed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided amplifying sustain talk by pivoting to dental concerns, which reduced defensiveness and preserved goodwill. However, this was achieved by avoiding the topic rather than strategic double-sided reflections.
