# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, affirming the client's honesty and validating his engagement despite initial irritability. Unconditional regard was implicit rather than explicitly voiced at vulnerable moments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured ambivalence and key values, including echoing the client's own words. Some reflections summarized rather than deepened emotion, leaving fear and grief underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus, progressing steadily from lunches to wind-down habits to a concrete one-week trial with relapse planning and a follow-up. Discussion stayed tethered to actionable goals without feeling coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative, with plans co-constructed and terms (15-minute check-ins, one-month timeline) negotiated mutually. Occasionally relied on polished summaries rather than checking in on direction before moving forward.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding support of choice: all commitments originated from the client, negotiated limits were accepted, and the 'maybe' walk was explicitly left as his to take up. No unsolicited advice or pressure at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited almost entirely from the client, surfacing his deepest reasons—his father's heart trouble and his children. Textbook evocation with no prescribed reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly positioned the client as the expert, and his contributions genuinely shaped the session's structure and plan. Polished summaries occasionally dominated the floor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions consistently drew out change talk, from identifying wine/takeaways as targets to a concrete one-week commitment and deep intrinsic motivation. Change talk was reflected and amplified throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, using effective double-sided reflections and redirecting doubt toward the client's own problem-solving. Occasionally met with questions rather than intentional strategic reframes.
