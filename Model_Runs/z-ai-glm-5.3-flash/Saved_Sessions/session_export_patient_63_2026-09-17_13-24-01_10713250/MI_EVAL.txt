# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Neutral, nonjudgmental stance with respectful engagement, but minimal warmth and no affirmations of Jordan's honesty or courage; acceptance is present but shallow.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was largely absent; question barrages replaced understanding, and Jordan's guilt, fear of losing his social group, and the significant 'deadweight' insight went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, logical focus on shoplifting recidivism throughout, moving from assessment to planning to role-play without drift, though the direction was pursued through rapid-fire assessment rather than collaborative goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jordan's choices (mum as check-in, deflect line, rubber band) shaped the plan, but the counselor dominated with expert solutions and monologue-style advice; power-sharing was real but uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choice points and accepted Jordan's 'no promises', but the core change plan was largely dictated by the counselor and relied on external legal threats rather than fostering ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Answers came from the counselor via external consequences and pre-packaged tips rather than elicited from Jordan; his best motivational moments emerged unprompted and were never mined with evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine choice-offering, but the dominant stance was expert-driven, delivering pre-packaged plans and scripts instead of drawing out Jordan's own ideas when he said he didn't know what to do instead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jordan spontaneously offered change talk ('I can't keep doing it', 'tired of looking over my shoulder'), but the counselor never deliberately evoked or deepened it, instead supplying reasons and doing the motivational work for the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and occasionally reframed ('walking away is strength'), but failed to actively manage ambivalence about losing friends with double-sided reflections, and even introduced its own sustain-relevant framing.
