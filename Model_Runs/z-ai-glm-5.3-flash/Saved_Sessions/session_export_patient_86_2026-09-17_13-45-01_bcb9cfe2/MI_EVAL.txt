# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and explicitly honored Marco's 'no lectures' request. Few affirmations of his honesty or openness, limiting warmth and depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but sparse and generic, rarely exploring the emotional weight of his health history or partner's concerns. Often pivoted quickly to option lists rather than deepening understanding of his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a steady focus on smoking cessation and converted vague openness into a concrete plan (delay, porch rule, GP consult, Day 1). Deducted slightly for wandering opening and option menus that diluted momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Checked in on Marco's preferences and adapted when he rejected homework, but often presented pre-formed advice blocks rather than co-generating ideas. The best plan elements (kettle cue, porch) came from Marco himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Marco's control and framed slipping as 'data, not failure.' Some turns still delivered prescriptive instruction lists despite choice-based framing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely drew out Marco's own motivations or confidence, relying instead on counselor-supplied tips and option menus. His change talk emerged from the nonjudgmental climate rather than skillful evocative questioning, and wasn't deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Marco's input, but frequently slipped into an expert role with bulleted advice and prescriptive instructions, edging past his stated 'no lectures' boundary. Power-sharing occurred more through Marco's pushback than deliberate collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk emerged ('I could give it a burl,' delay plan, GP consult) but was partly by luck rather than strategic elicitation. The counselor talked more than evoked, offering menus instead of drawing out Marco's own reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk briefly without amplifying it and pivoted to Marco's own practical ideas. No double-sided reflections were used to help resolve ambivalence, and one reflection risked legitimizing not changing.
