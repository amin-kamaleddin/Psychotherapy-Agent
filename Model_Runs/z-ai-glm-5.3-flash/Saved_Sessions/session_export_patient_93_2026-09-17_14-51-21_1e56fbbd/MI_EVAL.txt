# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with explicit statements of support and validation of the client's minimizations without shaming. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deep, individualized affirmation of her strengths and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening throughout, including a solid emotional reflection at the moment the client connected alcohol to her fall. Reflections occasionally summarized content rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning, such as her fear of falling or protectiveness of her social life.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol and fall risk across the entire session with no topic drift. The session progressed logically from engagement through information to a concrete, self-generated plan ending with a tracking tool.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative, with the client's own ideas driving the final plan and a checklist tailored to her preferences. Portions of the psychoeducation were delivered in a fairly expert-driven, didactic block rather than through fully shared dialogue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support: permission was requested before sharing information, choices were offered at every juncture, and her refusal to quit was explicitly respected. The final plan was entirely her own construction, reinforced by the counselor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from within, eliciting the client's own realization about the fall and using a confidence ruler that produced rising confidence and concrete commitments. Could have strengthened evocation by exploring why cutting back matters to her beyond fall risk or eliciting a summary commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned the client as the expert, asking which options she preferred and building the plan from her suggestions. Sometimes offered fairly structured option menus rather than open exploration, slightly constraining her authorship of the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk through permission-based psychoeducation and a targeted confidence question that elicited a specific, self-generated plan and rising confidence. Could have deepened commitment language by exploring her reasons for change or using an importance scaling question.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently acknowledged sustain talk with neutral, validating reflections and never argued for or attempted to persuade toward abstinence, avoiding reactance. Softening relied mostly on neutral acceptance rather than strategic double-sided reflections linking her own worries to her goals.
