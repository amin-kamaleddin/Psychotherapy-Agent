# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with affirmations like 'That's not nothing, Jordan' that validate without minimizing discouragement. Both sustain talk and change talk are accepted equally throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including complex double-sided reflections capturing ambivalence ('how drained you are, and at the same time, maybe starting small'). Accurately reflects the sister dynamic and Jordan's survival mode.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, progressive focus from past coping through to a concrete weekly plan, barrier planning, and a take-home message. Each response advances the arc while remaining patient-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way exchange with inclusive language ('let's think together'). Jordan's answers serve as the working material, and decisions are co-explored rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Jordan's ownership, acknowledged by the patient himself: 'Nobody made me do it. This was mine.' Uses permission-based language and honors Jordan's right not to over-promise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from within Jordan, eliciting his values, past strengths, and own barrier solutions. Jordan spontaneously articulates self-generated motivation by session's end.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Jordan as the expert ('You know yourself better than anyone'). The Tuesday/Thursday library plan comes entirely from Jordan, with the counselor building on his ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk by highlighting Jordan's 'maybe,' asking about what would make the biggest difference, and envisioning concrete steps. Change talk escalates from vague wishing to specific commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally pivots away from sustain talk with double-sided reflections rather than reinforcing it. Minor gap: the recurring 'I can't get a break' helplessness theme is not always explicitly reframed, though Jordan integrates it constructively by the end.
