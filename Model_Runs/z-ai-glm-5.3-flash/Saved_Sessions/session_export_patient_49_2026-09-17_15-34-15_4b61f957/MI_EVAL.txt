# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent affirmations and no labeling; slips normalized as 'information, not failure.' Falls short of a 5 because affirmations are somewhat generic rather than individualized to Hana's specific strengths like her honesty and self-awareness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures Hana's ambivalence (e.g., the double-sided reflection on benefits vs. gains). However, deeper emotional content — her shame about 'caving' to her sister and fear of failure on nights out — went unexplored and unvalidated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction throughout, steering every turn back to the target behavior. The session progressed logically to a concrete, time-bound plan with a scheduled Tuesday follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed almost entirely from Hana's own suggestions, with the counselor offering menus of options and asking permission before adding structure. The dialogue reflected equal partners rather than an expert-patient hierarchy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was explicitly and repeatedly reinforced ('You're in control of what we talk about'), and Hana's declines (nicotine gum, detailed Friday planning) were respected without pushing. Her closing statement that the plan felt 'mine' is textbook evidence of effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the client via rulers, open questions, and eliciting her own definitions of success — no reasons for change were imposed. Hana generated her own backup plans and motivations (appearance, fresh breath, proving it's 'not as hard as I've been telling myself').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor powerfully conveyed that expertise resides within the client, building entirely on Hana's contributions rather than substituting professional prescriptions. Asking what she'd like to be asked about on Tuesday explicitly handed evaluative authority to her, and her 'mine, I guess' confirms the power-sharing succeeded.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully and consistently evoked change talk through importance/confidence rulers, questions about what would move her rating, and eliciting her definition of a 'meaningful win.' Hana's importance moved from 4 to confidence of 7, and she described the plan as 'mine' — clear evidence the evocation worked.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was largely handled well via brief double-sided reflections followed by change-oriented questions, and the 'Friday nights don't count' rationalization was gently challenged without judgment. Not a 5 because sustain talk was occasionally restated without redirection, and ambivalence around weekend social smoking wasn't fully consolidated.
