# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently warm, respectful, and non-judgmental tone, affirming Anya without disapproval ('That takes guts'). Falls short of excellence because warmth was brief and formulaic, with little explicit normalization of her struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opened with accurate complex reflections capturing her core dilemma (connection and buzz vs. real costs) and reflected her fear of judgment well. Reflections became sparse in the second half as the session shifted toward tool delivery and role-play.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on gambling reduction with a logical progression from exploration to a concrete action plan (bank call Wednesday, two-week tracking, sister accountability, role-play). Lost a point for bypassing social-fear moments quickly and lacking an explicit consolidating summary or closure plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Checked in frequently and adapted to Anya's choices, including eagerly accepted role-play. However, content was noticeably counselor-driven, with Anya mostly selecting among pre-packaged options rather than co-creating solutions in her own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Anya's choice ('I'll support whatever you decide') and offered options rather than directives, with her ownership evident ('I'll send it tonight'). Heavy provision of scripts and templates edged toward the counselor shaping the 'how,' with limited emphasis on her owning setbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effective early evocative techniques (importance/confidence rulers, linking Egypt as 'the why') drew out genuine motivation. The session then shifted from evoking to instructing, with most solutions originating from the counselor rather than Anya's own reasons, ideas, and language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Anya as the decision-maker and offered menus of options rather than prescriptions. Most concrete ideas (bank freeze, scripts, templates) were counselor-generated, diluting the sense that wisdom resides within the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully used importance and confidence rulers to elicit strong change talk ('Egypt's on the line, so I've got to get tougher with myself,' 'cutting up the credit cards'). Missed opportunities to deeply reflect and affirm change talk when it emerged, with much session energy spent providing tools instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and occasionally reframed doubt positively ('feels doable and not humiliating'). Several doubt statements ('They'd judge me,' 'I don't know if I've got the guts') passed without double-sided reflections or capability affirmations, making the handling neutral rather than skillful.
