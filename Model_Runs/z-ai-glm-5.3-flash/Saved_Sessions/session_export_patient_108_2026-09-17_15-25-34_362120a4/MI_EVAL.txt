# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirmed Oliver's compassion. However, acceptance occasionally felt formulaic and the emotional difficulty (guilt, fear of conflict) was not deeply normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate, but the session shifted heavily toward advice-giving and scripting. Emotional nuances like Oliver's self-label 'weak' and fear of 'folding' were rarely reflected back, and many turns contained no reflection at all.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the boundary-setting goal throughout. Every exchange moved purposefully from ambivalence exploration to scaling, scripting, role-play, relapse planning, and the reminder text with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative elements like role-play and building on Oliver's broken-record strategy existed, but the overall pattern was expert-driven, with multi-item advice lists delivered in nearly every turn rather than negotiated back-and-forth.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, asked permission before advising, and let Oliver pick scripts and scenarios. However, the volume of counselor-supplied plans slightly undermined ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good scaling questions and open prompts evoked strong change talk early, but in the latter half the counselor supplied solutions rather than eliciting Oliver's own scripts, plans, and words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and role-play supported partnership, but from the midpoint the counselor dominated with expert-generated scripts and plans, so Oliver's own expertise did not substantially shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used open questions, scaling, and role-play; Oliver's confidence rose from four to eight and a half with strong change talk. However, opportunities to reflect and deepen change talk (e.g., 'Like I'm allowed to want it') were missed in favor of more tips.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and occasionally reframed it, but there was no strategic use of double-sided reflections; guilt and ambivalence were sometimes bypassed rather than softened and redirected.
