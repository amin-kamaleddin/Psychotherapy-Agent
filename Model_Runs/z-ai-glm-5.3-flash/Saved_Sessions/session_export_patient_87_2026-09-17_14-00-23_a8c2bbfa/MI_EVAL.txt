# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with normalized slips, but affirmations were generic and few of Marco's specific strengths were acknowledged. Warmth felt somewhat mechanical rather than individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections, but emotionally meaningful content (fear of failure, saying no feeling rude, his wife's struggle) was repeatedly skipped in favor of tactics. Hopefulness ('higher than I've felt in a while') went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight, consistent focus on the target behavior with no topic drift. Each turn advanced a concrete plan ending with clear next steps and accountability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction was heavily counselor-directed, with advice offered before exploring Marco's perspective and the plan architecture fully counselor-authored. Marco shaped only details, not the overall strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice was offered (menus, tweaking scripts, check-in timing), but framing remained prescriptive and controlling. Marco's ownership of the plan was never explicitly affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Strategies, scripts, and contingencies were supplied by the counselor rather than drawn from Marco; his spontaneous insights were never elicited or amplified. Confidence scaling was used well but in isolation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor consistently assumed the expert role, prescribing solutions and pre-answering questions. Little exploration of Marco's own ideas about what might work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions successfully produced rising confidence and commitment ('I think I'm ready'), but notable change talk was never deepened or amplified. No evocative questions about Marco's values or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Doubts and fears were repeatedly met with problem-solving rather than acknowledgment, double-sided reflections, or exploration. Fixes implicitly treated ambivalence as something to engineer away rather than resolve.
