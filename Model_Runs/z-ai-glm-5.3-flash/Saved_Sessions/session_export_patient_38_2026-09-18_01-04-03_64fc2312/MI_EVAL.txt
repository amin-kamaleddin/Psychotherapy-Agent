**Comments:** Unwavering acceptance throughout; explicitly validates ambivalence, normalizes her feelings, and reassures her that embarrassment about knowledge gaps is not a personal failing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeated, accurate reflections of her fear, relief, ambivalence, and specific worries (pain, mood, memory); validates each emotional disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully follows the patient's agenda while gently steering toward the referral goal; she arrives at information-seeking and even consideration of LARC entirely on her own terms.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Truly shared decision-making — asks what she wants to explore, lets her pick starting topics, and checks in before providing information.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Rich open questions that elicit her needs, values, and priorities — what a safe conversation looks like, which worries matter most — driving the session's direction.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Asks permission before informing, offers choices at each step, and frames the patient as the expert and decision-maker in her own care.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces her own motivations ("never want to feel like that again," "I'd forget"), leading to her spontaneous movement toward considering the implant.
