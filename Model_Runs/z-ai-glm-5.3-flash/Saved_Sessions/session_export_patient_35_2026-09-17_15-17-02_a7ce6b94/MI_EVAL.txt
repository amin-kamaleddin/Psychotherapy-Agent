# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental stance with consistent affirmations of Elena's efforts, though affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic; deeper regard around her fear of being judged as a mother could have been expressed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing her stress, ambivalence, and need for private time, but deeper emotional themes (loneliness, shame after the pediatrician's reaction) were acknowledged without full exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus from exploration through scaling to a concrete, measurable plan (three tea nights, backups, do-over rule), with every turn advancing the agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed plan — Elena generated the confidence-boosters, chose her coping line, and requested failure planning, while the counselor supplied structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent choice-offering, permission-seeking before information, honoring her pace and 'I'm not promising anything,' and a non-punitive do-over framing strongly supported autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out self-generated motivations and reasoning via scaling questions, but some opportunities were underdeveloped — 'Why would this time be different?' was deflected into scaling and deeper emotional meaning wasn't explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena was positioned as the expert, choosing options, nights, and her own coping line, though the counselor still supplied pre-made menus rather than evoking more of her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic, well-timed use of importance/confidence scaling elicited escalating change talk, from 'I've thought about quitting' to 'maybe I'll actually buy the lozenges,' culminating in a confidence jump from 4–5 to 6–7.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was tolerated and never reinforced, but responses were mostly neutral reflections or pivots to logistics rather than strategic double-sided reflections to actively resolve ambivalence.
