# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout; David's limits ('evenings are mine') and slips are accepted and normalized. However, warmth is somewhat procedural and David's strengths or honesty are never explicitly affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent accurate reflections capture David's ambivalence and the social function of smoking breaks. Reflections are largely summary-style and the emotional meaning beneath his defensiveness is not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, focused guidance toward a concrete, time-limited plan with defined steps and no topic drift. Direction is strong but slightly premature given David's contemplation-stage ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and menu options show collaborative elements, but the plan is largely counselor-designed with David merely accepting or tweaking. Genuine co-construction from David's own ideas is limited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support via repeated choice questions, acceptance of limits, and framing slips as information. Falls short because David frames the outcome as the counselor's responsibility ('you tell me how it went') and this goes unchallenged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is assumed rather than drawn from the client; after one double-barreled opening question, the counselor shifts to expert provision of options, dosing, and scripts. David's own reasons and confidence are never explored or amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asking permission before informing supports power-sharing, but the intervention is a pre-built package with David's role reduced to sign-off. His expertise about break rituals and social dynamics is reflected but not leveraged as the plan's foundation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited and consolidated ('Monday makes sense... I could give it a bash'), but David's reasons and confidence are not evoked. Much commitment language was pre-built by the counselor for David to accept.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is mirrored neutrally and never argued with, preventing reactance, but there is little active softening. No double-sided reflections or strategic pivoting to help David resolve ambivalence.
