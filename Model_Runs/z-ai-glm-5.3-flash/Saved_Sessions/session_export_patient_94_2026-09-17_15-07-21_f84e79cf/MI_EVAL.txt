### Acceptance

**Comments:** Maintained consistent warmth and respect despite initial hostility, with unconditional positive regard throughout ('That's all valid,' 'totally reasonable').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that tracked Maria's layered ambivalence well. Emotional nuances like her self-criticism ('feels kind of stupid') were lightly acknowledged rather than deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on asthma management from opening to concrete plan, gently refocusing digressions. Moved logically from ambivalence to an actionable, client-owned plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered menus of options and let the client's questions shape each response. However, some turns were heavy with counselor-supplied content (checklists, dosing figures), slightly tilting the balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as decision-maker ('I won't push a decision — you're in charge') and respected all disclaimers without challenge. The final trial plan was client-owned and framed around her own preference.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within, producing self-generated realizations about her avoidance. Occasionally led with information rather than eliciting curiosity, and deeper motivations (family) went unexplored.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional trajectory from 'that's not happening' to 'I'll book the appointment' via choices and rehearsal, directly eliciting commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
