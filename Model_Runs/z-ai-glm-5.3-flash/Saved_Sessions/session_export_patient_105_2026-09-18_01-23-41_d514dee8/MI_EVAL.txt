# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; sustain talk was met with acceptance (e.g., 'Both of those are true...') rather than disapproval, consistently normalizing ambivalence and validating honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including complex and double-sided reflections that captured both surface content and deeper meaning, such as the bind between needing income and fearing unfamiliar venues.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on Naomi's social anxiety, with every response linking back to the target behavior and the session progressing logically toward a concrete first step without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was framed as working 'together,' and each turn invited Naomi's input, with the direction genuinely emerging from her contributions and an equal power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Naomi's ownership and choice were reinforced throughout; the plan was entirely client-generated and framed on 'your own terms,' with no persuasive or coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within via evocative questions about hopes and confidence; by the end, Naomi articulated her own desire, ability, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Naomi was explicitly positioned as the expert on her own experience, with every key idea in the plan coming from her and repeatedly credited back to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and highlighted, with desire statements ('I do want to feel less like this') and commitment language ('I'd like to try') reflected back as coming from Naomi herself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly, reframed as part of ambivalence via double-sided reflections, and gently redirected with forward-looking questions rather than elaborated.
