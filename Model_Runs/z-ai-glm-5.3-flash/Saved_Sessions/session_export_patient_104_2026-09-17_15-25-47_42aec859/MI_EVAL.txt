# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of Nadine's efforts. However, warmth is somewhat procedural, missing deeper affirmation of her worth beyond her actions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects Nadine's exhaustion and self-doubt. Several responses shift quickly to information-giving rather than deepening emotional understanding, e.g., fear of 'another failure' is acknowledged only briefly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear, consistent focus on infant sleep disruption and Nadine's exhaustion. Every exchange advances the goal with no drift, from normalizing to concrete planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with option menus and a co-constructed plan shaped by Nadine's input. Falls short because the counselor often supplies content and solutions wholesale rather than drawing ideas out of Nadine first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine choice is repeatedly offered and ownership reinforced ('Whatever you choose, I'll support you'). Advice is framed as optional and Nadine's decisions are honored without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions largely come from the counselor rather than being elicited from Nadine. Rarely asks what has worked before or what strengths she's drawing on; her hope stems from reassurance and planning rather than her own surfaced reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Nadine as the decision-maker and credits her ideas. However, much of the session is counselor-supplied advice with limited acknowledgment of her expertise about her own baby.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change-oriented language emerges ('I'd try almost anything tonight,' 'a tiny bit hopeful') but largely from practical problem-solving rather than deliberate evocation. Change talk is not consistently reflected or amplified when it appears.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Normalizes Nadine's doubts but does not strategically reframe them toward agency, missing double-sided reflections linking exhaustion to motivation. Reassurance around failure fears stays in reassurance mode rather than pivoting to her strengths.
