# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no judgment, even when the client is defensive. Validation of the client's fear of labels and of honest uncertainty exemplifies warm acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, nuanced reflections capture both content and emotional undertones (wariness of labels, fear of bad news). Effective double-sided reflections deepen the client's own exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on alcohol use, advancing step by step from exploration to a concrete client-owned plan. The gentle momentum question ('What would it take to move that visit up?') is well-paced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction operates between equal partners, with the client's contributions (blood work, cutting-back plan) shaping the session's direction. The clinician builds on client ideas rather than steering toward a preset agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is reinforced explicitly and constantly: no pressure to promise, decision explicitly left with the client. The client names this approach as what works for him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from within rather than supplied, with no lecturing about alcohol harms. The client's self-generated reasons (father example, hangover changes) demonstrate successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the expert on his own life, and his ideas visibly drive the session's plan. The clinician affirms client agency throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and amplified, from the client's own signals ('hangovers hit different at 34') to the self-generated middle path of 'four instead of six.' By session end the client articulates intrinsic motivation on his own terms.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally acknowledged briefly and redirected effectively without reinforcement. Minor refinement possible: some sustain talk (e.g., liking beer's taste) receives somewhat extended acknowledgment before pivoting, though this supports rapport.
