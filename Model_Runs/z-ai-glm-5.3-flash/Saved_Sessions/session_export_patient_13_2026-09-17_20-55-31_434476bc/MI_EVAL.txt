# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, normalizing fear and affirming honesty and imperfect progress. No criticism of months of avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally deep reflections capturing ambivalence and the hidden cost of avoidance, and mirroring the client's own language ('hopeless-nervous' to 'okay-nervous').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on seeking medical care, moving strategically from barriers to hope to a concrete, client-owned action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Truly equal partnership with shared decision-making on follow-up and problem-solving; the client explicitly noted she wasn't pushed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client choice ('You get to decide...') and acceptance of her pacing, including not committing to the appointment yet.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within—nearly every turn was an open question evoking her reasons, ideas, and strengths, resulting in a self-generated plan and coping statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located in the client ('the plan you built, in your words'), with her ideas shaping the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk, culminating in strong client-generated commitment ('Friday. Text my sister.') and preparation for obstacles.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced and was well handled with double-sided reflections; occasionally the fear of 'bad answers' could have been pivoted more actively toward change.
