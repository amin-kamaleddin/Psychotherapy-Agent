# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with nonadherence normalized ('Both of those can be true') and strengths affirmed. Samir's humor was respected without disapproval, reinforced by closing affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, especially the reframe of medication as 'something that's done to you, marking you as sick,' which Samir explicitly confirmed. The clinician also caught subtle shifts from defensiveness to plan-making.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on medication nonadherence and progressed from barriers to a concrete behavioral plan. Minor deduction for a few mid-session questions exploring general follow-through rather than medication specifically, though this proved useful.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—the plan was co-constructed entirely from Samir's own life (coffee routine, brother's example), and the check-in was negotiated, not imposed. Client preferences actively shaped the session's structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit ownership support throughout: 'this is your pace, your choice' and asking what would make it feel like his idea. Honored his request for no family announcement and agreed to drop 'speeches.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation was drawn from the client, never supplied—the clinician never gave reasons for change. The change talk cascade from 'I've thought about it more' to a specific self-owned commitment was entirely self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client from the start ('You're the expert on your own situation'), and every question presupposed Samir's wisdom. Samir himself noted his unusual engagement: 'I keep actually answering them.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation produced concrete commitment language: 'bottle by the coffee machine, this week' and 'small enough that even I can't argue with it.' Remarkable movement for a guarded, precontemplative client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was effectively rolled with via acceptance, gentle reframing, and a double-sided reflection. Deducted for occasionally reflecting sustain talk directly and giving the status quo some airtime with the 'both can be true' reflection.
