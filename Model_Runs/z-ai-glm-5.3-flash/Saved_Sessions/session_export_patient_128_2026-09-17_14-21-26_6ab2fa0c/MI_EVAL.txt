# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained a neutral, nonjudgmental stance and included a safety check, but offered only generic warmth with no individualized affirmations of Tyler's honesty, safety efforts, or insight.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no reflective listening—Tyler's core ambivalence about social life versus scholarship went unreflected and unexplored, keeping the session at a factual, checklist level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained consistent focus on the alcohol-related incident, hearing prep, and next steps, though oriented toward logistics and document preparation rather than the target behavior change itself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction was largely counselor-driven with advice lists and drafted documents; Tyler's ideas influenced only minor edits and the harm-reduction plan was handed over rather than co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offered some choices but was largely prescriptive, inserting 'avoiding large off-campus parties' into Tyler's statement despite Tyler's explicit rejection—only revising after Tyler pushed back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** All motivation came from Tyler unprompted; the counselor asked virtually no open questions about values, reasons, or confidence, supplying advice instead of eliciting the client's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumed the expert role and deflected Tyler's request to focus on his drinking-academics balance, with little recognition that expertise resides with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Tyler spontaneously offered meaningful change talk (scholarship concerns, not wanting police trouble), but the counselor never evoked, reinforced, or deepened it—pivoting instead to checklists and drafts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk (a strength), but made no skillful attempts to work through it—no double-sided reflections or reframing—leaving the ambivalence unresolved.
