# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations conveying positive regard. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and occasionally focus on performance praise rather than deep, individualized regard for Elena.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but superficial content summaries. Vulnerable disclosures (racing heart, history of caving, guilt, apologizing as her 'tell') received tactical responses rather than deep emotional reflection of the fear underneath.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on boundaries, self-respect, and protecting personal time from opening through planning, scripting, and rehearsal. Each turn advances a logical agenda with no drift, tied to Elena's stated values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine teamwork: Elena's revisions (weekly outings, texting Dana, casual script tone) demonstrably shaped the final plan. Falls short of 5 because the plan and scripts originated as counselor-drafted materials that Elena edited rather than emerging jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames choices as Elena's and affirms her ownership ('those tones fit you'). Stacked rapid-fire questions mildly pressure pacing, and the counselor occasionally steers toward his own agenda (drafting scripts, role-play).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws motivation from Elena via values questions, a confidence ruler, and rehearsal that elicited self-generated insights. Over-supplies content (scripts, tactics, plan) instead of consistently evoking her own solutions and deeper motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Elena as the expert with permission-seeking and genuine power sharing—her edits substantially shaped the plan. Not a 5 because the counselor initially presented a fully drafted plan rather than co-constructing it from Elena's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces Elena's change language through open questions, values exploration, and a confidence ruler (confidence 9, 'I feel ready'). Elena's change talk largely pre-existed; the counselor could have deepened it further (e.g., why a 9, how success would feel).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce Elena's sustain talk (guilt, old habits, history of caving), which is appropriate, but also does not actively soften it—no double-sided reflections or exploration of the guilt's meaning. The approach is neutral-avoidant rather than strategically working through residual ambivalence.
