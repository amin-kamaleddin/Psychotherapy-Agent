# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, affirming the client's strengths, normalizing guilt, and honoring her wish to stay warm rather than pushing a colder style.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured the client's experience well, but reflections were sometimes brief and mixed with multiple questions or option lists, diluting emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus throughout: identifying drift risk, choosing a boundary phrase, applying it to a concrete upcoming event, and planning rehearsal with a success marker tied to the client's core value.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed with the client selecting and modifying options, but several turns presented counselor-generated scripts rather than jointly generating wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice at every step; the client made all key decisions and the counselor explicitly affirmed her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client's values and articulated success criteria effectively, but some evocation opportunities were traded for supplying ready-made phrase options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's expertise was central to shaping the final plan, though handing over pre-written scripts slightly shifted the balance toward counselor expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced the client's commitment language and success criteria, though supplying solutions occasionally preempted client-generated language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated the client's doubts (backpedaling, hour-option worry) as useful self-knowledge and kept focus steadily on protective action rather than barriers.
