# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and respectful throughout ('You did really well,' 'You've earned that coffee') with no judgment, though praise occasionally feels slightly formulaic. Unconditional positive regard could be deepened at vulnerable moments, e.g., when Elena expressed that self-care 'feels almost selfish.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening, accurately naming the urge to soften and the belief that self-care feels selfish. Empathy could be deeper — the emotional weight of family guilt and the internalized 'self-care is selfish' belief were named but not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on one concrete target behavior (the boundary-setting text), with every exchange building momentum toward it. Directional questions were skillful without being pushy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scripts were co-drafted and role-play was joint, reflecting an equal partnership. Fell short of 5 because the psychiatrist sometimes supplied ready-made options and scripts before fully drawing out Elena's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Elena's ownership — all decisions (text route, timing, reward, replies) were hers, framed as honoring her own values. No pressure was applied at any point in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited from within, including Elena's own reasons, coping strategy, and reward. Slightly reduced by advice-giving moments (supplying three script versions unprompted) and the core belief work not being deeply evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power sharing — Elena's words shaped the final script and her ideas (text route, reward walk) were incorporated. Slightly limited by pre-packaged options and advice-giving preceding full client input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, systematic evocation — Elena's confidence rose from 7 to 8 and her commitment language strengthened progressively ('I'm leaving feeling even more solid'). Rulers, practice, and scripting each produced stronger change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well with practical relapse-prevention planning and the broken-record technique, though opportunities were limited. The psychiatrist occasionally echoed reasons against change without fully reframing them toward change.
