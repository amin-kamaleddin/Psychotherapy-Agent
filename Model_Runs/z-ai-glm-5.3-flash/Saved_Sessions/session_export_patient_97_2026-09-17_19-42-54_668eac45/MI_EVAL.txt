# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard is consistent throughout. The clinician validates guardedness and honesty, and responds respectfully to humor and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture key tensions and emotional nuances. However, deeper feelings like loneliness and grief over losing friendships are acknowledged but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on shifting social connections toward long-term goals and produces a concrete, testable step. The opening is somewhat abstract, and earlier linkage to broader goals could strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions are genuinely curious and open-ended, with the session shaped by the client's answers. The client explicitly confirms the interaction felt non-lecturing and equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice or pressure—the only suggestion (coffee with Darnell) originates from the client. Choice is framed as the client's, and partial failure is normalized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client, who articulates his own 'why' and mechanism of change. Every question is designed to elicit his reasons and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client ('You're the expert on your own life'), and the plan originates entirely from him. The clinician names this ownership shift directly at the close.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully elicited, reflected, and amplified—progressing from 'it'd be good' to 'I actually want it,' with a named person, step, and timeline. Textbook-quality cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated without being amplified, and pivots are made with double-sided reflections. A few sustain statements are reflected back somewhat heavily, though reframed constructively.
