# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with affirmations of Elias's honesty and courage. However, affirmations occasionally feel formulaic and shame/ambivalence is not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early on, accurately capturing Elias's shame, pride, and ambivalence. Reflections become briefer once planning begins, leaving key emotional material (fear of needing rescue, the daughter-wish) under-reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus progressing from relapse reflection to triggers to a concrete action plan and values-based goal. Some redundancy in re-confirming the bottle plan, and deeper relapse exploration was cut short in favor of action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is largely a joint enterprise: options are presented as menus, permission is sought, and Elias's tweaks to the script are adopted. Falls short because the counselor supplies most of the solution content rather than drawing problem-solving from Elias.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support — every plan element is offered as a choice, and the change is explicitly framed as Elias's own decision, directly supporting his 'that's me choosing' breakthrough. No directive or pressuring language appears.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Elias rather than lectured, with role-play evoking powerful emotional change talk. Occasionally supplies rationale rather than asking what steps mean to him, and confidence talk is under-explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias is consistently positioned as the expert, with his wording shaping the final message. The counselor still drives the menu of options and doesn't reflect Elias's spontaneous insights back as his own wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Active evocation of change language through open questions, with strong reinforcement of Elias's commitments and values-based goals. Confidence talk needed deeper development to reach a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about uncertain motivation and fear of failure was acknowledged once but never actively softened or reframed toward agency. The counselor pivoted to action rather than strategically working through the ambivalence.
