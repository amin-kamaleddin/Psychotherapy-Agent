# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic respect and a safety check are present, but warmth is thin — Camila's vulnerable disclosures are met with logistics rather than affirmation of her honesty and self-awareness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; responses are dominated by checklists and generic acknowledgments. Her core insight about fearing the 'buzzkill' gets one sentence before pivoting to tactics, and her final question goes unanswered.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays well-focused on alcohol reduction throughout with no drift, but the direction leans toward logistics rather than progressively building toward change around her emerging realizations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Camila's contributions (tracking-first, cash-only, coded texts) genuinely shaped the plan, but the counselor's default is issuing prescriptive plans that Camila then negotiates down, positioning her as plan-amender rather than co-creator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** No coercion and options are framed as optional, with Camila successfully shaping the plan. However, rapid-fire prescriptive advice subtly presses solutions, with little explicit messaging that the choice is hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no open questions are used to draw out Camila's own motivations; the counselor supplies tactics instead. Her most evocative insights emerged spontaneously and were neither elicited nor developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor occasionally asks for preferences and adopts Camila's ideas, but the overall posture is expert-driven, with bulleted strategy lists that Camila reacts to and amends.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Camila generated strong change talk organically, but the counselor made no effort to systematically evoke or deepen it — her musings that drinking may no longer be fun went unexplored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor never reinforces sustain talk, which is a strength, but also doesn't actively soften it — ambivalence is met with problem-solving tips rather than double-sided reflections.
