# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently showed unconditional positive regard, validating David's boundaries around evenings/weekends and responding to resistance without judgment or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both sides of David's ambivalence and reaching deeper meaning like the health scare and father's heart attack.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing daytime smoking, with every exchange advancing toward a concrete, self-generated plan without aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functioned between equal partners, with the plan co-constructed entirely from David's own ideas and the counselor responding as an honest advisor when asked for straight information.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly reinforced throughout ('this is your call,' 'no big commitment'), with choice-based questions and David explicitly noting 'you didn't lecture me.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the client—his own reasons (blood pressure, father, girlfriend, self-discipline) and solutions—rather than supplied by the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was clearly located within David, who defined what was workable, identified his own triggers, and shaped every decision, while receiving honest, non-salesy information.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and amplified, moving David from 'no promises' to 'I'll give it a shot' with a concrete, self-generated plan (quiet week, pocket lozenge, girlfriend's support).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally acknowledged briefly and redirected toward change effectively, though a few reflections (e.g., 'you're not giving those up') lingered on sustain talk slightly longer than necessary.
