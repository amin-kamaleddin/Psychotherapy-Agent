# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard with no judgment, even when the client admitted past excuses; soreness and setbacks were normalized and affirmations felt genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured the client's ambivalence and frustration, but some stayed at the content level rather than deepening the emotional meaning of his discouragement and lost sense of self.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus moved the session purposefully from vague ambivalence to a fully specified plan with specifics, backups, and accountability, without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with iterative refinement — the plan was unmistakably co-constructed, incorporating the client's own soreness insight, accountability ideas, and script edits.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client made all key decisions, but counselor-proposed tools (scaling, if-then plans, scripts) were sometimes adopted largely as given rather than co-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of scaling and open questions drew out the client's own solutions and commitment, but early exploration of values and deeper reasons for change was brief before shifting to action planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Options were offered rather than imposed, and the client's contributions substantially shaped the plan, though some structured plans and leading framing slightly constrained client authorship.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation and development of change talk, with confidence scaling tracked from 5 to 7.5 and the client voicing strong commitment language ('I'm as ready as I'm gonna be').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and effectively redirected into solutions (if-then plans, self-script), though more double-sided reflections could have actively resolved ambivalence rather than planning around it.
