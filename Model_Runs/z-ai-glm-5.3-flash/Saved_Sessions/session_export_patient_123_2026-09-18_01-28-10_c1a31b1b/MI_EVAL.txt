# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and affirmations toward a guarded, involuntary teen with no judgment of her disengagement. Falls short of 5 because the praise is somewhat generic rather than deeply individualized, keeping the connection polite rather than genuinely intimate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate and complex reflective listening throughout (e.g., naming the avoidance-of-parents-disappointment alongside intrinsic enjoyment). Emotional depth is rarely explored — Sofia's stress, social anxiety, and ambivalence are reflected but not deepened.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The referral concern (alcohol-risk screening) is never raised or bridged, and the target shifts organically to phone/homework rather than being purposefully negotiated. Within the chosen topic focus is good, but overall clinical direction relative to the referral is inconsistent.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: the psychiatrist builds questions from Sofia's statements and the plan co-constructs from her friend's idea and her own modification. The clinician retains editorial control of meaning, with summaries accepted rather than actively negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: 'nothing you said today obligates you to anything,' acceptance of her refusal to promise, and the low-stakes experiment framing. Choice and ownership reside entirely with Sofia, with no pressure at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits rather than prescribes, drawing out Sofia's own motivations (GPA, friendships, self-management). Evocation stays at the level of ideas and logistics; deeper personal values and explicit confidence/importance are never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is explicitly positioned as the expert and her plan is entirely her design — strategy, scope, and day all hers. The clinician still steers the agenda fairly tightly toward a behavior that was never Sofia's stated concern.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes Sofia's own change language ('homework first, then phone after,' 'probably Tuesday') and reinforces and crystallizes it. Early change talk is thin and the clinician sometimes supplies the framing rather than letting Sofia generate it fully.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoids amplifying sustain talk, using double-sided reflections and pivots toward agency and experimentation. Some reflections briefly restate the sustain position without an immediate strategic pivot or active reframing.
