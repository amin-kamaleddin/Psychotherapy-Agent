# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with Jamal's minimization and peer-pressure struggles; affirmations present but somewhat formulaic and tied to compliance rather than deep individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections are accurate, but key emotional openings (the sting of 'thinking you're better than them,' fear of the first 'no') were met with tactic lists rather than reflection of the underlying social vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Impeccable focus on the target behavior throughout, with logical progression from values to plan to rehearsal to confidence-building; no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine cooperation in role-plays and choice-confirmations, but the counselor frequently hands down solution packages rather than co-creating them with Jamal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jamal exercised real choice, rejecting ideas the counselor accepted gracefully; however, choices were always within counselor-constructed menus, subtly constraining ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The scaling question and confidence-building elicited real change, moving Jamal from 5 to 6.5; but the dominant pattern was supplying scripts and strategies rather than evoking Jamal's own ideas, especially at his most vulnerable moment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jamal's input was solicited at junctures, but the overall expert-driven stance with repeated pre-fabricated option lists meant expertise resided more with the counselor than with Jamal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation of desire, confidence scaling, and mobilizing small steps, with Jamal generating his own plan and insight; however, the counselor often supplied strategies and never consolidated commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed without amplification, with doubts answered affirmatively; but no double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk were used.
