# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with good validation ('22 days is a real achievement', 'that's understandable and unfair'). Falls short of 5 due to somewhat generic warmth and limited explicit affirmation of Timur as a person beyond his behaviors.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening capturing both surface and deeper concerns ('mates earning big money, your record... feel like a locked door'). Reflections occasionally gloss over emotional depth, such as the shame of 'I'm lazy' and fear of being written off.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent sustained focus on staying crime/drug-free and building pro-social structure. Every turn ties back to the target: high-risk times identified, concrete counter-activities arranged, employment barrier addressed, and coping skills rehearsed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction largely co-constructed with options as a menu and role-play framed as joint rehearsal. The call script was largely pre-authored by the psychiatrist rather than drafted from Timur's own language, limiting full power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice regularly affirmed ('Which of these feels most doable now?') and Timur's 'not promising anything' accepted without pressure. The psychiatrist sometimes proposes rather than elicits, especially regarding the script content.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The pivotal question about what a life he'd accept would look like drew out Timur's own reasons for change (Fiona, pride, freedom). However, much of the session involved supplying content—strategies, scripts, reworded lines—rather than drawing from Timur, with no confidence scaling or self-efficacy exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Timur's expertise consistently treated as central, with his choices (gym, Thursday call) genuinely shaping the session. A slightly directive thread remained in scripting the call before exploring what Timur would want to say himself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk, eliciting Timur's strongest commitment language about Fiona and rehearsing readiness to 'prove it'. A 5 would require more systematic evocation such as importance/confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was neither reinforced nor actively softened—'I'm lazy' and 'I just won't do it' were met with quick pivots to planning rather than reflection and reframing. The 'people like me' exclusion belief was validated but not strategically reframed toward change.
