# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with well-timed validations of Irina's struggles. Affirmations were somewhat concentrated in transition moments and less focused on her worth independent of problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections consistently captured and extended Irina's meaning, from reframing drinking as exhaustion to the double-sided Marta ambivalence. Irina repeatedly built on the reflections, showing they landed accurately.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus on the target behavior while remaining patient-centered, gently redirecting deflections by linking drinking to her stated concern. The session advanced purposefully to a specific three-part plan without ever feeling coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine dialogue between equals: the agenda was deferred to Irina, every intervention built on her contributions, and all concrete solutions originated from her. The clinician's role was to reflect and evoke, not direct.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively supported from the opening question to framing the one-drink test as 'information, not pass or fail.' No advice or pressure was offered, and choices were consistently framed as hers to test and evaluate.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within Irina, with every significant insight and the entire action plan originating from her. Questions were precisely calibrated to elicit and develop self-motivational statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Irina was explicitly positioned as the expert on her own life throughout. Her own plan was credited to her, and her contributions visibly shaped the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions tied to Irina's values evoked sustained, self-generated change talk, including a concrete plan. Change talk was consistently reflected back and deepened by exploring which parts felt solid vs. shaky.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed effectively, including a double-sided reflection for the Marta ambivalence. However, summaries occasionally restated status quo language rather than minimally engaging with it.
