# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with normalization of worries and appreciative affirmations. Affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic, lacking deeper validation of Marek's social identity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but often shallow, and the counselor repeatedly rushed to advice rather than exploring ambivalence. Missed opportunities to reflect the fear underlying statements like 'maybe if I got sick.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on alcohol use and progressed logically to a concrete behavioral experiment with follow-up. Sometimes rushed ahead of Marek's readiness, pivoting to action planning before resolving ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely egalitarian: permission-seeking, offered choices, and Marek's input genuinely shaped the plan. The counselor still dominated option generation with pre-packaged menus rather than joint brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Marek's ownership: 'no pressure to promise,' 'running this experiment your way,' and Marek himself cited 'my call, my rules' as why he'd try. A clear strength of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited genuine client change talk via the importance ruler and open questions, but shifted toward supplying advice and tips once planning began. The 'why' of change was never deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Marek as expert and decision-maker, with his contributions clearly shaping the plan. Fell short of 5 due to counselor-generated option menus limiting genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evoked change talk (e.g., 'something I gotta think about,' self-proposed alcohol-free Fridays) but never capitalized with elaboration questions or reflections to strengthen it. Importance rating of 2-3 was never developed further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, framing the plan as Marek's own experiment without arguing. Lost points for asking directly about 'benefits' of drinking early on and repeating minimizing language without double-sided reflections.
