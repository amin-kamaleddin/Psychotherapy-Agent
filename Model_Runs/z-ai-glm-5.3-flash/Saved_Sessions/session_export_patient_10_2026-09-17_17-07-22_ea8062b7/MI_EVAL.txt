# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, non-judgmental affirmations throughout, but they are brief and formulaic ('You're doing great') with limited individualized affirmation of Amina's resilience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections of Amina's frustration and worry, but reflections are sparse and the counselor moves quickly to problem-solving rather than deeply exploring emotional themes like feeling like a burden.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on securing reliable childcare to enable interviews; every exchange ties back to the target behavior with clear next steps and zero drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely egalitarian with permission-seeking and client-shaped planning, but the counselor repeatedly hands over fully-formed scripts, checklists, and sitter lists rather than jointly generating them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent menu-based choices let Amina shape the session, though providing ready-made scripts and checklists subtly reduces her authorship of solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation is elicited (confidence rating, barrier analysis), but the counselor relies heavily on supplying content; Amina's best motivational material emerges largely unprompted.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina is positioned as decision-maker at times, but the counselor frequently defaults to an expert advice-delivery role with limited client input on solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A strong evocative question elicits confidence and commitment, but the counselor misses opportunities to deepen commitment language like 'It's my turn now' rather than moving to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced; the counselor briefly validates fears then redirects to practical solutions, though without strategic reframing or double-sided reflections.
