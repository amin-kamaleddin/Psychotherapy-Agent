# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental with frequent affirmations; however, affirmations were somewhat generic and slips were not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening accurately captured ambivalence, but reflections stayed at summary level and never explicitly validated the shame and harsh self-judgment about morning-after feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on reducing alcohol use from opening question through concrete, measurable planning with a scheduled follow-up. Every turn advanced the target behavior coherently.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed plan with inclusive language and shared choices, but the counselor often proposed options in batches rather than eliciting the client's own ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was repeatedly emphasized and the client's goal was accepted without pressure, though clustered suggestions sometimes subtly steered and ownership of successes/setbacks could be affirmed more explicitly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and doability questions elicited strong client-owned motivation, but key moments (e.g., 'proving to myself') went undeveloped and the counselor supplied the self-compassion phrase rather than evoking one.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client input was invited, but many concrete plan elements (tea, alarm, breathing exercise, rewards) originated from the counselor, making power-sharing only moderate.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance/confidence rulers and targeted questions elicited strong change talk, but client change statements were rarely deepened with reinforcing reflections and confidence probing was repetitive.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I've earned it,' 'I like having it there') was skipped over rather than softened with double-sided reflections; ambivalence was redirected into logistics more than resolved.
