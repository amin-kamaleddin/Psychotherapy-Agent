# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, nonjudgmental regard with individualized, genuine affirmations. Vulnerability and past failed attempts are validated without any trace of judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture both content and emotional nuance, landing on deeper meaning (e.g., the pre/post exercise experience gap, the unbidden decision at the door). Reflective listening is highly accurate rather than parroting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering, purposeful focus on returning to exercise, moving from vague ambivalence to a concrete plan with relapse prevention and follow-up structure. Strategic direction is maintained without steering away from client autonomy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions fully between equals, with the check-in format and follow-up focus co-constructed. All key plan elements emerged from the client in response to open, facilitative prompts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces the client's ownership ('this is your plan, and you get to decide what support looks like'). No advice is imposed, and the client's agency is explicitly celebrated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from within the client, never argued for or supplied. Successive evocative questions elicit self-generated reasons, confidence, and high-quality commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is explicitly positioned as the expert, and his contributions shape the entire plan. Every intervention builds on what the client has offered, exemplifying power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked (exception questions, anticipated commitment to his wife) and amplified through reflection and affirmation. Strong commitment and preparation language results.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is largely redirected toward exceptions and agency, though it is occasionally engaged directly (e.g., elaborating 'driveway debates' and hard 5:30 moments). This serves a self-monitoring purpose but slightly extends focus on the problem side.
