# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment or disapproval. Affirmations like 'That's a real shift, and it's yours' reinforced Aisha's self-worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both pain and underlying motivation. Consistently moved beyond content to meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, purposeful focus on starting exercise with no drift. Each response built progressively from motivation to concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative, with the plan co-constructed from Aisha's expertise. The counselor never assumed an expert role and integrated her suggestions immediately.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed choices as Aisha's own and never prescribed advice. However, explicit affirmations of her ownership of outcomes were somewhat limited given her fear of flaking.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key plan element came from Aisha's own words, drawn out through systematic open questions. Her closing commitment language demonstrated deeply evoked motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly credited Aisha's expertise ('That's your expertise talking') and let her contributions shape the entire plan. The counselor built on her ideas rather than redirecting them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire, commitment, and activation language ('I want that version of me back,' 'texting Maya tonight'). Reflected Aisha's change talk back to reinforce momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk briefly without amplifying, pivoting toward realistic solutions. However, missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframing of the 'why bother' voice.
