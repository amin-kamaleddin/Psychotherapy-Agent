# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, thanking Sofia for honesty and normalizing her hesitation. Warmth was somewhat sparse and transactional, with quick moves to checklists and scripts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections, but emotionally meaningful disclosures (guilt about money, fear of failure) went unreflected in favor of practical tips. The emotional core of her ambivalence—family expectations, guilt—was largely unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed anchored to cannabis use and built toward a concrete next step using Sofia's chosen money focus. Points deducted for a mid-session drift into broad psychoeducation and a long assessment battery untied to her goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission, offered choices, and let Sofia pick scripts and tones, but supplied nearly all content herself with Sofia as approver rather than co-creator. Sofia began co-constructing her plan late in the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered menus, asked permission, and accepted Sofia's incremental pacing without pushing. Choice was often framed as picking among counselor-generated options rather than Sofia authoring her own path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was largely supplied by the counselor via information and advice rather than evoked from Sofia. The importance ruler answer of 2–3 was never explored, and her own reasons (money, guilt toward parents) were not drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's input did shape the session, especially the money focus, but the counselor frequently took the expert role with long psychoeducation lists and pre-made plans. Explicit acknowledgment of Sofia's expertise in her own life was absent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk was elicited via the importance ruler and Sofia's own tentative commitments. However, these statements were never deepened with elaboration questions, confidence rulers, or reinforcing reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's minimization and coping rationale were mostly met with neutral acknowledgment or data rather than strategic softening. No double-sided reflections pairing her sustain talk with her own change talk (e.g., money guilt vs. relaxation benefits).
