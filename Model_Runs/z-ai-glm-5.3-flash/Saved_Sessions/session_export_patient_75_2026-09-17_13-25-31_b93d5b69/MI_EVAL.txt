# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a neutral, respectful tone and never judged Linda, but warmth was limited — few affirmations (only one explicit validation) and responses were largely instructional rather than individually affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal — Linda's ambivalence ('stuck between worrying about the inhaler and worrying without it'), fear of dependence, and insightful self-observations were met with information and tips rather than exploration or reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on asthma management and appointment preparation with a logical progression to a concrete plan, though achieved through agenda-driven delivery rather than collaborative goal-focusing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Despite superficial check-ins, the session was dominated by counselor-directed advice, scripts, and checklists; Linda's ideas rarely shaped the direction and joint problem-solving was never invited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some choices ('Want this shortened or personalized?') but much of the interaction was prescriptive, and Linda's self-directed steps (starting rinsing/spacer tonight) were never explicitly affirmed as her own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor relied almost entirely on providing information rather than drawing out Linda's own motivations and solutions; no open questions to evoke motivation and Linda's insights were never deepened or built upon.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed the expert role, delivering facts, tips, and scripts in rapid succession, and rarely tapped Linda's own expertise about what had worked or what mattered to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged spontaneously from Linda rather than being deliberately evoked — no importance/confidence rulers, no questions about her reasons, and her commitment opportunity ('When should I call?') was answered with facts instead of reinforcement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk, but responded to fears about side effects and dependence with corrective information rather than MI-consistent softening — no double-sided reflections or pivots back to Linda's reasons for change.
