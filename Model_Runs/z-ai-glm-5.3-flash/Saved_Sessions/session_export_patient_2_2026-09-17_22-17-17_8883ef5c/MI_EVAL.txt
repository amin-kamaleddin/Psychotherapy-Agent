# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained even during client deflection and minimization; potential relapse normalized ('Slipping isn't stopping' is met with acceptance, not judgment).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing the deeper meaning of the evening cigarette as sacred personal time and skillfully holding the client's ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong focus on evening smoking reduction with a concrete client-generated plan; minor drift from a broad opening question, and alcohol from the patient profile was never directly revisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan emerged entirely from the client's own ideas (tea, sister accountability, calling tonight); the counselor asks rather than tells, culminating in a mutual 'Deal' rather than a prescribed treatment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy actively and repeatedly reinforced — 'you get to decide this, fully'; even her 5/10 readiness is honored without any pressure or imposed advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within via evocative and projective questions, eliciting her own reasons and strategies; the client's closing remark about leaving with a plan she made herself is direct evidence of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within the client, whose contributions genuinely shaped the entire plan and session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked through the projective future question and open prompts, drawing strong commitment language ('I'll call her tonight... No point waiting') that was then amplified by reflecting her agency back to her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged but never argued with; dual reflections ('Both things can be true') and honoring her 5/10 readiness allowed ambivalence to soften naturally without reinforcement.
