# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard; no judgment even when Margaret firmly rejected abstinence or minimized her drinking. Validated her irritation and accepted her stance without pushing change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective summaries captured content and ambivalence well, but reflections occasionally felt formulaic ('I hear you'). Missed opportunities to reflect deeper emotions such as fear of falling or pride in resilience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol-related fall risk and cutting back, progressing logically toward a concrete plan. Kept momentum on the target behavior throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with co-construction from Margaret's routines, but the counselor periodically shifted into expert mode with dense tactic lists, slightly unbalancing the equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed choice via menus of options and accepted her refusal to quit without pressure. Margaret ended the session with clear ownership of her plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within via confidence rulers, coping ideas, and values linkage. Relied somewhat on delivering information rather than consistently evoking her own reasons and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Margaret as the expert, and her input demonstrably shaped the plan. Dense expert information blocks prevented a full 5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked commitment and confidence language through scaling and obstacle planning, with confidence self-raised from 6 to 7.5. Closing commitment statement was directly cultivated by these techniques.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged minimization and refusal to quit neutrally without amplifying sustain talk. Could have more deliberately developed discrepancy using the fall or son relationship as anchors.
