### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with client boundaries respected, but warmth occasionally feels formulaic and lacks explicit affirmation of Amira's courage in personal terms.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Amira's core concerns and validate her worries about consistency and the transition. However, reflections stay brief and don't deepen emotional content like the salsa jar discouragement or body confidence.

### Direction

**Comments:** Unwavering focus on establishing a strength routine, with every exchange tied to the plan while honoring Amira's boundary to keep nutrition off the table. Closes with a concrete, specific commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan is genuinely co-constructed, with Amira's answers visibly shaping session direction. Some moments are more directive, offering counselor-generated options rather than generating ideas together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support through choice architecture, honoring her reporting preference, and ending with an open invitation. Deducted for repeatedly supplying advice rather than eliciting Amira's own solutions, and checklist-like confidence ratings.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira articulates her own motivations, but evocation is mostly confined to repeated confidence scaling (asked three times) while the counselor supplies many ideas. Deeper exploration of values, hope, or 'why now' never occurs.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers menus and lets Amira's contributions substantially shape the plan. Some advice is delivered expert-style before checking whether she wants suggestions, diluting full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira offers abundant spontaneous change talk, but the counselor relies on confidence scaling rather than evoking or deepening it. Missed opportunities include exploring 'why now' and elaborating on the envisioned payoff.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about overwhelm, snoozing, and quitting is acknowledged without reinforcement, and fallback options normalize imperfection. Deducted because solutions were suggested rather than elicited, and the snooze barrier was problem-solved for rather than with her.
