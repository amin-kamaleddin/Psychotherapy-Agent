# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a neutral, nonjudgmental stance and avoids lecturing despite Tyler's testing, but offers no explicit affirmations, validation of strengths, or normalization of his struggle. Acceptance is present but shallow.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some accurate reflections early and later about feeling forced, invaded, and pre-judged, but reflections are brief and quickly displaced by scripts and option menus. Deeper emotional content about feeling written off goes unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session focus drifts entirely to mother-conflict logistics (scripts, timers, texts) and never links back to the substance use referral concern. By the end Tyler is planning better hiding spots with no counter-steer from the counselor.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers menus, invites Tyler to tweak wording, and role-plays at his pace, accepting his edits without defensiveness. However, collaboration is largely accept-or-reject of counselor-proposed solutions rather than genuine co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, incorporates Tyler's edits, and avoids pressuring him about substance use, giving him meaningful control over session content. Somewhat undercut by the rapid-fire advice format and lack of affirmation of Tyler's ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly every response supplies content (scripts, tips, plans) rather than drawing out Tyler's own reasons, values, or concerns. Tyler's emerging reflective moments are met with the next script instead of curiosity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks Tyler what he wants to focus on and his choices shape the content, but the interaction is often menu-driven advice delivery with expertise implicitly positioned with the counselor. Partnership is present but lukewarm.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No effort to evoke Tyler's own language about change regarding substance use; Tyler's implicit reflection about being more careful is left unexplored. No open questions about how use fits into his life or what he values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I'm not addicted,' 'it's just weed') is essentially ignored as the counselor moves to logistical scripts. No double-sided reflections or reframes tied to Tyler's values; the session implicitly validates a frame where the mother's reaction is the only problem.
