# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; reframed 'depressing' insight as 'clarity' and normalized failed timer attempts without any labeling or pressure, even when the client repeatedly defended scrolling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was deep and accurate, including the double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence and naming the 'nothing-required quality of scrolling' as the heart of the struggle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on phone use and its costs (sleep, grades) while building toward a concrete plan; each turn advanced the work without feeling pushy, closing with specific commitments and next-step structure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing: the plan emerged entirely from Amina's ideas, and her surprise at not having to 'argue the whole hour' is strong evidence of successful collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice consistently reinforced — explicitly declined to judge, framed reduction as optional, handed the next-session agenda to her, and affirmed the plan was 'your ideas, not mine.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from within; every key insight (connection-lite, 2am scrolling causing missed lectures) originated from Amina, making this a textbook demonstration of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly located expertise in the client and turned her direct request for suggestions back to her, which her subsequent idea-generation validated and shaped the session's outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and reinforced, escalating from vague concern ('blocking two specific things I want') to concrete commitments ('I actually sort of want to try it now').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without reinforcement and redirected via reframing, but some responses simply accepted it without double-sided reflections that could have resolved ambivalence more efficiently.
