# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains basic respect and never labels or shames the client, but the opening is misattuned to a mandated referral, and affirmations are formulaic rather than tailored to Dylan's specific positives (first-time record, cooperating with the RA).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers consistent but surface-level summaries that mirror content without touching deeper emotion. Misses key opportunities to reflect the fear and pride beneath Dylan's internship concerns and his vulnerability in admitting he was 'pretty drunk.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifts entirely toward procedural/PR management of the conduct meeting rather than the drinking behavior itself. The clinical purpose of the referral — addressing underage drinking — is never pursued.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and genuinely incorporates Dylan's edits into the statement, but power-sharing is uneven — the counselor supplies statement content largely unilaterally, and proposals are initially poorly calibrated ('None of those, really').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Frequently offers choice and respects Dylan's refusal of counseling or AA. However, autonomy support applies mainly to meeting logistics, not the change process, and is undercut by offering attorney-like strategies before being asked.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies on information-gathering and option menus rather than evoking the client's own motivations. Dylan's stated values (internships, hard work) offer a natural hook that is never drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Follows Dylan's lead and incorporates his input into the statement revision, but occupies the expert role by providing pre-packaged strategies rather than drawing on Dylan's own knowledge of what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes few deliberate efforts to evoke change talk; questions elicit outcome preferences rather than motivation around drinking. Dylan's spontaneous admission of heavy drinking goes unreflected and undeepened, and the values-drinking discrepancy is never explored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Repeatedly mirrors sustain talk in reflections, including echoing 'partying's part of college,' which risks amplifying rationalizations. No strategic double-sided reflections or pivots toward change are used.
