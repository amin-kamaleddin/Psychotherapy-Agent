# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, affirming Sofia's choices and normalizing her frustration. Did not fully validate her irritation at the repeated safety question.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on, but turns were often long option lists rather than deep reflections. Sofia's fear of rejection and hurt beneath her dismissiveness went largely unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on a concrete target (repairing friendship via a text, managing school avoidance), ending with a specific next step and follow-up. Some drift from repeated safety check-ins and option lists.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some co-selection and plan-checking, but the counselor dominated with menus of options, drafted scripts, and set the follow-up agenda. Sofia's ideas were incorporated into counselor-supplied frameworks rather than driving the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy language throughout ('optional,' 'no pressure') with Sofia controlling wording, timing, and session frequency. Pre-packaged solutions slightly limited her ownership of generating options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation (her coping idea, commitment to text) but much of the time was spent offering solutions and scripts rather than drawing out Sofia's own reasons, hopes, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Periodically acknowledged Sofia's agency and let her pick message and timing, but assumed an expert advice-giving role with scripted openers and unrequested backup plans; multi-question turns dominated the structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and consolidated change talk — Sofia generated her plan, chose wording, and committed to a concrete step with follow-up. Could have deepened commitment by exploring what success would mean to her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and occasionally reframed it, but few deliberate double-sided reflections or strategic pivots; ambivalence was mostly sidestepped rather than used to build discrepancy.
