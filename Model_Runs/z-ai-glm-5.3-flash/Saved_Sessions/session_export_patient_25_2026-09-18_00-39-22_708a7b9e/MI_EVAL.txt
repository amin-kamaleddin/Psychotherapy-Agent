# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, unconditional positive regard with frequent, genuine affirmations tied to Daniel's strengths. No judgment about his drinking; ambivalence was normalized and validated throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate, including complex double-sided reflections and reflections of deeper meaning. The patient's elaborations and epiphanies confirmed he felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session kept a clear, steady focus on alcohol use in social settings, with each exchange advancing the target behavior. It culminated in a concrete commitment (Saturday's poker night) while remaining patient-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was fully co-constructed through dialogue, with the psychiatrist never lecturing or prescribing. Summaries explicitly credited Daniel as the author of his own plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Daniel's ownership and choice, framing change as 'on your own terms' and leaving all strategies to him. Relapse was framed non-punitively.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from Daniel's own experiences and strengths, connecting his smoking-cessation success to the current change. The payoff was visible: he moved from ambivalence to self-authored motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniel's ideas (three-drink limit, cooler strategy, telling Sarah, exit line) formed the entire plan. The psychiatrist explicitly credited his expertise and incorporated his own words into reflections.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled open questions evoked desire, ability, and commitment language, which the psychiatrist then amplified and consolidated into a concrete plan. Commitment was anchored to a specific next step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged but not reinforced, handled with double-sided reflections and pivots toward change. Minor deduction: sustain talk was occasionally mirrored a bit directly, though always within balanced reflections.
