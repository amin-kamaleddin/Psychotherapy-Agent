# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently affirmed Samir's autonomy and expertise, repeatedly positioning him as the one who 'knows your days better than anyone.' Samir explicitly noted feeling unscolded and hopeful for the first time.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflected both content and emotion, including the chest tightness fear and ambivalence about quitting. Reflections like 'I hear both sides' validated his ambivalence without judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stayed focused on reducing smoking and drinking throughout, with each exchange building toward a concrete, self-generated plan. No drift into unrelated topics occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was entirely patient-generated ('that's mine, from my own life'), with the counselor offering open questions and letting Samir set the pace and scope of change. Samir explicitly contrasted this with feeling lectured in the past.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Samir chose the glovebox approach, the substitutes, the timeline, and the follow-up measures himself, with the counselor deferring to his judgment at every turn. His ownership of the plan was a direct product of this support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor drew out Samir's own motivations—his daughter's nagging, his son's disappointed look, the nighttime chest tightness—rather than imposing external reasons. Samir articulated these values in his own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open-ended questions ('what feels possible,' 'what would tell you it's working') invited Samir to lead at each stage, and the counselor's reflections built on his responses. The tone was consistently side-by-side rather than expert-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions elicited strong commitment language: 'I could probably live with that,' 'I'll try the glovebox thing this week,' and 'I'll tell you straight.' Samir also expressed self-efficacy by recalling his past success halving his smoking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Samir's sustain talk ('quitting feels like a mountain,' 'no promises') was reflected neutrally without argument, and the counselor paired it with his change talk ('I hear both sides'). This non-confrontational stance allowed sustain talk to soften naturally over the session.
