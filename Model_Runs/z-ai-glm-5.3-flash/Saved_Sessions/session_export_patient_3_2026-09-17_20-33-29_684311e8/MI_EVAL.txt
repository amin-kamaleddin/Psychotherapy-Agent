# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental regard with strong affirmations ('that's the voice of someone who's genuinely ready') and normalization of the client's fears and self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening capturing both content and emotion, including precise double-sided reflections and deep reframes like 'coming home to yourself.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior, with a logical progression from motivation to a concrete, committed plan (tonight's walk, three walks, accountability, follow-up agenda).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Equal-partner dynamic throughout, co-constructing the plan and follow-up agenda, with the client's ideas serving as the engine of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership and choice; all decisions originated from the client, who explicitly noted the contrast with being told what to do.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation, especially the 'what would you say to your sister' intervention that drew out self-generated insight and self-compassion ('I guess I do know the words').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert on her life, elevating her contributions rather than substituting professional prescriptions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk with strategic questions ('What will you want to remember?'), reinforcing momentum through affirmations and reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled the client's fear of repeating past failure well with a double-sided reflection, but missed an opportunity to explicitly reframe past quitting as a source of learning.
