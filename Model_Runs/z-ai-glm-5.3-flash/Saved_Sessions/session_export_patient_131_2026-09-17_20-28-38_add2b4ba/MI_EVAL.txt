# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated unconditional positive regard throughout, affirming the client's character and efforts with warmth. No judgment regarding the violence history or drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured emotional nuances like loyalty, pride, and love for his boys. Slightly limited depth — opportunities to explore guilt and fear of relapse more deeply were not taken.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept focus firmly on target behaviors and moved progressively from ambivalence to a concrete plan. Minor deduction as the alcohol reduction goal was not revisited or sharpened with the same specificity as the housing plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative interaction with open, invitational questions throughout. The client's own ideas determined the action plan, demonstrating equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client ownership with questions like 'what would moving on that look like, in your time?' No advice-giving or pressure; the plan was entirely of the client's making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicited motivation from within by exploring what would make the move workable and what support would help. The client articulated his own reasons, showing motivation drawn out rather than supplied.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly located expertise in the client and attributed all progress to the client's own efforts. The client's contributions directly shaped the session's plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and built on change talk, producing rich commitment and plan language from the client. The change talk was consistently reflected and affirmed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed rather than amplified, with effective double-sided reflections and pivots to the client's own insight. Occasionally echoed ambivalent content without immediately redirecting.
