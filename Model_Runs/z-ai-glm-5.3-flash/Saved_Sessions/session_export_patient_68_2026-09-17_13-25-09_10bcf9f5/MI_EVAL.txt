# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental; affirmed Kevin's choices and normalized potential slips. Falls short of top marks due to a somewhat checklist-like warmth and lack of explicit affirmation of Kevin's honesty and courage in attending.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal — the counselor rarely reflected or paraphrased Kevin's disclosures. Key moments of vulnerability and ambivalence (e.g., the 'middle ground' comment, feeling relieved at not being lectured) went unacknowledged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, logical focus on alcohol use throughout, moving smoothly from screening to a concrete plan and follow-up. Direction was achieved through structure and efficiency rather than strategic MI guidance, with little consolidating summarizing of Kevin's own motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin chose options and shaped the plan (safer nights, 'booze boss' text, buddy signal), showing some collaboration. However, the session was dominated by clinician-delivered advice lists and recaps, positioning Kevin as a consumer of solutions rather than a co-creator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was consistently respected: permission-seeking, option presentation, acceptance of Kevin's rejections, and normalizing slips. The heavy volume of prescriptive advice and limited reinforcement of Kevin's self-direction in moments of doubt kept it from a 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was assumed rather than evoked — almost no open-ended evocative questions were asked, and Kevin's own stated motivations (pride, the ankle wake-up call) were passed over. Kevin's engagement emerged despite, not because of, the counselor's technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inclusive framing and genuine incorporation of Kevin's choices demonstrated partnership. Still, the counselor dominated with expert content and provided scripts rather than first eliciting Kevin's own ideas and wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Kevin offered genuine change talk ('the ankle was a wake-up call,' 'I'll actually try') that went largely unreflected and undeepened. No elaborative questions, confidence scaling, or affirmations were used to build on it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor neither reinforced nor debated Kevin's sustain talk ('drinking's how I hang out with my mates'), which is a strength. However, there were no double-sided reflections or values-linked reframing to actively resolve ambivalence — neutral avoidance rather than skillful redirection.
