# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance, validating Sofia's annoyance and framing suggestions nonjudgmentally. However, acceptance was somewhat formulaic, lacking deeper normalization of ambivalence and affirmation of her strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Surface reflections were accurate, but question barrages early on and skipped emotional nuance weakened empathy. Deeper feelings—her lifelong anxiety, fear of social exclusion, and fear of failure—were not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed consistently focused on the target behavior with concrete next steps and no drift. However, action planning was rushed for a contemplative-stage client before consolidating ambivalence and connecting the plan to her values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative moments existed (incorporating the shoe excuse, role-playing at her request), but the counselor often defaulted to expert mode with numbered plans and scripts. Sofia largely reacted to counselor-generated solutions rather than co-constructing them as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was honored through frequent choices and respecting Sofia's pacing ('I'm not promising anything'). Deducted because prescriptive tip lists subtly controlled the process, without explicit reinforcement that ownership rests with Sofia.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was drawn from Sofia (money concern, humor style), but evocation remained superficial. No importance/confidence scaling, and the family-values discrepancy—a prime evocation opportunity—was never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission was asked once and choices offered, but the session was largely advice-heavy with delivered plans and scripts. Sofia's deeper expertise on family and social dynamics was never actively elicited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was successfully evoked and reinforced, with Sofia's money concern anchoring her commitment ('I could try it and see how it goes'). Occasionally the counselor supplied content rather than drawing it from her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified, which is good, but softening was passive. No double-sided reflections or strategic reframes were used, and ambivalence was left unresolved as the counselor moved on to tips.
