# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing avoidance as self-protection and affirming brave moments. However, warmth is occasionally formulaic and the deeper emotional experience of fear is not fully individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections capture avoidance as protection and respond well to her fear and overwhelm. Reflections are somewhat sparse—emotionally significant disclosures like 'That's twice more than I've ever told anyone' went unreflected as the counselor shifted to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior of seeking medical care. Every exchange moves progressively toward the concrete action of making the call, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Options are offered rather than imposed, and Anara's preferences (phone call, 11:00 check-in, tea treat) are incorporated into a co-constructed plan. Early on, the counselor supplied most of the content rather than inviting her own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly offers choices, accepts hesitation without pressure, and never pushes Anara beyond what she agreed to. Abundant concrete scripts and protocols somewhat reduce her sense of authoring her own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evocative questions early on draw out fear and ambivalence, but the counselor then shifts to providing reasons and solutions rather than consistently eliciting Anara's own motivations and coping ideas. No confidence rulers or exploration of her own reasons for change beyond the counselor's framing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** While input is occasionally requested, the session is largely counselor-led, with expert-supplied scripts, exact phrasings, and step-by-step instructions. Anara's contributions shape the plan somewhat, but power-sharing is limited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is effectively evoked and reinforced, with Anara progressing to firm commitments ('Tomorrow, ten o'clock. I'll call.'). Much of the momentum came from the counselor's concrete suggestions rather than deep amplification of her own desire and reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by reframing avoidance as self-protection rather than arguing against it, and the status quo is never reinforced. Statements like 'I'm not promising anything' were not explicitly acknowledged, staying instead on the action plan.
