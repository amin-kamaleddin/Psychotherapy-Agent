# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Basic respect and thanks are present, but the stance is largely corrective and informational with few genuine, individualized affirmations of Ahmed's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal and formulaic (repeated 'I hear you' openers) with brief acknowledgments immediately followed by dense informational blocks; the emotional meaning of Ahmed's minimization and family dynamics is never explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, persistent focus on tobacco-related oral symptoms with a concrete plan and follow-up, though direction was rigidly prescriptive rather than shaped collaboratively from Ahmed's own values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session is largely counselor-led with assessment, correction, and a pre-formatted plan; collaboration appears only late when Ahmed negotiates terms, and the plan structure was counselor-defined rather than co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some choice is offered (printable vs. message, cutback vs. referral), but the overall posture is directive, with recommendations and instructions delivered before eliciting Ahmed's preferences; meaningful autonomy support came only after his pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no open evocative questions or importance/confidence exploration; Ahmed's motivations surfaced spontaneously or from external pressure rather than from counselor skill.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes an expert role with unsolicited questions, risk facts, and prescriptive advice; Ahmed's own ideas are accommodated only when he insists, with no acknowledgment that he knows his habits best.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ahmed generated meaningful change talk ('maybe I could cut back a bit,' 'I'll give it an honest shot'), but this arose from his wife's pressure and the plan structure rather than skillful evocation; opportunities to amplify desire statements were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but it is met with factual rebuttals inviting argument rather than double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance; Ahmed's core claim ('we're looking at the dust, not the cigars') remains unresolved.
