# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental support throughout — affirmations like 'It counts to me that you went' and validating Jordan's self-protective doubt as understandable. No labeling, disapproval, or pressure anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate complex reflections (e.g., 'hope peeking through' and the double-sided doubt/effort reflection). Falls short of 5 because the emotional depth of Jordan's hopelessness is touched but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive arc from hopelessness to a concrete plan — persistence, reachable step, plan details, barriers, self-talk, memory aids. No topic drift while ownership stays with the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully equal-footed interaction with inviting language ('I'm curious,' 'I'm wondering') and plans co-constructed from Jordan's own contributions. The clinician's role was limited to reflecting, affirming, and asking.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Jordan's ownership: 'I'm not asking you to promise anything will come of it' and explicit credit ('it came from you'). No pressure, persuasion, or credit-taking.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Jordan's own reasons for change rather than imposing them, with questions systematically developing his desire to feel 'lighter.' His shift from 'nothing works out' to 'the part of me that's tired of feeling stuck is louder today' shows successfully evoked change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Jordan as the expert ('You're the expert on your own life'), and every plan element originated from Jordan. Client contributions substantially shaped the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation with questions designed to elicit Jordan's own change language, producing escalating commitment ('I could knock it out this week'). Each piece of change talk is then reflected and reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids amplifying sustain talk using double-sided reflections and explicitly declining demands for optimism. Some reflections restate sustain talk fairly prominently before pivoting, slightly elevating rather than softening it.
