# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with no labeling of the client's drinking or defensiveness. However, warmth feels utilitarian and coach-like, with few genuine personal affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections are decent but surface-level and increasingly replaced by task-focused coaching. Emotionally rich openings, such as fear of a record affecting internships, receive logistical handling rather than emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused with no aimless wandering. However, the focus shifts almost entirely to hearing logistics rather than the client's relationship with alcohol.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements exist, including role-play and accepting the client's wording edits. But the counselor supplies nearly all content while the client merely approves or tweaks it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and honors the client's edits to the statement. Autonomy is undermined by heavy prescribing of exact wording and advice, with choices confined to the counselor's agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely draws motivation from within the client, offering no open questions exploring his own values or concerns about alcohol. The one role-play panel question is immediately answered for him with a script.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers menus of options early, supporting some power sharing. The counselor then repeatedly takes the expert role, supplying scripted statements and rewriting the client's words with minimal exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal deliberate evocation of the client's own change language; most change-relevant statements are scripted by the counselor and adopted by Jason. Change talk is manufactured for impression management rather than genuine motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk ('everyone drinks', 'the underage thing fixes itself') is ignored rather than softened with double-sided reflections or discrepancy work. The counselor implicitly reinforces it by helping craft language to minimize the incident.
