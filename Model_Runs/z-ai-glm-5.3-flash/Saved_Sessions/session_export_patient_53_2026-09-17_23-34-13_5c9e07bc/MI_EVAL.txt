# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, framing avoidance patterns as self-protection rather than flaws. No judgment or disapproval present, even when the client describes sabotaging behaviors.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, complex reflections capture both sides of ambivalence and the client's deeper emotional experience (e.g., 'good-scary'). Reflections land well and progressively deepen disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains steady focus on the target behavior, guiding step-by-step from vague openness to a concrete, time-bound plan with built-in accountability. Forward movement without pushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every element of the plan is co-constructed from the client's own ideas; the counselor only reflects and builds. Genuine power-sharing demonstrated by the client-generated accountability plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly releases the client from promises and honors her 'just the looking part' boundary without pushing. Frames the client's self-knowledge and choice as central to the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation and plan elements emerge from the client's own words. The culminating question about 'walking in with a name to share' elicits powerful intrinsic motivation tied to self-worth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise in the client ('You'd know better than anyone'), and the client's contributions shape the entire session. The client recognizes her own agency in the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and progressively strengthens change talk, from vague 'maybe' to concrete commitment ('tonight, before the couch'). Deepens motivation by exploring meaning and self-image.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk briefly and neutrally before pivoting to change-focused questions. Never reinforces avoidance; client language shifts steadily toward change.
