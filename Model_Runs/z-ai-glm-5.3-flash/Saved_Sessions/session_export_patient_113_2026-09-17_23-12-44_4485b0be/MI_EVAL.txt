# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with no judgment or disapproval. Client's own feedback ('you didn't lecture me') confirms acceptance was felt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were highly accurate, using the client's own words and progressively deepening understanding, including emotional layers beneath logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unwavering focus on the target behavior across all turns, ending with a specific, observable commitment plus a contingency plan for rough nights.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine two-way exchange; every plan element originated from the client and the counselor invited his expertise throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed change as the client's choice with genuine options and zero pressure. The client's response shows the autonomy-supportive stance was both felt and clinically impactful.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice-giving; the counselor drew out the client's own reasons, solutions, and confidence, evidenced by his shift from refusal to 'I'll try it.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned expertise within the client, with all plan elements originating from him. Client's feedback confirms power-sharing was experienced, not just performed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions elicited a clear arc of commitment language from 'I dunno' to 'one plug tonight. I'll try it,' with change talk consistently reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with or reinforced, and it visibly diminished across the session. However, some sustain statements were allowed to fully stand before redirection rather than being softened in the moment.
