# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates warm, nonjudgmental regard, affirming Amira's self-knowledge and reframing past failures as learning. No judgment or disapproval appears anywhere in the session, even when she describes past setbacks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and consistent, capturing both content and emotional nuance (e.g., 'There's a steadiness in your voice when you say you're ready'). Reflections added meaning rather than parroting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity, progressing logically from importance to specifics, barriers, and backup plans. It converged on a concrete, actionable commitment (Monday morning walk, scheduled).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session unfolds as a genuine dialogue between equals, with Amira's answers directly steering the evolving plan. The accountability discussion emerged collaboratively and receptively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor never prescribes; Amira generated every element of the plan, and her choice to defer nutrition was honored. Her ownership is explicitly reinforced, e.g., affirming that 'it has to feel like mine.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently elicited client-generated motivation and amplified her sense of capability. It falls short of exemplary only because commitment language could have been deepened and high baseline motivation left evocation less tested.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveyed that expertise resides within the client, with every question building on Amira's prior contributions. The expert role was never assumed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was evoked effectively through open questions about values and readiness, producing strong client statements ('I'm honestly ready for this one'). However, evocation relied partly on Amira's naturally high motivation, with less explicit exploration of confidence or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and skillfully converted into problem-solving (e.g., exploring what the 'little voice' was worried about). More deliberate strength-based reframing of the all-or-nothing pattern would elevate this further.
