# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of Rosa's honesty and normalization of ambivalence. Affirmations were somewhat generic and could be more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, layered reflections capturing both the emotional and identity dimensions of Rosa's ambivalence. Rosa repeatedly confirmed feeling understood ('Yeah, exactly').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on smoking cessation throughout, moving from ambivalence to a concrete, time-bound Thursday plan with supports. No drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian interaction; Rosa's ideas drove the plan and the counselor offered information only when explicitly requested. Consistently invited figuring things out together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Rosa's ownership ('define on your own terms, truthfully either way') with no pressure to commit beyond her 'maybe.' Rosa set her own pace and chose her own supports.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within Rosa — her niece's baby, her own harm-reduction reasoning, and identity-level change talk — via open questions. The counselor never argued for change or supplied Rosa's reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Rosa as the expert, eliciting her ideas before offering input. The specific plan came entirely from her, with power returned after any psychoeducation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully and consistently evoked change talk through questions about small steps, coping, and supports. Rosa's language grew from 'maybe' to a concrete plan and identity-level commitment ('I'm somebody trying something').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally acknowledged fears briefly before pivoting toward change, and Rosa often softened her own sustain talk. A few reflections (e.g., anticipating crankiness) briefly re-amplified sustain talk before redirecting.
