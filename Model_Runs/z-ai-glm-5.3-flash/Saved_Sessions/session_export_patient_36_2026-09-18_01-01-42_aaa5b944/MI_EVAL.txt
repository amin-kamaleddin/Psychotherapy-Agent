### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently affirming, notably praising Elena's honesty and self-discovery. Deep respect for autonomy shown throughout, though explicit affirmation could occasionally be more specific to fully meet level 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reflects Elena's exact words back to her, showing deep understanding. Phrases like 'self-respect being quieter than that' are accurately caught and amplified, helping Elena feel understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, shared focus on protecting one self-respecting evening per week. All questions and reflections serve this focused agenda without drift or distraction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan emerges entirely from Elena's own words and ideas. The therapist positions her as the expert, asking open questions and avoiding any prescriptive suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasizes 'this plan came entirely from you' and 'you're the only one who can answer it.' Actively supports Elena's ownership of the pause, anchor question, and self-compassion strategy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits Elena's own reasons for change, such as 'which choice leaves me feeling like I did on Sunday?' Questions consistently draw out her motivations, values, and personal insights.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conversation flows as a genuine dialogue with shared decision-making, such as collaboratively agreeing on follow-up check-ins. The therapist builds on each of Elena's contributions rather than directing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is abundant and reinforced: 'I want to try that,' 'I can actually picture myself doing that,' and 'I'm kind of looking forward to Wednesday.' The therapist mirrors and deepens each commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk like 'the yes comes out before I even think' is skillfully reframed as opportunity for the pause strategy. Empathy for self-doubt is maintained while gently shifting toward possibility; occasional further exploration of ambivalence could strengthen this.
