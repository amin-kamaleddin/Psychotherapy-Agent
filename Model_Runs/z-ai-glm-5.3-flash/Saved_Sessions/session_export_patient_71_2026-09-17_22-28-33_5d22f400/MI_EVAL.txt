# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment even around lapses and guilt. The counselor normalizes tiredness and models the self-acceptance the client is working toward.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections capture both content and feeling, including the pivotal 'gentle with everyone... just not yourself' reflection the client found profound. Skepticism and self-criticism are reflected without distortion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays purposefully focused on aligning behavior with values, progressing from values clarification to a concrete commitment. Pacing follows the client's leads without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interview proceeds as a genuine exchange between equals, with tentative framings and the plan co-constructed through back-and-forth. The counselor supplies process structure while the client provides all content and decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is reinforced at every decision point ('a stretch, a lunch walk, or something else?'), and the counselor never prescribes. The client designs and owns her plan—'it's small, but it's mine.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation and key insights emerge from the client in response to open, evocative questions. No advice-giving; the closing summary simply mirrors her own words and readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located within the client ('that's your wisdom, not a poster'), and the entire plan originated from her. Her lived experience is affirmed as authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open evocative questions skillfully draw out and amplify change talk, linked to the client's values. The session culminates in strong commitment language ('I'll write the note tonight,' 'that feels like real change').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and reframed as wisdom (planning for tired nights so one skip can't unravel everything). Guilt is transformed into self-compassion, resolving the ambivalence driving her relapse cycle.
