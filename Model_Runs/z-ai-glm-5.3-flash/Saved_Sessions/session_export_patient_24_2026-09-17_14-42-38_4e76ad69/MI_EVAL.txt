# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations ('Smart move') and validation of skepticism, though warmth is somewhat procedural and unconditional regard beyond the plan mechanics is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures minimization and ambivalence, though some reflections are reused verbatim and deeper emotional exploration (the ritual's meaning, athletic identity) is not pursued.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol and its interaction with sleep/recovery/training throughout, with each exchange advancing toward a concrete experiment and a scheduled Friday 10am follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal partnership: the experiment was framed as exploring together, and the patient's preferences shaped every element—test design, tracking method, nights, and check-in time.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary choice emphasis with permission-seeking before sharing information, repeated 'which would you prefer' questions, and acceptance of the patient's refusal of nightly check-ins without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from within, eliciting the patient's own observations and reasons, but is uneven—sometimes supplying solution lists before eliciting ideas and not probing why importance was only 3/10.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the patient as the expert and co-constructs the plan, but stacked multi-question turns and a suggestions list edge toward leading rather than eliciting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation via open questions, importance/confidence rulers, and a self-experiment frame yielding notable change talk ('Maybe it's worth paying attention to'). Could deepen by exploring the ruler responses and amplifying his own discrepancy between marathon goals and drinking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing but tolerated rather than actively softened—no double-sided reflections or reframing of skepticism as fuel for the experiment.
