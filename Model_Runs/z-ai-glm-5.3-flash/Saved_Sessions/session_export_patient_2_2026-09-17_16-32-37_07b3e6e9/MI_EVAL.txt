# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, asking permission and reframing smoking as self-care. However, explicit affirmations of Aisha's strengths and efforts (e.g., protecting her son, rising confidence) were sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening, including an insightful complex reflection on cigarettes as her decompression that shifted her from guarded to cooperative. Reflections were sometimes thin and interspersed with question barrages, missing deeper emotional exploration of her cancer fear and motherhood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on evening smoking reduction with a concrete, time-bound plan emerging. The opening question stack was unfocused and triggered defensiveness, and direction felt pre-scripted rather than following Aisha's pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed from Aisha's own ideas (tea, music, non-class nights, kettle note, quitline). The counselor's structure (rulers, option menus) was imposed rather than negotiated, and the opening ran ahead of her agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently strong autonomy support: choice offered at every juncture, advice prefaced with permission, and Aisha's limits honored without escalation. Her rejection of options (walk, lozenge) was respected, and her three-cigarette boundary was affirmed as her choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew motivation from within via confidence rulers, open questions, and inviting her to design her own plan. Evocation was technique-driven rather than flowing from her story; her core value of motherhood was never explored in depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial power-sharing through permission-seeking, option menus, and inviting Aisha to shape the plan. Counselor turns were dense and agenda-heavy with stacked questions, effectively steering the session rather than renegotiating the agenda when Aisha pushed back.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk — Aisha moved from defensiveness to 'I'll try it,' and her confidence gain from 5 to 6 was highlighted. Change talk was elicited somewhat mechanically via question stacks rather than by deepening her spontaneous change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk and minimization ('I smoke way less than coworkers,' 'not beating myself up') repeatedly passed without strategic response. No double-sided reflections were used to pair her health fears and motherhood motivation against her rationalizations.
