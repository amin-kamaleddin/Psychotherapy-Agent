# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; ambivalence and setbacks normalized without judgment. Repeated genuine affirmations reinforced the client's strengths and self-compassion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate, including complex reflections capturing both meaning and ambivalence (e.g., wine 'promising relaxation but leaving her sluggish'). Each response built directly on the client's previous statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed consistently focused on reducing alcohol use with no topic drift. Each turn advanced from awareness through ambivalence to a concrete plan for tonight.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance with the client's own ideas shaping the entire plan; power balance explicitly acknowledged by the client ('they're my steps, not something someone else handed me'). No prescriptive language used.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change framed as optional throughout, with the client's rejection of the seven-drink target accepted without pressure. Ownership of both the plan and the right to slip was affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from the client rather than supplied; open questions elicited her own reasons, confidence, and commitment ('Tonight, actually. Why wait?'). Textbook evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located within the client ('you know your evenings best'); all plan content originated from the client's own strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and explicitly reinforced, then deepened from desire to commitment and concrete planning (first test evening, coping strategies).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with neutral or double-sided reflections and quickly pivoted toward the client's own reasons for change. Never amplified, argued with, or dwelt upon.
