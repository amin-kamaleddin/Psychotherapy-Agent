# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful regard throughout, with normalization of self-doubt and explicit affirmation of Erik's own wisdom. Never critical or labeling; closing reflection models unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening capturing both emotional and cognitive content (e.g., 'discouragement AND real wisdom'). Erik's repeated 'it does look different' responses confirm feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on target behaviors with each question advancing the agenda from coping strategies to concrete success criteria. No aimless drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal-partner exchange; the counselor asks rather than tells, and every element of the plan emerged from Erik's reasoning and was built upon.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Erik's ownership ('Which of those feels most doable?') and accepts his stated imperfection without pressure. No prescribed solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every turn draws out Erik's own reasons, confidence, and plans. Erik's closing thanks — 'making me see what I've already done' — directly evidences evoked rather than installed motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located in the client, with the note, pause, wife's involvement, and 'no-beating-yourself-up' rule all originating from Erik and credited to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and deepening of change talk via open questions ('What would success look like this week?'). Client-generated commitments like 'I want to try' and 'pausing even half the times is a win'.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and effectively redirected, often via double-sided reflections. Minor point off for occasionally revisiting comparison and diet concerns at length before reframing.
