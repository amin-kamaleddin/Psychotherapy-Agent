# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no judgment despite the mandated context, affirming Ryan repeatedly. The closing normalizes setback ('whichever way the first weekend goes — no judgment'), fully embodying acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are consistently accurate and deepen the conversation, capturing nuances like Ryan's fear of failure and his core friends-vs-family dilemma. Ryan's progressive disclosure ('I actually kind of want to figure this out?') reflects effective empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focus stays firmly on the target behavior with clear momentum from exploring values, to identifying pregaming as the risk point, to a concrete plan and success criteria. There is no topic drift across the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session operates as a genuine partnership using inclusive language ('let's figure it out together'), with ideas co-constructed rather than prescribed. Ryan explicitly notes this felt different from expected lectures.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently located in Ryan ('That wanting came from you, not from me or the school') with no advice-giving or pressure. The closing respects his choice even regarding relapse.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major element — the strategy, the ally, the success criteria — comes from Ryan's own words, drawn out through open, evocative questions. His surprise at his own motivation ('I actually want to try this') marks successfully evoked change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is placed within the client: Ryan generates the pregame-skipping plan and Marcus alliance himself, with ownership explicitly reinforced. This is exemplary power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor strategically highlights and affirms change talk, guiding Ryan from tentative acknowledgment to commitment language ('maybe this pregame thing is the start... it's mine'). The closing summary consolidates the shift.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally not reinforced, with skillful double-sided reflection reframing ambivalence as a solvable goal. Minor deductions for occasionally normalizing the unfairness narrative and limited explicit redirection away from minimizing comparisons.
