# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful tone with no judgment; boundaries around salespeople affirmed. Affirmations were generic rather than personalized to deeper values like body confidence and identity reclaim.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate opening summary and reflection of motivation, but reflections stayed at content level. Missed meaning-level reflections on statements like 'I'm getting back to someone I used to be' and the couch 'danger zone.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on the target behavior with every turn advancing the plan. No topic drift; progressively built a fully specified action plan from exploration to concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely sought preferences and incorporated Leila's ideas. Collaboration diluted by the counselor doing substantial planning labor (backups, scripts, logistics) rather than asking Leila first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed choices as Leila's and affirmed her boundary-setting. Some turns presented leading suggestions and pre-made scripts instead of trusting her to generate her own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opened with values and used scaling questions to evoke Leila's own reasons and readiness. Later turns focused on logistics rather than deepening her motivational core; the identity statement went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited input but undermined by rapid-fire stacked questions and pre-fabricated scripts. Power-sharing was inconsistent, leaning toward counselor-directed planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively linked Leila's statements to goals and elicited strong commitment language through readiness and confidence questions. Evocation was somewhat prompt-heavy, supplying scripts rather than fully drawing them from her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled barriers briefly as problem-solving targets without amplifying them. Obstacle-planning exchanges occupied session space that could have deepened change talk.
