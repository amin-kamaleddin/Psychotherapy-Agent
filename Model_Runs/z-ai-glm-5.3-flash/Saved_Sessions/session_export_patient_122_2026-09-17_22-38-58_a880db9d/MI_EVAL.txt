# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout with specific, individualized affirmations and no judgment. Sofia's self-critical 'lazy person' story is gently questioned rather than reinforced, and the closing warmth feels genuine.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are consistently accurate and deepen to meaning level, e.g., 'pressure itself is what derails you, not willpower.' Sofia repeatedly confirms feeling understood ('you actually listened to the whole picture').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and progresses logically from current state to a specific, time-bound commitment. Every counselor turn advances the arc with no aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine co-construction between equals: the counselor offers ideas tentatively and Sofia actively designs the plan herself. Her closing realization — 'I kind of made my own' — is direct evidence of shared power.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sofia's ownership is explicitly reinforced ('on your terms,' 'whatever happens, this is yours'), and the counselor declines to set outcome expectations. Every plan element originated from the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key insights — pressure derails her, boring is protective, pre-deciding the return — come from Sofia in response to open, curious questions. She ends with her own commitment and protective rule.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor positions Sofia as the expert from the opening line and contributes only questions and reflections, not prescriptions. The plan is entirely Sofia's design.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions systematically evoke change talk, culminating in strong unprompted commitment ('Tonight or tomorrow evening, ten minutes. That's it') that the counselor mirrors and amplifies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
