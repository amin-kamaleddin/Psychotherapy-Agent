# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates consistent respect and warmth, accepting Elena's hesitations and 'not promising anything' without judgment. Affirmations of Elena's own strengths are sparse, keeping this from a higher score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captures Elena's relief and exhaustion, and validates her preferences and pacing. Reflections remain somewhat surface-level, missing opportunities to explore her admitted fear more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session maintains a general thread but drifts into relaxation technique delivery, consuming much of the session without linking back to the contraception focus. Momentum toward the target behavior comes mostly from Elena herself rather than counselor steering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Notably collaborative — checks in before each step, offers menus rather than prescriptions, and adapts to Elena's preferences (text vs. phone script). Occasionally slips into consultation-style information delivery rather than joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: offers menus of choices at every step, honors Elena's refusal to share her location, and explicitly frames next steps as hers ('Just looking is a low-pressure first step'). Elena ends the session feeling clear ownership ('it's in my hands, you know? Literally.').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids lecturing and allows Elena's own motivation to surface naturally, but does little active evocation. No open questions probing Elena's values, hopes, or what a future without scares would look like.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Elena as the decision-maker, and her preferences shape the session direction. Counselor contributions trend toward information delivery, with less acknowledgment that Elena holds expertise about her own life and body.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges largely spontaneously from Elena and the counselor creates conditions for it through low-effort options and a no-pressure script. However, the counselor rarely deepens or reinforces change talk with evocative questions, summaries, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Responds to sustain talk in the moment without arguing, effectively lowering resistance. However, sustain talk is accommodated rather than strategically softened — no double-sided reflections linking her fear to her hesitation.
