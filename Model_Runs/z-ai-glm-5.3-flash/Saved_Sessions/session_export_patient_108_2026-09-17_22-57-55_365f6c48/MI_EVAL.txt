# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor communicates warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, reframing self-criticism ('I'm being a bit of a doormat') into an affirmation of strength. There is no judgment or disapproval, even when Oliver admits to breaking promises to himself.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and consistent, with complex reflections like 'the guilt quietly reschedules it to maybe next week' capturing both content and emotional ambivalence. The counselor also attunes moment-to-moment, reflecting Oliver's surprise at his own progress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a clear focus on the target behavior and guides progressively from ambivalence to a specific date (Friday) with accountability structures. Each turn advances the agenda without ever drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as genuine dialogue between equals, with the counselor building each response on Oliver's answers and co-constructing the plan rather than prescribing it. Oliver explicitly notes the collaborative effect: 'saying it out loud here has made it feel more doable.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly reinforces Oliver's ownership ('that choice is yours,' 'you know best what timing you'd thank yourself for') and never pressures toward a particular choice. Even the shift to Friday is framed as Oliver's own reasoning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major insight and plan element originates from Oliver rather than the counselor, who elicits values and definitions of success through open questions. Motivation is entirely evoked and developed, never argued for or supplied.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Oliver ('you know how you work better than anyone'), and all solutions—the script, the timing, the witnesses—come from him. Oliver's contributions substantially shape the session direction, exemplifying high-level power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully evokes and deepens change talk, eliciting vision talk, prompting a commitment shift from 'end of the month' to 'Friday,' and drawing out accountability planning. Oliver's change talk escalates from desire to commitment to concrete action steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally handled well through double-sided reflections honoring both guilt and the need for space, with effective pivots toward solutions. The score is 4 rather than 5 because deliberate exploration of ambivalence means sustain talk occupies noticeable session space rather than being consistently minimized.
