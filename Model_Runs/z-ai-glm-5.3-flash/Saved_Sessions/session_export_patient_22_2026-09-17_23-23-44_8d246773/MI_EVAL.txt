# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed warmth and unconditional positive regard, with specific, genuine affirmations and normalization of ambivalence and fears. Camila's minimization and self-doubt were met with respect rather than judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and underlying feelings such as fear of teasing and the midnight 'just one more' voice. Reflections like the Tuesday-drinking drift from her values showed deep understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking, progressing logically from values to rule-setting, obstacles, accountability, and weekend execution. Each turn advanced the target behavior toward a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was fully collaborative, with the psychiatrist acting as a thinking partner who asks rather than tells. Camila's ideas determined the direction at every step, demonstrating true power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively reinforced through open questions, offering choices about session direction, and framing all strategies as Camila's own design. No advice was imposed; the plan was framed as hers to own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within using questions about her values, confidence, and anticipated success. Each expression of Camila's own reasons and commitment was reflected back and amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in Camila, and her contributions — the drink cap, accountability plan, and ordering strategy — substantially shaped the session. Each turn built on her ideas rather than supplying expert solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited and deepened in nearly every turn, drawing out desire, ability, reasons, and commitment. The psychiatrist reinforced Camila's own change language and helped convert wishes into concrete commitment steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged without reinforcement and redirected toward change. The only gap was occasional reassurance (e.g., fear of slipping, being 'boring') where a double-sided reflection or reframe could have deepened ambivalence resolution.
