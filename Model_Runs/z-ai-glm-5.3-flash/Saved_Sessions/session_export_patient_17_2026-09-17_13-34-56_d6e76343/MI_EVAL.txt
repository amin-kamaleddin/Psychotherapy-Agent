# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with good normalization of past failures and affirmations. However, warmth felt somewhat formulaic and Anisa's shame and stigma went unexplored.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some good early reflections, but most responses quickly pivoted to advice. Emotionally rich material (daughter's disappointment, workplace stigma, the morning ritual) received minimal reflective exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, unwavering focus on smoking cessation throughout, progressing logically to a concrete one-week goal with a start date and follow-up intent.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements like letting Anisa choose her focus, but the counselor's dominant expert-delivery style meant plans and scripts were designed largely without joint creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-offering language and framing the plan as a one-week experiment reinforced client control. Fell short of 5 due to the volume of prescriptive steps leaving little room for Anisa's own strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all content was counselor-supplied advice with virtually no open questions eliciting Anisa's own reasons, strengths, or ideas. Her motivation emerged despite rather than because of the counselor's technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-style questions and offering choices showed some partnership, but the advice-dense, prescriptive interaction positioned the counselor as the strategy expert, undermining power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anisa generated substantial change talk on her own, but the counselor rarely amplified it. No deliberate evocative or commitment-strengthening questions, and missed chances to reflect her hope back to deepen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced and met with reassurance, but there was no skillful reframing—no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to her motivations—and some fears were left hanging.
