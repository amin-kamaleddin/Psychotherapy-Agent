# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with validation of Jamal's frustrations. Affirmations were mostly plan-focused rather than individually person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and feeling, but were sometimes brief and quickly followed by stacked questions. The key 'it's for me' moment deserved deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on punctuality with a clear progression from problem to barriers to plan to commitment. Topic drift was skillfully deferred ('one thing at a time').

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal-partner work: the text message was co-drafted, and plan elements came from Jamal's own preferences. Jamal explicitly noted it felt like 'figuring it out with someone.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong choice support via menus of options and deference to Jamal's judgment, which he explicitly acknowledged. Question density and a somewhat directive final recap kept it from a 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from Jamal, whose reasons evolved from external compliance to intrinsic ownership. Deeper exploration of personal values would have strengthened this.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership with Jamal choosing options and confirming his expertise was respected. Later exchanges became somewhat checklist-driven, tilting slightly toward directing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of rulers and open questions produced growing change talk from 'for me too' to 'for me, not just so everyone's off my back.' Peak change talk moments could have been consolidated with fuller reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about nagging and control was acknowledged without amplification and skillfully reframed toward autonomy. More deliberate double-sided reflections on the reactance theme would have raised this.
