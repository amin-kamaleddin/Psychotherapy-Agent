# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with consistent affirmations and a backup plan countering self-blame. Affirmations occasionally felt brief and formulaic rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early on, accurately capturing both behavior and emotion. Reflections became sparse in the latter half, and deeper feelings (shame, self-disappointment) were not explicitly reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on the target behavior with progressive guiding from exploration to a fully specified plan (cue, timer, task, accountability, backups). The session closed with a concrete weekly action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed across turns, with the client's ideas incorporated into each next step. Pace and agenda felt shared throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was consistently reinforced; she selected the casual script, the alarm over texting, and personalized wording. Setbacks were framed as her choice to retry kindly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from the client herself — her reasons, solutions, and success criteria — with scaling questions eliciting measurable confidence growth (4–5 → 7) and explicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira's own ideas drove the plan and the clinician consistently invited her choices rather than prescribing. Occasionally supplied multiple options and scripts rapidly, slightly diluting full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful scaling questions on importance and confidence with repeated 'what would raise it?' follow-ups. Confidence rose measurably from 4–5 to 7, culminating in commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement and reframed kindly in the backup plan ('pause kindly and try again'). Some sustain talk (e.g., 'Even ten minutes, I'll avoid it') was met with solution generation rather than reflective pivoting to her change language.
