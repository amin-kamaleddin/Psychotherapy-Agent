# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard ('I'm not here to tell you you're wrong,' 'I'll take it at your pace') and affirmed Viktor's honesty even amid deflection. No judgment or disapproval appeared anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including accurate double-sided reflections and the friends-vs-home tug-of-war. Reflections occasionally stayed at surface content without naming Viktor's deeper shame and fear of repeating his shouting childhood home.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on drug use and its relational consequences, with every exchange building purposefully toward a concrete plan and outcome evaluation. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—offered genuine choices, sought permission before deepening, and let Viktor author the plan entirely. The session exemplified interviewing between equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support ('this is your call,' 'let's build it your way'), and the final 'no promises' framing was honored. Persistent forward questioning at times assumed change as the direction rather than equally holding the option of no change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from Viktor's own values—his childhood promise, dislike of feeling like a jerk, and desire for a quieter house—through open questions. The counselor never argued for change; Viktor arrived at returning 'on my own terms' through evoked self-reflection.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Viktor as the expert ('You know her better than anyone'), and his contributions genuinely shaped the session—the text message, the buffer, and the success criterion were all his, with the counselor crediting him explicitly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation moved Viktor from 'maybe if I pulled back a little' to 'midnight is the deal' and ultimately 'maybe I come back here. On my own terms.' Strategic amplification and commitment questions drove steady progression of change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently avoided reinforcing sustain talk—used double-sided reflections to acknowledge blame ('she needs to see it's under control') without amplifying it, and pivoted obstacles like predicted failure toward Viktor's own solutions.
