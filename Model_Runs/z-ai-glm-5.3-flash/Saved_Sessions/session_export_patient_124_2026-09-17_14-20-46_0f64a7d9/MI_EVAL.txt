# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, but warmth is minimal and formulaic with no affirmations of Sofia's strengths, values, or willingness to experiment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; the counselor responds to meaningful disclosures with question barrages, tips, and scripts rather than complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on marijuana use with a concrete, measurable harm-reduction plan and follow-up, though achieved by steering toward a pre-selected menu rather than Sofia's own values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing exists (options, letting Sofia set the cap), but the interaction is asymmetric with the counselor supplying nearly all content while Sofia mostly reacts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine choices are offered and Sofia's framing is accepted, but the counselor is also quite prescriptive, dispensing tips, rules, and checklists that crowd out Sofia's own problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is assumed to come from tools and information rather than being elicited; the readiness ruler is dropped and Sofia's own reasons and values are never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choices are offered, but the overall stance is expert-driven; Sofia's expertise about her own social world is engaged only at her request.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia spontaneously offers meaningful change talk, but the counselor fails to reflect, deepen, or reinforce it, pivoting instead to tracking instructions and logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is neither reinforced nor argued against, but there are no double-sided reflections linking it to her change talk to resolve ambivalence.
