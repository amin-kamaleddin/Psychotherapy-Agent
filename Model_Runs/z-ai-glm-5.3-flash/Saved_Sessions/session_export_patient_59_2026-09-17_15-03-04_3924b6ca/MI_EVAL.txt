# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout — validates Jamal's resistance and never criticizes his use. However, warmth is somewhat procedural, with little affirmation of Jamal as a person beyond task compliance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes adequate but brief, formulaic reflections ('I hear you' recurs). Deeper emotional content — resentment of mandates, friction with his mother, peer norms — is acknowledged superficially but never explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stays focused, but on the wrong target: PO-script rehearsal instead of the clinical behavior (marijuana use). The referral issue is abandoned after the opening exchange and discrepancy is never developed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers options and accommodates Jamal's impatience, but the power balance is uneven — the counselor generates all solutions while Jamal reacts. Jamal's expertise about his PO, mother, and social context goes untapped.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice via menus and validates Jamal's refusal to overpromise; never pressures him to admit a problem. Autonomy support is limited to script selection rather than substantive change decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does little to draw motivation from Jamal — all persuasive language is counselor-supplied. Misses the golden opportunity when Jamal volunteered the session 'wasn't as painful as I thought'; no open questions or values exploration occur.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Fundamentally counselor-directed: the counselor supplies all content (scripts, tips, checklists) and Jamal merely performs. Jamal's own ideas are acknowledged briefly but not incorporated into the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no effort to evoke Jamal's own reasons for change; the opening question is quickly abandoned. Scripts are counselor-supplied compliance language, not client-generated change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Doesn't argue with or amplify sustain talk, but also misses chances to develop discrepancy. Minimizations like 'it's California' pass without reflection or gentle challenge — avoidance rather than active redirection.
