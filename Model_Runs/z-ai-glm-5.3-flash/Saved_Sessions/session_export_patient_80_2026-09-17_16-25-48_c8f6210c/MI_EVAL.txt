# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of Luka's efforts and explicit respect for his reluctance around abstinence and breathing exercises — Luka even notes 'you not lecturing me about the drinking.' Warmth occasionally felt formulaic and repeated safety checks were slightly scripted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections such as capturing Luka's anger sequence ('quiet/tense to chest-tight then loud/standing') and validating his breathing skepticism without dismissing it. Missed opportunities to reflect deeper emotional themes like his pride and how much he's changed over the past year.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained unwavering focus on alcohol-related aggression, systematically progressing through triggers, early warning signs, if-then coping, drink limits, and backup contacts. Every exchange advanced the plan, with tangents folded back into concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine partnership tone with Luka's contributions (brother as backup, drink limit of three, walking home) actively incorporated into the evolving plan. The initial safety-step list was somewhat clinician-generated rather than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered genuine choices ('Which would you prefer to start with?', 'rehearse now or later?') and accepted Luka's declinations without pressure. Luka explicitly owning the plan ('that plan's actually mine') is the strongest indicator of effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited Luka's own ideas at key moments, including the 0–10 confidence scaling that raised his rating from 7 to 9. Much of the session was technique-delivery with Luka selecting from clinician-proposed options, and deeper exploration of his values and reasons for change was largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Luka as the expert, with his final comment confirming genuine power-sharing. Early plan steps were presented as a pre-formatted menu rather than co-constructed from his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Luka offered substantial change talk largely spontaneously, and the confidence scaling successfully deepened commitment from 7 to 9. The psychiatrist rarely deliberately reflected or amplified change talk when it emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided arguing against sustain talk about abstinence, pivoting instead to harm-reduction steps, and neutrally reframed the breathing skepticism by pairing it with a strategy Luka already endorses. Responses were brief pivots rather than strategic double-sided reflections.
