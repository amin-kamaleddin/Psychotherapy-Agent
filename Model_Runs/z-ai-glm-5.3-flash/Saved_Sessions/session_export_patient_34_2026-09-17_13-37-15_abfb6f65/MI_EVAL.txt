# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance without labeling Dylan's drinking, and validated his frustration. However, warmth was limited and the tone was more task-oriented/case-manager than warmly person-centered, with few affirmations of Dylan as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered accurate early reflections of Dylan's frustration at being unfairly blamed. Reflective listening largely disappeared after the opening as the counselor pivoted to plans, templates, and scripts, leaving deeper feelings unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session was organized and consistent, but focused on the wrong target—managing the student conduct process rather than exploring the actual behavior of underage drinking. The behavioral concern was largely never addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Showed genuine responsiveness by adjusting drafts when Dylan objected to the apologetic tone and offering menu-based choices. However, collaboration was uneven, with the counselor frequently presenting pre-packaged plans and templates for Dylan to merely approve or tweak.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered menu-based choices twice, supporting Dylan's decision-making. But after initial options were presented, the counselor shifted to directive advice-giving with prescriptive steps, and never reinforced Dylan's ownership of decisions about his drinking or future choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and wisdom were treated as residing in the counselor, who supplied nearly all content (plans, scripts, drafts). Virtually no open questions elicited Dylan's own reasons, values, or hopes regarding his drinking or goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor assumed an expert role, delivering numbered plans and scripts with little genuine input from Dylan. Incorporating Dylan's details (the 1:00 a.m. text) reflected information-gathering rather than true power sharing or drawing on his expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to evoke or reinforce change talk. Flickers of openness from Dylan ('not the worst idea,' 'more useful than I thought') were never reflected, affirmed, or built upon, and no questions explored his own reasons for reconsidering drinking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor did not reinforce Dylan's substantial sustain talk, which is a strength, but also never softened, reframed, or used double-sided reflections to develop discrepancy. Sustain talk was bypassed rather than addressed, leaving precontemplation untouched.
