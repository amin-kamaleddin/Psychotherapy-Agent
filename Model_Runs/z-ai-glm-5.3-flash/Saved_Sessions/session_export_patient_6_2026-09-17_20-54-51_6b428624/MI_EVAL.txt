# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, validating ambivalence and affirming the client's growth. The client explicitly appreciated not being lectured.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections tracked the client's emotional shifts and the meaning behind his words. The counselor mirrored both surface content and deeper concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Steady focus on alcohol use and its link to athletic goals, with every exchange building toward a concrete, measurable plan. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functioned as joint inquiry, with the 'playbook' co-constructed from the client's own contributions. The client confirmed the collaborative quality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership were repeatedly reinforced, with the plan framed as entirely self-generated. The client independently escalated commitment without imposed advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within; the client generated his own reasons and implementation plan. A fully realized evocation of client-owned motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located within the client, who generated every strategy himself. The counselor explicitly credited his ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions strategically elicited rich change talk, which was reinforced at every opportunity. The client moved from uncertainty to clear commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with accepting double-sided reflections rather than argument, allowing ambivalence to resolve naturally. The counselor never reinforced status quo reasons.
