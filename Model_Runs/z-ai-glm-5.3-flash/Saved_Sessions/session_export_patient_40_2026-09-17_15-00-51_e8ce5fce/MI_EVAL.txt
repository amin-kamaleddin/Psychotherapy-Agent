# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with skillful handling of shame disclosures and safety screening. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and repetitive ('practical, protective').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening capturing shame, confusion, and family importance. Reflections often stay at surface content and pivot quickly to options rather than exploring deeper emotional meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, clear focus on relapse prevention with every turn tied to the target behavior. The session progresses logically from exploration to a layered, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing: Elias's preferences shape the plan, options are offered rather than imposed, and the counselor supports in-session action as a helper rather than director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decisions consistently left with Elias, choices offered as options, and his modifications (rejecting scripts and hiding spots) immediately embraced. His hesitation about his son is left as his decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation drawn effectively from Elias through evocative questions rather than persuasion. Missed opportunities to deepen his revealed motivations (family as his 'why,' pride in three months of sobriety).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias's contributions treated as authoritative and his ideas shape the final plan. The counselor tends to supply option menus and sample scripts rather than drawing solutions from Elias first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation of concrete commitments ('no excuses,' 'I'll save that crisis number today'), culminating in an in-session text. Change talk rarely deepened, summarized, or amplified to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and some effective reframing occurs, but little active softening: no double-sided reflections, no strengths-based affirmations during self-doubt, and hesitations left largely unexplored.
