# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating David's skepticism ('That makes sense', 'sensible'). Falls short of 5 due to few explicit affirmations of David's honesty or efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured David's core conflict and desire for practical control. Reflections sometimes pivoted quickly to option menus rather than deepening the emotional meaning of his fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus from opening to final written two-week plan, with each exchange advancing toward a concrete outcome and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions framed as joint and the plan incorporated David's own modifications. Diet and activity advice was largely counselor-generated rather than co-created from David's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: 'You stay in control' and the 'I CAN STOP THE PILL ANYTIME' condition written prominently into the plan, with repeated menus honoring David's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concerns, preferences, and doable steps, culminating in self-generated commitment. Deeper values and reasons for health (family, work identity) were not explored, and key psychoeducation was delivered rather than elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited David's choices and honored his conditions, but substantial stretches were counselor-delivered information; David's own expertise about his job, wife's cooking, and habits could have been leveraged more.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked commitment via the monitored trial question and goal summary, producing 'Maybe that's not the worst idea' and 'a real deal this time'. Some change talk emerged spontaneously rather than through deeper deliberate evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk (dad's longevity, buddy's muscle damage, sacred Friday beers) without reinforcing it, and the monitored trial with exit option directly disarmed the central concern. Early A/B/C menus somewhat amplified arguments against medication before resolving them.
