# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, normalizing Elena's anxiety and validating her ambivalence without shame. Falls short of 5 because some affirmations feel formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening throughout, including a skillful double-sided reflection on IUD ambivalence and accurately naming 'peace of mind, not perfection.' Short of 5 due to one off-pace reflection that circled back to the pregnancy scare after Elena had moved to practical questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear progressive focus from exploring the scare to ambivalence to concrete next steps, aided by well-targeted narrowing questions. Slightly undermined by a mid-session reflective detour back to earlier material and a close on logistics without a motivating summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine joint enterprise — tasks proposed collaboratively and Elena's choices visibly shaped the session, moving from hesitant to directing. Information was framed as resources for her decision, not prescriptions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary, textbook-level autonomy support: repeatedly hands control to Elena ('you don't have to have it figured out,' 'no promises needed') and frames her boundary-setting as legitimate. Never applies pressure or persuasive tactics.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within Elena rather than supplied — her own words ('a plan,' 'the deal I'm making with myself') became the drivers of change. The reasons for change all came from Elena and were reflected back amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Elena as decision-maker and offered agenda control, with her contributions shaping the session's closing task. Short of 5 because the information-provision phase answered multiple questions in one expert turn without checking what she already knew.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest domain: skillfully selected and amplified Elena's change talk and used low-threshold evocations ('which of those feels even slightly worth learning more about?') that yielded 'Maybe the IUD.' Reflected her self-observed progress to reinforce momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well without amplifying it, including an excellent non-confrontational pivot when Elena disclaimed commitment ('no promises needed at all'). Occasional double-sided reflections ended on the fearful side rather than re-anchoring in her change talk.
