# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard: validating defensiveness, affirming client ideas, and reflecting minimization neutrally without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including complex double-sided reflections that consistently land and are confirmed by the client building on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on meth use and health risk, moving purposefully from ambivalence to a concrete four-point plan with self-defined success markers.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership throughout—decisions offered as options and the plan fully co-constructed from the client's own ideas with no imposed agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice consistently reinforced ('that's completely your choice,' 'you tell me what feels doable'); client internalized autonomy, framing the plan as staying in charge with no pressure or persuasion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within via open questions eliciting the client's own reasons, strategies, and success criteria; every plan element originated from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client explicitly positioned as the expert on his own patterns repeatedly, with his contributions directly shaping the session plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and developed, progressing from cautious admissions to commitment language and concrete written intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged briefly, then reframed and redirected toward the client's own health goals—never argued with or amplified.
