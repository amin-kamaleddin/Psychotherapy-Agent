# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally respectful and nonjudgmental, including a slip-friendly stance ('don't shame—review what triggered it'). However, tone is transactional, no affirmations of Anya's strengths, and an inaccurate reflection risks labeling her with shame she didn't voice.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; the counselor relies on questions, scripts, and plans. Rich emotional content (fear of bills, sadness about motherhood, feeling 'light') was never reflected or explored beyond one acknowledgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, consistent focus on the gambling/financial target with good session momentum toward concrete steps. Kept from a 5 by checklist-driven direction that never links back to Anya's stated values or checks the plan against her priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine collaboration (offering options, script edits, letting Anya choose mini-golf and scripts). Power-sharing is limited by rapid expert problem-solving, with plans handed over rather than co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers menus of options and asks permission before drafting texts, which supports choice. But the style is prescriptive and paced by the counselor, with no explicit affirmation that change is Anya's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is outsourced to practical structures (check-ins, auto-transfers) rather than elicited. Anya's powerful motivational material—Egypt, 'decent mum'—arose spontaneously but was never explored; no evocative questions or values exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some partnership elements exist (choices, preference questions), but the dominant stance is expert-driven, with prescribed plans following most disclosures. Anya is rarely positioned as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler worked (5→6) and Anya generated her own plan. However, her volunteered change statements ('That's flights, plural,' 'I feel weirdly... light') were never deepened, affirmed, or reflected.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not amplified or echoed—a strength. But ambivalence is passively bypassed rather than managed: no double-sided reflections, reframes, or responses to her fears about social pressure and creditor hostility.
