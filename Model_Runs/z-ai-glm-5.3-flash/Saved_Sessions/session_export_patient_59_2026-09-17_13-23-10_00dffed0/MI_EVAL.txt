# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained a neutral, nonjudgmental tone without labeling or criticism. However, the interaction was purely procedural with generic, formulaic responses and no genuine warmth or rapport-building.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was nearly absent, with responses limited to transactional checklists and disclaimers. The counselor failed to reflect the frustration beneath Jamal's minimization and asked no exploratory questions about his experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Focus was directed toward procedural completion rather than the target behavior of marijuana use and related risk. No questions were asked about use patterns, context, or consequences, and the session drifted entirely into logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The counselor dictated structure and managed the interaction like a documentation task rather than a joint effort. There was no shared agenda-setting, no 'we' language, and no meaningful incorporation of Jamal's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered basic administrative choices, such as note delivery options and framing next steps as optional. However, no meaningful autonomy was supported regarding the session's content, and change itself was never framed as Jamal's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No attempt was made to elicit the client's own motivations, reasons, values, or goals. The session devolved into completing a safety checklist and discharge logistics, representing a fundamental absence of MI's evocative spirit.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The exchange resembled administrative intake rather than partnership, with no acknowledgment of the client's expertise on his own life. Jamal was never invited to share his views or shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There was virtually no effort to evoke change talk; Jamal's only quasi-change statement was never explored or developed. No questions addressed his values, goals, or how the charge might fit into his life.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor did not actively reinforce sustain talk, which is a minor strength. However, Jamal's minimization was ignored rather than acknowledged, with no double-sided reflections or attempts to develop discrepancy.
