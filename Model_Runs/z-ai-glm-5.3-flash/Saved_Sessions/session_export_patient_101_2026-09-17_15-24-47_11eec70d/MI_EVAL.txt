# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, normalizes fears, and affirms efforts, though affirmations are somewhat generic rather than personalized to Mei's specific strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good complex reflections capture Mei's core concerns and situational confidence, but the counselor often pivots quickly to advice, missing chances to explore the emotional weight of family history and past lapses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays focused on drinking and contraception and ends with a concrete plan, though it briefly pushes drinking when Mei wanted to consolidate and some logistics detours dilute focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The role-play is co-created and permission is sought, but the agenda, solutions, and checklists are largely counselor-generated, so power-sharing remains uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, seeks permission, and lets Mei make real decisions, though options are counselor-constructed menus rather than reinforcement of her ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses rulers and open questions to evoke motivation, but the default mode is supplying information and options rather than eliciting Mei's own reasons and solutions, and confidence talk was not deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Mei's input and incorporates her ideas, but a persistent expert stance with multiple unsolicited advice lists means counselor-generated solutions dominate over Mei's own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective scaling questions and invitations for Mei's own solutions generate substantial change talk, but the counselor does not consistently amplify or deepen her confidence and commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses a clever one-night experiment reframe, but lacks double-sided reflections and sometimes moves past doubts without addressing them directly.
