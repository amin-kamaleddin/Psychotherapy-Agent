### Acceptance

**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations like 'That's not all-or-nothing you anymore' and no judgment when Marcus declared the dog off the table. Warm, respectful closing maintained acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections capturing ambivalence and relational stakes (e.g., 'part of you feels it's all too much... another part knows you've got to do something'). Also reflected emotional tone: 'I hear the relief in that, and some real tiredness too.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear progressive focus: appointment burden → grouping → one call → reminders → contingency → follow-up. Gently redirected overwhelm while allowing natural expansion to the dog-bed issue tied to asthma triggers.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—Marcus's ideas (grouping appointments, phone reminders, wife as nudge) genuinely drove the session. Invited Marcus to shape the agenda and follow-up plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within via evocative questions that amplified Marcus's own insights ('I just need one thing that doesn't feel impossible'). Result was Marcus voicing his own change talk: 'I think I actually want to try.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Marcus as expert ('You're the expert on your own life'), which he explicitly recognized: 'Instead I'm sitting here making my own.' Elicited rather than prescribed throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation through open questions ('What would make even one of these burdens a little lighter?') and reinforcing change talk ('you landed somewhere solid: one call, this week'). Marcus moved from ambivalence to commitment.

**Comments:** Used balanced double-sided reflections and quickly pivoted to change talk; wisely let Marcus raise the dog issue himself. Minor deduction because reflections like 'Both of those can be true' occasionally gave equal airtime to sustain talk without an explicit pivot.
