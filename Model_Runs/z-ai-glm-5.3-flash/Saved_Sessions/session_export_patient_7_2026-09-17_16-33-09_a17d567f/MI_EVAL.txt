# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and nonjudgment, even when Amina is hostile ('It's not really any of your business'). Falls short of 5 due to somewhat generic, repetitive warmth ('I hear you') and few individualized affirmations of Amina's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening, accurately capturing loneliness and fear that she's 'the problem.' Reflections sometimes parrot surface content rather than deepening the emotional meaning behind vulnerable moments.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Focus stays appropriately on engagement, safety, and confidentiality given the presenting concerns. Rapid menu-offering dilutes focus, and no consolidating summary ties together the phone incident and feeling blamed at home.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is consistently framed as a joint endeavor, co-creating privacy agreements and ground rules. Collaboration is sometimes menu-driven and generic rather than deeply built from Amina's specific ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session's standout strength: explicit and repeated reinforcement of Amina's control, honoring silence and refusals without persuasion, and honest plain-language confidentiality limits with offers to check before any disclosure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Patient, open invitations drew out meaningful disclosures from a minimally responsive teen — the phone invasion, fear of things being 'thrown back in her face.' Surfaced motivations are reflected but not developed into change-oriented meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina's input visibly shapes the session, including willingness to literally sit in silence at her request. Her significant phone-search disclosure receives only brief reflection before pivoting to confidentiality logistics rather than steering the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Prioritizing engagement over change talk fits Amina's precontemplative state, but natural openings were missed — the phone violation disclosure and 'that's actually kind of what I needed to hear' could have been explored to evoke client-voiced desires.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with acceptance and redirection rather than argument, and debates are avoided. Missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections to bridge 'That's just how she is' toward Amina's hopes for home life.
