# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout; Sofia is never judged for her drinking or ambivalence, and her shame is validated without amplification. Her staged disclosure to her mother is affirmed as 'wise' rather than avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening captured both content and emotional undertones — e.g., 'caught between two worlds' and the stakes of friendship acceptance. Surface and deeper feelings were tracked throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use during pregnancy with progressive momentum: weekend struggle → sober attendance → Marissa text → timing/barriers → mom's support → concrete plan. Guiding questions stayed purposeful without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit team language ('let's figure this out together') and co-construction of the plan; Sofia's answers visibly shaped the session's course. Genuine two-way exchange rather than a top-down interview.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ownership reinforced at every decision point: 'you're choosing your timing,' and 'it came from you — nobody handed it to you.' No advice was imposed; the plan was explicitly framed as hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight — designated-driver idea, text strategy, and final commitment — originated from Sofia via values-based open questions. Motivation was consistently drawn from within rather than supplied by the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client expertise explicitly positioned ('You know your friends and your world better than anyone'). The plan's elements all came from Sofia and were attributed to her, the hallmark of strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and reinforcement of change talk, including highlighting Sofia's shift from 'I can't cut off my friends' to brainstorming solutions, culminating in her spontaneous commitment: 'I'm not drinking for the rest of this pregnancy.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective double-sided reflections and non-amplified acknowledgment of sustain talk about her old identity. Minor limitation: repeating Sofia's self-blame language ('Why keep adding to that?') brushed close to dwelling on guilt rather than pivoting fully toward change.
