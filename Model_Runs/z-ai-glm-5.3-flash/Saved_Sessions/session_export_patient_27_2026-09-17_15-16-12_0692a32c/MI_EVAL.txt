# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout and normalizes Daniela's mixed feelings. Deducted for somewhat formulaic warmth lacking deeper individualized affirmations of her courage and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections capture both sides of her ambivalence (valuing friends vs. fearing being seen differently). Deducted for reflections that occasionally stay at surface level rather than reaching the emotional core of secrecy and fear of rejection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear, consistent focus on disclosing diabetes to friends, with every exchange advancing the goal from exploration through rehearsal to a concrete plan. Builds logically to a client-owned plan, as Daniela notes this is the most prepared she's ever felt.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely a joint endeavor through co-constructed scripts and role-play, with Daniela making key decisions. Deducted because the counselor often presents solution menus, making the flow counselor-proposed rather than genuinely co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent autonomy support: the client is explicitly in charge of focus, timing, method, wording, and contingency planning, with choices offered rather than directives. Her stated pace ('not today, maybe') is accepted without any pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from within via open questions, role-play, and helping Daniela discover her own insights, producing escalating change talk. Deducted because motivation is occasionally supported through supplied options and reassurance rather than consistent open-ended evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly conveys that expertise resides with the client, asking which options sound most like her and letting Daniela shape her own script. Deducted for frequently supplying pre-written menus, positioning the counselor as the idea source.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through choice questions, rehearsal, and role-play, with change talk escalating to 'I think I might actually do this.' Deducted for reliance on counselor-provided option menus and lack of explicit consolidation of commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk respectfully without arguing (e.g., 'keeping it private protects you'). However, reflections of sustain talk are often left standing alone without double-sided pivots toward change, and ambivalence is frequently managed by jumping to counselor-supplied problem-solving options.
