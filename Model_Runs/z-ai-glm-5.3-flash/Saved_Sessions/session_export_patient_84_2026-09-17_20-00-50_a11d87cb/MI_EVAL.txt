# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, non-judgmental stance throughout, including when Malik minimized or joked about his use. Withheld a 5 because affirmations were implicit rather than explicit statements of regard for Malik as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured Malik's ambivalence and priorities (freedom, trust, peer belonging). Fell short of a 5 as reflections stayed content-level, missing chances to mirror emotional undercurrents like fear of peer rejection or his mother's disappointed look.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior with every exchange building toward it, from imagining things going well to a concrete, client-generated behavioral framework. No aimless drift, all achieved without pushing an agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as equal partners with ideas emerging exclusively from Malik while the counselor reflected and invited the next step. Malik's own words confirmed the power-sharing was felt.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly disclaimed any push and framed change entirely as Malik's choice, directly neutralizing his 'I hate being told what to do' stance. Malik's own closing words confirmed he felt ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every motivation for change was drawn from Malik through strategic open questions, with no reasons offered by the counselor. His closing line about not telling his mom demonstrated genuine internally-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Malik as the expert on his own world, with his contributions shaping the session's substance and direction. His closing comments confirmed the power-sharing was felt.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evoked change talk through open questions building on Malik's own motivations, producing a clear escalation from minimization to self-generated rules. Cleverly leveraged his peer example (Deon) rather than adult authority.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirected sustain talk with double-sided reflections and let Malik's own counterarguments work. Some sustain talk passed without strategic softening, and deliberate double-sided reflections to develop discrepancy were limited.
