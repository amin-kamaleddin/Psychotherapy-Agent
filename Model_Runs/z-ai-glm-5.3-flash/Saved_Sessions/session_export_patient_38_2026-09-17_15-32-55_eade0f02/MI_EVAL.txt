# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, honoring Elena's limits without pushback ('I'll pass on the draft' accepted). Slightly procedural at times, with limited individualized affirmation of her courage in attending.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflections capturing both content and emotion ('still shaken by how close that felt'; 'downplay your worry to avoid burdening her'). Did not deeply explore the emotional meaning behind Elena's avoidance of contraception talk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good focus and concrete plan-building within the negotiated topic (fear from the pregnancy scare), but the referral concern (contraception) was never revisited or linked to the coping work, even at closure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: permission-seeking, co-constructing the plan step by step (breathing, text, code word). Falls short because options were largely counselor-structured menus rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, explicit autonomy support: agenda choice, repeated permission-seeking, offered choices, and honoring every decline. Elena's ownership of the plan was reinforced throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Readiness/confidence rulers, 'what might get in the way,' and option menus elicited Elena's own reasons and change talk. Missed evocation around the core contraception goal and missed deepening follow-ups (e.g., 'what would make it an 8?').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Elena as the expert and incorporated her ideas into the final plan. Agenda and technique menus were largely counselor-defined, with Elena choosing within those options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and reflective reinforcement evoked change talk ('I think I could actually do that,' 'mention it to her this week'). Change talk was channeled toward coping rather than linked back to contraception motivation, and commitment language was not deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argued with or confronted sustain talk ('maybe another time'); accepted Elena's pacing and reduced threat with choices. Missed a light double-sided reflection linking her fear of pregnancy to future contraception readiness.
