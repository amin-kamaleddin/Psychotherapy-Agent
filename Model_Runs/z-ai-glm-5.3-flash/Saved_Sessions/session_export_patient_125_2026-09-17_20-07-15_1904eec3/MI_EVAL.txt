### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian interaction with permission-style questions and co-constructed agenda. The client's feedback ('Thanks for actually listening instead of just lecturing me') confirms the collaborative feel from his side.

**Comments:** Actively reinforced client choice at every juncture ('Want to give it a shot, or would you rather talk through it first?'). All strategies originated from Tariq, with no advice imposed — 'That's your plan, not mine.'

#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within the client — his mother, his father's birthday, his release goals. Tariq articulated his own cost-benefit analysis and self-efficacy, showing successfully evoked and developed change talk.

### Partnership
