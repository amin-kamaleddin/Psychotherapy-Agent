# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, validating Isabella's frustration and skepticism without labeling. Few explicit affirmations of her strengths limited deeper individualization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but often shallow, quickly pivoting to information-giving or planning. Missed opportunities to explore the emotional meaning of family tradition, nighttime worry, and identity conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on alcohol use in pregnancy throughout, progressing from skepticism to a concrete plan. Minor drift into document-format logistics consumed time without clinical value.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission and offered choices, and role-play was genuinely collaborative. However, the plan was largely counselor-authored with Isabella as endorser rather than co-designer.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored Isabella's autonomy with permission-asking and choice-offering; she explicitly left feeling the change was her own decision. Ready-made plans occasionally preempted client-generated options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Relied on providing information and a pre-built plan rather than drawing out motivation. Isabella's values (good mom, healthy baby) emerged spontaneously but were never developed via rulers, values exploration, or open evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Positioned Isabella as expert at times and responded well to her request for role-play. Overall pattern leaned toward counselor-as-plan-generator rather than facilitator of the client's own solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Captured Isabella's spontaneous change talk but did little to actively evoke or deepen it. Lacked deliberate evocative questions or strategic reflections to amplify her motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and gently reframed once, but responses were primarily corrective information. No double-sided reflections linking family tradition to her own 'good mom' value.
