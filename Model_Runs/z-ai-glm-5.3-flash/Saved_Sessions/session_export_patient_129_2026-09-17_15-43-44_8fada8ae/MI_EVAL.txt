# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout ('I'm not here to judge'), accepting Tyler's defensiveness without correction. Held at 4 due to sparse affirmations of Tyler's strengths, which would be expected for a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but often surface-level and formulaic. Missed a significant opening when Tyler revealed feeling 'nothing special' before his peer group — a key emotional undercurrent left unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally kept focus on substance use and family conflict, steering back after side topics. However, a substantial portion of the middle session drifted into de-escalation coaching, diluting focus on the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behaved largely as an equal partner, with Tyler's input visibly shaping the session and the 'weekdays off' plan. Not a 5 due to unilateral delivery of psychoeducation and scripted phrases rather than genuine co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest domain — consistently reinforced Tyler's ownership ('You're in charge') and offered every intervention as a choice, with no pressure even when risks were minimized. The experiment was framed entirely as Tyler's own decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some genuine change talk (likes/dislikes of use, willingness to experiment), but frequently defaulted to providing facts and scripts rather than drawing out Tyler's own reasons. Deeper personal motivation around belonging was never evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Tyler as decision-maker, and his responses demonstrably shaped the session direction. Held at 4 because pre-written scripts and advice menus were handed over in an expert-driven way rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited pivotal change talk ('Maybe. If there was a reason', 'weekdays off') via the self-experiment offer. Failed to deepen these moments — no amplification, commitment language, or confidence exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing, which was a strength. But sometimes mirrored Tyler's minimizations and lacked double-sided reflections or strategic pivots after statements like 'I could stop whenever'.
