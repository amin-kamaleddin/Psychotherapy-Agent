# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed warmth, unconditional positive regard, and respect, affirming Luka's wins and normalizing his nervousness without any judgment. His self-defined choices (own drinks, text timing) were accepted without pushback.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captured Luka's situation and ambivalence ('nervous but ready'). However, emotionally weighty statements like 'I don't want to be that bloke again' and 'One's never just one with me' were passed over in favor of moving to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, logical focus on reducing drinking and managing pub-night risk, building cumulatively toward Friday. Luka's closing confidence ('then I'll be right, I reckon') reflected the coherent direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were largely co-constructed, with Luka rewriting scripts into his own voice. At times the psychiatrist offered ready-made lines and backup lists before Luka had fully generated his own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent choice-framing ('Which feels most doable?') and full acceptance of Luka's modifications reinforced his ownership. The 'I'm doing this for me tonight' framing actively strengthened his internal locus of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and the confidence ruler skillfully drew out Luka's own reasons and commitment ('I want to try,' 'Friday, I'm having this exact stink'). Behavioral rehearsal produced strong client-generated change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Luka was positioned as the expert and shaped the session substantially. The clinician sometimes supplied pre-formed suggestions rather than consistently eliciting them first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI strategies (open questions, confidence ruler, rehearsal, drafting his own text) generated strong commitment language throughout. Luka moved from preparation to explicit, confident commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I might crack Friday,' 'a few drinks don't make me a bad person') was never amplified and was redirected constructively. A minor gap: ambivalence wasn't explicitly acknowledged or reframed before pivoting to solutions.
