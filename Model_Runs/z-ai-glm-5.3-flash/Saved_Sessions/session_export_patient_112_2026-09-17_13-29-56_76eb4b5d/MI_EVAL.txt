# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of Rosa's honesty and her small step, though warmth was somewhat formulaic and deeper fears were not individually validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was limited; Rosa's core concerns (smoking as identity, withdrawal, weight gain, finances, work crankiness) were acknowledged once at best and never explored, as the counselor repeatedly rushed to practical steps.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with a specific, actionable first step (delaying the first cigarette by one hour) that Rosa agreed to attempt. Direction was narrow and task-focused, missing connection to Rosa's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rosa shaped minor details (gum choice, script for Dee, 5 pm check-in), but the agenda and plan were essentially counselor-designed, and her past quit attempts were never explored.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some permission-asking and choices were offered, but the counselor prescribed exact techniques and scripts and repeated them relentlessly, undermining Rosa's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to draw motivation from Rosa herself; her change talk emerged despite rather than because of counselor technique. No evocative questions, confidence rulers, or discrepancy development were used.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor positioned herself as the expert with a counselor-generated plan delivered prescriptively, with Rosa's contributions having limited impact on session structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rosa spontaneously offered change talk ('I'll try it tomorrow,' 'I've actually got a plan') but the counselor never deliberately evoked, elaborated, or deepened it, jumping instead to prescribing tasks.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not actively reinforced but was also ignored rather than softened; no double-sided reflections or reframing were used, leaving Rosa's ambivalence unresolved.
