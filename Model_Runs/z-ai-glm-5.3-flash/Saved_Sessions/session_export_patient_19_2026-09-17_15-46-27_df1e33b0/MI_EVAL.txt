# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of Anya's honesty and unconditional regard ('proud or honest'). Falls short of 5 due to limited deep validation of her shame and self-deprecation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections capture her ambivalence and triggers, but responses skew toward questions and planning. Emotional nuances like her fear of caving and 2am despair go largely unmet with reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary focus on gambling and debt from opening question through concrete commitments and a scheduled follow-up. No topic drift; every exchange ties back to the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative feel — asks before advising, incorporates Anya's own ideas, lets her set the check-in. Falls short because the plan scaffolding (scripts, if-then templates) originates from the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames actions as Anya's choice with no pressure or moralizing about the probation context. Choices are somewhat menu-style and constrained by the pre-built plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions successfully elicit her motivations (Egypt dream, self-generated strategies), but evocation is confined mostly to the opening. Powerful material like 'me, boring and winning' is acknowledged but not explored or amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anya selects among options and her contributions shape details, but the overall posture is expert-driven with the clinician supplying the plan structure, scripts, and framework. Power-sharing is only partial.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of importance/confidence rulers and small-step framing elicits sustained change talk ('cutting up the credit cards,' 'a 7 now,' 'I might actually do this'). Reinforcement is brief and lacks deepening follow-ups linking values to commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced and ambivalence is briefly acknowledged, but softening is minimal. No double-sided reflections or normalization of past failures; worries about social pressure are met with logistics rather than exploration.
