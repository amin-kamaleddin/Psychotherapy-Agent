# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, respectful tone and offered affirmations throughout, though they were somewhat generic and not deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good accurate reflection early on, but reflective listening largely disappeared afterward, with missed opportunities to reflect Amina's worries about friends, autopilot use, and self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus that narrowed a vague concern into a concrete three-night experiment, though sometimes pushed forward rather than through collaborative goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine collaborative moments (app choices, message wording), but the overall structure was expert-led with pre-packaged plans and checklists dominating most turns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and framed the plan as a 'try' with permission to slip, but generated nearly all options personally rather than eliciting Amina's own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Weakest domain — motivation and solutions were largely supplied rather than drawn out, with no exploration of Amina's values, confidence, or reasons for change despite rich volunteered material.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina's preferences were incorporated, but the counselor positioned themselves as the problem-solver, folding her ideas into their own structure rather than letting them shape it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina generated meaningful change talk that the counselor occasionally capitalized on, but never strategically deepened or amplified with explorations of meaning or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and briefly acknowledged worries, but used no double-sided reflections or reframing, often pivoting straight to problem-solving instead.
