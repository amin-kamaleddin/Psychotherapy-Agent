# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with strong affirmations countering Marco's self-doubt ('not weak, a real useful change'). However, praise sometimes felt formulaic and was mostly tied to plan compliance rather than unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections about his dad's heart attack and drained evenings, but emotional depth was limited. Marco's fear, shame, and fatigue from past failures were often bypassed in favor of logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on skipping seconds with steady progress toward a concrete plan (tactic, message, start date, Thursday contingency). Every exchange tied back to next steps and confidence checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent check-ins before finalizing, incorporating Marco's edits and adjustments (Thursdays-only texts, pistachios). The expert stance occasionally emerged in detailed prescriptive guidance delivered rather than co-developed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Verbally affirmed choice and allowed wording adjustments, but frequently steered toward preferred solutions with leading questions and unsolicited tips. Marco's autonomy was respected but not consistently fostered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited his core value early and used confidence scaling effectively, but much problem-solving was counselor-supplied. Marco's best insights emerged with limited deliberate evocation or deepening.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Marco's choices and let him select strategies, but supplied most solutions via menus of tactics. Positioned more as an advisor than treating Marco as the primary source of expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective scaling questions elicited progressively stronger change talk (6 → 6.5 → 7, ending at a 'steadier seven'). Connected the plan to his values, prompting spontaneous commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sometimes reframed sustain talk constructively (Thursday-drain became a targeted text reminder), but often ignored doubts. Concerns about his wife policing him and Thursday failure were passed over rather than actively softened.
