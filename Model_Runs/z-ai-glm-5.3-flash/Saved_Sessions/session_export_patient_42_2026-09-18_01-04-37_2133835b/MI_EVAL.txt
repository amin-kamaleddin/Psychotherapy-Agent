### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Therapist skillfully nurtures change talk ('I think I actually want to try that') and reinforces it without pushing, reflecting commitment language back to the patient.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Doubts about being left behind and all-or-nothing thinking are met with acceptance and gentle reframing, transforming sustain talk into readiness and self-efficacy.
