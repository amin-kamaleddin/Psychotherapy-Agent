# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard communicated throughout ('You matter here regardless of anything else'); no judgment despite Maya's minimization of drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong double-sided reflections and accurate naming of her fear about the abuela's dream. Occasional reflections restated rather than deepened — e.g., the fear of peer pushback deserved fuller emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on alcohol use and its interface with Maya's goals, progressively moving from engagement to a concrete, client-generated plan without feeling directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative — every strategy emerged jointly from Maya's ideas, with no prescriptions. Client noticed: 'I came in expecting a lecture and instead I'm kind of making my own plan.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of ownership ('You get to shape this'); the plan is entirely Maya's, and 'doable-ish' was accepted rather than pushing abstinence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key motivations (abuela's dream, fear of being 'that girl') were drawn from the client via strategic open questions and amplified. Maya's own words confirm success: 'I feel more ready to than I did an hour ago.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maya explicitly positioned as the expert on her world; her Dani idea and check-in format were adopted rather than replaced with clinician-generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions ('What would it mean to you to make her proud?') elicited rich change talk and concrete plans, reinforced with affirmations like 'That's your insight, not mine.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with or reinforced; the double-sided reflection on ambivalence ('part of you feels... at the same time...') modeled ideal handling without amplifying the sustain side.
