# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; guilt and pride are met with normalization ("It's not silly, not really"). Affirmations occasionally cluster but feel genuine.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both content and emotion, e.g., the double-weight reflection on self-blame and worry about the baby. Emotional attunement is consistent and precise.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on exhaustion, sleep, and self-doubt, progressing logically to a concrete plan. Purposeful questions maintain momentum without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power is genuinely shared—summaries end with invitations and the plan builds entirely on the client's own resources and ideas. The counselor checks understanding and builds rather than redirects.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All plans originate with the client, with permission for imperfection ("you're allowed to figure this out as you go"). No prescriptions or directives given.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from the client through open questions and the confidence ruler; she leaves with a self-generated plan and her own three anchors. No persuasion used.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is repeatedly positioned as the expert ("You know your baby best," "as the expert on your own life"), and her ideas shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully amplified via the confidence ruler ("what would move that six just one point higher?") and commitment rehearsal (practicing words to say to her partner), yielding a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (guilt, pride, fear) is validated without amplification, using double-sided reflections to let the client resolve ambivalence herself. Pivots to solutions she generates are handled well.
