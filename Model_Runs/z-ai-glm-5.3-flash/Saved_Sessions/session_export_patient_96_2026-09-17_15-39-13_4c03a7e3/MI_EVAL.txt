# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental stance throughout—piercing never labeled irresponsible and autonomy is respected. Affirmations are sparse and formulaic, lacking individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured both frustration and the self-expression/autonomy theme. Reflections tapered off as the session became advice- and rehearsal-focused, with little exploration of underlying emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session stayed focused and agenda-setting was explicit, but momentum drifted toward dentist impression management and jewelry shopping rather than the health behavior itself. No health-motivated thread developed for a precontemplative client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely non-authoritarian with menus, preference checks, and iterative co-construction of the dental script. Falls short because the plan's substance was counselor-generated, with Marisol mostly fine-tuning wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session's strongest domain: explicit affirmation of choice at every turn with options offered and no pressure toward removal. Consistently reinforced Marisol's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little expectation that motivation resides within the client—almost no open questions eliciting her own values or concerns. The counselor supplied the rationale, steps, and scripts, bypassing a precontemplative client's motivation rather than drawing it out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered agenda choices and asked permission before informing, but then shifted into an expert/advisor role with a dense list of steps. Marisol shaped wording but not the substance or direction of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to evoke the client's own reasons for change; her 'titanium' commitment was compliance shaped by counselor suggestions, not self-generated motivation. Emerging change talk was never strategically deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without endorsement and reframed skillfully, never amplifying defensiveness. However, the status-quo frame was largely worked around rather than used for double-sided reflections to develop ambivalence.
