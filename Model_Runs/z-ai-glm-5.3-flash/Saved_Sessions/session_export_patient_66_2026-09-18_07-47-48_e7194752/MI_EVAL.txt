# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific, genuine affirmations and no judgment of the smoking. Kareem directly confirms the effect: 'Thanks for not pushing the quit thing down my throat.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing both surface frustration and deeper meaning (father's death, ten minutes of peace). Falls short of 5 because emotionally loaded material like the father's death is acknowledged but never explored further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A clear arc from resistance to a concrete client-owned experiment, but focus wobbles: the success question was asked twice (which Kareem flagged) and some exchanges were redundant without new momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: the walking plan, success criteria, and fortnight follow-up all came from Kareem and were framed as his. Even the repeated-question misstep was handled collaboratively with acknowledgment and a pivot.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: explicitly honoring the quitting boundary, framing the plan as a no-pressure experiment, and reinforcing ownership. Kareem's relief and voluntary offer to return confirm choice was preserved and experienced as such.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits rather than supplies motivation—Kareem generated his own alternatives and success criteria. Thin on confidence and commitment: never asks how confident Kareem feels or explores what might get in the way.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located in Kareem, whose contributions visibly shaped the session's direction. When corrected about the repeated question, the clinician accepted the feedback gracefully and adjusted.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk, moving Kareem from 'I'm not gonna quit smoking' to considering the experiment and self-generated success criteria. Not a 5 because commitment language is never explicitly deepened or consolidated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently met with nonjudgmental reflection and gently redirected, including a textbook double-sided reflection. Not a 5 because some reflections dwell on the status quo's value with redirection that's occasionally implicit.
