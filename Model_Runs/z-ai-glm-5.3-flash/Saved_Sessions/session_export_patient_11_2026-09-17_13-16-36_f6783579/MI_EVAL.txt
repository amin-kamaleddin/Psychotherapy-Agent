# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations and normalization of small steps. However, affirmations were generic and formulaic rather than tied to Amira's specific strengths like her insight about past burnout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal — feelings like the pride sting from the jar and fear of burnout went unreflected, and advice came before understanding. No summaries of Amira's core motivations were offered.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on increasing physical activity throughout, producing a concrete, time-bound micro-goal with accountability. Kept on target without chasing tangential topics like nutrition.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Plans and protocols originated from the counselor with client input accepted only at the margins. Little 'we' language or joint problem-solving; Amira's own ideas about activities were never explored.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine choices were offered and Amira's decisions were respected, but mostly within counselor-defined menus. Her statement about not needing reminders and wanting to do it for herself went unaffirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied primarily on information-giving rather than drawing out Amira's motivations, which were largely self-generated. The confidence scaling question was the only clear evocative move, and it was partially client-requested.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Frequently assumed the expert role with prescriptive advice in nearly every turn. Repetitive closings like 'You've got this' functioned as cheerleading rather than genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira offered abundant change talk, but it was mostly client-generated rather than counselor-evoked. Opportunities to deepen motivation through reflections or exploring anticipated benefits were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was minimal, and the counselor avoided amplifying it, reframing barriers with 'one rep still counts.' A 5 would have required double-sided reflections actively pivoting hesitations toward her motivations.
