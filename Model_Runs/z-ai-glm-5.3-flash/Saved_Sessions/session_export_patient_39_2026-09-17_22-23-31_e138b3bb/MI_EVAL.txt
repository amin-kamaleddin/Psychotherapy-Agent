# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard from start to finish. Elias's fears of embarrassment and past failures were met with acceptance and normalization rather than judgment or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, progressive complex reflections captured both sides of ambivalence and named deeper fears (e.g., being 'the old man everyone watches struggle'). Reflections were emotionally attuned and used Elias's own words meaningfully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on activity and medication adherence while flexibly following Elias's lead. Each exchange advanced toward a concrete written plan (mailbox walk + pill with morning coffee, two weeks).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative partnership with shared agenda-setting and co-constructed plans. Elias's own ideas drove every decision, which he explicitly contrasted with 'another lecture I'd nod through.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently returned choice to Elias ('you tell me if it fits,' 'would you shape it differently?') with no pressuring or directive advice. His closing — 'something that's actually mine' — confirms autonomy support landed effectively.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully drew out Elias's own reasons, ability statements, and desires through open evocative questions ('what might make it possible?', 'how will you feel about yourself?'). No lecturing; motivation came entirely from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing: positioned Elias as the expert from the opening line and let his ideas (seated movement, mailbox plan) shape the session direction. Real impact on session outcomes.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evoked and deepened change talk, including anticipatory future framing that produced powerful self-motivational statements ('I want that beautiful feeling back'). Elias's commitment language ('I could probably promise you the mailbox') was amplified effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframed sustain talk as careful planning and proactively shrank goals to defuse fear of failure. Score withheld from 5 because a few reflections briefly restated the discouragement/chair imagery before pivoting, though never reinforced it.
