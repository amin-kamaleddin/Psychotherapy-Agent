# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment even when Dante admits repeated failed Mondays. Notable reframes include treating doubt as self-protection and affirming identity reclaiming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing ambivalence and the unspoken doubt beneath Dante's stammering. Reflections consistently go beyond content to underlying meaning, such as identity and self-trust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains unwavering focus on re-establishing exercise, progressing logically from barriers to a doable first step to obstacle planning. Each response moves the conversation forward, culminating in a concrete plan (Tuesday/Thursday, ten minutes, note, Maria).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative, using invitational language and co-constructing the plan from Dante's stated preferences. Dante's closure comment ('it's mine') confirms power-sharing was experienced as genuine rather than performative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently and explicitly supported—every element of the plan originated from Dante, framed as his choice. The closing 'it's yours to take—and I'll be here either way' reinforces ownership while removing pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from within the client, eliciting Dante's own reasons (his son, Maria, feeling like himself) and problem-solving. His spontaneous 'want to, not just should' demonstrates evocation at its best.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positions Dante as the expert on his own life and repeatedly deflects credit back to him ('you built it today, not me'). Dante's own ideas became the substance of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions ('What would that first small win mean to you?') elicit Dante's strongest commitment language, which is then explicitly highlighted and reinforced. Consolidation at closure produces self-motivating statements he plans to write down.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is neither reinforced nor argued against; doubt is normalized and reframed as self-protection, then pivoted toward doability. The Wednesday 'old voice' is proactively addressed by having Dante generate his own coping strategies.
