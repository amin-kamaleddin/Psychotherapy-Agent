# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing setbacks ('slips are just information, not failure') and promising a nonjudgmental debrief. However, affirmations of Claudia's personal strengths (honesty, courage) were sparse, limiting warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was solid, accurately capturing her ambivalence about drinking and her nervousness and hope. Emotional exploration of the low-mood drinking and her fear that two glasses may not 'be enough to enjoy the night' remained limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently focused on alcohol reduction with every exchange moving toward concrete planning: specific goal (two drinks/night), strategies, Friday start date, and Sunday 7 pm follow-up. Excellent goal-directed structure without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborated well, actively soliciting Claudia's input on strategies, timing, and check-in format, which substantially shaped the plan. Slightly limited by a tendency to present pre-formed strategy menus rather than brainstorming jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Claudia's ownership, accepting her modest goal rather than pushing clinical guidelines and accommodating her preference for a brief, nonjudgmental debrief. Change was repeatedly framed as her choice throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Claudia's own motivations through open questions and confidence scaling, eliciting reasons like avoiding 'foggy, guilty' mornings. Evocation was intermittent, with stretches devoted to counselor-provided strategy suggestions instead of her own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Claudia as the expert, letting her contributions shape the plan (Sunday 7 pm check-in, debrief over accountability). Somewhat limited by strategy menus drawn from expert options that shifted expertise toward the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk via doability questions, confidence ratings, and asking what would raise her confidence from a 6 to a 7 or 8, drawing out strong statements like 'I'd like to believe I can do that.' Missed some opportunity to deepen resolution of her attachment to wine as 'the best part of my day.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not argue with sustain talk; when Claudia rejected the seven-drink goal, the counselor pivoted to exploring what felt doable instead. Her attachment to wine was acknowledged but could have been addressed with a stronger double-sided reflection.
