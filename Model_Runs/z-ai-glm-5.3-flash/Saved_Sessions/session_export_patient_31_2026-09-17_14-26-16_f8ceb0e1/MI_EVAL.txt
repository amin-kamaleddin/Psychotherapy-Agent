# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental responses to David's defensiveness and relapse normalization, but few explicit affirmations of his strengths or honesty, keeping it brief and formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections early on, but the session quickly shifts to question batteries and advice lists, leaving emotionally meaningful material (the evening ritual, medication fears, social smoking) unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking with a concrete follow-up plan, though direction came through structured advice rather than guiding David to shape his own goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-asking and option-picking show collaborative intent, but the counselor supplies most content in expert lists while David mostly reacts rather than co-constructing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice is verbally respected ('no lecture,' conditional framing, 'no judgment'), but pervasive recommendation lists pressure toward the counselor's agenda rather than actively fostering ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no effort to draw out David's own motivations, values, or concerns; nearly all change talk emerged spontaneously rather than through skillful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor rapidly assumes the expert role with repeated advice bundles, incorporating David's input only minimally and delivering solutions rather than drawing them out.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good use of the readiness ruler elicited useful change talk, but it was never deepened ('Why a 5 and not lower?'), and David's spontaneous curiosity about quitting was met with an information list rather than exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing, but it is never actively softened — David's repeated 'I still like smoking' gets no double-sided reflection, and plans fully protect the status quo.
