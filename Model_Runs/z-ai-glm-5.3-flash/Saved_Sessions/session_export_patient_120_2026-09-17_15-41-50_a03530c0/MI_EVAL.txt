# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental — the counselor neutralized the 'irresponsible' label and normalized early-pregnancy drinking without shaming. Falls short of a 5 because warmth sometimes felt procedural, moving to strategies rather than sitting with Sofia's guilt and fear.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Sofia's core ambivalence and the hurt of the 'irresponsible' label, but the 'you're torn' reflection was repeated nearly verbatim and deeper emotional layers (fear of harm already done, loneliness, identity loss) were not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol abstinence and the concrete weekend goal, with rulers meaningfully tracking progress. Lost points for early menu drift and a lack of explicit tying of the weekend plan back to the larger goal of protecting the baby throughout pregnancy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative — the counselor checked in before each step, and the role-play was genuinely joint, with Sofia actively shaping details. Menus dominated agenda-setting, however, and key ideas (mocktail, Dani, exit time, scripts) originated from the counselor rather than shared exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were consistently framed as Sofia's, with no pressure or coercion; she selected her own lines, timing, and plan. The dense menu format and pre-drafting the Dani text occasionally steered or preempted Sofia's own solutions, and ownership reinforcement was limited.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions successfully evoked Sofia's own readiness, confidence, and self-generated strategies, with strong closing change talk. Missed a 5 because the deeper 'why' — her maternal identity and wish to make the 'irresponsible' label obsolete — was never explicitly evoked as motivational fuel.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor frequently asked for Sofia's input, but session content was heavily counselor-generated through rapid-fire menus of strategies, scripts, and drafted texts. Sofia functioned more as a selecter of options than as the primary source of expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and 'what would feel most doable?' elicited concrete self-generated commitments and visible growth in Sofia's own change talk. Strongest statements ('I wanna be responsible for this thing', 'thirty seconds of awkward and then everyone moves on') were not reflected or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor never reinforced sustain talk and steered toward practical solutions, which helped. However, no double-sided reflections or exploration were used, and sustain talk about loneliness and identity ('I'm not gonna get another chance at being this age') went unaddressed.
