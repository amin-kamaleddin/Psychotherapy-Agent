# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains consistent unconditional positive regard, including gracefully handling a garbled message without judgment. The patient's autonomy and worth are affirmed throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the patient's meaning, e.g., reframing 'I need a minute, but I'm not leaving this' as her lifelong commitment spoken aloud. Deep understanding of her shutdown pattern and values is evident.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Each response keeps focus on the patient's goal of staying connected to family happiness during hard days. Questions systematically move from barriers to solutions to practice and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is explicitly framed as the patient's own ('the plan is entirely yours, and it fits you'). The clinician elicits rather than prescribes, using open questions at every turn.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient chooses whether to practice first or share the agreement with her husband, and the clinician supports whichever she selects. Ownership is repeatedly credited to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully drawn out and reinforced — her hope that 'the wall wouldn't build,' her imagined husband's response, and her commitment language are highlighted and deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician acts as a guide while the patient supplies all solutions: the body cue, the phrase, and the practice plan. A genuinely equal, side-by-side stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions like 'what do you imagine shifts between the two of you?' elicit rich preparatory and mobilizing change talk. The clinician then reflects and reinforces it effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (shutting down when stressed) is met with curiosity and reframed into actionable steps rather than argued against. Minimal sustain talk remained to soften, handled appropriately when it appeared.
