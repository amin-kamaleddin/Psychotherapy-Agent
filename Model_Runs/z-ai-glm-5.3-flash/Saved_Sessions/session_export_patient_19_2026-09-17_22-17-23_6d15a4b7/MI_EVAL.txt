# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, affirming both ambivalence and change talk without judgment. Validated self-protective humor and courageous statements alike with no labeling or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate deep reflections captured surface content and emotional undercurrents (shame, FOMO, embarrassment), including a well-placed double-sided reflection. Anya repeatedly confirmed the counselor understood her.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear, progressive focus on reducing gambling and building financial stability, moving from reflection to concrete same-day action. Tangents were gently redirected without forcing the agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way exchange where Anya's answers visibly drove each next step. The counselor co-explored and let her evaluate the plan's weaknesses herself, evidencing full power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Anya's ownership and choice, with no advice imposed. The counselor asked rather than told, and the client explicitly owned the plan ('Decision's made at home, not in the moment').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within the client, eliciting her own values (Egypt, mother's approval) and solutions. By the end, Anya articulated her own resilience narrative, showing motivation was evoked, not instilled.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Anya was repeatedly positioned as the expert on her situation, friendships, and self. Every strategy originated from her, with counselor questions letting her contributions shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled open questions evoked and deepened change talk, eliciting strong commitment language ('I can do one weekend') and even excitement. The counselor named and reinforced shifts such as the desire to save for a trip.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, using double-sided reflections and brief acknowledgment before redirecting toward values and solutions. Minor deduction: some reflections restated her fears directly, briefly sustaining focus on barriers before pivoting.
