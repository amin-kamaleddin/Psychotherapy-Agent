# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, supportive stance throughout, with no labeling of years of avoidance. However, warmth sometimes reads as formulaic and checklist-driven rather than fully individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening that accurately captures Hana's push-pull ambivalence. Reflections lack emotional depth at times, e.g., the shame of potential failure and years of accumulated guilt are not fully explored before pivoting to new questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps clear focus on exam avoidance and study behavior, moving logically from problem to values to a concrete action plan. Turns packing 3-4 questions at once dilute focus and occasionally redirect before themes are fully developed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed (time, task, reward, fallback all chosen by Hana) with strong two-way exchange. Rapid-fire question menus and counselor-drafted text messages tilt some exchanges toward counselor steering, with no explicit agenda negotiation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers choice ('explore first or strategies?', 'pick or tweak') and affirms Hana's agency in designing her plan. At times presses structure (e.g., demanding an exact time) and proposes rewards/fallbacks rather than letting the client generate them.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws motivation from within via rulers, values exploration, and strengths evocation, including a textbook confidence re-check with the one-point tweak question. Evocation is sometimes mechanical, and key change-talk moments (e.g., Hana's closing commitment) are not developed into deeper commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Hana as the expert by eliciting her own analysis of past success and inviting her to choose directions and plan elements. Falls short of 5 because menu options are sometimes rushed through and advice (fallback tricks, reward) is offered rather than co-discovered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evokes change talk using importance/confidence rulers, values questions, strengths evocation, and commitment deepening, producing strong client change talk and confidence rising from 4 to 6. Does not consistently deepen or affirm change talk when it emerges, notably Hana's closing 'I'm making that first step now.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without amplifying it and reframes a 'bad' session as progress. However, directly soliciting worst-fear content ('What's the worst thing you imagine happening?') expanded Hana's embarrassment narrative, and doubting language is not consistently softened or reframed.
