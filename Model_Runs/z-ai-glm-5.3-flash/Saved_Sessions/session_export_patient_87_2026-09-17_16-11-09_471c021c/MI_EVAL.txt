# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating Marco's honesty about 'performing' for past therapists and his jinxing worry. Praise was frequent but somewhat formulaic, preventing a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections early on, but the session shifted to advice and checklist questions, leaving key emotional disclosures ('performing' for therapists, fear of day-three failure) only briefly acknowledged rather than deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight, consistent focus on evening snack temptations with a concrete plan (kitchen setup, tea/show routine, backup cookie, alarm, Friday check-in). Every exchange advanced the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with menus of options and genuine co-construction of the plan. Advice-heavy numbered lists at times tilted expertise toward the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actions consistently framed as Marco's choice, and his rejections of options were accepted without pushback. Occasional nudging toward specific solutions kept it from a 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opening evocative question and confidence scaling drew out motivation, but the counselor mostly supplied solutions rather than continuing to evoke Marco's own ideas after the first turn.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated choices let Marco shape the plan and steer the session. The counselor still authored most specific strategies rather than asking Marco to generate them first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited meaningful change talk (kids as motivation, confidence scaling 6→7→8), but rarely deepened these moments with reflections and often supplied plan content himself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and not reinforced with generic reassurance, but no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots toward Marco's stated 'why' were used, diluting the ambivalence work.
