# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, affirming Lars's strengths and saying 'I believe you' despite his criminal history. However, warmth felt somewhat formulaic and checklist-like, with repeated scripted safety screeners diluting individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections showed basic perspective-taking, but reflective listening was limited overall. Rich emotional themes—fear of reoffending, guilt, shame about literacy, fatherhood—were largely left unexplored as the counselor moved quickly to practical advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on target behaviors and produced a concrete, structured plan with next steps (photos tonight, text Donna, boredom busters, code word). Minor weakness: focus narrowed quickly to task logistics at the expense of broader motivational work.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was largely co-constructed, with the page-by-page format, softened script wording, and code word emerging from mutual back-and-forth. Fell short because the counselor still dominated problem-solving content rather than first asking Lars to generate his own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices rather than directives, respected Lars's pacing, and incorporated his edits to the Donna script. Genuine support, though rapid-fire option menus and advice occasionally felt prescriptive rather than fully client-owned.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on pre-formatted options, checklists, and ready-made scripts rather than open questions to draw out Lars's motivations. Almost all change talk came spontaneously from Lars; values, hopes, and meaning were never explored—a major missed opportunity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked Lars's preferences and incorporated his choices, but often shifted into advice/expert mode, supplying tips, plans, and scripts. Lars's influence on the plan happened more by chance of his initiative than deliberate power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anchored some responses to Lars's substantial spontaneous change talk, but rarely paused to reflect, deepen, or amplify it with evocative questions. Change talk emerged mostly from Lars himself rather than systematic evocation or reinforcement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and gently paired change-oriented options with it, but used no explicit double-sided reflections or strategic pivots. Sustain talk about Friday risk and embarrassment was mostly bypassed rather than softened.
