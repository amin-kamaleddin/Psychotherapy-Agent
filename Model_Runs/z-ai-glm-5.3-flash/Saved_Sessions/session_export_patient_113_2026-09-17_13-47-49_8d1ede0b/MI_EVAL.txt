# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone despite Rowan's bluntness and resistance. However, acknowledgments are somewhat formulaic and quickly pivot to problem-solving rather than affirming Rowan as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Brief accurate reflections are present but shallow, and are frequently cut short by advice or option menus. Key emotional moments — like Rowan's realization about stroke risk — go unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session well-focused on the target behavior (charging and using the monitor) with no significant drift. Focus is maintained largely through information delivery rather than guiding Rowan's own exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers real choice through option menus and co-constructs the one-week trial, but slides into expert mode with ready-made scripts, logs, and reminders. Practical content originates mostly from the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A clear strength: repeatedly affirms Rowan's choice, supports renegotiation with his doctor, and uses no pressuring language. Falls short of 5 by relying on option menus rather than reinforcing Rowan's self-directed capacity in moments of doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Relies heavily on providing information rather than eliciting Rowan's own reasons and values. Self-generated motivators — like 'if it caught something bad, that'd be bad' — go unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and responds to Rowan's requests, but most substantive content comes from the counselor. Rowan's expertise about his own routine and preferences is not explicitly leveraged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges ('I guess it's not that hard,' 'one week, I'll try it') but as a byproduct of agenda-setting rather than strategic cultivation. The counselor never deepens or amplifies change talk when it appears.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not amplify or dwell on sustain talk, which is appropriate. However, redirections are superficial acknowledgments serving as preambles to advice rather than double-sided reflections linking resistance to emerging concerns.
