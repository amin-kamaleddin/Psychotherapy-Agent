### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on increasing activity throughout, building step by step from past experiences to a concrete behavioral plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Everything framed as the client's choice, with no imposed advice; even self-protective hedging is validated rather than pressured.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions evoked strong change talk, which was highlighted and consolidated in summaries leading to a self-generated action plan.
