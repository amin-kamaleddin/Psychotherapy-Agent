# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout: honored the 'evenings are mine' boundary, promised 'no lectures, ever,' and affirmed his honesty without judgment even when he said 'I like smoking.' Affirmations, while genuine, were occasionally frequent enough to risk feeling performative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, nuanced reflections captured the workday/evening distinction and reframed ambivalence in his own values ('taking back the workday from smokes you're not even enjoying'). Deeper emotional layers like partner friction and 'told you so' fear were acknowledged but not explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight focus on workday smoking from open to close, with each exchange building toward a concrete plan. Direction was highly efficient, though it left little room to explore stress or partner issues.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: the Monday start, informal pop-in check-in, and lunchtime smoke allowance all came from David, and the closing 'we figure out the next move together' reinforced equal partnership. Affirmations occasionally dominated rather than leaving room for self-evaluation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was repeatedly emphasized ('it's your call'), information was neutral, and the plan was entirely David's — 'Monday's plan is yours.' The counselor explicitly protected his evening-smoking boundary rather than pushing full cessation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every plan element — patch choice, success criteria, safety valve, start date — originated from David himself, with the counselor contributing only neutral information. Near-exemplary evocation of the client's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located in the client, and his contributions demonstrably steered the session (Kofi anecdote reframed his break-time fear; 'don't make it a whole thing' shaped the check-in). True power sharing throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions drew out David's own motivations and produced strong commitment language: 'Monday. Fresh start with the week,' and 'I'll grab the patches today.' He moved from resistant ('I like smoking') to committed action within one session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated and strategically contained rather than argued against ('You're not quitting; you're taking back the workday'). The break-time concern dissolved through David's own reasoning about Kofi's experience.
