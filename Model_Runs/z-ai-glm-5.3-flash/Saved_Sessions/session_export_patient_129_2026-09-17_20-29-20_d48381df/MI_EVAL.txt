### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental toward a guarded, resistant teen, including validating his frustration and affirming his disclosures without making them 'a big deal.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections (e.g., the trust cycle) captured both content and systemic dynamics; Tyler explicitly noted his words were repeatedly echoed back and reached insight as a result.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior despite repeated deflections, with every exchange advancing toward ambivalence resolution and a concrete next step ('talk to her this week').

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly disclaimed the expert/adversarial role, asked rather than told at every juncture, and let Tyler's insights shape the session — his warming responses suggest the power-sharing felt authentic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice, persuasion, or pressure anywhere; Tyler's boundaries and non-negotiable conditions were accepted without pushback, and the final plan was entirely self-generated and self-conditional.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically sequenced evocative questions drew out Tyler's own reasons, values, and plan — culminating in his surprise at his own insight and a self-generated commitment, with no lecturing about drugs or honesty.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened by affirming client expertise ('You know your world better than anyone'), and all key insights, terms, and conditions originated from Tyler, with the counselor consistently positioning him as the authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled open questions and affirmations moved Tyler from precontemplation ('I'm fine') to desire, ability, and commitment language, ending with a concrete, self-imposed plan and timeline.
