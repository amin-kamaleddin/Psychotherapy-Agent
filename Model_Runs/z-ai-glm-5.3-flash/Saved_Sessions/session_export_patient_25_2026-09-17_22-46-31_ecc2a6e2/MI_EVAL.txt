# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; Daniel's minimization is accepted without confrontation, and his agency and past success are repeatedly affirmed. A failure-tolerant stance normalizes potential slips.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, deep reflections capture both content and underlying meaning, including identity as a dad and social dynamics. Reflections consistently track his smoking-cessation comparisons and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear, consistent focus on managing alcohol at social gatherings with progressive momentum from problem identification to contingency planning. Each turn advances the arc without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully equal, collaborative exchange; every plan element originates from Daniel while the counselor supplies structure and reflection. No expert-driven posture appears anywhere.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforces Daniel's ownership, never advises or pressures toward abstinence, and honors his choice not to cut out alcohol entirely. Framing the BBQ as his chosen test supports autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivations are drawn from Daniel himself through open questions rather than supplied externally. His reasons, ability talk, and commitment language expand steadily across the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniel is explicitly positioned as the expert, and all roadmap elements (soda water, telling his wife, the driving line) originate from him. The counselor builds each question directly on his prior statements.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is systematically evoked and reinforced, from desire/need talk through ability, commitment, and even excitement, which the counselor explicitly names and amplifies. Every turn reflects or invites more change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never amplified and is consistently redirected toward change, but the counselor misses chances to use explicit double-sided reflections to voice and resolve ambivalence. A minor gap that matters more with highly ambivalent clients.
