# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic respect and non-judgment are maintained, but warmth is generic and perfunctory with no genuine affirmations of Dante's strengths or past successes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; responses jump quickly to advice, and Dante's specific fears (flaking, quitting on his wife, embarrassment) go unreflected and unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on re-establishing physical activity, culminating in a highly specific plan (5-minute walk, Saturday 10 AM, cues, contingency plans, follow-up check-in) with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely unilateral, with the counselor supplying strategies while Dante's role is mostly to accept and comply; only the 'experiment' framing and time adjustment show collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice is offered and Dante's adjustments accepted, but rapid-fire prescriptive advice and subtle pressure toward commitment create a controlling tone rather than reinforcing ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is supplied through technique rather than drawn from the client; Dante's own motivations (wife's encouragement, past routine, feeling good about a plan) emerge spontaneously and are left unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** An expert-driven stance dominates, with prescriptive instructions in nearly every turn; Dante's own ideas surface only because he volunteers them, not because the counselor integrates them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effective evocation drew out change talk ('maybe a short walk,' 'Saturday, 10, five minutes'), but the counselor fails to deepen or reflect Dante's own desire and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about flaking is never reinforced and the 'lower the stakes' reframing moved Dante forward, but doubts are bypassed rather than softened through double-sided reflections.
