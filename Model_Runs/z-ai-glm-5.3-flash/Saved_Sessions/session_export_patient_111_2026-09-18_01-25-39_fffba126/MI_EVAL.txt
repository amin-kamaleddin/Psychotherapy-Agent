# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard despite Richard's initial hostility, never expressing disapproval or judgment. Warmth remained genuine and specific even at moments of resistance, though praise occasionally bordered on effusive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, capturing both content and underlying emotion (e.g., 'That's you hearing her heart, not her nagging'). Reflections tracked Richard's ambivalence and both sides of his conflict without distortion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained exceptionally clear focus on smoking behavior throughout, guiding Richard progressively from external compliance to self-generated change commitments. Direction was achieved through strategic questions rather than directives.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange between equals, with the psychiatrist soliciting Richard's input before drawing conclusions and co-constructing rather than proposing plans. Richard's own words ('doesn't feel as much like being railroaded') confirm the collaborative tone landed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and sustained autonomy support: 'you set the pace here,' 'I'm not here to convince you of anything,' never prescribing a quit method. Richard's boundaries were honored immediately, and the takeaway was framed entirely as his choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary evocation — every substantive forward movement was elicited rather than supplied, culminating in Richard's own powerful statement ('Just me, around, for longer'). The psychiatrist never lectured, argued, or provided reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively located expertise within the client ('you know your own routine best'), and Richard's contributions substantially redirected the session, including the condition that Linda 'back off.' The closing question handed full authorship of the outcome to Richard.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk from a pre-contemplative, externally motivated client — a genuinely difficult task executed at a high level. Strategic questions and amplifying reflections produced Richard's key commitment language ('me, around, for longer').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely avoided reinforcing sustain talk and rolled with resistance effectively, pivoting gently away from minimization and immediately defusing the 'if' correction. Richard's doubt about patches based on Dave's experience was acknowledged but left unreframed — the only gap preventing a perfect score.
