# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and non-judgment, with repeated affirmations, though praise was somewhat formulaic and repeated across turns rather than individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections of Mai's frustration and unease, but empathic responding declined later as the session shifted to advice-giving, leaving emotional content underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on statin understanding, adherence, and appointment preparation throughout, with only minor drift beyond counseling scope.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative menuing and role-play, but the counselor often slipped into directive expert mode, supplying lists and polished scripts rather than co-constructing from Mai's own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were framed explicitly and options offered repeatedly, though frequent unsolicited tips and advice slightly presumed counselor recommendations should be adopted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little deliberate evocation of Mai's own motivations, values, or confidence; the session relied on counselor-supplied content, with the role-play being the main genuinely evocative moment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shared decision-making was limited; the counselor generated most content and polished versions of Mai's ideas, positioning itself as the language expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mai's spontaneous change talk was affirmed but rarely deepened; few open questions or confidence-scaling techniques were used to deliberately evoke and summarize change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, but no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots were used to actively soften ambivalence.
