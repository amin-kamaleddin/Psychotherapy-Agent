# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic respect and nonjudgment present, with reassuring normalization of slips, but warmth feels formulaic and acceptance seems conditional on plan compliance, with little explicit affirmation of strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal; responses jumped to practical suggestions, and key emotional disclosures (fear of judgment, protectiveness of her son) went unreflected and unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Focus stayed consistent on childcare and substance use, progressing to a concrete plan and scheduled follow-up without drift, though achieved through directive task lists rather than collaborative goal-setting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The clinician acts as an advice-dispenser with multi-step plans each turn; input from Daniela occurs only when she pushes back, and few open questions invite her ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Occasional offers of choice, but the dominant mode is prescriptive (specific goals, drink caps, scripts), with the client having to negotiate down targets herself; autonomy is acknowledged superficially.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reasons and solutions were provided rather than drawn out; Daniela's own motivation ('my son deserves better') emerged spontaneously and was never developed, with almost no evocative or confidence questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly assumed the expert role with prescriptive step lists; the client had to initiate collaborative elements herself, positioning expertise clearly with the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk was elicited via goal questions (e.g., 'maybe start with one less cigarette a day'), but it was not deepened with evocative questions about meaning or vision of success; plans were supplied instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about trust and difficulty was bypassed with next steps rather than explored or reframed; no amplification or double-sided reflections, but also no reinforcement of the negative.
