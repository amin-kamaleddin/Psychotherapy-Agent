# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with genuine, specific affirmations and no judgment despite the patient's weight regain.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured the patient's frustration and mixed emotions, but reflections stayed mostly surface-level without deeper exploration of the emotional meaning of regain and the lost morning ritual.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on juice consumption, progressively moving from exploration to a concrete, measurable plan with a scheduled follow-up check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely equal partnership with the patient generating his own plan, though occasional advice-giving and a provided script without asking permission introduced expert-driven moments.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was regularly emphasized and the patient made all key decisions, though some option menus and a suggested target leaned toward persuasiveness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly drew motivation from the patient via open questions eliciting his insight, plan, and confidence rating, but could have deepened exploration of the meaning of past success and consolidated commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The patient was treated as the expert and created nearly the entire plan himself, though advice-giving without permission slightly tempered full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own change language and reinforced his volunteered confidence rating of 10, but commitment language was never explicitly evoked or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged the patient's love of OJ and promptly redirected toward actionable options without amplifying sustain talk, though a double-sided reflection linking sustain talk to values was not used.
