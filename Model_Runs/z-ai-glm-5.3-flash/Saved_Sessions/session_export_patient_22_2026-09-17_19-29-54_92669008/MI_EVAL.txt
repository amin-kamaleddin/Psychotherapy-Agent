# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard is conveyed from opening to closing, including explicit normalization of relapse and failure. The client confirms the nonjudgmental stance ('thanks for not making this feel like a punishment').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, succinct complex reflections capture ambivalence and emotional nuance throughout, and are consistently paired with open questions. The client repeatedly validates feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains tight focus on reducing alcohol use, with each exchange building purposefully toward a concrete, time-bound action plan. No topic drift occurs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plans are co-constructed from the client's own ideas, with the psychiatrist acting as a thinking partner. The client sets terms, requests accountability, and leaves with ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are offered at every decision point and the harm-reduction goal is accepted without pressure toward abstinence. The client's closing ('don't wish me luck, I've got this') reflects full ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation, solutions, and commitment language are elicited from the client rather than supplied—the hallmark of expert evocation. Self-assessed importance (80/100) and a voluntary accountability request emerge naturally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is explicitly positioned as the expert on her own life, and every strategy (spin class, Dani, the cover line) comes from her. Power-sharing is seamless and confirmed by the client's own language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoke and deepen client-generated reasons, plans, and commitment, with change talk growing progressively stronger across the session. The psychiatrist reinforces momentum without supplying the motivation herself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (fear of social loss, labels, resolve going 'fuzzy') is acknowledged briefly and pivoted toward change via double-sided reflections and problem-solving. No sustain talk is reinforced.
