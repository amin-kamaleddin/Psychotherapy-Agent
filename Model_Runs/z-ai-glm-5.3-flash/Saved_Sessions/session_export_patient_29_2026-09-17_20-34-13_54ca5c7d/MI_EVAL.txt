# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout ('There's no judgment here'), with client explicitly reporting feeling unjudged. Ambivalence was consistently normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captured underlying meaning, including reframing smoking as 'belonging.' Client confirmed key reflections ('squeezed is the right word').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation while weaving in medication, triggers, and social situations. Session advanced logically to a concrete action plan with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance with a co-constructed plan ('we can keep meeting here to build your plan'). Client's own ideas substantially shaped the session trajectory.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client choice and ownership regularly supported, and the plan was framed as a suggestion. Slightly limited by less explicit presentation of the full menu of options and deferring medication discussion to the medical team rather than co-exploring it in depth.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from the client himself via the future-vision question and trigger-strategy inquiry. Client's closing commitment language ('I'm in') demonstrates successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client positioned as the expert ('You know your triggers better than anyone'), with his ideas forming the substance of the plan. Counselor explicitly credited the client's own insights.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk through the six-month vision question and affirmed client-generated solutions. Session ended with strong commitment language ('Book me in... strike while this feeling lasts').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without reinforcement, then pivoted toward change ('What would fits my life look like for you?'). Ambivalence was held with double-sided reflections rather than dwelling on barriers.
