# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations of Milan's honesty and no moralizing, but acceptance occasionally feels formulaic and lacks deeper warmth or normalization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track Milan's perspective and name his ambivalence well, but remain somewhat surface-level without exploring the emotional weight beneath his minimization.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, clear focus on the target behavior with purposeful progression from exploration to a concrete, client-owned action plan; no topic drift despite deflection.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative—asks permission, offers choices, and adapts the text to Milan's voice—but the strategy menu was counselor-generated, with Milan selecting rather than co-creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly and repeatedly reinforced at every step ('It's your choice,' 'It's your call'), with no pressure to quit and a transparent, respectful confidentiality boundary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly draws motivation from within via open questions, a confidence ruler, and client-selected commitments, but the expert-supplied strategy menu and injected external risks slightly undercut pure evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Milan's expertise as central, adopting his code word and language, but occasionally shifts to an advice-driven expert mode before fully exploring his own solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked genuine preparatory change talk ('I'd probably listen,' 'that's like an 8'), though change talk centered on harm-reduction steps rather than deeper values, and advice came quickly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk met with neutral, accepting reflections rather than argument, but some reflections restated sustain talk verbatim without strategic double-sided pivots toward change.
