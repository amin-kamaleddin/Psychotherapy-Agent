# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, explicitly welcoming even avoidant behaviors and hard disclosures ('there's nothing you could tell me that would change how much I respect what you did today'). No judgment, labeling, or disapproval appeared anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were consistently accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing Sofia's exhaustion, ambivalence, and hidden care for Maya. The counselor caught subtle content, such as naming her protection of Maya as 'real care on your part,' which Sofia implicitly confirmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a clear focus on peer conflict and school avoidance, progressively building from vague complaints to a concrete change step with a scheduled check-in. Direction felt collaborative rather than forced throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session unfolded as a fully equal partnership — the text-to-Maya plan, its safeguards, and the accountability structure all originated from Sofia. Her comment that the counselor was 'just letting me figure out my own stuff' confirms the power-sharing was genuinely experienced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor reinforced Sofia's ownership and choice at every turn and honored her confidentiality request regarding her mother ('Deal'). There was no pressure, persuasion, or directive language at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation emerged from Sofia herself through evocative questioning ('what would you tell yourself to feel ready to send it?'). Her closing remark that 'probably' was 'the most optimistic I've been in months' confirms motivation was elicited, not imposed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly located expertise within Sofia ('you know her better than anyone here does') and never prescribed a solution. Sofia explicitly noticed this stance, contrasting it with how everyone at school treats her as 'the problem.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoked Sofia's own reasons and desires for change, which the counselor then amplified and affirmed ('You came up with this whole plan. That was you'). This culminated in a self-generated commitment with a built-in accountability check.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was effectively softened with double-sided reflections that paired it with change talk, and it diminished across the session. The only minor gap was occasionally restating sustain talk (e.g., skipping school) without an immediate pivot toward change.
