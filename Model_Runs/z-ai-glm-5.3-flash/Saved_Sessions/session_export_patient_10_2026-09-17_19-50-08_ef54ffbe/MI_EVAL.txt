# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout; missed interview framed as a logistical problem, not personal failure. Warm, individualized affirmations ('That's your own idea') and validation of protective skepticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture ambivalence ('it's exhausting to be torn like that') and emotional weight of past setbacks. Amina confirms understanding ('Exactly—you get it'). 

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive focus on childcare barriers: backup plan → first step → clarifying ask → future vision → between-session strategies. Momentum builds without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Work explicitly framed as joint ('let's map this out together'); fairness structure and timeline co-constructed from client's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership consistently reinforced ('completely in your control', 'what feels possible to you'). Advice framed as tentative options; client's preference to not rehearse accepted. Plan is 'mine, not perfect.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly all substantive ideas (playground swap, emergency-only framing, texting sister) come from Amina via evocative open questions. Never argues for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in client ('You know your situation and your kids better than anyone'). Plan built entirely from Amina's contributions rather than substituted professional solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions elicit desire, ability, and commitment language ('Maybe I'm more willing than I thought', 'I'd book into every interview'). Motivation attributed to the client and anchored into concrete action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubt talk acknowledged without reinforcement, met with double-sided reflections and reframing failure as practice. Minor deduction for occasionally re-engaging doubt talk in detail (2am planning), though this arguably builds resilience.
