# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, validating bluntness and resistance without judgment ('That makes sense to me'). Affirmations are specific and genuine, even when Rowan admits avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections capture both surface content and emotional core, e.g., reflecting frustration at being pushed and the ambivalence between feeling fine and noticing 'off' days. Reflections track Rowan's evolving stance from refusal to commitment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior from start to finish, guiding progressively from ambivalence through information to a concrete, achievable first step ('Tonight') while letting Rowan set the sub-agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates between equal partners with inclusive language ('We could start there, together') and genuine agenda negotiation. The plan was co-constructed entirely from Rowan's own routines and past successes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Rowan's ownership ('no one else gets to decide how you handle this'), and even the psychoeducation stayed within his chosen question. Rowan's closing insight — 'Feels different deciding something myself' — is direct evidence this succeeded.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws all motivation from Rowan himself via questions like 'what would need to be true for this to feel worth your time?' and 'what's worked for you before?'. Every key shift — curiosity, the gym-routine plan, 7/10 confidence, 'Tonight' — was self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Rowan as the expert throughout, and his contributions demonstrably shaped the session's direction and plan. Textbook power-sharing, with ideas repeatedly credited to Rowan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically evokes and amplifies change talk, from the first opening ('if I understood what it actually does') through a 7/10 confidence rating and commitment ('Tonight even, probably'). Consistently highlights Rowan's own shifts as his honest weighing, not external pressure.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk, pivoting toward change and reframing worries about flaking into strengths. Minor deduction: the 'what happens if you don't' segment relied on clinician-provided information rather than eliciting Rowan's own reflections, though it was permission-based and well-timed.
