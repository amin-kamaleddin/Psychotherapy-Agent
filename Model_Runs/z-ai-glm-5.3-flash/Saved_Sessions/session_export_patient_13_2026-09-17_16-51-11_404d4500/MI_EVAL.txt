# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth and unconditional positive regard are consistent throughout ('You're doing a brave thing'), with no criticism of her avoidance and fear normalized. Her request to add 'it's okay to be scared' was honored without judgment, and accountability was offered gently, preserving dignity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflections captured core fears ('You're afraid seeing a doctor will make a feared outcome real') and her need for accountability. However, reflections were somewhat brief and did not deeply explore her stated fear of handling bad news alone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every exchange advanced the target behavior of making a medical appointment, culminating in a concrete plan (Thursday 2 pm, 1:50 reminder, 3:00 check-in). Direction was firm yet pressure-free.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The tone was joint, and Anara's ideas (phone appointment, check-in, card wording) were integrated into the plan. Slight deduction because the counselor supplied most concrete content (scripts, cues, timing) rather than eliciting her own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were offered consistently (telehealth vs. home visit vs. rehearsal), her limits were honored immediately ('No more rehearsal'), and she designed her own accountability. Change was framed entirely as her choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions drew out some of her own reasons ('I'd finally know instead of lying awake wondering'), but the approach leaned heavily on supplying scripts, plans, and solutions. Elicitation of her motivations and internal resources was effective but not sustained.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anara was positioned as the decision-maker, and her contributions directly shaped the plan (Thursday 2 pm, phone visit, reminders). Deducted because the scripts and concrete plans were counselor-generated rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Readiness and confidence rulers skillfully elicited movement (3 → 5), and commitment language emerged ('Thursday at two. I'll do it'). Deducted because evocation relied on rulers and scripts rather than deeper exploration of her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('maybe it'll just go away on its own') was acknowledged but not amplified or strategically reflected. Some ambivalence was met with practical problem-solving rather than reflection and gentle reframing.
