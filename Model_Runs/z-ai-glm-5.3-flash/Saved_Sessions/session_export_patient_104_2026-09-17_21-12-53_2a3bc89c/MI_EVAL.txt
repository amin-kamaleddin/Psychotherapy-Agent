# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout; no judgment even when the patient expresses resentment or guilt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures ambivalence and deeper meaning; reflections consistently land and the patient builds on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive arc from clarifying the baby's development to maternal sleep deprivation to a concrete, self-authored plan; no aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Work framed as joint from the opening line; the plan is genuinely co-constructed and the patient feels less alone with it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice consistently returned to the patient, advice offered only when requested, and plan authorship explicitly credited to her at the close.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within via vivid evocative questions; nearly all change language originates with the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient positioned as the expert, with her ideas shaping the session's direction; counselor offers ideas only when asked and hands choice back.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilfully evokes and deepens change talk through pivotal and future-oriented questions, reinforced with strategic summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally pivots effectively away from sustain talk, though occasionally echoes doubt-adjacent content about the partner rather than actively reframing it.
