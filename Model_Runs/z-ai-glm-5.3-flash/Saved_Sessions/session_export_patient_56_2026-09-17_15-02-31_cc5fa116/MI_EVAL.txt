# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with consistent affirmations, though some were brief and formulaic, with limited explicit unconditional regard around potential setbacks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Isolde's values and barriers well, but exploration stayed cognitive and plan-focused rather than delving into emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior throughout; every turn advanced the plan coherently toward a specific, actionable commitment with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan was substantially co-constructed from Isolde's ideas, though early planning turns featured a counselor-generated step list that tilted the balance toward the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were consistently offered and Isolde's decisions honored, but initial suggestions were presented as a pre-packaged list before fully exploring her own ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was effectively drawn from within via scaling questions and values exploration, though questions often stayed at the logistics level rather than deeper emotional significance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Isolde was positioned as the expert with plans built from her contributions, but the counselor occasionally moved quickly into structured plan-giving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful and consistent evocation of change talk through scaling questions (7 to 9), definitional questions, and commitment/ownership prompts that elicited strong client commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** No amplification of sustain talk in a low-ambivalence case, with barriers redirected to solutions; slight deduction for repeated 'what might get in the way' questions inviting obstacle focus.
