# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard; Sofia explicitly thanked the counselor for not making her feel like a failure over past diets.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening tracked Sofia's ambivalence and self-compassion shift, though reflections sometimes summarized content rather than deepening the emotional core of shame and fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight, consistent focus with a concrete, time-anchored plan including fallbacks, reminders, success indicators, and a midweek check-in; emotional eating was appropriately deferred without dismissal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Sofia selecting and refining every element and shaping next session's agenda; no expert-driven prescription anywhere.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Options were offered rather than prescribed, Sofia's declines were respected, and her closing 'small and mine' confirms genuine autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation via confidence rulers, obstacles, and success-sign questions, but the counselor sometimes supplied strategy menus instead of first inviting Sofia to generate her own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Active power sharing throughout — Sofia's own ideas became the plan, she chose wording and celebrations herself, and declined playlist help without pushback.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful and consistent change talk cultivation via small-step questions, confidence scales (twice), success indicators, and celebration invitations; Sofia's change talk was abundant and grew stronger, culminating in 'real ready' and hope.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers were acknowledged and pivoted to solutions without dwelling, but summaries sometimes repeated barrier language and the diet-failure history was never reframed as useful learning.
