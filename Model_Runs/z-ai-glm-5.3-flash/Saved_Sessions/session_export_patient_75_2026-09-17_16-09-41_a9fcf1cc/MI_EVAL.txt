# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout with normalization of Linda's fears and no criticism of skipped doses. Falls short of 5 due to brief, formulaic affirmations rather than explicit affirmations of her strengths like her health advocacy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections captured Linda's ambivalence about medication well. Empathy sometimes stayed cognitive, missing deeper emotional layers like guilt about snapping at her husband and her 2am-googling anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on asthma management and inhaler adherence with every turn tied to the target behavior. Session progressed logically from exploring ambivalence to concrete next steps without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a partner, jointly selecting next steps and co-creating scripts, with Linda's ideas driving the plan. Some exchanges were structured by the counselor's menus of options and expert content rather than fully emerging from Linda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: every step framed as optional, permission-seeking throughout, choices repeatedly handed back to Linda, and no imposed advice. Linda's own words confirm a strengthened sense of agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions drew Linda's own reasons and hopes rather than persuading her. Moving quickly to offering options limited deeper elaboration of motivation and confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently asked permission, positioned Linda as decision-maker, and provided adaptable scripts; her choices shaped the session agenda. The steroid-risk information was delivered as an expert-driven monologue rather than integrated with her own research.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk via targeted questions, choice of steps, and role-play that produced mobilizing language. Limited deepening of confidence talk and no explicit summary consolidating her change statements kept it from a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk; concerns were acknowledged briefly and redirected to information and manageable steps, with second-guessing normalized. A 5 would require more strategic double-sided reflections pairing her concerns with her own desires for change.
