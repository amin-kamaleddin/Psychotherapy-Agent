# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout; ambivalence normalized and no criticism of smoking. Affirmations were somewhat generic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured Hana's ambivalence well. Emotional content ('more worrying me than I admit') was reflected but not explored more deeply, and structured questions sometimes crowded out emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on smoking from the start with no topic drift, progressively narrowing to a concrete, measurable plan. Converted vague contemplation into a defined experiment with accountability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was built from Hana's own ideas and co-drafted materials. Occasional slips into expert mode with rapid strategy lists and pre-formulated texts outpaced her readiness.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Hana's choice with option menus, explicit 'whatever you choose' framing, and permission-seeking before drafting texts. Her closing 'less preachy' comment confirms ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Core method was eliciting Hana's own motivations and solutions, with visible motivation growth in-session. Occasionally pre-filled space with suggested answers and menus, reducing purely client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana was positioned as the expert and her contributions genuinely shaped the session and plan. Stacked multi-part questions sometimes created an agenda-driven, checklist feel.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk via open questions and readiness/confidence rulers, moving Hana from 4-5 to 6 and eliciting commitment language. Culminated in Hana's own self-generated one-sentence plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without amplifying it and kept focus on worries and values. Did not use strategic double-sided reflections to actively resolve ambivalence, mostly sidestepping rather than pivoting.
