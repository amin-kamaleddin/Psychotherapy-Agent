# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard — Yasmin's wish for weight loss is met with affirmation, and imperfection is pre-emptively granted permission ('every version of your week is allowed in here'). Affirmations are specific and grounded in her own words, with no judgment anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with double-sided reflections ('the exhaustion AND the persistence') and accurate tracking of her shift to concrete planning. Reflections occasionally lean toward reframing rather than deep emotional mirroring — the shame around treating her body 'like the enemy' is validated but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, focused trajectory from what didn't work to livable alternatives, barriers, supports, and concrete first steps without drifting. Even the weight-loss tangent is acknowledged and folded back into the shared agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed: Yasmin designs the gym invite and noticing practice, negotiates the check-in format, and the clinician accepts her terms. The tone is consistently 'we,' with no claims of superior knowledge about her life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are framed as Yasmin's at every juncture, including honoring her pace on the weight-loss question. Her request to report setbacks 'without it counting as failure' is granted unconditionally, and no advice is imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and insights are elicited rather than instilled — the self-kindness-as-skill idea, her solutions (sister as anchor), and her resolution of ambivalence all originate from her. No persuasive arguments for change are offered at any point.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician explicitly positions Yasmin as the expert on her own life, and the session's takeaway is her self-generated insight ('maybe I was just going about it all wrong'). When she requests an accountability structure, he adopts hers rather than substituting his own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open-ended evocative questions consistently draw high-quality change talk ('working with myself instead of against myself'), which is then reflected and amplified. Her request for accountability is converted into client-owned commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (weight-loss ambivalence, all-or-nothing thinking) is neither argued with nor amplified — the response to the weight-loss wish is exemplary. Barrier language ('the mean voice gets loud') is repeated in several closing reflections, slightly re-centering the obstacle rather than fully pivoting to her capability.
