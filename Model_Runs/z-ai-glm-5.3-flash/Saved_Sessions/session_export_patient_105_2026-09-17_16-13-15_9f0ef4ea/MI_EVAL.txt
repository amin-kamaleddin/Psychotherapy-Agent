# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with individualized affirmations and validation of ambivalence; some affirmations feel formulaic and repeated, limiting explicit unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes accurate reflections of Naomi's physical symptoms and avoidance, but often bypasses emotional depth in favor of problem-solving; vulnerable disclosures are met with tactics rather than reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear and consistent focus on managing social anxiety during shoots; every exchange advances a concrete plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi's choices shape the plan and decisions are ratified with her, but the counselor's stacked questions and pre-packaged options position the counselor as plan designer more than equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and permits declining, but the overall tenor is directive; Naomi chooses among counselor-generated options rather than authoring her own change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits useful insights via scaling and open questions, but relies heavily on this single tool and fills space with advice rather than exploring Naomi's deeper values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi's preferences genuinely shape the plan, but the counselor's expert, checklist-driven style with rapid-fire questions limits true power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective scaling, open questions, and reinforcement helped confidence rise from 3 to 7 with genuine commitment language; much change talk is counselor-prompted rather than organic.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not amplified and uncertainty is briefly validated, but responses jump to solutions instead of using double-sided reflections to explore and resolve ambivalence.
