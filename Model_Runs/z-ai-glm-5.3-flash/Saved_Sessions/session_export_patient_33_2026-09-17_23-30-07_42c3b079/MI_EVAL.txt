# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard was maintained throughout, from 'there's no judgment in this room' to validating the courage it took to attend. Shame around past judgment was met with reframing rather than criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections captured both content and meaning, e.g., heroin as escape from unbearable noise and the double-sided acknowledgment of ambivalence. Vulnerable material the patient only partially disclosed was accurately reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on heroin use and harm reduction, progressing logically to a concrete, time-bound plan. Off-topic threads were acknowledged and woven back into the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Information was shared with permission, and the plan was built entirely from the patient's own words and choices. The check-in arrangement was co-negotiated to the patient's exact specification.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was affirmed explicitly, options were offered rather than directives, and 'I'm not there yet' regarding the clinic was accepted without pressure. The patient's ownership ('they're mine,' 'locked in') confirms the stance landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from the patient via open questions about his own reasons, ideas, and confidence. His 10/10 readiness, safety steps, and request for accountability were all self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient was positioned as the expert on his own life, with his priorities (no judgment, no records) directly shaping recommendations. Options were presented for him to judge for himself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was actively evoked through open questions and reinforced when offered ('10 out of 10 ready'). Commitment was deepened by having the patient verbalize the plan aloud with a specific day and time.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and reframed toward the patient's own concerns and strengths. Barriers were met with redirection to what felt within reach rather than argument.
