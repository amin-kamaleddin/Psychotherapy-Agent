# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, honoring imperfection and embracing skepticism without pushback. No lecturing, labeling, or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured both surface content and deeper meaning, including Lucía's exact language. Reflections consistently resonated and prompted further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear progressive arc from exploring smoking's role to a concrete, client-owned first step and follow-up plan. Each exchange advanced the agenda while remaining patient-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction operated between equal partners, with Lucía's contributions fully shaping the session and the closing agreement negotiated collaboratively. Team-like tone without expert steering.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit, repeated reinforcement of Lucía's ownership of the plan, including affirming her 'that's my call' assertion. Slips reframed as part of her self-directed experiment, with no coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation—work breaks, her daughter, her mother's story—came from Lucía in response to open, evocative questions. The clinician never argued for quitting or supplied the motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within the client; the entire change plan was Lucía's creation. Her closing statement ('a plan I actually made myself') confirms successful power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions drew out substantial change talk, including a concrete plan and commitment to counting cigarettes. Change talk was then reinforced with affirmations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was largely avoided and ambivalence handled well with double-sided reflections and reframing. Minor re-voicing of sustain talk without an explicit softening pivot (e.g., after 'nothing would make it feel like enough') kept this from a 5.
