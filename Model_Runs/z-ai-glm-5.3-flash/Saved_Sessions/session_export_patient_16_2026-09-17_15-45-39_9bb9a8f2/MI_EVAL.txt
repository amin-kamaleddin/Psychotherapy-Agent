# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout; respected the client's boundaries and validated her plan without labeling or disapproval despite defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of her ambivalence, but emotional content like her fear of post-season relapse was met with a menu rather than deeper empathic exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained consistent focus on vaping and basketball values, progressing logically from goals to confidence rating to coping strategies, slip plan, backup plan, and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ideas and the plan were genuinely co-constructed using the client's own words ('State's > a pen'), with decisions made jointly via menus and feedback incorporated ('less cringe' version adopted).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Directives were offered as choices, her refusals were respected immediately, and her 'only for season' limit was honored, reinforcing ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rich client-generated change talk and ownership were elicited (own reasons, triggers, alternatives, phrasing), but longer-term values like college ball and health were left unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent power-sharing: asking permission, offering menus, inviting edits, and letting her choices shape the plan — the highest level of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent evocation and reinforcement of change talk with strong client self-generated statements, but commitment language beyond the season wasn't deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without arguing and reframed positively, but 'I'd probably go back to it' post-season was never actively softened or challenged, and a menu option reinforced status-quo reasoning.
