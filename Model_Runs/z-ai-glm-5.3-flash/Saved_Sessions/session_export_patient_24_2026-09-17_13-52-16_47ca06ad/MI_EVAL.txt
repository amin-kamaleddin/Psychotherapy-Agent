# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained a nonjudgmental, respectful stance ('this is about gathering facts, not judgment'), but affirmations of Daniel's honesty and strengths were rare and the repeated identical closings felt formulaic rather than warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal; emotionally significant statements like '18 sounds like a lot' and 'a kick in the teeth' went unreflected, and no complex reflections explored his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use with a concrete monitoring plan, but the agenda was prescriptive and closed abruptly without summarizing Daniel's own goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative moves (choosing tracking, joint log review), but the overall pattern was question-answer-instruct, and Daniel's ideas (e.g., linking long runs to the log) weren't incorporated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered genuine options and didn't push the break he rejected, but unprompted directive advice ('set an alarm titled Quick drink log') and repeated instruction restatements felt controlling — Daniel even noticed the 'sneaked' changes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to elicit Daniel's own motivations; his marathon identity — the richest source of intrinsic motivation — was never explored, and his closing openness ('I like a good marathon time more') was left untouched.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Honored Daniel's choice to track first, but shifted quickly into expert mode with a pre-packaged plan of detailed directives rather than co-constructing it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniel spontaneously voiced change talk ('maybe the weekend stuff adds up more than I realize') but the psychiatrist failed to reinforce or deepen it with reflections or evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with or confronting Daniel's skepticism ('I have a hard time believing it's causing a problem'), but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections that could have used it to develop discrepancy.
