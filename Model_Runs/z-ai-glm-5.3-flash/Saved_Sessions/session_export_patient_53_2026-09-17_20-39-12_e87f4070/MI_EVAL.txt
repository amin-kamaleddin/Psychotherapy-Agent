# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with strong affirmations ('real courage,' 'real insight'). Even avoidance behaviors were normalized and reframed respectfully without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured content and ambivalence well through double-sided reflections. Fell short of 5 because deeper emotions—the shame of past no-shows and social anxiety—were acknowledged but not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on community engagement from start to finish, moving deliberately from manageable options to a concrete action plan without rushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership in which the client's own solutions (art classes, solo browsing, two-week check-in) defined the plan. No expert-driven advice-giving; the counselor's skill showed in questioning rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Robust support for client choice with explicit options ('together or on your own?') and zero pressure, including acceptance of the right to hate everything found. The plan was framed as entirely hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every plan element was drawn from the client's own ideas and insights rather than supplied by the counselor. The 'even badly' question skillfully dismantled perfectionism and evoked organic change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the client as the expert ('you know yourself best'), and all plan elements originated from her. Slightly short of 5 because some affirmations centered the counselor's evaluative voice rather than fully returning ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled use of open questions to evoke desire and commitment language, with strategic reinforcement of each increment. The client progressed from 'yes, I guess' to concrete, self-generated commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledged sustain talk without amplifying it, using double-sided reflections and turning 'loose never works' into a strategy. Not a 5 because a few sustain statements received only brief reframing without deeper resolution of the underlying ambivalence.
