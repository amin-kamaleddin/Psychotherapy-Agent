# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and explicitly counteracted referral-based shame ('You deserve to be met with respect'). No judgment or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and emotion, including her ambivalence, the invisibility of holding a soda, and the shaky-but-good feeling. Both sides of her tension were consistently honored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on abstaining from alcohol during pregnancy and its social challenges, progressing from ambivalence to a specific first step and contingency planning. No aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—tasks framed as joint ('write those lines down together'), and every plan originated from Sofia's contributions. Counselor acted as an equal partner rather than an expert.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support: asked permission before writing the note, let Sofia set the next session's agenda, and honored her request for accountability as her choice. No advice imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from within the client—her strengths, her own words for the reminder, her definition of success, and existing coping resources. Sofia ended with self-generated commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise located in the client throughout ('you did it yourself, not me'), with questions drawing on Sofia's own resources. Her ideas drove every plan in the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation of change talk—highlighting her soda moment, deepening commitment language, and converting it into a lock-screen reminder. Amplified commitment by asking for a specific first step and timing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally effective reframing (e.g., 'Saturday-me is a coward' into evidence of courage), but sometimes explored sustain talk fairly extensively before redirecting. Tighter pivoting toward change talk would improve this.
