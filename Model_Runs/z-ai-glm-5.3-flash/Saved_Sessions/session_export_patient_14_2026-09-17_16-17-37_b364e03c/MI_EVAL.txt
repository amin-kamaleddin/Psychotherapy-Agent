### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful of Andrei's irritability and skepticism, validating his frustration about lost billable time without judgment. Warmth remained at professional courtesy rather than deeper personal connection.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected Andrei's practical concerns (tax season, time barriers) and matched his efficiency-driven style. Missed deeper emotional content such as the fear in 'huffing and puffing on my own staircase' and shame around failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every exchange stayed tied to the target behavior, translating vague intent into a concrete plan with schedules, cues, metrics, and a Friday check-in. The session never drifted despite client tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plans were co-constructed at each decision point, with Andrei's own expertise visibly shaping the plan, backup strategies, and pre-commitments.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, let Andrei pick metrics, alarm wording, times, and backup plans, and accepted refusals without pushback. The plan was framed as Andrei's own 'test' without controlling language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The readiness ruler moved Andrei from 3-4 to 5, and questions drew out his own reasons ('proof convinces me') and self-identified risk. Evocation was sometimes procedural, emphasizing logistics over deeper values or personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Andrei's expertise was positioned as central and his contributions genuinely shaped the plan. Menu-style options each turn felt slightly formulaic rather than open co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and built on change talk ('the weight is something I'd like to get hold of'), raising readiness from 3-4 to 5 and drawing out concrete commitments. Could have deepened exploration of why the stairs test mattered personally.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about time and skepticism was acknowledged briefly and pivoted to time-respecting micro-steps, avoiding argument. Did not explicitly use double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
