# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, accepting Camila's resistance to abstinence without criticism. Praise was somewhat formulaic with limited explicit normalization of setbacks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and emotion, including the hurt of friends' concern. Reflections could have dug deeper into loneliness and fear of being 'the boring one.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on weekday alcohol reduction, with every turn tied to the target behavior. Weekend concerns were acknowledged and appropriately deferred to keep the weeknight plan front and center.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila's own ideas became the plan, and the counselor asked permission before offering suggestions. However, frequent multi-question barrages made some exchanges feel interview-like rather than fully dialogic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Camila's ownership, honored her limit of not quitting weekends, and offered advice only as optional. Supported a self-compassionate slip plan respecting her control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within, eliciting Camila's own reasons, past successes, and coping ideas. Occasionally pre-supplied options (mocktail, scripts) before letting her explore her own first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Camila as the expert, with plan elements originating from her and suggestions offered permission-first. Some batched rapid questions slightly reduced space for shared agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk via open questions and rulers, with Camila generating her own commitment language. Commitment statements could have been deepened further before moving to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification, redirecting toward strengths and solutions. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections linking social fears to hangover exhaustion.
