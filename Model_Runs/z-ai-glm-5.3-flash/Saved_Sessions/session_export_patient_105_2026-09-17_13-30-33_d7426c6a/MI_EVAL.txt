# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental with some warm affirmations, but the opening rapid-fire intake questions overwhelmed the client ('that's a lot at once'). Pacing of engagement felt rushed and mechanical at times.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal; the counselor rarely reflected Naomi's feelings (e.g., her fear of the intro task) and instead pivoted quickly to advice after brief validation. Emotional meaning behind canceling gigs and her self-doubt went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on managing social anxiety at gigs, converging on a concrete plan (first 10 minutes, checklist) with a defined follow-up. Minor drift into extra problem-solving tips like caffeine reduction and CBT/med consults.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration — Naomi chose strategies, negotiated the checklist, and co-decided follow-up timing. However, the counselor supplied most content while Naomi reacted and selected, and the rapid-fire questioning felt authoritarian early on.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were consistently offered and the client's refusal of the intro script was accepted without pressure. Options were always counselor-generated menus rather than open exploration of what Naomi herself wanted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no questions elicited Naomi's own reasons, values, or confidence for change. Her motivation emerged spontaneously and was never deepened or reinforced.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing occurred (client choices, accepted declines), but the counselor largely adopted an expert stance with pre-packaged plans and checklists. Naomi shaped the session by choosing among counselor-generated options rather than co-creating strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged mostly unprompted from the client and was not deliberately evoked or deepened. No open questions probing what makes change worth trying, and little skillful reinforcement of her commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoided reinforcing sustain talk and responded with neutral normalization, but used no strategic double-sided reflections or reframes. Ambivalence was managed passively rather than actively pivoted toward change.
