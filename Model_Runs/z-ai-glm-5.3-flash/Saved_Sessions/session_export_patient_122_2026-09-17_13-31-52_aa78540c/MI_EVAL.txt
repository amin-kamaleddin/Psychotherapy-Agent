# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally respectful and nonjudgmental with affirmations like "Amazing — that's a real win." However, warmth feels formulaic and tied to checklists, with little normalization of Sofia's discouragement or unconditional acceptance regardless of outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; the counselor relies almost exclusively on questions, instructions, and checklists. Sofia's fear of failure, self-criticism, and stress triggers were acknowledged briefly at best but never deeply explored or reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (energy, movement, evening snacking) and appropriately narrows to a single walking habit with concrete action steps and check-in times. The initial 5-item plan was too broad and overwhelming, but direction was consistent once narrowed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks for input and adjusts when Sofia resists, but the overall flow is counselor-directed — plans are designed by the counselor and merely approved or amended by Sofia. True co-creation (exploring what she has tried and what she thinks would help) is absent.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices and honors Sofia's rejections of suggestions, and her autonomy is visibly operative in the final plan. However, options are pre-packaged menus rather than elicited from Sofia, and advice is frequently front-loaded.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely draws out Sofia's own motivations or solutions; her change reasons (energy, pride) surfaced largely on her own without exploration or amplification. A golden opportunity was missed when her question "What would you start with if you were me?" was answered with advice rather than turned back to her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasionally shares power and honors Sofia's pushback on pre-portioning and tracking, but the dominant stance is expert-driven. Sofia had to actively resist elements of the plan to regain collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged ("Ten minutes in the morning sounds doable," "Tomorrow") and was reinforced, but was never deliberately evoked with questions about why change matters or what makes it feel doable. The confidence check was dropped when Sofia pushed back, with no commitment-strengthening follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced, and the plan was appropriately scaled back when Sofia felt overwhelmed. However, opportunities for double-sided reflections connecting her doubts to her strengths were missed, and reassurance was generic rather than helping her reframe perfectionism.
