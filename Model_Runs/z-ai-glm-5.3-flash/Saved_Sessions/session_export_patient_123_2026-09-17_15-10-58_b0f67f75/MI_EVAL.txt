# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone even during pushback, with no disapproval expressed about alcohol use. However, warmth felt somewhat formulaic and unconditional positive regard was not deeply conveyed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made consistent efforts to reflect Sofia's statements, but reflections remained surface-level and repetitive. Missed opportunities to explore emotional depth, such as her frustration at being singled out or discomfort with deep thinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session lacked focus on the target behavior, spending most of its time on hobbies and books until Sofia herself raised the screening late in the session. Once alcohol was broached, the counselor did not steer back when Sofia deflected.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided an authoritarian stance and offered options collaboratively, but Sofia effectively controlled the session's direction and premature ending. The counselor did not negotiate a shared agenda or jointly explore the school referral concern.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A clear strength: repeatedly offered choices, respected Sofia's pacing, and presented options without pressure. However, autonomy support occasionally tipped into passivity, following Sofia away from the target behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Almost no effort was made to draw out Sofia's own motivations, values, or ambivalence. The single attempt to link the book's themes to her life was deflected and not revisited, leaving intrinsic motivation never elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used inclusive language and offered Sofia choices about session direction, but her contributions were incorporated only lukewarmly. When Sofia wanted to end the topic, the counselor conceded rather than negotiating a shared agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There was virtually no effort to evoke change talk — the counselor never asked about Sofia's reasons for concern, feelings about her drinking, or what she might want to be different. The session ended with no change-oriented statements from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's minimizing statements about drinking were met with neutral reflections that effectively amplified sustain talk rather than pivoting toward exploration. No double-sided reflections were used, and the topic was closed without gentle redirection.
