# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with consistent affirmations, but praise tends to be generic and plan-focused rather than reflecting deeper personal strengths like self-awareness and perseverance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good early reflective listening captured the client's loss and frustration, but empathy was not consistently deepened—emotional meaning gave way to rapid-fire planning questions that felt procedural.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus on the target behavior throughout, with each turn advancing toward a fully specified action plan (YMCA visit, contingency planning, accountability texts) without any drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highly collaborative co-construction of plans and interactive role-play, though the psychiatrist sometimes front-loaded her own suggestions (drafted texts, celebration ideas) before eliciting the client's.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client ownership through choice-offering, supporting her boundary-setting, and handing full authorship of success criteria to the client; no coercive language appeared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of open and scaling questions evoked vivid self-motivating statements and a success visualization, though later sessions focused on logistics and occasionally supplied motivational content rather than evoking it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert and consistently checked rather than prescribed, but occasionally led by drafting reminder and text messages in her own wording before offering tweaks.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited strong commitment language ('I've got this,' 'I showed up for myself') via tiny-step and confidence questions, but sometimes supplied change content herself, reducing client-generated change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled minimal sustain talk well by immediately pivoting to problem-solving and converting the sales-pressure barrier into a rehearsal of client agency, though occasionally prompted barrier discussion that briefly spotlighted obstacles.
