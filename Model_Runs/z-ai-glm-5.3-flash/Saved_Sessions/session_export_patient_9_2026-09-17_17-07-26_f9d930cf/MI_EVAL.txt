# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, receiving even Amina's criticisms without judgment. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and could be more individualized to her specific strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early reflections capturing content and emotion, such as acknowledging how seeing her son 'shrink' would worry any parent. Later reflections thinned, missing the deeper hurt in her doubt about whether anything would matter.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on managing co-parenting conflict and protecting her son, with concrete next steps and confidence checks. The pace moved quickly through strategies without consolidating, and direction sometimes came from the counselor's agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina's contributions were welcomed and incorporated, but the structure was largely counselor-driven with pre-packaged option menus. Amina reacted to choices rather than co-constructing the plan as an equal expert.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy regularly affirmed through options, acceptance of her rewording, and no pushback when she rejected journaling. Deduction for presenting menus of options rather than first asking what she thought might work.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective open questions and confidence rulers elicited Amina's own values and key insight about never letting herself recover. Advice menus sometimes followed evocative questions, cutting short further development of her own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine partnership, such as inviting her to choose and adapt wording, but frequent slips into an expert role with rapid-fire technique lists. Her best ideas emerged despite, not because of, the counselor's prompting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation and reinforcement of change talk, with confidence rising from 6 to 7.5 and progress from a baseline of 2 named and celebrated. Her doubt about her ex pushing back was met with advice rather than exploring her own coping ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified, which is good, but responses jumped to problem-solving rather than double-sided reflections or strategic reframing. Doubt was acknowledged minimally without pivoting back to her motivation and prior successes.
