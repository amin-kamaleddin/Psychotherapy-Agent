# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout — no judgment, labeling, or pressure even when Seamus deflects or resists. Honors his agenda ('your teeth, your time') and affirms his autonomy consistently.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections tracking both content and feeling — ambivalence, the granddaughter drawing, and his fatigue with lecturing are all captured and deepened. Client explicitly validates that the session felt different from past experiences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear strategic focus on smoking while accommodating the dental agenda; each question builds progressively from past motivated moments → values → doability → supports, closing with a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing: open questions, soliciting Seamus's interpretations and needs, and accepting his negotiation of the relationship ('bring your best small talk, not a leaflet'). Solutions emerge entirely from the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: 'like isn't will,' no commitments written down, door open with no pressure, and resistance validated as legitimate. Client's voluntary return to the topic confirms its effectiveness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Almost purely evocative — never argues for quitting or supplies reasons. Change talk is entirely client-generated and grows noticeably from 'nothing would' to 'maybe I'll mention it to the wife.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client; asks rather than tells and positions himself as one of Seamus's supports ('me included'). Seamus's contributions steer nearly every turn of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and amplification: highlights 'want it for myself,' explores the wedding day, elicits feasibility and needs, and reinforces the September statement as a desired future. Change talk grows measurably across the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying sustain talk with effective double-sided reflections and reframes toward family meaning. However, briefly mirrors the 'not possible' skepticism on multiple occasions where a stronger pivot toward change could have been used.
