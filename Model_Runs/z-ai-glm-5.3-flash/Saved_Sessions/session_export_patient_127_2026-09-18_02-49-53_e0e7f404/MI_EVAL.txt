# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout; the therapist never judges Timur's ambivalence about the old life and affirms both sides of his conflict as legitimate ('both feelings belong here'). The patient clearly feels accepted, evidenced by his candid self-disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections ('afternoons with nothing to do... that's real insight'; 'the pride sits right in the middle of it'). The therapist captures the emotional core of each statement, including fear of loss and pride barriers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is purposefully aimed at change talk and planning: exploring the pull, identifying danger zones, building a weekly plan, and eliciting values. The session moves clearly from ambivalence to commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The therapist repeatedly positions Timur as the expert ('You're the expert on your own life', 'you didn't wait for me to hand you a plan'). The plan is fully patient-generated, not prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and self-direction are honored at every turn; open questions like 'what would actually feel worth doing to you?' and 'you know yourself best' reinforce the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful eliciting of change talk: 'What would it mean to you to be that person?' draws out strong desire and commitment language ('he bleeding well meant it. I want that back'). Patient-generated reasons, abilities, and commitments dominate.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction flows as a genuine dialogue; the therapist builds on the patient's own words ('finding my way back', 'making days count') and hands credit back to him for every insight.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Abundant change talk elicited through targeted questions — recognition ('boredom and being skint, that's the danger zone'), ability ('I did say I'd stick to decisions'), and commitment ('I did something').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** When Timur labels himself 'lazy' and 'a mug', the therapist gently challenges the label without dismissing him ('I don't buy it, but more importantly, maybe you're starting not to either') and reframes work as a 'bridge', defusing sustain talk.
