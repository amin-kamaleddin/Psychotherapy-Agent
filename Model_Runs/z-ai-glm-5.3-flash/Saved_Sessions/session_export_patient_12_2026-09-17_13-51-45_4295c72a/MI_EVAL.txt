# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, accepting Amira's hedged commitments and low readiness without pressure. Deeper expressions of unconditional regard for her worth beyond her actions were largely absent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some accurate reflections, but many responses quickly pivoted to questions, safety checklists, or advice. Core themes of isolation, hopelessness, and shame were acknowledged superficially rather than deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on substance use and progressed logically from safety screening to a concrete commitment (pharmacy visit with alarm). Direction was achieved mostly through advice and task assignment rather than collaborative, values-based goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative gestures, but the pattern was largely counselor-as-advisor with Amira choosing from counselor-generated options. Little invitation for Amira's own ideas or agenda negotiation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed next steps as Amira's choice and accepted 'Not today' without pushback; no coercive language. Autonomy support was sometimes undercut by rapid-fire recommendations that narrowed her options to counselor-supplied ones.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was supplied through information and resources rather than drawn out from Amira. Beyond the readiness ruler, her reasons for change, values, hopes, and the meaning of her week of abstinence were rarely explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited choice at several points, but advice-dense delivery positioned the counselor as the source of solutions. Amira shaped the plan only marginally.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited modest change talk via the readiness ruler and tentative commitments ('I'll ask. That's all I'm saying'). Change talk was never reinforced with affirmations or deepened by exploring what success or hope might look like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and used one decent reframe, but no double-sided reflections or deliberate pivoting from hopelessness toward expressed willingness. Sustain talk was sometimes met with more resources rather than ambivalence resolution.
