# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard with individualized affirmations like 'That's your expertise talking' and 'That kind of honest self-reflection takes courage.' No judgment or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and deep, capturing core concerns like 'your daily life actually reflects that' and the busy-mind obstacle. Accurately mirrors the client's maintenance-stage clarity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on aligning daily behavior with the family happiness value, progressing logically from identifying drift to a concrete plan and follow-up. The final summary consolidates a coherent plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Equal partnership is evident through 'we' language ('look at it together') and co-constructed decisions, with the counselor providing structure while the client supplies all content and direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice is consistently reinforced through questions like 'what feels realistic to you?' and 'which one feels more like you?' The plan is entirely client-authored with explicit credit for her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client via open questions that elicit her own reasons, confidence, and commitment. No lecturing or counselor-supplied rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located within the client, and the entire plan (two evenings, buffer, notes) originated from her. Exemplary power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully elicited rich commitment language ('I could start this week,' 'Ready, even'), which the counselor then reflected and amplified while tying it back to her core value.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Obstacles are framed as solvable and pivoted to strengths and plans, appropriate for a maintenance-stage client. The repeated 'what might get in the way' question slightly invites obstacle focus, though handled collaboratively and briefly.
