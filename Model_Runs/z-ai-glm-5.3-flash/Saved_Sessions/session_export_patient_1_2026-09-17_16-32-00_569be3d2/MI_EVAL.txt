# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout; Ahmed's minimization is never shamed and his plan is affirmed. However, praise is somewhat formulaic with little individualized affirmation of his strengths or honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately summarizes content but stays list-like and content-focused. Emotional layers (embarrassment about his wife, fear behind the dust rationalization) are handled practically but never reflected at an emotional level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on reducing mini-cigar use; every exchange ties back to the target behavior and progresses logically to a specific, measurable commitment with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-asking and menu choices show partnership, and Ahmed shapes the plan, but the pattern is often clinician-proposes, patient-approves. True two-way brainstorming occurs only late around Friday strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are repeatedly framed as Ahmed's, and he retains final say (refusing gum, rejecting full quit). Falls short because option menus are clinician-generated and advice comes in rapid prescriptive bursts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Beyond the importance and confidence rulers, the clinician relies on information and prescribed solutions rather than drawing out Ahmed's own motivations. His intrinsic motives (wife's disapproval, throat irritation, pride in not being addicted) are never explored in depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative elements exist (asking permission, offering choices), but the psychiatrist slips into directive mode—listing risks and steps, drafting scripts unprompted, and booking the recheck without exploring Ahmed's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of importance and confidence rulers plus evocative questions moves Ahmed to concrete commitment and tentative change talk. However, classic follow-ups (why a 2 and not 0, why 7 and not lower) are missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is frequent and is not reinforced, but the main counter is corrective information, which edges toward expert confrontation rather than double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance. Ahmed's dust blame persists to the end.
