# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout: affirms Aisha's self-knowledge, honesty, and efforts, and normalizes past quitting and guilt without judgment. Strong closing affirmation: 'That's not luck. That's you.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that go beyond surface content to underlying meaning, e.g., 'the real hurdle isn't time — it's guilt' and 'I can hear the hope in your voice.' Consistently captures both concrete plans and emotional states.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on resuming evening walking from opening to closing. Session progresses logically: first step, past pattern, coping strategies, barriers, concrete plan, and follow-up structure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance: 'what might get in the way that we could think through together' and joint design of the check-in format. Aisha co-constructs the plan and even names the process as helpful.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Aisha's ownership: 'what feels true to you now,' 'you got there yourself,' 'your life moves on your terms.' No advice imposed; all ideas elicited and attributed to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All plan elements originated from Aisha via open questions ('what do you think she'd say'). The guilt insight and mom-conversation idea came from her own exploration, then reflected back as her discoveries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Aisha as the expert: 'You know your life better than anyone.' Walking, mirror note, texting sister, talking to mom, and the check-in question all came from her, not the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk ('what might be different this time,' 'what will tonight's walk feel like'). Aisha's commitment language escalates from hope to 'I'm properly ready,' each statement reinforced with affirmations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (guilt, self-consciousness, fear of bailing) was handled well through reframing and exploring the all-or-nothing pattern. Score slightly lower as the counselor occasionally explored barriers rather than actively pivoting back to desire/confidence language.
