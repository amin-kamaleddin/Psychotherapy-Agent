# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental affirmations throughout ('That's a real strength you carry'; 'You're not starting from zero'). Falls short of 5 because warmth occasionally highlights the counselor's own reframes rather than unconditional regard independent of progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are precise and land consistently, capturing ambivalence ('Both things are true'), somatic experience ('tightness in your chest'), and meaning, not just content. Client repeatedly confirms and elaborates on reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on exam avoidance and the concrete first step of telling her sister. Clear arc from pattern exploration to a time-bound follow-up plan, with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—the client generates the plan's content and 'What would be most useful from me?' is exemplary agenda negotiation. Minor deduction for occasionally stacked questions ('What do you think it would take... Or is there someone you'd trust?') that subtly steer.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is repeatedly affirmed—steps are 'ones you sized yourself' and the check-in is negotiated, not imposed ('I'll hold you to Friday gently'). The counselor accepts 'that's all I can promise' without pressure and validates contingency entirely.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is explicitly located in the client ('that's not me convincing you'), and questions draw out Hana's own resources—her past success, her sister, her framing of honesty. Her language visibly shifts from self-doubt to self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly placed with the client ('You know your patterns better than anyone'; 'you found all of that in yourself'). All key ideas—the sister, the script, the small steps—come from Hana and are reflected and amplified by the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions richly evoke change talk, which builds from desire ('weight lifted off my shoulders') to commitment ('I could tell her this week'). Each instance is reinforced with affirmations and evocative follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with double-sided reflections and reframes ('it's not a test you can fail') rather than amplification, and it diminishes as change talk grows. A few reflections briefly restate the negative side without an immediate pivot.
