# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard with no judgment of gambling, debt, or lifestyle. The closing reframe 'You're not a mess' directly countered Anya's internalized shame, and affirmations were specific and genuine.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and feeling. The 'nobody needed a win to have a good night' reflection lifted the emotional core of her disclosure, and the final double reflection on ambivalence was textbook.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, focused trajectory on gambling reduction and financial stability from the first utterance. Progress was logical with no drift, ending with specific actionable commitments (text tonight, cards on the weekend).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was a genuine joint exploration between equals, with Anya's ideas (dinner, Emma, sister) shaping the entire action framework. The clinician asked rather than told and co-created the follow-up without prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was actively reinforced, and no advice was imposed — the weekly plan was entirely client-generated. The clinician explicitly separated Anya's worth and ownership from outcomes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within; the client articulated her own core motivation ('I want the Egypt version of my life'). She even internalized the clinician's belief ('I almost want to prove you right'), demonstrating evocation at the highest level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician explicitly positioned Anya as the expert on her own life and credited her for every plan element. Anya confirmed the ownership dynamic, and expertise-sharing was exemplary.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and strategically reinforced, with desire, commitment, and concrete steps elicited throughout. Anya's language escalated from tentative to committed over the session — clear evidence of skillful evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and reframed without reinforcement, such as validating the fear about cards before pivoting forward. The 'wobbling doesn't undo what you've decided' pivot kept ambivalence from becoming the focus.
