# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with affirmations of Elena's efforts and explicit normalization of slips; Elena confirmed she felt unjudged. Falls short of 5 because warmth occasionally feels formulaic and bundled with advice-giving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, accurate reflections, including a strong opening complex reflection that defused her sense of being blamed. Reflections sometimes rush toward solutions before emotional content like fear of failure and resentment toward the pediatrician is fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays well focused on smoking and her son's exposure, progressing from ambivalence to a concrete one-week plan and a Wednesday 4:30 check-in. Minor drift into psychoeducation and a somewhat checklist-driven rather than motivation-deepening trajectory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were co-constructed from Elena's own suggestions and the check-in time negotiated around her schedule. The counselor periodically shifts into expert mode with option lists and tips, tilting the power balance slightly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Elena's ownership—supporting whatever she decides, offering choices, accepting her stated limits without pressure, and framing slips non-judgmentally. Her closing comment that she didn't feel like garbage confirms she experienced genuine choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation is drawn out from Elena, but much session content is counselor-supplied strategies and psychoeducation. Missed openings to explore her own values, motivations, and confidence, such as her 'maybe I'd think about it more seriously' remark.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, and her contributions genuinely shaped the plan (shirt, gum placement, ashtray removal). The counselor nonetheless takes the expert role at points rather than consistently drawing solutions from Elena.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The opening question invites change talk and Elena offers meaningful change language, but these moments are not amplified or deepened strategically. Evocation is present but shallow.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with acceptance rather than argument, validating concerns before pivoting to options. Some responses enumerate obstacles in detail, which can amplify the sense of burden instead of redirecting toward her motivations.
