# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic respect and non-judgment maintained, with some reframing of ambivalence. However, warmth is generic and Oliver's specific strengths, courage, and self-reported progress go unaffirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; emotionally rich material (loneliness, the knot in his stomach, fear of losing the friendship) is largely not reflected back. Responses are mostly superficial acknowledgments followed by advice and scripts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on the target behavior and moves efficiently toward concrete action with deadline, script, role-play, and reminders. Direction is pushy and plan-driven rather than paced to Oliver's own exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The role-play was genuinely interactive and options were offered, showing partial collaboration. But most decisions (deadlines, templates, checklists) are counselor-generated rather than co-created with Oliver.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some format-level choice is offered (script options, role-play preferences). Overall style remains prescriptive, implying a right way that the counselor knows rather than asking what Oliver thinks will work.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions are almost entirely counselor-supplied despite Oliver being highly self-motivated. Virtually no evocative questions explore his reasons, confidence, or what keeps him stuck — the core MI error.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, dispensing numbered plans and checklists while Oliver's own contributions and insights are not leveraged. His best insights come from him, not counselor facilitation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Oliver spontaneously offers strong change talk ('My job is done', 'Friday. I'm doing this.') that is never evoked, deepened, or reflected. Clear opportunities to amplify his momentum are missed in favor of moving to the next tip.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about comfort, missing his flatmate, and fear of friendship loss is largely bypassed rather than explored. No double-sided reflections are used, and the core barrier he named is never addressed.
