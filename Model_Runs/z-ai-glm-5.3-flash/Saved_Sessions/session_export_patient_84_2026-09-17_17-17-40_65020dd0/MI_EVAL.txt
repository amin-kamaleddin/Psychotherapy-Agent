# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, with explicit normalization ('I'm not here to judge') and genuine affirmations ('a solid, mature step'), despite Malik's minimization and defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures ambivalence, e.g., 'your mom's worry feels frustrating.' Reflections are often bundled with multiple questions and rarely linger on underlying feelings like fear of disappointing his mother.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus from exploration through a concrete plan, rehearsal, and follow-up, with no aimless drift; each turn advances toward change and ends in committed action ('I'll probably do it tonight').

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Malik's edits (cutoff time, softer wording, one-month frame) substantially shape the plan. The middle portion leans on counselor-generated option lists and scripts, shifting toward advice-giving before Malik's input restores partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Malik's ownership ('Which of those feels doable for you this week?', 'pick when you'll try the one-month talk') with no pressure to quit entirely. Builds on his partial commitment and offers continued choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation drew out Malik's own reasons ('hiding is the worst') and self-generated commitment ('first time I've got an actual plan'). Several turns supply reasons and scripted lines that could instead have been evoked from Malik.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Malik as the expert, eliciting his ideas and adopting his revised wording. Occasionally fills space with option menus rather than waiting for him to generate ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk ('the hiding part is honestly the worst') and elicited Malik's self-generated plan with clear closing momentum ('I'll try it'). Early evocation relied on counselor-generated option lists rather than drawing options from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects sustain talk without reinforcing it and pivots toward change ('Which of those feels doable for you this week?'). Some scripted anti-change framing and occasional echoing of sustain talk in summaries; double-sided reflections are not consistently used.
