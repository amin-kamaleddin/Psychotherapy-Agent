### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance; multiple affirmations of client strengths ("That takes courage," "That's strength that's already in you"). Sustain talk met with acceptance, not judgment; unconditional positive regard modeled in closing ("something here for us to work with either way").

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally deep reflections. Double-sided opening captures core ambivalence; meaning-level reflections ("a cage with a door") built from client's own words. Each reflection confirmed by "Yeah, exactly" or deepened disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Clear progressive focus: ambivalence → exceptions (Marie) → barriers → supports → self-determined next step → follow-up secured. No drift; every exchange advances toward change while remaining client-paced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Interaction reads as two equals. Experiments framed as invitations; agenda and plan co-constructed from client's material. Client visibly empowered — generates own strategies and sets follow-up terms.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly reinforced: "You get to decide the pace." Refusal to set a quit date accepted without pushing; even exploring failure endorsed ("especially if I bailed"), removing performance pressure. No coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from within the client — never argues for quitting. Questions elicited realizations: talking matters more than the cigarette, Marie as ally, smoking as "kind of a cage." Closing self-insight is pure client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Expertise located in the client: "you actually had an idea yourself." Asks rather than tells; the emerging plan (telling Marie, walks on breaks, no quit date) is entirely hers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation — exceptions ("What made it work?"), past successes, and "what changed for you?" produced the session's strongest change talk ("it's also kind of a cage"). Change talk reflected and amplified, culminating in client-generated commitment ("one conversation with Marie this week").

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☑ **5**
**Comments:** Sustain talk never argued against or reinforced. Fears ("I always do cave") pivoted to strength ("you've made it through a bad day, once or twice"). Client's language shifts from "It's good" to "a cage" — evidence sustain talk was softened, not suppressed.
