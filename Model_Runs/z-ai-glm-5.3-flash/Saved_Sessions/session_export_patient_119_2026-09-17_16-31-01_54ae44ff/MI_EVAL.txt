# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and non-judgment, honoring Selene's request to stop framing this as a big deal. Warmth was somewhat formulaic and ambivalence/lapse history wasn't normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections captured both sustain and change talk. Summaries often restated content verbatim, and the emotional core (mother's complications, self-neglect pattern) was never explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, unwavering focus on medication adherence from start to finish, funneling every exchange toward concrete strategy, lapse planning, and scheduled follow-ups. No topic drift occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely joint work — Selene's own ideas (toothbrush pairing, self-grace rule, text format) shaped the plan. At times the counselor slipped into a checklist stance, presenting menus and lengthy plan summaries rather than co-constructing in the moment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Selene's ownership, with options framed as choices and all logistics negotiated to her preferences. Pressure was dropped gracefully on request without conceding that adherence doesn't matter.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Selene's motivations, success definition, and coping self-talk were elicited, and her change talk grew from hesitant 'I'll try' to committed action. Evocation relied on a narrow question repertoire without deepening follow-ups.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Selene was consistently positioned as the expert and explicitly valued the approach ('figuring it out myself'). The opening turn's burst of pre-packaged options slightly tilted early agenda-setting toward the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was actively evoked and developed, moving Selene from ambivalence to commitment ('call it a seven') and concrete planning. Confidence scaling was repeated mechanically rather than explored (e.g., 'What would move you from 6 to 7?').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimization was met with neutral double-sided reflections rather than argument, and the counselor pivoted effectively to change talk. Some residual sustain talk ('not convinced one pill makes or breaks anything') was left unexplored at session's end.
