# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance; affirmed Rosa's self-discoveries and normalized her ambivalence. Affirmations were somewhat sparse early in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured meaning beneath content, e.g., fear of failing in front of loved ones. The grief around losing the smoker identity was touched but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stayed firmly on smoking cessation, redirecting tangents with prioritizing questions and moving progressively toward concrete next steps. Minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session read as a conversation between equals; co-created the written questions and invited Rosa to edit them. Occasionally supplied structure rather than letting it emerge fully from the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly framed change as Rosa's own — no quit date, no pressure, control over pacing and wording of her questions. Autonomy-affirming statements came mostly in the latter half.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Key insights came from Rosa herself — confidence talk and 'checking the story' — elicited through open questions. A couple of reframes were supplied by the clinician rather than evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Rosa as the expert; her contributions visibly shaped the session. Minor shaping (e.g., suggesting the written list) before fully drawing it out of her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Systematically evoked and deepened change talk — past successes, elaborated plans, and reflective mirroring of commitment language. A few reframes were therapist-generated rather than client-driven.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk accurately with double-sided reflections, but several reflections restated barriers at length before pivoting, giving sustain talk considerable airtime.
