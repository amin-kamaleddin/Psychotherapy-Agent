# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout; ambivalence about abstinence received without pressure. Praise is somewhat generic, with underdeveloped affirmation of Luka's specific strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections, but advice and strategy lists dominate. Key emotional content—fear of losing control, shame about calling his partner, worry about being 'the boring one'—receives tactical rather than deep reflective responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction with logical structure toward a concrete weekend plan. The violence/aggression thread is touched but never explicitly explored or integrated into the plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine collaboration (co-chosen role-play, client-edited scripts), but the default is clinician proposing and client approving. Luka's own ideas are not explicitly built upon, making it feel more like expert consultation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support—client explicitly felt unpressured to quit and chose his own lines and limits. Tactic lists, however, are delivered prescriptively and at length.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Useful material is elicited (triggers, self-doubt, partner involvement), but motivation is treated as something to equip rather than evoke. No open questions about Luka's own reasons for change, values discrepancy, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing (offering role-play vs. written plan), but the session often slips into an expert stance with pre-packaged strategies delivered before the client's own ideas are explored.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is reflected and refusal language effectively rehearsed, but opportunities to deepen commitment ('this is doable') are missed. No confidence scaling or elaboration on the client's own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids lecturing and respects Luka's reduction stance, but sustain talk is often met with more tactics rather than double-sided reflections. Repeated focus on slips and relapse risk can invite further sustain talk.
