# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit unconditional positive regard from the first utterance, honored throughout. Affirmations are individualized and tied to specific client content rather than generic praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and often complex, including well-constructed double-sided reflections. However, the psychiatrist pivots from emotional content to the next task, leaving deep material like the family history of alcoholism unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on reducing drinking and its link to contraceptive reliability, with clear forward momentum through each stage of planning. Both sides of the dual focus were preserved and connected by the client herself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction operates between genuine equals with no psychoeducation, recommendations, or confrontation. The client's material shapes the session's direction at every turn.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly and repeatedly reinforced through open choice-points and ownership attribution. The client's strategies are entirely self-generated with no unsolicited advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the client rather than supplied — her own reasons, resources, and self-talk. Her confidence rising from 4 to 7 within the session is direct evidence of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire change plan (designated driver, texting Sarah, the phone note, monthly routine) is generated by the client. Power sharing substantially influences the nature of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation of desire, ability, commitment, and mobilization change talk, each reflected and built upon. Notable strengths include the confidence ruler execution and the client's spontaneous reframe of drinking less as 'showing up, one weekend at a time.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every instance of sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and skillfully pivoted toward change — e.g., the doubting voice becomes a springboard for confidence talk, and low future-weekend confidence becomes a planning target. Sustain talk is never dwelt upon or amplified.
