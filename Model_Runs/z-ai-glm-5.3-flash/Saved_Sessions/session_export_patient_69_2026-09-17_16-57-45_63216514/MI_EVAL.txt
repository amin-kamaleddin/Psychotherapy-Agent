# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicated respect and unconditional positive regard despite Kieran's dismissive, flat presentation, avoiding any judgment of his apathy. A 5 would require deeper individualized warmth and explicit normalization of his ambivalence and cynicism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured both content and feeling, e.g., 'the constant checking feels exhausting and like they expect you to be dishonest.' Reflections were sometimes brief and did not fully explore the deeper layer of futility ('nothing ever changes').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus, moving steadily from venting frustration to identifying the worst part, evoking a concrete skill, and finalizing a practical action step. The session ended with a concrete, client-owned action and a follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration between equals — the counselor offered drafts and ideas, and Kieran edited and selected until the final note was substantially his own voice. Language like 'Any words you want changed?' consistently incorporated his contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly and explicitly supported Kieran's control: pace-following, multiple options for next steps and delivery methods, and no pressure when he said 'No promises.' Framing the action as 'a small move you control, not a big commitment' was model autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from Kieran, eliciting his strengths ('decent with my hands') and having him rehearse and edit his own script, producing client-generated change talk by session end. Could have deepened evocation by exploring his hopes or confidence more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Kieran's expertise as central — his wording edits were adopted verbatim and his preferences shaped the entire plan, resulting in a final product entirely in his voice. A 5 would require explicitly naming him as the expert in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk from a precontemplative client via questions like 'what's one realistic thing you'd tell a caseworker you could do?', surfacing strengths and producing self-authored commitments ('I showed up today, didn't I?'). Could have deepened by exploring confidence and reflecting his self-affirming statements more deliberately.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('it never changes', 'they'll just nod, file it away') was never reinforced, but also not actively softened — few double-sided reflections, reframes, or affirmations when Kieran voiced doubt. Missed opportunities to strategically pivot toward his expressed agency.
