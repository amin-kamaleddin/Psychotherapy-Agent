# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard ('That's a real insight, Marco,' 'That all counts'). No judgmental statements; slips are framed as earned wisdom rather than failures.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, capturing both surface content and deeper meaning (e.g., 'you're not talking about giving something up, you're talking about getting something back'). Reflections are emotionally attuned and reframed effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, progressive focus on the target behavior, moving from awareness to environment change to ritual to concrete first step. Each exchange builds momentum toward commitment without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Proceeds between equal partners using inclusive language ('So here's what I'm wondering...') and co-constructs the follow-up plan. No advice imposed; questions let Marco draw his own conclusions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports full client ownership; all plan components originate from Marco. Explicitly centers his choice ('which one feels most like something you'd actually look forward to?'), and Marco reports feeling ownership ('that's something that's mine').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out intrinsic motivations (childhood memory, pride, wife's support) and Marco's own insights. Could have pushed further into developing discrepancy and elaborating on what sustains his high confidence rating rather than moving on quickly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Marco as the expert on his own life ('You know your household and your habits better than anyone'). Every plan element is client-generated and visibly shapes the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions generate rich change talk, and the confidence scaling elicits a '10' commitment. Some evocations are somewhat formulaic; commitment language could be deepened more deliberately and scaling used strategically earlier.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('boy, do I love that OJ') is acknowledged but never reinforced, and is skillfully transformed via the breakfast ritual question. Ambivalence resolved almost entirely by mid-session.
