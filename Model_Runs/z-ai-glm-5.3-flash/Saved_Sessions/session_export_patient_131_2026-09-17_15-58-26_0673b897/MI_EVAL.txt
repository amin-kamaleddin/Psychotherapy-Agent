# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations of Wiremu's commitment and non-judgmental responses to his self-disclosure and past. Score: 5/5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing both content and emotional nuance, though depth was limited when exploring Wiremu's fear of whānau rejection — the counselor moved quickly to problem-solving rather than reflecting that fear more fully. Score: 4/5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus throughout — the client set the target, and every exchange returned to alcohol reduction, housing, and family reunification, converting ambivalence into a concrete plan with no topic drift. Score: 5/5.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely joint problem-solving with iterative editing of drafts, but the counselor often presented near-final plans for approval ('Want this tweaked?') rather than genuinely co-generating them, making Wiremu sometimes a consultant rather than equal author. Score: 4/5.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was consistently reinforced — Wiremu selected his own sober days, contact approach, check-in timing, and plan conditions. Options were offered rather than directives, honoring his sense of ownership. Score: 5/5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from Wiremu rather than lectured, eliciting his own reasons and steps, but scripted language and solutions were supplied fairly quickly, reducing his need to fully articulate his motivations. Score: 4/5.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Wiremu's expertise was consistently positioned as central and his contributions shaped the plan, though the counselor sometimes took the driver's seat by supplying ready-made scripts rather than co-constructing from his words first. Score: 4/5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked commitment language ('that's the goal,' 'I could give that a crack,' 'I've actually got a plan'), though the counselor sometimes supplied change language himself via scripts and drafts rather than letting Wiremu develop his own words. Score: 4/5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about freedom and Tristan was acknowledged briefly and redirected toward values and action, but the counselor missed the chance to reframe Wiremu's 'sounds dumb when I say it out loud' remark to affirm the legitimacy of his ambivalence. Score: 4/5.
