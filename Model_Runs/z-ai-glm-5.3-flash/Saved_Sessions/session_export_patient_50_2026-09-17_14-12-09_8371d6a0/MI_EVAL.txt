# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout and never disapproves of Hana's avoidance. However, affirmations are brief and formulaic, often immediately followed by instructions, diluting the sense of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal — each turn opens with a one-line acknowledgment before pivoting to prescriptive advice. Hana's emotionally rich material (shame, nightly 'what if I fail' spirals) is rarely reflected back or explored; the session stays at a task level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintains excellent, unwavering focus on the target behavior — starting a small study session — from the second turn onward, tying every response back to a concrete plan with clear commitment and accountability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is noticeably asymmetrical, with each counselor turn being a monologue of directives and only one real open question in the entire session. There is no joint agenda-setting or collaborative problem-solving dialogue; the counselor solves problems for Hana.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some autonomy-supportive elements, such as an early choice of experiment and Hana picking her own time, reward, and reminder strategies. However, the overwhelming volume of prescriptive instructions crowds out choice and edges toward controlling the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions largely come from the counselor rather than being drawn out of Hana. The best material in the session — the recording success and 'proving myself wrong' — was offered by Hana herself, and the counselor failed to mine it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumes the expert role, delivering a micro-plan in nearly every turn. While Hana's contributions shape details, the overall structure and content of the intervention are counselor-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Hana generates notable change talk herself ('I want that feeling again. Of proving myself wrong'), but the counselor does almost nothing to evoke or deepen it. Change talk is met with generic affirmation followed by more instructions, missing repeated opportunities to amplify her own motivational language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor does not reinforce or dwell on Hana's sustain talk, which is a strength. However, there are no strategic double-sided reflections or gentle reframes using her ambivalence to move toward change; sustain talk is largely ignored rather than skillfully resolved.
