### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizing Elena's avoidance and hesitancy without any judgment or pressure.

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on contraception after the pregnancy scare across every turn, building steady momentum toward concrete action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behaves as an equal partner by inviting Elena to steer, but much content (option lists, scripts) was counselor-supplied rather than co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Elena's ownership: permission-asking, menu choices, 'you don't have to decide today,' with no coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Elena's own motivations and values, though evocation relies partly on menus rather than open-ended exploration, and her confidence for change is never explicitly explored.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Frequently frames Elena as decision-maker and uses role-play to build her efficacy, but could better acknowledge her expertise and co-create the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes and reinforces Elena's desire, interest, and mobilization statements, but could deepen change talk further with confidence scaling and explicit follow-up reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing and pivots to small steps, but could use strategic double-sided reflections to more actively resolve ambivalence.
