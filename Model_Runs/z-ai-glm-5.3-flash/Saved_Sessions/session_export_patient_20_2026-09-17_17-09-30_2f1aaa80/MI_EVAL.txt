# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, validating Brandon's perspective on drinking without labeling or disapproval. However, the repeated boilerplate safety question (asked five times) felt impersonal and slightly undermined warmth and rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening, including a strong double-sided reflection capturing Brandon's ambivalence ('the pull both ways'). Reflections were often brief and pivoted quickly to checklists, missing chances to explore emotional layers like his irritation at his sister and fear of social judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained exceptionally clear, consistent focus on weeknight drinking, progressively building a concrete plan with obstacles, supports, and a specific Friday 5:00 pm check-in. Every turn tied back to the change goal, ending with a specific, testable one-week experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operated as genuine joint problem-solving, with Brandon actively negotiating terms and shaping the plan. Collaboration was occasionally uneven, as the counselor tended to pre-package solution menus rather than co-generating options from Brandon's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Brandon's ownership through choices at every step (action type, delay duration, check-in timing) while accepting his rejection of alcohol-free nights without pressure. All plan elements were Brandon's selections, framed entirely as his choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions surfaced some self-generated motivation, but the counselor quickly shifted from eliciting to supplying solutions. Little exploration of deeper values or the personal meaning behind Brandon's change talk (e.g., 'Why does proving you've got this handled matter to you?').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Brandon as the decision-maker, and his contributions genuinely shaped the plan (dog walk, 45 minutes, Friday check-in). Fell short because the counselor often supplied full menus of options and scripts rather than eliciting Brandon's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used readiness/confidence scaling questions and built well on Brandon's spontaneous change talk, eliciting self-generated reasons for change. A 5 would require deeper development of commitment language and exploration of what makes change personally meaningful.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk, using double-sided reflections and pivoting gracefully when Brandon rejected alcohol-free nights. Did not consistently reframe or strategically redirect Brandon's minimization, at times leaving it unaddressed.
