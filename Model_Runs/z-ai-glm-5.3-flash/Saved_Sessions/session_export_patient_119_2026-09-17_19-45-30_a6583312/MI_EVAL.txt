# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through warm, client-referenced affirmations and compassionate reframing of shame without any judgment or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deeply accurate reflections capture meaning beneath the client's words, e.g., 'the shame around missing a day might be heavier than the missed dose itself,' with precise mirroring of her own language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on medication adherence with progressive momentum from barriers to a concrete, confidence-rated commitment plan, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance, exemplified by 'let's figure out the weekend version together'; every plan element originates from the client and the counselor amplifies rather than directs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly reinforced ('You get to set that boundary'); no solutions are prescribed and the client herself notes she does better 'when it's my choice.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from within via evocative questions and future-focused visualization; client's closing comment that the counselor 'let me talk myself into it' is the strongest evidence of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located in the client ('You know your rhythms better than anyone'), and her ideas shape the entire session and final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and reinforced, including future visualization and a confidence ruler; the client's arc from 'not a big deal' to 'I want to show you that bottle' demonstrates sustained evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk like minimization and defensiveness is briefly acknowledged, then reframed toward change without dwelling on it or reinforcing minimization.
