# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone maintained throughout despite Viktor's denial, with affirmations like 'Great insight.' Warmth and validation of his frustration were somewhat sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections, but most were thin and transitional. Rich moments (curfew feeling like 'giving something up,' never talking sober) were acknowledged briefly rather than explored deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on a concrete, sequential plan (script, curfew, text, readiness ratings) with each turn advancing the goal. The substance use itself was never explicitly named or revisited as the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative on scripts and wording, with Viktor shaping key content. He acted more as editor than co-author since the counselor supplied most structures unilaterally.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated choice-offering and acceptance of Viktor's modifications. Weakened when 'limit drinking' was simply dropped rather than affirming Viktor's right to set the scope of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler and open questions drew out genuine client insights ('she matters to me'). Too often the counselor filled space with advice and scripts instead of evoking Viktor's own solutions and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing through options and choices, but the overall stance was expert-driven with pre-designed plans. Viktor's own expertise was not systematically drawn upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler effectively moved Viktor from 5 to 6.5, and spontaneous insights emerged. However, change talk was harvested but not cultivated — no elaboration questions deepened these pivotal moments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I don't need fixing,' 'it's not that serious') was repeatedly accommodated or ignored rather than reframed. No double-sided reflections or linking of use to Viktor's stated goal of marital peace.
