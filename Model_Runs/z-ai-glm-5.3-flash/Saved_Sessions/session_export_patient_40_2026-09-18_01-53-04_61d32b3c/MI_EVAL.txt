# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, validating both sides of ambivalence and meeting relapse shame without judgment. The client visibly opens up over time, evidence of felt acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally deep, capturing nuanced feelings like the kids being both motivation and fear. Every reflection lands, with the client confirming or building on each one.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains steady focus on target behaviors while progressing from reflection to motivation, concrete planning, contingency planning, and success criteria. Each question builds strategically on the last with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The process is fully co-constructed, with the clinician scaffolding and the client shaping each next step. The client ends the session actively solving his own problems.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is consistently reinforced, with no advice, persuasion, or directives; even the note idea originated from the client. His self-defined success standard is explicitly validated as superior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly all motivation and every plan element come from the client, elicited through open questions. The clinician never supplies reasons for change or pre-written solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly names the client as the expert and hands ownership back at each step. All substantive plan elements originate from the client, with the clinician only reflecting and organizing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk, drawing out reasons (kids' hug), a concrete commitment ('Saturday, sober, no question'), and identity-level language. The closing question produced a full self-defined standard for recovery.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally does not amplify doubt, pivoting to the change side when fear or uncertainty arises. Occasionally re-voices sustain talk within double-sided reflections, which is acceptable but keeps slightly more emphasis on the negative than ideal.
