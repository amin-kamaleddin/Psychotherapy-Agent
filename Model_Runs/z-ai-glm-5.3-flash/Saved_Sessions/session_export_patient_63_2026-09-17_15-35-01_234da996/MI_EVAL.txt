# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance, validating Jordan's ambivalence and normalizing his nervousness. However, affirmations are brief and formulaic, with few explicit affirmations of Jordan as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track Jordan's core dilemma between fear of jail and peer loyalty. Falls short of 5 because deeper emotional themes like feeling like 'a different person' around friends are noted but not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior from the first exchange, systematically building toward a concrete sequential plan with no drift. Every exchange progresses toward change, culminating in specific commitments tied to the next real-world test.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently invites Jordan's input before advancing, and his contributions visibly shape the plan. Falls short of 5 because the ideas pipeline remains counselor-initiated, making collaboration more like guided selection than equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames choices as Jordan's and embraces his own adaptations. Not a 5 because the counselor steers heavily toward prepared solutions and asks formulaic ruler questions that can feel controlling.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits motivation through confidence rulers and rehearsal, with self-efficacy growing from 4 to 7.5. However, most substantive content originates from the counselor rather than being drawn from Jordan's own values, ideas, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks which options feel most possible, but supplies nearly all the content itself, with Jordan adapting and selecting rather than generating his own strategies. Genuine power-sharing is limited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully uses confidence rulers to elicit progressive change talk from 4 to 7.5, with Jordan articulating concrete plans and commitments. Limited by rarely exploring Jordan's own reasons and values for change beyond jail avoidance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without amplifying it and consistently pivots toward solutions and rehearsal. Not a 5 because the counselor responds with immediate fixes rather than double-sided reflections, and the underlying pull of temptation is never revisited.
