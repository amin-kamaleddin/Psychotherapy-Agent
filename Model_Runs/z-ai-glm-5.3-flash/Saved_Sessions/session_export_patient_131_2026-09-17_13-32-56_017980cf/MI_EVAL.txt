# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, but warmth is formulaic and applied mechanically. Little acknowledgment of Wiremu's remorse or the cultural weight of whānau and independence in his life.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections, but the session is dominated by questions and instructions rather than reflective listening. Emotional statements about freedom, shame, and being watched are bypassed in favor of task-planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on target behaviors with each exchange moving toward a concrete next step — a genuine strength. Only caveat: direction sometimes becomes urgency, pushing deadlines rather than letting Wiremu set the pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early collaborative spirit gives way to expert-delivery — the psychiatrist drafts the email, corrects drafts, and enumerates steps. Wiremu's own ideas shape little of the session beyond agreeing with clinician proposals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Choice is offered only through clinician-defined A/B menus rather than genuine open space. Supplied deadlines and drafted communications subtly control the change process rather than reinforcing Wiremu's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Wiremu's motivation is visible, but the clinician supplies reasons, solutions, and plans rather than eliciting his own. Motivation is assumed and channeled, not evoked through questions and reflections.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist assumes the expert role, providing scripts, drafting the email verbatim, and prescribing coping plans. This reinforces Wiremu's dependence on expert output rather than his own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Wiremu generates substantial change talk himself, and some is selected and reinforced; asking him to draft his own caseworker message was a good evocative move. However, change talk is rarely deepened with follow-up questions, and momentum comes mostly from clinician-generated scripts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is repeatedly elevated rather than softened — directive framing trades on fear of relapse and 'strengthening the case.' Concerns about freedom and feeling watched are overridden with option menus instead of being explored.
