# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, especially when handling Sofia's annoyance and fear of her mother's disappointment. Falls short of top marks because warmth is brief and opportunities to affirm her deeper struggles (anxiety, family expectations) are missed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but empathic understanding is thin—emotionally rich material (wasted money, persistent anxiety, her mother's sacrifices) is met with checklists and pivots rather than reflective responses. Reflections are brief and rarely explore feeling or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on marijuana use and converges on a concrete, achievable plan (Wednesday, tracking, refusal script, follow-up). Weakness is steering rapidly through bundled agendas that overwhelm rather than letting Sofia guide the focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functions as a joint project—rehearsing the refusal line together, co-building the checklist, and a follow-up Sofia herself requested—while asking permission before offering resources. Tilts toward professional-led because the default is dispensing expert advice lists before inviting her input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers menu choices, accepts Sofia's limits ('No promises on anything else'), and supports her pacing on the mother conversation; the plan is genuinely hers. Loses points for lengthy pre-packaged plans implying a preferred answer and no explicit affirmation of her capacity to own the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Motivation-relevant material emerges incidentally (financial concern, doubts about effectiveness, mother's hopes) rather than through skilled evocative questioning. Rich discrepancy statements pass without exploration, as the counselor moves to logistics instead.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's preferences shape the plan (Wednesday, tracking method, joking refusal line) and choice is offered at key points. But the counselor assumes the expert role with prescriptive bullet-point plans, incorporating her ideas only after already proposing them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers genuine change talk (smoke-free day, commitment to Wednesday, discrepancy about her mother's hopes) that is never reflected back or deepened. The counselor jumps to structured plans and tips rather than reinforcing or evoking more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's frequent sustain talk ('It's not a big deal,' 'pot just helps me') is neither amplified nor reinforced, though mild double-sided reframes are offered. Advice often crowds the space immediately after sustain talk, so ambivalence is navigated but not deliberately softened.
