# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, even when Sofia discloses continued drinking. However, warmth is brief and reassurance feels somewhat formulaic, pivoting quickly to instruction without deeply exploring her fears.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal, with almost no reflections of Sofia's feelings or content. Her core ambivalence about losing her social identity is labeled generically and immediately redirected to practical checklists.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on alcohol cessation during pregnancy with concrete next steps, and Sofia leaves with an endorsed action plan. Direction is achieved through instruction rather than collaborative goal-setting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is heavily weighted toward the counselor as advice-giver, leaving Sofia in a receiver role. Collaboration emerges only late and only after Sofia explicitly requests role-play, and suggestions are rarely checked against her reality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The style is predominantly prescriptive, with imperative directives and pre-written scripts. Meaningful choices like the honest OB script came from Sofia's own pushback rather than facilitated choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all interventions are righting-reflex responses (plans, scripts, checklists) rather than eliciting Sofia's own motivations. Powerful change talk like 'I don't wanna let my kid down' passes without exploration or reinforcement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes an expert role, dispensing directives with little power-sharing. Sofia's own insights shaped the plan despite, not because of, the counselor's approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia spontaneously offers strong change talk that goes largely uncaptured, with no affirmations, deepening questions, or reflections of her stated commitments. The momentum toward change comes entirely from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk is bypassed with lists of practical countermeasures rather than amplified or strategically resolved. No double-sided reflections or deliberate pivoting toward her reasons for change.
