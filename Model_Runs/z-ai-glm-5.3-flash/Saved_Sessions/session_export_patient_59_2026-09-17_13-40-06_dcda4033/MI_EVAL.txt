# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with basic acceptance ('Totally get the annoyance'), but warmth stays thin and transactional with few affirmations of Jamal as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reliance on brief token acknowledgments ('Totally fair') followed by logistics; the emotionally meaningful moment about jobs and his cousin's denial was met with a checklist plan rather than reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session drifted toward procedural logistics—timelines, checklists, scripts—rather than staying focused on the target behavior of marijuana use and its risks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Expert-driven, service-oriented interaction supplying pre-packaged checklists and scripts; Jamal's own ideas about his use, friends, and family did not shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered menu-style choices and did not pressure change, but choices centered on handouts/scripts, and advice-giving tone ('The safest move is...') subtly directed rather than empowered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Essentially no evocation of Jamal's own motivations; his spontaneous change talk ('keeping it clean isn't the worst idea') was ignored and replaced with external reasons and procedural advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor acted as an information/service provider rather than treating Jamal as the expert on his own life, never drawing on his knowledge of his use patterns, friends, or family.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Missed Jamal's spontaneous change talk about keeping things clean and his genuine question about record consequences; never asked open questions to evoke desire, ability, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk, preventing escalation, but used no double-sided reflections or rolling with minimization, and never built discrepancy with Jamal's own concerns about his record and jobs.
