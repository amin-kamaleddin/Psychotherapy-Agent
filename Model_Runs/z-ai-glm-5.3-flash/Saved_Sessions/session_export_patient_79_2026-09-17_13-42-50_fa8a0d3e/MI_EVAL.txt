# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone even amid Luis's sarcasm, offering validation and affirmations, and honoring his confidentiality requests. Falls short of 5 due to somewhat formulaic warmth and safety instructions repeated nearly verbatim four times.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening that captured Luis's core frustration and consistently echoed his language. Reflections were often brief and quickly pivoted to checklists or tool offers, missing chances to explore deeper emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session anchored to asthma management with appropriate safety information and a concrete closing plan. Direction was maintained but shallow—core target behaviors (medication use, follow-ups) and long-term goals were never explored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently asked permission before offering tools and co-constructed a plan shaped by Luis's own constraints, confirmed by his comment that the counselor 'actually listened.' The counselor's pre-packaged tools dominated the content rather than eliciting Luis's own strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support—repeatedly offering explicit options, affirming client control, and honoring refusals and confidentiality requests without argument. The session's strongest dimension.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on dispensing tools rather than drawing out Luis's own motivations; open questions evoking reasons for change were largely absent. Spontaneous change talk was briefly affirmed but never explored or amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Luis as the decision-maker, with his contributions visibly shaping the session's final plan. Rarely explicitly named his expertise or invited his own ideas beyond pre-packaged tools.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Affirmed Luis's efforts ('I did the breathing today') and reinforced his tentative commitment in the closing reflection. Missed key opportunities to deepen change talk, such as exploring what made the session 'not as bad as I thought,' and never explicitly elicited reasons for change, confidence, or visions of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing against Luis's sarcasm and dismissiveness, responding with neutral, accepting reflections. However, sustain talk was tolerated rather than softened—no double-sided reflections or strategic reframes (e.g., his unwillingness to attend weekly visits implied motivation that went unused).
