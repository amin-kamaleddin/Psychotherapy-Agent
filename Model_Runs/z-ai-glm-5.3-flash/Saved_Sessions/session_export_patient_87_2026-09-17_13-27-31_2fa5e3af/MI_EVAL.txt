# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental tone with a few affirmations, but praise is mostly tied to plan compliance rather than unconditional regard. Marco's discouragement ('part of me's still waiting to fail') goes unacknowledged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; disclosures about shame, fear of failure, and family stress are repeatedly bypassed in favor of advice. The one empathic gesture is immediately followed by strategy suggestions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical focus on the target behavior from triggers through contingency planning and follow-up. Only limitation: focus stays on logistics and never links back to Marco's deeper values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Despite 'want to...?' framings, the session is largely expert-driven with bulleted strategies, prescribed cues, and unilateral scripts. Marco is positioned as a plan-follower rather than an equal contributor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and asks permission, but the fast-paced, prescriptive style creates pressure to comply. Little explicit reinforcement of Marco's ownership, especially when he voices doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is treated as something to install via tools rather than drawn out from Marco. His own reasons for change surface despite, not because of, the counselor's technique and are never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine collaborative gestures (choice of nights, role-play), but the counselor mostly operates as the expert delivering strategies. Marco's expertise about his own household is minimally used.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marco generates strong change talk ('for my kids', 'maybe an eight') that the counselor fails to reflect, affirm, or deepen. The confidence scale is used, but change talk is acknowledged mechanically rather than cultivated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but worries are met with barrier-management and if-then problem-solving rather than double-sided reflections. Attention stays on difficulty rather than Marco's capability and motivation.
