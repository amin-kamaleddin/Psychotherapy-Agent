# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard from start to close, with specific individualized affirmations ('real evidence of your strength,' 'that took courage to say out loud'). Minor note: 'I love that' repeated in consecutive turns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening in every turn, including complex double-sided reflections and vivid summaries. Deduction for deflecting Hana's direct question about what to do when her mind blanks without acknowledging the request or asking permission to share information.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on the exam avoidance goal throughout, progressing logically toward a concrete plan. The double-barreled questions occasionally diluted focus but direction remained steady.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evocative questions consistently invite the patient's own ideas ('what would help you most,' 'what feels like it would actually reach you'). The patient explicitly owns the plan ('it feels like mine, not something being forced on me').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient selects the pace and sequence herself ('one scary thing at a time'), which the clinician affirms: 'You chose this pace, these steps, and your own words.' No pushing or directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Bridge question converts past success into present change talk; patient produces desire, need, and ability statements ('I can't wait to feel ready because I never will'). Each change statement is reflected and reinforced rather than dropped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently builds on patient's ideas, echoes her metaphors ('trapped is better than stuck,' 'one scary thing at a time'), and reframes her own words ('building proof instead'). Genuine back-and-forth exchange throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook arc from desire → plan → commitment, ending with a commitment question that elicits concrete, measurable language ('I want to have written that reminder — actually done it'). Strongest dimension of the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective pivots reframe sustain talk (canceled once → studying rebuilds trust; avoidance → planning). One deduction: the 'scariest part' probe invited a full turn of negative focus, though the paired hopeful reflection contained the damage.
