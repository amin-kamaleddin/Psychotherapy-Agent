# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, explicitly disclaiming judgment and affirming the client's courage and worth with no trace of disapproval about the drinking disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections consistently captured both sides of ambivalence, deepening disclosure as evidenced by the client saying 'saying it to you made it click.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use while guiding progressively from exploration through ambivalence to a concrete plan and relapse-prevention anticipation, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A non-hierarchical tone was set from the start and every strategy came from the client; the plan was fully co-developed and the client left with his own self-generated solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership with no persuasive or coercive language; the client explicitly framed the plan as his own choice made on his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All reasons for change and strategies were elicited from the client through skillful open questions, then developed by exploring feasibility, obstacles, and how it feels to self-generate solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently positioned within the client, with no advice offered; the client's contributions substantially shaped the session direction and plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions strategically escalated from exploring concerns to commitment-oriented planning, evoking strong client change talk like 'That's probably smart for me' and 'it's just a plan. Mine.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally handled well with double-sided reflections and redirection toward solutions; one question briefly invited elaboration of reasons against change, though it was used productively.
