# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains consistent respect and warmth, with no judgment of Jordan's hopelessness. However, affirmations ('That's real progress') feel somewhat formulaic and lack individualized depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening accurately captures Jordan's discouragement and worry. However, reflections occasionally shift to reassurance and problem-solving, and the self-critical 'voice in my head' is acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the follow-up target behavior from opening question through concrete action planning. Every exchange moves toward a specific, achievable step without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Options and preferences are consistently checked, and Jordan's input shaped the plan (times, email format, accountability request). However, the agenda is driven by prepared menus rather than genuinely co-generated ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is regularly offered and Jordan sets his own pace, but autonomy is weakened by the counselor's pattern of drafting materials and pre-packaging steps, reducing Jordan's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine evocation occurred via the small-change question and confidence ruler, but the counselor frequently supplies content (drafted emails, intro bullets) rather than eliciting from Jordan. Missed follow-ups like 'What would make it a 7?' weakened this.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jordan's input is invited, but much of the session follows an expert-driven pattern where the counselor offers menus and drafts materials rather than fostering Jordan's own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk through the small-change question, intro rehearsal, and confidence ruler (2 to 6). Asking Jordan to self-echo his reasons would have deepened it further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not dwelled on, but validation sometimes occurs without pivoting toward change ('It's okay not to expect miracles'). Hopelessness language is occasionally echoed rather than redirected toward Jordan's agency.
