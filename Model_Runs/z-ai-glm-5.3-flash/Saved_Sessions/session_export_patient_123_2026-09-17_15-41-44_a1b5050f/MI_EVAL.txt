# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with repeated acknowledgments of Sofia's honesty and courage. Affirmations are somewhat generic and her resistance to the 'binge' label is not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Sofia's perspective as a precontemplative teen who sees her drinking as normal. They remain content-level, without exploring the emotional layer beneath her minimization or fear of being judged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains an exceptionally clear focus on alcohol use, progressing logically from screening reaction to quantity, consequences, willingness, confidence, and a concrete plan. Even with choices offered, the session never drifts off-target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed, with Sofia's preferences visibly shaping the outcome (water rule, script line, telling Mia). The harm-reduction information segment shifts to a more expert-driven, lecture-like delivery.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength—consistent choice framing ('Your call,' 'totally your choice'), permission sought before advice, and a plan built entirely from options Sofia selected. Her low-commitment framing is accepted without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions, the willingness question, and the confidence ruler effectively elicit Sofia's own reasons and self-generated solutions. Deeper personal values and goals are not explored, leaving motivation anchored to convenience.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is repeatedly positioned as the decision-maker, and her contributions directly shape the plan. The risk-information delivery remains counselor-directed, and permission is sought only after the agenda is largely set.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled use of open questions, the confidence ruler, and scaling follow-ups draws out incremental change talk ('maybe pacing drinks isn't the worst idea,' 'I can try that much') and concrete planning. Change talk remains thin and could be deepened by linking to Sofia's own goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk ('it's just normal stuff,' 'binge sounds dramatic') is mostly mirrored back neutrally rather than met with double-sided reflections or pivots to Sofia's own concerns. Risk information delivery risks evoking defensiveness rather than softening resistance.
