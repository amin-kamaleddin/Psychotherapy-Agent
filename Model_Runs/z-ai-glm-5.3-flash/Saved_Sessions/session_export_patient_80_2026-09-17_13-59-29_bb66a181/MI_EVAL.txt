# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalized slips without blame. However, affirmations were somewhat generic and formulaic rather than individualized to Luka's specific strengths and insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were occasionally accurate, but the counselor relied heavily on questions and plan-giving rather than reflective listening. Meaningful emotional material (shame, fear of spiraling, partner noticing his agitation) was rarely mirrored and often met with logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on reducing alcohol use and managing risk, with concrete goals, triggers, tracking, and a clear check-in plan. Minor deduction for narrowing quickly to alcohol mechanics without linking back to Luka's deeper stated motivation or the violence dimension.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Luka chose the goal and modified the plan, but the power balance tilted toward the counselor, who repeatedly delivered pre-packaged plans and then asked for sign-off. Genuine collaboration would involve eliciting Luka's ideas before offering options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly offered choice, asked what felt doable, and accepted Luka's rejection of abstinence and scripts without pushback. Deduction because the rapid-fire plan presentation took on a mildly directive checklist tone, especially in repetitive closing turns.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Goal and trigger questions elicited some genuine motivation, but evocation was superficial and inconsistent. The counselor supplied strategies rather than exploring values, confidence, or discrepancies, and Luka's strongest motivational statements were met with logistics instead of deepening.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor let Luka choose the drink limit, but the style was largely expert-driven, with pre-packaged bulleted plans presented first and tweaked after. Luka's best ideas came from himself, but the counselor did not explicitly credit his expertise or co-construct strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Goal-setting invitations produced genuine change talk ('I've come too far to let one night undo everything'), but the counselor did not deliberately deepen or reinforce these moments. Change talk emerged largely from Luka himself rather than skilled evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not amplify sustain talk, but also made no deliberate attempt to soften or reframe ambivalence about pub culture and quitting. The harm-reduction framing implicitly respected his reluctance, but no double-sided reflections or strategic pivoting toward change language were used.
