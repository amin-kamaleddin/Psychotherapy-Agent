# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor was consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, affirming Ingrid's proactive safety check and accommodating her time constraints. Warmth was somewhat formulaic, with little explicit affirmation of her beyond her actions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal; the counselor did not mirror Ingrid's relief, worry about the shortened washout, or time pressure. Responses were largely informational rather than demonstrating understanding of her perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on the medication safety concern was maintained throughout, with a concrete follow-up plan established. Some drift toward logistics (reminder timing/format) occurred but the core agenda stayed on target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early interactions were expert-driven Q&A, but genuine co-planning emerged later when Ingrid shaped the follow-up time, format, and agenda. Power-sharing was uneven overall.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor offered scheduling options, asked for her preferences, and set no pressure. Autonomy support was mostly procedural rather than reinforcing her ownership of health decisions like dosing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was assumed to come from the clinician's information rather than drawn from the client. Ingrid's spontaneous motivation statements were not explored or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invited Ingrid's input and let her agenda shape the follow-up, but the session's first half was largely expert-driven information delivery. Her expertise was never explicitly acknowledged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ingrid offered self-motivated statements about safe medication use, but the counselor did not deepen or reflect them. Questions stayed logistical rather than exploring her motivations or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not dwell on her stated barriers and gently maintained next steps despite rushed exits. However, there was no active reframing of her time pressure, making the handling neutral rather than skillful.
