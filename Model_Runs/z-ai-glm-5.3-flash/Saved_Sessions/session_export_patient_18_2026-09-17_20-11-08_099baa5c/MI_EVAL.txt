# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with explicit nonjudgment framing ('a person you can talk to honestly, without judgment'). Anya confirms this landed, noting she expected a lecture and instead left with a plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture emotional nuance, such as the shift from 'white-knuckling it' to feeling calm and in control. Anya repeatedly confirms feeling understood ('Yeah, exactly,' 'Thanks for this, seriously').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol-related decision-making and protective planning throughout the session. The conversation progresses logically from values to a concrete plan and ends with a commitment, with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The tone is that of equal partners, with the counselor co-exploring and letting Anya's answers shape next steps. Power-sharing is strong but not fully mutual, as the counselor drives the questioning sequence fairly consistently.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice with no advice-giving or pressure, framing the plan as entirely Anya's ('you built it yourself'). Would need more explicit emphasis that Anya owns the timeline and outcome, including that texting her sister tonight is her call.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within by asking what matters most to Anya, what would work for her, and what would feel different at her next party. Her key insights emerged from evocative questions, not counselor-provided reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly locates expertise in Anya ('You know her best — how do you think that conversation would go?'), and her contributions visibly shape the session. Falls short of 5 because she is rarely explicitly framed as the decision-maker for the overall agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked commitment language, notably prompting 'I'm actually going to text her tonight.' Could have deepened commitment further with techniques like confidence ratings or explicit commitment strengthening.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** When Anya voiced ambivalence ('I don't want to be the boring one'), the counselor used an effective double-sided reflection and pivoted toward her values and planning. No reinforcement of sustain talk occurred.
