# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental regard throughout; the relapse is reframed positively ('three days is three days longer than nothing') and resistance is welcomed without pressure or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections capture both sides of ambivalence and underlying meaning, e.g., the cigarette as 'the one thing that's mine' is honored rather than pathologized.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear, progressive focus on cessation, building from what derailed the last attempt to a concrete, slip-survivable plan anchored in her own 'why.'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative — the sister strategy, the book, the 2am plan, and the reason card all originate from Elena, and the plan is written 'exactly as you said them.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are explicitly offered ('If you wanted to...'), pacing is deferential, and her hedging ('I'm not promising anything') is accepted without pushback, with ownership returned to her throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight is self-generated, from 'I'd still be trying' to the final 'I'm doing this' — the clinician never supplies reasons, only systematically draws out Elena's own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located in the client, her contributions demonstrably shape the plan, and the closing solution (reading her words after a slip) is her own, not overridden.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and amplified, culminating in strong commitment language ('I'm doing this') that Elena generated herself; pivotal self-insights are reflected back and slowed down deliberately.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated and pivoted toward change with well-executed double-sided reflections, though the fear of slipping is explored at some length before redirecting — an exploration that ultimately produced the session's key reframe.
