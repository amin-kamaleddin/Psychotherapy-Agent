# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with an angry, defensive client; however, affirmations were at times formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured both surface content and subtle underlying concerns, though reflections sometimes served as springboards into option-listing that Nabil experienced as minimizing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus throughout, with each turn building logically from validation to boss-conversation priority, script rehearsal, role-play, and skill generalization.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary collaborative practice: permission-seeking, shared decision-making, and co-created scripts and role-play with Nabil's ideas substantially shaping the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offered menus and honored Nabil's choices while avoiding pressure; could have more explicitly reinforced his ownership and self-directed capability.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The pivotal 'change one thing' question successfully evoked Nabil's own priority and change talk, but early turns leaned on expert options and confidence/importance were never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated permission-asking and choice-offering let Nabil genuinely shape the session, though pre-packaged scripts sometimes positioned the counselor as expert rather than drawing wording from Nabil.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully recognized and built on Nabil's change statements through rehearsal and role-play, though early evocation was limited before the boss-conversation hook emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument, but the counselor partially reinforced external blame and never used double-sided reflections to pivot toward Nabil's own agency.
