# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with explicit affirmation of Margaret's strengths and autonomy. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and brief, keeping this from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was consistently accurate, capturing her ambivalence and the fall's significance. Reflections were summative rather than emotionally deep, not exploring the loneliness or boredom underlying her drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol from the opening question through closing arrangements. Every exchange advanced a concrete plan with specific nights, numbers, and accountability, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was largely co-constructed, with Margaret shaping nights, substitutes, and logistics. A strategy-heavy turn tilted toward prescriptive advice before fully exploring her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision point was framed as a choice, and slips were handled non-judgmentally. Margaret set terms, declined role-play, and the counselor respected her limits without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor drew out Margaret's own reasons, strengths, and words for the plan, producing genuine internalized motivation. Occasionally supplied reasons and solutions rather than consistently letting her generate them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Margaret was positioned as the expert, and her preferences substantially shaped the plan and logistics. A dense bundle of prescriptive strategies offered without first asking which appealed to her kept this from a 5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance/confidence rulers skillfully evoked change talk and concrete commitment, culminating in Margaret's closing insight. Change talk was evoked mainly through structured questions rather than deeper strategic exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced, and double-sided reflections honored both sides without amplifying status quo arguments. Sustain talk was largely neutralized by redirection rather than actively reframed to tip the balance toward change.
