# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout, directly addressing anticipated stigma ('I'm not here to judge you') and validating both sides of ambivalence. No judgmental language even when Derrick minimizes, and affirmations feel genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and deep, capturing both content and emotion (e.g., the blackout fear, the humiliation). Reflections build progressively on each disclosure rather than repeating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear, progressive focus on drinking/driving risk and relapse prevention from start to finish. Every question ties back to preventing another DUI and rebuilding a sustainable life.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction operates between equal partners, with active co-construction ('let's work on that line for your boys'). Derrick's engagement visibly grows from checking a box to genuine shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Derrick's ownership and choice, avoiding pressure and accepting his stated pace. Change is framed as his decision to protect his own license and job.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivations for change come from Derrick himself, drawn out through strategic questions about worry, past success, and barriers. His spontaneous commitment ('I'll give this a real shot') demonstrates masterful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise clearly resides with the client, who generates his own plan, refusal lines, and first steps. The counselor repeatedly attributes ideas to Derrick ('That's your own plan coming from your own head').

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully and consistently evokes change talk through questions targeting consequences, ability, and commitment, explicitly highlighting Derrick's momentum. Moves him from 'bad luck' to 'worth figuring this out for real.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk without arguing or dismissing it, gently challenging minimization while respecting Derrick's pace. Reluctance to promise big changes is met with acceptance and a shift to small steps.
