# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with affirmations of Tariq's compliance and effort. However, affirmations were somewhat generic and repetitive ('that matters').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but brief and repetitive, quickly followed by solution offers. Deeper emotional themes (fear of disappointing his mother, sense of unfairness) went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on restoring contact with his mother and supporting compliance through release. Direction leaned heavily on logistical problem-solving rather than linking actions to Tariq's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving; Tariq edited drafts and shaped the plan, and explicitly felt heard. The counselor's early ready-made drafts shifted the early phase toward counselor-driven solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated choices were offered and Tariq's preferences (simplified note, hand-delivery) were respected. Some early multi-option menus felt prescriptive, and the counselor occasionally told Tariq what he should value.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation emerged in Tariq's own words, but evocation was superficial; open questions about reasons, confidence, and hope were rare, with quick shifts to providing solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tariq's input meaningfully shaped the drafts and delivery plan, and he reported feeling genuinely heard. The counselor's fast production of complete documents limited space for co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tariq's change talk was largely spontaneous; the counselor anchored to it occasionally but used no strategic open questions to evoke commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about the 'unfair' officer and futility was acknowledged without amplification, but not actively softened with double-sided reflections or pivots toward Tariq's stated goals.
