# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, validating Matteo's perspective and framing setbacks as 'information, not failure.' Would be stronger with explicit affirmations of his honesty, insight, and social challenges.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures Matteo's ambivalence and practical realities. Rarely reflects emotions, leaving his anxiety about social exclusion unaddressed at an emotional level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in the injury context from the opening question onward. Every exchange builds progressively toward a concrete, testable behavioral plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving, with Matteo's own ideas substantially shaping the plan. Occasional expert-style information dumps of strategies and scripts briefly shift away from full collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Matteo's ownership ('I'm here to explore options and support your choice'), never pressuring or moralizing. The 'information, not failure' framing and support for self-experimentation are exemplary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Matteo's own motivations, including his curiosity about the four-drink experiment and morning-after reflections. Falls short of a 5 due to occasional psychoeducation filling space rather than deepening his stated reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Matteo as the expert by repeatedly inviting him to select options and co-construct the plan. The counselor at times leads by providing lists rather than first eliciting Matteo's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk through rulers, choice-point questions, and open exploration. Matteo generates nearly all the specific change ideas, commitment language, and self-insight himself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing or dwelling on Matteo's minimization about the ankle and denial of problems, pivoting instead to practical exploration. Some sustain talk passes without strategic reframing or double-sided reflection.
