# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental despite Elena's defensiveness, validating her efforts and countering the shaming she experienced. Warmth occasionally felt clinical/formulaic, e.g., the early safety check seemed procedural rather than attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captured her ambivalence and validated feeling blamed. Reflections were often brief and quickly pivoted to advice, cutting short exploration of deeper feelings like guilt and fear of 'falling apart.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and secondhand/thirdhand exposure, ending with a specific, measurable plan. The target behavior was defined by the counselor rather than collaboratively clarified, with some drift into generic stress tips.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena co-created the limited gum trial and the counselor offered to tweak the plan together. However, the counselor often responded to disclosures with unsolicited multi-part advice and pre-packaged scripts rather than joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly and consistently reinforced Elena's choice with no-pressure language and framing the plan as an 'experiment.' She explicitly tested autonomy ('I get to say so') and the counselor affirmed it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely elicited Elena's own reasons, hopes, or confidence—motivation was built mainly through counselor-supplied psychoeducation and technique. A potent conditional commitment ('If it was really hurting him... I'd do it') went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tone was respectful but the psychiatrist frequently assumed the expert role with dense instructional blocks, leaving Elena's contributions modest. The session's direction was largely counselor-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Capitalized on Elena's change talk with the gum offer and experiment framing, eliciting a commitment to try it tonight. Little explicit evocation of her own reasons or confidence; momentum came mostly from counselor-driven structure.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged defenses without reinforcing them, reframed via psychoeducation rather than arguing, and accepted her limit ('I'm not saying I'm quitting') without challenge. Some detailed cessation tips subtly kept the risk narrative salient instead of pivoting to her motivation.
