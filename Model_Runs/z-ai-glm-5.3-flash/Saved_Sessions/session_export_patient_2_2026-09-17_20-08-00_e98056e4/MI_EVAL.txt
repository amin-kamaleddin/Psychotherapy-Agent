# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout ('no pressure and no agenda here'), affirming the client's worth even when she expresses resistance. No judgment appears at any point.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Delivers consistently accurate, deep reflections capturing both content and emotion, e.g., 'I hear two things at once: you need that evening break to stay yours, AND you're willing to experiment.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on evening smoking reduction while honoring the client's pacing, anchoring each exchange to the concrete plan and gently planting seeds on alcohol without derailing focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint throughout, asking permission-style questions and letting the client define the follow-up agenda. The plan emerged entirely from the client's own parameters.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces choice and ownership ('that conversation will be yours'), leading the client to realize her past attempts failed because 'it was never really my choice.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from within via open questions about success markers, coping strategies, and ownership, eliciting strong change talk like 'I can do this' and 'my son's worth more than my pride.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Places expertise with the client ('You know your life and schedule better than anyone'), with client contributions substantially shaping the session. She confirms leaving with 'a plan I chose myself.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and strengthens commitment language through questions like 'What would tell you at the end of the week that it's actually working?' The client produced rich change talk and self-efficacy statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Meets sustain talk (fear of quitting all at once, 'no promises' about drinking) with acceptance rather than argument, normalizing pacing and pivoting to the client's own curiosity.
