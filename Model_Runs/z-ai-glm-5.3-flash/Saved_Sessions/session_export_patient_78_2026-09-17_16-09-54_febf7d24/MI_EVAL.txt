### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently validated Lucía's fatigue with lectures, honored her 'no lectures' and 'no big deal' rules, and accepted her topic pivot without judgment. No disapproval or labeling anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, accurately capturing her stress, the meaning of her smoke break, and her confidence fluctuations. Reflections occasionally stayed at summary level rather than exploring the emotional meaning beneath her defensiveness (e.g., fear of failure).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus, skillfully converting her work-stress detour into a bridge back to smoking. The session ended with a concrete, client-owned behavioral plan without pressure beyond her readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner, offering menus of options and incorporating all of Lucía's modifications without correction. Multi-option prompts subtly steered rather than fully opening direction to her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: repeatedly offered choice ('your call'), framed the plan as a 'tiny experiment, not a promise,' and respected her boundaries. No coercive or persuasive language used.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited her own reasons, confidence ratings, and commitment language rather than lecturing. Missed chances to deepen surfacing motivation (her daughter's concern, her closing 'it's a start').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Lucía as the expert, and her choices genuinely shaped the session. Frequent option-listing slightly structured the agenda for her rather than acknowledging her expertise more explicitly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited client-generated commitment through open questions and confidence scaling, producing meaningful change talk. Missed opportunities to explore her reasons more deeply and amplify her closing 'it's a start' statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing for change, accepting her pivots and reframing potential failure as data rather than failure. Extended some time validating her reasons for maintaining the status quo.
