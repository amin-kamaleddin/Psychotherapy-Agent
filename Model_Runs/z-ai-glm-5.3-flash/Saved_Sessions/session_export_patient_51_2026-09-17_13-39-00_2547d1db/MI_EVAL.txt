# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with no disapproval, but warmth was formulaic and missed natural affirmations of Ingrid's proactive checking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were no reflective listening statements at all; Ingrid's time constraint, reassurance, and desire for peace of mind were never acknowledged or validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor kept clear, accurate focus on the safety-critical citalopram-phenelzine interaction, though direction became rigidly repetitive in the closing exchanges.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction was a one-way information transaction with no shared agenda-setting or joint problem-solving; the client's contributions did not shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** An optional tailored message service acknowledged some choice, but the overall tone was directive and caution-heavy, with advice delivered rather than choice fostered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Essentially no evocation occurred; no open questions about motivations, confidence, or plans, and the client's stated intentions were met with instruction repetition rather than curiosity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session was strongly expert-driven with unidirectional information delivery and no inquiry into what the client already knew or wanted; her decline of the tailored message was simply overridden.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client generated commitment statements about messaging her doctor and ringing her GP, but the counselor did not reinforce, reflect, or deepen them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** While sustain talk was not amplified, the counselor ignored disengagement cues and repeatedly re-delivered the same emergency warning long after the client said goodbye, missing chances to consolidate her intentions.
