# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates warmth and unconditional positive regard, normalizing uncertainty, affirming the patient's caution, and celebrating self-generated ideas without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including catching the deeper meaning of the patient's 'brushing off' habit and echoing his father-related motivation back powerfully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, progressive focus on target behaviors with concrete steps, timeline, and accountability; no aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative interaction where the patient's ideas shaped the agenda and plans; the closing negotiation of next visit's focus demonstrates equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces ownership throughout by positioning the patient as expert, offering choices, and accepting his conditionality without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully draws motivation from within the patient, eliciting his deeply personal reason for change (his father's heart trouble) and linking it to concrete action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions the patient as the expert and builds his contributions into the session agenda without imposing any plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertly evokes and reinforces progressively stronger change talk, from 'maybe I could cut back' to 'maybe I'll surprise myself,' amplifying self-generated ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
