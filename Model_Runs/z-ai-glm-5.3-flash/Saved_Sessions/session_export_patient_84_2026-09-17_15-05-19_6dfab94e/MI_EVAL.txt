# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, affirming Malik's bluntness and validating his desire for peace without criticizing his cannabis use. Warmth occasionally feels generic with little explicit affirmation of Malik as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers consistent, accurate reflections incorporating new information, staying attuned to Malik's perspective. Misses deeper emotional content like his anxiety about becoming his cousin and the weight of 'I'm not him'.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on a concrete, client-endorsed target: reducing mother-son conflict. Though the focus never shifts to cannabis use, this is a defensible patient-centered choice given Malik's precontemplative stance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently checks in and adjusts based on Malik's feedback, such as simplifying the scripted line to teen language. Several plans arrive as pre-packaged clinician products rather than co-constructed solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers genuine menus of options and accepts Malik's rejections without pressure, pivoting to his choices. Falls short of a 5 because some advice-giving subtly undercuts autonomy and explicit affirmations of his ownership are absent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits Malik's own goals ('get off my back', 'less yelling wouldn't suck') but channels motivation only toward conflict management. Never explores his ambivalence about cannabis use or what the cousin's story means to him personally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and offers choices, but repeatedly hands over expert-crafted scripts and pre-packaged plans despite Malik's pushback ('I don't need you writing scripts for me'). Little recognition that Malik holds expertise about his own family.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges ('less yelling wouldn't suck', 'I could actually try that') but is prompted by agenda-setting rather than skillful deepening. No confidence/importance scaling and missed chances to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and doesn't reinforce sustain talk, but uses no double-sided reflections or the cousin story to develop discrepancy. Some prompts even focus on avoiding mom's detection rather than examining the substance use.
