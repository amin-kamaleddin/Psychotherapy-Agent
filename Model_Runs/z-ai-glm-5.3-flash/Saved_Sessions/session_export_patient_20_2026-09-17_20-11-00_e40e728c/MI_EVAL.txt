# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated unconditional positive regard throughout ('no judgment either way,' 'it's information, not a verdict'). Never labeled or disputed the client's minimization, treating his perspective with genuine respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including an accurate double-sided reflection of his ambivalence about Thursday nights. Fell slightly short by not exploring the emotional weight of disappointing family or his self-conscious shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing drinking with every turn advancing toward a concrete target. Session concluded with a specific, measurable behavioral commitment and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian interaction built on permission-style questions and the client's own suggestions. Plan content and accountability structure came entirely from the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized client choice and accepted his caveat ('all bets are off') without pushback. Framed the plan as something he can own, with no pressure or judgment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully elicited change talk through Socratic questions that led the client to argue against his own sustain talk. Affirmations reinforced internal motivation ('that came from you, not me').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert on his own life. His ideas shaped the low-key strategy, reminder, and accountability, which the counselor amplified rather than replaced.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions directly drew out the client's own motivations, from the 'good-enough first step' to curiosity about Friday morning. Reinforced his self-generated plan and progress.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by using double-sided reflections and gently testing the social worry ('is that something you know for sure?'). Kept focus on change without confrontation.
