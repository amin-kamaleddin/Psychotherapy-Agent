# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained despite the client's irritation about the wait. Affirmations were specific and genuine, and past failures and the biscuit habit were never judged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured both desire and barriers, including the deeper all-or-nothing insight. Emotional nuance beneath the pattern and the client's guardedness was named but not deeply unpacked.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on weight/activity despite time pressure and initial resistance. Every exchange advanced toward a concrete, contingency-planned intervention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor operated between equals, inviting input before proposing anything and co-constructing the plan. The client's own ideas became the centerpiece of the intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was consistently reinforced and no advice was imposed. The client's ownership of the plan was explicitly affirmed throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited almost entirely from the client, who self-generated the reasons, measures, and full intervention. The counselor consistently developed rather than supplied expert content.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in the client ('you know your schedule best'). The entire plan originated from Andrei's ideas, with the counselor only shaping and affirming.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation and amplification of change talk, including reframing 'it all fell apart' as commitment. The client's arc from 'I haven't got time' to booking a follow-up demonstrates masterful cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged but not dwelt upon, with skillful pivots that reframed barriers into planning. Occasional reflections briefly mirrored the impossibility framing before redirecting.
