# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, including nonjudgmental handling of Mateo's admission about 'stretching the truth.' No labeling or conditional regard present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures Mateo's frustration, values, and emotional barriers. Reflections tend toward summary-style, and deeper shame/resignation is touched but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior (physical activity/rehab progress) with no meaningful drift. Every exchange connects to walking, pain management, or follow-through metrics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed, with Mateo rejecting options, inventing the indoor backup, and correcting the follow-up agenda. The counselor explicitly offers to adjust and practice together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy repeatedly affirmed with no pressure and safety suggestions offered as a question. Rapid four-option menus slightly crowd the client's generative space.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation via open questions and a confidence ruler that produced client-generated solutions. Sometimes supplies solutions/menus rather than trusting open questions, and misses following up on 'maybe she sees something I don't.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mateo's contributions authoritatively shape the plan, and agenda-setting is transferred to him. Menu-style question stacks subtly lead rather than fully open space for his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes motivation, producing genuine change talk ('I'll give it a shot,' 'I already know I'd feel better afterward'). Does not always deepen or strategically reinforce these moments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but never amplified, with effective pivots toward change and fallback planning. Occasionally listing barriers back ('poor sleep, cold/damp days') briefly re-focuses on reasons against change.
