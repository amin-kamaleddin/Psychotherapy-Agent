# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with validation of defensiveness and strengths. However, affirmations are brief and formulaic, missing chances for deeper, individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections consistently capture both content and emotion, such as acknowledging the 'gut punch' moment. Some repetition in structure and occasional skimming of the emotional surface limit depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use throughout and guides the session from defensiveness to a concrete, reviewable tracking plan with follow-up. Occasional meandering into generic risk education slightly dilutes momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames work jointly, checks in before sharing information, and lets Marek choose the tracking plan. Collaboration weakens during didactic delivery of guidelines and cheat-sheet, where the counselor supplies most content rather than co-constructing meaning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongest domain — offers choices at nearly every turn, repeatedly emphasizes no pressure, and immediately accommodates limits like avoiding an app. Marek's ownership of the process is consistently reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client motivation emerges, but the counselor relies heavily on supplying information rather than drawing out Marek's own values and confidence. Rich change-talk seeds ('I don't love that', 'if I got sick') were left unexplored, with no rulers or open questions probing meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids an authoritarian stance and asks permission repeatedly, but expertise-sharing is one-directional, delivering information without inviting Marek's own knowledge or ideas. Power-sharing is polite but lukewarm rather than genuinely client-expert-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged and the menu of options helped Marek select a step, but key opportunities to deepen it were missed. Moments like 'I guess I've got something to think about' were pivoted past rather than explored for ambivalence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without arguing or reinforcing it, using neutral reflections and effective double-sided reflection. A slightly more deliberate pivot toward Marek's emerging concerns could have strengthened this.
