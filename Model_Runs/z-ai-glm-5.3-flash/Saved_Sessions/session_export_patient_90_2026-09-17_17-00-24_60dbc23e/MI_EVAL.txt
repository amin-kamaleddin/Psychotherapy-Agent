# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and honored Marcus's values, including the dog boundary. However, explicit affirmations of his strengths and honesty were limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early reflective listening that captured both practical and emotional content, including his fear of nighttime attacks. Reflections became sparse later as the focus shifted to scripts and logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session consistently anchored on asthma management, gently redirecting when Marcus drifted to finances and work. Progressed logically from exploration to a concrete plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed around Marcus's constraints and preferences. Fell short of full collaboration because the counselor tended to offer pre-formed menus rather than open brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength — every step was framed as Marcus's choice with no pressure applied. Explicitly reinforced his ownership of decisions, including boundary-setting at the appointment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers effectively elicited Marcus's motivations, but much of the session relied on counselor-provided solutions rather than drawing out his own ideas. Missed opportunities to explore his vision of success and values more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marcus's input shaped the plan, but the counselor's directive style and rapid, stacked questions limited genuine power-sharing. Pre-packaged scripts dominated much of the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used importance/confidence rulers and small-step prompts, moving Marcus from ambivalence to clear commitment (confidence 8, plan to text his wife). Could have deepened commitment language further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argued against sustain talk; instead accommodated it respectfully, such as drafting the clinic note about the dog. Lacked strategic double-sided reflections to actively resolve ambivalence rather than sidestep it.
