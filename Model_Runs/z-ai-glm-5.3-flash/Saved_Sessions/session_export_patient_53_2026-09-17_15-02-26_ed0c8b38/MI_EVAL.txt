### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout ('I'm glad you're here,' 'Even four minutes is progress'). Isabella's hesitancy and caveats are accepted without pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of her ambivalence and avoidance ('staying home feels safer'). Reflections occasionally stay on safety/ease themes without deepening into emotional meaning of isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained focus on community engagement from opening question to a concrete plan (Tuesday 10:00 AM café, timer, scheduled send). Each exchange moved toward actionable change without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Permission-seeking, invited edits to drafts, and joint brainstorming. The final plan was fully co-constructed from Isabella's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** 'You're in charge' framing throughout; options offered neutrally, and refusal to lock in was met with flexibility. No coercive or pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Readiness ruler and open questions drew out her own reasons and solutions (timer, reminder, support person). Pre-formed option menus somewhat limited client-generated ideas, and confidence was never explicitly explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent power-sharing with Isabella shaping every element of the plan—café, timing, exit cue, reminder time, message wording. Counselor positioned her as expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Readiness ruler effectively elicited openness ('maybe a 3... I'm not saying I'd never go'), with commitment language strengthening to 'I'll give the café a shot.' More explicit confidence/ability exploration would elevate this.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without reinforcement and gently pivoted toward solutions ('what would help if that happens?'). Morning motivation loss was met with a practical fix rather than a reflective reframe.
