# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with no judgment, even when Jordan shared sustain talk or misinformation. His skepticism was affirmed as thoughtful, and his 'no lecture' condition was explicitly honored.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both content and underlying meaning—autonomy, distrust of 'trust me' answers, and family responsibility. Notable examples include reframing sustain talk as 'show me it's really minor, and maybe' and reflecting his grandmother-centered motivation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on flu vaccination throughout with no topic drift. Each turn advanced toward resolution, ending in a concrete plan and firm commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as a genuine exchange between equals, with Jordan setting the agenda and the counselor sharing information only on request. Jordan's questions and conditions directly shaped the session's course.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fostered choice, honored Jordan's 'no lecture' condition, and offered information as material for his own weighing. The final decision was repeatedly attributed to Jordan rather than to persuasion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited Jordan's own motivations—the grandmother-centered 'why' and his own cost-benefit analysis—through open questions. The counselor never supplied the conclusion; Jordan discovered it himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Jordan as the expert from the first turn, and his contributions substantially shaped the session. The counselor repeatedly credited Jordan with his own insights.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evoked and amplified change talk, leading Jordan to articulate his own reasons and ultimately clear commitment language. The counselor reinforced that the commitment 'came from you.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Responded to sustain talk and misinformation with affirmation, reframes, and neutral data rather than argument. Jordan was allowed to draw his own conclusions, and sustain talk was never reinforced.
