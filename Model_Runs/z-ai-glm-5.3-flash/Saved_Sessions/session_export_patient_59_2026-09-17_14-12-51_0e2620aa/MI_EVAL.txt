# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor remained nonjudgmental and respectful throughout, but interactions were largely transactional, with no affirmations or warmth beyond a single 'I'm not here to judge' statement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal reflective listening; the counselor did not track Jamal's frustration or minimization, even repeating the same closing script four times after the client said he'd already heard it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Clear administrative focus on completing the court form, but no therapeutic direction toward the referral concern (cannabis use), which was touched only as a data field.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The session was a checklist dictated by the counselor, with Jamal reduced to filling in fields and no shared agenda or joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor offered choices and respected Jamal's 'no treatment' preference, but the support was hollow—no emphasis on Jamal's ownership or capacity for self-directed change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No motivation was evoked from the client; the counselor never explored Jamal's values, goals, or feelings about the citation, leaving motivation purely external (court compliance).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor acted primarily as a paperwork processor, with no recognition of Jamal's expertise about his own life and no agenda co-created beyond a menu of options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No effort to evoke change talk; there were no open questions about Jamal's use, its role in his life, or any downsides he perceives.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Jamal's minimizations ('one small bag,' 'no one cares') were documented verbatim and accepted uncritically, with no double-sided reflections, reframing, or gentle pivoting toward change.
