# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, affirming Eric, normalizing potential slips, and explicitly offering 'no judgment either way.' Honored Eric's request for pride ('don't tell Sarah') with playful respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflections throughout, capturing Eric's fear, ambivalence, and the value of his unwind cigarette. Reflections were especially complex around disclosure of fear of test results and his father's death.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking throughout, steering from minimization to conditions for change, a concrete test, a specific first step, a slip plan, and a scheduled follow-up. The session progressed logically without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—Eric's ideas were solicited at every juncture and the plan was co-constructed from his own words. Check-ins and scheduling were negotiated jointly rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Eric's ownership with 'something you chose for yourself' and 'on your own terms,' accepting 'no promises' without pressure. Asked permission, offered choices, framed slips as information, and let Eric set the follow-up pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from within Eric via open questions about his personal conditions for concern, family fears, and vision. The key takeaway—that change doesn't have to happen all at once—came entirely from Eric.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently located expertise in the client ('You're the expert on your own life and body') and had Eric generate his own conditions, steps, and slip strategies. Eric's contributions directly shaped the session's direction and final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertly evoked change talk with questions like 'What would have to change for quitting to feel like it mattered more to you?' and amplified momentum around Eric's 'less impossible' statement. Ended by eliciting Eric's self-generated commitment to skip the lunch cigarette, get lung numbers, and prepare a slip plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly avoided reinforcing sustain talk; redirected from rationalizations to Eric's own conditions for change. Could have used more explicit double-sided reflections when Eric voiced 'quitting sounds miserable' and attachment to the unwind cigarette.
