# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, non-judgmental throughout, with affirming framing of slips as 'information, not failure.' Tone occasionally felt formulaic and checklist-like, reducing the sense of individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections and a good double-sided reflection, but deeper emotional content (sadness about her mother, fear of isolation, shame) was often met with problem-solving rather than emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on gambling and finances from start to finish, progressing coherently to a specific, dated, measurable plan with a scheduled review.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative moments (permission-seeking, letting Anya pick first steps and edit texts), but the interaction often resembled a consultation delivering menus and scripts rather than joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated emphasis on choice, tweakable scripts, and non-punitive framing of slips. However, prescriptive suggestions (exact amounts, tactics) sometimes crowded out Anya's own problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Standard evocative tools were used and Anya articulated her own motivations, but the richest change talk (the Egypt comparison, motherhood, 'felt proud') was passed over rather than deepened, and evocation felt somewhat mechanical.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission and invited choice, but frequently shifted into expert-driven advice-giving with scripts and tactic lists that took over the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Readiness and confidence rulers elicited genuine change talk, but key statements ('could've had me in Egypt,' 'I haven't actually tried properly') went unreflected and unamplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk and offered a helpful middle path on the social-life fear, but responses tended toward neutral reflection plus option lists rather than strategic double-sided reflections linking sustain talk to her values.
