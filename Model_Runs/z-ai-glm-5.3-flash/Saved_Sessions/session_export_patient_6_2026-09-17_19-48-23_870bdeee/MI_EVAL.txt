# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with genuine affirmations ('That took guts today') and explicit patient confirmation ('Thanks for not just lecturing me'). Ambivalence was normalized without any shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and emotional nuance, from the opening double-sided reflection to the 'shield' quality of excuses. Alexei repeatedly confirmed feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on alcohol use and its connection to soccer goals, progressing logically from ambivalence to a concrete plan and follow-up. No drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully co-constructed interaction ('we'll figure out what worked and what didn't, together') with genuine power-sharing. Alexei's own ideas shaped the entire intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership reinforced throughout; no advice imposed. The clinician asked rather than told, ending with Alexei owning both plan and follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from Alexei's own values (soccer, scholarships, avoiding addiction) via questions rather than persuasion. His closing summary demonstrates motivation was evoked, not instilled.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Alexei was explicitly positioned as the expert ('it came from you, not me'). Every plan element originated from him, with the clinician merely organizing his contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong desire, need, and commitment language was consistently evoked and deepened through values-based open questions. Consolidated at the close by asking what Alexei was taking away.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('just a couple beers') was acknowledged without reinforcement and redirected within double-sided reflections. Slightly more deliberate re-pivoting of specific minimizations early on would have earned a 5.
