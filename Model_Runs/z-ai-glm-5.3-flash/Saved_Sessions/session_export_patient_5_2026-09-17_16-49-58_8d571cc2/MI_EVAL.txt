# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with consistent affirmations, though they were somewhat repetitive and formulaic ('sensible,' 'cautious') rather than individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but the session was heavily information-delivery focused and rarely explored or validated Alessia's emotional experiences like relief, anxiety, or her concern about the care gap.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the safe medication transition and the phenelzine/citalopram interaction, with every turn advancing the agenda toward a completed action plan and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Active co-creation of the prescriber message and phone script with client input genuinely woven in, though the interaction was somewhat transactional with large blocks of expert information delivered without checking understanding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently deferred decisions to Alessia and her prescriber and offered rather than imposed choices, though some turns were dense with directive safety instructions and client ownership was not explicitly emphasized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The opening evocative question successfully drew out goals and readiness, but thereafter the counselor defaulted to information delivery with little ongoing eliciting of reasons, confidence, or fears.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions were framed as residing with Alessia and her prescriber, and her contributions substantially shaped the session outputs, though the task-oriented format somewhat constrained the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A strong opening question elicited clear change talk and commitment language, but the counselor did not consistently reinforce, deepen, or reflect this change talk back, favoring content delivery instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mild sustain talk was not amplified and focus stayed on action and safety, but the counselor missed opportunities to actively reframe uncertainty into motivation, softening mostly by omission.
