### Acceptance

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some acknowledgment of her time pressure and plan, but empathy broke down by continuing to offer content after she stated at least six times that she needed to leave.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Started with reasonable focus but produced four consecutive failed endings, reopening with new offers after clear goodbyes instead of summarizing once and closing cleanly.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power sharing through choices and invitations for input, but the stance was primarily expert-driven, with unsolicited offers steering rather than following the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to evoke the client's own motivations; the one attempt went unanswered and no exploration of readiness or goals occurred.

### Softening Sustain Talk
