# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations that validate without condoning self-harm ('That relief makes complete sense'). Some affirmations feel slightly formulaic and normalization of relapse/struggle is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflections including complex and double-sided ones that capture the somatic experience of stress and ambivalence. Occasionally moves too quickly from reflection to question, missing chances to sit deeper with hopelessness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on reducing self-harm through safer coping, with each exchange building toward a concrete next step. Strategic sequencing gives the precontemplative client clear momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely joint interaction, with each turn building on the client's preceding words to co-construct the plan. Session content and direction are dictated by the client's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support on a safety-sensitive topic: 'You don't have to promise anything to anyone' releases pressure, and the client's limits are respected rather than overridden. The psychiatrist never prescribes or pressures, consistently reinforcing the client's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation and strategy drawn from the client via skillful open questions, with no reasons or solutions supplied by the psychiatrist. Client's shift from hopelessness to tentative optimism shows motivation evoked from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly attributes expertise to the client ('That's problem-solving that came entirely from you'). Every plan element originated from her and demonstrably shaped the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: skillfully elicits, amplifies, and reflects change talk across the session arc. Client progresses from 'Nothing really' to 'I could set it up this week'—clear commitment language shaped by evocative questioning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk consistently met with reframes and double-sided reflections rather than argument, including releasing the client from promises. Some pessimistic predictions ('probably won't be enough') are left unaddressed rather than gently reframed.
