# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with explicit reassurance ('I'm not here to push you either way') and genuine, personalized affirmations. Both sides of ambivalence validated without judgment, though affirmations occasionally lean formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, often complex reflections including effective double-sided reflections tracking both content and feeling. A few reflections paraphrase rather than deepen, leaving some emotional material (e.g., shame) untapped.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on smoking cessation from the opening line with no drift. Firm without being pushy, even when the patient attempted to redirect.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way exchange; each question built from the client's answers, with no directives or unsolicited advice. The entire plan was client-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support—the session's central engine—with explicit outs, choice menus, and framing change as 'yours.' Patient's autonomy language ('it's mine') confirms it landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent eliciting of the patient's own motivation; every key idea came from the client. No advice-giving or problem-solving for him at any point.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Patient consistently positioned as the expert, with his contributions visibly shaping the plan. Occasional polished, therapist-shaped summaries slightly limit full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk evoked systematically through open, forward-looking questions, progressing from desire to ability, reasons, and commitment-adjacent steps. Every instance reinforced with reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged and validated rather than argued with, with consistent pivots toward change via double-sided reflections. Some negative content re-voiced at length before pivoting, slightly amplifying it.
