# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and respect with affirmations like 'You've got this' and no judgment about drinking or past lapses. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic, and her shame about close calls could have been more explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture her ambivalence and risky contexts throughout. Emotional content like her fear of becoming her family's history and shame about past lapses was acknowledged but not explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on reducing drinking to protect contraceptive reliability with every exchange tied to concrete action. The session progresses logically from exploration to a fully formed action plan with accountability follow-up and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mei's ideas substantially shape the plan, but the counselor frequently supplies complete solutions, scripted lines, and full checklists rather than joint brainstorming. Her direct request for the counselor's opinion was met with directive advice rather than collaborative exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices, and Mei's selections directly shape the plan. Advice is delivered in an expert register, and ownership of outcomes is not explicitly reinforced during moments of self-doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits Mei's own motivations through scaling questions and open exploration of her reasons for change. However, the session shifts quickly to advice-giving, and opportunities to deepen motivation and explore discrepancy were underused.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks which options Mei prefers and incorporates her answers, showing some power sharing. The counselor retains the expert role in generating strategies, offering pre-structured menus rather than turning expertise back to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evokes change talk via open questions and a confidence scaling prompt, and Mei produces strong motivational statements that are reinforced. Change talk is not always deepened with follow-up evocative questions before moving to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids dwelling on sustain talk, responding to doubts with practical supports rather than exploring reasons against change at length. Few deliberate double-sided reflections or strategic reframes; low in-the-moment confidence is sidestepped rather than resolved.
