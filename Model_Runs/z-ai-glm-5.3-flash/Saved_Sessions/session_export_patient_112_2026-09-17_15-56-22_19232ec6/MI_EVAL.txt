# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, and Rosa reports the session exceeded her expectations. Affirmations are somewhat brief and formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections show accurate attunement, but the session shifts heavily into information-giving, leaving Rosa's fears, identity concerns, and relationship worries largely unexplored. Empathic listening is present but superficial and inconsistent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with each turn moving toward concrete next steps. Minor drift into detailed pharmacology that momentarily shifts focus away from Rosa's ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative — options offered rather than prescribed, and Rosa's edits are adopted verbatim. Long informational monologues create brief expert-driven stretches that reduce equal-partner dynamics.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames choices as Rosa's and explicitly honors her non-commitment without pressure. Could more explicitly reinforce that change and its timing are entirely her decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opening questions invite Rosa's concerns, but much of the emerging motivation comes from informational provision rather than evocation of her own values, reasons, and confidence. No confidence rulers or exploration of personal meaning of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing — Rosa's input substantially shapes deliverables, including verbatim incorporation of her cue card edits. Counselor retains the expert role in generating most content, keeping it from a 5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rosa offers meaningful change talk, but the counselor rarely reflects, amplifies, or deepens it, and there are no targeted evocative questions. Change talk emerges more from psychoeducation than deliberate cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframes fear-based sustain talk with actionable options, and Rosa notes relief. Some responses indirectly reinforce sustain talk by extensively detailing withdrawal management rather than pivoting to her motivations.
