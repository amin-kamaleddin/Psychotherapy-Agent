# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental — normalizes self-doubt and reframes 'lazy' as constrained. Little explicit validation of the emotional weight of last year's gym failure kept this from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and recap statements clearly landed with Farid. Reflections were somewhat functional and content-focused, missing deeper feelings like shame or fear of repeating past failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on increasing physical activity, producing a concrete, time-bound plan with calendar block, backup strategies, and a 12:15 check-in. Every exchange moved toward actionable commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was genuinely co-constructed — Farid's ideas (walking buddy, shoes at work, waiting on a second day) directly shaped the final plan, exemplifying equal-partner problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered options rather than directives and respected Farid's choice to wait on a second day. Some advice-giving was slightly prescriptive, and more explicit reinforcement of client ownership would strengthen this.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence ruler drew out some of Farid's own levers (accountability, a buddy). However, intrinsic reasons and values for change were not deeply evoked, and the rapid pivot to logistics did the motivational work for the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed decisions as shared, with checks before each step and genuine incorporation of Farid's contributions. The counselor proposed most concrete tactics rather than drawing them out of the client first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of the confidence ruler moved Farid from 6 to 8, with clear commitment language evoked. Could be stronger by evoking more of Farid's own reasons and values for change before moving to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was redirected effectively through reframing and contingency planning so obstacles didn't become reasons for inaction. Sometimes answered with problem-solving rather than reflective softening, and occasional counselor-voiced barriers briefly invited sustain talk.
