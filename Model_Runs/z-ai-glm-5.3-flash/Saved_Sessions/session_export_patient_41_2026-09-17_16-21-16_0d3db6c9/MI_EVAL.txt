# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows warmth and respect, affirming Elira's persistence and normalizing setbacks. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic, repeating the same theme of effort rather than reflecting regard for her whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captured the avoidance mechanism and self-criticism, such as the scrolling after practice. Reflections often moved quickly into multiple questions, leaving the emotional core of fear of failure under-explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained tight focus on the target behavior of starting study time after practice. Every exchange advanced a concrete, actionable plan with specific details on the phone, timer, accountability text, and alarm.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were genuinely co-constructed, with Elira's adjustments immediately incorporated into the final plan. The counselor tended to present pre-formatted choices rather than inviting Elira to generate solutions first, making power-sharing counselor-scaffolded.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Everything was consistently framed as Elira's choice, including permission to defer sending the text until tonight. Her closing assertion of her own retry rule shows the autonomy-supportive stance was internalized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and open prompts drew out Elira's own reasons and solutions, with her closing 'I actually want to try' showing internalized motivation. The counselor was sometimes quick to propose options before first evoking Elira's own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira's expertise was positioned as central, with her contributions materially shaping the plan. The counselor still supplied the menu of options rather than eliciting her ideas first in most instances.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful and consistent evocation of change talk through scaling questions and prompts about what Elira wanted and found doable. Elira's change talk escalated from 'maybe a four or five' to 'I actually want to try,' with confidence rising from 4-5 to 7.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was briefly acknowledged and redirected toward problem-solving without reinforcing avoidance. Some questions, like asking what would raise confidence, occasionally invited Elira to articulate doubts, though these were reframed productively.
