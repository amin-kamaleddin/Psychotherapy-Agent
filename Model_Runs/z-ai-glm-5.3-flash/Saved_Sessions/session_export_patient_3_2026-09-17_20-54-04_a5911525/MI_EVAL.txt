# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed warmth and unconditional positive regard ("You're worth this," reframing "selfish" as courage) and normalized imperfection ("messy is okay"). Acceptance was maintained regardless of outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were precise and emotionally attuned, capturing underlying meaning (e.g., the guilt bind around her mother, "nervous but in a good way"). Demonstrated highly accurate reflective listening throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on resuming exercise and progressively guided from motivation to concrete planning (days, path, podcast, barriers, check-in). Every turn advanced the agenda without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed through questions rather than prescriptions, with the counselor crediting Aisha's agency ("you did the building"). Aisha's ideas substantially shaped the session direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All decisions originated from the client, with no directives or pressure. Consistently reinforced ownership ("you've already promised yourself the plan").

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out the client's own reasons, capacity, and commitment ("What feels different now?"), evidenced by her closing statement that the conversation gave her words she didn't have before.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert ("you're the expert on your own life") and framed growth as self-discovered. Her contributions drove the entire plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicited commitment and mobilizing language ("I think I'll actually do that this week"), which the counselor then amplified by reflecting the client's words back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently reframed self-doubt and self-criticism ("selfish" as courage, "zero" as someone who found her footing). Some reframe opportunities (e.g., "set myself up to fail again") could have been handled with explicit double-sided reflections.
