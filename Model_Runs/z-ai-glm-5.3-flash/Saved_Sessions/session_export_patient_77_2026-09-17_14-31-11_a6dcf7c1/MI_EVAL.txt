### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Interaction was markedly expert-driven; Liyana adopted the counselor's plan rather than co-creating one, and her own expertise was never systematically drawn upon.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership

**Comments:** Despite one power-sharing question, the counselor operated largely as the expert, delivering prescriptive bullet lists with minimal input from Liyana.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Liyana's change talk emerged spontaneously but was never deliberately evoked or deepened; no rulers or confidence-building questions appeared.
