# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic respect and a nonjudgmental stance are maintained, including 'If you slip, no judgment.' However, warmth is minimal and transactional, with no affirmations of Ahmed's honesty or curiosity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; the clinician responds to Ahmed's minimizations with repeated risk facts rather than reflecting his perspective. His frustration ('you keep saying the same thing') directly reflects feeling unheard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the mini-cigar use and converges on a concrete one-week abstinence experiment starting Saturday. However, direction is achieved through prescriptive structure rather than guided goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Plans and tips are handed to Ahmed rather than co-constructed; he negotiates the plan down himself ('one week, that's the deal'). Collaboration appears only at the level of final scheduling choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some autonomy-supportive elements exist ('no pressure', offering options, 'I'll support whatever you decide next'). These are undercut by heavily directive, repeated unsolicited advice despite the client's protests.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reasons for change come from the counselor's risk information rather than the client. Ahmed's own motivations (gum irritation, wife's opinions, self-efficacy) were never evoked, reflected, or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The clinician assumes the expert role, delivering repeated advice lists and a pre-structured plan. Ahmed's contributions shape the plan only as concessions after the clinician's prescriptions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerges from Ahmed himself ('a one-week thing wouldn't hurt', 'curious if the gums clear up'), but the clinician does little to evoke or deepen it. Opportunities to consolidate commitment language are squandered with repeated checklists.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician does not amplify sustain talk and offers a brief nonjudgmental reframe about slipping. However, counter-arguing and risk lecturing entrench defensiveness, with no double-sided reflections or strategic pivoting.
