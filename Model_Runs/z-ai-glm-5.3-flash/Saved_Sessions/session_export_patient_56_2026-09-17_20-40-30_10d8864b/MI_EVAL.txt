# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout—affirming honesty ('I really hear you wanting to report even the imperfect attempts'), normalizing imperfection, and validating self-critical struggles without pathologizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening with reflections that add meaning ('a lot of quiet longing for connection, and also for peace with yourself'), which the client repeatedly confirms ('you put that well').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally stays focused on the target value and moves efficiently to a concrete plan (Thursday dinner), but briefly drifts into childhood history without explicit linking to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully equal, collaborative exchange built on permission-style invitations ('I'm curious...'), with the client's own ideas (Thursday, cooking together, the grandmother template) steering the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership via open-ended evocations, non-coercive follow-up ('whatever it looked like, perfect or not'), and celebrating her self-trust; no pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major insight originates from the client—dinner idea, grace imbalance, grandmother template—drawn out by questions eliciting her own reasons and resources rather than supplied motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions the client as the expert ('You know your husband best'), and her contributions genuinely shape the session's direction and plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocations ('if this week went well, what would be different?') produced rich change talk—'Thursday. I can do Thursday,' 'I do know some answers already'—each reinforced with affirmations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responded skillfully to the client's doubt about past broken promises via reframing through her commitment value, but sustain talk was minimal so this skill was only lightly tested; more deliberate double-sided reflection would be needed under higher ambivalence.
