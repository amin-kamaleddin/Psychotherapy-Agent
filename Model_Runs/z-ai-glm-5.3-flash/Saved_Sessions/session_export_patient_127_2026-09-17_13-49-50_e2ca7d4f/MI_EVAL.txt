# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, affirming Timur's 22-day progress and his temptation without alarm or disapproval. Regard was supportive but somewhat formulaic and brief, falling short of a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal — deep personal material like feeling unaccepted ('people like me aren't accepted into that world') and shame about quitting everything was acknowledged briefly then skipped. The counselor pivoted to practical option lists rather than exploring the emotional core of his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed tethered to leaving crime and building stable employment, converging on concrete steps (forklift cert, phone calls). However, no clear agenda was set early on; focus developed somewhat accidentally rather than through deliberate negotiation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Power was noticeably uneven — the counselor delivered plans, scripts, and checklists like a case manager rather than an equal partner. Good co-created moments (role-play, job message wording) were the exception; Timur's own ideas were folded in only lightly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menus of options gestured at choice, but the overall tone was directive and advice-saturated, with solutions supplied before Timur generated his own. Few explicit statements reinforcing Timur's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation came from Timur despite the counselor, not through skilled elicitation — open questions were rare, and key motivations (Fiona, 'the man she fell in love with,' porch moments) were never deepened. The counselor supplied reasons and strategies rather than drawing out his own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed the expert role with nearly every turn ending in prescriptive lists and drafted scripts. Menus constrained Timur's choices rather than genuinely drawing out his own ideas about what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was noticed and affirmed when it emerged naturally (forklift cert interest, 'remind her of who I was'), but there was little deliberate evocation. When Timur asked for 'something bigger to aim at,' the counselor pivoted to generic options rather than developing his own vision.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced, and strengths-based reframes were used ('You've already shown grit: 22 days, Fiona'). However, the pivots were abrupt, skipped acknowledging the legitimacy of doubts, and no double-sided reflections were used.
