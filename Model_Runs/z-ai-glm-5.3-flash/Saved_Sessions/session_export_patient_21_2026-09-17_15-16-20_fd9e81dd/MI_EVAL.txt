# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with consistent affirmations, though praise is somewhat formulaic and there is little explicit normalization of struggles like her week-long slips.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture both sides of her ambivalence, but reflections tend to summarize rather than probe the emotional depth behind slips or her partner's worry.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on increasing activity with a fully specified, scheduled behavioral plan and follow-up; exemplary directional steering without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely an equal partnership with client input shaping the plan, though the counselor sometimes drives process mechanics (confidence ruler, message drafts) without first asking if she wants that structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated reinforcement of choice and no pressuring language, though multiple-choice menus occasionally constrain options to counselor-preselected ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client's own resources and reasons, yielding strong self-generated insights, but sometimes supplies content (reframes, scripted messages) rather than evoking it and never explores her deeper personal 'why'.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Camila as the expert, with her contributions substantially shaping the plan, though pre-formed option menus occasionally make the counselor the idea generator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong change talk evoked via targeted questions and a textbook confidence ruler, but the counselor sometimes moves to logistics quickly rather than deepening change talk or eliciting her values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and effectively reframed ('data, not failure'), but redirects sometimes jump to solutions rather than using strategic double-sided reflections, occasionally feeling counselor-led.
