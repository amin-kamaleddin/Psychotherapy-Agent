# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with affirmations like 'that's you protecting yourself' and 'that takes some courage.' No labeling or disapproval regarding heroin use, and Diego's experience of feeling judged elsewhere is explicitly validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and feeling, including subtle process statements. The counselor opens the deeper 'why' at Diego's own pace, which Diego notes nobody had asked about before.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on harm reduction and progressively builds toward concrete action (Narcan conversation, clinic visit, safe injecting). Each reflection and question advances the agenda while honoring Diego's boundary against abstinence pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is genuinely two-way—options are examined 'together,' and Diego actively requests the counselor's input. Next steps are co-constructed, with the counselor checking in before structuring each phase.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently reinforced through choice-offering questions, pacing at Diego's speed, and affirmations like 'that's you taking charge of this.' Information is offered only after Diego asks and framed as usable knowledge, not directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently elicited from within via open questions about Diego's vision, strategies, and evaluative criteria. Diego's reasons, plan, and standards for trust all emerge from the counselor's evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Diego, whose contributions (dry run, Narcan rehearsal, clinic questions) directly shape the session. The counselor also consults Diego on format preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and reinforced, escalating from vague hopes to concrete commitments ('maybe even this week,' '10 out of 10'). The counselor amplifies commitment language and rehearses change behavior via role-play.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement using neutral, accepting reflections, and Diego's boundary against preaching is respected. Improvement possible: no double-sided reflections or deliberate pivoting of sustain statements back to Diego's change reasons.
