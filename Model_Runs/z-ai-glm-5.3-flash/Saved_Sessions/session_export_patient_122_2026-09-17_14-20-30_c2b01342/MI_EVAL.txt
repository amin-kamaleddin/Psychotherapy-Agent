# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing missed days and affirming Sofia's realistic plan. Warmth is somewhat formulaic and repetitive, with little individualized affirmation beyond the plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections landed well, but reflective listening is sparse; Sofia's guilt spirals and shutdown experience were never explicitly reflected back as the counselor shifted quickly to offering options.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior with no topic drift, though through repetition rather than deepening; emotional eating was identified but never revisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia picked the habit, cue, and phrase, but the counselor dominated by proposing options and restating instructions; little joint problem-solving occurred.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Sofia's choice and used no-judgment framing, which Sofia explicitly appreciated. The counselor never checked whether Sofia wanted to commit versus explore other directions, and prescriptive tip-lists subtly pressured compliance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some desire, willingness, and hope from Sofia, but did not explore her deeper reasons for change or evoke confidence talk; most momentum came from the counselor's enthusiasm.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia was given some ownership through choices, but the counselor generated most of the plan content, and Sofia's role was largely selecting among pre-packaged options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked commitment language by inviting Sofia to select options, and she voiced clear willingness and hope. Change talk was not deepened with follow-ups about confidence, importance, or her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia voiced notable sustain talk about past failures and shutdowns, but the counselor neither reinforced nor actively softened it; no double-sided reflections, and her worry was bypassed by moving to logistics.
