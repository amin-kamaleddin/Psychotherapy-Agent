# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with genuine individualized affirmations and normalized setbacks. Unconditional positive regard was consistently delivered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing deeper meaning (e.g., activity as 'feeling like yourself again'). Understanding was verified through the client's continued elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on re-establishing exercise, progressing from exploration to a concrete, specific plan with follow-up markers. Every exchange advanced momentum without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—the entire plan originated from the client, with the psychiatrist mirroring and organizing rather than directing. Agenda-setting was handed to the client at the close.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced via structured choices, deference to the client's pacing ('It's your plan, your pace'), and framing setbacks as informative. Ownership was repeatedly returned to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All meaningful ideas came from the client via open questions about her experience, past successes, and values. She ended the session self-motivated and forward-looking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located within the client ('you already know the recipe'), and her contributions genuinely shaped the session. Meets the highest standard of power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled, repeated evocation of commitment and success talk ('a 10 out of 10,' 'I want to do that again') flowing directly from targeted questions. Change talk was consistently reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (weather doubts, 'might bail') was never reinforced, but pivots away were implicit rather than strategic. A brief double-sided reflection could have softened the weather barrier more actively.
