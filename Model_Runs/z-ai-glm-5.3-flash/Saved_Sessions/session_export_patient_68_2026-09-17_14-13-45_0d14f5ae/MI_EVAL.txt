# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor communicates consistent warmth and nonjudgment (e.g., 'accidents happen,' 'we'll review it without judgment'). However, acceptance feels somewhat formulaic and is repeatedly paired with immediate advice rather than deeper exploration of Kevin's embarrassment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; emotionally rich statements like 'it's really stupid' and 'I don't want to be that guy nursing a soda all night' go unreflected. Most responses consist of questions or advice lists rather than genuine understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical focus on alcohol use progressing from assessment to a concrete Saturday plan with a scheduled Monday follow-up. The main weakness is that the target behavior was never deeply clarified collaboratively—the direction is strategy-driven rather than motivationally grounded.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaboration exists (offering menu options, adapting the plan when Kevin rejects the drink limit), but the interaction follows a question-answer-advice pattern with the counselor supplying nearly all strategies. Kevin's contributions shape the plan only when he actively pushes back.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin's choices are consistently respected, from selecting focus areas to adopting his scaled-down plan without pressure. The score is limited by autonomy support being embedded within heavy advice delivery and lacking explicit affirmation of Kevin's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The approach is predominantly educational and prescriptive rather than evocative; the importance ruler is used superficially and immediately followed by advice. Kevin's spontaneous motivation ('beats ending up in here again') was left undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission is sought and choices are offered, but three rapid-fire tip lists position the counselor as the advice expert. Kevin's own expertise about his social scene (Dave's teasing, pub dynamics) is never mined for solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Notable change talk ('it's really stupid,' 'I'll give it a shot,' 'beats ending up in here again') goes unreflected and undeepened. After the '3' importance rating, the crucial 'Why a 3 and not a 0?' follow-up is skipped in favor of advice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('going out with my mates is the best part of my week,' the 'pointless' drink limit) is not reinforced but also never redirected through double-sided reflections or reframing. Ambivalence is left unaddressed, allowing it to persist without resolution.
