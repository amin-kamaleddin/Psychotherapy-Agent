# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, even when Derrick minimizes his drinking. Warmth is somewhat functional rather than personal, and affirmations of Derrick's honesty and effort were sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures both practical concerns and emotional nuance (embarrassment, fear of blackouts). Missed deeper exploration of isolation during his six months sober and the emotional weight of the blackout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on preventing drunk driving, with every exchange tied to concrete steps (cab money, alarm, Uber, Marcus backup, if-then plan). Strong momentum toward testable commitments without digression.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way plan building: Derrick chose the text wording, adjusted the alarm to 11:30, and added his own tactics. Clear power-sharing language throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit autonomy support from the start ('keeping your choices front and center'), with Derrick free to reject options and frame his own decisions. His final statement reflects genuine ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivations are elicited from Derrick himself via strategic 0-10 confidence scaling and questions about what would raise his number. In-session commitment (sending the text) converted intention into action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Derrick's ideas substantially shaped the plan and the counselor supported rather than directed. Slight imbalance from offering several menu-of-options lists in quick succession.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation and development of change talk through scaling, if-then planning, and in-session commitment. Derrick's change talk progressed from tentative to strong commitment ('first one I've actually felt like I could stick to').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is reflected neutrally and redirected into problem-solving rather than argued against. The 'bad luck' and 'just beer' minimizations were never directly reframed, missing a chance to deepen insight.
