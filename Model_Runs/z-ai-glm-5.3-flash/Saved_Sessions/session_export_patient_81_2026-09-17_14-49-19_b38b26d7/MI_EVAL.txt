### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance with effective affirmations ('you did well naming concrete steps') and accepted Lukas's weekend boundary without pushback. Affirmations were somewhat brief and task-focused; deeper affirmation of his honesty and self-awareness would have strengthened positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate complex reflections capturing ambivalence and patterns, and validated the sting of Dean's skepticism. Reflections sometimes served as brief pivots to options, with limited exploration of embarrassment with flatmates and identity-level fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus with a coherent progression from agenda-setting to priorities, concrete steps, skills practice, and commitment ('Thursday. I'll do it Thursday'). Every exchange was anchored to the target behavior with no aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was largely collaborative, with the counselor asking permission, incorporating Lukas's edits, and adapting to his preferences. However, the counselor supplied most concrete solution content, with Lukas refining rather than co-generating ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice through menu-style options and accepted Lukas's boundaries without pressure. Option lists were somewhat prescriptive in substance, and explicit reinforcement of Lukas's ownership of the change process was minimal.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Lukas's own motivations, eliciting values-based change talk ('once I send it, it's real') and his reasoning about not bailing on mates. Evocation was strongest around practical steps and thinner around deeper intrinsic motivations like identity and belonging.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned Lukas as the expert, offering drafts for him to edit and role-playing Dean at his request, with his edits reshaping the intervention. Early agenda-setting presented counselor-framed menus, and the counselor's drafting sometimes overshadowed Lukas's own idea generation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited change talk through open questions, drawing out statements like 'I need to sort my rent out' and reinforcing them. Missed opportunities to deepen Lukas's spontaneous insight that weekday use 'is not really social anymore' or evoke longer-term commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, using effective double-sided reflections and redirecting to concrete actions when Lukas insisted weekends stay his. Occasionally restated sustain themes rather than pivoting toward change, and did not explore the identity-based weekend barrier.
