# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout; validates both eagerness and homesickness, reframes modest goals as wisdom, and counters self-criticism without any judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including double-sided reflections and capturing Sofia's own phrases like 'quiet proud' and the insight that eating is about stress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on target behaviors, progressively guiding toward a concrete plan with walking, checkmarks, relapse rule, and follow-up while remaining client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully egalitarian; the plan was built entirely from Sofia's contributions and the counselor co-created follow-up logistics by asking what works for her.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Sofia's ownership ('This is your plan, from your own discoveries'); no advice imposed, all strategies emerged from her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client ('You know your patterns better than anyone'); all key plan elements originated from Sofia.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire, ability, and commitment language through open questions and anticipatory visualization, then reinforced by reflecting it back as Sofia's own insight.
