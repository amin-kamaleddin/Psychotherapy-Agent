# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and supportive tone with no judgment about emotional eating or past failures; self-compassion actively encouraged. Warmth is at times formulaic, with quick pivots to problem-solving limiting depth of positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects Sofia's experience, including her stress cycle and fear of repeating failure. Reflections tend to be brief, moving quickly to suggestions rather than deepening exploration of her self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on realistic small-step physical activity throughout, with no topic drift. Session progresses logically from exploration to planning to relapse rehearsal, ending with a client-confirmed action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers options and allows Sofia to modify plans, but the pattern is often counselor-proposes, client-approves with bulleted plans and scripts handed over. Sofia's role is mostly to accept or lightly edit rather than truly co-create.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and frames missed walks as information rather than failure, and Sofia makes her own key decisions. Autonomy support is somewhat undercut by the volume of prescriptive advice delivered before Sofia generates her own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions largely come from the counselor, who supplies plan structure, cues, scripts, and even drafts the husband text. Sofia's genuine change talk emerges despite, not because of, evocative technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's contributions do influence the plan, but the session frequently shifts into an expert-driven coaching stance with ready-made solutions. The counselor holds more of the expertise than ideal for full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia generates meaningful change talk, but much emerges organically rather than through deliberate evocation. Powerful statements like 'the walk isn't for him, it's for me' are met with to-do lists rather than amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and redirected to practical coping strategies like if-then fallbacks and self-compassion scripts. Reframing failure as information effectively softens sustain talk, though redirections are more advice-based than reflective.
