# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth and unconditional positive regard were consistent throughout, with genuine affirmations like 'That's your own evidence, not mine.' Samuel's doubts and relapse fears were met with acceptance rather than pressure, and no judgment appeared anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including a powerful double-sided opening reflection. The psychiatrist precisely captured Samuel's key insight ('it's not the cigarette, it's what I'm doing with the stress') and tracked nuanced ambivalence without repetition.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained unwavering focus on tobacco reduction, moving progressively from journal review to trigger identification to a concrete plan with follow-up. Every exchange advanced toward the target without off-topic wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction functioned as a genuine partnership, with the counselor checking in on formats and co-constructing the accountability deal on Samuel's terms. Samuel's ideas visibly drove every decision in the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was actively reinforced — 'whatever pace feels right to you' — and Samuel set his own goal, timeline, and accountability terms. The plan was explicitly attributed to Samuel with no directives imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the client, eliciting his own insights, reasons, strategies, and metrics of success. No expert advice was dispensed; every plan element originated with Samuel.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly positioned Samuel as the expert on his own habits, and Samuel's contributions genuinely shaped the session. All key strategies — pause technique, journal marks, wife accountability — were his ideas, amplified rather than replaced.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked through open questions that drew out desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language. Each instance was strategically deepened, culminating in Samuel's own concrete plan and self-made accountability deal.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and used productively, reframed as forward planning rather than dwelled upon. However, some sustain talk (morning difficulty, relapse history) was reflected back at length before pivoting; more rapid redirection toward change language would improve this.
