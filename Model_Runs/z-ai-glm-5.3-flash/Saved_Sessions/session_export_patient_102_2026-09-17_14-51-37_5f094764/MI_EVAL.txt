# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with validating phrases, which the client explicitly appreciated. Acceptance occasionally slid into passively endorsing minimization, and warmth felt formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate content-level reflections early on reduced defensiveness, but reflections stayed at the surface and recycled phrases like 'that makes sense.' Missed opportunities to explore emotions beneath the minimization, including the client's implicit test about a 'sin' conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Focus drifted from underage drinking entirely to reputation management and social scripts. The target behavior was never explored in any depth.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Polite back-and-forth with client choices shaping concrete outputs like scripts. Collaboration operated only within the counselor's framing; the clinical agenda was silently dropped rather than jointly negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong choice-offering and respect for the client's declines with no pressure applied, which the client explicitly noticed. Autonomy support was extended to avoiding the clinical topic rather than supporting informed choice about drinking itself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No questions explored the client's values, concerns, or reasons for change. The client's stated safety practices and 'I'm not saying I'll never drink again' opening went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inclusive, choice-offering language and honoring refusals showed some power sharing. The counselor functioned as a script-writing service rather than drawing on the client's own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Virtually no effort to evoke change talk about alcohol; menus centered on reputation management. The client's small opening about drinking was completely unexplored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The counselor actively reinforced sustain talk by reflecting and validating minimization ('clear that you don't see your drinking as a problem—that's understandable'). No double-sided reflections or discrepancy development occurred.
