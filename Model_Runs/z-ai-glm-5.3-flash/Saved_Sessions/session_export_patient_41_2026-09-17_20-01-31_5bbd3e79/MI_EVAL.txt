# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor conveyed consistent warmth and respect, affirming Elira's self-generated ideas and reframing her self-criticism as problem-solving. Falls short of 5 because warmth occasionally remained at the level of technique rather than deeply individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening throughout, including the double-sided opening and the emotionally resonant 'One yes' reflection that deepened Elira's insight. Reflections sometimes paraphrased without touching the shame beneath, and the final exchange slightly missed the emotional weight of her 'sudden fog' description.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus and built purposeful momentum toward a concrete plan with scaling and a consolidating summary. The final exploratory question about the 'fog' moment reopened analysis after the commitment was already consolidated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative—Elira chose the roommate check-in, the definition of success, and the witnessed reporting ritual, with the counselor acting as witness rather than authority. Her closing remark that she left with 'an actual thing to do' confirms genuine equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered options rather than prescriptions and explicitly deferred to Elira's judgment as 'the expert on your own life.' She set her own deadline, defined success on her own terms, and left with self-generated language, with no persuasive pressure anywhere.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully mined Elira's own words and prior successes, converting them into a plan rather than supplying reasons for change. Her escalating language from 'maybe I could try it once' to 'I want to do that' demonstrates motivation drawn from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elira's contributions substantially shaped the session—she designed the intervention, defined the win, and created the accountability ritual, with the counselor repeatedly positioning her as the expert. The closing summary was built almost entirely from her own words and decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, skillful evocation moved Elira from vague 'maybe once?' to concrete commitment ('Tonight, not this weekend sometime') and a self-designed accountability plan, with confidence rising from 4-5 to 6.5. Strategic questions, agency-affirming affirmations, and a consolidating summary culminated in genuine commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally handled well through reframing ('that part doesn't get to drive'), pivoting to exceptions, and pre-emptively defusing the skip scenario. Some sustain talk was left without an explicit pivot, and past failed attempts could have been reframed as useful information rather than steered past.
