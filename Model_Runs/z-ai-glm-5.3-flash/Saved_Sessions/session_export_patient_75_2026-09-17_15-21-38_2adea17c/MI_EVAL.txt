# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, validating tone with affirmations of Linda's efforts, but affirmations are brief and her guilt about pushing back isn't explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of her ambivalence, though reflections stay brief and often pivot quickly to problem-solving rather than deepening emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on preparing Linda to advocate for herself at her appointment; every exchange builds toward that goal with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda's contributions shaped the role-plays, but much content was delivered didactically (question lists, clinical facts, checklists) with partial rather than equal power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent menu-based choices and respect for Linda's right to negotiate with her doctor, though pre-packaged scripts subtly positioned the counselor as the source of solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions mostly came from the counselor; few evocative questions about Linda's own reasons, values, or confidence, and strong change talk was missed rather than amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices were offered, but materials were largely pre-packaged and counselor-generated, with the counselor often in an expert/teacher role rather than co-creating with Linda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk was evoked, but key statements like 'I really can do this' were not deepened—no elaboration, confidence scaling, or exploration of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged as reasonable and effectively reframed, though occasionally barriers were restated rather than actively softened before moving on.
