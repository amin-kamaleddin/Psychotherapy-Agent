# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations throughout, but praise is formulaic and discouragement/setbacks are never explicitly validated or normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on, but the counselor repeatedly skips reflection to deliver advice, leaving Marcus's deeper emotional material (fear of failing again, dancing memory, family motivations) unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on physical activity and weight management with no topic drift, though direction occasionally tips into over-direction by re-imposing the same checklist rather than guiding the client to his own conclusions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some input is invited and client ideas are incorporated, but the power balance is uneven — Marcus's contributions are grafted onto the counselor's template rather than shaping the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional choice is offered and a fallback provided, but the overall stance is prescriptive, with detailed plans handed over and no explicit affirmation of Marcus's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marcus's own motivations (dancing, family support, mood over scale) surface spontaneously but are never explored or amplified; there are no open-ended questions designed to elicit reasons or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role with prescriptive bullet lists each turn; the client's ideas are minimally incorporated and the session flow is counselor-prescribes, client-agrees.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all change talk was client-generated; the counselor made no deliberate attempts to evoke it through open questions, rulers, or strategic deepening of his spontaneous motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not amplified, and shrinking goals ('10 minutes,' '2 minutes counts') is an effective practical pivot — but doubts are bypassed rather than explored with double-sided reflections or affirmations of ambivalence.
