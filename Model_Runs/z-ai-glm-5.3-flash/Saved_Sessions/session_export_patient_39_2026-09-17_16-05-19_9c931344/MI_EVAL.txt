### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with affirmations like "that's a brave, clear step" and validation of Elias's frustration. However, affirmations are somewhat brief and formulaic; deeper, individualized affirmations would elevate the score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured both the emotional meaning of the class and the physical discouragement. Reflections could dig deeper into the emotional meaning of the experience (e.g., the loneliness of "TV and the walls") rather than moving quickly to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with no topic drift. Every exchange advanced toward the target behavior, culminating in a specific, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed: Elias chose the drop-off time, requested the back-row chair, edited the staff script into his own words, and defined success on his own terms. Counselor suggestions were framed as offers and adapted to Elias's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Elias's ownership by offering options rather than directives, asking permission before every addition, and accepting "no promises beyond that" without pressure. Elias explicitly noted "you didn't push, you just listened" — strong evidence of effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from within Elias — his values (connection, music, being out) powered his commitment, and rulers elicited self-assessment. Elias's closing words ("I haven't looked forward to something in a while") demonstrate genuinely evoked, not imposed, motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias was positioned as the expert, with permission sought before offering scripts, notes, and reminders, and his wording edits were accepted. Slightly less than 5 because small-step options were introduced fairly early rather than fully drawn from Elias.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk through open questions and rulers, moving Elias from readiness 5 to confidence 7 and from "no promises" to "I'll go Thursday." Elias's self-generated success definition and tentative future planning show excellent change talk cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement through double-sided reflections and reframing (e.g., "permission to rest" neutralized fear of failure). Occasional sustain talk could have been more actively reframed toward Elias's values.
