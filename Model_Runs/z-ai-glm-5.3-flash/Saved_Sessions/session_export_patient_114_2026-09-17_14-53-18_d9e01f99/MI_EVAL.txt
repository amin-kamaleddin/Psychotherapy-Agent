# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, avoiding labels despite Ryan's fear of being treated like an alcoholic. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and repetitive, with little explicit validation of his ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but emotional content was underexplored; the significant blackout disclosure ('not remembering the whole night') was never reflected or explored, and defensiveness was bypassed in favor of planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior of risky drinking tied to the referral; every exchange tied back to harm reduction, the Saturday plan, or readiness, progressing logically to a co-created plan with contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with permission-based advice and plans built from Ryan's own ideas (e.g., 'pizza' code word). Occasionally stacked multiple questions/options, and the agenda was more counselor-initiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced: Ryan's choices drove every decision, advice was always permission-based, and boundaries were respected. Ryan noted he came in expecting a lecture and left with a plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and evocative questions elicited Ryan's own reasons and readiness rose from 4 to 5, but the counselor relied heavily on the same technique and frequently supplied options himself, leaving deeper values-consequence discrepancy undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Ryan as the expert, asked permission before advising, and built the plan from his contributions. However, the counselor filled most conversational space with structured options and questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Readiness rulers effectively moved Ryan from 4 to 5, and questions about what would nudge him and what a 'win' would look like elicited meaningful change talk. Missed chances to deepen or amplify change statements like 'saying it out loud made it feel more doable' or evoke explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Largely avoided arguing with sustain talk ('everyone drinks in college,' 'not a health nut'), which is a strength. However, minimizations were tolerated but never reframed, and the blackout disclosure was passed over, missing a key discrepancy opportunity.
