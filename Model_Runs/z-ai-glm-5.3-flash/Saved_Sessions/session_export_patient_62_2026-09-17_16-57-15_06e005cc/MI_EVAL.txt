# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations throughout, never criticizing Jordan's pessimism or work history. Warmth was somewhat generic at times, and reassurance occasionally rushed past discouragement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were adequate but often brief and formulaic ('I hear you'), skipping emotional nuances like interview fear and the 'nothing ever works out' narrative. Deep complex reflections of hopelessness and repeated setbacks were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on employment with steady forward momentum toward concrete deliverables (resume, bullets, cover statement). Direction was largely agenda-driven via counselor menus rather than following Jordan's own priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session operated as a joint working relationship, with Jordan actively shaping content and choosing options. Power-sharing was somewhat uneven, as Jordan mostly selected from counselor-built menus rather than co-creating from scratch.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, respected hesitations (e.g., delaying the mock interview), and provided fallback options. Menu-style questioning constrained options to counselor-defined paths, with little explicit affirmation of Jordan's ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Jordan's own reasons and latent strengths, visibly shifting his self-perception ('reads like somebody who shows up'). Confidence and commitment language were under-explored; doubt was met with solutions rather than evoking Jordan's reasons for hope.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jordan's contributions were central—his skills became the resume bullets and his honesty concerns were honored. An occasional expert stance (recommending job types without first evoking Jordan's ideas) slightly limited full partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change-oriented content, building momentum from 'maybe a resume tweak wouldn't hurt' to a completed resume and interview prep. A 5 would require deeper exploration of confidence and success imagery.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('worst case it doesn't work,' 'I'll probably tank it') was neither reinforced nor actively resolved, often bypassed with task pivots. Only occasional softening attempts (e.g., 'I hear the doubt—and the willingness to try') were made.
