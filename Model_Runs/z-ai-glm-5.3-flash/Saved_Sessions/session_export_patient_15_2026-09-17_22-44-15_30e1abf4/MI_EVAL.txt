# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations of the client's initiative and insights and no judgment about his minimizing or passive style.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both content and emotional undertone, deepening the client's self-understanding, including the pivotal insight that 'it's the stopping that does it, not the drinking.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on hypertension and alcohol reduction while following the client's lead, progressing logically to a concrete, time-bound action plan with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine equal-partner dynamic: the counselor solicited input, treated the client's knowledge as essential, and co-constructed a plan the client fully authored.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced client ownership and choice ('it's your own idea, and that matters') without prescribing, pressuring, or insisting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All major motivations—work, his wife, avoiding stroke—were elicited via evocative questions, culminating in strong client self-motivational statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the client as the expert on his routines, and the entire plan (walking, drink-skipping, salt reduction) originated from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation drew out concrete ideas and commitment language directly from the client, ending with 'I reckon I'll see a difference, and my wife certainly will.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally met with acceptance and reframing; a minor deduction for briefly offering the alcohol-BP link as provider reasoning before the client fully arrived there himself.
