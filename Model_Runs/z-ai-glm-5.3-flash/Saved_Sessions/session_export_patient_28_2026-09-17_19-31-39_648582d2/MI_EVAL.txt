# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, with specific, genuine affirmations (e.g., "that's real knowledge about yourself") and validation of both sides of Dante's ambivalence without any hint of judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate throughout, capturing the emotional core of Dante's ambivalence and even pausing on his subtle shift in self-efficacy ("I feel like I could actually do that").

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on re-establishing physical activity with no topic drift, moving methodically from exploration to a specific, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is explicitly framed as joint problem-solving, with Dante generating the ideas (lunchtime walks, buddy accountability, shoe-packing) while the clinician structures and affirms them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan originates entirely from Dante, with steps framed as tentative choices ("smallest first step you'd be willing to try"), and the clinician avoids prescribing even when Dante asks for an opinion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently asks rather than tells, operating from the conviction that motivation resides within Dante, and his motivation visibly grows within the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in Dante ("That's your own problem-solving, not mine"), and his contributions genuinely shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Targeted evocative questions elicit Dante's own motivations, culminating in a concrete self-generated plan, a textbook confidence scaling question, and a clear trajectory from desire to commitment and action steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but never reinforced, though early ambivalent statements ("does it even fit anymore") were sometimes mirrored neutrally rather than actively tilted toward desire; the pivoting strengthens as the session progresses.
