# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard was maintained throughout, even amid David's initial dismissiveness. Warmth was genuine and personalized, and his skepticism about his doctor was validated without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate double-sided reflections capturing David's ambivalence. The one gap: the counselor did not reflect the fear and grief in David's disclosure of his father's fatal heart attack, missing a chance for deeper emotional resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused and progressed from vague 'maybe' to a concrete, specific plan. However, some early questions were broad and the therapeutic focus was never explicitly named or negotiated at the outset.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was built from David's own ideas, and his contributions genuinely shaped the direction. A brief joint agenda check at the start would have strengthened early collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ownership of every decision was consistently reinforced, with no coercive or persuasive language anywhere in the session. The final plan was entirely self-generated, and the counselor explicitly removed pressure about the medication outcome.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation came from David's own words — his father's heart attack, Lucia on the swing, being around at 70 — drawn out through skilled open questions. The counselor never supplied reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located within the client, with no lecturing or expert posturing. David explicitly confirmed the non-directive stance landed ('Thanks for not just lecturing me').

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was systematically evoked, amplified, and consolidated, with a textbook reflection of David's own shift from 'a number on paper' to 'for the grandkids.' David moved from 'maybe' to 'deal' — direct evidence of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Heavy sustain talk was handled skillfully through double-sided reflections and pivots, never arguing with resistance. A point is withheld because the intense fear around the father's death was moved past into planning rather than first softened.
