# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard from the opening line and maintained throughout, with no judgment even during Jason's dismissiveness and blame-deflection. The clinician validates his frustration and normalizes holding two truths at once.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including complex and double-sided reflections that capture Jason's deeper values and ambivalence. Reflections occasionally pivot to questions too quickly without fully exploring the emotional weight of the blackout experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on the drinking behavior with each exchange building momentum from assessment to self-generated action plan. No aimless wandering despite the client's attempts to drift toward blame and minimization.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session operates between equal partners, inviting Jason's priorities and redefining the check-in on his own terms. Power-sharing is consistent and authentic throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is reinforced at every turn, with the change plan entirely client-generated and client-owned. No persuasive or coercive language appears at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently draw out Jason's own reasons rather than supplying them, resulting in him spontaneously articulating a discrepancy between his stated stance and actual concerns. Textbook evocation and development of change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located within Jason, with the plan and follow-up idea coming entirely from the client. This demonstrates full power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions evoked Jason's trajectory from 'everything's pretty much fine' to articulating a specific self-generated plan. Change talk is then reinforced through affirmations and selective reflection.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently softened using double-sided reflections and acceptance without ever arguing or reinforcing it. The clinician pivots away from minimization while honoring the client's stance.
