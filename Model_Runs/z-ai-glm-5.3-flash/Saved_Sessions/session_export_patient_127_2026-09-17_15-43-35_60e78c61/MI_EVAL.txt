# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and warmth with no judgmental language despite Timur's history and self-labels. Affirmations are somewhat generic and formulaic, often bundled with multiple questions, diluting their individualized quality.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately mirror Timur's ambivalence and stress ('pulled back by money, the action, and Fiona's last-chance pressure'). Deeper emotional nuance, such as the shame behind 'people like me aren't accepted' and identity pain ('don't know if that guy's still in there'), went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior throughout, with every turn tied to staying crime-free and building stability. The session progresses logically to a specific, time-bound commitment ('half seven, quarter to eight tomorrow') with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is largely collaborative, with frequent check-ins and adaptation to Timur's constraints. Rapid-fire menus and detailed checklists at times tip the power balance toward service delivery rather than joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reinforcement of Timur's ownership through permission-seeking, menu choices, and honoring his request to search himself and delay the message draft. The emphasis on his own pace and proving something to himself fully supports his perception of choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions effectively drew out Timur's own reasons and plan, keeping motivation with the client. However, reliance on menu-providing and missed chances to deepen change talk (e.g., 'maybe I'm not as lazy as I keep telling myself') kept this from a 5.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor respects Timur's insistence on doing things himself and consistently offers choices, but occasionally slips into a prescriptive expert-planner role with pre-built lists and detailed checklists. Genuine partnership shows in the closing 'we can sort it together' exchange.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation of change talk via open questions and rulers, eliciting importance 7-8 and concrete commitments ('I'll do it after the tea tomorrow'). The counselor misses chances to deepen pivotal self-revaluation statements, pivoting to logistics instead of amplifying them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids amplifying sustain talk and uses double-sided reflections, but responses to Timur's doubts ('nobody'll take me,' 'not yet') are largely neutral acceptance rather than active reframes or strategic pivots. Missed opportunities to gently challenge the 'not yet' stance and tip the balance toward change.
