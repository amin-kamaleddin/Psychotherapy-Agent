# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations and an explicit commitment to a non-critical response to setbacks. Some affirmations felt brief or formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opened with an accurate complex reflection and captured ambivalence well (e.g., tiny step feels safer but possibly pointless). Did not deeply explore the emotional weight of Sofia's harsh self-talk and history of failure before moving to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior throughout, with each turn advancing a progressively refined, concrete plan culminating in a scheduled check-in. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed from Sofia's own ideas, and her input changed the plan. However, rapid-fire multi-question turns and counselor-supplied content tilted the dynamic toward coach-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Sofia's choice, and she freely downsized the plan and picked her own reward, phrase, and accountability. Bundling many options into single turns and offering ready-made solutions subtly steered the agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from within through open questions, and Sofia generated her own insights and hope. Occasionally answered its own questions with packaged solutions, and change talk was developed via scaling mechanics more than deeper values exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's contributions substantially shaped the final plan, showing real power sharing. But the style was often directive, with multiple leading questions per turn and counselor-proposed structure rather than full co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited change talk through targeted questions and confidence scaling, with Sofia's confidence rising from 6 to 9. Change talk was not consistently reflected back to amplify it, emerging more through structured coaching than deep MI evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence once with a double-sided reflection and reframed skipping as 'not starting over.' However, fear of failure and the harsh self-critical narrative received only brief validation before pivoting to logistics, with missed opportunities for deeper exploration and reframing.
