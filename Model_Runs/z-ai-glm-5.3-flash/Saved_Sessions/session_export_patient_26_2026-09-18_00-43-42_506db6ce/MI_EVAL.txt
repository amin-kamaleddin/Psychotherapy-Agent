# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with genuine individualized affirmations ('That's you problem-solving, Daniela'). Her pride and fear of rejection were treated as legitimate, not flaws.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both content and emotional nuance, including the fear of rejection and shifting self-perception ('maybe I'm not as stuck as I feel'). Each reflection landed, as Daniela built directly on them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on childcare stress and cutting back smoking/drinking, progressing steadily from exploration to a concrete, client-generated plan with a timeline.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine dialogue between equals—the client's own ideas (meal bartering, Head Start) drove the plan, and the counselor positioned himself as a curious companion rather than a director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices consistently framed as Daniela's, with no advice or pressure imposed. Falls short of 5 due to few explicit reminders of her freedom to choose differently, and some affirmations steering toward action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from the client—her reasons (Lucas, finishing the culinary program) and her own strength of persistence were elicited through questions and mirrored back in her own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positioned Daniela as the expert ('You know your life better than anyone'), and every key insight and plan element originated from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully elicited deepening change talk ('I could probably do that,' 'That feels doable') which was then reinforced by reflection. The session closed with self-affirming commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, with effective double-sided framing around ambivalence. Not a 5 because some sustain talk (worry about the neighbor resenting her) was passed over rather than reworked.
