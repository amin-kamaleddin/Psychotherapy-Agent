# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with frequent affirmations. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and task-focused rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures her values and motivation. Reflections occasionally shift quickly to planning logistics rather than deepening the emotional meaning of her experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently clear focus on the target behavior, with every turn advancing toward a concrete, testable plan. No aimless drifting occurs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is largely co-constructed with genuine client input shaping outcomes. Some turns lean advisor-like, delivering packaged solutions rather than fully negotiating each element jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support throughout, with consistent offering of choices and explicit framing of the client as in control. The client's choices determine the plan's final shape.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from within the client by eliciting her own success criteria, urgency rule, and transition preference. Deeper evocative exploration of her lifelong commitment to family happiness remains untapped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client contributions are treated as central and her ideas directly shape the final plan. The counselor's tendency to supply detailed structure slightly limits full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes change talk through confidence scaling and success indicators, and the client responds with strong commitment language. Missed opportunity to amplify and reflect the commitment language back to deepen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk given the maintenance-stage presentation, with collaborative pivots when barriers arise. Barrier discussion, while necessary, occupies moderate session space.
