# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental welcome throughout ('welcome here exactly as you are,' 'not here to judge'), with affirmations of honesty and self-awareness. Sustained unconditional regard even when Kenji expressed doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate complex reflections capturing ambivalence, underlying meaning, and insight (e.g., beer 'standing in for something else, like connection'). Reflections went well beyond surface content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and its function, progressing from awareness to patterns to a concrete plan. Guided steadily toward change without rushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan was co-constructed from Kenji's own ideas with permission-style questions and choices offered. Patient explicitly noted it felt different from being lectured.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice ('you don't have to land anywhere today') and the plan was self-generated with an 'experiment, not a promise' framing. Could have been slightly more explicit about ownership of outcomes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within by eliciting Kenji's own meanings, patterns, and solutions. His closing request ('don't let me off the hook too easy') reflects internalized motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Kenji as the expert on his own life, and his contributions fully shaped the session plan. Occasionally led exploration with fairly structured questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk through targeted questions, yielding strong client statements ('hard to unsee,' 'experiment, not a promise'). Reinforced change talk through reflection and affirmation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Responded to sustain talk with double-sided reflections that never amplified the sustain side, then pivoted toward change with open questions. Never argued with resistance.
