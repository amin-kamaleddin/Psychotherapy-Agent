# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific affirmations tied to Elena's own strengths and values. Acceptance extends even to potential slips, and the closing line conveys regard independent of outcome.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and complex, capturing Elena's core insight, emotional experience, and meta-awareness. She repeatedly confirms feeling understood, demonstrating deep understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive trajectory from values awareness to concrete, client-owned planning with a measurable outcome. Each question builds deliberately on the previous exchange without wandering or pushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction functions as a genuine partnership with Elena's contributions shaping every step. However, the exchange is somewhat linear given her high motivation, with little visible back-and-forth co-construction of the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No directives or unsolicited advice are given; every plan element originates from Elena. Her choice and ownership are consistently affirmed, including autonomy in how she handles setbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions are consistently evocative rather than advisory, drawing out Elena's own reasons, plans, and confidence. Her final statement that the change 'came from me' is direct evidence of success.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly positions Elena as the expert on her own life, with the clinician framed as 'a sounding board.' All plan elements originate from her, powerfully conveying that wisdom resides within the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and reinforced, escalating appropriately from noticing the gap to specific commitments and self-efficacy statements. Each instance is reflected and affirmed rather than left to pass.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled competently—reflected as self-knowledge and pivoted toward problem-solving—but it was minimal given Elena's stage of change. There was little opportunity to demonstrate more sophisticated softening techniques.
