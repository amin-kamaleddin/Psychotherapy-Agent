# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor communicates unconditional positive regard, affirming Dante's self-discovery and validating his ambivalence without judgment. Warmth is genuine and individualized, referencing his 5K history and family context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are consistently accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and underlying meaning. The counselor mirrors Dante's emotional shifts and notices micro-processes like catching himself shrinking his commitment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on re-establishing physical activity and progresses logically from barriers to a concrete plan with relapse-proofing. Each response ties back to the target behavior without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction functions between equals, with the plan, accountability strategy, and goal reframing all co-constructed. The counselor's expertise appears only as process facilitation, never content authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly reinforces Dante's ownership, offering options while explicitly leaving choices to him. There is no pressure or persuasion; Dante defines success on his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is elicited almost entirely from Dante through strategic questions rather than supplied by the counselor. His own reasons—the 5K pride, connection with Maria, and self-defined standard of not quitting—drive the change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positions Dante as the expert on his own schedule and lets his contributions shape the session. Questions build on Dante's statements rather than steering toward a counselor-generated agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk, highlighting Dante's own possibility and commitment language. Open questions consistently elicit client-generated motivation, culminating in strong commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through double-sided reflections rather than argument. The counselor occasionally voices the negative side himself, slightly re-introducing sustain talk even within a balanced frame.
