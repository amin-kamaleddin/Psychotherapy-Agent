# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgment throughout. Normalized crankiness and playfully honored Rowan's request not to disclose the session's ease. No disapproval, labeling, or conditional regard anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 — ☑ Exceptional ☐ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Reflections consistently accurate and emotionally attuned. The double-sided opening reflection captured ambivalence precisely; the reframe of "fear" as "caring about your future self" demonstrably deepened self-exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 — ☑ Exceptional ☐ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Session tightly focused on target behavior from start to finish. Each exchange moved progressively toward action — barrier exploration, scaling down, lapse analysis, concrete plan, follow-up scheduling — with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 — ☑ Exceptional ☐ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving between equals. Asked rather than told, incorporated resistance into the plan (crankiness allowed, "miss once, still fine" buffer), and built follow-up around Rowan's Thursday schedule.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 — ☑ Exceptional ☐ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Autonomy support woven throughout: "You get to decide how much structure serves you here." Never prescribed; validated Rowan's right to self-define success. Rowan's "rules I made up myself" confirms this landed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 — ☑ Exceptional ☐ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within the client. Elicited Rowan's own concern rather than lecturing; even accountability motivation ("if I know someone's gonna ask...") came from Rowan himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 — ☑ Exceptional ☐ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Rowan as the expert ("You know your life better than anyone"). Every strategy originated from Rowan, and client recognition at the end confirms genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 — ☑ Exceptional ☐ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Standout dimension. Systematically evoked and developed change talk via scaling-down questions and the "if that part of you got a real say" prompt, escalating Rowan from "I guess so" to explicit commitment ("I'll try it this week").

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4 — ☐ Exceptional ☑ Strong ☐ Adequate ☐ Developing ☐ Needs Work
**Comments:** Generally handled sustain talk well via double-sided reflections and exploring rather than debating. Minor gap: some doubt statements ("I don't know if small actually fixes anything") were left standing without affirmations to tip the balance further toward change.
