# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and supportive, affirming Dante's plan and effort without judgment. The repeated crisis-line reminders after he twice said he was fine felt formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections (e.g., 'committing feels risky after past stops'), but most turns deliver advice checklists rather than reflective listening. Dante's discouragement and fear of failing again were only briefly acknowledged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on rebuilding exercise, moving efficiently from exploration to a specific plan (Tuesday 7pm walks, backup, accountability, follow-up). Exemplary guiding with a clear target behavior throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante's input shaped plan details (Tuesday, wife, 7:15 check-in), but the counselor proposed the structure each time and he negotiated within it. The plan was largely counselor-designed with client tweaks rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Dante chose the goal, start day, accountability person, and reminder time, and misses were framed as 'information, not failure.' Advice was nonetheless dense and prescriptive before he requested it, slightly constraining perceived choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Dante's motivations (feeling strong, sleeping well) surfaced spontaneously but were never drawn out or amplified. Questions were logistical rather than motivational, with confidence and importance never assessed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante picked his start day, backup, and reminder time, but each counselor turn pre-packaged tips before he asked. He influenced details, not the session's direction, making him more a plan-executor than expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante produced meaningful change talk ('I liked how I felt after,' 'maybe that's enough to start') and committed to specific walks. However, the counselor never deepened or amplified it with reflections or open questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante's sustain talk ('I just want to sit,' 'I still don't know if I'll keep it up') was acknowledged neutrally then met with logistics. No double-sided reflections or deliberate pivots toward his own reasons for change.
