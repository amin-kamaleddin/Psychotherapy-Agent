# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unconditional positive regard despite Maria's initial hurry and skepticism, affirming 'that decision stays yours too' and validating her distrust rather than labeling her noncompliant.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, e.g., reframing 'it's not about time' to distrust of advice, and weaving together her fears about breathing, her aunt, and feeling dismissed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept steady focus on asthma management throughout, moving logically from exploring distrust to a concrete, self-generated action plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated between genuine equals by asking rather than telling and co-constructing the plan from Maria's ideas, acknowledged by her thanks for 'not just lecturing me.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced ownership with statements like 'that decision stays yours too,' offering options without prescribing and framing the plan as Maria's own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argued for change; all key motivations emerged from Maria's own words, culminating in her realizing 'I've managed fine' may be falling apart — a textbook example of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Maria as the expert from the start ('You're the expert on your own life') and let her choices drive the session, from prioritizing the question list to identifying the pharmacist.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully reflected Maria's shift language and converted vague intention into a concrete plan, ending with clear commitment language: 'I'll ask the pharmacist... maybe I'll actually use the thing.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally acknowledged sustain talk without arguing (e.g., accepting her non-commitment to the inhaler), though early reflections occasionally restated sustain talk without a double-sided pivot toward change.
