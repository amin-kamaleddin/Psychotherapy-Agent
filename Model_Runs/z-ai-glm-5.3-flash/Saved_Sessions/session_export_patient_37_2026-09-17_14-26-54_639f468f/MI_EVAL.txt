# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, including at the moment of vaccine-hesitancy disclosure, with a warm opening and appropriate safety check. However, affirmations are sparse and generic, missing early opportunities to affirm Elena's strengths as a parent and information-seeker.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several accurate validating reflections landed well, but reflective listening is thin overall. Key emotional disclosures—feeling talked down to, fear of making the wrong call, and her pattern of backing down—were met with advice lists rather than empathic exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays well focused on the target behavior and builds consistently toward a concrete, actionable plan, with the safety screen handled briefly and appropriately. The slight deduction reflects occasional drift into broad advice-giving not tied to Elena's stated goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is genuinely two-sided, with Elena's requests directly shaping the session content and direction. However, the counselor tends to react to Elena's choices rather than co-create the agenda up front, and delivers several info-dumps with limited back-and-forth.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly offers menus of options and hands choice back to Elena, with no pressure toward vaccination and the decision left in her hands. A strong autonomy-supportive session, though more explicit framing that Elena owns the final decision would be ideal.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The opening question is appropriately evocative and role-play draws out valuable self-insight, but motivation is largely supplied through information rather than elicited from Elena. Key evocation techniques—exploring her reasons as a parent, hopes, and confidence—are largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is treated as capable and her contributions substantially shape the session, confirmed by her 'thanks for not lecturing me' remark. Sizable blocks of prescriptive content delivered in advisory mode modestly undercut full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena's change talk emerged and was reinforced, but the shift toward openness appears driven by information provision rather than deliberate evocation. No targeted evocative questions, readiness scaling, or commitment-strengthening summaries were used to deepen her own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor wisely avoided arguing against Elena's sustain talk, but responded primarily with corrective education rather than MI techniques like double-sided reflections or developing discrepancy. The shift came from new information, not from redirecting Elena's own ambivalence toward change.
