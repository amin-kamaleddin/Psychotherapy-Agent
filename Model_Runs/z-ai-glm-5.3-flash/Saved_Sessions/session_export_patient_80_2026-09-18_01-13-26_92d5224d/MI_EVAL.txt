# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates nonjudgment and positive regard ('There's no judgment in this room,' 'That honesty matters'), normalizing partial progress. Falls short of a 5 because affirmations are mostly tied to Luka's honesty or plan rather than his inherent worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently capture emotional subtext, such as the text to his partner reflecting investment in her and the shame behind hiding slips. Luka repeatedly confirms and builds on the reflections, showing they land accurately.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on reducing drinking and preventing outbursts, with each exchange advancing a concrete behavioral plan. No drift; the session closes by tying everything back to the client's own success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative language and genuine power sharing, with Luka's ideas driving the plan. However, the session follows a fairly scripted MI arc (rulers, prioritizing, success criteria), so the agenda structure is clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is repeatedly and explicitly reinforced ('that's your call to make,' 'You'll be the one judging, not me'), with all strategies elicited from Luka. The clinician hands ownership of both the plan and evaluation of success to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates from the assumption that motivation lies within Luka, consistently drawing out his own reasons, strategies, and definitions of success. His change talk escalates from 'it's a start' to self-authored commitments like 'gets me past seven.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise in the client ('You know your situation better than anyone'), and the entire relapse-prevention plan originates from Luka. The psychiatrist primarily organizes and reflects his ideas back.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk via confidence rulers, outcome questions ('what would tell you it worked?'), and strategy prioritization. Each change statement is reflected and reinforced, and Luka's change talk strengthens across the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk respectfully, acknowledging Luka's wish not to quit without amplifying it and redirecting toward 'where a few beers stays a few beers.' Falls short because the pivot moves quickly to strategy rather than reframing sustain talk toward broader values.
