# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with supportive closers, but warmth is formulaic and disclaimers feel mechanical, with little individualized affirmation of Claudia's honesty or ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; the counselor rarely reflects Claudia's feelings about her drinking ritual and low mood, pivoting quickly to advice and SMART planning instead.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on alcohol reduction from disclosure through concrete planning and follow-up, though flooding the conversation with steps sometimes substitutes momentum for exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Goals were partially negotiated, with Claudia shaping the two-drink, two-week plan, but most content is counselor-delivered expert advice and her ideas are absorbed rather than jointly brainstormed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly offers choices, accepts Claudia's smaller goal, and reframes slips as 'data, not failure,' but gives unprompted advice in long prescriptive blocks rather than asking permission.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is rarely drawn from the client; beyond one readiness ruler, her own reasons and hopes go unexplored, and spontaneous change talk is met with logistics rather than elaboration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor largely adopts an expert advice-giver stance with long tip lists, though it defers to Claudia's judgment when she proposes a smaller start, showing some power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A single readiness ruler question yielded some change talk ('two a night... feels doable'), but it was never deepened; no open questions evoked her reasons and change talk was met with plan details instead of reinforcement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor does not argue with sustain talk and accepts the client's smaller goal, avoiding resistance, but makes no active reframing or double-sided reflections to soften ambivalence about the ritual and low mood.
