# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with respectful treatment of ambivalence, but affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deep individualization of Marco's specific efforts and resilience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but empathy was often shallow and quickly bypassed in favor of offering options; key emotional disclosures (all-or-nothing pattern, 'that hits me hard') were not deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior with no topic drift; every exchange built logically toward a fully specified action plan within one session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with repeated check-ins and incorporation of Marco's additions, but the counselor often presented solution menus rather than genuinely co-generating ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed next steps as Marco's choice ('Which feels best to try?'), though frequent pre-drafted scripts, calendar text, and templates shifted some problem-solving away from the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong evocation early via scaling and open questions, but as the session progressed the counselor increasingly supplied solutions and structures rather than drawing strategies from Marco.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mixed evidence: invited Marco's wording and choices, but often fell into expert-driven advisory mode with counselor-supplied lists, scripts, and templates.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk via an opening values question and repeated confidence scaling, building commitment language; a 5 would require more open-ended evocation and deeper development rather than quick movement to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was mostly avoided being amplified with brief normalization, but no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to stated motivations; ambivalence was managed rather than softened.
