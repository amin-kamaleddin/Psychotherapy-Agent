# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Kevin's harm-reduction plan rather than pushing abstinence. Falls short of a 5 due to few genuine affirmations of Kevin's personal strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections early on, but reflections largely disappear mid-session as logistics take over. Emotionally significant disclosures (fear of bailing on mates, social pressure) go unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Disciplined focus on the target behavior with logical progression to a concrete plan. However, direction narrows prematurely to fall-avoidance logistics, leaving the broader drinking pattern unaddressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with the script co-edited and Kevin shaping the plan. The counselor's rapid pacing and suggestion density slightly undermine the equal-partner feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin's choices visibly shape the plan and his rejection of certain options is accepted without pushback. Autonomy support relies mainly on menu-offering rather than affirming Kevin's ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions mechanically elicit motivation, but evocation is confined to scaling and logistics. Kevin's deeper motivations and values are rarely drawn out or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin acts as decision-maker within the plan, but most content (options, scripts, signals) is counselor-generated. Kevin mostly selects from menus rather than generating his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance/confidence rulers with follow-ups; Kevin's confidence rose from 6 to 8.5 and he generated his own change language. Change talk is not deepened with reflections, only met with the next question.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and integrated into the harm-reduction frame without argument. Occasionally sustain talk passes without any pivot toward change, such as the people-pleasing pressure around 'one more.'
