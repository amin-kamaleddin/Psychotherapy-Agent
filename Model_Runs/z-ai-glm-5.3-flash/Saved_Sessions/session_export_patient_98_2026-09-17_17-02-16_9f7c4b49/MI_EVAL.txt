# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates warmth and unconditional positive regard with affirmations like 'That's real effort,' and no disapproval even when the patient is discouraged or resistant. The patient explicitly felt accepted, noting 'Thanks for not just telling me to tough it out.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening accurately captures the patient's pain and discouragement. Reflections are sometimes brief and the session pivots quickly to problem-solving, leaving deeper emotional themes (resignation, spousal friction) not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing activity engagement and managing pain, with every exchange advancing toward concrete steps. No topic drift occurs; even the spouse discussion is tied directly to plan adherence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely two-way, with the patient's modifications (standing exercises, calendar tracking, 'gentle nudge' reminder) immediately integrated. His closing comment that 'we've got it all sorted' reflects a truly co-constructed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is consistently emphasized through menu-based options ('Which of those steps feels most doable?') rather than directives. The patient's rejection of breathing exercises is accepted without pressure, and he retains full ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is effectively drawn from the patient, eliciting his own goal ('not feel so wiped out') and self-insight ('That's probably half my problem'). Missing deliberate confidence talk (e.g., scaling questions), and the counselor sometimes supplies solutions faster than drawing them out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The patient's expertise is repeatedly honored, and his preferences visibly shaped the plan's structure. Some plan content (warm-up duration, stop rule) was presented in a fairly prescriptive package before full mutual negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and builds on change talk, culminating in clear commitment language ('I'll give it an honest try'). Commitment-strengthening techniques like confidence scaling and obstacle anticipation were not used.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement, and rejected suggestions are dropped smoothly without debate. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections linking pain-avoidance to his values, and no explicit reframe of his fear of slow progress.
