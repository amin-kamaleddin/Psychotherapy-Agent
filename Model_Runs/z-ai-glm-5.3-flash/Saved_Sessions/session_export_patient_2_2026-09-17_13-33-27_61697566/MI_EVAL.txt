# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, affirming her steps and framing slips as information. Warmth was somewhat formulaic and her emotional disclosures were not explicitly affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections showed understanding of her stress, but key disclosures (fear of cancer, evening cigarettes as her only moment to herself) were never reflected. Reflections were scarce overall.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on evening smoking with concrete plan details and structured follow-up. Strong focusing, though at the cost of exploring motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some input was invited and adjustments made at her request, but a fully formed plan was delivered before soliciting her ideas. Her role was largely accept/reject rather than co-creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicit permission-seeking, choice within the plan, flexibility, and no pressure on the alcohol topic. Fell short because the plan was largely counselor-authored with little emphasis on her own expertise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler elicited useful motivation and she voiced her own reasons, but these were never mined or deepened. Motivation emerged spontaneously rather than being actively evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and option-offering were present, but the session became plan-delivery with the client reacting to a counselor-designed plan. Her own expertise about her routine was never tapped.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Questions successfully evoked change talk (cutting back goal, fear for her son), but it was never deepened with elaboration. The pre-written plan answered 'how' rather than evoking her own strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled deflection and boundaries well without pushing on alcohol, and did not reinforce minimizations. However, minimizations were left unaddressed rather than met with double-sided reflections.
