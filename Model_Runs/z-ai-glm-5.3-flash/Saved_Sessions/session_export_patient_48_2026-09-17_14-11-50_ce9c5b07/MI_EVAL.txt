# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tone is polite and nonjudgmental with some generic validation, but warmth is formulaic. Missed chances to affirm Farid's specific disclosures, e.g., his embarrassment about fitness level.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal reflective listening; the one empathic acknowledgment is followed by rapid questions and advice. Emotional content like fear of failure and embarrassment is never reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on exercise and converges on a concrete, actionable plan. However, direction is achieved through prescriptive guidance rather than MI-consistent goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The plan is largely expert-driven — the counselor prescribes exercises, times, rewards, and scripts with only narrow preference questions. Farid's own ideas are never woven into a co-constructed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine choice is offered (home vs. gym, tweakable plan) and the tone is not coercive. But heavy prescription of details leaves Farid passive and compliant rather than owning the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Farid offers rich motivational material (health, fatigue, guilt over wasted memberships) that the counselor fails to draw out. Questions focus on logistics rather than values, meaning, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, setting the session's direction with pre-packaged plans. Farid's insights surface organically but are not incorporated, making power-sharing superficial.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Farid spontaneously produces meaningful change talk that goes unreflected and unreinforced. No evocative questions are used to deepen or build on his commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, which is positive, and barriers are occasionally reframed via practical solutions. However, there is no active softening — no double-sided reflections or exploration of ambivalence; doubts are bypassed with advice.
