# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard ('no lecture attached', 'worthy of good care exactly as you are'). Seamus's resistance ('no promises today') was explicitly accepted without judgment, with warmth individualized to his circumstances.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including precise quotations of the client's own words ('have to go', 'breakfast isn't painful'). Fell short of 5 because emotional undercurrents — feeling 'like a naughty schoolboy' and possible shame ('maybe the problem is me') — were never deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear dual focus, respecting the dental agenda while keeping smoking on the table via well-timed interventions after each pushback. The latter portion of the session shifted almost entirely to dental logistics, leaving the smoking thread as a planted seed rather than sustained momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing ('practical partners') and genuine agenda negotiation, with the counselor yielding the session to the tooth. Final procedural planning shifted toward expert-delivery of the plan rather than equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: 'your call, your timing', 'no strings attached', and the client leaving with 'a plan you chose yourself'. The counselor never pressured, explicitly endorsing Seamus's right to defer while keeping his change talk his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from the client via open questions and strategic reflections, producing new insight ('I never thought of it that way before'). Evocation was intermittent — confidence and ability were never explored, and the second half moved to logistics rather than continued elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicit power-sharing ('you're the expert on your own life') visibly changed the session — Seamus noted the counselor was 'easier to talk to than the others'. When Seamus asked 'lay it out for me', the counselor shifted toward expert-delivery rather than co-constructing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of change talk through open questions, the future-oriented breakfast prompt, and reflecting Seamus's own language back as 'yours to keep'. Change talk was never deepened with follow-ups about hope, confidence, or what readiness would look like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and paired with reframes and normalization of ambivalence. Self-blaming sustain talk ('maybe the problem is me') passed without reframing or an affirmation of capability, and failed-method talk was validated rather than redirected.
