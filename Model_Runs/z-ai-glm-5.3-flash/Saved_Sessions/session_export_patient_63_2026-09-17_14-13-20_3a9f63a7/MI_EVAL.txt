# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, affirming Jordan's courage without labeling his shoplifting. Kept from a 5 by brief, formulaic praise that lacks individualized affirmation of his deeper values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal after the opening; vulnerable disclosures (shame, loneliness, the insight about Marcus) are answered with bulleted advice rather than empathic reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on reducing shoplifting and converges on a concrete, testable plan. Falls short of 5 because direction is checklist-driven rather than collaboratively shaped and key meaning is never consolidated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is predominantly clinician-led, with most turns ending in bulleted recommendations; Jordan's own suggestions go unbuilt upon. The role-play is the one genuinely collaborative segment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice is periodically offered ('Which of those feels doable?'), but dense prescriptive advice and scripted tools create a controlling undertow with no explicit affirmation of Jordan's expertise or decision-making authority.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is supplied top-down with almost no evocative questions; Jordan's most powerful self-generated insights (Marcus's friendship, pride in saying no twice) pass unamplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** An expert, advice-dispensing stance dominates, with agenda-setting clinician-driven and Jordan choosing from a menu rather than co-creating. Some client direction in the role-play lifts this above a 1.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Existing change talk is reinforced and the refusal role-play effectively strengthens commitment. But change talk comes from structured exercises, not evocative exploration of Jordan's own reasons or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk (peer belonging, fear of ridicule, ongoing craving) goes largely unreflected, bypassed with more strategies instead. No active reinforcement of sustain talk prevents a 1, but there are no double-sided reflections.
