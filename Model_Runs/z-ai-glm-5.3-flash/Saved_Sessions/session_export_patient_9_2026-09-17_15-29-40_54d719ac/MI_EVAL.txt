# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with validation, normalization, and affirmations of Amina's strengths. Affirmations are somewhat brief and generic, and her moments of self-doubt ('That's bad, isn't it?') were not explicitly reassured with deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that captures her values and emotional pain accurately. Some reflections remain at surface content—her shame about 'forgetting her son' and resentment at always compromising were not deeply explored, and reflections sometimes get bundled with multiple questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus with clear progression from exploration to a concrete, rehearsed plan (pause script, pushback responses, 8:30 follow-through). Each exchange builds on the last, with gentle redirection when Amina self-deprecates.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: Amina's ideas (the 8:30 time, script selection, grounding preference) materially shaped the intervention. The counselor co-created the script and role-play, adjusting difficulty at her request.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Amina's ownership through offering choices ('pick or tweak one to match your voice') with no pressure or persuasion. Her final hope emerged from her own practice, not counselor insistence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from her own values and elicited strong self-generated change talk ('if it keeps one argument from happening... it's worth it'). At times supplied solutions and scripts rather than evoking her own formulations, and did not explicitly explore her confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treated Amina's expertise as central, building the final plan around her contributions. Occasionally slipped into a slight expert posture by pre-generating lists of scripts rather than drawing formulations primarily from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked commitment-oriented statements through scaling questions, choices, and confidence-building role-play. Her hopeful closing ('maybe this is actually possible') could have been savored and reinforced more deliberately rather than moving quickly to the next request.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled doubt and pessimism well without amplifying it, validating briefly and pivoting to actionable options. Occasionally echoed sustain talk more than necessary, slightly diluting the response.
