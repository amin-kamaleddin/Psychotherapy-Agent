# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental throughout; validated Erik's defensiveness and normalized his ambivalence. Warmth occasionally felt formulaic with brief, generic affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured the pills concern, wife's pressure, and fear of failure. Reflections sometimes stayed at surface level, missing deeper exploration of 'pills forever' and the social meaning of drinking with workmates.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus maintained throughout, tied consistently to diet, alcohol, and diabetes management. Session advanced steadily to a concrete plan with tracking, doc appointment, and scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Work consistently framed as joint, with the final plan genuinely co-constructed from Erik's own suggestions. At times the counselor provided structured menus and ready-made scripts rather than full co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly and explicitly reinforced Erik's choice with no pressure or persuasion; advice framed as options. Erik's own 'Cheers for not giving me a row' confirms he experienced genuine autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled use of open questions and importance/confidence scaling drew out Erik's own reasons and self-generated commitments. Sometimes replaced by providing options rather than deeper exploration of Erik's values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed Erik as the expert, with his contributions substantially shaping the session. Occasionally supplied fairly complete plans, leaving Erik to pick rather than co-create.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance ruler and confidence scaling elicited meaningful change talk (importance 5, confidence rising 6→7). Change talk was sometimes met with planning rather than deeper reflection of Erik's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and moved the conversation forward adequately. Sustain talk was frequent and often deflected with option menus rather than resolved through strategic double-sided reflections.
