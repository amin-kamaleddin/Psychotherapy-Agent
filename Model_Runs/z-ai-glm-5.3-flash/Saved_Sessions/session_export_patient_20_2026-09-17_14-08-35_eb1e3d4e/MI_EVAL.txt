# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with affirmations like 'Thanks for coming and for your honesty.' Warmth is somewhat formulaic, with limited individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some perspective-taking (acknowledging frustration at being pushed to attend), but reflective listening is sparse. Emotional moments like the patient's 'score kinda stings' were skipped over in favor of question batteries and checklists.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use from screening through to a concrete reduction plan with no topic drift. A 5 would require guiding more consistently through the patient's own ambivalence rather than moving quickly to logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Options were offered, but the session often tilted clinician-driven with pre-packaged plans and checklists. The patient's ideas (Dave, the gym) were absorbed but not deeply co-developed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated choices offered and the patient's rejection of cold turkey and full tracking was respected. Falls short of 5 because prescriptive checklists sometimes overshadow reinforcing the patient's own decision-making capacity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation emerged mostly from structured tools rather than deliberate open evocation of the patient's own values and reasons. Few open questions like 'What would cutting back do for you?' were asked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-asking for the screening and offering choices supported partnership, but much of the session was expert-driven (score interpretation, SMART options, instructional checklists), leaving uneven power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk was elicited (cutting back, gym, honest shot) largely through screening and goal-structuring questions, but it was rarely reinforced or deepened with reflections or further exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified or reinforced, and slips were helpfully reframed as 'data, not failure.' However, there were few double-sided reflections or strategic pivots; sustain talk was largely sidestepped.
