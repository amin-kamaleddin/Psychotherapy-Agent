# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, judgment-free tone with affirmations of Samuel's progress. However, affirmations are somewhat generic and could be tied more deeply to his specific values and efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening accurately capturing key insights, such as the social function of smoking. Reflections tend to be brief and quickly pivot to questions rather than dwelling on emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, unwavering focus on the lunch cigarette goal with each turn advancing the plan toward concrete commitment. Summaries effectively consolidate progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were co-constructed and acceptability checked at each step, with Samuel's input shaping the final plan. Counselor tended to propose solutions first rather than eliciting Samuel's own ideas as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames actions as Samuel's choice and seeks permission before advising. Option menus subtly steered toward counselor-generated solutions, and explicit ownership statements were absent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocative questions and confidence rulers drew out Samuel's own reasons and readiness. The counselor sometimes filled space with advice menus instead of deepening the client's best self-generated insights.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and check-ins were present, but the counselor generated most strategy ideas, positioning himself as the expert. Samuel's genuine contributions shaped the plan but often arose despite, not because of, counselor facilitation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled use of confidence rulers showed clear progression (5 to 7.5), and evocative questions elicited strong client change talk. Missed opportunities to deepen Samuel's most powerful change talk with follow-up exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified, but responses to doubt typically offered more strategy options rather than double-sided reflections or reframing. Samuel's own recovery self-talk emerged without counselor facilitation—a missed opportunity to deliberately pivot ambivalence toward change.
