# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgment throughout; Jamal expected a lecture or punishment and instead found unconditional positive regard and affirmations without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections repeatedly named the core ambivalence (belonging vs. health) and his fear of being 'the kid,' and Jamal's responses confirmed they landed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive focus on team drinking games, advancing from exploration through readiness, a concrete plan, rehearsal, and a Monday follow-up with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session operated as a dialogue between equals; the plan followed Jamal's answers, and the counselor honored his 'no promises' boundary immediately.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced throughout—'no promises needed,' 'whatever it looks like'—with every plan element originating from Jamal and no advice or directives given.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn almost entirely from the client, with questions evoking desire, ability, reasons, need, and commitment, and repeated crediting that insights 'came from you.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within the client, and the plan was entirely client-generated, with the counselor simply highlighting what Jamal built himself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and reinforcement of change talk, progressing from vague recognition ('it's bad for me') to a specific self-generated plan with rehearsal and a buddy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk met with neutral acceptance then redirected—using Marcus's precedent to let Jamal discover the feared fallout was smaller than his fear, without arguing.
