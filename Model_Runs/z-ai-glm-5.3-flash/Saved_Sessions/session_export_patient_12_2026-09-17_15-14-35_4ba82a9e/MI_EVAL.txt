# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, opening without judgment and repeatedly affirming Amira's honesty. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic, and opportunities to deeply normalize her fear of her sister's disappointment were missed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections captured both content and feeling, such as mapping the shame-loneliness-use cycle. Some emotional depth was missed, particularly the fear beneath her sister's anticipated disappointment, with some reflections staying at the level of mapping rather than meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior (evening drug use) and its antecedents, with each turn advancing the work. The session moved progressively from helplessness through functional analysis to a specific, testable plan, with safety checked appropriately at each juncture.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interview functioned largely between equals, with permission-seeking, option menus, and a co-constructed final plan with key elements from Amira. The counselor sometimes presented options as pre-packaged menus rather than openly inviting Amira's own ideas first, tilting idea generation toward the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was regularly affirmed through pacing language and respecting Amira's wish to draft the text privately rather than in session. Some suggestions (scripts, timer lengths, grounding protocols) were delivered as instructions rather than collaborative offers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently elicited from Amira rather than supplied, through open questions, mapping that drew her own insight, and the confidence ruler, with Amira generating the plan herself. Evocation was somewhat front-loaded; deeper exploration of values, hopes, and past successes could have strengthened intrinsic motivation further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently positioned Amira as the expert, and the plan was substantially her own design (texting her sister, music fallback, doing it privately). Occasionally the counselor supplied fairly prescriptive structure without always negotiating whether it fit Amira's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and reinforced through open questions, the confidence ruler, and reflective reinforcement, culminating in Amira's commitment ('I'll try the text first'). The counselor could have deepened change talk further, such as exploring why the 'maybe' mattered or asking what success would look like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several reflections repeated Amira's hopeless and skeptical language nearly verbatim, sometimes amplifying sustain talk without pairing doubt with her demonstrated capability or desire for change. The counselor never argued against or dwelled excessively on reasons against change, avoiding a lower score.
