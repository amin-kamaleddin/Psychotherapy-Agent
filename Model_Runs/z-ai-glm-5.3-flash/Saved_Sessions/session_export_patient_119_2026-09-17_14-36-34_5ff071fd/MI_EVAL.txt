# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout; client explicitly noted feeling unlectured. Missed opportunities to affirm her specific strengths and self-awareness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections and validation, but deeper emotional content (fear of complications, isolation) was glossed over. Reflections stayed summary-level rather than emotionally attuned.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed consistently focused on medication adherence and barriers. Direction occasionally became rigid repetition (safety question four times, verbatim closing instructions) rather than progressive movement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered a menu of reminder options and adjusted plan scale after pushback, but did not invite the client's own ideas or past successes. Check-in agenda was set unilaterally.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Options offered and client noted absence of lecturing, but advice and instructions were prescribed repeatedly without asking permission or checking fit. Choice-honoring was inconsistent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied on information-giving and logistics rather than drawing out motivations. The richest motivational material came from the client spontaneously, never evoked or developed by inquiry.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing via choice of options, but the stance remained expert-driven with the counselor generating ideas and structuring the plan. Client's insights were not picked up or developed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Client spontaneously offered change talk, but the counselor rarely reinforced or deepened it, pivoting instead to logistics. No open questions eliciting desire, ability, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk, but met it with problem-solving rather than strategic reflection. Key ambivalence ('is it really worth all the fuss?') was not addressed with a double-sided reflection.
