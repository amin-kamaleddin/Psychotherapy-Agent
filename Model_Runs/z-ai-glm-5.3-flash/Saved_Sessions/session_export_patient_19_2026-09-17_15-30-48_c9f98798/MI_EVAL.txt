# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard, genuine specific affirmations ('that's a strong plan'), and warm normalization of the patient's fear of failure ('all talk') with no judgment anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early complex reflections captured her ambivalence and deeper fears well, but empathy was sometimes diluted by quick pivots to questions and problem-solving before fully exploring the emotional core of her shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained exceptionally clear focus on gambling and financial behavior with no topic drift; the session progressed logically from ambivalence to a concrete, well-structured action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A genuine joint enterprise with co-constructed elements (Sunday check-in, sister as accountability partner, patient-defined win criteria), though collaboration was occasionally asymmetric when the counselor supplied pre-formed options rather than eliciting the patient's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed choice as the patient's ('which of those — or something else'), but rapid-fire advice lists (apps, drawer tricks, scripts) occasionally felt prescriptive, and ownership of successes could have been more explicitly affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from within via open questions and the confidence ruler, eliciting strong value-linked change talk (Egypt dream), though sometimes supplied tactics before exhausting the patient's own ideas and left deep material (fear of being 'all talk', sister shame) underdeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing, including letting the patient self-identify the 'hard question' for Sunday, but several turns leaned toward prescriptive advice-giving that diluted the message that wisdom resides within her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-executed evocation produced strong client-generated change talk ('cutting up the credit cards', 'Egypt money sitting there instead of lights and noises'), though counselor solution lists sometimes substituted for deeper client elaboration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged minimization and doubt without dwelling, and the reframe 'slips are data, not proof you've failed' was excellent; however, some reflections gave equal airtime to the status quo side, and sustain talk about friends was never reframed toward her values.
