# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout with affirmations like 'Love this, Marcus' and no criticism of past dieting. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and rarely highlight specific individualized strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening accurately captures Marcus's frustration and underlying values. Reflections sometimes give way quickly to advice, and deeper emotional tolls of repeated failure ('lost cause,' 'always quit') go unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity from start to finish. The session progresses logically from ambivalence to a fully specified plan with cue, phrase, backup, and scheduled check-in without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine co-creation — Marcus selects approaches, sets the Friday review time, and negotiates the plan. Still, pre-packaged menus of options show the counselor steering content more than a fully equal partnership would.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Marcus's choices and frames the plan as his own. The sheer volume of counselor-generated suggestions means he is often choosing among offered options rather than generating his own ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions initially draw out Marcus's own motivations (kids, family, dancing). Much of the session is idea-provision and logistics, missing rich opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivation around dancing and family meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and invites Marcus's choices, reflecting some power sharing. The interaction is notably advice-heavy with counselor-generated tips, keeping the counselor in a prescriptive, expert-leaning posture.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance/confidence rulers tracking growth from 3 to 7, with evocative questions that elicit strong change talk. However, Marcus's excitement (dancing) is not amplified with reflections before moving on to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about past failures is not reinforced and slips are reframed helpfully ('One night doesn't erase progress'). Yet doubts are largely met with problem-solving rather than double-sided reflections or validation before redirecting.
