# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, unconditional positive regard throughout — affirming courage, celebrating insights, and reframing hesitation as strategy. No judgment or disapproval present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and emotion, with reflections repeatedly confirmed by the client ('pretty spot on'). Emotional nuances like the absence of guilt as 'peace' were well named.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive arc from values clarification through a concrete, self-generated action plan and confidence assessment. Each question builds logically without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership — agenda co-constructed, client's metaphors treated as valuable contributions, and each next step determined by her answers.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ownership consistently reinforced through questions like 'What would you like to do with that momentum?' No advice, pressure, or directives; all choices remain the client's.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within the client — her definition of success, buffer phrase, sequencing rationale, and confidence rating all self-generated. Textbook use of a 0–10 confidence ruler.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently positioned within the client; the plan, starting point, and success criteria all came from her, with the psychiatrist reflecting and building on her ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and deepened via open questions, a concrete plan, success criteria, and a confidence ruler — each generating specific commitment language from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk handled well — the 7/10 confidence doubt was not amplified, and hesitation about the sister was reframed as strategic sequencing without reinforcing barriers to change.
