# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a polite, respectful, non-judgmental tone throughout. However, warmth is minimal and formulaic, with no explicit affirmations of Andrei beyond basic courtesy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; feelings and ambivalence are rarely reflected, and concerns like 'this is all a bit much' receive only token acknowledgment before pivoting to information. Responses are mostly informational delivery rather than understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays well focused on home BP monitoring, a concrete and achievable proximal target. However, the underlying target behavior — alcohol reduction — is never named or pursued, narrowing the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely one-directional, with the counselor delivering plans, checklists, and templates while the patient complies. There is no negotiated agenda or joint problem-solving, and Andrei's suggestions are accommodated but not built upon.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One genuine offer of choice (log template vs. cuff recommendations) is made and follow-up is framed as optional. Most of the session, however, is prescriptive advice delivery with detailed instructions rather than active support of the patient's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is assumed to come from information rather than the client; no open-ended questions explore Andrei's concerns, values, or reasons for change. This is a notable miss given the patient explicitly signaled he was attending mostly for his wife.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, dispensing protocols and log fields without asking what Andrei knows or wants. Expertise is never located with the client regarding his own habits.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is virtually no deliberate evocation of change talk; the pivotal opening when Andrei asked 'Do I really need to skip the evening drink?' was answered with a physiology lecture instead of exploring his relationship with alcohol.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk (alcohol minimization, the father anecdote, 'prove I'm fine') is glossed over or met with facts. No double-sided reflections or reframing, and past successes like quitting smoking are never affirmed to counter it.
