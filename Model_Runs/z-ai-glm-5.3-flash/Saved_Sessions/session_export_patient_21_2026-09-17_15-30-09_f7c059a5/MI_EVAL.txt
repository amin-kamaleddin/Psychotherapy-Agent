# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with barriers normalized. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and generic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture both content and ambivalence, but most are brief paraphrases. Deeper exploration of the emotional layer beneath her self-talk would strengthen this.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on increasing physical activity, with plan details and confidence ratings anchoring momentum. Minor drift when listing multiple supportive options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing with menus of ideas and client co-construction of the plan. Occasionally the counselor offers pre-packaged ideas, tilting idea generation toward the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is repeatedly reinforced, and the 'if not, I'll still go' reframe reduces external pressure. Client retains full ownership of the plan and its modifications.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the client, who generates nearly all ideas herself (TV cue, notes, garden backup, partner ask). Only occasional counselor-supplied strategies slightly reduce pure evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila is treated as the expert and her contributions substantially shape the plan. At times clustered suggestions come before fully exploring her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful use of confidence rulers, including the 'why a 6 not a 4' question, elicited strong change talk. Client confidence rose from 6 to 9 with a concrete plan and rehearsed ask.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is briefly acknowledged, never amplified, and reframed toward change via double-sided reflections. Slightly more validation before pivoting could strengthen this.
