# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard with specific, individualized affirmations and normalizes ambivalence. No judgment even when the patient is defensive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections, including complex reflections capturing the device as a symbol of feeling 'broken' and exhaustion behind 'I'll do it later.' Each reflection advanced the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior with strategic sequencing from obstacles to feelings to first steps to maintenance and relapse prevention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions framed as curiosities, expert role explicitly disclaimed, and the plan fully co-created from the patient's own ideas. Joint problem-solving throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces ownership ('That's yours, and it counts,' 'on your own terms') and honors the small self-defined commitment without pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivations drawn from the patient himself, culminating in the strengths question that produced genuine self-discovery. Change talk consistently deepened into insight.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient repeatedly positioned as the expert on his own routine, and every plan element (charger placement, one-night trial, sticky note) originated from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions tied to the patient's experience evoked progressively stronger change talk, from 'less stressed' to a concrete commitment and self-generated maintenance plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Double-sided reflections honor resistance while amplifying the change side, and sustain talk (e.g., 'don't get any ideas') is acknowledged without being dwelt on.
