# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations like 'I love that you're thinking like someone who's been through this before.' Affirmations are somewhat generic and could be more individualized to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections capture Hana's experience well, but several counselor turns are written in first person as if speaking as the client, blurring the reflective-listening structure. Emotional experiences like the shame cycle remain under-explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps clear focus on moving from avoidance to taking the exam, with good momentum from exploration to concrete planning. Occasionally lingers on emotional description rather than advancing behavioral steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with the plan co-constructed from Hana's own ideas. First-person counselor turns slightly muddy the two-partner structure but the substantive stance is collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames choices as Hana's own, with all strategies emerging from her and no pressure or prescribing. Could more explicitly reinforce her ownership of both successes and setbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effectively draws on Hana's past success story and elicits rich self-motivational statements, but some turns supply the client's motivation in first person rather than trusting her to voice it. Misses chances to deepen confidence talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Hana as the expert and builds plans from her ideas, reflecting strong power-sharing. A few first-person self-disclosure turns momentarily occupy the client's role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits change talk through open questions tied to past successes and future planning, with Hana ending ready to try. Little deliberate deepening of commitment language, such as confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without amplifying it and redirects toward strengths, but some reflections dwell on the fear side of ambivalence without pivoting back to change. No double-sided reflections used to tilt toward change.
