# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and warmth; validates unfairness and affirms competence. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and peer-pressure normalization is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening capturing both sides of ambivalence and reframing assumptions. Reflections consistently move the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on alcohol use in team settings from the opening, maintained throughout without being directive. Culminates in a concrete next step while remaining client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals; the counselor asks Jamal's take first and builds on his contributions. Plan is co-created from Jamal's own experiences and credited as 'your plan.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces ownership and never gives advice or directives. Autonomy support is implicit rather than explicitly voiced, with few direct statements of permission to choose or reject options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from the client's own values, strategies, and reasons for change, with textbook use of the confidence ruler. Client motivation rises measurably during the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing; every substantive strategy originated from Jamal. Client explicitly notes the session contrasted with his expectation of a lecture.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation including the confidence ruler, success visualization, and follow-ups that moved confidence from six to seven. Commitment language builds measurably across the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirects sustain talk toward problem-solving without reinforcing it. Pivots rely on momentum rather than deliberate double-sided reflections to dissolve ambivalence.
