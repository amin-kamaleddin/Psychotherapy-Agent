# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard; non-judgmental responses to ambivalence about abstinence ('that's information, not failure'). Client explicitly noted the absence of lecturing, and closing remarks affirmed acceptance regardless of outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including a double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence between belonging and self-control. Reflections also captured emotional meaning without minimizing the social difficulty of refusing drinks.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (party drinking) with a coherent arc from desired outcome to strategy, support, and follow-up. Every turn advanced the focus while staying within the client's agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative exchange with ideas and decisions originating from the client and joint problem-solving language ('we can figure out the next tweak together'). The counselor affirmed rather than directed at every step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices consistently left to the client, with no prescriptions, pressure, or contingencies ('you're welcome here exactly as you are, plan or no plan'). Setbacks framed as her data to use, and the client perceived the difference from being lectured.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reasons and strategies were elicited from the client rather than supplied, producing notable self-persuasion ('I just talked myself into my own plan'). Evoked change talk even expanded beyond the counselor's prompts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise clearly placed with the client, who authored her own plan ('you did most of the work here'). Her ideas shaped the session, including the accountability mechanism and follow-up request.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of desire, reason, and commitment language through open questions and requests for her own solutions. Client-generated planning was consistently affirmed and deepened rather than replaced with expert advice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('everyone drinks,' 'I might cave') was acknowledged without amplification and reframed toward change, with double-sided reflections keeping it balanced. A more deliberate reframe of the 'everyone drinks' normative belief could have been used.
