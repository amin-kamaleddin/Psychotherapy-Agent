# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, reframing self-criticism ('That's pathetic, right?') into affirmation and reassuring Daniela regardless of outcome. No judgment or disapproval appeared anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently captured Daniela's emotional core—identity concerns, exhaustion from secrecy, and fear of the first ten seconds—with the client repeatedly confirming feeling understood ('It's like you get it').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Focus on peer disclosure and self-management was steady and progressive, moving toward a concrete step. The only gap: Daniela's closing question about what happens after disclosure was left unanswered, a small missed opportunity to consolidate direction at the close.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work was explicitly framed as joint ('thinking it through as partners'), with menu options offered and decisions co-constructed. Daniela's own input visibly shaped each next step, including the Tuesday walk-home plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was exemplary, with repeated affirmation of choice and timing, and respect for Daniela's refusal to commit. The client directly named the impact: 'my choice, my speed... this felt different.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the client through evocative questions rather than lecturing or arguing for disclosure. All motivations and the plan came from Daniela's own words, which the counselor then reflected back.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniela was explicitly positioned as the expert on her friends and her own words, and permission was asked before the practice exercise. The Tuesday plan and the 'short, casual' approach were entirely her ideas, built upon by the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked and amplified, including highlighting 'maybe one person knowing wouldn't be the worst thing' and tracking her confidence from 0% to 60-70%. The session ended with a concrete plan in Daniela's own words.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I keep putting it off,' fear of being 'the diabetes girl') was acknowledged without reinforcement, using double-sided reflections and validating hesitation before pivoting toward change. Reasons against change were never amplified.
