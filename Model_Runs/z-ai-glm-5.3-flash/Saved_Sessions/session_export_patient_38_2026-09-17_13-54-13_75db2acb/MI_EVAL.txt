# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, but warmth felt formulaic; repetitive safety scripts replaced individualized affirmations of Elena's honesty and courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal and shallow; emotionally rich material (fear of life change, humor, flickers of openness) went unexplored in favor of pivoting to information-giving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The referral target — contraception decision-making after a pregnancy scare — was essentially abandoned after Elena's first deflection; the counselor never linked coping skills back to the change goal even when Elena raised it herself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements (menu options, shared pacing), but content was asymmetric: the counselor dispensed expert-formatted protocols rather than eliciting Elena's own ideas, making her more a recipient than a co-author.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session's clear strength: no pressure or persuasion, Elena's pacing and refusals honored throughout. Scored 4 because autonomy appeared as accommodation of avoidance rather than active affirmation of her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Almost no orientation toward drawing out Elena's own motivations; the counselor supplied techniques instead of using evocative questions, and generic plan restatements replaced follow-ups on her motivation hints.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena was positioned as the decider and pacing was adapted, but the counselor rarely acknowledged her expertise or asked what she thought would work, frequently offering pre-packaged solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena volunteered meaningful change talk ('maybe next time we can talk about not dodging bullets') that was never reinforced, reflected, or deepened; no open evocative questions or scaling were used.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** No arguing with sustain talk and deferring the topic avoided reinforcing it, but this was passive avoidance rather than strategic softening; no double-sided reflections linked her ambivalence with her fear.
