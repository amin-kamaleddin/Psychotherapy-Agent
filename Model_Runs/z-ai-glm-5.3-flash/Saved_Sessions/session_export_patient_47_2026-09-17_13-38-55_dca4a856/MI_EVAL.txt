# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout; never labeled her drinking a problem and acknowledged her view. However, affirmations were few and warmth somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections showed basic perspective-taking, but frequent shifts to rapid questions and advice menus crowded out deeper listening. Emotionally significant material (fear of the fall, loneliness, her son's opinion) was never explored or reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use, progressing coherently from assessment to a concrete plan with a two-week review. Direction was achieved largely through the counselor's agenda rather than collaborative goal-setting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission once and invited some input on options, but repeatedly delivered pre-structured plans and tip lists, positioning the counselor as plan-author. Collaborative spirit was present but uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evelyn could reject suggestions and choose her dry days, but detailed prescriptions came early and before commitment. Explicit statements honoring her ownership of change were absent, and her 'no promises though' went unacknowledged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to draw out Evelyn's own motivations; her most meaningful reasons for change were client-initiated and unexplored. The counselor supplied reasons through advice and risk tips rather than evoking them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction was notably expert-driven, with pre-packaged plans presented before exploring Evelyn's ideas. Her expertise shaped the plan only after she pushed back.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and a readiness ruler elicited some change talk, but these moments were never reinforced, reflected, or deepened. Change talk arose mostly independently rather than from skillful evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with her minimization, which is a strength, but sustain talk was met with fact-gathering or plan-pitching rather than strategic reframing. Her defenses were tolerated but not skillfully softened or resolved.
