# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, explicitly avoids labeling, and affirms Kevin's honesty. Remains warm and non-judgmental even during minimization, welcoming his humor.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and consistent, capturing both content and emotional undertone. Even subtle cues like 'probably' are caught and each reflection deepens the client's exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains steady focus on the target behavior while progressing logically from incident to patterns, functions, alternatives, and a concrete plan. Session closes with a specific, measurable action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is genuinely between equals; Kevin's ideas entirely determine the plan while the counselor reflects, affirms, and checks fit. Decisions are framed as Kevin's own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice-giving or pressure; control stays with the client through questions like 'is it something you actually want to try?'. Kevin explicitly notes the plan is 'small enough that it's not scary,' reflecting preserved autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently elicit Kevin's own reasons, solutions, and motivations, with every plan element originating from the client. The counselor never supplies reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Kevin as the expert on his own life, with all strategies coming from him and ownership consistently attributed. Client contributions substantially shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions evoke and amplify Kevin's own motivations, each highlighted and reinforced. Culminates in explicit commitment: 'Yeah... actually, yeah, I do.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and validated, then gently redirected toward the client's own concerns without argument. Never amplifies reasons for the status quo, instead using sustain talk as a springboard for change exploration.
