# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations of Camila's efforts and self-compassion. Affirmations were somewhat generic and lacked deeper unconditional regard beyond her planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections captured both sides of ambivalence and her fear of failure. Reflections faded in the second half, and deeper emotional themes like her history of letting herself down went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity throughout, progressing steadily from ambivalence to a concrete, actionable plan without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely joint problem-solving, including a genuine role-play moment. The opening strategy menu and script rewrite were somewhat expert-driven and imposed the counselor's phrasing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and let Camila select, adapt, and own the plan. Counselor-supplied menus and script tweaks reduced full ownership, with few explicit statements that change belongs to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of confidence rulers and reflective questions elicited Camila's own reasons, resources, and commitment language. The counselor sometimes supplied rationale and solutions rather than first drawing out her own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila was positioned as decision-maker with plans built on her routines. Early advice-giving and the script rewrite slightly undercut her ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced commitment language, with the confidence scaling raising self-efficacy from 7 to 8 and ending in strong commitment. Occasionally supplied solutions rather than letting Camila generate her own.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without amplifying it, using double-sided reflections and pivoting constructively to a 'no-fail' framework. Heavy focus on obstacle-planning kept some attention on barriers.
