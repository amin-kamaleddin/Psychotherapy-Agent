### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, honoring the patient's minimizing stance and privacy wishes, but explicit personal affirmations beyond the logging commitment are limited.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captures his perspective, fatigue with lectures, and family focus, though reflections feel somewhat formulaic and emotional undercurrents (defensiveness about his wife, self-reliance) go unexplored.

### Collaboration

### Partnership
