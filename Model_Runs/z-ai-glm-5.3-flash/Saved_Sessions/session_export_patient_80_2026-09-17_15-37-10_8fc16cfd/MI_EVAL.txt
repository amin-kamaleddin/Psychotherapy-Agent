# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations ('Good on you for those changes', 'That was strong'). However, affirmations were somewhat generic and praise-focused rather than deeply individualized to Luka's character strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening capturing both progress and ambivalence, including the emotional core ('I hear that hits your pride'). But the emotional weight of Luka's history of fights and fear of sliding back was not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on managing drinking in high-risk social situations, building progressively from triggers to strategies to a concrete if-then plan. Every exchange advanced the change goal without drift — exemplary MI direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely interactive role-play and co-constructed if-then plan reflected joint problem-solving. Falls short of 5 because the counselor dominated idea generation, offering pre-packaged solution lists rather than eliciting Luka's solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered menu-style choices and adapted when Luka rejected options. However, the structure remained suggestion-heavy, with no explicit statement affirming Luka's ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within via 'What's helped you reduce drinking so far?' and the confidence ruler, eliciting Luka's powerful self-motivating statement about not needing to prove anything. Some turns slipped into advice delivery rather than continuing to evoke.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for Luka's input, but frequently took an expert stance, generating most ideas (drink limits, deflection lines, text cues) in list form. Missed opportunities to draw out Luka's own ideas before offering suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evoked change language, producing significant self-motivating statements ('Maybe the strongest thing is being the guy who doesn't have to prove anything anymore'). Some change-talk moments, like 'since I figured out I like my life better this way', were passed over without reflection.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Luka's sustain talk about continued drinking and fear of caving was acknowledged but never amplified or reinforced. The frequent option-listing kept the focus on the difficulty of resisting drinking rather than strategically pivoting to Luka's strengths.
