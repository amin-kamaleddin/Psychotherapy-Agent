# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with a sarcastic, defensive patient, offering unconditional regard and affirming his honesty. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deep individualization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections captured the patient's ambivalence well. However, emotional undercurrents—fear of lectures, shame about non-adherence—were not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on asthma self-management and secured a concrete commitment (calling for a telehealth visit this week). The counselor accepted deferral of smoking, a relevant target, without flagging it for later.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered menus and shaped the plan around the patient's preferences (checklist over rehearsal, phone visit first). The counselor generated most of the content, with the patient mostly approving rather than co-creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear strength: opened with 'it's your call how much you engage,' repeatedly offered choices, normalized imperfection, and validated the patient's conditions without pushback. The patient's self-owned commitment reflects genuinely fostered autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited, but much of the session involved supplying solutions, scripts, and logistics. There was no exploration of the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence for managing his asthma.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the patient as decision-maker, and his choices genuinely shaped the session. The counselor occasionally slipped into advice-heavy mode, diluting full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced the patient's own commitments ('Maybe... the phone visits,' 'I'll call this week'). Missed opportunities to deepen commitment language, such as confidence scaling or defining success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and sometimes reframed it effectively, but pivoted to practical scripts rather than strategic MI techniques like double-sided reflections. Also acquiesced to avoiding smoking rather than skillfully softening the topic.
