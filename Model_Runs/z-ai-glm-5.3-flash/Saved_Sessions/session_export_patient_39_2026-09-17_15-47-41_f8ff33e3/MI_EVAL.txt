# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental with individualized affirmations ('You did something brave by going'). Falls short of a 5 only because affirmations occasionally feel slightly formulaic rather than deeply attuned to Elias's emotional experience of aging and limitation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing both sides of ambivalence, including his wife's smile and breathing barriers. Reflections sometimes transition quickly into question batteries, limiting depth of emotional exploration—e.g., the harsh inner voice was not probed earlier.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on physical activity with each turn advancing the agenda from barriers to a concrete, time-limited plan. No drift into unrelated content, with every exchange converted into momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way, with the plan co-constructed from Elias's own materials—his bolero, kitchen, wife, and breathing technique. The counselor actively incorporated Elias's contributions, including adopting 'half a song counts' as the official backup.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently honored through open choices and seeking permission before the breathing exercise, with all decisions originating from Elias. Some question sequences subtly steer toward the counselor's preferred structure, and ownership could be reinforced more explicitly at closing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from within the client—rulers and open questions elicited Elias's own values, solutions, self-talk, and insight about his harsh inner voice. Textbook evocation with no arguing for change or counselor-supplied reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias treated as the expert, with his own ideas (kitchen practice, the bolero, Rosita's presence) becoming the actual plan and permission sought before guiding breathing. Some turns are question-dense and interview-driven, and the backup plan suggestion leads a bit rather than fully co-constructing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation of change talk through micro-goals, importance/confidence rulers, and confidence-raising questions, producing rich client-generated language ('I think I owe myself that much'). The session moved from vague hope to commitment-level language ('Then it's settled') with confidence rising from 5 to 8.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent redirection of sustain talk, reframing 'I couldn't keep up' as brave, and converting the half-song barrier into a success criterion. Lacked explicit double-sided reflections before pivoting, and the inner critic ('You're 68, Elias, stay in your chair') was not directly worked with in MI-consistent fashion.
