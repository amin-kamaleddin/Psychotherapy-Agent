# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and affirming with no judgment about violence history or drinking. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked explicit normalization of his ambivalence and fear of being infantilized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing both emotional content and practical dilemmas. Missed deeper exploration of Wiremu's remorse, shame, and the whānau/cultural context.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on target behaviors with progressive momentum from dilemma identification to rehearsed action. Every turn ended with a concrete next step or choice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely equal partnership — co-constructed the trial agreement and engaged in genuine collaborative roleplay. The counselor sometimes drafted materials alone rather than co-writing line-by-line with Wiremu.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Wiremu's ownership through constant option-framing and honoring his preferences (text over call). No coercive or persuasive language appeared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Methods consistently drew motivation from within the client via evocative questions, the three-conditions exercise, and roleplay. Wiremu's strongest change talk and relapse plan emerged from evocative prompting, not counselor persuasion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise clearly placed in the client — his words shaped the trial agreement, text draft, and relapse plan. Wiremu's contributions demonstrably drove the session's content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and developed change language, converting vague motivation into concrete commitment through the three-conditions exercise and roleplay. Wiremu's own text draft and 'this feels doable' were direct products of the counselor's evocative structure.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk without amplification, reflecting it neutrally and pivoting to solution-focused questions. Lacked deliberate double-sided reflections to strategically soften ambivalence.
