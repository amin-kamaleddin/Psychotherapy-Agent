# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed David's practical wisdom and respected his boundaries without judgment. Patient explicitly noted he 'walked in braced for the usual lecture,' evidence the non-judgmental stance landed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently captured core meaning, e.g., distilling the smoking as 'that break—stepping outside, five minutes to yourself.' Patient confirmed feeling heard and understood throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Focus stayed tightly on the daytime smoking target, moving progressively from exploration to concrete planning. Minor drift: one question about the swap's requirements was redundant, drawing 'we've kind of covered this' from the patient.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was genuinely co-constructed, with the counselor brainstorming alongside rather than directing. David's own ideas (pouch trial, success criteria, limited scope) shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evening smoking was accepted without pressure and the plan was explicitly framed as David's own ('it's yours to decide'). Patient responded by setting his own terms and limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within via open questions about what the swap would need and what David would 'look forward to.' His own reasons (cost, chest concerns, the value of the break) drove the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** David was repeatedly positioned as the expert on his own routine, and his contributions substantially shaped the session. The counselor built on each of his ideas rather than imposing a protocol.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled use of open questions and affirmations evoked sustained change talk, from 'that's maybe worth a go' to designing his own trial with success criteria. Health motivation (chest/cough) also emerged from the patient himself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk, responding to 'I don't see the point' with neutral, complex reflections. Some reflections briefly restated sustain content (evenings untouched) without always pivoting toward change.
