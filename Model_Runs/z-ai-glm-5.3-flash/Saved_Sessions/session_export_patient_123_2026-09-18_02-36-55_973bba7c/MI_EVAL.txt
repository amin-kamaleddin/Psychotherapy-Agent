# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout; explicitly normalizes Sofia's lack of goals and releases her from apologizing. Kept from a 5 by generic warmth and affirmations not tied to specific strengths or values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, often complex reflections ('your friends are kind of your anchor'). Mostly content-level; misses opportunities to reflect the anxiety beneath Sofia's hedged statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Critical weakness: the alcohol screening that prompted the referral is never named or explored. The session drifts into hobbies and vague future hopes, losing the target behavior entirely.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asks open, curiosity-driven questions built from Sofia's own words and shares the agenda. Stays at friendly conversational partnering without an explicitly co-constructed working agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Explicitly hands over control ('we can start wherever you'd like') and avoids pressure or directives. However, autonomy support is a passive absence of control rather than active fostering of client choice, and the target behavior is never engaged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocative stance — elicits Sofia's own interests, ideas, and future vision ('you know yourself better than anyone'). Limited by staying at general life-direction and never consolidating elicited motivation toward a change goal.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Sofia as the expert ('help me understand', 'I'd rather hear from you than from any screening') and asks rather than tells. Slightly conversational rather than fully power-shifting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine, well-executed evocative questions elicit modest desire and reason language ('it would feel nice to not panic'). Change talk is never deepened with refined reflections, confidence questions, or commitment-strengthening follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles mild sustain talk acceptably — acknowledges without amplifying and gently pivots toward the future. Redirection is light-touch, and the actual behavior of concern (alcohol) was never addressed, so no skill demonstrated on the core issue.
