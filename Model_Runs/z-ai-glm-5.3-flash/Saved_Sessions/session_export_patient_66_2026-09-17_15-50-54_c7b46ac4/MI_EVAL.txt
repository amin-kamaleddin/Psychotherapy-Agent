# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental despite Kareem's resistant opening; explicitly avoided pushing quitting and honored his conditions. Warmth was somewhat generic, and affirmations were sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Early complex reflections accurately captured meaning, naming autonomy and social connection as core values. Reflections became scarce in the second half, shifting toward advice and logistics with less emotional mirroring.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed anchored to the client-defined goal of getting through smoke-free workdays, ending in a concrete testable plan. The broader cessation goal was never linked back, leaving the ultimate change goal unexamined.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong equal-partner tone early, with Kareem co-designing the break, tracking method, and follow-up. The middle gum-dosing section became a one-way didactic block that flattened the collaborative feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced choice with menu-style options and the closing frame "You're steering this"; Kareem's no-lecture condition was respected without protest. Exemplary autonomy support throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good early evocation (designing an acceptable break, defining success markers). The second half relied on counselor-supplied information rather than eliciting motivation; values (family, health) were never explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kareem was consistently positioned as the expert, and his preferences genuinely shaped the plan. The counselor slipped into information-delivery mode without always checking what Kareem already knew or wanted.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged ("I don't see why that couldn't work," "part of me is curious") and exploratory questions helped evoke it. Missed chances to deepen, especially letting Kareem's "Maybe" about the longer term pass unreflected.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced, and reframing the goal as "surviving cravings" wisely disarmed resistance. However, sustain talk was mostly bypassed rather than strategically redirected toward Kareem's own change reasons.
