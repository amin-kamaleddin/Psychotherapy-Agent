# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout; deflection was never punished and even the closing affirmed Samir without conditions. Exemplary acceptance of a resistant client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent, mostly accurate reflections tracked Samir's humor and change talk well. Early reflections slightly over-interpreted, and some emotional nuances (the shame in 'hating admitting it') could have been explored more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on medication adherence with each question building toward a concrete plan. A brief detour into family dynamics, though productive, took a couple of exchanges from the target behavior before returning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session operated between equals, with the counselor asking rather than telling and co-constructing the follow-up plan. Samir's own comment about expecting a lecture confirms the non-authoritarian feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, explicit reinforcement of Samir's ownership, including welcoming his conditions and checking 'Does that fit?' before scheduling. The closing door-open metaphor is exemplary autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within rather than persuaded, moving Samir from precontemplative deflection to his own reasons and a self-generated commitment. This is precisely the outcome evocation aims for.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in the client ('I'm not the expert on your life—you are'), and the entire plan originated from Samir. His contributions visibly drove the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation and immediate amplification of Samir's own ideas, drawing commitment language ('I'll give it a shot. A real shot'). Questions built anticipation of success effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Abundant sustain talk was consistently met with pivots rather than argument, including reframing and double-sided reflections. Resistance never escalated and Samir progressively softened.
