# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, even in the face of sarcasm and defensiveness ("Oscar hasn't made me die yet"). Affirmations are genuine and individualized: "you just did something most people don't," "that's problem-solving, not carelessness."

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate, layered, and emotionally attuned. The opening reflection validates exhaustion and the wish to "just get on with living." The counselor captures meaning, not just content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus on asthma self-management from the opening line through the commitment to start "tomorrow morning." Each exchange advances the agenda; no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Works "with" rather than "on" the client: "let's figure it out together," "what do you need from me." Power-sharing is genuine—the client sets the terms ("that's the deal") and the counselor builds on them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Autonomy continuously reinforced: "I'm not here to pile on more demands," "you tell me if it fits," "on your terms." Explicitly validates the right to withhold commitment ("I respect the 'not promising anything'").

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client via open questions ("what would it mean for you if this actually worked," "which one would matter most"). Elicits his own reasons—freedom from dread, his wife's happiness—and amplifies them, including the emotionally significant piece he tried to minimize.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client: "You know Oscar and your routine better than anyone." The two-item plan, doctor communication strategy, and start date all come from the client, not the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5 ☑
**Comments:** Change talk systematically evoked: concrete plan (brown inhaler with brushing teeth, rescue inhaler in truck), hope ("maybe the stairs don't leave me winded"), self-efficacy ("maybe I just... don't do that this time"). Each instance reflected and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4 ☑
**Comments:** Sustain talk consistently acknowledged without reinforcement and gently pivoted toward change; effective double-sided reflections used. The "third thing and I'm done" ultimatum was left unexplored rather than directly softened.

---

**Overall Global Ratings Score:** **4.9/5** — An exceptionally strong MI session. Minor room to grow in directly softening the client's all-or-nothing ultimatum.
