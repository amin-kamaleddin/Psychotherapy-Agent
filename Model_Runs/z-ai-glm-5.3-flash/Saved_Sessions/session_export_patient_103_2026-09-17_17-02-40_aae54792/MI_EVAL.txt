# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveyed warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard despite Nabil's hostility and defensiveness. His refusals (no role-play, no complaint email) were accepted without judgment or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured both content and feeling, with pivotal reflections on his anger and snapping at home that noticeably softened him. Could have probed deeper emotions, such as the shame implicit in the 'angry patient' label or the hurt behind feeling dismissed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus on the target behavior throughout, steering digressions back efficiently. The session progressed logically from validation to concrete tools to a clear, time-bound action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was a genuinely collaborative exchange, with the counselor offering menus of options and co-constructing the plan from Nabil's choices. Nabil's own insights, like anger following him home, shaped the direction of the intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforced Nabil's ownership of choices and respected refusals without pressure, pivoting gracefully when he pushed back. Options were framed as Nabil's decisions ('which feels most doable?'). 

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions, the confidence ruler, and leveraging intrinsic motivators (his wife's comment, professional self-image) drew out Nabil's own reasons and commitment. Deducted for solution-heavy exchanges and absence of hope/importance scaling alongside confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nabil's expertise was treated as central, and his refusals and preferences genuinely shaped the plan. Occasionally moved too quickly into providing ready-made scripts before fully exploring Nabil's own problem-solving ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation of change talk, with Nabil progressing from 'I don't need strategies' to explicit commitment language ('I'll try it this week'). Confidence rose from 5 to 7-7.5, and the key motivational hook ('knowing it works once') was captured.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally acknowledged barriers briefly and pivoted to practical options rather than dwelling on hopelessness. Minor deduction as some responses reflected Nabil's concerns back ('you're right to worry'), briefly reinforcing the barrier before redirecting.
