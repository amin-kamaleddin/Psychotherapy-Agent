# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout — validated the client's minimization ('a real tension to sit with') and honored his 'no lecturing' request without defensiveness. Specific, genuine affirmations like 'you've done something real here tonight.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captured both sides of ambivalence and named the emotional core, e.g., 'she shouldn't have to pick up the slack.' Each reflection moved the conversation deeper.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on drinking/smoking while remaining patient-led: ambivalence → morning-after incident → good vs. bad night contrast → concrete plan → obstacle → two-week commitment. No aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Agenda and plan built jointly — the three-part plan, excuse scripts, and follow-up timing all originated from the client. Counselor organized and affirmed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent autonomy reinforcement: 'You're the one in the driver's seat,' 'it's your call,' plus offering choices (check-in vs. weekend first). Setbacks framed as information, protecting ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason for change came from the client — daughter's watching eyes, wife's relief, independence from the lads. Counselor never supplied reasons; client concluded 'I'll give it a proper go' on his own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client ('You know your life, your friends, your triggers better than anyone'). Client confirmed it worked: 'Nobody's actually asked me that before.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation arc from 'it wasn't really that much' to 'I'll give it a proper go.' Strategic questions drew the first admission, a self-generated plan, and self-motivating statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk consistently reflected neutrally as ambivalence rather than argued against, with double-sided reflections keeping the change side alive. Never reinforced the minimization.
