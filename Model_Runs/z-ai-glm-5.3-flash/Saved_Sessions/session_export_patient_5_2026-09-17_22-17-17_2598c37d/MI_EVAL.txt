# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, reframing hesitance as strength ('careful steward of your own health'). Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with no labeling or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captures her ambivalence and values precisely. Falls short by restating content rather than probing deeper emotional meaning, e.g., fear of being left without mood support.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus moving from safety to washout logistics, side effects, and prescriber preparation. Transitions follow the patient's stated priorities with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative, checking in before providing information and inviting her to guide the agenda. Some informational blocks (serotonin syndrome, adjustment period) shift the flow toward education rather than joint construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly reinforces choice throughout — asking what feels right and framing dosing adjustments as her decision 'with guidance.' Patient explicitly notes liking the flexibility; no pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions about her priorities draw motivation from within, with her own reasoning supplying momentum. Relies somewhat on reframing rather than evoking deeper personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert on her own life, and her preferences clearly shape the plan. Slightly reduced by substantial informational delivery in a somewhat leading educational fashion.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reinforces change language, immediately affirming 'that's settled for me.' Limited exploration of confidence or commitment regarding the medication switch itself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Gently reframes hesitance as reasonable care rather than amplifying it, pivoting quickly to actionable planning. Double-sided reflections briefly keep both sides in focus without tilting toward change.
