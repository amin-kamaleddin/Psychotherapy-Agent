# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, affirming Samir's autonomy ('I respect that') without labeling him noncompliant. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and responses sometimes leaned toward problem-solving lists.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captured Samir's frustration, humor, and exhaustion with lectures. Reflections were occasionally summary-like rather than emotionally deep, missing chances to explore being 'kept in the dark.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on medication nonadherence from the opening question through concrete next steps. Every turn connected to the target behavior without drift while respecting Samir's pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative framing with menu-based, co-selected options and invitations like 'we can pick one that might fit your life.' However, the counselor sometimes handed over finished products (questions, scripts) rather than co-authoring them with Samir.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary reinforcement of Samir's ownership: options framed as declinable menus, explicit honoring of his refusal to commit ('You'll ask, not commit. I respect that'). No pressure applied at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evocative open questions were used, but when Samir stayed guarded the counselor shifted to supplying content rather than persistently drawing out his own reasons and hopes. Deeper exploration of his values was missed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samir was consistently positioned as decision-maker and his choices visibly shaped the session. Some turns delivered pre-packaged content with limited solicitation of Samir's own wording or ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions elicited genuine movement ('maybe I'll think about it,' 'I'm showing up'), but the counselor did not consistently reflect or amplify these emergent change statements, instead moving quickly to provide content.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Abundant sustain talk was acknowledged briefly without amplification, and the counselor consistently pivoted toward what Samir could control. Occasional option-listing brushed against sustain-talk territory but never reinforced reasons against change.
