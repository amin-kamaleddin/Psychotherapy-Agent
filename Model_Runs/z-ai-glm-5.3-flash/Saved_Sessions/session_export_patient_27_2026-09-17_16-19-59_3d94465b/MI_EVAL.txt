# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout; normalizes Daniela's fears, rejects self-criticism ('you're not bratty'), and responds to hedging with backup plans rather than disapproval. No labeling, judgment, or conditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects the core dilemma (acceptance vs. health) and validates fears, but reflections are brief and sometimes skipped in favor of option-giving; deeper emotional reflection of feeling 'fragile' and the weight of secrecy is limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior with logical progression from naming the fear to a concrete Wednesday-night plan; every counselor turn tied back to the goal with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed session: Daniela's edits (mixing scripts, removing 'random thing', choosing Thursday backup) were immediately integrated, and the final plan is composed entirely of her own words and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every turn offers menus and choices, explicitly permits hesitation, validates the client's preferred pace and channel (texting), and co-creates a backup plan rather than pushing when Daniela hedges.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws motivation from within (storytelling as a strength, 'Which feels most like you?'), but early turns supplied scripts and psychoeducation before eliciting Daniela's own reasons for disclosure and her own solution ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly centers the client's contributions, which shaped the plan throughout; however, moments of steering with pre-packaged script menus and no explicit naming of Daniela as the expert on her own life keep this from a 5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary evocation and reinforcement of change talk through option menus, guided practice, and affirmations; each instance of client change talk ('Wednesday night. I'll actually do it') was affirmed and built upon, culminating in self-generated confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Briefly validates sustain talk and pivots to change-oriented options without dwelling on barriers, but relies on supportive reframes rather than strategic double-sided reflections linking ambivalence to the client's own values.
