# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Polite and nonjudgmental with a welcoming tone and crisis disclaimer, but warmth is generic and no affirmations or explicit validation of Naomi's self-criticism are offered. Basic respect is present without deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent; disclosures of shame, fear of crowds, and texting anxiety are met with tool lists and plans rather than emotional reflections. Naomi does her own meaning-making with little counselor help.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on social anxiety in photography settings and progressively narrows to a concrete action plan. Loses a point because the direction is clinician-imposed and the emotional dimension is never addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is predominantly counselor-directed with numbered instructions and ready-made plans; Naomi is consulted only on minor details like shot types and dates. Power-sharing is token rather than genuine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Binary menu choices gesture at choice but remain within the counselor's predetermined framework, with no explicit statement that Naomi is in charge of change. Her choices operate only inside the counselor's agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor never asks what matters to Naomi, why change feels important, or how confident she feels. Her change talk arises opportunistically rather than through skilled elicitation—a core MI deficit.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly positions itself as the expert handing down protocols and drafted messages rather than drawing out Naomi's expertise. Her contributions shape the plan somewhat, but the stance remains advice-dispensing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerges ('maybe trying something small wouldn't hurt') and the plan-confirmation capitalizes on her commitments. However, motivation is not deliberately evoked—no confidence rulers or exploration of her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Naomi's doubts and briefly reframes avoidance as understandable. However, sustain talk like 'I'll just freeze anyway' is passed over rather than resolved with double-sided reflections.
