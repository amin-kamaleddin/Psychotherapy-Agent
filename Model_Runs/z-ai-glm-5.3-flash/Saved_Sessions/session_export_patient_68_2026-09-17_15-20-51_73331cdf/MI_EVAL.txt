# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental tone throughout, with validating affirmations and explicit acceptance of Kevin's choice not to quit. Some affirmations felt formulaic, and Kevin's self-critical moment ('pathetic that I need a script') wasn't explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured Kevin's ambivalence and values well. Emotional content (embarrassment, fear of mockery) was acknowledged but not deeply explored, with quick pivots to option lists.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed focused and built a concrete harm-reduction plan with clear momentum toward action this weekend. The broader drinking pattern and its stress-coping function were avoided, limiting depth of direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor behaved as an equal partner, building the plan on Kevin's own solutions and inviting his judgment. However, the counselor supplied most option menus, making Kevin more a selector than a co-generator of ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent autonomy support—choice was repeatedly emphasized, Kevin's limits were fully honored ('totally valid you're not quitting'), and no pressure was applied beyond the weekend. His growing self-efficacy (confidence rising to 8) reflects successful ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin's own reasons and strategies were effectively elicited, driving motivation from within. Reliance on confidence scaling left deeper motivational exploration (what drinking means to him, values beyond the weekend) untouched.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin was positioned as the expert, and his ideas substantially shaped the plan with reinforced ownership. The counselor tended to provide option menus first rather than drawing ideas primarily from Kevin.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence scaling moved Kevin from 5.5 to 8, and he generated his own strategies and commitment language ('I'll actually give it a go'). Missed chances to reflect change talk back and deepen it (e.g., exploring what moved him from 5.5 to 7) kept this from a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with skillful double-sided reflections and explicit honoring of Kevin's pace, without amplification. Frequent option-offering sometimes worked around sustain talk rather than resolving it, and drinking as a coping tool was never directly addressed.
