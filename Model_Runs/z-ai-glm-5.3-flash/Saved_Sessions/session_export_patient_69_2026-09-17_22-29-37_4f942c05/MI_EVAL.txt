# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validating the client's cynicism and honesty without judgment—even the '3' derailment was met with respect and curiosity rather than disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Delivers accurate, emotionally attuned reflections capturing both content and feeling (e.g., 'feeling unseen inside them,' 'the ground keeps moving'), including the client's fear of abandonment by revolving staff.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus from opening to close, channeling frustration into employment as the target and ending with a concrete commitment; even the '3' derailment didn't disrupt direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works between equal partners with collaborative framing ('set up together today'); the client shifts from passive compliance ('Where do you want me to sign?') to proposing the plan himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly and repeatedly supported ('Whatever you share—or don't—is up to you,' 'your call entirely'), with no pressure or coercive language; the client even notes 'Never had the choice before.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation comes from the client's own words—desire for a real job, need for follow-through, definition of a working relationship—with strategic questions rather than lectures; 'Maybe this is different' signals internally generated hope.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise in the client ('You know your situation better than anyone'), and the client's own priorities shaped the agenda and next step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk from a reluctant client via forward-looking questions, drawing out his desire for 'a job. Real one' and a concrete step (researching realistic jobs), each affirmed and built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and consistently pivots toward change, but occasionally reflects sustain talk at length (e.g., 'programs just end' themes) before redirecting; tighter pivots would be more efficient.
