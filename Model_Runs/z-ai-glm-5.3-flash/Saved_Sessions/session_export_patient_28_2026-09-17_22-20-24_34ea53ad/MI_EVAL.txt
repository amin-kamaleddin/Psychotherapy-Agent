# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard—validating Dante's busy life, affirming his insight, and normalizing imperfection. No judgment or labeling appears anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, consistent reflective listening throughout, including an excellent double-sided reflection balancing desire for energy against feelings of unreality. The counselor also catches subtle themes like the gap between how Dante treats his friend versus himself.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on re-establishing exercise, moving logically from barriers to values to a concrete plan. Each question builds momentum with essentially no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as a partnership between equals, with ideas co-developed through curiosity and the plan entirely Dante-generated. The counselor serves as a reflective companion rather than a director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly reinforced ('You get to set the terms here'), and Dante chooses the behavior, timing, and accountability with no imposed advice or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from within the client—his values, his own solutions, and his self-compassion insight. Affirmations highlight Dante's own resources, and final reflections anchor change in his identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positions Dante as the expert, and every major insight (lunch window, all-or-nothing pattern, plan) originates from the client. Dante's contributions substantially shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked through open questions, the friend-advice reframe, and future-focused questions, eliciting strong desire and commitment language. Each emergence of change talk is reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but not reinforced, using double-sided reflections that pivot toward change. Minor drawback: one early reflection introduced negative framing ('avoiding the gym feels safer') Dante had not explicitly voiced.
