# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, respectful stance throughout with no labeling of Alexei's experimentation. Affirmations are somewhat brief and formulaic, missing chances to affirm deeper strengths like his initiative to tell his mom himself.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects Alexei's frustration and core values (soccer, avoiding lectures), but reflections remain brief and cognitive. Few complex reflections explore the emotional weight of nearly being caught or fear of his mother.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on alcohol use linked to soccer goals, confidentiality concerns, and the upcoming playoff, moving coherently from awareness to skills rehearsal to disclosure planning. Minor drift into logistical details (confidentiality rules, chart access).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely egalitarian—Alexei chooses the refusal line, invents the Coke redirect, and proposes telling his mom himself. The counselor's tendency to deliver prepared lists and plans rather than jointly building them keeps it from a full 5.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers menus of options and defers to Alexei's choices, who freely generates his own plan. Sometimes provides prescriptive steps and scripts without first asking what Alexei already knows or wants to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Relies heavily on supplying information, scripts, and strategies rather than eliciting Alexei's own reasons, confidence, and solutions. Rich moments—like his plan to self-disclose to his mom—go unexplored with evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly asks permission and offers choices about the session agenda. The session often slips into advice-giving mode with pre-built scripts and plans, and Alexei's own ideas are acknowledged but not substantially elaborated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws out strong change talk ('I already know what I need to do,' 'I don't wanna become addicted') and rehearses commitment through role-play. Does not consistently deepen these statements with follow-up evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responds to minimization with normalization and redirects toward Alexei's goals rather than arguing. Occasionally implicitly agrees with sustain talk instead of using double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
