# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with consistent normalization ('that's normal to feel unsure', 'you're not in trouble'). Falls short of 5 due to somewhat scripted phrasing and limited individualized affirmation of Sofia.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal, with heavy reliance on question batteries and menu options rather than capturing Sofia's meaning. When she voiced confusion about why she was there, the counselor responded factually rather than reflecting her unease.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifted into light chat about movies and weekends despite a positive alcohol-risk screening. The target behavior was briefly touched in a question battery and never revisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Options were offered, but the session was structured as counselor-led assessment with no joint agenda-setting about the screening result. Sofia's input shaped little beyond topic selection.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor's strongest area—consistent choices, 'you're in charge here,' and no pressure or directives. However, autonomy support was largely procedural (menu selection) rather than fostering ownership of any change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The approach was fact-gathering rather than evocative; Sofia's own motivations and concerns about the alcohol screening were never drawn out. The session ended with advice-giving (study tips), an expert stance inconsistent with MI.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Verbal emphasis on 'you're in charge' and choices superficially supported partnership, but the agenda remained the counselor's. Sofia's questions about the session's purpose were not explored or handed real influence.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's disclosure of drinking at parties was summarized as 'occasional partying' and dropped without exploring her thoughts, risks, or values around alcohol. No change language regarding the target behavior was ever cultivated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor appropriately did not reinforce Sofia's minimizations ('everyone does,' 'not a big deal'), but also did not actively soften them. No double-sided reflections or reframes connected her values to her drinking.
