### Acceptance

**Comments:** Basic respect and validation of Eric's honesty, no judgment shown, but little warmth or affirmation of his strengths; session is largely transactional.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Minimal reflective listening; only one accurate reflection ('cough as normal, wife worried'), while Eric's ambivalence and minimizations go unreflected.

### Direction

**Comments:** The target behavior of smoking was abandoned at Eric's first resistance, and focus shifted to communication coaching and logistics without gentle revisiting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2

### Autonomy Support

### Partnership

**Comments:** Counselor treated himself as the expert, supplying scripts and solutions instead of drawing out Eric's own ideas about what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
