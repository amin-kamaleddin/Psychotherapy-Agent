### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently communicates warm, unconditional positive regard with no judgment about avoidance or self-doubt. Normalizes her careful weighing and affirms her existing professional skills.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captures both surface content and deeper meaning, e.g., 'hiding takes energy' and mirroring her core fears of exposure and being stuck.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear progressive focus: triggers, coping failures, vision, concrete strategies, low-stakes practice, and definition of success, ending with commitment to continue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals; the plan is co-constructed from Naomi's own ideas, with her self-narration strategy becoming the centerpiece—true power sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced: 'you get to decide what counts as success' and 'on your terms.' Naomi explicitly registers and appreciates this control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client via open questions evoking vision and solutions, never arguing for change. Payoff evident in her closing commitment to continue.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk with vision questions and reflects and deepens each change statement, culminating in clear mobilization ('I'd like to keep working on this').
