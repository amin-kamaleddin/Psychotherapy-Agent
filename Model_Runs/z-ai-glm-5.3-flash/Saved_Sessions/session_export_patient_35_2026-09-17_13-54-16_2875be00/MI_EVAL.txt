# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance even when Elena was defensive. However, repeatedly reinserting smoking items after pushback subtly signaled an agenda, preventing full unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections, but quickly pivoted to lists and practical advice. Elena's core emotional experiences — single-parenting stress, protectiveness, defensiveness — were only superficially acknowledged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed tied to the son's health concerns but lacked a coherent strategic thread toward the smoking target, addressing it indirectly and reactively via scripts rather than focused exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements (asking which option was helpful, offering role-play), but the power balance tilted toward advisor, with Elena reacting to pre-formed content rather than co-creating it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and built choice into the doctor script, with low-burden framing that earned explicit client gratitude. Stronger if ownership had been affirmed more explicitly and the smoking agenda paused when it felt like too much.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine change talk emerged, but largely because Elena volunteered it rather than being elicited. Motivation was often imported via advice and scripts instead of drawn out through open questions about her values and hopes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client input was invited at points, but the interaction was largely advice-driven with checklists and scripts. Elena's own expertise about her life was never explicitly elicited or affirmed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged naturally and low-burden framing made change feel accessible. But opportunities to deliberately deepen it were missed — her most powerful statement ('my son's worth more than my breaks') received a generic next-steps reply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with or reinforced, but repeatedly re-raising smoking after Elena pushed back re-triggered her resistance each time. A more skilled approach would have followed her lead or used double-sided reflections.
