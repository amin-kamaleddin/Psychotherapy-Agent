# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and respect with no judgment, even when Diego declines abstinence. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and missed chances to individualize (e.g., Diego's honesty and planning insight).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but the session was dominated by information delivery rather than deep listening. Key emotional moments (nervousness about Marcus, the 'weirdly lighter' relief) went largely unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on harm reduction with a concrete sequenced agenda: safety plan, Marcus rehearsal, solo-use precautions, and timed next steps. Every turn advanced actionable planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration occurred (co-created plan, roleplay, choices offered), but content was largely clinician-authored. Diego's substantial self-knowledge could have been drawn on more to generate plan content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Diego's ownership, accepting his refusal of MAT, counseling, and abstinence without pressure. The plan was explicitly adapted to fit his actual life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and the roleplay elicited strong commitment language, but much of the session involved telling rather than drawing out. Diego's personal reasons and emotional meaning were left unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and choice invitations were present, but the session leaned expert-driven with lengthy instructions and resource lists. Diego largely selected from counselor-generated options rather than shaping the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation and consolidation of commitment language through scaling, doable-step questions, and the Marcus roleplay, yielding '10 out of 10' and scheduled actions. Relied more on structured planning than on deepening Diego's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged without argument, and the plan was adapted to Diego's refusal to quit. Supplying extensive MAT/counseling options invited additional deflection ('maybe down the line').
