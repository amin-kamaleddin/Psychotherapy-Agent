# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with explicit affirmations of Maria's courage and efforts. However, warmth felt somewhat formulaic and shame/ambivalence were not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were often brief and generic (repeated 'I hear...'), bypassing deeper emotional content like shame about asking her sister, feeling like a burden, and grief over the missed birthday. Few complex reflections captured meaning beyond Maria's own words.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on suicidal ideation and safety throughout; every exchange built incrementally from risk assessment to means restriction to crisis planning, with measurable risk reduction from 9 to 3-4.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was largely a joint effort, with Maria choosing text wording and plan steps. However, the counselor's expert role dominated risk assessment and several options were offered as pre-packaged menus rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actions consistently framed as Maria's choice, and she exercised genuine choice (declining the neighbor option, writing down 988 on her own terms). Frequency of safety directives and 'Would you consider...?' phrasing created some implicit pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully drew out the niece as a protective motivation and used scaling questions effectively, but relied heavily on closed, multiple-choice safety prompts. Rarely explored Maria's own vision of a life worth living or what ending her suffering would look like.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria was repeatedly positioned as decision-maker and her contributions materially shaped the session. Falls short due to the counselor heavily driving the risk-assessment agenda and offering pre-packaged scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully surfaced and reinforced nascent change talk — the hesitation question elicited the niece, and protective statements were affirmed. Missed opportunities to deepen the talk, such as exploring what would make the 'small part' bigger.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying Maria's hopelessness, pivoting instead to protective actions and reasons for living. Redirections sometimes felt abrupt, absorbing pain statements with quick pivots rather than double-sided reflections.
