# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with anxiety normalized and avoidance validated without labeling. However, the warmth occasionally feels formulaic, and there is no explicit affirmation of Isabella's courage in committing despite fear.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on, but reflections are sparse and quickly pivot to advice or questions. Missed opportunities to sit with deeper feelings like fear of freezing, shame, and her tight chest during roleplay.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on the target behavior with every exchange building progressively toward a concrete micro-goal with time, script, checklist, and accountability. This is the session's strongest domain.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative phrasing is used, but the plan's architecture (grounding breath, scripts, ratings, checklist, pre-commitment) comes entirely from the counselor. Isabella acts mainly as an agreeable recipient rather than shaping the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated offers of choice and permission with no pressure, and non-attendance is framed nonjudgmentally. However, options are mostly binary choices between counselor-generated alternatives rather than open invitations for Isabella to shape her own path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Isabella's own reasons, values, and hopes are never explored; motivation is manufactured through external scaffolding like scripts, rewards, and accountability. Her commitment is compliance-shaped ('I'd feel bad if I didn't do it') rather than internally evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-style questions signal some power sharing, but the content is strongly expert-driven with Isabella choosing among counselor-generated options. Her expertise about her own patterns is acknowledged but not used to co-design the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Concrete behavioral commitments were secured (walking past Thursday at 10, alarm, script practice, texting Dana), but driven largely by the counselor's structured plan. Isabella's own change talk remains vague, and her motivations and values are not amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and fear-based avoidance is occasionally reframed, but the counselor does not use double-sided reflections to pair sustain talk with change talk. The 'I'll probably fail' language goes largely unaddressed rather than actively softened.
