# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental stance throughout, affirming Matteo's own ideas and honoring his casual style. Occasional affirmations felt slightly effusive/formulaic, and normalization of setbacks came only at the end.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, consistent reflective listening, including complex reflections capturing meaning and emotion ('Something about that night stuck with you'). Matteo repeatedly confirmed accuracy by building on the reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on alcohol use linked to the presenting injury, moving progressively from ambivalence to concrete steps and follow-up planning. The session never drifted, even through Matteo's humor.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal interaction with 'we' language, active solicitation of Matteo's solutions, and co-designed plan and follow-up structure. Matteo visibly experienced the partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent affirmation of Matteo's ownership ('a plan that's completely yours') and validation of his reluctance to over-commit. No advice or pressure imposed; choice was woven into every plan element.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client — his reasons, strategies, and confidence — with no lecturing or arguing for change. Even risk factors like exam stress were self-identified after open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Matteo explicitly positioned as the expert ('your limits far better than any screening form'), with every plan element originating from him. The counselor asked before structuring at each step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation and deepening of change talk through open questions that elicited strong commitments ('I'd rather figure out my limits now than at 30'). Matteo's self-generated strategies were consistently reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled via double-sided reflections tilted toward change rather than argument. Some early reflections lightly restated sustain themes instead of pivoting away more quickly.
