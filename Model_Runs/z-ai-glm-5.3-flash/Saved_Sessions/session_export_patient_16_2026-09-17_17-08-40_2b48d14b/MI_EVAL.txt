# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, normalizing Anika's annoyance at the referral. However, affirmations were somewhat formulaic and missed opportunities to affirm her honesty and self-awareness about ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated consistent reflective listening, accurately capturing Anika's core motivation around basketball and her protective goals. Reflections occasionally pivoted too quickly to questions rather than deepening the emotional layer of her fears and resentment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent, consistent focus on the vaping behavior tied to Anika's basketball motivation, with every exchange moving the plan forward. Even when starting open-endedly, the clinician skillfully connected her interests to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a partner throughout, co-creating plans and inviting Anika's input on decisions. Collaboration was somewhat uneven when the clinician supplied pre-packaged solutions (scripted one-liners, coping menus) instead of joint brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Anika's ownership, letting her drive every decision—refusal line, timing, coping strategy, and check-in format—with no pressure or parental tone despite her being a minor with a mandated referral.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew motivation from Anika through scaling questions, rehearsal, and anchoring change to her values. Relied heavily on rating scales and option menus without evoking deeper personal meaning beyond this season.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Anika as the expert, with her choices genuinely shaping the session's outcomes. Occasionally supplied menus of solutions rather than drawing ideas primarily from her, shifting some problem-solving expertise back to the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk using confidence scaling and value anchoring, with Anika producing strong commitment language. Evocation was somewhat procedural rather than exploring her deeper personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, instead reflecting protective motivations and redirecting toward basketball goals. Missed opportunities for active reframing via double-sided reflections, and the post-season 'I can go back to whatever after' comment went unaddressed.
