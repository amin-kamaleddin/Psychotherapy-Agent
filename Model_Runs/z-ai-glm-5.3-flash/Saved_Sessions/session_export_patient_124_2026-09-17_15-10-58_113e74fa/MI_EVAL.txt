# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Establishes a warm, nonjudgmental tone and maintains respect throughout, even when Sofia minimizes her use. Affirmations are positive but brief and formulaic; deeper individualization would reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is generally accurate, capturing Sofia's ambivalence about cost, legal risk, and effectiveness. Reflections stay at surface content at times, missing deeper emotional layers like family pressure and fear of making things 'real' with Maya.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains unwavering focus on marijuana use and cutting back from opening to close. Every exchange advances the target behavior, building progressively toward a concrete, time-bound follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong joint problem-solving with permission-seeking before offering ideas and incorporation of Sofia's edits. At times presents fairly complete option lists rather than drawing ideas out first, giving menus a mild expert flavor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces ownership and choice through flexible options, permission-seeking, and accepting Sofia's rejection of rigid rules. No pressuring or controlling language; Sofia clearly perceives the plan as her own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently and effectively draws out Sofia's own reasons (money, self-knowledge) and her own solutions (delay, walking, Maya). Deeper intrinsic motivators like family expectations and future goals were never explicitly explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Sofia's expertise as central, honoring her edits to the Maya text and her preference for starting next session with positives. Falls short of 5 because proposing menus and drafts early slightly directs the process before exploring her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and develops change talk through open questions, confidence scaling, and structured experiments, eliciting sustained commitment language. Sofia's confidence rose from 5 to 7 and she articulated her own concrete plan — exemplary MI practice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk (anxiety, peer belonging, fear of failure) without amplifying it, pivoting with affirming reframes. Lacks double-sided reflections linking sustain talk to Sofia's values (family expectations, money goals) to more actively soften ambivalence.
