# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with consistent affirmations of small wins. However, affirmations feel somewhat formulaic and occasionally rushed rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but they are sparse relative to questions and proposals. Deeper emotional content — shame of avoidance, fear of not knowing what to say — was missed as the session moved quickly to action planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the craft group with concrete micro-steps and no topic drift. Momentum was partly achieved through a slightly pushy agenda at the expense of client-paced exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration via permission-seeking and option menus, but the counselor supplied nearly all ideas. Interaction resembled guided task assignment more than equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong repeated affirmations of choice and honoring of the client's limits. Persistent offering of 'one more optional step' created mild pressure, leaving autonomy feeling somewhat constrained.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to elicit the client's own motivations, values, or reasons for change; the counselor supplied rationale and techniques instead. This is the weakest MI domain in the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices are offered, but the overall stance is expert-driven with the counselor designing tasks, scripts, and rewards. Little exploration of the client's own ideas, so expertise is not located within the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk emerged from effective micro-steps lowering barriers. However, the counselor did not deliberately evoke or deepen it — the change talk arose from task completion rather than skillful evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly and the counselor pivots toward choice and small steps, appropriately normalizing avoidance. Softening relies mostly on moving to tasks rather than strategic double-sided reflections.
