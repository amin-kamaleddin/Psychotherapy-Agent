# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing the screening and reframing Sofia's self-criticism. Falls short of 5 because warmth is brief and deeper affirmations of Sofia's qualities are missed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; responses tend to be thanks plus a new question or a list of tips. Sofia's ambivalence and discomfort (e.g., 'everyone does,' 'practicing saying no feels weird') were glossed over rather than reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session eventually converges on alcohol and party safety, but the focus emerged indirectly from Sofia's disclosure rather than counselor guidance. The goal is never explicitly named or tied back to the screening, and there is no closing summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing through permission-seeking and honoring Sofia's scenario choice, but the counselor supplies nearly all content, leaving Sofia in a compliant student role. Her existing strategies (buddy system, eating first) were not integrated into a joint plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and asks permission before offering advice or role-play. Stops short of 5 because the approach is advice-heavy and Sofia's ownership of decisions about her drinking itself is never reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Motivation is treated as residing in the counselor's advice; Sofia's own values, concerns, and reasons about alcohol are never explored. Change-relevant language comes from counselor-provided scripts, not evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and choice-offering show some power-sharing, but the session is largely counselor-directed with Sofia complying. Her existing knowledge and strategies were not built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No effort to evoke Sofia's own motivations or desire for change; the strategy is psychoeducation and role-play. Sofia never articulates a personal reason to cut back or change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('everyone does,' 'it's not a big deal') is neither reinforced nor strategically softened — it is simply sidestepped. No double-sided reflections or affirmations were used to pivot toward her own concerns.
