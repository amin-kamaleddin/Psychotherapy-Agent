# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, normalizing grief and validating Sofia's awkwardness and courage. However, warmth feels somewhat formulaic and template-like rather than individually expressed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but brief and often repeated verbatim, rarely lingering on emotional depth. Deeper feelings—fear of harm already done, grief over lost identity, shame about the referral—are left unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains unwavering focus on abstaining from alcohol during pregnancy with concrete proximal goals (Saturday party, telling Jes). Sofia leaves with a clearly defined behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Joint rehearsal and incorporation of Sofia's ideas show collaborative elements, but the counselor's turns are dense with expert-delivered checklists while Sofia mainly selects and reacts. Sofia's key ideas emerge spontaneously rather than being drawn out.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice ('pick one that feels most you') and never pressures Sofia; her own choices structure the plan. However, autonomy operates within counselor-generated menus rather than open-ended ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence ruler effectively tracks Sofia's growing motivation, but most reasons, scripts, and strategies are supplied by the counselor rather than elicited. Open questions like 'What makes this matter to you?' or 'Why now?' are absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing through choice questions, but the dominant pattern positions the counselor as expert providing menus of options. Sofia's own contributions shape the plan but are rarely explicitly invited or named.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence ruler, repeated check-ins, and rehearsal effectively evoke change talk, with confidence escalating from 5–6 to 9 and explicit commitment statements. Limited by reliance on the same confidence-scale question rather than varied evocative strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk by reframing toward strengths and action steps (e.g., walking away as 'smart, self-protective'). Missing double-sided reflections that would fully join the ambivalence before pivoting.
