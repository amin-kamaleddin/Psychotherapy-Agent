# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with a court-mandated client, including strong affirmations ('You didn't choose to be here, and yet you're showing up anyway'). Warmth is somewhat tied to change talk rather than positive regard for Julian himself.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections that captured avoidant ambivalence and elicited deeper disclosure. Reflections occasionally served evocation over understanding, and emotional content ('I feel like crap') was affirmed but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear directional thread from diffuse anxiety to a specific, sequenced plan without feeling coercive. Target behavior focus never drifted.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine two-way building of the plan, with the final shelter exchange inviting Julian's preferences. The psychiatrist still led pacing and pivots somewhat firmly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and ownership, accepting Julian's refusal to promise without pushing. Autonomy support was implicit — the plan could have been more explicitly framed as his to adjust or discard.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation entirely from Julian — his own fears, relief, insights, and solutions — with no arguing, lecturing, or supplied reasons for change. Motivation visibly grew within the client over the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Julian as the expert ('You know your week better than I do'), and every plan element originated with him. A model demonstration that expertise resides within the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation and reinforcement, moving Julian from vague ('I guess I could call her') to specific commitments (call tomorrow, two questions, sign up within the week). The psychiatrist deliberately mirrored and deepened Julian's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without amplifying, using double-sided reflections and reframes effectively. Some doubt language (e.g., 'I'm not gonna sit here and promise') was met with affirmation rather than gentle pivoting.
