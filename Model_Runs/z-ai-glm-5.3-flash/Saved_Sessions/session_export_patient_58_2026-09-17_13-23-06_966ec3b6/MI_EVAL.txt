# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with affirmations, though these tend to be generic rather than specific to Jamal's individual efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are sparse and mostly serve as bridges to advice; the central autonomy-vs-compliance tension ('my mom would like that at least') goes unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor keeps consistent focus on punctuality and the morning routine with no drift off-topic, though the focus feels somewhat premature given Jamal's preparation-stage ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session is largely expert-driven, with the counselor supplying problem analysis, solutions, checklists, and scripts while Jamal's contributions shape the plan only lightly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice is offered ('Which part feels most helpful?'), but the overall tone is directive with prescriptive language and menus limited to counselor-generated options, missing chances to reinforce Jamal's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Questions are mostly logistical, and Jamal's own motivations about proving himself and acting 'for me' are passed over without exploration or development.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumes the expert role with prescriptive advice lists and even a scripted message to mom, overriding Jamal's bid for autonomy when he says he can tell her himself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jamal volunteers meaningful change talk ('I wanna fix it,' 'that's on me'), but the counselor rarely deepens it, skipping open questions about desire, ability, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged neutrally without reinforcement, but no double-sided reflections are used to link it to change talk, and obstacle questions may invite sustain-oriented focus.
