# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, affirming Maria's autonomy and self-management efforts despite defensiveness. The client's closing remark ('you didn't treat me like I was crazy') directly confirms she experienced unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Maria's perspective (e.g., frustration, steroid concerns, desire for control). Not a 5 because reflections stayed at surface content, missing deeper emotions like her fear of being dismissed or future health worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained consistent focus on asthma management and inhaler engagement from start to finish, with no topic drift. Ended with a concrete, measurable plan for Thursday's appointment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong two-way exchange with permission-seeking and menu-based options; the script reflected Maria's own words. Fell short due to didactic informational blocks and insufficient incorporation of Maria's own research as a genuine knowledge source.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with genuine choices at every decision point and no pressure toward daily steroids. Client explicitly confirmed full choice ('you just gave me the facts and let me decide').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Motivation emerged incidentally rather than through deliberate evocation; no strategic open questions or importance/confidence scaling were used. The counselor rarely drew out Maria's own reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Maria as the decision-maker, confirmed by her comment that the script sounded like her own words. Occasionally supplied expert content didactically and didn't explicitly acknowledge her expertise about her own condition.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged (e.g., 'probably isn't great either,' 'that I could do. Maybe') but was client-generated without deliberate evocation. The counselor missed opportunities to strategically reflect, amplify, or deepen these self-motivating statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argued with or reinforced Maria's sustain talk, consistently pivoting toward agency and options. Not a 5 because responses sometimes foregrounded problem indicators without pivoting toward discrepancy, and no double-sided reflections were used to resolve ambivalence.
