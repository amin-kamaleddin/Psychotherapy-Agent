# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout; no judgment despite minimization ("I'm not an alcoholic"). Repeated affirmations of client self-knowledge ("you know yourself best," "you know your history best") and respect for her choice not to pursue abstinence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening. Captured ambivalence ("both a comfort and a source of concern") and emotional core ("'I don't want to bother anyone' feels heavier than the bottle"). Client responses ("that hits hard," "I hear myself") confirm she felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on alcohol reduction; clear arc from ambivalence exploration through vulnerable-moment identification to a concrete first-week plan (dinner with Dani, wine removed, Saturday gym class).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Fully egalitarian — asks rather than tells; plan emerged organically from client's own words. Comfortable, mutual dynamic with shared humor ("that's sneaky, making me pick").

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Client's pace and choices actively honored ("what pace feels right to you," "what feels like the right first step"). No advice imposed; all strategies self-generated. Respected her stated limit against abstinence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within via open questions ("which one feels most doable"). Client articulated intrinsic motivations unprompted — pride, self-trust, dating sober — and even resolved her own worry about bothering Dani. Exemplary evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Client consistently positioned as the expert; all plan ideas originated from her. Counselor's contributions served to mirror and organize her own wisdom — exemplary client-centered power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation and deepening throughout. "What would it feel like if this month worked?" elicited vivid desired-outcome talk; closing question deepened commitment talk ("I want to be someone who follows through"). Change talk grew progressively stronger each turn.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, responding with neutral/double-sided reflections ("that's designing a life you actually want"). However, some sustain talk (weekend drinking fears, "I'm not an alcoholic") passed without strategic double-sided reflection or gentle pivoting — acceptable, but limits this from a 5.

---

**Overall Global Ratings Score:** 4.9/5

**Summary:** Exceptionally strong MI session demonstrating masterful reflective listening, progressively deepened change talk, and a co-constructed, self-generated action plan fully honoring client autonomy. Client moved from ambivalence to specific commitments within a short exchange. Growth area: strategic softening of sustain talk.
