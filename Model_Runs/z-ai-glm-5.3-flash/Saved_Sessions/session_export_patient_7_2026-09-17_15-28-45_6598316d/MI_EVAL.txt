# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and non-judgment despite Amina's dismissiveness, though affirmations were light and somewhat formulaic ('That makes sense'). Missed chances to more specifically and warmly affirm Amina's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective efforts were generally accurate, capturing Amina's autonomy frustration and music as coping. However, deeper emotional disclosures ('when everything's just... done') were passed over rather than gently explored, and some reflections were repetitive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session drifted entirely into music and playlist-building with no bridge back to the referral concerns. Rapport-building was legitimate, but the session ended without a therapeutic foothold toward the focus area.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina's input materially shaped the session, including co-constructing the playlist with her additions. Initial agenda and ruler questions were counselor-driven and rejected, and some choice menus felt scripted.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest domain — autonomy was explicitly and repeatedly reinforced with genuine menus, honoring refusals without pressure ('You're in control,' 'Your call,' 'no pressure'). Also validated the legitimacy of her anger at imposed decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine evocative effort drew out meaningful material about music as escape, but these glimpses of meaning were passed over for playlist logistics. Amina's own words about what matters to her were rarely deepened, leaving evocation superficial.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina was consistently positioned as the expert, and her choices shaped the session from refusal to co-building a playlist. Early agenda-setting felt imposed, and playlist suggestions were counselor-generated before her input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk was minimal — the rulers were rejected and not deepened, and the session never bridged toward Amina's own reasons for change. Understandable for a first engagement session, but by MI criteria cultivation was weak.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor reflected rather than argued when Amina expressed resistance, respected boundaries, and reframed the '2' as making sense. Lacked strategic double-sided reflections linking Amina's values toward possible change directions.
