# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations like 'you were actually looking out for yourself.' Score falls short of 5 as some affirmations feel formulaic and unconditional regard for his defending heavy drinking is not fully articulated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening capturing both sides of ambivalence and nuanced emotional costs. The empathic reframe of Ryan's safety intentions visibly moved the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, progressively narrowing focus from the incident to concrete strategies. However, no summary or explicit next step—closes with the plan implicit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Work built on Ryan's contributions with strategies from his own brainstorming. Occasionally leading (the 'him winning' framing) and no periodic agenda check-ins.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors client ownership, asking what strategies he could 'actually own' without forcing anything. No advice or pressure toward abstinence or labels; the plan is entirely Ryan's.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Ryan's own reasons, past successes, and values, producing genuine change talk and insight. Relies at times on counselor-supplied frames and misses deepening tools like importance/confidence scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Ryan as the expert, and his ideas shape the plan. Falls short of 5 for not explicitly naming his expertise or collaboratively defining the target behavior.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled evocative questions drew out commitments ('I could see myself actually doing those') and amplified discrepancies. Lacks confidence/importance exploration and deeper commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reframes rather than debates disclaimers ('drinking smarter isn't about having a problem'). Several sustain statements pass without gentle redirection toward discrepancy.
