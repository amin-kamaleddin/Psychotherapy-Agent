# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of effort and sensitive handling of the safety check, though warmth occasionally feels procedural and affirmation of her worth independent of behaviors is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections are accurate, but empathy thins as the session progresses, with emotional disclosures (skepticism about the balanced statement, fear of her mom's lectures) met with checklists and plans rather than reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong focus on target behaviors with appropriate pivots from mood to cognitive work to a concrete plan, with only minor drift into extended medical education.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The work is consistently framed as joint, with Liyana actively shaping the plan (notebook over app, weekly check-ins), though long unidirectional information blocks weaken collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are repeatedly offered and refusals (self-compassion, hiding the scale) are accepted without pushback, though some advice delivery is dense and prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions elicit genuine client insights (e.g., her realization about nighttime snacking), but motivation is evoked inconsistently with no values exploration, confidence rulers, or deliberate development of her reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Options are consistently presented for Liyana to choose and her refusals are respected, though some lecture-style information delivery slips toward an expert-driven tone.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges and is supported through choices and preference elicitation, but commitment language is never deliberately deepened (no evocative questions or confidence scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('part of me doesn't buy it,' reluctance about weekly weighing) is acknowledged but not strategically softened with double-sided reflections or exploration of ambivalence; positively, it is never reinforced.
