# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations like 'good trial mindset' and validation of worries without judgment. Ambivalence was accepted rather than pathologized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided reflections captured ambivalence well, though reflections sometimes served as brief pivots to questions rather than deepening emotional content. Her comment about 'admitting it's a real problem' went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus from the opening question through a concrete behavioral plan (DND at 11pm, phone on fridge, playlist substitution, Monday check-in). No meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was genuinely co-constructed, with the plan refined iteratively from client input (fridge location, water strategy, Saturday start). The counselor never asserted expert authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were consistently returned to the client with options offered rather than advice imposed. Her 'no promises' was accepted without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within via rulers and open questions, producing genuine client commitment language. No lectures or persuasion, as the client explicitly noted.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently positioned the client as the expert, and client-generated ideas (fridge location, roommate line, accepting slips) were adopted directly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic use of importance/confidence rulers with measurable movement (4 to 8, named by the client). Commitment language emerged organically from skillful evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and redirected toward agency (e.g., 'accept a slip and restart'), but some responses briefly dwelled on barriers without double-sided reflections to pivot toward change talk.
