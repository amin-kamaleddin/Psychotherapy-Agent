# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout; Jordan's pessimism and grumbling are accepted without judgment or pressure, and he is affirmed for effort regardless of outcome.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captures facts and feelings (e.g., the layoff, the quiet wish beneath hopelessness). Occasionally pivots to hope before fully sitting with the depth of Jordan's despair and fear of rejection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressively narrowing focus from global hopelessness to a concrete plan (tomorrow, coffee, twenty minutes). The closing reframe keeps direction on controllable behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian; the counselor asks permission to problem-solve and builds the plan from Jordan's own ideas, culminating in co-constructed commitment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Jordan's ownership is repeatedly affirmed ('it's something, and it's yours'), with genuine choice offered and his self-generated plan accepted without modification.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within Jordan via evocative questions surfacing his values (breathing room, catching up on bills) and ignored resilience, ending with his own meaning-making.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary power-sharing: Jordan is positioned as the expert, and all key decisions — the job, timing, method, and accountability — originate from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk, from the quiet wish to commitment language ('one application this week') and a concrete, self-generated action plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without amplification and paired effectively with the wish via double-sided reflections. Occasional brief dwell time in helplessness before redirecting slightly limits the score.
