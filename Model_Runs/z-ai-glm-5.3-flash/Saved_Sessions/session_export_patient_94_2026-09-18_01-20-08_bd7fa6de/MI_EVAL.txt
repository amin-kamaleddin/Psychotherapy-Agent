# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; Maria's skepticism, frustration, and resistance were all validated as reasonable self-advocacy, with no judgment or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, accurate complex reflections captured both content and underlying emotion and deepened as the session progressed, never parroting and consistently confirmed by the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on medication adherence with strategic momentum, building from barriers to a concrete plan and rehearsal, without ever becoming directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; every substantive step (plan, scripts, next steps) was elicited from Maria rather than proposed, with the session direction visibly shaped by her answers.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit, consistent reinforcement of Maria's ownership; her hesitancy ('I can't promise anything today') was protected rather than challenged, and her self-generated plan was followed without clinician agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within — questions like 'what would it mean for your life...' elicited Maria's own reasons, which she recognized as more powerful than any external pressure.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Maria as the expert and decision-maker; the entire plan originated from her, with the psychiatrist reflecting, affirming, and asking evocative questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation of DARN-to-CAT progression, explicitly naming and reinforcing change talk, anchoring motivation to her values (playing tag with her daughter), and consolidating commitment through closing questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well with double-sided reflections and effective pivots to motivation, but substantial early session time was spent elaborating the content of her fears, keeping the spotlight on sustain talk longer than optimal.
