# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and warmth, even when the client expressed ambivalence about her drinking goals. Judgment-free acceptance was maintained throughout, including full acceptance of her partial goal of two drinks per night.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captured the client's values and ambivalence accurately. However, reflections were somewhat formulaic and repetitive, and the emotional core (low mood, self-criticism after slips) was acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a clear focus on reducing alcohol use from the first turn, guiding the session progressively from assessment through rulers, goal-setting, if-then planning, slip planning, and follow-up. Each turn advanced the target behavior without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative, with plan options offered for the client to select or modify and suggestions framed tentatively. Both partners contributed knowledge, with no expert-driven directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was repeatedly and explicitly reinforced, with advice offered as optional options to select or tweak. The counselor accepted the client's rejections (tea, texting) and partial goal without pressure, and her closing statement that the plan was 'mine' evidenced successful autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance and confidence rulers and open questions drew out the client's own reasons, solutions, and self-motivating statements. Deducted for limited deliberate development of deeper values (e.g., the depression-drinking link) and occasionally moving to planning too quickly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client was consistently treated as the expert on her own life, with all plans originating from her choices and modifications. Her contributions visibly shaped the session direction, which is the hallmark of strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions effectively evoked strong change talk, including self-generated strategies and commitment to first steps. Deducted because some change statements (e.g., 'it's a start, and it's mine') passed without explicit affirmation or deepening.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, reflecting the client's realistic goal without arguing when she rejected the seven-drink guideline. Minor deduction for occasionally restating ambivalence without actively pivoting toward change.
