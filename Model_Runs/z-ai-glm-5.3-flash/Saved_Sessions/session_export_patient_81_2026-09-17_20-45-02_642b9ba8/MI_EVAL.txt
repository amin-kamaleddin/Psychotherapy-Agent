# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, reframing shame as values-alignment and honesty. No judgment even when the patient minimizes or predicts relapse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections capturing emotional and identity-level meaning, including a powerful double-sided reflection and catching the pivotal 'alone, sometimes' shift.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on meth use and consequences, with each exchange building toward pattern recognition, exceptions, and a concrete change plan. No aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Full equal partnership — the counselor seeks the patient's ideas before offering anything, and every plan element originates with the patient. Patient confirms feeling genuinely asked, not told.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership ('your way, not mine', 'for you, not for them'), with no coercive or prescriptive language. Patient explicitly notes no one has asked him these questions before.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client via values questions, exception-finding, and future-self elicitation. The counselor offered no advice — only questions and reflections that elicited the full change plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located with the client, and the closing plan is affirmed as genuinely his. Patient contributions entirely shaped the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of desire, ability, and commitment talk ('that's not who I want to be', 'So I know I can do it', 'I'd quite like to keep him around'), repeatedly amplified and reflected back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally pivots away from sustain talk, e.g., acknowledging doubt then using an exception question. A few sustain statements ('I'm not saying I'd never touch anything again') were reflected neutrally without active reframing.
