# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance, affirming Sofia's maternal instinct and directly addressing her fear of being lectured ('you didn't mess up beyond repair'). Sofia's explicit gratitude — 'Thank you for not making me feel like a screw-up' — confirms unconditional positive regard was conveyed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate throughout, capturing Sofia's ambivalence ('the pull to stay connected and your concern about the baby') and nuanced social fears with double-sided reflections that landed and were built upon by Sofia.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session kept a clear, consistent focus on alcohol abstinence during pregnancy and its social barriers, with every exchange advancing the target behavior toward a concrete, timed commitment ('What feels like a doable first step?').

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was between equals — the psychiatrist asked rather than told, let Sofia develop the script herself, and adjusted the follow-up plan to her request. The closing summary credited Sofia with building the plan herself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership were consistently reinforced ('what feels like a doable first step for you,' 'you can adjust it if it doesn't go how you hope'), with options offered rather than directives given. Only a light suggestion framing around telling Dani first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation was elicited from Sofia herself — her maternal identity, her script, her own commitment — never supplied by the counselor. Questions like 'What would staying in your spot look like if it didn't require a drink in your hand?' skillfully evoked possibility language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located within the client ('You know your friends better than I ever could'), and Sofia's contributions — choosing Dani, her script, the follow-up request — shaped the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully flagged and amplified, e.g., 'Did you hear what you just said? 

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about social exclusion was acknowledged without amplification and pivoted through double-sided reflections. Slightly extended exploration of the fear of being left out kept focus on reasons against change a bit longer than ideal.
