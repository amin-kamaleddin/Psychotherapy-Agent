# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth and unconditional positive regard are maintained throughout, even in the face of heavy sustain talk. Praise is specific and genuine, with no judgment or disapproval of the client's pessimism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both sides of the client's ambivalence and the loneliness beneath his helplessness. Client confirms their depth, noting he'd 'never actually said some of that out loud before.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session moves clearly from vague hopelessness to a concrete, collaboratively defined plan for drafting the 'endured it' interview answer. Every exchange advances the target behavior with no aimless wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is genuinely two-way, with the client shaping the agenda, strategy, and logistics. The clinician repeatedly solicits the client's assessment and co-constructs the final commitment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is actively supported, including options for how to draft the plan, with no pressure or coercion. A 4 rather than 5 only because explicit affirmations of the client's ultimate ownership and control over pace are relatively sparse.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client through open questions about past resilience and what would feel true, with no arguing for change. The client arrives at key insights himself, the hallmark of skilled evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client's contributions visibly shape the session—he chooses the focus, generates the 'more airtime' strategy, and sets his own terms, all of which the clinician accepts. Expertise is explicitly located in the client ('that was your idea, not mine').

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is systematically evoked through vision questions, highlighting desire, and a readiness ruler. The client's language strengthens progressively from 'wish something would go right' to 'worth a shot' and 'that's worth something.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently met without invalidation and pivoted toward exceptions, such as reframing walking out as a smart, protective choice. The session ends with the client himself revising his 'can't get a break' narrative to 'maybe I stopped waiting for one.'
