# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations and normalization of the client's social attachment to smoking. However, affirmations feel somewhat formulaic and lack deep, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured Elise's core ambivalence about connection versus health. Reflections leaned practical rather than emotional, leaving deeper fears (being forgotten, panicking mid-craving) unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk emerged via the readiness ruler and experiment framing, but pivotal statements like 'this doesn't feel impossible anymore' went unreflected and unaffirmed. Few elaboration questions were used to deepen the client's own motivations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without arguing, but there were few strategic attempts to soften it, such as double-sided reflections linking social benefits to her own concerns. It was largely sidestepped rather than actively rolled with.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elise was positioned as the expert, choosing the script, timing, and pacing, with the plan genuinely shaped by her input. Some turns were advice-dense and preset rather than fully co-constructed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some of Elise's motivations were drawn out through scaling and pros/cons, but the counselor frequently supplied reasons and coping strategies instead of eliciting her own ideas. Intrinsic values and confidence were rarely evoked through open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and client choice were consistent, with options framed as Elise's decisions and her pacing explicitly affirmed. Rapid-fire suggestion menus occasionally subtly steered toward the counselor's preferred experiments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was evoked inconsistently and capitalized on unevenly, with key statements going unreflected. More elaboration questions about successes and what made the experiment feel possible would have deepened it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** No reinforcement of sustain talk occurred, but redirection happened mainly through action-planning rather than skillful verbal pivots. Double-sided reflections pairing social benefits with health concerns were absent.
