# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with genuine affirmations, but warmth occasionally feels formulaic and the identity/community dimensions of Lukas's use go unacknowledged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that captures ambivalence well, including attuned responses to shame. However, reflections are brief and the psychiatrist sometimes rushes past deeper emotional material like identity loss and loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus — every exchange builds toward concrete targets (Sunday budget, Monday flatmate conversation, Mia accountability), closing with a specific follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way session: role-plays at client's request, incorporates client edits, and lets Lukas's own ideas drive the plan, producing solutions he describes as his own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-offering ('Which feels doable this week?', 'Send or change the tone?'), but some advice is directive in structure (prescribed scripts, budget categories), and Lukas's autonomy over the larger substance use decision goes unaffirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and evocative questions produced strong client-generated motivations and plans (importance 5, confidence 6→8). However, evocation stays narrowly on financial repair, leaving the client's deeper ambivalence about meth use and identity unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client expertise is repeatedly drawn upon and his contributions shape the session. Occasional drift into directive coaching mode (prescribing budget steps and script content) slightly undercuts the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance and confidence rulers yielded concrete change talk and self-generated solutions. Change talk is channeled narrowly toward the financial task, with little evocation of Lukas's own reasons for changing use itself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently avoided and redirected without argument, and Lukas's closing 'it's not all a problem' is neither contradicted nor reinforced. But sustain talk is sidestepped rather than strategically softened with double-sided reflections.
