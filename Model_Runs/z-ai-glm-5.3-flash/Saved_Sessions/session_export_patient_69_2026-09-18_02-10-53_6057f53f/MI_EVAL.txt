# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with a resistant, mandated client; frustration validated without pressure. Affirmations are somewhat sparse and intellectualized, with little direct affirmation of Kieran as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and reaches meaning-level depth (e.g., the frustration about not knowing what sessions lead to). Early reflections are occasionally formulaic and the client's emotional flatness is never explicitly reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus with genuine momentum from disengagement to a concrete step, timing, and obstacles. The early phase circles the monotony complaint, and the fence plan is never explicitly anchored back to the counseling/compliance context.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: the goals, plan, timing, and coping strategy all come from Kieran, with the counselor acting as a facilitative sounding board. No prescriptive agenda is imposed; client ideas demonstrably steer the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision point is returned to the client ('If you were in charge of these sessions...', 'when would feel like a good time?'), with explicit reinforcement of ownership. No advice or pressure is imposed, directly counteracting Kieran's hoop-jumping resentment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn out from within the client: his own reasons (usefulness), the self-generated fence idea, spontaneous commitment language, and his own obstacle plan. Each piece of change talk is developed through reflection and open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located with the client ('You're the expert on your own life'), and the session's entire direction comes from him. The final plan is entirely client-generated and built upon rather than redirected.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session's standout strength: skillful evocation of a goal, a concrete client-generated step, commitment language, obstacle planning, and projected benefit. Each change-talk utterance is reflected back and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Heavy sustain talk is consistently acknowledged briefly and pivoted away from, with effective reframes toward action. Some early reflections literally echo the status quo ('ticking boxes') before pivoting, without an immediate change-oriented counterweight.
