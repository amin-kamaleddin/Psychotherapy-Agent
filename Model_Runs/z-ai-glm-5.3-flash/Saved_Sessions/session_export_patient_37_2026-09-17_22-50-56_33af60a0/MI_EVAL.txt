### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with frequent, individualized affirmations that validate Elena's ambivalence without judgment. Patient explicitly reports not feeling lectured or judged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both surface content and underlying feelings. Elena's responses confirm reflections landed, with complex reflections often articulating her meaning before she fully states it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on the vaccination decision with progressive momentum, culminating in a concrete follow-up plan. No topic drift; even educational content stays anchored to Elena's own stated questions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is fully collaborative, with decisions like the two-week follow-up, written materials, and husband's involvement negotiated jointly. Elena's feedback ('a real choice instead of just pressure') confirms the equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively reinforced through genuine choices about session direction and timing, with no pressure to decide. Elena's lingering uncertainty is fully accepted and her ownership of the decision is preserved throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Elena's own values, criteria, and reasoning rather than imposed, with skillful elicitation and development of change talk. Nearly all persuasive content emerges from her own questions and conclusions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor consistently treats Elena as the expert, using her standards for trustworthy information to guide the session. Her contributions directly shape the follow-up agenda and session plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk, deliberately flagging commitment language and attributing shifts to Elena herself. She moves from guarded hesitancy to tentative commitment through her own reasoning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying sustain talk using double-sided reflections and redirects toward values. Some minimization was handled through information delivery rather than strategic reframing, and sustain talk was occasionally revisited at length before redirection.
