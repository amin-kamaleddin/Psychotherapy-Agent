# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and honored Lucía's terms (no speeches, no disappointed reactions). Affirmations were occasionally formulaic rather than personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Multiple accurate reflections captured smoking as a private break and her fear of failing in front of her kid. Deep emotional material (wanting it for her vs. for herself) was summarized but not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on smoking and built momentum toward a concrete, measurable plan. The pace occasionally outstripped readiness, which Lucía flagged ('Whoa, we're at plans already?').

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed from Lucía's own idea (coffee cart walk) with genuine negotiation ('Deal?'). Option menus sometimes felt rapid-fire and list-like.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently strong: 'You're in charge,' genuine choices at each juncture, explicit acceptance of her conditions (privacy, self-defined success), and no pressure despite the referral context.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and 'what would raise that number?' effectively elicited self-generated motivation and a client-designed experiment. The strongest motivator (her daughter) was evoked but left unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Lucía's expertise was treated as central and her own idea became the plan rather than an imposed one. Occasional quick moves to option menus and logistics slightly limited full client ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and follow-ups elicited meaningful change talk ('a 6 feels more than I've felt in a long time'). The key moment about her daughter was not deepened further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced and slips were reframed well ('information, not failure'). However, directly inviting discussion of smoking's benefits gave extended airtime to sustain talk, with little strategic double-sided reflection.
