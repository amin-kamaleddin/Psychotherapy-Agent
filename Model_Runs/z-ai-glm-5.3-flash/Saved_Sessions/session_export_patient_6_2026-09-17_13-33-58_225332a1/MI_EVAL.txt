# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with acknowledgment of the client's concerns, though affirmations of his strengths were limited and warmth somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional genuine reflections (e.g., linking the cop incident to soccer fears), but reflective listening was sparse and key disclosures like social fears and friendship worries were never reflected back.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on preventing repeat drinking incidents tied to soccer goals, with some drift from offering extra scripts and safety boilerplate.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices were offered at each step, but the pattern was largely counselor-as-provider, client-as-picker, with the client's own ideas only minimally incorporated into plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed next steps as the client's choice and answered confidentiality honestly, though options were counselor-generated and the client's judgment about what applied to him was under-affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely drew motivation from within the client; advice and checklists dominated, with almost no open questions exploring values, importance, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered choices that invited input, but the expert-driven style, step lists, and scripts positioned the counselor as the source of solutions rather than an equal partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged (scholarship goals, desire to avoid addiction) but was not systematically evoked or deepened; missed open-question opportunities and no scaling or commitment-strengthening techniques.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and was sometimes reframed toward goals, but met minimization with more information rather than double-sided reflections or strategic pivoting.
