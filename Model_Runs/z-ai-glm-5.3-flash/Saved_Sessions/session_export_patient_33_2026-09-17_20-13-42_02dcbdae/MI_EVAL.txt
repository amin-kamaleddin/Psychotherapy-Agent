### Acceptance

**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, affirming Diego's honesty and honoring his request that slips not end the relationship. Non-judgmental responses effectively dismantled defensiveness from the start.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections capturing both content and emotional meaning, including the core insight that heroin quiets the noise rather than chasing a high. Attunement to Diego's guardedness visibly deepened trust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on heroin use and harm reduction with each exchange building toward a concrete, client-owned plan. No topic drift despite emotionally rich territory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equals co-creating every step, with Diego's own ideas forming the substance of the plan. The final relapse-support agreement was a negotiated contract initiated by the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly and repeatedly reinforced ('this is your space,' 'on your terms'), with every plan element originating from Diego. No unsolicited advice or directives given.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within, with Diego producing the session's key insights himself ('keep something on the calendar'). Readiness and commitment language emerged organically.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Diego positioned as the expert, with his contributions demonstrably shaping the session agenda, relapse-prevention focus, and therapeutic contract. Expertise clearly resides with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and amplified through strategic questions, eliciting strong commitment statements and a concrete self-generated plan. Consolidated effectively by asking Diego how it felt to lay out his plan.

**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced or challenged directly, with smooth non-judgmental reframing honoring the function of use while pivoting toward change. Withheld from a 5 because Diego's high motivation meant strategic softening skills were largely untested.
