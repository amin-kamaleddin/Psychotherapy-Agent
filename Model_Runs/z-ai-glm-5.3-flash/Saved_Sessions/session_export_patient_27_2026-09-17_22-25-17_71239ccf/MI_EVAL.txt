**Comments:** Open questions skillfully draw out Daniela's own reasons for change ('being actually known,' 'less lonely,' 'having a backup'), which are then strategically reflected and amplified, culminating in strong commitment language ('I know what I need to do... I think I could. Soon-ish.').
