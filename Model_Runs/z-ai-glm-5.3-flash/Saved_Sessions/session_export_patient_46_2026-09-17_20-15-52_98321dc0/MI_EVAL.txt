### Direction

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within Ethan via evocative open questions, with his own values (soccer, Saturdays, self-respect) driving change. He spontaneously generated both the key insight and the plan, without any lecturing.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Skillfully evoked, highlighted, and reinforced change talk, from the first desire statement ('the mornings are rough') through commitment language ('I'll try it this weekend'). Quoting Ethan's own words back strengthened his momentum toward a concrete plan.
