# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard maintained throughout, even when the patient blames peers. Affirmations ('that sounds like real wisdom') and playful confidentiality reinforced unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured both surface content and underlying emotion (feeling trapped, lonely, burned). Patient directly confirmed feeling understood ('Finally someone gets it,' 'nobody's really just listened').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on school avoidance and peer disconnection, with a clear arc from venting toward a self-generated concrete first step. Direction was achieved without imposing an agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions rather than directives; the patient's own ideas shaped the entire plan. Relaxed, peer-like tone (including gentle humor) suited an adolescent wary of authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasis on 'your terms,' no advice or pressure, and no pushing when the patient said she wasn't promising anything. Patient's closing ownership statement ('that part's kinda on me') confirms internal locus of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every turn ended with an evocative open question eliciting the patient's own reasons and ideas. Her motivational progression from 'no winning' to commitment talk demonstrates textbook evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key ideas originated from the patient: her test for reconnection, the casual text, timing it to a school day. Her comment 'you didn't tell me to be different' directly evidences shared power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently evoked desire, ability, and commitment language ('I keep talking myself into it more'). Counselor explicitly reinforced that the reasons were her own.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated then pivoted effectively using double-sided reflections. Minor deduction for occasionally restating sustain-laden content, though functionally it mobilized dissatisfaction with the status quo.
