# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, validating Alexei's perspective and offering affirmations. Warmth was somewhat general rather than deeply individualized, preventing a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections captured both surface content and emotional core (trust, reputation, soccer). Occasionally leaned on paraphrase rather than exploring deeper emotional nuance like peer pressure and fear of disappointing his mother.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear, progressive focus from the alcohol incident through consequences, goals, skill-building, and rehearsed refusal practice. Each exchange moved deliberately toward the target behavior with no aimless drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely egalitarian interaction with Alexei's ideas shaping the session and a genuinely joint role-play. The skill menu was somewhat pre-packaged; more strategies co-generated by Alexei would strengthen the rating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice with explicit statements ('You're in control; I'll follow your lead') and repeated option menus. Alexei made every meaningful decision and the plan explicitly 'felt like me,' indicating genuine ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Alexei's own values, surfacing soccer/scholarship goals unprompted. Some evocation opportunities were replaced by counselor-supplied strategies, and no confidence/importance exploration occurred.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Alexei as the expert, and his choices visibly shaped the session (scenario, refusal line, comeback). A 5 would require more co-creation of the agenda and explicit acknowledgment of his expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited strong change talk tied to soccer goals and scholarship, plus commitment language after role-play ('I want to get this right'). Could have deepened further with confidence scaling or exploring what success looks like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, then pivoted toward what matters using a double-sided structure. The pivot was sometimes quick and mechanical; more strategic developing discrepancy (e.g., linking the cop near-miss to his goals) would earn a 5.
