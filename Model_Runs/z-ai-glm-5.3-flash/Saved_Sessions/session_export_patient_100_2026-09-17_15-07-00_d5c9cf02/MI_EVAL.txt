# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, offering a safe, nonjudgmental space and normalizing potential failure without criticism or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including an accurate complex reflection about weekend patterns affecting grades. However, reflections were sparse and the emotional layer beneath Maya's embarrassment went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Focus was strong but misaligned with the referral concern. The alcohol use, which Maya herself linked to her grade slide, was deferred and never revisited or bridged to her academic goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Maya shaping each step and editing the text to her mom. Rapid multi-question clusters occasionally tilted pacing toward the counselor's agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforced Maya's ownership through permission-seeking, option menus, and accepting her right to defer the partying topic without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions effectively drew out Maya's own motivation, with confidence rising from 6 to 8.5. Evocation centered entirely on academics, with no attempt to elicit change talk about alcohol use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maya's expertise was central to agenda-setting and plan design, including invitation to edit suggested text. Options were offered somewhat rapidly and the alcohol referral agenda was never explicitly negotiated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation through rulers and first-step questions produced progressively stronger change talk, which the counselor affirmed. No change talk was elicited regarding the alcohol use itself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor appropriately avoided arguing with Maya's sustain talk and followed her priorities. However, the ambivalence about drinking was never gently revisited with a double-sided reflection or permission-based question.
