# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, validating Maya's pushback and affirming her honesty and plan without any judgment despite discussing underage drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured Maya's core dilemmas around belonging and school. One point docked because reflections sometimes preceded deeper emotional exploration, and the fear of exclusion wasn't fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and its connection to Maya's values throughout. A premature push toward change strategies briefly exceeded the client's readiness, though the counselor recovered well.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was genuinely two-sided, with Maya's input redirecting the counselor multiple times and the final plan co-constructed. Docked one point because pre-packaged menus of options skewed slightly toward counselor-generated content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with repeated choices offered throughout and explicit acceptance when Maya rejected planning. Her ownership of the change was preserved at every step with no coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Maya, eliciting her own insights and self-motivating steps via the confidence ruler. Docked one point because the refusal-script menu was provided rather than evoked from Maya's own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Located expertise in Maya, letting her choose topics and redesign plans substantially. One point docked because the counselor's menus and suggestions leaned slightly toward expert content delivery, though always framed as optional.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent evocation of change talk via the confidence ruler, moving Maya from 'I'm not trying to quit' to designing her own safety plan and practicing refusal lines. These moments were consistently amplified without pushing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well by reflecting rather than countering Maya's reasons to keep drinking, and pivoting skillfully during her pushback. Docked one point because early introduction of refusal scripts assumed change readiness and invited some sustain talk.
