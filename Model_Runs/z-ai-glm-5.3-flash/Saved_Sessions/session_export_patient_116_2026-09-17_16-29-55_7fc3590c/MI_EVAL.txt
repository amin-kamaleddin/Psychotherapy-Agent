# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout; Samir felt accepted ('didn't lecture me like the others'). Warmth was somewhat formulaic and specific affirmations of his strengths were lacking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were generally accurate but formulaic and repetitive—Samir noted 'you've said it all twice now.' Emotional nuance behind his deflection (family pressure, resentment) was noted but never deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on medication adherence and established a concrete behavioral experiment (mint tin trial with follow-up). Mild drift into repetitive closing summaries slightly diluted the focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Final plan was genuinely co-created; Samir rejected unwanted suggestions and defined his own implementation. Early prescriptive 'shopping list' of solutions was experienced as being told what to do.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session's strongest suit—choice was repeatedly and explicitly reinforced ('it's your call,' 'you're fully in charge'), and when Samir insisted on doing it his way, the counselor immediately honored it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation remained external and counselor-driven; the counselor never probed for Samir's intrinsic reasons for adherence after his deflections. Change talk reflected only pressure ('so everyone gets off my back').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor consistently positioned Samir as the decision-maker and adapted to his choices. Falls short of a 5 due to unsolicited solutions offered before adequately exploring his perspective.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some commitment was elicited (trying the mint tin with specific locations), but it was driven by external motivation and the counselor settled for thin commitment without probing importance, confidence, or personal meaning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk was frequently mirrored back without double-sided reflections pivoting toward Samir's own reasons for change. Heavy premature advice-giving provoked resistance ('you've got a whole shopping list going').
