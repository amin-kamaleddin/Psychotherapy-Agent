# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with normalization of slips, but warmth is brief and formulaic; the counselor pivots to planning rather than fully accepting and exploring Rafael's discouragement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A few decent early reflections, but most turns shift quickly to directives and checklists, leaving Rafael's deeper emotional content about failure and skepticism unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on exercise throughout, progressing logically to a concrete action plan with specific times and built-in accountability with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The plan is largely counselor-generated—options, if-then scripts, times, and rewards—with Rafael merely approving a pre-built plan rather than engaging in genuine joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choices are offered and slips framed as acceptable, but the pace is highly directive, rushing commitment to times, rewards, and check-ins and telling Rafael what to think.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is assumed rather than evoked: almost no open-ended evocative questions, confidence exploration, or development of Rafael's own reasons and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role, supplying ideas and instructions, with little acknowledgment that Rafael holds expertise about what will work in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rafael's spontaneous change talk is captured and built upon, but there are no deliberate attempts to evoke or amplify it through open questions about reasons and hopes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and the 'next week' loop is briefly reframed, but doubt is largely bypassed via rapid pivots to planning rather than double-sided reflections or ambivalence work.
