# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Polite, nonjudgmental, and encouraging, but warmth is formulaic and paired with task lists rather than individualized affirmation. Positive regard is superficial, with little affirmation of Camila's feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; meaningful disclosures about worry, low energy, and hopefulness are rarely mirrored or validated. The one decent reflection normalizes rather than deeply understands her.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with a well-defined target behavior and concrete logistics. The only weakness is that direction is narrow and task-focused, with little exploration of values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely counselor-directed; the plan, strategies, and follow-up structure were all generated by the counselor. Camila's preferences are incorporated reactively rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission is asked once and minor choices are offered, but the plan is delivered as a pre-formed package with prescriptive tone. Autonomy is accommodated rather than actively fostered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Almost no effort is made to draw out Camila's own motivations, values, or confidence. The session functions as advice delivery, with motivation-talk coming almost entirely from her spontaneous remarks.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes an expert role, delivering a full prescriptive plan before asking what Camila wants or what has worked before. Her joke about the counselor 'doing the exercises' highlights the detached, instructional style.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Camila spontaneously offers change talk about willingness and hope, but the counselor does not evoke or deepen it. No open questions explore her reasons, confidence, or vision of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Camila voices sustain talk twice about missing days and letting tired days win, but the counselor never directly reflects or reframes it. The fallback plan only implicitly addresses her fear without processing her relapse history.
