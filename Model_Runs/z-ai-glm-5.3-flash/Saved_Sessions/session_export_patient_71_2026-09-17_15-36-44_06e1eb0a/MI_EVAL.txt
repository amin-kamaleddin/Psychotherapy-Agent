# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, normalizing lapses and difficulty. Affirmations are somewhat sparse and formulaic, and unconditional positive regard beyond the plan is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections accurately captured Laura's ambivalence, but reflective listening is limited and the session shifts quickly to strategies. Emotional statements like 'small feels more honest' were not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on consistent yoga practice from the start, with every exchange moving the concrete plan forward. No topic drift, culminating in a specific actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission and invited preferences, but the structure leaned expert-driven with strategy menus and pre-drafted scripts. Laura's contributions received brief acknowledgment rather than genuine two-way power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent menu-based choices allowed Laura to select, modify, and reject suggestions, and her self-generated solutions were affirmed. The volume of pre-packaged advice and scripts somewhat constrained her authorship.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on providing information rather than drawing out Laura's own motivations, which emerged mostly spontaneously. Beyond the confidence scale, open questions exploring her reasons for change were largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered choices respecting Laura's input, but often assumed the expert role with prescriptive strategies and scripts. Laura's own best solutions emerged despite, rather than because of, the counselor's prompting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Confidence scaling successfully evoked change talk and raised Laura's confidence from 6 to 8. However, this change talk was rarely deepened or reflected back, with quick pivots to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced and was implicitly addressed via if-then framing. However, there was no active, strategic softening—no double-sided reflections or reframing of the 'one night' pattern.
