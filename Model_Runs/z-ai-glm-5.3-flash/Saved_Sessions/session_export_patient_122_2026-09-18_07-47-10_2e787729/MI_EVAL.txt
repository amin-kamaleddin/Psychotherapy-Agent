# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout. Self-doubt (calling past efforts 'failing,' worrying the plan is too rigid) is reframed nonjudgmentally as wisdom and legitimate self-awareness, with no disapproval or pressure anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and captures emotional nuance, not just content — e.g., 'walking gives you something rare right now — a piece of home' linking exercise to migration loss, and reflections on guilt, dread, and cautious hope.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays well focused on sustainable movement and progresses logically from doability to flexibility to lapses. Minor detours into adjacent emotional territory (calm-vs-guilt taxonomy, door note logistics) and no explicit summary or shared next step stated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed — rest nights, the door note, and the calm-vs-guilt test all originate from Sofia and visibly shape the session. The counselor incorporates her corrections and she leaves having generated her own framework.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively reinforced by checking the plan against her values ('a cage, or breathing room?') and returning ownership to her throughout. No advice is imposed; even coping strategies are Sofia's own ideas, and her choice to try emerges freely.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn out, never supplied — open questions elicit Sofia's own reasons, confidence, and relapse plan. She ends with her own hope ('I actually want to try'), her own reframes, and strong change talk, exactly what high-level evocation produces.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** An exemplary client-as-expert stance — nearly every turn ends with a question placing authority with Sofia, and the counselor never prescribes a plan or strategy. All concrete plan elements originate from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and developed with open questions, producing increasingly strong statements ('I want to try... which hasn't happened in a while'). Each change statement is reflected back and deepened rather than left alone.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about rules, failure, and the all-or-nothing voice is acknowledged and redirected toward Sofia's own solutions, with the 'information, not a verdict' reframe actively undercutting it. Not a 5 only because exploration of the fear/guilt spiral sometimes extends longer than needed before pivoting to change.
