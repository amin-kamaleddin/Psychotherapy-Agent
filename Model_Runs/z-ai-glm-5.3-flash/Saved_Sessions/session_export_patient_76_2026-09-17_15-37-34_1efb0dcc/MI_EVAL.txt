# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Liora's reluctance and attachment to cutting, though affirmations are somewhat formulaic and the checklist-like safety questions dilute warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections early on capture her exhaustion and ambivalence, but empathy thins as the session shifts to directive safety planning, with poignant disclosures receiving quick pivots rather than reflective responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on self-harm and coping, moving purposefully toward a concrete 24-hour commitment, though focus is achieved partly through stacked questions and rapid topic transitions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asking permission and incorporating Liora's preferences show collaboration, but long prescriptive option lists and pre-drafted texts mean Liora largely accepts or modifies counselor proposals rather than co-generating ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Liora's choice ('you're the one in charge') and accepts her boundaries and 24-hour limit, though some exchanges feel like yes/no pressure and option lists gently steer.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions and a confidence ruler to elicit motivation, but much content is counselor-supplied and promising moments like Liora's post-breathing hope ('it's not nothing') go unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Explicitly rejects the expert role and lets Liora shape plan details, but the agenda and structure remain counselor-driven with limited power-sharing beyond细节.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully evokes change talk (24-hour commitment, trying ice and breathing), but misses chances to deepen it, notably failing to reflect or amplify Liora's nascent hope after breathing helped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing, but does not actively soften it through double-sided reflections or elicit Liora's own reasons against cutting—the counter-position is largely counselor-supplied.
