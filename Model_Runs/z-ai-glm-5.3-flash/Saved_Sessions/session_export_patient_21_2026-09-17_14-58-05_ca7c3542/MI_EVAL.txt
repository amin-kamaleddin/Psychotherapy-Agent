# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone maintained throughout with no disapproving statements. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked individualized depth referencing Camila's specific values such as her grandkids.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captured her ambivalence, including the tension between enjoying walks and low-energy days. Reflections sometimes merely restated content rather than probing emotional meaning, such as her worry about 'letting the whole thing slide.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with no meaningful drift. Each turn built momentum from exploration to a specific weekly plan, implementation aids, and a concrete check-in focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor framed work as joint and incorporated Camila's ideas into the plan, confirmed by her comment that it 'felt more like sorting it out together.' The option menu and plan details were generated before inviting her input rather than letting her ideas shape them first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Camila's ownership, sought permission before advising, and framed suggestions as optional. Camila's remark about not being pushed to 'run a marathon' confirms strong autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ruler questions effectively drew out her own motivations (health, grandkids) and she generated her own backup plan. Evocation was somewhat technique-driven, with the counselor occasionally supplying answers rather than eliciting them from Camila.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently solicited Camila's input and built the final check-in focus from her answer. The counselor sometimes supplied full plan content (times, cues, script) rather than co-creating each element.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance and confidence rulers skillfully elicited strong change talk about her health and grandkids. Missed opportunities to deepen change talk after it emerged, such as exploring why confidence rose from 5 to 8.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers without dwelling on them and effectively redirected Camila's worry into her own backup plan. Option lists occasionally shifted focus to problem-solving rather than letting Camila resolve ambivalence verbally.
