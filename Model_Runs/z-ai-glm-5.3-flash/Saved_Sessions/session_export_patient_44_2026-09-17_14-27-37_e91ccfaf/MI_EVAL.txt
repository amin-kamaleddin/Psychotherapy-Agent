# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with validating statements ('that's normal and understandable'), but affirmations were generic and never specifically praised Erik's own strengths or self-generated insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal and shallow; the counselor often jumped to advice without exploring emotional content like Erik's discouragement about comparing himself to younger men.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on gym consistency and diet with clear progression from goals to concrete plans, though direction was achieved through prescriptive advice rather than guiding, with some drift into general wellness tips.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some co-development occurred (calendar entry, reward choice), but power-sharing was uneven—the counselor proposed most solutions and Erik reacted, rather than ideas being built upon jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik's plan modifications were accepted without correction, but the interaction was dominated by directive advice with no explicit autonomy-affirming statements, undermining perceived client choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor supplied reasons and strategies rather than drawing out Erik's own motivations; his strong self-motivational statements ('my back feels better, that's the win') were never reflected or deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumed the expert role, prescribing exact schedules, scripts, and routines with minimal client input; Erik's own solutions were not credited or built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good evocative questions elicited meaningful change talk, but the counselor never reinforced, deepened, or reflected it back—change talk emerged largely because Erik brought it himself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified, which is a strength, but it was sidestepped with problem-solving advice rather than strategic double-sided reflections linking Erik's own stated benefits to his doubts.
