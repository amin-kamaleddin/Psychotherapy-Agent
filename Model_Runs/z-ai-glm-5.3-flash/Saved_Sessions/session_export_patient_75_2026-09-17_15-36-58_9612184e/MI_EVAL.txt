# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor opened with nonjudgmental support and validated Linda's concerns, but validation was often brief and formulaic, quickly pivoting to questions or advice rather than sitting with her fears about steroids and dependence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early reflections captured her ambivalence and worries, but reflections diminished mid-session as the counselor shifted to teaching mode, missing her deeper feeling of powerlessness until she named it herself.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on asthma management, progressing logically from exploring ambivalence to concrete appointment preparation, with only minor drift into communication coaching.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Linda meaningfully shaped the session and co-developed the final card, but the mid-session information dump was counselor-driven, with little explicit acknowledgment of her own expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered choices about activities and framed the plan as Linda's to bring to her doctor, though some advice was delivered prescriptively without emphasizing her ownership of treatment decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early questions evoked useful change talk, but the counselor quickly moved to providing solutions; Linda's most powerful insights emerged incidentally rather than through deliberate evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Though Linda's input shaped activities, the mid-session exchange was expert-heavy with unsolicited question lists and scripts; her surprise at being collaborated with suggests the counselor's early stance reinforced an expert dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Forward-looking questions elicited strong change talk about lowering doses and having a plan, but the counselor missed opportunities to deepen or reflect this change talk back when it emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about side effects and dependence was acknowledged but not actively reframed; her repeated 'it's really difficult' went largely unredirected, making softening mostly passive avoidance rather than active resolution of ambivalence.
