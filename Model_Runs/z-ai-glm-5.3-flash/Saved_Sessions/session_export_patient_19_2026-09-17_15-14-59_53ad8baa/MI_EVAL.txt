# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with respectful responses to Anya's humor and meaningful affirmations. However, praise is somewhat formulaic ('Nice choice,' 'Nice work') and lacks deeply personalized affirmations of her strengths, such as her honesty about past failed attempts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are reasonable but often shallow surface-level paraphrases. Missed opportunities to reflect the emotional sting Anya explicitly voiced ('That stings a bit'), her fear of being weak, or the deeper meaning of her friendships and shame about debt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on the target behavior (gambling/spending) throughout every turn, with each response funneling toward concrete action. The session progresses logically from ambivalence to a specific, testable plan for Saturday with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely builds on Anya's ideas (card-cutting, brunch plan, sister as support) and role-playing as her mate is a strong collaborative move. However, several turns deliver pre-packaged lists of tactics she had little hand in designing, making some exchanges feel coach-driven rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Anya's ownership with choice-giving language ('What feels most helpful to try first?', 'pick one that sounds like you') and never pressures or moralizes. Her final line that 'it's already half-decided' reflects genuine internalized ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws motivation from Anya's own values (Egypt dream, self-image, frustration) using scaling questions and confidence prompts that elicited rising change talk (4 → 8). The main limitation is reliance on structured techniques rather than open exploration, and her emotional core motivations are never explicitly evoked or elaborated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames decisions as Anya's, and her contributions demonstrably shape the session (brunch plan, comeback line, sister as exit contact). However, fairly dense lists of options in each turn slightly tilt the balance toward expert-driven agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of scaling questions and 'what would raise your confidence' prompts successfully evoked strong change talk, with confidence climbing from 4 to 8. Relies heavily on the 0-10 scale (asked at least five times) and misses chances to deepen change talk with elaboration questions like 'What makes this time different?'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not amplify sustain talk, but also rarely actively engages with it. Anya's sustain talk ('I'm weak,' 'I haven't managed it to this point') is met mostly with pivoting to options rather than double-sided reflections that legitimize ambivalence.
