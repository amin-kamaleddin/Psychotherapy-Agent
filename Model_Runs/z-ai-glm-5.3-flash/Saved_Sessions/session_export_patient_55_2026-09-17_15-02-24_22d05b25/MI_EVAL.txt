# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with repeated affirmations, but praise is formulaic ('Nice work') and lacks individualized affirmation of Isabelle's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections, but reflections remain shallow and miss deeper emotional meaning around her need for downtime and her ambivalence about being there.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus established early with logical progression to a concrete plan and follow-up, though the target emerged more from counselor-offered options than Isabelle's priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Isabelle contributes meaningful plan elements, but the counselor drives each step with stacked leading questions, making the interaction feel checklist-driven rather than co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the plan as Isabelle's choice and she retains control over specifics, though prescriptive additions (energy ratings, alarm labels, telling mom) occasionally steer rather than follow.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions are largely supplied by the counselor rather than elicited; few questions draw out Isabelle's own values, reasons, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Begins with permission-seeking and inviting input, but shifts into expert-driven prescriptive mode where the counselor's ideas substantially shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited (phone cutoff idea, commitments, success markers), but it is never deepened with questions about reasons, hopes, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged only briefly and superficially; no double-sided reflections used, and the counselor pivots quickly to planning without resolving ambivalence.
