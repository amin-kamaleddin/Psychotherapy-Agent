### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of scaling moved confidence from 6 to 8.5, with rich patient change talk and reinforcement of commitment language via concrete steps. Evocation is at times technique-driven and question-heavy rather than deepening emotional meaning.

**Comments:** Barriers are acknowledged without dwelling, and sustain talk is flexibly redirected to options Marco chooses (voice notes instead of journals, plate swap instead of policing snacks). More direct acknowledgment/reframing of his history of failure would strengthen this.
