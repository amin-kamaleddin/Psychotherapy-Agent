# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with respectful acknowledgment of limitations. However, affirmations are generic and repetitive, lacking individualized recognition of Camila's specific strengths and insights.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; the counselor rarely reflects statements back. Vulnerable disclosures like fear of failing and past quitting were met with advice lists rather than emotional reflection or exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on increasing physical activity throughout, with appropriate safety screening. Direction was achieved through prescriptive instruction rather than guided discovery, which is the only deduction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is predominantly expert-driven, with a pre-formed plan presented rather than co-constructed. Camila's useful knowledge emerged only because she volunteered it, not because the counselor drew it out.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some autonomy-supportive moments exist, such as permission-seeking and flexibility on scheduling. However, the counselor set most parameters and repeatedly re-issued instructions at closing, subtly undermining Camila's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to elicit Camila's own motivations; no exploration of values, goals, or her confidence rating. Her motivation surfaced spontaneously despite, not because of, counselor technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed the expert role, delivering fully prescriptive plans in bullet lists without soliciting Camila's ideas. Repetitive instruction-giving at the end further weakened the sense of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Camila offered notable spontaneous change talk that was never acknowledged, reflected, or deepened. The confidence rating of five or six was asked but not explored, a classic missed MI evocation opportunity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The 'missed days as data, not failure' reframe and if-then fallback plan effectively soften sustain talk. However, ambivalence was mostly handled with logistical problem-solving rather than strategic MI techniques like double-sided reflections.
