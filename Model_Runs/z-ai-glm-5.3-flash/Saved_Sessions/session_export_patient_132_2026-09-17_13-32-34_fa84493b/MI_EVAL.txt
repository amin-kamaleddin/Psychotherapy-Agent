# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally respectful and nonjudgmental with some affirmations, but they are generic and formulaic. Missed opportunities to normalize Yasmin's ambivalence and affirm her courage after seven years of struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on (e.g., acknowledging the restrict/binge cycle), but responses quickly pivot to information-gathering and advice. Emotional core disclosures—discouragement, fear of spiraling—were not deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on target behaviors with a logically sequenced plan and concrete commitments. Deducted because the agenda was counselor-driven rather than tied to Yasmin's values and unresolved ambivalence about weight loss.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration in sequencing decisions, co-drafting the RD message, and negotiating check-ins. However, the core intervention plan was presented as a package to accept rather than co-constructed from Yasmin's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered genuine choices at several decision points, but most content consisted of prescriptive directives. Also implicitly steered Yasmin away from her weight-loss goal rather than supporting her right to define her own outcomes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** After one initial evocative question, no open questions explored Yasmin's reasons, values, confidence, or hopes. The counselor supplied all rationale for change while Yasmin's spontaneous motivation went unpicked up.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor rapidly assumed the expert role, delivering meal plans, protocols, and scripts with little patient input on substance. Clear openings to leverage Yasmin's self-knowledge ('I know myself') were missed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged mostly from the patient herself rather than being evoked by the counselor. When it appeared ('I want something sustainable'), it was never reflected, deepened, or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Yasmin's ambivalence about restriction and weight loss was met with reassurance and psychoeducation rather than double-sided reflection. The sustain talk was effectively sidestepped and never revisited.
