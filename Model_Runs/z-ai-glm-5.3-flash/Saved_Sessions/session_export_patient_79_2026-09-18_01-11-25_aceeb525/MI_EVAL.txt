# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance maintained throughout, including skillful handling of sarcasm with double-sided reflections. Affirmations are somewhat sparse and ambivalence is not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, with accurate summaries capturing Luis's core ambivalence. The emotional layer beneath his defensiveness (feeling misunderstood) is not explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on asthma self-management from the first turn, with each exchange progressing logically toward a concrete, client-defined behavior. Textbook directional guiding.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every step is co-constructed through open questions, and the plan emerges entirely from Luis's preferences. He freely sets limits that the clinician accepts without negotiation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are consistently framed as Luis's ('if it were up to you,' 'if any'), and the clinician adapts when he rejects charts or check-ins. No persuasive or controlling language appears.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from Luis's own reasons and experiences, even naming the change talk as his own. Confidence and ability talk receive less systematic exploration, with no scaling or confidence queries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Luis is repeatedly positioned as the expert, and the entire plan originates from him. His closing remark shows he experienced the plan as genuinely self-authored.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled open questions elicit rich client-generated change talk, which the clinician explicitly names and reinforces. Some opportunities to deepen confidence/ability talk were only lightly touched.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently met with double-sided reflections that pivot toward Luis's own change arguments without amplifying it. The strategy is effective but relies on a single technique rather than varied approaches.
