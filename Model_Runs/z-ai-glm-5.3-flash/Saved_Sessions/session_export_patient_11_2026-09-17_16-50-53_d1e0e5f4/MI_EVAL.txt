# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, non-judgmental tone throughout, and Amira's preference to delay nutrition work is respected. Praise is somewhat generic at times, drifting toward cheerleading rather than individualized regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections early on (move-related drain, the salsa-jar moment) show good understanding. Reflections are often brief pivots to advice, with limited depth exploring her emotional experience of feeling scattered.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on increasing physical activity, moving steadily toward a concrete plan and scheduled follow-up with no topic drift. Some momentum comes from the counselor's checklist-style prompts rather than guiding Amira's own path.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed from Amira's own ideas and she says it fits her life. Collaboration is weakened by the counselor prescribing add-ons and logistics (alarm times, charger placement) rather than jointly negotiating each element.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices and accepts Amira's decisions, including her wish to self-manage reminders. Autonomy is diluted by repeated unsolicited instructions about alarms, podcast queueing, and chargers, edging toward control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good evocative opening questions and use of a confidence scale eliciting her intrinsic reasons for change. Evocation tapers off quickly, with the counselor supplying options rather than deepening what 'feeling capable' means or why past attempts failed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amira is positioned as the expert, and her contributions substantially shape the plan (her cue, if-then, podcast reward). The counselor introduces several suggestions unilaterally, tilting slightly toward a coach/expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes motivation via open questions and a confidence rating, and amplifies Amira's rich change talk by linking behavior to mood benefits. Missed systematically deepening commitment language or exploring why the '8' isn't lower.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mild sustain talk (hesitance about nutrition, past burnout) is acknowledged neutrally without arguing. It is bypassed rather than strategically softened or reframed with double-sided reflections.
