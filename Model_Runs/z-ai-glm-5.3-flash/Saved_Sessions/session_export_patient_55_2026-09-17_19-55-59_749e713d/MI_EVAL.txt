# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental, explicitly disarming the anticipated lecture and affirming friendships as a strength. The final challenging exchange could have more explicitly reaffirmed unconditional acceptance before pivoting to a probing question.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, accurate complex reflections that captured meaning, not just content (e.g., 'your brain finally gets to be quiet'). Empathy was occasionally used as a setup for strategic questions, and emotional moments (guilt about sax, fear of the lecture) deserved fuller dwelling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Agenda-setting was effective and the session stayed connected to the target concern while honoring the client's chosen focus on workload. The approach to the risk behavior created defensive drift rather than a guided trajectory toward resolving ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing: agenda co-created, summaries offered for verification, repeated deferral to the client's read. The final analytical framing of weekend costs subtly took the lead in defining the problem.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout: acknowledged the referral was unchosen, disclaimed the paperwork agenda, refused to dictate what to cut, and framed every question as the client's call. No pressure or controlling language anywhere.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from within, eliciting desire and need talk (sleep, music, less conflict with mom) and self-generated insight. Confidence/ability talk was never elicited, and the final question pushed analysis rather than drawing out intrinsic evaluation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly located expertise within the client ('You're the expert on your own life') and let her contributions demonstrably shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked genuine desire and need language about workload, sleep, and music, and reflected aspirations back. Change talk was evoked but never developed toward commitment, confidence, or specific next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk and refused to target friendships, but the direct question about weekend costs came before sufficient trust-building, triggering defensive sustain talk ('it's the one I've got'). Double-sided reflections or affirmations of her coping would have softened this better.
