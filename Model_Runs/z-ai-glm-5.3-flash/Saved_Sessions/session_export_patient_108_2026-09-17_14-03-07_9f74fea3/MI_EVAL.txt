# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with effective normalization of Oliver's guilt. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and rarely honored Oliver's underlying strengths as values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate early reflections, but empathy was inconsistent and quickly pivoted to problem-solving. Deeper emotional threads—guilt, the 'weak' self-label, loneliness—were addressed only superficially.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the boundary-setting goal with no topic drift. Session progressed logically from exploration to rehearsal to commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Role-play segments showed strong collaboration and joint decision-making. However, large portions tilted toward counselor-delivered advice and checklists rather than true partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine choices were offered (script options, role-play preference), but the stance leaned directive with prescriptive scripts and checklists. Oliver's ownership was endorsed rather than explicitly reinforced or explored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged from Oliver's own initiative rather than skillful evocation. The counselor supplied answers and strategies instead of asking open questions about Oliver's reasons, confidence, and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power sharing in offering choices and shaping the role-play, but the overall framing positioned the counselor as strategist and Oliver as executor. Oliver's own plan was not explicitly built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reinforced Oliver's change talk through role-play and a concrete action plan. However, change talk was rarely explicitly elicited or deepened through direct evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One strong reframe of guilt as evidence of integrity that Oliver found relieving. Sustain talk was otherwise bypassed in favor of scripts rather than explored or resolved with double-sided reflections.
