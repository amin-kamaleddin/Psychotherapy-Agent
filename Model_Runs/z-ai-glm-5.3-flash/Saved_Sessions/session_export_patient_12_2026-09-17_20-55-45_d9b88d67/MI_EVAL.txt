# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard even during pushback, countering Amira's self-criticism ('that's pathetic') with warmth and affirmation that showing up 'means a lot.' Warmth is genuine and individualized rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Makes consistently accurate, deep reflections, including reframing drug use as 'pain looking for relief' and capturing Amira's loneliness and ambivalence. Mirrors Amira's own words and stays attuned even as she experiences unfamiliar hope.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps focus anchored to drug use and isolation and builds progressively toward concrete action and follow-up planning. A point is withheld because some exploratory segments (the Nadia-text visualization) linger before returning to action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is consistently co-constructed ('what would be most helpful for us to work on together'), with every plan element originating from Amira. The counselor facilitates rather than prescribes, which Amira explicitly notes as a positive surprise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Amira's choice and control ('we don't have to start there,' 'both chosen by you') and asks permission before follow-up. No pressure or coercion; realistic caveats are accepted without challenge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from within Amira via open questions that elicit her own aspirations, insights, and hope, rather than supplying reasons to change. The counselor reflects and amplifies this material, resulting in Amira articulating her own commitment by session's end.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Amira as the expert on her own life from the opening and lets her define the agenda and first step. Amira's contributions visibly shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk, highlighting the part of Amira that 'hasn't given up entirely' and amplifying emerging hope. This culminates in Amira's own plan, self-affirming language, and commitment ('I actually feel like I might follow through. That's new.').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally validates hopelessness without amplifying it and normalizes reluctance while pivoting to change ('we don't have to start there'). A point is deducted because sustain themes occasionally get re-reflected directly rather than being pivoted more decisively toward change.
