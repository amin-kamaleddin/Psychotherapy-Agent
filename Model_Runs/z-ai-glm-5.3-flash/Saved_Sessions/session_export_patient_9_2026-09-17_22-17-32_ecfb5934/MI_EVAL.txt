# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, unconditional positive regard was maintained throughout, with affirmations like 'that's not silly — that's you building your own anchor' and 'says a lot about the mother you are.' No judgment about her self-criticisms; her honesty and efforts were consistently affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and emotionally attuned, e.g., 'you've been trying harder to keep the peace... that's a hard thing to notice.' Ambivalence was held skillfully with double-sided reflections ('Both things are true'), and Amina built on each reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, progressive focus on staying calm at handoffs for her son, advancing steadily from problem identification to a concrete plan, success markers, and follow-up. No topic drift occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The agenda was negotiated and the plan's content came entirely from Amina, demonstrating equal partnership. A point is withheld because the counselor sometimes interpreted her experience for her ('that's a real turning point') rather than inviting her own conclusions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was consistently affirmed ('which one feels most doable for you this week?') and no technique was pushed. A point is withheld because affirmations and closing framing slightly steered toward the plan she was already forming.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn almost entirely from the client via open questions like 'what has worked before, even once?' and 'what would tell you it went well?' Her values, hope, self-compassion language, and plan were all self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The expertise-resides-with-client stance was explicit ('You know him better than anyone'), and the plan (phone note, brother as support, Friday timing) originated wholly from Amina. The closing handed control entirely back to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked through open questions, yielding concrete commitment ('I'll try it Friday'), ability talk (the birthday party), and self-motivational language ('my boy not watching mommy lose it'). Each change statement was reinforced and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (fear of being seen as cold) was briefly validated and pivoted to change-oriented questions about past successes. A point is withheld because the counselor occasionally re-voiced the barrier rather than pivoting fully.
