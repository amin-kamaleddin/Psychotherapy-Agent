# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, affirming Malik's honesty and safe choices. Fell short of deeper, individualized warmth and affirming his worth beyond compliance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured his frustration and fear of failing (e.g., the 'search-hide-get-caught loop'). Some reflections stayed at surface level, missing deeper hurt about his mother's distrust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session moved purposefully through exploration, planning, and rehearsal with clear focus. However, emphasis shifted from cannabis use itself toward conflict management.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Malik edited the text, chose scenarios, and set trial terms, giving him real ownership. Power tipped toward the counselor during drafting, where she supplied most content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and accepted Malik's edits, keeping plan ownership with him. Provided ready-made options rather than consistently eliciting his own ideas first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited motivations around family peace and responsibility, but never explored Malik's own reasons or concerns about cannabis use. Motivation elicited was shallow and externally driven.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for Malik's input and let him choose options at times. Defaulted to expert role when drafting texts and scripts rather than drawing solutions from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evoked change talk around family peace and Malik's willingness for a trial. Did not develop ambivalence about cannabis use itself, which Malik stated was 'not changing.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Engaged directly with sustain talk rather than rolling with it, notably offering smoking safety tips after Malik said he'd keep smoking. Echoed sustain talk without strategic double-sided reflection.
