# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout—normalizing fears, affirming strengths, and reframing self-doubt without judgment (e.g., 'asking for a time that works is just being professional, not difficult').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening, including double-sided reflections and reflections capturing deeper meaning (e.g., the daughter's question, 'this time would be different').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior of attending interviews, with strategically sequenced questions producing a concrete, dated action plan by session's end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal partnership—the client's ideas shaped the session's direction, and the counselor co-constructed rather than prescribed throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership consistently reinforced by offering options without prescribing, leaving timing to the client, and crediting her agency explicitly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client via open questions eliciting her own meanings, past coping resources, and insights—no lecturing or supplied reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor positioned Amina as the expert ('you know your life best'), and her contributions directly shaped the plan, script, and accountability strategy.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and developed, escalating from tentative ('I guess I could try asking?') to concrete commitment ('Tomorrow morning. I'll write it down').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ambivalence acknowledged without amplification, using strategic double-sided reflections that pivoted toward change; occasional summaries restated barriers (daycare costs, mom's availability) in ways that briefly dwelt on obstacles.
