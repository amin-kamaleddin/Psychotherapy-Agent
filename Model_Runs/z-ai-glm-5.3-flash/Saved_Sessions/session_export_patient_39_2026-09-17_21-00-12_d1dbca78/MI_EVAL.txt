# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and consistently nonjudgmental with specific affirmations that embrace Elias's humor and pride. Falls short of 5 as some affirmations lean formulaic rather than fully normalizing his discouragement and pride-wounds.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including a powerful double-sided reflection and attention to tone ('grin in your voice'). Not a 5 because the emotional weight of shame and fear of being stared at is acknowledged but never deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Steady focus on the target behavior with progressive guidance toward a concrete step and follow-up plan. Some exchanges linger in reflective affirmation rather than advancing action, and the plan isn't consolidated in a single summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely an interaction between equals with co-constructed plans and genuine client agency. Occasionally slips into an evaluative stance ('solid plan'), and the agenda is counselor-initiated in the opening.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support: 'This yes is yours, no one else's,' explicit acceptance of possible failure without judgment, and no imposed advice. Even Elias's wish that the change feel self-generated is honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key motivational element originates from Elias himself, drawn out through skillful questions and amplified back to him. His 'Part of me actually wants to now. That's new.' demonstrates evocation at its best.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides with the client, building the closing plan directly from Elias's words and adopting his language. Accepts his correction of framing without defensiveness.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes progressively stronger change language through strategic open questions, then reinforces each instance. The sequencing from possibility to commitment is exemplary MI practice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk using double-sided reflections and strategic reframing of barriers. Some barriers like self-consciousness and pride are restated in summaries without being reframed or balanced with confidence talk.
