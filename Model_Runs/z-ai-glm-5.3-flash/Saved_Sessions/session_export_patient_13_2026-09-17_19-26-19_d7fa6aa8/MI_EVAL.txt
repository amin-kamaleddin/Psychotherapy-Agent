# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional regard; the patient's pace ('not yet to the doctor') is honored without disappointment, and affirmations are individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally deep reflections that track subtle cues like her fear of commitments 'sneaking in' and the exhaustion of 'starting from zero every night.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Laser focus on moving toward medical evaluation; every exchange builds toward a concrete, client-owned first step and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative tone; every suggestion is tentative and shaped by the patient, with the plan co-constructed entirely from her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy protection—accepting her limits with zero pressure and even checking whether writing things down feels like a sneaky commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client through open-ended questions; even the final summary only restates her own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client; her contributions visibly shape the session and become the entire plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation and selective reinforcement of client-generated change talk, culminating in a concrete commitment without imposition.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is neither argued with nor reinforced; the clinician consistently pivots to change-side language while validating her need to sit with it.
