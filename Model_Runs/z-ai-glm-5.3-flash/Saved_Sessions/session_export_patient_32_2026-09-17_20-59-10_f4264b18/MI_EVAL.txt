# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and affirms the client's efforts and expertise without any judgment, even when he minimizes ('I'm not some alcoholic'). Warmth and validation are maintained throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are complex and accurate, capturing core ambivalence and meaning beneath the surface ('the real fight is the crew, not the drinking'). Mirrors the client's change-oriented language back to advance the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use and DUI prevention from the start, with each turn progressing toward a concrete, time-bound plan. No significant drift occurs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equals — the counselor co-constructs the plan through questions rather than directives, and the final summary is built entirely from the client's own contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces the client's ownership and choice ('protect yourself on your own terms'), offers options rather than directives, and frames the plan as temporary and self-defined to preserve his sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client via skilled open questions; no advice or persuasion is imposed. All change talk, plans, and commitments are self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions the client as the expert on his own world, and his contributions substantially shape the session. The plan (Marcus, gym, cab money) comes entirely from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open evocative questions consistently elicit rich change talk ('I'd rather choose it than have a judge choose it for me'), which is then reinforced and deepened with a confidence ruler.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk, letting minimization pass without arguing and redirecting to viable options. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections — notably the isolation that ended his prior sobriety attempt could have been reframed more deliberately.
