# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with Sofia's self-criticism met with validation rather than correction. Affirmations are brief and formulaic, occasionally paired with advice, limiting depth of positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections ('tired, stressed, stuck in stress-eating cycles'), but reflections are sparse and shallow. Rich emotional material (fear of failure, relocation losses, self-criticism) is acknowledged but not reflectively deepened.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays well-focused on target behaviors and moves coherently from assessment to concrete experiments with next steps. Movement is driven by the counselor's agenda rather than Sofia's own values, and rapid-fire assessment questions briefly pull focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's input shapes some details (messaging preference, choosing Option A), but the counselor supplies most solutions, scripts, and structured plans. The dynamic resembles expert prescribing with client sign-off rather than genuine co-design.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated choices offered and decisions respected, including Sofia's preference to self-report rather than be monitored. Many choices are between counselor-designed options, and prescriptive reminder lists edge toward directive coaching.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Readiness ruler and open questions elicit some meaningful client statements, but evocation is inconsistent and superficial. When Sofia asks how to work on eating without failing, the counselor offers a technique rather than exploring her own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some honoring of Sofia's contributions and preferences, but the overall pattern is expert-driven with prescribed plans, exact scripts, and pre-packaged options. Little explicit acknowledgment that expertise resides with Sofia.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Readiness ruler and choice questions generate meaningful change talk ('I kind of want to prove myself wrong'). However, change talk is not consistently deepened; much of Sofia's motivation language emerges spontaneously rather than through skillful evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement, and an alternative framing ('name the thought') is provided. But no double-sided reflections are used to hold ambivalence, and the counselor pivots quickly to planning rather than strategically reflecting the change side against doubt.
