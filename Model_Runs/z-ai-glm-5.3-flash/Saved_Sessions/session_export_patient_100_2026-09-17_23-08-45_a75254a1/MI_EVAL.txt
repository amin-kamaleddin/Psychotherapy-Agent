# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard was communicated throughout, with no judgment even when Maya disclosed drinking. Maya explicitly noted the absence of the lecture she expected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, often complex reflections deepened Maya's insight, including a skillful double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence. Reflections consistently landed and moved the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on alcohol use and weekend behavior, progressing from ambivalence to a concrete commitment. Each response advanced the conversation one step without derailing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction functioned between equals, with Maya's view actively sought and her contributions visibly shaping each direction of the session. The follow-up plan was co-created at Maya's own suggestion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforced Maya's ownership of her choices with no advice, pressure, or warning-giving. Maya's plan was explicitly credited as her own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from Maya's own reasons through open questions, reflections, and affirmations. Her closing insight ('I already knew some of this stuff') confirms evocation drove the change process.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor positioned Maya as the expert from the start, with all plan elements originating from her. Counselor contributions were limited to reflecting and questioning, never directing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A skillful question sequence elicited rich desire, need, ability, and commitment language from Maya. The counselor explicitly flagged and affirmed her commitment language ('I feel weirdly good about this').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and redirected through values-based reframes rather than argument or persuasion. No confrontation or reinforcement of sustain talk occurred.
