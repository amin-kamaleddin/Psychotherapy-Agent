# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental; Naomi's doubts are normalized. Affirmations remain somewhat generic rather than personalized to her strengths or courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captures her experience of anxiety and doubt. Reflections are sometimes followed too quickly by advice, and deeper emotional themes (self-criticism, shame, fear of freezing) go unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on managing social anxiety at work. Every exchange advances toward concrete, time-limited actions ending in a specific one-week commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed from Naomi's own habits and preferences. The counselor generates most of the option menus rather than inviting her to brainstorm first, slightly tilting idea generation toward the expert role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Permission is consistently sought before advising, options are framed as choices, and Naomi's preferences (ring cue, coffee anchor, her own phrasing) shape the plan. No pressure or persuasion appears anywhere in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk grows over the session, but motivation is evoked mainly through logistics and feasibility questions. Her own reasons, values, and hopes for change are minimally explored, with no confidence scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi is repeatedly positioned as the expert and her choices genuinely shape the plan. The counselor sometimes fronts multiple strategies in advice-giving mode rather than first letting her generate solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is frequently elicited and reinforced, with Naomi voicing meaningful commitment ('one week, I can manage that'). Deeper evocation of her own reasons for change and values is largely absent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with validation rather than argument and consistently pivoted toward small, doable actions. Strategic double-sided reflections pairing her doubts with her expressed hope are missing, limiting skillfulness.
