# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations of progress and normalization of slips; however, affirmations are somewhat brief and formulaic, keeping this below top marks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but emotionally meaningful material (weakest willpower in mornings, father's toothpick story, nicotine identity concern) receives little emotional reflection as the counselor jumps to strategies.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on smoking reduction — every exchange ties to the target behavior with concrete next steps and a specific high-risk context, no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Agenda is negotiated and Samuel's own ideas (walk, toothpick) form the core of the plan, but rapid-fire advice blocks and scripted monologues limit back-and-forth dialogue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices and accepts Samuel's rejection of NRT without pressure, but frequent directive multi-step instructions and micro-managing details (drafting his alarm message) undercut true ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Relies heavily on supplying information and scripts rather than drawing out motivation; strong client material (the doable five-a-day goal, identity concerns) is never explored or deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine power-sharing and choice, but the counselor often takes an expert stance with dense prescriptive checklists without checking what Samuel thinks would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Samuel generates change talk himself, but the counselor does little to actively evoke or deepen it — no importance/confidence questions, and change talk is affirmed generically rather than reflected back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles doubt and NRT resistance non-defensively, reframing slips as data without arguing; however, no double-sided reflections are used and ambivalence is sometimes skipped rather than resolved.
