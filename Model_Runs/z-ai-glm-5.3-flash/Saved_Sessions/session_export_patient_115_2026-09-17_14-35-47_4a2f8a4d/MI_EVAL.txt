# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental, never criticizing Samir's smoking or drinking and honoring his stated limits. However, warmth feels formulaic, with generic affirmations ('good call,' 'well done') rather than specific recognition of his efforts as a working father.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections like 'That catch-22 is really unfair' show accurate understanding of Samir's bind. But deeper emotional cues — his fear of chest tightness and his daughter as a motivator — go unreflected, and empathy erodes into checklist repetition by the end.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains clear focus on asthma and related lifestyle behaviors, opening with an agenda invitation and converging on a concrete, trackable weekly plan. Minor drift occurs via the tangential 5-4-3-2-1 grounding suggestion and scattered closing reminders.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is collaborative framing ('we'll adjust together') and Samir feels part of a team, but the actual plans and checklists are largely counselor-authored. Samir selects from pre-set menus rather than co-creating with his own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** This is the counselor's strongest domain: choices are offered repeatedly, Samir's limits are accepted without pressure, and the week is framed as a self-directed experiment. Samir explicitly notes the payoff — 'a plan I actually chose' and appreciation for not having quitting 'shoved down my throat.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor relies almost entirely on advice-giving and information delivery, with virtually no open questions exploring Samir's values, confidence, or reasons for change. Powerful intrinsic motivators (fear of chest tightness, his daughter) surface spontaneously but are met with logistics instead of being developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some inclusive language and choices are present, but the counselor positions himself as the knowledge source with extensive expert advice lists. Samir's own expertise about his triggers and what has worked before is rarely solicited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor's menu of options helped evoke Samir's air-quality commitment, but the strongest change talk — the daughter motivation and fear of chest tightness — is never deepened or explored. No targeted evocative questions are used.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully avoids arguing with sustain talk, pivoting to harm reduction when Samir defends cigarettes as his 'little escape' and honoring his pace. However, double-sided reflections are never used to hold Samir's ambivalence productively.
