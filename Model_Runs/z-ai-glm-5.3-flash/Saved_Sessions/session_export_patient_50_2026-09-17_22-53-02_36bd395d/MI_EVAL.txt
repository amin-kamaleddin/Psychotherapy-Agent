# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, unconditional positive regard throughout — even sustain talk is met with acceptance. Affirms Hana's courage, reframes avoidance as follow-through, and expresses confidence without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections capture both content and emotional nuance, including the protective function of avoidance. Double-sided reflections skillfully track her ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every exchange maintains clear focus on facing the exam despite anxiety, with each question advancing toward concrete action. The session progresses logically to a same-night commitment with contingency planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner, never prescribing solutions and building each reflection on Hana's own statements. Her ideas (chunking, texting her sister, celebrating wins) form the substance of the intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice and ownership, framing the plan as entirely Hana's ('the plan is really yours'). No advice, pressure, or directives; she sets her own timeline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client — her own evidence, values, and solutions — with no supplied reasons to change. Confidence is built by reflecting her past success and self-generated coping self-talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Hana as the expert on her own life throughout ('You're the expert on your own life,' 'You know your mind better than anyone'). The entire action plan originates from her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilfully evokes and amplifies change talk, culminating in strong commitment language ('My plan is tonight... I'm ready to try'), which is then reflected back verbatim to strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly, validated, and pivoted toward change. Questions like 'What would you want to tell yourself in that moment?' let Hana generate 'data, not a verdict' rather than amplifying doubt.
