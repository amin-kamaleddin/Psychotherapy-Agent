# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with specific genuine affirmations ('It's my season, not theirs — that takes a real kind of confidence'). Anika's expectance of a lecture was disproven; sustain talk was validated without disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately captured both content and emotion ('nervous and pumped can live side by side'). Anika repeatedly confirmed and built on reflections, indicating deep understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session progressed logically from triggers to a concrete, measurable change plan. Held at 4 because no consolidating summary of the full plan was given and Anika's follow-up check-in question went unanswered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan was co-constructed entirely from Anika's own material, with options offered as equals. She explicitly noted the non-authoritarian tone: 'I came in here thinking you were gonna lecture me.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Anika chose her own start date, substitutes, and measurement method, with the counselor highlighting her ownership ('How does it feel picking your own start date?'). Her response — 'it's my call, my timing' — shows internalized autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from Anika rather than supplied, with pivotal insights (vaping/performance link, 'why am I waiting') evoked from her. Her motivation visibly grew from minimizing to 'Okay, I'm actually doing this.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major plan element (Sarah plan, backup, sprint timing, start date, check-in) originated from Anika, who was positioned as the expert. The counselor acted as a guide, never an authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open evocative questions systematically elicited and deepened commitment talk ('What would it mean for you if you walked into tryouts knowing you'd kept that promise?'). Spontaneous change talk was immediately reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Double-sided reflections held ambivalence without amplifying sustain talk ('both of those are true right now'). Held at 4 for occasionally echoing sustain content ('one last easy goodbye') and letting 'It's not like I'm doing it every day' pass without a pivot.
