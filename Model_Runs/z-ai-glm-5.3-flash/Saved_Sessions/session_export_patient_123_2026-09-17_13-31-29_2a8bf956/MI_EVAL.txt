# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a neutral, non-judgmental stance and thanked Sofia for her honesty, but warmth was formulaic and lacked genuine affirmations of her strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was nearly absent; emotional cues like feeling embarrassed or 'it's not really a problem' were skipped over in favor of question batteries and advice lists.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor stayed focused on alcohol use and harm reduction throughout without drifting, though direction was checklist-driven rather than collaboratively goal-set.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session was largely counselor-directed, with plans imposed (flashcard regimen, strategies) rather than co-created, and Sofia's own simple plan was replaced by a counselor-designed one.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine permission-seeking and option-offering were present, but these were often overridden by prescriptive advice and answering Sofia's own questions for her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all motivation, reasons, and strategies came from the counselor; Sofia's own values and reasons for change were never explored, which she herself noted ('stuff I already know').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor positioned himself as the expert, delivering bullet-pointed tips and checklists with little acknowledgment that Sofia holds expertise about her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A single direct question about change was asked, but Sofia's mild willingness was met with more advice rather than deepening reflections to develop her own commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's repeated minimization ('it's just normal stuff', 'I doubt anything will change') was never addressed with double-sided reflections or strategic pivots, leaving her status quo arguments unexplored.
