### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing evening wine consumption from start to finish, with each exchange progressing toward a concrete plan. Closed with a coherent, focused summary.

**Comments:** Fully power-shared; the entire plan was client-generated and questions handed agenda control to the client. Client's closing remark that it 'feels like my own plan' confirms true equality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
