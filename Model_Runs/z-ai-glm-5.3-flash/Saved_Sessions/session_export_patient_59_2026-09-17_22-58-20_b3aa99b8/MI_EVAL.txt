# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental regard toward a dismissive teen, including embracing the 'blame the court' idea with humor and normalizing peer pressure without disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing minimization, ambivalence, and peer pressure nuance. Emotional undercurrents like fear of disappointing his mother and the 'sober one' identity strain were acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every turn stayed focused on the target behavior and advanced a clear trajectory from agenda-setting through ambivalence exploration to problem-solving and future vision, even when the client deflected.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session unfolded as joint problem-solving between equals, with brainstorming invited, the client's own ideas embraced, and strategies left for him to evaluate and select.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Jamal's ownership and choice with no advice-giving or pressure; all solutions came from the client and were validated as his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within the client — his own reasons, coping strategies, and confidence language — producing spontaneous commitment talk ('That'd be worth it') in a precontemplative client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Jamal as the expert on his own life, and the plan was built entirely from his generated ideas rather than counselor-imposed solutions — model power-sharing with a resistant adolescent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation of change talk: eliciting Jamal's own plan, highlighting and deepening commitment language, brainstorming prompts, and a future-vision question that yielded strong change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally effective double-sided reflections that acknowledged sustain talk without amplifying it, though some responses restated minimizations at length and missed chances to actively reframe them before pivoting.
