# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment despite patient's minimization and defensiveness; affirms his honesty and desire for respect throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections capturing both content and emotion (e.g., 'relief in mostly good news,' frustration at being compared to cigarette smokers); the patient repeatedly builds on them rather than correcting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on cigar use and health impact with zero topic drift; each response ties back to the target behavior while progressing logically toward a plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership: the counselor co-creates the agenda around Ahmed's stated conditions and the plan emerges from his own ideas via invitation-style questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces Ahmed's ownership ('my choice at the end of the day' met with agreement, slip-tolerant framing); no advice or directives—all guidance comes from the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation elicited almost entirely from the patient, surfacing intrinsic reasons (his daughter, self-image); no lecturing or risk-listing by the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Ahmed as the expert ('you know your habits best'); the plan originates from his own ideas drawn out through skilled questioning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions evoke progression from sustain talk to a concrete preparation-level plan (weekend-only use, Monday contingency, wife as ally), consistently reflected and affirmed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk honored but never amplified—handled with neutral double-sided reflections; sustain talk visibly diminishes across the session.
