# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, non-judgmental tone throughout, including around emotional eating and skipped plans. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and repetitive, lacking deeper individualization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured Sofia's experience and ambivalence. Reflections occasionally pivoted too quickly to planning, leaving emotional layers like her fear of failure unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on Sofia's stated goals, moving from vague dissatisfaction to a concrete, specific plan. Later session time drifted into recipe logistics, moving away from the motivational core.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative, with Sofia's ideas directly shaping the plan and options offered as menus. The counselor occasionally took the lead in structuring logistics rather than fully co-creating content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client choice was consistently affirmed, including honoring her wish to troubleshoot barriers herself and respecting her pacing. Lacked explicit reinforcement that success belongs entirely to her, especially during self-doubt about whether ten minutes counts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective open questions elicited Sofia's own reasons, strategies, and values-aligned motivation. The counselor sometimes supplied rationales herself instead of asking Sofia to voice her own predictions or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia was consistently positioned as the expert, with her choices genuinely shaping the session, including honoring her preference to self-troubleshoot. The counselor sometimes supplied plans, drafted messages, and options quickly rather than letting her generate more content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled evocation produced robust client change talk about starting three walks, laying out shoes, and feeling hopeful. Some change talk was not deliberately deepened or strengthened through scaling or elaboration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about ten minutes feeling 'pointless' and past diet failures was acknowledged and reframed, but no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots were used. Some ambivalence was passed over without resolution.
