# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental, but affirmations are sparse and formulaic, with no normalization of Sofia's guilt or past drinking before she knew she was pregnant.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections ('scared and stuck,' 'torn') show decent perspective-taking, but reflective listening fades after the first third as the counselor shifts into rapid advice lists, leaving emotional content like shame and fear unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on stopping alcohol during pregnancy with a logical progression from ambivalence to a concrete plan, though the counselor occasionally scatters focus across too many options at once.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely between equals—permission-seeking, role-plays, and Sofia's preferences shaping the plan—but most content still originates top-down from the counselor's lists rather than from joint elicitation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices framed as Sofia's with permission requested and her selections honored, though the volume of prescriptive suggestions slightly undercuts her ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good opening evocative questions, but mid-session the counselor increasingly supplies answers (scripts, wording, strategies) rather than drawing out Sofia's own ideas; her reasons for change are voiced but not systematically developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission is asked before advising and Sofia's ideas shaped the plan, but persistent bullet-point menus of expert solutions shift the dynamic toward advice-giver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia generated substantial change talk and the counselor created space for it, but key opportunities to deepen or amplify it were missed, with no commitment-strengthening questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** No reinforcement of sustain talk and one decent double-sided reflection, but sustain talk about willpower and fear of folding was bypassed with advice rather than explored or reframed.
