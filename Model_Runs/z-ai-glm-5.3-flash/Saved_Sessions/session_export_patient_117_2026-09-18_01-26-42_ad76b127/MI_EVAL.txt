# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with affirmations like 'There's real insight there, Samuel.' Ambivalence and slips are treated as legitimate rather than criticized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately capture core meaning, including identifying the 'pause' as what Samuel truly values. Emotional nuance, such as the wife's hopeful face versus disappointment, is well attended to.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session progresses clearly from pattern recognition to a concrete first-step plan, with a closing summary tying the threads together. Directional control is excellent without being imposed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan (breaks test, journal tracking, telling his wife) is co-constructed entirely from Samuel's ideas, with power-sharing evident in how his answers shape each next question.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Samuel defines his own success criterion, ritual, and how to involve his wife, with no advice or pressure given. His hesitation ('I'll think about it') is accepted respectfully.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within via questions like 'What do you imagine that proof would change for you?' Samuel ends by articulating his own self-motivating statement and reporting increased readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in Samuel, and his own contributions (stress ball, timer, success criterion) drive the plan. Questions are genuinely curious rather than leading.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic open questions elicit increasingly strong change talk, from 'I could test it next week' to 'I feel a bit more ready for Tuesday.' Each step is reflected and built upon, moving Samuel to concrete commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Double-sided reflections acknowledge sustain talk without reinforcing it, though not always actively softened. Hesitant statements like 'I can't promise I'll do it this weekend' receive only brief reflection rather than exploration pivoting toward commitment.
