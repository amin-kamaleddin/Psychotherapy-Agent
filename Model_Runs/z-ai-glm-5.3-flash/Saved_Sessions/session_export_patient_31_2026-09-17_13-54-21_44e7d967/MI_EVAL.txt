# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating David's evening cigarette boundary with affirmations. However, warmth feels somewhat formulaic, lacking deeper unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some good reflections early on, but the session quickly shifted to bullet-point advice rather than reflective listening. Deeper feelings like fatigue, social awkwardness, and feeling pressured went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing daytime smoking throughout, culminating in a concrete plan with follow-up. Direction leaned toward a counselor-supplied plan rather than goals tied to David's own values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative moments exist (asking permission, inviting choices), but monologue-style information dumps dominated. The plan resembled discharge instructions more than a co-created strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly affirmed David's ownership and accepted his non-negotiable evening boundary without argument. Weakened somewhat by prescriptive, checklist-style advice delivery and repetitive recaps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One strong early values question was abandoned when David redirected to practicalities. David's motivations (money, experimentation) emerged incidentally rather than through deliberate evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Intermittent invitations for David's input, but the session was dominated by expert-delivered pharmacology information. The overall stance leaned advisory rather than treating David as the expert on his own change.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged ('I can give that a shot,' concrete commitments), aided by open questions and invitations to choose limits. But David's change statements were never deliberately deepened or reflected.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was wisely tolerated without argument, keeping David engaged, but it was never strategically softened. Repeated recaps re-emphasized the plan rather than using double-sided reflections.
