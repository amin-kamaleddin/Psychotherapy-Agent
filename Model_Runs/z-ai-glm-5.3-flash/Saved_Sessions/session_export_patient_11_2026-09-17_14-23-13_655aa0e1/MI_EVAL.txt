# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm and non-judgmental, but positive regard was formulaic and cheerleader-like, which the client pushed back on. No individualized affirmations of her strengths, honesty, or insight.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic understanding and accurate restatements were present, but reflective listening was largely absent. Emotionally charged material (jar embarrassment, history of failed attempts) went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on physical activity with a concrete, actionable plan. Slightly diluted by layering extra tasks (grip work, breathing) and delivering direction via advice rather than guided clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements like asking what felt doable and co-adjusting reminder wording, but the plan's core structure was counselor-designed with yes/no approval rather than genuine co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menu-style choices were offered and client adjustments accommodated, but the stance was often directive ('try this plan,' 'add grip exercises'). No explicit affirmation of her ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor relied on the client's pre-existing motivation without ever asking evocative questions about her 'why,' confidence, or hopes. Turns were overwhelmingly informational and logistical.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly assumed the expert role, prescribing plans and checklists before exploring the client's ideas. Client input shaped only marginal details like reminder wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Amira generated rich change talk herself, but the counselor made no deliberate effort to evoke or reinforce it. Her 8/10 readiness rating and 'why' went unexplored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoided amplifying sustain talk by respecting her wish to skip nutrition, but this was by omission rather than skill. No double-sided reflections or strategic use of her hesitations.
