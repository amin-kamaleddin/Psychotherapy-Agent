# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard ('I'm here to listen without judgment'), validated the client's perspective even during minimization, and showed no judgment, labeling, or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured both content and emotion, landing so well the client progressively opened up from guarded minimization to candid self-admissions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained unwavering focus on alcohol use throughout, with each exchange building toward a specific, concrete change plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint enterprise with menu-based options and co-constructed plans; the final plan was composed almost entirely of the client's own words and ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership reinforced throughout—options offered as menus, no pressure or persuasion, and the client made every decision as his confidence rose from 5 to 7.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason for change came from the client himself, developed through open questions and confidence rulers; his closing 'I'm actually feeling decent about this one. Weird.' is direct evidence motivation was elicited, not instilled.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the client as the expert whose own ideas drove the plan, but occasional rapid multi-part question clusters slightly reduced his room to steer.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation of change talk through a self-generated plan, confidence rulers moving the client from 5 to 7, vision questions, and deepened commitment language (specific day, cash limit, accountability person, backup plans).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimizations were acknowledged neutrally rather than challenged, but some reflections restated the sustain side without explicitly reframing it; double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk would strengthen pivoting.
