# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental, supportive tone throughout; discouragement never judged. However, affirmations are somewhat brief and formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures the client's discouragement and realization about enjoyable activity. Reflections are sometimes brief and stacked with multiple questions, and the emotional weight of repeated failure and fear of relapse is not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on physical activity, moving from vague discouragement to a fully specified plan with backup plan and scheduled follow-up, without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently egalitarian tone with co-constructed decisions; the plan was largely driven by the client's own ideas. Occasionally packaged options (text drafts, activity lists) tip into the ideas-provider role rather than fully eliciting the client's solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly and explicitly reinforces the client's ownership, asking permission, offering choices, and affirming the client's own success criteria. Lets the client pick check-in timing and the type of support wanted at follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the client himself — his past successes, reasons, definitions of success, confidence ratings, and plans. The counselor never argues for change or supplies the reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats the client as the expert, with his own ideas substantially driving the plan. Occasionally pre-shapes choices by offering menus of options instead of purely open elicitation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through past-success questions, future imagining, confidence scaling, and self-defined success, producing strong commitment language ('I'm kind of excited about this'). Consistently translates commitment into concrete plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Does not dwell on barriers, reframing with acceptance and pivoting to strengths and solutions. Occasionally restates barriers in reflections before pivoting fully to the change side.
