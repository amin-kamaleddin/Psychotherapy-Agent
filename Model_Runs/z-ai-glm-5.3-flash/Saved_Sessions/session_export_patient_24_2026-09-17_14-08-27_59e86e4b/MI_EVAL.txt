# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalized Daniel's skepticism. Kept from a 5 by warmth that was somewhat brief/formulaic and closing language that presumed a problem ('cravings or slip up').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good early reflections and acknowledgment of his surprise, but little exploration of the emotional side of his defensiveness, athlete identity, or ambivalence. Empathic depth gave way to structured planning as the session progressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on alcohol use throughout and guided toward a concrete behavioral plan without drift. Direction sometimes came from jumping to structured plans rather than emerging through more exploration of ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed with Daniel choosing focus areas, volunteering the dry weekend, and refining measurement details. Early assessment leaned question-answer, and plan templates were provided rather than fully co-designed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, asked permission, and emphasized 'You decide — I'll follow your lead,' framing change as experiments Daniel controls. His closing 'Thanks for not lecturing me' directly confirms he experienced genuine choice and non-coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The importance ruler and linking to marathon goals elicited some client-generated motivation, but evocation was inconsistent. Much of the session involved supplying information and protocols rather than exploring Daniel's own reasons, confidence, or the 'little voice' about his marathon time.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly conveyed that Daniel's expertise mattered, letting him choose focus areas and interpret data collaboratively; his contributions shaped the plan. Fell short of a 5 because the counselor still supplied most of the structure and never explicitly named Daniel as the expert on his own habits.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk via the importance ruler and experiment framing anchored to Daniel's own values (Boston qualifying, training data). Fell short of a 5 with limited deepening of commitment language or explicit confidence exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce Daniel's substantial sustain talk, but mostly sidestepped it rather than actively softening with double-sided reflections. Some closing language ('cravings or slip up') arguably amplified problem framing without resolving ambivalence.
