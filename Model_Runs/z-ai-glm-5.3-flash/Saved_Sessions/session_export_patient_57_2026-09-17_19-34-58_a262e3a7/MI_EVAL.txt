# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with genuine affirmations ('That takes some guts to say out loud'). Jamal's own remarks ('Thanks for not just lecturing me') confirm his experience of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally attuned, often holding ambivalence ('Both of those things are real at the same time'). The counselor repeatedly captures both the pull toward belonging and the worry about consequences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tight, consistent focus on a specific target behavior: avoiding alcohol chugging games. Every exchange advances this, from identifying risky moments to rehearsing the hardest one (midnight peer pressure).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative — every concrete strategy (designated driver, soda in hand, Marcus alliance) was elicited from Jamal, not prescribed. The final plan is entirely built from his ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise deliberately located in the client: 'That's not me telling a rule, that's you figuring out where your limit is.' Jamal's self-defined limit of one or two at celebrations is honored without pushing abstinence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within via open questions ('What do you think could work?', 'which one feels most like something you could actually pull off?'). Jamal produces his own reasons, plan, and even spontaneously requests follow-up — strong evidence of effective evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power-sharing is explicit ('You know the team better than anyone', 'you did the heavy lifting here'). Jamal's closing observation that he 'figured out what I'd actually do' confirms the client shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk systematically evoked and reinforced — the counselor names and amplifies it ('notice what you just did there — you drew your own line'). Jamal's confidence visibly rises across the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (team tradition, peer belonging, 'one or two is different') is honored and reframed rather than argued against. Minor growth edge: occasionally dwelling on sustain talk slightly longer than necessary, e.g., the 'tradition' exchange, though momentum is never lost.
