# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, unconditional regard throughout, with specific, genuine affirmations ('Trusting your own gut like that is a real strength'). No judgment expressed even when the client used deficit language about herself.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that moved from content to meaning, e.g., 'That might be the heart of it — not the gym itself, but proving to yourself that you can follow through.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear, consistent focus on establishing an exercise routine, progressing from activities to concrete days (Saturday YMCA, Wednesday walk) with no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully equal partnership: reflections built directly on the client's language and her ideas visibly shaped each next step; final plan voiced as shared and client-owned.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located in the client ('You know your workday and your body better than anyone') with genuine choices offered and no prescribed activities. Honored her rejection of swimming and contracts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from the client through evocative questions ('What will tell you this one is going to stick?'), producing escalating change talk and her own key insight ('I'm starting smarter'). No advice or persuasion present.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions built on the client's contributions so her answers shaped the session direction; she explicitly credited the counselor's approach for helping her commit.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions systematically elicited desire, reasons, and commitment, yielding strong change talk ('I'm signing up on the spot,' 'I want that back') which was then reinforced and highlighted.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided dwelling on barriers and pivoted to strengths, but a few reflections briefly restated barrier content (sales pressure, weather) without deliberate double-sided reframing toward change. Refinement needed mainly for clients with heavier sustain talk.
