# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Camila's self-awareness. However, affirmations and recaps were formulaic and repeated, reducing the sense of individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some reflections were accurate, but the counselor largely skipped emotional content, jumping to advice when Camila shared fatigue and self-criticism. Deeper reflection of the emotional meaning behind her statements was mostly missing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained unwavering focus on increasing physical activity, progressing logically from motivation exploration to a concrete if-then plan, barriers, and follow-up. There was no topic drift, and each turn advanced the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Camila's contributions were incorporated into the plan, but the counselor frequently delivered expert-style bullet-point instructions. Genuine two-way brainstorming was limited, with Camila mostly accepting or adjusting the counselor's suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice-oriented questions at the end of turns respected autonomy, but the overall style was highly directive, with unsolicited prescriptive advice in nearly every message. Permission to advise was never sought, and Camila repeatedly indicated she could handle things herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions effectively elicited Camila's motivation and her own solutions, like walking with her neighbor and treadmill backup. Evocation was front-loaded, though, as the counselor shifted to providing information and scripts later in the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Camila's ideas shaped the plan, showing some power-sharing, but the counselor often assumed the expert role with prescriptive tip lists. Even when Camila demonstrated her own competence, the counselor continued offering scripts rather than trusting her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Importance/confidence rulers successfully elicited change talk, and asking about a small goal and start date evoked commitment language. However, the counselor did not deepen the change talk with follow-ups like 'Why a 7 and not a 4?' to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not amplify sustain talk and normalized energy fluctuations appropriately. But when Camila expressed doubt about quitting, the response was neutral accommodation rather than skillful softening with double-sided reflections or strategic pivoting.
