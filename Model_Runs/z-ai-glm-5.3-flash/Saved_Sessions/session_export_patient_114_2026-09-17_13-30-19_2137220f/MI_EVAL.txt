# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, normalizing Ryan's annoyance and minimizing. However, affirmations of his honesty and insight are largely absent, and warmth sometimes feels procedural.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; early turns string together multiple questions, and emotionally rich material (roommate's fear, broken promises, ambivalence) goes unreflected. Empathy is limited to brief labels rather than deep reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays well-focused on alcohol use and the conduct referral with concrete next steps. However, direction is task-focused on logistics rather than building motivational foundations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ryan's input shapes outputs (text choice, statement edits, alarm detail), but the counselor generates most content while Ryan merely reacts. Co-creation is limited; the counselor proposes and the client edits.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some autonomy support via choices and experimental framing, but the counselor is notably directive—handing over checklists, scripts, and pre-written texts before Ryan articulates his own goals.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation emerges only when Ryan volunteers it; the counselor never explores his reasons for change, values-behavior discrepancy, or meaning of the incident. Golden evocation moments are met with more alternative statements instead.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Despite inviting Ryan's agenda initially, the psychiatrist quickly assumes the expert role with pre-made options rather than drawing out Ryan's own ideas. Power sharing occurs despite, not because of, the counselor's approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ryan generates meaningful change talk ('something I can actually control,' valuing education), but the counselor never explicitly evokes or amplifies it. Evocation is incidental rather than strategic.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and some reframes work well (leaving earlier without losing social life), but key sustain talk ('how everyone my age has fun') is bypassed rather than explored. Softening happens by avoidance.
