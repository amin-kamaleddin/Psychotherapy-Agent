# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and non-judgmental; Sofia's dismissiveness was met with acceptance rather than pushback. However, warmth was somewhat formulaic and ambivalence was never explicitly normalized or affirmed as a person beyond her cooperation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate summaries and good content reflections, but reflections rarely explored emotional nuance. When Sofia expressed low importance and tiredness, the counselor moved to problem-solving rather than reflecting the meaning behind it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A significant weakness: the session was initiated after a positive alcohol-risk screen, yet alcohol was never mentioned. The counselor entirely followed Sofia's drift toward minor homework issues, missing the clinically indicated target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked permission, co-constructed the plan from Sofia's own choices, and checked in repeatedly. Sometimes filled space with pre-packaged suggestions rather than letting Sofia generate more content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Sofia's ownership with 'no pressure,' 'whatever feels right,' and full respect for her preference to work independently. Options were offered throughout without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used scaling questions effectively, but frequently shifted into advice-giving with menus of ideas, supplying content rather than evoking it. Sofia's low importance rating (3) was never explored for her own reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Sofia as the expert, and her preferences directly shaped the plan. Slightly limited by reliance on pre-packaged option menus rather than open elicitation of her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used scaling questions and barrier/solution prompts to elicit ability statements and raise confidence from 6 to 7. The low importance rating (3) was not explored to develop desire/need or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and labeled it 'normal ambivalence.' However, reflections sometimes amplified sustain talk by restating it verbatim without double-sided pivots toward change, and minimization was not strategically redirected.
