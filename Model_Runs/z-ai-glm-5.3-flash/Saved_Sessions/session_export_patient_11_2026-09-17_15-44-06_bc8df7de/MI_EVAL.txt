# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with warm affirmations throughout, but some affirmations feel generic and there is little explicit unconditional acceptance around her history of quitting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early reflections capturing both content and meaning, including her fear of burnout and guilt concerns; however, reflections thinned mid-session as logistics took over, leaving emotional themes unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear and consistent focus on the physical activity target, with each exchange building toward a concrete, actionable plan and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine back-and-forth and frequent check-ins, but the counselor tends to propose solutions first and then asks the client to pick, rather than eliciting her ideas before offering suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices framed as Amira's with no pressure and tweakable plans offered, but the approach is largely menu-driven rather than inviting her to generate her own ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira's motivations emerged mostly spontaneously; the counselor rarely asked why change matters, didn't explore her 8/10 readiness, and focused energy on logistics rather than amplifying internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing through asking her input and preferences, but the counselor slips into an expert stance with pre-set plans and scripts rather than positioning her as the authority on her own change.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira offered abundant change talk, but the counselor did little to deliberately evoke or deepen it, quickly moving to options and logistics instead of exploring her motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately steered around hesitation and reframed missed days without reinforcing sustain talk, but the softening was passive, with no double-sided reflections to help resolve her enthusiasm-versus-fear tension.
