### Acceptance

**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with genuine and specific affirmations. Rosa's self-doubt is consistently met with validation, e.g., reframing 'am I making excuses?' as smart pacing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, deep reflections capturing both emotion and meaning, including the identity dimension of quitting and walking replacing smoking's head-clearing function. No misattunements evident.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive focus from doctor's warning to a concrete commitment, with each exchange moving the agenda forward. Guidance respects Rosa's stated readiness without pushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals, with all plan elements (walking, Denise, two-week criterion) originating from Rosa. The psychiatrist checks in and visibly incorporates her ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice actively reinforced; Rosa's pacing honored ('walking isn't a quitting promise yet'). No advice or pressure; the final question leaves coping strategy entirely to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation systematically drawn from Rosa — imagined-future questions yield her own reasons, deepened into a personally meaningful motivator (her mother's breathing). Her motivation visibly grows within the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in Rosa ('that's your own wisdom showing, not something I handed you'). The psychiatrist offers only questions and reflections, never advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout use of evocative open questions with strategic reinforcement of Rosa's change language. The arc from vague ambivalence to a concrete plan (text Denise tonight) is skillfully constructed.
