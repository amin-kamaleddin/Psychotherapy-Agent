# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, specific affirmations and unconditional, nonjudgmental regard throughout; Thomas's minimization and skepticism were honored rather than corrected, and acceptance was never contingent on commitment to change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately captured both meaning and emotion. Points lost because emotionally rich material (guilt about letting his daughter down, the 'sleepwalking through weekends' realization) could have been reflected more explicitly before moving on.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus on alcohol use and weekend patterns tied to Thomas's values, with no topic drift. Each turn advanced the conversation toward a concrete, client-owned next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange between equals, with Thomas's input sought at every decision point and directly shaping the session trajectory. No expert-driven advice anywhere in the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly reinforced throughout ('on your terms,' 'which feels most doable'), with no prescriptions and full deference to Thomas's choices, including his rejection of strategies that felt 'preachy.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason and strategy came from Thomas himself; the counselor never supplied motivation. The session culminated in spontaneous self-generated insight — the hallmark of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Thomas was consistently treated as the expert on his own life, with the entire plan built from his words and the counselor positioned as a curious collaborator. Reflects the highest level of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation and amplification of change talk, from the opening values-based question through elaboration, a confidence ruler, and strengths exploration. Thomas's change talk deepened progressively into self-generated insight.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain statements were never argued with or reinforced; each was met with a brief acknowledgment and a strategic pivot toward change-relevant material. Sustain talk diminished over the session.
