# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated warmth and unconditional positive regard, never reacting judgmentally to Lucía's defensiveness or boundary-testing. Calmly affirmed 'I won't lecture' and accepted her complaints and jokes without defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, nuanced reflections capturing both the emotional and functional meaning of smoking (e.g., the 'pocket of calm' vs. grandmother's illness and daughter's fear). The client visibly responded with increasing openness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, progressive focus on smoking without lecturing, moving from framing to ambivalence to a concrete behavioral experiment. Each turn moved the conversation forward with no meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was genuinely co-constructed, with options offered as menus and the final plan combining counselor suggestions with Lucía's adaptations. The client confirmed the collaborative stance was felt ('I like that you're not jumping straight into it').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly reinforced throughout with permission-based advice and 'You're in charge' used naturally. Lucía retained full ownership of the plan, timing, and backup strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation entirely from within the client — her own reasons, solutions, timeline, and predictions of follow-through. The counselor never argued for quitting, which is the hallmark of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Lucía as the expert with permission-seeking and explicitly optional suggestions. The final plan was built entirely from her own contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk using a paired like/worry question, then amplified it. Lucía generated her own ideas and made commitment-like statements by the end ('Tomorrow, lunch, phone call... I'll try it').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk and pivoted toward change. Minor deduction for giving considerable airtime to barriers (teasing comebacks, backup plans), which slightly increased focus on reasons against change.
