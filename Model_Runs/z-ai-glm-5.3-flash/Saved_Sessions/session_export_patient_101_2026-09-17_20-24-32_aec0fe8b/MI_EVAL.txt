# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, with affirmations and a final reflection separating Mei from her family's history. Falls short of 5 only because some affirmations felt slightly formulaic rather than individually calibrated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured both ambivalence and emotional nuance, including the family-history fear at the close. A 5 would require exploring the deeper emotional weight earlier in the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus moving from exploration to analysis to a concrete self-chosen experiment with planned follow-up. Every reflection and question advanced the change agenda without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely between equals—asking permission, inviting Mei's ideas first, and co-constructing the follow-up around her preferences. Mei's input directly shaped the session's course, including the 'offer moment' focus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Mei's ownership of choices, with setbacks framed as data rather than failure. No coercive or pressuring language anywhere.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from within the client via open questions, evocative probing, and confidence rulers. Every insight emerged from Mei's own words—no lecturing or counselor-supplied reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Mei as the expert, and the entire plan was self-generated. Mei explicitly confirmed this: 'Thanks for not handing me a whole program.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk through strategic confidence rulers and immediate reflection of Mei's motivating language. Session built from vague intent to a concrete, self-generated experiment—exemplary.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged but never reinforced, with effective redirections that helped Mei test fears against reality. Some reflections repeated her worry language verbatim before pivoting; more double-sided reflections weighted toward change would earn a 5.
