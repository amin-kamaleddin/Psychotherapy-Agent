# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opened without judgment and consistently validated Sofia's frustration, even when she disclosed skipping classes. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic, with limited explicit affirmation of her personal strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Sofia's perspective, including the unfairness of being blamed and her desire to avoid school. Occasionally moved too quickly to questions/suggestions rather than exploring the deeper hurt and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus on reducing avoidance throughout, with every exchange moving the plan forward (friend, text wording, timing, no-reply plan, reminder). Session ended with a clear, self-stated action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked permission, offered choices, and co-constructed a plan driven by Sofia's preferences. However, the counselor generated most of the options and scripts herself rather than eliciting Sofia's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy through repeated menus of choice, explicit permission to modify suggestions, and no pressure when Sofia declined rehearsing aloud. Sofia's final ownership of the plan reflects this.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked motivation via open questions, confidence rulers, and coping preferences, with Sofia producing genuine change talk. Some content (scripts, wording) was supplied rather than evoked, and her own reasons for reconnecting could have been explored more.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Sofia as decision-maker, and her contributions shaped the final plan. Slightly limited by fairly prescriptive scripts and coping menus rather than first eliciting her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilfully evoked change talk through open questions and confidence rulers, tracking Sofia's confidence rising from 4 to 6. Could have deepened change talk further with elaborative questions about why the plan felt less scary.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged Sofia's sustain talk without reinforcing or arguing with it, gently pivoting toward what she could control. No explicit double-sided reflections were used, and the blame narrative was never gently examined.
