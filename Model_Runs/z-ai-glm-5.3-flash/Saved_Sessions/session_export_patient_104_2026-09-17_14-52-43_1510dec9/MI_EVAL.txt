# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, normalization, and compassionate reframing of self-blame; affirmations of Nadine's own strengths were somewhat sparse and generic, keeping this just below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured her exhaustion and worry, but deeper emotions (feeling like a failure, relational strain with boyfriend and critical mother) were not explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus: the session moved coherently from normalization to a concrete target, action plan, and structured follow-up without drift, while respecting Nadine's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative joint problem-solving: Nadine chose the strategy, shaped the script's tone, requested the checklist, and set the follow-up schedule.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client ownership through options rather than directives; Nadine's own decisions drove every element of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Nadine's own motivation and plan language, though deliberate evocation of deeper values and strengths was limited amid counselor-provided information.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally treated Nadine as the expert and adapted to her input, but at times slid into an advice-giving expert role without asking permission, making power-sharing occasionally uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked and reinforced change language culminating in commitment ('Pump tonight... I can do that'), though more explicit confidence- and hope-focused evocation would elevate this.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated without amplification and reframed compassionately, with practical redirection to doable steps; occasional echoing of problem language slightly delayed pivots.
