# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and non-judgment, including affirmations and framing slips as information rather than failure. Warmth was occasionally brief/formulaic with limited normalization of her ambivalence or defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately captured her ambivalence, e.g., the nightly 'me time' vs. guilt, plus her practical constraints. Reflections sometimes summarized rather than deepened emotional content, notably guilt about time with her son and fear of losing pleasures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and stress coping throughout with no topic drift. The session progressed purposefully toward a defined target behavior and weekly check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every plan element (tea, high-risk nights, timer, goal) came from the client, with co-construction and invitation to edit the social worker message. The result was a genuinely client-generated plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Permission-asking, a client-chosen 'small experiment' framing, and emphasis on slips as information exemplified MI autonomy support. No directive or prescriptive advice about drinking was given.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was largely elicited from the client via open questions and rulers, producing her own commitment language. Some turns bundled multiple questions at once, diluting evocation, and next steps occasionally preceded full development of emerging motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client was positioned as the expert and all key plan elements were hers. The counselor did take structural lead in drafting the message and setting check-ins, with only brief consultation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and reinforcement of change talk, with confidence rising from 7 to 8 and her self-generated plan affirmed. Emerging change talk was not always deepened before moving on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with neutral validating reflections and pivots to her own concerns rather than argument. Double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk were underused.
