# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental stance throughout, validating that inactivity is 'about circumstances, not willpower' and honoring Farid's courage. Could have affirmed his small self-generated steps more explicitly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurate, including complex double-sided reflections. A few reflections were slightly leading, and the fear of 'proving himself right' could have been explored with more emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on increasing physical activity with a clear arc from ambivalence to concrete steps (texting Dev, calendar structure). Every question built on the previous exchange with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative between equals — the clinician framed problems as shared puzzles and every plan element came from Farid himself. The tone was curious, non-hierarchical, and genuinely two-way.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Farid's ownership and accommodated his correction without pushing. 'When would you send that text' slightly assumed the action, and his freedom to decide could have been stated more explicitly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful eliciting of Farid's own insights — accountability, Dev, and the calendar strategy all emerged from him. The session reads as Farid talking himself into change, the hallmark of high-level evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook power-sharing: expertise located in the client and all solutions originated from Farid. His contributions demonstrably shaped the session's entire direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent open questions tied to Farid's values produced steadily building change talk ('it feels possible, more than an hour ago'). The clinician deepened each statement rather than supplying solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated briefly and redirected toward agency, with the 'two voices' question skillfully externalizing ambivalence. Occasionally validation gave sustain talk a bit more emphasis than necessary before pivoting.
