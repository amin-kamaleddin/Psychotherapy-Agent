# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with warm, normalizing affirmations. However, affirmations were brief and skill-focused rather than expressing deeper positive regard for Alexei as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening, accurately capturing Alexei's minimization and underlying fears. Reflections were often paired with questions or suggestions, and deeper emotional content was acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior tied directly to Alexei's stated values. Every exchange served the goal, and the session progressed logically from exploration to rehearsal to planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highly collaborative in middle and later phases through joint role-plays and co-created scripts. However, the brainstorming phase was noticeably counselor-driven, with the counselor supplying the strategy list and script menus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Alexei's ownership of every decision, with choices repeatedly offered rather than imposed. Alexei freely rejected options, which the counselor adopted without resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation through goal-linking questions and the confidence ruler, producing strong client-generated commitment language. Early phases leaned on counselor-provided solutions, and the uncle disclosure was not explored further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Alexei as the expert on his own life, with his input genuinely shaping the session direction. The initial strategy menu was counselor-generated rather than co-created, making Alexei reactive to options early on.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited strong change talk by linking alcohol to Alexei's own goals, producing client-generated motivation. Missed opportunities to deepen and reinforce change talk when it appeared, such as the uncle's drinking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk, using an effective double-sided reflection to pivot from minimization to goals. Early redirection relied on moving quickly to strategies rather than strategically softening ambivalence.
