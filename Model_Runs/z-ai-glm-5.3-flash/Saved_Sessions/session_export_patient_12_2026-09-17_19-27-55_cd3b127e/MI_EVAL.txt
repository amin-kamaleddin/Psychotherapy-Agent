**Comments:** Sustain talk ("helpless," "nobody's really there," "too far gone") acknowledged and validated but never reinforced. Not a 5 because early exchanges stayed within the hopeless frame before redirection, and phrases like "why bother" were reflected back verbatim, briefly amplifying sustain talk before softening.

**Overall Global Ratings Score: 4.5/5**

**Summary:** A strong MI session, particularly impressive given the client's flat affect, precontemplative hopelessness, and substantial sustain talk. Excels at evoking client-owned change talk, converting it into a concrete, self-designed plan. Autonomy support and collaboration outstanding; every element of the plan originated from Amira. Areas for growth: (1) linger longer with the emotional weight of hopelessness before pivoting to solutions; (2) avoid pre-shaping options (e.g., suggesting a group); (3) make affirmations more specific and less formulaic.
