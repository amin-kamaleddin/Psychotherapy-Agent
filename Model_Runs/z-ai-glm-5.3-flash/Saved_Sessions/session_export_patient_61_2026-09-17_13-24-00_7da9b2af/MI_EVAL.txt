### Acceptance

**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations like 'nice call' and 'No judgment if you slip.' Loses a point for formulaic, repetitive warmth (e.g., 'thanks for committing' repeated) and a generic crisis disclaimer.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some accurate reflections (soda as pick-me-up, feeling swamped), but misses deeper emotional content like Jonathan's fear of failure and skepticism. Reflections stay surface-level as the counselor jumps to tips and plans.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on one concrete target behavior, successfully negotiating a week-long plan down to a 3-day test without drifting. Loses a point because the focus is pushed via a pre-formed agenda rather than collaboratively confirmed as Jonathan's priority.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers menu options and co-decides check-in format, but the stance is largely expert-driven with the counselor supplying nearly all strategies. Jonathan's sarcasm about repeated recaps suggests he experienced the session as one-sided.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and normalizes slips, respecting Jonathan's 'that's all I'm agreeing to' boundary. The excessive repetition of tips despite his pushback ('Twice, honestly') slightly undermines his control over the session's pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely elicits Jonathan's own motivations, with no open questions about his values or reasons for managing his diabetes. His engagement is driven by the counselor's structure rather than personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some power sharing by letting Jonathan pick the soda, timing, and check-in format. However, the exchange is dominated by counselor-generated tips and recaps, with little exploration of Jonathan's own ideas—his nuts-at-desk suggestion is absorbed rather than highlighted.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully evokes change talk ('I'll give it a shot,' 'I'll show up for it') through concrete planning. But these moments are never deepened or reinforced—no exploration of why the sparkling option appeals or what success would mean.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Gently counters fear of failure with 'slips are okay' language, but frequent sustain talk ('what's the point,' 'this is pointless') is largely left unaddressed. Misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframing doubt into incremental change discussion.
