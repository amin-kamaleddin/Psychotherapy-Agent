### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent, specific affirmations, though praise occasionally centers the plan or insight rather than unconditional regard for Derrick himself, including his ambivalence and past relapse.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including an excellent early double-sided reflection, but emotional undercurrents (isolation, the relationship ending, shame around the second DUI) were captured cognitively rather than reflected emotionally.

### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving throughout, with the plan co-constructed at nearly every turn and Derrick's revisions welcomed, so his investment visibly grew as the session progressed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly reinforced with pervasive choice language, and the clinician honored Derrick's rejections and rewrites while affirming his ownership—culminating in 'I can send that now, actually.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Virtually every strategy, commitment, and insight came from Derrick's own mouth, with skilled confidence-ruler follow-ups extracting his own reasons for hope and his deeper motivation about social belonging.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Derrick consistently positioned as the expert, with his contributions shaping the plan at each turn, though frequent menu-format suggestions steer somewhat rather than purely drawing out his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook change talk cultivation using confidence rulers twice, future-oriented questions, and brainstorming prompts, moving Derrick from 6 to 8 confidence and eliciting concrete commitments in his own words.

**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with and was handled well with an early double-sided reflection and forward redirection, but minimizations like 'it's just beer' and 'bad luck' were never revisited or strategically softened.
