# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, but praise is generic and repetitive; misses chances to affirm Amira's specific strengths like her insight that 'the decision is the hard part.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens with a good complex reflection, but reflections largely vanish afterward, replaced by rapid logistical questions. Emotional material ('hard on myself since the move,' pride in the plan feeling real) is never reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with every exchange advancing the walking plan. Occasionally becomes interrogation rather than guided exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Solicits her preferences and lets her edit the reminder, but the overall structure resembles a checklist interview with stacked questions and an agenda the counselor sequences unilaterally.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices and respects Amira's decisions, including declining help setting things up. However, repeated demands for exact times and confidence re-polls edge toward controlling the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is largely assumed rather than elicited; questions are overwhelmingly logistical. Amira's own 'why' (strength, body confidence, mood, feeling at home) is never drawn out or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some partnership language, but granular micro-monitoring (exact times, repeated confidence polls, rewriting her text) positions the counselor as director — Amira ultimately says 'I've got it' and corrects the suggested text herself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira offers abundant change talk and the counselor occasionally affirms it, but it's never systematically deepened — no elaboration, no reflecting change statements back, no exploring how success would feel.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Doesn't amplify barriers, which is appropriate, but the recurring 'what might get in the way' question invites barrier focus rather than pivoting to strengths. Misses chances to reframe the snooze concern by affirming her self-generated solutions.
