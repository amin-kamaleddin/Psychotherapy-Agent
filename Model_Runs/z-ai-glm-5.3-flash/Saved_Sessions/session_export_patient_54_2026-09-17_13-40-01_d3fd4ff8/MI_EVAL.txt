# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance maintained even when Isabella is dismissive and challenging. Validation of her autonomy is present, though affirmation is mostly task-focused and warmth feels somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on, but reflections are sparse and often skipped in favor of psychoeducation. Key emotional moments—her sense of loss over wine being 'something that's mine' and fear about the baby—go largely unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy with logical progression to concrete steps: resources, husband's message, refusal lines, and follow-up plan. Focus never drifts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration exists (co-editing the husband's message, offering options), but the middle of the session is counselor-led content delivery with substantial unsolicited information before exploring her own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A clear strength, well-matched to this autonomy-driven client—choice emphasized throughout and her edit of the message accepted. Minor deduction for delivering strategy lists and recommendations without first eliciting her ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies primarily on information delivery rather than drawing out motivation. Isabella's powerful change talk emerged spontaneously; evocative questions tied to her values (healthy baby, good parenting, autonomy) were rarely asked and opportunities were missed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Isabella's input and respects her preferences, but much of the session consists of counselor-driven advice delivery (question lists, statistics, strategies). She is not consistently framed as the expert on her own change process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Isabella generated strong change talk, but largely through her own processing rather than deliberate evocation. Key openings (e.g., 'part of me already wonders') were met with facts instead of deepened exploration, and change talk was not systematically reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk was mostly met with facts and drinking guidelines rather than double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance, risking reactance in this autonomy-focused client. Little active redirection toward change talk.
