# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with frequent, specific affirmations ("That's your insight, not your sister's"). Kept from a 5 by affirmations tied mainly to change content rather than the person, and limited emotional attunement to Brandon's frustration with his sister's pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is generally accurate, including a good complex reflection capturing ambivalence about looking "lame" versus fearing embarrassment. Kept from a 5 by one notable error — asserting an unstated "fifth beer" detail the client had to correct — and limited reflection of the emotional undercurrent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on Thursday-night drinking, progressing logically from defensiveness to concrete strategies and implementation planning, with effective transitional summaries. Minor deduction for the "fifth beer" premise that momentarily misdirected, though the clinician recovered well.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is genuinely dialogic, with the plan co-constructed entirely from Brandon's own ideas and his correction accepted non-defensively. Kept from a 5 because the clinician sometimes steers the direction, notably the leading "fifth beer" question that introduced content the client hadn't offered.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support: the plan is explicitly framed as Brandon's own, choice is repeatedly handed back to him, and no advice is imposed. Minor deduction because questions funnel toward Thursday implementation without leaving open space to choose not to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician consistently draws motivation from the client's own experience and never lectures, eliciting desire, ability, and commitment talk. Kept from a 5 only because one evocation attempt was built on the false "fifth beer" premise, showing evocation not perfectly grounded in listening.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Power sharing is explicit and genuine — Brandon's ideas shape the entire plan, and his correction is accepted gracefully. Slightly withheld from a 5 because the clinician sometimes reframes his contributions in the clinician's own words rather than preserving his language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Near-exemplary change talk cultivation: skillful open questions evoke rich client-generated motivation ("proof I can still handle my own life," "the off switch still works"), which is then strategically reflected and deepened. Culminates in genuine commitment language and implementation planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk and minimization are met with neutral or double-sided reflections rather than argument, accepting the social-benefit framing while pivoting to the identified problem. Kept from a 5 because some sustain talk (e.g., "not swearing off anything") passes without a deliberate reframe.
