# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, explicitly validating Isabella's autonomy ('I respect that it's your choice') and responding to her skepticism without judgment. Role-play feedback was genuinely affirming, though some affirmations were brief rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate, including a strong double-sided reflection capturing her ambivalence between wine as connection/identity and worry for her baby. However, emotional moments (her 2 a.m. worry, 'giving up a piece of who I am') were passed over quickly in favor of information-giving rather than deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy from the opening question through a concrete same-night plan, progressing logically from ambivalence to evidence to action steps. Direction was firm but never coercive, culminating in her clear commitment: 'Tonight... sparkling juice at supper, wine out of the house.'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative — the counselor co-constructed take-home materials, offered options (fact sheet, links, script), invited tweaks, and integrated Isabella's ideas (firm tone, sparkling juice ritual) into the plan. Her contributions substantially shaped the session's direction, a hallmark of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength: the counselor explicitly acknowledged choice from the outset, asked permission before sharing information, and framed all decisions as Isabella's. Her own words ('it has to be my decision — nobody's forcing me,' 'Thanks for not just lecturing me') confirm her commitment was entirely self-authored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within via open questions and a confidence scaling question, eliciting her own values ('being a good parent matters more than any tradition') and solutions. However, the pivotal shift relied heavily on counselor-supplied facts; deeper elicitation of her own reasons before providing information would have earned a 5.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Isabella as the expert on her own life, soliciting her preferences on materials, tone, and role-play while deferring to her choices at every step. Her decisions — tailoring the script, selecting the firm tone, choosing option B — genuinely drove the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced commitment language through open questions ('what would feel most doable for you next?') and a 0–10 confidence scale, and affirmed her role-play success as 'a real win.' Could have deepened change talk further by elaborating on what 'being a good parent' means to her and exploring the meaning behind her 7 rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk without arguing or amplifying it, reflecting skepticism neutrally and asking permission before sharing risk information rather than debating. Missed an opportunity to strategically amplify her own insight — 'keeping the ritual, losing the wine' — as a bridge to change.
