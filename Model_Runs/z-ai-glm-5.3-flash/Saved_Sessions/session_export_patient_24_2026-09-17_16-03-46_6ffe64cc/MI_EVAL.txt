### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, framing the plan as Daniel's experiment with explicit reassurance like 'no pressure to change beyond that.' Falls short of a 5 due to generic, procedural warmth lacking personalized affirmations of Daniel's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured Daniel's rationalizations and pride in training, which he confirmed. Empathy thinned later, with his defensiveness and worry about being judged acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The drinking-marathon goal anchored the session and progressed logically from ruler to plan to follow-up. The final third drifted into administrative logistics, and Daniel's lingering skepticism was never revisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine two-sided interaction with permission-seeking, menu options, and a plan co-constructed from Daniel's choices (nights, tracking method, reminder text). Some instruction-heavy segments slightly narrowed the joint back-and-forth.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: the session was framed as Daniel's own experiment with 'no pressure,' genuine choices at every step, and acceptance of his negotiated terms. Only minor prompts to keep instructions clear detracted slightly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The importance/confidence rulers and experiment framing successfully drew out Daniel's own tentative motivation ('maybe I learn something'). Evocation was mostly structural rather than conversational, with little exploration of his deeper values beyond the marathon frame.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Real power-sharing: Daniel's expertise shaped the plan's format, his negotiation was accepted, and his skepticism was treated as legitimate input. The psychoeducation and tips segments flowed one-way with little invitation for Daniel's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The rulers and experiment framing elicited some change-adjacent language ('maybe I learn something'), but evocation was sparse relative to heavy information-giving. Daniel's closing change talk was never reflected or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was reflected neutrally without reinforcement, and the double-sided opening planted discrepancy well. However, no strategic techniques (amplification, exploring costs) were used, and Daniel's closing defensiveness passed without redirection.
