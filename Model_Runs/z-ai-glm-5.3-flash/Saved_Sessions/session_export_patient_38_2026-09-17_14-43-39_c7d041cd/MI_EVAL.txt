# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, directly reassuring Elena ('I won't judge') at her moment of vulnerability and normalizing her feelings without any labeling or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and consistent (e.g., capturing her relief, embarrassment, and ambivalence), but reflections occasionally stayed generic and didn't deepen into the emotional core, such as partner pressure and fear of seeming 'a nag'.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear arc from the pregnancy scare toward contraception planning, with transitions tied to the target behavior. Clinically appropriate detours (safety check, grounding, feelings-naming) somewhat diluted the tight behavioral focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functioned as equal partners through permission-seeking, choice menus, and offering information only on request. Menus, while respectful, structured the conversation more than fully co-constructing it with Elena's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was strongly and repeatedly supported: offering choices at every step, respecting her boundary ('I'm not promising to pick anything'), and providing balanced information including side effects with no pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited Elena's own motivations (fear of another scare, desire for something 'just mine') rather than supplying them. Moved to information-giving fairly quickly rather than deepening her reasons and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Elena as the decision-maker ('You're in control of this conversation') with neutral, on-request information. Sometimes steered via pre-packaged menu options rather than letting her contributions shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively built on her natural change talk ('I dodged a bullet') with menus connecting her fears to concrete steps, and Elena progressed from 'maybe another time' to asking about the shot and clinics. Did not explicitly deepen her commitment language or use confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by accepting hesitation without pushing ('It's okay to take this slowly') and pivoting to lower-threat options to keep engagement alive. Occasionally, method-focused questions kept some attention on her hesitations, though sustain talk was never amplified.
