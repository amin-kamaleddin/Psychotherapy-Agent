# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific, genuine affirmations ('the kind voice was in there all along—it just needed a job') and no judgment even around struggles like skipping days.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including double-sided reflections and meaning-level reflections ('Same directness, completely different message') that Erik repeatedly confirms ('That's exactly it').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on gym consistency, comparison discouragement, and diet, progressing logically from exploration to strategy to planning and a concrete follow-up commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully equal partnership: Erik designed the tracking system and note himself, and the counselor adapted his 'math' insight into the gym context via questions rather than instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine choices offered ('plan now, or trust yourself in the moment?') and no imposed advice; Erik's plan was entirely self-generated ('Nobody handed me any of it').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary evocation—nearly every turn was an open question drawing out Erik's own wisdom and motivation, culminating in his own theory of change ('It only counted once I said it myself').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Erik positioned as the expert on himself ('let me ask you as the expert on yourself'), with all intervention elements originating from his own ideas and explicitly credited back to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation of commitment and ability language that grew stronger across the session ('I'll start this week,' 'I'll tell you how Thursday goes'), with existing change talk amplified and self-generated strategies reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification and often reframed as strengths, but was sometimes absorbed into reflections rather than actively pivoted toward change language.
