# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental with affirmations present, but affirmations are formulaic and repeated nearly verbatim ('You sound ready/organized/motivated'), lacking individualized depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Leila's autonomy needs and ambivalence well, but reflections were often shallow, quickly followed by advice, and key emotional openings (her all-or-nothing pattern, frustration with sluggishness) went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tight focus on exercise with a concrete plan (Thursday YMCA visit, walk, backup, check-in); the weakness is focus on logistics over consolidating motivation and a circular, repetitive ending.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Interaction was friendly and incorporated client input, but the counselor acted as planner and problem-solver—supplying backups, scripts, and rewards—making it feel more like coaching than genuine co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were consistently framed as Leila's with no pressure, which she explicitly appreciated; however, the counselor frequently supplied options and scripts rather than eliciting Leila's own ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all ideas, scripts, and solutions originated from the counselor; open-ended questions exploring why this matters, values, or past successes were largely absent, relying on Leila's pre-existing motivation instead of actively evoking it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asked for input, but pre-loaded option menus and rapid-fire suggestions conveyed an expert, coach-like stance; power-sharing resulted more from Leila's self-direction than deliberate handover of expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Leila produced abundant natural change talk, but the counselor failed to deepen or amplify it, repeatedly pivoting to logistics and summarizing rather than exploring her motivations and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers were not dwelt upon and the counselor quickly redirected to solutions without reinforcing sustain talk, though problem-solving was done for the client rather than eliciting her own resolutions, and the all-or-nothing worry went unaddressed.
