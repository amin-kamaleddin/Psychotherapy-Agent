# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; Richard's minimization and ambivalence were normalized and affirmed rather than criticized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective statements captured his values and barriers, though emotional meanings like his fear of 'slowing her down' were never explicitly reflected, and reflections often led into question batteries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Laser focus on walking as the entry point to cholesterol management, with every exchange advancing a concrete plan (timing, duration, cues, backups) and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely egalitarian with Richard generating most of the plan, though rapid multi-question exchanges at times gave the session a checklist feel rather than mutual exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Richard owned all key decisions, but practical advice (knee tips, phone timer) was sometimes delivered without asking permission, slightly shifting toward a directing stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation of Richard's own values (national parks, keeping up with his wife) and self-generated insights, culminating in strong commitment language with no lecturing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client-generated plans were central (shoe cue, if-then backup), but some practical suggestions were offered expert-style without first eliciting Richard's own ideas or asking permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent use of values questions and confidence rulers producing measurable escalation from 6 to 8, with Richard voicing increasingly strong commitment ('Yeah, I'll do it. One night at a time.').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk and excuses were acknowledged briefly and redirected toward solutions without amplification, though some 'what might get in the way' questions invited additional barrier talk.
