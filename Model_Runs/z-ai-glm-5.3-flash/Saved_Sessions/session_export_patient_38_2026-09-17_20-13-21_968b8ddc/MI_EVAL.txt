# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout — sustain talk is met with validation ('Both of those make sense') rather than judgment, and the patient's closing thanks for 'not pushing' confirms she felt fully accepted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections capture both content and feeling, e.g., 'It sounds like that scare left a real mark on you' and 'living with that monthly anxiety has been taking a real toll.' Reflections consistently demonstrate deep understanding of Elena's specific perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on contraception decisions, progressing logically from ambivalence to a concrete step (talking to her friend) with no topic drift. Every question and reflection ties back to the target behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor operates as an equal partner — 'we could figure out next steps together' — and the plan is co-constructed from Elena's own ideas rather than prescribed. Options are offered and Elena chooses, resulting in genuine shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is repeatedly and explicitly affirmed ('You don't have to commit to anything,' 'you set the agenda,' 'your pace, your timeline'). The final step is entirely Elena's choice, and her self-directed pace is celebrated as a strength.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Elena herself through open questions ('What's weighing on you most?', 'What would feel different if that weight were lifted?'), with no advice or persuasion given. She generates her own reasons for change and her own plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena is explicitly positioned as the expert ('You know yourself well, Elena'), and her contributions visibly shape the session. Her preference for talking to her friend becomes the agreed next step, and her requests are incorporated into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and developed, moving Elena from 'I don't really want to go there' to 'I can't keep living like that' and 'I could see myself doing that.' Strategic questions directly elicited this progression, and each change statement was reinforced and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently normalized rather than challenged, and double-sided reflections acknowledge it without reinforcement. Sustain talk could occasionally have been pivoted more actively toward Elena's own change reasons.
