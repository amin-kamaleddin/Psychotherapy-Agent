# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with repeated validation of Jordan's reluctance; however, affirmations were generic and strengths/autonomy were never personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections captured ambivalence well, but reflections were sparse and the counselor missed emotional content like annoyance, fear of chickening out, and worry about grandpa.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on vaccination throughout, but direction was somewhat mechanical — pushing logistics and repeating procedural content rather than strategically advancing the conversation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some preferences were invited, but the structure was counselor-driven with menus, scripts, and checklists; Jordan's own simpler plan was not built upon, leading to visible irritation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were consistently offered without pressure, including a non-vaccination option; however, re-offering checklists and reminders after Jordan declined them slightly undermined client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One well-timed question about infecting others sparked genuine change talk, but beyond that the counselor relied on information-giving and menus rather than eliciting Jordan's values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some inclusive language was used, but the counselor frequently slipped into an expert role with repeated checklists and scripts despite Jordan's explicit declines, minimizing his expertise and input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged effectively at key moments and culminated in commitment language, but the counselor failed to deepen or consolidate it, pivoting instead to scripts and logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not deliberately reinforced, but the opening menu invited reasons against vaccination and the extensive harm-reduction plan amplified it before the counselor pivoted to Jordan's simpler framing.
