# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and respectful throughout, with an explicit 'I'm not here to judge' and developmentally appropriate confidentiality explanation. Warmth occasionally feels procedural, and affirmations of Tyler's honesty and effort are sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate reflections tracking Tyler's annoyance, exhaustion, and desire to get his mom off his back. Reflections stay content-level, missing deeper themes like the trust rupture and his anxiety during role-play.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus from exploration to a client-owned goal, role-play, and a concrete negotiated plan. Tyler ends with 'an actual plan,' showing strong directional skill without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely interactive, with check-ins, adapted openers, and role-play at Tyler's request. However, plans were often presented as finished products rather than jointly brainstormed from Tyler's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and reinforces 'in ways you choose,' with Tyler shaping the session pace and scenarios. Heavy provision of scripted lines and pre-made trades edges into advice-giving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good evocative questions and effective confidence scaling (5 to 8), but elicited motivation centers on reducing parental conflict rather than Tyler's own relationship with substances. Counselor often supplies content instead of drawing motivation from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites input and lets Tyler choose scenarios, visibly shaping the session. Fully scripted lines and pre-packaged plans position the counselor as the source of solutions rather than a co-creator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence ruler and channeling the 'get mom off my back' goal moved Tyler from 'I don't see why I should' to committing to a two-week plan. Change talk stays about parental conflict, never evoking Tyler's own reasons regarding his use.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argues with sustain talk, reflecting and redirecting it skillfully while avoiding lecture. Ambivalence about the substance use itself is sidestepped rather than resolved.
