# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance toward Elena's hesitancy, validating her fears without dismissal. However, explicit affirmations of her strengths (honesty, courage, love for her daughter) were sparse, keeping this from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and frequent, capturing her ambivalence well ('You're torn — scared by stories... wanting above all to keep your daughter safe'). Reflections sometimes stayed at the factual level rather than validating the deeper emotional toll of her anxiety and self-blame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on the vaccination decision and progressed logically toward booking an appointment. Some turns expanded into general anxiety-management tips without checking whether they served the core goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions were made jointly, with Elena's preferences shaping the session's direction. Some dense, lecture-like information blocks (red flags, action plan) temporarily tipped the balance away from equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered choices and applied no pressure, letting Elena reach her own decision. Explicit affirmations of her ownership of the change process ('this was your decision') were absent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong evocative questions early on drew out Elena's core value of keeping her daughter safe, but the middle of the session shifted heavily toward expert information delivery. Deeper evocation techniques (confidence rulers, exploring past successes) were largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena was positioned as the expert on her own life, with choices offered at every step and her contributions visibly shaping the session. The counselor's expertise was at times foregrounded through dense information blocks, slightly reducing full power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective questions evoked meaningful change talk, culminating in Elena's own statement of intent to book the appointment. Elicitation was uneven mid-session and change talk was not deliberately deepened (e.g., confidence scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged with brief neutral reflections and skillful double-sided reflections without amplifying it. Redirects were sometimes abrupt, moving to information-giving rather than using sustain talk as a doorway for deeper reflection.
