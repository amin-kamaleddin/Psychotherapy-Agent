# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor remained respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, validating Andrei's time constraints and frustrations despite his initial irritability. However, warmth was somewhat formulaic and transactional, with sparse individualized affirmations of his strengths and honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was generally accurate, capturing Andrei's time pressure and health concerns. Reflections occasionally skewed toward solution-planning rather than exploring the emotional weight of exhaustion, failed plans, and self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained exceptional focus on the target behavior throughout, with every exchange moving toward a fully concrete, specific plan including a scheduled Tuesday check-in with negotiated yes/no questions. No topic drift occurred despite the client's defensiveness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, building on Andrei's calendar-blocking idea and adding his requested fourth check-in question. Dense, multi-question turns and occasional pre-packaged suggestions kept some asymmetry in the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client choice was repeatedly affirmed, with Andrei selecting his own strategies, backup plans, and check-in preferences. Some options were presented in a prescriptive, menu-like fashion, and rapid questioning occasionally created pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively drew motivation from within, using open questions, confidence rulers, and commitment queries rather than persuasion, with confidence rising from 6 to 7. Motivationally rich statements about exhaustion and values were left unexpanded into deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor positioned Andrei's knowledge as central, and his contributions genuinely shaped the session direction. Rapid-fire multiple questions per turn and persistent suggesting of options slightly leaned toward expert direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was effectively evoked through confidence scaling and concrete commitment questions, producing explicit commitment language ('Sunday night, alarms set') and rising confidence. The counselor did not deeply elaborate on change talk when it appeared, such as what 'getting hold of' the weight would mean.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubt and failure narratives were acknowledged and pivoted toward recovery-focused planning without amplification. The near-exclusive focus on logistics sometimes bypassed deeper exploration and resolution of ambivalence.
