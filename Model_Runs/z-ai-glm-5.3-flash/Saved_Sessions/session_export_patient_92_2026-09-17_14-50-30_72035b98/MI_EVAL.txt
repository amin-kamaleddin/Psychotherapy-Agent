# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalized defensiveness well, but offered few individualized affirmations of Marek's strengths or honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured both sides of Marek's ambivalence, though opportunities to deepen emotional reflections around identity and the social meaning of drinking were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on alcohol use throughout, but the session stalled at the end without a summary or collaboratively anchored next step for follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Inclusive language and Marek's ideas genuinely shaped the plan, but information-giving was largely one-directional and no joint follow-up plan was constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong choice-offering and explicit acceptance of non-commitment, though strategy menus subtly steered toward action without affirming Marek's capacity for self-directed change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Motivation came largely from the counselor's didactic risk information rather than being drawn out from Marek; evocative open questions and value-based exploration were underused and Marek's own change talk went undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently asked permission and positioned Marek as decision-maker, but long expert lectures on BAC, guidelines, and risks occupied an informational expert role without acknowledging Marek's expertise on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marek produced meaningful change talk, but it emerged mainly from psychoeducation rather than deliberate evocation, and his change statements were rarely reflected or amplified; no importance/confidence scaling was used.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used some double-sided reflections, but extensive didactic risk information likely amplified defensiveness, and sustain talk was acknowledged without strategic pivoting toward change language.
