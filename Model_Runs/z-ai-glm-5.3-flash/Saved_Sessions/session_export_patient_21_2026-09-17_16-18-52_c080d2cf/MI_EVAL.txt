# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed warmth and unconditional regard, normalizing low confidence and framing missed days without criticism ('celebrate steps, not perfection'). Deducted for somewhat formulaic, repetitive affirmations ('I'm cheering you on') that weren't fully individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor made accurate situation reflections and acknowledged strengths, but rarely reflected the emotional meaning behind Camila's meaningful statements (e.g., 'this one feels like something I chose'), moving quickly to the next planning question instead. Reflective depth was adequate but inconsistent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with no topic drift, progressing logically from exploration to a concrete, client-owned plan with follow-up criteria. Exemplary directional guidance without being controlling.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila's contributions (stove cue, partner involvement, weekend flexibility, energy notes) substantially shaped the final plan in a genuinely two-sided interaction. Slight deduction for multi-option question barrages and counselor turns that ran longer than necessary, edging toward guiding rather than fully joint pacing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was explicit and consistent, with options framed as choices and ownership reinforced throughout. Camila's closing comments ('this one feels like something I chose,' 'thank you for not pushing') directly validated this stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicited Camila's own reasons and solutions, using a confidence ruler that raised her confidence from 6 to 8. Deducted because questions were often option-heavy, supplying candidate answers before Camila could generate her own, and deeper personal meaning went unprobed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor genuinely positioned expertise with the client, incorporating her ideas into the plan and asking how she wanted follow-up structured. Stops short of 5 because turns were frequently dense with pre-formulated option menus rather than waiting for client-generated ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The confidence ruler successfully evoked Camila's own problem-solving and substantial change talk, with her confidence rising from 6 to 8. Deducted for not deeply elaborating or amplifying her change talk when it appeared (e.g., 'feeling less drained — that's really what I'm after').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about low energy, skipped days, and weekend disruption was acknowledged and reframed without reinforcement, always paired with solution-focused framing. Deducted because barrier questions were repeated across turns, keeping some attention on obstacles.
