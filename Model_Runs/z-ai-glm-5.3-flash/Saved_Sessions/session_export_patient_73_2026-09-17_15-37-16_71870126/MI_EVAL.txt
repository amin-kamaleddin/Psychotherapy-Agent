# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with well-timed affirmations ("three months is meaningful," "that took courage"). However, affirmations are somewhat brief and not fully unconditional of her progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture both behavioral and emotional layers of ambivalence. At times reflections are compressed and move quickly to problem-solving, limiting deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus: opens with the patient's agenda and builds purposefully from past attempts to triggers, strategies, rehearsal, and ally recruitment without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way problem-solving with role-play at her request and feedback after rehearsals. Option generation remains predominantly counselor-supplied rather than drawn from Linda's own experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent permission-seeking and genuine menu choices throughout, with no pressure or directive language. Explicitly supports Linda defining what feels honest and doable for her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits her own reasons (health, money, daughter) and uses rehearsal to draw out commitment language ("I'm quitting"). Middle portion shifts to menu-delivery, and confidence is never explicitly built via scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Linda as the expert ("I'll follow your lead"), checks permission, and lets her choices shape the session. Falls short as the counselor still supplies most concrete options and some turns feel advice-dense.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocative questions and rehearsal led Linda to say "I'm quitting" aloud, which was reinforced. Limited deepening of her strongest statements and no use of importance/confidence rulers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but often skipped past or met with immediate option-listing rather than double-sided reflections. Self-doubt is validated but not strategically reframed toward her demonstrated capability.
