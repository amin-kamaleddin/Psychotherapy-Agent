# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout. Relapse is reframed as evidence of persistence rather than judged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capturing both sides of ambivalence and underlying emotional meaning. Rosa's progressively deeper disclosures confirm she feels understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Steady focus on smoking cessation through a natural arc from ambivalence to concrete next steps. Direction is achieved by guiding rather than dragging.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully co-constructed session — the plan originates entirely from Rosa's ideas. The clinician acts as a curious partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is repeatedly and explicitly reinforced, including accepting her stated unreadiness without argument. The plan is consistently framed as Rosa's own choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is elicited from within via open questions rather than supplied by the clinician. Rosa ends with her own commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Rosa is explicitly positioned as the expert ('you know yourself best'), and all decisions come from her contributions. No solutions are prescribed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation and development of change talk, producing escalating commitment language across the session. Change talk is strategically reinforced when it appears.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is briefly acknowledged, then paired with change talk via double-sided reflections. Her 'not saying I'm ready' is accepted and redirected without argument.
