# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with strong affirmations of Timur's 22 days. Warmth occasionally felt procedural, and his ambivalence was treated more as a problem to fix than a fully legitimate stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured his core ambivalence between respect/status and Fiona. Deeper themes of shame, identity, and fear of rejection were acknowledged only briefly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on desistance and pro-social stability throughout, progressing logically from ambivalence exploration to concrete commitments with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was largely co-constructed from Timur's preferences, with ideas offered as options. Early solution lists and the pre-built Fiona script were counselor-generated, tilting the balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Timur's choice and month-limited commitment were respected without pressure. Occasional slips into prescriptive mode narrowed his perceived space to choose his own path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of open questions and scaling drew out Timur's own motivations and raised confidence from 5 to 7. Some motivational content was provided rather than evoked, and values weren't consistently linked back to his commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Timur was repeatedly positioned as the expert and decision-maker, and his input shaped the plan. Early expert-mode option lists slightly undercut the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation of change talk, including confidence scaling and rehearsal of commitments, yielding notable statements and a confidence rise to 7. Some change talk was affirmed rather than deepened, and evocation was sometimes bundled with premature solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally tolerated and sometimes reflected double-sided, but key opportunities were missed — the 'I'm lazy' label wasn't reframed against his 22-day persistence, and fear of job rejection was met with reassurance rather than self-exploration.
