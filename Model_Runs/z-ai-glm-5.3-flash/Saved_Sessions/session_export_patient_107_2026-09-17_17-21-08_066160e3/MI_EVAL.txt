# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental stance with no disapproval even amid client skepticism. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deeper, individualized affirmations of Nathaniel's honesty and engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early on that accurately captured core ambivalence about coffee and BP. However, empathy was front-loaded — the session shifted heavily toward logistics, missing chances to reflect underlying pride and self-protective skepticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, unwavering focus on the coffee/BP question with no topic drift. The session progressed logically from exploration to a concrete, time-bound plan with a booked review — a clear strength.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with repeated check-ins, negotiated decisions, and adaptation to the client's preference for simplicity. However, the core log structure and BP protocol were delivered as a near-complete package for approval rather than jointly generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client ownership through menu options, honoring his refusal of reminders, and explicitly agreeing to his non-coercion condition. The client left confirming he had a plan that didn't require quitting anything yet.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relatively little was done to elicit Nathaniel's own motivations, values, or reasons for change, with no evocative questions or importance/confidence rulers. Pivotal client-initiated change talk was passed over without deepening, leaving motivation resting on the clinician's structured proposal rather than his own concerns.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing on format, reminders, and scheduling, but the stance slipped toward expert-driven territory with the log structure and BP protocol prescribed. The client's contributions mostly shaped logistics rather than the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged modestly, aided by the decisional framing of the three-option menu. The psychiatrist did not actively amplify pivotal statements like 'I could probably live with cutting back a bit,' leaving change talk underdeveloped and ending with commitment only to data collection.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced, and the data-oriented reframe sidestepped arguing appropriately. However, no double-sided reflections paired sustain talk with change talk, and no strategic pivoting toward his stated concerns — ambivalence was tolerated rather than resolved.
