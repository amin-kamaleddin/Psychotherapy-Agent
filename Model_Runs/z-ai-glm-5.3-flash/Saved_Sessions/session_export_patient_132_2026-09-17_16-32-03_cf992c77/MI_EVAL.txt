# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing struggle ('setbacks are normal') and accepting her ongoing calorie-counting thoughts. Falls short of 5 due to formulaic affirmations ('I love that...') and lack of explicit affirmation of her courage in confronting her dieting history.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing her core ambivalence ('stuck between wanting weight loss and wanting to stop crash-and-burn') and nuanced reflections linking guilt-free eating to her goals. Deeper emotional layers — guilt voice, years of failed diets, body dissatisfaction — were acknowledged only briefly rather than explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus from first turn through a concrete committed plan: Thursday lunch, specific sandwich, coping script, backup plan, Wednesday reminder, and 3pm Thursday call. Every exchange moved forward without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving: she picked the day, meal, script tweaks, and check-in format while counselor provided structure and backup plans. Occasionally one-directional, with counselor supplying most scripts and frameworks for her to edit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice ('I'm here to support whatever you choose') and honored her weight-loss ambivalence without pressure. Fell short of 5 because choices were often structured with leading options rather than fully open invitations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from the client ('eating without guilt — that's huge for me'; gym 'clears my head') via targeted questions and confidence scaling, with no lecturing. Evocation was frequent but occasionally stopped at the surface without deeper elaboration of values and reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Her input shaped the plan (day, meal, script edits, check-in), but the counselor generated most intervention content — pre-written scripts, grounding phrases, specific steps — some of which she rejected as 'cheesy.' Less explicit framing of her as the expert on her own change.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective, targeted evocation drew out change talk ('things I can actually keep doing long-term,' 'feels kind of freeing') and confidence rose from 5 to 6.5. Reinforced change talk well but could have deepened her own reasons and commitment-strengthening further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk skillfully without amplifying it, meeting her persistent weight-loss desire with acceptance ('noticing it without following it is enough'). However, the ambivalence was set aside rather than explored or reframed with double-sided reflection.
