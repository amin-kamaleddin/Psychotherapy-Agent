# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout—no judgment, and even ambivalence about weight loss and the scale is honored rather than criticized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections such as 'Peace in my own head, not just a smaller body' and capturing the fear alongside willingness to change.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Steady focus on moving from dieting toward sustainable non-diet behaviors, ending with a concrete commitment without rushing or pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative exchange with permission-seeking questions and co-construction; the plan emerged entirely from dialogue rather than expert prescription.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy strongly reinforced ('You don't have to give anything up today'); every choice originated with the client and she responded that it felt 'newer.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently elicited from within, with the client named as the source of insight and all change talk self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client positioned as the expert ('You're the author here'); she chose the gym, designed the check-in, picked Friday and pasta, and recruited her sister as support.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions evoked strong client-generated change language, culminating in a fully client-articulated plan for Friday's guilt-free meal.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about the scale and weight loss was acknowledged without amplification, but the underlying ambivalence was never deeply explored or resolved.
