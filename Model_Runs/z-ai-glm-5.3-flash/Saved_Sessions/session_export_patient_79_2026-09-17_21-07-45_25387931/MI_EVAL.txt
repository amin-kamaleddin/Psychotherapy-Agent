# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard despite sarcasm and defensiveness, framing honesty as a strength and never criticizing the client's conditions or resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflections captured both sides of ambivalence and validated frustrations, earning an explicit 'finally somebody gets it' from the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on asthma medication adherence throughout, progressively moving from values to a concrete, negotiated action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative, with the client's own ideas shaping every element of the plan and the counselor explicitly framing themselves as a partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support — repeatedly affirmed client ownership, used permission-style questions, and accepted the client's conditions without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All major motivations came from the client himself, drawn out through strategic questions; the counselor never lectured about health risks.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert on his own life, and his conditions became the entire plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked rich desire, need, and commitment language through open questions, reinforcing change talk with reflections and affirmations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk and redirected toward what was manageable, but a few reflections initially echoed the sustain side and the counselor accepted the 'no follow-ups' condition without exploring that barrier.
