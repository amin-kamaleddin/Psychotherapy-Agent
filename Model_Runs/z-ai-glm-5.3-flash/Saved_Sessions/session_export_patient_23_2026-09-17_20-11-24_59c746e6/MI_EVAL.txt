# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone with explicit no-pressure framing and unconditional positive regard. Patient noted she expected to be 'told off' but instead left feeling lighter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, layered reflections capturing both the functional role of alcohol and her ambivalence. Normalized her nervousness and echoed her key insights (e.g., 'clear-headed morning means more energy, a better mood').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol use throughout with no topic drift. Each exchange advanced the target behavior from exploration to a concrete, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership: asked rather than told, invited input at every decision point, and co-constructed the follow-up plan. The final plan was entirely built from the patient's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced patient choice ('you get to decide the pace', 'no pressure'). Patient explicitly affirmed ownership: 'It's my routine, my choice.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Powerful evocative questions drew out the patient's own reasons (better mood, clear mornings) and self-generated solutions. Patient moved from ambivalence to 'this is something I can actually do.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the patient as the expert on her own life and credited her authorship of the plan. Patient recognized this: 'this is mine to figure out.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocative questions elicited rich change talk and commitment language ('I could probably commit to that', 'small enough, and mine enough'). The counselor reflected and amplified this language effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided dwelling on sustain talk and pivoted well toward change. However, briefly echoed sustain-related content (what she'd miss, the pull of bath nights); a quicker pivot with double-sided reflections would strengthen momentum.
