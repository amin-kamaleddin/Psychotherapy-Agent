# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, non-judgmental responses to minimization, validating honesty without shaming. Affirmations were sparse and formulaic, missing chances to praise strengths like his planning initiative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured ambivalence and core concerns about goals. However, reflections were occasionally brief and moved quickly to menus rather than deepening emotional content like social pressure and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol risk linked to Alexei's soccer and scholarship goals with coherent session flow. Frequent multi-option menus stalled momentum, and periodic summarizing was absent.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner, co-constructing plans from Alexei's own wording and seeking permission before advising. Structured option lists tilted the balance toward counselor-led structure with limited shared agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary — consistently sought permission, offered choices rather than directives, and let Alexei shape every intervention. Alexei's ownership of the plan confirms strong autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Alexei's own reasons tied to soccer and scholarships, and rehearsal built self-efficacy. Evocation relied heavily on the first exploratory question, then shifted into planner/advisor mode, missing chances to deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and let Alexei's contributions drive the session (sober buddy, emoji code, if-then plan). Most steps were presented as pre-packaged options rather than openly inviting Alexei to generate solutions first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicited strong goal-linked change talk, reinforced by connecting strategies to Alexei's values. Change talk was rarely reflected back deliberately or followed with evocative questions like confidence probes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk skillfully without arguing, reflecting ambivalence and redirecting to what felt worrying. Redirections were somewhat generic, without sophisticated double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk.
