# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout. Honored family traditions and autonomy without labeling or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, accurately capturing her core ambivalence. Missed opportunities to reflect deeper emotions, e.g., the baby's liver comment and anxiety about her mother's judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing prenatal alcohol exposure. Every exchange built toward a concrete, scheduled plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plans were genuinely co-constructed, with the client selecting, modifying, and adding elements. Even the reminder message wording was negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the client's ownership of every choice, offering menus and asking permission before sharing information. The client confirmed: 'you just handed me the facts and let me drive.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew motivation from within using open questions, readiness scaling, and value-linked options. The client entered planning to change nothing and left with a self-generated, scheduled plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the client as the expert and decision-maker. Power-sharing was genuine, shaping the session substantially.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk via the readiness ruler, value-linked options, and direct evidence-sharing. Abundant commitment language was consistently affirmed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argued against sustain talk and respected skepticism with balanced evidence. Some responses restated sustain themes more often than actively reframing them.
