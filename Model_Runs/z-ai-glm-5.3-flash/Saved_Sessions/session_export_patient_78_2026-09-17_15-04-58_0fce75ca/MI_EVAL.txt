# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and respect with a resistant client, honoring her conditions without pushback. Warmth occasionally felt formulaic, lacking affirmations of her worth beyond progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured ambivalence and the value of her smoking break. Reflections tended to summarize rather than deepen emotional meaning, e.g., missing her mother's example and her daughter's pamphlets.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on the target behavior throughout, progressing logically from exploration to a concrete, measurable plan and booked follow-up with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-created piece by piece from Lucía's own choices. The four-item options list was delivered as a package and the agenda wasn't explicitly negotiated at the start.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent autonomy support: her conditions (no reminders, short check-in, no daughter involvement) all accepted, choice repeatedly affirmed, and follow-up booking left entirely to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and realistic-goal framing led her to generate her entire plan herself, with confidence rising from 3 to 7. Early in the session the counselor offered options before drawing out more of her own motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Lucía was repeatedly positioned as the expert and her contributions genuinely shaped the session. The options list moment tilted toward expert-driven advice before full negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and scaling evoked real change talk, rising confidence, a concrete plan, and spontaneous openness to next steps. Change talk was elicited mostly later; earlier momentum toward options left her initial 'common sense' remarks underdeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well via autonomy, reframing smoking as a needed break, and 'no drama, just data' framing of slips. However, the counselor delivered a full options list right after Lucía said she didn't want 'a lecture dressed up as options.'
