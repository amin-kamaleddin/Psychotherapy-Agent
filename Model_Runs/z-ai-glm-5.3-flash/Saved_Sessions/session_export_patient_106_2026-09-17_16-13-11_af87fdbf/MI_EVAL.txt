# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, and Natalia explicitly felt heard. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and lacked deeper recognition of her strengths as a resilient single parent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate early complex reflections captured her ambivalence well. Later reflections became checklist-like, missing deeper emotional content such as her fear of failure, self-criticism, and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on smoking cessation with a concrete, structured plan (tea ritual, backups, scripts, one-week trial, scheduled call). Each exchange advanced the target behavior and tied back to her values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Natalia shaped the plan substantially—choosing the tea, backup strategies, check-in format, and timing. The initial options list was counselor-supplied rather than drawing out her own ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership repeatedly reinforced: no pressure, accepted her refusal to practice aloud, honored the one-week commitment, and normalized slips without judgment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within via open questions, rulers, and eliciting her own solutions; client confirmed the approach fit her life. Some reliance on structured tools rather than ongoing deeper elicitation of values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regular permission-asking and genuine tailoring to her constraints; her contributions drove the plan. The pre-packaged options segment slightly tempered power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of importance/confidence rulers and evocative questions; confidence rose from 4 to 6 with clear commitment language. Occasional repetition of summary lists instead of deepening the newest change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was neither argued with nor amplified; the plan honored smoking's function instead of fighting it. Little strategic reframing or double-sided reflection to actively resolve ambivalence.
