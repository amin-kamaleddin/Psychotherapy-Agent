# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with consistent reassurance, but warmth felt formulaic and missed easy opportunities to affirm Ingrid's proactive safety-seeking behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No reflective listening — Ingrid's worry, relief at having asked, and time pressure were never reflected. Responses were purely informational and repeated verbatim.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The focus stayed appropriately on the medication interaction question, but the session then devolved into repetitive, identical closing statements with no purposeful forward movement or consolidation of next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The exchange was information-dispensing rather than joint problem-solving, with no 'we' language or co-created follow-up plan. Ingrid's offer to call later was met with a generic 'come back anytime.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Communication was directive and imperative ('Don't stop or switch meds,' 'Call now'), with no acknowledgment of Ingrid's choice or ownership despite her explicitly stating her own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No attempt to elicit Ingrid's motivations, concerns, or understanding — no open questions at all. Her motivation emerged unprompted and was never drawn out or expanded upon.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed the expert role with directive instructions and never invited Ingrid's perspective. Her stated plan to call her doctor did not shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ingrid spontaneously offered strong change talk ('I'll ring my doctor today'), but the counselor never reflected, affirmed, or deepened it — opportunities like 'What will you say to your doctor?' were missed entirely.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The only sustain talk was Ingrid's time constraint, which the counselor neither argued against nor skillfully worked with — neutral handling with no acknowledgment or offer of a follow-up call.
