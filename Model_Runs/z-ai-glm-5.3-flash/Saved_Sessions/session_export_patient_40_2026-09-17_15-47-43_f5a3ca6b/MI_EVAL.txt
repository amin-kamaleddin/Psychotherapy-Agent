# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment about the relapse or pill use; relapse treated as information rather than failure. Warmth is genuine and individualized (e.g., referencing the daughter's mug as a meaningful cue).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captures Elias's core ambivalence, including shame and uncertainty. Reflections occasionally skew toward summarizing/strategizing rather than deepening the emotional layer, keeping this from a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on evening alcohol/pill use and sustaining sobriety. Every exchange advances the change goal—trigger identification, planning, confidence scaling, and contingency planning—with no aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ideas offered as options, plan tweaks invited, and Elias's lived experience drives the solutions. His feedback (e.g., calling every night being too much) was immediately integrated into co-constructed decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is consistently reinforced through menu options and questions like 'Which option helps most right now?' The plan is framed as Elias's own, and his decisions (no role-play, short text) are accepted without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within: client's own reasons, evidence of success, and coping ideas are elicited rather than advice dispensed. Confidence ruler and contingency questions produced strong commitment language by session's end.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client expertise treated as central: Elias chose the focus, selected his own steps, and rejected role-play without pushback. His contributions (brother call, mug, text wording) substantially shaped the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent evocation of desire/ability language ('I'd like to feel that way again — like I actually own this'). Confidence scaling (6 rising to 7) and commitment language were skillfully elicited and reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not amplified; doubt is met with a double-sided reflection validating it while pivoting toward his investment in sobriety. A 5 would require more active reframing of fear-based motivation as a foundation to build on rather than mainly moving past it.
