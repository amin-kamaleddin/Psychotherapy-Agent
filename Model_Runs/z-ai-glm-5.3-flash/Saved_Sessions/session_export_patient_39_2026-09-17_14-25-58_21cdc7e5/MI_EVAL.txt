# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations for Elias's efforts, though they remain somewhat formulaic and brief rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections captured Elias's ambivalence, but the deeper emotional themes—dignity, independence, fear of being seen as 'the old man in the chair'—were largely left unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on returning to group activity with seated modifications; every exchange advanced a concrete pathway and culminated in a specific commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with rehearsal and co-constructed final plan, but the counselor supplied most of the raw material, leaving Elias more a consumer of options than an equal co-author.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent menus and choices let Elias's preferences drive the plan, though options were largely counselor-designed rather than reinforcing his own solution-generating capacity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Heavily prescriptive style delivered options and scripts rather than evoking Elias's own motivations; valuable material (improved mood for his wife, not wanting to be treated like glass) emerged spontaneously and went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elias was invited to choose, rehearse, and self-evaluate, but the counselor's expert posture (scripts, tips, contingency plans) meant power-sharing was uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong arc from 'why bother' to a concrete commitment ('Thursday. I'll let you know how it goes'), but momentum came mostly from counselor-generated menus rather than open-ended evocation or deepening reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement but handled through practical problem-solving rather than double-sided reflections or strategic reframing.
