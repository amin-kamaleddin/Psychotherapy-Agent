# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental, but warmth is sparse and there are almost no genuine affirmations of Marco's strengths, self-awareness, or problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal, with brief token reflections immediately followed by advice; Marco's emotional experiences went largely unexplored and unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target behavior and progressed logically to a concrete plan and follow-up, though direction came largely from directive information rather than collaborative goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is uneven, with the counselor lecturing while Marco complies; his ideas end up in the plan but conclusions are delivered rather than co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Dense prescriptive advice subtly pressures a specific course; only one token autonomy gesture, and the counselor never explicitly affirms Marco's ownership or freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation resided almost entirely with Marco himself; there were no open questions about his reasons for change or values, and the counselor missed the rich opportunity to explore his high confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed the expert role throughout, responding to Marco's request for input with more prescriptive advice rather than exploring his ideas or actively fostering power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marco spontaneously generated substantial change talk, including a volunteered 10/10 confidence statement, but the counselor failed to reinforce, reflect, or deepen any of it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** When Marco voiced his attachment to his morning OJ ritual, the counselor bypassed the ambivalence entirely and jumped straight into practical tips instead of using double-sided reflections.
