# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with normalizing statements like 'Not messed up at all.' However, warmth felt somewhat generic, with no explicit affirmations of Jamal's courage in trying something new.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal—key emotional disclosures like the fear of being seen as 'better than' teammates were skipped over entirely. The session was strategy-driven rather than understanding-driven.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained consistent focus on the target behavior of drinking at team parties with no aimless drift. Direction was clear but narrow, maintained through task-focus rather than resolving ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Default mode was expert instruction with lists of solutions, though Jamal's own ideas did shape the plan. Genuine collaboration appeared mainly in the role-play rehearsals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Barrage-style advice and prescriptive scripts dominated, with limited choice among counselor-generated options. Jamal's uncertainty about quitting forever went unacknowledged, and ownership of the change process was never explicitly affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and ideas were largely supplied by the counselor rather than drawn out; almost no open questions about Jamal's reasons or confidence. Client insights emerged spontaneously but were not elicited or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumed the expert role, delivering prescriptive solutions before understanding Jamal's perspective. Jamal's own ideas were incorporated but in a lukewarm, tactical way without acknowledging him as the expert on his social world.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged ('that felt kinda doable') largely from Jamal himself and the rehearsal format, not from skillful evocation. Ambivalence about quitting forever was not explored or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk (minimizing, team loyalty, fear of pushback) was largely ignored rather than addressed with double-sided reflections. The 'dry week' framing actually reinforced sustain talk without exploring the underlying ambivalence.
