# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tone is respectful and non-judgmental, with brief warmth, but affirmations are formulaic lead-ins to advice and emotional struggles (shame, fear of collapse) are never explicitly validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal; Erik's feelings of shame and discouragement are acknowledged in one line then bypassed for tactics, and he must supply his own emotional content without it being mirrored or deepened.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on gym consistency and evening snacking with concrete, defined steps, though the direction is driven by a counselor-imposed agenda rather than Erik's own priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is unequal, with the counselor dispensing numbered expert plans and Erik choosing from menus; the best collaborative moments emerge from Erik taking over, not from joint agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice-offering occurs (pick one step, alarm options), but the style remains directive—prescriptive plans, unrequested scripts, and pre-set check-in requirements override Erik's autonomy at times.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ideas and motivation are supplied from outside rather than drawn from within; Erik's strongest insights emerge unprompted while the counselor asks only logistical questions and fails to amplify them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes the expert role for nearly every turn, delivering prescriptive bulleted packages while Erik's expertise is rarely invited; his contributions emerge despite, not because of, the approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Erik offers rich spontaneous change talk (treadmill insight, alarm commitment), but the counselor never evokes or deepens it with open questions about his motivations or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but it's also not strategically engaged—no double-sided reflections pair Erik's doubts with his demonstrated successes, leaving ambivalence unresolved.
