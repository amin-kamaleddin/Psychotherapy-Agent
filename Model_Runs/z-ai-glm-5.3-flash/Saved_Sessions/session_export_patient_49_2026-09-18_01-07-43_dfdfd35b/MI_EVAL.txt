# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard throughout; both sides of ambivalence honored and affirmations specific and genuine. No judgment, labeling, or condescension anywhere, including the closing moment honoring the patient's disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures content and emotional undercurrents, including anticipatory empathy for social difficulty. Falls short of 5 as reflections occasionally leaned toward reframing rather than deeply exploring the patient's fear of failure and self-critical streak.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on smoking cessation from start to finish with no drift, advancing through ambivalence, problem-solving, timeline, and relapse-proofing. Direction achieved entirely through the client's own momentum rather than imposition.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint enterprise with invitational questions at every step; the clinician supplied structure while the patient supplied all content. Ideas immediately built upon, and the plan demonstrably shaped by the patient's own answers.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy actively reinforced at every decision point, including explicitly honoring the option of no change and offering the start date as optional. The patient's closing remark about leaving with a plan she wants to try confirms intrinsic commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every motivation and solution (teeth, gums, cost, pride, social accountability) was elicited from the patient, with none supplied by the clinician. The self-generated, intrinsically motivated plan is strong evidence of skilled evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient was explicitly positioned as the expert ('You know your life better than anyone'), and every plan element originated from her. Her closing thanks for 'not lecturing' confirms she experienced genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook arc of change talk from 'maybe there's something there' to a specific commitment ('Monday. Weekdays only'), systematically evoked and amplified via open evocative questions and commitment-strengthening prompts. Each instance of change talk was strategically reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, mainly through double-sided reflections, and always resolved toward change. Not a 5 because a few reflections briefly restated the sustain side without an immediate pivot toward change.
