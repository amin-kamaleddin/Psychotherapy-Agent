# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations like 'You've kept trying, which shows commitment' and normalizing the freeze response. A 5 would require deeper, more individualized affirmations of Amina's worth beyond persistence and competence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, validating reflections such as 'interviews getting interrupted has been really frustrating — that makes sense.' Reflections are mostly brief; deeper exploration of the emotional toll of setbacks and the awkwardness of asking the neighbor would elevate this.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent, consistent focus on overcoming childcare-related employment barriers with each turn logically advancing the agenda from barrier identification to role-play and backup planning. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and responds to requests, but supplies most content (scripts, checklists, job boards) with limited joint brainstorming. Amina is treated more as a recipient of materials than a co-creator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers menus of options and lets Amina's choices shape the session. However, some turns are prescriptive (full scripts rather than co-authored), and there is minimal explicit reinforcement that Amina owns the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Draws on Amina's stated confidence and self-knowledge, but motivation and content largely flow from the counselor's expertise. Few open questions invite Amina's own reasons, hopes, or solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices, but the session becomes advice-dense with pre-written scripts, listings, and checklists positioning the counselor as content expert. Little elicitation of Amina's own solution ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina produces genuine change talk ('I'll actually text her this week. I mean it this time.'), but largely through her own momentum rather than deliberate evocation. Missed opportunities to amplify commitment language or consolidate with summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Briefly acknowledges the legitimacy of Amina's doubts ('You're right to worry... that makes sense') then pivots effectively to solutions. A double-sided reflection pairing her doubt with her confidence would have been stronger.
