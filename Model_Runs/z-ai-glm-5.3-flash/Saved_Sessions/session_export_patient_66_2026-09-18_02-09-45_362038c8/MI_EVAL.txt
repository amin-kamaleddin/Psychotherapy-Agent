# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental stance with no pressure, though the warmth is somewhat understated and professional rather than deeply personal, with few explicit affirmations of Kareem's character.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary empathic attunement through highly accurate, consistent reflective listening — complex reflections capture both sides of ambivalence, reflect feeling, and every reflection is confirmed by Kareem's constructive elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus from first utterance to a concrete follow-up plan, with each question building progressively toward clarifying readiness and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The work is repeatedly framed as joint and Kareem's priorities directly shape the follow-up plan, but the sequence of exploration is clinician-orchestrated rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively and repeatedly reinforced — the psychiatrist explicitly returns ownership ('that plan is yours'), never pressures a timeline, and frames commitment-adjacent questions as hypothetical and optional.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Kareem's own values and experiences (kids, Ramadan, his own checklist), and change is visibly developed within the session from 'how am I gonna get through the day' to 'maybe worth a shot.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kareem is consistently positioned as the expert on his own life, and his stated priorities (cravings, discretion, affordability) become the organizing framework for follow-up, though the psychiatrist does steer the agenda with persistent questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully and strategically evoked through open questions targeting desire, ability, reasons, and need, then highlighted and deepened, with summaries explicitly contrasting early sustain talk with emerging movement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but never amplified, and is consistently pivoted toward change within double-sided reflections, though it is sometimes reflected back at length, mildly amplifying ambivalence rather than pivoting fully.
