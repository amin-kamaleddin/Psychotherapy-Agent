# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental regard throughout despite Evelyn's minimization; her attachment to the pub and honesty were explicitly validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections captured ambivalence ('both of those can be true at once') and voiced unspoken feelings about the fall and living alone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus on alcohol reduction from start to finish, advancing logically to a concrete, measurable plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal-partner exchange; the plan was co-constructed from Evelyn's ideas and check-ins rather than prescriptions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was consistently preserved with no lecturing or unsolicited advice; Evelyn herself credited this as why she was willing to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivations (the fall, her son, self-respect) were elicited from Evelyn and amplified; commitment language originated within her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evelyn was repeatedly positioned as the expert, and the plan drew entirely from her own life, ideas, and pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was selectively highlighted and reinforced; open questions elicited reasons and commitment, culminating in the client-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and skillfully reframed rather than debated, though openings like the 'just clumsiness' dismissal could have been redirected earlier.
