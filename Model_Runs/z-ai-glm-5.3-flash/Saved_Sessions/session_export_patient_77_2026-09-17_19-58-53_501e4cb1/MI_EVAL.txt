# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with validating statements ('It's not silly at all'). Affirmations occasionally felt slightly effusive, and normalization of adolescent body-image struggles was limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate and deep reflective listening, including capturing the emotional core of the mother's criticism and the client's fear of hoping again. Reflections repeatedly built on the client's exact words and moved her toward significant reframes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on sustainable weight-loss efforts across the entire session, with each response advancing the arc toward commitment. The focus never drifted and built logically to a concrete plan and success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently functioned as equal partners, with permission-seeking and summaries that synthesized the client's contributions rather than imposing interpretations. The client's ideas demonstrably shaped the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every next step originated from the client, with no prescriptive advice anywhere in the session. Realistic limits were acknowledged without judgment or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently drew out the client's own values (strength, pride, patience with her brother). Change talk moved visibly from 'I don't know' to 'I'll try it. I want to believe that.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly credited the client as the author of her own plan ('I want you to hear how you got there yourself'). Open, client-directed questions and full adoption of her ideas reinforced ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk, including a hypothetical question that elicited rich desire language and follow-ups that generated ability and commitment statements. Momentum was amplified without forcing it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed without reinforcement, honoring the client's fear before gently pivoting. Could improve by using more double-sided reflections to explicitly pair sustain talk with change talk.
