# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with warm, non-moralizing responses to minimization and jokes. Affirmations feel somewhat formulaic and repetitive, and normalization of setbacks is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures ambivalence, fears, and insight. Reflections occasionally summarize prior content rather than deepening into the emotional layer (e.g., shame, fear beneath card dependence).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus maintained throughout, with every exchange explicitly linked to gambling and its consequences (Egypt, debt, probation). The session progresses logically with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Permission asked before offering material, and Anya's contributions shape the final plan. The psychiatrist tends to propose solutions first, with Anya selecting from options rather than true joint brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames next steps as Anya's choice, with advice offered as optional. Pacing is fairly directive, and ownership of change is not explicitly reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of values questions and confidence rulers to elicit Anya's own reasons and solutions. Some motivational tools were counselor-initiated, and discrepancy was stated for her rather than explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anya's ideas (cash-only plan, Tash, Egypt jar) drive the plan and she is positioned as decision-maker. The psychiatrist frequently supplies options and scripts, slightly tipping the expertise balance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation using confidence rulers (4→6→7), commitment questions, and concrete planning that produced progressively stronger change talk. Each step was reinforced, ending with a self-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (card-cutting fear, social loss, 'win it back' voice) is acknowledged without amplifying, with practical solutions and role-play rehearsal. Sustain talk is sometimes restated in summaries, and double-sided reflections are underused.
