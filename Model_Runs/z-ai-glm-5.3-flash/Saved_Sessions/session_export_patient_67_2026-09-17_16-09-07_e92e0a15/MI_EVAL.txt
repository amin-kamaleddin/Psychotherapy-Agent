# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout — client explicitly noted it wasn't a lecture as expected. Missed chances for personal affirmations of his courage and insight.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and validating (e.g., 'it makes sense drinking helps with the quiet at home'). However, they often stayed at content level and didn't explore the emotional core of loneliness Kenji raised twice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus with a concrete, agreed plan (week log, reminders, voice memos, follow-up). Focus narrowed to tracking mechanics rather than periodically reconnecting to Kenji's underlying motivations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kenji's preferences and ideas actively shaped the plan, and he felt safe negotiating. The counselor leaned somewhat on pre-packaged tools rather than inviting Kenji's own problem-solving first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kenji's ownership was reinforced at every decision point, with no pressure when he declined the walk idea. Low-pressure framing ('observing, not committing') actively fostered his sense of choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was drawn out (confidence rising 6 to 7), but much energy went into supplying tools rather than eliciting Kenji's own reasons. Missed opportunities to explore what mattered to him or why the counting stuck.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kenji was consistently positioned as decision-maker, and his input redirected the plan. The counselor was quick to supply tools and tips, occasionally tipping toward an expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited (concerns question, confidence ruler) but not deepened — no follow-up on why a 6 rather than lower or what success would look like. Also missed affirming Kenji's surprise at agreeing to more than expected.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled neutrally without amplification ('It makes sense you're skeptical but willing'). The counselor sometimes moved past it instead of using double-sided reflections, and the closing 'quiet apartment' concern was left unaddressed.
