# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout ('Thank you — that took real courage'), though rapid checklist-style questions undermined regard and required repair after Maria's pushback.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflections at key moments (the 'lead in her chest,' Biscuit's significance) and respected her request to sit in the pain, though empathy was sometimes secondary to safety directives and her fear of failed hope went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Appropriately focused on suicidality and safety while following emotional content; concrete anchors like the urge scale (9→6→4) gave direction, though repeated re-asking about the pills created some drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong co-construction in the second half (Maria chose and edited the text, chose the fridge plan, picked 'talk about the pain'), but early triage-style questioning created a top-down dynamic she had to resist.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed choices as Maria's ('pick one or tell me what to change') and accepted partial compliance with limits, but some choices were forced binaries and improvement was not explicitly attributed to her own agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine evocative questions elicited her reasons for living, but the overall style leaned on directives and menus rather than unpacking her own meaning (e.g., what 'real help, not a robot' means to her).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission and offered editable options, but the session was often directive with scripted question batteries; Maria's expertise was incorporated only after pushback.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully evoked change talk (sister, Biscuit, 'a tiny part of me that wants help') but rarely deepened these moments with affirmations or follow-up exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk by pivoting to her pain rather than debating means restriction, though repeated pill-checking kept focus on a resisted task instead of reframing ambivalence.
