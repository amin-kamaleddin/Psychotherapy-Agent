# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and warmth, never criticizing avoidance and repeatedly affirming the client ('You're not being difficult — this is hard').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was strong and accurate, tracking her fear and shutdown patterns well, but reflections were sometimes brief and pivoted quickly to option menus instead of deepening emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept focus anchored on the target concern and converged on a concrete step (one unedited sentence tonight, breathing backup, gentle follow-up), though momentum was occasionally diluted by menus and grounding exercises.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner with real power-sharing and permission-seeking, though frequent pre-set multiple-choice menus tilted agenda-setting toward the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support — repeatedly emphasized the client's ownership ('It's up to you,' 'You can stop anytime') and honored her pace and preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Drew out some of the client's own material and choices, but relied on counselor-supplied structured options rather than open questions eliciting her own reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treated the client's contributions as authoritative and shaped the plan around her preferences, but offered pre-packaged menus rather than inviting her to generate her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged ('I can manage one sentence,' 'No excuses') but evocation was incidental rather than strategic, with no deliberate deepening or confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcement, sidestepping the doctor visit by pivoting to a tiny client-chosen step; a double-sided reflection on the ambivalence would have strengthened this.
