# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, with affirmations and explicit reassurance ('no judgment here'). Warmth felt somewhat formulaic, and the counselor accepted the 'false alarm' framing without gentle exploration.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate summaries were used, but reflections mostly restated content rather than emotion or meaning. Opportunities to explore feelings beneath statements like 'it's for other people, not me' were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the alcohol screening and risk throughout with no topic drift. Focus stayed on risk assessment rather than guiding collaboratively toward change or a protective plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission and offered choices, but the session followed an expert-driven, assessment-style structure. The client stayed passive rather than co-constructing the agenda or understanding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support: 'whatever you choose is fine,' honoring her preference for no follow-up, and no pressure or lecturing. Options felt somewhat pro-forma and safety messaging was script-like rather than reinforcing her own capacity for healthy choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was never drawn from the client; the counselor supplied safety information and pre-packaged strategies instead of exploring what mattered to her about staying alcohol-free. Rich material (first chair, exams) went unused to elicit change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission before questions, supporting partnership, but the session was driven by the counselor's fact-gathering agenda. When the client redirected, the counselor complied without negotiating or honoring her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Virtually no change talk was evoked or reinforced, despite the client being in precontemplation. Strategies were offered rather than eliciting her own reasons for protecting her music and academic goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and was reframed neutrally, but the 'false alarm' framing was immediately accepted and recorded verbatim. A double-sided reflection linking her values to alcohol decisions would have softened it better.
