# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with explicit promises of 'no lecture' honored throughout; client's closing comment confirms the acceptance was felt. Affirmations conveyed genuine positive regard without labeling or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Brandon's ambivalence and sustain talk well. However, reflections sometimes restated rather than deepened emotional meaning, with little exploration of feelings behind family pressure or defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on drinking from the start, progressing logically from exploration to a specific behavioral commitment with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Joint framing was present with permission-seeking, and Brandon's choices materially shaped the plan. Falls short due to counselor-driven structure with stacked questions and option menus creating a slightly agenda-driven rhythm.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, explicit autonomy support ('whatever you decide, I'll support it'), with the experiment framed as Brandon's choice. His guarded stance was met with respect rather than pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew motivation from the client, who moved from resistance to voluntary commitment and self-generated curiosity. Relied heavily on structured tools (rulers, menus) rather than open-ended exploration of values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Brandon was repeatedly positioned as the expert, and every plan element was chosen by him. Options were still counselor-generated menus, limiting fully client-driven agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation via open questions, rulers, and success-marker questions produced notable change talk ('wouldn't mind not waking up feeling like garbage'). Key moments, like 'feeling more in control,' could have been deepened further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk and defensiveness were acknowledged neutrally without argument. Some reflections repeated the sustain side without double-sided pairings, and ambivalence was addressed by offering solutions rather than reframing.
