# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and non-judgment even when Elena minimized risk. Affirmations felt somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening capturing her protective efforts and reliance on smoking, but reflections were brief and often followed by stacked questions. Emotionally significant moments, like her admission about her son's ears, were not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, unwavering focus on smoking reduction throughout, with every exchange moving toward concrete action. Session ended with specific, measurable weekly commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was co-constructed around Elena's constraints and her ideas shaped the outcome, but the counselor frequently supplied ready-made menus and drafts rather than eliciting her solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was explicitly and repeatedly affirmed, and Elena embraced the freedom. Dense turns with pre-packaged options occasionally felt like gentle steering.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence ruler and win questions effectively elicited intrinsic motivation and commitment language. Elena's most powerful value statement was voiced once but not developed further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena genuinely shaped the session — the 1-hour backup, 5/7 goal, and Priya plan originated from her. Quick provision of menus and drafted texts slightly undercut the message that expertise resides in her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Active evocation through open questions and confidence rulers produced clear commitment and preparation language. Evocation was more plan-focused than values-focused.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was largely avoided or acknowledged briefly before pivoting to options, and the self-compassion reframe softened fear of failure effectively. The opening question briefly invited defensiveness, and some reflections restated sustain talk without pivoting.
