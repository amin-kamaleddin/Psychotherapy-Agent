# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone with good affirmations and reframing of self-criticism ('that doesn't mean you're lazy'). Deducted for formulaic repetitive warmth and no explicit exploration of his discouragement about past failures.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections (his fear of 'another failed promise,' 'a walk absolutely counts'), but the counselor often jumped to plan mechanics instead of reflecting the emotional weight of his ambivalence, fatigue, and self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the exercise goal with no topic drift. The session moved logically from ambivalence to a concrete plan with days, times, backups, cues, rewards, and a follow-up check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Farid supplied schedule and reward, but the counselor proposed the entire framework (walks, three weeks, implementation intentions) and later messages became one-sided instruction recaps with Farid merely agreeing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Farid made key choices (days, times, reward) and affirmed the plan 'feels like mine'; permission was asked for planning and check-in. Deducted for the prescriptive delivery and unprompted repeated crisis-line reminders.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was drawn out via the 'what could you stick with' question, but his intrinsic reasons for change were never explored—no open questions about why exercise matters, no confidence/need development, reliance on advice and technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Farid's input shaped some details (days, times), but the session was largely expert-driven, with his role narrowed to confirming a counselor-designed plan rather than co-constructing it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced commitments ('Tuesday and Saturday,' 'I'll get the alarms set tonight'), shifting vague intent to concrete plans with self-generated elements. Deducted for not deepening commitment with confidence scaling or exploring why this time might differ.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled well without debating—reframed the 'lazy' comment, affirmed walks count, and addressed all-or-nothing thinking with backup options. Deducted for repeated reassurance instead of exploring and resolving sustain talk ('no promises,' 'I've tried before').
