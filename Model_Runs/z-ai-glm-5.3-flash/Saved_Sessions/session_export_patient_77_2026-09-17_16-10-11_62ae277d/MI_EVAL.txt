### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with no criticism of Liyana or her mom, but affirmations are brief and formulaic, lacking deep individualized validation of her emotional pain.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture her frustration, loneliness, and fear of failure, but reflections often feel brief before pivoting to questions or planning, without lingering on deeper emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on weight-loss efforts from start to finish; each exchange advances the plan, ending with a concrete scheduled experiment and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing and genuine negotiation (Liya trimmed reminders, set check-in cadence), though some proposals like checklists and alarm labels were presented as done deals rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced: no prescribed diet plan, photos kept private by choice, mom conversation deferred, and Liyana exercised genuine choice throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler and preference questions drew out some motivation, but the counselor rarely explored Liyana's deeper values, reasons, or hopes, and much of the session was logistics driven by counselor proposals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly conveyed that expertise lies with Liyana ('you stay in control'), and her contributions visibly shaped the plan, though the counselor occasionally assumed planning authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked commitment language via the readiness ruler and collaborative planning ('I can commit to that'), but did not deepen change talk by exploring her own reasons, values, or vision of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Responded to sustain talk with empathy and pivoted to the tracking experiment, but re-raised the mom topic and check-up after repeated declines, keeping attention on resistance rather than change.
