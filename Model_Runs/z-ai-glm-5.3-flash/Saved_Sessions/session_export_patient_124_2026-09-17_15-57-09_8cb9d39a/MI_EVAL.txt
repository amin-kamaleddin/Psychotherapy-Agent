# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, including honoring Sofia's request for no lectures. However, affirmations of her honesty and self-awareness are sparse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early on, but turns packed with multiple questions crowd out reflective listening. Sofia's anxiety, family pressure, and worry about being lectured go largely unexplored and unvalidated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus maintained from opening to a concrete, specific plan (Monday 4:30 delay, jar, 7pm check-in). No topic drift and clear momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia substantially shaped the plan (chose delay, day, time, check-in) and permission was sought before advice. But rapid-fire questions and option menus tilted power toward the counselor, with Sofia at times responding passively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice consistently honored ('pick one to try,' 'jar optional,' acceptance of 'no promises'). Yet the counselor sometimes prescribed specific tools rather than letting Sofia generate solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions drew out some of Sofia's own reasons (money, doubts, parental approval), but her deeper motivators — anxiety, family expectations, future goals — surfaced unprompted and were never developed. Advice-giving replaced sustained evocation mid-session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-asking was good practice, but the middle of the session became advice-heavy with option lists and prescriptions. Sofia's expertise was not explicitly positioned, and several turns read as counselor-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful scaling questions elicited concrete change talk and confidence rose from 5 to 6. However, change talk was not consistently amplified or deepened when it appeared.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without arguing, but often met with immediate problem-solving rather than double-sided reflections. Missed opportunities to link Sofia's values (family, money, future) to her ambivalence.
