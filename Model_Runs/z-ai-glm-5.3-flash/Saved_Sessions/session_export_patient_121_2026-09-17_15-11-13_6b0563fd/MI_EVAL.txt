# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirmed Sofia's efforts, without labeling her anger or avoidance. Acceptance was occasionally instrumental, with warmth often pivoting into advice-giving rather than affirming her broader strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered accurate, feeling-focused reflections that captured Sofia's frustration and her sense of a double-bind. Deeper emotional experiences (hurt, isolation, washroom hiding) were touched but not fully explored, and reflections sometimes pivoted quickly to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target behaviors and built coherently toward a concrete plan (phone notes plus teacher script). Extensive advice menus occasionally diffused focus, but the counselor re-anchored by asking Sofia to choose.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plans were co-constructed through menus, the role-play was joint, and Sofia's feedback shaped the script. However, the counselor generated most of the strategies and content rather than drawing ideas out of Sofia, making the substance somewhat counselor-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear strength: the counselor repeatedly offered choice menus, sought permission before advising, and accepted Sofia's hesitations without pressure. Every next step was framed as Sofia's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited from within, such as asking what helped when Sofia ignored provocation. Much of the session relied on supplying tools rather than evoking and developing Sofia's own reasons and capacity for change, with no rulers or values exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia was explicitly positioned as the decision-maker, and her choices demonstrably shaped the session. Dense advice menus occasionally edged toward an expert stance, and Sofia's own expertise was drawn on less than it could have been.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicited some change talk through menu-choice questions and Sofia responded with commitment-oriented statements. Change talk was harvested passively rather than deepened with evocative questions, rulers, or elaborative reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's pessimistic sustain talk was acknowledged and occasionally reframed, but not skillfully softened. There were no double-sided reflections pairing her doubts with her strengths, and redirection was neutral rather than strategic.
