# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, validating Aisha's exhaustion and honesty. Falls short of a 5 because warmth occasionally feels tied to 'correct' answers and there's no explicit affirmation that slips are accepted unconditionally, despite Aisha anticipating them.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures her core dilemma and emotional nuances like 'tired beats guilty.' Missed deeper reflection on underlying emotions—the fear for her health, the grief over her sister's calls, and the strain of not having to pretend to be strong.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on evening smoking reduction with questions building toward a concrete plan and follow-up. However, the alcohol topic is never acknowledged, and some exploration lingers where a consolidating summary of the plan might have added purpose.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly uses collaborative framing ('figured out together,' 'decide together what comes next') and genuinely co-constructs the plan. Some question sequences feel like guided discovery toward a predetermined goal, and the agenda wasn't explicitly negotiated at the start.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support throughout—'a pace you chose,' 'the call is yours to make,' and offering choices—while respecting her limit of not promising beyond the week. Could have more explicitly framed follow-up as optional and affirmed that slips don't revoke her ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from Aisha herself through open questions, eliciting her past coping resources and emotional connection to change. Missed opportunities include explicitly exploring importance/confidence and digging deeper into her son and health fear as core motivators.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Aisha as the expert—every element of the plan (three cigarettes, tea, pack placement, sister call, Thursday appointment) originated from her. Counselor contributions were limited to the requested leaflet and scheduling.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk with open, future- and obstacle-focused questions, highlighting Aisha's own commitment language. Lacks explicit exploration of confidence/ability and tools like importance/confidence rulers to amplify commitment further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Receives sustain talk without pressure or argument, but does little to actively soften it. Missed chances to use double-sided reflections pairing her fears ('a promise I'll break') with her motivations, or to reframe her gradual approach as a strength.
