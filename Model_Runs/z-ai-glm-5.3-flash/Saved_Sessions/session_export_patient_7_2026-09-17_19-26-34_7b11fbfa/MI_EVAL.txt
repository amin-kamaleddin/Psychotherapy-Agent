# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and nonjudgment throughout. Honored the client's boundary immediately and offered genuine, specific affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex, accurate reflections at nearly every turn, including double-sided reflections that landed well. Client progressively opened up, evidencing felt understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, coherent arc within the client's chosen topic (grades → past coping → library plan). However, the original referral target (household conflict) was never addressed or gently bridged.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: agenda was negotiated, permission asked before pivots, and the client's ideas drove every decision. Her contributions formed the substance of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support — explicitly invited the client's agenda, granted her boundary, and reinforced ownership throughout. No advice imposed; all content came from her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Zero prescriptive advice; strategic evocative questions drew out the client's own reasons and plan. Moved a precontemplative client from monosyllables to a self-generated concrete first step.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the client as the expert, and her smallest steers substantially reshaped the session. High-level power sharing with a hard-to-reach client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional evocation of change talk, eliciting desire, ability, and commitment ('Tuesday, then') plus specific action planning. The client articulated doing it 'for me, not them.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk never reinforced and steadily diminished as the session progressed. Occasionally re-reflected sustain talk before pivoting, keeping focus on barriers briefly.
