# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and specific, well-placed affirmations ('There's real resilience in what you just said'). Falls short of 5 due to occasional formulaic praise and moving past moments of flat discouragement too quickly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture the emotional core (e.g., fear of rejection hurting worse than not trying). Some reflections pivot immediately to questions, rushing emotional material, and Jordan's weary irritability isn't always explicitly reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on Jordan's job situation, guiding progressively from problem exploration to a concrete self-generated plan. Clear, purposeful movement without becoming directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely open questions and a tone of equal partners ('as we sit here together'). Not a 5 because the agenda is never explicitly negotiated or the client invited to steer the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy repeatedly and explicitly reinforced ('no pressure here—this is your space, at your pace'); no advice given, and ambivalence is accepted without pressure. All plans originate from Jordan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from the client through evocative questions ('what was working?', 'where does that idea come from in you?'). Jordan ends the session generating his own plan—the hallmark of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client ('That's your own wisdom talking, not mine'), and Jordan's insights shape the session. The effort-tracking idea and one-application plan come entirely from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary systematic evocation and deepening of change talk, progressing from vague wishing to desire, ability, and commitment ('I could try one decent application this week'). Change talk is reinforced and consolidated into a client-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently handled with double-sided reflections pairing discouragement with change talk. Sustain talk is sometimes engaged at length, and the fear-of-failure material could have been reframed more strategically toward past resilience.
