# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and warm opening, but no affirmations of Dylan's honesty about drinking and no normalization of feeling unfairly singled out. Regard remained basic rather than warm and unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections, but repetitive without deepening. Key emotional material — the fear of being 'set up' for round two and the identity claim 'I'm not some problem kid' — went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session drifted almost entirely into conduct-meeting logistics (exit lines, emails, appeals) rather than the target behavior of alcohol use. Dylan was directly redirected away when asking whether his behavior was 'that bad.'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Expert-advisor mode dominated: numbered plans, counselor-drafted emails, scripted lines. Dylan's observation 'Isn't this session supposed to be about... me?' captures the lack of co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices were consistently offered, but felt pushy ('You're really not letting up, huh?') and plans were imposed immediately. A grounding suggestion was pushed despite explicit rejection.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No open questions explored Dylan's values, goals, or concerns about drinking. His pivotal change talk ('if I got caught again, that'd actually be a problem for me') was never evoked or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Numbered option menus functioned as agenda-driven steering rather than genuine choice-giving. No solicitation of Dylan's own ideas about what would help; the counselor assumed the fixer role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Dylan spontaneously offered an admission of drinking and self-relevant concern about future consequences, but the counselor never followed up. No questions were asked to evoke desire, ability, reasons, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk dominated ('I did nothing wrong,' 'almost 21') and reflections merely mirrored it back without double-sided framing. The 'almost 21' rationalization was acknowledged but never reframed toward discrepancy.
