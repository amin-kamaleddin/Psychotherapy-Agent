# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Jason's frustration early on. However, quickly aligns with his consequence-minimization goal ('Good choice') without affirming his worth or normalizing his ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offers one early reflective statement but then shifts into script-provision mode. Deeper concerns—fear of a permanent record affecting grad school and feeling the lines are performative—are never reflected back.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session has a clear focus, but it is the wrong one: consequence and impression management rather than the alcohol use itself. The drinking behavior is never revisited after Jason's brief dismissal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements exist, such as agenda options and role-play with Jason's buy-in. However, the counselor generates all content while Jason's ideas never shape the intervention, and pushback is accommodated only by swapping scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Superficially supports choice through menu options, but actively steers Jason toward deception-adjacent strategies like 'I prefer not to discuss specifics.' Jason's stated disinterest in drinking-related meetings is met with policy information rather than exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Motivation is treated as residing in the counselor's scripts rather than within Jason. No open questions about his reasons, values, or hopes are asked, and his own motivation statements are ignored rather than developed toward discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offers some choice-giving, but the stance is fundamentally expert-driven, supplying scripts and strategies while Jason passively performs them. Jason's expertise about his own situation is never incorporated into the direction of the work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Essentially no effort to evoke change talk about alcohol use; the alcohol agenda option is abandoned when Jason picks another. A fleeting opening about feeling performative goes unexplored, and no questions about values or goals are asked.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Actively reinforces sustain talk by coaching deflection strategies and validating consequence-avoidance. Jason's statement that drinking 'is not a problem' is never explored, and the session strategy aligns entirely with maintaining the status quo.
