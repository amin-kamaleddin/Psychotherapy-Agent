# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicated consistent respect and nonjudgment despite the patient's resistance and defensiveness. Affirmations were somewhat generic and did not validate his decades of struggle or self-worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening visibly de-escalated resistance (e.g., 'thanks for not hammering on the smoking thing'). Emotional layers like shame and fear of tooth loss were noted but not explored more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on dental health outcomes tied to smoking, progressing logically to a concrete plan. Did not explicitly name the smoking–dental connection as a working focus or end with a summary of the patient's motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner offering menus and inviting input, producing a practical pocket card. Some exchanges were counselor-driven information delivery rather than co-constructed exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout, repeatedly affirming client choice ('your call,' 'revisit quitting only if you choose'). The patient confirmed the style made autonomy safe: 'not because I was talked into it.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Motivation emerged largely spontaneously; few questions drew out the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence. No exploration of discrepancy or confidence, and the closing 'maybe' went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the decision-maker with concrete deliverables serving his self-identified goal. Occasionally presented information in expert/educational format without first asking what he already knew.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Passively allowed change talk ('I'd listen. Maybe') to emerge but made little deliberate effort to evoke or deepen it. The meaningful 'maybe' near the end was not reflected or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk, offering facts without moralizing and never arguing. Did not use double-sided reflections to link sustain talk (misery with past attempts) to his change talk.
