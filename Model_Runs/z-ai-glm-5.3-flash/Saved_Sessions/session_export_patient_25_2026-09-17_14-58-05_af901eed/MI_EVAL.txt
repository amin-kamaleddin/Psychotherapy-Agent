# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and nonjudgmental throughout, with a genuine affirmation of the smoking quit, but affirmations are somewhat formulaic with few personalized acknowledgments of Daniel's specific strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries capture Daniel's words well, but reflections tend to restate surface content rather than explore deeper meaning around fatherhood and social identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus and momentum throughout — assessed patterns, used rulers, built a specific plan for a named upcoming event, and closed with a concrete follow-up, with no meaningful drift despite the opening tangent.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel's own ideas meaningfully shaped the plan and were incorporated into recaps, but the counselor's question-plus-option-list structure made the collaboration feel somewhat scripted rather than jointly generative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-offering and respect for Daniel's decisions, including letting him send the Mike text himself, but frequent pre-packaged option menus subtly steer toward the counselor's preferred solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions drew out some of Daniel's own motivations and strong change talk, but the counselor frequently supplied content (scripts, strategy menus) rather than eliciting Daniel's ideas, leaning on psychoeducation-by-menu.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel was consistently treated as the expert, choosing focus areas, strategies, and timing, but option lists meant he was often choosing from the counselor's menu rather than co-creating from scratch.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of importance/confidence rulers that captured a confidence rise from 6 to 7, but missed opportunities to deepen emerging change talk or explicitly elicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel's sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, with quick pivots toward change, but a double-sided reflection honoring the social value of drinking while linking to its costs would have been stronger.
