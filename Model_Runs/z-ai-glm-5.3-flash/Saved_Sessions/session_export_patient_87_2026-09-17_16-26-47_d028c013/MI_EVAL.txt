# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Marco's strengths and reframed slips as 'data, not failure' without judgment. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and bundled with rapid-fire instructions, diluting warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening, especially around Marco's motivation for his kids and his reframing of slips. Emotional exploration was thin, moving quickly past his discouragement over failed diets and fear of in-the-moment failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on late-night grazing throughout, with every exchange tied to that target behavior. Session progressed purposefully to a concrete, rehearsed plan with a start time and follow-up, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the work jointly, offered menus of options, and incorporated Marco's feedback into the plan. The overall structure and most solution content originated with the counselor, with Marco mainly refining rather than shaping direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Marco's choice through options, permission-seeking, and acceptance of his modifications. The heavy volume of counselor-generated tips gave a slightly directing quality at times.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out Marco's core motivation (his kids), confidence reasoning via scaling, and his own coping ideas. Frequently followed elicitation with pre-packaged solutions, reducing reliance on Marco's internal resources.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Marco's input at points and his contributions shaped plan details, but frequently assumed the expert role, generating solutions (if-then plans, reminders, scripts) before Marco explored his own ideas. The pace of prescriptive tips partially echoed an expert-driven stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and consolidated change talk through values questions, scaling (6→7→8), and commitment language, including generalization to Saturday's party. Sometimes supplied change strategies himself rather than letting Marco fully generate them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and redirected sustain talk, reframing obstacles into planning questions and slips as data. Underused double-sided reflections and sometimes substituted his own solutions rather than drawing out Marco's rebuttals to his own sustain talk.
