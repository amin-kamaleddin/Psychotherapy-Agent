# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicated consistent warmth and respect with no judgment, opening with a safety check and affirming Elena's progress. However, affirmations were somewhat generic rather than deeply individualized to her specific strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged Elena's stated worry but used very few reflective listening statements, jumping quickly to scripts and advice. Deeper feelings—her love for the nephews, fear of her sister's reaction, years of over-giving—were never explored or reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on boundary-setting with Elena's sister, with every exchange tied to the goal and concrete follow-up planning. However, direction came primarily through advice-giving rather than guiding Elena's own exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered a menu of focus options and refined Elena's own draft rather than replacing it, which she explicitly appreciated. The counselor did tend to supply solutions quickly rather than fully co-constructing them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Elena's choice at every step—selecting the focus, drafting her own text, and choosing the version matching her tone—without any pressure. The high volume of prescriptive scripts slightly tempers this, but it remains a strong demonstration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Motivation and content came largely from the counselor, who supplied five scripts, mantras, and ideas rather than evoking them; only one question genuinely elicited from Elena's experience. A skilled MI practitioner would have asked 'What would feel kind but firm to you?' before offering scripts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine power sharing—Elena selected the topic, authored the message, and chose the final version, materially shaping the session. The repeated barrage of ready-made scripts, however, partially undercuts the principle that expertise resides within the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively built on Elena's existing momentum, evoking elaborated change talk ('lighter... more energy in the mornings') and consolidating commitment into plans. Missed opportunities to deepen commitment further, such as confidence scaling or exploring what success would look like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was nearly absent, and the counselor addressed the mild guilt worry constructively without amplifying doubts. The guilt moment could have been handled through reflective reframing rather than reassurance plus advice.
