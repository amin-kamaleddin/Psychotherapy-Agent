# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Polite, nonjudgmental tone throughout with respectful dismissal, but regard is generic and scripted with no affirmation of Isabelle's strengths (saxophone, IB workload management). The warmth feels formulaic rather than personally attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflections are nearly absent—the counselor never reflects Isabelle's identity as a musician/IB student or her mild deflection about the screening. Normalization serves as reassurance rather than genuine empathic understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The referral concern (elevated alcohol-risk) is never named or addressed; the session drifts into generic stress screening and a disorganized, repetitive closing. There is no guiding direction toward the reason for referral.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session is counselor-driven via a rapid-fire checklist of questions and pre-packaged closing options. Isabelle's answers are received but never built upon, undermining any sense of partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices (close, tips, check-in), which respects autonomy superficially. However, the options are narrow and pre-framed, and the counselor concedes immediately without ensuring the risk concern was addressed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No attempt is made to draw out Isabelle's own motivations or reasons for change; questions are closed and informational. Even her spontaneous flicker of engagement with the breathing tip goes unreinforced.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Isabelle's input does not meaningfully shape the session, which resembles fact-gathering rather than power-sharing. The closing options are pre-packaged rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Change talk is never evoked—the target behavior is never named, and closed screening questions elicit only factual denials. No client language favoring change is developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Isabelle's sustain talk ('I'd rather not add another appointment,' 'I really don't think they will') is never explored or softened. The counselor simply agrees with the status quo and allows premature closure.
