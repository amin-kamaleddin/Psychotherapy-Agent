# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with kind reframes like 'progress, not perfection.' However, affirmations are generic and surface-level, missing chances to affirm Amira's specific strengths like her self-awareness about needing small starts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is nearly absent — brief acknowledgments replace complex reflections. Emotionally significant disclosures (past routines sliding, loneliness in a new city, feeling down on herself) were bypassed in favor of tactical tips.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained unwavering focus on increasing physical activity with a concrete, realistic plan from intake to close. Appropriately resisted scope creep when nutrition was raised, keeping focus on movement per her request.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely expert-driven, with monologue-style advice lists dominating consecutive turns. Collaborative openings offered only narrow choices within a pre-determined framework rather than genuine co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some genuine autonomy gestures, including asking if the plan feels doable and respecting her refusal of the slip script. Autonomy was undermined by pre-selecting exercises, rewards, tracking, and schedule, reducing Amira to ratifying a pre-built plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The most significant MI failure — the counselor never elicited Amira's own reasons, goals, or ideas, instead filling every gap with expert strategies. All the abundant change talk was patient-generated despite, not because of, counselor technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist occupied the expert role for most of the session, prescribing exercises, reps, timing, rewards, and even scripting her reminder text. Amira's expertise (her history of routines sliding, need for simplicity) was never actively drawn upon to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Amira spontaneously offered rich change talk (8/10 readiness, desire for strength and energy), but the counselor did nothing to evoke it. Change talk was repeatedly met with another advice list rather than reflections that deepened it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately honored Amira's boundary around not addressing nutrition yet. However, her doubt about past routines sliding was not explored or reframed — it was simply bypassed with a barrage of barrier-planning tips.
