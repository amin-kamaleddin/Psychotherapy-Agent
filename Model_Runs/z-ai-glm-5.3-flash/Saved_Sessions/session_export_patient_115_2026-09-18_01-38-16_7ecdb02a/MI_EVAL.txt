# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently affirmed the patient's efforts (crediting the cut to half) without judgment, treating ambivalence as normal rather than resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured the patient's bind between needing relief and protecting his lungs, including the 'both sides' framing of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stayed focused on smoking reduction throughout, with each exchange building toward a concrete behavioral experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient set his own terms ('you get to set the terms here'), chose the goal number himself, and generated his own strategies (radio breaks, moving the pack, texting his wife).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were consistently returned to the patient — 'if you were going to experiment with something small,' and explicitly validating that a 'maybe' is acceptable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions drew out the patient's own change talk about control, his daughter's influence, and what a slip would mean, rather than the clinician supplying reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Planning was genuinely shared: the patient identified barriers, devised the pack-in-the-boot and gum solutions, and reframed slips as 'data' largely on his own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician evoked and reflected commitment language ('I'd have to think harder about the rest,' 'I can do that much') and linked it to the patient's own hope about control over his asthma.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (smoke breaks as the only quiet, gum failing for his brother) was acknowledged and normalized rather than argued against, though the clinician could have explored the evenings/'beer territory' barrier in slightly more depth.
