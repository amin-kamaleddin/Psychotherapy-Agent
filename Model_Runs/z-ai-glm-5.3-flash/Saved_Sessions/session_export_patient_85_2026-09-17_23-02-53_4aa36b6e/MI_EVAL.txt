**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment or condescension. Warmth felt genuine and individualized, validating both struggle and success.

### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Fully collaborative, with Marco's answers visibly shaping the session each turn. The follow-up plan was co-constructed at his request.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn entirely from the client through evocative questions about his values (family, self-respect). Never argued for change; culminated in self-designed reminders and a confidence rating of 10.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
