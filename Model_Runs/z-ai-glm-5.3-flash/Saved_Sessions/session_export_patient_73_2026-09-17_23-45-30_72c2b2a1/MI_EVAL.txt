# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, unconditional positive regard throughout, with failed attempts framed as courage rather than failure and no judgment ever expressed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, emotionally attuned reflections captured both content and nuance, and Linda repeatedly confirmed feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on smoking cessation from opening to close, building progressively toward goals and culminating in a booked appointment and specific task.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; Linda's answers shaped the agenda, and the session closed with explicitly co-created planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice repeatedly reinforced with no advice imposed; Linda authored every decision, including the timeline and noticing task.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from the client, with every key insight originating from Linda rather than the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing ('you as the expert') that Linda explicitly embraced, showing the partnership landed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful open questions elicited abundant client change talk ('I want to do it — for my health, for the money'), which was then reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged with double-sided reflections and gently pivoted toward change, and slips were reframed effectively; however, sustain talk sometimes received lengthy reflection, giving it more airtime than ideal.
