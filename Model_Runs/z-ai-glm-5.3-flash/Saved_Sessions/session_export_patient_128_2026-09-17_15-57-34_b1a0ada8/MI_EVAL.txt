# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and supportive with no judgment about Tyler's drinking; affirmations were somewhat brief and generic rather than deeply individualized, and ambivalence about party culture wasn't explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured Tyler's core ambivalence, but emotional moments—his fear about the scholarship and relief during rehearsal—weren't reflected back before moving to questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent goal-directed focus throughout, moving from vague ambivalence to a specific, time-anchored plan with accountability (Friday text, Saturday dinner, three-drink cap, Sunday check-in) tied to Tyler's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed: drafts were offered with invitations to revise, role-play was equal, and all decisions emerged from negotiation with Tyler's own wording shaping the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Tyler's ownership was reinforced at every step through offered choices and preference checks, with no pressure or directive language; plan, wording, and backup strategies were all his selections.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from within Tyler rather than supplying it—confidence rulers and 'what would raise it' questions elicited his own reasons, discrepancy, and coping strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tyler's contributions genuinely shaped the plan (his text phrasing, his note wording), though some turns bundled multiple questions and pre-written drafts that slightly steered content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful, consistent evocation via confidence rulers, follow-up probes, if-then planning, and behavioral rehearsal—culminating in Tyler's insight that refusal is 'just a sentence' and strong commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided dwelling on barriers and pivoted to doable plans, but repeated 'what might get in the way' questions briefly sustained focus on obstacles rather than consistently reinforcing change language.
