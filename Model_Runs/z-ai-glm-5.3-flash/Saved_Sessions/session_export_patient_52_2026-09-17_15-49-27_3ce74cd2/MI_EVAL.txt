# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard maintained throughout despite the patient's initial defensiveness, with consistent affirmations (e.g., smoking cessation, role-play courage) and respectful tone from opening to close.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of ambivalence and stress (conflicted feelings, childcare relief, craving ritual) showed strong understanding. Fell short of 5 as deeper emotions like guilt about me-time vs. her son and shame about asking for help were left unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and related stress supports, advancing purposefully toward concrete action with defined success markers and follow-up. No meaningful topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True partnership throughout: co-created role-play, jointly negotiated plan (sparkling water ritual, measured drinks, cheat-sheet, Thursday check-in), with patient input sought at every decision point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice via menus of options, explicit invitations to prefer alternatives, respect for her 'just a week' boundary, and patient-selected check-in time and success criteria. No pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from the patient herself via scaling questions and priority-focused inquiries, with the childcare idea originating from her. Fell short of 5 as early option menus somewhat pre-empted her brainstorming and deeper values weren't systematically evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Patient positioned as the expert, with her ideas (sparkling water swap, childcare referral) visibly shaping the session and plan. Slightly below 5 because initial option menus were somewhat counselor-generated before her own ideas emerged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful open questions elicited change talk ('He's two, he needs me sharp') and commitment to a one-drink experiment; confidence scaling deepened self-efficacy from 6 to 7. Change talk was not always reflected back or amplified explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (minimization, resistance to 'another project') was not argued with; reflections acknowledged her view and pivoted toward priorities, with 'small, reversible experiments' framing effectively defusing resistance. More consistent double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change reasons would be needed for a 5.
