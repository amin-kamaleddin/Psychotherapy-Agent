# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing slips and Marcus's hesitancy; warmth is genuine but some bulleted strategy lists read as checklist-like.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured ambivalence and sensitivity to nagging; reflections sometimes stayed functional rather than exploring the emotional weight of a new chronic diagnosis.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on diabetes self-management throughout, with every turn advancing toward a concrete plan and a scheduled follow-up; no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing — Marcus shaped the coffee cue, fridge placement, and recovery text; some psychoeducational blocks were counselor-generated before inviting his input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Marcus's ownership by offering options rather than prescriptions and letting him choose plan components and check-in timing; autonomy was genuinely honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation was largely drawn from Marcus himself via open questions about what felt doable; could go deeper by exploring why managing diabetes matters to him personally to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Expertise was positioned with the client, who repeatedly chose plan elements; some materials (cheat sheets, strategy lists) were delivered pre-formed rather than co-created, slightly limiting power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evoked change language, leading Marcus to propose a coffee-tied plan, express desire to show his doctor progress, and commit to a follow-up; could be strengthened with explicit confidence exploration (e.g., scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced; the counselor normalized slips and pivoted to recovery strategies, shifting Marcus toward commitment language; less explicit use of double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
