# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with validating statements like 'That makes total sense' and acceptance of Sofia's ambivalence. Affirmations are somewhat generic and formulaic, lacking individualized, specific positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures Sofia's perspective and tracks her closely across the session. Reflections stay at the level of summaries rather than exploring the emotional core—social fear, family pressure, and anxiety driving her use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with each exchange building coherently toward concrete next steps. Direction manifests mainly as offering menus rather than guiding Sofia's own momentum, and her closing statement about not wanting to quit forever goes unaddressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia generated her own refusal line, drafted her own text, and set her own confidence ratings. However, the repeated pattern of counselor-supplied option menus with Sofia choosing among them makes power-sharing uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Sofia's choice and accepts her stated limit of not quitting forever without pressure. The volume of counselor-generated advice crowds out space for Sofia's own reasoning and ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions elicited genuine change talk and Sofia's own problem-solving was drawn out. But key evocative material—the discrepancy between family expectations/college goals and her use—is acknowledged once and bypassed in favor of counselor-provided solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and Sofia's contributions genuinely shaped the session. Yet the counselor repeatedly slips into an expert-driven pattern with bulleted option menus, leaving Sofia in a reactive picking role rather than generating strategies herself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions effectively evoked Sofia's own motivation and commitment language, with confidence rising from 5-6 to 6-7. Missed opportunities to deepen change talk with follow-ups like 'What makes it a 6 and not a 3?' and some change talk was glossed over.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce or amplify sustain talk and frequently pivots toward solutions. However, reflections often restate sustain talk without double-sided reflections, and opportunities to reframe her minimization and develop discrepancy are missed.
