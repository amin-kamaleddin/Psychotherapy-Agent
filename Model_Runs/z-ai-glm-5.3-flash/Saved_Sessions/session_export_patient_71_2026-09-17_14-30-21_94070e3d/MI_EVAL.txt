# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and nonjudgmental tone, framing slips as 'useful data, not failure' and supporting Laura throughout. However, repeated checklist-style recaps feel scripted rather than individually attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional accurate reflections ('enjoyment predicts stickiness'), but the counselor responded to Laura's values exploration with a psychoeducational lecture and only superficially acknowledged deeper emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus converging on a concrete, values-linked plan with minimal drift. The focus narrowed to habit mechanics quickly, bypassing deeper values clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some incorporation of Laura's input, but many turns read as expert-to-client instruction with numbered advice lists and checklists. Genuine co-construction was limited to the final third.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices around habit, tracking, and check-in timing, with Laura's plan largely self-directed. Autonomy was supported through menu options more than reinforcing her intrinsic capacity to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and ideas came almost entirely from Laura spontaneously; the counselor supplied techniques rather than eliciting her own reasons and expertise. Powerful statements about self-love and values went unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Laura was invited to pick and tweak elements, but the stance remained largely expert-driven, with the counselor supplying the core strategies. Laura's best ideas were endorsed but not actively elicited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong change talk emerged but was mostly spontaneous, not skillfully evoked; questions were logistical rather than evocative. Change talk was affirmed but rarely deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Laura's skepticism and 'it's just one night off' talk was neither reinforced nor strategically softened; the counselor bypassed it with more tips rather than double-sided reflections or reframing.
