# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintains warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, explicitly validating Anya's framing and avoiding pathologizing her lower-risk drinking. Affirmations are specific and genuine, consistently affirming her competence and self-knowledge.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is consistently accurate and captures underlying meaning, e.g., 'the limit is the linchpin' and 'the obstacle isn't the run itself, it's the drained version of you.' Complex reflections demonstrate deep understanding of Anya's internal experience, including her fear of being 'the boring one.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on the target behavior without being pushy, moving logically from protecting exams to identifying the limit as the key lever to a sustainable action plan. Each question builds on the last, guiding toward change while respecting the client's pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is fully collaborative, with Anya's ideas driving the session and the counselor asking rather than telling. Power-sharing is genuine—Anyas contributions visibly redirect the session, such as the shift from party management to pre-party stress coping.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decisions are consistently framed as Anya's own ('on your terms'), with options drawn out from her rather than imposed. Her closing spontaneous commitment ('I could start this week, honestly. Nothing's stopping me') reflects genuine internal ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions are consistently elicited from the client rather than supplied, via open questions like 'what have you seen others do' and 'what was different that time.' The session ends with Anya articulating spontaneous commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly and repeatedly positions Anya as the expert ('You're the expert on what pulls you off the couch'), and every plan element originates from Anya. The counselor only reflects and asks, meeting the highest partnership standard.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through evocative questions, and Anya responds with rich commitment language. Ability and commitment statements emerge naturally rather than through pressing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but not reinforced, using double-sided reflections and pivots away from the status quo. The minor limitation is that sustain talk could be pivoted even more decisively in the opening exchanges.
