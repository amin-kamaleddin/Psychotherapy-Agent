# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental despite the client's hostility and defensiveness, using 'that's okay' and 'no pressure' to convey regard. Warmth is somewhat generic and repetitive, with little individualized affirmation of Amina's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes accurate reflections such as noting uncertainty and resentment about being mandated, and validating 'Tired.' Reflections are often formulaic, and the counselor missed the vulnerability beneath 'It's just a lot at home lately.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session cycles through 'you're in control, what do you prefer' without consolidating gains or establishing a focus. The meaningful opening about home being 'a lot lately' was not used to build direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative through offering choice menus, asking permission, and accommodating silence. Somewhat one-directional, as the counselor generates options rather than co-creating based on Amina's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every turn reinforces Amina's ownership of the process, directly countering her complaint of having no choice. No coercive language, and the rejection of the breathing exercise was honored without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Attempts evocative questions like 'What made you come today?' but abandons them quickly after minimal response. The 'a lot at home' disclosure was not explored to evoke what matters to her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly conveys that Amina holds expertise and control, adapting to her preference for silence. Falls short because power-sharing is asserted verbally rather than demonstrated through the client shaping session content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No change talk was evoked, and no strategic efforts were made to elicit it — no questions about values, hopes, or what would make things better. Session remained entirely at engagement level.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Absorbs resistance well without reinforcing it, responding with acceptance and redirection. Lacks active softening techniques such as double-sided reflections or reframes to transform the resistance.
