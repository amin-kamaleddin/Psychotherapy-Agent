# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, but warmth feels somewhat procedural and checklist-like. Individualized affirmations of Anya's specific courage are missing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are brief and content-level, missing deeper feelings like her shame over the debt, fear of failure, and pride in role-play. Reflections often pivot quickly to advice, limiting emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, coherent focus on gambling reduction with a concrete, achievable plan. Direction comes largely through counselor agenda-setting; Anya's broader values are never explicitly explored and linked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Power-sharing is uneven — the psychiatrist repeatedly offers expert-driven, pre-packaged solutions rather than co-creating. Many of the best strategies came from Anya, but her expertise is not credited or built upon.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices are offered, but dense advice delivery suggests 'right' answers, and Anya notes 'that's a lot at once.' Autonomy is respected in form but not consistently reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation arises mostly spontaneously from Anya; the counselor does little to deliberately elicit or develop it. No evocative questions about values, hopes, or discrepancy are posed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist predominantly assumes the expert role with prescriptive advice menus and limited inquiry into Anya's own ideas. Her contributions (Egypt account, skipping the pub) are not recognized as her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges indirectly (Egypt account, cards locked tonight) but is rarely explicitly evoked or amplified. No open questions or confidence scaling are used, and Anya's change statements go unreflected.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but neither is it actively managed. Doubts like 'I've broken promises by Friday' are left unaddressed rather than met with double-sided reflections linking her strengths to her goals.
