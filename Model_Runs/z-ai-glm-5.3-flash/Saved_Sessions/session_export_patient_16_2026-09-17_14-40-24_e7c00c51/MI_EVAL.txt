# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, avoiding criticism of her ongoing use or seasonal-only goal. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic, with little explicit affirmation of Anika as a person beyond her plans.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections track Anika's ambivalence well and acknowledge her basketball priorities. Reflections are often compressed and quickly followed by questions or option lists, leaving emotional meaning (e.g., fear of being 'lame' to her friend) unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (vaping in relation to basketball eligibility) from opening to close. Every exchange advances the change process with no drift into irrelevant territory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative, with permission-seeking before activities and co-constructed role-play. Structure remains somewhat menu-driven, with the counselor presenting option lists rather than jointly generating possibilities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames everything as Anika's choice, honoring her rejection of the firm script and her preference to improvise. Does not push beyond her self-defined seasonal goal and explicitly reinforces her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client motivations via confidence rulers, open questions, and role-play, producing strong client-generated change talk. Somewhat limited by the counselor's frequent provision of ready-made scripts and coping menus that substitute for Anika's own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking is consistent, but the counselor frequently supplies the expertise itself, with Anika selecting from menus rather than generating ideas. Anika's own emerging solutions could have been leveraged more; power-sharing is only partial.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk through scaling questions and rehearsal, with confidence rising from 7 to 8. Less attention is paid to deepening desire/need language or connecting change to Anika's values beyond basketball.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk, but does little to actively pivot it—minimization ('nobody really notices,' 'not quitting for good') is acknowledged neutrally or skipped rather than met with double-sided reflections. Adequate but not strategic softening.
