# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout — opened by affirming her courage to reach out and normalized partial efforts without criticism. Never labeled or disapproved her phone use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were specific and emotionally attuned, e.g., capturing her ambivalence ('relax and stay connected, but it's costing sleep and focus') and naming FOMO and the comfort-seeking barrier. Showed genuine understanding of her experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on nighttime phone use from start to finish, gently redirecting her aside about daytime use. Session progressed logically through exploration, planning, confidence-building, and a scheduled follow-up with a tight recap.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed the plan — she rejected what felt 'intense,' embraced the podcast and status ideas, and told the psychiatrist not to add more. Asked permission before advising and used teamwork language ('let's,' 'we'll check in').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced her ownership with explicit permission-seeking, option menus, and acceptance of her limits ('I'm not promising anything'). She set her own scope (nighttime only), pace, and check-in time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the client via open questions ('what would improving look like?'), morning-success markers, and confidence rulers that moved her from 5 to 7.5. Her own words ('I think I can actually do two nights') show motivation was elicited, not imposed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated her as the expert — every suggestion was a menu ending in 'which sounds doable?' and her choices (podcast, water fallback, offline status) shaped the final plan. Dropped the phone-outside-bedroom idea when she called it intense.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk with structured confidence rulers, follow-up prompts, and success-marker questions. Client progressed from 'maybe I just need to manage it better' to committing to two specific nights.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly acknowledged then pivoted away from sustain talk effectively, but occasionally responded with practical fixes (e.g., DND workarounds for FOMO) rather than exploring the feeling. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections to deepen discrepancy.
