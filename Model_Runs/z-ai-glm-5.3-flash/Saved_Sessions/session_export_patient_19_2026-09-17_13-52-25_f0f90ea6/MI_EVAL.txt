# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirms Anya's honesty and insight. Warmth is somewhat brisk and task-focused, with rapid-fire questioning feeling slightly clinical.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some accurate reflections, but largely skips deep reflective listening for checklists and advice. Key emotional disclosures (fear of Friday nights, losing her friend group, mum's past bailouts) went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on gambling reduction and financial stabilization, steering toward concrete behavioral steps. Direction sometimes comes at the cost of depth, leaving values and emotional barriers underexplored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers options, drafts scripts, and role-plays effectively, but power-sharing is uneven with counselor-generated agendas and recommendation-style suggestions. Anya's own ideas shape the plan more than collaborative technique does.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers menu-style choices and respects Anya's pacing and self-set limits without pressure. Some early prescriptive step lists, and autonomy could be strengthened by explicitly affirming her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens with evocative questions, but the session favors information and menus over drawing out motivation. Rich value statements (Egypt, motherhood, self-respect) were left unexplored, with no rulers or elaboration questions used.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission loosely and offers choices, but the overall stance is expert-driven, supplying most strategies with little inquiry into Anya's own ideas. Her contributions emerged largely on her own initiative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Consolidates Anya's change talk into concrete plans, but the change talk is mostly client-generated despite, not because of, the counselor. Little deliberate evocation, and change talk is rarely reflected back or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk, but also does not actively work with it—loophole-seeking, ambivalence about the girls, and fear of failure pass largely unaddressed. No double-sided reflections or reframing of ambivalence.
