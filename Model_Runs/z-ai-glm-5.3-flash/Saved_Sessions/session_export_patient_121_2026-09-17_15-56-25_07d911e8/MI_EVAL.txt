# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicated respect and nonjudgment consistently, validating Sofia's frustration and affirming her efforts without labeling. Warmth was at times procedural, with less explicit affirmation of Sofia as a person beyond her coping efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captured Sofia's emotional experience, including the weight of being blamed since fifth grade. Missed opportunities to deepen reflections on her fear of rejection and emotional toll, sometimes shifting quickly to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus, tying every exchange to the target concerns and building concrete next steps progressively. Sofia's tangents were acknowledged and redirected toward actionable change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal-partner collaboration with co-edited written content and decisions co-constructed at every step. Sofia visibly engaged as a collaborator, offering edits and evaluating options critically.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength — choices were consistently framed as Sofia's, with menu-style options and explicit offers of control over content. Sofia clearly directed the pace and content of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within, eliciting Sofia's own solutions and client-generated insight about her avoidance. Could be stronger with confidence scaling and deeper exploration of why reconnection matters.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently treated Sofia as the expert, asking permission and offering choices, with her contributions materially shaping the session. At times provided dense expert-formatted content that slightly shifted the expert role toward the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced Sofia's change talk, from selecting strategies she found 'doable' to self-generated observations about avoiding. More explicit confidence and commitment exploration would elevate this.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged Sofia's doubts briefly and pivoted to concrete options rather than amplifying them. Some plan-B responses briefly gave airtime to failure scenarios rather than reframing toward hope.
