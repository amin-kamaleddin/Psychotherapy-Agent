# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with respectful affirmations and normalization of pride and defensiveness. Affirmations occasionally feel formulaic ('Nice work,' 'Good pick'), reducing individualization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening, accurately capturing the post-work habit and the pride barrier. Emotional content like 'being that bloke' and 'deserves better' was passed over quickly in favor of problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on alcohol reduction and anger management from the first utterance. Session flows coherently from triggers to a concrete, consolidated behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-sided plan co-constructed across turns, with Luka selecting, editing, and adding. The question-suggestion-question pattern means the agenda shape remains somewhat clinician-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice offered ('Which feels most doable?', 'Want me to tweak the text?'), with Luka controlling pace and content. Volume of pre-packaged suggestions dilutes autonomy at times.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong client-generated motivation elicited, including 'My partner deserves better than that' and confidence rising from 7 to 9. Many solutions supplied directly by the clinician rather than drawn from Luka's own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Luka positioned as the expert, with his own words shaping the text and partner's intervention line. Occasional solution menus direct the session rather than drawing ideas primarily from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of change talk through success questions and confidence scaling, producing commitments like 'I'll give it a shot this week.' Relies heavily on scaling and planning, with less exploration of values and discrepancy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged respectfully without lingering, but limited use of double-sided reflections. Ambivalence about weekend drinking and full abstinence is left unexplored.
