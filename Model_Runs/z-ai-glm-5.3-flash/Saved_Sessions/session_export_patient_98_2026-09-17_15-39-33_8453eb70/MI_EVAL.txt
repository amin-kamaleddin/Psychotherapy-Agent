# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance maintained throughout, with Mateo's fear of sounding like a complainer consistently validated. However, warmth is somewhat procedural, with brief affirmations and quick pivots to options rather than fully embodied positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Mateo's content and desire for function, but are compressed and quickly followed by questions or option lists. The counselor misses chances to reflect the emotional weight of discouragement and feeling 'wrecked' after sessions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear, consistent focus on communicating with the rehab team and tracking pain, with every exchange building toward the concrete plan. No topic drift; momentum sustained from opening to Mateo's stated commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely consultative, with Mateo editing scripts and the counselor adapting to a lighter tracking method. Power-sharing is real but uneven — Mateo largely selects from counselor-generated menus rather than co-generating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are consistently framed as Mateo's, with his preferences driving final wording and reluctance respected. Heavy option menus, however, can feel like the counselor controls the solution space, with little emphasis on Mateo's ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early open questions effectively elicit Mateo's motivations (truck, yard, walking with his wife), but the counselor then shifts to providing solutions and scripts. Little exploration of Mateo's own confidence, past successes, or reasons to persist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens collaboratively and invites Mateo to choose options, but much of the session is counselor-generated content — scripts, tracking method, backup line, next steps. The expertise structure leans counselor-directed rather than drawing out Mateo's own solutions.
