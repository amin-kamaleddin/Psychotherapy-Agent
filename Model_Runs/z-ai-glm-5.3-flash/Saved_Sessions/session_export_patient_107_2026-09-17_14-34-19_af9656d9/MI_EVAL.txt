# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained nonjudgmental tone and explicit permission to revert, but warmth felt formulaic and checklist-like; no affirmations of the client's strengths or honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was nearly absent — client pushback and ambivalence were met with more advice menus rather than reflections of feelings; content was echoed only factually, never emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on caffeine reduction and secured a concrete, time-limited experiment; moved to action planning too early before ambivalence was explored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session was counselor-driven, delivering pre-packaged plans, templates, and recaps; the client's own ideas were never invited and he had to push back against the plan-heavy approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed choice and reversibility, which the client explicitly valued; the repetitive advice style and repeated recaps subtly undermined this by pushing the counselor's agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely drew out the client's own motivations, values, or solutions — no open exploratory or scaling questions; change talk surfaced spontaneously rather than through skillful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Assumed the expert role with didactic lists, prescriptive menus, and recaps; the client's expertise shaped the direction only superficially.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged via a low-barrier experiment, but was never deliberately evoked, deepened, or reflected back; commitment owed more to the client's own style than skillful technique.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk (fear of crashes, hoping nothing changes) went unaddressed rather than reframed; no double-sided reflections were used, and the counselor mostly sidestepped ambivalence with logistics.
