# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirmed the client's proactive safety check and plan. Warmth felt somewhat formulaic, with repeated perfunctory closing phrases preventing a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal — the counselor never restated the client's core concern in her own words. Responses were largely informational with little reflection of her emotions, urgency, or relief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Stayed on the client's stated target of medication safety without topic drift. However, direction was passive: no change goal was established or explored beyond delivering facts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction was largely one-directional information transfer after the initial agenda-setting question. The client's contributions were acknowledged but did not shape the session, and her perspective was not invited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respected the client's choices throughout, offering printable summary options and honoring her time constraints without pressure. Autonomy statements were implicit in safety advice rather than explicit affirmations of client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Essentially no evocation of the client's own motivations, values, or reasons for change. Motivation was assumed rather than drawn out — no open questions explored her reasons, hopes, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor positioned themselves as the information authority, delivering facts without asking what the client already knew or wanted. The closing offer to help debrief only gestured toward partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client generated some change talk herself, but the counselor only mildly affirmed it without deepening, amplifying, or exploring it. Opportunities to strengthen commitment language were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acceptably handled the client's time constraints by respecting her limits and offering a constructive resource. However, no strategic reframing or double-sided reflection was used to soften the sustain talk skillfully.
