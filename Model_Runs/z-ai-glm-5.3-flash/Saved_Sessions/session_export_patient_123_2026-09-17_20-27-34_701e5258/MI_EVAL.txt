# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, unconditional positive regard with repeated affirmations of Sofia's honesty and worth. Her defensive minimization ('I'm not saying I want to skip them') was left without explicit normalization, keeping acceptance implicit rather than fully explicit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, insightful reflections captured peer-pressure dynamics and emotional meaning (duty and pride). However, the anxiety beneath Sofia's final minimization ('I've got college to worry about') was not reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a purposeful arc from broad topics toward the alcohol-related target, with each question building logically. The early surface exploration and Sofia's detection of a 'hidden agenda' without explicit signposting kept this from a 5.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way exploration where Sofia's responses shaped the session trajectory, framed as shared ('I'd love to hear your perspective'). The questioning funneled toward alcohol without transparently negotiating that agenda, which Sofia noticed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: no advice or pressure, questions framed as curiosity, and explicit choice-validation ('there's no pressure here'). Sofia was consistently allowed to draw her own conclusions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out Sofia's own values (college, family sacrifice) and genuine insight about whose opinions matter. Evocation stalled at insight rather than mobilizing her conditional change talk into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly positioned Sofia as the expert ('you know yourself best,' 'You're the expert on your friend group') with agenda control handed to her from the start. Her contributions demonstrably shaped the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evocative questions elicited genuine preparatory change talk and spontaneous insight ('I don't know why their opinion would matter'). Commitment/ability language was never elicited, and her conditional change talk ('if one of my close friends wasn't drinking') was left undeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly avoided amplifying sustain talk, reframing defensiveness without dwelling on it. The question assuming readiness to 'skip' parties invited a defensive rebuttal; a more graduated, double-sided approach would have preserved momentum.
