# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard throughout, with explicit 'no judgment' framing and affirmations of courage. Client directly confirmed he did not feel judged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally resonant reflections (e.g., 'your legs have taken a lot of choice out of your hands'). Reframes generated meaning the client explicitly confirmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on re-engaging with the community group, moving steadily toward a concrete first step. No meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative — the plan was built entirely from the client's ideas (call ahead, front-row chair, wife driving). Counselor sought input at every step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent autonomy reinforcement: 'on your terms,' honoring the client's limits ('my legs will decide that part'). No persuasion or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited from within; the client articulated his own values, solutions, and insights. No external reasons for change were supplied.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client positioned as expert ('You're the expert on your body'); his contributions shaped the session and plan. Client confirmed feeling heard and equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation progressing from desire ('I want to feel like myself again') to commitment ('one phone call, one chair, one song'). Change talk reinforced with affirmations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk about limitations and fear was acknowledged but consistently reframed toward strengths and pacing solutions. Sustain talk diminished as planning and hope language grew.
