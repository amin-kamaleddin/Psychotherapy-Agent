# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental affirmations. No labeling or shaming even when the client minimizes or discloses uncomfortable truths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and nuanced, capturing both sides of ambivalence and emotional depth. Client goes deeper each time, indicating felt understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear progressive focus on methamphetamine use and consequences, building momentum to a concrete first step. Direction is firm without feeling coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framing as shared curiosity and co-constructed planning; the client's ideas drive the direction while reflections organize them. No expert-driven stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly reinforced from the opening ('you get to decide what happens in this room'). All plan elements are self-generated with no directives or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from the client's own values and solutions rather than supplied. Client articulates the full change rationale in his own words by session's end.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client-as-expert stance is explicit and repeated; all plan elements (middle path, drama classes, Soren plan) originate from the client. Textbook partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is systematically evoked through open questions and a confidence ruler, producing rich desire, need, ability, and commitment statements. Each change statement is reflected and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally acknowledged briefly and pivoted constructively. The 'year from now' question gives some airtime to the status quo, and slightly more strategic double-sided reflections could deepen dissonance resolution.
