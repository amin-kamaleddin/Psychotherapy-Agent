# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with no criticism of missed interview or financial constraints. However, affirmations were generic rather than reflecting deep, individualized regard for her resilience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Brief accurate acknowledgments were present, but the counselor repeatedly jumped to problem-solving scripts instead of reflecting Amina's emotional experience. Vulnerable disclosures about shame and self-criticism went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed firmly focused on childcare barriers, moving through a goal-directed sequence to a concrete plan (Tuesday 4:30 interview, Zara covered). Solutions were pursued so quickly that deeper barriers received no attention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Surface-level collaboration existed through option menus and interactive role-plays, but the counselor supplied virtually all content while Amina performed. Her own suggestions were acknowledged but not built into a co-created plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated choices were offered with no pressure, and Amina directed the sequence of exercises. Heavy script-provisioning subtly undermined ownership, as the 'right way' was authored by the counselor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina's already-high motivation was channeled through rehearsal rather than evoked or expanded. The counselor never explored why employment mattered to her or where her confidence came from.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor largely assumed the expert role, providing pre-written scripts, lists, and directives while Amina's own ideas rarely shaped the intervention. Her insight about catastrophizing went unincorporated into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behavioral rehearsal effectively elicited strong change talk and confidence ('I did it and she didn't even hesitate... That feels good. Really good.'). Building on Amina's inherent confidence through rehearsal was a genuine strength.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with reassurance rather than reinforced, which was adequate but quick. Concerns like flakiness and her pattern of backpedaling were dismissed rather than explored or reframed.
