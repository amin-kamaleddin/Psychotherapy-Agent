# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance ('no judgment here') and frequently affirms Luka's efforts and values. Affirmations are well-placed but occasionally slightly formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and consistent, capturing both sides of Luka's dilemma and the worry beneath the surface. Luka builds on every reflection — a hallmark of precise empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session keeps exceptional focus on alcohol use and its link to violent outbursts, building progressively toward a concrete, client-owned action step. Every question serves the arc; there is no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative in spirit — the clinician asks permission, consults rather than directs, and Luka's ideas shape the agenda, including extending partnership to his relationship with his partner. The exchange is slightly more guided discovery than fully mutual, with the clinician's own perspective rarely woven in.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is reinforced throughout — 'That's your judgment, not mine' — with no advice, pressure, or prescribing. All strategies and even the relapse-prevention backup plan originate entirely from Luka.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key element — reasons, insight, plan, and relapse-proofing — is elicited from Luka, never supplied. By session's end he has articulated his own reasons, plan, and commitment language in his own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Luka as the expert ('you know your early signals better than anyone'). The clinician's contributions are purely facilitative questions that let Luka's own ideas drive the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout domain: change talk is systematically evoked and deepened, transforming 'I cut down' into purposeful commitment ('this week... Thursday'). Luka ends the session speaking almost entirely in change language, including his own cost-benefit analysis.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well with double-sided reflections that pivot toward change without agreeing or dwelling. Minor deduction for briefly elaborating the reward-function of alcohol ('what might come close?') before pivoting.
