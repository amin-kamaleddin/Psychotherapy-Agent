# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with frequent individualized affirmations and explicit permission to adjust without judgement. Camila confirms feeling respected rather than dismissed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both surface content and subtle meaning (e.g., the 'knowing-it-and-feeling-it gap'). Camila's closing feedback confirms she felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity, advancing step-by-step from doability to a concrete, client-owned plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed from Camila's own knowledge, and she is invited to shape the session's focus and future check-ins. She experiences the work as equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is reinforced at every stage; the counselor deliberately returns Camila's direct question to her own experience and frames failure as adjustable. Camila explicitly values this: 'You didn't hand me answers—you made me find my own.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from within—past successes, self-knowledge, hope, and personal meaning—rather than persuasion. Camila names the evocative questioning as the most useful part of the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is repeatedly located in the client, and her own ideas (shoes by the door, after-dinner timing, fallback plan) become the actual intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful elicitation of change talk via experience questions and confidence ratings, culminating in strong commitment language ('willing to give it a go this week') and ownership ('It's mine').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (couch, energy dips, 'why bother') is acknowledged briefly and reframed via double-sided reflections into usable self-knowledge, never amplified.
