### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance without confronting denial or labeling. Affirmations are generic and lack specific validation of Viktor's strengths (honesty, willingness to sacrifice buddy time).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers accurate summaries but reflections are brief and often followed by stacked multi-question turns. Underlying emotions—irritation, fear of his wife's disappointment, pride in self-control—are largely bypassed for practical problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on the Sunday fights and steadily guides toward a concrete, testable plan. The relational target rather than substance use appropriately follows Viktor's readiness, though it's only indirectly tied to the referral issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Checks preferences, lets Viktor pick strategies, and incorporates his ideas into the final plan. However, the counselor supplies most option menus and defaults to a checklist rhythm rather than fully joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Viktor's choice with options and accepts his 'for the peace, not a problem' framing without challenge. Falls short by not explicitly reinforcing his ownership of success and slightly pre-framing his attributions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some motivation (quieter home, admission of defensiveness), but much of the session is technique-driven rather than sustained elicitation. Missed opportunities to explore the meaning of the 'disappointed look' and develop discrepancy between his values and weekend behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and offers menus at times, but several turns feel agenda-driven with rapid scaling questions and counselor-generated suggestions. Viktor's closing 'That's what you want to hear, yeah?' suggests he perceives an expected agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively builds on Viktor's small flicker of change talk into a concrete plan with commitment language and follow-through. Could deepen further by reflecting and elaborating change talk rather than moving quickly to planning mechanics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoids arguing with denial and redirects sustain talk toward relational consequences Viktor cares about. Occasionally restates sustain talk without a double-sided reflection pivoting toward change.
