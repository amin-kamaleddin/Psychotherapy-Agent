# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental ('No judgment — just info'), with no disapproval even when Brandon minimized his drinking. Affirmations were somewhat brief and generic ('Nice plan'), lacking deeper individualized affirmations of strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening, including a strong ambivalence reflection capturing guilt over the missed birthday. Reflections stayed at summary level rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning behind the stress, guilt, and family tension.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on drinking throughout, moving purposefully from ambivalence exploration to a concrete, client-owned experiment and follow-up plan. Each turn advanced the change agenda with no meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Brandon's modifications (Monday start, Friday exception, his own texts, the walk) shaping the final design. Brandon explicitly experienced it as 'figuring it out myself.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong ownership language throughout ('You're in charge here'), with menus of options and slips framed as information, not failure. Brandon directly confirmed feeling in control: 'I'm handling it, not being handled.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within via pros/cons exploration, confidence scaling, and eliciting Brandon's own obstacles and solutions. Evocation stayed within the one-week frame, missing exploration of deeper values and longer-term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located with the client, accepting Brandon's rejection of drafted texts without pushback. Brandon's reflection that the session felt like 'figuring it out myself' is direct evidence of successful power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilfully evoked change talk, including 'hangovers are getting worse,' a confidence rating of 6, and self-generated strategies (delay, Mike, the walk). Commitment language stayed tied to the one-week experiment rather than longer-term change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argued against sustain talk, using neutral double-sided reflections and framing the Friday exception as 'useful information, not failure.' Sustain talk was acknowledged but not consistently pivoted back to Brandon's own reasons for concern.
