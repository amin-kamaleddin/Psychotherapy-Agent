# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with frequent normalizing and affirmations throughout. Affirmations tended to be somewhat generic and could more explicitly honor Kevin's strengths like his honesty and self-awareness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately captured Kevin's core ambivalence between enjoying nights out and recognizing risks. Deeper emotional reflection of his embarrassment and work-stress drinking was missed at times.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on the target behavior with a clear arc from scaling to a concrete commitment plan. The central coping-drinking issue was deferred rather than addressed, and early stacked questions diluted focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong co-construction in the second half, but early option generation was clinician-driven with stacked questions shifting power toward the counselor. Kevin mostly selected from the counselor's menu rather than shaping the session from the start.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Kevin's choice with no pressure toward abstinence and respect for his rejections of certain ideas. Autonomy was supported more through menu-offering than reinforcing Kevin's ownership of the underlying motivation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of rulers and open questions drew out Kevin's own concerns, motivations, and insight about social support. Evocation became more procedural later, and the deeper stress-relief motivation was surfaced but not explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited Kevin's plan and his articulation of the if-then strategy, but frequently supplied pre-formed option menus in an advice-giving role. Kevin was never asked to generate his own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of rulers and evocative questions drew meaningful client change talk and rising confidence across the session. Importance (4/10) was never revisited, and coping-drinking alternatives were not amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about coping-drinking was acknowledged nonjudgmentally but not reframed or paired with double-sided reflections linking it to Kevin's concerns. This central barrier was deferred to a future session rather than resolved.
