# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with a defensive teen, validating resistance ('totally okay,' 'totally fair'). Affirmations are brief and generic, limiting unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening and an accurate summary of Marcus's drinking and concerns. Reflections stay at surface content, missing deeper emotional themes like peer pressure and fear of judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and risk management with no wandering. Direction subtly shifts toward 'not getting caught,' and there is no closing summary or agreed next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance with check-ins, menus of options, and Marcus's ideas shaping the final plan. Falls short because initial tips were clinician-generated and explicit joint problem-solving language is absent.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear strength: repeatedly asks permission, offers explicit choices, and lets Marcus steer decisions without pressure or lecturing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some of Marcus's own motivations (hangovers, parental discovery, peer pressure) but never develops them as reasons for change or explores his goals and values. Relies largely on providing strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites input and tailors suggestions to Marcus's world, but the session tips toward advice-giving with Marcus in a reactive consumer role and little acknowledgment of his expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk ('maybe I'd try something like that,' 'I could see using this stuff at Danny's party') but does not deepen it through commitment amplification, confidence exploration, or scaling questions. Emerging change talk is left unaffirmed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoids arguing with minimizations, meeting them with neutral or reframing reflections. Occasionally engages with sustain-adjacent framing (tips to avoid getting caught) that implicitly accepts minimization.
