# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout. The counselor affirmed the patient's honesty and skepticism (e.g., 'That honesty is exactly what makes this work') with no disapproval or conditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captured both sides of ambivalence and deeper meaning (autopilot drinking, father's routine, desire for clarity). Reflections precisely tracked subtle shifts like 'doable' and 'can't un-notice that.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction from start to finish with zero topic drift. Each turn advanced toward the concrete two-week plan and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; the entire experiment (duration, measures, tracking, slip rule) originated from the patient with the counselor reflecting and amplifying rather than prescribing. Closing framing ('we look at it together') embodied equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support—the counselor explicitly asked what would make the experiment feel self-chosen and honored the possibility of keeping the beers. The patient's ownership ('my call, my data') confirmed it worked.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited from within rather than supplied; no advice or persuasion was offered. Strategic questions drew out intrinsic reasons (marathon goals, sharp mornings, honesty, choosing deliberately) with motivation visibly deepening across the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly positioned the patient as the expert ('You're the expert on your own body and training') and let his contributions drive the plan. Reflection and amplification replaced prescription throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk via open questions and affirmations, with commitment language strengthening progressively. The session culminated in a concrete, self-authored plan with start date, tracking method, and relapse rule.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used double-sided reflections and reframes toward self-experimentation rather than argument. Minor growth area: some sustain talk ('I still think I'm probably fine') was allowed to stand without an active pivot, though this arguably reflected wise acceptance.
