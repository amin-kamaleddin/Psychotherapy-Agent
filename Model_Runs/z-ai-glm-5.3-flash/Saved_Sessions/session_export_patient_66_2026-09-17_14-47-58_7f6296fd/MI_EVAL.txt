# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, explicitly stating 'I'll listen without judgment and support your choices.' Even when Kareem pushed back ('I'm forty-one, not a robot'), the counselor stayed warm and non-judgmental without defensiveness or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Kareem's perspective, including what he valued most about smoking breaks. However, reflections were sometimes brief and lacked depth exploring emotionally loaded moments, such as his worry about how slips escalate ('that's how it starts, doesn't it').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on workday smoking and moved steadily toward a concrete plan: anticipating barriers, rehearsing peer-pressure responses, planning slip recovery, and securing a specific commitment (text Dara tonight). Kareem left with an explicit, actionable plan without any coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely joint problem-solving, with Kareem's own ideas (courtyard backup, Dara strategy) genuinely shaping the plan. Fell short of a 5 because the counselor at times drifted into advice-giving with pre-packaged suggestion lists rather than fully drawing out Kareem's own solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Kareem's ownership of decisions, offering options as choices rather than directives. His refusals (no script rehearsal, no phone reminder) were respected without pressure, and his closing 'this feels more sorted than I expected' reflects autonomous ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key motivations and commitments came from Kareem's own words, with no lecturing about health consequences. Kareem self-generated insight ('that's how it starts, doesn't it'), the hallmark of skilled evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed that expertise resides with Kareem, and his contributions shaped the plan substantially. It fell short of a 5 because the counselor supplied ready-made menus of options (coping strategies, sample scripts) rather than consistently letting Kareem generate ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and developed change talk through open and scaling questions ('What one change would raise your confidence from a 6 to an 8?'), eliciting repeated commitment language from Kareem. He ended with 'I reckon I've got that handled now.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and pivoted quickly to strengths and readiness. However, some exchanges amplified status-quo reasons, and ambivalence ('one smoke wouldn't be the end of the world... though that's how it starts') was acknowledged only lightly rather than reframed with double-sided reflections.
