# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and warmth with a resistant client; honored his confidentiality request. Affirmations were somewhat sparse and generic rather than personalized to his values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured his ambivalence and relational motivation well. Reflections were sometimes summary-style and missed deeper emotional meaning (e.g., the emptiness when his wife stopped talking to him).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus kept every exchange tethered to tobacco despite provocations. Ended with concrete, scheduled next steps directly serving the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan was genuinely co-created with Richard setting his own conditions. The options segment was somewhat list-heavy and expert-toned, and agenda-setting wasn't explicitly negotiated early.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: explicit support for his choice, permission-seeking, and no pressure even during conditional commitment. The closing plan was entirely Richard-constructed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew self-generated motivation via the importance ruler and Richard's own 'actual proof' condition. Pivotal change talk ('I don't want to lose her') was acknowledged but not deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked permission, offered choices at every decision point, and let Richard set session terms. The mid-session information list briefly shifted toward an expert stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions evoked notable change talk from a pre-contemplative client, including commitment-adjacent language. Missed chances to reflect and amplify powerful statements like 'she's worth more to me than the smokes.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoided arguing or reinforcing sustain talk, rolling with resistance effectively. Fewer double-sided reflections could have strategically weighed his relational motivation against sustain talk.
