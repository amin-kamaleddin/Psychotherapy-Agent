# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgmental framing of slips as information rather than failure, with affirmations of Anisa's motivation. Deducted because her deep shame ('leper in a non-leper colony') was never explicitly acknowledged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate simple reflections appeared early, but empathy faded as the session became plan-driven. Emotionally rich statements about shame, guilt over broken promises, and nervousness were met with logistics rather than emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained exceptionally clear focus on smoking cessation with no drift, moving consistently toward concrete, actionable steps. Anisa left with a trigger map, NRT plan, environmental cues, a daughter note, and a follow-up appointment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative framing and incorporation of Anisa's own ideas (balcony, walking shoes). However, the power balance was uneven, with substantial expert content delivered before negotiation and Anisa largely reacting to the counselor's proposals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent choice-offering and reframing slips as information support Anisa's ownership of the change. Deducted because the volume of prescriptive advice (dosing rules, drafted messages, pre-made scripts) at times positioned the counselor as the decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opening questions effectively drew out Anisa's own motivations, but evocation largely disappeared in favor of information-giving. Her readiness statements and own coping ideas were passed over rather than developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Menus and invitations for Anisa's input were offered, but the stance often shifted into expert mode—prescribing mapping steps, gum techniques, and drafted messages without first soliciting her ideas. Anisa's contributions shaped the plan only somewhat.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong opening questions elicited meaningful change talk from Anisa. However, key commitment statements ('this time I want to actually see it through') were passed over rather than reflected, amplified, or consolidated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about past failures and craving fears was consistently met with normalization and reframing toward action. Deducted because it was sometimes bypassed rather than acknowledged with a double-sided reflection before redirecting.
