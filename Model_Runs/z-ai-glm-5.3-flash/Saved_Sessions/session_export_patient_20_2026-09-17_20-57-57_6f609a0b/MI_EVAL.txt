# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with explicit anti-judgment statements ('no lectures waiting when you come back') directly validated by Brandon's surprise at not being lectured. No labeling, disapproval, or subtle shaming anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurate, capturing emotional undertones like 'The mornings are mine.' However, reflections sometimes stay at the content level rather than exploring the emotional weight of Brandon's minimization and all-or-nothing failure pattern.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on alcohol use from open exploration to a concrete behavioral experiment, with each exchange advancing momentum. A mid-session summary and acknowledgment of the riskier weekend/Friday drinking pattern would have strengthened directionality.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully egalitarian interaction where Brandon's answers visibly determine each next step, with every plan element supplied by him. The co-negotiated 'deal' at the end reflects equal footing and no trace of an expert-driven stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced at nearly every turn ('on your terms, for your mornings'), including the experiment framing that honors Brandon's control over scope and pace. His internalized ownership ('taking full credit... I'll be honest about that too') shows effective autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is elicited entirely from within the client, with Brandon articulating his own reasons (mornings, gym, stress) and generating his own success metrics. Strategically sequenced evocative questions produced commitment language and a self-built plan, exactly as MI intends.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary power-sharing: Brandon generates every concrete plan element (sparkling waters, gym, two-drink cap, Monday start) and the psychiatrist explicitly attributes ownership ('you did the heavy lifting'). Brandon's contributions demonstrably shaped the entire session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation and strategic reflection of change talk across desire, ability, reasons, and commitment, culminating in Brandon's self-generated plan. Selective reflection ('you're noticing what's in it for you') consistently amplifies his own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is neutralized with autonomy-affirming reflections and smooth pivots rather than being amplified. Double-sided reflections deliberately linking sustain talk to change goals were missed opportunities to consolidate ambivalence resolution.
