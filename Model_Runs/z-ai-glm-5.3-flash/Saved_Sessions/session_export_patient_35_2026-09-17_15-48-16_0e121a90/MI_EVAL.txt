# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and non-judgment, including skillful handling of Elena's defensiveness ('I'm not stupid') by affirming her protective efforts. Affirmations are somewhat formulaic and there's no explicit normalization of her past failed quit attempt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections capture both the emotional and practical sides of Elena's ambivalence. However, reflections are sometimes brief, moving quickly to questions and missing deeper feelings like guilt about her son's infections and shame about past failed quits.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, clear focus on smoking and child smoke exposure throughout, with every turn tied to concrete smoking-related actions. Session ends with a specific incremental plan (jacket tonight, gum this weekend, if-then at work).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely equal partnership, with Elena's ideas adopted into the plan and the counselor asking rather than telling. The informational blocks (thirdhand smoke, gum instructions) are delivered as long expert monologues without checking what she already knows, briefly shifting power toward the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit permission-seeking, brainstorming invitations, and a choice-preserving plan design ('if I still want a cigarette after that, I may smoke'). No coercive language; Elena declines options freely without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of rulers, 'what would move the number up' questions, and eliciting her own strategies. Falls short of 5 because motivation stays channeled toward her son without developing Elena's self-referential reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is consistently treated as the expert, with permission-seeking and her own ideas (gum pocket, if-then rule) shaping the plan. Slightly limited by the counselor supplying most of the option menu rather than eliciting her ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent evocation via importance/confidence rulers and 'what would move the number up' questions. Elena's change talk grows across the session, culminating in her own plan design and 'maybe that matters too' about not smelling like an ashtray.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor mostly avoids arguing, reflecting Elena's protective intent and accepting her harm-reduction framing rather than correcting it. Only limitation is the thirdhand-smoke psychoeducation, which briefly shifts into an expert-lecturing register that could invite counter-argument.
