# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirmed Jason's perspective (e.g., 'Thanks for being straight with me'). Acceptance was somewhat procedural, pivoting quickly to problem-solving rather than conveying deeper warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate simple reflections appeared early but declined as the session became advice-driven. Deeper emotional layers — Jason's anxiety about his future and self-image — were rarely reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused but narrowed to appeal logistics rather than the actual clinical concern of alcohol use. The target behavior was essentially never addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partial collaboration through preference questions and incorporating Jason's facts, but the counselor repeatedly supplied content while Jason reacted rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some menu-based choices were offered without coercion, but heavy advice-giving and pre-written drafts undermined Jason's ownership. Autonomy was limited to procedural decisions rather than the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Aside from one importance ruler, motivation was largely counselor-supplied. Jason's values surfaced naturally but were never evoked further or connected to drinking behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor largely assumed the expert role with detailed legal instructions and drafted statements. Jason's own ideas shaped the session only marginally.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** One well-timed importance ruler elicited change-adjacent talk (importance of 8), but it was never deepened. Jason's rare accountability ('I can own that part') went unaffirmed and undeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced but was tolerated rather than strategically softened. Rationalizations were occasionally reflected without double-sided reflections pairing them with change-oriented material.
