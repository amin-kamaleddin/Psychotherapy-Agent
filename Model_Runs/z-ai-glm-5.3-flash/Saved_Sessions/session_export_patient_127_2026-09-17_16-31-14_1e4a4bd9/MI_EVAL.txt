# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout with no labeling despite talk of crime and self-criticism; slightly limited by occasionally formulaic affirmations and little explicit normalization of ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate opening summary and reflections capturing Timur's situation and values, though reflections rarely reached deeper emotional layers like shame and fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Relentless focus on staying crime-free, funneling a broad opening into a concrete Friday 9am call with backup, script, alarm, reward, and accountability check-in with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way: permission sought at each step, Timur's ideas incorporated, and role-play joined at his request, resulting in a plan Timur clearly owned.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices consistently offered rather than directives; Timur set the backup time, alarm label, and reporting style, and explicitly owned the change ('This one's mine to prove').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation effectively drawn from Timur (Fiona, 22 days clean, porch routine), though evocation was front-loaded and confidence was never directly explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Timur's input shaped the plan (day, backup, alarm label, check-in format), but the counselor supplied most of the structural elements while Timur mainly approved.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation and consolidation of change talk culminating in 'consider it decided'; limited by no explicit confidence assessment or deepening of identity-related themes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged briefly and consistently reframed into concrete action; the 'lazy' self-label was met with a plan rather than an explicit affirmation challenging it.
