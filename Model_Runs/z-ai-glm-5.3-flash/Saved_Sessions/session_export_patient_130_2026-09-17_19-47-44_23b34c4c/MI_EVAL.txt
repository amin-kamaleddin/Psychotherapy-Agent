# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, even when Viktor blames his wife; affirmations are frequent, individualized, and delivered warmly. Closing explicitly accepts possible relapse without conditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening highly accurate and emotionally attuned, with complex double-sided reflections capturing emotional meaning. Viktor's deepening disclosures evidence feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus on drug use and relational consequences, advancing steadily from ambivalence to a concrete plan. Brief wife-blaming moment allowed to stand without steering back to target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan co-constructed from Viktor's ideas with genuine inquiries and mutual agreement ('Deal?'). Occasional rhetorical flourishes slightly position the clinician as interpreter of the client's progress.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced: 'You're in charge of the plan,' 'No signatures, no pressure.' Outcomes framed as Viktor's choice regardless of his wife's reaction, with no coercive or persuasive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every motivation drawn from the client rather than supplied by the psychiatrist. Evocative questions produced Viktor's most significant shift ('coming home without the dread would be for me').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client, and the plan emerged entirely from Viktor's own words. The clinician never prescribes content, only builds on Viktor's exact language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and reinforcement of change talk through open questions and amplified reflections. Pivotal question ('worth it regardless of her reaction') produced Viktor's key shift to self-motivated change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk met with neutral or double-sided reflections rather than argument, and never reflected more than change talk. The coworker comparison could have been redirected slightly more actively.
