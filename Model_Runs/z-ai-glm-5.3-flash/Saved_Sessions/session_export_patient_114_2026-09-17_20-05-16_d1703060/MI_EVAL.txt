# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental throughout, with affirmations of Ryan's honesty and validation of his ambivalence. Short of 5 due to occasional formulaic warmth and lack of explicit normalizations of struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections consistently captured Ryan's perspective and deepened understanding. Emotional nuance like his scholarship fears was noted but not always explored at a deeper emotional level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use with no topic drift; session progressed logically from the incident through ambivalence to a concrete plan with evaluation criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session framed as joint exploration with Ryan's ideas driving the plan. However, one late counselor response drifted into first-person language echoing Ryan, briefly blurring role boundaries.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices framed as Ryan's own with no coercive advice, and his right to test the plan validated. Not a 5 due to no explicit ownership statements and pacing somewhat leading toward pre-selected options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within Ryan, eliciting his own reasons and strong change talk. Relied on a similar questioning pattern; importance/confidence rulers were never used to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly attributed expertise and insight to Ryan ('Those are your insights, not mine'), shaping session direction. Occasionally bordered on scripted praise rather than genuinely negotiated power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced strong commitment and desire language, highlighted change talk explicitly, and elicited concrete planning. Some change talk was reflected rather than strategically deepened with confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged through double-sided reflections without reinforcement, consistently pivoting toward Ryan's own reasons for change. Redirection was sometimes implicit rather than explicitly strategic reframing.
