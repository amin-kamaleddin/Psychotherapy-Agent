# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental ('choosing to talk takes courage,' 'we'll troubleshoot nonjudgmentally'), with warm acceptance even when the patient admits minimizing. Warmth is somewhat brief and task-focused, with limited deep affirmations of her strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture both change and sustain talk, including deeper motivations like avoiding her mother's complications and wanting a blunt check. Occasional pivot to planning leaves less room to explore the emotional weight of her fears and family history.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on medication adherence throughout, moving logically from barrier identification to anchoring, backups, tracking, and a time-bound check-in without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed: the patient's coffee habit became the core intervention, and the check-in format, question style, and reminder wording were all negotiated. The patient's closing comment about walking out 'with a plan' confirms effective partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforced patient ownership consistently ('I'm here to support whatever you decide,' offering choices on backups and check-in style) while still guiding toward action. Patient's remark that it felt 'less like a lecture' is strong evidence of success.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within using confidence rulers twice (6 to 7), eliciting her own solutions and reasons ('luck runs out,' family, avoiding mum's path). Less time was spent developing the emotional core of her motivation before shifting to logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Patient's expertise was clearly honored — she chose the pillbox, wallet backup, car dose, checklist format, and blunt check-in style. Rapid option-lists occasionally felt slightly leading rather than purely client-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence rulers, if-then planning, and celebration/troubleshooting questions evoked strong commitment language ('I do want this to work,' 'I'm ready to try'). More elaboration of her own reasons for change beyond the initial reflection would elevate this to a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated rather than argued with ('you don't want to blow it out of proportion'), then effectively redirected to barriers and solutions. Some minimization ('I'm not promising perfection') was met with planning rather than gentle reframing of her history of failed attempts.
