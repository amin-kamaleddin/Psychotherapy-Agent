# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful tone with affirmations that Erik receives well, but praise becomes formulaic and repeated nearly verbatim ('protects the real wins'). More individually tailored affirmations needed for a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are mostly content-level summaries; Erik's emotional material (the sinking feeling of comparison, fear of bailing) was repeatedly bypassed in favor of logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior throughout, guiding every exchange toward concrete, testable commitments with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely two-sided with the plan co-constructed and Erik's ideas driving it, but rapid multi-question turns feel interrogative and expert-paced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices consistently framed as Erik's, and his rejection of suggestions was respected without pushback, though pre-selected option menus slightly constrain his choice space.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik's reasons and confidence were elicited and motivation visibly built, but emphasis stayed on logistics rather than deepening his powerful self-generated statements ('my race, my pace,' 'my body saying thanks').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Erik positioned as the expert with his own ideas substantially shaping the plan, though the counselor steered the agenda with multi-part questions and predetermined solution menus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Solid change talk emerged (confidence rising from 7 to 8, 'the plan feels real'), but mostly through logistics-oriented questions rather than deliberate evocation of the client's reasons and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged briefly and redirected efficiently into problem-solving, though emotional sustain talk (the sting of comparison) was ignored rather than worked through.
