# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; specific individualized affirmations ('You just did something that matters') and explicit decoupling of behavior from character ('doesn't mean you're lazy').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, forward-moving reflections including a textbook double-sided reflection capturing both the workload barrier and the client's openness to a lunch walk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained unwavering focus on physical activity, moving strategically from exploration to barrier-solving to relapse prevention without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership—asked rather than told, incorporated client ideas at every step; the client explicitly noted the difference from expecting to be told to exercise more.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed participation as optional ('if anything'), repeatedly invited client-generated solutions, and offered no direct advice—every plan element originated from the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew out the client's own motivation and insights, culminating in his self-authored commitment ('I want to see if I can') and realization about all-or-nothing thinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly located expertise with the client ('you know your workdays better than I do') and repeatedly credited him with building his own plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation and reinforcement of change talk; client's language deepened from vague intent ('I need to find the time') to concrete commitment ('I'm going to aim for two').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Briefly validated sustain talk then immediately reframed it—past calendar failures were transformed into an asset for planning around setbacks.
