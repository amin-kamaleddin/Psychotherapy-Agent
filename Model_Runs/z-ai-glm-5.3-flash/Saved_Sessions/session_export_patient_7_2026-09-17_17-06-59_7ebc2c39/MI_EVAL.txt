# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with a resistant adolescent, tolerating dismissiveness without defensiveness. Some responses felt formulaic ('You're in charge here' repeated), and warmth could be more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, accurately capturing that being 'painted as the problem' felt invasive rather than the fight itself. Early question-stacking and some brief restatements instead of deepening emotional meaning lowered the score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on de-escalating conflict with mom, moving to a concrete, rehearsed line Amina agreed to try tonight. Opening contained four scattered questions and no closing summary tied the plan to Amina's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered menus of options rather than directives, and Amina's input reshaped the plan (she rejected breathing exercises and authored her own line). Client ideas drove direction only after engagement was established rather than from the start.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: repeatedly handed control to Amina ('You're in charge here,' 'No pressure — you choose'), honored her request for silence, and accepted 'No promises' without coercion. Consistent, skillful reinforcement of client ownership throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew motivation from within — Amina generated her own insight, solution, and words for the plan. Early evocation was blunt question-stacking, and values/confidence were not systematically explored to deepen emerging motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Deferred expertise to Amina ('tell me one word you want in it and I'll shape it'), and the final plan line was entirely hers. The counselor still structured most choice menus, and early question-bombing undermined equal footing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited change talk: Amina spontaneously said 'I might actually try it. Like tonight' and voiced her own insight about walking away instead of yelling. Early evocation was thin, and no confidence/importance scaling deepened the commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently acknowledged sustain talk ('Neither of those fixes anything') without arguing, using double-sided reflections to gently soften resistance. Some reflections briefly amplified the futility frame ('mom already seems decided') without an immediate pivot to agency.
