### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard despite defensiveness, with genuine affirmations and no judgment or lecturing. Sofia explicitly confirms the warmth landed ('this is... not what I thought').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures emotional nuance beyond surface content, e.g., the college-pressure paradox and the fitting-in-vs-craving distinction Sofia immediately validates. Accurately reflects ambivalence in the client's own words.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear trajectory from ambivalence to discrepancy, small step, coping plan, and check-in design, with every exchange advancing the target behavior without feeling pushed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as an explicit equal ('figuring this out alongside you'), and Sofia's answers genuinely shape the session — the plan and check-in design are her construction. Follow-up structure is negotiated rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is repeatedly reinforced ('your life and your choice,' 'no promises, your pace'), and the plan is framed as bounded ('one weekend, your terms') to counter all-or-nothing fears. Honesty about setbacks is embraced, reinforcing ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from the client via values, ideal-self, and discrepancy questions, never supplied by the psychiatrist. Sofia's closing 'weirdly kinda want to know now' reflects client-generated intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client, and Sofia's own strategies (money step, Priya, 'I'm broke') form the plan. Questions seek her input rather than prescribing, with visible power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evokes and deepens change talk, producing concrete commitments (cutting back, saving $30, studying sober) and amplifying them with selective summarizing. The 'real you' question elicits powerful discrepancy language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never amplified and is skillfully converted into change talk (e.g., pivoting minimization to her doubt). Minor gap: the anxiety-as-justification theme ('pot helps me cope') is acknowledged but deferred rather than actively reframed or explored further.
