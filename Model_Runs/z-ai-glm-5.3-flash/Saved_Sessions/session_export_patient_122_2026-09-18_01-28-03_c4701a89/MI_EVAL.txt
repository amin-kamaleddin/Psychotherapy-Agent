# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental with frequent, personal affirmations. Affirmations tend to be tied to progress and insight rather than unconditional regard for Sofia independent of outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate complex reflections capturing both content and emotion. Reflections sometimes lean toward reframing rather than dwelling in deeper emotional material like the loneliness of relocation or past self-disappointment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on building a sustainable exercise routine with each turn moving the conversation forward. Purposeful, sequenced questions guided toward change without drifting or rushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully equal-partner interaction with substantive content supplied by Sofia and the counselor contributing mostly questions and reflections. The plan's direction emerged jointly, with Sofia co-constructing every element.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice-giving or pressure; every idea originated with Sofia and choices were framed as hers throughout. Autonomy was actively fostered, including her right to miss nights without it meaning failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn out from Sofia rather than installed, producing her own reasons (sleep, energy, pride) and powerful insights. The entire change plan was evoked from her own expertise and words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor repeatedly positioned Sofia as the expert ('That's your own wisdom, not mine') with all plan content Sofia-generated. Genuine power sharing was visible in Sofia's recognition that her own words carry belief.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language through open questions. Change statements were reflected back and deepened, moving Sofia from 'maybe' to 'shoes, note, walk — I can do that much.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and pivoted to Sofia's own solutions, and closing doubt was handled gracefully. The couch trap was reflected at length across turns; more strategic double-sided reflections could have minimized focus on barriers.
