# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Timur's wisdom and reframed his 'lazy' self-label without judgment, even when he disclosed temptation to reoffend. Client explicitly reported feeling respected and 'not talked down to.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured both content and emotional nuance, including double-sided reflections. Occasional reflections paraphrased rather than deepened the emotional core, such as the shame of social exclusion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session followed a clear arc from ambivalence to envisioning, exceptions, and concrete client-owned commitments with a follow-up plan. Each turn advanced this trajectory while following Timur's lead.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as two equals problem-solving, with Timur's ideas shaping the session's direction and the accountability plan co-created at his request. The counselor deferred to Timur's expertise throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was strongly supported through 'if any' framing, affirming 'just looking' as sufficient, and explicitly removing pressure about pacing. All decisions and next steps belonged to Timur.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions consistently drew motivation from Timur himself, with every major insight originating in his own words. The counselor amplified rather than persuaded, exemplifying high-level evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Timur as the expert on his world, with open, client-directed questions and next steps originating entirely from him. The closing accountability agreement was explicitly negotiated at his request.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional evocation and reinforcement of change talk, from selective reflection of 'not yet becoming no' to forward-looking questions. Resulted in concrete commitments and Timur recruiting the counselor as an accountability partner.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I'm lazy,' fears about employment) was met with reframes grounded in Timur's own strengths rather than reinforcement. Some barriers, like the pattern of quitting jobs, were acknowledged without always being actively transformed via double-sided reflection.
