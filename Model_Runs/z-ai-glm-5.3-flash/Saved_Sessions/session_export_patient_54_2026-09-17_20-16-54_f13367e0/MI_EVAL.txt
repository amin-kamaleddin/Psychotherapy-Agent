# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard maintained throughout, with no labeling or disapproval despite initial defensiveness. Both her autonomy and her concern for the baby were explicitly validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex and double-sided reflections captured both surface and deeper emotional layers. The client's response ('I feel lighter saying that') confirmed she felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior while allowing organic exploration. Each exchange advanced the arc from ambivalence to a concrete, specific plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange between equals: the counselor asked rather than told and built on the client's own ideas to co-construct the plan. The client explicitly noted this approach worked when lecturing hadn't.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: everything was framed as her call and her choice, with no advice-giving or pressure. Especially attuned given the client's autonomy-driven presentation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from the client's own values rather than supplied by the counselor. The client's own words carried the session's motivational weight, culminating in strong commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client was explicitly positioned as the expert, and every solution came from her. The counselor named this: 'nobody handed you this plan. You made it.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked through vision, confidence-scaling, and reasons questions, then reflected and amplified. Session culminated in strong commitment language: 'Thursday, we start.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged then redirected via double-sided reflections and reframing toward her values, without arguing or dismissing. Skepticism about evidence and friend judgment were effectively dissolved rather than amplified.
