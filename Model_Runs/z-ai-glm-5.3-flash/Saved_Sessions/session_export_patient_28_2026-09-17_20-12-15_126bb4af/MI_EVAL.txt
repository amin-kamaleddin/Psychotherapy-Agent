# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, explicitly normalizing the client's struggles ('no judgment here') and reframing self-criticism with warmth. Affirmations throughout convey respect without judgment or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflective listening, including excellent double-sided reflections capturing ambivalence ('both voices matter'). The client repeatedly confirms feeling understood ('you got me there').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, progressive focus on re-establishing physical activity with no topic drift; every exchange advances the arc toward the target behavior. Each reflection and question moves the session forward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session operates as genuine dialogue between equals, with the plan co-constructed from the client's own material and joint language ('pieces of a puzzle we could work with together'). The client explicitly notes it wasn't 'another lecture.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces client ownership, framing options as the client's choice and crediting his agency ('you made it happen, not me'). No directives or pressure appear at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplifies evocation by drawing motivation from within the client—his enjoyment of lifting, accountability, and identity as a father—rather than imposing reasons. Results in authentic client-generated commitment ('finding my own reason again').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Locates expertise within the client, repeatedly crediting his own insights and never lecturing or prescribing. Every direction emerges from the client's own memories and values.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through strategic questions, with progression from vague ('maybe something small') to concrete commitment ('The driveway's waiting tomorrow,' confidence 6/10). Amplifies change talk and elicits the client's own values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk without dismissing it, validating underlying concerns and redirecting toward change. Elicits the client's own reframing ('miss Tuesday, say yes on Wednesday'), allowing him to soften the sustain talk himself.
