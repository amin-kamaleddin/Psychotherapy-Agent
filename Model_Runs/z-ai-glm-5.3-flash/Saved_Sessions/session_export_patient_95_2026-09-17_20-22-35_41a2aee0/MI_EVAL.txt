# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, even amid suicidality and skepticism ('I won't flinch, and I won't panic'). Non-defensive ownership of the initial misstep modeled genuine accountability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, accurate reflections including the 'second layer of pain' of feeling like a burden. Patient explicitly confirmed being understood: 'That's exactly it. Nobody's named it like that before.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on suicidality and suffering, including a direct safety assessment, then a clear path toward a concrete behavioral commitment. Focus never drifted despite patient resistance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and co-constructed definitions of success ('What would make this week's attempt feel like it counted?'). Session direction was rebuilt entirely around what the patient said actually helps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient's hesitation was honored without pressure, and the final plan was entirely self-generated. Patient experienced genuine ownership: 'I can't believe I'm saying yes to something.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation effectively drawn from within (niece, 3am call, music, aunt). Slightly limited by the premature opening 'first step' question before sufficient evocation, though recovery was strong.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise clearly located within the patient ('You're the expert on your own life'). The psychiatrist explicitly affirmed the self-designed plan rather than a prescribed one.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk (reframing survival as choice, eliciting 'what's the smallest thing that could count?'). Could have deepened commitment language further, e.g., confidence or importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged without amplification, with effective reframes (3am call as agency, hope as 'dangerous but possible'). Brief lingering on hopelessness before redirection was acceptable but not maximally efficient.
