# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard with explicit affirmations (e.g., reframing smoking precautions as protective love). Never critical or defensive, even when the patient pushes back.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening capturing exhaustion, ambivalence ('Both things can be true'), and deeper meaning beneath the smoking. Reflections like 'it's about letting someone in' were confirmed by the patient.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on smoking cessation and related concerns while permitting natural exploration. Every question ties back to the target behavior, anchoring tangents back to the change process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative exchange where the patient's ideas shaped the session direction and the working plan. The clinician posed questions rather than prescribing solutions and left the next step to Elena.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly reinforced ownership ('No promises needed — this is yours to move at your own pace') and closed with a choice question. The patient's response ('it's mine') directly reflects successful autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently elicited from the patient rather than imposed. Her own reasons for change (her son, wanting to stop surviving) emerged unprompted through open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was located within the client ('I'm not here to talk you into anything. I'm curious what you think'). The patient experienced her own insights as new, and the session shifted based on her contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of change talk, with the pivotal question about what would need to be in place first producing the patient's own plan. Language progressed from sustain talk to concrete commitments ('I might actually call her tonight').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged with brief neutral reflections but never amplified or argued with. The clinician immediately pivoted to change-oriented exploration using open questions.
