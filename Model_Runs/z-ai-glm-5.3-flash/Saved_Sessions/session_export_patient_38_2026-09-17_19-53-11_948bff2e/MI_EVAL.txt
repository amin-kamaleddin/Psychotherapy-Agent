# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment; the psychiatrist normalized Elena's questions ('You're not being difficult; you're being informed') and affirmed her efforts respectfully, including her bathroom-floor moment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and emotional depth ('carrying all that alone'; 'it hit you in a really deep way') and named her double-sided ambivalence and fear of permanence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on contraception decision-making, with each exchange advancing the target from ambivalence to concrete next steps without rushing, even redirecting facts with 'What stands out to you in that?'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The agenda emerged entirely from Elena (cost, pain, info to take home) and the psychiatrist framed work as joint ('we could script it together') while checking in rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and pace were repeatedly reinforced ('You get to decide the pace here,' 'no pressure on the timeline'), options were offered, and Elena's readiness level was honored throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within Elena via open questions, generating her own reasons ('I never want to feel that again'), plan, and reframing; information was given only on request with ownership immediately returned.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena was explicitly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert on your own body here'), and the session's direction visibly evolved from her own statements and questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation from desire talk ('what would feel different than hoping?') through commitment-adjacent language ('I'll actually read it... not just maybe another time'), consistently reflecting and amplifying rather than supplying Elena's motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement and gently pivoted to exploration ('What would you want to know first?'); no argumentation or premature persuasion, and Elena's own 'trying it' reframing was elicited through the psychiatrist's non-pressuring stance.
