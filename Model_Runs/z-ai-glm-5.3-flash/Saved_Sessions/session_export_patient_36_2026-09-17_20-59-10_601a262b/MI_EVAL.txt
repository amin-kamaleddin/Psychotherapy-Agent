# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations like 'I love where you landed' and 'That's wisdom right there.' No judgment or disapproval appears anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are consistently accurate and deep, capturing core insights and beautifully mirroring the client's 'full cup' metaphor.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear progressive focus on protecting the client's own time, with each turn building logically toward the concrete action step. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is between equal partners, with decisions co-constructed and structured choices offered rather than prescriptions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership is consistently reinforced, closing with a genuine choice about planning for guilt or letting it be hers. No persuasion or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All plan content was elicited from within the client rather than supplied. Would reach 5 with slightly more deliberate exploration of confidence/ability language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client is explicitly positioned as the expert ('You're the expert on your days'), and her contributions genuinely shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled open questions evoke strong commitment and desire statements ('I'm ready to actually do the thing'), which are reinforced effectively. Slightly more explicit confidence/ability exploration would earn a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Momentary doubts about guilt and failure are not allowed to expand, with effective pivots to the client's strengths and choices. Minimal sustain talk required redirection.
