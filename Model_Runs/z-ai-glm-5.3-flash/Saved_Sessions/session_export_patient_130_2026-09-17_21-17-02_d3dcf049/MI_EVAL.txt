# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, explicitly respecting Viktor's limits ('the weekends and the guys stay yours for now'). His defensiveness softened as he felt genuinely accepted rather than judged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections captured emotional undercurrents, such as reflecting 'I miss that' back as the first thing to come entirely from Viktor himself. This deepened his self-exploration throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior while respecting Viktor's pacing, guiding him from external pressure toward personal motivation. The session moved from ambivalence to a concrete, dated action step (dinner Saturday).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated between equal partners, co-constructing the plan by asking rather than advising. Viktor's own ideas ('small tell, big show') determined the session's direction and outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively reinforced at every turn, never pressuring on quitting and accepting off-limits topics without protest. Viktor retained full ownership of the plan and what to share with his wife.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major shift in motivation came from Viktor in response to open, evocative questions rather than counselor persuasion. Textbook evocation with a precontemplative client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was repeatedly located in the client, and Viktor's own framing became the working plan. The counselor affirmed 'You designed your own version of this.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out progressively deeper change talk, from 'maybe it's worth a shot' to 'I don't like who I've been at home lately' and a self-generated concrete plan. Questions consistently evoked desire, need, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handled heavy sustain talk masterfully with double-sided reflections and pivots to what Viktor wanted, never arguing or confronting. Limit-setting was accepted and incorporated into the plan, keeping momentum without amplifying resistance.
