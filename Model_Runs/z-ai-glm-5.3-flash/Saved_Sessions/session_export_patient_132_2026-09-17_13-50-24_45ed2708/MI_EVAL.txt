# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout with validating responses. However, warmth at times felt procedural and affirmations were somewhat generic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections were offered, but the session was dominated by information delivery and structured plans. Yasmin's vulnerable disclosures about control, fear, and shame frequently went unreflected, with quick pivots to practical instructions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on target behaviors with concrete, behaviorally specific next steps each exchange. Direction was sometimes achieved by racing to solutions rather than building shared exploratory groundwork.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements like offering choices and check-ins, but interaction was often uneven with long expert monologues followed by picking from pre-set options. Yasmin's own ideas shaped the plan only once (the scale compromise).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered neutral choices and let Yasmin decide on experiments without pressuring her to abandon weight-loss desires. Could be stronger in explicitly reinforcing her ownership of the process and capacity to self-direct.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited through structured experiments and choice points, but the counselor relied heavily on psychoeducation and directives. Key motivational material, like Yasmin's fear of losing control, was never explored via open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some inclusive elements allowed Yasmin to shape the plan, but large portions were expert-driven with extensive technique lists delivered without inquiry into her own ideas. Power-sharing was inconsistent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited commitments through low-threat choice-based experiments, with Yasmin articulating her own reasons for change. Missed opportunities to deepen change talk by reflecting it back or exploring what hopefulness meant to her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about weight loss was neither amplified nor argued with, but also never strategically softened. No double-sided reflections or reframes were used to work explicitly with her ambivalence.
