# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard was communicated throughout, with no judgment about lateness, phone use, or ambivalence. Affirmations were frequent, specific, and genuine, and Jamal's closing remark directly evidenced feeling accepted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, consistent reflective listening with complex reflections capturing core ambivalence and emotional costs of change. Every reflection was met with expanded disclosure, a strong indicator of accurate understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear focus on punctuality and its antecedent (late-night phone use), advancing progressively from motivation to planning to follow-up. Direction was kept despite terse responses, without rushing the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No expert content or lecturing was offered; the plan was co-constructed entirely from Jamal's ideas through collaborative questions. The client's explicit endorsement ('This felt more like my plan') confirms genuine partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively supported from the opening turn and every plan element was framed as Jamal's choice, with no advice-giving or pressure. Even the setback fallback was the client's own idea, affirmed rather than dictated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was masterfully drawn out through questions that elicited Jamal's own reasons, commitment language, and confidence. By session's end the client voiced intrinsic commitment and ownership of the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly positioned Jamal as the expert, with every suggestion originating from the client. The closing statement about the plan being his own is direct evidence of achieved power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was strategically evoked and deepened, pivoting from external compliance toward intrinsic motivation and eliciting commitment and planning language. Jamal progressively produced strong change talk and a self-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was largely redirected skillfully with reframes and double-sided reflections rather than argument. Minor deduction for occasionally restating sustain talk content (e.g., the 'one more video' obstacle), though always in service of planning around it.
