# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, supportive tone with affirmations of progress and no judgment about his history. However, affirmations are brief and formulaic, missing chances to affirm his character strengths like courage in rehearsal and insight about tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of his situation and concerns, with responsive adjustments to his feedback. Deeper emotional content (fear of caving, relief at feeling in control) is not explicitly reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol-related conflict situations throughout. Session progresses logically from assessment to planning to rehearsal, ending with a concrete test date.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Joint work is invited and Luka's modifications are incorporated, including interactive role-play. However, solutions are often counselor-generated first with Luka editing afterward rather than co-created from the start.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are consistently offered and the counselor adapts when Luka pushes back. Some pre-packaged plans imply compliance, and Luka's ownership of the change is not explicitly reinforced enough.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation elicited through open questions, and Luka articulates his own reasons. Missing are rulers, values exploration, and eliciting his own coping ideas before providing lists.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Luka's preferences are asked about, but the session is heavily structured by counselor-generated templates and lists. His own ideas driving the plan are rarely acknowledged or positioned as expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Commitment and action are repeatedly invited, and Luka generates strong change talk in response. Missed chances to deepen this with confidence rulers, elaboration questions, or amplifying summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement but not strategically softened. No double-sided reflections or pivots linking his reservations to his own goals for discrepancy.
