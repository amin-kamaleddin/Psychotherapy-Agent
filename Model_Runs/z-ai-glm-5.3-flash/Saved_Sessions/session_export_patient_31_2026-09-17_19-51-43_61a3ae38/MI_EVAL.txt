# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, honoring David's evening boundary and responding to his humor with warmth. Acceptance was communicated independent of outcome ('whether Monday goes smoothly or sideways').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections captured both content and meaning, e.g., reframing partner pressure as pushing him toward smoking and work smoking as 'filling a gap' rather than fighting craving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking behavior change with each exchange moving purposefully forward from motivation to barriers to a concrete plan and follow-up. No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan genuinely evolved through back-and-forth, with the clinician building on David's self-corrected answers. Resulted in a co-constructed agreement David described as his own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered choices, accepted David's conditions without protest, and reinforced his ownership ('your deal your way'). Removed pressure by framing setbacks as learning opportunities.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never lectured about health risks; all motivation was elicited from David himself ('twelve cigarettes I wouldn't even miss'). Consistently attributed motivation back to its source.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned David as the expert, with every plan element originating from him. David confirmed the impact: 'I came in expecting to be lectured, and instead I'm actually walking out with a plan I made myself.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk through open questions, labeled commitment language ('Listen to you — "that's the plan"'), and drew on David's gym success for self-efficacy. Session ended with strong commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with acceptance and strategic sidestepping, narrowing focus to daytime to shrink sustain-talk territory. Minor deduction for briefly restating the evening boundary, though it functioned as alliance-building.
