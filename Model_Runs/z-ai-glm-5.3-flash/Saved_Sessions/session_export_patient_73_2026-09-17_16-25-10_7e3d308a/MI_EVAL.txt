# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and warm, framing slips non-judgmentally ('slips are information, not failure'). Affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic, with limited normalization of her fear or affirmation beyond her efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capturing both content and ambivalence, such as linking the automatic reach with Carol to the friendship routine. Reflections were often brief and pivoted quickly to options, leaving her self-distrust and fear not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus culminating in a specific, achievable goal: one smoke-free coffee with Carol on Thursday. Every exchange tied back to this target with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine partnership, co-constructing the Thursday plan with Linda's ideas (money as motivation, crossword as distraction) and interactive role-play. However, the counselor frequently offered pre-packaged option menus rather than letting Linda's ideas lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, let Linda select strategies, and accommodated her preference for a booked follow-up over texting. Some responses were advice-dense, at times supplying solutions rather than purely supporting her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited her core reasons (health, money) and produced genuine self-persuasion through role-play ('hearing myself say it plainly might help me believe it'). Could have probed more into her hope, values, and confidence before moving to technique selection.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Linda as the decision-maker, and her choices clearly shaped the session (Carol line, delay strategy, follow-up plan). Reliance on handing over option menus and scripts slightly limited full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evoked strong change talk ('I'm serious this time — for my health and the money') through open questions and role-play rehearsal, reinforcing it well. Deeper, more systematic exploration of her confidence and hope could have strengthened this.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk, reframing friendship concerns and redirecting doubt toward her two-week success. However, redirects were somewhat quick, validating the doubts fully before reframing.
