# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, accepting Sofia's choice not to quit ('this is an experiment'). Affirmations are somewhat generic ('Nice,' 'That sounds great') with limited explicit normalization of her ambivalence or anxiety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures both sides of her ambivalence, including family pressure and practical concerns. Misses deeper emotional exploration of her anxiety, fear of parental discovery, and meaning of being 'the first in her family'.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains unwavering focus on marijuana use as stress coping from opening to close, with gentle redirections when Sofia drifts. Concludes with a clearly structured plan and Friday check-in without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal partnership — asks permission before topic shifts and co-designs every plan element with Sofia's ideas central. The final plan is entirely co-constructed, with Sofia taking clear ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support through permission-seeking, explicit permission not to quit, and framed choices. Option menus are sometimes counselor-generated and rapid questioning subtly pressures agreement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within — Sofia's own reasons (cost, fog, untested curiosity) emerge, culminating in 'I never actually tested it.' Occasionally supplies options and rationale himself rather than persisting in eliciting her ideas, and doesn't explore deeper values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Sofia as the expert, and her contributions (walk choice, friend accountability, knot-rating) substantially shape the plan. At times supplies full menus of options and structure before Sofia generates her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk throughout — open questions elicit Sofia's own doubts, and confidence scaling moves her from 5–6 to 7. By session end she volunteers strong spontaneous change talk ('curious if I feel better without the fog').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk neutrally within double-sided reflections rather than arguing, accepting her reframes without defensiveness. Occasionally echoes sustain talk in summaries without pivoting efficiently, and early reassurance slightly reinforces the status quo.
