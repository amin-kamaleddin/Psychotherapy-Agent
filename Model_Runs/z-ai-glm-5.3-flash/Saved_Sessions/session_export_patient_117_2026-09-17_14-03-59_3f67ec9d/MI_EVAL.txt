# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with consistent affirmations, but praise feels formulaic and repeated safety questions became mechanical—Samuel had to explicitly ask the counselor to stop.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is sparse, with almost no complex reflections; vulnerable moments like Samuel's fear of weeks 2–3 and his tentative slip-as-data hope were met with advice rather than deeper understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on tobacco reduction and produced a concrete, actionable plan. Points deducted because direction came through prescriptive advice rather than guided discovery, and closing exchanges became repetitive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is genuine back-and-forth and the plan was adapted to Samuel's constraints, but the counselor dispensed detailed pre-built protocols and Samuel mostly selected from them rather than co-creating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent menu of options with genuine choices (reminders, check-in format, nonjudgmental slip framing) supports ownership. Falls short because options came from the counselor's toolbox rather than inviting Samuel to generate his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was largely self-generated despite the counselor, not evoked—no exploration of his reasons for change, no importance ruler, and the confidence rating of 6 was never explored with follow-up questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some responsiveness to Samuel's feedback (shortening the delay plan) shows partnership, but the expert-driven stream of prescriptive advice dominated, with little exploration of Samuel's own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged mostly unprompted from the patient; the counselor missed key opportunities to evoke and reinforce it, including failing to follow up on why confidence was a 6 rather than higher.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not amplified (positive), but it was passed over rather than strategically softened—no double-sided reflections linking his 'no promises' doubt to his proven week of success.
