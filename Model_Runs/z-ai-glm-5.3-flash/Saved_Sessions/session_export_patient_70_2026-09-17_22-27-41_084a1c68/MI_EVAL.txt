# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth, affirmation, and non-judgment were consistent throughout, even when the client discussed drinking, offending, and shame about asking for help. Regard felt genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections accurately tracked both content and feeling, including the father theme and shame about asking Donna for help. Emotional core of each disclosure was captured consistently.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, progressive focus from values to a concrete, time-bound first step (forms tonight) with no drift. Each exchange built momentum toward the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was largely generated by the client, and next steps were co-constructed. Falls short of a 5 due to lack of explicit agenda negotiation at the session's opening.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice and client-led agenda were evident with no pressure or coercion. A point withheld because declarative endorsements ('I believe you can do this') could better reinforce the client's ownership of his decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every turn was an open question drawing out the client's own motivations, which emerged entirely from him (being a different dad, Donna's smile, the visit room). The method was almost purely evocative.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was explicitly located in the client ('You know yourself better than anyone'), and every strategy came from Lars himself. The counselor's role was limited to reflecting, affirming, and evoking.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoked rich desire, need, commitment, and taking-steps language ('I'll do that tonight,' 'proud, maybe for the first time in ages'). Commitment language was consistently reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally reframed effectively, but was occasionally reflected directly without an immediate pivot to change-side language. These reflections served accurate summarizing rather than reinforcement.
