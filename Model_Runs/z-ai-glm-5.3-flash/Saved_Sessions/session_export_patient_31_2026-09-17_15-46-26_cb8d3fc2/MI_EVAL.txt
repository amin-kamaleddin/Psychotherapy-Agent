# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, accepting David's boundaries and honesty. However, affirmations are infrequent and less individualized than ideal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early reflective listening capturing ambivalence and the meaning of the smoking ritual. Reflections thinned later as the counselor shifted to advice-giving, leaving emotional content like spite-smoking under-explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking throughout, from exploring its function to a concrete follow-up plan. Direction was somewhat action-forward for a contemplation-stage patient.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David shaped key plan elements (nights, swap, script), but the counselor defaulted to a prescriptive, information-heavy stance with pre-made plans, tips, and scripts rather than jointly generating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: offered choice, accepted boundaries without pushback, framed the plan as an experiment, asked permission before advising, and let David define success and set his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ambivalence questions and confidence rulers elicited some change talk (5→6), but the counselor never explored why change matters to David or his values — much substance came from counselor-supplied options rather than the patient's own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David chose plan details and the counselor checked feasibility, but the counselor often occupied the expert role, offering unsolicited practical advice rather than drawing solutions from David first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and confidence rulers successfully evoked partial change talk and a confidence gain. However, change talk was never deepened — the 'why a 5 and not lower?' elaboration was missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I like smoking,' weekends off-limits) was met with neutral reflection and accommodation rather than argument. A 5 would require more strategic pivoting toward change talk.
