# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, explicitly defusing fear of judgment and honoring the value smoking holds. No judgmental language appears anywhere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistently landing reflections capture both sides of ambivalence and deeper emotional currents (fear of failure, secrecy, his father). Patient repeatedly confirms with 'exactly' and deepens disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on smoking behavior change with sequential momentum toward a plan. Each reflection advances progress without rushing the contemplative patient.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals; patient's answers drive each subsequent reflection and co-create the plan. Psychiatrist frames contributions as thinking partner, not authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforcing ownership and choice ('in your pace, on your terms'), never prescribing or persuading. Patient explicitly names the change as self-directed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully elicits the patient's own motivations—curiosity, marriage, his father's death—and develops them into commitment-adjacent identity talk. No clinician-supplied persuasion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Kareem as the expert on his life; every plan element (tracking, note, wife's involvement) originates from the patient. No expert-driven advice given.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully amplifies tentative change language, highlights discrepancies, and uses open questions to deepen motivation. Patient's change talk escalates steadily to identity-level commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Pivots away from sustain talk by reflecting its function rather than arguing, then redirects toward possibility. Sustain talk diminishes steadily across the session.
