# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acceptance is a notable strength, with warm, nonjudgmental validation of Elena's right to delay decisions. However, affirmations of her strengths are limited and repeated safety check-ins feel somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections are offered, but responses frequently pivot quickly to options and checklists rather than deepening emotional understanding. Complex reflections exploring the fear behind 'what if I hadn't been lucky?' are largely absent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A clear focus on contraception is maintained despite Elena's initial deflection, progressing logically toward an info-only visit and follow-up. Early extended grounding and safety content somewhat dilutes the focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inclusive language is used, but the session leans toward the counselor delivering pre-packaged resources rather than co-constructing them with Elena. Her contributions shape the plan, but her own expertise is not actively elicited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is the strongest domain: Elena is given explicit permission to delay, offered menu options at every turn, and never pressured. The 'info-only, no commitment' framing directly honors her need to decide later.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena's motivations occasionally surface, but often through her own reflection rather than skillful elicitation. Information-giving frequently substitutes for evocative questions about importance, confidence, and her own reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice is repeatedly offered and Elena shapes the plan's pace, but the counselor positions themselves as the source of expertise with extensive scripts and question lists. Elena's own knowledge and ideas are not actively drawn upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively builds on Elena's change talk, converting avoidance into a concrete self-authored plan with a low-pressure framing. However, deliberate open-ended evocative questions are rare, relying instead on menu-based offers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is appropriately not argued with, preventing resistance, but there is little active softening or strategic pivoting. No double-sided reflections pair her sustain talk with her stated fears; the counselor largely sidesteps it.
