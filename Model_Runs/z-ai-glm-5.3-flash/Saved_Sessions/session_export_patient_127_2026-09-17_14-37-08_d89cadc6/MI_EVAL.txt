# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental throughout; reframe of 'not laziness' and affirmations of client wins. Affirmations remain brief and formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections of triggers and shame, but the session is advice-driven and emotionally loaded statements (shame, Fiona's ultimatum, fear of rejection) receive minimal reflection before pivoting to tasks.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on recidivism, abstinence, and employment with no drift. Focus is maintained through an expert-driven checklist rather than collaboratively negotiated goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor supplies nearly all content—plans, scripts, resume bullets—while the client executes. Client-generated ideas (blocking mates, porch ritual) are absorbed without acknowledgment rather than jointly developed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Turns regularly end with choice-based questions, but the overall style is directive: prescribed techniques, verbatim scripts, and urgency ('do it now'). Client's stated autonomy ('I don't make promises I can't keep') is not honored or reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation and ideas are predominantly counselor-supplied; few open questions about the client's own reasons, values, or confidence. Genuine motivators (Fiona, porch, pride in 22 days) surface spontaneously but are never explored or deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor positions himself as expert dispensing solutions; client's choices are limited to picking from a counselor-generated menu. Client expertise (e.g., temp agencies 'just need bodies') is not built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is recognized and affirmed (22 days clean, telling Fiona), but there is little deliberate evocation of the client's own reasons, values, or vision for change. Most change talk arises spontaneously rather than being drawn out and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced, and the 'lazy' self-label is effectively reframed. However, redirection is abrupt—ambivalence ('not saying I'm never going back') is bypassed rather than explored with double-sided reflections.
