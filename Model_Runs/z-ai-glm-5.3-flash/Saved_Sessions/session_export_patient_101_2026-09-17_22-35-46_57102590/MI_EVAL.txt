### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Operates from the stance that motivation resides within Mei, eliciting her deepest motivations (her father's story, future motherhood 'on purpose') rather than providing reasons to change. ### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Mei as the expert ('You're the expert on those nights'), and all key ideas in the session originated from her. The counselor builds the entire intervention around her contributions rather than imposing professional solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes Mei's vision, strengths, and commitment through open questions, and she produces strong change talk ('I want to know the truth instead of just fearing it'). The counselor consistently reflects and amplifies this language rather than supplying her own reasons for change.
