# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, even when Kieran tested the relationship ('Why does it matter to you?') and offered minimal responses. Affirmations like 'that's not nothing, that's you being honest about how you work' and self-deprecating humor kept warmth and equality.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including an accurate double-sided reflection ('part of you actually wants it — and part of you is dreading a room full of strangers'). The 'sad thing to realize' about connection could have been reflected more deeply before pivoting to a question.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus converging on attending the footy group and an initial phone call, with logical progression from values to a concrete first step. Early exploration was necessarily broad, and fear exploration delayed consolidating the plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Collaborative tone set from the opening by giving Kieran agenda-setting power, with explicit division of labor ('what could you find out yourself, versus what might I help dig into with you?'). The plan was built entirely on Kieran's own terms and ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kieran's ownership consistently reinforced ('your call, your agenda'), and his 'maybe with a calendar entry' was accepted without pressure or escalating demands. His chosen scope ('Just the call') was fully honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation was client-generated — Kieran identified what mattered, named the footy group, and created his own coping strategies and commitment mechanism. His shift from 'Dunno' to 'I'll ring them' demonstrates successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Kieran as the expert ('you know your life better than anyone, including me') and explicitly transferred expertise. His contributions genuinely redirected the session and formed the entire plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary evocation of escalating change talk, from desire ('it bugs me') to ability ('I could ring the centre myself') to commitment ('if it's in my phone, I'd actually do it'). Each step was strategically reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, using effective double-sided reflections ('Staying home keeps you safe from that. And you keep thinking about that footy thing anyway'). Extended exploration of attendance fears gave sustain talk somewhat prolonged airtime before redirecting.
