# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating frustration and reframing failure as 'gathering data.' However, warmth feels somewhat procedural, pivoting quickly from empathy to checklists rather than deepening unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Liyana's discouragement, with reflections like 'that fear of wasting time is valid' landing well. Deeper emotional content—loneliness at home and comparison with her brother—is only briefly acknowledged before pivoting back to the tracking plan.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains clear focus on the weight-loss target, sets a concrete two-week experiment, and closes with a structured agenda. Direction is maintained through counselor-imposed structure rather than collaborative goals, and emotional themes like loneliness are dropped rather than integrated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are genuine collaborative moments, such as accommodating Liyana's photo-tracking preference and co-arranging the follow-up plan. However, the session flow is largely counselor-generated, with plans and templates delivered in bulk and limited space for the client's contributions to shape direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly offers choice ('Which sounds most helpful?', 'now or later?') and honors the client's pacing, presenting options as a menu rather than directives. Yet the volume of prescribed instructions per turn gives an expert-led texture that partially undercuts client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor rarely elicits motivation from within the client, asking few open questions about Liyana's own reasons, values, or hopes for change. Content comes predominantly from the counselor's expertise, and the client's spontaneous motivations are left undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor shares some power by deferring the family message to the client's timing and soliciting preferences. Much of the session remains expert-driven, with dense prescriptive content and little exploration of Liyana's own ideas or expertise about her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered choices prompt the client to voice commitments ('I'll do the photo tracking for two weeks'), and her 'doable' comment is genuine change talk. The counselor does not deepen these statements with evocative questions or confidence scaling, so change talk emerges from plan structure rather than skillful evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reframes effectively once ('you're not failing; you're gathering data'), which the client receives well. Sustain talk is substantial, but double-sided reflections are rarely used and ambivalence is often bypassed in favor of new tactics, leaving it under-processed.
