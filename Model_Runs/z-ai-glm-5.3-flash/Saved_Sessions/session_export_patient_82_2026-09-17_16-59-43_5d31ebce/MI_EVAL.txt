# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and handled the confidentiality disclosure honestly. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic, with limited individualized positive regard for Lukas's values and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered accurate reflections capturing both sides of his ambivalence. However, did not linger on emotionally vulnerable moments like the memory lapse that 'spooked' him or his fear of becoming the outsider.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on the target behavior throughout, moving systematically from disclosure to consequences to skill rehearsal. The rapid-fire question battery briefly shifted the tone from exploratory MI to structured assessment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative — asked permission before probing, co-created the plan, and the role-play was genuinely two-way with Lukas directing the drills and shaping the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices rather than directives, letting Lukas select refusal lines, rehearsal targets, and pace. Could more explicitly reinforce his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used the importance ruler and elicited self-generated motivation and insight, with strong commitment language emerging. Some content (refusal lines, tactics) was counselor-supplied rather than evoked from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Lukas as the expert on his own life, with his contributions visibly shaping the session. Already well demonstrated, though could be made even more explicit.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully used the importance ruler and follow-up, eliciting self-motivational statements and a client-generated plan. Confidence strengthening happened mostly through behavioral rehearsal rather than verbal exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence briefly but rarely used sustain talk strategically as a bridge (e.g., double-sided reflections linking social belonging to athlete identity). Redirection was adequate but not consistently skillful.
