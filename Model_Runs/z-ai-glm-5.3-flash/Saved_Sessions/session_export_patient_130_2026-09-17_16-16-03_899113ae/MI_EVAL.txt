# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance despite Viktor's defensive, externally motivated presentation, and honored his boundary around scope. However, warmth occasionally felt formulaic, and explicit affirmations of Viktor's effort were limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening that accurately captured Viktor's position and barriers throughout. Reflections sometimes stayed at the content level rather than exploring the emotional weight (wife's crying, Viktor's shame/protectiveness).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained excellent focus on a concrete target—reducing Friday-night fights—building a progressively more specific, actionable plan with no topic drift. Every turn contributed to the plan: script, backup, timing, confidence check, and success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint enterprise with the plan built from Viktor's own ideas (garage, truck, Friday), who actively practiced and contributed success criteria. Occasionally front-loaded suggestions ('set a timer, take a drive') rather than eliciting them from Viktor first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Viktor's choice and ownership through permission-seeking and option-offering, and fully respected his 'fighting, not quitting' boundary without pressure. Viktor's closing commitment emerged from his own choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Viktor using rulers and values questions, eliciting his marriage as the core driver and a confidence rise from 6 to 8. Newly expressed motivation was sometimes left unelaborated, with the counselor moving quickly to logistics instead of deepening.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Viktor as the expert, asking permission and letting his contributions shape the plan. Occasionally slipped into a slightly expert/directive tone, which Viktor even called out ('What's your genius plan?').

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of the readiness ruler elicited key change talk ('maybe something's not zero'), and confidence scaling prompted a rise from 6 to 8. Missed opportunities to deepen or reflect change talk when it occurred, sometimes jumping straight to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not argue with or reinforce Viktor's sustain talk, and respected his stated boundary. However, missed several chances to develop discrepancy (e.g., linking continued use to the marriage he values) and at times simply restated his minimization back to him.
