# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, even when Jordan expressed dismissiveness, and affirmed his health habits and autonomy. Jordan explicitly thanked the counselor for 'not just lecturing me,' reflecting deep respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening was demonstrated throughout, including a skilled double-sided reflection capturing Jordan's ambivalence between self-reliance and worry about his grandma. Jordan's own confirmations showed he felt deeply understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear, progressive focus on flu vaccination from start to finish, moving from resistance to a concrete plan (call Monday, after Saturday basketball). Every question served the target behavior without dragging.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was fully collaborative, with the counselor building every reflection from Jordan's own words and co-constructing the plan through open questions. Jordan's closing comment confirmed he experienced it as a respectful, non-hierarchical exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was exemplary, with explicit statements like 'this is completely your decision' and no pressure or directives. The counselor consistently credited Jordan's self-generated reasoning, reinforcing ownership of both decision and plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor operated from the core MI stance that motivation resides within the client, evoking Jordan's own reasons (family protection, freedom from Saturday-night worry) rather than supplying facts or arguments. All pivotal change statements were client-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positioned Jordan as the expert, and his contributions (teammates' story, grandparents, Saturday-night check) became the material the session built on. Jordan owned the entire plan, evidence his ideas shaped the outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions elicited key change talk ('my grandma probably wouldn't handle it very well'), which the counselor then explicitly highlighted and reinforced. Sustained commitment talk was consolidated into a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, using effective pivots after brief validation. The only minor weakness was that sustain talk was sometimes reflected fairly fully, though strategically to build trust rather than entrench it.
