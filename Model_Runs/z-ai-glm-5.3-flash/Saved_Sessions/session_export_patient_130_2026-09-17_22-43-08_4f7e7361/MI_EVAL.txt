**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validating Viktor's ambivalence ('Both of those can be true at once') and never judging his use or minimization. Single-session format limited deeper affirmation of Viktor beyond his change efforts.

### Direction

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Strong autonomy support: 'What would need to change, if anything' grants permission not to change, and the counselor never advises or pressures. Explicitly attributes the plan and pace to Viktor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation clearly drawn from within Viktor, who shifts from external compliance ('wife made me come') to internal desire ('worth more than one more beer'). Every substantive question was designed to elicit his own reasons and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly locates expertise with the client ('You know your marriage better than anyone'). Viktor's contributions genuinely determine the session's direction rather than being token acknowledgments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout domain—skillful questions elicited commitment ('I think I can make that happen'), ability statements, and vivid anticipatory change talk, which the counselor consistently mirrored back. Confidence scaling was underused.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well with textbook double-sided reflections that never amplified resistance. Could have more actively normalized or reframed sustain talk (e.g., fear of losing his friend group) rather than relying solely on neutral reflections.
