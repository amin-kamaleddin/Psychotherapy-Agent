# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and non-judgmental toward Luka's continued drinking and limits. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and could be more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening, notably validating the plan feeling 'dramatic.' Deeper emotional content (shame about alcohol removal, partner's burden) received only surface acknowledgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol-related risk and coping throughout, ending with a concrete, agreed written plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely joint plan-building, with Luka's edits shaping the final plan. Middle section tilted toward counselor-supplied solutions rather than joint option generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and accepted Luka's rejections and deferrals without pressure. The final plan was entirely Luka's construction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened with strong evocative questions but shifted to supplying techniques and drafted content mid-session. Missed opportunities to elicit Luka's own ideas and build on his existing successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Luka's input and choices, and his contributions genuinely shaped the plan. Initially presented prescriptive menus before exploring Luka's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked motivation ('keeping her') and a strong commitment ('that I'm in on. Full stop'). Missed deepening commitment language when Luka said saying it aloud 'makes it feel more real.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk without confrontation or reinforcement, respecting his harm-reduction path. Limited use of double-sided reflections to develop discrepancy around continued drinking.
