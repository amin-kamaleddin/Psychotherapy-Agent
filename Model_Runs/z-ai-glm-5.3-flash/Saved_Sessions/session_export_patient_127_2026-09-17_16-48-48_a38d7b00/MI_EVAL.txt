# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with genuine affirmations of Timur's 22-day abstinence and no moralizing about his criminal history. Falls short of 5 because acceptance feels somewhat formulaic (repeated nearly verbatim praise) with little explicit normalization of relapse risk or unconditional regard in moments of doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective responses accurately capture Timur's core stressors, peer comparison pain, and fear of rejection. However, empathy stays at summary level and does not deepen into his loneliness, shame about broken promises, or the emotional weight of Fiona's ultimatum.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional focus and momentum, converting vague ambivalence into a concrete, time-bound plan (20-minute course search, drafted message, Tuesday send date, 5-7 day reply check, and a risky-window coping plan). Exemplary directional steering appropriate for a contemplation-stage client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed, with Timur's edits shaping the message draft's tone and content. Falls short of 5 because the counselor tends to offer pre-packaged option sets that frame the decision space on his terms rather than open-ended co-generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Everything is framed as choice, with Timur's rejection of reminders and rehearsal accepted without pressure and his 'don't push' explicitly honored. His self-paced decision style is validated as fitting 'how you do things,' reinforcing ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation of Timur's own reasons and conditions for change via readiness scaling and practical questions, with spontaneous change talk (Fiona, quiet mornings) emerging in the counselor's space-making. Falls short of 5 due to reliance on practical scaffolding questions rather than open evocative questions about values and future vision.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Timur is consistently positioned as the expert, with his veto of the 'choirboy' framing materially shaping the plan and his rejection of reminders respected. Stops short of 5 because Timur mostly chooses among counselor-generated menus rather than co-creating strategies from his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evokes and builds on change talk, eliciting Timur's self-generated plan ('Maybe looking up one of those certificates'), commitment language, and relationship motivation. Falls short of 5 because the counselor leans on structured option menus rather than open evocative questions and doesn't deepen Timur's 'something to get up for' hope talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never reinforced and ambivalence is met neutrally, but it is not strategically softened either. Significant sustain talk ('I'm lazy,' 'people like me are unaccepted,' 'one message, not a career') received only neutral acknowledgments rather than double-sided reflections or reframes — the unchallenged 'lazy' self-label was a notable missed opportunity.
