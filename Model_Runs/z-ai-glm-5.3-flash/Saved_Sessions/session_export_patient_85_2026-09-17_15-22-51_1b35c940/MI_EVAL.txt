# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental with supportive framing around setbacks and slips. Affirmations were somewhat formulaic ('That's excellent,' 'That sounds doable') and lacked individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured both content and emotion early on, including Marco's frustration about weight regain. As the session progressed, reflections became briefer and deeper emotional exploration of his sense of defeat was missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on juice consumption and weight-related lifestyle change throughout with no topic drift. Each response progressively guided Marco toward commitment and concrete action with a defined follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with permission-seeking, preference invitations, and incorporation of Marco's ideas into the plan. Frequent expert-mode information dumps (juice physiology, structured plan) created moments of uneven power.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and validated Marco's self-designed weekend treat compromise rather than insisting on abstinence. The directive menu-style plan was presented before Marco had fully shaped it himself, slightly constraining his authorship.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited motivation through scaling and future-visioning questions, but much of the session's substance (swaps, plan structure, cue ideas) came from the counselor rather than Marco. Little elicitation of Marco's deeper values or personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently sought permission and framed the work as shared, treating Marco as the expert on his own life. Sometimes dominated with expert content rather than drawing the plan primarily from Marco's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited strong change talk through readiness/confidence scaling and built momentum with future-oriented questions. High '10' ratings were accepted at face value rather than deepened with evocative follow-ups about why change matters.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk; explored the 'OJ love' functionally and quickly pivoted to change-oriented questions. The exploration of what Marco liked about juice briefly invited sustain talk without a double-sided reflection steering toward change.
