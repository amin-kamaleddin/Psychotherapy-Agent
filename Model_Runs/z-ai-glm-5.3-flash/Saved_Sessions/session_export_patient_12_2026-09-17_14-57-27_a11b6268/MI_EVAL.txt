# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with frequent affirmations, though some affirmations feel formulaic within rapid question stacks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures Amira's core feelings, but reflections sometimes stay surface-level and are quickly followed by multiple questions, limiting deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus on the target behavior with a brief, appropriate safety check; the session flows coherently from exploration to a concrete, achievable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interaction pattern is counselor-driven, with Amira mostly selecting from pre-packaged menus rather than co-creating ideas; her preferences shape the plan but true joint building is limited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is consistently affirmed and Amira's declination of rehearsal is respected, but counselor-generated option menus and offers to script the text subtly undermine her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked key motivations (the three-week clean stretch, sister's support) leading to genuine self-efficacy statements, though offering answers before Amira generates her own slightly undercuts full evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira's choices are respected, but the counselor frequently supplies solution menus, positioning herself as the idea-generator rather than explicitly eliciting Amira's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk culminating in 'this week, sending one text? That I can do,' but these statements are not always deepened with reflections when they first emerge.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective redirection to exceptions (past clean stretch, sister's support) without pressing on resistance, though occasional reflections repeat hopelessness language, mildly reinforcing sustain talk.
