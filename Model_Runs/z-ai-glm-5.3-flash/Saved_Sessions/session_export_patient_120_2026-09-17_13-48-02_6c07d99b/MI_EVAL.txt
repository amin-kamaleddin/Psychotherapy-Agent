# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains basic respect and normalization, but warmth is formulaic and repetitive; no affirmation beyond generic praise and Sofia's emotional question is answered informationally rather than with attunement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent — repeated vulnerable disclosures (fear of failing, shame, distrust of herself) are never reflected back, with responses jumping straight to advice. The single empathic gesture ('I hear how scared you are') comes too late in the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol cessation with a logical progression from assessment to skills practice to relapse prevention. Points lost because movement is prescriptive and checklist-driven rather than collaboratively guided.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is asymmetric — the counselor delivers expert instructions while Sofia executes, with template-like advice blocks that don't build on her own contributions (refusal line, code word, leave time). Partnership energy comes from Sofia pulling the counselor, not the reverse.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Despite offering option menus, the underlying stance is controlling, framing compliance as the correct path. Sofia's necessity-driven motivation ('I don't have any other choice') is never reframed toward intrinsic choice or ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No open questions explore Sofia's reasons, values, or confidence — all her motivation surfaced spontaneously and was never harvested. A rich motivational moment ('I actually feel more like a mom') is answered only with logistics about the next appointment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor consistently assumes the expert role, delivering bulleted prescriptive instructions with Sofia cast as recipient rather than co-author. Sofia's own plan components are never acknowledged or leveraged.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia spontaneously offers strong change talk ('I wanna be responsible,' 'I need to do this for them') that is never reflected, amplified, or built upon. No evocative questions are used, so change talk development is entirely accidental.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Substantial sustain talk (wanting one last stretch of young life, fear of failure) is neither reflected nor strategically softened. Directive solutions steamroll ambivalence rather than using double-sided reflections to help Sofia resolve it.
