# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, normalizing ambivalence and respecting the client's skepticism. The client's remark 'Thanks for not just lecturing me' confirms her experience of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were consistently accurate and layered, capturing ambivalence and the ritual function of drinking, which the client confirmed. Emotional states like relief and anxiety were also well reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on alcohol use in pregnancy while progressing logically from ambivalence to a concrete plan with accountability. Every intervention tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed from the client's own ideas, with materials tailored to her preferences and communication style. Her closing gratitude confirms the non-hierarchical dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was a standout strength, with the counselor framing information for the client's own decisions and honoring her insistence that the plan be hers. She left with full ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited from within via open questions and confidence scaling, with all change reasons originating from the client. Her own words—'Being a good mom matters more than a glass of wine'—became the engine of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Permission-seeking was exemplary and the client was repeatedly positioned as the expert on her own life. Her comment that the plan 'has to feel like my plan' reflects successful partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk escalated progressively through evocative questions, confidence scaling, and concrete first steps. Commitment language culminated in the client scheduling her own follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly without amplification, then redirected toward change without argument. It naturally diminished as the session progressed.
