# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard—even when Nabil is hostile or self-critical ('That's not pathetic at all'). Honors his right to keep complaining about delays while affirming his honesty and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections that move from surface frustration to core meaning (loss of control), and mirror his ambivalence, his daughter's comment, and the difficulty of accepting help.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on anger in medical settings while allowing purposeful widening; uses 'What feels most important to you right now?' to let Nabil choose the focus, building progressively from venting to a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine exchange of equals—asks rather than prescribes, builds on Nabil's work expertise, and lets him set the agenda; Nabil explicitly recognizes the difference ('it's mine'). 

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and repeated autonomy support ('you get to decide,' 'it's your call'), with Nabil confirming genuine choice ownership: 'Nobody's handing me a leaflet.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn almost entirely from Nabil himself—control, transferable delegation skills, his daughter's observation—culminating in his self-generated commitment ('maybe how I sit with them can').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the client; the entire plan (ringing ahead, calendar blocking, involving his wife) originates from Nabil, with ownership affirmed at closing: 'it all came from you.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and deepened—Nabil shifts from 'It's the system that needs fixing' to 'I could ask her... but I could'—with strengths, values, and self-generated ideas consistently reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally pivots well away from sustain talk using double-sided reflections and redirects toward agency, though some reflections (e.g., 'if the system ran smoothly') briefly validate system-blame before pivoting—strategically useful but not fully softening.
