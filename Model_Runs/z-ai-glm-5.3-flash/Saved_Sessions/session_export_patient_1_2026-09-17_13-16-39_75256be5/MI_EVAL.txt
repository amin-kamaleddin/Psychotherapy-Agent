# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validated Ahmed's reassurance and attachment to cigars without shaming. However, warmth was somewhat formulaic and lacked explicit affirmation of his strengths and honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections captured both his barrier and commitment, but empathy was brief and quickly pivoted to medical checklists. The emotional meaning behind his minimization was never explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent focus on the target behavior with no topic drift, culminating in a concrete, testable plan. Focus leaned heavily on symptom monitoring rather than developing motivation about smoking itself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session opened collaboratively with menu options, but the middle was dominated by one-way instruction delivery. Ahmed received rather than co-constructed most of the medical content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely offered choices throughout and honored Ahmed's selections without pressure. Slightly limited by dense prescriptive instruction blocks and one early directive ('pause the mini cigars if possible').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ahmed's own reasons, values, and beliefs about cigar risks were never elicited, leaving his motivation external and purely instrumental. The clinician missed repeated openings to develop intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened collaboratively and offered options, but mid-session exchanges became clinician-directed with long instruction blocks. Ahmed was not positioned as the expert on his own habits and triggers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully secured a concrete commitment (halving, lozenges, tracking) via menu options. However, change talk was never deepened—no confidence/importance exploration or articulation of Ahmed's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not argue with or reinforce sustain talk, and gently corrected misinformation once. Sustain talk dominated the session without strategic double-sided reflections, and the closing sustain statement went unreframed.
