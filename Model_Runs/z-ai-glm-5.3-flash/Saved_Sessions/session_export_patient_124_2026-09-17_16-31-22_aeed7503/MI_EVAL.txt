# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, framing suggestions as optional and treating Sofia's minimization and ambivalence as normal without any moralizing about her use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captures Sofia's ambivalence and validates her honesty, but deeper emotional content—like her fear of disappointing her mother—was met with practical scripts rather than empathic reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains consistent focus on marijuana use, progressing logically through exploration, planning, obstacle management, role-play, and next steps, closing with specific time-bound commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed through dialogue, with decisions negotiated and an interactive role-play where Sofia's own responses determine the intervention's content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly frames everything as Sofia's decision, asks permission before suggestions, validates her self-rated confidence rather than arguing it higher, and lets her raise it herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor draws out Sofia's own motivations and solutions effectively, but at times supplies ideas quickly (coping strategies, refusal scripts) and misses opportunities to evoke deeper values like family expectations and future goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sofia's choices substantially shape the session—strategies, dollar limits, and scripts come from her words—and the counselor actively solicits her preferences at every decision point.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation of Sofia's own change language, evidenced by her progression from tentative openness to concrete commitments ('Deal') and confidence rising from 6 to 7.5 through her own work.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally redirects sustain talk with balanced reflections rather than arguing, but some responses restate sustain-talk content and double-sided reflections tilting toward change could be more strategic.
