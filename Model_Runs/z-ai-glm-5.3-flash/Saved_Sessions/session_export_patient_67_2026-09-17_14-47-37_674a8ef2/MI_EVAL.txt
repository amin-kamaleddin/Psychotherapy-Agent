# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of honesty and normalization of worries. Affirmations could be more individualized and warmer to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures ambivalence and the tension between habit and curiosity. Some reflections remain brief restatements rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning, such as loneliness or fear of resembling his father.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on alcohol reduction, progressing logically from ambivalence to a concrete, client-owned plan. Each exchange moves steadily toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed with permission-seeking and joint decisions. Some option lists were pre-formatted, and the patient's comment about 'a lot of questions at once' suggests pacing could be better attuned.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently reinforced with explicit no-pressure framing, permission to decline, and repeated choice points. The patient's growing ownership of the plan reflects this strong support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is effectively drawn from within via confidence scaling, imagined change, and the patient's own insights. Early stacked questions diluted evocation, and some options came from the counselor rather than the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Expertise is framed as residing with the patient, with choices offered and his contributions visibly shaping the plan. Slight deduction for a somewhat advice-heavy tactic list, though it was consented to.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is effectively evoked through scaled confidence questions and invitations to imagine change, moving the patient from uncertainty to a concrete plan at a 6. Early double-barreled questions and under-amplified change talk moments limited the score.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and gently redirected toward doable steps, avoiding argument. Redirection is sometimes implicit rather than using deliberate double-sided reflections.
