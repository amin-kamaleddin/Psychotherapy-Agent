# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and acknowledged Milan's perspective without criticism, but warmth was brief and formulaic, lacking deeper affirmation of Milan as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections capturing Milan's stated views well, but rarely reached the emotional layer beneath, such as embarrassment about being called out or tension between faith identity and party behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on alcohol use and risks with logical session progression, though drifted into logistics like signal wording and accepted Milan's framing without re-linking to the broader behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was largely a joint endeavor with Milan's preferences shaping the plan, but the counselor supplied most content (scripts, consequence lists) rather than co-generating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and accepted refusals without pressure, but the consequence list edged toward persuasive pressure and Milan's ownership of decisions was rarely explicitly reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened with strong evocative questions and built on Milan's photo fear, but frequently shifted to counselor-supplied advice rather than drawing out Milan's motivations, and never explored his deflected values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited Milan's input and his contributions genuinely shaped the plan, but parts slipped toward expert-driven advice-giving with pre-packaged scripts rather than drawing from Milan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evoked some change-oriented thinking via open questions and the confidence ruler, but most change talk centered on harm reduction rather than reevaluating drinking, and commitment language was thin and rarely reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and sometimes pivoted well, but met it with neutral acknowledgment rather than strategic softening (no double-sided reflections), and Milan exited still rejecting that he has a problem.
