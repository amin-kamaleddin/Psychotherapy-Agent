# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated unconditional positive regard throughout, even amid Dylan's defensiveness and blame-shifting. No labeling or lecturing about underage drinking; warmth remained consistent even when Dylan called the process rigged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurately reflected both content and emotional nuance, including Dylan's fear of giving 'ammo' and tension between honesty and self-protection. Dylan's responses ('it doesn't sound as bad as it's been in my head') confirm the reflections landed and deepened his self-understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the conduct incident with each exchange advancing toward resolution. Repeated circling on the drinking-ownership question drew mild pushback ('you keep pushing on that part'), suggesting some momentum loss before Dylan reached ownership.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint inquiry — Dylan's ideas were treated as working hypotheses to explore together. The final statement was co-developed, with the counselor providing structure while Dylan supplied all content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision was framed as Dylan's own, with no advice or prescribed outcome. Dylan retained full ownership, even strategizing himself while the counselor supported his exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within the client; questions elicited Dylan's own meaning-making and he invoked his own reference point (a friend who owned a mistake) unprompted. Arc moved from externalized blame to self-generated commitment language and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Dylan as the expert ('you know this process better than I do') and asked what felt fair to him. The final statement was entirely Dylan's formulation, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions evoked progressively stronger change talk, culminating in unprompted commitment language ('I take responsibility for that... the rule still applies to me right now'). Reinforced and consolidated the commitment by asking how Dylan would feel entering the meeting.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirected sustain talk through reframing toward values and double-sided reflections without arguing. Some responses briefly restated Dylan's grievances, slightly prolonging his defensive stance before resolution.
