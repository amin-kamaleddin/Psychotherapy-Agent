# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, validating Maria's anger and affirming her handling of the situation. Warmth is somewhat sparse and transactional, with affirmations sometimes skipped in favor of jumping to options.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections early, but the counselor repeatedly pivots to information and menu options instead of reflecting emotion. Most notably, she missed the emotional weight of Maria's frightening asthma night disclosure, reframing it rather than acknowledging the fear underneath.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stayed anchored to asthma management with clear, goal-linked outputs: doctor questions, pushback script, checklist plan, and a follow-up deal. Deduction for a dense risk-education lecture in the middle that briefly lost the motivational thread.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing evident in choice menus, asking before delivering, and a co-constructed final plan shaped by Maria's pushback. Deduction because the counselor sometimes defaults to producing deliverables herself rather than jointly generating them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support: Maria left owning every decision, never pressured toward daily medication, and explicitly framed the trial as conditional on her own criteria. She confidently stated she wasn't agreeing to anything today.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk emerged organically from Maria's own concerns, scary night, and demand for straight answers. However, evocation is partly incidental — few open questions explored Maria's values or reasons directly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maria is positioned as decision-maker and her choices shaped the direction, but dense expert-advisor delivery (risk lists, pre-packaged scripts) diluted true partnership. The counselor rarely explored what Maria already knows or believes beyond her initial statements.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited self-generated change talk: 'maybe I'd think about it,' conditional low-dose trial talk, and acknowledgment she takes it more seriously than she lets on. Deduction for not deliberately deepening or reflecting these change statements when they occurred.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without amplification, and skepticism was validated while gently naming potential unseen harms. Sustain talk visibly diminished over the session, indicating effective redirection.
