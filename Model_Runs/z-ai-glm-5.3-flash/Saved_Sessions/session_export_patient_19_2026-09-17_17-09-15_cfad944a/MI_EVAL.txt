# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, nonjudgment, and affirmation ('that's insight and courage'). Fewer individualized affirmations tied to Anya's specific strengths (e.g., her humor) would raise this score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capturing ambivalence, FOMO, and fear of losing her safety net. Reflections sometimes stay cognitive and move quickly to problem-solving at emotional moments (e.g., when Anya teared up in the role-play).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on gambling with steady progression from scaling questions to a concrete, dated, rehearsed plan (Thursday autopay + sister text + call script) with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine co-construction of the plan, incorporating Anya's tweaks and the creative role-play. The counselor tended to propose next steps and generate drafts rather than brainstorming from scratch together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicit choice support ('I'm here to explore options and support whatever you choose') with Anya controlling plan details. Rapid-fire option lists occasionally steered rather than purely supported choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation via scaling questions and confidence exploration, eliciting Anya's own reasons and rationale. Deeper exploration of core values (travel, Egypt, family) was missed — Anya offered the 'Egypt money' connection but it wasn't developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anya positioned as the expert with her contributions clearly shaping the final plan. At times the counselor became checklist-oriented and directive in structuring options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful scaling questions elicited strong commitment language ('cutting up the credit cards. For a starter,' 'I'm 25... not planning on spending my life in debt'). Deeper exploration of motivations (e.g., the Egypt connection) would strengthen this further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and responded to failure predictions by affirming commitment. More strategic double-sided reflections reframing sustain talk toward her values would improve this.
