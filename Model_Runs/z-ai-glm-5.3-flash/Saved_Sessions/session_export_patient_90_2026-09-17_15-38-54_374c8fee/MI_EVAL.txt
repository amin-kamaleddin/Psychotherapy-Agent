# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; gracefully accepted the dog as non-negotiable ('I hear you about the dog — I won't bring that up'). Closing affirmations and non-lecturing tone were explicitly appreciated by the patient.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong early complex reflections capturing distress and ambivalence accurately. Later reflections stayed at summary/confirmation level rather than deepening emotional exploration of stress and exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained excellent focus on asthma management with a coherent progression from exploration to commitment to implementation. No topic drift; the plan included concrete cues, backups, a missed-dose rule, and a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with every element (coffee cue, blunt alarm, wife nudge, car spare) chosen or modified by the patient. A true equal-partner exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced patient ownership ('You're in charge of where we focus') and offered advice as options for selection, never imposed. The patient's final comment confirms successful autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from within — personal reasons, self-corrected problem-solving, and rising confidence (5→6.5→7). Motivation and the plan visibly belonged to the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the patient as the expert and his input genuinely shaped the plan. Occasionally presented tweak menus before purely eliciting his own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked desire, commitment, confidence via repeated 0–10 scales, and anticipated outcomes. Patient language strengthened progressively toward self-generated commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively deflected sustain talk without amplifying it (e.g., the dog resistance). Responses leaned toward avoidance/neutral acknowledgment rather than strategic double-sided reflections pivoting toward change.
