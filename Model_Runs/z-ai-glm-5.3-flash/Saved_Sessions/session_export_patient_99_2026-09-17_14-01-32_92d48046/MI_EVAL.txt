# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout without criticizing Matteo's minimization, but warmth was somewhat formulaic with few genuine affirmations of his honesty or strengths and no normalization of ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered accurate but brief reflections and validated Matteo's pushback on the 2-drink limit, yet rarely explored emotional nuance—missing his peer-identity concerns, fear of caving, and mild surprise at his own drinking frequency.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session anchored to drinking within the ankle-injury context and efficiently produced a concrete two-week plan with a check-in; at times structure came at the expense of fully exploring ambivalence first.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan was largely shaped by Matteo's own preferences through repeated option-offering, but the checklist/Q&A structure and expert delivery of advice before exploring his own ideas tempered full collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering choices, a menu of plans, and emphasizing the plan should feel doable; however, medical advice was delivered in a lecturing format without first asking permission.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Matteo generated his own plan elements and expressed hope, but the counselor relied heavily on providing information and pre-formatted options rather than drawing out Matteo's own values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Matteo's contributions genuinely shaped the session (his plan preference, his humor in scripts), but the counselor positioned himself as the source of expertise with little acknowledgment that Matteo knows his own social world best.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited meaningful change talk including 'Maybe I could skip Wednesdays' and 'Let's try it for these two weeks,' though this emerged somewhat accidentally rather than through deliberate evocative questioning or deepening of commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and offered some reframes, but statements like 'everyone I know drinks more' were bypassed rather than strategically softened with double-sided reflections to build discrepancy.
