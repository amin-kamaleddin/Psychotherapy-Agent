# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance, affirming Daniela's plan and normalizing her ambivalence. However, warmth occasionally felt formulaic and was quickly followed by lists of options rather than deeper individualized validation of the emotional toll of two years of secrecy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate, but empathy increasingly gave way to problem-solving with scripts and checklists. Emotional disclosures—fear of being left on read, hurt about Maya's discretion, exhaustion of 'carrying a secret 24/7'—went largely unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained excellent focus on the target behavior of peer disclosure throughout, with every exchange tied to the disclosure plan. The session ended with a concrete, dated action step (sending the message Thursday night).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally honored Daniela's ideas, adapting scripts to her voice and building on her choice of Lena over her mother as support. Still, pre-packaged content (pros/cons lists, three scripts, a five-step backup plan) was often delivered before she finished exploring her own thoughts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice was emphasized consistently ('It's your choice,' 'you can absolutely make it sound like you'), with options rather than directives. The sheer volume of prescriptive advice at times implied the counselor knew the best path, slightly undercutting full ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation occurred early, drawing out Daniela's own reasons for change (safety, exhaustion of hiding). But as the session progressed, the counselor supplied content rather than eliciting, missing chances to explore what Thursday means to her, the meaning of telling Priya, and her confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniela's preferences clearly shaped the session, but the counselor often occupied the expert role with ready-made scripts, checklists, and step-by-step plans. This limited power-sharing despite Daniela showing strong problem-solving ability of her own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniela generated meaningful change talk and a self-generated plan ('write it, sit on it, send Thursday'), which the counselor affirmed. However, the counselor did not strategically deepen it—missing chances to explore her confidence or what makes Thursday feel possible.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and ambivalence was briefly normalized. However, extensive worst-case contingency planning arguably amplified Daniela's fears rather than softening them, with no double-sided reflections or deliberate pivots back to her reasons for change.
