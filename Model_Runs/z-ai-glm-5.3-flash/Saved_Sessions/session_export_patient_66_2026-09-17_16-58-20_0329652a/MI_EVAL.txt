# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations and warm reframes; Kareem explicitly noted he felt respected ('Cheers for not just lecturing me'). Affirmations were somewhat formulaic and prior failed quit attempts weren't explicitly normalized as understandable.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured ambivalence and the emotional meaning of the nighttime smoke (quiet/recharge time) and the sting of his daughter's comment. Early reflections sometimes bundled content rather than probing deeper feelings, and exploration of past failure was brief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with every exchange advancing toward a concrete plan (ruler → options → inhaler → day-one plan → nighttime ritual → check-in). The session ended with a specific, dated commitment — exemplary direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited Kareem's input at each decision point and the final plan was composed almost entirely of his own ideas. The medication discussion became a counselor-directed mini-lecture, and the day-one plan was largely counselor-authored.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly and repeatedly reinforced ('only if you want,' 'support whatever you decide') with honest framing of contingencies rather than pressure. Kareem responded by taking ownership, and choice language was woven throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The importance ruler and open questions elicited Kareem's own reasons (wife, daughter, dignity at work), and his self-generated plan with spontaneous 'I might start tonight' showed internalized motivation. Some content (efficacy statistics, coping tips) was provided rather than evoked, and confidence was never explicitly explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Kareem as the expert with his plan built from his own ideas (coffee runs, garden tea, starting on his hardest day). The medication discussion shifted to an expert-driven delivery of options and efficacy data rather than co-construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The importance ruler and open questions effectively evoked self-motivational statements and commitment language ('Monday it is,' 'I might start tonight'). Commitment language wasn't deepened once momentum built (no confidence ruler or elaboration on what makes this time different).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged neutrally and briefly, followed by pivots toward what Kareem wanted, and the reframe 'slips aren't failures' directly countered his all-or-nothing thinking. Early reflections gave some airtime to sustain talk (enjoyment, social smoking) without double-sided reflections tilting toward change.
