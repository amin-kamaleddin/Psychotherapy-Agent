# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental respect with affirmations of Marcus's honesty and choices. Occasional effusive praise ('you're giving me way too much credit') slightly weakened credibility.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captured his fears about parental consequences and peer scrutiny. Some reflections tracked the counselor's agenda rather than his feeling of being surveilled by adults.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained steady focus and progressed from ambivalence toward a concrete harm-reduction plan. The goal (cutting back vs. safety vs. risk management) was never explicitly negotiated and remained somewhat ambiguous.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely open questions and co-developed strategies; Marcus's own ideas shaped the plan and he freely rejected one option. Power stayed balanced throughout with no lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently honored choice, accepted his refusal to quit, and offered a menu of options rather than prescriptions. The final question mildly presupposed commitment before Marcus explicitly stated it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model evocation—every reason and strategy for change came from Marcus's own words, moving him from precontemplation to self-generated harm-reduction ideas. He even noticed the shift himself ('I heard myself just now').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Marcus as the expert on his own world and shared information only after he requested it. His contributions genuinely redirected the session's plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocative questions drew substantial change talk, which was effectively amplified. A missed opportunity to consolidate with a commitment-strength question or confidence ruler.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook handling: minimization was never reinforced or confronted, double-sided reflections pivoted toward his own concerns, and 'I'm not saying I have a problem' was immediately validated rather than argued.
