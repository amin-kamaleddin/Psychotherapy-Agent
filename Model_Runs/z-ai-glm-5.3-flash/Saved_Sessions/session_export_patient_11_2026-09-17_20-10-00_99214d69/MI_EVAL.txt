# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with affirmations like acknowledging her 'own wisdom from your old routine' and framing stumbles as acceptable. No judgment or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently and accurately captured both content and emotional nuance, e.g., 'You've separated the plan from the person' and the identity-level reflection 'I'm someone who walks before work.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained steady focus on establishing a physical activity routine from start to finish, strategically sequencing the anchor, timing, minimum action, obstacles, and accountability with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session operated between equal partners, with 'let's figure it out together' framing and options offered as prompts rather than prescriptions. Amira's ideas directly determined the plan's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforced client ownership at every turn ('what feels like the right first step... or something else entirely?') and even negotiated the counselor's follow-up role rather than assuming it. The plan was entirely Amira's design.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Relied almost exclusively on evocative questions, eliciting all motivational insights (salsa jar, identity, becoming) from the client rather than supplying reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly located expertise in the client ('These are your tools, not mine'), with key plan elements (coffee-walk anchor, ten-minutes rule, shoes by the door) originating from Amira.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled open questions drew out strong commitment and identity-level change talk ('I can do this,' 'I'm someone who keeps small promises to herself'), each reinforced and deepened rather than left unexplored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently pivoted away from sustain talk by reframing the failure-quit pattern and couch temptation as self-knowledge to design around, converting barriers into planning material.
