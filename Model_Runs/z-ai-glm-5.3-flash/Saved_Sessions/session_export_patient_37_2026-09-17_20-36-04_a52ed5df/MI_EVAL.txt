### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, with meaningful affirmations ('you're being a thoughtful parent,' 'it takes courage') and no judgment or condescension.

**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including complex and double-sided reflections that capture emotional nuance; the counselor adjusts pace to Elena's stated processing style.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains steady focus on the vaccine decision, with each exchange advancing the agenda and active momentum management without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction operates between true equals, with the counselor seeking input before offering information and Elena's ideas visibly steering the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

**Comments:** Motivation is largely drawn from Elena's own values and reasons, but a key shift relies on the counselor's reframe about inaction being a choice, and confidence/ability talk is under-elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Elena — her request for topic order and learning-style preference genuinely shape the session structure.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective open questions elicit substantive change talk ('protecting her comes first,' 'let's look at the data'), though commitment language could be deepened further (e.g., exploring what vaccinating would mean, confidence statements).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
