# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout with affirmations and no labeling, but affirmations are somewhat generic rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Brief accurate reflections are present, but reflective listening is limited; the session is dominated by instructions and scripts rather than complex reflections of Linda's fear, shame, and the social meaning of smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with no topic drift, though direction is achieved through behavioral coaching rather than motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda's preferences shape parts of the plan, but the style is largely expert-driven with templates, scripts, and menus offered rather than genuine co-creation from her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent menu-based choices, honoring Linda's decisions, and reframing slips as information support autonomy, though advice-giving is often prescriptive and expert verdicts replace eliciting her judgment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Linda's motivation is largely self-generated; the readiness ruler lacks the essential 'why not lower' follow-up, and there is little open-ended exploration of her values, hopes, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing occurs through choice questions, but the overall interaction is prescriptive, with Linda's request for reassurance answered expertly rather than returned to her own wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerges from Linda herself rather than deliberate evocation; the readiness ruler is used without follow-up, and no open questions invite her reasons, hopes, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is neither reinforced nor actively softened; it is minimally acknowledged or sidestepped, with no double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with change talk.
