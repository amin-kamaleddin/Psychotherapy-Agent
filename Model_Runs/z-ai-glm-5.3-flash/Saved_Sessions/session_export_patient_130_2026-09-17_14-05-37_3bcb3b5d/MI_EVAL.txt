# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with warm affirmations ('Thanks for being straightforward,' 'That's a solid try'). Warmth was somewhat formulaic, and ambivalence was not explicitly normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made adequate reflective statements capturing Viktor's perspective, but often fell into question batteries and advice. Missed the chance to explore his fear of losing his marriage with emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained steady focus on weekend substance use and relationship fallout, guiding toward a concrete, testable plan. Direction was achieved partly through prescriptive advice rather than values-based goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sought Viktor's preferences and adopted his edits to the text message, but the counselor supplied nearly all ideas and strategies. Functioned more as advisor-and-client than equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some choices and let Viktor set his return time, but heavily prescribed scripts and repeated the checklist three times. Viktor's comment 'You sound like my wife now' signals his perceived autonomy was undermined.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied on checklists and scripted solutions rather than drawing out Viktor's own motivations. Missed pivotal change statements like 'she matters more than a Friday night' instead of evoking and deepening them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasionally invited input and incorporated Viktor's wording edits, but the overall style was expert-driven. Viktor's contributions modified the counselor's plan rather than shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created conditions for change talk and Viktor produced meaningful statements ('the fighting sucks,' 'she matters more than a Friday night'). The counselor failed to strategically deepen or consolidate these moments, responding with checklists instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk ('this is your time and you value it') without arguing. Lacked double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to actively soften and resolve his ambivalence.
