# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a neutral, nonjudgmental stance throughout with a brief closing affirmation. However, warmth is limited and the tone occasionally reads as technique-driven, with insufficient normalization when Kieran showed resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers several accurate reflections, including a good attempt at deeper understanding around numbness and disappointment. Reflections are often formulaic and repetitive, and Kieran's terse replies suggest the empathy did not fully open him up.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session lacks a clearly defined target behavior, and direction is muddled between exploration and premature action planning in precontemplation. Repeated closing instructions also diluted focus, which Kieran explicitly flagged.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is largely collaborative, with options offered rather than directives and Kieran co-constructing a loose, low-key plan. Collaboration is uneven, however, as the counselor delivered homework structure despite Kieran explicitly rejecting it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy language throughout, including permission to use time flexibly, no-pressure framing, and explicit acceptance of Kieran's request to keep things loose. Falls short of 5 because structured menus and repeated reminders subtly steer toward the counselor's preferred activity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation — Kieran's wish to 'care about something again' and agreement to try the song emerged from open questions. However, much of the session relies on counselor-supplied interventions rather than drawing out Kieran's own solutions, and his motivations are never explored in depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and lets Kieran's preferences directly shape the intervention format. Stops short of 5 because the options menu feels pre-packaged and the counselor does not explicitly acknowledge Kieran as the expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully evokes change talk, with Kieran stating he'd 'actually care about something' and committing to the song exercise. This change talk is not deepened strategically, as the counselor jumps to task assignment rather than exploring his stated wish to care again.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is mostly acknowledged without amplification, and a reasonable reframe about numbness is offered. There is little strategic pivoting back to change, and the repeated 'even forgetting is useful data' may inadvertently legitimize disengagement.
