# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental with warm statements, but warmth is brief and formulaic, quickly pivoting to checklists. There is no explicit affirmation of Camila's courage in seeking help after friends' comments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is minimal — only one token empathic statement was offered, and emotionally rich disclosures were met with bulleted action lists. The counselor missed numerous opportunities to reflect her frustration, self-criticism, and relief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking with concrete behavioral steps that tied back to the target behavior. Minor deductions for rushing past emotional exploration and brief safety-screening shifts in focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial collaboration — Camila's input shaped key decisions — but the counselor defaults to prescriptive bullet-point plans, and one collaborative question ('Does that sound too soft?') went unaddressed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Camila is offered choices and allowed to reject options, but they are menus of clinician-generated options rather than genuine ownership. There is little explicit reinforcement that she is in charge of her own change plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor supplied scripts, plans, and tips rather than drawing out Camila's own motivations and resources. Her motivations were stated by her but never elicited or developed through exploratory questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly adopted an advice-dispensing, checklist-driven stance that positioned expertise with the clinician. Camila's own ideas emerged but were never explicitly validated as her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Camila generated change talk spontaneously, but the counselor did not evoke or deepen it, responding with task instructions instead. One good moment (asking which small goal felt doable) drew commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced, but ambivalent statements ('two feels impossible', 'am I overreacting?') were bypassed with more planning steps rather than met with double-sided reflections or reframing. Her ambivalence was left unresolved.
