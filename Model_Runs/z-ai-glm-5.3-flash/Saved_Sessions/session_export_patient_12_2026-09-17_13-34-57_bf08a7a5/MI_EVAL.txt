# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Regard is largely procedural; repeated formulaic reassurances ('You're not alone' twice verbatim) and immediate task lists steamroll Amira's disclosures. No affirmations of her honesty or courage, and her self-blaming admission 'I kept choosing the pills' receives no affirming response.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Acknowledgments are brief and consistently displaced by question barrages and unsolicited advice. No complex reflections; Amira's shame, fear of her mother, and vulnerable moments are never deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on substance use and ends with a concrete commitment (call mom today) with plan and fallback. However, direction comes through rapid problem-solving rather than MI-guided focusing, with some redundant risk assessment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is markedly asymmetric: safety plans, messages, and scripts are delivered wholesale rather than built together. Offered choices are collaborative in form only, since all options originate from the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine menu choices and respect for Amira's pacing request ('coach me first') are strengths. However, direct recommendations abound and the counselor positions herself as the source of plans, scripts, and numbers rather than cultivating Amira's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes Amira needs external input rather than evoking her own reasons and confidence; open evocative questions are nearly absent. Her powerful insight 'I kept choosing the pills' goes entirely unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly occupies the expert role—diagnosing, prescribing, drafting, and listing resources. Amira's own wisdom is never drawn upon or credited; her input is incorporated only when directly requested.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk emerged ('maybe something's better than nothing,' 'I want to actually call her today') largely due to the role-play structure, not deliberate evocation. Pivotal commitment statements receive no affirmation or deepening.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Heavy sustain talk ('my willpower's garbage,' 'talking about it feels pointless') is bypassed rather than softened—no double-sided reflections or reframing of helplessness as ambivalence. Doing everything for Amira inadvertently reinforces her helplessness.
