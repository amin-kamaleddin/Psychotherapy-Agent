# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, non-judgmental regard throughout, with no disapproval of drug use or helplessness. Self-criticism was reframed ('Not pathetic — hopeful') and ambivalence normalized ('Both things can be true').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were highly accurate, capturing both content and emotional subtext, including complex double-sided reflections. The patient explicitly confirmed feeling heard: 'someone actually listened instead of lecturing.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus while strategically narrowing scope from morning dread to a specific, self-generated first step ('one text, before I overthink it'). The clinician guided without steering and consolidated the plan effectively.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine co-construction: the target (mornings), the action (a text), and the conditions were all generated by the patient, with the clinician shaping only through questions and reflections. No agenda was imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was woven into nearly every exchange ('on your terms and your timing,' 'if you wanted to'), no advice was given, and the clinician explicitly attributed the plan to the patient. The choice of the smallest possible step was honored without upselling.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key insights and motivation came from the patient via precisely calibrated evocative questions suited to early contemplation. The result was self-generated hope ('I can try that much') — the hallmark of strong evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician explicitly positioned the patient as the expert ('you know yourself best') and framed questions as genuine curiosity. The plan originated entirely from the patient, with active power-sharing confirmed by her feedback.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skilfully evoked and progressively strengthened, from 'maybe part of me knows' to a specific self-generated plan and commitment-adjacent language. Ownership was reinforced by reflecting and deepening change talk rather than moving past it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged without amplification and paired with change-oriented double-sided reflections. A minor deduction as some sustain statements were mirrored fairly fully before redirection, leaving slightly more airtime on barriers.
