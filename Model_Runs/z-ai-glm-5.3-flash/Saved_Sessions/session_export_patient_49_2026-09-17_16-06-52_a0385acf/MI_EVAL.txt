# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout and normalizes slips ('treat it as information, not failure'). Warmth is somewhat brief and formulaic given the dense multi-question opener.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some decent reflections capturing Hana's perspective, but reflections are sparse overall. The emotional weight of her worry and fear of sliding back during stressful weeks is acknowledged but not deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on smoking reduction with clear momentum toward concrete action (Thursday start, follow-up). Direction is driven more by the counselor's structured agenda than by summaries tying steps back to Hana's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tone is largely joint and the final plan is genuinely co-constructed. Early turns are advice-dense, with the counselor supplying most of the content and Hana reacting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is repeatedly emphasized and Hana's pacing is respected without pressure. Rapid option menus frame the choices for her rather than fostering her generation of options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The readiness ruler and open questions drew out ambivalence and self-motivated language. However, the counselor quickly pivots to supplying solutions rather than drawing out Hana's own reasons and hopes in depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Hana is asked to choose options and her contributions are incorporated, but the stance often slips into expert mode with pre-packaged menus. Her own ideas are rarely explicitly invited or explored.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of readiness ruler and choice-points elicited meaningful change talk ('guess I'm actually doing this'). Change talk is not consistently deepened with reflective reinforcement or elaboration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Hana's doubts about sliding back and peer pushback are met neutrally and the conversation moves on. No double-sided reflections or deliberate reframing; softening is passive rather than active.
