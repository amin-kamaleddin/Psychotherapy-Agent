# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, including 'You deserve that same compassion you give so freely to the people you love.' Validates her honesty and never judges lapses or defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong accurate complex reflections capturing ambivalence and all-or-nothing thinking. Reflections occasionally restate her words rather than deepening into the emotional layer beneath (guilt, fear of self-failure).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on medication adherence with each exchange building progressively. Detours are skillfully woven back into the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; Selene's ideas shape the entire direction. Co-created the tracking system and invited her input at every juncture.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice consistently affirmed and the closing readiness check leaves the decision with her. No advice, pressure, or coercion; her 'no promises' pace is honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently drawn from within the client, shifting from 'nothing has before' to 'I'll get them out tonight.' The psychiatrist explicitly names this: 'That wasn't me—that was you finding your own reason.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise-within-client stance explicit: 'You're the expert on your own life.' Every plan element originated from Selene, with repeated credit for her ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled questions and affirmations evoked sustained change language ('I could try it,' 'ready enough. Nervous, but ready'). Her language shifts dramatically from hopelessness to concrete commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Doubt consistently met with validating double-sided reflections and gentle pivots toward change. Each expression of sustain talk is turned into planning rather than amplified.
