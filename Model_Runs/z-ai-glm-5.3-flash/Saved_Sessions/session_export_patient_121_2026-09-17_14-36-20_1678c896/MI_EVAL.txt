# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates consistent respect and warmth toward a guarded adolescent, opening supportively and validating her frustration without judgment. Missed opportunities to affirm her courage in opening up and admitting she missed her friend.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to understand Sofia's perspective, but reflections stay superficial and repetitive ('that sounds unfair'), failing to capture her deeper hurt, loneliness, and vulnerability about missing her friend.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on peer conflict, school avoidance, and relationship repair, progressing logically to a concrete plan. Some drift into tactical drafting rather than motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses permission-style questions and incorporates some of Sofia's tweaks, but the counselor generates most content while Sofia primarily approves or slightly modifies it. True co-creation rarely occurs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices ('pick your vibe,' 'want to rehearse one?') and never pressures Sofia into action. However, the heavy volume of prescriptive advice subtly directs the change process rather than emphasizing her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early evocation of what 'stop' would look like was a strong move, but the session then shifts almost entirely to providing solutions rather than eliciting Sofia's own reasons, values, or confidence. Her motivation emerges despite, not because of, deliberate evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Frequently assumes the expert role, supplying scripts, plans, and drafts while Sofia operates largely in a consumer role. Rarely asks what Sofia thinks would work or invites her expertise about her own social world.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully moves Sofia from 'maybe I just want it to stop' to concrete commitment language ('sending one text tonight... that's actually doable'). Reliance on drafted scripts rather than amplifying Sofia's own words limits the score.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Doesn't argue with Sofia's sustain talk (fear of screenshots, distrust, avoidance), but responses jump straight to practical advice. No double-sided reflections, affirmations of strengths, or strategic reframes — sustain talk is bypassed rather than resolved.
