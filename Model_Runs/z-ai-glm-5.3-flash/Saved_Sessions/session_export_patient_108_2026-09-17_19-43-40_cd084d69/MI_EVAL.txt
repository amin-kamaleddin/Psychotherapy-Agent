# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout; Oliver's self-criticism, guilt, and humor are all received without judgment, and affirmations are specific and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflections that capture core ambivalence and go beneath surface content to meaning and identity, using the client's own key phrases.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior with no topic drift; strategic questions and consolidating summaries guide a clear trajectory from ambivalence to commitment and action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals; session direction is repeatedly handed to Oliver and his contributions shape each next step, with knowledge exchange fully bidirectional.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No advice-giving or persuasion anywhere; choices are explicitly foregrounded and Oliver's own conclusion that 'that's on me' evidences full perceived ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key motivational material — the script, insights, coping strategies, and the plan — is drawn from the client, ending with self-generated confidence ('I've got this').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Oliver is repeatedly and explicitly positioned as the expert; every plan element comes from his own words and the clinician's role is limited to reflecting, affirming, and asking.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully selects, mirrors, and names Oliver's change talk, using strategic evocative questions to elicit commitment, ability talk, a concrete plan with a date, and a visible move to preparation/action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged briefly and redirected into reasons for change without reinforcement; deeper explicit validation of the genuine losses (e.g., what Oliver will miss) before pivoting would make redirection even more robust.
