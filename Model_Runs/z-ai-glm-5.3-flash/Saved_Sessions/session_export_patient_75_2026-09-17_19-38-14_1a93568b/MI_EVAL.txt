# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; fears validated and midnight googling reframed as informed choice rather than resistance. Affirmations were specific and individualized, attributing insights to Linda herself.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate double-sided and complex reflections consistently captured her ambivalence and deep emotions, including the 'guessing game' and fear of poisoning herself, which Linda explicitly confirmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on inhaler adherence with no drift; every exchange advanced the agenda from barriers to information to a concrete behavioral commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Information was offered responsively and 'together' language pervaded the session; the final plan (toothbrush reminder, consistency trial) was Linda's own idea with the clinician facilitating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and self-direction were repeatedly reinforced; information was offered without pressure and her preference for lowest effective dose and informed decision-making was honored throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from within via targeted open questions, with her reasons and hopes reflected and amplified. The session culminated in her desire, ability, commitment, and self-devised plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Linda was positioned as expert on her own life from the start, and her choices (starting point, concerns, reminder idea) genuinely drove the session—an active power sharing, not polite consultation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematic evocation of change talk with selective reinforcement of her own motivations, culminating in strong commitment language and a concrete self-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (dependence fears, side effects) was acknowledged but not reinforced, with double-sided reflections and pivots toward change. Minor growth area: deeper strategic reframing of the deepest fears (e.g., osteoporosis) beyond acknowledgment and information correction.
