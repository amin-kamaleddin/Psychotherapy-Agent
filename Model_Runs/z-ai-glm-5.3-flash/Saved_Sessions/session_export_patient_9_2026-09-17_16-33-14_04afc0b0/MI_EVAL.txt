# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated respect and warmth, normalizing Amina's exhaustion and never judging her conflict with her ex. However, warmth occasionally felt formulaic, with limited explicit affirmation of her strengths as a mother beyond her willingness to try.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured Amina's ambivalence, fears, and the logistics of her situation. Reflections focused more on content than deeper emotion—her fear of hope being dashed and the 'knot in her chest' were not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus throughout—quickly narrowing from broad concerns to specific flashpoints, then to a single target (bedtime) and one concrete, testable step. Every exchange tied back to the target, ending with a clear plan, follow-up timing, and success indicators.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeated permission-asking, role-play proposals, and genuine incorporation of Amina's feedback demonstrated a strong collaborative stance. However, the counselor's rapid-fire questions and ready-made scripts tilted the interaction toward the counselor as the ideas person.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered choices about timing, wording, and follow-up, and Amina exercised real agency. Slightly below 5 because most specific strategies were counselor-supplied rather than drawn from Amina's own ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicited Amina's values and heartfelt change talk ('he's worth it,' 'I can do this'). Missed opportunities to deepen this motivation—exploring what her love for her son means to her—before moving to tactics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Permission-seeking, invitations for input on wording, and Amina's contributions visibly shaping the plan demonstrated strong partnership. The text scripts and reply lines were largely counselor-generated rather than elicited from Amina's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation questions produced strong change talk about trying for her son and believing she could do it. The counselor sometimes jumped to solution-mode rather than deepening motivation, and did not use confidence scaling or explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced, and double-sided reflections were used occasionally. However, when Amina voiced doubts ('I'm scared to hope... every time we try, it falls apart'), the counselor skipped past her discouragement to offer a plan, missing chances to strategically soften ambivalence.
