# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, including responding to Sofia's alcohol disclosure without alarm or judgment. However, warmth occasionally felt scripted, and there was no explicit validation of her honesty in disclosing drinking at parties.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered adequate simple reflections and decent summaries (e.g., reading as escape, hiking as head-clearing), but stayed largely at surface level. Missed the underlying vulnerability in Sofia's minimization of her drinking after the screening flag.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session's major weakness: despite the alcohol-risk screening being the referral reason, most of the session drifted into reading, hiking, and a walking plan. Alcohol was only addressed in the final minutes after Sofia herself asked if the screening required more.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The tone was consistently collaborative, with options offered rather than directives and the plan co-constructed from Sofia's own idea. Fell short because joint agenda-setting was absent at the start, letting the session drift without shared steering.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was regularly affirmed through permission-seeking and allowing Sofia to decline commitments. Some scaffolding edged toward over-structuring (drafting the text, prescribing the if-then plan), with limited emphasis on her ownership of decisions about drinking itself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on questioning, suggesting, and planning rather than eliciting Sofia's own motivations. Her stated values (feeling calm, clear-headed) were identified but never connected to her choices about alcohol, so motivation for change was never genuinely evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly handed choices to Sofia, and her input (the alarm idea, text choice, Wednesday timing) visibly shaped the plan. Partnership energy was invested in the peripheral walking agenda rather than jointly addressing the screening flag.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk was minimal and centered only on walking, not the target behavior of alcohol. When Sofia explicitly said she didn't need to cut back, the counselor did not evoke any desire, ability, reasons, or need language regarding drinking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk about alcohol was acknowledged without being reinforced or amplified, which is a strength. However, it was never actively softened through double-sided reflections or linked to her values, leaving minimization essentially unaddressed.
