# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with validation of both the gym enjoyment and the wish for weight loss. Warmth is somewhat brief and checklist-driven, with limited affirmations of the client's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate early reflections captured the client's core conflict well. However, emotionally significant disclosures about shame and fear of failure were met with logistics and plans rather than reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on sustainable health habits from start to finish, progressing logically to a concrete plan with follow-up. This is a clear strength of the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked what felt doable and incorporated the client's tweak to 3 protein breakfasts. However, the agenda, plan content, and troubleshooting were delivered top-down rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered meaningful choices at key points and validated the client's right to her weight-loss goal. The overall structure still felt prescriptive, with the clinician supplying most content.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Never explored the client's own reasons for change, values, or perspectives on past failures. The session relied on providing information and structure rather than drawing out her internal motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A largely expert-driven stance with prescribed plans, scripts, and closed logistical questions. The client's contributions were only marginally incorporated and did not shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Strong spontaneous change talk ('This feels different, more doable') was rarely evoked or deepened. Open questions about her own reasons and confidence were largely absent in favor of checklists.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged nonjudgmentally and reasonably reframed with the weekly weigh-in and delay techniques. However, detailed scale protocols sometimes amplified sustain talk, and no double-sided reflections were used.
