# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment about avoidance. Notable pre-permission for failure: 'honest either way is all I could ask.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture both content and emotional nuance, e.g., 'Friday-you and today-you are finally on the same side.' Skillfully tracked subtle ambivalence and language shifts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear arc from exploring ambivalence to a concrete, client-owned commitment ('Friday. Just a walk.'). No drift; each exchange built momentum toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan fully co-constructed from the client's own walking-past-the-center idea, explicitly acknowledged ('you found this idea yourself'). Accountability structure also arose from her suggestion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No directive or pressuring language; the plan 'fits you, not the group, not anyone else.' Pressure removed while preserving agency ('even turning around halfway already counts for something').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation and change talk originated from the client—values, coping strategies, and self-motivating statements. Client's closing 'I didn't expect to say yes to anything today' demonstrates the effectiveness of this approach.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located within the client ('you know yourself best here'). The counselor's follow-ups built directly on the client's words, letting her contributions drive the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation via desire, ability, envisioning, and deepening questions; change talk progressed from 'maybe' to explicit commitment ('Friday. I'll try... I will').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk normalized and pivoted to problem-solving rather than reinforced. Minor deduction because a few double-sided reflections re-voiced sustain talk without an immediate pivot to change language.
