# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, judgment-free regard throughout: 'There's nothing cheesy in what you said' and 'That's a mom taking shape' directly counteract the shame from prior medical experiences, validating both her need for belonging and her maternal protectiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and emotionally deep, capturing the core fear ('Friday nights held all your friendships... being left behind'). Sofia responds with visible relief, confirming the reflections landed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on abstaining from alcohol during pregnancy while following Sofia's lead. Every exchange builds momentum: support needs, the painting plan, anticipating obstacles, and concrete commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: suggestions are invitations, and Sofia's ideas (Dani, painting, telling Maria) shape the entire plan. The counselor validates her perspective and lets her agency drive the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership are reinforced consistently, with open-ended questions and emphasis on self-generated action ('texting Dani is mine. Nobody's making me do it'). The counselor frames the plan as Sofia's own rather than externally imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within via open questions ('what would make that feel good rather than lonely?'). Sofia's strongest motivation — 'I don't want to let this kid down' — is client-generated, and she explicitly notes the difference from being told what she did wrong.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positions Sofia as the expert ('You know your world best'), and she remarks that nobody had asked her that before. All plan ideas originate from her, with the closing 'it's all yours' making ownership explicit.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and explicitly highlighted ('I want to try' and 'I have to try — both of those are pulling you toward something'). Sofia's commitment language escalates in strength across the session, culminating in concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and met with double-sided reflections ('Saturday will still be hard AND you're doing it anyway'), then quickly pivoted toward change. A few reflections briefly elaborated the sustain side (loneliness, social exclusion), though always with therapeutic purpose.
