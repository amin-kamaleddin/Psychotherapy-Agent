# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance maintained throughout ("I'll listen without judgment"; "no guilt, no judgment"). Affirmations ("That's a solid, doable choice") were supportive but somewhat generic and occasionally premature relative to Marco's request not to be praised before earning it.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Marco's perspective well ("smoked since 14... the idea of changing feels unrealistic and unwelcome"). However, reflections were sometimes brief summaries rather than deep exploration of the emotional attachment ("been through a lot with that little stick"), leaving some emotional nuance unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking behavior from opening question through concrete plan. Each turn moved forward: ambivalence → ritual → rulers → 15-minute delay experiment → implementation (coffee timer) → relapse plan → check-in logistics. No topic drift despite Marco's guarded deflecting style.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Largely equal partners — Marco selected delay length, start date, and slip strategy. However, the counselor at times supplied menu options and structured questions (0–10 scales, "what will you do tonight") in a slightly formulaic way; the rapid checklist closing felt more clinician-driven than co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Excellent. Repeated emphasis on choice: "No pressure," "I'll support whatever you decide," "you're doing this for you." Adjusted to Marco's preference for a brief, no-nonsense check-in and affirmed his ownership ("I'm doing this for me") rather than claiming credit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Marco rather than lecturing — asking what smoking gives him, what would need to be different, and using rulers. Moved a precontemplative client to concrete commitment. Could be stronger: did not explore the personal meaning of the teeth concern or invite deeper reasons, relying somewhat on standard MI tools.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located with Marco: "I'll follow your lead," offering options and letting Marco choose. Marco's contributions clearly shaped the plan (coffee timer, 15-minute delay). A 5 would require more explicit acknowledgment of Marco's expertise; occasional slips into checklist-like problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk via importance/confidence rulers and small-experiment framing, eliciting "maybe I could stretch it out a bit," "I could give that a shot for a week," and concrete commitment. Missed opportunities to deepen talk (why teeth mattered, what success would feel like); moved quickly to logistics rather than amplifying motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Consistently avoided reinforcing sustain talk; reflected without amplifying resistance and reframed slips as "information, not failure." Pivoted from "open-heart surgery didn't do it" to Marco's own concern (teeth) rather than arguing. Some sustain talk restated in reflections ("unrealistic and unwelcome") — acceptable but slightly prolonged focus on the negative.

---

**Overall Global Ratings Score:** **4.2/5**

**Summary:** A strong MI session with a guarded, precontemplative client. Key strengths: exceptional direction and autonomy support, accurate empathy with reflections that rolled with resistance, and skillful evocation that moved Marco from "can't see myself changing" to a concrete 15-minute delay commitment with an implementation plan. Areas for improvement: deepen exploration of the emotional meaning of the smoking ritual and dental concern; amplify change talk once elicited; and reduce formulaic checklist-style questioning in favor of more organic, client-led elaboration.
