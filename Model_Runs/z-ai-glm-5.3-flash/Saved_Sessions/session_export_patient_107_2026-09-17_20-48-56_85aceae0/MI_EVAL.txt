### Empathy

**Comments:** Highly accurate, complex reflections — captured the peace/sleep paradox and the shift from "not much of a change" to "manageable"; reflections consistently validated by the patient's responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on coffee/sleep/health throughout; each response advanced the conversation logically from exploration to a concrete commitment ("Saturday: cup three goes"). No topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; asked rather than told, incorporated patient's modifications (weekend-only, cup three and later), redirected expert questions back to the patient's own resources. Plan demonstrably shaped by the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Repeated reinforcement of choice and ownership ("that's completely your call"); patient set start date, scope, and success criteria himself; patient confirmed the plan was his own and therefore acceptable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Every key insight originated from the patient — identifying cup three, designing the experiment, defining success (asleep by 11:30), linking to broader self-efficacy. Questions systematically evoked and deepened his own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned the patient as the expert ("That's you being the expert on what's workable"); counselor's contributions only shaped and amplified what the patient generated — direct evidence of power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Skilled evocation via open questions; language progressed from desire ("I kind of want it to work") to ability ("manageable") to concrete commitment ("Saturday, cup three goes"). Each piece of change talk was amplified.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Sustain talk not reinforced; double-sided reflections and reframing used effectively. Minor point: early sustain talk ("things staying the same seems fine") was acknowledged neutrally rather than actively pivoted — an opportunity for earlier double-sided reflection.
