# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Samuel's honesty and efforts as his own ('That took real effort, and it's yours'), validated doubts without judgment, and received relapse history neutrally with no labeling or moralizing about smoking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, capturing situational cravings, the social meaning of the smoke shed, ambivalence about journaling, and deeper themes like 'You've carried someday for years.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear, consistent focus on tobacco reduction, progressing from journal patterns to triggers, strategies, and a concrete measurable goal (five cigarettes a day) without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned between equal partners; the journal redesign, walking plan, and goals were all self-generated by Samuel, with the counselor limited to reflecting, affirming, and inviting next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Samuel's ownership with 'your ideas, not mine,' never prescribed a plan, and highlighted his shift from assigned journaling to self-designed motivation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from within Samuel via questions like 'what would it mean to you if it works?', eliciting deeply personal drivers (his wife, identity, the front-door moment) with no lecturing or unsolicited advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Samuel as the real expert repeatedly ('you're the real expert here'), and all key decisions and strategies originated from Samuel in response to open invitations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked strong desire, ability, and commitment language ('I'm willing to try it', 'Thursday's coming either way') through questions about meaning, agency, and moments worth looking back on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with validation plus pivots toward Samuel's own solutions, though occasionally the counselor briefly amplified sustain talk; more double-sided reflections or strategic reframes (e.g., on the smoke shed's social pull) could have strengthened this.
