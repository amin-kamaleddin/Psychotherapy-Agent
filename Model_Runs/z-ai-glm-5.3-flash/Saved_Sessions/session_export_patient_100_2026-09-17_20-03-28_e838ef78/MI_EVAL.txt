# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated unconditional positive regard throughout ('there's a lot more to you than one label') and normalized Maya's ambivalence without any judgment despite the referral context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including a textbook double-sided reflection that prompted Maya's first honest disclosure about blacking out. Could have explored deeper the fear beneath her minimization (loneliness, mother's surveillance).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use relative to Maya's goals (college, trust, identity). The focus was somewhat broad at times and no concrete next step was co-created before the session ended.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was genuinely two-way, with the counselor checking in and building summaries from Maya's own words. Could have collaborated more explicitly on summarizing themes and negotiating a joint plan toward the end.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Maya's ownership with no advice imposed; offered choice about what support might look like, and Maya generated her own strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn entirely from Maya through open questions that elicited her own values (college, trust, identity) and strategies. She moved from guarded minimization to self-generated commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Maya as the expert ('This is your space'), invited her perspective at every turn, and let her answers shape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked substantial change talk tied to Maya's values, from 'maybe I'd want to not get that far gone again' to the concrete commitment 'actually deciding before I go.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with Maya's sustain talk and held both sides via a double-sided reflection. Sustain talk like 'normally I'm totally in control' went unaddressed rather than being strategically reframed.
