# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with genuine affirmations of Amina's resilience. Falls short of 5 as warmth is at times brief and formulaic, with little affirmation of her worth beyond task-related strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening, accurately capturing both Amina's hope and disappointment. Empathy thins mid-session when the psychiatrist shifts quickly to drafting texts and problem-solving without deeper exploration of her fears and shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent, consistent focus on securing reliable childcare for employment throughout. The session progresses logically from exploring barriers to commitment to concrete action planning with contingency preparation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina's ideas shape the plan and she co-edits messages, but the overall flow is counselor-led with supplied drafts, timelines, and reply scripts. Amina largely approves or lightly adjusts content rather than generating it herself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly returns decisions to Amina about which options to pursue, when to send texts, and how to practice. Autonomy support is diluted by heavy pre-scripting of message content, which defaults to counselor-authored language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens with evocative questions and elicits Amina's own solutions, but with her confidence already at a 10, misses rich opportunities to deepen her internal motivation. The session moves quickly into advice-giving, so much of the content is counselor-provided rather than evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Amina's input at key moments and offers role-play, but frequently slips into expert/directive mode by authoring nearly all message content. Amina's own competence is under-recognized, with expertise residing more with the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively channels Amina's motivation into action with questions that evoke commitment and planning talk, which Amina supplies. Missed opportunities to amplify her change talk through evocative questions about hopes and how she sustained confidence despite setbacks.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce Amina's doubt that 'everything falls apart,' but also never directly softens it with a double-sided reflection. The pivot to resilience is indirect, and the response quickly moves to offering options rather than helping resolve ambivalence.
