# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations like 'You're doing thoughtful, protective parenting.' Warmth was somewhat brief and practical rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate, but reflective listening faded as the session shifted to rapid information delivery. Emotionally significant statements like 'I feel like a weight's lifted' and her needle anxiety went unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on the target behavior (MMR hesitancy) with minimal drift and concrete next steps established. Focus was maintained more through education than MI-guided movement toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some choices and provided requested materials, but the session was largely counselor-provides, patient-receives. Did not invite Elena's own ideas or negotiate an agenda explicitly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered menus of options and accepted her right to decide ('I'm still not saying yes to everything today' was respected). Falls short of a 5 because ownership was not explicitly reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation came from outside information rather than being drawn from the patient. Almost no evocative questions about her values, hopes, or confidence were asked, and her spontaneous change talk was never developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing through option-offering, but the session became increasingly expert-driven with extensive lists and scripts. Little inquiry into Elena's own expertise about her daughter.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Strong change talk emerged spontaneously ('I want to do right by her') but was never actively evoked or deepened. No elaborating questions or reflections of commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with facts rather than argument, but the primary strategy was counter-information rather than MI technique. No double-sided reflections were used to resolve ambivalence.
