# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with warm handling of Linda's sarcasm, though warmth is somewhat formulaic and personal affirmations are limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections of ambivalence, but deeper emotional content (guilt about bad moods, fear of failure) goes unreflected, with plan-heavy replies replacing reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoke-free indoor air culminating in a concrete commitment, though direction sometimes outran Linda's readiness with quit-oriented suggestions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Plans were delivered as pre-packaged instructions and repeated verbatim rather than co-constructed; Linda's own ideas were incorporated only superficially.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-offering and acceptance of Linda's framing without pressure, but the counselor supplied most plan content rather than reinforcing Linda's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied on providing information and solutions rather than drawing out Linda's own motivations, which emerged despite the counselor; only the confidence question was evocative and even that lacked follow-up.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** An expert-driven, prescriptive stance dominated, which Linda explicitly noticed ('You move fast, don't you?'), with generic prescriptions rather than her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk was elicited but rarely reinforced or deepened; the confidence rating wasn't explored further and her intrinsic reasons were capped by external framing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with, but the counselor repeatedly echoed and validated it ('this is for the kids, not quitting') without double-sided reflections to leverage ambivalence.
