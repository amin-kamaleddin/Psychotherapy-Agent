# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard even during Kieran's most dismissive moments; normalizes withdrawal, affirms persistence, and explicitly disclaims pressure throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, persistent reflective listening that builds on Kieran's exact words and captures his resentment, resignation, and grief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on disengagement, progressively narrowing to a concrete change idea; falls short of 5 due to lack of a summary or explicit next-step commitment before the session ends.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing—Kieran's own suggestion became the session's focal outcome—but the joint exploration is not yet translated into a co-created plan with shared next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced through explicit disclaimers of pressure, no promises of change, and repeated return of choice to the client; no coercion present.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out desire talk from a highly resistant client using his own reasons for change; however, evocation remains at the desire level without systematically eliciting confidence, importance, or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise within Kieran and frames the work as equal; the session's concrete plan came entirely from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilfully evoked first desires via a conditional question and deepened them with follow-ups; commitment and confidence language never fully develops and the session ends before consolidating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles heavy sustain talk by validating without arguing or amplifying; one notable misstep voicing sustain talk directly for the client ('So why bother wanting something?').
