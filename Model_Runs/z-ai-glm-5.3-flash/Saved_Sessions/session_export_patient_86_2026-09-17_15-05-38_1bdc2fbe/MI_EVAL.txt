# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with genuine affirmations suited to Marco's style. Could deepen warmth with more personalized affirmations of his strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Content reflections were consistently accurate, including capturing his core value of self-determination. Emotional layers—fatalism, the guarded morning ritual, semi-retirement boredom—went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus throughout, moving purposefully from stained teeth to a specific, concrete behavioral plan with obstacle planning and clear follow-up commitments. No aimless drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Marco's edits shaping the outcome. Advice menus were sometimes delivered densely before drawing out Marco's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongest domain—control was explicitly and repeatedly handed to Marco, including accepting his NRT refusal without protest. Client directly confirmed feeling he'd chosen rather than been nagged.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Marco's own reasons and solutions, which anchored the plan. Missing deeper development: no scaling questions or exploration of longer-term meaning of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marco was consistently positioned as decision-maker and demonstrably shaped the final plan. Pre-packaged advice menus occasionally undercut the message that expertise resides in the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframed quitting into a two-week experiment, producing concrete commitment and planning. Missing explicit confidence/importance scaling and exploration of why this time might differ.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with or reinforced, a real achievement with a precontemplative client. However, it was tolerated rather than strategically softened—no double-sided reflections or discrepancy development when Marco cited his medical history.
