# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Evelyn's attachment to the pub without shaming her minimization. Warmth felt slightly formulaic, with repeated scripted safety reminders rather than individually attuned responses.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening captured her ambivalence and values (e.g., 'Nice balance: you keep your pub time, reduce risk...'). However, reflections stayed at content level, missing deeper emotional themes like her loneliness and fear after the fall.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on alcohol-related risk throughout, with every exchange tied back to the target behavior. Session concluded with a specific, measurable plan and a follow-up appointment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Evelyn rejecting the diary, choosing the fridge tally, and adding Fridays herself. Occasional lecturing tone in educational content and safety instructions slightly undermined the joint spirit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly reinforced Evelyn's choice, letting her select options and shape the plan, which was entirely self-authored. Occasional slides into advice-giving mode and repeated expert-voiced safety instructions tipped the balance somewhat.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out her own motivations (fear of falling, worry for Mark), and the confidence ruler moved her from 6 to 7. Relied heavily on menus of options rather than classic evocative probes like importance rulers or elaborative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary power sharing — Evelyn authored nearly every plan element (smaller pours, Beryl line, taxi strategy, fridge tally, Friday adjustment). The counselor consistently asked which options felt most doable to her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk through open questions and a confidence ruler, with Evelyn producing substantial commitment language. Missed chances to deepen change talk, such as exploring the fall's meaning or using an importance ruler.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoided arguing with her minimization, using affirming reflections and pivoting toward achievable options. Sometimes moved too quickly to menu options rather than letting her resolve ambivalence, and reflections were neutral rather than strategically double-sided.
