# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, directly countering the patient's expectation of a lecture. Habit and minimization were normalized without disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections tracked ambivalence, the evening ritual, and subtle shifts in commitment language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on evening alcohol use and hypertension, progressing logically to a concrete plan with relapse preparation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan was co-constructed entirely from the patient's own ideas with shared decision points throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was repeatedly affirmed and framed as the patient's own; no persuasive or coercive language used.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within, culminating in the patient voicing his own reasons and self-affirming commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was placed with the client, with questions redirected to his own resources rather than expert answers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evocative questions elicited progressively stronger change talk, reinforced and developed into a concrete self-generated plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and redirected without argument, but not always explicitly reframed; occasional empathy statements gently restated doubt before pivoting to change.
