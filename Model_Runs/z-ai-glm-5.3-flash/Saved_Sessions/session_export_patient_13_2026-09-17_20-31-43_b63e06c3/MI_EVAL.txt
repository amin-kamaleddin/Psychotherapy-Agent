# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizing avoidance and affirming the patient's courage. No judgment or disapproval appears anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both sides of ambivalence and the function of avoidance. Skillfully elevates pivotal statements like 'ignoring it hasn't exactly made me feel better' and 'fear, not fact'.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, progressive focus on seeking medical care throughout every turn. The conversation moves steadily from barriers to a concrete plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with decisions co-constructed and the counselor asking rather than prescribing at every juncture. The patient's own ideas substantially shape the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces client ownership ('This is your pace, your words'), and the plan is entirely authored by the patient. Skills are framed as hers, not the counselor's.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses evocative open questions to draw out the patient's own reasons, resources, and capacity. The patient's closing self-affirmation ('I actually feel proud of myself') demonstrates successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly conveys that expertise resides with the client ('those are smart strategies, and they're yours, not mine'). The patient's contributions shape the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully selects and amplifies the patient's change talk, mirroring her progress and highlighting her own strategies. Her change talk grows steadily throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, using double-sided reflections and reframing avoidance as self-protection. Occasionally lingers slightly in fear-imagery exploration rather than pivoting quickly, keeping it from a 5.
