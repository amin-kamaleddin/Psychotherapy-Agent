### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, including affirming Derrick's capability without criticizing his minimization. Kept below 5 due to formulaic warmth and few individualized affirmations of his honesty about the DUI.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate summaries captured Derrick's ambivalence and structured the role-play to surface his feelings. Reflections sometimes stayed list-like at content level rather than exploring deeper emotional pain around isolation, the breakup, and the 2.8 BAC.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained tight focus on drinking and DUI prevention across every exchange, tying each turn to the license, plan, or barriers. The session built progressively from exploring ambivalence to a concrete, rehearsed action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative: agendas were elicited, plans co-constructed step by step, and the counselor served as a practice partner at Derrick's request. Derrick's own ideas directly shaped the final plan rather than being prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Derrick's choice with options framed rather than directives, letting him set timing and wording. Rapid-fire option menus and practice nudges slightly constrained space for Derrick's own direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from Derrick, with questions and confidence scaling that moved his confidence from 5 to 7. Some opportunities were replaced by counselor-generated suggestions, and Derrick's own deeper meaning was not fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Derrick as the expert on his life, inviting him to choose options, rehearse, and shape the plan substantially. The counselor still supplied most option menus rather than eliciting Derrick's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk with questions that produced Derrick's key change statements, plus repeated confidence scaling that moved him from 5 to 7. Refusal rehearsal and specific planning deepened commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Neutral double-sided reflections and pivoting to the concrete license goal avoided arguing with sustain talk. The 'it was just beer' minimization was never gently reexamined, leaving that rationalization largely unsoftened.
