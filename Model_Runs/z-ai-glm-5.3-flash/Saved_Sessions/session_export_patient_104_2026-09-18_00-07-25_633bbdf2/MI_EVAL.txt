# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, unconditional positive regard with no judgment anywhere in the session. Nadine's self-doubt is normalized and validated ('that's not backwards at all... exactly what a thoughtful mom would do').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, capturing emotional nuance like the 3am exhaustion and the shift from self-blame to recognizing her baby's thriving. Complex reflections consistently land.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus moving from exhaustion and doubt toward concrete next steps (guide, tracking good moments). Not a 5 due to some repetition—the guide-vs-check-in question was asked twice nearly identically—and a somewhat broad target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed session with inclusive language ('let's figure that out together') and Nadine's preferences directly shaping the plan. The interaction operates between equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every decision point is offered as a genuine choice (quick vs. detailed guide, broader snapshot, no-schedule note-taking). The counselor fully accommodates her limits without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocative questions drew meaningful self-reflection ('maybe I'd just feed her, put her back down, and breathe'). Not a 5 because some key moments, like reassuring Nadine the wakings are normal, were delivered as information rather than elicited, and her existing strengths weren't explored in depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nadine is explicitly positioned as the expert ('you're the expert on her; I can bring the what's-typical part'), and her contributions substantially shape the session's direction. Genuine power sharing at the highest level.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation of strong change language, e.g., 'It would mean everything... maybe I'd just feed her, put her back down, and breathe.' Not a 5 because cultivation stayed preparatory—confidence and commitment language toward a defined change goal weren't explicitly deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I'll quit by Friday,' 'my judgment can't be trusted') is consistently met with reframes and pivots toward good signs and realistic limits. Not a 5 because pivots rely mainly on reassurance rather than strategic double-sided reflections that resolve ambivalence.
