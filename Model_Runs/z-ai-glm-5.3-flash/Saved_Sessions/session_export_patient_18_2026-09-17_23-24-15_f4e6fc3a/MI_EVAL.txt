# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates warmth and unconditional regard ('You're not in trouble,' 'Please don't apologize') with no judgment present. Falls short of 5 because affirmations, while genuine, are somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent reflective listening with accurate complex reflections capturing both surface content and emotional subtext (e.g., the weekend 'refilling has a cost attached'). Anya's increasing disclosure and self-correction are strong behavioral evidence of felt empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on weekend recharge, balance, and decision-making connected to the screening context without confrontation. However, alcohol is never explicitly named and the exploration is never linked back to the referral reason, keeping direction implicit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way exchange between equals — the agenda emerges entirely from Anya's answers, and the counselor shares power through transparency (honestly answering her question about the check-in). Client explicitly confirms the collaborative feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary and explicit autonomy support ('nowhere I need this conversation to go,' 'if you got to design it'). The counselor never advises or prescribes; every strategy comes from Anya, and ownership of the change process remains fully with the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits rather than supplies reasons for change, drawing out Anya's own insights ('some things refill me and some just borrow from next week'). All motivation and solutions originate from the client and are then deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly locates expertise within the client ('You know your life better than anyone') and builds each question on her exact words (coin flip, Thursday moment, Priya's fast no). The client visibly drives the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes increasingly concrete change talk — from designing a two-out-of-four weekend balance to owning the decision ('nah, I'm taking this weekend easy'). Reinforces change statements with reflections and metacognitive encouragement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well with double-sided reflections and normalization, and Anya self-corrects without the counselor arguing. Falls short of 5 because the counselor occasionally lets ambivalence statements stand without strategically pivoting back to change language.
