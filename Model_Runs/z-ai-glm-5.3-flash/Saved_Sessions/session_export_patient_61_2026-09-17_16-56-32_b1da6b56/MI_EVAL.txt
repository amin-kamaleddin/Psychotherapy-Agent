# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and explicitly validated Jonathan's limits (e.g., 'That's a fair boundary'). Warmth felt somewhat procedural, lacking deeper individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections of overwhelm and ambivalence were consistently accurate, but stayed surface-level and never explored the emotional meaning beneath feeling overwhelmed and controlled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on one concrete target behavior—three fasting glucose checks with a defined response plan. Every turn moved the plan forward with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered menus and choices, but the response plan, log format, templates, and reminders were counselor-authored with Jonathan mostly in an approving role rather than truly co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy language throughout, honoring choice and boundaries with the bounded three-day experiment. Occasionally moved too quickly into prescriptive mode before Jonathan fully owned the choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal evocation of motivation from within; the counselor passed over Jonathan's direct invitation ('not sure what would make any of this feel worth it'). Motivational statements that emerged were spontaneous, not cultivated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked for input and offered choices, but repeatedly supplied the plan and materials himself. Jonathan shaped logistics, not the substance of the intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged (three checks, meter placement, 'might get my attention') and confidence rose from 5-6 to 6-7. This was largely incidental—the counselor never amplified these statements or asked evocative questions about his own reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently neutralized sustain talk without arguing or expanding tasks, using acceptance and bounded redirection. Missed opportunities for strategic double-sided reflections linking actions to his values.
