# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with affirmations present. However, affirmations are somewhat generic rather than individualized to Leila's specific strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate reflections appear early on, but the session quickly becomes task-focused. Emotionally meaningful statements (e.g., sales-pitch anxiety, 'the old active version of me is coming back') go unacknowledged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on re-establishing exercise from start to finish, with every exchange advancing concrete action. No topic drift at all.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asks permission, seeks input on feasibility, and incorporates Leila's ideas substantially into the plan. The balance tilts, though, when the counselor rapidly supplies plans and scripts without first eliciting Leila's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are consistently framed as Leila's ('You're in charge of the pace'), with no pressure or coercion. Frequent offers to draft materials occasionally edge toward doing the work for her rather than reinforcing her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Leila's motivation is rich and spontaneous, but the counselor does little to elicit or deepen it — never exploring why change matters, past successes, or the meaning of her 'old active self.' The session becomes mostly tool delivery.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens well with shared agenda-setting and permission-seeking, but increasingly shifts into a plan-provider role. Leila's expertise is under-drawn relative to the counselor's supplied tools and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Leila offers abundant change talk and one well-placed confidence scaling question occurs. Yet the counselor misses key opportunities to elaborate on her excitement or deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers are skillfully reframed as problems to solve via collaborative backup planning, never reinforced as reasons against change. Handled more through logistics than reflective softening of ambivalence, keeping it below a 5.
