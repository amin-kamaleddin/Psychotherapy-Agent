# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Ahmed's skepticism and honoring his conditions (weekends off, dust theory). Warmth felt somewhat functional rather than deeply personal, keeping it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captured Ahmed's ambivalence and framing of the plan as a test. However, the emotional meaning of the cigar ritual and family dynamics were never deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior throughout, with every turn advancing the goal: rulers, a two-week experiment, tracking plan, relapse handling, and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was largely co-constructed, with Ahmed shaping specifics (weekends exempt, walk, Tuesday check-in) and negotiated outcome criteria. The early risk summary was somewhat one-way and didactic, though offered as a choice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support: Ahmed chose his own tracking method, replacements, and phrases; weekends and the dust theory were explicitly honored; slips were framed non-punitively as data. No pressure beyond the agreed experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Ahmed via importance and confidence rulers, eliciting his own solutions and reasons (wife accountability, kids' complaints). Could have deepened these by linking his stated values more explicitly to the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Ahmed as the expert on his life, incorporating his choices into the plan and asking what he wanted to add or change. The counselor supplied most factual content in a fairly didactic early block.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers elicited solid importance (7) and confidence (6–7) ratings, plus Ahmed volunteered his own motivation ('peace at home') and made concrete commitments. Missed chances to deepen the change talk after it emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ahmed generated substantial sustain talk (minimization, dust theory). The counselor cleverly converted some of it into a testable experiment, but repeatedly restated the minimization without double-sided reflections to shift the balance toward change.
