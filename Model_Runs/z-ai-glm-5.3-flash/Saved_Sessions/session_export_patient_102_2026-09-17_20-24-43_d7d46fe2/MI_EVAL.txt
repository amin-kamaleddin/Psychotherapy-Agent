# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates respect and unconditional positive regard, never moralizing despite Milan's minimization, and validating his rejection of the 'troubled kid' label. Affirmations, while frequent and genuine, occasionally feel strategically deployed rather than arising from deeper exploration of Milan as a whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Complex, accurate reflections track Milan's ambivalence well, including the emotion beneath his minimization ('That disappointed-mom thing stung too'). Empathy sometimes functions as a setup for the next evocative question rather than resting fully in the emotional moment, and underlying social anxiety isn't fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Crystal-clear focus on weekend alcohol use with zero topic drift despite many opportunities; each exchange advances toward the goal. The session converts vague motivation into a concrete behavioral experiment with defined success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is fully co-constructed from Milan's own ideas ('That's the experiment, right?'), framed as options with Milan as designated decision-maker. The closing 'Deal?' and Milan's reciprocal honesty commitment establish a genuine alliance between equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: 'No big promises needed... it's your life to steer,' with all strategies originating from Milan and imperfection pre-authorized. Meeting adolescent defensiveness with choice rather than confrontation dissolved resistance into commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits rather than installs motivation, culminating in Milan's self-generated insight that blacking out 'kinda defeats the point' of going out. The clinician explicitly reinforces this: 'And you figured that out yourself, just by talking it through.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely locates expertise in Milan ('That's your expertise talking'), and client-generated strategies (eating first, Dejan's trick) became the intervention plan. The opening question subtly presupposes the minister's framing, and some affirmations position the clinician as evaluative authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: skillfully evokes change language progressing from minimal ('pace myself better') to robust commitment ('One weekend, see how it goes. I can do that much') with concrete success criteria. Uses open evocative questions, amplifying reflections, and natural consequence talk to generate client-owned motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully with acceptance before gentle redirection, and the hypothetical reframe ('if the label didn't exist...') elegantly pivots toward the behavior itself. Occasionally allows sustain talk to accumulate before pivoting, as with Milan's 'Everyone has those nights' response.
