# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; explicitly refuses to push abstinence and validates the client's skepticism rather than correcting minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong accurate reflections ('Fridays aren't the same night as Wednesdays'), but reflections sometimes paraphrase rather than deepen into the emotional core—loneliness and self-dislike are touched but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive focus from ambivalence through values, past successes, mechanisms, and a concrete testable plan; questions build purposefully with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal-partner work with 'we' language; client's own ideas (shower, calling Mom, success criteria) drive the plan, and the counselor embraces the client's corrections rather than defending suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: explicitly renounces pressure, frames the plan as a self-defined experiment, and honors pacing concerns about not overloading alongside smoking cessation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly evokes the client's own reasons and solutions with strong guided discovery; however, two turns speak the client's inner experience in first person, placing words in her mouth and blurring the counselor/client boundary.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly locates expertise in the client ('You're the expert on your own evenings'), and client contributions substantively shape the intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation through open questions about desired futures, values, and past successes; produced strong change talk ('no threes—that's where I stop liking myself') and a commitment-adjacent plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Excellent reframing from 'replacing wine' to 'getting low enough that one is enough,' but early questions about obstacles and weaker alternatives gave extended airtime to sustain talk.
