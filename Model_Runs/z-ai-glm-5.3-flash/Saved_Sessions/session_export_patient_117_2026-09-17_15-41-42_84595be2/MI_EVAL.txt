# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout—opens with 'I'm here to listen without judgment' and normalizes struggles without any labeling or disapproval. Even doubts and setbacks are met with acceptance and curiosity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Samuel's experience (e.g., 'stressy work moments still pull you back'). Falls short of 5 because deeper emotional nuances like his fear of 'fooling myself' and shame about giving in weren't explicitly reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent focus on smoking reduction from open to close, with no topic drift. Each turn advances the target, culminating in a concrete, measurable plan and scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Goals emerge from Samuel, and strategies are co-selected, showing genuine power-sharing. Slightly below 5 because the counselor supplies structured options and scripts, tilting somewhat toward clinician-provided content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Advice is always offered with permission, and every choice point returns control to the client. Samuel self-generates the goal, selects Tuesday, and designs his own backup plan with no pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Samuel's own reasons, solutions, and confidence ratings (5→7); the confidence ruler is textbook evocation. Falls short because the strategy menu occasionally shifts to informing, and deeper values-based motivation wasn't explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samuel's expertise is central—his ideas (walk, coffee with Dave, journal trick) form the plan's backbone, and strategies are offered with permission. Not a 5 because several pre-packaged strategies were offered before fully exploring his own solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk via open questions, a confidence ruler, and forward-looking prompts. Client-generated change language is built into a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids dwelling on sustain talk and pivots effectively toward change and confidence scaling. Not a 5 because some validating reflections briefly acknowledge the sustain side without explicitly reframing toward change.
