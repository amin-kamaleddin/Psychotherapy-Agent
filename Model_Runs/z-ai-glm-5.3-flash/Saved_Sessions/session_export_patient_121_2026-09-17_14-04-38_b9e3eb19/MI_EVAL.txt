# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, validating Sofia's anger and affirming her efforts despite her hostile opening. Affirmations were somewhat generic and repeated, and the counselor did not deeply honor the emotional weight of years of being blamed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate, but empathy gave way to instruction-heavy responses as the session progressed. Sofia's vulnerable disclosures (heart pounding, feeling the script 'sounds fake,' loneliness, hurt at unfair blame) were often met with logistics and tips rather than emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session well-focused on the target behavior of explosive/avoidant reactions to peer accusations, with each exchange moving toward a concrete skill. The broader referral concerns (absences, isolation) narrowed, and Sofia's raised 'friend' thread was deferred rather than integrated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent menu-based collaboration, with role-play co-negotiated and Sofia setting its intensity. The counselor supplied nearly all content (scripts, tips, templates) without jointly generating ideas or asking what Sofia thought would work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and check-ins, with Sofia's selections driving the session content. Some responses read as instruction lists delivered at the client, and ownership of progress was not explicitly reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on providing solutions and structured tools rather than eliciting Sofia's own motivations and ideas. Almost no open questions explored her reasons, hopes, or confidence for change; her change talk emerged spontaneously rather than through evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered choices that invited some client input, but the session was largely structured by the counselor's pre-set agenda. Sofia's contributions shaped the session only modestly, and her own problem-solving was not drawn out.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia produced meaningful change talk, aided by the counselor's menus and role-play practice. However, the counselor rarely reflected, amplified, or deepened these moments, and never explicitly elicited confidence, importance, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor did not reinforce Sofia's pessimism and kept momentum toward skills, preventing escalation. However, sustain talk ('nobody believes you anyway,' 'doesn't mean it'll work') was largely bypassed rather than actively softened or reframed toward hope.
