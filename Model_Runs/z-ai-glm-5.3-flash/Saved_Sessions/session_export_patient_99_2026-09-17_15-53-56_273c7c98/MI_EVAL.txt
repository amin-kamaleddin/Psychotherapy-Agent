# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and nonjudgment throughout ('I'm here to listen without judgment,' 'No pressure — your choice'). Matteo's minimizing is received without disapproval, though affirmations were somewhat formulaic and rarely individualized to his specific strengths like his cash-only backup idea.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Matteo's social framing and priorities, with helpful summaries of his plan. However, stacked questions followed many reflections, and the pivotal 'it sounds like a lot' emotional moment passed without a reflection of feeling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent focus: the agenda was negotiated upfront and every exchange tied drinking to Matteo's ankle-healing goals. The session progressed logically from exploration through commitment, confidence, barriers, and a check-in plan with clear momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely between equals — Matteo co-created the plan, set parameters, and shaped the check-in questions. The counselor's question-dense, idea-heavy turns (offering lists of coping tricks) slightly tilted the conversational load away from the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was affirmed repeatedly and authentically ('No pressure — your choice,' 'I'm here either way'). Matteo set his own scope, designed his own strategies, and ended with genuine ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from Matteo — his values, confidence rating, barrier solutions, and success criteria all came from him. The counselor never argued for change; Matteo articulated his own reasons and ended expressing self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly framed expertise as residing with Matteo, who generated his own solutions (cash-only, texting Danny, ordering early). Slightly limited by the counselor supplying dense idea menus, edging toward expert problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation including a confidence ruler, obstacle exploration, and a specific commitment request. Matteo produced strong change talk ('I'll commit to this Wednesday, beer only, no shots,' confidence rising to 8), showing the techniques worked well.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged briefly and neutrally without reinforcement, then pivoted toward Matteo's own goals. A 5 would involve more deliberate double-sided reflections pairing sustain talk with his emerging change talk.
