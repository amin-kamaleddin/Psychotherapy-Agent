# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental, with some affirmations ('brave first step', 'thanks for being honest') that Maya appreciates. However, warmth feels formulaic and feelings like 'I don't get why everyone's so obsessed with me' go unexplored.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening is largely absent; vulnerable disclosures (blackout drinking, 'it's not like I'm out of control') are met with more questions rather than reflections. Responses are consistently question- or advice-focused rather than understanding-focused.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor keeps focus on relevant targets (study habits, alcohol safety) and reaches a concrete plan with follow-up. However, the agenda pivots abruptly, and Maya's redirection away from party safety is simply complied with rather than negotiated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Power is notably uneven — the counselor generates nearly all content (breathing exercise, plan, safety tips, code word) and fires bundled questions in an interview-like manner. Maya receives a plan rather than building one with a partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some autonomy support is present (offering options, letting Maya defer the party-safety plan), but the session is heavily prescriptive with checklists and detailed instructions, yielding low client ownership ('okay, fine', 'no promises it happens perfectly').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation strategies are imposed from outside via checklists, reminders, and external accountability, with no exploration of Maya's values or what change would mean for her. Her self-motivated statements are left undeveloped.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor assumes an expert stance, delivering prescriptive advice and a counselor-generated study plan and safety tips. Maya's contributions are superficially acknowledged but do not meaningfully shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Maya spontaneously offers change talk ('maybe we can talk about party safety', 'not getting in a car with someone who's been drinking isn't crazy'), but it is never reflected, deepened, or reinforced. No open questions elicit her own motivations or values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Repeated sustain talk ('I'm not like an alcoholic', 'everybody does it') is left unaddressed — no double-sided reflections or exploration of ambivalence. Minor credit for never arguing or confronting directly.
