# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with frequent, specific affirmations. Even sustain talk is validated honestly, modeling acceptance of the whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate, including sophisticated complex and double-sided reflections that reorganize Jamal's experience and track subtle shifts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the morning routine with each exchange building progressively from barriers to a concrete action plan. No drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates fully as equals, with all strategies originating from the client and pushback genuinely shaping the session's direction. Tone is one of joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly frames change as the client's own project and offers open-ended choice throughout. Defers immediately to the client's self-knowledge rather than persuading.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key question elicits the client's own motivations, producing a genuine shift from external pressure toward intrinsic motivation. Affirms existing internal resources rather than installing external ones.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise within the client, and every plan element originated from Jamal. The counselor's contributions are limited to reflections and questions that hand authorship to the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk with questions targeting intrinsic motivation, drawing out desire, reasons, ability, and commitment language. Each instance is reinforced and built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying sustain talk, acknowledging it briefly and reframing toward agency, and adapting when strategies risk backfiring. A few reflections momentarily repeat sustain themes (e.g., the 'half-asleep version of you' motif), though always in service of building counter-strategies.
