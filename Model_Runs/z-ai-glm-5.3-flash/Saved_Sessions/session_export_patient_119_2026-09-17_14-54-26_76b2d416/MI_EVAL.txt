# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, normalizing missed doses and never labeling her as irresponsible. However, warmth is somewhat formulaic and there are few explicit affirmations of her strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture her core ambivalence, fear of failure, and guilt theme. Reflections stay mostly at the content level, missing chances to explore the emotional weight of her mother's complications and pattern of fizzling out.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on medication adherence with every turn tied to the target behavior. The session progresses logically from exploration to a specific, measurable commitment and concrete follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed, with the client as the primary architect of the plan—she chose the coffee-maker strategy, set her own success criteria, picked the follow-up time, and added the guilt item to the agenda. The interaction reads as two equal partners problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames change as a tiny, no-pressure experiment and validates her self-set success threshold, directly addressing her fear of failure. Some counselor-dense turns with pre-packaged options reduce the space for her own solutions and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the client through open questions, confidence scaling, and personally meaningful outcome measures. Evocation is concentrated in the planning phase; deeper values exploration earlier would have strengthened it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently seeks permission and lets the client select from options and shape the plan. Rapid-fire multi-question turns occasionally tilt toward an expert-driven flavor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and builds on change talk via goal questions and confidence scaling, eliciting strong intrinsic motivation tied to family and self-efficacy. Does not consistently amplify change talk when it emerges, often pivoting quickly to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses double-sided reflections pairing barriers with motivation; gently softens resistance to the pillbox with low-stigma framing. Asking about potential barriers occasionally extends sustain talk, though framed constructively.
