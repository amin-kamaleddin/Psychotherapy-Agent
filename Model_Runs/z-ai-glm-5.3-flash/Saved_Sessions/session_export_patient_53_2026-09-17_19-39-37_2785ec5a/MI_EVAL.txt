# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard, including warm reframing of her refusal to commit as self-protective strength. Zero judgment about non-attendance or avoidance, creating the safety that allowed deeper disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, complex double-sided reflections consistently landed, as shown by Isabella's progressive disclosure ('you just listened'). Caught subtle meaning, not just content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Steady focus on the target behavior throughout—narrowing to the Tuesday small group and a calendar step—with no topic drift. Every reflection tied back to the engagement goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; the client's ideas shaped the session's direction, including the pact about next week's opening. The psychiatrist explicitly rejected the expert stance and co-constructed all decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy explicitly and repeatedly reinforced; the refusal to commit today was honored without pressure. Client's own feedback ('thanks for not pushing') confirms genuine experienced choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out the client's own reasons and solutions, with motivation visibly growing across the session. Falls short of a 5 because deeper values and meaning around social connection were not evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power sharing at the highest level—the client's requests and ideas genuinely became the plan, and the psychiatrist credited her for building the session herself. Expertise consistently located within the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk, from 'Yes, I guess' to 'maybe doable' and 'I'll look at that calendar.' Commitment language was never deepened toward readiness, confidence, or success—though this respected her stated need not to promise.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement, using double-sided reflections and clever reframes ('hearing the reason without letting it make the decision'). A 5 withheld because some avoidance elaboration was allowed before pivoting, which is defensible but not maximally efficient.
