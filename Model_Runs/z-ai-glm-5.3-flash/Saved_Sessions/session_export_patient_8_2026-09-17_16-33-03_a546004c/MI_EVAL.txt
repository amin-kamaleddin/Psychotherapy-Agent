# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, honoring Amina's 'manage, not quit' stance without criticism; however, affirmations are somewhat formulaic ('proud you're trying' repeated) and lack deep, individualized affirmation of her specific strengths and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers accurate reflections of her ambivalence and doubt, but reflections are brief and repetitive, with limited exploration of the emotional layer (feeling 'trapped,' the sting of the missed deadline, being 'wrecked' in mornings).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing late-night social media use with strong momentum from readiness through concrete planning and follow-up; only minor drift and never loses the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly invites Amina's preferences and integrates her tweaks (wardrobe, water over breathing), visibly shaping the plan; falls short of a 5 because options come from counselor-generated menus rather than joint brainstorming.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong respect for choice and preference, honoring 'nothing about forever' and giving fair warning about setbacks; slightly weakened by repeated prescriptive tips that tilt control of plan details toward the counselor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Amina's own motivation through scaling questions, obstacle planning, and asking what morning evidence would show success; sometimes jumps to supplying tips before evoking her own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Seeks Amina's input and incorporates her contributions, but tends to drive the agenda with pre-prepared option menus each turn, offering little explicit acknowledgment that she is the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and reinforces change talk via readiness/confidence scales and success-marker questions, producing commitment language ('I'd start tonight,' confidence rising to 6); missed chances to deepen her hope about feeling less wrecked.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges Amina's substantial sustain talk neutrally but mostly sidesteps it rather than using double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to actively resolve her ambivalence.
