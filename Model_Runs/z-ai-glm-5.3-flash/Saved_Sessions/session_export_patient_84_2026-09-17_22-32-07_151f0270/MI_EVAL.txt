# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard to a guarded, externally motivated adolescent without judgment. Validates peer loyalty and never sides with the mother or labels the behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening throughout, including complex and double-sided reflections that capture meaning beneath surface statements. Notably captures the underlying longing for closeness with mom and normalizes peer anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on cannabis use and its ripple effects with no topic drift. Each exchange builds progressively from ambivalence toward a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint exploration with no unsolicited advice, psychoeducation, or expert prescription. Questions are open and co-constructive, building directly on the patient's answers.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames everything as the patient's choice, which allowed him to reframe stopping as self-initiated rather than caving to his mother. Reinforces his own words ('my call either way') throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Extracts motivation entirely from within the patient, never imposing risks or benefits. Elicits his own reasons, confidence, and coping solutions, resulting in self-generated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the patient as the expert and transfers ownership of the plan. All plan elements (weekends-only, phone transparency, Dre's trial) came entirely from the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully moves the patient from precontemplation to a concrete, self-generated plan through strategic questions and amplified reflections. A textbook trajectory of change talk cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well using discrepancy and values reflections rather than argument. Minor deduction because a few early reflections briefly restated sustain language before pivoting.
