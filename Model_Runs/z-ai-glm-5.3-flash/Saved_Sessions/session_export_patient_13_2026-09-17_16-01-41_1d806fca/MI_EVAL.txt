# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Anara's fear and affirming her efforts. Warmth occasionally felt slightly procedural, and unconditional regard for ambivalence could be voiced more explicitly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected her core dilemma and validated emotional and practical barriers well. Later reflections gave way to option-listing, and deeper emotional layers (shame, fear) went unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained unwavering focus on the concrete target behavior of contacting a doctor, with every exchange funneling toward it. No drift off-topic, with concrete, patient-paced forward movement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—scripts were modified per Anara's requests and plans were built jointly around her schedule and ideas. However, the counselor generated most options and materials rather than inviting her own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support: explicitly stated 'your choice matters,' offered multiple pathways, and accepted her 'maybe' without pressure. Anara directly thanked the counselor for not pushing too hard.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Anara's own reasons, strengths, and commitment language. Could have explored her deeper intrinsic motivations (health, child, hopes) rather than focusing mostly on task mechanics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly deferred to Anara's judgment about what felt doable, and the plan was co-constructed from her own ideas. The counselor still supplied most actual solutions, which kept power-sharing from being fully equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked progression from 'overwhelming' to 'a real maybe' and a concrete 9am plan with rehearsal. Could have deepened commitment further, e.g., asking what makes this time different.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Neither argued with nor dwelled on her sustain talk, validating briefly and pivoting to what feels doable while honoring her 'not promising anything.' Sustain talk could have been met with strategic double-sided reflections linking her own change reasons against her fears.
