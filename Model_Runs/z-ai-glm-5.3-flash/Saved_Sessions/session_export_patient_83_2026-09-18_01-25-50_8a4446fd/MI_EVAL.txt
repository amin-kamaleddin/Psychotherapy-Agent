# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates the patient's feelings and self-advocacy, affirming her worth and right to understand her care without any judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections like 'that feels unsettling, especially when you're someone who takes your health seriously' demonstrate deep, accurate understanding of the patient's emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on the patient's stated needs — why the medication is needed, how to take it, and side effects — with a smooth path toward her own motivation and plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician frequently offers choices ('Would you like me to start with the why, or the practical stuff?') and asks permission before advising, working with the patient rather than directing her.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient generates her own success markers and adherence strategies (nightstand routine, alarm), with the clinician building on them rather than prescribing solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions draw out the patient's values, motivation ('I know I won't stick with it if I don't understand why'), and her own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The exchange is consistently two-sided; the clinician checks in ('Does that change how the number feels to you?') and lets the patient set the agenda and pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicits and reinforces change talk — reflecting the patient's insight about adherence, her desire to 'get ahead of it,' and her plans for consistency.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ambivalence (fear of muscle pain, 'just a number' skepticism) is acknowledged and gently reframed with information and reflection, never confronted or dismissed.
