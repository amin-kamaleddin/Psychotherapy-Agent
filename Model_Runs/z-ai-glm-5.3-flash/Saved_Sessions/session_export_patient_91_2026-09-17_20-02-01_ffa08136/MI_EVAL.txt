# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and warmth, positioning Marcus as the expert and normalizing early struggles. Affirmations were somewhat brief and formulaic ('I like that') rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including an excellent capture of Marcus's shift from 'huge mountain' to 'something I do in the morning.' Missed opportunity to more fully reflect the emotional weight of his self-criticism pattern.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on blood sugar monitoring with steady progression from hurdle to concrete week-one plan, slip recovery, and follow-up. No topic drift and momentum built toward commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative session framed as joint work ('side by side,' 'adjust anything together'), with Marcus's ideas driving the entire plan and the psychiatrist consulting rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Marcus's ownership ('a real goal, and it's yours'), non-coercive throughout with no imposed advice, and the follow-up framed as collaborative and optional-feeling.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently drew out Marcus's own reasons and strategies, culminating in strong self-motivational statements ('Seven mornings. I can do seven mornings.'). The counselor never argued for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Marcus as the expert and drew out his own solutions; the entire plan (mornings only, phone reminder, wife's involvement) came from him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk, from the phone reminder idea to self-efficacy language, directly strengthening Marcus's commitment by session's end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively pivoted from doubt to forward-looking questions and excellently reframed slips as bounce-back opportunities. Sustain talk about self-doubt could have been more explicitly normalized with double-sided reflection.
