# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with effective reframing of laziness talk and regular affirmations; however, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and tied to plan compliance rather than unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections were present, but the style was dominated by advice-giving and stacked questions; deeper emotional content like fear of failure and months of inaction was often skipped rather than reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary focus on increasing physical activity, progressively building a fully specified plan with days, times, cues, backups, accountability, and a check-in, with no drift across turns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed, with Farid's ideas (shoes by the door, Kamal, rewards) integrated into it; but clinician-initiated tools like the confidence ruler and if-then planning drove structure with limited negotiation of fit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Farid retains ownership of days, strategies, accountability, and check-in format, with options offered rather than prescriptions; occasional advice menus and rhetorical stacked questions slightly pressured his choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine evocation of values, past successes, and self-generated reasons for change, but motivation was frequently crowded out as the counselor immediately supplied expert menus instead of developing Farid's answers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Real power-sharing is evident as Farid's preferences shaped the plan, but the counselor frequently supplied solution menus from an expert position and used multi-question barrages that limited his agenda leading.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation including the confidence ruler moved Farid from a 5 to a 6 and elicited his own levers plus a strong closing commitment; points lost because evocation was often paired with counselor-supplied solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective reframing of laziness talk and the all-or-nothing concern kept focus on change without dwelling on barriers; though reframes sometimes became quick pivots to solutions rather than reflective softening.
