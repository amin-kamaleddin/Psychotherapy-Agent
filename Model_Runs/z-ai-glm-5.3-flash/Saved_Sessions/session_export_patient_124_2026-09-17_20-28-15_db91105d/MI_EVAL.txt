# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout with genuine, specific affirmations. Minimization is met by seeking strengths rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and go beyond surface content to underlying meaning, including ambivalence, family pressure, and social belonging. Sofia repeatedly confirms accuracy by elaborating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on marijuana use across values, discrepancies, and concrete experimentation. The psychiatrist guides momentum without pushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint inquiry with permission-seeking at every turn; the plan is co-constructed entirely from Sofia's own ideas. No expert-driven advice appears.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy affirmed from the opening through the close, framing the plan as an experiment Sofia can't fail. Her boundaries and success criteria remain entirely self-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn out rather than supplied — reasons, doubts, plan, and success criteria all come from Sofia. The clinician explicitly credits her for doing the thinking herself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Sofia ('You know your life better than anyone'); her material shapes the entire plan. Full power-sharing without prescribing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding elicitation and strategic reinforcement of change talk, culminating in Sofia's own commitment language ('One weekend, fully sober'). The psychiatrist credits the shift to Sofia, strengthening ownership.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through reframing and pivoting toward values without arguing. Not a 5 because a few reflections briefly restate sustain framing before pivoting, and the automaticity talk isn't explicitly reframed.
