# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing Lars's disclosures about literacy struggles and shame without disapproval. Affirmations are somewhat sparse and formulaic, missing chances to affirm his courage in admitting embarrassment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate, e.g., capturing Lars's fear of reoffending and the anchor of Donna and the car job. Reflections tend to stay at the content level, missing the emotional weight of his shame and fear of becoming like his father.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on reoffending prevention and education/employment goals, moving purposefully from fears to the concrete barrier of the polytech forms. Momentum is preserved throughout, with each response tied back to the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session operates as a joint effort, with the personal statement genuinely co-constructed using Lars's language and values. The power balance tips slightly when the counselor supplies exact wording for form answers rather than eliciting his own phrasing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is regularly affirmed with Lars deciding which strategies to use and which fields to tackle, with no coercive language. The repeated numbered option menus sometimes convert open choice into picking from counselor-generated lists, with limited emphasis on his ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some meaningful motivation is elicited, but Lars's powerful spontaneous change talk about fatherhood and his future with Donna is not explored or expanded. Counselor-generated suggestion lists sometimes substitute advice for evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Lars's own ideas (phone script, the 'stubborn bolt' metaphor) substantially shape the session, and his expertise is validated. The counselor becomes somewhat directive with exact wording lists and does not explicitly name Lars as the expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is evoked via open questions, goal exploration, and the confidence scaling question, producing rich client motivation. However, the counselor does not deepen or consolidate it—no elaboration after the confidence rating and no strategic summary tying his aspirations together.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about boredom, embarrassment, and shame is acknowledged but redirection to practical solutions is sometimes abrupt. Missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections or reframe his self-reliance into motivation.
