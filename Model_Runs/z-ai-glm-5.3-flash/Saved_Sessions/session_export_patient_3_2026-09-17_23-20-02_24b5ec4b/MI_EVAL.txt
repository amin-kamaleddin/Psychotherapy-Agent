# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard from start to finish, with specific, individualized affirmations ('that's such an Aisha solution'). No judgment anywhere, and judgment is returned to the client when requested.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture both surface content and emotional meaning, repeatedly landing on core feelings like pride, shame, and fear of failure. Aisha expands rather than corrects, indicating high accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session progresses logically from why exercise matters to a concrete, time-bound plan without ever feeling pushed. The closing summary and start-date question consolidate direction well.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every plan element originates with Aisha, with the psychiatrist acting as curious partner rather than expert. The final plan is entirely co-constructed and client-authored.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is actively reinforced, most notably when the psychiatrist declines to answer Aisha's request for validation and returns the judgment to her. Aisha demonstrates internalized ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reasons for change are consistently drawn from Aisha rather than supplied, yielding deep intrinsic motivation ('for me, not for anyone else'). Motivation visibly grows within the client across the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently located within Aisha, and her ideas genuinely shape the session's direction and outcome. This is power-sharing at the highest level.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions evoke rich intrinsic motivation, with change language reflected and amplified throughout. Motivation transitions smoothly into strong commitment language with a concrete start date.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubt about excuses and failure is neither dwelled on nor dismissed — it's reframed as self-knowledge with focus kept on learning from past failures. Sustain talk was minimal, so skill under heavier ambivalence remains untested.
