# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations and normalization of imperfection. Affirmations were somewhat generic and lacked deep, individualized validation of Linda's emotional pain.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections captured Linda's ambivalence well. Reflections sometimes compressed content rather than exploring the emotional core, e.g., her Saturday social anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, unwavering focus on smoking cessation that progressively narrowed into a concrete plan (patch, dishes cue, daughter check-in, 10am follow-up). Each turn advanced the plan coherently.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plans were genuinely co-constructed from Linda's own ideas, with follow-up logistics negotiated around her preferences. Both partners' knowledge visibly shaped the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regular choice-offering and respect for Linda's limits, normalizing imperfection. Some prescriptive suggestions (gum, breathing) leaned toward steering rather than reinforcing ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Confidence scaling and evocative questions drew out genuine change talk and client-generated commitment. Occasionally supplied coping content herself and left self-efficacy doubts under-explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Linda as the decision-maker, offering choices and letting her select steps. Occasionally slipped into an advice-giver role without explicitly naming her as the expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evoked change talk via scaling questions and eliciting past successes, producing commitments like 'the patch before dinner is the one I'll actually commit to.' Some advice-giving reduced reliance on Linda's own strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and gently reframed (three-week success) but not strategically softened. Few double-sided reflections, and her 'six months and-a-day' deferral and relapse rumination went largely unexplored.
