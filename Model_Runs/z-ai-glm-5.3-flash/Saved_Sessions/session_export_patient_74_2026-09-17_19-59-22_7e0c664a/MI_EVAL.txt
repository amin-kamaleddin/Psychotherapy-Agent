# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor conveyed unconditional positive regard through warm, individualized affirmations and explicit non-judgment ('Deal — no lectures, ever'), while proactively exploring what would make returning feel safe. No judgment, labeling, or disapproval appeared anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and emotionally attuned throughout, capturing pivotal images (her daughter at the assembly) and holding ambivalence with a two-sided reflection. The counselor even reflected back Linda's own words to highlight her internal shift.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking and role-modeling from start to finish, with each exchange advancing toward concrete next steps (patch info, crochet, husband conversation, two-week follow-up). A clear closing summary confirmed understanding without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor invited Linda's input before offering direction, co-constructed the support and safety plan around her stated needs, and verified the summary collaboratively. Support took only the form Linda requested.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was actively reinforced ('your pace, your call,' 'no pressure either way'), change was framed as optional and self-owned, and Linda's request for no lectures was honored. Linda explicitly identified the pace-setting as what made change feel less scary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from Linda rather than supplied — her values, ideas, and confidence were elicited through open questions, and her own reasons for change were mirrored and amplified. Her movement from 'no plans to quit' to a possible week-long trial and 'maybe a little for me too' demonstrates effective evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Linda was consistently positioned as the expert on her own change ('You know yourself best,' 'This is your pace, your call'), and her ideas shaped the session's direction. The plan was attributed entirely to her rather than prescribed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked with open questions targeting vision, values, and self-efficacy, and Linda's change statements were selectively reinforced. Her movement from pre-contemplation to a tentative experiment shows the evocation landed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk ('I don't have any plans to quit,' 'I'm not saying I'm ready') was met with acceptance and normalized without debate, then gently redirected toward her vision and own ideas. No argument or confrontation occurred at any point.
