# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental, and affirming throughout, even when the patient joked or expressed ambivalence. Never judged the phone use and maintained unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated precise reflective listening, including a skilled double-sided reflection of the patient's ambivalence ('Both things are true at once'). Mirrored the patient's language and self-deprecating humor back accurately.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, unwavering focus on nighttime social media use while systematically guiding toward change. Each turn built on the last, creating momentum without being pushy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session proceeded as genuine dialogue between equals, with all decisions (phone to roommate, bed by midnight, week-one trial) entirely client-generated. The counselor never took an expert stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the patient's ownership ('You're doing this on your own terms') with no advice imposed. Framed the experiment as fully self-determined.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation emerged from the patient through skillful evocative questions about what would make her want to keep going. Change talk was then amplified by reflecting it back with emphasis.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed expertise as residing with the patient ('You know yourself best'), and every plan element originated from her. Explicitly highlighted the client's authorship of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced change talk, including highlighting the patient's own midnight idea and eliciting success markers and motivation for persistence. This generated concrete change talk ('I could try it this week') and an experiment plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally redirected sustain talk effectively through double-sided reflections, and the patient solved her own obstacles (calling friends, watching with roommate). Minor deduction for occasionally inviting discussion of obstacles, though this was arguably strategic relapse-prevention framing.
