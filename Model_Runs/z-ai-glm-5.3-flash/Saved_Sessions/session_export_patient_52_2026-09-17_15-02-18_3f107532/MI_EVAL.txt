# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and nonjudgment, with affirmations like 'asking for help is a strong step.' Affirmations were somewhat brief and generic, with no explicit normalization of her ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that softened defensiveness, including the childcare gap reflection. Missed deeper exploration of her guilt about presence versus the drink.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing evening drinking despite deflections. Every exchange moved toward a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-constructed; Irina's corrections and preferences (tea ritual, walking out the walk) materially reshaped it. Tone reflected equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced her ownership and accepted her limits ('no promises past that') without pushback. Her closing that it 'doesn't feel like losing anything' reflects autonomy-preserving change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful elicitation of self-generated motivation, including her own coping line about her son. Occasional slips into counselor-provided solutions rather than sustained elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Irina as the expert and let her shape the plan. Options were somewhat counselor-generated without explicit acknowledgment that she knows her own life best.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ruler and planning questions elicited strong change talk and self-authored commitment language. Limited deepening once change talk appeared and no confidence exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk but also rarely used double-sided reflections or reframes. Willpower and loss statements passed without strategic response, though the honesty question was handled well.
