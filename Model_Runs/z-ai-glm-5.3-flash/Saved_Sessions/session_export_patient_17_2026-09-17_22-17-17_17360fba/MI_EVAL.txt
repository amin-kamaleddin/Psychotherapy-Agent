# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; shame and fear of asking for help were met with warmth and validation, with no criticism or disapproval anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that went beyond content to emotional meaning, e.g., 'it hurts that your daughter pulls away' and capturing the fear of asking for help.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on smoking cessation with a logical arc from motivation to a concrete plan and follow-up, with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative session where every plan element originated from the client and the clinician acted as facilitator, co-reviewing findings together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client ownership actively reinforced through invitations like 'at your own pace' and 'something that fits your life'; the client freely chose her first step and timeline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation consistently elicited from within via values-focused questions (daughter, freedom from shame), producing visible deepening of the client's own commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise clearly located within the client, with repeated crediting of her contributions ('You've just laid out a real plan') and her ideas shaping the entire plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk skillfully evoked and reflected back, progressing from desire ('wanting to quit') to commitment and action ('This feels like an actual beginning').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk generally redirected effectively toward strengths, though past failures and difficulty were explored at length before pivoting to change; quicker double-sided reflections could tighten focus.
