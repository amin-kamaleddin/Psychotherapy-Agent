# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard; no judgment of the patient's discouragement or hedging, with dignity consistently affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, deep reflective listening throughout, including a textbook double-sided reflection and reflections tracking emotional core (dignity, invisibility, hesitation).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the target behavior, progressively tightening from 'what's the point' to a specific plan (one call, after breakfast, this week) with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals; the plan was co-constructed entirely from the patient's own ideas and the closing summary invited correction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy reinforced constantly—choice-framing language, respect for limits on commitment, and explicit protection from overcommitment ('That's the whole assignment. Nothing more.').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from the patient, not supplied; every reason for change (wife's happiness, coffee mornings, dignity) came from Elias himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located in the patient ('That's your expertise talking'); every plan element originated from Elias, who recognized this directly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation and deepening of change talk step by step, moving Elias from 'maybe' to a verbal commitment ('yes, I'll make the call') and anchoring personal motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubt was held without amplification and redirected effectively; minor deduct for briefly re-engaging the painful failed-attempt narrative early on and occasionally restating doubt-adjacent material before pivoting.
