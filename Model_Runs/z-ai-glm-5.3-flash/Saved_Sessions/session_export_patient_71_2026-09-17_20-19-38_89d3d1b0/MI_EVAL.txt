# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout, with affirmations like 'There's real wisdom in noticing that' and honoring both sides of ambivalence. No judgment or disapproval even when lapses and self-criticism were described.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, emotionally attuned complex reflections captured both content and feeling nuance. The client repeatedly confirmed being understood ('Yeah, exactly,' 'That's it exactly').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained steady focus on value-congruent yoga attendance while progressing logically from first step to obstacles to success criteria. Strategic guidance without ever feeling directive or rushed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine exchange between equals—all plans, metaphors, and coping strategies originated from the client and shaped the session's direction. The counselor asked rather than told at every juncture.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice was reinforced at every decision point, and her rejection of the social anchor was fully respected. Ownership of both success and setbacks was placed squarely with the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evocative questions drew out rich change talk—desire, commitment, ability, and reasons—tapping the client's values and anticipated future self. No advice or external persuasion appeared anywhere.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was clearly located within the client ('That idea came from you'), and every plan element originated from her own reasoning. The client explicitly recognized this: 'Thank you for not handing me answers.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions targeting commitment, confidence, and values elicited strong, escalating change talk ('I actually want that,' 'Yeah. I can do that'). The counselor deliberately paused on and deepened confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met with validating acknowledgment paired with redirection toward strengths ('hope against history, and both deserve space'). It progressively diminished, transforming into concrete planning.
