# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated nonjudgmental acceptance: 'I'm not here to judge,' affirmed the patient's honesty and self-awareness, and closed warmly with 'Bring whatever the notes say, good or bad.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated highly accurate reflective listening, including double-sided reflections capturing ambivalence and precise attention to emotional shifts ('kinda curious' beside 'not promising anything').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use while addressing the ankle holistically; the session progressed logically from exploration to a concrete two-week experiment and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner—'let's figure it out together'—with the plan fully co-constructed from the patient's own ideas. Patient confirmed the tone ('less preachy than I expected').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforced client ownership throughout ('your data, your call on what it means'), honored the 'no promises' stance, and let the patient decide what the data means.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from within the client via open questions ('What's your own take on your drinking?'), eliciting the patient's own reasons, solutions, and commitment language with no imposed advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned expertise with the client ('you're the one raising it'), and every plan element originated from the patient rather than the counselor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and deepened change talk: elicited the patient's own curiosity, solutions, and commitment ('I'll do it'), then reflected each back to strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never amplified sustain talk; minimization and peer concerns were acknowledged briefly via double-sided reflections and redirected toward curiosity and agency.
