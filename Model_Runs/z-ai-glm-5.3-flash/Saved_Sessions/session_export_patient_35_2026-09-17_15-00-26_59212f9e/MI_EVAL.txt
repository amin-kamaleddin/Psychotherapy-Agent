# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained unconditional positive regard, responding nonjudgmentally when Elena disclosed smoking and anticipated blame. The tone remained consistently warm and supportive even as Elena defended her smoking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captured both content and emotional undercurrents, including exhaustion, guilt, and stigma. The psychiatrist accurately named the stigma barrier before Elena fully articulated it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The ENT referral became a concrete achieved goal, but the primary target of smoking cessation was largely deflected. The session drifted into pediatric navigation without gently re-linking to the smoking behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scripts were genuinely co-constructed, revised per Elena's preferences, and role-played at her request. The psychiatrist functioned more as a resource than a co-thinker on the core smoking issue, limiting mutual problem-solving depth.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena's choices were repeatedly and explicitly supported throughout, and her decision to exclude the smoking mention and defer quitting was fully respected without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation emerged from Elena herself, including her past successful cutback and self-generated idea to ask about exposures. However, the psychiatrist did not strategically develop this change talk through exploration, scaling, or values linking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena was consistently treated as the expert, with options offered rather than prescribed and her script edits incorporated without resistance. The psychiatrist positioned themselves as a helper rather than an authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist created space for Elena's own change talk, including her disclosure of prior successful cutback, and offered autonomy-supportive options. However, change talk was not actively deepened or reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Elena's minimizations, instead reflecting the underlying concern about blame and pivoting to actionable steps. Her choice to remove the smoking mention was honored without re-raising the issue.
