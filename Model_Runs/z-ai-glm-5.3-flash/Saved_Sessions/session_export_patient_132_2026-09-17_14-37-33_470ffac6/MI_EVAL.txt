# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and non-judgmental, with patient expressing feeling unjudged, but affirmations are sparse and generic. Repeated formulaic crisis disclaimers undercut the sense of being fully seen.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections and validation of body-image worry, but reflections are shallow and infrequent. The emotional core — fear of failure and discouragement — is never deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on shifting from diet cycling to sustainable, non-punitive exercise habits, with each turn advancing that goal. Focus occasionally narrows to logistics at the expense of deeper motivational work.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Options are offered and patient preferences shape the plan, but the pattern is largely counselor-as-planner with pre-packaged programs and closed questions. The patient is more an agreeable consumer than a co-author of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Menu-style choices are offered throughout and the decision to scale back to tracking only is respected, with no pressuring or over-promising. Would be stronger with more explicit framing of the plan as fully the patient's to modify or abandon.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies heavily on providing reasons, frameworks, and solutions rather than eliciting the patient's own motivations. Open-ended evocative questions about values, vision, and confidence are largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Patient's input is invited and her request to start with feeling-tracking was honored. However, the overall stance is plan-delivery heavy rather than drawing the plan out of the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient generates meaningful change talk about sustainable, enjoyable approaches, supported by choice-offering and reframing. The counselor does not strategically deepen this talk with open questions about values or success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One good double-sided validation on weight loss, and sustain talk is reasonably redirected to non-weight goals. However, ambivalence is not explored or resolved — later worries about body image are acknowledged briefly and moved past.
