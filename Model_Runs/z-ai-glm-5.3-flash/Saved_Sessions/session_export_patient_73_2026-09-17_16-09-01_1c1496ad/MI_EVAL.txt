# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, supportive tone with affirmations and normalizes failed quit attempts. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and lack deep statements of unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures Linda's ambivalence, including smoking's tie to identity and routine. Reflections often pivot quickly to planning and advice, leaving the emotional core — health fears and past failures — under-explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains unwavering focus on delaying the first morning cigarette, with every turn tied to the plan, barriers, or follow-up. Skilfully steers from exploration into goal-setting, rehearsal, and a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Decisions are largely co-constructed, and the roleplay of Margaret is genuinely collaborative. Still, the counselor's solution menu dominates early on, with Linda mostly selecting among counselor-generated options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices and respects Linda's decision to scale back to the morning delay. Autonomy is partly undermined by the volume of counselor-suggested options and persuasion-adjacent framing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation is elicited from Linda (health, money, her kettle-note idea). Much of the session supplies solutions and scripts rather than evoking her own reasons, strengths, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda's choices are invited and respected, but the counselor frequently assumes the expert role, proposing the delay plan, replacements, and scripts before Linda generates ideas. Power sharing is only partial.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reinforces change talk, strengthening Linda's hope ('maybe this time could be different'). Change talk is mostly channeled into logistics rather than deepened exploration of her reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk without judgment and reframes slips as 'info not failure.' Limited active redirection — no double-sided reflections, and extensive coaching around social pressure keeps focus on barriers.
