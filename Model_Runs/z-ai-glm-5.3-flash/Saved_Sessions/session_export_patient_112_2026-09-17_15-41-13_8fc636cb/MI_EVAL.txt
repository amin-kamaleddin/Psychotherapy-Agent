# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations and normalized ambivalence, but affirmations were brief and generic rather than deeply individualized (e.g., courage of confronting smoker identity never validated).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening capturing both facts and feelings, but stayed at summary level — Rosa's poignant identity-loss statement ('who I'd even be without a cigarette') was never explored in depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, focused progression from ambivalence to concrete steps with real momentum, but pushed slightly past Rosa's pace with the early quit-date proposal, and later turns became repetitive menu-offering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative agenda-setting and co-developed tools, but the counselor tended to draft texts and solutions for Rosa rather than eliciting her own ideas and words first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, explicit ownership support: permission-based advice, menus at every juncture, graceful acceptance of Rosa's rejection of the quit date. Patient explicitly said 'Thanks for not pushing me too hard.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and scaling drew out Rosa's own reasons, but the session shifted into advice-giving mode mid-way — counselor generating solutions while Rosa selected, rather than consistently eliciting her ideas and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently asked permission, offered choices, and pivoted to Rosa's preferences when she pushed back on the quit date, though drafting content for Rosa slightly underutilized her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and the 'confidence up one point' strategy effectively drew change talk ('I know I've got to stop'), but emerging change talk ('less impossible than an hour ago,' 'if she could, maybe...') passed without reinforcement or amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged neutrally and fears reframed, but no double-sided reflections pairing it with change talk, and identity-loss doubts were never strategically softened.
