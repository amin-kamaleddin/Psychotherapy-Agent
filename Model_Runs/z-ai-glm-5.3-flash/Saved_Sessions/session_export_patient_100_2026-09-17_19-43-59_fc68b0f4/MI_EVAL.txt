# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit unconditional positive regard ('You matter to me regardless of what's been reported') delivered at the exact moment defensiveness was likely, with no judgmental language throughout. Exemplary handling of a guarded adolescent in a mandated-adjacent context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening consistently captures content and emotional subtext (loneliness, frustration, guardedness), with strong double-sided reflections Maya repeatedly confirms. Emotionally attuned and accurate throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong focus maintained on weekend drinking tied to Maya's goals (soccer, chem), with each turn advancing toward that focus. However, the session closes without a consolidating summary or exploring Maya's important 'then what?' question about alternative weekend activities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as two equal partners; Maya's ideas (game-week moderation, Jess as ally) genuinely define the intervention. Socratic questioning replaces lecturing, and even confrontation is celebratory rather than corrective.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly and repeatedly reinforced ('Not quitting necessarily. Your call.'), with no imposed goals and full ownership returned to Maya. Discrepancy feedback is framed as questions she answers herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every reason for change comes from Maya's own mouth — never supplied by the counselor — producing authentic self-persuasion ('Maybe there's something there'). Textbook evocation with an adolescent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located within Maya, and her contributions substantively shape the session's direction and plan. Genuine, credited power-sharing rather than token collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is systematically evoked and amplified through goal-eliciting questions and celebratory highlighting of Maya's own words ('did you hear what you just said?'). Maya generates substantial self-directed change talk tied to her values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing and pivots well (reframing 'not quitting' as 'your call'), but some minimizations ('it is what it is,' 'mostly it's fine') receive less immediate redirection. Pivoting is good but not always systematic.
