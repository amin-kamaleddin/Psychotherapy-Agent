# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard throughout ('I'm glad you're here,' 'that's huge,' 'you did brilliant') with no judgment despite sensitive material about violence and alcohol. Affirms Wiremu's worth and effort consistently.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening capturing ambivalence ('You're really torn...') and commitment language. However, the emotional depth around Wiremu's hurt at his aunty's skepticism ('that stings a bit') was passed over in favor of moving to next steps.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol, leaving the drinking environment, and rebuilding family accountability. Every exchange moves purposefully from values exploration to concrete planning with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely an exchange between equals, with Wiremu's ideas (fridge list, catching up with Tristan away from the flat) shaping the plan. Slightly reduced by pre-packaged scripts and checklists rather than co-constructed materials.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers menus of options and lets Wiremu set the pace and sequence ('What feels most doable next?', 'Which do you want to do?'). His ownership ('If I muck this up, that's on me') was not overridden.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within ('What matters most about that to you right now?'), letting Wiremu generate his own reasons. The sceptical-aunty roleplay masterfully evoked sustained commitment in the face of doubt.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Wiremu as the expert ('What feels most doable next?', 'Which small step feels best this week?') and lets his choices shape the session. Slightly less than 5 because most concrete scripts and checklists were supplied rather than drawn from his own words.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies commitment language through questions like 'What would proving you're serious look like?' The rehearsal of the aunty call generated live commitment behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about freedom and Tristan's environment was handled with double-sided reflections and redirected toward his goals without dwelling on barriers. Limited explicit affirmation of the legitimacy of his autonomy concerns before pivoting.
