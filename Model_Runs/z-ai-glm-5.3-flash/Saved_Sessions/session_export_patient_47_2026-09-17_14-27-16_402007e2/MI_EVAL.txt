# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and repeatedly thanks Evelyn for sharing without dismissing her minimizing. Falls short of 5 because warmth feels procedural, with a checklist-like opening and no explicit affirmations of her courage or honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers accurate reflections of content ('it's normal to enjoy a drink') and acknowledges her embarrassment about the fall. However, the session is dominated by information-giving rather than deep reflection on her ambivalence, pub life, or the emotional meaning of her wine.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anchors on the alcohol-sedative interaction and converges on a concrete action, but early safety/medical triage diluted the focus. Alcohol reduction was never established as the working focus; the counselor followed Evelyn's shift to the sleeping tablet rather than steering back to drinking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Goal menus, a shared plan, and an interactive role-play show collaborative elements. But the structure is expert-driven, with the counselor supplying scripts verbatim and directing the plan while Evelyn acts as a compliant recipient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers menus of choice and lets Evelyn select the goal and timing, with no pressure to reduce alcohol. Falls short of 5 due to frequent directive instructions issued without affirming Evelyn's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn primarily through psychoeducation and ready-made solutions rather than from Evelyn herself. Open-ended evocative questions about her worries, values, or confidence are notably absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission-adjacent questions and invites Evelyn's input on goals, but the middle of the session is advice-dense with an expert tone. Evelyn shaped the plan, yet the counselor supplied most of the content and direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged via open questions about goals and was capitalized on with a concrete plan and rehearsal. However, it centered on the sleeping tablet rather than alcohol, and her conviction that 'seven a week isn't a problem' was left largely unexplored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce Evelyn's substantial sustain talk and used some reframing alongside risk information. But the approach relied on psychoeducation rather than MI-consistent techniques like double-sided reflections or developing discrepancy.
