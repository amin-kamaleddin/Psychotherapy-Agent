# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance ('I won't lecture,' 'I'll listen nonjudgmentally'). Validated Elena's efforts and normalized her fear of failure without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured ambivalence, parenting stress, and fear of failure. Reflections occasionally stayed at surface content rather than deepening into the emotional weight of the pregnancy relapse or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking throughout with steady progression: ambivalence exploration, rulers, plan selection, a concrete 3-day log, and a scheduled Thursday follow-up. Each exchange advanced toward a specific achievable step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan was co-constructed through Elena's choices (log over patch, paper over phone, reading aloud over prompts). The counselor solicited her input throughout and treated her as an equal partner with useful knowledge.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice with menu-style options, permission-seeking, and explicit no-commitment framing. Elena's preferences on format, timing, and check-in style were fully honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used open questions and rulers, eliciting Elena's own reasons and growing confidence (3 to 6). Relied heavily on ruler questions and offered a pre-set menu before fully elaborating her motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise placed with the client from the start ('You're in charge of where we start'). Elena's contributions substantially shaped the session, and the entire plan was her construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rulers effectively evoked change talk, with confidence rising from 3 to 6 and commitment language ('I can do this'). Missed opportunities to deepen with elaboration questions after ruler scores (e.g., 'Why a 4 and not a 2?').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never reinforced; minimization and overwhelm were met with validation and redirection to doable steps. Some reflections briefly restated sustain content without double-sided pivots toward her desire to protect her son.
