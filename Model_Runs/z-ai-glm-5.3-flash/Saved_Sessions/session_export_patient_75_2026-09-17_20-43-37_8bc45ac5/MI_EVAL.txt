# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard; affirmations are specific and genuine, and Linda's research/half-adherence are reframed as strengths rather than failings. Only minor note: occasional enthusiasm ('I love that plan') borders on performative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deep, meaning-level reflective listening, including the pivotal reframe 'you're not resisting the inhaler, you're asking for trustworthy information,' which Linda confirms. Reflections rarely parroted; client repeatedly validates accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on inhaler adherence from start to finish, with every exchange advancing toward concrete change despite tangents. Tangents are absorbed and refocused without abrupt redirection.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative 'we' language, co-constructed plans, and Linda's preferences steering the agenda in real time; the counselor even takes a subordinate role in the role-play so Linda can lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are repeatedly returned to Linda ('What feels like the bigger piece?', 'Would you rather take one piece at a time?'), and her pace is explicitly respected. No pressure or persuasion; even the wrap-up hands control back to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and answers are drawn from Linda rather than instilled; her insights and assertive script are fully self-generated. Behavioral rehearsal converts evoked motivation into practiced skill.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit client-as-expert stance ('You know your days better than anyone'); Linda's contributions substantially shape the session and she articulates the collaborative parallel herself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions elicit strong desire, ability, and commitment language ('I need to understand this to stick with it'), which is then reinforced and deepened. Commitment is consolidated through behavioral rehearsal.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged briefly, reframed as information, and redirected effectively, diminishing markedly over the session. Minor deduction: the opening reflection and pre-role-play comment give some early airtime to barrier language.
