# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental with a resistant client; honors preferences on privacy, praise limits, and no nagging. Kept at 4 due to few explicit affirmations of client strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections captured Rowan's frustration and both sides of his ambivalence. Reflections stayed brief, with limited exploration of deeper emotional meaning (autonomy, being monitored).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering focus on the charging behavior across every turn — barriers, benefits, strategies, plan, scaling, and follow-up — with no topic drift despite terse, deflecting responses.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client's input shaped the plan (brushing anchor, sink location, backup alarm) and terms were negotiated fairly. Early menu of suggestions slightly led the agenda before his own ideas were explored.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout — consistently reinforces choice ('You're in control'), accepts self-set alarms and minimal support terms without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the client via benefits, own solutions, confidence ratings, and support preferences. Evocation stayed instrumental (avoiding nagging) without deeper personal values exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed client expertise ('Which of those sounds doable?', 'Which exact place and time?') and accepted corrections without pushing. Early suggestion menu gave a slightly expert-tilted move.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change talk from a resistant client, with confidence moving from 6 to 7 and a self-generated plan. Missed opportunities for deeper values exploration and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk met with reflective, non-argumentative responses and effective double-sided reflections. Could have pivoted more actively toward change language at peak resistance moments.
