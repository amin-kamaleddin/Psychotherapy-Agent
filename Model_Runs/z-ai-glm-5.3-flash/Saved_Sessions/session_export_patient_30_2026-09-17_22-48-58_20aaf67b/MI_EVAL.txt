# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicated unconditional positive regard to a guarded, skeptical client, normalizing his worry about seeming 'difficult' and affirming his honesty without any disapproval of his medication resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were consistently accurate and deep, capturing ambivalence, pride, the need for agency, and self-doubt — e.g., 'part of you feels fine... and part of you takes seriously what your doctor found.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on engagement with cholesterol management, with each exchange building momentum toward a concrete plan (question list, Tuesday appointment, follow-up) and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-created the plan as equals, with David's own ideas (phone notes, wording, conditions) driving it; his closing comment confirms he experienced a non-authoritarian partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the prescription decision as open and validated David's terms; David explicitly recognized this stance ('it's my call, not just me caving') and no pressure appeared anywhere in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited motivation from David himself through open questions about his values, reasons, and solutions; every summary amplified his self-generated movement, and the plan and commitments came from his own voice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned expertise within David by inviting his answers and letting his contributions substantially shape the session, explicitly affirming ownership ('That readiness came from you').

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and strategically highlighted change language, moving David from 'not even certain I need it' to a concrete plan, a return commitment, and a possible prescription fill.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk by pivoting with balanced double-sided reflections; a few early reflections briefly restated sustain talk before pivoting, keeping this just below top marks.
