# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, with some warm affirmations at the end. However, warmth was limited and formulaic, with few individualized affirmations of Irina's strengths (e.g., her work as a single mother).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The opening reflection defused defensiveness effectively. But when Irina shared emotionally meaningful content (guilt, fear of becoming her mother), the psychiatrist jumped to checklists instead of reflecting these feelings back.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed well focused on evening alcohol use and consistently tied interventions to it. Direction came partly through structured checklists rather than collaborative goal clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative moments included asking permission and offering choices. However, the style was often prescriptive, with pre-built plans and scripts delivered with limited input from Irina.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Robust options offered, honoring the 'experiment' framing without pushing abstinence. A 5 would require more explicit reinforcement of her ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist largely supplied solutions rather than drawing out Irina's own motivations and ideas. Her deepest motivations emerged spontaneously but were never actively elicited or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices were offered and permission repeatedly requested. But the substance was expert-driven, with the counselor supplying menus and plans rather than drawing ideas from Irina.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was reinforced when it appeared (mocktail trial, one-glass limit). However, there was little deliberate evocation, and her final expression of hopefulness was not amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effective reframing ('earned a stress-relief ritual') pivoted away from sustain talk. But no double-sided reflections were used, and ambivalence was largely avoided rather than resolved.
