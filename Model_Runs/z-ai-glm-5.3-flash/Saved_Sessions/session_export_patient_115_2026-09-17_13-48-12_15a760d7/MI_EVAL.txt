# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations like 'no judgment' when discussing slips. Warmth was somewhat formulaic and brief, lacking deeper statements of unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but emotional content — nighttime chest fear and stress about his wife's criticism — went unexplored. Most counselor turns pivoted quickly to advice rather than demonstrating understanding of his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on asthma management and smoking reduction with concrete steps each turn. Lost a point for focusing narrowly on logistics without returning to quitting or Samir's insight that stress is the root issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Samir's input shaped specifics (alarm times, cleaning day), and agreement was checked repeatedly. However, the overall plan, tools, and review format were counselor-generated with Samir mostly approving or tweaking.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respected pacing and choices — offering rather than imposing plans and normalizing slips. Much content was directive in form (prewritten plans, instructions) with few explicit statements reinforcing Samir's ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation appeared only from Samir unprompted and was never amplified or used to build commitment language. No evocative questions (importance/confidence rulers, reasons, values) were asked, making this the weakest domain.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited some input and incorporated Samir's suggestions, but largely assumed the expert role, prescribing plans, gum doses, and templates. Samir's expertise surfaced only through his own initiative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerged spontaneously from the client but the counselor made almost no effort to evoke or deepen it. Rich opportunities, like Samir's insight about stress as the real trigger, were met with a tracking template instead of exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and responded neutrally to it, but there was no active softening or strategic redirection. The reduction-based plan essentially accepted sustain talk without helping Samir resolve his ambivalence.
