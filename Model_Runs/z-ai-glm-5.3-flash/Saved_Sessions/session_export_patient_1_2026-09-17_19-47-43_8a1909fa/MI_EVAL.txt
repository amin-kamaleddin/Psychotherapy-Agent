# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; Ahmed's minimizations and defenses were respected rather than challenged, and his skepticism was validated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, nonjudgmental reflections including complex double-sided reflections that captured Ahmed's ambivalence ('part of you hoping it's the dust... both can be true').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Steady focus on the mini-cigars and symptom link from start to finish, progressing from rationalizations to a concrete, actionable plan with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine partnership: Ahmed defined the test length, success criteria, and tracking method, with the clinician's only suggestion offered tentatively and immediately owned by the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and control were repeatedly and explicitly affirmed ('you get to decide what this means'); even the possibility of the test exonerating the cigars was honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All key motivational content originated from Ahmed via evocative questions; he visibly moved from precontemplation toward preparation, designing and committing to his own experiment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise was consistently located within Ahmed ('you know your body and your routine better than anyone'); the plan was entirely his contribution, and the patient explicitly appreciated the non-lecturing stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilfully drew out and reinforced Ahmed's own change language, from 'a little break wouldn't kill me' to commitment ('honest shot — no sneaking one in') and 'maybe we talk again'.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged respectfully and joined with 'AND' rather than amplified. Minor weakness: Ahmed's closing sustain talk ('I'm still not saying they're bad') was left without a gentle pivot or affirmation, though this preserved rapport and autonomy.
