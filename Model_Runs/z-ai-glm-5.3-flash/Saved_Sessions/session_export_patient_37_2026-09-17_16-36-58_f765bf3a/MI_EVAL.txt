# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, warm, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations, but these were somewhat generic rather than individualized, and the emotional weight of Elena's fears was not explicitly acknowledged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate opening reflection and summary, but reflections were sparse and emotional nuances (anxiety, relief, feeling 'less alone') were frequently bypassed in favor of information delivery.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained exceptional focus on vaccine hesitancy throughout, with every turn advancing toward booking the appointment and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited input on format and preferences, but content (pros/cons, scripts, evidence) was largely counselor-generated rather than co-created with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered meaningful process choices and respected pacing, but relied on persuasion via information and rarely affirmed Elena's ownership of the core health decision itself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied on providing information rather than eliciting the client's own motivations; never explored her values, confidence, or reasons for change, and missed commitment talk. Weakest domain.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made genuine efforts to share power and offer choices, but much of the session was didactic, with the counselor assuming the expert role in content delivery.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerged largely from the client rather than being deliberately evoked, and key statements like 'thinking about booking her in' were not explored, deepened, or reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with hesitancy and reflected it neutrally, but relied on direct counter-persuasion through education rather than double-sided reflections or strategic use of her own motivation.
