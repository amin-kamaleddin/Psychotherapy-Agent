# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveys respect and unconditional positive regard, explicitly avoiding labels and affirming the client's worth independent of drinking. Affirmations are warm but occasionally formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including an accurate double-sided reflection and deep emotional reflections that capture the core of Camila's ambivalence. Exploration of the loneliness and social anxiety beneath the drinking was somewhat limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every response steers toward the target of reducing drinking and building concrete steps, with no topic drift. The session progresses logically to a specific, time-bound behavioral experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is a genuine joint exploration built on Camila's own answers and co-constructed solutions. The counselor's polished, leading question chains occasionally shape the path more than true equal partnership would.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly reinforced throughout ('at your own pace, in your own terms'), with strategies offered as options for Camila to select. Camila explicitly leaves with a plan that feels hers rather than imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently draws motivation from within, eliciting Camila's own reasons, resources, and coping strategies without arguing or lecturing. Motivation visibly grows from Camila's own words, ending in intrinsic commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila is positioned as the expert and all solutions are drawn from her, with authorship credited back to her. The counselor's elaborately framed questions occasionally carry the session's direction more than Camila's contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is evoked consistently through questions about goals, doability, and coping, producing rich responses like 'I'll actually tell her this week.' The counselor strategically reinforces autonomy-authored change and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully — the label debate is dissolved without argument, and social fears are acknowledged empathically then redirected. Fears are sometimes reflected at length, slightly amplifying sustain talk before pivoting to change.
