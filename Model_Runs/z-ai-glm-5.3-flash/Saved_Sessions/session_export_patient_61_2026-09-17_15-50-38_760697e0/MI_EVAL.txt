# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone maintained throughout despite client resistance; no labeling or pushing. However, affirmations were somewhat formulaic and validated actions more than the person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening tracked both content and ambivalence well. Reflections were often bundled into multi-question turns, and the father's death disclosure received only brief acknowledgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on medication adherence with a concrete, time-bound plan. The opening turn crammed three questions prematurely, and the diet theme the client raised was dropped without acknowledgment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Plan was genuinely co-constructed with the client's fingerprints throughout. The session retained a question-answer cadence without explicitly negotiating the agenda with Jonathan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary — every choice was framed as the client's, with no pressure or control language anywhere. The client's preference to keep things 'maybe' was respected rather than converted into a commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client-generated motivation emerged strongly (granddaughter reason, confidence ruler, voluntary sugar check). Richest motivational material (father's death, granddaughter) was evoked but not developed before returning to logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client expertise was explicitly honored and most plan elements originated from him. Several turns stacked 2–4 questions at once, subtly reclaiming conversational control.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong change talk elicitation — confidence ruler and values questions produced the granddaughter motivation and measurable commitment. The most potent change talk (granddaughter theme) was closed too quickly rather than deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently contained through reframes and double-sided reflections without argument. The wife reactance theme was handled pragmatically rather than reframed as an asset, and some sustain statements passed without strategic reflection.
