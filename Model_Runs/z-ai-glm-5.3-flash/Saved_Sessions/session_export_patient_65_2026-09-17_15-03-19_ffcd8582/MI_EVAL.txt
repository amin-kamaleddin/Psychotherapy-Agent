# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with validating affirmations, though warmth was somewhat brief and rarely affirmed strengths beyond task performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but sparse and surface-level, missing opportunities to explore Julian's shame, fear of failure, and emotional disclosures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear, organized focus on completing and verifying community service hours with concrete next steps, though direction sometimes tipped into premature action-planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Julian co-edited messages and chose formats, but the counselor often assumed the expert role with pre-written scripts and numbered checklists rather than eliciting his own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered menu-based options that let Julian's choices shape the session, though heavy upfront advice-giving tilted toward directing rather than eliciting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation was largely supplied through checklists and scripts rather than evoked; only the confidence ruler elicited Julian's own reasoning, with no exploration of his values or personal goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered choices and invited input, but the session was largely counselor-driven with prescriptive lists that reduced the sense of shared expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence ruler effectively elicited a concrete commitment and self-generated reasoning, but change talk stayed confined to task compliance without deeper exploration of values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk neutrally without arguing, but missed opportunities to reframe or use double-sided reflections to pivot toward change.
