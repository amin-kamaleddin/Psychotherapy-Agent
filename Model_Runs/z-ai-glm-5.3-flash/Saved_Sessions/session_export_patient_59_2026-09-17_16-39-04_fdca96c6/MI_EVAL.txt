# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone without lecturing, which Jamal explicitly appreciated, and accommodated his preferences (e.g., relabeling the plan). Score limited by sparse, generic affirmations of his strengths and honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently and accurately reflected Jamal's perspective and ambivalence about external motivation. Reflections leaned toward summary paraphrase rather than deeper emotional exploration of feelings about his mom, the arrest, or social context.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on the target behavior and concrete outcomes, culminating in a signed written plan. Direction narrowed quickly to risk management around carrying rather than opening a broader conversation about use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed the plan with Jamal's own details and preferences shaping the outcome. Occasionally slipped into expert mode listing practical steps before Jamal generated ideas, and the early agenda was counselor-framed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission, respected refusals (door note, roleplay), and checked before finalizing the document. Some early turns presented counselor-generated menus rather than reinforcing Jamal's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocative work using open questions and rulers to elicit Jamal's own plan, refusal language, and confidence (6 to 9). Sometimes preempted evocation with advice and did not explore longer-term values beyond hassle avoidance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Solicited and incorporated Jamal's input on plan specifics, but took an advice-heavy role that undercut client expertise. Jamal shaped the details, but not the overall direction of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk from a precontemplative client via the small-step question and rulers, producing his own plan and solutions. Relied heavily on advice-giving mid-session rather than consistently drawing solutions from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with minimization but offered only neutral reflections that sidestepped it. Some suggestions accommodated continued use without developing discrepancy between Jamal's goals and ongoing use; no double-sided reflections or strategic pivots toward change.
