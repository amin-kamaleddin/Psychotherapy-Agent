# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and respect throughout, including honoring Hana's requests about reminder wording without judgment. Falls short of 5 due to occasional pre-packaged praise and premature reassurance slightly outpacing the client's pace.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening capturing her fear, somatic spiral, and ambivalence. Reflections occasionally feel formulaic and the counselor moves to planning before fully exploring years of avoidance and shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of scaling, open questions, and linking her past recording success to self-efficacy. Change talk is rarely deepened after it emerges, with a quick shift to logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids amplifying sustain talk, responding to doubts with normalization and action planning. Misses 5 due to little use of double-sided reflections that reframe sustain talk toward her own values for change.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Hana is treated as the expert, with her requests (texting preference, reminder wording, no early praise) incorporated verbatim into the plan. Genuine, visible power sharing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior from opening to closing, with every exchange tied to enabling the first study session. No topic drift.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces ownership through offering choices and letting Hana shape topic, time, reward, and communication format. Her boundaries are respected without pushback.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is co-constructed, with Hana's modifications shaping the final plan. Early on, the counselor sometimes presents complete plans before inviting input, in a slightly advice-giving register.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reliably draws motivation from within via scaling, contingency questions, and eliciting her own coping ideas. Occasionally supplies strategies rather than eliciting them first, and does not explore the deeper meaning of the certification.
