# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout; validated Sofia's honesty, normalized her fears, and never lectured. Sofia explicitly noted: 'you didn't lecture me like everyone else.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections captured both sides of ambivalence and deeper emotional meaning (e.g., her mother's sacrifices). Reflections repeatedly landed, shown by Sofia's escalating openness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on marijuana use with purposeful movement from ambivalence exploration to values discrepancy to a concrete experiment. Each response advanced the arc without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed plan built entirely from Sofia's input, with consent-seeking framing throughout. The session felt like two equal partners problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced; no advice imposed and Sofia's caveats ('I'm not saying I'm quitting') were honored. Her buy-in ('I actually want to start now') flowed directly from this.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation drawn from within—Sofia identified her own coping strategies, values discrepancy, and success criteria. Textbook evocation producing genuine self-persuasion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positioned Sofia as the expert ('You know yourself best'). The plan—two weeks, music, yoga with her sister—came entirely from her contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic open questions elicited Sofia's own reasons, with explicit reinforcement of change talk. Session culminated in strong commitment language: 'I can do two weeks.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk with effective double-sided reflections and a clever reframe of 'either way I win.' However, the opening question embedded sustain framing ('part of you finds marijuana genuinely helps') and early social-belonging exploration was somewhat extended.
