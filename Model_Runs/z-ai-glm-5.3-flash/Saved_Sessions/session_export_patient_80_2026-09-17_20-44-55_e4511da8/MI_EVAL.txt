# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and unconditional positive regard, e.g., validating the urge to escape as human and explicitly positioning as 'in your corner.' Never judgmental, even with self-doubt or resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, layered reflections including a complex double-sided reflection on safety vs. social connection. Uses Luka's own language and captures subtle content like his desire for quiet from mental noise.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive focus on alcohol and aggression throughout, moving logically from successes to risks to a concrete plan. No drift; every exchange serves the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint enterprise — permission sought before reframing and the plan fully co-constructed from Luka's ideas. Counselor positioned as an ally rather than authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice and ownership repeatedly affirmed ('no promises needed,' 'a plan built by you'). Minor deduction for mild steering and counselor-led enthusiasm toward the gym option.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every turn draws motivation from the client; the gym idea, texting Mia, and the insight about 'settling for alright' all came from Luka. Masterful evocation of the discrepancy between drinking and his values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise explicitly located with the client ('you know your patterns better than anyone') and every plan element originated with Luka. Counselor consistently transfers credit and power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions skillfully evoke desire, ability, and commitment language, which is then reinforced through reflection. Luka built his own plan ('one session,' 'I'll text Mia') rather than being prescribed one.

**Comments:** Sustain talk acknowledged and redirected via double-sided reflections. Slight deduction for directly rebutting the 'too old' concern rather than letting it resolve through reflection.
