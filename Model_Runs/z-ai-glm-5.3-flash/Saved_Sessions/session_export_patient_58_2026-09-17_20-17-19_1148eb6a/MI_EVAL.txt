# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard was maintained throughout, with no judgment about tardiness or phone use. Self-critical remarks were consistently met with affirmations like 'That's real honesty.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate, capturing both surface frustrations and subtle motivational shifts. The psychiatrist consistently mirrored the client's own words, demonstrating careful perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained unwavering focus on punctuality and its drivers, building progressively toward a concrete Tuesday experiment. Every question and reflection advanced the change goal with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was fully collaborative, with the plan emerging from the client's own suggestions (phone placement, showering, Tuesday). The psychiatrist acted as a curious partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was strongly supported by framing the change as a self-designed, low-stakes experiment honoring the client's 'one night' condition. All plan elements came from the client, culminating in ownership: 'this one's mine.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was consistently drawn from within via strategic open questions, eliciting the client's own reasons and intrinsic motivation ('For me, not just her'). The psychiatrist never lectured or argued for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly framed the client as the expert on his own routine, and the session plan visibly came from him. The closing affirmation 'That's not luck, that's you' reinforced client expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was skillfully evoked, tracking the client's shift from external compliance to intrinsic motivation. The session culminated in strong commitment language: 'Tuesday, probably,' 'first time, no snooze,' and 'this one's mine.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged rather than argued with, then redirected toward agency and solutions. The only refinement needed: sustain talk was occasionally restated in reflections without an immediate pivot toward change.
