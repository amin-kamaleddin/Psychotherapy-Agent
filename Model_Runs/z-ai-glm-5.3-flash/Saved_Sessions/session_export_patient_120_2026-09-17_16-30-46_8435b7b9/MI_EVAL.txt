# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with affirmations for Sofia's honesty and courage. Affirmations tended to be brief and generic, missing chances to affirm deeper strengths like her insight that hiding the pregnancy is part of the problem.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurate simple reflections early on, but emotional disclosures (fear of rejection, grief over identity loss, fear of Dani's reaction) were often skipped over in favor of offering tool menus. Deeper reflection of emotional meaning was largely absent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on alcohol abstinence during pregnancy and the social situations that trigger use, with regular check-ins on focus. Direction was slightly mechanical, moving tool to tool, but never wandered from the target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with check-ins before each step and joint role-play, conveying partnership. However, the psychiatrist predominantly generated solutions while Sofia merely selected among them, so ideas were not genuinely co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices without pressure, and Sofia's decisions drove the role-play content. Fell short of top marks because the psychiatrist rarely affirmed Sofia's ownership of the change, sometimes framing success as dependent on the tools rather than her own capacity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Powerful change talk ('done drinking—completely,' 'this baby's kinda forcing me to learn') went undeveloped, with no open questions exploring her values, confidence, or vision as a mother. The session relied on psychoeducation and skills practice rather than drawing out Sofia's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice menus and role-plays conveyed some power-sharing, but the expertise flow was mostly counselor-to-client, with the psychiatrist supplying phrases and scripts. Sofia's own ideas rarely shaped the substance of strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia produced strong commitment language that was never deepened or strategically affirmed, with the psychiatrist pivoting quickly to the next menu of options. Little evocation of confidence talk or consolidation of commitment occurred.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced, but it was often met with problem-solving lists rather than double-sided reflections pivoting toward Sofia's stated motivations. Her recognition that 'this baby's kinda forcing me to learn' was a natural pivot left unused.
