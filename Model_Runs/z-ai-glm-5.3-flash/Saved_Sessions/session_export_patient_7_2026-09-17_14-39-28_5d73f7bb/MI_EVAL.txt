# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental handling of Amina's overt resistance, with no defensiveness. Lacks explicit affirmation of strengths and deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening (e.g., nuanced exploration of 'tired'). Reflections stay surface-level and don't touch the loneliness or hurt beneath her guardedness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No collaboratively identified focus; the session drifts across topics without anchoring to the referral concerns. Even engagement-only sessions could maintain a clearer thread.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice-based framing and negotiated pacing. Options mostly originate from the counselor rather than being co-created with Amina.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength: repeated, explicit reinforcement of Amina's control with genuine choices throughout. No advice imposed or pressure applied; her choices are honored fully.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Questions are mostly closed or menu-based, drawing out little of Amina's own motivations or values. Missed openings, like her frustration with constant prying, were never explored as her own goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine effort to make Amina the decision-maker, but reliance on counselor-generated menus subtly positions the counselor as the source of solutions. Amina's own ideas are never explicitly sought.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is nearly absent and little is done to evoke it in this precontemplative client. No scaling questions, values exploration, or reflection of embryonic desire for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk handled well by reflecting rather than arguing or countering pessimism. Occasionally echoes sustain talk directly without a double-sided pivot toward anything different.
