# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout with no disapproval of the patient's coffee use. Warmth is somewhat formulaic, with few individualized affirmations of his strengths or honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections, but they are brief and quickly followed by option menus or advice. Deeper ambivalence (the 'tiny what if,' fear of losing his routine, 'can't wave it off twice') was left unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays firmly on the coffee/BP topic and progresses toward a concrete action with follow-up. Direction was toward assessment and reassurance rather than the target behavior itself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Frequent check-ins convey collaboration, but ideas flow mostly from counselor to client. Patient tweaks counselor-generated content rather than co-creating plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly validates keeping things as they are, offers options, and states support for whatever he decides. Pre-built option menus and plans subtly steer him into the counselor's framework.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Motivation is assumed to reside in the BP number rather than evoked from the client. Little exploration of his own reasons, values, or confidence; his sparse change talk is quickly diverted to logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inclusive language and choices convey some power sharing, but the counselor supplies plans, scripts, and thresholds. Little acknowledgment that expertise about his habits resides with Nathaniel.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is minimal and largely client-generated; the counselor rarely reflects, amplifies, or asks questions designed to evoke it. The BP check is a clever probe, but the motivational dimension goes undeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is repeatedly accommodated without strategic reframing—endorsing 'keep as is,' approving a smug text, and calling the status quo 'sensible and realistic.' No double-sided reflections linking benefits to risks.
