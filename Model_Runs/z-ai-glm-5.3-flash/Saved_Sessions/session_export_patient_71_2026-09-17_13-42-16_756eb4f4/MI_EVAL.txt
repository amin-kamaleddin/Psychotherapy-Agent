# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm and respectful tone with no judgment, but affirmations are formulaic and moments like Laura's fear of failing again are rushed past rather than met with unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective listening was minimal — rich emotional material (self-compassion struggles, evening vulnerability, fear of failure) went unreflected, with no complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed consistently focused on a concrete, value-congruent behavior with a lapse plan and check-in, though direction came through directive structuring rather than guiding.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Laura co-designed the final plan and chose the check-in interval, yet the default posture was prescriptive, with genuine two-way problem solving only when she asserted preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent choice points were offered and her modifications honored, but advice was pre-packaged before permission and autonomy was rarely affirmed in words.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nearly all ideas came from the clinician; Laura's own reasons (groundedness, self-love, community) surfaced but were never drawn out with open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some power-sharing occurred and Laura's tweaks shaped the plan, but the counselor repeatedly jumped into expert mode with pre-written wordings, prompts, and reminders.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Laura generated substantial change talk spontaneously, but the counselor rarely evoked or deepened it — e.g., the confidence question wasn't followed by 'Why a 6 and not a 3?'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Lapses were gently reframed as 'information, not failure,' but sustain talk was often bypassed rather than resolved, with no double-sided reflections linking ambivalence to values.
