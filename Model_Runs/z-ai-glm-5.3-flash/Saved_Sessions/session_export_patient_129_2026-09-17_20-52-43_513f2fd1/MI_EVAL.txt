# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful throughout, even when Tyler was dismissive. Normalized his frustration, affirmed his honesty, and never labeled his substance use as problematic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening, including skilled complex reflections on privacy invasion and ambivalence. Occasionally stayed at a strategic/cognitive level rather than exploring deeper emotional layers around his mother conflict and peer belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, purposeful arc from forced referral to tentative behavioral intention with no topic drift. Each question built logically on Tyler's responses despite repeated deflection attempts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acted as a collaborative partner with inclusive language and constant invitations of Tyler's perspective. However, agenda and pacing were largely counselor-structured, with no explicit co-creation of session goals or format.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support with repeated explicit statements that the choice was Tyler's, and no directive or pressuring statements. Framed the entire change conversation around Tyler's own agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully elicited Tyler's own values and used them to surface self-generated insight and motivation. Moved a precontemplative, coerced client to self-motivated intentions without lecturing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned Tyler as the expert, crediting him for the session's central 'driver's seat' theme and the change direction. Score reduced because the counselor tightly orchestrated the arc without explicitly negotiating an agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout skill in progressively evoking change talk — from values exploration to Tyler's own commitment and tentative action language. Consistently fed back Tyler's change statements as his own.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided amplifying sustain talk, validated boundaries, and redirected agency back to Tyler. Some reflections gave extended airtime to sustain talk before redirecting rather than pivoting immediately.
