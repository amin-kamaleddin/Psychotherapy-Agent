# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful tone with affirmations and reframing slips as information rather than failure. Some affirmations felt slightly formulaic, and ambivalence/past failed attempts could have been normalized more explicitly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening early on that accurately captured her frustration and the personal meaning of smoking. Deeper emotional moments—like her mortality/motherhood statement—received only brief affirmation rather than meaningful exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation, with every exchange moving toward a concrete, actionable plan (Friday start, smoke-free home, backup strategy, Saturday 8:30 check-in). Direction was exemplary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely equal footing: asked permission before advising and co-constructed the plan around Lucía's ideas (daughter's 'look' system, gum by the wine). Some pre-packaged option lists tilted the session toward the counselor's agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Lucía's choice at every step—start day, backup plan, and check-in terms were all hers, with explicit 'I won't say I told you so' reassurance. Client's own words ('nobody's asked me to pick my own plan before') confirm strong autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from Lucía herself, producing powerful self-motivational statements tied to her daughter. Occasionally supplied solutions (breathing, scripts, reminders) instead of evoking her own coping ideas, and some of her insights went unamplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively positioned Lucía as the expert—asking permission, offering options with 'if any,' and letting her drive all decisions, which she explicitly acknowledged. Pre-structured step lists occasionally organized the session more than her own ideas did.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful evocation—open questions and confidence scaling ('what would raise your 5 to a 7?') produced rich self-generated motivation, including her daughter-focused commitment. Asking her to choose her own start day and strategies deepened commitment language throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolled with resistance non-judgmentally ('no lecture') and reframed predicted failure as useful information rather than amplifying doubt. Could have used more double-sided reflections leveraging her own change talk against sustain talk.
