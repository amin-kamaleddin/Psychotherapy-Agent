# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates consistent, unconditional positive regard with specific, genuine affirmations tied directly to Yasmin's own words. Even her ambivalence and lingering weight concerns are affirmed without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate, including skilled double-sided reflections that capture both sides of ambivalence. Reflections evolve with the client, tracking her shifting language from 'failure' to 'curiosity' to 'living, not preparing.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains steady focus on the target behavior with each exchange building progressively toward change. No drift occurs; every question connects to the client's previous statement and advances the arc from exploring past diets to agreeing on concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Collaboration is explicit and genuine, with real power-sharing where Yasmin's ideas visibly determine the session's content and plan. Her own comments ('don't go easy on me') confirm she experiences the relationship as an equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are consistently framed as Yasmin's own, with the plan self-generated and self-paced. There is no pressure or subtle steering, and her weight-loss question is left entirely to her to explore or not.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every meaningful insight and commitment was drawn out of Yasmin rather than supplied — the clinician offers no advice or psychoeducation, only questions and reflections. Her self-designed success question and closing statement ('I'm doing it to actually live in my life') demonstrate textbook evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located within the client, most powerfully when the planning question is turned back to Yasmin, who then designs her own progress measure. The session's direction and final plan originate from her contributions, not the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically evokes change talk through open questions, producing rich client-generated commitment language — from desire ('I want something sustainable') to commitment ('I'd be willing to try it this week') to ownership ('That feels like mine'). Change talk is also deliberately reflected back and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without judgment, validating her need to measure before pivoting to alternatives. However, the enduring weight-loss wish is acknowledged but never deeply explored or reframed as potentially coexisting with the non-diet approach — a missed opportunity for fuller resolution.
