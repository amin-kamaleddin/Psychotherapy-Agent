# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates unconditional positive regard ('nothing about you or your struggle is too much for me') and stays warm and nonjudgmental even when Maria accuses them of acting 'like a case instead of a person,' validating her anger and fear of abandonment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are precise and emotionally attuned throughout, e.g., 'the pain being everywhere, all the time — that sounds absolutely exhausting.' Reflections consistently land, evidenced by Maria progressively disclosing the plan — a major trust milestone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on suicidal ideation and building alternatives, progressing from validation to concrete micro-steps to direct plan inquiry when safety requires. The final question about the plan shows purposeful direction appropriate to risk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative: 'can we work together, you and me' and plans emerge from Maria's own ideas (shower, aunt) rather than clinician-prescribed interventions. The counselor actively solicits her definition of success.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Maria's ownership ('I'm not asking you to promise anything tonight') and responds to her threat to leave if hospitalized without coercion. Choice and pacing remain explicitly in Maria's hands throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within ('what would actually different look like to you?'), drawing out Maria's own reasons for living. Culminates in her spontaneous 'I don't actually want to say it now' — motivation genuinely evoked, not imposed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly locates expertise in Maria ('chosen by you, not thrown at you'), and her contributions visibly shape the session. Her final trust in revealing the plan demonstrates her input determined the interaction's depth.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk, e.g., 'what does that part know that the pain doesn't?' Maria moves from 'nothing works' to 'I want more of that quiet' and committing to answer her aunt's calls — hope she generated herself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently pivots away from sustain talk using double-sided reflections and reframing despair as self-protective hope. However, redirection is at times gentle rather than strategic; more explicit affirmation paired with directional pivots could strengthen this.
