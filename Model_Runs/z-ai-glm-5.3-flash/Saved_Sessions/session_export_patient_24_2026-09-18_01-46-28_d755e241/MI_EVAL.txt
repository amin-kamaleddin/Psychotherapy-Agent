# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, unconditional positive regard with no judgment of the client's drinking. Ambivalence is normalized and his attachment to the beer ritual is validated without disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both content and emotional nuance, including the hidden fear beneath the client's humor and his evolving stance. The counselor also mirrors his identity language ('runner's mindset').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear, progressive focus on the target behavior with no topic drift, moving deliberately from discrepancy to a concrete experiment and plan. Momentum builds while staying patient-paced rather than pushy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction operates between genuine equals, with the plan fully co-constructed from the client's own ideas. The counselor consistently seeks his input before summarizing rather than offering expert advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is affirmed from the opening through the closing plan, and the plan is framed as the client's own experiment with his own success criteria. The counselor respects his pace rather than pressuring for larger commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn entirely from within the client, who arrives guarded and minimizing but leaves with self-authored change talk and a values-based plan. The counselor's closing summary correctly attributes all motivation to him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly located in the client, whose contributions (dry Friday, girlfriend accountability, swap drink) shape the entire plan. This is textbook power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The discrepancy question about the 'runner you're working to become' produces the client's own recognition of contradiction, and follow-up questions elicit progressively stronger change talk. The session culminates in a fully self-generated plan ('Friday it is'), with each step deliberately reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (minimization, refusal to quit) is consistently acknowledged but never amplified, using double-sided reflections that pivot back to change. It is treated as legitimate ambivalence, and the client's language progressively shifts toward change.
