# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgment toward Alessia's concerns. Acceptance is warm and functional, though it does not fully express deeper unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates adequate reflective listening, but mostly at a cognitive/informational level. Important emotional nuances, such as anxiety about being in medical limbo, are not fully reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is highly focused on the target behavior and follows a clear, logical sequence. There is no topic drift, and each step moves toward a safe, informed medication decision.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by offering choices and co-developing scripts with Alessia. However, the psychiatrist does most of the drafting, making the collaboration somewhat asymmetrical.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms Alessia's right to decide and frames guidance in a non-coercive, safety-based way. Autonomy support is strong, with clear respect for the patient's final authority over treatment choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation is present, but much of the session focuses on information provision and practical problem-solving. The psychiatrist could have more intentionally drawn out Alessia's values, motivations, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions herself as a resource rather than a directive authority and repeatedly checks in with Alessia. The necessary medical expert role slightly limits full power-sharing, but the partnership is strong.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges naturally, but the psychiatrist does not consistently use MI techniques to evoke it. More exploration of Alessia's goals, values, and reasons for change would strengthen this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully validates Alessia's preference to remain on phenelzine without arguing against her position. The response could be more nuanced by explicitly acknowledging the two-sidedness of the decision.
