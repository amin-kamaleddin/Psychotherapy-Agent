# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor shows respect, nonjudgment, and affirmation of the client's struggles and efforts. Positive reframing is frequent, which can feel slightly interpretive rather than fully unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses strong reflections to track the client's distress, shame, and ambivalence about the gambling spiral. Emotions are sometimes met with meaning-making or reframing before being fully sat with.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on reducing gambling-related risk and creating a pause before urges. The work consistently moves toward practical, behavior-level steps without notable drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor builds the plan collaboratively using the client's ideas, such as the drawer, note, cards, and one-shift test. Frequent summarizing and interpreting can reduce the sense of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports choice through open questions and frames the work as optional and self-directed. Some statements attribute permission or motivation to the client before she fully names it herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently draws out the client's own values, concerns, and strategies rather than supplying reasons for change. At times, the counselor connects meanings or values before the client makes those links herself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's input strongly shapes the direction and final plan, from triggers to the Responsible Anya test. The counselor still offers many interpretations that could subtly position them as the meaning-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently and skillfully evokes the client's desires, abilities, needs, and commitments toward reducing gambling. The change talk is specific, frequent, and directly tied to the target behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoids arguing and reframes sustain talk into manageable steps, such as protecting one hour. The appeal of spontaneity and social ease could be validated more explicitly with double-sided reflections.
