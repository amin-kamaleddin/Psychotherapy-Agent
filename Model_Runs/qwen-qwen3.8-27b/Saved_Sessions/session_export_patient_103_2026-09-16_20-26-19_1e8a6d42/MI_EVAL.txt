# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Nabil’s anger and sense of unfairness in a respectful, nonjudgmental way. Acceptance is strong but could include broader affirmations of his worth beyond the immediate problem context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections that capture Nabil’s frustration, ambivalence, and physiological arousal. Some reflections feel somewhat formulaic and do not fully explore deeper shame or helplessness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on appointment delays, uncertainty, anger, and protecting Nabil’s workday. The target could be made more specific by clearly naming the behavior or goal being addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a fully collaborative stance, with Nabil’s ideas shaping the conversation. There is little hierarchy, lecturing, or unilateral problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids prescriptive advice and respects Nabil’s boundaries and agency. The session could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming his freedom to choose or not change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes Nabil’s values around fairness, respect, and control over his schedule. Evocation is somewhat external and could more deeply explore his internal motivation and personal willingness to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Nabil’s perspective and avoids taking an expert role. The session is collaborative but still somewhat therapist-guided rather than fully client-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful change-oriented ideas, such as wanting specific updates and a name and time. He does not consistently amplify or build on Nabil’s change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Nabil’s sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing it. The conversation could better pivot toward change by using more double-sided reflections and focusing on what Nabil can do or want.
