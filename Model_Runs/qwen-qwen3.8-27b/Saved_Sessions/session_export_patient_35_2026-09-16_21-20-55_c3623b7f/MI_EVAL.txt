# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating, but the acceptance becomes somewhat formulaic. It does not fully deepen into explicit unconditional positive regard during Elena’s most emotionally charged statements.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers reasonable perspective-taking and accurate reflections, but the empathy remains surface-level and repetitive. There are missed opportunities to explicitly reflect her fear, fatigue, unfairness, and sense of being alone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays mostly focused on smoking cessation and the relevant work/stress barrier. At times the discussion drifts toward generic coping strategies instead of explicitly connecting them back to smoking-related change talk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a collaborative, open stance and invites Elena’s perspective throughout. However, the repeated focus on finding a “small” or “doable” step makes the work feel somewhat counselor-directed rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently leaves decisions to Elena and avoids coercive language. The repeated emphasis on finding a small step may subtly create an expectation that she should identify something, even when she feels overwhelmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently uses open, client-centered questions to draw out Elena’s own experiences, constraints, and strengths. The evocation remains somewhat surface-level and could better explore her values, confidence, or identity-based reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and avoids imposing an expert-driven plan. The partnership is slightly limited by repeatedly steering toward small, manageable options rather than fully affirming Elena as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are occasional attempts to elicit change talk, and Elena offers some change-relevant statements. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect back, or build on those statements to deepen her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing with or dismissing Elena’s sustain talk and acknowledges the coping function of smoking. However, the responses do not consistently use more sophisticated MI techniques such as double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance to help her move through ambivalence.
