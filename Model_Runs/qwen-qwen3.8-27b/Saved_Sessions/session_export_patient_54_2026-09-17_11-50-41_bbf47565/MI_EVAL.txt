# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, validating Isabella's decisions as wise and her reasoning as coherent. Never moralizes or positions her as a problem; warmth feels genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reflects at the level of feeling and meaning rather than just content, capturing the cultural and emotional significance of wine for the patient. Reflections build progressively and accurately mirror subtle shifts in her emotional state across the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with consistent momentum from ambivalence to planning to commitment. Slightly less purposeful in its arc; could benefit from a more explicit structural summary of progress made.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is fully co-constructed, with the psychiatrist offering options rather than directives and treating Isabella as a partner in deciding the approach. Practical collaboration (e.g., offering to prepare materials) respects her time and effort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Arguably the strongest element of the session, with explicit autonomy language used consistently throughout. Every key decision emerges from Isabella's own thinking; the psychiatrist never suggests, recommends, or prescribes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides an external reason for change; every motivation that emerges is Isabella's own language elicited by skillful questioning. The final question about her work habits is masterful evocation, drawing on her existing identity as someone who 'does things.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Isabella as the expert in her own life, giving her genuine choice in how to proceed at every turn. The power dynamic is clearly equal, shaped entirely by her contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically creates conditions for change talk to emerge and reinforces it when it appears, lowering the bar to make it easier for the patient. Isabella's change talk increases dramatically from abstract values to concrete plans across the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged but never expanded upon, with responses that gently redirect momentum toward change. Never engages with or amplifies the 'it's just a glass' justification or anecdotal safety claims.
