# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Milan's functioning while avoiding moralizing. However, some responses feel scripted and do not fully affirm Milan's feeling of being accused.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist uses strong double-sided reflections and tracks Milan's own standards to capture ambivalence. The reflections sometimes remain repetitive and do not consistently explore the underlying emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on alcohol use and safety but moves among several related topics. A clearer collaborative summary and agreement on one specific target would improve focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist invites Milan's perspective and allows his ideas to shape the conversation. However, the repeated focus on early warning signs and monitoring remains somewhat therapist-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The therapist strongly emphasizes Milan's choice, avoids directive or coercive language, and respects his preference not to track or count weekends. This is a major strength, especially with a defensive adolescent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist elicits Milan's own standards and thresholds rather than imposing external reasons for change. However, evocation remains more superficial, drawing out criteria rather than deeper values, goals, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist clearly positions Milan as the expert and lets his criteria drive much of the conversation. The structured sequence of questions makes the interaction less fully co-constructed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist asks questions that elicit some change-relevant language about missing things Milan cares about. However, this change talk is not consistently amplified or followed up to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist avoids arguing with Milan's sustain talk and reframes the check-in as a fair safety check. The session remains heavily focused on Milan's reasons for not changing without consistent redirection toward change values.
