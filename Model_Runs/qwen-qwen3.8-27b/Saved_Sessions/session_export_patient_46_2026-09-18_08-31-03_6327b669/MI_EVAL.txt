# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful stance and validates Ethan's perspective without reinforcing or dismissing it. Could be elevated with more explicit statements of unconditional positive regard or deeper normalization of ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, consistent, and nuanced reflective listening throughout the session. The psychiatrist precisely tracks Ethan's emotional shifts from defensive to reflective to hopeful and reflects them with deep understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation moves logically from referral concern through ambivalence to consequences, alternatives, and barriers without drifting. Could be slightly sharper in progressively narrowing toward a specific target behavior or concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session demonstrates full collaboration with a genuinely balanced power dynamic, where Ethan's ideas clearly drive the session's progression. The psychiatrist builds on every contribution Ethan makes, treating them as valuable and shaping the exploration together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces Ethan's autonomy, never prescribing or implying he 'should' change, and leaves the decision entirely with him. Language like 'your experience is the expert' and 'That's your idea' firmly centers Ethan as the authority on his own life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never tells Ethan why he should change, instead asking questions that lead him to articulate his own motivations. This results in Ethan generating rich change talk on his own, which represents masterful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist establishes a strong partnership stance from the very first utterance and maintains it throughout with inclusive, egalitarian language. The session feels like a genuine collaborative exploration rather than a clinical interview.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is exceptionally skilled at evoking and amplifying change talk, consistently labeling, reflecting, and bouncing back Ethan's own words to help him see his motivation growing. This is textbook high-level MI evocation of change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk around social awkwardness and peer pressure is validated with empathy before gently redirecting to the change talk that followed. The peer pressure barrier (Jake calling him a baby) is addressed but not fully resolved through deeper discrepancy exploration.
