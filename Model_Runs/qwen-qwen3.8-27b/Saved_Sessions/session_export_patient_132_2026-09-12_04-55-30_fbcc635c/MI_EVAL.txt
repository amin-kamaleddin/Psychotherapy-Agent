# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Yasmin’s ambivalence throughout. Acceptance is strong but not yet deeply individualized or explicitly affirming of her inherent worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the conflict and distress around the scale, dieting, and authenticity. At times, they remain more cognitive than emotional and could more directly name underlying shame, fear, or self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on body dissatisfaction, dieting cycles, and moving toward a more sustainable approach. However, it shifts into problem-solving and plan-building, which narrows broader MI exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Yasmin’s input and uses her preferences to shape the session, including the script and tracking measures. Collaboration is strong, but the clinician still supplies much of the structure and options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, respects hesitation, and avoids pressuring Yasmin to abandon the scale. Autonomy support is good, though the available options remain largely therapist-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open questions invite Yasmin to define her own goals and values. The session moves too quickly into specific suggestions and structured interventions before deeper change talk and confidence are explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Yasmin rather than for her, incorporating her concerns, timelines, and language. The collaboration is strong, though the clinician still leads by providing frameworks and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Yasmin offers useful change talk about enjoying movement, authenticity, and honest self-observation. The psychiatrist does not consistently reinforce, reflect, or expand this language to strengthen motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responds nonjudgmentally to Yasmin’s desire for weight loss and scale feedback. Sustain talk remains prominent, and the session could better use double-sided reflections and values-based exploration.
