### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as a collaborative space and allows Tariq's statements to shape the direction. The partnership is undermined by the verbatim repetition and the failure to repair the rupture when Tariq calls it out.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reinforces meaningful change talk with well-timed affirmations of Tariq's self-awareness and strength. Could have pushed further by exploring confidence levels or what 'keeping it together' looks like concretely in practice.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Neither amplifies nor sufficiently dissolves Tariq's sustain talk about the system being unfair and staff targeting him. Validates frustration without using double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to reduce the power of the 'system is unfair' narrative over his behavior.
