# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, affirming, and nonjudgmental, with multiple statements validating Marco’s effort. However, praise is somewhat generic and the final concern about boredom/habit is not fully accepted, limiting warmth to a 4.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture frustration and practical progress, but emotionally meaningful moments around juice, disappointment, fear of relapse, and habit are not deeply reflected. A 5 would require more nuanced exploration of ambivalence and emotional impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is strongly focused on reducing juice intake to protect weight progress, moving clearly from problem identification to plan, barriers, and follow-up. This purposeful focus is the session’s strongest strength.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and builds the final plan from Marco’s own ideas. However, occasional expert suggestions, such as the wife script and urge sequence, reduce full co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marco is frequently offered choices, preferences, and a noncoercive stance, which supports his agency. Yet direct instructions for the conversation and coping plan feel more advisory than autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling and implementation questions effectively elicit Marco’s own strong change talk and commitment. The psychiatrist sometimes shifts from evocation to advice, limiting a 5.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marco’s perspective clearly shapes the session, including target, plan, and follow-up. At times, however, the psychiatrist supplies solutions rather than fully inviting Marco to generate and refine his own strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions, readiness/confidence scaling, and implementation prompts draw out clear commitment and concrete change talk. To reach 5, the psychiatrist would deepen questions about Marco’s values, ability, need, and confidence without advising.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Marco’s love of juice or perceived health benefits, and incorporates a small splash flexibly. The residual ambivalence and final boredom/habit worry, however, are not explicitly reflected or softened.
