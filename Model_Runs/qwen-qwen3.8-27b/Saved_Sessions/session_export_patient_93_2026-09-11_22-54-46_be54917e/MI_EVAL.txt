# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, avoids judgment, and offers supportive statements. The stance remains more clinical and task-oriented than deeply affirming or warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes some effort to understand Margaret’s perspective and practical concerns. Empathic reflections stay fairly surface-level and miss deeper ambivalence, independence, and social-life themes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on reducing alcohol use and fall-related risk. The main weakness is that direction is strongly medical/behavioral rather than motivational.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Margaret is invited to choose options and shapes the final plan. However, the psychiatrist remains the main source of structure, advice, and safety instructions, limiting true collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options, respects Margaret’s choices, and builds the plan around her preferences. Detailed safety instructions and a clinician-driven structure slightly reduce the autonomy-supportive tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some small commitments and practical steps. Deeper evocation of Margaret’s own values, independence, and internal reasons for change is limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and uses Margaret’s preferences in the final plan. The session remains mainly clinician-led, with the psychiatrist driving structure, safety concerns, and recaps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Margaret produces several change-oriented statements and practical commitments. The psychiatrist supports these but does not consistently amplify, reflect, or deepen the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepts many of Margaret’s sustain talk concerns and adjusts the plan. The responses are practical but not especially skillful in using double-sided reflections or strategic pivots.
