# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains consistent, nonjudgmental positive regard and respects Maria’s skepticism without arguing. He honors her boundary about the inhaler while still validating her concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** He uses strong reflective listening to understand her time pressure, fear, and need for evidence. However, the reflections sometimes stay practical and do not fully capture the deeper emotional experience behind her resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on asthma management, inhaler concerns, heart safety, and documentation. It could be stronger by more consistently building toward a concrete next step in change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria’s input meaningfully shapes the session, especially in how her heart-safety concern is documented. The psychiatrist still controls much of the agenda, so collaboration is strong but not fully client-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** He consistently supports Maria’s decision-making without pressure, guilt, or coercion. He respects her refusal of the inhaler while keeping the door open for future discussion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist mainly evokes Maria’s own values, concerns, and priorities rather than providing reasons for change. It would be stronger if he more deeply evoked her confidence, ability, and reasons for considering change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Maria as an important source of expertise about her life and preferences. The session is generally collaborative, though it remains somewhat clinician-led in agenda and structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** He elicits some relevant change talk, but does not consistently amplify or deepen it. More use of reflective amplification and DARN-CAT questions could strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Maria’s sustain talk and acknowledges its validity. He does not consistently use that sustain talk as a bridge toward change talk or alternative next steps.
