# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist never pathologizes the self-harm or expresses judgment, instead framing the behavior's function as protective and validating without condition.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture both surface content and deeper emotional architecture, building progressively in sophistication across the session (e.g., naming the gap between desire and perceived possibility).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a coherent, logical progression through the client's experience with appropriate focus for the precontemplation stage, though could benefit from slightly more behavioral specificity (e.g., exploring typical weekly patterns).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structured as genuine collaboration where the client's contributions directly shape direction; the psychiatrist follows interruptions and uses the client's own images as anchors rather than following a pre-planned path.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces ownership and freedom to choose ('you don't have to finish that thought'), never prescribes alternatives, and the closing intervention invites the client to make her own meaning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change from within through reflective questions, contradiction highlighting, and the client's own images, though the technique repertoire could be broadened with scaling questions, values exploration, or past-exception elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the authority on her own experience using curiosity-based questions; the psychiatrist never interprets, diagnoses, or assumes the expert role at any point.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through ambivalence reflections and subtle evocative questions, but could push further to elicit more structured DARN-CAT language, particularly ability and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every instance of sustain talk is met with full validation followed by a gentle opening—using protective reframes and highlighting internal contradictions rather than arguing or minimizing.
