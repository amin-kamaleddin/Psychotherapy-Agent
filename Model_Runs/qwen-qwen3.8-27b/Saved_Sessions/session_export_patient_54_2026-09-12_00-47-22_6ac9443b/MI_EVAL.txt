# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental tone, but often procedural and directive. Medical abstinence messaging can feel pressuring rather than deeply accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are accurate reflections of pressure and cornered feelings, but they remain surface-level. Deeper ambivalence about family identity, autonomy, and parenting values is missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use in pregnancy, risk, OB questions, and partner communication. It occasionally narrows into information delivery rather than a client-centered change path.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative moments appear when refining OB questions and partner language. The session remains uneven because the clinician supplies much of the content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Autonomy is acknowledged through choices and avoiding forced role-play. However, repeated abstinence advice and directive suggestions weaken consistency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation occurs through questions about worry, choices, and feelings. The session relies too much on external information and suggestions rather than Isabella’s own reasons and motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks permission, offers options, and adapts language to Isabella’s preference. The interaction is still often expert-driven, limiting true equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks about worry and harm, but does not strongly build on Isabella’s own values. Intrinsic motivation around being a good parent and autonomous choice is underused.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about family tradition, enjoyment, and not being ready is prominent but not effectively softened. Direct medical counters risk increasing defensiveness rather than exploring ambivalence.
