# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming tone throughout the session with multiple instances of unconditional positive regard. The warmth occasionally feels slightly performative rather than deeply natural, preventing a full 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and consistent empathic understanding with sophisticated complex reflections that capture emotional nuance and underlying meaning. The psychiatrist consistently reflects not just content but feeling and meaning, demonstrating deep perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (increasing physical activity) with a logical progression from contemplation toward preparation and commitment. No drift into unrelated topics or vague generalities at any point.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed with Camila bringing content and the psychiatrist bringing structure through reflective questions and affirmations. The interaction feels like two equal partners working together on a shared goal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support with the opening statement establishing no pushed agenda and consistent use of open questions that invite Camila to generate her own solutions. The closing reinforces that this is HER plan, not the psychiatrist's prescription.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation through open, strategic questions that draw out Camila's own motivations, ideas, and plans rather than providing information or advice. The entire plan is evoked from Camila, not provided by the psychiatrist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Camila as the expert in her own life, never positioning herself as having the answers or as an authority on exercise. The session is shaped entirely by Camila's contributions with the psychiatrist facilitating rather than directing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions strategically designed to elicit and deepen change language across desire, ability, need, and commitment dimensions. The psychiatrist skillfully reinforces and amplifies Camila's change statements, progressively moving her toward concrete commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Camila's concerns well without arguing or dismissing, reframing them positively (e.g., 'protective wisdom,' 'the good kind of nervous'). Could be slightly more deliberate with double-sided reflections that acknowledge both the concern and the confidence simultaneously.
