# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with clear affirmations and explicit validation of Jason's autonomy. Warmth is somewhat measured and professional rather than deeply personal or vulnerably connected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening with accurate complex reflections of Jason's dual experiences. However, reflections sometimes land at the cognitive/content level rather than exploring the underlying fear or vulnerability beneath his deflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear session arc from frustration through values to practical implications, with consistent focus on the target behavior. Could have been tighter in redirecting from repeated themes and would benefit from a mid-session summary to maintain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed as a joint exploration with collaborative language and no evaluative or expert-driven stance. The dynamic remains somewhat interviewer-led in structure, with the psychiatrist consistently asking questions rather than co-constructing meaning or inviting Jason to weigh in on her perspectives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support throughout; never imposes goals, uses directive language, or pressures acknowledgment of a problem. Consistently treats Jason as the author of his own change process, asking him to define his own goals in his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire session is structured to elicit Jason's own motivations, values, and goals without imposing external reasons for change. Jason spontaneously generates multiple forms of change talk (desire, ability, need, values) without being pushed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and consistently invites Jason's perspective while avoiding lecture or imposed frameworks. The partnership is somewhat one-directional, lacking explicit agenda negotiation or moments where the psychiatrist defers to Jason's expertise in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies multiple forms of change talk through strategic questions and reflective feedback. Could have more explicitly summarized the cumulative weight of change talk and elicited confidence or commitment language to consolidate gains.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively rolls with resistance and validates Jason's right to maintain his lifestyle without triggering the righting reflex. Could have used more double-sided reflections to gently pivot sustain talk toward change rather than simply validating the minimization.
