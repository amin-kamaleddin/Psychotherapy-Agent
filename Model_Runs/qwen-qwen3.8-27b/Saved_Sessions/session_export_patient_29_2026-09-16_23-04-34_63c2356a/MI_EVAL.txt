# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming, validating the client's ambivalence, stress, and practical concerns. Affirmations are sometimes lightly evaluative and could be more explicitly unconditional and less performance-focused.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client's ambivalence, emotional meaning, and need for control and reliability. The psychiatrist tracks content and underlying feelings with strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on smoking cessation, cravings, stress-related triggers, medication considerations, and coping plans. All discussions remain directly tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and treats the client as an active problem-solver. The client's own coping plan becomes the central product of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy through open-ended questions and avoids direct pressure, but could more explicitly state the client's full freedom to choose. Some suggestions have a mild directive edge that could be softened.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the client's motivations, concerns, and self-generated coping plan. Deeper values, identity, and confidence-based change talk could be evoked more strategically.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a strong partnership stance and builds on the client's own ideas and bodily knowledge. More explicit power-sharing and agenda negotiation would strengthen the collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions effectively elicit desire, need, and ability to change, leading to a concrete self-management protocol. Change talk could be further amplified with targeted summaries, importance/confidence questions, or value-based elaboration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing or pressuring and is redirected into practical planning. More skillful use of double-sided reflections or balanced pivots could better transform sustain talk into change talk.
