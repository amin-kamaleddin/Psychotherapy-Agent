# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance. However, the acceptance remains somewhat general and professional rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows strong empathic accuracy and perspective-taking through reflective listening. However, the repeated shift toward practical problem-solving limits deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the client's most urgent concern: unreliable childcare. The psychiatrist effectively narrows broad overwhelm into a concrete, manageable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist fosters a collaborative stance by inviting client input and adapting to her preferences. However, the interaction becomes less balanced when the psychiatrist supplies very specific scripts and structured options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports the client's sense of choice and avoids pressuring decisions. Yet, the repeated provision of specific scripts subtly reduces open-ended autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes some effort to evoke motivation by asking about the client's priorities and immediate needs. However, the session relies more on external problem-solving than on eliciting internal motivation and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and respects the client's preferences throughout the session. However, the interaction shifts toward a clinician-led, problem-solving role when highly specific scripts and dollar amounts are offered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change-related language from the client, such as a concrete intention to call her neighbor. However, there are missed opportunities to consistently reinforce and deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist appropriately avoids reinforcing the client's barriers and normalizes her hesitation. However, the responses remain primarily problem-solving oriented rather than strategically redirecting sustain talk toward change.
