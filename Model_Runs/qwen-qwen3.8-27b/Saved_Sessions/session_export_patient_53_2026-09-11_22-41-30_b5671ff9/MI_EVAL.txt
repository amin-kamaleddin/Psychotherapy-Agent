# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, repeatedly validating Isabella’s distress and uncertainty. Acceptance is slightly limited by a formulaic, task-focused reassurance style.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects some feelings and physical sensations but often stays at a surface level. Empathy is reduced by quick shifts to coping plans rather than deeper exploratory reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains generally anchored to the target behavior of leaving and using the mailbox or landing. Direction is weakened by repeated safety checks and practical planning that pull from the MI process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor involves Isabella in selecting time, backup steps, and text length, which shows some collaboration. Collaboration is uneven because the counselor supplies most of the plans, scripts, and coping content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently uses invitations, accepts uncertainty, and avoids pressure, supporting Isabella’s sense of choice. Autonomy support is somewhat reduced by repeated commitment-enhancing plans and prescriptive advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks some open and readiness questions but quickly moves to advice and planning. Evocation is weak because the client’s values, hopes, and personal reasons for change are not deeply drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks for input and incorporates Isabella’s preferences, showing a collaborative tone. Partnership is limited by a counselor-led agenda and brief, passive client participation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client offers some statements of desire to be less alone and willingness to try, but the counselor does not consistently amplify or expand them. Change talk cultivation is low due to rapid shifts to readiness ratings and practical steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor normalizes avoidance and staying home without arguing or shaming, which helps soften resistance. Softening is limited because sustain talk is not consistently pivoted toward ambivalence resolution or change.
