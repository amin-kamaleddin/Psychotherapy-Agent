# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental tone with supportive affirmations. Acceptance could be more consistently person-centered when Daniela expresses worry, shame, or uncertainty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are accurate early reflections of Daniela’s stressors and emotional states, but empathy becomes thinner as the session shifts into practical script-building. Deeper reflections of ambivalence, fear, and emotional meaning are missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has clear and coherent direction, moving logically from identifying childcare stress to a concrete plan. It would be stronger with more consolidation of the final plan and explicit connection to Daniela’s smoking and stress-reduction goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniela’s ideas directly shape the final plan, including the neighbor option, trade, notice time, and response wording. The collaboration is practical and useful but remains somewhat task-focused rather than fully MI-process-centered.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently offers choices and invites customization without pressuring Daniela to send the message. Autonomy could be strengthened by more consistently eliciting Daniela’s own wording and affirming her right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses some evocative questions to help Daniela identify priorities and practical details. However, evocation remains mostly logistical rather than exploring values, hopes, fears, confidence, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a collaborative stance and invites Daniela’s input throughout the session. The partnership is slightly limited by the counselor’s role in generating much of the script and problem-solving content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor makes only limited efforts to draw out, amplify, or deepen Daniela’s change talk. More evocative questions about her goals, confidence, and what change would mean would strengthen this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk and barriers are managed supportively without reinforcing them. More MI-consistent softening techniques, such as double-sided reflections or pivoting hopelessness toward alternatives, would improve this area.
