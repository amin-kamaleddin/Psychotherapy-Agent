# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive, especially when affirming Amina's privacy and accepting correction. The warmth is somewhat formulaic and does not fully affirm her fear, boundary-setting, or experience of being forced to come.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are generally accurate but remain surface-level and repetitive. The psychiatrist does not adequately explore deeper meanings such as mistrust, fear, shame, or need for control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session lacks a clear, consistent focus on the referral concern. It remains centered on logistical choices rather than progressing toward a collaborative goal or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and offers options, but Amina does not meaningfully shape the session. There is little evidence of co-constructing a shared plan or agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports choice, accepts refusals, and stops when corrected about talking. Repeatedly offering menus after Amina says she is not picking can create subtle pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocative questions are limited and early, then the conversation shifts to external options rather than drawing out Amina's own motivations. There is no effective use of scaling or evocative reflections.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites preferences and responds well to correction, but the agenda remains largely therapist-directed. Amina's own perspective does not actively guide the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are only weak efforts to invite change-related language. The psychiatrist does not develop DARN-C change talk or elicit the client's own reasons for engagement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and accepts refusal without confrontation. However, sustain talk is tolerated but not strategically redirected toward a possible direction of change.
