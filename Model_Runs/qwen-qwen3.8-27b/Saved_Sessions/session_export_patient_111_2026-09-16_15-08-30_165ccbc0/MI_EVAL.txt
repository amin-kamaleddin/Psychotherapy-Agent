# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating toward the patient’s resistance and current stage. It could be stronger with warmer, more individualized affirmation of the patient’s worth beyond process.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects the patient’s ambivalence, autonomy, and key concerns, including wife’s worry and personal annoyance. It could deepen by exploring underlying fears, burden, and self-respect more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking, health, and relational impact with a logical conversational progression. It would be stronger if the session more clearly narrowed toward a specific target behavior or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites patient input and uses inclusive, shared-responsibility language, but still sets much of the agenda through structured questions. More open co-construction of priorities would be needed for a top score.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly affirms the patient’s right to decide and avoids pressure, advice, or persuasion throughout. This is a clear and consistent strength of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits personal values and an internal pull rather than providing external reasons for change. It could be stronger by further deepening and consolidating the patient’s own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert on his body, life, and personal markers, allowing his concerns to shape the session. Slight therapist-directed structure prevents a perfect score.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits meaningful change talk, especially about not becoming a burden and noticing personal health markers. It could be strengthened with more reflection, scaling, or summarizing to increase ownership.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles resistance skillfully without arguing, while validating the patient’s current position. It effectively redirects attention toward personal values and markers, making this a standout strength.
