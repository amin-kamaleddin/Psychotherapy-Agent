# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance. Oliver’s guilt, fear, and ambivalence are normalized without shaming or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The reflections are accurate, nuanced, and capture both the content and emotional meaning of Oliver’s ambivalence. Minor attention to humor would strengthen the empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on assertiveness and boundary setting. The conversation moves clearly from ambivalence to concrete action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the conversation with Oliver rather than prescribing what he should say. Oliver’s ideas directly shape the wording, timing, and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist supports Oliver’s sense of choice and avoids imposing pressure. The main gap is not explicitly emphasizing his right to choose or keeping some questions fully open.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Oliver’s own values, language, and motivations. Some interpretive frames or multiple-choice prompts slightly reduce the purity of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist repeatedly invites Oliver’s perspective and allows his ideas to shape the session. Explicit power-sharing and agenda negotiation are somewhat limited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist encourages Oliver to generate assertive language and reinforces emerging change talk. Systematic exploration of confidence, readiness, and commitment is less developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully acknowledges guilt and fear without reinforcing sustain talk. More consistent double-sided reflections would further soften sustain talk.
