# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent, warm, unconditional positive regard with zero judgment about the shoplifting or court-mandated referral. Acceptance is specific and individualized, responding to the exact emotional content Jordan brings in each moment rather than formulaic reassurance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate perspective-taking with complex reflections that capture underlying meaning beyond surface-level paraphrasing. Empathically acknowledges the specific difficulty of Jordan's situation rather than offering generic understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from rapport-building through exploration to a concrete moment of choice. Never allows the session to drift into unrelated topics while remaining flexible and non-mechanical.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is genuinely co-constructed with Jordan's ideas shaping direction at every point. No moment where the therapist imposes an agenda or redirects based on their own clinical framework.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Jordan's ownership and right to choose, never using directive language. The closing question acknowledging 'even if you end up saying yes' represents the highest level of autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is designed to draw out Jordan's own internal motivations with zero external reasons for change provided. The entire session is structured around evoking Jordan's internal resources, which is the hallmark of expert-level MI evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Jordan as the expert on his own life with genuine power-sharing throughout. The psychiatrist's role is explicitly framed as facilitator, not guide or expert, with session direction shaped by Jordan's contributions at every turn.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently evokes and amplifies change talk with skillful evocative questions and affirmations. Could have more systematically explored DARN-CAT categories or elicited more explicit commitment language to build a clearer trajectory toward change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged, validated, and gently opened for exploration from a different angle without arguing, dismissing, or reinforcing reasons to continue. Textbook handling of peer influence, wanting, and doubt about lasting change.
