# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance with clear affirmations. It falls short of a 5 because acceptance is sometimes generic and not deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are useful reflections of Lukas’s mixed feelings and practical concerns. However, empathy remains somewhat surface-level and does not consistently capture deeper shame, fear, isolation, or identity threat.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session has a reasonably clear focus on substance use and its practical consequences. However, the conversation becomes more logistics-driven and does not consistently stay connected to the core ambivalence about change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Lukas’s choices clearly influence the plan, showing genuine two-way interaction. The collaboration is limited by the counselor’s frequent reliance on clinician-generated options rather than a fully client-led process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Lukas’s freedom to choose and respects refusals without pressure. It is not a 5 because repeated prescriptive options and pushes toward concretizing the plan introduce mild pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks some evocative questions but quickly shifts to advice-giving and practical planning. There is limited elicitation of Lukas’s own values, hopes, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A collaborative stance is recognizable, and the client’s choices shape the session. However, the counselor often structures the conversation and offers solutions before fully following the client’s own framing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lukas expresses some change talk, but the counselor does not consistently amplify or deepen it. The session relies more on selecting from practical options than on eliciting Lukas’s own motivations for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Lukas’s sustain talk and accepts it nonjudgmentally. However, the counselor often accommodates it by planning around maintaining the status quo rather than consistently softening it toward change.
