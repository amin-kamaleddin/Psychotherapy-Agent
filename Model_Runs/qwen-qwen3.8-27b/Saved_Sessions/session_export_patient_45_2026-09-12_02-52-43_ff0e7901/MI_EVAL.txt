# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Erik’s practical concerns. The score is limited because the warmth is efficient rather than deeply attuned to underlying emotions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses good reflections of frustration and practicality. Deeper ambivalence around tradition, autonomy, and fear of loss is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on diet, alcohol, and diabetes management with clear momentum toward a practical plan. Broader medical and nutritional advice occasionally dilutes the MI process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and revises the doctor question based on Erik’s preferences. The collaboration remains somewhat limited because the psychiatrist still leads with expert information.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Erik’s choice by respecting his decisions about beer and Friday fish and chips. More explicit emphasis on Erik owning the decisions would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some choice-based questions but often supplies reasons and practical advice instead of drawing out Erik’s own values. Erik remains largely externally focused rather than internally motivated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Erik’s input and shares some decision-making. The frequent expert role makes the interaction feel more advisory than fully collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some action talk, such as swapping Coke for water and planning around cravings. The work would be stronger if it more consistently amplified Erik’s own reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and validates Erik’s concerns about keeping traditions. Using double-sided reflections would make the MI response to sustain talk more sophisticated.
