# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently nonjudgmental stance, validating Ethan's minimization without pushback. Acceptance is effective but could reach deeper emotional resonance by more explicitly normalizing his ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate, nuanced reflections that capture the complexity of Ethan's experience. Reflections follow a fairly predictable pattern and could be more emotionally penetrating, particularly around the shame underlying his defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session follows a clear logical progression from understanding the referral to exploring function and alternatives. It lacks a clear landing point with a collaboratively identified next step or action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation feels like genuine dialogue with co-constructed understanding and inclusive language. The session remains somewhat therapist-driven in its structure rather than truly co-directed by Ethan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is the foundational tone of the entire interaction, reinforced through every question, reflection, and pause. The psychiatrist never pressures, ultimatums, or implies a 'right' answer, consistently positioning Ethan as the expert on his own experience.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently draws out Ethan's own reasons and values rather than providing information or advice. Evocation operates primarily at the level of consequences and preferences rather than tapping into deeper values and identity for stronger momentum.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and follows Ethan's lead without lecturing or assuming authority. The session could be deeper if the psychiatrist more explicitly invited Ethan to set the agenda or indicate what feels most important to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively catches and affirms change talk, particularly when Ethan acknowledges that alternatives 'would matter.' Evocation is present but not consistently pursued to its fullest potential, missing opportunities to deepen and amplify change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through validation and exploration of underlying function rather than argument or minimization. The psychiatrist could have used more strategic reframing to explicitly soften the assumption that belonging and drinking are inseparable.
