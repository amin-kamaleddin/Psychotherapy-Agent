# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance without shaming or moralizing. The acceptance could be deeper by adding more individualized affirmations of Maya's courage and worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was strong, particularly in capturing Maya's goals and stress function. Deeper empathy could be shown by exploring underlying emotions like shame, fear, and peer pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remained focused on alcohol-related risk, stress, and coping strategies. Direction could be strengthened by using more explicit agenda-setting and summaries to anchor the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was co-created with Maya's choices shaping the final strategy. Collaboration could improve with more mutual exploration of values before moving into implementation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maya's autonomy was well supported through frequent choices and respect for her preferences. A few directive statements, such as setting the reminder immediately, could be framed more as invitations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session began with a strong values-based question and explored drinking triggers. Evocation could be stronger by more consistently eliciting Maya's own reasons, confidence, and meaning for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveyed a collaborative stance and invited Maya's input throughout. The partnership was somewhat limited by a more expert-guided approach when offering structured coping tools.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited some initial change talk connected to Maya's goals and school. More consistent development of change talk through importance and confidence questions would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Maya's concerns and acknowledged her doubts. More strategic double-sided reflections could help pivot sustain talk toward change more effectively.
