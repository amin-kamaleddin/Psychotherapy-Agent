# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider is generally respectful and nonjudgmental. Warmth could feel more individualized and less transactional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are present but remain somewhat surface-level. They do not fully capture the patient's emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session mostly stays on topic, but there are moments of drifting into general options. A clearer strategic focus would strengthen the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider engages collaboratively and helps co-construct a plan. At times, the approach remains somewhat directive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider respects the patient's choice and avoids coercive language. Autonomy could be reinforced more explicitly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The provider draws out some motivation, but change talk is not consistently developed. More deliberate evocation would strengthen the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider invites patient input and shares decision-making. The partnership could be made more explicit.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The provider elicits some change talk, but does not consistently deepen or amplify it. Additional follow-up questions could strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged, but there is limited strategic redirection. The provider could more effectively soften resistance.
