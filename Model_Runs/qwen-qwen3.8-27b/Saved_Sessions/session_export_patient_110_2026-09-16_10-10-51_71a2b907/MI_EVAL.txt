# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating. Warmth is generally strong, though it could be more individualized and explicitly normalizing of ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture mixed feelings, goals, and practical concerns. Some deeper exploration of underlying emotions and relational context is missing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays generally focused on cholesterol management and relevant behavior change. Detailed medical education occasionally pulls focus away from the MI process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist often invites input and adapts to the patient's preferences. Collaboration is sometimes less reciprocal when scripts and plans are supplied rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is supported, and direct pressure is absent. Autonomy could be strengthened by more explicitly reinforcing the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and open-ended prompts elicit some change talk. However, frequent medical explanations and scripts limit deeper evocation of the patient's own motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally conveys a collaborative stance and welcomes patient input. The session remains somewhat task-directed when information or scripts are introduced quickly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient offers useful change talk, and some statements are affirmed. The psychiatrist could more consistently build on the patient's own values, ability, and confidence to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Resistance is acknowledged without argument or dismissal, and the conversation is redirected to manageable steps. Some sustain talk is answered with reassurance or information rather than more MI-based reframing.
