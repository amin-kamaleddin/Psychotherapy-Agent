# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent, warm, and genuine unconditional positive regard throughout, affirming Wiremu's honesty and struggle without judgment. No labeling, disapproval, or conditional warmth is present anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are layered, specific, and emotionally attuned, capturing meaning-level content well beyond surface paraphrasing. Notable examples include connecting childhood experience to present fear and acknowledging unspoken pain in the trailed-off reference to violence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains generally clear focus on target behaviors with a logical session progression from priorities to concrete commitments. Minor drift occurs during the 'I carry it' exploration, which, while therapeutically valuable, temporarily shifts away from the specific change agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a joint exploration where Wiremu's own language and ideas directly shape the direction and content. The psychiatrist builds on the client's formulations (e.g., 'adult in their house,' 'how's it going?' vs 'what are you doing right now?') as the driving force of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Wiremu's autonomy with zero directive language, affirming his right to maintain identity and make his own choices. Framing the action as his own decision ('not as a kid asking for permission, but as a man working out his own plan') is particularly effective.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major turning point is driven by Wiremu's own words, which the psychiatrist reflects, deepens, and amplifies without imposing external motivation. The client's final commitment statements emerge organically from his own process, facilitated by strategic evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Wiremu, using open-ended questions that invite self-discovery. No directive language, 'you should' statements, or expert-driven advice-giving is present.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk across desire, reasons, ability, and commitment through open-ended questions, amplification, and values-connected reflections. The evocation is strategic, well-timed, and consistently deepens the client's own motivation rather than imposing it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by reflecting tension and exploring concerns without arguing, though the sustain talk around independence is addressed somewhat indirectly. Could have used more explicit double-sided reflections to fully honor both sides before the client resolves the ambivalence himself.
