# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, nonjudgment, and appreciation for Viktor’s attendance. Deeper, more individualized affirmation of Viktor’s values and struggle would strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections capture several key themes, including attendance motivation, perceived control, and the desire for peace. The responses remain somewhat surface-level and repeat similar phrasing rather than exploring deeper ambivalence or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session shifts from drug use toward managing marital conflict and concrete home routines. A stronger MI direction would keep connecting Viktor’s values, goals, and actions to reducing use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Viktor’s ideas substantially shape the conversation, including walking, texting, DND, and washing his face. More sustained co-construction, with Viktor generating more options, would make collaboration less guided.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses choice-oriented language and avoids commanding reduction. Repeated scripts, options, and confidence scaling can subtly push compliance rather than emphasize Viktor’s full choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Useful questions are asked, but the psychiatrist often moves to examples and scripts before fully exploring Viktor’s own motivations. Deeper evocation of values, discrepancy, and reasons for reducing use is needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and offers options, but multiple questions and suggestions per turn can feel counselor-driven. Reducing density and more explicitly positioning Viktor as the expert would improve true collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited around small steps, peace, and confidence. The focus remains narrow, mostly on reducing conflict with his wife rather than reducing drug use itself.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct argument but invites sustain talk about control, limits, and benefits of use. More skillful double-sided reflections and strategic pivots are needed to soften and redirect that talk.
