# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, never labeling or stigmatizing Anya's behavior. Gently corrects self-deprecation without judgment, maintaining a warm and validating tone from first to last utterance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate and nuanced reflective listening, using Anya's own metaphors (the tangle, the alarm) to demonstrate deep engagement. Shows micro-attunement by noticing and following up on trailing-off moments, reflecting emotional complexity with remarkable precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful session arc moving from exploration to concrete action without feeling rushed or forced. Questions progressively narrow from broad to specific while remaining entirely client-led, with natural transitions guided by Anya's responses.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration with balanced power dynamic; the psychiatrist builds on Anya's metaphors and co-constructs the plan rather than imposing a framework. Active participant who contributes structure and amplification while Anya drives content and direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never offers advice, suggestions, or prescriptions; every decision point is presented as Anya's to make. The entire plan is explicitly framed as Anya's own creation, with consistent language positioning her as the agent of her own change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every intervention is designed to elicit Anya's own reasoning, values, and solutions rather than providing external reasons for change. The session results in a complete, concrete, values-aligned action plan arriving entirely through Anya's own thinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Anya, using open questions and 'I'm wondering with you' language. Never says 'you should' or prescribes action; every question is designed to elicit her own thinking.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elevates Anya's passing remarks into commitment language without being heavy-handed, consistently amplifying values-based and ability-based change talk. The frequency and quality of change talk elicited by session's end is directly attributable to the psychiatrist's evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well but misses an opportunity to directly address Anya's 'I might still blow it up by Wednesday' with a double-sided reflection. Would benefit from more explicit validation of ambivalence before pivoting to positive framing.
