# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, nonjudgmental language and affirms Camila’s choices without blame. Support is strong but sometimes repetitive and cheerleading, limiting individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately summarize content and track preferences, but they often miss the worry, pressure, or self-doubt underneath key statements. More feeling-focused reflections would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains consistently focused on making physical activity sustainable, moving from goals to a concrete plan. Focus narrows toward logistics and repeated recaps, keeping it below 5.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Camila’s choices shape the plan, and the psychiatrist adapts to her preferred simple, low-pressure approach. The heavy clinician-driven structure prevents a fully equal collaborative exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice and avoids pressure, especially by honoring a skipped walk as not failure. However, multiple suggested reminders and expectations feel mildly controlling when taken together.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some questions elicit goals, confidence, and self-generated solutions, but the session quickly becomes planning-focused. Change talk is present but not consistently deepened or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites client input and respects her expertise on her routine. Frequent questions, recaps, and structure make the interaction feel more guided than co-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Camila articulate goals, confidence, and a realistic plan. There are fewer questions that specifically draw out reasons, benefits, or importance for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about fatigue, weather, and fear of failure is reframed into flexible, workable accommodations. It could be stronger by more actively linking these concerns to change-relevant meaning.
