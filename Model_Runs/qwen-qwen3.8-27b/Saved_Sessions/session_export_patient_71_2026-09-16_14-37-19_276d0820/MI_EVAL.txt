# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard without judgment or pressure. Explicitly validates the client's values and efforts, creating a safe therapeutic stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate and nuanced reflections that capture the client's complex ambivalence and systemic challenges. Go beyond paraphrasing to show deep perspective-taking around health, service, and willpower.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, purposeful focus on the target behavior and specific barriers. Skillfully guides the conversation from values to actionable steps without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Builds meaningfully on the client's own ideas, such as a non-negotiable appointment and visible cue. Could be strengthened by explicitly co-creating a shared plan or summary.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and freedom to choose. Uses open-ended questions rather than advice or prescriptions, allowing the client to define success.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's own motivations, values, and solutions. Could be stronger by more fully exploring core identity and values related to the change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, client-expert stance and invites the client's perspective. However, the psychiatrist remains somewhat dominant in structuring the conversation, limiting fully shared power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open questions to evoke desire, ability, and agency for change. Could be stronger by explicitly amplifying and reflecting back the client's change talk to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalizes barriers and avoids argumentation by reflecting doubts and redirecting toward success criteria. Could use more complex reflections of sustain talk itself rather than relying mainly on questions.
