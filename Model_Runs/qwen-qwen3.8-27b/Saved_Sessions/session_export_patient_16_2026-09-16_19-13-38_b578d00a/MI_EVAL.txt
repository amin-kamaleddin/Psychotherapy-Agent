### Acceptance

**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Anika without challenging her minimization. Acceptance is strong but somewhat general, with limited explicit affirmation of her worth beyond her choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections to track ambivalence, triggers, and the basketball-related conflict. Some reflections stay more cognitive and plan-focused, with less depth on fear, identity, or emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on vaping use and its impact on basketball, friendship, and stress. The conversation moves purposefully from ambivalence toward practical, client-relevant planning.

**Comments:** Frequently elicits Anika’s own motivations, coping ideas, and confidence through open-ended questions. Evocation is effective but more behavior-planning-focused than values, confidence, or identity-level exploration.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Clearly positions Anika as the expert and consistently returns the interpretive work to her. Language such as “that’s your idea, not something I’m pushing” reinforces an equal, collaborative stance.

**Comments:** Frequently elicits and reinforces Anika’s reasons for change, especially around playing better and protecting her season. Could be stronger by more explicitly amplifying and summarizing change talk at key moments.
