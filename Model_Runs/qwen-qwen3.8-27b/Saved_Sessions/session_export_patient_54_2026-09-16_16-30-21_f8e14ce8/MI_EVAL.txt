# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, with strong respect for the client's values and autonomy. No moralizing, coercion, or disapproving language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects ambivalence and key concerns accurately, including autonomy, partnership, and fear of judgment. Misses some final emotional nuance, including remaining uncertainty and fear of anticipated guilt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stays focused on the target behavior and moves toward a practical next step. Direction is slightly weakened by repeating an earlier question instead of addressing the client's new concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner and co-constructs a plan using the client's own ideas. The interaction is genuinely collaborative rather than expert-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the client's self-determination and frames the decision as hers. Avoids persuasion, guilt, or prescriptive advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open questions to elicit the client's motivations, values, and preferred plan. Could evoke more confidence, importance, or handling of remaining uncertainty.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions the client as the expert on her own life and repeatedly invites her perspective. The session is shaped by the client's contributions rather than imposed direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes concrete change talk, including specific steps and a script for the partner conversation. Could develop change talk further with scaling, importance statements, or summarization.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids challenging or amplifying sustain talk and keeps focus on values and preferred direction. Misses opportunities to use double-sided reflections to gently address remaining ambivalence.
