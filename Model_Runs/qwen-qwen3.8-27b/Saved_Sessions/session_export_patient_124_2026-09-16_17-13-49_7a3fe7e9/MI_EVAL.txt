# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Sofia's ambivalence without shaming her. The stance could have included more explicit warmth and individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Sofia's internal conflict and social pressure. Empathy could be deeper by addressing financial strain, self-criticism, and family-related fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed connected to the target issue of marijuana use and related stress. Direction was less strategic because a specific change goal was not clearly negotiated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation was generally collaborative and driven by open questions and invitations for Sofia's input. Some leading or interpretive statements slightly reduced the sense of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided pressure and direct advice, which supported Sofia's autonomy. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming that the decision is entirely Sofia's.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used evocative questions to draw out Sofia's perspective. Evocation could be stronger by more consistently eliciting deeper values, confidence, and change talk rather than moving toward solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited Sofia's views but occasionally took an interpretive or guiding role. More explicit power-sharing would strengthen the collaborative partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offered several change-oriented statements, but the psychiatrist did not consistently amplify or deepen them. More explicit importance, confidence, and commitment questions would strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged the perceived benefits of using without arguing against them. More double-sided reflections would help reduce the dominance of sustain talk and gently turn toward discrepancy.
