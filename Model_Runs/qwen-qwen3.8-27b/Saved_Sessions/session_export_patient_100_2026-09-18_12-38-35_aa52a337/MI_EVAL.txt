# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations throughout the session. Warmth is present but somewhat measured and professional rather than deeply attuned to Maya's self-worth beyond her behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening with attention to emotional subtext, particularly in the final reflection about feeling 'looser.' Could have gone deeper into the implicit loneliness or social anxiety behind Maya's self-description as 'pretty serious' and 'keep to myself a lot.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains general focus on the presenting concern but the session lacks a clear strategic trajectory toward a change goal. The conversation feels meandering, moving through topics without summarizing themes or guiding toward a specific exploration of ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Framing the session as collaborative is present ('a conversation between us') but the interaction remains largely counselor-led. Maya is passive and the counselor does not actively co-construct the agenda or invite her to shape the session in meaningful ways.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Maya's autonomy with non-pressuring language and repeated affirmations of her right to choose what she shares. Could reach a 5 by more explicitly affirming her ownership of any future decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used but limited in strategic depth and purpose. No specific evoking techniques like scaling questions or importance ruler questions are employed to draw out Maya's own values, goals, or reasons that might support change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with appropriate language and avoids an expert-driven approach. However, the partnership is somewhat one-directional, as the counselor does most of the steering and doesn't fully leverage Maya's request to be guided as a partnership moment.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes minimal effort to cultivate change talk, with no explicit questions designed to elicit change language. The counselor does not strategically redirect or amplify any seeds of change talk, which is a notable gap for a session exploring motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with a non-judgmental response that avoids reinforcing it, but does not use more strategic MI techniques like double-sided reflections to acknowledge both sides of the ambivalence and gently invite further consideration.
