# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating of ambivalence, though at times more procedural than deeply affirming of the client’s worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of ambivalence and concerns, but could be more emotionally nuanced and value-based.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on contraception after a pregnancy scare while guiding toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a joint-exploration stance, but the psychiatrist still sometimes leads agenda-setting and information structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the client’s control, pace, and right to choose or stop without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits the client’s own motivations and ideas, but could deepen exploration of values and readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats the client as the expert on her own choices, though some therapist-led options may feel slightly leading.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes planning-focused change talk, but could better expand it toward deeper values and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk supportively and helps reframe concerns, but could more consistently redirect them toward change.
