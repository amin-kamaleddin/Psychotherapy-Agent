# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional, consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with no moments of disapproval or impatience. Directly addressed Kenji's fear of judgment ('I'm not here to label you') and honored his agency with the closing affirmation 'You gave yourself that. Not me, not your doctor, you.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently captured emotional nuance beyond surface content, such as detecting Kenji's hedging ('like you're still checking if you're allowed to believe it') and subtle recognition moments. Reflections deepened throughout the session as Kenji's self-awareness grew.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, logical progression from rapport to self-awareness to concrete action planning, consistently returning focus to the target behavior. Lost a point for lacking an explicit session summary or specific follow-up plan at the close.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the entire interaction as a joint exploration between equals, using language like 'I'm curious' and co-constructing meaning ('Those two things can both be true'). Never lectured, provided unsolicited information, or positioned itself as the authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently removed pressure, offered options rather than prescriptions, and preserved Kenji's freedom to continue, modify, or stop at any time. Explicitly affirmed his right to his own pace: 'You don't have to go from a few beers to never again.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire session was built on drawing out Kenji's own motivations, connections, and confidence without providing a single external reason for change. The progression from 'I just didn't ever really think about it' to 'I'll try it' was entirely self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Kenji as the authority on what would help, what 'in charge' means, and what to try next. Explicitly attributed all insights and plans to Kenji: 'Counting, skipping a night — those are your ideas.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited change talk through open questions and strategic amplification, often drawing it out where it would not have emerged spontaneously. Key move was reinforcing Kenji's own words: 'I want you to hear yourself say that — maybe small is okay.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validated every instance of sustain talk without arguing, minimizing, or making Kenji feel wrong for his position. Consistently found and reflected the change talk embedded within or adjacent to the sustain talk, such as redirecting the 'alcoholic' fear to his stated value of staying in charge.
