# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor communicates basic respect and uses accepting statements such as validating stress and exhaustion. However, acceptance remains somewhat surface-level and task-oriented rather than deeply affirming of Daniela’s values and struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are limited empathic reflections, but the counselor often shifts to problem-solving without fully exploring underlying emotions. Important moments of fear, embarrassment, and feeling stretched thin are missed or only partially addressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela offers some change-relevant language, but the counselor does not consistently notice or build on it. The session would benefit from more evocative follow-ups that deepen her own desire, ability, and commitment to change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and makes some collaborative moves, but often takes the lead by providing scripts and resources. The interaction can feel expert-driven rather than a true partnership in which Daniela’s ideas are prioritized.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniela is given choices and low-pressure language, which supports some degree of autonomy. However, the counselor’s prescriptive suggestions and ready-made scripts can subtly shift decision-making away from the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks some open questions but often moves quickly into advisory mode. There are many evocative themes in Daniela’s statements, but they are not consistently drawn out or connected to her values and motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela influences some decisions, but the counselor frequently takes the lead by offering options and scripts. A stronger partnership stance would involve following her lead more closely and treating her as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela expresses several change-relevant statements, but the counselor does not consistently reflect, expand, or amplify them. More active development of her own motivations would strengthen the MI process.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Daniela’s concerns but does not strategically reframe or redirect sustain talk. More double-sided reflections and evocative questions would help move the conversation toward change.
