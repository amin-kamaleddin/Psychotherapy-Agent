# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout — no judgment or labeling at any point. The psychiatrist validates Elena's experience, normalizes her uncertainty, and explicitly removes pressure, creating a genuinely safe environment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections accurately capture Elena's ambivalence and emotional undercurrents. However, reflections occasionally read as formulaic or structured, lacking the occasional 'aha' moment of deeper granular attunement that would distinguish exceptional from very good.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on the target behavior with good pacing that builds toward a concrete, client-generated action plan. Minor opportunity missed to more aggressively cultivate peak change talk moments, such as after Elena's 'I didn't realize it was so dangerous' statement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a masterclass in collaborative practice — education is delivered as joint exploration, and the final plan is entirely co-constructed by Elena. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with Elena's ideas shaping the session's direction and outcomes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a hallmark of the session, with repeated explicit affirmations of Elena's right to choose and no implicit urgency or pressure. The final exchange presents options without steering, and her self-generated plan is validated rather than optimized by the clinician.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits Elena's own motivations and values rather than imposing external reasons for change. The evolution from contemplation to a concrete next step is almost entirely client-generated, with the psychiatrist serving as a skilled evocator rather than a persuader.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise resides within the client, explicitly positioning Elena as the decision-maker and framing the session as collaborative exploration. Even educational content is offered as a resource rather than authoritative instruction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions and reflections skillfully lead Elena to articulate her own motivations, and the psychiatrist explicitly names and amplifies the shift from sustain talk to change talk. The session moves Elena organically from hesitation to genuine, client-generated commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently honored without being reinforced — the psychiatrist validates Elena's chickenpox experience and online concerns while gently introducing the other side through double-sided reflections. The adversarial dynamic is avoided, and sustain talk is used as a springboard toward change talk.
