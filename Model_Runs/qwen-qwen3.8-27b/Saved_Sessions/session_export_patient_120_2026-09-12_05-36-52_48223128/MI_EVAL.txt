# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with validating statements like 'That's a really hard spot to be in.' However, quickly reframes her fear as a 'practical, protective step' rather than deeply exploring the emotional weight of her ambivalence about being a 'bad mom.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture her fear, guilt, and torn feeling about losing her social life. Empathy stays at a surface level at times—doesn't explore deeper grief, identity loss, or fear of inadequacy as a mother.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, focused trajectory toward the health question, risk reduction, and a concrete step to contact the OB. Direction becomes overly task-focused around the wording of a message rather than staying on broader motivational targets.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Sofia to choose among options and co-creates message wording, asking whether the language fits her voice. Collaboration is practical and responsive but uneven since the psychiatrist remains the primary generator of solutions and scripted language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices ('Which of those would you like to focus on first?') and validates Sofia's selections without shaming. Becomes somewhat more directive when drafting the message and repeatedly offering to send it immediately, slightly reducing her sense of self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Draws out some concerns and strengths ('What strengths or resources do you already have?') but depth is limited. No importance/confidence rulers or future-oriented questions; shifts to practical problem-solving before fully evoking Sofia's own reasons and identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative tone present through questions and offered choices supporting shared work. However, frequent shifts into an expert/problem-solving role (medical information, drafted messages) reduce Sofia's own voice and agency in driving the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk elicited through open questions and affirmation of her agency ('You've already taken strong steps'). Lacks follow-up questions to expand reasons, confidence, or values; much of the session shifts into practical planning rather than amplifying her own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges her fear of losing friends and worry about going to a bar, with a mild reframe ('not avoidance'). Limited active softening—no double-sided reflections to integrate the social-identity conflict more fully into the change process.
