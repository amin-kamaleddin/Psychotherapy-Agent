# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, nonjudgment, and positive regard throughout the conversation. Warmth remains somewhat clinically functional rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and understanding of the client’s perspective. Reflections focus more on reasoning and problem-solving than on deeper emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on cholesterol management and medication adherence. There is little drift, and the conversation repeatedly returns to concrete behavioral goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with the client’s ideas shaping the session and the plan. The psychiatrist builds on the client’s language rather than imposing a predetermined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports the client’s autonomy by emphasizing that the decision is his own. There is little pressure, no direct persuasion, and no implied obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits the client’s values, confidence, and reasons for change through open-ended questions. Evocation is occasionally guided by the psychiatrist’s suggested framing, reducing its full openness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and invites the client to direct the session. The psychiatrist still provides a fairly structured path and occasionally leads toward specific interpretations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encourages the client to express reasons for change, including desire, ability, and commitment. The evocation could be more explicit and skillful in naming and amplifying change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and reframes concerns without hardening resistance. Responses could be strengthened with more double-sided reflections and explicit acknowledgment of the status quo’s appeal.
