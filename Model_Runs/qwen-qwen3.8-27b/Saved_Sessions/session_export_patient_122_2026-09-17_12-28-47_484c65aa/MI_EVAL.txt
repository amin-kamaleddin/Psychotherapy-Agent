# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with powerful reframes like 'you're a person who just moved and is still unpacking' that remove moral judgment. Every reflection carries warmth and individualized respect without a single label or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and emotionally attuned reflections that progressively deepened, capturing precise internal states like 'the fog, the heaviness, and this quiet loss.' The 'pre-loading shame' reflection was precisely targeted and immediately validated by Sofia.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on target behaviors with logical progression from problem identification to concrete action steps. Minor gap where the final food/buzzing thread was left somewhat exploratory without a concrete next step or brief summary to close the loop.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration between equals with Sofia leading every thread; all questions were open-ended and designed to elicit her own thinking rather than steer toward a predetermined outcome. The psychiatrist followed each emerging thread faithfully.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Sofia's ownership and freedom to choose, using phrases like 'what feels closest to true?' and 'you designed that whole thing yourself — not me, not a program — you.' Never created accountability pressure even when Sofia expressed reluctance about follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Gold-standard evocation where every significant insight — the cycle, the five-minute plan, the shoes mitigation, and 'maybe it's not food I need, maybe it's the stopping' — emerged entirely from Sofia. Questions were masterfully timed to create space for self-generated insight.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positioned Sofia as the expert on her own life, explicitly stating 'that feels like you really know yourself.' The session structure was entirely Sofia-driven with a purely facilitative counselor role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully created conditions for Sofia to generate all of her own change language, from 'that feels doable' to 'maybe it's the stopping.' The targeted affirmation 'Doable. I like that word' amplified Sofia's own positive language without any external injection of reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong double-sided reflection on the past failure fear ('you're allowed to try something and have it not be a transformation'), but the 'what's the point' voice was addressed somewhat briefly without full strategic exploration or a dedicated softening technique.
