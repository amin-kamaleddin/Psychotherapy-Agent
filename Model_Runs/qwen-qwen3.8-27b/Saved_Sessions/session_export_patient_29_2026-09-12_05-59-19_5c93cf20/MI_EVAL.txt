# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental throughout. Acceptance remained professional but did not fully affirm David’s broader effort, values, or personal strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflected David’s concerns, stressors, and practical worries about smoking and triggers. Some reflections stayed surface-level and did not fully capture underlying fear of failure, shame, or exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking cessation and relevant triggers such as dining out, stress, and the 4 PM slump. Direction was strong but occasionally became overly informational and lacked a final consolidated plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist engaged David in decision-making and built on his own ideas, such as stopping after lunch. Collaboration was present but remained partly clinician-driven through structured options and lists.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offered options, respected refusals, and avoided coercion. There was mild implicit pressure toward immediate action, which slightly limited the pace of autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist made some effort to elicit David’s reasons and agency early in the session. However, the conversation shifted too often into information-giving and practical problem-solving rather than deeply evoking personal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a collaborative, non-controlling stance and invited David’s input on multiple choices. The partnership was strong, though not fully client-led due to frequent expert guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was supported through open questions and some affirmations of David’s intentions. The psychiatrist did not consistently deepen, amplify, or elaborate on change-related statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument or confrontation. Advanced softening strategies, such as double-sided reflections or value-based reframing, were used only minimally.
