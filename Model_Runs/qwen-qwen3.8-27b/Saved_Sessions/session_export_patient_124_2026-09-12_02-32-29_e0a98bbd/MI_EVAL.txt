# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, while normalizing Sofia’s ambivalence without shame. The main limitation is that some responses feel more categorical or clinical than deeply affirming of her worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is accurate content-level reflection of Sofia’s perspective and mixed motives. Empathy would strengthen if the psychiatrist reflected more of her underlying emotions, such as fear, worry, shame, or concern about failing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on marijuana use, anxiety, and a concrete short-term target. It becomes plan-focused a bit quickly and does not fully connect the change goal to broader values or long-term concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is meaningfully involved in choosing Tuesday, the wallet, walking, and texting Mike. However, many of the solutions and scripts are clinician-generated rather than fully elicited from Sofia.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice, permission, and Sofia’s authority over the plan. It would be stronger if fewer prescriptive suggestions were offered before fully inviting Sofia to generate her own options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is drawn out, including saving money, wanting less anxiety, and willingness to try Tuesday. The psychiatrist often moves too quickly to suggestions instead of fully exploring Sofia’s own motives, confidence, and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a collaborative stance and frequently invites Sofia’s input. The main limitation is that the agenda and proposed options remain largely clinician-led rather than fully patient-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some change talk, but it is not systematically deepened or amplified. The psychiatrist misses several opportunities to explore what saving money, managing anxiety, or trying Tuesday means to Sofia.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument, which avoids confrontation. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently use complex or double-sided reflections to validate the function of use while opening space for change.
