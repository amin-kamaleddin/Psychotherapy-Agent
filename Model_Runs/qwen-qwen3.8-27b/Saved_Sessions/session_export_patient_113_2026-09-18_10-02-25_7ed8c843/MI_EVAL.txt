# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating from the first interaction to the last. Never challenges Rowan's ambivalence or expresses disappointment, instead meeting him exactly where he is.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go well beyond surface-level paraphrasing to name the underlying emotional and cognitive dynamics (e.g., 'it's the wondering, not the device'). Each reflection is specific, accurate, and progressively deepens the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, logical arc from precontemplation to tentative commitment with no off-topic drift. Every intervention builds on the previous one and serves the goal of moving Rowan toward device compliance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The problem, solution, and next step are all jointly constructed by both parties. The interaction feels like two people thinking together rather than one directing the other.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Rowan's ownership and right to choose, using language like 'that's a line you drew for yourself' and 'you get to choose when you're ready.' Respects his boundary about not keeping written notes without pushing back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external reasons for change; instead uses strategic questions that lead Rowan to articulate his own motivations and solutions. The final commitment ('I'll try. A few days.') is entirely in Rowan's own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian throughout, with the psychiatrist positioning herself as a curious collaborator rather than an expert with answers. The session's direction is shaped by Rowan's responses at every turn.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions progressively build specificity and commitment, guiding Rowan from 'I guess so' to a concrete statement of intention. Change talk is explicitly reflected and amplified to reinforce the shift.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively handles sustain talk by acknowledging it without reinforcing or dismissing, and using it as a springboard toward change. One minor moment where a question briefly gives airtime to the fear of the number before redirecting.
