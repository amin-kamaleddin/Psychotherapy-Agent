# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist never judges, pathologizes, or imposes narrative. The correction moment ('It's not a victory. It's not a triumph.') is a masterful act of acceptance that honors Maria's lived experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, nuanced, and deeply attuned to Maria's specific emotional landscape without over- or under-reflecting. The psychiatrist holds multiple truths simultaneously and tracks Maria's oscillation between openness and resistance with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains a general thematic focus but lacks strategic direction or a concrete change goal. The 'lunch plan' emerges almost accidentally rather than being strategically developed through scaling or agenda-setting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Maria's input and a shared framing of the work. However, collaboration is somewhat one-directional, with the psychiatrist primarily validating and reflecting rather than actively co-constructing meaning in real-time.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook autonomy support — consistent, explicit, and never perfunctory. The psychiatrist never pressures, imposes, or uses persuasion, and affirms Maria's small commitment as her own decision ('Not a promise to anyone else. Just a you-decision').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes a few well-timed evocative moves but largely relies on Maria to generate her own insights. Misses opportunities for scaling questions, values-based exploration, or future-focused evocation that could have drawn out more change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Maria, never positioning themselves as authority. The session is genuinely client-led, with Maria's contributions substantially shaping the direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative moments elicit change talk, but the psychiatrist does not systematically work to amplify or develop it. The change talk that emerges is mostly Maria's own, not skillfully built upon by the psychiatrist.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids arguing against or dismissing sustain talk, instead validating the pain underneath and gently pivoting. Could be stronger by using double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to help Maria resolve ambivalence more actively.
