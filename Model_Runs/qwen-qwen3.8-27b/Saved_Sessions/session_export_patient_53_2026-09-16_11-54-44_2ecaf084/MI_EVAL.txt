# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, nonjudgmental language and validates Isabella’s anxiety without blame. Some affirmations feel generic or prematurely positive, limiting deeper normalization of her fear of being seen.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are accurate content reflections, but empathy often stays surface-level and repetitive. Deeper emotional themes such as fear of judgment and ambivalence are not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and narrows from broad activities to very small, manageable steps. It occasionally becomes too task-focused without reconnecting to Isabella’s motivations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choices and uses collaborative language, but often remains the primary problem-solver. More open-ended exploration would better center Isabella’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly affirms choice, permission to stop, and freedom from obligation. Some repeated micro-step planning begins to create subtle pressure toward action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Initial questions explore meaningful activities and past connection, but the session soon shifts to behavior planning. Values, hopes, and internal reasons for change are not deeply evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and responds to Isabella’s concerns, maintaining a collaborative form. However, the psychiatrist remains more directive than a true partner in generating solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Isabella offers some change talk, but the psychiatrist mostly affirms rather than explores its meaning. There is limited use of scaling or follow-up questions to strengthen her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and validates Isabella’s reluctance. However, responses sometimes reinforce avoidance and do not fully redirect toward her underlying wants or values.
