# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates nonjudgmental, warm, and respectful regard. Mateo’s pain, effort, and protective “tough it out” stance are validated without moralizing or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captures ambivalence, fear of judgment, and the need for safety. Some reflections remain more cognitive or slightly leading than emotionally deep.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior of continuing rehab while managing pain. It strategically narrows into a concrete, actionable plan for taking a brief pause when needed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed, with the psychiatrist building on Mateo’s note idea, signals, and reframe. The psychiatrist still drives much of the agenda and supplies several formulations, preventing a perfect 5.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently keeps the decision with Mateo and avoids pressure or controlling language. Suggestions are offered conditionally and respect the possibility that Mateo may not be ready yet.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits motivation from within Mateo rather than supplying reasons for change. Mateo’s own values and self-redefined meaning of strength emerge clearly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Mateo is positioned as the expert on his own body and experience. The psychiatrist follows Mateo’s lead and shares power rather than imposing a predetermined plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections effectively evoke and reinforce Mateo’s change talk. It could be strengthened by more systematic elicitation of confidence, ability, and next-step commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and reframed without arguing or reinforcing stuckness. Some self-labels and failure-focused ideas remain in focus and could be softened further with stronger double-sided reflections.
