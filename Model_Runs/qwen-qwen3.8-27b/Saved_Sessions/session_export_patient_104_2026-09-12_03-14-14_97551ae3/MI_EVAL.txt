# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, normalizing Nadine's exhaustion and self-criticism. Warmth is occasionally interrupted by quick shifts into advice or correction, which can subtly reduce the feeling of unconditional support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflections that capture Nadine's fear, guilt, exhaustion, and relational concerns. However, the responses sometimes move too quickly into psychoeducation or strategies, limiting deeper emotional processing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on Nadine's acute exhaustion, infant sleep concerns, and need for reassurance. Focus occasionally broadens into general education without explicitly linking topics to one clear client-driven goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are moments of collaboration, such as adjusting scripts to Nadine's voice and offering choices. However, the psychiatrist often remains the primary source of information and solutions rather than co-creating the plan with Nadine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by asking permission and offering options, but does not consistently emphasize Nadine's ownership, capacity, or right to choose. Providing full scripts and strategies may reduce the sense that she is generating her own solution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some open-ended and evocative questions, especially at the beginning and when offering choices. Later in the session, the focus shifts to advice and practical strategies, limiting deeper elicitation of Nadine's values, confidence, and motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some partnership by asking permission, checking in, and adapting to Nadine's feedback. The session remains largely counselor-led, as the psychiatrist often decides the content and sequence of information and strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few evocative questions, but the session is largely advice-driven rather than focused on eliciting Nadine's own reasons for change. Nadine's expressed motivation is not consistently developed, amplified, or connected to values or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist occasionally softens Nadine's doubts with reassurance or practical alternatives. However, some sustain talk is addressed with information rather than reflective exploration, limiting a stronger MI-based pivot toward change.
