# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys respect, warmth, and nonjudgment through validating statements such as acknowledging exhaustion and worry. However, acceptance is sometimes brief and service-oriented rather than deeply individualized or affirming of strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects practical burden and validates emotions, but often shifts quickly into problem-solving. Deeper concerns about feeling like a burden, dependency, or being turned away are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on childcare stress and moves efficiently toward a concrete action. Pacing is purposeful, keeping the conversation anchored on a specific, time-bound step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs scripts and adjusts to Daniela’s concerns, showing genuine teamwork. However, the heavy use of clinician-generated options and scripts can reduce the sense that the client is fully leading the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offers choices and protects Daniela’s agency without pressure or urgency. Language and confidence-check questions reinforce that Daniela is in charge of her next step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The use of confidence ratings and small-step questions helps elicit client-owned action. However, the session relies heavily on clinician-provided advice rather than drawing out values, meaning, and intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choice and input, and Daniela’s decisions clearly shape the session. The amount of structure provided makes the interaction feel more expert-guided than fully collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session brings out some change talk, especially as Daniela’s confidence rises and she commits to making the call. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or expand Daniela’s values, hope, or richer change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges barriers and redirects them into practical next steps without arguing. The session lacks more skilled MI techniques such as double-sided reflections or reframing to move beyond sustain talk.
