# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Rowan without judgment, pressure, or disapproval. Language remains respectful and normalizing even when Rowan expresses self-criticism or resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Rowan's ambivalence, frustration, and exhaustion. Empathy could be stronger by more deeply naming shame, self-blame, or fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation remains tightly focused on the target behavior of using and charging the medical device. The session steadily moves from concern toward practical setup.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the plan with Rowan, using his language and building on his ideas. It could be even stronger by giving Rowan more explicit control over the agenda and sequence.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure and supports Rowan's ability to choose a workable version. Autonomy support could be more explicit by repeatedly reaffirming his right to decide what works for him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist primarily elicits solutions from Rowan rather than giving advice. Evocation could be strengthened by more explicitly drawing out values, confidence, hope, and readiness for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Rowan as the expert on his own routines and lets his ideas shape the plan. A higher score would require more explicit power-sharing, such as inviting Rowan to set priorities or the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes practical change talk and reinforces Rowan's suggested solutions. It could be stronger by more explicitly eliciting confidence, commitment, or hope around the plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Rowan's reasons for avoiding the device without arguing. However, the responses do not consistently use double-sided reflections to pivot sustain talk toward change.
