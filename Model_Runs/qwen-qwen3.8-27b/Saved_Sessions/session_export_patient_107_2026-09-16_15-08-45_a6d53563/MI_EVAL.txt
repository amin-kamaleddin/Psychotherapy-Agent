# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, non-judgmental stance but initially overstates the client's emotional intensity. Later affirming statements improve the acceptance tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures the client's ambivalence, values, and practical concerns. The opening reflection is slightly overcalibrated, and some responses remain more cognitive than emotionally nuanced.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays mostly focused on coffee timing, quantity, jitters, and a one-week test. There is some drift into sleep, work stress, and phone/email use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session feels co-constructed, with the client's ideas strongly shaping the conversation. The psychiatrist builds on client-generated plans rather than imposing a predetermined approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently supported through non-controlling language and emphasis on the client's choice. The psychiatrist avoids pressure and respects the client's boundaries around a fair experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits the client's own values, concerns, and potential actions rather than lecturing. There is room to deepen motivation with stronger evocative follow-up, summaries, or scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the client as the expert and invites input on the process and plan. Some counselor-generated structure is introduced before fully eliciting the client's preferred direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions effectively prompt the client to generate change ideas, such as limiting cups, adjusting timing, and testing sleep/focus. The change talk could be further amplified, summarized, or expanded.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with the benefits of coffee and validates the client's reasons for maintaining current habits. More double-sided reflections could more explicitly soften sustain talk and support movement.
