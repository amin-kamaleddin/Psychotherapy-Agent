# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with explicit attribution of agency and wisdom to the client (e.g., 'That's yours'). Zero judgment, labeling, or condescension, even when the client acknowledges her own patterns of inattention.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture emotional texture and underlying meaning, not just content. Reflections are varied in form (simple, complex, amplified) and consistently convey the client's internal experience with remarkable precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on the target behavior with a coherent session arc from values to concrete action. Transitions between phases could be slightly more strategically bridged with summarizing reflections to tighten focus further.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance where the client's ideas genuinely shape the session's direction and content. The client's own language ('ripples,' 'on purpose,' 'shoulders') becomes the working framework of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent autonomy support with zero coercion, persuasion, or directive language. The client is consistently positioned as the agent of her own change, and the therapist never adds guidance beyond affirmation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation of motivation and change language from within the client, progressing from awareness to vision to barriers to solutions to commitment. Every piece of change content originates from the client; the therapist never provides reasons or advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and implicit power-sharing with the therapist's role purely to evoke and reflect. Statements like 'I just asked questions… That's yours' and 'it happened inside you, not because I prompted it' fundamentally shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and skillfully cultivates change talk, with the client progressing from abstract values to highly specific committed language ('four-thirty is for her'). DARN-C framework is well-represented throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk present (low-ambivalence client), but both instances are handled skillfully by acknowledging the validity of the status quo while gently reframing it. The therapist never argues against or minimizes the client's concerns.
