### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating throughout the interaction. Slightly constrained by the task-oriented, time-limited nature of the exchange, preventing fully unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reflects Ingrid's core tension between wanting an immediate answer and the practical need to defer to her GP. Stays at a cognitive level without exploring the underlying anxiety or feeling of being stuck.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question and suggestion is tightly focused on resolving the specific medication interaction question. A model example of maintaining crisp, purposeful direction within a time-limited interaction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with co-constructed solutions and consistent invitations for input. Could be elevated by more explicit power-sharing language and acknowledging the patient's expertise more directly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Ingrid's ownership of decisions, never pressures or prescribes a course of action. Closing statements frame future engagement as her choice, not an obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Ingrid's own plans and articulation of the problem rather than imposing solutions. Limited by session brevity; deeper connection to broader values or self-care goals would have elevated the score.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-hierarchical stance with inclusive language throughout. The suggestion about mentioning dose count was slightly directive rather than fully co-constructed, a minor distinction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and affirms Ingrid's agency, desire, and commitment to act within the context of a brief medication question. Limited in depth by the task-focused nature of the session, preventing exploration of broader goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Ingrid's time constraint well without pushing or amplifying disengagement reasons. Could have more explicitly reframed her time pressure as a reason to be strategic in the GP call.
