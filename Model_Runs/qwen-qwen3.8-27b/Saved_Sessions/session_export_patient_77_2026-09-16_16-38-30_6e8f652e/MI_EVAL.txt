# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and uses affirming phrases to validate Liyana. It feels somewhat formulaic at times and could add deeper individualized warmth and explicit normalization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows accurate reflective listening to Liyana’s exhaustion, ambivalence, and loneliness. It could go deeper in validating shame, self-worth doubt, and the pain of feeling unseen.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on measuring effort and weight-loss progress. It becomes circular around the scale, jacket, and stairs without clearly moving toward a concrete, client-owned next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally treats Liyana as a partner and reflects her concerns rather than imposing solutions. It remains somewhat counselor-led due to repeated similar questions and limited joint plan-building.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently avoids pressure and affirms Liyana’s right to choose how to measure progress. It could further affirm that she is free to choose any path, including not changing her measurement habits right now.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks open questions that bring out Liyana’s ideas about alternative progress signs. It does not fully draw out deeper values, identity-based motivation, or meaning behind her wins.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a collaborative, nonexpert stance and invites Liyana’s perspective often. It is somewhat constrained by repeated question structure and limited co-creation of a broader agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some alternative progress signs such as less winded, stairs, and jacket fit. However, it does not consistently amplify or develop desire, ability, need, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct argument but repeatedly returns to the scale, leaving the scale as the central source of conflict. It needs stronger double-sided reflections, normalization of plateau, and reframing to reduce the power of sustain talk.
