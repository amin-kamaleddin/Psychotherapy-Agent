# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Jordan's honesty and difficulty. However, the warmth is somewhat formulaic and does not fully acknowledge the deeper shame, fear of rejection, or coercive context of the court referral.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Jordan's ambivalence and helps him feel heard. However, empathy is often interrupted by a quick shift into problem-solving rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on shoplifting, triggers, and a concrete response plan. It is somewhat prescriptive and lacks a final MI-style summary or consolidation of change talk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jordan contributes to the plan and participates in role-play, showing some partnership. However, the psychiatrist frequently supplies the structure, options, and scripts rather than fully co-constructing them with Jordan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choice and avoids direct commands, supporting some agency. However, repeated directive language and a strong menu of clinician-generated options can make the process feel less patient-led.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions and confidence scaling help draw out Jordan's own motivations and self-assessment. The psychiatrist often moves too quickly into solutions, limiting deeper evocation of intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Jordan's input and shares some power in planning. However, the expert problem-solving role dominates much of the interaction, making the partnership uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some change talk through scaling and reflection of Jordan's motivation. However, he often shifts away from change talk toward concrete options and scripts instead of deepening it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing against Jordan's reasons to continue shoplifting and accepts them neutrally. However, he does not consistently use double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to turn sustain talk into ambivalence work.
