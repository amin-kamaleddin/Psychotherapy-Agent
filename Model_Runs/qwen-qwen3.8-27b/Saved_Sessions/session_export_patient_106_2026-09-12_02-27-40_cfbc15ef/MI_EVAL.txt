# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance while normalizing Natalia’s exhaustion. However, acceptance occasionally became over-reassuring rather than fully ambivalence-tolerant.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used accurate reflective listening to identify Natalia’s mixed feelings and emotional weight. The main limitation was that empathy remained somewhat repetitive and did not fully deepen into identity-level meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stayed close to the target issue of smoking and its impact on her son. However, focus drifted toward broader emotional support, venting, and self-compassion rather than maintaining a clear MI target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used collaborative language and often framed the session as shared work. However, collaboration was uneven because the psychiatrist frequently steered the session by offering a menu of options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently affirmed Natalia’s choices, avoided pressure, and respected her stated limits. The main weakness was continued offering of options after she repeatedly declined new strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist made some effort to evoke Natalia’s own experience and feelings. However, evocation was often interrupted by reassurance and therapist-generated interpretations rather than deepening her own change-oriented language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used some collaborative language and invited Natalia’s input throughout the session. However, the session remained noticeably guided by the psychiatrist’s own agenda rather than Natalia’s stated priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were a few early attempts to evoke change talk, but the psychiatrist did not consistently notice, amplify, or expand it. The session relied more on supportive reassurance than strategic development of change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** This was a major weakness, as sustain talk was often reflected in ways that inadvertently reinforced it. The psychiatrist rarely pivoted toward discrepancy, values, or the costs of maintaining the current coping pattern.
