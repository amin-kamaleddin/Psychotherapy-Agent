# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong validation of Lukas's discomfort with the referral and explicit normalization of his feelings. Could be slightly more individualized beyond the referral context to acknowledge who Lukas specifically is as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate and skilled reflective listening using Lukas's own language, with a particularly strong double-sided reflection on coexisting feelings. Some reflections stay at the surface level without exploring deeper emotional undercurrents like fear of identity loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains good focus on target behavior with a logical progression from general feelings to specific disclosure to values and warning signs. Could benefit from a clearer directional arc toward a concrete next step or commitment by the session's end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative tone and language throughout, using Lukas's own metaphors and avoiding expert or lecturing stances. Primarily in tone rather than structural co-creation; does not explicitly invite input on session goals or direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent reinforcement of autonomy — never pressures, advises, or imposes timelines. Redirects questions back to Lukas's own judgment and values rather than providing external answers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of open questions to elicit internal values and self-generated warning signs without providing external reasons for change. Does not fully amplify or develop the strongest change talk moments to their fullest potential.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance and follows Lukas's lead throughout. The overall structure and pacing still feel somewhat therapist-led; could include explicit agenda negotiation or asking what Lukas wants to accomplish.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes genuine change talk through well-timed questions about values and warning signs, with effective amplification of key statements. Could more explicitly summarize and reinforce the full range of reasons Lukas articulates for wanting to avoid a pattern.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well with double-sided reflections that validate both sides without dismissing or arguing. Could be more strategic in redirecting toward Lukas's own answer rather than simply following up.
