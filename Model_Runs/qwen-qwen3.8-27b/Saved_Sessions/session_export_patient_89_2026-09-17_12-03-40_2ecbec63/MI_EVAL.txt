# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with a warm, non-judgmental tone from the opening. Validates self-doubt immediately and celebrates progress without being performative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate, emotionally attuned reflective listening that captures both the logistics and emotional weight of the all-or-nothing cycle. Reflections are specific and build progressively across the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing physical activity while allowing natural conversational flow. The session follows a logical arc from problem exploration through to concrete, self-generated commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as a true collaborator using exploratory language ('I'm curious,' 'what do you think?') rather than prescriptions. The final plan is entirely Marcus's construction, with the psychiatrist contributing only reflections and questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Marcus's ownership and choice, from the opening 'no pressure' to the final question asking Marcus to generate his own coping statement. Never uses directive language or positions himself as the expert on Marcus's life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is designed to elicit Marcus's own motivations rather than providing reasons for change. All motivation emerges from within Marcus, progressing from broad exploration to specific, self-generated commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language consistently places expertise and authority with the client ('I'd love to just hear from you,' 'what would you want to hear from yourself?'). The session is shaped entirely by Marcus's contributions, interests, and solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes diverse change talk across all DARN categories through precisely calibrated questions. Change talk builds progressively from initial desire to concrete commitment ('I'll make an appointment and I'll start dancing right away').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reframes sustain talk by acknowledging the reality while shifting the frame from permanent failure to an understandable pattern. Enables Marcus to generate his own alternative rule ('shoes on') rather than telling him to change his thinking.
