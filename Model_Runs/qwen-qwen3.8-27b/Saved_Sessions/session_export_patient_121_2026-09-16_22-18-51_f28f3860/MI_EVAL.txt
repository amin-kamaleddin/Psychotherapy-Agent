### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental throughout. Acceptance could be strengthened by more explicitly normalizing Sofia’s emotional pain, shame, and fear of being labeled.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence, competing goals, and practical concerns. Empathy could deepen by exploring stronger emotional themes such as hurt, loneliness, shame, and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on helping Sofia feel safer and less targeted at school. The conversation moves smoothly from broad concerns to specific, actionable coping strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative, exploratory language and builds on Sofia’s own ideas. Collaboration could be stronger by reducing therapist-generated options and more fully co-constructing the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia’s choices are respected, and the psychiatrist does not pressure her to commit. Autonomy support could be enhanced by more directly affirming her ownership and freedom to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits Sofia’s own motivations, ideas, and planned steps. Evocation could be more skillful by exploring values, confidence, and the importance of change more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Sofia as the expert on her experience and avoids an authoritarian stance. Partnership could improve by more explicitly sharing power and reducing therapist-guided structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia generates meaningful change talk about desire, ability, and intention. The psychiatrist could strengthen this by reflecting back her strengths and using importance/confidence scaling more often.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument or reinforcement. The psychiatrist could more consistently reframe or redirect Sofia’s concerns directly into change-focused language.
