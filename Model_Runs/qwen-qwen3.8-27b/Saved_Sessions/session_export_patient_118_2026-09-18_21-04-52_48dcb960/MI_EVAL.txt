# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Seamus's frustration and apologizing when pushing too far. Could be warmer with more explicit affirmations of Seamus as a person beyond his choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening capturing Seamus's frustration with repeated advice and the emotional weight of his father's dental loss. However, reflections occasionally feel surface-level and slightly formulaic, missing deeper feelings of being dismissed by providers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session completely abandons the smoking cessation goal by the midpoint, shifting entirely to dental logistics. The psychiatrist could have maintained direction by exploring the connection between smoking and gum disease as a bridge.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Follows Seamus's lead and avoids being directive, but essentially concedes the entire session to his framing without collaboratively exploring how smoking connects to his oral health. Treats teeth and smoking as separate topics rather than a shared goal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy with no pressure, respecting his choice and affirming 'no promises needed.' Could be stronger by explicitly reinforcing Seamus's agency in the change process specifically, not just by dropping the topic.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** No scaling questions, no exploration of values, and no reflection of change talk related to smoking. The approach of validating resistance and moving on misses the core MI principle of drawing out motivation from within the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with open questions and acknowledges Seamus's expertise in his own life. Becomes one-sided in the latter half when simply accepting the redirection and dropping the change goal entirely.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to elicit Seamus's own reasons for change, with no scaling questions, readiness exploration, or amplification of any change talk that emerged. The session ends without meaningful cultivation of change talk related to smoking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not argue against or reinforce sustain talk, which is appropriate, but also doesn't actively soften it with double-sided reflections or reframing. Essentially takes sustain talk at face value and drops the topic rather than gently keeping the door open.
