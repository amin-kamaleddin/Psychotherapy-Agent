# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance throughout. Acceptance is warm but somewhat formulaic, with limited deep individualization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures multiple key themes, including meaning, concern, and autonomy. Empathy remains mostly cognitive and factual rather than deeply emotional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and moves toward a concrete plan. Minor redundancy occurs near the end with repeated summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, and Isabella’s ideas strongly shape the session. Some counselor-generated suggestions and resources limit fully client-led co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Isabella is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, and her choices and boundaries are strongly respected. There is no pressure, persuasion, or controlling language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Readiness and confidence questions effectively elicit internal motivation and practical change plans. Deeper exploration of values, identity, or ability could strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses permission-seeking and invites Isabella’s input, supporting a collaborative stance. Partnership is strong but not fully client-led because the counselor introduces several options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes change talk through questions about readiness, plans, and barriers. Change talk could be further amplified by exploring values and strengths more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and validates frustration without amplifying sustain talk. However, sustain talk is not consistently softened or transformed into change talk.
