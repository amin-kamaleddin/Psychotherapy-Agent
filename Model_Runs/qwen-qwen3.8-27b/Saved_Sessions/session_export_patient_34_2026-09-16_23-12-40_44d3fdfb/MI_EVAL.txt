# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental with validating language and an effective rupture repair after Dylan felt lectured. Slightly procedural and risked over-accepting his minimization ('didn't pull the strings') without holding a broader perspective.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, strong reflections of Dylan's core fairness and being-singled-out experience, including an apology when a question landed poorly. Several reflections circled the same themes and missed deeper emotional layers (shame, fear of stigma, desire to be seen as normal).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session stayed on the incident but lacked progressive movement toward a concrete target behavior or next step. Questions repeated similar framings ('fair conversation,' 'easier to explain') without building momentum toward the drinking decision or accountability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance with validating language, an effective repair, and client choices. Dylan's role remained more reactive than co-creative; limited evidence that his ideas reshaped the session structure beyond choosing Jake's account.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong skill: repeatedly invited Dylan to choose the direction, respected his 'plain and simple' preference, and closed with a clear two-option choice. A few subtle steering moments (shift to 'what tipped you') lacked explicit reaffirmation that Dylan was in charge of whether to explore that.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriate evocative questions on fairness and procedural concern that elicited Dylan's perspective. Stopped at surface-level themes and missed opportunities to evoke deeper values ('being fair beyond this incident'), identity, or discrepancy around the drinking decision.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly treated Dylan as the expert on his own experience with frequent client-choice prompts and a strong final two-option offer. Still controlled much of the flow through a question sequence and occasionally steered toward an agenda Dylan hadn't chosen.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal evocation of change language; Dylan's own statements ('want the facts to matter,' choosing Jake's account) weren't reflected back as change-related intentions. No exploration of importance, confidence, discrepancy with values, or what a responsible decision would look like; session stayed dominated by sustain talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoided arguing with Dylan's minimization and used mild double-sided reflections. However, largely reflected his sustain themes ('basically 21,' 'bad luck,' 'just a guest') without effectively introducing discrepancy or connecting his stated value of fairness/accountability to the behavior.
