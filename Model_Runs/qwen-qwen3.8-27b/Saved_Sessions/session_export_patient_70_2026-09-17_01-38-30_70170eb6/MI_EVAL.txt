# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses warm, respectful, nonjudgmental language and affirms Lars’s strengths and effort. The score is not a 5 because the counselor could more explicitly normalize Lars’s dignity, shame, and ambivalence for even deeper unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates reflective listening and captures Lars’s broader goals, ambivalence, and physical experience. The score is 4 because the counselor does not fully name the underlying shame, fear, or guilt in a deeper reflective way.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, strategic focus on Lars’s re-entry risks and high-risk moments. The counselor skillfully narrows the discussion from broad stuckness to a concrete, actionable five-minute coping plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is a genuine collaboration, with Lars’s own ideas driving the plan. The counselor co-constructs a practical solution around Lars’s resources, such as the car and hands-on activity, rather than imposing advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Lars’s sense of choice and ownership throughout the session. The counselor avoids pressure or imposed disclosure, respecting Lars’s preference to keep the plan low-key with Donna.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits motivation from within Lars rather than supplying external reasons for change. Lars generates the motivational content, coping strategies, and self-cue, demonstrating highly evocative practice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Lars as the expert on his own life and repeatedly invites his judgment. Lars’s contributions substantially shape the session, resulting in a client-generated plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully elicits and amplifies Lars’s own reasons, abilities, and planned commitments. The session moves effectively from general motivation to a detailed, client-generated coping plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with, minimizing, or reinforcing Lars’s sustain talk and risk-related language. The score is 4 because the counselor could use more explicit MI strategies, such as double-sided reflections, to fully reconcile competing pulls.
