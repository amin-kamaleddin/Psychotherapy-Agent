# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently used validating, nonjudgmental language and normalized the client's struggles. The validation was somewhat formulaic and repetitive, limiting the depth of positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections captured practical concerns accurately but often remained factual and logistical. The psychiatrist did not consistently reach the deeper emotional weight behind the client's ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on reducing social media use during study time and moved logically toward a concrete plan. The direction was strong, though quite task-focused rather than motivation-focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited the client's input and incorporated several choices, such as the 10/2 timer and casual text reply. However, the psychiatrist generated most options, making the collaboration more menu-based than truly equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist framed the plan as an optional experiment and emphasized flexibility without shame. The highly prescriptive, step-by-step advice somewhat undermined the client's sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** After an open opening question, the session quickly shifted into advice-giving and problem-solving. The psychiatrist made limited effort to draw out the client's own motivations, values, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client's preferences shaped parts of the plan, and the psychiatrist invited input at several points. The dynamic remained asymmetric because the psychiatrist was mainly the architect of the solution.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client offered some change talk, but the psychiatrist did not follow up to expand or amplify it. The session became more of a logistics exercise than an exploration of the client's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged sustain talk neutrally without reinforcing it, but did not strategically soften it. There was limited use of double-sided reflections or discrepancy work to explore the tension between maintaining phone use and wanting better sleep and focus.
