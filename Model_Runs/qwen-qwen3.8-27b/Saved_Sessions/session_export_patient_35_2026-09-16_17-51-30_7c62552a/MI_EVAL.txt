# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms Elena’s efforts without blaming her. Acceptance could be stronger with more individualized affirmation of her worth and emotional experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses solid reflective listening to capture Elena’s ambivalence between protecting her son and managing stress. Empathy would be stronger with deeper reflections of guilt, fear, frustration, and exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a manageable target behavior: reducing smoking while protecting her son. Direction would improve with less repetitive questioning and more strategic follow-through on Elena’s concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Elena’s input and helps her shape a small, self-defined plan. Collaboration would be stronger with fewer counselor-generated suggestions and more explicit co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently frames change as optional, respects Elena’s limits, and supports her ownership of the process. Autonomy support is the strongest aspect of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits change talk from Elena rather than providing reasons for change. Evocation could be deeper and more varied by drawing out values, confidence, hope, and past strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally positions Elena as the expert on her own life and asks for her perspective. Partnership could improve by reducing scripted repetition and counselor-generated options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena generates several change-related ideas, especially around skipping the first cigarette. The psychiatrist could better amplify and summarize this change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor responds to sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing reasons to continue smoking. It would be stronger with more skillful reframes that turn sustain talk into opportunities for change talk.
