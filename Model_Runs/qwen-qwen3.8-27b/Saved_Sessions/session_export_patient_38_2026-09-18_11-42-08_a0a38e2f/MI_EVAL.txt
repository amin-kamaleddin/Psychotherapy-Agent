# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and supportive throughout the session with no moments of disapproval. Could benefit from more individualized, specific positive regard (e.g., naming specific strengths Elena has demonstrated).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections that accurately capture emotional core (e.g., the 'no good option' pattern). Occasionally stays at the surface level of what Elena says rather than exploring deeper underlying meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session lacks strategic momentum toward a specific change goal, with no clear progression from awareness to action or building toward a concrete plan. The psychiatrist follows Elena's lead rather than gently guiding toward a specific change-related outcome.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with consistent joint-exploration language and no imposed solutions. However, the collaboration remains within a somewhat counselor-directed framework rather than truly co-created with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook demonstration of autonomy support with multiple explicit statements reinforcing Elena's ownership and timeline. The psychiatrist never pressures, imposes recommendations, or implies that not choosing is a failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some good evocative questions but relies heavily on reflecting and affirming rather than varied strategies. No scaling questions, values exploration, or eliciting of confidence or ability statements are present.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance using inclusive language and avoids the expert role throughout. However, the partnership is somewhat one-directional in practice, with the psychiatrist structuring the conversation rather than truly co-creating the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges change talk but does not explore, amplify, or expand on it, and lacks scaling questions or future vision exploration. Evocation is present but surface-level and could be significantly deeper.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One strong moment of softening ('I don't get the sense that you're overthinking today at all'), but in other instances the psychiatrist amplifies barriers without gentle redirection. Would benefit from double-sided reflections or curiosity questions to soften sustain talk.
