**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates unconditional positive regard throughout, never labeling Ahmed as resistant or irrational. Ahmed's minimization is validated before gentle exploration, and his self-awareness is explicitly affirmed as 'pretty valuable.'

**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Ahmed as the expert on his own life, using language that defers to his judgment. Ahmed's construction-site practicality is treated as a legitimate decision-making framework rather than a deficit.

**Comments:** Multiple instances of change talk are skillfully evoked, including Ahmed's own practical health behaviors and his self-generated action plan. The change talk could have been further amplified and consolidated into a stronger change narrative.
