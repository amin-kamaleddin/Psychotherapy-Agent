### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates unconditional positive regard throughout, validating Amina's identity, capabilities, and emotions without judgment. Even self-critical statements are met with warmth and reflection of underlying values rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond surface-level paraphrasing to capture complex emotional dimensions, including bittersweet past successes, ambivalence, and identity-level concerns. Each reflection is tailored to Amina's specific emotional landscape rather than generic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on the target behavior (overcoming childcare barriers to employment) with a logical progression from barrier identification through past exploration to concrete relational design. No drift into unrelated territory, even when emotional content arises.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation functions as a genuine dialogue between equals, with Amina's ideas driving session direction at every turn. The psychiatrist's role is consistently to reflect, amplify, and ask the next question rather than redirect or impose structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Amina's ownership of the change process is consistently reinforced through open questions, explicit positioning of her as the agent, and offering choices rather than directives. The final relational design is entirely her own creation, facilitated but never imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All interventions are designed to draw out Amina's own motivations and resources rather than providing external reasons for change. The session culminates in Amina spontaneously recognizing her own progress, which is the hallmark of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise about change is consistently positioned as residing within Amina, with the psychiatrist following her lead at every turn. No advice is offered; all questions are designed to elicit Amina's own insights and problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully amplifies Amina's statements into affirmations of capability and clarity, using strategic reflections and questions to evoke further change talk. The shift from 'I don't know where that person is' to naming Mrs. Adeyemi is explicitly recognized and reinforced.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Sustain talk (fear of being a burden, social embarrassment) is addressed through reframing as values and highlighting counter-evidence without argument. However, the psychiatrist could be slightly more active in pivoting away from fear-based language at times, as the concern persists in Amina's final statement.
