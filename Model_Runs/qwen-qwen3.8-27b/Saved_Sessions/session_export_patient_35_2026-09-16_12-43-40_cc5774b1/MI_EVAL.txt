# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys respect, nonjudgment, and autonomy support, especially by accepting Elena’s refusal of a check-in. Warmth is somewhat formulaic and the repeated focus on protecting her son felt judgmental to Elena.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers useful reflections of Elena’s protective, overwhelmed, and torn position. Reflections are sometimes cognitive and repetitive, missing deeper exploration of her fear of failing, exhaustion, and the meaning of her outdoor break.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking cessation, secondhand smoke exposure, and a clear short-term patch trial goal. At times, the psychiatrist moves into practical patch planning before fully exploring Elena’s ambivalence and motivations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds the plan with Elena, offers choices, and adapts when she requests a simpler approach. The process remains partly clinician-directed because of the amount of medical information and structured recommendations provided.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong autonomy-supportive language and respects Elena’s decisions about the plan and follow-up. Early emphasis on a multi-step plan, the check-in suggestion, and repeated reminders create a mild controlling undercurrent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions are used to elicit importance and confidence, supporting some change-related exploration. Evocation is often superficial and interrupted by practical suggestions rather than deeper exploration of Elena’s values, fears, strengths, and own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally collaborative stance and allows Elena to shape key decisions, such as the patch, lozenge, start day, and no check-in. However, the psychiatrist still functions partly as the expert by offering specific plans and clinical guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited through scaling questions and Elena’s agreement to try the patch. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, develop, or reflect back Elena’s change talk before shifting to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Elena’s sustain talk and respectfully reflects her efforts to smoke outside and protect her son. However, repeated affirmation of her current practices can reinforce the status quo, with limited use of double-sided reflections or strategic pivots toward change.
