# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, explicitly affirming the client’s ambivalence and culturally relevant concerns. No shaming, moralizing, or labels were evident.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate and consistent reflections capture the client’s values, conflict, dignity, and fear of being controlled. Could have explored deeper grief and shame more explicitly, but overall empathy was strong.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains logical focus on alcohol use, Stopping Violence, and a practical support plan. Could more explicitly consolidate the plan back to the core target behaviors at key points.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a strongly collaborative, joint problem-solving interaction using inclusive language and building directly on the client’s ideas. The session is client-led rather than expert-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports client choice by avoiding pressure and inviting the client to shape the trial, check-ins, and language with whānau. Could be strengthened with more explicit statements about the client’s freedom to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open questions to elicit the client’s own conditions, values, and solutions. Could be deeper and more strategic in evoking personal importance, confidence, and future-oriented motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly conveys that the client is the expert, asking him to define what would work and following his lead throughout. Power is shared in practice through client-generated plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits substantial change talk and affirms the client’s emerging plan. Could more deliberately amplify and consolidate the strongest change talk as ownership, desire, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledges concerns about independence and monitoring without challenging or minimizing them. Could use more strategic double-sided reflections that actively link sustain talk to change.
