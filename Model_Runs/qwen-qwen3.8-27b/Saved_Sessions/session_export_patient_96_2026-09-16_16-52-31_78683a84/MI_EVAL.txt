# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms Marisol’s choices, respect, and independence without judgment. The stance is warm but somewhat formulaic, relying on repeated reflective phrases rather than deeper individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Marisol’s dual priorities and avoids taking her position as a mistake. Reflections remain mostly cognitive and do not fully explore her underlying emotions or defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the lip piercing and oral-health behavior, moving logically from concerns to practical self-care. Direction is clear but narrow, with limited connection to broader values or goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses “we” language, offers options, and pivots when Marisol rejects suggestions. Collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist still steers through a structured sequence of questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly respects Marisol’s choice and consistently positions her as the decision-maker. Support is excellent but somewhat one-dimensional, not deeply affirming her identity-level self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Questions are open-ended but mainly logistical, asking about practical care mechanics rather than intrinsic motivation. The psychiatrist does not adequately draw out Marisol’s values, reasons for change, or self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys that Marisol knows her own mouth best and frames the work as collaborative. Partnership is present but structured, with less explicit co-creation of the agenda than ideal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marisol offers limited change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen it. The focus remains on practical steps rather than strengthening her motivational language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist validates Marisol’s resistance without arguing or creating a power struggle. It does not actively use double-sided reflections or strategic exploration to shift her ambivalence.
