# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently non-judgmental and validating, with explicit expressions of unconditional positive regard (e.g., 'You matter, even in the middle of all this'). Warmth is genuine but measured and professional rather than deeply personally invested.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with complex reflections that capture both sensory experience and temporal dimensions of suffering. The self-correction about 'getting caught up in my own hope' demonstrates genuine attunement to Maria's perspective rather than the psychiatrist's narrative.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Follows Maria's lead closely, resulting in a somewhat circular quality where the same themes are revisited without progressive movement. Lacks a clear strategic arc or identification of a 'next step' to anchor the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Maria to co-construct the therapeutic process and an explicit collaborative feedback loop. Somewhat passive in that the psychiatrist mostly invites input but does not actively weave Maria's specific language into the therapeutic work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently removes pressure, avoids all directive language, and never gives advice or pressures toward a decision. Could be stronger by more explicitly affirming Maria's capacity to make her own decisions rather than primarily framing things in terms of what she doesn't need.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some evocative moves (e.g., 'Can you tell me more about what that feels like?') but remains at a surface level. Tends to reflect Maria's words back rather than asking questions that would draw out deeper motivation or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as collaborative, invites Maria to shape the session, and models vulnerability through self-correction. Closing moments are somewhat one-directional in the psychiatrist's reflections, slightly reducing the collaborative quality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some attempts to identify and name change talk ('That's not nothing') but is limited in frequency, specificity, and skill. Misses opportunities for more targeted evocative questions that could have gently opened space for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflects and validates sustain talk without reinforcing it, but does not use double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to actively soften it. Avoids making things worse but also does not actively pivot or create space for change talk alongside the sustain talk.
