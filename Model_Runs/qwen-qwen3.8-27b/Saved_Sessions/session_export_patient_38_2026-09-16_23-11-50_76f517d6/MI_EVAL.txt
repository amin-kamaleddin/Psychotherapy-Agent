# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates a respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental stance, explicitly affirming Elena's autonomy and pacing. Slight deductions reflect occasional interpretive language that, while supportive, is not purely unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening effectively captures Elena's ambivalence, fear, and physical sensations, such as the "chest knot." The empathic depth is somewhat limited by repeated summaries and a focus on stated feelings rather than deeper underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session is relevant but lacks strategic focus, frequently drifting between emotional processing and the target behavior of choosing a contraception method. A clearer agenda or consistent momentum toward a concrete next step would improve the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist fosters a collaborative tone by inviting Elena's perspective and responding to her priorities. However, the use of structured choice-based prompts subtly steers the conversation, reducing the level of open co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** This is a standout strength, with consistent validation of Elena's right to decide and clear avoidance of pressure or advice. The psychiatrist successfully frames Elena as the expert on her own body and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses evocative questions to help Elena identify her own values and goals, such as wanting something to "set and forget." The evocation could be stronger by more consistently deepening or amplifying the change talk that emerges.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A collaborative stance is maintained by treating Elena as the expert and repeatedly checking in on her preferences. The partnership is slightly hindered by counselor-led structure and repeated menu-style options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena expresses meaningful change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or build upon these statements. The session would benefit from more explicit work on Elena's confidence and the importance of her goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responds to sustain talk without confrontation, maintaining a safe and accepting environment. However, there is a missed opportunity to more skillfully pivot these statements toward change or explore the implications of the status quo.
