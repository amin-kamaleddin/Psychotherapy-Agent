# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing the client's discomfort and reframing his behavior as understandable rather than judgmental. No moment of implicit criticism or dismissal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture the emotional arc and dual nature of the client's ambivalence without oversimplifying. The psychiatrist precisely mirrors the tension between the pull of the quiet space and the awareness of its cost.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from understanding the problem to exploring its function to brainstorming, maintaining focus on the target behavior. Slight drift at the end toward meta-discussion about the MI process itself rather than explicitly tying insights back to the behavioral goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed, with the client's ideas directly shaping direction at every turn. The psychiatrist follows Jamal's threads (the quiet, the bench, the mother's reaction) rather than steering toward a predetermined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of client ownership and the freedom to choose, including explicit permission to not commit and validation of ambivalence. The psychiatrist never prescribes a solution, never says 'you should,' and frames the entire conversation around the client's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within the client through open questions and reflections of his own language. The psychiatrist never provides external reasons for change, instead asking Jamal to generate his own conditions and vision for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, using language like 'let's figure this out together' and highlighting when Jamal generates his own ideas. The power dynamic is genuinely equal throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reflects back and amplifies the client's own change talk, such as 'I'm here about it' and the shift from 'I don't want to leave' to 'where could I get that quiet?' Could be slightly stronger in further eliciting and building on emerging change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating its legitimacy, using double-sided reflections, and never arguing or dismissing. Occasionally drifts slightly toward problem-solving mode ('let's figure this out together'), which shifts marginally away from pure MI stance.
