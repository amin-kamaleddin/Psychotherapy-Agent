# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental, validating Seamus's right to focus on dental care without labeling him as resistant. Tone is professional and warm but somewhat clinical rather than deeply conveying unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening that mirrors Seamus's language and captures his behavioral shifts well. Reflections remain primarily cognitive and behavioral rather than exploring deeper emotional experiences like frustration, exhaustion, or loss of autonomy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Follows Seamus's lead appropriately but the session lacks a clear therapeutic trajectory after the initial frames. Does not find a way to subtly connect the dental work to the broader health context, resulting in a somewhat aimless logistical focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Seamus as an equal partner, consistently seeking his input before offering suggestions and framing plans as options. Slightly departs from full collaboration by generating the three-step plan unprompted rather than eliciting it from Seamus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent throughout; respects Seamus's boundary about no check-in calls without pushing back and frames all actions as his own choices. Offers support without imposing it and never contradicts autonomy with directive behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively uses open questions to elicit Seamus's own plan, script, and boundaries rather than providing them. Could go deeper by exploring his values or broader life narrative to strengthen intrinsic motivation beyond the practical.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Seamus as the expert on his own needs and offers choices rather than directions. Occasionally takes a slightly directive role in framing next steps and makes one interpretive statement about Seamus's internal state.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully amplifies Seamus's own forward-moving language (e.g., 'I'll get through it') and affirms his self-generated solutions. Does not systematically target DARN-CAT language or explicitly evoke confidence in his ability to make the call.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframes the anticipated dentist lecture as something Seamus can control, prompting him to generate his own script and boundaries. Could benefit from more explicit strategies such as double-sided reflections to further minimize the sustain talk's power.
