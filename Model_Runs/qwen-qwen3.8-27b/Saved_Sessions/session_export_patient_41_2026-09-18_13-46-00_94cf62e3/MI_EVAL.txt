# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard throughout the session, using validating phrases like 'That's not nothing' and 'You're not broken.' The non-judgmental, warm tone is maintained from the opening statement through every interaction, never dismissing Elira's self-consciousness or implying she should feel differently.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening captures both cognitive and somatic dimensions of Elira's experience with remarkable nuance. Standout reflections include 'You're not asking to feel good, you're asking for it to be allowed to be hard,' which precisely articulates a subtle distinction Elira was still forming.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from identifying the pattern to a concrete plan for Tuesday. Transitions between phases could be slightly more explicitly tied back to Elira's stated goal of 'stopping the loop' to tighten directional coherence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a true joint exploration, with the Tuesday plan fully co-constructed by Elira who generates the action, timing, and exit strategy. The psychiatrist's contributions are framed as observations and invitations rather than prescriptions, positioning both as equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elira's autonomy is consistently reinforced through phrases like 'I want this space to be yours,' 'No obligation. Just the line,' and 'You're giving yourself permission to try AND permission to stop.' The psychiatrist never imposes or pressures, and acknowledges uncertainty (noticing 'I guess') without pushing for firmer commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from within Elira through strategic questions that elicit her own problem-solving, concrete plans, and self-reflection. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change externally, instead creating conditions for Elira to access her own motivations and generate the Tuesday plan independently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently positioned as residing within Elira, with the psychiatrist acting as a curious partner using 'I'm noticing' and 'I'm wondering' language. The psychiatrist never provides advice or solutions, instead repeatedly returning to Elira as the source of answers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of strategic questions and amplifying reflections (e.g., 'You just built a version that actually fits how your brain works') successfully elicits and reinforces change talk. Could be slightly more systematic in explicitly eliciting confidence or ability language and drawing out more of Elira's own DARN-CAT statements beyond the implicit desire to stop the loop.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled with exceptional skill—concerns are validated while being gently reframed (e.g., 'That questioning is actually you thinking carefully, not avoiding'). The psychiatrist never argues against the voice or dismisses the anxiety, consistently honoring sustain talk while creating space for change.
