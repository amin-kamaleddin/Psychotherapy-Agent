# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and validating, but occasionally uses premature reassurance or a didactic tone. This limits the depth of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is adequate reflective listening, but reflections are often brief and quickly followed by advice. The counselor misses deeper empathic opportunities around identity loss and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the main concern, but often drifts into practical advice and logistics. This dilutes the MI focus on motivation and ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely counselor-driven, with frequent offers of plans and options. The psychiatrist does not consistently co-create direction with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers some choices and non-binding language, but also uses directive statements. This weakens the client's sense of ownership over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides more reasons for change than helps the client generate them. There are missed opportunities to evoke the client's own values and motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Moments of collaboration are present, but the psychiatrist frequently assumes an expert role. This limits the client's leadership in the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is present, but the counselor does not consistently amplify or deepen it. There are few MI-style questions to strengthen the client's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with sustain talk but shifts quickly to practical alternatives. This misses opportunities to reflect and redirect the client's ambivalence.
