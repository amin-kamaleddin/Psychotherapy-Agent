### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validating Elira's experience without judgment and never reinforcing her negative self-labels. Statements like 'you're allowed to be stuck in it' powerfully normalize her struggle.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy, including complex formulations like the 'double freeze' that capture the layered nature of Elira's avoidance. The psychiatrist tracks emotional shifts precisely and uses metaphors (bridge, double freeze) that resonate deeply with Elira.

### Direction

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative interaction where Elira's contributions shape the session direction, such as the mother insight and the 'opening the laptop' goal. The session feels like a joint exploration rather than a clinical interview.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Elira's autonomy through open-ended questions, exploratory framing ('I'm curious'), and explicit validation of her right to be stuck. The psychiatrist never imposes recommendations but invites Elira to explore her own possibilities.

**Comments:** Skillful use of evocative questions like 'what's the smallest version of just doing the thing?' that elicit Elira's own answers and motivations. Could have gone further in eliciting confidence talk or deeper change talk to maximize the evocation throughout.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary partnership that positions Elira as the expert on her own experience, with formulations offered as hypotheses rather than conclusions. 'You found that step on your own' explicitly credits Elira with her own insight.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes meaningful change talk, particularly 'I do want to be that person' and the concrete 'opening the laptop' step. Could have amplified and deepened this change talk further, such as reflecting back the strength in her desire or exploring confidence in the small step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk by validating feelings while gently introducing reframes, and contextualizes the 'snap out of it' narrative as coming from the mother. However, some sustain talk persists at the session's end, suggesting the self-label 'I'm lazy' was not fully dissolved from Elira's identity.
