# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong unconditional positive regard throughout; never labels Kieran as resistant despite flat affect and dismissive responses. Slightly focused on the therapeutic relationship rather than explicitly affirming Kieran's right to be tired or not ready.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy — tracks micro-cues and emotional subtext with high precision, reflecting the feeling behind words rather than just the content. The reframe of 'why bother' as a question rather than a wall is particularly masterful.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains appropriate focus on Kieran's experience but lacks a clear strategic arc toward a concrete goal or next step. Session ends at 'Yeah' without any consolidation or forward-looking element.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative language and real-time adjustment to Kieran's lead. However, the reflective and evocative work is somewhat one-directional, with Kieran remaining in minimal-response mode and no collaborative closing or joint summary.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with multiple explicit statements reinforcing freedom of choice and never pressuring for more engagement. 'For now' is honored as sufficient without trying to convert it into a commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation through open questions and turning Kieran's own words back to him; avoids giving advice or providing external reasons for change. Could be more targeted toward specific values, identity, or goals that might provide deeper motivational fuel.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI partnership — explicitly positions Kieran as the expert on his own experience and consistently lets his responses shape the direction. Power dynamic is explicitly addressed and rebalanced in the opening.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited ('not performing,' 'I'll... see') and appropriately reinforced, but it is not systematically developed or connected to deeper values or goals. Missed opportunities to explore what 'not performing' might look like in practice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argues against Kieran's sustain talk; uses acceptance and gentle curiosity to soften its impact effectively. Could be slightly more active in helping Kieran notice the gap between his stated experience and what he might actually want.
