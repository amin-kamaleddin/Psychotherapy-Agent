# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates respect and positive regard, validating Luka without judgment even when he admits not stopping drinking. However, the warmth occasionally feels slightly rehearsed (e.g., repeated 'that's real' patterns) and could be more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening with accurate captures of the 'buildup' metaphor and identity concerns. Could go deeper in reflecting the emotional vulnerability behind statements like 'I don't trust it on all days,' which was addressed more cognitively than emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a coherent, purposeful flow from check-in through warning signs, coping strategies, and alcohol exploration. Could benefit from a brief consolidation moment at the end to help Luka integrate the multiple threads (partner support, garage strategy, the 'number') into a coherent picture.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fundamentally a collaborative exploration; the psychiatrist invites input at every turn and genuinely incorporates Luka's answers into the direction of the conversation. The interaction feels like two people thinking together, not an expert extracting information.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength; the psychiatrist unconditionally affirms Luka's right to set his own pace and goals without any conditional language. Never implies cutting down is insufficient or that abstinence is the only valid goal.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within the client through skilled open questions that elicit Luka's own insights and metaphors. Could be deepened by more systematically targeting ability and need talk, such as scaling questions or reflections of past successful navigation of hard moments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary; the psychiatrist explicitly positions expertise in the client, never directs or introduces external recommendations, and consistently uses client-generated language as the working frame. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies the client's own change talk (e.g., 'you're not that guy anymore,' 'I can see myself doing it'). Could push more systematically on confidence and future-oriented language across all four DARN categories, particularly ability and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk by validating without pushing back, honoring Luka's distinction between 'that guy' and his current self. Could have used a double-sided reflection on the 'I don't trust it on all days' statement to hold both confidence and doubt more explicitly before moving forward.
