# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard through specific, genuine affirmations rather than formulaic praise. Directly counters Marcus's self-deprecation without condescension, and even when he backtracks, reframes positively while maintaining warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflections that go beyond content to capture emotional and relational dimensions (e.g., 'a whole different game' when the wife joins). Attuned to Marcus's self-deprecating tendency and fear of overthinking, catching these patterns in real-time.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior throughout with a logical progression from understanding the problem to identifying enjoyable activities to solidifying concrete commitment. The session has a clear arc from contemplation to preparation, managed with skilled pacing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marcus's ideas clearly shape the session direction at every stage, with the psychiatrist acting as a mirror and amplifier rather than a director. Joint language and co-construction of the plan are evident, with the closing question inviting Marcus to shape the conclusion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Marcus's ownership and freedom to choose, including explicitly offering the option of not acting ('letting that one breathe and coming back tomorrow'). Never pressures or implies obligation; all actions are framed as choices rather than requirements.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All change talk and motivation is elicited from Marcus through strategic questions rather than provided by the psychiatrist. The progression from 'why bother' to 'I'm kind of excited' to 'I'll just show up' is entirely client-generated, demonstrating skilled evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Marcus, using open questions to elicit his own knowledge rather than positioning as the expert with solutions. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian, with the closing explicitly attributing the strategy to Marcus's own insight.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk by reflecting back Marcus's own words with emphasis and highlighting his shift from 'why bother' to excitement. The psychiatrist never provides the change talk — it always comes from Marcus, which is the hallmark of skilled evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk exceptionally well by validating concerns and immediately redirecting toward what would fit, without ever arguing or minimizing. Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged, validated, and gently pivoted without force (e.g., 'it is' in response to 'stupidly small').
