# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a calm, respectful tone and acknowledges Dylan's frustration without judgment. However, acceptance remains neutral and often mirrors Dylan's minimization rather than affirming his worth or dignity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides adequate surface-level reflections of Dylan's perspective and distress. The reflections are generally accurate but somewhat repetitive and do not fully explore deeper emotions such as shame, fear, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the conduct matter and alcohol-related context, with occasional prompts toward the target behavior. However, the dominant direction remains procedural fairness rather than consistently guiding toward responsibility and safer alcohol-related choices.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interaction has a collaborative tone, with open-ended questions and willingness to follow Dylan's lead. The collaboration is limited, however, because there is little joint planning, shared agenda-setting, or co-construction of a common goal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Dylan's sense of choice and avoids pressure, directives, or threats. This strength is slightly limited because autonomy support is applied mainly to the conduct process rather than explicitly to Dylan's choices around drinking.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks several open-ended questions, but evocation is mostly limited to Dylan's feelings of unfairness and desire to have the matter dropped. There is insufficient strategic evocation of internal motivation, values, discrepancy, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Dylan's perspective and avoids a dominant expert stance. The partnership is uneven, however, because Dylan is not strongly positioned as the expert on his own life, values, or change process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few relevant questions that elicit some forward-leaning statements, such as staying away from a specific dorm or attending a meeting. Change talk is not actively cultivated, amplified, or consolidated throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** A major weakness is that Dylan's sustain talk, minimization, and externalization of responsibility are repeatedly reflected and accepted rather than gently softened or redirected. The session becomes centered on proving innocence rather than exploring personal responsibility, consequences, or safer behavior.
