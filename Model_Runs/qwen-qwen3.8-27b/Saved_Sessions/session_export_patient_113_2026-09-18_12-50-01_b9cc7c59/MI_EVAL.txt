### Evocation

**Comments:** All reasons for change are drawn from Rowan himself, never provided externally. The amplification 'That's you looking for a reason that's yours, not someone else's' masterfully reinforces intrinsic motivation.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Effectively evokes and builds on Rowan's change talk, particularly the articulation of 'catching things early before they become a mess.' Could push further by eliciting confidence statements or exploring what a manageable first step might feel like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
