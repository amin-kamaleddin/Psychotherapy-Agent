# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and normalizes Anika’s social/relaxing reasons for vaping. Acceptance is competent but could be stronger with more explicit warmth, positive regard, and individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate complex reflections of Anika’s ambivalence, especially the tension between peer fun and basketball performance. Empathy could be deeper by naming emotions such as social anxiety, fear of missing out, exhaustion, or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on vaping use and consistently connects it to basketball performance, sleep, stress, and eligibility. Direction is clear, but it could be strengthened with a final summary that ties goals, risks, plan, and next step together.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates by building on Anika’s own ideas, such as keeping the pen in her bag and using Sarah or video games as alternatives. Collaboration could be more fully mutual by explicitly negotiating the agenda or priorities with Anika.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure and generally respects Anika’s decision-making capacity. Autonomy support would be stronger with explicit language that the choice is entirely hers and no decision is required today.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Anika’s own motivations, especially her desire to play better and stay sharp. Evocation could be deeper by exploring identity values, confidence, commitment, or using importance/confidence rulers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and invites Anika to lead the discussion. Partnership could be more explicit by acknowledging Anika’s expertise in her own life and checking in on session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful change talk tied to basketball, including desire, ability, reason, and practical planning. Change talk could be further developed by exploring confidence, commitment, and broader personal values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Anika’s minimization and reflects her sustain talk nonjudgmentally. Handling would be stronger with double-sided reflections or gentle pivots that help Anika reframe minimization toward change.
