# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and validates Sofia’s privacy concerns, but moments of plan approval and commitment language can feel mildly directive. This slightly weakens the sense of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Sofia’s ambivalence and practical concerns about money, stress, and numbing. Deeper empathy could be shown by exploring underlying feelings such as shame, exclusion, and family expectations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing marijuana use and building a concrete short-term plan. The counselor effectively guides the conversation from ambivalence to a realistic, client-informed strategy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with Sofia’s preferences shaping the plan, scripts, and check-in format. Some counselor-led structure appears when multiple options and strategies are offered at once.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently supports Sofia’s choice, control, and privacy preferences. Some language, such as “pledge no use” and the fee showing commitment, introduces mild pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks helpful questions that elicit Sofia’s reasons for change and coping ideas. Evocation could be stronger by spending more time on Sofia’s values and internal motivation before moving to practical tools.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and invites Sofia’s input throughout. The partnership is slightly weakened by structured advice and a fairly detailed menu of suggested strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is able to articulate several change-related plans, including weekend abstinence, the jar, and coping tools. The counselor could strengthen change talk by elaborating on Sofia’s own statements before introducing additional suggestions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responds to sustain talk about social fit and calming effects without arguing or rejecting it. More nuanced, two-sided reflections could better validate belonging needs while exploring discrepancy with Sofia’s goals.
