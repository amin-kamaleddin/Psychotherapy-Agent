# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently nonjudgmental, validating, and accepts Ryan's ambivalence without labeling or criticizing. It is slightly less than ideal because the warmth is procedural and lacks deeper individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects the practical conflict between grades, social life, and drinking. It could be stronger by more explicitly reflecting shame, fear, and identity conflict behind Ryan's statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol-related academic and conduct concerns and narrows them into a concrete, manageable morning-after plan. It avoids drift while maintaining an appropriate target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input, adapts to Ryan's feedback, and helps co-create a practical plan. The score is limited by frequent therapist-led option lists and structured questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses choice-focused language, respects Ryan's rejections, and frames the plan as optional and experimental. This clearly supports Ryan's ownership without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions, importance questions, and confidence scaling to elicit Ryan's goals and motivation. The main gap is insufficient deep exploration of Ryan's values, meaning, and internal reasons for change before moving to logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly treats Ryan as the expert and frequently invites his perspective. Partnership is somewhat limited because the interaction is still more guided by therapist-generated options than fully co-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out some change talk around the degree, spacing drinking, and confidence scaling. The score is limited because the psychiatrist does not consistently deepen, amplify, or expand Ryan's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles Ryan's sustain talk with normalization and avoids argument or direct confrontation. It could be stronger by using more double-sided reflections before offering concrete alternatives.
