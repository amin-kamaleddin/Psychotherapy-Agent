# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, normalizing, and free of judgment or shaming throughout. Even when Camila self-criticizes, the psychiatrist responds with validation and reframing rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture emotional undercurrents and functional patterns with precision. Reflections move beyond simple paraphrase into complex, meaning-focused responses that deepen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear strategic arc from vague desire to stop, through identifying function and barrier, to the client generating her own concrete solution. Maintains focus on alcohol reduction while following the client's lead with excellent pacing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as a joint exploration with a clearly egalitarian power dynamic. Psychiatrist's reflections are offered as hypotheses for Camila to confirm or correct, not as interpretations to be accepted.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Camila's ownership of the change process and her right to make her own choices. Never imposes solutions or uses directive language; everything is framed as her choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight in the session is generated by Camila herself; the psychiatrist creates conditions for self-discovery rather than providing reasons for change. The final solution (removing the bottle) is a textbook example of evocation at its highest level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client consistently positioned as the expert in her own life; every question is directed at Camila's own knowledge, values, and preferences. Her contributions substantially shape the nature and direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk through purposeful questions and affirmations. Could push further into exploring Camila's confidence, ability, and specific commitment language across all DARN categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully with acknowledgment and immediate pivoting to explore possibility. Could have used a more explicit double-sided reflection to more fully honor and then redirect the ambivalence.
