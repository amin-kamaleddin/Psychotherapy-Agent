# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Elena without judgment, normalizing her protective minimization as understandable rather than labeling it. Warmth is genuine and specific, with a clear acknowledgment of trust when Elena reveals a vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening consistently goes one level deeper than what Elena states, capturing subtle emotional cues like the 'silly' in her voice and the 'weird in a good way.' The psychiatrist names the protective function behind minimization and the disarming nature of being seen clearly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, logical progression from concrete examples to deeper patterns to relational insights without drifting. However, it could benefit from a stronger sense of forward momentum toward future action or concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session exemplifies full collaboration with the conversation shaped entirely by Elena's responses, never imposing a topic or agenda. The 'I can argue with it' exchange is particularly strong, expanding on Elena's insight rather than taking ownership of it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never gives advice and consistently frames Elena as the agent of her own change, attributing agency explicitly ('You identified what was missing, and then you actually gave it to yourself'). Autonomy support is woven naturally into reflections rather than feeling like a separate intervention.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended, invitational questions effectively draw out Elena's own motivations and language rather than providing them. The session would benefit from slightly more future-oriented evocation (e.g., 'What would you want this to look like in a month?') to push toward action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Elena as the expert on her own experience, using phrases like 'I want to hear what it looks like from your side.' The power dynamic is balanced at every turn, with the psychiatrist reflecting, asking, and following Elena's lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively highlights and amplifies Elena's own change talk language ('show up for myself,' 'that counted') and elicits statements of realization and confidence. The session is primarily maintenance/strengthening, limiting scope for evoking new change talk, but could push further into future-oriented change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** When Elena minimizes her progress, the psychiatrist responds with a textbook double-sided reflection that acknowledges her perspective while gently pushing back against minimization. Validating the protective function before exploring it softens the sustain talk naturally without being dismissive.
