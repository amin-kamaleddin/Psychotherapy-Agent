# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, nonjudgmental engagement throughout with strong validation of Rosa's feelings and ambivalence. Occasionally veers into slightly performative warmth that could feel less grounded, but overall tone is respectful and supportive without being saccharine.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that captures both cognitive and emotional complexity of Rosa's experience with precision. Reflections go beyond surface paraphrasing into genuine understanding of her internal world, including nuanced distinctions like external judgment versus internal self-perception.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear and consistent focus on smoking cessation with a logical arc from ambivalence to identity loss to social concerns to self-compassion to slip management. Could benefit from more explicit strategic summaries at transitions to strengthen the sense of purposeful guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance with inclusive, non-hierarchical language and a genuine peer-like dynamic. The self-disclosure about the all-or-nothing pattern and the 'what would a friend say' question create a session that feels like a true partnership rather than a clinical encounter.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never tells Rosa what to do, never suggests specific interventions, and consistently affirms her right to choose without judgment or pressure. Language like 'this is your space' and 'you don't have to resolve this today' models exceptional respect for Rosa's ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions are well-designed to draw out Rosa's own values, motivations, and self-knowledge rather than providing reasons externally. Could be deepened by more explicit exploration of long-term vision and by more deliberately amplifying the change talk that emerges.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Rosa as the expert on her own life with language that communicates answers already reside within her. Occasionally guides the conversation slightly more directive than a pure partnership model would require, but the overall stance is strongly collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits organic change talk including the 'I've got to stop' recognition, self-compassion language, and identification of the all-or-nothing pattern. Could more explicitly reinforce and amplify these change statements and explore Rosa's confidence more directly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging losses and fears without arguing or minimizing. The strategic reframing of social rejection fear into a manageable internal self-perception concern is a subtle but effective softening technique.
