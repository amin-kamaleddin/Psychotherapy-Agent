# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment while discussing risky behaviors. The client is treated with unconditional positive regard even when exploring doubt and relapse risk.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers highly accurate reflections that capture both content and underlying feeling. Responses track the client’s ambivalence, exhaustion, and fear of failure without overinterpreting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing drinking and managing anger in high-risk moments. The clinician efficiently narrows broad goals into specific, actionable targets.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is genuinely collaborative, with the plan emerging from the client’s own suggestions. The psychiatrist refines and builds on the client’s ideas rather than imposing a predetermined approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports the client’s freedom to choose and avoids pressure toward abstinence or a rigid plan. Language consistently reinforces ownership and permission to proceed at the client’s pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist largely elicits motivation from within the client through open, client-centered questions. Some therapist-directed framing limits the score by reducing space for the client to define values and next steps more independently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly conveys that the client is the expert on what feels right. The client’s judgments substantially shape the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks open questions that help the client articulate goals, preferences, and agency. The main gap is not consistently amplifying or affirming strong change talk when it appears.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk by acknowledging concerns without arguing or dismissing them. The responses could be more strategic with explicit, balanced reflections that more clearly reduce the pull of sustain talk.
