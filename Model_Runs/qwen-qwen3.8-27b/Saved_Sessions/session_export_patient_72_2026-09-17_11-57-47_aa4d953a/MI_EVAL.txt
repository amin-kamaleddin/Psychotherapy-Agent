# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with validation, normalization, and no labeling or disapproval. Explicitly removes implicit judgment (e.g., 'a place you can land, not a test').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurately reflects both content and underlying emotion, progressing from simple to complex reflections. Demonstrates genuine curiosity about the client's internal experience rather than mere paraphrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains unwavering focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from exploration to preparation to action planning. Zero topic drift; each question builds momentum toward concrete action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conversation structured as a genuine dialogue between equals with no instance of the counselor driving with their own agenda. Co-constructs understanding with the client rather than delivering insight.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question framed as an invitation rather than a directive, and the client's commitment is elicited rather than prescribed. Explicitly removes coercive connotations from the accountability structure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All significant change talk is generated by the client in response to evocative questions. The counselor never provides reasons for change or fills silence with advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert on her own life, affirming her self-knowledge and insights. The session is shaped entirely by the client's contributions, with no unsolicited advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully and consistently elicits all four forms of change talk (desire, ability, reasons, commitment) through targeted open questions. The counselor never inserts their own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledges barriers without reinforcing them and elicits the client's own solutions. Minor missed opportunity to use a double-sided reflection to more actively soften the pull of the status quo around home distractions.
