# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard without ever pathologizing or expressing disapproval of Matteo's drinking. Explicitly rejects labeling and validates his right to feel as he does throughout the entire session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate complex reflections that capture the precise quality of Matteo's ambivalence and emotional shifts. Reflections go beyond paraphrase to articulate the cognitive and emotional complexity of his experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical progression from the ankle entry point through exploring the Wednesday pattern to identifying a concrete starting point. However, the session ends somewhat open-endedly without explicitly consolidating the target behavior or setting a clear focus for next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a genuine collaboration with inclusive language and joint exploration throughout. The psychiatrist builds on Matteo's contributions rather than redirecting, and even tracks the process meta-level ('I notice you're not jumping to conclusions').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Matteo's ownership of choices without creating urgency or implying obligation. Honors his own language ('rearranging a little') without trying to make it sound bigger, and explicitly removes pressure to decide in the moment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Matteo's own motivations using his own words and experience rather than providing reasons for change. Could be strengthened by more systematically linking change to his core values and personal identity to deepen the motivational contrast.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Matteo as the authority on his own life ('You know your friend group better than I do') and explicitly rejects the expert role. The session is structured around Matteo's contributions driving the direction, with the psychiatrist functioning as a guide.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully guides Matteo from 'I'm not a problem' to 'I'm just rearranging a little' through evocative questions and effective amplification of emerging change talk. Could be further strengthened by explicitly eliciting confidence or ability language (e.g., a confidence scale) to solidify self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk non-confrontationally using acceptance-based responses that leave space for change talk to emerge naturally. Could be enhanced with more explicit double-sided reflections that hold both sides simultaneously to create productive cognitive tension.
