# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently shows respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. Validation is strong and avoids criticism or minimizing the client's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence and mixed feelings. Some responses stay more cognitive or problem-focused rather than fully reflecting the emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on medication adherence. Work and family are used as context, not distractions, and the conversation progresses logically toward a plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor works as an equal partner rather than an advisor. The final plan is co-created by building on Selene's own idea.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor supports choice, avoids pressure, and normalizes imperfect follow-through. There is no coercion or attempt to force commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor largely draws out Selene's own motivation and solutions. Occasional counselor-led reframes or suggestions slightly reduce the purity of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is clearly positioned as the expert on her own life. The session is driven by her constraints, ideas, and preferred solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively amplifies Selene's desire, intention, and planning language. It would be stronger with more systematic elicitation of importance, confidence, and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles minimization and rationalization without argument or blame. Some sustain talk remains unexamined and could be more fully connected to the client's stated health values.
