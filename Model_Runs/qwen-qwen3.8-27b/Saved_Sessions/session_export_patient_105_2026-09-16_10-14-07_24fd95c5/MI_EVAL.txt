# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys consistent warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental acceptance of Naomi’s anxiety and hesitation. It falls short of a 5 because the acceptance remains somewhat procedural and does not fully affirm her identity, values, or strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate and helpful reflections of Naomi’s physical symptoms, self-consciousness, and ambivalence. Deeper emotional meaning, such as grief about losing enjoyment or identity threat, is not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on a concrete, observable target: managing acute anxiety during photography. The psychiatrist effectively narrows the work to a discreet, doable behavior without drifting or over-pathologizing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative because Naomi’s preferences and choices shape the final plan. However, the psychiatrist still generates much of the agenda and many of the specific behavioral options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy through invitations, options, and respectful responses to Naomi’s refusals. It would benefit from stronger explicit reinforcement of her freedom to modify or abandon the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation occurs through preference and scaling questions, which elicit useful client-generated details. The session does not consistently draw out Naomi’s values, hopes, identity, or deeper motivation for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and allows Naomi to choose among options. The partnership is somewhat limited because the psychiatrist remains the primary source of techniques and structured solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change-oriented language through planning and confidence questions. More frequent reflection, affirmation, and amplification of Naomi’s own reasons, abilities, and desires for change are needed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk by validating concerns, avoiding argument, and offering smaller, low-pressure alternatives. Some doubt is accepted rather than consistently redirected into change talk.
