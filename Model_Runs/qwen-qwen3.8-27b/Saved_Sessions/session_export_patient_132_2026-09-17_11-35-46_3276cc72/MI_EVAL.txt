# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, nonjudgmental stance, and validation throughout, though occasionally slightly performative rather than deeply individualized. Lacks a moment of deeper emotional attunement to fully embody unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with complex reflections consistently capturing both content and emotional meaning. Progressively deepening reflections that are varied and never repetitive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with logical session progression. Could have maintained tighter strategic momentum with a bridging summary in the middle.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is genuinely co-constructed with Yasmin's ideas consistently shaping where the conversation goes next. Textbook equal power dynamic with clinician as question-asker rather than teacher.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All suggestions framed as explorations of possibility with no directives or conditional language. Consistent, explicit autonomy support woven naturally throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent and varied evocation using open questions, scaling, and reflective questions to draw out client's own motivations. Produces rich, authentic change talk without any didactic input.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently locates expertise in Yasmin, never explains or lectures, and lets her language and insights drive the session. Exemplary non-authoritarian mirror-and-question-asker stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocative questions successfully elicit multiple instances of significant change language and commitment statements. Could have more explicitly reinforced and amplified the change talk when it appeared.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of double-sided reflections to validate the sustain talk while gently introducing the change perspective. Handling is effective but not consistently at the highest level of strategic redirection.
