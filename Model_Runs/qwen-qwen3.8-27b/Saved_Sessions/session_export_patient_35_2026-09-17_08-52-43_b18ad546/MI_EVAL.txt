# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, with affirmations that validate Elena’s values and efforts. Minor limitation is some reassurance-like language, but overall acceptance remains strong.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing stress, protectiveness, and need for a break. Main gap is not directly reflecting the hurt or defensiveness when Elena feels unfairly blamed for “poisoning” her son.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays generally focused on smoking reduction and secondhand smoke exposure, moving toward a concrete, workable target. Direction could be sharpened by locking in the specific behavioral target earlier with a more concise summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Elena’s reality and uses her constraints to shape the plan. The porch-first plan is clearly co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong emphasis on Elena’s choice, ownership, and right to define the limit of change. Avoids pressure, tracking, or controlling language while respecting her refusal to commit to a full quit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent open questions elicit Elena’s own motivations, cues, and solutions, supporting a client-generated plan. Could be stronger by giving her more space to elaborate her own meaning before the psychiatrist names or interprets it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena is consistently positioned as the expert on her own life and choices. The intervention is developed collaboratively, with her input guiding each step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and strengthens change talk around motivation, ability, and a concrete action. Could be enhanced by more explicitly eliciting commitment, confidence, and readiness talk to deepen engagement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by avoiding argument and respecting Elena’s need to keep her break. Some reflections may subtly reinforce the value of the smoking break, so stronger double-sided reflections could better pivot toward change.
