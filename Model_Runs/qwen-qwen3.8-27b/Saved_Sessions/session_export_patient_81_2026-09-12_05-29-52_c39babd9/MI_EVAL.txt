### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates the client’s experience and self-awareness. Some responses remain practical rather than fully affirming deeper identity concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections of ambivalence and practical tensions around rent, auditions, and social life. Deeper emotional meanings such as fear of losing belonging or identity are not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps clear focus on methamphetamine use and connects it to money, career, and social pressure with a logical progression to a concrete plan. Occasionally moves too quickly into tactical problem-solving before fully processing ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as shared and asks for the client’s input on plan details, producing client-selected choices. Still frequently supplies the structure and menu of options rather than allowing fully client-generated exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice and avoids pressure, allowing the client to decide the cash amount, refusal line, and text. Repeated multiple-choice prompts can channel the client into pre-selected options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Draws out useful client-generated ideas and confidence changes, especially around the refusal line and contacting Mateo. Often shifts to practical suggestions before fully evoking deeper values and self-image.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites the client’s agenda and uses collaborative language at the start. The tendency to present structured option lists makes the interaction more expert-guided than fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client expresses meaningful change talk about being present, protecting rent, and choosing a low-key refusal. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or build on the strongest change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing or judging, including concerns about the vibe and belonging. Does not fully hold or reframe ambivalence before moving into practical planning.
