# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates warmth, respect, and non-judgment throughout the session. However, expressions of positive regard sometimes feel slightly rehearsed rather than deeply personal and spontaneous.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening with accurate complex reflections that capture the emotional weight of Maya's experience. Reflections are accurate but occasionally stay at the cognitive level rather than fully accessing the emotional core.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistent and logical focus on alcohol use and its context, with each question building on the previous response. The session stays in exploration mode without strategically guiding toward considering change or building momentum for next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and frames the conversation as a joint exploration. However, the collaboration is somewhat one-directional in practice, with the counselor leading the exploration while Maya responds rather than substantially reshaping the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Maya's autonomy with clear non-directive language and explicit affirmations of her right to make her own choices. Could be strengthened by more explicitly reinforcing her agency in moments of guilt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks open questions that elicit some client-centered insights and appropriately highlights Maya's self-identified cycle. However, evocation is limited in depth and does not systematically draw out Maya's values, hopes, or confidence in her ability to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveys a partnership stance and positions Maya as the authority on her own experience. The overall structure still follows a predictable therapeutic arc rather than being genuinely co-created in real-time.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately amplifies some change talk and asks forward-looking questions. Misses opportunities to deepen rich change talk (e.g., values and loss statements) and does not systematically elicit DARN-CAT categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing it, and appropriately recognizes the function of drinking. Does not use more advanced strategies such as double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance to actively soften sustain talk.
