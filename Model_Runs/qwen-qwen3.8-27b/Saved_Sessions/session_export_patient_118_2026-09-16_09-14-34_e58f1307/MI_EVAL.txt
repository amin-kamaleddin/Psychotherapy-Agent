# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating stance while avoiding pressure to quit smoking. Acceptance is consistent but somewhat procedural and could be warmer or more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Seamus’s frustration with lectures, past difficulty with pills, use of smoking for stress, and concern for his wife. Empathy is present but remains somewhat surface-level and could explore emotional weight more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session has clear clinical direction, but after Seamus’s resistance it shifts mainly to dental logistics. The counselor does not consistently guide the conversation back to smoking cessation or connect dental goals with change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves as an equal partner, offering options and allowing Seamus to shape the dental plan. Collaboration is strong, practical, and respectful of the client’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is very strong, with repeated emphasis on choice, no pressure, and respect for Seamus’s decisions. The counselor also supports client control during the procedure by honoring pauses and preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initial questions invite Seamus’s views, but the counselor does not continue eliciting internal motivation after resistance. There is little exploration of values, readiness, confidence, or future-focused reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and invites Seamus’s input throughout. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicitly treating Seamus as the expert on his own life and readiness.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few initial attempts to elicit change talk, but the counselor does not follow up or amplify Seamus’s change-relevant statements. Techniques such as scaling, importance/confidence questions, or future-focused reflections are largely absent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and acknowledges Seamus’s sustain talk without becoming defensive. However, he does not use double-sided reflections or skillfully pivot ambivalence toward change.
