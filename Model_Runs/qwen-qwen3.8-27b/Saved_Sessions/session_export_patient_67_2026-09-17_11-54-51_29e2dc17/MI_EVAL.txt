# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warm, unconditional positive regard, validating both sides of Kenji's experience without hierarchy and explicitly honoring his 'not yet' as his honest place rather than a wall.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is consistently accurate and emotionally nuanced, capturing both behavioral shifts and their emotional significance while holding ambivalence in both hands simultaneously across the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use with a logical progression from ambivalence to a concrete, client-identified action step, never drifting into unrelated territory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as genuine collaboration where Kenji drives the content and the psychiatrist drives the process, with reflections offered as invitations rather than imposed interpretations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces Kenji's ownership and right to choose, never prescribing or pressuring, with the culminating statement 'not for a goal — just for yourself' serving as masterful autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight Kenji articulates is evoked through questions pointing him back to his own experience rather than external reasons for change, culminating in a fully client-generated action plan and rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions themselves as a partner in exploration rather than an expert, using 'I'm curious' language and following Kenji's leads without assuming authority over his experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk emerges progressively from concern to desire to recognition to intention through well-designed questions, though the session could have pushed slightly further into confidence and ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully with double-sided reflections that validate without amplifying, though a more explicit naming of the push-pull on the 'it's not a big deal' moment could further soften its grip.
