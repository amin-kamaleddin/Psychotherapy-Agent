# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalized Camila’s concerns. Acceptance could be stronger with more individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Camila’s ambivalence, fatigue, and worries. The therapist sometimes moved quickly from reflection to planning, limiting deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on increasing after-lunch movement. The conversation steadily narrowed into a clear, workable plan with minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist co-constructed the plan and invited Camila to choose cues, tracking, and follow-up. Some therapist-driven structure kept the collaboration from being fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist offered options, asked permission, and reduced pressure by shortening the trial. At times, the proposed tracking and structure felt somewhat directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist elicited some concrete change ideas from Camila. However, deeper values, hopes, and confidence were not consistently drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist treated Camila as a partner by asking for input and shaping the plan around her preferences. The session remained somewhat counselor-guided due to detailed menus of options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Camila produced useful change talk through her chosen cues and backup plan. The therapist could have more consistently amplified her own reasons, benefits, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubts and fear of failure were normalized and reframed rather than challenged. The experiment structure could have been de-emphasized further to soften the concerns even more.
