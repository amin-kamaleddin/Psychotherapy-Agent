# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, nonjudgment, and positive regard while avoiding shaming. Warmth is solid but could be more individualized and emotionally attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some accurate reflections but mostly stays factual or plan-based. Deeper emotional themes, such as feeling flat afterward and fearing that something is missing, are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Focus remains clear on reducing evening wine use and building a concrete plan. The session is practical and on target, though it is more behavior-planning than MI-guided exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Claudia’s ideas, but frequently adds lists and additional steps that can feel expert-driven. More equal power-sharing and checking whether she wants practical or exploratory work would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Claudia is allowed to choose goals, reject tracking, and create her own rules and phrases. The amount of directive advice and scripts slightly reduces emphasis on her self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and plan choices draw out some client-generated goals and coping language. However, there are many missed opportunities to evoke deeper motivation, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Claudia’s preferences shape the plan, but the psychiatrist often leads with detailed suggestions and expert content. More explicit power-sharing and client-driven agenda-setting are needed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms some change statements but does not consistently expand them with evocative questions. More work is needed to draw out desire, need, importance, and confidence talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and accepts a reduction goal rather than abstinence. It would be stronger if double-sided reflections were used more actively to resolve ambivalence.
