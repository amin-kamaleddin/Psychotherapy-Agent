# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental, normalizing stance with affirmations of the client's effort and practicality. Missed opportunity to explicitly meet the final fear of failure with acceptance or self-compassion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflections accurately captured the client's ambivalence, pressure, and need for a low-pressure space. Left the final emotional content about fear of missing days and self-criticism unreflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior with logical progression from broad overwhelm to one manageable step, a simple rule, a reliable cue, and barrier planning. No drift into unrelated topics throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client treated as equal co-author; frequent collaborative language such as "If we were to pick one piece." The entire plan was naturally co-constructed from the client's own routines, preferences, and metaphors.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's freedom to choose and framed all suggestions as options rather than prescriptions. Strong ownership language including "You know your own limits and schedule better than I do" and "permission to write 'weird.'"

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective open questions and affirmations drew out increasingly proactive client language about what is doable. Evocation was primarily practical and behavior-focused rather than values-based or confidence-based; final sustain talk was not met with an evocative response.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent expression of partnership with "You know your own limits and schedule better than I do" and consistently collaborative questioning. The client's ideas shaped every element of the session from problem identification to final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent elicitation of client-generated change talk and planning language with effective affirmations amplifying those statements. Did not explicitly elicit deeper DARN change talk or importance/confidence rulers; final sustain talk was not followed by a change-talk evocation attempt.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several effective softening moves including reframing as "not a test" and "low-pressure practice." Inconsistent application, and the critical final sustain talk about missed days and fear of feeling "back to zero" was left entirely unaddressed.
