# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates nonjudgmental warmth and unconditional positive regard, normalizing and validating Anara's ambivalence. There are no signs of labeling, shaming, or pressure, with only a minor opportunity to explicitly affirm her worth or parenting capacity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurately captures multiple aspects of Anara's experience, including the tension between health concerns and caregiving. However, the empathy could be deeper by more fully reflecting the emotional burden of possible illness and caregiver anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on the target behavior of getting medical care. Each turn logically advances toward a concrete, manageable first step without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acts as a collaborative partner by co-constructing a plan from Anara's own ideas rather than imposing one. The client's language and choices shape the session, demonstrating a true collaborative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through nonpressuring questions and by keeping decision-making with the client. This could be further strengthened by explicitly stating that there is no obligation and the choice to call remains entirely hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes the client's own reasons, ideas, and solutions, leading her to generate a specific plan. The evocation could be deepened by eliciting more intrinsic motivation, confidence, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Anara as the expert on her own life and needs, driving the session with open-ended questions. The client's ideas clearly shape the direction of the work, reflecting a strong partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist actively evokes and reinforces important change-focused statements from the client, helping her see her plan as viable. The session could more fully develop language around importance, ability, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled as a major strength, with the psychiatrist validating and gently reframing the client's fears and avoidance as protective strategies. This effectively reduces blocking effects and supports movement toward a doable next step.
