# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently nonjudgmental and normalizes ambivalence. Warmth is strong but sometimes tied to plan completion rather than unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture frustration, ambivalence, and fear of failure. Deeper exploration of the personal meaning of running and identity is a missed opportunity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior and a concrete action step. Even related topics such as bedtime and phone use stay connected to re-establishing exercise.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed with Dante selecting and modifying key elements. The psychiatrist still provides many options, making the partnership somewhat consultant-like.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Dante’s choice is repeatedly emphasized and respected throughout. Specific suggestions can subtly shape the plan rather than leaving more space for his own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes motivation, confidence, and self-generated commitments. More open-ended questioning before offering solutions would strengthen this area.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and follows Dante’s lead in narrowing the plan. Greater explicit deference to Dante’s expertise and more client-generated options would improve this.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited and reinforced. Deeper DARN-CAT questioning is needed to strengthen Dante’s own reasons and ability for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and gently redirected toward small steps. More consistent double-sided reflections and pivots to change talk would improve this.
