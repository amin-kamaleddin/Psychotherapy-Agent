# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows respect, warmth, and nonjudgmental support while normalizing Julian's stress. Acceptance remains somewhat formulaic and task-oriented rather than deeply validating ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some accurate reflections of Julian's stress, phone anxiety, and fear of making a mistake. Empathy is inconsistent and often interrupted by quick advice rather than deeper exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior of completing court-ordered hours by the deadline. The counselor efficiently moves from barriers to practical scheduling and documentation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaboration is evident in role-play and adapting to Julian's preference for texting. The counselor still carries much of the conversation through detailed plans, scripts, and instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, asks for preference, and avoids direct pressure. Frequent prescriptive advice and reassurance can undercut Julian's sense of autonomous ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** This is the weakest area, with the session relying more on advice, scripts, and logistics than eliciting Julian's own values or reasons for change. The counselor rarely draws out importance, confidence, or personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Julian is invited to contribute and is given some options, but the session remains largely expert-led. More equal power sharing and co-creation would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor praises role-play and affirms readiness, but does not consistently amplify Julian's own change talk. Questions could better evoke his reasons for acting, confidence, and personal stakes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoids arguing and normalizes Julian's worries while redirecting toward manageable action. Some responses are quick reassurance rather than reflective exploration of ambivalence.
