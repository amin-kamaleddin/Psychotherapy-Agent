# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard from the first utterance, never labels or judges Elena's behavior. Explicitly validates her as a caring parent and responds with warmth even when she minimizes or defends.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate empathic understanding that captures both content and underlying emotion, going beyond surface-level paraphrasing. Reflections are varied (simple, complex, double-sided) and consistently hit the emotional core of Elena's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical progression from acknowledging the situation to understanding the function of smoking to exploring potential alternatives. Could benefit from slightly more explicit connection to a concrete decision point or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a joint exploration, actively seeking Elena's input before offering reflections. The session direction is shaped substantially by Elena's own contributions (the kitchen, the window, the signal).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Elena's autonomy, validating her pushback on 'just stop' and never prescribing solutions. Respects her pace and frames all exploration as her choice, reinforcing her role as the decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Elena's own motivations through open questions and strategic reflections rather than providing external reasons for change. Could benefit from more deliberate evocation of confidence/ability talk or scaling questions to assess readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Elena, explicitly positioning her as the expert on her own life. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with Elena's contributions substantially shaping the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively creates conditions for Elena to generate her own change-oriented language through open questions and affirmations. Could be more strategic in amplifying her desire for change or using summaries to highlight change talk versus sustain talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk with double-sided reflections and redirects focus from justification to function without challenging or arguing. Could be slightly more strategic in explicitly pivoting toward the change side of ambivalence after sustained sustain talk.
