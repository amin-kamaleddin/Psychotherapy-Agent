# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance with repeated affirmations of Liora's effort and distress. The affirmations are somewhat formulaic, and frequent crisis-language insertions subtly undermine unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture surface content and basic emotions but remain largely factual. They miss opportunities for complex reflections of Liora's confusion, metaphorical pain, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on reducing self-harm and immediate safety, with logical progression from assessment to action steps. It lacks a broader MI strategic arc toward values and sustained change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaboration appears in offering choices and joint next steps, but the psychiatrist generates most interventions and content. Liora mostly selects from a pre-selected menu rather than contributing her own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly offers choices and avoids pressure, respecting Liora's freedom to decide. Autonomy is occasionally weakened by directive instructions, a pre-written text, and repeated external crisis directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is limited to the opening question and does not develop throughout the session. The psychiatrist provides behavioral options rather than eliciting Liora's own motivations, values, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partnership language is present, but the session is more expert-led, with the psychiatrist supplying most substantive interventions and a draft message. Liora's expertise in her own experience is not fully used.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is not strategically cultivated; when Liora notices the ice makes things quieter, it is not explored. The focus remains on behavioral compliance rather than evoking her own language for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without direct argument, but softening is limited. The psychiatrist does not use double-sided reflections or explore the costs and deeper ambivalence around cutting.
