# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout; the psychiatrist explicitly affirms Kevin's worth and autonomy without ever labeling or expressing disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are specific, nuanced, and progressively deepen from surface content to underlying meaning, accurately capturing both the tension in Kevin's words and his unspoken social costs.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from the injury through Kevin's framing to his own insight, while staying focused on the target behavior. The session ended without a brief collaborative summary or concrete next step, which slightly weakened the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kevin's ideas consistently shaped the session's direction, and the power dynamic was genuinely equal — the psychiatrist positioned themselves as a curious partner rather than an authority throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported at multiple points — the psychiatrist never imposed a plan, respected Kevin's 'I'm not looking to stop,' and reinforced that Kevin owns his insights and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively drew out Kevin's own insights (especially the 'not a choice, a trade-off' reframe) rather than providing reasons for change. Kevin's concrete 'two max' plan could have been further evoked and developed before the session ended.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kevin was consistently positioned as the expert on his own life, with the closing 'you're the one who saw it, not me' powerfully reinforcing his ownership of the session's key insight.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questions effectively evoked genuine change talk ('what if next time it's not just a sprain?', 'two max'), and the psychiatrist built on these moments. However, the 'two max' idea was not explored to its full potential before the session moved on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was validated without reinforcement and effectively reframed (e.g., willpower → social trade-off). A double-sided reflection near the end could have better integrated Kevin's sustain talk and emerging change talk into a coherent self-narrative.
