# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout the conversation. They validate the client's perspective and avoid moralizing about smoking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures the client's behavior and underlying experience. The clinician could deepen exploration of identity, values, and emotional meaning beyond stress relief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on a specific, manageable behavior: smoking in the morning. It would benefit from a stronger link between this micro-step and broader health-related goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is highly collaborative, with the psychiatrist co-constructing the conversation with the client. The client's ideas directly shape the discussion and action plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a major strength, with consistent emphasis on the client's freedom to choose. Suggestions are framed as optional and client-driven, reducing reactance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client's own reasons and behavioral logic for change. There is room to further evoke deeper motivation related to values or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions the client as the expert on their own life and decisions. The collaborative style avoids imposing an agenda and respects the client's lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician skillfully guides the client to articulate small, owned changes. They could further strengthen change talk by explicitly highlighting the client's ability or confidence in making the change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Resistance and sustain talk are handled well by reframing around autonomy and avoiding argument. The session could more strongly redirect minimization toward broader personal values or health motivation.
