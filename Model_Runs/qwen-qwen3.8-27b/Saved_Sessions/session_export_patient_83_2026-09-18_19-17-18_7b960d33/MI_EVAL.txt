# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations throughout the session. Affirmations sometimes feel slightly formulaic and lack explicit normalization of ambivalence to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening, accurately capturing Mai's emotional state and self-knowledge. Reflections occasionally focus more on cognitive content than deeper emotional layers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from understanding to commitment. A few tangential points (e.g., mom's experience) could have been more explicitly tied back to the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as joint exploration and actively seeks Mai's input on structure and preferences. Still carries a slight facilitator undertone rather than full equal co-creation of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support woven naturally into every interaction — no pressure language, offers choices, validates Mai's self-knowledge, and frames the target as her own. A model of autonomy support in MI.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Mai's own motivations, interpretations, and goals through well-timed open questions. Could be deeper with more explicit elicitation of values-based and ability-based change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites Mai's input and positions her as the expert on her own preferences. Could be stronger by more explicitly negotiating the agenda and deferring to Mai's expertise throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and reinforces Mai's spontaneous change talk, amplifying commitments and framing the target as her own definition. Could be more systematic in eliciting deeper change talk language (values, ability, need).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Mai's confusion and mild skepticism well by validating feelings while providing context, without arguing or dismissing. Could have used double-sided reflections more explicitly to fully honor both sides.
