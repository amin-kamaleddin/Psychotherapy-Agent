# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, immediately normalizing and removing shame from the opening. The counselor never pathologizes, labels, or minimizes the client's experience, meeting each disclosure with warmth and affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are precise, nuanced, and progressively deepen, capturing emotional truths the client hadn't yet articulated (e.g., 'That's a lonely kind of present'). The counselor never misses or misreads the client's emotional state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear thematic focus with a logical narrative arc from defensiveness to self-insight to a vision of authentic self. Slightly lacks a closing consolidation or summary that would tie the session's themes together and provide a sense of completion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine collaboration where the counselor co-constructs meaning using the client's exact words and metaphors ('background character,' 'performing,' 'the loop'). Power sharing is complete; the session's shape is determined entirely by the client's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's agency and choice, framing all insights as her own discoveries rather than the counselor's. No prescriptive or directive language; every question is an invitation, not a demand.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is designed to elicit motivation from within the client through a sophisticated progression (normalize → externalize → evoke cost → evoke insight → evoke identity-based change talk). All change talk is evoked, not implanted.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly positions the client as the expert on her own life and uses genuinely open, collaborative language throughout. The power dynamic is clearly equal with no information advantage or imposed agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through self-observation, reframing, and future-self questions rather than generic 'benefits of change' prompts. Change talk emerges naturally and intensifies from 'I know my limit' to 'I'd just... be a person.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with curiosity and normalization rather than challenging or dismissing it. Could be strengthened with more explicit double-sided reflections that acknowledge the social pressure while highlighting the personal cost of blending in.
