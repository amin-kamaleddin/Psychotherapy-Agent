# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides consistent, respectful, nonjudgmental reflections and avoids moralizing. Acceptance is strong but somewhat procedural rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers accurate reflections of ambivalence, worry, and strength, tracking the client’s perspective well. It would improve by exploring deeper emotional meaning, such as vulnerability or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused and moves strategically from ambivalence to a concrete next step. It could be strengthened by connecting this step more explicitly to broader re-entry goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates outstanding collaboration by co-constructing the plan and giving the client control over wording and boundaries. The client’s contributions clearly drive the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respects choice, avoids pressure, and keeps the client in control of decisions. Could be improved by more explicitly affirming his freedom to adjust or not proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical change talk and helps the client formulate his own plan. It could become deeper by drawing out values, identity, confidence, or hope more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and invites the client’s viewpoint throughout. It would benefit from more explicit acknowledgment of him as expert in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open-ended questions and reflections that evoke clear change statements and support client-authored planning. Could be stronger with more targeted affirmations of confidence or readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without argumentation and acknowledges the competing sides. A more advanced response would gently reframe or explore the secrecy/low-profile theme further.
