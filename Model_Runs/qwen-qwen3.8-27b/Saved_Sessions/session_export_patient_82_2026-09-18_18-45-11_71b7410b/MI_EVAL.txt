### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently maintains a warm, non-judgmental, and validating tone throughout the session. Could be elevated by explicitly affirming Lukas as a whole person beyond his struggle rather than solely within it.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional empathic accuracy, consistently capturing identity-level meaning beneath surface content. Reflections precisely track emotional undercurrents like shame, loss of control, and identity threat.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with logical session progression from rapport through identity exploration. Lacks a slight forward edge, such as a small collaborative next step to give the process gentle trajectory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing with language that reinforces joint exploration and teamwork. Could be stronger with explicit power-sharing, such as acknowledging the client's contributions are shaping the session in real-time.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports the client's autonomy, never imposing plans or advice. Language reinforces the client's ownership of the process and right to be in the middle of something without owing a resolution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out client-generated insight and powerful personal metaphors through well-constructed open questions. Could be stronger by explicitly connecting these insights to change motivation rather than understanding alone.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and open exploratory questions. Could be deeper by explicitly acknowledging the client as the expert on his own experience and checking in on whether reflections land correctly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk by exploring values and the dissonance between ideal self and experience. Does not consistently amplify or strategically deepen the client's own change-oriented language, and lacks techniques like scaling questions to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well with neutral validation that neither reinforces minimization nor pushes back against it. Could be stronger with double-sided reflections to hold both the sustain talk and emerging awareness simultaneously.
