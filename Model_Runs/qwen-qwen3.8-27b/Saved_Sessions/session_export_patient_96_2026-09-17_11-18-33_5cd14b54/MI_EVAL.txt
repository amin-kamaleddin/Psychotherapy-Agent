# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist validates Marisol's choices without judgment and absorbs her dismissiveness gracefully without any defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced empathy that captures the precise emotional dynamic (autonomy violation, retroactive judgment) and is consistently attuned to Marisol's specific frame rather than imposing an external narrative.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical flow from framing through resolution to closure. The final tangent toward a broader relational pattern represents a minor drift from the specific behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with the session structured as joint exploration rather than clinical evaluation, though the overall arc remains largely clinician-orchestrated with the client influencing content more than structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Arguably the strongest domain; the psychiatrist consistently and explicitly supports Marisol's autonomy, never pressures, never uses 'should,' and frames all outcomes as the client's own decisions rather than compliance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of open questions and reflections to elicit the client's own language and reasoning; the psychiatrist never states reasons for change and all information is sourced from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative, non-expert stance maintained through repeated use of curiosity language and client-defined goals, though occasional slight clinical framing (e.g., 'I want to ask') subtly positions the psychiatrist as the one steering.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change-relevant language is evoked (agreement to six-month checkups, commitment to cleaning) but the change talk is largely about compliance rather than the piercing itself, with missed opportunities to deepen with confidence or ability questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful reframing of autonomy sustain talk as personal strength and validation without amplification, though the psychiatrist does not use double-sided reflections or actively redirect the strongest risk minimization theme.
