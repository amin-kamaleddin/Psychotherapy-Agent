**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with genuine, specific warmth throughout. Validates Jonathan's doubt and resistance without judgment, normalizing his experience even when he expresses skepticism.

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on diabetes self-management with a purposeful arc from overwhelm to a concrete plan. Every question and reflection serves to move Jonathan closer to an achievable action, ending with a specific two-week breakfast check commitment.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Frames all work as a joint endeavor using 'we' language and consistently seeks Jonathan's input before moving forward. Jonathan's own ideas (once-daily checking, oil-in-car metaphor, two-week timeframe) shape the session's direction without an imposed agenda.

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Explicitly gives Jonathan control over direction and affirms his right to doubt and set his own pace. All actions are framed as Jonathan's choices rather than the psychiatrist's recommendations, removing perceived external pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external reasons for change; instead uses strategic questions that draw out Jonathan's own motivations and insights. The final commitment ('I'll try the breakfast thing') was entirely Jonathan's own words, evoked through skilled questioning and reflection.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently attributes insight and expertise to Jonathan rather than himself, acting as a guide rather than a teacher. Never provides information or advice; all solutions emerge from Jonathan himself and are framed as his own realizations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk through strategic questions, affirmations, and pointing out shifts in Jonathan's own language. The session ends with a clear commitment statement that was evoked, not imposed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill by validating the doubt, then gently pivoting to the kernel of change talk within or adjacent to it. Never argues against or reinforces sustain talk; the 'oil in the car' metaphor demonstrates successful reframing of the test/grade framing.
