# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist validates Ryan's framing without challenging or correcting, never uses stigmatizing language, and meets him exactly where he is.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently deep reflective listening that goes beyond surface paraphrasing to capture emotional nuance, such as naming the powerlessness behind 'going with the flow' and the dissonance between intellectual awareness and emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical arc from ambivalence to concrete action, with each question building on the previous answer and the session closing with a specific, actionable next-step question about the next party.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative dynamic where Ryan's responses shape the conversation's direction; the psychiatrist never positions as the authority and rolls with resistance rather than steering to a predetermined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook autonomy support with repeated explicit statements affirming Ryan's choice and right to decide his own path, woven naturally into the conversation without pressure or implied obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful use of evocation techniques including the close friend question, threshold exploration, and avoidance loop identification; Ryan's motivation clearly emerges from within, progressing from tentative acknowledgment to concrete self-generated strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Ryan, explicitly rejecting the expert role and attributing all insight to the client; never prescribes or directs, framing questions as invitations for Ryan's own wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits rich, progressive change talk through evocation questions and explicit amplification, with Ryan shifting from minimization to concrete planning (texting a friend, saving a ride number) by session's end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging Ryan's framing and reframing through curiosity, but could have more actively employed double-sided reflections or strategic summarizing to further pivot moments like 'I'm not a problem person' toward change.
