# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows warmth, respect, and nonjudgment, even when Rowan is resistant. Rowan’s ambivalence and self-doubt are validated without being labeled or corrected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor uses accurate two-sided reflections that capture Rowan’s conflicting feelings and emerging openness. There is clear attunement to fatigue, self-doubt, and the client’s need for autonomy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior of charging and using the medical device. The conversation progresses coherently from desired setup to practical cues and sustainability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is largely co-created, with Rowan’s practical ideas woven directly into the approach. However, the psychiatrist occasionally proposes specific options before the client has fully generated them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids coercive language and repeatedly honors Rowan’s need for choice and control. Some therapist-generated suggestions may slightly narrow the client’s independent exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively draw out Rowan’s own standards, preferences, and practical ideas for change. The therapist still introduces some motivational content and solution frames rather than relying solely on client-generated reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rowan is treated as the expert on his own life, and his ideas shape much of the conversation. At times, the psychiatrist gently steers the direction with specific suggestions, limiting full client-led development.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor evokes and amplifies meaningful client statements, such as checking the device on his own time and needing a simple signal. The session could more fully develop change talk around importance, confidence, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rowan’s resistance is acknowledged without argument and is gently redirected toward values and possible conditions for change. The counselor could use more advanced ambivalence-resolution strategies, and sustain talk remains fairly prominent.
