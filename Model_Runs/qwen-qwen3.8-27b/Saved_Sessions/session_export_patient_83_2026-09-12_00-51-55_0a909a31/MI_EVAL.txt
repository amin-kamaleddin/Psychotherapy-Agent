# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor communicates respect, nonjudgment, and support throughout the session. Tone remains practical and safety-focused rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Empathy is adequate but mostly surface-level and content-focused. The counselor misses opportunities to reflect deeper emotions such as feeling overlooked or needing control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on understanding and adhering to the new statin. There is a slight drift toward general anxiety management near the end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor follows some of the patient's cues but remains largely information-driven. More joint problem-solving would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices are offered, but autonomy is not consistently reinforced. Several directive statements may reduce the patient's sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor provides much of the motivation and safety rationale rather than eliciting the patient's own reasons. Key moments to draw out values and confidence are missed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some partnership behaviors are present, such as asking for focus and offering options. Overall stance is more expert-driven than collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient expresses plan-based change talk, and the counselor affirms it. There are few MI-specific moves to actively evoke stronger reasons for adherence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing and does not reinforce sustain talk. Responses are mainly educational rather than MI-consistent softening or pivot statements.
