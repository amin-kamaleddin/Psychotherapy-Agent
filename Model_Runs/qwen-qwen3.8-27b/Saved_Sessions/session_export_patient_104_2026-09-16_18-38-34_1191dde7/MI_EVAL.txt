# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client's exhaustion, worry, and fear of being a burden. The tone remains warm and nonjudgmental throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects the client's ambivalence, fatigue, and need for reassurance. It would strengthen empathy to go deeper into the emotional meaning of the client's fear of failing as a parent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the client's specific concern about infant sleep and reassurance. The counselor effectively narrows the issue from general worry to a concrete, actionable red flag.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses joint problem-solving language and allows the client's ideas to shape the plan. However, offering structured options and suggested wording makes the interaction somewhat counselor-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports choice by asking what feels manageable, doable, or safe for the client. Providing scripts and multiple-choice options slightly limits the client's opportunity to generate her own solution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor draws out the client's own motivations, concerns, and standards. Stronger use of scaling or deeper values exploration could further enhance evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a collaborative stance and respects the client's expertise about her baby. The interaction feels slightly guided by the counselor's use of options and suggested phrases.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor elicits meaningful change talk by exploring preferences, feasibility, and meaning. More amplifying or summarizing the client's strengths and change statements would improve this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges the client's sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing it. Using more double-sided reflections or value-based pivots could further weaken the client's worry.
