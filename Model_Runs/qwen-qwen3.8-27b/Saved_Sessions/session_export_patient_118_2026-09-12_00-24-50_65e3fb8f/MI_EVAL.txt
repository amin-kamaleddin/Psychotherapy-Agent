# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist showed some respect by not explicitly judging Seamus and by acknowledging “no lectures.” However, the tone remained transactional and did not fully validate the emotional frustration of being repeatedly given smoking advice.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Brief reflections acknowledged Seamus’s desire for a practical plan and some frustration. Deeper empathic understanding of his defensiveness, fatigue, and need to be heard was limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session had a clear practical focus on dental care, but lacked clear MI direction toward the relevant target behavior, such as exploring how smoking may relate to gum health. The conversation did not move collaboratively toward change or ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The interaction was largely expert-driven, with the psychiatrist providing plans, explanations, and scripts. Seamus was not consistently treated as an equal partner in shaping the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respected the request not to lecture about smoking and allowed Seamus to set a boundary. However, repeated directives and scripts undermined Seamus’s sense of being in control of his own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There was very little effort to evoke Seamus’s own motivation, values, or reasons for change. The conversation shifted quickly into information-giving, advising, and script-writing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A few questions invited Seamus’s preferences, but these were superficial and did not create genuine collaboration. For most of the session, the psychiatrist remained in an expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Seamus offered limited change-relevant language, but the psychiatrist did not build on it. There were no meaningful attempts to draw out his own motivations for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Seamus expressed clear sustain talk, including not wanting a lecture and wanting to avoid smoking discussion. The psychiatrist did not reinforce this stance, but also did not actively work with the ambivalence or help him hold both sides.
