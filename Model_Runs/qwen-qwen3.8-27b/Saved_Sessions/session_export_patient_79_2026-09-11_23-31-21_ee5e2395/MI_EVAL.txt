# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates the patient's frustration. However, the early shift to a direct asthma management plan can feel clinically corrective rather than fully affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The strongest empathic responses capture the patient's feeling of being reprimanded and shut down. Earlier reflections are adequate but sometimes surface-level or repetitive, without deeper exploration of his fear and anger.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays within the asthma and medical-care domain, but the focus is not consistently tied to one target behavior. A clearer collaboratively selected goal would have strengthened the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and asks for input, creating some collaborative atmosphere. However, much of the content is clinician-supplied rather than co-created with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by offering low-effort options and avoiding overt pressure. That said, the unsolicited breathing plan and repeated suggestions can feel directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** This is the main weakness. The counselor quickly shifts from an opening question into instruction and problem-solving instead of eliciting the patient's own goals, values, fears, and motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some partnership by asking preferences and offering choices. Still, the counselor frequently leads the session with scripts, notes, and instructions, limiting power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The patient offers useful change talk, such as having read materials and using the inhaler when symptoms are severe. The psychiatrist does not consistently build on this with evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and normalizes forgetting, which reduces blame. However, the counselor does not effectively redirect the patient's sustain talk toward change using double-sided reflections or goals.
