# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids arguing when Luis rejects reminders or check-ins. Acceptance could be deeper by adding more explicit, warm affirmation of Luis’s struggle, effort, and emotional experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Luis’s desire for simplicity, control, and low-stakes management. Emotional depth could be strengthened by more fully exploring his fear, overwhelm, and feeling infantilized by monitoring.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on making asthma management simpler, safer, and self-directed. Questions progressively narrow the conversation toward a concrete one-line action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses shared language such as building the wallet sheet together and invites Luis’s preferences. Some therapist-generated options remain, so collaboration could be more fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Luis’s choice and control are consistently emphasized, and his boundaries are respected without pressure. Safety concerns are framed in terms of finding a workable option on Luis’s terms rather than imposing rules.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out Luis’s own solutions, knowledge, and language rather than providing reasons for change. This leads directly to Luis generating a concrete self-directed action plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Luis as the expert on his own day-to-day life and asthma experience. Luis’s ideas substantially guide the direction and outcome of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicit useful client-generated change talk, including the final one-line asthma response. The psychiatrist could more explicitly amplify, label, or strengthen Luis’s confidence and commitment to the plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance well by avoiding argument, correction, or pressure when Luis rejects reminders and monitoring. Stronger strategic reframing of his 911-only stance and distrust of monitoring would more fully soften sustain talk.
