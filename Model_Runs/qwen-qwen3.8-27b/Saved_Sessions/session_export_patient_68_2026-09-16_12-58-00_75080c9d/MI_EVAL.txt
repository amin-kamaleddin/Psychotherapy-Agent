# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Kevin’s concern and responding to ambivalence with acceptance. Warmth is functional rather than deeply affirming of Kevin’s emotional experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Kevin’s practical concerns and social pressures, showing adequate understanding. Emotional themes such as embarrassment, shame, and fear of missing out are not deeply explored or validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on alcohol use in the context of ankle recovery and social functioning. The discussion moves toward concrete next steps, though it can become overly tactical.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Kevin’s input and builds a plan around some of his choices, creating partial collaboration. The interaction is still largely clinician-driven because the psychiatrist frequently introduces options and adjusts the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin is given choices and his pace is accepted, supporting some autonomy. The psychiatrist’s repeated prescriptive suggestions can make the process feel more guided than client-owned.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and confidence scaling help Kevin generate some self-directed statements and small commitments. The psychiatrist often supplies reasons and tactics instead of deeply evoking Kevin’s own values and motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin participates actively and his preferences shape parts of the plan. The psychiatrist supplies many expert-driven options, which reduces the balance of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin offers several change-relevant statements, and the psychiatrist reinforces some of them. Change talk is not consistently amplified or expanded into broader motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without challenging or dismissing it. It is not reinforced, but it is also not effectively redirected using more MI-consistent techniques.
