# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with genuine affirmations of Jordan's struggle. Undermined by the verbatim repetition of a reflection and a missed opportunity to normalize the shame of being court-mandated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, particularly in capturing ambivalence and the insight-action gap. The repeated reflection error and failure to engage with Jordan's deepest identity question at the close limit the score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains focus on shoplifting with a loose progression from problem to self-awareness to identity. The repetition derails momentum, and the session ends without a clear shared goal or concrete next-step focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements present, but the session remains largely therapist-driven with structured, leading questions. Jordan's identity concern at the end goes unexplored, suggesting his lead was not fully followed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly rejects a directive approach and consistently invites Jordan to generate his own solutions. Could be strengthened by more directly addressing the tension between the court-mandated referral and genuine personal choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Moderate use of evocative questions and affirmations to draw out change talk from within. Remains at a surface level without structured exploration of values, future vision, or self-efficacy, and ends before Jordan's most evocative moment can be fully developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good moments of collaboration ("I won't hand you a script, because it'd be mine, not yours"). The verbatim repetition of a reflection and therapist-driven question structure undermine the sense of a true partnership between equals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Identifies and affirms instances of change talk (self-monitoring, "looking at it from a step back"). However, change talk is not consistently reinforced, amplified, or explored deeply enough to build sustained momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument or dismissal, which is appropriate. However, no active techniques (double-sided reflections, cost exploration, pivoting) are used to redirect sustain talk toward change.
