# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating, often normalizing David's stress and concerns. The stance is somewhat procedural and task-focused rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections capture David's practical stress, urgency, and frustration effectively. However, they often summarize content and obvious emotion rather than exploring deeper feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on smoking cessation, triggers, medication access, and immediate next steps. The end becomes somewhat repetitive with repeated offers to script or rehearse the plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for David's input and adapts to his priorities, supporting a collaborative stance. Collaboration is reduced when the psychiatrist provides detailed lists, medication information, and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently asks permission, offers options, and avoids coercion. Repeated offers to draft, shorten, or rehearse the script slightly push toward a recommended plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions elicit some motivation, but the session shifts quickly into information-giving and planning. There are limited attempts to draw out David's deeper values, strengths, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites David's perspective and tailors suggestions to his needs. However, the frequent provision of detailed advice makes the interaction less fully collaborative at times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David expresses clear change talk, and the psychiatrist affirms his decision to seek care. The counselor does not consistently deepen or amplify change talk around importance, confidence, and ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Concerns about side effects, stress, and coping tools are validated without argument. The psychiatrist does not consistently help David reconcile ambivalence or turn sustain talk into stronger change talk.
