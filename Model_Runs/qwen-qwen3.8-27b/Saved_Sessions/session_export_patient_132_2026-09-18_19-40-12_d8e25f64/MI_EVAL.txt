# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates warm, unconditional positive regard with no judgment, labeling, or disapproval throughout the entire session. Validates ambivalence as worthy of exploration and celebrates the client's insights as genuinely significant.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflective listening that captures both the content and emotional/existential weight of the client's statements. Skillfully tracks shifting emotional states and employs double-sided reflections to validate ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, progressive arc from identifying 'actually healthy' to envisioning concrete alternatives, without drift or premature action planning. Could benefit from more explicit midpoint check-ins to confirm the client feels co-directed rather than led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance that builds on the client's language, metaphors, and ideas as the foundation for further exploration. Could reach a 5 if the client more visibly shaped the session structure and agenda rather than the counselor's questions guiding each next topic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and right to choose, never saying 'you should' or 'you need to.' Stays with the client's fear of autonomy without rushing to reassure or offer solutions, respecting her right to feel scared.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically draws out the client's own motivations, values, and insights through evocative questioning rather than providing information or advice. The client arrives at key insights independently (e.g., 'ignoring evidence in my body'), which is the hallmark of expert-level evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise about the client's life and body resides within her, positioning her as the expert on her own experience. Occasionally leads with interpretation before asking for clarification, and could more explicitly invite the client to set or check the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evokes and deepens change talk through strategic questions that elicit the client's own reasons and values. Could be more deliberately structured around the DARN-CAT framework, particularly around confidence and ability elicitation to build self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill, acknowledging the weight loss desire without dismissing, arguing, or rushing past it. Gently challenges binary framings (e.g., 'giving up') while opening alternative possibilities without creating a power struggle.
