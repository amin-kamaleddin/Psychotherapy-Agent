# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, avoiding shame or argument around Elena’s smoking. The main limitation is that affirmations become somewhat repetitive and focus heavily on “protecting your son” rather than broader individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are generally accurate and validating, especially around stress, workload, and the desire to avoid pressure. Empathy could go deeper by reflecting Elena’s fear of judgment, guilt, exhaustion, and single-parent burden.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking reduction and child exposure, and it narrows to a manageable one-week plan. Direction could be stronger by clarifying a specific target behavior and building clearer momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and co-creates a low-burden plan with Elena. However, the clinician remains the primary generator of options and recommendations rather than fully letting Elena lead the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported throughout, with repeated invitations, no pressure language, and respect for Elena’s boundary not to be checked in. This is the clinician’s strongest area.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some useful evocative questions, including scaling and preference questions, but much of the session becomes advisory. The psychiatrist does not consistently follow up on scaling scores or elicit deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Elena’s input and the plan reflects her preferences. The partnership could improve by more explicitly positioning Elena as the expert on her own life and reducing the clinician’s leading role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena expresses some importance and agrees to a small step, but change talk is not deeply developed. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to explore what would increase confidence or connect the plan to Elena’s values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids confrontation and reframes sustain talk in nonjudgmental ways. However, key sustain talk is not actively softened or redirected toward discrepancy, values, or change.
