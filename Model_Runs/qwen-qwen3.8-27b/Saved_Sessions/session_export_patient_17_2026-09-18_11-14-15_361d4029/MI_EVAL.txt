**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and individualized unconditional positive regard throughout the session. The psychiatrist validates mixed feelings, corrects any perceived judgment immediately, and never minimizes Anisa's struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy, consistently identifying the emotional core beneath Anisa's words. Standout reflections include the cigarette as 'the one moment everything else can wait' and the protective function against being 'looked at sideways.'

### Direction

**Comments:** Clear strategic progression from surface motivation to deeper relational function to future vision, with no drift. The session would benefit from a briefer summary or collaborative 'landing' to consolidate discoveries and identify one small focus for the coming week.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative stance sustained throughout, using 'we' and 'together' language naturally. The psychiatrist follows Anisa's lead as she shifts topics, tracking and deepening rather than pulling back to a predetermined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Anisa's ownership and freedom to choose, woven naturally into the therapeutic stance rather than stated formulaically. The psychiatrist never pressures for commitment or minimizes her right to continue.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Strong use of open questions that elicit Anisa's own insights, particularly 'what is it about those two minutes that actually does the work?' ### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside with Anisa. Uses inclusive language, returns questions to her rather than answering them, and stays in a stance of curiosity without offering advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out multiple forms of change talk (desire, reasons, ability hints) through strategic open questions and reflections. Could push slightly further by explicitly amplifying and building on each emergence of change language.
