# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing ambivalence and acknowledging the client's attendance. The warmth is pragmatic and sometimes procedural, limiting consistently affirming presence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are adequate but often factual rather than emotional. Key feelings of being controlled, defensive, and proud are not deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on weekend use and marital strain, moving from broad concerns to a concrete experiment. Direction is strong, though occasionally didactic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Parts of the plan are co-constructed, but much content is counselor-led. The client's concern about being aligned with his wife suggests the collaborative stance was not fully felt.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and avoids explicit coercion, but frequently supplies a detailed plan and script. This can make the client feel managed rather than fully supported in deciding.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some initial questions but relies heavily on advice, scripts, and safety planning. There is limited elicitation of the client's own motivations or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some choice-making and plan adjustments show collaboration, but the session often feels expert-driven. The client's input does not consistently shape the direction enough for true co-leadership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers useful change talk about being sober Saturday and saying no, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or expand it. Evocation remains occasional rather than skillful.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but not effectively redirected through rolling with resistance or developing discrepancy. Several responses pivot into advice or harm reduction, which can increase pressure.
