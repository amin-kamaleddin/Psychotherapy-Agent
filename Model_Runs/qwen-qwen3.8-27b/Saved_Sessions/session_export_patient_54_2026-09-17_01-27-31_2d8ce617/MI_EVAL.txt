# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows consistent respect, normalization, and nonjudgment toward Isabella’s ambivalence and autonomy. The warmth is strong but remains more tied to her decision-making process than unconditional personal regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Isabella’s autonomy, cultural connection to wine, and concern for the baby. Empathy could deepen by exploring emotional undercurrents such as fear, guilt, and identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and moves toward a practical next step. It would benefit from naming the target behavior and plan more concretely.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and lets Isabella’s preferences shape the conversation. Collaboration could become more fully client-shaped by inviting her to define sources, structure, and process in greater detail.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces that the decision is Isabella’s and avoids pressure. Autonomy support is the strongest aspect, helping her frame choice as using her own judgment to protect her baby.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Isabella’s values, standards, and reasons for change. More attention to confidence, past competence, and personal capability would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Isabella to participate in defining useful information and a decision that feels like hers. The partnership is strong, though the psychiatrist still takes a somewhat expert role in offering guidance and a summary.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encourages authentic, value-based decision-making and amplifies emerging change talk. Change talk could be developed further across confidence, ability, commitment, and specific next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Isabella’s sustain talk and gently reframes it without challenging her. The response could more consistently pivot her statements toward change-related language and motivation.
