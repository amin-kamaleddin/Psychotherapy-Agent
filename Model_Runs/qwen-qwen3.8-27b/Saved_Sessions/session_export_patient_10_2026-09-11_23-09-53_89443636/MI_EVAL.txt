# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Amina’s frustration and skepticism. However, warmth is often procedural and quickly shifts into problem-solving, limiting deeper acceptance of her as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor provides adequate reflection of Amina’s frustration and concern, but misses richer emotional themes such as shame, fatigue, and unfairness. Empathy often remains surface-level and is followed quickly by advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the main issue of employment access complicated by childcare. The main limitation is that direction is task-focused and advisory rather than strategically guiding Amina toward her own change agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina’s concerns shape the wording of scripts and the plan evolves in response to her feedback. However, the counselor remains the primary source of ideas, so the interaction does not fully feel like a partnership between equals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers some choices and respects Amina’s preference for honesty rather than an apologetic tone. Frequent directive language and scripted solutions, however, reduce the emphasis on Amina’s ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor frequently provides reasons, options, and scripts rather than eliciting Amina’s own motivations. Her strong self-belief is present but not consistently drawn out through values-based, confidence-based, or future-focused evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks for input and adjusts based on Amina’s feedback, showing some power sharing. Overall, however, the stance remains expert-driven, with the counselor supplying most scripts, options, and steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Amina offers clear change talk, but the counselor does not consistently reflect, amplify, or expand this language. There are few evocative prompts that deepen her sense of capability, values, or desired future.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Barrier-focused and sustain talk dominate the session, and the counselor often responds by adding logistical plans. Rarely does the counselor use double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to soften these concerns and redirect toward change.
