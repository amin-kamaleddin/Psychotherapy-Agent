# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, validating stance without pressure. Acceptance becomes somewhat formulaic and does not fully address Elena’s explicit request to hear that she is not a bad mom.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections show solid perspective-taking of Elena’s stress, guilt, and ambivalence. They remain surface-level and do not deeply explore the emotional weight behind her defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking-related concerns and low-pressure next steps. Excessive attention to reminder wording limits strategic momentum toward goals, values, and confidence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates on reminder wording, length, and tone. The interaction is responsive but more procedural than deeply client-led in shaping change.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly emphasizes choice, noncoercion, and respect for Elena’s boundaries. This is a major strength of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early questions elicit some client language and a small step forward. The session shifts to information and logistics before consistently drawing out Elena’s motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena’s input influences logistical details, but the clinician still largely drives the agenda with menus and resources. There is limited explicit acknowledgment of Elena as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions produce mild change talk, such as importance ratings and willingness to check a text line. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and normalizes Elena’s not being ready to quit. Sustain talk is repeatedly present without sufficient redirection through double-sided reflections.
