# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizes slips, showing strong positive regard. Affirmations are sometimes formulaic and bundled with planning, which slightly reduces pure acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers adequate reflections of Hana’s situation and ambivalence. Empathy remains more content-focused than emotionally nuanced.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking cessation and a concrete, doable plan. At times it becomes procedural rather than consistently linking the behavior back to Hana’s values and concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan is clearly shaped by Hana’s choices and boundaries. The counselor’s detailed options and repeated recaps make the interaction feel somewhat more counselor-guided than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently affirms Hana’s right to choose and avoids pressure. Continued addition of backup steps after Hana signals she does not want a system can subtly undermine full autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions help Hana propose a plan and define success. The counselor does not consistently evoke deeper motivation, values, or confidence related to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows Hana to choose among options. The interaction can feel expert-led because of the detailed option list and frequent recaps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor evokes some change talk and affirms small steps. There are missed opportunities to deepen, amplify, and reflect back Hana’s own change-related language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well without arguing or dismissing Hana’s reasons. Occasionally returning to structure after Hana asks for simplicity limits the effectiveness of softening.
