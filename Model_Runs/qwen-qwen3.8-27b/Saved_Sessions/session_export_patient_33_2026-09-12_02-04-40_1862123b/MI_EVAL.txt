# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates both Diego's continued use and his concern about risk. The warmth is supportive but somewhat generic, with room to affirm Diego more explicitly as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Diego's ambivalence and later relief, including the tension between coping through heroin and wanting to stay safe. A limitation is that the reflections remain practical and brief rather than deeply exploring the underlying fear and pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on safer heroin use and harm reduction without drifting to unrelated topics. The psychiatrist appropriately keeps the target on reducing risk rather than pushing abstinence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Diego's choices, invites input, and supports a joint problem-solving approach. However, the amount of structured information provided can make the interaction feel more counselor-led than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Diego's choices are consistently respected, including his readiness level, preference for a face-to-face conversation, and boundary around sharing his exact ZIP code. The psychiatrist avoids pressuring him to quit or impose a program.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Diego articulate concrete plans and reasons for staying safer, such as telling his housemate and obtaining test strips. Deeper evocation is limited when the conversation shifts into information delivery before Diego's readiness is fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative questions and asks permission before offering options, supporting a partner stance. At times, the provision of scripts and checklists shifts the dynamic toward information delivery rather than shared exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Diego's own goals and affirms statements of readiness, such as telling his housemate and making a practical plan. These change statements could be further amplified by exploring their meaning and what they say about Diego's values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepts Diego's continued use without argument or shame and keeps the focus on harm reduction. A stronger move would be a double-sided reflection that honors heroin's function while gently linking it to safer-use change.
