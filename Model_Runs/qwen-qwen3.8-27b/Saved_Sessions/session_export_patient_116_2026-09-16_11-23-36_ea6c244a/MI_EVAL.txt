# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, and validating stance throughout most of the session. There are a few problem-focused or labeling phrases that keep this from being consistently deep and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture practical and emotional aspects of Samir's experience. They often remain surface-level rather than fully exploring deeper themes such as shame, identity, or loss of autonomy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on medication adherence and progressively narrows to a specific, low-effort action. Direction is maintained well even when the client is guarded or brief.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Samir's perspective and incorporates his preferences into the final plan. However, the therapist frequently generates options and the final plan, making the collaboration feel more guided than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is strong support for choice, privacy, and no-pressure decision-making. The structured set of clinician-provided options slightly limits the sense that the client fully generates the next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some internal motivation is elicited, including a small confidence rating and tentative willingness to try. The session often shifts toward practical problem-solving rather than deeply evoking values, reasons, or change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samir's preferences meaningfully influence the session and final plan. The interaction feels somewhat expert-guided because the psychiatrist often presents multiple options before the client generates them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges some change talk and invites small steps. There is limited use of DARN-C-focused questions to expand importance, confidence, benefits, or values-based motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is accepted without arguing, blaming, or pushing. The psychiatrist could use more double-sided reflections and rolling-with-resistance strategies to move Samir toward exploring change.
