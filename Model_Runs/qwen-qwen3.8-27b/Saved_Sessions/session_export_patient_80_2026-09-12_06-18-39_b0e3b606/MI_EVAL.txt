# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm and affirming stance with clear nonjudgmental language and recognition of strengths. Could be more consistently rich in unconditional positive regard to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provides adequate, often factual reflections of the client’s goals and concerns. Could more deeply reflect underlying emotions, social pressure, and fears related to anger and partner dynamics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly focused on alcohol reduction and anger management with a clear, practical trajectory. Moves coherently from strengths to a concrete coping plan, safety check, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the client to co-create the plan and adapts to the client’s simpler approach. The counselor still provides considerable structure and specific recommendations, keeping it below a 5.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choice and respects client preferences, including a simple plan and declining role-play. Could more explicitly affirm the client’s freedom to choose any path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits client resources, intended actions, and some self-efficacy. Remains more task-based than motivational, with less exploration of values, importance of change, and the client’s own reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with clear invitations for the client to choose the signal, action, and follow-up approach. Interaction remains somewhat counselor-guided due to supplied options and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits useful change talk through practical questions and confidence scaling. Misses opportunities to amplify or deepen the client’s own motivations before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids argument and tolerates reasons for maintaining the status quo. Could more actively soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections or reframing around mates, social awkwardness, and partner pushback.
