# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance and avoids blaming or shaming. A few interpretive frames make the responses slightly leading rather than fully accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers strong, accurate reflections of Elira’s ambivalence and emotional burden. Some reflections add interpretation that goes slightly beyond Elira’s own stated experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on anxiety-driven avoidance and small steps toward showing up. Direction becomes somewhat repetitive and could better summarize themes or connect steps to broader values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acts as an equal partner and follows Elira’s emerging ideas. There is less explicit joint-work language to strengthen shared ownership of the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses invitations, hypotheticals, and respect for Elira’s limits without pressuring action. It could more explicitly affirm Elira’s right to choose not to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes Elira’s own ideas and emotions rather than giving advice. It could more fully develop confidence, importance, and commitment from Elira’s change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Elira as the expert on her own experience. Her ideas clearly shape the session’s direction and decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some small steps and change-oriented ideas. These are not consistently amplified or reflected as desire, ability, reasons, need, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by reflecting it without arguing or reinforcing avoidance. It could use more double-sided reflections that pivot toward change, values, or possibility.
