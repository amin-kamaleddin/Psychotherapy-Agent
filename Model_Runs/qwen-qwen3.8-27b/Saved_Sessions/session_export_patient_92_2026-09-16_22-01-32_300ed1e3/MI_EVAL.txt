# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with repeated normalization of the client’s concerns. Slightly formulaic and could deepen personal regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of values, health concerns, and social life. Reflections remain mostly content-focused and could explore deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, focused direction on alcohol use, screening, and possible changes. Could guide more consistently toward motivation and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative; client’s ideas shape the session, including blood work and small tweaks. Interaction feels cooperative rather than prescriptive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports client ownership by avoiding pressure and allowing him to define fair outcomes. Could more explicitly reinforce his decision-making power.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation, especially when asking about small tweaks. Could more deeply draw out values, confidence, and personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly partnership-oriented with open questions and respect for the client’s perspective. Could co-create the agenda more explicitly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change talk about reducing intensity and alternatives. Could better reflect, amplify, and explore commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and acknowledges sustain talk about taste and friends. Could better balance sustain talk with health concerns and change reasons.
