### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional and consistent unconditional positive regard throughout the session. Validation is specific, contextual, and individualized to Erik's exact words rather than formulaic; never labels negatively or expresses disappointment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently captures the emotional texture beneath the surface content, accurately tracking each shift in Erik's tone from surprise to discouragement to self-compassion. Complex reflections hold multiple dimensions simultaneously (e.g., achievement and emotional invalidation).

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintains a clear and logical focus on target behaviors with a natural session arc. Could be strengthened by more explicitly bridging the gym and candy bar topics and closing with a unified collaborative summary tying both goals together.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a genuine equal partner throughout; Erik initiates most content and the psychiatrist responds by reflecting, asking, and amplifying. The session concludes with Erik generating his own behavioral plan, demonstrating truly client-led outcomes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Erik's ownership and freedom to choose, embedded naturally in every interaction rather than as a separate statement. Explicitly rejects perfectionist framing and removes pressure to have things solved in one session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Serves as the structural backbone of the session, progressively deepening from behavioral to cognitive to emotional to identity-level insight. Never provides a single reason for change; every insight Erik generates comes from his own reflection prompted by well-crafted questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Erik as the authority on his own experience, explicitly crediting his insights and attributing solutions to his own wisdom. Never assumes the expert role or provides unsolicited advice; the power dynamic is genuinely egalitarian throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through open questions and gentle hypotheticals that Erik picks up and develops into his own insights. Each change talk statement is met with affirmation and amplification, culminating in a concrete client-owned behavioral plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with textbook skill—neither reinforcing nor dismissing, but gently reframing each instance as evidence of self-awareness. The 'two bars' worry is transformed from a reason not to try into proof of readiness to notice.
