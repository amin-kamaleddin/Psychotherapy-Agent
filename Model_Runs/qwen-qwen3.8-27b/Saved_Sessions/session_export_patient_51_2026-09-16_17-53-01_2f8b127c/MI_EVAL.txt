# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgmental support even under time pressure. The acceptance is somewhat formulaic and repetitive, which prevents a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Ingrid's urgency and need for a quick, safe answer. The empathy could be more emotionally specific and less repetitive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on the medication safety question and a concrete next step. The opening is slightly broad, and repeated farewells reduce efficiency and pacing control.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Ingrid's input and incorporates her preferred short phrase and brief email format. The collaboration could be stronger with more client-led pacing and joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions to position Ingrid as the decision-maker and avoids coercive language. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her ownership of the decision and follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Ingrid's own plan and wording but relies heavily on information-giving and summarizing. More evocative questions about motivation, confidence, and values would strengthen this criterion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates a collaborative stance and lets Ingrid help shape the next step. The psychiatrist still retains considerable control over content, pacing, and session closure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ingrid is guided to generate a practical next step and a clear question for her prescriber. The session does not deeply explore or expand her change talk across importance, confidence, need, or ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist appropriately accepts Ingrid's time constraints without arguing or reinforcing them. Sustain talk could be more actively reframed as part of the change process rather than simply accommodated.
