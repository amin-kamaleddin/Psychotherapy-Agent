# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone with clear affirmations and normalizing statements. Acceptance is somewhat formulaic and could more explicitly acknowledge the difficulty of re-entry and mandated participation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are present but mostly task-focused rather than emotionally nuanced. Missed opportunities include reflecting ambivalence, fear of failure, and low confidence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on smoking cessation and narrows to a concrete target behavior. Repetitive restatement of the plan weakens forward momentum and strategic guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaboration occurs at a surface level through goal selection and check-in preference. The plan is largely psychiatrist-authored, with limited input from Mateo's own experience and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice points and non-controlling language are present, but the detailed prescriptive plan creates an implicit expectation to follow exact instructions. The client's right to modify or reject parts of the plan is not strongly emphasized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used, but the approach quickly shifts to providing information and a detailed plan. More open-ended eliciting of reasons, confidence, and preferred strategies would strengthen this criterion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and asks for preferences, but the interaction remains largely clinician-led. Mateo's expertise about his habits, triggers, and social world is underutilized.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Values and goals are identified, but change talk is not strongly drawn out or amplified. The session would benefit from deeper questions that connect change to the client's stated values and priorities.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but often moved past rather than explored. Double-sided reflections and strategic redirection could better address ambivalence and resolve discrepancies.
