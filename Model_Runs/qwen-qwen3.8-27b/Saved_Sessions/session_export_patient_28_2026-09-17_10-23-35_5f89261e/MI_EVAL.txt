# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, nonjudgmental language and normalizes Dante’s fatigue and family demands. Some counselor reframes interpret his experience rather than fully reflecting it uninterpreted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections track Dante’s ambivalence, fear of failure, and affect. Minor interpretive leaps are clinically useful and expanded by Dante.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on re-establishing exercise and narrows toward a concrete action: a short 7:30 walk after dinner. Momentum moves from abstract ambivalence to a doable behavioral experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Small plans are co-constructed using Dante’s language and preferences. More explicit co-creation or invitations for Dante to direct next steps would make it fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids prescribing frequency, duration, or rules, and repeatedly asks Dante to define a “good enough” version. Dante retains ownership of the decision and self-credit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Many questions evoke Dante’s own meanings, reasons, and solutions, producing client-generated change-oriented language. Occasional counselor interpretations provide meaning before Dante fully generates it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Dante is positioned as the expert, with his ideas about shoes, timing, and phone placement built into the plan. A few counselor-introduced frames subtly position the psychiatrist as meaning-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicit desire and ability statements such as wanting to feel less out of shape and seeing the walk as “better than nothing.” Change talk could be deepened through labeling, summaries, or confidence/commitment scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Obstacles are normalized and reframed rather than disputed, reducing rigidity around failure and fatigue. The session could pivot more consistently from barriers to values, exceptions, or change talk.
