# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental tone with clear statements of unconditional positive regard. The warmth occasionally feels slightly scripted, and acceptance is not absolute in moments of sustain talk around the fall.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong complex reflections and emotional attunement, particularly in capturing ambivalence between Evelyn's minimization and her son's concern. Empathy occasionally stays at the cognitive level rather than fully entering deeper emotional experiences like fear of lost autonomy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use with logical topic progression and well-managed pacing. The session ends at a natural inflection point but lacks a summarizing reflection or collaborative next step to reinforce direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed as a joint exploration with strong transparency and equal footing. The psychiatrist does most of the steering and could invite Evelyn to more visibly shape the direction or pace of the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout, with consistent reinforcement of Evelyn's choice, internal standards, and right to self-define. Never uses directive language, and the key question separating her internal 'fine' from her son's expectation is a sophisticated application of this principle.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently uses evocative questions and never provides reasons for change, keeping motivation firmly internal to Evelyn. Could more explicitly consolidate and amplify the change talk once it appears, particularly at the session's peak moment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance with explicit power-sharing language and honesty about limitations. Could be deepened by more explicit co-construction of the agenda and a shared plan for next steps before closing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through strategic open questions, with Evelyn's final statement representing a clear shift in self-identification. The psychiatrist could have more explicitly reflected and amplified that change talk to consolidate the shift.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively with affirmations and double-sided reflections that honor both sides without argumentation. The response to the fall gently pushes back rather than fully softening the sustain talk first, which slightly limits the score.
