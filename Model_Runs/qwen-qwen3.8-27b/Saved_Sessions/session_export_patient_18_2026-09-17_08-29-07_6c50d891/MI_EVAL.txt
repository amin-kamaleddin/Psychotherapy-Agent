# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes the referral without moralizing. Acceptance could be stronger with more explicit affirmation of the client’s courage and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is good reflective listening and perspective-taking on the client’s social concerns. Deeper empathy is needed around the emotional meaning of feeling like a “nerd” or “trying too hard.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on risk limits and an exit plan with a coherent flow. It could be sharpened by more consistently anchoring the discussion to alcohol-specific behavior and a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as an equal partner and co-constructs the plan using the client’s own ideas. The interaction is balanced, non-authoritarian, and client-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the decision-maker and is invited to define her own limits and choices. There is no pressure, persuasion, or minimization of the client’s right to maintain her current choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits client motivation through open questions about personal standards and preferred ways of leaving. It could be strengthened by more explicitly exploring the client’s deeper values, confidence, and importance of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the client to set the focus and follows her lead throughout the session. The conversation feels like a joint exploration rather than a clinician-led interview.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client generates meaningful change talk about respect, self-control, and natural exits. The psychiatrist could more consistently amplify, summarize, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client’s sustain talk about fitting in and avoiding judgment is reflected without challenge. More advanced strategic pivots could more explicitly transform sustain talk into change talk.
