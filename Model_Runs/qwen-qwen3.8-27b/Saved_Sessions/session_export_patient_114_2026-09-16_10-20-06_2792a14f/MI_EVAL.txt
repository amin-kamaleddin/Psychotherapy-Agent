# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental, affirming tone and validates Ryan’s feelings without moralizing. The warmth is strong but sometimes tied to performance rather than being fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Ryan’s academic, social, and emotional conflict, including peer pressure and embarrassment. The reflections are helpful but sometimes fast-moving, which limits deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing risky alcohol use while preserving school and social goals, with purposeful movement toward a concrete plan. The target behavior is somewhat broad, and the multiple options slightly dilute a single focused line of change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ryan’s ideas, such as Sarah, a casual text, and using his bag as a cue, shape parts of the plan. However, the psychiatrist frequently supplies lists of options and tactics, making the work more counselor-guided than fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly frames choices as Ryan’s, avoids commands, and respects his reluctance by offering a private rehearsal. The large amount of provided strategies still makes the session less fully client-led.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Confidence scaling and next-step questions help Ryan articulate motivation and increase confidence. However, the counselor also supplies much of the advice and scripts, so the evocation is mixed with direction.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some partner-like moves, such as asking Ryan to choose options and building on his ideas. The session becomes quite expert-driven because of the volume of specific strategies, which reduces the sense of equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes meaningful change talk through values, confidence, and next steps, and Ryan’s confidence increases. Some change talk resembles acceptance of counselor-provided options rather than being fully self-generated and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes Ryan’s sustain talk, such as staying connected and avoiding awkwardness, without arguing or shaming. It does not consistently redirect that sustain talk toward Ryan’s goals, limiting more skillful pivoting.
