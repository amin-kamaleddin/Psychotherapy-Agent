# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and avoids blaming or correcting the client. Acceptance becomes somewhat formulaic and does not fully affirm the client's identity or worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are generally accurate and attuned to the client's surface-level frustration. Empathy does not consistently explore deeper emotions such as fear, embarrassment, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays organized and focused on the conduct referral and practical next steps. Direction is more administrative than MI-focused, with limited connection to values, behavior, or future change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor invites input and collaborates effectively on drafting the letter. The collaboration is strong for the task but less focused on exploring the client's values and change agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor consistently offers choices and avoids coercive language, supporting the client's sense of control. Autonomy support is strong but not always tied to ownership of deeper change decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and scaling encourage the client to reflect on readiness and preferences. Evocation remains superficial and does not deeply draw out internal motivation or discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor invites input and respects some client preferences, showing a collaborative stance. The interaction becomes task-oriented and does not consistently position the client as the expert on their own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some evocative questions invite change-oriented thinking, but the counselor does not meaningfully develop or amplify change talk. The session quickly shifts to problem-solving, limiting motivational exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor avoids arguing with the client's sustain talk but does not effectively redirect it. The drafted letter reinforces the client's resistance rather than encouraging responsibility or change.
