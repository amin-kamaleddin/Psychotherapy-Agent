# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout. Even when Samir offers sustain talk, the clinician never responds with disapproval or pressure, instead affirming his self-knowledge and autonomy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with several powerful moments, particularly around Samir's daughter's comment. However, some reflections lean toward safe, standard formulations rather than the sharper, more emotionally precise responses that would elevate this to a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from opening through deeper exploration. It could benefit from a strategic summary toward the end that consolidates key insights and collaboratively identifies a next step or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language, open-ended questions, and builds on Samir's input throughout. The collaboration could be deeper by more explicitly inviting Samir to co-create the agenda or evaluate the direction of the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support, with the psychiatrist consistently positioning Samir as the authority on his own life and the decision-maker. Phrases like 'you're still in the driver's seat' and 'you know your own rhythms better than anyone' reinforce this without being preachy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful use of evocative questions that draw out Samir's own motivations and insights, particularly 'What would make that managing feel a little more like living?' and the final question that elicited the 'permission to stop' insight. The clinician never provides reasons for change, consistently using Samir's own language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise about Samir's life resides within Samir himself, crediting him with insights rather than providing directives. The partnership could be slightly deeper by more explicitly inviting Samir to steer the session's direction in a structured way.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highly effective at eliciting change talk through well-crafted questions and skillful reinforcement using Samir's own language. The psychiatrist could further amplify or summarize Samir's change talk at key moments to deepen the emotional impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Arguably the strongest element of the session, with every instance of sustain talk met by acknowledgment without judgment and a gentle redirect. The psychiatrist never creates a power struggle and consistently reframes resistance as useful information rather than obstruction.
