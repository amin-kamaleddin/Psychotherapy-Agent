# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, avoids taking sides, and explicitly validates the patient's perspective. A slight moment of subtle pressure when probing 'the thought underneath' briefly tests unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy capturing the emotional weight of being labeled 'the problem' after 26 years of service. Reflections are complex, nuanced, and consistently hit the emotional core while holding both sides of ambivalence without collapsing them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on smoking with a coherent arc from practical attendance through self-examination to emotional engagement. Briefly loses momentum when the patient pushes back and the thread is dropped entirely rather than being gently redirected.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative space from open to close, using subtle 'we' language and inviting co-construction of meaning. The patient's contributions substantially shape the session's direction without the psychiatrist dominating or overriding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the patient's right to choose, never pressures to quit, and validates his right to be irritable and defensive. Explicitly states 'I'm not trying to get anything out of you' and works within the patient's frame throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits rather than instructs with open, curiosity-driven questions that draw out the patient's own insights. When the patient resists deeper exploration, the psychiatrist drops the thread entirely rather than using it as an opportunity to evoke further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions as a curious listener rather than an expert with answers, explicitly stating the patient knows his own experience better. No advice, information, or expert knowledge is offered that would shift the power dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully creates space for change talk to emerge organically without directly asking for reasons to quit. The change talk is present but could have been further developed with explicit elicitation of confidence statements or specific reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles minimization and resistance skillfully without arguing or inflating the challenge, meeting sustain talk with acceptance and gentle curiosity. Could have employed more double-sided reflections to hold both sustain and change talk simultaneously.
