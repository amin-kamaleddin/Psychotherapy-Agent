# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with no judgment, condescension, or implicit pressure. Validates Kevin's perspective throughout, including when he minimizes his drinking or pushes back on being labeled.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are precise and tailored to Kevin's specific language, capturing his ambivalence with sophistication. Skillfully holds multiple perspectives simultaneously (e.g., wanting fun vs. not wanting friends to check on him).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical flow from referral through incident to potential solution. Could have ended with a slightly clearer consolidation of Kevin's identified approach and next concrete step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is structured as a joint exploration where Kevin's ideas directly shape the direction. The psychiatrist's expertise is clearly in the process rather than the content, with Kevin's own solutions amplified as his contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook autonomy support with no language of obligation or pressure. Consistently frames any potential change as Kevin's own choice and reinforces that he is the expert on his own life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Kevin's own motivations and solutions through open questions rather than providing them. Could be more systematic by incorporating specific MI techniques like rulers or scaling questions to target different types of change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides advice or uses directive language; consistently positions Kevin as the expert on his own life. The psychiatrist's role is clearly that of a facilitator, with Kevin's contributions driving the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies Kevin's own change talk, particularly the moment affirming that the solution was Kevin's own. Could push slightly further on Kevin's confidence level or explore his reasons for change more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating rather than arguing (e.g., 'one night doesn't make someone a drinker'). Could more consistently use double-sided reflections to hold both sides and gently pivot toward change.
