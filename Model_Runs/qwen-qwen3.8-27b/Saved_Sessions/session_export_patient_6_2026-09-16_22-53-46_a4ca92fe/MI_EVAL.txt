# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates the client's honesty, values, and efforts. Could deepen acceptance by more explicitly normalizing ambivalence, fear, and the 'not a big deal' stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections of ambivalence, practical concerns, and the need for a low-drama plan. Emotional reflection could be stronger around embarrassment, peer pressure, and fear of being labeled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol risk and its impact on soccer, scholarships, and future goals. Progressively guides the session from concern to concrete situational planning without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client actively contributes ideas, and the counselor builds on them rather than imposing external advice. Could increase equal partnership by reducing counselor-led framing and structuring.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice, pace, and self-determination through noncoercive, hypothetical language. Avoids persuasion, pressure, or imposing a plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's own reasons, values, and practical plan. Could deepen evocation with more systematic use of scaling, importance/confidence questions, or value elaboration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and treats the client as the expert on his own life and relationships. Could strengthen partnership by waiting longer before offering suggestions and using more explicit power-sharing language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits strong client-generated change talk around protecting soccer, avoiding embarrassment, and using a low-effort exit. Could more systematically elicit and amplify desire, ability, reasons, need, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by avoiding argument, correction, or direct challenge. Could use more deliberate double-sided reflections or strategic redirection to fully expand agency around barriers.
