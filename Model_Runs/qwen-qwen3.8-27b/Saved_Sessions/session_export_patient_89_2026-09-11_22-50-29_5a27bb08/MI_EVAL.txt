# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental, but acceptance feels more task-focused than warmly individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some perspective-taking, especially around practical frustration, but deeper emotions are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on a specific, manageable behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is built with input from the patient, though the counselor still guides the overall structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are offered and pressure is avoided, but some directive instructions limit full ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** This is the weakest skill; the counselor shifts too quickly into planning instead of drawing out intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative moments, but the counselor often takes on an expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, but it is not consistently deepened or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Concerns about failure and relapse are handled well through normalization and reframing.
