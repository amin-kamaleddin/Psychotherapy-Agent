# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids pressuring Jason to accept a drinking-problem frame. The acceptance is somewhat procedural and does not fully normalize the underlying emotional stress or ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is adequate but mostly stays at the surface/content level. It does not consistently capture the emotional or relational meaning behind Jason's defensiveness and desire for control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session has a clear procedural direction centered on obtaining and reviewing the incident report. However, the original MI target related to alcohol-related behavior and values is not consistently maintained.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Jason's input and co-constructs the email request, treating him as an active participant. The collaboration is strong but remains largely task-based and managerially structured.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jason is offered choices, and his preference to handle the matter independently is respected. Autonomy support is consistent but remains mostly procedural rather than tied to broader motivational change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Questions are mostly logistical, and the initial opportunity to explore goals is not followed up. The session does not effectively draw out Jason's own motivations, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist often behaves as a partner by inviting preferences and allowing Jason to make decisions. The interaction remains somewhat expert-driven and structured, limiting it from reaching full equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are only weak early attempts to open space for change talk, and these are not pursued after Jason chooses the administrative track. The session does not use scaling, importance/confidence, or discrepancy work to generate genuine change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jason's minimization and resistance are acknowledged but not effectively softened or redirected. The psychiatrist does not use double-sided reflection, rolling with resistance, or discrepancy to examine the tension in Jason's justifications.
