# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental toward Nathaniel’s ambivalence and self-criticism. There is no pressure to quit, and the stance remains accepting throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurately captures Nathaniel’s ambivalence between needing coffee and health concerns. Some reflections remain more cognitive than emotional, missing deeper feelings such as frustration, fear, or self-judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on excessive coffee consumption and progressively narrows the target to concrete, manageable steps. There is no topic drift and the direction remains coherent and actionable.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as a collaborator, building on Nathaniel’s ideas rather than imposing solutions. The interaction reflects a shared, non-authoritarian process in which the client’s suggestions shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports choice, ownership, and freedom without pressuring a specific outcome. The client is repeatedly positioned as the expert on his own experience and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicit Nathaniel’s own ideas, motivations, and potential low-effort change steps. Evocation could be stronger by more consistently deepening the significance, importance, and confidence behind his change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Nathaniel’s perspective and avoids taking an expert role. However, some interpretive frames place a small amount of conceptual direction with the counselor rather than fully with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist creates space for Nathaniel to generate concrete change talk, such as reducing the 3 PM cup and pausing before the third cup. These ideas could be more explicitly affirmed and amplified to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged respectfully without being challenged or dismissed. The session could more actively redirect status-quo reasons toward discrepancy or change rather than leaving the focus anchored in the difficulty of change.
