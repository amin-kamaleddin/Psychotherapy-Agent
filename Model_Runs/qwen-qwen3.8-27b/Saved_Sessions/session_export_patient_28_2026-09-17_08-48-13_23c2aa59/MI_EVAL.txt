# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizes Dante’s fatigue and schedule constraints. It is not a 5 because the emotional warmth could be deeper around Dante’s concern about “taking up space.”

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician accurately reflects Dante’s tiredness, ambivalence, and desire for control. It falls short of a 5 because some guilt or self-consciousness about burdening his wife is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on re-establishing a small physical activity routine and progresses logically to a concrete next step. There is no meaningful topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Dante’s ideas strongly shape the plan, including a 10-minute walk, shoes by the door, Thursday, and a low-pressure text. It is not a 5 because the clinician occasionally introduces options that slightly steer the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by emphasizing willingness, reducing pressure on Dante’s wife, and keeping the door open for alternatives. It is not a 5 because some questions assume forward movement rather than explicitly reaffirming complete freedom to choose otherwise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician consistently invites Dante to articulate his own reasons, barriers, and possible next steps. It is not a 5 because the emerging commitment is not fully developed through scaling, confidence, or deeper evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks rather than tells, and the plan emerges mainly from Dante’s own suggestions. It is not a 5 because occasional clinician-generated ideas reduce the fully collaborative, client-led stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Dante is helped to express change talk such as not wanting to sit around, missing control, and possibly sending the text. It is not a 5 because this change talk is not fully amplified into stronger readiness or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Dante’s barriers without arguing and reframes obstacles in a manageable way. It is not a 5 because later doubt, such as “maybe I don’t go at all,” is not fully redirected into a concrete change-focused statement.
