# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, nonjudgment, and validation of Elena’s mixed feelings. Acceptance could be deepened with stronger affirmations of her worth beyond her performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures Elena’s ambivalence, guilt, and self-protective concerns. The interviewer sometimes moves quickly toward planning without fully exploring emotional intensity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on limiting after-hours messages and supporting self-respect through boundary-setting. The conversation remains anchored in Elena’s values and the target behavior throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids acting as the sole expert and co-constructs the plan with Elena. The final steps emerge from Elena’s own ideas and collaborative refinement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly invites Elena to choose and avoids pressure while exploring options. It would be stronger with more explicit reinforcement of her ultimate freedom to decide what, if anything, to implement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interviewer effectively draws out Elena’s own values, motivations, and self-compassionate language. Change talk is largely generated from Elena rather than imposed externally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently treats Elena as the expert on her own life and invites her perspective at each step. Elena’s ideas substantially shape the session and resulting plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interviewer consistently asks questions that elicit reasons for change and reinforces Elena’s sense of agency. Strong reflections amplify her self-respect, capability, and movement toward balance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Elena’s fears without arguing and gently redirects them toward possibility. A stronger performance would more consistently pivot from guilt and fear back to her identity, values, or strengths.
