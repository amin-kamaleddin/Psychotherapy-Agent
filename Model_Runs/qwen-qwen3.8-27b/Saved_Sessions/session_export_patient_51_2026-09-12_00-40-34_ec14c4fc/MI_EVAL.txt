# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was respectful and nonjudgmental, but the acceptance felt procedural and not consistently warm or individualized. It did not fully affirm Ingrid's initiative to seek a quick safety clarification.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There was limited reflective listening of Ingrid's time pressure and concern for safety. Responses were mostly factual rather than accurately understanding her perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained clear focus on the medication safety issue and prescriber follow-up throughout. Direction was strong, though it emphasized risk communication more than guiding a personally meaningful action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction followed a one-way question-answer format with limited shared agenda or co-creation. Ingrid was not actively invited to shape the plan or preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respected Ingrid's choice to leave and supported her plan without pressure. However, explicit affirmation of her ownership and decision-making was limited.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initial open questioning was appropriate, but the psychiatrist moved quickly into factual medical explanation. Ingrid's own reasons, values, and confidence were not consistently elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session was mainly expert-driven, with Ingrid as a passive recipient of information. There was little shared decision-making or invitation for her input on how to proceed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ingrid produced some change talk, but the psychiatrist only briefly affirmed it without expanding, reflecting, or deepening it. Motivation was not actively cultivated through change-talk elicitation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist did not reinforce sustain talk and accepted the time constraint without argument. However, it did not actively redirect the session into a concise client-centered commitment using MI language.
