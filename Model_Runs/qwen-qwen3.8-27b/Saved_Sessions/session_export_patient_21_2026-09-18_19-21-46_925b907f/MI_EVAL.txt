**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing physical activity with a progressive arc from exploring ambivalence to a concrete, self-owned plan. ### Collaboration

**Comments:** The entire plan—wording, location, timing, and rule—is co-constructed with Camila driving the content and the psychiatrist facilitating refinement. The power dynamic is genuinely equal; the psychiatrist brings no external recommendations and works entirely within Camila's frame.

The entire plan is framed as Camila's own creation—her words, her location, her timing, her rule—and her closing statement confirms full autonomy.

**Comments:** Never states external reasons for change; instead, every intervention draws out Camila's own motivations (enjoyment of walking, desire to reduce guilt, wish to follow through on something). The progression from mild ambivalence to an emotionally meaningful, concrete plan demonstrates motivation genuinely evoked from within.

### Partnership

Explicitly credits Camila's wisdom ('You just did something really important,' 'You've already got a solution') and positions herself as a facilitator rather than an expert.

**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and expands on change talk through open questions that draw out Camila's own solutions, then amplifies them ('You've already got a solution,' 'That sounds like it might be the actual rule you need'). **Comments:** Handles sustain talk (low energy, fear of inconsistency, scorecard fear) by validating fully and then finding the seed of change within it, rather than dismissing or arguing.
