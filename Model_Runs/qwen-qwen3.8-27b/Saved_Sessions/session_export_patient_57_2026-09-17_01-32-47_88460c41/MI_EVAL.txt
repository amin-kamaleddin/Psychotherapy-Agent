# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses validating, nonjudgmental language that normalizes Jamal’s dilemma. It falls short of a 5 because the warmth could be more individualized by affirming his courage and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects Jamal’s ambivalence, social pressure, and fear of being judged. It is not a 5 because deeper emotions such as shame, humiliation, or fear of losing status are not fully named.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on drinking, team belonging, and practical safer steps. It could be stronger if the target behavior and session focus were framed more explicitly and consistently.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Jamal as a partner and builds on his own ideas rather than imposing solutions. It does not reach a 5 because the conversation structure remains largely therapist-guided rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids directives and invites Jamal to identify his own options and priorities. It could be stronger by explicitly affirming his freedom to choose and ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks questions that draw out Jamal’s values, concerns, and possible strategies. It falls short of a 5 because the most evocative material is not consistently deepened into stronger internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, non-expert stance and invites Jamal’s perspective throughout. It is not a 5 because the psychiatrist still controls the agenda and flow rather than explicitly inviting co-decision about focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jamal offers meaningful change talk, and the counselor makes one useful affirmative reflection. The score is limited because the counselor does not consistently amplify, summarize, or strategically strengthen the change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing or dismissing it. However, the counselor does not consistently reframe or redirect sustain talk, and one statement may unintentionally reinforce an external locus of control.
