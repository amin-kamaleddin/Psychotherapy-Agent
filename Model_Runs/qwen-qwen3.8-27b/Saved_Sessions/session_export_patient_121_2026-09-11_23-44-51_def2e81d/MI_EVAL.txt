# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally nonjudgmental and validates Sofia’s frustration, but the acceptance feels somewhat formulaic and procedural. There is not enough warmth or individualized affirmation of Sofia’s pain, ambivalence, or worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate surface-level reflections but often remains at a shallow level. Deeper emotions such as hurt, loneliness, shame, and fear of humiliation are not fully explored or reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on the presenting problem, but the target behavior or goal is never clearly negotiated. The conversation shifts among several topics, which makes the focus feel scattered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is some collaboration, but the psychiatrist mostly drives the session with menus of options and scripts. Sofia does not meaningfully shape the agenda, goals, or intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and avoids overt pressure, which supports autonomy at a basic level. However, frequent prescriptive scripts and structured plans can undermine Sofia’s ownership of her own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is limited, with an initial open question followed mostly by practical advice. The psychiatrist does not consistently explore Sofia’s own values, motivations, or confidence in making change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The overall stance is strongly expert-driven rather than collaborative. The psychiatrist positions themselves as the one generating solutions while Sofia mostly selects among them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are only rare and weak efforts to cultivate change talk. When Sofia expresses potential change-oriented language, the psychiatrist does not explore or expand it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but not effectively softened or redirected toward change. At times, agreeing with Sofia’s framing reinforces externalization and maintains the barrier to change.
