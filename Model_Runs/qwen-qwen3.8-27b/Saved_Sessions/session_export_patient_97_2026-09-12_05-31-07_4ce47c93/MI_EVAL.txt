# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, nonjudgmental language and normalization to reduce shame. However, warmth is occasionally more procedural and tied to plan completion than unconditional regard for Mateo.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is good reflective listening and tracking of ambivalence and pressure. Deeper emotions such as shame, isolation, and fear of rejection are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear target behavior and progresses logically into a concrete safety plan. There is minimal drift and the discussion remains actionable.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with shared language and client input shaping the final plan. However, the psychiatrist generates most of the options and steers the problem-solving process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mateo is repeatedly affirmed as the decision-maker and his preferences are generally respected. A minor limitation is the repeated use of numeric confidence scales despite his stated discomfort.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client-driven planning is elicited through open-ended questions. The session relies more on practical problem-solving than drawing out intrinsic motivation and deeper values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Mateo’s input and allows his choices to shape the session. It would be stronger with more explicit acknowledgment that Mateo is the expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change language around doable steps and protective choices. Change talk is not consistently amplified or connected to Mateo’s deeper values and future goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without judgment and occasionally redirected toward smaller steps. It is mostly accepted rather than reframed or explored to deepen ambivalence work.
