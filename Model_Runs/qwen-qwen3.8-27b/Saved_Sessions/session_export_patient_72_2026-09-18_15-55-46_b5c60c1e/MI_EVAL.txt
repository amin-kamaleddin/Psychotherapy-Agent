# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, non-judgmental stance with specific affirmations tied to Leila's contributions. Could be elevated by adding more statements of unconditional positive regard that separate her worth from her performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic understanding that goes beyond surface-level reflections to capture deeper cognitive and emotional mechanisms (e.g., 'structure problem,' 'the edge where it shifts from thought to action'). Reflections deepen progressively throughout the session, demonstrating genuine understanding rather than mechanical mirroring.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Remarkably clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with no topic drift. Each exchange builds logically from broad wellbeing to specific, actionable plans (6am YMCA, 3-4x/week, 5-minute minimum on hard days).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook collaboration where the plan is entirely Leila's construction and the psychiatrist serves as a mirror and gentle prober. The tone is one of genuine curiosity ('I'm curious...') rather than expertise or direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unwavering reinforcement of Leila's ownership, choice, and self-determination throughout. The psychiatrist never prescribes, recommends, or offers directive advice; all planning and insight is attributed to Leila herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Leila's own motivations and plans through open, exploratory questions, producing rich and specific change talk. Could reach a 5 with explicit confidence/commitment scaling and a more formal commitment elicitation at the close.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise and wisdom are consistently positioned as residing within Leila, with language like 'That's your own solution forming' and 'You just connected the dots for yourself.' The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian, with the session direction shaped entirely by Leila's contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategically designed questions elicit Leila's own reasons, plans, and confidence, with explicit affirmations amplifying her change talk. Slight opportunity to push further by having Leila articulate commitments in her own words as a formal summary or exploring confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk elements (gym sales pressure, all-or-nothing thinking, overthinking) are handled well through reflection and reframing without argument or dismissal. Sustain talk is relatively minimal given Leila's high motivation, limiting the opportunity to demonstrate more advanced techniques like double-sided reflections.
