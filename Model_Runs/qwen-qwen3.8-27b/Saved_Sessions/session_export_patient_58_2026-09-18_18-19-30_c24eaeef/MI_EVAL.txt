# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout with normalizing statements and no dismissal or minimization. Occasionally reads as slightly structured or therapist-like rather than fully organic, and lacks a moment of deeper personal validation that would push it to a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate and consistent empathic understanding with skillful double-sided reflections that precisely capture ambivalence. Consistently tracks and reflects back the emotional undercurrents of frustration, exhaustion, and self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on the target behavior while progressively narrowing from broad exploration to specific action planning. Never allows the session to drift into unrelated topics, always remaining tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a partnership of equals using inclusive language and seeking Jamal's input before moving forward. His ideas clearly shape the session's direction, making it feel like two people working together rather than a professional directing a patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Jamal's ownership and freedom of choice, never using directive language like 'you should' or 'you need to.' The entire session is structured around Jamal's autonomy rather than compliance, and his resistance is honored without being overridden.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Jamal through evocative questions about his own goals, values, and past successes. Never provides external arguments for change; every motivational push comes from Jamal's own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Jamal, using language like 'I'm curious,' 'I'm wondering,' and 'What do you think?' Never positions themselves as having the answer and treats Jamal as the authority on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently works to elicit and expand change talk through evocative questions and amplification of Jamal's own statements. Could have gone deeper into evoking confidence and self-efficacy language, and the session ended before a clear commitment was solidified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging it as understandable while gently redirecting toward intrinsic motivation and agency. Some moments could have used more deliberate double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to be consistently exceptional.
