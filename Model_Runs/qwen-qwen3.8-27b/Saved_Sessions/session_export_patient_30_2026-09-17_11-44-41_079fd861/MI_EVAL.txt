# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental tone throughout; David's resistance met without pushback or frustration. Could benefit from slightly warmer, more individualized affirmations to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that accurately captures David's concerns about muscle weakness and his identity as a strong, capable man. A 5 would require deeper emotional exploration of the underlying fear of bodily change and identity threat.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains general focus on medication but allows the session to drift toward a vague 'wait and see' conclusion without a concrete behavioral target. Lacks strategic sequencing to move David from ambivalence toward a specific, actionable commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as collaborative exploration using inclusive language like 'let's sort through' and 'from your side.' Could be stronger by more explicitly inviting David to co-define session goals or rank his own priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces David's ownership, validating his choices, removing pressure, and respecting his decision to defer. Never imposes a recommendation or attempts to override David's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes several evocative attempts, particularly 'what would make it feel worth it to you?' and the threshold question. However, David's responses remain minimal and the psychiatrist does not systematically use scaling questions or values exploration to elicit more change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance, uses inclusive language, and adjusts rather than persists when David pushes back. Could be deepened by explicitly co-creating next steps or inviting David to set his own agenda items.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David's change talk is minimal throughout, and the psychiatrist does not consistently build on the small openings David offers. Missed opportunities to explore values (health, fitness, longevity) or elicit confidence statements about managing the medication.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles David's doubt about urgency gracefully without arguing or providing counter-evidence, redirecting to his own monitoring threshold. A 5 would require more active strategic pivoting, such as using double-sided reflections to hold both sides open.
