# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and affirming, but the acceptance feels task-oriented and formulaic. They missed a clear opportunity to validate the client's worry about 'wasting time.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes the client's concerns and shows some perspective-taking. However, the empathy is limited by the focus on advice and a lack of deeper reflection on ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is well-organized and maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity. The direction is somewhat limited because it is driven by the clinician's agenda rather than the client's exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial collaboration as the client's input shapes the plan. However, the psychiatrist remains the primary source of ideas and structure throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers flexibility and various options, which supports the client's sense of control. This is weakened by the frequent and directive nature of the specific recommendations provided.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is a weak area, as the psychiatrist provides many strategies without consistently drawing out the client's own motivations. The clinician rarely explores what physical activity means to the client personally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session includes some client choices, but the overall stance is expert-driven. The psychiatrist's position as the problem-solver prevents a true collaborative partnership from developing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers brief affirmations of the client's choices but does not strategically amplify the client's own language for change. There is little effort to elicit the client's deeper reasons for wanting to move more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively softens the emotional charge of the client's worries by normalizing difficulty and providing fallbacks. However, these concerns are not consistently transformed into opportunities to move toward change.
