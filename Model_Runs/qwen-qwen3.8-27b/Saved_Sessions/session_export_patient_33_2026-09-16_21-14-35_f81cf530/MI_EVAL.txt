# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist creates a safe, nonjudgmental space and avoids moralizing when Diego rejects abstinence. Acceptance is strong but remains more problem-focused than fully unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects Diego's ambivalence and practical pressures accurately, including the "tightrope" of heroin use and multiple stressors. Empathy could go deeper into shame, fear, housing insecurity, or emotional burden.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear harm-reduction focus and moves efficiently toward a concrete safety plan. Direction would be strengthened by a closing summary, timeframe, and broader harm-reduction consolidation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A major strength; the psychiatrist co-constructs the plan and repeatedly builds on Diego's ideas. The patient's contributions clearly guide the session from prioritization to final note wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects Diego's pacing, avoids abstinence pressure, and positions him as the decision-maker. Could include more explicit statements affirming his freedom to choose or not act.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits patient-generated solutions rather than providing advice, such as the approach, first sentence, and note content. Evocation could be deeper by exploring values, confidence, or intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and lets Diego set priorities. Could more explicitly name Diego as the expert in his own life and reduce counselor-generated options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical safety-related change talk around Narcan, housemate communication, and sterile kits. Would be strengthened by explicitly eliciting values, confidence, desire, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without arguing, correcting, or pressuring, and uses double-sided reflections to hold risk and coping. Could more explicitly reframe sustain talk into change talk while preserving autonomy.
