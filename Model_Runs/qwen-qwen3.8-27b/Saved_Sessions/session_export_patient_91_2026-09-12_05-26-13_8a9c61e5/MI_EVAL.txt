# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys respect, nonjudgment, and validation throughout the session. However, the support can feel somewhat formulaic and occasionally shifts toward problem-solving rather than deeper relational support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections and demonstrates good understanding of Marcus’s ambivalence and anxiety. Empathy becomes less consistent as the session moves into advice and planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on the target behavior of glucose monitoring and moves toward a concrete plan. There is some brief drift into CGM and insurance, but the conversation stays centered on the main goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marcus contributes ideas to the final plan, showing genuine collaboration. However, the psychiatrist often initiates the agenda and provides much of the problem-solving content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently emphasizes choice, ownership, and support for Marcus’s decisions. The main limitation is that detailed suggestions and options can subtly shift the interaction toward the clinician’s agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses rulers and asks what would make the plan easier, helping Marcus articulate motivation. Deeper exploration of personal values, future benefits, or internal reasons is limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are clear attempts to convey choice and support, but the psychiatrist often takes on an expert advisory role. The collaboration is present but not consistently equal throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, and Marcus’s confidence increases as the plan becomes concrete. The session does not consistently deepen, amplify, or expand this change-relevant language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges barriers without arguing and reframes concerns into manageable steps. The session could more actively pivot sustain talk into change talk, but the overall effect is nonconfrontational and supportive.
