# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard maintained throughout, with specific, contextual affirmations rather than generic praise. No moments of judgment, impatience, or hidden agenda.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections that captured nuanced emotions and subtext, such as the Friday-night split and the weight in 'letting myself off the hook.' Goes well beyond paraphrasing to articulate feelings and meanings Luka hadn't fully expressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical arc with consistent focus on the drinking and aggression, each turn building on the previous. Direction is somewhat implicit; a briefer orientation at the start would have strengthened the shared sense of purpose.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully co-constructed session with no pre-set agenda; direction emerges from Luka's own statements. The closing exchange models true collaborative problem-solving, resulting in a client-generated strategy he owns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional and explicit autonomy support, from opening agenda question to closing strategy question. No directive language or external standards imposed; suggestions are consistently framed as possibilities.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation with zero external advice or reasons for change provided; every key insight and strategy emerges from Luka himself. The closing question asking what 'would land' for Friday-night-self is peak evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative, equal-power stance with the client placed in the driver's seat from the opening question. Insights consistently framed as observations ('I notice...') rather than declarations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective cultivation of desire, reason, and some ability talk through well-timed open questions and language amplification. Could have been more systematic across all four DARN categories, particularly confidence/ability talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled use of double-sided reflections and reframes to soften sustain talk, such as the 'only passing grade is zero' reframe. One moment ('one beer and suddenly I'm the guy I was before') could have used a more active double-sided reflection.
