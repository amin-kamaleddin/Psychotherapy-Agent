**Comments:** The conversation is genuinely co-constructed, with the psychiatrist asking open questions, reflecting, and inviting Jamal's perspective at every turn without dominating. Jamal does the heavy lifting of meaning-making, and the session flows naturally between both parties.

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Jamal's autonomy and ownership through language like 'you don't need my permission to do that' and 'you don't have to fix it all today.' The psychiatrist never pressures, implies obligation, or suggests Jamal's choices are wrong.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire session is structured around open questions that draw out Jamal's own understanding and motivation, with the psychiatrist never providing external reasons for change. Jamal himself articulates the pattern, identifies the solution, and takes ownership — the hallmark of skilled evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance by positioning Jamal as the expert on his own experience and acknowledging his autonomy. Could be slightly stronger by more explicitly inviting Jamal to name patterns before interpreting them, maintaining true equality throughout.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully cultivates change talk through strategic questions that elicit concrete change statements and notices shifts toward change without being heavy-handed. ### Softening Sustain Talk
