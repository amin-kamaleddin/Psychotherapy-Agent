# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm throughout a highly challenging session, receiving compliments without overinflating them. Slightly formulaic and performative quality of presence (e.g., 'You caught me') prevents a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening overall, but initially missteps by reframing Amina's 'it's just a fact' as a feeling before correcting course. The correction is good but represents empathy filtered through a clinical lens rather than meeting the client exactly where she is.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Follows Amina's lead appropriately but lacks a clear through-line, strategic momentum, or gentle movement toward even a small goal. No summaries or identification of focus for future sessions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions self as co-participant through language and real-time behavior change in response to feedback. Collaboration is somewhat asymmetric in practice, as the therapist does most of the verbal work while Amina remains passive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding and consistent affirmation of Amina's right to choose, with no lapses in pressure or implied obligation. The self-correction moment acknowledging their own violation of her autonomy is particularly powerful.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Only one evocative question attempted, with no follow-up after Amina's resistant response. Could have used alternative low-pressure approaches such as offering a choice or a minimal scaling question.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with explicit power-sharing language and genuine self-correction. Could be deeper by actively inviting Amina to co-create the session's agenda or structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Only one evocative question attempted, and Amina produces no change talk in the session. Could have made one or two additional subtle attempts to plant seeds of change talk given the limited opportunity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Amina's resistance well without arguing, dismissing, or reframing it as progress. Met primarily through avoidance rather than active pivoting with techniques like double-sided reflections.
