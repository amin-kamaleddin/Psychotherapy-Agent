# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with multiple affirmations of Thomas's choices and values. However, the warmth occasionally feels slightly performative in delivery and does not reach the consistently deep, individualized warmth of a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening with accurate reflections of ambivalence and complex push-pull feelings, including the tension between noticing and dismissing. Some reflections could have gone deeper into the emotional meaning, such as exploring what the wife's 'look' means to him.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a reasonably clear focus on Thomas's health behaviors with a logical session flow from opening through exploring his boundaries and what would feel good. Could be sharper in consistently anchoring discussions back to the specific target behavior rather than general life stress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as a collaborative exploration and actively seeks Thomas's input before proceeding without imposing information or judgments. However, the collaboration is somewhat asymmetrical in practice, with the psychiatrist doing the majority of talking and structuring.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Thomas's autonomy with statements like 'no pressure at all' and avoids any directive or pressuring language. Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming that Thomas is in complete charge of his own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes good evocative questions like 'what would a week in a good spot feel like?' but lacks depth and follow-through when Thomas provides change talk. Misses opportunities to develop statements like 'I like when I'm sharp' into deeper exploration of what that means to him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a non-authoritarian stance with language like 'I'm not here to have a preset agenda' and consistently invites Thomas's perspective. However, the session is somewhat one-directional in practice, with limited evidence of truly ceding control of the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some attempts to elicit change talk through open questions, but does not consistently follow up on, amplify, or deepen Thomas's brief change statements. Notable gap in building momentum from statements like 'I like when I'm sharp' and 'not feel so foggy.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not argue or counter Thomas's sustain talk, but responses are mostly neutral acknowledgments rather than active strategic pivots. Lacks consistent use of double-sided reflections to honor the sustain talk while gently tilting toward change.
