# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates Amira’s concerns without pushing past her ambivalence. Warmth is strong but could be deeper by affirming her worth more consistently beyond her effort or plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is solid and captures key goals, fears, and practical concerns. Some responses remain more cognitive and planning-focused than fully emotionally exploratory.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and moves clearly toward a realistic, concrete action plan. Direction is especially strong in guiding Amira from ambivalence to a specific first step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs a practical plan and adjusts based on Amira’s input. However, the psychiatrist supplies much of the structure and options before fully eliciting the client’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice and low-pressure language are used well, especially around missed days and avoiding rigid commitments. A few directive prompts, such as repeatedly asking when she will start, slightly reduce the emphasis on client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling and implementation questions effectively elicit client-driven planning language and confidence. The session could deepen evocation by exploring values, meaning, and personal change reasons more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows Amira to shape the final plan. The partnership is somewhat uneven because structured options and solution components are introduced before fully drawing out the client’s own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amira offers meaningful change talk, and the psychiatrist supports it with affirmations, scaling, and targeted questions. Change talk could be more consistently amplified, summarized, and expanded around values and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles barriers and fear of failure without argumentation and reframes misses as data rather than failure. More sophisticated techniques, such as double-sided reflections, could better convert sustain talk into change talk.
