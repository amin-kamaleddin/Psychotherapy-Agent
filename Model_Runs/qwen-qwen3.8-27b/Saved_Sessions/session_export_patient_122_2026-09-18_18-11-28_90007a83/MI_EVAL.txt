# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and specific unconditional positive regard throughout. Acceptance is genuine rather than formulaic, tailored to Sofia's specific situation and never dismissive of her self-doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflective listening that captures multiple emotional dimensions simultaneously. Reflections are precise and specific, demonstrating deep understanding of Sofia's internal experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally clear focus on the target behavior with logical progression from exploration to commitment. A brief drift into the loneliness/social connection topic slightly broadened the focus, though it was handled skillfully.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative practice where all plan elements (walk, shoes before sitting, sister text, drop-in class) are co-constructed. We-language and joint framing throughout signal genuine partnership rather than direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never prescribes or pressures; consistently reinforces Sofia's ownership and right to choose. Even the concrete action step emerges entirely from Sofia, with the psychiatrist framing options as trials rather than obligations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All questions are designed to draw motivation from within Sofia rather than imposing external reasons. The psychiatrist names and amplifies Sofia's own language, ensuring motivation feels genuinely self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Sofia as the expert on her own life, explicitly crediting her insights and problem-solving. The psychiatrist serves as a mirror and question-asker, never assuming the expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions consistently elicit rich change talk, and the psychiatrist skillfully amplifies Sofia's own commitment language. Sofia moves from ambivalence to clear action commitment ('Maybe Thursday after work') through evocation alone.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated as legitimate, then strategically reframed (e.g., 'the voice kills plans, not the plans') without arguing or discounting. Double-sided reflections and gentle pivots consistently redirect toward change while honoring the concern.
