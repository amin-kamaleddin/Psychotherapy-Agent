# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental tone with strong normalization of Marco's struggles. Occasionally gets subsumed by the strategic MI process, preventing fully deepened affirmation of the client's worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Excellent reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, including noticing subtle shifts in Marco's voice. Occasionally prioritizes MI strategy over sitting with deeper emotional pain, such as the shame spiral.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong logical progression from exploration to concrete planning with clear focus on the target behavior. Could be more streamlined at times and would benefit from a stronger closing with summary and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook example of MI collaboration — every major insight emerges from genuine co-construction between both parties. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian, and the client feels safe enough to be deeply vulnerable.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong non-directive stance with consistent return of ownership to the client and explicit removal of pressure. Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Marco's right to NOT change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent and effective use of open-ended questions that elicit the client's own language and strategies. Lacks strategic depth in tools like confidence/important rulers and could more explicitly evoke the client's core values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance maintained throughout with consistent use of the client's own language and agenda. Occasionally positions the psychiatrist as the one who 'names' insights rather than a true equal partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits substantial change talk through strategic questions and reflections of the client's own words. Lacks explicit consolidation or summarization of the rich change talk generated, and misses the opportunity to use a confidence ruler.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframes sustain talk as information rather than failure without argumentation or dismissal. Could explore deeper sustain talk about hopelessness from repeated past failures more thoroughly before moving forward.
