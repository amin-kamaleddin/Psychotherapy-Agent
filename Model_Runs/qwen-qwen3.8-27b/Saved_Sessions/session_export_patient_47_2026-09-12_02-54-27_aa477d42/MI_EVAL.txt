# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations of Evelyn’s efforts. It falls short of a 5 because acceptance is more procedural than deeply affirming of her values and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture the behavior and ambivalence well. Empathy remains somewhat cognitive, missing key feelings such as embarrassment, loneliness, and fear of being seen as “weird.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on pub drinking with little drift and a clear behavioral goal. It becomes solution-focused a bit early, reducing space for deeper exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evelyn actively shapes the plan through her choices and preferences. Collaboration is effective but therapist-led, with the psychiatrist contributing many of the practical ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong choice language and “experiment” framing support autonomy. Frequent options subtly narrow her sense of freedom and could be balanced with more explicit ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session evokes confidence and specific action steps. It is limited by not exploring Evelyn’s deeper motivations, values, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evelyn’s input is invited and used in planning. Repeated structured suggestions place the psychiatrist in an expert problem-solving role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling, future-focused questions, and affirmations produce clear behavioral change talk. It could be strengthened by exploring the personal meaning and values behind change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally not reinforced or met with argument. Several key ambivalent statements are left unprocessed with limited double-sided reflections.
