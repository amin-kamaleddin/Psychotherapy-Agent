# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Jonathan’s exhaustion. Acceptance is slightly limited by continued task suggestions after Jonathan repeatedly signals he does not want to commit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture the surface-level burden of diabetes management but remain mostly factual. Deeper emotions such as loss of control, fear of failure, and ambivalence are underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on diabetes self-management, especially medication and monitoring. Direction becomes fragmented by many microtasks, drifting away from Jonathan’s central concern of being overwhelmed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and offers choices, creating a collaborative tone. However, the counselor largely prescribes the menu of options rather than co-creating direction from Jonathan’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Autonomy support improves late in the session by offering “do nothing today” and validating the need to pause. Earlier, repeated tasks and confidence ratings create pressure and reduce autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used, but the counselor quickly shifts to concrete suggestions and confidence ratings. Jonathan’s own values, motivations, and implicit change talk are not sufficiently elicited or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and offers options, but the interaction remains expert-driven. Jonathan’s contributions do not fully shape the session until he clearly states he wants to stop.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are few attempts to cultivate client-owned language in favor of change. Jonathan’s minimal change talk is not amplified with values-based questions or deeper follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Jonathan, but sustain talk remains central and is not consistently softened. There is limited use of double-sided reflections, rolling with resistance, or pivots to values and concerns.
