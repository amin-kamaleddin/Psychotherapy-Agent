# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirming statements, though some responses became formulaic and could have deeper individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and captured ambivalence, but could have deepened the emotional and values-based meaning behind the client's statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on presence and the target behavior, though pacing could have moved more efficiently toward commitment or next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited client input and responded well to the client's choices, though equal partnership and joint agenda-setting could be more explicit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice, ownership, and low pressure, offering options and permission to stop or tweak the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited the client's own goals and change talk, but could have explored values and intrinsic motivation more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited the client's perspective in shaping the plan, though more explicit power-sharing could strengthen the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk, but could have more consistently amplified and elaborated the strongest change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged the client's effort and reframed resistance without arguing, though more double-sided reflections could have been used.
