# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and nonjudgmental warmth throughout, with clear refusal to lecture. However, some reflections occasionally feel slightly performative or scripted rather than fully organic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with nuanced, specific reflections that consistently land on the emotional core of what Marco is expressing. The reflections name exact dynamics (e.g., the 'doorbell I don't answer' metaphor, the 'record' logic) without overreaching, demonstrating therapeutically precise perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and coherent focus on smoking with well-managed pacing and logical progression from lecture fatigue to a small testable action. A slightly more concrete collaborative closing step would have provided a clearer directional landing point.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a genuine collaboration shaped entirely by Marco's own metaphors (door, doorbell, record, tradesman), with the psychiatrist building on them rather than imposing structure. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, and the statement 'I don't want to hand you another script to perform' powerfully reinforces collaborative intent.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with explicit language such as 'I'm not here to tell you what to do,' 'you don't have to answer,' and 'I'm not going to rush that.' All interventions are framed as Marco's choice, with no directive language or pressure present.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Marco through open questions, never providing external reasons for change. All change talk (noticing breath changes, the door metaphor, the counting idea) emerges organically from the client, though change talk could have been more explicitly amplified to deepen self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Marco, never providing advice or labeling his thinking as wrong. The session is genuinely shaped by Marco's contributions, with the psychiatrist's role limited to reflecting, deepening, and opening space.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully creates conditions for change talk to emerge naturally through masterful evocative questions (e.g., 'what would a small check look like?'). However, once change talk appeared, it could have been more explicitly amplified and reflected back to deepen the moment further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well without challenging or arguing, using sophisticated redirection (e.g., showing Marco he is already questioning his own 'record' logic). Could have used one or two more explicit double-sided reflections to more actively hold and create ambivalence.
