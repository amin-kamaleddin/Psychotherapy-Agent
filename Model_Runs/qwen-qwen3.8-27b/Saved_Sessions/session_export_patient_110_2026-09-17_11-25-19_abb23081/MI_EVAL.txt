# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with warm, non-judgmental language throughout. Richard's contributions are validated as valuable and wise, with no moments of labeling, dismissal, or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are specific, emotionally attuned, and frequently go one level deeper than Richard's surface statements. The psychiatrist accurately captures underlying motivations and values rather than interpreting statements as resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session progresses logically from initial impression through plan development while maintaining focus on the target behavior. The ending feels slightly abrupt, with the final side-effect response being minimal and lacking a clear closing direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Richard's spontaneous contributions consistently become the next focus of exploration, demonstrating genuine co-construction rather than counselor-driven structure. The session flows as a dialogue between equals with Richard setting the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never pressures or implies obligation, consistently framing all actions as Richard's choices. His self-generated plan is explicitly affirmed as his own, reinforcing ownership and freedom throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively draw out Richard's own motivations, solutions, and success criteria rather than providing information. However, some powerful change talk statements could have been more explicitly amplified and consolidated before moving on.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never gives advice and consistently positions Richard as the expert on his own life, marriage, and values. The session is shaped entirely by Richard's responses rather than a predetermined structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is frequently evoked and amplified through specific, skillful reflections of Richard's agency and motivations. The development of ability and confidence language could have been more explicit to further build self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently reframed or explored without reinforcement, with Richard's concerns validated but not amplified. The final side-effect worry could have been addressed with a more active softening strategy such as a double-sided reflection.
