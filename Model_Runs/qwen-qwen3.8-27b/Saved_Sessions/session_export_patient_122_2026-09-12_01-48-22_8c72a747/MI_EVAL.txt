# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Sofia’s concerns without blaming her. However, acceptance feels procedural and is often followed by problem-solving rather than deeper warmth or appreciation for her self-awareness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some acknowledgments of Sofia’s fear, self-criticism, and ambivalence, but the reflections remain mostly surface-level. The psychiatrist often moves quickly into rules and strategies instead of fully exploring her emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, progressive focus on increasing movement and addressing stress-related eating. However, the direction is more behavioral and task-oriented than fully MI-consistent or collaborative.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia’s preferences shape some details, such as time, location, reminders, and the water-first approach. Still, the psychiatrist largely supplies the main plans, scripts, and decision rules, keeping the balance uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia to choose some options and accepts her preferences, but the session remains directive. Frequent instructions, scripts, and rules may reduce Sofia’s sense of ownership, especially given her fear of rigid plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides many reasons for change and specific strategies rather than drawing out Sofia’s own motivations. Her values, goals, and personal reasons for change are not deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist takes an expert-driven role and introduces much of the plan and language. Sofia is treated as a collaborator on details, but not consistently as the expert on her own life, priorities, and readiness.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia offers some change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently deepen, amplify, or revisit it. The session lacks scaling, exploratory questions, or follow-up that helps her develop her own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia’s sustain talk and ambivalence are acknowledged, but the responses mainly redirect her into additional rules and contingencies. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to use double-sided reflections and gently roll with her resistance.
