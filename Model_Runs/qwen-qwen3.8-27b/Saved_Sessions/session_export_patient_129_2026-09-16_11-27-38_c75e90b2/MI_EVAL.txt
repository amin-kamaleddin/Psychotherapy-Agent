# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong nonjudgmental stance with repeated validation and no moralizing. The psychiatrist respected Tyler's guarded position without challenging him.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was generally accurate, especially around feeling forced and valuing the peer group. It occasionally stayed surface-level and could have explored deeper shame or loyalty conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the referral concerns, including the stash, peers, and parental conflict. It could have more consistently moved toward a specific change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited Tyler's input and built on his stated priorities. Collaboration would improve with more explicit co-creation of a shared agenda or plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong support for choice and agency, with clear statements that Tyler is in charge. The psychiatrist avoided pressure, lecturing, or coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were useful open questions inviting Tyler to identify values and goals. The psychiatrist could have used more follow-up evocative questions to deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The overall stance was collaborative and respectful of Tyler's expertise. Some therapist-generated options made the interaction slightly less fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evoked some client-centered change language, such as saying no and handling his own life. Change talk could have been more systematically developed and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met without arguing or amplifying resistance. More double-sided reflections could have gently connected peer belonging with safety or future risk.
