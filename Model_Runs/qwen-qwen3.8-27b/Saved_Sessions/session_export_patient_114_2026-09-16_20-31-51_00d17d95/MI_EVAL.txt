# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, affirming, nonjudgmental language that normalizes guilt and defensiveness. Warmth is consistent, though it sometimes relies on predictable reflection-plus-question patterns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both surface and deeper motives, such as protecting the transcript and avoiding being sent home. Some responses feel structurally repetitive and miss richer emotional deepening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on drinking in social contexts and progresses logically toward a concrete, testable action step. A brief midpoint summary of the plan would have reinforced the overall direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ryan is consistently positioned as the expert on his own life, and his ideas shape the session. The interaction could feel more mutually collaborative with occasional parallel thoughts or alternative angles.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions repeatedly invite Ryan’s own choice, experimentation, and values without coercion or implied obligations. It is nearly expert-level, with only slightly less explicit affirmation that the decision is entirely his.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evocative questions effectively draw out Ryan’s own motivations, deeper needs, and concrete steps. The cycle would be completed by explicitly exploring confidence or ability with a scaling question.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids an expert stance and shares power by crediting Ryan’s ideas and inviting his perspective. The pattern can feel mechanical and lacks more genuinely bidirectional exploratory language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicit strong desire, ability, and need statements about staying in school, controlling drinking, and trying a non-alcoholic option. It would be stronger if key change talk moments were explicitly amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement, but responses are mostly simple two-sided reflections. More strategic double-sided or discrepancy-based responses would better move the session past the reasons to stay as-is.
