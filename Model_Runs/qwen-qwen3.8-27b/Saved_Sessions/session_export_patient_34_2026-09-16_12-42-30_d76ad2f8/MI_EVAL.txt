# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids arguing or moralizing. However, the acceptance feels somewhat procedural and task-focused rather than deeply warm or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers some reflective listening early in the session, especially around frustration and concern. However, the empathy remains surface-level and often shifts to practical options rather than exploring deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays relatively focused on the conduct incident and the desired outcome. However, the MI target broadens less effectively than needed, as the conversation drifts into problem-solving and advocacy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and offers options, which supports some partnership. However, the counselor remains the primary strategist, making the collaboration uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently affirms Dylan's choice and freedom to decide. However, the repeated scripting and tactical guidance reduce the strength of autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks a few useful open questions but does not consistently draw out Dylan's own motivations, values, or readiness to change. The counselor often supplies the reasoning and language instead.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Dylan's choice and presents multiple options. However, the expert-driven style and scripted advice limit genuine power-sharing and partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are only weak or rare attempts to cultivate change talk. The session focuses more on minimizing consequences than evoking Dylan's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct contradiction but does not effectively soften or redirect Dylan's minimization. The proposed script actually reinforces sustain talk rather than challenging it.
