# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys unconditional positive regard by normalizing Mateo’s pain, slow progress, and concerns without judgment. The tone remains warm, respectful, and validating throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both the content and emotional meaning behind Mateo’s statements, including his ambivalence and fear of being dismissed. Perspective-taking is strong, nuanced, and consistently maintained.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on rehab progress and pain management without major drift. It also moves strategically from ambivalence toward a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is a genuinely shared exploration rather than clinician-led advice. Mateo’s ideas clearly co-construct the solution and shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice through open questions and avoids pressure, but some prompts subtly steer toward a specific outcome. More explicit affirmation of Mateo’s freedom to choose, including not implementing the plan, would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently elicits Mateo’s own reasons, values, and strategies rather than providing external motivational content. Change language remains client-generated and effectively used throughout.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Mateo is treated as the expert on his own body and experience, and the counselor avoids assuming the expert role. His ideas directly drive the plan, including the hand signal, pain thresholds, and pause strategy.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicit concrete change talk and a workable pause plan. The counselor could more consistently amplify and consolidate Mateo’s own change language to further strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without argument by reframing barriers as self-protection and safety. The session could more frequently pivot toward values and positive identity to make the softening more transformative.
