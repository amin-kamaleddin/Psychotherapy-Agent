# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Ryan's resistance and normalizing his fear without labeling or shaming. Slightly clinical polish in tone prevents it from reaching the depth of unguarded positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and nuanced reflections that simultaneously capture content, emotion, and underlying meaning. Empathic understanding progressively deepens from cognitive to somatic levels throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive arc from exploring tension to understanding the specific incident to embodied awareness, always staying focused on the target behavior. Pacing is patient but purposeful, with each question building on the previous answer.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is genuinely co-constructed, with Ryan's contributions consistently shaping the direction. The interaction feels like a conversation between equals rather than a clinician directing a client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly removes pressure throughout with phrases like 'no solutions needed' and 'not a rule, just a thought.' Never uses directive language; the entire session is framed as optional exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external rationale for change; consistently draws motivation from within Ryan using open, evocative questions. Progressively deepens from cognitive to somatic levels, culminating in Ryan's powerful self-generated insight about 'already knowing.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently locates authority in Ryan's perspective with language like 'what do you make of' and 'from your side.' The psychiatrist never positions themselves as the expert, treating Ryan as the authority on his own experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk by taking Ryan's tentative observations and giving them weight and significance. Creates a cumulative effect where change talk becomes increasingly specific and owned by the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never reinforces or argues against sustain talk; consistently validates it before gently pivoting toward deeper exploration. Every instance of minimization is met with a masterful double-sided reflection that honors resistance while deepening change talk.
