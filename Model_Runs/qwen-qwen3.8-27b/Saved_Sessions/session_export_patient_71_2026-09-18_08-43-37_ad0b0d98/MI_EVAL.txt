# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout and never judges Laura's all-or-nothing thinking or lapses in consistency. However, the warmth remains somewhat professional and lacks explicit, individualized affirmations of Laura's worth independent of her performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates exceptional empathic accuracy with complex reflections that capture ambivalence without oversimplifying, such as identifying the underlying fear of becoming a character flaw and reframing Laura's growth in real time. Reflections are consistently deep, emotionally resonant, and never generic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains exceptional focus on the target behavior from start to finish, with each exchange building logically on the previous one. Even when Laura introduces broader concerns, the psychiatrist keeps them tethered to the specific behavior and concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight is co-constructed through dialogue, with the psychiatrist using language like 'I'm curious' and 'I wonder' to position the exploration as joint. Laura's own metaphors are picked up and extended, demonstrating genuine collaborative building with a truly equal power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a standout feature, with the psychiatrist consistently reinforcing Laura's ownership and never using directive or pressuring language. Even suggestions are framed as questions, and her bracing and ambivalence are met with respect rather than gentle pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight comes from Laura's own mouth, with the psychiatrist using questions specifically designed to elicit rather than direct. The session moves from ambivalence to a concrete, self-generated plan entirely through evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise about Laura's life and change resides within her, never telling her what to do or what the solution should be. The session is structured around Laura's own language and metaphors, with the psychiatrist serving as a skilled questioner and reflector.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes multiple instances of change talk through strategic questions and reflections, and amplifies them effectively. However, there is room to push slightly further on confidence and ability language and elicit more explicit commitment beyond Laura's voluntary statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk skillfully by validating its logic before gently exploring the cost, using normalization and strategic reflection rather than direct confrontation. The softening is done effectively, though the evaluation notes it could be pushed slightly further in some moments.
