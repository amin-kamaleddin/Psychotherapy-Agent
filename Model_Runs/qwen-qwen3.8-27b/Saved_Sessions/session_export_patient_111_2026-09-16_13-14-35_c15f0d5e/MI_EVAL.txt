# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and avoids arguing with Richard's resistance. The stance is supportive, though somewhat procedural rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Richard's perspective, including his routine, reluctance, and family tension. Deeper emotional themes such as autonomy, shame, or fear of conflict could have been explored more consistently.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on smoking-related conflict and narrows it to a practical target behavior. The early multi-question approach slightly dilutes focus, and the target shifts away from cessation itself.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Richard's input and adapts when he rejects scripts or a formal plan. Collaboration is strong, though the clinician still drives much of the structure and agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently affirmed, including Richard's right to choose, avoid scripts, and decline follow-up. The early structuring and scaling questions make the pacing slightly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes some evocative attempts, such as exploring what would move the importance score from 2 to 3. The session often moves too quickly into problem-solving without fully eliciting Richard's own values or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and follows Richard's lead on key decisions. Partnership is not fully equal throughout, particularly in the opening and when introducing structured options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Richard offers some action talk, such as trying the porch or garage and avoiding smoking inside. The psychiatrist does not consistently reinforce, amplify, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids reinforcing sustain talk or arguing with Richard's resistance. More skilled redirection and double-sided reflections could have helped Richard examine the meaning of his sustain talk.
