# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently normalizes, affirms, and supports Nadine without judgment. It falls short of a 5 because some acceptance is framed more practically than as deeper, explicit regard for Nadine as a parent and person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections recognize fatigue, worry, and self-doubt, but they are often brief and quickly shift into psychoeducation or problem-solving. There are missed opportunities to explore underlying emotional meaning and ambivalence more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on relevant concerns and moves Nadine toward a concrete plan for the night. Direction is slightly diluted by repeated checklists and additional instructions near the end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor collaborates well on the text message and nightly plan, offering options and accepting Nadine's version. Collaboration is not fully equal because the counselor remains a strong expert presence throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices, affirms permission, and supports Nadine's decisions throughout. It is not a 5 because repeated directives, checklists, and safety instructions still add a controlling element.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some motivation, such as Nadine's need for rest and willingness to try a short break. However, much of the plan is clinician-generated rather than drawn from Nadine's own values, strengths, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nadine's input shapes parts of the session, and the counselor follows her lead in many moments. However, the interaction is often expert-led, with the counselor supplying milestones, options, and step-by-step instructions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively reinforces Nadine's movement toward concrete, doable actions. A 5 would require more skillful development of Nadine's own values, confidence, and deeper reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor gently softens self-critical and status-quo language through normalization and permission. It would be stronger with double-sided reflections that actively help Nadine resolve the ambivalence itself.
