# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating stance throughout. Some responses feel more evaluative than fully unconditional positive regard, so the highest level of acceptance is not consistently demonstrated.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist captures some emotional tone but often shifts quickly into problem-solving. Greater depth is needed around Sofia’s self-doubt, self-criticism, exhaustion, and fear of avoidance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear focus on small morning movement and practical implementation. The early introduction of multiple options is manageable, though a single target could be focused on more consistently.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia actively shapes the plan, and the psychiatrist adapts to her preferences and limits. More client-generated solutions would strengthen the collaborative stance further.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Sofia’s choices, preferences, and ownership with options rather than directives. The degree of structured advice and prescribed tools may slightly limit her role as the primary decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia is asked about what feels doable and generates some motivation for change. Deeper exploration of her values, meaning, and confidence would strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia’s input and adapts to her choices, creating some sense of collaboration. The interaction remains somewhat expert-led, so Sofia is not consistently positioned as the expert on her own change process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers meaningful change talk, and the psychiatrist asks helpful choice-oriented questions. The counselor does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen that change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia’s concerns are met without judgment, and some statements reduce pressure effectively. More skilled double-sided reflections or reframing would better turn ambivalence into change motivation.
