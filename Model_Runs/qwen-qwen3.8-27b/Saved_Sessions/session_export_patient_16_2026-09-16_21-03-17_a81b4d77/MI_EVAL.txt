### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and reduces the stigma of the referral. Warmth could be strengthened with more explicit affirmations of Anika’s worth or courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening effectively captures Anika’s ambivalence between social vaping with Sarah and her basketball/school concerns. The empathy is strong but remains more cognitive than emotionally explicit.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior: marijuana/vaping use related to basketball, school eligibility, and performance. Each question moves toward a concrete, behavior-specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Anika’s own ideas rather than imposing a predetermined intervention. The interaction has a clear, equal-partner dynamic throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Anika’s choice is strongly supported through low-pressure, optional, and self-controlled monitoring plans. Her boundary against journaling and overanalysis is respected and incorporated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Most of the plan emerges from Anika’s own statements, motivations, and solutions. The score is limited by a few moments where the psychiatrist suggests ideas before fully evoking Anika’s own answer.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Anika is consistently positioned as the expert on her own life, preferences, and needs. The psychiatrist invites her wisdom rather than driving the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully evokes clear change statements about protecting court time, avoiding suspension, and a short-term test. Change talk could be amplified and summarized more explicitly as a core value or identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing Anika’s sustain talk and acknowledges the social appeal of vaping with Sarah. More double-sided reflections could more actively shift the balance toward change.
