# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. Richard's concerns were validated without moralizing, labeling, or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Richard's ambivalence and emotional shifts. The counselor stayed attuned to his values and perspective throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation remained clearly focused on cholesterol management and medication adherence. Questions consistently moved the session toward a concrete, client-centered plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally treated Richard as the expert in his own life. Some clinician-led summaries and structured pacing slightly reduced the collaborative, client-led quality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor strongly supported Richard's autonomy and internal choice. Language emphasized his pace, ownership, and self-defined reasons for change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicited Richard's own motivations and confidence. The score is limited by occasional therapist-generated labels and mildly leading interpretations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Richard's ideas substantially shaped the plan and conversation. The session was still somewhat guided by the initial medication-adherence focus and a structured pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicited desire, reasons, ability, and commitment. The main limitation was occasionally interpreting change talk rather than continuing to draw it out.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled respectfully without arguing or minimizing Richard's concerns. Some ambivalence could have been resolved more consistently with double-sided reflections.
