# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, validating Sofia's defensive patterns (minimizing, externalizing) without correction or disappointment. Falls short of a 5 because acceptance is sometimes channeled through interpretive reflections rather than simply being present with her pain without immediately reframing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections that capture ambivalence and emotional contradictions (e.g., wanting to reach out while fearing being 'the weird one'). Could have sat longer with the accumulated emotional weight (loneliness, repeated social rejection) before pivoting toward solution-building.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from exploring the problem to a concrete, manageable action step (texting a meme). No wandering or off-topic drift; pacing is well-managed without rushing or losing momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely positions Sofia as the expert on her own experience and never prescribes solutions. The resulting plan is entirely Sofia's own, explicitly credited as such ('You built yourself a way to find out').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, natural, and non-performative; frames choices as Sofia's own and never pressures her to commit. Avoids all controlling language; the final commitment comes entirely from Sofia's mouth.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multi-layered and progressive, drawing motivation from within Sofia at each stage (problem → desired outcome → barrier → solution → confidence). Every motivational element is elicited from Sofia rather than imposed externally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear equal-partner stance with curious, non-directive language and never assumes authority over Sofia's experience. Falls short of a 5 because some reflections feel slightly interpretive rather than genuine co-exploration invitations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful and consistent progression from initial ambivalence to genuine client-generated commitment ('I'll try it. I guess'). The psychiatrist never tells Sofia what to do; every step toward change comes from her own language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledges the validity of sustain talk and uses gentle contradiction to reveal Sofia's own ambivalence without confrontation. Could have more explicitly normalized the reasons to maintain the status quo before redirecting toward change.
