# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental tone throughout. It could be elevated to a 5 with deeper, individualized affirmation of the client’s values and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor provides accurate complex reflections of the client’s practical concerns and fears. Deeper exploration of emotional undercurrents such as defensiveness or identity-related meaning would improve empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on reducing alcohol use within diabetes management. The psychiatrist purposefully moves from diagnosis meaning to a concrete, testable behavior plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist uses inclusive language and allows the client’s ideas to shape the plan. The collaboration could be stronger by reducing therapist-led options and increasing true co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes choice, ownership, and self-direction without pressure or controlling language. Autonomy support is a major strength and highly aligned with MI.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits the client’s own motivations, values, and goals. Evocation could be stronger by reducing leading choices and therapist-generated structure.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally positions the client as the expert on his own life and context. Partnership could be improved by more explicit co-creation and fewer therapist-framed decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist frequently elicits and reinforces meaningful change talk from the client. Some questions are leading or menu-driven, which limits the consistency of change talk evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing sustain talk and reframes concerns effectively. More explicit double-sided reflections would help more fully resolve ambivalence.
