# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, affirming, and nonjudgmental. However, the warmth is somewhat formulaic and could better normalize the client's shame, fear, and difficulty rebuilding trust with his children.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects the client's ambivalence about independence and support, but the empathy remains mostly cognitive. It misses deeper emotions such as grief, remorse, and fear of failing his children.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is strongly focused on the treatment goals and target behaviors. Each turn advances a concrete, actionable plan tied back to the client's stated values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites client input and works within the client's cultural context. However, the volume of supplied options, scripts, and planning structures limits fully client-led collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly asks permission and respects the client's choices. Continued planning prompts and suggested scripts can still feel somewhat directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some evocative techniques, such as asking for a first step and confidence rating. However, the strong problem-solving stance limits deeper client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally presents as a partner and allows the client to choose key steps. The interaction would be stronger with fewer preloaded solutions and more open questioning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Useful change talk is elicited and affirmed, particularly around protecting his children and avoiding Tristan. The psychiatrist could better deepen and expand the client's own values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepts the client's concerns about independence and strict goals without arguing or minimizing. The approach would be stronger with more consistent double-sided reflection.
