# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating without shaming Jamal’s alcohol use or social concerns. The tone is supportive but somewhat task-focused rather than fully individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track Jamal’s peer pressure, fear of losing his spot, and concern about fitting in. The empathy is strong but sometimes shifts quickly into practical problem-solving rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior of reducing alcohol use in post-practice social settings. It effectively guides Jamal toward a concrete, actionable plan without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jamal’s ideas clearly shape the plan, and the psychiatrist frequently invites his input and builds on his suggestions. The collaboration is strong but less fully equal because the psychiatrist often supplies options and scripts first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and avoids pressure, but frequently supplies scripts and strategies rather than consistently affirming Jamal’s freedom and ownership. Autonomy support is present but not explicit or client-led enough for a higher score.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open and scaling questions elicit some of Jamal’s own reasons for change and confidence. However, the process often shifts quickly to directive planning, limiting deeper exploration of values and identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well and invites Jamal to select among options, making the plan partly client-generated. It is not fully client-led because the clinician frequently presents menus of scripts and behavioral strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully draws out desire, confidence, and action-oriented change talk, especially around protecting his season and using a team-focused message. It could be developed more deeply, but change talk is frequent and effective.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jamal’s sustain talk is acknowledged respectfully, but the session often remains focused on social risks and scripts rather than reframing sustain talk toward values and change. The captain concern remains unresolved at the end.
