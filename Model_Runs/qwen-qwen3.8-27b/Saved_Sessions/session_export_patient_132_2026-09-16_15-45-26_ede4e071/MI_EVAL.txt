# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a nonjudgmental, validating tone and normalizes Yasmin's concerns without blame. Full credit would require more explicit affirmation of her worth independent of progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and nuanced, showing strong understanding of Yasmin's ambivalence. Some deeper reflections around guilt, binge fear, and nighttime rumination would make this a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on a sustainable routine and moves toward concrete steps. It falls short of a 5 because there is no clear collaboratively agreed next step or explicit summary of the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs options with Yasmin and lets the plan evolve based on her input. Full collaboration would require more explicit power-sharing and less therapist-led pacing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions, choice language, and minimal directives consistently support Yasmin's autonomy. The psychiatrist effectively returns decisions and ownership to the client throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently elicits Yasmin's own motivations, concerns, and solutions rather than providing advice. More amplification of change talk or confidence scaling would justify a 5.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Yasmin is treated as the expert on her own life through reflective questions and plan ownership. Full partnership would require more explicit agenda negotiation and reduced counselor direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Yasmin produces meaningful change talk around trying for three days, using a one-word note, and trusting hunger cues. The counselor does not fully amplify this talk or move toward explicit commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and is skillfully reframed when it emerges. It remains central much of the session, so a stronger, more consistent pivot toward change would be needed for a 5.
