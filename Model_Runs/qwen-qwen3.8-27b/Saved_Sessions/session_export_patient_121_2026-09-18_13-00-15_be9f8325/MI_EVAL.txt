# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm with strong validation of Sofia's experiences, including powerful statements like 'You don't owe anyone a single version of yourself.' Affirmations are well-placed but occasionally feel slightly formulaic and could be more individualized in moments of deepest vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening with reflections that progress from simple to complex, capturing emotional texture with remarkable accuracy (e.g., 'bracing,' the hallway ambivalence, the somatic experience). The psychiatrist consistently goes beyond surface content to articulate Sofia's unspoken logic and emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a strong session arc from problem identification to exploring the hallway approach as a concrete, actionable goal. The session ends somewhat open-endedly without an explicit summary tying exploration to a specific agreed-upon next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative language framing the conversation as joint exploration, with the psychiatrist actively seeking Sofia's input before offering reflections. However, the psychiatrist still controls the pace and direction of each question, and there is no explicit acknowledgment that Sofia's contributions are shaping the session's focus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent autonomy support woven naturally throughout — the psychiatrist never prescribes a specific action, honors Sofia's ambivalence without pressuring resolution, and consistently frames options as possibilities rather than directives. The opening statement and responses to Sofia's fear of appearing 'desperate' demonstrate deep respect for her right to choose her own path and timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation strategies including excellent open-ended questions and the effective technique of 'What was that sentence trying to say before it stopped?' to invite Sofia to complete her own thought. Could be more systematic in building on each instance of change talk (e.g., deepening the friend thread or exploring readiness more explicitly).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a genuine collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Sofia to lead and explicit power-sharing language ('I'm not going to minimize it'). Could be strengthened by explicitly acknowledging Sofia's expertise in her own experience and more explicitly co-constructing next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes multiple instances of change talk across desire, hope, ability, and emerging commitment language. The psychiatrist could more explicitly amplify and reinforce each instance when it appears rather than moving on relatively quickly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles the dominant blame narrative skillfully without ever reinforcing it, using balanced reflections that validate both sides. Could more actively and strategically pivot toward what Sofia can influence (her own actions, the hallway approach) rather than leaving focus on others' behavior.
