# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Amina’s competing parent and work needs. It falls short of a 5 because the warmth is mainly problem-focused rather than deeply affirming of Amina as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates accurate and consistent reflective listening, tracking both practical and emotional meaning. Reflections effectively capture ambivalence, the childcare barrier, fear of rejection, and Amina’s shift toward a practical frame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior: attending job interviews despite childcare responsibilities. It moves coherently from the broad concern to a specific barrier and then to concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Amina as an equal partner and co-constructs the solution rather than prescribing one. Amina’s ideas directly shape the request wording, plan for a “no,” and sticky-note strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Amina’s autonomy by asking what feels doable and letting her choose the action and reminder. There is no pressure to commit to a specific action, and her values around caregiving are respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes Amina’s own solutions, confidence, and language rather than telling her what to do. It falls short of a 5 because the therapist occasionally provides scripts or options that may lead the client rather than rely on pure evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Amina as the expert on her own life and consistently follows her lead. Power is shared throughout, with Amina’s concerns and choices shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes Amina’s ability and plan statements and reinforces her competence and agency. It is strong but not maximal because it does not consistently evoke deeper change talk around desire, values, importance, or readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing and redirects toward possibilities and reduced emotional weight. It falls short of a 5 because the childcare barrier remains highly salient and could be reframed more consistently toward the benefits of taking the step.
