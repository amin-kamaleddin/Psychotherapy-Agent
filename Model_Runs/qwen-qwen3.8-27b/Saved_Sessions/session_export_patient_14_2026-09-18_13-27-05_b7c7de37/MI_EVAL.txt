# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently maintains a respectful, non-judgmental tone, reframing apparent resistance as logical behavior (e.g., 'you're not fighting you'). Warmth is occasionally slightly strategic rather than purely warm, and no explicit statement of unconditional positive regard is made.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with layered, specific reflections that capture underlying patterns (binary thinking, the 2am vulnerability) rather than surface content. Andrei validates each reflection, confirming their precision and depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains remarkably clear focus on the target behavior with a logical arc from acknowledging barriers to co-constructing a concrete action step. Each question is strategically designed to move toward the change goal without drift, and the session closes efficiently respecting the client's time.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A masterclass in collaboration — consistently frames work as joint, incorporates Andrei's own language (deadlines, contracts, consequences) into the MI process, and treats each contribution as genuinely informative. The final plan is entirely Andrei's construction, with the psychiatrist explicitly crediting him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces autonomy by withholding the urge to prescribe (e.g., 'I'm not going to add a dietitian or a walking group'), never uses 'should' or creates obligation, and explicitly acknowledges the client's expertise over his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Andrei's own reasoning through questions designed to elicit internal wisdom, resulting in a plan entirely constructed by the client. The final question ('what you'd notice' rather than 'what you'd do') is a particularly skillful evocative move inviting internal experience.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly frames the session as a shared, client-paced process from the opening, and the power balance is consistently maintained throughout. The psychiatrist resists the urge to add solutions and trusts Andrei's own wisdom to shape the direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively amplifies key change talk statements (e.g., the 55-year-old image, the shift from 'a bit high' to 'trajectory') and separates desire from barriers. Could have more explicitly elicited ability and commitment language across all DARN-CAT categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never argues against or dismisses sustain talk; instead integrates it into reflections that preserve its truth while opening space for change, and strategically works within the client's own logic (deadlines, consequences). Could have used a double-sided reflection more explicitly in at least one moment.
