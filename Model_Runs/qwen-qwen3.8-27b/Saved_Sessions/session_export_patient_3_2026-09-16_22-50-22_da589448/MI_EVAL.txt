# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated unconditional positive regard through nonjudgmental, validating language. Aisha’s ambivalence, self-criticism, and physical cautions were treated as understandable rather than challenged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used accurate reflective listening that captured both the content and emotional meaning of Aisha’s experience. The reflections moved beyond paraphrase to capture her internal critic, fatigue, and need for permission.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear and strategic focus on initiating a safe, realistic morning exercise routine. The conversation moved progressively from broad motivation to concrete, actionable details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked with Aisha as an equal partner rather than imposing an expert-driven plan. Aisha’s ideas directly shaped the timing, movements, duration, and tracking method.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supported Aisha’s sense of choice and ownership over the plan. Language avoided pressure, commands, or external standards while encouraging a self-defined realistic approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicited Aisha’s own motivation, values, and preferred actions through open-ended, future-oriented questions. Aisha generated much of the change talk and the specific details of the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction clearly positioned Aisha as the expert on her body, schedule, and feasible next steps. The psychiatrist followed her lead while affirming her competence and agency.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively drew out Aisha’s reasons for change, including wanting to feel lighter, less gross, and capable. The reflections reinforced her own language and helped strengthen her commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Aisha’s sustain talk around “cheating,” being “enough,” and fear of not doing enough was handled without argument or dismissal. The psychiatrist acknowledged the tension and gently reframed it toward a sustainable, self-respecting plan.
