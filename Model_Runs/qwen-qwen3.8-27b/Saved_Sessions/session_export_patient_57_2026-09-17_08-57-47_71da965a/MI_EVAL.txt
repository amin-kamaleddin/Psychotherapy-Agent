# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental and respectful stance, normalizing Jamal’s ambivalence without labeling him. Acceptance is strong but largely cognitive and structural, with limited explicit affirmation of his worth or courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflective listening to capture both the cognitive and emotional dimensions of Jamal’s conflict. Empathy could deepen further by exploring underlying identity fears such as weakness, shame, or self-image.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on reducing alcohol use within team activities. The conversation moves purposefully from ambivalence to practical strategies without notable topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates effectively by building on Jamal’s ideas, such as using Mike as a social buffer and framing refusal as “I’m wrecked.” More co-creation, such as having Jamal rank or choose among options, would strengthen this area.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports agency through open questions and avoids pressure or directive commands. More explicit reinforcement that the decision to use these strategies is entirely Jamal’s would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently draws out Jamal’s own motivations and practical ideas for change. Evocation could be strengthened by using scaling questions, decisional balance, or summaries to deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Jamal’s input and avoids an authoritarian expert role, allowing his suggestions to shape the conversation. The partnership would be stronger with more explicit power-sharing, such as negotiating the session focus or prioritization.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk through open-ended questions and lightly reinforces Jamal’s motivations. However, the change talk is not consistently amplified, structured, or developed into stronger commitment or confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects sustain talk without arguing and gently redirects it toward compatible goals such as maintaining belonging. More deliberate strategies, such as double-sided reflections, would further soften sustain talk.
