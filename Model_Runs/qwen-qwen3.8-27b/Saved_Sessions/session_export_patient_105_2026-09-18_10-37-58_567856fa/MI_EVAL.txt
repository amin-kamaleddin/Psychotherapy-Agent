# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and genuine unconditional positive regard throughout, validating effort without ever labeling anxiety as a flaw. Frames Naomi's experience with respect and never minimizes her struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that precisely captures emotional complexity and names the protective function of avoidance without pathologizing. Tracks shifts in confidence and self-doubt, adjusting depth and tone accordingly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear logical progression from problem acknowledgment through solution testing with strategic summarizing. Could benefit from slightly more explicit signposting or collaborative check-ins on whether the focus feels right.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals with every therapeutic contribution responding to or elaborating on Naomi's statements. The psychiatrist never imposes an agenda or takes the lead in generating content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook autonomy support — never prescribes, recommends, or tells; all solutions originate from Naomi. The camera-grounding technique is explicitly attributed to her own experience and agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expert-level evocation where every significant insight originates from Naomi rather than being provided externally. Questions are designed to draw out her own wisdom without psychoeducation or technique suggestions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Naomi as the expert on her own experience with questions like 'What do you make of that?' The psychiatrist acts as a facilitator rather than authority, never providing interpretation or direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategically evokes change talk through questions that elicit vivid descriptions of desired change. Could have gone further in systematically exploring DARN-CAT dimensions like confidence ratings or specific reasons this approach might work.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles doubt skillfully with validation before gentle redirection, never arguing or dismissing. However, the final exchange where Naomi expresses 'what if it doesn't work mid-set' ends without a full double-sided reflection.
