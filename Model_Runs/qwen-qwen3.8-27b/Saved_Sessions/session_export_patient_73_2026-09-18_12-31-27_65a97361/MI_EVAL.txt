# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates warm, unconditional positive regard with no judgment or disapproval. Validates the client's corrections rather than defending the clinician's framing, and explicitly normalizes her experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflective listening that goes beyond surface content to emotional meaning. Sophisticated double reflections capture both the cognitive and emotional shifts in the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on tobacco cessation with a logical, purposeful progression through the session. The session ends at the exploration stage without a clear directional marker toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structured as genuine co-exploration between equals with the session's direction shaped entirely by the client's responses. The clinician models vulnerability and uncertainty rather than leading with a pre-determined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and agency throughout the session. Questions are framed as invitations rather than directives, with zero evidence of pressuring or directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Gold standard evocation with all key change statements generated by the client herself. The clinician uses open questions, strategic reflections, and the client's own metaphors to create conditions for insight rather than providing external reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates consistently from a not-knowing stance, explicitly attributing insight back to the client. Cedes ground immediately when the client pushes back, demonstrating that the client's experience overrides the clinician's narrative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Remarkably skillful at evoking and amplifying the client's own change language. Consistently catches moments of change talk and reinforces them, helping the client generate substantial self-motivating statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively without arguing or dismissing, meeting it with curiosity and validation. However, the sustain talk about past failures lingers without being fully reframed using strategic techniques such as double-sided reflections to pivot toward agency.
