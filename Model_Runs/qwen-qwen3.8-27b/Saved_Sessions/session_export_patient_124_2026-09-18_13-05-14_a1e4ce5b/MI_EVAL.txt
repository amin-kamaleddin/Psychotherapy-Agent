# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental stance with validating language ('this space is totally judgment-free,' 'that's yours to figure out'). Occasionally slightly formulaic in affirmations, preventing a fully effortless feel.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections capturing multiple layers (pressure + internal experience), with charitable reframing of behavior. Missed opportunity to deeper-reflect the emotional weight of the 'first in the family' identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from stress → function → alternatives → money loop → gap → small plan, with well-managed pacing and a concrete ending goal. Slightly guided by counselor's agenda rather than fully collaboratively structured.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Entirely joint exploration; builds on Sofia's own metaphors ('mutes it for a bit'), uses inclusive/partnership language, and lets Sofia generate the final plan herself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful and natural throughout—no prescriptive language, all decisions explicitly framed as Sofia's ('That's YOUR reason,' 'what feels doable?'), and final success criteria defined by Sofia herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All key change talk elicited via evocative questions (gap question, money question, doable plan); Sofia articulates her own reasoning end-to-end. Could have further evoked confidence/ability talk (e.g., 'what would make that easier?').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative language, shares power ('I'm not going to argue with that'), and invites Sofia to name what feels heavy. Could have explicitly invited Sofia to set or redirect the session agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful strategic questions elicit values (trip, textbooks), self-evaluation ('am I doing the same thing?'), and a clear self-generated reason. Session ends before fully locking in commitment or confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well—validates ('you make a fair point'), uses double-sided reflections on social belonging without amplification. Could have used the social-connection theme more strategically as a pivot to alternative connection.
