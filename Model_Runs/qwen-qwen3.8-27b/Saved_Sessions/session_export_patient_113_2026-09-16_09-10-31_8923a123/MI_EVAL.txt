# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect and nonjudgment toward Rowan’s resistance. The tone is supportive, though it can feel procedural rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Rowan’s perspective and ambivalence. However, they often remain content-based and miss deeper emotional layers such as exhaustion, fear, and irritability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and moves clearly from barriers to a concrete plan. The main limitation is the lack of a final consolidation or closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rowan’s preferences meaningfully shape the final plan, especially the “charge when I get home” step. The psychiatrist still provides many expert-generated solutions, which limits full client ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently uses invitations and respects Rowan’s stated boundaries. Repeated plan suggestions, however, can subtly reduce his sense of complete choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are useful questions about preferences and confidence, but the counselor often supplies solutions before fully eliciting Rowan’s own motivation. More exploration of his values, reasons, and self-efficacy is needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rowan is invited to choose, but the psychiatrist often takes the lead in problem-solving. This limits the sense that Rowan is the primary expert on his own routine.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges Rowan’s emerging willingness to try a small step. There is less consistent effort to elicit, amplify, or build on his own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Rowan’s barriers but does not use enough double-sided reflections or gentle reframes. The ambivalence is often acknowledged and then quickly moved past.
