# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows consistent respect, normalization, and validation without arguing or blaming. Acceptance could be strengthened by more explicitly naming parental fear and uncertainty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client's perspective and assumptions about chickenpox and risk. Empathy would be stronger if the emotional weight of parental anxiety and fear of making the wrong choice were reflected more directly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the vaccination decision and co-constructs a practical plan. Direction would improve by moving more deliberately from information-gathering toward readiness, confidence, or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well in building a client-driven next step with clear structure. More explicit shared agenda-setting and less clinician-framing of options would deepen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports the client's freedom to choose and does not push for a decision or appointment. Language consistently reinforces client ownership and the right to take time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful self-awareness about the client's assumptions and information needs. Evocation would improve by drawing out deeper values, confidence, and readiness for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the client's input and follows her preferences in shaping the conversation. Partnership would be stronger with more explicit recognition of the client as the expert on her own family and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerges and is acknowledged, but the psychiatrist does not consistently cultivate it. More DARN-C-style questions and stronger summaries would help deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument or resistance. The session would benefit from stronger double-sided reflections and redirection from fear-based concerns toward the client's protective values and goals.
