# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. There is no pressure, criticism, or labeling, and Marcus's constraints are validated throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Marcus's perspective and concerns. However, they sometimes remain at a summary level rather than fully exploring deeper feelings such as frustration or disappointment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on increasing physical activity in a realistic way. It progresses coherently from the target behavior to a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Marcus at nearly every step and uses the client's ideas to shape the session. The final plan is co-constructed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently supports Marcus's sense of choice and ownership. The plan is framed as a low-pressure experiment that Marcus can accept, modify, or stop without guilt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions effectively draw out Marcus's own preferences, constraints, and solutions. Emerging motivation is reinforced by reflecting back his own hope and willingness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly treats Marcus as the expert on his own life and schedule. Authority is not assumed, and the client is invited to define what is realistic and doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is frequently evoked and reinforced, especially around willingness and specific steps. More explicit amplification of confidence, importance, or commitment language would make it a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing and is redirected toward workable, low-pressure experiments. It could be addressed more consistently with strategic reflections or explicit reframing for a 5.
