# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance throughout. Affirmations are specific, and the client's drinking is not moralized or labeled as a disorder.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurately captures the client's ambivalence and social concerns. The reflections could be slightly deeper in exploring underlying emotions and meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the target behavior of weekend alcohol use. The conversation steadily narrows toward a concrete, testable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by inviting input and building on the client's ideas. Some consecutive turns and health-related steering slightly reduce the fully client-driven rhythm.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently emphasized through language about choice, perspective, and self-defined goals. The psychiatrist avoids pressure and supports the client's right to stop if the plan does not work.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the client's own motivations, solutions, and self-evaluation. There is room to more explicitly elicit values, confidence, readiness, and identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys that the client is the expert on his own life and social world. Some health framing and consecutive turns slightly reduce the natural give-and-take.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist actively evokes and amplifies the client's change language around small shifts and a concrete plan. It could be stronger by more explicitly developing DARN-C change talk such as confidence and importance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by reflecting both sides without challenging the client's reasons for drinking. The sustain talk could be more fully transformed into change talk in a few instances.
