# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids blaming for missed doses or reliance on the partner. Acceptance is strong but slightly procedural, and the opening interpretation was not explicitly stated by the client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician accurately reflects Selene's ambivalence, practical burdens, and emotional concerns. Reflections are generally accurate but do not consistently probe deeper emotional undercurrents such as fear of failure or chronic fatigue.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on medication adherence and moves logically from barriers to a concrete plan. The conclusion is incomplete because there is no final summary, review, or follow-up consolidation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Selene's ideas, integrating her suggestions into the plan. The collaboration is strong but occasionally shifts toward clinician-led problem-solving through repeated option lists and specific scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician consistently offers choices, asks for willingness, and respects Selene's modifications. Some prescriptive details, such as scripts and two-week experiments, reduce client-led autonomy somewhat.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some of Selene's values and intentions but moves quickly into practical problem-solving. More client-led exploration of reasons, confidence, and personal meaning would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows Selene to shape the plan, including timing and the partner reminder. The interaction is collaborative but not fully client-led because the clinician frequently introduces multiple options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session evokes some change talk through open questions and confidence scaling, but the clinician does not consistently amplify or deepen it. More follow-up on values, abilities, and past successes would strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids challenging or dismissing Selene's sustain talk, which is a strength. However, the response often moves quickly into problem-solving rather than skillfully exploring or reframing resistance.
