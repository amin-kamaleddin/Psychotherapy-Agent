### Empathy

**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy throughout, including the powerful moment of naming the weight behind 'I guess it was scary' while respecting her boundary to not unpack it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear strategic arc from rapport-building through emotional exploration to a concrete closing action, adapting well to Maria's cues. Slight drift between emotional and practical focus, though transitions are handled smoothly.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative interaction between equals, with Maria's corrections welcomed and acted upon. The psychiatrist positions himself as needing Maria's input before offering anything.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support—removes pressure, respects boundaries around emotional disclosure, and consistently returns the decision to Maria. Notably resists the expert role even when Maria explicitly invites it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocative questions, particularly the 'safe next step' reframing that successfully elicits genuine change talk from a highly resistant client. Consistently draws Maria's own motivations and reasoning from within her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power dynamic is consistently flipped to position Maria as the expert on her own body and life. The psychiatrist never assumes the expert role, even when Maria explicitly asks to be told what to do.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits change talk despite strong resistance, including the powerful 'I think if I knew that, maybe I'd stop being so... yeah.' Session ends before the psychiatrist can further develop or amplify this emerging change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates Maria's concerns rather than correcting her misinformation, and reframes decisional language to practical terms without invalidating her position. Effective but limited by session brevity in fully addressing the depth of her distrust.
