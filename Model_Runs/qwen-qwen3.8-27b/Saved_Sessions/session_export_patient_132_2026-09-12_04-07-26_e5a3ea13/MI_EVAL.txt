# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, accepting stance and reduces shame around dieting and body dissatisfaction. Praise is often plan-focused rather than deeply person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate, validating reflections of Yasmin’s ambivalence and normalizes mixed feelings. The main limitation is that reflections often remain summarizing rather than deeply exploring the emotional pain underneath.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on moving away from punitive dieting toward a more sustainable approach to food, exercise, and body relationship. The focus becomes quite narrow and behavior-planning-oriented at times.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is largely collaborative, with Yasmin’s choices shaping the final plan. The psychiatrist still frequently supplies the main ideas and techniques rather than co-creating them from Yasmin’s own wisdom.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally supports Yasmin’s sense of choice and avoids coercive language. Repeatedly offering specific scripts and techniques can be subtly directive and may reduce her sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits motivation by asking about past successes, small steps, and confidence. The evocation is often practical rather than deeply values-based, and the therapist sometimes supplies the answer too quickly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is collaboration, but the psychiatrist frequently drifts into an expert or coach role by introducing many specific interventions and scripts. More explicit power sharing would require asking Yasmin to generate the strategies and solutions herself before offering suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes change talk by asking about sustainability, past successes, and coping choices, and Yasmin generates meaningful change talk. The evocation is often channeled into concrete task planning rather than deeper exploration of values and identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk in a generally nonargumentative and validating way, acknowledging Yasmin’s desire for weight loss and fear of eating more. The sustain talk is not consistently and skillfully defused, and some language may feel subtly directive.
