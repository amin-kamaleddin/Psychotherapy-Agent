# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, affirming, and nonjudgmental while accepting Hana’s pace. However, some responses are formulaic or interpretive rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are adequate at the content level, especially for Hana’s mixed motivations. Emotional concerns about social awkwardness and feeling left out are not consistently reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and moves clearly toward a concrete plan. It remains task-oriented and well organized.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana contributes key ideas, and the plan reflects genuine co-creation. However, the psychiatrist introduces many options, making the process somewhat therapist-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice, preference, and low-pressure planning are consistently supported. More explicit reinforcement that Hana is in charge could strengthen this further.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks about importance, confidence, and values, which helps Hana voice some reasons for change. Follow-up is often quick, leaving Hana’s motivations less deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Hana’s input and lets her preferences shape the plan. The balance shifts somewhat toward expert guidance when many practical suggestions are offered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Hana offers some change talk, including confidence and concern for gum health. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or expand Hana’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana’s statements about not being ready to quit are validated rather than argued against. Advanced use of reflections to turn sustain talk into change talk is limited.
