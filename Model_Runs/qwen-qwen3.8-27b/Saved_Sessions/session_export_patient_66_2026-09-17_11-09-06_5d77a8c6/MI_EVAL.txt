# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone and validates Kareem's enjoyment of smoking without disapproval. Acceptance remains at a professional level rather than demonstrating deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with effective complex reflections capturing Kareem's experiential qualities. An early reflection was slightly premature (Kareem had to correct it), and deeper emotional exploration was absent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, logical arc from understanding the loss to planning the first walk, with each question building on the previous. Ends somewhat open-endedly without tying to a specific change goal or re-evaluation timeline.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asks for Kareem's perspective, incorporates his language, and builds on his contributions without lecturing or imposing a framework. Interaction is somewhat counselor-driven, with the psychiatrist steering most of the conversation's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength of the session. Consistently and explicitly supports Kareem's right to choose, never pressures, and frames the walk as an experiment on his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits Kareem's own motivations without imposing external reasons for change. Powerful moment explicitly crediting Kareem for his key insight, reinforcing self-efficacy and internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent power-sharing language reinforces that expertise resides with Kareem. Slightly one-directional in that the psychiatrist drives the conversation's structure and pacing rather than co-creating the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates progressive change talk through strategic questioning and skillful amplification. Change talk remains surface-level, centered on trying a walk rather than exploring deeper values, confidence, or long-term motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk appropriately by validating the value of smoking without arguing or minimizing. Sustain talk was relatively mild in this session, limiting the depth of this criterion's test.
