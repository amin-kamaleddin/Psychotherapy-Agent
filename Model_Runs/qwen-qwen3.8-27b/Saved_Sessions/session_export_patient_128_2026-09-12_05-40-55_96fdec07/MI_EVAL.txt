# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Tyler’s ambivalence. Warmth is strong, though it is occasionally tied to practical progress rather than unconditional regard for the person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The reflections accurately capture Tyler’s torn feelings between education, friends, and fear of consequences. However, the reflections are often summarizing and do not consistently explore deeper emotions such as shame or social fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the alcohol-related issue and moves toward concrete next steps. The direction is clear, though it becomes somewhat task-driven rather than consistently MI-focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts the plan to Tyler’s preferences. The interaction is collaborative, but the frequent supply of options and tips makes it feel more guided than fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tyler is regularly given choices and is not pressured to commit to a specific plan. However, the frequent provision of scripts and practical suggestions slightly reduces the emphasis on autonomous decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks useful questions about preference, confidence, and doability. Evocation is limited by the directive problem-solving style and limited exploration of values, discrepancy, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance by inviting Tyler to lead the pace and topic. The partnership is strong, though the clinician’s frequent suggestions can make the interaction feel less equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, especially around confidence, education, and trying a low-risk plan. However, the evocation is shallow because the psychiatrist mainly asks Tyler to choose from pre-generated options.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk without arguing or criticizing and normalizes Tyler’s concerns. Strategic softening is limited, with fewer double-sided reflections or reframes that connect his wants to change.
