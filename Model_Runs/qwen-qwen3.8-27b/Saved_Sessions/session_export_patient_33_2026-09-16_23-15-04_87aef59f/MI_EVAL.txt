# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance and explicitly communicates that this is a safe space. There is no blame, coercion, or moralizing, even when discussing heroin use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor uses nuanced, accurate reflections that capture Diego’s ambivalence, shame, exhaustion, and fear. Responses show strong perspective-taking and help deepen the client’s self-exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on heroin use and harm reduction without unnecessary drift. It moves logically from ambivalence to a concrete safety plan involving Narcan and a cue-based reminder.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Diego’s input and builds the plan from his ideas, such as the red box, bathroom counter, and 10 a.m. cue. However, the interaction could be more consistently client-led by confirming that summaries and interpretations fit his experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Diego’s choices and readiness are strongly respected, including his statement that he is not ready to quit today. The counselor repeatedly returns decision-making to him and supports self-directed action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Diego’s own reasons, values, and goals rather than supplying them. This could be strengthened by more explicitly eliciting confidence, importance, readiness, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally positions Diego as the source of direction and affirms his agency. At times, however, interpretations and summaries can feel slightly leading rather than fully co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces Diego’s change talk and moves him toward concrete commitment. It could be stronger by drawing out more DARN-C language, especially self-efficacy and readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing or moralizing, and the counselor reflects both the relief and the risks of use. A few questions risk prolonging focus on the benefits of use before redirecting toward change.
