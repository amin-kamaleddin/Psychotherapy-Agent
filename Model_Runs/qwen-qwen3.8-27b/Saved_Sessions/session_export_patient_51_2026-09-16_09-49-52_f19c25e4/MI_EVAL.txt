# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys warmth, respect, and validation despite the client's time pressure. Affirmations are supportive and nonjudgmental, with only minor contingencies in some praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate, especially regarding the client's busy schedule and need for a quick answer. Empathy remains practical and surface-level rather than deeply attuned to underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the medication safety issue and a clear action plan. There is no drift from the main concern, and the discussion remains consistently goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates closely by incorporating the client's preferences into the script and follow-up plan. The interaction is shaped by the client's constraints rather than being purely one-way instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is encouraged to contact the prescriber and is offered choices about how to follow up. However, repeated directive safety instructions limit a fully autonomy-supportive stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used, especially at the start and around follow-up preferences. The psychiatrist mostly provides safety information rather than consistently eliciting the client's own motivation or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows the client's preferences to shape the session. The stance is collaborative, though still somewhat expert-driven due to the urgent clinical content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms some of the client's safety-oriented statements and plans. There are missed opportunities to deepen or amplify the client's own motivation and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's time constraint is handled well by offering a brief script and a simple message-based follow-up. Repeated closing reminders slightly prolong the session, but sustain talk is overall managed effectively.
