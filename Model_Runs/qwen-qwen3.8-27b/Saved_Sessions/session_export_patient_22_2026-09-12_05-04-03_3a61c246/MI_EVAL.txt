# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was warm, validating, and nonjudgmental, offering several affirmations about Camila's effort and ambivalence. Acceptance was consistent, though at times it shifted toward problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used accurate reflections of Camila's frustration, ambivalence, and social pressure. The empathy was strong but stopped short of deeper exploration of grief, loneliness, or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on reducing weekday drinking and moved clearly from exploration to a concrete plan. The pacing was purposeful, though it sometimes moved quickly toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited input and responded to Camila's preferences, but frequently supplied specific scripts and plans. This made the collaboration uneven and more therapist-led than truly co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist often invited choice and respected Camila's pace and goal of reduction rather than abstinence. However, the drive toward planning and script-giving subtly pressured her sense of self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions were used, but the psychiatrist often filled the space with expert-generated content instead of eliciting Camila's own ideas. This limited the depth of client-generated change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for preferences and invited rehearsal, supporting a collaborative frame. However, the frequent provision of specific scripts, plans, and coaching tips made the partnership less balanced.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Camila produced some meaningful change talk, such as not wanting to hide and wanting to test the social part. The psychiatrist did not consistently amplify or deepen these statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged Camila's social fears without arguing or dismissing them, and used a Plan A/B approach to honor ambivalence. The handling was supportive, though it could have used deeper double-sided reflections.
