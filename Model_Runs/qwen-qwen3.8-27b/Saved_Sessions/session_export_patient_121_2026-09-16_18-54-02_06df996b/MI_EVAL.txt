**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently respects and validates Sofia’s experience without judgment. Affirmation could be strengthened by more explicitly recognizing her resilience and worth outside the problem.

**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and consistently track both emotional content and ambivalence. The clinician holds opposing feelings such as relief, fear, and the desire for connection with strong sensitivity.

### Direction

**Comments:** The session stays focused on school safety and narrows the concern into a concrete, manageable step with Maya. It would benefit from more explicit linking of the micro-plan to broader goals like attendance or navigating peer conflict.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Sofia’s language, ideas, and plan rather than imposing solutions. More explicit co-creation, such as letting Sofia choose how to proceed, would make the process more fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia’s choice, control, boundaries, and ability to set a safe pace are consistently supported. The final suggestion is mildly directional and slightly reduces the purely evocative stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits Sofia’s own criteria for trust, safety, and first steps. Adding scaling questions, values connection, or confidence-building would further develop internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia to define the issue and allows her ideas to shape the session. More explicit invitations for Sofia to set the agenda or choose next steps would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session successfully encourages Sofia to articulate reasons, hopes, and a realistic plan. The change talk could be more clearly labeled, amplified, and connected to confidence in change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia’s skepticism, fear of betrayal, and risk concerns are acknowledged without being dismissed or argued with. These protective thoughts could be more explicitly reframed as reasons to proceed cautiously rather than not proceed at all.
