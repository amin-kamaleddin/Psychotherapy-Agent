### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully and progressively evokes change talk from general to specific, strategically reflecting and amplifying each contribution. The session culminates in Mateo spontaneously generating a concrete, self-directed plan.
