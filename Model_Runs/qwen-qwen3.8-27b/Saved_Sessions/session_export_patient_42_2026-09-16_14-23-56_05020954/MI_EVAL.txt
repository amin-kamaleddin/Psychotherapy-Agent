# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently used nonjudgmental, warm, and respectful language. Elise’s ambivalence and social concerns were normalized and validated without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist gave accurate reflections of Elise’s feelings and metaphors. Empathy was slightly formulaic and did not always deepen emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking-related social connection and the break room. It became somewhat circular by repeatedly asking for a way to be present without progressing toward a clear next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used collaborative questions and allowed Elise’s metaphors to shape the conversation. The session remained somewhat therapist-directed, limiting full co-creation of the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used non-directive questions and avoided advising Elise to quit. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming Elise’s freedom to choose either option.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elise was invited to describe her own views and possible smoke-free connections. Evocation was limited because values, importance, confidence, and discrepancy were not deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Elise as the expert on her experience and invited her perspective. Partnership was constrained because the psychiatrist continued to steer the agenda and revisit the same solution-finding question.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited some change-oriented language and reflected subtle statements of change. These moments were not consistently amplified, scaled, or developed into stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elise’s sustain talk was acknowledged, but the psychiatrist sometimes amplified the cigarette’s social usefulness. Responses did not consistently redirect toward discrepancy, values, or change.
