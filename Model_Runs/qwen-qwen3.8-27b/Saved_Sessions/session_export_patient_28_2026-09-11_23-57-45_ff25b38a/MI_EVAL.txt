# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally uses respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating language. Acceptance is warm but somewhat formulaic and occasionally interrupted by safety reminders and directive planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are several brief reflections and attempts to understand Dante’s perspective and emotions. However, empathy is often surface-level and quickly shifts into advice or planning before deeper meaning is explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on increasing physical activity and a specific low-barrier action plan. Direction is strong, though the repeated plan and safety language can feel somewhat repetitive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante’s preferences are meaningfully incorporated into the plan, such as timing, alarm, clothing placement, and check-in time. The collaboration is uneven because the psychiatrist supplies much of the structure and details.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and asks for Dante’s input and commitment. Some directive language and repeated instructions, however, weaken the sense that the plan is fully his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out some of Dante’s experience early in the session. The largest gap is limited elicitation of Dante’s own values, motivations, past successes, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante is invited to contribute, and his input shapes the final plan. The interaction remains somewhat expert-driven, with the psychiatrist providing a detailed plan rather than fully co-creating it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante expresses some willingness to try, set an alarm, use quiet stretches, and return for a check-in. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, summarize, or strengthen this change language with importance, confidence, values, or ability questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes Dante’s concerns and lowers the stakes by framing the plan as a short experiment. Responses are helpful but mostly practical rather than actively turning sustain talk into change talk.
