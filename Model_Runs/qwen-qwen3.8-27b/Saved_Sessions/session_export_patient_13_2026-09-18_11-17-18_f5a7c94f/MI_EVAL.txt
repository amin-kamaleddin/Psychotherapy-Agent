# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally warm, validating, and non-judgmental throughout the entire session. Consistently reframes avoidance as protective strength and treats Anara's experience with deep dignity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture the emotional core, including the 'what if' spiral, the isolation of not knowing, and the throat-sticking barrier. Each reflection hits the specific phenomenological experience rather than just the content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, progressive MI arc from rapport through exploring ambivalence, identifying the core barrier, evoking a small step, practicing it, and assessing readiness. No drift from the target behavior of seeking medical care.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal partnership where Anara's contributions substantially shape the session's direction. The psychiatrist follows her lead rather than driving with a preset agenda, co-creating elements like the phone call script.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never prescribes action, timeline, or expectation; consistently reinforces Anara's agency with phrases like 'you're the one who found it.' When Anara expresses ambivalence about actually calling, the psychiatrist does not push further, fully preserving her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful elicitation of Anara's own motivations, fears, and solutions through open, curiosity-based questions. The psychiatrist never states a reason for change on Anara's behalf; every insight and step forward is self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Anara, functioning as a mirror and sounding board rather than an authority. Never provides medical information, diagnoses, or directives—always invites Anara to lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through targeted questions that lead Anara to articulate her own readiness and small steps. The session culminates in Anara generating her own change talk: 'I'm not not gonna try. I think.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively validates and reframes sustain talk as protective rather than resistive, avoiding argument or dismissal. Could be strengthened with more double-sided reflections that explicitly hold both sides in tension while tilting toward change.
