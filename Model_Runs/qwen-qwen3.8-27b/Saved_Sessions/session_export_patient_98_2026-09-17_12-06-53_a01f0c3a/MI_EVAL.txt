# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific validations tied to Mateo's situation. No minimization, labeling, or disapproval at any point; acceptance is woven naturally into the clinical work.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflective listening that moves beyond paraphrase to capture underlying meaning and emotional weight. Skillfully reflects ambivalence without forcing resolution and names patterns (e.g., fear/holding back/doing less) without judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally clear focus on increasing activity and overcoming rehab barriers, with a logical progression through the session. Minor weakness in spending meaningful time on pain itself (the 8/10 discussion) that could have been more tightly connected to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative; Mateo's ideas consistently shape the session's direction and the final concrete step emerges entirely from his own contributions. Inclusive co-constructed language throughout with no imposed information or hidden agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Mateo's ownership of choices with no prescriptive language or directives. The psychiatrist positions themselves as a guide who helps clarify Mateo's own thinking, leaving all decisions to him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is designed to elicit motivation from within Mateo rather than providing external reasons for change. The session is structured as an evocation process where Mateo articulates his own values, goals, and insights.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveys that expertise resides within Mateo, using open-ended questions that elicit his own thinking. The session genuinely follows Mateo's leads, with his observations substantially shaping the direction and focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes ability, need, and intention change talk through targeted questions that follow Mateo's own insights. Affirmatively reflects and amplifies change talk when it appears rather than introducing external reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively validates and gently pivots sustain talk through reflection and exploratory questions rather than argument. Slight weakness in keeping pain as the central frame during some exchanges rather than fully shifting focus to activity-as-value.
