# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist remains respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive, validating frustration and normalizing the juice ritual. Warmth is consistent but somewhat task-focused and could be deeper for a higher rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are good empathic moments, especially around frustration and the importance of ritual. However, empathy is often brief and quickly shifts into practical advice rather than exploring deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on weight loss and reducing orange juice consumption. The conversation is organized and consistently guided toward practical, target-specific next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco’s own ideas help shape the plan, and the spouse-script sequence is a strong collaborative element. Overall, though, the counselor often functions as the expert by providing structured advice rather than co-creating the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites preferences and offers choices, which supports client ownership. Autonomy is limited by frequent, directive recommendations and prescriptive scripts before fully eliciting Marco’s own ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tools such as the confidence ruler and asking which option feels doable help draw out motivation and commitment. Evocation is present but not consistently developed, as the counselor often supplies answers too quickly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates on direction and asks for Marco’s input on next steps. The balance is undermined by the counselor’s frequent expert-driven problem-solving and detailed planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco spontaneously expresses strong desire, ability, and intention to change. The counselor uses some evocative prompts but does not consistently reflect back or build on this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges the value of the juice ritual without judgment or argument, which helps avoid confrontation. The response could have more effectively used dual-focused reflections to soften sustain talk and connect it to change.
