# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with warmth that is individualized to Nadine's experience as a new parent. The psychiatrist never expresses judgment and responds to self-doubt with validation rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate empathic understanding through complex reflections that capture the intersection of sleep deprivation, exhaustion, and developmental worry. The 3am reflection in particular shows strong emotional attunement to disorientation and entrapment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the target concern with a coherent logical arc from initial complaint through exploration to identifying a concrete need. The session lacks a strong closing summary or consolidation of accomplishments and specific next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is genuinely co-constructed with Nadine's responses directly shaping the dialogue direction. The psychiatrist treats her as an equal partner, with decisions like whether to provide a written list left entirely to her judgment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently centers Nadine's preferences and decision-making, explicitly placing knowledge and authority in her hands. When she expresses uncertainty about whether a written list would help, the psychiatrist validates without pushing back, fully respecting her autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out Nadine's own motivations and insights through evocative questions rather than providing information or reasons for change. Could be deepened by more systematically exploring values and ability-based change talk throughout the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance by positioning Nadine as the expert on her own baby and avoiding unprompted information-giving. Could be strengthened by more explicitly acknowledging her expertise and inviting her to shape the session agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully highlights and amplifies change talk, particularly the strategic reflection of 'I think she's okay' to help Nadine own her own positive statements. Could have pushed further on ability and confidence language to more systematically solidify change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging its emotional impact while reframing, and uses Nadine's own words to counter doubt without arguing. Could have more explicitly used double-sided reflections to hold both the worry and the confidence simultaneously.
