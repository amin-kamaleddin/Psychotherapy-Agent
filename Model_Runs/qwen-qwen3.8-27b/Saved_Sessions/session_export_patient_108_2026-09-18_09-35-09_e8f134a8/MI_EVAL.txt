# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone and explicitly pushes back on Oliver's self-judgment. However, the warmth is somewhat polished and professional rather than deeply personal or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate perspective-taking and genuine attunement to Oliver's emotional core. Some reflections lean toward strategic purpose or slight interpretation rather than pure empathic accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is remarkably well-directed with clear progressive momentum from presenting concern to concrete action plan. The conversation never drifts, and each exchange builds on the previous one toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as joint and actively seeks Oliver's input, creating a genuine dialogue. However, the collaboration follows a somewhat formulaic ask-respond pattern with the psychiatrist as the clear architect of the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a significant strength; the psychiatrist consistently frames Oliver as the decision-maker and never gives advice or imposes timelines. Even strategic questions are framed as invitations to Oliver's own wisdom.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight in the session is self-generated by Oliver, from the decision to the specific plan to the anchor phrase. The psychiatrist's questions are precisely calibrated to draw out deeper layers of internal motivation without ever providing external justification.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance with client-directed language and treats Oliver as the expert on his own life. The power-sharing is genuine, but the psychiatrist still clearly sets the agenda through question selection.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength; the psychiatrist consistently evokes change talk without pushing, and Oliver's confidence language builds progressively from hesitation to committed action. Change talk is reflected and amplified back to Oliver, strengthening it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and not reinforced, with good double-sided reflections that validate both sides. The self-critical 'I'm weak' label, however, is not explicitly reframed or softened, representing a missed opportunity.
