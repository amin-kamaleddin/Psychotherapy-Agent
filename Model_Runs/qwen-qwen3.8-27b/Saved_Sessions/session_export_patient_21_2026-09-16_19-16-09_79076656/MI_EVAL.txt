# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally shows respect, nonjudgment, and acceptance of Camila’s ambivalence. Acceptance is sometimes formulaic and slightly frames the referral as a burden early on.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Camila’s ambivalence and her preference for gentle, optional movement. Empathy could go deeper into the emotional meaning behind her sustain talk, and the repeated question slightly disrupts attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on increasing physical activity and builds toward a concrete walking plan. Direction could be stronger if the plan were more explicitly tied to the client’s stated goals and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Camila to co-create the plan and uses inclusive language. Collaboration remains somewhat therapist-led because the psychiatrist sequences most of the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports choice by emphasizing flexibility, optional participation, and client-owned decision-making. There is little pressure or rigid rule-setting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Camila generate her own plan through open, client-focused questions. Evocation could be stronger by more deeply eliciting values, confidence, or the personal meaning of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and allows the client’s preferences to shape the plan. Partnership could be stronger by explicitly acknowledging the client’s expertise and inviting agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session elicits several forms of change talk, including desire, ability, commitment, and a concrete plan. It could be stronger by more systematically eliciting importance, need, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responds to sustain talk without arguing and integrates it into the plan as protective. Use of double-sided reflections could further soften resistance and strengthen motivation.
