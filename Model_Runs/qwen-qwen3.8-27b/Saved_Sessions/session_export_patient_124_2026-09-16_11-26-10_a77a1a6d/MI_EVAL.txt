# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, nonjudgment, and support while avoiding shame or labels around Sofia’s use. Positive regard is consistent but could become warmer and more individualized beyond her willingness to try a plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening to capture Sofia’s ambivalence, costs, and desire for calm without feeling left out. The reflection could go deeper around her fear of being different and the social pain of not using with friends.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the referral concern and target behavior of reducing marijuana use in a specific social context. The psychiatrist moves clearly from exploration to a concrete, realistic plan for Tyler’s party.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language, invites input, and builds on Sofia’s preferred low-key strategy. The session would be stronger if Sofia generated more of the plan and scripts herself rather than the therapist supplying many options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly offers choices, respects Sofia’s boundaries, and reinforces that she is in control of the plan. More explicit emphasis on her ownership would strengthen the session, especially when introducing options or safety guidance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses importance and confidence rulers, open questions, and affirmations to elicit motivation, and Sofia’s confidence increases from 3 to 5. Evocation could be deepened by exploring her values, goals, and worries about continued use more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and adapts when Sofia declines to rehearse or make the plan feel like a big commitment. More explicit recognition of Sofia as the expert on her own social world would strengthen the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out change talk by asking about importance, confidence, feasibility, and timing, and affirms Sofia’s commitment. The elicitation could focus more on her deeper personal reasons, values, and self-image.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not reinforce reasons for continued use and gently normalizes Sofia’s preferences without arguing. More active MI strategies could be used to reframe the strongest sustain talk about peer belonging and anxiety coping.
