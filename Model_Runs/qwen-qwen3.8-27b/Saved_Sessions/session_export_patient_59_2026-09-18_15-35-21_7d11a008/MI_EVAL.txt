# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with zero judgment, labeling, or disapproval. Effectively honors the client's self-assessment without disagreement while maintaining warm presence throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional reflective listening with sophisticated attunement to linguistic cues like 'I guess' and 'a ways off.' Reflections are accurate, emotionally precise, and never overreach or inflate the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session drifts into general life exploration without tying themes back to the target behavior or establishing a concrete focus. Lacks a clear arc toward any specific behavioral goal or summary connecting themes to next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative interaction between equal partners using shared inquiry language ('I'm curious,' 'I'm wondering'). The client's contributions substantially influence where the session goes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent and explicit autonomy support woven into clinical technique, including 'you don't owe me a decision or a plan' and 'that's your call, full stop.' Never once implies change is expected or uses coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly draws out the client's own values and motivations, particularly around autonomy and identity. However, evoked values are not explicitly connected to the target behavior, limiting directness for the clinical goal.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert on his own life through open questions and by ceding interpretive authority. Never diagnoses, labels, or prescribes — every question is an invitation for the client's own wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully cultivates change talk around identity and values using open questions and amplification of the client's own statements. However, lacks explicit DARN-CAT structure and a clear articulation of behavioral change goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by rolling with resistance and validating the client's perspective before gently exploring gaps. Could be strengthened with more explicit double-sided reflections to acknowledge and pivot.
