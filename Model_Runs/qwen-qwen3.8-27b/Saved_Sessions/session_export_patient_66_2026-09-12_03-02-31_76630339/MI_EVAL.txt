### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician is respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Kareem's preference to continue smoking and his practical concerns. Some responses feel formulaic and less individualized, preventing a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects the client's practical and relational concerns accurately. Misses deeper emotional/identity-level conflict about losing control or balancing enjoyment with job security.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Topic stays on smoking and workplace policy but drifts from cessation/reduction to practical discreet smoking. Strategic direction toward the MI target behavior is inconsistent.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Client contributes key choices, such as car breaks, fan/gum, and Monday start. Collaboration is partly menu-based, with clinician generating much of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong support for choice by respecting non-quit stance and offering options. Ownership of the smoking decision could be made more explicit and repeated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used, mainly for logistics and confidence in the car plan. Deeper client-driven reasons, values, or motivation to reduce smoking are not fully elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician invites choice and allows client preferences to shape the plan. Interaction is somewhat counselor-led because many concrete options and steps are supplied.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Client offers useful change talk about being able to cut back, but clinician does not amplify or build on it. Focus shifts to supporting car-break plan rather than exploring reduction or self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Sustain talk is accepted without arguing, but the session remains centered on continuing smoking discreetly. Limited use of double-sided reflections or redirection toward possible change.
