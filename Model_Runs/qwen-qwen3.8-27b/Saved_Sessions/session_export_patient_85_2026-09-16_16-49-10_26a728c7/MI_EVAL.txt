# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, nonjudgmental language and validates the client’s ambivalence and effort. There is no criticism or shame related to previous weight regain or juice use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client’s perspective, including the tension between protecting progress and missing juice. The counselor tracks both the practical and emotional experience of the change process.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing fruit juice in a way that supports the weight goal. The conversation moves strategically from ambivalence to a concrete morning routine and action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is structured as joint exploration rather than expert-driven advice. The counselor builds directly on the client’s ideas, such as environment changes, water substitution, and lemon.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently uses noncoercive, choice-based language and invites the client to decide what is doable. The client remains the primary decision-maker throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open-ended questions draw out the client’s own motivations, strategies, and confidence. The counselor relies on the client’s internal reasoning rather than supplying reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor positions the client as the authority on his own habits and preferences. The session feels like a shared, respectful problem-solving process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently evokes and reinforces the client’s desire, ability, reasons, and commitment to change. Change talk is reflected and expanded rather than imposed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor handles resistance or attachment to juice without arguing or minimizing. Sustain talk is acknowledged and gently reframed as part of an honest, manageable experiment.
