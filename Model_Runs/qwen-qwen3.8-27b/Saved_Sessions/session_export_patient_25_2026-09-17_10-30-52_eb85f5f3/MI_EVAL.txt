# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone, affirming strengths and normalizing imperfection. Some interpretive statements are slightly presumptive, preventing a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures ambivalence, values, and emerging motivation. One generic response missed an opportunity to deepen the client’s social discomfort and control concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on alcohol use in social settings. The flow from ambivalence to planning, values, and coping is coherent and strategically guided.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client’s ideas clearly shape the session and strategy. The psychiatrist works as an equal partner without imposing a prescribed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently supported in choosing what feels authentic and doable. There is no pressure, directive advice, or imposed commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is primarily drawn from the client’s own values, goals, and imagery. Occasional leading interpretations reduce the depth of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is positioned as the expert on his own life and options. Some therapist framing is helpful but slightly leading rather than fully neutral.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session elicits strong client-generated change talk, including plans, values, and self-efficacy. Deeper use of confidence or commitment scaling would strengthen the work.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and reframed without argument or pressure. The response to the killjoy/control concern was initially generic rather than maximally reflective.
