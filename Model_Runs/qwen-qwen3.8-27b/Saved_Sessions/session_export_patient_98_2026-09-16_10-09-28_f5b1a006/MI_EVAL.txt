# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows basic respect and nonjudgment, but validation remains surface-level. The stance is respectful but somewhat transactional rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are reasonable reflections of Mateo's perspective, but they are often brief and fact-focused. Important emotional themes such as fear, resignation, and feeling unable to contribute are not explored deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on physical activity in rehab and pain-related barriers. The discussion remains relevant to the presenting concern, though repeated instruction adds some rigidity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adjusts the plan based on Mateo's preferences. However, the process remains therapist-led rather than truly co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by offering options and encouraging Mateo to stop if pain worsens. The client is given meaningful room to make his own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used to draw out Mateo's values and goals. However, frequent psychoeducation and procedural instruction reduce client-centered exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Mateo's input is invited, but the interaction remains expert-driven. The psychiatrist repeatedly instructs and guides rather than sharing power with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited when Mateo identifies goals such as helping his wife and trying breathing before chair work. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen this client-initiated motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not directly argue with Mateo's doubts or pain-related barriers. There is no sustained MI strategy for reframing sustain talk toward exceptions, values, or change motivation.
