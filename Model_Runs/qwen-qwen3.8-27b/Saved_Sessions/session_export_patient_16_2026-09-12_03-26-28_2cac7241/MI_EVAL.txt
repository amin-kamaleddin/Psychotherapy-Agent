# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, including normalizing slips and appreciating Anika's effort. Acceptance could be warmer or more individualized when Anika showed defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and useful, especially in capturing ambivalence about vaping, basketball, and social connection. The reflection could go deeper into underlying emotions such as peer pressure and fear of awkwardness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on vaping related to basketball season, with clear movement toward importance, confidence, a plan, fallbacks, and follow-up. Some broadening occurred when offering multiple options or practice suggestions, but the overall direction remained consistent.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anika's choices shaped the final plan, and the interaction avoided an authoritarian stance. Collaboration was strong, though the psychiatrist still provided a fairly structured list of strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support was a clear strength, with frequent invitations for choice and respect for Anika's refusals. The psychiatrist consistently avoided pressure and supported Anika's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Importance and confidence ratings helped evoke some motivation and planning. The psychiatrist often offered suggestions before fully exploring Anika's own motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist fostered partnership by repeatedly asking Anika to choose among options and allowing her preferences to guide the plan. The stance was collaborative, though still somewhat counselor-structured.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anika produced useful change talk related to playing well, feeling sharper, and avoiding being benched. The psychiatrist could have amplified and deepened this change talk more effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist did not challenge or reinforce sustain talk, which was appropriate. More active softening, such as double-sided reflections, could have helped Anika examine ambivalence more directly.
