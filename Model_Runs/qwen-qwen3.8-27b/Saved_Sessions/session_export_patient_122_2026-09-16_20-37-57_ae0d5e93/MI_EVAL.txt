# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained warm, nonjudgmental respect and validated Sofia’s concerns without moralizing. Language such as “It makes sense” and “really practical insight” supported a safe, accepting atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflected Sofia’s ambivalence, worry, and cautious optimism using complex, two-sided reflections. The responses captured both the desire for change and the fear that small actions might not “count.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed mostly focused on building a small, sustainable movement practice despite stress and self-criticism. However, the target behavior and session goal were not explicitly negotiated, and closure or next steps were not clearly summarized.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was collaborative, with Sofia’s ideas largely shaping the session. The stance could become more fully equal-partner if the counselor used more explicit co-creation language and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supported Sofia’s ownership by inviting her to choose what would work for her. The counselor avoided prescribing, persuading, or minimizing difficulty, reinforcing self-direction and choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively drew out Sofia’s own practical solutions and reasons for change. The evocation could be strengthened by more explicitly exploring values, confidence, identity, or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a generally collaborative stance and allowed Sofia to lead with her own ideas. It fell short of a 5 because the counselor did not explicitly name Sofia as the expert or invite her to set the session agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia produced strong, concrete change talk through open questions and reflection. The psychiatrist could strengthen this by more consistently labeling and amplifying desire, ability, need, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sofia’s sustain talk about stress, fatigue, and small actions not counting was handled skillfully without argument or pressure. The counselor acknowledged the barriers and redirected them toward realistic, client-generated alternatives.
