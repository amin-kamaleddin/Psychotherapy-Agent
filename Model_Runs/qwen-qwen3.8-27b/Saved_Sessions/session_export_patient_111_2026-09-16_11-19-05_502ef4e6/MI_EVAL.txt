# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, avoiding blame, shaming, or forced insight. Acceptance is strong but not peak because the warmth is somewhat formulaic and does not fully normalize Richard's anger or fear of control.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Richard's routines, reluctance, and desire for marital peace. Empathy could deepen by exploring underlying emotions and using more double-sided reflections of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on smoking and related relational stress. The direction is somewhat plan-focused and could better guide Richard toward deeper values and internal motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates the plan with Richard, using inclusive language and adapting to his choices. The resulting behavior plan is clearly shaped by the client rather than imposed by the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Richard's right to choose is strongly and consistently respected throughout. The psychiatrist avoids pressure when he declines strategies, scripts, and scheduled follow-up, while leaving the door open for support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions, scaling, and invitations help elicit some of Richard's own reasons and plan. Evocation remains somewhat superficial, with less exploration of values, identity, and discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally conveys that Richard is the expert on his own life and adapts to his preferences. Partnership is stronger if the agenda were more explicitly negotiated and less structured upfront.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Richard produces some change talk, especially around peace, stopping the fighting, and trying a small step. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect, or deepen that change talk into stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepts resistance without arguing and makes some useful reframing moves. More active rolling with and redirecting sustain talk would strengthen the response to resistance.
