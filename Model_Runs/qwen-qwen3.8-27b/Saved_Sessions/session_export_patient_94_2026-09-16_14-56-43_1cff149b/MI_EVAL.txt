# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and reflects Maria's frustration without arguing or shaming. The stance is strong but not fully deepened by affirming her strengths, effort, or underlying values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides accurate complex reflections of Maria's practical conflict between time constraints and breathing safety. Reflections are mostly cognitive/logistical and do not fully explore the deeper emotional meaning behind her resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior of inhaler use and moves toward a concrete next step. However, the direction becomes narrow and procedural, centered on drafting a prescriber message rather than promoting a broader change decision.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works jointly with Maria, checking wording and inviting her to define an acceptable rule. Collaboration is strongest around logistics and the prescriber message rather than exploring her broader goals and priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure, respects Maria's requests, and affirms that she decides what to do with the answer. Autonomy support is strong but functions more as non-interference than active empowerment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions draw out Maria's preferences and practical constraints. The psychiatrist does not consistently evoke deeper motivation, health values, confidence, or willingness to try a simple asthma management approach.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Maria as the expert on her life and co-constructs the prescriber question. Partnership is strongest around message wording and logistics, with less shared agenda-setting around the larger asthma decision.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks appropriate MI-style questions that invite Maria's input. The session remains focused on logistics and medical clarification rather than cultivating and amplifying change talk about asthma management.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Maria's concerns about dependency, side effects, or daily medication. However, sustain talk is given considerable space and is not consistently reframed or redirected toward values and change.
