# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, validation, and nonjudgment, reducing shame around asking for help. Acceptance could be warmer and more relational before moving into problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections acknowledge stress and practical burdens, but often remain surface-level. The psychiatrist could better reflect deeper fear, loss of control, and worry about failing her son.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on Daniela’s urgent childcare concern and moves clearly toward actionable steps. The counselor appropriately does not pursue smoking or drinking without Daniela’s preference.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates next steps and responds to Daniela’s preferences. Supplying many scripts, lists, and plans makes the collaboration strong but less fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly offers choices and avoids pressuring Daniela to act. More explicit reinforcement of her freedom to decide would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor tends to move quickly into practical guidance rather than eliciting Daniela’s own motivations. There is limited effort to draw out her values, hopes, confidence, or internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniela’s choices shape the session, but the psychiatrist frequently takes an advisory role. The counselor often positions themselves as the primary problem-solver rather than fully sharing power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela offers spontaneous change talk, but the psychiatrist does not skillfully reflect, amplify, or deepen it. Evocative questions to strengthen her own motivation are largely absent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Barriers are acknowledged without argument, and some concerns are gently reframed. The session would benefit from more MI strategies to redirect sustain talk toward change, values, or self-efficacy.
