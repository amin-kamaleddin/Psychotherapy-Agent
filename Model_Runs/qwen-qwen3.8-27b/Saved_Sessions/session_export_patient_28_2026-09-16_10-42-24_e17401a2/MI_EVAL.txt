# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows consistent respect, validation, and nonjudgment while normalizing Dante’s doubt. The opening is slightly presumptive, and repeated approval of the plan makes acceptance feel a bit procedural.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses accurate complex reflections of ambivalence and practical barriers. Reflections remain mostly cognitive and do not consistently name deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on re-establishing a regular exercise routine with minimal drift. The psychiatrist steadily moves from ambivalence to a concrete, target-specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and builds the plan around Dante’s real constraints. The structure remains therapist-led, making the collaboration feel more like consultation than full co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Options are offered and Dante’s low-pressure preference is respected. Some prescriptive statements about what counts and success rules reduce fully client-defined autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits Dante’s own motivations, values, and confidence. Evocation is sometimes blended with directive advice and explanations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Dante’s choices and respects his judgment in shaping the plan. The session is still fairly therapist-directed in agenda and structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Dante move from ambivalence to client-generated action steps and increases confidence. Some therapist-generated rules and options limit fully client-led change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and redirected toward smaller, manageable steps. Some sustain talk is addressed more quickly with definitions rather than client-led exploration.
