# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, but the session can feel agenda-driven and utilitarian despite the client's repeated time concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few empathic statements, but the response is mostly factual and does not consistently reflect Maria's fear of being forced into long-term medication use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on daily inhaler use and asthma safety, but it could be guided more strategically toward client-evoked motivation and a concise collaborative next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is largely expert-led, with limited equal partnership, shared agenda-setting, or consistent use of Maria's perspective in shaping the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by framing the plan as a short trial and saying Maria is in control, but earlier directive medical advice makes this support inconsistent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some concerns are elicited, but the psychiatrist relies heavily on providing reasons and options rather than helping Maria articulate and develop her own change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative moments around the short trial, but the psychiatrist often drives the session with medical explanations and safety instructions despite the client's request for brevity.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk emerges mainly from Maria, such as willingness to try a short trial, but the psychiatrist does not consistently evoke, reflect, or amplify it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist often responds to resistance with direct medical information rather than using double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance, leaving Maria's ambivalence underacknowledged.
