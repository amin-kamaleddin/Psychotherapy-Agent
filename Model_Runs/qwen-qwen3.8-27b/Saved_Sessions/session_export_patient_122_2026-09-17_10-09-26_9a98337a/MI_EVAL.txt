# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys warm, nonjudgmental regard and normalizes Sofia’s experience without blaming her. Acceptance could be strengthened by affirming her worth independent of performance or productivity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening to capture Sofia’s rhythm, ambivalence, and fear of self-judgment. Some responses are interpretive rather than fully checking meaning with the client before assigning it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is a clear initial target, but the conversation frequently drifts from the specific behavioral goal. The session lacks a consolidated, client-owned next step tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is largely collaborative, building on Sofia’s ideas rather than prescribing a plan. It could be stronger with more explicit shared decision-making about how to frame the next step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently supports Sofia’s ownership and choice without using pressure or should statements. Occasional therapist-supplied interpretations slightly reduce her freedom to define her own experience.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out Sofia’s own solutions, values, and self-observations. Evocation is weakened when the therapist offers interpretations instead of asking Sofia to generate them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is treated as the expert on her own life, and her contributions shape the session. The partnership is occasionally shifted by therapist-generated interpretations or options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers some forward-moving language, but the psychiatrist does not consistently elicit or amplify change talk. More explicit questions about benefits, confidence, ability, and commitment would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing sustain talk, and acknowledges barriers respectfully. However, the session dwells on reasons against change without enough strategic redirection toward values or change talk.
