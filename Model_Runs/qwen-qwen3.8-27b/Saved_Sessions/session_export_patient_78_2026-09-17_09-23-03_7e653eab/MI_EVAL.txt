# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, especially when the client expressed frustration about being managed. The clinician avoids moralizing and affirmatively reframes the client’s pause as self-protection.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, nuanced reflections capture the client’s exhaustion, need for control, and the function of the cigarette. These reflections deepen engagement without distorting her experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session remains relevant to smoking but often shifts toward relational and stress topics. It lacks a clear collaborative agenda or strategic momentum toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician frames the session as joint work and asks how support should feel different for the client. Client input directly shapes the tone and focus of the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly respects client choice and avoids pressure, persuasion, or directive advice. Even when exploring a small step, the clinician preserves client ownership and avoids framing it as compliance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out the client’s own perspective on stress, control, and the value of the break. Could more consistently elicit and expand desire, ability, need, or commitment to reduce smoking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear power sharing by inviting the client to set the pace and lead the conversation. The client’s priorities, especially stress and not being managed, substantially shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions open space, but the clinician does not consistently amplify or build on change talk. Statements like “I know I should quit” and “maybe I’ll try it once” are not further developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles resistance without argumentation and normalizes the client’s reluctance to quit. Could more consistently pivot sustain talk toward change or use double-sided reflections.
