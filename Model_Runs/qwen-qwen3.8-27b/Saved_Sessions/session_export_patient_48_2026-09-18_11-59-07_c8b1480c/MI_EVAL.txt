# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and unconditional positive regard throughout with zero judgment. The psychiatrist skillfully reframes Farid's self-criticism (calling his belief 'dumb') and offers genuine, specific, non-contingent affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are complex, emotionally attuned, and capture both sides of Farid's ambivalence with precision. The psychiatrist picks up on subtle emotional cues in real time (e.g., 'there's something almost tender in that') and uses sophisticated metaphors to distill the emotional core of the struggle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, logical progression from general ambivalence to a specific, actionable step, with each exchange building on the last. Focus never wanders, and momentum toward a concrete action is maintained without feeling rushed or forced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses first-person language ('I'm curious,' 'I wonder') that positions her as a participant in shared exploration rather than a clinician assessing a client. The content of the session is shaped entirely by Farid's responses, and the interaction feels like two equals thinking together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No directive language, no 'you should,' and no imposition of the psychiatrist's agenda at any point. The final concrete step (parking further away) is entirely Farid's idea, and his doubts and hesitations are validated rather than talked out of.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** This is the standout strength of the session—the psychiatrist consistently draws out Farid's own motivations, insights, and solutions rather than providing them. She resists the natural urge to suggest answers, and the final plan emerges 100% from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions herself as a partner in exploration using collaborative language, never telling Farid what to do or what the solution should be. The power dynamic is clearly equal—she follows his lead and builds on his contributions rather than steering toward a predetermined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Each instance of change talk is caught, reflected back, and expanded (e.g., affirming 'trying' as a word that shifts the frame from judgment to process). Strategic questions are used to elicit deeper change talk, progressing from vague desire to concrete intention.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk (all-or-nothing thinking, fatigue, self-doubt) is acknowledged and gently explored through strategic questions rather than argued with. Each instance is validated and then redirected toward possibility without ever being reinforced or dwelled upon.
