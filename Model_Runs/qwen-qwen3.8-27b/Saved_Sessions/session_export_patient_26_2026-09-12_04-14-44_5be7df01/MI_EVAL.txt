# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and frequently affirms Daniela's efforts and choices. Acceptance is strong, though it could be deeper by normalizing the broader difficulties of being a single mother with limited resources.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate and helpful reflections of Daniela's stress, ambivalence, and worry. However, the empathy often remains practical rather than deeply exploring the underlying fear, disappointment, loneliness, and exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a concrete target, moving clearly from an urgent childcare concern to a small, actionable plan. The direction is somewhat narrow because it does not meaningfully connect childcare stability to Daniela's broader goals, values, or substance-reduction efforts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Daniela to choose and incorporates her ideas, such as the neighbor arrangement and 2-1-1 backup. However, the frequent offering of scripts and prepackaged options makes the interaction feel more like guided problem-solving than equal collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses autonomy-supportive language and respects Daniela's right to make her own decisions. This is undermined by repeated directive suggestions, scripts, and offers to draft the text even when Daniela prefers to handle it herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some client-driven motivation, readiness, and confidence ratings throughout the session. However, evocation is limited by the tendency to fill gaps with suggestions rather than fully eliciting Daniela's own resources, ideas, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally cooperative stance and allows Daniela's input to shape the plan. The balance is weakened by frequent advice-giving and problem-solving behaviors that position the psychiatrist more as an expert than an equal partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some change talk by inviting Daniela to identify an urgent target, choose a small step, and rate confidence. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently deepen, amplify, or expand Daniela's own statements of desire, ability, values, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Daniela's barriers and avoids arguing or dismissing her sustain talk. However, doubts and barriers are often met with additional options or practical strategies rather than MI-consistent techniques that gently soften and redirect ambivalence.
