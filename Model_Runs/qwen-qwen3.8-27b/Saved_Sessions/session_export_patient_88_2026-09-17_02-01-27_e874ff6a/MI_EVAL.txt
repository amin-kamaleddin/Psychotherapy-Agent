# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, using validation without moralizing, but occasionally makes premature positive assumptions and mirrors shame-laden labels without fully normalizing them.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track Marcus's ambivalence, fear of exclusion, and social concerns, including a clear repair when he worried the psychiatrist implied he would lose his friends.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on drinking in peer contexts and belonging, but it does not explicitly consolidate a specific target goal such as reducing, delaying, or avoiding drinking in parties.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by building on Marcus's ideas, but could more explicitly invite joint problem-solving and shared goal-setting to strengthen equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language consistently emphasizes Marcus's choice and avoids pressure, warnings, or advice, strongly supporting his autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions draw out Marcus's own values and concerns, but the strongest change talk could be more consistently amplified and expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Marcus's perspective and lets his concerns shape the session, though the agenda remains somewhat therapist-directed without explicit agenda negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits several change statements about hating drinking to fit in and wanting easier social presence, but does not consistently deepen or summarize the strongest change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without argument or warning, and reflections reduce defensiveness, but more active pivots to values or autonomy would further neutralize it.
