# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently normalizes ambivalence and avoids judgment or pressure. Acceptance is strong, though occasionally tied to the client's insight rather than unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and repeatedly uses the client's own metaphors and language. The empathy closely captures both behavioral and emotional meaning, with only minor limits in exploring broader values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on smoking during work breaks and does not drift. Each question and reflection progressively builds toward a concrete, target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on the client's ideas and lets Kareem's contributions shape the conversation. Collaboration is strong but could be more explicit in agenda-setting and equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly affirms Kareem's right to choose and avoids pressing for commitment. Autonomy support is a standout feature, especially in framing change as client-led design.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn out from within Kareem through open, client-centered questions. The psychiatrist avoids advice-giving and allows the client to generate his own plans and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Kareem as the expert on his own life and seeks his perspective throughout. However, the therapist still leads much of the structure and repeatedly frames the goal as a small shift.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes and amplifies change talk by reflecting the client's emerging motivations as meaningful progress. A limitation is the lack of systematic exploration of confidence, commitment, or a clear summary of change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument using respectful double-sided reflections. However, sustain talk remains prominent, and more strategic exploration of costs or inconsistencies could further soften it.
