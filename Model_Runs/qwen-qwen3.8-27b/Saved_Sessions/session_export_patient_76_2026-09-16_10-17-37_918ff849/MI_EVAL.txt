# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Liora’s pain and small efforts. The warmth is slightly formulaic and occasionally interrupted by repeated procedural safety questions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers several accurate reflections of Liora’s burden and relief. However, the empathy remains surface-level and misses deeper feelings such as shame, isolation, and helplessness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on self-harm safety, urges, and a manageable action step. The repeated risk-assessment questions make the flow feel somewhat mechanical.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Liora’s choices shape the plan, and the psychiatrist adapts to her limits. Still, the psychiatrist generates much of the content and safety options, limiting a more balanced partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly emphasizes choice and respects Liora’s decisions to decline follow-up and additional steps. Some repeated invitations to act create subtle pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for doable options and past coping strategies, eliciting a few client-driven commitments. It does not deeply explore Liora’s values, hopes, or internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is collaborative language and willingness to stop at the client’s limit. The interaction remains somewhat counselor-driven and does not fully center Liora as the expert on her own experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Liora voices some change talk around the zippered bag and trying water. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or amplify this talk with follow-up questions or reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and reduces pressure by accepting Liora’s limits. It does not consistently reframe sustain talk in ways that open space for change.
