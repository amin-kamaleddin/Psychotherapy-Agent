# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows warm, nonjudgmental acceptance of Maria’s suicidal ideation, exhaustion, and ambivalence. Phrases such as “I’ll let no be enough” communicate unconditional regard without pressure or shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and emotionally attuned to Maria’s pain, exhaustion, and ambivalence. The psychiatrist captures the pull between escape and caring for loved ones without minimizing or overinterpreting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on immediate safety and manageable short-term steps. Some broader well-being or “after this step” questions could reduce precision in an acute crisis.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Maria as an equal partner and adjusts to her stated needs, such as quiet, a low voice, and not being rushed. The session is shaped collaboratively rather than driven by the clinician’s agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through repeated choice, respect for limits, and a no-pressure stance. The psychiatrist avoids demanding bravery, compliance, or quick problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes Maria’s internal resources, such as not wanting to be alone and the value of not being pushed. Evocation could be deepened further by drawing out longer-term motivations, values, or prior coping strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly conveys that Maria is the expert on her own experience and invites her to guide the session. Questions are used to follow Maria’s pace and preferences rather than leading her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is present, such as staying for the hour and not wanting to decide yet. The psychiatrist could more consistently deepen and expand Maria’s own reasons for staying and confidence in coping.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk and hopelessness are generally acknowledged without argument, minimization, or amplification. Some language around “this step” is potentially risky and could unintentionally reinforce suicide as a concrete action.
