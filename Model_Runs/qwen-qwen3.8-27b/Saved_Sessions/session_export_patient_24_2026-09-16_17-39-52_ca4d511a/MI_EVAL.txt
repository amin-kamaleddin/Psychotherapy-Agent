# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses nonjudgmental, validating language and respects Daniel's defensiveness. Acceptance is competent but somewhat formulaic and does not strongly affirm his personhood or strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Daniel's perspective, ambivalence, and protective reasons for maintaining his routine. The main limitation is that reflections stay somewhat surface-level and miss deeper emotional undertones.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The conversation stays broadly focused on alcohol use and training but becomes circular. It does not clearly move toward a concrete, client-chosen target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the session as joint exploration and incorporates Daniel's training language and preferences. Collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist remains the primary agenda-setter.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Daniel's choice, avoids pressure, and supports his sense of control over the process. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming the legitimacy of choosing no change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open-ended questions and reflections rather than giving advice. However, evocation is limited to problem identification and does not fully draw out Daniel's motivation or values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally positions Daniel as the expert on his body, training, and goals. Partnership could be improved by more explicit co-creation of the agenda and shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few attempts to connect alcohol use to Daniel's values, but the psychiatrist does not systematically evoke or develop change talk. The session does not build sufficient momentum toward change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing but allows sustain talk to remain central. Responses do not effectively soften Daniel's resistance or redirect toward discrepancy and possible change.
