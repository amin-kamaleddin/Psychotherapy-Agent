# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys respect, nonjudgment, and acceptance of Kareem’s right to continue smoking. The warmth is sometimes somewhat formulaic, which prevents a full 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is good reflective listening and perspective-taking, especially regarding Kareem’s ambivalence and practical concerns. The empathy remains largely cognitive and does not consistently explore deeper emotional or motivational meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on smoking management in the new workplace and follows a logical sequence toward a plan. It becomes somewhat repetitive and would benefit from a clearer summary or consolidation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Kareem’s preferences and ideas rather than imposing a predetermined plan. The collaboration is slightly constrained by repeated structured multiple-choice prompts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Kareem’s freedom of choice and ownership without pressure or persuasion. Statements such as “if you still smoke after, that’s still your call” are highly MI-consistent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kareem’s own ideas and practical solutions are frequently elicited throughout the session. The evocation could be stronger by connecting more deeply to intrinsic motivation, values, or confidence in change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kareem is positioned as the expert on his own routine, and his contributions clearly shape the session. The psychiatrist’s structured agenda and repeated option prompts keep the stance slightly counselor-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, but it is not consistently expanded, amplified, or developed. The clinician often moves to the next practical option rather than deepening Kareem’s change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is accepted without argument, which is MI-consistent. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently pivot, reframe, or balance sustain talk with future-focused or value-based change language.
