# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone with normalizing and affirming statements. The relational style could be warmer and less procedural, especially with frequent advice-giving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Basic reflective listening and perspective-taking are present, including acknowledging automaticity and discomfort. Deeper, complex reflections of Elena’s ambivalence and emotional complexity are often missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on people-pleasing at work and the specific boundary with the principal. The main weakness is occasional drift from motivational exploration into skill rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is collaboration through role-play and client choice of which script to practice. However, the psychiatrist often supplies scripts and step-by-step instructions, making the process more expert-driven than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and validates Elena’s sticky-note strategy. Frequent direct instructions such as rehearsal steps and sequences reduce the sense of client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some open questions and affirmations are used, but the session relies more on external strategies than on evoking Elena’s own motivation. Values, reasons for change, and confidence are not explored deeply enough.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Collaborative invitations are present, but the interaction is largely expert-driven with scripts, sequences, and skill-building. The psychiatrist does not consistently position Elena as the primary source of wisdom in the room.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena offers useful change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently develop or amplify it. More questions about values, ability, and reasons for change would strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is normalized and not reinforced, such as worries about sounding cold or hating conflict. The psychiatrist could better soften sustain talk by reflecting ambivalence and pivoting toward change language.
