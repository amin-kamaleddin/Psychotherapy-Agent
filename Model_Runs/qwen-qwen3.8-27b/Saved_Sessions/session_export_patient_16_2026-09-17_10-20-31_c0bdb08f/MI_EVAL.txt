# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated warmth, respect, and nonjudgment while validating the client's ambivalence. Positive regard could be strengthened by more unconditionally affirming the client's peer attachments and social context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured the client's competing desires and emotional meaning. Could benefit from deeper exploration of underlying feelings, such as fear of disappointing others.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the target behavior (vaping/marijuana use) by connecting it to specific situations like basketball practice. Early conversational anchors were somewhat vague and could be more behaviorally specific.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction felt like a working partnership, utilizing the client's own ideas and scripts. Full collaboration could be improved by more explicit agenda co-creation rather than offering pre-defined options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supported the client's agency by avoiding advice and returning decisions to her. Could further enhance this by using more explicit ownership-affirming language such as 'this is your call'.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client-centered questions effectively elicited intrinsic motivation and values without being persuasive. Opportunities were missed to further deepen the elicited motivation into a formal commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed that the client is the expert on her own life and change process. Session direction was genuinely shaped by the client's personal goals and linguistic contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evoked and amplified the client's change talk, reinforcing her desire and ability to change. Could be more skillful in systematically moving the client from confidence to higher levels of commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully normalized the client's pull to 'chill' and reframed sustain talk without moralizing or contesting. Responses to sustain talk regarding social breaks could be more explicitly double-sided.
