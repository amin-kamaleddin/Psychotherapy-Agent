# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirms Amina’s perspective. However, acceptance remains somewhat generic and focused on approving the plan rather than warmly affirming her as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects practical concerns such as FOMO, tiredness, and difficulty using DND. A deeper response would more fully capture the emotional ambivalence between enjoying connection and feeling out of control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing late-night social media use and improves sleep. It maintains good direction but becomes overly focused on tactical solutions rather than exploring meaning and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adjusts plans when Amina objects, which shows some collaborative flexibility. Collaboration is limited, however, because most ideas and options are still supplied by the psychiatrist rather than co-created with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice and reduces pressure by inviting Amina to select doable steps and emphasizing that the plan is not permanent. Some statements remain mildly prescriptive, and offering to write the sticky note can reduce Amina’s ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session is largely problem-solving rather than evocative, with limited exploration of Amina’s values, motivations, or discrepancy. Strong evocable material, such as fatigue, slipping grades, and fear of punishment, is not fully drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Amina’s input and avoids a purely expert-driven stance. Partnership is weakened because the psychiatrist remains the main strategy provider and does not consistently position Amina as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Amina offers small commitments, but the psychiatrist does not systematically evoke her desire, ability, reasons, or need for change. Her concerns about fatigue and grades are powerful but not amplified or reflected to build motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and acknowledges concerns about FOMO and limits feeling punitive. However, the response often adds more behavioral controls rather than using reflections to soften sustain talk and redirect it toward change.
