# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician is respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive, normalizing the patient’s concerns without shaming. Acceptance is good but could be warmer and less agenda-driven to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but remain largely surface-level, missing deeper feelings such as stress, fear of failure, and identity. Deeper emotional reflection is needed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on mini-cigar use, harm reduction, and a practical next step. Direction is clear but somewhat checklist-like and could better connect to the patient’s values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician asks for permission, invites preferences, and allows the patient to shape the plan. Collaboration is strong but still partly clinician-directed in content and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a major strength, with repeated affirmation of choice, acceptance of refusal, and no pressure, tracking, or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician asks for the patient’s perspective but often moves to practical advice instead of deepening motivation. Evocation is present but superficial.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician maintains a collaborative stance and incorporates patient preferences into the session. Partnership is good but not fully power-shared due to repeated plan-oriented summaries.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, but the clinician does not amplify or explore its meaning, values, or confidence. Deeper elicitation would strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing and accepts sustain talk, but does not strategically reflect back ambivalence or reframe it toward change. Sustain talk remains relatively strong.
