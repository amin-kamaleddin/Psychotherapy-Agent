# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a warm, nonjudgmental tone and reinforces Daniel’s strengths. Validation is generally supportive, though it could be more individualized to his ambivalence and social concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** He validates Daniel’s feelings about social pressure and discomfort, but often moves quickly from reflection to practical problem-solving. Deeper emotional reflections would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on alcohol use in social situations, narrowing to shots and a specific target response. Direction is clear, behaviorally specific, and well sustained.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choices and collaborative rehearsal, showing responsiveness to Daniel’s preferences. However, he remains the main source of strategies, limiting true co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniel is given options and asked to choose, which supports autonomy. The advice-heavy approach and repeated success framing reduce emphasis on his full ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The initial open question elicits Daniel’s own goal and concerns, and his responses show emerging motivation. The session then shifts to directive advice rather than continuing to evoke internal reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites input on which strategies fit Daniel. Partnership is uneven because much of the plan content is clinician-generated rather than client-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniel offers meaningful change talk, including his goal to avoid “blowing a night out” and confidence that managing shots would help. The psychiatrist does not sufficiently amplify or build on this language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about restriction, honesty, and social awkwardness is acknowledged without argument. More MI-consistent exploration would be needed to help Daniel resolve ambivalence in his own words.
