# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance throughout. However, acceptance remains somewhat formulaic and not fully individualized or warmly affirming of Jamal as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and captures the emotional core of Jamal's frustration and ambivalence. Empathy could deepen by exploring underlying feelings such as loss of autonomy, embarrassment, or resentment toward authority.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally maintains focus on school tardiness and the morning routine, but the session narrows toward stopping the mother's nagging and reducing morning stress. It does not consistently return to Jamal's own meaningful reasons for being on time.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates some shared process by inviting input and adapting to Jamal's preferences. However, collaboration becomes uneven and somewhat transactional because the psychiatrist frequently offers concrete solutions rather than co-creating with Jamal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by asking permission, offering choices, and respecting Jamal's boundary against lectures. Slightly reduced because specific scripts and practical tips still feel mildly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses appropriate open-ended questions and importance/confidence rulers to elicit information. However, evocation remains superficial and does not sufficiently explore low importance, intrinsic motivation, or the personal benefits of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows some partnership by inviting choices and responding to Jamal's input, but still takes on an expert problem-solving role. The psychiatrist could better share power by treating Jamal as the expert on his own morning routine and household dynamics.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes initial attempts to evoke change talk, but does not follow up on Jamal's change-relevant statements such as knowing how to get there. The conversation quickly shifts to problem-solving rather than expanding Jamal's own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk, which is appropriate, but does not effectively soften or redirect it. The session remains centered on stopping the nagging rather than linking it to Jamal's autonomy, values, or personal reasons for change.
