# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental throughout. Positive regard could be slightly more explicitly unconditional rather than tied to the client's insight or effort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate, nuanced, and consistently capture both content and emotional meaning. This is one of the strongest parts of the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior of family presence without drifting into advice or abstraction. The conversation moves clearly from values to concrete, workable actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor co-constructs the work using the client's ideas and language. More explicit shared-ownership language would make the collaboration even stronger.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports the client's sense of choice and avoids pressuring language. Reinforcing the client's freedom to adjust or abandon the plan would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client generates much of the meaningful change language in response to well-chosen open questions. Deeper questions about identity, importance, or confidence could increase impact.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is treated as the expert, and her contributions shape the session. The collaborative stance would be stronger if the power-sharing approach were stated more explicitly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits substantial change talk and affirms the client's emerging plan. More direct questions about confidence or commitment would deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges exhaustion and fear without arguing, then gently reframes them into manageable options. More deliberate double-sided reflections would make the softening even more strategic.
