# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance without blaming Kevin for his alcohol use or injury. Acceptance was strong, though it could have felt warmer and more explicitly affirming of his personal difficulty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and helped Kevin feel heard, especially around the tension between social fun and safety. Empathy could have been deeper by exploring the emotional and identity-based meanings behind his drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on Kevin’s alcohol use in social situations and safety-related risks. The direction was generally clear, though it could have been less repetitive and more strategically consolidated toward one concrete focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly collaborated by letting Kevin’s own ideas shape the session. The interaction used Kevin’s language and avoided a lecture-like or expert-driven approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin was consistently positioned as the decision-maker, with invitational language and no pressure to cut down. Autonomy support could have been more explicit in later parts of the session when discussing specific strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited useful problem-solving ideas and some change talk, but did not deeply draw out Kevin’s values, goals, or confidence. Deeper evocation would have helped connect his safety concerns to personal meaning and motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a collaborative stance and regularly invited Kevin’s input. The partnership was strong but could have been made more explicit through shared decision-making and agenda negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist created opportunities for Kevin to generate change-oriented ideas and affirmed his contributions. Change talk was present but could have been more consistently deepened, amplified, and summarized to build motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged respectfully rather than challenged, which is appropriate. However, the psychiatrist did not consistently use strategic reflections to gently soften sustain talk or build momentum toward change.
