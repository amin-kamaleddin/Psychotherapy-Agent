# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming, but the warmth can feel somewhat generic. Support is clear, though the session moves quickly into advice rather than deep affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections of frustration, worry, and social concern are present, but they often remain superficial. Deeper ambivalence, shame, or peer-rejection tensions are not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Focus stays clear on alcohol use on Friday nights and its academic, legal, and social consequences. The session proceeds with little drift from the target issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for input and includes Tyler's ideas, but supplies many strategies and drives problem-solving. The dynamic is collaborative on the surface but more counselor-led than equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tyler is invited to choose options and rehearse, which reduces overt pressure. However, frequent scripts and the drafted text weaken emphasis on his full freedom and self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Importance and confidence scaling elicit some motivation and client-generated ideas. The psychiatrist also supplies many reasons and strategies, so evocation is present but not deeply client-centered.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are partnership moves, such as asking which step feels most doable. Yet the psychiatrist often assumes an expert role by providing exact phrases and a draft text.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tyler generates useful change talk, including high importance, rising confidence, and not wanting to mess up the semester. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about missing out, being boring, and fear of Mike's disappointment is addressed, but mostly with scripts and logistics. It is managed rather than skillfully redirected toward change.
