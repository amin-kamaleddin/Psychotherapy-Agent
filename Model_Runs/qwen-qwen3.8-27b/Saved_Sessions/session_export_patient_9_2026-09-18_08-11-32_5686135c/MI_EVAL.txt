# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental regard throughout, with specific and individualized affirmations (e.g., 'I want to honor what you just said'). They explicitly create psychological safety and normalize her struggle without a single instance of judgment or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflects are exceptionally precise, capturing both emotional and meaning-level content (e.g., the dual experience of wanting to protect her son while being positioned as the problem). The psychiatrist skillfully uses Amina's own metaphors and holds paradox without resolving it prematurely.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, logical arc from identifying the problem through values clarification to exploring alternatives, with no drift or meandering. Could be elevated by explicitly summarizing progress to reinforce momentum (e.g., 'you've gone from going in circles to seeing you can be steady').

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is structured as genuine joint exploration, with the psychiatrist following Amina's lead and using her language and metaphors back to her. A 5 would require more explicit co-creation language (e.g., 'let's figure this out together') to signal equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is woven naturally into every interaction through open questions, permission to be uncertain, and reframing that preserves agency without directive advice. The psychiatrist never uses 'you should' or 'you need to' and consistently frames choices as open.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Every major question is designed to elicit Amina's own voice, successfully producing rich change talk including her vision of her son 'just being a kid' and her boundary-setting insight. Could be strengthened by further evoking confidence talk and commitment talk in later stages.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a genuinely collaborative stance, consistently positioning Amina as the expert and using invitational rather than directive language. A 5 would require more explicit power-sharing language acknowledging that Amina is driving the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questions successfully elicit change talk (e.g., 'What would settling look like?' 'What's yours to hold?'), and the psychiatrist builds on Amina's own language. Could be improved by more explicitly amplifying and reinforcing change talk moments as they emerge.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully through validation without reinforcement (e.g., acknowledging that pushing keeps him safe is a real lesson from real experience) followed by gentle pivoting. Could be more active in helping Amina see that her boundary-setting insight already represents a path forward that doesn't require pushing.
