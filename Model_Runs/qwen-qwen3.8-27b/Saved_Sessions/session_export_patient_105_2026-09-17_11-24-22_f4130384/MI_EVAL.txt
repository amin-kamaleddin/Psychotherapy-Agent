# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, and validating tone throughout, normalizing Naomi's struggle without minimizing it. The slight limitation is occasional formulaic phrasing (repeated 'that has to be' constructions) that keeps it from a perfect 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and nuanced reflective listening that tracks Naomi's metaphors ('trying to catch water,' 'room becoming a mirror') and deepens them with increasing precision. Reflections are specific, timely, and consistently advance understanding beyond simple paraphrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on social anxiety with a logical progression from physical experience to cognitive layer to pattern identification. It lacks a clear arc toward a specific change goal or collaboratively identified next step for future work.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine sense of equal partnership throughout, with language that positions the psychiatrist as a collaborator rather than authority. Metaphors offered are collaborative invitations Naomi can use or discard, maintaining a consistently balanced power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Naomi's ownership and right to choose, explicitly rejecting a prescriptive stance ('I'm not going to just tell you that'). The 'settings menu' metaphor reinforces that the agency to change resides within Naomi, with no coercive language or implied obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation that consistently draws out Naomi's own motivations, insights, and evidence of capability rather than imposing the psychiatrist's interpretation. Each question is designed to elicit self-analysis, counter-evidence, values, and existing capability in a progressively deepening manner.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Naomi, using collaborative and inquisitive language rather than directive statements. The psychiatrist explicitly disclaims the expert role and never prescribes, lectures, or fills silences with their own interpretation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk at key points, drawing out Naomi's own counter-evidence and vision of change. The main gap is the lack of explicit affirmation, amplification, or consolidation of change talk when Naomi offers statements of desire or capability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by gently pushing back on the 'forever' belief without arguing and pivoting to counter-evidence. The handling is occasionally reactive rather than proactive, missing opportunities to more actively pivot to evidence Naomi has already provided.
