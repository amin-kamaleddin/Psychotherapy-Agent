# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, non-judgmental language, but it often sounds scripted. Acceptance remains surface-level and does not deeply affirm Amina as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects Amina's perspective several times, but the reflections stay fairly surface-level. There is limited exploration of deeper feelings such as frustration, fear, or lack of control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays generally focused on the presenting issue, but it becomes repetitive and lacks a clear strategic arc. More patience with silence and a clearer next step would improve direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and offers choices, but continues to drive the session with repeated question menus. Collaboration is present in tone but not yet a true sharing of direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes Amina's choice, control, and right to remain silent. This is one of the stronger dimensions, though repeated prompts after refusals can still feel subtly pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited effort to draw out Amina's own values, motivations, or reasons for change. Most questions are closed or preference-based rather than evocative.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and avoids an expert stance, but the interaction remains more counselor-managed than truly shared. Amina's minimal responses do not clearly shape the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is one early attempt to invite change-relevant language, but no follow-up to develop it. The session does not effectively evoke desire, confidence, or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or punishing Amina's resistance and reframes it in understandable terms. This is a relative strength, though more skilled pivots could gently open the door to change.
