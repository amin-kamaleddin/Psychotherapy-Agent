# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes Kenji’s ambivalence. Some affirmations still tie praise to task completion, which slightly limits unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Kenji’s tension between the relaxing effects of drinking and health/work concerns. A few reflections remain surface-level or slightly misattribute the source of the plan.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing alcohol use and moves logically from ambivalence to a concrete plan. Pacing occasionally becomes too task-focused without returning to the central change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan is largely co-created, with Kenji selecting the timer, text, and sticky note. The counselor still generates much of the strategy, leaving room for more client-initiated ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently respects choice and avoids pressure, including Kenji’s conditions for follow-up. The high number of suggestions could subtly steer Kenji toward specific actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions elicit some change talk and self-assessment. Key emotional statements about silence, turning off the brain, and self-judgment are underexplored as sources of motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for preferences and positions Kenji as having useful options. However, the counselor’s many specific suggestions make the interaction feel more therapist-directed than truly collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kenji expresses desire, ability, and commitment, especially around the timer and saved text. The counselor often moves into problem-solving before amplifying or expanding Kenji’s own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges the appeal of drinking and avoids argument. Sustain talk is not consistently explored or resolved, especially around immediate relief, friends, and routine fatigue.
