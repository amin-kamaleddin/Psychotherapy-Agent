# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming. Some warmth feels broad or performance-like rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects ambivalence and the 'not enough' theme. Emotional nuances such as shame, social comparison, and fear of failure are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on increasing physical activity and moves toward a workable plan. A clearer summary of the target behavior and plan would strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is conversational and builds on the client’s ideas. It could feel more collaborative with explicit joint decision-making language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently places decision-making with the client and avoids pressure. The client is allowed to define flexibility and meaning without being told what to do.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits the client’s own motivations, ideas, and solutions. Some questions are slightly leading by suggesting possible markers or frames.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is treated as an expert on her life and her ideas guide the session. The psychiatrist still offers suggestive options rather than fully sharing agenda-setting and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client generates useful change talk about walking, dancing, the jaw unclenching, and protecting the coffee break. The psychiatrist could more explicitly deepen confidence, commitment, and meaning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges barriers without arguing or agreeing with them. More explicit double-sided reflections would strengthen the softening of sustain talk.
