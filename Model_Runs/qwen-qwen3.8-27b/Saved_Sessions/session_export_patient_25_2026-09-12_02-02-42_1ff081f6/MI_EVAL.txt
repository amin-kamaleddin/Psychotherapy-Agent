# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental while affirming the client's progress and self-awareness. It was slightly reduced by quick shifts into problem-solving and planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured the client's behavior, social tension, and next-day guilt. It could be stronger by exploring deeper emotional undercurrents and internal conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed consistently focused on alcohol use in social gatherings. It moved logically from exploration to a concrete next step without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist built on client ideas but frequently generated the plan's content and options. More equal co-exploration would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist used noncoercive language and supported low-effort client choice. Some directive implementation details slightly reduced the client's sense of full ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist used open questions and rulers but often moved quickly to advice. More time evoking the client's own reasons, values, and ability would be needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client input was invited, but the therapist frequently led with structured options and scripts. More open-ended exploration before advice would improve the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client produced useful change and commitment statements, but the therapist often interrupted evocation with strategies. More amplification of the client's own change talk is needed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was normalized without confrontation, keeping the client engaged. It could be stronger with double-sided reflections and deeper values exploration.
