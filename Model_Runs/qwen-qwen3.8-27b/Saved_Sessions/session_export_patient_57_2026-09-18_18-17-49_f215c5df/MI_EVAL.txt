# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with specific, contextual validation throughout the session. Zero labeling or disapproval; the psychiatrist explicitly reframes the encounter away from punishment and normalizes Jamal's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate double-sided reflections capturing both identity and belonging concerns. Occasions where empathy could go deeper emotionally, as reflections sometimes stay at the cognitive level rather than fully inhabiting the emotional weight of Jamal's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with logical progression from rapport through exploration of ambivalence. The session lacks a final crystallization toward a concrete behavioral goal or specific next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaboration with consistent joint exploration framing and the psychiatrist building on Jamal's ideas rather than overriding them. The clinician's strategic guiding role keeps the session just below full mutual direction-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, explicit autonomy support throughout with open questions inviting Jamal to define his own answers. No coercive language, guilt-tripping, or implied obligation at any point in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Jamal's internal motivations through values-based questions without imposing external reasons for change. Could push deeper with scaling questions or more thorough exploration of the 'why' behind Jamal's stated values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance with explicit rejection of an expert-driven approach and frequent use of curiosity-based language. The psychiatrist still maintains a clear therapeutic agenda that guides the conversation strategically, keeping it just below full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits meaningful change talk through strategic questions and skillfully amplifies emerging change talk (e.g., 'That's not nothing — that's you finding a third option'). Could push further into confidence/ability language or concrete commitment to develop change talk to its maximum potential.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and uses the effective MI move of reflecting sustain talk while amplifying embedded change talk in the same utterance. One moment of all-or-nothing sustain talk ('everyone else is just vibing') is acknowledged but not fully softened before moving to the next question.
