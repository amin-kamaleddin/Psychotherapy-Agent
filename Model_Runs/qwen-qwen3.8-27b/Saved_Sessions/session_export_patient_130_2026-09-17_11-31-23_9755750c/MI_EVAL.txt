# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with no judgment or labeling throughout. Warmth is genuine and individualized; Viktor's minimizations are reframed positively rather than corrected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and nuanced reflective listening that goes beyond surface content to capture emotional and relational dynamics. Notable double-sided reflections that name both caring and defensiveness without taking sides.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on Viktor's drug use and its relationship to his daily life and marriage, with logical progression from defense to emotional function. Could be slightly more deliberate in connecting emerging insights back to the specific target behavior of reducing use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructs the session with Viktor controlling the pace and direction. Language is exploratory rather than authoritative, and the psychiatrist stays with not-knowing rather than filling silences with interpretation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Viktor's autonomy, putting goal-setting and evaluation criteria entirely in his hands. The final open invitation ('Can you tell me more about that?') is textbook autonomy support for a precontemplative client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All meaningful insights ('the silence,' 'whether I'm enough,' 'I need to wind down') come from Viktor's own mouth, drawn out by careful questioning. Never supplies external reasons for change or imposes dissonance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly cedes interpretive authority to Viktor and consistently positions himself as curious rather than authoritative. Viktor's contributions clearly shape the session's direction, with no predetermined therapeutic arc imposed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out change talk through strategic questioning, particularly the skillful 'when was the last time you looked at that Friday evening and felt good about it?' Could have pushed further into developing discrepancy between Viktor's stated values and his behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Viktor's defenses skillfully by never arguing or amplifying sustain talk, instead redirecting toward exploration. Could have used slightly more explicit double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while gently tilting toward the change side.
