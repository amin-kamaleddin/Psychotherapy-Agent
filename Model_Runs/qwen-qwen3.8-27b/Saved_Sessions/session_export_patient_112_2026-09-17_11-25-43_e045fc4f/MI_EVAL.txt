# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing Rosa's discomfort about the referral and validating each concern as legitimate. Warmth is consistent, non-formulaic, and deeply individualized to Rosa's specific fears.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are complex, accurate, and emotionally attuned, capturing meaning and emotion rather than just content. The clinician tracks Rosa's emotional arc from defensive to vulnerable without rushing her process.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a coherent strategic arc from rapport-building through exploring the functional meaning of smoking to the social concern. The session ends on an exploratory note without a clear collaborative synthesis or identification of a focus point.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a fully joint exploration where every forward movement is based on something Rosa has said, with reflections incorporating her exact language. The clinician never leads with information or advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Rosa's right to choose and positions the decision as entirely hers, never implying that non-quitting is failure. Autonomy support is woven naturally into every interaction rather than being a separate intervention.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every piece of motivation that emerges comes from Rosa's own mouth; the clinician never provides external reasons for change. The role is to create space for Rosa's own wisdom and past capabilities to surface.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Rosa, explicitly deferring to her judgment and positioning themselves as a curious companion. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully amplifies Rosa's own change language (e.g., 'even if I want to quit,' 'I can't argue with a stethoscope') and invites her to generate her own solutions. Could have pushed further by explicitly revisiting and expanding her strongest change statements or eliciting confidence/ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with validation rather than argument, using strategies like holding ambivalence and reducing pressure. Could be stronger with more explicit double-sided reflections on the most resistant sustain talk rather than relying primarily on evoking change talk as a counterweight.
