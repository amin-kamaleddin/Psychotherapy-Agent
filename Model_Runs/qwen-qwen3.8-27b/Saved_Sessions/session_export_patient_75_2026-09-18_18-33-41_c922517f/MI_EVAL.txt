# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, non-judgment, and validation throughout; never labels or expresses disapproval. Acceptance is solid but remains within good clinical practice rather than reaching exceptional depth of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathy that captures both cognitive and emotional dimensions with specific, accurate reflections that progressively deepen. Goes well beyond surface-level paraphrasing to convey genuine understanding of the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behavior with a logical progression from ambivalence to the fear loop to identity distinctions. Lacks a clear directional arc toward a concrete next step or strategic endpoint, ending with an unresolved concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with language like 'figure things out together' and active seeking of Linda's input before offering reflections. Remains somewhat implicit rather than actively co-constructing a plan or next step together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy without pressuring or using directive language; frames the work as Linda's own process. Could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her capacity for choice and ownership, especially at moments of doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Linda's own insights through strategic questions, with key realizations (the loop, the routine comparison, identity vs. process) emerging as her own. Could more consistently elicit explicit change talk language around confidence, ability, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance throughout with frequent invitations for Linda's perspective and no lecturing or unsolicited advice. Could be stronger with explicit power-sharing language acknowledging Linda as the expert on her own experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasionally elicits change talk and acknowledges it when it arises, but misses multiple opportunities to deepen and expand it through strategic probing questions. The change talk that emerges is primarily Linda's own insight rather than something actively cultivated by the counselor.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk reasonably with one effective move externalizing the problem ('the loop is keeping me from doing this'), but does not consistently use double-sided reflections or reframing to redirect toward change. The session ends with unaddressed sustain talk ('I'm not sure I believe I can just choose differently').
