# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates the client's stress and constraints. Affirmations could be warmer and more individualized to strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses reflective listening to capture both practical burdens and emotional worry. Deeper reflections of underlying emotions would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target issue of reliable childcare. Related topics such as work, school, and finances remain connected to the main goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by building on the client's ideas and using joint language. More explicit agenda negotiation and power-sharing would improve collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by inviting the client's perspective and avoiding directive advice. Some questions are slightly leading, especially around the backup plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes practical plans and client-generated ideas. Deeper elicitation of values, confidence, and internal motivation would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adopts a non-expert stance and invites the client's input. More explicit statements that the client is the expert on her life would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client's own change talk, especially practical desire, ability, and activation. More explicit reflection and reinforcement of change talk would strengthen this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges barriers without arguing or reinforcing sustain talk. More advanced techniques, such as double-sided reflections or reframing, would further improve this skill.
