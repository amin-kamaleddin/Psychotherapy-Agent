# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with a warm, genuine, and individualized tone. Never judges the client's struggles and validates self-awareness without being condescending.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and emotional meaning, including underlying patterns like all-or-nothing thinking and outsourcing permission to confidence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on physical activity with logical progression from goal identification to specific plan to concrete action steps. Each question serves a strategic purpose in moving toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a masterclass in collaborative MI with the client identifying goals, naming barriers, proposing plans, and articulating insights. The psychiatrist amplifies and builds on these contributions rather than driving the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently returns locus of control to the client, never imposes advice, and respects her pacing. Powerfully reinforces ownership through reflections like 'You're the one who figured out the plan' and 'Choice instead of obligation.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out the client's own motivations through open questions rather than providing reasons for change. The final 'choice instead of obligation' insight emerged from within the client, with the psychiatrist creating conditions for it to surface.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within the client, never assuming the role of expert or telling her what to do. The client discovers her own insights and the psychiatrist reflects them back as her own discoveries.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies the client's own change language in real-time, helping her see her own progress. The final reflection crystallizes her emerging insight about choice versus obligation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively validates the client's worry about not following through while gently redirecting toward action, and normalizes skipping days to soften all-or-nothing thinking. However, the sustain talk is not explored with the same depth and strategic attention as the change talk.
