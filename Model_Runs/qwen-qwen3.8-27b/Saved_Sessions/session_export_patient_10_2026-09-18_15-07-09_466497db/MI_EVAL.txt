# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, responding to guardedness with 'I'm not going to push' and validating concerns without premature solutions or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections progressively deepen from content to meaning to pattern level, capturing underlying relational dynamics and cognitive patterns like 'blurring together' with remarkable accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear progressive focus on the target behavior, moving from identifying barriers to a concrete action plan. Could be slightly tighter by explicitly connecting solutions to specific interview timelines or deadlines.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing with joint framing language and consistent seeking of client input. Could be elevated by more explicit co-creation of next steps between sessions rather than therapist-led strategic guiding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is woven throughout in both language and structure—never prescribes, creates urgency, or makes decisions for the client. The final action plan is entirely self-generated by Amina.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Amina's own motivations, insights, and solutions through strategic questions. Could use more open (non-suggestive) questions at key junctures and more explicitly evoke her stated confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Amina as the expert in her own life, with the session shaped entirely by her contributions from problem identification through action plan. Never overrides, corrects, or imposes an alternative direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates natural escalation of change talk from 'I still believe I can do this' to 'less impossible' through strategic questions and amplifying reflections. Could have more explicitly elicited confidence language or drawn out additional 'I can' statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with textbook skill—validates concerns without arguing, then gently reframes by reflecting back the client's own alternative perspective. Sustain talk is given full space without being reinforced or amplified.
