# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Ryan’s experience without moralizing. The main limitation is that affirmations become somewhat formulaic and could include more individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ryan’s ambivalence about fitting in versus protecting his safety and education is accurately reflected. However, reflections remain somewhat surface-level and repetitive, with less exploration of underlying shame, fear, or social loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor evokes practical change ideas such as a soda, saying no earlier, a soda team, and stepping outside. Change talk is not consistently reinforced, amplified, or linked to importance, confidence, need, or ability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist treats Ryan as a problem-solving partner and builds on his ideas, especially the soda-team concept. Collaboration is slightly limited by a fixed safety/education agenda and several counselor-generated suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ryan is given space to generate ideas, and the therapist avoids commands and premature commitment. Autonomy could be strengthened by more explicitly emphasizing Ryan’s freedom to choose, including the option to continue current behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions elicit useful practical problem-solving language from Ryan. The counselor does not consistently evoke deeper values, discrepancy, confidence, or internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Ryan to direct parts of the conversation and builds on his perspective. The session remains somewhat counselor-directed due to repeated emphasis on protecting safety and education.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ryan offers meaningful change-related ideas, including non-alcoholic drinks, a supportive peer, advance agreement, and taking a brief break. The counselor could more skillfully amplify and summarize these statements to strengthen their motivational value.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or shaming Ryan’s desire to fit in and have fun. Sustain talk is mostly accepted neutrally rather than gently redirected through double-sided reflections or values-based discrepancy.
