# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used a warm, nonjudgmental tone and effectively validated the patient's self-criticism and progress. However, the pace sometimes felt procedural, and the patient's final worry was not explicitly acknowledged before closing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured the patient's physical sensations and cognitive concerns well. The response missed an opportunity to deeper empathize with lingering insecurity and fear near the end.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed consistently focused on the target behavior and goal of increasing follow-through. Scaling questions and planning steps kept the conversation purposeful and on track.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist actively worked with the patient to develop a safety plan, incorporating her choices for the seat, text, and rehearsal. It could be improved by exploring the patient's own solutions before offering pre-made scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respected the patient's need for control by emphasizing her right to choose and providing an easy out. The frequent provision of specific scripts and external supports slightly reduced emphasis on the patient's internal autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked scaling questions to evoke change, but frequently offered solutions before eliciting the patient's own ideas. More evocative questions would better support the patient in discovering her own motivations and strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a collaborative stance by asking for the patient's input on doable steps and safety needs. The interaction occasionally leaned toward clinician-led planning rather than a balanced partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Importance and confidence scaling successfully elicited some goal articulation and increased confidence. The session's focus on concrete planning interrupted opportunities to amplify the patient's intrinsic reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively addressed the patient's concerns about following through without dismissing them. However, the conversation remained centered on the status quo rather than being gently redirected toward the patient's broader values.
