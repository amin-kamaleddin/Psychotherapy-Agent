# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with warmth and normalization met at every moment, including self-deprecating statements. Zero judgment or implicit pressure; client's self-awareness is treated as a strength.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond paraphrasing to capture emotional and existential meaning (e.g., the jar as a 'signal'). Accurately reflects ambivalence and the underlying self-concept issue with remarkable precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session progresses logically from goal identification to concrete action steps, maintaining general focus on the target behavior. Slightly diluted when expanding into nutrition without explicitly re-confirming priority or ranking goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal-partner dynamic where Amira's contributions substantially shape the session's direction. The psychiatrist acts as a co-explorer rather than a director, building on the client's leads without a predetermined script.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All suggestions framed as exploratory ('What if...?') with no directives, and all plans explicitly attributed to Amira ('you found it yourself'). The psychiatrist removes pressure to be convinced and reinforces freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions consistently access Amira's own knowledge, values, and self-awareness rather than providing external reasons for change. All motivation is generated internally, with no statistics, guilt, or moral arguments used.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language consistently communicates that expertise resides within the client ('you found it yourself,' 'you've just named something really important'). The psychiatrist acts as a facilitator of the client's own wisdom rather than an authority with answers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently amplifies and reflects back Amira's change talk across multiple forms (desire, reasons, ability, commitment). Strategic questions elicit further change talk, making the client's own words the primary source of motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argues, dismisses, or minimizes sustain talk; instead uses double-sided reflections that validate the barrier while highlighting the change side. Gently pivots toward the client's existing strengths without reinforcing the sustain talk.
