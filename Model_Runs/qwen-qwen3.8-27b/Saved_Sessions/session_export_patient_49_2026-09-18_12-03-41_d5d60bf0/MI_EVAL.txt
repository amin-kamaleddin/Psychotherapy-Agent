# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming engagement throughout. Affirmations are specific to what the client just said rather than generic, demonstrating genuine unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture emotional undertones and deeper meaning beyond surface-level content. Double-sided reflections precisely articulate the client's ambivalence and the evolution of their thinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the client's relationship with smoking with a logical progressive arc from initial reaction to self-motivated intention. No topic drift, and pacing is well-calibrated for contemplation-stage change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook collaboration where the client's ideas consistently shape session direction and the psychiatrist builds on each contribution rather than imposing their own framework. The session feels like two people thinking together as equal partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports the client's autonomy, never pressuring or implying she should quit. The final framing of the client's intention as curiosity rather than a plan is a particularly strong example.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire session is structured around evoking the client's own motivations and solutions, with no advice or external reasons offered. The client arrives at a complete, self-owned plan entirely through the psychiatrist's evoking questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside with the client, not the psychiatrist. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with the psychiatrist functioning as a thoughtful collaborator rather than a guide.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes progressive change talk from awareness to intention to action, with explicit reinforcement of the client's own change talk. The progression moves naturally without pressure, with each response building on the previous one.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk appropriately without arguing or dismissing, using validation and double-sided reflections. Score limited because the client presents minimal resistance, providing limited opportunity to demonstrate advanced softening techniques.
