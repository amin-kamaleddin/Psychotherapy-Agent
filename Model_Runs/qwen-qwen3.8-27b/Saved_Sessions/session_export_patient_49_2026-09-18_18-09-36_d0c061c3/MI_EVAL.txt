# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains warm, non-judgmental unconditional positive regard throughout, with zero labeling or disapproval. Hana's ambivalence and doubts are met with validation (e.g., 'I don't think you're wrong to wonder') rather than pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond paraphrasing to capture the emotional and physical dimensions of Hana's experience, including the 'unfairness' of holding tension all day. The psychiatrist consistently uses Hana's own language ('the pause,' 'timer,' 'shoulders drop') rather than clinical jargon.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent thread (habit → need → alternative → experiment) without wandering into unrelated topics. Pacing is appropriate with gentle forward momentum from ambivalence to a concrete, testable experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Hana's ideas (the exhale, shoulders dropping, one break) drive the session direction rather than a pre-determined agenda. The psychiatrist builds on Hana's metaphors and never overrides her lead, creating a genuinely equal power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses language centering Hana's agency ('what's your own take,' 'for you,' 'what's the smallest thing'). Affirms her competence ('You already know what to do') and explicitly invites her to direct the therapeutic relationship.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No external reasons for change are provided; all motivation is drawn from within Hana through precisely calibrated open questions. Each self-generated insight is amplified and affirmed, with the final question prompting Hana to assess her own internal shift.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Hana as the expert and co-creator from the opening statement through the closing question. Never corrects Hana or introduces information she hasn't already generated, maintaining a genuinely collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully progresses Hana from ambivalence to concrete experimentation language through a series of open questions that elicit self-generated solutions. Every piece of change talk comes from Hana herself, never from the psychiatrist's agenda.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validates Hana's concerns ('That's a fair worry') without dismissing, arguing, or minimizing. Reframes sustain talk as useful information and explores the underlying need rather than countering the 'habit' framing directly.
