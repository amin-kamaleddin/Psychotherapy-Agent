# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates warm, unconditional positive regard throughout the session with genuine, individualized validation. Never judges, labels, or disapproves; acceptance feels authentic rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate understanding of Elena's perspective with reflections that capture both content and emotional nuance. Shows genuine attunement to subtle shifts in her language and evolving experience within the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on integrating values into daily behavior with a logical progression from values clarification to practical application. Could benefit from a clearer closing summary that consolidates the work and identifies specific next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance using Elena's own metaphors (muscle, key, roommate, kitchen) to build the conversation. Could be strengthened by more explicit co-construction moments and acknowledging the psychiatrist's own role in the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Elena's autonomy, never prescribing, advising, or pressuring. Fully honors her statement that she's 'not looking for something new' rather than redirecting toward a plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out Elena's own motivations and reasons for change rather than providing them. Could deepen evocation by exploring more dimensions of change talk, particularly ability and confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Elena, attributing insights to her and affirming her agency. Occasionally takes a slightly more leading role in the metaphorical work, offering interpretations before inviting Elena's own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out and effectively reinforces Elena's change language through skillful questions and reflections. The session is somewhat limited in evoking future-oriented change talk or explicit confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is minimal in this session, and what does appear is handled well by normalizing rather than dismissing. Could use slightly more strategic double-sided reflections, though the low level of sustain talk makes this less critical.
