# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, but the warmth feels practical rather than deeply affirming. Sofia's specific struggles are acknowledged without much individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture the surface content of Sofia's statements, but often miss the emotional meaning underneath. Anxiety, family expectations, and identity pressure could have been reflected more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays broadly focused on marijuana use, stress, and cost, but drifts into many practical tactics. A stronger MI direction would keep the conversation more consistently focused on motivation and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and offers choices, but frequently leads with solutions and strategies. Sofia's role as an equal partner in selecting issues and goals is underdeveloped.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms Sofia's choice and avoids controlling language, which is a strength. Frequent suggestions for action create some subtle advisory pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and scaling are used to explore motivation, but advice interrupts the process. Sofia's own values and concerns, such as family expectations, are not deeply evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session begins from a collaborative stance and follows Sofia's lead on the money issue. However, repeated advice and multiple strategies make the interaction feel more therapist-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, especially through scaling questions and Sofia's statements about money and self-doubt. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect, or expand this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is tolerated without argument, but it is not effectively softened using double-sided reflections or discrepancy work. The psychiatrist often shifts to practical strategies instead of exploring the conflict in Sofia's statements.
