# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, supportive stance and normalizes David’s difficulty. The main gap is a lack of more explicit affirmations of David’s effort, values, or courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows strong perspective-taking and accurately reflects the emotional and relational meaning of David’s experience. Some reflections remain at the content level and could go deeper into shame, fear, or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on smoking cessation and relevant practical concerns. Even emotionally or relationally charged content is kept tied to the goal without forcing it.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is strongly collaborative, with the psychiatrist building directly on David’s ideas. David’s contributions substantially shape the plan, success indicators, and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently avoids taking over, pressuring, or prescribing a course of action. Language throughout supports David’s ownership, choice, and self-determined decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits motivation from within David through open-ended, future-oriented questions. David’s change talk clearly strengthens as the session progresses.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions David as the expert on his own life and what is doable. The session direction is driven by David’s priorities, concerns, and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes David’s reasons, confidence, and ability statements in favor of change. However, the change talk is not consistently amplified, consolidated, or deepened enough.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk without arguing and validates the function of smoking. The main weakness is a less strategic pivot away from balanced ambivalence toward agency and change.
