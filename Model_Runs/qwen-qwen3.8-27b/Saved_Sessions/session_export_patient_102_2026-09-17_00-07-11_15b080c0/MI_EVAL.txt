# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently avoids judgment and moralizing while using respectful, validating language. Acceptance is strong, though it remains somewhat procedural rather than deeply reparative at times.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures several surface-level concerns and ambivalent feelings. Empathy is present but not consistently deep enough to fully explore Milan’s underlying fears, peer dynamics, or emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on Milan’s alcohol use, weekends, grades, practice, and sleep. Direction is generally clear, but momentum remains uneven by repeatedly returning to monitoring and warning signs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative language invites Milan’s perspective and uses his own standards in the discussion. However, collaboration becomes uneven when the psychiatrist continues asking questions despite Milan repeatedly wanting to stop.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through affirmations of choice, respect for Milan’s lead, and avoidance of pressure or advice. The continued focus on monitoring and signs subtly undermines fully honoring his autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation is present, as Milan identifies his own standards around control, grades, practice, and sleep. The psychiatrist does not fully deepen these into stronger change talk or explore Milan’s personal values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Milan as the expert and avoids an authoritarian stance. Partnership is not exemplary because the agenda still leans toward monitoring and the session does not fully negotiate Milan’s desire to stop.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Milan offers some change-related statements about grades, sleep, practice, and feeling in control. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or develop these into stronger motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally met with validation rather than argument or reinforcement. Softening is limited because questions often keep the conversation centered on risk monitoring instead of linking Milan’s concerns to his own values.
