# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates the client's prior effort and current frustration. Warmth is present but can feel formulaic when paired with rapid problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects ambivalence and captures the tension between wanting change and enjoying juice. However, the response often shifts to planning before fully exploring the emotional impact of the setback.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing fruit juice and moves clearly toward a concrete weekly plan. The focus is generally strong, though it occasionally drifts toward coaching-style advice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and co-constructs the plan with the client. At times, the counselor acts more like a problem-solver than an equal partner eliciting the client's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor checks doability, preferences, and options, supporting the client's sense of choice. Autonomy is slightly limited by the counselor's frequent suggestions and the authority-framed "doctor's orders" script.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and planning prompts elicit useful change talk about concrete actions. Deeper intrinsic motivation, values, and identity are not fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and allows the client to shape much of the plan. The stance becomes less collaborative when specific actions and scripts are introduced before fully eliciting the client's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits concrete change talk about limiting juice, using water, walking, and preparing a brief response. Elicitation remains mostly behavioral rather than deeply motivational.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk around still liking juice is accepted and integrated into a realistic plan rather than resisted. Active softening is limited to acceptance and redirection rather than deeper double-sided reflection.
