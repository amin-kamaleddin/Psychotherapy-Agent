# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is nonjudgmental and validates Amina’s ambivalence, reducing pressure. Some warmth feels slightly formulaic and lacks deeper affirmation of her worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective statements accurately capture both the benefits and costs of phone use and the emotional burden. They could go deeper into underlying values, shame, or identity-level conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays on social media use but shifts among grades, triggers, and multiple experiments, diluting focus. It lacks a clearly consolidated target behavior and strategic MI focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and invites Amina to choose, creating a collaborative tone. However, many counselor-generated options limit Amina’s role in driving the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is frequent low-pressure language emphasizing choice, experimentation, and no commitment. The high volume of suggestions still leans toward counselor-driven options rather than full client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks useful questions that elicit client material about triggers and the function of scrolling. It does not consistently explore values, confidence, or importance of change, and moves to strategies too quickly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids authoritarianism and invites shared decision-making, which supports partnership. Yet Amina mostly responds to the clinician’s ideas, so client-led direction is not strong enough for a top score.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina offers meaningful change statements, such as trying a playlist and putting the phone in the bag. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, affirm, or amplify these statements with evocative MI techniques.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist validates Amina’s need for connection and escape rather than arguing with it. Softening is uneven, as alternatives are often introduced without using double-sided reflections or pivots to turn sustain talk into change talk.
