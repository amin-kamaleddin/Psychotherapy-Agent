# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirms Oliver without shaming him. Warmth is occasionally reduced by coaching-style advice and scripted language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Oliver’s ambivalence, exhaustion, guilt, and need for a clear boundary. The empathy is solid but remains somewhat surface-level rather than deeply exploratory.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior: asserting a boundary, maintaining the 15th, role-playing, and planning a follow-up text. The direction is practical, though it can feel more like behavior rehearsal than guided change-process exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Oliver’s choices shape much of the session, including the role-play style and follow-up text. Collaboration is strong, though it is limited by the frequent offering of ready-made phrases and recommendations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking Oliver to select scripts and role-play scenarios. However, the frequent use of scripts, quick tips, and directives can reduce Oliver’s ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open questions help elicit Oliver’s motivation and clarify his goal. Evocation is limited by the frequent shift to counselor-supplied scripts rather than deeper exploration of values and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Oliver influences several session decisions, and the psychiatrist asks for his preferences. Partnership is uneven because the psychiatrist often takes an expert or coaching role rather than fully collaborating.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Oliver produces meaningful change talk, and the psychiatrist reinforces some of it. The psychiatrist could more consistently deepen statements of desire, ability, need, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk around guilt, fear of being the villain, and the sofa temptation is acknowledged without being reinforced. The psychiatrist effectively redirects these concerns toward maintaining the boundary, though with less nuanced double-sided reflection.
