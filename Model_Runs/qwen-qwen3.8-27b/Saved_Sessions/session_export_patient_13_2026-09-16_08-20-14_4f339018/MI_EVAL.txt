# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently shows warm, nonjudgmental positive regard. Anara’s fear, overwhelm, and caregiving limits are validated without disapproval or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are specific and accurate, capturing both the factual and emotional meaning of Anara’s experience. The counselor tracks her ambivalence and changing emotional state throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on obtaining a safe, manageable form of medical advice. The conversation moves logically from fear and barriers to a concrete, low-pressure next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed rather than prescribed. The counselor invites feedback and revises the script and options based on Anara’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforces Anara’s right to choose and her ability to set limits. Options are offered without coercion, and Anara is supported in deciding only what she can manage.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor draws out practical readiness and preferred steps, but relies heavily on offering options and scripts. There are fewer open-ended questions that deepen Anara’s intrinsic motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Anara is treated as the expert on her own life, limits, and preferences. Her choices directly shape the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, such as identifying a manageable option and choosing a helpful script line. However, the counselor does not consistently amplify or expand Anara’s own reasons, confidence, or capability for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully avoids arguing with Anara’s reasons for avoiding care and validates her need to protect her limits. The use of gentler language and normalization of fear helps reduce the intensity of sustain talk.
