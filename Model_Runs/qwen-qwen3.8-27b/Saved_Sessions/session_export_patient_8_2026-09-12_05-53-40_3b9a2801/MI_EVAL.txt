# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental language. Acceptance could deepen by more explicitly affirming Amina's values, courage, and self-insight during self-critical moments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening around ambivalence, fear of failure, and the tension between connection and sleep. Empathy could improve by exploring deeper emotions such as loneliness, academic anxiety, guilt, or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on reducing excessive social media use and its impact on sleep, focus, and coursework. The psychiatrist consistently connects related concerns back to the central target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates effectively by inviting Amina's input and co-creating a practical plan. A stronger level would involve relying more heavily on Amina's own ideas before offering multiple practical options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Amina's choice and avoids direct pressure or coercion. The pace toward implementation could slow slightly to further emphasize Amina's ownership of the decision and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully elicits Amina's own ideas and confidence through readiness and confidence scales. Evocation could deepen by exploring Amina's values, meaning, and discrepancy more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and allows Amina to guide the plan. A 5 would require less expert-driven option-giving and more explicit acknowledgment of Amina as the expert in her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes concrete change talk and increases Amina's confidence. The session could benefit from more explicit amplification and reflection of DARN-CAT change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not reinforce sustain talk and acknowledges ambivalence appropriately. However, sustain talk such as FOMO and 3 a.m. temptation is often redirected into planning rather than softened with double-sided reflections.
