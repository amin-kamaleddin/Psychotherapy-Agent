# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirmed Aisha’s efforts without correction or shame. Acceptance could be strengthened by explicitly communicating unconditional worth during moments of self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate, complex, and consistently attuned to Aisha’s perspective and emotional tone. The counselor captured both hope and worry without distorting the client’s meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on restarting exercise and related concerns such as body comfort, self-talk, and confidence. Guidance moved strategically from motivation to a realistic, client-centered plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor built the plan from Aisha’s ideas rather than driving the content. Collaboration would be stronger with more explicit agenda-setting and power-sharing language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work was repeatedly framed as optional, choice-based, and free of pressure. Aisha’s ownership of the plan was clearly affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was elicited from within Aisha through open questions and amplification of her values. The resulting change talk came primarily from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor clearly positioned Aisha as the expert on her own life and needs. Her contributions determined the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Aisha’s own change talk was consistently elicited, deepened, and reinforced. The counselor strengthened the client’s motivation rather than imposing an agenda.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Limited sustain talk was handled well by respecting boundaries and reframing guilt toward self-compassion. A richer use of double-sided reflections would further soften ambivalence.
