# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Jason's perspective without moralizing. It could be warmer by more explicitly affirming Jason's worth beyond the behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Jason's ambivalence between social connection and avoiding consequences. Deeper attention to underlying emotions such as fear of being labeled would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session moves logically from fairness concerns to health risks and concrete in-the-moment strategies. A clearer co-created behavioral target, such as reducing blackout risk, would make the direction more strategic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds effectively on Jason's own ideas, including pacing, non-boozy drinks, and a supportive friend. More explicit joint decision-making language would strengthen the collaborative quality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Jason's choice to continue drinking is respected without pressure, while the conversation stays focused on safer boundaries. This is one of the strongest MI elements in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, client-centered questions help Jason articulate his own values, reasons, and strategies. Deeper questions about future self or importance of avoiding labels could further strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jason is positioned as the expert and invited to define what a fair path and fair next step would look like. More explicit agenda negotiation would make the partnership feel more co-owned.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits concrete change talk about avoiding blackouts, protecting health, and staying connected with friends. More deliberate summarizing and amplification of change talk would enhance its impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Jason's minimization or his statement that he will not stop drinking. Stronger double-sided reflections could better hold both sides of his ambivalence.
