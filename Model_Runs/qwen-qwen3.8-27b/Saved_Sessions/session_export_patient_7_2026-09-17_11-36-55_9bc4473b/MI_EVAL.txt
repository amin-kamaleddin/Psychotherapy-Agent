# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no moments of judgment or labeling. Amina's minimal responses are validated as sufficient, reinforcing a safe, non-judgmental space.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy throughout, with sophisticated reflections that add emotional weight and meaning to Amina's minimal responses (e.g., reframing 'a normal day' as something she shouldn't have to ask for). The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates understanding of Amina's underlying experience rather than just her surface words.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and appropriate focus on initial engagement and rapport-building without wandering into unrelated topics. A slightly clearer articulation of what 'success' looks like for the session in Amina's own terms would have strengthened the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a joint exploration rather than an interview, with questions framed as invitations and Amina's contributions genuinely shaping the direction. The power dynamic is consistently flattened, with the psychiatrist following Amina's lead rather than directing the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout, explicitly validating Amina's right not to perform or say the 'right' thing. The psychiatrist repeatedly reinforces that Amina controls the pace and content of the interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently draws out Amina's own motivations, preferences, and boundaries rather than telling her what to think. However, the evocation remains primarily at the relational level (what makes this space tolerable) rather than exploring deeper values or connecting to the presenting concern.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside with Amina, using language like 'you set the speed' and 'no wrong answer.' The psychiatrist never positions themselves as the expert on Amina's life or needs, and accepts her corrections without defensiveness.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes change talk appropriate for the precontemplation stage, including Amina articulating her own boundaries and preferences. A slightly more explicit reflection and amplification of Amina's shift from refusal to conditional openness would have pushed this to a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Resistance is met without defensiveness, argumentation, or subtle counter-persuasion. The psychiatrist consistently validates the underlying need while not reinforcing surface resistance, using textbook softening techniques such as fully conceding the point before gently redirecting.
