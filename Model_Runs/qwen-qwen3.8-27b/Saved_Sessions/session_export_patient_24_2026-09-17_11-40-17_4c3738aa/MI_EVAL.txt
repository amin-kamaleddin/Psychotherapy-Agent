### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and genuine unconditional positive regard throughout. The psychiatrist never judges or labels Daniel, and validation feels individualized rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are masterful, capturing both behavioral patterns and underlying emotional meaning (e.g., 'that bridge has a beer in it'). Attunement is progressively deeper and emotionally resonant without being pushy.

### Direction

**Comments:** Clear focus on alcohol use with a logical arc from acknowledgment to self-generated insight. However, the session ends mid-exploration without converging on a specific, actionable next step or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session direction is clearly shaped by Daniel's responses at every stage. The psychiatrist asks for input before offering perspective and builds on Daniel's own language as the foundation for next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every suggestion is framed as an invitation with explicit pressure removal (e.g., 'not a decision, just…what would be your first instinct?'). Daniel's right to say no is consistently respected without any directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniel arrives at the key insight ('I need something to do') entirely through his own thinking, guided by skilled open questions. No external reasons, statistics, or directives are ever introduced.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly credits Daniel as the expert on his own life and positions themselves as a curious guide rather than an authority. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniel's change talk evolves significantly from minimization to self-generated solutions through strategic, curiosity-driven questions. The psychiatrist explicitly reinforces and names Daniel's own progress ('You've actually already done the hard part').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never met with argument or dismissal; instead it is consistently redirected through curiosity into deeper self-exploration. Daniel's firm line at the nightly beer becomes a doorway rather than a wall.
