# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating toward Elena’s perspective. It falls short of a 5 because the warmth remains somewhat cognitive and does not fully affirm her as a good parent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows good reflective listening by accurately capturing Elena’s core tension between risk, fear, and protection. It is limited to mostly cognitive empathy rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally remains focused on the vaccination concern but is inconsistent in moving toward a decision. It becomes overly process-focused rather than guiding Elena toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses joint language and follows Elena’s lead in structuring the discussion. It is not a 5 because the counselor still designs the frameworks rather than fully co-creating the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports autonomy by avoiding pressure and repeatedly asking what would feel fair, safe, or useful to Elena. It could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her right to choose vaccination or not.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks questions that draw out Elena’s preferences and reasoning, but the evocation is mostly informational. It does not consistently elicit deeper values, protective motives, or personal reasons for vaccination.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Elena as an active decision-maker and lets her preferences shape the conversation. It is not a 5 because the counselor still leads the structure and does not fully position Elena as the expert on her own values.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some attempts to evoke change talk, especially around protecting her child and being careful. The counselor does not consistently develop or amplify this change talk into a stronger motivation for vaccination.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena repeatedly minimizes risk and expresses hesitation, and the psychiatrist does not effectively redirect or soften this sustain talk. The session remains anchored in low-risk framing rather than helping resolve ambivalence.
