# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and offers frequent affirmations and safety checks. However, the warmth remains surface-level and does not fully acknowledge the emotional weight of Dante's ambivalence or frustration.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses normalizing and clarifying questions to understand Dante's perspective. However, reflections stay largely factual rather than deeply mirroring the underlying fatigue, discouragement, or fear of overcommitment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session begins with the referral goal of exercise but quickly shifts to sleep hygiene without reconnecting sleep improvement to the broader exercise goal. The focus stays practical but lacks strategic coherence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites client input and incorporates some of Dante's ideas into the plan. However, the psychiatrist remains the primary architect of the plan rather than an equal partner in co-creating it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and respects Dante's decisions without pressure. However, the detailed, prescriptive nature of the advice limits the client's sense of ownership and self-directed problem solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes some evocative moves, especially by asking what previously helped with workouts. However, evocation is not sustained and is quickly replaced by directive planning rather than further exploration of the client's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative moments, as the psychiatrist asks for preferences and uses some of Dante's own ideas. However, the dynamic remains more advisor-client than equal partners, with the psychiatrist generating most of the plan structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** One strong question elicited rich change talk about Dante's past routine and social support. However, the psychiatrist did not follow up to amplify or expand this change talk before shifting into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Dante's sustain talk and ambivalence are acknowledged in passing but not effectively worked through. The psychiatrist does not use double-sided reflections or help Dante resolve the tension around overcommitment and difficulty.
