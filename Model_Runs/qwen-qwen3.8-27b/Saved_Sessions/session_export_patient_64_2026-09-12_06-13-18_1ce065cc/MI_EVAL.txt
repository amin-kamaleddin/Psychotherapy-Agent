# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, repeatedly affirming Jordan's right to decide. Positive regard is strong but somewhat procedural rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Jordan's ambivalence and concerns. They are more content-focused and could better name underlying feelings such as guilt, fear, or fear of feeling foolish.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on the flu vaccine decision and related barriers. Direction is sometimes diluted by information-giving and practical options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses menu options and asks for Jordan's preferences, supporting shared decision-making. Collaboration is occasionally limited by a more expert-driven information role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jordan's freedom to choose is repeatedly respected, including declining to book or schedule check-ins. A few suggestions lean slightly toward steering but overall are noncoercive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some questions invite consideration of change, such as what would need to change and what small step feels doable. Evocation is limited because it is often followed by information rather than deeper elicitation of Jordan's values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and treats the decision as Jordan's. Partnership is uneven because the counselor frequently supplies facts, options, and a middle path.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jordan generates some change talk around protecting grandparents and small protective steps. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or build on that language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is mostly acknowledged without argument or judgment. However, frequent factual reassurance and effectiveness data can reinforce certainty-seeking rather than soften sustain talk.
