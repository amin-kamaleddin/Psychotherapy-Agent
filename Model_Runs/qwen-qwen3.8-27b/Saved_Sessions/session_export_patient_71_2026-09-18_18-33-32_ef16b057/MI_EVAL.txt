# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no instances of labeling or condescension. Laura's corrections and self-awareness are met with warmth and immediate validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond surface-level paraphrasing to capture emotional nuance and underlying meaning, progressively deepening from content to feeling to meaning level. The psychiatrist checks accuracy throughout, demonstrating genuine commitment to representing Laura's perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with logical progression from values-behavior gap to a concrete, client-generated action step. Could have more explicitly tied the final step back to the broader values framework for stronger closure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session feels like a conversation between equals, with Laura's ideas substantially shaping the direction. Occasionally the psychiatrist offers interpretations that take a slightly leading position rather than purely co-creating the next move.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout — never prescribes solutions, never uses directive language, and consistently invites Laura to design her own answers. The entire session is structured around eliciting Laura's own choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out Laura's own reasons for change and internal resources without providing external justifications. Could be more systematic in developing confidence and commitment language, such as using a confidence ruler or amplifying existing change talk more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Laura as the expert on her own life through open questions and validation of her pushback. A few moments where the psychiatrist offers interpretive hypotheses could be framed more tentatively to reinforce the power balance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits multiple instances of change talk through strategic questions that lead Laura to discover her own solutions. Could more explicitly amplify and deepen change talk when it emerges, such as building on her confidence statements with targeted follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by normalizing barriers and validating difficulty without arguing or dismissing. The session ends with Laura expressing doubt that the psychiatrist does not get to respond to, and sustain talk could be more explicitly acknowledged as a legitimate challenge before being reframed.
