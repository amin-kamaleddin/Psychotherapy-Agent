# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, encouraging, and nonjudgmental tone. Acceptance is somewhat tied to the client's practical plan rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurately reflects practical concerns and confidence shifts. Empathy is more cognitive/problem-focused than emotionally deep about discouragement and past failures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly focused on a concrete physical-activity behavior and implementation. The session remains efficient and avoids broad dieting or lifestyle drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is jointly constructed with client-generated cues, backups, and follow-up. The clinician acts as a facilitator rather than an instructor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices and invites the client to set size, timing, and follow-up. The client retains ownership without being pressured into a prescribed regimen.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits implementation-focused motivation, confidence, and commitment. It could go deeper into values, meaning, and identity-based change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the client as the expert on what is realistic. The interaction is collaborative, equal, and client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open, scaling, and implementation questions generate substantial client-driven change talk. Affirmations reinforce concrete plans, confidence, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Gently reflects barriers and redirects toward manageable alternatives without arguing. Some fixed or defeatist statements, such as being stuck, are not fully reframed.
