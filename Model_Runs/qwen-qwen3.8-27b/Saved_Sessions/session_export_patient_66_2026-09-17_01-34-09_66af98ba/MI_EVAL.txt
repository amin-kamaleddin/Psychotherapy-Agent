# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, non-judgmental tone with clear positive regard. Occasional positive assumptions make the acceptance feel slightly less unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures the client’s frustration and value of the daily pause. Empathy is strong but could explore deeper emotional meanings of smoking more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the smoke-free policy and narrows the goal to daytime non-smoking with a concrete Friday marker. The final plan could be tightened with more specific action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves as a partner and builds directly on the client’s own ideas rather than prescribing solutions. The interaction is genuinely collaborative and client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice, ownership, and freedom from pressure are consistently reinforced. The clinician also respects the client’s right to keep potential slips private.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client’s own reasons for change are effectively elicited, including being tired of the hassle and wanting control over the day. There are minor moments of leading before the client fully generates the idea.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and explicit statements place the client as the expert on their own journey. The client’s ideas clearly guide the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session evokes ability, desire, and tentative commitment statements from the client. The change talk is strong but could be deepened with more systematic MI strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing, moralizing, or pressure. It is mostly accepted rather than actively reframed with double-sided reflections.
