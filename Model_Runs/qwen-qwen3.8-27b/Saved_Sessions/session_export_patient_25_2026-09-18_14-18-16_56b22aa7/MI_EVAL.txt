# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm unconditional positive regard throughout with no instances of judgment. Opens by offering safety ('this space to be whatever you need') and frames concerns in terms of Daniel's own values rather than moral failings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections that capture emotional texture beyond surface content. Notable examples include naming the social dynamic of being 'the only one saying no thanks' and reflecting back Daniel's own 'gliding past' metaphor in a deepened way.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from awareness to strategy to commitment to self-monitoring, maintaining focus on alcohol in social contexts throughout. Minor deduction for absence of a brief consolidation summary and slightly tangential philosophical ending on 'attention' vs. 'battle.'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as genuine joint exploration where Daniel drives content and direction. Psychiatrist consistently builds on Daniel's ideas (e.g., 'just a couple tonight') rather than offering alternatives or prescriptions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent autonomy support with no directive language ('should,' 'need to'). The Friday plan is framed as an experiment ('try that once... and see what happens'), and Daniel's self-assessment of 'not in crisis' is honored without pressure to escalate urgency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Entire motivational arc (ambivalence → rule identification → strategy → commitment → self-monitoring) generated by Daniel through evocative questioning. No external reasons for change, medical facts, or statistics provided—all reasoning is Daniel's own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise and wisdom consistently positioned within Daniel ('What's your honest read?', 'What would a rule that feels yours look like?'). Every intervention is a reflection, question, or affirmation of Daniel's own thinking—never information or advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically timed questions elicit rich change talk across desire, ability, reason, and commitment domains. Skillfully reflects and amplifies Daniel's own words ('That's not just a rule, that's a strategy'), reinforcing existing competence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mild sustain talk (concern about sounding 'weird' or 'the guy who's got a problem') is gently reframed to Daniel's own values without being amplified. The session's low-resistance nature limits opportunity for more sophisticated techniques like a double-sided reflection, but the sustain talk is appropriately contained.
