# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally respects and validates Brandon, but the stance is task-focused rather than warmly individualized. It avoids explicit judgment but does not consistently express unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures practical ambivalence and conflict, but often stays at the behavioral level. Deeper emotions such as shame, fear, fatigue, and bodily anxiety are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifts from alcohol use to boundary-setting and argument management. A key question about whether the plan will reduce drinking is not used to re-center on the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Brandon participates actively, chooses options, and co-creates the role-play and weekly check-in. The main weakness is that the psychiatrist controls much of the content and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choices and avoids pressure to stop drinking, but repeatedly supplies scripts and behavioral strategies before deeply exploring Brandon’s own preferences. Autonomy support is present but mixed with direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early questions show basic evocation, but the session quickly shifts to advice, scripts, and rehearsal. It misses key opportunities to draw out Brandon’s own reasons, values, and motivation regarding alcohol.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is genuine participation and some power sharing, but the psychiatrist often acts as an expert coach rather than an equal partner. Brandon’s own perspective does not consistently drive the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not effectively elicit or amplify change talk about reducing alcohol. Emerging ambivalence is mostly neutralized into boundary scripts rather than expanded into reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids argument but leaves key sustain talk intact, such as beer as decompression and not choosing beer over his wife. It does not gently explore costs, values, or alternatives while preserving the client’s stated needs.
