# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout the session. The psychiatrist never minimizes Camila's concerns and validates her ambivalence and pushback with genuine acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly nuanced and emotionally attuned, capturing self-protective language, paradoxes, and contradictions beyond surface-level paraphrasing. Notable examples include reflecting on the weight of 'I guess' and accurately reframing the spotlight metaphor.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, strategic progression from establishing context through pattern exploration to a concrete action plan. No wandering or off-topic drift; each question logically builds on the previous response.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is clearly co-constructed around Camila's ideas, with the psychiatrist acting as a curious facilitator rather than a director. Camila names the escalation pattern, identifies the critical moment, and proposes the 20-minute rule that anchors the session's conclusion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Camila's ownership of the change process without ever prescribing or imposing a course of action. Every suggestion is framed as a question or invitation, and the psychiatrist explicitly acknowledges that total abstinence is not required.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is elicited entirely from within Camila through open-ended questions and amplification of her own insights. The psychiatrist never provides external evidence or lectures, and the session concludes with a self-generated action plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently placed within Camila, with questions inviting self-interpretation rather than providing answers. Genuine power-sharing is demonstrated when the psychiatrist immediately concedes to Camila's pushback on the 'hardest part' framing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently evokes and amplifies change talk by reflecting and affirming Camila's own insights about her competence and self-awareness. The session culminates in a significant piece of change talk as Camila proposes a concrete, doable action step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it but misses opportunities to fully explore the most potent sustain talk—the social identity fear of being 'that person who's doing better.' Camila's final doubt ('Is that even a win?') also goes unaddressed as the session ends.
