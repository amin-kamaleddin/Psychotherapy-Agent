# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone. Elena’s concerns, hesitancy, and requests for plain language were validated rather than corrected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor showed strong empathic understanding of Elena’s responsibilities, fear of making a mistake, and need for clear information. However, reflections sometimes stayed practical and did not consistently reach the deeper emotional core of her ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stayed focused on vaccination decision-making, side effects, and practical safety planning. Direction could be strengthened by more explicitly linking information and logistics back to motivational readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently treated Elena as a collaborative partner in shaping the conversation and planning. Elena’s preferences clearly guided the agenda, source selection, organization of information, and monitoring checklist.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor strongly supported Elena’s right to choose without pressure, coercion, or persuasion. She invited Elena to define what felt safe, important, and ready for her family.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor used open-ended questions to draw out Elena’s concerns and preferences, but evocation was not consistently focused on her internal motivation for vaccination. There was limited use of stronger motivational questions that would deepen change talk or connect values to readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor clearly positioned Elena as the expert in her child, her values, and her decision-making process. Power sharing was strong, and Elena’s contributions actively shaped the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena produced some change-oriented language, especially near the end about being ready and considering vaccination. The counselor made efforts to invite her perspective but did not consistently amplify or systematically develop her change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledged Elena’s sustaining concerns without arguing or dismissing them. However, she did not consistently use double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to soften sustain talk and move it toward ambivalence resolution.
