# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional and consistent unconditional positive regard throughout the entire session. Key phrases like 'this space is yours' and 'I want to honor that' convey non-judgmental acceptance of Elena's ambivalence and emotional state.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflective listening that captures not just content but emotional undertones and distinct layers of Elena's experience. The psychiatrist distinguishes between procedural anxiety and identity/future uncertainty, reflecting both with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a generally clear focus on contraception decision-making with a logical conversational arc. However, the session drifts in the later portion into general discussion about ideal follow-up and ends open-endedly without tying back to a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a joint exploration rather than a clinician-led interview, with consistent invitations for Elena's input and her ideas driving the conversation. The psychiatrist models vulnerability ('What you just said really landed for me') and explicitly communicates equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly reinforces Elena's autonomy and right to choose throughout the session. Phrases like 'you're in the driver's seat' and 'not yet is a totally valid answer' communicate that the decision belongs entirely to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation throughout — the psychiatrist consistently elicits motivation from within Elena rather than providing external reasons for change. Questions are open, directed back to her internal experience, and use her own words to deepen exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Elena, never taking the expert role or imposing solutions. When Elena mentions the IUD, the psychiatrist asks her to evaluate it against her own criteria rather than educating her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and builds on Elena's change talk multiple times, reflecting back her stated values and criteria as personal strengths. Could have gone further in evoking confidence talk and commitment language, and the session ends without a scaling question to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill — when Elena says she's not ready to commit, the psychiatrist honors it without arguing or countingering. The response validates her position while gently exploring what 'settling down' would look like, keeping the door open.
