# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard, consistently validating David's concerns without judgment. Even reluctant statements like 'I guess' are met with warmth rather than disappointment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and nuanced, capturing both surface concerns and deeper meaning such as David's identity as a provider. The psychiatrist consistently tracks subtle emotional and cognitive shifts throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, logical arc from exploring David's concerns to identifying the real barrier, defining readiness, and designing a specific plan. The psychiatrist never drifts from the target behavior while pacing appropriately to let David explore.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a joint exploration rather than a clinical interview, with decisions co-constructed rather than prescribed. Collaborative language like 'Let's set aside the doctor's urgency for a moment' establishes genuine partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces David's ownership, using questions like 'What do you think it would take for you to feel like this is a decision you're making?' Every plan element is generated by David and framed as his choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits David's own motivations, values, and solutions through evocative questioning rather than providing external justification. David moves from resistance to a self-designed plan entirely through his own reasoning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise resides within David, acting as a facilitator who reflects and asks questions that draw out David's own wisdom. No course of action is ever prescribed; all plan elements are generated by the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evocative questions like 'What would ready look like for you?' and 'What would make starting that month feel doable?' prompt David to generate his own criteria and plan. The psychiatrist skillfully amplifies and reinforces David's shift from precontemplation to a concrete action plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled respectfully and never reinforced, with gentle reframes and redirects. However, a few moments—particularly David's 'guinea pig' comment—could have benefited from more strategic double-sided reflections or explicit reframes.
