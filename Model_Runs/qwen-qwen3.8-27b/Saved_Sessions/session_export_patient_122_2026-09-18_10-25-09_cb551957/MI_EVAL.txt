# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with zero judgment and consistent validation of Sofia's emotional experience. Notably handles 'I'm broken' without rushing to fix, and affirms Sofia's self-worth independent of performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are consistently sophisticated and precisely tailored to Sofia's specific language and emotional state at each moment. Tracks emotional shifts in real time and uses empathic curiosity rather than directive questioning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the behavioral target with logical progression from exploration to concrete planning. Pacing in the middle section could be slightly more strategic to maintain momentum toward the target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as joint exploration between equals, with language like 'I'm curious' and 'does that land?' signifying genuine partnership. Sofia's ideas shape the entire direction of the session and meaning-making is co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Sofia's ownership of choices and frames insights as her own achievements rather than the psychiatrist's gifts. Never prescribes goals or sets standards; the entire plan is generated by Sofia.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within Sofia—never provides external reasons or health facts. Reflects and amplifies Sofia's own change talk and evokes ability and commitment language through strategic questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Sofia as the expert on her own priorities, needs, and solutions through open questions and collaborative language. Never offers external expertise or says 'I think you should'; every insight emerges from Sofia.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions and permission-giving phrases (e.g., 'even if it feels too small to count') effectively remove pressure and elicit concrete change language. The progression from vague desire to specific commitment ('a date with myself') was entirely driven by the psychiatrist's evocative approach.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframes several sustain talk statements (fear of goals, all-or-nothing pattern, 'small means failure') without reinforcing barriers. Could more explicitly use double-sided reflections to acknowledge lingering doubt before affirming the plan.
