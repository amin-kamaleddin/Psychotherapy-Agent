# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no judgment or condescension. Normalizes Oliver's fear, humor, and ambivalence while treating his conflict-avoidance as understandable rather than a character defect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflections that capture not just behavior but underlying cognitive patterns and self-awareness. Tracks emotional shifts with appropriate granularity, from fear to humor to self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with logical progression from ambivalence to concrete action planning. Never allows the conversation to drift, even when humor is introduced, and consistently steers toward a specific date.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation reads as a genuine partnership with inclusive language and genuinely curious, non-leading questions. The plan is co-constructed from Oliver's ideas, with the psychiatrist shaping and amplifying rather than overriding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Oliver's ownership of the decision and never pressures or suggests what he 'should' do. The final shift from 'I should' to 'I'm going to' is explicitly attributed to Oliver's own realization.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every intervention is designed to help Oliver access his own motivations rather than providing external reasons for change. Skillfully names the significance of Oliver's own statements back to him, allowing him to own the change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fosters power sharing with Oliver's contributions substantially shaping the session direction. The psychiatrist never assumes the expert role; all key ideas (breakfast setting, sticky note, date) emerge from Oliver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies Oliver's change talk, helping him see the weight of his own statements. Oliver's change talk increases in frequency and conviction across the session, from tentative to declarative.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing or arguing against it, and redirects worry toward problem-solving. Could have done more with explicit double-sided reflections or strategic reframing of the guilt as a separate voice from the change voice.
