# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and affirming stance, but acceptance can feel more like performance validation than deeper positive regard. It would benefit from warmer, more individualized acknowledgment of Camila’s mixed feelings and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is adequate at the content level, but it remains largely cognitive and plan-focused. Greater empathy would involve exploring the emotional undertones of concerns such as the “chore” worry and fear of burdening her partner.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays highly focused on the target behavior of increasing physical activity. Each exchange advances a concrete, actionable plan with strong momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician collaborates by offering options and inviting Camila to choose what fits her preferences. However, many specific alternatives and scripts are clinician-generated, which limits the extent to which her own ideas emerge first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is generally supported through choices, nonjudgmental framing, and client preference-setting. Some language is mildly directive, and the clinician could more explicitly emphasize Camila’s control over the plan and any adjustments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes internal motivation through values, confidence scaling, and rehearsal. The main limitation is supplying specific scripts and language before allowing Camila to generate her own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session conveys that Camila is the expert on her own life, and her preferences meaningfully shape the plan. The partnership is less fully client-led because the clinician frequently introduces concrete options and scripted language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is consistently elicited and strengthened, with Camila expressing values, confidence, and intention. The use of specific scripts and options slightly limits the purity of evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled reasonably well through normalization, flexibility, and gentle reframing. More active ambivalence-resolution techniques, such as double-sided reflections, would further strengthen this area.
