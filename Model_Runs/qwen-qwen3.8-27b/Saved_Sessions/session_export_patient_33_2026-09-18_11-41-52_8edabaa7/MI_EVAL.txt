# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates a nonjudgmental, warm, and affirming stance with multiple examples of positive regard and normalization. Could go deeper in normalizing the heroin use itself and the ambivalence that accompanies it.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, capturing identity conflict and emotional weight. Tends to move slightly ahead of Diego's emotional processing, reframing uncertainty as engagement before fully sitting with the fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical progression from initial concerns through concrete harm reduction planning without drifting. Could benefit from more explicit midpoint summaries and a clearer target behavior defined at the outset.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative behavior, consistently building on Diego's ideas and treating him as an equal partner. The dynamic remains facilitator-driven rather than genuine co-creation, with the psychiatrist doing more of the structural work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Diego's autonomy throughout without any lapses into directive language or implied obligation. All decisions are made by Diego, with the psychiatrist's role purely facilitative.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-crafted questions consistently draw out Diego's own motivations, ideas, and solutions rather than providing them. Could be strengthened by more explicit amplification of change talk and tools like a confidence ruler to consolidate self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-hierarchical stance with questions that position Diego as the expert on his own life. Questions are somewhat leading, subtly positioning the psychiatrist as holding the strategic map rather than genuinely co-exploring.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions effectively elicit substantial change language across ability, reason, plan, and commitment dimensions. Could more explicitly reflect back and amplify the strongest change talk statements to consolidate them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is minimal in this session, making the criterion less extensively tested. The limited sustain content is handled appropriately without reinforcement, with gentle redirection toward practical safety planning.
