# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a generally nonjudgmental stance and affirms the patient's right to choose, but repeated safety checks and procedural prompts make the interaction feel less consistently warm and person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflects the patient's frustration and intrusive feelings, but empathy often remains surface-level and is interrupted by options or advice rather than deeper exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays connected to the device issue, but it drifts into practical problem-solving and lacks a clear, collaboratively clarified focus for change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and accepts the patient's decisions, but the session is often driven by counselor-generated options rather than fully shared agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supports the patient's freedom to choose and avoids overt pressure, although the large number of suggestions can still feel mildly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Asks a few exploratory questions, but mostly offers external suggestions rather than consistently eliciting the patient's values, concerns, or internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites preferences and respects refusals, yet frequently supplies expert-generated menus that keep the patient from being the sole expert on his preferred direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited effort to draw out the patient's own reasons or confidence for change; small openings, such as trying the cloth, are not amplified into stronger change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Acknowledges resistance without arguing, but rarely redirects sustain talk or helps the patient consider ambivalence in a way that moves the conversation toward change.
