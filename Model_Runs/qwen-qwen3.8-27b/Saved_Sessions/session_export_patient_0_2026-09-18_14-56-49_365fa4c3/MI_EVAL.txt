# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, non-judgmental tone and sets a strong foundation with clear statements of respect. The warmth remains somewhat general and formulaic rather than deeply individualized, missing opportunities to affirm Adrian's courage in being honest.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Demonstrates adequate reflective listening but tends to paraphrase rather than explore the emotional subtext behind Adrian's statements. Misses the underlying fear of being overwhelmed, shame, and vulnerability present in his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Significant failure to maintain focus on the target behavior (methamphetamine use), which was the reason for the MI referral. The session drifts to sleep hygiene and general infection recovery, leaving the core issue largely unaddressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and positions as a partner, but the session's direction follows a therapist-driven trajectory rather than being genuinely shaped by Adrian's contributions. True co-construction of agenda and focus is limited in practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Adrian's right to choose and avoids directive language or imposed conditions. Tends toward removing pressure rather than actively reinforcing Adrian's capacity, agency, and ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Critical weakness: excessive accommodation ('you don't have to figure it out today') forecloses the opportunity to elicit Adrian's own motivations for change. Adrian leaves with unexamined ambivalence ('I'll think about it. I guess.') rather than meaningful change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and consistently positions as a supporter rather than authority. The partnership is present in tone but not fully realized in structural dynamics, as the session's direction is largely therapist-set.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Missed the critical moment when Adrian acknowledged weekend use by closing the door instead of exploring it with amplification or evocative questions. No exploration of desire, ability, need, or reasons for change was attempted.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids reinforcing sustain talk but also fails to actively redirect using double-sided reflections or gentle pivots. Remains too neutral — accepting minimization without strategically steering toward change.
