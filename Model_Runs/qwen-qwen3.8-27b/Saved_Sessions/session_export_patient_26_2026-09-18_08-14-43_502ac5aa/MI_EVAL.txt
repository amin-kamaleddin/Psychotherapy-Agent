### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses joint problem-solving language and allows Daniela's ideas to shape the session direction. Decisions are co-constructed rather than prescribed, and the session flows organically from the patient's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never prescribes and consistently frames choices as Daniela's own, respecting her pace and definition of progress. Language of ownership and permission (e.g., 'permission to be a human instead of a project') is used throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits motivation from within Daniela by asking her to articulate her own values, reasons, and solutions. This high-level evocation effectively moves Daniela from contemplation toward preparation and her own plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly positions Daniela as the expert on her own life and maintains consistently egalitarian power dynamics. Daniela's contributions clearly shape the direction of the session rather than following a clinician-driven structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently and skillfully evokes and reinforces Daniela's change talk, amplifying her own ideas and definitions of progress. The score is slightly limited by a missed opportunity to use a confidence ruler or more explicitly elicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without judgment and redirected toward Daniela's own resolutions rather than being reinforced. The score is limited by a tendency to acknowledge and redirect rather than using the full range of techniques, such as double-sided reflections.
