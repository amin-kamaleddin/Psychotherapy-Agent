### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Elias’s concerns. Acceptance could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming his worth, courage, and effort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both practical and emotional dimensions of Elias’s ambivalence, including helpful double-sided reflections. Deeper emotional meaning, such as fear of being a burden or failure, could be explored more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and moves logically toward a specific, realistic plan. The medical adherence thread mentioned early in the session is not revisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is strongly collaborative, with clear joint language and a plan built from Elias’s and his wife’s ideas. The session is driven by the client’s responses rather than a pre-made clinician agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions, avoids directives, and centers Elias and his wife as decision-makers. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Elias’s freedom not to try or to choose his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes client-generated solutions, signals, and problem-solving. Broader values, meaning, confidence, and self-efficacy could be evoked more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a collaborative stance and positions Elias as the expert on his own body and life. Partnership would be stronger if Elias were more explicitly invited to set the direction before rehearsal began.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias is encouraged to generate his own change talk involving desire, ability, and planning. The counselor could more systematically elicit broader change talk categories such as importance, confidence, commitment, or identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist carefully handles sustain talk without challenging, minimizing, or arguing with Elias’s concerns. Some reframing is subtly leading and could hold ambivalence more explicitly by exploring both sides.
