# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and used supportive language without pressuring Andrei about alcohol. Warmth was somewhat formulaic and task-focused rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and content-level, but mostly factual. The psychiatrist missed opportunities to reflect deeper emotion or meaning around Andrei’s evening drinking and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on blood pressure management and a concrete two-week self-monitoring/walking plan. Alcohol was not consistently integrated as a connected target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist engaged Andrei in collaborative problem-solving by offering options and letting him choose key plan elements. The collaboration was strong but remained more “choose from my options” than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was consistently supported, including explicit respect for Andrei’s decision to keep alcohol as his choice. The fairly prescriptive clinical plan and multiple initial questions slightly reduced the client-led feel.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used some evocative strategies, such as scaling readiness and confidence. However, much of the session relied on clinician-supplied options and education rather than eliciting Andrei’s own motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invited input, and Andrei’s preferences shaped the plan. The interaction remained strongly expert-driven, limiting a fully equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evoked some readiness and confidence statements, including plans to start the cuff and walks. Change talk was not consistently amplified, deepened, or extended to alcohol.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing against Andrei’s sustain talk and respected his autonomy around alcohol. However, the session did not actively soften sustain talk through double-sided reflections or values-based pivots.
