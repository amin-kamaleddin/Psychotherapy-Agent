# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalized Aisha's constraints and private, low-key goal. Warmth was somewhat procedural and did not fully affirm Aisha's deeper values or dignity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections captured role strain, privacy concerns, and practical constraints, but stopped at content level. It did not consistently name underlying fatigue, shame-avoidance, or maternal values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on reducing evening smoking and moved clearly toward a concrete plan. Logistics, follow-up timing, and check-ins remained tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed through Aisha's choices about gum, wallpaper, location, follow-up format, and timing. However, the psychiatrist frequently supplied prepackaged options and guided the sequence.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Aisha's preferences were respected and she selected actionable steps, including a private plan and two-week phone check-in. The psychiatrist could have more explicitly affirmed her autonomy, ownership, and right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling and practical questions helped Aisha define goals and increase confidence, but deeper values and motivations were rarely evoked. Statements like 'For You, Breathe' were not fully explored for their meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for input and incorporated Aisha's choices, but also acted as an active planner and advisor. Balance of power tilted toward the clinician rather than positioning Aisha as the expert on her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session elicited practical action talk and increased confidence from 5 to 7, including a meaningful value-linked wallpaper idea. It did not consistently amplify, reflect, or deepen change talk around desire, importance, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Aisha's sustain talk and preserved rapport by accepting a private reduction goal. It did not actively use double-sided reflections or otherwise turn sustain talk into change talk.
