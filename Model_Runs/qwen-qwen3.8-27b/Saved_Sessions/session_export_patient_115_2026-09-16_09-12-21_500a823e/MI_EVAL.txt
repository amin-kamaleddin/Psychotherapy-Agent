# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone throughout the session. Some affirmations are generic, and continued offers after stated boundaries slightly reduce full acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician reflects practical barriers and concerns accurately, showing reasonable understanding. However, the reflections remain surface-level and do not consistently explore deeper emotions, values, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear, actionable target behavior with specific next steps and follow-up. This is one of the strongest MI domains in the evaluation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites client input and adapts the plan based on feedback, reflecting genuine collaboration. The approach remains somewhat counselor-guided by offering multiple specific solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician frequently supports Samir’s sense of choice and respects his preferences. Mild directive moments, such as proposing exact reminder wording and scripts, slightly limit autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses readiness and confidence rulers to elicit some client-owned motivation. Evocation remains limited in depth, with early practical solutions reducing opportunity to explore internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shares power by inviting Samir to choose priorities, preferences, and plan details. At times, the interaction shifts toward counselor-generated options rather than fully client-led planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are several attempts to evoke change talk through questions and rulers, and Samir expresses some motivation. The follow-up questions are limited, and the session often moves quickly into practical problem-solving.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing with barriers and frames the plan as realistic and doable. The softening is not consistently skillful, as sustain talk is not fully explored or reflected from both sides.
