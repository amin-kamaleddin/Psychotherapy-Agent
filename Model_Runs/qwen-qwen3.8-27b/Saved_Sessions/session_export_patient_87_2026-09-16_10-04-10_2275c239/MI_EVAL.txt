# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout the interaction. Warmth is strong but often tied to Marco’s plan or performance rather than being explicitly unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses reflections and summaries to understand Marco’s perspective. However, many responses remain cognitive and do not deeply reflect underlying fear, shame, self-identity concerns, or all-or-nothing thinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on a concrete, achievable post-dinner walk. The psychiatrist consistently guides Marco toward specific actions, times, cues, and fallbacks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds the plan using Marco’s own ideas and language, such as the fruit swap, shoe cue, and DND. The interaction is collaborative, though the counselor still controls much of the agenda and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking Marco to decide among options and emphasizing his preferences. Autonomy support could be stronger with more explicit language about his freedom to choose differently or postpone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks useful evocative questions about what matters most and what would increase confidence. However, much of the session relies on closed questions and planning rather than deeply drawing out Marco’s values, hopes, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally collaborative stance and lets Marco’s ideas shape the plan. The counseling remains somewhat structured and counselor-led, limiting the impression of equal co-ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco offers change talk about his kids, a realistic plan, and increased confidence. The psychiatrist affirms this well but does not consistently deepen or expand desire, ability, reasons, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with barriers and redirects toward a manageable plan. However, sustain talk is often moved past with problem-solving rather than skillful double-sided reflections or normalization of ambivalence.
