# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, non-judgmental tone with strong individualized affirmations (e.g., smoking history). Warmth occasionally reads slightly strategic, and lacks a moment of explicit unconditional positive regard that would elevate to a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, validating Thomas's minimization without judgment. Reflections stay at the cognitive/factual level rather than exploring the emotional undercurrent beneath his statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Allows the session to drift across alcohol, sleep, and portions without consistent strategic summarizing. The conversation is patient-led and responsive but lacks tight focus toward a clearly defined endpoint.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Structures the session as a shared conversation, with Thomas's responses directly shaping direction and genuine co-construction of language. Could be deepened with more explicit invitations for Thomas to evaluate the process and negotiate goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout — explicitly removes pressure, never imposes or invokes external authority, and ensures the entire plan is Thomas's own. The psychiatrist simply helps him articulate what he had already decided for himself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Thomas's own motivations, resources, and reasoning through open questions. Occasionally steps into an interpretive role with mildly leading statements that could be replaced with more open evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as collaborative, avoids expert-driven language, and lets Thomas's contributions shape the direction. Could be strengthened by more explicit acknowledgment of Thomas as the expert on his own life and more visible co-creation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk and uses appropriately open questions, but does not consistently reinforce or amplify the change talk that emerges. Misses opportunities to deepen Thomas's personal language about why the change matters to him.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well without arguing or countering, with excellent corrections that honor Thomas's reality. One moment of defensiveness could have been more skillfully navigated with a double-sided reflection that explicitly acknowledged both sides.
