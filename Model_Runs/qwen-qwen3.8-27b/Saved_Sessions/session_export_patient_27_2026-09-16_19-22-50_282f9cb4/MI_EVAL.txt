# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and refrains from blaming Daniela for her secrecy. The session is warm and validating, though it could more explicitly affirm Daniela’s broader worth, courage, or integrity beyond the specific disclosure issue.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, accurately tracking Daniela’s limits, fear of stigma, and strategy of using texting to manage anxiety. Empathy could be deeper by more directly reflecting the emotional intensity and bodily distress behind her statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on peer disclosure about diabetes and safe communication about her condition. Each question moves purposefully from broad concern to concrete, actionable steps such as choosing one friend, setting boundaries, and drafting a low-pressure text.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist allows Daniela’s ideas to shape the session, working within her preferred frame of telling only one friend and using texting. The interaction is clearly joint, with Daniela deciding the direction and the psychiatrist adapting to her lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniela’s ownership, choice, and self-determination are strongly reinforced throughout, including statements such as “Your ‘I’m done’ line stays yours either way.” The psychiatrist avoids pressuring disclosure and emphasizes Daniela’s control over how and whether she proceeds.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist largely evokes Daniela’s own goals, scripts, and preferred methods rather than persuading or directing the content. Evocation could be strengthened by more open exploration of deeper values or personal meaning, rather than narrower practical planning questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adopts a collaborative stance and repeatedly invites Daniela to identify her own solutions and boundaries. Partnership could be further strengthened with more explicit power-sharing language and framing Daniela as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions effectively elicit Daniela’s own change talk and concrete disclosure plans, and the psychiatrist affirms her emerging scripts and agency. Cultivating change talk could be deeper by explicitly strengthening confidence, scaling, or amplifying her reasons for wanting to disclose.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles Daniela’s desire to stay private well by reframing it as protective rather than oppositional and by avoiding argument. Softening sustain talk could be more deliberate through double-sided reflections that explicitly hold both her need for safety and her openness to sharing a little.
