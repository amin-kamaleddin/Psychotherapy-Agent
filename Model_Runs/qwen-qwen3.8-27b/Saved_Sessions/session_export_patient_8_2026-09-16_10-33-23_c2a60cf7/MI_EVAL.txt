# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates warmth, respect, and nonjudgment while normalizing Amina’s ambivalence. Minor deduction because some affirmations reinforce change-oriented behavior rather than unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and helpful for key concerns such as doom-scrolling, sleep disruption, and fear of a high screen-time number. Deeper exploration of shame, fear of failure, and ambivalence would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing problematic social media use and protecting sleep and daytime focus. The therapist consistently moves the conversation toward a specific, testable behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina actively co-creates the plan by choosing tracking, voice-to-text, the drawer, and other details. The collaboration is slightly less balanced because the therapist supplies much of the plan structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, avoids commands, and allows Amina to modify the plan multiple times. Some language such as “we could try” and “good plan” remains subtly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used early, but the session quickly becomes planning-driven. More elicitation of Amina’s values, motivations, and self-generated reasons for change would strengthen this area.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist invites Amina’s input and adapts the plan to her preferences, showing genuine power sharing. The interaction is still therapist-structured because many options are offered before Amina’s own ideas are fully elicited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina expresses several change statements, especially around reducing 2 a.m. scrolling and protecting sleep. The therapist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled in an MI-consistent way by acknowledging it without arguing, moralizing, or invalidating. More double-sided reflections could further soften ambivalence while gently turning toward change.
