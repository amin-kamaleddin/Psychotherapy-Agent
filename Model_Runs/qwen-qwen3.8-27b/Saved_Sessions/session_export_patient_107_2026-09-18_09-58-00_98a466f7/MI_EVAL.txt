# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates consistent, warm unconditional positive regard throughout, never judging Nathaniel's coffee consumption. Validation is genuine and non-formulaic, even when Nathaniel expresses sustain talk.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently capture both cognitive and emotional dimensions of Nathaniel's ambivalence with high accuracy and nuance. The psychiatrist names the protective, self-preserving motivation beneath Nathaniel's stated position, and the final reflection skillfully helps him see his own growth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the coffee issue with appropriate pacing for the contemplation stage, ending with a concrete next step. A minor deduction for not being slightly more strategic in exploring discrepancy between Nathaniel's stated values and behavior during the middle portion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-created with inclusive partnership language and real-time adaptation to Nathaniel's responses. The power dynamic is truly equal, with the psychiatrist having no agenda beyond supporting Nathaniel's own process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly acknowledges Nathaniel's right to choose, never gives advice or uses 'you should' language, and consistently frames all potential changes as his own ideas. Pressure and obligation are removed, reinforcing that the decision is entirely his.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within Nathaniel through perfectly crafted evocative questions that invite his own ideas and criteria. The psychiatrist never provides a single reason for change or makes a recommendation, meeting the gold standard of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Nathaniel as the authority on his own life, never assuming the expert role or providing information about coffee and health. The session structure is entirely client-led with the psychiatrist serving as a facilitator, not a director.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nathaniel generates meaningful change talk statements that the psychiatrist consistently catches and amplifies, including the powerful final naming of his own shift. A slight deduction because evocation was somewhat restrained in the middle portion when Nathaniel was most entrenched in sustain talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Arguably the strongest aspect of the session; sustain talk is fully honored without being countered, minimized, or argued against. Double-sided reflections effectively acknowledge the sustain talk while gently opening a door without confrontation.
