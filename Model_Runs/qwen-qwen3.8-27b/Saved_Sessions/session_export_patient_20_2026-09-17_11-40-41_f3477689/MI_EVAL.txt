# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, directly addressing the client's fear of labeling. Could be slightly warmer in moments of vulnerability, such as when the client mentions his wife going quiet.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening with nuanced, progressively deepening reflections that capture the client's dual experiences and internal shifts. Reflections are accurate and sophisticated rather than formulaic, including acknowledging the difference between the safety of the session and the real world.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on alcohol use with a logical progression from exploring the problem through to implementation. The session could benefit from a clearer opening agenda and a more decisively structured closing with a concrete follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative practice with a genuinely equal power dynamic; the client is treated as the expert on his own life. The psychiatrist consistently invites input before offering reflections and co-constructs solutions rather than prescribing them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with explicit removal of pressure, consistent framing of options as possibilities rather than prescriptions, and all decisions placed entirely in the client's hands. Never pressures or suggests the client 'should' do anything.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation where all motivation emerges from the client's own mouth, with no advice, statistics, or appeals to authority. The client moves from minimization to a concrete self-generated plan without ever being told he should change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance with curious, non-hierarchical language and questions that return explanatory power to the client. Slightly implicit rather than explicitly stated, with no moment of explicit agenda negotiation or acknowledgment of the client as expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully picks up on and amplifies the client's change talk, asking questions that elicit further self-generated motivation. Could have more explicitly elicited confidence talk or commitment language to prepare for implementation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with notable skill by working with underlying values and redirecting toward compatible identities rather than challenging directly. Could have more actively used double-sided reflections to hold both sides and create additional cognitive space.
