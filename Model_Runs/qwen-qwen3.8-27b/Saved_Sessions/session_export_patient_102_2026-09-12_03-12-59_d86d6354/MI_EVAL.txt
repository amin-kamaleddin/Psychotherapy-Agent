# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates a nonjudgmental and respectful stance without moralizing. However, acceptance remains somewhat formulaic and does not fully validate Milan’s deeper values and identity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but mostly surface-level. The psychiatrist misses the deeper emotional weight of stigma, autonomy loss, and frustration with being pushed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use but lacks strategic momentum. The psychiatrist repeats similar options without creating a clear collaborative agenda or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and offers choices. However, the approach remains uneven because Milan’s stated concerns do not sufficiently shape the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** This is a relative strength, with clear noncoercive respect for Milan’s choices. It could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming his ownership over decisions about drinking.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions are used appropriately to invite Milan’s perspective. Evocation is limited because the psychiatrist shifts to provider-generated risks and does not expand Milan’s own motivational statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers a collaborative stance by inviting Milan to choose topics. Nevertheless, the agenda remains heavily driven by the provider, especially when Milan rejects the offered options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk but does not skillfully amplify it. Emerging strengths, such as self-control and school functioning, are not developed into motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Milan’s sustain talk and acknowledges it respectfully. However, the session does not effectively redirect sustain talk toward change or explore the costs of current drinking.
