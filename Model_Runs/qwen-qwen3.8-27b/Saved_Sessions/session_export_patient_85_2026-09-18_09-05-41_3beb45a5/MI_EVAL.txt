# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout. Particularly powerful when correcting Marco's self-blame ('you're not stupid') and validating the specific pain of the yo-yo cycle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that captures emotional and symbolic meaning beyond surface content, progressively deepening as the session advances. Highlights include identifying the 'endless cycle' theme Marco hadn't fully articulated and reflecting the juice as a ritual rather than just a drink.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear logical arc from awareness through ambivalence to plan to confidence without any drift or sidetracking. Focus stays tight on the juice decision and first-week strategy, with no unnecessary lectures or tangential content.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is entirely Marco's creation; the psychiatrist's role is limited to strategic questions and reflections that deepen thinking. Never adds content, gives advice, or directs the conversation—Marco's ideas consistently shape the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers genuine 'no' options, never imposes a timeline, and positions Marco as the agent of his own change at every step. Autonomy is woven into every interaction rather than stated as a separate principle.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation originates from Marco himself—no external data, statistics, or health risks are introduced. The final reframing ('cutting the worry') is a masterful evocative move that expands Marco's own insight and elicits his liberating response.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Marco as the expert on his own life, delegating the definition of success and direction to him at every point. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with the psychiatrist acting as a skilled guide rather than an authority figure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies Marco's change talk through open questions and reflective emphasis, producing substantial commitment language. Could be strengthened by systematically eliciting confidence ratings (e.g., 1-10 scaling) and exploring Marco's ability to handle obstacles beyond the first week.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validates Marco's attachment to the juice fully without arguing or minimizing, then uses a double-sided reflection to hold both truths simultaneously. Marco moves past the sustain talk organically, generating his own solution without any persuasion from the psychiatrist.
