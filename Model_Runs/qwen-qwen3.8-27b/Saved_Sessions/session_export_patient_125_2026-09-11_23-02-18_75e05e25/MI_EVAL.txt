# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, but acceptance remains somewhat generic. It misses stronger affirmation of Tariq’s strengths and responsibility.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows understanding of frustration and family worry, but reflections stay surface-level. Empathy overidentifies with the complaint instead of exploring deeper values and fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stays focused on phone access but drifts into practical problem-solving. It lacks clear MI direction toward rule compliance, release preparation, or constructive family connection.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Collaborative language is present, but the psychiatrist dominates the agenda with scripts and options. Tariq’s contributions do not substantially shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some autonomy-supportive statements, but repeated advice-giving undermines Tariq’s ownership. The approach feels controlling rather than autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supplies solutions and scripts instead of eliciting Tariq’s own motivations. There is little use of open questions, scaling, or future-oriented evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The interaction is largely expert-driven and prescriptive. The psychiatrist repeatedly offers problem-solving options that Tariq rejects, weakening the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change-relevant statements are not sufficiently reflected or expanded. There is limited use of values exploration, future thinking, or motivation-focused questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reinforces the problem side by agreeing that the situation is unfair and terrifying. Sustain talk is not effectively softened through double-sided reflections or redirection toward agency.
