# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental but feels more functional than warmly accepting. There are few individualized affirmations of Linda’s worth beyond behavior-focused praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and recognize ambivalence, but they remain conceptual rather than emotionally deep. The psychiatrist often shifts to problem-solving instead of fully exploring Linda’s feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on tobacco use, cravings, and concrete next steps. However, it is sometimes overly tactical and less consistently guided toward Linda’s values and internal motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda influences some decisions, but the psychiatrist remains the primary source of strategies and scripts. The interaction is more clinician-led coaching than true co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites choice and avoids overt pressure, which supports autonomy well. However, directive instructions like 'try this' slightly weaken the autonomy-supportive stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes only a few evocative attempts and then quickly moves into advising. There is little systematic elicitation of Linda’s values, hopes, confidence, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses partner language and asks Linda to choose, which conveys some collaboration. Yet frequent expert guidance and planning make the stance more directive than genuinely shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited and affirmed, especially around small progress and control. The psychiatrist could better deepen and expand Linda’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and is sometimes normalized, but it is not consistently softened through MI-consistent reflection. Ambivalence about social identity and fear of failure is more addressed with coping scripts than explored.
