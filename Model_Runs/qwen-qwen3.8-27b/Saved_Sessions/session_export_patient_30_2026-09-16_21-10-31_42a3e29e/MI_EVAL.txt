# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, normalizes David’s concerns, and avoids arguing or labeling him as noncompliant. This supports a clear, nonjudgmental accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track David’s ambivalence about protecting his health while fearing weakness, fatigue, or feeling foolish. Despite minor repetition, the empathy is nuanced and responsive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on cholesterol management and moves from concern toward a concrete decision framework. It does not drift and ends with a workable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the plan rather than imposing one, integrating David’s preferences for simplicity, walking, numbers, and backup options. The interaction reflects shared expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** There is no pressure to start medication, moralizing, or overriding David’s skepticism. He is supported in defining his own decision rules and right to stop if side effects occur.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions effectively draw out David’s own standards, safety concerns, and practical plan. It could go further by eliciting deeper personal values and confidence behind change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions David as the expert on his daily life and repeatedly invites him to define what is doable and fair. The session direction is clearly shaped by David’s contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** David expresses several reasons for change, including walking, simple swaps, and conditional commitment if numbers stay high. The psychiatrist could more consistently amplify deeper reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Concerns against medication are acknowledged and integrated rather than countered, then redirected into a fair trial and backup plan. This reduces defensiveness and turns sustain talk into a workable process.
