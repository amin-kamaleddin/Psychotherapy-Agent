# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental, but the warmth is generic and shifts quickly to advice and task-setting. This limits deeper affirmation of the client's strengths and courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses basic reflections and normalizes the client's experience. However, it does not fully explore the emotional undertones, ambivalence, or fear with complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, specific, and measurable target behavior with a concrete follow-up time. It could better connect the small task to the client's broader concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client contributes plan details, but the counselor controls the agenda, structure, and problem-solving. The frequent therapist-led advice limits true equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers some choices and reassures that missed steps are not failure. However, many prescriptive suggestions make the plan feel therapist-generated rather than client-owned.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites some client input and self-observation. Yet the evocation is often superficial and quickly replaced by advice rather than drawing out values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client helps choose plan details, showing some collaboration. However, the counselor remains the dominant expert and does not strongly position the client as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers some change talk, and the counselor invites a small experiment. It does not consistently amplify or deepen the client's reasons and hope for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor normalizes worry about failure without dismissing it. However, it rarely redirects sustain talk into change talk using double-sided reflection or ambivalence work.
