# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing the screening flag without shame. Warmth remains somewhat procedural and could include more individualized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture surface content but stay mostly factual. They miss deeper emotional undercurrents such as embarrassment, privacy concerns, or fear of being labeled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session begins broadly and drifts into school/social topics before returning to the screening. It does not consistently stay organized around alcohol-related risk and the change process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaboration through permission-seeking and option-offering, but the interaction remains counselor-driven. More co-created agenda-setting would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently offers choices and avoids pressuring disclosure or goal-setting. It could better affirm the client's full right to decide, as repeated options still feel somewhat steering.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor relies heavily on psychoeducation and lists rather than drawing out the client's own motivations. Deeper evocative questions about values, the meaning of the flag, or desired change are missing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices are offered, but the counselor remains the main agenda-setter and information provider. The client mostly selects from predefined options rather than shaping the direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A few open questions are asked, but the session focuses on explaining the screener rather than eliciting the client's reasons or desire for change. There is limited use of importance/confidence rulers or values-based follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor accepts the client's reluctance without arguing but does not skillfully reflect ambivalence or redirect toward values/safety. Continuing to offer structured options and frequency questions keeps the conversation investigative.
