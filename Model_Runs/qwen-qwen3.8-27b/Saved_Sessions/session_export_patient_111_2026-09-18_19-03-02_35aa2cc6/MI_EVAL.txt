### Collaboration

### Partnership

**Comments:** Richard does offer meaningful change talk ('I don't want to be the thing she's worried about,' 'I just wish she didn't have to') but it is not consistently reflected, amplified, or built upon. Significant missed opportunities to deepen Richard's own emerging motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without arguing or dismissing; rolls with resistance appropriately. Missed opportunities for strategic double-sided reflections to acknowledge both sides of Richard's ambivalence and soften the sustain talk more effectively.
