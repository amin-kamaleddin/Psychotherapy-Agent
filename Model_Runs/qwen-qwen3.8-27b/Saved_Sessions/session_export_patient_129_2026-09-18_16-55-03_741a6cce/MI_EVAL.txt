### Empathy

**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy, consistently capturing and reflecting back Tyler's internal experience with remarkable precision at the complex level. Demonstrates genuine attentiveness to subtle shifts in language and perspective-taking from an adolescent's viewpoint.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, logical focus on the target behavior with strong momentum and no wandering into unrelated topics. Could reach 5 with a brief collaborative summary to consolidate the session's trajectory more explicitly.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely balanced power dynamics — Tyler's contributions naturally shape the conversation direction, and the psychiatrist follows his leads rather than imposing a structure. The collaboration is natural, not performative, creating a genuine sense of teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never uses 'should,' never implies Tyler ought to stop, and consistently invites self-reflection without pressure or assumption. Exemplary respect for Tyler's agency while creating space for his own insights to emerge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently creates space for Tyler's own motivations to surface rather than providing information or reasons for change. Tyler's words increasingly reveal his values (belonging, feeling seen, autonomy) and the tension between them and his behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a partnership, explicitly disclaiming the expert role and returning authority to Tyler. Textbook MI partnership with Tyler's contributions genuinely shaping the direction of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk by creating space for insights to emerge rather than pushing or lecturing. Could reach 5 by more explicitly amplifying or deepening Tyler's change statements, which remain relatively thin by the end.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without confronting or arguing, using questions to create space for Tyler to examine his own assumptions. Could reach 5 with more explicit double-sided reflections and less focus on the consequences of the status quo.
