# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mostly respectful, but not consistently warm or affirming of Lucía beyond her issue. When she expresses guilt, the psychiatrist responds with problem-solving rather than unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures frustration, autonomy, and ambivalence. Some reflections remain somewhat formulaic and do not fully deepen the emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains some structure but drifts toward boundary-setting and communication strategies. It does not keep the smoking-cessation target clearly in focus or tie the work back to it.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers options and follows Lucía's lead, allowing her choices to shape the session. Sometimes feels advisor-like when scripts and behavioral suggestions are supplied before eliciting her own plan further.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong support for choice and self-direction, including her decision to avoid the quitting topic. Some suggestions and boundary instructions are mildly directive, keeping it just short of full mastery.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some useful open questions early and later about confidence and a win. However, the psychiatrist frequently offers concrete solutions before drawing out Lucía’s own motivations or deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear collaborative stance with invitations for Lucía to choose focus, consequence, and coping cue. The counselor sometimes supplies too much script-like guidance, reducing the equality of the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited evocation of change-relevant language about smoking cessation. Lucía’s subtle acknowledgments of risk and wanting to be a better example are not reflected or built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids confrontation and generally reframes sustain talk in a respectful way. There is less effort to double-reflect or keep the door open to change while honoring her boundary.
