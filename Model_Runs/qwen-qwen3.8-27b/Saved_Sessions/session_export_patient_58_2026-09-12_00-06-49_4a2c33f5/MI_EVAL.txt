### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and accepts Jamal’s refusals. Acceptance is somewhat surface-level and tied more to the plan than to unconditional regard for Jamal as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early reflections capture frustration and the desire to avoid drama. Empathy becomes limited afterward, with fewer complex reflections of Jamal’s ambivalence and emotional tension.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on improving punctuality and moves toward a concrete plan and follow-up. Direction is organized but more behavioral management than MI motivational focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jamal chooses among options and shapes the final check-in, showing partial collaboration. The therapist still supplies much of the agenda and structure, making the partnership uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects some choices, such as declining a self-check log and shortening the check-in. Autonomy support is weakened by frequent directive advice and prescriptive problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Most questions are logistical rather than evocative. The therapist rarely explores Jamal’s deeper values, desires, or own reasons for being on time.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some collaborative moments, but the stance is largely expert-driven. The therapist does not consistently treat Jamal as the expert on his own life, mornings, or family dynamics.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal produces some change talk, such as choosing the app and a three-day check-in. Cultivation remains weak because the therapist does not deeply explore importance, confidence, or intrinsic goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The therapist avoids arguing against Jamal’s desire for less nagging. However, he does not actively soften or redirect sustain talk toward responsibility or intrinsic change.
