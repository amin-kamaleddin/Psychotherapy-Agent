# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently used warm, validating, and nonjudgmental language. Hana’s fears and self-criticism were met with acceptance rather than pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captured Hana’s anxiety, goals, and ambivalence. However, the deeper emotional meaning behind her shame and past avoidance was not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on exam-related avoidance and building a manageable study step. Each question and intervention advanced the target behavior without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborated by offering options and adapting to Hana’s preferences. Some limitation remains because the psychiatrist provided much of the plan’s structure rather than fully eliciting it from Hana.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Hana’s right to choose, stop, and set her own pace was repeatedly affirmed. The psychiatrist avoided coercion and emphasized Hana’s control over the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively drew out Hana’s goals, past coping strategy, and confidence rating. Evocation could be stronger by eliciting more of Hana’s own solution language rather than providing it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a collaborative stance by inviting input and respecting Hana’s expertise about her experience. The provision of specific techniques and scripts slightly reduced the fully partner-like MI stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was noticed and affirmed at several points, especially after the behavioral experiment. Missed opportunities included deeper reflection on Hana’s pride, hope, and self-efficacy statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled gently without arguing or amplifying Hana’s fears. The response was mostly permissive and validating, with fewer active pivots to transform sustain talk into change talk.
