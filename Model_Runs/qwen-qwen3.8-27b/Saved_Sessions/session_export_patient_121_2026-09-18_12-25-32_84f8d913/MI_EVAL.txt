# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout the session, never judging or dismissing Sofia despite her defensive presentation. Explicitly validates her distrust as logical and honors her boundaries without labeling or pushing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and consistent reflective listening that captures both content and feeling, escalating in depth as the session progresses. Reflections are specific, individualized, and precisely attuned to Sofia's emotional state at each moment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear arc from broad grievance to specific relationship to concrete action while honoring Sofia's pace and agenda. The session narrows focus gradually without forcing direction prematurely or allowing drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuinely collaborative dynamic where Sofia's ideas consistently determine the session's direction. The action step and safety conditions emerge entirely from Sofia, with the psychiatrist co-constructing understanding rather than interpreting for her.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Zero coercion or pressure throughout; consistently communicates that Sofia is in full control of whether, when, and how she acts. Never uses directive language and explicitly affirms her right to refuse any action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every motivation articulated in the session is Sofia's own, drawn out through open-ended, internally-focused questions. The psychiatrist introduces no external reasons for change and skillfully evokes ability language from a guarded adolescent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Sofia as the authority on her own experience, including giving her veto power over the therapeutic frame. Functions as a mirror and amplifier rather than a guide or expert, never offering unsolicited advice or solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies genuine change talk from a resistant 15-year-old through well-timed evocative questions. Could have more explicitly affirmed confidence/ability language and elicited slightly more commitment language to reach mastery level.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every instance of sustain talk is met with validation and gentle opening rather than debate, dismissal, or abrupt redirection. Textbook responses that acknowledge the logic behind resistance while noting the implicit change talk within it.
