# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids shaming or moralizing. Acceptance is sometimes weakened by moving quickly into advice before fully validating ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some accurate understanding and validation, especially of the conflict between fun and future consequences. Empathic reflections are limited after the opening and often shift to practical problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing gambling-related financial risk and practical weekend steps. Direction is strong, but overly task-focused and directive relative to MI-guided exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and incorporates client preferences such as tone and specific cards. Collaboration is uneven because the clinician often supplies lists and multiple options rather than co-constructing the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices are offered and the client is allowed to start small. Autonomy is partially undermined by a prescriptive agenda, repeated scripts, and the clinician managing the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some relevant information and client-generated ideas such as cutting the card and using a workmate. Evocation is superficial, with little use of open questions to connect behavior to values or amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative moves such as asking preferences and tailoring messages to the client's style. The overall structure remains expert-driven, with the clinician providing the main plan and interventions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers meaningful change talk about stopping, protecting the job, and using safer payment methods. The psychiatrist affirms but does not consistently deepen, amplify, or strategically elicit this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and does not reinforce reasons to continue gambling. Sustain talk is not consistently softened with double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance.
