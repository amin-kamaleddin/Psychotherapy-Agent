# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently normalizes Elira's experience and communicates nonjudgment. However, the warmth remains somewhat functional and does not fully affirm her as a person beyond her avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Elira's ambivalence, anxiety, and physical sensations. Deeper emotional meaning, such as shame or self-criticism, could have been explored more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the target avoidance behavior and is broken into small, manageable steps. At times, the direction becomes overly procedural rather than connected to Elira's broader values or goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elira's input is incorporated into the plan, but most options and techniques are therapist-generated. The session feels more like the client choosing from clinician-designed options rather than co-creating the direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, normalizes stopping, and avoids pressure. However, frequent procedural instructions still leave the overall frame therapist-directed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist highlights Elira's agency and completed actions, but does not consistently draw out her own values, hopes, or personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Elira to choose among options, but the session remains fairly clinician-driven. There is limited explicit language affirming Elira as the expert in her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elira produces some change talk and receives affirmations, but the psychiatrist uses few open-ended questions to deepen or expand her own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally normalized and redirected toward small, doable actions rather than argued against. Stronger double-sided reflections could have better balanced her ambivalence.
