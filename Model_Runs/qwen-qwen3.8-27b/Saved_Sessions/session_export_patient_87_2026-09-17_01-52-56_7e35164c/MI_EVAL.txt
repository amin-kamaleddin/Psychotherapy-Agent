# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming regard without labeling or blaming. This stance helped normalize Marco’s struggle and supported a safe, respectful therapeutic atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflected Marco’s emotions, ambivalence, and bodily experience with nuanced and timely responses. Reflections captured both the meaning and felt texture of his struggle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on a clear, specific target: making a healthier choice during the high-risk first minutes after coming home. The conversation progressed logically from values to triggers to a concrete, testable action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked as an equal partner, using Marco’s own ideas to shape the plan and define success. The session felt co-constructed rather than directive or imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasized choice, ownership, and noncontrolling support. Marco was invited to generate and adapt his own plan without pressure or overcommitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited Marco’s motivations, values, and self-generated strategies. Evocation could be strengthened by asking more open questions before offering interpretations or affirmations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly positioned Marco as the expert on his own routine and built on his emerging ideas. This reinforced a collaborative, client-led approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully drew out meaningful change talk and helped Marco generate a self-owned action plan. Further evoking and amplifying desire, ability, and confidence talk could strengthen this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled skillfully through normalization and reframing rather than direct confrontation. The psychiatrist helped Marco separate small wins from all-or-nothing failure.
