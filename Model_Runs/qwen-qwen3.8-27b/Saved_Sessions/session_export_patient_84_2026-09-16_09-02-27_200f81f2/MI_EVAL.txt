# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and supports Malik’s autonomy. It would be stronger with more explicit affirmation and less subtle alignment with his minimization of his mother’s concern.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurately captures Malik’s surface position and feelings. More complex reflections could deepen empathy by naming underlying ambivalence, shame, or peer-related fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session begins by addressing substance use but quickly shifts to reducing yelling and building trust at home. The core target behavior of cannabis use is not consistently kept in view.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and adapts to Malik’s preferences, such as privacy and self-directed communication. Introducing a specific plan before eliciting Malik’s own ideas slightly reduces equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Malik is repeatedly told he is in charge, can stop the experiment, and is not pressured to admit a problem. Some mild steering through the specific text plan slightly weakens full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks useful open questions about goals, trust, and consequences of use. However, the session moves too quickly into advising and planning, limiting deeper evocation of intrinsic motivation for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks permission, offers choices, and respects Malik’s decisions, conveying a collaborative stance. The early introduction of a structured seven-night plan makes the role feel more like advisor than partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Small change talk emerges around trying the texting experiment and proposing the plan himself. More work is needed to elicit change talk specifically related to cannabis use, personal values, independence, or harm reduction.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Malik and does not reinforce his minimization directly. Advanced MI techniques, such as double-sided reflections and discrepancy work, would help transform sustain talk into change talk.
