# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor shows respect, warmth, and nonjudgmental acceptance, including when Luka discloses risky behavior or ambivalence. It is strong but could become more explicit in separating Luka’s worth from his performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses accurate, thoughtful reflections that track Luka’s ambivalence and internal conflict. Empathy could deepen further by attending more explicitly to fear, shame, and pride.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol use and preventing alcohol-linked aggression. Direction could be strengthened by more frequently connecting the pause plan to the broader goal of safety.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor works with Luka as a partner, co-constructing the plan from the client’s own ideas. The interaction is genuinely collaborative rather than advice-giving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor strongly supports autonomy by avoiding pressure, moralizing, or abstinence demands. It could be improved by making the client’s freedom to choose even more explicit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively evokes client-generated strategies and motivations. Some questions are too multiple-choice, reducing the chance for deeper, values-based evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently treats Luka as the expert on his own experience and needs. Power is shared in a way that strongly centers the client’s own knowledge and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits and reflects change talk, keeping the session centered on Luka’s own language. It could be more advanced by amplifying the strongest change talk and connecting it to values or identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles sustain talk without arguing, shaming, or minimizing. It could be more strategic by using more double-sided reflections and explicitly pivoting toward change or values.
