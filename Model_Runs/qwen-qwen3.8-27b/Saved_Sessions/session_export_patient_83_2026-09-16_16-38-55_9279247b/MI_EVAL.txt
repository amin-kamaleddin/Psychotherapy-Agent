# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates the client's careful approach. Warmth is solid but somewhat formulaic and could more explicitly normalize frustration with the doctor's vague explanation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurately captures the client's concerns about control, safety, and avoiding overmonitoring. It could be deeper by reflecting the emotional impact of vague medical communication and anxiety about an unfamiliar medication.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the new medication, side effects, and evening routine with minimal drift. Direction could be strengthened by confirming specific adherence targets such as timing, dose, and a concrete next action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client's preferences clearly shape the session, and the psychiatrist uses inclusive, co-creative language. The interaction demonstrates full collaboration in building the medication plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offers choices, avoids pressure, and supports the client's self-defined monitoring rule. Autonomy is strongly respected throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the client's own reasoning, standards, and practical values. Evocation could be deeper by more fully exploring confidence, personal motivation, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites the client's preferences without imposing decisions. Partnership could improve by more explicitly affirming the client's expertise in her own body and values.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client produces useful change talk about routine, monitoring, and follow-up. The psychiatrist elicits some of this but does not consistently amplify, reflect, or deepen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skepticism is handled respectfully without arguing, minimizing, or reinforcing resistance. Responses could be more transformative by explicitly connecting the client's concerns to autonomy, safety, and self-direction.
