### Acceptance

**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Jason's frustration. Warmth is somewhat procedural and lacks more individualized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections of ambivalence and captures emotional tensions well. Misses deeper exploration of shame, fear, and defensiveness at times.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stays focused on the referral issue and related behavior with little drift. Moves too quickly toward solutions before fully developing a clear, client-owned target.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Invites Jason's perspective and builds on his ideas about social pressure. Still shows therapist-led pacing, reducing the sense of equal collaboration.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some client-generated change ideas, such as managing socially without drinking. Does not sufficiently amplify, summarize, or consolidate these statements.
