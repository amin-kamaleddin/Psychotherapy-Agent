# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepts Adrian's minimization and self-view without correcting or shaming him. Acceptance could be strengthened with more explicit affirmations of his strengths, honesty, or effort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections capture both the practical and emotional meaning of Adrian's statements, including boredom, health fear, and ambivalence. The empathy is strong but could explore deeper shame, isolation, or the emotional impact of the infection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation generally stays focused on substance-related harm, weekend use, and safer sourcing. Direction could be improved by more clearly consolidating the target behavior and maintaining a clearer trajectory toward a specific change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Adrian's own ideas and develops a self-generated plan rather than imposing an agenda. Collaboration could be stronger with more explicit co-creation of session goals and acknowledgment of Adrian as the expert in his own life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Adrian's autonomy by not pressuring him to quit and by building the plan around his own choices. Minor phrasing could subtly frame the issue as something that should change beyond safer use.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions consistently draw out Adrian's own motivations and lead to a self-generated rule. Evocation could be strengthened by exploring values, readiness, confidence, and summarizing his reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Adrian's perspective and treats him as the source of solutions without positioning himself as the expert. Partnership could be stronger with explicit agenda negotiation and clearer shared decision-making language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes meaningful change talk related to health risks, safer sourcing, and a simple plan. More consistent amplification, affirmation, and summarizing of change talk would make the skill more advanced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without arguing, by reflecting and honoring Adrian's boundaries. More strategic double-sided reflections or active pivoting could better move the conversation toward broader change.
