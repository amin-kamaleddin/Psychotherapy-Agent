# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive of Sofia’s concerns and ambivalence. Acceptance is strong but sometimes tied to task completion rather than affirming her worth independent of progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of worry, exhaustion, and concern about relapse. However, some deeper emotional meanings, such as past diet disappointments or the self-critical inner voice, are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on a small, realistic movement and energy habit. The psychiatrist guides the conversation with clear momentum toward concrete, non-diet-based change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adapts quickly to Sofia’s preferences and treats the plan as a joint problem-solving task. At times, the counselor still supplies many structures and options, making the collaboration slightly clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently offers choices and avoids commands, supporting Sofia’s sense of control. Some supportive language remains subtly evaluative, which can imply a preferred path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Sofia’s own practical ideas and problem-solving. Evocation is less developed in exploring intrinsic motivation, values, or the deeper personal meaning of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia’s input and builds the plan around her preferences. The counselor occasionally moves into an advising role by providing specific options and scripts before the client generates them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses confidence questions and affirmations to strengthen Sofia’s plan and increase her confidence from 4 to 7. The focus is practical, with less emphasis on values, desire, or identity-based change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes barriers and avoids arguing with Sofia’s concerns about exhaustion and failure. The session would benefit from more strategic pivots away from barriers toward strengths and values.
