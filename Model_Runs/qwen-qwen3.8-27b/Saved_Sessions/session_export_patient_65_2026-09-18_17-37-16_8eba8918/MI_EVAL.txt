### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental with multiple affirmations of Julian's honesty and effort, and normalizes his lack of intrinsic motivation. Could be deepened with more explicit acknowledgment of his broader life circumstances (e.g., being in the justice system at 19).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections that accurately capture underlying emotions (e.g., fear of escalation, lack of drive). Could go deeper by more fully exploring the emotional weight of his legal situation and his fear of re-offending.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with progressive momentum from broad problem to specific actionable step. Could benefit from a consolidating summary at the end and more explicit connection to overarching goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with question-based approach that helps Julian generate his own answers rather than imposing solutions. Could be elevated by more explicit co-creation of the agenda and acknowledging the psychiatrist's own role and limitations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Julian's sense of choice with no pressuring or implied guilt, and explicitly normalizes his ambivalence. Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming his right to make different choices and acknowledging how the court mandate limits his autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Predominantly evocative approach using open-ended questions that led Julian to self-generate his complete action plan (leaving a voicemail at 7:30). Could be deepened by evoking values, identity, future self, and using scaling questions to assess confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive, non-prescriptive language and avoids an expert-driven approach. Could be strengthened by more explicit acknowledgment of Julian's expertise in his own life and negotiating the session's pacing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits Julian's own planning language (ability, desire, commitment) and reflects it back to reinforce his capacity. Could be more varied with deliberate evocation of importance and confidence language, and a scaling question to assess readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by reframing (e.g., shifting from 'how do I motivate myself' to 'how do I make it happen anyway') without invalidating Julian's experience. Could more fully leverage Julian's fear of negative consequences as a source of change talk.
