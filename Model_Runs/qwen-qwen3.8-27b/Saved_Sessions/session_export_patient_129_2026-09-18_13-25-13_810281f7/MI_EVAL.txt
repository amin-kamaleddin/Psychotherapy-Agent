# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently non-judgmental and respectful, with no labeling or lecturing. Warmth could be slightly deeper in a few moments, occasionally feeling procedural rather than fully warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with notable attunement to micro-moments of ambivalence (e.g., catching the "maybe" shift). Occasionally stays at the cognitive level without fully accessing the emotional undercurrent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from resistance to change talk while following the client's lead without being heavy-handed. Could benefit from strategic summary statements that explicitly tie themes together.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint exploration with open-ended questions and builds naturally on the client's responses. Follows a somewhat linear path the psychiatrist is still largely guiding rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary and consistent explicit autonomy language throughout ("your space," "your call," "you don't have to commit to anything"). Respects the boundary of the client's resistance without trying to break through it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws intrinsic motivation from a precontemplative client using open questions, evocative reflections, and strategic follow-ups. Could have gone further with confidence/ruling questions or expanded on the change talk already offered.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clearly collaborative stance, never positioning as the expert, and frequently asks the client's opinion rather than providing information. Could be strengthened with explicit acknowledgment of the client's expertise in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits multiple instances of change talk (desire, expectation, need) from a resistant adolescent starting in precontemplation. Could have pushed slightly further into ability and confidence statements and summarized the change talk themes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles minimization and "everyone does it" without confrontation, using skilled redirects and hypotheticals. Could incorporate more explicit double-sided reflections that name both sustain and change talk simultaneously.
