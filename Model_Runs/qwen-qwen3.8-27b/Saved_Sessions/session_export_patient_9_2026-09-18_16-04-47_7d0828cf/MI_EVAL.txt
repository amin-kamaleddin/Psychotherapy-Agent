# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone with embedded affirmations throughout. Some affirmations feel somewhat formulaic, and there is no explicit statement of unconditional acceptance or normalization of Amina's struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections that accurately capture ambivalence and emotional complexity. Could go deeper in moments of peak vulnerability and soften analytical reflections with more emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behavior with a well-structured arc from ambivalence to preparation. A brief exploration of deeper beliefs could be seen as a slight detour but ultimately serves the MI process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is co-constructed with language signaling partnership and consistent invitation of Amina's perspective. Could be strengthened by more explicit acknowledgment of the psychiatrist's limits and modeling vulnerability alongside Amina.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Amina's right to choose without prescribing solutions or rushing moments of vulnerability. Autonomy is implied but not explicitly stated (e.g., 'This is your decision, not mine'), keeping it at a 4.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally skillful at drawing out Amina's own motivations rather than providing reasons for change. The session ends with Amina articulating her own powerful reframe, which is the hallmark of effective evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with Amina clearly positioned as the expert on her own life. Could be elevated with more explicit co-creation moments, such as asking whether offered framings resonate or if she sees them differently.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions consistently elicit Amina's own change language, which is then reflected and amplified back to her. Creates a clear upward trajectory of motivation, culminating in Amina's self-generated 'compass instead of a checklist' reframe.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging difficulty and exploring underlying beliefs without confrontation. One instance could have used a double-sided reflection to more explicitly acknowledge the legitimacy of Amina's choice while exploring its cost.
