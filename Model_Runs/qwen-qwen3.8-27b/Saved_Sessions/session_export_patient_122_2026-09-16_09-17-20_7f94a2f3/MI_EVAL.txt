# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, nonjudgment, and support while normalizing Sofia’s ambivalence and fatigue. Some affirmations such as “wise and realistic” subtly steer the client’s experience of what is acceptable.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the heat, decision fatigue, tracking-related judgment, and exhaustion. The psychiatrist could sit longer with deeper self-critical emotion before moving into problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on a small, realistic post-dinner movement routine and related barriers. It progresses logically from goal-setting to confidence scaling, implementation, fallbacks, and commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as joint problem-solving, with Sofia meaningfully shaping the final plan. The psychiatrist adapts when Sofia rejects or modifies options and follows her lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses permissive language and repeatedly asks Sofia which option feels doable. Some prompts to act “now” or “this minute” create subtle pressure, limiting fully open ownership of timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is drawn out to articulate many plan details, values, and fallback choices. The psychiatrist could ask more evocative questions before offering therapist-generated options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adopts a collaborative stance and allows Sofia to choose or modify the plan. The stance is not fully at the highest level because the psychiatrist still supplies many options and structures the decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits desire, ability, values, and reasons for change through scaling and choice questions. More consistent amplification of Sofia’s own change talk would strengthen this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is normalized and reframed without arguing, persuasion, or confrontation. The psychiatrist could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly hold ambivalence and help Sofia resolve it in her own words.
