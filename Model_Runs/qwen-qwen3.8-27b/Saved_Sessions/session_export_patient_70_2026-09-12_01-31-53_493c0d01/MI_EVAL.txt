# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful and non-judgmental tone while validating the client's choices. However, the warmth is somewhat transactional, tied more to task completion than to deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initial emotional reflections are offered but remain brief and surface-level. The psychiatrist quickly shifts to problem-solving without fully exploring the client's ambivalence or underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the immediate safety and behavioral target of managing the urge to contact old friends. Pacing is steady and avoids drift from the main risk-management goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaboration in completing tasks, such as creating a checklist and planning a timed activity. However, the psychiatrist remains the primary source of tools and tactics rather than fully partnering in exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects the client's choices by asking which option feels doable and accepting the decision not to text Donna immediately. Autonomy is still limited because the client is mostly selecting from pre-structured clinician-led options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supplies most of the coping strategies, scripts, and action steps. This reduces opportunities for the client to identify and own their own resources and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is highly directive, with the psychiatrist providing structured options and plans. The client's insights and preferences do not sufficiently guide the session, weakening the collaborative MI stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client expresses some change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen it. The focus remains more on compliance and task completion than on strengthening internal motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes the client's continued curiosity about the old crew, which prevents confrontation. However, there is limited use of deeper reflections to turn that tension into change talk.
