# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance while accepting the client's ambivalence and concerns. Acceptance is slightly limited by a practical, formulaic tone rather than deeper emotional attunement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, especially for the client's practical concerns and plans. Empathy could be deeper by more fully reflecting emotional meaning behind feelings about strength, rhythm, and pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on increasing physical activity and steadily moves broad intentions into concrete, low-pressure steps. There is little drift and each question advances the client toward an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works collaboratively, with the client's preferences shaping the final plan. Some of the plan is therapist-guided, including specific scripts and options, so it is strong but not fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is regularly positioned as the decision-maker and is asked to choose among options. Autonomy support is strong, though the psychiatrist occasionally introduces specific scripts and time-bound suggestions that feel somewhat directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client's motivations, preferences, and self-generated solutions. Evocation could be deeper by exploring values, confidence, and the meaning behind the client's change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and invites the client's expertise. Partnership is strong but sometimes feels therapist-guided because of pre-supplied options and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist creates good opportunities for the client to express motivation, confidence, and specific plans. Change talk could be deepened by exploring the basis of confidence and amplifying desire, ability, need, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers are handled well without argument and are redirected into flexible action plans. Softening could be more classic MI by using double-sided reflections and reframing ambivalence itself.
