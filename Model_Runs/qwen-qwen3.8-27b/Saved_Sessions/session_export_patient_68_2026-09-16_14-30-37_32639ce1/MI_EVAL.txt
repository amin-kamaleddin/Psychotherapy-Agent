# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with frequent validation such as “makes sense.” It does not reach a 5 because the warmth is somewhat procedural and less individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers good reflective listening that captures ambivalence, social concerns, and fear of feeling weak. It is not a 5 because reflections remain somewhat surface-level and miss deeper embarrassment or identity conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on drinking, injury risk, and getting home safely. It falls short of a 5 because some questions are multi-layered and the ending lacks clear consolidation of a client-owned plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Kevin’s leads and uses collaborative language such as “what do you think.” It is not a 5 because some framing and steering still make the conversation feel mildly therapist-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking for Kevin’s own view and avoiding pressure to quit. It is not a 5 because autonomy is not consistently explicit when Kevin expresses self-doubt or uncertainty about willpower.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions and avoids giving direct reasons for change. It remains a 3 because evocation is somewhat superficial and does not fully connect change talk to values, identity, confidence, or future self.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a non-expert, inviting stance and lets Kevin guide the focus. It is not a 5 because co-construction of the agenda and explicit client expertise are not always made clear.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits and affirms some change talk, such as the goal of walking home straight. It is not higher because there is limited use of scaling, summarizing, and deeper strengthening of Kevin’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges the social pull of drinking without arguing or moralizing. It is not a 5 because stronger double-sided reflections or reframing around relationships and safety would be needed to fully integrate sustain talk.
