# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, validating Naomi's anxiety and shame. It is strong but sometimes procedural rather than deeply affirming her worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture anxiety triggers, shame, and fear of failing, but often stay at the cognitive level. Deeper, emotionally nuanced reflections of ambivalence and shame would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains consistently focused on managing anxiety during photography shoots and builds a concrete, testable plan. Pacing and target behavior are clear with little drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi’s preferences shape the final plan, including the ring, cue word, and home practice. However, the psychiatrist provides many ready-made options, limiting more balanced co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Permission is sought, choices are offered, and pressure is reduced by defining doing the step as success. It would be stronger with more explicit reinforcement of Naomi’s authority over what feels possible.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some early questions elicit Naomi’s goals and change talk, but the therapist often shifts to offering solutions. More elicitation of her values, confidence, and reasons for change is needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi is invited to choose elements of the plan, but the psychiatrist frequently acts as the expert menu provider. More open invitations to draw on Naomi’s own experience would improve partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps identify small goals and affirms Naomi’s choices, but evocation is limited. Little use of scaling, importance ratings, or questions expanding her own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Concerns about failure, self-consciousness, and ineffective techniques are met without argument, often by making steps smaller and less threatening. More explicit double-sided reflections of ambivalence would strengthen the skill.
