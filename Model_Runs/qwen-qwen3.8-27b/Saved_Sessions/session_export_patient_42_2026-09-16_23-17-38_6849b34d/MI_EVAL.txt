# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently respectful and nonjudgmental stance toward the client's ambivalence. Affirming statements show positive regard without moralizing about smoking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client's mixed feelings about health, connection, and possible loss. The psychiatrist attends to both emotional and somatic experience with strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on smoking during social breaks and gradually moves toward a specific, manageable plan. The pacing is strategic, with each question building toward a concrete behavioral step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves as an equal partner rather than an expert giving advice. The final strategy is co-constructed from the client's own ideas, such as coffee, staying in the room, and using a simple phrase.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decision-making is consistently placed with the client through open, optional, and low-pressure language. The psychiatrist does not impose a plan, demand quitting, or frame change as obligatory.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client's own motivations, images, and problem-solving ideas. It could be deepened further by more explicitly drawing out values, confidence, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is clearly positioned as the expert on her own change, with the psychiatrist inviting her to generate and evaluate options. The client's ideas actively shape the course of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits and reinforces useful change talk about staying connected while protecting health. However, change talk is not yet systematically deepened through more explicit confidence, ability, or commitment exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about friendship and belonging is acknowledged without argument and gently reframed to preserve social connection. It could be strengthened by more consistent double-sided reflections or explicit rolling-with statements.
