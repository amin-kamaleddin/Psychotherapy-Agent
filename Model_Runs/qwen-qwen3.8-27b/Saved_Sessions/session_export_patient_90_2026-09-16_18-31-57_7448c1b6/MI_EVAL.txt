# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates the client’s concerns and boundaries. However, the warmth is somewhat procedural and could more explicitly express unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and captures both ambivalence and the emotional impact of the client’s struggles. Some reflections remain content-focused and could more deeply explore underlying fears, identity, or emotional toll.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on asthma management and a concrete, manageable plan. The conversation moves steadily from overwhelm to specific, actionable steps with little drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as an equal partner, co-constructing the plan directly from the client’s ideas. The interaction feels genuinely joint rather than clinician-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client’s sense of choice is consistently supported, and his boundaries are explicitly honored. Suggestions are framed as options rather than directives, reducing pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the client’s own ideas, motivations, and practical solutions. Evocation is more action-oriented than deeply motivational and could further explore personal values, readiness, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is positioned as the expert on his own life, and his input strongly shapes the session. There is no imposed agenda or expert-driven solution.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits change talk by inviting the client to identify doable steps and affirm his capability. The interaction would be stronger with more consistent amplification of change talk and explicit summarization of the client’s reasons and readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk around the dog, finances, and medical systems is acknowledged without argument, helping reduce resistance. The psychiatrist could more actively reframe or deepen the exploration of the values and concerns behind this sustain talk.
