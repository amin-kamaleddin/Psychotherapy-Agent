# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveys respect and nonjudgment while validating Camila’s ambivalence. Some language feels formulaic and lacks stronger affirmation of her strengths or insight.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor offers strong reflective listening, capturing the social function of alcohol and Camila’s fear of seeming “boring” sober. At times, reflections become repetitive and do not fully deepen the underlying shame, loneliness, or exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on reducing weekday alcohol use and concrete, low-pressure options. It would benefit from periodic summaries to consolidate the central focus and maintain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor builds on Camila’s ideas, such as smaller groups, activities, and dating needs. The process remains somewhat therapist-led in structure and could include more explicit co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor respects Camila’s right to choose and supports her preference for a “middle way” rather than abstinence. Autonomy support could be strengthened with more explicit language about ownership and choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor evokes Camila’s own motivations and possible steps rather than supplying external arguments for change. The evocation remains more practical and could go deeper into values, identity, confidence, and readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites Camila’s perspective and avoids imposing advice or an abstinence goal. The stance could become more explicitly collaborative with clearer power-sharing language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits specific change talk, such as wanting clearer evenings and a shorter, low-pressure meet-up. However, the counselor does not consistently amplify or build on this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor respectfully reflects sustain talk rather than arguing with or reinforcing it. The session remains fairly centered on reasons against change and uses limited strategic softening to resolve ambivalence.
