# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Therapist consistently conveys respect and nonjudgment, without blaming or moralizing. The warmth remains somewhat procedural and does not deeply affirm Elena's individual struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Therapist accurately reflects Elena's ambivalence and practical concerns. The empathy is more cognitive and surface-level, missing deeper emotional undercurrents such as fear and vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on contraception, side effects, cost, and next steps. The direction is more informational than change-focused and lacks consolidation toward a client-chosen goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Therapist adapts to Elena's preferences and offers choices, but the session remains largely therapist-led. There is limited use of truly collaborative language to co-create the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Therapist strongly supports Elena's pace and decision not to book an appointment. Some directives and recommendations could be framed more as client-controlled choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Therapist asks some evocative questions, but does not consistently draw out internal motivation, values, confidence, or client-generated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Therapist invites input and tailors the handout to Elena's concerns. However, expertise and control remain largely with the therapist, and the client is not fully positioned as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena offers meaningful change talk, but therapist rarely reflects, amplifies, or expands it. Questions often elicit information or barriers rather than motivation, desire, or ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Therapist accepts sustain talk without defensiveness or argument. There is limited strategic softening, such as using double-sided reflections or exploring the cost of the status quo.
