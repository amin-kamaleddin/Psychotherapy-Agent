# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, consistently validating Mei's experience without judgment or labeling. No instances of disapproval or conditional regard in the entire session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing Mei's ambivalence and emotional content. Occasionally adds interpretation rather than pure reflection, and could go deeper on moments of emotional vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear trajectory from problem identification through exploration to next steps, with good focus on target behaviors. Could be more strategic in connecting the exploration back to concrete change goals and action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as a joint exploration, actively seeking Mei's input and co-constructing decisions. Could be elevated by more explicitly acknowledging that Mei is the expert on her own life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never pressures Mei to make a decision, using language that reduces urgency and honors her pace. Could more explicitly affirm Mei's ownership of her choices (e.g., 'This is your call').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently uses open-ended, evocative questions that draw out Mei's own motivations and self-discoveries. Could more consistently link her insights back to change talk by asking how those discoveries connect to what she wants to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance throughout, positioning Mei as the expert on her own experience and avoiding directives. Occasionally takes a slightly more guiding role that subtly positions the psychiatrist as leading the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk but does not consistently reinforce or expand upon Mei's powerful change statements. Misses opportunities to deepen change talk with follow-up questions and does not elicit confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with relative skill through positive reframing and honest acknowledgment. Does not consistently use double-sided reflections to hold both sides of ambivalence and gently pivot toward change.
