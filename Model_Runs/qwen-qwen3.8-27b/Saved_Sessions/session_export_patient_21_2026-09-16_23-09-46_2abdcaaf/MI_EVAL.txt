# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, warmth, and respect without judgment. Client statements and ambivalence are validated rather than corrected or minimized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and nuanced, capturing the client’s feelings, ambivalence, barriers, and self-doubt. Reflections go beyond paraphrasing and track the client’s perspective closely.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and moves purposefully from ambivalence to a concrete action plan. Questions strategically guide the conversation while remaining client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally acts as an equal partner and allows the client’s ideas to shape the plan. However, the collaboration could be more explicit through visible co-construction and shared agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client’s freedom to choose, set limits, and define success is consistently emphasized. Language supports choice and avoids pressure, guilt, or expert-imposed goals.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally elicits the client’s own motivations, values, and plan rather than supplying reasons for change. Some questions are slightly leading or narrow the client after the counselor introduces specific ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions the client as the expert on her own life and change. Client choices substantially shape the session, including timing, cue, activity, and definition of success.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits meaningful change talk through open, client-centered questions. The evocation is frequent but could be deepened by more explicitly reinforcing the client’s own arguments for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing, minimizing, or pressuring, and is often reframed to preserve autonomy. More consistent double-sided reflections would better hold both sustain talk and change talk together.
