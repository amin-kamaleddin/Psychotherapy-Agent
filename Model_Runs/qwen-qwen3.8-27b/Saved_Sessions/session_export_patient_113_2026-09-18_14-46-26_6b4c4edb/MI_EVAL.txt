# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, non-judgmental, and validating of Rowan's identity throughout the session. The warmth is professional and appropriate but could be slightly more personal and specific to his lived experience to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures both content and feeling, including the tension in his ambivalence. Could have explored his fear of losing control more deeply, and a few reflections were slightly interpretive beyond what he said.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from exploring resistance to practical barriers. Could be more purposeful in building toward a concrete change goal or action plan by the end of the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and frames the conversation as a joint exploration with Rowan positioned as the expert on his own body. The collaboration is somewhat one-directional, as the psychiatrist does most of the talking and guiding rather than co-creating the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest aspect of the session — consistently supports Rowan's right to make his own choices, never imposes advice or directives, and frames the entire conversation as his decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes Rowan's own motivations through open-ended questions and notices change talk as it emerges. Could be more consistent and strategic in drawing out his values and amplifying change talk throughout the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and invites Rowan's perspective frequently, but the dynamic remains somewhat one-directional. Could have more explicitly shared power or co-created the conversation's direction with Rowan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Notices and reflects back change talk effectively, including the subtle shift from 'I'd never use it' to 'I'd check it when I feel off.' Could have dug deeper into stated values (e.g., not ending up in the hospital) to more sustainably amplify change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well without arguing, correcting, or being dismissive, and validates the underlying concern. Could have used more strategic double-sided reflections to actively help resolve the ambivalence between wanting to be safe and not wanting to be controlled.
