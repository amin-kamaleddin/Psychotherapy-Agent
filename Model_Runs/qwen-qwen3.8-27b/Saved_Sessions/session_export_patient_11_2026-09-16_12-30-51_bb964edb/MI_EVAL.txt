# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance, validating Amira's relocation, low energy, and small-step plan. However, some praise sounds contingent on choosing a doable plan, making acceptance slightly performance-oriented.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is present but often stays surface-level before shifting to problem-solving. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to explore deeper feelings about disorientation, low energy, body confidence, and fear of overwhelm.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and moves clearly from Amira's preference for activity to a concrete plan. The direction becomes narrow toward logistics rather than continually connecting the plan to deeper motivation and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed, with Amira shaping the phone strategy, cue, timing, and check-in wording. Collaboration is not fully equal because the psychiatrist still controls much of the structure and session agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amira is offered choices, flexibility, and low-pressure options, supporting her sense of control. The check-in system and expressions of pride could create subtle pressure toward monitored compliance rather than self-directed change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some of Amira's own motivations are elicited, but the psychiatrist supplies much of the content, rationale, and structure. Deeper values, identity, and long-term goals are not consistently drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira is invited to choose among options and contributes to the final plan. However, the psychiatrist remains relatively leading and agenda-driven, with uneven sharing of power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira expresses change language, but it is not deeply expanded or amplified. The session moves quickly into planning, limiting development of desire, ability, need, commitment, and values-based change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Concerns about distraction, low energy, and pressure are handled well by making the plan smaller and more flexible. The responses are practical rather than fully MI-based, with less use of reframing or double-sided reflections.
