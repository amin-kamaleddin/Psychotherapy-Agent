# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone while normalizing the patient’s uncertainty. The warmth is supportive but somewhat task-oriented rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers adequate reflections of the patient’s ambivalence and desire for clear answers. Empathy remains relatively surface-level and often gives way to information delivery.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the medication decision and adherence plan. The counseling is purposeful, though the amount of clinical instruction makes it feel less like an MI-driven process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites the patient’s input and preferences while offering practical options. The interaction becomes uneven because the counselor frequently leads with long stretches of education.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor supports choice by offering options and checking readiness. However, the advice is prescriptive and does not consistently affirm the patient’s full ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor uses a readiness ruler to evoke some confidence, but the session relies heavily on counselor-provided reasons and safety rules. Deeper evocation of the patient’s values and personal motivations is limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks for the patient’s priorities and incorporates routine into the plan. The interaction often shifts into an expert-driven model, reducing the sense of shared power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits statements of readiness and a concrete plan to start the medication. Change talk is not consistently amplified, reflected, or linked to the patient’s values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor normalizes the patient’s concerns without arguing against them. The heavy focus on side effects and safety information keeps the conversation anchored to risk rather than moving strategically toward change.
