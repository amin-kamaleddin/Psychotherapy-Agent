# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance and repeatedly normalized Maya's ambivalence. Some affirmations were somewhat formulaic and did not fully validate the emotional vulnerability behind her concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used accurate reflections to capture Maya's mixed feelings about drinking, school, and fitting in. Empathy was strong but remained more cognitive/behavioral than emotionally deep.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on alcohol use and its practical impact, narrowing to a manageable Sunday morning plan. Parts of the planning became task-oriented and slightly distracted from the motivational process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked collaboratively and incorporated Maya's ideas into the plan. The frequent use of structured options and suggested steps created a subtle expert-driven tone.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maya's choices and limits were respected throughout, including rejecting drafts, reminders, and more formal plans. Occasional offers to draft or set reminders were mildly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used evocative questions and scaling to explore importance, confidence, and barriers. There was less time to deeply evoke Maya's values, ability, and intrinsic reasons for change before moving into planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction reflected partnership by inviting Maya's perspective and using her preferences to shape the plan. The psychiatrist could have stepped back more often to let Maya lead the agenda and generate more options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerged around school, future concerns, and the bus backup. The psychiatrist did not consistently reflect, expand, or amplify Maya's change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepted Maya's sustain talk without arguing or confronting her. The response was more passive and could have used double-sided reflections or gentle pivots to open space for change.
