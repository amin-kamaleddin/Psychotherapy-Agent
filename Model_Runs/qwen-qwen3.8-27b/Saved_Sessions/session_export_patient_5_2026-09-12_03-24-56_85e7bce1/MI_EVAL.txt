# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Alessia's concerns without arguing. The tone is supportive, though at times slightly procedural rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is adequate reflective listening, but the responses often stay at a surface level. The psychiatrist could better reflect the fear, loss, and emotional weight behind Alessia's ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the medication decision, including safety, timing, and next steps. There is minor drift into broader coping suggestions, but the overall direction is strong.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates by co-creating questions and a script, but the session is still partly expert-driven. Alessia could have been invited to generate and prioritize more of the content herself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly respects Alessia's choice, avoids pressure, and includes language that preserves her decision-making ownership. The large amount of medical information still slightly reduces the client's sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open questions invite Alessia to identify priorities, but the counselor often shifts quickly into information-giving. More evoking questions about her values, goals, and reasons for change would strengthen this.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and collaborates on practical tools, but the agenda remains largely counselor-led. The client's expertise in her own life is not consistently foregrounded.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are moments where Alessia expresses reasons for change, but the psychiatrist does not consistently expand or amplify them. The session would benefit from more scaling, future-focused, or reason-eliciting questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Alessia's hesitation, but does not effectively soften or redirect her sustain talk. Double-sided reflections and reframing around values and goals would be needed to better address ambivalence.
