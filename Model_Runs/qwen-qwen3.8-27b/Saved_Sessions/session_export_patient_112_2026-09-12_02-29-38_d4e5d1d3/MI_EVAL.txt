# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Rosa's concerns and effort without judgment or shame. Positive regard is strong but remains somewhat functional rather than deeply personal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track Rosa's ambivalence, worries, and practical concerns. They would benefit from deeper emotional and identity-specific explorations of her sustain talk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking cessation and moves steadily toward a concrete, measurable plan. Client autonomy is respected while the work remains strategically directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan is co-constructed through options, adjustments, and Rosa's choices. It is slightly clinician-led because Rosa mostly selects from clinician-generated options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** There is no pressure or coercion; the psychiatrist repeatedly frames the plan as a reversible experiment and invites Rosa's choice. Rosa's autonomy is central to the final plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling and commitment questions successfully elicit Rosa's own motivation and willingness. More follow-up questioning would strengthen pure evocation by expanding her change talk before offering options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses client-guided questions and adapts plans to Rosa's feedback. Partnership would be stronger with more client-generated options and less reliance on clinician-provided menus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes meaningful change talk and affirms Rosa's stated commitments. It would be stronger if the clinician more consistently amplified and expanded Rosa's own pro-change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rosa's sustain talk is acknowledged and reframed without argument or reinforcement. More consistent double-sided reflections would further reduce the intensity of her resistance.
