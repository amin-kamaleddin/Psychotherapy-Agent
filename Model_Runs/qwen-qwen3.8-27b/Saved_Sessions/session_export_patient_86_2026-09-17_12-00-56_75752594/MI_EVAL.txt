# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, including explicitly accepting a correction without defense or reframe. Never uses judgmental, labeling, or dismissive language; consistently treats apparent resistance as a legitimate emotional response.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows highly accurate and nuanced empathic understanding, including sophisticated complex reflections that identify underlying feelings beneath apparent resistance. Consistently calibrates accuracy with checks like 'Is that fair, or am I reading into it?' rather than over-interpreting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking as the target behavior while skillfully allowing the conversation to expand into broader themes of agency and identity. The session ends somewhat open without a brief summary or collaboratively identified next step, which would have tightened strategic direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed around Marco's own metaphors and language rather than a clinician's agenda. Power sharing is real, not superficial — the psychiatrist immediately adapts when corrected and consistently positions Marco as the expert on his own life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is the consistent backbone of every interaction, with explicit non-imposition, respect for pacing, and no use of directive language like 'should' or 'you need to.' Marco defines readiness, values, and meaning entirely on his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation — every piece of motivation is generated by Marco himself, elicited through open questions rather than supplied by the clinician. The psychiatrist never states reasons for quitting, offers health information, or makes arguments for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly cedes interpretive authority to Marco ('I'm not going to tell you whether this is that thing') and places him as the expert on his own experience. The session is structured entirely around Marco's direction, metaphors, and pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk organically, successfully expanding Marco's language from 'quitting' to the broader theme of living on purpose and making choices. However, the change talk remains emerging and not yet developed into explicit commitment or activation language (DARN-CAT).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively without arguing, dismissing, or reinforcing — gently reframes and validates rather than pushing back. Could have been more strategically active with techniques like double-sided reflections or explicit pivots at key moments.
