# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental toward Amira’s choices and limitations. Some affirmations sound mildly evaluative or parental, which keeps this below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Amira’s fatigue, caution, and ambivalence about the plan. Deeper emotional themes, such as feeling out of place after the move, are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on a specific, realistic walking routine and next steps. The direction becomes somewhat plan-heavy and task-focused at times.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira is invited to choose among options and her preferences shape the final plan. However, the psychiatrist frequently generates the specific solutions and structure rather than co-creating them from Amira’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally respects Amira’s choices and honors her boundary against adding another alarm. Autonomy is weakened by occasional directive suggestions and prescriptive plan details.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence scale and follow-up questions help elicit Amira’s own ideas and motivations. Evocation is limited because the psychiatrist often supplies new solutions before fully drawing out Amira’s reasons and preferred next steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are clear moments of collaboration, including asking which options feel best and acknowledging Amira’s boundary. The process is still largely expert-driven, with the psychiatrist leading most of the planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Amira offers several meaningful statements of motivation and self-efficacy, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect or amplify them. The response often shifts quickly into approval or problem-solving instead of deepening change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Amira’s barriers and accepts her safety and fatigue concerns. However, the session spends more time adding countermeasures than reframing or softening sustain talk.
