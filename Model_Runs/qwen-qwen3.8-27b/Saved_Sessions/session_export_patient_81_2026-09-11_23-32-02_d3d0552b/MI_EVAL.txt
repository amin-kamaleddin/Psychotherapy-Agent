# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance without shaming Lukas. However, acceptance is somewhat formulaic and does not fully normalize his deeper social identity conflict.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some reflective listening and validation of Lukas’s stress and confusion. However, empathy remains surface-level and misses deeper feelings of emptiness, fear of exclusion, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on methamphetamine use and practical risk-reduction goals such as auditions, money, and sleep. The direction could be more strategically tied to Lukas’s own values and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adjusts the plan based on Lukas’s refusals and preferences. However, the psychiatrist supplies most of the solutions, so Lukas does not consistently appear as an equal collaborator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Lukas is allowed to reject several suggestions and choose the 48-hour dry plan. However, repeated additions of tracking, reminders, and escalation options reduce the sense of autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers some space for client direction but mostly provides reasons, rules, and practical options. There is limited eliciting of Lukas’s own values, confidence, hopes, and internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks Lukas to choose options and adapts to some of his refusals. However, the psychiatrist remains the primary source of structure and solutions, creating an uneven power balance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reinforces some of Lukas’s change talk, especially around protecting auditions and his career. However, the session does not consistently deepen, amplify, or strategically extend this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Lukas but does not skillfully soften or redirect sustain talk. Some suggestions, such as trying 72 hours or cutting weekend use, may increase resistance by pushing against stated barriers.
