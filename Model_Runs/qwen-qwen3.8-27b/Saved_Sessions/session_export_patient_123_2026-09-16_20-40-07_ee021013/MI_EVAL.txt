# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental, and low-pressure stance. This created a safe environment, though it was slightly generic in places.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Sofia's factual experience and preferences. However, they remained mostly surface-level and missed the deeper emotional undercurrents and autonomy concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session lacked a clear focus on the alcohol risk screening referral or any specific target behavior. There was minimal agenda-setting or guidance toward the presenting concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used inclusive, collaborative language and generally followed Sofia's lead. Full client-led collaboration was not achieved as the clinician still largely guided the focus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong, non-directive autonomy support was evident through low-pressure invitations and respect for Sofia's preferences. A few subtle nudges toward self-monitoring prevented a higher score.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Questions invited Sofia to draw on her own experience but were shallow and disconnected from the referral. Intrinsic motivation for a health-related change was not effectively evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia was consistently positioned as the expert on her own life, which strengthened the partnership. This was slightly limited by the clinician's continued guidance of the session's focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No change talk related to the alcohol risk screening or any specific target behavior was elicited or reinforced. The conversation focused on general coping and self-monitoring instead of change motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk was met gently and without argument or reinforcement. However, it was not skillfully redirected to open the door for change.
