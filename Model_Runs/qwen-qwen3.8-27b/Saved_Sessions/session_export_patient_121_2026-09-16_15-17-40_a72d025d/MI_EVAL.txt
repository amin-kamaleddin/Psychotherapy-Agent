# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Sofia’s distress without criticizing her defensive behavior. Acceptance is strong, though it is somewhat structured and less warmly affirming of her resilience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses accurate reflections of Sofia’s ambivalence between connection and safety, helping her feel understood. Empathy could go deeper into shame, fear of humiliation, and the pain of being isolated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on Sofia’s fear and isolation at school and moves clearly toward a specific, manageable behavior. The progression from broad concern to a small hallway step is well maintained.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works jointly with Sofia by asking for her ideas and using her suggestions to shape the plan. Collaboration is strong, but the clinician still offers structured options that can make the session more guided than fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Sofia’s choice, control, and right to decide without pressuring her. Her own boundaries and exit plan are treated as the central guide for the intervention.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively invites Sofia to generate her own steps and resources rather than imposing advice. Evocation could be deeper by exploring values, hopes, and confidence rather than mostly practical safety steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is positioned as the person who knows best what feels safe, and her ideas clearly shape the conversation. The partnership is solid, though the psychiatrist still sets the sequence and offers menus of options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful change talk around a small, controllable step toward reconnection. The skill would be stronger with more explicit development of confidence, commitment, and values-based motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician honors Sofia’s safety concerns without arguing or dismissing them, and often redirects them into low-risk options. The softening would be stronger if it more explicitly linked her safety concerns back to her desire for connection.
