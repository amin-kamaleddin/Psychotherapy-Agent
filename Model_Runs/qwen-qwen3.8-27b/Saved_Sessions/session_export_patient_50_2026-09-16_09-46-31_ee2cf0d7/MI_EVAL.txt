# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, validating, and nonjudgmental stance, normalizing Hana's fear and avoidance. Acceptance is strong but could be slightly deeper and less interpretive in affirming motivation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately connect Hana's behaviors to underlying emotions and bodily sensations. Empathy is solid but could go deeper into her ambivalence and emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on the target behavior of preparing for and taking the exam. It effectively moves from exploring barriers to a concrete, manageable two-minute action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana meaningfully shapes the plan, including the task, time, reward, and backup options. Collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist introduces many specific techniques that could be more client-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through frequent choices, permission to stop, and client-owned decisions. The psychiatrist avoids pressure and reinforces Hana's control over the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Hana's values and motivation, especially around passing the certification. However, the session shifts quickly into planning and technique, limiting deeper evocation of change talk and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with Hana's preferences guiding the direction and plan. Partnership could be further strengthened by emphasizing more of Hana's own coping strategies and ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms small steps and elicits some value-based change talk. There are missed opportunities to deepen, expand, and consistently develop Hana's change talk throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled respectfully without argument or invalidation, and concern is reframed in a supportive way. The session could more consistently redirect focus toward values, goals, and change language.
