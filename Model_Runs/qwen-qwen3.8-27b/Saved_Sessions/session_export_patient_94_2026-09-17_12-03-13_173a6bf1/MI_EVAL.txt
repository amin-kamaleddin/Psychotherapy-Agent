# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard without judgment throughout the session. Even when Maria pushes back or expresses resistance, responses remain warm, respectful, and non-judgmental.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflection of Maria's internal conflicts, emotional shifts, and specific concerns. Reflections are non-formulaic and track her journey from resistant and hurried to slightly more open.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear and consistent focus on the target behavior throughout, with a well-structured session arc. Could be slightly stronger by more explicitly tying Maria's stated values (autonomy, self-management) to the specific action of inhaler use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maria's ideas consistently shape the session's direction, with the psychiatrist following her leads rather than driving the conversation. The session is a genuine partnership where the client's contributions substantially influence what happens next.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Maria's autonomy, never pressuring her to commit or creating urgency beyond what she expressed. The final statement 'Go be on time' powerfully removes even implicit clinical pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Maria rather than providing reasons for change, using open questions that draw out her own thinking and self-efficacy language. The final summary is purely evocative, reflecting Maria's own words and choices back to her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Maria, never assuming the expert role or providing information unilaterally. The session is genuinely shaped by Maria's contributions, which become the framework for the entire interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reinforces change talk by asking Maria to define her own terms and explicitly naming her shift in thinking. Could be deeper in eliciting confidence and ability statements regarding her capacity to follow through on her own plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating concerns without arguing or dismissing them. Could be more active in using double-sided reflections to hold both the fear and the motivation simultaneously, particularly around the grandmother's story.
