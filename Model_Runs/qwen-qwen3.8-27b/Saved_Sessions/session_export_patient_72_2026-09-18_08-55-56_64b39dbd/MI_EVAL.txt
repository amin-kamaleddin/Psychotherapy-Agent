# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warm, unconditional positive regard, explicitly pushing back on Leila's self-deprecating language ('it's not silly at all'). There is zero judgment or conditional regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are emotionally precise and often deepen the client's understanding (e.g., 'The waiting version is always shopping for conditions'). The psychiatrist tracks both content and the emotional courage in Leila's disclosures without mischaracterizing her experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a laser-focused trajectory toward a specific, actionable outcome (walking through the YMCA door on Thursday). Every exchange moves toward concrete action with zero topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session exemplifies full collaboration between equals — the therapist contributes almost exclusively reflections and evocative questions while Leila shapes the direction of every segment. The final plan is entirely Leila's architecture; the therapist added no elements.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is pervasive: the client sets the agenda, the therapist never offers advice or recommendations, and all insights are attributed back to Leila. Leila leaves with a plan she built entirely herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist systematically draws out Leila's own motivations, beliefs, and strategies without ever imposing external reasons for change. By the end, Leila has articulated her complete motivational framework — all evoked, never told.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions herself as a collaborator rather than an expert, ceding agenda-setting to Leila and using genuine inquiry language. The power dynamic is equal throughout; the therapist's role is to attend, reflect, and amplify.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and amplified at each stage, from reinforcing her plan's completeness to eliciting her own coping strategy for anticipated self-sabotage. Every motivational statement is drawn from Leila herself and reflected back with amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Barriers are fully validated before being gently moved past (e.g., 'That's a real risk, and you're not pretending it isn't'). The psychiatrist never dismisses, counter-argues, or reinforces sustain talk as insurmountable.
