# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and honors Andrei’s time pressure and boundaries. Acceptance is strong, though it remains somewhat procedural rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Andrei’s practical constraints, values, and need for clean data. Empathy is mainly cognitive and task-focused, with less exploration of underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on a concrete blood pressure plan without drifting into generic advice. The conversation efficiently moves from concern to a specific, testable behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and co-constructs the plan with Andrei rather than imposing a finished solution. Andrei’s ideas directly shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms Andrei’s ownership, boundaries, and decision-making. The plan is framed as Andrei’s own, with no reminders, pressure, or external control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Andrei generates most of the plan, including the drink swap, morning measurement, cue, missed-day rule, and baseline definition. The psychiatrist elicits these ideas rather than giving reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Andrei as the expert on his own schedule, priorities, and practical limits. The session is shaped by the client’s contributions rather than an external framework.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively reflects and reinforces Andrei’s readiness, commitment, and capability. There is less explicit amplification of deeper values, confidence, or the meaning of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Andrei’s time constraints and incorporates them into the plan. Sustain talk is managed well, but could be further pivoted toward richer change talk and ambivalence resolution.
