# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance. The tone is warm, though occasionally more clinical than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Empathic responses are present but limited, often shifting quickly to explanation or advice. There are missed opportunities to reflect the client's frustration, guilt, and self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on weight loss, exercise, dietary change, and consistency. The direction is strong, though it leans more toward task completion than the MI process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial collaboration, as the client chooses the phrase, cue, tracking method, and review time. However, the psychiatrist still directs much of the agenda and method.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by offering choices, reducing pressure, and emphasizing flexibility. However, many options are therapist-generated rather than fully client-driven.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is limited, with many questions focused on choosing among therapist-provided options. There is little effort to draw out the client's own values, reasons, or motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session has a collaborative surface, but the psychiatrist frequently assumes the expert role. The balance leans toward counselor-led problem-solving rather than true MI partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk appears at times, especially around progress and willingness to try a phrase. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen, amplify, or summarize it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and gently redirected without confrontation. The responses are helpful, but the technique is not consistently skillful or nuanced.
