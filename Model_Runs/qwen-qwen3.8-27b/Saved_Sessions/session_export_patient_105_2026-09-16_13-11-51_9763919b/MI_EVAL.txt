# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive throughout. Acceptance is generally strong but sometimes feels more functional than deeply warm or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Naomi's physical anxiety and fear of being seen as unprepared. The counselor sometimes moves quickly to problem-solving, limiting deeper exploration of shame or self-judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on managing anxiety during shoots and practical next steps. Direction is steady but could better connect to broader goals, values, and reasons for change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and adapts the plan to Naomi's preferences and concerns. Collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist still generates much of the practical content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi's choice is repeatedly affirmed, and the plan is framed as low-pressure and optional. The counselor is still quite directive in offering specific tools and steps, which limits full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some effective questions that draw out preference and commitment. Evocation is limited by the counselor's frequent provision of solutions rather than exploring Naomi's values, confidence, or internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and asks Naomi to choose among options, supporting collaboration. However, the psychiatrist often assumes the expert problem-solving role, making the session more counselor-guided than fully power-shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi offers some change talk, including willingness to try a small experiment. The counselor relies more on concrete options than on drawing out Naomi's own deeper reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor normalizes Naomi's doubts and reduces pressure by framing the plan as low-stakes. There is limited use of double-sided reflections or strategic redirection of sustain talk into change talk.
