# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, affirming Richard’s right to decide. Acceptance is slightly procedural and could more explicitly affirm Richard as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures ambivalence and relational concerns well. Empathy would be stronger with deeper validation of anger, fear, and loss of routine or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking in the car and avoids drifting into marital conflict or health lecturing. It strategically moves toward a concrete, manageable step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Richard’s ideas and co-constructs a practical step. More open invitations for Richard to set or summarize the next step would strengthen mutual co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a major strength, with repeated emphasis on Richard’s space, choice, and ownership. Pressure and persuasion are avoided, which is well suited to his resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes Richard’s own practical reasons and solutions for change. Evocation would be stronger if it more deeply connected the small step to health, values, or self-respect.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a collaborative stance and invites Richard’s perspective. Some therapist-driven framing and repeated questioning slightly reduce the sense of full partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session successfully elicits concrete change talk, such as keeping the pack in the house and keeping hands on the wheel. It would benefit from more consistent amplification and confidence or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and reframes around Richard’s boundaries. The repeated “not quitting” phrase keeps sustain talk somewhat salient and could be pivoted more skillfully.
