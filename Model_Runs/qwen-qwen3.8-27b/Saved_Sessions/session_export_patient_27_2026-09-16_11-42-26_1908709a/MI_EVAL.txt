# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated respect, warmth, and nonjudgment while normalizing Daniela's fear and hesitation. Some affirmations felt formulaic or generic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking around Daniela's practical concerns and boundaries. The responses focused mainly on content rather than deeper emotions such as shame, embarrassment, or loss of control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target behavior of creating a brief, low-risk diabetes disclosure plan. The conversation lacked a stronger progressive arc from ambivalence to a clearly client-owned next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborated with Daniela to create a usable disclosure line and incorporated her preferences into the final wording. However, much of the problem-solving was still driven by the therapist's scripts and options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supported Daniela's autonomy by offering optional choices and respecting her decision to keep the plan low-risk. Repeated suggestions of specific scripts and next steps could have subtly steered the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist made some effort to elicit Daniela's own concerns, preferences, and motivation. There was limited deeper exploration of her values, identity, confidence, or what disclosure would mean to her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Daniela as the expert on her own voice and allowed her edits to shape the final disclosure line. The session remained somewhat therapist-directed because the psychiatrist frequently offered scripts and options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited some change-oriented language around creating a manageable disclosure line. The therapist often supplied ready-made scripts instead of consistently expanding Daniela's own motivation or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist appropriately avoided arguing with Daniela's hesitation and supported her right to revisit the decision. There were fewer strategic double-sided reflections or value-based turns to soften sustain talk.
