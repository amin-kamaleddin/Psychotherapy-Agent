# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating throughout. Some affirmations are formulaic and miss deeper personal meaning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several accurate reflections show understanding. Deeper fear, ambivalence, and motivation are not consistently reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use and lab-related next steps. Some medical education expands the conversation but remains relevant.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Options and invitations support a collaborative stance. The clinician still provides much of the structure and agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice, control, and noncoercion. The patient’s self-direction is strongly respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open questions elicit the patient’s perspective. The session remains informational and does not deeply draw out reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites input and offers choices. The expert-led information provision limits fuller co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk appears but is not consistently amplified or built upon. This is a major missed opportunity for evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing and reduces threat. Sustain talk is not consistently redirected toward change or used to resolve ambivalence.
