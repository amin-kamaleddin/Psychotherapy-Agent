# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and non-judgmental, with multiple affirmations that normalize the client's struggle and explicitly counter self-criticism. The warmth feels genuine and individualized rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflective listening that captures emotional undercurrents beneath surface content (e.g., identifying the 'how am I gonna find time' question as a shutdown mechanism). Reflections escalate in depth and specificity as the session progresses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful arc from ambivalence exploration to concrete action planning, with each intervention building logically toward the target behavior. Even explorations of internal dialogue are directly in service of removing barriers to action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as a genuine collaboration between equals, with the final plan entirely client-generated and the psychiatrist contributing only reflections, strategic questions, and amplifications. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and freedom of choice, never using directive language and validating 'I'll try' as a real commitment rather than pushing for more. The client's reframe is attributed to himself, not the counselor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key shift in motivation is self-generated by the client, with the counselor creating conditions for insight through strategic questions and amplification rather than providing external reasons for change. No external justification or health statistics are offered.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the expert on his own life, with the counselor never prescribing or directing. Dante's contributions substantially shape the session's direction and outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk, guiding the client from 'I guess I could' to a concrete 'I'll try tonight' with strategic reinforcement at each shift. Future-oriented evocation ('what would you feel coming back inside') produces powerful self-generated change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill by validating the frustration while strategically redirecting through questions that allow the client to resolve his own doubts. Never argues or dismisses, but also never reinforces the sustain talk.
