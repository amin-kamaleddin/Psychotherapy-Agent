# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance, but sometimes shifts into a problem-solving posture that may minimize the client's ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects some ambivalence and key concerns, but does not consistently track or validate the underlying emotions such as exhaustion, defensiveness, and relational strain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session remains focused on alcohol use and home strain, but the move to concrete steps feels somewhat premature before the client's ambivalence is fully explored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds a small plan with the client and incorporates his ideas, but the frequent introduction of counselor-generated options makes the process more guided than fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Autonomy is supported through choice and avoiding direct commands, but continued suggestions after expressed resistance make the autonomy support less consistent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist brings up the client's reasons for change, but the evocation is often interrupted by suggestions and not fully connected to personal values or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inclusive language and client choices support partnership, but frequent therapist-generated options make the interaction more expert-driven than collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits several meaningful change statements, but often moves quickly into planning instead of reflecting, amplifying, or deepening them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing, but does not effectively soften or redirect it through deeper exploration of ambivalence and values.
