# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and validating, often normalizing Naomi’s concerns without judgment. However, the supportive warmth shifts quickly into advice-giving, limiting deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Naomi’s bodily symptoms, worry, and concern about awkwardness. Reflections remain mostly surface-level and do not fully explore the emotional core of her social anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on practical anxiety management and on-shoot coping. The direction is skill-oriented rather than moving toward Naomi’s personal reasons for change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi’s preferences, such as the clip-on fan and strap, influence the plan. Nonetheless, the psychiatrist supplies most of the techniques and structure, making collaboration uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers several choices and accepts hesitation without pressure. Some directive language and detailed prescriptive steps reduce support for Naomi’s own pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides many reasons and solutions instead of eliciting Naomi’s own motivation. More use of Naomi’s values, confidence, and personal goals would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi contributes practical ideas and choices that shape the plan. The psychiatrist still acts as the primary expert, supplying most of the strategies and structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few evocative prompts and affirmations of Naomi’s willingness. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify Naomi’s own reasons for change or expand her change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist validates Naomi’s doubts and fears without arguing. However, the response often adds more problem-solving rather than redirecting ambivalence toward change.
