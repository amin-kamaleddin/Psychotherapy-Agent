# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and autonomy-supportive stance. The warmth is slightly efficient and could more deeply normalize the emotional and cultural complexity of the situation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The reflections accurately capture Isabella's core concerns and cognitive perspective. Deeper emotional reflections, such as guilt, fear of judgment, or cultural loss, would further strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the target behavior, alcohol use during pregnancy. It moves logically from concerns to information, practical alternatives, and a behavior-specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates effectively by offering options and inviting Isabella to select, adapt, and own the plan. This creates a strong co-constructive rather than directive interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently emphasized through language such as "without pressure," "keeps the decision yours," and "it's your choice." The psychiatrist avoids controlling language and supports client-led follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encourages Isabella to articulate her own reasons for change and preferences. More client-generated, value-centered exploration could deepen motivation before moving into practical planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Isabella is treated as the expert on her own life, relationships, and preferences. Her choices substantially shape the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces practical change talk related to readiness and problem-solving. Deeper development of values, confidence, and self-efficacy would strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk skillfully without arguing or dismissing Isabella's concerns. More consistent use of double-sided reflections would further soften sustain talk.
