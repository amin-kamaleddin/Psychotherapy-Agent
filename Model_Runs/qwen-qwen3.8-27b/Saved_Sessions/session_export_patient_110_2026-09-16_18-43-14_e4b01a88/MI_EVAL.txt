# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance. Acceptance is strong, though it could be deepened with more explicit normalization of ambivalence and unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client’s values and ambivalence about treatment burden. Empathy could be stronger by exploring the deeper emotional meaning behind the fear of failure and impact on independence and marriage.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on cholesterol treatment adherence and a manageable lifestyle change. The direction is collaborative, goal-guided, and free of meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client’s values and concerns clearly shape the conversation and final plan. Collaboration could be strengthened by reducing therapist-led structure and evaluative language to emphasize more explicit co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports the client’s right to choose and avoids pressure or over-prescription. The plan is framed as something the client and his wife can adopt if it feels workable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively elicit the client’s own motivations, plans, and self-management ideas. Evocation could be stronger by giving more space for the client to fully articulate the personal meaning of success.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative, inclusive language and invites the client’s perspective throughout. Partnership could be maximized with more explicit power sharing and less appraiser-like evaluation of the client’s ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist actively evokes and builds the client’s own reasons, plans, and confidence for change. Cultivation could be stronger with more deliberate amplification and structured evocation of desire, ability, reasons, need, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids challenging the client and respectfully reflects concerns about treatment burden and failure. Softening could be more explicit through double-sided reflections that connect autonomy and values to change.
