# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental while honoring Sofia's privacy and choices. However, repeated advice-giving and mildly evaluative comments prevent a fully unconditional stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects Sofia's frustration, embarrassment, and distrust reasonably but often remains surface-level and repetitive. Deeper emotions such as shame, fear, loneliness, and betrayal are not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the peer conflict, rumors, and a concrete next step around communicating with Maya. Repeated summaries, safety reminders, and brief coping suggestions occasionally fragment the focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia's input and asks her to choose among options, showing some collaborative intent. However, the counselor remains the primary problem-solver, and much of the plan content is clinician-generated rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia's control is frequently affirmed, including her decision not to be checked in and her choice to leave. The support is strong but is occasionally undercut by prescriptive scripts and backup plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few appropriate evocation questions, but the counselor quickly shifts into providing solutions and scripts. This limits the development of Sofia's own motivation, values, and confidence in change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses collaborative language and respects Sofia's choices, creating some partnership. Nevertheless, the interaction is uneven in power because the psychiatrist supplies much of the content while Sofia mostly responds.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor makes some attempts to evoke change, such as asking what Sofia would like to be different and what small step she might try. Overall, the session relies more on supplying strategies and scripts than on helping Sofia generate her own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk is acknowledged without mockery, including the idea that skipping feels safer. The counselor does not sufficiently use double-sided reflections or help her resolve ambivalence between wanting connection and fearing rejection.
