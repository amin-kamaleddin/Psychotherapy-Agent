# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently showed unconditional positive regard, respect, and nonjudgmental support. Affirmations were frequent and strengthened Marcus's self-awareness without blame or invalidation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used accurate and frequent reflective listening to understand Marcus's ambivalence. Empathy could be deeper in capturing underlying shame, frustration, or fatigue related to past weight-loss failures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on increasing physical activity in a sustainable, realistic way. Each question moved the conversation toward concrete, client-owned action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaved as an equal partner rather than an authority figure. The plan was co-constructed using Marcus's ideas rather than prescribed recommendations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marcus's choice, ownership, and agency were consistently highlighted. The psychiatrist avoided pressure, moralizing, or directive language while encouraging self-directed decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited motivation from within Marcus instead of providing external reasons for change. Marcus generated his own values, preferences, and commitments throughout the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Marcus as the expert on his own life and built the plan from his suggestions. Power sharing was strong and the session felt genuinely client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marcus consistently expressed desire, reasons, ability, and commitment to change. The psychiatrist skillfully elicited and reinforced this language with affirmations and reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument or minimization. The psychiatrist gently redirected discouragement and fear of failure toward realistic, supportive next steps.
