# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful tone, validating Ryan's contradictory feelings without labeling or pushing back. Could be deepened by more explicit normalization of the struggle and naming Ryan's worth beyond his behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking throughout, accurately reflecting Ryan's emotional experience and internal tensions. Could explore the emotional experience of the incident more fully to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on Ryan's drinking behavior with a logical progression from ambivalence to discrepancy to a concrete solution. Could be more deliberately structured by explicitly tying back to the presenting concern at strategic points.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the conversation as a joint exploration and co-constructs decisions rather than prescribing them. Could include more explicit co-construction of the agenda and evidence that Ryan's ideas are actively shaping the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout dimension—consistently reinforces Ryan's autonomy and ownership, never prescribes action, and ensures every suggestion emerges from Ryan himself. Powerful autonomy-supportive language such as 'you generated that yourself' highlights Ryan's own agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Ryan through open-ended questions that draw out his own values, concerns, and solutions. Never provides external reasons for change, instead helping Ryan discover his own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Ryan's input and avoids an expert-driven approach. Could be more explicit in sharing power, acknowledging Ryan's expertise in his own life, or negotiating the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest dimension—the psychiatrist consistently draws out and amplifies Ryan's own change language, including textbook evocation of ability change talk ('proof that I can do it'). Skilled reflection of discrepancy and amplification of self-generated insights throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating commonality without dismissing Ryan's concerns, gently reframing rather than confronting. Could push slightly further in rolling with certain resistance moments to maximize softening effect.
