# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validating Elena's fears without judgment and explicitly reframing pacing as healthy rather than avoidance. Warmth feels genuine with no hint of disappointment or impatience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that captures deeper identity themes beneath surface concerns, such as the self-sufficiency threat and the weight behind 'my body, my future.' Reflections are complex, accurate, and go well beyond surface-level paraphrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior while following Elena's lead, with logical progression from processing the scare to planning next steps. Could be strengthened in the final moment where Elena requests specific information, which risks shifting into information-giving mode.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is structured as a genuine joint exploration with equal power dynamics, using language like 'I'm here with you' and 'would it help to think through that together?' Elena's ideas directly shape the session's trajectory throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never pressures Elena to decide, explicitly frames pacing as healthy rather than resistance, and consistently communicates that Elena is in charge of her own timeline. Offers choice in next steps and respects 'maybe a week' without pushing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within Elena rather than providing reasons externally, with all motivation appearing from Elena's own mouth. Could have probed deeper on commitment statements like 'I want to go because I actually want to' to elicit stronger change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions herself as a co-explorer rather than authority, using questions that invite Elena to define the agenda and set direction. Elena's contributions substantially shape the session at every turn.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Elena's own change-oriented language and amplifies forward-moving statements, but could have gone deeper using DARN-CAT frameworks to explore confidence and commitment. Change talk is evoked but not always strategically expanded with follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully with double-sided reflections and never creates an adversarial dynamic around Elena's resistance. The main area for improvement is more actively pivoting 'not ready' language toward change talk through strategic questions.
