# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a nonjudgmental stance and respects the client’s choices, but early medical advice and repeated safety reminders create a paternalistic undercurrent. Explicit affirmation of the client’s experience as a person remains limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but mostly cognitive, missing deeper feelings of frustration, autonomy, and being medically overridden. Empathy is repetitive and does not progressively deepen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on asthma and inhaler use without drifting into unrelated topics. However, the direction is repetitive and compliance-focused rather than strategically guiding the client toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Despite some collaborative-sounding phrases, the session is largely counselor-driven and does not effectively co-create the agenda. The client’s time constraint and preferences do not meaningfully shape the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently uses explicit autonomy-supportive language and avoids overt pressure or commands. Repeated safety reminders after the client states she is leaving undercut the message that she is truly in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is limited, with most of the session focused on providing information and safety guidance rather than eliciting the client’s own motivations. The client’s implicit motivation and concerns remain largely unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor offers some choice, but the session remains expert-driven rather than co-created. The client’s own expertise and stated preferences do not meaningfully shape the direction of the interview.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are minimal attempts to cultivate change talk, with an appropriate opening followed by a shift into medical information and scripts. The counselor misses opportunities to build on the client’s research and intent to manage her asthma.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor avoids confrontation but uses corrective framing and repeated safety advice that may strengthen resistance. There is little use of double-sided reflections or strategic softening to help resolve ambivalence.
