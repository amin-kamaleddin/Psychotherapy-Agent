# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, never judging or labeling. Warmth feels individualized to Marcus's situation, from acknowledging his fullness of life to celebrating his progress as genuinely earned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and nuanced, capturing emotional meaning beneath surface content. Standout moments include reflecting the 'am I allowed to believe this will work?' subtext and the tender quality of 'I just... stopped.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity through enjoyable, sustainable methods while following Marcus's lead. The session has a logical arc from discouragement to concrete commitment without drifting or rushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marcus's ideas shape the session direction at every decision point, from identifying activities to choosing logistics. The tone is that of two equal partners problem-solving, not a clinician delivering an intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly removes obligation and permission throughout ('you're not on any hook,' 'you don't need my permission'). All suggestions are framed as invitations and all commitments as Marcus's own choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external reasons for change; all motivation is drawn from within Marcus. The deeper reframe from 'fix my body' to 'fix my presence' is entirely Marcus's own insight, facilitated by reflective questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively positions Marcus as the expert on his own life and invites him to be the strategist for his own behavior. The session is clearly co-directed by Marcus's contributions at every stage.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk through open questions and reflective affirmations ('that's not nothing,' 'a genuine shift'). The session ends with strong commitment language that was clearly evoked rather than prescribed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without arguing, minimizing, or countering—instead reflecting meaning and pivoting to partnership. Consistently acknowledges the validity of Marcus's concerns while creating space for change to emerge naturally.
