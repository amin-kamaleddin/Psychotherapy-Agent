### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** The session flows naturally but meanders between topics without a clear arc or strategic progression toward a specific target. No summarizing of themes, collaborative focus identification, or concrete next step was offered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance that treats Liyana as a co-expert, with no lecturing, advising, or prescribing. Could be more actively demonstrated through explicit co-construction (e.g., 'So what I'm hearing is… does that sound right?').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoids advice, pressure, or pathologizing, with strong statements like 'You don't have to solve this today.' Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Liyana's ownership of her choices within her ambivalence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are present but limited in depth and frequency. Lacks scaling questions, exploration of past strengths or values, and consistent drawing out of Liyana's own motivations and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, non-directive stance with inclusive language and no expert-driven pushing. Partnership is somewhat implicit; could be more explicit in acknowledging Liyana's expertise in her own experience and negotiating the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk (desire, recognition, goal statement) but does not consistently build on or amplify it. Lacks follow-up evocative questions and exploration of confidence or ability for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers some reframing, but does not actively employ MI-specific techniques such as double-sided reflections or strategic pivots toward Liyana's own agency and evidence of capability.
