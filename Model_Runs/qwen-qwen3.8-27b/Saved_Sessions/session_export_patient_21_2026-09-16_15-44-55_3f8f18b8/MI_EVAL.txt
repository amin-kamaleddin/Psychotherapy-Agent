# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently expressed warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental regard. The client’s discomfort, motivation, and hesitation were all received without pressure or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflected the client’s ambivalence and practical concerns accurately. Deeper emotional exploration of fear, frustration, or past failure could have strengthened the empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stayed focused on increasing physical activity in a realistic and sustainable way. The target behavior was clearly narrowed from general movement to a specific evening routine.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was genuinely collaborative, with the client’s ideas shaping the plan. The psychiatrist used shared language and built on the client’s lived experience rather than imposing an agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client’s right to choose was repeatedly affirmed without pressure. The psychiatrist supported the client’s self-defined, low-pressure goal without trying to raise the standard.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited the client’s own motivations, ideas, and plan. A 5 would require fewer therapist-generated frames and more consistent use of the client’s own language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client was positioned as the expert on her own body, energy, and realistic limits. The session was not directive, and the client’s contributions directly shaped the direction of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encouraged the client to generate several change statements and realistic action ideas. More deliberate eliciting of confidence, commitment, and future-self language could improve this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handled the client’s doubts without arguing or dismissing them. Slightly faster and more strategic redirection toward values, strengths, or future self could have strengthened this criterion.
