# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and normalizing, but some corrective or deficit-framed statements reduce the warmth and explicit positive regard needed for a higher level.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client’s experience and emotions, though they sometimes remain at the surface and do not fully explore deeper guilt, fear, or values conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the target behavior, but occasionally drifts into implementation details that pull it away from the client’s underlying motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client actively shapes the plan, especially the car pause and keys-in-cupholder cue, though the psychiatrist still presents pre-selected options more often than fully open-ended collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice, permission, and the client’s preferences, but some prescriptive plans and repeated suggestions limit full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evocative questions are used, but the conversation often shifts too quickly to advice, reframes, or solutions instead of deeply exploring the client’s own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A collaborative stance is maintained and the client’s input clearly shapes the session, though the psychiatrist still takes a somewhat leading role in framing options and interventions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited early on, but it is not consistently built on, amplified, or reflected back in a way that strengthens the client’s motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges the client’s doubts and resistance well, but does not consistently redirect sustain talk into change or use double-sided reflections to soften resistance.
