### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with statements that normalize Julian's stress and uncertainty. Somewhat situational and practical rather than deeply affirming, with few explicit affirmations of his honesty or willingness.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections tracking both Julian's concerns and his preferred low-pressure style. Effectively captures his fear of mistakes, deadline anxiety, desire to minimize effort, and preference for texting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with logical pacing from broad problem to concrete client-generated action. Only minor redundancy near the end where two prompts ask very similar questions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative joint problem-solving where the plan is entirely co-created using Julian's own ideas. Final text is client-generated, confirming he is an active participant rather than passive recipient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices (now/later/texting mom, tonight/later/backup) without pressure for immediate action. Respects Julian's control when he says "won't" and avoids all directive or advisory language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions successfully draw out Julian's own solutions, culminating in a strong client-generated action plan. Remains focused on practical problem-solving rather than exploring deeper values, confidence, or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens with "Let's look at it together" and maintains collaborative stance with repeated "What do you think?" invitations. Julian's contributions directly shape every step, showing high power sharing and mutual engagement.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client generates several forward-moving statements including texting mom, timing after dinner, and a backup plan. Change talk is mainly logistical; lacks amplification of change statements or exploration of desire, ability, or values.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Skillfully reflects and works with avoidance statements ("calling feels like too much," "paperwork hassle") without argument or moralizing. Could strengthen by using double-sided reflections that connect the avoidance to Julian's own goals.
