# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, never labeling, judging, or disapproving. Warmth is genuine and age-appropriate; defensive statements are met with gentle exploration rather than pushback.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture the emotional architecture of Sofia's experience with remarkable depth, including complex reflections that name underlying loneliness and loss of control. The psychiatrist also honors discomfort with genuine pauses rather than rushing through emotional beats.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical flow from rapport through exploration to mechanism identification. However, the session lacks a strategic arc toward a concrete behavioral goal and does not preview or summarize the direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a joint exploration, using hypothesis language and explicitly rejecting the expert role. The session feels like two people figuring something out together rather than a professional evaluating a patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never gives advice, pressures, or uses persuasive language; the client sets the agenda, defines success, and states preferences freely. Autonomy support is woven naturally into every interaction, including when the client is resistant.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Sofia's own insights and language through open, exploratory questions rather than providing reasons for change. However, evocation is not specifically targeted at eliciting desire, ability, need, or commitment talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Language and stance consistently communicate that Sofia is the expert on her own life, with the client setting the agenda and defining success. However, the psychiatrist still drives the session structure, preventing a full demonstration of equality in shaping the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers several statements of change talk, but the psychiatrist does not strategically reflect them back to amplify her own motivation or elicit confidence and commitment language. The session ends at the exploration stage without evoking next steps or self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by reflecting underlying experience without arguing or reinforcing the 'everyone is against me' narrative. Could be more active in explicitly softening statements that imply permanence or inevitability of her reactions.
