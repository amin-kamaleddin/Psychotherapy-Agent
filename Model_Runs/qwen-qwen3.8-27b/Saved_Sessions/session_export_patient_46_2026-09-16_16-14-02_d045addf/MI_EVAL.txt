# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Ethan’s concerns without criticism or blame. Acceptance is strong, though it could more explicitly affirm Ethan’s self-awareness and maturity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflective listening that captures Ethan’s ambivalence and social concerns. Empathy is effective but could deepen further by addressing shame, fear of rejection, and identity-level meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on alcohol use in social settings and moves purposefully toward a concrete behavior plan. Direction is clear, purposeful, and clinically well anchored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates effectively by building the plan from Ethan’s own ideas and suggestions. Collaboration could be strengthened by more explicitly framing the session as a joint effort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by asking Ethan to think for himself and avoiding coercive language. This area could be stronger by more explicitly affirming that the decision is entirely Ethan’s.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently evokes Ethan’s own motives, options, and plan rather than imposing reasons for change. Evocation could be deepened by more explicitly linking his behavior to personal values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Ethan’s perspective and follows his leads in developing the plan. Partnership is strong, though the therapist still provides a fairly structured direction throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently asks questions that lead Ethan to generate concrete change talk and actionable steps. Change talk could be further strengthened by more explicitly amplifying desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Ethan’s sustain talk about fitting in without arguing or dismissing it. This area could be more effective with deliberate double-sided reflections that gently tilt toward change.
