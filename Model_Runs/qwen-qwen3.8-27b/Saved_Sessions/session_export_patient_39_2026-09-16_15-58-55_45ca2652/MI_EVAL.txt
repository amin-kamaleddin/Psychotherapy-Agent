# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental, warm, and validating, with phrases that normalize Elias's experience. Slightly formulaic and less explicit in affirming character, effort, or worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex and double-sided reflections accurately track ambivalence and changing goals. Repetitive openers and limited probing of underlying emotion keep it from being fully penetrating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Keeps focus on physical activity with a logical progression from general activity to mailbox, trash cans, and door. Repeated near-identical questions create circularity and weaken momentum toward an actionable commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine back-and-forth dialogue builds on Elias's language and follows his lead. Collaboration remains surface-level because repeated questions and lack of explicit co-planning limit power sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions consistently return choice, values, and perspective to Elias without advice or pressure. Could be strengthened by explicit statements that he is in charge of his decisions and body.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively elicit Elias's own reasons and definitions of success. The pattern becomes repetitive and lacks strategic variety, reducing the evocative depth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Elias as the expert on his own experience and avoids directive advice. Partnership is present but implicit, with little explicit affirmation of his authority or shared agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is evoked through small goals such as mailbox, ten minutes, and doing it twice. It is not consistently amplified, summarized, or built into deeper commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses some gentle redirection away from all-or-nothing thinking. It sometimes mirrors fear or failure language without actively reframing the function of sustain talk.
