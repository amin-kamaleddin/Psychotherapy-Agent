# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent, warm, and unconditional positive regard throughout the session. When Anya minimizes her gambling or expresses self-criticism, the psychiatrist validates rather than challenges or dismisses.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses sophisticated complex reflections that capture the emotional and cognitive complexity of Anya's ambivalence, going beyond surface content to identify deeper identity concerns. Reflections simultaneously name internal conflict, cognitive distortion, and emotional tension.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and logical progression from ambivalence to values to concrete change steps without drifting off-topic. However, the session sequence is primarily psychiatrist-driven rather than emerging from the client's spontaneous direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive, curious language and frames the session as a joint exploration rather than an assessment. The structure remains primarily counselor-led in a one-directional ask-respond-reflect pattern, limiting full mutual exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently non-directive with no prescriptive language; all suggestions are framed as exploratory questions. Could be strengthened with more explicit acknowledgment of Anya's right to choose her own path and ownership over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight, motivation, and concrete plan in the session is elicited from Anya's own mouth through carefully crafted questions. The psychiatrist never provides a single reason for change or states a benefit of quitting.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative stance with exploratory language and no expert positioning. Could be elevated with more explicit power-sharing language and client-led agenda setting rather than the psychiatrist setting the session trajectory.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully connects Anya's present behavior to her stated values and future self, naturally eliciting change talk in virtually every exchange. Actively amplifies and reflects back Anya's own commitment language and concrete planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating without reinforcing, using dual-focus reflections that hold contradictory truths. Stops short of actively redirecting or reframing sustain talk toward change with a gentle pivot.
