### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target issue with a logical arc from venting to understanding to concrete action plan. Some moments linger in emotional processing without quite pushing toward forward movement, though the session ends with a clear next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is entirely co-constructed with Tariq; his ideas (three questions, approach, timing, success markers) drive the session's direction. Language throughout is collaborative ('let's figure this out,' 'what would that look like to you').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never prescribes, advises, or pressures; the action plan is entirely Tariq's own choice and framing. Key moves like 'What would feel worth your energy right now?' and 'You know this place better than I do' reinforce his ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight comes from Tariq himself — he defines fairness, articulates the plan, names his own shift, and arrives at his action independently. Questions are consistently designed to draw out internal motivation rather than provide external reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sets a collaborative tone from the opening ('not to lecture') and consistently acknowledges Tariq's expertise over his own situation. The power dynamic is genuinely equal — Tariq's ideas drive the session's direction throughout.

**Comments:** Skillfully reflects back Tariq's own words to highlight shifts and helps him articulate a specific behavioral intention. Could have pushed further on confidence and ability talk, such as exploring past successes or rating his confidence in the conversation going well.
