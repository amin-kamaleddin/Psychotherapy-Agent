# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating stance throughout the session. The approach is strong but somewhat templated, with much of the warmth directed at managing rejections rather than affirming strengths beyond the task.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides good reflective listening, especially around the social and identity-level meaning of weekend drinking. Reflections are generally accurate but often brief and shift quickly to advice rather than deeper validation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stays tightly focused on the target behavior of weekend alcohol use with clear momentum toward a concrete plan. The session becomes slightly scattered across multiple micro-strategies, diluting a single clean direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint and iterative, with the final plan genuinely shaped by the client’s preferences. Collaboration is strong, though the clinician still generates many options rather than the client driving the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly protects the client’s freedom of choice and accepts rejections without pressure. Autonomy support is a relative strength, though the detailed, layered plan remains somewhat prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some client change language but does not deeply draw out intrinsic motivation using core MI tools. The clinician supplies reasons for change rather than evoking them, which competes with the MI spirit.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is real partnership at the planning level, and the client’s input shapes the final plan. However, the clinician remains the main source of knowledge and tactics, making power-sharing uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk emerges and is acknowledged, but it is not consistently or skillfully amplified. The session lacks strategic evocation, rulers, and values-based questioning to strengthen the client’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with the client’s sustain talk and receives resistance without judgment. However, it relies too much on advice and repeated strategy generation rather than double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance.
