# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, explicitly normalizing Camila's experience and affirming her self-awareness. The warmth occasionally feels slightly formulaic, and there is limited deeper validation of the emotional weight of her social anxiety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent empathic understanding with complex reflections that accurately capture both sides of ambivalence. Reflections are specific, emotionally attuned, and progressively deepen throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on alcohol use with logical progression from ambivalence through consequences to exploring change. Could have been slightly more strategic in progressing toward a concrete next step or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with Camila's input genuinely shaping the conversation direction. Could be elevated by more explicit co-construction of next steps or the psychiatrist sharing their own perspective to demonstrate equality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Camila's autonomy, never prescribing or pressuring, framing any change as her own choice. The autonomy support is woven naturally into the conversation rather than feeling forced or mechanical.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes Camila's own motivations and reasons for change through strategic questioning, never providing external reasons or lecturing. The evocation is well-timed and builds progressively, leading Camila from confusion to clear articulation of her own goals and barriers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative, non-hierarchical stance, explicitly positioning Camila as the expert in her own life. Could be strengthened by more explicit power-sharing language and inviting Camila's perspective on the process itself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes change talk through strategic questioning, eliciting substantial self-generated motivation from Camila. Could have more explicitly reflected back and amplified change talk, and elicited more ability and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk thoughtfully without arguing or dismissing, validating fears while gently keeping the door open for change. Could have used double-sided reflections more explicitly and strategically to help resolve ambivalence.
