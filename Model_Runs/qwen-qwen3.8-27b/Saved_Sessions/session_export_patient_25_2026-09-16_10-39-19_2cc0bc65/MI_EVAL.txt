# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, affirmation, and a nonjudgmental tone. Acceptance is strong, though somewhat procedural and plan-focused rather than deeply emotionally attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are adequate reflections of Daniel’s concerns, but the psychiatrist often shifts quickly to problem-solving. Deeper empathy around social awkwardness, family responsibility, and self-respect is limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is highly focused on the target behavior and moves toward a specific, actionable plan. The direction is clear, practical, and consistently tied to limiting alcohol at social gatherings.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniel contributes meaningful details, but the psychiatrist frequently supplies lists of scripts and options. The interaction is collaborative, though somewhat expert-driven rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choices and avoids coercion, but the level of therapist-generated planning may reduce Daniel’s sense of full ownership. More explicit autonomy support would strengthen the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation is elicited through importance/confidence ratings and preference questions. However, the psychiatrist often moves from evocation to advising, limiting deeper exploration of Daniel’s own values and reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial collaboration, with Daniel shaping parts of the plan. The psychiatrist remains the primary generator of ideas and scripts, which reduces the sense that Daniel is the expert on his own change.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniel offers meaningful change talk, and the psychiatrist reinforces some of it. However, the change talk is not consistently amplified, reflected, or explored in depth before scripts are introduced.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not reinforce sustain talk and responds to concerns with normalization and redirection. However, sustain talk is more managed than deeply softened or transformed using MI-style reflections.
