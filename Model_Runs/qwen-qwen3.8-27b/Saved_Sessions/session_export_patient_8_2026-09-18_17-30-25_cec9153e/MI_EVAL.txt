# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates respect and nonjudgment, normalizing Amina's experience and framing her doubts as healthy. Falls slightly short of a 5 by not explicitly normalizing the difficulty of change itself in the face of her self-doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional empathic understanding, accurately capturing both cognitive and emotional dimensions of Amina's experience. Reflections are precise, progressively deepen, and are therapeutically strategic—advancing the conversation toward insight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from ambivalence to a concrete plan. Slightly linear and could benefit from a summary before transitioning to planning to ensure alignment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is co-constructed in real time, with Amina's ideas driving the direction and the plan entirely self-generated. The psychiatrist serves as a facilitator, using inclusive language and inviting her perspective throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is woven throughout every interaction, never prescribing or pressuring, and explicitly affirming Amina's ownership of the plan. The framing of the plan as an experiment rather than an obligation is particularly strong.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Amina rather than providing reasons for change, with the 'light' moment being entirely her discovery. Could be more systematic with additional evocative questions on importance and discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveys that expertise about change resides within Amina, with the power dynamic firmly equal. Amina's contributions substantially shape the session direction, making partnership the primary mode of interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes multiple forms of change talk (desire, reason, ability, need) and strategically uses Amina's own language to build momentum. Falls short of a 5 by not eliciting confidence language or scaling questions, and not amplifying change talk with sufficient depth before moving on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with considerable skill by validating while gently pivoting, avoiding counter-arguing and instead redirecting toward underlying needs. Could more actively reframe sustain talk at key moments, such as when Amina worries about checking her phone under the table.
