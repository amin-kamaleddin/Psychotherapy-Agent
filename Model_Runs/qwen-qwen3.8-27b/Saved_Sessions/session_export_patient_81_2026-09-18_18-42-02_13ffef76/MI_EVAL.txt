### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and nonjudgmental acceptance throughout, with explicit statements like 'No judgment' that are crucial given the client's fear of being labeled. Could be slightly more explicit in affirming Lukas as a whole person beyond his use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathy with nuanced, accurate reflections that capture both content and feeling, including complex reflections of ambivalence and identity tension. The psychiatrist picks up on subtle cues (pauses, word choices) and explores them skillfully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear strategic progression from practical concerns (money, rent) through emotional themes (identity, belonging) to the specific behavior (meth use), maintaining coherent momentum throughout. Could be strengthened by more explicit linking to change goals or concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and 'I' statements to model vulnerability and equality, co-constructing explorations with Lukas. Could be stronger by more explicitly inviting Lukas to co-create the session's direction or goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook autonomy support with explicit permissions ('You don't have to name it'), reinforcement of client control ('at your pace'), and a consistently non-directive approach that never pressures for change. Could serve as a model for MI autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation that consistently draws out Lukas's own insights rather than providing them, using questions that open space for self-awareness and self-assessment. Honors the MI principle that change must come from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with appropriate pacing and inclusive language, positioning Lukas as the expert on his own experience. Could be deepened by more explicitly acknowledging Lukas's wisdom and inviting him to shape the session structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully builds on Lukas's emerging change language (e.g., 'this time it feels different') and evokes self-awareness without being pushy. Could be strengthened by more explicitly eliciting DARN-CAT language and reflecting back change statements to amplify them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by reflecting it accurately and exploring underlying needs (belonging, identity) rather than challenging or dismissing. Could be more strategic in softening the 'I'm not one of those people' belief by reflecting the tension between self-concept and behavioral reality.
