# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a consistent nonjudgmental and affirming stance throughout the session. There is no moralizing, labeling, or pressure regarding the patient’s drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses reflective listening effectively to acknowledge the patient’s experience and concerns. However, the reflections could go deeper to better capture the emotional undercurrents of loneliness, low mood, and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on reducing alcohol use and developing a concrete plan. Each exchange logically moves the conversation toward a workable, client-generated goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor works as an equal partner, inviting input and co-creating the intervention plan. The patient’s suggestions are directly incorporated into the final plan and follow-up structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently affirms the patient’s right to choose and control her own goals. Permissive language and acceptance of the patient’s chosen level of change strongly support autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively draws out the patient’s own motivations and confidence using rulers and open questions. A slightly more evocative approach would involve asking the patient to generate coping options before the counselor offers suggestions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor conveys that the patient is the expert on her own life and choices. The session is guided by the patient’s priorities and decisions rather than an imposed expert agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses scaling and open-ended questions to elicit meaningful reasons for change and planning. The session could benefit from further amplifying and deepening the patient’s own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor appropriately acknowledges the patient’s ambivalence and realistic concerns without challenging her. The use of more explicit, double-sided reflections could further soften sustain talk.
