# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently shows nonjudgmental respect for Marisol's choice to keep the piercing and her right to decide about her body. Acceptance is strong but slightly procedural and formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor shows adequate perspective-taking regarding Marisol's autonomy and lack of current symptoms. However, empathy remains mostly surface-level and does not fully name the emotions, values, or frustration behind her statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the lip piercing and dental follow-up, showing clear topic focus. However, the direction feels more administrative and task-driven than strategically MI-oriented toward Marisol's own concerns or motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some collaborative moments, such as asking Marisol to choose a summary format or wording. Overall, though, the counselor retains much control over the agenda and repeatedly offers clinical plans despite Marisol's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor strongly supports Marisol's autonomy by affirming her choice and avoiding coercive language. The support is slightly weakened by continued advice and monitoring instructions after Marisol signals she does not want a plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor begins with an appropriate open question but does not follow up by drawing out Marisol's own motivations, values, or reasons for engaging. The session relies more on providing information than eliciting client-driven meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites some input and offers choices, indicating a partnership stance. However, the interaction remains somewhat expert-driven, especially when continuing structured clinical language after Marisol says she is ready to end.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited effort to elicit or expand Marisol's own language in favor of change or self-care. Her statements about taking care of the piercing and watching for problems are not reflected back as resources or agency.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Marisol's sustain talk but does not actively soften or redirect it. The conversation remains anchored in dental risks and monitoring rather than exploring ambivalence or possible middle-ground options.
