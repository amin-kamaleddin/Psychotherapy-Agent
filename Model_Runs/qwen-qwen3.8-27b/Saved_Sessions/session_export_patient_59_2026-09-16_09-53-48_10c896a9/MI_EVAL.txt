# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a nonjudgmental, respectful tone, normalizes Jamal’s perspective, and honors his autonomy. Acceptance is somewhat brief and task-focused rather than deeply affirming his struggle or shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and capture key content. They remain mostly factual and miss underlying irritation, defensiveness, fear, or autonomy threat.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on marijuana risk reduction and moves toward a concrete, realistic action step. Some initial questions are broad, and linking sustain talk to consequences could be more strategic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed respectfully, with choices offered and a cooperative tone. The therapist still controls much of the agenda, leaving Jamal somewhat passive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy support is strong through repeated affirmations of choice and acceptance of a simple plan. Some directive advising about specific if-then rules could be softened by first evoking Jamal’s solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Questions and confidence/commitment scaling elicit practical motivations and obstacles. Deeper evocation of values, importance, ability, or commitment is limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input, offers choices, and avoids an expert-driven tone. The agenda could be more fully negotiated and Jamal more explicitly positioned as the expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session evokes some change talk around graduating, limits, and trying a plan tonight. It does not expand change talk enough in importance, ability, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, and responses use appropriate roll-with-resistance reflections. More strategic double-sided reflections or discrepancy work would strengthen this.
