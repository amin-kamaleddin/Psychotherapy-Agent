# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout; never labels, shames, or expresses disapproval of Jason's drinking. Accepts his resistance as information rather than defiance, with genuine and individualized tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections at both content and emotional/identity level, each adding a new layer of understanding. Notably captures deeper meanings such as the identity threat behind 'putting you in a box' and the future-self concern beneath his dismissal.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and purposeful arc from incident to future goals to concrete strategies with good progressive momentum. Minor gap: lacks an explicit transition signposting and a brief closing summary tying together session gains.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session agenda is co-created and Jason's ideas consistently drive the conversation's direction and content. Collaborative language throughout ('look at together,' 'what do you think') with honest acknowledgment of own limitations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Everything framed as Jason's optional choice; never uses directive language or rigid commitments. Particularly strong in validating his autonomy framing of 'I'm choosing this' versus 'I'm not allowed to go.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is elicited from Jason himself through open questions; no psychoeducation, external consequences, or arguments for change are introduced. Successfully produces client-generated reasons for change and a concrete self-developed strategy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:**  psychiatrist consistently positions herself as a learner and defers to Jason as the expert on his own experience and life. Power balance is clearly shared, with Jason's contributions substantially shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:**  Skillful use of open questions, affirmations, and amplifications that progressively build Jason's change talk. Jason moves from minimal acknowledgment to articulating a concrete personal strategy by session's end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:**  Consistently rolls with resistance using strategic reflections rather than arguing or challenging. Minor opportunity: could have more explicitly contrasted his repeated 'not a big deal' framing with his own emerging change talk about not wanting to make a habit.
