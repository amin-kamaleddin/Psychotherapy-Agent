### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; zero judgment or labeling at any point. The psychiatrist consistently communicates that Jamal is a whole person, normalizing his experience and validating his fears without conditions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate, deep perspective-taking that captures emotional and relational nuances (e.g., 'the ground just shifted under you,' the cascade of feared consequences). Not surface-level reflections but genuine understanding of Jamal's specific social fears and perceived injustice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, coherent focus on alcohol use and its social barriers, with each question building logically on the last. Could be sharper in progressively moving toward change-focused exploration (e.g., 'what could be different') rather than staying in the problem-exploring phase.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative from start to finish; every major theme was raised or deepened by Jamal himself. The power dynamic is clearly that of two people exploring together, with the psychiatrist adapting in real-time to Jamal's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Establishes choice from the very first message and never pressures, persuades, or implies Jamal should change. The framing is consistently exploratory ('I'm curious,' 'does that land right?') with zero coercion or directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Jamal's own motivations, fears, and insights (e.g., evoking the specific social fears, reflecting back his insight about identity). Could be pushed further toward eliciting explicit change talk about values, confidence, or what a different scenario would look like.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise about Jamal's life and choices resides within him; never assumes an authority role or offers solutions. The session is structured as a collaborative exploration shaped entirely by Jamal's lead and contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes change talk through strategic reflections and questions (e.g., reinforcing his stated capacity to say no, clarifying his values around belonging). Could have more explicitly reinforced and amplified change talk moments to strengthen the shift toward change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by reflecting, exploring, and validating rather than arguing or minimizing (e.g., 'that's a real cost'). Could have more strategically pivoted away from sustain talk by highlighting the contrast between his stated values and the status quo behavior.
