# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, normalizing language and validates Sofia’s ambivalence, guilt, and self-criticism. It is not a 5 because the responses can feel somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There are accurate reflections of Sofia’s feelings, concerns, and conflict, such as worrying about quitting and feeling out of sync after the move. However, the reflections are often more behavioral or summarizing than deeply emotional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on a specific, realistic non-diet goal related to stress, inactivity, and emotional eating. Each exchange builds momentum toward a concrete, doable plan without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Sofia’s input and builds the plan from her ideas, such as the fridge note, five-minute walk, and self-compassion script. The score is not 5 because the psychiatrist sometimes offers therapist-generated options and sequences that can feel mildly expert-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is repeatedly offered choices, and the language emphasizes preference, experimentation, and control over the plan. The support is strong but occasionally becomes more prescriptive when the psychiatrist introduces a structured sequence of steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful client-generated ideas, such as the walk, the fridge note, and the preferred self-compassion phrase. However, there is less depth in exploring Sofia’s underlying values, hopes, or intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally uses a collaborative, non-directive stance and allows Sofia’s preferences to shape the plan. At times, the psychiatrist shifts toward a more expert-like role by presenting specific strategies and a simple plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers some change talk around small steps, realistic goals, and confidence with the plan. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or expand this change talk through values-based questions or stronger amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and reframed with normalization, such as it being okay if the walk does not always reduce stress. The psychiatrist does not consistently use advanced techniques to pivot barriers back toward change-related motivations.
