# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgmental stance throughout, with strong normalization of Kenji's discomfort and avoidance of pathologizing language. The warmth occasionally feels slightly structured rather than fully spontaneous, preventing a top score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy that consistently goes beyond surface-level content to capture emotional weight, ambivalence, and identity. Standout reflections connect Kenji's drinking to his core identity as an organizer and name the precise emotional mechanisms behind his hesitation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on Kenji's alcohol use with a logical arc from initial discomfort to emerging awareness and consideration of next steps. Could be tightened at the end with a brief collaborative summary or check-in to consolidate progress and identify a concrete focus for future sessions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaboration that builds on Kenji's own metaphors and co-creates meaning without imposing clinical terminology. Occasionally makes interpretive leaps presented as statements rather than invitations for the client to confirm or refine, and could more explicitly invite Kenji's perspective on the conversation's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support that consistently frames all decisions as Kenji's own, validates his pace, and never uses directive language. A model of autonomy support — the psychiatrist reflects difficulty rather than problem-solving, and all suggestions are positioned as Kenji's choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-timed evocative questions consistently draw out Kenji's own motivations and reasons for change rather than providing them. The final segment misses opportunities to follow up on Kenji's expressed doubt with targeted evocative questions that could push toward stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative stance with no lecturing or imposed agenda; the session is clearly co-constructed around Kenji's responses. Could be deepened with more explicit power-sharing language and moments where Kenji is clearly driving the session's direction in every exchange.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reflects and amplifies Kenji's change statements, helping him recognize and own his motivation (e.g., highlighting the distinction between 'the doctor' and 'that's what got me'). Could be stronger with more explicit labeling of change talk and direct evocation questions when Kenji expresses doubt about follow-through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by acknowledging it without arguing or invalidating, using double-sided reflections that keep the change side visible. A few more explicit pivot strategies after sustain talk statements (e.g., reframing ambivalence as a sign of thinking) would elevate this to a top score.
