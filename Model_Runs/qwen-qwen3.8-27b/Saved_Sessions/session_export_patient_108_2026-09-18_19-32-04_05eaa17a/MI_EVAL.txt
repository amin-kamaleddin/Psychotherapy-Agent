# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental throughout, gently reframing Oliver's self-label without being dismissive. The 'I don't hear someone who's weak' moment is slightly corrective rather than fully accepting Oliver's experience at face value.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic attunement with accurate, deepening reflections throughout, capturing both emotional weight and cognitive distortions without labeling. Particularly skilled at naming what's happening beneath the surface, such as the deeper meaning behind the song reference.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent direction toward the target behavior with well-structured pacing from exploration to reframing to action planning. Never wanders; every question and reflection serves the progression appropriately for Oliver's stage of change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaboration with joint-exploration language and questions that invite Oliver's expertise. Some affirmations are somewhat one-directional (therapist evaluating the plan) rather than keeping all evaluative power with Oliver.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Oliver's autonomy, never prescribes or pressures, and the closing question gives Oliver full control over session direction. Reinforces that knowledge and agency reside with Oliver, not the therapist.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation that consistently draws motivation from within Oliver rather than providing external reasons for change. Every intervention is a question or reflection that invites Oliver to generate his own answers, reinforced by the closing 'You know what to do.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance with consistently open questions inviting Oliver's own wisdom. Occasionally steps into a slightly more directive or affirming role (e.g., 'That plan is so clear and fair') that reduces pure power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully amplifies and reinforces Oliver's change talk, particularly the shift from 'I'm weak' to 'the fact is stronger than the feeling.' Could have pushed further on confidence/ability talk and self-efficacy to maximally develop the change arc.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging feelings and inviting exploration rather than arguing or dismissing. Could have more explicitly normalized the in-the-moment fear as a common human response before directing toward a solution.
