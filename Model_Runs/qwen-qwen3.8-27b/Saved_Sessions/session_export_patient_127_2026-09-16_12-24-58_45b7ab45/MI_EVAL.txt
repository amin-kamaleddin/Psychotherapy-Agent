# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect and affirmance while avoiding moralizing about Timur’s history. Acceptance is slightly limited because the warmth feels task-focused rather than fully emotionally individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A good early reflection captures Timur’s ambivalence and later rehearsal experience. Empathy is inconsistent because deeper feelings around fear, shame, and peer comparison are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on a concrete, realistic job-centre step. Direction is consistently MI-appropriate and client-owned.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites Timur’s input and adapts the plan to his chosen wording and rehearsal. The session would benefit from less counselor-supplied script content for stronger partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:**  Timur is supported in choosing timing, wording, fallback lines, and plan details. Autonomy is somewhat constrained by the frequent use of directive coaching and scripts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:**  A few MI-style prompts evoke confidence and felt experience. The session relies too much on supplied behavioral content rather than consistently eliciting Timur’s own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:**  Timur’s ideas clearly shape the ten-minute plan and chosen line. The clinician’s expert coaching role reduces the stronger collaborative spirit of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:**  Some useful scaling and reflection elicit commitment statements. More evocative reflection could better amplify Timur’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:**  Sustain talk is managed indirectly through scripts and problem-solving. A stronger MI approach would have used double-sided reflections to gently turn the sustain talk toward change.
