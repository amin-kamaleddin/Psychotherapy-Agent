# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful stance and accepts Sofia’s ambivalence without rushing resolution. Acceptance could be deeper by adding more individualized affirmation of Sofia’s values, effort, and maternal commitment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Sofia’s mixed feelings about adulthood, pregnancy, fear, embarrassment, and social pressure. Empathy could be strengthened by exploring the emotional depth of her guilt, shame, fear of harm, and loss of identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and the specific challenge of refusing drinks with friends. Direction could improve by explicitly naming the target behavior and negotiating a shared agenda or summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with the psychiatrist inviting Sofia’s ideas and co-constructing a refusal response. Collaboration could be stronger by giving Sofia more explicit ownership over the session priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids coercion and repeatedly invites Sofia to choose a response that feels authentic. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming that Sofia is the decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively help Sofia generate her own practical solutions, such as a short refusal and subject redirect. Evocation could be deeper by eliciting more of Sofia’s values, identity, and future self as a mother.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative questions and avoids imposing a specific script or solution. Partnership could improve by more clearly acknowledging Sofia’s expertise in her own life, relationships, and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers useful change talk, including wanting to do this right and avoid drinking around friends. The psychiatrist could better amplify and expand this change talk by connecting it to Sofia’s maternal values and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through double-sided reflections and without argument or judgment. The clinician could more consistently pivot Sofia’s social sustain talk toward her longer-term values and autonomy.
