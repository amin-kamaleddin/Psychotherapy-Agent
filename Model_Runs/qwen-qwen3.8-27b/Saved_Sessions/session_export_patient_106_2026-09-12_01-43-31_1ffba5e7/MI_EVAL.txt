# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, but acceptance is somewhat task-focused rather than deeply warm. More consistent affirmation of the client’s strengths and emotional needs would be needed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflections are often surface-level and miss deeper feelings such as fear, guilt, and parental tension. Stronger emotion-focused reflections are needed to accurately capture ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on smoking reduction, child exposure, and a concrete evening plan. The main limitation is that direction is agenda-driven rather than collaboratively MI-paced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client’s choices shape the plan, but the therapist supplies most of the structure and options. More equal partnership, letting client priorities guide the session, is needed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist frequently emphasizes choice, small steps, and nonjudgment after slips, supporting client control. More emphasis on the client owning her own goals would make it fully exemplary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Open questions are limited and quickly give way to information, options, and planning. More strategic evocation of the client’s values, reasons, and confidence is needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist invites input, but the session is often driven by a clinician-generated menu and plan. More consistent co-creation and client-led priorities would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client expresses some change talk, but the therapist does not consistently evoke, amplify, or strengthen it. More emphasis on the client’s own reasons, desires, and confidence would improve motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced, but responses are often corrective or option-repeating rather than strategically softened. More double-sided reflections and rolling with resistance are needed.
