# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and normalizing throughout. Could be slightly deeper in explicitly validating the emotional weight behind the client's self-doubt and social fears.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections that capture both content and underlying emotional meaning. Each reflection builds on the client's statements rather than simply paraphrasing, showing genuine attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and coherent logical arc maintaining consistent focus on the target behavior. The session ends at the exploration/visualization stage without tying back to a specific, actionable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration where the client's ideas substantively shape the session direction. The psychiatrist's contributions are limited to questions, reflections, and affirmations requiring active client participation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and right to choose without any prescriptive language or implied obligation. Autonomy is woven naturally into the collaborative process rather than stated as a principle.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external reasons for change; all key insights (filter vs. real, Tuesday as normal, the 'bigger thing' frame) are the client's own words elicited through curiosity-driven questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, who generates every insight himself. The psychiatrist functions as a skilled guide who asks and reflects, never as an expert who tells.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through strategically placed open questions. Change talk progresses from abstract consideration to a concrete, self-generated behavioral vision by session's end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively incorporates sustain talk into change questions rather than opposing it, and validates before gently reframing. Could benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections on the social identity sustain talk.
