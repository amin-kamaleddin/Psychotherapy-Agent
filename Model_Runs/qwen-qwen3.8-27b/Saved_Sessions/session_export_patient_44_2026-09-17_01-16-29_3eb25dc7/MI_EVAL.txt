# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms Erik's strengths. However, the response is sometimes more interpretive than warmly accepting, and does not fully normalize setbacks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is strong perspective-taking regarding Erik's ambivalence between wanting strength and avoiding comparison. Deeper affective empathy would better capture his self-criticism, fear of damage, and frustration with pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior of gym attendance, back protection, and sustainable exercise. The conversation progresses logically toward a concrete, doable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds consistently on Erik's own ideas, such as consistency, maintenance mode, scheduling, and proof of progress. The exchange remains collaborative rather than clinician-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Erik is strongly positioned as the decision-maker about his body, goals, and level of success. The psychiatrist avoids prescribing a plan or pressuring him to adopt a higher standard.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Erik's own reasons for change, including protecting his back and keeping promises to himself. Some reflections are mildly leading or introduce counselor-generated concepts before Erik fully generates his own language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that Erik is the expert on his own body and goals. This creates a clear sense of shared expertise and mutual respect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits clear change talk around concrete goals such as showing up three times, taking a 20-minute walk, and using a wince-free morning as proof. It would be stronger with more explicit confidence ratings and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and reframed without being reinforced or argued against. The psychiatrist could more consistently use double-sided reflections or strategic redirection to move the conversation away from the problem.
