# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, validating language and acknowledges the difficulty of Amina’s co-parenting concerns. Acceptance is strong but slightly formulaic, with room for deeper, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures Amina’s ambivalence, desire for stability, and concern for her son. Some reflections could go deeper into the emotional meaning behind her exhaustion and protective parenting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing co-parenting conflict and improving pickup communication. Each question moves efficiently toward a specific, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Amina’s own ideas and language, creating a joint problem-solving tone. Collaboration could be strengthened by more explicitly co-creating a shared agenda or plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites Amina’s preferences and avoids pressuring her to commit to specific actions. Autonomy support could be more explicit in affirming that all options remain her choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions effectively elicit Amina’s own plan, ideas, and perceived strengths. Evocation could be deeper by more fully exploring her values, confidence, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adopts a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and positions Amina as the expert on her family. Partnership would benefit from more explicit statements about shared control of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina produces meaningful change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or elaborate it to strengthen motivation. More focused DARN-C work would better develop her reasons and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by avoiding argument and gently redirecting it toward Amina’s agency. More double-sided reflections or value-based pivots could further reduce the impact of sustain talk.
