# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming tone while validating Wiremu's efforts.
Some warmth feels formulaic and focuses more on actions than on deeper personal struggles and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects some emotions and stress, especially around feeling stuck and fear.
However, empathy remains surface-level and shifts quickly to problem-solving rather than exploring ambivalence and complex feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a concrete, achievable goal: avoiding a second beer after 8 p.m.
The focus is helpful but somewhat narrow and repetitive, with less connection to broader values and long-term goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is partial collaboration, with the patient's ideas shaping some details of the plan.
The overall dynamic remains uneven, with the psychiatrist driving the conversation through structured advice and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and accepts some patient decisions, such as using the anonymous number.
The interaction is frequently directive and prescriptive, which limits true autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor begins with an open-ended question and acknowledges the patient's motivation around fatherhood.
However, the session relies heavily on providing solutions rather than eliciting and amplifying the patient's own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some collaborative questions are asked, and the patient's preferences influence parts of the plan.
Overall, the psychiatrist remains expert-driven and directs the plan more than co-creating it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Wiremu offers strong spontaneous change talk, but the psychiatrist does not systematically elicit or build on it.
The session moves into planning rather than using MI strategies to expand his own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with sustain talk and keeps the tone non-confrontational.
Sustain talk is mostly bypassed rather than addressed with MI-consistent strategies such as double-sided reflections or linking independence to values.
