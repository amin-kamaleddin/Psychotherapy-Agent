# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone and validates Viktor's perspective without challenging his denial. The warmth is professional and structured rather than deeply personal, and lacks an explicit statement of unconditional acceptance that would push this to a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with sophisticated complex reflections that accurately capture Viktor's ambivalence and deeper emotional meaning. Could be slightly deeper in moments of emotional vulnerability, such as when Viktor says "it's tiring."

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on drug use with a logical progression from resistance through exploring the cycle and home impact. The transition from work stress to home life could have been more explicitly bridged, and the session carries a slight risk of drifting into general life stress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuinely collaborative interaction where Viktor's contributions directly shape the conversation. The balance of speaking is appropriate, with Viktor doing most of the talking and the psychiatrist primarily reflecting and asking open questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support; never pressures Viktor to change and consistently validates his right to choose. "There's no agenda here" is a strong autonomy-supportive statement, and the psychiatrist redirects back to Viktor's own experience rather than providing answers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully elicits motivation from within Viktor using his own language rather than providing external reasons for change. The exploration of "faking it" when he imagines a quiet evening gets at the deeper identity issue without being preachy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Viktor, with genuine power-sharing and no hidden agenda. When Viktor pushes back, the psychiatrist respects the boundary rather than insisting, and the session is clearly co-constructed around Viktor's own terms.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and immediately flags change talk (e.g., "I don't like it — that's not nothing"). Could have more explicitly reinforced and amplified Viktor's change talk when it appeared, such as exploring what "quieter" means to him more fully.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging without arguing and gently questioning permanence through softening reflections. A 5 would require more explicit double-sided reflections or a stronger strategic pivot at the moments of strongest resistance.
