# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations throughout, though warmth became somewhat transactional when Eric expressed deeper frustration rather than receiving fuller emotional validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequate surface-level reflections accurately summarized Eric's ambivalence, but missed the deeper emotion of feeling controlled and losing identity when he stated everyone wanted him to be someone else.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking with logical pacing from initial rating through pros/cons exploration to a small manageable experiment, with no aimless wandering or topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good initial effort with inclusive language and shared input, but reliance on multiple-choice menus and unilateral lists shifted the dynamic away from true organic co-creation, especially with the final tips against his wishes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initially respected Eric's preferences, but significantly violated his autonomy by pushing new directives about jackets, shirts, and hand-rinsing immediately after he explicitly stated he was not doing any more experiments or tracking.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used some evocative questions early on, but bypassed core evocation by supplying a list of downsides in response to Eric's request and ending with informative tips instead of drawing out his own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session began with a good collaborative stance, but eroded in the second half when the psychiatrist shifted to an expert-driven approach by unilaterally providing lists of downsides and quick tips.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Standard MI techniques elicited some change talk around cost and smell, but failed to consistently reinforce and deepen Eric's own statements to amplify his internal motivation for an easier life independent of his wife's complaints.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally avoided arguing with Eric's sustain talk, but inadvertently reinforced it by providing a unilateral list of six downsides that likely triggered his defensive rationalization about feeling uncontrolled.
