# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with phrases like 'that's not a contradiction' and 'you're not lazy or not trying' conveying deep respect and non-judgment. The warmth is genuine, individualized, and never formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that goes beyond surface content to capture the emotional and existential weight of Liora's statements. Key reflections like 'holding onto something you believe is supposed to save you, and it's not doing that' demonstrate highly accurate, consistent, and deeply attuned perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on self-harm as a coping strategy with logical progression from the 'noise' metaphor to the daily cycle to guilt. The session remains largely exploratory without progressively narrowing toward a concrete change focus or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative language and tone with inclusive phrasing and no expert stance, but the actual session structure remains therapist-driven. Liora does not have a clear role in deciding what to explore or in what order, limiting the depth of power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent and explicit autonomy support woven naturally into the interaction, including 'you don't owe anyone a decision you haven't made' and a non-directive approach that never pressures for change. The psychiatrist never uses directive language and consistently frames everything in terms of Liora's own experience and choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent use of evocative questions that draw out Liora's own thinking, resulting in several important self-generated insights. However, when Liora offers change-oriented statements, the psychiatrist does not explicitly elicit more from these or build on them strategically.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-authoritative stance with inclusive language, but does not fully cede direction to Liora or explicitly acknowledge her expertise in her own experience. The collaboration is strong but operates more within a therapist-guided framework than as fully client-led direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some attempts to evoke change talk through open questions, but does not explicitly affirm, amplify, or build on Liora's nascent change-oriented statements. The session remains exploratory without actively deepening or reinforcing emerging change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively handles sustain talk with validation rather than resistance, and avoids reinforcing or arguing against Liora's beliefs. Could be more proactive in using double-sided reflections or gentle reframes to redirect hopelessness embedded in statements like 'I've done this a hundred times and I still end up in the same place.'
