# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional nonjudgmental validation throughout; the counselor reframed school avoidance as a logical response to injustice rather than a behavioral problem. Never once labeled, pathologized, or implied Sofia should feel differently.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were consistently accurate, nuanced, and emotionally resonant, capturing both emotional state and the functional purpose of avoidance behaviors. Responses were specific and layered rather than generic, demonstrating genuine attunement to Sofia's inner world.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session followed a clear, coherent arc from validation through understanding the problem to exploring safety conditions and identifying a small step. The ending lacked a brief collaborative summary or concrete next step that would have provided stronger forward momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation was a genuine dialogue shaped by Sofia's contributions in visible, specific ways. Questions were curious and open rather than leading, and the counselor offered possibilities for Sofia to choose from rather than declaring interpretations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly offered the option of not changing and never imposed a timeline, urgency, or directive language. The entire session communicated that this was Sofia's life, her choice, and her pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and insight were consistently elicited from within Sofia rather than provided externally. Every insight articulated by Sofia was client-generated, drawn out by well-crafted exploratory questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A fully collaborative, power-sharing stance was maintained throughout, with session direction shaped entirely by Sofia's contributions. The counselor explicitly resisted the expert role and followed Sofia's lead rather than a pre-planned agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully evoked hope and vision language, particularly in the latter portion of the session. Could have more consistently amplified and built on each instance of change talk as it emerged, and explored ability or confidence language further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was never argued with or dismissed, and concerns were validated while keeping the door open. Could have been more strategic in using double-sided reflections to pivot blame language toward Sofia's own agency.
