# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Mei’s concerns. There was no judgment, blame, or minimization of her drinking, social choices, or contraceptive lapses.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and nuanced, capturing both emotional and motivational meanings. Reflections tracked ambivalence, social values, and tension between safety and connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on nighttime alcohol use and pill-taking reliability. Discussion progressed logically from values to a concrete, behavior-focused plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaved as an equal partner rather than an authority figure. Mei’s lived experience and ideas substantially shaped the plan, with the psychiatrist reflecting and refining without dominating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly emphasized that the decision was Mei’s own. Language and questioning consistently reinforced choice, self-direction, and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited client-generated motivations, ideas, and solutions. However, there was less deliberate evocation of self-efficacy, confidence, or deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positioned Mei as the expert on her social life, evenings, and options. The conversation avoided directive advice and reflected a strong collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mei’s change talk was frequently evoked and naturally emerged through questions about realistic and doable steps. The psychiatrist could have more explicitly amplified change talk and strengthened confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally acknowledged without argument and reframed toward autonomy and smaller steps. The final concern about self-blame and failure was left unresolved, which limited the skill slightly.
