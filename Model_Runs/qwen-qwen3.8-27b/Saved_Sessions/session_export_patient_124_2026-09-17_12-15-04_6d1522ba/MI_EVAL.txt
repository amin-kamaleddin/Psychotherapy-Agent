# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens with warm, nonjudgmental framing but degrades into mechanical, repetitive responses in the latter half. Lacks active affirmation of Sofia's strengths, struggles, or validation of her social exclusion and anxiety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows good empathic effort early with complex reflections and skillful probes, but collapses into the same generic prompt four times during Sofia's most emotionally significant disclosures about social exclusion and identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a general focus on marijuana use, but the later half drifts into tangential social identity topics that are never strategically tied back to the target behavior or a concrete change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early collaborative framing is present with open questions and non-directive language, but the repeated identical responses in the second half are passive and lack the back-and-forth quality of true collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respects Sofia's right to decide without pressuring her to stop using, using non-directive language like 'experiment' and 'what do you think.' Could be stronger with explicit affirmation of her freedom to continue using if she chooses.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Initiates evocation with good open questions and Sofia produces meaningful change talk, but the psychiatrist fails to deepen or sustain the most evocative material, such as Sofia's powerful statement about not wanting to be 'the quiet one.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early language supports partnership ('I'm curious,' 'what's your honest read'), but the four consecutive identical responses feel more like a disengaged interviewer than a true partner who would reflect, build, or redirect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes reasonable attempts to elicit change talk and Sofia does produce some, but the psychiatrist fails to capture, reflect back, or build on the change talk that emerges, moving on instead of reinforcing openings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk about fitting in socially dominates the second half with no double-sided reflections, strategic summaries, or attempts to create discrepancy between her social identity concerns and broader values.
