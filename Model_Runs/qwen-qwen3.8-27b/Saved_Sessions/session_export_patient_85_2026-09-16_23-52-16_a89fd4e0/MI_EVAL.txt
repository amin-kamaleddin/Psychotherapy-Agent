# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. The stance is supportive and affirming rather than corrective or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and tracks the patient’s practical and emotional concerns well. The reflections could deepen further into stronger feelings such as betrayal and frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on reducing fruit juice and increasing water. It moves consistently from problem identification to a concrete plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor co-constructs the intervention with the patient as an equal partner. The plan is built from the patient’s ideas, routines, and home context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient is repeatedly offered choice and ownership over the plan. The counselor avoids pressure and reinforces the patient’s ability to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully elicits internal motivation through open questions and reflective amplification. At times, added interpretation slightly reduces the extent to which motivation remains fully patient-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient is positioned as the expert on his own life and realistic options. The session is framed as shared exploration rather than expert-led instruction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor effectively draws out and reinforces strong patient-generated change talk. The patient articulates clear reasons, confidence, and commitment to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled by softening it through flexible, nonpunitive framing and problem-solving. More sophisticated MI techniques could be used to more actively pivot ambivalence toward change.
