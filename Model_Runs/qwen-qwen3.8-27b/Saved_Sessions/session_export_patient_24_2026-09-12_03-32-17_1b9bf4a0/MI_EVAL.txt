# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalized Daniel's ambivalence without labeling or blaming. Acceptance could deepen by more explicitly validating the discomfort and identity implications of being flagged for alcohol use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were generally accurate and captured both the routine and concern about training. Empathy was mostly cognitive rather than emotionally deep, especially around fear of being 'broken' and loss of the ritual.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on alcohol use in relation to marathon training and recovery. It moved logically from concern to exploration to a concrete, behaviorally focused plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel was repeatedly invited to choose and shape the conversation, and his preferences influenced the plan. The counselor still supplied much of the structure and options, limiting fully collaborative client-led generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offered options, respected Daniel's choices, and adapted to a lower-effort plan. Autonomy support would improve by more explicitly reinforcing Daniel's ownership in moments of sustain talk.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicited some client-generated motivation by asking about training effects, pros and cons, and confidence. Evocation was limited because the session moved quickly to options and planning without deeper exploration of values or intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor adopted a collaborative stance and allowed Daniel to direct several parts of the conversation. Partnership would strengthen by more consistently positioning Daniel as the primary expert on his own body and goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor brought out some change talk, such as wanting evidence and trying non-alcoholic beer. Change talk was not consistently reinforced, reflected, or deepened through follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoided arguing with Daniel's sustain talk and used some balanced reflections. More active softening was needed to explore the ambivalence behind statements like returning to alcohol if the results were not impressive.
