# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone maintained throughout with genuine positive regard. Occasionally reads slightly polished or therapeutic rather than deeply unscripted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, well-calibrated reflections with deep attunement to emotional meaning. Particularly powerful in capturing the 'I'm not invisible to myself' insight and tracking the cognitive-emotional loop.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear logical progression from behavior identification through solution-building with no topic drift. Could benefit from a brief consolidation moment that explicitly ties the session's threads together.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as genuine joint exploration with an equal power dynamic. Client's ideas drive direction and the psychiatrist builds on contributions rather than steering toward a predetermined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never prescribes or applies pressure; consistently frames all actions as the client's own choices. Self-compassion is placed entirely within Elena's own voice with no external framework imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits motivation from within the client by connecting behavior to personal values and patterns. Could be pushed further with confidence scaling, commitment language, or vivid future-self imagery.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client. Session direction is entirely shaped by Elena's contributions with no expert positioning or hidden agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and amplifies rich change talk including desire, reasons, and ability. Could include more explicit evocation of commitment statements or confidence work to reach full potential.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles the limited sustain talk effectively by reframing concern as evidence of seriousness and exploring self-compassion responses. Could include more explicit normalization or a double-sided reflection acknowledging the fear while maintaining forward momentum.
