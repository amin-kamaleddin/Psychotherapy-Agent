# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgment. The warmth remains somewhat task-focused, with attention to performance risk rather than broader unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate, double-sided reflections that capture Samuel's ambivalence well. There is room to explore deeper values, identity, or emotional meaning behind the smoking habit.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing smoking in high-risk situations. Each question progressively narrows the conversation to a concrete, manageable cue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Builds directly on Samuel's ideas and co-constructs a small, workable plan. The counselor still maintains a clear agenda, so collaboration is strong but not fully mutually generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers options without imposing them and respects Samuel's preference for a tiny, low-effort step. Could strengthen with more explicit autonomy affirmations and less gentle steering toward the pen-based solution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws motivation from within Samuel, eliciting his own cues, success criteria, and self-statements. Occasionally supplies interpretation before Samuel has fully articulated it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Places thinking and decision-making in Samuel's hands through repeated questions. The partnership is strong but mostly implicit, with less explicit power-sharing or agenda negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits useful change talk about trying the pen, pausing, and breaking autopilot. Could more explicitly elicit desire, need, or broader commitment to reduction or cessation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Samuel's doubt without arguing or reinforcing it. Could more proactively pivot away from failure and autopilot language toward strengths, values, or past success.
