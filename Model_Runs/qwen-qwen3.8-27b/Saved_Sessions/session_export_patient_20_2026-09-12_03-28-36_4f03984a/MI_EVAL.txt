# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and acknowledges Brandon’s fears and choices. Acceptance is strong but occasionally becomes evaluative rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures both practical and emotional concerns. Deeper feelings around identity, loneliness, or stigma are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on specific drinking-related goals, especially weekend use, late-night risk, and morning clarity. The approach is practical but could more deliberately connect to broader values and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is meaningful collaboration through options and Brandon’s input. However, the therapist still generates much of the plan, which limits fully co-created change.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is consistently supported through open-ended invitations and respect for hesitation. The repeated confidence scaling and many therapist-generated options slightly reduce emphasis on full self-determination.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, especially around clearer mornings and small changes. Evocation is uneven because the therapist often directs the process with specific options rather than drawing out deeper client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are moments of shared decision-making and respect for Brandon’s preferences. The session still feels partially therapist-led, especially when multiple options and scripts are introduced.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Brandon expresses several change-related statements and agrees to a small plan. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen these statements into stronger client-owned motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally acknowledged without direct confrontation. Important sustaining statements, especially those tied to identity and stigma, are not fully processed before moving into planning.
