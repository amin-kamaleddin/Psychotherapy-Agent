# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, nonjudgment, and practical affirmation of Samir's perspective. It could reach a 5 by adding more explicit, value-based regard for Samir independent of his behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of Samir's ambivalence and the function of smoking in his daily routine. It could be stronger by exploring the emotional layer, such as fear, frustration, or the meaning of the cigarette pause.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on improving asthma control by modifying morning smoking and inhaler use. The conversation moves logically from goal selection to barriers, alternatives, implementation, and evaluation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samir's ideas strongly shape the plan, and the psychiatrist asks for his input throughout. It could be slightly stronger by making the equal-partner stance more explicit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Samir's choice and ownership of the plan. Language such as 'It keeps you in the driver's seat' and client-selected options demonstrates high autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits Samir's own motivations, concerns, and solutions through open-ended questions. This avoids imposing reasons for change and helps motivation emerge from within the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly conveys that Samir is the expert on his own day-to-day life. The session is shaped by Samir's contributions rather than being driven by the counselor's agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively encourages Samir to articulate desire, reasons, and emerging commitment to change. It could be stronger by explicitly amplifying and consolidating this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by reflecting both sides and redirecting toward alternatives without arguing. It could reach a 5 by more consistently using double-sided reflections or value-based reframing to convert sustain talk into change motivation.
