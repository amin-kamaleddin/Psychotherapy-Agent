# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains consistent non-judgment and validates Marek's defensiveness throughout. Falls short of a 5 because affirmations of strengths remain somewhat generic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled reflective listening that accurately captures Marek's defensive stance and need for concrete data. Occasionally overshoots into interpretation (e.g., the filler word moment) and doesn't deeply explore the emotional weight of his social identity tied to drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, logical focus on alcohol and health implications with purposeful progression from defensiveness to emerging acknowledgment. Could benefit from a more explicit directional summary to sharpen the emerging focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is structured as a genuine joint investigation with shared power and adopted standards. Falls short of a 5 because the psychiatrist retains subtle editorial control over agenda-setting rather than fully ceding it to Marek.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Marek's right to decide without advising or pushing. Falls short of a 5 because autonomy is supported implicitly but not explicitly reinforced with direct language about his ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes occasional evocative questions that produce meaningful responses but doesn't consistently use evocation as a primary strategy. Misses key moments (e.g., Marek's pause, 'is it gonna fix itself') to deepen emerging concern with follow-up questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative stance with explicit invitations for Marek to judge the data and call out overreach. Falls short of a 5 because the psychiatrist still drives the agenda and doesn't fully follow Marek's leads when they might redirect the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some meaningful change talk but largely through spontaneous patient contributions rather than skillful cultivation. Misses opportunities to deepen emerging concern (e.g., 'is it gonna fix itself') with follow-up evocative questions or reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging its truth without reinforcing it, as seen in the 'you're clearly not' reflection and the cousin Jan comparison. Could use double-sided reflections to more explicitly hold both certainty and emerging doubt.
