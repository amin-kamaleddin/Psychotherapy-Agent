# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist remains respectful and nonjudgmental, explicitly affirming Kareem’s autonomy and right not to quit. Acceptance is solid but somewhat procedural rather than deeply warm or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Kareem’s frustration, practical compromise, and low confidence. The empathy remains more cognitive than emotionally deep, especially around self-doubt and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on managing smoking urges at work without sneaking. It moves clearly from the presenting problem to a concrete, testable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kareem’s choices shape the plan, and the clinician incorporates rejections smoothly. Collaboration is slightly limited because the psychiatrist introduces several practical options before fully eliciting Kareem’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly invites Kareem to choose and respects his decisions without pressure. Some suggestions and explanatory rationale sound somewhat directive or solution-focused.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicits useful information about timing, confidence, and plan details. Evocation is mainly task-focused and does not deeply explore Kareem’s values, identity, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session reflects a collaborative stance, with Kareem controlling the agenda and plan. The partnership is slightly weakened by the clinician taking on a visible problem-solving role early on.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked through questions about feasibility and confidence. The clinician misses opportunities to quote, amplify, and deeply develop Kareem’s own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing sustain talk. However, he does not actively soften it with double-sided reflections or gentle redirection toward change.
