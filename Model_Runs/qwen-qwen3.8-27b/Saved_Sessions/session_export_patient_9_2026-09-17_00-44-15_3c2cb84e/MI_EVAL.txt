# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated warmth, respect, and nonjudgment. She validated Amina's protective alarm and fear of being 'not enough' without labeling or blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both content and affect, including ambivalence and somatic experience. The counselor tracked meaning and emotional cost rather than merely paraphrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target concern of parenting under co-parenting stress and responses to urgent texts. It would benefit from a clearer definition of a concrete, client-chosen next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor used collaborative language and built on Amina's words to shape a shared understanding. More explicit co-creation of agenda, priorities, or plan would strengthen this criterion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided advice-giving and validated Amina's capacity and choices. Adding explicit statements reinforcing that Amina owns the decision and pace would elevate this further.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions effectively elicited Amina's values, goals, and self-efficacy rather than supplying reasons for change. Broader use of change talk dimensions such as confidence, importance, or commitment would improve this criterion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a collaborative stance and positioned Amina as the expert on her son and family. More explicit power-sharing and co-created agenda-setting would make the partnership more fully visible.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina produced meaningful change talk about feeling grounded, putting the phone down, and being steady. The counselor could have more consistently amplified, summarized, or deepened this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was present and was reframed through Amina's values without reinforcing fear. More explicit double-sided reflection or linking sustain talk to her own change goals would elevate this criterion.
