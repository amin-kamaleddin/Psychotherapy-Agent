# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating of Rosa's ambivalence around health, weight, identity, and friendship. Warmth is sometimes repetitive and could include stronger affirmation of Rosa's worth to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Rosa's conflict and the emotional meaning of smoking. Empathy is limited by repetition and by not fully exploring fears of identity loss, shame, or feeling "weird" without cigarettes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays connected to smoking cessation and narrows toward a smoke-free pause with friends. Direction is weakened by repeated broad questions and a lack of a clear strategic line toward a specific next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses joint language and builds on Rosa's ideas, such as walks and trying with one friend. Collaboration is slightly limited by repetition and returning to the same problem-solving frame without fully co-creating a clear agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids advice, pressure, and dismissal of Rosa's concerns. Autonomy support would be stronger with more explicit language affirming Rosa's freedom to choose any path, including not changing right now.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks open questions and reflects Rosa's health motivation and existing walks. Evocation is inconsistent because the counselor often summarizes for Rosa and underuses readiness, confidence, values, or future-self questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rosa is treated as the expert on her life, and her concerns and ideas shape the conversation. Partnership is slightly reduced by repeated prompts such as "what feels most useful to explore first?" that can make the session feel circular.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks about a healthier future and reflects Rosa's desire to be healthier. However, the counselor rarely expands Rosa's own change talk or uses follow-up questions to strengthen desire, need, ability, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged respectfully and balanced without argument. The session remains heavily focused on obstacles, with only moderate and inconsistent pivots toward Rosa's values or possible benefits of change.
