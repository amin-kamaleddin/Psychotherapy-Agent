# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone while clearly honoring the patient’s time constraints. The warmth feels somewhat efficient and transactional rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects the patient’s practical concerns but keeps reflections largely factual. There are missed opportunities to explore the underlying stress or frustration of the patient’s demanding schedule.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and the specific barrier of time. Every exchange efficiently steers toward a concrete, actionable plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The structure is collaborative, offering options and asking for input, but the psychiatrist often acts as the primary source of solutions. The patient selects from the clinician’s menu rather than co-creating the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces patient ownership by offering choices and leaving the final decision to the patient. The patient’s boundaries around follow-up are fully respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the patient’s own solutions and commitments through targeted questions. Deeper exploration of the patient’s internal confidence or past change experiences could strengthen this further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative language is used, but the psychiatrist frequently assumes the expert role when answering factual questions. This limits the power-sharing and shifts the dynamic toward instruction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Structured choices help the patient generate practical change talk, such as keeping desk snacks and using “better fuel” language. The psychiatrist could more explicitly reinforce or amplify this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without argumentation or defensive provocation. The psychiatrist reflects the patient’s pragmatic test plan and keeps the session moving forward.
