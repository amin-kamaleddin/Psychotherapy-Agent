### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Lukas’s sense of choice and ownership throughout the session. They avoid direct advice or pressure, and instead explore how to make Lukas’s own ideas feel safer.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently evokes motivation from within Lukas rather than supplying reasons for change. Open questions and accurate reflections help Lukas articulate his own values around connection, dignity, and steadier Sundays.

### Partnership

**Comments:** The psychiatrist takes a collaborative stance and invites Lukas to lead the content. The partnership could be improved by more explicitly acknowledging Lukas as the expert on his own life and by making the shared decision-making process more visible.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes change talk through open, forward-looking questions and affirmation. The skill could be strengthened by more explicitly amplifying, summarizing, or expanding Lukas’s emerging change talk across multiple dimensions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully handles sustain talk without arguing, correcting, or minimizing Lukas’s concerns. Sustain talk is validated and then gently redirected toward client-generated problem-solving and future-focused possibilities.
