# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect and nonjudgment, normalizing Luis’s skepticism rather than challenging it. The tone is generally warm but could feel more individualized and less formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Luis’s annoyance, fatigue, and frustration with being told what to do. Some reflections remain surface-level and could better capture deeper fears about control, work demands, and independence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target issue of asthma management and inhaler use. It would benefit from brief summaries that consolidate direction and align the plan with Luis’s priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and lets Luis shape the final plan. Collaboration would improve if Luis were invited to generate more of his own solutions before the psychiatrist offered suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A major strength is consistently honoring Luis’s choice, offering low-pressure options, and respecting his rejection of alarms, reminders, or cards. Some invitations to try a step are still slightly leading.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks questions that elicit some action language and preferences. However, there are missed opportunities to draw out Luis’s own values, reasons for change, and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist largely acts as a partner by offering choices and allowing Luis to reword the plan. The expert role becomes a bit too strong during explanations of inhalers, technique, and triggers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Luis does make small commitments, such as carrying the blue inhaler and using a simple backup plan. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen this change talk into stronger importance, confidence, or values-based motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing or lecturing about risk, which is appropriate given Luis’s resistance. Some responses overly validate sustain talk about the medication being pointless and do not pivot enough toward change.
