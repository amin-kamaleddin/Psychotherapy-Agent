# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout the session. Normalizes Sofia's feelings without minimizing or redirecting, and reflects the specific emotional texture of her experience rather than using generic affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture Sofia's specific emotional states, including the gap between 'I am reading' and 'also not really.' Empathy deepens progressively and tracks the courage behind Sofia's disclosures, not just the content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session was initiated as a follow-up to an alcohol risk screening, yet the conversation drifts entirely into general emotional exploration without ever connecting back to the target behavior. The session becomes a general supportive conversation rather than a focused MI intervention for alcohol-related concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with open questions and no imposed agenda, framing the session as mutual exploration. However, the collaboration is somewhat one-directional in practice, with the psychiatrist guiding rather than co-creating with Sofia.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Sofia's autonomy through explicit statements like 'I'll follow your lead' and 'I'm not here to judge you or have a specific agenda.' Never gives advice, suggests specific actions, or implies Sofia should do anything.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks appropriate open questions that allow Sofia to reflect, but evocation is primarily aimed at understanding her current state rather than drawing out her own motivations for change. Does not strategically elicit DARN statements related to the target behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly states 'You're the expert on what's going on in your world' and consistently positions Sofia as the authority on her own experience. The session structure is entirely Sofia-driven, with no assumption of diagnostic authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some forward-looking language from Sofia but does not consistently build on it or deepen it with follow-up questions. The session ends without Sofia articulating any clear change talk or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates and gently opens doors when Sofia brushes things off, without challenging or arguing. However, the session never truly encounters significant sustain talk, so the criterion is demonstrated in a somewhat limited context.
