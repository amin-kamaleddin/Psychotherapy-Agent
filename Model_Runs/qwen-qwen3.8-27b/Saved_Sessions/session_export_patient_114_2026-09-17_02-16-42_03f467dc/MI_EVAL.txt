# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Ryan's experience throughout the session. Acceptance is strong but sometimes remains more task-focused than emotionally affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of Ryan's ambivalence, social pressure, and emotional burden. Empathy could be deepened by exploring underlying emotions such as fear, exhaustion, or disappointment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays generally focused on alcohol use, safety, and the impact on Ryan's education. A summary or midpoint recap would have helped consolidate direction more clearly.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaboratively explores options based on Ryan's own ideas and social context. The session is still somewhat counselor-facilitated rather than fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses noncoercive, choice-based language and avoids pressuring Ryan to commit to a strict plan. More explicit affirmation that Ryan is ultimately in charge of his decisions would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Ryan's own practical strategies and self-monitoring ideas rather than supplying advice. Evocation could be strengthened by exploring values, confidence, readiness, and personal meaning more directly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative questions and invites Ryan's perspective, avoiding a lecturing or directive stance. Some consecutive questioning and counselor steering prevent a fully client-directed partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and affirms Ryan's own change ideas, such as limits, rides, and a buddy system. The focus could shift from practical problem-solving to deeper change talk about values, confidence, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Ryan's sustain talk and acknowledges his social motives. However, there are fewer double-sided reflections that gently connect those motives to discrepancies with his safety and future goals.
