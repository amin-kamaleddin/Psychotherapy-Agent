# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental, clearly affirming Sofia's frustration and fairness concerns. The warmth is sometimes procedural and shifts quickly into problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects frustration, unfairness, and exhaustion, but often remains at a surface level. Deeper hurts, isolation, and hopelessness are not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on the presenting concern and moves toward concrete next steps. Focus is slightly diluted by spreading attention across many interventions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and builds on Sofia's existing coping strategies, showing some shared problem-solving. However, much of the interaction remains counselor-led rather than truly collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges Sofia's choices and respects some refusals, which supports autonomy. The directive list of steps and restrictions reduces the sense that Sofia is fully in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia contributes some change-relevant solutions and preferences, such as the text message and exit strategy. The counselor often supplies scripts and reasons instead of deeper eliciting questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor occasionally invites input, but the session frequently takes an expert-driven stance with scripts, rules, and coping tools. This positions the counselor more as the source of expertise than a partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some willingness to try actions like sending a text or using an exit strategy. There is limited deeper exploration of importance, confidence, values, or desired outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor accepts Sofia's resistance without arguing and validates some of her stance. However, repeated pushes to involve the school and limited reframing make this only moderately MI-consistent.
