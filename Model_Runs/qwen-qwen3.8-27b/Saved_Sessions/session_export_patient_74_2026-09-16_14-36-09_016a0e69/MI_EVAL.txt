# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warmth, unconditional regard, and nonjudgmental acceptance. Minor repetitive opening language slightly limits fullness but does not affect the score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers complex, accurate reflections of Linda's ambivalence between needing a break and concern for role modeling. Could have explored the emotional pain and guilt more deeply in the final segment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Remains focused on smoking after meals/work and the small private counting task. Early section spends excessive time on why she is here and the school program, delaying clearer target focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and invites Linda's input on next steps. Could have been stronger by more explicitly allowing Linda to set the agenda and priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports Linda's freedom to choose, avoids pressure, and respects her boundary against programs or grading. Clear autonomy-affirming language such as 'your plan, not mine.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effective questions elicit personal meaning and discrepancy around children. Overall evocation is limited, with fewer questions targeting deeper reasons, hopes, confidence, or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a cooperative stance and invites Linda into planning. Lacks a clearer explicit moment of shared power and Linda as expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Encourages some change-related statements about noticing smoking and concern for kids. Could have systematically elicited more desire, reasons, ability, and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well with balancing reflections and without argumentation. Could have used slightly more nuanced language to tilt gently toward change.
