# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Psychiatrist shows consistent nonjudgmental respect and accepts Rowan’s choices without blame. However, acceptance sometimes over-validates avoidant plans and feels more procedural than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections track Rowan’s frustration and burden but are not consistently deep or nuanced. The clinician often moves quickly from emotional understanding to options, leaving underlying fear and ambivalence underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays on the device topic but increasingly drifts toward avoidance strategies. It does not maintain strong strategic direction toward Rowan’s engagement with the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is cooperative and adjusts when Rowan rejects ideas. Still, much of the structure and options are therapist-generated rather than fully client-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rowan’s right to choose is repeatedly emphasized, and the clinician backs off when he resists scheduling. Some early options and rapid planning still feel somewhat directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and a confidence scale invite some reflection, but the focus is mainly on managing non-use. The clinician does not consistently draw out Rowan’s own internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for preferences and avoids being purely expert-driven. However, the interaction remains fairly therapist-led, often offering menus for Rowan to choose from.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are limited attempts to evoke change talk, and small openings are not amplified. The session misses opportunities to explore Rowan’s own values, benefits, or reasons for engaging.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing but repeatedly validates and builds on Rowan’s resistance to change. More double-sided reflections and gentle pivots are needed to soften sustain talk.
