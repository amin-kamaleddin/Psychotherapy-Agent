# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids shaming Isabella for avoidance. However, acceptance can feel formulaic and lacks deeper, individualized affirmation of her worth or courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurately captures Isabella’s ambivalence and practical concerns. It could go deeper into emotional meaning, and the repeated question slightly undermines attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and narrows possibilities into manageable steps. Direction becomes mechanical near the end, especially with a repeated question that interrupts momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Isabella’s ideas and uses collaborative language, allowing her concerns to shape the conversation. However, leading problem-solving prompts and repetition reduce the sense of equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through non-pressuring language and emphasis on Isabella’s choice. The clinician could further normalize doing nothing and reduce subtle steering toward selecting a step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions are used to elicit Isabella’s ideas, but evocation remains mostly logistical. More attention to values, motivations, hopes, and confidence would deepen client-generated change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adopts a partner stance and follows Isabella’s contributions, such as the café and short time limit. Full partnership is limited by a persistent agenda and repeated clinician-led prompts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Isabella offers some meaningful change talk about a short café visit and an easy exit. The psychiatrist does not sufficiently amplify, summarize, or develop this change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses some double-sided reflections. However, repeated validation of staying home is not consistently redirected toward Isabella’s values, agency, or reasons for change.
