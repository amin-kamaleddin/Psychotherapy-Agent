### Collaboration

**Comments:** Genuine dialogue in which Kevin's contributions shape the direction, focus, and depth of each exchange. The clinician builds on Kevin's specific language and invites his perspective at every turn, with no sense of hierarchy.

**Comments:** Skillfully elicits motivation from within Kevin by helping him access his own values and self-observations rather than providing external reasons for change. Kevin's final statement ('I want to not fall down again') is a product of evocation, not persuasion.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Operates entirely from a stance of partnership, never positioning as an authority or providing advice. The session's direction, pacing, and focus are determined by Kevin's contributions in real time.

**Comments:** Skillfully creates conditions for change talk and explicitly notices and reflects Kevin's own shift ('that's a pretty big shift'). Could be strengthened by eliciting confidence ratings, exploring ability in depth, or asking direct commitment questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Exceptional handling of sustain talk—never argues, corrects, or pushes back. Each instance is met with validation followed by a gentle pivot toward Kevin's own stated values or contradictions.
