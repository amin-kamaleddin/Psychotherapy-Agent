# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and genuine unconditional positive regard throughout the session. The psychiatrist meets Kieran exactly where he is, validates small goals without judgment, and never expresses frustration at his dismissiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic attunement that captures not just content but the emotional and experiential meaning beneath Kieran's words. Reflections are complex, accurately attuned, and perfectly matched to his pace and depth of engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and coherent progression from broad monotony to a specific, concrete focus (five minutes of quiet with music), with each step building logically on the previous one. Minor limitation: the final unanswered question from Kieran leaves the arc slightly incomplete.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively treats the conversation as joint exploration, following Kieran's logic rather than a predetermined agenda. Could be deeper with more explicit power-sharing, therapist vulnerability, and co-constructed agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support woven naturally into every interaction — acknowledging lack of choice, explicitly stating non-coercion, retracting perceived pressure immediately, and affirming Kieran's right to keep goals small. No directive language is ever used.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful application of evoking Kieran's own motivations rather than providing them; every piece of forward movement comes from Kieran himself. Subtle, patient, and perfectly paced to his level of engagement without any advisory or persuasive attempts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance with functional shared decision-making and genuine invitations for Kieran's honest assessment. Could be strengthened by more explicit acknowledgment of Kieran as the expert in his own life and more genuine negotiation of direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-timed evocative questions successfully draw out Kieran's motivations and skillfully amplify change talk with slight intensification. However, change talk remains limited to comfort-level goals (sleep, quiet) rather than deeper values or readiness language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully through validation and exploratory reframing rather than argument or dismissal. Occasional risk of pushing slightly ahead of Kieran's readiness, such as a briefly leading question before he fully opened up.
