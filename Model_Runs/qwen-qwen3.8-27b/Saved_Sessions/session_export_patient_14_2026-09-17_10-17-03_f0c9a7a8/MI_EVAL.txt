# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. Andrei’s limits and practical constraints are treated as legitimate rather than as avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, especially for Andrei’s efficiency-focused reasoning and boundaries. The main gap is less attention to deeper feelings such as frustration, pressure, or self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains focused on weight-related health, especially blood pressure and small lifestyle changes. Each turn keeps the conversation tied to a concrete, workable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed rather than imposed. The psychiatrist builds on Andrei’s own systems, such as his spreadsheet use and month-end review.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Andrei is consistently positioned as the decision-maker and owner of the plan. The psychiatrist avoids pressure, guilt, or directive advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions effectively elicit Andrei’s own reasons, values, and criteria for action. Motivation and the final plan mainly emerge from the client rather than being supplied by the psychiatrist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly conveys that Andrei is the expert on his own life and schedule. The session is shaped by Andrei’s contributions rather than therapist expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Andrei to generate his own reasons and language for change. A minor limitation is occasional therapist-initiated suggestions before the client fully generates the idea.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully by acknowledging it without arguing or amplifying it. Andrei’s concerns about time and burden are reframed as boundaries that help narrow the plan.
