# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgment while avoiding shame or pressure. It could be slightly warmer and more explicit in normalizing discouragement, lapses, and self-doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and accurate tracking of ambivalence and motivation. Some reflections are slightly interpretive, and emotional attunement to fatigue and discouragement could be deeper.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and steadily narrows the goal into concrete, testable steps. The pacing stays action-oriented without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a genuine partnership, with the client's ideas shaping the plan and conversation. The counselor co-creates options rather than prescribing a finished solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the plan as the client's choice and adapts it to his energy and tolerance. Avoids coercive or prescriptive language while supporting self-regulation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own ideas, cues, and motivation. Could be stronger by more openly exploring values, confidence, and deeper reasons for change rather than using slightly leading questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power is shared well, with the client driving the session's direction and content. Any directive language remains aligned with the client's stated concerns.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Helps the client generate and affirm useful change talk around small, realistic steps. Could use more deliberate change-talk strategies, such as confidence scaling or reflecting back the client's strongest change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers and doubts without arguing or dismissing them. Could pivot more explicitly from concerns about failure toward the client's values, capacity, or reasons for change.
