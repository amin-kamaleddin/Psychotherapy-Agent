# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, warmth, and a nonjudgmental stance. Patient concerns are affirmed without pressure, labeling, or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurately captures ambivalence and self-protective concerns. The reflections could be even deeper in exploring underlying emotions such as shame or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on reducing smoking during work transitions and lunch. There is clear, progressive movement from journal review to plan building and slip response.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions, reflections, and invitations to co-construct the plan with the patient. The patient’s ideas clearly shape the direction and content of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported by avoiding directives and respecting the patient’s choice of a smaller test rather than quitting. The psychiatrist could further emphasize the patient’s ongoing freedom to modify or abandon the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the patient’s own motivations, ideas, and language rather than giving advice. Deeper motivational themes such as importance, confidence, or future self could be explored more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient is consistently treated as the expert on his own life, work context, and coping capacity. The interaction conveys shared expertise and genuine respect for the patient’s perspective.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits several change statements, including wanting to reduce, choosing a low-stakes test, and committing to Wednesday. These could be further amplified through deeper change talk categories and summarization.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and often redirected toward a manageable, data-based plan. Some later doubts are reflected but not fully transformed into confidence, commitment, or problem-solving.
