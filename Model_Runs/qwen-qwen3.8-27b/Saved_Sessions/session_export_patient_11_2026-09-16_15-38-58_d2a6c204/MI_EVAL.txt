**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out Amira's values, goals, and self-direction through open, evocative questions. Using scaling or confidence questions could further strengthen internal motivation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits substantial change talk and positively reframes Amira's language. More deliberate amplification through importance, confidence, or commitment questions could strengthen the effect.
