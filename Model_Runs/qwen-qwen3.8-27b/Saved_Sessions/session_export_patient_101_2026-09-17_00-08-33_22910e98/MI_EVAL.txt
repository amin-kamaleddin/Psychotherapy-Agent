# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, with strong normalization of ambivalence and protection of self-worth. Affirms the client’s efforts without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both cognitive conflict and emotional weight. Some reflections are slightly interpretive or assumptive and could be checked more explicitly with the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent, and strategic focus on reducing alcohol use and improving contraceptive reliability. The session progresses well from ambivalence to planning, coping, and self-efficacy without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively by building on the client’s ideas and making the plan feel jointly developed. Could be strengthened by using more explicit equal-partner language and checking in more often about the client’s preferred direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the client’s sense of choice, ownership, and self-direction. Avoids advice, pressure, or external rules, helping the plan feel personally generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the client’s own values, identity, and internal cues. A few leading or suggestive questions prevent the process from being fully client-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly shares power and lets the client’s ideas shape the work. Could become stronger by more explicitly co-creating the agenda and confirming the client’s comfort with the direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and expands the client’s own change talk, generating concrete plans and confidence language. The responses are consistently amplified and reflected back in ways that strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by acknowledging difficulty without arguing and redirecting toward workable solutions. Could improve by balancing statements of difficulty more quickly with resources or change-oriented possibilities.
