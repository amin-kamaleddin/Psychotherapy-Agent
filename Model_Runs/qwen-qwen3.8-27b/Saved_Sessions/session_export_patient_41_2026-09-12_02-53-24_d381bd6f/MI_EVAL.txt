# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates the difficulty of anxiety and avoidance. Some affirmations focus more on the plan than on affirming Elira as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture frustration and fear of failure, but some remain at the behavioral or plan level. Deeper emotional layers such as shame, disappointment, or self-criticism could be reflected more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on improving follow-through after practice and in school-related tasks. The conversation moves logically from barriers to a concrete, behavior-specific plan with strong momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira contributes key choices such as the five-minute timer, Tuesday, sticky note, and grounding cue, and the psychiatrist builds on them. The session remains slightly clinician-led because many options are generated by the psychiatrist before exploring Elira’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently invites Elira to choose the agenda, target action, day, reminder, phrase, and backup plan. Explicit permission to stop and reduced pressure strongly support her decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and choice-making successfully increase Elira’s confidence from around 4-5 to 8. The evocation is effective but more procedural than values-based, with less exploration of hope, identity, or deeper meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira is treated as the expert on her experience and chooses many components of the plan. However, the psychiatrist still provides many concrete options and structures the session, so partnership is strong but not fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira produces clear change statements such as 'I did what I planned,' 'I can do that,' and increased confidence. The psychiatrist reflects these positively but could more explicitly amplify values, competence, or future benefit.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoidance, doubt, and fear of failure are met without argument or judgment by making the task smaller, optional, and safer. A few sustain statements could be handled with more explicit double-sided reflection or ambivalence work.
