# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys respect, nonjudgment, and avoids lecturing or labeling. The warmth is sometimes formulaic and could be more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Richard's irritation, ritual, and resentment. The counselor does not fully follow the emerging fear of being seen as sick.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Focus drifts from smoking to marital conflict, boundaries, and harm-reduction. There is no sustained movement toward a specific change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses permission-based language and adjusts when Richard rejects suggestions. It is less fully collaborative because the psychiatrist often introduces the next topic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms choice and avoids commands or pressure. Support is slightly weakened by repeated offers of scripts and alternatives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early questions are evocative, but the session shifts to information, suggestions, and problem-solving. It misses key opportunities to elicit Richard's own reasons and fears.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and offers choices, preventing a fully authoritarian stance. However, the psychiatrist often supplies ideas rather than drawing out Richard's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are present but weakly developed. Change talk is not consistently drawn out, especially regarding health, identity, or marriage.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct confrontation and often respects the client's choice. However, it tolerates sustain talk more than strategically redirecting it toward personal values.
