# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating Thomas's perspective multiple times. Warmth is professional but doesn't reach deep unconditional positive regard; repeated circling back to alcohol could feel slightly pressuring.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening with effective complex reflections capturing ambivalence. Occasionally stays at the surface level of content rather than exploring the deeper emotional undercurrent, such as Thomas's defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session drifts across alcohol, exercise, walks, and general overwhelm without a clear strategic thread. Lacks a collaboratively established specific target behavior to work toward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with inclusive language and willingness to adjust based on Thomas's pushback. The psychiatrist still largely drives the conversation through questions, limiting true power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respects Thomas's right to choose and avoids directive language. Occasionally subtly implies problems to be solved (e.g., framing the drink as something to replace), slightly undermining autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of open-ended questions that draw out Thomas's own motivations. Could be more consistent; sometimes introduces own framing (e.g., suggesting exercise) rather than purely evoking the client's reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-authority stance and adjusts when Thomas pushes back. Thomas remains largely in a responsive role rather than co-creating the session agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked (e.g., feeling more energetic, knowing he should exercise more) but not consistently built upon or amplified. Missed opportunities to reflect and expand on change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids arguing against sustain talk but does not skillfully redirect or reframe it. Misses opportunities to use double-sided reflections to help Thomas resolve ambivalence.
