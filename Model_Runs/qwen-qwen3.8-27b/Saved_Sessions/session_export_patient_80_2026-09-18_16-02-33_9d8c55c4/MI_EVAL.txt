# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, never labeling Luka as a failure or expressing judgment about his drinking. Warmth is genuine and specific, normalizing self-criticism rather than dismissing it.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate empathy by capturing the emotional and relational meaning beneath Luka's words, including holding both relief and self-doubt simultaneously and distinguishing between reflex and choice. Reflections are specific, nuanced, and demonstrate genuine perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on target behaviors with logical flow from progress review to practical application. However, could have more explicitly tied back to the violence/aggression component, and there is a minor structural repetition near the end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation reads as a genuine dialogue between equals, with the psychiatrist building on Luka's language and positioning himself as a thinking partner. Session direction is substantially influenced by Luka's definitions, metaphors, and insights.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Luka's autonomy by affirming his right to define his own progress and set his own pace. Never prescribes a plan, imposes a timeline, or implies Luka should be further along in his change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Luka by asking questions that draw out his own values, goals, and insights rather than providing external reasons for change. All motivation and planning emerges organically from within the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Luka, using genuine invitations rather than techniques to extract predetermined answers. Luka's contributions substantially shape the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits rich change talk from Luka about his goals, progress definitions, and self-awareness. However, could have been more explicit in affirming and amplifying specific change talk statements, such as when Luka says he can 'work backwards.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill by validating complexity without challenging it, and never arguing against or minimizing the difficulty. The self-critical voice is named and its function honored rather than dismissed or fought against.
