# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys respect, nonjudgment, and acceptance of Sofia’s experience. It would be stronger with more explicit warmth, affirmation of strengths, and deeper normalization of past diet failures and self-critical patterns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening and perspective-taking accurately track Sofia’s concerns about pressure, failure, and self-negotiation. The empathy is strong but occasionally more conceptual than emotionally deep, with room to explore underlying fear, grief, and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on a concrete, low-pressure target behavior. It would benefit from more explicitly connecting the micro-behavior to broader goals such as energy, movement, and reducing emotional eating.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is genuinely co-constructed from Sofia’s ideas, preferences, and rejections. The psychiatrist builds on her contributions rather than imposing a preplanned intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is excellent, emphasizing choice, ownership, and permission rather than obligation or rules. The psychiatrist respects Sofia’s rejections of alarms, written rules, self-monitoring, and performance-based success.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes client-generated language through practical questions but does not deeply explore values, future self, confidence, or reasons for change. There is no use of scaling, DARN/CRAFT questions, or strategic deepening after strong change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is positioned as the expert on her own routines and needs, and her choices clearly shape the session. More explicit power-sharing would strengthen the partnership, since frequent option menus can feel somewhat direction-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers meaningful change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, affirm, or expand it. The session would benefit from deeper values-based questions, scaling, or DARN/CRAFT exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively softens sustain talk by lowering the behavioral threshold and avoiding debate about failures. More explicit double-sided reflections would more directly acknowledge and soften Sofia’s ambivalence.
