

# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 4  
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive; normalizes ambivalence and adherence difficulty. Affirming language reduces shame, though acceptance is somewhat task-focused and less individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 4  
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of ambivalence. Emotionally significant themes such as burden, sister’s complications, and prior failures are present but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 4  
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on diabetes medication adherence and uses confidence scaling to maintain momentum. Slightly diluted by repeated option-generating cycles after the client preferred simplicity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 3  
**Comments:** Client contributes to plan selection and final plan is co-constructed. Collaboration is uneven because many practical options and scripts are counselor-generated rather than fully client-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 4  
**Comments:** Uses invitational language and respects the client’s decision to keep the plan simple. Occasional over-scaffolding occurs by introducing additional options or scripts despite concerns about added burden.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 3  
**Comments:** Uses open questions and confidence scaling to elicit useful client-generated strategies. Evocation is limited by a quicker shift to practical solutions and less deep exploration of intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 3  
**Comments:** Demonstrates a collaborative stance and invites client input throughout. Partnership is weakened by frequent counselor-generated options, which makes the session more therapist-led than fully collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 3  
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited and occasionally reinforced, especially around confidence and practical plan components. Key change talk related to health, avoiding burden, and preventing complications is not consistently amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** [x] 3  
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not challenged or moralized, and barriers are normalized. The counselor does not consistently use double-sided reflection or redirect sustain talk toward change talk.
