# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Natalia’s experience without judgment and frames smoking as a coping response rather than a moral failing. There is strong affirmation of her strengths, intentions, and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses nuanced, accurate reflections that integrate the function of smoking with the emotional pain of ambivalence. The listening goes beyond paraphrasing and demonstrates strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking cessation, particularly the first cigarette and morning coping. The conversation moves clearly from broad ambivalence to a concrete, manageable change focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with the psychiatrist building on Natalia’s language and ideas rather than directing or lecturing. The counselor invites the client to evaluate what might work in her daily life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Natalia’s autonomy by affirming her right and capacity to make her own decisions. There is no pressure, advice-giving, or implicit coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits motivation from within Natalia by asking open questions that draw out her values, concerns, and ideas. Natalia generates much of the change-relevant content herself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly conveys that expertise resides with the client and consistently invites her perspective. The counselor avoids assuming the expert role or imposing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, client-centered questions effectively elicit change talk about wanting calm without smoke and considering a smaller test. Change talk could be further strengthened by amplifying change statements or using importance/confidence rulers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing, such as the cigarette’s role in helping Natalia cope and stay calm. More explicit reframing or strategic pivots from sustain talk toward discrepancy would strengthen this criterion.
