# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone throughout. Acceptance is strong, though it could more deeply affirm Marcus as a person struggling with a new diagnosis.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captures Marcus’s burden and worry about follow-through. However, some emotional cues are addressed briefly before moving into practical problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior of blood sugar monitoring. The conversation remains anchored to concrete, relevant implementation steps without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan reflects Marcus’s preferences and is developed through choice-based questions. The collaboration is strong, though the therapist often generates options and clinical content first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently offers options and respects Marcus’s limits. Autonomy support is solid, but the initial plan is somewhat directive before fully eliciting Marcus’s own preferred structure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are useful attempts to elicit Marcus’s own ideas and confidence. Evocation is limited because the session often shifts to advice-giving and practical planning before deeper internal motivation is explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marcus’s input clearly shapes the plan, showing a collaborative partnership. However, the psychiatrist sometimes takes on a more expert problem-solver role by generating specific plans and medical information.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses appropriate questions and a confidence scale to invite change talk. Client statements of ability and intention are not consistently reflected, amplified, or expanded.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally handled well through normalization, nonjudgment, and de-escalation of burden. More advanced strategies for resolving ambivalence, such as double-sided reflections, are not consistently used.
