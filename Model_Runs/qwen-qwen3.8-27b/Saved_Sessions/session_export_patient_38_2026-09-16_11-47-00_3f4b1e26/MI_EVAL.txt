# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive, validating Elena’s fear and reluctance without shaming or pressuring. Warmth is strong but somewhat formulaic, and deeper emotional undertones are not fully affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of mixed relief, shock, and hesitation, capturing Elena’s need for control and low pressure. Empathy remains mostly content-focused and does not consistently explore underlying embarrassment, judgment, or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on preventing another pregnancy scare and reducing fear while guiding Elena toward a concrete, low-pressure next step. Focus occasionally drifts to general anxiety management without consistently connecting it back to contraceptive decision-making.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Elena as a partner, co-creates the doctor question list, and preserves her wording to support active involvement. Collaboration is sometimes limited by the psychiatrist initiating multiple options and scripts before Elena has fully explored her own preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly affirms Elena’s right to choose, set the pace, and decline decisions, respecting her boundaries throughout. Autonomy support is slightly weakened by frequent counselor-generated options, scripts, and problem-solving suggestions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some open-ended questions and reflections of Elena’s values, but does not consistently deepen her change talk. The session frequently shifts to practical options and scripts instead of evoking her own reasons, resources, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a strong partnership stance by inviting input, treating Elena as the decision-maker, and adapting when she resists. Partnership is not fully maximized because the counselor often leads with multiple options before following Elena’s exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena expresses change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently ask evocative questions or reflect it in ways that strengthen motivation. The focus on practical options reduces opportunities to expand Elena’s desire, ability, need, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepts Elena’s sustain talk without arguing and keeps the session low-pressure. However, MI-specific strategies such as double-sided reflections and gentle discrepancy work are rarely used to soften resistance.
