# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, validating tone and avoided judgment throughout. The structured, list-based approach slightly reduced emotional warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking on the patient's distress and practical constraints. The shift to planning limited deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on the target behavior and moved the patient from vague goals to a concrete plan. Momentum toward change was consistently maintained.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient's ideas shaped the final plan, but the clinician often presented pre-formulated options rather than co-creating solutions. This leaned toward expert-led decision making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently offered choices and respected the patient's decisions about timing, tracking, and disclosure. Repeated suggestions and tips slightly bordered on directive behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effectively evoked goals and problem awareness early in the session. Later, the clinician often supplied solutions rather than eliciting the patient's own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative questions were used, but frequent structured options created an uneven power dynamic. The patient noted the clinician occasionally leaned into a fixer role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supported the patient's existing motivation and strong change talk, but did not actively expand or elicit new change talk later in the session. Focus on logistics limited deeper evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized barriers such as lack of time, fear of failure, and concern about feeling judged. Offered low-pressure, nonjudgmental solutions that redirected attention toward manageable steps.
