# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Leila’s difficulties. It would benefit from warmer, more individualized expressions of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses strong reflections and captures both practical and emotional dimensions of the client’s experience. Some responses remain generic and do not fully deepen understanding of underlying conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused and moves strategically from broad goals to a concrete first step. There is little drift and a clear trajectory toward sustainable behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor collaborates well and builds on the client’s own ideas rather than providing expert solutions. Fuller collaboration would include more explicit shared agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently frames exercise choices as the client’s own and avoids pressure, commands, or direct advice. Language around low stakes and personal choice strongly supports autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits the client’s own motivations, preferences, and problem-solving ideas. It would strengthen evocation by more explicitly drawing out deeper values and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative and client-directed rather than expert-driven. Partnership could be stronger with more explicit power-sharing language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits useful change talk but does not consistently amplify or summarize it. More deliberate strengthening of the client’s reasons for change would improve this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles barriers without reinforcing them and redirects the conversation toward change. Some pivots could be more strategically explicit to better resolve sustain talk.
