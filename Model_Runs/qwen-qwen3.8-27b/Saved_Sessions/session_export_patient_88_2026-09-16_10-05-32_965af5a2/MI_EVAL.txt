# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes Marcus’s perspective without shaming. It is strong but somewhat professional and formulaic, not reaching a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening effectively captures Marcus’s ambivalence, social framing, and key concerns. Empathy could deepen by exploring underlying emotions such as autonomy, embarrassment, or stress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use at parties and practical low-risk steps. It is generally well directed but occasionally becomes too prescriptive when Marcus resists formal planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites choices, checks permission, and adapts when Marcus pushes back, allowing the client to shape the plan. Collaboration is strong, though it remains somewhat guide-driven with multiple options supplied.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly emphasizes choice, optional change, and no pressure if Marcus slips. Autonomy support is good, though repeated suggestions for plans and scripts create subtle pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions about importance, confidence, and concerns successfully draw out Marcus’s own motivations and confidence increases. Evocation is limited by the counselor frequently supplying specific options and solutions before fully eliciting the client’s own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marcus’s choices shape the session, and the counselor follows the client rather than imposing a fixed plan. Partnership is strong but could be more fully client-led with less menu-driven guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk, such as holding a soda and keeping the phone charged. However, he often supplies options and scripts rather than deeply expanding Marcus’s own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is reflected without arguing, and ambivalence is normalized. The score is limited by initial invitations to sustain talk and continued planning suggestions after Marcus signals he wants to “wing it.”
