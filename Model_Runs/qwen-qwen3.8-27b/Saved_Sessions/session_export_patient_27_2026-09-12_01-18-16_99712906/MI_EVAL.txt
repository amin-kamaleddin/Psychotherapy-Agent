# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Daniela's concerns about disclosure and stigma. Acceptance is sometimes limited by rapid problem-solving and script-giving when Daniela expresses discomfort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some surface-level reflections acknowledge Daniela's fear and need to fit in, but deeper feelings about rejection, normalcy, and being perceived as a burden are often missed. The reflections remain mostly factual rather than fully emotionally attuned.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on the target behavior of disclosing diabetes to Lena and managing follow-up questions. The focus is somewhat narrow, emphasizing wording and scripts more than broader values or readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some collaborative invitations, such as choosing lines that sound like Daniela, but the psychiatrist remains the primary source of scripts and advice. Daniela's expressed discomfort with rehearsal and scripted language is not fully addressed by shifting to a client-led approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice with statements like 'you control how much you say' and 'pick one that sounds like you.' However, continued script provision and rehearsal prompts can undermine Daniela's sense of being in charge, especially when she expresses hesitation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session begins with an open question and occasionally invites personalization, but quickly shifts to advice-giving and scripted responses. Daniela's own motivations, values, concerns, and confidence are not consistently elicited or explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for input and offers choices, but the interaction remains expert-driven through repeated script suggestions. Daniela's resistance to robotic scripts is met with more language options rather than a full return to client-led exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela offers several pieces of change talk, such as wanting a brief, less awkward way to tell Lena. These statements are acknowledged but rarely expanded, amplified, or connected to stronger motivation or ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela's sustain talk, including fear of being seen differently and discomfort rehearsing aloud, is acknowledged but not strategically softened. Responses often redirect back to scripts or boundary statements rather than exploring ambivalence.
