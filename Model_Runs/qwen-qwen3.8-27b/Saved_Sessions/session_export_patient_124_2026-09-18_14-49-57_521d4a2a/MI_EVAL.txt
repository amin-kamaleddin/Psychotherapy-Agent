# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful with strong positive regard throughout. Occasionally slightly over-effusive, which subtly reduces the authenticity of the validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and nuanced perspective-taking, accurately capturing identity concerns and social belonging needs. Occasionally slightly misses the emotional core or reflects prematurely.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session follows a reasonable arc but drifts at times, particularly in the identity exploration that isn't clearly linked back to the target behavior. Ends open-endedly without connecting insights to a concrete change focus or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and follows the patient's lead effectively without imposing an agenda. Could be more active by inviting the patient to shape the session direction more explicitly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never pressures for a specific decision and acknowledges the patient's right to choose throughout. Could be strengthened with more explicit affirmations of the patient's ownership of both the decision and the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses good open-ended questions to invite self-exploration, but fails to consistently identify, amplify, and build on the change talk the patient offers. The evocation is present but not strategically leveraged.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance without an expert-driven or lecture-based approach. The tendency to over-reflect and over-affirm subtly undermines the egalitarian dynamic by keeping the psychiatrist in a leading role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk, particularly around the insight that marijuana isn't fixing anything long term. However, these statements are acknowledged but not explicitly reflected back, amplified, or explored further to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by validating the logic without arguing or dismissing, and holds both sides without taking sides. Could do more to help the patient see the discrepancy between her values and the sustain talk about social belonging.
