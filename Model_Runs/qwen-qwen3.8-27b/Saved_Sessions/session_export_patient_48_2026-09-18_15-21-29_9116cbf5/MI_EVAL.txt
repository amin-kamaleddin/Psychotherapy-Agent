**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates warmth, respect, and non-judgment, validating Farid's struggles without minimizing them. However, the warmth tends toward a somewhat clinical tone rather than deeply personal, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Excellent reflective listening and perspective-taking throughout, with reflections that go beyond simple paraphrasing to capture emotional meaning, underlying values, and the specific tension in Farid's experience. Particularly skilled complex reflections reframe the problem in ways that are both accurate and liberating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains an excellent, coherent focus on the target behavior while allowing natural exploration of barriers, with clear momentum moving from understanding the problem to identifying the psychological barrier to arriving at a concrete action. The session has a clear arc with appropriate pacing and no off-topic wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation feels like a joint exploration rather than a clinical interview, with collaborative language and building on Farid's ideas to create a sense of co-construction. However, there is a slight asymmetry in that the psychiatrist still asks all the questions and guides the flow, and could more explicitly invite Farid's agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Farid's autonomy and ownership, explicitly centering his own values and choices, and never prescribing specific exercises or implying obligation. This is one of the strongest aspects of the session, with the psychiatrist reframing what Farid already knows rather than filling silences or making choices for him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Farid's own motivations, insights, and language through well-timed open questions and reflective statements that lead to progressive self-discovery. The psychiatrist never gives information, provides a solution, or tells Farid what to do—everything emerges from within Farid.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with humble language and positions Farid as the expert on his own life, avoiding advice or imposed solutions. Could be elevated by more explicit power-sharing language and framing directional suggestions more clearly as invitations rather than gentle guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic open questions and by reflecting and amplifying Farid's own motivations and insights. Could push further into ability talk, confidence building, and explicit commitment language to strengthen the change talk further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by reflecting tension, exploring origins of rigid thinking, and using the 'both-and' approach rather than arguing against or reinforcing it. Could be slightly more active in pivoting toward change with double-sided reflections that acknowledge the pull of sustain talk while exploring independent values.
