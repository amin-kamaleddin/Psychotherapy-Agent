### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally conveys respect, normalizes concerns, and affirms progress. The stance remains slightly plan-focused, which can subtly reduce unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There are accurate reflections of Rafael's practical concerns and preferences. However, the emotional weight of ambivalence, discouragement, and fear of failure is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on returning to exercise and building a sustainable routine. Each question and reflection stays connected to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rafael meaningfully contributes to the plan, including timing, preparation, and preferred supports. The psychiatrist still drives much of the structure and supplies many of the options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist honors Rafael's desire to avoid being managed and frames the plan as self-directed. The continued addition of steps and repeated confidence checks can feel subtly pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation occurs through confidence scaling and questions about past success. The session relies more on suggestions and planning than on drawing out Rafael's deeper values and intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and builds the plan around Rafael's preferences. Collaboration is present, but the psychiatrist remains the primary architect of the intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rafael does express some change-oriented steps and modest confidence gains. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or amplify his own reasons, desires, or hope for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids reinforcing sustain talk and often normalizes Rafael's doubts. However, the session moves quickly into problem-solving rather than fully exploring and resolving ambivalence.
