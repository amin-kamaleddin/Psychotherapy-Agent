# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations and normalizations throughout. Delivery occasionally feels slightly scripted in its quote-reflect-affirm pattern, preventing a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate capture of the client's ambivalence and cyclical patterns. Empathy occasionally stays at the cognitive level without fully probing the emotional depth, such as the physical 'tightness' and nighttime experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on medication adherence with a logical arc from ambivalence to concrete plan. Could benefit from slightly more explicit anchoring of the target behavior at the start or through progress summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model collaborative practice with inclusive language and a clearly balanced power dynamic. The client's ideas—coffee anchor, one-week trial, physical note, success criteria—drive the session forward while the psychiatrist reflects and builds upon them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support with explicit rejection of a directive role and consistent attribution of motivation to the client. The final plan is entirely self-generated, with the psychiatrist never imposing decisions, timelines, or solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation with all motivation, solutions, and success criteria originating from within the client. The psychiatrist never states a reason for change externally, using strategic questions to draw out the client's own vision and plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook partnership with explicit resistance to the expert role and consistent return of the problem to the client's own knowledge. The session is substantially shaped by the client's contributions, which the psychiatrist amplifies rather than directs.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout area with consistent evocation and amplification of the client's own change talk through strategic questions and reflections. The session ends with a concrete, self-generated plan that was fully evoked rather than prescribed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional handling of sustain talk without ever challenging, arguing, or reinforcing the client's resistance. Each instance of doubt or minimization is validated and gently pivoted toward the client's own change-oriented statements.
