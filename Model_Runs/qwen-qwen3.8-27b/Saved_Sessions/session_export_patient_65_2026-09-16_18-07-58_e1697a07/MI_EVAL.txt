# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating stance throughout the session. Acceptance is strong but remains somewhat procedural and task-focused rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client's concerns and underlying tension, especially around uncertainty and performance anxiety. Empathy could be deeper by more consistently naming the emotional meaning beneath the practical worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior of completing court-mandated service on time. The counselor consistently guides the client toward a concrete, actionable next step without losing empathy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's choices shape the plan, and the counselor uses a joint problem-solving stance. Collaboration is strong, though the counselor still sets the sequence fairly tightly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids directives and repeatedly invites the client to choose what feels doable. Autonomy support is strong but could be more explicit in emphasizing the client's ownership and right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively draws out the client's own ideas, ability talk, and practical next steps. Evocation could be stronger by eliciting deeper values, importance, and intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and places responsibility with the client. Partnership is good but could be strengthened by more explicit acknowledgment of the client's expertise and agenda-setting role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers several statements of desire, ability, and commitment, and the counselor facilitates some client-led movement. The counselor could more consistently reflect, amplify, and build on the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoids challenging the client's doubts and gently acknowledges ambivalence. Softening sustain talk could be improved by more actively reframing hesitations into change-oriented exploration.
