# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demos consistent warm, unconditional positive regard throughout, validating Sofia's experience without judgment even when she is defensive or blame-oriented. Handles her defensive speech with presence and attunement rather than correction, communicating that she is worthy of respect regardless of behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go well beyond surface-level paraphrasing into genuine emotional attunement, capturing vulnerability beneath dismissive tone and identifying deeper existential pain beneath surface complaints. The final reflection on the guitar metaphor demonstrates remarkable precision in capturing both metaphorical and emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target concerns with logical progression from vision through identity to the guitar. However, direction is somewhat passive — the psychiatrist follows Sofia's lead very closely without strategically redirecting toward a more concrete target behavior or collaboratively identified next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the session as a joint exploration between equals, using inclusive non-prescriptive language and modeling vulnerability. Sofia's final comment — 'I didn't expect you to just... let it be a normal thing' — confirms the collaborative, non-hierarchical dynamic was felt and appreciated by the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Sofia's ownership of her change process, framing all decisions as her own and removing external pressure. Never pressures, gives advice, or implies she should do something specific, even when she expresses helplessness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Sofia's own reasons for change, values, and strengths through strategic questions and reflective amplification of her statements. Could be stronger with explicit confidence questions, scaling, or deeper eliciting of values-based motivation and future self-talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Sofia, never imposing an agenda or assuming to know what she needs. The session direction is shaped entirely by Sofia's contributions, with the psychiatrist acting as a facilitator of her own insights rather than an expert providing answers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questions and reflections that draw out Sofia's own motivations, culminating in her voluntary statement 'I'll keep practicing at least.' Could have pushed slightly further in explicitly reflecting and reinforcing that commitment or eliciting additional confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Sofia's blame and externalization skillfully by validating her experience without reinforcing the sustain talk, using double-sided reflections and reframing. Could more consistently and actively pivot away from the dominant blame frame toward Sofia's own agency and values.
