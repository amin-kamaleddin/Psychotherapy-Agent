# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently expresses nonjudgmental, accepting regard, including when Matteo resists performative recovery. Warmth and validation remain present without lecturing or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects ambivalence, values, and practical tensions, but does not consistently deepen into the emotional layer around self-judgment and social identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use in the context of ankle healing, moving logically from concern to a smaller step and practical monitoring. The target behavior remains central throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as an equal partner, using Matteo’s own language and integrating his ideas into the plan. The session feels co-created rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly invites Matteo to identify what would fit his life and respects his resistance to pressure or all-or-nothing expectations. Choice and low burden are strongly supported.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes motivation from within rather than supplying reasons to change, helping Matteo articulate his own concerns and practical checks. Questions draw out client-generated motivation and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Matteo as the expert on his social life and avoids acting as authority. Client contributions directly shape the plan, demonstrating strong power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful client-generated change talk, such as skipping keg nights and using a shoe check. Deeper or more systematic consolidation of confidence, commitment, and personal meaning is needed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and instead reflects it in a low-confrontation way. More consistent pivots from sustain talk toward change language would strengthen the performance.
