# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and respectful stance with validation of Irina’s perspective and choices. Slightly less than full acceptance because some reflections subtly attribute motives, making the stance a bit therapist-driven.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, nuanced reflections capture Irina’s ambivalence, exhaustion, and emotional burden. The counselor accurately validates both the desire for wine and concern about dependence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear focus on alcohol use as stress coping and quickly narrows to a specific evening routine. Each question moves toward a concrete, actionable behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction feels collaborative, using Irina’s own language and inviting her input. It falls short of full collaboration because some questions subtly guide toward preferred solutions without explicitly sharing control.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids directives and emphasizes choices that fit Irina’s life and values. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her freedom to choose and ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor successfully elicits Irina’s own reasons for change without lecturing about risks or consequences. Irina generates concrete change talk, including reducing to one glass and trying music first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is generally client-led, with Irina’s ideas shaping the plan. Partnership could be stronger by more explicitly inviting Irina to set the agenda and affirm her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively evokes and builds on Irina’s emerging change talk and concrete steps. It misses a full score by not adding confidence, commitment, or summary questions to strengthen the plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without argumentation, using balanced reflections to keep the conversation collaborative. The counselor could further strengthen this by using more explicit double-sided reflections on the perceived effectiveness of alcohol.
