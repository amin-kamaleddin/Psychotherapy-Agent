# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepted the client's preference for a simple, low-burden approach. Some comments were slightly evaluative, and emotional difficulty was not fully normalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected concerns about blame, busyness, and burnout. Deeper emotional meaning around shame or fear of quitting was not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Remained tightly focused on diabetes self-management and blood glucose monitoring. The session moved efficiently toward a concrete, behavior-specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan with meaningful client input. The psychiatrist still led much of the problem-solving by introducing specific solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently asked for permission and respected the client's limits. Some positive framing subtly steered the client toward the psychiatrist's preferred plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used scaling and doability questions to draw out motivation. Often filled gaps with suggestions rather than deeply eliciting the client's own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited client input and followed his preferences throughout the session. The interaction remained somewhat counselor-led due to the number of specific suggestions offered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited change talk around a realistic three-day plan and increasing confidence. Often shifted into planning instead of amplifying the client's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled resistance well by reframing concerns and creating low-effort coping strategies. Occasionally normalized avoidance without consistently connecting it back to client values or goals.
