# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses validating, nonjudgmental language and normalizes Daniel’s skepticism. It falls short of a 5 because the warmth is sometimes brief and the early didactic tone can feel corrective.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers adequate reflections about routine and pressure. However, the deeper emotional conflict of a health-focused marathoner being told his normal beer routine may be problematic is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on alcohol use, training, sleep, soreness, and follow-up without major tangents. It is not a 5 because the direction is sometimes broad and assessment-driven rather than tightly guided by Daniel’s own priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adjusts the plan toward a low-effort experiment. However, Daniel largely accepts clinician-proposed options rather than co-creating the plan as an equal expert in his own life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes choice, low pressure, and nonjudgment, and adapts when Daniel resists detailed tracking. It is not a 5 because many specific steps are still clinician-proposed rather than fully client-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks about effects on training, sleep, and mood, and Daniel offers some change-relevant concerns. However, the psychiatrist gives too much expert information instead of consistently evoking Daniel’s own values, discrepancies, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some partnership through questions and adaptation to Daniel’s preference for something low-effort. The interaction remains largely clinician-led, with assessment-style questions and a fairly specific proposed plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk about marathon performance, heavy legs, and trying a low-effort experiment. The change talk is not consistently expanded, amplified, or connected to Daniel’s deeper reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Daniel’s sustain talk and uses low-pressure language such as “experiment, not pass/fail.” However, more advanced MI strategies are not consistently used to reframe his routine, skepticism, or perceived lack of benefit.
