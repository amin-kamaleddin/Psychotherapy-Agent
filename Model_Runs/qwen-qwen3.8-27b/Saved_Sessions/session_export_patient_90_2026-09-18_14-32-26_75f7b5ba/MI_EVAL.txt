# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates respect, nonjudgment, and unconditional positive regard throughout the session. Could be elevated by more explicitly normalizing Marcus's struggle and affirming his worth beyond his diagnosis.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, with notable complex reflections capturing multiple emotions. Occasionally stays at the cognitive level rather than fully inhabiting the emotional depth of role reversal and identity loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains general focus but lacks a clear strategic arc toward a specific target behavior or actionable goal. Periodic summaries and gentle refocusing would strengthen forward momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with consistent use of open questions and following Marcus's lead throughout. Falls just short of a 5 because the psychiatrist occasionally introduces interpretive frames rather than purely co-constructing meaning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Marcus's autonomy, never using directive or controlling language, and honors his right to keep the dog. Could be elevated by more explicitly affirming Marcus's capacity for self-direction in moments of doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation skills, particularly in drawing out Marcus's internal values and connection to his former self. Could be more strategically targeted using DARN-CAT to deepen specific change talk elements like ability and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert-driven stance that positions Marcus as the expert on his own experience. Could be strengthened by more explicit power-sharing and co-creating session structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change talk emerges naturally but is not consistently amplified, reflected back, or strategically deepened. Missed opportunities to use scaling questions or elicit more specific change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating rather than pushing back, particularly around the dog and financial stress. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections to help Marcus actively resolve ambivalence.
