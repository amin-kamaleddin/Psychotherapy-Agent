# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms Jordan’s autonomy. This is strong but slightly reduced by a formulaic, task-oriented advisory tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Jordan’s ambivalence between independence and concern for his grandmother. However, empathy is not consistently deep, and reflections are sometimes followed quickly by advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on flu vaccination and related concerns without major drift. Direction is somewhat limited by frequent introduction of scripts, plans, and information.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Jordan’s preferences and offers choices, especially at the start. However, the session becomes more clinician-directed when the psychiatrist provides much of the content and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Autonomy is supported by affirming that the decision is Jordan’s and that the shot is not mandatory. This is weakened by prescriptive advice and detailed alternative protective plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist relies heavily on psychoeducation and option generation rather than eliciting Jordan’s own motivations. Emerging change talk is not consistently amplified or explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some partnership by asking for Jordan’s preferences and offering options. Partnership is uneven because the psychiatrist frequently assumes an expert role and returns to preselected plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are limited efforts to evoke or build on Jordan’s own change talk. When Jordan shows openness, the psychiatrist often responds with information, scripts, or options instead of amplifying his motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not confront Jordan but also does not effectively soften or redirect sustain talk. The response often keeps the status quo salient through detailed non-vaccination options and factual explanations.
