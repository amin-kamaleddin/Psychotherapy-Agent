# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows warmth, respect, and validation without judgment. However, responses to Elira’s self-critical and vulnerable moments remain somewhat procedural and could deepen further.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are generally accurate and helpful, especially early in the session. They often focus on content and problem-solving rather than naming and deepening Elira’s underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior of reducing avoidance and increasing follow-through. Planning, scaling, and action steps remain directly connected to the presenting concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and builds the plan with Elira’s contributions. However, the frequent use of clinician-generated options and drafts creates a somewhat consultant-like rather than fully equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elira is offered choices and is not pressured into larger commitments than she is ready for. Autonomy could be strengthened by eliciting more of her own solutions before offering structured options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses useful questions to draw out Elira’s ideas and confidence. However, the session often shifts quickly to providing options, limiting deeper evocation of client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and allows Elira to shape many parts of the plan. A stronger 5 would include more consistent power-sharing and less reliance on clinician-supplied choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elira produces meaningful change talk and some useful self-realizations. The psychiatrist affirms this but does not consistently amplify or strategically build on her change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without being challenged or dismissed. However, the psychiatrist often moves to problem-solving instead of using double-sided reflections to help resolve ambivalence.
