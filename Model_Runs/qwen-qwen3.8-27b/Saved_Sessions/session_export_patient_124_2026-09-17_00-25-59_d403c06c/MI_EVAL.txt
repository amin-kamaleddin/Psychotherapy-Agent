# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates respectful, nonjudgmental responses that validate ambivalence, though warmth is somewhat professional rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate complex reflections to track Sofia’s concerns and ambivalence, but sometimes repeats themes without fully exploring deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps focus on marijuana use and related stress, peers, money, and sleep, moving toward a small behavioral experiment; target behavior could be stated more explicitly.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly treats Sofia as a partner, building the plan from her own language rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice by framing small steps in Sofia’s terms and avoiding coercion, though some questions still assume movement toward change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits Sofia’s internal values and concerns, but does not consistently deepen change talk or develop confidence, desire, and ability.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys that Sofia knows herself best and invites her to lead interpretation, though agenda steering is still fairly deliberate.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites and amplifies emerging change talk, but does not systematically develop DARN-C language or fully extend the strongest change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk without challenging it, but does not always pivot effectively toward change, especially near the end.
