# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming stance throughout. The transactional safety check briefly interrupted the warmth but did not undermine respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate but remained at a surface level. The powerful moment about his daughter’s look was acknowledged but not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target behavior with a clear and logical progression. Brief safety detours created only minor departures from the MI process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative language and client choices were present, but the interaction remained more transactional than co-created. The safety plan was presented by the psychiatrist rather than built jointly with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supported the client’s choices and respected his decisions without pushing. The volume of specific suggestions slightly reduced the purely autonomous nature of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good evocative questions were used to draw out the client’s motivations. However, frequent shifts to information and practical tips interrupted deeper self-exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited client input and positioned him as the decision-maker. The structured safety plan and informational tips created moments where the psychiatrist was more expert-driven than an equal partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evoked several change talk statements from the client. These were not consistently amplified, deepened, or strategically reflected to strengthen the client’s own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist appropriately validated the client’s sustain talk without arguing against it. However, sophisticated MI strategies to actively soften or work with the sustain talk were absent.
