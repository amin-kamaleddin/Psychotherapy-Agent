# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, but support is sometimes procedural and quickly shifts to instructions. Acceptance could be deeper and more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects several key experiences, but empathy is often brief and shifts quickly to psychoeducation. Deeper tracking of the client’s emotional weight and self-consciousness is needed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and relevant focus on on-set social anxiety and specific triggers. Direction is strong, though it leans more toward symptom management than clarifying change goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor invites some input, but the session remains largely driven by counselor-provided lists, scripts, and techniques. The client does not co-define the focus or approach in a meaningful way.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some autonomy-supportive questions and options, but many prescribed techniques make the session feel directive. The client’s freedom to choose her own priorities could be affirmed more consistently.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are initial evocative questions, but the session quickly moves into assessment and skill instruction. The counselor does not consistently draw out the client’s own motivations, values, or hopes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaborative intent, such as offering choices and role-play, but the interaction remains largely counselor-led. The client is not consistently positioned as the expert on her own life and coping.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The client expresses some change talk, but the counselor rarely reflects, amplifies, or builds on it. More MI-consistent questions about personal reasons for change would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges doubts but often responds with reassurance and more instructions. More MI-consistent responses would include double-sided reflections and redirection toward the client’s own motivations.
