# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful throughout, with clear statements setting a safe tone. Occasionally leans toward formulaic affirmation rather than deeply individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate complex reflections that capture Diego's emotional nuance. Could go deeper into the underlying grief and loneliness implicit in carrying stress and unemployment alone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on harm reduction with logical progression from understanding to concrete actionable steps. Could more explicitly connect immediate behavioral targets to Diego's broader values or goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is structured as a joint exploration with Diego's contributions genuinely shaping the direction. Could be elevated by more explicitly co-creating the agenda at the outset.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary and consistent autonomy support throughout, from opening statements to the final follow-up question. Never pressures, imposes, or implies what Diego should do beyond his own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Diego's own motivations, readiness, and rationale through open-ended questions. Could explore deeper identity and values beyond the immediate behavioral level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance with shared ownership language. Could be strengthened by more explicitly acknowledging Diego as the expert in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reflects and amplifies Diego's change talk, naming his progress and reinforcing his agency. Could push further into eliciting more detailed change talk about values, hopes, and identity beyond harm reduction.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged implicitly through acceptance but not actively softened using advanced techniques like double-sided reflections. Adequate but not exceptional in reframing the relief function of heroin use.
