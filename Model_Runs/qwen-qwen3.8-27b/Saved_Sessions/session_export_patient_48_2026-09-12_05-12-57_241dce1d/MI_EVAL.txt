### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and nonjudgmental stance throughout. Validation is consistent but somewhat formulaic.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Reflective listening captures Farid's mixed motivation and fear of overcommitment. Some reflections remain surface-level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on increasing physical activity and moves toward concrete steps. The focus occasionally shifts to tracking mechanics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Farid's concerns actively shape the conversation and options are adapted accordingly. The psychiatrist still supplies many ready-made frameworks.

**Comments:** Choice and client control are repeatedly emphasized. Explicit reinforcement of the right to choose no option would strengthen this further.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Some client-generated motivation emerges, especially around energy and fitness. Evocation is limited by frequent problem-solving and clinician-provided justifications.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts to Farid's preferences. The interaction remains somewhat clinician-led in offering solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Meaningful change goals are identified, such as feeling less wiped out and not falling apart. The session shifts quickly into structured options.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Concerns are validated without confrontation. More deliberate reframing of sustain talk into motivation would strengthen this criterion.
