# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations and explicit normalization of Samir's struggle. Slight over-accommodating tendency (e.g., 'You're not failing') occasionally borders on rescuing rather than simply reflecting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections that capture meaning and internal conflict (e.g., 'You're carrying a critic and a patient at the same time'). Could go deeper into emotional undercurrents, particularly when Samir expresses identity-level threats like 'I'm not sure who I am then.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking and drinking reduction tied to asthma context, with logical progression from broad goals to concrete targets. Lacks a brief consolidating summary near the end to strengthen closure and clarify next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as joint exploration using curious, non-hierarchical language and avoids lecturing. Somewhat one-directional in that the psychiatrist chooses topics and questions while Samir primarily responds.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional throughout — explicitly removes perceived control, affirms Samir's expertise in his own choices, and never prescribes or pressures. Even when Samir minimizes progress, the psychiatrist explores what's already there rather than pushing for more.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The evocative question about what Samir's lungs would say is a standout moment generating rich, personal change talk. Could go further in evoking values and identity beyond the physical (e.g., fatherhood, self-image) to deepen the motivational foundation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Samir with language like 'You know your life best' and 'Not what anyone else expects.' Could be strengthened with more explicit power-sharing language and active negotiation of session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully generates concrete behavioral plans and amplifies Samir's own insights about committing to a number. Could more systematically develop all four DARN/CRAFT categories, particularly confidence and ability statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Textbook softening — validates the function of smoking and drinking without reinforcing their permanence, and pivots to what Samir CAN do. Could more explicitly use double-sided reflections to hold both the sustain and change sides in tension.
