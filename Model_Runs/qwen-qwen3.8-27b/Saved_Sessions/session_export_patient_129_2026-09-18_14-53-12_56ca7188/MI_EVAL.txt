# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no hint of judgment, disapproval, or moralizing. Validates resistance and frustration throughout, framing even small shifts as positive without being pushy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are specific, nuanced, and emotionally attuned rather than generic. Particularly skillful in tracking subtle shifts in language (e.g., noticing the word 'actually') and helping Tyler see his own evolving perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on substance use and its relational consequences without drifting. Could benefit from a slightly clearer trajectory toward a concrete next step or thought, though pacing is appropriate for precontemplation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is genuinely shaped by Tyler's contributions, with his specific words becoming the building blocks for interventions. Power dynamic is balanced, and meaning is consistently returned to Tyler rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never pressures Tyler to admit wrongdoing or commit to change; presents options without prescribing. Validates his lack of control over being in the session and consistently honors his right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All questions are designed to draw out Tyler's own values and internal contradictions rather than providing external reasons for change. The 'truly there for someone' question is a particularly sophisticated evocation that subtly invites self-reflection without direct challenge.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions self as a learner rather than expert, using open exploratory questions throughout. Never lectures, provides information, or positions as having more knowledge about Tyler's own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully notices and reflects back Tyler's own change talk in a timely, non-pushy way. Well-paced for precontemplation, though could have gone slightly deeper in eliciting personal reasons for reconsidering behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk masterfully by focusing on feelings rather than debating right/wrong about substance use. Textbook example of rolling with resistance—immediately reflects and amplifies Tyler's own shifts (e.g., 'not the enemy') to help him notice his movement.
