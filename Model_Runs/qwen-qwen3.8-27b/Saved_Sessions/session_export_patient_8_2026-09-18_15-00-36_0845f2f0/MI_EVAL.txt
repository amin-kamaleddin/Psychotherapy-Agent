### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental acceptance throughout, with strong moments of validation (e.g., affirming Amina's self-compassion). Occasionally leans toward validating the problem rather than offering unconditional positive regard for Amina as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with reflections that consistently move beyond surface content to emotional and experiential meaning. The reframe of dissociative scrolling and the final question about quiet moments demonstrate deep attunement to the core of Amina's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from presenting concerns through function, mechanism, and emotional undercurrent, maintaining focus on the target behavior. The session lacks a concrete directional closure or next step, which is a minor gap.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed conversation shaped entirely by Amina's contributions, with the psychiatrist modeling collaborative language and adapting in real-time. The willingness to take back the 'replace it' framing when challenged is a strong indicator of authentic collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently places the locus of control with Amina through open questions and avoids directive advice. Could be strengthened by more explicit acknowledgment of her freedom to choose (or not choose) change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All insights emerge from Amina's own internal resources rather than being provided externally. Could be deepened by more explicit follow-up and amplification when meaningful change talk appears (e.g., 'maybe I'd notice when I'm done').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing with the psychiatrist taking back positions when Amina pushes back and letting the conversation be shaped by her responses. Occasional moments of subtle leading (e.g., 'So maybe it's not about the phone being the enemy, but...') prevent a perfect score.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-timed questions effectively evoke values-based, ability, and commitment change talk from Amina. The evocation is strong but lacks consistent amplification or reinforcement of the change talk moments as they emerge.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates the function of the behavior (connection, stress relief) without reinforcing it, and strategically reframes to reduce the perceived cost of change. The sustain talk about connection and stress is explored somewhat extensively and could be more efficiently pivoted toward change.
