# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and explicitly accepts Aisha’s limits about alcohol, medication, and complete cessation. Warm support is present but sometimes generic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers brief supportive statements but rarely reflects Aisha’s emotional burden, maternal concerns, or ambivalence. Responses remain largely factual and task-focused instead of emotionally attuned.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing evening smoking, with consistent attention to cutoffs, triggers, tracking, and follow-up. Repetition of the same plan is the main limitation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Aisha’s preferences are accepted, including the 9 p.m. cutoff, pack storage, and one-week check-in. However, the psychiatrist supplies most plan content rather than helping Aisha generate more of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Aisha is allowed to choose the focus, decline medication, and avoid a complete quit, with some explicit choice-giving. The repeated instructional details can still feel directive rather than fully autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers many strategies rather than eliciting Aisha’s own reasons, values, and confidence for change. The readiness ruler is used but lacks follow-up scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some open questions and incorporates Aisha’s ideas into the plan. Yet the conversation remains more guided by the psychiatrist’s suggestions than co-created with Aisha.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Aisha makes several change-talk statements, but the psychiatrist mostly affirms and moves to planning. Missed opportunities include deeper scaling and value-based questions to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing, lecturing, or pressuring Aisha when she declines alcohol discussion, medication, or abrupt quit. It does not actively use reflections or rolling techniques to work through ambivalence.
