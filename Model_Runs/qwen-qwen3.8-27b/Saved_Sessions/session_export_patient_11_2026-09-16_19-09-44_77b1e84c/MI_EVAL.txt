# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, validating stance throughout. Acceptance could be strengthened by explicitly affirming the client's worth independent of performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening to capture exhaustion, dread, and self-criticism. Some reflections remain summary-like and miss deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on building a small, sustainable physical activity routine. Each question advances the conversation toward a concrete, doable action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on the client's ideas rather than imposing a plan. Direct invitations to co-create the next step would make collaboration more explicit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids directives and reinforces the client's ownership of the plan. Explicitly normalizing the possibility of modifying or abandoning the plan would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client's own motivations and implementation plan. Evocation of deeper values and confidence scaling would make it more fully developmental.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as shared and allows the client's ideas to guide the session. More explicit invitations for the client to shape the agenda would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits client-generated change language around small, doable actions. Change talk could be deepened by connecting it more explicitly to values, energy, mood, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes concerns and redirects them toward a low-stakes behavior without arguing. More use of double-sided reflections would help hold the ambivalence more fully.
