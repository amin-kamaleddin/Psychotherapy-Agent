# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is consistently respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental. However, repeated problem-solving suggestions subtly imply that certain choices are preferable.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects ambivalence and the emotional meaning of the smoking ritual. Empathy is limited by frequent shifts from reflection to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking and parenting concerns. However, the direction leans toward practical behavior change rather than deeper MI-style exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and offers options rather than directing the client. Collaboration is limited because the counselor frequently supplies the ideas for the client to choose from.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor affirms the client’s choices and readiness level. Repeated suggestions to try specific actions may reduce the sense of autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are few open-ended questions and limited effort to elicit the client’s own motivations. The session remains more advice-focused than evocative.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses collaborative language and asks for the client’s preferences. The interaction becomes uneven when the counselor repeatedly presents options for the client to select.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited reinforcement of the client’s own change talk. Important concerns about modeling for her children are not fully explored or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor accepts sustain talk without arguing or confronting. However, the session remains centered on small adjustments rather than gently redirecting toward change.
