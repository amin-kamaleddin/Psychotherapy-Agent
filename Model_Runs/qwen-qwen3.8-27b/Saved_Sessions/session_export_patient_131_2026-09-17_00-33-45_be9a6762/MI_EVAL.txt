# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, validating, and culturally responsive throughout. It falls short of a 5 because unconditional positive regard is not always explicit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and tracks ambivalence well. It could go deeper into shame, fear of rejection, and parental loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear target behavior: changing housing/support to reduce alcohol use and support program completion and parenting. Direction is purposeful without being controlling.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the plan using Wiremu's own language and ideas. Some questions steer toward a particular reframe rather than fully inviting open exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** There is strong support for choice, dignity, and self-direction. The psychiatrist avoids pressure and helps Wiremu frame help-seeking as autonomous.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Wiremu's own motivations, values, and concrete plans. It could use more structured evocation such as scaling or deeper future imagery.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Wiremu is consistently treated as the expert on his own life and goals. A few direct reframes reduce the level of mutual co-construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections successfully elicit specific change talk and self-owned planning. The process could be more systematic with readiness, confidence, or importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and gently reframed without argument or dismissal. It could be strengthened with double-sided reflections and more explicit resolution of ambivalence.
