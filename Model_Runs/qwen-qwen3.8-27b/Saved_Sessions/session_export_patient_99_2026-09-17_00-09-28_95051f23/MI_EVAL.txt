# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys respect, warmth, and nonjudgmental acceptance throughout the session. Client statements are affirmed without correction, shaming, or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician demonstrates accurate reflective listening and captures much of the client's ambivalence. However, reflections remain somewhat interpretive and could explore social and emotional concerns more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a coherent focus on alcohol use, ankle injury, healing, and a concrete behavior plan. The plan could be more explicitly connected back to the broader treatment goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is strongly collaborative, with the plan built directly from the client's own ideas and preferences. The client is clearly positioned as a co-author of the strategy rather than a passive recipient of advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids pressure and repeatedly validates the client's preference for a non-rigid, self-directed approach. The clinician could more explicitly affirm the client's full choice to modify or abandon the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician frequently uses open-ended questions to draw out the client's own ideas, cues, and problem-solving. Evocation would be stronger if it more fully explored values, identity, confidence, and deeper motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician clearly establishes a collaborative, noncontrolling stance and treats the client as the expert on his own life. The client's contributions consistently shape the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively evokes ability-focused change talk and helps the client develop a practical self-regulation plan. Broader change talk, such as desire, importance, need, or confidence, could have been developed more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician handles sustain talk by normalizing and validating the client's concerns rather than arguing against them. Some barriers remained in focus longer than necessary before pivoting toward values or small successes.
