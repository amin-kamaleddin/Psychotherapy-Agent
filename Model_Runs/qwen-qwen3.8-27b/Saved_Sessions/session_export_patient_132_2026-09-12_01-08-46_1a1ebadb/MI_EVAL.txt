# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment while validating Yasmin's struggles. However, the rapid shift into advice and structure occasionally weakens the consistent affirming alliance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Empathy is present but inconsistent, with some useful reflections on the restriction–overeating–guilt cycle. The counselor often moves to planning or psychoeducation before fully processing emotional and identity-based concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, relevant focus on eating, exercise, body image, and sustainable habits. The conversation generally moves coherently from concerns to practical next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts to Yasmin's preferences, but the session remains largely counselor-driven. Yasmin often chooses from pre-made options rather than fully co-creating the direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Yasmin's autonomy is acknowledged through choices and agreement to avoid crash dieting. However, frequent specific directives and structured plans limit space for her to generate and own her own approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some early evocative questions are used, but much of the session is driven by advice, definitions, and behavioral planning. The counselor does not consistently draw out Yasmin's own reasons, values, or motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial partnership through asking Yasmin to choose among options and incorporating some of her decisions. The interaction is still expert-led, with the psychiatrist frequently setting the agenda and providing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Yasmin expresses clear change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently notice, amplify, or build on it. The counselor tends to move into concrete plans rather than developing Yasmin's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk around weight loss, the scale, and calorie adjustment remains influential and is not consistently reframed. The session does not fully shift focus toward broader values such as self-trust, body neutrality, or sustainable well-being.
