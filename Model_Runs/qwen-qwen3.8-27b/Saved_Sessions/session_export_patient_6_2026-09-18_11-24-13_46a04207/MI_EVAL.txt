# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opens with warm, non-judgmental stance and consistently validates Alexei's honesty throughout. However, the warmth follows a standard MI script and doesn't reach the level of deep, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening with nuanced reflections like 'performing' and 'survival mode.' Some reflections slightly over-interpret or lead the client toward meaning he hasn't fully articulated yet.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus with logical progression from the incident through values to practical next steps. Could have more explicitly connected to the referral reason and broader goals like college soccer and scholarships.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a genuine dialogue with 'we' and 'us' language, and Alexei's responses consistently shape the direction. The practical solution emerges entirely from Alexei, with the psychiatrist simply inviting him to generate it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Alexei's ownership of decisions without prescribing, pressuring, or implying expectations. The final question is fully autonomy-supportive, leaving the answer entirely to Alexei.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk through well-designed open questions that draw out Alexei's own reasoning. The evocation stays at the surface level and could explore values and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as collaborative exploration and follows Alexei's lead without imposing an agenda. There is no hint of power imbalance or predetermined script.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively connects Alexei's values (earned achievement, structure, payoff) to his decision-making, generating substantial change talk. Could have pushed further on confidence and ability using DARN-C framework.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges minimization without reinforcing it, using subtle reframing like the 'performing' reflection. The sustain talk is relatively light in this session, limiting the opportunity to demonstrate this skill.
