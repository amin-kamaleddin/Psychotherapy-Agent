# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with validation, but the warmth feels formulaic and procedural.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflects surface-level frustration accurately but misses deeper relational, identity-based, and ambivalent meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Loses clear focus on tobacco cessation and drifts toward venting, safety checks, and termination without a strategic next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites preferences and avoids taking over, but the interaction remains therapist-managed rather than genuinely co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and avoids pressure, though the repeated emphasis on ending the session limits support for ongoing agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Uses some open invitations, but does not draw out the client's values, confidence, or future-oriented reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers collaborative choices and respects the client's decision to end, but the psychiatrist still directs the agenda and closing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Offers generic options and a scripted response but does not effectively evoke the client's own language or motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk but does not reframe or gently redirect the client's resistance toward change.
