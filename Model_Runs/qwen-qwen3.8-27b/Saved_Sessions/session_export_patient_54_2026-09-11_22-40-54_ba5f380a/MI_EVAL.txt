# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirms the client’s autonomy and choices. Warmth remains somewhat procedural and does not fully normalize the client’s deeper ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of the client’s factual and strategic concerns. However, empathy remains uneven and does not fully explore the client’s underlying emotions and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and practical prevention plans. The direction is somewhat logistics-heavy and less consistently tied to the client’s values and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choices and incorporates the client’s preferred strategies. Collaboration is uneven because the counselor frequently supplies information, scripts, and suggestions rather than consistently co-creating the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports the client as the decision-maker and avoids coercive language. Frequent offers of scripts and suggestions can subtly shift the interaction toward advice-giving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is limited, as the session is dominated by psychoeducation and practical advice. The client’s own motivations, values, and confidence are not consistently drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some collaboration by offering options and respecting the client’s input. The partnership becomes uneven when the counselor repeatedly shifts into expert problem-solving and script delivery.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges and affirms the client’s change talk. There is limited use of amplifying reflections, summaries, or scaling to deepen and expand motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with the client and generally does not reinforce sustain talk. MI-consistent strategies such as double-sided reflections and rolling with resistance are not consistently used.
