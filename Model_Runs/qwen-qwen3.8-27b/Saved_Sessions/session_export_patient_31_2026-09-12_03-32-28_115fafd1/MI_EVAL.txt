# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor is respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming of the client’s limited goal and boundary. Acceptance is somewhat procedural and plan-focused rather than deeper affirmation of the person and struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections of ambivalence and practical concerns. Empathy is mostly cognitive, with missed emotional exploration of stress, identity, or meaning tied to smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stays focused on daytime shift cravings and narrows to a specific lozenge plan. There is early drift into general NRT education and a reduction plan before fully aligning with the client’s limited goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborates on gum vs. lozenge, cue, and quantity. The interaction remains advisor-centered because the counselor supplies much of the structure rather than fully co-creating it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice, flexibility, and low commitment well after the client resists a quit plan. The earlier directive push toward a patch and reduction plan briefly conflicted with the client’s readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes the client’s own cue, preference, and confidence. The counselor gives more information than elicits deeper motivations, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes client input in choices and the “keys” cue. Overall, the intervention remains counselor-led, especially with detailed dosing and a plan not fully requested.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk through confidence and willingness questions. Change talk is not consistently evoked, amplified, or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accepts sustain talk without arguing or moralizing. Lacks active softening through double-sided reflections, values-based exploration, or resolution of ambivalence.
