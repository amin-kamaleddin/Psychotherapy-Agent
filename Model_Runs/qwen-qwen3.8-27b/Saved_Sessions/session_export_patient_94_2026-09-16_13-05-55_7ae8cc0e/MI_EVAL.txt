# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizes Maria’s concerns. The warmth is strong but somewhat scripted, and the opening could still feel like pointing out nonadherence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Maria’s doubts, time pressure, and fear of ER episodes. Empathy is solid but mostly content-focused rather than deeply exploratory of underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on asthma management, barriers, and a concrete next step. It remains efficient and aligned with the referral concern despite Maria’s urgency and resistance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Maria’s input and adjusts language to make it less stiff. Collaboration is strong, though the clinician still leads the agenda and provides scripted options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria’s choice is repeatedly respected, with clear statements that she need not commit now. Autonomy support is strong, though repeated clinician-provided options and scripts can partially steer the conversation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks useful questions but often supplies information and options before Maria generates her own motivations. More evocative questions would help elicit deeper client-generated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses permission, choices, and client-centered language, supporting a collaborative stance. Partnership is good but still somewhat clinician-driven in setting the agenda and drafting statements.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are meaningful change-talk moments, especially around avoiding another ER visit and not saying no forever. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen Maria’s own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria’s sustain talk is generally reflected and validated without argument. Softening is effective, though more strategic double-sided reflections could more clearly pivot toward safety and change.
