# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent, warm unconditional positive regard throughout the entire session without any moment of disapproval or conditional regard. The psychiatrist validates Mateo's experience, normalizes his ambivalence, and reinforces his agency as a closing affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates nuanced, accurate perspective-taking that goes beyond simple restatement, capturing complex emotional dynamics like the mailbox metaphor and the relational tension with his wife. Reflections articulate Mateo's internal world with depth and specificity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from exploration to a concrete action step. Slightly less streamlined in transitions between phases, with a few moments of lingering in exploration without tightening the thread toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a genuine dialogue between equals, with the psychiatrist using tentative language and inviting Mateo's perspective throughout. Mateo's responses directly shape the conversation, and the final action plan is entirely his own idea refined through dialogue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Mateo's right to choose with zero pressure or implication that he should do anything. The closing explicitly affirms his agency, and when Mateo sets a boundary about not promising to read the list, the psychiatrist honors it without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every piece of motivation comes from Mateo's mouth through strategic questions that draw out his own goals, insights, and problem-solving. The psychiatrist never provides external reasons for change, uses fear appeals, or argues for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Mateo as the expert on his own experience and the primary agent of change, never assuming expertise or prescribing solutions. The closing 'That's you figuring out your own path' reinforces that the work is his own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk through strategic questions and amplification, drawing out ability talk and commitment language from Mateo. Could have been more systematic in exploring desire, ability, need, and commitment, such as using confidence or commitment scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill by validating barriers without challenging or minimizing them, then gently pivoting toward possibility. The psychiatrist never reinforces the 'you can't' narrative and consistently finds a way to acknowledge barriers while maintaining a forward orientation.
