# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates warmth, respect, and nonjudgment while affirming Adrian without moralizing methamphetamine use. Acceptance is strong but somewhat formulaic, with less normalization of emotional difficulty such as fear, shame, or referral stress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses reflective listening to accurately capture ambivalence, dual goals, and the role of paranoia in continued use. Falls short of a 5 because emotional depth around the infection, shame, or vulnerability is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on safer methamphetamine use and health-risk reduction, moving from ambivalence to a practical plan. Could be stronger by explicitly stating the target behavior and using summaries to anchor direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses an equal-partner stance and repeatedly invites Adrian’s perspective. The session direction is shaped collaboratively around his suggestions rather than a predetermined intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports Adrian’s perception of choice without pressure to quit, affirming his agency and respecting his own definition of success.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from Adrian rather than providing reasons for change. Could be deeper by using scaling questions, exploring underlying values, and more explicitly amplifying his change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Adrian as an expert on his own life and lets his ideas shape the session. Could be stronger by explicitly naming his expertise, negotiating the agenda, and reducing structured guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client language about safer use, avoiding infection, one-hit limits, and test-kit use. Could be stronger by more systematically exploring DARN-CAT, scaling confidence or importance, and amplifying change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rolls with sustain talk rather than arguing, redirecting toward safety and practical options. Handling is inconsistent at a higher level, with limited double-sided reflections and at times accepting the harm-reduction plan as the endpoint.
