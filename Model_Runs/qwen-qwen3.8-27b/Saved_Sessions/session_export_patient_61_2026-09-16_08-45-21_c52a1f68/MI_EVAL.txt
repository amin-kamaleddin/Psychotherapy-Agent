# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizes Jonathan's overwhelm without blame. Acceptance is somewhat professional and quickly shifts into planning, reducing the sense of deeper warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture burden, fatigue, and the desire to keep things simple. They become somewhat formulaic and do not consistently explore deeper emotions such as fear, loss of control, or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on diabetes self-management and narrows a broad concern into one concrete bedtime check. There is some drift into repeated safety problem-solving, but it remains clinically relevant.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and works toward a low-burden plan, but provides much of the structure and medical information. Jonathan largely responds to the options presented rather than generating his own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly asks what feels doable, offers choices, and respects Jonathan's preference to start with checks only. Repeated safety-focused suggestions, however, can feel somewhat persuasive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions, confidence scaling, and follow-up scaling, which are useful evocative moves. Much of the session relies on providing advice and reasons rather than eliciting Jonathan's own values and personal motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and offers choices, which supports some partnership. The stance becomes expert-driven with detailed medical advice, thresholds, and repeated safety planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jonathan offers small pieces of change talk, such as agreeing to one check and a one-time bedside candy. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without direct dispute and offers simpler, lower-effort options. There is limited use of double-sided reflections, reframing, or redirection toward Jonathan's values and reasons for change.
