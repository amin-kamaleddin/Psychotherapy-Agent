# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and avoids shaming or pushing abstinence. Warmth is solid but somewhat procedural, with repeated safety and plan restatements making it feel more administrative than personally affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers brief supportive reflections, but they remain mostly surface-level. There are missed opportunities to reflect the emotional complexity behind Luka’s frustration, fear of failure, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is clear, consistent, and strongly focused on reducing alcohol-related risk and violent outbursts through concrete behavioral goals. It is behaviorally directed, but could more consistently connect that direction to Luka’s internal motivation and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some shared decision-making, as Luka chooses the walk, start date, and check-in time. However, the psychiatrist repeatedly introduces and restates a pre-structured plan, which limits truly collaborative co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and respects some of Luka’s preferences, such as a one-week trial and no scorekeeping. The interaction remains somewhat directive, with repeated instructions that can reduce Luka’s sense of personal ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence ruler and questions about next steps elicit some motivation and commitment. Evocation remains limited because the session moves quickly into advice and planning without deeply exploring Luka’s values, fears, or personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for Luka’s input and allows him to influence several decisions. The session is still therapist-driven, especially when the psychiatrist presents a detailed plan before fully exploring Luka’s own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Luka produces some useful change talk, including choosing the walk, starting Monday, and being honest about partial progress. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect, or expand this change talk in a way that strengthens motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and normalizes urges and slips without imposing abstinence. There is less strategic redirection of sustain talk toward change, such as using double-sided reflections or elaborating on Luka’s own reasons to move.
