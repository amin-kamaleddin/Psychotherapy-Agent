# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental stance and respects privacy, avoiding blame or labeling. Acceptance remains somewhat task-focused and could more explicitly affirm Maya’s courage and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are several accurate reflections, but the empathy stays somewhat surface-level. Important emotional cues, such as fear of social rejection and feeling trapped, are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use, its impact on school, and a concrete next step. The focus sometimes narrows too quickly into problem-solving before fully exploring motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maya is invited to choose options, and her preference to step outside is respected. The interaction is still more counselor-driven, with the psychiatrist providing much of the plan content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by offering options, respecting privacy, and accepting that Maya will not make a full promise. Some repeated suggestions and push toward a tiny experiment remain somewhat directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence rating and open start show some evocation, but the psychiatrist does not consistently draw out Maya’s own reasons for change. More questions about values, goals, and self-efficacy would strengthen this area.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maya is asked to choose, which supports some partnership. The overall framing remains advisor-driven, with the psychiatrist presenting a menu of strategies rather than co-creating the agenda with Maya.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms some change talk and supports a selected plan. Change talk is not consistently elicited or expanded through deeper questions about motivation and desired outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not argued against or dismissed, which is appropriate. However, much of it is met with problem-solving rather than reflective softening or double-sided reflections.
