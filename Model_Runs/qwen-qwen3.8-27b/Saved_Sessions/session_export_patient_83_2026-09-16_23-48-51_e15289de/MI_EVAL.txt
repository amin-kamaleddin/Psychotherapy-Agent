# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and treated Mai's concerns as reasonable. Her uncertainty about the statin was met without judgment, correction, or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor showed strong reflective listening by capturing Mai's need for clarity, control, and self-care. However, the empathy could have gone deeper into underlying emotions such as frustration with poor communication or anxiety about side effects.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stayed focused on understanding the statin and creating a practical adherence plan. Some broader opening prompts and a fragmented therapist turn slightly weakened the overall focus and flow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor and Mai worked together as equal partners to develop the explanation, note, and call-versus-wait thresholds. Mai's ideas directly shaped the final plan, making it practical and personally meaningful.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly emphasized Mai's choice, ownership, and role as the expert on her own life. Rather than pushing compliance, the counselor helped Mai define her own criteria for confidence and readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor elicited Mai's internal values and reasons for change rather than providing external reasons to take the statin. Mai's motivation became clearly self-generated, linking the medication to protecting her heart and maintaining her quality of life.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently positioned Mai as the authority on her own experience, body, and decisions. The session was guided by Mai's preferences, priorities, and contributions rather than by the counselor's agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully drew out specific, confident change talk about taking the statin and creating a reminder system. Mai moved from uncertainty to clear statements of intention and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor validated Mai's doubts and sustain talk without arguing or dismissing them. However, the counselor could have used more explicit double-sided reflections or reframing to turn ambivalence into change talk.
