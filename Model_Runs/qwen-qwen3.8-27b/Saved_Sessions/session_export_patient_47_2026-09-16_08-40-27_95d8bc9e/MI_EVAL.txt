# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental language with no labeling or moralizing about drinking. Acceptance occasionally tied to her making the desired choice rather than unconditional affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections of meaning, goal, and discomfort. Remains more cognitive than emotional; fear of failure and closing nervousness go unaddressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on reducing alcohol use with purposeful movement toward a concrete plan. Slightly diluted by a broad opening question and later tactical problem-solving.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Joint recap and collaborative problem-solving with Evelyn's ideas visibly shaping the plan. Psychiatrist frequently leads with clinician-generated strategies rather than eliciting Evelyn's own solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice-based language throughout and plan adapted to Evelyn's preferences. Could more explicitly normalize her full freedom to choose not to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions and the confidence scale but frequently shifts into advising and supplying specific strategies. Client's underlying motivations and own reasons not deeply drawn out or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evelyn's contributions substantially shape the final plan, showing strong partnership. Too many clinician-supplied options and scripts make the interaction more clinician-generated than client-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk via scaling questions but does not consistently reinforce or amplify it. Session shifts toward practical problem-solving rather than deepening her own motivation language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging values and incorporating them without challenging or dismissing. Could more actively pivot sustain talk toward change by exploring integration with her goal.
