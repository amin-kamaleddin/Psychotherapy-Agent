# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental, while reducing blame around stress and practical limits. However, warmth felt somewhat formulaic and occasionally prioritized problem-solving over deeper acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist showed good understanding of Daniela’s urgency, stress, and fear of appearing desperate. Deeper emotional themes such as shame, guilt, or fear of failing as a mother were not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the immediate childcare crisis and moved coherently toward a practical plan. The direction was clear but somewhat narrow and crisis-managed rather than linked to longer-term goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniela actively edited the text and plan, and the psychiatrist adapted to her preferences. However, the psychiatrist still supplied most of the content and maintained a stronger lead in the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered options, avoided pressure, and let Daniela choose her preferred actions and timing. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming that the decision and responsibility belong entirely to Daniela.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** This was the weakest area, as the psychiatrist relied more on advising, scripts, and problem-solving than on drawing out Daniela’s own motivation. There was limited effort to elicit her values, confidence, reasons for change, or preferred future outcomes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used collaborative language and invited input throughout. However, the interaction remained more functionally collaborative than genuinely equal, with the psychiatrist as the main agenda-setter.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniela produced some change talk and the psychiatrist affirmed it with supportive comments. The psychiatrist did not deeply expand or connect this change talk to values, goals, confidence, or long-term motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist did not argue with or reinforce Daniela’s barriers, but often moved quickly into practical scripts instead of fully addressing resistance. There was limited use of double-sided reflections or roll-with-resistance to soften doubts.
