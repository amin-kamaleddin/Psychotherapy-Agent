# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Samir’s frustrations and avoiding argument about medication dislike. Warmth is sometimes brief and formulaic, limiting deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture Samir’s view that pills are an interruption and family monitoring feels infantilizing. Empathy remains mainly cognitive and misses deeper emotional undertones and the final insight about the medication ritual.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on medication adherence, family oversight, and a practical plan. It is occasionally weakened by multiple broad opening questions and an abrupt ending after a key statement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites input, offers choices, and co-constructs a minimal plan around Samir’s preferences. Collaboration is less balanced when the psychiatrist still leads with structured options and summaries.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy support is strong, validating Samir’s need for space, control, and a low-effort approach. It is slightly diluted when the psychiatrist introduces specific options and a follow-up plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks about past successes and what would prompt Samir to return, eliciting some client material. Evocation is underdeveloped because the session quickly shifts to options and misses the chance to deepen Samir’s own change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and allows Samir to shape the plan, including the fridge note and direct family message. Partnership is not fully equal because the psychiatrist continues to drive the agenda early on.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are brief efforts to evoke and reinforce change talk, such as noting Samir manages well when given space. The session does not consistently expand Samir’s limited statements into stronger commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Samir’s dislike of medication and acknowledges autonomy concerns. It does not consistently soften sustain talk, especially when exploring reasons for skipping or leaving the final ritual complaint unaddressed.
