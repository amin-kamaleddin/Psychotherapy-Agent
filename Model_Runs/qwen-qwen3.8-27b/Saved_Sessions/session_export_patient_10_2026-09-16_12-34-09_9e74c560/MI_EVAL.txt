# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms Amina’s efforts and validates her frustration and worry. This shows clear acceptance, though at times the counselor quickly moves to problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor uses repeated, accurate reflections of Amina’s fear of burdening her sister and anxiety about interview demands. This demonstrates strong empathic responsiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target employment barrier and moves from childcare concerns to concrete action steps. The counselor repeatedly steers toward a doable next step and role-play.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Amina to choose options and co-creates scripts, showing joint problem-solving. Some responses supply multiple scripts before Amina selects, making it strong but slightly less fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor uses nonjudgmental language such as "if you’d like" and "which feels most helpful," leaving key decisions to Amina. This strongly supports her freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits Amina’s priorities and confidence with open questions and affirmations. It could be stronger by asking more evocative questions about her values and reasons for choosing a specific step.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Amina as the expert on her life and invites her to direct the session. Occasional reliance on a list of options makes the partnership strong rather than exemplary.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor highlights Amina’s skills, confidence, and successful practice to amplify her change talk. Further reflecting her spontaneous change statements would make it stronger.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor validates Amina’s barriers, such as fear of burdening her sister and fear of sounding difficult, then reframes them as manageable. It offers alternative action steps without confronting or reinforcing the status quo.
