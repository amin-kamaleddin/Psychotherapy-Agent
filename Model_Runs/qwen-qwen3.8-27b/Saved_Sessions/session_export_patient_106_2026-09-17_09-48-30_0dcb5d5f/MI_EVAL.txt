# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and free of shaming or moralizing. Could more explicitly affirm the client as a person and parent beyond the smoking issue.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses highly accurate, sophisticated reflections that capture ambivalence and the emotional/identity-level meaning of smoking. Minor room to add more compassion toward the client's self-critical voice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stays focused on the target function of smoking as a quick stress break and explores non-smoking alternatives. Would benefit from more explicit summaries tying themes back to smoking cessation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client's concerns, ideas, and criteria strongly shape the conversation. The interaction reflects genuine co-construction and equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids pressure, advice, and directive problem-solving, positioning the client as the decision-maker. Could be stronger with more explicit language affirming client ownership of the decision and change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the client's own values, needs, and change-relevant motivations rather than lecturing. Could deepen discrepancy, readiness/confidence, and strategic use of the client's change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the client to define the problem and criteria for change rather than prescribing solutions. A few therapist-shaped questions slightly limit full power-sharing and agenda co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and reflects change talk around guilt, sleep, self-permission, and not setting a bad example. Could use stronger amplification, summarizing, and exploration of readiness or next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk by reflecting both sides and normalizing the client's concerns without confrontation or reinforcement. Could more consistently use double-sided reflections and pivot sustain talk toward change talk.
