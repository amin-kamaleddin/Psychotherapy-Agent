# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, though occasional corrective reframing can subtly minimize the client's worry. Overall supportive, but not uniformly warm enough for a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening is used for the practical plan and ambivalence. However, there is less explicit reflection of the client's emotional stress, fear of being cranky, and anxiety about work or home.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on smoking cessation goals, including the quit date, medication decision, side-effect concerns, and coping plan. Each response advances a concrete direction toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs options with the client rather than prescribing a plan. Client preferences meaningfully shape the safety and coping plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through repeated emphasis on the client's choice, ownership, and decision-making. Advice is framed as support for the client's own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits client-generated strategies and language, such as the quit date, timer, walk, and texting Karen. Deeper evocation of intrinsic motivation, values, and self-efficacy would strengthen the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the expert on his own life, and his ideas directly shape the session. Power sharing and mutual decision-making are strong throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is frequently elicited, including statements such as 'Not today' and 'I can handle ten minutes.' The evocation is effective but mostly behavioral and strategic rather than deeply values- or confidence-based.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about stress, deadlines, side effects, and slips is not reinforced and is often redirected toward coping and agency. Sometimes barriers remain centered rather than fully transformed into change momentum.
