# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and acknowledges Sofia's experience without blame. Acceptance is clear but could be deepened through stronger normalization of her ambivalence and explicit recognition of the difficulty of being referred.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows accurate reflective listening and tracks key themes such as stress, cost, and the speed of relief. However, empathy misses deeper emotional and identity-based concerns, especially around loss of control and family expectations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session remains generally focused on stress and marijuana use rather than unrelated topics. However, the target change goal, such as reduction, delay, or increased awareness, is not clearly established or revisited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative elements through inclusive language and invitations for Sofia to share her perspective. The conversation remains more therapist-guided than truly collaborative, with limited agenda negotiation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Sofia's autonomy by avoiding pressure, direct advice, or coercive language. Some questions subtly assume a preferred direction, such as trying a faster non-marijuana alternative.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist tries to draw out Sofia's own goals and views without lecturing. Evocation remains limited because important concerns, especially automaticity and loss of control, are not fully explored or reflected back.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia's perspective and signals that her experience matters. However, the interaction remains therapist-led, with less co-creation of the agenda and a stronger push toward finding an alternative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia expresses meaningful change statements, such as wanting to feel normal and be in control. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, deepen, or connect this change talk to values, goals, or motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing and acknowledges some sustain talk without pushing back. Later sustain talk about automatic use and minimizing the issue is not balanced with double-sided reflections or discrepancy.
