# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment or labeling. Affirmations are specific and individualized to Samuel's words, such as explicitly rejecting the 'addict' label and reframing caution as wisdom.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and precise reflections that capture both the content and emotional undertone of Samuel's experience. Multiple sophisticated reflections identify the functional impact beyond the substance and the quiet, non-dramatic quality of his goals.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on smoking reduction with a logical session arc from review to goal-setting to commitment. Could be slightly more strategic in pivoting from feeling exploration back to target behavior and in explicitly summarizing the session's key themes before closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional equal-partner dynamic where Samuel's ideas are the primary content and the psychiatrist's contributions add structure and momentum. All decisions—the lunch walk strategy, the data reframe, and the one clean lunch goal—are co-constructed with Samuel as the driver.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Samuel's ownership and freedom to choose through language like 'a plan that's yours, not someone else's prescription' and 'you're in the driver's seat of your own experiment.' Respects his self-set pace, goal, and timeline without pushing for more.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major insight in the session is Samuel's own, drawn out through strategic questions and reflective amplification rather than external reasons for change. The final declaration of readiness ('I think I can do that') is entirely Samuel's, not the psychiatrist's prescription.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise about change resides within Samuel, using language that positions him as the authority on his own experience. The session is shaped entirely by Samuel's contributions, with the psychiatrist serving as facilitator and mirror rather than director.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully and consistently evokes change talk through open-ended questions, evocative follow-ups, and affirming Samuel's own reframes. Samuel's change talk grows in specificity and confidence across the session, moving from vague intention to a concrete, self-identified goal.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill, never reinforcing or dismissing it but consistently finding the 'change grain' within each expression of doubt. When Samuel says 'I'm cheating myself,' the psychiatrist reflects the underlying meaning, allowing Samuel to reframe it himself.
