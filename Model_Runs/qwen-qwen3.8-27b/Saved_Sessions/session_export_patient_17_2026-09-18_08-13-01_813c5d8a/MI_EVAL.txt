# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with genuine validation of the client's experience and worth. However, the warmth occasionally borders on performative or over-structured, preventing a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional reflective listening with complex, emotionally attuned reflections that progressively deepen throughout the session. The reframe that 'it wasn't that you were stronger— the shape of your day was different' is a textbook example of high-level MI empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear logical progression from acknowledgment through emotional exploration to evocation of change. However, the session ends open-endedly without gently steering toward a concrete next step or goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with the client's ideas clearly driving the conversation's direction. However, the interaction remains primarily one-directional in question-asking rather than involving explicit co-creation of the session agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's agency, never prescribes, and validates her right to want more than 'doing well.' Could more explicitly reinforce her ownership of the process at key decision points.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws motivation from within the client through strategic questions without providing external reasons for change. The evocation is strong but episodic rather than systematically woven into every interaction.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance with language like 'I'm curious' and 'What do you make of that?' that positions the client as partner. Could include more explicit negotiation of direction and shared decision-making about where to go next.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through strategic questions and effectively reflects and amplifies it when it emerges. Could more systematically elicit DARN-CAT statements or use targeted evocation questions to deepen and reinforce change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively using double-sided reflections that validate without reinforcing, keeping the door open for change. Could have been more nuanced in one moment where sustain talk was embedded within the client's question about busyness versus loneliness.
