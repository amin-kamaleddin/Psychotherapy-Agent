# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional acceptance throughout, consistently normalizing Kenji's ambivalence and reluctance without judgment. Statements like 'there's no judgment in this room' and 'you were already there, I just gave it a shape' powerfully remove any sense of deficit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture both cognitive and emotional dimensions, including complex reflections like holding two contradictory truths in tension. The final reflection elevating Kenji's own words into 'being awake in it' is masterful.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains good focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from general life to a concrete small goal. The session could have been slightly stronger in facilitating a clear transition to a next step and summarizing direction to build momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional co-construction where Kenji's own words ('drifting,' 'awake,' 'noticing') shape the entire session direction. The psychiatrist never imposes an agenda, and the power balance is genuinely equal throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support with explicit honoring of Kenji's pace and refusal to pressure for commitment. 'That's yours, not mine' clearly positions Kenji as the owner of his own motivation and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Kenji's own motivations through open questions and by building on his language rather than providing external reasons for change. Could be slightly stronger with more explicit exploration of values or confidence scaling to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently egalitarian power dynamic where the psychiatrist explicitly positions Kenji as the expert on his own experience. The session is clearly shaped by Kenji's contributions, pacing, and boundaries rather than a predefined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk emerges naturally and is well-reinforced through amplification and return to Kenji. Could be strengthened with more structured elicitation such as confidence scaling or questions about what he would do with new awareness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively validates sustain talk without pushing back, gently opening space for the other side through curiosity rather than challenge. The limited sample of sustain talk in the session makes it harder to fully assess the depth of this skill.
