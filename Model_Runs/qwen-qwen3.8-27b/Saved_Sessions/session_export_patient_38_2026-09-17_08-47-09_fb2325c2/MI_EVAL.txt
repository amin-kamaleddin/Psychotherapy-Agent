# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.
There is no evidence of blame, moralizing, or pressure, and Elena's boundaries are clearly affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurately captures both content and emotion.
There are a few missed opportunities to deepen affective resonance around regret and fear of losing control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on the pregnancy scare and contraception concerns.
It lacks consistent strategic progression, summarization, and movement toward a clear client-driven next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Elena's input and builds on her own language, making the session feel collaborative.
Some therapist-generated suggestions slightly limit Elena's independent role in leading the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena's freedom, timing, and ownership of the decision are consistently honored.
There is no coercion or excessive persuasion, and suggestions remain optional and client-centered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes useful client language through open questions.
It does not consistently elicit confidence, self-efficacy, or commitment through scaling or future-oriented work.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is generally positioned as the expert on her own life and choices.
Some therapist-generated suggestions and interpretations slightly reduce the fully client-led nature of the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Useful change talk is elicited, but it is not consistently amplified or developed.
The psychiatrist does not explicitly evoke desire, ability, reasons, need, or commitment in a strategic way.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena's hesitation is handled respectfully without arguing or minimizing.
The psychiatrist does not consistently use strategic reflections to transform sustain talk into a platform for change.
