# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms Evelyn’s willingness to engage. Acceptance is present but feels somewhat formulaic and could be more emotionally individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of Evelyn’s ambivalence, safety, and recent fall. Empathy remains mostly content-level and does not consistently explore deeper emotional or relational significance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on alcohol use and produces a concrete two-week plan. Direction is more procedural and plan-driven than strategically motivational for a client still in contemplation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is collaborative in tone, and Evelyn’s ideas shape the final plan. Collaboration would be stronger with more open-ended invitation for her to generate options rather than selecting from clinician-provided menus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evelyn’s choice is respected through optional language, refusal acceptance, and no pressure to adopt tracking or formal check-ins. Some autonomy is slightly reduced by the volume of clinician-generated optional strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some client-generated motivation through importance/confidence ratings and asks what leads to heavier drinking. Evocation is limited because powerful topics such as the fall and son’s concern are not fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids an authoritarian stance and repeatedly invites Evelyn’s input into the plan. Partnership is slightly constrained by the structured, checklist-like pacing and frequency of clinician-led suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evelyn offers useful change talk, and the psychiatrist affirms her insight and confidence. However, the therapist often moves quickly into planning instead of deepening or amplifying her own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist appropriately avoids arguing with Evelyn’s sustain talk and respects her preference against tracking or formal check-ins. There are missed opportunities to gently explore the ambivalence behind her minimization and self-management statements.
