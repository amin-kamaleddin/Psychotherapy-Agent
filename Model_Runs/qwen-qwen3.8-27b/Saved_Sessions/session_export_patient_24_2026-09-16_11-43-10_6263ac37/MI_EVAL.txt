# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone without labeling or blaming Daniel. Slightly procedural, and could more explicitly normalize ambivalence and express deeper unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflected Daniel’s perspective on the survey, training goals, and discomfort with being monitored. Reflections were often content-focused rather than deeply affective, leaving underlying ambivalence underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on alcohol use, training goals, and a low-risk trial. Some time on logistics and wording reduced momentum, and a brief consolidation of change talk would strengthen focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-created a practical plan and adapted to Daniel’s preferences and objections. Somewhat directive with detailed clinical information and structure, which limited fully peer-like collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized choice, ownership, and low pressure throughout. Effectively supported Daniel’s freedom to decide and avoid coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened with client reflection and offered a choice of focus. The session shifted into psychoeducation and planning before fully eliciting values, discrepancy, and intrinsic motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited Daniel’s input and adjusted the plan to his preferences, reflecting power sharing. Did not explicitly position Daniel as the expert on his body and readiness, and remained somewhat informative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk through choice, doable steps, and affirmations. Limited use of values clarification, importance/confidence rulers, or deeper exploration of personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolled with resistance and avoided challenging Daniel for not being ready to quit. Could have further reframed residual sustain talk about uncertainty and feeling graded.
