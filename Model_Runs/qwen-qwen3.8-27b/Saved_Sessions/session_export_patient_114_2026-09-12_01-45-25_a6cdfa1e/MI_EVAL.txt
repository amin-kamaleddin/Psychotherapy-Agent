# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, validating tone and nonjudgmental statements. Support is present but feels formulaic and quickly shifts into practical problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows adequate empathy and correctly identifies Ryan's stress and degree concerns. Reflections are generally surface-level and do not deeply explore his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays broadly connected to the incident, academic risk, and drinking concerns. Focus becomes inconsistent as the conversation shifts toward practical steps and scripts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some collaborative invitations for Ryan to choose options. The psychiatrist frequently takes an expert-driven role, providing detailed advice rather than co-creating a plan with Ryan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for Ryan's preferences and acknowledges some choice. Autonomy support is inconsistent because structured plans and scripts are repeatedly offered without affirming Ryan's freedom to adapt or reject them.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes limited attempts to elicit Ryan's own motivations and priorities. The session moves into advice-giving rather than exploring Ryan's values, discrepancy, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist sometimes invites Ryan's input and preferences. Collaboration remains superficial because Ryan is not consistently treated as the expert on his own life and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ryan offers meaningful change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or build on it. Opportunities to deepen change talk, confidence, or goals are missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ryan expresses significant sustain talk about fitting in and not wanting to stop drinking. The psychiatrist acknowledges but does not effectively soften or redirect this ambivalence toward change.
