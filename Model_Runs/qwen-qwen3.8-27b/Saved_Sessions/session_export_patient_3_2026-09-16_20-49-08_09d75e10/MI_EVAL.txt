# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and avoids blame, judgment, or shame. Language such as 'welcome here exactly as you are' and normalization of struggle supports a strong accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing ambivalence, fatigue, self-criticism, and the meaning behind Aisha's statements. The reflections go beyond summary to validate her experience while maintaining precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on movement/exercise and body image without drifting into unrelated topics. There is a coherent trajectory from motivation to a small, testable plan and a self-regulation cue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is co-created rather than prescribed, with Aisha's ideas shaping the content and direction. The interaction reflects two partners working together rather than an expert delivering recommendations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports choice using non-pressuring language such as 'might,' 'could,' and 'if you tried.' The plan remains framed as something Aisha is designing for herself, reinforcing ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is evoked from Aisha rather than supplied by the psychiatrist, leading to strong client-generated change talk. Questions help her articulate her own reasons, goals, and a self-supportive phrase.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Aisha is treated as the expert on her own life, energy, schedule, and preferences. The psychiatrist draws out her ideas and builds on them rather than overriding or steering her toward a pre-planned solution.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and builds on concrete change talk, including specific plans and a self-generated coping phrase. Slight deduction because there are moments of framing or subtle steering instead of allowing more client elaboration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Concerns about fatigue, burnout, and missed days are acknowledged and redirected without argument or dismissal. The rating is 4 because some reframing is more direct than MI-style reflections and the sustain talk is not strongly developed.
