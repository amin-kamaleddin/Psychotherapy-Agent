# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental stance with multiple genuine affirmations throughout the session. Slightly formulaic phrasing patterns emerge, and deeper acknowledgment of Tyler's fear of social exclusion could strengthen the response.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures Tyler's internal conflict between self-perception as someone in control and the reality of the incident. Occasionally stays one level below the emotional depth Tyler is reaching toward, particularly around identity threat and social exclusion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook example of maintaining clear focus on alcohol use in social contexts with a logical therapeutic arc. Each step builds on the previous one without drift, always tethered to Tyler's stated values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conversation feels like a joint exploration with Tyler's responses clearly shaping the session's direction. Slightly therapist-led in question sequence and could benefit from more explicit co-creation language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** No directive language or advice-giving; the psychiatrist consistently invites exploration without prescribing. Could more explicitly affirm Tyler's freedom to choose at key decision points.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist's strongest skill in this session — all key insights and change talk are drawn from Tyler's own mouth through strategic questions. Never introduces external arguments or reasons for change; consistently matches Tyler's contemplative stage.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Tyler as the expert on his own experience with inclusive, non-directive language. Could be strengthened by more explicitly acknowledging Tyler's own wisdom and capacity in the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of strategic questions that lead Tyler to articulate his own reasons for change, producing multiple instances of change talk. Could push slightly further in evoking confidence statements, such as leveraging Tyler's mention of friends who drink less.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating concerns while gently expanding the frame, never arguing or dismissing. Could more explicitly help Tyler distinguish between context and choice in moments where he frames drinking as simply 'what everyone does.'
