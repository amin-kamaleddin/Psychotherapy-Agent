# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, non-judgmental tone and explicitly normalizes the client's struggle. Occasionally reads slightly scripted rather than deeply personal, which prevents a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening with powerful, nuanced reflections throughout. Missed an opportunity to explicitly reflect the grief and fear that surfaced when the client mentioned his uncle's death.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, coherent arc from the workplace trigger through to a concrete self-identified step. Slight widening of focus during the values-elicitation about his children represents a brief tangential moment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook example of collaboration; the session's direction is entirely shaped by the client's responses with no imposed agenda. The power dynamic is genuinely equal throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional and consistent affirmation of the client's freedom to choose; never pressures, imposes a timeline, or sets standards for change. The response 'I'm not here to judge whether that's enough' is a masterful example of returning ownership to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The standout strength of the session; every piece of motivation is elicited from within the client rather than provided externally. The psychiatrist never cites statistics, argues, or states a reason for quitting.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a partnership between equals, explicitly locating expertise in the client. Never once makes a declaration or gives advice; every intervention is a question or reflection.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes genuine change talk through open questions about future self and values. Could have further amplified and expanded on the client's change talk once it appeared.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with notable skill, using double-sided reflections and avoiding confrontation. One moment of minimization could have been more explicitly acknowledged before pivoting.
