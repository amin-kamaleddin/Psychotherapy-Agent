### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates respectful, nonjudgmental regard and validation without lecturing or criticizing. Some subtle corrective reassurance prevents a fully accepting stance.

### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Julian to identify solutions and incorporates his ideas into the plan. The counselor still takes a somewhat organizing and evaluative role.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses evocative questions to draw out Julian's concerns and practical motivators. The session does not deeply elicit intrinsic values, confidence, or stronger internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Julian as a contributor by asking for his perspective and following his suggestions. Occasional guiding interpretations slightly reduce the sense of equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session elicits some change talk related to avoiding problems and reducing stress. The change talk remains mostly practical and compliance-oriented rather than deeply motivational.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Julian's concerns without arguing and gently redirects toward action. Stronger double-sided reflections could further soften sustain talk and resolve ambivalence.
