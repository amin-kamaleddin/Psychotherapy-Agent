# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Lars's struggles without blaming or moralizing. However, the acceptance feels somewhat procedural rather than deeply warm or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects surface experiences and triggers adequately but does not fully explore deeper emotions such as embarrassment, guilt, and ambivalence. Empathic responses remain somewhat factual and are often interrupted by quick shifts to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on relevant reentry concerns, including boredom, peer pressure, alcohol use, and avoiding reoffending. The counselor keeps the discussion practical, though it could better connect tasks to Lars's broader values and future goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor provides many solutions, scripts, and plans, making the interaction feel more expert-led than collaborative. Lars contributes useful ideas, but the counselor remains the primary problem-solver rather than a partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and invites input, supporting some degree of choice. However, frequent specific directives, such as exact scripts, times, and action steps, reduce the emphasis on Lars's ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor begins with some open questions but quickly shifts to providing information, scripts, and practical steps. There is limited effort to draw out Lars's own motivations, confidence, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lars is asked for preferences at times, but the overall stance remains expert-driven and solution-focused. The counselor does not consistently communicate that Lars is the expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lars spontaneously expresses motivation and interest, but the counselor does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen this change talk. Questions are often closed and task-oriented rather than evocative.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with sustain talk and uses some normalizing language to reduce shame. However, ambivalence is often addressed by adding more tactics rather than exploring the conflicting sides with deeper reflections.
