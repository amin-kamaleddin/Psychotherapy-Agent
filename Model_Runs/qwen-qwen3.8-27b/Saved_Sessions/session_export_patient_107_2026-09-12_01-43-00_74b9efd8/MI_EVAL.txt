# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, but acceptance feels transactional. It mainly validates the plan rather than expressing sustained, warm, unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Empathy stays largely factual and logistical, missing Nathaniel’s core ambivalence about coffee, alertness, and jitters. Complex reflections capturing his conflict, identity, or fears are absent or minimal.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on the target behavior and a concrete 2 PM cutoff with a follow-up plan. Direction is effective, though it is more advice-driven than MI-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nathaniel’s preferences shape parts of the plan, especially the 2 PM cutoff and simplified reminder wording. However, the psychiatrist supplies most of the content and structure rather than co-creating with the client as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are moments of choice and acceptance, such as allowing a simple test and accepting no tracking. Yet the repeated directives and specific recommendations undercut stronger autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is limited; the psychiatrist offers guidelines, tips, and a plan rather than drawing out Nathaniel’s own motivations. There are few questions about values, importance, confidence, or what success would mean to him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and incorporates the client’s preferences, showing partial partnership. The counselor mostly assumes an expert role by supplying caffeine guidelines, tapering advice, and behavioral tips, limiting true collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Nathaniel offers some change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen it. The conversation moves quickly into advice and planning, which limits development of change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with sustain talk and accepts smaller steps. However, key statements about enjoying the boost and coffee taste are bypassed rather than softened through reflection or discrepancy.
