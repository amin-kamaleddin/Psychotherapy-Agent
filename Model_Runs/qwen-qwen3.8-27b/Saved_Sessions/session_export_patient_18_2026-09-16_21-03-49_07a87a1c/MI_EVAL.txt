# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician communicates a consistently nonjudgmental, normalizing, and affirming stance. Specific praise validates the client's effort and autonomy without shame or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client's practical concerns and preferences. Deeper attention to underlying emotions, such as worry or fear of losing control, could strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on alcohol risk, personal limits, and a practical pause strategy. The conversation moves consistently toward a concrete, behavior-specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician invites the client to shape the plan and builds directly on her contributions. The interaction feels like a genuine joint exploration rather than clinician-directed advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly emphasizes choice, ownership, and the absence of rules or pressure. The client is consistently positioned as the decision-maker in her own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively draws out the client's own goals, limits, and solutions rather than supplying external reasons for change. Deeper exploration of core values and long-term motivation could further strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treats the client as the primary expert on her own life and choices. The client's answers directly guide the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions frequently elicit the client's own safety-plan ideas and reasons for change. More explicit elicitation of confidence, commitment, and ability could strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client concerns are acknowledged and reframed without argument, correction, or pressure. Sustain talk is handled in a way that supports autonomy while keeping the plan change-compatible.
