# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Dante’s ambivalence. The warmth is consistent but somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers accurate cognitive reflections of Dante’s goals and concerns. Reflections are less emotionally attuned to his fear of failure and hesitation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on the target behavior. Consistently guides the conversation toward a concrete, realistic action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively by building the plan around Dante’s preferences and choices. Frequent clinician-provided options reduce the sense of full co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports Dante’s autonomy by offering choices and asking for his preferences. Repeated scripts and planning introduce a mildly directive feel.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Draws out some internal motivation by exploring values, past success, and confidence. Evocation is intermittent and often shifts too quickly into problem-solving.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Dante as the expert on his life and invites his input on the plan. The frequent supply of options and scripts can feel slightly expert-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk through values-based questions and confidence scaling. Change talk is present but not consistently deepened or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging concerns without arguing and reframing the action as small and manageable. Could use more strategic reflections to resolve ambivalence.
