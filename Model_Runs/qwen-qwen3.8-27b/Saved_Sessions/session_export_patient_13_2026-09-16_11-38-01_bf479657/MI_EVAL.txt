# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses a warm, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes Anara's fear, but sometimes becomes task-focused rather than deeply relational. Overall, it is supportive though not consistently deep enough for a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician accurately reflects ambivalence and connects emotion to practical constraints, but often stays at the surface. It misses deeper meanings such as self-criticism, fear, shame, and shaking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on arranging a manageable medical check without drifting. It is slightly narrowed by repeated script options rather than one clear next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites input and adapts the plan around Anara's preferences, but remains the main organizer of options. Collaboration is useful though somewhat clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly offers choices and respects the need to wait, but ready-made scripts can reduce full ownership. It would benefit from more explicit statements that the decision is entirely hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions early on help Anara articulate concerns, but the session shifts to supplying solutions and closed prompts. It does not fully build on values, confidence, or her own change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anara contributes meaningfully, but the clinician frequently provides menus, scripts, and next steps rather than positioning her as the primary expert. Partnership is present but uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anara offers useful change talk, such as wanting to stop worrying and knowing being better than guessing, but the clinician does not consistently affirm or expand it. The conversation returns too quickly to practical scripting.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing and briefly acknowledges overwhelm, but often redirects to practical options rather than reflecting ambivalence. More double-sided reflections would better pivot her toward change.
