# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and conveys positive regard. Affirmation could be stronger by explicitly recognizing Selene’s strengths or values beyond the adherence issue.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence and concern, especially around pressure and fear of falling behind. Empathy could deepen by exploring underlying emotions such as frustration, shame, or exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on medication adherence and moves toward a realistic make-up plan. It could strengthen direction by consolidating the plan and moving more deliberately toward commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates with Selene and builds on her ideas throughout the session. More consistently inviting her to set the direction would make the collaboration stronger.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure and repeatedly invites Selene’s perspective. Explicitly affirming her right to make her own choices would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer draws out some of Selene’s own ideas, but often assumes her motivation rather than eliciting it. More evocative questions about values, self-efficacy, and personal meaning would improve this area.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and follows Selene’s lead. It would be stronger if Selene was more explicitly affirmed as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, but the psychiatrist does not consistently use MI-specific strategies to deepen or consolidate it. More use of DARN/CAT and importance/confidence questions would be helpful.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer avoids arguing and reframes a missed dose as a pause. However, key sustain talk about minimization is not fully addressed or reframed.
