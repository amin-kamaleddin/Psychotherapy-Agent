# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgmental tone throughout, with effective normalizing and shame-removing language. Occasionally feels slightly formulaic rather than deeply individualized, and lacks explicit statements of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and consistent reflective listening, capturing both cognitive and emotional experience precisely. Nuanced reflections track Marcus's emotional shifts and the quality of his worry with gentle amplification.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior (blood sugar monitoring) throughout the session. Logical progression from identifying the barrier to exploring a manageable step to collaborative planning, with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance with balanced power dynamic; Marcus's contributions substantially shape the session direction at every turn. Psychiatrist's role is facilitative rather than directive, consistently checking in before proceeding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Marcus's freedom to choose; never prescribes, frames all suggestions as options, and explicitly validates that Marcus doesn't have to be excited or do it all at once. Strong, sustained autonomy support throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external reasons for change; instead uses open questions that draw out Marcus's own motivations and amplifies his own change talk. Session ends with Marcus articulating his own plan, reasoning, and timeline—textbook evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently attributes solutions and agency to Marcus with language like 'that's you finding your own way in.' Could be slightly deeper with more explicit power-sharing and letting Marcus lead even more of the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly skilled at evoking and reinforcing change talk, amplifying Marcus's own language to increase motivation. Session ends with Marcus articulating a concrete, self-generated plan—a hallmark of effective change talk cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Marcus's nervousness well by validating it without reinforcing it as a reason not to proceed. Sustain talk was relatively mild in this session, limiting opportunities to demonstrate the skill with more robust doubt.
