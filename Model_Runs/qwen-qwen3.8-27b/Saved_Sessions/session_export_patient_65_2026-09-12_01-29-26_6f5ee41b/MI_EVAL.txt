# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes some of Julian’s stress. However, acceptance remains somewhat surface-level and does not strongly affirm his underlying fear, shame, or struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects frustration and logistical concerns adequately but inconsistently. The emotional meaning behind Julian’s statements, such as fear of failure and concern about being judged, is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior of completing the required hours and related logistics. The direction is clear, though it is more task-oriented than motivational.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites Julian’s input and builds the plan around his preferences. However, the counselor remains the primary generator of solutions, making the interaction feel unevenly counselor-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers some choices and avoids being overly forceful. Still, the frequent practical instructions and directive tone limit stronger support for Julian’s ownership and freedom of choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks mainly logistical questions and does little to draw out Julian’s own motivation, values, or reasons for change. Julian remains externally motivated, and his personal investment is not effectively evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor collaborates by asking Julian to contribute details about his availability and preferences. However, the counselor still takes on an expert-driven problem-solver role rather than fully positioning Julian as the expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Julian offers some change-related statements, but the counselor rarely reflects, amplifies, or deepens them. The session focuses on practical problem-solving rather than actively developing Julian’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor does not challenge or reinforce Julian’s sustain talk, which keeps the interaction safe. However, there is limited effort to redirect ambivalence toward personal motivation or responsibility.
