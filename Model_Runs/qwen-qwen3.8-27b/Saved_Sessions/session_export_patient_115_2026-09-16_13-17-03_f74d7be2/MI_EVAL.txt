# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and used affirmations and nonshaming language around possible slips. However, the warmth was sometimes formulaic and did not always deeply normalize ambivalence or family-related stress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflected some of Samir's perspective and validated his practical concerns. However, empathy was mostly behavioral rather than emotional, with limited exploration of underlying feelings and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on asthma control and smoking reduction, guiding Samir toward a concrete, actionable plan. Direction was consistently maintained despite some repeated questions and advice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Samir was invited to make choices and his responses shaped the plan, but the psychiatrist frequently provided one-way suggestions and advice. This reduced the sense of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly respected Samir's choices and used noncoercive, supportive language. However, the repeated specific tips and suggestions made the interaction more prescriptive than fully autonomy-focused.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions elicited useful change statements about importance and confidence. However, the psychiatrist did not consistently deepen or expand Samir's own motivations, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used some collaborative questions and acknowledged Samir's role in planning. However, the session often shifted into an expert-driven, advisory mode, making the partnership uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evoked some change talk by exploring importance, confidence, and doable steps. However, valuable change statements were not consistently reflected, amplified, or explored more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoided arguing and handled slip language without shame. However, sustain talk was mostly avoided rather than actively redirected or turned into change-oriented reflection.
