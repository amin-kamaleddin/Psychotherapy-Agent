# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and uses affirming language. However, acceptance remains somewhat procedural and does not fully balance respect with accountability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects frustration and unfairness accurately but does not consistently explore deeper feelings such as embarrassment, fear, or ambivalence. Empathic listening is adequate but not consistently deep.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the conduct meeting and does not wander aimlessly. However, the MI target shifts from responsible behavior and values toward minimizing the outcome.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The role-play format invites participation and creates some collaborative interaction. However, the psychiatrist supplies much of the content and strategy rather than co-creating a client-driven plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and respects some client decisions, showing initial autonomy support. However, the session becomes more directive as the psychiatrist provides exact wording and coaching.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few initial open questions, but the psychiatrist mainly supplies ideas, scripts, and procedural strategies. The client's own values, concerns, and reasons for change are not sufficiently evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shares some power by offering options and engaging Dylan in role-play. However, the session becomes expert-driven, reducing Dylan's sense of being an active partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early questions about outcomes and values could have opened change talk, but the session shifts quickly to tactical preparation. Classic evocation tools such as discrepancy, future-focus, and importance/confidence rulers are largely absent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not effectively soften Dylan's minimization of responsibility or the seriousness of the incident. At times, the psychiatrist even encourages scripted minimization, which undermines accountability.
