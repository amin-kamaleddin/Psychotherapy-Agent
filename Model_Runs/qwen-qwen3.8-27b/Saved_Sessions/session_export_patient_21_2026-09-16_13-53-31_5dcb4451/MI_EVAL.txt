**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, validating Camila's ambivalence and low-energy options. It could be slightly more explicitly normalizing to reach a 5.

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on increasing physical activity in a realistic way. The counselor moves smoothly from ambivalence to a small step, definition of success, and follow-up plan without drift.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Camila's input at nearly every step, from defining meaningful activity to shaping the check-in. Camila's ideas directly guide the plan and the follow-up approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and ownership are strongly emphasized throughout the session. The counselor avoids pressure, removes judgment from possible lapses, and reinforces that Camila's report is acceptable as-is.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Camila's intrinsic reasons for change, such as calm, better sleep, and more patience with family. Evocation could be stronger by more explicitly eliciting past successes and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently treats Camila as the expert on her own life, energy, and preferences. The plan is co-created rather than imposed, reflecting a strong collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor successfully elicits desire and reasoning for change, and helps Camila articulate a doable plan. It would be stronger if confidence, ability, and commitment language were more actively drawn out.
