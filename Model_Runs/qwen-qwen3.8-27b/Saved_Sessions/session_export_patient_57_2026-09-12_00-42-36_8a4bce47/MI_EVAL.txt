# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a non-judgmental tone and validates Jamal's perspective consistently. However, the language feels somewhat formulaic and repetitive, limiting the depth of warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Key themes are reflected accurately, but the responses remain brief and quickly pivot to problem-solving. The emotional and identity-level weight of Jamal's concerns is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on the target behavior of alcohol use at games. The pacing is strategic, but the focus is narrow and primarily behavioral.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adapts to Jamal's preferences and asks for input, which supports some collaboration. However, the psychiatrist generates most of the ideas and drives the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jamal's rejections and limits are respected, and the final plan is his choice. The psychiatrist does not actively foster ownership or explicitly emphasize Jamal's role as the decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Open questions are asked early, but the psychiatrist quickly shifts to providing specific solutions. Jamal's own motivations, confidence, and reasons for change are not deeply elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adopts an expert-driven stance, presenting a menu of options for Jamal to select from. There is limited shared power or genuine co-creation in the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session elicits commitment, but it is largely compliance-based rather than self-generated. Jamal's change talk is acknowledged but not amplified or explored further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal's sustain talk about fitting in and bonding is acknowledged but bypassed in favor of behavioral workarounds. True ambivalence work or double-sided reflections are not used.
