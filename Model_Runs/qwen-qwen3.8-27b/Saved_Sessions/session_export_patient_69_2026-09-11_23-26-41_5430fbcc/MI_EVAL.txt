# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and avoids shaming or pressure when Kieran is dismissive. However, acceptance remains mostly surface-level and task-oriented, with limited normalization of his ambivalence or disengagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Kieran's frustration with the runaround, signing, and follow-up burden. However, empathy is limited in depth and does not fully capture his resignation, need for control, or discomfort with being contacted.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on completing forms, reducing follow-up calls, and finishing the compliance task. From an MI perspective, the direction is more administrative and logistical than motivational, with little connection to broader goals or values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaboration because the psychiatrist asks for Kieran's preference and adapts to his request for practical help. However, the interaction is uneven and counselor-led, with the psychiatrist supplying much of the content rather than co-creating it with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports autonomy by offering choices, respecting Kieran's decisions, and not pressing him to stay or elaborate. The main limitation is that this autonomy support is embedded within a fairly directive practical task.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited evocation through some open-ended questions and an option to discuss what makes the process feel pointless. However, the session largely shifts into practical advice rather than eliciting Kieran's own reasons, values, confidence, or motivation for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some partnership by asking for Kieran's preference and respecting his choice to focus on practical steps. Nevertheless, the interaction becomes expert-driven, with less emphasis on Kieran's own expertise, goals, or insights as central to the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes minimal evocative efforts and Kieran offers a few small action-oriented statements such as 'I'll do it' and 'I'll send it in.' However, there is little systematic development of desire, ability, reasons, or need for change, and little amplification of Kieran's change-oriented language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with or become frustrated by Kieran's sustain talk, instead responding with acceptance and practical redirection. However, the response lacks active MI-style reframing, double-sided reflections, or gentle exploration of both sides of the issue.
