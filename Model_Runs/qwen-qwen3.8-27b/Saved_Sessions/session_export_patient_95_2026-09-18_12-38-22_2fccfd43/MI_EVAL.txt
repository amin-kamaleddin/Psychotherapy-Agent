# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental, and validating throughout the session, with no instances of labeling or disapproval. Acceptance occasionally comes across as slightly structured rather than fully spontaneous and deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional empathic accuracy, capturing the specific emotional texture of Maria's experience with complex, progressively deepening reflections. Shows genuine attunement when corrected, adjusting immediately without defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on suicidal ideation and emotional suffering without wandering into unrelated topics. The session lacks a concrete next-step agreement or explicit connection to broader DBT treatment goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative interaction with power-sharing, seeking client input and adapting when corrected. The psychiatrist still holds most of the structural initiative and could more explicitly invite Maria to redirect.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model-level autonomy support: explicitly honors the client's right to not commit, removes pressure to perform change, and never uses directive language. Particularly strong in a high-risk context where the temptation to be directive is very high.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Maria's own motivations and insights through gentle, pressure-free questions and reflections of her own words. Could be stronger with more systematic evocation, such as exploring values explicitly or helping articulate discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and explicit acknowledgment of the client's expertise in her own experience. The psychiatrist still clearly leads the direction of the conversation and could more frequently yield the floor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some attempts to evoke and reinforce change talk but remains relatively surface-level without amplifying or building momentum. Could more explicitly identify, reflect, and expand on Maria's reasons for staying alive and her desire to be seen.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging the reality of suffering without arguing or reinforcing absolute thinking. Could be stronger with more double-sided reflections that hold both the despair and faint hope simultaneously.
