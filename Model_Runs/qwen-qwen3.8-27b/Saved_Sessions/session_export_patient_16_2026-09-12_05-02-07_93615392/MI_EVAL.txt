# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveys warmth, respect, and nonjudgment without correcting or moralizing. Anika's minimization and limited commitment were met with acceptance and validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflected Anika's ambivalence, social pressure, and concern about losing basketball. The reflections were useful but sometimes stayed at the level of summarizing the conflict rather than deepening underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed consistently focused on vaping, basketball eligibility, and a practical short-term plan. The flow moved clearly from concern to importance/confidence, barriers, and follow-through.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was built around Anika's own ideas, including Sarah, the locker, and headphones. Some counselor-generated options reduced the experience of fully client-led co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly supports Anika's choice of focus, pace, and plan without pressuring full cessation. Anika was encouraged to create her own authentic language and decide on next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor drew out Anika's internal reasons, strengths, and solutions rather than giving external advice. Evocation could have gone deeper by exploring what playing well and feeling sharper mean to her.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anika was treated as the expert on her own life, with her ideas shaping the plan. Frequent offers of options and scripts slightly shifted the role toward counselor-generated problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicited desire, ability, and need statements related to protecting her basketball season. There was less expansion or reflection of specific change talk to deepen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accepted the benefits Anika identified and did not argue or amplify risks. Some sustain talk was acknowledged and redirected without using more advanced double-sided reflections.
