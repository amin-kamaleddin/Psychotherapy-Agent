# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids lecturing or pressuring Seamus. Acceptance is strong but could be warmer by explicitly normalizing his frustration with repeated smoking advice.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Seamus’s need for clarity, speed, and control over the dental visit. Empathy remains somewhat surface-level regarding the deeper ambivalence and emotional burden around smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session begins with a relevant oral-health prompt but then shifts mainly to dental logistics. The smoking-cessation target is not consistently guided or reintroduced in an autonomy-supportive way.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well on scheduling, scope, and practical dental decisions. Collaboration is less deep around Seamus’s core ambivalence about smoking.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Seamus’s boundaries and avoids controlling language, especially around dental care. Autonomy support would be stronger if explicitly tied to his smoking decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Questions mainly elicit practical information rather than intrinsic motivation for smoking change. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to explore or expand Seamus’s own change-relevant statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Seamus as the expert on his own history and invites his input throughout. Partnership is strongest on logistics and weaker on the central smoking issue.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited development of change talk related to smoking. Seamus’s weak change-relevant statements are not sufficiently reflected, amplified, or built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not challenge or reinforce Seamus’s resistance, which helps reduce defensiveness. However, there is limited active reframing to keep a change-consistent thread open.
