# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, nonjudgment, and affirming language while Rafael expresses self-criticism. It does not reach a 5 because the warmth remains somewhat professional and occasionally reframes his discouragement quickly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, especially in tracking ambivalence between wanting movement and fearing pressure or failure. Deeper emotions around self-judgment and all-or-none coping are acknowledged but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on returning to physical activity and narrows the topic into realistic, concrete steps. The psychiatrist guides toward change without drifting or becoming directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the session as a joint exploration and builds directly on Rafael’s suggestions. It is not a 5 because the conversation still follows a clear clinician-led sequence rather than fully client-directed structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently emphasized through language about choice, experiments, and the freedom to skip without guilt. Rafael is positioned as the decision-maker rather than being pushed toward a fixed plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Rafael’s own change language and useful self-generated ideas. It is limited because deeper values, past successes, and confidence are not fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance and lets Rafael’s ideas guide the session. The structure remains somewhat agenda-driven, limiting full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rafael generates multiple change-related statements, and the psychiatrist supports them with affirming reflections. The strongest change talk is not always deepened or amplified with scaling, commitment, or identity-linked language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by reframing goals as optional, realistic, and low-pressure. Some underlying fears, such as forgetfulness and all-or-none patterns, are not fully reflected before moving forward.
