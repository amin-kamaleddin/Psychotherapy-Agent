# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, nonjudgmental validation, and respect for the patient's time throughout. Slightly structured and professional rather than deeply personally invested, preventing a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes reasonable perspective-taking attempts (acknowledging her full plate, validating her health vigilance) but remains at a surface, informational level. Does not explore the emotional or psychological dimension underlying her need to know the what-ifs.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains focus on the medication question and respects the time constraint, with one attempt to redirect toward a deeper target. However, the session lacks a clear strategic arc and the repetitive closing sequence indicates loss of directional focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and a non-authoritarian tone, but the interaction is fundamentally transactional — she asked, he answered. No evidence of building on her existing knowledge or co-constructing understanding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong and consistent: all suggestions framed as open invitations, immediate accommodation of her time limit, and no pressure to stay or do more. The repeated closing messages slightly undercut the 'you can go anytime' message.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One good evocative attempt to explore whether her current medication is working well enough, but no follow-up when she closes the door. Could have reflected her pattern of preparedness rather than simply affirming and moving to closing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative tone and positions himself as a listener, but the power dynamic remains — he has the medical answer, she needed it, he gave it. Lacks genuine co-construction or acknowledgment of her expertise in her own body.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Very limited due to the clinical context, but the single opportunity to explore deeper motivation was only partially seized and not followed up. No scaling questions, no exploration of values, no confidence-building.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles her time constraint and preference for brevity well — never argues, guilts, or reinforces superficial engagement. Minor issue: the drawn-out closing sequence somewhat ironically contradicts the message of respecting her time.
