# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor showed respect and nonjudgment through validation and positive regard, but safety-focused directives and a parental tone reduced unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor made sincere reflections of frustration and defensiveness, but stayed surface-level and did not consistently explore deeper values, autonomy, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on drinking and safety without wandering off topic, but the agenda remained risk- and safety-oriented rather than collaboratively guiding toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There was some surface-level collaboration, but the session shifted into expert-driven questioning, risk framing, and safety recommendations rather than co-created direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor included autonomy-supportive statements and avoided forcing a plan, but repeated directives and warnings undercut the client’s sense of being in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor made a few evocative questions, but mostly supplied reasons for caution and safety advice rather than drawing out the client’s own values, motivations, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor began with collaborative invitations, but quickly became counselor-led and continued offering advice after the client said he felt parented and wanted to leave.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor made initial MI-consistent openings, but failed to amplify the client’s existing change talk such as “I’m careful” and “I never drive.”

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor avoided direct argument, but often added risk warnings and safety checklists, which reinforced sustain talk and defensiveness instead of gently redirecting to ambivalence.
