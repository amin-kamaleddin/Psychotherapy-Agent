# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validating Oliver's experience without judgment throughout the session. Affirmations are specific, tied to Oliver's actual words, and never label him negatively even when he expresses self-deprecating thoughts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and capture the underlying emotional dynamics rather than surface-level paraphrasing. Key moments like identifying the 'gap between knowing and feeling' and the 'trap' before Oliver is in it demonstrate deep attunement to his perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear therapeutic arc from clarifying tension through exploration, commitment, rehearsal, and practical planning. Each question builds logically on the previous one, moving Oliver from ambivalence toward concrete, self-generated action without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is structured as a genuine dialogue where Oliver's responses determine the direction of subsequent questions. The psychiatrist uses collaborative language ('I'm curious,' 'I'm wondering') and keeps reflections concise before returning the floor to the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces Oliver's ownership and right to choose, never using directive language or imposing solutions. Questions are framed as invitations for Oliver to access his own knowledge, and his pace is fully respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All change-relevant content — the three anchoring reasons, the specific phrase, timing, setting — is generated by Oliver himself through the psychiatrist's evocative questions. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change or gives advice, instead creating conditions for Oliver to articulate his own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise about Oliver's situation resides within Oliver, attributing insights to him rather than providing them. The interaction functions as a facilitation rather than a direction, with Oliver's contributions driving the conversation forward.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits and amplifies Oliver's own change language, reinforcing his movement toward decision and commitment. Oliver's change talk becomes increasingly specific and confident, evolving from vague wanting to concrete planning with timing, words, and follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled effectively through acknowledgment and gentle redirection without dismissal or argument. The session has limited sustain talk to manage since Oliver presented in the preparation/action stage, though the self-deprecating comment about overthinking could have been reflected back more explicitly as a pattern.
