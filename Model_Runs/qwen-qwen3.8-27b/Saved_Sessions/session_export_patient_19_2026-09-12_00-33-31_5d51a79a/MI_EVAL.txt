# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and uses several affirming statements when Anya discloses gambling and debt. The warmth is effective but remains brief and task-focused, with limited affirmation of her broader strengths or difficult context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate but surface-level reflections of Anya’s ambivalence, social pressure, and practical concerns. Deeper emotional themes such as shame, fear of failure, identity, and her final unresolved ambivalence are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing gambling and managing high-risk situations with specific, relevant behavioral targets. The focus is strong, though it remains more behavior-management oriented than MI-process oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interaction is interactive, and Anya’s “photographer duty” idea is a meaningful collaborative moment. However, the psychiatrist frequently leads with structured lists and options, making the process more expert-driven than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, asks for preferences, and respects refusals such as not using a prepaid card or giving money to a friend. The frequent provision of detailed options and rules still makes the approach feel somewhat directive rather than fully autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is the weakest area, as the psychiatrist supplies many practical solutions rather than drawing out Anya’s own motivations, values, and confidence. Missed opportunities include exploring travel goals, financial stability, future plans, and scaling questions for importance or readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and builds on Anya’s own “photographer duty” strategy. Overall, though, the session is driven more by the psychiatrist’s structured suggestions than by true collaborative direction-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anya produces some change talk, such as deleting saved cards, using a taxi card, and adopting “photographer duty.” The psychiatrist occasionally affirms these steps but does not consistently amplify or deepen the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Anya’s reasons for continuing to gamble without arguing or reinforcing them. The response is nonjudgmental but not highly strategic, as it lacks double-sided reflections and clear pivots linking sustain talk to change.
