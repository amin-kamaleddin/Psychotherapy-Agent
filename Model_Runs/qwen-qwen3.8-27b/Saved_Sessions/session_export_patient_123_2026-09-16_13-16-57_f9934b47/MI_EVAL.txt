# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a nonjudgmental, affirming tone and avoids pressure or explicit disapproval. Acceptance is somewhat superficial and does not fully acknowledge the alcohol screening or possible ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks about Sofia’s day, goals, and coping strategies, but reflections are mostly factual summaries. There is limited exploration of feelings, meaning, or the significance of the alcohol risk screen.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The session lacks clear focus on the referral concern, alcohol-related risk. The conversation drifts into general stress management and time-management strategies rather than the clinical target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few collaborative and permission-seeking phrases, but the psychiatrist largely sets the agenda and directs the session. Sofia’s contributions do not meaningfully shape the discussion or planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses optional language and respects Sofia’s choice to try strategies later. Autonomy support is inconsistent because the session still pushes specific strategies and a planned follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There is minimal effort to evoke Sofia’s own reasons, values, worries, or confidence related to alcohol. The counselor provides advice and skills rather than drawing out client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some open questions and asks permission, showing a minimal partnership stance. However, the agenda and flow remain counselor-driven rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The session does not meaningfully cultivate change talk about the clinical target, alcohol-related risk. The only mild change talk concerns trying the Pomodoro technique, not reducing or avoiding alcohol.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia minimizes the need for support, and the counselor does not gently explore or hold this ambivalence. Rather than reinforcing sustain talk, the counselor accepts the minimization and moves toward closing.
