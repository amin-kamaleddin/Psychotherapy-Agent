# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment or shaming. The patient's ambivalence and cautious commitment are treated with respect and acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates good reflective listening and accurately captures the patient's ambivalence. More depth in emotional tracking and validation of underlying feelings would bring this to a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on reducing problematic alcohol use. It progresses well toward a concrete, manageable target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves as an equal partner rather than an expert. The plan emerges collaboratively from the patient's own ideas and lived experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports the patient's sense of choice and ownership throughout the session. Commitments are framed as invitations and the patient's pace is explicitly honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits motivation and practical change ideas from the patient rather than supplying reasons to change. The session could be stronger by more explicitly drawing out values, self-efficacy, and readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly locates expertise in the patient and avoids imposing an expert-driven agenda. The patient's perspectives substantially guide the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively encourages the patient to generate change-oriented language and affirms these statements. Deeper focus on values, confidence, and personal meaning could further strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is normalized and gently reframed without confrontation or amplification. The responses reduce resistance, though more explicit double-sided reflections could further transform sustain talk into change language.
