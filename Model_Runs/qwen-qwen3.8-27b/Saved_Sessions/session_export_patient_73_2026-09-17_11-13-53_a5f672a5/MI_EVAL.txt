# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout the session. Every client statement, including sustain talk, is met with warmth and validation without judgment or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture complex emotional dimensions (e.g., fear and courage simultaneously). Moves consistently beyond surface-level to the existential core of the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear progressive arc from broad ambivalence to a specific behavioral target (the morning cigarette). Could be strengthened with a brief collaborative summary consolidating insights and agreeing on next steps before closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire session is structured as a genuine joint exploration. The client's own ideas (the bench, the morning cigarette, the permission) shape the direction of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's agency and ownership of change. Never prescribes or implies hesitation is a problem; skillfully shifts the locus of control from external permission to internal self-permission.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within the client rather than provided externally. The psychiatrist's role is to create conditions where the client's own reasons for change surface and grow.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly states 'this is your expertise, not mine' and uses language like 'I'm thinking with you here.' The psychiatrist never takes over, lectures, or positions themselves as the authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk throughout, from the opening question to the final behavioral plan. By session end, the client has moved from ambivalence to articulating a specific first step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently acknowledged, validated, and gently reframed without being reinforced or minimized. The psychiatrist never argues against the client's reasons for maintaining the status quo.
