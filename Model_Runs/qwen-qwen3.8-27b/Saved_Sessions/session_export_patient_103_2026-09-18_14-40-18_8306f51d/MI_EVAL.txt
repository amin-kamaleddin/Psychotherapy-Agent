# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and non-judgmental, validating Nabil's feelings without pathologizing his anger. Warmth remains somewhat professional and measured, with occasional formulaic patterns preventing a full score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening capturing both cognitive and emotional dimensions, including the sadness beneath Nabil's defensive exterior. Could go deeper in a few places, particularly when Nabil names feeling like he doesn't count or pushes back on being framed as a victim.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from trigger to pattern to core feeling to desired outcome. The session ends somewhat abruptly without a clear sense of next steps or a summarizing statement to consolidate progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with inclusive language and active use of Nabil's own contributions. Still holds a subtle asymmetry in who is steering, with questions structured by the psychiatrist rather than explicitly negotiated with Nabil.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent throughout — never imposes solutions, respects Nabil's boundaries, and explicitly affirms his ownership of his life. A model of autonomy support with no evidence of pressure or control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits Nabil's own motivations and thinking rather than imposing external reasons, producing concrete visions and change language. Could be more persistent in the face of Nabil's final sustain talk to draw out his confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance with open questions and respect for Nabil's boundaries, positioning him as the driver of the session. Could strengthen by more explicitly negotiating the agenda and inviting Nabil to set priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits concrete change talk through well-timed questions that draw out Nabil's own vision of alternatives. Does not fully capitalize on the generated change talk to build momentum or explore confidence in the closing exchanges.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by reflecting self-talk without agreement and using curiosity-based questions to open space. Could be more active in the final moments with a double-sided reflection that acknowledges validity while gently pivoting.
