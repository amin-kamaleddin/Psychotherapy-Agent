### Partnership

**Comments:** Elicits change-relevant language from Tyler (e.g., 'I just want it to be normal again') but does not consistently amplify, affirm, or deepen it. No scaling questions or strategic follow-ups to build on change-relevant statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing, which is appropriate. However, does not use double-sided reflections or strategic redirection to gently pivot the conversation.
