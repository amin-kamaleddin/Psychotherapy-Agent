# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained unconditional positive regard without judgment, shaming, or moral pressure. This nonjudgmental stance kept the guarded adolescent engaged and willing to disclose more.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Malik’s ambivalence between peer belonging and reducing family conflict. The psychiatrist tracked the client’s specific language, identity concerns, and emerging harm-reduction plan.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on cannabis use, peer influence, and family conflict with a logical progression toward a concrete plan. It occasionally narrowed toward avoiding maternal discovery rather than the broader target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited the client’s perspective and built the conversation around his own language and metaphors. The plan was co-constructed rather than imposed, reflecting strong collaborative practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently avoided coercion, directive advice, or pressure to quit. Change was framed as Malik’s own choice, preserving his agency and reducing defensiveness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections effectively elicited the client’s own motivations and practical next steps. The evocation could have been stronger if it connected more deeply to personal values, confidence, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a strong non-expert, collaborative stance and allowed Malik to shape the direction of the session. It could have felt more fully co-directed with explicit client-led agenda setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited concrete change talk about reducing risk, avoiding the supplier role, and managing peer requests. The change talk could have been broadened to include stronger internal motivation, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well through balanced reflections and non-confrontational redirection rather than debate. The session could have further transformed peer-driven reasons for continued use into stronger client-centered change talk.
