### Empathy

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as joint exploration rather than clinical assessment, with the psychiatrist using first-person observations and building on Elias's language verbatim. Client's ideas substantially shape direction at every turn.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Active, explicit affirmation of Elias's ownership and choice at multiple points throughout the session. Uses qualifiers like 'even a little' and explicitly removes pressure rather than merely avoiding directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within Elias through strategic questions; the psychiatrist never provides external reasons, advice, or personal stories. Textbook evocation with insight explicitly returned to the client ('You answered it yourself').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Elias, with genuine power-sharing and no overriding of client resistance. The session is shaped substantially by Elias's contributions, pace, and concerns.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Skillful evocation of change talk through strategic questions that elicit Elias's own language in favor of change, with effective amplification. Falls slightly short by not explicitly targeting ability and confidence language or systematically exploring all DARN-CAT categories.
