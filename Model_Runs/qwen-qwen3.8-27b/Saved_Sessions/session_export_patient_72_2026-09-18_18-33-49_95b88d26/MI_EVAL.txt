# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental with multiple affirmations of Leila's self-awareness and strengths. Slightly performative at times and could offer deeper emotional warmth in moments of vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures internal states and emotional nuance beyond simple paraphrasing. Occasionally prioritizes summarizing and reframing over fully inhabiting Leila's emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with logical progression from motivation exploration to practical planning. Transitions between topics could be slightly more deliberate with brief summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong joint exploration framing with tentative, invitation-based language and active seeking of Leila's input. Occasionally positions the psychiatrist as slightly more of a guide than an equal partner when offering reframes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites Leila's own criteria and solutions without prescribing, using tentative invitation-based language throughout. Explicitly supports her right to set her own standards and never imposes a plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out Leila's own motivations and skillfully reflects and amplifies her change talk. Could push slightly deeper into core values, identity, and explicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance positioning Leila as the expert, following her lead throughout. Occasionally steps into a slight expert-adjacent role when offering reframes as statements rather than questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective strategic questioning elicits Desire, Ability, Reasons, and Need language, with skillful catching and reinforcing of change talk. Could be slightly more systematic in structure, such as explicitly exploring readiness or specific outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges barriers without reinforcing, reframing them as solvable problems (e.g., the weather reframe is excellent). Sustain talk was limited in this session, somewhat constraining the opportunity to demonstrate the skill.
