# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance throughout. Warmth is somewhat functional and task-oriented rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflects ambivalence and key client perspectives, but often remains cognitive and repetitive. Misses deeper emotional undercurrents related to defensiveness and identity as a healthy runner.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use, training, and a concrete experiment. Slightly directive, and the follow-up plan is not fully clarified.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client input is included in the final plan, but the psychiatrist supplies much of the structure, rationale, and options. Daniel mostly responds to counselor proposals rather than leading the exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and offers choices without being coercive, but provides many specific options and planning details. This limits emphasis on Daniel’s own freedom to design his approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Moves quickly into advice and evidence rather than eliciting Daniel’s own motivations. Misses opportunities to explore the personal meaning of his marathon goal and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses permission-seeking and inclusive language, but the session remains largely counselor-driven. The psychiatrist assumes an expert role in setting the agenda and plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniel offers some change talk, and the counselor helps create space for it. The counselor does not consistently amplify, deepen, or build on Daniel’s own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally avoids arguing and acknowledges Daniel’s perspective without escalating. Does not consistently use skillful MI techniques to weaken sustain talk or resolve ambivalence.
