# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, never judges Timur's past, and gently reframes self-critical labels with warmth and respect throughout the entire session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic reflections accurately capture Timur's ambivalence, pain, and fear. However, some responses land on affirmation rather than penetrating the deepest emotional layer, such as the fear of not trusting one's own new identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on reducing recidivism with a logical narrative arc from sustain talk to change talk to identity work. However, it could be more purposeful in guiding toward specific, actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the interaction as occurring between equals, uses 'we/us' language, and consistently lets Timur's ideas shape the session's direction rather than imposing an expert agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Timur's perception of choice is consistently supported through open invitations, pacing language, and attribution of motivation to him. The psychiatrist never pressures decisions or implies Timur should choose one path over another.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Masterful evocative questions like 'Who's the guy who makes the tea?' effectively draw out Timur's own reasons and capacity for change. The evocation could be more systematically deepened with scaling questions and explicit plan talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that wisdom about change resides within Timur, positioning himself as a mirror and guide rather than an expert with answers. Questions invite Timur's own insight rather than providing directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questions effectively elicit change talk around respect, the porch vision, and emerging pro-social identity. However, key moments of change talk (e.g., 'I do want to trust him a little') could have been further amplified with follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully through double-sided reflections and gentle redirection rather than argument or reinforcement. Some moments of helplessness are acknowledged but not fully redirected toward exploring possible steps.
