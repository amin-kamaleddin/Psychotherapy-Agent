# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming tone throughout. Some warmth is limited by procedural, directive moments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is adequate for the client’s concerns and practical barriers. However, empathy remains surface-level and does not deeply explore emotions such as family history, shame, or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays generally focused on reproductive risk and pill adherence. It drifts into detailed logistics and misses broader alcohol-reduction goals and a stronger commitment close.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by co-creating texts and adjusting wording with the client. However, the clinician still drives much of the agenda and moves quickly to concrete scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is regularly supported in making choices and has her preferences respected. Some directive language and exact scripts reduce the invitational quality.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful practical plans and confidence-building steps. Deeper values, identity, and intrinsic motivation are not consistently evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client substantially shapes the plan, including choosing Sarah and adjusting the message. The partnership is slightly uneven when the clinician introduces options before the client has fully explored preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling and planning questions effectively elicit change-oriented language and increased confidence. The work could be stronger by more deeply amplifying the client’s values and desired identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges barriers without arguing or reinforcing resistance. However, sustain talk is not consistently softened using double-sided reflections or strategic pivots toward change.
