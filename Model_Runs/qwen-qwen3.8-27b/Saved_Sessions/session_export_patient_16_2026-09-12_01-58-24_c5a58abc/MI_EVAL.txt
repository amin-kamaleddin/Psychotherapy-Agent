# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance while the client minimizes harm. Acceptance is generally strong but remains somewhat task-focused and occasionally evaluative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurately reflects the client’s ambivalence about friends, relaxation, and basketball performance. Empathy is competent but stays mostly cognitive and does not deeply explore affective dimensions such as social pressure or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on reducing vaping during the basketball season and related risks. Direction is solid, though occasional multi-part questions reduce pacing and strategic precision.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites client input and builds the plan around her ideas, such as using music, Sarah, and a mirror note. Collaboration is meaningful but still somewhat counselor-directed due to long, multi-part prompts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports the client’s role as decision-maker and responds well to her request not to be lectured. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming her freedom to choose or change course.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation through confidence ratings and questions tied to basketball values. Evocation is strong but is sometimes paired with specific options and advances before fully amplifying the client’s change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a non-authoritarian, collaborative stance and incorporates the client’s suggestions into the session. Partnership is strong but occasionally over-structured by rapid-fire questions and menu-style options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicits change talk about playing well, using a cue line, and tracking performance. The psychiatrist moves quickly into behavioral planning without consistently deepening or expanding intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not challenge the client’s minimization and honors her autonomy, which helps reduce defensiveness. However, sustain talk is not consistently softened with double-sided reflections or strategic redirection toward change.
