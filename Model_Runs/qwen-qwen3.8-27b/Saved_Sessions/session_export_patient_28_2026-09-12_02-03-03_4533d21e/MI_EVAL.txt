# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and encouraging tone while affirming Dante's efforts. Acceptance is sometimes slightly task-focused and formulaic, which limits deeper personal validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Initial reflections capture Dante's frustration and care, and specific concerns are acknowledged. Empathic accuracy remains adequate but not consistently deep, often focusing more on content or planning than underlying emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior of re-establishing a simple exercise routine. The conversation moves logically from referral concern to a concrete, coherent plan without meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects, asks for preferences, and builds the plan around Dante's ideas using cooperative language. Collaboration is somewhat uneven because the therapist frequently introduces concrete steps and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Dante is repeatedly invited to make choices, modify the plan, and set the follow-up pace, with little coercion. Some detailed therapist-generated steps and exact wording make the session feel slightly more directive than fully client-driven.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The importance and confidence rulers and doable-step questions elicit meaningful client motivation and change talk. Evocation is interrupted by practical problem-solving and does not consistently explore deeper personal values or identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows Dante's contributions to shape the session, including goals, cues, and plan details. The interaction feels somewhat expert-guided because the therapist supplies much of the practical content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of rulers, small-step questions, and affirmations helps Dante express clear change talk and increasing confidence. It could be stronger by more consistently exploring why exercise matters to him and what past success shows about his ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles barriers without arguing and converts them into manageable coping strategies and fallback options. A higher score would require more explicit double-sided reflections or active pivoting against the barriers.
