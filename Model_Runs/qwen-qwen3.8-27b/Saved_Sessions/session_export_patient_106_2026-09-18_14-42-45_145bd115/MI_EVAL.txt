# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, deep unconditional positive regard with zero judgment or labeling throughout. Acceptance is specific and individualized, woven into clinical content rather than formulaic, with moments like validating an incomplete sentence as profound attunement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that accurately captures emotional texture and meaning-level experience, not just surface content. Natalia never corrects or resists any reflection, indicating high accuracy and attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation tied to parenting and identity with a logical therapeutic arc from validation to tentative commitment. Minor gap in lacking a strategic summary to consolidate themes and not addressing Natalia's final question about self-monitoring.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model-level collaboration where insights are framed as joint discoveries rather than the psychiatrist's observations. The entire session architecture is built on equal partnership with explicit power sharing and deferral to the client's pace.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never prescribes, advises, or directs; every suggestion is framed as something Natalia discovered or chose. Explicitly supports her right to not have answers and attributes all insights back to her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional evocation through strategic reflections that create conditions for client-driven insight without any direct advice, information-giving, or argumentation. Every change talk statement originates from Natalia, with the psychiatrist's role purely to amplify what she is already feeling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Natalia as the authority on her own life, framing insights as her discoveries rather than the psychiatrist's observations. The power dynamic is clearly equal — the psychiatrist is a witness and mirror, not a guide or authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful cultivation through strategic reflection rather than direct questioning, producing a clear arc from sustain talk to tentative commitment. All change talk emerges organically and is met with amplification and validation without any pushing or lecturing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully validates the function and identity weight of sustain talk rather than challenging or minimizing it. The sustain talk is given full weight, which paradoxically reduces its power and allows the client to arrive at her own conclusion.
