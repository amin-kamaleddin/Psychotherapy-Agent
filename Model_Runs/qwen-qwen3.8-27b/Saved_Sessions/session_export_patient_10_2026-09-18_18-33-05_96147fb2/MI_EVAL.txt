# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Amina's experience and affirming her capability without minimizing her struggles. While warm, the acceptance occasionally feels more like strategic validation than deeply individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both factual content and emotional texture throughout. Complex reflections consistently reach beneath surface content to the meaning-making layer, as demonstrated by the final reflection on Amina's internal process.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with logical progression from barrier identification to concrete action planning. Could be stronger by more explicitly connecting the immediate childcare solution to the broader employment goal at key transition points.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is fully co-constructed with Amina as an equal partner; the psychiatrist offers reflections and questions while direction and solutions emerge from Amina. Language consistently maintains an equal partnership rather than an expert stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Amina's ownership and freedom to choose, never pressuring, advising, or directing. All questions are framed as invitations for Amina to explore her own path, with explicit affirmations of her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently elicited from within Amina through evocative questions and strategic amplification of her own words. The closing question about how it will feel to hang up is a particularly powerful evocative technique.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Amina, positioning her as the expert on her own life. The session is shaped entirely by Amina's contributions, with the psychiatrist serving as a guide rather than an authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evokes and reinforces change talk, highlighting Amina's own words to amplify motivation and using strategic summaries to consolidate her narrative of capability. Could have gone further in explicitly eliciting more categories of change talk and using scaling or evocative questions to deepen exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by exploring rather than arguing, gently redirecting without invalidating, and never reinforcing or getting stuck in sustain talk. Could have more explicitly used a double-sided reflection to honor both sides of the ambivalence before turning toward change.
