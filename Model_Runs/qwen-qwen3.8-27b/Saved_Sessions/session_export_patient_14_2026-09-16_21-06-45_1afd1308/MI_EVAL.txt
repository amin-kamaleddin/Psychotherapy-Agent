### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validates Andrei's constraints, competence, and need for efficiency. Slightly less than full because emotional frustration and personal respect could be acknowledged more directly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captures the ambivalence and work-health conflict. The reflections are mostly cognitive and problem-solving-oriented, and could explore frustration, fatigue, or loss of control more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps focus on blood pressure and a specific, time-efficient 3 p.m. swap. It could be stronger by consolidating the process into a clear commitment and connecting more explicitly to broader health goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Builds the plan from Andrei's expertise, giving shared ownership to his specific ideas. It feels somewhat guided by frequent clinician framing and summarizing rather than more explicit equal agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids directives and pressure, repeatedly framing choices as voluntary, low-load, and non-prescriptive. It could be stronger with more explicit statements that Andrei is entirely in charge of whether and when to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits self-motivational language and useful internal cues, especially the 3:05 quiet note. The focus is somewhat repetitive around efficiency, and could be deepened with values, confidence, or readiness questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Andrei's knowledge of his work and lets his contributions shape the session. It could be stronger by explicitly checking the agenda and inviting Andrei to critique or revise the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open questions to generate solutions and reinforces Andrei's own change talk. It does not fully explore the major change-talk domains of desire, ability, need, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates time and workload concerns without arguing, turning barriers into design constraints. It could be stronger by explicitly naming the tension and inviting Andrei to choose which side matters in a specific moment.
