# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor establishes a warm, non-judgmental tone with consistent affirmations and validation of Sofia's discomfort. However, the acceptance remains somewhat surface-level and script-like, never reaching deep, individualized unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Consistent reflective listening captures content and some emotional undertones, with a good self-correction when a reflection felt too intense. However, reflections often miss deeper emotional layers (e.g., fatigue, low expectations behind 'just getting through'), and a therapist-centered comment ('Something just shifted for me') detracts from empathic accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session lacks clear direction toward the target behavior (alcohol use) and wanders through general life topics without connecting to the reason for the referral. A well-directed MI session would have at minimum acknowledged the screening and begun exploring Sofia's perspective on it.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses inclusive language, invites Sofia's perspective, and shows transparency through self-correction, fostering a sense of equality. However, collaboration is largely one-directional — the counselor drives the agenda, asks the questions, and serves as the primary interpreter of Sofia's experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Moderate autonomy support is shown through 'you don't owe' language and respect for Sofia's pace in sharing. However, the counselor never explicitly affirms Sofia's right to make her own choices about her behavior or uses 'you're the expert on your own life' language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There is virtually no evocation of motivation for change. Questions are directed at self-concept rather than connecting Sofia's values to her behavior or exploring whether her current life aligns with what she wants.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a collaborative stance with shared uncertainty and inviting questions, positioning themselves as curious rather than authoritative. However, Sofia largely responds to the counselor's questions rather than directing where the conversation goes, and there is no co-creation of agenda or plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No change talk is cultivated at any point in the session. The conversation remains entirely in rapport-building without eliciting Sofia's reasons for change, values related to health, or confidence in making changes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No sustain talk is present because the conversation never approaches the target behavior. The complete absence of engagement with this topic reflects a failure to advance the MI process to the stage where sustain talk management would be relevant.
