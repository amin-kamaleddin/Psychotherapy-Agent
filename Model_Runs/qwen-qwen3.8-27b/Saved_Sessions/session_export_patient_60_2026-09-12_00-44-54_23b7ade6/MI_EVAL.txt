### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist remained respectful and validating throughout. However, acceptance was somewhat surface-level and did not fully acknowledge Jason's deeper need for autonomy and reputation protection.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist made initial efforts to understand Jason's perspective. However, the focus shifted quickly to practical and legalistic steps rather than accurately reflecting Jason's underlying fears and concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained clear topical focus on the incident and next steps. However, the direction was procedural and legalistic rather than strategically focused on MI goals like discrepancy and change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction was dominated by the psychiatrist's practical and legalistic agenda. Jason's perspective was acknowledged but did not meaningfully shape the direction of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered choices and acknowledged Jason's decision-making. However, repeated directive instructions and checklists undermined genuine autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used limited open questions but did not effectively elicit Jason's own motivations. The session remained focused on external solutions rather than drawing out Jason's internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for input but maintained an expert-driven dynamic. The session was largely driven by the psychiatrist's practical agenda rather than a true collaborative partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jason offered several statements of motivation, but the psychiatrist did not effectively reflect, amplify, or build on them. There was little effort to develop Jason's own reasons or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
