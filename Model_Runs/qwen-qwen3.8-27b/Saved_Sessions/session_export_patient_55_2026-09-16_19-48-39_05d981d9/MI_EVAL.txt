# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids judgment and repeatedly normalizes Isabelle's reluctance and validates her perspective. The stance is strong and respectful but somewhat procedural rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor shows good reflective listening by capturing both content and underlying meaning, such as school and band filling her space. However, the empathy is mainly cognitive and does not consistently explore fear, shame, or feeling controlled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session begins with alcohol risk but quickly drifts to IB workload, band performance, sleep, and caffeine. The counselor does not consistently maintain focus on alcohol use, clarify the referral concern, or redirect back to it.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and uses inclusive language when discussing possible changes. However, the counselor's agenda remains prominent despite Isabelle's repeated resistance to framing the issue as a problem.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor regularly affirms Isabelle's freedom to choose and avoids direct advice-giving or pressure. However, continued asking for a small step may subtly pressure her to accept some form of intervention.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor attempts to elicit internal motivation but does so in a limited and somewhat repetitive way. There are missed opportunities to explore values, future goals, concerns, and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently positions Isabelle as the expert on her own system and avoids a prescriptive role. The main limitation is repeated steering toward a small step even after she says she does not need help.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is acknowledged, but it is not consistently amplified, deepened, or developed. There is little use of importance/confidence scaling or future-oriented exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor respectfully acknowledges Isabelle's sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing it. However, the handling is not highly skillful because ambivalence is not explored and the 'not a crisis' frame is accepted too readily.
