# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, nonjudgmental language that reduces defensiveness. Acceptance is consistent but somewhat formulaic and could more deeply affirm Ethan's values or worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections show strong understanding of Ethan's ambivalence and social concerns. Empathy could go deeper into the emotional core, such as fear of judgment or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The conversation stays connected to alcohol use but becomes repetitive. It lacks clear summarizing, goal-narrowing, or movement toward a specific next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Ethan's ideas and invites his input throughout. Collaboration is mostly question-based and could become more co-constructive with shared planning or joint summaries.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids directives and invites Ethan to choose his own small steps. Autonomy support is strong, though repeated focus on "one small thing" could imply change is expected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ethan is prompted to generate his own alternatives, such as soda, energy drinks, and activities. Evocation remains superficial and does not fully build Ethan's own motivation or shift away from sustain talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently positions Ethan as the expert on his own life. Partnership language is supportive but can feel repetitive and scripted.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk, such as trying a different drink or hanging out with friends who do not drink. These moments are not consistently affirmed, amplified, or turned into stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met without argument and often reflected respectfully. The session would benefit from more strategic double-sided reflections that reduce the weight of drinking as necessary for belonging.
