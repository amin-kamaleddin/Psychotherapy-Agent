### Collaboration

**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by refining Irina’s ideas and helping build a practical plan. The session is client-driven, though deeper emotional engagement and systematic ambivalence work would strengthen it further.

**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Irina’s autonomy by honoring her self-chosen goal and avoiding coercion. She is treated as the expert on her life, with decisions and plan-building remaining in her hands.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly conveys partnership by repeatedly inviting Irina to choose, define goals, and shape the plan. The client’s contributions substantially drive the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks open questions that lead Irina to generate meaningful change talk and practical next steps. Change talk is well elicited but could be deepened with scaling, commitment language, or explicit exploration of desire, ability, need, and commitment.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk without arguing or pressuring, and honors Irina’s current frame while redirecting toward healthier behavior. It would be stronger with more explicit double-sided reflections to fully hold and resolve ambivalence.
