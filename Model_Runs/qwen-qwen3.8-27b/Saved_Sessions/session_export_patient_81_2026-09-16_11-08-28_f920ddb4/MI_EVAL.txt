# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Lukas’s practical concerns without moralizing. Acceptance is somewhat formulaic and does not deeply address identity-related fears.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is adequate early on, especially around ambivalence between social life and practical costs. Empathy becomes less emotionally rich later, with more focus on plans than deeper feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a concrete target behavior: reducing Friday-night use and supporting sleep before the Tuesday audition. The direction is clear but somewhat narrow and task-oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts to Lukas’s preferences, but frequently supplies practical suggestions that feel expert-led. Collaboration is present but uneven, especially when advice does not fully fit Lukas’s social context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and confidence ratings, supporting Lukas’s role in decision-making. Autonomy is undermined by frequent directive suggestions and scripted problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some useful change talk related to protecting the audition, income, and sleep. Evocation is mostly practical and does not deeply explore values, identity, or broader motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist involves Lukas in choosing options and builds a plan with his input. The interaction often feels consultant-like because the psychiatrist structures much of the agenda and supplies solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful change statements around avoiding being “wrecked,” protecting paychecks, and being sharp for the audition. The evocation stays focused on concrete steps rather than deeper reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and frames the plan as a compromise. Some suggestions, such as holding a soft drink, may increase performance pressure and do not fully soften identity-based ambivalence.
