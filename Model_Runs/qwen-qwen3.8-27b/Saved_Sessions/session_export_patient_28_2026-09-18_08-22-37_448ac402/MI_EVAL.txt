# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard without any judgment or labeling. Validates Dante's small steps as strengths rather than limitations, maintaining warmth throughout the entire session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate complex reflections that capture the emotional and experiential core of Dante's experience (e.g., 'the ground shifted under you,' 'not resting, and not doing anything'). Goes well beyond surface-level paraphrasing to reflect nuanced internal states.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical arc from exploration through ambivalence to a concrete action step without losing focus on the target behavior. Lacks an explicit summary or agenda-setting moment that would further strengthen strategic structure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a genuine two-person exploration where Dante's language, metaphors, and ideas are actively incorporated into reflections. The final plan is entirely co-constructed through dialogue rather than prescribed by the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses permissive, exploratory language without any prescriptive statements or pressure. Honors Dante's self-awareness about not wanting to make promises, and the action step remains entirely his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external reasons for change — all motivation is drawn from Dante's own words, metaphors, and insights. Questions are specifically designed to elicit his internal reasons, vision of success, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Dante as the expert on his own experience and never assumes the authority role. Every insight and solution emerges from Dante himself, with the psychiatrist serving as a curious facilitator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically designed questions produce genuine client-generated change talk across desire, ability, need, and commitment levels. Actively amplifies change talk through affirmations like 'that's not a weakness, that's strategy' without ever offering external reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by reframing smallness as strategic advantage and building on what is working rather than challenging Dante's concerns. Could benefit from more advanced techniques like double-sided reflections in moments where simple affirmation is used.
