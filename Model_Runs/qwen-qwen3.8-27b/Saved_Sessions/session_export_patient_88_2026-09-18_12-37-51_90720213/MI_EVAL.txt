# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent, warm, and unconditional positive regard throughout. Validates Marcus's emotional experience without judgment and never labels or expresses disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that captures both surface and deeper emotional layers simultaneously. Accurately tracks Marcus's emotional arc from defensiveness to vulnerability with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on alcohol use while skillfully connecting it to underlying anxiety. Each step builds logically without feeling forced or agenda-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a collaboration between equals, positioning Marcus as the authority. Session flows based on Marcus's contributions rather than a pre-determined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Marcus's autonomy, removing pressure and returning decision-making to him. Questions are framed as curiosities rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation where every insight that emerges comes from Marcus himself rather than being imposed. The therapist never provides a single reason for change, drawing out Marcus's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Marcus, using curious rather than authoritative language. Co-constructed language reinforces that Marcus is generating the insights.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally skilled at evoking and amplifying change talk by strategically highlighting Marcus's own insights. Invites Marcus to own his change talk rather than having it imposed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging and validating without reinforcing. Could be more strategic in final moments, particularly when Marcus says 'maybe it is just me' — a double-sided reflection or pivot would have been more effective.
