# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgment. The main limitation is that the warmth remains somewhat procedural and does not fully address the client’s emotional fear of failure or guilt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening and accurately tracks ambivalence, barriers, and small wins. However, the reflections remain more cognitive than emotional, leaving the client’s shame and guilt under-explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity. It efficiently moves from ambivalence to a specific, concrete action plan without meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** This is a major strength of the session, with the client’s ideas directly shaping the final plan. The interaction feels like joint problem-solving rather than expert-led advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy through choices, low pressure, and respect for the client’s decisions. The frequent provision of options, scripts, and prompts slightly increases therapist-led direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits motivation, past successes, and confidence, raising the client’s confidence from 3 to 5. However, therapist-provided solutions limit the depth of client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally operates as a partner and invites the client to choose options. The interaction remains somewhat therapist-led due to the number of structured options and specific scripts provided.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully evokes meaningful change talk about values, confidence, and small wins. The main limitation is that offering many concrete options reduces space for the client to elaborate their own reasons and abilities.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through normalization and reframing barriers into manageable plans. The main limitation is that the final guilt/fear barrier is not fully addressed, and the focus sometimes stays on problem-solving rather than pivoting to change talk.
