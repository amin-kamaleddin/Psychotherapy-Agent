# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming of Luka’s effort and practical problem-solving. Acceptance could become stronger with more explicit, person-centered warmth and unconditional positive regard beyond the immediate plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and often complex, capturing Luka’s ambivalence and the difficulty of high-intensity moments. The empathy is somewhat cognitive and plan-focused, with less deep exploration of underlying emotions such as fear, shame, guilt, or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing risky drinking and managing anger, moving from ambivalence to a concrete behavior-specific plan. At times, the focus narrows to text logistics and could be more strategically tied back to the broader goals with brief summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Luka generates most of the plan, and the psychiatrist builds on his ideas rather than imposing interventions. The collaboration is strong but remains more therapist-guided than fully client-directed, with less visible co-ownership of the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently asks for Luka’s preferences, respects his choices, and avoids pressure. Autonomy support could be improved by more explicitly stating that the plan and decisions are entirely his.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Luka’s own ideas for practical strategies and supports him in refining his plan. Evocation is strong but less focused on deeper values, importance, confidence, or commitment-related change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and lets Luka’s ideas drive the session. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Luka’s authority and the equal, shared nature of the work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Luka generates substantial practical change talk, such as the kitchen pause, counting, and the text signal. The psychiatrist affirms this well but could more explicitly amplify DARN-CAT change talk around importance, confidence, values, and future self.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Doubts and sustain talk are handled without arguing or dismissal, keeping the focus on small, manageable steps. The psychiatrist could use more double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to actively pivot sustain talk into change talk.
