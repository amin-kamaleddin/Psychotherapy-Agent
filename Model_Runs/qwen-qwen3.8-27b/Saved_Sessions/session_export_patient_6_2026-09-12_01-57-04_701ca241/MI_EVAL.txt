# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed respect, warmth, and validation without shaming. Some comments felt procedural and were quickly followed by advice, limiting the depth of individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There were several accurate reflections of Alexei's ambivalence and goals. The empathy was more content-focused than emotionally nuanced and missed deeper fears of social rejection or identity loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use in relation to soccer and scholarships. The psychiatrist guided from general concern to specific refusal strategies without major drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Alexei's preferences meaningfully shaped the plan, especially through choosing refusal strategies and alternatives. The process remained somewhat counselor-led because the psychiatrist frequently supplied structured lists and suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy was strongly supported through open-ended questions, invitations, and non-coercive language. The advice-giving style slightly undermined the message that the decision and process belonged entirely to Alexei.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited some initial change talk early in the session. Overall, the counselor provided more reasons, steps, and scripts than he elicited and expanded Alexei's internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for preferences and adapted to feedback, supporting a collaborative stance. However, he often positioned himself as the provider of techniques rather than emphasizing Alexei's own expertise as the primary source of wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Alexei generated some change talk, and the psychiatrist elicited it early. The psychiatrist often shifted into strategies rather than amplifying or expanding Alexei's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalized sustain talk and avoided arguing or reinforcing it. He did not consistently use active MI softening skills such as double-sided reflections or exploring the values behind Alexei's need to belong.
