# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental, and affirming throughout the entire session. Never expresses disapproval or labels the client, and validates his experience even when he minimizes or rationalizes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and progressively deepening reflections, moving from surface behavior to social dynamics to identity-level fear. Each reflection is precise, individualized, and captures the specific emotional nuance rather than offering generic statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear strategic arc from safety through tension exploration to identity-level fear. However, the session ends somewhat open-ended without introducing a small, concrete next step as it winds down.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with inclusive language and creative moves like the 'friend' question that externalize the problem. Could be slightly more explicitly bilateral in a few moments (e.g., 'let's figure this out together').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent and explicit autonomy support throughout — granting permission to not proceed, framing questions as invitations, and never implying obligation. The entire session is structured around the client's own reasoning and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently evokes the client's own motivations rather than providing external reasons for change, with all change-relevant insights generated by the client himself. Could be more expansive in the range of change talk elicited (desire, ability, need, commitment).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a collaborative exploration between equals, positioning the client as the authority on his own experience. Never uses directive language or offers advice; the power dynamic remains consistently flat.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategically evokes change talk at key moments, particularly the 'fog in practice' observation that the client generates himself. The session could have elicited a broader and more sustained range of change talk across multiple dimensions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk (team belonging, peer pressure, identity) with excellent skill by fully validating it without reinforcing, then using the client's own emerging awareness as a counterpoint. Could have more explicitly explored the discrepancy between what the client says he wants and what the current pattern is producing.
