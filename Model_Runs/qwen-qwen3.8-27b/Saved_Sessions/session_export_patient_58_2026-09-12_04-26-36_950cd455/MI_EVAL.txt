### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect and nonjudgment while affirming Jamal’s effort. Acceptance could be warmer by validating his emotional struggle beyond practical problem-solving.

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains consistently focused on the target behavior of getting to school on time. Each question and intervention moves toward concrete change-related steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well on practical steps and incorporates Jamal’s ideas into the plan. Collaboration could be stronger by giving Jamal more open-ended space to set values and priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking what feels doable and highlighting Jamal’s own language. Autonomy support could be stronger by reducing the directive feel of scaling and rapid planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits intrinsic motivation, such as “my morning, my pace.” Evocation could deepen by exploring values and identity rather than relying heavily on menus and confidence scales.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and invites Jamal’s input throughout. Partnership would improve with less clinician-led structuring and more space for spontaneous client-led direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out and expands Jamal’s reasons, desire, and confidence for change. Change talk could be more consistently amplified and consolidated into stronger intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and offers some useful reframing. Softening would improve by more consistently redirecting maternal-pressure comments toward Jamal’s own values and change talk.
