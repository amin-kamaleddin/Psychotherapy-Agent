# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Elena's experiences without shame or correction. Acceptance is strong but could more explicitly affirm her worth beyond self-care success or progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures tension, ambivalence, fear, and hope. Deeper emotional meaning, such as shame or self-worth beliefs, could be explored more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior of self-respect through concrete self-care actions, especially taking a real lunch. The progression from values to obstacles to a workable plan is clear and well-sequenced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Elena to generate solutions and builds on her ideas, such as packing lunch and setting a timer. Collaboration is effective but could include more explicit statements about working out the plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Language such as "if you considered" and "what would feel most possible" supports choice and avoids pressure. Autonomy support would be stronger by more explicitly honoring the option not to try or exploring alternatives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections elicit Elena's own motivation, especially around self-respect, logistics, and boundaries. Evocation could deepen by connecting the plan more explicitly to broader values and identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is positioned as the expert on her experience and her ideas substantially shape the conversation. Partnership could improve with more explicit agenda negotiation or shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits desire and ability through forward-looking questions about a possible small step. Change talk could be strengthened by more deliberate amplification, summarization, or use of multiple change categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing, and some statements are reframed from moral testing to logistics. Softening could become more strategic by actively pivoting sustain talk into stronger change talk.
