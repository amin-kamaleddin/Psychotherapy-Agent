# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful throughout the session. The psychiatrist never labels, criticizes, or pressures, accepting Brandon's resistance and minimization as valid parts of his experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures underlying meaning and emotion, progressing from surface paraphrases to deeper reflections of ambivalence and self-disappointment. The psychiatrist skillfully tracks the shift from rationalization to genuine vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, purposeful arc from minimization through cost exploration to values clarification, always staying within the alcohol-use frame. Pacing is well-calibrated for a contemplative client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is a genuine joint exploration where the psychiatrist adopts the client's own metaphors and images rather than imposing external frameworks. Session direction is clearly shaped by Brandon's leads rather than a predetermined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No recommendations, advice, or controlling language at any point; all change is framed as Brandon's own choice and discovery. Key statements like 'You don't have to figure out who that person is right now' remove pressure while keeping the door open.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every significant insight is generated by Brandon himself through well-timed questions and reflections. The session ends with clearly evoked change talk ('I'm not as sure as I was this morning') that could not have been manufactured by the therapist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, client-directed stance with inclusive language throughout. Could be slightly stronger with more explicit power-sharing or invitations for Brandon to set the pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently creates conditions for change talk to emerge, and Brandon produces significant change talk throughout. Could have more explicitly amplified and reinforced the strongest change talk moments to build additional momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully with double-sided reflections that validate without reinforcing. Could be more strategic in using sustain talk as a springboard to explore alternatives or evoke ability talk.
