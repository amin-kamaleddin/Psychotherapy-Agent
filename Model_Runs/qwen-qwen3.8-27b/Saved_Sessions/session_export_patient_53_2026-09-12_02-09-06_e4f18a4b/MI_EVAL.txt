# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming tone throughout. The delivery is warm and supportive but somewhat formulaic, lacking deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and effectively capture ambivalence and concern about follow-through. However, they often stay at the surface level and do not consistently name deeper emotions such as fear, shame, or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and moves logically toward a concrete, doable micro-step. Pacing is generally appropriate for a hesitant client, though occasional option overload slightly dilutes focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites input, which supports a joint process. However, counselor-generated options and structure dominate, limiting truly equal, client-led collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through choices, privacy, and a no-pressure approach. Minor subtle steering occurs through confidence ratings and framing of preferred options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evocative questions are used and help generate some client-driven ideas and increased confidence. The psychiatrist often shifts to providing practical steps rather than deeply eliciting values, motivations, or client-generated solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are good partnership signals, including invitations and small-step framing. The session remains somewhat counselor-driven because of detailed menus and structured suggestions rather than client-led exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is present and reinforced, especially around small practical steps. It is not deeply cultivated across desire, ability, reasons, or need, and is not consistently amplified when it appears.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument, and some double-sided reflections are effective. However, persistent doubts about follow-through are not consistently redirected or strategically softened.
