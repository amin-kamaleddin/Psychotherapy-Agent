# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental throughout, with frequent validation of David's honesty and choices. Warmth is genuine but measured and professional rather than deeply effusive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture the emotional subtext with remarkable precision (e.g., 'being caught like a kid,' the gap between hating it and doing it anyway). Reflections are layered and progressively deepen understanding of David's inner experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from opening through exploring the habit's function to practical goals, with the target behavior remaining central. Could benefit from slightly tighter momentum toward a concrete, collaboratively agreed-upon action step by the end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue where David's responses determine the next step at every turn; the psychiatrist uses 'we' language and actively seeks input before proceeding. The session feels like two people working together rather than an expert advising a patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly reinforces David's autonomy and ownership throughout, from setting the tone ('there's no pressure') to affirming his right to choose his own path. Autonomy is the backbone of the entire session with no pressure or implied consequences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits David's own reasons and motivations through strategic open questions rather than providing information. Could have more deliberately amplified and expanded on David's change talk once it appeared, particularly reflecting his evidence of capability more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly acknowledges David as the expert on his own experience and positions his words as the primary source of direction. The power dynamic is genuinely equal throughout, with David's contributions substantially shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questions and reflections that elicit David's own practical goals and evidence of ability. Could have gone further in explicitly naming and reinforcing David's change talk as evidence of his capability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, using subtle redirection and honoring David's distinctions (e.g., 'choosing not to' vs. 'not being able to'). A 5 would require more explicit use of double-sided reflections or deliberate pivot techniques to actively transform sustain talk into change talk.
