# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental stance throughout. Acceptance could strengthen by adding warmer, more specific affirmations of Anisa’s strengths and courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures both ambivalence and practical constraints well. The deeper emotional pain, shame, and fear embedded in her daughter’s reaction could be reflected more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on smoking cessation and narrows well to the specific hallway transition. It would benefit from a clearer summary and a readiness or commitment question to strengthen momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Anisa to contribute ideas and designs the pause with her. Collaboration could be stronger by more explicitly negotiating the agenda and co-creating the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client choice is consistently respected, and the language avoids pressure or directive advice. Autonomy support could be reinforced by more explicitly affirming that success and setbacks remain her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively draw out Anisa’s concerns, coping needs, and concrete ideas. The evocation would improve by more intentionally eliciting change talk, confidence, and values-based motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anisa is positioned as the expert on her life, and her input shapes the session. Partnership could be deeper by explicitly acknowledging her as a co-creator and reducing repetitive therapist-driven questioning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist creates space for reasons and steps toward change. This is the clearest area for growth: more actively amplify, reflect, and expand Anisa’s own change talk and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met without argument, persuasion, or minimization. It could be softened further by double-sided reflections that link coping needs to values and a desired future.
