# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirmed Richard’s reasonable goals and preferences. Acceptance was strong, though slightly more transactional than deeply relational.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Richard’s concerns about energy, independence, and travel with his wife. Empathy could be deeper by reflecting the emotional meaning behind his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on starting the cholesterol medication and related adherence steps. Some time spent on detailed medical problem-solving reduced MI momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited Richard’s input and incorporated his chosen cue, simple note, and two-week test. Collaboration was strong but partly guided by clinician-generated options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Richard was given choices and his preference for simplicity was respected. More repeated affirmation that the decision was entirely his would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evoked some values around travel and activity but did not consistently draw out deeper change talk. More questions about importance, confidence, and personal meaning would improve evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was collaborative, with Richard’s preferences shaping the plan. The expert-guided delivery of medical information and options made the partnership slightly less balanced.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Richard expressed meaningful change talk about being ready for the trip and not making excuses with diet. The psychiatrist did not consistently amplify or deepen these statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Concerns about side effects, remembering the pill, and not overthinking were acknowledged. The session could better pivot sustain talk toward change using reflections and value-based questions.
