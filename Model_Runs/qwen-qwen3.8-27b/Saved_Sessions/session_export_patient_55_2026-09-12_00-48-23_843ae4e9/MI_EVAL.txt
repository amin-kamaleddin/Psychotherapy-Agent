# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful but the warmth feels transactional and quickly shifts into problem-solving. Repeated identical format requests create an impersonal, automated tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is an initial brief reflection, but emotional engagement is largely dropped in favor of logistics. The counselor misses key feelings such as fear of failing and family pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stays organized on a single topic, but that topic is off-target from the referral concern. Alcohol risk is never acknowledged or explored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks for client input, but the plan is still counselor-designed and client-fill-in. Collaboration feels superficial rather than truly shared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers some choices, such as block length and study times. However, the overall approach is directive, with the counselor making most of the key decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There is little effort to elicit the client’s own values, goals, or motivation for change. The interaction remains mostly advice-giving and information delivery.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks questions, but the session remains heavily counselor-driven. The client is positioned as a data provider rather than an equal partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There is essentially no attempt to elicit or build change talk. The referral concern is not connected to any client language about wanting or needing change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor does not reinforce sustain talk, but there is also no active softening of it. Sustain talk is mostly avoided because the core concern is not addressed.
