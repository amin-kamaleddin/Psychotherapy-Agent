# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout. Slips and past weight regain are normalized without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately summarizes the patient's experience and concerns, including his wife's reaction. Deeper reflections of fear, shame, or self-image could strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing juice intake to support weight management. The conversation moves logically from motivation to concrete action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is shaped by the patient's preferences, constraints, and wife's role. The psychiatrist frequently offers options and scripts, which can make the interaction somewhat clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes choice, avoids pressure, and positions the patient as the decision-maker. Lapses are normalized, supporting ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used, such as scaling importance and confidence. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to explore deeper values, past success, and personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and invites the patient to choose options, start day, and follow-up details. The interaction is collaborative but sometimes guided by the clinician's suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient offers useful change talk, including desire, ability, and commitment statements. The psychiatrist does not consistently expand or amplify the patient's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges the patient's resistance to quitting OJ forever and supports a manageable alternative. More strategic double-sided reflections could further soften sustain talk.
