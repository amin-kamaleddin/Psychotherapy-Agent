# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and offers multiple affirmations. However, the warmth is somewhat formulaic and does not deeply individualize Marco’s specific emotional pain.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides adequate reflections but remains surface-level, particularly in response to repeated failure and exhaustion. The reflections do not fully capture the emotional weight of Marco’s discouragement and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and progresses logically from barriers to concrete planning. Direction is strong and well anchored in Marco’s home context.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites input, but often drives the agenda by generating options and solutions. This makes the interaction feel more like an expert-driven consultation than a true MI partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers some choices and acknowledges Marco’s decision-making, but frequently fills silences with specific plans. This partially undercuts autonomy and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks useful evocative questions but moves too quickly toward advice and concrete planning. Rich opportunities, such as the 6/10 confidence rating, are underused.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative language is present, but the psychiatrist does most of the thinking and planning. Marco’s input is included, yet power-sharing is limited by the directive style.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk and affirms realistic plans, but does not systematically build on it. More amplification of Marco’s own reasons, hopes, and capabilities is needed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk in a generally nonjudgmental way but does not strategically soften or redirect it. Missed opportunities include double-sided reflections and gentle pivoting toward change.
