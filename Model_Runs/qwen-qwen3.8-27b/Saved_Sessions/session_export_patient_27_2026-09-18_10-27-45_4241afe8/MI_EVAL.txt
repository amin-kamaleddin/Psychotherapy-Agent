# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific, individualized warmth (e.g., 'That's not small, Daniela'). No labeling, judgment, or condescension; fear is validated without minimizing or over-pathologizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently go beyond paraphrasing to capture emotional and somatic nuance (e.g., 'your hands don't know what to do and the air just... stops'). Picks up on subtle emotional shifts and validates tension without rushing past it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every intervention circles back to the target behavior (disclosure to friends) with a clear progressive structure from exploration through plan to confidence-building. No topic drift; momentum maintained while respecting Daniela's pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session evolves organically from Daniela's contributions, with the psychiatrist building on her own words and metaphors ('one of your things,' 'that sounds like Daniela'). Power dynamic is genuinely equal — psychiatrist holds space and skill, Daniela holds content and direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions framed as invitations, never directives; Daniela's plan is explicitly credited as hers ('That sounds like you'). Right to choose is acknowledged and validated rather than pushed past.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All movement toward change is elicited from Daniela's own voice through open questions and reflections — no external reasons for change are provided. Textbook evocation; Daniela arrives at her own insight that she's 'bracing for it to be worse than it is.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise about Daniela's fears and solutions is consistently positioned as residing within her, with the psychiatrist's role limited to noticing, reflecting, and gently probing. Session shaped entirely by Daniela's contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniela's self-generated plan is explicitly affirmed and amplified ('You just built something really solid there'), and past positive experiences are probed to build confidence. All change talk is elicited, never imposed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fear of being 'the exception' is fully validated before a gentle counterpoint is introduced via double-sided reflection. Sustain talk is never reinforced or argued against; it is acknowledged and then softly pivoted.
