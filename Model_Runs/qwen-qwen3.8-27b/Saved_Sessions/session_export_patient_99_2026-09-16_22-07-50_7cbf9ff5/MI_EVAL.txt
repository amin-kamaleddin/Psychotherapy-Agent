# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout. Language normalized Matteo’s discomfort and protected his self-concept without correcting or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflected both the content and underlying ambivalence in Matteo’s statements. Double-sided reflections captured the value of social connection alongside physical concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on alcohol use and its impact on Matteo’s weekly routine and recovery. The discussion moved clearly from screening concern to understanding his perspective to exploring a practical first step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and Matteo worked as equal partners, with the session shaped by Matteo’s own ideas. Questions invited joint problem-solving rather than clinician-led instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasized Matteo’s freedom to choose and avoided pressure or directive advice. Decision-making authority was clearly placed with Matteo.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited motivation from Matteo rather than supplying reasons for change. Matteo’s own values and practical concerns guided the exploration of a lower-effort plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided taking an expert or deciding role and repeatedly emphasized Matteo’s own perspective. Matteo’s contributions substantially shaped the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evoked and reinforced meaningful change talk from Matteo. It fell short of a 5 because it could have more explicitly consolidated reasons, confidence, values, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handled resistance by reflecting sustain talk without argument or counter-persuasion. The response gently broadened the frame while honoring Matteo’s social values and concerns.
