# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent non-judgmental stance with clear affirmations like 'That's not a flaw, that's you being honest with yourself.' The warmth occasionally reads as slightly formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures both cognitive and emotional dimensions, particularly the 2 a.m. uncertainty reflection. Could deepen further in moments by exploring the broader emotional weight (e.g., fatigue of managing health while parenting and working) rather than staying at the content level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on asthma management and medication adherence with a logical progression from understanding barriers to identifying concrete goals. A brief collaborative summary or next step at the close would have further solidified the session's momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames work as joint and builds on Maria's own ideas rather than imposing external frameworks. Could be strengthened by more explicitly co-creating a concrete plan or next step with Maria rather than ending at the level of goal identification.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly positions Maria as the decision-maker, using clear autonomy statements and never employing 'should' language. Particularly strong in honoring her right to set her own criteria for adherence ('You don't need another instruction. You need to actually feel it working in your body').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-crafted open questions successfully draw out Maria's own goals and framework (set period, felt difference). Could push deeper into values and identity dimensions, such as connecting her desire to keep up with her kids to her identity as a mother.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative stance with explicit checking for shared understanding and acknowledgment of Maria's expertise in her own body. Could be elevated by more explicit power-sharing moments, such as inviting Maria to set or adjust the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and amplifies Maria's spontaneous change statements, particularly the reflection on 'I'd stop wasting energy being scared and just act.' Could explore Maria's past successes and strengths more deeply to support her confidence in following through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframes skepticism and doubt as legitimate concerns without arguing or dismissing, consistently pivoting toward change. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections when sustain talk arises rather than leaning slightly toward problem-solving questions.
