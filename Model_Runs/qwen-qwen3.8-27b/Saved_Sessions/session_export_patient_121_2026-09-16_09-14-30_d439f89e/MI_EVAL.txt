# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates basic respect and avoids overt judgment, but the tone remains more task-oriented than warmly affirming. There is limited explicit positive regard or normalization of Sofia’s emotional distress and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some reflective statements and acknowledges Sofia’s perspective, but the empathy often stays at a surface or behavioral level. Emotional depth is not consistently explored, particularly around anger, hurt, or isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays generally focused on the target issue of peer conflict and school avoidance. The psychiatrist repeatedly returns to specific situations and choices, though there is occasional drift toward broader problem-solving.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some collaborative language and invites input, but much of the content is therapist-generated. Sofia largely responds to options rather than co-creating goals or next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking Sofia to select options, but the repeated focus on “low-energy” or “least awful” choices can feel subtly prescriptive. This may limit a stronger sense of full ownership over the direction of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are limited attempts to evoke Sofia’s own motivations, values, or confidence for change. The interaction quickly shifts toward suggesting solutions rather than drawing out Sofia’s internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist occasionally invites Sofia’s input but maintains a more expert-driven stance. The interaction feels more like guiding a plan than genuinely sharing power or affirming Sofia’s own expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are few and weak attempts to strengthen change talk. Sofia’s brief expressions of possible interest are not sufficiently reinforced, expanded, or connected to reasons, benefits, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist sometimes acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation, such as recognizing that staying home feels safer. However, the continued focus on problem-solving can keep attention on avoidance rather than softening resistance.
