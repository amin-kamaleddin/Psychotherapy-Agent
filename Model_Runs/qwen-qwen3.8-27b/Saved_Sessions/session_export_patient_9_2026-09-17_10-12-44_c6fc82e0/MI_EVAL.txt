### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. Affirmations reflect positive regard for Amina as a person without labeling or coercive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Amina’s ambivalence, protective instincts, and meaning behind her shutdown. Perspective-taking goes beyond surface-level paraphrasing and remains therapeutically attuned.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on Amina’s language about her son’s father and moves toward a concrete, usable phrase. Direction could be strengthened by briefly summarizing how her shutdown connects back to the target communication goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Amina’s lead and invites her to co-construct goals, phrases, and next steps. Some interpretive labels, such as “steady” or “protective,” slightly reduce the sense that Amina is the sole author of her narrative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking Amina’s perspective and offering alternatives rather than prescribing a script. A few moments, such as saying “You’re clearly motivated,” could be more autonomy-congruent when she expresses hesitation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Amina’s own goals, values, and protective motivations rather than giving advice. Evocation could be deepened by explicitly exploring her confidence, commitment, and broader values related to being a steady parent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and treats Amina as the expert on her life and family. More explicit checking in and use of Amina’s own language would strengthen the sense of equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encourages Amina to generate her own change-oriented goals and phrases. The work could be strengthened by more explicitly eliciting confidence, commitment, and ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles exhaustion and hesitation without arguing, minimizing, or pressuring Amina. More advanced softening could use double-sided reflections to link her shutdown to her desire for steadiness.
