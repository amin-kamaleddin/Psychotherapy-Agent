# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates the patient's ambivalence and practical constraints. Acceptance is strong, though it could be more explicitly unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider demonstrates accurate reflective listening and captures the practical logic behind the patient's perspective. Empathy is somewhat limited by a focus on cognitive content rather than deeper emotional undertones.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session begins with a clear focus on reducing or modifying cigar use, but later drifts toward symptom monitoring, reassurance, and future urgent care. Direction would improve with more consistent refocusing on a concrete change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider builds on the patient's own ideas and uses inclusive, partnership-oriented language. Collaboration is present but could be stronger with more explicit agenda co-creation and power sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The provider consistently supports the patient's freedom of choice and avoids pressuring or overriding his decisions. This is one of the strongest aspects of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The provider asks open-ended questions and reflects the patient's own ideas to evoke some change-related statements. Evocation remains superficial and does not consistently explore values, confidence, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The provider conveys a collaborative stance and invites the patient to guide the conversation. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicit agenda negotiation and reducing subtle provider steering.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The provider elicits some practical change ideas, such as weekend-only cigar use or conditional reduction. However, change talk is not systematically explored, amplified, or consolidated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The provider avoids arguing and uses some double-sided language, but does not consistently soften or redirect sustain talk effectively. Sustain talk often remains dominant without being balanced by change-oriented reflections.
