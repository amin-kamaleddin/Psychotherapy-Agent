# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and avoids moralizing or pressure. The client’s frustration and autonomy are validated throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening that captures ambivalence and practical constraints. Empathy is slightly limited by being more cognitive than emotional, without fully exploring deeper exhaustion or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a clear and consistent focus on smoking cessation and the morning cigarette. The conversation moves logically from general frustration to a specific, manageable behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the client’s input and builds on the client’s own ideas. Collaboration is respectful but not fully co-constructed, as the psychiatrist still leads most of the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports autonomy by emphasizing choice, avoiding pressure, and rejecting a directive role. The client’s plan is framed as an option rather than a fixed rule.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes the client’s values, motivations, and self-designed experiment. Evocation could be stronger by more consistently building on the client’s own change talk rather than offering examples.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions the client as the expert on her own life and encourages client-generated solutions. The partnership is present but not fully explicit in co-creating the session agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully evokes meaningful change talk related to values and small steps. The client’s change talk could be further amplified and reflected back to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance respectfully without argument or persuasion. Softening could be more strategic by using double-sided reflections to redirect sustain talk toward change.
