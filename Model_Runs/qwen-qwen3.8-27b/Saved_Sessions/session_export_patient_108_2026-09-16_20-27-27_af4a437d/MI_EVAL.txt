### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance that validates Oliver’s ambivalence and care. Acceptance could be deeper by explicitly normalizing his shame and self-critical 'villain' feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and capture the practical and emotional conflict well. Some responses move quickly into interpretation or planning before fully sitting with underlying guilt, shame, or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior of asserting a clear, kind boundary. Questions are strategically linked to saying the boundary and preparing for pushback.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs solutions with Oliver rather than giving advice. Oliver’s preferences and feedback actively shape the final wording and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions consistently emphasize Oliver’s choice, voice, and agency. Options are offered without directive pressure, supporting ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions are elicited from Oliver rather than supplied by the psychiatrist. His values, concerns, and capacity are used as resources for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Oliver as the expert on his own voice and needs. The interaction is collaborative, balanced, and client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Oliver’s own change talk is repeatedly evoked, reflected, and amplified. The session effectively moves from ambivalence to concrete, client-generated action language.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Oliver’s sustain talk is acknowledged without challenge and gently redirected toward a workable boundary. The guilt and 'bad guy' theme could be more fully resolved before moving into rehearsal.
