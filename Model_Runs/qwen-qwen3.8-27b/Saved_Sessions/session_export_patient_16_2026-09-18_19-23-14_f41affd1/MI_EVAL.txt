#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes change talk by isolating the behavior from consequences and using the client's own values as mirrors. As a result, Anika generates substantial self-motivated reasons for change, including specific coping strategies and a realistic self-assessment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Sustain talk is consistently validated and acknowledged without argument or righting reflex, with the psychiatrist holding both sides of the ambivalence.
