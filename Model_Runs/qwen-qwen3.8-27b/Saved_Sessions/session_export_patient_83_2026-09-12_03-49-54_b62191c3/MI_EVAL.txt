# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalized Mai’s concerns. Acceptance was strong but could be deeper by affirming Mai’s broader values and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and often captured multiple feelings. Empathy remained somewhat surface-level and did not fully explore the deeper meaning of Mai’s ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on the medication concern, safety issues, and practical next steps. There was no meaningful drift from the target behavior or goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered choices and adapted to Mai’s preferences, such as shortening the script. Collaboration was functional but could have been more client-led by co-creating the plan rather than providing a ready-made script.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mai was consistently offered options and her preferences were respected. A few directive moments, such as the specific script and safety guidance, limited full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some eliciting questions helped Mai clarify her concerns and plan. Evocation was limited because the psychiatrist supplied much of the information, guidance, and solution rather than drawing out Mai’s own motivation and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for preferences and offered choices, which supported partnership. However, the clinician remained the main source of expertise and did not consistently position Mai as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mai expressed several intentions to take the medication safely and follow up with her doctor. The psychiatrist reinforced some change talk but did not deeply elicit Mai’s personal reasons, confidence, or values for adherence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mai’s hesitance and concerns were validated without argument or dismissal. The psychiatrist effectively redirected concerns into practical steps but could have used more MI-specific softening techniques.
