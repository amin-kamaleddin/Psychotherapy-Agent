### Direction

**Comments:** The session drifts from the specific behavioral focus on smoking into broader psychological exploration of self-worth and intergenerational patterns. Would benefit from periodic summaries connecting back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with consistent framing of shared inquiry and open questions. However, the question sequence feels somewhat predetermined rather than genuinely shaped by Linda's spontaneous contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Linda's right to choose, avoids being directive, and never recommends quitting. Could more explicitly affirm Linda's ownership of the change process rather than leaving it implicit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions to draw out Linda's values and desires but does not consistently amplify or build on the change talk that emerges. Lacks use of scaling questions, importance/confidence rulers, or other techniques to deepen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, curious stance and avoids giving advice or making recommendations. The flow is somewhat one-directional with carefully sequenced questions rather than genuine co-creation of the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda offers meaningful change talk (e.g., not feeling so tense, not being on a clock) but the psychiatrist does not consistently reinforce, amplify, or strategically follow up on these statements. The opportunity to build momentum from expressed desire for change is not fully capitalized on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce or argue against sustain talk, which is appropriate, but does not use double-sided reflections or discrepancy-based techniques to help Linda resolve ambivalence. Missed opportunity to highlight tension between her stated values and her behavior.
