# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, nonjudgmental language and affirms Sofia’s strengths and courage. Acceptance could be stronger by more clearly separating her worth from her behavior and validating her guilt, fear, and identity concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers good reflections of Sofia’s conflict between protecting the baby and maintaining her social life. Deeper, affect-focused reflections of her guilt, fear, and ambivalence would increase emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and practical steps for reducing alcohol use. Direction could be more flexible by allowing Sofia’s emotional concerns to guide the pace before moving into planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia participates by choosing tactics and testing phrases, but the psychiatrist supplies much of the plan’s content. Collaboration would be stronger if more strategies emerged directly from Sofia’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Sofia’s choice by asking what feels doable and reassuring her that slips are not failure. Autonomy support could be enhanced by more explicitly affirming her freedom to modify or abandon the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling and questions help elicit some client-generated strategies and motivation. The therapist could more consistently evoke deeper change talk by exploring Sofia’s values, hopes, and personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia’s input and avoids a purely directive stance, but often presents a menu of options before fully exploring her preferences. A more balanced partnership would prioritize Sofia as the expert on her own life and social context.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session builds some change talk through confidence ratings and affirmation of the plan. Change talk is not fully amplified, as the conversation quickly moves into problem-solving rather than expanding Sofia’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Sofia’s reasons for maintaining her current behavior without arguing or dismissing them. Softening sustain talk could be improved by using more double-sided reflections to explore and transform her ambivalence.
