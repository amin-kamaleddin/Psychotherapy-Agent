# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and normalizing throughout, with a strong opening that validates Kenji's experience of being referred. Falls short of a 5 due to a professional measured tone that lacks deeper personal affirmations of Kenji as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong double-sided reflections and accurate capture of emotional nuance (e.g., 'annoying but not in a bad way'). Could go deeper into the emotional underbelly of key moments, such as exploring what 'autopilot' felt like internally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from the reason for being there through to a self-generated action plan, with no drift off topic. Could benefit from identifying a more specific target behavior early in the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The tracking plan emerges organically from Kenji rather than being imposed, demonstrating genuine co-exploration. Could be more explicitly collaborative in co-constructing next steps (e.g., inviting Kenji to shape the follow-up plan).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook demonstration: never gives advice, consistently frames all decisions as Kenji's to make, and explicitly removes pressure ('I don't think you owe anyone a decision right now'). The final exchange where Kenji proposes his own plan and receives simple affirmation without conditions is particularly strong.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Kenji through strategic questions rather than providing external reasons for change. Could be more targeted in eliciting deeper DARN language (Desire, Ability, Reasons, Need), such as asking about confidence in tracking or what being 'the driver' would look like.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance throughout with language that conveys shared exploration. Could lean more toward genuine equality by explicitly inviting Kenji to shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reinforces each shift in Kenji's language and uses strategic questions that prompt self-articulation of change talk. Falls short of a 5 because it could more explicitly elicit desire, ability, and commitment language (DARN).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well without arguing or challenging—instead expressing curiosity that allows Kenji to soften his own position. Could use a more strategic double-sided reflection when Kenji says 'I'm not saying I'm going to quit' to subtly tilt toward the change side.
