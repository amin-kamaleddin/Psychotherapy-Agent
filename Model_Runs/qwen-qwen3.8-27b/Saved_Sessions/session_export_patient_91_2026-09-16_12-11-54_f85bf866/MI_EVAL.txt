# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Marcus’s concerns. Acceptance could be strengthened by affirming Marcus’s worth more independently of his adherence or choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections and validates Marcus’s practical worries. More nuanced exploration of underlying fear, anxiety, and ambivalence would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on diabetes self-management and moves coherently toward a concrete next step. Direction could be enhanced by more consistently tying practical tasks back to Marcus’s values and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates on options and co-creates a plan with Marcus’s input. The stance would be stronger if Marcus generated more of the solutions and wording rather than receiving ready-made scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly asks for Marcus’s preferences and avoids pressuring more testing or a CGM. Autonomy could be further supported by explicitly reinforcing that Marcus is in charge and can modify the plan at any time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk early but quickly shifts into advice-giving and scripting. More open-ended and scaling questions are needed to deepen Marcus’s intrinsic motivation and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows Marcus’s choices to shape the plan, but also takes a strong expert-driven problem-solving role. Partnership would improve by eliciting Marcus’s own language, solutions, and framing more consistently.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marcus offers useful change talk, such as starting with one check and feeling less nervous. The psychiatrist could better develop this by asking what these steps mean to Marcus or what he values about maintaining them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes Marcus’s hesitations without arguing and reframes them as reasonable. More explicit double-sided reflections of ambivalence would make the softening even stronger.
