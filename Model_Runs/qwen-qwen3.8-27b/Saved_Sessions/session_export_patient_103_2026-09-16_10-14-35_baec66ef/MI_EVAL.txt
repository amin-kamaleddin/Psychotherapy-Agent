# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor shows warm, nonjudgmental acceptance and normalizes anger, but can be overly agreeable and mirrors blame rather than affirming deeper needs and mixed feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture practical frustrations and key concerns, but less consistently reach deeper ambivalence, shame, dignity, and fear of being difficult.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, logical focus on the appointment delay and concrete response, though it could reconnect the administrative task to broader patterns, values, and skills.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client edits wording, timeline, and channel, showing partial collaboration, but the counselor remains the main problem-solver and finalizes the message rather than fully co-creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor offers choices and avoids commands, yet frequently supplies scripts, options, and next steps, limiting the client's own agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some opening and choice questions elicit input, but evocation is weak because the counselor supplies direction, scripts, and solutions rather than drawing out the client's motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client contributes and selects options, supporting some partnership, but the counselor still frames the agenda and provides most of the practical content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client offers some change talk about accountability and timely response, but the counselor rarely amplifies or builds on it with evocative follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor avoids argument but often validates external blame, such as the system should work, without double-sided reflections or helping client see personal agency.
