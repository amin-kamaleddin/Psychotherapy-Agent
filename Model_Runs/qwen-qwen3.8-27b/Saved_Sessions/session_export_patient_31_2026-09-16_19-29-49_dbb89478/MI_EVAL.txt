# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, but the warmth is inconsistent and often procedural. There are fewer explicit affirmations and normalizing statements than needed for a stronger acceptance rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections show awareness of David's practical constraints, but they are often more content-focused than emotionally attuned. Opportunities to explore deeper feelings, identity, or fear of being seen as 'hooked' are missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains well-focused on smoking reduction and nicotine replacement during the workday. The sequence is logical and task-oriented, though early turns are somewhat repetitive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates effectively by letting David's practical concerns shape the plan. The stance would be stronger if David's expertise and equal partnership were made more explicit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** This is a major strength: the psychiatrist consistently supports David's choice, pace, and freedom to stop without pressure. Advice and suggestions are framed as options rather than requirements.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively draw out David's own ideas and reasons for change. The evocation could be deeper by exploring values, confidence, and personal meaning more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative, partner stance and invites David to define a realistic plan. However, the partnership is not always explicitly reinforced, and the pacing can feel slightly clinician-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes and reflects change talk, such as wanting less stress, saving money, and reducing smokes. It would be stronger if this change talk were more actively amplified and deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by not challenging David's desire to keep his break smoke. The skill would improve with more active use of double-sided reflections and value-linked redirection.
