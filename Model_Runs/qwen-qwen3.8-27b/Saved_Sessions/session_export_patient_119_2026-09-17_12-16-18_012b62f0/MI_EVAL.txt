### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** [X] 4

**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Selene rather than providing external reasons for change. Never offers medical rationale. Strategic questions ("what would make it worth your while?") elicit authentic change talk. Reflects Selene's own problem-solving to evoke confidence. Could be elevated with more explicit elicitation of confidence ratings (e.g., "On a scale of 1–10, how confident are you…?") or more sustained development of Selene's change talk through follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** [X] 4

**Comments:** Sustain talk handled skillfully. When Selene says "I feel fine, so it's not like it matters" and "My doctor's the one who sent me here, not me," the psychiatrist does not argue, correct, or minimize. Instead, gently reframes and redirects while validating the sustain talk and subtly highlighting client agency. Never reinforces sustain talk or lets it dominate. A 5 would require more explicit strategic use of double-sided reflections or more deliberate pivots away from sustain talk moments.
