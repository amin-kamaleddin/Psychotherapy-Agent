# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a nonjudgmental, respectful, and supportive stance throughout. The acceptance was solid but somewhat procedural, with less explicit normalization of his frustration.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There was effort to understand the patient's perspective, but the empathy remained mostly factual and behavioral. Deeper emotional themes were not fully reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was exceptionally well focused and moved efficiently toward a concrete, sustainable behavior plan. Broader topics were appropriately staged and kept connected to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was clearly co-created, with the patient contributing important choices and problem-solving input. However, the psychiatrist still supplied much of the structure and content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasized choice, reduced pressure, and respected the patient's preferences. There were occasional prescriptive elements, especially in the structured plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some useful self-observation and change talk emerged, but much of the motivation was guided or supplied by the psychiatrist. More questions could have drawn out the patient's own values and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction showed strong collaborative partnership by inviting input and allowing the patient's ideas to shape the plan. The psychiatrist still maintained a noticeable expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient offered meaningful change talk, and some of it was affirmed. The psychiatrist could have more consistently expanded, reflected, and deepened that change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well by reframing the work as a small, flexible experiment without arguing or pressuring. The deeper sustain talk around fear of failure could have been explored more nuancedly.
