# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, supportive, and nonjudgmental, using warm statements such as 'nice work' and 'be gentle with yourself.' However, the stance becomes somewhat procedural when quickly shifting to options and planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are clear efforts to validate Camila’s perspective, such as normalizing low-energy days and worry about consistency. Empathy remains limited because reflections are brief and do not fully capture her ambivalence or emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity through a small, manageable routine. Direction could be strengthened by exploring Camila’s goals, values, and preferred definition of 'doing more.'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adjusts to Camila’s feedback, such as shortening the checklist and accepting a reduced routine. Collaboration is uneven because the psychiatrist still drives the conversation by offering structured options early on.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is generally supported through options, flexibility, and reassurance that missed days are okay. Some directive behavior, such as suggesting a specific routine and check-in, slightly reduces the client-led nature of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers plans and rationales rather than eliciting Camila’s own motivations, values, and reasons for change. There is limited use of open-ended questions to draw out her intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts to Camila’s preferences, showing some collaborative engagement. However, the session is more expert-led than fully partnership-based, as the psychiatrist does not explicitly position Camila as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reinforces Camila’s intentions with affirmations like 'nice work' and 'mini-wins matter.' Change talk is not consistently drawn out through questions about her hopes, confidence, or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by normalizing worry, reframing missed days, and emphasizing small wins. The psychiatrist could further reduce resistance by exploring the deeper meaning behind Camila’s concern about feeling lazy or burdened.
