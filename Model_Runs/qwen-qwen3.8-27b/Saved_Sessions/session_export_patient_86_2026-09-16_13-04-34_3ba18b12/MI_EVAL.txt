### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on smoking reduction/cessation with a clear, practical plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
