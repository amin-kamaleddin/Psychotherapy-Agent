# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and low-pressure, honoring Elena’s boundary to leave. Acceptance is warm but somewhat procedural rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides accurate reflections of relief, fear, and desire for low-pressure steps. Empathy remains practical and could deepen into ambivalence or emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on preventing another pregnancy scare and reduces the conversation to a manageable action. The checklist-like, problem-solving tone limits deeper motivational direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers options and follows Elena’s lead in selecting emergency contraception and a simple grounding plan. Collaboration is present but often resembles a counselor-provided menu rather than fully co-created conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supports Elena’s choice of pace, content, and a small manageable step. Repeated final reminders about vomiting and expiration may feel slightly directive after she wants to leave.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Asks an open initial question and preference-based questions, but quickly shifts to information and problem-solving. It does not deeply evoke Elena’s internal motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Elena to choose options and respects her decisions, but often functions as information provider and agenda-setter. Stronger partnership would more explicitly affirm Elena as the expert on her own body and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Affirms Elena’s statements about not wanting another scare and feeling safer, but does not consistently amplify change talk. More importance, confidence, or value-based questions would strengthen the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles resistance without argument or pressure, making her avoidance of a bigger commitment acceptable. It misses some double-sided reflections that could pivot ambivalence toward change.
