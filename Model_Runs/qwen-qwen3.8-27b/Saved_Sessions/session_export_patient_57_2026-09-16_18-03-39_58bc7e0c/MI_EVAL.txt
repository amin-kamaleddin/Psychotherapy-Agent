# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirmed Jamal's values without moralizing. Minor reflections sometimes assumed his concerns, but they remained supportive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered strong complex reflections of Jamal's ambivalence and social concerns. Empathy could be stronger by explicitly reflecting feelings such as shame, fear of rejection, or stress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on reducing alcohol involvement and building practical ways to maintain team belonging. The conversation progressed logically from refusal language to connection-focused alternatives.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treated Jamal as an equal partner and built the plan around his own ideas. His contributions clearly shaped the discussion and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supported Jamal's choice and avoided pressure, coaching, or imposed solutions. The emphasis on solutions coming from teammates rather than coaches was especially autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evoked Jamal's own values, motivations, and practical ideas rather than providing external reasons for change. Jamal generated much of the content, including refusal language and non-drinking connection points.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Jamal as the expert on his own social context and invited him to generate realistic solutions. This demonstrated strong power sharing and avoided a directive authority role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively drew out change talk through open-ended, client-centered questions. It would be stronger if change talk was more systematically amplified, summarized, or tied to confidence and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handled sustain talk respectfully without arguing or moralizing. Some of the strongest sustain talk could be reframed more strategically using double-sided reflections.
