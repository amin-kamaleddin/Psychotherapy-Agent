# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating tone throughout. The warmth is effective but sometimes feels formulaic and does not fully address Viktor’s broader emotional complexity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate summaries and strong reflections of Viktor’s perspective, especially the cost of losing his evenings. There is less depth in reflecting underlying emotion, such as irritation, defensiveness, or feeling scrutinized.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol-related work performance, particularly Monday grogginess and a concrete recent example. The direction occasionally shifts toward practical problem-solving, which weakens MI-style momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as collaborative and asks permission before suggesting options. However, multiple counselor-generated ideas can make the session feel more advised than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly emphasizes Viktor’s choice, control, and right not to lecture or explore. The repeated “pick one” options still subtly imply that some action is expected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks evocative questions that elicit some change talk, such as wanting less hassle and feeling sharper. The session does not fully develop Viktor’s internal reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Viktor’s input and follows his choice to focus on work rather than his wife’s concerns. The partnership becomes uneven when several counselor-generated options are introduced in succession.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some change talk around less hassle, fewer slow Mondays, and feeling sharper. Missed opportunities to deepen importance, confidence, or meaning limit the development of motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Viktor’s “I manage” statements and validates his perspective. More consistent use of double-sided reflections would better redirect sustain talk toward change.
