# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a calm, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Elena’s ambivalence throughout. Boundaries are respected, and the client is clearly invited to speak without fear of pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Elena’s mixed feelings, fear, and physical anxiety. Some moments move quickly toward reframing or problem-solving instead of fully deepening the emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on contraception, pregnancy-scare fear, and safety. The conversation is guided toward practical, paced next steps without drifting off target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the discussion with Elena rather than imposing expert advice. Elena’s preferences clearly shape the content, such as focusing on reliability and keeping information short.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly emphasizes Elena’s right to choose, her pace, and the optionality of any next step. Autonomy is supported even when she expresses readiness to delay decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively draw out Elena’s own motives and concerns. Evocation could be stronger by more deliberately amplifying and expanding her strongest change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Elena as the expert on her own readiness and choices. Authority over the session is clearly shared, with the client’s input guiding the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena generates meaningful change talk, such as wanting protection and feeling less anxious. The clinician invites this effectively but does not consistently deepen or intensify the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without argument or pressure, and boundaries are honored. Some resistance themes, such as fear of future regret, could have been explored and softened more directly.
