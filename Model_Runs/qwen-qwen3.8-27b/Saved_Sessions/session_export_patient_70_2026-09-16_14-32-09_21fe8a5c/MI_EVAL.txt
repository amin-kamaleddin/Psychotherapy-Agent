# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Lars’s ambivalence, but the validation mostly addresses content rather than offering warmer, individualized affirmations of effort, courage, or worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Lars’s competing pulls, shame, fear, and the emotional meaning behind the address issue. Empathy could be stronger by deepening reflections around underlying guilt, fear of prison, embarrassment, and values such as being a better father.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on a specific target behavior—staying out and taking a concrete reentry step. Each question logically moves Lars from broad boredom toward a smaller, actionable task such as completing a garage application.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Lars rather than prescribing a plan, co-constructing practical options and the address wording. The style is slightly expert-led in structure and information, and could include more explicit mutual problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, invites Lars’s input, and avoids pressuring him about the address wording. More consistent reinforcement of Lars’s ownership and authority over his own decisions would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Lars’s own goals, barriers, and readiness without telling him why he should change. Deeper evocation could connect the practical task to broader personal values such as fatherhood, protecting his future with Donna, or proving to himself that he can succeed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Lars as the person best positioned to know what will work and invites him to shape the session and plan. Partnership could be stronger by more explicitly emphasizing Lars as the expert in his own life and negotiating power more transparently.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes Lars’s desire, ability, and commitment to stay out and move toward garage work. The score is not 5 because the change talk could be more explicitly amplified, expanded, or summarized more powerfully.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk such as boredom, the lads, and beer without judgment and redirects it into problem-solving. It could be stronger by more explicitly reframing sustain talk in service of Lars’s values and resolving the ambivalence between action and safety.
