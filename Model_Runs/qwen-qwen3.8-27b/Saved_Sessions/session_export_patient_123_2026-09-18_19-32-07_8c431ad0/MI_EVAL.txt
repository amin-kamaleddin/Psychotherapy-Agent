# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout, never dismissing or labeling the client. However, the warmth remains somewhat surface-level and formulaic in places, not reaching the depth of truly individualized unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but remain largely at the surface level, reflecting words and behaviors back without probing deeper emotional meaning. Misses opportunities to explore what deflection means emotionally or to gently follow the undercurrents suggesting protective strategies.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The most critical failure of the session—the target behavior (alcohol use) is never mentioned, never explored, and never connected to the screening referral. The session wanders through reading preferences, school workload, and friend dynamics with no strategic movement toward the clinical purpose.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with open-ended questions and genuine mutual exploration rather than interrogation. However, the collaboration is somewhat empty in clinical application because it is not directed toward any shared therapeutic goal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's agency and self-knowledge, never pressuring, pathologizing, or imposing interpretation. However, autonomy support is not deployed in a clinically meaningful way because the target behavior is never addressed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some effort is made to evoke the client's own thoughts and values, but these are directed at irrelevant topics (reading preferences, time management) rather than clinically relevant material. The evocation is present in form but absent in clinical function.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-authoritarian tone and invites the client's perspective throughout. However, the partnership is directionless because there is no shared clinical purpose being negotiated or co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There is zero cultivation of change talk; the target behavior (alcohol use) is never mentioned or connected to the client's stated values or experiences. No change talk is evoked because the clinical target is entirely absent from the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** No sustain talk to soften because the target behavior is never discussed or explored. This reflects a complete absence of engagement with the clinical issue rather than effective skill in managing sustain talk.
