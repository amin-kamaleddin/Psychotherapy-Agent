### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and validates Sofia's perspective without judgment. The clinician respects her dignity and framing, even when she expresses frustration or avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking to clarify Sofia's experience. Deeper exploration of the underlying fear of being labeled, sadness, or loneliness would strengthen the empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target concern of being unfairly treated and unheard at school. It would be slightly stronger if the discussion more explicitly connected the note strategy to broader goals such as attendance, peer conflict management, or self-advocacy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites Sofia to co-create solutions and follows her lead in developing the note and follow-up plan. More explicit shared agenda-setting or negotiation of next steps would push this toward the highest level.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids advice-giving, pressure, and directives while honoring Sofia's dignity and preferences. The criterion would benefit from more explicit autonomy-affirming statements during moments of ambivalence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively draws out Sofia's own solutions, including the note, key sentence, and text confirmation. It would be stronger if the questions more deliberately evoked deeper values, confidence, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician clearly positions Sofia as the expert on her own experience and follows her contributions. Her ideas drive the session without the clinician imposing an external agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits practical change talk by reflecting Sofia's solutions and amplifying their strengths. It would be enhanced by more explicit elicitation of deeper change talk related to values, ability, importance, and future-oriented commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician validates Sofia's sustain talk about being ignored, begging, and unfairness without arguing or dismissing it. The sustain talk is redirected skillfully, though some concerns remain unresolved, preventing a perfect score.
