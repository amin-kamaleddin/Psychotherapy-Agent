# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms Isabella's effort. The acceptance is somewhat generalized and occasionally sounds formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Isabella's social anxiety and preference for safer virtual contact. Empathy remains surface-level and does not deeply explore shame, fear of judgment, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on a specific, low-pressure community activity. The focus becomes overly task-focused, moving quickly into logistical planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Isabella's choices influence the plan, but the psychiatrist drives much of the session with structured options and scripts. More client-generated planning would strengthen true co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly emphasizes choice, optional steps, and Isabella's ability to leave on her terms. Autonomy support is somewhat undermined by the large number of clinician-generated suggestions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk and confidence through options and scaling. The evocation is limited because there is little follow-up to explore Isabella's own values, reasons, and motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session is framed as collaborative, and Isabella's preferences shape parts of the plan. The psychiatrist's frequent generation of scripts and supports makes the interaction feel more expert-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Isabella produces some change talk by selecting a virtual session, drafting a message, and increasing her confidence. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or amplify this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing or dismissing it. The response is often brief and quickly shifts into action planning rather than fully exploring ambivalence.
