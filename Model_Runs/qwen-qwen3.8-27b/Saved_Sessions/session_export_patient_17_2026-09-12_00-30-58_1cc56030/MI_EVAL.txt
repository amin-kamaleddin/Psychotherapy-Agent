# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance without lecturing or blaming. Acceptance is strong, though it could be deepened by more explicitly affirming Anisa’s values and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some understanding and validation, but reflections remain mostly content-focused. The most emotionally significant moment, Anisa’s daughter walking away, was not fully reflected with deeper empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking cessation, work-break triggers, and a practical one-week plan. There is some drift into repeated safety and education language, but the overall direction is clear.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates a small, manageable plan and respects Anisa’s preferences. However, the interaction is still clinician-led, with much of the content and repeated reminders supplied by the psychiatrist.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anisa is offered choices and her preference to begin with coping strategies is respected. Some directive reminders about alarms, checklists, and scripts make the support slightly less autonomy-focused.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some motivation, including Anisa’s desire to quit and concern about her daughter’s reaction. More evocative questions about values, confidence, and meaning would have strengthened this skill.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anisa’s choice shapes the plan, but the psychiatrist still occupies a strong expert role. The session is more clinician-guided planning than true power-sharing partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited and affirmed, especially around wanting to quit and showing her daughter she is serious. The psychiatrist could more consistently expand and deepen Anisa’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and normalizes the difficulty of quitting, which helps reduce sustain talk. However, ambivalence and barriers are not consistently reflected or softened using MI strategies.
