# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, validating Naomi's struggle without minimizing or over-praising. Consistently accepts her ambivalence, self-dismissal, and caution without trying to talk her out of any of it.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening with genuine depth, capturing emotional paradoxes more precisely than the client has articulated them. Reflections are never generic and consistently build on Naomi's own words to push slightly deeper into the meaning-making layer.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with an organic session arc that builds logically from one step to the next. Could have closed with a slightly more concrete collaboratively-identified next step to give a clearer sense of forward motion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A masterclass in collaborative dialogue where meaning is co-constructed throughout the session. The power differential is minimized to the point where Naomi generates the insights and the psychiatrist is a skilled facilitator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently removes pressure for polished answers, never prescribes, and explicitly returns interpretive authority to Naomi. Powerfully reinforces agency by affirming that the resources she discovers are her own, not the therapist's gift.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional evocation where every insight in the session is discovered and named by Naomi herself. The psychiatrist asks the question and lets the answer land, never providing the answer or filling silences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Naomi as the expert on her own experience through inviting language and explicit deference. Every insight is facilitated into existence by Naomi rather than supplied by the therapist — textbook MI partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully and subtly evokes change talk through well-timed, open-ended questions appropriate for a contemplative client. Slightly under-leveraged at key moments where a brief, warm amplification of the change talk could have strengthened its impact before moving on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating, gently redirecting, and using it as a springboard rather than a dead end. Could have been slightly more active in differentiating in-the-moment awareness from abstract knowledge at the moment it arose.
