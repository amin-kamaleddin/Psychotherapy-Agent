# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validating Mai's questions as legitimate and her skepticism as strength. Never judges, labels, or minimizes her concerns throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate captures of Mai's emotional shifts and ambivalence. Could linger more deeply in emotional moments, particularly the disconnect of feeling healthy while being told she needs medication.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, logical progression from understanding the medication to practical integration and self-generated commitment. Slightly tighter focus in a couple of moments would strengthen the flow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative interaction framed as joint exploration, with Mai's responses directly shaping session direction at every decision point. The psychiatrist brings pharmacological knowledge while Mai brings expertise on her own life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Mai's ownership and freedom of choice, affirming her right to question and never using directive language. Mai's final commitment is clearly self-generated rather than coerced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategically designed questions effectively draw out Mai's own reasoning and turning point. Could incorporate scaling or importance/confidence questions to further develop and solidify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal power dynamic throughout, with the psychiatrist offering choices and deferring to Mai's preference at multiple points. Mai's contributions substantially shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively facilitates progression from skepticism to commitment language through open-ended questions and reflections of Mai's own plan. Could more explicitly amplify and reinforce change talk as a standalone before moving forward.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates and allows sustain talk to exist without reinforcing or arguing against it. Could include one or two more double-sided reflections to more actively hold the ambivalence before it naturally resolves.
