# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids shaming Sofia for avoidance, defensiveness, or rejecting advice. Acceptance could be stronger with warmer, more individualized affirmations when Sofia expresses self-blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Sofia’s pain, exhaustion, and reasonableness. However, empathy often remains surface-level and shifts quickly to practical options rather than exploring fear, isolation, and self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session eventually narrows to a relevant, concrete behavior: sitting near Maya and saying “hey.” Direction is diluted by repeated safety checks, scripts, coping techniques, and parent-facing advice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is partial collaboration because Sofia’s preferences influence the final low-risk plan. However, much of the interaction is expert-driven, with prepackaged options and scripts reducing Sofia’s role as an equal problem-solver.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Sofia’s refusals and offers choices rather than demanding compliance. Autonomy support is limited by frequent advice-giving and repeated suggestions for additional scripts or coping lines.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a few open questions early in the session, but mostly provides solutions, scripts, and coping techniques. There are missed opportunities to evoke Sofia’s own values, motivations, and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Sofia’s input and follows her chosen direction, showing some partnership. However, the frequent expert role and script-driven advice weaken the sense of Sofia as the primary expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia expresses important change statements about trying to stay in the room and not keep skipping lunch. The psychiatrist affirms these statements but does not consistently expand, deepen, or amplify them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia’s sustain talk is prominent, and the psychiatrist avoids arguing with her, which is appropriate. However, the sustain talk is not consistently softened or redirected toward change, and some responses may inadvertently reinforce avoidance.
