# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Daniela’s concerns about stigma and peer reactions. Acceptance is strong, though occasionally the repeated script offers make the warmth feel less individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections about Daniela’s worry about becoming “the diabetic” and losing normalcy. Empathy remains somewhat surface-level and does not fully explore the deeper fear, vulnerability, or sadness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on Daniela’s readiness to disclose her diabetes to friends. The focus is clear, though the repeated cycling through scripts and micro-steps feels mechanical rather than strategically MI-guided.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes some collaborative invitations but frequently supplies ready-made scripts and task lists. The interaction becomes expert-driven rather than co-constructed, especially when Daniela says she just wants to talk.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms Daniela’s right to wait and chooses, and avoids overt pressure. However, the repeated offers of texts and planning steps subtly steer toward disclosure rather than fully supporting her self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some early questions evoke Daniela’s concerns, but the session quickly shifts to advice-giving and script provision. There is limited exploration of her own motivations, values, ability, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist includes Daniela in the conversation but often returns to counselor-generated options and plans. Her stated preference to simply talk is not fully followed, making the partnership uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela expresses some change talk, such as wanting to tell someone short and simple. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or expand this change talk and instead focuses on scripts and steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist validates Daniela’s sustain talk about waiting and keeping things normal without arguing or invalidating it. The handling is supportive but does not use deeper MI strategies such as double-sided reflection or decisional balance.
