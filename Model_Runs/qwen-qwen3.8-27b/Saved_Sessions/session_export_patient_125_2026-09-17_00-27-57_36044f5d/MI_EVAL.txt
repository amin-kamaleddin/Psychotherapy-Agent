# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently showed respect, validation, and nonjudgmental acceptance. Warmth was steady but somewhat formulaic and could include deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Tariq’s ambivalence, frustration, and careful balancing. They could go deeper into the emotional weight and underlying anxiety about release.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on target behaviors and a concrete near-term plan. Redirection away from complaints was clear, logical, and effective.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked as a team, building on Tariq’s ideas and co-constructing solutions. Shared ownership could be made more explicit in how the plan and session are framed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tariq’s choices and agency were consistently supported without pressure or imposed solutions. The counselor could more explicitly affirm his self-direction during moments of doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open “what do you think” questions frequently elicited Tariq’s own strategies and motivations. The approach could be varied with scaling, DARN-CAT, or deeper evocative work.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Tariq as the expert and invited him to lead problem-solving. The partnership was strong but somewhat transactional and could involve more shared agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tariq produced ability and plan statements, but the psychiatrist did not consistently amplify or deepen the change talk. More strategic reinforcement, scaling, and DARN-CAT focus would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about the officer was acknowledged without arguing, dismissing, or over-validating. The counselor effectively redirected toward agency and controllable next steps.
