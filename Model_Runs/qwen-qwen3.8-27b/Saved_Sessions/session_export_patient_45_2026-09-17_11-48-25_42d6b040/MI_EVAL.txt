### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative interaction between equals, with Erik's contributions visibly shaping the session direction. The counselor positions himself as a thinking partner rather than an authority figure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly and consistently supports Erik's autonomy, presenting a full range of options without ever imposing a recommendation. The counselor never uses directive language and frames all decisions as Erik's to make.

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Erik, explicitly ceding medical authority while maintaining therapeutic partnership. The session direction is clearly shaped by Erik's contributions rather than the counselor's agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk, such as identifying Erik's emerging 'in between' and helping him specify what change would look like. Could have pushed slightly further on additional sources of change motivation (e.g., wife's perspective, medication independence).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill, working within Erik's frame rather than countering or arguing. Sustain talk is consistently met with validation and gentle redirection while avoiding reinforcement of the status quo.
