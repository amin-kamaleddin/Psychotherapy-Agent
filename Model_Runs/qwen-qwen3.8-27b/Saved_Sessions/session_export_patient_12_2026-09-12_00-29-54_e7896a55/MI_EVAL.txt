# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Amira's distress. However, the acceptance can feel somewhat formulaic and task-oriented rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects several of Amira's core feelings, but often at a surface level. Reflections become somewhat repetitive and miss deeper emotional undercurrents.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation generally stays focused on substance use, safety, and coping. The focus can become narrow and procedural rather than moving toward a client-owned change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Amira's input and uses inclusive language, but still controls much of the session's structure. Amira's contributions do not fully shape the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and asks for Amira's preferences, supporting some autonomy. However, repeated suggestions and directives can feel mildly controlling.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some evocative questions, but the session is more advice- and resource-driven. There is limited exploration of Amira's own values, motivations, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative language is used, but the session remains noticeably expert-driven. The psychiatrist frequently provides structured options rather than fully trusting Amira's own direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira expresses some change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or expand on it. More effort is needed to reinforce and deepen her reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Amira's suffering but does not consistently soften her sustain talk. There is limited use of double-sided reflections or redirection toward her own agency.
