# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with affirmations and positive regard. Could deepen by more explicitly normalizing struggle and affirming worth beyond behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections of ambivalence, practical concerns, and self-aware insight. Could go deeper into feelings such as shame, fear, loneliness, and self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use in a workable, realistic way. The session progresses logically from understanding the issue to a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner rather than an authority, inviting joint exploration and co-creation. The plan is shaped directly by the client’s practical ideas and lived experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice, ownership, and self-determination throughout the session. Avoids pressure or coercion while helping the client design a plan that fits her needs.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses effective open questions to elicit motivations, barriers, and solutions from the client. Could draw out deeper values, identity, and intrinsic reasons for change more consistently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly conveys that the client is the expert on her own life and change process. The psychiatrist collaborates without prescribing, allowing the client’s contributions to lead the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes client-generated change talk and concrete steps toward change. Could strengthen by explicitly reflecting and amplifying statements of ability, desire, and readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well without arguing or reinforcing reasons to maintain drinking. Could use more active double-sided reflections or pivots to turn sustain talk toward change.
