# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids escalating defensiveness. Some praise feels formulaic and mildly instructive rather than fully affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Marcus’s peer context, frustration with warnings, and desire to avoid being a buzzkill. The responses remain more cognitive than emotionally penetrating and under-explore peer belonging and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on weekend party drinking, risks, motivation, and a concrete safety plan. There is no significant drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marcus’s ideas shape the final plan, and the psychiatrist invites choice and modification. The session becomes somewhat clinician-driven when offering multiple lists of strategies and psychoeducation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure to stop and supports Marcus’s choice of risk-reduction strategies. The scaling follow-up briefly frames a higher confidence level as the desired outcome.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and scaling elicit a client-owned plan and confidence rating. The psychiatrist does not sufficiently draw out Marcus’s deeper personal values, reasons for change, or confidence rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses a collaborative style and lets Marcus help shape the plan. Repeated advisory lists create a somewhat uneven power dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marcus offers practical change statements around avoiding shots, using a soda, and leaving if pressured. The psychiatrist relies more on suggesting strategies than amplifying Marcus’s own internal motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk without arguing and normalizes Marcus’s concern about peer acceptance. It could more actively explore the tension between fitting in and staying safe through double-sided reflections.
