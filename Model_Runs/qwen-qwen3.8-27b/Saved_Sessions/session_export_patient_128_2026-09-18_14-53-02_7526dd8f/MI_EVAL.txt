# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, non-judgmental stance throughout, validated Tyler's self-critical language and affirmed his agency and worth. Never used labeling language; treated him as a whole person with natural, individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections captured the specific emotional and cognitive complexity of Tyler's experience with precision, including his ambivalence, the intention-behavior gap, and the cycle of relief/regret. Open-ended questions demonstrated genuine curiosity about his internal experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, strategic focus on alcohol use with a logical progression from general pattern to in-the-moment experience, values conflict, past successes, and a concrete behavioral lever. The session never wandered off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as a joint exploration with consistent use of 'us' and 'together' language, and Tyler's responses genuinely shaped the direction. The power dynamic was equal—the psychiatrist facilitated while Tyler generated all substantive insights.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly affirmed Tyler's freedom to choose and never made recommendations or pressured him toward any course of action. Explored barriers to change without pushing, respecting his right to decide at his own pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every intervention was designed to draw out Tyler's own motivations rather than providing external reasons for change. Tyler articulated his own values, strategy, counter-evidence, and solution independently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Tyler as the expert on his own life and avoided offering advice or expert opinions. All key insights (the first drink, values conflict, potential solution) were Tyler's own formulations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked change talk through strategic questions about past successes and the specific behavioral lever, then amplified and affirmed each instance. Tyler moved from ambivalence to concrete self-generated solutions by the end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk with grace by acknowledging it as real while opening space for exploration, using double-sided reflections and counter-evidence elicitation. Never directly contradicted or dismissed Tyler's reasons for maintaining the status quo.
