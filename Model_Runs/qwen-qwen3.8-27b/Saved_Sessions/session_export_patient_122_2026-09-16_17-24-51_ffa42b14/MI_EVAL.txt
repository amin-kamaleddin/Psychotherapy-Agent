# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes coping behaviors while validating uncertainty. Minor interpretive statements remain supportive and do not undermine acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening strongly captures the client’s tiredness, ambivalence, and underlying worry. A few reflections are slightly interpretive, making the empathy high but not perfect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on finding a small, sustainable after-work movement or pause. The conversation moves purposefully from distress to a concrete, client-relevant plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist engages in joint problem-solving and actively builds on the client’s ideas. The session feels like two partners working together rather than a clinician prescribing a plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice, ownership, and low pressure are consistently emphasized throughout. Examples are framed as possibilities, not requirements, keeping the client in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicit the client’s own motivation, language, and self-generated ideas. The psychiatrist occasionally supplies specific labels or options before the client has fully generated them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently follows the client’s lead and invites input on next steps. Sofia is treated as the primary authority on her own life and preferred approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encourages change talk through open questions and reinforces change language such as “air time” and “a reset.” The score is reduced slightly because the psychiatrist sometimes offers labels or options before the client fully generates them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk and concerns about tiredness or failure are handled without argument, moralizing, or pressure. Barriers are reframed as low-stakes wins, reducing the threat of sustain talk.
