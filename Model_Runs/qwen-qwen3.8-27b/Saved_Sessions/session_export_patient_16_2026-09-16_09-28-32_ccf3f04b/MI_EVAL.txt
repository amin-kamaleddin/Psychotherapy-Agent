# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist is respectful and nonjudgmental, but acceptance is tied mostly to approval of Anika’s choices and plan. There is limited normalization of her ambivalence, stress, or peer influence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist uses clear reflective listening, especially in early summaries of Anika’s mixed views. It could be stronger by exploring the emotional undercurrents, such as peer pressure or fear of looking weak.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on vaping in relation to basketball and high-risk situations. It quickly moves to a concrete plan, confidence scaling, role-play, and a clear exit strategy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist invites input and lets Anika’s choices guide the plan, such as choosing the refusal line and free-throw exit. Collaboration could be stronger if the practical steps were more co-created rather than presented as a list.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The therapist consistently supports autonomy by asking permission, offering choices, and respecting Anika’s decision to stop after a two-step plan. The final summary reinforces her control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist effectively elicits Anika’s own motivations and confidence using questions like what would raise confidence from 7 to 8. It could be stronger by evoking more values and concerns before offering practical strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist generally treats Anika as the expert and allows her preferences to shape the session. It becomes slightly less collaborative when providing a structured list of steps early on.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist successfully elicits useful change talk about playing better, stopping for the season, and using a refusal line. The score is limited by not consistently amplifying or deepening Anika’s change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist appropriately avoids arguing with Anika’s minimization of vaping. It could improve by using double-sided reflections or gently exploring the conflict between “not a big deal” and protecting her season.
