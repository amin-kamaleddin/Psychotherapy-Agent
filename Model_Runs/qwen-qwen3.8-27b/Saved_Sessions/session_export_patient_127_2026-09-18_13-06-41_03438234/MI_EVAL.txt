# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist handles deflections and self-critical remarks with warmth and non-defensiveness. Could be deepened by more explicit normalization of how common Timur's experience is and slightly more warmth when he's particularly self-critical.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and layered reflections that capture the emotional, somatic, and existential weight of Timur's experience (e.g., 'Your hands are still waiting for the old instructions'). Reflections consistently land with the client, who validates them and elaborates further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical arc from the pull of old ways through self-doubt to a concrete client-generated direction. The ending could be sharpened with a brief summary that consolidates themes and identifies a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance treating the session as a joint exploration; the psychiatrist follows Timur's lead and responds to deflections with warmth. Could be deepened by more explicit co-construction moments (e.g., 'How do you want to look at this?') and framing the interaction as two people figuring something out together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent and explicit autonomy support at every turn; the psychiatrist never prescribes, pressures, or recommends specific strategies. Timur generates all change language including the final direction, and the psychiatrist does not add to or redirect it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation drawing motivation entirely from within Timur, with the final question producing his own articulation of a change direction. Could be more systematic across all DARN-CAT categories, particularly ability and confidence talk (e.g., 'What in the last 22 days tells you otherwise?').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative, non-expert stance; the psychiatrist cedes direction to the client and accepts being compared to Fiona without defensiveness. Could be elevated by more explicit acknowledgment of Timur's expertise in his own life and co-construction of the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits Timur's own change language through strategic questions and reflections, culminating in a concrete self-generated direction. Could push further on confidence and self-efficacy talk (e.g., 'On a scale of 1-10, how confident are you that you can manage a week like that?').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk appropriately without dismissing or arguing, reframing 'lazy' by highlighting his standard and validating fear while noting meta-awareness. Could use one or two double-sided reflections to more explicitly hold both sides of ambivalence while gently tilting toward change.
