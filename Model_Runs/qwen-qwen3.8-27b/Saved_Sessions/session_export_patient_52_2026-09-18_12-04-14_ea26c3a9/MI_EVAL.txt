**Comments:** Skillfully elicits motivation from within Irina through open-ended questions that draw out her own reasons for change and solutions. Never provides advice or reasons for change; helps Irina discover and recognize her own motivations and progress.

**Comments:** Clearly conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Irina, positioning herself as a facilitator rather than an authority. The session is shaped entirely by Irina's contributions — the framework, solutions, and goal are all generated by the patient.

**Comments:** Consistently evokes and explicitly names Irina's change talk, helping her recognize her own progress within the session. Successfully moves Irina from 'I don't think I could just not have it' to 'that might actually be better than the wine' through evocation rather than persuasion.
