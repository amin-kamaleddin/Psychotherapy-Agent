# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates the client's fear and avoidance. Acceptance is sometimes formulaic and lacks explicit affirmation of the client's courage or strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects ambivalence, fear, and need for control, especially around the client's safety concerns. Reflections are strong but somewhat repetitive and do not consistently explore deeper emotions such as shame or feeling "in trouble."

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on taking a small step toward medical follow-up. The conversation remains somewhat circular and would benefit from collaboratively selecting one concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages in collaborative problem-solving and follows the client's lead in shaping the conversation. The counselor still leads with a series of questions rather than explicitly co-creating the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control while avoiding pressure or directive language. A few gentle suggestions slightly reduce the purely client-driven quality of the interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open-ended questions that draw out the client's ideas, fears, and preferences. Evocation focuses more on safety and practical conditions than on the client's deeper motivation for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative, non-expert stance and allows the client's concerns to shape the session. The counselor does not fully co-create the agenda or explicitly position the client as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and lightly evokes some change talk, particularly around wanting to know what is going on and protecting her child. Change talk is not consistently amplified, deepened, or strategically developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not argue with or reinforce sustain talk, and uses some mild double-sided reflections. The counselor does not consistently link avoidance back to values or evoke the change side in the presence of strong sustain talk.
