# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys unconditional positive regard without judgment, labeling, or moralizing. The client’s ambivalence, limitations, and worries are met with respect and acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurately captures ambivalence, pressure, soreness, and desire for manageability. However, some reflections remain more cognitive or summarizing rather than deeply emotional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity in a manageable way and steadily narrows the plan. The main limitation is the lack of a clear collaborative summary or concrete next step at the end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is strongly collaborative, with the psychiatrist inviting input and building on the client’s own words. The client’s ideas visibly shape the direction and emerging plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports choice and ownership, avoiding prescriptive or coercive language. Alternatives such as porch sitting, stretching, or skipping are honored without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from the client’s own values, benefits, and self-generated solutions rather than supplied reasons or advice. The psychiatrist expands and strengthens the client’s own statements effectively.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions the client as the expert on her own life, body, and preferences. The session is driven by the client’s ideas without the clinician assuming an authoritative role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits and reinforces the client’s own change-oriented language throughout the session. Questions and reflections help build the client’s sense of ability, agency, and motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without being reinforced and is often reframed into a less threatening form. However, some reflections remain in the problem frame longer than necessary without pivoting to change or resources.
