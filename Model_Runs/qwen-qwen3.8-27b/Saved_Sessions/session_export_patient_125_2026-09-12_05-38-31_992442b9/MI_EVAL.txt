# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental. Acceptance is sometimes surface-level rather than fully affirming Tariq as a whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects frustration and hurt accurately. However, empathy often stays at the surface and does not consistently explore deeper fear, loss, or vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the immediate phone issue. It does not consistently guide conversation toward broader MI target behaviors such as coping, compliance, family connection, and release preparation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and some role-play, but remains the primary agenda-setter. Collaboration is uneven because Tariq’s ideas do not fully shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and avoids explicit coercion. Autonomy support is weakened by repeated scripts and continued role-play after Tariq says he is done.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used early in the session. However, the psychiatrist often supplies the answers through scripts rather than eliciting Tariq’s own reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Tariq’s input, but the stance is largely expert-driven. The interaction feels more directive than a true partnership between equals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited development of change talk related to coping, compliance, and release. The psychiatrist focuses more on action scripts than on strengthening Tariq’s internal motivation for adaptive behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct argument but does not effectively redirect blame or externalizing sustain talk. There is limited use of double-sided reflection or values-based reframing.
