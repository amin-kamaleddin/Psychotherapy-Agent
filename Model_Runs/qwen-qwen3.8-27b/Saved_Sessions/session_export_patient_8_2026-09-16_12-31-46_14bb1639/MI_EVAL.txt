# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming stance throughout the session. However, the warmth is sometimes formulaic, and the clinician tends to move quickly from acceptance into problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is adequate and accurately captures surface-level ambivalence. However, empathy often remains behavioral rather than emotional, missing deeper fears, shame, and self-critical thoughts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a clear focus on reducing social media use to improve sleep and academic functioning. The direction is strong but can feel overly structured and problem-solving-focused rather than client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is interactive, and Amina’s input meaningfully shapes the plan. Collaboration is a strength, though it is moderated by the clinician’s expert-driven agenda and frequent steering toward implementation details.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina is invited to choose among options, which supports some autonomy. However, repeated confidence targets and concrete clinician-generated supports create a subtly directive dynamic.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions help Amina articulate goals and confidence. However, the psychiatrist often anticipates the client’s solutions before her motivations, values, and preferred approaches are fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A collaborative stance is present as Amina selects and modifies parts of the plan. However, the psychiatrist remains the primary generator of ideas and agenda, limiting true power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effective change talk is evoked through scaling questions and Amina’s trial commitment. However, the frequent introduction of specific options reduces opportunities for the client to generate her own reasons and motivations for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without argument or dismissal. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently use skillful MI strategies, such as double-sided reflections, to integrate pros and cons and resolve ambivalence.
