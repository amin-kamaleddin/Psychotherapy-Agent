### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and nonjudgment are evident throughout the conversation. Statements such as 'That makes complete sense' and 'Two minutes is not nothing' validate Aisha without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both the content and meaning of Aisha’s statements, including her fatigue with body image and self-compassion struggles. The empathy is clinically useful, though deeper emotional exploration could add nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on getting back into movement in a realistic, sustainable way. The conversation moves coherently from identifying a first step to a backup plan, reminder, and meaningful self-credit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses Aisha’s ideas to shape the plan, demonstrating genuine co-construction. More frequent pauses for Aisha to direct the agenda or negotiate level of detail would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly invites Aisha to define what would work rather than prescribing behavior. Explicitly affirming that the plan remains entirely her choice to start, stop, or modify would further enhance autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently drawn from Aisha through evoking questions rather than clinician-provided reasons. Her own language supplies the plan, values, and emerging self-trust.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Aisha is clearly positioned as the expert on her life and week, and her ideas substantially shape the session. The partnership could be strengthened by more explicit shared ownership of the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits desire, reason, need, and commitment language from Aisha. A stronger score would include more systematic development of importance, confidence, and future hope before moving into implementation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fear of failure, all-or-nothing thinking, and self-auditing are skillfully softened and redirected without argument. These responses preserve Aisha’s concerns while moving them toward agency and self-protection.
