### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment or labeling at any point. The psychiatrist validates Liyana's struggle and affirms her worth independent of performance, normalizing her experience repeatedly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, nuanced reflective listening that captures emotional undercurrents beyond surface content. The psychiatrist names deeper feelings (e.g., the scale as the only voice saying 'not yet') and reflects ambivalence without pushing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical arc from surface frustration to emotional impact to underlying needs, with each reflection building on the last. The focus could be tightened toward a specific target behavior or collaboratively identified next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary collaborative behavior with a genuine dialogue between equals; Liyana's ideas directly guide the conversation's direction. No sense of hierarchy or expert-driven agenda, with observations framed as curiosities rather than conclusions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** No directive advice or pressure to commit; questions consistently invite Liyana's own thinking and affirm her agency. Could be elevated by more explicitly reinforcing her ownership of decisions and freedom to choose how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Gold-standard evocation — every question is designed to draw out Liyana's own values and motivations rather than providing external reasons for change. The psychiatrist then reflects and amplifies Liyana's self-generated change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance with inclusive language and positioning Liyana as the expert on her own experience; no expert-driven advice-giving. Could be elevated by more explicitly negotiating the session's direction or sharing power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic evocative questions successfully elicit Liyana's own change language, which is then reflected and amplified. Could push further into eliciting confidence, ability, or commitment statements to reach a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully through reframing and gentle pivots without argument or dismissal, consistently avoiding reinforcement of the 'I'm broken' narrative. A 5 would require more explicit strategic pivots or double-sided reflections that actively transform sustain talk into a springboard for change.
