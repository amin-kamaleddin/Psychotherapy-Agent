# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes Sofia’s difficulty without shaming her marijuana use. It lacks explicit unconditional positive regard or deeper affirmation of her worth beyond her choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects accurately, including her need for fast relief and embodied distress, but often repeats surface-level formulations. It does not fully explore deeper emotions such as fear, shame, or what “bracing for impact” means to her.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Focuses broadly on stress and marijuana, with recurring themes of speed, cost, and risk. The target behavior or change goal is not clearly defined or consistently anchored through agenda-setting and summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and follows some of Sofia’s ideas, but the psychiatrist largely controls the question sequence. The interaction is not fully negotiated or shaped by Sofia’s priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids direct advice and pressure while using non-controlling language. Explicit affirmations of Sofia’s freedom to choose and no-matter-what support are not consistently present.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks open-ended, evocative questions about what would feel quick, real, or automatic. It does not consistently elicit or strengthen importance, confidence, values, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Sofia’s perspective and uses her language, respecting her expertise. Power-sharing remains limited because the session is driven by repeated problem-solving questions rather than a negotiated agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk around cost, legal risk, and family consequences, but does not consistently catch, reflect, or amplify it. Deeper importance, ability, or future-self-related change talk is not developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges the appeal and perceived effectiveness of marijuana without arguing, which is MI-consistent. It does not consistently use double-sided reflections to honor that pull while linking it to Sofia’s values, risks, or autonomy.
