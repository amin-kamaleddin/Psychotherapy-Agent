# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Alexei’s ambivalence without pressuring abstinence. Some comments are slightly evaluative, framing the preferred choice as smart or healthy rather than maintaining fully unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Alexei’s conflicting goals, especially balancing soccer success with peer belonging. Reflections remain more cognitive and do not consistently explore deeper fears, values, or emotional impacts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on alcohol use, soccer performance, scholarship risk, and peer pressure. The psychiatrist strategically guides Alexei from values to a specific, behaviorally relevant alternative plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Alexei’s ideas shape the session, and the psychiatrist builds on his suggestions to co-create a small plan. Collaboration could be strengthened with more explicit “we/let’s” language and more open-ended co-planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently returns decision-making to Alexei and avoids demanding promises or all-or-nothing commitment. The response to Alexei’s refusal to promise total abstinence is highly autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes Alexei’s own motivations by connecting behavior to soccer, scholarship, and skill improvement. A few prompts introduce specific risk factors or direct the conclusion, making the stance slightly less purely evocative.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Alexei’s expertise and avoids an authoritarian stance, allowing his ideas to guide the session. The partnership could be stronger with more explicit power-sharing and client-directed agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits and reinforces Alexei’s desire, reasons, and ability to protect his soccer future. Change talk could be developed more systematically using DARN-C, scaling, or explicit commitment exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles minimization without arguing or shaming, while gently linking Alexei’s perspective to his goals. More active softening would include stronger double-sided reflections that fully integrate minimizing language with change direction.
