### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates David's concerns without defensiveness. Occasionally edges toward slightly over-affirming or formulaic expressions that feel performative rather than organic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening, accurately capturing the disconnect between feeling fine and being told something is wrong. Some reflections stay at the surface level without deeper emotional exploration of underlying fears and autonomy concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with logical progression from ambivalence to concrete plan. Could benefit from clearer agenda-setting and more strategic pacing to maintain engagement given the client's resistant presentation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint exploration and follows David's threads (diet, muscle weakness). Sometimes slightly 'guided' rather than truly equal, occasionally framing David's statements more positively than he framed them himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongest domain of the session—consistently reinforces David's autonomy, never uses pressure or 'should' language, and explicitly respects his boundaries. Immediately backs off and corrects when David pushes back, framing all decisions as his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out David's own motivations and plans rather than providing external reasons for change. Could have more deeply explored his powerful value-based statement about being around for his kids.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and explicitly corrects himself when David pushes back. Occasionally leans toward a 'coaching' role rather than truly equal partnership in framing David's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes specific change talk from a notably resistant client, including a dietary plan, timeline, and conditional commitment. Could have pushed further on David's stated values to deepen the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating rather than arguing or minimizing David's concerns. The muscle weakness fear is acknowledged but not fully softened with a double-sided reflection or further evocation of David's own solution.
