# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, with a warm tone that respects the patient’s concerns. Warmth is sometimes formulaic, and the one numeric slip was handled well but kept the score from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and well timed, capturing ambivalence and fear of wasted time. Empathy stays somewhat surface-level and does not fully explore the deeper emotional cost of past dieting cycles.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on eating, activity, and body image and moves well from exploration to a concrete plan. It could be stronger by more explicitly tying each step back to the patient’s values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session feels like shared problem-solving, with input invited before finalizing the plan. It becomes less collaborative when the psychiatrist provides a prepared menu of options rather than fully eliciting the patient’s ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice and adapts the plan when the patient pushes back, framing steps as experiments. It could be stronger by more explicitly affirming the patient’s own capacity before offering structured options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses evocative questions to draw out the patient’s own motivations, but often shifts into providing structured solutions. Staying longer in evocation would better position the patient as the expert.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids a directive, expert-driven stance and invites the patient’s input throughout. Partnership is slightly limited by presenting structured options before fully eliciting the patient’s own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits meaningful change talk through open questions but does not consistently amplify or build on it. Stronger follow-through would deepen desire, ability, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about the scale is acknowledged without dismissal or argument. It is not deeply explored or strategically redirected, so weight loss remains an unresolved central concern.
