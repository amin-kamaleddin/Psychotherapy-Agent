# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout, with a clear opening that sets a respectful tone. Occasional slight interpretive edges (e.g., 'you want to be seen as just a student') keep it from a perfect 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate captures of Isabelle's confusion and identity discrepancy. Reflections are somewhat formulaic and rarely go deep enough into the emotional texture beneath the surface meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session moves in a circular pattern without establishing a clear target behavior or collaboratively setting a shared agenda. The conversation could have either focused on the screening result or pivoted to a more meaningful thread like IB stress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and validates Isabelle's perspective effectively, including self-correcting an over-interpretation. However, the collaboration is more responsive than co-creative, with limited evidence of actively building on Isabelle's ideas to shape new direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Powerful autonomy-affirming statements throughout, including explicitly declining to push or pretend there is a problem. Minor limitation from occasional interpretive statements that subtly shape the frame rather than purely reflecting back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative moves (open-ended questions, noticing qualifiers) but the evocation remains superficial. Missed opportunities for values exploration, scaling questions, or future-oriented questions that could have drawn out deeper self-motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as collaborative and avoids imposing an agenda. The partnership is somewhat one-sided in that the psychiatrist still leads the structure, and a 5 would require more explicit power-sharing and deferral to Isabelle's framing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes limited efforts to elicit change talk and does not capitalize on Isabelle's comment about being 'not exactly thriving' as a potential seed. The final response is appropriate but passive rather than actively cultivating motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating rather than challenging, and reframes 'wasting our time' positively. Could have used double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to turn sustain talk into a bridge toward greater openness.
