# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, encouraging, and nonjudgmental tone throughout. Acceptance was somewhat formulaic and tied more to plan completion than deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There was limited empathic reflection, mostly at a surface level. Leila's frustration, self-consciousness, and emotional shifts were not fully acknowledged or reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on increasing physical activity and moving toward a concrete walking goal. Direction was effective, though at times overly prescriptive rather than MI-focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Leila contributed important choices, such as walking, texting her sister, and using stairs as a backup. Collaboration was reduced when the psychiatrist provided a ready-made, detailed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were moments supporting Leila's ownership, especially around following up after the walk. Autonomy support was limited by frequent directives and prescribed steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Leila expressed change talk, but the psychiatrist did not consistently elicit or amplify her own motivations, values, or confidence. The session relied more on client readiness than evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative questions were used, and Leila helped shape the plan. The partnership was uneven because the psychiatrist often shifted into an expert, directive role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk was present but not actively strengthened through MI-style questions. The psychiatrist focused more on action planning than amplifying Leila's reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Leila's barriers and redirected toward a doable alternative. Sustain talk was handled acceptably but not with sophisticated MI strategies such as double-sided reflection.
