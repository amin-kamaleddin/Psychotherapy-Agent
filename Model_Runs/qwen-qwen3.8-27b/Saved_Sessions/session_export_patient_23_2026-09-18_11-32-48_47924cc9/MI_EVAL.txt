### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and genuine unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific, individualized validation of Claudia's words and concerns. Never uses labeling, shaming, or corrective language, even when Claudia's goal falls below clinical guidelines.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections that accurately capture both content and underlying emotional meaning, such as the tiredness beneath the drinking pattern and the self-protection in her careful planning. Goes beyond surface paraphrase to reflect emotional texture, values, and self-perception.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical arc from exploration through goal-setting to strategy planning and contingency planning, with each question building naturally on the previous response. Minor limitation in not explicitly anchoring the two-per-night goal as the session's target and lacking a brief collaborative summary at the close.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint exploration where Claudia's ideas (two per night, fewer bottles, bath without wine, self-monitoring) generate the entire plan. The psychiatrist's role is consistently to ask, reflect, and reinforce rather than suggest content or deliver expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively and explicitly reinforces Claudia's ownership, including working within her self-determined goal of two per night rather than pushing the clinical guideline of seven per week. No directive language, unprompted advice, or framing of actions as things she 'should' do.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within Claudia through open questions and reflective listening; no external reasons for change are ever provided. Claudia's final statements are entirely her own language, demonstrating textbook evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly frames the session as a partnership from the first sentence and consistently enacts it by deferring to Claudia as the expert in her own life. The power dynamic remains equal throughout, with the psychiatrist as a skilled facilitator rather than a director.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently amplifies and reinforces Claudia's change talk through strong reflecting responses that elevate her self-efficacy and competence. Open questions are strategically used to elicit further change talk, resulting in a session that ends with concrete plans and genuine confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argues, minimizes, or contradicts sustain talk; instead uses double-sided reflections to validate the legitimacy of Claudia's ambivalence while gently maintaining direction toward change. Sustain talk is consistently held with respect and worked around rather than reinforced as a reason to maintain the status quo.
