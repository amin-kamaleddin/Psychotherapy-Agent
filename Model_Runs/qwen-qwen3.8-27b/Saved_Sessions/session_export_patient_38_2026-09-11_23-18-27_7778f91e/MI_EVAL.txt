# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and repeatedly normalizes Elena’s experience without pressuring her. However, the tone feels somewhat procedural and task-focused, so acceptance is consistent but not deeply warm or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and often reassuring, especially around relief and lingering anxiety. They remain mostly explanatory rather than exploring the deeper ambivalence, fear, and internal conflict Elena expresses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays broadly connected to the pregnancy scare and contraception-related anxiety, and it does revisit next steps. However, the focus frequently drifts into general coping, grounding, and app setup rather than maintaining a clear strategic path toward the referral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is genuine collaboration around the reminder, with Elena choosing timing, wording, and “no decisions.” The overall interaction remains more counselor-directed and task-based than a fully shared exploration of her values and motivations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, respecting Elena’s preference not to decide today, and framing steps as optional and low-pressure. The main limitation is that repeated suggestions of micro-actions can still feel gently pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena offers clear spontaneous change talk, but the psychiatrist rarely uses open-ended questions to evoke her values, concerns, or desired future. The session misses several high-value opportunities to draw out and deepen her own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena is invited to choose among options and her choices shape the final plan, which supports a sense of partnership. However, the psychiatrist maintains an expert-led structure through repeated lists and instructions, making the collaboration uneven and somewhat directive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena produces meaningful change talk, including fear of another scare and a desire to prevent recurrence, but the psychiatrist does not consistently affirm, expand, or reflect that language. There are few active efforts to develop her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with or amplify Elena’s resistance and normalizes ambivalence, which helps keep the interaction safe. However, softening is mostly passive rather than strategic, with limited use of double-sided reflections to help her hold both sides of the conflict.
