# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and low-pressure, using supportive statements such as “It makes sense” and “without pressure.” The main limitation is that the warmth is somewhat formulaic and does not consistently affirm Matteo’s broader strengths or values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is highly accurate and nuanced, capturing Matteo’s ambivalence, social concerns, and in-the-moment experience. The reflections advance the conversation while remaining closely aligned with the client’s perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on alcohol use in Matteo’s social routine, progressively narrowing from general concern to a specific, manageable plan. Direction is strong without becoming prescriptive or off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as a joint exploration and builds directly on Matteo’s ideas rather than imposing a clinician-driven agenda. The client’s suggestions shape the central plan, demonstrating strong power sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice and self-determination are consistently supported through language like “without pressure” and “You know your routine best.” The psychiatrist avoids coercive advice and invites Matteo to generate his own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively uses open questions to draw out Matteo’s own ideas and strategies, such as using a zero beer, staying game-focused, and cueing off Jake. The main limitation is that evocation remains more behavioral and problem-solving-focused, with less development of deeper values, confidence, or identity-related change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Matteo as the expert on his own life and routinely asks for his perspective. The collaborative frame is maintained throughout, with the client’s contributions shaping the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully elicits several change statements, such as trying smaller steps, using a zero beer, and staying focused on the game. However, these statements are not consistently amplified or developed into stronger confidence or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully by being acknowledged rather than challenged, then gently redirected toward smaller change-oriented goals. Examples include reframing “boring” as “staying connected without losing the flow” and narrowing the goal to the first two hours with Jake.
