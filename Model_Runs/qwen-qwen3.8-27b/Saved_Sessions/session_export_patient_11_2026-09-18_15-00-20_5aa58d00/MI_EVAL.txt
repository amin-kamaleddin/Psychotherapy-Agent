# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, non-judgment, and genuine appreciation throughout the session. Occasionally borders on slightly over-articulated in its explicitness (e.g., 'I want to honor how clear that felt'), preventing a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, particularly in capturing the compound nature of loneliness and capability loss. Occasionally moves forward toward reframing before fully landing in the emotional depth of Amira's self-deprecation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent session arc from problem identification through motivation to a concrete plan and contingency plan. Pacing is well-managed with no drift into tangential topics; every response moves the session forward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session feels like a genuine joint exploration, with the psychiatrist building on Amira's contributions rather than redirecting. The psychiatrist occasionally leads slightly more than a fully equal partner would, particularly in longer reflective passages.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, explicit autonomy support woven into nearly every response — ownership language, no prescribing, no 'you should,' and the entire plan is generated by Amira herself. The psychiatrist positions every reframe as a perspective to consider, not a correction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of evocative questions that draw out Amira's own motivations and values rather than providing them. Could be deepened with more varied techniques such as confidence scaling or exploring what a '10' would look like.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Amira as the expert on her own life and never prescribes a plan. Slightly held back from a 5 by elaborate reflections that occasionally overshadow Amira's voice and a lack of explicit power-sharing language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amira generates substantial change talk, effectively facilitated by open questions and amplification of her own words. Could be strengthened with more deliberate use of specific change talk techniques such as confidence exploration or scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframes Amira's self-doubt pattern without reinforcing it, acknowledging the thought while shifting its meaning. Could be slightly more nuanced with techniques like double-sided reflections to explicitly hold both sides before pivoting.
