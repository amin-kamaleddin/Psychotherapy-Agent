# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Demonstrates a largely respectful and nonjudgmental stance with validating statements, though the tone occasionally shifts into task-focused, logistical mode. Acceptance is real but somewhat procedural and doesn't always explicitly affirm Kevin's worth or mixed feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening with nuanced, emotionally relevant reflections of Kevin's frustration, embarrassment, and conflicting desires. Could go further into underlying themes like fear of being seen as irresponsible to reach a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, consistent focus on a concrete target behavior (leaving at 2:00 with pre-booked cab) with no irrelevant drift. Strong example of effective MI direction tied throughout to readiness, barriers, and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is meaningfully co-constructed with Kevin choosing the cab option, leave time, and work excuse. Counselor still takes a somewhat leading role at times when presenting multiple options and detailed checklists.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently uses optional, invitational language and respects Kevin's reluctance to over-structure the plan. Still offers quite a few suggestions and implementation details, which can subtly pressure the preferred counselor structure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Makes effective use of open questions to draw out Kevin's own values, concerns, and capabilities, leading to useful change talk. Could have asked deeper questions exploring the personal cost of injury or discrepancy to fully develop his change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance with Kevin's preferences clearly shaping the agenda and final plan. Counselor occasionally feels more like they are guiding Kevin through a structured plan than fully deferring to Kevin as the expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly and effectively elicits Kevin's own motivations and confidence (7/10) with positive reinforcement of his statements. A stronger performance would have further expanded affirmations and drawn out deeper personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids arguing with Kevin's sustain statements but doesn't consistently use them as a springboard. Misses opportunities with double-sided reflections to pivot from 'don't want to lose the night' toward the discrepancy between values and risks.
