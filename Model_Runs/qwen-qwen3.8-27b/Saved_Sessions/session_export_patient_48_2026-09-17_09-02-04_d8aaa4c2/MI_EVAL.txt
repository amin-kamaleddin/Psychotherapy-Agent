# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful and validating, reducing shame and countering the 'lazy' narrative. It is strong but could be warmer and more individualized, affirming Farid as a whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures ambivalence and emotional undertones, such as the gap between intention and action. Empathy could be deeper around perfectionism, guilt, and fear that small efforts don't count.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on the target behavior of a short walk and the exhaustion barrier, with practical questions about steps, cues, and trust. It would benefit from tighter summaries linking reflections back to exercise behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaboratively elicits Farid's ideas, realism, cues, and trust concerns rather than prescribing solutions. It lacks full co-construction, such as ranking options or explicitly negotiating priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported with statements that the step should feel like a choice, not a rule. Some questions subtly assume the walk/shoe cue as the target rather than fully returning to Farid's own values.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes Farid's own experiences, such as brief clarity at work and the meaning of small moments. It misses opportunities to use rulers, values, and deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Farid's perspective and respects his self-knowledge, allowing his ideas to shape the conversation. Partnership is strong but not fully equal because the clinician maintains a structured agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Useful change talk is elicited, including short walks, stopping after two minutes, and feeling less guilty. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen that change language, so motivation is not fully built.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is reflected neutrally, but active softening is limited, especially around doubt about following through and whether small actions count. More double-sided reflections and redirection toward competence and values would improve it.
