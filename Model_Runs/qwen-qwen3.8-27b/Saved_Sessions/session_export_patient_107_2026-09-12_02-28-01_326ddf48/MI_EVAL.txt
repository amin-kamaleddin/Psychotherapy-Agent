# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently expresses nonjudgmental warmth and affirms Nathaniel’s choices and self-compassion. It is strong, though less than maximal because it emphasizes task affirmation more than unconditional acceptance of his ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the cognitive benefits, worries, and ambivalence, and normalize common patterns. They mostly summarize content rather than explore underlying feelings and meaning, limiting depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing coffee and follows a coherent arc toward a concrete experiment and follow-up. More of the time is spent on planning mechanics than strategic MI momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nathaniel’s input visibly determines the plan, and the tone is mutually respectful and co-constructive. Full collaboration is limited because the agenda and strategy menu are largely counselor-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly supports choice, simplifies tracking, and respects Nathaniel’s right to avoid self-punishment. Strong, though the specificity of scripts, cues, and timing makes it somewhat directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers and open questions invite Nathaniel to articulate motives, standards, and curiosity. Evocation is present but mixed with frequent advising and solution supply, making it less consistent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is collaborative, with Nathaniel’s preferences shaping the plan and permissions asked before adding steps. Power-sharing is strong, but the counselor remains the main source of strategy menus and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rulers, success questions, and curiosity evokes some change talk and commitment. The counselor does not consistently reflect, amplify, or use low importance/confidence scores to build discrepancy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor accepts sustain talk without judgment and uses balanced reflections. It does not actively soften or pivot the strong attachment to coffee toward change.
