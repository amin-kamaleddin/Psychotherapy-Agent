### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout, directly countering David's self-criticism and normalizing his ambivalence. Never expressed disapproval or labeled David negatively; treated his resistance as reasonable at every turn.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently went beyond surface-level paraphrasing to capture underlying emotion, meaning, and function (e.g., the relational significance of the 'reset button'). Accurately tracked David's shifts from structural explanation to frustration to exhausted resignation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, purposeful momentum from exploring ambivalence through understanding the ritual's function to landing on a client-generated first step. Each transition built on the previous without drifting or circling.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** David's ideas substantially shaped the session; the psychiatrist followed his leads on cutting down, the ritual question, and his own 'managing' framing. The power dynamic was genuinely equal, with David challenging frames and articulating his own plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never used directive language ('you should,' 'I recommend'); explicitly declined to answer on David's behalf and validated his own framing over prescriptive ones. Consistently positioned David as the decision-maker, affirming his choice to 'manage' rather than correcting it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was driven entirely by eliciting David's own motivations—his insight about the ritual's function, the recognition that structure matters more than substance, and his own articulation of a first step. No external reasons for change were ever imposed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly rejected the expert-advisor role ('I don't have an answer for that. What do you think?') and named David as the expert on his own pattern. The session was structured as a joint exploration where David's contributions drove the direction.
