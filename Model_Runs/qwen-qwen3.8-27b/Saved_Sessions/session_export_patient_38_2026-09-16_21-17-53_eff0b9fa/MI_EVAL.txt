# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and reassuring, especially around shame and pressure. Warmth is strong, though somewhat formulaic and not fully value-affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Elena’s mixed feelings, tension, and fear of the unknown. The main limitation is repetitive structure and a missed opportunity to go deeper into the emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on preventing another pregnancy scare and related contraception concerns. Direction becomes somewhat solution-focused before fully clarifying Elena’s motivational agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and avoids direct advice, making the session feel exploratory. Repeated therapist-led prompts prevent the conversation from being fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly emphasizes pace, lack of pressure, and Elena’s right not to rush. Some questions subtly assume a next step is expected, weakening explicit autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions but does not consistently evoke Elena’s own reasons, values, confidence, or ability to change. Therapist-provided frames such as “plan” and “consistency” reduce purely client-driven evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Elena’s expertise and agency, inviting her perspective throughout. The session would be stronger with more explicit agenda negotiation and less framing by the psychiatrist.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, especially around consistency and preventing another scare. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, deepen, or reinforce this change talk with importance, confidence, or ability prompts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Elena’s reluctance and discomfort without arguing. However, sustain talk remains active, and more effective change-leaning reflections are not consistently used.
