# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental, accepting David's ambivalence about medication and his concerns. Warmth is somewhat formulaic and at times may overendorse his chosen plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor offers consistent reflections of David's worries about strength, work, and health. Stronger emotional material about fear, frustration, and guardedness is not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays focused on cholesterol, medication concerns, and a one-month lifestyle trial. It could be more strategically directed toward a clearer decision point or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor offers choices and adapts when David rejects options, keeping the work collaborative. The balance remains slightly clinician-led at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor regularly invites David's choices and respects his refusals without pressure. Structured options could be paired with stronger affirmation of his right to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor elicits some client-generated actions, such as cutting red meat and walking after work. Deeper motivations, values, and confidence for change are not consistently drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are moments where David's agency is acknowledged, such as choosing a single tweak. The counselor's frequent generated plans make the partnership feel uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor evokes some change talk around diet, walking, and calling if symptoms occur. The change talk is not consistently amplified, deepened, or consolidated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor avoids arguing with David's resistance to medication and redirects toward lifestyle options. The conversation remains centered on maintaining the status quo without consistent pivoting to values or self-efficacy.
