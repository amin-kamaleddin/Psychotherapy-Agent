# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer is respectful and nonjudgmental but remains more neutral and procedural than warm. There are few explicit affirmations of effort, courage, or personal strength.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer reflects practical goals and concerns adequately but often moves quickly into problem-solving. Emotional cues such as fatigue, worry, and fear of losing momentum are not consistently validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays largely focused on sustainable physical activity and related cues. Focus occasionally drifts into technical logistics, though the direction remains generally relevant to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is built with client input and adapts to her preferences. However, the interviewer sometimes supplies multiple options and detailed steps, which makes the interaction feel more structured than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer regularly invites the client to choose activities, cues, and options. Autonomy is supported, but occasional direct problem-solving reduces the emphasis on the client's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and invitations to identify a small step are used effectively. The interviewer often shifts into practical suggestions before fully eliciting the client's values, motivations, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer maintains a collaborative stance and invites client input on many decisions. At times, the role becomes more expert/teacher-like when providing several suggestions and directing detailed setup steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers clear change talk, such as wanting to feel less drained and readiness to try Thursday. The interviewer does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen this change talk before moving to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer handles barriers well by reframing them into manageable actions and avoiding argument. The score is limited by less consistent use of MI-specific strategies such as double-sided reflections.
