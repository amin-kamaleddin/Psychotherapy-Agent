# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with clear positive regard for the client's independence and limits. It could strengthen by adding more explicit emotional affirmation and warmth beyond conceptual framing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately tracks ambivalence, safety, and logistical constraints. It could deepen by reflecting more of the client's emotional fear, embarrassment, and inner conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior of increasing social connection. The psychiatrist effectively narrows broad participation into increasingly concrete, manageable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's ideas shape the discussion and the psychiatrist builds on their suggestions. However, the session remains more counselor-paced than fully equal in agenda-setting and decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms the client's right to choose and avoids pressure. Options such as leaving without explanation and having no sign-up strongly support self-determination.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses evocative questions rather than advice, inviting the client to identify safe and meaningful conditions. It could more deeply evoke values, meaning, and confidence in the client's ability to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and invites the client to define what would make a step possible. It could strengthen by making co-ownership and shared decision-making more explicit.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk about low-key activities, a parking-lot minute, and possible success indicators. These moments are not consistently expanded, affirmed, or connected to the client's values and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoidant statements are met with understanding rather than resistance, reducing shame around staying home or driving by. It could be stronger by helping the client notice the cost of continued isolation or the value of a small step.
