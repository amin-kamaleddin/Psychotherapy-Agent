### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates Hana’s realistic approach. Acceptance could be slightly stronger by affirming Hana more deeply as a person rather than mainly affirming the plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections show good understanding of Hana’s ambivalence and practical concerns. Empathy could deepen by exploring the emotional weight of her desire for autonomy, fairness, and control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on smoking-related goals and moves clearly from awareness to a concrete plan. Pacing and strategic direction are strong.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as a partner and builds the plan directly from Hana’s ideas. The collaboration is natural, balanced, and client-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure, obligation, or directive advice, consistently framing change as Hana’s own choice. Autonomy is strongly supported throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Hana’s own reasons, preferences, and practical motivation. Evocation could be stronger by exploring deeper values, identity, or long-term personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Hana is treated as the expert on her own life, and the session is shaped by her input. The psychiatrist follows her lead and supports her decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana expresses multiple forms of change talk, including desire, ability, and intention. The psychiatrist could deepen this into stronger values-based or readiness-focused change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled appropriately without argument or pressure. The psychiatrist could use more advanced techniques, such as double-sided reflections, to soften resistance more fully.
