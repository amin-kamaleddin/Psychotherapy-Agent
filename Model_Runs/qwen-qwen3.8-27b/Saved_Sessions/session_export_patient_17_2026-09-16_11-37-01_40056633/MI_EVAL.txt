# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed respect, nonjudgment, and validation. Acceptance was strong but occasionally focused more on Anisa’s plan than her worth independent of success or failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician offered accurate content reflections and summarized key stressors well. Deeper, more emotionally nuanced reflections of shame, stigma, and fear of being seen as failing were limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking cessation, stress triggers, and a concrete follow-up plan. It moved efficiently, though some planning could have been more client-generated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan incorporated Anisa’s preferences, context, and realistic choices. The psychiatrist provided useful structure, but some parts of the plan remained more clinician-driven than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used permission, choices, and low-pressure language to support autonomy. Some mildly directive suggestions, especially around follow-up, prevented a higher rating.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicited important change talk through open questions and rulers. There were missed opportunities to continue drawing out Anisa’s values, self-efficacy, and personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session reflected genuine collaboration, with Anisa shaping key decisions. The psychiatrist’s expert role was evident when presenting options and a specific script early in the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anisa expressed clear motivation, confidence anchors, and commitment to a start date. The psychiatrist did not consistently amplify or deepen that change talk before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively reduced catastrophic thinking about failure and relapse. However, the session did not consistently redirect her ambivalence and fear of failure toward her own strengths and motivations.
