# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates warm, genuine unconditional positive regard with zero judgment despite the clinical context. Normalization and validation are individualized to Sofia's specific concerns rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently go beyond surface-level paraphrasing to capture the emotional and existential weight of Sofia's statements. Each reflection adds a new layer of understanding or connects dots Sofia hasn't yet seen herself.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the target behavior while allowing natural exploration of underlying issues, with a logical progression from ambivalence to concrete intention. A score of 5 would require more explicit anchoring back to alcohol abstinence in the later stages.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently follows Sofia's lead, co-constructs the conversation, and maintains an equal power dynamic throughout. The session direction is shaped entirely by Sofia's contributions rather than a predetermined agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly rejects the expert role and consistently attributes agency to Sofia through language like 'That's your own thinking' and 'That's you figuring it out.' There is no directive language, pressure, or implied obligation anywhere in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight Sofia articulates is drawn out from within through careful open questions and strategic reflections rather than being provided by the psychiatrist. The counselor never states reasons for change or offers external persuasion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly establishes a non-judgmental partner stance at the opening and consistently positions Sofia as the one with the answers. Reflections serve to reflect back Sofia's own language and insights rather than imposing an external framework.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully names and amplifies change talk while attributing it to Sofia, progressively moving her from ambivalence to concrete intention. Open questions are strategically used to elicit ability, desire, and identity-based change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated before being gently redirected toward change, never argued against or dismissed. The most skillful moments help Sofia see her own contradictions from her own perspective, which is the most effective form of softening.
