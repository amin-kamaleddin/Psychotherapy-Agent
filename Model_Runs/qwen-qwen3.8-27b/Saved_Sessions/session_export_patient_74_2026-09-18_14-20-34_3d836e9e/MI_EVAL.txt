# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout—warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming without being sycophantic. Never expresses disapproval or subtle pressure, even when Linda offers sustain talk or minimizes her attendance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are complex, nuanced, and consistently capture the emotional undercurrent rather than staying at the content level. Every reflection is accurate and additive, adding something new to the conversation rather than merely repeating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior while respecting Linda's pre-contemplative pacing, never rushing toward a quit date or action plan. Could benefit from slightly more intentional strategic guidance in tracking and building on each phase of the change process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is structured as a genuine dialogue between two people exploring a question together, not an expert assessing a patient. Questions are phrased as invitations, silences are not filled, and Linda's statements consistently determine the next direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Linda's right to make her own choices, using explicit language like 'This is your space' and 'You're allowed to enjoy your break.' Never implies she should, needs to, or ought to change, with no guilt-tripping or health warnings.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides health information, success statistics, or external reasons for change—all movement in the session comes from Linda's own words evoked by skilled questions. The session structures around eliciting Linda's own motivations and insights, which is particularly impressive.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative stance with inclusive language and consistent positioning of Linda as the expert on her own experience. Could be elevated with more explicit power-sharing language or moments acknowledging the psychiatrist's own uncertainty.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively notices and amplifies nascent change talk, validating small shifts while honoring their significance. However, occasionally stops at acknowledging change talk rather than strategically deepening it with further evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles all instances of sustain talk with masterful skill—validating as legitimate, using double-sided language, and never arguing or subtly challenging. Gently creates space for Linda to examine her own reasoning without making her feel wrong.
