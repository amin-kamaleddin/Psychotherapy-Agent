# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and reassures Sofia without pressure. However, acceptance feels somewhat formulaic and does not deeply normalize her ambivalence about being referred.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Empathic responses are limited to brief, practical reflections such as 'Finals can feel overwhelming.' The counselor does not fully explore Sofia's underlying feelings, autonomy, boredom, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The session does not address the intended alcohol-use risk concern or the reason for the referral. Instead, it becomes focused on study skills, workload, and a specific math task.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are moments of collaboration when Sofia chooses math and contributes practical ideas. However, the counselor drives much of the session with structured plans, checklists, and repeated advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and uses 'no pressure' language, supporting Sofia's decision-making. Yet the prescriptive study plan reduces her role as the primary decision-maker about goals and methods.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Evocation is minimal because the counselor quickly moves from an open question to menu-style options and practical advice. Sofia's own values, reasons, concerns, and motivations are not drawn out or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites Sofia's input and allows her to make some choices. However, the interaction quickly becomes more expert-directed than truly client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia offers small statements of intention, such as 'I can do that' and 'I'll give it a shot,' but these are not developed into meaningful change talk. There is little purposeful evocation of desire, ability, need, or commitment related to the clinical concern.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing or escalating when Sofia expresses resistance or reluctance. However, the responses rely more on practical redirection than strong MI-style reflection, re-framing, or exploration.
