# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance throughout. They validated Elena's concerns without criticizing her hesitancy or the online information she encountered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captured Elena's cognitive and emotional concerns about safety and making the right decision. It could deepen further by exploring the underlying anxiety, love, or guilt related to vaccination choices.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on vaccination-related concerns and moved toward a practical plan. Slight improvement is possible by using clearer summaries or agenda-setting to more strategically connect each step to the decision.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treated Elena as an equal partner and built the plan around her stated needs. The conversation was a joint exploration rather than a one-way explanation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elena's decision-making authority was consistently respected, with clear statements that the choice was hers. The psychiatrist invited her preferences rather than pushing for vaccination.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions helped Elena articulate her own criteria and next steps without being persuaded by the psychiatrist. Evocation would be stronger if it also tapped deeper values or motivation specifically related to vaccination.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently treated Elena as the expert on her own values and concerns. Her responses clearly shaped the direction and plan for the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena expressed motivation to become better informed and to prepare practical steps for a future decision. The psychiatrist could further strengthen this by eliciting more explicit change talk about vaccination benefits or protection.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena's sustain talk was handled respectfully without argument or dismissal. However, the session remained centered on risk comparison and could more consistently redirect her concerns toward change using double-sided reflections.
