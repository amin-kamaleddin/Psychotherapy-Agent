# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing ambivalence and reframing self-criticism as care. The warmth is somewhat formulaic and could be more individually attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Marco’s mixed feelings, including the pull of family routines and concern about being a good parent. It could go deeper by explicitly naming underlying emotions such as shame, fear, or grief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on weight-management-related eating choices in family contexts. It could be stronger by consolidating emerging plans and moving more strategically toward concrete commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates by inviting Marco’s input and building on his ideas rather than directing the conversation. The equal-partner stance would be clearer with more explicit joint-effort language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports autonomy through open questions, ownership language, and absence of pressure or advice. Marco’s choices clearly drive the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Marco’s own values, motivations, and solutions rather than imposing external reasons to change. It could more actively evoke confidence and past success when Marco doubts himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly conveys that expertise resides within Marco and avoids taking an expert role. The session is substantially shaped by the client’s own knowledge and preferred steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco generates useful change talk, such as wanting energy to play with his kids and reframing pizza limitation as saving energy. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or develop this into stronger motivation or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing reasons to maintain the status quo. It would benefit from more consistent pivots from acknowledged difficulty toward values and self-efficacy.
