# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and positive regard throughout the session with affirmations like 'I love hearing that' and 'You know yourself well.' However, some affirmations feel slightly formulaic rather than deeply individualized, and there is no explicit statement of unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening with effective complex reflections that accurately capture Elena's conceptual and emotional shifts. However, reflections occasionally add interpretation or editorialize (e.g., 'that tells me the shift is already taking root') rather than purely reflecting, and could capture subtler emotional nuances.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior with clear momentum from affirming progress to identifying strategies to planning maintenance. The session never drifts and pacing is well-calibrated to Elena's readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is genuinely co-constructed, with the psychiatrist actively incorporating Elena's specific language and metaphors into reflections in real time. The interaction feels like two equal partners exploring a topic together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Elena's autonomy by framing questions to elicit her own thinking and never imposing advice or pressing toward any specific action. Elena's own words are honored and reflected back as evidence of her wisdom.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Nearly every intervention is a question directing Elena's attention inward, and she produces the vast majority of the motivational content herself. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change or fills silences with their own agenda.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and acknowledges Elena's self-knowledge, but still leads the agenda and occasionally positions themselves as the one interpreting or adding depth. A 5 would require more explicit acknowledgment that Elena is the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective open-ended questions elicit rich desire, ability, and commitment language from Elena, who produces abundant change talk throughout. The psychiatrist could have more explicitly evoked confidence talk or pushed further into deeper commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is minimal in this session, but when it appears (the 'one slip and the whole thing's broken' pattern), the psychiatrist handles it well by normalizing it and smoothly redirecting toward Elena's current awareness and growth. Limited opportunities make full evaluation difficult.
