# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout the entire session. The psychiatrist never labels coffee use as problematic and treats all behaviors as understandable responses to circumstances.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflective listening that captures underlying meaning and emotional texture beyond simple paraphrase. Each reflection tracks the shifting emotional landscape and mirrors back the most therapeutically relevant layer.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and coherent focus on coffee with a logical progressive arc from general use to the thirty-second window. Could benefit from a culminating summary that ties together insights and collaboratively identifies a next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with language that frames the conversation as joint exploration. However, the session follows a somewhat predetermined arc, and the psychiatrist retains subtle editorial control over direction rather than being visibly redirected by the patient's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports autonomy, never using prescriptive language or implying any particular action is correct. Autonomy is directly named and affirmations of choice are woven naturally into the therapeutic language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently and skillfully draws out the patient's own reasons for and capacity toward change through open questions. Occasionally stops at reflection rather than pushing one more layer deeper into the patient's own meaning-making.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions the patient as the expert on his own experience with humble, non-authoritative language. Could be elevated by more explicitly acknowledging the patient's expertise in ways that visibly shape session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through well-constructed questions that draw out the patient's own reasons and capacity toward change. Could have more explicitly amplified and reinforced change talk moments when they appeared rather than simply reflecting them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with notable skill, validating concerns without reinforcing them and using effective double-sided reflections. Could have more actively pivoted away from sustain talk at key moments using more strategic reflections.
