# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and validates ambivalence without judgment or pressure. Affirmations and nonjudgmental reflections reinforce acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate and nuanced reflections, but sometimes misses deeper emotional layers such as loneliness, guilt, or loss of self. The focus occasionally shifts to problem-solving, limiting emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, strategic focus on a manageable target behavior and a concrete experiment. Direction becomes somewhat plan-driven before fully evoking the client's own motivation and change talk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client meaningfully shapes the agenda, options, and final plan. However, multiple clinician-generated ideas make the interaction feel more consultative than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice, self-determination, and the client's readiness stage. The clinician avoids coercion and reinforces the client's permission to experiment without passing or failing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks some open questions that draw out the client's perspective and priorities. Evocation becomes inconsistent when the session moves quickly into offering solutions and a plan rather than eliciting values, discrepancy, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally conveys a collaborative, non-expert stance and invites client input. The introduction of several clinician-generated techniques can subtly shift the interaction toward expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some useful change talk through open questions and confidence scaling. The clinician could strengthen this by more consistently elaborating, amplifying, and summarizing the client's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with or reinforcing sustain talk, while acknowledging the client's reasons for continuing. Sustain talk remains prominent and is not consistently turned into change-oriented exploration using double-sided reflections.
