# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains consistently warm, nonjudgmental language throughout, validating Hana's experience without ever disapproving or minimizing. Unconditional positive regard is evident from the first statement and sustained across the entire session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are sophisticated and capture both content and emotional meaning, such as identifying the shift from the exam to the story Hana would carry. The psychiatrist accurately identifies the core wound ('hitting right at your sense of self') demonstrating genuine perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation flows logically from identifying the avoidance pattern through emotional drivers to a concrete first step, with each question building strategically on the last. There is no topic drift, and the session maintains clear momentum toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist models vulnerability and uncertainty ('I'm curious,' 'I'm wondering') and follows Hana's leads rather than pushing a predetermined agenda. The session feels like a genuine dialogue between equals rather than a clinician-patient hierarchy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Hana as the expert on her own experience, using curiosity-based questions that place the answer in her hands. No directive language is used, and her right to choose whether or not to take the exam is respected throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every significant change-related insight in the session comes from Hana's own mouth, drawn out by evocative questions like 'What was happening for you that allowed it?' The psychiatrist skillfully connects her past change behavior (the recording) to her present capacity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never positions themselves as the authority, consistently using questions that frame Hana's knowledge as primary. The collaborative agenda is established from the opening question and maintained throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces change talk by connecting past success to present capacity and validating Hana's self-generated concrete step. Slightly more explicit elicitation of confidence or ability language (e.g., asking Hana to rate her confidence) would have strengthened this to a 5.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully by validating the worry without reinforcing it, then redirecting to Hana's agency ('That's the same voice that filed the recording away'). Use of a double-sided reflection in one or two additional moments would further soften the grip of sustain talk.
