# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. It is not a 5 because the positive regard is sometimes subtly interpretive rather than explicitly unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections capture Amina's tension, relief, self-concept, and ambivalence. Some deeper feelings, such as guilt about burdening her cousin, are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and coherent focus on employment and childcare barriers. The progression from concern to a concrete next step is strong and purposeful.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed using Amina's ideas and language. More explicit shared agenda-setting would strengthen the collaborative power balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions and avoids pressure or direct advice. Autonomy support would be stronger with more explicit statements that Amina remains in charge of her choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from Amina through evocative questions and her own language. Her statements of confidence, desire, and intended action drive the planning process.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Amina's lead and allows her concerns to shape the session. Full partnership would include more explicit agenda negotiation and checking in on her preferred focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful change talk about ability, desire, and action. It is not a 5 because the change talk is not systematically amplified or deepened further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers are acknowledged without argument and reframed to preserve agency. More explicit double-sided reflections or softening moves would strengthen this criterion.
