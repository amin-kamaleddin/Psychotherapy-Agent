# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is nonjudgmental, normalizes stress, and affirms some progress, but the tone is practical rather than consistently warm. There is limited deep affirmation of Daniela’s role, exhaustion, and protective identity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses basic reflections and acknowledges stress, but often stays at a surface level. It misses fuller reflection of fear, shame, exhaustion, and maternal protective concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on childcare and reducing smoking/alcohol and moves toward concrete next steps. It falls short of a 5 because procedural detail sometimes takes priority over motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is meaningful collaboration during the role-play, and the psychiatrist adapts to Daniela’s constraints. However, the broader session remains heavily structured and counselor-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, avoids forcing firm commitments, and respects Daniela’s boundaries. Yet frequent scripts, templates, and directive guidance can reduce the sense of client self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some initial evocative questions, but largely supplies answers, options, and scripts. There is limited effort to draw out Daniela’s own values, meanings, and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is often expert-driven, with the psychiatrist providing lists, eligibility details, and scripts. Daniela’s expertise in her own life is not consistently foregrounded, limiting true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela produces meaningful change talk, such as not smoking around her son and reducing cigarettes. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen this motivation through evocative questioning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes barriers and avoids arguing with Daniela’s concerns. However, the session remains anchored in obstacles without consistently pivoting sustain talk toward change.
