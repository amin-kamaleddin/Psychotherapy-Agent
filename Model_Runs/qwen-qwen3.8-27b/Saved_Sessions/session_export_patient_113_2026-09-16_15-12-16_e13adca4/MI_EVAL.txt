# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates respect, nonjudgment, and unconditional positive regard throughout the session. Even when Rowan expresses resistance, the counselor normalizes his perspective without criticizing or approving the avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Rowan's ambivalence, frustration, shame, and desire for simplicity. The counselor demonstrates strong perspective-taking by reflecting both emotional meaning and practical concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the target behavior of charging and using the medical device. The conversation stays connected to practical compliance, scheduling, and a concrete one-week plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor co-constructs the plan with Rowan, allowing his ideas to shape the intervention. The process is collaborative rather than directive, with Rowan contributing key solutions such as keeping the device plugged in and using quick resets.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly emphasizes Rowan's choice, ownership, and right to set boundaries. The final plan is framed as something Rowan is choosing to try, rather than an imposed medical obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor draws out motivation from within Rowan rather than supplying external reasons for change. Open questions and affirmations help Rowan articulate his own values, preferences, and plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor treats Rowan as the expert in his own life and routines. There is strong power sharing, with Rowan's ideas and boundaries directing the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully elicits change talk through open questions and affirmations. Rowan moves toward concrete commitment language, including a specific plan to try the device for one week and check in next week.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles sustain talk well by avoiding argument and reframing resistance as self-protection. A few stronger double-sided reflections could more explicitly pivot Rowan toward his own reasons for using the device.
