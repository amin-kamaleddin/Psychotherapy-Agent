# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance while validating Lukas's ambivalence and capacity. Acceptance could be stronger with more explicit unconditional positive regard beyond the drinking issue.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Lukas's values, social concerns, and tightrope metaphor. The session could go deeper into emotional undercurrents such as fear, shame, or sadness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stayed focused on alcohol use in the context of peer pressure, grades, training, and future goals. The progression moved clearly from ambivalence toward practical self-directed strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor followed Lukas's leads and treated his ideas as central to the process. The partnership could be more explicitly collaborative in agenda-setting and co-ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforced choice and avoided pressure or premature commitment. Language such as 'you know your own life best' supported client ownership effectively.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully elicited motivation from Lukas's own values, fears, and goals rather than providing external reasons for change. Client-generated change talk was strengthened through reflections and affirmations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoided an expert-driven approach and invited Lukas to identify what matters most. Partnership could be more explicit in negotiating the agenda and joint focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively drew out client-generated reasons, ideas, and values for change. It could be stronger by more systematically developing confidence, importance, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and normalized without being reinforced, then redirected toward protective values. The counselor could more actively reframe sustain talk as related to Lukas's own goals and identity.
