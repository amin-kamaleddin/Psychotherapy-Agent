# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses nonjudgmental validation and normalization. Warmth became slightly more transactional during later logistical planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Initial reflections accurately captured Amina's ambivalence and identity concerns. Empathy became more surface-level and task-focused as the session progressed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on phone use and its impact on sleep and studying. However, the direction was somewhat rigid and heavily centered on the behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist led the agenda and problem-solving, creating a hierarchical dynamic. Amina mostly responded to suggestions rather than actively co-constructing the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirmed Amina's choice and highlighted doable options. Autonomy was undermined by prescriptive, clinician-designed solutions that limited Amina's independent agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There was limited exploration of Amina's internal reasons for change. The psychiatrist bypassed key motivational moments, such as Amina's uncertainty about who she is without her phone.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist assumed an expert role and generated most of the solutions. This left Amina in a more passive role, choosing from options rather than co-creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Efforts to cultivate change talk were rare and weak. Missed opportunities to elicit or amplify Amina's own motivations, values, and identity-related reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was occasionally validated and gently reframed. Later, self-defeating beliefs were often met with task-focused suggestions rather than deeper exploration of the underlying resistance.
