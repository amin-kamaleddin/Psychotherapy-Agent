# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental, warm tone that normalizes ambivalence without disapproval. Occasionally formulaic in phrasing and lacks a moment of deep, individualized affirmation of worth beyond behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, particularly around ambivalence and the function of smoking. Some reflections remain surface-level and could capture more emotional nuance (e.g., the sense of loss in the failed quit attempt).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from intake through ambivalence to the function of smoking, staying focused on the target behavior. Could benefit from more strategic summarizing and a clearer sense of building toward a next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with open questions and Linda clearly shaping the session direction. Could be elevated by more explicit power-sharing (e.g., acknowledging her expertise in her own life, co-creating the agenda).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Linda's right to make her own choices without ever pressuring or implying she should stop. Explicitly validates her right to maintain current behavior while gently connecting it to her larger context.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk and self-motivational language through strategic questions and reflective amplification. Could be more sophisticated with techniques like importance/confidence rulers, decisional balance, or deeper values exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert-driven stance with language like 'I'm curious' and 'Can I ask you.' Lacks explicit agenda negotiation and invitation for Linda to set or redirect the session's purpose.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully highlights and amplifies change-relevant statements, linking values (caring for kids) to potential motivation. Could more systematically evoke different types of change talk (desire, ability, need, commitment) and use scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well through validation, double-sided reflections, and avoidance of confrontation. A couple of moments (e.g., 'I don't have any plans to quit') could have included a more active pivot toward exploration.
