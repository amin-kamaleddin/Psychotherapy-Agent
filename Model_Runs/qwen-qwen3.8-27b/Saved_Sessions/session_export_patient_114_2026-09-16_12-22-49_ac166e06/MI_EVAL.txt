# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and uses validating statements that acknowledge Ryan’s concerns. Acceptance is solid, though at times the warmth feels more task-focused than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is adequate reflective listening, but the empathy remains relatively shallow. The psychiatrist captures surface concerns but does not consistently explore the emotional weight behind Ryan’s statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior, risk factors, and a concrete safety plan. The direction is clear and actionable, though it may move too quickly toward action for the stage of change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Ryan’s preferences are incorporated into the plan, but the psychiatrist remains the primary source of structure and solutions. Collaboration is present but uneven, with the counselor driving much of the problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Ryan’s choice and frames the plan as an experiment. Autonomy support is undermined by repeated prompts to act immediately and by direct discussion of possible consequences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses evocative questions such as the readiness ruler and “what would move you from 4 to 6?” Evocation is helpful but inconsistent, as the counselor quickly shifts into advice and planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks permission and invites input, but the session remains counselor-led. Ryan responds to the agenda rather than substantially co-owning the direction of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, especially around protecting Ryan’s record and getting home safely. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect, or expand Ryan’s own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids amplifying Ryan’s sustain talk and responds with validation and redirection. The responses are generally helpful but do not consistently use MI-consistent strategies to explore ambivalence.
