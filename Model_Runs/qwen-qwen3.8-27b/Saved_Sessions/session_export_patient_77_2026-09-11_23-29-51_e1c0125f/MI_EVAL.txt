# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful, normalizing, and nonjudgmental. The support is slightly reduced when the conversation shifts into problem-solving and reassurance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Key emotions such as frustration, exhaustion, and feeling stuck are identified. Reflections remain fairly surface-level and are often followed quickly by advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the plateau, fatigue, and a concrete next step. Direction sometimes narrows to exercise and nutrition details rather than Liyana's values and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for input and adapts to a smaller step. The interaction remains partly expert-driven, with the psychiatrist often acting as the main problem-solver.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Liyana is offered choices and the plan is framed as low-pressure. The frequent detailed instructions can make the plan feel more prescribed than self-led.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides many reasons for change and practical solutions. There are fewer attempts to draw out Liyana's own motivations, hopes, and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Liyana has some influence, especially in choosing a rest day and Thursday. The partnership feels uneven because the psychiatrist frequently steers with expert recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Liyana offers some change talk, such as trying one rest day. The psychiatrist does not consistently reinforce, expand, or build on this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some self-critical statements are reframed with reassurance. However, repeated focus on under-fueling and the scale can keep doubt and sustain talk active.
