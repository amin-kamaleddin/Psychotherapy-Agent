# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist validates without being saccharine and consistently conveys that the client is okay even though things are hard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is exceptional, using specific metaphors that deepen rather than flatten the client's experience. The complex reflections capture both behavioral patterns and emotional exhaustion with accuracy and progressive depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains a coherent thread around stuckness but feels exploratory rather than strategically focused on a specific target behavior. It ends open-endedly without a clear sense of momentum toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaboration with natural flow and joint exploration, though at times the psychiatrist's metaphors do the interpretive work for the client rather than inviting him to find his own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent non-directive framing with strong autonomy-affirming statements like 'you know your life better than I do.' Could be slightly stronger at the end in explicitly affirming the client's right to set his own pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-constructed open questions successfully draw out the client's own language and motivation, including the 3am image and desire for change. Could be more consistent in building on the change talk that emerges rather than shifting to the concrete picture.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and explicit power-sharing, but occasionally takes on a slightly more expert position through metaphor-making and strategic pauses.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully evokes some change talk but does not consistently reinforce or amplify the positive statements the client makes. The session ends with the client reinforcing sustain talk rather than the emerging change talk being the last word.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validates feelings without reinforcing helplessness but does not actively pivot away from sustain talk or use double-sided reflections. The session ends with the client's sustain talk ('the picture makes it worse') as the last word rather than being strategically softened.
