# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, consistent respect and nonjudgment throughout, validating Maria's skepticism and self-research. The warmth occasionally reads slightly scripted or overly deliberate, preventing it from reaching a fully organic quality.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures both cognitive and emotional dimensions of Maria's experience. However, the psychiatrist sometimes reflects at a surface level rather than deeply exploring the underlying fear of losing control over her health decisions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The conversation generally stays on track but drifts in the middle sections into exploratory territory without immediately connecting back to a concrete next step. Lacks strategic pacing and brief summarizing statements to maintain momentum toward an actionable outcome.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a joint exploration and actively seeks Maria's input before moving forward. Occasionally steps into a slightly more directive role when naming shifts or suggesting next steps, which undermines the equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support woven naturally into every interaction. The psychiatrist never pressures Maria, never implies she should use an inhaler, and consistently frames all decisions as Maria's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Maria through strategic questions rather than providing external reasons or appeals to authority. Could go deeper in exploring Maria's personal values (her kids, independence, gym performance) to evoke stronger intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Maria's perspective and explicit acknowledgment of her expertise. Could be strengthened by more explicitly co-creating the agenda and visibly ceding control to Maria in moments of decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes meaningful progression in Maria's change talk from near zero to concrete statements like 'I'll look at it' and 'is there an option that's not daily.' The psychiatrist explicitly names and reinforces the shift, though could explore deeper values-based change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively using double-sided reflections without becoming confrontational or dismissive. The transition from validating Maria's concerns to redirecting sometimes feels slightly quick, and could more explicitly acknowledge the legitimacy of her fear before moving on.
