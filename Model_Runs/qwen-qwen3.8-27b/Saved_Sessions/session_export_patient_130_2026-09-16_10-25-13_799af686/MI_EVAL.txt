# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer maintains a nonjudgmental stance and uses MI-congruent language to reduce shame. Acceptance is somewhat task-oriented and not consistently warm or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections capture Viktor’s ambivalence and validate his perspective. Empathy becomes inconsistent when the interviewer shifts to planning before fully exploring feelings such as pressure or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing weeknight drug use and decreasing arguments with his wife. The main limitation is a heavy emphasis on behavioral planning over values and deeper motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Viktor influences the final plan, showing partial collaboration. The interviewer repeatedly introduces structured options, creating a more counselor-driven dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer emphasizes choice, normalizes uncertainty, and avoids direct demands. Repeated suggestions and planning requests create subtle pressure despite Viktor’s preference not to over-plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are asked, including about values and confidence. The interviewer does not deeply draw out Viktor’s own motivations or explore discrepancies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer invites input and avoids direct orders. However, repeated introduction of new strategies makes the interaction feel less like equal collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer occasionally elicits change talk, such as Viktor agreeing to try a small step. There are missed opportunities to deepen and reinforce Viktor’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer does not challenge Viktor’s sustain talk and maintains a low-pressure tone. More skilled double-sided reflections could better soften resistance while preserving autonomy.
