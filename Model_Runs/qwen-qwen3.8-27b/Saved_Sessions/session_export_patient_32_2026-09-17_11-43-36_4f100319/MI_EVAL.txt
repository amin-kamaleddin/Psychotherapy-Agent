# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with zero judgment and consistent treatment of Derrick as a capable adult. The explicit affirmation of his self-awareness moment ("You didn't have to say that... But you offered the other possibility") is a masterful example of deep respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections that capture multiple layers of Derrick's experience simultaneously, going beyond surface content to identify the emotional and existential core of his drinking. The reflections build progressively, showing genuine deepening understanding rather than repetitive summarization.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session follows a coherent, logical arc from rapport-building through exploring consequences to identifying deeper drivers and alternatives. However, the session ends somewhat open-endedly without a collaborative summary or defined next step that would consolidate the progress made.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with a genuinely equal power dynamic; the session's direction is shaped by Derrick's contributions rather than a predetermined agenda. The psychiatrist's clinical expertise serves Derrick's self-discovery rather than driving it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional and consistent autonomy support, explicitly emphasizing Derrick's choice throughout and never pressuring toward a specific outcome. The opening statement and repeated language like "I'm not asking you to have the answer" powerfully remove pressure while still inviting exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes change talk through strategic open-ended questions that draw out Derrick's own reasoning and solutions. Could be stronger by explicitly exploring confidence ratings and amplifying his strongest change talk (e.g., "that part's not up for debate anymore") with more emphasis.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with frequent invitations for input and consistent positioning of Derrick as the primary authority on his own life. Could be slightly more explicit in co-construction at a couple of moments where the psychiatrist guides the direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates substantial change talk through strategic questions that invite Derrick to articulate his own motivations and plans. Could be stronger by more explicitly reinforcing and amplifying Derrick's key change statements, such as asking him to rate his commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk through validating reflections and double-sided reflections that acknowledge ambivalence without reinforcing resistance. Could use slightly more active softening strategies at one or two points to help Derrick resolve his own ambivalence more explicitly.
