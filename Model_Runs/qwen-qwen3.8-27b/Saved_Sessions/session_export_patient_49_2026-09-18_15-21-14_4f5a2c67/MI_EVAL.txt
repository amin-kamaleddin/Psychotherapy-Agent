# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with validation of the client's uncertainty and right to be where she is. Falls slightly short of a 5 as the warmth remains professional rather than deeply emotionally resonant.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening with creative and accurate reflections of the client's experience. Occasional over-interpretive or editorializing overlays in reflections prevent reaching a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, logical progression focused on the target behavior from awareness through exploration of motivation. Could benefit from a brief summary to consolidate the session's progress before closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative stance with inclusive language and genuine adjustment based on client input. The psychiatrist still selects what information to present next, limiting the degree to which the client shapes session direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently removes pressure and affirms the client's freedom to explore without obligation or timeline. Could strengthen further with more explicit autonomy-supporting language in the later part of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's own motivations and change talk through strategic questions rather than telling. Could be deepened by broader exploration of values, abilities, and confidence (DARN-CAT).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a co-creating framework with explicit power-sharing language like 'we can decide together.' Occasionally shifts into an informational/educational role that introduces a slight expert-learner dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits multiple change talk statements and reinforces them with affirmations. The evocation is effective but does not fully explore the range of DARN-CAT dimensions such as ability and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging the client's perspective without arguing or dismissing. The few sustain talk instances that arose were well managed, though a 5 would require more creative softening strategies.
