### Acceptance

**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance with clear acceptance of ambivalence and the boundary against quitting. Could include slightly warmer, more explicit personal regard.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening for practical conflict and evening tension. Could deepen empathy for the emotional meaning of irritating his partner.

### Direction

**Comments:** Consistent focus on a narrow, concrete target: after-work NRT use to reduce irritability. The session progresses clearly without drifting into broader cessation.

**Comments:** Strong emphasis on choice, pace, and no prescriptive pressure. David remains the owner of the decision and plan.

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance with David treated as the expert on his life. Could make agenda-sharing and power-sharing more explicit.

### Cultivating Change Talk
