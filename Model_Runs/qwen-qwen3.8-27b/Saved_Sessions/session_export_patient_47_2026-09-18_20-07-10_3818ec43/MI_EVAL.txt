# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, validating Evelyn's enjoyment without stigmatizing labels. Slightly formulaic in structure and lacks an explicit statement of unconditional acceptance regardless of her choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate double-sided and complex reflections that capture Evelyn's ambivalence well. Missed opportunities to explore deeper emotional layers such as the loneliness of the quiet house or the vulnerability behind her defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from awareness through understanding to intention and self-efficacy, staying focused on alcohol use throughout. Could have arrived at a more concrete commitment statement or specific action plan with a timeline by the end of the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evelyn's ideas (water, pacing, not-all-or-nothing) naturally shape the session's direction, reflecting a genuine collaborative dynamic. Could be elevated by more explicitly co-constructing next steps together or inviting Evelyn to guide deeper exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength throughout; consistently reinforces Evelyn's freedom to choose and frames all change as self-directed. Never pressures, implies obligation, or suggests she 'should' change anything.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Evelyn's own motivations, values, and identity-based strengths rather than imposing external reasons for change. Could go one level deeper in developing commitment language and exploring the 'why' behind her importance of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary partnership stance with Evelyn positioned as the expert on her own life throughout. The session is genuinely co-directed, with a clear and balanced power dynamic where the psychiatrist serves as a guide rather than an authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questioning effectively elicits substantial change talk, including Evelyn's self-generated plan and identity-based strengths. Could more explicitly amplify and reinforce change talk and explore confidence levels more directly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully using double-sided reflections that honor both sides and gently redirect toward change without arguing or dismissing. Could more explicitly reflect the ambivalence when Evelyn minimizes the concern.
