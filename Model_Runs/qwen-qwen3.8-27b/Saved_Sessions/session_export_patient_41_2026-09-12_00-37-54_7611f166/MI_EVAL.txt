# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used a warm, nonjudgmental, validating tone and normalized the client's fear. However, the directive, plan-pushing approach occasionally undermined the client's pace.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were several accurate but surface-level reflections. The psychiatrist missed opportunities to explore the client's identity-level fear of being someone who starts but never finishes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on the target behavior of studying for the chem test despite anxiety. The main weakness was that the direction was clinician-led rather than client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction was mostly one-directional, with the psychiatrist delivering strategies and the client responding briefly. The client's ideas did not meaningfully shape the plan or session direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Although some choices were offered, the psychiatrist prescribed detailed steps and repeatedly re-offered options after a choice was made. This made the process feel pressuring rather than autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation was minimal after the initial open-ended questions. The client's own motivations, resources, and ideas were largely untapped in favor of external advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist took on an expert, directive role rather than exploring options with the client as an equal partner. The resulting plan appeared prescribed rather than co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist briefly acknowledged the client's change-related statements but did not strategically build on them. There was limited use of questions to amplify the client's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The reframe that freezing reflects anxiety rather than laziness was a strong MI-aligned response. However, sustain talk was often acknowledged briefly and then moved past without deeper exploration.
