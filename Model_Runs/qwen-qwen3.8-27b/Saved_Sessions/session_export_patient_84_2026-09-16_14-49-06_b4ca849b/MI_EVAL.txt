# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, using normalizing reflections such as “I hear two things” and “I'm not saying it's a big deal yet.” Acceptance is consistent but not always deeply affirming of Malik's worth or frustration.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows strong reflective listening, accurately capturing Malik's ambivalence between friendships, peer identity, and his mother's concerns. Empathy is solid but could go deeper into underlying emotions like resentment, fear, or feeling caught.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session moves coherently from broad goals to sports, sleep, and peer interactions. However, the focus is not consistently anchored to the primary target behavior of cannabis use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently invites Malik's input and follows his lead rather than driving the conversation. This is a strong example of shared decision-making and partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Malik's choice and ownership are strongly emphasized through statements like “I can take your lead” and open-ended invitations to design his own approach. The psychiatrist avoids pressure, directives, or imposed solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some open-ended questions that help Malik articulate his own thoughts, especially around sleep and performance. Evocation could be stronger by more consistently drawing out and strengthening Malik's own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly treats Malik as the expert on his own life and allows his concerns to guide the session. Power sharing and shared direction are strong strengths in this interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Malik offers useful change talk, such as getting more sleep and handling a random night off. The psychiatrist could do more to amplify, reflect, and consolidate these statements into stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance well by reflecting and redirecting rather than arguing or challenging. A few more strategic double-sided reflections would make the softening of sustain talk even stronger.
