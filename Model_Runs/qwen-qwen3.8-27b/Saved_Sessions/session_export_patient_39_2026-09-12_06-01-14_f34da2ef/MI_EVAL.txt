# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, affirming, and honors Elias’s independence and boundaries. Warmth is somewhat task-focused and formulaic, so it stops short of fully individualized acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture some of Elias’s goals and preferences, but emotional content such as fear, dignity, and the beauty of the day is not deeply reflected. Empathy is adequate but often stays at the level of plan approval.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a concrete target behavior and moves toward a specific, doable action step. There is some drift into safety and spouse support, but it remains tied to the movement goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias meaningfully shapes the plan through his choices, cues, reminders, and stop signal. However, frequent checklists and structured options make the session feel partly clinician-led rather than fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly affirms Elias’s choices, control, and self-direction. Heavy prescriptive safety advice and checklists partially undercut the autonomy-supportive stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early questions and confidence scaling elicit meaningful client motivation, including independence, beauty, and self-direction. Later, the counselor shifts toward advice and logistics rather than continuing to evoke deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and lets Elias contribute substantially to the plan. Some rapid questioning and advisory moves create a less consistent partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses useful MI prompts and the client generates some clear change talk. There are missed opportunities to amplify and deepen the client’s own motivation before moving into practical planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepts barriers without arguing and incorporates Elias’s limits into the plan. It does not consistently reframe sustain talk toward agency or use strategic reflections to soften concerns about incapacity.
