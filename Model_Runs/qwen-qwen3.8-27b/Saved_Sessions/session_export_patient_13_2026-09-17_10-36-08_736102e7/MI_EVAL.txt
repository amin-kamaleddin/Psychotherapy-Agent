# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard without judgment. Even when Anara expresses fear or shame, the response remains validating and normalizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections show strong understanding of Anara’s ambivalence, protective motives, and emotional burden. However, the deeper identity-based fear of being weak or a burden is not always fully named.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on a manageable step toward seeking medical care. The conversation progresses logically without drift and stays connected to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is largely co-constructed, with Anara’s ideas shaping the approach. At times, the psychiatrist still provides structured options, which slightly reduces fully client-led collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** There is strong emphasis on Anara’s choice, control, and ownership of the plan. The psychiatrist avoids pressure, persuasion, or implied obligation throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Anara’s own values and reasons for change rather than providing external arguments. The evocation could be deeper by expanding Anara’s strongest change talk further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally treats Anara as the expert on her own experience and uses her language. However, some questions and option menus are mildly leading rather than fully client-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully elicits Anara’s own change-oriented language and reinforces her emerging plan. The change talk could be further developed into readiness, confidence, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by normalizing, reframing, and avoiding confrontation. The psychiatrist could have used quicker double-sided reflections or strategic pivots to move the session toward change.
