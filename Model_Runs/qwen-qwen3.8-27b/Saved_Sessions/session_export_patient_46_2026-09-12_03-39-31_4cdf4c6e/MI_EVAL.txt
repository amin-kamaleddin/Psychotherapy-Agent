# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and accepts the client’s boundary around not quitting cold turkey. The stance occasionally leans on external medical concern, which slightly limits unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence, social value, and negative consequences. Empathy remains somewhat cognitive and does not fully explore deeper emotions such as shame, fear, or peer belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on drinking, consequences, and a concrete harm-reduction plan. There is some drift toward doctor/parent concerns and safety questions, with no clear closing summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor collaboratively builds on the client’s water-bottle idea and adapts to feedback. However, the counselor still supplies many options before fully drawing out the client’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice through open invitations, client-selected tactics, and confidence scaling. Autonomy support is occasionally limited by multiple-choice menus and prescriptive details.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some client-generated change talk and a practical plan. Frequent advice-giving and early introduction of options reduce the depth of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks the client to choose priorities and builds on his contribution. Partnership is weakened by frequent counselor-generated menus and scripts rather than full client-led exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers meaningful change talk and commits to a realistic strategy. The counselor does not consistently deepen, reflect, or amplify this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing and refrains from reinforcing abstinence pressure. Sustain talk is not skillfully softened through stronger double-sided reflections or full rolling with resistance.
