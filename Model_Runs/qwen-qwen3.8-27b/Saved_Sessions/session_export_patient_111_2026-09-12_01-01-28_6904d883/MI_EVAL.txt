# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental overall. Warmth is sometimes formulaic and not deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Captures surface-level themes but misses deeper emotions such as fear, relational values, and ambivalence. Reflections often remain at the content level rather than the emotional level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Begins with relevant smoking-cessation questions but drifts into marital communication scripts. The target behavior loses focus after the first few exchanges.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions and offers some options, which supports collaboration. However, frequent script-giving keeps the interaction more counselor-led than joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and avoids pressure to quit. Repeated script suggestions still create a mildly directive tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some initial evocation is present through open questions and the readiness ruler. The psychiatrist misses key opportunities to elicit the client’s own values, concerns, and motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client is invited to contribute and choose, but the psychiatrist remains the primary source of strategies and wording. Continued role-play and script offers, despite rejection, weaken the collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are early attempts to evoke change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently build on the client’s change-relevant statements. The focus often shifts toward sustain talk and boundary scripts instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct confrontation, which is appropriate. However, the session reinforces status-quo language and does not effectively redirect or soften sustain talk through double-sided reflections or reframing.
