# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, never labeling or moralizing, with strong affirmations of Milan's self-regulation. Slightly over-explains the nonjudgmental stance at times (e.g., 'you're not a lost cause'), which could be embodied more naturally.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy throughout, with complex reflections that capture dualities and underlying values. Highlights include identifying the real weight of 'my name' in a close community and the 'accountability without cruelty' synthesis.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical session arc, skillfully weaving social/identity concerns back to the drinking context. Could benefit from a more consolidated summarizing statement at the end that crystallizes direction and identifies a forward step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian power dynamic where Milan's responses directly shape the session's direction and content. The psychiatrist works within Milan's frame rather than steering toward a pre-determined agenda, and key insights emerge co-constructed from Milan's own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook autonomy support — explicitly cedes power, never imposes a plan, and presents genuine either/or choices that fully respect Milan's right to say 'it's fine.' Never frames not changing as failure or moral failing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Milan's own values and motivations through open questions and reflective amplification. Could push deeper into value-based evocation, future-self exploration, or readiness/confidence rulers to further strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Immediately establishes equality and shared agency from the opening line, consistently treating Milan as the authority on his own experience. The psychiatrist positions himself as a reflector rather than an expert, with Milan's contributions substantially shaping the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk indirectly through open questions, then reflects and amplifies it ('accountability without cruelty') to strengthen self-efficacy. Could have pushed further to elicit explicit confidence statements, readiness rulers, or a forward-looking commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategically handles minimization without arguing, acknowledging valid parts while gently exploring tensions ('that tension might be the most honest thing you've said so far'). Could more consistently and actively pivot away from sustain talk rather than sometimes neutrally acknowledging it.
