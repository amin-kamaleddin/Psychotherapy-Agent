# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirms Kareem's choices without pressure. Acceptance is strong but somewhat procedural and formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Kareem's frustration and practical concerns. The psychiatrist misses deeper ambivalence and the subtle glimmer of openness to reduction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on managing smoking under the new workplace policy. However, the direction remains more on maintaining the status quo than on gently exploring change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kareem's choices shape the plan, showing some collaboration. The psychiatrist often provides solution lists before eliciting Kareem's own ideas, making the interaction less equal-partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is strong non-coercive support for Kareem's current choices and rejection of unwanted interventions. Autonomy support is less developed for broader decisions about reduction or quitting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes useful practical details about breaks, timing, and location. Evocation of Kareem's own internal change motivation, values, or reasons for reduction is limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kareem is invited to contribute and his preferences influence the plan. The psychiatrist sometimes takes an expert/advisory role by presenting multiple options before drawing out Kareem's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited effort to elicit or amplify change talk. The psychiatrist does not explore Kareem's possible openness to fewer smokes or connect it to personal values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and respects Kareem's sustain talk, but mostly reinforces the status quo. There is little use of double-sided reflections or redirection toward change.
