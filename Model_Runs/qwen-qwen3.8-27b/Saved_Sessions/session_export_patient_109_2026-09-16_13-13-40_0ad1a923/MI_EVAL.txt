# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance. Discouragement and fear of failure are normalized without blame, correction, or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Rafael’s perspective and validates his honesty. However, the responses often remain practical rather than deeply exploring the emotional weight of his discouragement and fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior of returning to exercise. The conversation remains coherent and progressively moves toward a concrete, realistic plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with Rafael choosing many details of the plan. The score is limited by the psychiatrist’s active role in generating structural elements such as the 7-day experiment and if-then plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports choice and ownership throughout the session. Rafael’s preferences are respected, including self-tracking, simple reminders, and avoiding a report-card-style check-in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Rafael’s own ideas, strengths, and confidence. The evocation could be deeper by exploring values, meaning, and identity-related motivations before moving into planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Rafael as the expert on his life and allows him to shape the plan. However, the therapist still takes a leading role in structuring the intervention, which prevents a full 5.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks strong questions that draw out Rafael’s own plans, confidence, and solutions. The performance could be stronger by amplifying Rafael’s change talk more before introducing therapist-generated options.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by reframing the task as low-pressure and failure-tolerant. The main limitation is using problem-solving more than double-sided reflections to explore ambivalence.
