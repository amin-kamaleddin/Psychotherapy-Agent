### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responds to Jordan’s ambivalence without judgment and validates his perspective respectfully. Some responses feel professionally appropriate but generic, limiting the depth of warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong complex reflections that capture Jordan’s confidence, uncertainty, and concern for his grandparents. At times the empathy is brief or lightly interpretive rather than fully exploring the emotional weight of his statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor keeps the conversation focused on the vaccination decision and its relevance to Jordan’s values and grandparents’ safety. However, the direction could be strengthened with clearer agenda-checking and summaries that consolidate the main decisional issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Jordan’s perspective and allows his concerns to shape the conversation. The collaboration could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Jordan’s role as the expert on his own values and decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure and frames vaccination as one option among others, supporting Jordan’s decision-making. The autonomy support could be stronger with more explicit statements affirming Jordan’s right and ownership of the choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits internal motivation by inviting Jordan to articulate his own concerns and decision criteria. The evocation could be more consistent and strategically deepened through amplification, rating scales, or motivational summarization.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance and invites Jordan to define the issues. The partnership could be more fully developed by explicitly sharing power and confirming the client’s authority in the decision process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits desire, need, and intentionality through open, client-centered questions. The change talk could be strengthened through more consistent amplification, summarization, and commitment-oriented questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance without argumentation and reflects sustain talk respectfully. The sustain talk could be softened further by more consistently reframing concerns and redirecting them toward values and change.
