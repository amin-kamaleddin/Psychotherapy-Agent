# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates consistent respect, validation, and nonjudgmental regard without criticizing ambivalence or past regain. Warmth is strong but somewhat structured, with limited deeper normalization of frustration or disappointment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects both content and feeling, especially around cravings, family dynamics, and protecting progress. Reflections are clear but somewhat content-driven and do not fully explore deeper emotions such as pride, fear, or disappointment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus on reducing juice while protecting walking and diet momentum. The session steadily moves from barriers to concrete, workable planning without noticeable drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works with Marco to refine the plan, using his ideas and language as the basis for next steps. Interaction is collaborative but occasionally therapist-led through example phrasing and structured guidance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice through open questions and avoids commands or pressure. Falls short of a top score because it does not explicitly state that all decisions remain entirely Marco’s to make or reject.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Marco’s own solutions, predictions, and action planning. Could be stronger by more explicitly eliciting deeper values, personal importance, and confidence in change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative, non-expert stance and treats Marco as the expert on his routine and household. Power sharing is good, though supplying structure and example language shows mild leading.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evokes strong change talk through forward-looking questions that elicit plan, ability, and commitment. Could deepen impact by more explicitly amplifying change talk and exploring readiness or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging it without arguing and reframing it into manageable steps. Softening is effective but relies more on redirection than explicit double-sided reflections or rolling-with-resistance language.
