# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout, with no labeling or disapproval at any point. Validates Camila's self-assessment and physical reality without correcting or dismissing them.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture emotional undertones and use Camila's own metaphors (e.g., the 'hill to climb' image). Reflections progressively deepen understanding and are specific rather than generic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear focus on increasing physical activity with a logical progression from understanding barriers to a specific, actionable plan. No drift into unrelated topics at any point.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is clearly co-constructed, with the psychiatrist using 'we' language and genuine shared inquiry rather than expert interrogation. Camila's ideas shape every next step, and the power balance is consistently equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is so natural and consistent that Camila herself recognizes and names its importance. The psychiatrist never prescribes, and frames choices as tests rather than commitments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within Camila; the psychiatrist never provides external reasons for change. The evocation is progressive, moving from general ambivalence to a concrete self-generated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Camila as the expert on her own life and never offers advice or recommendations. The role is clearly facilitator, not prescriber, with an egalitarian power dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is designed to draw out Camila's own motivation, and the progression from ambivalence to a concrete plan is entirely driven by her words. The psychiatrist amplifies and expands change talk without ever providing the reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without dismissal and effectively redirected toward the change direction. Could have used more explicit double-sided reflections or strategic pivots at key moments of sustained resistance (e.g., 'this time will be different and then it isn't').
