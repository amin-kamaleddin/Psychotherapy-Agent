# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist remains respectful and nonjudgmental when Ahmed minimizes his mini-cigar use. Acceptance is strong but could be stronger by more explicitly affirming Ahmed’s values or courage in considering a change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses clear reflections of Ahmed’s perspective, including his desire for a straight answer and low-key approach. Deeper empathy could explore underlying fear, defensiveness, or the meaning of the after-work ritual.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on mini-cigar use and moves coherently toward a four-week pause. Direction is strong but could be consolidated with a clearer commitment check or summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ahmed’s ideas strongly shape the plan, showing a collaborative stance. The psychiatrist could strengthen collaboration by inviting more explicit co-creation of the next step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Ahmed’s choice and avoids pressure or coercion. Ahmed is helped to choose a small, self-directed experiment rather than being told to quit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully elicits Ahmed’s own language and plan for a four-week pause. Evocation could be deeper by exploring values, confidence, or long-term meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses questions and follows Ahmed’s lead, creating a collaborative feel. Partnership could be stronger by explicitly acknowledging Ahmed’s expertise in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Ahmed generates useful change talk, such as pausing for four weeks and trying a smaller change. The psychiatrist could strengthen this with confidence scaling, importance statements, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Ahmed’s minimization, which is appropriate, but does not consistently soften sustain talk. More double-sided reflections would help pivot statements like “mini-cigars aren’t the same as cigarettes” toward change.
