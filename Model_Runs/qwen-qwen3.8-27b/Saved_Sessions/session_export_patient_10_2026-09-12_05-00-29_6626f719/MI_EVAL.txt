# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates the client’s frustration and effort. However, acceptance remains somewhat task-focused rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects both practical and emotional concerns, but sometimes moves quickly into problem-solving. This limits deeper emotional processing at key moments.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains an exceptionally clear focus on the target behavior of returning to work and overcoming the childcare barrier. It consistently guides toward concrete next steps without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client’s preferences shape the session, including the remote interview option, rehearsal line, and neighborhood post. However, the counselor generates much of the content, making the interaction more advisor-driven than equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor regularly asks for the client’s choice and respects her boundary about not practicing aloud. Autonomy is somewhat limited because the counselor often provides the options the client chooses from.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions effectively elicit motivation and confidence. However, the counselor often shifts into advice and logistics before fully developing the client’s own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and asks which options feel doable, but repeatedly leads with lists, scripts, and strategies. The session feels more structured and advisory than truly client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client clearly expresses strong importance, confidence, and commitment to returning to work. The counselor elicits this change talk but does not consistently deepen or connect it to values and identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges barriers without judgment or blame, but often responds with additional practical options. More reflections and double-sided reflections are needed to help the client resolve ambivalence.
