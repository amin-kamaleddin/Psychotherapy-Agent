# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone but tends to be procedural rather than deeply affirming. There are fewer reflections that connect to Oliver’s values or unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some accurate reflections are offered early, but the counselor misses emotionally important moments such as guilt and fear of being the “bad guy.” Empathy is present but not sustained or sufficiently complex.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on the target behavior and moves efficiently toward concrete action steps. This focus is effective, though it becomes quite prescriptive rather than client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaboration, including using Oliver’s draft and inviting rehearsal. However, the counselor largely supplies the framework, making the interaction more advisor-driven than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and allows Oliver to select wording, but frequent direct instructions reduce his sense of ownership. Stronger autonomy support would emphasize Oliver’s control over how and when he acts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor uses some evocative moves, but relies heavily on scripts and behavioral strategies. There is limited exploration of Oliver’s intrinsic motivation, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor takes an expert-driven stance by supplying scripts, timing, and follow-up plans. This limits Oliver’s role as the primary decision-maker and problem-solver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor reinforces some change talk and role-play but does not consistently elicit or deepen Oliver’s own reasons for change. More evocative questions would help Oliver articulate his own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Oliver’s ambivalence and sustain talk are often bypassed in favor of scripts and contingency planning. The counselor does not effectively use complex or double-sided reflections to soften this ambivalence.
