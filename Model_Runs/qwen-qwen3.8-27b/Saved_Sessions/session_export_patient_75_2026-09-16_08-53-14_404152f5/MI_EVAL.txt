# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently expresses respect, nonjudgment, and normalization of Linda’s fears and ambivalence. The warmth is sometimes formulaic, with repeated stock phrases and limited individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and generally well-timed, capturing Linda’s ambivalence and concerns. However, empathy stays largely cognitive and often shifts to practical steps rather than fully sitting with her emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, logical focus on the target behavior of contacting the PCP about asthma management. The direction is effective but somewhat linear and task-oriented rather than fully client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist genuinely co-creates the message and coping plan with Linda through iterative dialogue. However, the psychiatrist does much of the drafting, so collaboration is strong but not fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Linda’s autonomy is consistently respected through choices, pacing, and lack of pressure. The provision of specific scripts and plans slightly adds directionality and reduces the purely autonomous framing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes Linda’s motivations and self-efficacy through strategic questions. However, the later provision of concrete solutions shifts the session toward direction rather than continued evocation of her own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance by inviting input and offering choices. The session remains somewhat counselor-directed because the psychiatrist provides many specific solutions rather than fully co-creating them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is evoked through importance and confidence questions, and Linda generates several useful statements. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen her change talk to strengthen its impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled respectfully through normalization, factual information, and reframing. The approach is effective but relies more on informational and reframing strategies than on reflective techniques to actively resolve ambivalence.
