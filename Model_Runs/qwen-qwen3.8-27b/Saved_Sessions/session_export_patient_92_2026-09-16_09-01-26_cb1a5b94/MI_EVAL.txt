# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, nonjudgment, and positive regard while normalizing Marek's ambivalence. The warmth is effective but somewhat formulaic and could more deeply affirm his internal conflict.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Marek's ambivalence, social identity, and concern about being seen as controlling. The empathy is strong but remains somewhat surface-level in exploring deeper emotions such as fear of judgment or loss of self.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on weekend drinking, the low-pressure experiment, and concrete Monday outcomes. At times, the focus becomes overly procedural by emphasizing tactics before fully exploring motivation and ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as joint exploration and builds the plan from Marek's own ideas. Collaboration is limited by frequent provider-generated options that reduce the extent to which Marek leads the problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly avoids pressure, coercion, and moralizing while repeatedly affirming Marek's right to choose. Repeated invitations to try experiments can create subtle pressure despite the supportive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some effective questions to evoke Marek's own motivations and desired outcomes. However, the session often shifts to practical suggestions rather than deeply eliciting Marek's values, reasons for change, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally positions Marek as the expert on his own life and invites him to direct the process. The partnership is somewhat weakened when the psychiatrist introduces multiple options and scripts before allowing Marek to generate his own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes reasonable attempts to invite change talk and reinforces Marek's tentative openness. Development is limited because the psychiatrist moves into specific behavioral options instead of continuing to evoke Marek's own reasons, values, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Marek's sustain talk and reflects his perspective without reinforcing denial. The skill could be stronger with more double-sided reflections or gentle reframing to balance sustain talk with emerging change talk.
