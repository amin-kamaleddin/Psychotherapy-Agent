# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained warm, nonjudgmental regard and normalized Irina’s need for rest without moralizing or labeling her drinking. Acceptance remained strong even when Irina expressed defensiveness about being lectured.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Irina’s tension between needing a break and wanting to be present for her son, including her concern about judgment. Some reflections were slightly interpretive, and deeper exploration of underlying guilt, fear, or emotional burden would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on alcohol use as a coping behavior and practical barriers such as childcare and time. It would be stronger with slightly tighter organization around the specific change goal and explicit linkage of the childcare step to alcohol reduction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist framed the work as a joint exploration and allowed Irina’s ideas to shape the intervention. The client’s contributions substantially determined the direction, content, and emerging plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasized choice, avoided direct advice or demands, and supported Irina’s boundary around alcohol-focused help. This strongly fostered ownership and a sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions elicited Irina’s own goals, constraints, and hope for a lighter experience. Evocation would be more advanced if it more explicitly explored values, confidence, identity, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Irina as a key contributor and invited her to co-create the session. Some reflective summaries were slightly directive, which could have been more client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively affirmed and built on Irina’s willingness to try the one-drink plan and helped her identify a practical next step. It would be stronger with more systematic elicitation of desire, ability, reasons, need, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed without arguing or amplifying concerns about abstinence, cost, and feasibility. More active softening techniques, such as double-sided reflections or values-focused exploration, would further reduce resistance.
