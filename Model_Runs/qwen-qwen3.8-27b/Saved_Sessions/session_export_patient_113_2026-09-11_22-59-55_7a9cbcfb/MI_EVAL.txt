# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses nonjudgmental, validating language but remains somewhat task-oriented. Acceptance does not consistently reflect deep, individualized warmth for Rowan's ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures surface-level distress, such as feeling overwhelmed. However, deeper emotions like resentment, loss of control, or shame about the device are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session frequently drifts into stress management, grounding, and prioritization. The target behavior of charging and using the medical device is not consistently maintained as the strategic focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and invites input, showing some collaborative effort. However, the interaction is often shaped by the counselor's clinical agenda rather than equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are clear autonomy-supportive moments, including choices and validation of reluctance. Some directive statements, such as suggesting the therapist pick, weaken consistent autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some open questions but does not deeply elicit Rowan's own values, motivations, or reasons for change. Evocation remains superficial and often shifts into problem-solving.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks for input and offers options, demonstrating partial partnership. The session still feels therapist-guided, especially when structured clinical tasks dominate the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited effort to strengthen change talk related to charging or using the device. The therapist does not effectively draw out or amplify Rowan's own reasons, goals, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids confrontation but does not strategically soften Rowan's repeated sustain talk. Statements like 'I don't need to charge it' are not redirected toward change using MI techniques.
