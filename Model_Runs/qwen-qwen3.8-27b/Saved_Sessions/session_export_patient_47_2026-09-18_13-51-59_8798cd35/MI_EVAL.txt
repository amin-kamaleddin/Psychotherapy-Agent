### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise resides within Evelyn, repeatedly asking for her perspective and never positioning themselves as the one with the answer. ### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully cultivates change talk through well-timed questions and amplifying reflections, such as celebrating Evelyn's own phrase 'stop a bit earlier' and exploring hypothetical alternatives. Each intervention deepens change talk by bouncing it back with curiosity and validation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk with remarkable skill, validating the underlying value in statements like 'seven drinks doesn't seem like that much' while keeping the door open for change. The psychiatrist never argues or dismisses but reframes all-or-nothing thinking to allow Evelyn to find her own middle ground.
