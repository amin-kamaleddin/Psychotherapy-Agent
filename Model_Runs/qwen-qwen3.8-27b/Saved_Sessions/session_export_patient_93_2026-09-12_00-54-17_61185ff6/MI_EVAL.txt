# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and accepts reduction goal, but task-driven pacing and formulaic praise reduce warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good initial reflections, but often factual summaries and misses emotional themes (embarrassment, social life, resistance).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol reduction, fall risk, and medical safety with concrete measurable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Patient helps shape plan, yet clinician introduces many tasks at once and does not fully co-create.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respects patient's choices and low-key approach, but prescriptive tasks weaken autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some useful questions, but relies on advice/tools and does not deeply draw out intrinsic motivation/discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites choices and patient input, but rapid questions and multiple directives make it feel clinician-managed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change talk but does not deeply amplify importance/confidence or link to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk and accepts low goal, but bypasses ambivalence rather than strategically using it.
