# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provider is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating, using accepting language when the client minimizes smoking. However, the acceptance remains somewhat procedural rather than deeply individualized or emotionally warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client’s perspective, ambivalence, and concern about being lectured. The provider misses opportunities to deepen emotional attunement around fear of being weak-willed or judged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on mini-cigar use, throat/gum irritation, and a concrete small experiment. Each step logically advances toward a behaviorally specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client’s choices clearly shape the plan, and the provider uses consultative, non-authoritarian language. However, the session is still partly structured by the provider’s options and tactics rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provider consistently asks permission, offers choices, and avoids pressuring the client to quit. The client explicitly acknowledges feeling supported rather than pushed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The provider asks some evocative questions about importance, confidence, and obstacles. However, the client’s own reasons, values, and motivation for change are not consistently drawn out or deepened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provider invites client input and respects his decisions, such as the chosen experiment, wording, and follow-up preference. The partnership is strong but remains somewhat therapist-directed in its structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers meaningful change statements, such as willing to try waiting and using gum. The provider does not consistently follow up to expand, summarize, or strengthen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with acceptance and reflection rather than argument, including the client’s self-critical language. The provider could more actively transform resistance into change talk using advanced reflections.
