# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance and avoids labeling Anya as a 'drinker.' Acceptance could be strengthened by more explicit, individualized unconditional positive regard beyond her behaviors.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses accurate reflections, including double-sided reflections, to capture Anya’s ambivalence and concern about being mislabeled. Empathy could be deepened by exploring underlying feelings such as fear, confusion, or identity concerns more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on the referral, the meaning of the screening, Anya’s drinking pattern, and early warning signs. Direction could be stronger by more consistently tying each step to a specific behavioral target, such as preventing progression to riskier use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses inclusive language and invites Anya to choose which topics to explore, allowing her ideas to guide the conversation. Collaboration could be enhanced through more explicit co-creation, such as negotiating the agenda or priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor respects Anya’s choices, avoids pressure, and encourages her to generate her own warning signs and check-in plan. Autonomy support could be made more explicit by clearly affirming Anya’s right to make her own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively elicit Anya’s own concerns, motivations, and self-monitoring ideas rather than imposing external reasons for change. Evocation could be further developed by linking her statements to personal values, identity, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a non-authoritarian stance and allows Anya to influence the conversation’s focus and content. Partnership could reach a higher level by making the collaborative role more fully egalitarian, such as by co-negotiating goals or inviting refinement of reflections.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor successfully evokes change talk related to noticing early warning signs and developing a simple self-monitoring plan. Change talk could be strengthened by reinforcing Anya’s values, strengths, and confidence in using her plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** When Anya seeks reassurance that she is 'totally fine,' the counselor gently redirects toward balanced exploration rather than reinforcing minimization. Softening sustain talk could be improved with more strategic double-sided reflections or affirmations that honor both sides of her ambivalence.
