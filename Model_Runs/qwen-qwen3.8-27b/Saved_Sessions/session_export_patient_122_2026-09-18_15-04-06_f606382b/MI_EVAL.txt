# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm unconditional positive regard throughout; never labels, judges, or minimizes. Reframes self-critical language into capability (e.g., 'That's not a character flaw, that's a really common response').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections move beyond surface content to capture meaning and emotional truth (e.g., identifying that the walk is about feeling her day had meaning, not just exercise). Progressively deepens understanding from displacement to the guilt within the spiral.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains remarkably clear focus on the target behavior with progressive momentum from problem identification through concrete planning. Each question builds on the previous answer with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language is consistently joint ('What if we held both of those thoughts?'); the session is shaped substantially by Sofia's contributions. The power dynamic is clearly equal; Sofia is an active co-creator, not a passive recipient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never says 'you should' or 'you need to'; all recommendations are framed as Sofia's own discoveries. The session ends with Sofia identifying her own rule and pattern, all elicited rather than imposed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Sofia rather than providing external reasons for change. The question about past evidence of ability ('Have you noticed a time when a small step carried you further?') is a masterful evocation move that draws out her own proof.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions Sofia as the expert on her own experience through open questions and explicit attribution of insight to her. The psychiatrist's role is clearly that of a curious facilitator, never prescribing or telling.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits rich change talk including desire, ability, and commitment language through strategic questioning. The question 'What makes you think that's true?' powerfully evokes Sofia's own evidence of ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively uses double-sided reflections to honor sustain talk without reinforcing it, and gently pivots with hypothetical language. A perfect 5 would require more explicit strategic moves to help Sofia fully resolve the 'what's the point' and all-or-nothing sustain talk.
