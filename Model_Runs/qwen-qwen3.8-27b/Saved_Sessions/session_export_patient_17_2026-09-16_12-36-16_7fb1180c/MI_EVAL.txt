# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with consistent positive regard. The warmth is somewhat task-focused and rarely affirms Anisa's individual worth, resilience, or long-standing struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses good reflective listening to capture ambivalence, fear of failure, and the need for a real win. The reflections do not fully sit with the deeper shame, humiliation, or fear of being judged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains excellent focus on smoking cessation, specifically delaying the first cigarette of the day. It builds clear momentum toward a concrete behavioral plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is built through Anisa's repeated choices and preferences, showing real collaboration. The psychiatrist still controls much of the agenda and offers structured options before fully eliciting the client's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options, respects client refusals, and reduces pressure around failure. The approach is less fully autonomy-supportive because the plan is still quite prescriptive and scripted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used, but the session moves quickly into options, scripts, and problem-solving. It does not consistently draw out Anisa's values, confidence, or internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites client input and allows Anisa to shape the final plan. The interaction can still feel somewhat expert-driven because several structured options and a morning script are introduced by the psychiatrist.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anisa expresses some meaningful change talk about wanting a real win and being tired of only surviving the morning. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or amplify this language before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles concerns about failure and half-measures by validating them and reframing slips as information rather than failure. Some sustain talk is addressed with next steps rather than with fuller MI-style exploration.
