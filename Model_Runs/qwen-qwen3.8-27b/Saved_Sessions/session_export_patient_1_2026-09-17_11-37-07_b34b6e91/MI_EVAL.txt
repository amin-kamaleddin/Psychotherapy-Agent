# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates respect and non-judgment, validating Ahmed's minimization without pushing back. However, the warmth remains somewhat clinical and procedural, lacking moments of genuine celebration or deeper personal connection that would signal unconditional positive regard at the highest level.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor demonstrates strong reflective listening with accurate, well-timed reflections that capture both content and feeling. Some reflections could go deeper emotionally, such as exploring the weight of Ahmed's ambivalence or the subtle self-efficacy in his statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation maintains a clear, logical progression from understanding Ahmed's perspective through defining thresholds, providing information, and building a monitoring plan. The brief medical information-giving moment is the most directive point, slightly shifting the balance away from fully client-led direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a genuine collaboration with the counselor inviting Ahmed to set the agenda, define terms, and build on his own ideas. The structure is determined by what Ahmed says he needs rather than what the counselor thinks he should hear.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently and explicitly supports Ahmed's autonomy, never pressuring or implying he should quit. All decisions are framed as Ahmed's to make, and even medical information is offered with the caveat that he can decide what it means for him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently elicits motivation from within Ahmed through open-ended questions rather than providing it. Ahmed spontaneously generates change talk ('maybe I could just quit') that emerges from his own reflection, facilitated by evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently operates from a partnership stance, treating Ahmed as the expert on his own life and body. Decision-making power is explicitly located with the client through language like 'so you can make that call yourself.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** This is the strongest element, with the counselor exceptionally skilled at noticing, reflecting back, and amplifying change talk. The session ends with Ahmed spontaneously generating change talk after being met with curiosity rather than pressure.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles sustain talk well by acknowledging logic and pivoting without arguing or reinforcing minimization. However, some sustain talk statements pass without a strategic double-sided reflection or subtle reframe that could have been more impactful.
