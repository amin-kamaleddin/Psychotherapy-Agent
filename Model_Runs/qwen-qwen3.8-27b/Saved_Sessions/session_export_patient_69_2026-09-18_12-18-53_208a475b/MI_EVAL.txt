# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with clear non-judgmental validation throughout. The affirmations are warm but occasionally read as slightly formulaic, preventing a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate perspective-taking, particularly in capturing the emotional undertones of the client's metaphors. Could have gone deeper into the underlying loneliness or pain beneath the monotony.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session flows respectfully with the client's pace but lacks a clear strategic arc or consolidation toward a specific focus. The psychiatrist could have used summaries to build momentum and collaboratively identify even a small next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a collaborative interaction with inclusive language and co-construction of meaning from the client's metaphors. However, the therapist does most of the talking and guiding, with the client remaining largely passive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout the session — repeatedly returns decision-making power to the client and explicitly removes any sense of obligation. Every invitation is framed as optional and non-urgent, with no pressure implied.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions and avoids external advice, but does not deeply pursue the change talk that emerges (e.g., 'quiet,' 'without a reason'). Limited evocation of ability, hope, or values prevents a higher score.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a non-hierarchical stance with language that positions the client as the expert on their own experience. Could be strengthened by more explicitly inviting the client to shape the session's direction rather than the therapist guiding all questions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerges naturally but is not consistently reinforced, amplified, or strategically built upon. The psychiatrist lets promising moments like 'quiet' and 'without a reason' pass without deeper exploration or eliciting ability/hope statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by reflecting difficulty without arguing or reinforcing resignation. Could have more explicitly acknowledged the validity of the sustain talk while still gently leaving the door open.
