# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental with explicit disavowal of judgment and genuine validation of the patient's perspective. The affirmation near the end borders on evaluative rather than purely accepting, keeping this just below the highest level.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures the patient's emotional experience and specific metaphors. Could have explored the underlying fear behind uncertainty more deeply, and the final reflection about self-awareness felt slightly presumptive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical arc from safety to exploration to a concrete step. The middle section slightly extends the same idea and could benefit from tighter strategic pacing toward commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely two-way dialogue where the psychiatrist builds on the patient's language and metaphors. The patient is positioned as co-author of his own plan, with the final question inviting him to design the night on his terms.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout — never pressures, moralizes, or implies the patient 'should' change. Consistently centers the patient's right to choose and respects his readiness level.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest aspect of the session; all change talk emerges from within the patient rather than being supplied by the psychiatrist. Open questions naturally draw out his own values, motivations, and meaning-making.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI partnership with a clearly egalitarian power dynamic. The psychiatrist follows the patient's lead, allows his responses to shape direction, and consistently cedes control.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits genuine change talk including a concrete plan and articulation of personal motivation. The evocation is strong but could more consistently reinforce and amplify the change talk as it emerges.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging validity without contradicting or reinforcing. Lacks explicit double-sided reflections or strategic reframing that would elevate this to the highest level.
