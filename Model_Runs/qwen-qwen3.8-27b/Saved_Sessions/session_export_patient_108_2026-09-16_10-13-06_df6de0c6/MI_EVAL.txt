# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms Oliver’s efforts, but the acceptance feels somewhat task-focused rather than deeply person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequately reflects Oliver’s conflict and nervousness, but does not deeply explore guilt, self-criticism, or the emotional weight of setting the boundary.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session remains tightly focused on the target behavior with little drift, consistently aligning the conversation with the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses role-play and invites input, but the psychiatrist frequently supplies wording and techniques, making the interaction feel more expert-driven than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices and uses permissive language, but the frequent provision of specific scripts and instructions limits Oliver’s ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some assertive statements and readiness, but often hands solutions and practical plans rather than deeply evoking Oliver’s own reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Seeks preferences and follows Oliver’s lead, but the counselor-supplied scripts and structure reduce the degree of true shared agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Draws out clear assertive language, especially in role-play, but does not consistently develop DARN statements or amplify Oliver’s own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing or amplifying sustain talk, but does not use advanced MI techniques to strategically soften ambivalence or preserve autonomy.
