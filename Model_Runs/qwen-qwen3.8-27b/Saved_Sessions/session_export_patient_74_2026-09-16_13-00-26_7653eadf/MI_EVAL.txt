# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental while normalizing Linda’s ambivalence. Positive regard is present but remains somewhat procedural rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Linda’s conflict about smoking and modeling. It becomes repetitive and misses deeper feelings such as shame and guilt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior of after-dinner smoking and visibility to children. Focus narrows into repetitive logistical problem-solving rather than broader motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates a practical plan and follows Linda’s choices around the garage and one-night experiment. Collaboration is strong but is steered within a fairly narrow problem-solving frame.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda’s freedom to choose is acknowledged by asking permission and offering options. Autonomy support is mixed with directive planning that can feel pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions help draw out Linda’s confidence and possible next steps. Evocation is useful but limited by the frequent supply of practical options rather than deeper motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and asks which option feels most doable. The interaction is uneven because he frequently assumes direction and supplies many suggestions quickly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks effective change-oriented questions and elicits some change talk. Change talk is not consistently deepened or expanded before shifting to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about smoking as calming is acknowledged but not effectively redirected or balanced. Much of the session focuses on making smoking easier to continue without being seen.
