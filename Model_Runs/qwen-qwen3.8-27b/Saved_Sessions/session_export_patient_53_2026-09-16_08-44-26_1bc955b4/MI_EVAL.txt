# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses nonjudgmental, validating language without pressure or blame. The main limitation is that validation can sound formulaic and may miss more individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Isabella's surface ambivalence and discomfort. The reflections remain mostly paraphrasing and do not consistently reach deeper emotions such as shame, self-doubt, or isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on low-pressure community engagement and a small concrete step. Direction becomes procedural too quickly before motivation, values, and ambivalence are fully explored.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive, optional language and offers multiple choice points. Collaboration is limited because the clinician still largely structures the agenda and proposes specific options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Isabella's control, choice, and freedom to not commit are strongly supported. Frequent specific suggestions can subtly shift the conversation toward externally supplied structure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some early evocation of what might feel meaningful or manageable. The session then moves into planning and suggestions rather than consistently drawing out Isabella's internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally collaborative stance and avoids a prescriptive expert approach. The main weakness is that the clinician controls more of the structure than the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked, such as trying 20 minutes or saving one place. It is not consistently developed or deepened into desire, need, ability, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or shaming Isabella's sustain talk. However, sustain talk is not consistently reframed or pivoted toward change.
