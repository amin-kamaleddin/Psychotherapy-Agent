# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating of Amina’s parenting, financial, and family concerns. The stance is strong but somewhat task-focused, with fewer moments of deeper emotional validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Amina’s ambivalence, practical needs, and preferred wording, including the need for calm before the interview. Empathy could be deepened by attending more to underlying emotions such as frustration, exhaustion, or fear of family awkwardness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on making job interviews realistic by addressing childcare reliability. Each step moves the conversation toward a concrete, doable next action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds collaboratively on Amina’s language, options, and preferences. The score is not a 5 because the psychiatrist occasionally proposes scripts or structured choices rather than consistently letting the client generate them first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Amina’s sense of choice without pressure or implied obligation. Amina’s own boundaries, preferences, and limits are respected and reinforced throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicit Amina’s own solutions, values, and motivation for change. Evocation could be strengthened by drawing out broader reasons for change, confidence, identity, or deeper DARN-C language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Amina as the expert on her own life, solutions, and preferred wording. The client’s ideas and choices clearly shape the session rather than the counselor’s advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes and reinforces Amina’s action-focused change language, such as deciding to ask her cousin and follow through. Development of broader DARN-C change talk could be more explicit.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk and barrier statements are handled without arguing, minimizing, or blaming Amina. The response could be stronger by more consistently using double-sided reflections that acknowledge barriers while strengthening agency.
