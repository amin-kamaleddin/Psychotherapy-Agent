# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a warm, nonjudgmental tone with unconditional positive regard throughout. Affirmations such as "You've been carrying that pressure alone while still showing up at work" validated the client's efforts without imposing judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were nuanced, accurate, and highly attuned to both cognitive and emotional experiences. The counselor effectively captured complex dynamics, such as the tension between maintaining composure at work and managing financial stress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, logical focus on reducing gambling and its related lifestyle factors. The progression from exploration to concrete planning was smooth, consistent, and goal-aligned.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was genuinely co-constructed, with the client's ideas consistently driving the session's content. The counselor acted as a facilitative partner rather than an expert directing the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently reinforced the client's ownership of the change process and freedom to choose. Questions were framed hypothetically and respectfully, avoiding directives or subtle pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation for change was consistently drawn from within the client rather than provided externally. The counselor skillfully amplified the client's own values, goals, and reasoning to build momentum for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly acknowledged the client's expertise over their own life and maintained an equitable power dynamic. The client's contributions substantially shaped the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was consistently evoked, amplified, and reinforced throughout the session. The counselor used effective questions and reflections to connect the client's desired future with concrete steps for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally handled thoughtfully, with careful validation of underlying needs and gentle redirection. A minor opportunity was missed by not directly reframing the client's self-deprecating use of the word "lazy."
