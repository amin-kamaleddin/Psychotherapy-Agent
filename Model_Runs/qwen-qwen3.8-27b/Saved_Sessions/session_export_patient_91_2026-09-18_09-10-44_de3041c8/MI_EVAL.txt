# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with warm affirming statements, normalizing Marcus's overwhelm and validating his fear of feeling stupid without any hint of judgment or impatience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and complex reflective listening that goes beyond paraphrasing, capturing underlying patterns like the all-or-nothing self-evaluation and the emotional shift toward grounded pride.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on blood sugar self-monitoring with logical progression from confusion to plan, though it follows Marcus's lead quite freely and could benefit from more explicit consolidation or follow-up commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as true collaboration between equals, with Marcus's contributions directly shaping direction and a genuine 'solving it together' dynamic rather than a clinician delivering information.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options rather than directives, invites Marcus to generate his own solutions, and explicitly reinforces ownership ('you made it yourself') without ever imposing standards or using directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within Marcus through evocative questions and powerful summaries of his own growth, rather than the therapist providing external reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Marcus as the expert in his own life, never assumes the authority role, and reinforces that Marcus's needs—not the clinician's protocol—are the guiding framework.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies Marcus's own change talk throughout, explicitly attributing the plan to him and highlighting the shift from 'I don't know how' to 'I can do one number.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Self-doubt is handled well through normalization and compassionate reframing, though sustain talk is relatively limited in this preparation-stage session with minimal deep ambivalence requiring more sophisticated techniques.
