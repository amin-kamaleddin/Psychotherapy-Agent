# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating throughout. Could be elevated by more explicitly normalizing ambivalence or acknowledging Eric's vulnerability in a warmer, more personal way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflective listening, including the brilliant identification of the word 'yet' as a 'small door.' Reflections consistently capture both content and underlying emotion with increasing depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from general ambivalence to the tickle as a concrete entry point to a manageable next step. Could benefit from a more explicit collaborative summary of where the session landed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine partnership throughout — the clinician builds on Eric's metaphors (scaffold analogy), uses inclusive language, and treats him as an equal partner. Eric's contributions substantially shape the direction and outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent support for Eric's autonomy, including 'on your own terms' and reinforcing his self-trust. The appointment is framed as Eric's own practical decision, never as a clinician prescription.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled use of open-ended questions that draw out Eric's own motivations rather than providing information. The session moves from pre-contemplation to early contemplation largely through Eric's own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently framed as collaborative exploration with Eric positioned as the expert on his own life. Could be elevated by more explicit co-creation of next steps or acknowledgment of Eric's decision-making wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Eric moves from 'I don't see a problem' to committing to an appointment through skilled evocation and amplification of his own change language. Could be stronger with more explicit elicitation of values or confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated without argument, and the 'yet' observation masterfully acknowledges resistance while gently redirecting. Could include more explicit double-sided reflections in the early phase when sustain talk is most dominant.
