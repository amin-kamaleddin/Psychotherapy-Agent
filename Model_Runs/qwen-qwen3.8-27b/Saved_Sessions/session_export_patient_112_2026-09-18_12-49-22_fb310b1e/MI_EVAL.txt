# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warmth, respect, and non-judgment with affirmations embedded throughout reflections. However, the acceptance is woven into reflections rather than standing out as explicit, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic understanding with genuine perspective-taking that tracks not just content but meaning. The clinician accurately follows Rosa's emotional shifts with precision and meets her exactly where she is without pushing toward solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session follows a clear, logical trajectory from general ambivalence toward specific, actionable insight with a well-structured progression. However, the session hasn't yet firmly established a specific behavioral target as the working goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration throughout, with the session clearly shaped by Rosa's contributions rather than the psychiatrist's agenda. The 'pause button' reframe is a collaborative synthesis built on Rosa's own language, creating something new together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional and consistent autonomy support with no pressure or directive language at any point. The entire session is structured so Rosa makes all decisions, with the psychiatrist merely creating conditions for her to find her own answers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The standout skill of the session — every major insight was evoked from Rosa rather than provided by the psychiatrist. Questions are precisely calibrated to draw out Rosa's own thinking, and the permission reframe is pure evocation using her own language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently operates from a stance of genuine partnership, never assuming answers or providing expertise about quitting methods. Rosa's contributions substantially shape the session's direction, and the power dynamic is genuinely equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully creates conditions for change talk to emerge, and Rosa moves from 'I don't know' to articulating specific ideas. However, the psychiatrist could more explicitly amplify and reflect back Rosa's change talk statements to build additional momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully with validation rather than dismissal, and identity-based sustain talk is met with understanding. However, the time spent exploring the functions of smoking keeps the status quo somewhat centered rather than strategically reducing its weight.
