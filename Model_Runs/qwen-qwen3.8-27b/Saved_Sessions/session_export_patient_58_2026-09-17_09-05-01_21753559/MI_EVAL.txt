# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Jamal’s experience rather than criticizing his tardiness. Acceptance is somewhat general and returns to a pressure/risk frame instead of consistently affirming his worth and agency.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflections that capture Jamal’s annoyance, conflict, and ambivalence. A deeper empathic response is missed when Jamal says he feels like he is always messing up, suggesting shame or self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session begins by keeping focus on the target behavior of getting to school on time. Over time, the focus narrows toward managing his mother’s reaction and avoiding kitchen conflict rather than consistently tying next steps back to punctuality.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Jamal’s ideas and avoids a didactic, expert-driven style. The collaboration is largely implicit and guided by the clinician’s sequence rather than explicitly co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is supported through open questions, “if you’re willing,” and letting Jamal generate the plan. It would be stronger with more explicit affirmations that the choice and timing remain fully his.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes practical changes and scripts, but not deeper values, intrinsic reasons, or confidence for change. More evocation of what punctuality means to Jamal would strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Jamal as the expert on his own mornings and invites him to lead ideas. The agenda remains intentionally structured by the clinician rather than fully negotiated as an equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked, such as wanting to stop being late and creating a win-win. The psychiatrist does not systematically deepen desire, ability, reasons, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk about stopping the nagging is acknowledged without argument. The session does not consistently reframe sustain talk or pivot it toward Jamal’s own goals for school punctuality.
