# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful stance and normalizes Mateo’s ambivalence and history. The interaction is warm and supportive, though it could include slightly deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective statements show good understanding of Mateo’s conflicting values and concerns. Some emotional themes, such as fear of freezing or shame, were not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a concrete, change-oriented goal: attending the job center on Tuesday at 10. Repetition of similar questions and options gives the flow a slightly mechanical quality.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mateo meaningfully contributes to the plan, and the psychiatrist builds on his choices. The collaboration could be stronger by reducing how often the psychiatrist supplies structured option sets.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, respects Mateo’s decisions, and avoids pushing him to practice a refusal line. A few phrases sound mildly directive rather than fully invitation-based.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling, future-focused questions, and confidence prompts help Mateo identify his own reasons for change. The psychiatrist could evoke more of Mateo’s own ideas before offering practical suggestions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally positions themselves as a partner and invites Mateo to choose next steps. The session feels somewhat menu-driven, which may limit spontaneous co-construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mateo produces meaningful change talk about his daughter, job center, and confidence increase. The psychiatrist affirms this talk but could more consistently amplify and reflect it back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged but not strategically softened or reframed. More double-sided reflections and deeper ambivalence work would reduce the pull of the old crew and fears of weakness.
