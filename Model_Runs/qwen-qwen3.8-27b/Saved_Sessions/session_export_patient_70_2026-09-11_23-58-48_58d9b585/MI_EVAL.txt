# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes embarrassment about forms. The relational warmth remains somewhat task-focused rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects surface-level frustration and practical concerns well. Deeper ambivalence, fear, and emotional conflict are not consistently captured.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear, practical re-entry task: forms and interview preparation. It does not consistently connect the job goal to broader recidivism-reduction targets.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is useful back-and-forth, and Lars influences practical choices. The power balance remains uneven because the counselor frequently supplies the content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and accepts Lars’s desire to do parts himself. Directive scripts and “copy this” language limit Lars’s ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early open questions are present, but the session quickly becomes advice-giving and problem-solving. Lars’s own motivations, values, and confidence are not consistently drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lars contributes meaningfully to the session, but the interaction remains expert-driven. The counselor often provides exact wording rather than inviting Lars to generate his own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Lars offers strong change talk, but the counselor rarely reflects, amplifies, or deepens it. Motivational statements are often bypassed in favor of practical scripts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reframes concerns about work history in a professional, honest, forward-looking way. The response is helpful but does not fully explore Lars’s ambivalence in an MI manner.
