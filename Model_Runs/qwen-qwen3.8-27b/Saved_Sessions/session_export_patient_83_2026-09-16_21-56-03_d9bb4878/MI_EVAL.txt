# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, nonjudgmental language and normalizes Mai’s uncertainty. It is not a 5 because the warmth is somewhat procedural and a repeated response briefly limits individualized attunement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections, including double-sided reflections of Mai’s ambivalence. It falls short of 5 due to repetition and missed opportunities to explore deeper frustration, anxiety, or fear of indefinite medication use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear through-line toward understanding the statin and creating a concrete plan. It is not a 5 because the opening is repetitive and the direct question about the medication’s rationale is only partially addressed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates the plan with Mai, using her preferred actions and language. It falls short of 5 because frequent multiple-choice prompts make the collaboration slightly counselor-guided rather than fully open-ended.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Mai’s freedom to choose throughout the session. Statements like “It keeps you in charge” and offering options reinforce ownership without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Mai articulate her own standards, plan, and next steps rather than lecturing. It is not a 5 because the evocation is mainly practical and does not fully explore values, confidence, or future self-image.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Mai’s input and positions her as the expert on her own routine. It is not a 5 because the conversation remains structured with prompts and predetermined options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mai generates substantial change talk, including checking the portal, calling the doctor, and setting a recheck plan. The psychiatrist could deepen this by more explicitly amplifying Mai’s ability, values, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Mai’s concerns and reframes skepticism as carefulness. It falls short of 5 because sustain talk is mainly softened through neutral validation and redirection rather than more advanced double-sided reflections.
