# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and validating, with skillful pushback on Hana's 'petty' self-label. Falls short of a 5 due to a lack of explicit unconditional positive regard statements affirming Hana's worth as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that captures emotional core and nuance well beyond surface content. Reflections accurately identify underlying processes, such as the social fear vs. competence distinction and the 'familiarity of the loop' as a barrier.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (sitting for the exam) with a logical therapeutic arc. The ending could have more explicitly connected the 'naming the thought' practice back to the specific exam context.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with genuine two-way dialogue and frequent checking in with Hana. Lacks a moment of explicit joint decision-making or co-created next steps to reach the highest level.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout, using exploratory language and never prescribing or directing. Frames all change as Hana's choice, with key statements like 'You don't have to make the fear go quiet' and 'what would make it believable to me?'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI evocation where every insight and solution emerges from Hana herself through well-timed, open questions. The psychiatrist never tells Hana what to think, feel, or do — all conclusions are Hana's own.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions Hana as the expert on her own experience and invites her input throughout. Could be stronger with an explicit collaborative closing that co-creates next steps rather than ending naturally.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and reinforces change talk through strategic questions, successfully surfacing Hana's ability, desire, and plan. Does not consolidate momentum into explicit commitment language or a confidence rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively handles sustain talk with double-sided reflections that acknowledge validity while naming function, without arguing or dismissing. Could further elevate by using the 'even if' technique to explicitly undermine the most persistent sustain talk.
