# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental stance with validation of the client's ambivalence. Could deepen by more explicitly affirming the client as a whole person rather than the issue.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and tracking of mixed feelings and practical concerns. Reflections are more content-focused than emotional/meaning-focused.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior. Each prompt narrows the target into a practical, manageable step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist collaborates by inviting the client to generate his own check-in and next steps. The session remains somewhat guided by the therapist's structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong emphasis on choice, ownership, and freedom from pressure. The client's autonomy is consistently central to the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist elicits some practical change ideas. Internal reasons, values, and motivation for change are not deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is positioned as the expert on his own experience. More explicit shared agenda-setting would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited around a low-pressure check-in. The therapist could more actively amplify the client's motivation and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist acknowledges the client's reasons for maintaining coffee without arguing. The sustain talk could be more consistently linked to a small change experiment.
