### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful, supportive, and nonjudgmental, using normalizing language. Acceptance is somewhat formulaic and could be warmer and more individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses clear reflective listening to capture Kenji’s routine, ambivalence, and discomfort. Empathy remains mostly cognitive rather than emotionally nuanced.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on reducing nightly alcohol use and moving toward a concrete test behavior. The direction is strong, realistic, and well organized.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kenji’s ideas meaningfully shape the plan, including the alarm, sticky note, walk, and Thursday focus. Collaboration is limited by the therapist offering many structured options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly invites choice and avoids explicit pressure, supporting Kenji’s self-evaluation. Autonomy is slightly undermined by rapid, directive planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions help Kenji express his own observations and concerns. Evocation is reduced when the therapist quickly supplies solutions before fully developing internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The opening language positions Kenji as the expert and invites his input. The session becomes more therapist-led when structured advice and step-by-step options are introduced.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kenji expresses some desire, reasons, and commitment to change. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen this change talk before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and normalized rather than challenged. The psychiatrist could use more MI-consistent reflections to reframe ambivalence and support change.
