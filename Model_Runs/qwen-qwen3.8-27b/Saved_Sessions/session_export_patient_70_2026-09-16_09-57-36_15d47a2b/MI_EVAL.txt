# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides consistent, nonjudgmental warmth and unconditional positive regard throughout. Slightly task-focused in emotional validation, missing a deeper reflection of Lars's underlying shame and fear.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers accurate but primarily factual/behavioral reflections rather than complex affective ones. Key emotional material (shame, fear of relapse, feeling 'back inside') is acknowledged but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally clear and sustained focus on concrete, actionable target behavior (one form, 15 minutes, Thursday 8pm). Every turn advances toward the specific plan without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine joint problem-solving with the counselor adapting to Lars's preferences and incorporating his own ideas. Could be stronger by inviting Lars to generate more of the plan rather than presenting structured options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Powerful and consistent emphasis on Lars's freedom to choose, sized and timed entirely by his own criteria. The counselor immediately adapts when Lars expresses boundary discomfort, with no pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits useful self-efficacy and confidence statements but remains at a practical, external-structure level. Misses opportunities to evoke deeper intrinsic motivation tied to Lars's values, relationship with Donna, or garage identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is clearly shaped by Lars's choices and preferences, and the counselor repeatedly invites his input. The psychiatrist still occasionally positions as practical coach offering two-choice options rather than fully deferring to Lars as expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several effective questions elicit concrete plans and useful affirmations are made. The counselor does not consistently deepen or amplify Lars's change talk into richer values or identity-based commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles all sustain talk without arguing or dismissing, effectively softening barriers into manageable client-owned steps. Could be further improved with more explicit double-sided reflections holding both sides of Lars's ambivalence.
