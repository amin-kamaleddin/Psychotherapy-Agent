# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and unconditional positive regard throughout, with multiple specific and individualized affirmations. The psychiatrist never labels, judges, or positions Anya as a problem.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening with strong examples such as capturing the relief of the session and using verbatim reflections. Empathic work is solid but stays relatively surface-level without probing deeper into underlying vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical structure with appropriate focus on the session's purpose. Could have included a slightly more purposeful arc, such as a small forward-looking reflection or client-initiated check-in plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session consistently feels like a conversation between two equals, with collaborative language, informal tone, and mutual exploration. Ends with a natural, non-clinical closing that reinforces the partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful reinforcement of Anya's freedom to choose and ownership of her experience, with no pressure or implied obligations. Even the closing offer is presented as an open option, not an expectation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A few evocative questions are offered as gentle options but not pursued when Anya doesn't immediately engage. Misses opportunities to elicit client-driven language about values, aspirations, or self-concept.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Anya as the decision-maker and authority on her own well-being, never assuming the expert role or lecturing. Exemplary throughout the session from opening to closing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Limited opportunity given the context, but several windows to evoke forward-looking language were not fully explored. Affirmations of strength are present but lack depth and frequency in eliciting client-generated change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The one instance of mild sustain talk is handled effectively by validating Anya's boundary while leaving the door open. Limited material in the session prevents a higher score due to insufficient opportunities to assess consistency.
