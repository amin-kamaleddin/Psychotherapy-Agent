# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, warmth, and nonjudgment, normalizing Sofia’s exhaustion and body-image distress. Acceptance is solid but could be deeper and less plan-focused.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence, fatigue, and fear of failure. Empathy is good but could explore deeper self-criticism and emotional meaning behind Sofia’s words.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on building a realistic movement routine and improving energy. Momentum is strong, with efficient movement from concern to plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Sofia’s input and co-constructs the plan. The use of multiple structured suggestions makes the interaction feel somewhat therapist-paced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports choice through flexible options, permission to skip, and self-selected goals. The plan is framed as optional, doable, and kind rather than prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and open prompts elicit Sofia’s own motivations, cues, and confidence-building ideas. The psychiatrist sometimes offers structured strategies before fully evoking Sofia’s own solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia clearly shapes the plan, and the psychiatrist treats her as the expert in her own life. However, the agenda is still led by the psychiatrist’s questions and technique menus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist creates space for concrete, client-owned change statements and reinforces them positively. It could more consistently amplify change talk and connect it to values, identity, or self-trust.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Concerns about exhaustion, heat, slipping, and failure are accepted and reframed without argument. The psychiatrist moves somewhat quickly toward planning and could use deeper double-sided reflections.
