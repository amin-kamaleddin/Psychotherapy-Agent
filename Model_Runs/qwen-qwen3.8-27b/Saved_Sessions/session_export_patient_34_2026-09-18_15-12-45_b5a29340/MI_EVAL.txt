# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, never labeling Dylan as irresponsible or reckless. However, the acceptance is somewhat formulaic, relying on a repeated validation + 'that makes sense' pattern without deeper individualization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures Dylan's emotional experience and notices subtle language discrepancies (e.g., 'one bad night' vs. 'a good time'). Occasionally stays at the cognitive level rather than accessing the emotional undercurrent, such as the vulnerability behind 'I'm not an idiot.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on the target behavior with logical progression from understanding perspective to evoking a concrete change strategy. Lacks a brief anchoring statement or summary that would make the target behavior explicitly clear to the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session feels like a genuine dialogue rather than an interrogation, with the psychiatrist building on Dylan's responses and validating his contributions. Somewhat one-directional in that the psychiatrist consistently asks while Dylan answers, with limited explicit co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respects Dylan's right to his own choices through non-directive language and explicit statements like 'I'm not going to push on that.' Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming that Dylan has the final say about his behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Dylan's own motivations and change reasons through well-timed open questions rather than lecturing or imposing external reasons. Could go deeper in evoking Dylan's values and long-term goals to create greater discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong partnership stance with open questions, explicit acknowledgment of Dylan's expertise in his own experience, and a clear non-expert role. Could be elevated by more explicitly co-creating the agenda or sharing the psychiatrist's own perspective in a balanced way.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully and naturally evokes change talk, leading Dylan to articulate his own concrete change plan about 'paying attention to the vibe.' Could push further on confidence and ability statements or explore the full range of DARN-CAT categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequately avoids amplifying sustain talk but primarily validates Dylan's frustration about the process rather than actively softening or reframing the repeated 'it's not my fault' statements. A double-sided reflection acknowledging both the frustration and the 'I was there' admission would have been more effective.
