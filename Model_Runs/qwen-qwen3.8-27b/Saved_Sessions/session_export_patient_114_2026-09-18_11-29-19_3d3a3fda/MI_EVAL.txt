# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating throughout; normalizes ambivalence and explicitly protects Ryan from feeling pathologized. Falls short of a 5 by occasionally feeling more like skilled technique than genuine relational warmth, and lacking explicit affirmations of Ryan's inherent worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures emotional undertones and names subtle shifts in Ryan's language. Occasionally stays at the cognitive level rather than fully accessing deeper emotional vulnerability, and can read as slightly mechanical in its precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus on the target behavior throughout with a clear, logical arc from incident to concrete first step. No topic drift; pacing is well-managed and the psychiatrist consistently brings the conversation back to specifics when Ryan generalizes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as joint exploration, building on Ryan's ideas rather than imposing their own. A 5 would require more explicit co-construction, such as the psychiatrist sharing their own uncertainty in a way that models the collaborative process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Ryan's agency through conditional language and explicit statements that this isn't a path he's on. Could be strengthened by more explicit affirmations of Ryan's right to choose as he sees fit and clearer separation of identity from behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Ryan through open, curious questions; never lectures or provides external reasons for change. A 5 would require more systematic expansion on each piece of change talk before moving forward and explicit elicitation of confidence/ability language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-hierarchical stance with language that positions the psychiatrist as a fellow explorer rather than an authority. Could be strengthened by more explicit power-sharing language and a slightly more co-equal position.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently evokes and amplifies Ryan's change talk, with strong reinforcement of his final commitment statement. A 5 would require explicit elicitation of confidence/ability statements and more systematic expansion on each piece of change talk before moving forward.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by reflecting underlying fears and validating feelings rather than arguing or dismissing. A 5 would require more explicit double-sided reflections that hold both sides simultaneously and more creative reframes that turn sustain talk into a springboard for change.
