# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect and nonjudgment with a warm, supportive tone. Some reflections are slightly assumptive, which reduces unconditional acceptance in a few moments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening and accurately captures ambivalence. Some reflections are interpretive rather than tightly client-led, occasionally leading the emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on increasing physical activity. The conversation moves efficiently from ambivalence to a concrete, realistic action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is strongly collaborative and avoids lecturing or directive pressure. The client's own plan, such as shoes by the couch, shows genuine joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently supports choice and respects the client's decision-making. A few phrases gently position meaning for the client, making the framing slightly less autonomy-centered.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out motivation from within the client. Questions help the client articulate his own reasons, values, and commitment to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently treated as the expert on his own life. The plan is shaped by the client's ideas rather than imposed by the psychiatrist.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open, forward-looking questions consistently elicit strong change talk. The psychiatrist reinforces the client's statements of willingness and self-ownership.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without amplifying or challenging it directly. Responses soften the negative pull and keep the conversation moving toward change.
