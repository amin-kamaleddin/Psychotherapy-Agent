# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with zero judgment or labeling throughout the session. The psychiatrist explicitly pushes back on David's self-critical language and validates skepticism as realistic rather than difficult.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, layered reflections that capture the emotional and experiential core of David's experience (e.g., 'the cigarette gives you permission to release'). Reflections go well beyond surface-level paraphrasing and build progressively on each other.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from motivation to trigger identification to medication to social triggers, staying on topic throughout. Could benefit from more explicit signposting and summarization to maintain momentum while preserving collaboration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative throughout; David's own metaphors and ideas ('nine down to a four,' 'background hum,' 'normal person at dinner') substantially shape the session direction. The psychiatrist consistently treats David's concerns as equal contributions to the plan rather than obstacles.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently places all decisions in David's hands with explicit autonomy-supporting language. The psychiatrist never pressures, never imposes an agenda, and every significant question is structured around David's own criteria and readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary evocation; every key moment draws out David's own thinking, language, and motivation rather than providing external reasons for change. David's powerful metaphors are his own words, elicited through well-timed open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent collaborative framing with explicit partnership language and shared decision-making. However, the psychiatrist sometimes guides the agenda in a way that, while collaborative in tone, is not fully co-constructed with David.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questions and affirmations of David's own language. Could have more explicitly amplified powerful change talk (e.g., the 'nine down to a four' metaphor) to build greater momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates concerns without arguing or reinforcing them (e.g., 'you're not being difficult, you're being realistic'). Some moments of sustain talk could have been more strategically redirected by reflecting underlying hope or exploring meaning.
