# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive, while accepting Anika's ambivalence and concerns. Acceptance could be deeper by more explicitly validating her fear of judgment and worry about seeming "lame."

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Empathy was shown through accurate reflections of Anika's mixed feelings, barriers, and supports. It could be strengthened by reflecting the emotional and identity-level tensions around friends, being an athlete, and self-image.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on the target behavior of reducing vaping for basketball. The psychiatrist effectively narrowed a vague intention into a concrete, behavior-specific plan for the first two games.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used collaborative language and built the plan around Anika's choices, such as the first two games, free throws, and Sarah. Some practical suggestions were counselor-led, which made the collaboration slightly more consultant-like.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anika was repeatedly supported as the decision-maker, and her preferences were respected when she rejected certain options. Autonomy support could be stronger by creating more space for her to generate ideas before the psychiatrist offered tips.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling and open-ended questions effectively elicited Anika's own plans and coping ideas. Evocation could be more powerful by exploring her values, self-image, and confidence-based change talk more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally worked side-by-side with Anika and allowed her input to shape the intervention. However, the counselor's structured options and tips occasionally shifted the interaction toward a more expert-guided stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited change talk through scaling, goal-setting, and implementation questions. More deliberate amplification of Anika's change talk, especially around performance and self-efficacy, would strengthen this further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responded well to sustain talk by acknowledging social value and gently redirecting toward a manageable goal. More consistent double-sided reflections would help soften sustain talk without contradiction.
