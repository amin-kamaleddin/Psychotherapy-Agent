# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment or labeling. Directly addresses patient's fear of being stigmatized and validates her cultural identity and role as a mother.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections that capture subtext beneath defensiveness (e.g., the quiet question underneath). Metaphors and emotional observations are individually tailored rather than formulaic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with strong logical arc from defensiveness to commitment. One open-ended question about 'something else underneath' introduces slight risk of drifting from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is structured as a genuine joint exploration with repeated invitations for patient input. Decisions about next steps and resources are co-constructed rather than imposed by the counselor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional and consistent affirmations of patient's right to choose ('your autonomy is not up for debate'). Never pressures or implies obligation; all decisions are framed as the patient's own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits motivation from within the patient rather than providing external reasons for change. Could push further into eliciting confidence and ability language to more fully develop self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently positioned with the patient from the opening statement onward. Session is shaped by the patient's contributions—her concerns about partner, family, and environment guide direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently and skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk through strategic reflections. Could be strengthened by explicitly eliciting confidence ratings and ability/past success language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk masterfully by reflecting ambivalence rather than arguing or correcting. Cultural tradition is acknowledged with respect and sustain talk is used as a springboard toward the patient's own values.
