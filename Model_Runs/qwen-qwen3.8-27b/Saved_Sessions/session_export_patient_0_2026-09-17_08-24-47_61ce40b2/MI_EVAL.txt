# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, validating stance and normalizes Adrian's concerns without pressure. Acceptance is strong, though slightly structured and less explicitly personal overall.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses reflective listening to capture Adrian's ambivalence, social tension, and emotional undertones. Reflections are accurate but somewhat repetitive and do not fully explore deeper shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on safer methamphetamine use after the recent infection. Each question builds toward a concrete, relevant plan tied to the clinical concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds the plan from Adrian's own ideas and invites his input throughout. Collaboration could be strengthened with more explicit equal-partner language and a more co-led structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes Adrian's choice, ownership, and expertise about his own life. The plan is framed as Adrian's own decision rather than clinician-imposed direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits most of the plan from Adrian through open and evocative questions. Evocation is practical and effective, but could be deeper by connecting to values, confidence, or future self.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Adrian as the expert on his own choices and does not assume the correct answer. Adrian's contributions directly guide the focus and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces Adrian's own safer-use and health-protective language. Change talk is strengthened but remains more behavioral than values- or confidence-based.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Adrian's reasons to continue using and reframes around safety and small steps. Ambivalence is handled skillfully, though the full function of sustain talk is not fully explored.
