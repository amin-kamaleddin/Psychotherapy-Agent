# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some nonjudgment and respect for Marco's burden, but acceptance is limited by frequent advice-giving and medical safety reminders. The interaction feels more procedural than consistently affirming of Marco as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are useful reflections of Marco's perspective, such as using smoking to quiet mental noise and wanting practical support. However, the empathy remains relatively surface-level and misses deeper fatigue, fatalism, and fear of being judged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a realistic smoking-reduction target, especially the after-work cigarette. The plan is concrete and low-pressure, though repeated safety and logistical details sometimes interrupt the momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and tailors the trial to Marco's work life, preferences, and resistance to formal plans. Collaboration is strong overall, though the psychiatrist still leads much of the information-giving and planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marco's autonomy is strongly supported through phrases like "your call," "no schedule," and "wing it." Repeated offers to draft scripts and recap the plan slightly weaken the sense that Marco is fully in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks questions that help Marco articulate a small change, but motivation is not deeply explored. The session relies more on practical problem-solving than on eliciting values, confidence, or deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts to some of Marco's preferences, showing some partnership. However, the session remains provider-led, with the psychiatrist frequently initiating options, summaries, and planning steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco offers some change talk, such as choosing the inhaler and agreeing to try one small slot. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, deepen, or build on this change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Marco and often responds to resistance with practical, low-pressure framing. However, resistance is not fully rolled with or redirected into change talk through more complex reflections.
