# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalized the client's ambivalence. Some warmth felt procedural rather than deeply individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured the client's mixed feelings and goals. The empathy could be stronger by exploring deeper emotions such as shame, fear of exclusion, or identity threat.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on safer alcohol use and concrete harm-reduction steps. It moved clearly from ambivalence to an actionable, client-centered plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructed the plan with the client and supported the client's ideas. More explicit power-sharing, such as agenda-setting or feedback on session direction, would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client generated options and was encouraged to own the plan. The clinician could more explicitly affirm the client's freedom to accept, modify, or abandon the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited the client's own strategies and motivations. Deeper values-based evocation, such as exploring the meaning of college, family, or responsibility, would strengthen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was collaborative and client-centered, with the plan largely generated by the client. The clinician still guided the structure and offered options, limiting a fully partner-like stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited many concrete change statements and reinforced the client's emerging plan. It could be strengthened by more consistently deepening desire, need, confidence, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument and redirected toward the client's goals. More consistent reframing of sustain talk into change-oriented language would improve this dimension.
