### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, normalizing Marcus's feelings and affirming his honesty throughout. Falls slightly short of a 5 due to generic affirmations that don't explicitly validate Marcus's worth beyond his goals, and a thin final response.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate, emotionally attuned reflections that capture ambivalence and deeper meaning. Could go deeper in moments of vulnerability (e.g., fear of feeling awkward at 42) and the final response is noticeably thinner than earlier reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical flow from ambivalence through discovery to concrete planning, with each question building momentum. The transition from exploration to preparation could have been bridged with a brief summary, and the final response is directionally vague.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently invites Marcus's perspective and follows his lead (e.g., dancing rather than redirecting to evidence-based activities). Occasionally functions more as a guide directing the arc than a truly equal co-creator, and the final response is less collaborative than preceding exchanges.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout — explicitly validates Marcus's right to go at his own pace, never pressures for commitment, and frames all questions in terms of his own preferences. Statements like 'I'm not trying to turn that into a firm commitment' reinforce genuine power-sharing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength — the psychiatrist never tells Marcus why or how to change, instead consistently drawing out his own motivations, language, and solutions. All key insights (parking further away, dancing, community center) emerge organically from Marcus himself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing throughout, with explicit acknowledgments that Marcus is the expert on his own life and schedule. The session direction is clearly shaped by Marcus's contributions, with the psychiatrist creating space rather than leading.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and highlights change talk, with effective questions that move Marcus from passive to active language. Could more systematically explore all dimensions (desire, ability, need, commitment) and further amplify Marcus's excitement or confidence level.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating concerns and reframing rather than confronting (e.g., 'what would make this feel less like another commitment'). Could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence before tilting toward change.
