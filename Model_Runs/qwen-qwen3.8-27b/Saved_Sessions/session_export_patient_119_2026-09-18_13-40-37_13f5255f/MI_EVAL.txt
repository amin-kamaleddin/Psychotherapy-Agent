# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; validates Selene's honesty and reframes self-criticism (e.g., 'obsessive') as a strength rather than challenging it. Never criticizes missed doses or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures underlying emotional meaning, internal conflicts, and subtle shifts in tone (e.g., noticing the difference in Selene's voice). Reflections go well beyond surface-level paraphrasing to identify the real goal behind the behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progressive structure moving from ambivalence through barrier identification to a concrete, self-generated commitment. Maintains focus on medication adherence even when tangential topics (diet) arise.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative dialogue where Selene's ideas (container, bathroom counter, teeth-checking) are the session's content and the psychiatrist builds on them. Explicitly frames the interaction as co-construction rather than expert-advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every strategy emerges from Selene's own thinking; the psychiatrist never prescribes solutions or uses directive language. The final commitment ('I'll set it up tonight') is entirely self-initiated and intrinsic.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivational content is drawn from Selene's own words rather than providing external reasons for change. Masterful questions like 'when the pill does get taken, what's different?' transform her experience into the foundation of her solution.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit power-sharing statements and consistent use of 'what do you think' position Selene as the expert on her own life. The psychiatrist serves as a facilitator of Selene's own wisdom rather than an authority figure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently notices and amplifies shifts in Selene's language (e.g., 'might work' vs. 'never seems to stick'). Helps her articulate her own change motivation ('lighter') and strengthens self-efficacy by reflecting back her problem-solving process.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argues against or minimizes Selene's sustain talk; instead validates it while gently introducing the cost or reframing it as useful information. Textbook skill in acknowledging concerns as legitimate while redirecting toward problem-solving.
