### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations of Marcus's perspective and choices; slightly formulaic in delivery and could have more explicitly normalized his history of feeling like a failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, including capturing emotional weight beyond the literal; occasionally stays at the cognitive level rather than fully exploring the shame and loss-of-control themes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on asthma/inhaler adherence with well-managed pacing from broad to specific; a brief collaborative summary at the end would have strengthened closure and consolidation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance throughout; the final plan is entirely co-constructed by Marcus with the psychiatrist as facilitator, a textbook example of MI collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent and explicit autonomy support, respecting Marcus's boundaries on the dog, reminders, and low-stakes approach; exemplifies MI with suggestions framed as options rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Marcus's own motivations and ideas rather than providing external justifications; could be strengthened with scaling questions and more deliberate elicitation of ability/confidence talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional partnership; opening question immediately establishes Marcus's authority, and his contributions substantially shape every aspect of the session plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently evokes and validates Marcus's change talk with skillful amplification; could include more deliberate confidence-building questions and fuller expansion of the change talk that emerges.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Handles resistance skillfully, validates Marcus's perspective on the dog, and acknowledges the shame loop without reinforcement; could more actively explore the gap between 'inconvenience' and 'out of control.'
