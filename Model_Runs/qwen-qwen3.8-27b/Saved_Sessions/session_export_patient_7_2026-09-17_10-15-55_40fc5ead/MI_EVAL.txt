# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental stance is strong throughout, including when the client becomes defensive (“You’re right. I’m not your mom…”). Some reflections are slightly presumptive, and the final question reopens disclosure after the client asked to stop.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Good validating reflections on guardedness, pressure, and need for space (“It makes sense…”). Inconsistent attunement to the client’s repeated boundary signals, culminating in the closing “Can you tell me more about that?”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Stayed loosely connected to rapport-building themes (home conflict, pressure, safety). Focus is somewhat diffuse without a brief consolidating summary or clearer strategic focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and adjusts stance to feedback. Continues to drive the interaction with repeated questions, limiting full power-sharing (final question after “We’ll see” is particularly limiting).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy framing (“You’re the expert on what you need,” “not trying to force anything”). Repeated questions after the client asked to stop undermine the message that Amina controls the pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriate open-ended questions but does not sufficiently build on Amina’s brief, meaningful statements. Missed opportunity to reflect “space” back as a client-identified goal and explore it further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicit partnership language and willingness to adjust (“on your side as a partner, not an authority figure”). Still leads much of the session through counselor-driven prompts rather than letting Amina decide whether to continue, pause, or shift.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some meaningful client change talk elicited (“If you’d just leave it alone, I’d breathe fine”) and identified as “a clear need.” Limited follow-up to deepen or expand Amina’s own language; motivation is not consistently developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not argue with or reinforce the resistance; validates its function (“It makes sense…”, “You’re right”). Continues to invite more disclosure and does not use stronger softening strategies (double-sided reflections, strategic redirection).
