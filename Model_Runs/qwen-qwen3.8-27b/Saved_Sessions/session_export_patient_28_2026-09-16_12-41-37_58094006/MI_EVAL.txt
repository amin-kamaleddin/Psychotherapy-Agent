# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone with clear affirmations and normalization. The acceptance is strong but somewhat task-focused and could include deeper, individualized positive regard for Dante’s emotional struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking regarding Dante’s ambivalence, barriers, and frustration. However, the empathy remains somewhat surface-level and does not fully explore the deeper emotional weight of his self-doubt and loss of identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains an exceptionally clear and consistent focus on the target behavior of re-establishing a small exercise routine. The conversation moves logically from importance and confidence to a concrete plan and relapse prevention without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed with Dante’s input on key steps, such as the mailbox walk, alarm, and phone placement. The interaction remains counselor-driven because the psychiatrist frequently supplies the plan structure, examples, and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by inviting Dante to choose and evaluate confidence without coercion. Autonomy support is weakened by prescriptive language and frequent provision of specific solutions rather than consistently emphasizing Dante’s ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling questions and open-ended prompts successfully draw out Dante’s reasons for change and increase his confidence. The evocation is limited because the counselor often supplies ideas and options, reducing Dante’s own generation of motivation and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Dante’s input and allows his preferences to shape the final plan. However, the partnership is uneven because the counselor frequently leads the session and positions themselves as the problem-solver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits and reinforces Dante’s values-based change talk and increases his confidence from 3 to 7. The score is reduced because the counselor sometimes leads toward specific plans and misses opportunities to amplify stronger change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges and normalizes Dante’s resistance without arguing or dismissing it. However, the counselor does not consistently redirect sustain talk toward change using double-sided reflections, reframes, or strategic rolling with resistance.
