# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard without labeling or minimizing. Validates both sides of the patient's ambivalence and affirms her self-awareness throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses complex reflections that capture emotion beneath surface content, accurately naming underlying fear and normalizing avoidance as self-protective rather than pathological.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear strategic arc from open exploration to a specific, time-bound action plan. Consistently funnels the conversation toward the single actionable target without allowing drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on her own experience with power consistently shared. The final plan (pull over in the car, send the message) is entirely patient-generated through collaborative process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly affirms choice and disclaims directive authority ("Not because I'm asking, but because you already know it's reasonable"). Never tells the patient what to do; helps her clarify her own values and priorities.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully draws motivation from within the patient using open questions, the message-writing exercise, and reflecting back her own words. All insight and the final commitment are self-generated by the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions herself as a collaborator, inviting the patient to define priorities and what a good answer looks like. The session is shaped by the patient's words, decisions, and priorities at every turn.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically builds change talk by amplifying the patient's own reasoning, using the message-writing exercise to make her motivation visible, and affirming the quality of her own words. Progression from uncertainty to concrete commitment is substantial.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses the patient's own words to reframe sustain talk without confrontation (e.g., "That's not a simple question"). Validates the function of avoidance before gently redirecting, and sustain talk naturally diminishes across the session.
