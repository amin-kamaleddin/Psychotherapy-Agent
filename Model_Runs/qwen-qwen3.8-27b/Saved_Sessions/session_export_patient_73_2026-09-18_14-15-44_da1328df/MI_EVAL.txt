# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, specific, and individualized unconditional positive regard throughout. The psychiatrist validates Linda's identity beyond her behavior and never labels or judges.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture emotional and relational depth beyond paraphrase, tracking cognitive shifts in real time and naming underlying needs rather than surface behaviors.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on tobacco cessation with deliberate pacing from motivation through ambivalence to concrete plan. Could be strengthened by an explicit co-created agenda at the start.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed conversation where Linda's ideas shape direction and the psychiatrist builds on contributions rather than steering toward a predetermined outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never prescribes, pressures, or uses directive language. The client generates all solutions (twenty minutes, fridge note, telling sister) and autonomy is demonstrated in every interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Linda rather than providing external reasons. All key change talk is client-generated, with the psychiatrist creating conditions for its emergence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing with language positioning Linda as the expert in her own life. Could be elevated by more explicit agenda negotiation and less subtly guiding reflections.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of questions, reflections, and amplification to elicit genuine change talk and highlight Linda's own reframing. Could push further by explicitly evoking confidence language or asking Linda to articulate what success looks like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges the legitimacy of sustain talk while shifting focus toward client agency and emerging change. Could be strengthened with more explicit double-sided reflections that hold both sides while tilting toward change.
