# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, nonjudgment, and acceptance of Samir’s guardedness without labeling him as resistant. Acceptance could be stronger with warmer, more individualized affirmation before reframing resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures key themes of independence, practicality, and boundary-setting. Some reflections remain somewhat surface-level and could better highlight the emotional texture of Samir’s fatigue and need for autonomy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on medication adherence and narrows from broad concerns to concrete, behavior-specific ideas. Direction could be improved by summarizing the emerging plan and explicitly linking it to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Samir’s preferences and constraints strongly shape the conversation and the emerging plan. The interaction demonstrates high-level co-construction rather than an expert-driven approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently frames medication adherence as Samir’s choice and avoids pressure, promises, or surveillance. This is a particularly strong aspect of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions invite Samir’s input, but mainly elicit practical preferences rather than deeper values or motivations. Stronger evocation would expand statements like “not opposed to staying healthy” into intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Samir as the expert on his own life and incorporates his ideas into the plan. Partnership could be strengthened with more explicit agenda-setting and shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some practical change talk and acknowledges Samir’s useful ideas. The session misses opportunities to amplify, reflect back, or deepen Samir’s own motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively avoids arguing with Samir’s sustain talk and redirects toward low-pressure action. More double-sided reflections could better honor the sustain talk while linking it to change.
