# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing the client's ambivalence and validating their right to not act. The warmth remains in a professional register rather than expressing deeper unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening that captures both content and emotional undertones with remarkable precision. Reflections go beyond paraphrasing to name the client's cognitive processes and self-corrections in real time.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from belief exploration to concrete action. Pacing is organic and could benefit from slightly more strategic summarization of emerging themes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with inclusive language and a genuine dialogue between equals. The psychiatrist consistently follows the client's leads and builds on their contributions rather than redirecting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent and explicit autonomy support woven throughout every interaction, including direct permission to not decide and attribution of authority to the client. The approach is entirely non-directive with no prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within the client using questions and reflections that draw out self-generated insight. Each response builds on what the client has already said to elicit the next layer of understanding.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, using language like 'your gut' and 'your tolerance.' The power sharing is genuine, with the client's contributions substantially shaping the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk throughout, often without the client realizing it, by drawing out and amplifying their own change language. The session shows a clear progression from sustain talk to concrete change talk and a specific small step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively through gentle questioning and validation rather than argument. Could be slightly more strategic by using double-sided reflections to actively reframe sustain talk into ambivalence that opens change.
