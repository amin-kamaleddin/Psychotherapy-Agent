# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor appears generally respectful and nonjudgmental. Acceptance is somewhat task-focused rather than fully warm and person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some validation is offered, but reflections are brief and may miss deeper emotional meaning. Empathic responsiveness remains partial.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the target behavior. Direction is present but prescriptive rather than client-led.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client input influences some plan details. The therapist still controls much of the conversation structure and solution content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Autonomy is acknowledged at times. Frequent suggestions and directives may reduce the client’s sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions are used, but evocation of the client’s own reasons, values, and motivation is limited. More strategic eliciting would strengthen this criterion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative moments are present, but the therapist often supplies structure and solutions. The interaction does not fully convey shared partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is acknowledged but not consistently amplified or expanded. The counselor could better deepen the client’s own expressed motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is accepted without argument. Responses rely more on reassurance than MI-consistent reflections of ambivalence.
