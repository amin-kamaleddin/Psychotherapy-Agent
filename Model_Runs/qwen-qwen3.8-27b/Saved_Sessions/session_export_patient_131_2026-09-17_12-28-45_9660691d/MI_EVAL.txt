### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance and validates Wiremu's self-doubt as a sign of seriousness rather than weakness. Could have gone deeper in affirming Wiremu's worth beyond his actions and more explicitly normalizing the struggle of rebuilding after violence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional empathic skill with reflections that capture the emotional and existential core of Wiremu's statements, such as naming the tension between independence and safety. Empathy is accurate, consistent, and progressively deepens throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on target behaviors with a logical progression from ambivalence exploration to co-creating a practical plan. Could have more explicitly connected the living arrangement decision to the broader treatment goals and consolidated the plan before closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is structured as a genuine joint exploration where the psychiatrist builds on Wiremu's own language and co-constructs the plan with him. The tone throughout is one of two people working together rather than one person advising another.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Wiremu's autonomy, validating his right to design his own arrangement and reframing choices in ways that honor both his freedom and his values. Never prescribes a solution or pressures a decision in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Wiremu rather than providing external reasons for change, with questions that progressively deepen from surface-level ambivalence to identity-level reframing. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change but instead invites Wiremu to articulate his own values and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Wiremu as the expert on his own life through phrases like 'I'd rather hear it from you' and 'I'm curious what you think.' The psychiatrist follows Wiremu's lead, explicitly names his contributions as important, and power sharing is genuine and consistent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through open questions that elicit concrete plans and values-based reasons from Wiremu. Could have more explicitly elicited confidence or ability statements to deepen the change talk further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by exploring rather than dismissing, using gentle reframing such as 'That's not giving up your independence, that's protecting your whole world.' Could have used double-sided reflections or more strategic pivoting on the freedom and fear-of-being-seen themes.
