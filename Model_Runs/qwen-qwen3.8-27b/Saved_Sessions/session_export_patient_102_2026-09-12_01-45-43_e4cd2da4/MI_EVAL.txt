# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating tone throughout. However, the warmth felt somewhat procedural and lacked deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor used appropriate reflections and recognized the client's discomfort with being monitored or labeled. However, deeper empathic exploration of embarrassment, fear, and ambivalence was inconsistent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on drinking at parties and practical behavior change. A stronger direction would have balanced behavioral strategies with deeper exploration of the client's values and goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were collaborative moments, such as tailoring refusal language to the client's natural speech. However, the counselor supplied most strategies rather than co-creating the plan from the client's own expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently invited the client to choose among options and avoided coercive language. Nonetheless, the advisory stance and structured advice limited fully client-led decision making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early questions elicited useful client motivation, but the counselor quickly shifted into advice and strategy lists. More follow-up questions about meaning, readiness, and confidence would have strengthened evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invited client preferences and choices in several places. However, the interaction remained largely counselor-driven, with the client selecting from counselor-generated options rather than shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicited useful change talk about wanting to cut down, feel more in control, and manage the pitcher moment. These statements were not consistently reinforced or expanded into stronger confidence or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor accepted resistance without arguing and reduced shame when the client rejected formal strategies. However, important sustain talk was not consistently reflected or redirected toward change.
