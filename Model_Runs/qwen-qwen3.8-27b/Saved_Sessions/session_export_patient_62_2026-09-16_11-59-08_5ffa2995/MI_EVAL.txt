# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently used respectful, nonjudgmental language to validate Jordan without shame. It could be even stronger by more explicitly affirming Jordan’s worth beyond productivity and outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist showed good reflective listening and captured Jordan’s exhaustion, financial stress, and fear of failure. The main gap is that the reflections did not always deepen into underlying shame or self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the core concern of work instability, financial stress, and feeling stuck. The psychiatrist could more explicitly connect each action step back to Jordan’s broader goal of stability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited Jordan’s input and adapted to his preferences, including online applications and the start-date wording. However, the therapist still led much of the content with scripts and techniques.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supported choice by offering options and respecting Jordan’s limits. A perfect score would include more explicit reinforcement that the next step is entirely Jordan’s decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited some internal motivation by asking what Jordan wanted to change. The session often shifted into practical advice rather than fully exploring Jordan’s own reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a partially collaborative stance by offering options and working alongside Jordan. However, frequent expert-style instruction reduced the sense of true power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist drew out some change talk and reinforced one application as meaningful. The main weakness was inconsistent amplification of Jordan’s hope and motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with sustain talk and redirected it in a low-pressure way. Some responses were mildly persuasive rather than fully MI-consistent.
