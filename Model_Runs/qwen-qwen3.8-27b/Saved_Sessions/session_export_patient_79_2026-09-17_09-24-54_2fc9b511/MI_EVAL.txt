# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, respect, and nonjudgment, even when the patient resisted. They validated the patient's expertise and avoided labeling or corrective language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist showed strong reflective listening, especially with the patient's mixed feelings about asthma care. However, one vulnerable moment about feeling infantilized received a more superficial response than a deeper emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stayed focused on asthma management, inhaler use, and a workable plan. At times the discussion became more process-centered and lacked a stronger final summary connecting the plan back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist engaged the patient as an equal partner and repeatedly invited his input. The patient's ideas clearly shaped the plan, the follow-up approach, and the therapeutic interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supported the patient's choice, ownership, and right to self-management. Language consistently avoided pressure, perfect compliance, or scorekeeping.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used open questions to draw out the patient's own ideas, plans, and motivations. Evocation could have been stronger by more systematically developing importance, ability, need, and confidence change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was clearly framed as shared work rather than expert instruction. The patient was treated as the expert on his own body and actively helped shape the agenda and plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicited and affirmed change talk related to the patient's plan. Change talk could have been further amplified by summarizing, magnifying, or exploring confidence in following through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handled resistance without argument, persuasion, or escalation. Sustain talk could have been softened further with more double-sided reflections and more consistent pivots toward change.
