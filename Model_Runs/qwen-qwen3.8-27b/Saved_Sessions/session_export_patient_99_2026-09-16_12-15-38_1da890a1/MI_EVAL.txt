# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Matteo without shaming or pressuring him. Acceptance is strong but not a perfect 5 because the opening is framed by a screening flag and remains somewhat procedural.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Matteo's ambivalence and underlying reasons for his behavior. Empathy is solid but could be deeper by addressing the emotional or identity-level meaning behind statements like not wanting to be that guy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on Matteo's drinking and its connection to his ankle, school, and work. There is clear momentum toward a practical plan and follow-up without significant drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is clearly co-created, with Matteo choosing and modifying the steps. The interaction feels like a partnership rather than a clinician-directed recommendation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Matteo's right to choose, seeks permission, and respects his boundaries. The low-pressure text check-in further reinforces autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling and open questions help evoke Matteo's own motivation and ideas. Evocation is somewhat limited by the early introduction of practical, counselor-generated options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and reinforces that Matteo is the expert on his own life. However, the early structured list of options makes the process less fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful change talk about safety, class, work, and not wanting to be that guy. The session would benefit from more consistent amplification of these statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled respectfully without argument or reinforcement. It would be stronger with more deliberate double-sided reflections to transform sustain talk into change language.
