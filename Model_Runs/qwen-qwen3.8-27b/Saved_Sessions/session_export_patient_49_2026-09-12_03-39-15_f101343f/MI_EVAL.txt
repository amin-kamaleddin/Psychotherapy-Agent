# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, validating language and respects Hana’s boundary around her morning coffee ritual. Acceptance is strong, though some praise is tied to the plan or progress rather than being fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects Hana’s ambivalence, stress-related smoking, and fear of failing. Empathy could be deeper by more fully exploring the emotions behind her worry and social pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on a specific, manageable target: reducing lunch smoking by using gum. The psychiatrist keeps the discussion moving toward a concrete, workable plan without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is largely co-created from Hana’s preferences, such as spearmint gum and a “restart tomorrow” rule. The psychiatrist sometimes still leads agenda-setting and introduces several strategies before fully eliciting Hana’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports Hana’s choice by respecting her decision to keep the coffee ritual and allowing her to reject suggestions. Autonomy support could improve by offering fewer provider-generated solutions before first asking what Hana thinks would help.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and open questions effectively draw out Hana’s own motivation, confidence, and plan. Evocation is strong, though the counselor occasionally moves into advice-giving before fully drawing out Hana’s ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana actively shapes the session by choosing the target, gum flavor, and coping plan. The psychiatrist is collaborative, but sometimes takes the lead in generating solutions before fully inviting Hana’s next ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses questions that elicit Hana’s reasons, confidence, and commitment to change. Change talk could be further strengthened by reflecting, amplifying, and expanding Hana’s own statements more consistently.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Hana’s sustain talk and reframes concerns as understandable. It could be more advanced by using double-sided reflections and exploring triggers such as Sarah more strategically.
