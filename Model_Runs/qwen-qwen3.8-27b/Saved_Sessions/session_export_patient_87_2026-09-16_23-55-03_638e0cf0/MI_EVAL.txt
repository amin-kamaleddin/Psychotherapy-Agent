# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental regard while validating Marco's struggle. Acceptance could deepen by more explicitly normalizing his ambivalence and affirming his repeated willingness to try despite setbacks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captures Marco's emotional experience, including the need for a personal pause. The empathy could be stronger by more fully exploring loneliness, exhaustion, and fear at key turning points.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, coherent focus on managing evening eating urges and building a realistic small step. The psychiatrist efficiently guides from broad concerns to specific triggers, options, and a concrete plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works in a collaborative, non-authoritarian way and allows Marco's ideas to shape the session. More explicit shared decision-making would strengthen the collaborative partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports choice and ownership by asking open questions and avoiding directive language. Marco generates his own plan, including water, a pause, and going to bed if needed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Marco's internal values, such as wanting calm, rest, and a minute that belongs to him. The evocation could be strengthened by explicitly exploring readiness, confidence, or importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marco is consistently positioned as the expert on his own life, evenings, and family context. The psychiatrist does not impose a plan and allows Marco's preferences to drive the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes change talk about manageable actions, such as water, a pause, and one song. A stronger level would include more explicit development of confidence, readiness, or future outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without argument or dismissal, often reframed toward manageable action. More active use of double-sided reflections or explicit pivots to values and exceptions would improve this area.
