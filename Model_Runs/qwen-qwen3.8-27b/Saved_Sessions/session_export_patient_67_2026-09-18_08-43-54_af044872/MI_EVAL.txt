# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and deeply respectful presence throughout with zero evidence of judgment or conditional regard. The psychiatrist explicitly normalizes Kenji's experience and validates his ambivalence without trying to fix it.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and consistent reflective listening that captures the emotional and experiential core of Kenji's statements, going well beyond simple paraphrasing. No missed cues or inaccurate reflections; reflections increase in depth as the session progresses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and logical progression from awareness to understanding to identifying resources to addressing barriers, all centered on alcohol reduction. Lacks a deliberate closing that consolidates the session's insights and collaboratively identifies a concrete next step or focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed dialogue where each step follows from Kenji's disclosures and the psychiatrist builds on them. Consistently uses non-hierarchical, invitational language positioning themselves as a fellow explorer rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent autonomy support, including directly addressing external motivation and affirming Kenji's right to own the process. Never prescribes or makes directive recommendations; the entire session is structured around Kenji's own choices and interpretations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within Kenji without providing external reasons for change or fear appeals. Could have pushed emerging change talk into stronger commitment language, particularly when Kenji articulated what the beer does for him.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Kenji, using recurring invitational language that keeps him as the expert. Never offers expert-driven advice; the session is entirely shaped by Kenji's responses and disclosures.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questions leading Kenji to recognize automatic behavior, identify alternatives, and articulate what 'awake' means. Could have amplified and consolidated this change talk more explicitly, especially a closing summary reinforcing the shift in perspective.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating the feeling without reinforcing the conclusion, using gentle reframes like 'who decided it has to be all or nothing?' The session ends with Kenji still in sustain talk without the psychiatrist getting a chance to further soften that final barrier.
