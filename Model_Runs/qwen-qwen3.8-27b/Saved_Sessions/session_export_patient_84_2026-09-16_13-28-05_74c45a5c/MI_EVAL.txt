# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepts Malik's anger without confrontation. Warmth is sometimes procedural, and an early assumption about his mixed feelings prevents a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provides adequate reflections but often in a brief, mechanical way. Misses deeper themes around autonomy, respect, and resentment toward being controlled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Stays focused on relevant topics such as use, school, peers, and mother. However, momentum shifts quickly toward practical closure rather than building toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites input and lets Malik shape the final script. Collaboration is strong but limited mainly to drafting the message rather than exploring values or goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently emphasizes Malik's control and right to choose or end the session. Still somewhat structured in offering options and leading the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Begins with reasonable open-ended questions but does not deepen them after sustain talk. Relies more on providing solutions than drawing out Malik's own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys that Malik's perspective matters and collaborates on the script. However, the psychiatrist still sets the agenda and leads the session structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes a few initial attempts to evoke change talk but does not sustain or develop it. The session shifts to managing parental conflict rather than exploring change motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with Malik and acknowledges his sustain talk. Does not effectively use strategic or double-sided reflections to redirect it toward change.
