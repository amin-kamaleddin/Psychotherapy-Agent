# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Anara’s ambivalence. Warmth is present but sometimes relies on repeated, template-like language rather than more individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, especially in capturing conflicting feelings about facing care. Reflections could be deeper and less repetitive in exploring shame, fear of being seen, and loss of control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on a specific target behavior: taking a small step toward seeing a doctor. The conversation moves logically from barriers to a manageable first action without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Anara to co-create a plan, and her ideas meaningfully shape the conversation. Collaboration is strong but remains partly counselor-guided because the psychiatrist still provides several suggested options and structures the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anara is positioned as the decision-maker, and the psychiatrist avoids pressure, advice, or urgency. Autonomy support is generally good but could be more explicit in affirming that the choice and next steps are entirely hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist largely draws motivation from within Anara, allowing her to identify small steps and personal preferences. Deeper evocation of values, confidence, or personal meaning could strengthen the emerging change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anara is treated as the expert on what would work, and open questions invite her to shape the plan. Partnership could improve by giving the client more control over setting the agenda and defining success without the counselor offering menu-like options first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Anara produces clear change talk about taking a small step and gaining relief. The psychiatrist could more effectively amplify, reinforce, and consolidate this change talk with follow-up questions about meaning, confidence, and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is accepted without argument, and barriers are gently redirected into smaller, manageable actions. Softening could be strengthened by using more double-sided reflections that connect her hesitance to values and possible movement.
