# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated respect, nonjudgment, and support for Marisol's autonomy. The main limitation was that the warmth sometimes felt procedural rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were adequate early reflections, but empathy became inconsistent as the session shifted into advice and scripting. Deeper feelings, values, and identity concerns were not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on the lip piercing, dental health, and interaction with the dentist. The main issue was some drift toward practical advice rather than MI-style exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were collaborative moments, especially in shaping the boundary statement with the dentist. However, the amount of unilateral advice and scripted options limited true MI-style partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supported Marisol's choice, ownership, and right to keep the piercing. Some instructional content and the roleplay setup made parts of the session feel less autonomy-fostering.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist relied heavily on practical suggestions and scripts rather than eliciting Marisol's own motivations and values. There was limited exploration of her readiness, confidence, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited input and allowed Marisol to influence the final boundary line. Still, much of the session felt clinician-led rather than a fully collaborative process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marisol offered some change-relevant statements, but the psychiatrist did not strategically reflect, amplify, or expand them. Most of the interaction became advice and rehearsal rather than evoking her own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Marisol's sustain talk and responded with validation and autonomy support. However, more active MI strategies to soften her resistance were not used consistently.
