# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist remained respectful and nonjudgmental, using validating statements without blame. However, the acceptance was somewhat formulaic and did not fully affirm Marek’s ambivalence or worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Empathy was limited and mostly stayed at the factual level of drinking amounts, guidelines, and health risks. The psychiatrist missed opportunities to reflect Marek’s surprise, defensiveness, and the social meaning of drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on alcohol use, binge criteria, health concerns, and a possible weekend trial. However, the direction became more advisory and behavior-prescriptive rather than following Marek’s own change agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were some collaborative moments, such as offering choices about what to focus on. But the psychiatrist quickly took on an expert role by providing guidelines, scripts, and action plans rather than co-creating a plan with Marek.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged that Marek was not ready to quit and framed actions as options. Autonomy support was weakened by repeatedly offering specific limits, scripts, tracking tasks, and a checklist.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked a few early questions, but the session was largely driven by information-giving and advice. Marek’s own motivations, values, and reasons for change were not deeply explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist occasionally invited input but quickly shifted to an expert-driven style. Marek’s preferences for a low-effort approach and no scripted conversation were acknowledged but not fully integrated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marek offered some change talk, such as wanting to see the numbers and possibly trying a water swap. The psychiatrist did not consistently notice, amplify, or deepen these statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Marek’s strong sustain talk, such as seeing drinking as fun and not a problem, was not effectively softened or redirected. The psychiatrist moved back to advice and planning without fully exploring his ambivalence.
