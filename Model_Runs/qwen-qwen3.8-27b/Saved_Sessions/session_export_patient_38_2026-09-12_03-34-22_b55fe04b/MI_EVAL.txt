# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validates Elena's emotions without judgment, and the client explicitly appreciates not being made to feel stupid.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections accurately capture Elena's relief, fear, and avoidance. Some reflections remain surface-level and miss deeper values or fears that would elevate this to a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session remains responsive to the client but drifts toward broader emotional regulation and sleep, losing central momentum around the contraception decision.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frames the interaction as joint exploration and co-constructs a manageable rest-and-wait plan. It is not a 5 because suggestions such as grounding and reminders sometimes lead rather than elicit Elena's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor strongly reinforces Elena's right to make her own choices and avoid controlling pressure, which the client explicitly appreciates.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evocative questions are used, but they remain superficial and miss opportunities to draw deeper change talk from Elena's fear and relief.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently invites Elena's input and avoids an expert-driven stance. Reliance on structured menus and specific scripts occasionally shifts the interaction toward counselor-provided solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are limited efforts to expand Elena's desire to find something better, and the counselor misses key opportunities to reinforce her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with sustain talk and validates Elena's need to wait, but leans into accepting the status quo rather than gently pivoting toward future readiness.
