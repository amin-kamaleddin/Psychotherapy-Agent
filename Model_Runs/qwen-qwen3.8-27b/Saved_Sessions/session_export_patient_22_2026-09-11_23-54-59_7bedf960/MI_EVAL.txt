# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently nonjudgmental, warm, and affirming stance throughout. Acceptance is reliable but somewhat formulaic, lacking deeper individualized validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Empathic responses are present but remain surface-level, often summarizing content rather than exploring emotion. Key feelings of nervousness, self-doubt, and social fear are not deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, logical, and consistent focus on the drinking-reduction goal. Direction is strong but remains largely logistical rather than expanding into deeper motivations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist uses collaborative language and invites client input on details. Collaboration is limited by a therapist-provided structured plan rather than fully co-created strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client choices are respected and recommendations are generally framed as options. Some directive planning language slightly reduces the autonomy-supportive tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The therapist briefly elicits goals but then mainly provides strategies, scripts, and techniques. There is limited effort to draw out the client’s own reasons for change or self-generated solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client’s preferences shape details, but the overall session structure is therapist-led. Partnership is present but uneven, with the client working within a pre-determined framework.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist affirms progress language and supports goal-setting, helping reinforce change talk. However, client change talk is not consistently explored, amplified, or developed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally not reinforced, and some all-or-nothing thinking is gently reframed. A significant sustain-talk concern about Friday nights is raised but left unaddressed.
