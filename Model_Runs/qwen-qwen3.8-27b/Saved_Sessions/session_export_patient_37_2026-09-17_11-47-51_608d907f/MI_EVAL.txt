# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; never labels Elena as irrational or overreacting, instead reframing her self-criticism as strength. Normalizes her uncertainty explicitly and maintains a warm, non-judgmental tone consistently.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate, non-repetitive, and progressively deepens from surface reflections to identifying the social identity dimension. Notably captures the nuanced dual pull between her own experience and new information, as well as her self-regulatory struggle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a generally clear focus on the vaccine decision with logical progression from ambivalence to a concrete plan. Slight dip due to occasional empathically valid digressions (e.g., the 'folding laundry' segment) that don't directly advance the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a collaborative exploration between equals using language like 'I'm wondering' and 'I'm curious.' Elena's responses directly shape where the session goes next, and reframes are offered as questions rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never pressures for an immediate decision and explicitly normalizes uncertainty ('that's okay'). Actively reinforces Elena's ownership of her process and her right to take time, without any 'you should' or 'I recommend' language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight in the session is Elena's own, elicited through open questions rather than provided by the psychiatrist. The psychiatrist never argues for vaccination or presents medical evidence directly — all motivation is self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Elena as the expert on her own situation, with her judgment serving as the standard for what's trustworthy and what constitutes readiness. Questions are structured to invite her perspective first before any clinical input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits change talk at each stage, moving Elena from 'I don't know if I'm comfortable' to a concrete, self-generated action plan. Affirms change talk when it appears ('That's you thinking clearly') and asks questions that deepen her own reasoning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validates Elena's logic without dismissing or arguing against it, using textbook techniques like 'it makes sense that you'd think that.' Creates space for new understanding to emerge by separating the medical question from the social identity question.
