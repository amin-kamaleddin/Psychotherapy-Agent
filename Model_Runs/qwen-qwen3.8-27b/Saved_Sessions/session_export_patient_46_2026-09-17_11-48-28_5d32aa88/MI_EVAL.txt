### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, and validating tone throughout, never lecturing or shaming Ethan's drinking. A slight opportunity remains to explicitly normalize his experience as a teenager navigating peer pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy, consistently reflecting meaning and feeling rather than just content. The psychiatrist tracks subtle shifts in Ethan's emotional state, including the weight behind a single word like 'honestly.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus on the target behavior while following Ethan's lead, with a clear logical progression from assessment to a concrete, actionable strategy. Each question builds on the last without wandering, keeping the discussion anchored in concrete Friday nights.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear example of two equal partners working together, with the psychiatrist treating Ethan's insights as valuable contributions to the work. The session feels like a conversation between equals, not a consultation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Ethan's agency and right to choose, never using directive language like 'you should' or 'you need to.' Ethan's own middle-ground solution is validated as his idea, keeping the entire session structured around his decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly skilled at eliciting motivation from within Ethan rather than providing it externally. All of Ethan's change talk emerges organically from his own reflections, facilitated by the psychiatrist's evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that Ethan holds the expertise about his own life and choices, using open questions that hand the steering wheel to him. The psychiatrist never assumes they know what would work, and Ethan's responses determine the next topic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and builds on change talk throughout by reflecting, amplifying, and validating Ethan's own language. The psychiatrist consistently picks up on Ethan's change statements and reinforces them as real options sitting in front of him.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk by exploring the experience neutrally without pushing back or arguing, and acknowledges group pressure without reinforcing it. The psychiatrist holds both the pull of the group and the pain of the consequences simultaneously without ever creating an argument.
