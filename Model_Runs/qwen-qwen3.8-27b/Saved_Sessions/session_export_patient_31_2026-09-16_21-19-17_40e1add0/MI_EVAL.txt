### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes David's ambivalence. It is supportive but could be warmer and more affirming beyond the task.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture David's practical ambivalence and preferred plan. The emotional weight of statements like feeling "buzzing" or "stuck" is not fully explored.

### Direction

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits David's own plan and language. The evocation is somewhat repetitive and could go deeper into motivation, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
