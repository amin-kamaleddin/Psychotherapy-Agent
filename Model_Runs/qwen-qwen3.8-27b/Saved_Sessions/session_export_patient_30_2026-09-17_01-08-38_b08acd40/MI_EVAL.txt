# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates David's concerns without challenging his skepticism. Falls short of 5 because warmth is somewhat procedural rather than consistently deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good complex reflections that capture both sides of David's ambivalence and practical concerns. Limited by mostly cognitive empathy and occasional overstatement of readiness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on cholesterol management and medication decision while moving toward a practical plan. Could be stronger by consolidating the plan into clearer commitments and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Builds the session around David's concerns, preferences, and proposed plan using an inclusive tone. Collaboration remains somewhat therapist-led rather than fully equal in session structure and goal-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports David's sense of choice by offering options rather than instructions. Avoids pressure or coercion while keeping the door open for future decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites David to generate his own plan, tracking ideas, and approach to the next test. More motivational depth would come from exploring values, future impact, and scaling confidence or importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys that David's experience and judgment are valuable and follows his lead on a diet-first approach. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicitly co-creating the agenda and sharing power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some practical change talk around diet, walking, and using the next check-up to assess progress. Change talk remains surface-level and is not consistently amplified through deeper MI techniques.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges medication concerns without arguing or dismissing them. The session remains centered on reasons to avoid pills, needing more active redirection toward long-term health and autonomy.
