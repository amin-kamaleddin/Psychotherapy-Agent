# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated the patient’s efficiency-focused framing. Minor deduction because the patient’s frustration about waiting and losing time was not directly acknowledged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The responses showed strong perspective-taking, especially around the meaning of breathlessness and the 3 PM crash. Some reflections were more summary-like, and the patient’s initial irritation was not deeply reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on a specific target behavior related to breathlessness and daily function. The discussion remained practical and did not drift into general health advice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-created using the patient’s own ideas and language. The psychiatrist acted as a collaborator rather than a clinician-led prescriber.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The patient’s freedom to choose, modify, or stop the plan was clearly supported. The response avoided pushing back when the patient rejected more rigorous exercise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited practical next steps and patient-generated strategies. Evocation was somewhat limited in depth, with some questions feeling more menu-like or steered.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed a partnership stance and positioned the patient as the expert. The session was shaped by the patient’s contributions and refined collaboratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited several change-related statements, such as wanting to fix the winded part and being willing to test the plan. Confidence and ability could have been amplified further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally softened by reframing the plan as non-additive and time-efficient. A more strategic double-sided reflection would have strengthened the response further.
