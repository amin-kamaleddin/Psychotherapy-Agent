# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating stance without pressure or blame. Acceptance could be stronger with more explicit warmth and direct affirmation of the client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate complex reflections to track ambivalence, fear, and the need for safety. Empathy would deepen by naming emotions such as shame, fear, and exhaustion more directly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on the target behavior of preparing for the certification exam. The counselor steadily narrows the focus from broad structure to a concrete, manageable microstep.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works collaboratively and builds on the client's ideas rather than imposing solutions. Frequent structured options make the interaction feel slightly counselor-framed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports the client's choice, flexibility, and self-guidance. There is no evidence of persuasion, coercion, or minimizing the client's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some of the client's own thinking, strategies, and ability statements. Evocation would be stronger with deeper exploration of values, confidence, and personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly conveys that the client is the expert in her own experience. The client's contributions substantially shape the direction and focus of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps the client generate concrete change-oriented statements and a manageable plan. The counselor could more consistently elicit deeper change talk such as desire, ability, reasons, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects sustain talk with acceptance and avoids arguing against it. The counselor could more consistently pivot away from sustain talk toward change talk, values, or ability.
