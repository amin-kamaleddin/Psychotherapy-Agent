# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with warmth that is genuine and individualized, never formulaic. There is not a single moment of judgment, labeling, or disapproval throughout the entire session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate and consistent empathic understanding that goes beyond surface-level paraphrase to capture the affective core of statements. Reflections are varied and used strategically, including deep attunement to linguistic nuance such as the emotional weight of the word 'just.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on asthma management, ensuring all questions and reflections connect back to the target behavior. However, the session feels slightly unresolved in consolidating the final insight into a concrete action plan or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a model of collaboration between equals, with all decisions co-constructed and the power balance genuinely equal. The psychiatrist never imposes an agenda and allows the client's associations to naturally shape the session's trajectory.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership of decisions and respects boundaries without any attempt to negotiate or reframe. Explicitly acknowledges that shutting down would have been fair, removing any implied obligation to perform in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within the client rather than providing external reasons for change, using the client's own language to explore barriers. The evocation is subtle, patient, and creates conditions for the client's internal wisdom to surface.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within the client, explicitly deferring to their knowledge of their own life and defining success by the client's criteria. The session is entirely shaped by the client's contributions, with the psychiatrist's role purely reflective and facilitative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes and amplifies change talk, including reframing 'I don't know' as evidence of movement rather than defeat. However, the evocation could be more systematic through scaling questions or further structured exploration of desire, ability, need, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill by fully validating it and taking it off the table entirely, which paradoxically opens space for change on other dimensions. Never engages in persuasion or counter-argument, consistently acknowledging and pivoting without creating a power struggle.
