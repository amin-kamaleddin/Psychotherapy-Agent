# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Anika's perspective without shaming or moralizing. Acceptance is strong but could be warmer with more explicit affirmation of her strengths and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Anika's ambivalence, practical concerns, and social meaning. The counselor could deepen emotional empathy by exploring the discomfort of saying no and the tension between belonging and protecting her basketball goals.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on Anika's vaping in relation to basketball, school, and social contexts. The conversation moves logically from ambivalence to specific, practical coping plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed with Anika's ideas shaping the focus and strategies. The counselor offers options collaboratively rather than prescribing, maintaining an equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently supports Anika's sense of choice and self-determination without pressure or directives. Her preferred low-pressure approach and pacing are respected throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions elicit some of Anika's own thinking, but evocation remains mostly logistical rather than motivational. The counselor could more deeply explore importance, confidence, discrepancy, or future-oriented values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Anika is consistently positioned as the expert on her own life, schedule, and goals. The counselor shares power and lets the client guide the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Anika generates useful change talk about playing better when clear and being able to handle the situation. The counselor could more consistently deepen and amplify this change talk with reflective expansion and values-based questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Anika and normalizes her desire to keep things low-key. However, some sustain talk, such as 'it's not a big deal,' could be more strategically softened using double-sided reflections or discrepancy work.
