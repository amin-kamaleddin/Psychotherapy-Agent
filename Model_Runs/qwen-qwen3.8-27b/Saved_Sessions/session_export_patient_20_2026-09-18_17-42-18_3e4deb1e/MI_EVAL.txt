# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; validates client's self-concept without judgment, labeling, or minimization. Warmth feels genuine and individualized rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture meaning and feeling beneath the words, including subtle emotional shifts (e.g., 'annoyed' vs. 'scared'). The psychiatrist tracks both content and nuance with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use and self-concept with a logical progression from minimization to exploring consequences. Session ends abruptly without a consolidation summary or collaborative direction-setting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative language throughout, actively incorporating the client's own framing ('autopilot,' 'put-together') into reflections. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian—the psychiatrist functions as a fellow explorer, not a director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and right to choose; never prescribes, advises, or pressures. When the client resists a framing, the psychiatrist immediately validates his preferred language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key question is designed to draw out the client's own reasoning rather than providing external reasons for change. The psychiatrist successfully moves the client from 'maybe I should' to connecting the behavior to his core self-image.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, never assuming authority over interpretation. The session is clearly shaped by the client's contributions—his language drives the direction of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes progressively stronger change talk through well-crafted questions, moving from 'maybe I should' to acknowledging the gap with his self-image. Could have gone further in explicitly naming and amplifying key change talk moments as summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by affirming the client's framing without reinforcing it, then gently exploring whether the pattern interferes with responsibilities. Could have more actively pivoted from the client's minimization toward exploring actual impact.
