# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Mateo’s pain and effort in a warm, nonjudgmental way. The stance is respectful but sometimes procedural rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both physical pain and emotional strain, though they often remain surface-level and formulaic. There is limited deeper exploration of feeling dismissed or powerless.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a concrete rehab barrier and an actionable step, making it practical and relevant. It does not consistently connect the small request to Mateo’s broader rehab goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mateo’s input is invited and his heat-pack choice is respected, but the psychiatrist often supplies options and scripts. This makes the interaction unevenly counselor-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for preferences, offers choices, and respects Mateo’s decision to ask without rehearsal. It could more explicitly affirm his ownership of both choice and outcome.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used to identify limiting factors and elicit a small commitment, but change talk is not deeply expanded. The therapist often shifts to problem-solving instead of drawing out reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some power sharing through choices and checking preferences, but the psychiatrist repeatedly provides ready-made scripts and solutions. Mateo’s expertise is not fully invited to lead the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mateo produces some change talk, such as agreeing to ask for the heat pack, and the psychiatrist affirms his agency. The therapist does not consistently deepen or expand this change talk through exploratory questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist validates discouragement and avoids arguing with sustain talk, keeping focus on a manageable step. However, it does not sufficiently transform feelings of dismissal and powerlessness into change talk.
