# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance with multiple affirmations and normalization of Liyana's experience. Warmth occasionally feels slightly scripted, and there is a missed opportunity to more deeply validate her adolescent vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with complex, accurate reflections capturing the emotional weight of the plateau and self-critical patterns. Some reflections remain surface-level and could delve deeper into underlying fears of being unworthy or unlovable.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session starts with a clear focus on weight loss but drifts into broader themes of self-worth without a clear plan to return to the target behavior. There is no clear momentum toward a specific change goal or action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as joint exploration with inclusive language and seeks Liyana's input. The collaboration could be deeper by more actively inviting Liyana to set the agenda or co-create goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently acknowledges Liyana's right to make her own choices and avoids imposing advice or prescribing specific behaviors. Could be stronger with more explicit affirmations of her freedom to choose, especially in moments of self-doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks open-ended questions but primarily focuses on emotional exploration rather than drawing out motivation for change. Few techniques such as importance rulers, confidence rulers, or values-based evocation are used to elicit change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and avoids an expert-driven approach. The partnership is somewhat one-directional at times, with the psychiatrist doing most of the guiding and Liyana's contributions not always shifting the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasionally evokes change talk by asking about goals, but does not consistently reinforce or strategically respond to Liyana's stated motivation. Missed opportunities to use scaling questions or build on her desire to fix things and stop feeling like garbage.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges Liyana's sustain talk about self-harshness as protection but does not consistently use strategic softening techniques like double-sided reflections or reframing. The belief that harshness is necessary is explored but not sufficiently balanced against change language or explored for its costs.
