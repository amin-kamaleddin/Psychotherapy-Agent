# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer is respectful and nonjudgmental but often shifts into problem-solving and compliance-oriented language. The stance is generally accepting, though not consistently deeply affirming of the client as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There are accurate reflections of the client's frustration, feeling overwhelmed, and need for simple trust. Some deeper emotional meanings, such as shame, powerlessness, or autonomy pain, are not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on morning punctuality and related barriers without drifting off-topic. However, the focus could be stronger by more consistently connecting practical steps to the client's values and intrinsic motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's ideas are incorporated into the plan, showing a collaborative approach. The balance still tilts toward the clinician guiding the structure through structured prompts and suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer supports choice and ownership by inviting the client to select a small step and respecting his preference to keep things simple. Repeated prompts and suggestions create a mild directive feel.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk and client-generated solutions are elicited through confidence scaling and practical questions. The interviewer also provides many suggestions directly, which limits deeper evocation of client-owned motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client is invited to contribute ideas and the plan reflects some of his preferences. The interviewer frequently takes on an expert or planning role, which weakens the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers some change-relevant statements, such as wanting a normal life and confidence increasing from 6 to 8. The session moves quickly into practical planning without consistently amplifying or deepening the client's reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer avoids arguing with or dismissing the client's concerns about being watched and checked. However, sustain talk is not consistently redirected toward intrinsic motivation or change.
