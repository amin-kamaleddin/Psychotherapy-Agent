# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids shaming Jason. Some reflections subtly normalize minimization and the warmth remains somewhat procedural.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures Jason's tension between being factual and protecting his social identity. The clinician does not consistently explore deeper emotions such as shame, fear, or belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused, but the focus is narrow and centered on the conduct hearing and self-presentation. It does not consistently guide the conversation toward alcohol-related behavior change or larger values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Jason's language and concerns rather than imposing an expert agenda. The session is still primarily therapist-guided rather than fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jason is repeatedly invited to choose his own words and stance without pressure to apologize or stop drinking. Some questions subtly steer toward a desired outcome or strategy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open, evocative questions are used without lecturing. The elicited content is often procedural or defensive rather than deeply change-oriented.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and invites Jason to think through his own position. The agenda remains fairly controlled by the psychiatrist around the conduct meeting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** A few change-oriented statements are elicited, such as focusing on school and not being a repeat offender. Change talk is not consistently amplified, deepened, or developed with DARN/discrepancy work.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jason's minimization is acknowledged but often reinforced rather than redirected. More double-sided reflections, rolling with resistance, and value-based discrepancy work would have strengthened this area.
