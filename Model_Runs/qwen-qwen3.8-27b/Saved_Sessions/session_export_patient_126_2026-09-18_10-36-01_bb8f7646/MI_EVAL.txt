# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout, with explicit validation of Thomas's perspective and character. Could reach a 5 by more explicitly acknowledging the emotional vulnerability in Thomas's father narrative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with reflections that capture emotional undercurrents rather than just content. The complex reflection naming Thomas's dual tension around his father's story and the reflection about 'the silence around it' demonstrate advanced attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target domain with a logical progression from minimization through the father narrative to personal warning signs. A mid-session collaborative summary or gentle narrowing would have tightened the thread further.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative dialogue with reflections offered as interpretations rather than assertions, leaving room for Thomas to refine. Could be more visible through explicit checks on direction, pace, and comfort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with repeated explicit affirmations of Thomas's right to choose and ownership of the process. Never pressures, implies obligation, or frames non-change as failure — models that inaction is a valid choice while keeping the space safe for self-directed exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A masterclass in evocation — every shift in Thomas's thinking is driven by his own words reflected back and questions inviting deeper self-exploration. The psychiatrist never provides medical information, statistics, or suggestions; the move from precontemplation to articulating specific warning signs is entirely self-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as joint exploration and explicitly positions Thomas as the expert on his own life. Power sharing is strong but could be more explicitly stated through agenda negotiation and invitations to redirect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out change talk from a precontemplation client, with Thomas articulating two specific personal warning signs by the end. A brief confidence or commitment question at the close would have further solidified the shift from contemplation to preparation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook softening — every instance of minimization is met with validation of the feeling behind it without reinforcing the content or offering counter-evidence. The sustain talk loses its power not through confrontation but through being fully heard, allowing it to naturally dissolve.
