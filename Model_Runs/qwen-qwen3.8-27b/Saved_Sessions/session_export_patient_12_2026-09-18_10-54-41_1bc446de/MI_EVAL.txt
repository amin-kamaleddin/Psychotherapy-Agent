### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance with strong validation of Amira's experience. While respectful throughout, the acceptance occasionally feels slightly scripted rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with nuanced reflections that capture cognitive content, emotional depth, and contextual factors simultaneously. The psychiatrist goes beyond surface-level reflection to identify underlying protective functions of Amira's behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with logically sequenced questions moving from exploration to evocation. The session lacks a clear consolidation or synthesis endpoint to tie themes together.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently inclusive language and building on Amira's contributions rather than imposing the psychiatrist's own. The dynamic remains somewhat one-directional, with the psychiatrist doing most of the questioning rather than co-constructing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional and natural autonomy support throughout, with the psychiatrist never imposing a narrative or pushing toward a decision. Explicitly honors Amira's reality and avoids any directive or controlling language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation that consistently elicits motivation from within Amira rather than providing external reasons for change. Successfully draws out meaningful change talk from a passive, low-motivation client through carefully designed open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance that positions Amira as the expert on her own experience, with explicit sharing of burden. The psychiatrist does not explicitly negotiate the agenda or ask Amira to set priorities, which would further strengthen the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits genuine change talk through open-ended questions about Amira's own vision and moments of 'light.' Could have done more to consolidate and amplify the change talk strategically to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with notable skill, particularly in acknowledging the reality of the 'wall' without arguing or dismissing. Could have more explicitly acknowledged the protective function of Amira's self-deprecation while still honoring embedded change talk.
