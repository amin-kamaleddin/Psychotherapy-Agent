# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, warm, nonjudgmental tone and affirms Elena’s efforts and values. Acceptance could be stronger by affirming her worth beyond successful completion of the plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflective listening about Elena’s goals and concerns. Empathy could deepen by exploring underlying emotions such as fear, frustration, or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on developing a specific, values-based self-care behavior plan. The conversation moves effectively from values to implementation without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the plan using Elena’s preferences and ideas. Collaboration is slightly limited by the clinician supplying many suggestions rather than consistently waiting for the client to generate next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is repeatedly invited to choose the anchor, timing, and check-in format. Some directive suggestions, such as specific implementation nudges, slightly reduce emphasis on full client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Elena’s own if-then plan, confidence level, and implementation ideas. Evocation could improve by more consistently eliciting the client’s own solutions before offering external strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Elena as the expert on her life and invites her to direct the work. Partnership is somewhat limited by the counselor-led introduction of multiple concrete strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena generates strong change talk, and the psychiatrist affirms and builds on it. The score is limited by occasional shifts into advising or structuring rather than amplifying the client’s own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reframes doubt into a flexible, self-respecting approach and reduces self-criticism. The session does not demonstrate a broad range of sustain-talk management skills, and a final concern about the plan becoming a “box to check” is not directly softened.
