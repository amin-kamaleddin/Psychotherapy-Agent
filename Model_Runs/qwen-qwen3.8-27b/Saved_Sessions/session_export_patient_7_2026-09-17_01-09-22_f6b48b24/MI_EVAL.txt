# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, nonjudgment, and validation of Amina’s need for space. Continued talking after she requests silence slightly undermines the felt experience of being accepted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Amina’s need for quiet, privacy, and autonomy. Empathy is somewhat limited by over-interpreting minimal responses beyond what the client offers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays appropriately focused on rapport, safety, quiet, and autonomy rather than pushing broader conflict. A clearer shared next step or minimal focus for future engagement is not established.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and frames the session as following Amina’s lead. Collaboration is weakened by therapist-driven talking after the client asks for silence, requiring correction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice and ownership are regularly affirmed, including acknowledgment that attendance may not have been her choice. Autonomy support is reduced by not consistently honoring the request for silence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some evocative attempts, such as asking what stability or “own space” would look like. These efforts are not developed into internal change motivation or skillfully followed up.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partnership language is present, with statements about following Amina’s lead and pace. The counselor’s continued talking after the client asks for quiet shows uneven power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Only limited attempts are made to elicit change talk, mainly early questions about stability and meaning of space. The therapist does not effectively elicit, affirm, amplify, or summarize client motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Amina’s resistance and validates her need for space. However, continued questioning and interpretation keep the session focused on disengagement rather than gently moving toward engagement.
