# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout. The psychiatrist validates Daniel's confusion without any trace of disappointment or corrective stance, and never labels the drinking negatively.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently capture identity-level meaning rather than just content, such as the dissonance between Daniel's health identity and the screening result. The empathy deepens as the session progresses, accurately naming the self-sabotage pattern without making it sound like a character flaw.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear logical arc from skepticism to acknowledgment, keeping alcohol use central while exploring related themes. The only gap is the absence of a concrete collaborative next step or follow-up plan at the close of the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as two people thinking together, with Daniel's contributions substantially shaping the direction. The psychiatrist uses inclusive language, follows wherever Daniel takes the conversation, and adopts his own terms like 'background noise' and 'ambient.'

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly and repeatedly supports Daniel's autonomy with phrases like 'no pressure,' 'you don't have to commit to anything,' and 'you don't have to decide what it means yet.' Information-gathering is clearly separated from decision-making, and the final question is an invitation rather than a directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight belongs to Daniel—he does the math, notices the heavier legs, identifies the self-sabotage pattern, coins 'background noise,' and arrives at the final acknowledgment. The psychiatrist's curiosity-driven questions are specifically designed to elicit Daniel's own thinking rather than impose it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Daniel as the expert on his own body and experience, explicitly resisting the expert role. The session is structured around Daniel's contributions shaping the direction, making it a genuine partnership rather than a performance of one.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniel moves organically from 'I don't really see what the problem is' to 'I need to stop pretending the form was wrong,' with each step evoked rather than imposed. The psychiatrist strategically amplifies change talk by reflecting it back with added weight and naming its significance without over-praising.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never confronted or argued with—instead it is absorbed, validated, and gently redirected. When Daniel minimizes the drinking as 'background noise,' the psychiatrist validates that framing and uses it to deepen exploration rather than push back.
