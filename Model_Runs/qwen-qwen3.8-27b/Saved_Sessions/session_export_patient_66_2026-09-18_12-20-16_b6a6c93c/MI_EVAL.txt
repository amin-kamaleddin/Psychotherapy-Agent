# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and non-judgmental throughout, even when the patient is resistant or dismissive. Multiple affirmations and validations are offered without labeling or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently go beyond paraphrasing to capture identity-level and meaning-level content (e.g., 'those are about who you are as a dad,' 'that's a different kind of loss'). The psychiatrist accurately names micro-moments of ambivalence with sophisticated attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking cessation with a logical progression through the session. The final third becomes somewhat exploratory without explicitly tying back to a concrete behavioral next step or forward momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses 'I'm noticing' language to invite confirmation rather than impose interpretation, and consistently builds on the patient's own ideas rather than redirecting. The interaction genuinely feels like two people thinking together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never uses directive language ('you should,' 'I recommend') and explicitly removes pressure to perform. Respects the patient's stated timeline without attempting to accelerate his process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every piece of change talk in the session was generated by the patient, not the psychiatrist. Questions are crafted to lead Kareem to discover his own competence and motivations rather than providing them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is consistently positioned with the patient through questions like 'What do you think?' and 'What's your instinct?' The psychiatrist never provides information or strategies, maintaining an equal power dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully creates conditions for the patient to generate his own change language through open questions and reflective summaries. The final question ('Is the break itself the point, or is it the smoke within the break?') directly elicits the session's most important insight.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated without argument ('You like smoking — that's real') and then explored deeper to help the patient move past it on his own terms. The psychiatrist never pushes back but creates space for the patient's own reframing to emerge.
