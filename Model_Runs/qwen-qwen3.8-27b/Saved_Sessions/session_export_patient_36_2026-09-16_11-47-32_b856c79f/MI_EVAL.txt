# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with frequent affirmations and minimal pressure. It falls short of a 5 because the affirmations are sometimes performance-linked and lack deeper unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows adequate perspective-taking, but reflections are mostly cognitive and practical. More complex reflections of ambivalence and underlying emotions would strengthen this area.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior of establishing a work boundary. The conversation moves clearly from values to a concrete plan with minimal topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist engages in collaborative problem-solving and incorporates Elena’s edits into the plan. The collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist still leads much of the content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites preferences and accepts Elena’s corrections, supporting client ownership. The score is limited by the amount of prescriptive scripts and solutions provided.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some client-generated motivation early in the session. Evocation becomes less consistent as the psychiatrist shifts toward providing solutions and implementation details.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Elena’s insight and allows her to shape key parts of the plan. The partnership is reduced by the psychiatrist’s frequent role as solution-provider.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes effective attempts to elicit change talk, especially early on. The session becomes overly solution-focused, limiting deeper client-generated motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Elena’s concerns and eventually supports a values-based frame. The response would be stronger with double-sided reflections that more fully soften sustain talk.
