# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with zero judgment. Validates uncertainty, affirms self-advocacy, and explicitly counters self-doubt multiple times.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic understanding with accurate reflections of emotional states and concerns. Slightly formulaic structure and less exploration of deeper emotional layers beneath the practical questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus on the target behavior throughout with clear momentum from uncertainty to concrete action plan. No drift or unfocused moments; pacing is appropriate.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates full collaboration with inclusive language ('let's,' 'with you,' 'together'). Builds on patient's existing knowledge and session direction is shaped by patient's questions and priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces patient's ownership of decisions and never prescribes an action. Even after providing critical safety information, immediately hands the decision back to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out patient's own motivations and reasoning through open questions. Somewhat constrained by the informational nature of the session, limiting space for deeper values-based evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance with explicit collaborative framing and invitations for patient to direct the conversation. Still retains primary information-giving role, so power dynamic is not fully equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out and reinforces multiple instances of change talk with affirming follow-ups. Session's narrow informational scope limits opportunity to develop broader change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
