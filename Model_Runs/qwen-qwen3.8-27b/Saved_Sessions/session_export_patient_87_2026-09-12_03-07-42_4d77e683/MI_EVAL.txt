# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes Marco's struggles without blame. Acceptance is solid but somewhat procedural, with less explicit affirmation of his effort or personal frustration.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture routine barriers and ambivalence about home habits. They remain mostly content-focused and miss deeper emotional nuances such as frustration, fear of failure, or feeling trapped.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on evening drinking and snacking as the target behavior. There is minor drift when broader options are introduced, but the discussion returns quickly to a practical step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco's preferences are respected, and he helps shape the plan. However, the psychiatrist frequently offers lists of suggestions, which can make the interaction feel less like equal collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choice is supported through scaling, permission-seeking, and respect for Marco's decisions. The repeated presentation of multiple options may feel subtly guided rather than fully client-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some motivation through past successes and confidence scaling. The work is somewhat superficial, with limited exploration of Marco's values and personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally treats Marco as the expert on his household and preferences. Offering several concrete choices can shift the interaction toward the clinician's agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited through questions about past successes and small realistic steps. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or reflect Marco's expressed capability afterward.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not met with argument, and concerns are normalized effectively. The psychiatrist could more consistently use double-sided reflections to hold ambivalence and widen change language.
