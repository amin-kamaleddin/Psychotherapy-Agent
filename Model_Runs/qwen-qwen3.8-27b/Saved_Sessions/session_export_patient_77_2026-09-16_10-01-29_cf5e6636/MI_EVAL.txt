# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor was warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, often normalizing Liyana’s struggle. Some language still centered on effort, weight, and scale progress, limiting unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor reflected frustration, exhaustion, and ambivalence accurately. It could go deeper into the shame, isolation, and self-image pain underneath Liyana’s statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused and moved from broad concerns to a concrete, organized plan. It was somewhat procedural and could better connect each small step back to Liyana’s broader values and goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor genuinely co-created the plan with Liyana, allowing her to choose the step, note wording, timing, and follow-up message. This was a strong, client-led collaborative process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently emphasized choice, avoided pressure, and respected Liyana’s privacy boundary. Her agency was strongly supported throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Readiness and confidence questions elicited some motivation, but the session focused more on behavior selection and logistics than deeper evocation. More open questions could have drawn out Liyana’s internal reasons and values more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invited input and let Liyana shape many parts of the plan. The session was collaborative, though still somewhat agenda-driven rather than fully co-led by the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited through evoking questions and scaling, but it was often labeled rather than expanded. There were missed opportunities to reflect, amplify, and deepen Liyana’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor responded to Liyana’s doubts without argument, normalizing forgetting and failure worries. It would be stronger with more strategic pivots from sustain talk into change talk.
