# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing Daniela's hesitation and validating even her self-critical moments without attempting to fix or minimize. No labeling, disapproval, or pressure was detected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with nuanced reflections that capture both content and emotion (e.g., 'that care would become a wall'). Reflections progressively deepen and go beyond surface-level understanding to name the exact psychological binds.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (disclosing diabetes to peers) throughout, with well-calibrated pacing that moves from exploration to a concrete plan. The session narrows effectively to a 'tiny, safe version,' appropriate for a contemplation-stage client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration between equals, with the psychiatrist using 'I' statements and inviting input at every juncture. Daniela's ideas drive the session forward while the psychiatrist's role is to help her articulate what's already there.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit and consistent autonomy support from the opening statement through the closing question, never implying that not disclosing is a failure. All options are framed as Daniela's to choose, removing pressure and obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within Daniela through skilled questioning rather than imposing external reasons for change. The final evocation ('What would a tiny, safe version look like?') produces the most concrete and client-generated plan of the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniela is consistently positioned as the expert on her own experience, with questions inviting her to set the agenda and provide her own plans. The psychiatrist never assumes expertise or prescribes, maintaining a co-creative dynamic throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk at multiple points by prompting Daniela to articulate her own solutions, evidence of capability, and concrete plans. Questions consistently draw out her motivations and abilities rather than providing them externally.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by acknowledging protective functions and gently exploring costs, but could have used double-sided reflections more explicitly when Daniela expresses strong fear (e.g., 'I can't unsee that'). The session still ends with Daniela articulating a concrete plan, showing effective navigation overall.
