# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout, with strong validations of Jordan's strengths. Slight interpretive edge with 'old story catching up' and could be deepened with more explicit affirmations of Jordan's character beyond physical health.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures minimization, ambivalence, and the grandmother connection. Occasionally stays at the cognitive level rather than fully exploring emotional undercurrents, such as the guilt Jordan briefly mentions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior with a clear progression from general health perspective to specific action planning. Never drifts off-topic and gently redirects toward the goal without being pushy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative interaction where Jordan's contributions substantially shape the session direction — the grandmother topic, imagery, and action plan were all client-generated. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with the psychiatrist acting as a facilitator and mirror.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Jordan's autonomy with no coercion, guilt-tripping, or implied obligation. Attributes the action plan to Jordan and validates his choices before gently exploring alternatives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful at drawing motivation from within Jordan rather than providing external reasons or facts. By the end of the session, all motivation and the action plan are entirely self-generated, which is the gold standard of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI partnership with collaborative language throughout and a clear opening statement framing the interaction as non-pushy. The psychiatrist never assumes the expert role and reflects Jordan's self-generated ideas back to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally skilled at evoking and deepening change talk, with a clear progression from minimization to a concrete self-generated action plan. Open questions consistently elicit client-generated change talk, and the session ends with Jordan generating his own plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without arguing or countering, using double-sided reflections to acknowledge and gently redirect. The phrase 'old story catching up' is slightly more directive than ideal; a purer double-sided reflection would be more aligned with MI principles.
