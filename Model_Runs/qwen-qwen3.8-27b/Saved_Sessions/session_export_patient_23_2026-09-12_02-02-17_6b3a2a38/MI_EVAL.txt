# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming tone throughout. However, some praise sounds evaluative and could feel more formulaic rather than fully individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are generally accurate and capture Claudia’s ambivalence well. The counselor could go deeper emotionally by exploring underlying feelings such as exhaustion, fear, shame, or the meaning of wine as self-soothing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on reducing evening wine use. The conversation moves clearly from exploring concerns to barriers, confidence, and a concrete bedtime plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Claudia meaningfully shapes the plan through her own choices. However, the counselor repeatedly offers structured options before fully inviting Claudia to lead with her own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for permission, offers choices, and avoids pressure or coercion. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly emphasizing Claudia’s ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and rulers help Claudia articulate concerns and strategies. The counselor could more consistently prioritize evoking Claudia’s own values and motivations before introducing suggestions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is generally collaborative and invites client input. The frequent introduction of multiple options can make the counselor seem like the primary problem-solver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Claudia expresses several reasons for change and her confidence increases. The counselor does not consistently amplify, summarize, or strengthen the client’s change talk before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing and uses balanced reflections. Sustain talk could be softened more effectively by reframing the underlying needs behind alcohol use in a way that preserves client values.
