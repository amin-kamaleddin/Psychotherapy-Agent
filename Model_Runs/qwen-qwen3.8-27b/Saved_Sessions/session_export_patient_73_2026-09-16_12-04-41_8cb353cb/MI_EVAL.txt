# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, nonjudgmental positive regard is evident throughout the session. The psychiatrist validates Linda’s feelings, courage, and mixed feelings without shaming or performance-based approval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and emotionally attuned, capturing both concerns about health/money and the social pull of smoking. The psychiatrist consistently summarizes Linda’s perspective rather than simply repeating facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on smoking cessation and moves logically from assessment to practical planning. There is little topic drift, with each step connected to actionable change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves as an equal partner, inviting Linda to choose, modify, and own the plan. The final plan is co-constructed rather than clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Linda is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, with options offered rather than directives. Her preferences are respected when she declines a script and chooses a simpler alternative.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and self-efficacy are drawn out from Linda rather than imposed by the psychiatrist. Questions and scaling help her connect the plan to her own goals, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power is shared effectively, with Linda’s perspective and choices substantially shaping the session. The psychiatrist offers options while affirming Linda’s problem-solving and authority over her own decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits and strengthens Linda’s change talk, including reasons, confidence, and concrete steps. Techniques such as importance/confidence rulers and small-step planning are well integrated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled respectfully and is not reinforced or argued against. There are a few missed opportunities to use deeper double-sided reflection to balance social smoking with health and financial goals.
