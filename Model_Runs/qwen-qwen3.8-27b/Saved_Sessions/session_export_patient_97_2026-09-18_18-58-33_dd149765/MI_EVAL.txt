# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with no labeling or disapproval directed at Mateo. Does not quite reach the depth of explicit, unconditional positive regard that would warrant a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and consistent reflective listening throughout, capturing nuance and emotional depth with remarkable precision. Consistently goes beyond surface-level paraphrasing to articulate underlying meaning and affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on target behavior with logical flow from general adjustment to concrete job-seeking actions. Transition to job topic is slightly quick, and the session could more explicitly tie each exploration back to the broader recidivism reduction goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint exploration and actively builds on Mateo's contributions to shape direction. Structure remains somewhat clinician-led, with the psychiatrist doing most of the talking and shaping the conversation flow.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Mateo's autonomy and ownership; never imposes advice or pressures toward specific action. Strong acknowledgment of the mandated nature of sessions while affirming that the space belongs to him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Mateo's own motivations with skillful evocative questions and never provides external reasons for change. Could be deeper by explicitly eliciting ability, importance, and commitment talk across all DARN-CAT categories.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance using inclusive language and invites Mateo's perspective throughout. Somewhat guided in practice—the psychiatrist still leads conversation structure without explicit power-sharing or agenda negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently evokes and effectively amplifies Mateo's change talk with affirming reflections and excellent evocative questions. Could go further in exploring ability talk and more explicitly eliciting commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk using double-sided reflections and reframes without reinforcing or arguing against it. The sustain talk about the record and repeated failures is not fully resolved or pivoted by session end.
