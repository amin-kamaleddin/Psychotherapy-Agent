# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard is consistent throughout—gently reframes self-judgment (e.g., 'I don't think you're being dramatic at all') without labeling or dismissing. Warmth is specific and attends to the particular emotional weight of Daniela's experience rather than offering generic reassurance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections capture emotional and relational nuance beyond surface content (e.g., 'the thing keeping you close to them is also the thing keeping a part of you out'). Accurately tracks the emotional arc from guardedness through vulnerability to insight without rushing key shifts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session progresses logically from opening space to identifying the secret, exploring ambivalence, naming the cost of hiding, and arriving at a concrete plan. Pacing is patient yet structured, maintaining focus on the disclosure decision without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is conducted as a genuine collaboration between equals, with the psychiatrist positioning herself as a reflective partner rather than an authority. The counselor explicitly states she wants to 'gently push back' and invites Daniela's perspective rather than imposing her own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces ownership and choice—'This is your space,' 'You don't have to know what I want you to say,' 'even with the maybe.' Never pressures for a decision or commitment; validates Daniela's own reasoning rather than offering external advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation for change comes from Daniela's own voice, drawn out through curiosity, silence, and precise reflections. The shift from 'I choose what to share' to 'I'm just hiding' is entirely Daniela's evolution, facilitated but not driven by the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Daniela as the expert on her own relationships ('You know them way better than I do'). The session's direction is shaped by Daniela's disclosures, and the counselor never prescribes, directs, or assumes the right answer.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully amplifies change talk by reflecting back Daniela's own progress ('You just went from I'm hiding to I think I could tell them in one breath'). Strategically redirects focus from doubt about others to Daniela's own knowledge and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with appropriate gentleness using dual-sided reflections that validate while opening space for change. Could be stronger at the peak moment by more explicitly exploring the growing cost of secrecy to actively tip the balance toward change.
