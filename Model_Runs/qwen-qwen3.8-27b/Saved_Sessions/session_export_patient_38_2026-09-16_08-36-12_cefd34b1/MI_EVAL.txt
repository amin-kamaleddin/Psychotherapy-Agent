# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, normalization, and nonjudgment while accepting Elena’s avoidance and fear. Some statements are slightly evaluative or direction-embedded, which keeps it from a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses accurate reflective listening to validate Elena’s fear and need for a simple next step. Deeper reflections on shame, body autonomy, or ambivalence were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the immediate concern of possible pregnancy and testing. It does not strongly guide the conversation toward the stated target of future contraceptive decision-making.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, adapts to Elena’s preferences, and lets her input shape the session. The plan is still largely clinician-generated rather than fully co-created in Elena’s own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly affirms Elena’s right to choose and respects her refusal of emergency contraception and clinic-based care. The detailed recommended plan makes the interaction slightly less client-owned.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initial open questions are present, but the session quickly shifts into medical information and problem-solving. There is limited exploration of Elena’s values, confidence, or intrinsic motivation around contraception.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites input on next steps. The role remains more information provider and plan-maker than a true partner in co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena offers some change talk about fear of recurrence and wanting to feel more careful, but the psychiatrist does not actively amplify or expand it. There is little use of readiness, confidence, or values-based MI questioning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing or reinforcing Elena’s resistance to pills, IUDs, and clinic care. The sustain talk is accepted, but not actively softened or redirected toward exploration of future prevention.
