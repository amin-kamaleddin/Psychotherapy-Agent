### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental with multiple affirmations of Amina's honesty and self-awareness. Occasionally reads as slightly scripted rather than deeply individualized in its positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and nuanced reflective listening that names emotional undercurrents Amina hadn't articulated, such as the dissonance between experiencing a good morning and not feeling she earned it. Consistently tracks and mirrors Amina's shifting emotional states.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical arc from exploration through ambivalence to small action steps. Could benefit from more explicit consolidation at key transition points to reinforce the direction being built.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session reads as a genuine dialogue between equals rather than a clinician-led interview. The psychiatrist builds on Amina's contributions in real-time and treats her ideas as valid regardless of feasibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Amina's ownership through exploratory questions that never pressure toward a specific answer. The 'design problem' reframe removes shame while the final question explicitly protects Amina's autonomy by building in a safety condition.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All significant change talk originates from Amina herself, prompted by well-crafted open questions that draw out her own values and insights. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change, instead using subtle, non-pressuring evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with open-ended questions positioning Amina as the expert on her own life. Could be strengthened by more explicit agenda negotiation and co-construction of session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk, affirming Amina's emerging insights and validating her self-awareness. Could push further by explicitly exploring all four DARC categories (Desire, Ability, Reasons, Commitment) more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively without argument or dismissal, and the 'design problem, not willpower' reframe is a particularly effective softening move. Could incorporate more double-sided reflections to hold ambivalence without collapsing it.
