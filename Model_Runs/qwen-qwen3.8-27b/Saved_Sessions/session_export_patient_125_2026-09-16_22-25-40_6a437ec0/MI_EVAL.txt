# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects the client without judgment and affirms his effort. However, the warmth remains somewhat conditional and lacks explicit unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's pressures around phone access, family, release, and the officer are accurately reflected. However, the reflections remain surface-level and miss deeper affect such as hurt, fear, or invisibility.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on compliance, release, phone access, and family connection. However, it becomes circular and needs stronger summarization to move toward a clearer client-driven plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on client-generated ideas such as a letter and staying calm. However, the psychiatrist still drives much of the session through repeated "signal" framing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids advice, pressure, and coercion while preserving client choice. However, it would be stronger with more explicit affirmations that the client is in charge and fewer compliance-sounding probes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions are used to explore next steps. However, evocation remains procedural rather than motivational, with limited exploration of values, confidence, self-efficacy, or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is treated as the expert on his own situation and is invited to contribute perspective. However, repeated focus on a "concrete signal" can feel agenda-driven rather than fully co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client's compliance and desire to get home are acknowledged. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify this change talk through stronger summaries, confidence, or commitment work.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with the client's fairness and "test" complaints. However, these views are not consistently redirected into change-focused values or agency using double-sided reflections.
