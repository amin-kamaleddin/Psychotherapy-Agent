# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, nonjudgmental, and low-pressure. Acceptance would be stronger if it felt more personally warm and less script-like.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflections are mostly factual rather than deeply empathic. The counselor misses opportunities to reflect the client’s ambivalence and reluctance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session lacks clear MI direction toward the alcohol-use referral. It drifts through general questions, safety checks, and tasks without a coherent target focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and menus, giving the interaction a collaborative surface. However, the counselor remains the primary driver of the session’s content and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Optional, low-pressure language and permission to stop are clear strengths. Deeper autonomy support would more explicitly link choices to the client’s values and ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initial open questions show evocative intent but are not followed up or deepened. The counselor mostly provides information and tasks rather than eliciting the client’s own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor partially supports partnership by allowing the client to choose and leave. The power balance remains uneven because the agenda is largely counselor-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Attempts to evoke change talk are rare and weak, with little follow-up after brief answers. The counselor does not effectively elicit or expand the client’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing or pressuring when the client resists or wants to leave. However, sustain talk is not skillfully redirected into change talk using double-sided reflections or invitations.
