# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes the client's struggle with smoking and stress. Acceptance could be more individualized by explicitly affirming her worth as a parent/person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence, needs, and the costs of smoking. Empathy could be deepened by more explicitly reflecting fear, vulnerability, or loss of control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking as a stress-coping behavior and progressively narrows to specific triggers. Direction would be stronger with periodic summarization and a clear collaboratively chosen next step toward cessation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites the client's ideas. Collaboration would be more equal if the client had more opportunity to steer agenda, pace, or next focus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is consistent and strong, emphasizing the client's pace, choice, and ownership of decision-making. The final question reinforces that she remains in control of whether to light up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes the client's own thinking rather than giving advice. Evocation could be stronger by drawing out deeper values, hopes for her son, or self-identity related to quitting.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the client as the expert on her life and avoids an authoritarian stance. Partnership could be enhanced by more explicit power sharing, such as letting the client choose the next focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several questions invite change-oriented thinking, but the session does not consistently amplify the client's own change talk. More use of importance/confidence rulers or summaries of reasons for change would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing, which is appropriate. The psychiatrist could better soften sustain talk by using double-sided reflections and value-based discrepancy tied to her role as a parent.
