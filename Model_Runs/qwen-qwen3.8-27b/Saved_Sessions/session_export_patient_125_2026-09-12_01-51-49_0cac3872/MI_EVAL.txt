# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor shows basic respect and validation, such as listening and naming Tariq's frustration. However, acceptance becomes inconsistent when the counselor shifts into corrective scripts and behavioral instructions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflects several emotions, including anger, fear, and unfairness, but often stays surface-level and repetitive. Important disclosures about missing his mother's voice and feeling personally targeted are missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stays mostly on the immediate issue of phone access and release concerns, but it lacks a clear MI target behavior. The focus often drifts into procedural problem-solving rather than a focused change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor asks for input and offers options, which shows some collaboration, but the interaction remains largely expert-driven. Tariq's stated preferences and concerns do not substantially shape the direction of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor occasionally acknowledges choice but frequently uses directive language and prepackaged scripts. This undermines autonomy, especially after Tariq explicitly rejects homework, scripts, and practice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Evocation is minimal, with only a brief initial attempt to elicit Tariq's goals. The counselor mostly provides external advice and plans instead of exploring Tariq's own motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and asks what would help, but still positions himself as the solution provider. Tariq is not consistently treated as the expert on his own values, needs, and preferred next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Tariq offers some change-oriented statements, such as wanting to get out clean and follow rules, but the counselor does not affirm or develop them. The session moves too quickly into advice and problem-solving to strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor does not reinforce sustain talk, but also does not effectively soften or redirect it. The focus remains on the officer and system rather than helping Tariq reconcile his frustration with his own goals.
