# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful and nonjudgmental stance with clear affirmation of Marcus's efforts. Warmth is strong but sometimes professional and formulaic, so it does not fully reach the highest level.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflects practical concerns and ambivalence accurately, showing adequate perspective-taking. However, reflections remain mostly cognitive and do not consistently explore deeper feelings such as frustration, shame, or fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on the target behavior: increasing short, practical physical activity. The discussion moves clearly from options to a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds the plan with Marcus rather than prescribing it. Marcus's choices substantially shape the final activity, tracking, and follow-up plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices, asks for preferences, and respects Marcus's boundaries in setting and describing the plan. Some parts remain mildly directive, especially when pushing toward immediate commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling questions and choice prompts effectively elicit Marcus's motivation and confidence. Evocation is useful but could be stronger by connecting the plan more deeply to personal values and family meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input, offers options, and avoids a purely expert-driven approach. The session is generally collaborative, though the structured agenda and rapid question sequence can feel slightly counselor-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marcus generates clear statements of desire, ability, and commitment, and the psychiatrist reinforces them well. Change talk could be deepened by exploring what success means to him beyond the practical plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes barriers and avoids arguing with Marcus's reasons for maintaining the status quo. Sustain talk is handled effectively, though with less use of advanced MI techniques such as double-sided reflections.
