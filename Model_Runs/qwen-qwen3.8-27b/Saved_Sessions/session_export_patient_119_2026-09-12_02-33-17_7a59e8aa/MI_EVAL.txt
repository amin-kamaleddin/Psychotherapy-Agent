# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance with frequent affirmations. However, the warmth remains somewhat formulaic and lacks deeper, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is generally strong, with accurate summaries of the client’s ambivalence and situation. At times, reflections are repetitive and do not fully explore the deeper emotional pain or meaning behind the client’s struggle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is a general focus on the client’s concerns, but the session sometimes lacks consistent strategic momentum toward change. More purposeful summarization and goal-focused movement would strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates excellent collaboration by consistently framing the work as a shared process. The conversation and plan are co-constructed rather than prescribed, reflecting high-level MI collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client’s choice and self-direction are clearly respected throughout the session. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming the client’s ownership of both successes and setbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open-ended questions and reflections, but evocation remains somewhat superficial. There are missed opportunities to draw out deeper values, confidence, and change talk from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive, client-centered language and invites input consistently. However, the partnership feels somewhat superficial because explicit power-sharing and co-creation are limited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is occasionally elicited, but not consistently reinforced or deepened. The psychiatrist could more strategically amplify the client’s motivations and explore confidence and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without being reinforced, but softening strategies are limited. More strategic redirection, double-sided reflections, or validation-and-pivot responses would be beneficial.
