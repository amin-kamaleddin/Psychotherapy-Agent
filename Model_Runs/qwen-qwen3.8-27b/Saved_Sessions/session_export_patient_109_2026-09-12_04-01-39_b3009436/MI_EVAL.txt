# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, warm, nonjudgmental stance and adapts language to support rapport. The main limitation is that the warmth can feel formulaic and does not fully affirm Rafael's worth independent of progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses accurate reflections that capture Rafael's ambivalence, frustration, and barriers. The main gap is that deeper emotions, especially the final statement about self-doubt and lack of control, are not sufficiently reflected or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on returning to the gym and moves efficiently toward a concrete, realistic behavior plan. The counselor consistently ties planning back to the target behavior without letting the session drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is strongly co-constructed, with Rafael's choices shaping the plan, backup, and message. The counselor functions as an equal partner rather than an expert-led planner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly invites choice, offers options rather than commands, and supports a nonpunitive stance. However, frequent scripts, scaling questions, and planning structures can subtly position the counselor as directing the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively draws out Rafael's own motivations and solutions using rulers, open questions, and small-step prompts. The process becomes less evocative when the counselor supplies several concrete scripts and planning options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally treats Rafael as the expert and invites his preferences throughout. Repeatedly supplying options and scripts can subtly shift the counselor toward the role of planner or expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rafael expresses clear desire, ability, and need to change, and the counselor supports this through affirmations and client-generated ideas. The counselor could more consistently amplify and expand Rafael's change talk, especially when he expresses doubt.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with or reinforcing Rafael's sustain talk and maintains a supportive stance. However, many sustain-talk statements are treated mainly as practical obstacles rather than ambivalence to reframe or pivot toward change.
