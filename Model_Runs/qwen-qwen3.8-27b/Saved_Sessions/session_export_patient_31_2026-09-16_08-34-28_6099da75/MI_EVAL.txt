# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated David’s ambivalence and limited NRT trial. The approach was somewhat procedural rather than deeply person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and helpful but mostly cognitive and logistical rather than emotional. Important feelings about stress, partner concerns, and image worries were not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on a practical two-week NRT trial for smoking reduction. It was somewhat plan-driven rather than strongly motivation-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adapted to some client preferences, such as patch over gum and a smaller reduction goal. However, the plan remained largely clinician-generated before the client fully co-constructed it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supported autonomy by emphasizing choice, avoiding coercion, and respecting David’s “not quitting” frame. A fully 5-level performance would more consistently emphasize client ownership of every step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk was elicited through readiness and doability questions, but it was not deeply explored. The session moved too quickly into practical planning before fully evoking motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited input on NRT options and start time, creating some collaborative tone. Nonetheless, the psychiatrist remained the primary expert and plan generator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David offered some willingness to try the patch and start tomorrow, which was acknowledged. The psychiatrist did not consistently amplify or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided confrontation and reframed the work as a “two-week test without a quit commitment.” It did not fully use advanced MI strategies to redirect sustain talk toward possible change.
