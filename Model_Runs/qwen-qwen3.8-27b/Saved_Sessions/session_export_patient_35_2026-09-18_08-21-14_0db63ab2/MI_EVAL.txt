# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout the session, directly addressing Elena's shame without lecturing. Affirmations follow a somewhat predictable pattern (reflect → normalize → affirm) that, while effective, lacks the spontaneity and individualized depth of a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture specific emotional states, including the self-protective function of giving up. Consistently tracks Elena's internal experience rather than the medical facts, demonstrating sophisticated complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking cessation with a logical arc from rapport through ambivalence exploration to trigger identification. The session ends somewhat abruptly at the trigger identification stage without a clear summary or collaboratively identified next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative language and invitational stance, but reads more as a skilled facilitator guiding a client than two equal partners exploring together. Could be deeper with more explicit power-sharing and inviting Elena to challenge or redirect the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent autonomy support, explicitly rejecting a directive stance and positioning Elena as the agent of her own change. Never uses 'should' or prescriptive language; consistently places decision-making in Elena's court.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook evocation at the highest level — consistently uses questions and reflections to draw out Elena's own motivations rather than providing reasons for change. The final question about triggers leads Elena to self-identify her own pattern without any suggestion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance with consistent invitational language and positioning Elena as the expert on her own life. Could be deeper with more explicit agenda co-creation and sharing of the psychiatrist's own perspective to create true equality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through amplification, reframing, and eliciting commitment language, and skillfully reflects change talk back. Could have more explicitly explored Elena's values to deepen change talk and develop her confidence in ability to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well with double-sided reflections and reframing that validates effort while opening space. Sometimes stays in the sustain talk reflection without clearly guiding momentum toward change.
