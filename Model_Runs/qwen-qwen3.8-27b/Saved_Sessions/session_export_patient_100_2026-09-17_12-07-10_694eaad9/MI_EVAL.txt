# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor consistently communicates warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard without judgment, labels, or disappointment. Maya's vulnerable disclosures about peer pressure and self-doubt are met with genuine validation and normalization throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond surface-level to capture the emotional texture and underlying meaning of Maya's ambivalence, such as naming the discomfort in the gap between her social pull and her authentic self. The counselor demonstrates genuine understanding of a teenager's social world with specific, nuanced reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session follows a clear, logical progression from understanding context through exploring ambivalence, identifying barriers, envisioning alternatives, and developing a concrete action plan. The counselor never allows the session to drift and maintains tight focus on the target behavior with well-calibrated pacing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is structured as a joint exploration with language like 'I'm wondering with you' and 'I'm curious' reinforcing an egalitarian partnership. Session direction is shaped by Maya's responses rather than a predetermined agenda, with the counselor pivoting to follow her emerging insights.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor never uses 'should,' 'need to,' or 'have to,' and never implies that not drinking is the only acceptable outcome. The entire action plan is Maya's own creation, with the counselor merely asking the right questions to help her articulate it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is designed to help Maya access her own motivations rather than providing external reasons for change. By the end, Maya has developed a specific, personalized plan that emerged entirely from her own thinking, guided by the counselor's evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Maya, acting as a skilled facilitator who helps her access her own knowledge. The power dynamic is clearly egalitarian — Maya generates all ideas, plans, and solutions while the counselor asks and reflects.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor's questions progressively move Maya deeper into her own change language, eliciting abundant and specific change talk throughout the session. Change talk is affirmatively amplified with responses like 'That's actually a really specific and real idea' and 'You're basically writing your own script.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled through double-sided reflections, reframing fears as assumptions to be examined, and separating the fear of social exclusion from the behavior itself. The counselor never argues against Maya's fears but helps her examine them, leading to her own realization that they may be untested assumptions.
