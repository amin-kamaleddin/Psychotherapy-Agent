# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect, warmth, and validation are shown, with normalization of discomfort and permission to be imperfect. Acceptance is strong but slightly task-focused rather than deeply emotionally attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and capture key feelings, such as being 'exposed but useful' and frustration with tiredness undermining values. The empathy could go deeper into underlying fear, guilt, pressure, or exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on closing the gap between values and daily actions. Each question moves the conversation toward specific, value-aligned behaviors and success indicators.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is highly collaborative, with the plan co-constructed around the client's values and ideas. Client contributions actively shape the next steps and final plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supports choice by asking what the client is willing to try and respecting her preferences. Language such as 'permission to be imperfect' reinforces ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the client's values, motivation, confidence, and practical ideas. However, introducing some solutions before the client fully generates them slightly reduces pure evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client's ideas strongly shape the session, and the clinician follows her contributions without imposing an agenda. The final plan is co-constructed and reflects shared ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes change talk and strengthens the client's motivation and concrete plans. Some leading suggestions slightly reduce the amount of client-generated change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers and sustain talk are effectively normalized and reframed into workable, value-congruent actions. More explicit double-sided reflections would further strengthen the pivot from ambivalence to change.
