# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently expresses unconditional positive regard and normalizes Hana's fear without judgment. Statements like 'completely understandable' and 'no-blame space' create a safe, accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects Hana's ambivalence and bodily experience well, such as feeling caught between fear and relief and a tight stomach. Could go deeper into the emotional meaning of possible failure or judgment to reach a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains steady focus on the target behavior of taking a concrete exam-related step. The conversation moves from anxiety to a specific, achievable plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructs the plan by offering options and then adapting to Hana's choices, such as looking at dates instead of booking. The amount of therapist-generated techniques slightly limits full collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly affirms Hana's choice, control, and right to stop, including permission to close the tab without judgment. Language remains permissive rather than pressuring throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses helpful prompts about success, small steps, and confidence to evoke some motivation. However, it does not consistently draw out deeper values, past strengths, or personal meaning behind change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects Hana's expertise and preferences, asking how she wants to be supported and allowing her to set details. The frequent provision of specific structures keeps it from being fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits relevant change talk such as looking at dates, trying one minute, and using a reminder. Misses opportunities to amplify or expand these statements before shifting to practical planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges fears of freezing, failing, and being judged without challenging them, and reframes closing the tab as useful information. Could use more double-sided reflections or deeper reframing for a top score.
