### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental, and affirming throughout the session. The psychiatrist validates Richard's honesty and minimization without any hint of frustration or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate and nuanced reflections, particularly the all-or-nothing pattern and the emotional weight of the retirement visualization. Could go deeper into the underlying shame of repeated failure and fear of letting his wife down.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful arc from ambivalence through values, barriers, strategy, and commitment. The medication effectiveness question was handled appropriately but slightly disrupted the session's flow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equitable power balance with Richard's ideas shaping the session at every turn. The psychiatrist serves as a reflective partner rather than a problem-solver, using his language and building on his contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** No pressure or obligation at any point; every strategy is Richard's own. Language consistently reinforces ownership ('you're the one who built that, not me') and frames choices as his.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions draw out Richard's own values-based motivation rather than providing reasons to change. The psychiatrist never tells Richard why he should change; instead, she creates conditions for his motivation to emerge naturally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions herself as a collaborator, using invitational language ('I'm curious,' 'can I ask'). Appropriately acknowledges her limits when redirecting the medication question to his doctor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertly catches, names, and reinforces Richard's change talk throughout the session. Highlights his shift from 'I can't do this' to 'I need a different way to do this' as a meaningful progression.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles minimization and 'I always quit' gently without challenging or arguing, reframing barriers into solvable problems. The session contained relatively little sustain talk to fully evaluate this criterion.
