# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor showed respect, patience, and nonjudgment toward Kieran's disengagement. Acceptance could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Kieran's worth and right to struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections captured Kieran's monotony, compliance, and exhaustion accurately. Some reflections were repetitive and did not fully explore deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor stayed connected to Kieran's main issue of mandated attendance and disengagement. The session lacked a clear target, plan, or consistent momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor used collaborative language, asked for preferences, and avoided an authoritarian stance. Active co-creation of a shared direction was limited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently supported Kieran's freedom of choice and non-coercion. Autonomy support was explicit, repeated, and central to the interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor made some open-ended and evocative invitations to explore Kieran's experience. Evocation remained superficial and did not fully elicit values, goals, or motivation for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a collaborative, non-expert stance and invited Kieran's input. The partnership was somewhat passive rather than actively co-creating a session focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were limited attempts to invite change talk or explore desired change. The session remained anchored in sustain talk and procedural compliance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor avoided arguing with Kieran's sustain talk but did not consistently redirect or reframe it. The session risks reinforcing the status quo by remaining in complaint and resignation.
