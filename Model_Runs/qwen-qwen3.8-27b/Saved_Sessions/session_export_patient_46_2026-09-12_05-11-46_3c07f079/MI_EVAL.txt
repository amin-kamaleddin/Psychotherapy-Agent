# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepts Ethan’s priorities and limits. The occasional casual language and lack of deeper affirmation keep it below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provides accurate and adequate reflections of Ethan’s concerns and values. However, the empathy remains mostly behavioral and does not fully explore the emotional underpinnings of his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing morning sickness while preserving the social experience. The focus occasionally expands into broader health advice, but overall direction is solid.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Ethan’s preferences and incorporates them into the plan, but the interaction is often counselor-led. The psychiatrist is the primary source of ideas and structure, limiting the sense of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly supports Ethan’s sense of choice and respects his stated limits. It could be stronger with fewer prescriptive ideas and more client-generated options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some client-generated motivation and concrete planning steps. However, frequent provision of expert content and ready-made solutions makes evocation intermittent rather than consistently client-led.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Ethan’s input and adapts to his constraints, showing some recognition of his expertise. The session still feels more counselor-guided than truly co-created collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses questions to evoke change talk and Ethan offers several practical change-related statements. The session focuses more on implementation than consistently amplifying deeper change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not confront or argue with Ethan’s sustain talk and accepts his limits. Responses are mostly neutral rather than strategically using double-sided reflections or discrepancy development.
