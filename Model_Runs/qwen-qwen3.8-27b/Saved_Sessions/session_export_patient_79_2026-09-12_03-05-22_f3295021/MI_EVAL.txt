# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and shows positive regard without arguing or shaming. The acceptance is somewhat formulaic and procedural rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good reflective listening to accurately capture frustration, overwhelm, and feelings of unfairness. Reflections are somewhat repetitive and do not fully explore deeper emotions or values underlying the resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on asthma management, medication use, refills, and communication with the doctor. The session lacks momentum and does not narrow enough to a clear client-driven next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and avoids a purely authoritarian stance, but the psychiatrist supplies much of the content. The interaction is more counselor-guided than genuinely co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly affirms choice, control, and the client’s right to stop at any time. The repeated offering of options after refusal subtly undermines the message that the client is fully in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some evocative opening and exploratory questions, but these are not sustained. Later questions are often menu-based rather than drawing out the client’s own motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and invites the client to choose among options. The psychiatrist’s frequent suggestions make the session feel more counselor-directed than a true equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Briefly affirms what the client is already doing, but does not develop or amplify change talk. Misses opportunities to explore importance, confidence, values, or future consequences.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally avoids arguing and acknowledges the client’s resistance respectfully. Lacks more advanced softening strategies, such as double-sided reflections or connecting resistance to change.
