# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating tone. It is not a 5 because the warmth is sometimes generic and does not fully individualize unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening effectively captures Jordan’s ambivalence and emotional burden. It falls short of a 5 by not consistently reflecting deeper layers such as shame, irritation, self-criticism, or exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on financial stress, feeling stuck, and wanting relief. It becomes somewhat circular and does not clearly move toward a specific target behavior or structured agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and offers options rather than prescriptions. It is not a 5 because Jordan’s input does not fully shape the session structure or co-create a plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions and non-directive language consistently support Jordan’s autonomy. The psychiatrist could more explicitly affirm Jordan’s freedom to choose and ownership of any next step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids giving reasons for change and attempts to elicit Jordan’s own motivations. Evocation remains superficial because it does not consistently draw out values, strengths, exceptions, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates a collaborative stance and positions Jordan as the expert on his own life. It does not reach a 5 because there is limited explicit evidence of Jordan actively shaping the agenda or direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects and invites some change talk, such as wanting less stuckness and a break. It does not consistently deepen change talk by connecting it to values, self-efficacy, or past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by acknowledging hopelessness without arguing or reinforcing it. It is not a 5 because the redirect toward agency, exceptions, or possibility is not consistently strong enough.
