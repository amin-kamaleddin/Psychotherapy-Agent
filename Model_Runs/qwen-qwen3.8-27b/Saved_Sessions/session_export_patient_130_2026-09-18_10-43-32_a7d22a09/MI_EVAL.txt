# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently nonjudgmental, validating tone, appropriately honoring Viktor's self-perception without challenging his denial. Occasionally tips into over-validation that subtly reinforces minimization (e.g., normalizing 'just feeling less tired' as the sole goal).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with nuanced capture of Viktor's emotional states (pride, defensiveness, ambivalence, social anxiety). Could reach a 5 by exploring deeper unspoken undercurrents such as quiet shame about being sent by his wife or the grief in 'I'm missing something I can't name.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical arc from acknowledging experience through exploring mornings to generating a concrete plan, with well-anchored questions on the target behavior. Would benefit from a brief mid-session summary and a clearer closing that explicitly ties back to the agreed-upon plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed conversation where Viktor's contributions shape the session's direction, with collaborative language throughout. Could be elevated by more explicitly acknowledging Viktor's expertise in his own life and sharing more of the clinician's own process to model equality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout—never imposes recommendations, consistently frames suggestions as questions or possibilities, and directly addresses Viktor's resistance to feeling controlled. The intervention reframing the plan as 'a quiet decision that only you know about' is particularly powerful and deeply resonant.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI evocation—consistently elicits Viktor's own motivations and solutions rather than providing external reasons for change. The reflection of Viktor's own words ('If I'm just sitting at home, I'm fine') as evidence against total loss of control is a standout moment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and positions herself as a guide rather than an expert. Could be strengthened by more explicit power-sharing language and negotiating the agenda or checking in on Viktor's preferred direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk, moving Viktor from 'I don't know' to articulating a concrete, self-owned plan. Could push further into confidence/ability language and consolidate Viktor's own change talk in a summary near the end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by reframing without arguing (e.g., 'you're not an addict, you're a guy who's tired') and helping Viktor see concerns as narrower than they feel. Would benefit from more active double-sided reflections on the social pressure sustain talk.
