# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated respect, nonjudgment, and validation of Amina’s circumstances. Acceptance could be deeper by expressing unconditional positive regard independent of her plans or outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used strong reflective listening and captured ambivalence, emotion, and physical experience well. Empathy was sometimes inferential and missed the final opportunity to validate Amina’s fear of sounding needy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on enabling Amina to attend job interviews despite childcare responsibilities. It moved coherently from barrier identification to a concrete, self-generated action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaved as an equal partner, with Amina’s ideas substantially shaping the session. Her contributions were repeatedly affirmed and built upon rather than replaced by advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently invited Amina’s perspective and avoided pressure to act. Autonomy support could be strengthened with more explicit statements such as “This is your decision” or “There is no pressure.”

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited motivation, solutions, and planning from Amina rather than providing reasons for change. Amina generated the plan, backup options, wording, and behavioral cue with minimal instruction.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treated Amina as the expert on her life and invited her to direct the conversation. Some mildly suggestive questions slightly reduced the impression that all ideas emerged from Amina.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicited statements of ability, intention, and planning. Change talk could be deepened by explicitly exploring confidence, commitment, and confidence-building steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalized concerns and redirected barriers into manageable action without arguing or dismissing. Sustain talk could be further softened with value-based pivots and double-sided reflections.
