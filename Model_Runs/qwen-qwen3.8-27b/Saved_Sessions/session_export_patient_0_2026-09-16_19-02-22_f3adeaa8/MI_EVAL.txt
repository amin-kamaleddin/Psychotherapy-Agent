# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and avoids moralizing about meth use. Acceptance is somewhat formulaic and does not deeply normalize Adrian’s fear of being labeled or exposed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective and double-sided listening accurately capture Adrian’s ambivalence and emotional cues. Empathy remains somewhat surface-level and does not fully explore deeper shame, fear, or loss of face.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on methamphetamine use, infection risk, and safer weekend behavior. The target behavior, such as safer use versus reduced use, is not explicitly clarified early.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor works as an equal partner and co-constructs a plan around Adrian’s own ideas. Adrian’s suggestions directly shape the final sleep check-in and casual text strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Adrian’s choices by avoiding pressure, advice, or demands to stop. It could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Adrian’s freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions draw out Adrian’s motivations, values, and practical solutions. Evocation is more procedural than motivational and does not deeply explore importance, confidence, or readiness for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently treats Adrian as the expert on his own life and lets his ideas guide the session. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicit negotiation of the target behavior.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some change-oriented ideas, especially around safer use and avoiding the hospital. However, the counselor does not consistently amplify or deepen change talk through strategic reflection.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Adrian’s sustain talk and uses balanced reflections. The session sometimes follows sustain talk into harm-reduction planning rather than strategically reducing its weight.
