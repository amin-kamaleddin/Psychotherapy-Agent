# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, including directly countering Jason's fear of being labeled and normalizing ambivalence without judgment. The psychiatrist never escalates tone or pressure, consistently validating Jason's right to his perspective even when dismissive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with skillful double-sided reflections that accurately capture Jason's ambivalence, such as the 'turn it off' metaphor exploration. However, some reflections stay at the cognitive level rather than fully accessing the emotional undercurrent of his defensiveness and fear of judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior while allowing the conversation to breathe, following a logical arc from safety-establishment to value exploration to alternative evocation. The session ends somewhat open-endedly without a collaboratively identified focus or concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance treating the conversation as a joint exploration between equals, consistently building on Jason's own language rather than imposing a framework. The session direction is genuinely shaped by Jason's contributions, with a truly equal power dynamic throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Jason's ownership and freedom of choice, explicitly stating 'you don't have to fix anything tonight' and never using directive language like 'you should' or 'you need to.' All questions are framed as invitations, communicating that any change is entirely Jason's decision at his own pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Jason's own motivations through well-crafted open questions and strategic amplification of his change talk without ever providing reasons for change. Could have been strengthened with explicit scaling or confidence-building questions to further develop his own change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Jason, never assuming authority or providing unsolicited expert opinions. Questions are framed as invitations for his own thinking, and the conversation moves in directions Jason introduces rather than a predetermined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits natural change talk from Jason through questions about values, future orientation, and discrepancy, then strategically amplifies it by reflecting it back with emphasis. Could have further developed change talk through explicit readiness or confidence scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by never arguing or confronting, using rolling-with-resistance moves like 'I'm not going to argue with that' and double-sided reflections. Could have more actively and strategically redirected key sustain talk (e.g., 'I'll be 21 in a month') toward change rather than simply not challenging it.
