# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental throughout. Acceptance is strong but remains somewhat task-focused and could include deeper, warmer affirmation of uncertainty and self-doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates good reflective listening and accurate understanding of the client’s concerns. Empathy could be deeper by more directly reflecting fear, shame, and anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on diabetes self-management, especially blood glucose monitoring. The conversation moves clearly from broad overwhelm to concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates effectively by building the plan around the client’s own ideas. Marcus is treated as the expert on his schedule, preferences, and practical needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids directive pressure and supports client ownership through open questions. Autonomy support could be stronger with more explicit affirmation of choice, revision, and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes practical solutions, such as timing, safe range, and scripts. Evocation could be deeper by connecting the plan to values, confidence, and intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows the client’s lead and invites his input on what is doable. Partnership is strong but could be more explicit in negotiating the agenda and sharing power over direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits frequent client-generated change talk, including practical next steps. Change talk is not consistently amplified, summarized, or connected to deeper motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges concerns about burden, uncertainty, and mistakes without arguing or reinforcing them. Softening could be more skillful by explicitly reframing sustain talk into explored ambivalence.
