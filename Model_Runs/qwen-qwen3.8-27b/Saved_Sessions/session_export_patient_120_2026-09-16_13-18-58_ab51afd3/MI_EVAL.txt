# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and validates Sofia without shaming her for prior drinking. However, the warmth sometimes feels procedural because the session quickly moves into advice and task management.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are several adequate reflections of Sofia’s fear, social pressure, and ambivalence. The empathic work is limited by frequent shifts to practical advice rather than deeper exploration of her guilt, maternal concern, and identity conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use during pregnancy, reassurance, future risk, and weekend pressure. Some task-focused practical steps slightly narrow the motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist offers choices and adapts to Sofia’s preferences, allowing her to influence the session. Collaboration is uneven because the psychiatrist supplies much of the content, scripts, and steps rather than eliciting Sofia’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia is given choices and her preferences are respected, especially around how to contact the provider. Autonomy is weakened by directive statements and pre-scripted recommendations rather than consistently framing change as her own decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks a few open-ended questions but quickly shifts to supplying reasons, scripts, and coping strategies. The session misses key opportunities to draw out Sofia’s own values, reasons, and motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some collaborative choices are offered, but the psychiatrist largely remains in the expert role. The interaction is more therapist-led than co-created, reducing the spirit of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session uses a confidence scale and asks what Sofia wants, but it does not consistently amplify or develop her own change language. External solutions and scripts are provided more than evoked client-generated reasons, desires, and abilities.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist avoids arguing with Sofia’s concern about friends and social pressure, and validates it. However, the session does not use double-sided reflections or strategic evocation to reduce the pull of sustain talk.
