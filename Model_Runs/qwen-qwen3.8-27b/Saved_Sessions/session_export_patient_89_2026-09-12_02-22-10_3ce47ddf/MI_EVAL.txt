# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys respect, warmth, and nonjudgment, validating Marcus’s struggles without shaming. It would be stronger if the emotional difficulty of repeated weight cycling were explicitly affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and validating but remain mostly cognitive and problem-focused. Deeper reflections of discouragement, shame, or loss of identity would be needed for a higher score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on increasing physical activity and efficiently narrows to a concrete, achievable plan. There is little drift and pacing consistently guides Marcus toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed with Marcus contributing the timer, walking route, texts to kids, and self-recognition. It could be more balanced by allowing him more space to generate solutions before offering options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices are framed as Marcus’s own and pressure is avoided, supporting ownership of the brief plan. Reinforcing that not acting would still be a valid client decision would strengthen it further.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions draw out Marcus’s motivations, confidence, and concrete intention rather than giving direct advice. Deeply exploring his values, dancing, and family support could make the evocation maximal.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marcus is invited to direct focus and selects the final small-step plan. The session feels somewhat therapist-led because many suggestions are offered before his own ideas are fully explored.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marcus produces useful change talk and increased confidence through questions about small steps, past successes, and next actions. The strongest change talk about dancing and family support was not fully amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers are normalized without argument, and all-or-nothing thinking is gently redirected into manageable problem-solving. More deliberate double-sided reflections could turn sustain talk into deeper client insight.
