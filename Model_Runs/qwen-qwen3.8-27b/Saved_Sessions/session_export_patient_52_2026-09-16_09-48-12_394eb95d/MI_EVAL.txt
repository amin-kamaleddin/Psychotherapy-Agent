# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently used a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone. Validation of Irina's stress and self-compassion for possible slips was clear.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflected Irina's ambivalence well, especially the value of the drink as a reward. Emotional nuances such as guilt or exhaustion could have been explored more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on reducing alcohol use and building a practical plan. Direction was clear, coherent, and change-oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Irina's preferences clearly shaped the final plan, including the one-drink limit and student services follow-up. The psychiatrist sometimes generated options before fully drawing out Irina's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly offered choices and avoided direct pressure. Some emphasis was lost by providing specific solutions and scripts before Irina generated more options herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited concrete, client-driven steps and confidence statements. Early in the session, more evocation of Irina's own values and past strengths would have strengthened this area.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Irina was invited to choose options and helped shape the plan. The psychiatrist often adopted an expert problem-solver role by offering many options, reducing the sense of shared partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited clear change talk about limiting drinks, checking student services, and using a backup plan. This could be stronger by more consistently drawing out Irina's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without arguing, but responses often shifted quickly to problem-solving. Double-sided reflections would have better validated the drink while expanding change talk.
