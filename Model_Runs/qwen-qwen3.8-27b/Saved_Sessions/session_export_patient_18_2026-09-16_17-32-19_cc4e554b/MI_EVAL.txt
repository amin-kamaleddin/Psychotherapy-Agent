# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor communicates respect, nonjudgment, and unconditional positive regard while normalizing the referral. The stance is strong but somewhat procedural, with fewer individualized affirmations of the client's strengths or values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures the client's concerns, intentions, and proposed coping plan. Empathy remains more cognitive and practical than emotion-focused, with less exploration of underlying worry or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on alcohol-related effects, school functioning, and practical monitoring. The target behavior could be sharper by linking the plan to a specific change goal and closing with a concise summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor treats the client as an expert and works with her in a true partnership. The client's preferences substantially shape the session, indicators, and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently honors the client's right to choose, including not counting drinks or immediately involving parents. There is no pressure, coercion, or expert-driven advice-giving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions and reflective prompts elicit the client's own priorities, warning signs, and solutions. Deeper evocation of values, identity, future goals, and confidence would further strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor explicitly invites the client to define the purpose, indicators, and plan. The interaction reflects a collaborative relationship rather than an expert-led agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor draws out the client's own reasons, desires, and abilities for monitoring and self-care. Change talk could be more effectively strengthened through explicit labeling, summarizing, and confidence-building.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled respectfully by accepting the client's frame and reframing it around self-care and prevention. The counselor could more consistently balance sustain talk by gently exploring the other side of the ambivalence.
