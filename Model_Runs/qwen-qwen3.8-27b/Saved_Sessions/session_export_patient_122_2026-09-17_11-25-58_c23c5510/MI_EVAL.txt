# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming language throughout, with no moment of judgment or disapproval. Normalizes Sofia's experience and reframes self-critical narratives without dismissing them, demonstrating genuine unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is exceptionally accurate and layered, capturing both logistical and emotional dimensions with original metaphors (e.g., 'filing system,' 'paused vs. gone'). Reflections are specific, emotionally nuanced, and consistently advance Sofia's self-understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from awareness to barrier identification to micro-commitment to self-monitoring strategy. No topic drift; every exchange builds on the previous one with increasing specificity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structured as genuine co-creation where Sofia contributes all concrete content (the 'paused' language, shoes, voice note idea) while the psychiatrist reflects, validates, and gently redirects. The session feels like two people thinking together rather than one being assessed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is woven into nearly every exchange, explicitly removing pressure ('Not a plan. Just a sliver.') and validating Sofia's own exit strategies. The psychiatrist never prescribes, pressures, or implies Sofia's chosen level of effort is insufficient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight emerges from Sofia's own mouth, prompted by open, evocative questions rather than external imposition. The session ends with Sofia generating her own concrete, self-designed micro-commitment—the clearest evidence of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Sofia as the expert on her own life through collaborative language and questions that transfer problem-solving authority. The power balance is genuinely equal throughout, with no prescriptive statements or assumptions of expertise over Sofia's lived experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategically evokes multiple forms of change talk (desire, ability, commitment) through well-designed questions that draw out Sofia's own agency. Change talk is reflected back with amplification, reinforcing and deepening Sofia's own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully handles self-deprecation, fear of failure, and all-or-nothing thinking by acknowledging, reflecting, and gently contextualizing rather than arguing or minimizing. Sustain talk is reframed as a pattern rather than a truth, the hallmark of advanced softening.
