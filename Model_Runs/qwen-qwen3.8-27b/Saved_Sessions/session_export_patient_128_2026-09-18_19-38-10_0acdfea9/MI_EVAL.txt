# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, never shaming or labeling Tyler's drinking. Could reach a 5 with more explicit affirmations of his worth beyond his behavior and less formulaic structure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate captures of tension and subtle emotional shifts. Occasional missed opportunities to reach deeper emotional layers, such as underlying shame when Tyler resists the 'system' label.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on alcohol use with logical progression from incident to ambivalence to values to practical considerations. Could be more strategically purposeful in building toward a concrete next step or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conversational and equal tone with language that invites Tyler's input throughout. Could be deepened by more explicit co-creation of goals and genuine co-direction rather than the psychiatrist leading most questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect for Tyler's right to define his own experience and pace, never prescriptive in language. Minor tension at the end when offering specific drink-limit suggestions edged toward being slightly prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent and well-timed evocative questions that draw out Tyler's own motivations and insights. Could be more systematic in eliciting all DARN-C dimensions (Desire, Ability, Reasons, Need, Commitment).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Curious, collaborative stance maintained throughout with observations shared rather than assertions made. Could be strengthened by more explicit acknowledgment of Tyler's expertise in his own life and less steering toward specific suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective at evoking and amplifying change talk by reflecting and reinforcing Tyler's self-awareness. Could push further into concrete DARN-C dimensions, particularly ability and commitment, when Tyler offers tentative change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk by validating, normalizing, and reframing rather than arguing or dismissing. Slightly shifts toward solution-focused advice at the end rather than fully evoking Tyler's own answers.
