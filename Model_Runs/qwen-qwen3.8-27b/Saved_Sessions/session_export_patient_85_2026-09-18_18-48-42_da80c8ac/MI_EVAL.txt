# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates consistent, warm, and individualized unconditional positive regard throughout the session. Affirmations are specific and genuine rather than formulaic, and sustain talk is acknowledged without judgment or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Perspective-taking is highly accurate and progresses in depth, with sophisticated double-sided reflections that capture emotional nuance. Key reflections go beyond surface content to identify deeper meaning, such as self-efficacy and identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session follows a logical, purposeful progression from validation through identification of the specific obstacle to a concrete, client-generated plan. There is no topic drift; each question builds on the previous one and stays focused on reducing juice consumption.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is a genuine joint exploration where Marco's ideas consistently shape the direction of the session. The psychiatrist builds on and amplifies the patient's own suggestions rather than introducing external ones.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never prescribes or imposes, consistently inviting Marco to define his own approach and explicitly attributing ownership of the plan to him. The patient's own language reflects successful autonomy support, e.g., 'I chose this. It's not some rule someone handed me.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses strategic questions and reflective amplification to draw out Marco's own motivations and change content. Nearly all of the plan, strategies, and commitment language are generated by the patient rather than provided by the psychiatrist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and positions Marco as the expert on his own life, but could more explicitly negotiate the session agenda or acknowledge the patient's expertise in more direct terms. The dynamic is strong but occasionally slightly more guiding than fully equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is consistently evoked and amplified through direct invitations, affirmations of the patient's own change language, and strategic reflections that strengthen ability and commitment. The cumulative effect moves Marco from frustration to confident preparation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The one instance of significant sustain talk (love of orange juice) is handled expertly with a double-sided reflection that validates the pull while gently pivoting toward change. However, the limited volume of sustain talk in the session restricts the ability to fully demonstrate this skill at the highest level.
