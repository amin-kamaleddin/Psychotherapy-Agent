# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone without criticism or pressure. Positive regard could be strengthened by more explicit affirmations of Daniela's resilience, values, or right to struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and helpful, especially in capturing the ripple effect of stress and practical concerns. It could deepen by exploring more vulnerable emotions such as disappointment, guilt, fear of rejection, or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on childcare-related stress and skillfully narrows to a concrete next step. It could be stronger if the target behavior or broader coping goal were explicitly stated or summarized.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and Daniela co-construct the plan through shared ideas, wording, timing, and problem-solving. Daniela's input substantially shapes the direction of the session, reflecting strong collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes optionality, client choice, and ownership of the plan. There is no pressure to contact the neighbor, and refusal is normalized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Daniela's own motivations, ideas, language, and solutions rather than giving advice. The plan emerges naturally from Daniela's statements, with room to further elicit deeper values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently treats Daniela as the expert on her own life and follows her lead. Questions invite her input and the session direction is shaped by her preferences and ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniela generates clear change talk, and the psychiatrist reinforces it with helpful reflections. It could deepen by more explicitly evoking values, confidence, commitment, or future goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers and sustain talk are not argued with or reinforced, and the psychiatrist redirects toward manageable action. It could improve by using double-sided reflections to hold ambivalence more fully.
