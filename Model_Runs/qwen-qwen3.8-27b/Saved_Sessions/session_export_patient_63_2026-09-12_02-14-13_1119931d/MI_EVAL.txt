# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and avoids shaming. Acceptance could be stronger by more explicitly affirming Jordan as a person despite the behavior during moments of guilt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence around wanting to stop and feeling pulled back by friends and cravings. They often remain cognitive and could better explore deeper shame, fear of exclusion, and identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on shoplifting, peer influence, urges, and practical steps. It becomes somewhat repetitive in generating coping scripts without fully advancing a deeper personal plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaboration through questions and adjusting to Jordan’s responses. However, the psychiatrist supplies many ideas and scripts, creating a less equal and more expert-driven dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jordan is regularly invited to choose what feels realistic and doable. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly emphasizing his ownership, right to decline, and role in evaluating what works.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative attempts appear through open-ended questions and confidence scaling. Much of the session provides external strategies rather than eliciting Jordan’s own values, motivations, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Inviting input supports partnership, but repeated lists of suggested strategies make the interaction more directive. The client’s expertise on his own life, friendships, and triggers is not consistently foregrounded.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms motivation and uses questions to elicit some change talk. He often shifts into advice-giving and strategy generation instead of deeply developing Jordan’s own change-oriented statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument, but the psychiatrist does not consistently use skillful MI moves to soften it. The session often returns to practical problem-solving rather than helping resolve underlying ambivalence.
