# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful stance and validates Dylan’s perspective. The warmth could deepen through more explicit affirmation of Dylan’s effort, values, or struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track Dylan’s perspective and key values such as friendships, schedule, record, and future. They are somewhat repetitive and do not fully explore deeper emotions like embarrassment or fear of stigma.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally remains focused on the conduct issue and future alcohol-related behavior. Some drift into administrative concerns and repeated similar questions slightly reduces momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites Dylan to co-construct meaning and next steps. It would be stronger if shared decision-making and plan co-creation were made more explicit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Dylan’s ownership and freedom of choice. Some wording frames next steps as light or simple, which can subtly steer Dylan, though autonomy is generally well supported.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes a reasonable effort to elicit motivation from within Dylan but remains procedural. More importance, confidence, or discrepancy questions would deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Dylan as the expert on his own life and follows his emerging ideas. However, the clinician still sets the overall agenda and repeats similar questions, limiting full co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dylan offers meaningful action-oriented change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or expand these statements. Framing next steps as light or simple can narrow Dylan’s own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct confrontation and does not reinforce Dylan’s minimization. More skillful double-sided reflections, rolling-with-resistance responses, and smoother pivots would reduce defensiveness.
