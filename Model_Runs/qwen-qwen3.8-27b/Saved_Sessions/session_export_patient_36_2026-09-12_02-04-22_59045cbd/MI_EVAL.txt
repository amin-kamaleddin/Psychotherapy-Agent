# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes slipping without shame. Acceptance is strong but sometimes more procedural than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Elena’s ambivalence, protectiveness, and concern about overplanning. Empathy remains largely cognitive and does not fully explore the emotional depth of guilt or perfectionism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on one concrete target: protecting time through a work/grading boundary. Each response narrows the conversation into actionable, testable steps with no meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed, with the psychiatrist adapting to Elena’s preferences and objections. It does not reach a 5 because the psychiatrist offers many concrete options, making it feel more guided than fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is supported through choice-based language, respect for Elena’s timing, and avoidance of pressure. The psychiatrist’s detailed plans and suggestions subtly reduce the sense that the entire process is client-owned.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some evocation through open questions, but the session is often driven by therapist-generated structures. More focus on Elena’s values, identity, and self-generated reasons for change would strengthen this area.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena’s choices meaningfully shape the plan, such as selecting the 6pm cutoff, red pen, and one-sentence prompt. Partnership is strong but not fully client-driven because the psychiatrist frequently introduces specific options and structures.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful change statements and affirms them appropriately. However, change talk is not consistently amplified, with missed opportunities to explore desire, ability, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by avoiding argument and normalizing Elena’s fear of leaving work undone. A 5 would require more explicit double-sided reflections or deeper resolution of the conflict between caring for students and protecting herself.
