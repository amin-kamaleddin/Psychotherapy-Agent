### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful throughout, with genuine positive regard and no judgment of hopelessness or avoidance. Could be slightly more explicit in affirming Jordan's worth as a person separate from his circumstances.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with reflections that are specific, layered, and capture underlying emotional meaning beyond surface statements. Reflections like 'it feels like proof' and 'there's a grief in that' demonstrate deep understanding of Jordan's unique experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear progression from broad exploration to concrete action (rent by Friday, calling landlord) with effective separation of specific problem from global narrative. Could benefit from slightly more explicit agenda-setting at the outset rather than emergent direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed as joint exploration between equals with collaborative language like 'let's figure out' and regular checking of Jordan's perspective. Jordan's ideas clearly shape the session's direction and structure, demonstrating true co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly rejects the expert role, positions Jordan as the source of solutions, and honors his boundaries without making him feel wrong. Never wavers into directive territory even when sustain talk might invite it; every question is open and non-directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong use of open questions and strategic silence to elicit change talk from within Jordan, never providing reasons for change or offering advice. Could be more systematic in developing ability and confidence talk, particularly when Jordan identifies concrete actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary egalitarian stance with explicit rejection of expert role and immediate adjustment when Jordan pushes back on boundaries. Jordan's contributions consistently shape the session's direction, demonstrating textbook partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively catches, names, and affirms change talk as it emerges, guiding Jordan from vague statements to concrete goals like clearing rent by Friday. Could push slightly harder on ability and confidence talk to more systematically develop forward-movement language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk through double-sided reflections that hold it without amplifying, and effectively separates specific problems from global narratives. Could use more targeted questioning to help Jordan examine the accuracy of global patterns like 'everything always falls apart'.
