# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepts Viktor's perspective without moralizing. Some agenda-setting and plan suggestions slightly reduce the sense of deep, unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Viktor's perspective, including the utility of the drugs and relational strain. Empathy is more cognitive than emotional and misses opportunities to explore deeper feelings, especially at the end.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays mostly focused on Viktor's drug use and its impact on his marriage. Some drift into scripts and Elena's responses pulls focus away from Viktor's own motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is largely co-constructed, with Viktor contributing key ideas such as going to Dave's and rejecting a written plan. The psychiatrist sometimes acts more like a consultant by supplying options and scripts rather than consistently letting Viktor lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Viktor's choice and autonomy are regularly supported, and his rejection of a written plan is respected. Repeated provision of specific options and scripts can subtly shape the client rather than allowing his own solutions to emerge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Viktor offers many practical ideas and preferences, but the psychiatrist's suggestions often interrupt the evocation process. Deeper exploration of Viktor's values, meanings, and internal reasons for change is limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Language and structure support a collaborative stance, and Viktor's ideas substantially shape the session. Occasional shifts into an expert-driven mode by offering scripts and plans can reduce the client's role as the primary source of expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked, particularly around peace at home and practical experimentation. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, summarize, or deepen this change talk with DARN-C or importance/confidence rulers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing with Viktor's sustain talk and reflects his views without resistance. However, there is limited use of double-sided reflections or subtle pivots to reconnect behavior to Viktor's values.
