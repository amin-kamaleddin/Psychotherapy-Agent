# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates respect, validation, and nonjudgment toward Linda’s ambivalence and concerns. The tone is warm and accepting, though it could include more explicit individualized affirmation of Linda’s worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate, complex reflections that track Linda’s mixed feelings, triggers, and desire to avoid conflict. Empathy could be deepened by exploring the stronger emotional pain and shame related to past failures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on tobacco cessation with a clear, logical progression from ambivalence to a concrete, doable plan. Each step naturally builds toward a specific next action without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates the plan with Linda, offering options while leaving final decisions to her. Collaboration could be strengthened by more explicit agenda-setting and shared goal evaluation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms Linda’s pace, choices, and ownership of the plan without pressure to commit. Autonomy support could be enhanced by more explicitly affirming her right to modify or stop the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Linda’s own ideas, motivations, and solutions through open-ended questions. Evocation could go deeper into Linda’s core values and intrinsic meaning behind wanting change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Linda as the expert on her own life and keeps the interaction collaborative. Partnership could be more explicitly framed as a true equality through agenda negotiation and stronger acknowledgment of Linda’s wisdom.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Linda generates substantial change talk, and the psychiatrist reflects and amplifies this language effectively. The psychiatrist could more actively evoke ability and confidence talk when Linda expresses self-doubt.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles some sustain talk by acknowledging triggers without reinforcing catastrophizing. However, the session ends with strong fear of failure and all-or-nothing thinking that remains insufficiently addressed.
