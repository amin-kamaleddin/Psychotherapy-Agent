# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepts Sofia’s marijuana use as coping without shame. Could be warmer with deeper individualized affirmation of her insight, courage, and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects stress, cost, social belonging, and the need for a quick reset. Could more fully explore underlying fear, urgency, shame, and helplessness before moving to problem solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on marijuana use while following Sofia’s concerns about stress, money, and connection. Could more consistently link specific behavior to broader values, goals, or discrepancy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructs practical steps from Sofia’s own ideas rather than imposing solutions. Rapid leading questions slightly reduce the sense of a fully equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respects choice and avoids pressure, even when Sofia says she is not quitting. Clearly positions Sofia as the expert on her own experience and decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete behavioral alternatives, but the evocation remains more logistical than motivational. Deeper personal reasons, values, and discrepancy are not fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly invites Sofia’s perspective and lets her concerns shape the session. Despite minor pacing issues, the counselor clearly treats Sofia as the expert and partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently prompts client-generated change ideas and affirms them. Could use more reflections, importance or confidence rulers, and summaries to consolidate stronger change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects reasons to maintain use without arguing, moralizing, or triggering a power struggle. Could use more double-sided reflections to turn sustain talk toward discrepancy.
