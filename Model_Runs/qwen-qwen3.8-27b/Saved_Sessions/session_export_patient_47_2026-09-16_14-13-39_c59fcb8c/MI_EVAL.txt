# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful, and validating tone throughout. Some responses felt formulaic or slightly assumed rather than fully acknowledging the client’s deeper concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening and perspective-taking are strong, especially around ambivalence and confusion about the questionnaire. Empathy could go deeper by exploring feelings such as fear, embarrassment, or defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use and the questionnaire results without drifting. Direction could be strengthened with a clearer summary or more specific behavioral goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses joint language and invites the client’s input throughout. More explicit invitation for the client to set the agenda or choose the tracking method would make collaboration more balanced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently avoids coercion, advice-giving, or pressure. The client clearly perceives the process as her own decision, especially with phrases like “without making it a rule.”

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively helps the client connect the questionnaire results to her own physical experience. Deeper exploration of values and future-oriented motivation could further strengthen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the client’s perspective and uses inclusive, collaborative language. More explicit acknowledgment that the client is the expert on her own life could further enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers meaningful change talk, but the psychiatrist does not fully develop or strengthen it. Scaling questions, deeper reflection, or summaries could better amplify motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids challenging the client’s desire to maintain pub drinking. Using double-sided reflections could further soften sustain talk while acknowledging the underlying concern.
