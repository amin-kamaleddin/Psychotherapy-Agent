# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgmental tone throughout, with genuine respect for Yasmin's journey. The acceptance occasionally feels slightly structured rather than fully embodied, and could deepen in moments of vulnerability like fear of lowering the bar.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with excellent complex reflections like 'same body, different misery.' Could go deeper in validating the emotional toll of dieting irritability and the fear embedded in 'lowering the bar.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from presenting concern through exploration to alternative values and examining internalized standards. Lacks an explicit collaboratively-set agenda and a clear consolidation or next step at the close.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is clearly shaped by Yasmin's own words, metaphors, and concerns, with the psychiatrist actively building on them rather than introducing frameworks unilaterally. The power dynamic feels genuinely equal and co-creative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Yasmin's ownership through open questions, never gives advice, and explicitly credits her agency. The overall message is uniformly: this is your life, your body, your choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully uses evocative questions to draw out Yasmin's own wisdom throughout. One moment of suggestion ('what if the outcome shifts') plants an idea rather than waiting for Yasmin to arrive at it independently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions are framed as invitations and the psychiatrist avoids lecturing or providing unsolicited information. Occasionally tips toward a slight guide role and could more explicitly acknowledge Yasmin's expertise in her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and affirms change talk multiple times, including the 'richer list' reflection of alternative goals. Could push further on confidence/ability talk and more actively consolidate the change talk that emerged by the session's end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively handles sustain talk without dismissing or arguing, using skilled questions like 'whose bar is that?' to help Yasmin examine her own beliefs. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections to fully honor the fear while opening the alternative.
