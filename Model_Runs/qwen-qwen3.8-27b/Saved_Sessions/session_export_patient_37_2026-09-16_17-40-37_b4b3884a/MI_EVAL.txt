# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids pressuring Elena to decide quickly. It falls short of a 5 because the warmth is somewhat formulaic and does not consistently affirm her worth beyond her good intentions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections show solid cognitive understanding of her mixed concerns and ambivalence. However, the fear, guilt, and vulnerability behind her statements are not consistently addressed with deeper emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on the vaccination decision and directly related concerns, with a logical progression. It does not fully move toward a concrete next step, and some repetition slightly weakens the sense of forward momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and allows Elena's preferences to shape the flow of the conversation. Collaboration is strong but not fully balanced, as the provider remains the primary agenda-setter.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Language such as 'without needing a quick answer' and 'so you can weigh them yourself' strongly supports Elena's ownership of the decision. It could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her right to choose differently without pressure or clinical penalty.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out Elena's information needs and some underlying protective values. Evocation is more focused on information preferences than on eliciting deeper reasons, confidence, readiness, or hopes for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is invited to set focus, priorities, and the order of discussion, reflecting a strong collaborative stance. It does not reach a 5 because the psychiatrist still controls much of the structure and does not fully position Elena as the expert on her values.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena expresses desire to protect her daughter and make a trustworthy decision, and the psychiatrist occasionally reflects this. The session does not consistently deepen or expand this into stronger change talk, confidence-building, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena's concerns are acknowledged without argument or invalidation, and some balanced reframing is present. The session still spends considerable time on risk comparison, which can keep sustain talk active, and lacks more active softening of the pull toward the status quo.
