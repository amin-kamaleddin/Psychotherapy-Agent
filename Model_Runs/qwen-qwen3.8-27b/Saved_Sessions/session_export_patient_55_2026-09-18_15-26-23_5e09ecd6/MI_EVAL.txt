# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful throughout, with multiple examples of unconditional positive regard and graceful acceptance of corrections. Could be elevated by more explicitly affirming Isabelle's worth as a person beyond her productivity and achievements.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures both content and underlying meaning, with nuanced tracking of Isabelle's subtle shifts in language. Occasionally stays at the cognitive/behavioral level rather than reaching deeper emotional territory.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session lacks a clear direction toward a specific target behavior or concern, and the original referral (alcohol risk) is never addressed. The conversation, while thoughtful, meanders without a clear arc toward a specific goal or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation feels like a genuine dialogue between equals, with the counselor following Isabelle's leads and building on her language. Could be stronger by more explicitly co-constructing the session's direction with Isabelle.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently avoids pressure or directive approaches, respecting Isabelle's boundaries and never telling her what she should do or feel. Could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her ownership of choices and capacity for change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some effort to evoke Isabelle's own thoughts, but occasionally steps in with interpretations before she has fully explored the feeling herself. Could use more strategic techniques like scaling questions and future-focused questions to draw out intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance with consistent use of inclusive, non-directive language and graceful acceptance of corrections. Could be stronger by more explicitly negotiating the agenda and letting Isabelle set the direction more consistently.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Very limited efforts to evoke change talk, with almost no explicit language from Isabelle constituting desire, ability, need, or reasons for change. The session largely stays in exploration of the status quo without creating space for Isabelle to articulate any reasons or desires for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles Isabelle's significant sustain talk by validating and reflecting it back rather than arguing or dismissing it. Could use double-sided reflections more frequently to acknowledge both sides while gently maintaining curiosity.
