# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, but validation often shifts quickly into advice. Affirmation feels somewhat surface-level and contingent on cooperation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture exhaustion, loneliness, and the need for a break, but remain surface-level and repetitive. Deeper emotions such as isolation, guilt, and identity as a mother are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking and child exposure, but mostly on harm-reduction logistics rather than change goals. It would benefit from linking practical steps back to Natalia's values and motivations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices but repeatedly supplies structured options, scripts, and micro-hacks. Natalia selects among clinician-generated ideas rather than helping shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices and permission to not quit now support autonomy, but repeated commitments to specific steps can feel directive. More explicit emphasis on Natalia's charge over decisions would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited eliciting of Natalia's own reasons, concerns, and values; the session quickly becomes didactic and solution-focused. More open-ended questions and evocation of change talk are needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Preference questions create some partnership, but the psychiatrist positions themselves as the expert and problem-solver. Natalia's expertise in her own life and priorities is not consistently centered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change-oriented statements are not effectively reflected, amplified, or built upon. The session relies on external suggestions rather than evoking Natalia's own reasons, desires, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without direct confrontation, but not effectively redirected through double-sided reflections or balancing statements. Providing more information and options can reinforce the client's fixed position.
