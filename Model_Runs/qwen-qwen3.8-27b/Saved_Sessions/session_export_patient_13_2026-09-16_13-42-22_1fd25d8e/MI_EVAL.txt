# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates acceptance and unconditional positive regard, validating Anara’s fear and concern without judgment. Her stance remains respectful and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong perspective-taking, especially in reflecting ambivalence between wanting understanding and feeling afraid. Some reflections are somewhat formulaic and could deepen shame, fear of dismissal, or final fatigue.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on preparing a safer step toward being heard by a trusted person or medical provider. It moves logically from fear to concrete language, a list, and evidence without significant drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally behaves as an equal partner, inviting input and co-creating a list and opening sentence. Frequent menu questions and suggested phrases make the interaction somewhat therapist-influenced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports choice by asking what would feel safest, doable, or clearer rather than directing action. She respects Anara’s pace, including the decision not to involve her mother yet.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits Anara’s own motivations, preferred next steps, and concrete evidence for her worry. Deeper evocation of values, identity, and intrinsic meaning would strengthen the interview.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly positions Anara as the expert on her body, family, and comfort level. She follows Anara’s lead in choosing the home visitor, a list, and manageable wording rather than imposing a plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits multiple client-generated change statements, such as asking the home visitor, using a list, and naming the shirt as evidence. She could better amplify and summarize the change talk after Anara offers it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles fear and worry with nonjudgmental, double-sided reflections that preserve Anara’s agency. She could more actively pivot toward values, identity, and longer-term benefits to reduce the dominance of sustain talk.
