# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental while validating the client’s values and concerns. Normalizes forgetting and slips without adding pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and accurately captures the client’s perspective on key concerns. Empathy is sometimes more cognitive than emotional, with less depth on guilt, fatigue, and self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on building a sustainable daily routine tied to self-respect. Progresses well from broad goals to a concrete, doable implementation intention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as a collaborative partner by offering options while returning the decision to the client. The final plan is co-constructed from the client’s ideas and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and avoids controlling language or rigid demands. The plan remains flexible and client-owned, reinforcing that the client is in control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client’s motivations, values, and confidence-building ideas. Could be stronger by more consistently asking for client-generated alternatives before offering counselor-created options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the client as the expert on their own life, values, and practical realities. The client’s choices drive the session content and final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out desire, need, ability, and commitment language. Uses the client’s own values and confidence-building strategies to strengthen the plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges worries about failure, guilt, and forgetfulness without arguing or dismissing them. Gently redirects concerns toward flexible, self-compassionate action.
