# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Marek’s ambivalence without shame. Acceptance is strong but sometimes formulaic and does not consistently reach the deeper emotional or existential concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures both sides of Marek’s ambivalence and tracks his shifting willingness to consider change. Empathy is good but occasionally repetitive and does not fully explore the emotional undercurrents beneath his statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains generally focused on weekend drinking and narrows toward a practical self-check plan. Direction would be stronger with clearer summarization, readiness or confidence scaling, and an explicit next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marek’s ideas clearly shape the session, from rejecting logging to proposing a mirror-based check. The psychiatrist builds on his preferences rather than imposing a clinician-led plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes Marek’s choice, expertise, and ownership of the decision. Advice-giving and pressure to cut down are avoided, allowing Marek to define his own guardrail.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions successfully draw out Marek’s own plan and change ideas. Evocation could be stronger by more consistently eliciting and expanding desire, ability, need, and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Marek as the expert and invites his input throughout. Partnership is solid but not fully exemplary because the agenda and priorities are not explicitly co-created or shared in a more collaborative way.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some practical change talk, especially around self-monitoring and possible reduction. Change talk remains limited in depth and is not consistently amplified or developed into stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and generally stays MI-consistent when Marek minimizes his drinking. Sustain talk is not consistently softened or reframed in a way that helps him resolve ambivalence more effectively.
