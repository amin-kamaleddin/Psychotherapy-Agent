# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental affirmation. The client’s honesty and self-awareness are acknowledged in a supportive, normalizing way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects both practical concerns and relational meanings, such as wanting a low-key plan and avoiding social attention. Perspective-taking is precise and well attuned to the client’s pragmatic concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the target behavior of alcohol use in social gatherings. Each exchange advances a specific, behavior-based plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on the client’s ideas and co-constructs the plan rather than imposing one. The final plan clearly reflects the client’s preferences and contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly supports the client’s choice by asking what would feel natural, doable, and trustworthy. A few suggestive options and normative phrases slightly reduce the purely client-led autonomy stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client’s own motivation, values, and past strengths rather than supplying reasons for change. The client spontaneously connects current goals to prior success with smoking cessation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates a collaborative stance and treats the client as the expert on his own social context. Occasional instructive framing or counselor-supplied suggestions prevent a fully shared agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open, client-centered questions consistently elicit and expand the client’s own change talk. The client generates most of the plan, motivation, and definition of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through normalization, reframing, and reduced pressure. The psychiatrist could use more explicit softening techniques, such as double-sided reflections, in a few moments.
