# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment around probation, crime, or unemployment. Conveys respect for Timur's perspective even on risky thoughts, maintaining a safe space for vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that integrates multiple parts of Timur's experience at once (relational motivation, financial worry, fear of autonomy loss). Captures his ambivalence between wanting stability and attraction to quick money with nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Stays focused on the core target behavior (employment, relational stability) with useful progression toward a concrete two-day trial step. However, becomes somewhat narrow by focusing heavily on scripting probation conversation rather than periodically broadening to identity or longer-term direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative interaction where Timur's suggestions are built upon rather than replaced by the counselor's plan. Notable collaborative moment where Timur is invited to generate and refine his own sentence for probation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes Timur's control and choice, helping him define conditions under which he would freely try without feeling trapped. No significant directive or pressuring language; change is consistently framed as his own decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Timur through evocative questions rather than supplying external reasons for change. Actively strengthens his emerging change talk by reflecting back amplified versions of his own language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear partnership stance with Timur treated as the expert on his own life and limits; counselor invites rather than prescribes. Session direction emerges largely from Timur's stated needs rather than a predetermined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies Timur's own language in favor of change using evocative, open questions that draw out his reasons. Reflects and strengthens his emerging sense of capability and confidence in his own plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk (fears about ghosting, probation oversight, bad jobs) by acknowledging concerns and redirecting toward smaller, safer steps rather than arguing or dismissing. Effectively reframes resistance into legitimate conditions for manageable change.
