### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurately tracks emotional shifts, culminating in the powerful 'Less alone' reflection. Could sustain more emotional attunement to the loneliness itself before pivoting to cognitive insight-naming.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus on the target behavior (nighttime social media use affecting sleep and academics) with zero topic drift. Clear logical momentum from problem exploration to mechanism understanding to concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framing work as joint exploration with language like 'let's work from there' and actively building on Amina's own ideas rather than redirecting. Could be elevated by more explicit co-creation of session agenda and next steps with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Amina's right to choose, never using prescriptive language and validating her self-designed plan. Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming her right to NOT do the experiment and reminding her she is in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Amina generates nearly all key insights and the entire Rania plan on her own, drawn out by well-designed open questions. The psychiatrist's role is to ask the right questions and reflect, which is the hallmark of high-level evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, guide-not-expert stance with frequent invitations for Amina's input and perspective. Could be elevated by more explicitly sharing power, such as asking Amina to rate her own progress or challenge the psychiatrist's reflections.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes statements of desire, ability, and commitment through well-designed questions and by reflecting back Amina's own change language. Could be more systematic in eliciting confidence ratings and directly asking about benefits of change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating ambivalence without arguing, and gently questioning assumptions rather than being confrontational. Could benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections and active strategic redirection.
