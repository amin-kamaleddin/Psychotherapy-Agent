# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys unconditional positive regard without judgment, labeling, or disapproval. Statements normalize Sofia's struggle and affirm her efforts while keeping a supportive, respectful tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers strong reflections of Sofia's ambivalence, self-criticism, and desire to shift from a “test” to safer movement. Some reflections remain more summary or cognitive, and the final “Can you tell me more about that?” is generic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on building a realistic, non-diet, low-pressure routine with small physical activity and reduced stress-related eating. There is a minor early redundancy around the “small anchor,” but it does not reduce the overall clarity or momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as an equal partner, inviting input and building on Sofia's contributions rather than prescribing an intervention. Sofia's ideas directly shape the plan, creating a co-constructed approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Sofia's sense of choice by emphasizing that she knows her own pace and what is possible. Suggestions are framed as possibilities rather than directives, avoiding pressure or persuasion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively draw out Sofia's own strategies, motivation, and internal resources. Some questions are mildly suggestive by offering a menu of options, and the final generic follow-up slightly reduces evocative momentum.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Sofia as the expert on her own life and follows her lead throughout the session. Her contributions substantially guide the direction of the conversation and the resulting plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully elicits and reinforces Sofia's concrete change ideas, such as the phone boundary, block walk, shoes cue, breaths, and water interruption. The session could be strengthened by using scaling, values-based questions, or commitment language to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by reframing self-doubt and fear of failure as flexible resets rather than rules to break. The counselor does not reinforce barriers but consistently redirects the conversation toward nonpunitive, doable action.
