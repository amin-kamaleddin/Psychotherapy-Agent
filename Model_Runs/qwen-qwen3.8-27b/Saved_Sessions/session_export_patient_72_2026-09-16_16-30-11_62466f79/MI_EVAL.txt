# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses validating, nonjudgmental language and avoids blaming or pressure. The stance is respectful but more task-focused than fully person-centered, so warmth is slightly contained.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, accurately capturing frustration, relief, and self-regulation strategies. A few reflections are slightly interpretive and do not fully explore deeper body-image or identity themes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on building a realistic, small-step exercise plan. The conversation moves logically from goals to concrete cues and fallbacks without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is largely collaborative, with Leila generating many of the key ideas. However, the counselor still organizes much of the process and does not consistently use explicit shared-decision language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Leila’s sense of choice and avoids coercive advice. It would be stronger with more explicit language affirming her right to modify or stop the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively draws out Leila’s own ideas and change-related statements. Deeper evocation of values, confidence, and commitment could have been used more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor asks open-ended questions and lets client ideas shape the plan. The partnership is good, though the psychiatrist remains the primary organizer rather than fully sharing conversational control.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Leila’s change talk is well elicited, including realistic goals, self-efficacy, and intrinsic value. The counselor could more consistently label, affirm, and amplify that change talk in explicit MI style.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers such as work interruptions and all-or-nothing thinking are reframed in a helpful, nonconfrontational way. More explicit double-sided reflections could further normalize ambivalence and reduce its grip.
