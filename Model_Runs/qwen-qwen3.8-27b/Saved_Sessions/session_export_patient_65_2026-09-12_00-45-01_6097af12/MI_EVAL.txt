# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, but the warmth often feels procedural and tied to task completion. Supportive statements are present, yet deeper unconditional regard for Julian's fear and sense of being controlled is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Empathy is mostly surface-level, with brief acknowledgments of stress rather than accurate reflective listening. The session focuses on logistics, and Julian's underlying anxiety, shame, and ambivalence are not deeply explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is clear, consistent focus on the target behavior of completing the court-ordered hours. The direction is effective but narrow, leaning toward compliance planning rather than motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers some options and invites limited input, showing partial collaboration. However, most of the plan, scripts, and steps are supplied by the psychiatrist, so the interaction remains counselor-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some autonomy-supportive moments, such as offering choices and accepting Julian's boundary. Repeated directive instructions, though, can make the process feel prescriptive rather than client-led.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session begins with an evocative question, but quickly shifts to advising, scripts, and problem-solving. Julian's own motivations, values, and confidence are not consistently elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist mostly assumes an expert or problem-solving role instead of treating Julian as the central authority on his own situation. The tone is informative and directive rather than genuinely collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change-relevant language from Julian is not consistently expanded upon with evocative questions or affirmations. Motivation remains largely external and compliance-based.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Julian's sustain talk and generally responds with acceptance. However, ambivalence is quickly bypassed with task-focused redirection rather than skillfully explored.
