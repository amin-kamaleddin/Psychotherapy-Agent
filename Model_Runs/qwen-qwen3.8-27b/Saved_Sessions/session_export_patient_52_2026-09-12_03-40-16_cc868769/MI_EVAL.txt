# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicates warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard. The client is validated throughout, even when minimizing risk or expressing ambivalence about change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses strong reflections to capture ambivalence, stress, and important values. However, some reflections remain more content-focused than emotionally nuanced, limiting deeper empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on the target behavior of reducing alcohol use. The conversation moves clearly from ambivalence to a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is largely co-created and respects the client's constraints and preferences. However, the clinician's structured guidance and menus of options keep the process from being fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician frequently supports choice and avoids pressure or coercion. The extensive use of suggested options still limits opportunities for the client to generate fully self-directed solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicits some change-related motivation through questions and confidence ratings. The session relies heavily on external structure and suggestions rather than deeply evoking the client's own values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client clearly shapes the plan and is treated as the expert on her life. The clinician still provides many structured options, which subtly leads the process and reduces pure client leadership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively reinforces practical change talk, such as trying one glass, noticing less fog, and increasing confidence. Deeper expansion of the client's values and long-term motivations is limited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician respectfully acknowledges sustain talk without arguing or invalidating. However, there is limited strategic redirection or exploration of the costs of maintaining current drinking patterns.
