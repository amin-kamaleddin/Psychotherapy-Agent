# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating without lecturing or judging Maya's drinking. Slightly performative at times—reads as well-crafted MI phrases rather than deeply individualized, embodied warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that accurately captures Maya's ambivalence and underlying motivations. Reflections tend to stay at the cognitive level, missing opportunities to name deeper emotions like fear of control or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on the target behavior with a logical session arc from rapport to closing. Somewhat abandons the thread when Maya challenges near the end rather than continuing to explore it strategically.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a collaborative frame with a joint exploration tone and positions self as a resource alongside Maya. Still the one asking most questions and setting pace; could explicitly co-create the agenda with Maya.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent throughout—explicit non-directiveness, validates Maya's right to disengage, removes pressure to commit, and reinforces freedom of choice. The strongest area of performance in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** One strong evocative question successfully draws out Maya's own criteria for concern, but the counselor doesn't build on it or use other techniques like scales or values-based exploration. Present but limited in depth and frequency.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent partnership stance with language affirming Maya as the expert on her own life. Partnership is somewhat one-directional in practice—Maya responds to questions rather than genuinely redirecting the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicits change talk around grades and track but doesn't push further to develop it or create momentum. Missed opportunities to explore what those values mean to Maya and link them to her behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequately acknowledges Maya's sustain talk without reinforcing it, but doesn't use double-sided reflections or strategic reframing. Response to Maya's challenge is somewhat defensive, missing a chance to gently reframe the conversation.
