# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated consistently warm, unconditional positive regard throughout. Masterfully handled Oliver's self-deprecation by neither validating nor dismissing the label, but holding both self-doubt and clarity in a compassionate frame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were precise, specific, and emotionally attuned, moving beyond paraphrase to capture underlying meaning. The closing metaphor mirroring Oliver's own language ('three months of a sentence sitting there') was particularly powerful.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained laser-sharp focus on the target behavior with clear, progressive momentum from exploration through evocation to a concrete behavioral commitment. The session arc was textbook MI: each step logically built toward the actionable plan (Thursday, 7am, kitchen table).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the interaction as joint work using 'let's' and 'we' language and negotiating the therapeutic relationship itself. Never spoke as an authority figure dispensing advice; maintained an equal-partner dynamic throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never told Oliver what, when, or how to say anything; every element of the plan was client-generated. Offered support without imposing it, and the entire session was structured around Oliver's own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never offered a single piece of advice, technique, or reason for change. Every insight, solution, and plan was evoked from within Oliver through strategic questions and reflections — arguably the strongest dimension of the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed that expertise and wisdom reside within Oliver, using questions like 'What do you think might make that feel slightly less scary?' The power dynamic was clearly one of two equals, with the client as primary knowledge-holder.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic questions directly elicited concrete change language and commitment statements, which were then skillfully amplified and reflected back. The psychiatrist consistently drew out and reinforced Oliver's own reasons and plans without injecting external ones.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argued against or minimized sustain talk; instead reframed it by reconnecting it to positive values while gently pivoting toward change. For example, 'weakness' was juxtaposed with Oliver's own clarity rather than challenged directly.
