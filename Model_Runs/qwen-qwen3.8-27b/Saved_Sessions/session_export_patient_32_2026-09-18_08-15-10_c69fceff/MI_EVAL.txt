# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently respectful and nonjudgmental stance, validating Derrick's defensiveness without labeling or correcting. Warmth is consistent and professional but somewhat measured rather than deeply warm or explicitly normalizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate understanding of Derrick's perspective throughout, consistently going beyond surface-level paraphrasing to capture emotional nuance and underlying meaning. Complex reflections like capturing the full ambivalence between fear of blackouts and the unsustainable nature of constant vigilance are particularly skillful.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and logical progression from legal context through pattern exploration to the core ambivalence, with each transition purposeful and tied to the target behavior. Would benefit from a bridging summary that explicitly connects the exploration back to the session's specific goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative dynamic with both parties positioned as equal contributors, using language like 'I'm trying to understand the full picture' and 'from where I'm sitting.' The session feels like two people thinking together rather than one person being assessed or treated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly reinforces Derrick's autonomy, never using directive language like 'should' or 'need to.' The reframing of the conversation as 'about you' rather than something being done to him is particularly strong and woven naturally into the therapeutic relationship.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out Derrick's own reasons for change through strategic questions that produce meaningful self-reflection and change talk. Could be slightly deeper in the latter half, particularly when Derrick offers his dilemma between feeling sharp or connected.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Derrick, never giving advice or assuming what he needs. The final exchange meets Derrick's dilemma with genuine curiosity rather than a push toward one answer, exemplifying a model of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and amplifies change talk effectively, with skillful responses like 'That's you telling me what actually matters to you.' Could have gone further in developing and expanding the change talk to its fullest potential, such as exploring confidence or ability when it emerges.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by not arguing or taking sides, using it as a bridge rather than a target. Could have been more strategically explicit with double-sided reflections to actively redirect away from the sustain talk when it appears.
