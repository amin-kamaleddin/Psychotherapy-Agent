# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and respectful, but generic. Warmth was mostly procedural and not individualized to the patient.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequate factual reflections on the ankle concern. Limited emotional exploration, especially around alcohol use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session remained focused on ankle rehabilitation. The stated MI target of alcohol use was largely abandoned.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative planning was effective for the ankle. Collaboration did not extend to the alcohol use topic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Patient choices were respected in the ankle plan. Autonomy support was not applied to alcohol use decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some evocative questions used for rehab priorities. Patient’s own change-related insight about drinking was not followed up.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor used collaborative language and invited input. Partnership did not maintain focus on the MI referral target.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to evoke or amplify change talk about alcohol use. The final insight about drinking and the fall was missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Patient’s sustain talk about drinking was bypassed rather than explored. No gentle reframing or double-sided reflection was used.
