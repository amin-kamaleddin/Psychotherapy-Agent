# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a warm, nonjudgmental tone and validated the client’s concerns about failure. Minor lapses occurred when re-asking about rehearsing a script after the client preferred to keep things simple.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor used reflective listening and acknowledged the value of simplicity. However, it missed deeper emotional nuances around fear of overthinking and turning a habit into a test.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on moving from values clarification to a concrete, manageable morning habit. Repetition at the end and unclear ownership of the plan slightly weakened flow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invited input and co-created parts of the plan, but remained counselor-led by offering many specifics. Re-offering a declined option suggested incomplete collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently supported client choice and framed the plan as an experiment. Repeatedly pressing a declined rehearsal option reduced autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicited some motivation by exploring values and confidence. The session moved too quickly into practical planning without deeply developing the client’s intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invited input and used collaborative language, but the client’s role felt less central. The phrase “I’ll set my phone” and re-offering rehearsal blurred ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor drew out useful change talk around action and confidence. It did not consistently deepen desire, ability, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handled concerns about failure and self-judgment with low-pressure, compassionate language. It could more actively pivot sustain talk into change talk.
