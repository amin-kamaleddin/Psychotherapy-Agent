# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates ambivalence without moralizing. Acceptance is clear but somewhat formulaic and does not fully affirm Amina's worth beyond the change process.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There are strong reflective statements that accurately capture Amina's ambivalence and circular pattern. The empathy is not yet deep enough to fully explore the shame, fear, and identity meaning behind her statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on a concrete, manageable target: a short phone pause around the tea. The focus could be strengthened by connecting the pause more explicitly to Amina's broader goals and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina's tea idea helps shape the session, and the counselor invites input rather than simply instructing. However, the counselor frequently introduces specific steps before Amina fully generates them, reducing the collaborative balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by emphasizing that the urge can remain and that the goal is not perfection or complete abstinence. Autonomy is somewhat limited by repeated specific behavioral details such as face-down phone and timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks evocative questions about what would make a small pause work and what success might look like. Evocation is limited because specific suggestions are offered quickly and deeper values or future-oriented motivation are not consistently explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites Amina's ideas and affirms her adjustments, showing some collaboration. However, the repeated introduction of a specific behavioral script keeps the counselor more directive than fully partnership-based.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina offers some change talk, especially around feeling less like she is missing out and actually drinking the tea. The counselor does not consistently amplify, reflect, or expand this change talk into stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Amina's sustain talk and gently normalizes the pull to check. However, there is limited strategic use of double-sided reflections, reframing, or discrepancy to more effectively soften the sustain talk.
