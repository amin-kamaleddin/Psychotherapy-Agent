### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Diego is consistently positioned as the expert on his own situation; the psychiatrist builds on Diego's ideas rather than introducing new ones, and the session feels like a conversation between equals.
