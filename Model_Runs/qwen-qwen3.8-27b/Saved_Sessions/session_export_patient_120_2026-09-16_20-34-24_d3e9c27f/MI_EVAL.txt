# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Sofia’s ambivalence without blaming. Some responses subtly frame worth through protecting the baby rather than unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening to capture Sofia’s mixed feelings, social fears, and sensory triggers. Deeper themes such as guilt, self-hatred, and loss of identity could have been explored more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear focus on avoiding alcohol in a high-risk social situation and moves logically toward a concrete plan. The direction becomes narrow, with limited connection to broader values or long-term abstinence goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works collaboratively, building on Sofia’s ideas for an exit strategy and coping anchors. The session is partly therapist-guided because the psychiatrist supplies many options rather than fully letting Sofia set priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Sofia’s agency by asking for her input and validating her preferred approach. Repeated maternal obligation language can subtly reduce her sense of free choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes Sofia’s own solutions and change-oriented statements through open questions and reflective prompts. There is less sustained evocation of deeper values, confidence, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a non-authoritarian stance and invites Sofia’s perspective throughout. Power sharing could be stronger by giving Sofia more control over agenda and direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia generates meaningful change talk about protecting her baby and not being the mother who caves. The psychiatrist could more consistently amplify and deepen this change talk before moving into problem-solving.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument, which is appropriate. However, some reflections keep the social identity problem central rather than softening it toward change motivation.
