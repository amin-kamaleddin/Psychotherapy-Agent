# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental language and repeated affirmations. No moralizing or disapproval, even when Matteo minimized the issue.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of ambivalence, boredom, and social pressure. Could go deeper into the emotional meaning of embarrassment, fear of standing out, and identity concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and related goals. Session stayed on target through planning, scaling, and behavior-specific strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative planning with permission-based offers and incorporation of Matteo’s ideas. Slightly uneven because the psychiatrist frequently supplied multiple options, scripts, and structured steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong respect for choice, low pressure, and permission-based language. Some detailed suggestions and scripts may subtly guide Matteo toward preferred actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Importance and confidence rulers elicit some internal motivation and increase confidence. The session often shifts to practical planning rather than deeply exploring values, meaning, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Non-authoritarian stance with invitations for Matteo’s input and choices. The psychiatrist sometimes acts more like a planner/coach by offering many concrete strategies and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out change talk through importance/confidence scaling and doable-step questions. Could more deliberately amplify and reflect Matteo’s own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and often normalizes sustain talk and ambivalence. However, the session frequently moves to planning before fully exploring or reframing the “for and against” tension.
