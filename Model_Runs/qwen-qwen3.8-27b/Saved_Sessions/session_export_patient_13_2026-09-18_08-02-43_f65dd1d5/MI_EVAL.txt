# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, unconditional positive regard throughout with zero judgment of her avoidance or fear. Multiple instances of genuine affirmation and normalization of her ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate, emotionally attuned, and progressively deepen in complexity. Each reflection is specific to her language and captures underlying meaning (e.g., the 'freeze' image, the belief that her steadiness holds the world together).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear, progressive focus on the appointment target, moving from exploration through concrete plan formation. Ends with a specific next step (text tonight) and follow-up arrangement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session reads as an interaction between two equal partners; the psychiatrist never lectures or imposes an agenda. Client's ideas (sister, text format, timing, follow-up) drive the session direction completely.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, explicit reinforcement of Anara's ownership and choice throughout, with no directive or pressuring language. Phrases like 'you get to shape that too' and 'at whatever pace feels right' affirm her freedom.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open-ended questions skillfully elicit progressively deeper self-generated content, from initial exploration to concrete planning. The client speaks substantially and generates all key therapeutic material herself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates that wisdom resides within Anara, using language like 'you just found that path yourself' and 'you don't have to pick a side.' The session direction is entirely determined by her contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and amplifies change talk throughout, but misses one or two opportunities for deeper amplification. Statements like 'something just moved' and 'I think I will text her tonight' could have been more powerfully reflected back as commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging without amplifying and redirecting toward future possibilities. The 'everything falls apart' belief and 'made a huge deal out of nothing' fear are acknowledged but not more actively reframed or gently challenged.
