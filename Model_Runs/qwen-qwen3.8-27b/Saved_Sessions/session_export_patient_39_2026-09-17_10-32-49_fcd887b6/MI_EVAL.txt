# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows consistent warmth and individualized affirmation, reframing self-protective behaviors positively. It falls just short of explicit unconditional positive regard regardless of the client's choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are nuanced, often capturing emotional meaning and using the client's own language. A few deeper emotional points, such as "pills are easier" and the weight of feeling "heavy," are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays exceptionally focused on a specific target behavior and builds toward a concrete, time-limited plan. There is no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is framed as joint exploration and responds well to client input, creating a natural back-and-forth. It could be stronger with explicit agenda co-creation and a shared summary of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, with explicit emphasis on ownership and no directive pressure. Suggestions are offered as options rather than prescriptions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits internally generated change talk across desire, ability, reasons, and need. Motivational pushes come from the client's own words rather than external advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the client as an expert on his own life and body, consistently asking what feels realistic for him. Some suggestions still carry a slightly top-down quality despite being framed as options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions are well crafted and elicit rich change talk, with strong reinforcement of client agency. The psychiatrist could more consistently amplify each piece of change talk with a follow-up reflection.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is generally acknowledged with empathy and redirected toward practical, safer options. "Pills are easier" and the final worry about excuses are not fully softened before the session ends.
