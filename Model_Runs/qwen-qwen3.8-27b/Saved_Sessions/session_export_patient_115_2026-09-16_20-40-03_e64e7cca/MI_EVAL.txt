# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming of the patient’s efforts. Acceptance is strong but could be more deeply individualized and emotionally grounded.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures ambivalence and practical concerns. The patient’s fear of feeling trapped is not consistently tracked emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains focused on asthma and related triggers while narrowing from broad concern to specific, relevant behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as joint and builds on the patient’s own ideas. However, the topic progression remains largely therapist-guided rather than fully client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes the patient’s freedom to choose and avoids directive pressure. This strongly supports the patient’s sense of ownership over change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions draw out the patient’s own solutions and language. Deeper evocation of values, meaning, and self-efficacy would strengthen motivation further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites collaboration and positions the patient as the expert on his own life. More explicit shared agenda-setting would make the partnership more fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits and affirms useful change talk, such as wanting to be back in control. The strongest change talk could be amplified, deepened, and expanded more strategically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated without argument or moralizing. More skillful double-sided reflections would better hold ambivalence while opening a path to change.
