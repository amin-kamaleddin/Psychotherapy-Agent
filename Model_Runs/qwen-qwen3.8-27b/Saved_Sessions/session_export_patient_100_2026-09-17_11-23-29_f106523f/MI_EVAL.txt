# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful tone throughout the session with no labeling or disapproval. The opening framing, while effective, occasionally reads as slightly polished or scripted rather than fully spontaneous.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are consistently accurate, nuanced, and go beyond paraphrasing to capture emotional texture and subtle shifts in affect. Examples include mirroring Maya's own metaphors and validating her protective need to 'know something without it turning into a project.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session follows a clear logical arc from rapport-building through exploration of drinking's function and social context. It ends somewhat open without a summary or clear landing point that would tie together the key themes discussed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely collaborative in structure, with Maya's language and metaphors driving the conversation's direction. The counselor never lectures, advises, or imposes a clinical framework, staying entirely in Maya's experiential world.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is pervasive and consistent, with explicit statements like 'You're the expert on your own life' and no use of 'should' or 'need to' language. When Maya says she's 'not in a hurry,' the counselor validates without creating urgency or pushing back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All significant insights in the session are drawn from within Maya rather than imposed externally. The counselor's open questions and reflections serve to deepen Maya's own self-observation without introducing outside information or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently positions Maya as the expert and uses curious, non-directive language throughout. The power dynamic is explicitly addressed and honored when Maya raises concern about being 'sized up.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes desire and awareness-level change talk (e.g., 'it's not exactly helpful,' 'I'm not saying I'll never get there'). Could have gone further in eliciting confidence or ability language (DARC) to deepen motivation toward action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with remarkable skill, validating the social reality of party drinking and personality-based statements without challenging or minimizing. Sustain talk is reflected accurately and respectfully while preserving Maya's autonomy and keeping the door open for change.
