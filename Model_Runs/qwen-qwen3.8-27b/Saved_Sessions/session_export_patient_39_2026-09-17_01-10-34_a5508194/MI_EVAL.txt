# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental tone throughout. Warmth is generally strong, though it sometimes moves quickly into problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Elias’s fears, self-criticism, and ambivalence. The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates perspective-taking and emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains focused on increasing activity and medical routines. Each step logically moves toward concrete, realistic goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and Elias co-construct the plan rather than the counselor prescribing it. Elias’s ideas directly shape the direction and content of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly emphasizes Elias’s choice and decision-making authority. Options are offered supportively without pressure or imposed commitments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist often elicits Elias’s own motivations and solutions. The evocative quality could be strengthened by relying less on counselor-generated examples and more on deeper self-discovery.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Elias as the expert on his own experience. The interaction reflects a genuine equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias generates several concrete change statements and the psychiatrist builds on them. Change talk could be amplified more explicitly through reflection, summarizing, importance/confidence scaling, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged, normalized, and reframed without argument. The psychiatrist consistently reduces the power of defeat-focused thinking while maintaining realism.
