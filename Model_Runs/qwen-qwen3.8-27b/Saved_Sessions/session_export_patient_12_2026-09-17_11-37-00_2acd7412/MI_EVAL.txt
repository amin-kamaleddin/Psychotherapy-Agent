# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect and nonjudgment throughout, with strong affirmations like acknowledging Amira's strength in showing up. Falls short of a 5 due to warmth that remains professional rather than explicitly unconditional and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections that accurately capture ambivalence and emotional undertones, such as the 'not a contradiction, just where you are' reflection. Misses full emotional resonance on Amira's most vulnerable statements (e.g., 'I'm not just air'), tending to pivot to forward-looking questions too quickly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from safety-building to concrete planning without drifting off-topic. Could benefit from more time sitting in the contemplative space before introducing solution-focused questions, given Amira's early stage of change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently frames work as joint enterprise, offering options and inviting Amira's input on how to structure support. Slightly limited by the psychiatrist's guiding agenda toward predetermined outcomes rather than genuine openness to Amira leading the direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout—explicitly removes pressure ('no script,' 'no right answer'), defers decisions to Amira, and never implies she must make any particular choice. Consistently reinforces that all decisions are hers and the work is entirely within her control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within Amira through open questions rather than providing external reasons or advice. Could be more strategically targeted in evoking specific change talk categories (desire, ability, reasons, need, commitment).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with explicit invitations for input and open-ended questions that respect Amira's pace. Amira largely responds to prompts rather than leading the session, and there is no explicit negotiation of agenda or acknowledgment of her as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk through open questions, but fails to consistently amplify, reflect back, or build upon the change talk Amira offers. Misses opportunities to explicitly name her agency and resourcefulness (e.g., 'That's your idea, not mine').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates sustain talk without arguing or challenging, using normalizing language like 'that's not a contradiction, it's just where you are.' Could use more double-sided reflections and slightly more gradual transitions from sustain talk toward change talk.
