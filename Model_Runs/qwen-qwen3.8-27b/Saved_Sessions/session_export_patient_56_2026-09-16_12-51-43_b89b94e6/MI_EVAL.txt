# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming stance throughout. Warmth was solid but somewhat formulaic, with room for more individualized emotional acknowledgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were generally accurate and helpful, capturing key values and concerns. Some reflections stayed at paraphrase rather than fully capturing deeper self-doubt or emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on the target behavior and actionable plan. Direction was clear, consistent, and well-maintained from beginning to end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Isolde’s ideas clearly shaped the final plan and choices. The collaboration could become more balanced by inviting client-generated solutions before offering structured options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently honored Isolde’s choices and avoided pressuring her. Slightly more emphasis on her full authorship of the plan would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively drew out motivations, confidence, and practical resources. Evocation could deepen by connecting the plan more explicitly to core values and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited input and allowed Isolde to make meaningful decisions. The approach could shift further toward co-creation by asking her to generate ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was evoked and reinforced, with confidence increasing by the end. Responses could more consistently amplify the meaning and self-efficacy behind her change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument or dismissal. More strategic double-sided reflections could better reframe self-limiting language while maintaining momentum.
