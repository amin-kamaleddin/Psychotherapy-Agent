# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with warmth that is individualized and genuine rather than formulaic. Zero judgment or conditional regard at any point in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that precisely track Samir's emotional landscape, including the functional purpose of behaviors and the deeper significance of the son's behavior. Each reflection builds on the previous one with increasing depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical arc from establishing safety to identifying triggers to concrete environmental changes, without wandering. Could have moved slightly more toward a summary of shared understanding or concrete next step before closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative dynamic with open questions that invite Samir's perspective as the primary source of truth. Could be deepened by more explicit co-creation moments such as inviting Samir to prioritize among identified areas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is woven naturally into every interaction, never using directive language or expressing disappointment at Samir's pace. The underlying posture of the entire session consistently honors Samir's self-determination.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently evokes motivation from within Samir rather than providing it externally, trusting his own observations as more powerful motivators. Could be deepened by more explicitly eliciting confidence statements and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions as a partner rather than expert, frequently handing interpretive authority back to Samir. Power-sharing is consistent but could be further equalized by modeling vulnerability or acknowledging the psychiatrist's own uncertainty.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits desire, reasons, and ability language through well-timed questions that draw out Samir's own motivations. Could be elevated by more explicitly developing change talk across all DARN-CAT categories, particularly commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and validated while being gently redirected to the client's own data without challenge. Could be stronger when Samir minimizes the significance of his own data points, potentially using a double-sided reflection or rolling-with technique.
