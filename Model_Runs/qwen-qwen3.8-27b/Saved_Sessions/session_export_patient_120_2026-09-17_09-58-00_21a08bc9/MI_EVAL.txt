# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance throughout. Sofia was able to express guilt, fear, and ambivalence without being shamed or corrected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Sofia’s ambivalence between motherhood and social identity. Minor repetitiveness and limited deeper exploration of baby-related fear kept it below a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and related social triggers. Occasional drift toward broader identity/performance issues reduced the tightness of the MI focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was highly collaborative, with the therapist and Sofia working as equal partners. Client-generated ideas clearly shaped the session and problem-solving plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforced Sofia’s ownership of change using language like “you know” and “what do you think.” No pressure or directive “shoulds” were evident.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions helped Sofia access her own values, concerns, and solutions. Deeper exploration of intrinsic values or long-term identity could strengthen motivation further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The therapist clearly positioned Sofia as the expert on her own life and values. The session followed her lead rather than imposing an external agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia generated concrete change ideas, such as breathing on the balcony and a morning coffee routine. More explicit exploration of desire, ability, reasons, and confidence would elevate this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk around friendship and comfort was accepted and gently reframed rather than challenged. Additional double-sided reflections could more directly resolve ambivalence.
