# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Sofia's ambivalence without shame. It falls short of 5 because deeper guilt, fear, and emotional difficulty are not fully affirmed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Sofia's ambivalence, loneliness, and social conflict. It is not a 5 because the clinician sometimes summarizes more than deeply exploring underlying fear and identity loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on reducing alcohol use in social contexts and moves toward a concrete coping plan. There is no meaningful drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia's ideas strongly shape the plan, and the psychiatrist works with her rather than imposing solutions. It is not a 5 because the clinician still carries the primary steering and interpretation role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids coercion and frames actions as small, doable experiments. It could be stronger by more explicitly affirming Sofia's right to choose otherwise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions prompt Sofia to generate her own values, scripts, and coping strategies, producing natural change talk. It is mainly practical rather than systematically evoking deeper DARN-C change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is positioned as the expert on her own goals, and her contributions drive the session. It lacks more explicit shared-power language and negotiated agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits and affirms Sofia's change talk about choosing alternatives and keeping baby first. It could more fully expand deeper desire, ability, reasons, need, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk about friends and social judgment is reflected without argument and redirected toward her goals. It could use more double-sided reflections to reduce the intensity of fear-based reasoning.
