# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating of Samir’s concerns and life pressures. Approval sometimes focuses on the plan rather than unconditional regard for Samir as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Samir’s practical worries and ambivalence. There is less depth in exploring the underlying fear, exhaustion, and sense of being controlled by smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing smoking during the first hour of the shift. The conversation moves logically from goals to planning without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samir’s preferences meaningfully shape the plan, including patch-only use, water, podcast, and text follow-up. The psychiatrist still supplies many structured strategies, limiting some client-led co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Samir’s ownership, choice, and independence. Reassuring that silence does not equal failure reduces feelings of being managed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session uses helpful evocative questions such as importance/confidence scaling and past success. However, therapist-led advice and psychoeducation limit opportunities to deepen Samir’s own change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Samir to choose and respects his preferences. At times the psychiatrist takes a more expert-like role by offering detailed scripts and options before Samir generates more of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk, including Samir’s goal to keep his lungs clear and increased confidence. There are missed opportunities to amplify Samir’s past success, values, and ability to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing or pressuring Samir when he expresses readiness limits and preference for simplicity. The responses do not consistently use double-sided reflections to redirect sustain talk toward change.
