# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistent, nonjudgmental and validating stance while avoiding shame and labels. Some responses feel slightly formulaic or plan-focused rather than deeply affirming the patient’s worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects exhaustion, stuckness, ambivalence, and automatic behaviors. Misses opportunities to capture deeper feelings such as shame, self-doubt, or identity distress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively narrows a broad, distressing presentation into a specific and manageable target. The session becomes somewhat procedural and may narrow too quickly from broader life stress to a single coping behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and builds on the patient’s preferences and choices. Collaboration is uneven because the psychiatrist frequently supplies concrete options and solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice, frames the plan as low-risk, and avoids coercion. Could be strengthened by more explicitly stating that the patient owns the decision and that no outcome is required.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions, scaling, and confidence-building questions to elicit motivation and commitment. Evocation remains procedural and moves too quickly into concrete suggestions rather than exploring values and internal reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites input and allows the patient’s choices to shape the plan. The interaction still feels somewhat counselor-guided due to the number of options and tips supplied.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and affirms some change talk and commitment. Could more consistently reflect, amplify, and deepen the meaning of those change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with or reinforcing hopeless statements and often normalizes them. Softening relies mostly on neutral acknowledgment rather than strategic double-sided reflections or redirecting to exceptions.
