# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Elias’s ambivalence and progress. It falls short of a 5 mainly because the warmth becomes repetitive and lacks more explicit unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections capture ambivalence and meaningfully identify the identity-based struggle around being a “dad” versus fear of being broken. Some reflections remain somewhat surface-level and do not fully explore the deeper shame and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation stays focused on high-risk evenings and progresses toward concrete, client-generated actions. It would be stronger with clearer summarization, consolidation, and a stronger transition to commitment or follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds the plan from Elias’s own ideas rather than imposing a predetermined approach. It falls slightly short of fully collaborative because the psychiatrist still guides with a similar sequence and some framing suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elias is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, with language emphasizing choice, options, and his own plan. It could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming his right to modify or stop the plan at any time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits concrete self-generated strategies rather than giving advice. Evocation remains more task-focused than motivational and does not fully deepen values, identity, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session follows Elias’s perspective and treats him as the expert on his own life. It would be stronger with more explicit agenda negotiation and acknowledgment of Elias as an equal partner in directing the work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently amplifies Elias’s own goals and evokes useful change talk through concrete next steps. It would be stronger if change talk were deepened by exploring importance, confidence, values, or self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without being reinforced, but it is not effectively softened or resolved. The central ambivalence about stopping out of fear rather than true desire remains unresolved.
