# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, validating stance and reduces shame. Some opening language and praise can feel mildly pressuring or appraisal-centered rather than fully accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and supportive, especially around ambivalence and exhaustion. It does not deeply explore the emotional meaning behind her shame or family-related concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear smoking cessation target, especially delaying smoking after work. The opening could have been tighter before introducing multiple topics and benefits.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is clearly co-constructed from Anisa’s preferences, including the car, playlist, and simple text check-in. The counselor sometimes introduces multiple options early, which can feel partially counselor-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is consistent and explicit, emphasizing choice, non-binding steps, and no pressure. The counselor avoids judgment and supports Anisa’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Anisa’s own motivations and confidence. Providing several reasons and behavioral suggestions occasionally reduces the purely evocative quality.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses a collaborative stance and lets Anisa’s contributions shape the plan. Some expert-led suggestion-giving limits full partnership at times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits change talk and reinforces Anisa’s realistic goals. There are missed opportunities to further amplify her own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Resistance is handled without argument or pressure, and slips are framed as data rather than failure. Some barrier-focused questions keep sustain talk active instead of pivoting toward change talk.
