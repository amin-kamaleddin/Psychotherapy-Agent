# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates warmth, respect, and non-judgment without ever labeling or expressing disapproval. Slightly structured rather than fully spontaneous, lacking moments of deeper personal vulnerability or explicit unconditional acceptance language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and nuanced empathic understanding throughout, including complex reflections that capture identity-level exhaustion and the disconnect between guilt and reality. Empathy is consistent, deep, and rarely generic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical thread building naturally from alcohol use through stress, circularity, guilt, values, and connection to behavior change. Does not arrive at a concrete action step or negotiate a specific focus for next steps before the session ends.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally well-executed co-construction of meaning through genuine dialogue, with the psychiatrist picking up on the client's specific language and building on it as a partner thinking together. Power dynamic is genuinely equal, with all insight attributed to the client's own noticing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with zero directive language, explicit permission to not know, and consistent deferral to the client's own judgment. The psychiatrist never imposes a timeline, expresses disappointment about pace, or suggests that inaction is a failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful use of open questions, reflections, and strategic pauses to draw motivation from within the client rather than importing it from outside. All change talk emerges from the client's own mouth, with questions building like a staircase from surface behavior to values-based motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently operates from radical partnership with immediate power-sharing, explicit checking-in on the client's experience of the process, and genuine invitation of the client's perspective. The session is entirely client-led in content, with the psychiatrist serving as a skilled facilitator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk, explicitly highlighting and affirming key moments of client-generated insight. Could have more explicitly developed the client's confidence statements (ability talk) when she expressed self-doubt about past attempts at moderation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging and holding it without arguing, contradicting, or dismissing, while exploring the function of the behavior without judgment. Minor gap in that the final sustain talk/self-efficacy doubt statement lacks a visible response in the transcript.
