# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses respectful, nonjudgmental language and normalizes Marco’s ambivalence and self-criticism. Acceptance could be strengthened with more explicit affirmations of Marco’s strengths and agency.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurately captures Marco’s internal conflict. Empathy could be deepened by reflecting the emotional weight of shame, fear, and repeated self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the target behavior and progresses logically from general goals to a concrete, workable plan. There is no meaningful drift from the session focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Marco’s own contributions rather than supplying the plan. The interaction feels genuinely collaborative and tracks Marco’s own language and concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently avoids controlling language, advice-giving, or pressure. Marco is supported in defining success in his own terms and making his own choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open, evocative questions effectively draw out Marco’s own reasons, preferences, and sense of capability. The psychiatrist helps Marco access internal motivation rather than imposing external rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance and consistently invites Marco’s input. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicitly stating that Marco’s judgment and agenda guide the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes change talk, and Marco generates meaningful steps and reasons for trying. The skill could be strengthened by more actively amplifying and reinforcing Marco’s emerging change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by acknowledging concerns and redirecting toward more workable frames. The use of double-sided reflections could be more consistent to fully hold both the problem and change sides.
