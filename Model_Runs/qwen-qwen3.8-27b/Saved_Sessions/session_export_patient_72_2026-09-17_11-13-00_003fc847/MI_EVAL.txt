# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates warm, unconditional positive regard, meeting Leila exactly where she is without judgment or correction. Responses are specific and individualized rather than formulaic, matching her pragmatic tone while adding depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond paraphrasing to capture emotional nuance with precise calibration (e.g., 'almost tender,' 'a gap between who you were and where you are now'). Leila consistently confirms accuracy ('that tracks actually,' 'receipt is actually a good word').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains tight focus on the target behavior (re-establishing exercise) while progressing strategically from motivation through plan, coping, and specific action criteria. Each question moves the conversation forward without drift, ending with a concrete, actionable endpoint.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed, with Leila's ideas directly shaping the direction at every turn. Language is consistently collaborative ('I'm curious,' 'Is that fair?'), with no expert-driven stance or uneven power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every decision, plan, and criterion is explicitly framed as Leila's own, with no directive language or recommendations. The 'permission to say nah' framing powerfully reinforces her right to reject without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is drawn from within Leila through well-timed open questions that progressively deepen from general vision to specific action plans and self-monitoring criteria. At no point does the psychiatrist provide external reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently and explicitly positions Leila as the author of her own solutions ('That's not me saying that, that's you,' 'You've written your own criteria'). No advice is offered; every intervention is a question or reflection of Leila's own words.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk flows naturally and frequently from Leila, elicited through strategic open questions, affirmations, and reflections that amplify her existing resources. The evocation progressively deepens from motivation to plan to coping to specific action criteria.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Each barrier (all-or-nothing thinking, fear of rejection, sales pressure, shame) is acknowledged without dismissal or argument, then met with a client-generated resolution that the psychiatrist amplifies. No sustain talk is ever reinforced or minimized.
