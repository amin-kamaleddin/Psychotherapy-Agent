# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Andrei’s constraints and choices in a respectful, nonjudgmental way. It falls short of a 5 because the warmth is practical and situational rather than explicitly affirming his worth or emotional burden.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, accurately capturing ambivalence, practical limits, and self-regulation. It remains primarily cognitive and behavioral, with less exploration of deeper emotions such as frustration, fear, or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is well structured, moving from blood pressure concerns to a concrete caffeine-reduction plan. The main limitation is that the connection back to broader weight-loss and lifestyle goals is not fully developed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is clearly co-constructed, with Andrei proposing the swap, fallback, time limit, cue, and monitoring approach. The psychiatrist builds on these ideas rather than imposing a prescribed solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes choice, self-control, and freedom from extra demands or guilt. Andrei is given clear space to reject or modify the plan without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits client-generated solutions and self-management strategies. Evocation remains practical rather than deeply exploring values, confidence, fears, or future vision.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Andrei is consistently positioned as the expert on his own schedule and workload. His choices substantially guide the session and the resulting plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions successfully elicit clear change talk and practical planning language. The psychiatrist could more systematically evoke deeper DARN-C change talk, such as importance, confidence, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles barriers and sustain talk well without arguing, shaming, or amplifying resistance. It is not a 5 because these barriers are not consistently pivoted into broader change motivation or reduced in their dominance.
