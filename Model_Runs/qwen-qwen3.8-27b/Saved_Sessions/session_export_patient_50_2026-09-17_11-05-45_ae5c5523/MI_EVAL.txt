# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental opening establishes a strong foundation, but repeated identical generic responses in the final three turns convey a flat, disengaged affect that undercuts the warmth. The psychiatrist fails to re-engage with Hana's substantial and vulnerable disclosures in the closing portion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The first five turns demonstrate specific, emotionally attuned reflections tracking Hana's somatic experience, self-doubt, and ambivalence with precision. However, the final three turns collapse into a neutral, non-empathic continuation prompt precisely when Hana is sharing her most meaningful and vulnerable material.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a coherent logical progression from exploring anxiety impact to identifying a behavioral target, addressing resistance, and finding a minimally viable step. However, the session lacks strategic closure — no summary of the arc, no consolidated plan, and no mutually agreed-upon concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The early portions show genuine collaboration with reflective partnering, inclusive language, and a non-authoritative stance. The three consecutive identical responses in the final third represent a notable breakdown, replacing collaborative engagement with a simple prompt for the client to continue alone.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Hana as the agent of her own choices with key affirmations of her self-knowledge and pace. The generic responses in the closing turns, while not directive, fail to explicitly reinforce her ownership of the process and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-calibrated open questions in the first two-thirds effectively draw out Hana's own motivations, values, and self-generated strategies without imposing an external framework. In the final three turns, evocation reduces to the same generic prompt, missing the chance to deepen Hana's emerging insights.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriate partnership statements early on ('You know your own brain better than I do') establish a collaborative tone. However, the repeated identical responses in the final third convey disengagement and fail to weave Hana's significant self-generated insights back into a genuine co-creative dialogue.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist's open questions and reflective stance successfully elicit a rich progression of change talk, from self-doubt to a self-generated behavioral plan to recognition of incremental capacity. However, the strongest change talk moments in the closing turns are met with generic prompts rather than being labeled, amplified, or strategically consolidated.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled softening moves early on, including redirecting the 'voice doesn't listen' concern and reframing the 'too short' worry without dismissing or arguing. In the final turns, the opportunity to affirm Hana's honesty about the gap between showing up here and walking into the exam room while gently turning it toward hope is missed.
