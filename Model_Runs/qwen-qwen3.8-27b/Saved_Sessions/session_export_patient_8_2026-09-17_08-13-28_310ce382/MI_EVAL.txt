# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, nonjudgment, and normalization of Amina’s experience. Warmth is strong but occasionally polished, and the final “RESPONSE 2/3” labels create a mild rupture.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate, nuanced, and emotionally attuned, capturing ambivalence, fatigue, FOMO, and self-judgment. The “tether” reflection is especially strong because it links the phone to safety and connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays meaningfully focused on late-night scrolling, sleep, and connection, though it occasionally drifts into broader anxiety/being away from home. A clearer closing summary or single next step would strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina’s language and metaphors shape the session, reflecting strong collaboration. The counselor occasionally leads with elaborate interpretations, and the final multiple formulations weaken co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids directives and respects Amina’s goals, values, and pace. Autonomy support would be stronger with more explicit reinforcement that the choice is entirely hers, especially when she expresses self-doubt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Amina’s own values, discrepancies, and emerging change talk. However, the work does not fully develop change talk through scaling, ability/past success, or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generally invites Amina’s perspective and builds on her contributions, maintaining a collaborative stance. Some counselor-led interpretations and the final multiple response options slightly reduce equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions successfully elicit goals, values, and concrete change talk about sleep, connection, and healthier phone use. The counselor could more systematically amplify Amina’s change language and elicit DARN-C or confidence/importance talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is validated without arguing or minimizing, and both sides of ambivalence are held. The counselor skillfully reframes reasons to keep the phone in ways that remain compatible with change.
