# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates David's concerns about side effects and workload. Empathy is strong, though at times more logistical than deeply affirming of his ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections and paraphrases that show understanding of David's practical perspective. Some deeper emotional themes, such as fear of aging or resistance to medical labels, remain underexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on cholesterol management and a workable behavioral plan. Direction is mostly clear, but the connection between the small step and the larger medication/cholesterol decision could be made more explicit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with David contributing to the plan, alarm wording, and follow-up preference. The psychiatrist still provides a fairly structured plan, limiting full client-driven exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** David's autonomy is supported by emphasizing that medication decisions remain with him and offering choices. The detailed clinician-generated plan slightly reduces the sense of full client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks useful questions about feasibility and confidence, but many are logistical rather than motivational. There is less depth in eliciting David's values, reasons for change, or stronger confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites David's input and respects his preferences for a low-key plan. The session remains largely guided by the psychiatrist's structure and medical guidance rather than fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** David offers some commitment language, such as willingness to try the sandwich swap and call his doctor if symptoms occur. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify this change talk or explore stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with David's concerns and does not dismiss his side-effect fears. However, the sustain talk is addressed more by moving to a smaller plan than by using reflections or reconciliation to soften it.
