# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Isabella's ambivalence without pressure. Acceptance was solid but somewhat structured, with limited deeper unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used strong reflective listening and accurately captured conflicting feelings around safety, isolation, and connection. Some reflections felt slightly polished and did not fully explore deeper emotional impacts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on increasing low-pressure community connection and reducing social isolation. Direction was consistent but could have moved more strategically toward a concrete collaborative next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used collaborative language and invited Isabella's preferences into the discussion. The interaction could be more fully co-constructed, as the psychiatrist still largely guided the sequence of topics.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supported Isabella's choice, repeatedly emphasizing that any next step was completely optional. The client's autonomy was consistently respected without advice, pressure, or expectation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used open questions to draw out Isabella's own ideas and conditions for a possible step. Evocation remained surface-level and could have more deeply accessed values, confidence, or intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Isabella as the expert on her experience and invited her input in shaping possible options. Partnership was strong, though the counselor still led the conversation more than fully co-creating it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evoked some change talk around brief, low-pressure options such as getting air or peeking outside. Change talk remained tentative and could have been more amplified, summarized, and strategically expanded.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepted Isabella's sustain talk without arguing or invalidating her. The session could more effectively acknowledge the safety of staying home while reframing it toward compatible, small steps toward change.
