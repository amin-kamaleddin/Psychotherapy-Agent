# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental while normalizing Yasmin’s frustration. Acceptance could be stronger by affirming her worth independently of weight or adherence and staying longer with ambivalence before problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses accurate reflections of Yasmin’s exhaustion, fear of yo-yoing, and reliance on the scale. Deeper empathy would explore the emotional meaning behind her fear of the number rather than quickly moving to plans.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on Yasmin’s diet, weight, scale use, and sustainable habits. Direction becomes narrow when repeatedly shifting among scale, food, and sleep without consistently returning to the core non-diet goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites Yasmin to choose options and co-creates a time-limited plan. Collaboration would improve by generating the framework from Yasmin’s own ideas first rather than supplying many structured options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently emphasizes choice and avoids coercion, and Yasmin feels respected and not pushed. More explicit reminders that all steps are optional and fully hers to modify would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early questions elicit Yasmin’s goals around energy, food tension, and gym experience. Evocation becomes procedural later, with less exploration of her values, past successes, and confidence in change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Yasmin chooses from counselor-offered options, showing some partnership. The counselor still takes a strong expert problem-solving role by introducing checklists, safeguards, and stepwise plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some change talk around wanting a realistic plan and less exhaustion. Change talk is not consistently amplified, reflected, or deepened through MI techniques such as importance or confidence rulers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Yasmin’s sustain talk about weight loss and scale feedback remains central to the plan. The counselor acknowledges but does not sufficiently soften it, keeping the session focused on weight-related outcomes rather than broader well-being.
