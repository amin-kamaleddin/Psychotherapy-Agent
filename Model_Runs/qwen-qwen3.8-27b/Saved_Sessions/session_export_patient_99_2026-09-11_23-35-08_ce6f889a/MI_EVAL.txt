# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates nonjudgmental respect and validates the patient's perspective without moralizing. Warmth is generally appropriate, though occasionally procedural rather than deeply personal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates accurate reflective listening and summary of the patient's concerns. Empathy remains largely cognitive rather than deeply exploring emotional undercurrents such as shame or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on alcohol use as the target behavior and progresses logically from exploration to a specific plan. Direction is strategic, though repeated restatement of the plan slightly reduces forward momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is fully collaborative, with the patient consistently helping shape the conversation and plan. The final plan reflects the patient's chosen actions, thresholds, and evaluation criteria.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly affirms the patient's freedom to choose and accepts the patient's self-set limits without pressure. Autonomy support is strong but could more explicitly name the patient's agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The question 'Why a 2 and not a 0?' effectively elicits the patient's own reasons for change. Evocation is effective but does not deeply explore values, identity, or broader ability talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the session as collaborative and offers choices that respect patient agency. However, the psychiatrist still drives much of the agenda and structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questions successfully generate the patient's own change talk, especially regarding protecting the ankle. Change talk could be further developed by exploring values, identity, and future self-image.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing it. Active softening techniques such as double-sided reflections or reframing resistance are largely absent.
