# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepts Yasmin’s ambivalence without pressure. Warmth and positive regard could be more explicit and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Yasmin’s ambivalence, values, and concerns about the scale and self-worth. More affective empathy would strengthen the response to her fear, shame, and uncertainty.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist stays tightly focused on moving away from rigid dieting and toward a sustainable relationship with food and the scale. The session progresses clearly from broad goals to a workable two-week experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session feels collaborative because Yasmin’s ideas shape the plan, such as the scale frequency and gym-centered progress. Some therapist-generated frames make the interaction slightly less equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Yasmin is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, with invitations for her own perspective and minimal pressure. The language around low stakes and voluntary experimentation strongly supports ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently evokes motivation by asking Yasmin to identify what would make change possible and meaningful. Reducing therapist-framed questions could make the process more client-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Yasmin’s experience drives much of the session, and the psychiatrist avoids imposing a preset plan. More explicit invitations for her to shape the agenda and validate therapist framings would strengthen equality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Yasmin frequently generates change talk, and the psychiatrist often reflects and affirms it. There is room to deepen or amplify her strongest statements more deliberately.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respectfully normalizes Yasmin’s desire to lose weight and her concern about the scale without arguing against it. Deeper reframing of her fear that non-restriction means loss of control or weight gain would complete the softening.
