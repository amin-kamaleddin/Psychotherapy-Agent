# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opens with warm, affirming language and maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout. Normalizes the difficulty of being concerned about by friends without minimizing her experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening, accurately capturing emotional nuances such as loneliness and the paradox of the drinking pattern. One response ('I understand. Can you tell me more about that?') is somewhat thin compared to the other reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and logical progression from assessment through exploration to early change planning. Each question builds on the last, with a minor lapse in direction during the thin mid-session response.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and positions herself as a resource alongside the client rather than above her. The session ends somewhat abruptly without a clear collaborative wrap-up or mutual agreement on next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout aspect of the session. Consistently reinforces the client's autonomy, validates her own framing ('do it less'), and never pressures or imposes strategies without inviting input.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out the client's own motivations and concerns rather than prescribing. Could be strengthened by more explicitly exploring the client's values to deepen discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance throughout using inclusive language. The session feels like a genuine dialogue rather than an interview, with minimal areas for improvement.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully cultivates change talk through open questions that invite the client to articulate her own concerns and reflects change talk affirmatively. The client generates substantial change talk as a result.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk by redirecting to internal experience rather than arguing or dismissing. One area for growth: the final sustain talk statement ('I don't think I'm an alcoholic') could have been more actively softened before ending.
