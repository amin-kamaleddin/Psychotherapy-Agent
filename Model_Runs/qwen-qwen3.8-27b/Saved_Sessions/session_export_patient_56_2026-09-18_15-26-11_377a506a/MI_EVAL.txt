# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates consistently warm, nonjudgmental behavior throughout, never labeling or minimizing Isolde's experience. Validation is specific and individualized rather than formulaic, as seen in phrases like 'That's not overthinking. That's a person checking in with herself.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate and precise, consistently deepening rather than flattening the client's experience. Standout examples include 'The you underneath is getting quieter,' which Isolde herself picks up and builds upon.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on the presence-versus-performance thread with logical progression from naming the feeling to articulating next steps. Slightly limited by an open-ended closing that does not fully land on a concrete behavioral commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as a model of equal partnership, with Isolde's ideas consistently shaping direction and the psychiatrist never imposing structure or agenda. Language is consistently inclusive and co-constructed rather than prescriptive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reinforces Isolde's ownership and freedom to choose from the first sentence ('this space is yours') through the closing ('Not a plan. Just what might it look like'). No directive language, prescriptions, or conditions are imposed at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight in the session—'rhythm not destination,' 'making room,' 'still me in there'—is generated by Isolde, with the psychiatrist serving as a reflective mirror. Key evocative moves include 'I'm curious what that sentence would end with, if you let it,' which draws out her own completion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly disclaims expert authority and positions Isolde as the expert on her own life throughout. The session is structured as genuine exploration where the client's contributions drive direction—textbook MI partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits and amplifies Isolde's own change-oriented language, using her phrases as vehicles for further exploration without injecting external reasons for change. The session ends with Isolde articulating her own next steps, a clear product of cultivated change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is minimal given Isolde's maintenance stage and low ambivalence, limiting the opportunity to demonstrate this skill. The psychiatrist avoids any adversarial dynamic when mild resistance appears, though the criterion could not be fully scored at the highest level.
