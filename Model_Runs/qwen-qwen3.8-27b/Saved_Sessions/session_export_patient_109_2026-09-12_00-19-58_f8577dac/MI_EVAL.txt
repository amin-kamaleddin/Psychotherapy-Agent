# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalized Rafael’s difficulty without blaming him. The warmth was strong but often tied to task-focused advice rather than deeper personal affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist showed some validation and perspective-taking, especially around common frustration and fear of judgment. However, the session moved quickly into problem-solving, limiting deeper emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target behavior of rebuilding a small exercise routine. The direction was clear, though it became more implementation-focused than motivational.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited input and adapted to Rafael’s choices, allowing him to shape the plan. The interaction remained partly client-led, but the clinician supplied most of the ideas and options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered choices and respected Rafael’s preferences, such as not involving a coworker. However, the repeated prescriptive suggestions reduced the client’s sense of freedom and self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited some client-driven material, including the main barrier and a small first step. After the initial phase, the clinician supplied more content than they elicited from Rafael.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for Rafael’s preferences and allowed him to make several choices. At times, the rapid delivery of concrete strategies made the role feel more like a coach than a collaborative partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited some change talk around intention, ability, and small practical steps. More of Rafael’s own values, desires, and reasons for change could have been drawn out and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Rafael’s barriers and softened all-or-nothing thinking through small steps and fallbacks. There were missed opportunities to explore the function of his self-protective rationalizations.
