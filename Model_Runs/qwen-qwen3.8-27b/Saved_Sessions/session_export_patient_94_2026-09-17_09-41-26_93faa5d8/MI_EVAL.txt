# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Maria's concerns and time constraints. Occasionally adds positive motives that Maria has not explicitly stated, which slightly reduces unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening, especially with ambivalence about medication and distrust of external sources. Some reflections are leading or include details not clearly present in Maria's statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on asthma management, practical inhaler use, and a concrete next step. Direction is occasionally overly aimed at accepting the medication plan rather than fully negotiating the treatment goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and adapts the plan to Maria's preference for emergency use. The agenda is still largely clinician-led, and more explicit negotiation of goals would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects Maria's choice and avoids pressuring daily use or shaming her for resistance. Some statements subtly steer toward the medication plan, and the quick move to prescribing feels slightly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions and helps Maria generate a workable emergency-use plan. Evocation is moderate because the psychiatrist sometimes interprets for Maria and does not use deeper values, scaling, or future-focused questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Maria's input, respects her constraints, and works toward a shared emergency-use plan. The session begins from an adherence-focused frame and remains somewhat clinician-led early on.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits meaningful change talk, such as keeping the inhaler as a backup and using it in an emergency. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen Maria's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing, moralizing, or reinforcing resistance to medication. The session spends considerable time on sustain talk without more consistently linking Maria's safety concerns to her own values or goals.
