# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, meeting every self-dismissal with warmth rather than pushback. Never labels or judges the client, and powerfully reframes self-criticism into understanding.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Empathic accuracy operates at multiple levels, identifying metacognitive patterns (the 'catch-finding' behavior, the 'I'm the problem' voice) that the client may not have been able to articulate. Captures both cognitive and emotional dimensions simultaneously with remarkable precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, coherent trajectory from rapport-building through exploration to practical problem-solving with a logical arc. The middle section could be tighter, and the session lacks a clear closing summary or consolidated action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as joint exploration rather than therapist-led intervention, with language that explicitly positions the client as the source of answers. The power balance is evenly maintained with the client's contributions shaping direction and content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and choice without ever pressuring, giving unsolicited advice, or implying obligation. The session models change as the client's decision, process, and timeline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses open, non-leading questions specifically designed to elicit client-generated change talk rather than providing the therapist's expertise. Also effectively reflects back the client's own powerful statements and gently challenges self-limiting beliefs.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently collaborative, non-expert stance, immediately ceding agenda-setting to the client and never positioning as having answers. Explicitly disclaims authority with statements like 'you don't need my permission to name what you need.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highly skilled at evoking rich change talk, with the client generating substantial motivational statements throughout the session. Could more systematically develop this into explicit commitment or readiness language (e.g., confidence ratings or commitment scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframes sustain talk without arguing or dismissing, effectively identifying the underlying pattern behind self-doubt. Some moments of sustain talk could have been met with more explicit double-sided reflections holding both difficulty and capacity simultaneously.
