# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance. Concerns about side effects, long-term use, and feeling burdensome are validated without dismissal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate, nuanced, and emotionally attuned. The clinician captures both practical worries and deeper fears of being ignored or brushed off.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on asthma medication adherence and inhaler use. The conversation moves clearly from barriers to long-term concerns, planning, objective measurement, and a next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the plan rather than prescribing it. The client's ideas drive the choices, including the written question list, spirometry comparison, personal baseline, and technique check.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supports the client's choice, ownership, and self-direction. The client is encouraged to identify what feels useful, safe, and supportive rather than being given direct advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist often elicits the client's own concerns, values, and ideas rather than supplying external reasons. Evocation could be deeper by more explicitly exploring confidence, commitment, and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions the client as the expert on her own experience and body. The client's priorities, preferences, and ideas clearly guide the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is effectively evoked, including desire, need, reasons, and planning. The score is limited because change talk is not consistently amplified and confidence, past successes, or commitment language are not fully explored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully without arguing or dismissing the client's hesitations. A more expert approach would include more explicit double-sided reflections that balance long-term concerns with change goals.
