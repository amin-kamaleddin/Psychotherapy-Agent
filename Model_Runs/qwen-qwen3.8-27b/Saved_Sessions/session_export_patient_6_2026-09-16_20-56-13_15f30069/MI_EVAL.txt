# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, validation, and nonjudgmental respect for the client’s ambivalence and values. Minor limitation is that the warmth occasionally feels slightly formulaic rather than highly individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and nuanced, capturing the client’s social concerns, ambivalence, and emotional stakes. A few reflections are somewhat conceptual or leading, but overall perspective-taking is strong.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, purposeful focus on avoiding alcohol while protecting soccer, scholarships, and family obligations. There is little topic drift, though some double-barreled questions slightly reduce clarity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction feels collaborative and client-driven in content, with the client’s ideas shaping the work. However, the clinician still steers pacing and agenda through a fairly structured, sometimes leading sequence.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supports the client’s choice, self-direction, and ownership of the decision. A few suggestive prompts remain, but the overall message strongly preserves autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is effectively led to generate his own values and motivation, including family, soccer, and personal integrity. Some leading or compound questions narrow the client’s spontaneous voice, limiting full evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is treated as the expert on his own life, values, and social world. Power sharing is strong, though the clinician still guides the session through a fairly structured sequence.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively evokes and reinforces the client’s reasons for change, such as college soccer and family sacrifices. The session could be stronger by more explicitly eliciting confidence/commitment and consolidating change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing or reinforcing it, and social concerns are validated. The performance could improve by using more deliberate double-sided reflections to shift attention more clearly toward change.
