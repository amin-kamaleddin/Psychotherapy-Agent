# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout despite minimal, dismissive, and mildly hostile client responses. The psychiatrist normalizes, validates, and reframes dismissiveness as agency without ever becoming defensive or judgmental.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong attunement to non-verbal and minimal verbal cues with nuanced reflections appropriate to the context. Limited by the client's near-total non-engagement, the responses remain somewhat formulaic in structure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Focus is appropriate for the stage but the session lacks strategic progression or a clear arc. Interactions feel reactive rather than building toward even a small engagement milestone.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance is clearly present with active seeking of client input and willingness to adjust. However, the actual collaboration is limited to respectful distance rather than genuine co-construction of meaning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook demonstration of autonomy support with a resistant adolescent, consistently removing authority threats, pressure, and obligation. The psychiatrist immediately complies with client requests without pushback or guilt-tripping.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creative and context-appropriate evocative attempts, but infrequent and somewhat passive overall. Key opportunities to draw out client-driven language, particularly at the session's end, are missed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing demonstrated through explicit statements and consistent follow-through in letting the client lead. The final exchange perfectly illustrates co-presence as equals rather than a helping dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriate attempts given the precontemplation stage, including a skillful reframe of sustain talk as agency. However, attempts are limited in frequency and the psychiatrist doesn't consistently seek or amplify micro-moments of engagement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All sustain talk is handled well without amplification or defensiveness, using validation and reframing effectively. Could be elevated with more explicit use of double-sided reflections or rolling-with-resistance techniques.
