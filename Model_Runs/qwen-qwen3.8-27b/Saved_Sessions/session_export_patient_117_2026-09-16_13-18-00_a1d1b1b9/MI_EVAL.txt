# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance throughout. Some supportive phrases feel formulaic, and there is a missed opportunity to directly validate Samuel’s self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate, especially for practical concerns and progress. There is less focus on emotional undertones, such as fear of sliding back or discomfort in the car.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on smoking reduction and concrete behavioral targets. The direction is clear, though it leans more toward plan construction than deeper motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Samuel to build a practical plan shaped by his preferences. The counselor takes a central role in structuring the session, which slightly limits full collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Samuel’s choices are respected, including his preference against patches and not telling coworkers. Autonomy is supported, though ownership could be reinforced more explicitly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Samuel’s own plan, confidence, and preferred steps. The evocation is practical but does not deeply explore values, identity, or internal motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Samuel as the expert and invites input on key decisions. Some counselor-led suggestions reduce the sense of a fully equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Samuel generates useful change talk, including a stepwise reduction plan. The psychiatrist does not consistently evoke or amplify deeper DARN-CAT change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without argument, normalization, or coercion. More strategic double-sided reflections could further soften resistance.
