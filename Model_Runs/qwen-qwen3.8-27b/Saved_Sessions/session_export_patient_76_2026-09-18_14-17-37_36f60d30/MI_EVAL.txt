# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard with zero judgment throughout. The therapist validates Liora's pain without endorsing the behavior and affirms the adaptive function of the behavior while gently exploring alternatives.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate, nuanced, and progressively deepen throughout the session. The therapist consistently hears beneath Liora's flat, minimal responses and reflects subtle internal distinctions back in language the client affirms.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from understanding function to exploring limitations to identifying what 'real' relief would look like. Could benefit from explicit agenda-setting and a brief summary to consolidate progress and orient toward next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a masterclass in collaborative working with consistent language of shared exploration and an egalitarian power dynamic. The co-construction of the 'more' concept is a particularly strong example of genuine partnership rather than therapist-driven conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every invitation is framed as optional and curiosity-driven, and the therapist never says 'you should try' or 'have you considered.' The focus remains on what Liora wants and needs rather than framing self-harm as something she 'needs to stop.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits Liora's own criteria for effective coping in her own language, producing genuine shifts in self-understanding. Could be deepened by more fully exploring the client's values, identity, or future vision to broaden the evocative work.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The therapist consistently positions Liora as the expert on her own experience and never assumes or fills in her story. The session is genuinely co-constructed, with Liora's responses shaping the direction of every question that follows.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes genuine change talk from a client in precontemplation, with Liora articulating her own criteria in her own language. Limited by session length and the client's stage, as it does not reach desire, ability, or commitment to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every piece of sustain talk is met with understanding before being gently explored for its edges, without creating dissonance between Liora's words and the therapist's position. The therapist validates the function of the behavior while simultaneously exploring its limitations.
