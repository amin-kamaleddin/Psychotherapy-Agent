# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows unconditional positive regard without judgment or moralizing. The client is respected and affirmed even while maintaining strong boundaries around smoking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and accurately captures the client’s need for autonomy and low-burden change. It is slightly less emotionally deep than it could be regarding weariness, cynicism, and past being lectured to.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains focused on smoking and steadily moves toward a concrete, client-defined next step. Questions guide the conversation clearly without losing focus or imposing a plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the interaction as a true partnership between equals. The client’s preferences directly shape the conversation, the small step, and how future check-ins should work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice is strongly and consistently supported throughout the session. The psychiatrist avoids pressure, guilt, or hidden advice while inviting the client to define the terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits the client’s own values, preferences, and motivation for change. The client generates a realistic, self-owned plan rather than adopting an externally imposed one.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently treated as the expert on his own life and goals. Collaboration is substantive, with the client’s input driving the nature and direction of the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces the client’s own change-related language and realistic step. It could be strengthened by more explicit work on importance, ability, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance and sustain talk well by validating autonomy and avoiding argument or correction. It would be stronger if the client’s language were more consistently redirected toward change-oriented meaning.
