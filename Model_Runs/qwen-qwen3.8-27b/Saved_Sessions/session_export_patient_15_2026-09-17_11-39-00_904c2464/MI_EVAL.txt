# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth and nonjudgmental acceptance throughout, with explicit normalization of the patient's pace. Occasionally reads as slightly formulaic, preventing a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections accurately capture the emotional and philosophical core of the patient's ambivalence. Could have gone deeper into the affective dimension of the patient's fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from presenting concern to specific target behaviors with minimal topic drift. The alcohol-hypertension connection is left somewhat underexplored after the initial mention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses 'we' language and consistently invites the patient's perspective. Limited evidence of the patient's ideas reshaping the session's direction in real-time.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout criterion with consistent, explicit, and embedded autonomy support. Never uses directive language; the patient's timeline is fully validated and reframed positively.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent and well-timed evocation questions draw motivation from the patient rather than providing it externally. Stays at the practical level rather than exploring deeper values or identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong language of power-sharing and explicit non-directive framing. The session structure remains guided by the psychiatrist's pacing rather than being fully co-created with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Genuine evocation produces some meaningful change-oriented language from the patient. The psychiatrist does not deeply amplify or expand on change talk when it appears, leaving the commitment underdeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges the validity of sustain talk before gently pivoting without arguing or escalating. Minor gap in not explicitly addressing the 'it's just age' statement.
