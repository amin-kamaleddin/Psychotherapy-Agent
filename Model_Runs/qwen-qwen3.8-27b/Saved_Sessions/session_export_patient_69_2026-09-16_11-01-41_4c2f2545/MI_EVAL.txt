# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Kieran’s frustration while respecting his desire to end. Acceptance is slightly procedural and the opening compliance-focused line prevents a top score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Kieran’s surface-level frustration with the mandated process. However, the psychiatrist does not deeply explore underlying ambivalence, values, or the meaning of the process.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on compliance and disengagement rather than unrelated topics. Direction is limited because the psychiatrist does not guide Kieran toward a concrete, client-driven next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some collaborative language and asks for Kieran’s preference on next steps. Collaboration remains limited because the psychiatrist controls the session sequence and repeats the same options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is strong, with repeated emphasis on Kieran’s choice and respect for his boundary to leave it alone. The psychiatrist avoids pressure, persuasion, or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks open questions and attempts to evoke small changes. Evocation is superficial because Kieran’s brief statements, such as “less paperwork,” are not meaningfully expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Kieran’s input and uses language that conveys collaboration. Partnership is limited because Kieran’s contributions do not substantially reshape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are limited attempts to evoke change talk through questions about small changes or what Kieran actually wants. The psychiatrist does not sufficiently build on Kieran’s own language or develop meaningful change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Kieran’s negative statements and uses nonjudgmental reflections. However, the psychiatrist does not actively soften or redirect sustain talk using double-sided reflections or gentle pivots toward possible change.
