# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard from the opening statement through the entire session. Gently pushes back on patient's self-criticism ('dramatic') without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture both surface content and deeper identity-level meaning (e.g., 'mourning a version of yourself'). Uses patient's own language and sophisticated metaphors like 'passenger seat of your own nervous system.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook progression from broad exploration through pattern identification to a concrete, small step. Every question and reflection serves the overall trajectory without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session feels like genuine joint exploration rather than an interview. Builds on patient's language and offers reframings as possibilities ('maybe the question isn't...') rather than corrections.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Establishes patient control immediately and maintains it throughout with permissive language ('I'm curious,' 'if there is one'). Explicitly credits patient with her own insight: 'That's not something I could have suggested.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong open-ended questions that draw out patient's own insights, particularly the 'what it needs to hear' question. Could more systematically elicit confidence/ability statements and a commitment rating to solidify gains.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent collaborative stance with 'I'm curious' and 'I'd love to understand' language rather than directive positioning. Does not rush to solutions, staying in exploration while maintaining focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits desire statements ('I want the jobs, I want the freedom') and awareness of agency. Could more explicitly amplify and deepen change talk, and systematically explore DARN-C categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates the protective function of sustain talk without arguing or dismissing. Could use more strategic double-sided reflections to more decisively shift the balance toward change.
