# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental, and respectful tone with genuine positive regard throughout the session. Some affirmations verge on formulaic and could feel slightly scripted rather than individually attuned to a 16-year-old.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, including capturing the 'what if vs. what is' distinction and the automatic quality of the urge. Reflections tend to stay at the cognitive level rather than delving into deeper emotional undertones like loss of control or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from rapport to concrete plan with the target behavior never lost and each question building momentum. Could benefit from explicit summarization and a collaboratively set follow-up checkpoint to give the direction a clear endpoint.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative problem-solving where Anika generates ideas and the psychiatrist facilitates and probes, with effective moments like following her lead on the 'what if Sarah's not free' thread. Could be deeper with more explicit acknowledgment of client expertise and moments of genuine power-sharing where her ideas redirect the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with explicit statements like 'you're the one making the call, not me' and consistent reinforcement of client ownership. Never pressures, directs, or implies the client 'should' do anything; the session model is clear that Anika is the decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently uses open questions to draw motivation from within Anika rather than providing reasons for change, with all motivational content coming from her. Could push deeper into identity-level meaning behind her change talk and exploit the 7/10 confidence rating for more specific evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance throughout, positioning Anika as the expert on her own life and avoiding an expert-driven approach. The session structure follows a logical predetermined roadmap, making the partnership somewhat asymmetric in direction and pacing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits multiple DARN-C statements and reinforces them with emphasis, drawing out Anika's own reasons for change. Could do more to amplify and strategically build on the change talk, such as exploring what 'playing well' means to her identity or future.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles minimization acceptably by acknowledging it without arguing and gently redirecting toward Anika's own values. Lacks active softening techniques such as double-sided reflections or explicit exploration of the tension between sides of her ambivalence.
