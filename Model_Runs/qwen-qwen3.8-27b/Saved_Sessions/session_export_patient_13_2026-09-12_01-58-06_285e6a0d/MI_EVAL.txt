# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses a warm, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes the patient's concerns. Affirmations are supportive but sometimes formulaic, limiting it to 4.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate content reflections are used, especially around worry, sleep, and barriers. Empathy is good but not always deep enough to capture underlying emotions, so 4.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on medical follow-up and moves from barriers to a concrete low-pressure step. Repetitive question sequences and limited consolidation reduce strategic pacing, so 4.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-created around patient-chosen steps, but the counselor sometimes shifts into solution-providing with resources and scripts. This makes collaboration strong but not fully client-led, so 4.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is consistent, explicit, and respectful of the patient's choice, pacing, and information limits. It is the strongest criterion, deserving 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions and importance ratings elicit some patient-generated language. Follow-up to deepen motivation, confidence, or values is limited, so 3.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites patient input and the final plan reflects her preferences. However, the agenda and language are sometimes more counselor-led than fully power-sharing, so 4.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps identify a small step and affirms patient-generated confidence. Change talk is not consistently amplified or expanded, so 3.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Barriers are acknowledged without argument, but fear and cost are not consistently redirected toward change. The response to the 3 rating risks reinforcing low urgency, so 3.
