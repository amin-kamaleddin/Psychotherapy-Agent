# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental, and affirming throughout, with cultural awareness shown through 'Kia ora' and respect for whānau dynamics. Could be slightly deeper in moments where normalizing fear as a sign of care would strengthen the positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate complex reflections (e.g., 'carrying two heavy things at once') and effective use of the client's own language. Occasionally leans toward summarizing rather than reaching the emotional core of shame or sense of debt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear session arc moving from motivation through exploration to concrete action planning with good use of summary questions to maintain momentum. Could more explicitly tie abstract explorations (e.g., 'not flinching') back to specific behavioral goals like alcohol reduction or Stopping Violence attendance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a model of co-construction, with Wiremu's responses determining the direction at every turn and the psychiatrist building on his own metaphors and language. No imposed structure or agenda; every major step is jointly created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports autonomy with language like 'You don't have to decide today' and 'You don't need permission to know that's enough.' Never uses directives or imposes advice, framing all choices as Wiremu's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation from within using open, evocative questions and amplifying the client's own words as vehicles for self-exploration. Misses opportunities for deeper evocation through scaling questions and explicit confidence-building techniques.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook partnership stance maintained throughout, with the psychiatrist consistently positioning Wiremu as the expert on his own life and never assuming authority. The session is genuinely co-created, with explicit affirmations of the client's decision-making authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk through strategic questions, affirming specific change language, and having the client repeat 'Permission to be mid-way' to consolidate it. Could have more explicitly elicited ability and confidence language and used more scaling questions to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully honors sustain talk without arguing or dismissing, using classic MI moves like redirecting toward exploration and helping the client see contrasts themselves. Could have used more explicit double-sided reflections to hold both fear and motivation simultaneously when addressing whānau disappointment.
