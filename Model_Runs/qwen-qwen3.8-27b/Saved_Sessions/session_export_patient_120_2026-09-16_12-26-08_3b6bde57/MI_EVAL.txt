# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows warmth, respect, and normalization, but acceptance is somewhat task-focused. It would benefit from deeper unconditional regard and more explicit affirmation of Sofia’s worth independent of abstinence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of Sofia’s ambivalence and social concerns. Deeper empathy is limited by surface-level, repetitive reflections and missed opportunities to explore guilt, shame, or identity loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on the target behavior of abstaining from alcohol during pregnancy. The psychiatrist consistently guides Sofia toward concrete social coping and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is mostly collaborative, with Sofia’s preferences integrated into the plan. However, the psychiatrist often supplies structured options, which can feel more expert-directed than client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices, asks permission, and avoids direct commands. Autonomy support would be stronger with more explicit affirmation that Sofia is in charge and with fewer therapist-generated scripts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation occurs through ruler questions and initial open-ended prompts. The session relies too heavily on advice and planning rather than eliciting Sofia’s deeper values, motivations, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally maintains a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and invites Sofia’s input. The approach becomes less partnership-oriented when repeatedly providing scripted tactics and options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is present and occasionally reinforced, especially around Sofia’s personal promise to stay abstain. The psychiatrist does not consistently follow up on importance/confidence rulers or expand Sofia’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia’s sustain talk is accepted without argument and redirected toward practical strategies. The psychiatrist does not use more advanced MI skills, such as double-sided reflections, to balance or resolve the sustain talk.
