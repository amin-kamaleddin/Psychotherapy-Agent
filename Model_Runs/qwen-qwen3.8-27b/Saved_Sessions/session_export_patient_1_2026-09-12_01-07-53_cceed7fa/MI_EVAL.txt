# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and avoids shaming. The warmth remains somewhat procedural, especially when providing repeated medical advice.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician makes several useful reflections but remains more cognitive than emotional. Underlying defensiveness, need for control, and discomfort with change are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the presenting concern and moves toward a concrete, manageable step. Direction is clear, though somewhat repetitive and advice-heavy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some joint planning, particularly around the small alcohol-reduction step. However, the clinician supplies most of the content and guidance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician respects Ahmed's pace and repeatedly supports his choice rather than pushing him to quit. The detailed instructions still create a mildly directive atmosphere.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The clinician tends to provide reasons for change instead of drawing out Ahmed's own motivations, values, or confidence. Fewer evocative questions are used to activate change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician invites input and incorporates Ahmed's preference for a small step. The session is still largely driven by the clinician's medical explanations and recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is limited and mostly compliance-based around a practical action. The clinician does not consistently elicit internal motivations or desired outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ahmed's resistance is often met with factual correction rather than reflective redirection. Sustain talk is acknowledged but not consistently softened or reprocessed.
