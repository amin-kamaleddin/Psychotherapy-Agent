# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful and accepts the client's time constraints, though the stance is somewhat brisk rather than deeply warm. It lacks a bit of genuine affirmation for the client's frustrations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist accurately reflects the client's ambivalence and time pressure but does not consistently probe deeper into the underlying emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on practical, actionable steps, though occasional shifts to logistical protocol slightly reduce the MI quality.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist collaborates effectively to build a plan around the client's constraints, but could have invited more client-driven articulation of success criteria.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist well supports the client's sense of choice and respects his decisions, despite some slightly directive "plan" language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriate early evocation is present, but the therapist quickly shifts to rationale and logistics rather than consistently exploring the client's confidence and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist invites input and adapts to constraints but still takes a fairly expert-driven, plan-setting role that limits true equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Several evocative questions successfully elicit openness to change, but the therapist moves quickly to planning without consistently reinforcing the client's change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The sustain talk is respectfully acknowledged without conflict, but the therapist misses opportunities to use more skilled MI techniques to resolve ambivalence.
