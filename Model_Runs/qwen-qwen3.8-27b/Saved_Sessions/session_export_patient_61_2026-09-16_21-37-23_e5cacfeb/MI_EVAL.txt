# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, validating stance without shaming or moralizing. Acceptance could be deeper with more explicit unconditional positive regard and normalization of ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture exhaustion, burden, and concern about burdening his wife. Empathy is solid but remains mostly surface-level and could explore deeper emotions or fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on diabetes self-management and narrows into concrete, behaviorally relevant steps. Related topics are consistently connected back to small, realistic change steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Jonathan as a co-expert and lets his ideas drive the conversation. The client substantially generates the plan, with the therapist reflecting and guiding rather than advising.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids direct advice and pressing for commitment while inviting Jonathan to identify doable steps. Autonomy support would be stronger with explicit affirmations of Jonathan’s right to choose or not choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Jonathan’s own ideas, preferences, and existing resources. Evocation would be stronger if it targeted deeper values, goals, or concerns about diabetes and long-term health.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist consistently affirms Jonathan’s expertise about his life, schedule, and limits. The partnership would be enhanced by more explicit agenda negotiation or power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully notices and builds on Jonathan’s emerging change talk, such as short, simple, and doable steps. Cultivation would be stronger with more deliberate use of DARN-C language, scaling, or values-based deepening.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids confrontation and validates Jonathan’s resistance rather than arguing with it. Some sustain talk could be more strategically reframed using double-sided reflections toward change.
