# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, validating tone with clear positive regard and normalization. A minor lapse in individualized attention, such as the irrelevant menstrual-cycle comment, prevented a top score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reflected Marco’s frustration and discouragement effectively, but often stayed at a surface level. Deeper feelings such as pride, identity threat, and mixed reactions to the juice discovery were not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed largely focused on the juice-related barrier and moved toward a concrete, actionable plan. Some early drift into broad, generic health advice slightly reduced the focus before the main issue emerged.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited Marco’s input and incorporated his ideas into the final plan. However, the interaction often felt expert-driven, with the counselor supplying scripts, brands, and specific solutions rather than co-constructing them equally.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Autonomy was supported through choices and framing decisions as Marco’s own. This was inconsistent because frequent directive advice, product suggestions, and prescriptive steps made the plan feel partly clinician-authored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client-generated change talk emerged, and the psychiatrist used a few evocative questions. The counselor often supplied answers instead of drawing out Marco’s own values, reasons, and motivations more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco’s contributions were acknowledged and influenced the direction of the session. However, the psychiatrist often acted as the authority providing solutions, which limited a fully collaborative, power-sharing stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco generated strong change talk, including a concrete plan and high confidence. The psychiatrist affirmed this but did not consistently deepen or strategically build on his reasons, abilities, and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledged Marco’s attachment to juice without judgment. However, sustain talk was largely bypassed by moving quickly into practical problem-solving rather than exploring and reframing his ambivalence.
