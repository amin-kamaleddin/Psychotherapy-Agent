# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates consistent, genuine unconditional positive regard with zero judgment or labeling throughout. Ethan's minimization and deflection are met with warmth and validation rather than correction or pushback.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are nuanced and emotionally attuned, capturing complex tensions such as the loss/gain dynamic and the emotional weight of social exclusion. The psychiatrist goes beyond simple paraphrasing to name Ethan's inner experience with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, purposeful arc from establishing context through values exploration to a concrete self-identified experiment with no topic drift. A brief recap or explicit tie-back to a follow-up plan could have strengthened the closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is clearly between two equals, with the session's direction shaped entirely by Ethan's responses and contributions. The psychiatrist acts as a curious partner who reflects and questions rather than steers or imposes structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is respected at every turn, with explicit statements like 'I'm not here to tell you what to do' and no subtle pressure or implied obligation. Ethan defines the problem, goal, and solution himself, and his ambiguity is honored rather than pushed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All questions are designed to elicit motivation from within Ethan; no external reasons for change are ever introduced. Every instance of change talk and solution-building originates from the patient, with the psychiatrist serving as a mirror and catalyst.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Ethan as the expert on his own life, using open questions and reflections that deepen Ethan's thinking rather than imposing an external framework. The session is driven by Ethan's responses, with the psychiatrist following his lead throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits multiple forms of change talk through targeted questions and amplifies them with affirmations like 'You just named something really clear.' A confidence or commitment rating, or further exploration of values beyond basketball, could have maximized the effect.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with empathy and validation rather than argument, and is never reinforced. However, the most persistent sustain talk around social belonging could have been more actively dissolved using double-sided reflections or a summary that explicitly names the tension.
