# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates warmth, validation, and nonjudgmental support. Statements such as 'That makes a lot of sense' and normalizing Marcus's uncertainty reinforce acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate and meaningful reflections that capture Marcus's concerns about blood-sugar checks and uncertainty. Some reflections could be deeper, exploring the personal and identity-level impact of the new diagnosis.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior of daily morning glucose monitoring. Each exchange advances a practical, concrete self-management plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is highly collaborative, with Marcus actively contributing to the plan. Decisions about the coffee cue, photo log, coping steps, and backup plan emerge from shared discussion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offers choices and avoids coercive language. Marcus is supported in selecting his own thresholds, recording method, and coping strategy, strengthening ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Marcus's own motivations, concerns, and problem-solving ideas. Questions about feasibility, barriers, and preferred coping methods elicit his internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Marcus as the expert on his life and work situation. Information is shared without dominating the conversation, preserving a collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marcus repeatedly expresses increased confidence, readiness, and ability to follow the plan. The psychiatrist reinforces and amplifies this momentum effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Concerns about forgetting, high numbers, and feeling stuck are responded to supportively and redirected toward practical solutions. Slight improvement is possible by using more double-sided reflections to balance ambivalence.
