# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Jonathan’s overwhelm and guilt. Acceptance is strong but somewhat formulaic and not consistently individualized to Jonathan as a whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Jonathan’s fear of testing, shame, and burden. Empathy is generally good, but it occasionally remains surface-level and moves too quickly into problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on diabetes self-management and the specific issue of glucose testing around food. Direction is strong, though it sometimes becomes over-structured and not fully client-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adjusts the plan based on Jonathan’s safety concerns. Collaboration is present but uneven because the counselor remains the primary generator of options and solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly emphasizes Jonathan’s freedom to choose and frames the final step as optional. Autonomy support is a strength, though repeated option-giving can create subtle pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions elicit meaningful client language about guilt, safety, and exhaustion. Evocation is present but is often interrupted by counselor-generated ideas and premature problem-solving.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites Jonathan’s input. The interaction still feels expert-led because of the frequent use of structured options and behavioral plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some questions help Jonathan express change-oriented concerns about safety and feeling less like he is failing. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to affirm, amplify, and build on this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Jonathan’s sustain talk, but it is not effectively softened or redirected toward change. The session remains focused on the burdens of management without using more balanced reflections.
