# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates the client’s concerns without pressure. Growth opportunity: include more explicit positive regard and normalization of setbacks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and consistent, tracking the client’s perspective and practical concerns well. They could deepen by exploring emotional undercurrents such as fear of failure or feeling behind.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior of increasing physical activity. The counselor consistently guides the client toward a concrete, workable plan without drift or vague advice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and client co-construct the plan using the client’s own language and ideas. The counselor remains the primary question-asker and sequence-setter, which could limit the client’s role in leading the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports choice through open questions and respects the client’s preference for a low-effort plan. It would be stronger with more explicit affirmations that the client owns the decision and may change or stop at any time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits client-generated solutions, plans, and motivation. Evocation could be deepened by exploring values, identity, confidence, and what success would mean to the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites the client’s input frequently and allows the client’s ideas to shape the session. More explicit power-sharing language and less counselor-driven sequencing would strengthen the collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor creates opportunities for the client to generate change-oriented language and builds on her statements about consistency and low pressure. More explicit DARN-C probes and amplification of the strongest change talk would further strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk and barriers are handled well, including concern about tracking becoming a source of pressure. The counselor could more consistently use double-sided reflections and strategic pivots to strengthen change talk.
