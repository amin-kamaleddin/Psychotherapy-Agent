# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming positive regard throughout the entire session, never labeling, judging, or expressing disapproval. The warmth feels genuine and individualized, referencing Rafael's specific language and experience rather than being formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses complex, progressively deepening reflections that capture both cognitive and emotional dimensions of Rafael's experience. Double-sided reflections accurately name ambivalence without reducing Rafael's experience to a simple label.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on returning to the gym with a logical progression from establishing the problem through to a concrete client-generated plan. Pacing is well-calibrated, moving with the client's pace without losing momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Both partners contribute meaningfully, with Rafael's contributions directly shaping the session's direction and the final commitment being entirely in his own words. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with the psychiatrist positioning themselves as a thinking partner rather than authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces Rafael's ownership and freedom to choose, using options rather than directives. The validation of his 'not promise anything' stance as honesty rather than avoidance is exceptional autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every question is designed to draw out Rafael's own values, experiences, and solutions, with all insights emerging from the client rather than the therapist. The psychiatrist never tells Rafael why he should exercise or what he should do, creating conditions for his own insights to emerge.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise about Rafael's life and solutions resides within Rafael himself, using questions that direct his attention inward. The session is shaped substantially by Rafael's contributions rather than a predetermined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes change talk through future-oriented questions, past success reflections, and problem-solving invitations, all of which successfully elicit change talk from Rafael. The progression from 'I can't seem to get back in the gym' to 'Saturday. One door.' represents a full arc of evoked change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk masterfully by exploring barriers gently without arguing or reinforcing them as reasons to stay stuck. Rafael's own past behavior is used to soften the all-or-nothing belief without the psychiatrist engaging in a righting reflex.
