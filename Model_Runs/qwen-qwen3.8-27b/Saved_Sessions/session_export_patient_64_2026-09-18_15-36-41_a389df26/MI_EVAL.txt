# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates a warm, nonjudgmental stance, normalizing the referral and validating Jordan's reasoning. Slightly standard in delivery, lacking moments of deeper personal regard that would reach the highest level.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex and double-sided reflections capture both situational surprise and ambivalence effectively. Reflections occasionally stay at the cognitive level rather than fully exploring emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the vaccination decision with logical progression from personal risk to family-protective factors. The transition between topics could be more deliberate, and the session ends somewhat abruptly without a clear summarization or next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions are phrased as invitations and the psychiatrist builds on Jordan's contributions rather than imposing an agenda. Could be elevated with more explicit co-creation of session direction and acknowledgment of Jordan's expertise in his own life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout — every question is framed in terms of Jordan's own values and choice, with no pressure language like 'you should' or 'you need to.' The threshold question powerfully positions Jordan as the decision-maker with his own criteria.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully allows Jordan to self-generate key insights (e.g., asymptomatic transmission) rather than providing them directly. Could have dug deeper after evoked change talk emerged to further develop it before moving on.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative, non-hierarchical stance using language like 'I'm curious' and 'I'm wondering' to frame the interaction as shared exploration. Could be strengthened by more explicitly negotiating the agenda and acknowledging Jordan's expertise in his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively create conditions for change talk to emerge, with Jordan self-generating the protective motivation. The psychiatrist could have more explicitly affirmed and amplified the change talk moments as they occurred before moving on.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled effectively through validation and redirection rather than counter-argument, with no righting reflex. Could benefit from more active use of double-sided reflections to help Jordan see both sides of his ambivalence simultaneously.
