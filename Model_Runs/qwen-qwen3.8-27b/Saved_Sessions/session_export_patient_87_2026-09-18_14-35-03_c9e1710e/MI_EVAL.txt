### Collaboration

### Evocation

**Comments:** The entire session is structured as a collaborative exploration where Marco's contributions drive the direction, and the psychiatrist consistently positions Marco as the expert on his own experience. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with the psychiatrist serving as a skilled facilitator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Marco's change talk evolves dramatically across the session, from tentative statements to concrete strategies and self-generated interventions. The psychiatrist consistently uses evocative questions that draw out Marco's own reasoning and build on his insights.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk effectively by working with concerns rather than dismissing them, helping Marco explore what would need to be true for the concern to feel manageable. The approach validates the doubt while gently opening space for new perspectives.
