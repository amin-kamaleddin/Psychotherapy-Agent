# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance. Some evaluative praise and interpretive steering reduce the full neutrality of acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening effectively captures Erik’s ambivalence and internal conflict. Deeper empathy could more explicitly address the shame, discouragement, and fear of inadequacy underneath the gym comparison.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on Erik’s target behaviors: gym consistency, dietary choices, back health, and reducing the impact of social comparison. The conversation moves coherently toward a concrete, testable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds directly on Erik’s ideas rather than imposing a counselor-generated plan. The interaction feels like a genuine partnership in organizing and testing Erik’s own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Erik’s sense of choice and avoids direct advice, pressure, or imposed rules. Erik remains the decision-maker throughout the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicit Erik’s own motivations, values, and proposed solutions. The psychiatrist does not lecture; instead, motivation emerges from Erik’s own statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly invites Erik to define his own goals, criteria, and solutions. However, several interpretive frames subtly guide the client’s thinking rather than fully deferring to his expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions successfully evoke meaningful change talk about desire, ability, and commitment. The psychiatrist could further strengthen this by more explicitly reflecting, amplifying, or scaling Erik’s own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and gently reframed in terms of health, honesty, and protection. At times the psychiatrist moves quickly into a positive change frame, which can limit room for Erik’s ambivalence to remain present.
