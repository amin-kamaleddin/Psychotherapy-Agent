# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone with good affirmations and normalizing reframes. However, the acceptance remains somewhat professional rather than deeply personal, lacking the explicit affirmation of worth beyond the smoking issue.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate and consistent understanding of Linda's perspective, with reflections that go well beyond paraphrasing to capture emotional nuance, identity concerns, and relational dynamics. Exemplary empathic responding throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent, consistent focus on the target behavior with clear purpose and progressive narrowing from broad ambivalence to a concrete client-generated plan. No drift, appropriate pacing, and strong momentum throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative behavior with inclusive, non-directive language and genuine co-construction of ideas. However, the session structure remains somewhat counselor-led; a 5 would require the client's ideas to more visibly shape the overall direction and agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and skillfully supports autonomy through questions, never prescribes, and frames all suggestions as possibilities or exploratory. Explicitly affirms Linda's right to decide and her own standards, which is exemplary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook-level evocation; consistently draws out Linda's own motivations, ideas, and solutions without providing information or advice. The cold wash, the note, and the Dave idea all emerged from the client, not the counselor.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and frequently invites Linda's input, treating her as the expert on her own experience. Could be slightly stronger in explicitly acknowledging her expertise or co-creating a shared understanding of goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through open questions, amplification, and powerful reflections of Linda's own words. Could be slightly more systematic in eliciting commitment language and exploring confidence/ability talk more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by validating and reframing without reinforcing or pushing back aggressively. One moment (the money worry) could have more explicitly acknowledged the validity of the concern before redirecting.
