# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor consistently shows respect, non-judgment, and accepts Sofia's minimization without pushing. Acceptance is competent but somewhat procedural, missing deeper affirmation of her worth or emotional burden.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening captures tiredness, managing load, and phone use with some feeling meaning. Reflections remain fairly surface-level and miss deeper loneliness, exhaustion, or resignation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Session drifts among school, friends, sleep, phone use, and homework without focusing on the alcohol-use referral concern. There is no clear strategic thread toward a specific change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses shared language and invites Sofia's perspective in several ways. Collaboration is present but remains counselor-guided rather than fully mutually constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor supports Sofia's pace, choice, and self-reliance without pressuring advice. Autonomy support is mostly implied rather than explicitly reaffirmed as her full responsibility.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections draw out some thoughts about tiredness, phone use, and homework coping. Evocation is superficial and not directed toward values, ambivalence, or motivation related to alcohol use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor positions Sofia as the expert on her life and offers options rather than directives. Partnership is evident but more guided than fully co-created, with limited agenda negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is limited and not connected to the primary referral concern of alcohol use/risk. Small ideas about sleep or phone use are not expanded into meaningful change motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor avoids arguing with Sofia's sustain statements but only occasionally redirects toward possibility or change. Softening strategies such as double-sided reflections or values-based reframing are not used consistently.
