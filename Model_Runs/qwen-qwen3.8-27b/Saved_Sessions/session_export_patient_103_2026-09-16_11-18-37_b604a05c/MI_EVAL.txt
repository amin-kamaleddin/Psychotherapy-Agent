# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Nabil's anger, frustration, and time pressures. However, the focus quickly shifted to problem-solving, which limited deeper emotional attunement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered accurate reflections of Nabil's frustration, time loss, and ambivalence. Some reflections were surface-level or repetitive rather than exploring deeper meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session remained focused on appointment delays and practical scheduling issues. However, it moved quickly into logistics before clarifying values, readiness, or a target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborated effectively during email drafting, adjusting content based on Nabil's feedback. Overall, the interaction remained more advisor-driven than truly co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nabil was given choices and final approval over the email, supporting some autonomy. However, the psychiatrist repeatedly offered pre-made options rather than encouraging Nabil to define his own path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist began by asking what success would look like, which elicited useful client language. Motivation, values, and confidence were not consistently drawn out before offering solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited input and used collaborative language. However, the psychiatrist largely set the agenda and generated options, limiting a more balanced partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some change talk was elicited, particularly around being informed and maintaining control. The psychiatrist did not consistently build on or expand this change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Nabil's externalization of the problem and validated his frustration. MI-consistent strategies such as double-sided reflections or redirecting to agency were used inconsistently.
