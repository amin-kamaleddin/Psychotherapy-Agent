# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, unconditional positive regard throughout; meets Maria at her suicidal statement without reframing or fixing. Deeply honors her protective behaviors rather than pathologizing them.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate complex reflections that capture paradoxes in Maria's experience (e.g., inevitability AND being here). Deep perspective-taking on the adaptive function of her avoidance without missing the emotional core.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from safety check through understanding to gentle exploration, always building on the previous turn. Appropriately stays at Maria's level ('five minutes') without premature goal-pushing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria's statements consistently drive direction and language is inclusive/exploratory. Slightly stronger than full co-creation since the psychiatrist still selects which threads to pull and in what order.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly affirms Maria's right to refuse, no pressure or persuasion at any point. Autonomy is consistently reinforced ('You know yourself better than I do') without feeling formulaic.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent, skillful evocative questions that invite Maria to access her own meanings and values. Could be more pervasive—some turns are primarily reflective where an additional evocative follow-up would deepen exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria is explicitly positioned as expert ('You know yourself better than I do') and session is shaped by her responses. Retains slightly more structural control over conversation flow than full co-creation would involve.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes change talk through strategic questions (e.g., 'what would lighter be?') leading to articulated desires. Could further amplify and expand on the change talk when it emerges rather than reflecting it back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argues with or dismisses hopelessness; works within Maria's frame ('taking you at your word'). Identifies adaptive function of sustain talk while gently exploring its costs—exceptionally skilled.
