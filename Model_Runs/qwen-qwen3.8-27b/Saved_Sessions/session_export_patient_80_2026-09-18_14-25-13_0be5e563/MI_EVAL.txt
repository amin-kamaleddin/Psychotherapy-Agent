# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warmth, respect, and non-judgment toward Luka's partial progress and honesty. However, the warmth occasionally feels slightly structured rather than spontaneously genuine.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy throughout, with reflections that capture both cognitive and emotional dimensions of Luka's experience. Notable for going beyond surface-level paraphrasing to name the core fears and ambivalences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors with a logical conversational flow from stress through drinking triggers to body signals. The session could benefit from a clearer strategic arc and a brief collaborative summary to consolidate emerging themes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Luka as an equal partner, follows his lead, and validates his insights as valuable. Could be strengthened with more explicit co-construction language and power-sharing to balance the dynamic further.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support demonstrated by explicitly removing pressure to have a plan, validating Luka's right to uncertainty, and never prescribing actions. A masterful demonstration of honoring the client's agency without rushing to problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest element of the session; every question is designed to draw out Luka's own motivations, values, and insights without external direction. Luka generates profound change talk on his own, including 'I don't want to be that guy' and 'not being alone in my own head for ten minutes.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-hierarchical stance with language that signals partnership and avoids directive approaches. Could be elevated with more explicit power-sharing and co-creation of the session agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questioning that elicits self-efficacy and bodily awareness. Could more actively elicit specific change talk types and more explicitly reinforce statements of desire with deeper value exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating doubt without arguing or dismissing, using gentle reframing rather than confrontation. Could more actively employ double-sided reflections to further soften the impact of sustain talk.
