# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintains a non-judgmental, validating stance, clearly communicating that Julian doesn't owe anyone a transformation. Acceptance is strong but occasionally feels slightly rehearsed rather than deeply individualized to Julian's specific experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and the psychiatrist skillfully tracks Julian's progression from disengagement to concrete planning. However, empathy remains surface-level, not deeply probing Julian's underlying emotions such as shame, fear, or resentment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent focus on the target behavior (completing community service hours) with a logical progression from exploration to a concrete action plan. The session never drifts and ends with a specific, client-generated plan (text case worker after work tomorrow).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the interaction as a joint effort and actively seeks Julian's input throughout. However, the power balance still skews toward the counselor, who drives the conversation structure without explicitly inviting Julian to set the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong support for Julian's autonomy with clear language framing everything as his choice and his plan. A key missed opportunity was not directly addressing Julian's statement of low agency ('it's not like there's a lot of options for me').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful use of strategic questions that draw out Julian's own motivations, preferences, and plans without providing advice. Julian articulates his own plan, timeline, and method entirely through the psychiatrist's evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A collaborative stance is maintained throughout with language emphasizing joint effort and Julian's ownership of decisions. Partnership could be elevated by more explicit co-creation of the session's direction and acknowledging Julian's expertise more directly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocation of change talk through strategic questions and specific affirmations that reinforce Julian's own voice. Could be pushed further to elicit deeper commitment or confidence language (e.g., scaling questions).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Julian's disengagement and sustain talk are validated rather than challenged, with effective reframing of his progress. The statement 'it's not like there's a lot of options for me' was not directly addressed or reframed to explore agency within constraints.
