# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nonjudgmental and respectful tone with some validation, but warmth and individualized regard remain surface-level.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequate reflections of practical frustration, though deeper anxiety, ambivalence, and guarded emotion were not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent and purposeful focus on completing 40 hours by June 30, with logical progression and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing and practical teamwork, though the exploration remained mainly task-focused rather than values-based.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent choices and preference-based prompts supported client freedom, though ideation was largely generated by the counselor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal evocative questioning or effort to elicit the client's own motivations, interests, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative input was sought, but the counselor dominated content and the client remained mostly passive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Very little change talk was elicited or amplified, with no focus on personal values or intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoidant and externally attributed sustain talk was not reinforced, but it was also not systematically reframed.
