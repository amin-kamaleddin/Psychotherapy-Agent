# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently showed respect, warmth, and nonjudgment while normalizing Wiremu’s stressors. They validated his honesty about not promising zero without disappointment or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Wiremu’s ambivalence, values, and high-risk moments. Some responses were more cognitive than deeply emotional, especially around shame and missing his children.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on reducing alcohol and building a concrete plan for high-risk moments. The connection to the violence-prevention programme and family safety could have been tightened.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation felt collaborative, with Wiremu’s ideas shaping the plan. The psychiatrist sometimes guided the session with structured options, slightly limiting the client’s full leadership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supported Wiremu’s sense of choice and ownership. They avoided imposing abstinence and consistently invited him to define the next step in his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited Wiremu’s own motivations, values, and coping strategies. A few questions included suggestive options, so the evocation was strong but not fully open-ended.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Wiremu as the expert and invited his input throughout. Some questions were somewhat multiple-choice, which slightly reduced the purely client-led nature of the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encouraged and reinforced Wiremu’s change statements about being a dependable dad and making safer choices. Change talk could have been deepened further with scaling or stronger amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without argument or reinforcement, and the focus was gently redirected toward change. Some sustaining statements were softened but not always explicitly reframed.
