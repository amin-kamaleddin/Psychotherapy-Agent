# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout, with strong normalizing statements and affirmation. However, the pattern of affirmation sometimes feels slightly formulaic rather than deeply individualized to each moment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening and perspective-taking with nuanced, specific reflections that accurately capture the emotional texture of Elira's experience. Highlights include the reflection of 'two parts of you pulling in different directions' and the powerful capture of the exhaustion behind her apparent acceptance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus with logical progression from identifying the pattern to understanding the underlying fear and examining self-labels. The session could benefit from a clearer landing point with a concrete next step or collaboratively identified target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with inclusive language and genuine dialogue rather than interrogation. The psychiatrist still carries a slightly greater structural load in guiding the conversation and could more explicitly invite Elira to redirect or set the pace.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding autonomy support with explicit removal of pressure ('You don't owe me hope today') and consistent positioning of Elira as the decision-maker. The psychiatrist never imposes advice or prescribes solutions, consistently deferring to Elira's own expertise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major insight in the session comes from Elira herself, elicited through strategic questions and reflections rather than being told to her. The psychiatrist skillfully draws out self-compassion, motivation for change, and understanding of the nervous system without providing reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with explicit deferral to Elira's judgment and avoidance of the expert role. Could be elevated by more explicitly co-creating the session's direction, such as inviting Elira to set or adjust the focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questions and by reflecting back Elira's own desire for change. Could go further in explicitly amplifying and reinforcing Elira's change talk moments, such as using scaling questions about confidence in making small shifts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframes sustain talk without arguing, such as interpreting 'acceptance' as exhaustion and inviting examination of the 'laziness' label. A minor missed opportunity early in the session to more gently pivot away from Elira's initial avoidance framing.
