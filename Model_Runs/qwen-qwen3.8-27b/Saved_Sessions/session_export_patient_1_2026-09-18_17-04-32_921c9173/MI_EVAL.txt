# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, validating Ahmed's distinctions and concerns without arguing. Slightly formulaic in places; could affirm his comparisons more deeply to demonstrate unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening with accurate summaries of Ahmed's emotional stance, including his back-pain analogy and past overreaction. Could go deeper into underlying feelings like implicit judgment when Ahmed minimizes his smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior and skillfully avoids derailment by Ahmed's deflections. Could be tighter in quickly returning to the specific target when the conversation drifts into exploratory territory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with consistent use of questions and building on Ahmed's own contributions and analogies. Doesn't fully reach co-creating a specific plan or action together in a structured way.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional throughout—never uses coercive or directive language; every suggestion is framed as a question or option. Consistently affirms Ahmed's right to choose, positioning him as the decision-maker on his own body.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently uses evocative questions to draw out Ahmed's own motivations, thresholds, and criteria for concern. Could be more skillful by explicitly reinforcing and expanding on the moments when Ahmed offers change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance, explicitly acknowledging Ahmed's expertise on his own experience. Slightly counselor-led in session structure—doesn't explicitly co-create an agenda or check in about Ahmed's preferred direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some efforts through open questions that elicit forward-looking language like 'If it got worse, I'd see a doctor.' Does not consistently reinforce, reflect back, or strategically deepen these moments of change talk when they appear.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging distinctions and redirecting without arguing or contradicting. Could use more active double-sided reflections or strategic redirection when Ahmed explicitly pushes back.
