# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was neutral and nonjudgmental but felt transactional and script-like. The generic opening limited personalized acceptance and warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Reflective listening was absent, with responses focused on facts rather than the patient’s frustration or perspective. The patient’s feelings about repeated smoking advice were not reflected or validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking and dental health without wandering off-topic. However, direction was informational rather than strategically MI-guided.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The psychiatrist drove the agenda and provided solutions rather than co-constructing the plan. The interaction remained one-way information transfer instead of collaborative problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Closing language respected the patient’s choice, but the middle of the session was directive. Drafting the message for the patient undermined autonomy and client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supplied reasons for change rather than eliciting the patient’s own motivations. No questions were used to explore the patient’s values, desires, or barriers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained an expert role by providing risks and drafting the dentist message. This limited shared decision-making and the patient’s ownership of the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The psychiatrist did not actively elicit or affirm change talk. The patient’s future intent to call if deciding to stop was missed as an MI opportunity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Detailed smoking-related dental information reinforced the patient’s resistance and fear of being lectured. No MI-based redirection or softening of sustain talk was evident.
