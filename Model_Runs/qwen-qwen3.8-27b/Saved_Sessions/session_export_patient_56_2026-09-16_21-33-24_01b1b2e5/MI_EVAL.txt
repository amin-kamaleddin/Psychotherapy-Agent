# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect and validation without judgment. However, repeated phrases like "I hear..." feel somewhat formulaic and lack fully explicit unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track the client's cognitive and behavioral distinctions. They would be stronger if they more consistently named the deeper emotional weight beneath her statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on translating family values into concrete, day-to-day choices. The progression from problem identification to an actionable plan is logical, coherent, and well-paced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responds flexibly to the client's ideas and adapts the conversation in real time. The collaborative stance is effective but could be made more explicit through language about working together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, with all solutions generated by her. Explicit statements affirming her right to choose would further strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicit the client's internal motivation and self-generated strategies. The psychiatrist could more deliberately amplify and expand this change talk through complex reflections.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions are consistently framed around the client's expertise, perspective, and preferences. The partnership is functional but could be made more visible with explicit statements of shared focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client produces rich change talk, including her own criteria for success and concrete action steps. The psychiatrist could strengthen this by more explicitly summarizing and amplifying those key statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is limited, but the psychiatrist handles the client's concerns appropriately without arguing or dismissing them. There is little opportunity to demonstrate more advanced softening techniques.
