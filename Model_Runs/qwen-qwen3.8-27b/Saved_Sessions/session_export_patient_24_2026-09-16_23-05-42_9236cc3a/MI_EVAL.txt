# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, without pathologizing Daniel’s drinking. The stance is supportive but remains somewhat professional and procedural rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Daniel’s concern, ambivalence, and desire not to overreact. They could go further by exploring deeper emotions, self-image, or the identity conflict between running and after-work drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the referral concern of alcohol use in relation to marathon training and recovery. It could more consistently link Daniel’s values and goals to a clearer change direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Daniel’s existing tracking, preferences, and idea of non-alcoholic beer. Collaboration is strong, though the conversation is still partly structured by the psychiatrist’s monitoring agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel is repeatedly told he does not have to decide or fix anything, and the choice is kept in his hands. Autonomy support is solid, though the psychiatrist introduces fairly specific experiments and slightly assumes the test may happen.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions draw out Daniel’s own practical ideas for noticing effects on recovery. The evocation remains practical and does not deeply access Daniel’s values, health identity, or intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and treats Daniel as the expert on his own body. Partnership is strong, but the psychiatrist still provides a fairly clear exploratory agenda rather than being fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniel is prompted to generate some change talk, such as trying non-alcoholic beer and using easy-run sharpness as a signal. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, summarize, or deepen this change talk with DARN-style exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Daniel’s sustain talk and acknowledges points such as “I drink, I sleep, I run.” However, the sustain talk is only partially softened and is not consistently reframed through double-sided or discrepancy-based reflections.
