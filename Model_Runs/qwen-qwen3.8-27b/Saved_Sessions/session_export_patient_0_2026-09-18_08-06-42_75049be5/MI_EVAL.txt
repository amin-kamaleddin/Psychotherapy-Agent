# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; no judgment, labeling, or conditional regard at any point. Validates Adrian's ambivalence, self-awareness, and right to define his own experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and sophisticated reflective listening that captures both meaning and feeling. Reflections are non-repetitive and build in complexity across the session, with standout moves like distinguishing the 'alone vs. social' use pattern.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from referral through ambivalence to identity-based motivation and a concrete next step. The session ending lacks a strategic summary or consolidation of Adrian's stated values, making closure somewhat abrupt.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance with open-ended questions that invite Adrian's perspective at every turn. Adrian's own language ('smart about it,' 'that's not me,' 'alone in my apartment') drives the session direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Adrian's ownership; never uses directive language like 'should' or 'need to.' All next steps are framed as client-defined, and his stated boundaries are respected without pushing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All insights and motivation emerge from Adrian himself, never imposed by the clinician. Questions are perfectly calibrated to elicit self-defined reasons for change and small, realistic commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions self as a partner and cedes the agenda to the client from the outset. The psychiatrist's role is to reflect, deepen, and gently redirect—never to lead or prescribe.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits identity-based and ability-based change talk, with Adrian spontaneously producing reasons and a self-defined plan. Could have gone one level deeper by further amplifying the 'that's not me' identity statement or eliciting a confidence rating to consolidate motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional handling of minimization using double-sided reflections that honor both sides without amplifying the sustain talk. Every instance of 'I'm fine' or 'I'm not a junkie' is acknowledged as reasonable while gently keeping the door open for change.
