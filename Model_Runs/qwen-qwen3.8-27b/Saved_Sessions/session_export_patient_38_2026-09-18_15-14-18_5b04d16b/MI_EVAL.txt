# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, explicitly normalizing Elena's feelings and never expressing judgment about her ambivalence. The acceptance could be deepened by more explicit normalization of her identity struggle around control.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate capture of Elena's core tension and use of complex reflections. Empathy occasionally stays at the cognitive level rather than deeply reflecting the emotional weight of shame and the pain of feeling like a failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with well-managed pacing that moves logically from validation through ambivalence to a small actionable step. The session could more explicitly connect Elena's plan to a specific next-session check-in point.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently frames the interaction as collaborative and follows Elena's lead, offering her choices on next steps. The collaboration is somewhat patterned (ask-respond-reflect-ask) and could be more dynamic with moments of true co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent autonomy support with explicit honoring of Elena's pace, no imposed advice or pressure for immediate decision-making, and a powerful closing affirmation that her plan 'feels like yours, not like homework.' This is the strongest dimension of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic, well-designed questions effectively draw out Elena's own motivations and values, successfully eliciting change talk such as 'that's still my call.' The evocation could more consistently amplify and reinforce change talk once it appears.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and the session is clearly co-directed, following Elena's lead and deferring to her on boundaries. The question sequence occasionally feels structured by the psychiatrist's therapeutic arc rather than fully emergent from Elena's contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes change talk through strategic reframes and reflections, successfully helping Elena articulate her own motivations and values. Change talk could be more consistently reinforced with explicit amplification to maximize its impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk gracefully, accepting Elena's resistance without fighting it while gently maintaining the thread of exploration. The softening could be more active in some moments, such as using double-sided reflections that create a bridge rather than simply accepting a stall.
