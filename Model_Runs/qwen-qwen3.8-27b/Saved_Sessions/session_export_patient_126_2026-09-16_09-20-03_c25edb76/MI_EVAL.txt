### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, while repeatedly affirming Thomas's choices and strengths. Acceptance is slightly formulaic, with a mildly corrective tone in the opening.

### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** The counselor uses rulers and open questions to draw out change talk and confidence. The evocation remains procedural and does not fully explore Thomas's values or deeper motivations.

### Partnership

**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Thomas's input and allows his preferences to shape the plan. The structured options and focus on target scores can still feel counselor-led.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively uses evocative questions and rulers to elicit practical change talk and increase confidence. The change talk remains largely behavioral rather than deeply values-based.
