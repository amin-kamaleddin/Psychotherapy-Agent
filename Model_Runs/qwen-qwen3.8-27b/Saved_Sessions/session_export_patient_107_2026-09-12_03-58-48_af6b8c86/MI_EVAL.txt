# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, nonjudgmental, and repeatedly validates the client's perspective without pressure. However, warmth remains somewhat procedural and could be more explicitly affirming of the client's strengths and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects the client's ambivalence and key concerns. It does not fully explore the deeper emotional meaning, identity, or value content behind the client's attachment to coffee.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on coffee use, jitters, blood pressure, and a possible small step. The direction becomes somewhat narrow around the blood pressure check rather than broader motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor works jointly with the client and allows the client's preferences to shape the plan. However, the counselor still generates much of the structure and menu of options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently supports the client's freedom to choose and avoids coercive or precommittal language. The final plan is clearly client-controlled, with change contingent on the client's own decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses some open questions and confidence scaling, but evocation is limited. The client's own reasons for change and values are not deeply elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor respects the client's expertise and follows the client's lead in shaping the plan. However, the counselor still drives much of the option generation and session structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some action-oriented change talk, such as checking blood pressure and considering small steps if the reading is high. It does not consistently draw out broader reasons, importance, or values related to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with or reinforcing the client's sustain talk. It could use stronger MI moves to hold both sides and evoke discrepancy.
