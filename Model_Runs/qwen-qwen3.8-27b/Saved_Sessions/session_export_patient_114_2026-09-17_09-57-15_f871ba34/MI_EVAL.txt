# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintains a nonjudgmental and validating stance. However, the warmth is somewhat formulaic and lacks more explicit affirmation of Ryan as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate and sophisticated perspective-taking by capturing Ryan's underlying fears of rejection. Reflections effectively name the core paradox of his ambivalence regarding social acceptance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the target behavior and progresses purposefully toward actionable steps. It lacks a fully consolidated closing that reconnects the discussion to a specific, client-owned next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively builds on Ryan's ideas and uses collaborative language to keep the conversation interactive. However, the process is more reflective than explicitly co-constructed through shared agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports Ryan's sense of choice and avoids directive pressure or coercive language. The stance would be stronger with more explicit statements affirming Ryan's freedom to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions successfully draw out Ryan's own motivations, values, and practical solutions. The exploration remains somewhat surface-level and does not fully deepen into identity-based change talk or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interview follows Ryan's lead and treats him as the expert on his social world. Partnership is slightly weakened by moments where the psychiatrist assumes or inserts the client's values without asking.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes practical, value-based change talk from the client. There is room for growth in systematically amplifying this talk to increase confidence and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** While the psychiatrist avoids confrontation, they often validate Ryan's desire to drink without sufficiently redirecting toward values. There are missed opportunities to pivot back to safety and concrete change plans.
