# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently showed unconditional positive regard and normalized Elira’s anxiety without judgment. Statements like “it doesn’t sound pathetic” and “you’re not overthinking” affirmed her intentions and reduced self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was highly accurate and captured Elira’s ambivalence and emotional experience. The counselor clearly understood and validated both the desire to follow through and the fear of chickening out.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on the target behavior of showing up for practice despite anxiety. The conversation moved clearly from general avoidance to concrete, actionable first steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked with Elira as an equal partner rather than directing the conversation. Her ideas were used to shape the next steps, making the session feel like a joint exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elira’s ownership and choice were consistently reinforced throughout the interaction. The counselor avoided directive language and kept the proposed steps feeling small, low-pressure, and self-directed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively drew out Elira’s own strategies and reasons for change. It could be stronger by more explicitly evoking deeper values-based confidence talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Elira as the expert on her own life and invited her to generate the solutions. The session was shaped almost entirely by Elira’s contributions, showing strong power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited useful client-generated change talk, such as the five-minute rule and texting a friend. The session could be strengthened by more explicitly eliciting internal desire, ability, or values language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled respectfully without arguing or reinforcing avoidance. The counselor reframed self-critical thoughts, though more active double-sided reflections could further reduce their weight.
