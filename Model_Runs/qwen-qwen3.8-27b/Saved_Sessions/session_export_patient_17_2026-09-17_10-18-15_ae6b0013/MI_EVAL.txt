# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warm, unconditional positive regard without judgment or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses precise complex reflections that accurately capture both content and emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation remains tightly focused on smoking cessation and progresses logically from ambivalence to specific, observable target behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session reflects genuine equal partnership, with the client leading plan development and the psychiatrist co-constructing rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client agency is actively fostered through non-pressure, choice-focused language and respect for her own pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivations, reasons, and solutions are drawn from the client herself rather than supplied by the psychiatrist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the expert on her own life, with the psychiatrist avoiding prescribing or expert postures.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled evocative questioning elicits abundant, specific, and self-generated change talk about concrete cessation strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with reflective acknowledgment and gentle redirection rather than argument, debate, or reinforcement.
