# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and unconditional positive regard throughout the session. The psychiatrist never judges or disapproves of Diego's heroin use, validating his experience, fear, and right to choose harm reduction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and emotional undercurrents. Reflections go beyond surface-level paraphrases to name unspoken emotions and validate specific fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear session arc from emotional context through specific fear to concrete harm-reduction steps and follow-through planning. Stays responsive to Diego's state without drifting into irrelevant topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative interaction with equal power dynamics. Diego defines goals, identifies actions, generates strategy, and sets timeline—acting as co-creator rather than passive recipient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Diego's ownership and right to choose, never pressuring toward cessation. Explicitly affirms his autonomy in setting harm reduction as his own legitimate goal.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits motivation from within Diego rather than providing external reasons. Diego generates a complete, self-directed action plan including the texting strategy for follow-through.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently located within Diego through evocative questions that draw on his lived experience. The psychiatrist acts as a facilitator rather than director, never assuming the expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk emerges naturally and is skillfully amplified through reflection, affirmation, and connection to Diego's values. Diego articulates concrete, self-generated commitments with high confidence (10/10).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement and gently redirected toward harm reduction. Could benefit from one or two more explicit double-sided reflections that hold both sides in tension.
