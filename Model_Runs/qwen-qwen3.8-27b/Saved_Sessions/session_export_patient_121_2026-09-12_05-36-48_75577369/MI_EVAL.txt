# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Sofia's frustration and choices. Acceptance is present but somewhat formulaic and not deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects surface-level feelings such as frustration and unfairness. However, the empathy often stays at the situational level and does not consistently explore deeper emotions such as shame, fear, or loneliness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on Sofia's main concerns, including peer conflict, rumors, school avoidance, and communication with Jada. The counselor guides the conversation toward a clear, manageable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor works with Sofia by offering choices, allowing edits, and respecting her final message wording. Collaboration is strong but somewhat constrained by the counselor's preselected options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently supports Sofia's sense of choice and avoids overt pressure. Autonomy support could be stronger by inviting Sofia to identify her own goals and reasons rather than repeatedly offering prepared options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor makes limited effort to evoke Sofia's internal motivation and often shifts into problem-solving and script-building. Sofia's deeper values, hopes, and confidence are not consistently drawn out or developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses a collaborative stance by asking for input and offering choices. However, the counselor frequently supplies the agenda and content, so Sofia does not fully direct the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia offers some change talk, but the counselor does not consistently reflect, amplify, or develop it. The session shifts too quickly into practical scripts rather than exploring Sofia's reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Sofia and accepts some avoidant ideas as valid. However, sustain talk is not consistently softened using deeper MI techniques such as balanced or double-sided reflections.
