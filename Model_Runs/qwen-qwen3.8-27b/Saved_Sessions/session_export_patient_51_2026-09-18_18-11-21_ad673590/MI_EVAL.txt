# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental tone with phrases like 'you deserve a straight answer' and 'I respect your time.' However, affirmations feel somewhat formulaic and surface-level rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening that captures Ingrid's practical concerns and time pressure. Empathy remains one-dimensional, focusing on the surface-level time constraint rather than exploring deeper emotional undercurrents like anxiety about medication safety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains focus on the medication question but lacks a clear strategic arc toward a change goal. The session feels more like a brief informational exchange than a strategically directed MI intervention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows passive collaboration by asking for input and working within constraints, but never explicitly frames the interaction as a joint effort. The final repeated offers of availability shift the dynamic toward a one-sided professional-client hierarchy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support through respecting time constraints, affirming Ingrid's plan, and explicitly positioning her as the decision-maker in consultation with her doctor. Could be stronger by affirming her right to make any decision, including not proceeding with the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A significant weakness; the psychiatrist makes very limited attempts to elicit motivation from within Ingrid. The session is predominantly information-delivery rather than evocation, with Ingrid's change talk left unreflected and unexpanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a generally collaborative stance but largely assumes the role of information provider rather than a true partner. The psychiatrist does not explicitly frame Ingrid as the expert on her own life or invite her to co-construct the conversation's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal and thin attempts to cultivate change talk, with no strategic questions designed to expand Ingrid's own change language. Ingrid's statement 'I'd rather not find out the hard way' is never reflected back or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles Ingrid's time constraints reasonably by acknowledging them without pushing back. However, fails to strategically reframe or redirect sustain talk, such as using the time constraint as a sign of commitment to self-care.
