# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Anya's ambivalence without criticizing her gambling or finances. However, the acceptance remains more efficient than deeply personal, with occasional subtle steering that reduces unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses solid reflective listening to capture Anya's tension between protecting money and maintaining social connection. The reflections could be deeper and more individualized by exploring underlying shame, loneliness, or fear of losing her social identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing gambling-related spending and moves from broad financial worry to concrete, time-bound actions. The target behavior could be made more explicit and linked back to longer-term goals such as debt reduction, stability, and future plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor builds on Anya's ideas and collaborates on practical steps such as canceling cards, changing venues, and using Sarah as support. The partnership could be stronger by allowing more client-led framing and reducing counselor-initiated labels or structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports autonomy by asking Anya to choose realistic options and emphasizing that she knows her life best. It would be even stronger if the counselor more explicitly and repeatedly reaffirmed Anya's freedom to accept, reject, or modify the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits Anya's own motivations, plans, and change talk through open-ended questions rather than providing reasons for change. The counseling could be more purely evocative by following up more consistently on change talk with deeper exploratory questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor conveys partnership by repeatedly inviting Anya to define her priorities, first steps, and realistic options. The stance could be more equal by reducing counselor-initiated framing and showing even clearer co-creation and agenda negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor generates strong change talk by inviting Anya to articulate specific intentions, such as canceling credit cards and planning a non-gambling night out. The work could be deepened with summaries, confidence questions, and stronger connections to future values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles sustain talk well by acknowledging Anya's reasons for the status quo without arguing or amplifying them. The counseling could be improved by using more deliberate double-sided reflections to help Anya resolve the ambivalence more explicitly.
