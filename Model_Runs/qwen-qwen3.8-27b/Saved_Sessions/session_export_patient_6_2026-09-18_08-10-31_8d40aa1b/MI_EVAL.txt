### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with no labeling, disapproval, or implicit 'should' statements. Effectively validates minimization and honors the client's self-identity, particularly in the closing exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reflects both content and emotional undertone with sophisticated complex reflections that go beyond simple paraphrasing. Accurately captures subtle shifts in perspective, ambivalence, and social anxiety without over-interpreting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical, well-paced progression from initial resistance to a client-generated resolution. Could benefit from more strategic summaries at key transition points to reinforce momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue between equals where the client's ideas directly shape the session's direction. The power dynamic is truly balanced—Alexei pushes back and minimizes without any defensiveness or correction from the psychiatrist.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and right to choose, with no imposed expectations or conditions. The session ends with Alexei defining his own boundary rather than the psychiatrist prescribing a course of action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out the client's own reasons for change and produces genuine change talk through well-timed open questions. Could have been deeper in evoking ability talk and exploring core values beyond the surface level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook partnership—the psychiatrist consistently positions Alexei as the expert on his own life with no psychoeducation, advice-giving, or 'I think you should' statements. The closing 'That's enough' demonstrates full trust in the client's self-knowledge.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk through targeted questions and affirmations of the client's own statements. Could have pushed slightly further on commitment language or explored values more deeply to generate additional change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with soft, non-confrontational redirects and skillfully reframes identity-related sustain talk as continuity rather than regression. Missed an opportunity to explicitly address the client's doubt about his track record by exploring past successes.
