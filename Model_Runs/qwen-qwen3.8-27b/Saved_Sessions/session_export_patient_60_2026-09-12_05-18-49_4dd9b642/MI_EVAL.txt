# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer consistently uses a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Jason’s perspective. Warmth is somewhat formulaic, and a few practical suggestions feel slightly directive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and often accurate, especially around Jason’s frustration and ambivalence. Empathy stays mostly at a surface, practical level and could explore deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the presenting conduct issue and moves toward a concrete, manageable step. Direction becomes narrow later, emphasizing procedural compliance over broader discrepancy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jason contributes meaningfully to the final plan, showing real collaboration. The interviewer often supplies structure, options, and success criteria rather than allowing more client-generated content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer frequently affirms Jason’s choice, avoids pressure, and respects his refusal to use a script. Some prescriptive details reduce the sense that Jason is fully authoring his own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Confidence rulers and doable-step questions elicit useful client language about willingness. Evocation is limited because the interviewer often moves quickly to counselor-generated solutions instead of eliciting deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is genuine collaboration, with Jason selecting the one-night test, rideshare backup, and screenshot step. The interviewer frequently steers the session structure and supplies options, making partnership uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is present, but it is mostly external and compliance-focused rather than personally meaningful. The interviewer does not consistently deepen or amplify intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer avoids arguing with Jason’s sustain talk and keeps defensiveness low. However, he does not actively soften sustain talk with double-sided reflections or explore the underlying ambivalence.
