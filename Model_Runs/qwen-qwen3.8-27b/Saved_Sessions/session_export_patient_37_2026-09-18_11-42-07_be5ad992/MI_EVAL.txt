# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with genuine, individualized warmth throughout. Never expresses judgment or disapproval, even when the client minimizes risk or expresses hesitation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows progressively deepening reflective listening that captures both cognitive and emotional dimensions with increasing precision. Tracks the client's emotional shift from defensive to open with masterful accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and consistent focus on the vaccine decision with logical progression throughout. Could benefit from brief summarizing or wayfinding statements at key transition points to make direction more collaboratively explicit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary joint exploration where the client's ideas and responses shape the direction at every step. The psychiatrist co-constructs the plan, seeks input before proceeding, and never dictates the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's right to make her own choices without pressure, framing indecision as thoughtfulness. Could be strengthened by more explicit affirmations of the client's ultimate ownership of the decision at key moments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently uses evocative questions that draw motivation from within the client, skillfully leveraging her own past experience (pregnancy) to evoke competence. Could be more systematic in targeting specific change talk dimensions and deeper in amplifying the change talk that emerges.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative stance, positioning herself as a resource rather than an authority and letting the client shape the session direction. The power dynamic shifts slightly toward the expert role during the medical information transition, though handled appropriately.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes multiple forms of change talk (concern, desire, ability, reasons) and skillfully amplifies when it appears. Could be more deliberate in eliciting specific change talk dimensions and deeper in building upon strong change talk statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk in a neutral, non-reinforcing manner, appropriately avoiding arguing or amplifying reasons against change. Misses opportunities to use double-sided reflections, strategic pivots, or other techniques to actively shift the balance toward change.
