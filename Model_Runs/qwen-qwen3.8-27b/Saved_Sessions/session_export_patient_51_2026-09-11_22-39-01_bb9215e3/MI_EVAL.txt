# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a neutral, respectful tone with supportive phrases like "That sounds like a responsible plan," but warmth is limited and formulaic. Does not acknowledge Ingrid's stress, time pressure, or difficulty making decisions under pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited reflective listening; moves immediately from Ingrid's concern into a factual, informational response. Does not reflect her urgency or the push-pull between wanting safe information and needing to leave quickly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conversation stays clearly focused on the citalopram–phenelzine interaction, washout period, and emergency signs. However, direction is repetitive rather than progressive, repeatedly delivering the same safety script without moving toward a collaborative decision.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The interaction is predominantly one-way and informational after the initial open question. Does not invite Ingrid to co-create next steps, prioritize content, or decide how much detail she needs given her time constraint.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Not coercive, but repeatedly issues the same directives to call prescriber/pharmacist, which diminishes Ingrid's sense of being the decision-maker. Does not explicitly affirm her freedom to choose or her ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Session is dominated by direct medical information with no effective evocative questions about her goals, confidence, or preferred next steps. Ingrid's own change talk about confirming the washout is never drawn out or expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Opening question briefly invites patient input, but the remainder of the session is markedly expert-driven and repetitive. Does not convey that expertise over her own priorities and decisions resides with Ingrid.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Provides a mild affirmation ("responsible plan") but does not reflect, amplify, or deepen Ingrid's proactive concern about the interaction. Supplies directives rather than strategically drawing out her own motivation for safe decision-making.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does not argue with Ingrid's time barrier, which is appropriate, but also does not skillfully soften or redirect it. Repeatedly reissues the full safety message even after she multiple times states she must leave, focusing attention on constraints rather than her agency.
