# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, but acceptance feels more clinical and task-oriented than genuinely warm. There were missed opportunities to explicitly affirm Alexei's maturity, soccer commitment, and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflections were present but often factual and surface-level, focusing on administrative concerns rather than emotional experiences. The counselor did not consistently explore Alexei's anxiety, embarrassment, fear for his soccer future, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Although the target issue was alcohol use, the session frequently drifted toward policy, citations, eligibility, and consequence management. The conversation lacked a consistent, coherent direction around Alexei's motivations and readiness to change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were moments of collaboration, but the overall dynamic remained expert-driven with the counselor leading problem-solving and advice-giving. Alexei's stated priorities did not strongly shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some choice language was used, but frequent prescribed steps and prepared responses undermined client ownership. A stronger approach would have elicited Alexei's own plan and emphasized his role in decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The counselor primarily provided external reasons, risk information, and advice rather than evoking Alexei's own values and motivation. Missed clear opportunities to explore what matters most to him and his own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor made occasional collaborative offers, but the session remained largely directive and counselor-led. Alexei's expertise about his own goals, values, and risk tolerance was not fully centered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Alexei offered strong change talk about soccer, scholarships, and avoiding escalation, but the counselor only weakly reflected it. More strategic reflections or questions could have deepened his own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor did not argue with minimization, but responses often reassured and reduced urgency in ways that may reinforce sustain talk. Balanced reflections linking his wish to minimize with his concern about consequences were missing.
