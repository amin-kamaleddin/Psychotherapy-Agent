# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Liyana’s effort and self-care. Acceptance is strong, though it could be warmer and more explicitly affirming in the face of harsh self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses accurate complex reflections of frustration, relational needs, and fear of being seen as the problem. Empathy is strong but misses deeper contact around perfectionism, shame, and self-worth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays generally focused on confidence and support without drifting. However, it does not consistently connect the relational work to a specific health behavior, coping skill, or target change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor treats the conversation as a joint exploration and allows Liyana’s ideas to guide the session. Collaboration is solid but would benefit from more explicit shared agenda-setting and co-constructed next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids pressure, respects Liyana’s choices, and invites her to define doable steps. Autonomy is well supported, though it could be made more explicit by affirming her freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions draw out Liyana’s needs, first steps, and preferred support. Evocation is limited by less exploration of values, confidence, and intrinsic motivation for health-related change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently positions Liyana as the expert on her own life and avoids imposing a plan. Partnership is strong but lacks more explicit power-sharing and co-created agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits meaningful change talk about desire, need, and possible action. However, the counselor does not consistently amplify or consolidate that change talk into stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor handles doubts with nonjudgmental, double-sided reflections and avoids reinforcing sustain talk. There is less strategic redirection toward values, competence, or preferred outcomes.
