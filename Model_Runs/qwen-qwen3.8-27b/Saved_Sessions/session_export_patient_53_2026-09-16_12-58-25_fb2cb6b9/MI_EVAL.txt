# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist remains respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Isabella's hesitancy without labeling her as resistant. Acceptance is consistent but somewhat brief and task-focused rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Isabella's fatigue, fear of being noticed, and ambivalence about leaving home. Empathy is useful and understanding, but it does not consistently deepen the emotional meaning of her ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on a small step toward community engagement or social connection. There is minimal drift, with repeated redirection toward a concrete, low-pressure behavior plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as joint problem-solving and uses Isabella's preferences to shape the plan. Collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist still drives much of the planning and option-generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist emphasizes choice, permission to stop anytime, and Isabella's role in selecting the plan. Autonomy is well supported, though some concrete suggestions remain fairly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some evocative questions and an importance scale, but the session shifts quickly toward practical planning. There is limited exploration of Isabella's internal motivations, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Isabella's input and allows her choices to shape the plan. Partnership is strong, but the session remains more clinician-guided than fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Isabella offers several desire, ability, and commitment statements, and the psychiatrist affirms them. Change talk appears but is not consistently amplified, explored, or strengthened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist validates rather than contests Isabella's sustain talk and reduces pressure by shrinking the task and offering a fallback. Sustain talk remains salient at the end, limiting a higher score.
