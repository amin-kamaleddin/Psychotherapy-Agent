# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Tyler’s concerns without shaming. Acceptance was consistent, though somewhat procedural rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections captured surface-level worries about the incident, peers, and fitting in. However, they often stayed cognitive and did not fully explore embarrassment, fear, shame, or belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on drinking, the conduct incident, and a concrete risk-reduction plan. Momentum was clear, though the focus became somewhat task-oriented and narrow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist used collaborative language and invited Tyler to choose among options. The power balance was uneven because many strategies and plans were therapist-generated rather than client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Permission-seeking and offering choices supported client decision-making. The overall style remained directive, especially when drafting the text and pushing for immediate action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and rulers helped elicit some of Tyler’s reasons and confidence. The therapist quickly shifted to problem-solving rather than consistently drawing out deeper motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There was partial collaboration through consent, choice-making, and brainstorming. Tyler often selected from therapist-provided options rather than generating his own solutions first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encouraged change talk through importance/confidence rulers and planning questions. However, meaningful change statements were not consistently reflected, amplified, or expanded.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Tyler’s sustain talk about staying for the party and minimizing the police risk was not consistently addressed or reframed. The therapist occasionally introduced reasons for change rather than helping Tyler examine his own ambivalence.
