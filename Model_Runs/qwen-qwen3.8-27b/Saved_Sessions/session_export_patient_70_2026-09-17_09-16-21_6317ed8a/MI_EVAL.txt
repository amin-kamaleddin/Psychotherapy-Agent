# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming stance throughout the session. There was no moralizing, criticism, or pressure, even when Lars discussed boredom, alcohol, old peers, and previous risky patterns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used accurate, often nuanced reflections of Lars’s ambivalence, shame, and pressure. The empathy could be strengthened by deeper confirmation and exploration of underlying feelings such as fear, guilt, and identity concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed generally focused on reducing recidivism risk through boredom, peer pressure, and re-entry stress. It could be tightened by more explicitly linking practical steps to high-risk relapse-prevention situations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked as an equal partner, using open questions, reflections, and Lars’s own language to shape the session. There was no authoritarian stance, unsolicited advice, or imposed agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Lars was repeatedly invited to choose, decide, and own his plan, with strong respect for his independence from Donna. Autonomy support would be stronger by more explicitly emphasizing that any plan is optional and fully Lars’s to reject or change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited Lars’s own reasons, confidence, and commitment through open questions and reflections. Deeper evocation of values, fatherhood, identity, and long-term motivation would make it more consistent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positioned Lars as the expert on his own life and recovery. Lars’s choices drove the agenda, plan, and direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully invited Lars to express desire, reasons, ability, and commitment, and amplified meaningful change language. It could be more systematic in exploring confidence, values, and deeper personal goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without arguing and redirected toward Lars-controlled strategies. The approach would be more sophisticated with explicit double-sided reflections exploring both the appeal and risk of old behaviors.
