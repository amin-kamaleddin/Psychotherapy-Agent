# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, supportive, and nonjudgmental stance throughout. The tone was warm but somewhat formulaic and task-focused, limiting deeper normalization of emotional weight.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used useful reflections that accurately tracked Lars’s ambivalence and practical concerns. However, the reflections did not always deepen into underlying emotions such as shame, fear, or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on a concrete target: attending the job center and completing the form. At times, the focus became administrative and was not consistently linked back to broader personal motivation and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was collaborative, with Lars choosing the goal, timing, wording, and strategy. The psychiatrist still supplied a substantial amount of practical advice, making the process partly expert-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly invited Lars to make choices and respected his preferences. Some affirming and advisory language slightly reduced emphasis on his full ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used effective questions to elicit client-generated action steps and confidence ratings. The evocation remained procedural and did not consistently draw out deeper values or personal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adopted a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and allowed Lars to shape the plan. The session would be stronger with more explicit framing of Lars as the expert on his own life and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Lars expressed meaningful change talk, and the psychiatrist prompted it with some relevant questions. The psychiatrist did not consistently amplify, reflect, or build on this change talk to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged sustain talk without judgment and redirected toward practical action. However, he did not consistently use MI strategies to reframe resistance or explore the values behind Lars’s stated needs.
