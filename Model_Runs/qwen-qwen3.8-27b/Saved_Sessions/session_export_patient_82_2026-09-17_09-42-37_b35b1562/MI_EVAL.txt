# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful, and supportive stance. The client’s values, strengths, and emotional difficulty were affirmed without shaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor used accurate reflections of the client’s ambivalence, fear of rejection, and identity concerns. Some reflections felt slightly repetitive and could have deepened the emotional meaning further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on alcohol use, peer pressure, friendships, and its impact on school and athletics. The direction was clear, though it could have more consistently moved toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked collaboratively by inviting client input and using the client’s examples to shape the conversation. The partnership could have been stronger with more explicit co-creation language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoided pressure, advice, and expert-driven suggestions. However, explicit statements affirming the client’s autonomy, choice, and ownership were not consistent enough.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively used open questions and reflections to draw out the client’s own motivations and values. Advanced evocative techniques such as importance rulers, confidence rulers, or self-motivational summaries were not used consistently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client’s perspective strongly influenced the session, including ideas about hosting, watching games, and video games. The counselor was collaborative but still led the process more than fully co-directing it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently elicited change talk through client-centered questions about staying connected without drinking. These change statements were not consistently amplified, reflected back, or strategically built upon.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was accepted without arguing or confronting the client. The counselor did not consistently use double-sided reflections or other strategies to gently pivot sustain talk toward change.
