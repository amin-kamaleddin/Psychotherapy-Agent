# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates both the function of drinking and Irina’s values. Acceptance could be deeper with more explicit unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate complex reflections that capture the benefit and cost of alcohol. Some reflections remain more cognitive than affective, missing deeper emotional validation of guilt, exhaustion, and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use and related coping, with a clear logical flow from ambivalence to action planning. Repetitive prompts and early planning slightly disrupt the pacing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the work as joint and incorporates Irina’s ideas into the plan. Offering multiple options at once can make it feel less like true co-creation and more like choosing from a counselor menu.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses permission-seeking language and respects Irina’s choice to pursue a small change. It could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her freedom to adjust or stop the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes Irina’s values, confidence, and criteria for noticing change. Early introduction of options and less focus on deeper identity-based change talk limit the evocation slightly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows Irina to shape the experiment. However, prescriptive option menus and repeated prompts create an expert-driven feel that weakens equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Scaling and follow-up questions elicit clear change talk and increase Irina’s confidence. The focus is sometimes shifted away from client-generated motivation by premature suggestions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges the function of alcohol and offers harm-reduction options without arguing. More double-sided reflections and deeper ambivalence work would strengthen this criterion.
