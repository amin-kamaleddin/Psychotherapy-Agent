# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses nonjudgmental and validating language without moralizing about Sofia's drinking or ambivalence. The acceptance feels slightly formulaic and lacks deeper, individualized unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but mostly brief and cognitive, capturing stress and social pressure without exploring deeper feelings. There are missed opportunities to address shame, fear of harm, identity loss, and the feeling of 'wearing a mask.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on alcohol use during pregnancy and the upcoming high-risk weekend. The direction becomes somewhat tactical, but the target behavior remains central throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Sofia's preferences and helps refine a manageable plan, showing partial collaboration. However, much of the plan and language is provided by the psychiatrist, making the interaction less balanced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers options and avoids pressure, supporting choice. The high level of directive advice and scripted responses can subtly reduce Sofia's sense of being fully in charge of her own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative techniques are used early, including open questions and a readiness ruler. The session shifts toward advice-giving rather than continuing to draw out Sofia's values, motivation, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is evidence of partnership through asking for preferences and adjusting to Sofia's choices. The interaction still feels more expert-driven because the psychiatrist frequently supplies the content of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits meaningful change talk early using readiness questions and open exploration. This change talk is not consistently amplified, reflected back, or expanded before moving into practical planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument or reinforcement, but the counselor does not consistently use MI-consistent techniques. Responses sometimes shift into advice or factual information rather than exploring ambivalence.
