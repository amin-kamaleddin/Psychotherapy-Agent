# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and validates Seamus's preference for dental care. Falls short of top score because warmth is somewhat procedural and repetitive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers accurate reflections about Seamus's concern about teeth, prior bad medication experience, and practical visit worries. Could go deeper into the emotional meaning of his resistance and frustration with repeated provider advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, organized focus on dental care, which matches Seamus's presenting concern. Direction is weaker for smoking-cessation MI because the session quickly shifts away from change-related work after resistance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively by offering options and incorporating Seamus's preferences into the dental plan. Less fully collaborative at the start, where the opening question and early plan feel more clinician-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports Seamus's autonomy by avoiding pressure, explicitly stating no quitting push unless requested, and respecting his refusal of smoking-related interventions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks some evocative questions but does not fully use Seamus's statements, such as knowing smoking is not good for him, to draw out motivation. Lacks deeper use of scaling, importance, or ability questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Seamus as an active contributor, especially in planning timing, visits, numbing, and gag-reflex strategies. The opening is somewhat crowded and intake-like, reducing the sense of fully co-created agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes limited attempts to develop smoking-related change talk and does not build effectively on Seamus's own awareness that smoking is not good for him. Relies more on informational options than on evoking Seamus's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles resistance well by avoiding argument and accepting Seamus's refusal to discuss quitting. Could be stronger by using more strategic reflections to gently redirect sustain talk toward values or goals.
