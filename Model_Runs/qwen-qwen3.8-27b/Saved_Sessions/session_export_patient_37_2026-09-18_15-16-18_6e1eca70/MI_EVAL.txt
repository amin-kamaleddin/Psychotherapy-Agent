# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exemplary unconditional positive regard throughout; validates Elena's protective instincts and normalizes her hesitation without a single moment of judgment. The warmth is genuine and specific to her situation rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections capture multiple layers of Elena's experience simultaneously, consistently accessing the affective dimension beyond surface-level paraphrasing. The most powerful moment reflects the deep pain of watching a toddler sick with no way to explain what's happening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the vaccination decision with logical progression from hesitation to exploration to tentative next step. The middle portion meanders slightly through the chickenpox discussion before arriving at the key emotional image.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as genuine co-construction with Elena determining the direction and pace throughout. The final plan is entirely client-driven, and the psychiatrist follows her lead consistently without imposing an agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly reinforces Elena's right to choose and ownership of the decision, never implying a correct answer or creating urgency. When Elena says she doesn't want to decide today, the psychiatrist validates this without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questions successfully elicit Elena's own change talk ('I don't want to be that mom,' 'I just want to be able to sleep at night'). Could have gone further by exploring her values more deeply or asking her to rate her confidence or readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Elena, explicitly rejecting an expert-driven approach. The power dynamic is genuinely equal — her concerns, questions, and pace shape the entire session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively names and amplifies Elena's shift from skepticism to personal desire, highlighting that 'that's you, not anyone pushing you.' Could have pushed further by asking her to elaborate on what sleeping at night would mean or exploring her confidence more explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates the legitimate kernel in Elena's sustain talk (her body handled chickenpox) while gently creating space for new perspectives without arguing or dismissing. Could have used more double-sided reflections to explicitly hold both sides simultaneously.
