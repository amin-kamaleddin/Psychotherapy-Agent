# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Lars’s ambivalence, stress, and past challenges. Acceptance could be stronger by explicitly affirming Lars’s worth beyond the task, such as his courage or value as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, especially around Lars’s desire to maintain pride and avoid feeling dependent. Empathy could be deeper by exploring stronger emotions such as shame, fear of returning to prison, guilt, or hopes for being a better father.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear, clinically relevant direction and moves from broad reentry concerns to a concrete, manageable first step. Direction could be improved by more consistently linking the small form task back to larger goals such as avoiding reoffending or strengthening his relationship with Donna.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates with Lars in building a small plan and validates his choices rather than instructing him. Collaboration could be stronger by giving Lars more space to set the agenda and generate solutions without the counselor continuously offering structured options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Lars’s autonomy by emphasizing his ability to choose and doing the work for himself. Autonomy support could be enhanced by using more open-ended prompts rather than repeated multiple-choice options that may subtly steer the conversation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses evocative questions but focuses mainly on practical next steps rather than deeper intrinsic motivation. Evocation could be strengthened by drawing out Lars’s values, hopes, and reasons for change, such as his relationship with Donna, being a better father, or avoiding reoffending.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids a didactic stance and repeatedly invites Lars’s perspective and input. Partnership could be improved by more explicitly acknowledging Lars as the central decision-maker and negotiating the agenda more openly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some change-oriented language and affirms Lars’s agency, but the focus remains more task-focused than motivational. Cultivating change talk could be improved by systematically eliciting and expanding Lars’s desire, ability, reasons, and need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without judgment and reframes some barriers in less shaming ways. Softening sustain talk could be stronger by using double-sided reflections and helping Lars see the value in his current choices while gently connecting them to longer-term goals.
