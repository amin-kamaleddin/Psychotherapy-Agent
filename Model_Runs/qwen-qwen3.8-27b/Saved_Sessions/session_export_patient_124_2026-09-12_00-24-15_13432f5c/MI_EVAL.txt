# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses a nonjudgmental tone and normalizes the patient's coping. However, the warmth feels somewhat practical and repetitive rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects practical concerns and shows adequate understanding. Empathy remains mostly content-level and does not consistently explore deeper feelings, ambivalence, or complex tensions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and narrows the discussion to a practical spending plan. Direction is more task-oriented than MI-oriented, though the focus remains relevant.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaboration in choosing low-effort options and adjusting plans to the patient's preferences. However, the psychiatrist still drives much of the session through menus, scripts, and recaps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly supports choice by emphasizing that no strict plan is required and that the patient can stop if it becomes burdensome. The amount of suggested scripts, reminders, and options keeps it slightly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks a few evocative questions early, but mostly shifts into advice, planning, and practical steps. There is limited development of the patient's own motivations, values, or readiness for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the patient to choose options and respects some preferences. The interaction remains largely counselor-driven, with the psychiatrist providing much of the structure and direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some change talk is present, such as wanting to stop draining the account and trying the Friday plan. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or expand the patient's own motivation, confidence, or values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing sustain talk and uses low-pressure acceptance. However, sustain talk is not consistently reframed or pivoted toward change using double-sided reflections.
