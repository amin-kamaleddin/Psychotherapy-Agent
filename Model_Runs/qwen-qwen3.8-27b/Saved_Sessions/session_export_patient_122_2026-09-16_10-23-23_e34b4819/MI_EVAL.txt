# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect and nonjudgment by normalizing stress and affirming the client's efforts. Some responses validate quickly and move into problem-solving, so acceptance could feel more deeply unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects the client's need for a gentle, sustainable routine and respects boundaries around check-ins. Empathy could be stronger by exploring deeper shame, self-criticism, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on a specific, actionable movement plan. Each question moves the conversation toward concrete cues, success criteria, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client actively co-creates the plan, choosing the two-minute step, cues, timing, and follow-up format. The counselor builds on the client's ideas rather than imposing a preformed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor uses permission-based language and respects the client's comfort with check-ins and messaging. Choices are framed as low-pressure, preserving the client's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits the client's own ideas about action, confidence, barriers, and support. Deeper evocation of values, hopes, and intrinsic motivation would further strengthen the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and the client clearly shapes the plan. The interaction is slightly therapist-led at times, so more explicit power-sharing would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor evokes practical change talk by encouraging the client to identify doable actions and confidence levels. Amplifying deeper change talk around meaning, values, and self-efficacy would be beneficial.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles doubt and past failure without judgment, reframing missed steps as part of trying. More active double-sided reflection of ambivalence would further soften sustain talk.
