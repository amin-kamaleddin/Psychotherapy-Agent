# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoided lecturing, which supported a safe MI tone. However, acceptance felt somewhat formulaic and repetitive rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflected Marisol’s surface-level meaning accurately, such as choice, confidence, and looking authentically like herself. However, reflections missed deeper emotional or identity-based cues that could have been explored further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the lip piercing and related health concerns. However, it lacked a clear strategic focus on a specific target behavior or concrete change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There was a collaborative surface tone, but the psychiatrist still led the agenda and repeatedly offered information or red flags. Marisol’s priorities did not fully shape the direction of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly affirmed Marisol’s freedom to choose and avoided pressure or moralizing. This was occasionally weakened by repeatedly reopening information offers after Marisol declined them.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used some evocative questions about what the piercing means to Marisol. However, evocation was limited because promising client responses were not followed up or developed into stronger personal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicated that Marisol was the decision-maker, but the interaction remained fairly psychiatrist-led. The client’s concerns did not substantially reshape the session into a genuinely co-directed process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were some initial attempts to elicit change-relevant language. However, the psychiatrist did not effectively amplify, deepen, or follow up on Marisol’s emerging change talk, leaving it minimal and underdeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally accepted Marisol’s sustain talk without arguing or judgment. However, the psychiatrist rarely used MI-consistent strategies to gently soften or redirect that sustain talk toward self-care or future flexibility.
