### Empathy

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy and choice are consistently and explicitly reinforced throughout, woven into the fabric of the interaction rather than stated once. The psychiatrist never pressures or implies obligation, framing all decisions as Lukas's to make.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Lukas, never assuming the expert role or providing advice unasked. Language throughout positions Lukas as the authority on his own life and agent of his own change.
