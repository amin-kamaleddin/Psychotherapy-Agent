# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and individualized validation throughout without any trace of judgment. Each affirmation is specific to Rosa's words, never formulaic or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are deeply accurate, capturing the emotional and experiential core rather than offering superficial summaries. The psychiatrist demonstrates sophisticated understanding of identity, social function, and somatic experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and logical progression from ambivalence through weight, withdrawal, identity, social, and physical concerns. Could benefit from more explicit thematic signposting and a stronger tie-back to broader goals in the final portion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is fundamentally structured as two equals thinking together, with the psychiatrist following Rosa's lead rather than steering toward a pre-planned agenda. Rosa substantially shapes the content and direction of the conversation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Zero pressure or directives; Rosa is explicitly given permission to not be ready and is consistently positioned as the ultimate decision-maker. Autonomy language is woven naturally into every interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All identified motivations (breathing, cold, friendship) come from Rosa herself, with the psychiatrist never imposing reasons for change. Could be more systematic in targeting specific DARN-C categories like confidence and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions herself as a humble listener and curious partner, inviting Rosa's expertise with language like 'in your own words' and 'I'd love to know.' Rosa drives the content while the psychiatrist provides a reflective, supportive container.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Several genuine instances of change talk are elicited and acknowledged, including the breathlessness and friendship separation. However, the psychiatrist could more consistently circle back to deepen these moments and elicit confidence or ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is fully validated and normalized without arguing or minimizing, while simultaneously creating space for Rosa to find her own counter-arguments. The approach of acknowledging the full weight of difficulty without reinforcing hopelessness is textbook MI.
