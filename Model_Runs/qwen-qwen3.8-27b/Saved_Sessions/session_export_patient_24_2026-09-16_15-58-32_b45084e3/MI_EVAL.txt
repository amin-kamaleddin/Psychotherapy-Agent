# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses nonjudgmental, validating language and avoids correcting or challenging Daniel's self-view. Affirmations respect his competence and autonomy without pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Daniel's surprise, ambivalence, and the personal importance of his wind-down routine. The responses stay aligned with his athlete-focused perspective and avoid imposing interpretation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on alcohol use and its possible impact on training and recovery. It moves smoothly from broad ambivalence to a specific, testable plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Daniel as an equal partner and builds the plan around his own priorities, metrics, and training framework. The interaction feels like joint problem-solving rather than expert-led instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly frames the session as supportive rather than controlling and consistently uses conditional, client-directed language. The final decision, including whether to continue or stop the test, remains with Daniel.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Daniel's own ideas, values, and measures rather than supplying reasons to change. Daniel generates most of the change content, including the specific experiment and tracking thresholds.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Daniel as the expert on his own training and experience. Daniel's preferences and data sources clearly shape the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses forward-looking, evocative questions that lead Daniel to articulate concrete change ideas. Affirmations reinforce his generated change talk and confidence in the plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Daniel's sustain talk, such as denying being an alcoholic or resisting total abstinence. Instead, sustain talk is balanced and redirected into a neutral, client-controlled experiment.
