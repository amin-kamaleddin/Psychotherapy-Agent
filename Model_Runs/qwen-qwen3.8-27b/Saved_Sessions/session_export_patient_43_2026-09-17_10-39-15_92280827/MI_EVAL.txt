# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard without judgment or pressure. The psychiatrist directly counters Eric's expectation of being lectured while remaining nonjudgmental even when Eric sets boundaries.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate and insightful reflections of the function of smoking and Eric's need for quiet and autonomy. Some reflections are more cognitive than emotional, and the opening is slightly interpretive before full disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains relevant focus on smoking's role in managing work stress and guides toward a possible micro-behavior. The session would be stronger with a clearer, explicitly agreed-upon target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaves as an equal partner throughout, inviting Eric's input and co-constructing the problem. The therapist avoids lecturing, advising, or assuming the expert role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Eric's right to choose and avoids imposing advice or change. Repeatedly affirms Eric's authority over his own pace, goals, and what would be useful.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes Eric's own motivations, values, and concerns rather than providing external reasons to change. Could be enhanced by using additional tools such as rulers, values-based elicitation, or a deeper summary of change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong power sharing and treats Eric as the expert on his own experience. Eric's statements meaningfully shape the session, including the emerging idea of trying one smoke-free break.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits meaningful change talk, including Eric's willingness to consider not lighting up for one break. The therapist could more effectively amplify and consolidate Eric's own reasons and intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk respectfully without arguing, moralizing, or reinforcing resistance. Reframes sustain talk as legitimate constraints, though could use more strategic double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
