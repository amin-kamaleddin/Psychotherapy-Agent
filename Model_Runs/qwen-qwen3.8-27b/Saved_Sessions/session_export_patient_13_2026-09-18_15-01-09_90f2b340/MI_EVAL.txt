# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout. Zero labeling or disapproval; Anara's fear and avoidance are validated explicitly (e.g., 'That's a really human way to cope').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture the existential and emotional core rather than surface content. Particularly strong in distinguishing 'losing courage' from 'choosing to walk away on purpose' and reflecting the pain that choice would mean.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear arc from vague awareness to concrete steps (script, telling mom), with increasing specificity. Slightly weakened at the endpoint where the concern about mom pushing is addressed but not closed with a summary or consolidated next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as joint exploration; psychiatrist explicitly frames work collaboratively ('What if we built that script together right now'). Anara's contributions substantially shape direction, metaphors, and content throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Anara's ownership and right to her own timeline. No pressure, unsolicited advice, or directive language; the psychiatrist honors doubt rather than countering it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All reasons for change come from Anara's own mouth; the psychiatrist never supplies external motivation. Questions are designed to elicit Anara's own language, resources, and values (e.g., the phone script, the 'floating between us' insight).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently framed as residing within Anara; the psychiatrist invites her perspective rather than providing information. No moment of assumed authority or unsolicited clinical direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully highlights and amplifies Anara's own movement (e.g., 'from I don't know if I could to maybe just calling in one breath'). Could be strengthened by more systematically evoking ability and confidence statements across all DARN-CAT categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (fear, exhaustion, 'I might forget') is validated first and gently reframed into deeper values exploration. Slightly more use of double-sided reflections holding both fear and desire simultaneously would strengthen this further.
