# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates Elena’s concerns about her child’s safety. The stance is warm and safe, though not always deeply individualized or emotionally explicit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Elena’s uncertainty, values, and concern about vaccine reactions. They miss some deeper emotional layers, such as nighttime anxiety and parental pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the varicella/MMR decision and moves efficiently toward concrete next steps. There is no topic drift, and the plan is specific and action-oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician involves Elena in choosing the information format, vaccine preference, and printable details. Collaboration is strong, though the clinician still leads the medical content and comparisons.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician offers choices, avoids pressure, and respects Elena’s preference for separate MMR and varicella. Autonomy support would be stronger with more explicit emphasis on Elena’s final decision ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Useful early questions draw out Elena’s values and growing readiness. The session shifts quickly to factual information rather than continuing to elicit deeper personal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician frequently invites Elena’s input and tailors the plan to her needs. Partnership is clear, but Elena is not consistently framed as the central decision-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena expresses readiness and a plan to book, and the clinician affirms this progress. The clinician could more actively amplify and expand Elena’s own change talk rather than moving into information.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elena’s concerns are acknowledged without judgment and redirected toward balanced information. However, the clinician does not consistently use double-sided reflections to work through ambivalence.
