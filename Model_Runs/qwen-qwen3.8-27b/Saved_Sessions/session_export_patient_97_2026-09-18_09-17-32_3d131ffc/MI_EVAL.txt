### Empathy

**Comments:** Maintains a generally clear focus on the target behavior and discrepancy, with logical session progression. Could be tightened with a more explicit summary connecting values to specific behavior and a clearer closing or consolidation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and genuine two-way dialogue, sharing observations then inviting Mateo's interpretation. Could be strengthened by more explicit co-creation, such as asking how Mateo wants to think about the identified step going forward.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently strong throughout, opening with explicit autonomy language and never imposing plans or expectations. Frames all goals and discoveries as Mateo's own, and handles the mandated nature of sessions without trying to override it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Mateo rather than providing reasons for change. The entire session is structured around Mateo's own insights, with targeted questions that draw out his personal language and reframing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Mateo, using statements like 'I don't know what you make of that' and 'from where you're sitting right now.' Never assumes meaning or prescribes actions, treating Mateo as the authoritative expert on his own experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk by asking targeted questions that draw out Mateo's own reasons for change, such as exploring what 'actually working toward it' would look like. Could push further to elicit more spontaneous change talk from Mateo before consolidating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by reframing barriers as something other than willpower and acknowledging loss of freedom without reinforcing obligation. Could use more double-sided reflections to fully resolve Mateo's self-doubt and 'I can't do this' language.
