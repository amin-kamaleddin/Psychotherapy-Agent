# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates warmth, respect, and nonjudgment, even when the client expresses doubt or tiredness. This creates a safe space for ambivalence without moral judgment or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections generally capture the client’s experience and emotional tension well. However, a few statements move slightly beyond reflection into interpretation or assumption, such as assuming a history of failed routines.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity, especially walking, and moves toward a concrete next step. Direction could be stronger with a final summary that explicitly consolidates the target behavior and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor functions as an equal partner, building the plan around the client’s existing routine, ideas, and preferences. The client’s suggestions substantially shape the direction and content of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently respects the client’s choice and avoids controlling or pressuring commitments. The plan is framed as a gentle experiment, supporting ownership and self-directed adjustment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor effectively elicits the client’s own motivations, values, and problem-solving ideas rather than providing reasons for change. The resulting walking plan is largely client-generated and personalized.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor clearly conveys that the client is the expert on her own life and needs. Language such as “you know your days better than I do” reinforces a genuine collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently evokes and reinforces change talk, leading the client to generate a realistic plan. It could be strengthened by more explicitly amplifying confidence, importance, or values-based change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without argument, persuasion, or shame, and is often redirected into a manageable experiment. Some sustain talk is not fully transformed, and a double-sided reflection could have strengthened the response.
