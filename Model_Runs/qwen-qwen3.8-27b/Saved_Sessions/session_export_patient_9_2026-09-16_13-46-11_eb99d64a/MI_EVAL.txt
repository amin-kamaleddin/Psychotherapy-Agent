# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance with supportive language that reduces shame. Slightly solution-focused in places and some formulaic phrasing (repeated "I hear you").

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections accurately capturing ambivalence and multiple competing needs. Some reflections remain cognitive rather than fully exploring deeper underlying emotion and affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally maintained focus on target parenting and co-parenting communication behaviors with reasonable clinical direction. A few moments where focus broadened into financial stress without clearly tying back to a specific behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client's ideas clearly shaped the session and she was invited repeatedly to contribute her perspective. Could be stronger with more explicit co-creation of the agenda and invitations for the client to redirect.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized client's choice, control, and ownership with strong autonomy-supportive language. A few questions subtly pushed toward specific action formats.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocative questions that drew out the client's own motivations, values, and possible solutions. Some mildly suggestive questions included therapist-supplied answer options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative stance respecting the client as expert in her own life and following her ideas. Occasionally shaped direction by suggesting specific formats rather than explicitly sharing power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited several relevant change statements but did not consistently reflect, amplify, or strategically build on them. Missed opportunities to deepen statements like "Mom's got this" and "maybe it's a start."

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts to redirect toward autonomy but sustain talk around the father's unreliability remained prominent throughout. Ended without further softening or strategic pivot toward change regarding her final anxiety.
