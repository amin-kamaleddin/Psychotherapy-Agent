# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, affirming, and nonjudgmental, offering positive regard without challenging Amina's ambivalence. However, the warmth can feel somewhat formulaic or performance-based, with phrases like 'wise' and 'solid' that prevent a fully individualized expression of acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects Amina's stress, exhaustion, and conflicting concerns using strong reflective listening. The main limitation is that some reflections summarize and move quickly into planning rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning, such as her need for autonomy and emotional safety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear, meaningful target: creating a calmer bedtime routine and reducing co-parenting stress. The main limitation is occasional drift into detailed behavioral instruction and repeated offers of additional tools, which makes the direction feel somewhat directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites choices and responds to Amina's preferences, which shapes the final plan. However, the counselor often acts as the primary source of solutions, offering routines, scripts, reminders, and contingency plans before fully co-creating options with Amina.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports choice by asking Amina to select among options and honors her decisions to hold off on the co-parenting text and bedtime reminder. The limitation is that many concrete directives and suggested behaviors make the process feel more guided than fully self-directed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor makes some evocative efforts by asking about importance, confidence, and what would help Amina try a small change. However, much of the session is advice- and script-driven rather than focused on drawing out Amina's own values, motivations, hopes, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a collaborative tone and invites Amina's input, and her choices influence the direction of the session. The partnership is limited by an expert-driven approach, with the counselor frequently introducing detailed plans before Amina generates her own.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina offers meaningful change talk, including wanting her son to feel safe and describing the plan as less like a battle. The counselor does not consistently amplify or deepen this change talk and often moves too quickly into advice and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges Amina's concerns without challenging them and redirects toward what she can control. However, the counselor does not consistently use MI-consistent strategies to soften sustain talk, such as double-sided reflections or exploring the costs of the current pattern.
