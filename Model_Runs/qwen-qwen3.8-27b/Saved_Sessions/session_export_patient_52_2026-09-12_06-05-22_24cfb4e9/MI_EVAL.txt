# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgment without pressuring Irina’s choices. Acceptance is solid but somewhat procedural and formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Irina’s perspective, fatigue, and ambivalence about losing the calming effect of alcohol. Empathy remains more cognitive than deeply emotionally attuned.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays reasonably focused on alcohol use, coping, and a concrete target behavior: trying one drink on Friday. At times the focus drifts toward general stress management and problem-solving.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works collaboratively on practical options, such as drafting a message to the social worker. The style can feel slightly advisor-like because the psychiatrist offers many suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Irina is regularly invited to make choices, and her limits are respected without being overridden. The psychiatrist still provides many practical suggestions, which can reduce the sense of full client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some early questions invite Irina’s perspective, but the session quickly shifts into advice-giving and planning. There are missed opportunities to deepen her own change language, confidence, and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally maintains a non-authoritarian stance and invites Irina to guide the work. Stronger explicit power sharing would reduce the expert-driven quality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Irina articulate a small, concrete change step, such as trying one drink on Friday. However, there is limited exploration of her values, confidence, reasons for change, and deeper motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing Irina’s sustain talk and acknowledges the function of drinking. More strategic double-sided reflections or gentle exploration of cost-benefit would improve this area.
