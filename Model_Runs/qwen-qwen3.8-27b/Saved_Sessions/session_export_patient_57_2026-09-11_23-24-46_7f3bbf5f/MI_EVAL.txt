# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and supported Jamal without shame or lecturing. Acceptance was slightly reduced by occasional practical/compliance-focused framing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and captured Jamal’s practical concerns about consequences and reputation. They did not fully explore deeper feelings such as fear of exclusion, shame, or ambivalence about fitting in.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target behavior, alcohol use during games and hangouts, and concrete next steps. It was less focused on helping Jamal explore personal values or deeper reasons for change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal contributed preferences and details, but the psychiatrist supplied much of the solution content, including scripts and tactics. The interaction was more guided than truly collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered choices and avoided overt pressure, respecting Jamal’s need for a brief, practical plan. However, frequent directive recommendations reduced emphasis on Jamal’s full ownership and freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were some MI-style questions, but the session moved quickly toward advice and planning. The psychiatrist missed opportunities to draw out Jamal’s own motivations, values, and self-reasoning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some input was invited, but the session remained expert-led with structured options and scripts. The psychiatrist was positioned more as the problem-solver than as an equal partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal expressed some change talk, such as wanting to stay on the team and avoid being benched, but it was not consistently reflected or amplified. The session shifted quickly toward tactics rather than strengthening motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal’s sustain talk about group pressure, awkwardness, and wanting to keep it easy was acknowledged but not effectively redirected. The session largely accepted the status-quo framing rather than exploring deeper values-based change.
