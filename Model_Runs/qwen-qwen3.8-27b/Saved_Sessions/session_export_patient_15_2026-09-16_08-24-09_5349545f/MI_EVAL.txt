# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician maintains a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone throughout the session. Some praise feels contingent and the patient's minimizing stance is not fully explored.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the patient's stated concerns and practical barriers, but remain mostly factual. There is limited exploration of the underlying emotional content, such as worry, fatigue, or loss of control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stays focused on blood pressure and a walking goal, but it fails to address the core alcohol reduction target. This makes the overall direction incomplete for the stated case.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician co-constructs a realistic plan that incorporates the patient's ideas. Collaboration is strong, though the clinician remains the primary agenda-driver and problem-solver.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The patient is given meaningful choices and determines many specifics of the plan. Autonomy support is strong but limited by the clinician's frequent offering of concrete options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling and barrier questions help elicit some motivation and self-generated ideas. The evocation remains relatively superficial and does not deeply explore values, meaning, or alcohol-related change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient's contributions shape the final plan, but the clinician still drives much of the agenda. The partnership is present but not fully equal or client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited through scaling questions and goal-setting. The clinician often shifts into suggestion mode, which limits amplification of the patient's own autonomous change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient's barriers are acknowledged without direct challenge, but the clinician does not consistently use MI strategies to soften them. Likely sustain talk around alcohol is not addressed because alcohol is never explored.
