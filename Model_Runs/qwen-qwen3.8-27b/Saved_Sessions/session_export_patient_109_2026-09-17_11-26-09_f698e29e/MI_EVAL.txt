# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental stance throughout, with genuine validation that avoids labeling or disapproval. Could be slightly more explicit in affirming Rafael's worth beyond his exercise performance during moments of significant self-doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with reflections that capture Rafael's meta-level conflicts and progressively deepen from surface to identity-level understanding. Reflections like 'you're not even fighting the exercise, you're fighting the rule in your head' demonstrate sophisticated emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus on the target behavior with a clear logical arc from identifying past success to exploring current barriers to examining deeper identity issues. Never drifts into unrelated topics while still following the client's lead.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as a joint exploration with language like 'I'm curious' and 'I'm wondering' positioning the psychiatrist as a fellow explorer. Rafael's contributions genuinely shape the session direction from the social element to the identity piece to the ceiling fear.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Rafael's autonomy with phrases like 'there's no pressure' and 'you don't have to have the answer.' Never tells Rafael what to do or implies any particular outcome is expected, always returning decisions to him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful progressive evocation that elicits Rafael's own motivations, insights, and self-awareness without providing external reasons. Each evoked insight builds on the previous one, moving from past success to identity to the 'ceiling' fear.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance consistently positioning Rafael as the expert on his own life with language like 'what do you think?' and 'I'm curious.' Some complex multi-layered questions, while collaborative in intent, could be perceived as slightly directive in their framing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through open, curiosity-driven questions that lead Rafael to articulate his own motivations and reasons for change. The session could benefit from a clearer consolidation of the change talk that emerged before ending.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by exploring the function of Rafael's standards rather than arguing or dismissing, using double-sided reflections effectively. The 'ceiling' fear is explored well but not fully resolved, leaving Rafael somewhat stuck in ambivalence at the close.
