# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, respecting Samir’s ambivalence, humor, and brief replies without blame. Affirmations preserve his dignity even when he minimizes his own concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers strong perspective-taking reflections about protection, autonomy, and feeling controlled. It is not a perfect 5 because a few reflections are somewhat interpretive and do not fully explore the underlying frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and steady focus on medication adherence and treatment acceptance. It gradually narrows from general resistance to a specific, manageable change around morning routine and coffee.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is framed as a joint exploration, with the psychiatrist following Samir’s lead and using his own metaphors. Options are built from Samir’s stated concerns rather than imposed from the outside.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a major strength, with repeated emphasis on choice, self-direction, and ownership. There is no directive advice, coercion, or pressure to take the medication.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently invites Samir to generate his own reasons and ideas rather than lecturing. The evocation is somewhat repetitive and does not fully deepen into values, confidence, ability, or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly conveys that Samir is the expert on his own life. Key insights are returned to Samir as his own, reinforcing a collaborative rather than expert-driven stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses effective evocative questions that elicit small pieces of change talk from Samir. The change talk remains somewhat shallow and is not fully developed into stronger confidence or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes and reframes Samir’s sustain talk without arguing or amplifying it. It could be stronger by more consistently balancing autonomy concerns with health-related benefits.
