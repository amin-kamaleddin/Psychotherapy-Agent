# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment while affirming Daniela's efforts and burden. It falls just short of a 5 because some affirming statements introduce layered interpretations that slightly substitute the clinician's meaning for the client's own experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both Daniela's practical strain and emotional meaning. Empathy is not perfect because some reflections are slightly polished or interpretive, and the final statement about cooking feeling impossible is not fully reflected or validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, coherent focus on securing reliable childcare support while balancing work, school, and parenting. The conversation progresses logically from identifying the need for support to a concrete, actionable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is highly collaborative, with Daniela's ideas shaping the direction and content of the session. The psychiatrist helps refine and clarify Daniela's plan rather than acting as the sole expert or problem-solver.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Daniela's perception of choice, ownership, and dignity without pressure, coercion, or direct persuasion. Statements such as following her lead and emphasizing a plan rather than a rescue strongly reinforce her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits motivation from within Daniela rather than supplying reasons for change. Daniela's values, hopes, and concrete plan emerge primarily from her own statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client and does not impose a plan or direct advice. Daniela is positioned as the decision-maker throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, client-centered questions successfully evoke Daniela's motivations, values, and plans for change. The score is not a 5 because the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, summarize, or deepen the change talk once it emerges, especially around smoking and drinking reduction.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids reinforcing barriers and sometimes reframes ambivalence in a functional way. However, the softening is inconsistent and does not fully address the emotional weight of Daniela's concerns, particularly the final barrier of exhaustion and the difficulty of cooking.
