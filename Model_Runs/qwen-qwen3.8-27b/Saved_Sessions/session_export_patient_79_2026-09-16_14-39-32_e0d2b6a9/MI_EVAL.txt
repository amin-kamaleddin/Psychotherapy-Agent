# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful stance and avoids blame or moralizing. Acceptance remains somewhat procedural and does not include stronger explicit unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Luis’s preferences for autonomy, simplicity, and control. Deeper emotional content such as shame, judgment, or anxiety is not fully reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stays consistently focused on asthma management and the target behaviors. It moves logically toward a concrete, workable plan without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Luis to co-create the plan rather than impose a solution. The interaction feels genuinely collaborative and shared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Luis’s choice and boundaries are consistently respected and reinforced. The plan is framed as something he chooses rather than something assigned.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks client-centered questions that draw out Luis’s own preferences and ideas. Evocation focuses more on practical logistics than on deeper reasons for change, confidence, or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Luis as the expert on his own life and choices. The session is strongly shaped by Luis’s contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Luis offers some change talk, such as willingness to try a simpler setup. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, summarize, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and minimization. There is less skilled redirection or exploration of the ambivalence behind resistant statements.
