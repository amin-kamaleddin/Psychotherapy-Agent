# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, creating a non-judgmental space and validating Kareem's statements without challenge, correction, or conditional respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are specific and nuanced, capturing both surface content and emotional undercurrents (e.g., 'the anchor for the whole day's rhythm'). The psychiatrist accurately picks up on subtle language shifts and reflects them with precision without over-interpreting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on the target behavior within a coherent arc moving from initial resistance to a concrete, client-generated plan. Each step logically follows the previous one without wandering into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions themselves as a fellow explorer, actively seeking Kareem's input and allowing his contributions to shape session direction. The power dynamic is genuinely equal, with the final plan co-constructed through client-generated content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a standout feature—the psychiatrist never directs, pressures, or imposes, explicitly validating Kareem's need for it to be his own choice. The final plan is entirely client-generated in timeframe, behavior, and accountability mechanism.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits motivation from within Kareem without providing external reasons, using open questions that let him fill in his own values and vision. All change talk is generated by Kareem himself rather than prompted by the psychiatrist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never provides information, advice, or expertise about cessation methods, instead positioning all questions to draw out Kareem's own understanding. The session direction is substantially shaped by Kareem's contributions rather than a predetermined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strategic open questions and reflections naturally elicit high-frequency, high-quality change talk ('I could manage,' 'two days feels doable') without ever feeling pushy. The psychiatrist consistently builds on each instance of change talk rather than letting it pass.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively handles sustain talk without argument or correction, acknowledging and holding it alongside change talk. However, a couple of moments (e.g., 'I don't want to make it sound like I'm ready') could have been more actively reframed or strategically pivoted.
