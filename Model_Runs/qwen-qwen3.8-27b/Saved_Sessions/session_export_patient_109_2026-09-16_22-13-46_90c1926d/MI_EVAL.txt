# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance while treating Rafael’s ambivalence as understandable. The tone is solid but remains somewhat professional/procedural rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is good reflective listening, especially around Rafael’s logistical constraints and ambivalence. Empathy could deepen by capturing the emotional undercurrent of shame, self-judgment, or feeling inadequate.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on making a workable exercise routine happen, with repeated steering toward small, realistic versions. It would benefit from consolidating progress and moving toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and builds on Rafael’s own ideas rather than imposing recommendations. Collaboration is competent but could be more fully client-led in agenda-setting and framing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure, advice, and direct commands, preserving Rafael’s sense of choice. A few framings are subtly directing or challenge a core client rule before it has been fully explored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open-ended questions are used, but they tend to be more logistical and problem-solving than motivational. More evocative questions about values, meaning, and confidence would strengthen Rafael’s own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Rafael to be the source of the plan and avoids an expert-prescribing stance. The questions are somewhat repetitive and still fairly clinician-directed in sequence and framing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rafael produces several useful change statements, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or strategically build on them. Greater use of scaling, affirmations, and summaries of change talk would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing or overriding Rafael’s reasons for staying the same. More double-sided reflections could better validate the sustain side while turning the conversation toward change.
