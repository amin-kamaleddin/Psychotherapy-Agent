# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, non-judgmental, and validating throughout, never expressing disapproval of the client's biomedical framing. Slightly performative phrasing in places prevents a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally accurate and nuanced reflections that capture both content and emotional depth, including connecting fear to relational context and somatic expression of isolation. Goes well beyond surface-level paraphrasing to address underlying meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical session arc from validation through exploration to a concrete plan. Could have pushed more strategically toward a specific first commitment step by the end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal power balance with the client's ideas substantially shaping session direction and action steps. Language is consistently collaborative with no imposed agenda or framework.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly and consistently supports the client's right to hold her own views and make her own decisions, with zero evidence of pressure or directive language. Particularly strong in normalizing her needs and respecting her boundary about not being 'just lonely.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation emerges from the client herself — the psychiatrist never provides reasons for change, gives advice, or states medical information. The client generates her own insight, plan, and detailed tracking system in response to open, evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert on her own experience, acting as a facilitator who helps her access her own insights. The client's contributions substantially shape the session direction and outcomes.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits substantial change talk through open, evocative questions, with the client generating a detailed self-designed tracking plan. Could have more systematically amplified and summarized change talk to build momentum toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk without dismissal or power struggles, using double-sided responses and explicit non-argument. Could have more explicitly used double-sided reflections to hold both sides of ambivalence simultaneously.
