# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor remains respectful and nonjudgmental, affirming Kareem’s efforts without moralizing. The stance is supportive but somewhat procedural rather than deeply personally attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Kareem’s frustration and the hassle of smoking breaks. Empathy is good, though it misses opportunities to explore ambivalence and internal conflict more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking reduction in the workplace context. The focus is clear, but the momentum becomes more behavior-management than MI process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses Kareem’s schedule and incorporates some of his ideas. However, much of the planning is counselor-generated, reducing the sense of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and respects Kareem’s decision not to quit. Repeated structured plans and checklists still make the interaction feel controlling.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some evocative questions, especially at the start and during the check-in. The counselor misses opportunities to draw out Kareem’s values, reasons, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kareem’s preferences are used, and he is given options. The counselor remains in an expert role, which makes Kareem feel managed rather than co-creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, such as Kareem’s willingness to skip the 4 p.m. cigarette. The counselor does not consistently amplify or expand Kareem’s own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with Kareem’s sustain talk and rolls with resistance. There is limited active reframing or redirection toward Kareem’s values and change goals.
