# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids arguing with Kieran's resistant language. Repeated summarizing after Kieran says he is done may subtly override his stated boundary.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflects Kieran's frustration with the routine and low energy, but remains surface-level and content-focused. Misses deeper cues around pressure, powerlessness, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on a clear, concrete behavior: calling earlier using a phone alarm tomorrow at 10. Direction is realistic but overly logistical rather than motivational.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and invites input, but the interaction is largely counselor-led. Kieran remains passive while the psychiatrist organizes options and repeatedly summarizes the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices, respects Kieran's refusal of the text reminder, and supports his preference to handle the alarm himself. Slightly reduced by the early change agenda and repeated restatement of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Asks a few open questions, but quickly shifts to problem-solving logistics. Does not deeply draw out Kieran's internal reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Kieran's input and respects his chosen approach, supporting a collaborative stance. However, the agenda is set quickly and the exchange feels less like equal partnership due to structured menus and repeated summaries.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk about less waiting and calling earlier, but does not consistently develop, reflect, or amplify it. The session shifts to logistics before Kieran's own motivation is explored.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does not reinforce Kieran's minimization or disengagement, which is a strength. However, it lacks active softening, double-sided reflections, or autonomy-based reframing of sustain talk.
