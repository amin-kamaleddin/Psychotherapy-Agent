# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently shows nonjudgment and positive regard while normalizing Amina’s distress. Acceptance could be strengthened with more explicit, individualized affirmation of her worth as a mother.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate, nuanced, and emotionally attuned, capturing both content and ambivalence. The counselor consistently tracks Amina’s perspective and underlying needs.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on reducing chaos in co-parenting handoffs and decreasing Amina’s checking behavior. Each question builds steadily toward a specific, workable behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor takes a collaborative stance and invites Amina to co-construct possible next steps. It would be stronger with more explicit “what do you think?”-style invitations after options are introduced.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports choice with open, nondirective language and avoids pressuring Amina to stop checking or confront her son’s father. It would be stronger with more explicit affirmation of Amina’s right to choose her own pace and terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently evokes Amina’s values, hopes, and self-awareness through targeted questions. It could be deeper by relying more on client-generated ideas and confidence/ability language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina is positioned as the expert on her family, and her contributions substantially shape the session. Partnership could be stronger with more explicit acknowledgment of her expertise and less subtle leading when options are offered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor elicits meaningful change talk about wanting less chaos, trust, and a calmer co-parenting arrangement. More deliberate summarizing, values-based affirmation, or confidence questions would make this stronger.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met without argument and is reframed as protective concern, then redirected toward small testable actions. It could be stronger with more frequent pivots toward change, confidence, or Amina’s strengths.
