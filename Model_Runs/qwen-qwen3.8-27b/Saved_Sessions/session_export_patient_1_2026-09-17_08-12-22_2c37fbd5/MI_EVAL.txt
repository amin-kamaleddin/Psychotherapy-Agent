# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and accepts Ahmed's concerns without criticism. Warmth is somewhat procedural, limiting a top rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Ahmed's practical concerns and mixed feelings. Empathy remains mostly cognitive and does not fully explore deeper emotional undercurrents.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on mini-cigar use and a small, concrete test. The ending lacks a clear collaborative summary and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Ahmed's perspective and uses client-generated markers for the plan. Collaboration is strong but not fully co-constructed or explicitly negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently reinforced, with emphasis on Ahmed's freedom to choose and ownership of the process. This is one of the strongest MI behaviors in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions help Ahmed articulate his own preferences and possible next steps. Evocation is practical but does not fully deepen change talk, confidence, or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist largely avoids an expert-driven stance and invites Ahmed to contribute. Agenda and the main test are still clinician-guided rather than fully shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, such as wanting mouth comfort and considering a weekend skip. Change talk is not consistently amplified, summarized, or expanded into stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and acknowledges Ahmed's doubts. Sustain talk remains a dominant theme and is not consistently softened or redirected toward change.
