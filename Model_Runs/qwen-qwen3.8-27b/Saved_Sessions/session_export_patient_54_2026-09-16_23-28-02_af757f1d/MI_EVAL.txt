# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, clearly honoring the client’s perspective. Warmth is somewhat clinical and task-focused rather than consistently affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately track the client’s ambivalence about tradition, autonomy, and protecting the pregnancy. Empathy could deepen by exploring associated fear, guilt, or relief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and moves toward a concrete next step. There is no meaningful drift from the clinical concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the client as an equal partner in designing the plan. The client’s preferences and wording materially shape the session’s outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported by framing the decision as the client’s own. The client is invited to choose the format, wording, and timing rather than being directed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits the client’s own next steps rather than giving advice. Evocation could more deliberately draw out deeper motivations, values, and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power is shared through questions that invite the client to guide the session. The client’s ideas directly shape the intervention’s direction and content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client generates practical change language and a shared next step. The psychiatrist could strengthen this by explicitly reflecting and amplifying the client’s desire, ability, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about tradition and autonomy is acknowledged without being amplified or challenged. It is linked to protecting the pregnancy, though more explicit redirecting strategies could be used.
