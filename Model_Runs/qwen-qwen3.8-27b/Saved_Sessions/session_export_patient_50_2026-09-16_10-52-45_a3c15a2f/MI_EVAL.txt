# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment throughout the session. They validate Hana's concerns, choices, and need to avoid shaming language without blame or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows strong reflective listening and perspective-taking by accurately tracking Hana's anxiety and somatic experience. Some reflections remain more cognitive or plan-focused rather than fully penetrating the emotional vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on Hana's exam anxiety and a specific, manageable behavioral experiment. There is no major topic drift, and the conversation remains tied to the chosen change-oriented goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses joint language and invites Hana to help shape the routine, timing, and follow-up. However, the counselor still carries much of the agenda by initially proposing a fairly detailed intervention and restating it in a structured way.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Hana's sense of choice by repeatedly asking for her preferences, limits, and decisions. There is no coercion, and client-defined boundaries such as stopping if anxiety increases are explicitly honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some of Hana's past successes and preferences but often relies on providing structure and affirmations. There are missed opportunities to deeply elicit her reasons, values, confidence, or meaning for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys partnership by inviting Hana's input and co-creating aspects of the plan. Still, the counselor sometimes takes a noticeable lead in constructing the intervention, making the process less fully client-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked, particularly around ability and action, and is affirmed by the psychiatrist. However, the session frequently moves into practical planning without consistently deepening or amplifying Hana's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by validating Hana's doubts without arguing or dismissing them. The responses could be stronger with more explicit double-sided reflections or active redirection toward change.
