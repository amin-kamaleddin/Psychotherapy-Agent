# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming stance throughout. However, affirmations are somewhat formulaic and do not fully normalize Matteo's ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures Matteo's ambivalence well. Empathy is mostly cognitive rather than deeply attuned to the emotional and social dimensions of his drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear, logical progression toward a concrete plan. It is slightly prescriptive in steering Matteo toward specific actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and seeks Matteo's input before moving forward. Collaboration is somewhat one-sided, as most options and strategies are counselor-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is supported through language emphasizing choice, experimentation, and no failure. The frequency of specific suggestions and commitment prompts slightly undermines client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are genuine evocative attempts, but the session quickly shifts to problem-solving. Change talk remains mostly tied to external consequences rather than internal values or identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and avoids imposing an agenda. Partnership is somewhat uneven, as Matteo largely selects from counselor-provided options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited but not deeply developed. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to explore Matteo's personal values, goals, and identity-related reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing, debating, or reinforcing it. The psychiatrist does not fully work with sustain talk through deeper exploration or strategic redirection.
