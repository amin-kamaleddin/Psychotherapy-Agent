### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with no instances of judgment, labeling, or disapproval. Validates Ahmed's reasoning at every turn, even when he is rationalizing or minimizing, treating his perspective as understandable and logical.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflections that go beyond paraphrasing to genuine perspective-taking, capturing Ahmed's perceived unfairness, logical reasoning, and emotional experience. Consistently accesses Ahmed's internal frame of reference rather than imposing an external one.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the target behavior and follows a logical progression from the dental finding through exploring the cigar connection. Could be slightly stronger in progressively narrowing the focus, as the session stays somewhat broad in exploring dust vs. cigars as potential causes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as a joint exploration, actively seeking Ahmed's input before offering any information or perspective. The session is co-constructed, with the psychiatrist following Ahmed's lead and acknowledging the limitations of their own perspective.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces Ahmed's ownership of the change process and his freedom to choose throughout, explicitly stating 'I'm not here to take that from you' and 'I'm not here to say it definitely is.' Never pressures, persuades, or implies that Ahmed 'should' quit, structuring the entire session around his own reasoning and judgment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out Ahmed's own reasons for potential change through evocative questions and by highlighting discrepancies in his own reasoning, such as connecting his desire to 'see something specific' to the dental findings already present. Could be more sustained in eliciting and developing specific change talk, as the session ends with Ahmed still not fully convinced.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Ahmed, positioning him as the expert on his own body and choices. Functions as a curious guide rather than an authority, asking questions that help Ahmed explore his own reasoning without imposing conclusions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk at multiple points, with Ahmed expressing emerging curiosity such as 'it makes me think about it' and 'I'd want to know what actually is.' The psychiatrist could have gone further in amplifying and reinforcing Ahmed's emerging curiosity or eliciting more specific change talk about what 'thinking about it differently' might look like.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with notable skill by consistently acknowledging Ahmed's statements while gently widening the frame, never contradicting or arguing. Could have more actively used double-sided reflections or more strategic redirection given how persistent the sustain talk was throughout the session.
