# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes slips, which reduces defensiveness. However, the warmth feels somewhat procedural, and some emotional difficulty remains under-explored.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows reasonable understanding of Anika’s perspective and the awkwardness of role-play. Empathy is limited by reflections that remain more cognitive than emotionally attuned to ambivalence and doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and builds toward a clear, concrete goal. Direction is somewhat premature because planning begins before fully exploring ambivalence and underlying concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is collaboration in co-constructing parts of the plan and role-play. The therapist, however, often leads with pre-formed strategies, making the partnership uneven rather than fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by offering choices and accepting Anika’s decision to pause rather than quit. Autonomy support is weakened by frequent directives and a more therapist-led planning approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk and acknowledges Anika’s confidence level. Evocation is limited because the session relies more on advice and planning than on drawing out Anika’s own motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and shares decision-making in several areas. Partnership is inconsistent because the therapist often assumes the expert or planner role before fully exploring Anika’s own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist recognizes and affirms useful change talk related to basketball, confidence, and commitment. It is not maximized because there are missed opportunities to deepen values, confidence, and intention.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids reinforcing or arguing with sustain talk and keeps the conversation moving. Responses are sometimes advisory or corrective rather than reflective, limiting MI-consistent softening of ambivalence.
