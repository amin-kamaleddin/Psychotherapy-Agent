# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout, with explicit non-judgment and active countering of self-blame. Normalization is woven naturally rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures meaning, emotion, and underlying values beyond surface-level paraphrasing. Accurately tracks emotional shifts and names cognitive distortions precisely.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors with logical session progression. Could benefit from more explicit summaries or signposting statements to maintain directional momentum, particularly before the transition to smoking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently joint language with the client as primary contributor to content, decisions, and direction. The psychiatrist acts as a facilitator rather than a director, with a clearly balanced power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces client's ownership and right to choose, with all suggestions framed as questions back to the client. Autonomy support is natural and woven into every interaction rather than feeling inserted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently and skillfully draws out the client's own motivations and solutions with almost no unsolicited advice. Could be more systematic in eliciting DARN-CAT language, particularly confidence and ability statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, never assuming expertise or offering unsolicited advice. Session direction is substantially shaped by the client's own contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently and skillfully evokes change talk with strategic questions and amplification of client-generated solutions. Could be more systematic in drawing out DARN-CAT language, particularly ability and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively handles sustain talk by validating concerns while redirecting toward solution and gently challenging self-critical framing. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections and more consistent strategic pivoting.
