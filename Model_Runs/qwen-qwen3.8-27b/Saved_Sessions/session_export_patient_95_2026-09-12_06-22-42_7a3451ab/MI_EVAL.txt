# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Psychiatrist is warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, validating Maria's exhaustion and request to not be fixed. Minor limitation: repeated early safety questions can feel more clinical than relational.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers accurate reflections of exhaustion, fear, ambivalence, and the need to be seen. Could go deeper in reflecting shame/fear of burdening her sister and the final somatic sensations of sinking and chest tightness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, clinically appropriate focus on immediate safety, risk, and emotional stabilization. Could add more strategic movement toward a small, concrete change-oriented or coping target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Maria's preferences and co-constructs the moment-to-moment plan, including sitting quietly. The safety role limits full equality, but the interaction remains collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently offers choices and respects Maria's request to not be pressured into skills or lists. Autonomy support is bounded by necessary safety boundaries and duty-to-protect statements.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses evocative questions to invite Maria's experience, but does not consistently draw out her own reasons for staying or capacity. Missed opportunities to deepen change talk and internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, client-voiced stance and adapts to Maria's need for quiet presence. The psychiatrist's necessary safety role slightly limits full partnership equality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maria offers important change talk, such as wanting to be seen and not leaving without telling someone. Psychiatrist acknowledges this but does not consistently expand, amplify, or deepen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not argue with or reinforce Maria's hopeless statements, which is appropriate given the clinical context. Could more actively use double-sided reflections or gentle reframing to balance sustain talk with change talk.
