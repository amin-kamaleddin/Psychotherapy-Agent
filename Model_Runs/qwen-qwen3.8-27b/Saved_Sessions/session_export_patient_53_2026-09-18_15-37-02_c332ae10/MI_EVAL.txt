# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and accepting presence with genuine unconditional positive regard. Never labels, judges, or minimizes; warmth is individualized and deep rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections are highly accurate, emotionally nuanced, and consistently deepen the patient's own language with powerful metaphors. Strongly tracks emotional undercurrents including exhaustion, fear of obligation, and need to be met.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session has a clear, logical trajectory that consistently connects back to the target behavior of avoidance. Minor drift toward the therapeutic relationship at the close, though earned by the patient's material.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Entire session structured as joint exploration between equals with psychiatrist explicitly sharing vulnerability and limitations. Patient's contributions substantially shape every turn.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy consistently reinforced throughout with multiple explicit affirmations of the patient's right to self-determination. No pressure or imposed plans; closing honors her requested silence with clear boundaries.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-placed evocative questions that elicit meaningful self-reflection from the patient. Could have been more systematic, particularly after 'someone who gets it,' where a further evocative question might have been more developmentally apt.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise clearly positioned within the client throughout with questions like 'what would connection mean on your terms?' The session is genuinely co-constructed, with the closing inviting patient feedback on authenticity.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is present and reflected but not consistently amplified or strategically followed up with deeper evocative questions. Could have tracked 'that would be nice,' 'someone who gets it,' and 'I'd feel less weird' more thoroughly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Multiple sustain talk statements are validated but not actively softened with reframing or double-sided reflections tilting toward change. The 'haven't done anything' and 'exhausting' material could have been gently reframed toward potential.
