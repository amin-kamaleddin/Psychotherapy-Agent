# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warmth and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Rosa's ambivalence without pressure. Affirmations are well-placed but could be more deeply individualized to specific fears voiced.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy throughout, with reflections that capture specific imagery, emotional nuance, and underlying identity concerns beyond surface content. The depth progressively deepens, identifying patterns like the social identity issue behind the smoking behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, strategic flow from general ambivalence to specific workable barriers (finish line, social friction). A mid-session summary would have further reinforced momentum and direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of exploratory language and building on Rosa's own metaphors and examples rather than imposing external frameworks. Could be elevated with more explicit co-construction language (e.g., 'let's figure this out together').

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is woven naturally into every turn, with explicit statements that decisions are Rosa's alone and no performance is required. The psychiatrist never advises, recommends, or pushes, consistently inviting Rosa to find her own answers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of open-ended questions that pull information and insight from Rosa rather than providing it, including evoking her own resources and predictions. Could be strengthened by explicitly eliciting ability and confidence statements to build self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions herself as curious and exploring alongside Rosa, using invitational language and co-constructing the therapeutic space. Brief accuracy-checks after reframes (e.g., 'is that landing right?') would further distribute power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rosa produces meaningful change talk (daughter's wedding image, 'let me look that up'), skillfully amplified by the psychiatrist's reflections. More explicit elicitation of DARN-CAT language, particularly ability and confidence statements, would further strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled effectively through validation and reframing without debate, converting global fears into specific, workable problems. The weight gain concern could be further softened by exploring problem-solving within the change frame.
