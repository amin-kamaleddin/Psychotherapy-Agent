# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validated the client's caution and safety concerns without judgment or pressure. Acceptance was strong but could be more explicitly attuned to the emotional weight of her uncertainty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captured the client's priorities and reasoning about medication safety. The empathy could be deeper by more fully exploring her stress, fear, and sense of responsibility.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained tightly focused on obtaining a clear, prescriber-confirmed plan for citalopram and phenelzine. The conversation progressed logically from uncertainty to a concrete next step with little drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and client worked as partners to shape a practical communication plan. The client's preferences directly determined the final approach, including a phone call and written plan sent to the pharmacy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supported the client's choice to seek clarification without imposing a treatment decision. She was helped to define what would feel safe, clear, and trustworthy to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively drew out the client's concerns, preferences, and planned steps. The psychiatrist could further evoke deeper values, confidence, and intrinsic motivation for action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveyed a collaborative stance and invited the client's perspective throughout the session. The client's answers clearly shaped the discussion and final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently reinforced the client's own statements in favor of a safe, informed next step. It would be more skillful to more explicitly amplify confidence, ability, and emerging commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client's hesitations about citalopram and phenelzine were validated and redirected toward practical safety steps. The psychiatrist could use more explicit double-sided reflections to pivot these concerns toward change.
