# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, validates Erik’s concerns, and avoids judgment. Warmth remains somewhat clinical, and some affirmations reinforce the status quo rather than exploring ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes reasonable factual reflections of Erik’s situation and concerns. Empathy is often surface-level and does not fully capture deeper emotions such as fear of being “bad” or anxiety about losing control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays connected to diabetes, diet, and alcohol, but repeatedly drifts toward medication reassurance and scripting. A clearer focus on Erik’s own target behavior would improve direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works well with Erik’s preferences and co-creates a short, direct question for his doctor. Collaboration could be stronger by drawing out Erik’s own ideas before offering content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Erik’s choices, avoids direct orders, and respects his need for brevity. Some counselor-provided structure slightly reduces the sense that Erik is fully leading the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist has a few evocative moments, such as asking what Erik wants to work on. Overall, the session relies more on information and scripts than eliciting Erik’s internal motivation, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts to Erik’s preferences, showing partial partnership. The expert-driven style with many options and scripts limits a fully collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerges, especially around tracking and a small food swap. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Erik repeatedly expresses reasons to maintain the status quo, and the psychiatrist often validates them directly. The responses do not effectively redirect sustain talk toward Erik’s own values or internal reasons for change.
