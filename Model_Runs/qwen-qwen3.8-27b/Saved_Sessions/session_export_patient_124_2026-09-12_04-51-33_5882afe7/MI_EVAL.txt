# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and affirms Sofia’s honesty and effort. Acceptance is strong, though it could deepen by explicitly normalizing her mixed feelings, anxiety, and fear of failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Sofia’s content around anxiety, money stress, and family expectations. Empathy is mainly cognitive and problem-focused, with limited exploration of deeper emotions and concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear target behavior and moves effectively toward a concrete after-school plan. Some drift into logistics reduces consistent connection to broader values and change goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia’s ideas strongly shape the session, including waiting, stepping outside, using music, and contacting a friend. The therapist’s frequent structured options make collaboration effective but not fully equal throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking what Sofia is willing to try and emphasizing that she is doing it for herself. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming that not trying is also acceptable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist elicits useful practical ideas and invites Sofia to evaluate her confidence. Evocation is limited because it focuses on logistics rather than deeper personal reasons, values, and motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input, affirming Sofia’s competence and allowing her to select doable steps. The structured menu-like approach keeps the alliance collaborative but less fully co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia expresses several change-oriented ideas, and the therapist affirms and supports them. Cultivation remains somewhat shallow, focusing more on action steps than deeper desire, reasons, or values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument, such as concerns that marijuana helps anxiety or that the plan may not work. The therapist could more consistently use double-sided reflections and avoid moving too quickly into problem-solving.
