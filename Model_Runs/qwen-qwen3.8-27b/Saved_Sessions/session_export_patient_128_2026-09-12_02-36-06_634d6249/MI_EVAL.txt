# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses nonjudgmental language and generally avoids judgment. However, acceptance feels somewhat formulaic and not consistently deep.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections, especially early in the session. Empathy becomes less consistent as the conversation shifts toward advice and problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on alcohol-related risk and practical steps. The main limitation is moving somewhat quickly into action planning without deeper exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites client choices and builds around selected options. However, the interaction is still fairly therapist-directed through lists of strategies and rapid questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choice is supported through options, rulers, and acceptance of simpler plans. Autonomy support is weakened by quick movement into planning before motivation is fully developed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Readiness and confidence rulers elicit some client change talk. Evocation remains inconsistent because the psychiatrist relies more on therapist-generated options than open-ended exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist allows Tyler to shape some parts of the session. Partnership is uneven because the psychiatrist introduces structured scripts and directs the pacing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Tyler produces some change talk, especially around practical steps and not wanting another incident. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect back or deepen this language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and uses some double-sided empathy. Sustain talk is not consistently softened in a skillful MI way before moving to scripts and plans.
