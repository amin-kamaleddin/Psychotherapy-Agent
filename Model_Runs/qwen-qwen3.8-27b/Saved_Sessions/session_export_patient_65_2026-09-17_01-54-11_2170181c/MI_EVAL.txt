# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, nonjudgment, and validation without labeling Julian. Acceptance is strong but could be warmer with more explicit affirmation of Julian's effort and courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Julian's fear of judgment, freezing, and worry about appearing uncommitted. Empathy could deepen by naming emotions more explicitly, such as shame or fear of rejection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior and moves logically from confusion to a concrete first step. There is no meaningful drift from the court-mandated community service goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Julian to compare options, generate wording, and help shape the plan. Collaboration is strong but still somewhat guided by the psychiatrist's structured questioning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Julian's choice and independence are consistently supported without pressure or directive language. Action is framed as Julian's own decision, which is one of the session's strongest features.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful practical ideas about next steps and wording. However, evocation remains logistical and does not deeply access Julian's values, confidence, or intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly positions Julian as the expert on what feels doable and invites his input. The stance is collaborative, though the counselor still leads the session more than fully co-directs it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Julian expresses some change-oriented statements, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen them. More linking to values, confidence, or commitment would strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing and is reframed in a protective, nonjudgmental way. However, the counselor does not consistently pivot Julian's concerns toward capability or change language.
