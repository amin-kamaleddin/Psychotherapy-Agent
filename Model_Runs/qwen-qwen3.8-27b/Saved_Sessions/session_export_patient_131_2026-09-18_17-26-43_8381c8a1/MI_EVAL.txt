# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and culturally attuned unconditional positive regard throughout, using Māori language and affirming Wiremu's identity without judgment. Never labels, minimizes, or dismisses his shame about needing help at 34.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate understanding of Wiremu's emotional and relational world, including naming 'the look' as the real obstacle and identifying the story beneath surface concerns. Complex reflections used skillfully to deepen understanding rather than simply paraphrase.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with logical progression from goal to concrete plan (calling Aunty Thursday). However, the session ends with Wiremu's question about how Stopping Violence fits with the whānau plan going unanswered, preventing a perfect score.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as genuine collaboration with Wiremu setting the agenda and the psychiatrist building on his metaphors and language. The power dynamic is consistently equal, with Wiremu's ideas and language shaping the session direction at every turn.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports Wiremu's autonomy throughout, respecting his pace from 'maybe' to 'this week' and celebrating the shift without imposing it. The autonomy support feels embedded in every interaction rather than performed as a technique.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional evocation through strategic question progression that draws out Wiremu's own motivations, plan, and script. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change; instead creates conditions where Wiremu generates his own commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Wiremu through curiosity-based language and the strategic 'what would you say to a mate?' question. The session is clearly shaped by Wiremu's contributions — his language, metaphors, and priorities drive the direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits change talk through strategic questioning progression from desire to ability to commitment. Wiremu's change talk escalates naturally from 'I want them to not be scared' to 'I'll do it this week,' amplified and reinforced at key moments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles ambivalence skillfully by acknowledging both sides without arguing, then exploring the emotional experience rather than the content. Uses non-confrontational reflections like 'maybe that freedom wasn't really the freedom you thought it was' to invite self-examination.
