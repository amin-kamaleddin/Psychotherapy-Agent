# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard throughout the session, affirming Margaret's self-knowledge and treating sustain talk with full respect. No judgment, labeling, or disapproval at any point.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, complex reflective listening with emotionally attuned reflections that go beyond paraphrasing. Score limited by two instances of the generic prompt 'I understand. Can you tell me more about that?' which represent a drop in empathic precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a general focus on alcohol reduction and progresses toward a concrete goal, but lacks a clear session arc or structure. The session ends without a closing summary, explicit next steps, or follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as joint with language like 'I'd like us to work together' and allows Margaret's ideas to shape the session. Could be deepened by more explicitly co-creating a plan rather than occasionally leading with the psychiatrist's own prompts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports Margaret's autonomy, never imposing advice and framing all suggestions as possibilities rather than prescriptions. Fully respects her boundaries and acknowledges her right to make her own choices throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes Margaret's own motivations and reasons for change rather than providing external reasons, using open questions that draw out her internal values. Could be more systematic in eliciting confidence, ability, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with clear power-sharing language and positions Margaret as the expert on her own life. Slightly limited by two directive prompts that don't offer Margaret a choice in what to explore next.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully identifies and amplifies Margaret's change talk, reflecting back her own language and reframing past attempts positively. Could have more explicitly elicited confidence statements and probed deeper into ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids challenging sustain talk, validating Margaret's social costs and respecting her boundary about Thursday. Could have more strategically used double-sided reflections to help Margaret see both sides of her ambivalence.
