### Acceptance

**Comments:** Respectful, affirming, and nonjudgmental toward Sofia’s ambivalence, weight, and past failures. A few responses are somewhat generic or mildly interpretive rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captures her exhaustion, self-protective motives, and shame-based self-talk. One flat response and occasional conceptualizing prevent consistently deeper empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on improving energy through small, realistic movement. The session narrows from broad goals to concrete action steps without meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acts as an equal partner rather than an authority directing the session. Consistently builds on Sofia’s own language, ideas, and problem-solving without premature advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports Sofia’s sense of choice and self-direction, avoiding pressure or controlling language. Her redefined successes, such as putting the shoes in the bag and accepting rest, are well supported.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits motivation, values, and solutions from within Sofia using open questions and reflections. Could be strengthened by more targeted evocation of confidence, importance, or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally conveys that Sofia is the expert in her own life and co-creates the plan with her. A few interpretive statements and a possible redundant response slightly interrupt the collaborative flow.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and affirms Sofia’s own change talk, especially around small, manageable steps. Could be deepened by more explicitly eliciting confidence, commitment, and stronger DARN-C language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles doubt, fear of failure, and shame well with normalization, double-sided reflections, and smaller steps. At times stays within the failure or shame frame rather than actively pivoting toward resources and values.
