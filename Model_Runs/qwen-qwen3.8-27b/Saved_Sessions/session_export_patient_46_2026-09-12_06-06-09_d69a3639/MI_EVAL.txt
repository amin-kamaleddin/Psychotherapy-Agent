# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, respectful, nonjudgmental language and affirms Ethan without shaming. It falls short of a 5 because the positive regard is somewhat pragmatic rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects Ethan’s ambivalence well, especially the tension between enjoying friends and wanting to avoid getting sick. It is not a 5 because deeper feelings such as shame, fear, or frustration are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use, consequences, safety, and next steps. It is slightly limited by a strong logistics focus rather than consistently tying the plan back to values and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Ethan’s choices and adjusts based on his responses, creating a sense of teamwork. It is not a 5 because the counselor often remains the main planner and script generator.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks which options feel doable and respects Ethan’s preference not to rehearse. It is not a 5 because the counselor still provides substantial advice and drafts specific texts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some evocative questions that elicit Ethan’s own change ideas and confidence. It is limited because the session shifts too much toward advice and scripts rather than drawing out Ethan’s values and internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Ethan’s perspective and allows him to choose a simpler plan. It is not a 5 because the counselor supplies a menu of strategies and drafts texts rather than fully co-creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms Ethan’s practical change talk, such as using a soda, texting his dad, and avoiding a drunk driver. It is limited because the clinician does not consistently deepen, reflect, or amplify that change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and acknowledges Ethan’s desire to fit in without judgment. It is limited because there is little active softening or redirection of sustain talk into a pathway toward change.
