# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, with strong validation and normalization of Sofia's fear. Could be pushed toward a 5 by more explicitly affirming Sofia's worthiness independent of her choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with nuanced reflections like 'punished for being human' and the grief of losing a version of herself. Two instances of generic 'I understand, can you tell me more?' responses represent missed opportunities for deeper empathic engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with a natural progression from abstract emotional concerns to concrete behavioral planning. A 5 would require more explicit linking of each section back to the behavioral goal and a stronger closing summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framing the session as a joint exploration with inclusive language and genuine curiosity about Sofia's responses. Could be deepened through explicit power-sharing moments and co-creation of the session's agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy support, particularly in affirming Sofia's self-generated plan ('I love that you built it yourself') and avoiding directives. Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming her right to make any choice while exploring consequences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Sofia's own motivations rather than providing external reasons for change, effectively using her 'mopping up water' metaphor. The psychiatrist successfully evokes values around motherhood and self-worth as internal drivers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Establishes a collaborative frame early but partnership remains somewhat superficial, feeling more like a guided conversation than true power-sharing. The psychiatrist could have more explicitly acknowledged Sofia's expertise in her own life and invited agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questions and reflections that draw out Sofia's own reasons for change. The one-year-from-now question and 'what would Friday night look like?' prompts are well-placed evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk about friends reasonably but without particular skill or strategic depth. Could benefit from double-sided reflections or more nuanced redirection when Sofia expresses ambivalence about social connections.
