# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance throughout, validating Hana's honesty and self-doubt without dismissing or minimizing her struggles. Could be elevated to a 5 by more explicitly normalizing the difficulty of change and offering unconditional regard in moments of self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathy demonstrated through accurate, nuanced reflections that progressively deepen into Hana's experience. The psychiatrist consistently reflects both content and underlying emotion, capturing tensions Hana may not have fully articulated herself.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent direction, maintaining clear focus on the target behavior (approaching the exam despite anxiety) with a logical progression from exploration through behavioral planning to self-monitoring. The session never drifts into unrelated territory and stays specific to the exam, the book, and the date.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI collaboration — the interaction consistently feels like two equal partners exploring a problem together, with the psychiatrist following Hana's lead, building on her own language and metaphors, and never lecturing or directing. The power dynamic is genuinely balanced throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently strong autonomy support, explicitly honoring Hana's right to uncertainty and never pressuring toward optimism or specific outcomes. Every question is framed as an invitation to her own thinking, and her boundary about not being 'fine yet' is fully respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation, plans, and insights emerge from Hana's own thinking through strategic open questions directed at her values, past successes, and capabilities. At no point does the psychiatrist tell Hana why she should change or what the right answer is.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance with consistent positioning of Hana as the expert in her own experience, following her lead throughout. Occasional slight shifts toward a more guiding position prevent a full 5, which would require more explicit acknowledgment of Hana's expertise and genuine negotiation about focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes multiple forms of change talk (desire, ability, need, commitment) through open questions and affirmations. Could be elevated by more explicitly amplifying or summarizing Hana's change talk at key moments and adding confidence scaling questions to deepen her articulation of ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without reinforcement, validating doubt as pressure-testing and honoring ambivalence without false reassurance. Could be improved by more consistently using double-sided reflections to fully honor both sides of ambivalence before redirecting.
