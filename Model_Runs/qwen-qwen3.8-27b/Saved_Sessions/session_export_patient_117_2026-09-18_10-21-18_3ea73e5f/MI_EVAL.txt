### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and non-judgmental, with multiple affirmations of Samuel's effort and honesty. Falls slightly short of a 5 by not explicitly affirming his inherent worth or normalizing the struggle in a deeply individualized way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that accurately captures Samuel's ambivalence and the push-pull of his experience. Some reflections remain at the cognitive level rather than fully exploring underlying emotions like loneliness or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking reduction with a logical progression from journal review through triggers to a concrete plan. Could benefit from more strategic collaborative summarizing to build momentum toward commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely frames the session as joint exploration, building on Samuel's metaphors and insights to shape the conversation. The power balance remains equal throughout, with decisions emerging from Samuel's own words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Samuel's ownership and right to choose his pace, using language like 'on your own terms' and 'what would feel like yours to try.' Never pressures or minimizes his agency, even when sustain talk threatens to derail the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful evocative questions lead Samuel to generate his own plan and self-awareness, particularly the 'not ready as a shield' reflection. Could have further amplified his confidence in the final two-day plan to deepen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Samuel as the expert on his own experience, with session direction shaped entirely by his contributions. No imposed solutions; the psychiatrist's curiosity and questions keep the focus on Samuel's voice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strategic questions effectively elicit Samuel's own plan and self-insight, including his recognition of the 'not ready' pattern. Could have more explicitly summarized and reinforced his change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating concerns without dismissal and gently exposing the function of 'not ready' without confrontation. Could have more actively pivoted to explore values or next steps when Samuel expressed fear of failure.
