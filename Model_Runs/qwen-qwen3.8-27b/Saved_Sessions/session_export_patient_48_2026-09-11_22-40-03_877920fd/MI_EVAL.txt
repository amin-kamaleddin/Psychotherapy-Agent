# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes the client’s fatigue without blame. However, some affirmations feel plan-focused and may slightly undercut the client’s ongoing ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are supportive but often remain surface-level and shift quickly into problem-solving. Deeper ambivalence, fear of failure, and the emotional weight of chronic fatigue are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and appropriately narrows it to a realistic 2-minute evening routine. Direction could be strengthened by explicitly linking the action to the client’s broader health values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor adapts the plan based on client input and reduces complexity when needed. However, frequent provision of concrete options and tactics keeps the interaction unevenly expert-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some autonomy is supported through choices and checking willingness. Directive prompts and continued suggestions after the client’s pushback may reduce the client’s sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks some open, MI-appropriate questions but moves too quickly into advice and planning. There is limited deeper exploration of the client’s values, motivation, and confidence in change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and adjusts the plan based on the client’s constraints. Still, directive language and the counselor-supplied structure limit a fully collaborative, client-led partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, such as willingness to try a 2-minute routine. The counselor does not consistently amplify or expand these statements to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor normalizes barriers and reduces pressure by shrinking the goal. More consistent MI-concordant strategies, such as double-sided reflections or externalizing, would better soften sustain talk.
