# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming, validating Luka’s partial progress and avoiding labels. Acceptance is strong but occasionally blunt in safety language and could be warmer when Luka resists a formal safety plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects practical details and tracks much of the client’s perspective, but responses are mostly paraphrase-based. Deeper emotional concerns about shame, fear, and protecting the relationship are not consistently reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on anger, alcohol, safety, and concrete harm-reduction steps, giving it clear momentum. Direction could be strengthened with strategic summaries to consolidate the core change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates the plan by inviting Luka to choose the signal, break, drink limit, substitute, and script tone. Collaboration is strong, though the clinician still provides much of the agenda and practical content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly asks for preferences, avoids imposing abstinence or a formal safety plan, and affirms the client’s choices. Repeated crisis statements and practical advice can feel controlling if overemphasized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes change talk and raises confidence from 5 to 7 through questions about importance, confidence, barriers, and same-day action. Deeper values and intrinsic motivations are not consistently drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Luka’s input shapes the final plan, and the psychiatrist asks to tune wording and tone to match the couple. Partnership is solid, but the session is partly expert-driven in offering many strategies and safety structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses questions and affirmations to help Luka articulate motivation, strategies, and commitment, with confidence increasing over time. It misses opportunities to reflect and amplify the motivational significance of his own statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids shaming and accepts cutting down as a meaningful goal, while normalizing the calming function of alcohol. It does not consistently use double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance to resolve ambivalence.
