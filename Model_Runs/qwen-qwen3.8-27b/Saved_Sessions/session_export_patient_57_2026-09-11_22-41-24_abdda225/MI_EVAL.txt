# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist was respectful and normalized Jamal’s peer pressure, but the warmth was brief and formulaic. Deeper acceptance was limited by a quick shift into advice and problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist understood Jamal’s surface-level concerns about fitting in and avoiding a scene. However, reflections stayed mostly cognitive and did not fully capture his fear, embarrassment, or identity conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on the target behavior of drinking with the team and managing peer pressure. The main limitation was that the direction became overly narrowed toward practical scripts and tactics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for input and built on some of Jamal’s ideas, such as using a water cup and avoiding confrontation. Still, the session remained largely counselor-led with repeated unilateral suggestions and role-play prompts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered choices and avoided blaming Jamal, supporting some sense of decision-making. Autonomy was weakened by the frequent delivery of ready-made scripts, tactics, and practice prompts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist rarely helped Jamal articulate his own values, concerns, or reasons for change. The session relied more on external advice and tactics than on eliciting internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There was some collaborative language and shared planning, but the psychiatrist often took an expert-driven stance. The session felt more like advising or coaching than a true MI partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal offered meaningful change talk, such as knowing the drinking was bad and not wanting to drink. The psychiatrist did not consistently reflect, amplify, or build on these statements to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Jamal’s sustain talk about fitting in and not looking weak was acknowledged but not effectively softened. The psychiatrist moved quickly to tactics without using double-sided reflections to address ambivalence.
