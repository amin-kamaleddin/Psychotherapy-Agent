# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintains a nonjudgmental, supportive stance and respects Sofia’s choices. Acceptance is strong but sometimes procedural rather than deeply affirming her ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture key themes such as cost, secrecy, and low-effort preferences. However, deeper fears, identity worries, and peer/family pressures are not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on marijuana use, stress, cost, and a concrete plan. The pacing feels somewhat task-driven and rushed, which slightly limits clarity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia’s input shapes the plan, but the psychiatrist frequently drives the agenda and offers a prepared menu of options. More client-led exploration would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice, avoids pressure to quit, and validates Sofia’s small-step plan. Some directive prompts, such as asking her to agree to wait, limit full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks evocative questions but moves quickly into planning before deeply expanding change talk. Greater follow-up on values, discrepancy, and motivation would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia’s preferences are respected and her choices are honored throughout the session. The psychiatrist could more explicitly position Sofia as the expert and allow her to lead more.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia offers some change talk, especially around saving money and trying a walk. The psychiatrist does not consistently draw out stronger desire, ability, need, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by normalizing and reframing without arguing. More skillful double-sided reflections could further soften sustain talk.
