# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful throughout, with multiple examples of normalizing the client's experience. Falls short of a 5 because the acceptance remains at a professional level without deeply individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes a reasonable effort to reflect Sofia's perspective with some accurate reflections, but stays at a surface level and repeats near-identical phrasing. Fails to probe the emotional depth of her experience or connect empathy to the specific concern that brought her in.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The most critical failure in the session: the counselor never mentions alcohol, drinking, or the reason for referral at any point. The entire session drifts into general wellness conversation, rendering it clinically ineffective for its intended purpose.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open-ended questions and invites Sofia's perspective, but the approach is formulaic and follows a predictable pattern. The session feels more like a structured interview with collaborative elements than a genuine co-constructed process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids pressure and validates Sofia's choices, but does not explicitly affirm her right to self-determination or consistently reinforce her capacity for autonomous decision-making. Autonomy support is present but moderate and inconsistent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some effort to elicit motivation from within Sofia through open-ended questions and building on her language, but the evocation is superficial and never connects to the actual target behavior. No systematic exploration of her values, goals, or intrinsic motivations occurs.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and allows Sofia to guide the conversation to some extent, but occasionally slips into a more directive, guided-interview mode. The power sharing is present but not substantial enough to reflect a true equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal and weak efforts to evoke change talk, with only one brief moment ('it gets old after a while') that is not developed or reinforced. No exploration of Sofia's values, goals, or desire, ability, need, or reason for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk, which is positive, but also does not effectively soften or redirect it. Misses multiple opportunities to use double-sided reflections or gentle pivots, with only one effective softening attempt in the entire session.
