# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistent, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance throughout; validates Margaret's frustration without becoming defensive. Warmth is steady and professional but remains somewhat measured rather than deeply warm, with no explicit normalization of ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates sophisticated complex reflections (e.g., 'two stories in your head at once') and accurate identification of Margaret's internal tension. Could have explored the deeper emotional weight of identity loss and social connection tied to the pub more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful focus on alcohol reduction with logical progression from opening through to a concrete action step. The transition from the fall to the tracking idea could have been more explicitly connected to strengthen coherence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as joint exploration and avoids a top-down approach. Could be more explicitly collaborative in structure, such as asking Margaret to help shape the agenda or where to start.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary throughout—never prescribes or pressures, and consistently frames all decisions as Margaret's own. The self-correction ('I'll take that back') when Margaret resists being made to feel like a 'problem' is a particularly strong demonstration of respecting autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Margaret's own motivations and change language, culminating in a self-determined plan. Could have pushed further on eliciting confidence and ability (e.g., readiness scaling, 'what would make this easier?') to develop fuller DARN-CAT change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a non-hierarchical, inclusive stance and invites Margaret's perspective throughout. Could be strengthened by more explicit agenda negotiation and actively incorporating Margaret's ideas into the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reflects and amplifies Margaret's own change language and elicits a concrete, self-generated plan. Could have deepened change talk by asking about confidence in follow-through or what would make the plan easier to sustain.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by building on Margaret's stated boundaries rather than challenging them. Could have more explicitly normalized the sustainability of her small plan (e.g., 'small, steady things are often the ones that stick').
