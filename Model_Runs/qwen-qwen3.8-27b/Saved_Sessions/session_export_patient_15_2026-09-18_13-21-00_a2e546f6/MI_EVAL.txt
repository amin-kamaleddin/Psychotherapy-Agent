# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with validation throughout. However, warmth remains somewhat structured and formulaic, lacking deeper individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening with skilled complex reflections, particularly around the genetics preference. Could go deeper into the emotional subtext during moments of frustration or defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with logical sequencing from broad concern to specific alcohol use. Could benefit from more strategic narrowing and explicit co-creation of focus with the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing with joint exploration language and open questions inviting the client's perspective. The consistent reflect-then-question structure keeps the conversation somewhat counselor-controlled rather than genuinely client-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent autonomy support throughout. Never pressures or imposes; validates the client's need for evidence as a healthy standard and explicitly affirms his desire for his choices to be his own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively uses open questions to elicit the client's own reasoning, successfully drawing out change talk and confidence language. Could deepen evocation of ability, values, and identity dimensions further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with open questions and consistently invites the client's perspective. However, the session flow is still significantly counselor-led, with less evidence of the client genuinely shaping direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reflects back small change talk with positive framing. Could more explicitly affirm the client's self-efficacy (e.g., past smoking cessation) and develop confidence and ability language further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk with non-confrontational exploration and a well-executed double-sided reflection. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections to simultaneously honor doubt while developing change talk.
