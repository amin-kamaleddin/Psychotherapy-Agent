# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates the client’s frustration and practical stance. Warmth is effective but somewhat efficient, with occasional slight evaluative praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is a strong initial reflection of frustration and ambivalence, but later reflections focus more on plan content than deeper feelings, stress, or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing salty car snacking and remains relevant to the client’s time constraints and blood pressure concern. It becomes somewhat narrow and could more explicitly connect the small change to broader health goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client’s preferences shape the plan, especially in choosing cheese and using the key ring. However, the counselor supplies much of the intervention and structure, limiting truly joint solution-building.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Choices are offered, the client’s refusal of breathing exercises is accepted, and the plan is framed as non-punitive. Still, advice and detailed implementation steps can feel directive without more explicit autonomy language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The confidence scale and “what would move you from 6 to 8?” elicit some client-generated ideas. The counselor could more consistently draw out the client’s values, importance, ability, and internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is cooperation, with the client selecting the snack, reminder method, and timing. The counselor still takes on an expert role early by proposing specific interventions before fully eliciting the client’s own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client-driven language is elicited through practical questions and choices, such as trying cheese and clipping it to the keys. Change talk remains underdeveloped because the session moves quickly into implementation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing and reframes the work as a small, low-fuss experiment that fits the client’s life. More explicit double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance could further soften sustain talk.
