# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout the session. Warmth is strong but somewhat formulaic, preventing a top score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor reflects the client’s ambivalence accurately and tracks key concerns. Empathy is mainly cognitive rather than deeply emotional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on reducing alcohol-related risk while preserving social connection. The movement toward a concrete plan is well organized.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and adapts to the client’s preferences. However, the counselor often supplies options first, which reduces true co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently honors the client’s choice to cut back rather than quit. Repeated strategy suggestions and risk-focused framing slightly reduce client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks good evocative questions early on, such as importance and confidence. The session later shifts into advice and problem-solving before fully drawing out client motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is a collaborative tone and the client’s preferences shape the plan. The counselor often adopts an expert problem-solving role by offering many specific options and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client offers several useful change statements, including limits, an exit plan, and talking to the roommate. The counselor could deepen elicitation of desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges the client’s desire to fit in without arguing against it. Sustain talk is not strongly redirected or used to explore the cost of staying the same.
