# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Maria’s pain, guilt, and ambivalence with a respectful, nonjudgmental stance. Some crisis and diagnostic language felt slightly formulaic, limiting the score to a 4.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Maria’s distress and makes a corrective apology after asking too many questions. A few reflections remained more summarizing than deeply exploratory.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on safety, suicidal ideation, and Maria’s choice to stay. It lacks a stronger progressive move toward a clearly collaborative change direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Maria’s preferences and adapts when she declines grounding or a safety plan. Some expert explanations and procedural safety language reduce the co-creative nature of the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports Maria’s choice by offering options, accepting her resistance, and affirming her agency. Safety language is framed as support rather than pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some useful open questions, but evocation becomes limited after Maria asks for fewer questions. The psychiatrist could more consistently draw out Maria’s own reasons for staying or changing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shares decision-making and respects Maria’s requests to avoid plans and grounding. There is still some clinician-led structure, especially around safety checks and crisis resources.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist notices and affirms Maria’s statements about staying and caring for her sister. There are missed opportunities to deepen and consolidate this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with Maria’s statements that nothing works or that suicide is the only way out. There is limited use of double-sided reflections or evocative pivots to reduce the pull of sustain talk.
