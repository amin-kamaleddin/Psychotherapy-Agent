# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, nonjudgmental language and validates Daniel's concerns without criticism. Acceptance is strong but mostly embedded in reflections rather than stated as direct affirmations of Daniel as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Daniel's mixed goals and social concerns, including useful phrases like “five-second check” and “low-stakes experiment.” Empathy is clear and consistent, though it stays more cognitive than emotional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session moves logically from broad concerns to a specific, workable plan around non-alcoholic options and a backyard setting. The progression is focused, practical, and ends with a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel’s ideas drive the plan, and the psychiatrist builds on his language rather than imposing solutions. Collaboration is strong in content, though the overall structure and pacing remain counselor-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes Daniel’s ownership, choice, and personal decision-making. The language supports autonomy by avoiding prescriptions and reinforcing that the plan is Daniel’s, not someone else’s.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks open-ended questions that draw out Daniel’s own reasons, preferences, and next steps. Change talk is consistently elicited rather than supplied by the psychiatrist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Daniel as the expert and follows his lead in shaping the plan. Partnership is evident in practice, though it is less explicitly co-constructed in terms of agenda and session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Daniel generates strong desire, ability, and commitment language throughout the session. The psychiatrist effectively prompts this language, though it could be amplified or affirmed more explicitly at key moments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles Daniel's concern about awkwardness and social pressure without arguing or dismissing it. Sustain talk is limited in this session, but it is addressed appropriately through balanced reflections and reframing.
