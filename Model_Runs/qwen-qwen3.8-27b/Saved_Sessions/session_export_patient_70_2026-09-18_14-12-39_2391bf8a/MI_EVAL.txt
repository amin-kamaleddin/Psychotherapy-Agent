# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent respect, warmth, and nonjudgment throughout with multiple genuine affirmations, but the warmth remains somewhat measured and professional rather than deeply personal. Lacks explicit normalization of struggles and stronger emotional attunement to shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with reflections that capture emotional depth beyond surface-level restating, such as 'That's a heavy thing to sit with' and 'You're really caught between two things.' Consistently deepens understanding rather than repeating, and accurately tracks ambivalence without collapsing it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with good session momentum from boredom through to the 'shield' solution. Could be tighter by more explicitly tying each thread back to the overarching goal of avoiding reoffending.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a genuinely collaborative atmosphere with consistently joint language and equitable power balance. Could be more explicit in framing the session as 'us working on this together' rather than implicit facilitation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support through validating Lars's right to manage his own business, never using directive language, and framing all insights as his own discoveries. The statement 'I respect that you didn't sugarcoat it' powerfully affirms his honesty without pressuring him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Arguably the strongest skill in the session; every question and reflection is designed to elicit Lars's own motivations rather than providing external reasons. All key insights—the 'shield' metaphor, Donna's pride, not being 'the guy who makes excuses'—are Lars's own discoveries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides with Lars through open questions, explicit attribution of insights to him, and a complete absence of advice or direction. Functions as a mirror and amplifier of Lars's own thinking rather than a director.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally skilled at eliciting and reinforcing change talk through perfect open questions and amplifying the client's own language and metaphors. Produces rich change talk across desire, ability, reasons, and commitment dimensions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging and reframing rather than arguing, such as helping Lars find a solution that accommodates peer pressure. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections and more active pivoting when Lars lingers on the appeal of the Friday pattern.
