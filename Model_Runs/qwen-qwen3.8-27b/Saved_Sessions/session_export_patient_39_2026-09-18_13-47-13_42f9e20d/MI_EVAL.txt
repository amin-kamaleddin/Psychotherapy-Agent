# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout the session. Respects the client's skepticism without defensiveness and validates his experience without forcing a single narrative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and underlying feeling, matched to the client's register. Appropriately checks accuracy, showing genuine curiosity rather than assumption.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from ambivalence toward specific, self-generated actions. Could be slightly more directed toward a concrete next step or commitment before the session ends.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine equal partnership with shared exploration language and no power differential. The session's direction is shaped entirely by the client's responses while the psychiatrist provides structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly reinforces the client's ownership and decision-making authority. Never prescribes or implies failure; every choice is positioned as the client's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All change talk is generated by the client in response to skillful evocative questions and reflections. The psychiatrist never provides external reasons for change, instead drawing out the client's own values and capabilities.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within the client, explicitly returning interpretive authority to him. The session flows in directions the client chooses rather than following a predetermined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and skillfully evokes and amplifies the client's own emerging change language. The progression from 'I don't know how to get back to anything' to specific actions is largely the result of skilled evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframes 'should' as the problem and validates the client's fears without amplifying limitations. However, the 'I'm old' identity and the 'what if I make it worse' fear are not as actively addressed or redirected.
