# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently responds with respect, nonjudgment, and validation of Farid’s ambivalence and fatigue. Acceptance is strong but somewhat formulaic, with repeated phrases that limit deeper individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Farid’s conflict, exhaustion, and fear of failure. Some reflections remain surface-level and miss deeper feelings such as guilt or self-judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on barriers and quickly narrows to a concrete, small experiment. The final micro-step drifts slightly away from the original physical activity goal, reducing clarity of the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and builds a plan with Farid rather than imposing one. The stance is collaborative, though it remains somewhat clinician-guided because the psychiatrist frequently generates options first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Farid’s freedom to choose and reduces pressure by framing the plan as an optional experiment. The amount of specific suggestions and procedural detail keeps this from being fully exemplar autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some evocative questions about what would make change worth it and what small step is possible. Evocation is limited because Farid’s deeper values, reasons, and sense of ability are not fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a non-authoritative stance and follows Farid’s input as the plan evolves. Partnership could be stronger by giving Farid more space to generate ideas before offering options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Farid produce some change talk by narrowing to a small, manageable step. The session does not fully develop or amplify Farid’s own reasons for change, leaving his motivation somewhat fragile.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles Farid’s doubts well by reframing misses as information rather than failure. Sustain talk is softened but not fully resolved, especially around Farid’s belief that a tiny pause may be meaningless.
