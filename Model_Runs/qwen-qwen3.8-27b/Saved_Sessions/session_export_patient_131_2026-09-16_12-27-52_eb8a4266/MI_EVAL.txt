# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and consistently affirms Wiremu's goals and plans. The acceptance is solid but occasionally feels formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Wiremu's ambivalence, priorities, and practical concerns. There are missed opportunities to reflect deeper emotions such as shame, fear, grief, and parenting anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear focus on alcohol reduction, housing/support choices, and concrete next steps. The broader referral goal of violence reduction is not integrated consistently throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session is collaborative, with the plan co-constructed through client input and clinician summarization. The psychiatrist still performs some of the cognitive work by offering scripts and drafts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is generally supported through choice-focused questions and respect for Wiremu's independence-preserving plan. However, prescriptive scripting and drafting reduce opportunities for the client to generate his own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions help elicit motivation and practical next steps. Evocation is limited because the conversation moves quickly into problem-solving and scripted planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Wiremu as the expert on his own life and keeps the plan largely client-driven. Occasional shifts into a more expert or problem-solving role weaken consistent power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Wiremu produces clear change talk related to his children, independence, and sobriety goals. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or amplify this change language before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk about independence and reliance on whānau is acknowledged without argument or dismissal. Ambivalence could be explored more fully using balanced double-sided reflections.
