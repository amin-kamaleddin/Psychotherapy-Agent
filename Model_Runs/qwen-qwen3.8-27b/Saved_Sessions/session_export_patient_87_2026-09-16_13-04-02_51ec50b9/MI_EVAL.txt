# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental, with positive regard for Marco's motivation. Some leading assumptions and task-directed moments slightly reduce unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequate reflection of key themes, but often more cognitive and logistical than emotionally nuanced. Missed opportunities to explore fear of failure, discomfort, and ambivalence more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highly focused on starting a walking habit and efficiently moves toward a concrete plan. Overemphasis on logistics and added structure makes it feel less MI-like at times.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites client input and incorporates his concerns into the plan. Still somewhat counselor-led because many options and structures are introduced rather than fully co-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently supports choice and avoids overt pressure. Some directive suggestions and repeated focus on commitments slightly weaken autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation of motivation and self-generated ideas, but frequent advising reduces client-centered elicitation. Would benefit from more reflection and questions before offering options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Allows Marco to shape the session and avoids imposing a single solution. The counselor's leading role and multiple suggestions reduce equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits useful change talk about kids, walking, and confidence, but does not consistently deepen or amplify it. Shifts into advising rather than building client motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges concerns without arguing, but often responds with more structure or advice. Insufficient exploration and pivoting of ambivalence keeps sustain talk present.
