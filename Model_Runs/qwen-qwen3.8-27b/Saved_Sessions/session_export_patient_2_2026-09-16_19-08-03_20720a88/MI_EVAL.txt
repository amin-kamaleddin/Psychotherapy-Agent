# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes imperfection. The response to the client's fear of failing by Wednesday could have included more explicit emotional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses strong reflective listening to capture both behavior and meaning. Some reflections remain more content-focused and could go deeper into the client's underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a specific, practical goal of reducing evening smoking. The conversation moves logically from identifying barriers to building a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acts as an equal partner rather than an authority figure. The plan is co-created from the client's own ideas and practical suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently supports the client's sense of choice and avoids directive language. The client is encouraged to select her own limits, cues, and timeframe.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the client to generate her own reasons and solutions. The evocation could be stronger by more explicitly exploring importance, confidence, and readiness for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is positioned as the expert on her own life and evening routine. Her contributions substantially shape the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses open questions that elicit clear change talk, including desire, ability, need, and commitment. These statements could be more consistently amplified or summarized to strengthen momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids reinforcing reasons to maintain smoking and frames the plan in a low-pressure, non-authoritarian way. A stronger response could have directly addressed the client's worry about breaking the plan by Wednesday.
