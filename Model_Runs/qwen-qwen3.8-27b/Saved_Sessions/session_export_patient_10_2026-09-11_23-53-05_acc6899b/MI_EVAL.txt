# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, with several validating statements. However, acceptance remains somewhat task-oriented rather than deeply warm or affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are adequate surface-level reflections of frustration, risk, and exhaustion. The counselor often moves quickly into practical advice, which limits deeper empathic exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on managing the missed interview and planning next steps. Direction is a strength, though it remains largely task-focused rather than motivation-focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient’s preferences meaningfully shape the final message and plan. However, the psychiatrist often supplies options first, making the interaction less fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently invites choice and accepts the patient’s decisions without pressure. Some directive urgency slightly reduces the autonomy-supportive tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor does not consistently draw out the patient’s own values, motivations, or confidence. The approach is more advice-focused than evocative, despite the patient having strong change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor asks for input and adapts to the patient’s preferences. Yet the psychiatrist often takes an expert/advisory role, which weakens the partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The patient produces meaningful confidence and commitment statements throughout. The counselor acknowledges some of this but does not consistently amplify or deepen the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor respects the patient’s barriers without arguing or guilt-tripping. The focus remains heavily on logistical constraints, which can keep sustain talk more prominent than it needs to be.
