# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, normalizes difficulty, and avoids shaming when Lars expresses resistance or embarrassment. It falls short of 5 because acceptance is often tied to performance rather than unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Lars's ambivalence and central tension, including boredom, old friends, and alcohol. Reflections are more content-focused than emotion-focused, missing deeper shame, fear, and guilt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing reoffending and alcohol risk through a concrete Friday plan. The session stays tightly connected to the presenting concern without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is built around Lars's ideas, including the text, alarm, water backup, and push-up/walk strategy. Collaboration is not fully equal at times because the psychiatrist introduces many concrete options and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and respects Lars's decisions about timing, wording, and reminders. It could be stronger by more consistently emphasizing that the plan belongs entirely to Lars.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses scaling to elicit meaningful change-relevant language from Lars. However, evocation is not consistently strong because the psychiatrist provides much of the content rather than eliciting Lars's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Lars's input and lets his preferences shape the plan. It falls short because the psychiatrist still frequently supplies scripts and options, creating a more expert-driven feel.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session elicits useful change talk, such as wanting family instead of prison and wanting no exceptions. However, the psychiatrist often supplies options and planning steps instead of consistently evoking Lars's own reasons and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally does not argue with Lars's resistance and reframes it as practical. There is limited strategic softening, such as double-sided reflections or redirecting sustain talk into change language.
