# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, validating stance toward Jonathan's burden and ambivalence. Acceptance is strong but somewhat formulaic, with less explicit affirmation of Jonathan's worth independent of performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers solid reflections of both practical and emotional experience, including ambivalence. Empathy could be deeper by exploring feelings such as being policed, fear of failure, exhaustion, or loss of independence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on the target behavior of manageable diabetes self-management. The psychiatrist effectively narrows the conversation toward concrete, doable steps without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist involves Jonathan in problem-solving and builds a low-burden plan using his own ideas. Collaboration is solid but remains somewhat counselor-driven due to a repeated question format and limited explicit shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by asking what would be doable and avoiding coercive language. Autonomy support could be stronger by explicitly affirming Jonathan's right to decide his level of involvement or to choose not to test.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Jonathan's own motivations and practical reasons for change rather than lecturing. Evocation is effective but could be enhanced by drawing out deeper values, confidence, and future-oriented change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Jonathan as the expert on his daily life and allows his ideas to shape the session. Partnership could improve with more explicit power-sharing and less interviewer-led agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist creates opportunities for Jonathan to express change statements, such as wanting care to fit his life and checking when tired. Change talk could be amplified more deliberately using importance, confidence, and values-based reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without challenging Jonathan directly and often pairs it with a change-oriented side. Softening could be improved with more consistent double-sided reflections and clearer pivot toward values-based change.
