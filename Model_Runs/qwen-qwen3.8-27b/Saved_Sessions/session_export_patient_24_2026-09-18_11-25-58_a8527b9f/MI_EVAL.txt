# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, never labeling or judging Daniel. When Daniel pushes back on the 'protecting a story' framing, the psychiatrist immediately and fully validates him without being defensive, normalizing the entire experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and a skilled double-sided reflection near the end. However, the 'protecting a story' frame was perceived by Daniel as harsh, indicating a minor misread of his emotional state that slightly limits the score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on alcohol use anchored to marathon training, with a logical arc from opening to a concrete next step. The session feels slightly incomplete at the end without an explicit summary or tie-back to a specific plan or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong joint exploration framing with shared ownership language and active seeking of Daniel's input. The psychiatrist occasionally takes a more directive role in structuring the conversation rather than fully co-directing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout — the psychiatrist never prescribes, consistently frames decisions as Daniel's to make, and even concedes framing when corrected. The one-week experiment is framed as Daniel's own idea, not a suggestion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never provides external reasons for change and skillfully draws out Daniel's own observations and language. However, occasionally offering interpretive frames (e.g., the check-engine light analogy) before waiting for the client's own metaphors prevents a top score.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance positioning Daniel as the expert on his own body, avoiding expert-driven language. The 'protecting a story' moment placed the psychiatrist in a subtly more interpretive role than a fully partnership-oriented stance would allow.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk emerges organically and is reinforced effectively at key moments. The session could have gone further in amplifying change talk, particularly when Daniel said 'I've just accepted that's what late training looks like,' which was an underexplored thread.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is addressed reasonably but not consistently or strategically. The response to 'I'm probably overthinking it' felt analytical and pointed, and the psychiatrist doesn't consistently use double-sided reflections, which is the gold standard for softening sustain talk.
