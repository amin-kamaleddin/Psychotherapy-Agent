# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and offers clear affirmations of Lars’s efforts. The warmth is effective but somewhat procedural, with less emphasis on deeper individualized regard for his struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows adequate reflective listening and understands key concerns, including shame and risk. However, empathy remains more content-focused and does not fully explore Lars’s deeper emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays well focused on the main goal: reducing reoffending risk around Friday peer pressure and alcohol. Direction could be strengthened by more consistently linking the plan back to Lars’s broader values and identity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates by adjusting to Lars’s preferences and incorporating his choices into the plan. Collaboration is limited because the psychiatrist often supplies the structure and scripts before fully eliciting Lars’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Lars is regularly given choices and his decisions are respected, which supports his sense of agency. The psychiatrist still provides fairly specific scripts and plans, which makes the process somewhat directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively uses scaling questions and prompts to help Lars generate his own change steps. Evocation is somewhat surface-level and does not deeply draw on Lars’s values, past strengths, or identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for Lars’s input, offers options, and follows his lead in several places. The interaction is still somewhat therapist-led, especially when the psychiatrist introduces structured plans and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes change talk through targeted questions and confidence-based scaling. It could be stronger by more deliberately amplifying and expanding on Lars’s own motivation language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accepts Lars’s sustain talk without arguing and redirects it into practical steps. Softening is limited because the ambivalence behind the sustain talk is not deeply explored or nuanced.
