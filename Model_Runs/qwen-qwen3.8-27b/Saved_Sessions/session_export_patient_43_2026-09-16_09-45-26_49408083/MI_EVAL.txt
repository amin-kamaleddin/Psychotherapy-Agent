# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validated Eric’s perspective. However, repeated problem-solving suggestions and some managing language reduced the overall sense of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor made reasonable efforts to reflect Eric’s viewpoint and ambivalence. Still, empathy remained surface-level and missed deeper emotions and values such as pride, fear of illness, and marital frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stayed connected to smoking and related health or relationship concerns. Direction was somewhat scattered, moving between cessation, stress, communication, blood pressure, and coughing without a coherent focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor attempted to collaborate by offering options and respecting Eric’s rejections. Collaboration was limited, however, by a tendency to guide through structured suggestions rather than co-constructing ideas with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy support was strong, with consistent respect for Eric’s choices and resistance to pressure. The main limitation was the counselor’s frequent specific suggestions, which slightly shifted the interaction toward advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation was a major weakness, with limited effort to draw out Eric’s own reasons, values, or motivations. The conversation became dominated by counselor-provided options rather than client-generated exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor attempted to create partnership by inviting Eric to set the agenda and follow his lead. The interaction often became counselor-driven, weakening the sense of true collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor made limited efforts to cultivate change talk when Eric offered relevant cues. Instead of reflecting or amplifying these statements, the counselor often shifted to concrete advice or options.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoided arguing with Eric’s sustain talk and respected his resistance. However, the counselor sometimes reinforced the status quo rather than helping Eric explore ambivalence in a balanced way.
