# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and validating, but the warmth is often brief and formulaic. Acceptance is undermined by quickly shifting into problem-solving and advice.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers some accurate reflections, but the empathy is inconsistent and not deep enough. Amina’s ambivalence, exhaustion, and fear of being seen as unreliable are not fully reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior of attending the interview and solving the childcare barrier. However, the direction is more task-oriented and advice-driven than MI-guided.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist occasionally asks for input, but the interaction is largely clinician-led. The session would be stronger if next steps were co-created rather than presented as lists and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some moments of autonomy support, but the approach is often directive and prescriptive. Amina’s stated preference to figure things out on her own is not consistently honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session opens with a good evocative question, but the psychiatrist does not consistently draw out Amina’s own motivations, values, or confidence. The evocation remains superficial and infrequent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist assumes the problem-solving role and repeatedly provides plans and scripts. Amina’s concerns do not substantially shape the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Amina offers several spontaneous change talk and confidence statements, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or build on them. There are missed opportunities to strengthen Amina’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with sustain talk, but it is not consistently softened or redirected toward change. The focus remains on barriers and practical contingencies rather than Amina’s underlying fears and values.
