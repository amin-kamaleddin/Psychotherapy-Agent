# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect, nonjudgment, and support, especially with opening statements and affirming reflections. Positive regard is sometimes tied more to plan performance than to unconditional validation of the client's struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor demonstrates good reflective listening and accurately captures the client's exhaustion and practical barriers. Empathy is often content-focused and could more deeply reflect emotions such as frustration, shame, or hope.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session has a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity and stays on target throughout. Each question advances the conversation toward a concrete, doable plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and client jointly build a practical plan around shoes by the door, a porch-stand fallback, partner support, and music. Collaboration is strong, but the counselor often supplies solution menus and leads the sequence.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports autonomy by offering options and asking for the client's preferences rather than giving directives. Autonomy could be strengthened by more explicitly emphasizing the client's ownership and right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some evocative questions about confidence, prior successes, and barriers. Evocation remains superficial and could more deeply elicit the client's personal values, identity, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and invites the client's input throughout the session. The counselor is respectful but still often leads the agenda through structured options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor asks questions that help the client express readiness, confidence, and capability. Change talk is elicited but could be more consistently deepened and amplified by the counselor.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by acknowledging barriers without arguing and reframing them into manageable solutions. The counselor could more fully normalize past failure and the emotional pull of the couch.
