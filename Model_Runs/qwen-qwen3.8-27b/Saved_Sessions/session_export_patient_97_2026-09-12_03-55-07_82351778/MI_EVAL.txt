# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Mateo’s concerns without shaming. Acceptance is warm but occasionally stops short of fully acknowledging his vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture ambivalence and triggers but remain more cognitive and problem-solving than emotionally attuned. Important feelings such as fear, shame, and low confidence are not consistently reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a specific target behavior and produces a concrete, manageable step for tonight. Direction is clear but does not always link the behavior to Mateo’s broader goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is cooperative, with Mateo’s wording and choices shaping the plan. However, the counselor remains the primary architect of the problem-solving process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses preference-based questions and lets Mateo select the delay, activity, text, and backup. Some commitment-focused follow-ups create mild pressure, so autonomy support is strong but not perfect.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some client-generated goals and plan components but frequently shifts into suggestions and advice. Deeper personal motivations and intrinsic reasons for change are not consistently explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Mateo is invited to choose options and co-constructs the plan, but the counselor still provides much of the structure and agenda. Partnership is present but limited by the directive problem-solving frame.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites forward-moving goals and small intentions but does not consistently deepen or amplify Mateo’s change talk. Values-based elaboration and scaling follow-ups are limited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Obligation, doubt, and low expectation are present but largely unaddressed. The counselor acknowledges worry without using reflections or pivots to soften sustain talk.
