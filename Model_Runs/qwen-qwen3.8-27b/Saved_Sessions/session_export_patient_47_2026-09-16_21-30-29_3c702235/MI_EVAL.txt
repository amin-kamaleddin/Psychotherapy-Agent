# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgment regarding Evelyn’s ambivalence. It reaches a 5 only by adding warmer affirmation of her personal values and identity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows strong reflective listening and accurately captures Evelyn’s practical concerns and ambivalence. Deeper emotional meaning, such as the fear of being watched or what the pub represents, could be explored more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on safer alcohol use and Evelyn’s specific drinking pattern. The progression from ambivalence to a concrete first Saturday and self-monitoring is clear and clinically effective.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses client-centered language and builds on Evelyn’s own ideas, supporting a collaborative stance. A 5 would require more evidence that Evelyn is co-constructing the session agenda and pacing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports Evelyn’s freedom of choice and ownership throughout the session. Suggestions are framed as options rather than recommendations, preserving her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively invite Evelyn to articulate her own goals, barriers, and solutions. The psychiatrist could go further by exploring what her stated choices mean personally and how they connect to her values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and allows Evelyn’s contributions to shape the plan. A 5 would include more explicit sharing of power, such as inviting Evelyn to set or confirm the session agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes change talk by inviting Evelyn to generate her own plan and language. Some change talk could be amplified or connected more deeply to her values before moving to the next step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with resistance and reframes Evelyn’s sustain talk in ways that preserve autonomy. A 5 would include more explicit transformation of sustain talk into change language tied to her values.
