# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout. The psychiatrist consistently normalizes, validates, and affirms Claudia's experience without any judgment or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Remarkably high empathic accuracy, capturing the emotional and experiential core of Claudia's statements with precision. Reflections go well beyond paraphrasing to access underlying meaning and function.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and logical progression from understanding the pattern to goal formulation and planning. Slightly less strategic in the middle of the session where earlier connection back to the drinking goal could have improved efficiency.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative interaction where the psychiatrist offers reframes and metaphors that Claudia adopts and builds upon. The session feels like two people thinking together with an equal power balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Claudia's autonomy is the organizing principle of the entire session. The psychiatrist never prescribes a specific plan; Claudia arrives at her own goal and the psychiatrist simply reflects and affirms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes motivation through strategic questioning and reflective amplification rather than providing external reasons for change. Could have been more systematic in eliciting readiness and confidence language across DARN categories.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Claudia, using her own language and metaphors rather than clinical frameworks. Every intervention is framed as a reflection or invitation, never a directive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through strategic questioning and reflective amplification, with Claudia naturally articulating her own goal. Could have more explicitly elicited additional change talk across all DARN categories.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates sustain talk while opening space for change, transforming concerns into evidence of readiness. The opportunity to demonstrate advanced techniques was somewhat limited given the mild nature of the sustain talk present.
