# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and non-judgmental stance maintained throughout, with specific and individualized validation of Ryan's self-awareness and vulnerability. Never moralizes or labels; explicitly normalizes his experience with language like 'That's a real tension to sit with.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures dual experiences and underlying emotional meaning well beyond surface-level paraphrasing. Accurately identifies the vulnerability around belonging and the real-time shift in Ryan's thinking, reflecting it back with genuine sensitivity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent session structure with a clear logical arc from specific incident to pattern identification to underlying needs to concrete potential change. Every response advances the conversation without wandering, maintaining focus on the target behavior while exploring broader context.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely egalitarian power dynamic where Ryan's ideas substantially shape session direction; the conversation feels like two people thinking together rather than one person guiding another. Each major topic shift is driven by Ryan's own words and the psychiatrist's reflections of those words.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Ryan's right to make his own decisions in his own time, never pressuring for commitment or imposing a timeline. All suggestions are framed as exploratory rather than prescriptive, with explicit statements like 'You don't have to decide which one to believe tonight.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Ryan using open questions that draw out his own reasons and solutions, without providing external justifications. Could be slightly more targeted at building Ryan's sense of self-efficacy and confidence in his ability to make the change he's already identified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Ryan's perspective and avoidance of an expert-driven approach. Could be slightly stronger with more explicit power-sharing language or co-creation of next steps, as the psychiatrist still positions themselves slightly ahead as the facilitator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies concrete change statements from Ryan, such as not pre-drinking and showing up at a normal pace. Could have more explicitly elicited Ryan's confidence in making these changes or explored what would happen if he did try this approach.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk with sophisticated double-sided reflections that acknowledge Ryan's concerns while gently elevating the change talk. Minor omission in not explicitly addressing the normalizing quality of 'Everyone drinks. It's just...'
