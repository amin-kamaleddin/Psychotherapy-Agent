# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, encouragement, and nonjudgment while validating the client’s efforts. Acceptance remains slightly performance-focused and could more deeply affirm the client as a whole person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections and acknowledges practical barriers such as distraction, sedentary work, and the need for simplicity. Deeper emotional and thematic reflection would strengthen this criterion further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on restarting physical activity through a concrete walking plan. The counselor effectively guides the client toward specific, executable actions without meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-creates the plan with the client, incorporating her preferences for simplicity, structure, and low-pressure options. More open-ended collaboration would make the process feel more equally client-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor regularly invites choice and supports the client’s ownership of decisions. A few mildly directive moments slightly reduce the emphasis on the client being fully in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out the client’s own motivation, confidence, and reasons for change. Deeper evocation of personal values and identity-based motivation would elevate this criterion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor treats the client as an expert and builds the plan from her ideas and preferences. Occasional advisory or expert-driven moments reduce the partnership feel slightly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses scaling, commitment, and benefit-focused questions that elicit strong change talk from the client. More consistent amplification of the client’s change talk would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with barriers and reframes concerns into manageable, realistic solutions. More explicit MI-based softening would make this criterion even stronger.
