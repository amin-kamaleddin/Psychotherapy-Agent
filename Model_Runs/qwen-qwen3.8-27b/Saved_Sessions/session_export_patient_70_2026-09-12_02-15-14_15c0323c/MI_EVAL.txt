# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and validates Lars's ambivalence and progress. The warmth is somewhat formulaic and does not deeply affirm his fear of reoffending or identity concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Perspective-taking is adequate, especially around restlessness and the pull of his current friends. It remains mostly content-focused, missing deeper emotional weight around prison, loyalty, and embarrassment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing recidivism and stable re-entry. It moves logically from goals to risk factors to concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Lars's preferences shape the plan, such as choosing a text reminder instead of rehearsal. However, the psychiatrist often provides scripts and checklists rather than fully co-creating them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently asks which option feels most doable and lets Lars tailor the plan. Some suggestions, such as texting now or picking an exact activity, are slightly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Initial open-ended questions invite a steady life, but the session shifts to external solutions and scripts. This limits deeper evocation of Lars's intrinsic motivation and internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks permission and offers options, maintaining a collaborative stance. The partnership is occasionally disrupted when shifting into a more expert, checklist-providing role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Lars is encouraged to identify supports and practical next steps, and his actions are affirmed. However, change talk is not consistently amplified through values, scaling, or deeper DARN-CRAFT exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk around friends, action orientation, and form embarrassment is handled without direct confrontation. It is redirected toward safer, pragmatic alternatives such as sober meetups and simple messages.
