# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a non-judgmental stance and validated Jason's frustration with the conduct process. However, acceptance remained somewhat clinical and transactional rather than deeply warm or person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Empathic statements accurately reflected Jason's surface-level frustration and sense of unfairness. The deeper emotions, such as shame, anxiety, or need for belonging, were not explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the student conduct issue, but the direction was misaligned with MI. Instead of exploring alcohol use or ambivalence, the psychiatrist emphasized procedural and legal strategy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for Jason's input at times, such as choosing between a written statement or role-play. However, the collaboration was superficial because the psychiatrist largely generated the content, scripts, and strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were a few autonomy-supportive moments, such as respecting Jason's choice not to name his roommate. These were overwhelmed by repeated script-based advice and prescriptive guidance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There was little effort to elicit Jason's own motivations, values, or reasons for change. The session focused on logistics rather than drawing out Jason's own thoughts and perspective.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned themselves as the expert problem-solver and provided direct advice and scripts. This one-way dynamic was inconsistent with the MI spirit of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** There was virtually no effort to cultivate change talk related to Jason's alcohol use. The psychiatrist did not explore Jason's own reasons for changing behavior or values related to his actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist did not directly reinforce Jason's sustain talk, but also did not effectively address it with MI-consistent techniques. Instead, the sustain talk was largely bypassed by pivoting to practical advice.
