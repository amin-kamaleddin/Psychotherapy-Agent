# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental tone while affirming Amina's concerns and plan. The tone was supportive, though it did not fully affirm her worth beyond her goals and resilience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflected frustration, worry about burdening her sister, and feeling stuck. The reflections were strong but did not consistently explore deeper experiences such as self-blame or repeated disappointment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained tightly focused on enabling Amina to attend the Saturday interview. The psychiatrist consistently connected the conversation to a concrete next step and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina helped choose options and shape the text, but the psychiatrist supplied many specific scripts and backup plans. This made the collaboration somewhat uneven and less centered on Amina's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asked for preferences, respected Amina's decisions, and supported her plan to send the text after bedtime. The continued offering of specific scripts and contingency plans slightly reduced autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist drew out Amina's motivation, confidence, and intention to act through focused questions. It would have been stronger with more consistent exploration of her values, hopes, and reasons this matters.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positioned Amina as the expert and invited her to select steps and wording. The frequent provision of concrete scripts and options slightly shifted the interaction toward clinician-led direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina produced clear change talk, including her confidence of 9 and commitment to send the text. The psychiatrist did not consistently deepen or amplify this change talk with further exploration of values or strengths.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged Amina's concerns about burdening her sister and bracing for failure without arguing. However, repeated attention to backup plans and fallback lines kept the focus on barriers rather than redirecting toward goals and strengths.
