# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental stance, especially by not moralizing Sofia’s marijuana use. Acceptance is strong but could be deepened by affirming her broader stressors and self-worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective language is used often, but it stays mostly at the content level rather than naming underlying fear, shame, ambivalence, or hopelessness. Deeper emotional reflections would strengthen the therapeutic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session remains focused on marijuana use and stress management without major drift. However, it becomes repetitive and narrow around confidence ratings and micro-planning, with less strategic movement toward values or discrepancy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Sofia’s own language and offers choices, showing some collaboration. Nonetheless, the session is largely structured and task-driven by the psychiatrist rather than equally co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Autonomy-supportive language is present, and Sofia’s preferences are often respected. However, repeated pressure to choose, draft, or decide now can feel controlling and may reduce her sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor begins with useful evocative questions about likes, worries, and desired change, but quickly shifts into practical behavior planning. There are missed opportunities to evoke deeper motivation, values, and discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia is invited to contribute and the psychiatrist adapts to her language, but the session is more counselor-led than truly collaborative. Partnership is evident in surface choices, but power sharing is limited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Useful change talk is evoked around small steps, staying in, texting her cousin, and using the homework line. However, this change talk is not consistently reflected, amplified, or consolidated to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia’s sustain talk is not explicitly reinforced, but it is also not effectively softened because the psychiatrist repeatedly redirects to planning and behavior options. More double-sided reflections, normalization, and discrepancy work would be needed.
