# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance toward Naomi’s anxiety and ambivalence. Acceptance is professional but somewhat restrained, with limited deeper affirmation of her worth beyond her performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There is strong reflective listening that accurately tracks Naomi’s ambivalence, physical experience, and concern about failure. Deeper emotional attunement to implied shame or fear of appearing not “put-together” could make the empathy more complete.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on managing social/professional anxiety in Naomi’s photography work. It moves smoothly from the concern to a specific, low-risk experiment with clear, goal-directed flow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as an equal partner rather than an advice-giver. Naomi’s ideas about the camera anchor, solo shoot, and simple tracking directly shape the final plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Naomi is consistently positioned as the decision-maker about what to try, how to try it, and how to define success. There is no pressure to change, and her definition of success is explicitly honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist mostly elicits Naomi’s own motivation and generates a plan based on her ideas. A few mildly leading suggestions, such as tying a breath to camera movement or using one line, keep it from being fully evocative.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Naomi’s perspective and allows her concerns to guide the session. A few guiding suggestions reduce the impression of continuous, fully client-led co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps Naomi articulate desire, reason, and ability for change and reinforces it in a strengths-based way. More explicit amplification, such as summarizing motivations or affirming confidence, would strengthen this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument, dismissal, or overemphasis, using double-sided language to normalize ambivalence. More consistent reframing of doubts as thoughtful and connected to change would improve this area.
