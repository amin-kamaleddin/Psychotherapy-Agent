# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently normalizes and accepts the client's experience without judgment. Acceptance could be strengthened by adding more explicit affirmations and warmer positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections and strong perspective-taking of the anxiety pattern and internal conflict. It could go deeper by reflecting the underlying shame or fear of professional failure more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear, consistent focus on the client's target issue of managing anxiety during photography work. The direction is well matched to the client's current stage of change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates by building from the client's ideas, language, and concerns. Explicit invitation of the client to set or adjust the session agenda would strengthen collaboration further.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids directives and pressure, using open, exploratory questions. Autonomy support would be stronger with more explicit statements about the client's freedom and ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client's own motivations, ideas, and change talk. It could be improved with more evocative summaries and clearer eliciting of confidence or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the client as an expert and follows her suggestions throughout the conversation. More explicit power-sharing and co-creation language would make the partnership feel more complete.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes meaningful change talk and reflects a key shift in the client's thinking. Using DARN-C language, scaling, or evocative summaries would help consolidate the change talk more systematically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and uses helpful reflections and exploratory questions to redirect it. The final fear about shaky hands and ruining a photo could be softened or reframed more explicitly.
