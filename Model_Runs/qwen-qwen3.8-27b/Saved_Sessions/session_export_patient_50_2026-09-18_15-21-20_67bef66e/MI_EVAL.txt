# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental stance throughout. Slightly structured and occasionally leans toward a coaching tone rather than pure unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate perspective-taking and complex reflections that capture emotional core. Some reflections are slightly interpretive rather than purely empathic, and could go deeper into emotional pain at times.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear and consistent focus on the target behavior (reducing avoidance) with a logical session progression. Could benefit from explicit summarizing statements or a collaborative follow-up plan to push to a 5.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with frequent invitations for the client's perspective and co-construction of the action plan. Occasional subtle interpretive authority in reflections slightly shifts the power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support—explicitly affirms the client's freedom to choose, removes external pressure, and consistently reinforces client ownership without any hint of coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within the client rather than providing external reasons. The session ends with a client-generated commitment statement, the hallmark of successful evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert-driven stance with inclusive, egalitarian language. Some reflections carry a subtle interpretive authority that slightly shifts the balance away from true power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently evokes and amplifies change talk, catching and reinforcing the client's own DARN-C statements. Could be pushed to a 5 with more explicit labeling of change talk or a confidence scaling question.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating concerns without arguing and gently redirecting toward change. Could be strengthened with more strategic double-sided reflections to explicitly soften resistance.
