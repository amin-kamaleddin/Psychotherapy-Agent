# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and genuine unconditional positive regard throughout, with no judgment or minimization of Jordan's experience. Skillfully validates even self-deprecating statements (e.g., 'That's not small, Jordan') without ever implying he should feel differently.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced complex reflections that capture the specific quality of Jordan's emotional state rather than just paraphrasing content. Skillfully tracks subtle shifts, such as noting when Jordan is apologizing for his own goal or leaving a sentence hanging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from exploring stuckness to eliciting a concrete self-generated action plan, with each question building on the previous answer. Minor drift in the middle when exploring the protective function of 'it's not my fault' could have been redirected more quickly back to the 'small handle' thread.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed, with every question a direct response to Jordan's contributions and the direction shaped entirely by his answers. Hypotheses are offered as explorations to consider together, never imposed as diagnoses or interpretations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently explicit in positioning Jordan as the expert on his own experience, with language like 'I'm not here to talk you into anything' and 'just the first one.' Every question is framed as an invitation rather than a directive, and Jordan's right to say 'I don't know' is fully honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight and plan emerges from Jordan's own mouth, never provided by the psychiatrist. The session demonstrates a clear arc from vague stuckness to a specific, self-generated action plan entirely through evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently operates from a position of walking alongside rather than directing, explicitly rejecting the expert/fixer role from the outset. Jordan's answers determine the direction of each next question, making the session genuinely co-constructed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective skillful amplification of change talk, particularly when naming 'I've been meaning to' as evidence of a part that hasn't fully given up. Could have been stronger in the earlier stages by more explicitly eliciting change talk before the session had fully opened up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating the truth in it (e.g., 'You're right. It's not your fault') while exploring its protective function, which naturally opens the door to change without creating resistance. Could have incorporated more double-sided reflections to fully honor both sides of Jordan's ambivalence.
