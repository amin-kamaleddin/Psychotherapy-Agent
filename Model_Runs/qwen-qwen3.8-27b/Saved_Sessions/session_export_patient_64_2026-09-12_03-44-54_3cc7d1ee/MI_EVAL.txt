# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and uses normalizing, affirming language. Support is generally warm but somewhat formulaic rather than consistently deep.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture Jordan’s ambivalence and concerns accurately, but often stay at a surface level. There are missed opportunities to explore deeper feelings such as guilt, fear, or responsibility.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the flu vaccine decision and consistently guides Jordan toward a concrete action step. The counselor occasionally narrows too quickly into logistics, but direction is strong.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jordan is invited to make choices, and his preferences influence the session. However, the psychiatrist frequently generates the plan, scripts, and logistics, keeping the interaction counselor-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly offers options, respects Jordan’s refusal to share his address, and avoids direct commands. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming that the decision is entirely Jordan’s.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor draws out Jordan’s concerns and internal reasons, especially protecting his grandmother. However, the session often shifts to planning and advice rather than deepening client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites Jordan’s input throughout. Nevertheless, the counselor frequently assumes an expert or problem-solving role, limiting true power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, particularly around protecting Jordan’s grandmother. The counselor does not consistently amplify, reinforce, or develop this change talk further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Jordan’s reasons for resistance and acknowledges them without judgment. However, there is limited use of skills to reduce the pull of sustain talk or resolve ambivalence.
