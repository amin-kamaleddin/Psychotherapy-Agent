# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows a nonjudgmental, respectful, and normalizing stance. Acceptance could be deeper by affirming Jonathan’s worth or resilience beyond the diabetes task.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers strong reflective listening and captures both practical conflict and fear of failure. Some deeper emotions, such as resignation, exhaustion, and shame, are only partially explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on diabetes self-management and narrows the issue to pill-taking and daily routine. It could be improved by summarizing progress and guiding Jonathan toward a clear, client-chosen next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, uses inclusive language, and incorporates Jonathan’s ideas into the plan. The psychiatrist still leads much of the conversation with therapist-generated prompts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently emphasizes Jonathan’s choice, control, and lack of external pressure. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly stating that Jonathan does not have to commit to anything.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections are used, but they mainly evoke barriers and problem-solving rather than internal motivation. More exploration of values, confidence, past successes, or reasons for change would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Jonathan as the expert on his life and avoids lecturing or imposing an agenda. Partnership could be stronger by giving Jonathan more explicit control over the session agenda or priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jonathan offers some change-related language, but the psychiatrist does not frequently or strategically elicit change talk. More DARN-C focused reflections and questions could help increase Jonathan’s own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Jonathan’s sustain talk and occasionally reframes it constructively. The session still spends considerable time on barriers and failure, with fewer double-sided reflections that gently pivot toward change.
