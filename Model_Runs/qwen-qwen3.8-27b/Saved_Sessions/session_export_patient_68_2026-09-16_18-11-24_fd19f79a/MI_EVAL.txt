# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a consistently nonjudgmental and affirming stance throughout the session. Kevin's experiences were validated without labeling, moralizing, or minimizing his ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both content and underlying emotion, including Kevin's social anxiety and need to belong. The psychiatrist consistently went beyond surface statements to reflect deeper meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained focused on Kevin's alcohol use, safety, and concrete next steps. There was a clear, logical progression from values and concerns to a specific drinking limit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction felt dialogic rather than directive, and the psychiatrist built on Kevin's own language and ideas. However, collaboration could have been strengthened by more explicit agenda co-construction and invitations for Kevin to steer the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforced Kevin's ownership of his choices and goals. No pressure, urgency, or external agenda was imposed, and decisions were framed as Kevin's own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited motivation from within Kevin rather than providing external reasons or advice. Questions effectively drew out Kevin's values, insights, and desired outcomes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin was positioned as the expert on his own life, and the psychiatrist followed his leads well. The partnership could have been deeper with more explicit acknowledgment of Kevin's self-knowledge and shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-crafted open questions elicited meaningful change talk and helped Kevin move toward a concrete plan. The psychiatrist could further strengthen this by more explicitly affirming key change talk moments and exploring Kevin's confidence and ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged, normalized, and skillfully reframed without argument or dismissal. Examples include reframing the fear of ruining the night as protecting the part of Kevin that must live with consequences.
