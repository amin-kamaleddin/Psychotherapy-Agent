# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout. Never labels the patient, validates his perspective, and acknowledges his self-awareness in an individualized way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate perspective-taking that captures emotional and cognitive dimensions. However, reflections tend to stay at the surface level of stated emotion rather than exploring deeper undercurrents like shame or fear of parental disappointment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from minimizing to insight to a concrete plan. A couple of moments could be slightly more directed to maintain momentum, but overall the session flows strategically toward the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session feels genuinely co-created, with the psychiatrist building on the patient's contributions and following his lead. The patient's interests (soccer, social dynamics, his mom) consistently shape the conversation, demonstrating true equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the patient's ownership and self-determination without ever imposing a plan or using directive language. Even the soccer analogy is framed as a question rather than a prescription, and the patient's choice to not make a big promise is validated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits motivation from within the patient rather than providing external reasons, drawing out his own values and insights. Could push slightly further on evoking ability and confidence talk to build self-efficacy specifically.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates from a position of genuine partnership rather than expertise, explicitly positioning the patient as the expert on his own life. Every question is framed to elicit the patient's own wisdom, with a truly equitable power dynamic throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently identifies, reflects, and amplifies change talk using open-ended questions that draw out the patient's own motivations. Could have pushed slightly further on ability talk and commitment language to maximize the evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without dismissing or arguing, validating the patient's perspective while gently redirecting to his own goals. Uses double-sided reflections effectively, though one moment could have more explicitly reflected the legitimate fear of social exclusion before redirecting.
