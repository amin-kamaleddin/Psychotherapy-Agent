### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing Samir's presence and responding to defensiveness with specific, individualized affirmation rather than judgment. The self-correction after the 'endurance' comment and the closing line about Samir's silence both reflect deep respect for his frame and identity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Empathy goes well beyond paraphrasing, capturing not just content but the emotional quality of Samir's statements (e.g., detecting the self-deprecation in 'that's it'). The psychiatrist tracks subtle shifts in language with remarkable precision and is specific to Samir's voice, rhythm, and defensive patterns.

### Direction

**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on medication adherence while organically broadening to emotional content, and the closing brings a natural, client-defined conclusion. The primary gap is the lack of an explicit bridge from the 'sort it out' disclosure back to the medication target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is built on a genuine collaborative framework, with the psychiatrist consistently positioning themselves as a listener and witness rather than an expert. Samir's ideas substantially shape the session's direction, and the power dynamic remains equal from start to finish.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never uses directive language and explicitly validates Samir's autonomy in choosing how much to engage, including honoring his definition of 'useful' as simply being told to go home. The closing reinforces that Samir retains ownership of his experience independent of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully draws out Samir's own language and motivations through open questions and evocative scaling, leading to a significant emotional breakthrough. The gap is that the 'sort it out' disclosure was not fully developed into medication-specific evocation, leaving a natural bridge unexplored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that Samir is the expert on his own experience, inviting him to define the terms of the interaction rather than imposing a therapeutic agenda. The statement 'What would actually make this useful to you?' and working within Samir's definition of usefulness exemplify this stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively identifies and reinforces moments where Samir moves toward openness, reframing sustain talk as embedded change talk (e.g., 'part of you still wants this to work'). The limitation is that the session did not elicit or develop medication-specific change talk, which would have been a natural next step from the 'sort it out' material.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every instance of sustain talk is met with validation, reflection, or gentle redirection without argument, and the psychiatrist consistently finds change talk embedded within resistance. The self-correction after 'endurance' is a textbook example of rolling with resistance and acknowledging their own overstep without defensiveness.
