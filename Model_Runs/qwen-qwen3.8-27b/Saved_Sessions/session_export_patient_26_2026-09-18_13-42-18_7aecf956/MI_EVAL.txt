### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warmth, nonjudgment, and normalization throughout the session. Occasionally slightly formulaic and lacks explicit affirmation of Daniela's worth independent of her struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with complex, layered reflections capturing the emotional weight of each disclosure. Occasionally stays at the surface level rather than exploring deeper underlying themes like self-critical patterns or fear of being 'too much.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintains a relatively clear focus with natural flow from stressor to routine to solution. Could more strategically connect the childcare solution to the specific smoking/drinking reduction goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent co-construction with Daniela generating all solutions and the psychiatrist building on each contribution. The power dynamic is consistently balanced with the psychiatrist serving as a facilitator rather than an expert.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently puts decision-making in Daniela's hands through open questions and never prescribes solutions. Could be more explicit in affirming her right to choose (e.g., 'whatever you decide, that's your call').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits internal motivations through strategic questions that draw from within rather than providing external justifications. Could explore values more explicitly and use scaling questions to deepen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent non-hierarchical stance with frequent use of questions and allowing Daniela to generate her own solutions. Occasionally takes on a slightly guiding/interpretive role that positions the psychiatrist as seeing the shift more clearly than Daniela.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evokes and reinforces change talk through strategic questions and reflections that amplify Daniela's own insights. Could more explicitly label and celebrate her change talk to strengthen momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles Daniela's concerns well without dismissing, arguing, or minimizing them. Could use more double-sided reflections to actively pivot away from sustain talk and highlight the underlying motivation.
