# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates Leila’s goals and boundaries. Acceptance is sometimes more formulaic than deeply normalizing, especially when she expresses fear of starting and quitting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are helpful but often stay at the content level rather than exploring underlying feelings. The counselor misses key emotional material around past inconsistency, frustration, and worry about repeating past patterns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains an excellent and consistent focus on the target behavior. It efficiently moves from identifying the focus to concrete, behavior-specific steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor and Leila co-construct the plan, with her preferences clearly shaping the final choices. However, the counselor still supplies much of the structure and drives the problem-solving agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently invites choice, seeks permission, and respects Leila’s desire for a small, flexible plan. Some directive language and pre-made details slightly limit Leila’s full ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses some evocative questions about confidence, preference, and feasibility. Evocation is limited because the session does not consistently draw out Leila’s deeper motivations, values, or self-efficacy beliefs.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor conveys a collaborative stance and invites Leila’s input at multiple points. The session is not fully partnership-based because the counselor still provides much of the structure and content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some desire, confidence, and commitment language from Leila. The session misses opportunities to deepen change talk around values, importance, and identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor generally avoids arguing with Leila’s barriers and adapts the plan to her stated limits. Responses are often practical rather than using MI-specific reflections to explore ambivalence.
