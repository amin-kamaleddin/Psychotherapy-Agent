# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating of Aisha’s workload and stress. The stance is supportive, though it is practical rather than explicitly affirming Aisha’s worth as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Aisha’s ambivalence, exhaustion, and fear. Some responses lean toward interpretation or problem-solving rather than deeper emotional validation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on evening smoking and concrete coping steps. Direction is limited by implicit agenda-setting, lack of alcohol discussion, and an unanswered final question.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Aisha’s ideas clearly shape the plan and conversation. Collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist still guides through consecutive questions and suggestions rather than fully joint agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly avoids pressure, directive advice, or undermining language. Aisha’s choices, limits, and realistic plan are consistently treated as hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits Aisha’s own motivations, concerns, and coping ideas without providing reasons for change. Evocation is solid but could more fully connect to values, identity, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Aisha as the expert on her own life. Her ideas about the pause, limit, water, and pack placement drive the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Aisha’s reasons, goals, and small steps. Change talk is strong, but confidence and commitment language are not consistently amplified or deepened.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Concerns about quitting, low willpower, and cravings are handled without argument or resistance. Sustain talk is softened well, though it remains central and the final question is not redirected.
