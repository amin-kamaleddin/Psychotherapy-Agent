# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with multiple affirmations of Samuel's self-awareness and effort. Warmth is measured and professional rather than deeply warm; lacks explicit normalization of difficulty or statement of unconditional acceptance regardless of outcome.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with complex reflections capturing both content and emotional nuance, including sophisticated understanding of Samuel's ambivalence. Reflections occasionally feel more like sophisticated summaries than deep emotional reflections; does not explore the emotional weight of long-term smoking history or identity aspects.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with progressive build from pattern identification through to a concrete action step, with no topic drift. Focus is somewhat narrow, staying on the single break scenario without periodically zooming out to the broader cessation trajectory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model of collaboration between equals, with Samuel's ideas directly shaping session direction and the psychiatrist contributing structure, reflection, and reframing without overriding. Clear sense of two people working together, with neither dominating the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Samuel designs his own test, timing, self-talk, and definition of success, with the psychiatrist never prescribing, advising, or pressuring. Final line communicates complete non-judgment regardless of outcome, exemplifying full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits motivation from within Samuel rather than providing external reasons, with powerful questions that draw out intrinsic motivation and reflections of his own growth. Could have more deeply explored underlying values and identity to evoke more profound and durable change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Samuel, explicitly placing knowledge of his workday rhythm and patterns in his hands. Session is entirely shaped by Samuel's contributions with no moment of assuming an expert role or offering unsolicited advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes substantial change talk through questions designed to elicit Samuel's own language about testing, data, and self-talk, with a powerful final summary reinforcing his agency. Could have more explicitly elicited confidence language or explored values and identity to amplify change talk further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by validating concerns and reframing them from threats into data rather than arguing or minimizing. Sustain talk around the ritual aspect of smoking could have been explored more deeply with a double-sided reflection.
