# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warm, unconditional positive regard, never labeling Matteo or expressing disapproval. Acceptance is genuine rather than formulaic, treating him as a whole person exactly where he is.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with complex reflections that capture both sides of ambivalence simultaneously. The psychiatrist consistently reflects feelings underneath the words, not just content, showing deep attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on alcohol use with a logical progression from rapport to exploring impact to evoking goals. The ending feels slightly open rather than purposefully directed toward a concrete next step or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuinely collaborative dynamic where the conversation feels like two people thinking together. The psychiatrist uses 'I' statements for observations, follows Matteo's lead consistently, and maintains an equal power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model-level autonomy support with zero pressure, urgency, or implied obligation throughout. The psychiatrist consistently removes the power dynamic, never uses directive language, and keeps all decisions in Matteo's hands.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation with every piece of motivation drawn from Matteo's own mouth through open questions and reflections. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change or information, making this textbook evocation at the highest level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Matteo as the expert on his own life, never taking over the conversation or offering unsolicited advice. Questions are genuinely curious and reflections are offered as observations rather than imposed interpretations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through open questions, eliciting Matteo's own recognition of the shift and his goals for change. Could have pushed further on confidence/ability talk or specific steps in the final moments of the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by affirming the client's perspective while gently highlighting the change talk that emerged in the same breath. Could have used double-sided reflections more explicitly in a couple of moments to be more strategic.
