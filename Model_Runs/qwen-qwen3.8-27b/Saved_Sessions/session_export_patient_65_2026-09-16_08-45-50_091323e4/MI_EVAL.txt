# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and consistently validates Julian’s concerns. Acceptance is solid but remains somewhat formulaic and task-focused rather than fully individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Julian’s worries about documentation, deadlines, and social discomfort. However, reflections do not deeply explore shame, loss of autonomy, or fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays strongly focused on completing the court-ordered hours, documentation, location, and deadline. Direction is primarily logistical rather than motivational.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is collaborative language, but the psychiatrist supplies most templates, scripts, and plans. Julian mostly selects from counselor-generated options rather than contributing his own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites choice and respects Julian’s reluctance to practice or make calls. The repeated provision of structured options can still keep Julian in a more passive role.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Questions mainly elicit practical preferences rather than Julian’s internal motivation, values, or confidence. The counselor frequently provides solutions instead of drawing out client-generated change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses partnership language and invites Julian into planning. In practice, the counselor drives the session more like a practical consultant than an equal partner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is little evocation of desire, ability, reasons, need, or commitment. When Julian offers change-relevant statements, the psychiatrist often responds with practical support rather than amplifying the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and reduces the burden by offering simpler options. However, there are few double-sided reflections that honor reluctance while pivoting toward Julian’s goals.
