# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates Amina's resilience and practical thinking without blame. Acceptance is somewhat concise and task-focused, without deeper affirmation of her emotional experience or parenting role.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Amina's ambivalence and practical barriers, especially the gap between getting called and attending. Empathy remains more logistical than emotionally deep, with limited exploration of disappointment or fear of letting others down.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is tightly focused on the target behavior and moves efficiently from barrier to concrete step, backup plan, and follow-up. Momentum is maintained without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor proposes options while letting Amina select, revise, or reject them, so her ideas substantively shape the plan. The interaction functions as a shared problem-solving partnership rather than advice-giving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently offers choices, asks for preferences, and avoids pressure, reinforcing that Amina is in charge of the plan. She respects Amina's decision to wait for Mrs. G's answer before extra preparation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently draws out Amina's own solutions and first steps, making the plan client-generated. Direct suggestions such as drafts and checklists reduce the score, and the evocation is stronger for action than for intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina's contributions guide the session, and the counselor adapts to her preferred wording and timing. The counselor still holds considerable agenda and method control, though the interaction generally functions as collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor reinforces forward-moving statements such as progress and doing the right things. However, change talk is not consistently deepened through evocative questions about values, confidence, or identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges practical barriers and reframes them as manageable while offering backup options, preventing hopelessness. It would be stronger with double-sided reflections or deeper ambivalence work.
