### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist remains respectful and nonjudgmental, affirming Jamal’s efforts without moralizing. Warmth is present but somewhat formulaic, which limits the depth of unconditional regard.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Reflections are accurate but brief and somewhat repetitive, capturing stress without fully exploring underlying shame, fear, or ambivalence. Emotional depth is limited.

### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Jamal is invited to choose and modify lines, showing some collaboration. However, the psychiatrist frequently introduces multiple scripted options, keeping the agenda largely clinician-led.

**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers choices and invites Jamal to select options, supporting autonomy. Repeated prescriptive scripts and a detailed plan partially reduce client-led decision-making.

### Evocation

**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some of Jamal’s own reasons, such as protecting performance and avoiding feeling drained. However, evocation remains superficial and is often followed by advice rather than deeper exploration.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Inclusive language and invitations to choose create some partnership. The session is still fairly clinician-driven, with the counselor steering content and solutions.

**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms and occasionally elicits change talk, including limit-setting and wanting to protect team standing. There are missed opportunities to deepen desire, ability, and confidence language.
