# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently showed warmth, respect, and nonjudgment even when Kareem was ambivalent or hesitant. Statements such as “low-pressure plan” and “no failure” supported Kareem without pressure or evaluation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflected Kareem’s concerns, hopes, and practical perspective, helping him feel understood. However, reflections often stayed at the content level rather than exploring deeper feelings, identity, or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained tightly focused on smoking cessation, workplace policy, readiness, coping, and concrete next steps. The counselor guided the conversation toward change without losing focus or drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor and Kareem worked together as partners, with the counselor offering options and Kareem selecting or modifying the plan. The final plan was shaped by Kareem’s preferences, experiences, and comfort level.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently honored Kareem’s choice, avoided pressure, and supported his right to decide his own pace. Noncommitment, limited reminders, and optional next steps were all accepted respectfully.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently drew out Kareem’s own motivations, past strategies, and preferred next steps. Evocation could be strengthened with deeper questions about values, confidence, and future-oriented change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor positioned Kareem as the expert on his own experience by asking for his perspective and permission before offering information. Kareem’s input substantially shaped the direction and content of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor created space for Kareem to articulate reasons, hopes, and possible steps, producing useful change-related language. Change talk could be more consistently deepened using explicit DARN-C follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoided arguing with or amplifying Kareem’s ambivalence and redirected it toward manageable options. However, the counselor did not consistently use stronger softening techniques such as double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
