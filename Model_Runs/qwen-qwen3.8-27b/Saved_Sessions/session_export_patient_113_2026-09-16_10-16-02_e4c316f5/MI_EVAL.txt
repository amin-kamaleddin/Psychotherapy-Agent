# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally shows respect and nonjudgment, using supportive statements when Rowan expresses ambivalence. The opening framing felt mildly corrective, and more normalization of resistance would strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Empathy is present but remains mostly surface-level, capturing practical frustration without fully exploring underlying feelings. Deeper reflection of fatigue, resentment, fear of failure, or burden would be needed to reach a higher level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior of charging and using the device. Direction is strong but somewhat logistical, with less emphasis on exploring ambivalence, values, or motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaborative problem-solving, especially in selecting a small step and arranging a check-in. However, the psychiatrist does much of the generating and structuring, limiting fully client-led collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently preserves Rowan’s choice and respects his limits. The repeated specific suggestions, though low-pressure, become somewhat prescriptive and could reduce autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions are used early, but the conversation quickly shifts to practical planning. More follow-up questions about values, confidence, and reasons for change would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Rowan’s input influences the plan, but the interaction remains fairly clinician-led. The psychiatrist could allow Rowan to lead more consistently from his own priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are attempts to elicit change talk, but it is not consistently developed or amplified. The session would benefit from scaling, importance/ability questions, and deeper follow-up on Rowan’s willingness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Rowan’s resistance and acknowledges the burden. However, sustain talk is not consistently softened through balancing or strategic redirection.
