# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental with explicit declarations of unconditional positive regard. Slightly over-affirming at times, which risks feeling performative rather than purely accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening with textbook complex reflections that honor both sides of ambivalence simultaneously. Demonstrates deep attunement to Elena's precise emotional and experiential reality throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Follows Elena's lead organically but lacks strategic guidance toward a specific target behavior or focus. Feels more like a supportive conversation than a directionally guided MI intervention with clear momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structured as genuine joint exploration with no hidden agenda or predetermined plan. Session direction is shaped by Elena's real-time contributions, and the closing frames the interaction as shared time between two people.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly removes pressure and affirms Elena's right to choose throughout, with standout statements validating both options. Elena's final response shows she has genuinely internalized the autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses genuinely evocative questions and affirmations that highlight Elena's own agency and resources. Could reach deeper into values and future-oriented motivation to fully develop change talk to its potential.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Elena as the authority on her own experience, inviting her to correct or confirm understanding. Never assumes the expert role or provides unsolicited information.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk that emerges is largely Elena's own, which is appropriate, but the psychiatrist's role in actively eliciting and expanding it is underdeveloped. Lacks direct evocative questions and deeper values exploration that could build a stronger foundation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates sustain talk while gently expanding the frame without directly contradicting Elena. Could be more explicit and strategic with double-sided reflections to help Elena see her positions can coexist more productively.
