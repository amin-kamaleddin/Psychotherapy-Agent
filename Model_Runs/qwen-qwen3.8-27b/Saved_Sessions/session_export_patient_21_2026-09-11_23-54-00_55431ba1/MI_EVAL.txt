# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone. Warmth is slightly task-focused and does not consistently affirm Camila as a whole person beyond the physical activity goal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is present but inconsistent. The counselor captures general ideas but often moves quickly to advice rather than reflecting emotional nuance such as ambivalence, tiredness, or self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear, concrete, and achievable physical activity goal. Direction is strong, though sometimes achieved through directive advice rather than MI-consistent collaboration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Camila contributes important choices, such as 5 minutes, Thursdays, and using a notebook. Collaboration is limited because the psychiatrist frequently supplies options, structures, and correction plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges choice and avoids making the goal an obligation. Autonomy support is mixed because frequent directive language and prescriptive recommendations reduce emphasis on Camila’s ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is a good MI opening and some eliciting questions, but much of the session is externally guided. The psychiatrist provides reasons and strategies rather than consistently drawing out Camila’s own motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Input is occasionally invited, but the session is largely expert-led. The counselor quickly provides strategies, plans, and cognitive reframing rather than waiting for Camila’s own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Camila generates some change talk by choosing 5 minutes and committing to Thursdays. The counselor does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen this change talk to strengthen internal motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk about low energy and fear of failure is addressed, but mainly through advice and behavioral plans. MI-consistent softening, such as double-sided reflections and strategic reframing, is limited.
