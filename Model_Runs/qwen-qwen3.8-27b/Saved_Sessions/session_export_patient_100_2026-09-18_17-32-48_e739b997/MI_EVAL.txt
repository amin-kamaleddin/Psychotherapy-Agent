### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance with the conversation shaped by Maya's responses rather than a predetermined agenda. Power dynamic is clearly equal, feeling like a genuine partnership rather than a clinical encounter.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never pressures for change or implies obligation, consistently leaving decision-making with Maya. Avoids the expert advice pitfall by acknowledging gaps rather than jumping in with suggestions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
