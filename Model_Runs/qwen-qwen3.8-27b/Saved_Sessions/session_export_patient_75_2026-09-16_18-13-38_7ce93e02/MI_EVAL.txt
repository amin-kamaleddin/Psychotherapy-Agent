# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone while normalizing Linda's concerns. However, explicit affirmations of her effort, courage, or worth were limited, keeping this below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used accurate and complex reflections of Linda's ambivalence and goals. It fell short of a 5 because it did not fully reflect the emotional intensity of her fear, uncertainty, and vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on asthma management and inhaler adherence, moving from values to practical strategies. It would be stronger with an explicit summary or agenda-setting to consolidate the emerging plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treated Linda as the expert on her experience and co-created potential plan elements. More explicit joint decision-making and agenda negotiation would strengthen the collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided pressure and invited Linda to choose what would feel safe and helpful. Direct language affirming her freedom to choose and control over next steps was not explicit enough.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited Linda's own values, concerns, and ideas rather than providing reasons for change. Deeper evocation of confidence and self-efficacy would make it more skillful.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a collaborative stance by inviting Linda to contribute to the plan and avoid imposing a standard approach. More explicit power-sharing and agenda negotiation would improve this.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited meaningful change talk by asking Linda to generate her own plan. However, it was not consistently reinforced, amplified, or reflected back in a motivating way.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged Linda's sustain talk without arguing or dismissing it. More effective softening would use double-sided reflections and address the unprocessed fear and uncertainty near the end.
