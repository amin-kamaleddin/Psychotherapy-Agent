# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, explicitly removing judgment and validating Amina's self-awareness without being condescending. Warmth is genuine and individualized, responding specifically to what Amina says rather than offering generic affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate, consistent, and layered understanding of Amina's perspective, including complex reflections that hold contradictory truths simultaneously. Every reflection adds depth rather than simply paraphrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a generally clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from triggering event to deeper understanding. The session could benefit from a clearer sense of where it is heading and a collaborative next-step plan before closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Amina's input and active building on her contributions. However, the psychiatrist occasionally introduces frames (e.g., 'billion-dollar attention machine') that Amina then has to work with rather than co-creating them, and doesn't explicitly check in about whether the session is heading where she wants it to go.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently reinforces Amina's ownership and right to choose, explicitly attributing plans to her and never imposing advice or pressuring commitment. Amina's autonomy to disagree, be resistant, and challenge interpretations is consistently respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws out Amina's own motivations and insights through questions and reflections, avoiding providing reasons for change. Could be more systematic with scaling questions or structured evocation techniques to deepen confidence and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative stance with language signaling partnership and explicit credit for Amina's own insights. Minor lapses where the psychiatrist is slightly more directive than ideal, introducing their own framing or making interpretive observations that could feel like analyzing rather than sitting alongside.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes significant change talk including a concrete plan, self-identified loop, major cognitive shift, and a clear self-diagnosis. However, the psychiatrist doesn't fully explore Amina's confidence, ability, or reasons for her specific action, and the change talk isn't consolidated into a commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully using sophisticated double-sided approaches that validate concerns while distinguishing between fear and evidence. The psychiatrist never reinforces sustain talk and consistently finds the 'grain' in resistance that points toward change.
