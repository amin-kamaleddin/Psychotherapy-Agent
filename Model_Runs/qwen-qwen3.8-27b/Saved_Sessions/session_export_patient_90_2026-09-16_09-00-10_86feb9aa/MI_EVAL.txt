# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating stance throughout the session. Acceptance is strong but somewhat procedural rather than deeply emotionally affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections to capture Marcus’s practical burdens and ambivalence. Empathy remains surface-level and does not fully explore the deeper emotional meaning of the dog or fatigue.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on asthma improvement and a concrete bedroom-boundary plan. The session stays strategically focused without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The final plan is co-constructed with Marcus actively choosing and modifying steps. Collaboration is strong, though the counselor still supplies much of the structure and language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites choice and avoids imposing expensive or burdensome interventions. Autonomy support is occasionally weakened by provided scripts, reminders, and directive summaries.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses importance/confidence rulers and implementation questions to elicit some change talk. Evocation is limited by early advising and a lack of deeper value-based exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marcus contributes meaningfully to the plan and session direction. Partnership is uneven because the counselor introduces options and structure before fully exploring the client’s priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some practical change-oriented statements, such as committing to a one-week trial. The client’s change talk remains mostly logistical rather than values-driven or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges Marcus’s concerns about money, fatigue, and attachment to the dog without arguing. Sustain talk is softened through practical reframing, though deeper emotional ambivalence is not fully resolved.
