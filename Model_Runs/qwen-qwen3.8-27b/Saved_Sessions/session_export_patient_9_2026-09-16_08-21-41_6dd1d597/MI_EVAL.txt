# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Amina’s distress, often normalizing her concerns. Some statements are mildly evaluative or directive, preventing a fully unconditional acceptance score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections capture Amina’s exhaustion and protective motivation well. Later reflections become more surface-level and miss deeper conflicts such as guilt, fear of abandonment, or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on reducing arguing in front of the son and managing co-parenting conflict safely. The focus occasionally drifts into detailed scripting, but the target behavior stays clear.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina contributes preferences and choices, showing some collaborative participation. However, the psychiatrist often supplies specific solutions and scripts before fully eliciting Amina’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for Amina’s preferences at several points, supporting choice and control. Prescriptive suggestions, such as timers and specific exits, reduce Amina’s sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The use of rulers and early open-ended questions helps elicit readiness and confidence. The session later becomes more advice- and script-based rather than exploring Amina’s values and reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and includes Amina in decisions, but the session shifts toward expert-led planning. This reduces the sense of equal partnership and shared problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina expresses clear change talk, and the psychiatrist uses readiness and confidence rulers to access motivation. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or build on Amina’s own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles Amina’s concerns respectfully and does not argue against them. However, the session spends a lot of time on obstacles and contingency planning rather than gently redirecting toward values and self-efficacy.
