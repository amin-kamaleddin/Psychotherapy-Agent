# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout; validates feelings without frustration or pressure. Maintains a professional warmth rather than reaching the depth of deeply individualized unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with complex reflections that capture the client's ambivalence, emotional weight, and self-criticism with precision. Demonstrates deep understanding of the client's inner experience throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, purposeful arc moving from broad goals to specific, actionable insights without drifting off-topic. The final moments could benefit from stronger consolidation of what was discovered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative with explicit checking-in, following the client's lead, and co-constructing meaning throughout. The power dynamic is clearly equal, with the client substantially shaping the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses invitational language, explicitly removes pressure, and frames all exploration as the client's own discovery. Never imposes an agenda or pushes for commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation that consistently draws out the client's own motivations, values, and insights rather than providing external reasons for change. The psychiatrist never argues for change or tells the client what to value.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently defers agenda-setting to the client, checks understanding, and positions the client as the expert on their own experience. Never takes over the process or assumes expertise over the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through targeted questions and reinforces it with affirming language. Could more explicitly label and amplify the client's change talk to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by acknowledging without reinforcing, reframing as self-awareness, and gently challenging minimization. Could use double-sided reflections more explicitly to balance the sustain talk.
