# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, validating tone throughout with permission-seeking before suggestions. Slightly formulaic in repetition and limited in deeper affirmation of the client's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequately reflected frustration and fear of escalation at a surface level. Lacked emotional nuance regarding deeper hurt, isolation, or the ambivalence between wanting fairness and avoiding conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation of client-generated change talk (e.g., wanting to ask the teacher, wanting to not care as much). Missed opportunities to amplify these statements or explore confidence and personal reasons for deeper change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a strong collaborative style by adjusting scripts and options based on the client's feedback and preferences. Remained somewhat clinician-driven with predefined strategies rather than fully joint exploration of client goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear strength, with repeated invitations for choice, permission-seeking, and respect for stated boundaries. Withheld a 5 because many options were still preformulated and longer-term client ownership of the change process was not consistently emphasized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation of the client's perspective and practical preferences in opening questions. Evocation was often superficial and frequently interrupted by psychoeducation and problem-solving suggestions rather than drawing out deeper values or motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Collaborative stance evident through choices and invitations to adapt language. Limited by the degree to which the clinician supplied most of the content (scripts, breathing, texts), making the session more clinician-driven than co-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client produced some change language around wanting to ask the teacher and not care as much. Did not consistently reflect back or build on these statements to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Multiple sustain talk instances (didn't want to apologize, avoid counselor, prefer to leave) were validated in ways that reinforced avoidance and externalizing. Little strategic softening, double-sided reflection, or gentle pivot back toward resolving ambivalence.
