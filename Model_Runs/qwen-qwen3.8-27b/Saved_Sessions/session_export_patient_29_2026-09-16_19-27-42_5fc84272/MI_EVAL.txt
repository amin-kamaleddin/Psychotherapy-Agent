# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, validation, and nonjudgment toward David’s stress and ambivalence. The warmth is strong and affirming, with only a minor limitation in being somewhat efficient rather than deeply expansive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, with accurate tracking of David’s goals, metaphors, and underlying doubt. Some reflections remain more cognitive than emotional, and the session could go deeper into fear of losing control or emotional cost.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, coherent focus on smoking cessation and stress-trigger management. It moves logically from broad goals to concrete plan steps without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and David co-construct the plan as equal partners, with David’s ideas shaping each next step. The interaction strongly avoids an expert-driven or directive stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language consistently preserves David’s choice, including framing medication as something he would test rather than a requirement. The client remains clearly in charge of designing and evaluating his own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits David’s own solutions, definitions, and success criteria rather than instructing. Core motivational content is client-generated, keeping motivation internal and strong.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** David is consistently treated as the expert on his own experience, with decision-making authority placed with the client. The psychiatrist avoids premature prescribing, lecturing, or assuming the best solution.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, client-centered questions elicit desire, ability, and need statements, and David generates useful solution talk. The session could strengthen this criterion with more explicit amplification of change talk and direct elicitation of confidence, readiness, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged, normalized, and treated as understandable rather than argued with. This keeps barriers from dominating while redirecting the conversation toward a workable, client-led plan.
