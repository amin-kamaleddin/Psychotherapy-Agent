# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist never labels, shames, or dismisses, even when Liyana discloses guilt-eating cycles or self-deprecating thoughts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally nuanced reflective listening that tracks emotional shifts and names cognitive patterns (e.g., identifying the shift from 'it doesn't count' to 'I'm not worth the effort'). The psychiatrist consistently reflects emotional weight rather than just content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from frustration → emotional impact → identifying the loop → behavioral exploration, with no drift into unrelated topics. The transition from heavy emotional disclosure to practical exploration could have been more gently bridged for a 16-year-old.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative language throughout with a genuinely equal power dynamic; Liyana's contributions (the 'guessing' insight, the 'enough' question) substantially shape the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong non-directive stance with open questions and explicit statements like 'you're the expert on your own body.' Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming Liyana's right to choose otherwise and by avoiding subtly leading framing toward the 'eat more' solution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Liyana's own motivations, insights, and past experiences through evocative questions. Could go deeper in eliciting confidence/ability statements, values-based change talk, or scaling questions for readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides with the patient and frames everything as collaborative exploration. Could be deepened by more explicitly negotiating the agenda or pausing to confirm direction feels right for Liyana.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively names and validates change talk (e.g., 'I always come back. That's not small.') and uses strong evocative questions to elicit hope. Could more explicitly evoke confidence/ability statements or use summary reflections to consolidate the change talk that emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Limited sustain talk present in the session; the key fear about gaining weight appears at the very end without a response, so the skill has not yet been fully tested. The psychiatrist appropriately does not reinforce sustain talk but has not yet had a strong opportunity to demonstrate active softening.
