# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific, tailored affirmations rather than formulaic responses. Consistently normalizes and validates the client's resistance as a reasonable response rather than a character flaw.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate complex reflections that capture underlying motives, patterns, and meanings beyond surface-level paraphrasing. Precisely tracks and reflects the emotional arc, including protective mechanisms and learned responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with logical progression from frustration to concrete solution. Falls slightly short due to the lack of an explicit consolidation or summary of the co-created plan at the session's end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is genuinely co-constructed, with the client's specific questions, proposed solutions, and criteria directly shaping where the session goes. The psychiatrist acts as a facilitator rather than a director, with a genuinely equal power balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly reinforces the client's ownership and right to choose, preempting autonomy threats and ceding control of solutions. The entire session design is non-directive, with the client generating his own answers and standards.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every insight, solution, and commitment in the session is generated by the client, drawn out through evocative questioning. The progression from resistance to a self-generated plan with self-defined criteria is entirely the client's own work.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, explicitly ceding the expert role. Every significant insight is attributed to the client, and the session direction is substantially shaped by his contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently evokes and expands change talk by highlighting the client's implicit commitments, amplifying self-insights, and reinforcing his capability. Skilfully names and celebrates shifts in real time to deepen the client's awareness of his own progress.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with exceptional skill, acknowledging it without reinforcing it and naturally pivoting toward change. Validates the underlying need (e.g., autonomy) while redirecting it as a path toward the desired change rather than arguing against it.
