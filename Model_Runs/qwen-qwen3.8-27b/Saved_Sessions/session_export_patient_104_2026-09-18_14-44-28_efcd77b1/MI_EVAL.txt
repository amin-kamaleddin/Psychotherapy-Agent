# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, unconditional positive regard throughout with no judgment or dismissal. Validation is specifically tailored to Nadine's experience as a new parent feeling isolated and self-doubting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture the emotional core of Nadine's experience and consistently go one step beyond what she said, adding depth and meaning. The final reflection on 'cross-referencing in the dark with no real anchor' is particularly skillful.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear progression from diffuse worry to specific concern to articulating what information would resolve the concern, with no topic drift. The psychiatrist gently redirects when self-blame threatens to pull focus away from the practical question.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The dynamic is genuinely equal — Nadine is an active participant in defining the problem and solution rather than a passive recipient. The psychiatrist's role is clearly positioned as a thinking partner rather than an authority figure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never tells Nadine what to think or do; instead facilitates her own reasoning and ownership of her healing process. Explicitly asks Nadine to define her own criteria for reassurance rather than imposing what would count as 'enough.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation and insight from within Nadine rather than providing it externally. Helps Nadine recognize her own shift and growth, which is the hallmark of skilled evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Nadine as the expert on her own experience and baby. Nadine's contributions substantially shape the session, and the power sharing is genuine rather than performative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively helps Nadine move from diffuse worry to specific, actionable requests and recognizes her own progress. Could be slightly more explicit in reinforcing her confidence and problem-solving capacity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles self-doubt well by acknowledging the worry without amplifying it and reframing toward competence. Sustain talk is relatively mild in this session so the criterion is somewhat less tested.
