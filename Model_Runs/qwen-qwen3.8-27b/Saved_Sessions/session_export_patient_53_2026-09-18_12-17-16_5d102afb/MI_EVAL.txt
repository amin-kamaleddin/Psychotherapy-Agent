# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating without minimizing client's experience. Slightly formulaic in places and could more explicitly normalize ambivalence as a natural part of change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and layered reflective listening throughout, capturing both emotional content and underlying meaning with precision. Textbook empathic responding that tracks shifts in the client's speech in real time.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from rapport through identifying the specific barrier. Pacing is appropriate for the client's contemplation stage, though could have moved slightly further toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance reinforced through joint problem-solving language and following the client's lead. Question selection occasionally feels guided by the clinician's strategic plan rather than emerging purely from the client's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout, consistently reinforcing the client's ownership and right to choose. Never pressures or implies obligation; reframes even sustain talk as an expression of the client's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within the client rather than providing external reasons for change. Session content emerges from the client's own speech, not the clinician's agenda.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert on her own experience and invites her to define terms and generate solutions. Could be elevated by more explicit power-sharing moments, such as asking the client how they'd like the clinician to respond.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully highlights and amplifies emerging change talk, including a strong summary of the client's shift from avoidance to tentative action. Could have more consistently elicited additional change talk with targeted questions throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframes sustain talk as protective rather than as an obstacle, without ever arguing or using 'but.' Could be more strategic with double-sided reflections to explicitly pivot toward change talk while acknowledging the concern.
