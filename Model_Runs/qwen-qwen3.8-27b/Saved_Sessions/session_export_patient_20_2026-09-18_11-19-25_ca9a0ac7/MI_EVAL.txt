# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental stance with validation rather than correction throughout. Stays at the level of validating behavior rather than deeply affirming Brandon as a whole person beyond the presenting concern.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong complex reflections that capture emotional undertones and dualities, such as naming the frustration beneath minimization. Occasionally remains at the cognitive level without fully exploring the deeper identity threat and shame beneath statements like 'I'm a decent human being.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol with a logical progression from referral reason to concrete goal. Lacks a deliberate strategic arc that builds toward a specific consolidated outcome or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint exploration using questions rather than directives and invites the client's perspective before offering reflections. Client's ideas do not substantially reshape the session's direction in real-time to demonstrate deeper power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional and natural autonomy support throughout, consistently affirming Brandon's right to choose without pressure or persuasion. Statements like 'that's your call to make' and 'you don't have to have an answer' are embedded in the overall stance rather than performed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Draws out some concrete goals and produces a partial admission about alcohol and sleep, but does not systematically explore values or connect them to the change goal. Lacks strategic follow-up techniques such as importance rulers or deepening of emerging change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and positions the work as joint discovery. Could be deepened with more explicit acknowledgment of the client's expertise and client-driven direction of the session's focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk including concrete goals and a partial admission about alcohol and sleep, but does not consistently build on or amplify it. The session ends with the client back in sustain talk without a final strategic push to consolidate the change talk that emerged.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates rather than argues, using excellent double-sided reflections like asking whether the beer makes the 'done' feeling better or deeper. Could be applied more systematically, as some sustain talk passes without being used as an opportunity for a gentle pivot.
