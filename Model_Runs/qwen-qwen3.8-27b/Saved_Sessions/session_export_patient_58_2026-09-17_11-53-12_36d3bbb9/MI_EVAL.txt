# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong affirming reframe about effort being erased and validation of capability, but affirmations are clustered in specific moments rather than woven continuously throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate complex reflections capture emotional weight, but two consecutive identical responses break momentum and one reflection slightly over-interprets what Jamal stated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from exploration to concrete step, but the 'what's the point of school' question is never strategically addressed and identical responses suggest loss of direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language but the conversation feels one-directional with scripted identical responses; the small step of setting the alarm feels therapist-directed rather than fully client-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong autonomy-supporting language including 'I'm not here to add another person telling you what to do' and powerful questions returning control to Jamal, but could more explicitly affirm his right to choose a different approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some good evocative moves present, but inconsistent use of scaling questions, summaries, and strategic follow-ups; generic responses to clear change talk miss opportunities to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good partnership statements early on and respects client expertise, but identical responses and lack of explicit agenda negotiation or goal co-construction weaken the collaborative feel.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked through good questions, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect back, or deepen the change talk when it appears, relying on generic responses instead.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Significant weakness; sustain talk about school meaninglessness and mom's nagging is never adequately addressed, reframed, or strategically redirected, leaving it dominant throughout the session.
