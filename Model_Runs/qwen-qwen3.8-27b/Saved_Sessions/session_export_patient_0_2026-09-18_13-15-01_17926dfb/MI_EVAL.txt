# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with phrases like 'You know yourself best' and gentle pushback on self-deprecation. Occasionally feels slightly rehearsed and could go deeper in affirming Adrian's courage in sharing vulnerable things.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with notable captures of deeper meaning, especially behind the word 'quiet' and the identity question. Some reflections are slightly formulaic and occasionally stay at the surface of emotion rather than fully inhabiting it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from referral/infection through ambivalence to loneliness to identity, maintaining strategic focus on the target behavior. Could benefit from more explicit navigational markers and articulation of shared session goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing with responsive reflections and co-construction of meaning, frequently checking in with Adrian's perspective. The psychiatrist still drives most directional choices rather than fully negotiating agenda with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently invitational rather than directive, never pressures or implies Adrian 'should' do anything, and respects his right to minimize. Could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming ownership of both the decision and the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocative questions that draw out Adrian's own motivations, values, and self-awareness without providing external reasons for change. Could more consistently expand on and develop the change talk once it appears, such as after the weightlifting mention.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a strong collaborative stance, positioning Adrian as the authority on his own experience and avoiding an expert-driven approach. Could be elevated by more explicitly co-creating the agenda and visibly letting Adrian's contributions redirect the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicits several important change talk statements including 'I want to be more than that' and the weightlifting reference. Does not consistently amplify, reflect back, or strategically develop this change talk, limiting impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating without arguing, acknowledging valid points before gently redirecting. Could have used double-sided reflections more explicitly in a couple of moments to better acknowledge both the low frequency and the impact.
