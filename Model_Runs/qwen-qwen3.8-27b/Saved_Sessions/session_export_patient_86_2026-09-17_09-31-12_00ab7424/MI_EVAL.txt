# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently communicates warmth, respect, and nonjudgment, even when the client is skeptical or dismissive. Language avoids disapproval and reinforces the client’s dignity and safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects the client’s focus on control, autonomy, and practical dental concerns. Empathy could be stronger by more consistently exploring deeper emotions such as frustration, weariness, or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and purposeful focus on a specific, manageable behavior tied to dental health. The counselor effectively prevents drift while keeping the conversation connected to the client’s stated goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor builds the plan from the client’s own language, ideas, and preferences. Collaboration could be strengthened by more explicitly sharing agenda-setting and next-step decisions with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly affirms the client’s right to choose, ownership, and self-direction. Advice-giving and pressure are avoided, which supports a strong sense of personal agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor successfully elicits the client’s own reasons for change without supplying external justification. There is room to more deeply evoke values, confidence, and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invites the client’s perspective and shapes the session around his practical concerns. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming the client as the authority on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently elicits and reinforces change talk, including a concrete client-generated step. It could be stronger by more consistently amplifying confidence, desire, and ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing or pressuring when the client expresses resistance or skepticism. More active use of double-sided reflections and rolling with resistance could elevate this further.
