# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental tone throughout; never challenges the client's self-identity or uses labeling. Warmth is steady but remains somewhat clinical and procedural rather than deeply personal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate reflections of the client's frustration and perspective. Does not deeply explore underlying emotions such as loneliness, external pressure, or fear of losing control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session maintains general focus on drug use but lacks a clear progressive arc toward a specific change goal. By the end, the concrete target behavior and next step remain undefined.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and invites the client's perspective at each turn, treating him as a partner. Collaboration is genuine but somewhat therapist-guided rather than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently places decisions in the client's hands and never pressures him to admit a problem. Could be more explicit in validating the full range of possible choices, including continuing to use.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some client-driven motivation around values of self-control and functional identity. Lacks systematic exploration across DARN categories and does not build a robust change talk foundation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and explicitly positions the client as the expert on his own life. Remains somewhat one-directional in that the psychiatrist controls the flow without explicit agenda negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk around the client's values (e.g., not wanting to lose function or control). Does not systematically elicit change talk across Desire, Ability, Reasons, or Need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles the client's sustain talk well by affirming his confidence while introducing nuanced distinctions without arguing. Effectively avoids challenging or disputing the client's assertions of control.
