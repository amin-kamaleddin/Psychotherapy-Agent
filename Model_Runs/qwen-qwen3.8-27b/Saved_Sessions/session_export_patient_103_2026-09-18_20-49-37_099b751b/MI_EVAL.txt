# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Nabil's frustration without judgment. However, warmth occasionally feels formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic accuracy with effective metaphors and reflective summaries. Stays primarily at the cognitive level, missing opportunities to access deeper emotional layers such as shame or isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear, logical progression from validation through pattern exploration to forward-looking agency. Session ends somewhat abruptly without a clear directional closure or bridging to a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a genuine joint exploration dynamic where Nabil's contributions shape the conversation. Slightly one-directional in that the psychiatrist consistently drives the pace and asks the next question.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently keeps the ball in Nabil's court and never pressures him to commit to change. Could more explicitly affirm his right to choose NOT to change and further separate his identity from the behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks genuine evocative questions that draw out meaningful self-exploration, but follow-up is inconsistent. Evocative questions are often asked once and then replaced by the psychiatrist's own interpretation rather than layered follow-up.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a non-authoritative, collaborative stance with inclusive language and no unsolicited advice. Could strengthen by more explicitly acknowledging Nabil's expertise in his own life experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Nabil produces several instances of change talk (e.g., 'I don't want to be the angry guy') that are reflected but not amplified or deepened. Missed opportunities to follow up with questions that build momentum toward agency.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing but inadvertently reinforces the externalization of blame in places. The pivot toward personal agency is effective but comes late and is the primary softening strategy used.
