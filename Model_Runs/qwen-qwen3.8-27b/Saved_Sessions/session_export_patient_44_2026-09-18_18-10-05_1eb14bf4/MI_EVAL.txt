# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates unconditional positive regard throughout, never questioning or minimizing Erik's feelings. Affirmations are genuine and individualized, with no judgment or disapproval present anywhere in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures both the cognitive and emotional dimensions of Erik's experience with remarkable precision. Reflections go beyond paraphrasing to honor dual experiences simultaneously and track subtle emotional shifts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, coherent arc from comparison feeling through to a specific behavioral plan for Tuesday. It is held at 4 due to the lack of a closing summary integrating multiple insights and no explicit bridging to broader weight loss and diet goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation flows as a genuine dialogue rather than a Q&A format, with Erik's responses organically shaping the next question. The psychiatrist builds on Erik's own metaphors and framework rather than imposing an external one.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every strategy is explicitly credited to Erik as his own discovery, and the psychiatrist never imposes a plan or frames suggestions as expert recommendations. Erik's pace and uncertainty are respected without pressure for greater commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently asks questions that draw out Erik's own motivations, insights, and strategies without providing answers. Every key insight in the session — the reframe, phone strategy, and 'I got there' mantra — originates entirely from Erik.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Erik is positioned as the expert on his own experience, with the psychiatrist acting as a skilled guide who asks and reflects rather than directs. The language is consistently inclusive and non-hierarchical throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits and reinforces Erik's own DARN-C language at virtually every turn, progressively deepening change talk through targeted questions. Erik's shift from 'a kid trying to keep up' to 'it feels lighter' is explicitly highlighted and amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Erik's doubts and expectations of awkwardness are acknowledged and then gently reframed as self-knowledge rather than obstacles. The psychiatrist never argues against or dismisses sustain talk, instead weaving it into the plan as a feature.
