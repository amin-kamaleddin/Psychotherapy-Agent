# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard, never dismissing or minimizing Liyana's feelings while consistently affirming her worth separate from performance. The response to self-deprecating thoughts is validation rather than correction, and the tone is warm and genuinely supportive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that moves beyond simple parroting to capture emotional nuance, including complex dual reflections naming both surface frustration and deeper fear. Demonstrates deep attunement to underlying meaning, relational dynamics, and the client's emotional state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and logical progression from rapport-building through exploration to insight, with the target behavior remaining central throughout. Docked one point because the session ends with identification of the guilt cycle but lacks a concrete next step or collaboratively identified action focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative throughout, with every major insight co-constructed from the client's own words rather than imposed by the psychiatrist. Consistently seeks the client's input at decision points and maintains a genuinely equal power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally strong autonomy support with repeated explicit affirmations that the client is in charge of her own decisions, pace, and boundaries. Never uses directive language and frames every suggestion as an invitation or question rather than a requirement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out the client's own motivations and insights through well-crafted exploratory questions, particularly the 'more vs. differently' distinction. Could have been more targeted at eliciting specific change talk language such as importance, ability, or commitment statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook partnership stance established immediately and maintained throughout, with consistent use of exploratory questions that position the client as the expert on her own experience. The psychiatrist never takes over, provides unsolicited advice, or positions himself as the one who knows the answer.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through reflections that amplify the client's own change-oriented language and help her recognize her own shift in thinking. The session could have gone further in explicitly eliciting ability talk or importance talk before concluding.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with notable skill by validating feelings without reinforcing unhelpful beliefs, and connects resistance to positive values like fairness. Docked one point because the session ends before fully helping the client reframe the guilt cycle that sustains the 'not built for this' belief.
