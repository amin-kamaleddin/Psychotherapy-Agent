# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistently warm, respectful, and affirming language throughout. Validates the client's burden, normalizes without judgment, and affirms courage with specific, genuine expressions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflective listening with sophisticated attention to subtle linguistic distinctions (e.g., 'not ready' vs. 'never want to'). Captures the client's internal mechanisms without oversimplifying.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent strategic arc that progressively moves from exploration toward a concrete, manageable action. Each step builds logically on the previous one with no wandering or loss of focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaboration that consistently builds on the client's specific contributions rather than imposing a clinical framework. Could push further toward explicit co-creation and acknowledging the client as an equal partner in the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy through open questions and by drawing out the client's own motivations, values, and possibilities. Never imposes directives or prescribes solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Almost never provides reasons for change or tells the client what to think. Uses a consistent pattern of well-timed open questions that draw out the client's own motivations and possibilities.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clearly collaborative stance using 'I'm curious' framing and consistently following the client's lead. Could be slightly more explicit in acknowledging the client as the expert on her own life and in power-sharing language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently creates space for change talk through well-timed open questions and affirming the client's own insights. Could more explicitly amplify certain change talk moments, particularly identity-level statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with notable skill, never dismissing or arguing against it while subtly redirecting. Uses textbook dual reflections and precise reframing to honor sustain talk while keeping change talk alive.
