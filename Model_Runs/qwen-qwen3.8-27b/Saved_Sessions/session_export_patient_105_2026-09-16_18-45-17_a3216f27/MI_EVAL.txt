# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Naomi’s ambivalence and avoids judgment. Warmth remains somewhat task-focused and less explicitly affirming of her worth independent of change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Naomi’s anxiety, shame, and need to appear competent. Some deeper emotional themes are touched but not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on managing anxiety in professional photography settings. Each turn guides Naomi toward a concrete, manageable behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs solutions rather than lecturing, and invites Naomi to evaluate wording. The counselor still leads much of the strategic structure and proposes several options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and non-pressuring language support Naomi’s choice and uncertainty. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming her ownership and right not to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi’s own words and motivations are frequently elicited, supporting self-directed problem-solving. The evocation remains more behavioral than values- or confidence-based.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently invites Naomi’s perspective and lets her ideas shape the plan. The interaction can still feel somewhat therapist-guided due to structured suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi generates useful change statements about being organized and capable despite anxiety. Change talk could be further developed around values, confidence, and future success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument, including the perceived trap of asking for help. The final avoidance statement is left unresolved, limiting the full softening effect.
