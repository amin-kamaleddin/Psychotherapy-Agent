# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard throughout; warmth feels genuine and individualized, never wavering into judgment or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections capturing both content and emotional meaning, including the underlying tension between autonomy and vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear strategic arc with purposeful momentum, moving logically from exploration to a concrete, client-generated plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine conversation between equals; follows Evelyn's lead on metaphors and co-constructs the final plan with her.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout feature; consistently reinforces Evelyn's ownership and decision-making with zero directive or pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All meaningful change language is Evelyn's own; the psychiatrist never provides reasons for change or health information unprompted.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise consistently positioned within Evelyn; session direction is substantially shaped by her contributions and framing.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Excellent elicitation of desire, reasons, and commitment language, but change talk is not consistently amplified or reflected back when it appears.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
