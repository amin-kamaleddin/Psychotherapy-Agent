# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong unconditional positive regard throughout; validates choices, absorbs resistance without defensiveness, and normalizes Richard's guardedness in the closing. Slightly scripted 'safe space' opening keeps it from a full 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy — consistently reflects deepest emotional undercurrents (autonomy, identity, fear of being controlled) rather than surface content. The 'nobody talks to you' and 'learned to watch the door' reflections are masterful.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session moves through several themes but lacks strategic progression toward a specific focus or next step. Ends where it began, with no collaborative summary or small forward goal identified.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative language and non-hierarchical tone throughout, with genuine course-correction when Richard pushes back. Primarily collaborative in style rather than structure — Richard doesn't actually redirect the agenda or co-create a plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Flawless and consistent autonomy support; never pressures, directs, or implies obligation. The closing 'You kept it. That's your call' is textbook MI autonomy affirmation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Well-constructed open evocative questions, but responses tend to reflect rather than deepen or follow up on Richard's disclosures. The 'thinking about it' near-slip is acknowledged but not leveraged to elicit further change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear partnership stance with explicit power-sharing, a genuine correction ('you didn't sign up for that'), and consistent respect for Richard as the authority on his life. Slightly asymmetric — counselor still leads the conversational architecture while Richard remains largely reactive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges the near-slip and validates Richard's autonomy around it, but does not explore what 'thinking about it' might mean or connect his stated autonomy value to a possible change in behavior. No commitment language or explicit change-relevant summary by session end.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled rolling-with-resistance when Richard rejects the 'trade' framing; validates without reinforcing and pivots cleanly. Misses an opportunity to more fully separate Richard's own potential interest from his wife's agenda in the sustain talk about her 'pushing harder.'
