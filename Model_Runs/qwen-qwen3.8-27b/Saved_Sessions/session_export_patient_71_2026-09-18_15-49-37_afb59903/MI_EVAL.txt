# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard with no judgment or disapproval throughout. Validations are warm, genuine, and individualized rather than formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, deep reflective listening that captures emotional nuance and underlying meaning, not just content. Reflections consistently move the conversation deeper rather than repeating surface-level statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on the target behavior with an appropriate arc from surface behavior to deeper process. Could have been slightly more strategic in summarizing themes or collaboratively identifying a next step before ending.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative behavior with language signaling genuine curiosity ('Can I ask,' 'I'm curious,' 'I'm wondering'). Session direction is substantially shaped by Laura's contributions, with the psychiatrist following rather than steering.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Laura's autonomy and perception of choice without ever imposing solutions or creating urgency. Laura is positioned as the authority on her own life and the agent of any change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong focus on eliciting Laura's own motivations and insights rather than providing reasons for change. Slightly lighter on evoking ability talk (her capacity for self-awareness and strengths) compared to the strong emphasis on reasons and costs.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within Laura, using exploratory questions directed at her own knowledge. Reflections are framed as invitations to deeper self-exploration rather than interpretations imposed from outside.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates change talk through open questions that elicit Laura's own motivations and insights. Could have more explicitly reinforced and amplified Laura's strongest change talk moments (e.g., 'the marinating is getting expensive').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk without pushing or dismissing, using masterful double-sided reflections. Could have explored the sustain talk about 'not being ready' a bit more by explicitly acknowledging legitimate reasons while maintaining curiosity toward change.
