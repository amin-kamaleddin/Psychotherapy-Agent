# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warm, nonjudgmental acceptance and normalizes Linda's concerns. However, the tone remains somewhat professional and procedural rather than deeply personal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening to capture both practical and emotional impact. Some reflections could have gone deeper into underlying fears or emotional pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on asthma management and inhaler use with clear progression toward an actionable plan. There is only minor drift into extended psychoeducation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda's input is invited and some choices are incorporated into the plan. However, the psychiatrist remains the primary source of information and plan design.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linda is given choices and autonomy is acknowledged at several points. Some directive language and pre-made plans reduce her sense of ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Early open questions elicit some change talk, but the session quickly shifts to providing medical information and reasons for change. Linda's own motivations are not consistently explored throughout.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some partnership behaviors, such as asking for Linda's input on doable steps. The overall approach remains expert-driven rather than truly collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some initial change talk about goals and desired changes. This change talk is not consistently reinforced or expanded upon later in the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Linda's sustain talk, such as fear of dependence and steroid concerns, is mainly addressed with factual reassurance. MI-consistent techniques like double-sided reflections or ambivalence exploration are largely absent.
