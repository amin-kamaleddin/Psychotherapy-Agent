# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, acknowledging Marco's suffering and resilience without judgment or saccharine tone. When Marco resists being framed as having a breakthrough, the psychiatrist accepts his framing without pushing back.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional nuanced reflective listening, including complex reflections that capture the emotional weight of sustained ambivalence and precise metaphors (e.g., 'water rising slowly') that honor Marco's own language. Each reflection adds a new dimension of understanding rather than repeating or formulaic responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally clear focus on smoking cessation with a logical progression from ambivalence to a concrete action plan. Slight drift in the mid-session where the question about what the 'knowing' costs day to day is somewhat tangential to the behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a genuine collaboration between equals, with the psychiatrist acting as a thinking partner rather than a director. Every actionable idea comes from Marco, and his contributions substantially shape the session's direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Marco's ownership and right to choose, never using directive or prescriptive language. When Marco explicitly declines to make a commitment, the psychiatrist accepts his terms completely without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Marco rather than providing external reasons for change, using open questions that draw on his own history and self-knowledge. The evocation is progressive, moving from broad curiosity to specific behavioral planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise is explicitly placed with Marco throughout ('That's your expertise talking, not mine'), and the psychiatrist never gives advice or prescribes a plan. The power dynamic is clear and consistently maintained as a joint problem-solving exercise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally skilled at evoking and amplifying change talk, including catching subtle language shifts (from 'if I was' to direct description) and reframing sustain-talk into useful self-knowledge. Systematically evokes desire, ability, need, and values-based change talk while reinforcing internal locus of change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argues with or reinforces sustain talk; instead, respects Marco's frame while gently opening doors (e.g., 'What would make the maths work for you?'). Consistently finds the thread of curiosity within sustain talk to pull toward change without pushing past the client's stated position.
