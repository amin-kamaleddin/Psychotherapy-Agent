# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated nonjudgmental respect and validation without blaming or pressuring the client. Minor refinement could include more explicit affirmation of the client's worth independent of behavioral outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate and clinically useful reflections captured the client's exhaustion, avoidance, ambivalence, and emerging hope. The empathy went beyond content and meaningfully tracked underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on the target behavior and progressed logically toward a concrete, sustainable action plan. Momentum was maintained by consistently asking the next question that advanced the plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction functioned as a partnership of equals, with the plan co-constructed from the client's ideas and constraints. The psychiatrist avoided an expert-driven or instructive stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice, experimentation, and self-determination were strongly reinforced throughout. The plan was framed as optional and low-pressure, with no coercion beyond the client's stated willingness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and implementation strategies were consistently elicited from the client rather than supplied by the psychiatrist. Minor refinement could include more explicit eliciting of values-level motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positioned the client as the expert on his own life, schedule, and constraints. The session was shaped by the client's contributions rather than imposed by the psychiatrist.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly elicited the client's own reasons for change, preferences, and commitment language. Change talk was generated naturally rather than supplied externally.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently normalized and reframed in autonomy-supportive ways without argument or minimization. Barriers were steadily converted into manageable, low-stakes options.
