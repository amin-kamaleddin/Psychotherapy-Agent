# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizing stance throughout. It falls short of a 5 because positive regard is often delivered with formulaic phrases such as "that makes sense."

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections are consistent and generally capture both content and feeling. However, several key statements are paraphrased rather than deeply reflected, leaving underlying shame or self-judgment unexplored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and builds toward a concrete plan. The main limitation is that momentum is often therapist-driven, and there is no brief closing recap.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interaction is conversational and the client's choices influence next steps. However, the psychiatrist frequently supplies data, tips, and options, which makes the collaboration feel less fully client-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently supported through permission-asking, choice language, and an explicit right to stop. Frequent suggestions and prep questions create a mildly directive undercurrent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and scaling successfully draw out some change talk and client-generated ideas. The effectiveness is diluted by repeated informational and advisory input that shifts the counselor into an expert role.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client contributes meaningfully to the target, days, and selected strategies. The psychiatrist often assumes the generative, expert role, which limits the sense of equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited through scaling and some targeted questions. The counselor often supplies the content of the plan, so the client's own reasons and confidence are not deeply amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not reinforced and is met with neutral acceptance plus a small experiment. Active softening techniques such as double-sided reflections or discrepancy work are largely absent.
