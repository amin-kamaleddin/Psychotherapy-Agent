# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout with no moments of judgment or disapproval. Kevin's identity concerns and absolute statements (e.g., 'I'm not gonna quit') are fully accepted and honored rather than challenged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that consistently capture the emotional and relational meaning beneath Kevin's words (identity concerns, fear of being an outsider, loss of social energy). Reflections are varied in type and progressively deepen understanding of the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear and logical focus on the target behavior with a coherent arc from resistance to strategy exploration to identifying specific barriers. However, the session ends abruptly without a clear summary, consolidation, or collaborative next-step planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is fully client-driven, with Kevin's ideas and responses determining the trajectory at every turn. The psychiatrist acts as a skilled facilitator rather than a director, using collaborative language throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional autonomy support, most notably in fully accepting Kevin's boundary ('quitting's off the table, and I respect that. That's yours.') rather than gently pushing back. The psychiatrist never uses prescriptive language and consistently cedes control of session direction to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within Kevin, with all insights and solutions emerging from the client rather than being provided externally. Could be stronger by more explicitly evoking Kevin's values and connecting them to the change goal, and by building more strategically on his own language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Kevin, using explicit statements ('you know your life better than I do') and crediting him with his own insights. The psychiatrist never assumes the role of expert or provides unsolicited advice or solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes substantial change talk through well-timed evocative questions and reflective amplification. Could be stronger by explicitly summarizing and consolidating Kevin's change talk at a pivotal moment and exploring confidence or self-efficacy scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by accepting it fully and redirecting (e.g., 'quitting's off the table... let's figure out what this IS about'). Could be more strategic by using explicit double-sided reflections that acknowledge the pull of sustain talk while gently turning toward change.
