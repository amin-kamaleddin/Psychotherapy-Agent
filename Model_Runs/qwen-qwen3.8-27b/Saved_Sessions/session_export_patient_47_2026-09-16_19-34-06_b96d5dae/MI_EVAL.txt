# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, non-judgmental stance and avoids labels or verdicts about Evelyn's drinking. Acceptance is strong, though it could be warmer and more explicitly affirming of Evelyn as a person beyond her behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures Evelyn's desire for clarity, her concern about the fall, and her worry about losing her social life. Empathy could be deepened by exploring the emotional meaning behind her ambivalence more directly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use and moves logically from units to a typical week to a possible change. Direction could be strengthened by more consistently connecting the math to risk, values, and a concrete change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses Evelyn's preferred approach, follows her sequence, and lets her choices shape the session. Collaboration could become more equal by co-creating the agenda and outcome more explicitly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evelyn is given options and is not pressured, with clear language about pace and avoiding labels. Autonomy support could be stronger by explicitly naming Evelyn as the ultimate decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk, especially around dropping the cider or reducing to one glass. Evocation remains somewhat mechanical and could better draw out values, confidence, and deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive, non-hierarchical language and offers choices rather than directives. Partnership could be more explicit by clearly positioning Evelyn as the expert on her own life and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited and affirmed, particularly when Evelyn proposes a smaller change. The session could more skillfully amplify change talk using DARN-CAT and link it to her values and safety concerns.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by normalizing Evelyn's feeling that the guideline is tight or unfair. A higher score would require more strategic double-sided reflections that link her social life to health and safety.
