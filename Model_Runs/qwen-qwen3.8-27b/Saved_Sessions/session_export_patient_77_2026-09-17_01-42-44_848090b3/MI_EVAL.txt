# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows warmth, respect, and validation without judgment. Acceptance is strong but could more clearly affirm worth independent of performance and directly respond to the final self-critical statement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures feelings of isolation, exhaustion, and ambivalence. Some deeper emotional themes, such as shame, self-hatred, and fear of failure, are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays relevant to confidence, health, and weight loss, but lacks a specific target behavior or clear change plan. The focus broadens into general emotional support rather than anchoring to a concrete goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses the patient's language and builds on her ideas, such as the five-minute timer. At times, the therapist leads more than co-constructs, especially when suggesting specific self-questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The patient is positioned as the expert on her own body, with limited pressure or directive advice. Some autonomy support is slightly reduced by therapist-suggested questions and limited explicit reinforcement of personal choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively draw out the patient's values, hopes, and small-step ideas. The evocation is frequent but remains somewhat surface-level and does not fully develop deeper motivation or self-compassion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The stance is collaborative and non-authoritarian, with the patient's perspective invited throughout. Partnership is slightly weakened when the psychiatrist offers specific suggested questions rather than allowing the patient to generate them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk emerges around future hopes and small steps, but it is not strongly developed. More use of scaling, confidence, reasons, and commitment language would strengthen the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing, and self-critical statements are reframed gently. The use of double-sided reflections is limited, and the patient's final statement about pushing through remains unresolved.
