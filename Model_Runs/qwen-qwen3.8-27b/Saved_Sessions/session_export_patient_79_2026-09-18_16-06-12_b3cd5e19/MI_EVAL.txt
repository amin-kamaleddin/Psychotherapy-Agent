# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently non-judgmental and warm, never labels Luis as non-compliant or difficult. Explicitly normalizes his resistance (e.g., reframing 'I'm not a kid' as a desire for autonomy).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond paraphrasing to capture underlying themes of control, trust, and cumulative burden. Accurately summarizes Luis's stated needs and the cyclical nature of his experience with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical arc from general frustration to a concrete 3am plan, maintaining focus on the target behavior. Could be slightly tighter by reducing time in exploratory space before redirecting toward the behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Luis's ideas consistently shape the session's direction and the final plan is entirely his own articulation. No moment where the psychiatrist imposes an agenda or redirects away from the client's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly frames change as Luis's own choice throughout, using language like 'YOUR decision' and 'if you were writing your own plan.' Never uses directive language or implies failure by non-compliance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external reasons for change or medical statistics; instead, every question is designed to draw out Luis's own motivations and values. The 3am plan question is a masterful evocative question that respects autonomy while targeting behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Immediately inverts the traditional doctor-patient power dynamic by positioning Luis as the expert on his own life. The psychiatrist never offers medical advice or directives, consistently inviting Luis to be the authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-constructed evocative questions effectively elicit instances of change talk from Luis. Could be stronger by more explicitly amplifying and reinforcing the change talk Luis produces, and by eliciting confidence statements about following through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles defensiveness and resistance effectively by reframing sustain talk as understandable rather than confronting it. Could benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections or strategic redirection at peak resistance moments.
