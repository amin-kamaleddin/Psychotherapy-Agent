# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and affirms the client’s practical choices. Warmth is sometimes brisk and procedural rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers adequate reflective listening and validates frustration around the weight cycle. However, empathy remains cognitive and task-focused, with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions such as guilt, comfort, or self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on reducing orange juice consumption as the target behavior. Direction could be strengthened with more concise summaries and less broad, multi-option advice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works collaboratively by inviting the client’s input and building on his own ideas. However, the clinician’s advisory role and ready-made scripts slightly reduce the sense of full partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by offering options and reinforcing the client’s no-guilt approach. The specific script and structured strategies can subtly shift the session toward clinician-led advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits strong confidence and change talk through scaling and next-step questions. However, direct advice and concrete scripts limit consistent drawing out of the client’s own deeper motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The client clearly influences the plan, especially around smaller containers and the Saturday treat. The psychiatrist also steps into an expert role by providing a detailed script before fully exploring the client’s preferred approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes desire, ability, and commitment by asking about importance, confidence, and doable steps. At times, specific suggestions limit opportunities for the client to expand his own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with the client’s love of orange juice and acknowledges the difficulty of giving it up. However, the sustain talk is more redirected toward practical options than deeply processed with balanced or double-sided reflections.
