# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance. However, at times the response was overly reassuring, such as saying Kevin was "just unlucky," which may have minimized the role of heavy drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Reflective comments were present but mostly stayed at a surface level. The psychiatrist missed deeper opportunities to reflect Kevin's ambivalence, values, and emotional conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stayed focused on the presenting issue, but it often shifted toward practical problem-solving. A clearer, collaborative change target would have strengthened the focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were some collaborative moments, such as asking Kevin what felt doable. However, the psychiatrist often led the conversation and provided most of the ideas and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist showed some respect for Kevin's autonomy by asking for his input and accepting his preference to keep things low-key. Autonomy support was inconsistent because the advice was often prescriptive and detailed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There was limited effort to draw out Kevin's own motivations, values, and reasons for change. The session relied more on external advice than on evoking Kevin's change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist included some collaborative questions, but often took an expert-driven role. Kevin may have experienced the session as receiving advice rather than partnering in problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Kevin offered some small change-related statements, but the psychiatrist did not consistently evoke, amplify, or expand them. Missed opportunities included exploring what "being smarter" or "taking it easy" meant to Kevin.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Sustain talk was frequently normalized or agreed with rather than reflected and redirected. Statements such as "you weren't irresponsible, just unlucky" may have reinforced Kevin's minimization of risk.
