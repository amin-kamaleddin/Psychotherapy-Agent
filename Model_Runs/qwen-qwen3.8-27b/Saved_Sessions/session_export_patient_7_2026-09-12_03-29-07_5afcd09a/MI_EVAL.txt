# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental stance and respects many client choices. However, repeatedly delivering a scripted safety message after the client asks not to hear it weakens acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate paraphrases and acknowledges the client's frustration. Empathy stays surface-level and repetitive rather than deepening the emotional meaning of the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session follows a repetitive loop of offering options, receiving refusal, and offering more options. There is little strategic progression, agenda-setting, or forward movement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites client input. In practice, the counselor remains the main driver, and the client's boundaries do not substantially reshape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist verbally affirms the client's choices and avoids coercion. The repeated menus and repeated safety script partially undermine autonomy by continuing to structure the client's responses.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some open-ended questions but keeps them focused on immediate choices rather than deeper values or motivations. The questions do not effectively draw out client voice or self-exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the interaction as collaborative and respectful of client control. The repeated structured options make the process feel counselor-led despite the partnership language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not actively evoke desire, ability, reason, or need language. A clear missed opportunity is not affirming the client's agency in showing up despite resistance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with the client's resistance but does not skillfully reframe or deepen it. Sustain talk is tolerated with brief reflections rather than used as an opening for exploration.
