# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Amina's experience and normalizes her ambivalence without judgment or shame. Positive regard could be stronger with more explicit affirmations of her effort and courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, especially in capturing Amina's ambivalence about connection, rest, and quiet. Some deeper emotional undercurrents, such as loneliness, boredom, or self-judgment, are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on reducing social media use to protect sleep, study, and focus. Each question and suggestion is clearly connected to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and builds on Amina's own ideas, metaphors, and proposed solutions. However, the interaction remains more therapist-guided than fully co-led, with less explicit invitation for Amina to set the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina is encouraged to make her own choices, and the final intention emerges from her rather than being imposed. Some therapist-suggested options may still subtly narrow her sense of open choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions help Amina generate her own change ideas, such as turning off notifications, using timers, and trying a three-night plan. Evocation could be deeper by more consistently drawing out values, confidence, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Amina's lead and treats her as the expert on her own life. Partnership could be stronger by reducing therapist-proposed options and emphasizing mutual direction-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes multiple change statements and concrete action plans from Amina. Change talk could be further developed by explicitly strengthening desire, ability, need, values, and readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina's reasons for continuing the behavior are acknowledged without argument or reinforcement. The session could be stronger by more deliberately linking the current habit to its costs for sleep, study, and values.
