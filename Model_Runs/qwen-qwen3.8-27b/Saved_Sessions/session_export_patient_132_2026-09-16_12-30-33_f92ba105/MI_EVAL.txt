# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Yasmin's ambivalence without shame. Some warmth is more functional than deeply person-centered, missing a few opportunities to affirm courage and self-awareness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early complex reflections are strong, but empathy becomes less consistent as the session shifts into planning. Important feelings about guilt, control, identity, and the final scale ambivalence are not fully reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on moving from all-or-nothing dieting to a small, sustainable plan tied to energy and stress. It sometimes becomes overly detail-oriented and lacks a strong final values-based integration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Yasmin's preferences clearly shape the plan, reminder wording, and session direction, creating a genuinely collaborative process. The clinician still introduces several concrete suggestions before fully eliciting Yasmin's preferred level of structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently affirms choice, avoids pressure, and respects Yasmin's decision to keep the plan minimal. More autonomy support could be shown by asking how much structure she wants before offering specific suggestions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some effective questions to draw out Yasmin's preferences, readiness, and concerns. However, frequent advice-giving and practical problem-solving interrupt deeper evocation of values, confidence, and intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with Yasmin actively choosing the focus, reminder language, and scope of the plan. The psychiatrist still carries some expert-driven content, so the partnership does not fully reach the highest level.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Yasmin offers meaningful change talk, and the psychiatrist occasionally affirms and reinforces it. The counselor often shifts too quickly into advice and plan construction, limiting deeper elicitation and amplification of change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Yasmin's weight concerns and uses several helpful reframes such as data rather than failure. The core ambivalence around the scale remains under-processed and repeatedly returns to the surface of the conversation.
