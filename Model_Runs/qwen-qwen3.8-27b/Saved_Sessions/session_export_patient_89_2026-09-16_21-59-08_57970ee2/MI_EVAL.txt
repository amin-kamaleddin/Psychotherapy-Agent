# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect and nonjudgment while accepting Marcus's ambivalence and past struggles. Warmth is somewhat concise rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses strong reflective listening and accurately captures ambivalence and emerging motivation. Emotional exploration could be deeper, especially around shame, frustration, and fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on increasing physical activity related to energy and blood pressure. The conversation moves purposefully from ambivalence to a concrete, realistic plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Marcus's ideas and allows his contributions to shape the session. The therapist still carries some of the conversational structure and repeatedly frames the goal in terms of energy and blood pressure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice with open questions and frames the plan as Marcus's own. There are occasional subtle directives or repeated anchoring to specific goals that slightly limit open choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Marcus's own motivations and change language rather than providing reasons for change. The therapist could more fully amplify identity, values, and confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Marcus as the expert on his own life and invites him to generate options. Full power sharing is not complete because the therapist still structures the conversation and introduces specific options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits meaningful change talk, including a realistic morning plan and self-compassionate self-talk. The response could be strengthened by explicitly developing confidence and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without judgment and gently redirects it toward small, manageable steps. Some responses stay with the concern rather than more actively reframing or minimizing its impact.
