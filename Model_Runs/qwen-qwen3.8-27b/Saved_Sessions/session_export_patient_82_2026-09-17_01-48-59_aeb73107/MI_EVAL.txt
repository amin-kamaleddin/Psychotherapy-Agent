# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures ambivalence and uses the client’s language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation of client ideas, but not enough deliberate expansion of change talk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative and builds on client input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor provides space and choice, though explicit affirmations of client decision-making could be stronger.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions are used, but deeper elicitation of values, confidence, and change reasons is limited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a non-hierarchical, exploratory stance, though the session remains somewhat clinician-structured.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some change talk but does not consistently amplify or expand it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids dismissing the client’s concerns but does not frequently redirect sustain talk toward change.
