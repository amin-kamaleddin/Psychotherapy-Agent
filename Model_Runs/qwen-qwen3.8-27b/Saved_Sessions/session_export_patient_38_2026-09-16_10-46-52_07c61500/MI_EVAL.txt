# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, patient, and nonjudgmental, consistently affirming that Elena can proceed at her own pace. Acceptance is strong but can feel somewhat procedural or generic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects key feelings such as relief, fear, stress, and the desire to keep things simple. However, empathy remains somewhat surface-level and misses opportunities to explore deeper ambivalence and emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on contraception and preventing another pregnancy scare, moving toward a small, concrete step. Some content is broad, and stronger strategic direction would link more explicitly to Elena's values and goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates with Elena in choosing reminder wording, keeping the plan small, and stopping when she is ready. However, the counselor frequently supplies options and structure, making the interaction less fully co-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elena is consistently positioned as the decision-maker, with clear statements that she does not have to decide now and can set her own pace. Autonomy support is strong, though repeated suggestions can still subtly steer the conversation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses helpful preference-based questions that help Elena identify a manageable step. Evocation is limited by frequent psychoeducation and a tendency not to draw out deeper motivation or self-belief.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and respects Elena's choices regarding reminder, condoms, and stopping the session. Partnership is uneven because the counselor often leads with expert information before fully centering Elena's perspective.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena offers meaningful change talk, but the psychiatrist often responds with information or planning rather than amplifying her motivation. Skills such as rulers, importance/confidence questions, and expanding her language about safety are not consistently used.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles Elena's sustain talk without arguing, moralizing, or reinforcing resistance. However, the counselor does not consistently use double-sided reflections or actively help explore ambivalence.
