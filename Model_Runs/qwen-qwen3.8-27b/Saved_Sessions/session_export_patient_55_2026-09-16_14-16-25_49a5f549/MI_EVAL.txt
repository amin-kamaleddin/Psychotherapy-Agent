# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and creates a sense of safety. The warmth is somewhat procedural and lacks deeper affirmations of the client’s worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, capturing content and emotional nuance effectively. It does not consistently explore deeper feelings such as hurt, unfairness, or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stays coherent around stress and sleep but drifts from the high-risk alcohol use target. There is little effort to connect sleep, stress, or coping to alcohol risk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative and invites the client’s perspective. It remains partly counselor-directed through structured questions and menu-style choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor supports client ownership and avoids controlling language. It would be stronger with more explicit reinforcement of the client’s freedom to choose or not change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions invite the client to identify priorities and practical ideas. The work stays surface-level and does not deeply explore values, motivation, or alcohol-related change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a collaborative stance and invites the client’s input. Partnership is limited by counselor-led framing and less explicit power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor elicits some practical ideas but does not amplify or expand change talk. There is no targeted cultivation of motivation related to alcohol use or risk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with the client’s resistance and responds gently. The responses lack strategic reflections to reduce the weight of sustain talk, especially around alcohol.
