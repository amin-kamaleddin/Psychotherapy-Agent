# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids challenging or shaming Viktor. It does not reach 5 because the warmth is somewhat professional and lacks stronger explicit unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate, complex reflections about pressure, shutdown/snap, and the relational dilemma. It could be stronger by more fully reflecting Viktor’s exhaustion, fear, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on pressure, shutdown, and communication, which gives it a coherent thread. It is limited because it does not consistently connect the conversation back to the primary target of reducing drug use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as an equal partner and builds on Viktor’s ideas throughout. The final pause sentence is co-constructed rather than prescribed, showing strong collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist keeps decision-making with Viktor and avoids pressure, advice-giving, or insisting on quitting. It could be stronger by more explicitly affirming Viktor’s ownership of the decision at moments of resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses evocative questions to draw out Viktor’s own ideas and possible solutions. It is narrower than optimal because it does not consistently elicit deeper values, goals, or motivation to reduce use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently treats Viktor as the expert on his own life and follows his lead. Viktor’s concerns visibly shape the session direction and agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some change talk around a pause and avoiding regret, but the change talk remains limited. It would benefit from more strategic amplification and elicitation of desire, ability, reasons, and need related to reducing use.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Viktor’s sustain talk and acknowledges the function of using without contesting it. It does not reach 5 because it does not consistently balance sustain talk with stronger client change talk.
