# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and affirming throughout the session with no judgment or labels. Could benefit from occasionally simply being present with Elise's ambivalence rather than immediately amplifying her forward movement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking of Elise's inner world. Some reflections lean toward amplification and reframing in a more positive light rather than pure empathic mirroring.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with a logical arc from ambivalence to a concrete plan. Could be slightly more client-led rather than feeling like a gentle funnel toward a predetermined outcome.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally collaborative but significantly weakened by the presentation of three response options and long interpretive summaries that position the psychiatrist as the cognitive driver. The psychiatrist does more of the narrative and interpretive work than a true equal partner would.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never pressures or imposes; explicitly respects Elise's choice and positions her as the decision-maker. Could be strengthened by more explicitly acknowledging her right to maintain the status quo or approach this differently.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses strong evoking questions that consistently draw out Elise's own motivations, ideas, and plans. Occasionally races toward a conclusion rather than fully exploring the client's inner experience before moving forward.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and frequently invites Elise's input rather than taking an expert-driven approach. Somewhat asymmetric in practice, as the psychiatrist does much of the summarizing, framing, and amplifying while Elise mostly provides content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and amplifies Elise's change talk, skillfully guiding the progression from 'impossible' to 'not zero' to a specific plan. The amplification occasionally feels excessive, framing small steps in terms that could inadvertently create pressure to live up to the narrative.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates Elise's fears and sustain talk without minimizing or pushing back. Could be more MI-consistent by sitting longer in uncertainty before pivoting to forward-looking questions after Elise expresses doubt.
