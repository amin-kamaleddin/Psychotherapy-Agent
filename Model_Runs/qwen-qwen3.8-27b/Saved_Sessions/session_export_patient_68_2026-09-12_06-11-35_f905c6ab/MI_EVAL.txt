# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and treats Kevin's ambivalence as a workable starting point. Acceptance is solid but sometimes more procedural than warmly individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Kevin's embarrassment, value of nights out, and desire to avoid injury without giving up social connection. Empathy could deepen by exploring the emotional and social meaning underneath his statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the alcohol-related injury risk and the target behavior. Progression from incident to a concrete low-risk plan is clear and purposeful.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Kevin to choose and shapes the plan around his preferences, such as steak, a reminder, and discreet timer labels. However, the clinician still supplies much of the structure and many options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin is asked for permission, his limits are respected, and he selects several key decisions. At times the volume of specific suggestions moves the interaction toward clinician-led problem-solving.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some evocative questions that produce useful client language and planning. The session relies more on clinician-generated options than on deeply evoking Kevin's own values and motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kevin's perspective clearly shapes the details of the plan, and the psychiatrist avoids overriding his choices. Partnership would be stronger if Kevin more fully directed the agenda and order of discussion.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits commitment and confidence, especially around the next Friday experiment. Change talk is present but could be more consistently drawn out, reflected, and amplified from Kevin's own reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk such as not wanting to stop or kill the vibe is acknowledged without argument or pressure. The psychiatrist could use more strategic double-sided reflections to help Kevin resolve ambivalence.
