# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizes Kevin’s drinking and injury. Warmth is sometimes procedural and advisory, with limited stronger affirmations of Kevin’s strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accepting reflections and understands Kevin’s “middle ground” goal. Empathy remains practical rather than deeply attuned to embarrassment, social pressure, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on safer drinking and a concrete weekend plan. The direction becomes somewhat over-focused on behavior management and safety instructions rather than motivational direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin’s preferences shape the final plan, including water and waiting. However, the interaction often feels more like expert-led advice than fully collaborative plan development.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids coercion, accepts Kevin’s preferred plan, and emphasizes low-pressure follow-up. The high volume of suggestions can subtly weaken the sense that Kevin is fully in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A few useful questions are asked early, but the psychiatrist quickly shifts to strategies and reminders. There is limited evocation of Kevin’s own values, motivation, and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and accepts Kevin’s chosen approach. The session often becomes counselor-led, reducing the collaborative balance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Kevin offers meaningful change talk, such as avoiding another injury and not being a “lost cause.” The psychiatrist accepts these moments but does not consistently amplify, explore, or connect them to confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not argue with or reinforce Kevin’s sustain talk about enjoying drinking and social connection. However, there are missed opportunities to use balanced reflections that hold both concerns.
