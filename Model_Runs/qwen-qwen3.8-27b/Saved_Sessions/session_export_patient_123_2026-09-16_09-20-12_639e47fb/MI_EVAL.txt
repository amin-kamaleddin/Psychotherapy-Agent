# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor is warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, using supportive language such as no pressure and you're handling a lot. Acceptance could be stronger by explicitly affirming the client’s experience of being flagged/referral-related ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections capture the client’s routine and coping well, showing some perspective-taking. However, empathy stays surface-level when the client expresses reluctance, fear of conflict, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The session never connects to the alcohol-risk screening or a clear target behavior. Conversation remains focused on general stress, school, and study habits, limiting MI direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and offers choices, but often shifts into an expert role with scripts and problem-solving strategies. Collaboration is present but not consistently client-led or co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor repeatedly supports choice, pace, and low-pressure decision-making. Autonomy support could be stronger if tied to the client’s motivation and the target concern.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some open-ended questions encourage sharing, but they mostly gather practical information. There is little evocation of the client’s values, reasons for change, confidence, or discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor includes the client in choosing topics and options, but the overall agenda remains counselor-directed. Partnership is partial rather than consistently collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are few moments of client motivation, and the counselor rarely reinforces or expands them. Change talk is not meaningfully developed around a target behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor responds to minimize-stance comments in a low-pressure way and avoids arguing. However, sustain talk is not actively redirected toward change using MI-consistent reflections.
