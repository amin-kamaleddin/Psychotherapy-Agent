# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent warm, unconditional positive regard throughout; never judges or moralizes. Masterful validation of client pushback with genuine humility ('You're right. It is easier for me...').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, complex reflections capturing both content and underlying emotional meaning. Attuned to subtle cues such as defensiveness and the pattern of minimizing ('like you noticed it and then quickly put it down as age').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on alcohol use with a logical progression from social function to physical effects to desired change. Could benefit from more strategic collaborative narrowing toward a concrete focus or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative interaction where client input directly shapes the psychiatrist's approach. Co-creates session purpose and revises framing in response to client feedback.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly and consistently supports autonomy from the very first utterance ('it's your call'). Never uses directive language or pressures for change; frames all exploration as the client's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within the client without providing external reasons for change. All meaningful change talk ('fading a bit,' 'feel more sharp') is entirely client-generated.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise about Evelyn's life resides within her. Powerfully steps down from the expert position when challenged, validating the client's superior understanding of her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes meaningful change talk through well-timed evocative questions. However, the change talk is not yet developed into a sense of confidence, agency, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validates and softens sustain talk without arguing or amplifying it. The response to 'I don't want to give up the evening' is left incomplete, representing an area for further development.
