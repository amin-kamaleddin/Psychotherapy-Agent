# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently affirms the client without judgment and normalizes ambivalence, including possible slip-ups. Only minor caution is that affirmations can become slightly label-heavy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client’s ambivalence, stress, and worry about her son, but do not always deepen into underlying emotions. Some multi-question turns move toward problem-solving before emotion is fully processed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking cessation and narrows toward a concrete 10-minute porch pause. It occasionally drifts into multiple subtopics and asks several questions at once.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan emerges from the client’s own ideas and is integrated in a working-together way. However, the clinician often supplies many options, which can make collaboration feel less client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses choice-preserving language and respects the client’s preferred low-pressure follow-up. Repeated suggestion-giving can make the plan feel somewhat clinician-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician asks evocative questions early and draws out meaningful change talk. However, frequent suggestions interrupt evocation and miss opportunities to deepen powerful statements about identity and protecting her son.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client clearly shapes most plan elements, showing a collaborative stance. The clinician’s repeated menus of options can reduce the client’s sense of being the primary author.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits useful change talk and helps translate ambivalence into a concrete first step. It would benefit from more DARN-C or RULC follow-ups to deepen the client’s own reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and reframed into manageable steps rather than challenged. It could be strengthened with more explicit double-sided reflections that hold both sides before pivoting to change.
