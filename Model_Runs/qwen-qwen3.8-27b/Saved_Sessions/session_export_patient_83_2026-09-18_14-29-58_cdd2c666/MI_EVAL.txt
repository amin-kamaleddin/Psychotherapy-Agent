# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming tone throughout the session, validating the client's right to question the prescription. However, the acceptance occasionally borders on formulaic with repeated phrasing patterns (e.g., 'That's really [adjective]').

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor demonstrates strong perspective-taking, accurately capturing underlying emotions such as loss of agency and feeling depersonalized. A few reflections could have been more nuanced to fully explore deeper feelings of being unseen or undervalued.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear, logical arc moving from confusion to informed readiness, with each turn advancing toward the goal. Could benefit from more explicit summary statements and structural signposting to mark transitions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor uses collaborative language and seeks the client's input before proceeding. However, the collaboration is slightly asymmetric, with the counselor largely driving conversational direction and doing most of the talking in each turn.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout. The counselor never pressures the client, consistently frames information as tools for her own decision-making, and explicitly seeks permission before sharing details.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully elicits motivation from within the client rather than providing external reasons, and highlights her own growth in the session. A slight tendency to interpret and summarize rather than simply reflect and ask slightly reduces the evocative quality.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveys that expertise about the client's decisions resides within her and positions themselves as a facilitator. Could be strengthened by more explicit invitations for the client to co-create the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is skillfully evoked and reinforced, with the counselor explicitly drawing attention to the client's forward-moving language. Could be deepened with more deliberate use of DARN categories and importance or confidence scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor handles the client's mild sustain talk well by never reinforcing it and instead reframing concerns while steering toward agency. The limited sustain talk in the session restricts the opportunity to demonstrate advanced techniques like double-sided reflections.
