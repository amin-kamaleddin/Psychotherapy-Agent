# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Mateo’s pain and progress. It is slightly limited by repeated, formulaic phrases and occasional positive evaluation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects ambivalence, pain, and low confidence. It could be stronger by more consistently deepening the emotional meaning behind Mateo’s fear and discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on increasing physical activity and rehab progress. The conversation moves logically from motivation to barriers to a concrete, doable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by offering options and incorporating Mateo’s ideas. It is slightly structured because the counselor still functions as the main plan architect.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through permission, choice, and language that emphasizes stopping if needed. The plan remains low-demand and respectful of Mateo’s control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes Mateo’s goals, confidence, and problem-solving ideas effectively. It is limited by the quick shift from evocation to implementation planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and lets Mateo’s preferences shape the plan. A more equal partnership would include more explicit positioning of Mateo as the expert on his own body.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits meaningful change talk and uses scaling to build confidence. It could be stronger by more deeply expanding the emotional and personal significance of Mateo’s goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance without arguing and uses normalization and exit strategies. The strongest sustain talk is often addressed through planning rather than deeper emotional reflection.
