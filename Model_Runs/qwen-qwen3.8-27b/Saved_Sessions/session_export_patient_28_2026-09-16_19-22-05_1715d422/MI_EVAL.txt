# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates unconditional positive regard through specific, warm affirmations tied to Dante’s words. Dante’s self-doubt is met with validation rather than correction or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Dante’s ambivalence and underlying emotions, including fear, guilt, and self-protection. Some reflections are overly long and interpretive for his hesitant communication style.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity and moves from a broad concern to a specific, actionable plan. The middle sections on guilt and self-doubt could be more tightly tied back to the exercise goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as a genuine dialogue in which Dante’s ideas shape the direction and content. The psychiatrist builds on the client’s contributions rather than introducing a clinician-driven framework.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Dante’s choice is consistently respected through non-directive language and conditional, exploratory questions. No directive pressure appears despite his uncertainty and fear.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Key plan elements emerge from Dante’s own language rather than external persuasion or advice. A few advisory suggestions slightly interrupt the purity of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly positions Dante as the expert on his own life, body, and experience. The role remains collaborative and respectful throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions frequently elicit Dante’s own motivations, preferences, and ideas for change. Some questions are long and multi-part, which may dilute their evocative impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without judgment and often reflected with both sides of the ambivalence. The session gives guilt and fear substantial airtime, limiting quicker pivots toward change talk.
