# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance even around sensitive alcohol use during pregnancy. The tone is supportive, but the early move into factual education slightly reduces relational depth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Sofia’s fear, ambivalence, and desire to keep the peace. The empathy is helpful but somewhat brief, missing deeper exploration of guilt or lost social identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior by narrowing the conversation to weekend drinking and specific, practical steps. At times, the list-based advice-giving makes the direction feel more task-driven than collaboratively guided.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs a plan based on Sofia’s chosen options, such as focusing on weekends and meeting for coffee. The collaboration could be stronger by eliciting more of Sofia’s own ideas before providing options or scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice through questions like “Which of these feels most doable?” and respects Sofia’s preference to wing it. A score of 5 would require more explicit affirmation of her ownership when she says a plan feels like a lot of work.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses evocative questions early, including the readiness ruler and an invitation to generate her own line. However, the session quickly shifts into education and problem-solving, limiting deeper client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is evidence of partnership as Sofia’s choices shape the plan. The interaction becomes more expert-driven when the psychiatrist offers multiple options, tips, and scripted lines without fully exploring Sofia’s perspective first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked early, and Sofia’s preferred line is affirmed. The session could have more consistently helped Sofia articulate her own deeper reasons, values, and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist validates Sofia’s concern about keeping the peace and not making a scene, which reduces defensiveness. The score is not a 5 because more double-sided reflections could have been used to help her resolve ambivalence.
