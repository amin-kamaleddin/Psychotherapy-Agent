### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout. Reframes Luis's resistance as a valid response to an invalidating environment rather than a personal failing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows highly accurate and consistent empathic understanding of Luis's experience, including the exhausting weight of constant self-monitoring and the emotional cycle of compliance failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, progressive focus on asthma management with logical momentum from referral context through to a concrete self-generated action plan. Could have explored Luis's broader values or longer-term goals slightly more explicitly.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Luis's ideas consistently shape the direction of the conversation, from 'simpler' to the 'coffee and inhaler' strategy to the final commitment timeframe. The power dynamic remains equal throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Luis's ownership and freedom of choice, immediately establishing the frame as 'figure things out together.' The 'test' framing skillfully reduces perceived commitment burden while preserving autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The entire session is structured around Luis generating his own motivations rather than being given external reasons. Key strategies like 'what would it look like' and 'what do you think happens' consistently elicit client-driven insights.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within the client, affirming his own knowledge and self-awareness. The session could have further reinforced Luis's role as the expert on his own life in a few moments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively cultivates multiple change talk statements including 'I'm not lazy, maybe I'm just scared of a second job' and 'Coffee and the inhaler, I can do that.' Skillfully reflects and amplifies these statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without argument or dismissal, validating the logic ('niceness alone isn't going to change anything') while pivoting to Luis's own preferred future. Reframes self-critical sustain talk ('I'm just making up excuses') as evidence of progress.
