# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates consistent warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental positive regard throughout the session. Even barriers and ambivalence are met with validation rather than disapproval or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both the client’s content and underlying emotions, including tiredness, the pull of the couch, and fear of self-punishment. The empathic stance is strong, though phrasing could be varied slightly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation remains focused on the target behavior of increasing physical activity through a short, doable walk. Pacing is clear and change-oriented, moving steadily from ambivalence to a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed from the client’s own ideas, such as a walk playlist, shoes by the door, and a sticky note. The psychiatrist supports and refines the client’s contributions rather than directing the outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client’s pace, choices, and permission to stop are consistently honored. The plan is framed as a low-pressure experiment rather than an obligation or requirement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits the client’s own motivations, strategies, and reasons for change rather than supplying external arguments. The client articulates meaningful change talk, including wanting a reset and avoiding exhaustion.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positions the client as the expert on her own life and invites her to set the direction. The interaction is collaborative, with the client’s ideas clearly shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions successfully elicit the client’s reasons, ability, and commitment to try a manageable walk plan. Change talk is reinforced and built upon without being replaced by clinician-led persuasion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk related to tiredness, weather, and the couch is acknowledged without being amplified. The psychiatrist normalizes these barriers and redirects toward gentle, client-controlled solutions.
