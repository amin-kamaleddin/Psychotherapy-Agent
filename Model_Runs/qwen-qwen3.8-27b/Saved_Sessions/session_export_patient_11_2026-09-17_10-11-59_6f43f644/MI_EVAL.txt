# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, validating, and nonjudgmental language while affirming the client's agency. There is no evidence of disapproval, moralizing, or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects the client's ambivalence, exhaustion, and desire for control. Empathy could be deeper in emotionally sensitive areas such as body confidence or past failed attempts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior of building a small, consistent physical activity routine. The conversation moves clearly from motivation to concrete implementation without significant drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with the client as an equal partner rather than imposing a pre-designed plan. The client's ideas directly shape the session and the resulting action plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly emphasizes the client's right to choose the level of effort and pace of change. Options and permissions, rather than directives, are used consistently throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits motivation from within the client rather than providing external reasons to change. The client's change talk becomes more specific and detailed as the session progresses.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions the client as the expert in her own life and change process. Questions and reflections keep the client's perspective central to the work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits and strengthens change talk through open questions and strategic reflections. The client is guided toward concrete steps and amplified personal motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully softens barriers and sustain talk by framing the plan around the client's current energy and autonomy. Concerns are reframed in a way that reduces pressure and supports manageable change.
