# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys nonjudgmental acceptance by validating Milan and stating he is not messed up. Some early framing still treats drinking as a problem pattern, limiting fully unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects Milan’s frustration and desire for a normal weekend accurately. Stays mostly at paraphrase level and does not deeply name underlying shame, resentment, or fear of being labeled.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on alcohol use, weekends, and what feels manageable. Drifts toward the pressure theme and does not clearly connect values to a concrete alcohol-related target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses Milan’s language and invites his perspective, creating a collaborative tone. The agenda remains counselor-led rather than fully co-created with Milan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by emphasizing Milan’s right to choose and avoiding direct advice. Occasionally steers toward change, such as exploring “maybe I could drink less.”

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions to explore what a good weekend feels like. Does not sufficiently draw out discrepancy, personal importance, confidence, or future consequences to generate stronger motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance by inviting Milan’s perspective. The psychiatrist still controls topic selection and pacing more than Milan does.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Picks up a small change statement about drinking less and asks a follow-up. Does not consistently expand, reinforce, or strategically evoke stronger change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing or challenging Milan’s sustaining statements. Leaves many sustain talk statements largely unworked, with only limited strategic redirection or double-sided reflection.
