# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; consistently validates without judgment or condescension. Never uses labels or minimizes the client's feelings, and accepts both ambivalence and progress simultaneously.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and sophisticated reflections that capture the precise emotional and cognitive undercurrents beneath the client's surface statements. Consistently accesses nuances the client had not fully articulated, such as self-defeating patterns and internal tensions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a generally clear focus on the target behavior with logical progression from rapport through exploration to a concrete small step. Pacing between exploratory and action-oriented phases could be slightly more strategic and intentional.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance where the client's ideas genuinely shape every aspect of the session's content and direction. The psychiatrist never offers external solutions; all insights emerge from the client's own exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook demonstration of autonomy support; consistently asks for the client's own answers and never uses prescriptive language. Respects the client's pacing, hesitations, and right to be uncertain with no pressure or urgency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful use of open-ended questions and reflections to draw out the client's own motivations, insights, and solutions. The psychiatrist almost never offers content, instead providing tools for the client to find his own answers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent collaborative frame established from the opening, with the power dynamic consistently balanced. The psychiatrist explicitly attributes insights to the client and never positions themselves as the expert with solutions to dispense.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively names and amplifies the client's change language with powerful before/after contrasts. Could be strengthened by more explicitly eliciting confidence language and further developing commitment statements with scaling or follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by naming patterns (e.g., self-dismissal) without reinforcing them and reframing back toward the client's own insights. Some instances of sustain talk could have been more explicitly addressed with double-sided reflections or strategic pivots.
