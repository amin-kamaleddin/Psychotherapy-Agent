**Comments:** The session is a genuine co-construction of understanding and a plan, with the client generating both insight and action strategy. Inclusive language and joint exploration ('What do you make of that') ensure no power imbalance or expert-driven moments.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support—never recommends a specific intervention, always frames questions around Claudia's own values and choices. The client is clearly positioned as the decision-maker, generating her own plan, reasoning, and priorities.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation throughout; the entire session is structured to elicit motivation and change talk from within Claudia rather than providing external reasons for change. Questions consistently draw out Claudia's own self-evaluation, values, and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently operates from a position of genuine partnership, with the client positioned as the expert on her own experience. The opening framing ('I'm curious about what it's like for you') and collaborative questioning maintain an equal, non-hierarchical stance.
