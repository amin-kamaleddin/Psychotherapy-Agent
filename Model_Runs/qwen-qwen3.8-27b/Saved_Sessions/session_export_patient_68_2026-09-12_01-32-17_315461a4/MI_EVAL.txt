# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive, reducing shame around injury and drinking. Acceptance is slightly limited because the session quickly becomes task-focused rather than warmly validating Kevin's mixed feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some understanding of Kevin's frustration, embarrassment, and social concerns. However, reflections are often brief and surface-level, missing deeper ambivalence, regret, or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on alcohol use, the injury, and practical harm-reduction targets such as limiting drinks and planning a safe weekend. Direction is strong but less connected to Kevin's own values and change process.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Kevin's preferences and incorporates several of his ideas into the final plan. However, much of the session is expert-driven, with the psychiatrist supplying most strategies and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Kevin's choices are respected, and the psychiatrist emphasizes that he can try or adjust the plan. Autonomy support is weakened by frequent specific instructions and a prescriptive tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** This is a major weakness; the psychiatrist does not consistently draw out Kevin's own reasons, values, confidence, or discrepancies. The session shifts into advice and planning without deeply evoking internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some collaboration, as Kevin's preferences shape parts of the plan. The interaction becomes uneven because the psychiatrist frequently moves into an advice-giving stance with structured lists.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Kevin offers some change talk, but the psychiatrist rarely evokes, amplifies, or builds on it. Missed opportunities include exploring what avoiding injury means to him and how confident he feels about the plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or dismissing Kevin's reasons for maintaining current drinking patterns. However, sustain talk is mostly handled through practical problem-solving rather than MI-style reflective redirection.
