# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard with specific, individualized acknowledgment of Daniel's identity. Never judges, invalidates, or pushes back, even in the face of strong sustain talk.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate empathic understanding, capturing the deeper functional and emotional meaning of Daniel's behavior. Makes precise, nuanced distinctions in his ambivalence without being confrontational.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the target behavior with a logical arc from concern to experiment. Falls slightly short due to a less targeted closing that lacks a concrete next step or follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is co-constructed with Daniel's goals, language, and solutions driving the direction. The psychiatrist functions as a facilitator of Daniel's own thinking rather than a source of information or direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Daniel's ownership and right to choose, using explicit autonomy-supportive language ("You don't have to walk through it today"). All decisions — the experiment, frequency, and tracking method — originate from Daniel.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is sourced from Daniel — the psychiatrist never states reasons for change or provides health information. The progression from "I'm fine" to a concrete self-directed plan is entirely generated by Daniel's own thinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Daniel as the expert on his own life, explicitly disclaiming the expert role. Asks Daniel to interpret his own experience throughout and never introduces external authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through evocative questioning and reflections that amplify Daniel's own change-oriented language. Drives a clear progression from minimal engagement to a concrete, self-directed experiment plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids arguing with or contradicting sustain talk, using double-sided reflections to gently reframe. Could be stronger in addressing social normalization ("everyone drinks a little") and minimize-by-comparison statements.
