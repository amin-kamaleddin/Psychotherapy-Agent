# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently shows respect, nonjudgment, and unconditional positive regard while validating ambivalence and partial progress. Acceptance remains somewhat professional and formulaic, and could more explicitly affirm Luka as a person beyond his behaviors.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurately captures the client’s ambivalence, practical concerns, and metaphors. Empathy could be deeper by exploring the emotional weight beneath the behavior, such as fear, shame, guilt, or relational pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and logical focus on reducing drinking and preventing escalation into conflict. Direction could be strengthened by more explicitly linking the plan to the broader violence-reduction and safety goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly collaborates with the client by inviting him to co-create the pause plan, agreement, and support strategy. Luka’s ideas clearly shape the content and direction of the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is excellent, with the psychiatrist consistently returning choice and decision-making to the client. The psychiatrist respects Luka’s readiness level without pushing for full abstinence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively uses open-ended questions to draw out the client’s own ideas and practical steps rather than giving advice. Evocation could be stronger by exploring values, identity, confidence, and deeper emotional reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Luka as the expert on his own life and builds on his contributions rather than imposing solutions. Partnership could be stronger by more explicitly naming the client’s role and power in the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits change talk through forward-looking questions that help Luka identify concrete steps. This could be deepened by explicitly exploring DARN-C language, confidence, readiness, and values-based motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well by accepting partial change and avoiding pressure or defensiveness. The psychiatrist could more strategically use double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence in a more explicit and consistent way.
