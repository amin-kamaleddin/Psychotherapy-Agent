# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, clearly validating Marisol’s choice without pressure. Acceptance is effective but somewhat formulaic and does not fully affirm her broader worth or autonomy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflective listening is adequate and captures Marisol’s preference for practical care over lectures. However, it remains largely factual and does not fully explore her emotions, identity, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the lip piercing and oral health but becomes repetitive after the client chooses a simple plan. Strategic movement toward values, readiness, or longer-term goals is limited.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adapts to Marisol’s preferences, such as avoiding mouthwash and keeping costs low. Collaboration weakens somewhat when the plan is mostly repeated rather than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through language that honors Marisol’s decision and avoids pressure to remove the piercing. The support is consistent but somewhat repetitive and does not extend to the broader decision of keeping, modifying, or removing it long-term.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few evocative questions early on, but the session quickly shifts into practical advice and repeated safety reminders. The psychiatrist does not consistently draw out Marisol’s own motivations, confidence, or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and follows Marisol’s preference for a low-cost plan, but later becomes more advice-driven. The collaboration is present but not fully balanced throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk is limited and mostly tied to practical self-care rather than deeper motivation. The psychiatrist does not use scaling, amplification, or values exploration to strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Marisol and acknowledges her autonomy, which reduces defensiveness. However, they do not actively use reflective strategies to soften sustain talk or pivot it toward change.
