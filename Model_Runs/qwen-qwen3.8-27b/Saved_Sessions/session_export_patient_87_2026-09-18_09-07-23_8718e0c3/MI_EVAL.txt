# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, with strong normalization and explicit rejection of shame. Warmth occasionally feels slightly formulaic through repeated affirmation structures like 'That's not nothing.'

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening with masterful complex reflections that name emotions Marco hadn't yet articulated. The reflection capturing the loneliness of his wife not pushing back is particularly outstanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains excellent focus on the target behavior while naturally exploring underlying emotional and relational dimensions. Progression from ambivalence to concrete, actionable micro-intervention is clear and purposeful.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration between equals where the final plan emerges entirely from Marco's own thinking. The psychiatrist serves as a reflective partner who helps Marco see his own ideas more clearly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Marco's ownership and capacity for choice, explicitly attributing agency ('You built that idea yourself'). Never pressures or prescribes; all actions framed as Marco's own choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight in the session comes from Marco himself, drawn out through reflective questions and strategic prompts without supplying content. Motivation is clearly internal and client-generated throughout.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Marco as the expert on his own life with a genuinely egalitarian power dynamic. Uses 'I'm curious' framing and invites Marco's perspective throughout, never prescribing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk by affirming and expanding on Marco's own change-oriented language. Could have more systematically explored values, importance, or confidence to maximize development.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating feelings without reinforcing avoidance and consistently pivoting toward agency. Could benefit from more strategic use of double-sided reflections to help resolve ambivalence.
