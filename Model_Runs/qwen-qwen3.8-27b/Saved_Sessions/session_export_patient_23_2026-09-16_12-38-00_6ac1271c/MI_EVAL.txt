# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and consistently validates Claudia’s concerns. Acceptance is present but somewhat formulaic rather than deeply warm or individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows good reflective listening and accurately reflects the content of Claudia’s statements. Reflections remain primarily content-level and do not fully access emotional undercurrents such as fear of failure or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear, logical focus on alcohol use, sleep, mood, and a concrete tracking plan. Direction is efficient but somewhat procedural and could more explicitly connect the plan to Claudia’s values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well, allowing Claudia to choose the focus, tracking method, timing, and strategies. Collaboration is genuine but often structured through predefined menus and counselor-generated options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offers choices, follows Claudia’s lead, and avoids pressure. Autonomy support is strong, though it could more explicitly affirm Claudia’s right to reject approaches that feel controlling.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes initial motivations early in the session but then shifts primarily into an informational and logistical mode. Evocation remains surface-level and does not deeply explore Claudia’s intrinsic values or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and allows Claudia to shape the plan. Partnership is real but remains somewhat structured because the counselor generates much of the content and options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits meaningful change talk, including Claudia’s commitment to honest tracking and desire for a less resistant path. Change talk is present but not sufficiently amplified or reflected to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance without arguing, validates Claudia’s concerns, and redirects toward manageable options. A minor gap is that the emotional meaning behind some of her resistance is not explicitly reflected.
