# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, affirming, and nonjudgmental, normalizing Amina's ambivalence and reducing shame. It is not a 5 because acceptance sometimes emphasizes progress and cooperation rather than unconditional positive regard independent of change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Amina's mixed feelings, shame, and need for distraction. Deeper exploration of brief themes such as feeling gross or failing the test would strengthen affective processing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session keeps a clear focus on social media use and narrows toward a concrete, client-owned 15-minute tea pause. It is slightly broad at times when moving into general quiet or relaxation rather than the precise behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses co-constructive language and builds the plan from Amina's ideas, such as tea, a song, and checking after. It would be stronger if Amina's expertise and agenda ownership were made even more explicit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently emphasizes choice, avoids controlling directives, and frames change as a small experiment Amina may try. Support for Amina's ownership of the process is central and consistent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Questions elicit Amina's own goals, markers of success, and coping ideas rather than lecturing. It would be stronger with more sustained evocation of values, future self, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Inclusive language and the use of Amina's suggestions create a collaborative tone. The psychiatrist remains an active guide, which is appropriate but not fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, evocative questions and reinforcement generate meaningful change talk, including trying the pause once and noticing morning mood. It could deepen importance and ability talk more systematically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges the value of social media for distraction, connection, and rest without arguing. More explicit double-sided reflections of sustain talk would balance the change and stay talk more completely.
