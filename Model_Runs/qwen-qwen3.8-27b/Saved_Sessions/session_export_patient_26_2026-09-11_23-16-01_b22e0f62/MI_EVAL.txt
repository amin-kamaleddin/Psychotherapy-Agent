# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and responds without criticism. However, acceptance is often task-focused and does not strongly affirm Daniela’s strengths or values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses simple reflections of emotion, such as recognizing that Daniela is overwhelmed and exhausted. Deeper emotional meaning and ambivalence are not consistently explored with complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays broadly connected to childcare, finances, and substance use. However, the focus is fragmented by many options, which may dilute a clear, client-owned direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist adapts to Daniela’s preferences, such as using a casual message and avoiding role-play. Collaboration remains uneven because the clinician often supplies plans and scripts rather than co-constructing them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some autonomy-supportive moments, including asking for Daniela’s input and using low-pressure language. Overall, the approach is directive, with frequent scripts, lists, and specific action steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist relies heavily on information, advice, and practical problem-solving. There are few open-ended MI-style questions that elicit Daniela’s own motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist occasionally asks for Daniela’s preferences, but the agenda remains largely clinician-driven. The session does not consistently position Daniela as the expert on her own life and goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela offers several instances of change talk, including wanting reliable childcare and trying to delay smoking. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responds to barriers without judgment and does not reinforce Daniela’s sustain talk. However, there is limited use of double-sided reflections or other MI strategies to soften ambivalence.
