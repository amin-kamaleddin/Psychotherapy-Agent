# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance throughout. They validated Nadine's fatigue and fear without shaming and affirmed her protective intent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately tracked both content and emotion, including Nadine's ambivalence. The counselor could have deepened the emotional meaning of her fear of being harmful and reduced some repetitive phrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on parental stress and narrowed toward a concrete, manageable plan. Naming the target behavior earlier and linking the plan to values would strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor acted as an equal partner in problem-solving rather than imposing a solution. Nadine's concerns and ideas, such as "pink and moving," shaped the next step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor clearly supported Nadine's autonomy and did not pressure her to adopt a specific parenting style. The final plan emerged from her own choice to try the first attempt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicited motivation from within Nadine and used her own language effectively. The counselor could further evoke confidence and ability talk, as occasional suggestions were slightly more directive.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently conveyed that Nadine was the expert on her own experience. Power sharing was strong, with the session shaped by her concerns rather than a preset agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicited desire, need, and commitment talk, and the counselor reinforced Nadine's emerging plan. More explicit cultivation of confidence and ability talk would elevate this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor normalized and reframed sustain talk rather than reinforcing it, helping reduce guilt and fear. More consistent use of double-sided reflections and strategic pivots would improve this area.
