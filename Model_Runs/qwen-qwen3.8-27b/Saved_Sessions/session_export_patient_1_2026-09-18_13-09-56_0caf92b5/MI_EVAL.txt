# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance and validates Ahmed's perspective throughout without labeling or shaming. Could go deeper emotionally to more explicitly normalize his ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening that accurately captures Ahmed's pragmatic mindset and construction-worker identity. Could have gone deeper on emotional undercurrents such as pride in self-reliance or fear beneath the minimization.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking-related health concerns with a logical progression from minimization to awareness of a blind spot. Could have more explicitly summarized the conversation's progression to reinforce momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as a joint exploration, seamlessly adopting Ahmed's own metaphors (truck, oil light, bearing) and building on them. The interaction genuinely feels like two people thinking together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never tells Ahmed what to do or pressures him to quit; structures the entire session around Ahmed's own framework. Explicitly validates his right to his own timeline and decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open-ended questions and reflective statements to elicit Ahmed's own thinking rather than providing information or reasons for change. Could have pushed further into evoking confidence or self-efficacy language to deepen the process.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Ahmed as the expert on his own body, work environment, and life. Serves as a guide who asks questions and reflects back, never crossing into advice-giving or persuasion.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits change talk through strategic questions such as the 'oil light' metaphor and 'what's normal for you?' Does not explicitly elicit confidence language, self-efficacy statements, or a clear statement of intention toward change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk without arguing or correcting, then gently redirects with questions. Could have more actively pivoted or used double-sided reflections more deliberately at key moments.
