# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows consistent respect, validation, and nonjudgmental support. The delivery is strong, though slightly formulaic and less explicitly affirming of Samuel's unconditional worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Samuel's ambivalence, fear of failure, need for simplicity, and emotional meaning. The counselor demonstrates sustained emotional attunement and precise understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation remains tightly focused on smoking reduction, trigger management, and making the journal useful. Each question advances the session toward a concrete, behavior-focused plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently co-constructs the plan with Samuel using inclusive, collaborative language. The final approach emerges from Samuel's own ideas rather than clinician prescription.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor emphasizes optionality, avoids controlling language, and respects Samuel's concern about added burden. Autonomy support is strong but could be even more explicit in reinforcing Samuel's right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions successfully elicit Samuel's own ideas, preferences, and practical change talk. The evocation is somewhat narrow, focusing more on journal mechanics than deeper values, confidence, or broader readiness to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist clearly positions Samuel as the expert on his own routines, habits, and priorities. Session decisions are substantially guided by Samuel's contributions and perspective.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively draws out Samuel's change-oriented language and practical solutions. There are missed opportunities to amplify specific successes and explicitly evoke importance, confidence, or readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument and reframed in a way that reduces its power. Concerns about failure, burden, and self-deception are addressed while still being respected.
