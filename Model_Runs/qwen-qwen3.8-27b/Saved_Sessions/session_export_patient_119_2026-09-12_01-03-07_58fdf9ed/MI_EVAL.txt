# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses nonjudgmental, normalizing language and avoids blame, but warmth remains somewhat formulaic and task-oriented rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Acknowledges burden superficially but quickly moves to advice, missing nuanced reflections of shame, fear, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on medication adherence and concrete steps, though direction is practical problem-solving rather than motivational clarification.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites input and uses client's ideas, but overall plan construction remains counselor-driven rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and avoids pressure, but also prescribes specific plans and scripts without consistently affirming client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Opens with an open question but quickly shifts to education and advice, with limited evocation of client values, motivations, or change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites client input and builds on her ideas, yet the session remains largely expert-driven with counselor-led structure and advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Affirms client's choices and elicits some preference, but lacks open questions to amplify or deepen the client's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation but passively minimizes risk, inadvertently reinforcing status-quo language rather than using double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance.
