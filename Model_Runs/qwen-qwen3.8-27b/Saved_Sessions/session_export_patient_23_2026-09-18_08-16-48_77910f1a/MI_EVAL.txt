# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm unconditional positive regard throughout. Validates both sides of ambivalence without judgment or minimization, including when the client expresses self-doubt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures the felt meaning and emotional texture, not just content. Goes beyond paraphrasing to mirror the client's own metaphors and self-critical moments with gentleness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Logical and focused progression from health check result through strategy identification to barrier anticipation. Lacks a brief collaborative summary or 'next steps' consolidation at the end to provide forward momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue rather than interview; therapist uses shared inquiry language ('I'm curious,' 'What's interesting to me is…') and co-constructs meaning. Honestly acknowledges the limits of the therapist's role while inviting exploration together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces the client's self-determination and ownership throughout without ever using directive language. Explicitly positions the motivation as the client's own, not imposed by a doctor's letter.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Zero external arguments or health statistics; every key question is designed to draw out the client's own reasoning. The client's final insight about autopilot and environmental interruption is entirely her own, elicited by the therapist's question.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, explicitly attributing self-trust and grounding to her. The entire session is structured around the client's own metaphors, language, and definitions of success.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes substantial change talk (Desire, Ability, Reasons, Need) through well-timed open questions. Reinforces each instance using the client's own language without over-praising.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively refrains from pushing back on sustain talk and uses constructive reframing ('You're not fighting the wine — you're protecting the mornings'). Could have more explicitly normalized the gap between session confidence and real-world follow-through before moving forward.
