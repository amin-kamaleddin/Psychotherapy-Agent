# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific and genuine affirmations rather than formulaic praise. No judgment is ever expressed toward Camila's choices, ambivalence, or all-or-nothing tendencies; warmth is woven naturally into reflections.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Complex reflections are precise and go beyond paraphrase, capturing the emotional texture of Camila's self-negotiation, relational context, and subtle self-undermining patterns. The final response honoring the gap between the rule and her reality is a clean, non-defensive summary.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear arc from exploring ambivalence to identifying a specific barrier, a self-generated solution, and a practical workaround, with each question purposefully moving toward the target behavior. Even when touching on guilt or morning chaos, the conversation always loops back to physical activity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work is framed as joint exploration, with the session co-constructed in real time and the power dynamic genuinely equal. The clinician's statement 'I think I know what would help, but I'm not sure it's realistic' is a particularly strong collaborative move that inverts the typical power dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a standout feature, with repeated reinforcement of Camila's ownership and agency and the clinician never prescribing or implying obligation. The psychiatrist models autonomy by being transparent about their own uncertainty, implicitly communicating that Camila is the final authority.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every key insight emerges from Camila's own mouth, with questions specifically designed to draw out her motivations and solutions rather than supplying them. The psychiatrist never provides reasons for change or lectures, instead creating conditions for self-discovery and then reflecting back with precision.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The opening explicitly frames the session as being about what matters to Camila, and open questions consistently position her as the expert on her own life. The session is shaped entirely by Camila's contributions, which the clinician would never have known without her sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions are strategically designed to elicit change language, and the psychiatrist consistently amplifies and labels it without ever supplying it for Camila. The session moves from vague ambivalence to a concrete, self-generated plan, the hallmark of effective change talk cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through strength-based reframes and honest acknowledgment without reinforcement, but could have been slightly more strategic in the moment Camila says 'I'm not sure it's realistic.' A more explicit double-sided reflection might have been more MI-consistent in that moment.
