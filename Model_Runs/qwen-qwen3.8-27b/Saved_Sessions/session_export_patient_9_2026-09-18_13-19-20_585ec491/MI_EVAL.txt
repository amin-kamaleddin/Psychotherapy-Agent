# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and non-judgmental throughout the session. Affirmations occasionally feel slightly formulaic rather than deeply individualized to each moment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally nuanced reflections that track both content and emotional undercurrent with high fidelity. Includes sophisticated complex reflections that validate somatic experiences and capture underlying fears beneath stated concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression staying within the target domain while appropriately following the client's lead. Could have been slightly more consolidated toward a concrete next step or action plan at the end.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine dialogue with a clearly balanced power dynamic; the psychiatrist builds on Amina's own metaphors and adapts in real-time to her contributions. Session direction is shaped by the client's responses rather than a pre-planned agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Amina's ownership and agency, never rushing her or jumping in with solutions. Appropriately allows Amina to sit with uncertainty at the end rather than imposing a plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every motivation and insight is elicited from Amina rather than provided by the psychiatrist. Change talk escalates naturally from passive ('I want the best for him') to active ('maybe it's about being real with him').

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook MI partnership: the psychiatrist never gives advice, attributes all insights to Amina, and operates as a skilled mirror and guide. The role is clearly that of a collaborator, not a director or expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies Amina's own change talk through strategic open questions and reflective responses. Could have more explicitly elicited confidence or ability statements to further strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating without challenging and consistently finding the 'grain' within it to redirect. In a couple of moments, sustain talk could have been more explicitly acknowledged before pivoting.
