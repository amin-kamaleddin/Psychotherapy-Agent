# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and honored Amina’s boundary without pressure or disappointment. The stance was safe and supportive, though somewhat procedural rather than deeply warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledged Amina’s reluctance and boundary in an appropriate but surface-level way. Deeper perspective-taking, especially around the feelings behind her minimal responses, was limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on safety, minimal disclosure, and school, without wandering. However, it lacked a clear collaborative direction toward a change-related goal or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor invited Amina’s preference and followed her decisions, avoiding an authoritarian stance. Collaboration was present but remained largely a counselor-generated menu rather than true co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Amina’s right to choose, pause, or stop was consistently respected, which is important given her resistance. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly linking her choices to her own capacity for change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Basic open prompts elicited some minimal content, such as “school,” but the counselor did not continue to draw out Amina’s motivations, concerns, or change talk. Evocation was too limited to deepen engagement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor consistently signaled partnership by inviting Amina’s choice and following her lead. The interaction was less expert-driven, though Amina mostly selected from counselor-provided options rather than co-leading.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There were few opportunities used to affirm or expand Amina’s change-relevant language, such as “I can handle it.” The counselor missed chances to draw out her own reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoided arguing or reinforcing Amina’s resistance and accepted her boundaries without creating a contest. The response was safe, though it did not include more strategic reflections or reframes to gently soften sustain talk.
