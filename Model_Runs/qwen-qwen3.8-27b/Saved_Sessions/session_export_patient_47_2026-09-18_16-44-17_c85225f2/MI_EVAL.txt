# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and non-judgmental throughout, with genuine affirmations tied to specific moments. Affirmations occasionally follow a predictable validate-reflect-affirm pattern that feels slightly scripted, and lacks an explicit unconditional positive regard statement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional perspective-taking, including a particularly powerful reflection on the weight of grief and the quiet of the house. Consistently gets inside Evelyn's frame without overpathologizing or imposing an external narrative.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use while exploring its meaning and context, with a logical narrative arc from initial reaction to concrete changes. Could benefit from slightly more explicit connection back to the specific drinking goal at transition points.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A model of collaborative interaction with genuine equal power dynamics; every major shift is initiated by Evelyn's response. The psychiatrist explicitly acknowledges Evelyn's contributions as equal to her own and treats her as the sole authority on her experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout strength, with explicit affirmations of Evelyn's right to maintain the pub and frame change as entirely optional. The psychiatrist never uses prescriptive language, and the closing line reinforcing ownership ('That's your voice, Evelyn') is exemplary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every major insight comes from Evelyn's own mouth — she traces the grief-to-pub path, identifies the people over the wine, and generates her own change strategies. The psychiatrist never provides external reasons for change, cites statistics, or makes a case against drinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions herself as an explorer alongside Evelyn rather than a guide, using 'I wonder if' and 'I'm curious' language throughout. The session direction is entirely shaped by Evelyn's responses with no imposed agenda or predetermined plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes multiple instances of change talk, culminating in two specific, self-generated change strategies. Could have pushed slightly further into ability and confidence language (e.g., scaling questions) to deepen the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively using double-sided reflections and validation without argument or amplification. A 5 would require even more strategic deflection of the recurring 'it's not a big deal' narrative.
