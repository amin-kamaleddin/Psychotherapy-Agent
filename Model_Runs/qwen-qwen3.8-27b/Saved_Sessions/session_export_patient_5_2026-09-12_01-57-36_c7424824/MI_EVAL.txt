# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, validating tone, though some labels like 'sensible' and 'reasonable' feel mildly judgmental.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provides accurate reflections but remains surface-level, not deeply attending to fear, frustration, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on the medication decision and safety, though plan consolidation and ending are slightly less MI-focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows cooperative moments, but much of the session is information-giving rather than co-created by the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supports pacing and choice, but repeated offers and normative phrases slightly reduce neutrality.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses some open questions, but often supplies explanations and scripts rather than eliciting the patient's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respects patient preferences but remains expert-driven, with less emphasis on the patient as the expert on her own values.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Evokes some change talk but does not consistently amplify or elicit desire, ability, need, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk, yet does not strategically turn it into change focus or use double-sided reflections.
