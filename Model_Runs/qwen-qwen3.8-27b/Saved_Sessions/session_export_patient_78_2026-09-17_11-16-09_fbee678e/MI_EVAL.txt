### Acceptance

**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard, validating Lucía's act of showing up and meeting her exactly where she is without judgment. The phrase 'You don't have to feel like her to try' exemplifies meeting the client without demanding she be somewhere else.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows exceptional empathic accuracy, moving beyond surface content to capture deeper meaning and emotional weight (e.g., 'when would I get to just... be?'). Reflections are individually crafted and identify underlying needs like autonomy and self-identity rather than just surface behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a logical progression from surface complaint to deeper issues to a concrete action step, ending with a behavior-specific plan. The middle section exploring identity and autonomy slightly broadens focus beyond the specific smoking behavior, though the session stays on track overall.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative interaction structured as joint exploration rather than expert-led assessment. Inclusive language ('I'm curious,' 'I wonder if') positions both parties as co-investigators, and 'You figured that out yourself' powerfully acknowledges the client's contribution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently establishes client control over the session, from the open-ended opening to honoring Lucía's boundaries ('not for long,' 'I'm not making promises'). All suggestions are framed as questions, and the final plan is entirely self-generated by the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully draws out Lucía's own motivations and solutions through carefully crafted questions rather than providing arguments or reasons for change. The progression from 'I don't know who I am without that little pause' to 'I think I'd do it' represents pure evoked change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely cedes the agenda to the client and consistently positions her as the authority on her own life ('you're the only one who can do it'). Never takes over, provides unsolicited advice, or imposes a timeline, maintaining an equal power dynamic throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk by inviting Lucía to articulate her own thinking and building on her responses (e.g., 'what did just being look like?' leading to the watercolor solution). Consistently elicits the client's own solutions rather than providing them, landing on genuine, self-generated change talk.

**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging reality without arguing and reframing (e.g., 'You don't have to feel like her to try'). However, the tension between the cigarette's function as a 'break' and the desired alternative is somewhat left hanging before pivoting to the watercolor solution, which prevents a perfect score.
