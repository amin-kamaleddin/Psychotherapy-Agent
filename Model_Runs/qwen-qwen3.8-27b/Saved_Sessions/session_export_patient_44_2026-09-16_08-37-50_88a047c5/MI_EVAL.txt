# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, frequently affirming Erik's progress without blame. Acceptance could deepen by normalizing struggle and emotional difficulty more explicitly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Erik's hope, discouragement, comparison-related frustration, and key behavioral hurdles. Empathy is slightly limited by being brief and behavior-focused rather than deeply affect-focused.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is strongly focused and moves efficiently from a broad health goal to a specific, actionable step for tonight. Each question purposefully guides the conversation toward a concrete behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist partners with Erik by inviting his input and integrating his ideas, such as timing, backup rules, and support from his wife. Collaboration is slightly limited because the therapist supplies much of the plan structure and language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Erik's autonomy through choices, options, and final client decisions. Autonomy support is slightly weakened by structured planning and language that feels mildly directive, such as 'lock it in' and 'non-negotiable.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked through open questions, confidence ratings, and tiny tweaks. The session is more plan-driven and therapist-suggestive than fully evocative of Erik's own values, motivations, and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and regularly asks Erik to choose and refine the plan. Partnership could be stronger by offering less pre-packaged structure before the client generates options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively strengthens confidence and builds on Erik's own plan elements, with confidence rising from 6 to 8. Cultivation could be stronger by more deeply eliciting values, meaning, and personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing with or reinforcing Erik's sustain talk and reframes barriers as workable hurdles. Softening is inconsistent, with fewer double-sided reflections and missed opportunities to turn ambivalence into motivation.
