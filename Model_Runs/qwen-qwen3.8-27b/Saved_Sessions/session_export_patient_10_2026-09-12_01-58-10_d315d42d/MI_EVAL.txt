# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and avoids blaming Amina. Acceptance could be deeper by more explicitly affirming her competence and emotional experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture her frustration, confidence, and balancing of work and childcare. Deeper exploration of underlying emotions and fear was limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target goal of attending the job interview. Momentum is maintained through practical next steps related to the childcare barrier.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist often invites input and adapts to Amina’s preferences. The interaction can feel more guided than collaborative when many options and scripts are counselor-generated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is respected through supportive language and offering choices. It could be stronger by more explicitly positioning Amina as in charge of her decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open-ended questions draw out Amina’s ideas and plans. The psychiatrist relies more on practical problem-solving than eliciting Amina’s internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina is invited to contribute and influence the session. The psychiatrist’s strong advisory and problem-solving role reduces the balance of the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina expresses confidence and a plan, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or explore this change talk. More follow-up could strengthen her own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk and barriers are acknowledged without argument. The psychiatrist could better soften these barriers with double-sided reflections and broaden possibility beyond constraints.
