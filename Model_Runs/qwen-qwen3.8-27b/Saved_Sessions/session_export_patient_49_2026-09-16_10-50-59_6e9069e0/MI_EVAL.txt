# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer is consistently respectful and reassuring, normalizing slips as information rather than failure. Acceptance could be deepened by more explicitly validating ambivalence and the social difficulty of resisting smoking cues.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Hana’s pressure and torn feelings, but they often remain cognitive rather than emotionally probing. Stronger empathy would explore the social cost of saying no and the fear of being judged or standing out.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays sharply focused on reducing smoking during work breaks and moves logically from ambivalence to a concrete Saturday experiment. The target behavior, plan, and follow-up remain clear throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Hana helps shape the plan by choosing the shift, mint tin, apron pocket, and script, creating shared ownership. Collaboration could be stronger if the interviewer invited more open-ended co-generation before supplying options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer repeatedly affirms Hana’s freedom to choose and avoids demanding commitment. Autonomy support is slightly weakened by the amount of advice and specific behavioral suggestions provided.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Scaling and doability questions help raise Hana’s confidence and elicit a small commitment. The interviewer could better draw out underlying values and self-generated motivations before shifting into advice-giving.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interviewer invites Hana’s preferences and allows them to influence the plan, supporting a collaborative stance. Stronger partnership would involve more explicit co-construction, such as asking Hana to generate her own options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Hana articulates small commitments and confidence gains, showing that some change talk is evoked. The interviewer could more consistently reflect, amplify, and expand Hana’s own change language rather than supplying scripts and strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The interviewer avoids arguing and reframes slipping as information, while moving from a full quit to a smaller experiment. More double-sided reflections and evocative reframing are needed to pivot sustain talk toward change.
