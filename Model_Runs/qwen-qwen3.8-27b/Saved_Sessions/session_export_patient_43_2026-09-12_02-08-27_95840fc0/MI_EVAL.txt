# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance without arguing or moralizing. The interaction feels somewhat procedural and does not fully validate deeper identity-related concerns behind the resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of Eric’s perspective and practical concerns. Reflections mostly track content rather than deeper emotion, fatalism, identity, or tension with his wife.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on smoking and a specific target behavior, delaying the first morning cigarette. The flow moves logically from exploration to a concrete, workable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is strongly shaped by Eric’s own choices, such as gum, water, and a low-key message to his wife. At times, the psychiatrist offers multiple options quickly, making the interaction feel slightly more clinician-guided.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently respects Eric’s choice and avoids pressure, guilt, or pushing a full quit. Statements such as “slips don’t erase the effort” strongly support self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and the importance ruler are used, but the conversation moves quickly into advice and behavior planning. Eric’s intrinsic values and deeper reasons for change are not fully explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Eric to choose options and treats him as the decision-maker. Some therapist-generated menus and rapid questioning slightly reduce the sense that Eric is the primary source of solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some change talk around gum, delaying the first cigarette, and a smoke-free morning. The development of change talk is inconsistent and not deeply amplified from Eric’s own values or future self.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance without challenging or reinforcing sustain talk, and gently redirects toward a small step. Some statements, such as “the patch is for the weak,” are not fully reflected or reframed.
