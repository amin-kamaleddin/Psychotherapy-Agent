# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates warm, nonjudgmental acceptance and respects Aisha’s boundary about alcohol. Supportive affirmations maintain regard without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture ambivalence, fatigue, fear of failure, and protective motivations. Some reflections are repetitive or overly optimistic and do not deepen emotional exploration enough for a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicit change talk and a self-generated, realistic plan. The counselor does not fully use confidence, importance, or commitment-scaling strategies to deepen change talk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, respectful, and avoids argument or persuasion around sustain talk. The brief reopening of alcohol and limited exploration of its underlying ambivalence prevent a higher score.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently highlights Aisha’s choice, expertise, and control. The plan is presented as noncoercive and self-directed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains focused on reducing evening smoking and moves systematically from goal identification to a concrete coping plan. Alcohol-related content is deferred without losing focus.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions Aisha as the expert in her own life and co-creates the plan with her. Clinical recommendations are avoided in favor of client-driven ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor primarily elicits Aisha’s own values, goals, and solutions rather than providing reasons for change. Some questions are mildly suggestive, but evocation remains strong.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with respectful, nonargumentative responses and is used to strengthen the client’s sense of doability. This criterion overlaps with collaboration in the final form layout.
