# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and validates Sofia’s ambivalence and stated boundary. Acceptance is supportive but can feel formulaic and not fully emotionally attuned.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers useful surface-level reflections of behavior and goals. Deeper feelings such as anxiety, embarrassment, financial stress, and ambivalence are not consistently reflected.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on marijuana use and a safer-use plan without wandering. Direction is practical and clear, but more problem-solving driven than MI-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is a collaborative structure, with Sofia selecting limits, days, and coping options. The therapist still drives the main framework and pace rather than fully co-creating the plan from Sofia’s values.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges choice and avoids demanding quitting. Repeated specific limits and immediate decision points can create pressure and reduce the sense of free, self-directed change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Evocation is the main weakness, as the session quickly shifts to structured planning. The therapist does not consistently draw out Sofia’s own motivations, values, discrepancy, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia is offered choices and her input influences the session. The process remains uneven and counselor-driven, with rapid agenda-setting and expert-led structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some change talk appears, but it is mainly elicited through menus and practical planning. The therapist does not deeply explore reasons, benefits, needs, confidence, values, or discrepancy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or directly challenging Sofia’s sustain talk. However, there is little use of double-sided reflections or skillful pivots toward change.
