# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental but feels more procedural than deeply warm. Acceptance is adequate, though it lacks more explicit, individualized validation of Sofia’s worth and emotions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are present but remain mostly surface-level. The psychiatrist misses several opportunities to explore Sofia’s guilt, fear, shame, and identity conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and practical weekend coping. Direction could be strengthened by connecting the plan more consistently to Sofia’s deeper values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally invites input and co-creates practical options with Sofia. Collaboration is solid, though the session becomes somewhat therapist-driven by offering many solutions before fully exploring Sofia’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offers choices and avoids coercion, allowing Sofia to guide the session. Autonomy support is strong, though it could be further strengthened with more explicit ownership language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some evocative efforts, such as importance/confidence rulers and preference-based questions. However, the psychiatrist frequently moves into advice and scripts before fully eliciting Sofia’s own motivation and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes room for Sofia’s preferences, but the solution-focused approach can reduce the sense of a true partnership. More consistent emphasis on Sofia as the expert on her own life would strengthen this criterion.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia produces several change statements, and the psychiatrist acknowledges her motivation. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or amplify change talk in a way that would further build Sofia’s commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not reinforce sustain talk and responds with some acceptance. Still, there is limited use of MI-consistent techniques to actively reframe Sofia’s concerns about friends, judgment, and past lapses.
