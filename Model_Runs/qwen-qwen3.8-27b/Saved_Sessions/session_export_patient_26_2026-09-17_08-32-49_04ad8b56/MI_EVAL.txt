# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides strong nonjudgmental validation and normalizes the client's skepticism and fear throughout. Some interpretive framing and one generic prompt ('tell me more about that') keep it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects the ambivalence around needing relief versus her son breathing smoke and the exhaustion/panic theme. Empathy is uneven; misses deeper exploration of financial strain and distrust, and includes one weak generic prompt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session maintains a coherent focus on small stress/smoking coping steps with reasonable progression. The earlier childcare goal (neighbor, Head Start) is not revisited or integrated into a closing plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Builds on the client's own language and ideas without an imposed agenda; she clearly co-constructs the action sequence. Limited explicit collaborative language ('let's…together') and some idea generation remains counselor-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites the client's perspective and affirms her ownership ('you're the one holding that together, not me'). The menu of suggestions and 'if you were to try pressing those feet…' prompt subtly steers toward a specific strategy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client self-generates coping ideas (balcony, counting, ice cube, feet press) through effective open questions. Evocation is occasionally interrupted by therapist-generated options and doesn't probe deeper values or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions the client as expert and follows her lead from childcare stress into the smoking target. A few counselor-introduced categories (touch cold, press feet, hum) make it slightly more guided than fully equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client articulates concrete change ideas (asking neighbor, balcony, feet-press sequence) that are reinforced in her own words. No systematic DARN/CAT elicitation or end-of-session summary/consolidation of her change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk (skepticism, fear of 'no,' trust concerns) is acknowledged and reflected without argument. Lacks more active softening moves (scaling, reframing) especially on the financial and family-distrust barriers.
