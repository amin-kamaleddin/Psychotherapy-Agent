# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental throughout, with strong unconditional positive regard. Slightly undermined by repeated formulaic prompts ('I understand. Can you tell me more about that?') that feel rehearsed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy with reflections that capture the emotional and experiential core of Nadine's experience. Complex reflections go well beyond paraphrasing to name underlying feelings, meanings, and mechanisms.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Focus is somewhat diffuse, exploring multiple themes without building toward a clear target behavior or concrete next step. The session meanders through emotional exploration without strategic directional momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with consistent joint exploration and no lecturing or prescribing. Not fully realized at the highest level due to lack of co-created plans or explicit shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Nadine's agency and never imposes external standards or tells her what to do. Could be strengthened by more explicitly reinforcing her right to choose and her authority over her own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some meaningful evocation occurs, but it is inconsistent and often misses key opportunities to deepen self-motivation. The psychiatrist does not systematically draw out Nadine's values, aspirations, or own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a curious, non-authoritative stance and consistently invites Nadine's perspective. Falls short of full partnership due to lack of explicit power-sharing, goal negotiation, and the formulaic repeated prompts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change-relevant language from Nadine but does not consistently amplify, reflect back, or strategically deepen it. Opportunities to build momentum through change talk are partially missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, which is appropriate but largely passive. Lacks active softening techniques such as double-sided reflections or reframing to help Nadine see alternative perspectives.
