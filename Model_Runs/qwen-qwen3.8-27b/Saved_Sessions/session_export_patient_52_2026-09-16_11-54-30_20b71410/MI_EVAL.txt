# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys warmth, respect, and nonjudgment while normalizing Irina’s stress and ambivalence. Acceptance could be stronger by more explicitly validating her guilt, fear of becoming “damaged,” and feeling trapped.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong and captures her core conflict between needing a break and wanting to be present for her son. Empathy could go deeper by consistently exploring the emotional meaning beneath her statements rather than shifting quickly to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and moves logically from values to a concrete plan. There is little topic drift and the pacing is well aligned with MI.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Irina’s preferences clearly shape the final plan, showing genuine collaboration. The interaction could be more co-created by reducing clinician-generated lists and giving more space for Irina to lead the planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly supports choice, emphasizes a trial approach, and avoids pressure or guilt. Autonomy could be strengthened by more explicitly reinforcing that Irina fully owns whether to try, modify, or stop the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation is evoked, especially around protecting time with her son and trying one drink. The session would be stronger if it elicited more of Irina’s own coping ideas and reasons before offering multiple external options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Irina is treated as the expert on her own life, and her choices shape the plan. A more balanced partnership could be achieved by letting her generate more solutions before the clinician introduces structured options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Irina to identify her own values, priorities, and doable next steps, and reinforces her change statements. Change talk could be amplified more systematically through deeper questions about what change would mean to her.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well, with acceptance of her need for a break and framing slips as information rather than failure. Softening could be more strategic by using double-sided reflections before offering alternatives.
