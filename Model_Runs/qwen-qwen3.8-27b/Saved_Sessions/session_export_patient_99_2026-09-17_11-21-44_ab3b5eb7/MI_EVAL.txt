# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates warm, unconditional positive regard throughout, never judging or minimizing Matteo's experience. Pressure is explicitly removed and his 'whatever' energy is validated as legitimate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening with vivid, accurate reflections that capture both content and underlying emotional quality. Examples include the 'ground shifted' metaphor and honoring his ambivalence about the memory gaps.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains excellent progressive focus on alcohol use and its functional impact, moving logically from awkwardness to pattern exploration to Thursday consequences. No drift or wandering; each question builds with clear momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session functions as a genuine joint exploration, with the clinician using Matteo's own language ('the thread,' 'the stack,' 'the lever'). Decisions about direction follow his cues rather than an imposed agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently supported—'It's your life,' 'no pressure,' 'you don't owe me a big reaction.' When Matteo draws the line that 'I can't drink isn't an option,' it is respected completely without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All insight and motivation emerges from Matteo himself through evocative questions; the clinician never states reasons for change or provides information about harms. The psychiatrist acts as a mirror and questioner, never as a source of the answer.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise about Matteo's life is consistently positioned with Matteo, who is affirmed as the analyst of his own situation. The power dynamic is genuinely shared, with the clinician never assuming expertise or lecturing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Matteo produces meaningful change talk and the clinician effectively creates conditions for it to emerge. However, the clinician could have more explicitly amplified and reinforced these statements before moving on, such as reflecting back his desire to protect Thursday sharpness with emphasis.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well without being reinforced or dwelled upon, with appropriate validation followed by gentle pivots. A fifth point would require more active, strategic double-sided reflections that acknowledge the social value while also highlighting the cost side.
