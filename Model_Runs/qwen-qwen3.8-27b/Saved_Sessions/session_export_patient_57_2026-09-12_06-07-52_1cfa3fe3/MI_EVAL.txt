# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and offers validation, but acceptance feels more instrumental than deeply affirming. There is limited evidence of warm, individualized unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects surface concerns about unfairness and team belonging adequately. However, deeper ambivalence around fear, loyalty, and social punishment is not fully captured or explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the target behavior and moves consistently toward concrete next steps. The main limitation is that direction is more compliance-oriented than MI-consistent change focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Jamal contributes some ideas, and his wording is partly incorporated into the final plan. However, the psychiatrist supplies most of the strategies, scripts, and structure, making the partnership uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers multiple options and respects Jamal's refusal to roleplay. However, frequent prescribing and scripted language undermine Jamal's sense of ownership and personal choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist mainly provides advice, scripts, and behavioral strategies rather than evoking Jamal's own motivation. This limits Jamal's opportunity to articulate the importance, need, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Although some questions invite perspective, the overall stance is expert-driven. The psychiatrist frequently generates options and language, positioning the counselor rather than Jamal as the primary decision-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some change-relevant language is elicited, but it is not skillfully amplified, reflected back, or deepened. The focus remains more on compliance and concrete steps than on strengthening motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist tolerates sustain talk without arguing or judgment. However, there is limited strategic use of double-sided reflections or reframing to help Jamal resolve ambivalence.
