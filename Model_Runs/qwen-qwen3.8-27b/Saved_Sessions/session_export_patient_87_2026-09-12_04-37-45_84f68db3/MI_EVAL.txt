# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, warm, and generally nonjudgmental, validating Marco’s effort and practical concerns. Acceptance is somewhat weaker around his emotional self-doubt and fear of failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist gives useful reflections of Marco’s ambivalence and home-based barriers, tracking his stated experience well. Empathy remains more cognitive and logistical than deeply emotional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on weight-related eating and builds a concrete, actionable Wednesday plan. The approach is somewhat checklist-like and less client-led than it could be.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marco’s preferences clearly shape the plan, and the interaction feels collaborative rather than lecture-like. The psychiatrist frequently supplies ready-made options, which slightly limits co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly invites choice and offers low-demand options, supporting Marco’s sense of ownership. Repeated directives such as expecting a check-in create a subtle expert-driven push.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits useful statements of readiness, confidence, and redefined success. Evocation is limited by frequent clinician-generated problem-solving and less exploration of core values or identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Marco’s input and allows his choices to guide the plan, showing meaningful partnership. The role remains somewhat expert-led due to repeated pre-selected options and scheduling directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Marco offers helpful change statements, such as trying Wednesday and redefining a win despite minor setbacks. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or deepen these statements before moving into problem-solving.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Marco’s sustain talk without arguing or reinforcing it. However, the clinician often jumps into tactical options rather than using reflections or value-based pivots to resolve ambivalence.
