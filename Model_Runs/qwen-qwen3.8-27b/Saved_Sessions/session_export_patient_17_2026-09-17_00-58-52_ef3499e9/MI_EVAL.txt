# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating stance toward the client’s shame and difficulty. However, some statements are slightly interpretive rather than purely accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers strong reflections of the client’s ambivalence, exhaustion, and fear of judgment. The reflections could deepen further and avoid repetitive overlap near the end.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on smoking cessation and moves toward a concrete first step. It occasionally circulates around emotional weight without clearly summarizing toward an action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows the client’s leads and invites input on what might work. More explicit language about working together would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice, avoids pressure, and encourages a small self-selected step. Some affirmations subtly frame the client’s motivation in ways that could feel mildly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently invites the client to articulate her own values, goals, and reasons for change. It could more deeply evoke self-efficacy, past successes, or identity-level motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids a dominant expert role and lets the client’s concerns guide the conversation. More explicit client-as-expert and agenda-setting language would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and affirms change talk, including the client identifying a doable first step. It does not consistently amplify or deepen the client’s confidence and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and reframes goals as small and imperfect. However, the session spends too much time on failure and slipping, and lacks stronger double-sided reflections.
