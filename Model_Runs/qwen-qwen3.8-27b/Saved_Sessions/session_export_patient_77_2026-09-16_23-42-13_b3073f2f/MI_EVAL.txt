# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming stance throughout the session. Liyana’s fears and self-doubt are validated without correction, criticism, or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Liyana’s ambivalence, protective reasoning, and body-based signals. The psychiatrist moves beyond surface paraphrasing to reflect deeper emotional and cognitive meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a clear target behavior: reducing scale checking and experimenting with hunger cues at lunch. The progression from identifying the problem to developing a concrete plan is strong, though the opening was somewhat broad.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works as a thinking partner, helping Liyana refine her own ideas rather than prescribing solutions. The collaboration could be stronger by less frequently offering multiple options within single questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Liyana is consistently invited to make her own choices, and the psychiatrist avoids pressure or rescue. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming that all decisions are up to her and that there is no wrong answer.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Liyana’s own motivations, values, and intentions rather than providing external reasons for change. Deeper evocation of confidence, values, and future vision could further strengthen the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session clearly conveys that Liyana is the expert on her own experience and is the one building the plan. A few moments, especially when offering menu-style options, could be reframed to more fully invite her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits and reinforces change talk related to desire, intention, and ability. More exploration of confidence, need, and values-based motivation would make the change talk richer and more specific.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without being reinforced and is often gently reframed into experimentation or control. However, Liyana’s worries remain prominent by the end, suggesting the ambivalence was not fully softened.
