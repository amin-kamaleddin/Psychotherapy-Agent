# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, specific, and genuine unconditional positive regard throughout. Affirmations are individualized to the client's struggles rather than formulaic, and no judgment or disapproval is expressed even when sustain talk appears.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go beyond surface content to capture the emotional and existential meaning of the client's words. The clinician accurately tracks the evolution of understanding and reconstructs narrative significance with precision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical focus on the target behavior while exploring underlying concerns. The progression from social meaning to self-generated action step builds momentum without feeling rushed or forced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine joint exploration where the client's responses determine direction and content. The session ends with the client generating her own vision, the hallmark of true collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces ownership of choices with zero coercive or directive language. The framing of the one-weekend trial as the client's own experiment explicitly positions the decision as hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is drawn from within the client through strategic questioning rather than imposed externally. The final image is entirely the client's, generated through evocative questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that wisdom about the client's change resides within her. The clinician never provides unsolicited advice, and every reflection is framed as an invitation to confirm or correct.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently identifies, reflects, and amplifies change talk with well-timed questions. Could have gone deeper on self-efficacy and commitment language when the client proposed the one-weekend trial.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by validating without reinforcing, then gently introducing discrepancy. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections to hold both sides in tension simultaneously.
