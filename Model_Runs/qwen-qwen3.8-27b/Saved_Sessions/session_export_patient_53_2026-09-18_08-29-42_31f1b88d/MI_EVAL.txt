# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, non-judgmental stance throughout, validating Isabella's experience without labeling or minimizing her contributions. The warmth is somewhat clinical and could be slightly deeper with more explicit normalization of the emotional toll of chronic avoidance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional reflective listening with complex, nuanced reflections that capture both cognitive and emotional dimensions of Isabella's experience. The psychiatrist consistently builds on the client's own metaphors (the 'wall,' the 'picture,' the 'couch') and never misrepresents her experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session follows a clear, logical progression from establishing the client's experience to identifying barriers, exploring mechanisms, and evoking a preferred vision. A minor area for improvement is the lack of an explicit closing summary or collaboratively identified next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frames the conversation as a joint exploration, using language like 'I'm curious' and 'I wonder,' and builds reflections directly from Isabella's own language. Could be slightly strengthened by more explicit co-construction of meaning and power-sharing language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports autonomy through language emphasizing Isabella's choice and ownership, and never tells her what to do or imposes a plan. Could be enhanced by more explicit affirmations of Isabella's capacity to choose at key moments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All change talk and motivations emerge organically from Isabella herself; the psychiatrist never provides reasons for change or gives advice. Questions are skillfully designed to draw out the client's own values, hopes, and emotional responses.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative, curious stance rather than an expert-driven approach, consistently inviting Isabella's input. The partnership could be slightly strengthened by more explicit power-sharing language and open-ended agenda co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes, amplifies, and names Isabella's change talk, reframing fleeting moments of hope as meaningful evidence of motivation. Could be further developed by evoking more ability talk and commitment language toward concrete readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without arguing or reinforcing, with skillful redirects from the content of resistance to the emotional experience underneath. Could be improved with more explicit double-sided reflections that validate both the protective and change-oriented parts.
