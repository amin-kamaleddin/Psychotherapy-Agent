# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental, respectful stance and validates Mei's ambivalence without blame. Acceptance is strong but not always emotionally warm or individualized enough for a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening captures ambivalence and the link between drinking, social context, and pill use. Empathy is good but sometimes misses deeper emotional cues and moment-to-moment responsiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use in social situations and contraceptive reliability, moving toward a specific 11 p.m. ride plan. Clear forward movement is reduced by early repeated broad agenda questions and two related targets not fully sequenced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is framed as joint problem-solving and avoids lecturing or taking over. Collaboration is strong but occasionally therapist-generated options interrupt full client-led planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Mei's choices, avoids pushing abstinence, and emphasizes plans that feel keepable. Autonomy support is strong but could more explicitly reinforce freedom to modify, delay, or reject the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, evocative questions draw out Mei's own motivations, values, and a practical 11 p.m. hard stop. Evocation is effective but not consistently deep enough to amplify confidence or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Mei as the expert and builds on her contributions, such as non-drinking friends and a pre-booked ride. Partnership is strong but not perfect because the therapist sometimes supplies the options being built upon.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encourages meaningful change talk, including not wanting to be like her dad, reliability with birth control, and the ride plan feeling doable. Change talk is well evoked but not consistently amplified or linked to values and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with Mei's desire not to be the boring one and reframes all-or-nothing concerns into smaller steps. Sustain talk is handled well but is not always fully transformed into stronger motivation or commitment.
