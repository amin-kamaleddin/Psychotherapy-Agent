# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates respect, nonjudgment, and acceptance of Daniela’s pacing throughout. The support is strong but somewhat formulaic, lacking deeper individualized validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Daniela’s stated concerns about keeping the disclosure simple and low-key. However, empathy stays mostly at the content level rather than exploring the deeper emotional weight of her fear and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the target behavior of brief peer disclosure. The work is logically organized and keeps Daniela’s concerns central, though it becomes somewhat narrowed to wording and logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniela’s preferences meaningfully shape the plan, and the psychiatrist adapts to her desire for brevity. However, the psychiatrist generates most of the scripts and options, making collaboration more procedural than truly equal-partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses optional language and respects Daniela’s refusals to rehearse aloud or carry a script. Autonomy support is strong, though the repeated offering of structured options can still feel subtly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes some evocative moves by exploring worries and possible benefits of disclosure. However, the session shifts too quickly to solution-focused scripting and does not consistently draw out Daniela’s own motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Daniela’s preferences and follows her choices, showing genuine partnership. Yet the psychiatrist remains the primary generator of ideas, functioning more like a coach providing a menu than following Daniela as the expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited early, especially around the possible benefit of friends knowing without judgment. The psychiatrist does not consistently build on, amplify, or deepen this change talk throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist normalizes Daniela’s hesitation and avoids arguing with her sustain talk. However, the session lacks more advanced MI techniques, such as double-sided reflections that acknowledge the protective function of secrecy while opening space for change.
