# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, repeatedly reducing shame around fear, avoidance, and indecision. Acceptance is somewhat formulaic and could be deeper by affirming Elena's specific values and complex feelings more explicitly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist provides accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking around relief, shock, jitteriness, and self-protection. Empathy could be stronger by more fully reflecting ambivalence around contraception, safety, responsibility, and planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The stated target behavior is contraception/prevention after a pregnancy scare, but the session quickly drifts into general anxiety relief, scrolling, and breathing. The psychiatrist does not re-establish focus on contraception, summarize ambivalence, or collaboratively identify a small next step related to prevention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves as a partner, invites client input, offers options, and adapts to Elena's preference to stay with relief. Collaboration is limited by becoming more about accommodating avoidance than co-creating a concrete, client-driven plan related to contraception.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is strong, with repeated emphasis on Elena's right to choose, no pressure to decide now, and clear support for her pace and decision-making. The stance avoids coercion, lectures, or insistence on choosing a contraceptive method.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes some effort to elicit Elena's own motivations through opening questions and invitations to identify what would feel helpful. Evocation is limited because the session shifts to psychoeducation, breathing, and closure rather than systematically drawing out values, confidence, readiness, or future-self motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids an expert-driven stance, gives Elena options, and invites her input about what feels most useful. Partnership is present but not fully activated because the psychiatrist does not explicitly position Elena as the authority on her own body, values, and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some effort to evoke change-relevant language, especially around wanting to avoid being caught off guard again. The psychiatrist does not consistently develop or amplify this change talk, and there is no evocative summary of motivation before closure.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elena offers multiple forms of sustain talk, such as not being ready, being careful, handling it in the moment, and not making it a big deal. The psychiatrist avoids arguing but does not actively soften these statements with double-sided reflections or gentle pivots toward change.
