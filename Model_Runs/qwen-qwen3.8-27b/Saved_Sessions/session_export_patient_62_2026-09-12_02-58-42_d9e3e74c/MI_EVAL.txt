# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Jordan’s frustration. However, acceptance could be stronger by more explicitly affirming Jordan’s worth as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections of Jordan’s exhaustion, unfairness, and discouragement. Deeper empathy would explore the underlying fear of helplessness or futility.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the presenting concern of work-related overwhelm and guides it toward concrete steps. Direction could improve by fully negotiating the target with the client before offering options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates by offering choices and adjusting to the client’s energy level. More co-creation would occur if the client generated more of the content and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports autonomy by asking for preferences rather than giving directives. It could be strengthened with clearer language about Jordan’s ownership and choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** This is the weakest area, as the counselor frequently moves into concrete suggestions rather than eliciting Jordan’s own motivations. More exploration of values, confidence, and reasons for change is needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites client input and uses collaborative language. Partnership could be stronger by allowing the client to lead the agenda more fully.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some attempts to evoke and affirm change talk, such as wanting less overwhelm and taking a small step. Change talk is not consistently amplified or explored deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses some double-sided reflections to soften sustain talk. More consistent redirection toward exceptions, agency, or capability would be more effective.
