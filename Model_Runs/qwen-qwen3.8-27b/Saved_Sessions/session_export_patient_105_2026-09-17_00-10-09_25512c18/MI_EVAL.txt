# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental stance with validating language. A 5 would require more explicit, individualized affirmations of Naomi’s worth and courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor shows strong reflective listening and accurately tracks the anxiety cycle. Empathy could deepen further into shame, fear of being seen, and feelings of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on social anxiety in photography settings and moves through the problem logically. A 5 would include a clearer collaborative summary linking the discussion to a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Naomi’s responses shape the direction of the session, and the tone is exploratory rather than instructive. The counselor still offers some interpretive frameworks and micro-goals, which slightly limits full collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions and avoids direct advice, supporting Naomi’s agency. However, the session does not consistently emphasize that Naomi is in charge of choosing whether or when to try something.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor effectively uses Naomi’s own language, such as “oh, there’s the heat,” to elicit insights. Evocation is more focused on symptom management than on deeper motivation, values, or identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist repeatedly invites Naomi’s perspective and positions her as the expert on her own experience. Some leading suggestions slightly reduce the fully collaborative quality of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor creates several opportunities for change talk and reinforces Naomi’s idea of naming the heat. The session could explore and amplify importance, desire, confidence, and commitment more strongly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids reinforcing doubt and uses a gentle paradoxical reframe to reduce pressure. More strategic double-sided reflections or values-based pivots would better soften the final sustain talk.
