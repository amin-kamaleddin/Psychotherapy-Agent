# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating with no instances of disapproval or shaming. Slightly performative or overly polished affirmations at times prevent a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic attunement with reflections that capture identity-level meaning (e.g., the device as a witness to being broken). Tracks emotional nuance and underlying meaning rather than just surface content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from barrier identification to concrete commitment. Minor deviation when exploring Rowan's typical day briefly widens the lens to general life management.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative engagement where Rowan's ideas consistently shape the session direction. The psychiatrist acts as a skilled guide who follows the client's lead while maintaining strategic direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Rowan's ownership and freedom to choose, never using directive or obligation-based language. The reframing of the device from compliance to self-knowledge is a powerful act of autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every piece of motivation is generated by Rowan himself, elicited through carefully crafted questions. Textbook evocation — the psychiatrist never provides the 'right answer' but creates conditions for self-discovery.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly places expertise and wisdom within Rowan, never providing advice or clinical explanations. The power dynamic is consistently egalitarian with the client's contributions substantially shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits authentic, internally-generated change talk including desire, ability, and need statements. The shift from precontemplation to a concrete behavioral commitment ('Tonight. Just once.') demonstrates mastery.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by acknowledging without reinforcing and gently redirecting. Missed opportunity to more actively challenge the identity-level sustain talk ('that's just how I am') with a double-sided reflection.
