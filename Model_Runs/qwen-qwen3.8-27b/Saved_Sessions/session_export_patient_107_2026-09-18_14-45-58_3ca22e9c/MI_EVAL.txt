# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard without judgment throughout the session. Validates the client's perspective at every turn, normalizing his experience and affirming the ritual's significance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflections that capture both content and emotional undertone with precision. Demonstrates consistent attunement to subtle shifts in the client's ambivalence and emotional state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a logical progression from establishing the issue to a concrete next step without drifting off topic. However, the resolution feels somewhat emergent rather than deliberately guided toward a specific action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative stance throughout, with the session clearly co-constructed by both parties. The client's ideas—ritual, pushback on the number three, the adjusting frame—directly shape where the conversation goes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and freedom to choose, explicitly removing external pressure. The client's closing statement confirms the autonomy support was felt and effective.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out the client's own reasons for change through thoughtful open questions and reflections. Could be deeper with more values-based or identity-based elicitation rather than relying primarily on situational evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within the client, never assuming the expert role. The session is clearly co-constructed, with the client's contributions substantially shaping direction and outcomes.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively facilitates meaningful change talk through evoking questions and skillful amplification of the client's own statements. Could be elevated with more structured eliciting techniques such as importance or confidence scales.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk without arguing or dismissing, creating space for change talk to emerge naturally. Could benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections or strategic reframing of the client's resistant statements.
