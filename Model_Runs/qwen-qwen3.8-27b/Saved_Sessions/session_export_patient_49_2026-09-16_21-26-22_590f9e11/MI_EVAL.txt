# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor showed strong, nonjudgmental acceptance of the client’s choices and ambivalence. Warmth was respectful but somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Early reflections were accurate and nuanced, but empathy was reduced by repeating the same question multiple times after the client had already answered. This indicated inconsistent attunement to the client’s contributions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking and related dental and work-stress concerns, moving from ambivalence to a small plan and self-monitoring. The repeated question created some circularity and reduced forward momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor followed the client’s lead and incorporated the client’s ideas, such as keeping the pack in the car and using gum. The repeated question and language switch slightly weakened the collaborative flow.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor respected the client’s preference to cut back rather than quit and allowed the client to define success. It could have been stronger by more explicitly affirming the client’s freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor effectively drew out the client’s own reasons for change, including protecting her smile and managing stress. Deeper exploration of values, confidence, importance, or commitment could have strengthened evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintained a collaborative stance and avoided direct advice even when the client asked for an opinion. The repeated question and abrupt language shift slightly undermined the partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited through the client’s plan to cut back, use gum, and monitor the pack. The counselor did not consistently reinforce, amplify, or deepen the client’s competence and motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor validated sustain talk about work stress and not smoking daily without arguing or minimizing it. The response could have been stronger by connecting sustain talk more explicitly to values and then pivoting to change.
