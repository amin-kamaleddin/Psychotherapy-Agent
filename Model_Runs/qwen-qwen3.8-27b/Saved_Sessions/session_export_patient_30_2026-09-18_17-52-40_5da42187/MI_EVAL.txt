# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout, with multiple validations of David's thoughts and feelings. Slightly clinical polish and lack of explicit normalization of ambivalence keep it just below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional accuracy in capturing both content and emotional undercurrent, with nuanced reflections that deepen each turn (e.g., reframing the issue from medical to relational/autonomy-based). Attunement to micro-expressions of vulnerability demonstrates sophisticated perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from rapport through to a concrete action step. Minor missed opportunity to explore the 'pushy' comment more deeply before moving on.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with language positioning the psychiatrist as a partner in exploration, and the action plan emerges from David's own statement. Could be more explicit by inviting David's input on session direction or naming the partnership directly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports David's autonomy, validating both choices as legitimate and affirming his ownership of decisions. The masterful question about making change feel 'less like something being done TO you' directly addresses his core concern.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of open questions, self-of-self language, and affirmations to elicit change talk from David without persuasion. Could have been pushed deeper in the closing phase by exploring what success would look like in the doctor conversation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** David is consistently positioned as the expert on his own experience, with the psychiatrist asking rather than advising. The session structure follows a somewhat predictable trajectory that is slightly counselor-guided rather than fully client-directed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillful use of open questions and the 'part of you' technique to access a pro-change voice, resulting in David producing genuine change talk and a concrete action step. Could have amplified and elaborated on David's change talk more in the final exchanges.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook handling of sustain talk — each concern is acknowledged, validated, and gently reframed without argument or dismissal. The transformation of David's 'pushy' complaint into a discussion about autonomy is particularly skillful.
