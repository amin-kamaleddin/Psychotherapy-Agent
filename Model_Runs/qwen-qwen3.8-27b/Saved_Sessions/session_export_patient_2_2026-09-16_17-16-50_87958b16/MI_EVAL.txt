# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, validates the client’s experience, and avoids moralizing. Warmth is generally good, though it is slightly polished and interpretive rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects the client’s fatigue, ambivalence, and need for pauses. Deeper underlying emotions such as shame, loneliness, or health anxiety are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reducing evening smoking and narrows quickly to a concrete, client-driven goal. The client’s boundary about alcohol is respected without losing focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with the client as an equal partner and co-creates the plan and wording of the sister text. The final plan is mutually developed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client’s ownership is clearly affirmed, and options are offered without pressure. The psychiatrist supports the client’s sense of choice and self-direction throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client’s own strategies, barriers, and motivations. Some questions are structured or leading, limiting deeper elicitation of personal values and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently treated as the expert on her own life. Her ideas about timing, tea, and contacting her sister directly shape the session and plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits several change-related statements and reinforces practical steps. Change talk is not consistently deepened into stronger values, confidence, or future-oriented motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without argument or reinforcement. The session could use more double-sided reflections and value-based pivots to actively challenge minimization.
