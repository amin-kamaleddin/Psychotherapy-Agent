# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids shaming or moralizing about wine use. Acceptance is strong but somewhat professionally restrained, with limited deeper individualized affirmation of the person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects ambivalence between cultural tradition and pregnancy concern, and validates the client's need for autonomy and factual information. Empathy could be deeper in response to the client's request for evidence and the emotional meaning of the ritual.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on pausing wine during pregnancy and guides toward practical steps with the partner. Direction is clear but could more explicitly consolidate the agreed-upon behavior change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving and builds on the client's ideas for a shared household plan. Collaboration is strong, treating the client and partner as equal participants.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently avoids pressure, rules, or advice, and repeatedly frames any pause as the client's own strategic choice. This is a clear strength with high-quality autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open questions to elicit the client's values, strategies, and own reasons for a possible plan. Evocation could be more precise with targeted follow-up questions to deepen the client's own change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the client's input and positions her as the expert on her own life and family context. Partnership is good but could be more explicit about shared power, agenda-setting, and final client control.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and reinforces change talk, leading the client to articulate a temporary pause, partner collaboration, and environmental supports. Cultivation would improve with more explicit DARN-C moves such as scaling importance or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with or amplifying the client's skepticism about zero-tolerance evidence and validates the need for facts. It could more actively pivot sustain talk using double-sided reflections or reframes.
