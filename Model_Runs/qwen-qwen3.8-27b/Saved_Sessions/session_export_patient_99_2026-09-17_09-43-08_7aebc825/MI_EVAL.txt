# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance of the client's ambivalence and minimization. No disapproving, labeling, or pressuring language was present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured the client's internal conflict between social enjoyment and health concern. Emotional depth could be increased by further exploring the meaning of the injury, fear of rejection, or identity as the 'sober friend.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remained focused on alcohol use, weekend patterns, and harm reduction with little drift. A clearer anchor around a specific, measurable target behavior or next step would strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor consistently acted as an equal partner using 'we' language and joint exploration. The client actively selected and modified strategies, shaping the session through collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor supported client ownership by avoiding advice, commands, or pressure toward abstinence. Statements reinforced that choices and ideas came from the client, not the counselor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor skillfully elicited the client's own motivations, values, and solutions rather than providing reasons for change. Client-generated change talk was reflected and amplified effectively.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The counselor conveyed a clear power-sharing stance by treating the client as the expert on his own life and choices. The client's preferences substantially determined the session's direction and content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively invited the client to articulate his own reasons and ideas for change. The skill was not fully realized because the session did not consolidate change talk into a clearer commitment or concrete next step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor responded to sustain talk without argument, minimization, or reinforcement of opposition. Some sustain talk, such as continuing to pace drinks, was not fully examined in relation to the client's stated goals.
