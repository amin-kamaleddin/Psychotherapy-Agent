# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent, nonjudgmental respect is shown through normalization and affirmation, though it remains somewhat professional rather than deeply warm. There is less explicit affirmation of Mateo’s worth independent of performance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate, emotionally attuned reflections of ambivalence, shame, and fear. Some reflections are slightly interpretive or summarizing rather than fully exploring deeper pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on reentry goals and narrows to a concrete, values-based behavior. The link to reducing recidivism and a clear next-step summary could be stronger.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Joint language and client-centered questions create a collaborative tone. However, the psychiatrist still leads much of the structure and does not visibly let Mateo direct the session more.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids directives and invites Mateo to define what would count as progress. Autonomy support would be stronger by more explicitly affirming Mateo’s own decision-making and internal standard for “enough.”

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out Mateo’s values and a concrete change idea in his own words. The approach is sometimes suggestive and does not fully elicit confidence, readiness, or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a non-authoritarian stance and asks for Mateo’s perspective. Partnership is limited by the psychiatrist still leading the agenda and introducing interpretive frames.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked around stability, presence with his child, and a manageable step. The psychiatrist does not consistently use change-talk categories, scaling, or strategic amplification to strengthen agency.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled well through dual-focus reflections and validation rather than argument. The strongest ambivalence, that imperfection means failure, remains partly unresolved.
