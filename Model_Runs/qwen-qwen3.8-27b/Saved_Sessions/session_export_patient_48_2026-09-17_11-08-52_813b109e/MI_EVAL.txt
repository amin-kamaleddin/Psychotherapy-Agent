# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens with specific, genuine affirmation tied to the client's context and maintains a warm, non-judgmental tone throughout. Zero labeling or defensiveness when the client rationalizes or self-deprecates.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistently high-quality complex reflections that capture both behavioral patterns and emotional significance (e.g., picking up on 'bothered' over 'tired'). Progressively deepens to track the client's underlying values and the all-or-nothing thinking pattern.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical progression from ambivalence to concrete (if small) action planning. Minor pivot toward football history could be seen as slightly tangential, though it ultimately serves the practical goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a model of genuine equal partnership, with the client driving content and the psychiatrist providing the reflective, exploratory framework. Builds on the client's ideas rather than introducing external ones.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional power-sharing language ('that's your call,' 'you don't have to have the answer yet') with no instances of imposing, advising, or using directive language. Choices are consistently framed as the client's own.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Motivation is consistently sourced from the client's own experience and values, with no external reasoning or health statistics offered. Could be stronger in systematically targeting DARN-CAT categories, particularly ability and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert on his own life with genuinely open, curious questions. The session is clearly shaped by the client's contributions rather than a pre-planned agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk throughout, validating the client's own insights as meaningful. Could more explicitly reinforce ability and confidence statements (e.g., 'you've already figured out what makes it work').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully uses double-sided reflections and gentle reframing to honor sustain talk without argument or counter-persuasion. The session ends before the final sustain talk statement can be addressed, leaving that moment unevaluated.
