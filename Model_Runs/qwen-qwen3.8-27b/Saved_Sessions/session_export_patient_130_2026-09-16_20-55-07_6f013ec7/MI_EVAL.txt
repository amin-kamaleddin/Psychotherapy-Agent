# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a nonjudgmental and respectful stance while normalizing Viktor's ambivalence. The tone is supportive but somewhat scripted, with limited deeper individualization of his resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Viktor's feelings of being controlled, his need for autonomy, and his concern for the marriage. The empathy is strong but stays mostly at the content level and feels repetitive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session intermittently connects the presenting problem to drug use but largely drifts toward marital tension and the wife's behavior. More explicit focus on reducing drug use and developing discrepancy is needed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports Viktor's expertise and engages his practical idea of a quiet evening or week. Collaboration is slightly weakened by repeated menu-style questions rather than fully following his language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure, lectures, and forced admission while emphasizing Viktor's choice and readiness. It could be stronger by explicitly affirming his right not to change immediately.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some values around a quiet home, trust, and presence. However, evocation is limited because it does not consistently draw out motivation, ability, or commitment to reduce drug use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The conversation is collaborative and allows Viktor's ideas to shape the session. Repeated structured options make the interaction feel slightly leading rather than fully responsive to his language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Viktor expresses some change-related language around a calmer home and being present. This change talk is not consistently linked to reducing drug use and is not fully amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and uses two-sided reflections when Viktor minimizes the role of his use. The main limitation is insufficient redirection of sustain talk and externalization back to Viktor's own behavior.
