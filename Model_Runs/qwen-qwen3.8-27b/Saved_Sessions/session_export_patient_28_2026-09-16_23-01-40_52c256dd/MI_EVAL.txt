# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance but is not consistently warm or deeply affirming. Some statements attribute positive motives to the client rather than allowing the client to express them.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects the client’s ambivalence, fatigue, and fear of failure. However, the reflections do not consistently explore deeper emotions such as shame, grief, or identity concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on increasing physical activity and re-establishing a small movement routine. The conversation progresses clearly toward concrete, low-effort action steps without meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates with the client by using client-generated ideas to shape the plan. However, the interaction is somewhat therapist-managed because the psychiatrist frequently supplies multiple suggested options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently emphasizes choice, permission, and low pressure. Some mild steering toward specific timing or actions slightly limits the purity of autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently elicits the client’s own ideas and avoids lecturing. Deeper evocation is limited by suggestive prompts and insufficient focus on values, identity, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the client’s expertise and allows the client’s ideas to influence the direction of the session. Power sharing is good, but the psychiatrist still structures the conversation by offering several candidate solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some client-generated change talk through questions about small, doable actions. However, there is limited effort to strengthen desire, ability, values, confidence, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and reframes the behavior as optional and low-pressure. Some sustain talk remains central, and the psychiatrist does not consistently reduce its emotional impact.
