# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful unconditional positive regard throughout. Never labels Oliver negatively and normalizes his ambivalence without judgment, even when Oliver self-deprecates.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently precise and nuanced, with particularly sophisticated tracking of Oliver's real-time language shifts (e.g., 'kicking someone out' to 'closing the door'). Captures the dual nature of Oliver's experience without oversimplifying.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong focus on the target flatmate conversation for most of the session, but the later portion drifts into broader boundary patterns at work and with friends without explicitly reconnecting to the immediate task. A minor drift that prevents a perfect score.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal collaboration with 'I'm curious' language and reflections offered as observations rather than interpretations. The session feels like two people thinking together, with decisions about what to explore driven by Oliver's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently validates Oliver's timeline and decisions as his own, never using directive or controlling language. Explicitly reinforces that motivation, plan, and execution all reside with Oliver.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful evocation through open questions that elicit Oliver's own change language rather than providing reasons for change. The change talk that emerges is entirely Oliver's own, generated in response to the psychiatrist's evocative prompts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary positioning as fellow explorer rather than authority figure, with explicit statements locating expertise in Oliver. The session is clearly co-directed with no moment of assumed authority or overriding redirect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully cultivates change talk through subtle amplification of Oliver's emerging confidence and gentle invitations to feel the weight lifting. Methods are evocative rather than directive, allowing change language to emerge organically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging complexity without dismissing, but could have used a more explicit double-sided reflection honoring the pain of loss before pivoting. Slightly less consistent dual-focus than a 5 would require.
