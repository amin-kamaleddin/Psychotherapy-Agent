# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming, with statements such as "It makes sense" and "That's not nothing." Warmth is somewhat clinical at times, with occasional framing of ambivalence as progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, accurately capturing Jordan's ambivalence, protective thoughts, and exhaustion. It could go deeper into underlying shame, fear of inadequacy, or identity-level distress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the core concern and a manageable behavioral target. Direction could be stronger by more clearly linking the drawer metaphor to a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Jordan's lead and invites input rather than prescribing solutions. It remains somewhat clinician-led because the psychiatrist repeatedly generates prompts and options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently respected, with attention to choice, perspective, and low-pressure language. It could be stronger by reinforcing ownership over both trying and not trying without subtle preferred options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Questions invite Jordan to generate ideas, but they remain somewhat problem-solving-focused. There is less deep evocation of values, hopes, confidence, or intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a collaborative stance and explicitly values Jordan's expertise. Some therapist-generated options can feel slightly prescriptive rather than fully co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites and affirms some change talk, such as small steps and breathing room. It would benefit from more deliberate amplification of Jordan's values, hope, and capacity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with sustain talk and offers some softening. The session still centers heavily on barriers and futility, with limited pivoting to agency or exceptions.
