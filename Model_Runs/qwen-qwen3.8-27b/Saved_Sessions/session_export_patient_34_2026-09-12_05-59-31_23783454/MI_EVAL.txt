# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicated basic respect and nonjudgmental regard without moralizing. Acceptance remained somewhat task-focused rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflected Dylan's frustration, unfairness, and worry accurately at a surface level. Deeper emotions such as shame, fear, defensiveness, and attachment to his social life were not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the conduct referral, housing, classes, and the statement. However, the focus was narrow and procedural rather than consistently directed toward behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Some collaboration occurred through questions and offering choices, but the psychiatrist mainly acted as an advisor and statement editor. Dylan's broader values, concerns, and expertise were not fully integrated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledged Dylan's choices and allowed him to decide on wording. Autonomy support was weakened by the directive drafting, strategic advice, and statement-writing role.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** A few open questions were used early, but the session quickly shifted to closed, procedural, and factual questions. Dylan's own motivations, values, and readiness for change were not consistently drawn out.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered choices and asked for Dylan's perspective, but the stance was largely directive and expert-driven. The session became more like legal guidance than a collaborative MI relationship.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk was minimal and not fully developed. The psychiatrist did not effectively explore Dylan's values, reasons for change, confidence, or alternatives to future risky behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** This was a major weakness, as the psychiatrist reinforced Dylan's minimization and denial rather than gently softening sustain talk. The final statement was shaped to reduce Dylan's perceived role and accountability.
