# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming stance without moralizing. It remains somewhat task-oriented rather than deeply relational.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are adequate but often surface-level and functional. Deeper feelings such as guilt, self-doubt, and repeated failure are not explored enough.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on the target behavior with a logical, efficient progression. It feels somewhat rigid and CBT-like rather than flexible and MI-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses joint language and builds on Marco's contributions. The collaboration is still somewhat therapist-led because the main frameworks are pre-structured.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offers choices and respects Marco's limits without pressure. It could be stronger by explicitly affirming his right to choose or change the plan at any time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is limited exploration of Marco's values, personal meaning, or internal reasons for change. The session focuses more on practical problem-solving than evoking intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marco is treated as a co-decision-maker through repeated genuine choices. The psychiatrist could deepen partnership by inviting him to shape the method itself, not just select options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk appears but is not systematically elicited, reflected, or amplified. The session underuses the client's own motivations and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is normalized and not punished, which is appropriate. The psychiatrist misses stronger MI techniques, especially in the unresolved final concern about cheating or failure.
