### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with zero judgment or labeling throughout the session. Exemplary validation of Anika's self-knowledge without dismissing or correcting her minimization of use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate reflections that capture both cognitive and emotional dimensions, consistently going beyond surface-level to the meaning beneath Anika's words. Skillfully tracks the evolving conversation with precision, including distinguishing different domains of meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a generally clear focus on the target behavior connected to Anika's stated priority, following a logical progression from rapport through values to plan to coping. Could be slightly tighter in consistently tying broader coping strategies back to the specific behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is fully collaborative, with Anika's ideas consistently shaping the direction and the power dynamic remaining balanced throughout. The psychiatrist asks, Anika answers, and the session follows her lead without imposing any external framework.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary reinforcement of Anika's ownership and freedom to choose, including the masterful statement that the boundary is 'something you're setting for yourself, not something I'm asking you to do.' Never once tells Anika what she should do or uses prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and develops change talk throughout, progressively moving from values to plan to barriers to coping to contingency planning. All motivation is evoked from within Anika without any external pressure, psychoeducation, or argument for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Anika, positioning her as the expert on her own life. Never positions the psychiatrist as having more knowledge about Anika's choices or what would work for her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk without being heavy-handed, using Anika's own words as vehicles for her own insights. Powerfully amplifies Anika's agency by framing progress as her own achievement and strategic thinking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by validating and working within Anika's frame rather than challenging her minimization. Could have more explicitly reframed the social/sustain talk by exploring what the connection with Sarah looks like independent of vaping.
