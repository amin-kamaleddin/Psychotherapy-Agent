# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout; normalizes the client's need for a break without criticism. Warmth is somewhat functional and procedural rather than deeply person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate and client-centered reflections capturing practical constraints and ambivalence. Remains mostly cognitive and practical without fully exploring the emotional texture behind stress and loss of control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the smoking-reduction target behavior throughout the session. Moves efficiently from the general issue to specific, actionable targets with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative stance shaped by the client's stated practical constraints and preferences. Occasionally supplies clinician-generated options rather than leaving space for fuller co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Major strength; consistently frames the intervention as the client's own choice with multiple autonomy-supportive phrases. Strongly validates the client's preference for low pressure and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent, well-aimed open questions effectively evoke the client's own success criteria and plan. Remains mostly practical; does not probe confidence, importance, or deeper motivational meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Client clearly leads the agenda and sets the session direction. Some questions are slightly leading by inserting clinician-generated option menus as defaults.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits action-oriented, client-owned change talk around feasibility and self-management. Does not develop DARN/CAT language, probe confidence/importance, or amplify the client's final strong plan statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk appropriately without argument, persuasion, or dismissal, keeping focus on a smaller manageable step. Lacks more strategic double-sided reflections to actively transform the sustain content.
