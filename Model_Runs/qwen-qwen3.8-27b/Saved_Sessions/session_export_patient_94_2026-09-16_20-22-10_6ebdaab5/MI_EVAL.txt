# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a consistently nonjudgmental and respectful stance, even when Maria was skeptical or defensive. Acceptance was shown through validation of her time pressure, fear, and concerns without moralizing or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and specific, capturing ambivalence, safety fears, and distrust of medical advice. The psychiatrist helped Maria feel understood while keeping the interaction productive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target issue of making asthma management workable within Maria’s time constraints. Each step narrowed toward a concrete, practical next step with the pharmacist.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist worked as an equal partner, building on Maria’s own ideas rather than imposing a medical agenda. The final plan was co-created using her preferred format, timeframe, and decision criteria.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided pressure and repeatedly framed decisions as Maria’s to make. This supported her sense of control while still helping her define a realistic and safe next step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evoked Maria’s own concerns, standards, and practical solutions. The main limitation was that deeper values, confidence, or personal meaning were not explored as fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was collaborative, with Maria’s ideas strongly shaping the session. A stronger partnership move would have been to more explicitly affirm her as the expert on her own life and experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maria was helped to articulate a clear, client-driven plan using her log and a pharmacist check. The change talk could have been amplified further by reinforcing her confidence in following through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Resistance, distrust, and safety concerns were acknowledged without argument or dismissal. The psychiatrist could have more consistently turned sustain talk into stronger change-oriented language.
