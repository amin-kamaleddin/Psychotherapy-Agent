# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses warm, nonjudgmental language and normalizes Yasmin’s ambivalence, fear, and frustration. Acceptance is strong but occasionally generic and does not fully affirm her worth independently of weight or self-control.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers several accurate reflections of Yasmin’s concerns about scale anxiety, energy, strength, and fear of failure. Empathy is good but not always deep enough to fully explore the underlying shame and identity concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a sustainable, less punitive approach to eating, exercise, and scale checking. However, the direction becomes somewhat prescriptive around calorie gaps and weight-focused monitoring.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Yasmin to choose from options and incorporates some of her preferences into the plan. Nevertheless, the session is often led by expert advice rather than equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Yasmin is given choices and is able to accept or reject some suggestions, such as the closet and delay strategy. Autonomy support is reduced by frequent directive recommendations and detailed prescriptive planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks open-ended questions that bring out Yasmin’s values, such as energy, strength, and avoiding crash-and-burn. Evocation could be stronger by more consistently eliciting and amplifying her own reasons, ability, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative tone and asks for Yasmin’s input throughout. The partnership is uneven because the clinician frequently drives the agenda with specific nutrition and weight-management suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Yasmin expresses meaningful change talk, including wanting to feel strong, preserving the gym, and trying the delay strategy. The psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Yasmin’s scale-focused and weight-loss sustain talk remains central, and the session continues to reinforce weight tracking and calorie tweaks. The psychiatrist attempts to redirect toward non-scale wins but does not strategically soften the power of the sustain talk.
