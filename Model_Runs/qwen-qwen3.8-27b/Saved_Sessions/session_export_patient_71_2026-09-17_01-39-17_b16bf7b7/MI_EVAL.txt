### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys unconditional positive regard by normalizing the client's struggle without judgment. Statements such as “That's a fair tension, not a failure” affirm the client's worth and effort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client's perspective, including complex tensions between values and fatigue. The comment about the plate shows deep understanding of the client's internal struggle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on a small, value-based health behavior. Each question builds momentum toward a concrete action without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses inclusive language and co-creates the plan with the client. The client's ideas directly shape the session's trajectory.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client choice and ownership are repeatedly emphasized through questions like “what would that look like to you?” The client is consistently positioned as the decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out motivation from within the client rather than supplying reasons for change. The client generates key change talk about feeling grounded and stopping the guilt snowball.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions the client as the expert on her own life. The session is guided by the client's ideas rather than the clinician's plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits and amplifies client language in favor of change. The client produces substantial change talk about maintaining the line and rolling out the mat.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with normalization and reframing rather than argument. Each moment is redirected toward the client's values and change goals.
