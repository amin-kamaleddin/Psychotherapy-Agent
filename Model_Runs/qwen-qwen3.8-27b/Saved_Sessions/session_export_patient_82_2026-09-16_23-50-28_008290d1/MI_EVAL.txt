# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms the client’s strengths and self-awareness. Acceptance could be slightly warmer and more explicitly unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, often capturing the client’s ambivalence. Some interpretations feel slightly clinical or mechanistic, reducing experiential validation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on peer-influenced drinking and moves logically through key issues. It would be stronger with more explicit connection to a concrete next step or specific situation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist uses collaborative language and allows the client’s ideas to shape the conversation. The session is still somewhat therapist-led, with frequent questions and summaries.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids directive or pressuring language and emphasizes the client’s choice and expertise. Autonomy support could be further strengthened by more explicit invitations for the client to set priorities.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions effectively encourage the client to explore values, identity, and possible actions. Evocation could be enhanced with confidence or importance scaling and deeper follow-up on change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist conveys a collaborative stance and acknowledges the client as the expert on his own experience. Partnership would improve with more explicit shared agenda-setting and power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist elicits some change talk but does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen the client’s own reasons for change. More strategic follow-up and confidence building would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing, but does not consistently use double-sided reflections or reframing to reduce its weight. More strategic pivots toward integration and change would be beneficial.
