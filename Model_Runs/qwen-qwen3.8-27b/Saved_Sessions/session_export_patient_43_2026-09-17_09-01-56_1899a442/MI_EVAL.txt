# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with supportive language. Could have more fully affirmed Eric’s dignity and autonomy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections captured Eric’s ambivalence about needing a break and wanting less conflict. Empathy was inconsistent due to focus on the “1” and limited acknowledgment of deeper frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Session eventually focused on a concrete target: reducing smoking by two cigarettes per day. Early direction was unclear because of the tangential emphasis on the unexplained “1.”

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions and allowed Eric’s language to shape the plan. The collaborative stance was occasionally undermined by interpretive, analyst-like statements.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized Eric’s freedom to choose and avoided pressure to quit. Some prompts subtly assumed a small trial step, limiting full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Questions drew out motivation to reduce smoking and make the decision feel like his own. Evocation was limited by a lack of deeper exploration of Eric’s values, identity, or long-term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Eric’s concerns largely guided the session. Partnership was slightly limited by moments of therapist-driven interpretation, especially around the “1.”

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited useful change talk around reducing cigarettes by two and making it feel like Eric’s choice. Did not consistently amplify or deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk without confrontation and acknowledged the function of smoking as a reset. Did not strategically redirect or integrate sustain talk with Eric’s broader goals.
