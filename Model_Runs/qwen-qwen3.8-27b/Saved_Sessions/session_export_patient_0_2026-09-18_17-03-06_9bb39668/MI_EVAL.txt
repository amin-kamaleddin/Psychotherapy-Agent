# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations woven throughout. Occasionally leans formulaic in phrasing (e.g., repeated 'I appreciate your honesty'), and lacks explicit unconditional acceptance during the most challenging sustain talk moments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary, nuanced reflective listening that consistently goes beneath surface content to capture the emotional and cognitive layer. Particularly skilled complex reflections, such as naming the protective 'I know my people' story without judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from defensiveness to self-reflection with the target behavior remaining central throughout. Could benefit from one or two consolidating summary statements at key transition points.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Textbook high-level MI collaboration with a genuinely equal power dynamic. The session is structured as a co-exploration where Adrian's ideas substantially shape direction, and the psychiatrist explicitly yields authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with explicit permission to not decide, removal of pressure to quit, and emphasis on choice throughout. The client's right to choose is treated as non-negotiable with no directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** All change talk is self-generated by Adrian, elicited through thoughtful open questions rather than external reasons. Could be more systematic with scaling questions or deliberate amplification of the strongest change talk moments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise resides within Adrian and invites co-construction of meaning. Occasionally takes a slightly leading role in framing the next question rather than fully yielding direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully facilitates a rich range of self-generated change talk through strategic questions and validation. Could incorporate more explicit elicitation of confidence/ability language or scaling questions to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk without reinforcing it, and skillfully reframes it into change-compatible language (e.g., 'lighter, less risky' rather than 'quit'). Could show more strategic pivoting away from sustain talk at key moments.
