# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids reacting negatively to Amina’s resistance. However, the acceptance is somewhat procedural and repetitive, with repeated prompts that can feel mildly intrusive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate but surface-level reflections of Amina’s stated experience. The reflections do not consistently explore the underlying emotions, such as mistrust, fear, or guardedness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on immediate engagement and rapport-building, but it lacks a clear strategic direction toward a specific therapeutic goal. The interaction becomes repetitive without building meaningful momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works collaboratively by asking for permission, offering choices, and following Amina’s lead. Collaboration is strongest at the level of immediate interaction rather than shared therapeutic planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is strong and consistent, with repeated emphasis on Amina’s choice and control. The psychiatrist avoids pressure, coercion, or directive persuasion throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist attempts to evoke client-generated information by asking what Amina wants or feels helpful. The evocation remains superficial and does not effectively elicit intrinsic motivation for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates a non-authoritarian partnership stance by inviting input and respecting Amina’s choices. The partnership is practical but limited mostly to micro-level choices rather than broader co-creation of the session’s direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There is very little active elicitation or expansion of change talk. Questions remain broad and do not directly target Amina’s motivation, desire, or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing Amina’s resistance and offers autonomy. However, there is limited use of advanced MI strategies to soften sustain talk or redirect it toward openness and change.
