# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; every response validates before any other function, including when the client is defensive. The psychiatrist normalizes struggle and positions Irina as a person doing her best under enormous pressure without ever implying moral failing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflective listening is consistently accurate, emotionally attuned, and progressively deepening — capturing the underlying emotional meaning rather than just surface content. Each reflection advances understanding by a new layer, with particularly sophisticated identification of the exact emotional pivot points.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, purposeful arc from exploration to preparation while staying anchored to the alcohol/stress coping question. Each question builds on the previous response with clear momentum, and tangential topics are never allowed to derail the focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions herself as a curious learner rather than a director, with the session structure entirely shaped by Irina's responses. Reflections are offered as interpretations rather than truths, and there is no moment where the psychiatrist overrides or corrects the client's self-assessment.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is explicit and woven into every interaction — no recommendations, no 'you should,' and no imposed standards or timelines. When Irina proposes a modest one-drink goal, the psychiatrist affirms it as wise self-knowledge rather than pushing for more.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist exclusively elicits from within the client, never providing reasons for change, advice, or information. By session end, Irina has generated her entire change plan and coping strategies independently — the gold standard of evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expertise and wisdom are consistently positioned as residing within Irina; the psychiatrist never says 'I think you should' or offers clinical information. All questions are open and client-directed, making the psychiatrist a curious, attuned companion rather than an expert providing direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk is evoked and amplified skillfully across all categories (desire, ability, need, commitment) without being forced or manufactured. The psychiatrist waits for Irina to generate change language and then reflects it back with emphasis, anchoring to her own identity and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled with remarkable elegance — never argued, dismissed, or reinforced, while still creating space for change talk to emerge naturally. The sustain talk is met with such complete acceptance that it loses its defensive force and gives way to the client's own forward-looking language.
