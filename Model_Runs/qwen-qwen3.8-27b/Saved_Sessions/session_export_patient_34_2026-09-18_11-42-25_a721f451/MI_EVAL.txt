# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating throughout, with no labeling or condescension. The warmth follows a somewhat structured pattern (validate → reflect → open question) rather than feeling deeply individualized or emotionally resonant.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking, with notable perceptive observations about language shifts and emotional undercurrents. Reflections occasionally remain at the cognitive level rather than delving into deeper emotional layers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session has a logical arc but the focus is split between the conduct/legal process and the target behavior (alcohol use). A significant portion is occupied with practical conduct questions, diluting focus on the primary MI target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is framed as joint exploration with a conversational, non-hierarchical tone. However, collaboration is somewhat one-directional — the psychiatrist does most of the talking and Dylan primarily responds, with limited co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** One of the stronger dimensions, with consistent affirmation of Dylan's right to choose and ownership of the process. The 'I'm just noticing...' pattern creates a subtle frame where the psychiatrist guides interpretation, slightly tempering the autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-placed open questions that genuinely elicit Dylan's own perspectives without imposing reasons for change. Questions tend to stay at the surface level rather than probing deeper into values, abilities, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Dylan's input and avoids an expert-driven approach. The psychiatrist guides session direction significantly, creating a subtle asymmetry where Dylan responds rather than co-directs.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Identifies and follows up on change talk when it appears but does not systematically explore DARN-CAT elements or amplify forward-looking language. The overall evocation of change talk is limited in frequency and depth, with missed opportunities to explore confidence or specific steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well without arguing or counter-persuading, and the final separation of legal worry from drinking behavior is a smart move. However, sustain talk is mostly reflected back neutrally rather than being actively reframed or gently pivoted with double-sided reflections.
