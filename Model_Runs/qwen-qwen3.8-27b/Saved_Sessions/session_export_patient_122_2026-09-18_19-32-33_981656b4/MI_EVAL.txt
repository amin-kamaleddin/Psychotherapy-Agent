# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone, explicitly removing moral judgment from Sofia's coping behaviors. However, some phrases occasionally read as slightly formulaic (e.g., 'I love that' used in similar constructions), preventing it from reaching the depth of individualized positive regard of a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates highly accurate reflective listening that moves beyond paraphrasing into genuine understanding of Sofia's emotional and cognitive experience, including subtle cues like 'weirdly freeing' and the all-or-nothing pattern.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, coherent arc from vague dissatisfaction to a concrete, self-generated action plan with no topic drift. Each step logically builds on the previous one with appropriate pacing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is structured as a genuine exchange between equals, with Sofia's ideas substantially shaping the session at every level—from target behavior to strategy to commitment. The psychiatrist consistently positions herself as a curious partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All recommendations are framed as questions or explorations, never prescriptive statements. Key insights and commitments are explicitly attributed to Sofia as her own language, reinforcing her ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently elicits motivation from within Sofia rather than providing external reasons for change. The progression from 'I physically can't' to 'I just did the thing, and that's enough' is entirely Sofia's internal evolution drawn out by evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses open-ended questions and curiosity statements rather than positioning herself as the expert who knows what Sofia should do. The session direction is substantially shaped by Sofia's own contributions and insights.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Questions like 'What would feel like a win, not a failure?' directly elicit Sofia's own change language and the psychiatrist amplifies it rather than imposing external reasons. The session moves from vague dissatisfaction to concrete, self-generated change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** When Sofia expresses worry that small steps don't count or raises the 'not enough' voice, the psychiatrist never argues or dismisses but helps Sofia see through the patterns to generate her own counter-narrative. The all-or-nothing thinking is neutralized by reframing the shoes as just shoes, not a life change.
