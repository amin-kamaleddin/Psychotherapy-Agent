# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and accepts Andrei’s practical constraints. It is slightly procedural and could more explicitly validate his frustration and time pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and captures Andrei’s practical needs and data-focused perspective. The empathy remains largely cognitive rather than deeply exploring underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session has a clear, logical focus on blood pressure and a small snack-related change. It would be stronger if the plan were more consistently connected to Andrei’s broader health goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs a practical plan and treats Andrei as an equal partner. More explicit client-led agenda-setting or revision would improve collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported by consistently presenting Andrei as the decision-maker. The psychiatrist avoids imposing a fixed plan, pressure, or hidden obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits Andrei’s own motivations, criteria, and definitions of success. This is a standout strength because the client supplies the reasons and plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Andrei’s contributions clearly shape the session and the psychiatrist respects his expertise in his own life. The therapist still retains a fairly clear agenda, limiting full power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evokes useful change talk, including concrete goals, backup plans, and measurable success criteria. It would be stronger with more explicit amplification of this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled without arguing or moralizing and is reframed as a practical constraint. More skillful two-sided reflections would better connect sustain talk to change.
