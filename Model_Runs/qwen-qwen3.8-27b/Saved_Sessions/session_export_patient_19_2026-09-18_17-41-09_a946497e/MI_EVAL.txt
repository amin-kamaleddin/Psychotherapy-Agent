# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens with explicit, warm statement of unconditional positive regard and maintains a nonjudgmental stance throughout. No moralizing, labeling, or conditional regard at any point in the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, nuanced reflections that capture both surface content and deeper meaning, including the standout 'Tuesday night without the pub is missing a whole world' reflection. Reflections progressively deepen as the session moves forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from minimization through ambivalence to tentative commitment, staying within the target behavior domain. Could have benefited from a clearer agenda statement at the start and a more structured closing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Psychiatrist openly shares his own uncertainty and co-creates the 'experiment' framing from the client's own language. The session feels like a dialogue between equals exploring a problem together rather than an expert-client interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit, repeated affirmations of the client's right to choose, including the right not to change. Uses 'experiment' framing rather than 'commitment' or 'plan,' and explicitly lets her off the hook after one night.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation from within the client through open questions, never providing external reasons or arguing for change. Could have more explicitly drawn out confidence talk and used more summarization of accumulating change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert on her own life with a clearly egalitarian power dynamic. Never assumes the correct answer or directs the session toward a predetermined outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and amplifies change talk at multiple points with strategic questions like 'What does that part of you want you to do?' Could have more explicitly summarized the accumulating change talk and drawn out confidence/ability scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk by reflecting it back and pivoting to curiosity without debating or reinforcing doubt. Could have more explicitly normalized the fear of isolation before pivoting to exploration.
