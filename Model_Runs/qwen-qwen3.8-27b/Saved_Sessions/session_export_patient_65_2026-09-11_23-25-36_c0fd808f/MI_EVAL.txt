# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and provides basic reassurance. Positive regard remains fairly generic and lacks deeper personal validation of stress, fear, or ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offers one early empathic reflection about obligation, but then focuses mainly on logistics. Does not deeply explore or validate Julian’s worry, pressure, or fear of legal consequences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear and consistent focus on completing the mandated service hours and required paperwork. The direction is strong but overly procedural rather than motivational.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offers limited opportunities for client input, but the counselor supplies most of the plan and content. The conversation becomes counselor-led rather than jointly constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some autonomy-supportive choices and options, but also gives frequent directive instructions. Client ownership and freedom in the change process are not strongly emphasized.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does not effectively draw out Julian’s own motivations, values, confidence, or reasons for change. The session remains driven by external requirements and practical tasks.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Begins with a partnership-oriented question, but the psychiatrist mostly takes an expert problem-solving role. Julian does not substantially shape the direction of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Allows Julian to voice small commitments, but does not actively expand them. Missing evocative questions about importance, confidence, benefits, or next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accepts avoidant statements without arguing or escalating resistance. However, it does not use those statements to explore ambivalence or gently redirect toward forward movement.
