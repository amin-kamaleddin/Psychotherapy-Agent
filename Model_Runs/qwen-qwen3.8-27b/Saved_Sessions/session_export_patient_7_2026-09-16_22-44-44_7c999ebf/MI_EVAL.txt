# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained warm, nonjudgmental respect throughout, including when Amina said she wanted to be left alone. Validation such as “you don’t have to earn your space here” demonstrated strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offered useful reflections about home stress and the meaning of quiet and music. Some responses remained somewhat formulaic and did not always fully match Amina’s withdrawn, minimally communicative affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on rapport-building and Amina’s experience of home stress. The direction was clinically coherent, though the middle became somewhat repetitive before fully aligning with her request for silence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited Amina’s input and offered choices such as silence, distance, or pressure-free presence. Collaboration was strong but still somewhat therapist-led, as questioning continued after Amina repeatedly signaled she did not want to talk.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supported Amina’s right to choose by offering low-pressure options and immediately honoring her request for silence. There was no coercion, persuasion, or pressure to elaborate.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist made consistent efforts to elicit Amina’s own experiences, coping, and self-understanding through questions about stepping away, control, and music. Some questions were slightly too cognitive or expansive for her level of engagement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally adopted a collaborative, non-authoritarian stance and offered Amina a menu of options. The session remained noticeably therapist-led, with continued invitations to elaborate even after Amina signaled she wanted to be left alone.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist made reasonable attempts to evoke agency and coping-related change language, especially around music and protecting her peace. However, the session did not consistently build Amina’s minimal responses into clear statements of desire, ability, need, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist responded well to Amina’s resistance by reframing “leave me alone” as a need for space rather than arguing or pressuring her. The response was not a 5 because several follow-up questions occurred before fully and smoothly moving into the requested silence.
