# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates warm, nonjudgmental, and individualized positive regard. Acceptance is maintained even when the client expresses self-doubt or concern about being perceived as difficult.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately captures the client's ambivalence, values, and competing priorities. Deeper emotional exploration of underlying shame, fear, or anxiety could further strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on alcohol use in social situations. The conversation progresses logically from goals to specific behavioral strategies and evaluation markers.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates effectively by using the client's ideas to shape the plan and co-construct small experiments. More explicit shared agenda-setting or joint problem-solving language would make the collaboration even stronger.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure and supports the client's sense of choice and experimentation. Adding more explicit affirmations of the client's freedom and ownership could further strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation is strongly elicited from within the client rather than imposed externally. The client generates his own values, reasons for change, and practical solutions throughout the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the client as the expert on his own life and invites his input in shaping the intervention. The partnership could be further strengthened by more explicitly negotiating the agenda or direction of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully evokes the client's own change talk, including desire, ability, commitment, and self-advocacy. The client is guided to articulate realistic strategies and a compassionate self-evaluation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively softens sustain talk by normalizing the client's concerns and using balanced reflections. Some sustain talk related to social pressure and fear of being judged remains only partially processed.
