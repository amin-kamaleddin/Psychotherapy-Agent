### Partnership

**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for choices and incorporates some of Elias's preferences, but often assumes an expert problem-solving role. The tray suggestion after rejection weakens the sense that Elias is fully leading the plan.

#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elias offers some change-related statements and agrees to a small trial, but the psychiatrist does not strongly amplify his own motivations. More scaling, hope, and value-based evocation would strengthen change talk.
