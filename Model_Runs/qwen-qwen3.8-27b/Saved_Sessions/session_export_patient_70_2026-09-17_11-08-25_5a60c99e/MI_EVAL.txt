# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, with specific, individualized affirmations of Lars's self-awareness and realism. Zero labeling, disapproval, or judgmental language — warmth is consistent and never formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, nuanced reflective listening that captures complexity (e.g., the duality of restlessness vs. caution) without oversimplifying. Every reflection carries emotional and relational weight, and tracks Lars's emotional shifts precisely.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing recidivism through specific behavioral targets (Friday night scenario, limiting alcohol, polytech form). The transition from the Friday night discussion to the polytech form could have been more explicitly bridged with strategic guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing where the psychiatrist builds on Lars's metaphors as shared discoveries and co-creates meaning collaboratively. Lars's contributions directly shape the direction and content of the interview throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Pervasive autonomy support with explicit statements of non-directiveness and complete ownership given to Lars over the pace and scope of change. No directive language; Lars's self-determination is honored even when he resists full commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** All motivation is elicited from within Lars through well-crafted open questions and strategic reflections — never provided externally. The psychiatrist acts as a mirror and guide, with every reason for change articulated in Lars's own words.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent deferral to Lars as the expert on his own experience, with language that credits his own insight and avoids any assumption or lecturing. The session is shaped entirely by Lars's contributions rather than the psychiatrist's agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk at multiple points through strategic questions and amplification of Lars's own emerging confidence. Every reason for change is Lars's own, elicited through well-timed reflections and open questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and explored respectfully (e.g., examining 'looking weak') but not always actively redirected. Could more strategically use double-sided reflections to help Lars resolve the tension between his need for action and his desire to stop offending.
