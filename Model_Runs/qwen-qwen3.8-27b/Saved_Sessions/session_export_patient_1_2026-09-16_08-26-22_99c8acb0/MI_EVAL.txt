# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and avoided judgment, though somewhat repetitive and lacking warmer, individualized affirmation of the patient's ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided accurate complex reflections summarizing the patient's priorities, but occasionally repetitive and did not fully explore deeper emotional textures or stress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Responsive to the patient's resistance but lacked strategic momentum toward the target behavior, repeatedly circling around practical dental tips instead of cessation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good collaborative stance that allowed the patient to guide his action plan, but remained expert-driven when leading with specific medical facts and how-to instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices and affirming the patient's right to decide, though early risk information and repeated reminders were slightly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some early evocative attempts but relied heavily on counselor-provided information rather than deeply drawing out the patient's own motivations and values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and invited the patient's perspective, but still took on a significant expert role by delivering factual risk warnings.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal change talk regarding cigar cessation, focusing mainly on low-effort dental symptom management rather than eliciting desire or reasons for cutting back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with the patient's sustain talk to prevent escalation, but largely accepted his resistance instead of using double-sided reflections to work with ambivalence.
