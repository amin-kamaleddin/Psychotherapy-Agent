# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respectful and non-judgmental tone throughout, but procedural rather than warmly affirming. Limited explicit normalization of Sofia's ambivalence, anxiety, or social pressures.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers some perspective-taking on practical concerns (cost, fitting in) but misses deeper emotional content like fear of exclusion and feeling boring. Reflections are adequate but not consistently emotionally rich or accurate.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on marijuana use, frequency, triggers, and spending with a concrete one-week plan and follow-up. Minor drift into generic coping suggestions but overall direction remains relevant.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sofia's choices shape parts of the plan, but the psychiatrist frequently leads with structured lists and preformed strategies. The interaction feels more like selecting from offered options than genuine co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers multiple choices and responds appropriately to Sofia's concern about feeling managed by providing a less structured option. Slightly reduced by directive moments prescribing specific scripts and roles.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks about importance of change and biggest downside, drawing out limited change talk, but then shifts to psychoeducation and concrete advice. Little exploration of Sofia's values, identity, or deeper motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes collaborative language and Sofia's preferences influence the session focus, but the psychiatrist often positions themself as the source of information and options. The client's expertise is not consistently centered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some effort to elicit change-related language by asking about importance and inviting a small goal, and Sofia offers some change talk. However, the psychiatrist rarely amplifies or deepens this talk and misses opportunities to explore underlying values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk about calming, social fit-in, and risk rather than challenging it, and redirects toward a spending-focused harm-reduction plan. However, limited use of double-sided reflections or active strategies to soften, and the social benefits of use remain relatively unexamined.
