# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating resistance without labeling or frustration. Warmth is sometimes formulaic and does not fully reach deeper emotional exhaustion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate, well-timed reflections that capture ambivalence and fatigue effectively. Reflections mostly stay at the practical level and could explore deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the device/charging behavior with logical progression toward a concrete plan. Could benefit from a mid-session summary to strengthen the through-line.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint and lets client choices clearly shape the final plan. Collaboration is somewhat one-directional because the psychiatrist generates structured options for the client to choose from.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally supports choice and ownership while honoring the client's boundary about not being told he is progressing. Language like 'nudge that toward a 7' introduces slight goal-directed pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some client-driven solutions, especially through the confidence ruler and small-tweak questions. Evocation remains practical/logistical and does not deeply explore values, meaning, or intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly honors client authority and choice, seeking permission and adapting to preferences. Partnership is structured as options-and-selection rather than fully co-created collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Client offers useful change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen it. More evocative follow-up would build motivation rather than moving quickly to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without arguing or pushing, often accommodating it into practical solutions. Misses opportunities for sophisticated ambivalence processing, such as double-sided reflections.
