# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental throughout. Acceptance is warm but sometimes plan-focused and generic relative to Aisha’s body-image shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Aisha’s perspective and ambivalence. However, the session often moves quickly from emotion to problem-solving and misses chances to deepen her shame and self-critical pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, narrow focus on a realistic target behavior. There is little topic drift and strong alignment with Aisha’s request for concrete support.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by co-creating the reminder, reward, coping line, and plan. The work feels joint, though the psychiatrist still supplies many tools and options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports autonomy by emphasizing choice, permission to stop, and self-directed motivation. Some prescriptive details and scripts can subtly reduce Aisha’s sense of leading the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some evocation of past movement, coping language, and initial motivation. The psychiatrist often supplies solutions and wording instead of drawing out Aisha’s deeper values, confidence, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and allows Aisha’s preferences to shape the plan. The stance is collaborative but not fully client-driven because the psychiatrist structures much of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Aisha independently produces clear change talk, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, reflect, or expand it. The session shifts to planning before fully developing her motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist helps reduce pressure by normalizing stopping and framing five minutes as a win. More MI-specific strategies are needed to soften body-image, performance, and self-critical sustain talk.
