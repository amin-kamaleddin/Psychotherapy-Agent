# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental tone with multiple affirmations of Natalia's honesty and struggle. Occasionally reads as slightly scripted rather than deeply individualized, lacking a moment of profound or unexpected acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate captures of emotional duality (e.g., 'the quiet being loud,' protectiveness from two directions). Some reflections lean toward interpretation rather than pure reflection, occasionally feeling summary rather than deeply exploring emotional texture.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from understanding smoking's role to exploring function to identifying concrete next steps. The middle section on 'hollowness' could have drifted from the smoking target without strategic re-anchoring.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed as joint exploration with consistent use of open-ended questions and co-constructed next steps. Slightly one-directional in that the psychiatrist sets the frame and asks questions while Natalia responds within that frame.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support with no coercive or directive language anywhere. Explicitly respects her pace, offers choice, never pushes beyond what she volunteers, and consistently frames decisions as hers.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Never lectures or provides external reasons; instead draws out Natalia's own values through strategic questioning. Could have further deepened change talk moments (e.g., probing 'not as sure as I walked in') or used importance/confidence rulers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with frequent invitations for Natalia's input and positions her as the expert on her own life. Occasionally has a 'guided' quality where the psychiatrist leads direction quite deliberately rather than co-creating the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits multiple meaningful change talk statements including the shift from 'I smoke' to 'I'm a smoker' language and concrete next steps. Could have further amplified specific change talk moments or used rulers to deepen exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without arguing or dismissing, using strategic questions about function and holding discrepancies gently. A 5 would require more explicit double-sided reflections that actively help resolve the ambivalence.
