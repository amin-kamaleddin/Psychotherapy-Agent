# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating stance throughout. Acceptance is effective but can become formulaic through repeated phrases.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is accurate and often captures both content and emotional meaning. Some reflections could be deeper at moments of shame, guilt, or self-judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains clear focus on aligning values with a healthier Friday-evening routine. The direction moves logically from values to a concrete behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is strongly collaborative, building on Laura’s own language and ideas. The final plan and cues are co-constructed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently frames change as Laura’s own choice and invites her perspective without pressure. Ambivalence is respected rather than resolved prematurely.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out client-generated motivation and reinforces Laura’s own change language. Some questions embed the desired meaning rather than allowing Laura to define it more independently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys that expertise and understanding reside with Laura. Laura’s ideas drive the session direction and final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicit Laura’s values, reasons, and intentions for change. Evocation could be more systematic in exploring confidence, ability, readiness, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and gently reframes all-or-nothing sustain talk. The softening could be more explicit at moments of guilt or self-failure.
