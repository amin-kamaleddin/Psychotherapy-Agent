# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates the client’s time pressures and practical constraints. The acceptance is supportive but somewhat functional rather than deeply emotionally affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows good reflective listening and accurately tracks the client’s priorities, constraints, and decision-making style. The empathy is mainly cognitive and logistical rather than deeply emotionally nuanced.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is tightly focused on improving blood pressure through a very small, time-efficient action. Each question moves efficiently toward a concrete, doable plan with little drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves like an equal partner, co-constructing the plan and adapting to the client’s preferences. The client’s choices substantially shape the final action and follow-up plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports autonomy by asking the client to select the focus, action, timing, and follow-up format. There is no coercion, pressure, or imposed plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The use of importance and confidence rulers effectively draws out the client’s own motivation and first step. The evocation remains practical and does not fully explore deeper values or stronger internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist takes a collaborative stance by offering choices, asking which option feels doable, and adjusting when the client pushes back. The partnership is strong but still somewhat clinician-led in presenting structured options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits and strengthens concrete change talk, including the client’s choice to stand during the payroll call. Further development of values, benefits, or past successes would deepen the change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by reframing rather than arguing, such as emphasizing low burden and no added time. The main limitation is that barriers are mostly accommodated rather than more deliberately redirected toward change.
