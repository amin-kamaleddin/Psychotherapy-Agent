# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and avoids shaming Samir. However, the acceptance feels somewhat procedural rather than consistently warm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflections about Samir’s frustration and loss of control. Empathy remains surface-level and does not deeply explore the emotional undercurrents behind his humor and guarded responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays coherent and relevant, moving from Samir’s feelings about medication management to a concrete plan. The focus shifts more toward communication with the doctor than directly exploring medication adherence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats the session as a joint process by offering options and inviting Samir’s input. Collaboration is strong, though the psychiatrist still leads somewhat by generating multiple scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently supported by affirming Samir’s right to choose and respecting his refusal to rehearse. This support is occasionally overly accommodating and not always linked to Samir’s ownership of health decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks evocative questions early in the session, such as what “enough” means and what the meds do. Evocation becomes superficial as the session quickly shifts into problem-solving and script-building.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a clear partnership stance by positioning Samir as the expert and following his lead. The conversation remains somewhat solution-focused, limiting fully client-led flow.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some effort to elicit change talk about the benefits of the medications. The psychiatrist does not consistently expand or deepen this change talk with importance, desire, ability, or need questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing with Samir’s resistance and uses some double-sided reflection. However, the therapist does not consistently soften sustain talk or connect autonomy to health values.
