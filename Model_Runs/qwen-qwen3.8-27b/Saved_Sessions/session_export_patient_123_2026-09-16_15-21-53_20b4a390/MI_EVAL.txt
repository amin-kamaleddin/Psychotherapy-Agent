# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizing stance toward Sofia. The positive regard is supportive but remains somewhat generic and does not deeply affirm her agency or underlying anxiety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses adequate simple reflections to acknowledge school stress, starting difficulty, and phone distraction. Empathy stays surface-level, missing deeper anxiety, fear of missing out, and self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifts from the alcohol-risk screening to homework and phone use. It lacks strategic focus on the referral concern and does not tie the MI target back to the original risk behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works with Sofia in a joint, problem-solving way and reflects her ideas back in a shared manner. Collaboration is strong for the homework/phone plan but does not extend to the main reason for the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses choice-oriented, non-coercive language and allows Sofia to select small steps. It could better affirm her right to choose not to change and validate her statement about waiting until the weekend.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Draws out useful client-generated ideas about timers, notes, and starting homework. Evocation is practical rather than motivational and does not explore values, intrinsic motivation, or alcohol-related reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance and invites Sofia to generate and refine her own plan. It falls short of 5 by not explicitly positioning Sofia as the expert or negotiating the agenda around the referral reason.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicits change ideas only for the narrower homework and phone distraction problem. It does not cultivate meaningful change talk related to alcohol use, risk reduction, values, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflects Sofia’s uncertainty without reinforcing the status quo. The key sustain talk, “I’ll probably just wait until the weekend,” is left unaddressed and not strategically pivoted toward change.
