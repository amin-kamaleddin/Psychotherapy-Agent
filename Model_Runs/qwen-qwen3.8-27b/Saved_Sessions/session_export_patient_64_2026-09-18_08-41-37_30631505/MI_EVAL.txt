# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental with affirming language, but occasionally reads as somewhat scripted rather than deeply individualized. Does not fully reach the level of unconditional positive regard that a 5 would require.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with multiple accurate complex reflections and good affect attunement. Could have gone deeper into the emotional undercurrent of frustration or guilt in Jordan's ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a logical arc from personal minimization to family connection to evidence-seeking. Lacks a concrete closing summary or collaboratively identified next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language, builds on Jordan's specific contributions, and frames questions as genuine curiosity. Could be elevated by more explicit negotiation of session purpose or more visibly incorporating Jordan's priorities into the conversation's structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** One of the strongest aspects of the session, with consistent and explicit support for Jordan's autonomy throughout. Never imposes recommendations or pressures, explicitly normalizes the right to delay, and treats Jordan as the sole authority on his own decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong evocation with open-ended exploratory questions and no external reasons for change offered. Does not fully capitalize on the strongest evocation moments such as 'if I knew for sure it actually helped him' for deeper targeted follow-up.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear collaborative, non-directive stance with explicit agenda disclaimers and questions framed as invitations. Leans slightly more toward guiding the conversation flow rather than true co-creation of direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates space for change talk to emerge but does not explicitly reflect back, amplify, or strategically reinforce these moments when they appear. The evocation is present but remains moderate rather than actively deepening the change talk that surfaces.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk reasonably without reinforcing it, but does not actively soften or pivot it through strategic MI techniques. Uses gentle curiosity prompts rather than true double-sided reflections or active pivot moves toward change talk.
