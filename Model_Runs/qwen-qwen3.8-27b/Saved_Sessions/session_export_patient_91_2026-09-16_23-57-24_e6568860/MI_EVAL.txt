# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently uses respectful, nonjudgmental validation and normalizes Marcus’s concerns. Acceptance is strong, though it could be slightly more individualized in affirming Marcus’s worth beyond diabetes management.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates accurate and nuanced empathy, reflecting both content and underlying feelings such as worry, fear, and ambivalence. This shows strong perspective-taking without overinterpreting the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on blood sugar monitoring and diabetes self-management. The conversation progresses logically toward a concrete, behaviorally specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and Marcus work together as partners in developing the plan. Marcus’s suggestions directly shape the emerging steps, showing clear joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice through open questions, respects Marcus’s preferences, and avoids pressuring him into a more demanding schedule. Autonomy support is strong, though it could more explicitly affirm that the final decision remains Marcus’s.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Marcus’s own motivations, solutions, and plan rather than imposing them. Key ideas such as timing, frequency, and setup details emerge primarily from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treats Marcus as the expert on his own life and invites his judgment throughout the conversation. There is clear, consistent power sharing in shaping the session and the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist encourages and reinforces Marcus’s emerging change talk and forward-looking statements. It could be deepened further by more explicitly evoking confidence, ability, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles resistance and fear skillfully by acknowledging concerns and redirecting them toward manageable steps. Strong sustain talk is mostly softened, though some deeper fears are only partially neutralized.
