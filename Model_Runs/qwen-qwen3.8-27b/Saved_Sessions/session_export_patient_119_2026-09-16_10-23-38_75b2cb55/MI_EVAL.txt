# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently normalizes missed doses and validates Selene’s experience without blame. The stance is warm but somewhat plan-oriented, with occasional mild evaluative comments that keep it just below a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate reflective listening to Selene’s tiredness, ambivalence, and concern about added burden. Empathy could be stronger by exploring deeper feelings such as fear, shame, or identity-related meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on morning medication consistency and moves efficiently from barrier identification to a concrete plan. The follow-up summary is clear, actionable, and aligned with the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is co-constructed and shaped heavily by Selene’s preferences and ideas. However, the psychiatrist still leads much of the problem-solving by offering menus of options, which slightly reduces the fully collaborative feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses permission-based language, respects Selene’s limits, and supports self-determination throughout. Some repeated commitment questions and therapist-generated options make the approach mildly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits Selene’s own motivation, confidence, and practical solutions through open questions and scaling. Evocation remains more task-focused than values- or identity-focused, which limits deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Selene is treated as an active contributor and her ideas substantially guide the session. The psychiatrist occasionally positions themselves as the problem-solver by presenting options before fully waiting for client-generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes and reinforces Selene’s desire, ability, and practical commitment to change. It could be stronger by more powerfully reflecting Selene’s own change talk and connecting it to deeper values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled skillfully through normalization and validation rather than confrontation. It could be improved by using more double-sided reflections and explicitly redirecting toward client-generated change reasons.
