# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respect, nonjudgment, and validation while acknowledging Marco’s difficulties without blame. The tone is supportive but remains more task-focused than deeply affirming of his overall worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections that identify Marco’s main concerns, including energy, household temptations, self-doubt, and not wanting to burden his wife. The reflections are solid but often remain cognitive and plan-focused rather than deeply emotional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and moves logically from goal clarification to concrete planning and follow-up. There is clear, consistent direction with no meaningful drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Marco rather than imposing a plan, and his choices clearly shape the session. However, the psychiatrist still frequently generates options and structure, so collaboration is strong but not fully equal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently invites Marco’s input, respects his preferences, and avoids pressuring him to choose specific supports. Confidence scaling and language like “nudge you from 7.5 to an 8” slightly undermine autonomy at times.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Marco’s motivations, ideas, and commitment through targeted questions. The style is somewhat structured and suggestion-heavy, with fewer open-ended prompts before offering options.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Marco’s input and follows his lead, allowing his ideas to shape the plan. The frequent provision of specific options and structures makes the process feel somewhat counselor-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively strengthens confidence and commitment through scaling, progress statements, and eliciting clear change language from Marco. The change talk is solid but mostly behavioral rather than grounded in deeper values or identity.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges barriers and concerns without challenging or amplifying them, then redirects toward problem-solving. The handling is effective, but it could be more strategic with fewer double-sided reflections or explicit pivots.
