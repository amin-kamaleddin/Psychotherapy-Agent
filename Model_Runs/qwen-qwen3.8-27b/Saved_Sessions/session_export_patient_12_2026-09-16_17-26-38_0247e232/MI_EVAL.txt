# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and clearly validates Amira's fear of being seen as a problem. It could be strengthened by adding more individualized positive regard and explicitly naming her strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the emotional content and ambivalence, but there is a missed opportunity to directly address Amira's despair and explicit question about hope.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on safety and the target behavior, but the direction feels repetitive and does not progress toward a concrete goal or next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently frames the work as a joint exploration and positions Amira as the expert on her experience. In practice, the psychiatrist does most of the talking and could more fully follow Amira's lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids pressure and clearly supports Amira's choice, including the option to stay where she is for now. It would be stronger with more explicit affirmation of her agency and capacity for change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, especially the statement that she wants to stop, but it is not consistently developed. There is limited use of scaling, values, past strengths, or deeper exploration of her reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and avoids an expert-driven stance, creating a collaborative tone. A stronger rating would require clearer agenda negotiation and more visible power-sharing or co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some desire to stop but does not consistently amplify, deepen, or build on Amira's change talk. Commitment, confidence, and values-based exploration are underdeveloped.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without being challenged, but helplessness and isolation remain dominant. More active softening would involve identifying exceptions, costs, or small sources of support.
