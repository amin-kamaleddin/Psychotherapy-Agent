# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and low-pressure stance, repeatedly normalizing fatigue and fear of failure. Acceptance is strong but somewhat formulaic and behavior-focused, with limited deeper person-centered validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers accurate, safe reflections of fatigue, worry, and difficulty, but often stays at a surface level. The deeper ambivalence around lost identity, past exercise, fear of futility, and self-doubt is not consistently explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains clearly focused on the target behavior of increasing physical activity and establishing a small step. The direction is effective but narrow and repetitive, with limited connection to broader values or long-term motivations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts the plan based on Dante's constraints, but remains largely the source of structure and options. Collaboration is present but uneven and somewhat expert-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports choice by offering options, avoiding coercion, and framing the plan as a low-stakes experiment. Autonomy support is slightly undermined by directive language and repeated checklists or reminders.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes limited attempts to elicit internal motivation and mostly provides advice, strategies, and planning. Missed opportunities include exploring Dante's past exercise identity, values, confidence, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some collaboration by asking questions and adapting to Dante's stated limitations. However, the counselor frequently leads the process and does not consistently position Dante as the expert on his own life and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Dante is led toward small action statements such as putting shoes by the door and checking in tomorrow. The psychiatrist does not systematically evoke broader change talk related to values, desire, importance, confidence, or ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist tolerates and normalizes sustain talk without arguing or increasing pressure. However, the sustain talk is not consistently explored or reframed through double-sided reflections that could strengthen ambivalence resolution.
