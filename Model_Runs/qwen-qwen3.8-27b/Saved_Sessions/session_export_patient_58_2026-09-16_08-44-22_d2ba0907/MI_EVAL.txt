# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms the client’s ownership of the plan. The acceptance is strong but remains somewhat task-focused rather than deeply personal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Jamal’s frustration and desire for quiet mornings. However, the empathy stays mostly cognitive and misses deeper emotional themes such as feeling unseen or the effort feeling futile.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session keeps a clear focus on tardiness and builds a coherent, measurable plan. It is strong overall, though it becomes somewhat procedural and repetitive in the practical steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist collaborates well by inviting input and incorporating the client’s choices into the plan. It falls short of a 5 because the counselor still offers many concrete options and guides the direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently supports choice by offering options and respecting the client’s preference for independent follow-through. Some directive phrasing, such as “quick steps,” slightly reduces the fully autonomous tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions and scaling to draw out the client’s own motivation. However, he moves too quickly from intrinsic motivation statements into advice and problem-solving.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client actively chooses the alarm time, goal, reward, and follow-up method, showing a collaborative stance. The psychiatrist still drives parts of the process, so the partnership is strong but not fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits meaningful change talk, especially around the goal being “for me.” He does not consistently amplify or develop this language before shifting to practical planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with the client’s sustain talk and redirects toward controllable actions. At times, however, success remains tied to the mother’s behavior rather than being fully reframed toward the client’s autonomy.
