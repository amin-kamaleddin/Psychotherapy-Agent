# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental responses with several normalizations of the client’s concerns. At times feels procedural and moves quickly into planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers accurate reflections of ambivalence and practical worries, such as stress relief, social ritual, and fear of irritability. Reflections remain somewhat cognitive/behavioral with limited deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on smoking reduction with a clear progression from ambivalence to concrete steps. Drifts into operational details, making it feel less like strategic MI direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites client choice among several options, and those choices shape the final plan. Still functions largely as the expert providing tips and structure rather than two equal partners exploring.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asks about preferences, avoids pressuring quit commitments, and emphasizes that slipping is information. Some suggestions are quite prescriptive, reducing the sense of full client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses evoking questions about function, importance, confidence, and doable options, prompting some client change talk. Frequent advice and structured recommendations interrupt deeper elicitation of values and motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shares decision-making by asking which options sound doable and what the client would like. Often presents ready-made solutions first, weakening the sense that expertise resides primarily in the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits useful change talk, such as caring about teeth, willingness to try gum, and a social script. Could be stronger by reflecting/amplifying the client’s statements and leaving less room for counselor-provided planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk respectfully by normalizing stress and ritual without arguing or dismissing. Would benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections to more actively soften the reasons for smoking.
