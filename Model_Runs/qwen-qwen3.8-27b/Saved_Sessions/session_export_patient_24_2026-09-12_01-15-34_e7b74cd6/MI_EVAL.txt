# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a generally nonjudgmental tone and acknowledges Daniel’s defensiveness. However, repeated emphasis on health risks can make the stance feel more advisory than fully accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are adequate reflections of Daniel’s functional self-view and discomfort with being labeled. The empathy is often shallow and quickly shifts into medical education or planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session stays within the general topic of alcohol and health but frequently drifts toward labs, diagnostics, and logistics. It is less focused on clarifying Daniel’s personal goals and relationship to drinking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and some shared planning language. However, the clinician still supplies most of the structure and options, limiting genuine co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** This is one of the stronger areas, with repeated respect for Daniel’s choice and no pressure to change drinking. The main limitation is that autonomy is mostly supported by permitting the status quo rather than helping him explore both sides of his ambivalence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist leans heavily on external medical evidence and logistics rather than eliciting Daniel’s own motivations or values. There is limited exploration of discrepancy between his marathon identity and drinking pattern.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are occasional collaborative questions, but the overall stance is expert-driven with predefined plans and options. Daniel contributes, yet the psychiatrist mostly leads the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniel offers several change-relevant statements, but the psychiatrist does not consistently reflect, expand, or explore them. Evocative questions about his values, training, or desired self-image are largely absent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids confrontation but repeatedly validates maintaining the current routine. This reinforces sustain talk without adequately balancing it with curiosity about reasons for change.
