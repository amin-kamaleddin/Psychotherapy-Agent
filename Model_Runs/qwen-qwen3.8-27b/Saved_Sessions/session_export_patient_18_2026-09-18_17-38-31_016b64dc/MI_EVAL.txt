# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and supportive, with explicit normalization of Anya's experience and affirmation of her honesty. However, the warmth occasionally veers toward performative over-affirmation rather than subtle, grounded positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening with accurate dual-sided reflections capturing the tension between physical experience and self-perception. Reflections are accurate but don't consistently probe the deeper emotional meaning beneath what was said.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear logical progression from normalizing the referral through exploring perspective, physical experience, values, and social barriers to a natural close. Stays focused on alcohol use with appropriate pacing for a lower-risk teen.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as joint exploration with invitational language and reflections offered as interpretations rather than declarations. Could be deeper by more explicitly co-creating the session's focus with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent and consistent throughout, with explicit statements removing obligation and framing Anya as the decision-maker. A textbook example of autonomy support for a lower-risk adolescent in precontemplation/contemplation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from within Anya through the ideal weekend question and internal experience probes, never providing external reasons for change. Could be slightly more consistent in evoking the client's own capacity and past experiences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-hierarchical stance and explicitly attributes the shift to Anya ('that was yours, not mine'). The conversation still follows a somewhat linear, counselor-guided path rather than fully power-sharing direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully cultivates change talk through well-timed open questions and effectively amplifies Anya's insight about the fear being 'the story in your head.' Could more explicitly reinforce and expand on change talk moments as they arise.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk effectively by acknowledging it without arguing or dismissing, and gently redirects toward Anya's own specific experience. Never lets sustain talk dominate or engages in debate about whether drinking is normal.
