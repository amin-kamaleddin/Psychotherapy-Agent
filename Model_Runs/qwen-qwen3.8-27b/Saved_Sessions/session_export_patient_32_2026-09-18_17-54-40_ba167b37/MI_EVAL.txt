# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent warm, nonjudgmental stance with specific, genuine affirmations that never label or express disapproval. Slightly short of exceptional in moments where a deeper acknowledgment of grief or loss in Derrick's sobriety collapse could have been included.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, deeply attuned reflections that capture the emotional core and underlying meaning of Derrick's statements, going far beyond surface content. Empathy is consistent, deepens progressively, and tracks his shifting emotional landscape from defensiveness to vulnerability to tentative hope.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with a well-structured arc from pattern identification through emotional stakes, past success, new options, and concrete first steps. Strategic redirections keep the conversation on track without ever losing the thread.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative dialogue using 'we' language, inviting Derrick's perspective before offering reflections, and building on his contributions as the foundation of the session. Power-sharing is consistent and authentic, with the session clearly shaped by Derrick's responses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Derrick's ownership of the change process, explicitly honoring his boundaries (12-step) and realistic self-assessment without pressure. All suggestions are framed as Derrick's own ideas being reflected back rather than external recommendations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional use of questions and reflections to draw out Derrick's own motivations, values, and insights without ever providing information, advice, or arguing for change. Every motivational element comes from Derrick's own mouth, elicited through well-timed and progressively building questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Derrick as the expert on his own life, using curiosity-based language and returning interpretive authority to him throughout. The psychiatrist functions as a skilled guide rather than an authority figure, with an equitable power dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptionally skilled at eliciting, reinforcing, and amplifying change talk through reflections that highlight Derrick's own capabilities, past success, and self-efficacy. Change talk is consistently built on and consolidated, with the final question evoking both positive and negative consequences through Derrick's own words.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk through acknowledgment, gentle discrepancy, and validation without arguing or getting drawn into debate. A couple of points where sustain talk (e.g., 'that's just who I am') could have been more explicitly pivoted to soften the sense of fixed identity.
