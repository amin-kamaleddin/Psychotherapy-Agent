# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and validates Amira’s distress without moralizing. Warmth could be more individualized and less task-focused.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are several accurate reflections of shame, helplessness, and exhaustion, but they remain somewhat surface-level and repetitive. Deeper exploration of ambivalence, isolation, and meaning would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on reducing drug use and managing urges, including a concrete one-day goal. The safety check is also relevant to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amira contributes to selecting a song, washing her face, and using ice as a backup, showing some collaboration. However, the interaction shifts toward therapist-led task planning too quickly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks permission and offers choices, supporting autonomy in places. Later prescriptive instructions reduce Amira’s sense of ownership and need more emphasis on no obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions elicit small goals and coping ideas, such as not reaching for a bag for a day. More effort is needed to draw out Amira’s internal motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Amira’s input and offers options, showing some partnership. The session becomes increasingly therapist-led, limiting Amira’s role as the expert in her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes and affirms some change talk, such as wanting to get through the night without regret. There is limited work to deepen or expand this through values, importance/confidence ratings, or strategic reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is not directly reinforced or argued against, but it is not consistently softened or redirected. More double-sided reflections and gentle pivots toward change would be expected.
