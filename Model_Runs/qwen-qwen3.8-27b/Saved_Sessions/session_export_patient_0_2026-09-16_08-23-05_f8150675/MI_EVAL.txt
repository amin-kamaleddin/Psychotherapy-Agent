# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Adrian's goal of a small harm-reduction step. Could go deeper in communicating unconditional positive regard when Adrian expresses shame or fear.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers accurate content-focused reflections of Adrian's practical concerns and ambivalence. Misses some deeper feelings, such as shame, fear of judgment, and need to preserve his image among peers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear and consistent focus on reducing infection risk through safer snorting practices. Could more broadly connect the target behavior to Adrian's values, health goals, or life priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Builds a practical plan using Adrian's own ideas, such as the keychain case, wallet, and spare in the car. Still somewhat advisor-driven because the psychiatrist frequently initiates with lists of solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects Adrian's boundaries, avoids pressure to quit, and accepts his refusal of phone reminders and doctor disclosure. Could more explicitly emphasize that Adrian is the final decision-maker on what is realistic.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses some evocative questions and confidence ratings to elicit client input and planning. Relies heavily on education and clinician-supplied options, limiting deeper exploration of Adrian's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Adrian's input and incorporates his practical preferences into the final plan. Often remains more expert-driven by offering multiple solutions before fully exploring the client's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk around harm reduction and a concrete action plan. Does not consistently amplify, deepen, or expand Adrian's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accepts Adrian's continued use without arguing or pushing for abstinence. Uses few double-sided reflections or MI-consistent strategies to transform sustain talk into change talk.
