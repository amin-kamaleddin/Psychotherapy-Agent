# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a warm, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes the client's experience. Acceptance could be stronger by more explicitly affirming the client's worth and strengths beyond her difficulties.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening and perspective-taking are strong, especially in capturing worry as a safety behavior. Deeper empathy could better reflect shame, fear of failing, and feeling disconnected from self and friends.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session drifts from the alcohol screening referral toward generalized worry and fog without clearly connecting them. Stronger MI direction would maintain focus on the target behavior while still honoring the client's agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client frequently shapes the session, and the psychiatrist responds flexibly to her preferences. The clinician still leads the overall structure more than a fully equal-partner MI approach would.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently offers choices, asks permission, and respects the client's preference for a smaller step. Autonomy support could be stronger by more explicitly affirming the client's right to stop or change the plan at any time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively draw out the client's own values, meanings, and reasons for change. Evocation could be strengthened with scaling, importance-confidence questions, or values-based follow-ups.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and invites the client to choose the direction. Partnership could be enhanced by reducing the structured, clinician-led framework.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is evoked, especially around being more present with friends and feeling less drained. The psychiatrist could more consistently amplify, reinforce, and expand the client's change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is handled respectfully without arguing or escalating resistance. More strategic double-sided reflections or pivots could better transform sustain talk into change talk.
