# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and validates Aisha’s workload and request to avoid a lecture. Support is strong but slightly formulaic, limiting it from a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections capture practical burden and frustration but do not consistently attend to deeper emotion. Aisha’s fear of cancer and protective role as a mother could have been reflected more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session remains focused on reducing evening smoking and stays within Aisha’s stated boundary on alcohol. The opening is a bit broad before the target behavior is fully clarified.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Aisha to choose options and incorporates her practical ideas into the plan. Collaboration is effective, though the psychiatrist still leads with structured advice early on.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Aisha’s choices and boundary of “one, not six” are respected without coercion. Explicit affirmation of her ownership over the plan would strengthen this further.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited through readiness and doability questions. Deeper values, confidence, and personal meaning behind her motivation are not consistently explored.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shares power by asking Aisha to select feasible strategies and accepting her self-generated rule. It would be stronger with an explicit statement that Aisha is the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Aisha expresses desire, reasons, and intention for reducing from six to three cigarettes. The psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or elaborate this change talk before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles sustain talk well by accepting Aisha’s boundary around alcohol and normalizing slips. More double-sided reflections would improve the response.
