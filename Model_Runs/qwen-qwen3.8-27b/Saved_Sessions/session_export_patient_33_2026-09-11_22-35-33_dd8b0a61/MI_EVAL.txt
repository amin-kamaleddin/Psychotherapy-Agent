# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental about harm reduction. However, the acceptance feels somewhat procedural and could better normalize Diego’s ambivalence and difficult circumstances.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is surface-level acknowledgment of fear and safety concerns. The psychiatrist misses deeper reflections of Diego’s ambivalence, shame, and desire for control.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a practical harm-reduction goal: obtaining fentanyl test strips. Some drift occurs into broader safety checklists and extra support options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist follows Diego’s lead and offers choices about next steps. However, the clinician still supplies much of the plan and language rather than fully co-creating it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Diego’s boundary against treatment and his low-key plan. Yet the session is quite directive with repeated scripts, checklists, and suggested wording.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some readiness statements are elicited and affirmed. The psychiatrist does not consistently draw out Diego’s deeper motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Diego influences the direction of the session, especially the shift to finding strips. The psychiatrist often slips into a manager-like role by delivering repeated plans and options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reinforces Diego’s readiness for safety steps and strips. There is limited use of questions to expand Diego’s own reasons, values, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids challenging Diego’s continued use and accepts harm reduction as the goal. It would be stronger with more nuanced reflections of both sides of his ambivalence.
