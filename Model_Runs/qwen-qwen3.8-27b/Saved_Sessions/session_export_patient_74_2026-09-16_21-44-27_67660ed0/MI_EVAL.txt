# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor consistently validated the client’s choices and normalized ambivalence without judgment, pressure, or lecturing. Unconditional positive regard was strong throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor reflected the client’s tension, stress, and emotional burden well. Some reflections remained somewhat surface-level, especially around guilt and health-risk conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor kept a reasonable focus on smoking and its family impact, but often shifted toward communication coaching. More frequent summaries and tie-backs to readiness or change direction would strengthen the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor co-created the conversation by repeatedly inviting client input and building on the client’s own language. The client’s agenda clearly shaped the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Counselor strongly honored the client’s statement that quitting was not on the table. The client’s freedom of choice and pace were consistently respected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor used open questions and reflections to draw out client language, but mainly focused on self-explanation and family communication. Internal motivation, discrepancy, and readiness for change were not consistently evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Counselor invited client perspective and allowed the client’s concerns to guide the conversation. Some leading questions prevented the partnership from being fully maximal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Counselor made only limited and indirect attempts to elicit or amplify the client’s own reasons, values, or confidence for change. The session centered more on explaining smoking to the daughter than on developing change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counselor handled sustain talk respectfully by validating the client’s reasons for smoking and avoiding argument. More consistent double-sided reflections or discrepancy questions would help soften sustain talk further.
