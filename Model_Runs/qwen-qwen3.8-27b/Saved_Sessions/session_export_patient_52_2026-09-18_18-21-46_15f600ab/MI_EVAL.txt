# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout, consistently validating Irina's statements without minimizing, labeling, or disapproving. Even during moments of resistance, the psychiatrist responds with understanding rather than pushing back.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections go well beyond simple paraphrasing, accurately identifying Irina's core metaphors (landing, fog) and the deeper emotional significance behind her insights. Demonstrates precise, individualized understanding of this specific client's experience throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use as a stress-coping behavior while allowing the conversation to breathe naturally and follow Irina's own language. The progression is logical and purposeful, ending with a specific, actionable, client-owned plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a model of collaborative interaction between equals, with Irina generating all substantive ideas while the psychiatrist contributes through questions, reflections, and strategic timing. The final plan is 100% client-generated, demonstrating genuine co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Irina's ownership and freedom of choice, never using directive language or pressuring commitment. The plan is entirely Irina's—she chose the target, conditions, activity, and evaluation criteria.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never imports motivation from outside; instead uses open questions and empathic reflections to draw out Irina's own reasons for change. Nearly all motivational content—including the guilt insight, crossword solution, and one-night commitment—emerges from Irina herself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom reside within Irina, using language like 'You know your own evenings better than I do' and 'That's your idea.' The power dynamic is clearly equal, with the psychiatrist acting as a thoughtful companion rather than a guide.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully and consistently evokes change talk from within the client, then amplifies each instance when it appears without ever injecting change talk from outside. The session ends with Irina articulating her own commitment and conditions for success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by validating Irina's concerns and reframing change as smaller than feared, though the peak moment of resistance ('everything I enjoy is being taken away') could have been met with a more explicit double-sided reflection acknowledging both the real loss and the gain.
