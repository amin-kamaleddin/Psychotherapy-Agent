# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, nonjudgmental language and validates Sofia's feelings without moralizing. It is not a 5 because the responses sometimes feel procedural rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There are good reflections of Sofia's worry, unfairness, and concern about being seen as risky. Empathic depth is limited later by the checklist-like safety questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session opens with a broad change question but then shifts mostly to screening logistics and safety checks. It lacks a clear, strategic focus on a concrete target behavior or change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently offers options and lets Sofia choose how to proceed. Collaboration is somewhat reduced when the session becomes more structured and clinician-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sofia is repeatedly invited to take the lead, choose topics, and decide the pace, with clear confidentiality boundaries. It does not reach 5 because autonomy is not consistently connected to her ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Open questions are used initially, but the conversation mostly gathers factual safety and screening information rather than eliciting Sofia's own motivations or values. Client-generated reasons for change are not substantially developed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is a collaborative, low-pressure stance and Sofia is given influence over the conversation. The later rapid safety questions make it feel more clinician-led than truly co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia offers some relevant statements about protecting her reputation and wanting to be seen as a good student, but these are not amplified or explored. The psychiatrist does not systematically evoke or strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sofia's sustain talk is accepted without argument, but it is not strategically softened. Little use of double-sided reflection or redirection reduces its dominance.
