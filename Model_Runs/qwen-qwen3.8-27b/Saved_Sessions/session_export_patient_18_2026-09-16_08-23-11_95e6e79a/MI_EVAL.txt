# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist communicates respect, warmth, and nonjudgment while affirming Anya’s competence and plan. Acceptance is strong but occasionally sounds approving or formulaic rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections are accurate and track Anya’s stated priorities, importance, and confidence. Empathy remains somewhat surface-level and does not fully explore the emotional undertones of autonomy, pressure, or fear of being monitored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol-related choices, confidence, and practical support. Direction is clear and purposeful but leans more toward practical planning than deeper motivational exploration.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist invites input, offers choices, and follows Anya’s preference for a simple, low-pressure plan. Collaboration is slightly limited by the use of a structured list of options before fully eliciting what Anya already finds useful.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The therapist strongly supports Anya’s freedom, ownership, and right to decide what is useful. Language consistently avoids pressure, respects her boundary against proactive check-ins, and reinforces her self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist elicits some client-generated motivation early by asking about values, importance, and confidence. Evocation fades as the session shifts into advice-giving and practical planning, missing opportunities to deepen Anya’s own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist behaves as a partner by asking for input, following Anya’s lead, and allowing her preferences to shape the plan. A more fully collaborative partnership would have elicited more before offering practical options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist evokes some change talk, including Anya’s desire to stay sharp, avoid embarrassment, and maintain balance. The change talk is not consistently developed or amplified, as the session moves quickly into practical suggestions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The therapist responds to sustain talk without arguing, lecturing, or amplifying risk. There is a minor push toward additional intervention by continuing to offer options after Anya prefers to keep things as they are.
