# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and non-judgmental, explicitly countering Liora's self-criticism (e.g., 'I don't think that makes you a bad person'). The warmth occasionally feels slightly procedural rather than deeply individualized, and affirmations could be more frequent and varied.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy throughout, consistently capturing both the cognitive and emotional dimensions of Liora's experience. Particularly skillful in reflecting back her own metaphorical language (e.g., pain moving 'to the outside'), deepening her self-understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, logical focus on the target behavior with a coherent arc from understanding the function of cutting to exploring alternatives. The exploratory rather than goal-directed nature is appropriate for a precontemplation client, though the session doesn't yet reach a specific next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats the conversation as a joint exploration, building on Liora's language and avoiding imposed interpretations. Could be stronger with more frequent check-ins about whether the direction of exploration feels right to Liora, and allowing her ideas to more substantially shape the session's trajectory.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy with explicit language ('This space is truly yours') and a non-pressuring tone throughout. Could be slightly stronger by more explicitly affirming Liora's right to make her own decisions about her body and coping strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly evokes Liora's own insights (function of cutting, need for physical sensation, idea of cold water) rather than providing answers. The evocation of understanding is excellent; the evocation of motivation for change (e.g., DARN-CAT questions) is present but could be more systematic.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative, non-expert stance with curious, exploratory language and avoids directive positioning. Questions, while collaborative in tone, are somewhat leading and Liora's contributions don't yet substantially reshape the session's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates conditions for change talk to emerge, but doesn't consistently seize and amplify Liora's change-relevant statements (her tiredness, mention of cold water, hope the session 'actually helps'). Could benefit from more systematically evoking DARN-CAT change talk to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully without arguing or correcting, using validation and double-sided approaches. Could be more strategic in gently exploring the cost side of sustain talk (e.g., what it costs to keep doing something that only works briefly).
