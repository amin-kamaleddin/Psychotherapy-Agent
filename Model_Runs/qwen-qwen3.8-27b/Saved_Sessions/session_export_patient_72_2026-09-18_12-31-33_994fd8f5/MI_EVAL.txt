# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout. Validations are specific and individualized, responding directly to what Leila says in each moment without ever being formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate empathic reflections that move beyond surface paraphrasing into the emotional and meaning-making core. Masterful examples include connecting 'feeling strong' to agency and capturing the relief in 'enough for a Tuesday.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, focused trajectory toward the target behavior with smooth pacing from exploration to planning to commitment to handling obstacles. No drift into unrelated topics at any point.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration between equals with joint language and shared framing. The psychiatrist consistently follows Leila's lead—when she raises the walk, the Y, or the internal voice, the conversation follows there.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every suggestion is framed as Leila's own idea or choice; never uses prescriptive language like 'you should' or 'I recommend.' The 10/10 confidence is treated as Leila's own assessment, not something the therapist assigned.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent and effective evocation of Leila's own motivations and problem-solving. Could occasionally probe one level deeper, such as asking 'What does handling your days look like when you're strong?' to elicit more ability talk or future self imagery.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Leila as the expert on her own life, crediting her with insights she articulated herself. Never assumes the expert role or provides information unprompted.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Active and skillful cultivation through reflections and amplifying questions that elevate Leila's language from intention to commitment. Occasionally stays at affirming what Leila has already said rather than drawing out new change talk or deeper ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Minimal sustain talk present (weather, internal critical voice) and each instance handled skillfully without amplifying barriers. The all-or-nothing voice is reframed as something Leila can hear without obeying, and she never lingers in resistance.
