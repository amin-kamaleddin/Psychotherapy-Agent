# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated respect, warmth, and nonjudgment. There was no moralizing, labeling, or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and good reflective listening. However, the emotional meaning of some key concerns could have been reflected more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on medication adherence and lifestyle changes. The conversation moved strategically from ambivalence to a practical plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist used the client's own language and co-constructed the treatment plan. The counselor's strong agenda occasionally reduced the sense of perfect balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist returned decision-making to the client and supported his own definition of success. A direct suggestion to consult the pharmacist slightly reduced the explicit emphasis on autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicited the client's internal values, goals, and confidence. Some counselor summaries and the lack of scaling at key moments limited the evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist treated the client as the expert on his own life and invited his perspective. Some counselor-imposed structure and direct recommendations prevented a higher score.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions effectively elicited client change talk and commitment. The psychiatrist did not consistently amplify or strategically reinforce this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled well through normalization and non-confrontation. The counselor could have used double-sided reflections more consistently to pivot away from concerns.
