# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent nonjudgmental stance with powerful reframing moments (e.g., 'That's not stupid. That's a person protecting themselves'). Language occasionally feels polished and rehearsed rather than raw and spontaneous.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with complex, accurate reflections that capture Luis's emotional mechanisms and metaphors. Could have gone deeper into the pain of feeling like a burden to family or being seen as a failure by his boss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on asthma management with logical progression from rapport to barrier exploration to solution generation. Lacks a clear consolidation or action plan at the end, leaving the session somewhat open-ended.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative dynamic where the conversation feels like two people working through a problem together. Consistently treats Luis's ideas as valuable contributions and co-constructs solutions with him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful demonstration — explicitly offers choices, never uses directive language, and consistently frames suggestions as questions or invitations. Avoids the common MI trap of subtly pressuring toward a 'correct' answer.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-crafted questions that elicit client-generated change talk, with the powerful spontaneous 'I do want to get better' moment clearly facilitated by the safe space created. Could have been more systematic with scaling questions and deeper exploration of emerging change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional non-hierarchical dynamic established immediately and maintained throughout, with explicit ceding of expertise to the client and acknowledgment of the system's failures. One of the strongest areas of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes change talk through strategic questions and reflects/amplifies Luis's own statements (e.g., 'I do want to get better'). Could have more systematically explored confidence levels and developed the change talk into broader values and goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging without arguing and gently redirecting to more manageable questions. Could be slightly more deliberate in explicitly acknowledging legitimate parts of sustain talk before pivoting.
