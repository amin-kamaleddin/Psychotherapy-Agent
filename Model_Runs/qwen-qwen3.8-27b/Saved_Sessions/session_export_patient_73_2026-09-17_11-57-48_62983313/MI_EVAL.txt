# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, affirming agency and explicitly counter self-deprecation. Could be elevated with more explicit normalization of ambivalence and unconditional regard independent of progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional empathic accuracy, capturing emotional and cognitive layers rather than merely paraphrasing. Reflections track real-time shifts in the client's narrative with deep understanding of her identity concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on tobacco cessation with a logical progression from engagement through exploration to tentative planning. Lacks a deliberate closing or next-step consolidation to lock in momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is structured as a genuine collaboration with inclusive, exploratory language and a balanced power dynamic. The client's contributions clearly shape direction while the psychiatrist maintains gentle strategic focus without imposing interpretation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership and right to choose, explicitly removing pressure at multiple points. Exemplary practice—never uses directive language and frames all suggestions as possibilities.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expert-level evocation that consistently elicits motivation from within the client rather than providing external reasons. Key questions like 'what does that make you, underneath all of it?' bypass external arguments to reach core motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that expertise and wisdom about change reside within the client, using facilitative language throughout. Never jumps in with solutions; offers options while leaving decisions entirely with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and deepens change talk through strategic questioning, prompting powerful autonomous statements. Occasionally stops just short of fully amplifying and reflecting back the change talk that emerges.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by acknowledging it without arguing, gently exploring its function and helping the client see its costs. Missed opportunity to more actively soften the 'six months' timeline with a double-sided reflection.
