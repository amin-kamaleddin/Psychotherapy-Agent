# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistent non-judgmental tone with strong validation examples throughout. Slight opportunity for more individualized warmth in every interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good accurate reflections of the client's external motivation and perspective. Could have explored deeper emotional undercurrents beneath the surface statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Focus on the target behavior is present but the session lacks a clear strategic arc or progression. No MI-specific agenda (rulers, discrepancy) was used to give the session more structure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good collaborative stance with 'I'm curious' framing and avoidance of expert positioning. Could have more actively co-constructed the session direction with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Standout strength — consistent reinforcement of client ownership throughout. Examples like 'that's yours, not something anyone else is dictating' and 'you're free to go' perfectly exemplify the spirit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Present but limited in scope; one good question drew out genuine motivation but change talk wasn't further developed. No scaling questions, future projection, or values exploration were used.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance maintained with inclusive language and no expert-driven positioning. Could have more explicitly invited the client to co-set the agenda or direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made a skillful move to explore the client's stated plan but didn't deeply develop the resulting change talk. The brief expressions like 'getting caught ain't fun' were not expanded using MI techniques.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Well-handled rolling with resistance, no counter-arguing, and sustained reflections of the client's perspective. Could have used more double-sided reflections to gently introduce the change side.
