# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Jonathan’s burden and avoids judgment, though some reframing of his behavior remains slightly interpretive. Acceptance is strong but falls short of fully affirming him beyond his illness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captures Jonathan’s ambivalence and the weight of the blood-sugar number. It would benefit from deeper attunement to his shame, exhaustion, and self-critical pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Focus generally remains on diabetes self-management behaviors such as checking sugar and short walks. Occasional drift into broader emotional themes without a clear consolidated target keeps it from a 5.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites Jonathan’s perspective, but often drives the conversation with successive questions. More explicit co-creation of agenda and priorities would strengthen equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Advice-giving and coercion are absent, with repeated emphasis that Jonathan knows his own life and limits. Slightly pressuring hypothetical questions and lack of explicit affirmation of his right not to change prevent a 5.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor attempts to elicit Jonathan’s own ideas about support, checking, and walking. Evocation remains limited because small pieces of change talk are not consistently reflected, amplified, or connected to values and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Jonathan is positioned as the expert on his days and body, supporting a collaborative stance. However, the session remains somewhat counselor-directed without clear negotiated agenda-setting or client-led prioritization.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some open questions invite Jonathan to consider small steps like checking sugar or a short walk. The counselor does not consistently affirm, amplify, or build on his expressed willingness, limiting change talk development.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing and acknowledges the validity of Jonathan’s concerns. Sustain talk is not fully softened because there is limited redirection or balancing of his overwhelm with his own possible motivations.
