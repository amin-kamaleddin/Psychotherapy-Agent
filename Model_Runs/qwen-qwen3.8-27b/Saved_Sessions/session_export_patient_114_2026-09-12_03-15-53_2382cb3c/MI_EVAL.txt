# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor uses respectful, nonjudgmental language and validates Ryan's concerns about his degree. However, acceptance remains more content-focused than emotionally attuned to Ryan's fear and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers accurate paraphrases and some useful reflections of Ryan's situation. These reflections do not fully capture the deeper anxiety, defensiveness, or emotional weight behind his behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The session keeps a clear focus on probation, documents, and practical next steps. This direction moves away from the core MI target of exploring ambivalence around alcohol use and discrepancy with Ryan's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites Ryan's input and collaborates on wording for a meeting script. The collaboration is limited mainly to the practical task of drafting a statement rather than broader therapeutic exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges Ryan's choices at times but then provides prescriptive advice and structured safety rules. This reduces Ryan's sense of ownership over his decisions and goals.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some initial open-ended questions, but the counselor quickly shifts to providing information and advice. Ryan's own reasons for change are not fully drawn out or strengthened.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers options and asks Ryan to choose, creating some sense of collaboration. The interaction remains counselor-led, with the provider driving the agenda and providing expert solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ryan expresses several relevant concerns and values, such as caring about his degree and not wanting to mess things up. The counselor does not effectively reflect, amplify, or explore these as sources of motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Ryan minimizes the drinking and normalizes four beers over three hours, but this sustain talk is not addressed. The counselor does not use reflections, discrepancy work, or gentle redirection to soften his resistance.
