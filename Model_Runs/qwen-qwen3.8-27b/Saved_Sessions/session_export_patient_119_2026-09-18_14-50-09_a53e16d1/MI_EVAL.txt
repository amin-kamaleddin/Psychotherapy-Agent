# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and genuine acceptance throughout. The psychiatrist normalizes the client's experience in multiple ways without any labeling, disapproval, or condescension.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures the dual nature of ambivalence and emotional undercurrents at the level of meaning, not just surface content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on medication adherence with logical progression from ambivalence to concrete plan. Could benefit from a more explicit signal toward narrowing to a commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Model of collaborative interaction with the psychiatrist consistently positioning as a curious partner and building on the client's own language and ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's ownership of the change process. Never suggests, recommends, or prescribes — all solutions come from the client herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within rather than providing external reasons. Could be deeper with scaling questions or more explicit elicitation of DARN-C statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Entirely evocative questioning style with collaborative, non-hierarchical language. The session is clearly shaped by the client's contributions at every turn.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation of change talk through strategic questioning and affirming reflections. Could more explicitly draw out confidence and ability statements in the client's own words.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully handles sustain talk by validating while gently holding the other side without arguing. Good balance, though could pivot more decisively back to change.
