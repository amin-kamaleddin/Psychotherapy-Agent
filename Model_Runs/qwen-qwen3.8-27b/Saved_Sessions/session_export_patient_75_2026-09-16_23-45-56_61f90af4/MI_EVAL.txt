# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance and affirmed Linda’s experience. It fell short of a 5 mainly because some responses moved into interpretive reframing rather than staying in pure acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening was accurate and often nuanced, capturing Linda’s ambivalence and safety concerns well. A few reflections remained slightly surface-level and missed deeper emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on medication adherence and building a clear list of warning signs. The flow was logical and purposeful, with only minor room for stronger linking back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acted as a true partner, using joint language and offering options rather than deciding for Linda. The conversation remained a genuine two-way exploration in which Linda’s ideas shaped the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Linda’s right to choose was clearly centered throughout, with no pressure, prescription, or implied obligation. This was a textbook example of strong autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently used open questions to draw out Linda’s own ideas and motivations. Some questions were slightly leading, which slightly reduced the pure open-ended quality.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally treated Linda as the expert and invited her input throughout. It missed a 5 by occasionally framing or defining the process instead of fully ceding direction to the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively evoked and affirmed change talk, especially around capability and self-care. The main gap was not always amplifying or building strategically on Linda’s strongest change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled skillfully by validating concerns without arguing or dismissing them. It could have improved with more double-sided reflections that gently tilt toward change while still acknowledging safety worries.
