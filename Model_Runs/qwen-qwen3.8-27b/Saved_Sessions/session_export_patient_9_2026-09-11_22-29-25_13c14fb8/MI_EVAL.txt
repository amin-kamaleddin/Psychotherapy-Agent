# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful and supportive, but the acceptance feels procedural rather than warmly unconditional. Frequent directive, problem-solving language reduces the sense of deep, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers some reflective statements, but they remain mostly content-level rather than emotionally attuned. There are missed opportunities to reflect Amina’s exhaustion, loneliness, sadness, and ambivalence with stronger double-sided reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on bedtime consistency and co-parenting communication. The counselor effectively keeps the conversation moving toward a specific, actionable behavioral goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is moderate collaboration, with the client choosing among options and agreeing to a plan. However, most of the structure, scripts, and strategies are counselor-generated rather than co-created with Amina.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices and respects Amina’s decision to use a simple two-week trial. Still, prescriptive language such as “set and enforce” and “send the text now” weakens the sense that Amina is the decision-maker.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor includes a few evocative openings, but the style quickly becomes advice-driven rather than drawing out Amina’s own motivations, confidence, and values. More open questions and reflective follow-ups would better support client-generated change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The counselor adopts an expert-driven stance by providing scripts, routines, and plans early in the session. This limits the sense that Amina’s own expertise, insights, and preferences are leading the work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Amina produces strong change talk tied to her values and concern for her son, but the counselor does not consistently amplify it. Brief affirmations are not followed by deeper exploration or questions that expand her own motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor acknowledges difficulty without blame but does not consistently soften or redirect sustain talk. Responses to Amina’s concerns about conflict often remain problem-solving rather than balanced, reflective MI responses.
