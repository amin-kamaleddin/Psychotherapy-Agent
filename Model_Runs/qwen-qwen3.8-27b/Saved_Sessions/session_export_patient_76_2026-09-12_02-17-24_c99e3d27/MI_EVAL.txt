# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers warm, nonjudgmental acceptance and validation around the self-harm. Remains somewhat formulaic/task-oriented rather than deeply personalized, preventing a higher score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides accurate reflections of pain, exhaustion, and practical coping needs. Could have explored deeper emotional layers such as fear, helplessness, shame, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, relevant focus on self-harm reduction and immediate coping planning. Becomes somewhat repetitive around means removal, urgency, and safety checks, which limits MI momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint problem-solving and uses client choices to shape the plan. Still asymmetrical, as the psychiatrist supplies most of the content, options, and structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client control through invitational language, open choices, and respectful follow-through. Client’s preferences, including when and how to move items, are consistently honored.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some evocative questions that elicit client commitments and preferences. Does not consistently evoke deeper values, confidence, past successes, or personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and invites client input throughout. Still somewhat clinician-led because most options and agenda are psychiatrist-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Affirms meaningful change statements and supports practical commitments. Could have more consistently expanded and deepened the client’s own reasons, desire, and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Responds to sustain talk with validation and practical narrowing rather than debate. Lacks stronger MI strategies such as double-sided reflections to soften ambivalence more strategically.
