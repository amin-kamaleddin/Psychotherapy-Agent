# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, affirming Camila's current efforts and never judging her for not doing more. However, the acceptance occasionally feels slightly performative rather than deeply felt, with affirmations that are skillful but somewhat predictable in structure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional empathic accuracy throughout, consistently reflecting not just content but the emotional and existential meaning behind Camila's words. Reflections go beyond simple paraphrase into genuine understanding of the emotional weight, with each reflection building on the last to show deepening understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on increasing physical activity with logical progression from curiosity through barriers to concrete options. The session could benefit from more strategic momentum in the final minutes, particularly in steering toward a specific next step or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session feels like a joint exploration with reflective language inviting Camila to build on what was said, and the 'mapping out' metaphor frames the work as collaborative. The interaction could be deeper by more explicitly acknowledging that Camila's life experience is the primary source of wisdom.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist never prescribes or says 'you should,' consistently framing decisions as Camila's to make and affirming she already knows what she wants. Support could be strengthened by more explicit statements about her freedom and affirming her autonomy in choosing her own path distinct from her mother's example.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws motivation from within Camila using her own language ('durable,' 'anchor') and values rather than providing external reasons for change. Could be more systematic in explicitly drawing out confidence level, self-efficacy, and the personal meaning of her goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with open-ended questions and inclusive language, consistently treating Camila as the expert on her own life. Could be stronger through more explicit power-sharing, such as acknowledging her expertise or inviting her to guide direction more explicitly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies key change talk phrases like 'durable' and 'I don't want to become her,' building on them rather than simply accepting. Could more boldly amplify the strongest change talk moments and explicitly elicit confidence language to develop it to its fullest potential.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with appropriate care, validating the legitimacy of concerns like energy and fear of failure without reinforcing them as barriers. The softening is somewhat passive, as the psychiatrist validates rather than actively redirecting or minimizing the weight of sustain talk moments.
