# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental language that helps Amina feel safe. Acceptance is supportive but not consistently deep or individualized to Amina’s identity and values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Amina’s ambivalence and captures the phone’s role as a safety valve. Some emotional themes, such as fear of loneliness and shame, could be explored more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session generally stays focused on social media use and its effects on sleep, energy, and grades. It sometimes drifts into implementation details, such as reminders and specific experiments, which slightly dilutes focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for input and accepts Amina’s preferences, such as declining reminders. Much of the plan remains therapist-generated rather than fully co-created from Amina’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly emphasizes choice, nonjudgment, and optional, low-pressure experiments. Frequent suggestions create a subtle expectation that can compete with the patient’s autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks helpful questions about what scrolling does and what happens when panic starts. The work often moves quickly into behavioral experiments, limiting deeper exploration of Amina’s values, goals, and motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites Amina’s input on how she wants to proceed. The session can feel agenda-driven because of the frequent introduction of specific strategies and options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina expresses meaningful change talk about sleep, missed lecture, grades, and curiosity about the “buzz.” The psychiatrist could more consistently amplify, reflect back, and deepen this change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk without arguing and recognizes the phone as calming. It could more skillfully use double-sided reflections to integrate sustain talk into deeper ambivalence exploration.
