# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating of Jamal’s frustration. Acceptance is mostly task-focused rather than deeply person-centered.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening is strong, especially in balancing competing interests and validating feelings. Reflections often remain at the surface level rather than exploring deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on punctuality and related routines. The target behavior remains consistent, though rapid question sequences slightly affect pacing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs a concrete plan using Jamal’s own ideas. Collaboration is strong, but suggestions are sometimes offered before fully eliciting the client’s perspective.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks permission, offers choices, and respects the client’s preferences. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly reinforcing Jamal’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Practical questions elicit planning language and some client-generated solutions. Deeper intrinsic motivation, self-efficacy, and value-based reasons are not consistently evoked.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and asks the client to choose from options. At times, the psychiatrist takes on an expert role by quickly moving to problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits some change talk through importance, confidence, and action questions. There are missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally avoids arguing with sustain talk and reflects it neutrally. Sustain talk is not always redirected toward personal benefit or internal motivation.
