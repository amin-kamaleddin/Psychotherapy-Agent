# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor is respectful and nonjudgmental, accepting Amina's silence and refusals without pressure. Repetitive prompting after refusal slightly limits unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor makes reasonable surface-level reflections of Amina's discomfort. Deeper recognition of her need for control, mistrust, and ambivalence is missing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on the immediate need to engage a quiet client. It lacks a clearer MI-based strategic trajectory and concise co-created plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor offers options and lets Amina's choices shape the session. Collaboration is strong but could better acknowledge Amina's unique expertise about her own experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor frequently affirms Amina's choice and control over the session. Repeated questions after clear refusal slightly undermine autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few initial attempts to elicit client input, but limited ongoing evocation of internal motivation. The counselor misses opportunities to draw out what staying or checking in might mean to Amina.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor works with Amina rather than directing her, inviting her lead throughout. Partnership would be stronger if the counselor acknowledged Amina's self-knowledge more explicitly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initial MI-style questions open the door, but there is little consistent development of change talk. The counselor does not fully use Amina's small engagement choices to evoke motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor avoids arguing with or reinforcing Amina's resistant statements. It would be stronger if double-sided reflections were used to link resistance with her continued presence.
