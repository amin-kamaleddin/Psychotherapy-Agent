# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and supportive, without judgment or labeling. Occasional assumptive framing slightly reduces fully unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture the client’s ambivalence and practical concerns. However, some reflections are mildly interpretive and explore deeper emotion less fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on the target behavior of managing alcohol offers. Each exchange advances toward a concrete, practical plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction is collaborative, with the client shaping the session and co-constructing strategies. The psychiatrist still sets much of the agenda, limiting fully client-led collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports choice, ownership, and the client’s own values. There is no pressure to adopt a specific solution, and the client is treated as the expert.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits the client’s own motivations, plans, and confidence. Core change reasons are generated by the client rather than supplied by the psychiatrist.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions the client as an expert and invites his input throughout. However, frequent suggested options make the partnership strong but not fully equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently builds on the client’s change talk and strengthens it through questions and reflections. The client clearly articulates reasons, desires, and plans for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without being reinforced and is redirected toward values and agency. More explicit double-sided reflections would make the softening more consistent.
