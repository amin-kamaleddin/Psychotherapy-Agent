# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong nonjudgmental stance with effective normalization of the referral and explicit disclaimer of taking sides. Warmth is professional and measured rather than deeply personal or emotionally resonant.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening that accurately captures ambivalence and tension in the client's experience. Reflections are largely cognitive in nature, tracking thinking but not deeply exploring the emotional undercurrent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains consistent focus on the target behavior with a logical, purposeful progression from exploring daily routines to identifying specific barriers. Generic closing questions ('Can you tell me more?') represent a minor opportunity for more targeted direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses strong collaborative language and invites the client to define what would be useful. However, the process remains primarily one-directional (psychiatrist asks, client responds) with limited evidence of true co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support throughout, consistently reinforcing that the client is the ultimate decision-maker. Explicitly honors the distinction between 'I'll hear you out' and 'I'm doing it,' never pressuring or implying obligation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the client's own solutions (phone charger analogy) and concerns (permanence) rather than providing them. Could be deeper by exploring values more explicitly or using confidence ratings to amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as collaborative, explicitly sharing power and ceding agenda-setting to the client. The psychiatrist still drives the questioning sequence, and the client's contributions don't always redirect the session's trajectory.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes change talk through open questions and notices/reinforces the client's own shifting language. Could have gone further in amplifying or summarizing change talk more explicitly, or asking the client to rate confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without judgment but uses relatively limited softening techniques. Misses opportunities for strategic double-sided reflections that could more effectively reframe resistance.
