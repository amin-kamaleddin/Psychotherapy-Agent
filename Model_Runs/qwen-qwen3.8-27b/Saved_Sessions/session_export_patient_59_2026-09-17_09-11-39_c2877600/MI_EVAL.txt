# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a consistently nonjudgmental and accepting stance without labeling or moralizing. Affirmations such as 'not because you're weak' and 'not here to judge' support positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections track court pressure, maternal concern, and peer context, but some statements overstate emotional intensity. Deeper, double-sided empathy around fear, autonomy, or identity would strengthen this area.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session eventually narrows to staying away from weed and a small boundary, but early discussion drifts toward logistics and compliance. A clearer summary linking values, consequences, and change would improve direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Jamal to shape the session and incorporates his preferences for efficiency. However, therapist-initiated tools and the court-driven frame limit fully equal collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly respected through repeated emphasis on choice, willingness, and the absence of pressure. Jamal is allowed to define his goal and plan in his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist evokes some small-step planning and confidence, but the focus remains mainly on compliance. More evocation of personal values, what Jamal wants to protect, or intrinsic motivation would be stronger.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids an expert-driven stance and asks for Jamal's perspective throughout. The introduction of a confidence scale by the clinician slightly limits the sense of shared power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited, such as 'not today' and a confidence rating of 6. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify or expand internal motivations, values, or ability statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and does not reinforce minimization, but uses limited double-sided reflections or discrepancy. Connecting 'not a big deal' to probation, school, freedom, or his mother would strengthen this criterion.
