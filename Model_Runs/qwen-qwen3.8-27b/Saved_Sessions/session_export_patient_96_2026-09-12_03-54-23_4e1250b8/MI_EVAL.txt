### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms Marisol as the decision-maker. Some agreement feels formulaic and could better acknowledge the complexity of her referral.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflects key parts of Marisol’s perspective accurately, including her choice, appearance preference, and independence. Reflections remain somewhat surface-level and miss deeper identity or control themes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session generally stays connected to the referral concern but lacks a clear strategic focus on the specific issue. It becomes directionless toward the end and does not build a coherent, client-centered step forward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Marisol’s input, follows her lead, and avoids imposing an expert agenda. Collaboration is respectful but somewhat passive, with limited joint exploration or co-creative meaning-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Marisol’s choice and avoids pressure, advice-giving, or argument. This is one of the strongest MI domains in the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Asks a few open-ended questions but does not effectively draw out Marisol’s own values, motivations, or reasons for change. The counselor mostly reflects her current stance rather than evoking client-centered change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and repeatedly positions Marisol as the expert on her own life. The partnership is somewhat passive and does not actively invite Marisol to help shape a shared understanding.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are few attempts to elicit reasons for change, confidence, values, or behavior modification. Small openings about self-care are met with reassurance rather than exploration of change-relevant language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoids direct confrontation but often validates sustain talk in ways that reinforce the status quo. More effective MI would have used balanced reflections or gentle pivots to create room for reconsideration.
