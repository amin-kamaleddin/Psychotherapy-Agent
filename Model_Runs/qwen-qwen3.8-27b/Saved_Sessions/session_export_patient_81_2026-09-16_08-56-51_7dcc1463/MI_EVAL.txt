### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, avoiding blame or labeling. Acceptance could be deeper and more explicitly affirming of Lukas as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Lukas' ambivalence about rent, acting income, and social life. It could go deeper into shame, fear, identity loss, or audition anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on concrete financial goals, especially rent and avoiding borrowing. The broader substance-use and social-identity connection remains underdeveloped.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds the plan around Lukas' preferences and ideas, creating a practical collaborative step. Collaboration is strong but not fully equal, as the psychiatrist still sets much of the structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports choice, control, and noncoercive decision-making. Autonomy support is slightly limited by repeated structured options and prompts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits motivation, confidence, and concrete next steps from Lukas. Evocation is practical but could explore deeper values, fears, and intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Lukas' input and positions him as the decision-maker. The session remains somewhat driven by the psychiatrist's options and structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist draws out some change talk, including the immediate $20 transfer and follow-up plan. More questions about values, future goals, and reducing meth use would deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and gently acknowledges ambivalence. Sustain talk is often bypassed rather than directly softened using double-sided reflections or reframing.
