# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental with strong affirmations and reframing. Slightly patterned in expression (repeatedly reframing negatives as positives), which prevents a perfect score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate and nuanced reflections that capture emotional undercurrents like exhaustion, grief, and self-awareness beyond surface content. Goes well beyond simple paraphrase to name the feelings beneath what is said.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear strategic arc from exploration to a concrete client-generated plan with logical momentum at each step. Excellent pacing—patient yet purposeful, never drifting into generic advice or unrelated territory.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely equal power dynamic where the client's ideas substantially shape the session direction. The psychiatrist builds on client insights rather than steering toward pre-determined answers.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never uses directive language; consistently frames everything in terms of the client's own choices and preferences. The final plan is entirely client-generated, with autonomy woven naturally into every interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never provides external arguments for change; every question is designed to draw out the client's own values, preferences, and motivations. The resulting change talk is entirely client-generated and authentic.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert on his own life, with the session genuinely co-constructed around client insights. Explicitly acknowledges when leading was inappropriate, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own change language through strategic questions and amplifies generated change talk with naming. Could have been extended slightly into confidence language for a perfect score.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reframes doubt and resistance as useful self-knowledge rather than blockers, never arguing or dismissing. One or two moments where the pivot from sustain talk to change talk could have been slightly more gradual.
