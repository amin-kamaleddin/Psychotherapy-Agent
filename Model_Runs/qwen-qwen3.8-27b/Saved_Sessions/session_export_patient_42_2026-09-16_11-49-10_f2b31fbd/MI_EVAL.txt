# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Elise's ambivalence and social concerns. Acceptance is solid but could be deeper and more explicitly affirming of Elise as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects key concerns, such as smoking as an anchor and fear of sounding judgmental. Reflections are strong but could explore the deeper emotions behind Elise's need for belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on reducing smoking while preserving social connection. The work moves clearly toward a specific, practical behavior goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elise is involved in choosing options and practicing, but the psychiatrist remains the main source of strategies. More co-creation would strengthen the collaborative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly offers choices and avoids coercion. Autonomy support is slightly limited by the heavy use of scripted lines and directive guidance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocative questions and role-play help Elise express commitment. However, the psychiatrist often provides language and instructions rather than consistently drawing out Elise's own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for input but often functions as an expert directing the session. A stronger partnership would involve more equal co-creation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elise offers meaningful change talk, and the psychiatrist acknowledges it. More deliberate amplification and expansion of this talk would strengthen the MI process.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is accepted and reframed rather than challenged, which is effective. Using more advanced MI techniques, such as double-sided reflections, could further resolve ambivalence.
