# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist remained nonjudgmental and normalized Daniel's ambivalence without shaming. Acceptance could be strengthened by explicitly affirming Daniel's values and identity, not just his plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured ambivalence around drinking, training, and control. Deeper reflections on emotional concerns, such as fear of being monitored or loss of routine, would increase empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on alcohol use and created a clear, practical tracking plan. Direction could be improved by linking the plan more consistently to motivation, values, and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Daniel's preferences strongly shaped the final plan, including method, timing, and follow-up format. The interaction consistently reflected equal-partner collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently used optional language and respected Daniel's desire to avoid pressure. There was no coercion, and choice was clearly supported throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Daniel was asked to identify signs that would make change worthwhile, which elicited some self-generated language. The session did not deeply explore values, confidence, or the meaning of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited input repeatedly and avoided a directive stance. Partnership could be enhanced by more explicitly affirming Daniel as the expert on his own body, goals, and readiness.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk was elicited through questions about warning signs and small-step options. The psychiatrist could have amplified and built more strongly on Daniel's own change-related statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing with Daniel's minimizing statements and kept the tone low-pressure. Sustain talk was not actively softened through double-sided reflections or deeper exploration of what Daniel is protecting.
