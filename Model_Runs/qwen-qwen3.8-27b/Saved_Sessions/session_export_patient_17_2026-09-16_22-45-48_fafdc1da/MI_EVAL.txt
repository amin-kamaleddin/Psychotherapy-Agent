# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally communicates respect and nonjudgment without labeling the client. Some affirmations feel slightly counselor-appraised and do not fully normalize shame or past struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses good reflective listening and accurately captures the client’s ambivalence and meaning-making. However, the emotional intensity of her shame and stigma is not reflected as deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains consistently focused on smoking cessation and the immediate behavior-change target. There is no meaningful drift from the chosen agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan is largely built from the client’s own ideas and suggestions. However, the psychiatrist still controls much of the pacing and agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports the client’s sense of choice, ownership, and control. The framing avoids pressure and makes the plan contingent on the client’s readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist frequently evokes the client’s own motivations and generates a client-centered plan. There are moments with more counselor-generated suggestions and instructional framing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites the client’s perspective and builds on her ideas. However, the psychiatrist still directs the agenda and pacing more than a fully equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces the client’s change talk. It is not consistently developed across the full DARN-C range or amplified with scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist appropriately normalizes and refrains from arguing against the client’s sustain talk. More advanced softening techniques are not consistently used.
