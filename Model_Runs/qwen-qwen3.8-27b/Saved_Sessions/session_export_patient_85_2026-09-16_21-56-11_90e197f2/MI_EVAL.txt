### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist communicates respect and validation without blame, though acceptance could be warmer and more explicitly affirming of Marco’s strengths and confidence.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Marco’s concern about rules and self-worth, but become repetitive and miss his pride, high confidence, and enjoyment of juice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on juice habits and implementation, but it becomes somewhat circular and does not clearly consolidate a next step or commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist builds on Marco’s own ideas and language, though repeated conditional questions can make the process feel more guided than fully negotiated.

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Marco to lead and uses his language, but could strengthen partnership with clearer power-sharing and less repetitive questioning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong change talk emerges, including plans and confidence, but the psychiatrist does not consistently amplify, summarize, or explore it more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
