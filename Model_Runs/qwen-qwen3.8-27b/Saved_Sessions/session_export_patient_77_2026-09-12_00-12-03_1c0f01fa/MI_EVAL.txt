# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses respectful, nonjudgmental statements and normalizes Liyana’s discouragement. However, the warmth remains somewhat procedural and does not fully affirm her worth apart from the scale, body, or plan adherence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are adequate reflections of frustration, loneliness, fear, and guilt, but they stay relatively surface-level. The psychiatrist does not consistently explore the deeper shame, self-doubt, or isolation behind Liyana’s statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, practical focus on a small, realistic behavioral step with a two-week plan. A higher score would come from more consistently linking that behavior to Liyana’s broader values, self-esteem, or long-term health goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Liyana contributes meaningfully by choosing the snack, tracking method, and start day. However, the psychiatrist still supplies most of the structure and ideas, keeping the interaction more therapist-led than fully collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist offers options and reduces tracking burden, which supports some sense of choice. Yet repeated prompting toward the plan and directive language limit consistent autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are limited attempts to elicit Liyana’s own motivations, values, or reasons for change. The counselor mainly provides information and a structured plan rather than drawing out her internal motivation and hopes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and uses collaborative language such as planning together. However, Liyana is not consistently positioned as the expert on her own life, habits, or coping patterns, so the power balance remains uneven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Liyana offers small statements of willingness, such as “I can do that” and choosing the snack plan. The psychiatrist does not skillfully reflect, amplify, or systematically develop her change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and offers useful reframes about stalls and slips. However, the continued emphasis on the scale and weight change may reinforce Liyana’s fear that the plan has failed.
