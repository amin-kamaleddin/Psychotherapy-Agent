# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating tone throughout. Acceptance is solid but somewhat procedural and could more explicitly affirm Anya's maturity and self-worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Anya's ambivalence and behavioral patterns. The empathy remains more cognitive and content-focused rather than deeply exploring feelings such as nervousness, embarrassment, or social pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use and its consequences without drifting into unrelated topics. Direction is limited by repeated cycling through similar options without clear momentum or a strategic progression.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and seeks Anya's input on how to proceed. However, frequent menu-style options make the process feel more counselor-driven than truly co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, avoiding pressure, and using importance scaling. It could be strengthened by more explicitly affirming that any decision—change, wait, or monitor—is Anya's call.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open-ended questions, reflections, and a 0–10 importance question to elicit motivation. Evocation is limited by repeated closed, menu-style questions and inconsistent follow-up on Anya's own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Anya to make choices and follows her stated preferences to some extent. The repeated structured options create a slightly counselor-led process rather than a fully equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully evokes some change talk through questions about change, consequences, and importance of change. The effort is limited by insufficient amplification, summarizing, and follow-up on Anya's emerging reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with or reinforcing sustain talk and redirects the conversation toward consequences and pros/cons. Sustain talk is not fully softened using techniques such as double-sided reflections linking pros to costs and values.
