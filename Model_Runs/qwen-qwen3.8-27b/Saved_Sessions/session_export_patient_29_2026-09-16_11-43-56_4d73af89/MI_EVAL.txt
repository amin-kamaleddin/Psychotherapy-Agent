# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist generally demonstrates respect, warmth, and nonjudgmental support. However, acceptance is not consistently deep or fully individualized, with some positive but subtly evaluative language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes a reasonable effort to understand David's perspective and uses accurate reflections. However, empathy is often factual and summary-like rather than emotionally penetrating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a clear and consistent focus on smoking cessation. The session progresses with momentum, keeping the work purposeful and task-oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates a collaborative stance by working toward a plan with David. However, the psychiatrist supplies most of the content, structure, and decision framework, making the collaboration uneven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist supports client autonomy by inviting David's input and allowing his preferences to shape the plan. However, the session occasionally drifts toward a plan-approval style.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks some evocative questions early on but does not consistently elicit motivation from within David. The session shifts to educational and planning content rather than deeply exploring David's own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some partnership by inviting David's input and allowing his preferences to shape the plan. However, the interaction is often clinician-led and somewhat expert-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are some initial efforts to evoke change talk, but the psychiatrist does not systematically develop or deepen the client's change talk. The session moves quickly into medication options and practical coping strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not reinforce or argue with sustain talk, responding with normalization. However, sustain talk is addressed practically rather than motivationally, without consistent redirection toward change.
