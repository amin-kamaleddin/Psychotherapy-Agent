# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent, warm unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing discomfort and explicitly stating non-judgment. Never lectures or expresses disapproval, even when the patient minimizes or resists.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Goes beyond surface-level reflection to capture the emotional function behind the patient's words (e.g., identifying the avoidance pattern, validating the developmental perspective of a 16-year-old). Reflections are precise, nuanced, and consistently accurate.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior tied to the patient's specific goals (soccer, college, scholarships) without drifting into unrelated topics. The session has a deliberate arc that progressively deepens engagement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a joint exploration rather than a clinical assessment, with the patient positioned as an equal partner in inquiry. The final insight emerges entirely from the patient's own reasoning, representing the gold standard of collaborative MI.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the patient's ownership of choices and never prescribes or directs behavior. Even informational offers are paired with questions that keep the power with the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every piece of insight in the session comes from the patient's own mouth, drawn out through skilled open questions rather than delivered as external advice. Textbook high-level evocation that facilitates genuine self-motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions the patient as the expert on his own body and goals, consistently asking him to generate his own answers and conclusions. The power dynamic is clearly one of equal partnership with the psychiatrist as a guide.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully identifies and amplifies moments of shift in the patient's language, drawing attention to and crediting his own change talk. The session culminates in the patient articulating a clear, self-generated insight about his avoidance pattern.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every instance of minimization is met with validation followed by gentle curiosity that redirects toward the patient's own values and goals. Sustain talk is consistently honored and softly redirected without pressure, debate, or contradiction.
