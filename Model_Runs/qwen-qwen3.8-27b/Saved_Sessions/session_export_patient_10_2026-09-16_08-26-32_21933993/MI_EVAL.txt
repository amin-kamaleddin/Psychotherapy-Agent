# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor maintains a respectful, validating tone and affirms Amina’s efforts. Some affirmations are slightly performance-based rather than fully unconditional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor accurately reflects frustration, fear, and professional concern. Empathy often stays at the practical level and does not consistently explore deeper values or underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays mostly focused on the interview barrier and moves toward a concrete next step. There is slight drift into general problem-solving and self-regulation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor invites input and adapts the message to Amina’s voice, but supplies most of the options and content. Amina often selects from counselor-provided solutions rather than generating her own.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor offers choices, respects pacing, and avoids most coercive language. Some directive recommendations and instructions reduce full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some evocative questions, but the session is driven more by practical suggestions than by eliciting Amina’s motivations, values, and confidence. Deeper evocative follow-ups are not consistently used.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina’s preferences influence the session, but the counselor often remains in the expert problem-solver role. More equal partnership would involve letting Amina lead the exploration of her own resources and ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Amina offers meaningful change talk, and the counselor reinforces it appropriately. There are missed opportunities to expand or deepen this change talk with evocative follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor generally normalizes barriers without arguing, but does not consistently use MI-specific strategies to soften sustain talk. Some reflections may amplify the sense of being stuck.
