# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and clearly affirms the patient's efforts and struggles. However, it lacks deeper warmth and unconditional positive regard regarding her worth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening of the patient's ambivalence and constraints, including some emotional undertones. The reflections occasionally become repetitive and remain more focused on logistics than deeper emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a consistent focus on the target behavior of finding an adherence anchor without drifting into unrelated topics. Could have been more deliberate in its strategic direction toward MI-defined change momentum or next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative, building on the patient's ideas and using inclusive language. However, the counselor still led the structure and introduced hypotheticals, limiting a fully client-led agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized that the solution should feel like support rather than a demand and avoided pressure or directives. Could be stronger by explicitly affirming the patient's right to choose and full ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used MI-consistent open-ended questions to elicit the patient's ideas, but remained largely practical. Missed opportunities to consistently evoke deeper internal motivation, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited the patient's input and positioned her as the source of knowledge about her own life. However, lacked explicit agenda-setting or power-sharing, maintaining a somewhat structured approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some initial change talk but did not consistently deepen, amplify, or summarize it. The conversation remained heavily focused on logistical problem-solving rather than strengthening motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing against sustain talk and occasionally redirected toward change. However, mostly relied on neutral reflections rather than actively using double-sided reflections or exploring underlying values.
