# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Jordan's concerns without shaming. Acceptance is somewhat procedural and could more deeply affirm his personhood and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening accurately tracks Jordan's reasoning and concerns about protecting his grandmother. The empathy is mainly cognitive and could explore emotional fear, guilt, or personal significance more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on flu vaccination, transmission risk, side effects, and next steps. Direction could be strengthened by more explicitly linking discussion to Jordan's values and readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites Jordan to choose the most useful options. Collaboration is practical but remains more menu-based than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist respects Jordan's refusals and offers options rather than pressuring a decision. More explicit reinforcement that the choice is entirely Jordan's would strengthen autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The opening questions elicit Jordan's concerns and priorities. After that, the session becomes more informational and misses opportunities to draw out importance, confidence, or personal next steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and adapts to Jordan's preferences, such as facts first and avoiding role-play. The interaction leans toward clinician-provided information and option menus rather than balanced power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There is some change talk around protecting his grandmother and reducing household transmission risk. The psychiatrist does not consistently deepen or expand this change talk through follow-up MI questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and responds neutrally to Jordan's concerns. The softening would benefit from more strategic reflections connecting reluctance with his values, and repeated booking offers may create mild pressure.
