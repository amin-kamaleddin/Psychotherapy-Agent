# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout; the psychiatrist never judges or labels, and masterfully validates the function of behaviors without endorsing them.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Outstanding empathic attunement with reflections that go beyond paraphrasing to capture emotional and existential meaning, such as connecting the phone to identity and autonomy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and consistent focus on evening smoking with logical progression to an actionable plan, though the early mention of chest tightness was not revisited as a concrete motivator for change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A model of collaborative interaction where the psychiatrist builds on Aisha's ideas, uses inclusive language, and never positions herself as the expert with a solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan is entirely Aisha's; no directive language is used, and the psychiatrist creates conditions for autonomy without ever exerting it or escalating the commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The strongest skill in the session; perfectly timed questions elicit motivations, plans, and emotional insights from Aisha herself rather than providing them externally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates that expertise about Aisha's life and solutions resides within her; every intervention is a question or reflection that invites Aisha to generate the next step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk emerges naturally through well-timed open questions and reflections rather than being forced; the psychiatrist highlights Aisha's own agency and progress explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is never argued with, dismissed, or amplified; it is consistently met with acceptance and then gently navigated around to make barriers feel more manageable.
