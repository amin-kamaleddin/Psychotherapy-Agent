# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates warmth, respect, and unconditional positive regard throughout the session without any judgment or disapproval. The tone remains genuinely supportive even when the patient redirects, and the closing affirmations reinforce deep positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates good empathic understanding by recognizing the patient's time pressure and practical nature, with a strong complex reflection capturing both cognitive and emotional dimensions. However, the empathy could have been deeper in exploring the emotional weight of managing competing demands or underlying anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear arc from the initial question through confirmation to a concrete action plan, with each response building toward the next step. The closing slightly broadens from the specific medication concern to general self-care messaging, which could have been more tightly tied back to the action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is structured as a partnership between equals, with the patient setting the pace, topic, and duration while the psychiatrist adapts in real-time. The language is consistently inclusive and co-creative, making this a textbook example of collaboration in a brief encounter.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist explicitly affirms the patient's right to set the terms of the interaction and offers genuine choices rather than leading questions. At no point is there pressure or coercion; autonomy is consistently and naturally woven into the conversation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully elicits and amplifies the patient's own motivations and agency rather than imposing reasons for change. The closing affirmations are somewhat declarative rather than evocative, though this is a minor limitation given the time constraint.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist positions the patient as the expert on her own life from the very first utterance and consistently follows her lead without defensiveness. The partnership fundamentally shapes the session's direction, with genuine offers of choice and no assumption of authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully reflects back and amplifies the patient's own change talk, connecting it to her values of self-advocacy and self-care. The session's brevity and the patient's time constraint naturally limit the depth and frequency of change talk that can be evoked.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist handles the patient's time constraint well by validating her approach and working within the provided timeframe without resistance or guilt. The gentle closing redirection toward self-care subtly softens the rushing pattern without being preachy, though the time pressure could have been more explicitly reframed as an opportunity.
