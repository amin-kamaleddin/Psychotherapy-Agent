# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently validates Isabella’s values, autonomy, and family traditions without shaming or moralizing. The stance is respectful, though somewhat procedural and less deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are good reflections of Isabella’s ambivalence and preferences, but some key emotional moments are missed or moved past quickly. Deeper feelings such as fear, guilt, or feeling pathologized are not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use during pregnancy and a concrete next step involving the brochure. The direction is clear, but it could have been more strategically tied to motivation and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works jointly with Isabella and adapts the plan to her preferences, such as removing scripts and avoiding scheduled check-ins. The client influences practical decisions well, but the psychiatrist still sets much of the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Isabella’s autonomy is strongly supported, with repeated emphasis on her right to decide on her own timeline. Some suggestions still carry a slight pressure that could feel mildly directive for a highly autonomy-focused client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist relies heavily on providing external information and options rather than drawing out Isabella’s own motivations. More evocative follow-up on her values, fears, and desired next steps would have strengthened the session.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and invites Isabella’s input throughout. The partnership is strong, but the psychiatrist remains somewhat the organizer and guide of the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited and affirmed, particularly around protecting the baby and possible compromise with nonalcoholic wine. However, the psychiatrist does not consistently develop or amplify Isabella’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing and does not reinforce Isabella’s sustain talk. That said, the response to conflicting beliefs is more informational than strategically MI-consistent, with limited use of double-sided reflections.
