# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is respectful, nonjudgmental, and normalizes the social function of drinking without blame. Acceptance could be stronger by adding more explicit unconditional positive regard toward Tyler as a person.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses accurate reflections of Tyler’s ambivalence and practical concerns. Deeper emotional meanings such as shame, fear, loneliness, or identity could be explored more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear and consistent focus on alcohol use, the conduct incident, safety, and consequences. The conversation moves logically from exploration to ambivalence to planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist works with Tyler’s ideas rather than imposing a specific plan. Collaboration could be stronger by allowing more explicit client-driven agenda setting and co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses open questions and avoids prescribing choices, positioning Tyler as the decision-maker. Autonomy support could be strengthened by explicitly emphasizing Tyler’s freedom to choose not to change now.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Tyler’s own reasons for change and possible strategies. Evocation could be deeper by exploring core values, identity, and self-efficacy more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Tyler’s input and treats him as the expert on his own life. The session could be more fully equal by explicitly negotiating the agenda and checking in on what matters most to Tyler.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits and reinforces Tyler’s change talk around grades, safety, and self-control. Change talk could be deepened by exploring past successes, confidence, and core values more explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist acknowledges Tyler’s desire to stay social without arguing or moralizing. Softening could be stronger with more balanced double-sided reflections that fully hold both sides of ambivalence.
