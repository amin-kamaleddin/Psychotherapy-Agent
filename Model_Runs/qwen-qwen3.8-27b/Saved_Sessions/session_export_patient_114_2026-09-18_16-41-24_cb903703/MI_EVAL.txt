# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, warm, and individualized positive regard throughout the session. The psychiatrist never judges or challenges Ryan's self-concept, responding to minimization with validation rather than correction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture the emotional and cognitive complexity of Ryan's experience, including his identity threat, social pressure, and ambivalence. The psychiatrist also attunes to narrative shifts (e.g., 'a few drinks' to 'I lost track') with sophistication.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, logical progression from establishing context through exploring values to brainstorming a concrete step. Could have moved more decisively toward a specific, measurable behavioral goal or commitment step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is fully co-constructed from Ryan's responses, with the psychiatrist using his exact language and following his leads. Questions and reflections deepen Ryan's own thinking rather than imposing an external framework.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reinforcement of Ryan's ownership and freedom to choose, with no prescribing, advising, or pressure. Even when Ryan expresses tentative willingness, the psychiatrist keeps the ball in his court without grabbing for commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ryan generates his own change talk and solutions in response to well-crafted open questions. The psychiatrist never provides external reasons for change, drawing out Ryan's values, concerns, and goals instead.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positions Ryan as the expert on his own life and follows where he leads. A clear power-sharing dynamic is maintained throughout, with Ryan's contributions substantially shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes meaningful change talk related to Ryan's degree, sleep, and social identity, with effective amplification through reflections. Could have more systematically explored confidence and ability (DARN-CAT categories) to push toward a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with textbook skill—acknowledging, validating, and gently redirecting without confrontation. Uses double-sided reflections and rolling with resistance effectively throughout the session.
