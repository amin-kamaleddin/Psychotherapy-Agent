# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates exceptional unconditional positive regard throughout with consistent validation and zero judgment. Even self-critical language is met with warmth rather than agreement with the self-judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture both cognitive and emotional dimensions, using the client's own metaphors ('headspace,' 'back and forth'). Skillfully notices and names shifts in the client's state in real time.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear strategic progression from ambivalence exploration to a concrete, self-determined action step. Each question builds logically on the prior response while following the client's lead rather than imposing an agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is structured as a genuine dialogue between equals with decisions co-constructed from the client's own ideas. The psychiatrist explicitly positions the client as the expert on her own experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Client sets the agenda, defines her own goals, and makes all key choices throughout the session. No directive language, prescriptions, or pressure is present at any point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits motivation from within the client through open questions and affirming existing strengths rather than providing external reasons for change. The session progresses from contemplation to preparation through evocation alone.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys that wisdom about change resides within the client, using language like 'you know your own brain better than I do.' Never positions self as having the answers; genuinely follows the client's direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterful progression from initial validation through curiosity, values evocation, and amplification of the client's own change statements. Strategic use of 'noticing the shift' technique helps the client recognize her own movement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledges fears without reinforcing them, redirecting from content of worry to emotional experience. Could be more strategically sophisticated by using double-sided reflections to simultaneously hold fear and hope.
