# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is generally respectful and offers basic validation such as “that makes sense” and “you’re not broken.” However, acceptance remains surface-level and often shifts quickly into advice and problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist shows some understanding of Daniela’s fear, embarrassment, and ambivalence. Reflections are present but often brief and do not fully explore the emotional depth of her concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on the target behavior of disclosing her diabetes to Sarah. Direction becomes overly focused on wording and practical plans, with less attention to ambivalence, values, and readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist asks for input and offers role-play options, which shows some collaborative intent. However, most of the content is counselor-generated, making the session feel more guided than truly collaborative.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist affirms Daniela’s choice and acknowledges that she can decide when to disclose. Frequent scripts and suggestions, though, make the interaction feel mildly pressuring and less autonomy-supportive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There are a few prompts for Daniela to share her own words and sample message. Overall, evocation is limited because the psychiatrist often supplies options rather than eliciting her motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** The session is largely expert-driven, with repeated scripts, delivery tips, and action plans. Daniela’s concerns about feeling defensive or rushed are not fully integrated into the direction of the conversation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela expresses several change talk statements about wanting to be honest and get it off her chest. The psychiatrist does not consistently notice, reflect, or amplify these moments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Daniela expresses fear that the disclosure will become awkward or make Sarah feel distant. The psychiatrist acknowledges these concerns but responds mainly with more scripts and contingency plans, which limits softening of sustain talk.
