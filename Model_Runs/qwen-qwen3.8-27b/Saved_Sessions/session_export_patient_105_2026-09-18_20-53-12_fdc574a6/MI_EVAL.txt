# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent respect and warmth, validating Naomi's uncertainty and countering her self-criticism without judgment. However, affirmations feel slightly formulaic and occasionally gently steering rather than purely accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate captures of the body-mind disconnect and self-undermining patterns. Reflections tend to stay at the cognitive level and don't always reach deeper emotional layers such as the loneliness beneath 'it's quieter when it's me.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains excellent focus on the target behavior with a clear progressive structure from understanding the problem through to a concrete action step. The 'quieter' metaphor serves as a consistent thread, and the session never drifts into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative framing that positions Naomi as a co-investigator and frequently builds on her contributions. However, the collaboration is somewhat one-directional with few moments of explicit co-creation or power-sharing language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy through open questions that invite Naomi's own insights, vision, and plan without ever prescribing treatment. Could be strengthened with more explicit acknowledgment of her right to make different choices, such as 'whatever you decide, that's your call.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Naomi's own motivations, solutions, and language throughout the session without ever providing answers. Could reach a higher level by more explicitly naming and reinforcing her evoked insights and eliciting confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with genuinely open and curious questions that position Naomi as the expert on her experience. Questions are quite structured and strategic, guiding the session in a clear direction rather than allowing Naomi's contributions to unpredictably redirect focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes multiple forms of change talk including desire, ability, need, and commitment through strategic open-ended questions. Could have further amplified or reinforced some of Naomi's strongest change talk moments to solidify them more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively handles sustain talk by validating uncertainty and skillfully reframing objections as familiar dismissive patterns rather than genuine concerns. The session could benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections to directly work with ambivalence.
