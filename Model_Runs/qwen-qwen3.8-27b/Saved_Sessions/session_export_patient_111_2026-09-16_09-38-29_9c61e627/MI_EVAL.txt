# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming of Richard’s choice without pressure. The psychiatrist avoids moralizing even when Richard is defensive or dismissive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately reflects external pressure, the reading/smoke ritual, and irritation with weekly lectures. However, it often stays surface-level rather than reaching deeper ambivalence or relational concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Initially focused on smoking and cessation concerns, but later drifts toward protecting the reading hour and managing interruptions. The session ends without a clear next step toward tobacco change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers options and lets Richard choose, adapting when suggestions are rejected. Less fully collaborative when generating practical ideas such as signs, scripts, or check-ins.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by repeatedly stating no pressure and respecting Richard’s right to refuse or stop. The closing leaves the door open without conditioning return.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses some open-ended questions to elicit Richard’s perspective, but does not continue drawing out deeper motivations or values. The counselor shifts to practical problem-solving after resistance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites input and avoids imposing an expert agenda, giving Richard control over topics. Could more explicitly affirm that Richard is the expert on his own life and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes only a few attempts to explore what smoking does for Richard or what a reduced ritual might look like. Change talk is not systematically amplified, and sustain talk remains dominant.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoids arguing but also does not effectively soften statements such as “I’m not quitting” or “I’m not rethinking anything.” Little use of double-sided reflections or cost exploration to create ambivalence.
