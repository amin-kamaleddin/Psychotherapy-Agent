# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with genuine positive regard shown through validating phrases and normalizing the client's experience. Slightly formulaic at times, preventing a full 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with accurate perspective-taking, particularly in the 'permission slip' reflection which showed genuine emotional depth. Missed opportunities to deepen attunement when the client expressed feeling 'flat' or when exploring the father's blood pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear and logical progression from understanding coffee's role through deeper meaning to a concrete change idea, without rigidity. Could benefit from tighter consolidation of the 'permission slip' insight back to the target behavior and an explicit session summary.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites the client's input and frames the conversation as shared exploration using language like 'I'm curious' and 'what do you think.' Could be strengthened by more explicit process-checking and consistently building on the client's ideas rather than occasionally steering with leading questions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently and explicitly supports autonomy throughout, using phrases that remove pressure, affirm self-direction, and emphasize self-generated motivation. Never resorts to advice-giving or subtle persuasion; this is the session's standout strength.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes the client's own motivations, particularly through the 'permission slip' exploration and non-threatening questions about the father's blood pressure that lead to spontaneous change talk. Could be more consistent in drawing out all DARN-CAT dimensions and using open questions rather than reflective statements at key moments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and clear communication that expertise resides with the client. Some leading questions subtly reduce the sense of equal partnership, and the session could benefit from more explicit co-creation of direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively validates and builds on the client's self-generated change ideas, reinforcing agency and progress. Could more explicitly elicit confidence statements or explore readiness to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well without argument or confrontation, instead exploring the underlying experience and gently introducing discrepancy. Could more actively use double-sided reflections to hold both sides and further soften the 'later' rationale.
