# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Unconditional positive regard is consistent throughout, with no trace of judgment or disapproval. The psychiatrist validates Luka's honesty, normalizes his struggle, and explicitly removes pressure, creating genuine psychological safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Empathic responses are deeply nuanced, tracking the emotional complexity of Luka's experience rather than offering surface-level paraphrases. Key reflections capture multi-layered feelings, such as the cost of progress and the difficulty of dropping the 'I was drunk' explanation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a coherent thread from progress through to self-insight without drift. However, it is exploratory and insight-focused rather than strategically directed toward a specific behavioral goal or concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is structured as a genuine collaboration with equal power dynamics, using inclusive language and mirroring the client's own metaphors. The psychiatrist contributes understanding and reflection without imposing solutions or expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is consistently honored through explicit permission-giving, returning questions to the client, and never using directive language. The psychiatrist treats Luka as the author of his own change process throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist skillfully draws out Luka's own motivations and insights through open, non-leading questions. Evocation could be slightly stronger by extending toward future-oriented change talk or confidence-building for specific actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The client is consistently positioned as the expert on his own experience, with questions that convey the answers reside within Luka. The session is shaped entirely by his contributions rather than a predetermined agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is consistently evoked and amplified, particularly around the distinction between the 'version of me' he doesn't like living in. The session falls slightly short in eliciting confidence language or future-oriented commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A standout element — the psychiatrist never pushes for abstinence or frames partial change as insufficient, instead validating the walk as protective. Sustain talk is gently reframed without argument, allowing Luka to find his own distinction between anger and expression.
