# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently expresses warm, respectful, nonjudgmental positive regard without criticism or pressure. Minor formulaic affirmations do not undermine the overall accepting tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist accurately reflects Samuel’s ambivalence, worry, and fear while maintaining perspective-taking. Empathy could deepen further into the emotional meaning of his struggle before moving to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays consistently focused on the target behavior and progressively narrows the conversation toward a concrete plan. There is no meaningful drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist behaves as an equal partner, co-constructing the conversation around Samuel’s ideas. The plan is client-generated rather than prescribed by the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reduces pressure and supports Samuel’s freedom to choose manageable steps. Autonomy support could be strengthened by more explicitly reinforcing that Samuel is in charge of how much and when to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively draws out Samuel’s own strategies, motivations, and hope. Deeper evocation of values, confidence, identity, and commitment would strengthen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently invites Samuel’s perspective and treats his expertise as central to the process. Suggestions are framed as invitations rather than directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits clear change talk and reinforces Samuel’s goals and confidence statements. The session would be stronger with more systematic use of DARN-C language and confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged skillfully without arguing or amplifying reasons against change. More explicit double-sided reflections would better turn ambivalence into change talk.
