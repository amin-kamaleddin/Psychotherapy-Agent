# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently conveys respect and nonjudgment while accepting Marisol’s autonomy. However, the acceptance is competent but somewhat formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Marisol’s perspective and maintain perspective-taking. The main limitation is that reflections remain mostly surface-level and somewhat repetitive, with limited deeper exploration of underlying concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear, consistent focus on safer oral health practices related to the lip piercing. Direction is clinically appropriate, realistic, and steadily guides toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites Marisol’s input and builds a plan from her own stated preferences. Collaboration is strong but somewhat structured and predictable, with room for more explicit equal-partner language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy support is a major strength, with repeated emphasis on Marisol’s choice, ownership, and lack of coercion. The psychiatrist avoids pressure and supports her perception of control over the behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits motivation from within Marisol through open, client-centered questions. Evocation remains somewhat shallow and would benefit from deeper exploration of values, confidence, or consequences of inaction.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a collaborative stance and allows Marisol to shape the plan. Partnership is effective, but it is not yet fully explicit in framing Marisol as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Marisol produces useful change talk in response to open, client-directed questions. The psychiatrist could strengthen the session by affirming, amplifying, or reflecting back her change statements more consistently.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoids arguing with resistance and helps reduce defensiveness. However, softening sustain talk is more passive than strategic and could include more double-sided reflections or reframing.
