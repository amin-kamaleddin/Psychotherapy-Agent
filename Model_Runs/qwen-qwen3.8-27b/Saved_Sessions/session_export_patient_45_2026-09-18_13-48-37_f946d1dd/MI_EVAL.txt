# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validating both sides of Erik's experience without judgment or labeling. Affirms his autonomy and self-knowledge repeatedly with warmth that feels genuine and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections capture the emotional and experiential core of Erik's statements rather than offering generic paraphrases. Notable use of metaphors and specific phrase-attention shows deep listening and accurate attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk emerges naturally from Erik's own reasoning, scaffolded through evocative questions and reflections. The psychiatrist notices, names, and reinforces change talk without being pushy or premature.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation is genuinely co-created, following Erik's leads and building on his specific language and phrases. The session feels like a conversation between equals rather than a clinical interview.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly states the role is not to tell Erik what to do, and consistently attributes all change ideas back to him. Respects his boundaries and honors conditional commitments without trying to make them more definitive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Masterfully elicits Erik's own motivations and reasons for change through evocative questions rather than providing information or advice. Every reason Erik expresses comes from within, which is the essence of MI evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions Erik as the expert on his own life and maintains an equal power dynamic throughout. Uses collaborative language and shares observations as invitations rather than declarations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully draws out Erik's change talk through gentle attention to pauses and specific phrases, allowing it to emerge naturally. Strong commitment language ("I can do two weeks") is reached through Erik's own reasoning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Never argues against or minimizes Erik's sustain talk; instead validates it as legitimate and uses it as a bridge to explore what change could look like that fits his identity. Sustain talk is honored while being gently expanded rather than confronted.
