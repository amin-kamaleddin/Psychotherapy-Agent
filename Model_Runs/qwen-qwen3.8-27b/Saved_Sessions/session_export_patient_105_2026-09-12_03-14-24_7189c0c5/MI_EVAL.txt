# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist is consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental, validating Naomi’s discomfort and ambivalence. Some affirmations are generic and could more explicitly normalize long-standing anxiety or Naomi’s courage.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor demonstrates good reflective listening by accurately capturing Naomi’s physical sensations and functional distress. Deeper reflections on shame, fear of disappointment, and identity as a photographer would improve the score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on Naomi’s social anxiety in photography work and follows a coherent path from symptoms to impact and action. The main limitation is that plan-building begins before the client’s motivation is fully consolidated.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist uses collaborative language and invites Naomi to choose among options, and her selections do influence the session. However, most options, scripts, and plan details are generated by the counselor rather than fully co-created with Naomi.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The counselor regularly supports choice with low-pressure invitations, such as “I’ll follow your lead” and multiple options. Later prescriptive instructions around the micro-plan slightly reduce the consistency of autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session includes some effective early questions that evoke concern and desired change. The score is limited by a quick shift into structured options, with fewer importance, confidence, or future-oriented evocation questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi’s preferences shape parts of the session, but the psychiatrist frequently acts as the primary option-generator and plan-maker. More client-led idea generation would make the partnership more fully equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Naomi offers meaningful change talk about wanting to keep shots, avoid failing clients, and value her photography. The counselor does not consistently expand, summarize, or amplify this client-generated motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The counselor responds to sustain talk without judgment and gently reframes concerns about failure and added rules. The score is limited by a lack of more advanced techniques, such as double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance.
