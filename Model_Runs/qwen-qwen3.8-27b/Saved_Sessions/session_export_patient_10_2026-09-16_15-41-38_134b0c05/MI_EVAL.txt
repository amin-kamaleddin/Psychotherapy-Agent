# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist maintained a respectful, validating, and nonjudgmental tone throughout the session. It could be stronger by more explicitly affirming the client's self-worth during moments of guilt or self-criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening captured the client's conflict, hope, and logistical concerns. Some reflections were interpretive and did not fully explore the deeper fear of rejection or shame before the session ended.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained tightly focused on the employment and childcare barrier. Each response purposefully advanced the conversation toward a concrete, manageable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invited the client to co-construct the plan and built directly on her ideas. More explicit 'we' language or joint framing could strengthen the equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open-ended questions and small-step planning supported the client's choices and pace. The interaction could be stronger by explicitly reinforcing the client's right to choose, pause, or not make the call.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited the client's own solutions, wording, and confidence rather than prescribing advice. It could be stronger by evoking deeper values, identity, or long-term meaning tied to employment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The client substantially led and shaped the session's content and plan. The psychiatrist could further strengthen partnership by more explicitly acknowledging the client as the expert on her life and options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and affirmations effectively elicited the client's own change statements and action-oriented thinking. More deliberate amplification of ability, desire, and hope language would strengthen this criterion.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers were reframed without arguing or minimizing, reducing all-or-nothing thinking. More explicit double-sided reflections could more directly resolve the client's ambivalence.
