### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated nonjudgmental, affirming regard and framed Amira's efforts as learning rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist showed strong perspective-taking and accurate reflections, though a few responses were more generic or enthusiastic than deeply empathic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on building a realistic activity routine and progressed steadily toward concrete, actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation felt collaborative, with the psychiatrist inviting Amira to shape the plan and treating her as an equal partner in problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Amira's choice and self-direction were consistently supported, with minimal directives and emphasis on what feels manageable for her.

### Evocation

**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently drew out Amira's own motivation, values, and self-beliefs rather than supplying external reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently positioned Amira as the expert on her own life and invited her perspective to guide the session.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
