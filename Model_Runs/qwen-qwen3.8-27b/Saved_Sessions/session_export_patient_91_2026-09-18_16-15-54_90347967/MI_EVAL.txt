### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Session flow is determined entirely by Marcus's contributions with no expert-client hierarchy. The psychiatrist builds on his input rather than steering toward a predetermined agenda, modeling genuine curiosity.

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Consistently uses choice-based language and never tells Marcus what to do, explicitly honoring his timeline and right to pace himself. Treats Marcus as the ultimate authority on his own change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Every piece of motivation comes from Marcus's own mouth through carefully crafted open questions. The psychiatrist never provides external reasons, uses fear appeals, or tells him what he should do.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Deeply collaborative stance with consistently equal power dynamic—the psychiatrist follows Marcus's lead on every topic. Positions self as facilitator who invites confirmation rather than directing the process.
