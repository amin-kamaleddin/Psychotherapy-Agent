# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys a respectful, nonjudgmental, and supportive stance while reducing shame. Acceptance is generally warm but somewhat formulaic and does not deeply affirm Sofia’s identity, fears, or grief.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist reflects some emotional burden accurately, especially around worry, guilt, and pressure. However, the reflections remain surface-level and do not fully explore the deeper meaning behind her fear of losing her life and social identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on alcohol use during pregnancy and weekend social situations. The direction is agenda-driven toward practical coping rather than strategically building motivation and resolving ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some collaborative elements, such as inviting role-play and asking which option feels doable. Overall, the interaction is counselor-led, with the psychiatrist supplying options and scripts rather than fully co-creating the direction with Sofia.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist regularly supports Sofia’s choice and avoids coercive language. The frequent provision of scripts and practical tips can subtly reduce autonomy by positioning the counselor as the problem-solver.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist tends to tell rather than evoke, providing psychoeducation, strategies, and scripts. There is limited use of open-ended questions to draw out Sofia’s own motivations, values, reasons for change, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist invites input and asks for permission, supporting a collaborative stance. However, the session quickly becomes expert-driven, with the client choosing from the counselor’s menu rather than being treated as the expert on her own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The psychiatrist makes only weak efforts to cultivate change talk and misses opportunities to expand on Sofia’s own reasons for change. The session moves quickly into advice and practical options instead of consolidating motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The psychiatrist does not reinforce sustain talk and makes some mild efforts to separate friendship from drinking. However, MI-consistent softening strategies are not consistently used, and some suggestions may inadvertently reinforce avoidance or dishonesty.
