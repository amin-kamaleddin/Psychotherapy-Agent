# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, normalizing Tyler's resistance and validating even minor reasons for showing up. Falls short of 5 because the acceptance pattern is somewhat predictable and lacks a moment of deeper, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening with one excellent deep reflection capturing the emotional core of Tyler's social pressure experience. One moment of projecting meaning (reliability interpretation) that required correction, and a missed opportunity to more deeply reflect the pain of feeling trapped.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Logical progression from why he's here through peer dynamics to what would make it different, with the baseball detail skillfully used as a bridge. Lacks a clear resolution or directional closure, and the practice detail could have been more explicitly tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative throughout—shares thinking process, invites correction, builds on Tyler's own language and metaphors. The session feels like two people thinking together rather than a clinician assessing a patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exemplary autonomy support; never tells Tyler what to do, frames all suggestions as curiosities, and explicitly removes pressure to perform motivation. Consistently reinforces Tyler's own agency as the author of his choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong open-ended questions that consistently draw out Tyler's own observations, feelings, and insights rather than providing information. Could have been more systematic in targeting DARN categories and evoking future-oriented or confidence-building change talk, particularly in the closing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exceptional partnership with a clear collaborative frame set from the opening; consistently invites Tyler's perspective and adapts in real-time based on his feedback. Never adopts an expert-driven stance, and Tyler's contributions substantially shape the session's trajectory.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and amplifies change talk across desire, ability, and reasons. Could have more systematically developed DARN categories, and the session ends back in sustain talk without a final evocative push toward change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully rolls with resistance without arguing or contradicting, with a particularly strong exploration of 'in or you're not' that opens a door without dismissing. The final sustain talk is acknowledged empathically but not fully redirected toward change talk.
