# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's sarcasm and resistance, honoring preferences and avoiding judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate reflections of practical concerns and ambivalence, but could probe deeper into underlying emotions and offer more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on nightly controller use for two weeks with targeted questions and concrete planning, avoiding tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in true partnership by offering options, asking the patient to choose strategies, and honoring constraints and preferences in co-created decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy through choices, permission-seeking, and reinforcement of the patient's ownership of the plan, including acceptable skips and resumptions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out motivations, confidence, and links to meaningful outcomes, though could probe deeper into intrinsic values and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as equal problem-solving with genuine power-sharing; the clinician consistently invites input and defers to the patient's expertise about what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and strengthens change talk via scaling questions and concrete plans, but could more actively amplify spontaneous commitment and tie changes to long-term values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully normalizes and avoids amplifying sustain talk, yet could use more double-sided reflections to further soften resistance and draw out ambivalence.
