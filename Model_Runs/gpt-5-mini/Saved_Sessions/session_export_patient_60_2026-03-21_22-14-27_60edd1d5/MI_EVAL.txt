# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician uses respectful, nonjudgmental language and normalizes Jason's perspective, avoiding moralizing; could increase explicit unconditional acceptance and more individualized affirmations to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures defensiveness and desire to avoid blame; could deepen emotional exploration with more complex reflections of unspoken feelings and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, consistent focus on the conduct meeting and reducing consequences, with early agenda-setting and interventions tightly tied to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is joint problem-solving with role-play and script adjustments based on Jason's input, though clinician-supplied scripts sometimes shift power and more client-generated wording would strengthen balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offers choices, invites tweaks, and reinforces that Jason decides how to present himself and what actions to take, supporting client control throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to elicit motivation (impact on school/goals) but session largely provides language and options rather than drawing out Jason's values, reasons, hopes, or confidence for broader change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician invites collaboration, solicits preferences, and role-plays with Jason, but occasionally provides expert-crafted scripts before eliciting client-generated language which could be improved.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change-relevant statements and concrete steps, but tends to move quickly to scripts and solutions rather than systematically eliciting, amplifying, and exploring Jason's own reasons, confidence, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician rolls with minimizations and avoids confrontation, preventing escalation, but rarely uses double-sided reflections or reframes sustain talk to highlight discrepancy and more active redirection is needed.
