# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Jordan's struggle, validating small steps and maintaining a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with timely reflections and paraphrases that echoed Jordan's language and deepened rapport and disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on shoplifting and recidivism, using questions and interventions tightly linked to concrete behavioral strategies and a specific action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly co-constructed—using inclusive language, asking preferences, role-playing, and jointly creating messages and plans rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Jordan's sense of choice by offering multiple options, checking permission before suggestions, and inviting him to select what felt realistic.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used open questions and scaling to draw out motivations and practical solutions, but at times moved into offering many clinician-generated options instead of eliciting deeper client-generated values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear sharing of power by inviting Jordan to choose which options to explore, offering drafts to be tweaked, and checking what felt doable to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change language using exploratory questions, scaling, and positive feedback, which strengthened Jordan's expressed intentions and concrete steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward planning, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections and lingering on ambivalence to more strategically defuse sustain language.
