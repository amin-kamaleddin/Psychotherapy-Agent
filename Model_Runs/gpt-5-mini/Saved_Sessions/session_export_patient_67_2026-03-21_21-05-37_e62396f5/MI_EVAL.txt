# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding criticism or labels.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that captured ambivalence and drinking patterns, but missed some opportunities to probe deeper emotions and underlying meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use with concrete, practical next steps (tracking, reminders, check-in) while remaining nondirective.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was co-constructed through shared problem-solving and joint planning (templates, reminder times, backups), demonstrating strong partnership rather than an expert lecture stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Robustly supported patient choice by offering options, asking preferences, acknowledging the right to decline, and requesting permission for follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation and confidence ratings that led to a tracking commitment, but did not consistently draw out deeper personal values or long-term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively treated the patient as the expert, offering choices and soliciting preferred wording/times, and co-creating the plan based on the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced initial change talk with evocative questions and low-barrier steps, but could do more to amplify and deepen reasons and values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and skillfully redirected sustain talk toward a neutral tracking plan, though more double-sided reflections and strategic summaries could better highlight discrepancy.
