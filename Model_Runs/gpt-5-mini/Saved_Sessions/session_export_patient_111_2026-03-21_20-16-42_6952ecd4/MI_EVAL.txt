# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, nonjudgmental regard from the start; consistently validates resistance and care without blaming, normalizing ambivalence and offering options.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries convey perspective-taking and make Richard feel heard, but the clinician missed opportunities for deeper emotional naming and more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on smoking and relationship conflict with concrete, actionable steps, though direction moved quickly into planning rather than further exploration of values and motivation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction reads as cooperative and tailored to the patient’s style, inviting input and tweaks, but offering scripts and specific solutions sometimes tilted the exchange toward clinician-led guidance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly honors Richard’s choice throughout, offers options instead of ultimatums, and repeatedly checks preferences, strongly reinforcing patient autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk tied to the relationship and practical compromises, but does little to draw out Richard’s internal motivations, strengths, or to amplify and deepen change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructs a plan and asks which options feel doable, demonstrating good partnership, though providing ready-made scripts reduced opportunities for more client-generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Succeeds in surfacing relational reasons and pragmatic commitments, but moves quickly into solution planning instead of eliciting and strengthening desire, ability, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes toward workable compromises, acknowledging sustain talk, but could use more double-sided reflections and strategic softening to further diffuse resistance.
