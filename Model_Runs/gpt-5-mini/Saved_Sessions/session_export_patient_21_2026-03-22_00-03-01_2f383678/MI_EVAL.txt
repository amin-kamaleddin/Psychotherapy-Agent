# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing statements and supportive affirmations, maintaining a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening is used repeatedly, paraphrasing both content and emotion and acknowledging ambivalence to convey clear perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remains tightly focused on increasing brief physical activity and moves logically from goal to concrete implementation intentions without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction models an equal partnership: questions invite input, solutions originate from the patient, and the clinician tailors suggestions to her responses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly emphasizes choice and control, offering options and framing the plan as kind and self-compassionate rather than prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicits the patient's own ideas and plans around implementation, but could more deeply draw out intrinsic motivations, values, and confidence/importance levels.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear collaborative stance is maintained with frequent solicitation of the patient's preferences and co-construction of the plan using her suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits and reinforces specific, actionable change talk leading to a staged plan, though could better amplify pros of change and probe commitment strength.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing and responds empathically to barriers, shifting to manageable alternatives, but missed chances to use double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of sustain talk's function.
