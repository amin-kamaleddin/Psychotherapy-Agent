# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance with validating openings and frequent affirmations, honoring Lars' choices and avoiding criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used solid reflective listening and acknowledged emotions (nerves, embarrassment, ambivalence), but missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections exploring shame, guilt, and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing reoffending and risky drinking by steering to a single concrete next step (calling polytech), creating a script, backup plan, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was a joint enterprise with co-created plans, inclusive language, negotiated follow-up, and decisions shaped by Lars rather than imposed by the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Lars' sense of choice by offering options, respecting his embarrassment and preferences, and framing offers (practice, check-ins) as supports rather than prescriptions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew motivation out by asking evocative questions and linking actions to values (Donna, kids), but could have probed more deeply for vivid reasons and stronger, autonomous commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively partnered with Lars by inviting his input at each decision point, asking permission to practice, and sharing control of the plan, reflecting strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and shaped change talk and turned goals into concrete steps, yet could have more consistently amplified spontaneous change talk and pushed for firmer commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain-talk themes and avoided confrontation but tended to pivot quickly to action without using double-sided reflections or explicitly exploring the appeal of the old life.
