# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed respect, validation, and nonjudgmental support, normalizing Naomi’s experience and tailoring responses to her specific symptoms and work impact.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking deepened rapport and mirrored Naomi’s ambivalence, summarizing both progress and struggle in a timely way.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on social anxiety in shoots with targeted questions and concrete next-step planning (breathing, grounding, one-line script) that kept momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician worked as an equal partner, inviting Naomi to choose and tweak strategies, asking permission, and offering support rather than directives throughout the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were offered and respected, permission was sought before suggestions, and the clinician emphasized Naomi’s control and ability to try or decline steps without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited emerging motivation and affirmed commitments (e.g., rehearsing the script) but could probe more into personal values, reasons for change, and use scaling to amplify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A strong collaborative stance was evident as Naomi co-designed the script and practice plan, with the clinician supporting adjustments and offering role-play only when requested.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good elicitation and reinforcement of tentative change talk (choosing and rehearsing a script), but missed opportunities to deepen 'why' for change and assess confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician validated resistance and avoided confrontation, yet used few double-sided reflections or explicit pivots that would more deliberately reframe sustain language toward change.
