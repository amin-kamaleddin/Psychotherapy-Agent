# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician shows consistent respect and nonjudgmental tone, validates constraints and priorities, but acceptance feels somewhat transactional and lacked an explicit acknowledgment/apology for the long wait to deepen rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking capture ambivalence and practical concerns, though reflections are sometimes repetitive and the clinician rarely probes deeper emotions or underlying values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on weight-related behaviors with concrete, time-limited experiments and measurable success criteria; the session stays on task and builds actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance—patient choices are solicited at every decision point and the plan is co-constructed from his suggestions rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong support for autonomy with voluntary language and repeated invitations for the patient to choose options, reinforcing ownership of the plan without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits the patient's reasons and concrete plans and uncovers intrinsic motivators, but could probe deeper into personal values, past successes, and confidence strength.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently treats the patient as the expert on his life, asking for input and building the plan from his preferences, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and turns tentative commitment into a concrete test, but missed chances to use scaling/confidence questions or elicit past successes to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and skillfully redirects toward workable solutions using double-sided reflections, yet could more explicitly validate sustain talk and explore when barriers were previously managed.
