# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance with validating remarks and repeated safety checks throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used timely, accurate reflections and perspective-taking, naming emotions and normalizing the burden to demonstrate strong empathic understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on relieving parenting stress and securing partner support but did not systematically address the substance-use targets (no pros/cons, readiness, or linkage to values).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a partnership by soliciting Thomas's priorities, offering options, and co-developing exact phrasing and a mini-plan rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Thomas's right to choose, offered optional plans, checked consent for practice, and respected refusals to preserve patient autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation around partner support and self-care but failed to evoke intrinsic reasons or concerns specifically about smoking and drinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach with co-creation of scripts, multiple options, and negotiation of action steps that shared power with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened change talk about getting help and self-care, but only minimally explored reasons, values, or confidence for changing tobacco/alcohol use.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and did not reinforce minimizations, but missed opportunities to soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections, strategic reframing, or eliciting ambivalence.
