# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with normalizing statements and repeated affirmations, maintaining a warm, respectful tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking by summarizing barriers and emotions, but reflections were mostly surface-level and missed opportunities to explore deeper feelings and meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on re-establishing exercise, with each intervention tied to that target and the session moving logically from exploration to planning to implementation intent.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative co-creation of specific implementation steps, inviting the client's input at every stage and following the client's lead to shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported client choice by offering options, asking preferences, and emphasizing nonjudgmental re-attempts, reinforcing the client's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited relevant material and prompted change talk and concrete plans, but could have drawn out deeper motives, values, and used scaling questions to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a shared, client-centered approach by asking permission, offering choices, and co-constructing the plan, demonstrating strong power-sharing and respect for the client's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and implementation intentions with concrete steps, though could have amplified the client's own motivations and probed importance/confidence to deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and barriers nonjudgmentally while offering fallback plans, but at times moved quickly to specific solutions instead of using reflective strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to gently shift sustain talk.
