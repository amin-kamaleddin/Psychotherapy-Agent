# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance with frequent affirmations and normalization of Tyler's efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with validations and scaling, but missed some deeper emotional reflections and chances to normalize ambivalence more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on risky alcohol use and logically guided the session from exploration to concrete harm‑reduction planning and practice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as a mutual exploration with co‑construction of the plan, soliciting Tyler's reactions and adjusting strategies based on his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering options, asking which felt most doable, and letting Tyler choose and shape the specific plan and practice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and used scaling to produce self‑motivated reasons to change, though could have drawn out more of Tyler's own solutions earlier.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly asked permission, invited Tyler to pick strategies, and collaborated on wording and practice, demonstrating clear power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk through questions and planning, but sometimes moved quickly to offering many options rather than first inviting Tyler's own ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered harm‑reduction that honored Tyler's social goals; could use more double‑sided reflections to explicitly link both sides of ambivalence.
