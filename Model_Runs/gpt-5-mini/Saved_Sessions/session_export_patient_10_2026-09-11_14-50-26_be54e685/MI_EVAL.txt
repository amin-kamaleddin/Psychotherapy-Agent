# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes constraints, naming strengths and barriers without blame. Language is warm and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good reflective listening and paraphrases specifics, showing attention to detail and ambivalence. Could deepen feeling‑based reflections to more explicitly acknowledge disappointment and awkwardness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on securing interviews and resolving logistical barriers, moving logically from options to a concrete action plan. Guidance is steady and appropriately goal‑directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is co‑constructed with the patient; clinician solicits ideas and offers help only after patient preference is clear. Patient drives choices and materials are created together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and letting the patient select wording and timing. Avoids pressure and reinforces ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation and reasoning from the patient rather than supplying arguments, eliciting confidence, preferences, and concrete implementation intentions. Builds on patient‑expressed change talk to craft steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the patient's expertise, repeatedly asking which parts to help with. Power‑sharing is evident in choices and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, moving from desire/ability to specific first steps and commitments. Repeated reflections and summaries strengthen the patient's stated intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers without judgment and redirects to problem‑solving, offering brainstorming and practical supports. Could more often use double‑sided or minimizing reflections to further reduce sustain talk.
