# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, using supportive language and normalizing slips to create a safe atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used reflective listening and perspective-taking to capture ambivalence and surprise, but reflections were mostly content-level and missed opportunities to name deeper affect (e.g., defensiveness or frustration).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol in relation to running, guiding the conversation to a specific, measurable two‑week experiment and follow‑up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited patient input at each step, adjusted materials to his preferences, and co‑constructed the monitoring and reminder plan, demonstrating genuine reciprocal teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by asking permission, offering options rather than directives, and tailoring suggestions to the patient's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited the patient's reasons and motivation (linking goals, asking how change would be noticed, using a confidence ruler), but sometimes moved toward advising rather than deepening intrinsic motivation or amplifying change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision‑making and power‑sharing by presenting options, asking which experiment felt easiest, and inviting the patient to choose reminders and review timing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk via experiments and confidence scaling, but could more often amplify/summarize change statements and explicitly elicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected toward change but relied on neutral moves rather than strategically softening sustain talk (e.g., double‑sided reflections or rolling with resistance).
