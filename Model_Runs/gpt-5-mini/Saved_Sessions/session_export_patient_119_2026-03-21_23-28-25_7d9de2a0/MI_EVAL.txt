# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental, respectful language; normalizes difficulty and avoids blame to create a safe stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking demonstrating active listening, but reflections are occasionally repetitive and could deepen by reflecting emotional nuance and meaning of minimization.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on medication adherence with clear, actionable goals and repeated refocusing without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducts a collaborative problem‑solving exchange by inviting the patient's ideas, validating them, and building plans together rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces choice by offering multiple options and explicit permission to try a plan without pressure, supporting patient ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out intrinsic motivation and concrete strategies but could strengthen evocation by exploring deeper values and eliciting confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treats the patient as the expert, repeatedly inviting choice and co‑creating decisions, which fosters shared decision‑making and power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and amplifies change talk with small, realistic plans and success indicators, though the clinician could more deliberately stack and summarize change language and probe confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges minimizations and redirects toward planning, avoiding argument; could further soften sustain talk using double‑sided reflections or gentle reframes to explore tradeoffs.
