### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist conveys consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support through warm openings, validations, and affirmations, normalizing Matteo's context throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries capture behavior and ambivalence, though deeper probing of emotional nuance and more complex reflections could further surface underlying values and feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol use and moves efficiently toward concrete goals and steps, including strategy selection, a start date, confidence rating, and supports to maintain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician co-constructs the plan with Matteo, inviting his input on strategies, supports, and wording, and offers suggestions rather than directives throughout the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supports choice and control by asking permission, offering multiple options, checking preferences, and avoiding pressuring language while honoring Matteo's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits Matteo's own reasons and motivations for change (protecting grades/work, better mornings) through targeted questions and confidence scaling, though prompting longer-term personally meaningful goals could deepen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is clearly collaborative with power-sharing—options are offered, decisions are deferred to Matteo, and the plan is jointly developed and rehearsed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is actively elicited and reinforced via options, planning, and asking for a start date and confidence, but selectively reflecting and amplifying Matteo's own change language could strengthen and lengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing and neutrally acknowledges sustain talk while pivoting to planning, yet could further soften sustain talk using more double-sided reflections and selective reinforcement to tip the balance toward change.
