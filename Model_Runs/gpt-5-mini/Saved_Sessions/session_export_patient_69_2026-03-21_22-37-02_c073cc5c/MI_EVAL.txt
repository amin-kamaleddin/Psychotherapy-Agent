# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the client's reluctance with warm, nonjudgmental language, creating a safe stance for terse responses.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and captured practical concerns, but reflections stayed pragmatic and summary-focused rather than exploring deeper emotions or sources of irritation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior and steadily guided the session from broad issues to a concrete experiment and action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as an equal partnership—preferences were solicited at every step and the clinician integrated the client's ideas without taking an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's choice, offered options instead of directives, and validated reluctance while emphasizing the client's control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the client's own reasons for change and personal consequences, drawing out intrinsic motivators that led to clear, self-generated goals and commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the plan with the client by asking what would make participation tolerable and inviting the client to choose and tweak the specifics.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, moving the client from vague willingness to concrete, specific commitments and plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used reframing/bracketing effectively, though could more often employ selective reflections or emphasize costs of staying the same to shift ambivalence further.
