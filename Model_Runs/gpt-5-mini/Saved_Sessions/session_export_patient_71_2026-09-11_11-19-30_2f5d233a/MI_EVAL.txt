# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, validating ambivalence and normalizing choices to create a safe space for exploration.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with specific reflections of content and feeling, consistently showing understanding of Laura's experience and barriers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, focused goal-directed work by narrowing to a specific tiny habit and developing concrete implementation details (cue, timing, backup plan).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine joint planning by soliciting the patient's judgments, offering options, and co-creating the specifics so power and decisions are shared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports agency by offering choices, asking which options feel doable, and respecting the patient's decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivation by drawing on the patient's values and existing routines and moving those reasons into concrete, self-generated steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative and invites input, but at times the clinician lists options and nudges toward strategies instead of explicitly foregrounding the patient's expertise first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and specific steps, though could more strategically amplify and summarize key change statements to strengthen commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes barriers, and offers contingency plans, but could add more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence before pivoting to change talk.
