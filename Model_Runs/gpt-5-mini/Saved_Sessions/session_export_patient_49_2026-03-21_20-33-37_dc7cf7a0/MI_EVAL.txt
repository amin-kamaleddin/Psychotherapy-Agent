# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, using validating language and affirmations; no blaming or pressure throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases of ambivalence; could deepen empathy by probing emotional nuance and using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking throughout and offered specific, behavior-focused options and a time-bound tiny plan while respecting autonomy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—co‑created the tiny plan, solicited Hana’s preferences, and revised plans based on her input, framing the work as joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and framing change as an experiment rather than a mandate.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Hana’s motivations, values, and learning goals and used them in plan-building; could more actively amplify change language into a clear commitment and elicit past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered client expertise and power-sharing by offering choices, asking which option Hana preferred, and tailoring suggestions to her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with importance/confidence questions and a doable experiment, but missed some opportunities to more frequently reflect and consolidate a commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and neutrally reframed sustain-talk (stress relief, social smoking) without arguing; could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly link sustain and change talk.
