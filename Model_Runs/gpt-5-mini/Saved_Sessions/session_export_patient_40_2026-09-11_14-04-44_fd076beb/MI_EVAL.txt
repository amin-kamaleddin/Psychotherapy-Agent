# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys clear unconditional positive regard and repeatedly normalizes ambivalence, avoiding judgment and validating the client's experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures content and some feeling, but misses opportunities to deepen exploration of shame and the emotional weight of relapse.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior and purposefully steers the session toward concrete implementation and a measurable goal, avoiding drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acts as an equal partner and co-planner, offering options and repeatedly inviting the client to choose so decisions are co-constructed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering choices, respecting the client's preferences, and eliciting the client's own plan without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes practical motivations and commitment language for the plan, but could more explicitly draw out deeper intrinsic motives and confidence (e.g., scaling or values exploration).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-creates the plan with the client, defers to the client's judgment about feasibility and timing, and treats the client as the expert on their life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, secures specific commitments and a measurable 5/7 goal, and repeatedly reflects and summarizes the client's change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and effectively redirects toward change-oriented planning, though could more strategically use double-sided reflections or explicitly summarize ambivalence before pivoting.
