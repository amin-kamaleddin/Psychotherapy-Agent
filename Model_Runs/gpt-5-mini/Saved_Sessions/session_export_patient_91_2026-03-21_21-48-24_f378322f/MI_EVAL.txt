# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Marcus’s experience, validating concerns without judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, naming specific worries and ambivalence, but could have deepened emotional reflections to better capture anxiety and underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on blood‑sugar self‑monitoring with a concrete, behaviorally specific plan (time, cues, supplies, contingencies, day‑7 check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—options and suggestions were offered while Marcus selected what felt doable, and his tweaks were incorporated into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by asking permission, offering choices rather than directives, and framing the plan as Marcus’s decision to increase ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivators and used scaling, but did not sufficiently probe deeper intrinsic values or follow up on the 5/10 confidence to amplify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reflected a strong partnership orientation with frequent invitations for Marcus’s input and co‑creation of the plan, positioning him as the expert on what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and concrete steps, but missed chances to amplify Marcus’s own change language and to elicit longer‑term reasons or stronger commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk non‑judgmentally by normalizing hesitancy and reframing slips as acceptable; more use of early double‑sided reflections could further reduce sustain talk.
