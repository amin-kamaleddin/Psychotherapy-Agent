# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing concerns and supporting client choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and validation of the client's perspective, though reflections are mainly content-level and could probe emotional nuance (shame, fear) more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on safer meth use and harm reduction, moving the conversation toward concrete steps (clinic date, harm-reduction resources) without getting sidetracked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructs the plan with the client by asking preferences, tailoring options, and offering to carry out requested tasks, framing the work as joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice with repeated statements emphasizing autonomy and by offering options rather than pressuring for a specific outcome.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation with evocative questions and elicits spontaneous change talk, but could probe deeper into values, past successes, and confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership by inviting input, sharing responsibility for next steps, and deferring to the client's preferences throughout planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change talk through importance/step questions and commitment to specific actions, yet could amplify and summarize change language and elicit stronger commitment/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and acknowledges ambivalence, but misses strategic opportunities for double-sided reflections, reframing sustain statements, and more actively pivoting to change talk.
