# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized struggles, and affirmed Daniela’s efforts and resilience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections captured both logistical strain and emotional experience, making Daniela feel understood and keeping her engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on securing reliable childcare with concrete, actionable next steps (neighbor vs Head Start, drafting/sending a text).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was framed as joint problem-solving with frequent solicitation of Daniela’s preferences and co-creation of the plan (text drafting, role-play, backup options).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly emphasized choice and Daniela’s control by offering options and asking which phrasing or step felt most realistic to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited Daniela’s reasons for change and benefits of childcare, but could more systematically deepen motivation by exploring confidence and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful of Daniela’s expertise with choices and invitations, though at times the clinician offered scripted options rather than fully eliciting her language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited and amplified change talk about childcare and its downstream benefits and encouraged concrete action, but could more often summarize and shape explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Barriers and sustain talk were acknowledged but not consistently explored or reframed with double-sided reflections, so ambivalence was sometimes bypassed in favor of problem-solving.
