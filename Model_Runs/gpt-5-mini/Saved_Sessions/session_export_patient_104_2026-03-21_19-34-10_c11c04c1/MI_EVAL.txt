# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and multiple empathic validations throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and accurate, timely reflections explicitly name Nadine's feelings and normalize her experience, demonstrating excellent empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on feeding, sleep, maternal exhaustion, and safety with practical, actionable next steps and no off-topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative with invitations to plan and tailored suggestions, but much of the session is clinician-driven and could invite more of Nadine's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly preserves Nadine's choice by offering options, suggestive language, and multiple feasible alternatives that reinforce client agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some factual information needed for planning but relies more on offering solutions than on open, evocative questions to draw out Nadine's motivations or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally conveys that Nadine's perspective matters and offers choices, though at times the clinician provides directives or scripts without first eliciting permission or preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively supports practical planning and actionable options, but misses opportunities to more actively elicit and amplify Nadine's own reasons, confidence, and past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes ambivalence, and offers alternatives, but uses few explicit double-sided reflections to fully acknowledge both sides of ambivalence.
