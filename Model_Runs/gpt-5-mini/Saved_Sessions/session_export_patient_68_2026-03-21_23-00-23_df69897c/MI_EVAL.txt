# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, explicit unconditional positive regard throughout the session with individualized warmth and validating language that reduced blame and supported cooperation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, accurate reflections captured both content and affect, deepening understanding and maintaining an empathic tone while still moving toward practical planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained steady focus on alcohol-related risk and injury prevention, progressing logically from values/concerns to concrete, time-bound harm-reduction steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way collaboration: the clinician solicited Kevin's ideas, refined suggestions with him, and helped operationalize his chosen strategy into a client-generated plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Kevin's choice and ownership, avoided pressure, and asked which options felt most doable, strengthening intrinsic motivation and reducing reactance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Kevin's reasons and concrete commitments, but could have probed deeper into values, past successes, and confidence to further strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Kevin as the expert, offered options rather than directives, and refined the plan based on his input, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited specific change talk and a concrete plan (alternate drinks, timer, text Miguel), though the clinician could have amplified and reinforced those motivational statements more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected to risk-reduction without arguing, but relied on neutral-to-positive redirection rather than more artful double-sided reflections to loosen sustain talk.
