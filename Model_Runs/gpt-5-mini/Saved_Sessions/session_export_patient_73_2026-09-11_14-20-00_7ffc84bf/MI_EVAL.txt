# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard, validates Linda's efforts, and avoids judgment or blame, creating a warm, nonshaming tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening capture both content and emotion, with reflections that resonate with the patient and advance the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on smoking reduction/cessation and moves logically from exploration to concrete action planning without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician and patient co-construct the plan, routinely checking preferences and deferring to Linda's lived experience to shape practical steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice is emphasized throughout by offering options, asking which feels most doable, and honoring Linda's decisions rather than prescribing a single course.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and solutions are consistently drawn from the patient—her reasons, plans, and specific strategies are elicited and strengthened rather than supplied by the clinician.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction models an equal team approach with shared problem-solving and language that positions the clinician alongside the patient in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and focused prompts effectively elicit and deepen change talk, converting general desire into concrete preparatory language and specific plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and redirected toward solutions, but the clinician misses some opportunities for double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of smoking's functions to better soften resistance.
