# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates unconditional positive regard and a consistently nonjudgmental tone—validates successes and normalizes concerns (e.g., congratulating smoking cessation) without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and pragmatic reflections (links drinking to family/work values) but uses mostly content-focused reflections and could deepen emotional/ambivalent material with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on alcohol harm-reduction and moves the session from assessment to a concrete, actionable plan with specific strategies and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative—co-constructs scripts, invites patient edits, role-plays together, and leverages the patient's expertise to shape the intervention.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking permission before proposing plans, and avoiding pressure, which reinforces patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motives (pride in quitting, desire to be a better dad) and links them to strategies, but could more deliberately amplify values and use scaling or 'why' questions to deepen intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates an equal, client-driven stance—invites collaboration, role-plays the buddy, and integrates the patient's language and preferences into plans and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk (asks what to work on, affirms realistic plans) but misses some opportunities to deepen desire/reasons/confidence with elaborative questions and scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk into practical supports and brainstorming, though could further soften sustain talk with double-sided or reflective techniques that explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
