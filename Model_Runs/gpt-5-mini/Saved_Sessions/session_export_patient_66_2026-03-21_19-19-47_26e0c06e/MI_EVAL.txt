# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, validated Kareem's experience and avoided shaming or labeling throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, grounded reflections and acknowledged context and feelings, but missed some opportunities for deeper exploration of underlying emotions and stressors.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking reduction, moved progressively to a concrete plan (mid-morning walk) and scheduled a follow-up check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative: the plan was co-created, the clinician solicited Kareem's input and tailored strategies to his preferences, supporting teamwork rather than top-down instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered multiple options, invited choice, normalized slips, and emphasized Kareem's control over decisions to reinforce ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons, preferences, and self-generated solutions that produced change talk, but could more systematically probe values, importance/confidence, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative posture and invited Kareem to choose strategies, though occasional detailed advice edged toward an expert role rather than explicitly emphasizing the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and a realistic first step, but did not consistently amplify or deepen change talk with scaling questions or exploration of personal motives.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and offered harm‑reduction alternatives, but used few strategic double-sided or amplified reflections to actively pivot sustain talk toward change.
