# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental options, affirming the patient's choices and pace throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided accurate, perspective-taking reflections about the patient's pragmatic stance, but relied more on cognitive/logistical empathy and did not deeply explore affect around the son’s concern or the fall.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on alcohol use with a clear, time-limited goal (one-week tracking) and actionable steps leading to a concrete follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan step-by-step, repeatedly checked preferences, and invited patient input at each decision point, demonstrating strong joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored autonomy by offering choices, asking what felt most doable, and accommodating privacy preferences and consent for message wording.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivation and confidence (e.g., 7/10) and encouraged commitment to a tracking experiment, but missed opportunities to draw out deeper intrinsic motives and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as the expert and shared decision-making, adapting the plan to the patient's preferences (notebook, post-it, text timing) throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited, reflected, and reinforced change talk, converting tentative statements into a specific, doable plan with concrete steps and follow-up.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and minimized reinforcement of sustain talk by offering low-threat options and normalization, though could more actively use double-sided reflections or summaries to resolve ambivalence.
