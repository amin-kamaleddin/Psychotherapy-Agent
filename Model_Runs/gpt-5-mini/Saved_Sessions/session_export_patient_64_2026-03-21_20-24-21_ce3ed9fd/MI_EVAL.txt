# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, nonjudgmental positive regard throughout the session; validates ambivalence and normalizes concerns without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries using the patient's words and scales; could deepen empathy by exploring underlying feelings and the personal meaning of protecting grandparents.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (getting the flu vaccine) and practical barriers, guiding the session toward concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership with repeated invitations for preferences and co-developed plans; offers to perform tasks contingent on the patient's permission and gives the patient real influence over next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, normalizing ambivalence, and avoiding pressure; frames recommendations as adaptable to the patient's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some of the patient's motivations (scaling, reasons for protecting grandparents) but could further draw out internal motives, feelings, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters collaboration and invites input, co-creating scripts and plans; at times supplies extended information without explicitly checking permission or fully co-shaping wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk using scaling and reflective statements and offers aligned options; could strengthen by amplifying the patient's own arguments for change and eliciting commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing, validates sustain talk, and offers alternatives while using double-sided reflections; could more actively pivot sustain talk toward change by summarizing costs of remaining the same.
