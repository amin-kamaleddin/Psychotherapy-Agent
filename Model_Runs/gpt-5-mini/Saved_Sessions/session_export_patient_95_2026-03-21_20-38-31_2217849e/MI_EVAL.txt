# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, explicitly acknowledging Maria's pain and thanking her for disclosure throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-level reflective listening and perspective-taking were used, staying with Maria's affect and validating her reasons for staying alive rather than immediately problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on immediate safety (plan, means, and concrete steps) and consistently steered the conversation toward doable safety behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—invited choices, consent, and offered to help—but at times leaned toward clinician-driven suggestions and missed some opportunities to co-create language or solutions with Maria.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported client agency by asking permission, offering options, respecting refusals, and accepting partial steps while preserving Maria's decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation (identified the part that cares, used scaling, asked for a next step), but could have probed more deeply into values, reasons, and past strengths to amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance through permission and offers to stay/help, yet sometimes shifted to providing multiple specific instructions instead of inviting Maria to generate alternatives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited brief change statements and asked scaling questions, but did not sufficiently amplify or explore reasons for living or elicit stronger desire, ability, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used reflective validation while redirecting toward safety and small steps, though more strategic double-sided reflections could further weaken sustain talk.
