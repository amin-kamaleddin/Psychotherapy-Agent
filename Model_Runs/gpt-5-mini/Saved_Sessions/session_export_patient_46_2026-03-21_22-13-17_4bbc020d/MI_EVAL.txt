# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Displayed clear unconditional positive regard with validating statements and a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, behavior-focused reflections and perspective-taking, but reflections stayed at the surface and could probe deeper emotions and underlying values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on weekend drinking and harm-reduction goals, guiding the conversation toward specific, achievable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like a joint project with co-constructed decisions, role-plays, and repeated invitations for Ethan to choose strategies that fit him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, asked permission before advising, and emphasized Ethan's right to choose, reinforcing his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Ethan's own reasons and motivations using rulers and concrete planning, building the plan from his language and preferences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative stance—solicited preferences, co-created plans, and positioned Ethan as the expert on what will work for him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly drew out and reinforced change talk with readiness/confidence rulers, concrete options, affirmations, and invitations to plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and often redirected toward change, though there were missed opportunities for more strategic double-sided reflections and explicit discrepancy work.
