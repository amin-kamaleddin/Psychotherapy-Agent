# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrated unconditional positive regard and a consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone, honoring Irina’s preferences and validating the difficulty of her situation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections showed accurate perspective-taking and captured practical pressures, but could deepen emotional attunement by naming stronger feelings (shame, exhaustion) and exploring their intensity/history.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, goal-oriented focus on reducing nightly drinking and guided the session toward a concrete, time-limited plan with specific strategies and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co-constructed: the clinician solicited Irina’s ideas, offered options, and adapted interventions to her preferences without adopting an expert stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and offered multiple options, framed suggestions as possibilities, and honored Irina’s decisions (e.g., text wording), reinforcing client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited practical change talk and concrete commitments, but could better evoke deeper, values-driven motivation and draw on past successes to boost intrinsic drive and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing evident—clinician asked permission before offering help, invited choice, and let Irina lead decisions, fostering an equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change language with scaling and action planning, though missed opportunities to amplify values-based statements and translate them into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated reasons for drinking, using ambivalence reflections, but used few double-sided reflections or strategic reframes that could further soften sustain talk.
