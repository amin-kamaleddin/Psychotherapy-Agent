# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and nonjudgmental support, honoring boundaries and using warm, validating language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that validated motives and ambivalence, though deeper probing of emotional nuance (e.g., feelings about being a mother) was sometimes missed before moving to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus on reducing evening smoking and produced a concrete, actionable plan while resisting drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine partnership by soliciting preferences, affirming the patient's ideas, and adjusting the plan based on her input and constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering options, checking permission, and leaving final decisions to the patient, reinforcing her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out motivation with readiness and confidence ratings and some elicitation of reasons, but could increase open, curiosity-driven questions to further mobilize internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction with co-constructed decisions, repeated invitations for input, and clinician responsiveness to the patient's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked and amplified change talk and concrete planning, though more strategic open questions about desire/reasons/need could deepen and extend change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and respected the patient's boundary about alcohol, but could use more double-sided reflections to gently surface and resolve ambivalence.
