# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized and affirmed Elena's feelings and parenting, and avoided judgmental language. The clinician repeatedly acknowledged the difficulty of her circumstances, creating a warm, respectful stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that matched Elena's expressions. Reflections captured both emotion (exhaustion, fear) and practical constraints, facilitating disclosure and planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing the child's smoke exposure throughout the session. Questions and reflections steered toward practical, time-limited steps (gum trial, reminders, tracking) without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by asking permission-like questions, offering options, and negotiating decisions. The plan was co-constructed and tailored to Elena's life rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported Elena's choice, emphasized that she was not being forced to quit, and invited her to select flavors, reminder times, and whether to learn techniques. The clinician respected boundaries and fostered engagement through choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Elena's own motivations and ideas and elicited a self-generated plan (gum trial), producing concrete change language. Evocation could be strengthened by systematically eliciting reasons/values, using rulers, and amplifying change statements to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Elena's input and co-created the plan, demonstrating genuine power-sharing and respect for her expertise. The clinician explicitly problem-solved with Elena about feasibility and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and operationalized concrete change talk (3–7 day gum trial, tracking, reminders) and invited realistic steps. Could have more intentionally amplified and consolidated commitment through selective reflections, summaries, and confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double-sided reflections, redirecting toward harm-reduction strategies. Missed opportunities remain to more explicitly explore discrepancy between values and behavior and to use strategic reflections to diminish sustain-talk rationales.
