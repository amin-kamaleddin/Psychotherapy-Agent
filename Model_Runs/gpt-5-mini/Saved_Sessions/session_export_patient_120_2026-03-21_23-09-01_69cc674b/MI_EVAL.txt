# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Sofia’s emotions, affirming her care and commitment without judgment. Warm, validating stance was sustained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflections and good empathic listening that helped Sofia elaborate her conflict and emotions. Could deepen exploration of the intensity/history of guilt, shame, and fear with more complex emotional reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on avoiding alcohol during pregnancy and strategically guided the conversation toward a concrete, time-bound plan for the weekend. Questions were well sequenced to narrow ambivalence into specific actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative: the clinician co-constructed the message, offered role-play, and repeatedly sought Sofia’s preferences. Sofia’s ideas shaped the plan, demonstrating genuine joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored Sofia’s choice by offering options and avoiding directives, reinforcing her ownership of decisions and the plan. Clinician supported autonomy through questions like which option 'feels most doable.'

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Sofia’s motivations (baby’s health, responsibility) and translated them into practical steps. Could strengthen evocation by eliciting deeper personal values and more direct confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative tone by inviting Sofia to choose options and co-create wording, offering to role-play and plan backups together. To reach top score, the clinician could have negotiated the agenda earlier and had Sofia more often lead summarization and next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk and helped convert desire into concrete plans (naming friends, mocktails, exit plan). Missed opportunities to ask scaling/confidence questions and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without judgment and redirected to coping strategies, which helped shift focus to protective behaviors. Could more often use double-sided reflections and deeper empathic reflection of potential loss to further soften sustain talk.
