# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating values and normalizing realistic limits without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections of Thomas's situation, but reflections were sometimes surface-level and could probe deeper emotions or fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and alcohol goals, using scaling and implementation planning to guide the session toward specific, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—invited Thomas to choose strategies, tweak language, and co-construct the plan at each step through mutual problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by asking permission, offering multiple options, respecting limits, and emphasizing client choice and ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons and resources and linked habits to values, but shifted to solution-giving relatively quickly, limiting deeper elicitation of intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveyed shared expertise and choice with the patient, asking permission before advice and repeatedly inviting Thomas's input to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language with values questions and scaling, but missed some follow-ups to deepen change talk and build stronger commitment beyond 'I'll try.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reframed sustain talk into acceptable small steps and plans, though double-sided reflections to explicitly surface ambivalence were used less consistently.
