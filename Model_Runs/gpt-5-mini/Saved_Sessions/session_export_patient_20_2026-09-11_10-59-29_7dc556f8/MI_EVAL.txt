# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and offering options without blame or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the client's language and validating both sides of his ambivalence to create safety for disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing weeknight drinking and skillfully guides the conversation from exploration to a specific, measurable action plan without pushing beyond readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses inclusive language and co-constructs the plan with the client, checking feasibility and inviting preferences at every decision point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly presents choices and asks which option feels most likely to be followed, reinforcing the client's control and treating the strategy as an experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the client's own motivations and practical ideas, drawing out client-generated solutions and commitment language rather than supplying reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as a joint exploration and defers to the client's expertise, inviting him to select methods and shape the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, narrowing to concrete commitments (NA beers, buy six-pack, text Jake) and converting intentions into an actionable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk and redirects toward planning, but misses some opportunities for deliberate double-sided reflections that validate perceived benefits before pivoting to change.
