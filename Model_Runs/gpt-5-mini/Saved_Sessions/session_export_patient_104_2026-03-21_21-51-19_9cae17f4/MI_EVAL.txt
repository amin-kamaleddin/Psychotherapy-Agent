# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing and validating Nadine's feelings and efforts. This steady warmth facilitated disclosure and rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with precise reflections capturing both content and affect, which made Nadine feel understood. Empathy was consistent and kept the conversation focused on her experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on infant sleep and parental rest, repeatedly steering toward concrete strategies and practical follow‑up. The session avoided drift and moved toward specific next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed plans and tools with Nadine, soliciting her input on options, wording, and logistics (crib card, message script, timing). Decisions were negotiated rather than imposed, showing shared expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, checking what felt doable, and avoiding pressuring language. Emphasized Nadine's control and right to decide which steps to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited preferences and some behavioral commitments but relied more on offering solutions than drawing out intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence. Could strengthen use of scaling questions and deeper exploration of motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the interaction as collaborative, asking permission and inviting Nadine to choose the plan and tools. The clinician honored Nadine's expertise about her baby and shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific, actionable change talk and commitments (home‑visitor visit, trying the routine) but missed opportunities to amplify Nadine's own reasons and assess confidence. More open questions could have deepened resolve and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized skepticism while offering a minimal, testable option that softened resistance. Could have used more double‑sided reflections to explicitly link sustain and change talk and reduce defensiveness.
