PSYCHIATRIST: Head Start basics (quick)
- Programs: Head Start (ages 3–5) and Early Head Start (birth–3). 
- Eligibility: low income (federal poverty guidelines), TANF/SSI, homelessness, foster care, disability; priority goes to most vulnerable. 
- What they offer: free/low-cost preschool, family supports, meals, health/screenings, home-based options.
- Enrollment steps: find local program (Head Start locator or county/social services), call/apply, submit proof of income, child’s birth certificate, immunizations/health forms, proof of residency; expect waitlists.
