# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, validating Daniela’s experience and parenting without blame; honors constraints and normalizes slips. This created a nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, tracking emotional shifts and checking safety compassionately. Reflections and validations matched the patient’s stress, efforts, and worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (childcare, Head Start, smoking reduction) and moves discussion toward concrete actions. Provided practical steps (Head Start process, scripts, manager note, NRT plan) without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving, solicits preferences, tailors materials on request, and asks permission before offering suggestions. Language and actions consistently positioned the patient as an active partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly offered choices and framed options as the patient’s decision, including one-time trials and no-pressure language. Avoided coercion and reinforced ownership of next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical intentions and small commitments (Head Start interest, neighbor ask, try gum) but used fewer open evocative questions. Could increase prompts that elicit personal reasons, values, and confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited co-creation and followed the patient’s lead in selecting tools and next steps, sharing power in decision-making. Offers and supports were collaborative rather than directive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change language through concrete planning and affirmations, encouraging small commitments (try gum, one-time ask). Missed some strategic prompts to deepen change talk (importance/confidence scaling and long-term benefits).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers and normalized sustain talk without reinforcing it, redirecting toward problem-solving and pragmatic options. Could more often use double-sided reflections and selectively amplify change statements to further reduce sustain talk.
