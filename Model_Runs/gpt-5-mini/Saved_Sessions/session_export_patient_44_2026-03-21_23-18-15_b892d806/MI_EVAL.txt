# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms progress without blame, maintaining a supportive tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, paraphrasing the client's language and checking understanding to validate feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on target behaviors (gym attendance and protecting back relief) and moves the session logically toward a specific, time‑bound plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co-constructed — the clinician drafts the text with the patient, elicits preferences, and treats the client as an equal partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently offers choices, avoids pressure, and reinforces the client's ownership of the plan while affirming worth regardless of outcome.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits valued outcomes and concrete strategies, but could strengthen evocation by asking for the client's own summary of reasons and using confidence/importance scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative in stance and language, inviting the client's ideas and preferences and shaping the session around those responses.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces several pieces of change talk and a concrete commitment, but could more consistently amplify and summarize change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and redirects to planning, though missed opportunities exist for double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of underlying fears.
