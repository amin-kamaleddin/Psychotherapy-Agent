# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating struggles and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, but stops short of deeper emotional exploration and more complex reflections to name underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the blood-glucose checking routine and moves the conversation toward a specific, small experiment without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint effort by inviting brainstorming, checking preferences, and co-constructing a practical plan that incorporates the patient's constraints and partner support.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice, offers options, and validates non-commitment, consistently reinforcing the patient's sense of control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical ideas and some rationale (e.g., supporting family) but focuses heavily on logistics and does not sufficiently explore intrinsic motivation, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters collaboration and invites the patient's expertise, though at times the clinician offers multiple solutions quickly instead of eliciting more spontaneous patient ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies tentative change talk and encourages doable experiments, but could probe more for desire, importance, and confidence language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk without confrontation, reducing resistance, but rarely uses double-sided or strategic reflections to highlight ambivalence and discrepancy.
