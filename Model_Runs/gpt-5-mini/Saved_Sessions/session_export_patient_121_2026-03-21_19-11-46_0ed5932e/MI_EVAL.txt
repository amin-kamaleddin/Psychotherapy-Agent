# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed warm, nonjudgmental regard, invited disclosure and safety concerns, and consistently validated Sofia's feelings while avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and pragmatic validation early on, but empathy stayed somewhat surface-level and did not probe the softer vulnerabilities or deeper emotions Sofia skirted around.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear agenda and consistent focus on concrete targets (managing peer drama, message drafting), with succinct, actionable options and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with options and adjustments to scripts, but the clinician frequently offered pre-made solutions instead of eliciting Sofia's own phrasing from the start.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored Sofia's choices, offered alternatives, and framed actions as her decision, reinforcing her sense of agency without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some surface-level elicitation (honoring Sofia's choice to draft a message) but limited efforts to draw out her deeper motivations, values, confidence, or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed that Sofia's preferences mattered and offered to co-develop responses, yet often moved into expert-delivery by supplying scripts rather than co-creating early on.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created openings for change talk and supplied supportive language, but did not deliberately elicit or amplify Sofia's desire, ability, reasons, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided joining sustain talk and offered alternatives, but did not use double-sided reflections or explore ambivalence, risking inadvertent collusion with avoidance.
