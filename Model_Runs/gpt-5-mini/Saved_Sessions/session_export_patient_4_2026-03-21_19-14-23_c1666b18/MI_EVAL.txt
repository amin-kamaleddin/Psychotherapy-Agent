# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—validated distress, offered help and practical supports, and avoided labeling or minimizing the patient's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic statements and perspective‑taking (distress rating, validation), but missed opportunities for deeper reflective listening about emotional meaning and impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the patient's priority (urgent testing/GI referral) and consistently guided the session toward concrete, actionable steps and outputs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—solicited and incorporated the patient's edits into letters and scripts, co‑constructing wording and escalation plans step by step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before drafting, and providing scripts and escalation options without pressuring the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did not meaningfully elicit the patient's internal motivations or goals and shifted quickly to giving medical actions without asking evocative MI questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally framed the process as collaborative and invited input, though at times assumed an expert role when listing extensive tests without exploring the patient's preferences or tradeoffs.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to draw out the patient's own language or motivations for change; clinician largely reinforced the biomedical pathway rather than eliciting goals or self‑management ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did not actively soften sustain talk—accommodated and amplified the patient's insistence on testing instead of using double‑sided reflections, reframes, or exploring alternatives.
