# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes resistance, and stays warm and nonpunitive throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, timely reflections that capture both content and emotion, facilitating perspective-taking and collaborative problem‑solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on improving inhaler adherence, moving logically from barriers to a concrete, actionable plan and success metric.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructs the plan with the patient, offering options and deferring to the patient's preferences so the patient's ideas drive the solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly preserves choice and control by avoiding pressuring language, offering options, and checking what the patient wants to try next.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation and ideas out of the patient using open questions, reflections, and a confidence ruler, leveraging the patient's values and resources.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as a joint enterprise, explicitly invites the patient's input, and shares power in deciding concrete steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with open questions, requests for implementation details, and invitations to commit to small steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and appropriately redirects toward change, but missed some opportunities for double‑sided or more affect‑focused reflections to further soften sustain talk.
