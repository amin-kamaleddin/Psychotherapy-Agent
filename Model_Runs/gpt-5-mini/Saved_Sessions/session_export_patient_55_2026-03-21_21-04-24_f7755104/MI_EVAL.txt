# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validation (e.g., “I’ll listen without judgment,” “That’s understandable”), avoiding blame or judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and affect (e.g., paraphrases about exhaustion, lost sleep, and feeling stretched).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on managing academic workload and asks goal-relevant questions that steer toward actionable next steps and priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving, repeatedly elicits the client's preferences, and integrates the client's choices into the plan, reflecting a cooperative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice and control by offering options, asking permission before advising, and affirming no-pressure pacing for change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client's reasoning and specific plans (e.g., choice of review over timed practice) but moves into structured solutions relatively early, limiting deeper elicitation of values or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative and non-hierarchical—shares decision-making, offers choices, and treats the client as the expert on what will work for them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change language and commitments (e.g., 2–3 hours of review) but could do more to amplify and deepen that talk by asking follow-ups about meaning, confidence, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and respects the client's preferences, though missed some micro-opportunities for double-sided reflections or brief exploration of concerns about timed practice to reframe sustain language.
