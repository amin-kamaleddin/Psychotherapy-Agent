### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm with explicit validations and normalizations; no shaming or blaming, supporting a top rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and perspective-taking with acknowledgement of emotions and safety, but missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional nuance around identity and belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on meth use and concrete harms, steering toward specific, actionable targets and a one-week experiment that prevented drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—used inclusive language, co-created messages and plans, checked back frequently, and respected the client's input and revisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by asking permission, framing suggestions as options, and repeatedly deferring to the client's preferences and choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out reasons, confidence rating, and a commitment to a trial, but relied on offering solutions at times rather than eliciting deeper personal motives and alternatives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Softening Sustain Talk
