# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with specific, individualized validations and normalizations that reduced shame and created a safe space for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate empathic listening with precise reflections of feeling and motivation and a tone that invited further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on seeking medical evaluation and guided the conversation toward a concrete, manageable action (drafting/sending a brief message).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Read as joint problem-solving with frequent requests for the patient's preferences and co-creation of message wording and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed the patient's choice by offering options, respecting decisions (e.g., not adding her number), and avoiding directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out internal motives and imagined outcomes but did not sufficiently deepen exploration of core values, past successes, or confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively shared power with the patient through repeated invitations to direct decisions and collaborate on wording and plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk and a small actionable step, but did not fully develop commitment language or use scaling to probe importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully normalized and reframed avoidance to reduce defensiveness, but tended to accept sustain talk rather than strategically countering it with double-sided reflections or amplifying pro-change statements.
