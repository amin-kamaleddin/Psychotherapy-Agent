# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician establishes clear unconditional positive regard and a consistently nonjudgmental, supportive tone, normalizing frustration and affirming the client's efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident (naming sensations, thoughts, and ambivalence) with complex reflections and useful summaries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on micro-tasks (5‑minute Pomodoros), consistently guiding the session from exploration to concrete action planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is strongly collaborative: clinician and client co-construct experiments, check preferences, and jointly shape the plan with client-led tweaks.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, asking permission, and reinforcing that the client decides whether and how to proceed without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's reasons, motivators, and barriers using scaling, but could probe deeper values and more systematically elicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is collaborative and power-sharing is strong, though the clinician at times offers clinician-generated strategies before eliciting more of the client's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits desire/ability language and uses scaling well, but misses opportunities to amplify change talk into stronger commitment statements and link to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects toward change, but relies on neutral reflections rather than strategic double-sided reflections or reframing.
