# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, validated Laura's priorities, and avoided judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good reflective listening and perspective-taking but missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions and the emotional meaning of Laura's values and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with concrete goals, times, and action planning tied to values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan with Laura, solicited her input on nearly every decision, and maintained an equal, negotiated stance throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options, honored Laura's preferences and boundaries, and avoided pressuring language to support choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and linked behavior to values but could have probed deeper intrinsic meaning and elicited stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine power-sharing by positioning Laura as the expert and inviting her to shape specifics of the plan and support.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with specific planning and confidence scaling but did not consistently amplify or summarize statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered pragmatic contingencies, though double-sided reflections and explicit softening of ambivalence were used infrequently.
