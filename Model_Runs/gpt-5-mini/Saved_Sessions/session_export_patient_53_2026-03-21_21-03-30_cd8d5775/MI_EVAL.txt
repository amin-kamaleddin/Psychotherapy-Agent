# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Isabella’s experience, avoiding labels or pressure. Affirmations and nonjudgmental reflections were sustained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases and validations. Could increase use of complex reflections that name deeper emotions (e.g., loneliness, shame) to deepen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on attending the art meetup and guided the conversation toward an actionable, small-step plan. All interventions (scaling, planning, rehearsal) stayed tightly linked to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively, inviting Isabella’s input at every decision point and co-developing the plan with multiple options. The session read as joint problem-solving with the client’s preferences driving choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and framing steps as suggestions rather than prescriptions. Avoided pressure and reinforced that Isabella decides whether and how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used questions and exercises (scaling, if–then planning, rehearsal) to draw out motivation and increase confidence. Could probe more into personal values and amplify emerging change talk to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Isabella as the expert on her experience, inviting choices and asking permission before interventions. Co-constructed concrete steps and rehearsed actions together, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk via value questions, scaling, and in-session practice that raised confidence. Missed some opportunities to amplify existing change language and link attendance to deeper personal goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and strategically redirected toward small, manageable steps and options. Could further soften sustain talk by using more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
