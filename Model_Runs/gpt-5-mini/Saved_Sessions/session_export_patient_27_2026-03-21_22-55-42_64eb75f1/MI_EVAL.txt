# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalizing, validation, and supportive pacing, avoiding judgment. Continue this tone across more emotionally charged disclosures to maintain strength.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries of ambivalence. Could deepen empathic work by using more affect-focused and complex reflections and allowing silence to invite feeling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on disclosure behavior with questions and interventions tied to actionable steps. Resists topic drift and repeatedly returns to the target goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative, co-constructing language and rehearsing together while checking what feels like the client's voice. The clinician genuinely solicits input and shares the work of planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly honors the patient's choice and avoids pressure, offering multiple options and emphasizing that decisions are theirs. This fosters ownership and reduces reactance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation by prompting small doable options, linking to past success, and drawing out preferred language and a plan. Could strengthen by probing internal motives (why it matters), using importance/confidence scaling, and eliciting implementation intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the work as a joint project with collaborative phrasing and invitations to choose. The clinician shares power and respects the patient's expertise about their own voice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with evocative questions, concrete options, and rehearsal, linking plans to past successes. Could more often reflect and amplify specific change statements and explicitly elicit commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence without confronting, but tends to reflect sustain content rather than strategically softening it. Use more double-sided reflections, highlight discrepancies gently, and query costs of staying silent to shift momentum toward change.
