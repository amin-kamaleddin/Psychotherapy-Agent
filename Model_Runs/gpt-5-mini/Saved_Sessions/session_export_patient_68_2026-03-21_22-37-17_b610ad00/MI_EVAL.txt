# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—normalizes hesitation, validates priorities, and expresses belief in Kevin’s ability, demonstrating unconditional positive regard throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking—explicitly names embarrassment and ties it to goals; some reflections are repetitive and there are missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, sustained focus on reducing alcohol-related harm, repeatedly tying questions and options to concrete protective strategies and a near-term plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-develops plans, solicits Kevin’s prior methods, and uses inclusive language that positions them as equal partners in problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and ownership—normalizes ambivalence, asks which options feel doable, and avoids pressuring for long-term commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out motivations, values, and self-generated strategies, but could more systematically deepen and sequence evocation toward confidence and a specific commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites Kevin’s input and frames the work as joint problem-solving, deferring to his experience and sharing decision-making power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and summarizes desired outcomes, though it stops short of converting this into stronger commitment language or using rulers/scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and ambivalence but often repeats sustain language verbatim rather than using double-sided reflections or reframing to down-weight reasons for staying the same.
