# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, affirmed honesty and harm‑reduction efforts, and avoided judgment throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and pragmatic reflections with safety checks, but missed deeper exploration of the patient's emotional reasons for use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on safer heroin use and harm reduction with concrete, actionable next steps and minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the visit as a joint effort and solicited input, but at times shifted into an expert/instructional role with checklists and scripted language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly respected the patient's choice not to quit and offered options and choices that supported the patient's control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced patient‑expressed readiness but often moved quickly to providing information instead of eliciting underlying values, reasons, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and involved the patient in decisions, though could more explicitly co‑create priorities to strengthen equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk around harm reduction and readiness, but missed follow‑up questions to deepen and multiply reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and redirected sustain talk toward safety and options, but did not use double‑sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
