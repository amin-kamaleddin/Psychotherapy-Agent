# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, using normalization and validation throughout; avoided blaming or labeling and responded calmly to defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and summaries captured the patient’s perspective and concerns, helping soften defensiveness and engage the patient.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol reduction tied to health, used agenda setting and scaling, and moved the session logically to a concrete, time‑specific first step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like joint problem solving—clinician solicited the patient’s ideas, adjusted interventions, rehearsed lines, and co‑created the plan rather than imposing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced patient choice with permission‑seeking, offered multiple options, and avoided pressuring language to support ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited importance/confidence and a doable step, but at times shifted into offering many suggestions rather than deepening intrinsic motives, values, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly framed the interaction as collaborative—asked permission, invited choices, and repeatedly deferred to the patient’s preferences when selecting tactics.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced actionable change talk and a specific goal, but missed opportunities to amplify reasons/ability and to deepen tentative statements into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and validated sustain talk, allowing ambivalence, but used few double‑sided reflections or reframes that would more directly weaken sustain talk and highlight discrepancy.
