# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through validating statements, normalizing, and affirmations. Warm, nonjudgmental tone reinforced safety and self‑kindness throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective‑taking, normalizing Sofia's struggles and checking safety. Could deepen empathic work by more often naming underlying emotions (e.g., disappointment, shame, hope) and sitting with them before problem‑solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus on small, specific activity goals and repeatedly guided conversation back to concrete planning. Minimal topic drift and strong momentum toward actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑created plans with Sofia, incorporated her requests into the checklist, and rehearsed messages together, demonstrating equal partnership. Decision‑making was shared and responsive to her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options, affirmed Sofia's choices, and normalized imperfection to support ownership of change. Encouraged modification of plans and emphasized that it's completely up to her what feels right.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete intentions and plans that drew on Sofia's own goals, moving her toward preparation. Could further strengthen internal motivation by asking about personal reasons/values and using scaling to elicit importance and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly asked permission and preference, invited Sofia to shape tools (checklist, alarms), and responded collaboratively to concerns. The interaction felt jointly constructed rather than prescriptive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk leading to a specific plan and start date. Missed opportunities to deepen desire, ability, and reasons with follow‑up reflections and scaling questions to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized skepticism and offered fallback strategies without arguing, which reduced pressure. However, the session leaned into directive tips rather than using more double‑sided reflections or eliciting Sofia's own pros/cons of change.
