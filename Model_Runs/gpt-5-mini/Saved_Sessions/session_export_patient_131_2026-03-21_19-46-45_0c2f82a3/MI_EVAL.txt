# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, offering unconditional positive regard and validating the client's efforts; avoids labeling or moralizing and supports the client's capability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows accurate, validating reflections of ambivalence and important emotions (e.g., about the children and remorse), but could deepen emotional reflection to mirror affect and elicit stronger feeling statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (reduce alcohol, Stopping Violence, reunification) and keeps the conversation tied to concrete next steps like the trial stay, review dates, and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as a joint enterprise: the clinician invites co‑design of the proposal, accepts edits, and provides tools while leaving final choices to the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, asking preferences at decision points, avoiding coercive language, and integrating the client's tone and priorities into the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation and concrete commitments by linking actions to values, but could more systematically draw out commitment/confidence language (scaling, past successes, identity statements).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power and responsibility—inviting the client to choose wording, timing, and supports—fostering client influence over decisions and the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk by highlighting steps already taken, asking which step to tackle first, and offering role‑play and drafting to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids strengthening sustain talk and redirects ambivalence toward planning, but could use more double‑sided reflections and selective amplification of change language to further soften resistance.
