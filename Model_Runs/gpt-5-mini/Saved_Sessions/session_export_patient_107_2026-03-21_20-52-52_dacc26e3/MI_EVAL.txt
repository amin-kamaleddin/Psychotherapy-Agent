### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence using warm, validating language while avoiding judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and summaries that capture ambivalence and affect, but could deepen empathy by exploring the emotional meaning of the coffee ritual and the patient’s subtle worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on reducing late-day caffeine and translates it into a specific, measurable experiment with concrete follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint exploration and co-constructs the plan, letting the patient’s preferences directly shape strategies and language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, asks permission before advice, and affirms the patient’s ownership of the plan while respecting their desired level of accountability.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical motivations, scaling, and planning, but missed opportunities to probe deeper personal values and discrepancy to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits change talk through open questions, scaling, and commitment prompts, but could more deliberately reflect and summarize change statements to amplify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
