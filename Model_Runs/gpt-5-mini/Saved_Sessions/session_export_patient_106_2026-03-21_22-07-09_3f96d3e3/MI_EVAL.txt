# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, validated Natalia's competing concerns, and avoided judgment or pressure when she declined to promise quitting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both practical and emotional aspects of her ambivalence and safety concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking with a specific, achievable target (1-week patch+gum trial) and repeatedly steered the session toward practical steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked jointly with Natalia, soliciting her preferences for logistics and offering to take concrete actions (call/email pharmacies) so the plan fits her life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, asking permission before giving advice, and explicitly honoring her boundary of not promising to quit beyond the trial.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used importance ruler and probes to draw out reasons (protecting her son, wanting simple steps) but missed deeper elicitation of past successes and confidence statements to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Natalia as the expert on her life and co-created the plan through negotiation, permission-seeking, and shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and shaped some change talk (importance question, exploring what would move her) and framed a concrete experiment, but could have amplified her own change language and asked more about commitment/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and offered harm-reduction options, but did not consistently use double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly soften sustain talk and tip toward change.
