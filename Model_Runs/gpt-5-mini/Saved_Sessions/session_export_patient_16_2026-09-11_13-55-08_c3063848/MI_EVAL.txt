# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validates the client's burdens and strengths. Affirms concrete steps and intentions without shaming, creating a warm, respectful stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking around competing priorities and ambivalence. Could deepen affective reflections on family/school stress and feelings about peer pressure to strengthen emotional resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on vaping in the context of basketball and school and moves steadily toward concrete behavioral goals. Balances exploration with clear action planning and relapse-prevention steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as a clear collaborative partner, offering options and checking what the client thinks. Co-constructs implementation steps and lets the client's choices shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the client's control and offers choices at each junction, asking which option feels most doable. Avoids pressure and reinforces the client's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the client's own motivations and resources, linking behavior change to valued outcomes like better play and avoiding suspension. Draws reasons for change from the client and builds them into concrete plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert and shares power in decision-making. Presents suggestions as options and invites the client to select and refine specific steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicits and amplifies change language, highlighting specific commitments (no vaping during season, texting Sarah) and confidence ratings. Uses strategic questions to deepen commitment and surface coping strategies and backups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with double-sided reflections that validate minimization while pivoting to change. Avoids arguing and redirects the conversation toward goals and practical strategies to reduce use.
