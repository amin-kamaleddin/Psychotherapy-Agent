# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful—normalized reactions, affirmed small efforts, and avoided labels throughout, reflecting steady unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening with a safe tone, but missed opportunities to probe emotional nuance and the patient's ambivalence before moving to logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol reduction tied to marathon training from assessment through a concrete 4-week plan and scheduled follow-up, showing strong directional consistency.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by co-creating texts and tracking and negotiating plans, though at times the clinician supplied detailed solutions rather than eliciting more patient-generated options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission, and emphasized no-pressure language and nonjudgmental handling of slips, strongly supporting the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively linked change to the patient's values and used a readiness ruler to draw out motivation, but could have asked more evocative questions to deepen reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative interaction with permission-seeking and incorporation of the patient's wording into the plan, though the clinician sometimes presented solutions before eliciting the patient's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk via goal-setting and a readiness ruler and tied change to training benefits, but did not systematically amplify or explore desire, ability, reasons, or need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and validated minimizations, yet used few active strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or strategic summaries) to resolve ambivalence.
