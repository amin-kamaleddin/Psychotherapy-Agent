# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding judgment; could occasionally add explicit normalization of teen experimentation to further reduce shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking that deepens understanding and helps refine Lukas's concerns; minor repetition but reflections are precise and effective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and its impact on performance, moving the conversation from exploration to concrete planning without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely co-constructed with frequent invitations for input, but offering ready-made scripts occasionally reduced the sense of equal partnership; more role-play and client-generated wording would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks which options feel doable, and affirms Lukas's agency without coercion, supporting his control over decisions and plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Lukas's motivations and ideas and generates a concrete plan, but could probe deeper into values, costs/benefits, and use scaling or amplification to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with shared decision-making and invitations to tailor wording, though at times the clinician shifted into expert scripting without explicit permission; more co-creation and role-play would enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and commitments (specific steps to try), but could more deliberately amplify change statements and elicit stronger commitment/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects toward change, yet underuses double-sided reflections and strategic summaries that would better juxtapose values and weaken sustain talk.
