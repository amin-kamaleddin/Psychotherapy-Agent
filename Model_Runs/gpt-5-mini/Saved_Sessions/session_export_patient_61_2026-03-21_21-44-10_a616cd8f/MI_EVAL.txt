# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing overwhelm and acknowledging practical barriers while avoiding blame or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking with frequent reflections and summaries that matched affect and made the patient feel heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on medication/self-care integration with stepwise, actionable plans and a one-week experiment to preserve momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way problem-solving: invited choices, co-created plans, and incorporated the patient’s ideas rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasized options and low-pressure experiments, avoided coercive language, and reinforced the patient’s right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons, options, and confidence ratings but could have probed deeper into personal values and longer-term motives to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative—invited preferences, offered options, and conveyed that the patient’s judgment about what will work is central.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Prompted several tentative change statements and used scaling, but could have amplified and reinforced commitments to move toward firmer change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized skepticism without confrontation and reframed tasks as low-threat, though missed chances for double-sided reflections and deeper ambivalence work.
