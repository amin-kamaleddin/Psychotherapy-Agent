# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Marek’s ambivalence, avoiding labeling or shaming language throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking with clear summaries of Marek’s experience; more complex emotion-focused reflections could deepen empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on weekend drinking and guided the session toward concrete, measurable steps (e.g., three‑beer cap, an alcohol‑free night, drafting a check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as a genuine partnership—clinician solicited preferences, left choices to Marek, co‑constructed the plan, and summarized his decisions back to him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Marek’s autonomy and right to choose by offering options, avoiding pressure, and supporting a self‑directed test rather than prescribing change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Marek’s own motivations and past success strategies, but could further deepen evocation by drawing out confidence/importance language and amplifying change talk into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance and invited Marek to lead decisions, though could enhance explicit power‑sharing (e.g., asking permission before offering information and co‑creating follow‑up in his words).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, producing concrete experiment language; to strengthen commitment the clinician could more often amplify and chain client change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk with balanced reflections and double‑sided summaries, but could use earlier, more strategic softening techniques to reduce sustain talk’s influence.
