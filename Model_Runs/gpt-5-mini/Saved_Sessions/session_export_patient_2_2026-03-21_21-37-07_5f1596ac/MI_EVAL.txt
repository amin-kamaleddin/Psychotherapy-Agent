# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated warm, nonjudgmental acceptance—validating constraints, honoring boundaries, and normalizing setbacks. Language was respectful and affirming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent perspective-taking with reflective summaries that captured content and feeling and tuned to shifts in tone. Reflections encouraged elaboration and demonstrated strong empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing evening smoking through agenda-setting and repeated steering to concrete, time-bound behavioral plans. Balanced client-centeredness with purposeful direction toward a measurable goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was co-constructed with the patient’s ideas shaping the plan; clinician used inclusive language and integrated real-life constraints. Strategies and supports were developed jointly rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced patient choice and avoided pressuring language, offering options and asking permission before brainstorming. Normalized slips and emphasized the patient’s control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient’s own motivations (concern about cancer, being present for her son) and used those reasons to shape the plan. Probed what success would look like and asked about confidence and supports.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed that expertise about the patient’s life resides with her, inviting choices and following her lead. Decisions (tea, nights, reminders) were co-created and tailored to her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, reflecting and reinforcing language about health and parenting and translating desire into a concrete, testable plan. Asked confidence and identified supports to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and respectfully honored limits about discussing alcohol, effectively redirecting to evenings/smoking. However, there were missed opportunities for strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframing) to more directly resolve ambivalence.
