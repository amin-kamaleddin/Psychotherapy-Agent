# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing concerns and acknowledging priorities throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic moves (complex reflections, validation, invitations to elaborate) demonstrated perspective-taking, though deeper naming of emotions and exploration of underlying fears would strengthen empathic accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the vaccination decision, tying questions and information directly to that goal and moving the session toward concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a genuine two-way partnership with co-created resources, solicitations of preferences, and negotiated next steps rather than directive recommendations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported through permission-seeking, offering choices of format and detail, and avoiding pressuring language while emphasizing the patient's role in deciding.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited the patient's motivations and concerns effectively, but could more systematically probe values, importance/confidence, and elicit a specific commitment or small action step.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed shared expertise and power-sharing by asking permission, offering options, and inviting the patient to lead next steps and resource choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked and supported emergent change talk with targeted questions and risk/benefit framing, yet could amplify and reflect change language more and elicit explicit desire/ability/reasons/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated hesitancy while asking permission before correcting facts, but could use more double-sided reflections and reframing to actively shift sustain talk toward change.
