# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens with unconditional positive regard and maintains a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone, validating and affirming the patient's feelings and efforts throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, mirroring ambivalence and affect and acknowledging emotional nuance in a supportive way.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, specific focus on increasing activity with practical planning, confidence scaling, and concrete steps, balancing exploration with steady guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative with invitations to tweak wording and preferences, but the clinician supplies many scripts and steers the plan more than the patient at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the patient's choice and control by offering options, normalizing backing out, asking permission, and avoiding coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and resources through evocative questions and scaling, drawing out personally meaningful reasons and supports for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters collaboration and invites patient preferences, but occasionally becomes clinician-led through provision of many concrete suggestions and pre-formulated messages.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits concrete change talk and uses confidence scaling to strengthen motivation, though could more explicitly amplify and summarize those statements to boost commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk effectively with validation and 'no-pressure' plans, but could use more double-sided reflections to highlight discrepancy and resolve ambivalence.
