# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveys clear unconditional positive regard and uses warm, nonjudgmental language throughout. Ambivalence is normalized and concerns are acknowledged without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, accurate reflections capture both sides of Maya's experience and validate emotions and risk. Timely reflections deepen rapport and invite further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on alcohol-related behavior and safety, linking history (the blackout) to concrete planning. Discussion remains action-oriented with specific behavioral goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician and Maya co-construct the safety plan and the clinician solicits preferences at each step. Language and options position Maya as an equal partner in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician asks permission before offering advice, presents options rather than directives, and invites Maya to choose what feels doable. Pressure is avoided and Maya's control is affirmed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits internal motivations with pros/cons questions and readiness/confidence scaling, producing useful change language. Opportunities remain to deepen evocation by reflecting and amplifying change statements and eliciting concrete commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative: plans, wording, and choices are made together and the clinician checks Maya's preferences frequently. This demonstrates strong shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and scaling successfully elicited desire and specific ideas for change (e.g., no-drinking school nights). The clinician could more often reflect and amplify these change statements to strengthen commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses balanced reflections to acknowledge both sides. More proactive use of double-sided reflections and selective reinforcement of self-motivational statements would further neutralize sustain talk.
