# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, using affirmations and validating reflections while highlighting strengths (e.g., past success) to convey unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that accurately captures both content and emotion, repeatedly mirroring the patient's concerns and motivations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on completing court-ordered hours, efficiently narrowing conversation to logistics and a concrete action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as a genuine team effort—offers to draft/rehearse, incorporates the patient's wording, and coordinates supports to co-create the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices and defers to the patient's preferences (which step to take, wording, timing), reinforcing ownership and control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical solutions and past successes but could probe more for intrinsic motives, confidence/importance ratings, and stronger personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a co-equal stance and shared decision-making by asking the patient to choose steps, using their language, and negotiating timing and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes change talk and translates it into actionable steps, though it could better amplify and consolidate commitment language and ask for explicit ratings or commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects toward planning, but missed opportunities for strategic double-sided reflections or reframing to further reduce resistance.
