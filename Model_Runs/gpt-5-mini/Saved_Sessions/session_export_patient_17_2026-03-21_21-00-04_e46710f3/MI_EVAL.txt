# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's struggle, using validating language without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections of emotions, concerns, and ambivalence that encourage disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused and goal-directed on smoking cessation, repeatedly steering to concrete strategies, medication options, and follow-up steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—invites patient input, asks permission, and co-creates tools (doctor script, stress plan, symptom log) that fit the patient's life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, seeking permission before advice, and avoiding pressure to set a quit date.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation and planning (readiness scale, past successes) but could use more evocative questions and deliberate amplification of change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a strong partnership stance, treating the patient as an equal collaborator and honoring her expertise in decisions and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk (readiness, reasons, tentative plans) but often shifts quickly to information and planning instead of amplifying and exploring change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges ambivalence and redirects toward coping, yet misses some opportunities for double-sided reflections and strategic reframing of sustain talk into planning.
