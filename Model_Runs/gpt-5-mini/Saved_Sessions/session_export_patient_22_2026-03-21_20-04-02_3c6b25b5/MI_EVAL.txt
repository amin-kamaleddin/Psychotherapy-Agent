# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard with frequent validating, nonjudgmental language and explicit support for the patient's autonomy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries show good perspective-taking and acknowledgement of ambivalence, but deeper emotional content and complex reflections were used only rarely.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a strong, consistent focus on reducing problematic drinking with concrete goals, scaling, and actionable tactics, repeatedly steering conversation back to behavior and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative—options are offered, the patient selects strategies, and role‑plays follow the patient's lead, reflecting joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supports choice, avoids coercion, offers alternatives, and respects the patient's preference against total abstinence, reinforcing ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits the patient's motivations and resources (values, importance/confidence) and uses them to build options, but could more strongly amplify intrinsic reasons and link them to commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership evidenced by asking permission, offering options, inviting feedback, and sharing power in shaping the plan and rehearsals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits and reinforces specific change talk (goals, scaling, tactics) and encourages rehearsal, though strategic amplification and summarization to elicit commitment were limited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and the clinician redirects to practical strategies, but missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections and explicit normalization to more effectively soften sustain talk.
