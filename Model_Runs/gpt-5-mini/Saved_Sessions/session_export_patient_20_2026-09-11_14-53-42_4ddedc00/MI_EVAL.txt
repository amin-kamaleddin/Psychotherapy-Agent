# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgment, validating ambivalence and avoiding labels throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely, and specific reflections that captured the client's ambivalence and how drinking functions for them, demonstrating strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and progressed from exploration to a concrete, testable plan tied to measurable behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, offering options and repeatedly inviting the client to choose what felt most realistic and doable.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized testing rather than committing and avoided pressuring language, affirming the client's control over decisions and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out practical, client-generated steps and criteria for progress but did not probe deeply for intrinsic motivators or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan with the client, shared decision-making power, and repeatedly checked that strategies fit the client's life and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change talk and small testable steps, but could have more consistently amplified and reinforced change statements and sought stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not argue or reinforce sustain talk, but missed opportunities for strategic softening (e.g., double-sided reflections or discrepancy summaries) to shift momentum toward change.
