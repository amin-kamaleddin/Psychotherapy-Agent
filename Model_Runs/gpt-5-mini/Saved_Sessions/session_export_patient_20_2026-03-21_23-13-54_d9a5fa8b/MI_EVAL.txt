# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently uses nonjudgmental, supportive language and normalizes ambivalence, creating a warm accepting stance. Labels and moralizing are avoided, making the client comfortable to disclose both sustain and change talk.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections and summaries accurately capture the client's cognitive ambivalence and emotions, and are tied to the client's values. The empathic statements invite elaboration and demonstrate strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conversation stays clearly focused on the pragmatic goal of two alcohol-free weeknights, with each turn tied to generating options and specifying indicators of success. The clinician maintains goal-directedness while remaining client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction functions as an equal-partner exploration: the clinician elicits the client's ideas, integrates them into summaries, and lets the client select the concrete plan. Frequent invitations to choose reinforce genuine collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician honors the client's choice by offering options, framing the plan as a voluntary experiment, and avoiding directives. Language emphasizes 'not all or nothing' and respects the client's decision to keep weekends intact.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Evocative questions draw motivation from the client's own values and goals, eliciting personally meaningful reasons and concrete change talk. The clinician avoids supplying reasons for change and lets the client articulate motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician adopts a collaborative stance, inviting the client to generate options and pick supports, which centers the client's expertise in the plan. This co-creation reinforces partnership throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk with concrete planning questions and summaries, generating doable options and success indicators. Missed opportunities include explicitly eliciting confidence/commitment (e.g., 0–10 scaling) to deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses double-sided reflections and validation to avoid reinforcing sustain talk and reframes reasons for drinking into workable strategies. Could more directly explore and gently challenge minimizations like 'it helps me relax' to further reduce sustain talk.
