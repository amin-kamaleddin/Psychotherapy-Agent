# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing difficulties and offering help without pressure; conveys unconditional positive regard throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides timely empathic statements and some reflective listening (e.g., acknowledging stress) but misses deeper emotional reflections and naming of feelings like anxiety or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reliable childcare and harm‑reduction, moving the session to concrete, actionable steps (resource lookup, backup list, drafted messages).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑creates texts and plans, invites edits, and centers the patient's preferences and expertise in decision‑making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and self‑direction by framing offers as options, asking permission, and soliciting the patient's preferences on wording and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete planning details and intentions (zip code, days, budget) but rarely probes for internal motivations, values, or confidence to amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as an equal partner with shared decision‑making, iteratively drafting and refining messages based on the patient's input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Encourages action‑oriented change talk through planning and supports but does not consistently evoke intrinsic motivations, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirects away from barriers by offering alternatives (subsidies, barter, backup caregivers) but could strengthen use of double‑sided or summary reflections to further soften sustain talk.
