# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, validating Elena's distress and offering options. The clinician maintained respectful tone and explicit positive regard throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, summarizing concerns and normalizing emotions. Short clarifying reflections demonstrated strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the vaccine decision with concrete, practical steps (drafting questions, summarizing sources, role‑play). The clinician kept the session purposefully oriented toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a co-created process, tailoring information to Elena's needs and rehearsing scripts together. Elena's preferences shaped content rather than a didactic approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options and checking preferences. Encouraged ownership (e.g., choosing phrasing, whether to practice).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out concerns and tentative change talk but could have probed deeper into personal values and motivations. More elicitation of confidence and commitment language would strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treated the encounter as a shared endeavor, inviting Elena to shape the session and decisions. Repeatedly asked for preferences and co‑constructed wording and plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified statements supporting movement toward change and produced tentative change talk. Missed opportunities to convert willingness into explicit commitment or a specific next step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, validated concerns before offering corrective facts and redirected to risks/benefits. Could increase use of double-sided reflections and affirm ambivalence to further soften resistance.
