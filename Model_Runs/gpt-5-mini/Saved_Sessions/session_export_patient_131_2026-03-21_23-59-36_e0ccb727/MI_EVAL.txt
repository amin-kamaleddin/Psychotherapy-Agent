# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating worries and affirming efforts. Language avoids labels and supports the client's sense of being respected and seen.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflective listening captures both content and feeling and ties competing concerns together. Clinician checks specifics in a way that demonstrates understanding of the client's life and priorities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on target behaviors and moves steadily toward concrete, measurable goals. Questions and prompts tighten focus on implementation without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly a joint enterprise with open questions and co-created decisions that use the client's language. Clinician follows the client's priorities and helps refine the client's solutions rather than imposing plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options and eliciting the client's preference. Encourages ownership of the plan without pressuring a particular decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled at drawing motivation from the client's values and eliciting self-generated reasons and strategies. Evocative questions deepen commitment and produce concrete implementation intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reflects genuine power‑sharing, repeatedly asking for the client's ideas and building on them. The clinician co-constructs the plan and respects the client's expertise about his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, linking it to core values and prompting specific first steps. Successfully converts general motivation into actionable, measurable behaviors and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects sustain talk without amplifying it, shifting toward practical solutions. Missed some opportunities for more nuanced moves (e.g., double-sided reflections and stronger discrepancy elicitation) to further soften sustain talk.
