# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (validations, normalizing ambivalence), which reduced defensiveness throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and summaries that mirrored Eric’s language and perspective (routine, unwind, crew), demonstrating strong understanding and perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on smoking and harm-reduction, logically narrowing from function exploration to a specific, time-bound experiment (Thursday mid-shift coffee).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Approached the interaction as a joint exploration, soliciting Eric’s preferences and co-creating a low-pressure plan that honored his choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Eric’s choice and control, offered options rather than directives, and normalized any outcome to support autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions and a readiness ruler to elicit some motivation and a concrete experiment, but could have done more to draw out and amplify client-generated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong power-sharing by asking permission, offering choices, and deferring to Eric’s expertise when selecting the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited limited change talk and a measurable commitment (holding a coffee) but missed opportunities to amplify desire/confidence and explore stronger reason language before commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided confrontation and normalized sustain talk, but could have employed more strategic double-sided or selective reflections to subtly pivot language toward change.
