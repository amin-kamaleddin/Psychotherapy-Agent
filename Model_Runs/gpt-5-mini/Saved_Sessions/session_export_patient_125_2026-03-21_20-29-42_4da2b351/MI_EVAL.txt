# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Tariq's feelings (e.g., 'I’m here to listen without judgment'). Multiple validating statements and nonjudgmental tone make the patient feel heard and respected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate reflections of content and affect (unfairness, frustration, family concern). Mirrors patient language and reinforces understanding throughout the interaction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior of obtaining a brief phone call and handling staff interactions. Clinician consistently brings conversation back to concrete goals and organizes actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—clinician invites input, incorporates Tariq's phrasing, and co-creates scripts and notes. Tariq's ideas substantially shape the plan, reflecting a team approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices (script vs. plan, wording options) and respects Tariq's preferences and refusals (e.g., declining role-play). Language emphasizes agency and avoids pressuring a single option.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivations (family connection, dad's birthday) and turns them into concrete actions, but stops short of deeper exploration of values or long-term meaning. More evocative questions could have drawn out stronger intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions Tariq as a collaborator by soliciting preferences and accepting edits to wording and approach. Power-sharing is evident in co-creation of scripts, notes, and contingency steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical change language and plans (script, note, supervisor steps) and secures Tariq's willingness to try, but misses opportunities to deepen desire, reasons, or confidence (e.g., scaling questions or exploring meaning).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to problem-solving with validating statements and practical strategies. Could further reduce sustain talk using double-sided reflections and more explicit acknowledgment of ambivalence.
