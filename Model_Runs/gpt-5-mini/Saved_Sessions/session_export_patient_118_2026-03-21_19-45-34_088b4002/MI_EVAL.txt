# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validates the patient's wishes and boundaries, explicitly respecting preferences throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and accurately names frustration and side-effect concerns, though could add deeper affect-focused or complex reflections to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on appropriate dental-care goals and safety while leaving a brief, permission-based door open to smoking; could more strategically link oral health to motivation for change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the encounter as a joint effort by soliciting input, checking consent, and adapting care to the patient's stated preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient's right to choose, offers options rather than directives, and avoids pressuring language, reinforcing patient control throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offers optional invitations to discuss change but does not actively elicit the patient's own motivations or use targeted questions or scaling to draw out change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Negotiates the agenda, seeks permission for procedures, and solicits communication preferences, demonstrating genuine power-sharing and respect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to elicit or amplify change talk; missed opportunities to follow up on brief change-relevant remarks or use micro-elicitation questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and honors resistance but seldom uses double-sided reflections, reframing, or strategic reflections to gently pivot toward ambivalence or change.
