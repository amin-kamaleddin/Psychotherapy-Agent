### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating language; affirms the patient's ideas and creates safety for honest disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections capture both content and affect, demonstrating strong perspective-taking and empathic understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use during pregnancy and effectively moves from values and motivation to concrete planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is co-constructed: the clinician elicits patient ideas, uses the patient's language, and offers help only after permission, underscoring shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports the patient's right to choose, asks what feels realistic, and reinforces ownership of decisions without directing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out motivations, past successes, and practical steps, but could more actively amplify and develop the strongest motivations into explicit commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as collaborative, invites the patient's expertise, and shares power in planning and wording with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk and concrete planning, yet could better amplify strong change statements and assess confidence/commitment (e.g., scaling or direct commitment elicitation).

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and neutrally reframes ambivalence, but missed opportunities for targeted double-sided reflections around the patient's skepticism about evidence.
