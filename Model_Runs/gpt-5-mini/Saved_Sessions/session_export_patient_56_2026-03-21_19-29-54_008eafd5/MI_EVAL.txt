# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes uncertainty using affirming, nonjudgmental language. Warm, respectful stance maintained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic effort with reflections and tailored questions, acknowledging constraints and challenges. Could deepen empathy with more complex reflections of underlying feelings and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on target behaviors (evening/morning routines) and repeatedly returns to concrete, practical steps. Goal-directed without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as joint problem-solving, co-creating the plan and adapting suggestions to the patient's feedback. Invites patient choice and follow-up to refine the plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy through offering choices, permissive language, and framing strategies as experiments. Emphasizes patient control over whether and how to try ideas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits values and practical motivations (family happiness, peaceful mornings) but leans toward offering many specific ideas. Could increase open questions to draw out intrinsic reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters client expertise and shared decision-making by soliciting preferences and offering multiple options. Adapts recommendations based on the patient's practical knowledge and feasibility concerns.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and strengthens change talk with choice questions, affirmations, and a follow-up plan. Missed opportunities to deepen reasons, importance/confidence, and explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully minimizes sustain talk by normalizing ambivalence, offering low-pressure experiments, and providing flexible options. Avoids argumentation and redirects toward doable next steps.
