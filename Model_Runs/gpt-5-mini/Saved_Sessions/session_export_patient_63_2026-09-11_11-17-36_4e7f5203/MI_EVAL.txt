**Comments:** Elicits the client's own ideas for change (wallet, scripts, phone backup) but focuses more on concrete strategies than deeper personal reasons or confidence. Could probe values, importance, and confidence more explicitly to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and specific steps, steering toward doable behaviors like limiting cash and rehearsed lines. Would benefit from more amplification of intrinsic reasons and explicit commitment language (importance/confidence questions).

**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and pivots to protective strategies, using neutral-to-supportive reflections. Could increase use of double‑sided reflections and reframing to further reduce the pull of sustain talk.
