# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, using validating language and normalizing ambivalence. Reflections explicitly affirm the patient's cautious, practical stance throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with repeated, precise reflections of both content and emotion. Clinician captures the patient's concerns about side effects and living alone, reinforcing understanding and trust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (starting the statin) and ties each turn back to concrete supports and planning. The clinician strategically guides toward actionable steps without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative, framed as joint problem-solving with the patient’s preferences integrated into the plan. Clinician checks in repeatedly and adjusts the plan based on the patient's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options and honors the patient's choices at every decision point, using nondirective language. This approach reinforces the patient's ownership of the decision to start medication.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reliably elicits the patient's own motivations and conditions for change, turning ambivalence into concrete commitments. Open questions and targeted follow-ups helped the patient articulate readiness and specific actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert on her needs and invites co-design of the plan, deferring to her judgments. Choices (e.g., pharmacist first, text vs call) were incorporated, demonstrating strong power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions and reflections, then concretizes commitments. Follow-ups translated the patient's preparatory language into specific, actionable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and downplays sustain talk without reinforcing it, then pivots to practical supports. Could have more deeply explored the source of initial doubts or used double-sided reflections before moving to planning.
