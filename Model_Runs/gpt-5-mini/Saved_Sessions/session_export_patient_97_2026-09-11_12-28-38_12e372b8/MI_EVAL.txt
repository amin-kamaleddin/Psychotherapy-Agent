# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental acceptance and respect, validating Mateo's preferences and framing goals without blame. A minor increase in normalizing common post-release struggles could further strengthen this skill.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that summarized concerns and surfaced ambivalence, helping Mateo elaborate. Reflections stayed mostly at the content level and could be deepened by more explicit emotional reflections (fear, shame, etc.).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on connecting with re-entry staff and establishing weekly check-ins, keeping each turn action-oriented. The session avoided drift and built sequential decisions toward a concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co-created with frequent invitations for Mateo's input and decisions, offering practical help while keeping him central. The clinician reinforced an equal, team-based approach throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered explicit choices and asked Mateo to select what felt best, preserving his ownership of the plan. Even offers of direct help were framed as supportive rather than taking control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from Mateo by eliciting his goals and preferences and amplifying his change language. Questions and reflections successfully surfaced and strengthened Mateo's commitment and readiness to act.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by sharing decisions and offering to act alongside Mateo while ceding expertise to him. The clinician repeatedly invited Mateo to choose how to proceed, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk through realistic options and clarifying specific plans, amplifying Mateo's intent and planning. Repeated reflections and choices strengthened commitment to actionable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward autonomous reasons and practical steps. Could more often use double-sided reflections and explicitly validate sustain talk before pivoting to further reduce defensiveness.
