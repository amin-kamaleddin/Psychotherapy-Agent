# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming—validates choices, thanks the patient, and explicitly promises curiosity over criticism to maintain acceptance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear empathic reflections and summarizes constraints well, but could deepen emotional exploration and use more complex reflections to fully capture ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on incremental activity change with concrete, actionable steps and a scheduled check-in, maintaining clear purposeful direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in collaborative problem-solving by inviting input, integrating the patient's choices into the plan, and tailoring supports to her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, accepting her preferred start date and reminder method, and framing missed days permissively to reduce pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivations and commitments but often supplies solutions; could use more open-ended, values-oriented questions and scaling to draw out internal reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a genuine partnership—power is shared through options and co-created decisions (e.g., post-it vs phone reminder, Monday start, check-in content).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with doable micro-goals and commitments, but could further probe personal reasons and confidence/importance to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and neutralizes sustain talk with compassionate fallbacks and normalization, though more double-sided reflections and eliciting the patient's own rebuttals would improve this area.
