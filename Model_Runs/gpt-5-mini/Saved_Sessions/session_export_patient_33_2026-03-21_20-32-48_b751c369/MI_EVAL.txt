# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established unconditional positive regard from the start with nonjudgmental, validating language and avoided labeling or moralizing, creating a safe stance for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated both escape and safety concerns, though reflections remained pragmatic and did not deeply probe emotional roots of the escape.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on harm-reduction behaviors and steered the conversation toward actionable, time-bound steps without losing a patient-centered stance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—co-created scripts, checklists, and options, invited Diego to pick and tweak wording, and role‑played only when requested.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by asking permission before advice, offering multiple options, and affirming Diego’s selections without pressuring him to quit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited immediate motivations and specific commitments and translated them into plans, but could do more to draw out deeper internal values and broader reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shared control throughout, treated Diego as the expert on his life, co-constructed plans, and honored his preferences in how to proceed and rehearse.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced practical change talk and specific commitments, though missed opportunities to amplify broader change language about long-term benefits, confidence, and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and used balanced reflections to acknowledge ambivalence while redirecting to safety, but could more actively explore pros/costs or amplify discrepancy to further reduce sustain talk.
