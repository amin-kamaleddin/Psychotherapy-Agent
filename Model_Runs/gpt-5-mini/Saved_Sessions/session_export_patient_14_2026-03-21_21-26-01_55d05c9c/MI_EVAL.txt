# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—apologized for the wait, validated frustration, and normalized practical constraints—creating a respectful, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with concise summaries of Andrei's situation, though reflections stayed pragmatic and missed deeper emotional or values-based exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on medication adherence and BP monitoring throughout, steering quickly to concrete, achievable targets and closing with a concise plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed the plan by inviting input at every decision point, accepting choices, and tailoring options to fit Andrei's work routine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking which he preferred, confirming readiness, and honoring his request for no scheduled check-ins.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited actionable motivation with a readiness ruler and questions about what would move him, but could have probed values, longer-term reasons, or what success would feel like to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership and power-sharing were evident—clinician invited Andrei's input, deferred to his expertise, and worked jointly to create practical backup plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively encouraged and amplified change talk (readiness ruler, 'what would move you'), and translated it into a concrete one-week plan, but could have elicited more reasons and importance for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and skillfully redirected sustain talk toward small, manageable options, though more double-sided reflections could have further normalized ambivalence.
