# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental tone, validating concerns and respecting limits while offering choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries that normalized stress and elicited details; could deepen rapport by probing more into the emotional experience behind guardedness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on completing court-ordered community service and moved the conversation from problem identification to concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: clinician solicited constraints, offered tailored options, and iterated the plan with the client, framing the work as teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice by offering multiple options, asking permission before actions, and providing editable templates for the client to control outreach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited decision-relevant details and a behavioral commitment but did not sufficiently draw out intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated the client as an equal partner and solicited preferences throughout, though at times the clinician led with detailed plans without explicit early agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some action-oriented change talk and planning but missed opportunities to amplify internal reasons for change or explore importance and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to logistics, but did not use advanced softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or selectively amplifying change language).
