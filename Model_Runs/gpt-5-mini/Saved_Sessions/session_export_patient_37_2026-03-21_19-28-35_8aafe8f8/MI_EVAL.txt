### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently displayed nonjudgmental positive regard and normalized the parent's concerns, validating worries and avoiding labeling or shaming throughout the interaction.

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the vaccination decision, providing concise factual corrections, safety planning, and concrete scripts tied directly to the parent's concerns.

### Collaboration

#### **Score (1–5):** 5
