# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client's struggles, validating effort and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, high-level empathic listening with reflections that capture both content and feeling, helping the client feel heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (starting tasks after practice) and ties questions and reflections to concrete, actionable strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint exploration, synthesizing the client's ideas and refining options rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly acknowledges the client's choice and offers options, inviting her preferences and framing suggestions as collaborative brainstorming.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations, values, and a past success, linking those reasons to specific, repeatable strategies for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters client expertise and shared decision-making by asking for input and allowing the client to co-create the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits desire and reasons and explores small doable steps and past wins, but could better amplify ability and commitment language (e.g., scaling questions or direct commitment prompts).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes doubts, redirecting toward experiments, but misses chances to strategically reframe sustain talk (double-sided reflections or exploring costs) to weaken it.
