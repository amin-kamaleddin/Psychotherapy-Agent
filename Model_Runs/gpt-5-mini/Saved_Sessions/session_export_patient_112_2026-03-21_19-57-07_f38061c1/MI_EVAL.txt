# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with repeated normalization and unconditional positive regard (e.g., "I’m here to listen and support you").

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate listening and validated specific fears, but often shifted quickly to solutions instead of offering deeper, complex reflections of emotion and identity-related sustain talk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation throughout, tying interventions directly to quitting and concrete planning (quit date, dosing, first-week plan).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine joint problem-solver by inviting personalization, checking constraints (work, cost), and adapting recommendations to Rosa’s circumstances.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and ownership with invitations and options rather than directives (e.g., asking permission, offering tentative plans).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some prompts to act (quit date, cigarettes/day) but the session was largely information- and solution-focused, missing open, evocative questions to draw out Rosa’s personal motives and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance throughout, checking preferences and power-sharing decisions while acknowledging Rosa’s expertise about her life and constraints.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created opportunities and reinforced movement toward change (permission to plan, pressing on quit date), but could more actively elicit and amplify Rosa’s intrinsic reasons and imagined benefits.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered solutions, but rarely used MI-specific softening techniques (double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance) to reduce ambivalence intensity.
