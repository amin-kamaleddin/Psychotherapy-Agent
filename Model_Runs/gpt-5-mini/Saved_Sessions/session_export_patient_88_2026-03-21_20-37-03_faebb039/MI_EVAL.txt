# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing concerns and affirming priorities. Boundaries (refusing cover stories) were framed respectfully and harm-reductively.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking with complex reflections and summaries. Validated Marcus's feelings and matched tone to his guarded, pragmatic stance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on teen alcohol safety and avoiding legal/parental consequences. Repeatedly steered to practical planning and concrete next steps while avoiding unethical advice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting choices, role-playing at Marcus's request, and co-developing specific plans and backups. Marcus's ideas substantively shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by offering options, using permission language, and honoring Marcus's choice not to quit. Avoided prescriptive or coercive language throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Marcus's own protective strategies and asked confidence ratings, drawing out concrete change plans. Could have probed deeper values or long-term motives to strengthen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Marcus as an equal collaborator, asking which options he preferred and deferring to his choices. Practiced wording and logistics together, reinforcing shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited and reinforced change-oriented language, offered options, and elicited concrete planning and intentions. Could amplify and summarize Marcus's change talk more and probe underlying motives to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed toward safety and harm-reduction without confrontation. Could use more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to further soften sustain talk and highlight ambivalence.
