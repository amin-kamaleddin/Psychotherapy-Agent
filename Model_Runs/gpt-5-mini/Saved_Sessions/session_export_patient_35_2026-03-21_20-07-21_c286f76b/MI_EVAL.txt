**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard, normalizing efforts and framing slips non-punitively while validating protective actions. Could further reduce shame by occasionally normalizing ambivalence explicitly.

When quit-oriented remarks arise, amplify with reflective and open questions to elicit desire, reasons, ability, and need.
