# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating the patient's concerns and accommodating his preference for blunt wording throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate and responsive reflective listening with repeated paraphrases of the patient's core worries and priorities, though probes into deeper emotion were limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction remained tightly focused on the medication decision and produced concrete, actionable options (scripts, monitoring plan, stop rule) for the patient to take to the doctor.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach—asked which form of help the patient wanted, negotiated wording, and co‑constructed the materials (scripts/card) to match his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice, honored the patient's timeline and preferences, and avoided pressuring him to start medication.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete preferences and logistics well but did little to draw out the patient's deeper intrinsic motivations, values, or stronger reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the patient as an active partner in decision‑making, offering options and inviting his input on wording and stop‑rule choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently asked questions that prompted modest change talk (diet/exercise, monitored trial), but did not consistently amplify or deepen that language to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed reluctance into concrete plans, but often mirrored sustain talk verbatim instead of using double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to shift it further.
