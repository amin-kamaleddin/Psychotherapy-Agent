# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard by normalizing and validating the patient's experience, honoring privacy, and avoiding judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking—accurately paraphrased the patient's goals and burdens and acknowledged emotional context like skepticism and busy mornings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on improving maintenance inhaler adherence with concrete action planning, summaries, and problem-solving while resisting topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving and consistently invited and incorporated the patient's preferences into each decision point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, normalizing limits, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's reasons and resources for change (sleep, less coughing, stamina) but moved to solutions relatively quickly without further amplifying internal motives to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine power-sharing was evident as the patient shaped alarm times, inhaler placement, check‑in method, and message wording, reflecting true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and a small-test commitment, but could have amplified the patient's own reasons (e.g., sleep, work, stairs) into stronger commitment language before planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argumentation and pragmatically redirected sustain talk with supports, though missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or explicit contrasts to more actively reduce resistance.
