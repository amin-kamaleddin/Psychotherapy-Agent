# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through nonjudgmental reflections, validation of pace, and permission language, normalizing the client's overwhelm and avoiding blame or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate perspective-taking with frequent reflective listening and summaries that matched the client's tone and concerns about overwhelm and limits.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on diabetes self-management and narrowed the session to a concrete, manageable behavior (one pre-dinner blood‑sugar check) tied to the client's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by offering options, asking for the client's preferences, and co-creating a plan that was shaped by the client's choices and practical supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's choice and control, accepted limits, avoided pressuring language, and checked what felt doable while respecting the client's right to stop.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited internal motivation and values-based change talk (keeping work, not being a burden) and asked for a 'tiny, realistic win' drawn from the client's priorities.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative, client-centered interaction by inviting input on what would work and sharing power in decision-making about supports and steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk, including a commitment to a single check, but could have amplified and summarized the client's change language more deliberately and drawn out confidence/commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk, reframing change as small and manageable, though there were missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to shift sustain talk further toward change.
