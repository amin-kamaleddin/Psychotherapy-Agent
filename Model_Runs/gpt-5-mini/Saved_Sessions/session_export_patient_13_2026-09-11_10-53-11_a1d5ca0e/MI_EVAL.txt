# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard with normalizing statements and no blaming, accepting the patient's hesitation and limits.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely, and specific reflections that conveyed nuanced understanding of fears, logistics, and vulnerability, guiding the interaction compassionately.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and moved the session toward a concrete, achievable plan (specific time, reminders, script, voicemail).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True co-construction of the plan with options offered rather than prescribed and practical support provided only on the patient's terms.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported choice by asking which option felt doable, honoring limits, and avoiding pressure while reinforcing patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivators and specific steps well but did not sufficiently explore deeper personal values, reasons for change, or confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner throughout, soliciting the patient's preferences and shaping the plan around her expertise about what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk about small, concrete steps, but missed opportunities to deepen commitment via values linkage or confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and redirected sustain talk toward small steps effectively, but relied mostly on neutral reflections and pragmatic fixes rather than double-sided reflections or reframing.
