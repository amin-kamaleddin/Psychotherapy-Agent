# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental; normalized worries and validated small wins throughout the encounter, conveying unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening and occasional reflections of ambivalence, but missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections exploring underlying feelings and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on a single, values-consistent target behavior with clear implementation intentions, cues, alarm labels, and relapse plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a team effort—offered options, asked permission, and co-created materials—treating the patient as an equal partner whose preferences shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressuring language, supporting ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation of values and choice, but the clinician frequently shifted to solution-giving instead of systematically drawing out the patient's own motives, meanings, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance with permission-seeking and choices, though at times became more prescriptive by offering many detailed strategies rather than co-constructing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific change talk and implementation intentions, but used fewer prompts to deepen intrinsic reasons, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well by normalizing slips and offering reframe strategies, though could use more double-sided reflections to further soften and acknowledge sustain talk.
