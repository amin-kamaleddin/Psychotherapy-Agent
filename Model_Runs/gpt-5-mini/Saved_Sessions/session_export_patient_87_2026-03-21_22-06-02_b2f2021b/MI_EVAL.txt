# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect with validating, nonjudgmental reflections that normalize difficulty and encourage disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate reflections of both barriers and feelings, but could deepen affective exploration (shame, meaning) to strengthen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior throughout, linking questions and reflections to concrete, actionable steps and resisting tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as a true collaboration—solutions are co‑created, the clinician solicits preferences, rehearses language, and adapts suggestions to Marco's context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy by offering choices, checking feasibility, and affirming Marco's ownership of decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk around practical plans and confidence but relies heavily on logistics and could do more to draw out deeper values and intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power—invites input, offers options, and co-constructs the plan, reinforcing that decision-making resides with Marco.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk using scaling and concrete commitments, though missed opportunities exist to probe for deeper desire, reasons, and long‑term motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk nonjudgmentally and redirects to manageable strategies, but could employ more double-sided reflections and explore the function/consequences of sustain talk.
