# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Linda's ambivalence and normalizing her experience. The clinician avoided criticism and respected her stance while offering options.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate, reflective listening that captured Linda's feelings and barriers. Reflections and summaries consistently mirrored her ambivalence and skepticism in an empathic way.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking and reducing children's exposure and progressively moved to specific, behaviorally defined goals. The session ended with a concrete, time-bound plan and agreed check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co-constructed: the clinician invited Linda's priorities, offered choices, and helped translate her selection into a one-step plan. Inclusive language and joint decision-making supported teamwork throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by asking permission before advice, normalizing slips, and accepting Linda's decision not to quit while offering harm-reduction options. Reinforced client ownership of decisions and sequencing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited importance/confidence and client-generated strategies, moving toward commitment, but could have probed more deeply into the personal meaning of being a role model. More targeted reflections could have amplified intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the session as a joint exploration, asking which strategies felt doable and co-creating the action plan. Permission-seeking and shared planning preserved Linda's expertise and agency.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and built some change talk (scaling, strategy generation, behavioral commitment) but stopped short of amplifying and exploring desire/reasons more deeply. Additional reflections on Linda's role-model concerns could have strengthened change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not argue and used reframing to reduce pressure, validating enjoyment and stress-relief, but missed opportunities for more double-sided reflections and selective amplification to further dilute sustain talk. More strategic reflections could have softened sustain statements further.
