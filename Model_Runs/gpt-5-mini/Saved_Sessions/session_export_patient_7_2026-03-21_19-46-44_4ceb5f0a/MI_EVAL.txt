# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, honoring the patient's choices and avoiding labeling or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid empathic statements and some reflective listening, but misses opportunities for deeper, affect-focused reflections or naming emotions to deepen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on engagement and safety throughout, with interventions that consistently support the limited agenda for this encounter.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structures the interaction as a joint effort by offering options, asking preferences, and positioning the patient as an equal partner in deciding the session's course.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and control, repeatedly normalizing the patient's ownership of decisions and honoring requests to pause or return later.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes limited attempts to elicit internal motivations or reasons for change; the brief 'calmer' response was not followed with exploration to draw out motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the session as collaborative and defers to the patient's expertise, inviting input and co-constructing options even with low engagement.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to evoke change talk or explore goals/values; the clinician offers permissive options but does not strategically elicit desire, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoids escalating resistance by respecting boundaries, offering low-pressure alternatives, and reframing toward safety rather than confrontation.
