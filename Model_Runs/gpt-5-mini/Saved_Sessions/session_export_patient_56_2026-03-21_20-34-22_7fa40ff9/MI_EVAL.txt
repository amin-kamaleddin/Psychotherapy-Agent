# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear unconditional positive regard from the start and used affirming, nonjudgmental language throughout, validating the client's efforts and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, paraphrasing values and barriers, but missed opportunities for deeper, affect-laden reflections of exhaustion and overwhelm.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus linking values to a specific, time-bound plan (one device-free dinner) and guided the session from values to obstacles to concrete steps without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan with frequent invitations for the client's preferences and edits, sharing power by offering options, scripts, and rehearsal while honoring the client's language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice and self-direction by offering multiple options, asking what the client preferred, and avoiding coercive language so the client retained ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated from an evocation stance by drawing on the client's stated value of family happiness, eliciting concrete change ideas, confidence ratings, and personally acceptable wording for scripts and reminders.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as expert and partner throughout, inviting selection and tweaking of options and jointly constructing materials like reminders and child-friendly scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and reinforced change talk with targeted open questions, affirmations, scaling of confidence, and practical supports that translated desire into specific action steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers and avoided reinforcing sustain talk, but relied mainly on practical problem-solving rather than MI softening techniques like double-sided reflections to explicitly shift sustain talk toward change.
