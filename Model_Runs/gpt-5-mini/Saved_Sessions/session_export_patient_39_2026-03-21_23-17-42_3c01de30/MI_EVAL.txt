# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validates feelings, uses the client’s name, normalizes uncertainty, and avoids blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking that echo the patient's language and summarize concerns, with only occasional repetitive phrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing activity and moves logically to a concrete, testable plan tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner—invites choices, checks safety, and co-constructs the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, normalizing limits, granting permission to stop, and avoiding pressure or nagging.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivations and past habits and links outcomes to values, but at times offers option-laden prompts and could probe deeper for intrinsic reasons or use scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a collaborative stance throughout, routinely inviting the patient's input and refining the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces specific change language and steps, but misses opportunities to amplify commitment and assess confidence or personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk with supportive reassurances, yet often repeats similar reflections and could use more double-sided reflections or counterevidence to resolve ambivalence.
