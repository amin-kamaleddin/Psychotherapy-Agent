### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking at social gatherings and steadily narrowed to actionable steps (limits, water, Luka, and texting as the first step).

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and agency (e.g., offering options, respecting 'not promising' caveats) and avoided pressuring Milan toward a particular course of action.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively elicited Milan’s own strategies and ideas (limits, signals, bottle‑swap) but did not sufficiently explore underlying values or personal reasons that would deepen motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co‑constructed the plan by asking for Milan’s ideas first, offering options, and aligning support with what Milan preferred, demonstrating shared power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several change‑oriented statements and implementation details, but missed opportunities to probe importance/confidence or elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting or amplifying sustain talk and normalized ambivalence, yet did not use double‑sided reflections or explicit pros/cons exploration to further shift emphasis toward change.
