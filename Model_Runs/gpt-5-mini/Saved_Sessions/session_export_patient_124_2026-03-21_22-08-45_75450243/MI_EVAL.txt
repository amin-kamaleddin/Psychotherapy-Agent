# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalized ambivalence, validated Sofia's experience and gave her control over the session focus.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displayed strong reflective listening and perspective-taking about function and benefits of use, but missed some opportunities to probe deeper emotional nuance (shame, fear, family expectations).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on marijuana use as a coping behavior and moved the session toward a concrete, time‑limited experiment and measurable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicited Sofia's input at every decision point and co‑created tools and plans, treating her as an equal partner in problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, honored Sofia's readiness level, and provided options and advice with permission to reinforce her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Sofia's own ideas and a small experiment, but could have more deeply drawn out intrinsic motives, values, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative throughout, invited Sofia to design the plan, and shared power in planning and support strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited change language using the readiness ruler and action questions, but could better amplify and sequence desire/ability/reasons/need to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double‑sided reflections to open space for change, though more targeted discrepancy work linking to values/future goals would strengthen impact.
