# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating; offers practical support without criticism and normalizes Dylan's feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing Dylan's concerns, but could probe deeper into underlying emotions and ambivalence with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on the conduct referral and preparation tasks, though does not broaden the focus to longer-term behavior-change targets like safer drinking or responsibility.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—asks permission, co‑writes the statement, rehearses together, and integrates Dylan's input and preferences in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Dylan's choice and offers options without pressure, explicitly leaving decisions (e.g., mitigation or denial) in his hands.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits immediate goals and briefly asks about semester fit, but does not systematically draw out Dylan's motivations, values, or discrepancy between goals and behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the work as a partnership, inviting Dylan's perspective, following his lead, and sharing power in decision-making and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicits very little pro-change language and focuses mainly on preparing a defense; there is minimal exploration of values, consequences, or reasons for changing behavior.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Largely accepts and supports sustain talk by helping craft denials and rehearsals, without using strategic reflections, double-sided reflections, or eliciting discrepancy to soften sustain talk.
