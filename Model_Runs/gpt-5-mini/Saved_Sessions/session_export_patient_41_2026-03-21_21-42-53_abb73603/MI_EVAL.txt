### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Elira’s experience, validating effort and avoiding judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate understanding and reflective listening by naming emotions and somatic cues, which deepened the client's expression and validated her struggle.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing avoidance with a single, concrete behavioral plan (10-minute start, script, timer, fallback) and used scaling to gauge progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—the clinician repeatedly solicited Elira’s input, co-designed the plan with her, rehearsed language, and offered practical help like formatting the message.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Elira's sense of choice by offering options, giving permission to stop after ten minutes, and checking that the plan fit her preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evoked internal motivation through evocative questions, inviting options, and having Elira rehearse the plan to strengthen commitment and ownership.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong power-sharing by inviting preferences, deferring to Elira’s judgment, and offering options rather than directives throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk via rehearsal, low-pressure options, and concrete planning, which increased commitment; could further deepen by exploring Elira’s personal reasons and values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward change using normalization and low-pressure choices, but could more often use double-sided or strategically flipped reflections to further reduce resistance.
