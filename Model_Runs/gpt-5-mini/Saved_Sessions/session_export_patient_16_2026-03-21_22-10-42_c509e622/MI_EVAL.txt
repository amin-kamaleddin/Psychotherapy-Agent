# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalized priorities and validated ambivalence without shaming; worked from the client's goals rather than confronting minimization of risk.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening with accurate reflections and check-ins; could deepen affect-focused reflections and use double-sided reflections to capture more emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on vaping and protecting the season, repeatedly returned to concrete behavioral strategies, and guided the session toward a short-term actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed—offered options, invited role-play, and frequently solicited the client's input while giving space to decline or reshape suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by asking permission, offering multiple options, avoiding pressure, and adapting to the client's preferences and limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation using a readiness ruler and exploring triggers/reasons, but could draw out deeper personally meaningful reasons and more systematic DARN-C elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a collaborative partner—asked permission to brainstorm, deferred to the client's judgment, and invited the client to choose strategies and language that felt authentic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evoked and reinforced change talk with readiness ratings, summaries, and strategy selection/role-play; missed opportunities to deepen desire/reason/need language and elicit firmer commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated the pull of vaping while pivoting to priorities and alternatives; could strengthen impact with more double-sided reflections to gently tip ambivalence toward change.
