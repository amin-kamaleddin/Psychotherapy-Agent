# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful—opened warmly, normalized struggle, affirmed persistence, and celebrated small wins, conveying clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shown accurate understanding with solid reflective listening and targeted questions about triggers and past attempts; could use more complex feeling reflections and deeper exploration of emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, sustained focus on smoking cessation with agenda-setting and concrete behavioral plans (10‑minute delay, scripts, rehearsals) and resisted topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co-creative and egalitarian—asked the patient to choose focus and strategies, co-authored scripts, and engaged in joint rehearsal throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and framing the experiment as reversible and patient-selected.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons, importance/confidence ratings, and invited a self-chosen experiment, but could strengthen by asking more values-based and discrepancy-eliciting questions to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the patient as the expert, shared power in decision-making, and invited the patient's input when co-creating plans and role-plays.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change language with scales, support for small steps, and a concrete experiment; could more actively amplify reasons and elicit commitment/confidence-change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and redirected sustain talk to manageable tactics and experiments; could further diffuse resistance with double-sided reflections and explicit validation of ambivalence.
