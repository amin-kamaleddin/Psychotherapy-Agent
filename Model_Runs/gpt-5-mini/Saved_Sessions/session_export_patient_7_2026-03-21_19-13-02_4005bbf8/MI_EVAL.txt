# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing privacy and avoiding labeling or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and presence with brief reflections, but could deepen empathy by naming emotions and using more complex reflections to capture underlying experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains an appropriate focus on engagement and safety and repeatedly returns to nonpressuring offers; could more explicitly summarize and suggest a very small next step when cues allow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as collaborative by offering choices and deferring to the patient's preferences, though the clinician does most of the talking and seldom invites co-creation of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and control throughout, repeatedly reinforcing the patient's ownership of timing and content and avoiding pressure or direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited elicitation of internal motivation or values; missed opportunities for permission-based or hypothetical prompts, scaling questions, or reflections that might draw out change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance by offering options and asking what would be helpful, positioning the patient as decision-maker; could strengthen by explicitly soliciting the patient's expertise on what helps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal efforts to elicit language favoring change—offers options but no evocative questions or strategies to elicit desire, reasons, need, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk and respects boundaries with neutral acceptance and offers to stay present; could occasionally use strategic double-sided reflections to gently invite consideration of change.
