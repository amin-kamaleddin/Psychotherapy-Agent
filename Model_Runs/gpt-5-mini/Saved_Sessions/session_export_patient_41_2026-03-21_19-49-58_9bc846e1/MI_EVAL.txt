# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing feelings and affirming efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening with empathic reflections and frequent, sensitive safety checks that captured emotional tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the concrete target behavior with every intervention tied to the action plan and troubleshooting steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with joint decision framing and options, but the clinician often led by proposing specific scripts and solutions rather than fully co-creating them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supported choice by offering multiple options, inviting the patient to select steps, and emphasizing voluntary control without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some commitment language and small change talk, but relied on prescriptive plans and missed opportunities to draw out the patient's own values, reasons, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements were present, but the clinician at times assumed an expert role by prescribing exact wording and sequences rather than fully sharing power in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and reinforced small commitments and change-oriented language, though could have used more open questions to deepen the patient's own reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing doubt, offering smaller alternatives and contingency plans, and gently redirecting toward manageable next steps.
