### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Mateo’s experience, validating his pain and discouragement without blame. This created a safe stance for exploration throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening that captures both practical barriers and emotional tone, helping Mateo feel understood. Reflections and summaries deepen disclosure and rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, systematic focus on the target behavior (mailbox walk) and guides the conversation from broad concerns to a concrete, achievable plan. Progressively narrows to specific supports, stop-signals, and timing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is co-constructed with frequent invitations for Mateo’s input and approval of supports (note, text, role-play). The clinician builds on Mateo’s ideas rather than prescribing, demonstrating genuine teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy through option-giving, permission language, and asking before offering help (e.g., drafting notes). These moves reinforce Mateo’s control over decisions and pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Mateo’s own motivations and resources by asking about past supports, drawing out specific change language, and using a confidence scale. Techniques consistently draw motivation from Mateo’s values and experience.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance that cedes expertise to Mateo, inviting choice and co-creating the plan (e.g., selecting day/time, supports). Offers tangible, approved supports and integrates Mateo’s preferences at each step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces Mateo’s change language with open questions, concrete planning, and follow-up (confidence ruler, what would raise it). Responses amplify desire, ability, reasons, and commitment for the mailbox walk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes doubts and sustain talk without amplifying it and uses a safety-framed plan to mitigate risk. Could more often use double-sided reflections and strategic redirects to convert ambivalence into stronger commitment.
