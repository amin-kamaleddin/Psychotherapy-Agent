# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing Tyler's perspective and asking neutral factual questions. This conveys unconditional positive regard and reduces threat.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and perspective-taking, validating Tyler's feelings and priorities. Opportunities were missed to explore deeper emotions (shame, fear) beyond brief, solution-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on substance use and the immediate conflict with Tyler's mother, steering toward concrete, actionable strategies. Emphasis is on short-term problem management rather than linking behavior to longer-term goals or values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as genuine joint problem-solving: clinician invites input, adapts scripts, and role-plays with Tyler. Tyler is given agency to choose phrasing and decline elements he dislikes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Tyler's choice and agency by offering options, asking preferences, and inviting tweaks to scripts. Clinician avoids pressuring Tyler and reinforces his ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited effort to draw out Tyler's own motivations, values, or reasons for change; clinician quickly shifts to offering language and behavioral tips. Few questions or reflections were used to elicit internal motivation or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and invites participation in planning and practice. Occasionally moves quickly into advice-giving without fully co-creating options with Tyler.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to elicit change-leaning language and offer choices, but the session mostly shifts into problem-solving and scripts. Missed opportunities to amplify brief change statements or explore underlying motivations and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and rolls with resistance, which prevents entrenching sustain talk, but does not consistently use strategic techniques (double-sided reflections or discrepancy) to shift sustain talk toward change. Focus on compromise and practical scripts reduced opportunities to soften sustain talk.
