# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language from the start, normalizing ambivalence and affirming worth throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, often complex reflections that named emotions and context and repeatedly validated Elena’s experience, demonstrating strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on contraception and immediate risk-reduction steps, repeatedly tying questions and reflections back to Elena’s decision needs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed—clinician invited Elena’s input on content and format, prioritized her preferences, and built a one-page summary together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and offered options rather than directives, explicitly supported delay in decision-making and affirmed that Elena remained in charge.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons for wanting to avoid pregnancy and preferences, but did not deepen intrinsic motivation with scaling, values exploration, or elicitation of a specific commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine power-sharing by asking for Elena’s priorities, inviting her to shape the one-page content, and centering her expertise about her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reflected change-relevant language and choices, yet missed opportunities to probe for deeper reasons, confidence/ability language, or to elicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized hesitation, redirecting to practical steps; could strengthen practice by using double-sided reflections or explicit discrepancy to tip sustain talk toward change.
