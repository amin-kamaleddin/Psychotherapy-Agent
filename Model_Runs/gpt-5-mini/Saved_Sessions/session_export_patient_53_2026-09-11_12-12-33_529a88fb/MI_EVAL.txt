# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing ambivalence and reducing shame. The clinician affirms Isabella's perspective without blaming or directive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking with accurate reflections of hesitancy and practical needs. Reflections focus more on logistical barriers than deeper emotional affect or evocative empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and repeatedly narrows to actionable tiny steps (e.g., click link, five minutes). Questions and summaries consistently return to planning and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as joint problem-solving with frequent invitations for Isabella's input and preferences. The clinician follows her lead and offers assistance in ways she chooses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering multiple options and asking which she prefers. Help is framed as optional, preserving Isabella's ownership of decisions and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes practical, implementation-focused reasons and specific ideas for small steps. Missed opportunities to probe deeper intrinsic motives, values, or to assess confidence/importance to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Isabella as the source of preference and choice, repeatedly asking what she wants help planning. The clinician demonstrates clear power-sharing and collaborative decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and scaffolds incremental change talk, converting openness into specific tentative intentions. Could more often amplify tentative change statements and elicit commitment/confidence (e.g., scaling) to solidify intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by neutral reflection and redirecting to very small next steps. Could further reduce sustain talk using double-sided reflections and selectively amplifying weaker sustain statements while reinforcing change language.
