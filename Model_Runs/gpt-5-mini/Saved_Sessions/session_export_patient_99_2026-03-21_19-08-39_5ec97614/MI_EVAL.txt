# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—praises the patient, normalizes slips, and affirms safety without shaming, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good attunement by eliciting the patient's story and validating realistic plans, but offers relatively few complex reflections of emotions or ambivalence that would deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use and its relation to the ankle, with concrete, measurable tasks (2‑week log, tracking template) and a specific follow‑up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites input on scheduling and check‑in priorities and negotiates the plan, but often provides ready‑made tips and templates instead of co‑creating strategies with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, normalizing missed days, clarifying record-sharing, and inviting the patient to choose techniques without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits factual information and a commitment to tracking but uses few evocative questions to draw out the patient's values, reasons, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters partnership through permission-seeking and option-offering, but leans toward expert input with detailed instructions rather than fully co‑constructed planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully secures some change talk (agreement to track) but does not consistently elicit or amplify the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence before offering strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing for continued drinking but makes limited use of MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided or complex reflections) to soften sustain talk and explore ambivalence.
