#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits reasons for change and uses importance/confidence scales, leading to concrete steps, but often shifts to providing information rather than deeper questions/reflections to draw out intrinsic motivation. Could more consistently elicit past successes and values to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly fosters client expertise and shared decision-making with frequent invitations for input and collaborative planning. Offers practical supports (role‑play, scripts, clinic help) tailored to Mei's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk (importance/confidence, choosing contraception) but misses multiple opportunities to amplify and deepen Mei's voiced reasons and hopes. Tends to move to information rather than using follow-ups or reflections to strengthen change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and offers alternatives, but there is limited active work to soften ambivalence (few double‑sided reflections, reframes, or strategic summaries). Session moves quickly to practical planning without fully eliciting the two sides of Mei's ambivalence.
