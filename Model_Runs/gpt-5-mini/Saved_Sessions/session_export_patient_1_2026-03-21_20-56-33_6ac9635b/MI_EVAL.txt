# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed the patient’s autonomy and efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening repeatedly, capturing pride, uncertainty, and ambivalence in ways that validated the patient’s experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on mini cigars and oral health, guiding the conversation toward practical, behavior-linked harm-reduction steps and a specific numeric goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a cooperative exchange by soliciting the patient’s input at each step, offering options, rehearsing responses, and letting the patient shape the final plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported choice by offering options (information vs. exploration), avoiding pressure, and reinforcing that change decisions belong to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew out the patient’s own motivations and resources with open questions and scaling, then used those elicited reasons to build a patient-centered plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shared power and co-constructed an action plan by inviting preferences, rehearsing language together, and collaboratively selecting doable strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited change talk and concrete planning (goals, confidence rulers), but missed some opportunities to more actively amplify and deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation, validated minimizing statements, offered neutral brief information and harm-reduction options that respected sustain talk while steering toward change.
