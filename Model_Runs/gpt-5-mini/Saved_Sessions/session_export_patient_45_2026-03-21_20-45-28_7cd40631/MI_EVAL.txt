### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, using validating reflections that help Erik soften and engage. Warm, respectful language is sustained throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, repeatedly paraphrasing Erik’s concerns (side effects, cost, feeling of failure). Reflections are well-attuned and deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol, diet, and medication management and moves from exploration to concrete action planning. Session progresses to specific, achievable steps and a practical doctor script.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a true partnership with co-created goals and shared work (e.g., drafting the doctor script). Clinician regularly invites Erik’s input and choice about next steps.

**Comments:** Effectively elicits Erik’s own motivations and concrete plans rather than supplying reasons for change. Could further strengthen evocation by exploring values, importance, and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance throughout, jointly deciding goals and co-creating materials like the one-page script. Language positions Erik as an equal contributor to the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and shapes concrete change talk (two alcohol-free days, protein breakfast) and affirms willingness. Missed opportunities to more systematically amplify, reflect, and summarize commitment language or ask scaling questions.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing and uses double-sided reflections and options to reduce defensiveness. Could more actively pivot sustain talk toward change with targeted reflections pairing the sustain and change sides.
