# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's dual concerns without blaming or minimizing; maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures both content and feeling, prompting the patient to elaborate and feel heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on symptom management and addressing cancer worry, guiding the session toward concrete decisions and implementation details without wandering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the conversation as a joint endeavor and invites preferences while co-creating steps, though occasionally provides directive wording/options that shift some control back to the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering multiple options, asking what feels manageable, and involving the patient in drafting referral wording without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own reasons and specific, patient-generated steps and links them to values, but misses opportunities for deeper evocation (e.g., scaling confidence/importance, exploring past successes).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative and power‑sharing through choice offers and co-created steps, but at times the clinician shapes the plan more than fully co‑creating it with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, secures a concrete implementation intention and short commitment, but could better amplify and explore confidence/importance to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validates and contains sustain talk about cancer worry but largely accedes to testing requests rather than exploring pros/cons or the limits of further investigations, which may reinforce sustain talk.
