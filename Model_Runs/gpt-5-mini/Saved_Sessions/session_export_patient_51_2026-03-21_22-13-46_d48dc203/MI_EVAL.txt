# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys respect and nonjudgmental support with warm, validating statements and affirmations of Ingrid's actions. Some supportive remarks were brief and task-focused rather than offering deeper normalization of feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking by acknowledging time pressure and offering practical options tailored to Ingrid's situation. Missed a few opportunities for deeper reflective listening of her worry and emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains tight focus on medication safety, serotonin syndrome risk, and immediate practical steps throughout the encounter with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invites Ingrid's input, co-creates texts and ER phrasing, and repeatedly asks what she wants next, demonstrating strong joint problem-solving. 

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, asks permission before modifying messages, and cautions against unilateral medication changes, supporting patient choice and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some attempts to elicit what would help but generally focuses on pragmatic actions rather than drawing out Ingrid's reasons, values, or confidence. Brief evocation questions could have deepened internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making by asking preferences and co-constructing messages, though could more explicitly acknowledge Ingrid's expertise and negotiate a follow-up plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supports immediate protective actions and praises sensible steps but does not actively elicit or amplify Ingrid's own change language about importance or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing reasons against change and reframes toward safety and action with practical alternatives. Could further use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge any ambivalence.
