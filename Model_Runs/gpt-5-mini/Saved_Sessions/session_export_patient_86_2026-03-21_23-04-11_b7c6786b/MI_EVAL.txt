**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Marco's stance, avoiding judgment and lecturing throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, repeated reflections that captured both content and tone, helping Marco feel understood and heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking reduction via a concrete one-week experiment, tying questions back to actionable, behavior-linked goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Solicited Marco's input at each step and co-constructed the plan, positioning the clinician as a helper rather than an authority.

#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control, honoring boundaries (no calls, no nagging) and letting Marco pick methods and terms.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicited practical motivations and specific steps but did not probe deeper values, importance/confidence ratings, or stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as co-created, invited Marco to design the one-page plan, and explicitly shared power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk by offering a low-pressure experiment and options, though could have further amplified and summarized change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully rolled with resistance and avoided arguing, reducing defensiveness; could add more double-sided reflections to explicitly contrast sustain and change talk.
