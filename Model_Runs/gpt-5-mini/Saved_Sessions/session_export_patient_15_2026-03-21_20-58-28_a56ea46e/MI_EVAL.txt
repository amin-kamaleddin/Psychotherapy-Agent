# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth with validations and nonjudgmental language. Normalizes ambivalence and respects the patient's perspective throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and summarizes key content well. Could deepen empathic work by adding affective and values-based reflections to engage emotion and meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol and blood pressure and moves the patient toward a specific experiment and follow-up. Elicits concrete measures, start date, supports, barriers, and a backup plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—invites the patient's ideas, checks preferences, and co-constructs the plan. Avoids an expert-dominant stance and uses mutual problem-solving language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by asking permission, offering options, and emphasizing the patient's choice. Respects the patient's decisions and elicits what they are willing to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical motivations and resources (relaxation, supports, small wins). Could probe more for personally meaningful reasons, values, and stronger self-generated commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and shares expertise while co-creating plans and supports. Could further foreground the patient's expertise by prompting them to generate more solutions before offering suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and cultivates change talk leading to agreement on a three-night experiment and identified supports. Would be strengthened by more selectively reflecting and amplifying the patient's own change language to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses reflective, nonconfrontational language, pivoting to possibilities and experiments. Could more actively soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections and reframing to reduce its influence.
