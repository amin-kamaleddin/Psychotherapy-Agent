# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect (validated the 45‑minute wait), honored time constraints, and maintained a nonjudgmental tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used solid reflective listening and perspective‑taking (acknowledged irritation and time pressure), but could deepen emotional empathy with more complex reflections of stress or fatigue.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on standing during calls and steadily narrowed to a single, measurable implementation plan (cues, timing, start date) without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative—co‑constructed the plan through back‑and‑forth problem solving and integrated the patient's concrete solutions and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported the patient's sense of choice by offering options, inviting prioritization, and avoiding pressure while acknowledging the right to tweak or abandon the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly drew motivation and solutions from the patient with open questions that elicited reasons, benefits, and self‑generated strategies rather than supplying advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the work as co‑created using inclusive language and repeatedly invited the patient's expertise, deferring to his judgment in decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and supported change talk with open questions and concrete options leading to specific plans, but could better amplify and summarize desire/reason/confidence language and elicit importance/confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected it toward workable solutions and backups, though could more actively use double‑sided reflections and explicitly validate sustain talk while linking it to change reasons.
