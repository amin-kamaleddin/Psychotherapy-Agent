### Acceptance

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking capture Marcus’s concerns and worries, though reflections often restate content and could probe deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on blood glucose monitoring and steers the conversation toward a specific, achievable one-week plan with concrete steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Elicits Marcus’s own solutions and some motivating reasons (alarms, backups, photo method), but could more deliberately probe intrinsic values and confidence to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructed session with active power-sharing—questions repeatedly ask Marcus to choose what feels most likely to work for him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
