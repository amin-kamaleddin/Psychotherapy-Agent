# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental support, validated readiness, and affirmed the patient's choice throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Several empathic moves and validations were used, but deeper reflective listening and exploration of feelings and sustain talk were limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician kept a clear, tight focus on vaping and a concrete goal (no vaping during season) and linked all interventions to that target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with co-created plans and wording edits, though the clinician offered comprehensive solutions early instead of eliciting the patient's ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offered choices, treated the plan as the patient's decision, and respected edits and preferences, supporting strong autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited (goal, confidence rating, start date), but the session shifted into advising and planning rather than systematically drawing out underlying motives and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician fostered shared decision-making through choices and co-construction, but could increase partnership by asking permission before offering advice and eliciting the patient's solutions earlier.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was reinforced and commitment steps were elicited, but more open questions to deepen reasons, importance, and convert desire into stronger commitment were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and the clinician redirected to change, but did little to explicitly acknowledge or explore the perceived benefits of vaping using double-sided reflections or roll-with-resistance techniques.
