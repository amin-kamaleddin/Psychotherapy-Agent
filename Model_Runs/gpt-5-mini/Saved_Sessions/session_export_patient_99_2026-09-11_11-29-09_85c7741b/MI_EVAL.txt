# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys nonjudgmental warmth and normalizes Matteo's competing priorities, accepting his pragmatic choices without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately capture Matteo's situation and practical concerns, but are mostly content-level and could be deepened with more affective or complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, consistent focus on safer drinking with each question and reflection tied to actionable steps and a specific time-limited plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative and co-constructed, repeatedly soliciting Matteo's preferences and building a plan that reflects his priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician actively fosters choice by offering options, asking which feels most doable, and framing the plan as Matteo's decision rather than giving directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits concrete reasons for change (fewer hangovers, protect ankle, keep social life) but could probe deeper values and elicit confidence/commitment scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is framed as shared problem-solving with repeated invitations for input, though the clinician could more explicitly highlight Matteo's expertise and invite him to lead planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Therapist effectively elicits and amplifies change talk into specific planning, but could more consistently amplify spontaneous change language and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoids reinforcing sustain talk by acknowledging ambivalence and pivoting to practical strategies, though double-sided reflections could better resolve ambivalence and increase discrepancy.
