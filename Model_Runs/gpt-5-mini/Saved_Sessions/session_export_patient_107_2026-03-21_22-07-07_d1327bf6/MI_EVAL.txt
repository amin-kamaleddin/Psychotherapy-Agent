# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed respect and positive regard, normalizing doubts and validating the client’s preferences without judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with frequent reflections and summaries that captured both sides of the client’s ambivalence and concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on coffee use and its effects, moving the conversation from values/ambivalence to a concrete, measurable behavioral experiment and check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in equal partnership by soliciting preferences, incorporating client constraints into the plan, and using co‑constructive language to build the test together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and presented options, asked permission before suggestions, and fostered ownership so the client chose and committed to the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client-generated motives and success criteria (e.g., how they’d judge results) but could have probed deeper into intrinsic reasons and used scaling to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance throughout, inviting the client to choose, tweak, and agree on the plan while sharing responsibility for follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited a specific, low-effort commitment (half‑caf trial) and asked evaluative questions, but could have amplified and deepened the client’s sparse change statements and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not confront sustain talk, using double-sided language to soften it, though there were missed chances for stronger double‑sided reflections or a pros/cons summary.
