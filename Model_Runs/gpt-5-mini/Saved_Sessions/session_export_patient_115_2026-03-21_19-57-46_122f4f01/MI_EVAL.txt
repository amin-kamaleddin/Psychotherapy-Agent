# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent affirmations and validation; tone remained warm and supportive throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and reflective listening with concrete reflections, but missed opportunities to deepen emotional exploration of fear and family/work concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on asthma-related targets with a concrete agenda and actionable next steps (inhaler technique, triggers, spacer, action plan, NRT, follow-up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely a partnership with shared planning and tailored recommendations, though occasional expert-led checklists and scripted instruction reduced mutual shaping at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supported patient choice and asked permission before offering plans or coaching, reinforcing ownership of decisions and avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-related statements and a practical plan but did not consistently probe the patient's deeper motives, values, or confidence to build intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited collaboration, practiced skills together, and co-produced scripts and follow‑up plans, though at times the clinician took a more directive teaching role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created opportunities for change talk and reinforced agreed steps, but did not systematically evoke or amplify the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and accepted 'not ready to quit,' affirming partial progress and offering pragmatic alternatives; could further use double-sided reflections to address ambivalence.
