# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrated unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, and validated honesty without blame or shaming throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that captured Adrian's perspective and triggers, but missed deeper exploration of shame and the emotional nuance behind secrecy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on meth use and infection risk and moved to concrete harm-reduction steps, though offering multiple lists briefly shifted toward information provision and could be streamlined.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with frequent solicitation of Adrian's preferences and a negotiated, co-created two-week experiment rather than imposed directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by asking permission, framing suggestions as optional experiments, and reinforcing Adrian's ownership of choices and planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited change-related statements and used scaling and barriers exploration, but could probe intrinsic reasons and amplify brief change statements into stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated true partnership—power-sharing, co-creation of wording and plan, and adaptation to Adrian's low-drama style throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and shaped specific change talk and a concrete experiment, but could further draw out deeper reasons, values, and stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and neutrally reflected minimizations while shifting to manageable risk-reduction, though could stronger use double-sided reflections to pivot more explicitly toward change.
