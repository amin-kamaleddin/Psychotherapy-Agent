# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys warmth, unconditional positive regard, and normalizing language while praising workable steps; no signs of blaming or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases and summaries of barriers and supports; could deepen empathic responding by naming emotions earlier and using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors and moves the session logically from values to concrete action (scaling, scheduling, backups) without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as collaborative problem-solving with co-created plans, iterative drafting of the email, and frequent solicitation of the client's preferences and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure while respecting hesitations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits values, intrinsic reasons, and supports using scaling and prompts, but could further invite the client to articulate longer self-motivational statements about change impact.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power with the client—offers help after eliciting preferences, iterates based on input, and centers the client's decision-making throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces specific change talk through scaling and concrete planning, though there were missed opportunities to amplify brief change statements into stronger self-statements or ask for an explicit commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to practical recovery plans and micro-practices, but could more often use double-sided reflections and explicitly link sustain reasons to values.
