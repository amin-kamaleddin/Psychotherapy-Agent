# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating throughout the session, normalizing Erik’s concerns and accepting his skepticism about medication, which conveyed clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening and summaries that matched Erik’s language and emotional tone, helping him soften and engage.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on diet, alcohol, and diabetes, narrowed to a practical target (night-shift food) and moved logically to specific, measurable steps like a three-shift trial and pack list.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operated as a collaborative partner by checking preferences and inviting input, but often shifted into an expert-led approach by offering detailed recommendations and practical instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, asked which options Erik preferred, and framed suggestions without pressure, validating his control and honoring his pace and hesitations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Erik’s motivations and reasons for change and invited planning, but could have probed values, confidence, and commitment language more consistently to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative and co-created a doable plan, yet the clinician sometimes provided solutions and a detailed menu before eliciting Erik’s own ideas, reducing fuller power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (options menu, small-win framing, trial), but could have amplified and summarized Erik’s change statements to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and normalized concerns about meds, though missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or explicit ambivalence summaries to further soften resistance.
