# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance with validating reflections, honoring the patient's wishes and normalizing ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and accurate paraphrases that showed perspective-taking, though some reflections were formulaic and could probe deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking and alcohol use and consistently steered the session toward concrete next steps (tests and follow‑up) without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration throughout—invited the patient's input on tests, scheduling, and contact methods and co‑created the plan rather than prescribing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice with explicit 'no pressure' language and multiple options, repeatedly leaving the final decision to Thomas.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited conditional motives and tied options to values, but could further probe intrinsic motivation with scaling, values elicitation, or evocative summaries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a partner with frequent invitations to choose among options, respected boundaries (not involving the wife), and incorporated the patient's preferences into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and supported change talk using open questions and low‑burden experiments, yet missed opportunities to amplify and deepen the patient's emerging change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reflected sustain talk neutrally while redirecting to small experiments; could strengthen impact with more double‑sided reflections and selective amplification of weak change talk.
