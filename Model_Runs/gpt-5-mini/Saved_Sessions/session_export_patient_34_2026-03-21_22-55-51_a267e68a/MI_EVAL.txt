### Acceptance

**Comments:** Uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Dylan’s perspective repeatedly, but at times drifts into implicit endorsement by helping script avoidance strategies rather than holding space for responsibility or consequences.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate paraphrasing that mirrors Dylan’s concerns, yet misses opportunities to explore underlying emotions like fear, shame, or defensiveness for deeper empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on resolving the conduct referral and protecting record/housing/GPA, but the direction centers on avoiding sanctions rather than evoking motivation to change the risky drinking behavior itself.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
