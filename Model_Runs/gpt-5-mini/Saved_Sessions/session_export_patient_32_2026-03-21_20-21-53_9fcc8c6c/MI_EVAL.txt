# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance (e.g., opening the session as a safe space, affirming choices) and normalized ambivalence throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking captured ambivalence and consequences accurately, which helped the patient feel heard and prompted further disclosure and planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on preventing repeat DUIs and moved purposefully from brainstorming to selecting a first step, scheduling actions, and rehearsing responses.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a collaborative exploration with frequent invitations for the patient's input, co‑created solutions, and joint practice (role‑playing) of planned responses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced autonomy by offering options, asking permission to practice or draft messages, and avoiding pressure while supporting the patient's choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical motives and strategies and used confidence scaling, but could more often evoke deeper, personally held values and summarize/amplify change talk to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine power‑sharing by treating the patient as the expert, asking preferences for wording, and inviting the patient to choose which strategy to try first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk well (brainstorming options, confidence scale, specific next steps), yet there were missed opportunities to amplify successes and translate desire/ability statements into firmer commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double-sided reflections to mitigate sustain talk, but could more proactively explore and reflect minimizations (e.g., 'it's just beer') and gently juxtapose them with concrete consequences to resolve ambivalence.
