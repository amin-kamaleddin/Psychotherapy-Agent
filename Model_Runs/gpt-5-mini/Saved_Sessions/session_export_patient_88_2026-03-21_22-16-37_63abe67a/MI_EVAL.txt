# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone (e.g., thanking Marcus for honesty, normalizing his drinking) and avoided admonishment while offering practical help.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective summaries and open questions to show understanding (e.g., linking drinking to grades/college) but reflections were somewhat transactional and missed deeper emotional/identity exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on underage drinking and risk-reduction tied to Marcus's priorities, consistently steering toward concrete, time-limited steps and avoiding digressions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, solicited Marcus's preferences at each step, and co-constructed practical strategies (refusal lines, buddy signal) with shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly preserved Marcus's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission before advising, affirming he need not quit, and inviting his preferred next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Marcus's motivations and self-generated solutions (e.g., harm-reduction ideas and scaling questions) but could use more evocative questions and amplification to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership by treating Marcus as the expert on his life, asking which strategies fit him, and inviting his input on wording and backup plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change language with pros/cons and scaling, and supported tentative commitments, though could more actively amplify spontaneous change talk and draw out DARN statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and redirected to values and low-effort strategies, but could strengthen use of double-sided reflections and elicit Marcus's own expression of the costs of sustain talk.
