### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic reflections that captured content and emotional tension, but often moved quickly to strategy and missed opportunities to explore deeper affect or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on a single concrete target (repairing friendships via a low‑risk message/role‑play) and consistently steered toward actionable steps and safety planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered joint problem‑solving and invitations to role‑play, but frequent clinician‑provided scripts made the interaction somewhat clinician‑led rather than fully co‑created.

### Autonomy Support

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
