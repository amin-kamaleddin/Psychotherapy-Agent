# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Established a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and offered supportive, polite language and availability; warmth was brief and more functional than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provided reassurance and clear safety directives but did not reflect or name the patient's feelings, time pressure, or concerns, so empathic understanding was present but limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a tight, consistent focus on medication safety and switching guidance, answering questions directly and reiterating concrete next steps without drifting from the issue.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened the interaction collaboratively and encouraged follow-up, but moved into an expert, directive stance with definitive medical instructions rather than eliciting the patient's perspective or co‑constructing the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supported patient choice by advising consultation with the prescriber and offering follow‑up, while appropriately using necessary clinical recommendations that made the stance somewhat directive for safety reasons.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did not actively elicit the patient's motivations, confidence, or plans; the patient expressed intent to call the prescriber but the clinician did not draw this out or explore barriers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Started with an invitation to collaborate and offered ongoing availability, but shifted to authoritative medical advice without fully engaging the patient as an equal partner in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Affirmed the patient's stated plan and gave clear advice, but did not use questions or reflections to evoke, deepen, or amplify the patient's own change talk or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There was little sustain talk and the clinician avoided reinforcing inaction, emphasizing safety and concrete steps; could have been stronger by briefly normalizing barriers like time constraints.
