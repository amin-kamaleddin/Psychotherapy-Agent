# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment, offering choices and honoring the client's preferences (e.g., concise summary on request). The clinician normalizes ambivalence and validates limits throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking with accurate, validating reflections and follows the client's cues (e.g., matches request for a short summary). Reflections of barriers and social context are treated nonjudgmentally, supporting clear understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on weekend heavy drinking and practical risk-reduction steps, pacing the session logically toward a concrete near-term plan. Keeps target behavior central without getting sidetracked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving and co-constructs the plan by soliciting the client's ideas and preferences (e.g., which strategy feels doable). Decisions and language choices are shared rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and respecting readiness to test changes without pressuring commitment. Repeatedly honors the client's preferences in pacing and rehearsal.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical motivations and a concrete plan but does not probe deeper personal values or why the change matters beyond immediate risk. Further exploration of intrinsic reasons could strengthen motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Establishes a collaborative stance and invites the client to shape the session (e.g., offering choices about how to proceed and framing suggestions as invitations). The clinician relies on the client's expertise about what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk, securing a specific plan (water swap, leave time) and boosting confidence slightly. Could amplify and selectively reflect change statements more to deepen importance and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids challenging sustain talk and redirects to harm-reduction strategies and coping plans, reducing sustain-talk momentum. Could add more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both sides and help resolve ambivalence.
