# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveys clear nonjudgmental positive regard from the first turn and maintains a warm, validating tone throughout, respecting the client's priorities and constraints.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and accurate reflections of content and barriers are present, though deeper affective reflections that capture emotional nuance could strengthen empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on a specific target behavior (a brief family check-in) with successive questions narrowing to actionable details and a clear, testable experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative with joint problem-solving and co-construction of the plan, repeatedly inviting the client's input on timing, prompts, and backup options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supports choice and invites the client's control over decisions, offering options and avoiding pressuring language to reinforce ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicits intrinsic reasons tied to the client's values and meaningful outcome measures, but could probe more into deeper motivations and past successes to build readiness.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician defers to the client's expertise, asks for her ideas and preferences, and co-creates the plan rather than prescribing solutions, demonstrating a true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation of concrete change talk and invitation to commit are present, though the clinician could more often amplify and reflect change statements and explicitly assess confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and neutralized through practical options and backup plans, avoiding challenge and skillfully pivoting toward feasible implementation.
