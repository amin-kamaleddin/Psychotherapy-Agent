### Empathy

**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on relapse prevention and moved the conversation purposefully from options to a concrete, actionable plan without overriding autonomy.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Session felt co-created with frequent invitations for Elias to choose and tailor options, role-offers, and joint problem-solving language.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicited concrete commitments and practical motivations but relied more on strategies than exploring deeper internal motives, values, or past successes to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and commitments, but missed opportunities to deepen and amplify intrinsic reasons, importance, and confidence language.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
