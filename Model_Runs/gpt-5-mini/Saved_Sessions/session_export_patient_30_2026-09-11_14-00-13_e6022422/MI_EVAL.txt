# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with validating reflections and offers choices; missed a top score because affirmations are more pragmatic than warmly personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking about concerns and practical barriers; could deepen emotional empathy by exploring the meaning or impact of the worry more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the medication decision and preparing for the doctor visit with actionable elements and repeated summaries; no wandering into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as mutual problem-solving with the clinician soliciting input and adapting materials to the patient's stated needs; the patient's choices shape the plan and format.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient's right to choose and offers multiple options without pressure, co-creating resources rather than imposing a single recommendation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical intentions and logistics (3-month trial, question list) but does not probe deeper motivations, values, or confidence; limited amplification of personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the patient as an active collaborator, asking preferences about wording and priorities and sharing power in decision-making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some mild change talk and a concrete plan, but does not strategically amplify or deepen it (no scaling/confidence questions or reflections that highlight reasons for change).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and accepts concerns about side effects, redirecting toward manageable steps and preparation; could further reduce sustain talk using double-sided reflections or selectively amplifying existing change talk.
