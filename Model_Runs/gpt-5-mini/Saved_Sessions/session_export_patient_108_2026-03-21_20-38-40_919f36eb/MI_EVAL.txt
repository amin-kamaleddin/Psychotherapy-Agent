# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, using warm, nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows high-level reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing ambivalence and emotional/physiological reactions with validating summaries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on assertiveness and boundary-setting, repeatedly steering the conversation toward concrete actions like scripting, rehearsing, and picking a date.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving, offers choices, invites feedback and rehearsal, and treats the client as an active partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors the client's choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and affirming the client's role in deciding tone and timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivation and readiness through planning and rehearsal but misses opportunities to draw out deeper intrinsic motives or assess confidence/importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with shared rehearsal and choice, though reliance on clinician-provided scripts sometimes shifts phrasing away from co-creation with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and supports change-oriented language and planning, but does not always amplify or expand spontaneous change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and contains sustain talk and redirects to planning, but could more strategically use double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence and contrast change talk.
