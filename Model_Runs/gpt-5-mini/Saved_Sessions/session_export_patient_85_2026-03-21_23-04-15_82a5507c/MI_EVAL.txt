# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validates feelings, normalizes setbacks, and emphasizes strengths without blame or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate affective and content reflections that mirror language and nuance, deepening the client's expression rather than simply parroting facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused trajectory toward removing juice and relapse prevention, progressing from values to concrete planning, rehearsal, and coping strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint endeavor by inviting brainstorming, offering options, and letting the client shape the plan, consistently positioning the clinician as partner not director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely supports choice and control by offering options, asking what feels doable, and affirming the client's ownership of decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's own motivations, reasons, and strategies through evocative questions, leading to clear change intentions and high confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly solicits the client's ideas and preferences, shares tactics without imposing them, and repeatedly invites the client to choose next steps, demonstrating strong power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with open questions, reinforcement of intent and planning, and summaries that strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects toward planning, but could more often use double‑sided reflections or explore ambivalence to deepen resolution before moving to action.
