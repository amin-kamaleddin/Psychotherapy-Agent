# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through respectful tone, nonjudgmental reflections, and affirmation of the patient's preferences with no prescriptive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures both content and feeling; opportunities exist to probe deeper emotion but the empathic stance is strong.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on increasing low-intensity physical activity with concrete behavioral goals and feasibility checks and no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a genuine collaborator and co-designer of the plan by offering options, inviting patient prioritization, and negotiating decisions while following the patient's lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, avoiding pressure, and explicitly allowing the patient to accept or ignore reminders, fostering ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and strategies, but could deepen evocation by drawing out intrinsic values and probing importance or confidence to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed with repeated requests for the patient's preferences and shared power; plans are proposed as options rather than directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with reflections and invitations to specific steps, though could more frequently amplify strong change statements and elicit confidence/importance ratings to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes barriers into workable options, but relies more on neutral reflections than strategic double-sided reflections or explicit contrasts to more actively resolve ambivalence.
