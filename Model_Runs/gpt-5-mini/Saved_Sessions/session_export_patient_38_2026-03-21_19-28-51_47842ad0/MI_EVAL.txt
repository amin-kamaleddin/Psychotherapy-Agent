# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalized the patient's feelings, and respected limits without judgment throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely empathic reflections and invitations to share were used repeatedly, demonstrating strong attunement to the patient's emotions and perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on post‑scare testing and contraceptive planning, with concrete, relevant tasks and practical logistical support offered without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician used inclusive language, co‑constructed the question list, and adapted phrasing and timing to the patient's preferences, showing a genuine two‑way partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently honored the patient's choice and timing, offered options rather than directives, and provided information to support informed decision‑making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some content was elicited about needs and logistics, but the clinician did little to draw out the patient's internal motivations, values, or confidence regarding change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly framed as collaborative with repeated invitations for the patient's input and shared planning (scripts, clinic search, wording preferences).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were useful offers to help and some prompts, but the clinician mainly provided information and missed opportunities to evoke and amplify the patient's own change‑talk language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and gently redirected toward practical future steps, though they missed some chances for double‑sided reflections to strengthen softening of sustain talk.
