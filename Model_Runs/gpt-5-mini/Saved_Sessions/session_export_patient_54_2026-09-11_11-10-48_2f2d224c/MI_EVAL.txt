# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, repeatedly acknowledges the cultural/familial meaning of wine and frames decisions as the patient's choice.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately summarizes the patient's ambivalence and captures both sides, but reflections are somewhat formulaic and rarely deepen emotional exploration or probe underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy and concrete next steps, repeatedly returning to actionable plans without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner by listing options, checking what is doable, and soliciting the patient's preferred wording and timing to co-construct the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly affirms autonomy throughout, asks what would make the decision feel 'truly yours,' and refrains from directing, reinforcing patient ownership of the choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits logistical commitments and wording but focuses less on eliciting deeper intrinsic motives or scaling confidence and importance to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as collaborative, invites the patient to lead decisions, and supports shared planning that places authority with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change-oriented statements leading to concrete commitments, but could better amplify and summarize underlying reasons, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Normalizes sustain talk by naming both sides and pivots to planning rather than arguing, though it rarely uses double-sided reflections to explicitly soften sustain talk.
