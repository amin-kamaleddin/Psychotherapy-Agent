# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, validating the patient's choices (e.g., Narcan, reluctance to call 911) without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Showed strong perspective-taking and reflective listening, checked safety, acknowledged ambivalence, and addressed emotional and practical concerns with validating responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on harm-reduction throughout the session, tying each intervention to concrete, achievable safety steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt co-constructed: clinician and patient drafted and refined scripts/checklists together and the clinician offered help only with the patient's go-ahead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offered options, sought permission before advising, and respected the patient's choices (e.g., about calling 911), reinforcing the patient's control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited readiness and translated it into action steps but relied more on information-giving than open questions and reflections to draw out deeper personal motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the work as a joint effort, inviting patient input, role-play, and shared decision-making, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced concrete change language and plans, but at times shifted into instruction rather than using reflective techniques to deepen the patient's own change talk and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and redirected sustain talk toward pragmatic harm-reduction, though more use of double-sided reflections or strategic softening techniques could have strengthened this area.
