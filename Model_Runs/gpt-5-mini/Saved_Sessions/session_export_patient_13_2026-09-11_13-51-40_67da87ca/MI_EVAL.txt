# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support through validations and offering choices, avoiding blame or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking about the patient's ambivalence, but could deepen emotional nuance with more complex reflections of overwhelm and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the target behavior (seeking medical evaluation) and steadily progresses to a concrete proximal action (drafting a brief 5‑minute text).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invites patient input at each decision point, adapts phrasing to the patient's voice, and co-constructs the plan so the patient is a genuine partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and respecting the patient's pacing and voluntary tiny steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits preferences and small-step motivation, but could further draw out intrinsic reasons and link the action to broader values or confidence to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as collaborative, shares control over next steps, and centers the patient's expertise in selecting feasible options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change-oriented language and tiny-step thinking and moves toward action, but could more systematically reflect and summarize desire, reasons, and confidence to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change with normalization and low-pressure options, though more double-sided reflections could further weaken sustain statements.
