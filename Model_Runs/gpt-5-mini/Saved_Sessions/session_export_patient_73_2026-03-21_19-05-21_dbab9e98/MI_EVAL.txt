### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and affirms emotions (e.g., ambivalence, small wins), but could probe deeper affect and offer more complex feeling-laden reflections to deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages Linda and co-constructs practical tools and choices, though some detailed recommendations lean more toward clinician-led advice rather than eliciting client-generated solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits motivations (health, cost) and practical commitment, but could more often probe intrinsic motives and amplify Linda’s own change language to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership with permission-seeking and shared decision-making, while some structured lists and recommendations slightly shift the balance toward clinician direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical change talk around methods, triggers, and planning, but does not systematically elicit or amplify intrinsic desire, reasons, and ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers pragmatic strategies, but rarely uses strategic MI moves (double-sided reflections, rolling with resistance) to reframe social or identity-based sustain talk.
