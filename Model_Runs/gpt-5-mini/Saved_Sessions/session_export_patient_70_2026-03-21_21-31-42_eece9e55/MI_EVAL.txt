# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing embarrassment and offering help without blame. Language and tone affirmed Lars' goals and struggles throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing ambivalence and mirroring key concerns. Reflections matched Lars' feelings (boredom, pride/shame, triggers) and helped him elaborate.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on target behaviors tied to recidivism risk and steered toward concrete, time‑bound actions. Repeatedly shaped the plan into specific steps (Friday strategy, time window, backup plan, appointment).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like genuine joint problem‑solving with co‑creation of the plan and inclusive language. The clinician followed Lars' lead (role‑play preferences, choice of strategies) and negotiated details together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Lars' sense of choice and control by offering options and asking him to choose what felt doable. Respectfully honored boundaries (private help with forms) and framed supports as invitations rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Lars' internal motivations and values (staying out of prison, family goals) and scaffolded them into practical steps. Questions about confidence and what would increase it elicited concrete preparatory actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Lars as the expert on his life and shared power in decision‑making, asking him to pick options and negotiate specifics. Co‑construction of the plan (who to call, timing, rehearsal) demonstrated strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk by asking about values, offering choices, requesting a confidence rating, and following up on how to raise it. Reinforced Lars' goals and translated them into actionable, short‑term steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and redirected sustain talk with practical alternatives (call‑list, gym, leaving the flat) but missed some opportunities for more double‑sided reflections. Could more explicitly integrate both sides of ambivalence and probe emotional drivers to further reduce sustain talk.
