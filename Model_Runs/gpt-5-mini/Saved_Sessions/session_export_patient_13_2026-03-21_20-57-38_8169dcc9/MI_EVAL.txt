# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used explicit unconditional acceptance and frequent validating, nonjudgmental language that normalized fear and affirmed the patient’s experience, demonstrating a consistently warm stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective‑taking and reflective listening, capturing both content and emotion (fear about diagnosis, childcare, cost) throughout the interaction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on moving toward a medical appointment and addressing barriers, with every question and offer tied to that target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was co‑constructed with the patient—clinician invited choices, offered role‑play and logistics options, and negotiated plans together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and control by offering multiple options, asking preferences, and honoring the patient’s decisions, reinforcing agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivators and small actionable statements but did not sufficiently probe intrinsic reasons, values, confidence, or past successes to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invited the patient to co‑direct the session, solicited preferences, and deferred to her choices, demonstrating genuine power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited small‑step change talk and offered concrete supports, but missed opportunities to amplify and reflect the patient’s change language and probe commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and reframed steps as manageable, yet could further soften resistance using double‑sided or selectively strategic reflections.
