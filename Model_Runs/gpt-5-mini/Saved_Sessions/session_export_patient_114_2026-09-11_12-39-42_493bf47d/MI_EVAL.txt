# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and validates the client's perspective without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate empathic listening with timely paraphrases and attention to emotional drivers like feeling left out or pressured late at night.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing risky drinking and consistently narrows options to concrete, measurable steps without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine joint problem-solving, checks feasibility frequently, and integrates the client's feedback to co-construct the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces the client's sense of choice and control, invites modifications, and negotiates realistic compromises that preserve client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivation by linking the client's values to concrete options and drawing out self-generated coping ideas and commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative relationship by inviting the client to choose doable steps and offering help only when requested, sharing power and expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk through open questions and progressive prompts, culminating in a specific, client-generated one-sentence plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward planning, but missed opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections and reframes to further weaken sustain arguments.
