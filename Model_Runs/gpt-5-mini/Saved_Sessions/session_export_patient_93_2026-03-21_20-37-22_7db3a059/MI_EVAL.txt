# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and frequent affirmations, validating the patient's priorities and normalizing incremental change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures key concerns, but reflections are often pragmatic and miss deeper emotional exploration of loneliness, grief, or meaning tied to the pub.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol to increase safety and independence, repeatedly returning to concrete behavioral goals and producing a specific, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative with the clinician soliciting and integrating the patient's input at each step, co-constructing message drafts, checklists, and reminders.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician supports choice by asking permission before suggestions, offering multiple options, and checking that wording and plans feel right to the patient, reinforcing ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful elicitation of the patient's own motivations and resources through open questions, scaling, and drawing out values (independence, son's concern) to motivate practical commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing evident—clinician treats the patient as the expert, asks permission, invites choices, and collaboratively drafts and edits plans driven by the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk using concrete questions, confidence scaling, affirmations, and follow-ups that produce and amplify patient commitment and specific plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician avoids confrontation but moves quickly to planning, missing opportunities for double-sided reflections or explicit acknowledgment of ambivalence to soften sustain talk more strategically.
