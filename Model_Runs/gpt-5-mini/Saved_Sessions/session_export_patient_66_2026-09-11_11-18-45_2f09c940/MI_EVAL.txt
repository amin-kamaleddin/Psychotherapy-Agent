# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys respect and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and repeatedly affirming the patient's choices to protect dignity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases, though reflections are sometimes formulaic and could probe deeper affect and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on smoking reduction/cessation, tying questions and reflections to specific, measurable actions throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly two‑way and egalitarian, with the clinician soliciting preferences and adapting the plan to the patient's context so the patient’s ideas drive decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice and control by offering options, accepting reluctance to quit, and repeatedly emphasizing the patient's authority over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and concrete strategies and obtains specific success metrics, but could deepen exploration of intrinsic values and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as collaborative, co‑constructing plans (alarms, cues, tactics) and inviting the patient to choose what feels doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with open questions and requests for concrete steps, though it misses some opportunities to amplify change language and assess confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and does not reinforce sustain talk but does little active softening—few double‑sided reflections or reframes to reduce the pull of reasons for maintaining smoking.
