### Acceptance

**Comments:** The clinician is consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating—normalizing fatigue and acknowledging cautions which models strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and accurate summaries that keep Farid engaged; could increase affective reflections to explore underlying frustration or discouragement more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing brief mid-day activity and progresses logically from elicitation to specific, timed action planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages Farid in joint problem-solving and asks for preferences, but often provides multiple concrete options and wording which shifts some initiative to the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits change talk using scaling and concrete planning that leads to actionable steps, but could probe more for intrinsic motives and amplify the client's own reasons for change.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively uses importance/confidence scaling and asks about small steps which produced change talk; could strengthen and selectively amplify Farid's change statements to elicit firmer commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
