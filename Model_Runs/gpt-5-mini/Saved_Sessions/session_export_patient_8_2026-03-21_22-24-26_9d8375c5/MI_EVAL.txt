# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently communicates respect and normalizes the client’s experience, reducing shame; delivery can feel formulaic with repeated phrases and missed chances for more individualized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and summarizes ambivalence effectively, capturing feelings and barriers; many reflections are surface-level and repetitive, and deeper exploration of underlying emotions would strengthen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on phone use and related outcomes, repeatedly steering toward workable strategies and concrete next steps with little topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is strongly collaborative with frequent invitations for the client to choose options and co-construct the plan; clinician positions themselves as a helper rather than an expert prescriber.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes client choice, offers options instead of directives, and frames setbacks non-punitively, reinforcing the client’s agency throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client’s motivations, values, and past successes to inform change; could more clearly link those elicited motivations to the chosen step and use scaling/confidence probes to prepare for commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the interaction as an equal collaboration, solicits the client’s ideas, and lets the client’s preferences shape decisions and the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk through values questions and concrete options, producing specific change statements; could do more to amplify and summarize strong change talk and probe for commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes sustaining reasons while redirecting toward workable options; rarely uses double-sided reflections or selectively emphasizes costs of current behavior to more strongly soften sustain talk.
