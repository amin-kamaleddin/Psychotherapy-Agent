# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing Seamus's position and honoring his choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and summaries that validated Seamus's emotions and perspective repeatedly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on the patient's chosen target (dental care) and steered toward actionable steps, though could have more explicitly linked the dental goal to longer-term tobacco change when appropriate.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted the session as a team effort by soliciting preferences and co-creating a practical plan (booking and dentist questions), negotiating decisions throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed choice, offered options rather than directives, and respected limits (no lectures), reinforcing client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked motivation tied to appearance and social outcomes but missed some opportunities to deepen elicitation of health-related reasons, importance/confidence, or move toward commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely positioned the interaction as collaborative and offered choices, though at times provided suggestions without explicitly inviting Seamus to co-create wording or alternatives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified change talk about appearance and behavior producing usable statements, but could have more strategically summarized and amplified those statements to build momentum toward intention.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently avoided reinforcing sustain talk, honored Seamus's pushback about medications and lectures, and redirected to his priorities which effectively softened sustain talk.
