# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validated Maria's priorities and constraints, and avoided judgmental language. The clinician normalized concerns and affirmed choices throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries of concerns and goals. Missed opportunities to deepen empathic reflections of affect and resistance by exploring fear and mistrust more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on asthma management and adherence and guided the conversation toward a specific, actionable plan. Repeated summaries and concrete implementation steps kept momentum toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative: the clinician invited input, co-constructed the portal message, and honored Maria's boundaries and preferences. Patient ideas meaningfully shaped the final plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Maria's right to choose and offered options without coercion, supporting her decision to try montelukast and defer inhaler initiation. The clinician provided conditional guidance while respecting patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used questions and reflections that elicited some of Maria's motivation and reinforced incremental commitments. The clinician leaned toward providing factual information and could probe deeper into personal reasons, importance, and confidence to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the interaction as a partnership, asked permission before giving information, and allowed Maria to choose among options. Power-sharing language and co-created solutions supported mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced Maria’s small change talk by asking preferences and confirming implementation steps, leading to clear commitments (montelukast, reminders, two-week check). Could further amplify and reflect her reasons for change and ask targeted importance/confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged sustain-talk concerns, offering non-steroid or lower-steroid alternatives and practical strategies. To improve, the clinician could use more double-sided reflections to surface and help resolve ambivalence.
