# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with multiple validations and normalizing statements, avoids blame, and affirms the difficulty and the client's values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality empathic listening and accurate reflections, mirroring the client's language and attending to emotional tone to capture ambivalence effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, focused target on stopping shoplifting and consistently moves from ambivalence to specific, actionable planning steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed—clinician invites the client's ideas, refines them together, and shapes plans from the client's preferences rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the client's sense of choice and control by soliciting input, offering options, and asking the client to choose what feels doable without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits reasons and past supports for change (values, examples when it worked) but could probe deeper into intrinsic meaning and systematically elicit importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clearly collaborative stance with repeated invitations for the client to choose and tailor strategies, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and amplifies change talk and develops concrete steps, but rarely asks for strength/confidence ratings or explicit commitment language to consolidate change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to change-oriented problem-solving, but relies more on solutions than targeted MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or cost-focused evocation) to reduce sustain talk.
