# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, with normalizing statements and warm validations throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections of ambivalence, but could have probed deeper into the underlying emotions (anxiety/shame) before shifting to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing phone/social media use and moved progressively to a concrete, time-limited SMART experiment with a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was co-constructed with the client: the clinician solicited input, offered options rather than mandates, and integrated Amina's preferences into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice by offering multiple options, asking which felt doable, and framing suggestions tentatively so the client retained ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out motivations and used scaling and client-generated strategies, but could have used more open, evocative questions linking change to broader values or long-term outcomes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance throughout, asking permission to experiment and repeatedly inviting the client's specific input on timing, accountability, and activities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with reflections and scaling questions and affirmed concrete steps, though more frequent amplification and summarizing of the client's change language would strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk using double-sided reflections and validations, but missed some opportunities to strategically leverage sustain language to further resolve ambivalence.
