# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Elena's stress and caregiving role. Accepted minimization and affirmed realistic choices without confrontation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, reflective empathy and perspective-taking throughout, naming competing pressures and mirroring feelings. Double-sided reflections captured ambivalence and sustained empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with concrete, time-bound steps (3‑day tracking, a specific swap, check-in time). Balanced client-centered options with actionable guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a genuine partnership, soliciting Elena's input on choices and adjusting plans to her preferences. Offered supports while deferring to her judgement and decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Elena's choice and control by offering options and accepting her boundary about the patch. Avoided pressure and reinforced her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited pragmatic motivations and willingness to try tracking and a swap, but did not deeply probe values, past successes, or identity-related reasons for change. More targeted questions could have strengthened intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co-created decisions (log format, start day, reminders) and invited Elena's preferences for supports. Tone and actions fostered equal collaboration rather than expert-driven direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk through scaling and offering small, doable steps, producing concrete change language. Missed some opportunities to amplify motivation and elicit stronger commitment language tied to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk skillfully with normalization and double-sided reflections, avoiding argumentation. Reframed focus toward manageable actions which minimized sustain talk and kept momentum toward change.
