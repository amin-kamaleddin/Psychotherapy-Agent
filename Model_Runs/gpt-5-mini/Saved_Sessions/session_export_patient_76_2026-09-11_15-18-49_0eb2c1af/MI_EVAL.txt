# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizing and validating the client's experience without judgment. Repeated affirmations reduce shame and reinforce honesty as a strength.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate understanding with complex and frequent reflections that mirror affect and content. Uses reflective listening followed by invitational questions to deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on reducing self-harm and developing safer coping steps, moving from exploration to concrete implementation. Clinician maintains momentum and specificity in plan development.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions decisions as co-created, inviting input at each step and adjusting proposals to the client's responses. Language and actions consistently frame the clinician as a partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers options and asks which feels most doable, reinforcing the client's control over choices and pace. Clinician emphasizes working with the client rather than prescribing solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own solutions and produces concrete self-generated steps (ice, delay, text), but could probe more for intrinsic motives and amplify mild change talk. Using scaling or deeper elicitation of personal reasons would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invitations like 'I want to figure this out with you' and collaborative drafting of a text foster a genuine partnership. Client expertise is centered and decisions are shared throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits actionable change talk and small commitment language leading to a doable plan, yet misses opportunities to deepen motivation tied to values or to elicit stronger commitment statements. Adding importance/confidence scaling or value exploration would enhance change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk neutrally and redirects to alternatives without arguing, avoiding reinforcement of resistance. Could more strategically use double-sided reflections and explore statements of helplessness to tip toward change.
