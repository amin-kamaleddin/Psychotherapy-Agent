# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating language throughout the session, honoring the client's values and strengths. Praises and normalizes the client's efforts without blaming or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening and perspective-taking focused on goals and plans, with frequent checks for understanding. Missed some opportunities to deepen connection by exploring underlying emotions or the felt meaning of self-respect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on setting and keeping work boundaries, guiding the conversation from values to a concrete time-bound action. Questions and reflections all tie back to the target behavior and implementation planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction models an equal partnership with frequent invitations for client input and choices, and the clinician shapes suggestions around the client's selections. Decisions and wording are co-created and responsive to the client's voice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers options and frames questions to elicit client preference, preserving client control over the plan. Asks whether the client wants help or drafts, reinforcing ownership of next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits the client's own motivations, reasons, and specific strategies through open invitations and follow-ups. Amplifies the client's change language by having them rehearse and commit to concrete steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invites the client's choices, but occasionally offers multiple specific strategies without first asking permission to advise. While options were accepted, more consistent permission-giving would strengthen power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with open questions, requests for specifics, confidence ratings, and rehearsal of scripts. Follows up to deepen commitment and plans, effectively amplifying desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and redirects concerns into practical problem-solving and manageable solutions. When barriers arose, the clinician reframed them into contingency plans and supports rather than debating reasons to maintain the status quo.
