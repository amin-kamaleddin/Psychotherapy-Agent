# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validating and normalizing Jordan's experience throughout the session. Warm, nonjudgmental tone was maintained from start to finish.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that made Jordan feel heard, accurately capturing affect and circumstances. Some reflections were brief or reiterative and missed opportunities to deepen emotional nuance (e.g., shame, grief, anger).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, sustained focus on the agreed target behavior (calling the temp agency) and moved conversation toward concrete, achievable steps. Avoided drifting while remaining noncoercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-constructed the plan with Jordan, soliciting preferences for check-ins and scripting and tailoring supports to his needs. The clinician maintained a partner stance rather than dictating actions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options, asked permission, and respected Jordan's choices (e.g., declining role-play), reinforcing his control over decisions. Fallbacks and alternatives were presented to reduce pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used evocative prompts and reinforced reasons for change (stable paycheck, fewer late notices) to elicit internal motivation. Could have further drawn out values, past successes, and importance/confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly asked which steps felt manageable and honored Jordan's preferences, sharing power in planning and follow-up. Suggestions were framed as options and Jordan's expertise guided the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and affirmed several strands of change talk (envisioning question, scheduling the call, reward), reinforcing commitment language. Could have amplified change talk more actively with double-sided reflections, scaling, or deeper summaries of reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized doubts without arguing, redirecting to manageable options and fallbacks to avoid strengthening sustain talk. More strategic use of double-sided reflections could further resolve ambivalence.
