# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone, validated Marcus's feelings, avoided blaming language, and reinforced strengths throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good empathic statements and some reflections that made Marcus feel heard, but did not deeply explore the emotional toll of repeated setbacks or his discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on increasing sustainable movement with concrete action planning (blocked times, backups, scripts) and guided Marcus to specific next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Felt like a joint effort with solicitation of preferences and iterative refinement, though offering many ready-made plans at times reduced the equal-partner generation of ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Marcus's choice by providing options, asking him to select what felt doable, accepting his tweaks, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete commitments and implementation intentions but relied more on providing solutions than drawing out Marcus's intrinsic motivations, values, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively by offering options and asking which felt doable and adapting language, but occasionally favored an expert role by supplying many prescriptive items without first eliciting priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked and reinforced change talk through choice questions and affirmations, supporting implementation intentions, though could more often reflect and amplify Marcus's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and reframed it into small feasible steps and backups, avoiding argument, but used few explicit double-sided reflections to name both sides of ambivalence.
