# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized the difficulty of change, acknowledging the patient's pace and choices without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the patient's language and capturing both content and emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on smoking reduction with concrete strategies and a clear one-week experiment and measurable indicators.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, inviting the patient's input at every turn and integrating their suggestions into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported the patient's freedom to choose, checked doability, and negotiated backup options that respected the patient's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited values-based reasons and built strategies from the patient's own motivations, keeping the source of change internal and chosen by the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance, treated the patient as the expert on their experience, and co-designed the experiment while checking preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with focused questions and affirmations, reinforcing progress and concrete intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected toward change, but occasionally repeated sustain-language verbatim which risked briefly reinforcing it; could use more immediate reframing to weaken sustain talk.
