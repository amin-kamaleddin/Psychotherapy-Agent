# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support from opening to close, using validation and affirmations without blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking with empathic reflections and clarifying questions that capture emotional and functional concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on rehab, activity, and pain management with a concrete weekly plan, safety rules, and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative—invites input and checks preferences—but often supplies structured plans, leaning toward expert-driven solutions rather than full co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and including explicit pause/substitution rules without coercion or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts some elicitation of motivation (open questions, check-in) but misses deeper exploration of intrinsic reasons, values, and importance/confidence scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the patient to shape the plan and repeatedly checks preferences, though several clinician-driven recommendations were offered before eliciting the patient's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk (reasons, small wins, commitment to a check-in) but does not systematically deepen or sequence desire, importance, and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk with neutral reflections and pacing strategies, but misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframing to more strategically soften resistance.
