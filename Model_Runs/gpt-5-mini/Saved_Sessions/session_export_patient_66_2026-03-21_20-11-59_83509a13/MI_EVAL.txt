# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (opening lines, normalization), helping the patient feel safe to share.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, affect-attuned reflections and summaries that captured the patient's perspective and facilitated elaboration and movement toward change.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction throughout the encounter and moved the patient toward a concrete, actionable plan (start date, if‑then rules).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted the session as joint problem-solving by soliciting input, offering options after elicitation, and co-creating specific strategies and wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's control and choice, offered options rather than directives, and respected the patient's pace and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's own motivations, strengths, and resources using past-success questions, confidence scaling, and trigger identification.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative, power-sharing stance with the patient choosing strategies and the clinician offering help to shape and rehearse those choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and reinforced change talk (small steps, confidence scale, choice of actions) but could have more deliberately amplified and evoked values-based reasons to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated enjoyment/uncertainty while redirecting to pragmatic options, but used mostly neutral reflections rather than advanced softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to reduce sustain talk.
