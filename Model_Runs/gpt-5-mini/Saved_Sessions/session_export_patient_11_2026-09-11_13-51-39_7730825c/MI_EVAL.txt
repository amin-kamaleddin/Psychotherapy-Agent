# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—affirming the patient’s plan, normalizing resistance, and using nonjudgmental supportive language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures content and some emotion, though stronger, more complex reflections probing deeper feelings or values would raise this further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing morning physical activity, guiding the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine teamwork is modeled—patient preferences shape the plan, options are offered, and the clinician checks in about what the patient will actually do.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy by offering choices and backup options, avoiding pressure, and deferring to the patient’s sense of what will work.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivators and specific preparatory change language (energy, confidence, small wins), but could more deeply probe values and strengthen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach—plans are co-created, the clinician repeatedly asks which options feel doable, and decision-making is shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk with reinforcement of readiness and concrete steps, though more consistent amplification and soliciting of commitment/confidence-change talk would improve it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Successfully avoids reinforcing sustain talk by honoring the patient’s preference to focus on activity and offering normalizing, pragmatic options rather than arguing or pushing.
