# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing surprises and slips and offering consistent affirmation without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses reflective listening and validates the patient's worries, but misses some opportunities for deeper emotional exploration of belonging and related feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on alcohol use and moves the patient from awareness to a concrete, actionable goal with clear implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a partner throughout, offering options and co-constructing the plan (scripts, support person, backup signal) rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes patient choice and control by offering alternatives, asking permission, and normalizing slips as part of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies the patient's own motivation and commitment through open questions and planning prompts that deepen change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters power sharing and invites the patient's input at every stage, ensuring the patient shapes decisions and the resulting plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and operationalizes change talk, guiding the patient to specify a goal (three-drink cap) and rehearsed supports.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk and effectively redirects toward harm-reduction, though could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to elaborate ambivalence.
