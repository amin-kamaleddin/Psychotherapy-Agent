# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, validates ambivalence, and avoids labeling, preserving the client's dignity and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates frequent, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both sides of ambivalence and naming emotions; reflections are timely and move the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on avoiding repeat DUI and harm-reduction strategies, linking questions and reflections back to concrete steps to protect license and job.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-creates a plan with the patient by soliciting input, offering options, and engaging in shared problem-solving so decisions arise from the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by presenting options, asking which feels doable, and affirming the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits personal reasons, intentions, and concrete commitments but could probe deeper into underlying values or use scaling/confidence questions to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert and collaborates on specifics (e.g., rideshare vs cash, alarm wording), demonstrating genuine power-sharing throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits multiple instances of change talk and turns ideas into concrete steps (rideshare credit, $30, alarm), but would benefit from more systematic amplification, summarizing, and commitment checks.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and accepts sustain talk while redirecting toward risk reduction, though missed opportunities exist for explicit double-sided reflections and reframing minimizing language like 'it's just beer.'
