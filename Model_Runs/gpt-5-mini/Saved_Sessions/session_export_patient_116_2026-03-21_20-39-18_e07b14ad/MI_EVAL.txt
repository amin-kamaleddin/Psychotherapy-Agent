### Acceptance

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited Samir’s input at each step and implemented his preferences (choosing focus, SMS wording, saving the draft), demonstrating co-constructed decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Samir’s right to choose, sought permission before actions, offered options rather than directives, and respected his timing and boundaries.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Asked open questions that produced some change-relevant statements (e.g., symptoms return), but did not deepen intrinsic motivators or use techniques like scaling, values, or probing pros/cons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

#### **Score (1–5):** 3
