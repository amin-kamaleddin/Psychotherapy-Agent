**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validation (warm openings, repeated affirmations, no blaming). Clinician respects Elias's values and normalizes difficulty throughout the exchange.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening with accurate paraphrases and reflections of frustration and meaning. Could deepen emotional resonance with more complex reflections to amplify discouragement and hope.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing safe, small physical activity and following medical guidance, progressively narrowing to a specific, measurable plan. Repeatedly brings conversation back to concrete implementation (day/time, safety rules, doctor clearance).

**Comments:** Effectively elicits Elias's own reasons and resources (scaling questions, value links) and converts them into concrete steps. Draws out supports (wife, doctor) and uses them to bolster actionable plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Elias as a collaborator with relevant expertise, repeatedly soliciting his choices and preferences. Shares power in planning (timing, wording, safety rules) and checks back frequently.
