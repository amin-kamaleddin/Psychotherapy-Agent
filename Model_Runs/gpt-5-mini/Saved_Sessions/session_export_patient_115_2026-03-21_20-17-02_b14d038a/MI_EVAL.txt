# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, affirming progress and normalizing ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate summaries captured Samir's situation and constraints, though deeper emotional texture (fatigue, resignation, fear) could have been explored more.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear focus on smoking, drinking, and dust tied to asthma and moved logically to concrete, behavioral steps and supports while preserving client choice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction modeled equal partnership with co‑creation of rules and plans, rehearsing language for the doctor and inviting Samir's input on doable options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported from the outset, with permission‑seeking before advice and offering menus of options so Samir retained control over goals and methods.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good elicitation of values, past successes, and confidence ruler, but could have probed deeper into personal motivations and used more imagery to strengthen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance with repeated invitations for Samir's input and shared decision‑making that positioned him as an active agent in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (goals, strategies, confidence ruler) but could have more consistently amplified spontaneous change language and elicited near‑term commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled respectfully with double‑sided reflections and affirmations of partial change, though there were missed opportunities to more actively redirect and elicit discrepancy.
