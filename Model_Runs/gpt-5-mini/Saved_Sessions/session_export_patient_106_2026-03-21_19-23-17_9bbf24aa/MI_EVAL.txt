# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing ambivalence and accepting the patient's limits while supporting incremental steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and acknowledgment of practical and emotional barriers, though reflections were often simple and the clinician moved quickly to solutions rather than exploring emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking and child safety, tying every intervention back to harm reduction and concrete action steps with timing and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with co‑created plans and inclusive language, but at times the clinician provided multiple prescriptive tips without first asking permission.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserved patient choice with permission‑seeking language and explicit reassurance that the patient need not commit to quitting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to draw out motivation and start planning, but the clinician relied more on offering solutions than eliciting deeper personal reasons, confidence, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invitational, co‑creating actionable steps, though partnership was reduced when the clinician introduced detailed strategies without negotiating agenda first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change talk and a concrete harm‑reduction plan, but did not systematically deepen or amplify the patient's own desires, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and moved toward harm reduction, but missed opportunities to use double‑sided reflections or strategic empathy to explore and resolve sustain talk more fully.
