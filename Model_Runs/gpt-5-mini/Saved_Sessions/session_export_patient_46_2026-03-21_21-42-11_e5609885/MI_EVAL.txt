# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—warm opening, normalizing language, and affirmations—while avoiding judgment across the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening and accurate summaries captured ambivalence well, but the clinician missed opportunities to probe deeper emotions (e.g., shame, fear, belonging) to further deepen affective understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session remained focused on adolescent alcohol use and concrete harm-reduction steps (NA drink, backup friend, reminder), consistently returning to actionable goals without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was a clear joint problem-solving process, integrating Ethan’s ideas into the plan and offering suggestions with permission rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offered options, asked permission, and validated Ethan’s choices, supporting his ownership of decisions and avoiding coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effective use of evocative questions and scaling drew out Ethan’s own motivations and converted them into specific, actionable commitments and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician fostered a collaborative stance, treating Ethan as the expert and co-constructing the plan while deferring to his preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Active elicitation and reinforcement of change talk (scaling, evocative questions, planning prompts) produced multiple concrete change statements and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided confrontation and reflected ambivalence, pivoting to planning, but could have used more double-sided reflections and explicit reframes to further soften sustain talk.
