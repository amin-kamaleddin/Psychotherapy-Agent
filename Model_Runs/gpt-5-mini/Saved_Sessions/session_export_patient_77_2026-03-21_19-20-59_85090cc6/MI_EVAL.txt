# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician communicated unconditional positive regard with multiple empathic validations and affirmations, normalizing struggle and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and sympathy, but some didactic lists of causes/solutions limited deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on breaking the plateau with practical, time-limited steps and a two‑week experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reflected equal‑partner collaboration—invited preferences, accepted edits, and co‑constructed scripts and plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing suggestions as optional experiments rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some commitments but relied heavily on providing solutions and scripts, missing opportunities to draw out the patient's own motives, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance with shared decision‑making, checking in and inviting the patient to shape the plan throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged concrete short‑term experiments and reinforced tentative commitments, though could have more explicitly elicited the patient's reasons or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged skepticism and reframed it into a two‑week test instead of arguing, but could use more double‑sided reflections and selective amplification of change statements.
