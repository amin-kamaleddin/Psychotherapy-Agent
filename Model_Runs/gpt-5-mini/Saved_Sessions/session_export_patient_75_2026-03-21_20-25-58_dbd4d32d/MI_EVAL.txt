# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened nonjudgmentally and used validating, respectful language throughout, creating a safe space for disclosure and avoiding blame or labels.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, but could deepen emotional reflection by naming feelings like fear or frustration before moving to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on asthma adherence and safe step-down strategies, consistently steering toward concrete, goal-relevant actions and safety measures.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint process, frequently seeking the patient's input and co-constructing plans (scripts, monitoring, reminders) together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and offered options, asked permission for steps like role-play, and framed plans as collaborative agreements without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivations and commitments and prompted concrete steps, but often supplied solutions early, limiting opportunities for the patient to generate her own reasons or confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invited the patient to choose agenda and rehearsal style, demonstrating genuine power-sharing and respect for the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively invited and reinforced change talk and coached toward achievable steps, though could more consistently reflect and amplify spontaneous patient statements of desire, ability, reason, need, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated and normalized sustain talk and offered compromises, but at times moved quickly into solutions instead of using more strategic double-sided reflections to soften resistance.
