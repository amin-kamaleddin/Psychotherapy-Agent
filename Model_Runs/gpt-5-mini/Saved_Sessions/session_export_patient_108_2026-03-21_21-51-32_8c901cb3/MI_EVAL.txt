# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—validated ambivalence, affirmed progress, and avoided blaming language while remaining supportive even when introducing firm steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid perspective-taking and reflective listening, but missed a few opportunities for deeper, complex reflections exploring guilt, shame, and the emotional cost of being conflict-avoidant.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on reclaiming the flat and setting a move-out date, though at times introduced fairly prescriptive scripts early rather than alternating more reflective consolidation first.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner—co-developed the script, invited client choices about rehearsal style, and adjusted interventions based on Oliver’s preferences and feedback.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly reinforced choice and control by offering options, asking permission to rehearse, and framing suggestions as offers of help rather than mandates.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation (confidence ruler, rehearsals, co-created core script) but could probe values, costs of inaction, and past successes more to deepen intrinsic motivation before supplying solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing and treated Oliver as the expert—invited input on wording, role-play preferences, and iteratively shaped the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change language well (questions about acceptable outcomes, confidence scaling, rehearsal), yet sometimes moved quickly to offering scripts instead of amplifying Oliver’s own elaborations first.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and redirected sustain talk through validation and planning, but could use more double-sided reflections and explicit summaries to strategically resolve ambivalence before offering solution-focused scripts.
