# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental, validating language and explicit empathic statements, signaling strong acceptance despite sensitive content.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and accurately summarized triggers and relief, but missed opportunities for deeper affective and somatic reflections to maximize empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on self-harm and immediate safety throughout, using repeated safety checks and a co-created safety plan to keep the session on target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as an equal partnership by inviting preferences, offering options, and co-constructing the safety plan with the patient, adjusting to her choices and tone.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by framing choices, validating refusals, and emphasizing the patient's control over decisions and action steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical, immediate change-oriented behaviors (texts, contacts) but did not probe values, reasons for change, or confidence to foster deeper intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative practice with repeated permission-seeking, option-giving, and patient-driven content that shaped the safety plan and messages.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of change-oriented actions (safety plan, naming contacts) but limited efforts to systematically draw out or amplify the patient's own reasons, importance, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and acknowledged its function, but underutilized techniques like double-sided reflections or reflective exploration of long-term costs to shift sustain talk toward change.
