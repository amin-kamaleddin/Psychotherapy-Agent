# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental acceptance throughout the session, with frequent affirmations and normalization of struggle. Praises effort and progress, conveying clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate, pragmatic reflections that validate the client's concerns. Could deepen affective/complex reflections (e.g., shame, pride, values) to reach a higher empathic level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction and handling work cravings with each question and intervention tied to that goal. The session builds logically from review to planning to rehearsal without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative co-construction of plans, including rehearsal of scripts and negotiated next steps informed by the client's preferences. Decisions are shaped by the client's expertise and constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy by asking permission, offering options, and inviting the client to choose actions; avoids pressuring language. Reinforces client ownership with fallbacks and choice points.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation and confidence using open questions, scaling, and problem-solving to draw out client reasons and plans. Could probe more for intrinsic motivators, values, and longer-term reasons to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session demonstrates a strong partnership stance with frequent invitations for input and shared decision-making. Clinician defers to the client's judgment and collaboratively refines the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, reflects goals, and prompts concrete, doable steps to build momentum. Missed opportunities include deeper exploration of underlying reasons for change and eliciting more explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges ambivalence and quickly redirects toward planning and coping strategies, thereby diminishing sustain talk. Could use more strategic double-sided reflections and gentler exploration of sustain language to further soften it.
