# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing concerns and avoiding judgmental language. Tone is supportive and nonpunitive throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent understanding by reflecting both content and feeling and linking emotions to behavior. Reflections capture vulnerability and perspective-taking effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior (securing a one‑time call and preparing for release) and sequences actionable steps logically. Conversation stays purposeful without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑creates the plan with the patient, solicits concrete input about phrasing, and acts as an ally rather than a director. Decisions are mutual and the clinician shares power in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks for the patient's preferences, and avoids pressuring language. Respects Tariq's ownership of the plan and reinforces his control over priorities and actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits reasons for change and links desired outcomes to future behavior, drawing out several change-motivated statements. Missed opportunities remain to probe deeper into intrinsic meaning, confidence, and barriers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative with repeated invitations for Tariq's input and prioritization of his goals. Clinician empowers Tariq to choose and participates as a supportive partner in action steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change-oriented statements and asks follow-up planning questions. Could be more strategic in amplifying and summarizing change talk to elicit stronger commitment and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing blame and redirects toward problem-solving and values, preventing sustain talk from dominating. Would benefit from more double-sided or complex reflections to acknowledge blame while guiding responsibility.
