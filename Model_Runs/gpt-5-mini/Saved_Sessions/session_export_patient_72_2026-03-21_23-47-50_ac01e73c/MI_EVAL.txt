# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, avoiding labeling or pressure while endorsing the client's steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases and reflections that tracked the client's language and emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on re-establishing an exercise routine, with questions and reflections tied to actionable steps and commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was collaboratively co-constructed: the clinician repeatedly invited input and shaped the plan around the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered options rather than directives and repeatedly emphasized the client's choice, asking her to select the single step she wanted to commit to.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic motivations and past supports, drawing out the client's own reasons for change and linking the plan to those motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the client as the expert on her life, soliciting which supports to use and how to check in, with the client's ideas directly shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified the client's change language by inviting desired outcomes, strengths, and specific choices, then summarizing commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected toward solutions, but occasionally repeated barrier language without using explicit double-sided reflections to balance ambivalence.
