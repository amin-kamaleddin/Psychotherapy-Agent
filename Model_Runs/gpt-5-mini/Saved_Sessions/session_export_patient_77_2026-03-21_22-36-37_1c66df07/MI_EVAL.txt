# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows clear respect, validation, and affirmation of strengths, creating a supportive climate. An early assumption about family support and some formulaic reflections slightly weaken unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate, emotionally attuned reflective listening that mirrors feelings and adolescent language. Sustained, nuanced reflections enhance rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on confidence-related behavior and narrows to concrete proximal actions. Guides toward measurable signs and a specific plan (song, time, timer).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction feels like a joint exploration with co-constructed planning and frequent checks about what feels doable. Occasional clinician-led suggestions early in the session slightly reduce the sense of equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, avoiding pressure, and asking the client to choose plan elements. Honors hesitancy and validates that not succeeding is possible while affirming the value of trying.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from within by eliciting values, personal signs of success, and past strengths. The sequence from values to specific actions powerfully surfaces intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the patient's preferences and repeatedly asks which options feel doable, reflecting a collaborative stance. At times the clinician offers specific suggestions before eliciting the client's ideas, which shifts some weight back to the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk, labels it explicitly, and moves from values to concrete, measurable steps. Repeatedly reinforces small actionable plans and past successes to bolster change momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by validating doubts and normalizing ambivalence, preventing escalation. Could be more strategic with double-sided reflections and summaries that explicitly link reasons for change to the client’s concerns to further soften sustain talk.
