# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (e.g., empathic statements and normalizing relapse) and offered safety support without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections but reflections were often brief and the clinician moved quickly to problem-solving instead of using deeper, layered reflections to explore helplessness and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on relapse, safety, and access to services with concrete behavior-linked actions (grounding, scripts, roleplay, and booking an appointment).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—used inclusive language, invited shared problem-solving, and co-created scripts and plans with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the client's choice by offering options, asking permission before interventions, and emphasizing agency in next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some practical movement (agreement to scripts/appointment) but relied on solution-focused prompts and missed opportunities to draw out internal motivation, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly conveyed a collaborative stance with client-led decisions, invitations to choose, and co-creation of plans that distributed power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered practical supports that generated limited change talk, but did not systematically evoke or amplify the client's own reasons for change or deepen brief expressions of regret.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized relapse, yet did not use double-sided or strategic reflections to acknowledge hopelessness while highlighting signs of agency.
