# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized concerns, and avoided judgmental language while affirming small steps, creating a warm non‑judgmental atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and emotion labeling that mirrored the patient's language, though a few reflections could probe deeper emotional nuance to increase empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the target behavior throughout and repeatedly steered the conversation to concrete, time‑bound steps (who, when, exact wording, alarms).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner by asking preferences, co‑designing the plan, inviting iteration, and consistently checking what felt doable to the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the patient's sense of choice with multiple options, non‑pressuring language, and normalization of doubt to strengthen perceived autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation by asking about past helpful strategies and building a small experiment, but could better amplify change talk and explore personal values to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly framed the conversation as collaborative, deferred to the patient's expertise about feasibility, and co‑constructed the plan with choice language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and built on change talk with concrete planning and confidence ratings, though more frequent reflection and amplification of the patient's change language would strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and neutrally reframed doubts while pivoting to solutions, but could employ more explicit double‑sided reflections to contain sustain talk and deepen ambivalence resolution.
