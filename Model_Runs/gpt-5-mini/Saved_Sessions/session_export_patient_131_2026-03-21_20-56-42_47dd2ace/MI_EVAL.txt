# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful throughout (opening statement, affirmations, validations), conveying unconditional positive regard and normalizing difficulty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate content and feeling reflections, though reflections rarely probed deeper emotional nuance (shame, fear, cultural/family identity).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on target behaviors (alcohol, accountability, housing) and steered the session toward concrete, actionable steps and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner by co-constructing scripts, negotiating wording, offering choices, and inviting the client to decide how to practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported client choice by offering options, asking permission, and consistently reinforcing Wiremu's control over decisions and practice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values and motivators that produced concrete change statements, but could more consistently draw out and amplify commitment and confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered client expertise and power-sharing through invitations to choose, permission-seeking, and joint planning, making the interaction genuinely collaborative.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified concrete change talk and plans (moving, Stopping Violence, support checks), though could more strategically amplify strongest change statements and elicit explicit commitment/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward change without confrontation, but could use more double-sided reflections and reframing to further soften sustain arguments.
