# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing Erik’s feelings and validating concerns about lifelong medication; demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate, empathic reflections and open questions that show attunement, but misses some deeper reflective opportunities to unpack underlying fears and guarded tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on medication concerns, alcohol reduction, and actionable lifestyle steps, repeatedly returning to concrete next steps and a doable one-week goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction feels like a team effort with solicitation of input and tailored plans, but frequent ready-made solutions limit full co-creation and power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports Erik's choice by offering options and using conditional language and invitations, though some prescriptive framing reduces the emphasis on his independent decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk and asks what the patient wants, but relies more on providing information and strategies than on drawing out Erik's intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Overall collaborative tone and occasional permission-seeking are present, yet the session becomes clinician-driven with prescriptive advice and multiple scripts that limit partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change talk with specific, doable targets (eg, cutting back alcohol) and asks for details, but does not consistently reflect or use scaling questions to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids argumentation and validates sustain talk while pivoting to practical options, though could use more double-sided reflections or explicit reframing to further weaken sustain talk.
