# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalizing the client's experience and acknowledging strengths and practical priorities; no labeling or confrontational language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and affect, with validating language that reduces defensiveness; minor missed opportunity to explore physical signs of anxiety more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on completing court‑mandated community service with logical sequencing from problem identification to concrete action planning and little topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session structured as a joint effort—clinician solicits input, co‑constructs the script, and defers to client preferences while providing helpful structure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers multiple options, asks the client's preference, and invites consent to practice, reinforcing the client's sense of choice and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical motivations and past successes and amplified change talk, but could have probed deeper for intrinsic reasons and elicited a clear near‑term commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and power‑sharing, inviting choices at key points, though the clinician sometimes offered solutions before fully eliciting the client's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced ability and reason talk and helped develop concrete steps, but did not use scaling questions or directly elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed nerves into manageable steps, though missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections and explicitly eliciting the downside of staying the same.
