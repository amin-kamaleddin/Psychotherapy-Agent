# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with timely, specific affirmations and respectful language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and accurate reflections of Luka's situation, but could deepen emotional resonance by using more complex reflections addressing shame, fear, and anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol use and managing anger, steering the session toward concrete, actionable steps tied to Luka’s goals without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a strong collaborative partnership by soliciting Luka’s preferences at each step and co-creating the plan (templates, alarms, backup callers, timing).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control—offers options, avoids pressure for abstinence, and respects Luka’s readiness while encouraging feasible strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation by probing values, past successes, barriers, and confidence, and by prompting Luka to articulate his own plan and near-term commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters partnership through repeated invitations for Luka to choose options and by sharing decision-making power when drafting messages and selecting supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with open questions, reflections, reinforcement of progress, commitment-level probes, and concrete planning steps.
