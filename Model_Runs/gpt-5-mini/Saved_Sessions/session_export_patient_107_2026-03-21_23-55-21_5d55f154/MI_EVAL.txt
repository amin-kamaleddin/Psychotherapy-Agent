# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed warm, nonjudgmental regard and affirmed the client's choices throughout, validating ambivalence and preserving valued routines without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking matched the client's language and affect, capturing both sides of ambivalence and linking to values and concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on coffee behavior and its consequences, guiding the conversation toward a specific, measurable experiment without pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was a joint problem-solving process: the clinician solicited and incorporated the client's ideas and past strategies, co-creating a practical plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's right to choose, emphasized testing and experimentation, and normalized slips to preserve ownership and reduce coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's own motivations, past successes, and feasible steps, mobilizing intrinsic reasons for change rather than supplying them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician treated the client as the expert on his life, inviting input and shared decision-making that resulted in a plan reflecting the client's values.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was frequently elicited and reinforced, yielding specific plans, but could be deepened by amplifying change language and probing importance/confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used double-sided reflections and normalization to honor reasons for maintaining coffee while gently steering toward small experiments, avoiding arguments or criticism.
