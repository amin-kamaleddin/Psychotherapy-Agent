# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized slips, and validated ambivalence, maintaining a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and matched affect, but missed opportunities to deepen emotional content and use more complex reflections about work and partner conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on alcohol use using the AUDIT, personalized feedback, options, and ended with a concrete SMART goal and plan without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively engaged the patient as an equal partner by offering choices, co-creating the plan, and inviting the patient's input on goals and start date.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, and explicitly emphasizing that the patient was in charge of decisions and pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used evocation tools (pros/cons, readiness ruler, diary/goal options) but did not sufficiently elicit or amplify deeper values, long-term reasons, or stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance, shared power in decision-making, and used inclusive language to co-construct the plan with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-relevant statements via pros/cons and a readiness scale, but failed to consistently reflect, amplify, or follow up to strengthen desire, ability, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and often redirected to change options with neutral feedback and normalization, though could use more double-sided reflections to gently pivot away from sustain language.
