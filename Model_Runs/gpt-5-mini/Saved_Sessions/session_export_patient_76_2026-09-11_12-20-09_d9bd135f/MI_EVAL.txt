# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence without judgment; affirmed the patient's choices and reduced shame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used strong reflective listening and validated somatic experience and doubt, but reflections were often paraphrastic and could include deeper, affect-focused or amplified reflections to capture hopelessness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing self-harm and steadily moved to a concrete, actionable safety plan (towel on doorknob, five-minute timer, grounding).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as mutual problem-solving, consistently solicited the patient's ideas, and let the patient's preferences shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported choice at each step, offered options and assistance but accepted refusals, reinforcing the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client-generated strategies and solutions effectively but focused mainly on tactics rather than drawing out deeper motives, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as a genuine collaborator, inviting co-creation and deferring to the patient's expertise while checking in about what she would actually do.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited low-burden commitments and alternatives, but missed opportunities to amplify, summarize, or strengthen tentative change talk with scaling or reflective emphasis.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double-sided reflections to neutralize ambivalence, but could more actively validate the function of cutting while gently elaborating costs to tip toward change.
