# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently displayed unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental stance, explicitly respecting Linda’s choices and stance. Offered options and acknowledged her perspective throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic understanding with accurate reflections and summaries of ambivalence and permission-seeking. Reflections were mainly content-level; more feeling-focused, complex reflections would deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking/harm-reduction and moved the conversation logically toward a specific, measurable experiment. Balanced values exploration with actionable planning throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an egalitarian partner, inviting Linda’s input at every stage and adapting suggestions to her preferences. The plan (tea type, place, backups, reminder) was genuinely co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and sought permission before offering ideas, framing the intervention as a voluntary experiment. Avoided coercive language and reinforced Linda’s decision-making authority.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic reasons for change (role-modeling) and linked them to a concrete replacement behavior. Could have used more evocative questions and scaling to deepen motivation and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Linda as the expert and co-created the plan through collaborative language and preference queries. Frequent permission-seeking and shared decision-making reinforced power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change-related language and translated values into a specific experiment, producing concrete change talk. Missed opportunities to amplify desire/ability/need/confidence and to elicit stronger commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double-sided reflections and low-pressure framing to reduce resistance. Could strengthen momentum by selectively reflecting sustain talk, using amplified reflections, or more directly reflecting costs in the client’s words.
