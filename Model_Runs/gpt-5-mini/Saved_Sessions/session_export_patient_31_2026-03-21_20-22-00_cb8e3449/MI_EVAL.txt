# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opens nonjudgmentally and consistently acknowledges the patient's perspective and pragmatic concerns. Occasionally shifts quickly into technical, prescriptive instructions without more individualized warmth or normalizing ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective listening and validates practical concerns, capturing ambivalence well. Misses deeper emotional reflections (stress, calming function, guardedness) that would deepen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on smoking reduction and nicotine replacement throughout the session. Moves logically from options to dosing, logistics, and a concrete start plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving, tailoring recommendations to the patient's schedule and preferences and offering a checklist. The clinician nonetheless often leads with expert recommendations and handouts, directing the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and invites the patient's decision-making while avoiding coercion. At times provides prescriptive instructions without asking permission or offering alternatives first, which limits perceived autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits a small commitment (1–2 week trial) and some self-monitoring but does not systematically draw out the patient's own motivations, values, or confidence. Motivation work is implied by planning rather than actively elicited.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-creates a practical plan and solicits preferences (pickup, start date), conveying partnership in logistics. The clinician still assumes an expert role frequently when delivering detailed instructions without checking understanding.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supports concrete planning and elicits agreement to try patch+gum, which generates some change talk. Fails to probe for the patient's own reasons, importance, or confidence, and does not amplify emerging change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids arguing, which prevents escalation. Tends to pivot quickly to information and problem-solving rather than using double-sided reflections or reframes to reduce defensiveness.
