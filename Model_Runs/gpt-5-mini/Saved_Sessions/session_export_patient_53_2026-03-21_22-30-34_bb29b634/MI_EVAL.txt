# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and validating the patient's safety preferences throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate reflections that capture both content and feeling, showing high perspective-taking across the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the target behavior and repeatedly guides the conversation toward a concrete, specific, and measurable step (15 minutes outside, reminder, logistics).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative, with joint problem-solving and frequent solicitation of the patient's choices about supports, wording, and who sends the reminder.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly reinforces autonomy with explicit permission to skip, multiple options, and respect for the patient's preferences (e.g., no calls), reducing pressure to comply.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical steps and tentative motivations but leans toward logistics rather than deeper exploration of intrinsic motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making and conveys that expertise resides with the patient, co-creating the plan and asking the patient to choose wording and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces incremental change talk and tentative commitments, but misses opportunities to probe desire, importance, and confidence to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk and uses reframing and double-sided reflections to defuse defensiveness, though could further explore specific fears and summarize costs of staying home.
