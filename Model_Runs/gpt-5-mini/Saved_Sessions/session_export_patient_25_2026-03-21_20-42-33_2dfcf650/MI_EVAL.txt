# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician establishes a warm, nonjudgmental tone and consistently affirms Daniel’s successes and efforts, conveying unconditional positive regard. There is no shaming or labeling and the clinician normalizes difficulty while offering support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician demonstrates solid empathic listening and accurate reflections that capture Daniel’s dilemma, validating feelings and pride. To deepen empathy, more complex reflections exploring the emotional meaning of 'feeling left out' would be helpful.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintains clear focus on managing alcohol use in social settings and ties each intervention back to that goal. Interventions remain goal-directed and actionable, resisting topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is clearly a joint enterprise with the clinician soliciting Daniel’s input at every step, inviting role-play, and adapting to his feedback. Decisions and plans are co-created and tailored to Daniel’s voice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supports choice by offering options, asking which strategy he prefers, and checking willingness to rehearse, respecting Daniel’s control. Language and approach avoid pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician draws motivation from Daniel through evocative questions, use of the confidence ruler, and prompting for concrete reasons and supports. The approach builds on Daniel’s own motivation and resources to increase confidence and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently acts as a collaborator, offering options rather than directives and treating Daniel as the expert on what will feel natural. Session decisions are co-created with clear shared responsibility.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits and amplifies change talk using questions, affirmations, and rehearsal, and elicits concrete steps and confidence gains. To reach highest proficiency, more frequent reflection and explicit amplification of each change statement would strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward workable strategies with collaborative problem-solving and backups. Using more double-sided reflections and explicitly naming the pull to drink before pivoting would further soften sustain talk.
