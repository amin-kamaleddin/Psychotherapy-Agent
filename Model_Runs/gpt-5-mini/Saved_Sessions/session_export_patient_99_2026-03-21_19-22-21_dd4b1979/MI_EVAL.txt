# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone (safety check, validations, praise) and accepted the patient's preferences without labeling. Unconditional positive regard was maintained throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good understanding by summarizing history and matching the patient's casual tone while tailoring recommendations to priorities. Missed some opportunities for deeper emotional reflections about impacts like missing soccer or social pressure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on ankle recovery and drinking with efficient assessment and concrete, time-bound actions. Summarized next steps and provided specific, practical guidance toward goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reflected true partnership: co-created plans, customized the support text, and invited patient input before finalizing steps. The clinician adjusted to the patient's choices and preferences throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's right to choose and avoided pressuring language, offering options and asking permission before advising. Suggestions were presented as choices, reinforcing patient ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some of the patient's own motivations and willingness (choice of goals, support strategies, rehearsal), producing tentative commitment language. Could deepen evocation by linking change to personal values (e.g., soccer) and assessing importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance by asking which topic to focus on, co-creating plans, and offering to role-play or help write messages. Power was clearly shared, with the patient selecting focus and phrasing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and supported change talk with practical, achievable goals and a support text that produced 'I can try' statements. Could have done more to amplify and deepen change talk through reflective elaboration and confidence/importance queries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered harm-reduction options instead of confronting justifications. Could more actively shift sustain talk with double-sided reflections and reframing toward change.
