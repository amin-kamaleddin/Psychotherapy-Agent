# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and avoids labeling or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, sustained perspective-taking with complex reflections that summarize feelings and link emotions to behavior.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on preventing another DUI and preserving the client's license by steering to concrete, time-limited behavioral steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative—uses inclusive language, elicits options, and co-constructs the plan with the client rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and frames the plan as a short experiment, respecting the client's agency and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the client by eliciting personal values, past strengths, and client-generated reasons and strategies for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the client as the expert, invites ownership of solutions, and refines the client's ideas while sharing power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits concrete change talk and specific steps, but could more strongly amplify and consolidate commitment language and confidence measures.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and acknowledges minimizations, though could use more strategic double-sided reflections or explicit labeling of minimizing language to further shift momentum.
