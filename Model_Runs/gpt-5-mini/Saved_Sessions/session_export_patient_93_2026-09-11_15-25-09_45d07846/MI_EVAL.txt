# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and validates Margaret’s values and constraints, using affirming language and avoiding criticism throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking accurately captured values and concerns, though some reflections were somewhat formulaic and could probe deeper emotional nuance or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on reducing alcohol-related risk and progressed toward a concrete, time-limited behavioral test with observable outcome indicators.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician co-constructed the plan with Margaret, invited her ideas, offered options, and built on her past strategies rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supported Margaret’s right to choose pace and options, avoided pressuring language, and framed the plan as Margaret’s decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited Margaret’s own motivations, past successes, and concrete indicators of change, drawing reasons for change from Margaret rather than providing them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction treated Margaret as the expert on her life, with power shared through collaborative questions and deference to her selections and sequencing of steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician asked evocative, change-oriented questions and reflected/amplified Margaret’s commitments, producing specific change talk and a clear actionable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided confrontation and often pivoted toward change, but sustain talk was mainly reflected neutrally rather than strategically softened with double-sided or discrepant reflections.
