# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming—validates insight, normalizes slips, and praises realistic planning, demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows solid reflective listening and acknowledges the patient's perspective, but reflections are often evaluative/encouraging rather than probing deeper emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use with appropriate safety checks, concrete goal-setting, and behavior-specific strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—solicits patient input at multiple decision points, implements preferences, and frames the work as joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, avoiding pressure, and emphasizing gentle self-compassion for slips.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits a concrete plan and some change talk but relies more on offering solutions than drawing out the patient's deeper motivations, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys that the patient's expertise matters and invites collaboration, though the clinician sometimes shifts quickly into recommendations rather than eliciting patient-generated strategies first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully elicits specific commitment language and planning but does not consistently amplify the patient's own motivations, importance/confidence, or underlying reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and accepts partial readiness, but uses few strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or exploring the functions of drinking) to soften sustain talk and resolve ambivalence.
