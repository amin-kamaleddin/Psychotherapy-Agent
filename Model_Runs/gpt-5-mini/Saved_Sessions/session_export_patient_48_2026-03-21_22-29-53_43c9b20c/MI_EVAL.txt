# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, validating the client's priorities and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening captures both content and affect (fatigue, worry, desire to prove oneself) throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing short bouts of exercise and moves the conversation toward specific, actionable steps without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a joint problem‑solving effort: the clinician invites, integrates, and checks that solutions align with the client's preferences and constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice, offers options rather than directives, and respects the client's chosen strategy to promote ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motives (less fatigue, proving to self) and practical indicators, but could probe strengths, past successes, and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and client‑centered; the clinician defers to the client's expertise and shares power in selecting and shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and builds on multiple strands of change talk and moves toward specifics, but misses opportunities to amplify and summarize change language and elicit stronger commitment/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk and redirects to practical small steps, though could more strategically use double‑sided reflections or selective amplification to further soften sustain talk.
