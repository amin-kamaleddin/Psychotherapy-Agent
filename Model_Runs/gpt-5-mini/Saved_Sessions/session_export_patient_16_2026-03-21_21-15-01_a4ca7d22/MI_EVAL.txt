# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental tone, validating the client and praising planning without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing ambivalence and triggers, but misses opportunities for deeper affective reflection about the emotional role of vaping.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on vaping and a concrete no‑vaping‑during‑season goal, with every question and intervention tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—offers options, asks permission, and co‑constructs the plan (texts and coping strategies) with the client throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors the client's choice and avoids pressure by offering staged options, normalizing slips, and checking what the client wants to do and when to start.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out practical change talk and internal reasons (performance, avoiding suspension), but could further deepen intrinsic motives and elicit stronger verbal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects the client's expertise and invites choice, offering to collaborate on texts and plans, though at times the clinician leads content rather than fully eliciting client‑led brainstorming.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (scaling, concrete strategies, goal setting), but could better amplify and sequence the client's language to foster stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk (minimization, social/relaxation motives) but does little active softening or use of double‑sided reflections, moving quickly to problem‑solving instead.
