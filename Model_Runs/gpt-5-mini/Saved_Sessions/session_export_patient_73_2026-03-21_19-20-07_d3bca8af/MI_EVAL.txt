# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with validating language and praise, but some affirmations felt procedural rather than deeply individualized; more explicit normalization of relapse and identity-linked losses would deepen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid listening and basic reflections (echoing priorities and concerns), yet misses deeper, complex reflections of frustration and the emotional meaning of smoking; more explicit validation of emotional burden is needed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on tobacco cessation with measurable tasks (log, skip-a-day, 10–15 minute delays) and a specific three-day follow-up, balancing exploration with goal-directed planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is largely co-created with offers to draft scripts and negotiated follow-up, but the clinician sometimes shifts to an expert, prescriptive stance instead of inviting more client-generated planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing suggestions as choices without pressure, including respecting skepticism about medications.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk (small change, three-day commitment, coping phrases), but could more fully evoke intrinsic reasons, past successes, and confidence to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative language and shared planning, yet occasional expert-driven advice and stepwise instructions limit maximal partnership and power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and concrete commitments, but missed opportunities for strategic reflections and questions to amplify Linda's own reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk but did not intentionally soften ambivalence with double-sided reflections or normalization, instead moving quickly to advice and action planning.
