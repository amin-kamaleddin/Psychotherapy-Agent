# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, validating Dylan's perspective and creating a safe, respectful stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections that capture ambivalence and practical worries, but could deepen affective attunement by exploring emotions like shame, fear, or embarrassment more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on the conduct referral with concrete next steps (hearing, witnesses, messages), but does not broaden direction to address underlying drinking or longer‑term risk behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as true collaboration: the clinician solicits preferences, offers tailored drafts, and supports Dylan in driving the agenda and decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, asks what feels most doable, and respects Dylan's choices, reinforcing his ownership of decisions and action planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits Dylan's practical solutions and helps operationalize them, but does not draw out intrinsic motivation, values, or use rulers to explore importance/confidence about changing drinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and client‑centered—frames planning as joint problem solving and repeatedly checks Dylan's preferences and comfort with proposed actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces some change‑oriented steps (keeping a lower profile, coordinating witnesses) but largely focuses on contesting the finding and misses opportunities to elicit desire/ability/reasons/need/confidence to change drinking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Largely accommodates sustain talk and helps assemble evidence to contest responsibility without strategically softening or redirecting it toward consequences, discrepancy, or alternative actions.
