# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and prioritized safety, avoiding labeling; incorporated patient requests into the interaction and scripts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provided accurate, reassuring information and safety checks but used few reflective or emotionally validating statements and rarely named the patient's fear or anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on safely switching from phenelzine to citalopram, covering washout timing, monitoring, ECG, bridging options, and emergency steps without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong collaboration by inviting the patient to shape phrasing, iteratively revising the prescriber script, and co-creating a concrete plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respected patient decision-making by advising supervised changes and offering options while balancing necessary directive safety statements that minimally constrained choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical commitments (calling prescriber, using a script) but did little to draw out the patient's intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance—asked what the patient wanted and incorporated requests into the plan—but could more explicitly acknowledge the patient's expertise and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supported concrete, safety-focused steps that enable change but did not actively evoke or amplify the patient's own reasons, importance, or confidence for switching medications.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and acknowledged concerns while offering alternatives and supervised planning; generally steered conversation toward practical solutions rather than dwelling on reluctance.
