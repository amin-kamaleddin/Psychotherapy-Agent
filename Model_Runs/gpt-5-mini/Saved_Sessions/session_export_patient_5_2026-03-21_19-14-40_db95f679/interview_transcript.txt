PSYCHIATRIST: Common citalopram side effects: nausea, dry mouth, drowsiness or insomnia, headache, sweating, tremor, sexual difficulties, and possible weight change. Some people notice improvement in 2–4 weeks; full effect can take 6–8 weeks.

Critical safety: phenelzine (an MAOI) must not be combined with citalopram — together they can cause life‑threatening serotonin syndrome. A washout period is typically required (usually 14 days after stopping phenelzine before starting an SSRI). Also note QT risk at higher citalopram doses.

Talk with your prescriber before changing meds. If you develop high fever, rapid heartbeat, severe agitation, rigidity, or confusion, seek emergency care immediately.
