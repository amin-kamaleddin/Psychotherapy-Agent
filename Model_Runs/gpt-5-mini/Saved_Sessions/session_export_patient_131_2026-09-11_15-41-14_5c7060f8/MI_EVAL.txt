# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming—validates the dilemma and normalizes the tension while affirming efforts, creating a safe space for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening that mirrors priorities and pulls; could deepen by reflecting complex emotions (remorse, shame, fear of losing identity) and probing emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on concrete target behaviors and next steps (two‑week sober rule, SV attendance, safety plans), maintaining momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a joint effort—clinician solicits priorities, invites co‑planning and role‑play, and avoids an expert-heavy stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and lets the client steer decisions, offering options and normalizing trials so Wiremu retains ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively links actions to core values and elicits feasible commitments, but could more explicitly probe underlying reasons, past successes, and use scaling to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with open questions and shared problem-solving, though occasional quick listing of clinician-framed options slightly reduces client-generated content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and sharpens concrete change steps and commitments, translating desire into action; would be stronger by reflecting and amplifying spontaneous change talk and asking about confidence/ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges ambivalence with double-sided framing and reframes options neutrally, but missed opportunities to explicitly explore costs of staying and more directly redirect sustain talk toward change.
