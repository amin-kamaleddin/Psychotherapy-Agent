# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Julian’s experience and avoiding blame. Tone and affirmations reinforce acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, especially about logistics and nervousness. Could deepen empathy by exploring the emotional texture of guardedness and avoidance more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior (completing court-ordered service) and translates ambivalence into concrete steps and timelines. Session stays tightly on task with actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is consistently collaborative and co-produced, with negotiated decisions, role-play, and co-created scripts/checklists. Clinician acts as a teammate rather than imposing a plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice by offering options and respecting preferences for format and timing. Choices are consistently offered rather than directives, reinforcing client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivations and preferences (choice of placement, script, orientation), but focuses mainly on logistics. Missed opportunities to draw out deeper internal reasons, importance, and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters partnership by asking which option Julian prefers and co-creating the plan, including role-play and drafts. Language emphasizes joint problem-solving and shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change-oriented language and concrete commitments (e.g., Monday at 6, bringing paperwork). Could more often reflect and amplify small change talk and explicitly elicit desire/reason/need and commitment strengths.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes anxiety, and redirects to problem-solving with practical tools and scripts. Could further soften sustain talk through double-sided reflections that acknowledge competing pulls while affirming change.
