# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained unconditional positive regard with frequent affirmations, normalization, and nonjudgmental language, validating Hana's concerns and supporting harm-reduction choices throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and accurate reflections of Hana's situation and barriers, but missed some opportunities for deeper emotional reflections to capture frustration or stress vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently kept the session focused on smoking behavior and moved the conversation from exploration to concrete planning and rehearsal without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a clear partner and coach, inviting Hana to choose strategies and co-construct plans, role-plays, and templates that fit her life and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Hana's choice by offering a menu of options, encouraging small steps, and asking her to select what felt doable, avoiding pressure throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Hana's own motivations and solutions using open questions, confidence rulers, and rehearsal, leading to multiple self-generated strategies and commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and power-sharing, inviting Hana's input on each step, though there were brief moments of unsolicited tips where asking permission first would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited change talk with open questions and planning prompts and used a confidence ruler, but could more often amplify and reflect Hana's change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by accepting ambivalence, validating barriers, and promptly shifting to harm-reduction options and concrete coping strategies.
