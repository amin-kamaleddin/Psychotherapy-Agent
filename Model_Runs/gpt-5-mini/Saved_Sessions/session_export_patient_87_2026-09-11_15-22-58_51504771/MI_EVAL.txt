# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and maintains a nonjudgmental, affirming tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking with accurate, practical reflections, but misses opportunities to reflect deeper emotions like discouragement or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and strategically steers toward concrete, actionable steps while staying client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine co-construction of the plan by inviting the client's ideas, checking preferences, and avoiding an expert stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Respects the client's choice by offering options, framing trials as low-pressure, and reinforcing the client's sense of control and agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own strategies and past successes to build motivation, but could probe values and more deeply amplify change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the client as the expert, uses inclusive language, and solicits input at each step to foster true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits specific change talk, uses scaling and concrete planning, yet could more actively amplify spontaneous change language and reinforce commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects toward problem-solving, but relies more on logistics than double-sided reflections that explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence.
