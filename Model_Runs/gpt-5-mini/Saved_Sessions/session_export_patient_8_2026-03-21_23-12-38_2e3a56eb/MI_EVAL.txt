# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms the patient’s strengths, creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflections capture both content and feeling and acknowledge contextual difficulty, showing high-level empathic attunement to the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a clear focus on phone use and protecting sleep, moving from values exploration to a specific, time-limited experiment with concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist and patient co-construct the plan with repeated invitations for the patient to choose actions, using language that clearly frames the work as shared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are consistently presented as the patient’s, the plan is framed as an experiment, and suggested supports are offered as optional enhancements rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and imagery draw out the patient's intrinsic reasons for change and led to patient-generated motives and concrete strategies for a trial night.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positions the patient as the expert, solicits her ideas and preferences, and refines plans based on her input, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Several strands of change talk are elicited and a concrete first step is agreed, but the clinician could more actively amplify and summarize commitment language and use scaling/confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged with double-sided reflections and steering questions, yet the therapist could do more strategic reframing or summaries to reduce the pull of sustain talk.
