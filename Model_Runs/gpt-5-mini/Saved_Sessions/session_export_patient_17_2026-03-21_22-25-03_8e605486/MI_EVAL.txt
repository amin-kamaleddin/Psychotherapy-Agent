# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing struggle and avoiding shaming language to create safety and openness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows strong, accurate empathic understanding and reflective listening, capturing both content and affect and validating the client's feelings and context.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on smoking cessation and moves purposefully from values and reasons to concrete, proximal action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a co‑created effort: the clinician solicits preferences, integrates them into the plan, and repeatedly checks feasibility and client ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces choice by asking what the client thinks would work, honoring boundaries, and framing options as the client's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits intrinsic motivators and has the client generate strategies and reasons for change, strengthening internal motivation rather than supplying reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a collaborative, client-centered stance by inviting the client's ideas, co‑constructing the plan, and genuinely sharing power in decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, drawing out specific tools, small steps, and values that support the client's commitment to try.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and fear, pivoting to planning to limit its impact, but misses some opportunities for strategic double‑sided reflections or reframing to further shift ambivalence.
