# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental—validates ambivalence and reduces shame through reflections and affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely reflections, but often stays task-focused rather than probing deeper discouragement or self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the single target behavior and repeatedly brings the conversation back to a concrete experiment without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a joint problem-solving process using inclusive language, checking preferences, and integrating the client's responses into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes client choice and ownership, offers options rather than directives, and reinforces that imperfect effort still counts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's motives, ideas, and indicators of success and draws concrete commitment, but could more deeply probe values, importance/confidence, and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the client as the expert, uses collaborative and invitational language, and co-constructs the plan by following the client's lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and translates it into a concrete experiment, though could more often amplify and reflect the client's own change language to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk, normalizes ambivalence, and plans realistic fallbacks, but sometimes offers multiple options in a way that risks keeping the sustain side in focus.
