# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validating Elena's feelings and repeatedly reassuring her there's no pressure to decide today.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good perspective-taking and reflective listening by naming emotions and concerns accurately; could use more evocative, complex reflections to probe deeper emotional impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on contraception and practical next steps, consistently bringing the dialogue back to Elena's information‑gathering goals without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engaged Elena as an equal collaborator by offering options and co-producing tools (phone script, checklist); could further strengthen co-creation by asking her to prioritize or shape the agenda more explicitly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy with explicit permissions, choice language, and repeated offers to provide information rather than directives, reinforcing client control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some preferences and concerns but relied more on providing information than drawing out intrinsic motivations or linking change talk to Elena's values; more open, evocative questions are needed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited input while offering practical help, but could enhance partnership by co-creating next steps using Elena's own wording and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced spontaneous change statements and invited options, but was largely reactive and did not consistently use targeted questions or reflections to deepen Elena's motivation or elicit pros/cons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing reluctance and offering permission to leave; could add more double-sided reflections and strategic reframing to acknowledge ambivalence while guiding toward change.
