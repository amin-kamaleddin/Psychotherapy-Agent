# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, naming the client's dilemma and affirming strengths. Patient preferences were honored throughout (e.g., preferring to rehearse with Lina).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, specific reflections and summaries captured the client's perspective and ambivalence. Clinician validated discomfort and tailored responses to the client's IB/band context.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on safety around alcohol and concrete exit strategies throughout the exchange. Each turn progressively guided toward a specific, feasible plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co‑constructed with options offered and the client’s input solicited at every stage. Decisions about wording, timing, and rehearsal were negotiated together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice by offering options and deferring to what felt doable. Avoided pressuring language and honored the client's preferences about rehearsing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motivations and concrete reasons for change (protecting grades, fitting rehearsals). Could further deepen intrinsic motivators by exploring values, importance/confidence, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and deferred to the client's expertise about what fits her life. Repeatedly asked for the client's preference and built the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicited and developed the client's own language for change, asking for specifics and supports. Followed up to deepen commitment with concrete plans (Lina, exact text, rehearsal).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Normalized doubts and avoided reinforcing sustain talk, preventing escalation. Could improve by more explicitly exploring sustain talk (double‑sided reflections or contrasting with reasons for change) to resolve ambivalence.
