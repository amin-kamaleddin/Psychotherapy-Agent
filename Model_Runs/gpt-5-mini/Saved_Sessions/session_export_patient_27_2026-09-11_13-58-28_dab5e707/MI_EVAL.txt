# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful with multiple affirmations and normalizing statements that convey unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening, but could deepen emotional reflection (intensity, bodily experience, or subtler feelings) with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, continuous focus on disclosure to peers with concrete next steps (texting one friend, role-play) and ties questions/interventions to that behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acts as an equal partner—offering options, inviting input, co-constructing the message, and following the patient's lead throughout the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering multiple options, asking permission before interventions, and honoring the patient's pacing and wording preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivation by exploring priorities and offering aligned steps, but could probe personal values, past successes, and ask importance/confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance with repeated checks for preference, offering help only when invited, and shared decision-making that centers the patient's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces planning and change language with concrete options and drafting, yet could more explicitly amplify desire/reasons/need/confidence and summarize change talk to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and validates sustain talk while offering low-threat options and shifting to small steps; could further use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and pivot to change reasons.
