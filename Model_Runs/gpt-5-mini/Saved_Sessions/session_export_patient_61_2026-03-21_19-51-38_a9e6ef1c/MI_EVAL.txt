# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with normalizing statements and explicit promises not to judge or lecture, creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate, short reflections about overwhelm and barriers, but reflections stayed mostly surface-level rather than exploring deeper emotions or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear behavioral focus by narrowing to one concrete target (one morning blood glucose check) and consistently returning to a stepwise one-week plan and specific follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑designed the plan with the patient, negotiated follow-up preferences, and integrated the patient's cautions into the plan rather than imposing decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by offering micro-options, asking permission, and framing suggestions as optional, reinforcing that change is the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited logistical input and a tentative commitment but did not sufficiently draw out intrinsic motives, values, or reasons for change or use scaling to explore importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through permission‑seeking and shared decision-making, though some implementation planning became clinician-directed rather than eliciting the patient's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited change talk and affirmed a small commitment, but missed multiple opportunities to amplify the patient's own reasons, benefits, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing resistance and forgiving missed days, though could have used more double-sided reflections or summaries to gently shift ambivalence.
