# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes the patient's feelings, and avoids blaming or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic tracking with accurate, specific reflections and paraphrases that capture both content and emotional nuance, enabling patient elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on managing anger in waiting-room delays, repeatedly steering toward concrete strategies and securing a behavioral trial with implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, invited the patient's ideas, and co-constructed the plan so the patient's input materially shaped the solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering options, asking preferences, and avoiding pressure, reinforcing that the plan is the patient's to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values, past successes, and personally relevant reasons for change, but could have deepened intrinsic motivation and used scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine partnership with collaborative language, invited co-creation of the script and rehearsal plan, and deferred to the patient's judgments about feasibility.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions and a concrete trial, but missed opportunities to amplify change language and quantify strength/confidence of commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged skepticism and avoided argumentation but relied mainly on neutral reflections rather than using double-sided reflections or discrepancy summaries to actively reduce sustain talk.
