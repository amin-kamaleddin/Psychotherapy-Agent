# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing prior attempts and avoiding lecturing to create a safe tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated frequent, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with complex double-sided reflections and timely paraphrasing that kept the patient engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking/harm-reduction goals while honoring dental concerns, narrowing exploration to a specific, measurable one-week gum trial.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative—asked for preferences, co-authored the plan with the patient, and offered assistance only in ways the patient wanted.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly emphasized choice and control, offered options rather than directives, and affirmed the patient's right to stop or defer, strengthening agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used evocative questions to draw out the patient's own motives and internal triggers, resulting in patient-supplied criteria and a self-directed plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, repeatedly invited input, and avoided an expert stance while co-creating the trial parameters.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with targeted questions and affirmations, leading the patient to generate multiple reasons and a concrete first step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively minimized sustain talk through validation, double-sided reflections, and gentle redirection to low-risk options that respected the patient's limits.
