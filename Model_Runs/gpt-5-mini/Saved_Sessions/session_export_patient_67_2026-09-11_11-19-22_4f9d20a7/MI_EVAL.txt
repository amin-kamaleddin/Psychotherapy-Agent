# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and affirms practical ideas, helping the patient feel heard; could add more explicit verbal affirmations of strengths and broader normalizing language about ambivalence to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and reflective listening with clear summaries and feasibility checks; could use more complex reflections that probe the emotions (anxiety, shame, relief) underlying ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on reducing alcohol use with specific, time-limited behaviors and moves from general ideas to concrete actions; demonstrates strong, appropriate goal orientation throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine collaborative problem-solving by eliciting the patient's solutions, refining them jointly, and shaping the plan together rather than prescribing advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice, offers multiple options, and emphasizes patient agency with noncoercive language, supporting the patient's sense of control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits numerous patient-generated strategies and implementation details, strengthening practical motivation; could further probe deeper personal values and use scaling or summaries to amplify internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as co-created, defers to the patient's expertise, and uses choice language and check-ins that demonstrate genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly elicits and amplifies specific change language and deepens plans with follow-up questions; could more actively elicit and reinforce commitment/confidence statements (e.g., scaling importance/confidence).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges tentativeness and redirects toward practical planning without arguing, thereby avoiding reinforcement of sustain talk; could employ more double-sided reflections and explicitly explore reasons for maintaining current drinking to further soften sustain talk.
