# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating language that normalizes struggles and affirms the patient’s efforts, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and paraphrases show understanding of ambivalence and situational stress, but the clinician often moves quickly to planning rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on vaping cessation tied to the patient’s goal (basketball), repeatedly guiding toward concrete, actionable steps and relapse-as-information planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is framed as cooperative rehearsal with role-play and invitations for patient choice, though some moments remain therapist-led with specific scripted guidance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects patient agency and offers choices while using noncoercive language, but could more consistently ask permission before delivering multiple directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some intrinsic motivation and past successes, yet frequently supplies clinician-generated solutions instead of drawing out the patient’s own strategies and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites patient input and practices shared problem-solving, but often leads with clinician-generated steps rather than co-constructing plans from the patient’s ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and builds actionable steps, with occasional missed opportunities to deepen and link motivations to longer-term values or meanings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk was largely unaddressed or moved past into planning; the clinician did not routinely use reflective or double-sided strategies to soften resistance.
