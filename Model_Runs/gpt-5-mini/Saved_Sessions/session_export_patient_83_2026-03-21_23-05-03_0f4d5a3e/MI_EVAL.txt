# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys clear unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence throughout, consistently acknowledging concerns without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with specific, validating reflections that capture both cognitive and affective elements of the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on medication initiation and safety planning, repeatedly steering the conversation toward concrete, behaviorally relevant steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as equal, cooperative problem-solving with the patient choosing sequencing and supports; decisions are co-created rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the patient's choice by offering options, checking preferences, and avoiding pressure, thereby fostering a strong sense of autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits motivations and practical reasons for change but at times provides scripts that reduce opportunities for the patient to generate her own language; could ask for more patient‑phrased benefits or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames the interaction as collaborative and defers to the patient's preferences, offering role‑play and shared planning to center her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and planning effectively but stops short of consistently strengthening commitment language or asking for timing/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and pivots to safety and planning without arguing; could further soften sustain talk with more double‑sided reflections to highlight ambivalence.
