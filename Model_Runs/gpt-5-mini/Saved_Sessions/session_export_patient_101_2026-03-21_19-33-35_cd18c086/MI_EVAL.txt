# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming (e.g., explicit statements of safety and praise), clearly conveying respect and unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and validated worry, but reflections were often pragmatic and missed deeper affective exploration of emotions like shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use and improving contraception with concrete, goal‑linked suggestions and steps throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited patient preferences, tailored scripts and texts to her requests, and adapted the plan based on her comfort, reflecting strong joint decision‑making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control (offered options, honored delay of LARC, and used non‑pressuring language), reinforcing the patient's decision‑making authority.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation (importance/confidence, linking behaviors to outcomes) but missed opportunities for deeper evocative questions to draw out intrinsic reasons and meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by offering options and asking preferences, though at times provided expert solutions quickly rather than inviting the patient to generate ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforced and amplified existing change talk and strengths, but relied more on offering solutions than systematically eliciting the patient's own reasons and elaborations for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reduced opportunities to maintain status quo, but did not use strategic techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to explicitly soften ambivalence.
