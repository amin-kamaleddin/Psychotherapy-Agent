# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates unconditional positive regard throughout—normalizes burden, validates fear and avoids blame while honoring the patient’s priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows accurate perspective‑taking and reflective listening, summarizing affect and impact in ways the patient confirms and feels heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on obtaining investigations and referrals and consistently translates concerns into concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint enterprise—co‑creates the GP letter, invites patient choices, and practices role‑play with iterative feedback.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports patient choice by offering options, asking which is most useful, and not pushing psychological explanations when resisted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits readiness for testing and action but relies on external plans rather than drawing out deeper intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fosters a collaborative stance—invites input, shares agenda and power, and adapts plans to the patient’s requests.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and reinforces commitment to pursue tests and a second opinion and helps plan concrete steps, but could deepen confidence and explore broader motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk and honors the request for tests, though could more actively use double‑sided reflections and strategic summaries to gently shift ambivalence.
