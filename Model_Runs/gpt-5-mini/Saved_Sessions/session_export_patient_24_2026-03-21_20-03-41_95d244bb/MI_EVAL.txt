# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance (opening invitation, neutral language, validations) and avoided blaming or moralizing throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking (early summary, double-sided reflection) that helped the patient feel understood; could deepen by exploring emotional nuances and values linked to the marathon.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use as it relates to training, specifying what to track and designing a concrete behavioral test to gather data.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated equal partnership by inviting the patient to shape goals, monitoring, strategies, timing, and follow-up, with decisions co-constructed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and repeatedly affirming that the patient decides the goal and methods.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited some internal motivation (links to marathon, confidence rating, willingness to experiment) but missed opportunities to probe values and amplify/ summarize change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and client-centered, repeatedly inviting the patient's input and co-creating the plan to reflect his expertise and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and amplified change talk through a low-risk experiment, asking confidence and next-step questions, and helping move vague intentions into a concrete action plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Softened sustain talk by acknowledging minimizations, reflecting ambivalence without arguing, and offering a small experiment rather than confronting or persuading.
