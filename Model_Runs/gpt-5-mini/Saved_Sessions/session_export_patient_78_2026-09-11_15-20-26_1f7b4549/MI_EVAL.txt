# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated nonjudgmental acceptance and respect, validated ambivalence, and honored boundaries (e.g., accepting the patient's pace and not involving her daughter).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate paraphrases captured frustration and pragmatic concerns; could have probed deeper affect (shame, fear, identity) for fuller emotional resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking reduction and protecting the daughter, steering to concrete, proximal goals (smoke-free house, delaying first cigarette, alarms).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like an equal partnership—clinician solicited input before offering suggestions and co-created a practical plan with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and patient control, avoided pressuring language, invited the patient to select what to try, and honored her limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own reasons and strategies and asked about past successes; could deepen by probing values (why protecting her daughter matters) and using scaling or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed the session with options and joint language, consistently framing the patient as the primary decision-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk around small, doable steps and testing strategies; missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate change talk into stronger commitment or use importance/confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolled with resistance and reframed toward manageable steps rather than arguing, which softened sustain talk; could further use double-sided reflections to explicitly diffuse sustain language and deepen movement toward change.
