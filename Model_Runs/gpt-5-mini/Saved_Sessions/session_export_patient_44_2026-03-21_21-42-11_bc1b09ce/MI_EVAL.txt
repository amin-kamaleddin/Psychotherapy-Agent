# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard with validating, nonjudgmental language and warm check‑ins that acknowledge both wins and frustrations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective‑taking with frequent reflections that label emotions and mirror specific concerns (e.g., comparisons, back pain, inconsistency).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors and moves smoothly from values/benefits to concrete, actionable steps, confidence scaling, and monitoring plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is genuinely collaborative—decisions and supports are co‑created with Erik and the clinician adapts to his preferences at every decision point.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Erik’s sense of choice by offering options, asking his preference for support, and avoiding coercive language so he retains ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Erik’s own motivations and solutions through questions about benefits, signs of success, confidence ratings, and patient‑generated strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Erik as the expert and co‑constructs the plan; clinician guides rather than directs, leading to patient‑owned concrete commitments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits and amplifies change talk by highlighting progress, asking planning questions, soliciting confidence ratings, and translating momentum into specific commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects sustain talk well with normalization and alternatives, but missed a few opportunities for deeper double‑sided reflections or explicit reframes to further reduce sustain pull.
