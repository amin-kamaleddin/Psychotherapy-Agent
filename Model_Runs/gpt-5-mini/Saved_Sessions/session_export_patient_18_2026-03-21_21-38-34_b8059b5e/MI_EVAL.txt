# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, affirming and normalizing Anya's concerns throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening, though reflections were more cognitive than emotionally deep and could explore feelings like peer pressure or anxiety about college more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on early alcohol use and harm-reduction strategies with concrete, behaviorally specific planning and repeated refocusing on what’s doable this week.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative session: the clinician co-constructed the plan, invited Anya's choices, practiced role-plays, and integrated her wording and feedback into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering options, asking permission, inviting tweaks to scripts, and reinforcing Anya’s ownership of decisions and the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Anya’s motivations and concrete change language using open questions and scaling, but could better link strategies to her core values and amplify commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reflected a strong partnership stance with shared power, joint decision-making, and invitations to shape the plan together rather than imposing directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with options, praise, and concrete tactics, but missed some opportunities to amplify reasons for change and deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and refocused on safety strategies, though could more strategically use double-sided reflections or rolling-with-resistance language to further soften ambivalence.
