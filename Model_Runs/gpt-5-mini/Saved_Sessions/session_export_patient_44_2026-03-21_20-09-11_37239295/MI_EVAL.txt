# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician adopted a clear nonjudgmental stance, used affirming language and normalized ambivalence, and consistently offered choices and respectful wording throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening that captured content and practical concerns, though reflections stayed at a pragmatic level and could probe deeper affect (e.g., shame, identity loss) beneath the behaviors.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behaviors (gym attendance and a healthier snack) and progressed logically from assessment to concrete, measurable action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly co-constructed—calendar entries, messages, role-plays, and wording edits were created together with frequent invitations for Erik's input and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Erik's choice, offered multiple options, respected his edits, and framed suggestions as supports rather than directives, reinforcing ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Erik's reasons, strategies, and confidence with scaling and concrete planning, but could further elicit deeper personal values and longer-term meaning to strengthen sustained motivation and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a strong partnership stance by asking permission, offering help, and sharing power in decision-making and plan development.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk using affirmations, a confidence scale, targeted follow-ups, and concrete 'how' questions that translated desire into implementable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk with double-sided reflections and redirection to planning, but tended to move quickly to action rather than using additional strategies to explore sustain talk's function or deeply soften its emotional hold.
