# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental respect, validated autonomy throughout, and avoided labeling or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking that matched the patient's values, but could have probed underlying emotions or ambivalence more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on piercing-related risks and actionable prevention/treatment steps, guiding the session toward a practical plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction felt collaborative with inclusive language and co-created aftercare plans, though the clinician supplied more solutions than eliciting the patient's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy through permission-seeking, offering choices, and reiterating respect for the patient's decision throughout the visit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relied heavily on information and advice rather than eliciting the patient's intrinsic motivations, values, or readiness; limited use of evocative open questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by inviting choices and summarizing agreed plans, but could have drawn more on the patient's expertise to shape the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made few and mostly indirect attempts to elicit change talk and did not follow up with open questions or reflections to amplify any tentative change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used neutral acknowledgements, but did not consistently employ double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to soften resistance.
