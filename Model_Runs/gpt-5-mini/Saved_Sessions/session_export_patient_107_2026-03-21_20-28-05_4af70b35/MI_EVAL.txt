# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing ambivalence. Validates the patient's reasons and creates a comfortable, supportive environment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses solid reflective listening and summaries that capture content and priorities. Could strengthen by naming deeper emotions and using more complex reflections to amplify unstated feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on caffeine use and its consequences throughout the session. Guides toward a specific, measurable exploratory action (tracking plan and check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative with co‑creation of the tracking template and choices driven by the patient. Clinician invites input and adapts suggestions to the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by asking permission, normalizing undecidedness, and offering reversible options. Emphasizes experiments rather than mandates to preserve agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively uses open questions and elicitation (importance rating, options to test) to draw out the patient's views. Could linger more to elicit deeper personal reasons, confidence, and intrinsic motivation before offering strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine power‑sharing and joint problem solving, repeatedly soliciting the patient's preferences. Co‑constructs the plan and acknowledges the patient's expertise about their daily life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk (e.g., conditional willingness, importance rating) but does not consistently amplify or deepen it. Misses follow‑ups to expand reasons, confidence, or commitment (e.g., 'Why a 3 and not a 1?').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and does not reinforce sustain talk, framing tracking as curiosity rather than pressure. However, the clinician rarely uses double‑sided reflections or reframes sustain statements to gently pivot toward change.
