# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive language; clinician validated Daniel's hesitancy and treated his preference to track with respect, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic moves — invited concerns and offered reflections about discomfort and training impacts — but missed opportunities for deeper, elaborative or emotion-focused reflections to explore guardedness and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and its links to training, provided a concrete tracking template, safety thresholds, and a defined two-week follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like a joint project: the monitoring plan and template were co-created and follow-up timing was mutually agreed, with the clinician inviting shared interpretation of the logs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options, accepted Daniel's choice to track rather than quit, and repeatedly reinforced his control over decisions without using pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some preliminary motivation by matching options to readiness and securing agreement to track, but did not sufficiently probe internal motives, values, or use scaling/readiness questions to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership demonstrated by inviting Daniel’s input on format, timeline, and next steps, and by sharing power in planning and follow-up decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Produced modest change talk (agreement to track) through offering options, but did not strategically amplify or elicit desire, ability, reasons, need, or commitment language to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and did not confront minimizations, but did limited active softening (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly surface ambivalence and gently shift sustain talk.
