# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental affirmations and no shaming, validating Elena’s efforts and strengths throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent perspective-taking with reflective listening that tracked Elena’s ambivalence, exhaustion, and practical constraints rather than reducing the exchange to facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on smoking reduction and protecting the child, moving logically from options to a specific, measurable one-week experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner by soliciting Elena’s ideas at every step and building the plan from her own solutions (gum, pocket ritual, ribbon) rather than imposing clinician-driven options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, honoring skepticism, and framing the plan as a client-owned one-week experiment to reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited motivation and solutions from Elena through open questions, culminating in a client-generated, concrete commitment (the one-week gum experiment with a pocket ritual).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong shared decision-making with language that invited Elena’s expertise and co-constructed the plan, reinforcing power-sharing throughout the exchange.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk leading to a commitment to try gum for a week, though could have strengthened desire/ability/importance/confidence with a ruler or explicit confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and redirected toward change via small experiments, but could have used more double-sided reflections or early summarizing to further soften sustain talk.
