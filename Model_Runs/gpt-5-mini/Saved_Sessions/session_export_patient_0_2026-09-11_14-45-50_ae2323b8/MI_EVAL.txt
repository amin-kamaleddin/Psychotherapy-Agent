# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed nonjudgmental regard, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed the client's preferences and choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking were used to mirror both content and feeling, helping the client open up and accept concrete steps.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on reducing infection risk with progressive, concrete steps from idea to implementation without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative, with the clinician inviting the client's input, checking feasibility, and co-designing practical implementation strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician preserved client choice by offering options, asking permission, and allowing the client to set the plan and timeline, which likely increased buy-in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited the client's own motivations, benefits, and solutions, drawing out intrinsic reasons and specific plans for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A strong collaborative stance was maintained, treating the client as the expert and repeatedly inviting him to choose and refine the most feasible approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicited and amplified change talk, asked for specifics and implementation plans, and strengthened commitment through reflection and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was skillfully de-escalated by accepting minimizations and pivoting to harm reduction, though more double-sided reflections explicitly naming both pulls would deepen ambivalence work.
