# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, creating psychological safety (e.g., normalizing ambivalence and avoiding moralizing). Repeated supportive statements and validation encouraged disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections of ambivalence and worries. Could deepen empathic exploration of the emotional weight of body dissatisfaction by asking more about day‑to‑day feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on shifting from unsustainable dieting to process-based goals with measurable behaviors and a 3–4 week review. Kept the session structured and goal-oriented without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functioned largely as a team process—solicited input and negotiated guardrails—but several interventions were offered top‑down rather than co-developed. Inviting Yasmin to brainstorm specifics first would strengthen equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirmed choice and offered options while framing rest as a valid choice, supporting autonomy. At times used directive language and specific prescriptions without explicitly checking if the exact parameters matched Yasmin’s preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Yasmin’s motivations and connected behaviors to her values, using questions about preferred session types and metrics. Could further draw out and amplify DARN statements and deeper personal meanings to build commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a mostly collaborative stance with inclusive language and co‑created metrics, which supported partnership. However, frequent clinician-driven prescriptions reduced Yasmin’s lead in shaping specific options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (e.g., asking for a tiny realistic change and shifting to process goals). Missed opportunities to more often reflect and amplify Yasmin’s own change statements to build stronger commitment and confidence talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledged sustain talk, normalized ambivalence, and offered reframes and stop‑rules to reduce guilt-driven behavior. Sometimes moved quickly into prescriptive planning instead of using double‑sided reflections to explicitly honor weight‑loss desires while steering toward alternatives.
