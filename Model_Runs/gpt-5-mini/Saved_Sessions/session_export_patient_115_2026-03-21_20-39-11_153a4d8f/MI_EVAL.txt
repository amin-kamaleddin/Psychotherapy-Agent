# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened with explicit nonjudgmental support and consistently conveyed respect and positive regard, using affirmations and validation throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with concise paraphrases and targeted reflections about stressors and barriers, making the patient feel understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on asthma management and reducing smoking/drinking, steering the session toward concrete implementation steps and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted the session as mutual problem-solving, repeatedly soliciting preferences and co-creating specific logistics, scripts, and plans with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the patient's choice and control, offered options, asked permission before advice, and framed suggestions to reinforce personal agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own motivations and resources with scaling questions and strategy generation, building motivation from the patient's own reasons rather than persuading.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an explicit partner by inviting input, co-creating the plan (timing, placement, supports), and offering role‑play and feedback in collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and amplified change talk through questions about benefits, small realistic steps, confidence ratings, and reinforcement of the patient's ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk nonconfrontationally and redirected to manageable steps, but could more often use double-sided reflections to name both sides of ambivalence before pivoting to change planning.
