# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental acceptance and normalized Kevin's experience, validating his feelings and removing blame which allowed honest disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections showed good perspective-taking and summarized ambivalence accurately, though they stayed at the surface and could deepen emotional nuance (shame, stressors, values).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session remained tightly focused on the target behavior (staying connected while protecting the ankle) and progressed logically from ambivalence to concrete, actionable planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly co-constructed: the clinician used inclusive language, solicited Kevin's input at each decision point, and built plans from his choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offered choices, avoided pressure, and asked Kevin to pick what felt realistic, preserving his sense of control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicited client-generated solutions and motivations, but could have probed deeper into underlying values and confidence to strengthen internalized commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned the work as collaborative—inviting Kevin to choose options and co-design specific steps, demonstrating strong power-sharing throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effective prompts and reflections elicited multiple pieces of change talk and concrete commitments, guiding Kevin to specific, realistic steps he agreed to try.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reflected without endorsement, but the clinician missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections and eliciting the client's own costs of the buzz.
