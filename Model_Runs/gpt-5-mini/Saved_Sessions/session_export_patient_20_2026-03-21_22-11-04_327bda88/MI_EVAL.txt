# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respected the patient's limits (no family contact, no lectures), normalizing choices and avoiding judgmental language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections that captured the patient's ambivalence and tone, validating feelings and summarizing the plan without minimizing or confronting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and the agreed small goal (one dry day), guiding implementation details and arranging a concise follow-up without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-created the plan by negotiating options, eliciting preferences, and adapting to the patient's constraints at each step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforced choice and control throughout, offering reversible, low-effort options and tailoring the check-in to the patient's wishes (text only, no pressure).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation and reasons for change from the patient, eliciting barriers, confidence, and commitment language and converting those into concrete steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a collaborative enterprise, repeatedly inviting input and sharing decision-making power in designing the plan and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using scaling questions and implementation intentions, turning expressions of desire into specific, actionable plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and pivoted to small changes, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections and subtle reframing to further blunt sustain talk.
