# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating the patient’s frustration and reluctance throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and timely reflections of frustration and overwhelm, but often moved quickly to problem-solving without deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on improving asthma self-management with concrete, behaviorally specific action planning and minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged the patient as an equal partner, inviting input at every step and tailoring scripts, alarms, and plans to the patient’s stated preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by offering options, asking which felt most doable, and avoiding pressure while facilitating ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivation and commitments for small changes (inhaler placement, portal message), but did not probe deeper intrinsic values or long‑term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative with co-creation of plans, role‑play, and joint drafting of messages, demonstrating genuine power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced commitment language and concrete intentions, yet missed opportunities to amplify underlying reasons, values, and broaden desire/need language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated sustain talk, successfully redirecting to practical steps, but used few strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to deepen ambivalence work.
