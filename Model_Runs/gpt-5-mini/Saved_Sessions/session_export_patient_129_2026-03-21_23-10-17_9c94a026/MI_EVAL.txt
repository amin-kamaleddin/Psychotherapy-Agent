# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates respect and nonjudgmental acceptance with validating reflections and use of client language, but statements sometimes feel formulaic and lack more personalized warmth or strengths-based affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and reflective listening that captures ambivalence and labels emotions, though reflections mostly restate content without deeper affective or layered exploration of underlying fears or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on substance use and immediate risk reduction, consistently steering toward concrete short-term plans with minimal topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is conducted as a true collaboration: the clinician invites Tyler's input, offers options rather than directives, and co-constructs the plan with shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Tyler's sense of choice by asking open questions, avoiding coercive language, and repeatedly inviting him to choose what feels doable given his readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Tyler's own immediate strategies and goals for harm reduction, but stops short of drawing out deeper motives, values, or long-term reasons for broader behavior change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as joint exploration, defers to Tyler's ideas, and makes him the primary decision-maker through invitations to choose and define success.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete harm-reduction steps that produce short-term change language, but does not effectively cultivate broader desire, reasons, or commitment to reduce or quit longer term.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and does not amplify sustain talk, using reflections and pragmatic options to soften resistance, but could more actively use double-sided reflections or highlight costs to shift sustain talk further.
