# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating, normalizing the patient's struggle and avoiding any shaming language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and matching of emotional tone, but empathy was sometimes abbreviated as the clinician moved quickly into solutions rather than exploring deeper feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a very clear, consistent focus on the concrete target behavior (10 minutes on the solo) and aligned all interventions with that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving with inclusive language and frequent check-ins, inviting the patient to adapt plans and choose options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering alternatives, asking permission, and emphasizing that the patient decides what to do, with no coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited commitments and small change talk (plans, confidence ratings), but relied heavily on providing scripts and solutions instead of drawing out deeper intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative tone and invited patient choices, though the clinician's detailed, prescriptive steps slightly reduced fully client-led exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific commitments and confidence ratings, but offered many concrete strategies and sample texts rather than amplifying more self-generated change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward small actions with normalization and coping strategies, though missed opportunities for double-sided reflections to acknowledge both sides.
