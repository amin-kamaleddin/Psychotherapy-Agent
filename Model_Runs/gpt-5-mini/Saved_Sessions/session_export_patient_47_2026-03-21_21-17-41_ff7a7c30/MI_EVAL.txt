# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard—opening with gratitude, normalizing ambivalence, asking permission, and validating Evelyn’s priorities with a warm, nonjudgmental tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that mirrored ambivalence and picked up emotions, though a few deeper affective or complex reflections could have further explored underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use and related safety concerns, with concrete goals, a start date, confidence ratings, and a relapse/slip plan to keep the conversation goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly a partnership: the clinician solicited Evelyn’s input at every decision point and co-constructed strategies she could accept, edit, or reject.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, affirming ownership of the plan, and checking confidence without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited motivations, supports, and concrete change talk using a confidence ruler and options, but could have more actively amplified key change statements and linked them to values or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A consistently collaborative stance was maintained—inviting Evelyn’s expertise, deferring to her preferences (e.g., about her son), and jointly developing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced change talk with options, a start date, and the confidence ruler, yet missed some chances to amplify and summarize Evelyn’s own change language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled gently—no arguing, normalizing ambivalence and offering alternatives—but more double-sided or strategic reflections could have further softened sustain talk and resolved ambivalence.
