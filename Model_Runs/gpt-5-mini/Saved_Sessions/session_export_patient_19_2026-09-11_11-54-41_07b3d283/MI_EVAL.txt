# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, warm regard—validating priorities, normalizing compromise, and avoiding labels or criticism throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking mirrored Anya's ambivalence and concerns, reducing defensiveness and deepening understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session kept a clear focus on gambling and financial protection, repeatedly returning to concrete proximal steps (freeze cards, delete apps, reminders) and actionable planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician solicited Anya's ideas, checked feasibility, and co‑created plans, offering to draft materials when invited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician affirmed Anya's choice and control, normalized partial change, offered options rather than directives, and asked permission before adding details.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited motivation and concrete commitments but missed opportunities to more strongly link immediate steps to Anya's broader values and to use scaling/confidence questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician fostered shared decision-making, positioned Anya as the expert on what will work, and invited her preferences about reminders and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician actively elicited and amplified change language, asked targeted questions to convert desire into specific commitments, and reinforced concrete plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without reinforcement; clinician used double-sided framing and reframing to validate social benefits while steering toward protective steps.
