# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalized her stance, and offered future help if needed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and concise summaries that conveyed perspective-taking and made Isabelle feel heard, though deeper affect-focused reflections could strengthen empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol-related safety and produced a practical harm-reduction plan, but did not pursue reduction of drinking as a target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicited Isabelle's input at each step and co-produced a checklist and plan, demonstrating genuine two-way decision-making and shared ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Isabelle's choice throughout, offering options and avoiding pressure while emphasizing her control over next steps and how to receive the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited minimal intrinsic motivation for reducing drinking, focusing on what she enjoys and safety behaviors without drawing out values, reasons, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited preferences when co-creating the plan, though could more explicitly invite Isabelle to set the agenda and highlight her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made few efforts to evoke desire, ability, reasons, or need to cut back; most questions yielded sustain or safety-oriented language rather than change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and respectfully accepted her stance, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframing to gently highlight discrepancies.
