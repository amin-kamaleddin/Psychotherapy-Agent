# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Elena's feelings and avoiding blame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and accurate paraphrases that validated relief and anxiety, but empathic responses were sometimes brief and moved quickly to logistics rather than exploring deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on avoiding unintended pregnancy and obtaining emergency contraception, steering consistently to actionable next steps without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as a joint effort using inclusive language, co-created practical tools (scripts, plans), and responded to Elena's preferences throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Elena's right to choose by asking permission, offering multiple options, and avoiding pressure, which reinforced her control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivations (relief, fear) but relied more on information and logistics than on open, evocative questions to draw out values, confidence, and deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by inviting preferences and co-creating plans; at times the clinician led with recommendations but kept Elena involved in decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created multiple opportunities for change talk and reinforced specific next steps (telehealth + pharmacy), but could have amplified Elena's motivators and linked them more to her values to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk without confrontation and offered pragmatic, nonpressuring alternatives, though more double-sided reflections and strategic rolling with resistance would further soften sustain talk.
