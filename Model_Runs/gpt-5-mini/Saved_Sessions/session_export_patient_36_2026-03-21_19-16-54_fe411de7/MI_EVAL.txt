# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, warm, and validating throughout the session, normalizing concerns without judgment and offering positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates perspective-taking and some reflective statements that mirror affect and values, but could deepen empathy with more complex, emotion-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the target behavior and moves the session from values clarification to concrete planning, scripts, and rehearsal with strong task momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving and checks preferences, but frequently provides concrete wording and options that shift some control toward the clinician's expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices, asks permission, and frames suggestions as options, preserving the patient's autonomy and allowing her to adapt suggestions to her style.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws on prior values work and elicits plans and rehearsals, but often pivots to clinician-provided solutions rather than systematically exploring the patient's intrinsic motivations and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session feels collaborative with invitations and shared tasks, though the clinician occasionally adopts an expert, directive stance that limits full power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change-oriented language and prompts concrete planning, but could strengthen change talk by asking more evocative follow-ups about meaning and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Successfully minimizes sustain talk by normalizing worries, reframing toward benefits, and focusing on solutions and planning rather than reinforcing resistance.
