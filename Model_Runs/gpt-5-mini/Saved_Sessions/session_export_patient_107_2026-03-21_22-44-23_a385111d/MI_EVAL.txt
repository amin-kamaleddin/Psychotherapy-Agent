# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence with frequent, genuine validations and nonjudgmental language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, complex reflections that capture both affect and content, showing strong perspective-taking and attunement throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on afternoon/overall caffeine reduction and moves logically to a specific, measurable experiment with concrete implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the exchange as joint problem-solving, soliciting preferences and co-constructing the plan so the patient’s ideas drive the supports and strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and frames changes as low-pressure experiments, protecting the patient’s right to keep their morning routine.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation and practical solutions from the patient with open invitations and questions, eliciting internally derived reasons and specific plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames the work as collaborative, centers the patient's expertise, and offers choices that reinforce shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change talk and a clear experiment, but could better amplify and deepen desire, reasons, and confidence with follow-up probes or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk and uses double-sided reflections effectively, though some reflections restate sustain elements without more explicitly enhancing discrepancy.
