# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing constraints and avoiding judgment while validating the client's priorities and competence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows accurate perspective-taking and useful reflective listening with double-sided reflections, though reflections are somewhat repetitive and rarely probe deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors and purposefully guides the conversation from ambivalence to a concrete, time-limited behavioral test without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session operates as mutual problem-solving: the clinician consistently solicits and integrates the client's ideas and constraints into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and flexibility by offering options, eliciting the client's preferences, and reinforcing that continuation depends on the client's criteria.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and concrete success criteria (e.g., 5–10 point BP drop), building the plan around intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly frames the encounter as collaborative and defers to the client's expertise, letting the client shape decisions and specifics of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with targeted questions and reflections, producing specific, actionable change language from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to practical options, but could more strategically diminish sustain talk by exploring costs of staying the same and using varied reflective techniques.
