# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently displayed unconditional positive regard with validating, nonjudgmental language and normalization of ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and empathic summaries that validated Hana's experience and helped her elaborate barriers and plans.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with a specific behavioral goal, timeline, and concrete tactics, repeatedly returning to the target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, offered options and adapted recommendations to Hana's context, and used role-play to co-create the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored Hana's choice, offered options rather than directives, asked permission for practices, and emphasized her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical, actionable motivation and commitment but moved relatively quickly to tactics and could have probed deeper personal values and meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Hana as the expert on what would work, invited her preferences, and collaboratively selected and refined concrete strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using questions about past efforts, readiness/confidence scales, asking for exact wording, and rehearsal.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk and redirected to solutions, but could strengthen softening with more double-sided reflections and explicit normalization of smoking's appeal.
