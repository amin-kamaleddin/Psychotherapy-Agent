# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent validations and normalizing statements that conveyed unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections summarized Naomi’s sensations and consequences well, though deeper naming of underlying feelings and more complex reflections could have deepened affective understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on social anxiety affecting work and moved efficiently to concrete, testable changes (email, warm-up, strap cue) with a brief measurement plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative—materials were drafted together, Naomi chose wording and cues, and the clinician framed the work as joint problem-solving rather than directive advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, sought permission, and emphasized Naomi’s control over which strategies to try, reinforcing her agency throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Naomi’s ideas, preferences, and small commitments (scaling, strap phrase), but could have asked more about personal reasons, values, or past successes to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership evident in co-creating scripts, emails, and practical cues with Naomi, sharing decision-making and inviting her edits and rehearsal preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced desire and intentions using scaling and collaborative planning, though change talk could have been amplified and reflected more to build stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did acknowledge doubts but tended to move quickly into solutions rather than exploring or diffusing sustain talk with double-sided reflections and explicit validation of ambivalence.
