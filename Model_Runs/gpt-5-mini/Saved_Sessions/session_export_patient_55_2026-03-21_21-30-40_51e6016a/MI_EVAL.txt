# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician demonstrated consistent unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental tone, repeated affirmations, and clear confidentiality assurances.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries that helped the patient feel heard, though reflections stayed mainly at the content level and could probe feelings or ambivalence more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on underage drinking risk and harm-reduction throughout, balancing exploration with forward movement to concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly equal-partner: the clinician invited agenda-setting, validated preferences, and co-created a realistic plan with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported patient autonomy through confidentiality assurances, asking permission, offering options, and framing decisions as the patient's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own motivations and strategies by asking about goals, preferences, confidence, and inviting the patient to generate the plan and language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as collaborative by repeatedly inviting the patient's input, offering choices, and jointly drafting the buddy/text plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk through open questions, offering options, asking for a specific plan, and using confidence scaling to secure concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well by avoiding confrontation and shifting to harm-reduction, though using more double-sided reflections could better acknowledge ambivalence and soften sustain talk further.
