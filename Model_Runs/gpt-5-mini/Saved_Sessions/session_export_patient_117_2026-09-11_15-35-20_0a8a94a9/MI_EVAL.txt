# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveys unconditional positive regard, validates effort, and normalizes struggle in a warm, strengths-focused, nonjudgmental way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening by mirroring ambivalence and affect and checking understanding before moving forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing lunchtime/work smoking, consistently orienting conversation toward concrete strategies, measurement, and actionable planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is co-constructed with frequent invitations for Samuel's input, inclusive language, and framing interventions as experiments rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports choice and control by offering options, asking which feels doable, and requesting permission before rehearsing or giving feedback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits motivation from Samuel by drawing out his reasons, anticipated benefits, and confidence-related details, then scaffolds those into concrete commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Samuel as the expert on his experience, involves him in decision-making, and builds the plan around his preferences and ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits and amplifies change language through specific prompts for small steps, asking how success will be noticed, and summarizing commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk neutrally and avoids reinforcing it, but leans toward pragmatic problem-solving rather than using more strategic double-sided reflections to shift emphasis toward change.
