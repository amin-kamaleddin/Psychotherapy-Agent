### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced actionable change talk (specific days/times, reminders) and secured commitment, but did not probe deeply for intrinsic reasons or hoped-for outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Effectively softened sustain talk by reframing self-criticism, framing the plan as an experiment, offering small fallbacks, and promoting curiosity over harsh self-judgment.
