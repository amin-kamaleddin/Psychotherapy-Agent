# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized feelings, and validated the client's experience while framing small attempts as worthwhile.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections, but mostly at a surface level and could have probed deeper emotions or underlying concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking with a specific, measurable plan and a follow-up to sustain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—used inclusive language, invited client preferences, and co-created the tracking and check-in plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced client choice and control, normalized low readiness, and avoided coercive language while honoring the client's pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation (readiness ruler, specific commitment) but missed opportunities to draw out deeper personal reasons or intrinsic motivators.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently adopted a partnership stance by offering options, asking the client to choose, and jointly developing a doable plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and helped specify plans, though could have deepened change language by exploring values, reasons, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk but did limited strategic work to soften it; missed moves like double-sided reflections or exploring discrepancy.
