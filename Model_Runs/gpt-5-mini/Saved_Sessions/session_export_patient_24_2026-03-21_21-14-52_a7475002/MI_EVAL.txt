# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding blame; clinician validates the patient's perspective throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that capture ambivalence and priorities, but empathy is mainly cognitive; could deepen by reflecting underlying emotions and using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use in relation to marathon goals, negotiated concrete, measurable steps and a follow-up to sustain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a partnership with goals and strategies co-constructed; clinician solicits input and offers troubleshooting only after the patient identifies barriers.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, and framing suggestions as optional; no pressuring language observed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient's values and elicits reasons and confidence, but could elicit more client-generated solutions and amplify the patient's own change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance throughout, treats the patient as the expert, and shares power in planning and implementation details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces specific change talk (short experiment, swaps, measurable outcomes) and asks for confidence ratings, yet could more often amplify and summarize change statements to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes plans as low-risk experiments to deflect sustain talk, but would benefit from more double-sided reflections that explicitly acknowledge both sustain and change language.
