# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and validation, normalizing the situation and accepting Andrea’s decision without blame. The clinician maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and some reflective statements, and checks in about mood and functioning. Misses deeper, more specific or complex emotional reflections that would deepen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol risk and concrete safety/harm-reduction strategies. Provides specific next-step options tied to the behavior appropriate for a brief school-based intervention.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative—asks permission, invites participation, and frames next steps jointly. However, the clinician retains more of the lead in information gathering rather than co-creating the agenda with Andrea.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports Andrea’s sense of choice and control by offering options and accepting declines without pressure. Avoids coercive language and emphasizes that she may return when ready.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies mainly on providing information and harm-reduction rather than eliciting Andrea’s motivations, values, or intentions. Misses opportunities to ask open, evocative questions that would draw out change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative tone and invites input, fostering shared decision-making. Could have more explicitly elicited Andrea’s agenda and co-constructed a brief, client-led plan even while she declined.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does minimal active elicitation or reinforcement of change talk and largely accepts factual responses without probing for reasons or desire to change. Missed openings to explore discrepancy or evoke desire/ability/need language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with or reinforcing sustain talk and accepts Andrea’s stance that she’s managing. Misses strategic MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or summaries) to gently redirect sustain talk toward consideration of change.
