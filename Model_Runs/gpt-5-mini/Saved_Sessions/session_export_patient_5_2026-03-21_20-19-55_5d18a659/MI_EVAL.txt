# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through validation, normalization, and supportive, nonjudgmental language that helps the patient feel heard and safe to express concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with reflective statements and attunement to emotions like protectiveness and worry, and addresses emotional content with suggested wording and pacing changes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on safe medication management (washout timing, tapering, monitoring, urgent signs) and repeatedly returns conversations to concrete safety planning and prescriber wording.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the session as a joint enterprise by co-creating and revising scripts, practicing together, and inviting the patient to shape wording, tone, and pacing in a mutual problem-solving process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice and avoids coercion by offering options, asking preferences (format, phrasing), and emphasizing decisions occur with the prescriber, preserving the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's concerns, preferences, barriers, and readiness (practice and script refinement), drawing motivation from her desire for safety while providing necessary information.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance—repeatedly asks the patient's preference, invites practice, and shares power in crafting and refining language so the patient's needs and choices guide the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces language supporting a safe plan through script prompts and rehearsals, but there are missed opportunities to more actively evoke and amplify the patient's own reasons, values, and goals for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and legitimizes hesitancy about stopping phenelzine without arguing, which prevents reinforcement of sustain talk, but could further reduce sustain talk using double-sided or summary reflections linking attachment to the medication with safety concerns.
