# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through validating reflections and affirmations. Language is warm and respectful with no evidence of blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening that captures both behavior and affect. Concise summaries help the patient feel understood and stay engaged in planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing manageable home activity and pacing. Questions and reflections consistently tie back to the concrete plan, tracking, and brief check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as a genuine partnership with shared problem-solving and iterative requests for the patient's input. The clinician adapts the plan to the patient's routines and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly presents options and asks the patient to choose, reinforcing the patient's sense of control. Invites patient-set criteria for continuation rather than imposing goals.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits concrete commitments and practical motivations but focuses mainly on logistics and behaviors. Missed opportunities to explore deeper values, hopes, or personally meaningful reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the patient as an equal collaborator, inviting preferences on specifics and offering to test and adjust together. Decisions are co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with open questions, reflections, affirmations, and by securing concrete commitments. Moves successfully from ambivalence to a small, testable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and acknowledges skepticism while redirecting to small experiments. Could further soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections or exploring the costs of the status quo.
