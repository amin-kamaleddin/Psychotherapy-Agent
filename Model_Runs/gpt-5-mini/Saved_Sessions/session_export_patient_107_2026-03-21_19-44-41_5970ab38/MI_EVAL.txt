# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating the client's experience and offering options; avoids labeling or moralizing about coffee use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and paraphrases that capture ambivalence and physical symptoms, showing perspective-taking. Could use more complex reflections to probe emotional nuance and deepen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on caffeine use and a concrete first step (tracking), with questions and resources tied directly to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats the client as a partner when designing the tracking tool and asks preferences, but much of the interaction becomes technical instruction rather than eliciting the client's ideas about ease or goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports choice, offers multiple options, and honors the client's readiness, avoiding prescriptive pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits limited self-motivational statements and primarily focuses on practical planning; misses opportunities to ask evocative questions about values, importance, and confidence to build intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership by asking permission and co-creating the tracker, though the clinician often supplies technical solutions without fully eliciting the client's priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites consideration of change and suggests small steps like tracking, but does not consistently evoke the client's own reasons or amplify emerging change talk to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges both sides and uses a double-sided reflection early, but frequently shifts to solutions instead of using reflective strategies to roll with resistance and gently soften sustain talk.
