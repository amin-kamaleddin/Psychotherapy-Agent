### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation throughout the session. Tone is warm and supportive with no shaming, minimizing, or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that capture Sofia’s emotions and intent. Reflections are accurate and followed by respectful open questions, showing consistent empathic understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on repairing the friendship, repeatedly returning to message wording, logistics, and coping steps. Resists drifting into unrelated topics and maintains concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functions as an equal partnership with co-developed scripts and real-time role-play practice. Clinician adapts input to Sofia’s preferences and avoids an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports Sofia’s choice by offering multiple options and inviting her to pick or tweak messages. Avoids pressuring language and leaves final decisions and pacing to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits Sofia’s own reasons and resources for change, drawing out desire to reconnect. Missed opportunities include deeper exploration of values and systematic use of scaling (importance/confidence/readiness).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance through invitations, permission to role-play, and repeated checks about needs. The plan is co-constructed with Sofia choosing and adapting the final wording and steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk via targeted questions, summaries, and role-play, helping Sofia articulate concrete steps. Could more systematically deepen and quantify commitment (e.g., importance/confidence scaling) and amplify key change phrases.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects attention toward action and coping strategies. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections and explicit pivots that juxtapose sustain and change language.
