# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Rosa’s mixed feelings, avoided labeling or criticism, and affirmed her pace and limits throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, often complex reflections and perspective-taking to capture ambivalence, but responses were somewhat repetitive and could have probed deeper into emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and narrowed to a specific, measurable one-week experiment while guiding toward concrete follow-up indicators.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the session as mutual problem-solving by inviting Rosa’s input at each step and letting her ideas shape the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Rosa’s freedom to choose, framed the experiment as her decision, and respected her limits and pacing without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Rosa’s motivations and resources with open questions and preference-based choices, but could have probed values and used scaling to deepen and quantify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a genuinely collaborative stance with shared power, frequent deference to Rosa’s knowledge, and co-creation of a doable plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk through small-step planning and asking about signs of success, but missed chances to amplify key statements and explicitly elicit commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without confrontation and used double-sided reflections to shift toward change, though repeated smoker-identity language at times risked subtly normalizing sustain talk and could be reframed more strategically.
