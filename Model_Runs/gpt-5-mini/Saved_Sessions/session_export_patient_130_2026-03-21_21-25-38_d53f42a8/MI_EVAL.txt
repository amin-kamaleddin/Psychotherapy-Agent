# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed nonjudgmental acceptance and respect—normalized spousal pressure, used supportive reflections and affirmations that reduced threat.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking but tended to shift quickly to action and missed deeper emotional reflections (e.g., identity, shame) that would deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing recreational drug use to protect the marriage and job, guiding the session toward a specific, measurable plan without major drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, invited Viktor's input, adjusted scripts to fit him, and coached while Viktor practiced, demonstrating genuine give-and-take.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported Viktor's choice throughout—framed suggestions as options, asked permission before role-plays, and reinforced that he is in charge of any change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Viktor's motivations with open questions and scaling and had him generate strategies, but could probe deeper into personal values and longer-term goals to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fostered a collaborative stance—offered help only after asking, co-created behavioral plans, and repeatedly checked Viktor's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified explicit change talk (scales, pros/cons, specific steps) and prompted concrete actions, but could more deliberately summarize and string together Viktor's change language to elicit stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk but underused strategic softening techniques (double-sided reflections, reflect-redirect, strategic summaries) to explicitly shift sustain talk toward discrepancy.
