# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently nonjudgmental, warm, and normalizing, with sustained positive regard and affirmations of Kevin's planning. No shaming or labeling is present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and validations of ambivalence, acknowledging practical barriers. Reflections remain mostly at the surface and could probe deeper emotions or values to deepen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol-related risk and returns to actionable, behavior-linked interventions throughout. Addresses barriers pragmatically while preserving a client-centered tone.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functions as a true two-way collaboration, inviting choice and co-constructing the plan (texts, reminders, cutoff times). Clinician repeatedly checks back and adapts to Kevin's context and concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirms Kevin's choice and offers multiple options without coercion, supporting autonomy. At times the clinician moves quickly to specific solutions and could more explicitly summarize options and invite final selection to maximize ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Kevin's motivations and uses them to generate concrete, client-driven strategies and commitments. Questions and reflections draw ideas and change language from Kevin rather than imposing clinician solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership marked by asking permission, inviting input, and rehearsing together; Kevin's social context directs planning. Clinician shares power and treats Kevin as collaborator in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk using open invitations, specificity prompts, and a confidence ruler, leading to concrete commitments. Moves reliably convert intentions into actionable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk but does not consistently soften or strategically redirect it with double-sided reflections or exploration of the positives of drinking. Missed opportunities to deepen discrepancy by briefly probing what 'fun' and social connection provide for Kevin.
