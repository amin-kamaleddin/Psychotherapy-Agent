# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient’s experience with affirmations and no blaming. Warm, nonjudgmental language supports trust and safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, emotionally attuned reflections and notes subtle details (wedding, husband away). Clear perspective-taking validates the patient’s feelings and impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on concrete targets (medical clarity and symptom management) and orients the session toward actionable steps. Consistently moves conversation toward specific tasks like calling, diary, and referral wording.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is co-constructed—frequently invites input, tailors wording, and offers to practice or adapt plans to the patient’s comfort. The clinician scaffolds rather than overrides decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports choice throughout by offering options, asking permission, and providing alternatives without pressuring. Reinforces the patient’s control over next steps and respects her preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete commitments and enhances self-efficacy through rehearsal and phrasing, but focuses more on external actions than on eliciting deeper personal motives or values. More systematic exploration of internal reasons and confidence would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works collaboratively with the patient, inviting choices and jointly constructing the plan (phrasing, fallback lines, practice). Power-sharing is evident and the patient’s preferences drive decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces specific change language and commitments (calling, diary) and operationalizes plans. Could further amplify intrinsic motives by exploring what change would mean for the patient emotionally or socially.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and validates fear while redirecting to actionable steps, reducing dwell time on sustain talk. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and greater exploration of the costs of staying stuck would further soften sustain talk.
