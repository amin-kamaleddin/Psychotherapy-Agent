# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding judgment while validating the patient's experience throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and affect and deepening the patient's self-report.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and concrete strategies, keeping questions and reflections tied to quitting and actionable planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as genuine joint problem-solving with inclusive language and co-constructed decisions (e.g., calendar break + alarm chosen together).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports patient choice by offering options and asking which is most doable, though occasional assertive positive expectation could be balanced with more explicit autonomy statements.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations, past successes, and practical ideas, building on the patient's language and concrete memories to evoke internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invites the patient to choose strategies, but occasionally interjects clinician belief in a way that is slightly more directive than ideal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with targeted questions and affirmations and elicits concrete steps, but could more consistently amplify and summarize change statements to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk empathetically and uses double-sided reflections, but would benefit from more strategic pivots (contrast summaries or pros/cons) to shift toward change talk.
