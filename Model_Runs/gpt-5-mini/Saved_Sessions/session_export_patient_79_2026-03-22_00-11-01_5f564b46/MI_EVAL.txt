# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's stance, affirming effort and avoiding labels or blame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening that captures pragmatism and reluctance, but could probe deeper into emotions like frustration or overwhelm with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, goal-directed focus on controller use and simple tracking, guiding the patient from goal-setting to concrete implementation and monitoring.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructs the plan with the patient, solicits preferences, and integrates the patient's prior strategies, showing strong mutual problem-solving and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports choice and ownership through open invitations and acceptance of limits, allowing the patient to select feasible options and control the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own reasons and links change to prior routines, but could more systematically deepen intrinsic motivations and elicit explicit commitment or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and honors the patient's expertise, offering choices and power-sharing while accepting pragmatic solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with forward-looking questions and reflections, drawing specific, observable commitment language from the patient.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk and acknowledges barriers neutrally, but relies more on logistical problem-solving than explicit double-sided reflections to reduce ambivalence.
