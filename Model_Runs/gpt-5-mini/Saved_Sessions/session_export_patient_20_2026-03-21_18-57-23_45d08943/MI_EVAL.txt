# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized disclosure, and affirmed actions (e.g., calling Mike, locking pills), creating a nonjudgmental, safe tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear validation and reflective responses and attended to affect/body experience (breathing, chest tightness); could deepen empathy with more complex reflections naming underlying emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on immediate safety and risk reduction, but did not sustain focus on alcohol as the longer-term MI target beyond short-term safety instructions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked jointly with the patient to co-create the safety plan, invited edits, checked preferences, and provided hands-on support (staying while patient called), demonstrating strong teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission, and integrated patient suggestions, reinforcing ownership of decisions and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Successfully evoked immediate safety actions (locking pills, contacting supports) but did not elicit deeper personal motivations, values, or discrepancies to support longer-term change around drinking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the patient as an equal partner by asking preferences, inviting edits, and sharing decision-making, which supported genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete, immediate commitments for safety but missed opportunities to draw out broader desire, reasons, or commitments to reduce drinking and explore related values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, redirected to harm-reduction, and incorporated the patient's 'no alcohol until safer' suggestion, though could more explicitly use double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
