# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, explicitly normalizing ambivalence and affirming planning and self‑awareness throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent accurate reflections and validation show strong perspective‑taking, though deeper exploration of affect (e.g., guilt or the appeal of drinking when down) would strengthen empathic rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol while preserving the wind‑down ritual, with each question and reflection tied to concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session exemplifies shared problem‑solving: clinician offers options, invites choices, and integrates the patient’s preferences into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes patient choice and expertise, avoids pressuring toward a specific goal, and asks the patient to select the most doable options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation and reasons for change from the patient with evocative questions and reflections that surface anticipated benefits and concrete strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the process as collaborative, defers to the patient’s expertise, and co‑constructs the plan with language that shares power and decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk via open questions, reflections, and prompts about specific plans and expected positive outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges ambivalence and redirects toward planning without reinforcing sustain talk, but misses opportunities for double‑sided reflections and reframing the appeal of drinking.
