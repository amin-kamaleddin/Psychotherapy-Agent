# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing Selene’s struggle and honoring her priorities and boundaries throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and perspective-taking were strong and accurate, but the clinician missed opportunities to reflect deeper affect (worry, frustration) beneath Selene’s ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear focus on medication adherence with stepwise narrowing to a concrete, time-limited plan and avoided drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was genuinely co-constructed: the clinician solicited Selene’s ideas, tested options with her, and let her prioritize what felt doable.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirmed Selene’s freedom to choose, offered options rather than directives, and avoided pressuring language, supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively drew out Selene’s solutions and reasons for change, but could have probed values and confidence more to deepen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction emphasized shared decision-making and power‑sharing, with inclusive language and explicit invitations for Selene to pick what felt realistic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and options elicited multiple change statements and intentions, though the clinician could have amplified and summarized those statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged without confrontation, but the clinician often echoed minimizations instead of using strategic reflections or gentle challenges to soften resistance.
