# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrates clear, sustained unconditional positive regard and repeatedly validates Seamus's concerns and boundaries without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening with accurate paraphrases and affect‑sensitive reflections that keep the patient engaged; could deepen by using more complex, emotion‑laden reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Well directed to the patient's immediate dental goals and logistics, but the clinician did not maintain focus on the training target of smoking cessation or link smoking to stated values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: offers options, asks preferences, and co‑creates the plan so the patient’s responses guide decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by honoring requests to postpone smoking discussions and repeatedly soliciting the patient's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to draw out Seamus's internal motivation about smoking; an early values question was not followed by targeted evocation (no rulers, past successes, or reasons explored).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats Seamus as an equal partner, checks his priorities, and defers to his choices in planning the care.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal attempts to elicit desire, ability, reasons, needs, or commitment about smoking—no scaling, exploration of prior attempts, or amplification of change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing and neutralized sustain talk by honoring his wish to avoid lectures, though could have used MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to gently shift toward change.
