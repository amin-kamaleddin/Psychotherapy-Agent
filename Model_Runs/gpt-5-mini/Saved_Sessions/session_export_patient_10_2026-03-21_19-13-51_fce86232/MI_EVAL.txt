# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm—validated feelings and praised effort throughout the session, avoiding blame and conveying unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with normalization and steadying statements, though there were missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of the patient's emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on securing short-term childcare with practical, concrete solutions and next steps, keeping interventions tightly linked to the stated goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—proposed ideas while soliciting preferences, co-designed checklists and wording, and negotiated plans jointly rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice with multiple options, 'no pressure' language, and affirmation of the patient's control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation through action planning and by referencing the patient's confidence, but focused more on logistics than drawing out intrinsic motivators, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as an active decision-maker, repeatedly sought input, and adapted materials to her requests, reflecting strong partnership throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively encouraged and amplified change-oriented language with scripts and concrete steps, but could more consistently expand the patient's repeated high-confidence statements into firmer commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected to practical alternatives with softened wording and contingency plans, though did not employ explicit double-sided reflections to more fully process ambivalence.
