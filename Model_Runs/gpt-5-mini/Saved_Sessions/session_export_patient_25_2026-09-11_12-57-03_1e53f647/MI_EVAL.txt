# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, affirming the patient's successes and explicitly validating difficulty in a supportive, non-shaming tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, specific reflections and paraphrases that capture both pride and worry, demonstrating strong perspective-taking and helping the patient expand on what matters.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on social drinking and steadily guides the session toward concrete, implementable steps while resisting topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving by proposing ideas and immediately inviting the patient's input, co-creating and rehearsing implementation details together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking the patient to choose what fits, and affirming the ability to layer or adjust strategies over time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivations, values, and solutions rather than supplying reasons to change, which produces concrete, patient-generated commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert, inviting collaboration and co-designing the plan while repeatedly checking which options feel most doable for the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with open questions and prompts for a single first step, amplifying the patient's commitment and producing specific plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and effectively redirects to concrete planning, though could more often use double-sided reflections and explicitly name ambivalence to further soften sustain talk.
