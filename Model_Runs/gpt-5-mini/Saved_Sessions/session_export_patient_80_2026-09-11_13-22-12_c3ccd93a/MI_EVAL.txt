### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and strengths‑based—validates efforts and normalizes lapses throughout the session.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and identification of relief, hope, and caution, though deeper exploration of underlying emotions (e.g., shame, anxiety) could strengthen empathic accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing drinking and preventing aggression, guiding the session toward concrete, measurable steps and an implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session feels like a genuine joint effort—offers options, asks permission, and integrates the client's ideas into a co‑constructed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice by offering options and asking the client to select what feels doable, while avoiding directive language and normalizing slips.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveys that expertise resides with the client—asks collaborative questions and defers to the client's lived experience when shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk and translates values into doable steps, but could more systematically prompt commitment/confidence language (e.g., scaling or explicit commitment questions).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and quickly moves to problem‑solving supports, though more double‑sided reflections could further shift momentum toward change.
