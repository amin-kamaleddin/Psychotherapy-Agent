# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone (explicit opening line, frequent affirmations and validations). There are no moments of criticism or pressure to change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and paraphrases that capture ambivalence and perspective. Could deepen emotional validation by probing how the ritual feels day‑to‑day or its personal meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, focused agenda on coffee reduction and moves the session toward concrete steps (specific trial, start day, success/stop rules, reminder time). Minimal drift and effective outcome orientation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a genuine partnership with inclusive language and co‑construction of the plan (options offered, patient chooses approach and tracking template). The client's ideas materially shaped the final plan and supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient's choice and control, offering multiple options and checking preferences without coercion. Clinician leaves final decisions to the client and supports self‑directed choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations, confidence ratings, and practical reasons for trying a change (experiment, barriers, supports). Could be more deliberate in probing personally meaningful values and amplifying change‑favoring statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert and shares power throughout (asks which option feels most doable, negotiates check‑in timing, tailors supports). Co‑creating the plan demonstrates strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and reinforces practical and commitment language and helps translate ambivalence into a doable experiment. Missed some opportunities to amplify change talk and link small steps to broader values to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk using neutral, double‑sided reflections and reframing barriers into problem‑solving strategies. Could more strategically highlight costs of staying the same or selectively amplify minor change talk to tip ambivalence further.
