# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes limits, validating the patient's priorities and accepting his pace without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, capturing both content and feeling (e.g., 'low-key and doable') so the patient feels understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking and drinking targets and a specific, actionable experiment, repeatedly returning to supports, barriers, and success measures.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed—clinician invites input, uses inclusive language, and iteratively builds the plan from the patient's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and readiness, framing the approach as the patient's experiment and soliciting preferences rather than prescribing changes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out motivations and self-generated solutions through open questions, leading to concrete intentions and contingency plans from the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Models an equal partnership by treating the patient as the expert, sharing power, and refining a patient-led plan collaboratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits concrete change talk and a one-night commitment, but could further strengthen desire/ability/confidence with scaling or more elaborated reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to harm-reduction, though strategic double-sided reflections to highlight discrepancy could further soften sustain talk.
