**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—normalized Tyler's upset, affirmed his courage, and offered options rather than blame, reducing shame and building rapport.

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on substance use, safety, and parent conflict throughout, guiding the session toward concrete behavioral objectives like safety planning and scripting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was joint problem-solving—co-created scripts, backup texts, and checklists, accepted Tyler's wording choices, and used role-play rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

**Comments:** Relied more on supplying solutions and strategies than on eliciting Tyler's own motivations; missed core evocative questions and scaling that would draw out intrinsic change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance—regularly invited Tyler's input, asked permission before exploring, and worked together to refine language and plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made reasonable attempts (offering options, normalizing small steps) but did not use open evocative questions to systematically elicit desire, reasons, or commitments, so change talk remained limited.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided direct confrontation and used reflections and choices to de-escalate resistance; could increase use of strategic double-sided reflections to gently shift toward change.
