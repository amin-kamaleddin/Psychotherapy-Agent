# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with validating reflections and normalizing language, creating warmth and nonjudgmental support throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and accurate, sometimes complex reflections that tracked the patient's ambivalence and feelings closely.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on disclosure to peers and moved the session toward concrete, actionable steps (drafting text, rehearsing replies, grounding plans).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine partner with the patient by co-creating the message, role-playing replies, and jointly planning coping strategies and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and offered multiple options, emphasizing the patient's control over if, when, and how to disclose without using pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical motivations and strengths (storytelling, desire for authenticity) but could more explicitly elicit values, importance/confidence scaling, and the patient's own arguments for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treated the patient as the expert by offering choices, soliciting preferences, and sharing power in decision-making and plan development.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change-related statements and concrete steps (choice of text, drafting, rehearsing), but missed some opportunities to probe importance, confidence, and past successes to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk through neutral reflections and small-step options, yet primarily paraphrased concerns rather than using double-sided reflections or exploring the function of staying silent.
