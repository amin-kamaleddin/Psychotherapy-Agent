# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Rosa’s ambivalence, affirming strengths and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections of feelings and content, repeatedly normalizing Rosa’s nervousness so she felt heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and steered the conversation toward an actionable one‑week gum trial while avoiding tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by offering choices, inviting Rosa’s preferences, co-creating small steps, and offering role‑play and resource support.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Rosa’s sense of choice and control by asking permission, offering multiple options, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited importance/confidence and past successes and offered fitting options, but missed deeper exploration of values, the doctor’s warning meaning, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invitational, though frequent information-giving and recommendations occasionally shifted the balance toward an expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (importance rating, past successes, choosing a small step) but could have amplified and deepened it with follow‑up questions and a commitment probe.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without arguing, yet could have used more strategic double‑sided reflections and selective pivots to further reduce sustain talk’s pull.
