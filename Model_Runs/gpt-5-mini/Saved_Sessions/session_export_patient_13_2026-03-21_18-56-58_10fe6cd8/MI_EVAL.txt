# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance (e.g., supportive opening, normalizing distress), avoiding blame or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening (asks symptom details, validates fear and anxiety) and stays attuned and calm during role-play and scheduling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on obtaining medical evaluation and repeatedly orients the conversation to specific, concrete actions (message drafting, triage, booking) without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner by co-creating messages, role-playing, and negotiating practical details while following the patient's preferences and needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports patient choice and agency by offering multiple options, honoring requests to delay texts/appointments, and explicitly resisting pressure to decide immediately.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits logistical and some motivational information and uses it to design feasible steps, but relies more on practical solutions than on drawing out deeper personal reasons or using scaling questions to elicit commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the work as a joint project, invites the patient's expertise and preferences, and lets the patient shape next steps and communication content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates multiple opportunities for change talk and supports small steps (drafting messages, role-play), but seldom elicits the patient's own personal reasons or uses scaling to amplify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes reluctance, and offers non-pressured alternatives (same-day list, delayed alerts), but could further soften sustain talk with more double-sided reflections acknowledging both sides.
