# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys warmth and nonjudgmental support with validations that reduce shame, but several supportive lines feel somewhat formulaic and deeper, individualized normalization of her emotions (guilt/fear) was limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and brief reflective listening (acknowledging fear/responsibility), yet reflections are mostly cognitive and miss more complex emotional nuance and double-sided reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on alcohol use during pregnancy with clear, practical next steps (scripts, exit plans, stop drinking, contact provider) and relevant role‑play.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely a collaborative problem-solving approach—co-creates scripts and invites role-play—but sometimes slips into more expert-driven instructions that reduce full co-production.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice by offering multiple options and asking if the patient wants help, although some recommendations are presented as directives without permission-seeking or exploration of readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes action planning and opportunities to practice, but does not sufficiently elicit Sofia's own motivations, values, or confidence and relies more on offering solutions than drawing out change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally works with Sofia and shares power through choices and role-play invitations, but occasional prescriptive moments (direct instructions) reduce the consistency of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provides concrete tools and asks about planning which support action talk, but misses opportunities to elicit and amplify Sofia's own desire, reasons, and confidence with open questions and follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses practical strategies to limit sustain talk (scripts, exit plans), yet lacks verbal techniques like double-sided or complex reflections to acknowledge and reframe ambivalence about friends and identity.
