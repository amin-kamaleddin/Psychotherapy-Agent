### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate empathic listening with timely reflections that captured both emotions and specifics. Mirrored the patient's language and followed up on barriers, deepening understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on improving rest and implementing partner-supported nights, tying each exchange to concrete goals. Questions and reflections consistently guided the interaction toward planning and actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking preferences, and avoiding pressure. Preserved patient ownership by implementing supports only when the patient agreed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own reasons and linked plans to meaningful outcomes like feeling human again and clearer thinking. Used those elicited motivations to reinforce the plan and sustain momentum toward action.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk through open questions, concrete follow-ups, and prompts for specific commitments. Amplified the patient's agency by summarizing and affirming the practical plan.
