# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard by validating symptoms, normalizing distress, and avoiding blame or minimization throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides frequent, accurate reflections of emotion and content that link feelings to context (unemployment, husband away) and mirror the patient's language effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, goal-directed focus on obtaining investigations and returning to work, repeatedly steering toward concrete next steps like checklists, referrals, and role‑play.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in co‑creation of the plan, asks permission to draft wording, uses inclusive language, and schedules rehearsals to support the patient's needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports patient choice by offering options, soliciting preferences (printed vs emailed, wording, timing), and respecting the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits priorities and practical commitments (tests, role‑play) well but relies more on logistics than probing intrinsic motivation, confidence, and deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a shared effort, invites joint problem‑solving, and incorporates the patient's exact phrasing to reinforce equality and shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces goals and strengths and converts intentions into plans, but misses opportunities to deepen desire, ability, reasons, and explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain‑talk without arguing, validates fears about missed diagnoses, and offers collaborative concrete steps that avoid amplifying resistance.
