# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalizing, nonjudgmental language and repeated validation, offering practical help and respecting time constraints.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate cognitive reflections and summaries, but misses some affective reflections (frustration, hurry) that would deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the conversation tightly focused on inhaler/steroid step‑down and safe asthma management with pointed questions and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Models genuine shared decision‑making by co‑creating a question list, tracker, and rehearsal lines while repeatedly checking the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by asking permission, offering options, avoiding coercive language, and framing plans as jointly negotiated and conditional on the patient's consent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits key motivations and outcomes (safety, fewer flares, no daily inhaler) and uses a confidence scale, but could probe deeper into values and discrepancies to build commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and invites input, though at times moves quickly to offer solutions instead of first eliciting more of the patient's ideas to strengthen ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change language and specific goals, but does not consistently amplify or reflect those statements to deepen commitment and follow through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses double‑sided/neutral reflections and clarifying questions to prevent escalation, but could use more strategic selective reflections and summaries to shift momentum toward change.
