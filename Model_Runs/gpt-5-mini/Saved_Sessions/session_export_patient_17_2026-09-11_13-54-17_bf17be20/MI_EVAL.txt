# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, reducing shame and avoiding labeling; tone is supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate reflective listening that captures both content and affect, naming emotions and using summaries to check understanding and deepen the client's expression.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on smoking cessation and progresses toward a concrete, actionable plan with practical steps and backups while avoiding tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is genuinely collaborative, co‑constructing the plan, soliciting the client's preferences, and adapting interventions to fit her life and requests.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Options are presented non‑coercively and decisions are explicitly framed as the client's, supporting choice and control throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits intrinsic motives and reasons for change effectively but could probe deeper into values and amplify pivotal statements or use scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician adopts a shared, power‑sharing stance, repeatedly asking for input and offering options so the client shapes the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited and reinforced with concrete planning, though there were missed opportunities to deepen commitment language and assess importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician skillfully avoids reinforcing sustain talk, uses double‑sided reflections to legitimize concerns, and redirects to coping strategies and contingency plans.
