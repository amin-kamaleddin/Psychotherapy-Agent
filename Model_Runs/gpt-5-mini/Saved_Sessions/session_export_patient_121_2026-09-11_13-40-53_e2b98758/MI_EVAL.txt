# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating of Sofia's ambivalence, which keeps her engaged; minor caution that repeatedly labeling the plan as “smart” could feel evaluative to a guarded teen.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening with accurate reflections that mirror Sofia's language and invite correction; could deepen attunement by naming underlying hurt or isolation more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on making school/lunch more manageable with concrete, testable steps and avoids going off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functions largely as an equal partner and co-creates the plan, but frequent summaries and option-sets occasionally steer the interaction rather than fully leaving the agenda to Sofia.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly honors Sofia's choices and preferences (including excluding adults) and asks how she wants to test the plan, which supports ownership and reduces resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits actionable motivation and commitment language (three-day test, wrist tap), but misses opportunities to probe personal importance, past strengths, and confidence scaling to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites Sofia to choose and problem-solve together, yet occasional strong labeling/summarizing subtly shifts the dynamic toward clinician framing; increasing open invitations to generate options would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits concrete, action-focused change talk and reinforces it with summaries, but could further draw out internal reasons for change and confidence/values language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges and accepts sustain talk without arguing, offering alternatives and safety planning that reduce resistance; later gentle exploration of pros/cons of adult involvement could be added as trust grows.
