# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveys clear, nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizes ambivalence, and honors Amina's boundaries (e.g., excluding the caseworker). A small improvement would be occasional explicit affirmations of Amina's courage for staying in the conversation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening capture both practical goals and underlying feelings, with good summarizing of what success would look like. Occasionally adding brief emotion-focused reflections could deepen the empathic tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on a single, achievable target behavior and guides the conversation from idea to concrete plan and outcomes. Pacing and sequencing effectively move toward a specific, observable goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a collaborative problem-solving exchange: the clinician elicits ideas, offers options, and iteratively incorporates Amina's responses into the plan. The clinician's role is facilitative, organizing the client's decisions into a coherent strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options and asks which choice feels most doable, reinforcing Amina's control and honoring her choice to keep the caseworker out. Language remains nonprescriptive and supports the client's locus of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses invitational questions to draw out Amina's own solutions and motivation, leading to concrete commitments like the sign, script, and headphones. The clinician elicits internal reasons and observable markers of success rather than imposing ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly invites Amina's input and treats her as the expert, co-constructing the plan (sign, script, routine) from her choices. This demonstrates genuine power-sharing in setting the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and reinforces change language through open questions, reflections, and planning, leading to specific behavioral commitments. Scaling and outcome questions help consolidate desire, ability, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively softens resistance with double-sided reflections, avoids arguing, and redirects to small, controllable steps that reduce sustain talk's influence. If resistance resurfaces, an additional explicit double-sided reflection could be used.
