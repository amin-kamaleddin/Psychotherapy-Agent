# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect, validating Marco’s perspective and normalizing difficulty. Praises concrete choices without judgment and avoids blaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, timely reflections that capture both practical constraints and emotional stance, helping Marco feel understood. Uses selective reflections to reinforce Marco’s self‑awareness and encourage elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on concrete, measurable actions (day/time, number of containers, alarm, text to wife). Questions and summaries are aimed at producing a specific, time‑bound commitment and contingency plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem‑solving, invites Marco’s input on feasibility, and integrates his choices into the plan. Positions clinician as partner/co‑investigator rather than authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking permission before advice, and respecting Marco’s limits. Invites commitment noncoercively and elicits preferred backup plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Centers the session on eliciting Marco’s own motivations and strengths through scaling questions and recall of past successes. Draws out self‑generated reasons and concrete steps rather than supplying them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Marco as the expert on his life, offering choices and checking exact wording for messages to his wife. Power is shared and Marco’s contributions shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk using scaling, past successes, and requests for specific plans and commitments. Reflects and reinforces Marco’s statements of intent to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and redirects toward planning, but leans toward neutral problem‑solving. Could more strategically use double‑sided reflections or discrepancy to further weaken sustain talk.
