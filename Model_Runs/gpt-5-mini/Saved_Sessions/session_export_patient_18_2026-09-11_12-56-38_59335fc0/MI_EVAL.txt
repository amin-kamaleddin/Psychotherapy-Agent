# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing competing priorities and offering choices without blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening around plans and priorities, but missed opportunities to explore and reflect the patient’s underlying nervousness about sounding "weird."

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused target (managing alcohol-related social situations to protect school functioning) and moved logically to specific, time-bound actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with co-creation of scripts, role-play options, and repeated invitations for the patient to choose next steps and wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and the patient's control by offering options and asking which ideas the patient wanted to try, fostering ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient’s own solutions and commitment to try the plan, but could further strengthen motivation by linking actions to values and eliciting importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative, shared expertise with the patient, and invited the patient to select phrasing and tone, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited change language and concrete steps, producing commitment statements, though more frequent amplification and summarizing of change talk would consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward strategies, but did not routinely use double-sided reflections or explicitly acknowledge reasons for the status quo to gently soften ambivalence.
