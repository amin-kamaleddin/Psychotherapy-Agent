# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and honors the client's privacy preferences without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring the patient's language and routinely checking what feels safe.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on small, specific habit changes and the support check‑in, guiding the conversation logically from options to an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Jointly constructs the plan with the client, repeatedly soliciting preferences (timing, wording, privacy) and adapting the approach based on her input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites choice, asks what the client would actually do, and honors limits (e.g., not involving parents), preserving the client's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's ideas and reasons for change but could deepen evocation by exploring values, past successes, or confidence/commitment strength.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exhibits a collaborative, power-sharing stance by deferring to the client as the expert and co-creating the exact message and plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicits and amplifies change language, asks about concrete signs of benefit, and converts vague desire into specific, testable actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects toward small experiments, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and exploring costs of staying the same to further soften sustain talk.
