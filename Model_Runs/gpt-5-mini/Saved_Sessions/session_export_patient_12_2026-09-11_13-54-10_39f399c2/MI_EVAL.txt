# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with reflective summaries, but offers few explicit affirmations or normalization; more unconditional positive regard would strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that keeps the client heard, but could probe deeper emotional nuance or bodily experience to deepen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing risk and coping with urges, consistently guiding the session toward concrete, actionable steps like drafting a short text to Leah.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative and egalitarian: the clinician asks permission, co-creates wording, and defers to the client's voice in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice by offering multiple options, asking what feels doable, and avoiding pressure while inviting the client to tweak plans to fit her voice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own ideas and a concrete change intention (texting Leah), but could further draw out intrinsic motives, values, and confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a genuine partnership—‘figuring this out with you’—and co-creates plans, demonstrating clear power-sharing and joint problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and scaffolds specific change-oriented language and small steps, yet could more strongly amplify and reflect commitment and confidence to strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validates and reflects sustain talk but rarely uses strategic moves (double-sided reflections, reframing, selective emphasis) to soften sustain language and pivot to change.
