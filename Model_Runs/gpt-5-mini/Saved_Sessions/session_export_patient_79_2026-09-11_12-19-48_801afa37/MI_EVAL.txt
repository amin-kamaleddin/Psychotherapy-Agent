# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and avoided judgment, explicitly affirming and normalizing the patient's preferences and skepticism. Tone remained respectful throughout with no blaming or moralizing language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately paraphrasing the patient’s concerns and feelings. Repeated reflective summaries showed clear understanding of the patient's viewpoint.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on simplifying and implementing the asthma regimen with concrete steps and timelines. The clinician repeatedly returned to feasible options and sequencing without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with decisions co-constructed and follow-up plans negotiated. The clinician offered actions only after asking permission and used language that promoted equal contribution to problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed the patient's autonomy by offering options and asking which tasks the patient preferred to handle. Avoided pressuring language and respected the patient's limits and communication preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the patient with open questions and amplified the patient's own reasons and ideas for change. Elicited concrete commitments and organized the patient's motivators into a doable plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint project and explicitly asked which parts the patient wanted to handle versus clinician support. Language and offers reflected clear power-sharing and treated the patient as the primary actor.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk through open, change-oriented questions and reflective summaries. Converted ambivalence into specific, actionable change language and small commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward practical actions, accepting skepticism and reframing around minimal burden. Missed a few opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections or deeper exploration of the emotional content behind sarcasm.
