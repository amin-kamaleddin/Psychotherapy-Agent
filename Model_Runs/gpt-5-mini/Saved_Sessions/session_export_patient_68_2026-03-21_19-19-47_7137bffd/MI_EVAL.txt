# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, respectful regard, normalized embarrassment, and offered options rather than directives, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening were used throughout, with empathetic summaries that matched the patient's tone and validated mixed feelings about drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing acute alcohol-related risk with concrete plans (taxi, scripts, rehearsal), though occasional teasing/pushy remarks briefly diverted the safety-oriented direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operated largely as an equal partner by offering options, co-creating language, and rehearsing, but moments of directive teasing momentarily shifted the tone away from fully mutual problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed choice and offered reversible, low-pressure options, but later teasing/pushy comments undermined autonomy on a few occasions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own reasons (embarrassment, avoid injury) and translated them into action through role-play and planning, but could use more systematic open questions and scaling to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the session as collaborative, invited input and choices, and shared suggestions without dictating, supporting genuine power-sharing in plan development.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk and turned it into a concrete, low-pressure plan (taxi commitment), though could more often elicit and amplify intrinsic motives and use scaling for importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used reframing to preserve social positives while reducing harm, but occasional direct push/persuasion and teasing reinforced sustain talk and weakened soft redirection.
