# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing and affirming Malik's perspective and strengths which supported rapport and engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captured content and some feelings, but reflections were sometimes surface-level and could probe deeper emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on a specific, behavioral target and consistently moved toward concrete, actionable steps without drifting from the goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction with the clinician soliciting Malik's preferences, integrating his ideas, and co-creating the plan rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported Malik's sense of choice and ownership by offering options, reflecting his priorities, and endorsing his self-generated plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited pragmatic motivations and past strategies, but could have probed deeper intrinsic values and longer-term goals to strengthen evoked motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear power-sharing was evident as the clinician repeatedly asked for Malik's ideas and deferred to his preferences when shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited and reinforced concrete change language and workable steps, but could more strongly amplify and summarize change talk to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and gently redirected toward compromise solutions, though more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries could deepen discrepancy.
