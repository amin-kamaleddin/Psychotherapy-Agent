# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalized skepticism, and avoided judgment while working with the patient's constraints.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used high-quality reflections and summaries that matched the patient's pragmatic, skeptical tone and allowed the patient to elaborate without feeling confronted.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on increasing brief physical activity and progressively guided the patient from options to a concrete, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan by soliciting and integrating the patient's practical ideas, demonstrating genuine shared decision-making throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered options, framed the approach as an experiment rather than a directive, and repeatedly validated the patient's right to choose or remain skeptical.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete reasons and commitments but relied heavily on logistics and implementation rather than probing deeper intrinsic motivators, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert on what fits their life, invited their ideas, and built the plan collaboratively rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited planning and intention language, but made fewer strategic attempts to amplify desire, importance, or confidence statements to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and pivoted to pragmatic experiments, though missed opportunities for more active softening such as double-sided reflections or reframing.
