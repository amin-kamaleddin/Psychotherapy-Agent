# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Set a clear nonjudgmental tone and used warm, validating language throughout, avoiding labels and moralizing to keep the adolescent engaged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that captured Tyler’s perspective and affect, and asked open questions that demonstrated understanding of his concerns and needs.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior and consequences, consistently tying discussion back to Tyler’s goal and moving toward concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, invited Tyler’s input at each decision point, and co-created practical plans while positioning the clinician as a resource rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and respected Tyler’s decisions (e.g., declining role-play), reinforcing his control over which options to try and how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focused on eliciting Tyler’s own motivations and solutions, using his goals (fewer fights, not hiding) as the basis for planning and asking questions that drew out intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently invited Tyler’s preferences and respected his choices, though occasionally offered bundled suggestions that felt slightly more directive than purely elicitive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced emerging change talk and helped specify actions, but missed some opportunities to deepen and amplify commitment language (DARN-C) earlier.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolled with resistance and gently pivoted to Tyler’s goals to avoid strengthening sustain talk, but could have used more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and soften sustain language further.
