# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (opening line, validating reflections, affirming protective behavior), creating a respectful, supportive tone throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and accurate reflections that captured practical details and some feelings, though the clinician could deepen emotional nuance (worry/shame/fear) with more affect-laden reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on childcare and linked it to broader goals, shifting promptly to concrete, implementable options and stepwise tools to maintain momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: the clinician co-produced materials and decisions, asked permission, invited patient preferences, and iteratively edited texts/scripts based on Daniela's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Daniela's sense of choice and control by offering multiple options, asking which felt most helpful, and avoiding pressuring language while preserving decision ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations and resources (what worked on better days, linking reliable childcare to other changes), but could probe deeper into personal values and confidence to amplify internal reasons for sustained change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine power-sharing by positioning Daniela as the expert, offering choices, repeatedly asking permission, and co-creating tailored, practical tools together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk about childcare and behavior change, yet missed opportunities to amplify stronger commitment language (scaling questions, specific next-step commitments).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and offered practical options, but used few deliberate techniques (double-sided reflections, reframing) to soften ambivalence and pivot more clearly toward change.
