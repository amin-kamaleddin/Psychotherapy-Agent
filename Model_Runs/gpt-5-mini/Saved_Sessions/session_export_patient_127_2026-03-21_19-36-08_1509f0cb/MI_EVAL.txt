# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, validates progress and mixed feelings without blame, and maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, affect-laden reflective listening and perspective-taking that helps the client feel understood about urges, guilt, and small stabilizing pleasures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on preventing relapse and building prosocial stability, with every intervention tied back to those target behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine joint problem-solving, solicits the client's lived experience, tests suggestions, and accepts client modifications to co-create the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves the client's sense of choice with permission checks and option-based suggestions, avoiding pressuring language and reinforcing ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation and moments of change talk (e.g., 22 days, porch rituals), but could more deeply explore values, identity, and use scaling to amplify commitment and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—clinician invites input, incorporates client choices, and builds the plan around the client's resources and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and planning, yet could probe more for intrinsic reasons, concrete value-linked goals, and use scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes ambivalence permissively while redirecting to strategies, though missed opportunities for strategic double-sided reflections to reduce sustain pull.
