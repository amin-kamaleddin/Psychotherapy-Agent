# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respects the patient's concerns, validating fear and accommodating requests; could add more explicit affirmations of coping efforts to deepen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, sustained empathic listening and reflective summaries and checks for urgent signs; occasionally could name deeper emotions (e.g., isolation or exhaustion) to further attune.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on obtaining an urgent GI referral and safety checks, keeping the conversation tightly on target and clinically relevant.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly cooperative interaction that repeatedly invites and integrates the patient's input, co‑constructing the plan and documents rather than issuing directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy through multiple choices, permission‑seeking, and tailoring options to the patient; could add brief affirmations of the patient's capacity to decide and act.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits goals and action-oriented motivation for tests and referrals but stays mostly instrumental; could deepen evocation by exploring values, desired outcomes, and confidence to try concurrent coping strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative stance with shared decision‑making (format, wording, priorities), positioning the patient as an active agent in planning and next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of change‑related options and task‑focused planning, but limited amplification of the patient's desire, ability, reasons, and confidence for symptom‑management changes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and validates resistance to purely psychological explanations, steering to actionable steps; could more actively use double‑sided reflections and selectively emphasize any pro‑change statements to shift language further.
