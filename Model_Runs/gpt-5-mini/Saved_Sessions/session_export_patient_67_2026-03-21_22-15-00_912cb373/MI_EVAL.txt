# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, validating language and avoids judgment, which created a safe, nonjudgmental atmosphere throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and accurately captures ambivalence and concrete concerns, but reflections are mainly content-focused with missed opportunities to deepen emotional resonance around feelings like nervousness or 'slipping.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction and moves logically from assessment to a specific, doable plan while keeping the conversation goal-directed without pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑creates the plan and message wording, invites Kenji's input and tweaks, and negotiates decisions in partnership rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by asking permission before suggesting options, offering multiple choices, and affirming Kenji's control over which plan feels right for him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits values, reasons, and barriers and uses scaling to draw out motivation, but could more actively amplify motivational statements and elicit an explicit commitment to consolidate change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the interaction as co‑constructed, uses permission language, and clearly positions Kenji as the expert on his life while offering collaborative support.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and shapes change talk with targeted questions and concrete planning prompts, but would benefit from more deliberate amplification, summarization, and elicitation of commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes ambivalence, and redirects toward change, though it lacks more advanced softening techniques like double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to further reduce sustain talk.
