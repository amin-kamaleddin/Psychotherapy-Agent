# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized slips, and respected the patient’s boundary around alcohol, with no shaming or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections of affect and motivation and asked about feelings, but moved fairly quickly to problem-solving without deeper exploration of the patient’s guardedness and stressors.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on evening smoking with concrete, time-bound goals and a logical progression from assessment to plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner throughout—language was invitational, preferences were solicited, and the plan (reminder wording, calendar entry) was co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored choice and preserved autonomy by offering options, accepting the decision not to address alcohol, and phrasing suggestions rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited key motivators (worry about cancer, son) and used the patient’s language in reminders, but could more systematically probe intrinsic reasons, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—invited input, co-created the plan and reminders, and shared power in decision-making rather than prescribing a single course.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk, praised the small goal and secured commitment to start tonight, but missed opportunities for more evocative open questions and scaling confidence/importance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and honored the alcohol boundary, though did not use techniques like double-sided reflections or selective reflection to actively weaken sustain talk.
