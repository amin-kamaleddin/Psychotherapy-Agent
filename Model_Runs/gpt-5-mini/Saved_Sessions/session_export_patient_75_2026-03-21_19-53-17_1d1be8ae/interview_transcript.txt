PSYCHIATRIST: For MDIs (like fluticasone/albuterol): shake the inhaler, attach to the spacer, exhale, seal lips around the mouthpiece, press the canister once and inhale slowly and deeply, then hold your breath 5–10 seconds before exhaling. Wait ~30–60 seconds between puffs. Clean the spacer per manufacturer (usually weekly, air dry).

Rinsing trick: after any corticosteroid puff, rinse your mouth and gargle with water, then spit; brush teeth if possible. This greatly lowers hoarseness and thrush risk. For DPIs, don’t use a spacer.
