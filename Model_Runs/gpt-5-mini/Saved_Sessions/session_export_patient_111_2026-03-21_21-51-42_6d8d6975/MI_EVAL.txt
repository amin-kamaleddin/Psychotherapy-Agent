# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicated respect and nonjudgmental regard, normalizing ambivalence and validating Richard's feelings. They avoided labeling or moralizing and accepted his stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, specific reflections and paraphrases that matched Richard's language and tone, demonstrating strong understanding. The clinician also checked permission and summarized both sides of his experience, indicating high empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking and moved the conversation toward a small, concrete next step (CO test Tuesday 7:30). The clinician avoided drifting and secured a time‑bound plan consistent with the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently worked as an equal partner, inviting Richard's input on decisions and co‑creating scripts and the action plan. The clinician tailored options to Richard's preferences and implemented his chosen wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Richard's right to choose, asked permission, and offered options rather than directives. The clinician respected boundaries (no lectures/no follow‑up) and emphasized that further steps would be the patient's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations tied to his wife and health and offered low‑threat options that surfaced some change talk. However, the clinician could have probed more deeply to draw out intrinsic reasons and personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Richard as an expert on his life and involved him in each decision point, co‑constructing the plan and scripts. Invitations to choose wording and timing reflect genuine power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced some change talk (importance scale, relational and health reasons) and secured a concrete next step (CO test). There were missed opportunities to more frequently reflect, amplify, and explore specifics to deepen desire, ability, reasons, and need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently rolled with resistance, avoided arguing, and offered harm‑reduction options aligned with his routine. The clinician could have used more double‑sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge sustain talk while guiding toward change.
