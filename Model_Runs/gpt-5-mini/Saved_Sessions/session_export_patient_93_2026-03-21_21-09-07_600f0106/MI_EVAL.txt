# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened explicitly with warmth and nonjudgmental stance and maintained affirming reflections and summaries throughout, showing clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate content reflections and validation demonstrating understanding, but missed opportunities for deeper affective reflections about the fall and meaning of independence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a steady, clear focus on reducing alcohol with concrete, behaviorally specific goals and implementation planning throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan by offering options, inviting selection, rehearsing language, and adjusting phrasing to fit the patient's voice, showing strong joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by asking permission before offering ideas, providing options rather than directives, and checking which strategies felt natural to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from the patient's own reasons and language, eliciting importance/confidence ratings and building on the patient's change statements to form a plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited patient input and shared power well, though could more explicitly acknowledge the patient's expertise and past successful strategies to strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with scaling and concrete steps, but could more consistently amplify and elaborate spontaneous change statements with evocative follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and neutrally reflected sustain talk while pivoting to change, but could increase use of double-sided reflections and strategic reframing to further reduce sustain talk.
