# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental language and normalization of ambivalence, never criticizing or labeling the patient.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking through frequent, specific reflections that capture both content and tone, using that understanding to advance the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol use and moves the session from exploration to a concrete, time-bound plan (trigger, cue, test night).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits the patient's input, equalizes expertise, and co-constructs the plan while building on the patient's past successes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces choice by offering options, honoring preferences, and accepting qualified commitments without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivators and past strengths to generate change talk, but could probe deeper into values and confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the patient's expertise, resulting in a plan that clearly comes from the patient's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies multiple change statements and helps form a concrete plan, though could more explicitly highlight and summarize benefits to deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with double-sided reflections and avoids arguing, but could more explicitly explore minimizing language to convert ambivalence into firmer commitments.
