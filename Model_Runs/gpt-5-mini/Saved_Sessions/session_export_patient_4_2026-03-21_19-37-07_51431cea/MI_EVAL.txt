# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows consistent respect, validation, and practical support which conveys warmth; however some clinical, directive language and limited explicit normalization of the patient’s fears slightly reduce unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective‑taking and reflective listening with safety checks and symptom-focused reflections; missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of underlying emotions or mistrust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on obtaining diagnostic evaluation and concrete next steps, aligning questions and interventions with the patient’s primary concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with repeated solicitation of the patient’s input and tailoring of materials; at times the clinician quickly prescribes specific tests without fully co‑prioritizing the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, respecting limits, and providing tools (draft letters/scripts) that reinforce the patient’s control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some immediate action preferences but relies more on expert solutions than drawing out the patient’s own values, reasons, strengths, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and co‑constructs documents with the patient, but occasional expert‑driven language and authoritative test lists reduce shared ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers practical behavioral suggestions that produced some change‑oriented language, yet does not actively elicit or amplify intrinsic motivation, values, past successes, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids directly reinforcing sustain talk and validates requests for testing, but lacks MI strategies (e.g., double‑sided reflections, strategic reframing) and some firm wording may unintentionally entrench defensive sustain talk.
