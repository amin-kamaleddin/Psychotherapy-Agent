# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through empathic statements and validation, and respected the patient's boundaries (e.g., not contacting the sister unless crisis threshold reached).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate empathic listening with timely reflections and summaries that matched affect and validated the patient's distress throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on imminent suicide risk and immediate safety, steering the conversation toward concrete, time-limited safety planning and means restriction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative with the clinician inviting the patient's input, co-writing messages and coping items, and negotiating plan details rather than taking an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice by asking permission, offering options for securing pills, honoring preferences about contacts, and avoiding pressuring language while clarifying safety limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical ideas and internal resources (lockbox, contacts, coping strategies), but did not sufficiently probe deeper values or systematically build commitment and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed the session as a joint effort, soliciting Maria's choices at key points and co-constructing the safety plan and materials in partnership with her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some brief change talk (a 'tiny part' wanting help, caring about sister, agreeing to delay/lock pills) but missed opportunities to amplify these statements or use rulers and follow-ups to produce stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward safety planning through validation and behavioral steps, though could have used more MI-specific techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframing) to further soften sustain talk.
