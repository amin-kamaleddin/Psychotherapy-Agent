# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, acknowledging ambivalence and normalizing difficulty while offering affirmations throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and reflections (validated worries and ambivalence), but could have used deeper, extended reflections to explore underlying emotions more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on parental modeling and exposure reduction with concrete steps and a specific behavioral experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session felt largely collaborative with choices and rehearsals, though collaboration was somewhat reduced by supplying many ready-made scripts instead of eliciting the client's own ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported client choice with repeated invitations, avoided pressure, and affirmed realistic limits and self-kindness if attempts fail.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation and values but relied more on offering solutions than drawing out the client's internal reasons, confidence, or past successes to deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited preferences and input at times but was often expert-driven with multiple prescriptive suggestions; more joint problem-solving would enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change talk around small steps, but missed opportunities to amplify desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language more strategically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and redirected to feasible harm-reduction strategies, though more use of double-sided reflections or explicit normalization could further soften sustain talk.
