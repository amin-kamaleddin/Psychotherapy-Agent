### Collaboration

**Comments:** Regularly elicits Erik's own reasons and specific plans, turning vague motivation into concrete, self-generated commitments. Questions draw out intrinsic motivators and implementation details.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and co-creates the plan as an experiment, fostering client ownership. Repeatedly invites Erik to choose and personalize strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and develops change talk with open, choice-oriented questions and requests for implementation details. Reinforces and deepens client-produced motivation and commitments.
