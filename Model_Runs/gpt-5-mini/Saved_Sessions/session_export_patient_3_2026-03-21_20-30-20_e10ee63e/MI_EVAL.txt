# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and positive regard with frequent affirmations and normalizing language, avoiding judgment and praising the client's realistic steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that validated concerns, but could deepen affect-focused and more complex reflections to capture emotional intensity (e.g., 'I feel gross').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a tight focus on the target behavior (starting to exercise) with concrete steps, cues, dosing, a first-try plan, and scheduled check-ins to guide action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted joint problem-solving with the client’s preferences shaping the plan, offered help only if desired, and used inclusive language to support shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering multiple options, inviting edits, avoiding pressuring language, and emphasizing the client's control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation through open questions and scaling, but at times offered solutions early; could further draw out client-generated strategies, values, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established a collaborative stance throughout, co-creating the plan with the client and repeatedly soliciting preferences for moves, cues, reminders, and check-ins.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicited and reinforced change talk using open questions, reflections, scaling, and follow-up prompts that strengthened the client's readiness and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk and tactically redirected to solutions and backups, but could better use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
