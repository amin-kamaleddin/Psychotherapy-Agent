# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with normalizing statements and affirmations, but some phrasing felt formulaic and there were missed opportunities for more individualized validation of effort and difficulty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate paraphrases and reflections helped the patient feel understood and prompted planning, but reflections were often surface-level and repetitive; deeper, more complex reflections of emotion would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity and moved the conversation toward concrete implementation intentions and a backup plan, effectively guiding from exploration to action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like genuine joint problem-solving, with the clinician inviting the patient's ideas for cues, support, and backup plans and incorporating those ideas into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice by offering options, deferring to the patient's judgment, and normalizing limits, which reinforced the patient's sense of control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out the patient's practical solutions and some motives with evocative questions, but could have probed deeper intrinsic values, long-term meaning, and confidence to better anchor motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveyed power-sharing and invited collaboration (e.g., asking what the patient thinks is realistic), demonstrating a strong partnership stance throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk leading to specific planning language, but missed opportunities to amplify commitments or assess confidence/importance to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized ambivalence and avoided arguing, yet relied mainly on neutral reflections rather than strategic double-sided reflections or summaries and missed deeper exploration of sustain drivers.
