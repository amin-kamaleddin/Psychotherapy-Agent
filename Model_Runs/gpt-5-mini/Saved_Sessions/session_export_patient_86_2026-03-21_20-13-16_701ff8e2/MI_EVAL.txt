# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard, validated the patient's pride, and honored boundaries against lectures throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good reflective listening and perspective-taking but missed chances to deepen empathy by reflecting emotional tone and exploring the meaning of past health scares.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking, moved efficiently to a concrete one-week behavior plan, and closed with a practical printable checklist.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—solicited preferences, adapted language to the patient, and co-created the one-page checklist the patient requested.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's choice by offering options, asking permission before advising, and emphasizing a small plan on the patient's terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited actionable motivation (teeth, money, delaying first cigarette) but could have probed deeper intrinsic reasons and used more evocative questions to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged the patient as an equal partner by asking permission, offering choices, and letting the patient's preferences drive the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change talk, invited a specific one-week plan, and helped the patient move from sustain talk to concrete steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolled with resistance and reframed toward small options, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and more strategic statements to further soften sustain talk.
