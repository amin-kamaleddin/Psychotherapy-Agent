# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client's choices without judgment, validating limits and avoiding pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, repeated reflective listening that captures both content and affect, mirroring tentative language to validate Isabella's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the single target behavior (attending one brief local activity) and narrows discussion to concrete, actionable elements (10–15 min, exit plan, check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint effort, inviting co‑creation of the plan and adapting questions based on the client's responses to foster an equitable process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice throughout, offering options and permission statements rather than directives and checking what feels doable for the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits internal motives (less alone, pride) with evocative questions, but could deepen intrinsic motivation by probing past successes and using scaling or confidence rulers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invites the client's input, though occasional clinician‑expressed belief statements slightly reduce client ownership and could be replaced by elicitation or reflections.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out change talk and concrete small steps, but misses chances to amplify and consolidate those statements into stronger commitment or use scaling to boost commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence with double‑sided reflections, yet could more strategically pivot sustain language toward change through selective reinforcement and evocative contrasts.
