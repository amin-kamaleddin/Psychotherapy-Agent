# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental support and validated the patient's perspective, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening with accurate reflections and summaries of concerns; could deepen affective and complex reflections to explore underlying emotions like worry or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on behavior change and moved the patient from general desire to specific action with concrete planning (times, duration, barriers, backups).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was a clear partnership with the clinician soliciting and integrating the patient's ideas into the plan, using joint problem-solving rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirmed the patient's choice, offered options instead of mandates, and emphasized that the patient was in charge of decisions and pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Practitioner drew out the patient's own motivations and reasons for change using open questions, scaling, and past successes rather than supplying arguments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed with shared decision-making, invitations to choose, and explicit empowerment language that demonstrated strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and amplified change talk, guiding the patient to concrete commitments (start time, frequency, goals) and perceived benefits of change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not confronted; the clinician skillfully redirected to small, manageable steps and supports, minimizing resistance and promoting action.
