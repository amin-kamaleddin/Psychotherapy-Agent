# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone throughout the session. Validated Linda’s perspective and normalized ambivalence without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used solid reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases of ambivalence. Missed opportunities to deepen empathic reflections of Linda’s internal emotional experience and low confidence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on protecting children from smoke exposure with questions and interventions tied to that target. Steered the conversation toward actionable steps aligned with Linda’s values while respecting ambivalence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in joint problem-solving, invited Linda’s input, and co-constructed a concrete, feasible plan with supports and a start date. Used inclusive language and sought agreement for next steps, producing a strong sense of shared responsibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Linda’s right to choose and avoided pressuring her to quit, offering suggestions only with permission. Accepted her decision not to quit, reinforcing autonomy and reducing resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Linda’s own reasons for change using rulers and open questions and linked motivation to parenting values. Relied somewhat more on providing practical solutions than on deeper elicitation and amplification of internal motives or narratives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by asking permission and inviting choices, acknowledging Linda’s expertise. At times offered multiple clinician-sourced solutions which shifted slightly away from eliciting client-generated options first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced child-focused change talk and helped translate it into a concrete plan and start date. Could more strategically amplify and expand on expressed reasons to build stronger momentum toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting or reinforcing sustain talk and shifted focus toward protective behaviors for the children. Could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly voice ambivalence and gently reduce the weight of sustain talk.
