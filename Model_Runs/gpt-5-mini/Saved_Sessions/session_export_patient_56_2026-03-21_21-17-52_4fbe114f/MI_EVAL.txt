# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently showed respect and positive regard, normalizing Isolde's priorities and validating her skepticism without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and summaries demonstrated strong perspective-taking and nuanced understanding of Isolde's concerns and values throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the specific behavior (phone-free dinners) with repeated steering toward concrete details, planning, and a clear action commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was joint problem-solving: the clinician offered options and tools while relying on Isolde's input, checking back and rehearsing language together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly reinforced Isolde's choice and control by framing the plan as an experiment, asking permission, and offering alternatives that preserved her ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited motivations and planning but moved toward offering solutions before fully deepening Isolde's own articulated motives or exploring past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear collaborative stance was maintained, with the clinician soliciting preferences, deferring to Isolde on wording, and co-constructing the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicited and reinforced change language by asking for specifics, confidence ratings, and criteria for noticing difference, and reflected those commitments back.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed as 'realism,' reducing resistance, but the clinician could have explored its roots more and used more double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
