# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, using normalizing language and validation without blame; repeatedly affirmed the patient's experience and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and surface-level reflections (burden, effort) but could deepen empathic resonance with more complex, evocative reflections exploring underlying feelings or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on charging the device throughout the encounter, linking every intervention back to the target behavior and keeping momentum toward a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by inviting input, staying with the patient during the task, negotiating rewards, and co-creating the low-effort plan that reflected the patient's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, emphasizing no pressure, and accepting limited commitment without shaming, which fostered patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some of the patient's observations and small motivations (e.g., noticing the light) but relied on external supports and missed deeper probing of intrinsic motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated shared decision-making throughout, inviting preferences and negotiating the plan so the patient's contributions shaped the outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced small change talk and a concrete micro-action, but missed chances to amplify the patient's own reasons and benefits to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed avoidance into a manageable experiment, though could have used more double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence and highlight small wins.
