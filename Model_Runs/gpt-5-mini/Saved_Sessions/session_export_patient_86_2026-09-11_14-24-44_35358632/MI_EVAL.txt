# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard, honoring boundaries and validating Marco's long smoking history without judgment. The warm, nonjudgmental tone and explicit acceptance of preferences supported safety and rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening and accurate reflections were used, matching Marco's language and naming emotions which deepened understanding. These reflections helped move Marco from guarded resistance to concrete planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a clear focus on smoking-related goals throughout, guiding the conversation from options to a specific, actionable plan. Questions and reflections consistently tied back to concrete steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treated Marco as an equal partner, co-creating the plan and soliciting his practical input on timing, wording, and tactics. Joint decision-making (plan, texts, pause word) demonstrated genuine power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported by offering choices, asking consent for follow-up, and respecting limits (no calls, no lectures). This approach fostered ownership and reduced pressure to comply.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicited Marco's own motivations and practical reasons for change, asking what would make change easier and what small signs would indicate success. These prompts drew out intrinsic motives and self-efficacy signals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session clearly demonstrated partnership through negotiated decisions, shared planning, and mutual agreement on supports and boundaries. The clinician repeatedly invited Marco's preferences and followed his lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician skillfully elicited and amplified change talk by asking about doable options and inviting a tiny first step, then reflecting and affirming Marco's commitments. This consistently deepened Marco's change language and led to concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician rolled with resistance rather than confronting sustain talk, affirming autonomy and reframing to reduce defensiveness. This approach avoided arguing and allowed exploration of low-burden change options.
