### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—normalized ambivalence, validated Marek’s experience, and praised small steps throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate empathic listening and reflections that matched Marek’s feelings and perspective across the encounter.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on weekend/social drinking with logical progression from feedback to concrete planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly collaborative—invited Marek’s input and choices—but frequent offering of specific tactics occasionally positioned the clinician more as an expert than an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Marek’s right to choose by offering options and asking him to select what to try, though one phrasing of the check-in sounded slightly directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited willingness, confidence, and pros/cons, but tended to follow up with many suggestions instead of deeper exploration of Marek’s own values or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively and co-constructed a plan, but repeatedly supplying numerous concrete strategies reduced opportunities for Marek to generate his own solutions first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk and concrete commitments, but missed opportunities to deepen spontaneous change language with targeted evocative probes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk while redirecting to options, but could strengthen practice with more double-sided reflections and asking permission before sharing normative info.
