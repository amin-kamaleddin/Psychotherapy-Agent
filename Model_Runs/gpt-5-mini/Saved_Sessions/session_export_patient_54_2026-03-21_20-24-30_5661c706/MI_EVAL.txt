# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, avoids judgment, and offers choices while affirming the client's priorities. This warm, respectful stance supported client comfort and engagement throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective listening, accurately capturing ambivalence and validating concerns. Could deepen empathic reflections by probing emotional nuance (fear, pressure, cultural meaning) more often.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy and moves the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps (script, handout, removal, substitutes, follow-up). The clinician balances exploration with steady movement toward a feasible plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuinely collaborative interaction by co-constructing the script and plan and repeatedly inviting the client's preferences. The clinician treats the client as an equal partner and integrates her ideas into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports client autonomy through agenda-setting, permission-seeking, and offering options rather than directives. The clinician consistently reinforces client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation from the client's values and reasons using scaling and solution-generation, helping move her toward preparation. Could more consistently sequence follow-ups to deepen change talk and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a strong partnership by sharing power, offering choices, and inviting input at each step. The clinician consistently involves the client in deciding next steps and plan details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with confidence rulers, reflections, and concrete small steps, and co-creates practical supports. Could more strategically amplify change statements and explicitly elicit time-bound commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk by asking permission before information and acknowledging skepticism, which prevents escalation. Could increase use of double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to further resolve ambivalence.
