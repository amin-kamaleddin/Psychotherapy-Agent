# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and normalized Elena’s concerns with affirmations and nonjudgmental language. The clinician repeatedly validated the patient’s perspective, avoiding dismissal or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening throughout, summarizing emotions and practical concerns. The clinician checked details and tailored responses that showed understanding of both feeling and facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the vaccine decision and progressed logically toward a concrete outcome (one-page handout and questions). Questions and options consistently advanced the decision-support plan without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan by soliciting Elena’s priorities at each decision point and adjusting the handout to fit her requests. Repeated offers to tailor wording, placement, and content demonstrated genuine partnership in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Affirmed choice and offered multiple options without pressure, inviting Elena to pick what felt most useful. The clinician repeatedly deferred to her preferences about wording, content, and next steps, supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicited Elena’s own motivations, concerns, and preferred next steps rather than prescribing solutions. Built on her responses to extract concrete, patient-centered goals and actions she felt would help.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative and invited patient input at every turn, sharing power in decision-making. The clinician’s repeated invitations to choose options and wording reinforced a partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively created conditions for change by co-creating concrete next steps and offering doable options, but did not consistently amplify tentative change language. Missed opportunities to reflect and elicit confidence or a specific commitment/timing statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward solutions, offering information and planning options. Could have used more double-sided reflections or selective amplification of change statements to more actively soften sustain talk.
