# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through empathic affirmations and normalizing language, avoiding labels or judgment and validating the client's preferences and limits.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, ongoing reflective listening and perspective‑taking by paraphrasing the client's concerns, echoing her language, and summarizing feelings and strengths.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior and steadily moves from values to a concrete, actionable plan with specifics on timing, frequency, and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine two‑person problem solving by soliciting ideas, integrating real‑world constraints, and finalizing a plan that clearly reflects the client's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and tailoring check‑ins and supports to her preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from within the client using evocative questions that elicit vivid, personally meaningful change talk and link those reasons to actionable steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by repeatedly inviting the client's input on options and co‑creating the plan, sharing power and decision making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions and reflections and secures a commitment to try three dances, though could more often amplify spontaneous change statements and use a confidence ruler to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles minimal sustain talk well through acknowledgment, reframing, and practical fallbacks, but could more frequently use double‑sided reflections to link sustain and change talk and resolve ambivalence.
