# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, acknowledging Nabil's frustration and responding respectfully throughout. No blaming or minimizing of his feelings was evident.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections that helped de‑escalate, including validating disruption to work and offering grounding. Could deepen by exploring emotional meaning and underlying concerns (long‑term impact, relationships, anxiety) more thoroughly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on managing anger and responding to appointment delays, progressively building a concrete plan (breathing, email, phone script, checklist). Resisted drifting and consistently returned to actionable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with joint drafting of messages, tone choices, and role‑play to rehearse the call. The clinician adapted to Nabil's preferences and co‑constructed the checklist and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options and affirmed Nabil's choices (grounding vs. message, firmer vs. formal tone), leaving final decisions to him. Normalized skepticism and avoided pressuring him to feel differently.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete commitments and preferences but relied heavily on offering scripted language rather than drawing out intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence. More open questions and scaling/confidence queries would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine partnership by sharing control, asking preferences, and co‑creating wording and next steps. The clinician positioned themself as a helper collaborating on Nabil's priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively moved toward concrete change actions and elicited commitment for sending the email and calling if needed, using options and action planning. Could further elicit intrinsic reasons, values, and stronger commitment language or scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated Nabil's skepticism and then pivoted to doable strategies, which redirected focus toward action. Could enhance by using more double‑sided reflections or explicit reframing to strategically address sustain talk.
