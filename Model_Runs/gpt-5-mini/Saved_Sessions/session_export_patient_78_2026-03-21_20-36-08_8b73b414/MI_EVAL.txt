# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment, validating ambivalence and praising practical planning. The clinician normalizes feelings and treats the patient with warmth and respect throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening that captures frustration and ambivalence. Could deepen with more complex reflections exploring emotional meaning to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on smoking reduction with a logical progression from goal selection to concrete strategies and contingency planning. Questions and reflections consistently tie back to the specific target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative with repeated solicitation of the patient's preferences and co‑construction of realistic steps. The clinician acts as a teammate offering suggestions rather than imposing directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces the patient's choice and control by offering options and checking preferences without coercion. The clinician supports ownership of the plan and respects the patient's right to try or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits patient‑generated reasons, strategies, and confidence ratings to draw out motivation. Could further strengthen by probing values (e.g., meaning of protecting her daughter) and amplifying change statements into a clear commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co‑constructs the plan with the patient, asking permission and offering alternatives while framing the patient as the decision‑maker. The interaction exemplifies mutual problem‑solving and power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with concrete if‑then plans, confidence scaling, and specific strategies. Could do more summarizing and magnifying of the patient's change language to convert desire and reasons into firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolls with resistance, validates frustration, and redirects to the patient's goals instead of arguing, which reduces sustain talk. To reach top score, the clinician could use more double‑sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both pulls and then shift toward change.
