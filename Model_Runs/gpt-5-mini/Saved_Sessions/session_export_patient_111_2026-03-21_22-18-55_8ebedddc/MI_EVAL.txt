# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, and honored the patient's limits without judgment; respectful and supportive throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, but reflections were often pragmatic rather than probing deeper affect (e.g., around 'I don't want to lose her'), so empathy could be deepened with stronger emotion-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (smoking) and a concrete goal (no smoking in the house for two weeks), with questions and interventions tied to that goal and a planned follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructed a practical, stepwise plan with the patient, responded to requests, and negotiated logistics, reflecting an equal partnership in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering options with permission, attributing expertise to the patient, respecting the two-week boundary, and avoiding pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation around relational reasons and used scaling to draw out confidence, but could further explore personal values, identity, and longer-term motives to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used inclusive language and invitations to choose, allowed the patient to shape the plan, and shared power throughout the session, demonstrating true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions and confidence scaling, though the clinician could better amplify and elaborate key change statements (e.g., 'I don't want to lose her') and draw out past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Successfully rolled with resistance and avoided reinforcing sustain talk or using confrontational tactics, instead normalizing ambivalence and redirecting to values and practical options.
