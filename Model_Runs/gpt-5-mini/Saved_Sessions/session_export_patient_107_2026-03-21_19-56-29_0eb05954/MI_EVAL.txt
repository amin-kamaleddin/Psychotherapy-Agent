# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding moralizing. Reassured safety and respected the patient's perspective throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing ambivalence and practical concerns. Could deepen empathic exploration of affect (e.g., emotional meaning of the routine or worry about BP) beyond logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on coffee consumption with a concrete seven-day tracking protocol and specified follow-up actions. Linked data collection directly to decision-making about next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed—solicited patient input, adjusted the plan (added cup#), and negotiated timing for a midweek check-in. Avoided an expert-only posture and invited the patient to shape the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking permission, and respecting the patient's skepticism and pace. Repeatedly framed choices and next steps as the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts to draw out motivation (questions about cups/symptoms and tracking) but relied more on pragmatic options than eliciting personal reasons, values, or goals. Missed opportunities for evocative questions and amplification of change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the interaction as a partnership and positioned the patient as the expert, offering choices and asking permission. The plan was co-created and adjusted to the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited modest change talk via the low-effort tracking experiment and small-step options, but did not consistently probe, amplify, or deepen brief change statements. More targeted questions about desire, ability, reasons, and need would strengthen cultivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by acknowledging coffee's benefits and offering nonthreatening options; framed tracking as information-gathering. Could increase use of double-sided reflections to explicitly name ambivalence and pivot to eliciting change talk.
