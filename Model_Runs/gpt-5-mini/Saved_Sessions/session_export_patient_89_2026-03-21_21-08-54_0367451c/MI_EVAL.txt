# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalized ambivalence, and asked permission before offering suggestions, honoring Marcus's preferences.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate complex reflections, but missed a few opportunities to deepen emotional empathy around discouragement and the impact of weight cycling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on increasing physical activity with logical momentum from barriers to brainstorming to a concrete plan and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was a true partnership—options were presented, Marcus chose the approach, and the clinician offered supports while checking his preferences at each step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly emphasized Marcus's choice and control, sought permission, and offered options rather than directives, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effective elicitation of Marcus's own motivations and ideas using open questions, scaling, and past-success exploration, which were then amplified into a concrete plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician and Marcus co-constructed the plan through shared problem-solving, offers to draft materials, and negotiation of a low-pressure follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful elicitation and reinforcement of change talk using targeted questions, scaling, and requests for concrete commitments that led to specific action steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and shifted toward small, feasible options, but the clinician used few targeted techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or exploring costs of staying the same) before moving to action.
