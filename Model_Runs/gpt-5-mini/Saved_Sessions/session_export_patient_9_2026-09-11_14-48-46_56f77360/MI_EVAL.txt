# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with early affirmations, nonjudgmental language, and validation of the client’s hesitation and feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent empathic listening and reflective summaries, effectively reflecting feelings (nervousness, safety concerns) and the client’s caring motivations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on a concrete goal (drafting/sending a neutral text and rehearsing delivery) and repeatedly brings prompts back to actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative—offers to do tasks with the client, asks which option she prefers, and tailors support (reading, role‑play, rehearsal) to her choice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client autonomy by offering multiple choices, honoring her rules (wait until child asleep), and affirming her right to decide and back out if it feels wrong.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client’s ideas and practical strategies through open questions, but could probe deeper into intrinsic motivators, past successes, and confidence levels.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys a strong partnership stance by sharing options, inviting the client to choose, and positioning the clinician as collaborator rather than director in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change‑oriented language and small doable steps that led to concrete plans, but missed opportunities to amplify desire/ability and elicit commitment or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and offers safety planning, but uses few deliberate techniques (double‑sided reflections, reframes, rolling with resistance) to actively soften or redirect sustain talk.
