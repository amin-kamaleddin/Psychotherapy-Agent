# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through validation, normalizing language, and a nonjudgmental tone that reduces shame and affirms the client's competence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with complex reflections, though there were a few missed opportunities to probe emotional nuance (e.g., anxiety about missing friends or restlessness).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on phone/social media use with actionable questions and reflections that keep momentum toward a concrete, time-limited experiment and measurable outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a genuine partnership: the clinician solicits preferences, integrates the client's prior strategies, and negotiates specifics rather than giving top-down advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, framing the plan as a voluntary one-week test, and emphasizing no pressure to be perfect, which reinforces ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and reasons for change, building on intrinsic drivers (less wrecked mornings, better grades) and asking the client to articulate success indicators.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance throughout—inviting the client's input on what feels doable and co-creating the plan so power and expertise are shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively and repeatedly elicits and strengthens change talk by prompting specifics, asking how the client will notice progress, and praising client-generated solutions and measures of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses double-sided reflections and normalization, but at times echoes sustain language rather than strategically shifting it by amplifying costs or selectively summarizing toward change.
