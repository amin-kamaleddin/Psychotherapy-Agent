# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating Linda's concerns and normalizing ambivalence. Clinician affirmed practical thinking and treated worries as legitimate rather than dismissing them.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing desires and worries. Reflections were generally content-level and missed opportunities for deeper, more complex emotional reflections about fear, shame, or life impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on inhaler adherence and incremental change, moving from elicitation to planning and troubleshooting. Questions consistently honed in on concrete, doable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner throughout, co-creating wording and inviting the patient's edits and preferences. Solicited input before acting and followed the patient's lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options and asked the patient’s preference, reinforcing that the patient decides what steps to take. Avoided pressuring language and supported choice and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited patient-generated solutions and small commitments that revealed motivation to try changes. Could strengthen evocation by probing personal reasons/values, importance/confidence, and past successes to build self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: invited preference, offered options, and deferred to the patient's expertise about feasibility. Co-created the plan and invited edits, demonstrating active power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete change talk and planning (alarm, spare inhaler, scripts) and produced commitments to try. Could better amplify and reinforce change talk by reflecting/summarizing commitments and eliciting importance/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and gently pivoted to problem-solving and safety planning. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframes to more actively reduce the persuasive pull of sustain talk.
