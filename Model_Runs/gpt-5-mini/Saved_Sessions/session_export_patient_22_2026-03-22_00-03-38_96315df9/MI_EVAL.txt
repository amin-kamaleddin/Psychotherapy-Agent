# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Camila’s experience, avoids labeling, and affirms her priorities to create a warm, respectful tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate content-and-feeling reflections; could sometimes probe deeper into underlying emotions (shame, loneliness, fear) with more affect-laden reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing problematic drinking with concrete, measurable goals and a logical progression from values to specific experiments and an implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative: the clinician invites Camila to set the agenda, jointly generates options, and checks back while framing suggestions tentatively and respecting her choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supports choice by offering options, asking which steps feel doable, validating priorities, and avoiding pressuring language to reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Camila’s reasons for change and elicits doable steps, but could more systematically deepen intrinsic motivation by linking values to change and eliciting confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine partnership by sharing expertise with Camila, co-creating the plan, offering options, and repeatedly inviting her to choose what feels most doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk and translates it into practical experiments, though there are missed opportunities to amplify spontaneous change language and to explicitly assess commitment/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges social-cost concerns but does not consistently use double-sided reflections or deeper exploration to diminish sustain talk, often moving quickly to solutions and leaving some resistance unexamined.
