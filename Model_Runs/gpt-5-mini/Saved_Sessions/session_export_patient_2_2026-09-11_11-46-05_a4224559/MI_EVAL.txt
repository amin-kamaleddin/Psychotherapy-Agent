# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, validates the patient's constraints and efforts, and maintains a warm nonjudgmental tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective‑taking and accurate reflections of practical concerns, but could deepen emotional exploration by naming feelings like overwhelm or guilt and using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, narrow focus on preventing burnout and establishing short study sprints, guiding the conversation logically from options to a concrete implementation plan without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative, inviting co‑problem solving and building on the patient's ideas so that the plan is genuinely co‑created and shaped by the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choice, avoids prescriptive language, and affirms the patient's right and ability to choose among feasible options throughout the exchange.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and solutions with open, evocative questions, leading to specific self‑generated implementation intentions and a confidence rating.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as collaborative and positions the patient as the expert, repeatedly asking which options 'feel most workable' and co‑creating the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk and commitment language by narrowing choices, reinforcing endorsed steps, and securing concrete implementation intentions and a confidence rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids reinforcing reasons not to act and redirects to solutions, though could more explicitly use double‑sided reflections to soften any residual resistance.
