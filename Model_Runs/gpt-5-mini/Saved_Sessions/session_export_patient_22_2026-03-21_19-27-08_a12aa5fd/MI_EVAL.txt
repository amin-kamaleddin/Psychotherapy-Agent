# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, normalizes Camila's feelings, and affirms her plan choices. Could add more individualized affirmations that explicitly acknowledge her resilience and the difficulty of the situation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses some empathic language and summarizes social concerns but provides limited reflective listening and few complex affect-focused reflections. More nuanced reflections of ambivalence, loneliness, or anxiety would improve empathic accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on reducing drinking in social situations and offers concrete, actionable strategies while rehearsing them. Maintains clear, goal-directed momentum throughout the interaction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a cooperative exchange through rehearsals and preference checks, which fosters teamwork. However, the clinician often supplies scripted solutions instead of eliciting Camila's spontaneous ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering multiple options, asking which feel manageable, and avoiding pressure or coercion. Language consistently reinforces Camila's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some commitment behavior (strategy selection and rehearsals) but does not sufficiently probe values, long-term goals, or internal motivations. Missed opportunities to amplify Camila's intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites input and co-constructs options, conveying collaboration and respect for Camila's perspective. The session remains somewhat expert-driven; asking Camila to brainstorm before offering options would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces some change talk and helps rehearse commitment language, yet spends more time giving strategies than eliciting Camila's own reasons, values, or confidence. Could reflect and deepen existing change talk to enhance motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk indirectly by focusing on cutting back without losing friends and offering social strategies. Does not consistently use MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly explore or redirect sustain talk toward change.
