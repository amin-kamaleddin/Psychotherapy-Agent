### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, validating feelings and acknowledging constraints while avoiding blame. Could occasionally name concrete past strengths to deepen unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, reflective listening—summarizing content and feeling and inviting elaboration—which helped expand ambivalence. Could sometimes reflect the intensity of emotions more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on improving co‑parenting consistency and steered the conversation toward actionable steps. Could add a brief recap of exact next steps/timeline at a transition point.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Felt like joint problem‑solving with co‑created tools (scripts, role‑play) and repeated invitations for the patient's input. The clinician consistently followed the patient's agenda and tailored options to her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored the patient's choice by offering options and asking which approach she preferred, avoiding pressure. Supported her ownership of decisions and allowed her to select the starting step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the patient's own values and goals, linking them to concrete plans and eliciting commitment. Could further amplify key change statements verbatim to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance, inviting input on tone, timing, and rehearsal, and co‑creating plans together. Occasionally explicitly asking permission before offering solutions could be an added refinement.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, narrowed desires into small, doable steps and supported commitment language. Could incorporate brief confidence/commitment scaling to deepen and track readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
