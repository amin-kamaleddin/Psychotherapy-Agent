### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Regularly offers multiple options, asks which Linda prefers, and affirms small, realistic steps while respecting her choice not to quit, thereby supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation (e.g., acceptable small smoking changes and reasons for school involvement) but relies heavily on logistics instead of exploring values, past successes, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits occasional change language (harm‑reduction commitments) and actionable plans but does not consistently amplify, scale, or explore confidence and ability to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and accepts sustain talk, but misses strategic techniques (double‑sided reflections, discrepancy elicitation, targeted summaries) that could more actively shift sustain talk toward change.
