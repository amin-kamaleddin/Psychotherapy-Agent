# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the patient's dilemma, repeatedly validating practical limits and explicitly respecting boundaries (e.g., no follow‑ups unless asked).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated accurate perspective‑taking and reflective listening, capturing ambivalence and concrete preferences (checkbox, calendar times, 10‑minute limit).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a tightly focused agenda on a specific target behavior (three 10‑minute walks and tracking), using questions and summaries that kept the conversation on implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted the interaction as an equal partnership by asking permission, offering choices, and co‑constructing the recurring invite so the patient's practical preferences shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's right to choose by offering alternatives, asking which option they preferred, and honoring strict limits the patient set about follow‑up and contact.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited motivation and patient‑generated solutions by asking about doable options and success metrics, drawing out specific reasons and a concrete plan rather than supplying motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and positioned the patient as the expert, repeatedly inviting the patient's input on logistics and implementing those choices into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language by asking about small realistic steps and what would feel like a successful week, which led to concrete commitment (M/W/F walks, calendar blocking, checkbox).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged and mirrored sustain talk without arguing or amplifying it, validating time constraints while steering toward tiny, feasible steps to prevent sustain talk from dominating.
