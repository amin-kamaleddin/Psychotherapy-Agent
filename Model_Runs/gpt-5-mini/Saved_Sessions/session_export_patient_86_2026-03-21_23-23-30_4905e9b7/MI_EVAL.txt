# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently displayed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing Marco's experience and avoiding labeling. The clinician validated Marco's preferences (no nagging) and reinforced his ownership of the plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening that mirrored Marco's language and captured ambivalence and emotion. Frequent, tuned reflections maintained rapport with a guarded patient.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and progressed systematically toward a specific, concrete behavioral experiment. Repeatedly returned to implementation details and success criteria to narrow commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the experiment with Marco, offering options while deferring choices to him and accepting adjustments. The clinician solicited practical input and treated Marco as an equal partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly emphasized choice and preserved Marco's control over decisions and rules, avoiding coercive language. Reflections and questions reinforced ownership and reduced reactance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete commitment to try the hour‑delay experiment and coping strategies. Could more often draw out and link deeper internal motivations or values to strengthen intrinsic drive.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong power sharing by repeatedly inviting Marco to choose what would feel okay and affirming his ownership of the rules. The clinician avoided an expert‑driven stance and reinforced collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and deepened specific change talk around the hour delay and coping tactics, prompting concrete planning and success markers. Could further amplify values‑based reasons and explicitly reflect personal motives to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and redirected toward manageable change using acceptance and some double‑sided language. Opportunities remained to use more advanced strategies (explicit double‑sided reflections, strategic discrepancy) to subtly reframe sustain talk.
