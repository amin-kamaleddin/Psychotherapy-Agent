# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating Rowan's choice and offering options and practical help. Maintained warm, accepting tone even when Rowan repeatedly refused to charge the device.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections, acknowledging effort and strain. Could have probed and reflected more deeply on underlying feelings about the device to uncover emotional drivers of avoidance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Maintained clear structure around completing immediate tasks but did not keep charging the device as the sustained focal behavior. Gave a brief practical recommendation but quickly accepted refusal and shifted emphasis to other tasks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operated as a collaborative problem-solving partnership, offering choices and co-creating action steps while inviting preferences. At times the clinician supplied detailed checklists that shifted some authority toward expert guidance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy with multiple options and repeated offers framed as optional, reinforcing Rowan's right to choose. There was no pressure to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal elicitation of Rowan's intrinsic motivations about charging; the clinician mainly provided logistical advice rather than asking evocative questions to draw out values or reasons. Missed opportunities to have the client voice their own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through shared decision-making, offering to stay while tasks were completed and co-creating templates and messages. Occasionally moved into directive problem-solving without fully negotiating that level of guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did little to elicit or amplify change talk about charging the device and did not use targeted open questions or reflections to develop tentative change statements. Focus remained on task planning rather than drawing out reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and did not reinforce avoidance, but rarely used double-sided reflections or reframing to gently shift toward change. Provided a practical battery threshold note but few strategic softening strategies.
