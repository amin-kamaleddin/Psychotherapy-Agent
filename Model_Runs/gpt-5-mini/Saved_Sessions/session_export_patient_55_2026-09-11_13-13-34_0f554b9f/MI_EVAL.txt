# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful tone and validated the patient's priorities. Language avoided blaming and offered supportive options and normalizing statements.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used accurate reflections and perspective-taking, summarizing the patient's values and concerns in a timely way. These reflections deepened rapport and showed strong empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on alcohol risk and protecting music/academic priorities, moving from broad concern to specific steps. The clinician guided concrete planning (exam-week rule, friend support, draft texts).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as joint problem-solving with frequent checks and co‑creation of the plan. The clinician let the patient choose strategies and shaped wording together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently affirmed choice, offered multiple options, and avoided pressure. Patient control was reinforced by asking which ideas fit and allowing tweaks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited practical reasons and supports for change (protecting band/IB, friend checking in). Opportunities remained to deepen intrinsic motivation by asking more about values, benefits, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used collaborative language and explicitly invited the patient's input at each step. Power was shared as the patient shaped the plan and phrasing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited concrete change language and helped move from abstract ideas to a specific plan (exam-week rule, texts). Missed opportunities included amplifying commitment statements and eliciting scaled importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and redirected toward brainstorming supports, minimizing reinforcement of reluctance. More strategic use of double-sided reflections and explicit validation of competing pulls could further resolve ambivalence.
