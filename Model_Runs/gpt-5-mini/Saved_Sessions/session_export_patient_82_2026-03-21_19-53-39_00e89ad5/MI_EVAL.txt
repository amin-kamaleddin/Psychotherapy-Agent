# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Lukas’s experience, thanking his honesty and avoiding judgment; overall warm, validating stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening, reflects feelings and body sensations and uses grounding techniques, but sometimes moves quickly to solutions rather than exploring deeper affective meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on early alcohol use, peer influence, and balancing goals with targeted questions, role-play, and harm-reduction planning; no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative—invites choices and engages in role-play—but clinician often provides ready-made scripts and plans before eliciting Lukas’s own ideas, reducing co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering explicit options, asking permission for role-play, and tailoring plans to Lukas’s preferences, reinforcing his ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change language (reasons and consequences) but relies heavily on clinician-driven strategies and misses opportunities to amplify motivation with scaling questions and strategic reflections.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and inviting of input, yet leans toward offering expert solutions and scripts instead of co-constructing alternatives with Lukas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through questions and affirmations and supports Lukas’s reasons for change, though it seldom uses reflections or confidence/commitment probes to deepen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and gently redirects sustain-leaning statements toward harm-reduction and skills; could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to more explicitly contrast values and the pull to drink.
