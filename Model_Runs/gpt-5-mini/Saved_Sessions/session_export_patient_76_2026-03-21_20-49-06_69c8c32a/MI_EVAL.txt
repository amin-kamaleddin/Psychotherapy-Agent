# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with validating reflections and gentle safety reassurance throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, affect‑focused reflections and stayed with Liora's immediate emotional experience, checking urge levels and acknowledging exhaustion and pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on self‑harm risk and safety the entire session, moving logically from risk assessment to in‑the‑moment interventions and a brief plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively to co‑create immediate coping steps, invited choices, honored pacing, and supported Liora's preferences in the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by asking permission, offering options without pressure, and respecting boundaries (e.g., not forcing razor removal).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited actionable steps and urge ratings well, but did not sufficiently draw out deeper intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision‑making and solicited input, though offering many specific strategies unprompted limited opportunities to elicit Liora's own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language and small successes, but missed opportunities to probe deeper reasons, desire, and confidence for longer‑term change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed doubts effectively, though could have used more explicit double‑sided reflections and elicited reasons against the behavior.
