# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental language, affirmations, and normalization that create a safe stance for the patient to explore difficult feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely empathic understanding with complex reflections that capture ambivalence and follow the patient's cues and phrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and structures the session toward concrete action steps and planning without losing empathy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as joint problem‑solving by co‑creating scripts, soliciting rehearsal preferences, and inviting the patient to shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy by offering choices, asking what feels truest, and emphasizing the patient's agency while avoiding directives or pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivations and ideas through values‑linked questions and invitations to generate concrete steps, producing clear change language and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative and power‑sharing: asks preferences, offers to draft scripts together, and defers to the patient's phrasing and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk by presenting options tied to reclaiming space, validating plans, and inviting rehearsal and role‑play to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and normalizes guilt without amplifying it, but could more strategically soften sustain talk earlier using double‑sided reflections and exploring costs of the status quo.
