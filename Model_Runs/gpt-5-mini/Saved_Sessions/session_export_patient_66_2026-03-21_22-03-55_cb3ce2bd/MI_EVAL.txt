# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—normalizes ambivalence, avoids criticism, and affirms the patient's perspective to reduce shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, but tends to stay at content-level and misses opportunities for deeper emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on smoking reduction/cessation with concrete planning (3‑day experiment, strategies, follow‑up) while balancing exploration and action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner—solicits the patient's wording and preferences, integrates choices into the plan, and shares expertise when invited.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and ownership throughout (e.g., 'totally your choice'), invites selection of scripts and follow‑up, and frames actions as optional.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and problem‑solving using open questions and scaling, but could strengthen by linking change talk more directly to personal values and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and co‑creative—invites input on phrasing, plan details, and preferred support methods to share decision‑making power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk via scaling and targeted prompts and amplifies tentative change, yet could more deliberately convert motivators into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk neutrally and uses double‑sided framing to pivot toward change, though additional strategic reflections linking sustain talk to values could further reduce its influence.
