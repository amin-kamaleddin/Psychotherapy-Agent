# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance—normalizes doubt and affirms small efforts without blaming or minimizing the client's experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures ambivalence and concrete consequences, though reflections could more often probe deeper into affect (shame, panic) with more complex emotional reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on a single, actionable target (submitting one job application) and consistently orients the session toward specific, achievable steps and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—the clinician repeatedly invites the client's preferences, negotiates specifics, and co-constructs the plan with practical, client-selected help.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering multiple options, asking permission before intervening, avoiding prescriptive language, and framing the plan as the client's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's motivations and past strategies and reinforces nascent change talk, but could deepen reasons for change and more explicitly elicit confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a genuine partnership stance—sharing power, asking which supports feel most helpful, and involving the client in each decision about the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (small-step planning, past strategies), yet could better amplify and summarize client change language and use scaling to draw out commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk with double-sided reflections and redirects to low-risk steps, but could more proactively evoke exceptions and reflect selectively to counter global helplessness.
