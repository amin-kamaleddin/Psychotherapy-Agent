# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with normalizing statements and affirmations; some validations were brief or formulaic rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid reflective listening and perspective-taking that reduced defensiveness; reflections were mostly content-level with missed opportunities for deeper emotional or values-based attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on charging and device use, moving logically from barriers to a concrete, time-bound plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly a collaborative problem-solving exchange that incorporated Rowan's preferences and boundaries; clinician offered ideas but deferred to his input throughout the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Rowan's choice by offering options, asking permission, and respecting limits (eg, no texts), reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Rowan's own motivations and small commitments using targeted questions and confidence ratings; could more frequently amplify change talk and explore values to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated joint planning and deference to Rowan's expertise, inviting him to shape specifics; at times the clinician used directive suggestions instead of eliciting ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked and reinforced tentative change talk and translated it into a specific plan with observed increases in confidence; could more systematically amplify change language and probe past successes to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, validated ambivalence, and offered low‑pressure options to redirect toward change; could use more strategic double-sided reflections and explicit discrepancy to further tip ambivalence.
