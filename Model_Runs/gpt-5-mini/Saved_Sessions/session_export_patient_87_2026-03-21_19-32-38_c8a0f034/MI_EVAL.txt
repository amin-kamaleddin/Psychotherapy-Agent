# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive with an attentive safety check and frequent validating statements, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening about triggers and guarded tone, but often shifts quickly into problem-solving and misses opportunities for deeper emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on managing home food triggers with progressive movement from problem identification to concrete, rehearsed action steps and readiness checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as teamwork and solicits preferences, but much of the session is clinician-driven with lists of strategies rather than co-constructing solutions from Marco's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and ownership by asking permission, offering multiple options, and emphasizing trials and adjustments without pressuring Marco.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses confidence scales and invites commitments and rehearsals to elicit motivation, but relies more on offering strategies than drawing out Marco's past successes, values, and intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites input and rehearsals and accepts preferences, yet frequently supplies many concrete suggestions which shifts some balance toward advice-giving rather than equal collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change language through rulers, planning, and rehearsals, but could do more to amplify, reflect, and summarize Marco's own motivations and 'I'll try' commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and acknowledges sustain talk while redirecting to feasible options, though greater use of double-sided reflections and explicit naming of ambivalence would further soften sustain talk.
