### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing ambivalence and validating Margaret's priorities without labeling or criticizing. The clinician maintains a nonjudgmental stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring Margaret's language and linking concerns to her values. Reflections and summaries show high-quality empathic understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol-related risk and ties discussion to safety and independence. Questions and reflections consistently move toward practical, time-bound risk-reduction steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in clear two-way problem-solving, inviting Margaret's input and responding to her preferences at each step. Suggestions are framed as options to try rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports Margaret's sense of choice, avoids pressuring her to quit, and offers options rather than directives. The clinician affirms her control over decisions and respects stated limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation out of Margaret by eliciting strengths, past successes, and self-generated reasons for change. This leads to concrete, client-driven planning and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with shared decision-making and co-created planning, deferring to Margaret's expertise about her life. The clinician repeatedly invites choice and offers to map out steps together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicits and reinforces Margaret's own change language, asking which risk-reduction options feel doable and affirming intentions. This support deepens change talk and yields specific action commitments.
