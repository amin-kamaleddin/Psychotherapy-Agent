# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with repeated normalization and validation; no shaming or moralizing about the drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, validating defensiveness and social motives; missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of Marcus’s emotions and underlying anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and safer behavior throughout, repeatedly returning to plan formation and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session reads as joint problem-solving with role-play and rehearsal, but many options and content were clinician-generated rather than co-created with Marcus.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, and emphasizing low-effort experiments that respect Marcus’s preferences and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation (rulers, small commitments, plan selection), but could more actively draw out personally meaningful reasons and long-term values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—invites input and rehearses lines—but the clinician often supplied solutions instead of first eliciting Marcus’s own ideas for strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited modest change talk using rulers and experiments, but tended to move quickly to solutions instead of reflecting and amplifying Marcus’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided confrontation and normalized sustain talk while offering harm-reduction alternatives, though could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly highlight ambivalence.
