# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, honoring Richard's boundaries (no lecturing, no spouse involvement) throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that showed understanding of routine and ambivalence, but missed opportunities for deeper affective reflection when Richard softened about possibly losing his wife.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking behavior from scaling through a specific, time-limited experiment and scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functioned largely as an equal partnership—inviting choices and co-constructing the plan—but the provision of multiple concrete options limited time spent eliciting Richard's own strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Richard's sense of choice and control by seeking permission before advice and framing the gum swap as an optional small test.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited motivations with scaling and open questions, but relied more on offering solutions than deepening intrinsic motives or amplifying relational reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Promoted mutual problem-solving with inclusive language and negotiated plans, although somewhat directive information delivery reduced opportunities for joint exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and secured a small behavioral commitment, but could have amplified relational change talk to strengthen desire and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and reframed sustain talk while redirecting toward a doable experiment, but could more actively pivot sustain talk using techniques like double-sided reflections and selective amplification of change language.
