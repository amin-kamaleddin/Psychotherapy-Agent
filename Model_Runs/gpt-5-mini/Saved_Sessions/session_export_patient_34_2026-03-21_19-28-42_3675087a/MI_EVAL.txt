# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and honors the client's request for concise, factual language, but acceptance is instrumental and shifts quickly to task-focused problem solving rather than sustained unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers brief empathic moves and accommodates Dylan's preference to avoid a lecture, but most responses are directive and informational with limited reflective listening or deeper understanding of underlying concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on the conduct incident and administrative next steps, consistently organizing concrete tasks (timeline, evidence, statement, witnesses) to address the immediate concern.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes collaborative elements like drafting together and role‑play, but the interaction is task‑focused and asymmetrical with the clinician largely controlling the agenda and expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently offers options and affirms Dylan's choices (tailoring drafts, asking about role‑play), though some directive wording and specific advice could be perceived as steering rather than fully supporting autonomous exploration.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Does not elicit Dylan's internal motivations, values, or reasons for change; questioning is limited to factual details for administrative defense, so evocation of change talk is essentially absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Engages in pragmatic back‑and‑forth and invites input, but the stance is expert‑driven with corrective edits and clinician-led planning rather than a genuine shared partnership about broader goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Makes almost no attempts to evoke language in favor of behavioral change; the session centers on minimizing consequences and coaching denial rather than eliciting desire, ability, reasons, or commitment to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Actually facilitates sustain talk by helping craft minimizing language and advising against admissions; fails to acknowledge ambivalence or use reflections to soften sustain language.
