# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through nonjudgmental summaries, affirmations, and normalization; no shaming or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with helpful open questions. Could deepen rapport by naming underlying affect (anxiety/shame) and using more complex reflections of hesitation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on risky drinking and concrete risk-reduction strategies, guiding the session toward a specific, testable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative joint problem-solving with the client co-creating plans, choosing options, and shaping language without being lectured.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by offering choices, inviting preferences, and avoiding pressure while acknowledging doubts and respecting the client's agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values, motivations, and strategies using scaling and planning, but could probe deeper for intrinsic motives and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership and power-sharing by asking the client to choose strategies and co-creating the plan, treating the client as the expert on their life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with scaling and co-constructed plans, yet missed opportunities to amplify, summarize tentative change language, and strengthen ability/commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to harm-reduction while validating the client's desire to stay social; could use more double-sided reflections and explicit contrast of costs/benefits to deepen resolve.
