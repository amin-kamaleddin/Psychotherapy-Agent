# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance (e.g., “I’m here to listen,” reflective validation), affirming choices and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed strong perspective-taking and reflective listening that captured social meaning, but some reflections were brief paraphrases and the clinician could deepen complex emotional reflections to reach a top score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking in social contexts and guided the session toward concrete, actionable steps while checking the client's preferences.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑created lists, scripts, and role‑plays, adapted interventions to Elise’s preferences, and treated her as an active partner in problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing suggestions as experiments the client could accept or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked change-relevant language (miss/gain list, scaling, experiment planning), but could have probed the meaning of key gains and past successes to amplify commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative atmosphere by asking preferences and inviting choice, though the session occasionally drifted toward offering practitioner-led solutions rather than eliciting more client-generated alternatives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced reasons for change (ratings, selecting an experiment, commitment to try), but could have more frequently amplified subtle change statements and explored ability/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validated sustain talk and avoided confrontation, but tended to move quickly into solutions instead of using double-sided reflections or summaries to soften sustain talk and strengthen discrepancy.
