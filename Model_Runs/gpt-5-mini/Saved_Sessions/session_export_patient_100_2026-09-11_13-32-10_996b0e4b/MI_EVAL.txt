# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard through validating statements, normalizing ambivalence, and affirming honesty, creating a warm nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking, frequently summarizing the patient’s feelings and checking in to ensure understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a focused steer toward reducing risky drinking and protecting school performance, guiding the session toward specific, actionable steps and commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructs the plan with the patient, asking for input at multiple decision points and offering supports only after the patient requests them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors patient choice by offering options, inviting preferences, and reinforcing the patient’s ownership of the plan and supports.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient’s own motives, values, and practical ideas rather than imposing reasons for change, allowing the patient to generate and commit to steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Promotes shared decision-making and treats the patient as an equal expert in her life, using language like 'let’s co-create' and asking which options feel doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change language and concrete plans, prompting specific, achievable steps that the patient refines into a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Often redirects sustain talk toward practical alternatives and solutions, though could more frequently use strategic double-sided reflections or explicit reframing to further reduce sustain pull.
