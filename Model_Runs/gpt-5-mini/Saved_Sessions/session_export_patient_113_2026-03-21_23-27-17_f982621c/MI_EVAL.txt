# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, explicitly respecting Rowan's boundary against nagging. Language avoided blame and normalized his experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate, emotionally resonant reflections and summaries tying avoidance to Rowan's routine. Questions were grounded in his concerns and attuned to his feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on charging and using the device, guiding the conversation toward a specific, time-bound experiment. Every intervention moved toward actionable steps without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a genuine two-way problem-solving effort, inviting Rowan to generate solutions and negotiating a mutually acceptable plan. Rowan's choices directly shaped the intervention steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and respected Rowan's preferences, avoiding coercive language and honoring his 'no nags' boundary. Options were offered and the clinician deferred to Rowan's wisdom about what felt least intrusive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical, client-generated strategies and produced self-directed plans, but focused mainly on logistics rather than deeper internal motivators. Could have probed values, reasons, and confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach that co-constructed the plan and repeatedly asked for Rowan's preference on specifics. The clinician followed Rowan's lead and shared decision-making throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly elicited and reinforced tentative change language and secured specific commitments (e.g., Saturday, alarm, charger). Would benefit from more amplification and exploration of reasons/confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled resistance well by validating constraints and reframing the plan as a low-effort experiment, avoiding argumentation. Missed opportunities for more advanced softening (e.g., double-sided reflections or exploring costs of not changing).
