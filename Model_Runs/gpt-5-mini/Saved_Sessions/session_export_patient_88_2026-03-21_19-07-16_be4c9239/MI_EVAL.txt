# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and avoided judgment, honoring patient requests (e.g., no lecture, don’t text mom) and validating his perspective.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking that validated enjoyment and ambivalence, but could have probed deeper into emotions and the patient’s guarded tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on adolescent alcohol safety throughout and progressed logically to a concrete safety plan and ready-to-copy messages.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a joint effort, invited input, integrated Marcus’s choices into the plan, and adapted to his requests (message length, contacts).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control via permission-seeking, offering options, and honoring limits (e.g., not contacting mom), avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some tentative in-session motivation but relied mostly on practical harm-reduction; missed chances to explore values, goals, and deepen intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making and co-constructed the plan, though could have invited Marcus to generate more strategies to make his contributions more central.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited ambivalence and got tentative change language, but did not consistently amplify or strategically reflect change talk before moving to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk using neutral, nonconfrontational language and harm-reduction framing, though relied more on practical planning than strategic double-sided reflections.
