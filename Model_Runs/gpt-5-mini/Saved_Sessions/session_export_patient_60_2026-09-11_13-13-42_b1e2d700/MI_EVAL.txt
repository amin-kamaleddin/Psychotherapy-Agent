# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding stigmatizing language while accepting the patient’s perspective even when he minimizes the incident.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and helpful paraphrases that show perspective-taking, but misses deeper exploration of underlying emotions like shame or anxiety behind the defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on preparing for the conduct meeting and protecting housing/record outcomes with no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as a partnership by proposing options, soliciting input, and iteratively shaping the plan with the patient’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and asks which options feel truest to the patient, empowering him to decide wording and strategy without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out immediate priorities and concrete steps (script, rehearsal, roommate backup) but stops short of eliciting broader intrinsic motivations or longer-term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance throughout, with joint problem-solving language and deference to the patient’s judgment about tone and wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some preparatory change language (plans to rehearse, be more careful) but does not consistently amplify or probe for importance, confidence, or sustained commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and reflects both sides to prevent escalation, but rarely uses strategic double-sided reflections or reframes to actively shift sustain talk toward change.
