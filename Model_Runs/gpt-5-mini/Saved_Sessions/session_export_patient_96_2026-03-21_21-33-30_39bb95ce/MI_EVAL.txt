# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, using reflective summaries and validations that support rapport and reduce resistance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and reflective listening focusing on motives and constraints, though opportunities to explore deeper affect or ambivalence were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on piercing aftercare and risk reduction throughout, repeatedly summarizing and moving the session toward concrete action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co‑constructed—clinician offers options, solicits preferences, and integrates the patient's choices into a shared implementation plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, respecting declines, and reinforcing the patient's ownership of decisions and plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits motivations for protective aftercare and concrete implementation intentions, but does not deeply evoke intrinsic reasons for removing the piercing or broader health values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as collaborative, invites decision-making, and repeatedly asks the patient to choose next steps, reinforcing partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits commitment language and planning (confidence scaling, alarms, if‑then plans) but does not probe for intrinsic reasons or discrepancy that might promote change beyond adherence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and validates sustain talk while redirecting to protective actions, though could use more double-sided reflections or subtle reframing to elicit change talk.
