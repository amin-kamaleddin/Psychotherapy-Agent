# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful of Marco’s autonomy; the clinician validated feelings, affirmed small steps, and avoided admonition throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that reduced resistance, but missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional nuance (e.g., resignation after health scares).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, sustained focus on smoking behavior and moved the session toward a specific, measurable 1-week harm-reduction plan with a negotiated check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—options were offered rather than prescribed and the fridge plan and check-in format were co-created based on Marco’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice with permission language and honored Marco’s ‘on my terms’ stance, framing the plan as a voluntary test rather than pressure to quit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete reasons for small change (appearance, doing things his way) and a doable plan, but could use more open, evocative questions to deepen internal motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a shared process with genuine power-sharing—clinician asked which options felt doable and incorporated Marco’s input into the final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out and reinforced change talk through a menu of options and a concrete week-long plan, yet could further amplify desire, reasons, ability, and commitment with follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and rolled with resistance, reducing sustain talk; could enhance impact by using more double-sided reflections to surface and resolve ambivalence.
