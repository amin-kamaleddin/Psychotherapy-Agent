# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating; affirms limits and supports small steps throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid empathic listening with accurate reflections of stressors and plans; could deepen empathy by explicitly reflecting guardedness and the emotional weight of motherhood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on evening smoking and guides the session toward a specific, measurable plan without forcing other topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative with invitational language and co‑created plans; clinician acts as facilitator and resource while centering patient preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks permission before offering info, and leaves final decisions to the patient, supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's reasons and confidence and draws out concrete steps, but could further deepen intrinsic motivation by asking evocative, value‑focused questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership and shared decision‑making but at times shifts into an expert role with detailed info; could better elicit the patient's own problem‑solving ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and confidence ratings, yet misses opportunities to amplify the strong motherhood value statement and to secure a commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids arguing and respects the patient's boundary around alcohol, though using double‑sided reflections could more explicitly surface ambivalence.
