# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental tone, explicitly stating acceptance and validating the patient's caregiving concerns. This created a safe space for the patient to offer tentative plans without shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening that captures both practical constraints (time, weather) and emotional conflict (protecting her son vs. stress relief). Reflections stay aligned with the patient's language and support trust and exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on smoking reduction/child exposure with clear, actionable strategies and a one-week trial. Consistently steers toward behavior change without imposing directives.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is strongly collaborative and egalitarian, framing the work as joint problem-solving and co-designing realistic strategies. The patient's priorities and constraints drive the plan throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports the patient's sense of choice by offering options and asking which feels doable, avoiding coercive language. The clinician clearly respects the patient's right to decide which steps to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's ideas and intrinsic reasons for change but could more systematically deepen motivation by asking importance/confidence scales and eliciting commitment language. Provides practical scaffolding but less emphasis on amplifying internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert and invites shared planning, repeatedly soliciting patient preferences and yielding decision-making. This fosters strong patient ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk (outside-only, gum, stoop) and asks outcome-focused questions, but misses opportunities to more strongly amplify and deepen change talk and elicit importance/confidence. Summaries or targeted prompts to build commitment would strengthen momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects to practical solutions, reducing focus on reasons to maintain smoking. Could further soften sustain talk with double-sided reflections and explicit exploration of pros of change to tip the balance.
