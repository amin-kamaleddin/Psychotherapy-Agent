# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed small practical steps, creating a warm nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used repeated, accurate reflections and summaries that validated the client's experience and reduced defensiveness while deepening the client's expression.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on completing court-ordered community service and guided the conversation logically from brainstorming to a concrete action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, repeatedly sought the client's input, and co-constructed the plan (script, scheduled call, role‑play) rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasized choice and elicited preferences by offering multiple supports and letting the client select what felt most doable, thereby supporting the client's agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical commitments and small-step change talk but did little to draw out intrinsic motives or personal values beyond avoiding legal consequences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine shared decision-making, deferring to the client's judgment and co-creating solutions instead of prescribing actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and reinforced concrete change talk and commitments (call, script, scheduling), though could further probe reasons, values, and confidence to strengthen internal motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged doubts without confrontation and gently redirected to actionable steps, but could more often use double-sided reflections to more actively resolve ambivalence.
