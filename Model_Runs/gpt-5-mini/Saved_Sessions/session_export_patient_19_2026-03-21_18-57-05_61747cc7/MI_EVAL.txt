# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, using validating language and normalizing the client's feelings; acknowledges tentative efforts and avoids shaming around gambling and debt.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows accurate understanding with useful reflections and normalizing statements, but often shifts quickly to solutions rather than exploring the emotional nuance of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing gambling and financial harm throughout the session, progressing from problem identification to concrete, time-bound action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative in tone and adapts to the client's requests, but the clinician frequently supplies content and structure instead of eliciting more of the client's own problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely offers options, asks permission before interventions, and normalizes uncertainty, consistently supporting the client's sense of choice and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws on the client's motivations and language and builds aligned action steps, but relies more on offering practical solutions than sustained elicitation of internal motivations and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks permission and invites input, yet the session is largely clinician-driven with detailed plans presented by the clinician rather than co-created with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with invitations to plan and small commitments, but could use more questions that prompt the client to articulate desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward harm-reduction with practical strategies, though could employ more MI-specific softening techniques like double-sided reflections to gently resolve ambivalence.
