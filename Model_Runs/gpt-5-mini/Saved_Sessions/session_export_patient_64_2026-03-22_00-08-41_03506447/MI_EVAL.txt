# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and normalizes ambivalence, honoring the client's perspective throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses frequent, specific reflections that capture both cognitive and affective content and checks understanding, demonstrating strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on the vaccination decision and consistently ties questions and offers to concrete steps toward that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as an equal partner by offering options, asking preferences, and co-designing the information package based on the client's needs and past strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice by asking permission, offering multiple formats and options, and repeatedly emphasizing the client's control over the decision process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivations and strengths (e.g., concern for grandparents, past decision strategies) but could probe deeper into values, importance, and confidence to elicit more spontaneous change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and power-sharing, repeatedly asking which approach the client prefers and tailoring the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits conditional change talk and concrete steps, but stops short of amplifying commitment language or exploring importance/confidence and personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to targeted information, yet could more explicitly validate sustain concerns with double-sided reflections or explore consequences of maintaining the status quo.
