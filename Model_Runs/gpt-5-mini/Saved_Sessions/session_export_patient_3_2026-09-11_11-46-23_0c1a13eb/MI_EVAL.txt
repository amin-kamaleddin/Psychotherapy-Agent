# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect through normalizing reflections, affirmations, and nonjudgmental language; no blaming or shaming noted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, nuanced empathic listening with reflections that capture both content and feeling, integrating emotional and practical elements of the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on initiating short, regular exercise and moves purposefully from goals to specific, actionable steps linked to the client's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acts as a genuine collaborative partner—co‑creates the plan, solicits the client's input on wording and logistics, and follows the client's preferences throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Affirms the client's choice and control by offering options, using permission‑seeking language, and framing ideas as suggestions to support ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation and resources from the client using questions about values and past successes, scaling, and eliciting concrete implementation ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the client as the expert on her life, asking what she would like to try and leaving final decisions to her, demonstrating strong power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with open questions, targeted reflections, concrete planning, and scaling to strengthen desire, ability, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and validates ambivalence without amplifying it and redirects toward solutions, though occasionally could use more double‑sided reflections to actively resolve sustain talk.
