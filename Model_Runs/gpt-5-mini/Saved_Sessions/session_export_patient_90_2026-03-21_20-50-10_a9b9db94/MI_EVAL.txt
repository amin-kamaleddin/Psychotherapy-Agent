# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent unconditional positive regard from the opening line and throughout via validation and affirmation of Marcus's experience and choices. Avoids criticism about his attachment to Buddy and normalizes constraints like finances and time.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking with complex reflections that match affect and pacing. Consistently acknowledges Marcus's overwhelm and priorities, communicating clear understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on asthma-related behaviors with concrete, actionable steps (bedroom rule, inhaler check, 7-day log, reminder scripts). Minimal topic drift and repeated timing/planning questions keep the session purpose-driven.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages Marcus as a co-author of the plan by asking which options feel doable and tailoring scripts, logs, and reminders to his preferences. Decision-making and agenda-setting are shared throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice from the start and consistently asks permission before offering ideas, presenting options rather than directives. Respects Marcus's decision not to give up Buddy and offers acceptable alternatives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Marcus's reasons for change (better sleep, fewer coughs) and workable goals but stops short of exploring deeper personal values or linking change to life roles. Missed opportunities include not using scaling for readiness/confidence or drawing out a clear commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a genuine collaborative stance by inviting input at each step, offering to co-create scripts and logs, and positioning Marcus as the primary decision-maker. Plans reflect Marcus's constraints and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces specific change talk (e.g., keeping Buddy out of the bedroom, tracking symptoms) and praises doable choices. Could further amplify change talk by linking improvements to values, using scaling, and eliciting clearer commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids arguing and reframes sustain talk into compromise options that respect Marcus's attachment to Buddy. Validates concerns about finances/time while redirecting to practical, acceptable strategies.
