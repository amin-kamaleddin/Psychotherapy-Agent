# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys warm, nonjudgmental regard and validates the patient's limits and choices, normalizing concerns and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking capture both facts and meaning, including the patient's ambivalence, demonstrating high empathic understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on increasing physical activity with a specific, measurable target and concrete implementation strategies and backups.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as true collaboration with mutual problem-solving; the clinician solicits input before suggesting and co-constructs the plan with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently upholds patient choice, offers options rather than directives, and explicitly defers to what the patient thinks will work.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicits the patient's own values, motives, and solutions, using scaling and prompts to draw out intrinsic reasons and concrete strategies for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treats the patient as the expert, repeatedly soliciting preferences and co-creating a plan that reflects the patient's priorities and expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicits, reflects, and amplifies change talk, using a confidence ruler and concrete planning to deepen commitment and intent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without reinforcement; the clinician names ambivalence and redirects toward small, manageable steps and practical backups.
