# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalized ambivalence while affirming Sofia's context and constraints. No instances of blame or minimization were observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections that captured both content and feeling and demonstrated perspective-taking about anxiety, finances, and social pressures. Summaries and reflections invited elaboration rather than challenge.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on marijuana use and steered toward concrete, measurable goals (weekdays-only experiment) and practical strategies. The conversation moved from options to a specific, time-limited plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co-constructed the plan by soliciting Sofia's ideas, checking feasibility, and treating her as the expert on her life. The clinician avoided an expert-driven approach and worked alongside Sofia on solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Sofia's choice and invited her to select what felt doable, avoiding prescriptive or coercive language. The clinician reinforced that the plan should fit Sofia's preferences and circumstances.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focused on eliciting Sofia's own motivations and practical solutions through open questions and requests for client-generated success indicators. The clinician drew out internal motivation and allowed Sofia to articulate change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort with repeated invitations for Sofia's input and deference to her judgment. The plan was genuinely co-created with ongoing checks about what felt doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk via open questions, reflections, and probes for concrete first steps and success signs. Sofia produced specific change statements and realistic strategies as a result.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not reinforce sustain talk, gently redirecting toward planning and discrepancy. There were missed opportunities to use more explicit double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of the behavior's function.
