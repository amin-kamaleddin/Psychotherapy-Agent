# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard with explicit affirmations and warm, nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking that matches the patient's content and emotion and moves the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on managing alcohol linked to the patient's values and consistently advances concrete, actionable planning toward that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving by inviting the patient's ideas, reflecting them back, and co-constructing specific steps while avoiding an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports patient choice by offering options, asking which feels comfortable, and affirming the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivation and strategies through targeted questions, leading to specific intentions and concrete action steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as a collaborator, asking him to choose among options and offering to refine plans together, demonstrating shared power in the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reinforces change talk with open questions, reflections, and summaries that move the patient from general desire to concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change-focused planning, though could further soften sustain talk by using explicit double-sided reflections to name competing pulls.
