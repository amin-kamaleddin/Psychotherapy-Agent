# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly communicated clear, nonjudgmental acceptance and normalized Naomi's experience, creating a safe tone for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate paraphrasing captured Naomi's sensations and ambivalence, though reflections were sometimes repetitive and deeper emotional meaning (shame/fear) was not fully explored.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on managing social anxiety during shoots and progressively narrowed to a concrete, actionable plan (lens‑cap pause, three breaths, rehearsal).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was clearly co‑constructed with Naomi through joint problem‑solving, repeated checks, and invitations for her input, conveying an equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently honored Naomi's choices, presented options without pressure, and asked her to select the approach that felt most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited Naomi's reasons for change and observable indicators of success, but could have probed values, past successes, or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power was shared throughout the interaction, with the clinician deferring to Naomi's preferences and finalizing the plan based on her judgment.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited and scaffolded change talk and secured a small commitment, but could have amplified change language and elicited stronger commitment or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and the conversation was steered toward small steps, but more advanced reframing (double‑sided reflections or selective summaries) was not used to further soften sustain talk.
