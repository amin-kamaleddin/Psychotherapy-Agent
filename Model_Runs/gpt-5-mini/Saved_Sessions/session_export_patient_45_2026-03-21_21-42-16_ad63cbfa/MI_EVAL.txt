# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validating the patient's emotions and avoiding judgment; frames recommendations as options rather than directives.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows accurate perspective-taking with regular reflective listening and ties recommendations to the patient's life and constraints, helping the patient move from guarded to cooperative.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on diabetes-related behaviors (diet, alcohol, testing) and supports goal-directed, measurable steps throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine two-way problem-solving by tailoring options to the patient's constraints, checking feasibility, and adapting suggestions with patient input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves choice and control by offering options, asking the patient to choose what feels doable, and normalizing partial engagement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivations and priorities (e.g., staying able to work) and builds the plan from the patient's reasons and confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by presenting multiple concrete options, inviting selection, and checking in to co-create the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with open questions and commitment prompts, though it could further amplify the patient's reasons and values with more evocative follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and gently redirects sustain talk toward practical change, but could more strategically soften ambivalence using double-sided reflections or reframing the costs of the status quo.
