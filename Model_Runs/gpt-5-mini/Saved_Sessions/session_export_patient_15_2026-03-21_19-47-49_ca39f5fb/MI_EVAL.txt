# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard, normalized the patient's perspective, and treated slips nonjudgmentally while offering ongoing support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used validating language and reflective listening effectively, but empathy stayed somewhat functional and missed deeper exploration of feelings or underlying meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use as it relates to blood pressure and produced a concrete, time-limited experiment with specific measurement points.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the plan with the patient, elicited preferences, and adjusted actions to fit the patient's schedule and communication style.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, asked which felt realistic, and framed slips as learning rather than failure, reinforcing patient choice and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some pragmatic motivation and agreement to a trial but did not sufficiently explore personal reasons, values, or confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative, invited the patient's expertise, and allowed the patient to shape decisions like start date, limits, and reminders.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited change talk and a small commitment (1-drink limit), but did not systematically draw out or amplify the patient's own reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized minimization while offering a gentle experiment to reduce sustain talk, though more double-sided reflections could have strengthened the shift.
