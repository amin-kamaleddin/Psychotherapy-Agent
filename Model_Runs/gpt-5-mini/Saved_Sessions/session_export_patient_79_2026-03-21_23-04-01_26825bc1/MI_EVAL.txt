# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing autonomy and avoiding judgment. Affirms the patient's competence and preferences throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking to build rapport and summarize frustration and past successes. Could increase depth by using more complex, affect-laden reflections when the patient expresses irritation or sarcasm.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on improving routine medication use and sequences prompts from idea generation to concrete specification. Resists topic drift while keeping the process patient-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner, soliciting the patient's ideas and co-constructing a realistic plan while deferring to the patient's expertise. Validates and adapts suggestions to fit the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the patient's choices, avoids pressuring language, and offers options that reinforce ownership. Explicitly supports keeping the plan simple and acceptable to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and solutions, using past success (vitamin+coffee) as a bridge to change. Could deepen impact by probing personal meaning and eliciting stronger commitment language or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the exchange as a joint exploration and treats the patient as the expert on what will work. Asks collaborative questions that invite the patient to lead decision-making and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evokes and amplifies change talk, converting interest into concrete plans (inhaler by the mug, one puff with coffee). Could further strengthen by eliciting explicit commitment statements, scaling confidence/importance, and consolidating change talk with summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying resistance, legitimizes boundaries, and reframes sustain talk toward feasible alternatives. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both reasons for and against change before pivoting.
