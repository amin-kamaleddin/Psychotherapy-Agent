# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and consistently validated the patient's experience and lapses, showing strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking but moved quickly to planning without deeper exploration of emotions or complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on a single behavioral target and moved every intervention toward a concrete, operationalized plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-created the plan with the patient, invited decision-making, and adjusted language to match the patient's voice, showing strong teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored the patient's choices by offering options and asking preference questions, reinforcing the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited behavioral commitment and choices but missed opportunities to draw out deeper intrinsic motivations and explicit value-linking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and power-sharing, though the clinician provided many menu options which shifted some agenda-setting away from the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific change talk and commitments, but could have probed more for intrinsic reasons and linked actions to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and created a nonpunitive slip plan, though more double-sided reflections could have further softened resistance.
