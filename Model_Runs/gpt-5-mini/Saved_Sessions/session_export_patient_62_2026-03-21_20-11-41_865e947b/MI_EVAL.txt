### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (validated feelings and affirmed efforts). Could have more explicitly normalized setbacks and named strengths to further strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections that mirrored Jordan’s experience and doubt. Could deepen empathy by reflecting emotional nuance and using more complex/double-sided reflections to capture ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, goal-directed focus on securing short-term work with concrete tasks (rulers, drafting message, timing, follow-up). Consistently redirected hopelessness toward actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner, co-creating the outreach message and negotiating next steps while offering supportive scaffolding. Language and offers invited Jordan’s input and honored his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options and respected Jordan’s choices (tone, timing, follow-up), reinforcing ownership of the plan. Provided help without pressuring, supporting autonomy throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Jordan’s reasons and plans using open questions and the importance ruler, producing concrete change talk. Could strengthen evocation by eliciting confidence/commitment language and asking Jordan to summarize his own reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine partnership by soliciting preferences and jointly developing the message, timing, and follow-up plan. The clinician repeatedly deferred to Jordan’s expertise and shared control.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change-oriented language (ruler, small-step planning, affirmations) which produced concrete steps to take. Missed opportunities to amplify change talk into stronger commitment and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and often redirected toward action with validating reflections and practical planning. Could further soften sustain talk by using double-sided reflections and highlighting exceptions/past successes.
