# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalization and explicit acknowledgement of effort, with no blaming or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate, consistent empathic listening with complex reflections that mirror the patient's language and feelings, helping the patient feel understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on energy/weight-related functioning and moves efficiently to a specific, measurable proximal goal (daily quick breakfast) with practical troubleshooting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction feels team-like with frequent solicitation of the patient's ideas and joint planning, though occasional directive framing slightly reduces egalitarian balance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors patient choice and frames options as trials rather than mandates, leaving responsibility with the patient and offering feasible choices to promote ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out patient-generated reasons and practical solutions with open questions, but could probe deeper into core values and use scaling to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely co-creates the plan and invites patient expertise, though occasional clinician expressions of hope shift the balance slightly toward the clinician's voice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits desire, ability, and some commitment language and guides toward doable steps, but misses opportunities to amplify, summarize, and consolidate change talk or use importance/confidence rulers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reflects sustain talk without confrontation, but tends to repeat the patient's skepticism rather than reframing or using double-sided reflections, which risks reinforcing doubt.
