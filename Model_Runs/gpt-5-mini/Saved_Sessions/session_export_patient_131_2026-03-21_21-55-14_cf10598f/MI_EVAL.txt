# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing ambivalence and affirming efforts. Offered help noncoercively and maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate understanding and reflective listening, capturing key feelings and dilemmas. Checked the client’s perspective and modeled empathic language effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on target behaviors and moved the session toward concrete action. Elicited safety needs, drafted a 4‑week trial, and rehearsed practical skills tied to stated goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine collaborative partner, repeatedly asking for Wiremu’s input and co‑creating the agreement and script. Shared expertise while deferring to his lived experience and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly emphasized choice and offered options rather than directives, checking preferences before offering help. Encouraged language that preserved independence and avoided pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out core motivators and linked steps to values, but evocation was not consistently systematic. Missed some opportunities to probe deeper for commitment and confidence (e.g., scaling questions or eliciting a voiced commitment).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established strong partnership by inviting edits, role‑play, and shared planning. Framed interventions as joint problem‑solving and kept Wiremu’s agenda central.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk well, affirming commitments and asking for specifics, but could have amplified and sequenced change talk more deliberately. Fewer targeted prompts to deepen desire, need, and confidence were used.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected ambivalence into planning, validating concerns while pivoting to action. Could strengthen use of double‑sided reflections and explicit reframing to resolve sustain talk more directly.
