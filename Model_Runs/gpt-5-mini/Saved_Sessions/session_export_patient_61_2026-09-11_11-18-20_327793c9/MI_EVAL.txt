# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing the patient’s burden and validating feelings without shaming. Resistance is treated as understandable rather than pathological.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and accurately captures ambivalence and practical concerns. Could deepen empathy by reflecting more of the emotional texture (fatigue, resentment, shame) before moving to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on diabetes self‑care (medication adherence) and repeatedly narrows toward specific, testable behaviors. Resists drifting into irrelevant topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative with inclusive language and co‑construction of a pragmatic plan. The patient’s ideas drive decision‑making rather than being imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and feasibility, offering options and asking what feels least intrusive. Frequently restates that the patient can pick what works, reinforcing ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits practical, patient‑generated solutions and low‑barrier experiments. Could further evoke deeper reasons for change, values, and confidence/importance language to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the patient as the expert and solicits preferences at each step, reinforcing shared decision‑making. Repeated invitations to choose reflect true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out and amplifies small pieces of change talk and doable steps. Would benefit from more explicit elicitation of desire/ability/reasons/need and scaling questions to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reflects sustain talk without arguing, often using double‑sided reflections to pivot to acceptable changes. Could more strategically redirect sustain talk by emphasizing discrepancies and selectively reinforcing any change language.
