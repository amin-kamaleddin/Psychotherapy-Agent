# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard—validated Marcus's priorities, normalized his choices, and avoided shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and tone matching, but missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflections (e.g., naming fear of exclusion or frustration).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and safety and moved the conversation from exploration to concrete, actionable planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and co-constructive—invited Marcus to choose topics, rehearse lines, and pick steps so his decisions shaped the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported client choice and control, asking permission before advice and respecting Marcus's decision not to quit while helping plan safer options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motivations and plans but relied more on offering strategies than drawing out deeper internal reasons or values that would strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated Marcus as the expert and sought input, yet sometimes presented lists of suggestions before eliciting his own ideas; more co‑brainstorming would enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change-oriented statements (e.g., choosing safer steps, confidence ruler) but could have amplified brief ambivalence and followed up to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk skillfully by acknowledging social importance, avoiding argumentation, and pivoting to harm‑reduction strategies that reduced resistance.
