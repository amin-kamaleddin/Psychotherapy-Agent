# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect for the patient's boundary about not discussing smoking, avoiding blame or lecturing while affirming the patient's preferences.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and attunement (validated frustration, noted dental anxiety, offered accommodations), though reflections stayed fairly surface-level rather than exploring deeper feelings or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the agreed target (dental care), repeatedly steered to practical steps, and produced concrete, actionable items and logistics tied to the patient's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co-constructed: the clinician invited preferences, offered multiple formats, and built scripts/checklists together, leaving choices to the patient throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported the patient's control and choice by honoring the request not to discuss smoking, offering options, and avoiding pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited almost no in-session motivation for smoking change; left the door open for future discussion but did not ask open questions to draw out the patient's own reasons, values, or past experiences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance by asking which options would help and tailoring the plan, though could have further reinforced the patient's expertise by exploring past successes or broader priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal active evocation of change talk about smoking—the clinician acknowledged concerns but did not probe for desire, ability, reasons, or past successes that might elicit change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk by validating and redirecting to dental goals, but missed some opportunities for double-sided or strategic reflections to gently explore ambivalence.
