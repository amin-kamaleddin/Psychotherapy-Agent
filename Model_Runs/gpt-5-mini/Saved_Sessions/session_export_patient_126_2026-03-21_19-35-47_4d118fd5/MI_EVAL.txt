# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental, validated Thomas's perspective, and normalized exploring options, creating a safe, respectful tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and summaries accurately captured feelings and consequences and encouraged some expansion, but they tended to remain at a summary level rather than deepening emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on alcohol (and residual smoking) with clear, measurable goals and a concrete one-week tracking commitment and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The work was co-constructed throughout: the clinician solicited input at each decision point, incorporated preferences, and shared power in choosing tactics and follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently offered choices, asked permission, and emphasized Thomas's control over strategies, reinforcing ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited (past attempts, triggers, confidence rating), but the clinician relied more on offering solutions than drawing out Thomas's own values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance with joint problem-solving, frequent checking of willingness, and co-creation of the plan and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective questions and prompts produced commitments (tracking, max-3 goal, day‑4 check-in), though the clinician could have more actively amplified Thomas's personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician acknowledged minimizations and avoided reinforcing sustain talk but used few double-sided reflections or explicit discrepancy work, moving quickly into solutions instead.
