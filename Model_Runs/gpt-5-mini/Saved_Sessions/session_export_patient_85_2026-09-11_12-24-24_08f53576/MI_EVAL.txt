# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates clear unconditional positive regard—normalizes, validates, and affirms Marco's effort and values throughout. Avoids judgmental language and supports the patient's worth independent of outcomes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong perspective-taking and reflective listening, accurately summarizing feelings and goals. Could deepen reflections on the emotional impact of relapse and the nuance of mixed feelings to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing fruit juice and guides the session toward a concrete, actionable plan. Every question and summary ties directly to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is cooperative and co-creative, inviting Marco's input and jointly building plans. Occasionally supplies detailed solution lists that slightly shift toward expert suggestion.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Marco's sense of choice and control by asking preferences and offering options. Avoids pressure and affirms his right to choose strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits personally meaningful reasons and concrete steps, drawing out commitment language and planning. Could further deepen evocation by prompting the client to articulate his own arguments for change and amplifying confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a partnership stance by soliciting ideas and positioning the patient as an equal contributor. At times the clinician lists preferred options, which slightly reduces complete deference to the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change language with specific planning and if‑then scripting. Would be stronger by more consistently amplifying spontaneous change talk and eliciting more varied statements of desire, ability, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and shifts to planning but does so somewhat passively without exploring the pull of the juice. Could use double-sided reflections and explicit validation of enjoyment to better soften resistance.
