# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment, using validating language and affirming the client's motives throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and captures ambivalence well, though reflections are sometimes repetitive and could probe deeper into underlying values or emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on social media/phone use and guides the conversation toward a concrete, measurable experiment with contingency planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly a two-way collaborative process: the clinician proposes options, invites the client's choices, and co-creates practical plans and bump strategies together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's sense of choice and control, normalizing experimentation and framing slips as data rather than failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits intrinsic motivation and self-generated ideas effectively, but could further probe personally meaningful reasons, use scaling, and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership: clinician shares power, invites the client's ideas, and lets the client select the anchor step rather than prescribing a solution.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with a doable experiment and concrete commitments, though could more actively amplify DARN language and summarize change talk to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk nonjudgmentally and reframes it into collaborative planning, but could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly link sustain and change talk.
