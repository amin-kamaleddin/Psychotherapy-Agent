# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained consistent, nonjudgmental respect and validated Viktor’s priorities while praising pragmatic efforts; no shaming or punitive language was used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries, but could deepen empathic reflections by exploring underlying emotions (shame, fear, embarrassment) and their personal meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on reducing use at home and progressed logically from framing to a specific, measurable first step without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as a team effort—options were offered, the clinician co‑constructed language and plans, and the clinician avoided an expert-driven stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Viktor’s choice and control, offered alternatives and asked permission rather than prescribing, supporting client ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified Viktor’s own motivations (e.g., 'I can handle that') and had him generate specific implementation steps and supports.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Viktor as the expert on his life, offered choices at every step, and deferred to his selections while offering supportive help like drafting scripts and role‑play.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, moving from ambivalence to concrete, client‑chosen actions and repeated invitations to commit to specific steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not amplify sustain talk, pivoting to doable steps, but missed chances for double‑sided reflections and more explicitly linking sustain talk to Viktor’s values.
