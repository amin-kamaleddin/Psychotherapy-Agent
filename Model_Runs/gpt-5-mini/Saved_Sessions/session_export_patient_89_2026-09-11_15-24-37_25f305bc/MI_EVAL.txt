### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and avoided judgment, validating Marcus's concerns and praising pragmatic choices. Tone remained warm and respectful throughout the session.

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on increasing physical activity with concrete, actionable steps that moved Marcus toward preparation. Questions and reflections repeatedly tied back to the target behavior and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked cooperatively with Marcus to co-construct the plan, soliciting his wording and preferences and adapting accordingly. Decisions emerged from Marcus's input with clinician facilitation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored Marcus's choice by offering options and framing suggestions as choices rather than directives. Respected Marcus's voice and invited him to pick what felt most helpful.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Marcus's own reasons and generated specific change language and commitments. Could further probe underlying values, use scaling/confidence questions, and strategic summaries to amplify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative, sharing power and routinely inviting Marcus's preferences and choices. Clinician consistently positioned Marcus as the expert on what would work in his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, converting tentative ideas into concrete commitments (scheduling nights, scripting a text). Rephrased client language into practical actions that strengthened motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without confrontation and redirected to small, doable alternatives like backups and family involvement. Could increase use of double-sided reflections and more strategic rolling with resistance to further reduce sustain talk.
