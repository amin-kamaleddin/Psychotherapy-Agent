# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect through normalization and strength‑based affirmations, avoiding judgmental language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, tailored reflective listening and perspective‑taking (including a strong opening complex reflection) that validates ambivalence and client priorities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on protecting grades with concrete, actionable steps (study sessions, library booking, alarms) and repeatedly brings conversation back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a genuine two‑way partnership: the clinician solicits client input at each step and lets the client's ideas shape the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly honors client choice and limits, offers options instead of directives, and invites the client to select supports and commitments, reinforcing ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's values, strengths, past strategies, and feasible steps, prompting internally generated motivation and a specific plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as collaborative and asks for the client's expertise about what will work, indicating strong power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with direct evocation questions, reflective reinforcement of commitment language, and securing a concrete commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles hedging nonconfrontationally by normalizing and accepting the client's caution, though could more often use double‑sided reflections or explore social pressures to further reduce sustain talk.
