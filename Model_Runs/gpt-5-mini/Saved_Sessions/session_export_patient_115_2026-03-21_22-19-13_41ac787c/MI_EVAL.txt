### Empathy

### Direction

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine partnership by inviting Samir to choose focus and preferences and by co-creating concrete tactics and reminders. The clinician repeatedly sought Samir's selection and input throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions, past-success prompts, a confidence ruler, and concrete planning that amplified commitment. Summaries and specific targets strengthened Samir's change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and emphasized partial reductions and practical steps, but sometimes moved quickly to planning. Missed opportunities for more strategic double-sided reflections and explicit ambivalence summaries to better resolve sustain talk.
