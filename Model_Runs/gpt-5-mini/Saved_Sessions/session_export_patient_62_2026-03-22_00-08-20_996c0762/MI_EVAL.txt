# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Jordan’s experience, creating a safe, nonjudgmental tone. Statements validated distress and affirmed worth throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening with accurate, nuanced reflections of both content and feeling. Reflections captured frustration, hope, and weariness, showing solid perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (job search and concrete steps) from planning to specific actions. Questions and prompts repeatedly narrowed toward doable, behavioral next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative, using inclusive language and inviting Jordan’s input to shape the plan. The clinician proposed working together on scripts and timing, sharing decisions and power.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options and framing tasks as experiments. Avoided pressuring and emphasized Jordan’s ownership of timing and approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and past successes (e.g., tailoring resumes, referrals) but could more systematically draw out reasons, importance, and confidence. Would benefit from stronger links between values/goals and chosen behaviors.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled partnership through invitations to choose, co-creation of messages, and collaborative problem-solving. The clinician shared power and treated Jordan as an expert on what’s doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change talk (resume tweak, one application/day) and reinforced willingness to try, but missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate that language into explicit commitment or scaling. Could prompt for timing, confidence, or a brief commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and reframed/redirected caution into manageable experiments, validating ambivalence. Could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to highlight discrepancy and more directly explore negative predictions.
