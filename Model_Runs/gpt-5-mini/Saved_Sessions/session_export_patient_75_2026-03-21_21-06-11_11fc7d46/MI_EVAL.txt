# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating and normalizing Linda’s fears throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking (e.g., summarizing the panic sequence) and invited elaboration with empathic questions like “What would ‘feels safer’ look like?”.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on asthma management and inhaler decisions, steering the conversation toward concrete next steps (provider script, flare plan).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a true partner by co-creating materials, soliciting preferences at each step, and offering options rather than prescribing a single course of action.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before proposing strategies, and empowering Linda to choose wording and actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out values and motivations (safer, fewer scares) and elicited actionable statements, but at times shifted to solution-giving rather than further deepening and amplifying Linda’s own motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Foregrounded Linda’s expertise and preferences, used inclusive language, and iteratively refined materials with her input, demonstrating strong collaborative practice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited meaningful change talk (willingness to try breathing exercises and a script) and offered choices, but could have amplified and reflected those statements more to build stronger commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed concerns toward practical steps, yet missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and more deliberate techniques to steer sustain talk into change talk.
