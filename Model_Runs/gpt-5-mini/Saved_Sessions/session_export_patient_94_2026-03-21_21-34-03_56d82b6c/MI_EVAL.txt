# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing Maria's concerns and adapting to her stance throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflections of Maria's situation and needs; could deepen affective labeling (e.g., explicitly naming frustration or fear) to reach the top rating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on inhaler use and safety, steering every question and intervention toward practical next steps and the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed as a joint effort—solicits priorities, offers options, and co-creates the deliverable—treating Maria as an equal partner in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by asking permission, offering multiple options, and affirming that Maria remains in charge of decisions without pressuring her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concerns and brief change-relevant remarks but largely responds with information rather than systematically drawing out Maria's own reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters collaboration and checks preferences frequently, though at times the clinician takes more of an expert role by supplying wording instead of co-constructing language with Maria.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open questions that elicit small signs of change but misses chances to reflect and amplify those statements to deepen desire, ability, reasons, or need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects with factual information, but does not consistently use strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly soften sustain talk.
