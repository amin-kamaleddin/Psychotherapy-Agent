### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the patient’s caring intent without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections that tracked shifts in feeling and mirrored the patient’s language to deepen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, action-oriented focus on assertiveness and naming a move-out date, linking interventions to concrete preparatory and action steps like scripting and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
