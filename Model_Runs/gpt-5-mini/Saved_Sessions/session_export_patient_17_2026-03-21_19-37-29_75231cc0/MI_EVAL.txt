# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with validating language, normalizing slips, and repeated reassurance, creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and mirrors the patient's emotional language, though could deepen empathy with more complex or evocative reflections exploring underlying values and fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and guides the session to actionable, measurable steps (3‑morning experiment, check‑in time, concrete coping strategies).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the patient as a partner by asking preferences, building the plan from the patient's experience, and co‑creating the morning script and check‑in schedule.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice by offering options, framing plans as experiments, and avoiding coercive language, which promotes ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and past successes and uses them to form a plan, but could more systematically draw out the patient's own arguments for change and values to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful, inviting patient input and tweaks, though occasionally offers multiple specific suggestions quickly which risks slipping toward expert‑driven advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk and turns it into concrete steps, but could increase frequency of reflective prompts and follow‑ups to deepen and extend the patient's own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by reframing slips as information and redirecting to strategies, yet could further soften sustain talk with more double‑sided reflections acknowledging ambivalence.
