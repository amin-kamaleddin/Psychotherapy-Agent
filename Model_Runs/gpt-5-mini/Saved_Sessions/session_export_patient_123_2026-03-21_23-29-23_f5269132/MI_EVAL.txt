# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth, validating limits and using respectful, strengths-focused language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking with complex reflections that captured both content and ambivalence, helping the client feel understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on protecting schoolwork and hobbies and steadily progressed toward specific, measurable actions without overriding the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint exploration, offering options and repeatedly asking which felt best while co-creating the concrete plan with summaries and check-backs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the client's sense of choice and control, avoided pressuring language, and normalized nonadherence to reinforce ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the client's own motivations and resources using past successes to build feasible steps, leading the client to generate multiple personal strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned the client as the source of expertise, invited their choices, and allowed the plan to emerge from the client's selections, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with evocative, action-oriented questions and guided the client toward specific, achievable behaviors and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without reinforcing it, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or selective summaries to more effectively tip the balance toward change.
