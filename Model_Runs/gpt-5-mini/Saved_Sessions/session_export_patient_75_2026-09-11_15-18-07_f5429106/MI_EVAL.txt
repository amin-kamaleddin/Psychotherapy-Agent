# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Linda's struggle, but warmth and normalization feel somewhat procedural; could more explicitly normalize fears about dependence and side effects.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses accurate surface reflections and some perspective-taking, but misses opportunities for deeper empathic exploration of Linda's anxiety about side effects and dependence before shifting to logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on inhaler use with concrete, small, testable steps (reminders, husband nudge, three-day trial) and contingency planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction reads as teamwork with inclusive language and co-constructed plans, though the clinician largely controls structure and shapes Linda's contributions into the clinician's plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices and asks what feels doable, supporting autonomy, but tends to limit options to clinician-supplied alternatives rather than eliciting Linda's own ideas or affirming choosing not to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits specific commitments and some reasons for change, but could strengthen evocation by more routinely asking DARN-style questions and amplifying spontaneous change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Linda's input repeatedly and practices shared decision-making, yet does not frequently solicit broader ideas from her beyond the provided menu of options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws and reinforces change talk through doable options and summaries, but could further amplify desire, importance, and confidence with follow-up evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and risk but mostly redirects to planning rather than systematically softening resistance using double-sided reflections or exploring the legitimacy of concerns.
