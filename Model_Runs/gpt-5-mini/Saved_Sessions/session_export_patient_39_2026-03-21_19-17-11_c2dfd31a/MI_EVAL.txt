# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with validations, normalizations, and affirmations throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good brief empathic statements and validation of worry, but reflections are often short and could be deepened with more complex reflections of emotion and meaning (e.g., about the 'very, very beautiful' moment).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on the behavioral target and repeatedly returns to concrete planning (activity choice, duration, stop-rules, and follow-up) with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the client as an equal partner by co-developing the tiny step, soliciting preferences, offering follow-up, and using inclusive language rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering options, emphasizing reversibility, encouraging checking with the doctor, and deferring to the client's judgment about timing and pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some client-generated motivation and commitment (specific plan and timing), but leans on clinician-supplied strategies and would benefit from more open-ended, value- and confidence-focused questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as collaborative, inviting input and sharing power when setting the plan and agreeing on follow-up, which fosters genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reinforces change talk by prompting specific, doable actions, eliciting commitment language, and celebrating small wins leading to a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and offers alternatives and scaling, but could further reduce sustain talk with more strategic double-sided reflections naming both sides of ambivalence.
