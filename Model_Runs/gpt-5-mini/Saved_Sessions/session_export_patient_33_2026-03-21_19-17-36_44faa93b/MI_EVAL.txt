# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates respect and nonjudgmental support and affirms practical steps, but affirmations are often brief and the clinician shifts to information-giving instead of deeper individualized validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates understanding and tailors explanations to Diego's concerns, yet relies more on information and few reflective statements that would deepen emotional rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on overdose risk reduction and treatment access with concrete safety planning and logistics throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Diego's input and co-develops a safety plan with role‑play and scripts, though some exchanges are clinician-driven lists rather than co-constructed solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks permission before acting, and explains options without pressure, reinforcing Diego's ability to choose his path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some concrete actions and commitments but provides many solutions without probing Diego's internal motivations, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates shared control by asking what to focus and offering help, yet occasionally adopts an expert-information stance rather than co-constructing plans from Diego's language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits readiness and concrete steps but does not consistently amplify or link Diego's change language to his values and long-term goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids judging sustain talk and moves to safety strategies, but misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or affirmations that would strategically soften sustain talk.
