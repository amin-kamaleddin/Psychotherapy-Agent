# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed the patient's honesty and effort without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and understanding of sleep and social context, but reflections tended to be pragmatic rather than deeper affective or complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and its impact, moving logically from assessment to concrete, measurable strategies and a scheduled follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—invited preferences, co‑constructed a realistic plan, and negotiated a mutually agreed check‑in time, treating the patient as a partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice and control, offered options rather than directives, and framed slips as information rather than failure to support autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation around sleep and prompted concrete steps that produced commitment language, but did not deeply explore values, importance/confidence, or broader reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert, offered menus of options, and repeatedly asked which strategies felt most helpful, sharing power in decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and secured a concrete plan, though missed opportunities to amplify, summarize, and probe confidence/importance to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized social reasons for drinking while offering harm‑reduction, but could have used double‑sided reflections to gently highlight discrepancy.
