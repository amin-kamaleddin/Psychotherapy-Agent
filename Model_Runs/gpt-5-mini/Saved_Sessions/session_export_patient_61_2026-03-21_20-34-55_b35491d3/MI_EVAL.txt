# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened with explicit nonjudgmental support and used warm, validating language throughout, avoiding blame and normalizing overwhelm.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections that showed understanding and encouraged elaboration, but could probe deeper into underlying emotions (e.g., fear, shame) with more affect-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on home blood glucose checks and consistently steered toward concrete, actionable micro-goals with very little topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a two-way effort by eliciting preferences, offering concise options, and refining whatever the client chose in a pragmatic, cooperative way.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly provided choices, used permission language, avoided pressuring, and reinforced the client's ownership of the plan and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client's own ideas and supports (anchoring to coffee, sticky-note, meter placement) and used scaling, but could further probe personal motives and values to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the client as the expert, invited choice of focus, co‑created concrete plans, and offered help only when requested.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced small change talk and used confidence scaling, yet could amplify and explore expected benefits to strengthen desire and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validated resistance and avoided reinforcing sustain talk but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and explicit exploration of costs of staying the same.
