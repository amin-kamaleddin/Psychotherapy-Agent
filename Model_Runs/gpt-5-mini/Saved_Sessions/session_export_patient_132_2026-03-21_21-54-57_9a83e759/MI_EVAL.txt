# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently expressed unconditional positive regard, normalized Yasmin's experience, highlighted strengths, and avoided judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries of Yasmin's relapse pattern and feelings, but reflections tended to be summary-focused rather than probing deeper emotions, values, or daily meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on shifting from rigid dieting to sustainable habits and kept the session anchored to concrete, behaviorally specific steps with a logical sequence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician solicited Yasmin's views, co-created options, and problem-solved together rather than prescribing a single path.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering choices, honoring preferences (e.g., tracking energy/clothes), and framing suggestions as options for Yasmin to select.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently drew out Yasmin's own solutions and practical reasons for change, but could more actively elicit deeper intrinsic motivations and DARN-CAT language (importance/confidence/commitment).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a partnership stance, positioned Yasmin as the expert, invited her to choose strategies, and co-constructed the two-week experiment together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk through options and links to values, but missed opportunities to amplify explicit change statements and elicit stronger commitment or confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without arguing and offered reframes, but could better use MI-specific techniques (double-sided reflections, eliciting discrepancy) and avoid over-rationalizing with calorie math.
