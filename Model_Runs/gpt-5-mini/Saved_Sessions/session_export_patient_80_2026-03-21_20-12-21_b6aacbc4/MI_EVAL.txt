# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard with affirmations and gratitude, creating a warm, nonjudgmental tone from the start.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking and validates feelings, but rarely probes deeper emotional nuance like fear, shame, or guilt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing alcohol use and preventing violence, balancing safety planning with behavior-change steps and a concrete follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates true two-way collaboration by offering options, inviting choices, rehearsing scripts, and adapting plans to the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, framing plans as experiments, and asking the patient which steps feel most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical reasons and supports for change and amplifies progress, but could more deliberately explore values, importance, and confidence with scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance with inclusive language and co-creation (e.g., shaping routines together and inviting the patient to choose priorities).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and specific steps, yet misses opportunities to probe importance, confidence, and commitment to deepen and consolidate motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responds nonjudgmentally and avoids amplifying sustain talk, but uses few double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to actively resolve ambivalence.
