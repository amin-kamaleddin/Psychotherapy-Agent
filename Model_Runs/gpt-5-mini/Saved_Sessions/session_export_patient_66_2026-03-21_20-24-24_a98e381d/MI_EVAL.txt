# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating from the opening line, normalizing the patient's perspective and avoiding criticism or pressure throughout the session.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that showed understanding, though there was a missed opportunity to explore deeper emotional context or values to strengthen connection.

### Direction

### Collaboration
