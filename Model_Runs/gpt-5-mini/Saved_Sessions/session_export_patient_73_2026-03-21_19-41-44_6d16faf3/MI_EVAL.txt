# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalized struggles, affirmed past efforts, and offered unconditional support and help without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and brief reflective listening (e.g., 'frustrated but conflicted'), but reflections were pragmatic and could be deeper to capture fear/shame and underlying affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and immediate, achievable steps (delay method, alarm, friend text), guiding toward concrete action throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co-constructed—clinician invited preferences, refined messages with the patient, and honored edits, resulting in a jointly created plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked which option felt doable, and provided multiple ways to enact the plan, reinforcing Linda's control and ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited personal strategies and had the patient generate texts and alarms, mobilizing motivation, but could increase open questions and scaling to deepen reasons and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance with inclusive language and shared planning, though occasionally leaned toward offering expert solutions that made the stance slightly more directive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited change language about past successes and practical strategies and offered small-change options, but could better amplify and reflect specific statements of desire, reasons, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, normalized slips, and redirected to actionable steps, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain reasons while nudging toward change.
