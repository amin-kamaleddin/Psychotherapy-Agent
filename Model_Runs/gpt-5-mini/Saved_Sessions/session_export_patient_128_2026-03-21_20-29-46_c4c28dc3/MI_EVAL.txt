### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening that helped Tyler elaborate on his concerns. Could increase use of deeper affective and occasional complex reflections to capture shame, fear, or underlying values.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintains clear, focused direction on alcohol-related behavior with concrete targets (pre-gaming, drink limits, sober buddy, exit cue). Steers toward actionable harm-reduction strategies appropriate to the referral.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirms Tyler’s control and choices, asks permission before brainstorming or role-play, and presents options so he can decide what feels doable. Avoids pressuring language and supports self-directed decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation, confidence ratings, and specific intentions, drawing motivation from Tyler’s own statements. Could more often prompt personal reasons for change, evoke discrepancy, and use questions that deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Tyler as an active partner throughout, asking which strategies to lock in and how to role-play, demonstrating genuine power-sharing. Repeatedly defers to Tyler’s preferences and pacing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (brainstorming, confidence ruler, role-play) and produces tentative commitments. Would benefit from more deliberate reflection and amplification of Tyler’s change statements to build momentum toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by offering pragmatic harm-reduction alternatives and using double-sided reflections early on. Could use more strategic softening techniques and elicit Tyler’s own arguments against sustain-talk to shift the balance further.
