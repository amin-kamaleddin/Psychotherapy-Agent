# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental tone with explicit validation and normalization of difficulty. Repeated affirmations highlight strengths and avoid blame, creating a reliably accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and summaries capture both content and emotion, demonstrating strong attunement. This reflective listening fosters rapport and encourages disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on smoking reduction and a specific short-walk strategy. Questions and reflections steer toward actionable steps, contingency planning, and measurable signs of success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites co-creation and follows Samuel's leads, using dialogic language and shared problem-solving. The clinician and patient jointly construct the plan and options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers multiple options and explicitly asks which feels most doable, then honors the patient's choice. Language affirms Samuel's ownership and avoids pressuring directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Samuel's own motivations through value-oriented and evocative questions, then amplifies his change language. The clinician mines internal reasons for change and links them to the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as a collaboration, soliciting Samuel's expertise and preferences throughout. Repeated invitations to choose and co-construct solutions reinforce an equal-partner stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, highlights concrete successes, and asks evocative questions that build commitment. Reflections and summaries consistently amplify Samuel's goals and concrete plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and ambivalence and avoids reinforcing it, but misses some opportunities for stronger strategic softening. Could more often use targeted double-sided reflections or selective emphasis to more forcefully foreground change reasons.
