# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance with repeated permission statements and validations; supportive language was sometimes brief/pragmatic but reliably accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking that facilitated disclosure, but many reflections remained surface-level and missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions, meanings, or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on handling doctor pressure and medication refusal, guiding the session logically from options to a concrete plan and confidence assessment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the encounter as joint problem-solving and invited patient input, but frequently offered ready-made scripts which shifted some content-generation toward the clinician rather than the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized choice and control throughout, presenting options, asking permission, and validating the patient's right to set boundaries and decide next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited change language and confidence ratings and helped produce a plan, but could have better evoked intrinsic motivation by linking choices to the patient's values or asking for his own wording first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and incorporated patient preferences into the plan, though offering multiple clinician-generated scripts somewhat reduced the session's client-led quality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and supported change-oriented statements (choice of lines, confidence 7/10) and offered small options, but could have amplified and reflected change talk more and asked for specific commitment timing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and rolled with resistance, redirecting toward practical strategies to limit sustain talk, but did not use double-sided reflections or highlight discrepancy to further soften resistance.
