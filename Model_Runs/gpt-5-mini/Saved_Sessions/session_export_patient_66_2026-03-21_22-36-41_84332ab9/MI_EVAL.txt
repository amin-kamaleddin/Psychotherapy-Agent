# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Kareem’s ambivalence (e.g., 'nothing about you is wrong'), creating a nonjudgmental atmosphere. Acknowledges enjoyment of smoking without criticism and frames the plan as a reasonable test.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides frequent, accurate reflections that match both content and affect (e.g., complex opening reflection and repeated tension statements). Validates Kareem’s experience and fosters strong rapport throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a tight focus on smoking at work and moves logically from ambivalence to a concrete, time-limited plan. Questions and suggestions consistently tie back to practical strategies and Kareem’s priorities (keeping his job, reducing hiding).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-created with Kareem selecting options and the clinician integrating his preferences into the plan. The clinician solicits input and jointly develops specific strategies (four cigarettes, gum, alarms, walking).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Kareem’s control by offering choices and framing the approach as his test rather than pressuring for cold-turkey quitting. Repeatedly asks what feels doable and lets Kareem choose the plan components.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motives (protecting job, family concerns, wanting control) and Kareem’s own ideas for solutions, but could more consistently deepen and consolidate that motivation (e.g., elicit confidence/commitment language).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong power-sharing by inviting Kareem’s input and choices and avoiding prescriptive advice. Clinician and patient jointly troubleshoot barriers and build a shared, practical plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and builds on change talk with future-oriented questions and invitations to experiment, resulting in spontaneous change language. Missed opportunities remain to amplify and anchor that talk into explicit commitment or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, acknowledges it directly, and reframes it toward manageable options (e.g., calling the plan a 'reasonable test'). Could further reduce sustain talk using more double-sided reflections and consolidated summaries of sustain-plus-change.
