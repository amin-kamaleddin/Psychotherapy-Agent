# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing Brandon's perspective and explicitly acknowledging his preferences. Warm, respectful language avoided labeling or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and accurate paraphrasing, with helpful summaries Brandon accepted. Could increase use of complex, emotion-focused reflections to probe deeper feelings (e.g., shame, fear) for a higher rating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on drinking and moved stepwise from exploration to a concrete, measurable plan (limits, start date, coping strategies, role-play). Balanced guidance with client-centered approaches without losing direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged Brandon as an active partner in defining the problem and selecting strategies, using his language to shape interventions. Decisions and the plan emerged jointly rather than being imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored Brandon's choices, offered options instead of directives, and requested permission before role-plays or tweaks. Framed suggestions as choices that reinforced his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Brandon by asking evocative questions, amplifying change statements, and prompting him to articulate a concrete plan and coping responses. Helped translate internal motivation into actionable steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine co-construction by offering choices, checking preferences, and inviting Brandon to select doable tactics. Demonstrated shared power and respect for his expertise about his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and strengthened change talk using open questions, scaling, and targeted prompts, leading to concrete commitment (start tonight, specific limits). Amplified and built on Brandon's reasons for change and coping ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tactfully acknowledged sustain talk and redirected to harm-reduction strategies without arguing, preserving social values while nudging change. Could more systematically use double-sided reflections to explicitly name both enjoyment and costs to further soften sustain talk.
