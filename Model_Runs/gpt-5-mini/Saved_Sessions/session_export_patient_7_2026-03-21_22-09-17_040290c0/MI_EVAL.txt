# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful—normalized Amina's experience, offered explicit acceptance, and repeatedly followed her lead throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, simple reflections and validated Amina's affect and requests while tolerating silence, demonstrating strong attunement to her stance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the session's micro-goal (safety, comfort, engagement) and repeatedly returned to agreed priorities without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint decision-making by offering options, asking preferences, and committing to follow Amina's wishes, creating a co-constructed process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and control, respected explicit requests about timing and checking in, and avoided pressuring language to reinforce Amina's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened with appropriate evoking questions but did not pursue them after Amina declined, so efforts to elicit intrinsic motivation were limited though reasonable given her stance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Amina as the expert in her experience, asked permission before intervening, and consistently shared power in determining session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Initial change-focused questions were asked but not followed up after resistance, resulting in minimal elicitation of desire, ability, reason, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing or confronting sustain talk and accepted Amina's resistance while redirecting to options and safety planning, though deeper strategic redirection was limited.
