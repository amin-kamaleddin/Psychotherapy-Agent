# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and respect (opening line, nonjudgmental reflections) and avoided labels or blame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking (e.g., autonomy/control reflections) but reflections stayed mostly surface-level without deeper naming of emotions or underlying concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on charging/use of the monitor with clear practical steps (scripts, 15-minute plug-in, 8:00 PM follow-up) and requested confirmation of the plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a joint enterprise by inviting Rowan to choose options, co-creating scripts, rehearsing together, and checking willingness before moving forward.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, let Rowan pick and tweak scripts, acknowledged his right to refuse, and framed consequences as information rather than ultimatums.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited change language and relied on offered scripts rather than open-ended elicitation of Rowan’s own motivations, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Rowan as a collaborator by letting him direct choices, shaping the plan together, and practicing responses he selected.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Succeeded in securing small, behaviorally framed commitments (15 minutes, 8:00 PM) but did not sufficiently draw out or amplify Rowan’s internal reasons or benefits for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and validated sustain talk while offering options and reframes, though necessary statements about consequences/documentation may have unintentionally reinforced resistance in places.
