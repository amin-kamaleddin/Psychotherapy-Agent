# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing priorities and explicitly honoring the patient's wish not to be pressed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, summarizing the dental priority and the patient's negative experiences with past quit attempts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps smoking cessation as the overall target while prioritizing the patient's dental goal and arranging a concrete follow-up, but misses opportunities to elicit concrete change planning or commitment language.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative, soliciting preferences and co-creating a plan that fits the patient's wishes (timing, privacy, no calls/texts).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by emphasizing choice, offering options, and explicitly agreeing not to press or contact the patient except by his preference.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Begins with an evocative question but largely shifts to acceptance and logistics rather than drawing out the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites the patient's input and shares decision-making power, confirming preferences and jointly agreeing on a one-visit check-in plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some tentative change language and offers alternatives, but fails to follow up with reflections or probes to deepen and amplify the patient's conditional change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids arguing and validates resistance, using reframes and promises not to press, though could add strategic double-sided reflections or gentle redirection toward change reasons.
