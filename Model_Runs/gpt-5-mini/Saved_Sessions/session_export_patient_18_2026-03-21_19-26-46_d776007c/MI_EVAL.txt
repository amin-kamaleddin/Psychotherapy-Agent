# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, with normalization, validation, repeated affirmations, and explicit respect for the patient’s preference not to quit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good empathic statements and some reflections (e.g., validating stress and embarrassment), but could have explored emotional nuance more deeply and sustained reflection before moving to problem‑solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol safety and risk reduction throughout, linking role‑plays and planning directly to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑created the safety plan, edited the friend text together, and invited the patient into role‑play and decision making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering choices, accepting the patient’s decision not to quit, and encouraging language and plans that fit her preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited intentions and practical commitments but relied more on advice and skills training than on drawing out personal values or deeper intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership with repeated permission‑seeking, shared decisions, and clear honoring of the patient’s expertise about what felt natural.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change‑oriented talk with choices, affirmations, and commitments, but missed opportunities to reflect and amplify the patient’s personal reasons and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well by avoiding confrontation and using harm‑reduction and collaborative strategies that did not reinforce reasons to continue drinking.
