# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance through affirmations and normalization, which maintained respect and acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking to acknowledge the patient's emotions and constraints, helping the patient feel understood and prompting continued disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, sustained focus on smoking and child safety, steering the conversation toward specific, behaviorally defined goals and implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Built a genuine joint problem-solving approach by inviting input at each step, checking preferences, and tailoring suggestions to the patient's lived constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced choice and avoided pressure, honoring the patient's readiness and offering harm-reduction options rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical commitments and used a confidence ruler, but leaned toward strategy-focused questions rather than deeper values- or motivation-focused elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing through offering choices, asking permission, and co-constructing a plan that reflected the patient's priorities and decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change and commitment language (specific steps and plans) but could probe more for intrinsic motivations and amplify the patient's own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected minimization toward harm-reduction options, though could more explicitly use double-sided reflections to validate sustain talk while highlighting discrepancy.
