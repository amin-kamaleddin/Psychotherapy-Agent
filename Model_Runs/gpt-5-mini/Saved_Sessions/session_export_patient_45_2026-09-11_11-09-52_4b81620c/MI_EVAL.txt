# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and validating pragmatic choices; avoided blame and invited collaboration throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflections and perspective-taking, matching the client's language and constraints; could deepen empathic work by naming emotions and probing underlying feelings more often.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing after-shift alcohol and monitoring glucose, guiding toward a single measurable first step; questions and reflections tied back to concrete behavioral goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative tone with co-created plans and inclusive language, inviting the client's ideas; at times offered multiple clinician-generated options quickly rather than eliciting more spontaneous client solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's choice and control, offered options and asked permission, and reinforced ownership of the plan and fallbacks; avoided directive language throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's motivations and elicited concrete commitments (NA beer, reminder, glucose check); could strengthen evocation by using scaling questions and exploring longer-term values to increase commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly acted as a collaborator rather than an expert, using power-sharing language and deferring to the client's preferences; consistently fostered client expertise in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, prompting concrete change statements and planning; missed some opportunities to amplify desire, ability, and commitment with targeted follow-ups or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing, normalized both sides of ambivalence, and offered small practical options to reduce resistance; could more often use double-sided reflections or selectively amplify change language earlier.
