# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Begins with explicit unconditional positive regard and maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating feelings and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate reflections that capture affect and content, though could deepen empathic exploration of the emotional meaning of the missed opportunity and parenting identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on overcoming childcare barriers to attend interviews, but occasionally offers many options at once and could tighten agenda-setting and prioritize one concrete next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co-creates the plan, seeks client input and consent, and offers to role-play or tailor scripts only when requested, demonstrating an egalitarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly asks permission, offers choices, and supports the client's decisions without pressure, clearly respecting the client's agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivations, prior efforts, and practical steps rather than imposing reasons, but could more deliberately draw out deeper personal motives and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a partnership by inviting brainstorming, tailoring tools to the client's voice, and sharing power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change-oriented statements and concrete steps, yet misses chances to amplify the client's high confidence rating and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects sustain talk toward solutions and alternatives, but could use more advanced techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly soften sustain talk.
