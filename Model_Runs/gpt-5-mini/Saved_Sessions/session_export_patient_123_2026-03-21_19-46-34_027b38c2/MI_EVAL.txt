# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys respect and nonjudgmental support, affirming the patient's report and offering help without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good basic reflective listening and some perspective-taking (distress rating, follow-ups), but reflections stay factual with limited exploration of deeper emotions or nuances.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, practical focus on the chosen target (the English paper) and guides the session stepwise from topic selection to thesis and outline.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is cooperative with options and checks, but the clinician leans toward delivering ready-made content rather than fully co-creating it with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient's choices, offers multiple options, and asks permission, avoiding pressure and supporting the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicitation of intrinsic motivation and reasons for change is minimal; the clinician does not probe why finishing the paper matters or the patient's confidence and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally adopts a collaborative stance by asking the patient's preference and offering support, but becomes more directive when producing the thesis and outline.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some moments of eliciting actionable language (task choice, willingness to brainstorm) but limited systematic questioning to draw out desire, ability, reasons, or commitment to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is largely absent and not amplified, yet the clinician misses opportunities to use strategic softening (e.g., double-sided reflections) for minor hesitations.
