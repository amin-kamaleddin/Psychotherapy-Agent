### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician established immediate unconditional positive regard and maintained a warm, nonjudgmental tone, repeatedly validating Anika’s experience and normalizing ambivalence to create psychological safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries captured ambivalence and relevant triggers, but the clinician missed opportunities to probe deeper into specific emotions and bodily cues to strengthen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on vaping and behavior change tied to basketball, progressing logically from exploration to concrete planning, scripting, and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a team effort with co-created language, role-play, and iterative feedback, adjusting to Anika’s preferences rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported choice by asking permission, offering options, and emphasizing that Anika decides what feels authentic and feasible without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good use of open questions to elicit motivations, past successes, and confidence, though the clinician could have more frequently amplified change talk, explored underlying values, and asked for a specific commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High level of partnership demonstrated through invitations to choose wording, practicing lines together, and positioning Anika as the expert on what feels natural.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicited and reinforced change talk (past quit, confidence rating, alternatives) but could have amplified client statements more and probed for a verbal commitment or specific timeframe.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** While the clinician avoided arguing and did not reinforce sustain talk, there was limited active work to soften minimization; double-sided reflections or strategic summaries could have reduced sustain talk's influence.
