# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and validation, avoiding judgment and normalizing Sofia's feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate empathic reflections and summaries matched Sofia’s emotional tone and concerns across the interaction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear focus on improving school safety and concrete change steps, repeatedly steering interventions back to practical planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician and Sofia co‑constructed the plan with repeated invitations to choose options and refine steps, honoring Sofia’s preferences in real time.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently offered choices, avoided pressure, and reinforced Sofia’s control over which strategies to try and how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited Sofia’s priorities and small change goals but could have probed deeper for intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was framed as a joint enterprise with clear power‑sharing; clinician deferred to Sofia’s expertise and repeatedly invited her to direct next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited and reinforced multiple pieces of change talk and moved to concrete planning, but missed opportunities to amplify importance, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician validated sustain talk and worries but did limited strategic softening or reframing (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to shift sustain language toward change.
