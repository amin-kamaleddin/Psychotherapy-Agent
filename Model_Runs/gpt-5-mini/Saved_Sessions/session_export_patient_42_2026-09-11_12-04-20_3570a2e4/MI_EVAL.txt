# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician is respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Elise's ambivalence and practical ideas; however affirmations are brief and not warmly individualized, lacking explicit normalization of emotional costs.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking with accurate reflections that capture the tension between health and connection; rarely pursues deeper emotional content or uses more complex reflections to surface underlying feelings like fear of exclusion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on social smoking and a concrete, achievable first step (bringing a hot drink), consistently steering the session toward a measurable small experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as joint exploration using inclusive language and soliciting Elise's preferences, adapting plans to her schedule and allowing her to choose the test, demonstrating shared problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors Elise's choice by offering options, framing experiments as tests, and inviting her to pick what feels doable, avoiding pressure and supporting control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivations indirectly by asking what would indicate success and letting Elise pick experiments, but stops short of deeper value-linking, scaling, or confidence probes to strengthen intrinsic motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and invites Elise's expertise, yet at times the clinician quickly proposes logistics rather than fully eliciting Elise's own rationale or past successes to reinforce ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates opportunities and elicits concrete change language (willingness to test the drink), but often moves quickly to planning without consistently reflecting, amplifying, or sequencing DARN(C) to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and effectively redirects to experiments and alternatives, though could more consistently use MI techniques (double-sided reflections, empathic exploration) to further soften sustain talk.
