### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated highly accurate reflective listening, mirroring the patient's phrasing and linking concerns to the infection scare to show strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus on reducing infection risk, repeatedly steering the conversation toward concrete harm-reduction actions and implementation steps.

### Collaboration

### Partnership
