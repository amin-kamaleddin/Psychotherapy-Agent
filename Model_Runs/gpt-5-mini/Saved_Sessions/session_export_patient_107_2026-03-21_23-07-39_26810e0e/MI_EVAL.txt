### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with validating, nonjudgmental language and reflections; no blaming or minimization of the client’s perspective.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring both practical concerns and feelings to validate and clarify ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on coffee consumption and steered the conversation toward a specific, testable experiment with measurable criteria for success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equitable partner in designing the experiment, offering suggestions while routinely asking permission and keeping the client’s agenda central.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, normalizing reversible experimentation, and checking what the client wanted to lock in to reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client’s reasons for change and concrete success criteria, but could deepen evocation by exploring longer-term values, using scaling, or eliciting a commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely invited the client’s input and decision-making, co-producing plans and deferring to the client’s judgments about feasibility to demonstrate genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk through doable experiments and success criteria, but could more consistently amplify, summarize, and probe for confidence or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and gently redirected to solutions, though there were missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and immediate contrasts to further diminish sustain language.
