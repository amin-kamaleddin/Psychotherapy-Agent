# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating (e.g., normalizing the tension) and avoided blame when Ryan hedged. Maintained steady supportive regard while working with Ryan's priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid perspective-taking and reflective listening (accurate synthesis of goals vs social life). Could have deepened affective reflection on hedging language like “I guess” and defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, progressive focus on reducing drinking-related risk and moved from options to a concrete action (texting Jake, buddy system, leave-by time). Every question tied back to the target behavior and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co-constructed with frequent invitations for Ryan's input and choice (which strategy, which friend, exact wording). Clinician offered to write the message together and deferred to Ryan's expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering multiple options and asking Ryan what felt doable, avoiding coercion. Validated Ryan's desire to stay social while protecting safety and grades.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Ryan's own solutions and motivations, producing concrete change talk and a viable plan. Could have probed deeper into personal values or used rulers to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership evident through joint problem-solving and power-sharing (inviting choices, co-writing the text). Decisions were clearly collaborative rather than imposed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Systematically evoked and amplified change talk with open questions that moved from options to specific commitments. Could have more explicitly summarized and reinforced Ryan's change language or explored confidence/importance to solidify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and pivoted to planning, which prevented escalation of sustain talk. Missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections or selective reflections to more fully resolve ambivalence.
