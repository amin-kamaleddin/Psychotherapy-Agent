# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating; offers concrete supports and normalizes concerns, showing clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate empathic responses and some reflections, but misses opportunities for deeper emotional or double‑sided reflections to surface ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the conversation tightly focused on starting and safely managing a statin with each intervention tied to that target behavior and a logical progression to planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the patient as an equal partner by soliciting concerns, tailoring materials, rehearsing wording, and co‑constructing a practical plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing recommendations as patient‑driven without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some practical motivations and reasons but largely relies on information and planning rather than asking evocative questions to draw out deeper internal motivation or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong shared decision‑making—invites input on wording, timing, and actions, and adapts recommendations to the patient's needs and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces existing change talk and converts readiness into planning but does little active elicitation or amplification of desire, ability, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reduces sustain talk with clear information and a monitoring/safety plan, though could further use double‑sided reflections to strategically resolve ambivalence.
