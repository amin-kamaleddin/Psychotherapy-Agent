# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment, honoring the patient's boundaries and affirming practicality throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with frequent, validating reflections and summaries that help the patient feel heard and keep defenses low.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking behavior and guides the conversation from broad options to a concrete, time‑bound implementation plan and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters joint decision-making by offering options, checking wording and preferences, and positioning the clinician as a supportive helper rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes patient choice and control by offering alternatives, asking preferences, and honoring the patient's wishes about follow‑up and support style.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation and past successes and secures a small commitment, but relies somewhat on clinician-suggested options rather than amplifying the patient's intrinsic reasons (e.g., relational motives).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on what will work, co-constructing the plan and repeatedly deferring to the patient's judgment and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and plans, though could more deliberately amplify the most potent relational change language to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently rolls with resistance and reframes sustain talk into workable options, while more use of double-sided reflections could further soften sustain talk.
