# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validated Kenji’s efforts and doubts, avoiding judgment throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking by summarizing concerns like grogginess and ambivalence, but reflections remained content-focused and could probe deeper emotions or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing alcohol use with concrete, measurable steps (diary, readiness scale, focused follow-up) and resisted sidetracks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-created the plan by soliciting and implementing Kenji’s preferences for diary format, alarm time, start date, and check-in method.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission, and emphasized voluntariness, reinforcing Kenji’s control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited Kenji to express reasons and readiness (e.g., 'what would help you decide?', 0–10 scale) but could deepen elicitation of personal values and follow up on brief change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Kenji as the expert on his life, negotiated the agenda, and shared power in planning and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change language (concerns about sleep/energy, willingness to try a diary) but missed several opportunities to reflect and amplify those statements into stronger desire/reason/ability/confidence talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing ambivalence and reframing goals toward cutting back, though more double-sided reflections could have further resolved ambivalence.
