# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes Hana's experience, and offers frequent praise and encouragement without blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening at key moments, but could provide deeper affect-focused reflections around shame and embarrassment to fully capture emotional intensity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on preparing for study tasks with concrete, measurable actions (timers, specific micro-goals, scheduling) and repeatedly redirects to actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally works as an equal partner by offering options and checking back, though frequent scripted checklists and concrete instructions make the interaction somewhat clinician-driven rather than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering multiple options, validating smaller steps, asking Hana to choose, and respecting limits (e.g., not messaging proactively).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some self-motivation (choices, past success) but leans heavily on providing strategies instead of exploring Hana's values, reasons for change, or eliciting stronger intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters collaboration through options and invitations to debrief, yet at times adopts an expert stance with prescriptive guidance rather than eliciting Hana's own strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk by asking about doable steps and validating wins, but could deepen change talk by exploring importance, confidence, and how change would affect Hana's life.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation, but tends to move quickly to problem-solving and uses few double-sided reflections or reframing to resolve ambivalence.
