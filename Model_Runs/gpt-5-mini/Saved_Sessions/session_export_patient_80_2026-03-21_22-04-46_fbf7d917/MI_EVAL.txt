# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently displayed unconditional positive regard—validated progress and the partner's role, used affirming language, and avoided blame or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, mirroring Luka's feelings and responding to concerns like awkwardness in a validating way.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol use and preventing violent outbursts, tying questions and interventions directly to triggers, safety, and concrete steps without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Created a genuine team dynamic by inviting role‑play and rehearsal, co‑creating the plan, and soliciting Luka's preferences about timing, place, and who starts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized Luka's choice and control by offering options, asking which steps felt doable, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited internal motivation and practical solutions—used open questions, a confidence scale, and repeated invitations for Luka to choose and refine his plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision‑making and asked permission, but occasionally offered clinician‑generated plan elements before eliciting Luka's initial ideas, which reduced client-led planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions and scaling, yet missed some opportunities to mirror, amplify, and summarize specific change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and shifted attention to planning rather than sustaining talk, but could have used more double‑sided reflections and selective reinforcement to more strategically reframe ambivalence.
