# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently used nonjudgmental, validating language and conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalizing Sofia's experience and reassuring her without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with empathic reflections that validated Sofia's feelings and invited gentle elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session remained focused on peer conflict, rumors, and school avoidance while steering toward concrete, behaviorally specific strategies like scripts, grounding, and exit plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a partnership—clinician offered choices, invited Sofia's preferences, and co-created messages and role-plays tailored to her needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician reinforced Sofia's sense of control by offering multiple options, respecting her reluctance to involve teachers, and asking permission before drafting or role-playing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some relevant content was elicited (preferences, a possible friend), but the clinician relied more on offering solutions than on systematic evocative questions to draw out Sofia's intrinsic motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shared power throughout the session, inviting Sofia to choose next steps and engaging her in co-producing scripts and practice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were attempts to elicit change (offering options and scripts) and brief change language from Sofia, but limited amplification of desire, reasons, or commitment through evocative questions and reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered redirections and coping strategies, but used few strategic softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to resolve ambivalence.
