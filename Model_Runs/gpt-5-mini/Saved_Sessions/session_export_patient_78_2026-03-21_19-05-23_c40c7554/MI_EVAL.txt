# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing slips and offering pragmatic, nonmoralizing options throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and short reflections tailored to the patient's preferences, but opportunities were missed to explore deeper feelings (e.g., authority issues, family concerns) and emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on smoking reduction and handling repetitive medical advice, with concrete, measurable tasks and no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach—asked the patient to choose options, co-created scripts, and rehearsed responses together, avoiding an expert-only stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking which fit the patient's life, and respecting hedged commitments rather than prescribing one path.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited behavioral intentions and a week-long commitment, but did not sufficiently probe intrinsic motives, values, or confidence to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a shared project, honored the patient's preferences, and invited choice and rehearsal, conveying that expertise about the life rests with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively prompted concrete change talk and a specific plan (gum for a week), but could have amplified and explored reasons, confidence, and importance to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolled with resistance, validated frustration, and used role-play to deflect sustain talk, though more double-sided reflections or reframing would further soften sustain language.
