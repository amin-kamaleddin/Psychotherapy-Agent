### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, normalizing Malik's feelings and avoiding moralizing language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and named emotions, showing solid perspective-taking; could deepen connection with more complex, affect-laden reflections to increase emotional accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on cannabis use and harm-reduction goals, using scaling and planning to move to a clear, concrete one-week plan with minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as an equal partner by inviting Malik to choose actions, checking preferences, and offering support options rather than directing decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance throughout, co-constructing the plan and positioning the clinician as a teammate supporting Malik's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively invited and strengthened change talk with open questions and a readiness ruler, though could better amplify and extend Malik's own change statements before offering options.
