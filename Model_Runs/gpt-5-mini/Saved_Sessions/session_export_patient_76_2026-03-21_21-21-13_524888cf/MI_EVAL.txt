# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with validation, praise, and normalizing language throughout; a minor opportunity is to use more individualized affirmations naming specific coping strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections that matched pace and tone, though a few deeper, more complex feeling-focused reflections (e.g., helplessness or relief dynamics) would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate safety focus with explicit checks, time-bound action items, scaling measures, and concrete steps to prevent harm with minimal topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like a true joint effort—clinician invited input, co-constructed the plan, offered practical help with consent, and avoided an expert-driven lecture.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission for interventions, and affirming that decisions were the patient's, reducing coercion and enhancing control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used scaling and questions about antecedents to elicit some motivation and amplified small steps, but did not sufficiently explore values, longer-term reasons, or use targeted DARN-C prompts to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and power-sharing through choice, permission, and offers to stay in chat, positioning the patient as an active agent in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced small amounts of change talk (choices, scaling, noting urge reductions) and secured immediate steps, but missed opportunities to directly evoke desire/ability/reasons/need and stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontational responses and redirected toward safety and alternatives effectively, though could use more double-sided reflections and selective reinforcement to further pivot away from sustain talk.
