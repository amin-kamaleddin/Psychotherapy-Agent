# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established unconditional positive regard from the opening line and maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone while validating and normalizing the patient’s perspective.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and paraphrases and routinely checked understanding, demonstrating solid perspective-taking; could deepen emotional reflections about the ritual/attachment to smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on managing smoking at work and the one-week behavior test, with every question and suggestion tied to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan with the patient by tailoring suggestions, inviting preferences, and letting the patient shape choices like gum dose, stashing locations, and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed choice and ownership by respecting resistance, asking permission before advising, and framing options as optional and patient-led.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited change talk, success criteria, and confidence ratings to draw out motivation; could probe deeper into intrinsic values and past successes to strengthen internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership through permission-seeking and co-creation of tools, but at times offered multiple concrete solutions quickly instead of first eliciting more patient-generated ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced specific change talk (trialing gum, confidence scaling, success criteria) using open questions; could amplify and link these statements to personal values to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and neutrally reflected sustain talk, then redirected toward practical options which deflected resistance; could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly soften sustain language and resolve ambivalence.
