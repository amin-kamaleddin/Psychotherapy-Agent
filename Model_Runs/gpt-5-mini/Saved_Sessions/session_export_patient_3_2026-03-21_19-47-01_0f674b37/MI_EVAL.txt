# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and warmth, honors Aisha's preferences, and avoids judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening with accurate, validating reflections that reduce shame; missed some opportunities for deeper emotional exploration of nuances like 'gross' or mirror avoidance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, sustained focus on starting and sustaining exercise with concrete actions, tracking, and planned follow-up; repeatedly returns the conversation to specific behavioral goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-creates the plan and solicits preferences, tailoring recommendations to her context, but several moments are clinician-led and could better invite the patient to generate or prioritize options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors autonomy by offering choices, respecting boundaries (e.g., photos, family involvement), and confirming the patient's control over tracking and messaging; no coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some practical commitments and leverages existing motivation but largely relies on advice; misses deeper evocation of values, past successes, confidence scaling, and intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a partnership through shared decision-making and tailored check-in wording, yet the detailed clinician-created plan occasionally overshadows the client's expertise and choice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change language is present but mainly originates from the patient; the clinician could more actively elicit, reflect, and amplify the patient's own reasons, strengths, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively softens sustain talk by normalizing worry, offering low-pressure options and relapse-proof strategies, and agreeing to nonjudgmental responses that reduce shame and defensiveness.
