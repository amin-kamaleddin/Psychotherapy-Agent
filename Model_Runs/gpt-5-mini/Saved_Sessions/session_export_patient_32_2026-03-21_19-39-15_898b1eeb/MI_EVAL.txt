# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental tone and clear validation throughout; clinician accepted the client's reluctance about formal programs and offered alternatives without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective statements that acknowledged isolation and past success, but empathy could be deeper and more frequent with more complex reflections of emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on avoiding repeat DUI and managing peer pressure, translating goals into specific, feasible tactics and practical steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co-constructed and flexible, with the clinician inviting input and tailoring suggestions to the client's context and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multiple options were offered and the clinician explicitly asked what felt doable, respecting the client's choices (e.g., declining role-play) without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited the client's existing motivations and strategies and built on them with practical tools, but could have probed deeper intrinsic motives and used scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful of the client's expertise, though the clinician often supplied scripted lines rather than eliciting the client's own language before offering options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and helped make it actionable, but could have further amplified commitment and confidence with targeted questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided direct confrontation and redirected to planning, yet did not use double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly acknowledge sustain talk while highlighting costs.
