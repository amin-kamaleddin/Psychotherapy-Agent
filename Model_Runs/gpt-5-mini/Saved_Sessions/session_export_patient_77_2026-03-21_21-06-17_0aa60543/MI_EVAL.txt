# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validates feelings, and affirms effort throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate empathic listening with timely, nuanced reflections and summaries that validate the patient's emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on weight-related behaviors (nutrition and triggers), moves logically from problem identification to concrete planning, and secures a specific follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving by soliciting preferences, inviting choices, and co-creating the plan and check-in time, demonstrating shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice and control by offering options, using permission language, and asking the patient to select what feels doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's ideas, confidence ratings, and specific plans using scaling, but could deepen intrinsic motivation by exploring core values and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters a collaborative stance and invites patient input, though providing a detailed menu of options at times slightly shifts authority toward the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk and doable steps, but missed opportunities to further amplify personal reasons, values, and explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk and redirects to manageable steps, with room to use more double-sided reflections and highlight past successes to further soften sustain talk.
