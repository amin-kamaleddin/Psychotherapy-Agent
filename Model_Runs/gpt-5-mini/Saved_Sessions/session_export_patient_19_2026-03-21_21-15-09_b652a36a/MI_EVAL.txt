# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, normalizing difficulties and using specific, sparing praise to preserve dignity and create a safe space for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic reflections that capture ambivalence and stressors, though some reflections remain summary-level and could probe deeper affect (shame, loss, hopes).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on gambling/debt and moves toward concrete, time-limited steps (skip pubs, $30 cash, buddy/exit plan), balancing exploration with forward planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—co-constructs refusal scripts, exit plans, and checks logistics—with the clinician positioned as a partner responding to the client's ideas and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking which step feels doable, and leaving decisions to the client, reinforcing ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client's motivations and resources using open questions and a confidence ruler, but could probe deeper intrinsic motivators and more systematically summarize change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine partnership—asks permission, invites selection among options, and co-creates practical strategies that reflect the client's priorities and expertise about her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk well (open questions, ruler, planning), yet missed some opportunities to deepen commitment language and to expand aspirational language into stronger verbal commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without confrontation using double-sided reflections and validation, but could more often use strategic/amplified reflections and reframes to elicit discrepancy and reduce sustain talk further.
