# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and supportive (e.g., normalizing starting therapy, validating embarrassment) with no shaming or blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows solid reflective listening and perspective-taking (accurate summaries of ambivalence), but reflections were often summary-level and could probe affect and emotional nuance more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the drinking behavior and moves the session toward concrete, actionable steps (scaling, antecedents, specific cap and supports) without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed: the clinician elicits preferences, adapts language to Kevin's voice, and the client's ideas shaped the concrete plan and supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission before advice, and honoring hesitation, reinforcing Kevin's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Kevin's motivations and barriers using questions and scaling, but could strengthen by linking change to personal values and amplifying emergent commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally treats Kevin as the expert and invites choice, though providing numerous specific strategies (with permission) slightly shifted power toward the clinician at times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (open questions, scaling, eliciting steps) and increases confidence, but could more often reflect, amplify, and explore change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and acknowledges ambivalence but makes only modest, inconsistent use of double-sided reflections or strategic phrasing to actively reduce sustain talk.
