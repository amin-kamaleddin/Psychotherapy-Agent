# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth (e.g., validating discouragement, offering pacing and partnership) throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses high-quality reflective listening, accurately naming emotions and skepticism and mirroring the patient's language while inviting elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on a specific, actionable target (tiny job‑search/budget steps) with prompts that drive toward a concrete plan and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint endeavor, uses inclusive language, offers options, and repeatedly asks the patient to choose and shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly emphasizes choice and elicits the patient's preferences (e.g., pacing, wording, backups), avoiding pressure or a prescriptive stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out supports, options, and small commitments but could deepen internal motivation by using scaling, eliciting values, or amplifying hope statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a clear collaborative stance, defers to the patient's judgment, and co‑constructs practical steps together, sharing power throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and develops concrete change talk and commitments (Marco, alarms, fallback) but missed opportunities to amplify, summarize, and probe confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects sustain talk nonjudgmentally, avoiding argument, though sometimes mirrors skeptical wording; could use more double‑sided reflections and highlight exceptions.
