# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with warm greetings, affirmations, and normalization of worries, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and perspective-taking by restating priorities and barriers, but missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflection and more complex reflections to deepen engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking in the car and dust exposure with concrete planning, start dates, and follow-up, resisting drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with negotiated decisions and clinician offers of support, though at times the clinician supplied detailed plans before fully eliciting the patient's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respects the patient's choice by offering options, framing experiments, asking permission, and accepting his control over commitments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivations and links change to values and prior successes, but could probe deeper emotional meaning and more systematically amplify commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session reflects shared decision-making and choice, but occasionally shifts toward clinician-driven guidance rather than first eliciting the patient's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Routinely elicits and amplifies change talk using scaling questions and concrete steps tied to values, with some missed chances to deepen and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk and accepts partial change, but could more actively soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections, strategic summaries, and explicit exploration of costs of staying the same.
