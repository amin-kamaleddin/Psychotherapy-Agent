# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Liora’s experience and normalizing ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking to capture triggers and some affect, but missed opportunities for deeper complex reflections to explore underlying hopelessness and shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on self-harm risk and safety planning from assessment through collaborative planning and rehearsal without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a genuine collaboration with co‑creation of the safety plan, negotiated wording, and jointly agreed escalation criteria and supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, checked preferences, and framed actions as Liora’s decisions while avoiding coercive or prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical coping ideas and willingness to try steps, but relied more on offering solutions than on deeper elicitation of internal motives, values, or reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited input frequently, though at times the clinician led wording and drafting instead of shifting more initiative to Liora.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change language (willingness to try safety plan, coping steps, means restriction) but did not consistently deepen, amplify, or strategically evoke importance/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided reinforcing sustain talk and validated ambivalence while redirecting to small steps, though more double‑sided reflections and explicit naming of sustain talk would further diffuse its pull.
