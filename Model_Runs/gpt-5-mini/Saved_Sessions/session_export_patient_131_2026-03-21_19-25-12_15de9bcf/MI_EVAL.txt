### Direction

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation by highlighting values and steps taken but sometimes becomes solution-focused and could use more strategic open questions to draw out Wiremu’s own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as a collaborator rather than an expert—repeatedly solicits Wiremu’s preferences and jointly constructs plans and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, amplifying commitment statements and prompting concrete, accepted actions and a practical week-long plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain-talk and redirects toward change with autonomy-preserving alternatives, though stronger use of double-sided reflections would better normalize ambivalence before offering solutions.
