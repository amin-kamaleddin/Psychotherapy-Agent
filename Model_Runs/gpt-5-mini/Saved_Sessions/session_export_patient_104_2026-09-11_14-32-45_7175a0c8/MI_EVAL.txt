### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on short-term rest strategies and effectively moved from exploration to specific, actionable implementation steps.

### Collaboration

### Evocation

### Cultivating Change Talk
