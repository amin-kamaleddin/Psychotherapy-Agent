# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes Erik's reactions, and avoids blame, which lowered defensiveness and encouraged cooperation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing Erik's mixed feelings and contextual constraints to foster willingness to experiment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on diet, alcohol, and diabetes management and moves logically from ambivalence to a concrete, measurable first step and specific behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as an equal partnership by inviting Erik's input at each decision point and integrating his choices into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, emphasizing pace and control, and avoiding pressure, which reinforces Erik's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation and values (e.g., reducing meds, wanting evidence) but could more systematically amplify Erik's own reasons and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly defers to Erik's lived experience, co-constructs the plan, and uses options/questions to share power and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with practical tests and affirmations, but could deepen and amplify self-motivating statements with more follow-up questions about reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully reflects and neutralizes sustain talk and reframes testing as noncommittal, though many reflections simply restate sustain rather than using double-sided reflections or strategic reframes to shift language toward change.
