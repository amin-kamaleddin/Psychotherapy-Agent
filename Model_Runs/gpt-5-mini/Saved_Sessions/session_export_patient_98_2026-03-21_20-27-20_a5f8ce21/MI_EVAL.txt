# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard from the outset, using validating, nonjudgmental language that normalizes struggle and avoids labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with several complex reflections, but missed some opportunities to probe deeper emotional meaning (loss, frustration, fear).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on increasing manageable physical activity, moving logically from problem identification to a specific, actionable plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner throughout, co-constructing the plan, soliciting preferences, and integrating the client's choices into practical supports and materials.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and delegating final decisions to the client without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, ideas, and confidence ratings (0–10) and explores ways to raise confidence, but could probe values and personally meaningful reasons more deeply to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine partnership with inclusive language, shared decision-making, and practical co-creation of reminders and checklists.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and affirms the client's plan, though could more consistently amplify and link change talk to core values to build commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges ambivalence and uses double-sided reflections and feasible options to redirect toward change, but at times could validate sustain talk more fully before pivoting.
