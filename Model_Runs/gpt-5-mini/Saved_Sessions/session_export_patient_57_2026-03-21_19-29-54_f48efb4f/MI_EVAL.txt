# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, with explicit validations and affirmations that convey unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic reflections (including a clear reflection of Jamal’s bind) and supportive reassurance, but sometimes shifts quickly to solutions rather than deepening emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use in team contexts with concrete behavioral goals, rehearsal, and confidence checks that guide toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works as a team with Jamal by inviting choices and role-play and tailoring suggestions, but frequent clinician-generated scripts give the interaction an expert-driven feel at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, asking which strategies feel doable, checking readiness/confidence, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change language (desire/confidence) but relies heavily on clinician-supplied strategies and scripts rather than systematically drawing out and amplifying Jamal’s own reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—invites input and role-play and adapts to Jamal’s responses—but frequent directive suggestions and ready-made lines shift some control back to the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and shapes change talk using questions and a readiness ruler, producing explicit commitments, though it could better amplify Jamal’s own motivations with more evocative reflections and summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and the bind but uses limited strategic softening (few double-sided reflections or reframes) and often moves quickly to practical tactics rather than reframing sustain-language.
