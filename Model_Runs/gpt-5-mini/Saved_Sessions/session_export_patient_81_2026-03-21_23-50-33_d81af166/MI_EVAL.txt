# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence while affirming strengths. No shaming or blaming; warm, respectful stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening that mirrors Lukas’s language and social-emotional concerns. Clinician demonstrates strong perspective-taking and validation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior and guides the conversation to a measurable, time-limited experiment. Repeatedly returns to concrete steps, contingencies, and implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is an equal partnership with frequent solicitation of Lukas’s preferences and co-construction of messages and supports. Decisions are shaped by the patient rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves Lukas’s sense of choice and ownership by offering options and framing the plan as an experiment. Avoids pressuring language and reinforces agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits actionable motivation and practical commitments from Lukas, using his own words to build a plan. Could deepen elicitation of internal values and ask more evocative questions about confidence and long-term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician actively co-creates solutions, asks for input at each step, and shares power in planning. Rehearses delivery and negotiates supports with the patient’s preferences central.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively moves ambivalence toward a concrete experiment and elicits commitment language for specific steps. Could more frequently amplify intrinsic reasons for change and draw out confidence/ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and does not reinforce sustain talk, but mainly reflects sustain talk neutrally rather than using techniques to diminish it. Identity/belonging arguments remain under-challenged and could be countered with double-sided reflections or selective emphasis on change talk.
