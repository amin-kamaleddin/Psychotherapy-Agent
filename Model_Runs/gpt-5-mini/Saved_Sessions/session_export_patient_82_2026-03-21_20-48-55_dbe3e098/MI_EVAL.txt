### Empathy

**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections that captured both content and feeling, validating ambivalence and lowering defensiveness. Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and tracking of emotional shifts.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing/avoiding alcohol in contexts threatening athletics/grades with concrete steps and check-ins. Resisted drifting and moved toward actionable strategies and implementation planning.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Session felt clearly collaborative—solicited preferences, co-planned strategies, and offered to role-play only at the client's request. Followed the client's lead and sought feedback about how interventions felt.

### Autonomy Support

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change language using options, importance/confidence scaling, and rehearsal which raised confidence from 4 to 8. Built on the client's selections (rehearsal, ally, nonalcoholic drink) to strengthen commitment.
