# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed clear unconditional positive regard from the opening line and throughout, avoiding shaming and affirming the patient’s statements. The warm, nonjudgmental tone and consistent validation support a top rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening—summaries and validations accurately captured context and normal reactions. Could deepen empathic connection with more complex feeling reflections to invite emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session remained focused on alcohol use in social settings and protective goals, progressing from assessment to concrete strategies and roleplay. Roleplays and skills practice kept the conversation goal-oriented toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a genuine collaboration—patient was invited to set priorities and choose strategies, and the clinician adapted to requests. Co-created roleplays and repeated preference checks reinforced joint work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported the patient’s sense of choice by asking permission, offering options, and letting the patient choose which strategies to practice. Language avoided pressure and emphasized patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions and scaling to draw out the patient’s motivations and values, eliciting change-oriented statements. Could further deepen evocation by asking follow-ups to amplify change talk and elicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a genuine partnership stance—asked permission, offered options, and co-created roleplay scenarios with the patient. Power was shared and the patient’s preferences clearly guided the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk via importance/confidence scaling and linking behavior to goals, and provided aligned strategies. Opportunities remain to amplify and summarize the patient’s reasons for change and to elicit stronger ability/commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing social pressure and shifting to strategies, keeping momentum toward change. Could more explicitly use MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to reframe sustain talk when it arises.
