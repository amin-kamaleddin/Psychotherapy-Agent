# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard using affirmations and validating language; avoided criticism and conveyed unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, nuanced reflections that matched the patient's language and acknowledged ambivalence and feelings, inviting further exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol use with concrete, measurable goals and purposeful steering toward an action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as an equal partnership by soliciting the patient's ideas, inviting decisions, and co-constructing the plan at each step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized choice and pacing, offered options rather than directives, and reinforced the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations, strengths, and past successes and elicited concrete strategies, but could probe confidence and commitment more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used invitational, collaborative language and treated the patient as the expert, ensuring the plan reflected the patient's preferences and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited desire, reasons, and ability language and generated concrete change talk, but could better amplify and summarize those statements to build firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used neutral reflections and reframing to defuse sustain talk, though double-sided reflections and selective discrepancy could further weaken sustain reasons.
