# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, asking permission and honoring Marco’s preference to avoid lectures. Language remained nonjudgmental and affirming throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with concise paraphrases and validation of realism. Could deepen empathic connection by using more complex/affective reflections to explore the emotional weight behind statements like “I can’t see myself changing now.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking behavior and moved logically from exploration to a concrete plan and follow-up. Questions and reflections consistently returned to practical steps and obstacles.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was a true collaborative partnership—plans were co-constructed, preferences solicited, and clinician adjusted approach to Marco’s style. Language framed the clinician and patient as allies working together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly and consistently supported autonomy through permission-seeking, offering options, and leaving follow-up choices to Marco. Reinforced that answers and next steps were voluntary, promoting ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations and capacities (e.g., what he gets from smoking, triggers, confidence scaling) and elicited a feasible experiment. Could further probe intrinsic values and use selective reinforcement to chain change talk toward stronger commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered shared expertise and power-sharing by repeatedly asking permission and letting Marco choose tools, start date, and check-in method. The plan reflected Marco’s priorities and contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language, translated intent into a specific experiment (delay first cigarette), and used confidence scaling to problem-solve. Missed opportunities to amplify brief positive statements and ask evocative questions to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided arguing and rolled with resistance, honoring sustain talk while redirecting to pragmatic, nonthreatening steps. Could add more double-sided reflections to validate sustain reasons while gently pivoting toward change.
