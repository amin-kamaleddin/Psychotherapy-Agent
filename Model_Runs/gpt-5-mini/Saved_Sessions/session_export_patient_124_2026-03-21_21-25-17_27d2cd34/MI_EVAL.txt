# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental tone and repeated affirmations of Sofia’s choices. Avoided shaming or labeling throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening and accurate content reflections, acknowledging ambivalence and concerns. Could deepen emotional and meaning-focused reflections (e.g., family expectations, identity, anxiety drivers).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on marijuana use and moved systematically from exploration to concrete, behaviorally specific planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Sofia to choose strategies, rehearsed skills together, and co-created texts, reminders, and plan details. Decisions were jointly made and shaped by Sofia’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and affirming Sofia’s realistic goals and limits. Framed suggestions as choices and respected her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation with confidence rulers, choices, rehearsals, and commitment language, producing concrete steps. Could more frequently draw out values, reasons, and envisioned outcomes to amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative, non-expert stance and consistently invited Sofia’s input, co-constructing the plan and following her lead. Fostered genuine partnership in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and concrete commitments, but did not consistently amplify or deepen desire, reasons, need, or commitment with selective reflections or evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged both sides and avoided reinforcing sustain talk, but used few double-sided reflections, reframes, or strategic pivots to move sustain talk toward discrepancy. Further exploration of rationales for continued use was limited.
