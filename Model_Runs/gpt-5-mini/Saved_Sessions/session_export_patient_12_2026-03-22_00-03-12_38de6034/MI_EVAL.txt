# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth with normalizing statements and explicit validations; no blaming or shaming language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate, sustained reflective listening with complex reflections and frequent summaries that capture both content and feeling, mirroring ambivalence effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on small behavioral steps and a specific goal, but could more explicitly tie those micro-steps back to the overarching change target (e.g., reducing drug use or coping with cravings).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co-constructed—invites client input at each phase, affirms choices, and adapts suggestions to the client's preferences, creating an equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the client's choice and pace, avoids pressure, and frames steps as optional and normalizes doubt, supporting the client's sense of control and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client-generated ideas and small, client-led solutions, but could more often probe internal motivators, use scaling questions, and amplify signs of commitment to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert, uses collaborative language and co-creates steps, sharing power and letting the client's proposals direct the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and amplifies change talk and asks specific eliciting questions, but missed opportunities to deepen change talk by exploring personal reasons/values and more frequently reflecting desire/ability/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Accurately and nonjudgmentally acknowledges sustain talk but often repeats sustain statements verbatim, which can rehearse them; would benefit from double-sided reflections and reflections that foreground change.
