# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys nonjudgmental, supportive acceptance and normalizes/validates Elena's feelings while respecting her request not to be pushed. No blaming or correction of behavior was evident.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, compassionate reflective listening (e.g., naming chest tightening, fear) and checks how to respond to match Elena's emotional state. Responses show strong perspective-taking and emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the conversation focused on the pregnancy scare and condom use while prioritizing Elena's readiness, but often cycles through process options instead of advancing toward change planning. The cautious approach is appropriate for preserving rapport but limits strategic guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as joint problem-solving, co-constructing the agenda and inviting Elena's input on timing and style. Avoids an expert-driven stance and defers to Elena's preferences throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy with explicit choices about clinician behavior and clear assurances of no pressure; defers facts and plans until Elena asks. These repeated affirmations reinforce her decision-making authority.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits preferences about process (listening vs facts vs planning) but does not systematically probe internal motivations, importance, or confidence. Misses chances to deepen intrinsic reasons for avoiding pregnancy or to explore past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes working together as equals and offers choices about how to proceed, fostering shared decision-making. The clinician consistently invites Elena's preferences and timing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Missed multiple opportunities to elicit, amplify, or build on Elena's spontaneous change statements (relief, fear). The clinician accepted and processed change talk but rarely followed with evocative probes about importance, reasons, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and respects Elena's reluctance without confrontation, which prevents strengthening sustain talk. However, the clinician seldom used strategic double-sided reflections or reframes to gently tilt toward change.
