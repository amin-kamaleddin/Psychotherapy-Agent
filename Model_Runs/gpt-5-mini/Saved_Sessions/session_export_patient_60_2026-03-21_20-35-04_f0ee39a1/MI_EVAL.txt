# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with affirmations and praise; accepted the client's priorities (protecting his record) without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate summaries that validated ambivalence, though reflections were concise and focused more on behavior than deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on resolving the conduct incident and preventing a record, consistently guiding the session toward concrete steps and harm‑reduction options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly a joint problem‑solving effort—offered options, co‑wrote the statement, role‑played calls, and incorporated the patient's input into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly affirmed the patient's right to choose, offered options rather than directives, asked permission before coaching, and honored his preferences throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intentions and practical commitments (texts, witnesses, community service) but did not probe deeper intrinsic motivations or values behind change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative stance—asked what the patient wanted to focus on, offered choices, and co‑created scripts and plans reflecting shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced pragmatic, short‑term change talk (avoiding a record, concrete steps) but did not consistently amplify broader intrinsic reasons or long‑term goals for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and validated sustain talk appropriately, but made limited use of strategic double‑sided reflections or selective reinforcement to gently shift momentum toward change.
