# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently expressed nonjudgmental support, normalized slips, and used affirmations that conveyed unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There were several empathic reflections acknowledging shame and fear, but the clinician sometimes moved quickly into advice and missed opportunities for deeper, sustained reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with concrete behavioral targets and sequential next steps to guide progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interactions were framed as joint work—patient input shaped the agenda, clinician rehearsed language and regularly checked readiness, creating a cooperative process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician offered multiple options, invited the patient to choose next steps, and respected her decisions while reinforcing that slips don't erase progress.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited motivations (e.g., daughter, shame, desire to quit) and practical commitment, but used few evocative questions to probe values, importance, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician generally collaborated and invited choices, yet frequent detailed directives and technical recommendations sometimes shifted the role toward expert rather than co‑creator.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited and affirmed (triggers, quit plan, NRT), but the clinician could have amplified and followed up more to strengthen and deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician reframed slips nonshamingly and offered concrete responses, but often pivoted to technical solutions instead of using strategic reflections to explicitly contain sustain talk.
