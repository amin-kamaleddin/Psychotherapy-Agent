# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with early affirmations and nonjudgmental language, creating a safe space for honest disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking about ambivalence and practical constraints, but missed opportunities to probe deeper emotions (stress, guilt, fear) to strengthen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking reduction and protecting the child, progressively narrowing options to a concrete, on‑task behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Solicited the patient's ideas, checked feasibility, and co‑created decisions throughout, using language that reinforced genuine teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options, respected limits, and affirmed the patient's choice, supporting ownership of the plan without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out several self-generated strategies and concrete intentions, but could have further elicited values, past successes, and confidence to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert by repeatedly asking for her preferences and co‑designing the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited specific change talk and actionable weekly commitments, though missed chances to amplify intrinsic motives (importance, desire, commitment) beyond short‑term planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected minimizations toward practical steps, but could have used more double‑sided reflections and deeper exploration of sustain reasons (stress relief) to resolve ambivalence.
