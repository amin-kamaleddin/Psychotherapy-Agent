# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalized ambivalence and affirmed small steps. Language was nonjudgmental and avoided labeling, supporting a warm accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections and empathic summaries. The clinician frequently mirrored Margaret’s language and checked in, deepening rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol-related risk throughout the session. Questions and reflections repeatedly brought the conversation back to concrete goals and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative, with the clinician soliciting Margaret’s preferences and co-constructing the plan. Patient ideas were integrated rather than replaced by clinician solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Margaret’s choice and control, normalizing small tests and partial change. The clinician avoided directive language and preserved the patient’s sense of agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Margaret by eliciting her own reasons, strengths, and concrete strategies. Questions probed how she would notice progress and elicited internally generated change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership evident—clinician framed tasks as joint and invited Margaret’s input at each step. The interaction positioned both as contributors to the solution.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and amplified change talk with specific open questions and reinforcing reflections. This produced concrete change statements and linked them to valued outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and often reframed ambivalence to move toward change, validating sustain statements before pivoting. A missed opportunity was more frequent use of double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to further soften sustain talk.
