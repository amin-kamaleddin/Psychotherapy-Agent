# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with validating statements and warmth; avoided blaming language and normalized the patient's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated empathic tone and some reflections (e.g., 'That sounds frightening'), but missed opportunities to deepen reflections on ambivalence, isolation, and identity concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on preventing repeat DUI with actionable, goal-directed planning and minimal topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a partnership by soliciting preferences and co-creating scripts, though the clinician often led with concrete suggestions and scripted language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the patient's choices and framed suggestions as optional, asking permission and allowing the patient to select doable steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some behavioral commitments (choosing strategies) but relied more on offering options and scripts than on drawing out the patient's intrinsic motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—asked which strategies felt doable and offered to role-play or draft texts—but at times the clinician provided expert-driven solutions rather than eliciting the patient's own phrasing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced concrete change talk (e.g., stashing cab cash) and amplified commitments, but could further explore motives, importance, and confidence to deepen change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing or reinforcing minimizations but did not strategically use double-sided reflections or explicitly explore sustain talk to soften or resolve it before moving to solutions.
