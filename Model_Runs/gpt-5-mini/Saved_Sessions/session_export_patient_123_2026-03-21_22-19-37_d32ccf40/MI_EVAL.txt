### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power and decision-making—invites input, negotiates reminders/check-ins, and positions Sofia as the primary decision-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk, turning client ideas into concrete steps and securing modest commitments (planner, library hour, timer, check-in).
