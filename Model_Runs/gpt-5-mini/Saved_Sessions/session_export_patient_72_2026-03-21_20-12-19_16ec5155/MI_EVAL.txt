# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, respectful, and nonjudgmental; repeatedly affirms autonomy and the patient's progress throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and accurate summaries of pragmatic details, but reflections rarely probed deeper emotional nuances like body confidence or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on establishing an exercise routine and ties major questions to concrete steps, planning, and monitoring without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—solicits preferences, checks wording, and co-develops tools and plans with the patient, reflecting shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms patient choice and frames suggestions as optional, asks permission before offering help, and avoids pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits reasons, intentions, and concrete signs of progress, but could more strongly probe internal values and affect-driven motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert and involves them in decisions (e.g., friend choice, alarm times), fostering power-sharing and joint planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through targeted open questions, summaries, confidence scaling, and follow-up planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to solutions and backups, but uses few double-sided reflections and misses some opportunities to validate ambivalence before reframing.
