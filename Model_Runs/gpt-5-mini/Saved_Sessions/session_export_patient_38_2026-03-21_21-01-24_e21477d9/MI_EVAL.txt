# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing ambivalence and validating feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear empathic listening and accurate reflections capturing affect and ambivalence, though could use more complex reflections to deepen emotional nuance and validation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on contraception with a kept agenda and practical, behavior-linked strategies that guide toward harm-reduction actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner by offering options, co-constructing scripts, asking which to practice, and following the client's lead in decision-making and practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors the client's choices and readiness, offering alternatives and pragmatic steps without pressure while validating delay as acceptable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation and choices but relies more on information and coaching than systematic elicitation of the client's values, reasons, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative stance with shared decision-making, asking permission, and inviting the client to select agenda items and practice options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk around doable steps but misses sustained, strategic questions and prompts (e.g., scaling or values) to deepen and chain change talk toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids strengthening sustain talk and redirects to workable steps and roleplays, though could more explicitly use double-sided reflections or reframing to nudge toward change.
