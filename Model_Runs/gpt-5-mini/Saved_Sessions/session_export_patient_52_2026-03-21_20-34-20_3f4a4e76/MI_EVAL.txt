# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, warmth, and nonjudgmental language while affirming planning and normalizing struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening and accurate reflections that validated emotions and barriers; could use more frequent complex reflections to deepen emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing nightly alcohol use with concrete planning and barrier-focused problem-solving while keeping the client engaged.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solicited the client's ideas and co-created a plan, inviting preferences and check-ins. At times the clinician offered many options quickly, which reduced space for client-generated solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by framing suggestions as options, repeatedly asking for preferences, and offering nonjudgmental fallback plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation via scaling, values, and asking what would raise confidence, producing concrete change statements; could more consistently deepen intrinsic motivation and elicit explicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making and positioned the client as the primary agent. Could strengthen partnership by eliciting past attempts and client expertise before offering multiple clinician-generated options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with scaling, menus, and affirmations, which led to concrete plans. Would benefit from more systematic amplification, summarizing of change language, and direct elicitation of commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided argumentation and acknowledged the comfort alcohol provides, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and at times used dichotomous phrasing that could feel pressuring.
