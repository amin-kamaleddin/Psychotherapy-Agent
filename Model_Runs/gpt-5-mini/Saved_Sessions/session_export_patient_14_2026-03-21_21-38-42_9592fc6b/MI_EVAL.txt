# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard, validates frustration, and respects Andrei's boundaries and time limits. Warm, nonjudgmental stance and pragmatic affirmations sustain acceptance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that kept the client engaged and focused on realistic steps. Could add deeper affective reflections to explore the emotional significance of weight and frustration more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on a single, practical behavior change with concrete follow-up and measurable plans. Keeps the conversation on actionable steps without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session functions as mutual problem-solving with negotiated plans, alarms, and check-in format that reflect client preferences. Occasionally offers ready-made phrasing which is helpful but slightly more clinician-led than purely co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice by asking permission, offering options, and honoring the client's limits and preferences. Avoids pressure and reinforces the client's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical commitment and confidence (readiness/confidence rulers) but stays at logistics rather than exploring deeper values or intrinsic reasons for change. More values-oriented questions would strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys collaboration and defers to the client's expertise about daily life, inviting choices and preferences. Could more consistently elicit the client's ideas before offering suggestions to further shift agenda-setting power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with readiness/confidence rulers and summaries of commitments, consolidating the plan. Missed opportunities to deepen desire and importance with follow-up questions that would amplify change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward doable, small steps with negotiated backups and reframing of barriers. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections to further acknowledge ambivalence while leaning toward change.
