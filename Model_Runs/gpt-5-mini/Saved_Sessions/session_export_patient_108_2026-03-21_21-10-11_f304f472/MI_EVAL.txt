# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalized ambivalence, and respected the client's choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking repeatedly, asking targeted empathic questions that deepened understanding of the client's inner experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused aim on setting boundaries and moving from feelings to concrete actions, yielding an actionable plan and written message.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the work as joint problem-solving, negotiated decisions, coached in role-play on request, and co-constructed the final message with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, asked permission before interventions, and reinforced the client's ownership of tone, wording, and next steps without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation and reasons for change but relied more on offering scripts than drawing out deeper personal values, confidence, and commitment statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited the client's preferences, sought permission, and honored edits and choices, making the session highly collaborative and client-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change language (timeline, scripts, written plan) but missed opportunities to systematically deepen and amplify the client's own reasons and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated and redirected sustain talk with normalizing language and practical alternatives, though could have used more double-sided reflections and discrepancy work to transform guilt and loyalty language.
