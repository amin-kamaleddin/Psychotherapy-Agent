# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used a nonjudgmental, supportive tone and affirmed practical ideas, but some phrasing felt formulaic and there were few explicit normalizing statements or deeper individualized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and captured ambivalence well, though reflections were somewhat repetitive and missed opportunities to deepen emotion-focused understanding (e.g., defensiveness, tentative language).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing risky drinking and effectively moved from elicitation to concrete, actionable planning without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in genuine teamwork with inclusive language and co-constructed decisions, consistently seeking the patient's input and avoiding an expert/prescriptive stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and agency by offering options and asking the patient to select and shape strategies rather than prescribing actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's own motivations and solutions through open, specific questions and used the patient's language to elicit desire, ability, and planning statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveyed partnership by inviting the patient to choose options, shape wording, and map out plans together, demonstrating power-sharing and respect for the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively and skillfully evoked and amplified change talk with targeted prompts that produced concrete commitment-oriented plans and specific steps to try.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and used constructive redirection, but rarely employed techniques like double-sided reflections or explicit normalization to more directly soften sustain talk.
