# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth, validated Nadine's effort and distress, and avoided blame while normalizing infant patterns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displayed clear perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening, asking about emotions and validating exhaustion; could use more complex reflections to deepen validation of guilt and fear of judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on feeding, diaper counts, weight checks, and maternal sleep while balancing empathy with concrete next steps (scripts, safety thresholds, role‑play).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a true collaborative exchange by inviting input, co‑authoring scripts, and negotiating next steps rather than adopting an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice and control by offering options, asking permission before interventions, and avoiding pressure, which supported Nadine's decision‑making authority.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out some motivations and emotions and offered actionable options, but could more systematically elicit Nadine's personal reasons, values, and confidence (e.g., scaling importance/confidence).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine power‑sharing through repeated preference checks, co‑creation of scripts, and role‑play, making decisions a collaborative process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged action‑oriented talk with scripts and role‑play that increased readiness to act; could more consistently elicit personal reasons, importance, confidence, and an explicit commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing struggle, reframing concerns, and providing safety thresholds; could employ double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to further tip ambivalence toward change.
