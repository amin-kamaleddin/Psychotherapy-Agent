**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete preparatory change talk and small steps but does not consistently amplify desire/importance/confidence or seek an explicit commitment. Asking scaling or commitment questions would help convert preparatory talk into firmer intention.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes doubts while gently redirecting toward change, preventing escalation of sustain talk. Could be more strategic by exploring costs of the status quo and using explicit double-sided reflections or reframes.
