# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with multiple affirmations and nonjudgmental language, validating the patient's planning and values throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good reflective listening and summaries that show understanding of content, but empathic reflections rarely explored deeper emotions or affective nuances to increase depth and accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on restarting exercise and moves the conversation to specific, measurable actions, contingency plans, and follow‑through supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted as a joint enterprise—invites input, checks preferences, co‑creates scripts, and offers options that position the patient as an equal partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice and offers options rather than directives, reinforcing the patient's control over tactics and commitment without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's reasons, motivation, and concrete plans, but at times supplies polished wording rather than drawing more of the client's own phrasing and using amplification to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership by co‑constructing solutions, checking what the patient wants to do, and shaping the session around the patient's preferences and requests.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with concrete planning and a confidence rating, though could better amplify and sequence statements into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers and redirects to problem‑solving with backups and scripts, but seldom uses double‑sided reflections that explicitly validate sustain talk while pivoting toward change.
