# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses a nonjudgmental, respectful tone and normalizes the patient's situation while offering choices; could deepen personalized affirmations beyond pragmatic, procedural statements.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and adapts to the patient's practical style, but reflections remain surface-level and rarely probe emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on lowering blood pressure via addressing medication and narrows the conversation toward an actionable plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a genuine collaborative exchange by inviting the patient's preferences on format and pace and shaping the session around those choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, avoids pressure, and explicitly reinforces the patient's right to choose, supporting self-directed decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivation and preparatory commitment around bringing up meds but misses opportunities to draw out deeper values, importance, and links to alcohol use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as a joint enterprise with clear power-sharing—patient preferences repeatedly determine the next steps and methods used.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk about bringing up medication and rehearsal, but does not deepen change talk about other behaviors like alcohol use or elicit importance/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Frequently accepts sustain talk about evening drinking without using strategic or double-sided reflections to soften it or evoke discrepancy, missing chances to redirect toward change.
