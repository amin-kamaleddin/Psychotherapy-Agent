# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard and a consistently nonjudgmental tone, honoring the patient’s brevity requests and accepting the decision not to quit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, timely reflections that capture both behavior and underlying uncertainty, matching tone and wording to the patient’s expressed preferences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on mini‑cigar use and moves the visit logically toward actionable harm‑reduction goals and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducts a genuine collaborative exchange by soliciting the patient’s expertise, incorporating preferences, and co‑creating the checklist and follow‑up plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and control, offering options, asking what feels doable, and respecting the patient’s decision and format preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits motivation from the patient using evocative questions, a confidence ruler, and requests for concrete commitments, then anchors that language into a plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner—asking permission, offering choices, and letting the patient’s wording and preferences shape the plan and follow‑up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and builds change talk by exploring pros/cons, offering options, eliciting doable steps, and securing a specific limit and confidence rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids argument and mirrors minimization, but missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections or earlier selective amplification of change language to more strongly shift sustain talk.
