If he says no or sleeps through, small practical plans: place supplies and a checklist by the bassinet, set two alarms (his and yours), or plan a short nap swap with him earlier. For settling without full rocking: swaddle, dim lights, “drowsy-but-awake” put-downs, gentle shush/pat, or pick-up–put-down. Which backup and settling option would you like to try first?
