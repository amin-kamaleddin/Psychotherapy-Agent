# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, using reflective summaries and normalizing statements that acknowledged discouragement without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that matched Rafael's hesitant style and maintained contact with his emotional state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on returning to the gym throughout the session and progressively narrowed interventions to a concrete, time-limited plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, checked Rafael's preferences, and integrated his selections into a shared plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed Rafael's choice, offered multiple options, and avoided pressuring language while inviting him to choose what felt doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons for change and actionable steps but missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivation and amplify change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Rafael as the expert on his life and co-constructed the plan by repeatedly asking him to choose among options and incorporating his decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and shaped change-oriented statements and small-step commitments, but could have amplified and expanded expressions like 'I want more energy' to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reframed and normalized doubts and redirected toward solutions without confrontation; using more double-sided reflections could have further weakened sustain talk.
