# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, normalized disclosures, and expressed appreciation for honesty, creating a safe space for candid conversation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and some reflective listening linked alcohol to the patient's goals (sleep, training, injuries), but reflections were more pragmatic than emotional and missed deeper ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use with logical progression from assessment to feedback to a concrete plan tied to the patient's marathon training.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician acted as an equal partner, soliciting preferences and co-constructing the plan (template, days, reminders) while letting the patient's choices drive the content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multiple options were offered, permission was asked before setting specifics, and suggestions were framed as choices, reinforcing the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively drew out motivations and concrete change steps by linking to values and offering choices, but could have probed intrinsic motivators and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction reflected power-sharing and co-creation of the plan, with the clinician inviting input, practice, and wording for social situations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and shaped specific change language and commitments (tracking, alcohol-free days, caps), but could have amplified and summarized the patient's change talk and used scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and was redirected toward goals and concrete effects, though more double-sided reflections could have further softened resistance.
