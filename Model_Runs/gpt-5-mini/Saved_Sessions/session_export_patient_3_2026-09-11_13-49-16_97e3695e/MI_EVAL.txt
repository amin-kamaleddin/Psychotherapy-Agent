# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating small wins and avoiding blame. Tone and language remain respectful and encouraging throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both wanting and limits. Could deepen affect by probing what 'gross' feels like and using more complex or amplified reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on starting after-dinner walks with all questions and reflections tied to that target behavior. Progresses from idea to a specific time-linked plan and follow-up without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted as an equal partnership with the clinician soliciting and integrating the patient’s ideas into the plan. Co-designs accountability methods and experiments together, indicating strong collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports the patient’s sense of choice by offering options and avoiding prescriptive language. Accountability is framed as the patient’s preference, reinforcing control and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from within the patient by eliciting intrinsic reasons (regaining confidence, feeling less 'gross') and uses those motives to shape concrete steps. Consistently elicits and develops internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as collaborative and defers to the patient’s expertise, asking which options feel realistic. The plan is clearly co-constructed with the patient choosing specific steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, names, and amplifies change talk, asking for small realistic steps and specific commitments. Solidifies a concrete plan and elicits signs of progress to celebrate, reinforcing change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges worries (tiredness, talking oneself out) without amplifying them and redirects toward practical solutions. Could further soften sustain talk with more double-sided reflections and explicit normalization of ambivalence.
