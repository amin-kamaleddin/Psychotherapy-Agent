# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained consistent unconditional positive regard—warm, nonjudgmental language, validation, and affirmation of autonomy throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries captured Matteo's perspective and helped him recognize patterns, but could have used more complex reflections to deepen emotional nuance and ambivalence before shifting to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on alcohol use and its relevance to the ankle recovery, progressing clearly from assessment to a concrete, goal-directed plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative—clinician solicited Matteo's preferences, co-constructed scripts and supports, and checked back frequently to ensure fit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently offered options, asked permission, and reinforced Matteo's control over choices and pacing, supporting ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions, rulers, and elicitation to draw out motivation and concrete options, but could have explored deeper values, reasons, and past successes before introducing solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician positioned Matteo as the expert and partner, inviting input on focus, wording, and next steps and sharing decision-making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited change talk with open questions, a 0–10 ruler, and specific small-step planning, but missed chances to amplify and elaborate emerging change language and probe longer-term reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and normalized ambivalence, but often moved quickly to advice and strategies instead of using double-sided or complex reflections to explicitly soften sustain talk.
