PSYCHIATRIST: Citalopram (an SSRI) commonly causes nausea, dry mouth, sleep changes, sweating, headache, tremor, constipation and sexual side effects. Serious risks include serotonin syndrome (agitation, fever, stiffness), QT prolongation (dose‑dependent — lower max in older adults), hyponatremia, bleeding risk, and withdrawal symptoms.

Do NOT combine with MAOIs (phenelzine)—wait at least 14 days after stopping an MAOI before starting citalopram (and vice‑versa). Avoid other serotonergic drugs (triptans, tramadol, St. John's wort, linezolid), pimozide, strong CYP inhibitors, and other QT‑prolonging meds. Talk with your prescriber; seek urgent care for high fever, muscle rigidity, rapid heartbeat, fainting, or severe bleeding.
