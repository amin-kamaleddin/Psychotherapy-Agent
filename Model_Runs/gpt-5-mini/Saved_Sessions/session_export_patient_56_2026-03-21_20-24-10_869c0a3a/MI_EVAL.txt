# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout, affirming the client's priorities and normalizing their preference for small actions. Language consistently respected the client's values and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured content and some affect, with good perspective-taking and check-ins. Could deepen affective reflection by exploring emotional nuances (e.g., why family happiness is especially salient now).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on aligning daily choices with family happiness and narrowed to a concrete, measurable plan. Clinician balanced open exploration with steering toward implementation details and contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative, offering options while inviting client input and preferences. The clinician used role-play and rehearsal to co-construct the plan with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly supported client choice, offered options rather than directives, and adapted recommendations to the client's request for simple actions. Language reinforced the client's control over decisions and plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician drew out the client's own reasons, examples, and solutions rather than giving prescriptive advice. Questions elicited intrinsic motivation and a concrete plan from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician framed the work as co-created, asking permission to brainstorm and inviting selection of options and scripts. The clinician honored the client's lead and preferences throughout planning and practice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and amplified change talk by asking for specifics, narrowing to doable actions, and soliciting commitment and implementation details. The client articulated reasons, a plan, and a high confidence rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician minimized sustain talk by neutrally exploring barriers and quickly pivoting to collaborative problem-solving and protective rules. Scaling confidence and asking what would raise it further redirected potential sustain talk into planning.
