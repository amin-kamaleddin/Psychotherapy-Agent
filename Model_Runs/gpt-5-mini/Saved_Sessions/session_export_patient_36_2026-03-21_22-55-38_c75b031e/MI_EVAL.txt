### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk through open, change-focused questions and selective reflections, then converts desire into concrete planning. Consistently invites articulation of benefits, past successes, and realistic steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing doubt, using double-sided reflections, and redirecting to supports and backups. Does not argue or dwell on reasons to maintain the status quo, preserving forward momentum.
