# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating concerns and honoring choices (e.g., accepting patient’s wish to send the message herself). No shaming or corrective statements were used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent empathic listening with concise paraphrases that captured both content and feeling (e.g., 'conflicting priorities'), and used those cues to shape feasible options.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on a single target behavior and steered the session toward concrete planning (one‑week trial, dosing, PCP wording) without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative and co‑creative, offering options and inviting the patient to shape the plan and exact language while respecting her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by emphasizing reversibility, presenting options, and accepting the patient’s decision to handle PCP contact herself without pushback.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited pragmatic motivations and a small behavioral commitment (7‑day trial) but did not deeply explore intrinsic reasons, values, or use scaling to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert in her life, repeatedly offered choices and invited co‑construction of the message, demonstrating strong power‑sharing and respect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evoked and summarized incremental change talk and commitments, but missed opportunities to probe importance/confidence or link the plan to personal benefits and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and accommodated concerns with a reversible trial and clear messaging, though more targeted techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to explicitly soften sustain talk were underused.
