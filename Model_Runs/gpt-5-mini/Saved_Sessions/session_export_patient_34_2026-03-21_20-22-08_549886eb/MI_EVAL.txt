# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating Dylan’s perspective and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking throughout, paraphrasing Dylan’s feelings and concerns in a way that matched his emotional tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on the conduct referral and related tasks (incident report, statement drafting, risk mitigation) without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited Dylan to co-create the statement and asked about preferences, creating a joint effort while at times shifting into an advisory/scripted role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed Dylan’s control and offered clear options, respecting his choices (e.g., refusing to apologize) and letting him select next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Asked a few open questions about goals but quickly moved to procedural problem-solving, failing to elicit or deepen Dylan’s internal motivations or discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance by offering options and soliciting input, though early directive advice and specific scripting reduced fully client-led decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts to evoke change talk and elicited a tentative plan (e.g., avoid big parties before finals) but did not amplify or explore that language further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Mostly accepted Dylan’s sustain talk and provided tactical support that risked reinforcing denial rather than using double-sided or strategic reflections to soften sustain language.
