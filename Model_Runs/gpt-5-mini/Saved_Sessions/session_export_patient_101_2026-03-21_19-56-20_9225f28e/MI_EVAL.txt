# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with validating language and reinforcement of efforts, conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening with validating paraphrases and normalization, but reflections are pragmatic and there are missed opportunities to explore deeper emotional texture of anxiety and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol and improving contraceptive reliability, moving logically from assessment to SMART goals and concrete follow‑up steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is genuinely collaborative—frequently invites the client's input, offers choices, rehearses language, and tailors plans based on the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally supports choice by asking permission and offering options, but at times offers many unsolicited practical steps early, which risks slipping toward advising rather than eliciting the client's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out motivations and solutions and elicits a plan, but often supplies ready‑made suggestions instead of systematically eliciting the client's internal reasons, strengths, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong power‑sharing and co‑creation of the plan, repeatedly deferring to the client's choices and framing interventions as joint decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and builds on change talk and reinforces small wins while co‑constructing concrete steps, but could more actively amplify desire/ability/reasons/need language and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk and moves toward planning, but does little active softening (e.g., double‑sided reflections or exploring costs of staying the same) to resolve ambivalence.
