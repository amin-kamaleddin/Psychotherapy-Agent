# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with affirmations and nonjudgmental language, respecting Oliver's values and dilemma. No shaming or moralizing; options are offered respectfully.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflections that capture ambivalence and emotional weight, and checks in about how the client wants to be supported. Reflections match the client's language and affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on asserting boundaries and producing a concrete plan (one‑liner, date, role‑play, follow‑up text). Interventions consistently move toward actionable steps and resist drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative and egalitarian, with the clinician soliciting preferences and negotiating their role. Decisions and scripting are co‑created rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy by offering multiple options, asking what support the client wants, and confirming the client's control over wording and firmness. The clinician refrains from pressuring and reinforces ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently draws out the client's own motives, plans, and preferred wording rather than imposing rationale. Elicited motives are used to build rehearsal and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a true partnership by asking for preferences, offering scripting options, and inviting co‑construction of role‑play rules. Power‑sharing is evident as the client chooses the firm text and support style.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely elicits and strengthens change talk, inviting concrete planning and preference (date, wording, role‑play). The clinician amplifies client intent and moves him toward specific commitment and practice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively validates both sides and redirects to planning, avoiding reinforcement of sustain talk, but misses some strategic techniques. Could more often use double‑sided reflections or explicitly explore costs of the status quo to further diminish sustain talk.
