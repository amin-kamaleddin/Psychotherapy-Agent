### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, with frequent validation and respectful language that kept the patient engaged and supported.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking captured ambivalence and barriers, helping the patient soften and elaborate his concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol to improve diabetes/sleep and moved the session toward a concrete, time-bound plan with tracking and a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt clearly collaborative—options were proposed, wording and supports were negotiated, and the patient’s preferences guided the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered choices at every decision point, invited patient ownership, and avoided pressure while honoring the request for a nonjudgmental tone.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons, confidence, barriers, and coping strategies using scaling, but missed deeper probes into intrinsic values, personal meaning, and past successes to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing by repeatedly asking the patient to choose among options and co-constructing messages and check-in content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with questions, affirmations, and scaling, though could more actively amplify and summarize the patient’s change language to boost commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk to practical steps and supports, but could use more double-sided reflections or reframing to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
