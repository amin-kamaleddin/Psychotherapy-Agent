### Empathy

**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking with accurate layered reflections and timely paraphrases that deepened rapport and checked back for accuracy.

### Direction

**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on the target decision (starting citalopram safely with phenelzine) and moved toward concrete next steps like drafting a safety checklist and one-line script.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—solicited the patient’s priorities, co-designed materials (checklist, script), and framed the encounter as joint problem-solving rather than top-down instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by offering neutral options, inviting preferences, and explicitly reassuring that there was no wrong choice, reinforcing patient control over the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated true partnership through repeated offers of choice, checking alignment, and tailoring the plan to the patient’s expressed needs and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited pragmatic change-related language and facilitators for decision-making, but would benefit from more active amplification and reinforcement of the patient’s change talk and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
