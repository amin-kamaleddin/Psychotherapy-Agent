# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental language and affirmations, validating Hana's struggle and avoiding labels. Warmth and respect are maintained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with paraphrases and validations, but misses some opportunities for deeper affect-focused reflections (e.g., exploring shame/embarrassment). Empathic presence is reliable though could probe emotional nuance more often.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on behavior change with each intervention tied to concrete next steps like reminders, freeze scripts, and rehearsals. Keeps the session moving toward measurable action rather than drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative with co-constructed plans, joint drafting of messages, and repeated solicitation of Hana's preferences. Clinician treats Hana as an equal partner whose ideas shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and affirming Hana's decisions. Avoids pressuring language and reinforces Hana's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Hana's internal motivations and resources through values exploration, scaling, and increase-by-one techniques. Focuses on drawing out Hana's reasons, confidence, and plans rather than imposing external motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership by inviting input, offering options, and co-creating solutions (e.g., choosing text wording, rehearsal plan). Power-sharing is substantive and sustained throughout the encounter.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and reinforces change talk with targeted questions, affirmation of successes, and use of scaling/increase-by-one to deepen commitment. Repeatedly draws out desire, ability, and concrete steps for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirects sustain talk toward coping strategies and rehearsal but rarely uses double-sided reflections or explicit normalization of ambivalence. Moves quickly to planning which reduces sustain talk but misses some opportunities to gently summarize both sides.
