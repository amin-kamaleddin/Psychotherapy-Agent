# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys respect and nonjudgmental regard with validating statements and affirmations, but the tone sometimes shifts to brisk, didactic information-delivery rather than deeply individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and timely validations of feelings and safety, but missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflection on embarrassment and peer identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on early alcohol exposure, safety, and refusal skills throughout the session with no drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction feels like co-working with frequent checks, invitations to choose lines, and building on Anya's contributions, creating strong interactional equality.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and framing advice as suggestions, reinforcing Anya's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivations and invites practice, but often moves quickly to offering scripts instead of probing deeper personal values, motives, or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with permission-seeking and role-play, though parts of the encounter become more instructional with long lists rather than co-constructed solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and reinforces change-oriented language and practices, but relies more on tools and advice than systematically drawing out the client's own motives or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shifts quickly to safety and harm-reduction to avoid strengthening sustain talk, though occasional persuasive reassurance verges on justification rather than strategic reflection.
