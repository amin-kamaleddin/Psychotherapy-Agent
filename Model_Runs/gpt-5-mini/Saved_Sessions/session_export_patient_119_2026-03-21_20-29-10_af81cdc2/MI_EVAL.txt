# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Begins nonjudgmentally and maintains a respectful, normalizing tone while affirming the client's constraints. Could occasionally add more explicit strength-based affirmations (e.g., naming resilience).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with timely, complex reflections and double-sided summaries that validate ambivalence. Could sometimes deepen emotional reflection (e.g., mirror anxiety or frustration) but empathy is consistently strong.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, steady focus on medication adherence, linking questions and reflections to barriers, past successes, and a measurable plan. Balances following the client with steering toward concrete action effectively.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the client as an equal problem-solver, co-designing contingencies and inviting preferences throughout the session. Language and planning consistently reflect a collaborative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, eliciting ratings, and checking willingness before finalizing plans. This approach fosters ownership and control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits motivation from the client via scaling questions, evocative prompts, and by linking values (family, health) to concrete strategies. Draws out client-generated solutions and tracks shifts in motivation numerically.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative, repeatedly inviting the client's expertise and choices while co-constructing the plan. Could occasionally name the client's expertise explicitly, but partnership is well enacted.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, amplifies, and builds on change talk using open questions, reflections, and by translating talk into specific action steps. Tracks and reinforces numerical movement in importance/confidence effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids argument and uses double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while pivoting to planning. Missed a few opportunities to more directly highlight discrepancy or reflect costs/consequences to further reduce sustain talk.
