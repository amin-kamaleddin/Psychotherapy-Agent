#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change language and asked for doable options, helping Jamal articulate specific steps. Missed opportunities include amplifying reasons for change and probing importance/confidence to build firmer commitment.

**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward supports that reduced perceived risk, such as coach backup and role‑play. Could more proactively use double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly soften sustain language and contrast both sides of the ambivalence.
