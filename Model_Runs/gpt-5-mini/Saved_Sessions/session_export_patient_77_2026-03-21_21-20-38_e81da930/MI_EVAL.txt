# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—affirms efforts, honors boundaries (no parent notification), and normalizes setbacks, creating warmth and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking that validate frustration and loneliness, though could pursue deeper affective content (e.g., intensity of shame/isolation) with more complex empathic reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, specific behavioral focus on increasing run/walk activity and simple tracking, and ends with concrete, actionable follow-up arrangements.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative—the clinician offers options, the client tweaks the plan, and practical supports are offered while respecting the client's preferences, reinforcing equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, honoring boundaries about parental notification, and framing missed sessions with self-compassion to preserve agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws on the client's past successes and preferences to elicit motivation, but could deepen evocation by eliciting values, asking how change would affect life, or using rulers to surface commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates shared decision-making throughout—the plan is co-created, adapted to the client's suggestions, and the clinician agrees to relay only what the client wants, showing strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and builds on change talk by asking what matters and offering small plans tied to prior success, though more amplification and prompts for commitment intensity would strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and reframes toward self-compassion with practical if‑then fallbacks that reduce all‑or‑nothing thinking, while more double-sided reflections could further weaken sustain talk.
