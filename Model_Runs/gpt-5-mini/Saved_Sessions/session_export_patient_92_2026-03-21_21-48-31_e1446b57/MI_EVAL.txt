# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental acceptance and respect, normalizing slips and affirming Marek’s perspective throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective‑taking with accurate paraphrases of Marek’s concerns, though reflections were mostly content‑level and could deepen emotional/identity reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on weekend drinking and progressed to a concrete, time‑limited harm‑reduction experiment with observable outcomes and a scheduled follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: the clinician invited Marek’s input at every step, integrated his ideas, and co‑constructed a shared plan for change.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported autonomy by asking permission, offering choices rather than directives, and framing experiments as optional information gathering.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out motives and reasons (likes about nights out, change criteria, confidence ruler), but could more explicitly link values and amplify change statements to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Marek as the expert on his life, shared power in decision‑making, and repeatedly sought his preferences to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with options and a confidence ruler and got a concrete experiment, though there were missed opportunities to amplify and deepen Marek’s change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and normalized sustain talk, using pivots toward harm‑reduction; could add more double‑sided reflections to more artfully soften sustain statements.
