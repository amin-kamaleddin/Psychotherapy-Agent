# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validation, honors the patient's fear and treats requests as legitimate without blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely summaries that accurately name the patient's feelings and their impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, concrete focus on addressing abdominal pain with actionable steps (diary, walks, draft note, rehearsal) and avoids tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as joint problem-solving, solicits input on wording and options, and offers to rehearse or accompany the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy by offering choices at each decision point, asking what feels most doable, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits motivations and doable steps with open questions, but could deepen evocation by exploring values, long-term goals, and confidence/commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a genuine partnership stance, sharing decision-making and placing lived-experience expertise with the patient when shaping the plan and note.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change talk by inviting small, doable steps and choices, but misses opportunities to amplify commitment, ask scaling questions, or explore past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and balances biomedical concerns with self-monitoring, though could use more explicit double-sided reflections and selectively amplify change language.
