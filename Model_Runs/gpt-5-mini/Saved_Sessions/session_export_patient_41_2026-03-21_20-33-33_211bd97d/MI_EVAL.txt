# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validated difficult emotions and responded sensitively to shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided multiple accurate reflections capturing the problem, consequences, and emotional tone; could deepen by exploring the nuance of shame and using more complex feeling+meaning reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with pragmatic, specific planning (10-minute plan, if‑then statement, reminders).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by soliciting preferences, drafting messages together, and building the plan from the patient's input rather than an expert-only stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy through offering choices, invitational language, and repeatedly emphasizing the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited change-oriented ideas and commitment signals with open questions and scaling, but could probe more for intrinsic motives or personal values linking the change to what matters to the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated true partnership by asking permission to share ideas, offering options and tweaks, and co-constructing concrete steps with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, used scaling and co-created a concrete experiment; could more often reflect and amplify tentative change statements into stronger language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to small experiments and planning, though could use double-sided reflections and more exploration of fear-of-failure to further neutralize sustain talk.
