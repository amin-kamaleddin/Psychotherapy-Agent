# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows consistent unconditional positive regard with affirmations, validations, and normalization of setbacks; no judgmental language was used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing ambivalence and tying emotions to practical concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on a sustainable, non‑diet approach and consistently steers toward concrete, proximal actions linked to the patient's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician co‑creates the plan, offers options then quickly elicits the patient's preferences, and integrates her choices into the next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and control by offering neutral options, validating ambivalence, and emphasizing the patient's preferences without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations, values, and small commitments, but could more consistently deepen and sequence evocation to elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as collaborative and privileges the patient's expertise about what is doable, avoiding prescriptive advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly elicits and supports change talk and specific goals, though could more often reflect, amplify, and summarize change language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and holds ambivalence with double‑sided reflections and reframing, but could more strategically contrast costs of staying the same versus benefits of change and invite elaboration on reasons against change.
