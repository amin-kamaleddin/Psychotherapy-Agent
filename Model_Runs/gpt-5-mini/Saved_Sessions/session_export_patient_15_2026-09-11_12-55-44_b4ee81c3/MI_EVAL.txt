# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through affirmations and nonjudgmental language, normalizing limits and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking by paraphrasing feelings, goals, supports, and ambivalence throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused direction toward increasing physical activity with a measurable plan, but largely avoids addressing alcohol as a relevant co-target for blood pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is genuinely co-constructed—clinician elicits patient input, negotiates options, summarizes, and checks for agreement at each step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, normalizing realistic limits, and avoiding prescriptive or coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out intrinsic motives and concrete reasons for change, prompting the patient to articulate multiple strands of change talk and goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on his life—offers options, asks for his judgment, and shares decision-making in plan development.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reflects, and amplifies the patient's own change language, moving from general motivation to specific, doable steps and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward achievable behaviors, but misses an opportunity to gently explore or reframe the patient's minimization of alcohol use.
