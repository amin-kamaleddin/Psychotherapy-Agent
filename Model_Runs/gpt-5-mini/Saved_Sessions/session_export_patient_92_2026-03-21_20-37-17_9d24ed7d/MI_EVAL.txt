# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Empathy

**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking that matched tone and content, though more affective or complex reflections could deepen emotional resonance.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol risk reduction with concrete behavioral goals and a progressive, nonprescriptive plan leading to a real-world test.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—invited priorities, co-created plans, and repeatedly solicited Marek's preferences, sharing expertise while maintaining equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Marek's ideas and produced a concrete trial, but could have probed deeper for internal motives, values, and used scaling or amplifying reflections to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively created and responded to change talk, eliciting specific doable behaviors and a plan, but could amplify intrinsic reasons and assess importance/confidence to bolster commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided argument and normalized ambivalence appropriately, but did not consistently use targeted MI strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or exploring costs) to actively soften sustain talk.
