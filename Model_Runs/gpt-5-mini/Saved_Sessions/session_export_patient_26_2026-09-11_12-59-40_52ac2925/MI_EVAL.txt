### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth, using normalizing statements and no‑pressure language. Validated Daniela's mixed feelings and choices throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the immediate target behavior (securing childcare and making a concrete ask) and consistently tied discussion to actionable next steps. Questions and suggestions were purposefully aimed at moving from contemplation to preparation and action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co‑constructed solutions with Daniela—drafting messages, offering role‑play, and negotiating reminders—while checking preferences. The clinician responded to the client's input and shared power in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently presented options, used permissive language, and deferred final decisions to Daniela, reinforcing her ownership of the plan. Offered help without coercion and asked which choices felt most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited Daniela's own reasons and plans by asking what felt doable and prompting concrete steps, effectively strengthening internal motivation. Built on her values implicitly by focusing on workable childcare solutions tied to her goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a shared effort and repeatedly invited Daniela's input and choices. Offered to do tasks together (drafting, role‑play, reminders) while deferring to her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicited and amplified change language, scaffolded action with concrete supports (drafting, role‑play, reminders), and tightened intentions into specific next steps. Prompted commitments about timing and follow‑through.

**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used multiple closers to reduce pressure, but missed opportunities to explicitly reflect and reframe specific barriers (financial concerns, distrust of family).
