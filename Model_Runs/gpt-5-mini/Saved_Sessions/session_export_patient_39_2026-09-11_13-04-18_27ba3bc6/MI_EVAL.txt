**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, affirming Elias's choices and struggles without judgment. The clinician's respectful tone and validations reinforced safety and acceptance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, affect-centered reflections captured both content and feeling, making Elias feel understood. These reflections encouraged disclosure and deepened rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, specific focus on increasing safe, brief physical activity and moved the conversation toward a concrete plan. Questions and summaries consistently tied back to actionable details.

### Collaboration
