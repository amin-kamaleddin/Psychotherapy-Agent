# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and created a nonjudgmental atmosphere (inviting perspective, normalizing ambivalence). Could occasionally add explicit normalization of repeated attempts/relapse.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, acknowledging ambivalence and specific worries. Reflections tended to summarize content rather than use more affect‑focused or complex reflections to deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on phone use and guided the client to a concrete, time‑limited behavioral experiment with measurable outcomes. Excellent forward momentum from ambivalence to a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt collaboratively co‑constructed, with frequent solicitation of client input and adaptation of language to client preferences. The clinician consistently invited choices and follow‑up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported client autonomy by offering options, asking permission, and respecting the client's caveats. Language avoided pressure and reinforced choice and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the client's reasons, barriers, and confidence and used a small‑test framing to draw out motivation. Could probe personal values and future‑oriented meaning more to strengthen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated the client as an equal collaborator, co‑creating the plan and inviting choices throughout. Could more explicitly highlight the client's expertise and invite them to lead agenda‑setting more often.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and shaped change talk (desire for better sleep, specific test, measurable signs of success). Could amplify and reflect change statements more strategically to deepen reasons and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and redirected to a manageable experiment, offering practical supports to address concerns. Could incorporate more double‑sided reflections to help resolve ambivalence more directly.
