# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently communicates respectful, nonjudgmental regard and normalizes ambivalence, creating a safe space for the patient to express resistance and tentative plans.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections that echo the patient's language. Could probe affective/experiential nuances (e.g., emotional role of smoking, stress after work) more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on smoking and NRT options and advances the session toward specific, time-bound actions without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative, inviting the patient's choices and co-constructing a feasible plan that the patient helps shape.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, accepting limits, and emphasizing that the patient's preferences and control drive the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits practical motivations and commitments (e.g., start inhaler tonight) and asks evocative questions; could further probe personal values, past strengths, and confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites input and shares decision-making power, positioning the patient as the expert and co-creating the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and strengthens change talk, prompting concrete plans and reasons; could amplify and summarize the patient's change language and explicitly elicit confidence/commitment to consolidate gains.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, but uses limited active softening; would benefit from more double-sided reflections and exploration of costs of the status quo to reduce sustain emphasis.
