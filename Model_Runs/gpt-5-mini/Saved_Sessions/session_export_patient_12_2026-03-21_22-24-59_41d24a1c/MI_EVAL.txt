# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing Amira's distress and protecting her dignity throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflections captured affect and content well, though occasional deeper, more complex reflections could amplify subtle emotional shifts and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on immediate safety and concrete, time-limited behaviors (text, alarm, playlist, walk) and ended with a specific, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner—inviting input, co-creating wording and backups, and using client contributions to shape the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored choice by framing options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure while affirming Amira's control over whether and how to act.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited client-generated ideas and small motivations (text, walk, playlist) but could probe more for underlying values, past successes, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership through repeated invitations for Amira's input and shared decision-making in planning and wording the message.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete, doable options and affirmations, yet could better amplify and summarize client statements into stronger commitment language and confidence work.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk using reflections and double-sided framing, but could more proactively use complex reflections and elicit costs of staying the same to further diminish sustain talk.
