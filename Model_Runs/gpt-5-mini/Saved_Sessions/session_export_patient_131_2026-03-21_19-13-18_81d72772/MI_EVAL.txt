# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys respect and nonjudgmental support with affirmations and safety-focused options; however the warmth is somewhat brief and leans toward problem-solving rather than deeper unconditional validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Demonstrates some perspective-taking about practical risks and stabilizing steps but offers limited reflection of Wiremu’s emotional nuance such as shame, remorse, and conflict about independence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, task-focused agenda around safety, urge-management, and steps toward reunification; at times the session prioritizes procedural instructions over collaboratively negotiating a single actionable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Solicits priorities and adjusts advice to safety concerns, yet often supplies detailed plans and scripts instead of co-developing them with Wiremu, reducing a sense of equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and multiple options of support, but much language is directive and could more explicitly invite Wiremu’s preferences and reinforce his right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges motivation and asks an initial choice question, but seldom uses open questions or techniques to draw out and strengthen Wiremu’s own reasons, ability, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes collaborative moments and permission-seeking by offering options, yet frequently shifts to an expert-directed stance with ready-made solutions rather than co-creating plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces steps Wiremu has taken and links plans to goals, but misses opportunities to elicit, deepen, and amplify his own change language and commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and offers practical alternatives, but uses more problem-solving than strategic reflections (double-sided or rolling with resistance) to shift ambivalence toward change.
