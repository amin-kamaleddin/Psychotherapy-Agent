# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and affirming throughout the session, normalizing priorities and validating choices without admonishment. Tone and wording conveyed unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good understanding with accurate reflective summaries of values and motivation. Reflections were mostly content-focused; more affective/complex reflections could deepen emotional processing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the single target behavior with logical progression from values to specific implementation and troubleshooting. Questions and reflections continually returned to concrete steps, barriers, and confidence.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative, co-creating messages, auto-replies, and supports while repeatedly checking Elena's preferences. The clinician invited input before finalizing suggestions, evidencing equal-partner problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Elena's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure. Prompts were framed to reinforce ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Elena's intrinsic reasons for change and linked them to values, using importance/confidence scaling and evocative questions. The clinician drew out the client's own strategies rather than imposing reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Elena as the expert on her life, inviting choices and co-creating language while incorporating her wording preferences. Repeated invitations to tweak drafts demonstrate strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with scaling questions, specific planning prompts, and reflective summaries that deepened commitment. Follow-up planning and implementation details strengthened expressed motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Minimized sustain talk by exploring potential barriers as obstacles to plan solutions and normalizing concerns without debating them. Redirected focus to values-based actions and coping strategies to avoid reinforcing stay-the-same reasons.
