# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained nonjudgmental, respectful regard throughout, normalizing Aisha's experience and offering compassionate, practical options that honored her constraints.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective‑taking and timely reflections, validating emotions and adapting recommendations to Aisha's energy and schedule.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on resuming exercise and translated motivation into a concrete, time‑bound starter plan with measurable tracking and barrier solutions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like shared problem‑solving: the clinician elicited preferences, offered options, and co‑created reminders and check‑ins to be iteratively tweaked.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and control, offering alternatives, asking permission, and presenting advice as options rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited much of Aisha's motivation and specific commitments but at times shifted to detailed prescriptions, reducing opportunities to further evoke the client's own reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Aisha as an active partner by soliciting her schedule and preferences and co‑constructing the plan and tracking methods.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language and concrete steps, though there were missed opportunities to reflect/amplify strong client phrases and directly elicit confidence or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively deflected sustain talk by normalizing doubts, offering autonomy‑supporting alternatives (7‑min backup, time swaps), and discouraging self‑punishment while reframing progress markers.
