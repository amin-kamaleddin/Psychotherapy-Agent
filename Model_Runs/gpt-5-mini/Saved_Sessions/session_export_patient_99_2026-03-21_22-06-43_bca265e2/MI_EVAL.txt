# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Matteo’s perspective with affirmations and reflective language, avoiding labeling or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and reflective listening are evident, but the clinician moved fairly quickly into problem-solving and could have explored deeper emotional meaning or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained clearly focused on alcohol use with a specific, measurable plan (a dry midweek night) and concrete implementation steps throughout the encounter.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction functioned as an equal partnership: the clinician solicited Matteo’s input, offered options, and co-constructed the plan based on his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supported choice by offering multiple small options, asking which felt realistic, and only providing help (e.g., drafting a text) when Matteo agreed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and amplified Matteo’s own motivations and strategies using open questions, scaling, and guided planning rather than prescribing change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treated Matteo as the expert on his situation, inviting his decisions (which night, how to ask Jake) and sharing power in planning and follow-up steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist actively drew out and reinforced change talk through option-giving, confidence rulers, and repeated prompts to specify and strengthen Matteo’s commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to small changes, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections or deeper validation of the social pull of drinking.
