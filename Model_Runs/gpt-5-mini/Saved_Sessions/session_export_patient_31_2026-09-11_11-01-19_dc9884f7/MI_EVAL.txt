# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized David's preference to keep evenings; no shaming, confrontation, or pressure to quit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used frequent, accurate reflections and summaries that captured both practical and value-based aspects of David's ambivalence, demonstrating strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing smoking at work via NRT, with questions and reflections tied to concrete, measurable steps and a logical progression to a plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed decisions as shared, solicited David's input at each step, and adjusted proposals to his responses, showing genuine two-way problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed David's freedom to choose, offered multiple options and validated his decisions, reinforcing his ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited David's own reasons and indicators of benefit with evocative questions and turned those statements into a concrete, self-directed trial.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned David as the expert on what would work, co-created the plan, and invited him to choose supports and pacing, reflecting strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
