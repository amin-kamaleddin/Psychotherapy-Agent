# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys respect and nonjudgmental support, validating progress (e.g., "22 days") and normalizing struggles. Avoids blaming language and acknowledges nervousness compassionately.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, with a strong opening summary that captures multiple dimensions of Timur's experience. Reflections deepen understanding rather than merely restating facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on target behaviors (staying crime-free, finding work/training) and moves the conversation toward concrete actions. Balances exploration with forward momentum (confidence ruler → smallest step → role-play → booking).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and co-constructed, repeatedly inviting Timur into decisions and honoring his choices. Role-play and offers to help (while asking permission) exemplify equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fosters choice by offering options, checking preferences, and framing recommendations as offers. Consistently seeks buy-in before proceeding with plans or supports.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from within Timur using evocative questions and confidence rulers, eliciting values and internal reasons for change. Amplifies and scaffolds expressed motivations into concrete steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Timur as an expert on his life, asks permission to brainstorm, and co-creates the plan without imposing solutions. Offers support options while deferring final choices to the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk through evocative questions, confidence scaling, and prompts for the smallest doable action. Amplifies change statements and encourages concrete commitments (e.g., role-play, booking).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Often redirects sustain talk toward planning and reframes doubts, but misses opportunities for more explicit MI strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or exploring the function of sustain talk). More targeted reflections or curiosity about identity statements would further reduce resistance.
