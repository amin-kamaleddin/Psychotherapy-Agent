# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the client's values without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, matching the patient's language and emotional tone while checking priorities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the vaccine decision and moves the conversation toward concrete action steps, negotiating tangible next steps collaboratively.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the encounter as a joint enterprise, repeatedly soliciting preferences and offering to co-create materials that the patient can edit.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and control by offering options, validating pacing, and asking permission before preparing materials.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently draws out internal motivations and desired outcomes producing meaningful change language, though could deepen intrinsic motivation by exploring values and confidence more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on her values and invites choices, but could emphasize co-creation earlier by asking more content-shaping questions before proposing formats.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with concrete examples and planned steps, yet missed opportunities to amplify commitment language and use scaling/confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses double-sided reflections and avoids reinforcing sustain talk to reduce defensiveness, while additional normalization and selective amplification of pro-change statements could further tip the balance.
