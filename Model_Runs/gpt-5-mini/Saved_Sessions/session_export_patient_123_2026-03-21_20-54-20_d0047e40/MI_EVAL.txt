# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard and a consistently respectful, validating tone that normalizes the adolescent’s experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that validate the client’s stressors; could deepen empathic reflections by exploring feelings and the personal meaning behind the overwhelm.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on study habits with a concrete, measurable plan and a scheduled follow-up, keeping the session goal-directed and behaviorally focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as genuine collaborative problem-solving: clinician offers expertise while inviting the client's choices and co-producing the implementation and follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, avoiding pressure, and reinforcing the adolescent’s ownership of decisions and the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits the client’s ideas, plans, and confidence but leans toward practical logistics; more elicitation of personally meaningful reasons for change would strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance throughout—invites the client's expertise, shares decisions, and checks preferences for follow-up, demonstrating balanced power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and commitment with specific planning and confidence scaling; could more actively amplify and summarize the client's motivational language to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges ambivalence nonjudgmentally and problem-solves collaboratively to avoid reinforcing sustain talk; could add double-sided reflections or explicit normalization to further diffuse doubt.
