# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized difficulties, and affirmed the patient's efforts with validating statements that created a warm, supportive tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring both content and emotion which encouraged elaboration and rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing orange juice with concrete action steps and a 7‑day commitment, balancing open exploration with practical guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner by co-creating scripts, building on the patient's ideas, and integrating prior successful strategies to shape the plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored choice and agency by offering options, asking which phrasing felt truer, and inviting the patient to select the concrete step and supports.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified the patient's own motivations and values with evocative questions, leveraging intrinsic reasons (family, energy, pride) to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and power-sharing: treated the patient as the expert, invited preferences, and negotiated options rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and reinforced change talk through open, change‑directed questions, reflections, and practice of scripts, producing high confidence and concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed into family‑friendly solutions while allowing a small treat, but could have used more double‑sided reflections to directly address the pull of pleasure versus health.
