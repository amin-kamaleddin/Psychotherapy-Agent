# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalizing, nonjudgmental language that created a warm supportive environment; could add a brief, specific affirmation naming a concrete client strength.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-quality reflective listening and accurate mirroring of the client's language and affect helped the client feel heard; occasionally using more complex reflections to infer deeper emotions could deepen empathic capture.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on a concrete proximal target (short outreach) and progressively narrowed to a specific doable step (drafting one line).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was co-constructed with inclusive language and frequent invitations for the client's input, adapting plans based on the patient's preferences and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported client autonomy with repeated choice statements and normalization of hesitation, avoiding pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations for easing loneliness and small reasons for change but seldom probed importance/confidence or summarized change statements to build momentum; brief follow-ups and scaling could strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance and shared decision-making, offering options and inviting co-creation of the plan without reverting to an expert-driven approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and supported change talk with options and questions, producing incremental change statements, but missed opportunities to selectively reflect, amplify, and probe for reasons or confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk using neutral and double-sided reflections, yet tended to reflect sustain talk more than strategically reframing or highlighting retained values and past small successes to diminish resistance.
