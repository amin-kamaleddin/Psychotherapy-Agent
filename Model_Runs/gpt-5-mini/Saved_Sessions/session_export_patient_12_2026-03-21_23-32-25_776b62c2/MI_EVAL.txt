# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance with explicit acceptance statements and normalizing the patient’s small steps, affirming the patient’s agency.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated highly accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, using complex reflections and mirroring the patient’s tone to validate isolation and helplessness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on a concrete, immediate harm-reduction step (anonymous chat/message) and repeatedly steered questions toward doable actions and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving and invited co-creation of message wording and plans, though at times offered multiple suggested options that slightly guided the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient’s choice and control by offering alternatives, asking which option felt safer, and validating pacing without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete ideas and planning around an anonymous chat and message wording, but did not sufficiently probe deeper values, importance, or confidence to amplify intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and power-sharing, soliciting the patient’s input, though occasionally narrowed choices or provided phrasing that risked steering the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced limited change talk and helped specify small steps, but missed opportunities to explore importance, confidence, and deeper reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and avoided reinforcing sustain talk through reflections and redirection, but did not use stronger strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections, eliciting exceptions) to weaken persistent sustain statements.
