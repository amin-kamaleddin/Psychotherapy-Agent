### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on assertiveness and boundary-setting throughout the session, moving logically from planning to rehearsal. Interventions consistently tie back to the target behavior and avoid drifting.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed—clinician invites input, offers choices, and role-plays at Oliver's request while framing feedback supportively. Decisions and practice were handled jointly, keeping the client active in the process.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicits reasons, plans, and some concrete change language, using evocative questions and rehearsal to strengthen motivation. Could further deepen evocation by asking more about values, past successes, and amplifying spontaneous change talk to elicit commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and shared power through invitations and co-construction, but at times supplied ready-made scripts rather than eliciting Oliver's own wording first. Asking the client to try phrasing before offering templates would enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and supports change talk with evocative questions and practice, producing concrete planning language. Could more strategically amplify and sequence change talk and follow up with confidence/commitment questions to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and validates sustain talk without reinforcing it, then redirects toward action with practical strategies. Using more double-sided reflections and explicit summaries of sustain versus change talk would help resolve ambivalence more clearly.
