# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating Marcus’s loyalty to his dog and normalizing his struggle throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections but sometimes moved quickly into problem‑solving instead of lingering to deepen emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior (asthma management) and repeatedly steered toward concrete, actionable steps tied to Marcus’s goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session read as genuine collaboration: Marcus chose priorities, the clinician offered options and role‑play, and the plan was co‑created based on his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Marcus’s choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and framing suggestions as his to accept or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Marcus’s ideas and had him craft commitments, but could deepen intrinsic motivation by exploring values, meaning, and long‑term benefits.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Marcus as an equal partner and expert in his life, co‑creating the plan and inviting him to lead decisions about which strategies to try.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with goal questions and a confidence ruler, yet missed chances to probe why change matters and to elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and offered compromises and practical supports, but could use more double‑sided reflections and strategic summaries to shift ambivalence more artfully.
