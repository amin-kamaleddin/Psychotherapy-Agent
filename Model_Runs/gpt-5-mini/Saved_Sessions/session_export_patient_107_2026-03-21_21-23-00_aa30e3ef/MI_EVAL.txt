# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard by validating worries, normalizing ambivalence, and responding kindly to slips, avoiding judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate understanding with frequent reflective listening and summaries that capture both content and feeling, showing strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on coffee use with stepwise planning, a concrete one-week experiment, repeated summaries, and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co-creative—clinician repeatedly solicits the client's input, incorporates preferences into the plan, and invites tweaks before implementation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering multiple options, asking permission, normalizing slips, and inviting the client to select what feels doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and ambivalence and secures a modest commitment, but could more deeply evoke intrinsic motives and amplify client-generated reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly adopts an equal, client-centered stance—asks permission to suggest, invites decision-making, and co-constructs the plan with clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (evocative questions, rulers, and a specific experiment), though more frequent reflections and amplification of change statements would strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects toward change with low-effort experiments and nonjudgmental framing, but could employ more double-sided reflections to tip ambivalence.
