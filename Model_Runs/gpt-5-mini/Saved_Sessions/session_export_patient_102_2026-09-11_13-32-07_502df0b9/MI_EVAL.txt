# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalization of the patient's perspective without shaming or moralizing, creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections captured both content and affect (social belonging, discomfort with pressure), fostering safety and disclosure throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on weekend alcohol use and steadily guided the conversation from options to a concrete, implementable plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a co‑author of the plan by soliciting the patient's preferences at each step and integrating their choices into the next prompts and options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's right to choose, offered realistic options (including keeping things as-is), and normalized imperfect adherence to support autonomous decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own reasons, preferences, and implementation ideas and then amplified those motivators to build internally generated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, deferred to the patient's expertise, and invited their priorities when shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and amplified change talk and moved the patient from considering options to specifying actionable strategies and indicators of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and used normalizing language to reduce pressure, though there were missed opportunities for more explicit double-sided reflections to make ambivalence more audible.
