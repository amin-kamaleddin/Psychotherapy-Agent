# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established unconditional positive regard throughout, avoided blame or shame, validated Jamal's experience, and framed help as optional and supportive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that normalized ambivalence, but could deepen affect-focused reflections (e.g., shame, fear) and probe the meaning of team identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol use in team contexts and consistently moved the session toward concrete, behavioral steps (narrowing to one small step, role-play, rehearsal).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction read as a team effort with shared practice and choices, though the clinician sometimes took the lead in offering wording rather than explicitly co-generating language with Jamal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Jamal's choice by offering options, accepting limited commitments, and reinforcing ownership of decisions without pressuring for more than he was willing to do.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Jamal's motivations and practical ideas (teammate backup, refusal lines), but could probe deeper for intrinsic motives, values, and importance/confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and invited choices, yet occasionally shifted into coaching by supplying many specific lines instead of eliciting more of Jamal's own phrasing first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language that produced concrete intentions, but missed opportunities to amplify desire/reasons and move more toward commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to safer options effectively, though could strengthen this domain with double-sided or strategic reflections to explicitly reduce sustain talk's momentum.
