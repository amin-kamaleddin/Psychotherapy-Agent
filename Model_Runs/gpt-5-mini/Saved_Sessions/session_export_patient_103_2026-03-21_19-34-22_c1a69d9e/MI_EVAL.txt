# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental—apologized for the wait, validated the patient’s feelings, and allowed strong emotion without labeling or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, matching the patient’s tone and affect and reframing recommendations to address emotional needs.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on the appointment delay and job risk, steering toward concrete remedies, timelines, and follow-up tasks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, invited the patient’s input, recorded specifics, and sought consent before finalizing actions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options and asked for the patient’s preferences and permission, avoiding coercive language and reinforcing choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited elicitation of the patient’s internal motivations or past strengths; the clinician mainly supplied external solutions rather than drawing out intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and co‑constructed solutions, though the clinician sometimes took the lead and could more explicitly invite the patient’s expertise on implementation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Few evocative questions to elicit change talk—most queries were logistical and the clinician moved quickly to offer fixes instead of exploring reasons, confidence, or past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected to problem-solving, but occasionally echoed strong anger in ways that could reinforce sustain talk and relied more on operational redirection than strategic softening techniques.
