# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Started with an explicit nonjudgmental stance and maintained a warm, respectful tone while repeatedly validating effort and normalizing difficulty throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and acknowledged specific worries and ambivalence, demonstrating good understanding; could deepen with more complex, affect‑focused reflections to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with specific, measurable steps and minimal drift, using scaling and planning to steer the session toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently solicited the patient's input, co‑constructed plans, and adapted recommendations to the patient's preferences, treating him as an equal partner in decision‑making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice by asking permission, offering optional experiments, and framing suggestions as the patient's decision, avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation with evocative questions and confidence scaling and encouraged self‑generated strategies; could further probe personal values and selectively amplify change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated authentic partnership by asking permission, inviting the patient to choose strategies and timelines, and deferring to the patient's expertise about what is doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced desire, ability, and planning statements and reflected strengths, but could more often amplify and summarize the patient's change language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk while offering alternatives and backups without arguing, though missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections and stronger reframing to reduce sustain talk's influence.
