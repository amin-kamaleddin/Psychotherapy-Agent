### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard and normalized Nadine’s experience, offering warm, nonjudgmental support throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections and frequent naming of emotions and concrete details, which helped Nadine feel heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear focus on coping with fatigue and nighttime routines, progressing to a concrete, doable plan with specific steps and safety contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The encounter was framed as joint problem-solving with choices and co‑created plans, tailoring guidance to Nadine’s preferences rather than imposing a single approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently asked permission, offered multiple options, and affirmed Nadine’s control over decisions, reinforcing client ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited Nadine’s ideas and willingness to try strategies, but could further deepen intrinsic motivations and assess confidence to strengthen internal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and permission-seeking, though there were moments of brief advice-giving where explicitly foregrounding Nadine’s expertise could be strengthened.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicited and reinforced several change statements and offered choices, yet missed opportunities to probe reasons, confidence, and elicit a specific timed commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and normalized and reframed into small experiments, with room to use more double-sided reflections to name ambivalence while steering toward change.
