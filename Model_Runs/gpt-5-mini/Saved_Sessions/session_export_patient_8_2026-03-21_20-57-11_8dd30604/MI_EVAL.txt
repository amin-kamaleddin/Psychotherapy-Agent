# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with validation and normalizing language throughout the session, affirming Amina's experience and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured ambivalence, but limited deeper exploration of the emotional functions (e.g., FOMO, loneliness) before moving to suggestions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on reducing/managing phone use with concrete, behavior-specific strategies and measurable goals; no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction framed as joint problem-solving with invitations to choose strategies, though frequent clinician-led suggestions sometimes shifted the balance toward provider-driven content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice, offered multiple options, normalized slips, and emphasized small low-pressure steps, strongly supporting client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and resources (importance/confidence scaling, planning) but at times supplied many strategies instead of eliciting more of Amina’s own ideas and values first.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with invitations to choose and check preferences, but offering numerous specific strategies without always asking permission reduced the sense of fully co-constructed planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with scaling and concrete small steps, though could have amplified and summarized change statements more to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and normalized ambivalence but did not consistently use MI-specific softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to reframe or diffuse sustain themes.
