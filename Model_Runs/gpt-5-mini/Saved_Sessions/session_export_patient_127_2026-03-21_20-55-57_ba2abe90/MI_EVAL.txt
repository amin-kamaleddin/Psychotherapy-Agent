# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (opening invitation, affirmations, validation of ambivalence). Avoided blaming language and created safety for open disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed strong reflective listening and accurate summarizing that likely helped the client feel understood. Missed deeper probes into shame/identity (e.g., "I'm lazy," "unaccepted") where more complex reflections would strengthen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on avoiding reoffending/returning to the old life and elicited concrete, time-bound steps. Minimal topic drift and clear momentum toward actionable change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving and adjusted to the client's preferences, offering tools only when the client agreed. Co-created the plan and demonstrated responsiveness and shared ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the client's sense of choice by offering multiple options and asking what felt doable. Reinforced that the plan belonged to the client rather than imposing directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out intrinsic motivation by asking what helped and what matters most, linking values to specific actions. Elicited client-driven commitments (temp agency sign-up, craving script, accountability).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance by offering options, asking preferences, and co-creating the plan with the client. Invited rehearsal and supported the client's agency throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (asking what helped, offering doable options, requesting confidence ratings). Moves produced clear client change language and specific commitment steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected to coping strategies without amplifying it, using validations and problem-solving. Could use more double-sided reflections and targeted reframing of self-critical labels to further defuse sustain talk.
