# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalized Sofia's experience, and affirmed efforts (e.g., praised reduced use). The clinician avoided shame and acknowledged difficulty of change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely validations, but missed opportunities to probe deeper into emotional nuance (belonging, anxiety, family expectations).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, focused agenda on marijuana use with concrete, actionable goals (trigger mapping, sober windows, practice and monitoring). The session remained goal-directed without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner throughout, co-constructing plans and integrating Sofia's ideas while inviting her input and follow-up. Decisions were negotiated rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Sofia's choice and ownership by offering harm-reduction experiments and emphasizing that she controls next steps. The clinician avoided coercive language and respected readiness to change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited Sofia's motivations and reinforced self-discovered reasons through experiments and ratings, but could more consistently probe personally meaningful reasons and elicit confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance that privileged Sofia's expertise, offering menus of options and tailoring scripts and plans to her preferences. The clinician shared power and invited co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with options, ratings, and small-step plans, but missed chances for deeper open questions to strengthen desire, reasons, and explicit confidence/commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and often redirected toward change-oriented strategies, but could have used more double-sided or complex reflections to explicitly surface ambivalence and reframe sustain talk.
