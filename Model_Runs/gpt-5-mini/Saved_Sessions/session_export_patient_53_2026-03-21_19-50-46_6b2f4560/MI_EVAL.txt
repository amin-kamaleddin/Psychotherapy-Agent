# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and normalized avoidance, creating a nonjudgmental atmosphere that likely reduced defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening with accurate reflections of Isabella's experience, but many reflections stayed surface-level and missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions like shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and linked interventions to concrete, actionable steps with practical trouble-shooting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction: the clinician solicited preferences, offered to help rewrite/paste messages, and co-created the plan rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by presenting multiple low-pressure options, asking permission, and repeatedly deferring to the patient's choices without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited behavioral preferences and options but did not sufficiently draw out Isabella's internal motivations, values, or reasons for change to build stronger commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Isabella as a collaborator, seeking her input at every choice point and inviting joint decision-making about wording and strategy.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created conditions for tentative change talk with small options, but did not consistently amplify or follow up on change statements to strengthen them into preparatory or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently normalized and gently reframed sustain talk and offered low-pressure alternatives, though could have used more double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly resolve ambivalence.
