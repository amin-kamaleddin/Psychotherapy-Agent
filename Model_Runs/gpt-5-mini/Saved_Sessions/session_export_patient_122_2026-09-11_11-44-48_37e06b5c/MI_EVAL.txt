# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, validates limits, and frames the plan as realistic and gentle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, but reflections focus more on content and planning than deeper emotions like shame or discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, sustained focus on increasing brief movement and moves from general desire to specific implementation intentions without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructs a realistic plan and checks feasibility repeatedly, though the clinician often offers menus of options rather than eliciting the client's ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites choice, respects Sofia's pacing, and frames suggestions as optional, strongly supporting client control and decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Sofia's motivations and preferences and uses them to build the plan, but could draw out deeper personal values and summarize change talk into commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint exploration and defers to the client, yet frequent clinician-generated option lists can subtly steer decisions rather than fully privileging the client's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and scaffolds change talk with concrete prompts that produce client change language, but could more consistently reflect, amplify, and follow up on those statements to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward practical problem-solving (backups, cues), but does not consistently use double-sided reflections or elicitation/reframing techniques to resolve ambivalence.
