# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and used normalizing, nonjudgmental language while affirming the patient's ideas and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening and summaries that validated both practical concerns and emotional ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on early alcohol use and harm-reduction strategies, guiding the conversation from brainstorming to concrete, time-limited actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and egalitarian interaction, repeatedly seeking the patient's preferences and co-creating the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering options, asking permission before scripting, and emphasizing the patient's control over choices and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's internal motivations (e.g., grades, choir) and built on those reasons to generate specific change ideas and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert, invited her voice in wording and choices, and shared decision-making in developing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with open, change-focused questions, reflections, and repeated pushes for concrete next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence and avoided reinforcing sustain talk, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections or more strategic rolling with resistance before moving to solutions.
