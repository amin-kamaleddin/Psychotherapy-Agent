# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, honoring the patient's 'no lectures' request and using validating reflections and reassurance to reduce shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with targeted reflections and paraphrases that named frustration and ambivalence, keeping the patient engaged and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking reduction/protecting the home, quickly moving to a specific, actionable 'no smoking inside' plan and confirming alignment throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a collaborative problem-solving exchange by asking preferences at each decision point and jointly constructing the plan and follow-up to fit the patient's limits.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly foregrounded choice and control by offering options, deferring to the patient's rules, and repeatedly emphasizing that she was in charge of the plan and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivations (protecting daughter, tools, confidence scaling) and momentary commitments, but could more deeply explore personal values or meaning to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as the expert on her life, offered choices rather than directives, and co-created a plan based on her preferences, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and reinforced change talk and translated it into specific, doable actions (e.g., 3-day experiment, confidence rating), though occasionally could amplify the patient's own change statements further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing and rolled with resistance, honored boundaries, and framed slips as informational and normal, which reduced sustain talk's influence.
