# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing ambivalence and validating feelings while staying supportive even during firm coaching.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and accurate reflections and adapts to affect, but reflections are often concise and move quickly to skills rather than exploring deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, tight focus on boundary-setting throughout, progressing logically from eliciting goals to scripting, rehearsal, and escalation planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaves as an equal partner by co-creating scripts, soliciting feedback and preferences, and repeatedly checking readiness and agreement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing suggestions as alternatives that the client can accept or reject.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses scaling and invitations to rehearse to draw out motivation, but tends toward skill-teaching and could further elicit values, past successes, and internal motives to deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—negotiates role-plays and tone, shares control over wording, and centers the client's expertise and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change talk via scaling, scripting, and rehearsals, but could amplify and reflect the client's own change statements more directly to build stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk and reframes toward boundary-holding while offering comebacks, though sometimes shifts quickly to tactics instead of using double-sided reflections to further soften sustain talk.
