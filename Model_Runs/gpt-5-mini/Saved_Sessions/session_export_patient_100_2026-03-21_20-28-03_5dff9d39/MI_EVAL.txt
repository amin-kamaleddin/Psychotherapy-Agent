### Empathy

**Comments:** Consistently accurate, reflective, and validating (including safety checks) and pairs affective empathy with pragmatic supports and resources.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol use, related harms, and immediate safety needs, linking discussion to concrete next steps like a party plan and testing options.

### Collaboration
