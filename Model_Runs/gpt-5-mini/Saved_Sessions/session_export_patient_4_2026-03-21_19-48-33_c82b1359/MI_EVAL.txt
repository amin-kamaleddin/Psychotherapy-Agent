# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with empathic openers and validation, treating the patient's concerns as legitimate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows clear empathic listening and some reflections early on, but could deepen and more frequently reflect the patient's core fears and emotional nuance before shifting to action.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on diagnostic clarity and anxiety management, listing specific investigations and concrete next steps to keep the session on track.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—iteratively co-constructs the referral letter with the patient, incorporating edits and adopting a teammate stance rather than an authoritative one.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and following the patient's preferences (including copying her on referrals).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Largely supplies reasons for action rather than drawing out the patient's own motivations; misses opportunities to explore values or intrinsic goals linked to symptom improvement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invites co-production of the referral letter and incorporates the patient's priorities, clearly sharing power and recognizing her expertise about her experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes minimal effort to elicit or amplify change talk; does not follow up on patient cues (e.g., improvement during the wedding) to explore possibilities for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and validates the patient's desire for testing, but does not use MI strategies like double-sided reflections or strategic reframe to soften sustain talk.
