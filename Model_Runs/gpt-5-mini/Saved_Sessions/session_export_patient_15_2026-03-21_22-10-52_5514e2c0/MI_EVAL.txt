# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed clear unconditional positive regard with respectful language, multiple affirmations, and nonjudgmental responses when the patient minimized alcohol use.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening that captured facts and values, though reflections stayed mostly factual and did not probe deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a focused, appropriate agenda with a concrete two-week monitoring plan, safety thresholds, and a scheduled follow-up to review results.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was framed as a shared project with co-created monitoring plans, offers of format options, and repeated solicitation of the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission before giving information, and emphasizing that the plan was the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivations (health, work) and agreement to track, but relied more on offering tools than drawing out the patient's own reasons or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a genuine collaborative stance—inviting the patient to set priorities, honoring choices, and jointly planning logistics and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generated preparatory change talk via options and a low-burden experiment that led to tracking commitment, but did not systematically amplify or deepen change talk toward stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and offered neutral options, but used few strategic MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to actively soften minimization and resolve ambivalence.
