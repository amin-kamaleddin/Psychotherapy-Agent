Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

1. Global Ratings (Counselor)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Acceptance (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard
□ High: consistently respectful and supportive
□ Low: judgmental, disapproving
Comments: ___________________________________________

Empathy (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective
□ High: active reflective listening
□ Low: little effort to understand
Comments: ___________________________________________

Direction (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Maintains clear focus on target behavior or change goal.
□ High – keeps the session on-track and purpose-driven while remaining collaborative.
□ Low – allows session to drift without consistent focus.
Comments: ___________________________________________

Collaboration (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Works as an equal partner; shares power and decision-making.
□ High – minimizes power differential; invites client input and joint problem-solving.
□ Low – expert-driven, directive, or dismissive of client input.
Comments: ___________________________________________

Autonomy Support (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Emphasizes client choice and self-determination.
□ High – affirms freedom to choose; avoids coercion; validates client control.
□ Low – uses imperatives or pressure; limits perceived choice.
Comments: ___________________________________________

Evocation (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Draws out the client’s own reasons and motivations for change.
□ High – consistently elicits, explores, and reinforces change talk.
□ Low – provides reasons or advice without exploring client’s view.
Comments: ___________________________________________

Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Encourages client change language
□ 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)
Comments: ___________________________________________

Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance
□ 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)
Comments: ___________________________________________

Partnership (5-point Likert)
Expectation: working with the client as an equal partner
□ 1 (maintains imbalance or hierarchy) → 5 (actively fosters shared decision-making)
Comments: ___________________________________________



2. Self-Audit Prompts (Clinician Reflection)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Am I reflecting more than I’m asking? (R:Q ratio)
- Did I dwell on sustain talk too long?
- Have I affirmed autonomy and supported choice?
- Is commitment language strengthening toward the session’s close?
