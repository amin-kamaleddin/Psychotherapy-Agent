# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, repeatedly validating distress and normalizing skepticism throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with clear reflections, but many stayed at the surface and missed opportunities to capture deeper emotions like shame or self-blame.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on late-night snacking and routines, steering the conversation toward a concrete 7-day experiment, backups, and a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete planning and confidence scaling, yet missed opportunities to amplify motivation and secure a stronger commitment beyond 'I'll try.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
