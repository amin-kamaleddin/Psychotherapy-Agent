# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing feelings and affirming efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a warm, empathic tone and some reflections, but often moved quickly to suggestions and missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on starting exercise with concrete, actionable steps (scheduling, micro-commitments, habit-stacking) and consistent follow-up plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked jointly with the patient to build a realistic plan, incorporated her schedule and preferences, and co-created the sister messaging and follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, optional elements, and explicit permission language while reinforcing the patient's decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk but relied heavily on giving solutions rather than using evocative questions and reflections to draw out personal motivations and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful, but at times clinician-led with multiple concrete recommendations rather than explicitly inviting the patient's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reinforced and amplified existing change talk (doable plan, small steps), yet could do more to expand on values and deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Minimized sustain talk using reframes and micro-commitments and normalized setbacks, though double-sided reflections acknowledging both sides of ambivalence were infrequent.
