# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, using validating language and praise for small efforts. Avoided blaming and normalized Naomi's experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening with accurate, timely reflections and normalization of symptoms. Could deepen emotional exploration (shame, fear of judgment, identity impact) with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on managing social/performance anxiety and moved efficiently from assessment to skills practice and a concrete plan. Interventions and planning stayed tightly aligned with the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in collaborative problem-solving, inviting Naomi's input and adapting suggestions to her preferences and constraints. Co-created practical implementation steps and tailored tools (micro-script, lock-screen) together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice by offering options, asking preferences, and respecting hesitancy, while normalizing setbacks and encouraging self-kindness. Reinforced Naomi's ownership of the plan and practice decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation and willingness but relied more on offering solutions than drawing out Naomi’s internal motives, values, or reasons for change. Missed opportunities to probe importance/confidence or link improvements to Naomi's goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered client expertise and co-created the plan by repeatedly asking permission and preferences and adjusting interventions based on Naomi's feedback. Collaboration produced an accepted, tailored micro-script and implementation plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created opportunities for change talk and elicited several change statements but did not consistently amplify or deepen them. Could have reflected and expanded on desire, reasons, need, and confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used validation, normalization, and practical pivots to reduce doubts' power. Could further strengthen with strategic double-sided reflections that explicitly tip toward change.
