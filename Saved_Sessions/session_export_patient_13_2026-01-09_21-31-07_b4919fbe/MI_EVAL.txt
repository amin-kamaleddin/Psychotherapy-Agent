# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with normalizing reflections and affirmations, avoiding blame and explicitly supporting the patient's choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing emotions and practical pressures which helps the patient feel heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on seeking medical evaluation throughout and moves the conversation toward specific, time-bound, actionable options and a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the exchange as a genuine team effort by offering to write and practice a script, negotiating contingency plans, and agreeing on shared supports rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces patient choice and control by offering multiple options, asking preference questions, and supporting the patient’s decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits actionable motivation and builds self-efficacy with small steps and rehearsal but misses deeper elicitation of personal values, scaling confidence, and stronger explicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative with shared decision-making, frequent invitations for the patient's input, and co-construction of the plan that foregrounds the patient’s expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change-oriented statements by asking about barriers, offering manageable options, and reinforcing chosen steps to build momentum toward action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk (fear, overwhelm) without confrontation and successfully reframes it into low-pressure, practical alternatives that reduce resistance and promote action.
