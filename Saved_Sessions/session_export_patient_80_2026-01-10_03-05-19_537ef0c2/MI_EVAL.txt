# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone with warm openings and repeated affirmations; avoids blaming language and normalizes difficulty throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and reflective listening, recalling key details and tracking ambivalence; reflections are more behaviorally focused and miss deeper emotional meaning that would raise empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking and managing anger, guiding agenda and moving toward rehearsal and a concrete safety plan; keeps the session tethered to actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is strongly collaborative—permission is sought, decisions are co-created, and the clinician gives brief feedback then lets the patient try again; patient preferences drive the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports the patient's sense of choice by offering options, checking readiness, and avoiding pressure for abstinence; reinforces ownership of the plan and respects limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivation by asking about pride and linking behaviors to values, but focuses more on practical skills than exploring intrinsic reasons, confidence, or long-term goals to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters clear power-sharing and treats the patient as the expert—asks permission to brainstorm, offers options, and repeatedly invites the patient's choice about rehearsal and strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through questions, affirmations, and rehearsal, but could more strategically amplify spontaneous change language and elicit importance/confidence to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to reduction strategies and safety planning, but could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and soften sustain talk further.
