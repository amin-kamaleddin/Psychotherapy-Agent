# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, with explicit validation and normalizing language; supports self-kindness and accepts skepticism without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and practical validation, but uses few complex reflections that mirror Sofia’s discouragement and self-criticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing gentle movement and coping with stress, using concrete plans, habit-stacking, and a one-week follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites preferences and co-creates concrete plans (alarms, checkchart), though the clinician sometimes leans toward offering pre-made solutions rather than eliciting client-generated options first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and ownership by offering options, asking permission for exercises, and framing missed days as non-failures to reinforce autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some commitment language and a one-week trial, but is largely clinician-driven and misses opportunities to explore values, importance, and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively by asking Sofia to choose and verbalize plans, yet could share power more by prompting her to generate solutions before offering clinician-crafted options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete commitment (if–then phrasing, alarm, chart) but relies on offering solutions rather than systematically drawing out Sofia’s own reasons, values, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and provides pragmatic fallbacks, but uses few double-sided reflections or deeper exploration to resolve ambivalence.
