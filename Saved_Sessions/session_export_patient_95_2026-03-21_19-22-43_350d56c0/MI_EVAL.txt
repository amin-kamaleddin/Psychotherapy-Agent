# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation with multiple empathic reflections and explicit thanks for disclosure, avoiding blaming or minimizing language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-level empathic listening and accurate reflections matched the patient’s affect and needs, including validating the request to be heard before problem‑solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on suicidal ideation and safety with repeated risk assessment and concrete safety steps (delays, moving pills, grounding) while allowing space for emotion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and co-constructive—invited input about next steps and coping choices—though necessary clinician-led safety checks occasionally shifted the dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered choices and asked permission frequently, supporting the patient's control, but warranted firm requests about pills and delays slightly constrained autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-relevant material (reason to stay, a memory) but did not systematically deepen intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence to build momentum.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a partnership stance and solicited preferences for interventions and planning, with occasional directive language around urgent safety actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made clear attempts to elicit change talk (asked for one reason to stay and next steps) but missed opportunities to amplify and explore emerging change language further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by validating distress and redirecting to coping and safety; could strengthen use of double-sided or amplified reflections to further soften sustain talk.
