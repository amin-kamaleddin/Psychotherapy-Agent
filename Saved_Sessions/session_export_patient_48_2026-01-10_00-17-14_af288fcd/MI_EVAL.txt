# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (e.g., 'You’re not lazy'), using warm, respectful language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, capturing feelings and practical constraints which helped the client elaborate a concrete plan.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on increasing brief daily movement and moved logically from barriers to a specific, implementable action plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a true partner with the client to co-create the plan, invited input on specifics (times, wording, backup) and used inclusive language throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed client choice and control, offered options and low-pressure framing, and avoided coercive or directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the client’s own motivations and practical reasons (scaling, choosing small steps, alarm wording) and built action from the client's statements, though longer-term values could be explored further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered an equal, client-led process by offering options, asking preferences, and integrating the client's decisions into the final plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (importance/confidence, tiny steps, commitment to specifics) but could more actively amplify and deepen change talk by asking the client to elaborate on reasons and past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used normalization and reframing to reduce defensiveness, but sustain talk remained and could be softened further with double-sided or strategic summary reflections.
