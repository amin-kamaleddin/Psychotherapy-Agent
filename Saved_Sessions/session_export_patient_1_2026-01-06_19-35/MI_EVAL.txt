### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative: the clinician elicits the patient's input at every turn, defers to the patient's context knowledge, and co-creates a plan that fits the crew.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offers options, respects the patient's choices, and reinforces that any change is the patient's decision, supporting autonomy throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician effectively draws motivation from the patient with questions that elicit personally meaningful reasons for change and converts those motives into a manageable test the patient owns.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician frames the interaction as a team effort, asks the patient to generate solutions, and refines the patient's own plan rather than prescribing, demonstrating strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicits and amplifies change language by offering options, prompting specifics, and translating the patient's statements into a concrete, time-limited plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoids argument and uses reflective language to acknowledge sustain talk and redirect toward change, though could use more explicit double-sided reflections and reframing to resolve ambivalence.
