# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Luka’s mixed feelings, validating shame and relief without judgment. Acceptance is frequent and specific, preserving dignity and reducing threat.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections capture goals and affect (determination, shame, relief) and mirror Luka’s concerns and the partner’s role. Empathic statements are timely and match Luka’s emotional tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol-related violence and moves from goals to specific, actionable steps. The clinician steers the sequence (identify goal, elicit options, pick concrete step, set backups) without imposing solutions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner, inviting choices and repeatedly checking for Luka’s buy-in while synthesizing his ideas into a coherent plan. Language and process position Luka as co-author of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves Luka’s sense of choice by offering options, asking preferences, and framing suggestions as possibilities rather than directives. This approach reinforces ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Luka’s own motivations, reasons, and solutions and resists prescriptive advice. Evocative questions and prompts produce a detailed, self-generated plan and contingencies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly invites Luka’s expertise and decisions (e.g., which option to try first) and builds the plan from his selections. The clinician continually checks back to ensure joint agreement on the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicits and reinforces change talk, asking for concrete next steps and implementation details that produce specific desire/reason/action statements. Reflections and affirmations strengthen Luka’s articulated change goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids amplifying sustain talk and redirects to problem-solving and backups, but could more often use advanced softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or selective amplification of earlier change talk). The clinician prevents escalation but misses some opportunities to more actively diminish resistance.
