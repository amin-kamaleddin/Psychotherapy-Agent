# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing ambivalence and reframing slips as information. Validates feelings (e.g., nervousness living alone) and preserves the client's dignity throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and perspective-taking, paraphrasing patterns and summarizing upsides and downsides. Could deepen empathic exploration of affect linked to low mood/loneliness to reach an unequivocal top score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol reduction with specific, measurable goals and a logical progression from assessment to planning to commitment. Keeps the dialogue tied to concrete behavior change and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—frequently asks for preferences, reflects the client's ideas, and co-creates the plan. Language positions the work as a joint endeavor shaped by the client's contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports client choice, emphasizes that small changes matter, and offers options/templates while leaving decisions to the client. Reassures that slips will not be judged, preserving the client's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits reasons for change and uses the confidence ruler and probes to draw out motivation. Could strengthen intrinsic motivation further by more systematically exploring values, a hoped-for future, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and shares power by inviting choices about goals, strategies, and check-ins. Co-constructs the plan with the client with repeated invitations to choose and tweak.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through options, concrete planning, the confidence ruler, and probing what would increase commitment. Reflects and affirms specific intentions, helping the client articulate realistic steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects ambivalence with smaller options and normalization. Could employ more strategic techniques (double-sided reflections, selectively amplified change reflections, eliciting discrepancies) to further soften sustain talk.
