# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, normalizing difficulty and affirming prior success. Avoids labeling or blame and reinforces the client's competence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries of the situation. Could deepen emotional attunement by reflecting underlying feelings (e.g., shame, loss, disappointment) more often.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity and moves the session toward concrete, measurable plans. Repeatedly asks for specifics (days, times, cues, backups) to preserve momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructs the plan by eliciting preferences, offering choices, and incorporating the client's wording into texts and alarm notes. Avoids an expert-only stance and uses language that invites the client's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports client choice by offering options, asking permission, and affirming the client's control over decisions and rest. Avoids pressuring language and reinforces ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation through questions about past success, small steps, and supports, producing concrete reasons and strategies. Could more directly evoke intrinsic values and personally meaningful outcomes rather than focusing mainly on logistics.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a true partnership, inviting the client to pick or tweak options and share in decision-making. Incorporates client language and preferences throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and nurtures change talk with open questions and scaling, yielding multiple change statements. Sometimes shifts quickly to logistical planning instead of amplifying and reflecting change talk to build stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges doubts without reinforcing them and redirects toward small, realistic options and backups. Could further reduce resistance by using more double-sided reflections and reframing sustain talk toward autonomy and past success.
