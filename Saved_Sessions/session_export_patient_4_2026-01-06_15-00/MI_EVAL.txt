# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect through validating statements and nonjudgmental language, normalizing the patient’s distress and affirming her goals.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate, often complex reflections of content and affect, capturing fears, functional impact, and underlying values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on obtaining appropriate medical evaluation and practical steps, guiding the conversation from exploration to a specific, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint problem‑solving by inviting patient input, offering to perform tasks together (drafting, booking, role‑play), and centering the patient’s expertise and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering options rather than directives, asking permission before drafting the letter, and affirming that the patient decides next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and resources (values, strengths, concrete steps) but could deepen and amplify intrinsic motives and discrepancy to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative relationship through shared language and choice questions, though at times moves relatively quickly into solutions without more fully eliciting the patient’s own content ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with choice questions and strength prompts, but would benefit from more aggressive amplification of commitment language and readiness/confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates and redirects sustain talk toward action (referral, letter) without arguing, though could more explicitly use double‑sided reflections and reframing to further reduce sustain language.
