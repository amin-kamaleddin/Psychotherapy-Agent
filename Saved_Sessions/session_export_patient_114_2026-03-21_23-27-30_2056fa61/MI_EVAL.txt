### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizing the patient's feelings and avoiding blame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, validating emotions and asking what would help the patient feel more confident.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing risky drinking, repeatedly advancing concrete behavior-change planning and rehearsal toward that target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative, inviting the patient to choose strategies and rehearsal style and co-constructing plans rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, respecting pacing, and framing suggestions as the patient's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and resources by linking change to personal values and prompting concrete, patient-driven planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the work as a joint enterprise, asking preferences before proceeding and sharing power in decisions about practice and plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, leading the patient to specify realistic steps and strengthening commitment through planning and role-play.
