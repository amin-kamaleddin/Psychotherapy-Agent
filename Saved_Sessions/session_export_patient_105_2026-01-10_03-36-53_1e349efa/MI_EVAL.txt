# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated warmth and unconditional positive regard, normalizing Naomi's experience and avoiding blame throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and validation of Naomi's symptoms, but could deepen empathic work with more complex reflections exploring emotional meaning and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on social/performance anxiety and concrete coping strategies, with exchanges that advanced a pragmatic plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session felt cooperative with invitations to try exercises and negotiate follow-up, though much of the interaction involved clinician-led instruction rather than eliciting Naomi's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Naomi's choice and control, offering options and alternatives and avoiding pressure while emphasizing her ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation and small commitments (breathing, delaying coffee, check-in) but did not sufficiently explore deeper values, past successes, or confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with inclusive language and shared planning, but directive moments and detailed instructions reduced opportunities for Naomi to co-create adaptations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk leading to actionable commitments, yet could more often amplify Naomi's hopes and elicit stronger commitment language and specifics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently normalized difficulty while offering realistic options, but missed chances for double-sided reflections to more actively resolve ambivalence.
