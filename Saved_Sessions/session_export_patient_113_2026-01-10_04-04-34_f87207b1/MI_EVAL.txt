### Empathy

**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Rowan's choice, reduces pressure, and solicits preferences for how the experiment should run (explicitly agreeing not to nag), supporting client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Rowan's solutions (coffee routine, charger placement) and past successes, but could probe deeper into importance/confidence and elicit stronger change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions Rowan as the primary source of solutions and invites collaboration in planning; occasionally nudges toward a clinician‑preferred structure (the three‑morning frame).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces small change talk and turns it into a concrete experiment, affirming problem‑solving; missed chances to amplify 'I’ll try' and deepen commitment with scaling or summary reflections.

**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and reframes the plan as a low‑stakes test, avoiding argument; sometimes repeats sustain phrases rather than using more double‑sided reflections to contrast change.
