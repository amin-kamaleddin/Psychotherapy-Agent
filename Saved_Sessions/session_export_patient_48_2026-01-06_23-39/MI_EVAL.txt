# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalizing statements, creating a warm, nonjudgmental tone that reduced blame and shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used reflective listening and validated experiences well, but reflections were more pragmatic than deeply affective and missed richer reflections of discouragement and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on increasing activity and moved from exploration to a concrete, actionable plan with specific implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructed the plan by soliciting and using the patient's language and preferences at each step, sharing power throughout the interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored the patient's choices, offered options rather than pressure, and framed skips as data to preserve the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's ideas and concrete steps effectively but did not sufficiently probe intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and invited the patient to co-author the plan, repeatedly asking for preferences and integrating their choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific change language and co-created a feasible plan, but missed opportunities to amplify commitment language and explore the patient's underlying motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized and reframed doubts and offered fallback options to reduce shame, though could have used double-sided reflections to more explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
