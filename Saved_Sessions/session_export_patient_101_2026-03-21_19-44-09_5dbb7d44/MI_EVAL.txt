# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, highlights strengths and normalizes low confidence. Language conveys unconditional positive regard without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows perspective-taking and reflective statements and validates feelings, but misses deeper reflections of anxiety and the emotional weight of family history. Could probe affect and ambivalence more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, focused target on reducing risky drinking and improving contraception with concrete, time-bound steps. Repeatedly anchors the session to specific behaviors and tiny achievable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is collaborative with joint problem-solving and frequent solicitation of the patient's input. The clinician implements the patient's choices and co-creates the action plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by offering options, invitational language, and seeking permission before role-plays. Reinforces the patient's ownership of decisions throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own reasons and resources for change and builds strategies around internal motivators, but could probe deeper into values and long-term meaning to strengthen intrinsic motivation. Scaling follow-ups or value-focused questions would enhance evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert and shares power by offering choices and asking which steps to try first. The plan is clearly co-constructed and patient-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with specific, doable steps and commitment prompts, amplifying the patient's change language. Missed opportunities include deeper exploration of values and a confidence-ruler follow-up to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward planning, which is functional, but lacks strategic double-sided reflections or empathic exploration to reduce sustain talk's pull. A more active, nuanced approach would better soften sustain talk.
