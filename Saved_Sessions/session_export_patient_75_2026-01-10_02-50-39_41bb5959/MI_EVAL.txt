# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with warm validation and normalization, avoiding judgment and affirming the patient's feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate understanding and reflective listening (timely reflections, grounding exercise offer), which deepened rapport and helped the patient feel heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on medication adherence and a safe step‑down/inhaler plan, repeatedly orienting the conversation to concrete, actionable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine collaboration by co‑constructing scripts and plans, offering role‑play, and tailoring recommendations based on the patient's details and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient autonomy with a menu of options, asking which she preferred, and encouraging her to use her own language and lead next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited useful motivational material (worries, one‑thing goal, behavioral data) but relied heavily on information‑giving and missed deeper evocation of values, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and shared power by inviting choices, checking permission, and framing actions as joint problem‑solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk through options, scripts, and role‑play, but missed opportunities to amplify and explore the patient's desire, reasons, and confidence for adherence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reframed fears, normalized concerns, and offered alternatives, yet relied more on information than on strategies (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to pivot sustain talk toward change.
