# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opens warmly, uses respectful language, normalizes slips and avoids shaming; however responses are often pragmatic and efficiency-focused with few explicit empathy statements that deeply individualize the adolescent experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows basic understanding through targeted questions and acknowledgment of concerns, but offers few complex or reflective statements to validate ambivalence and feelings, favoring solution-focused moves instead.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on vaping and basketball eligibility throughout the session, with every intervention tied to quitting and a progressive move toward an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction feels like joint problem-solving with permission-seeking and co-created scripts, though some detailed checklists and step orders are clinician-driven rather than entirely client-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly invites choice and offers options while affirming the client's control, but occasional directive language (e.g., 'remove the pen now') slightly undermines full autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and commitment (confidence scaling, personal reasons, alternative strategies), but could use more open, curiosity-driven questions and selective reflections to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by asking permission and integrating client preferences, yet at times slips into directive instructions that make the interaction feel more expert-led than fully shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk through planning and repeated confidence ratings, but could amplify and selectively reflect the client's own change statements to build stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not argue and largely avoids reinforcing sustain talk, but does not actively soften or reframe sustain talk (e.g., double-sided reflections) and instead moves quickly to pragmatic planning.
