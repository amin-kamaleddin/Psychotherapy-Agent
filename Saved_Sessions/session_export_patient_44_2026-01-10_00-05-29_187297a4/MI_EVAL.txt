# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and validation, normalized setbacks, and avoided judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good empathic statements and brief reflections that acknowledged emotions and benefits, but missed opportunities for deeper, affect-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on exercise and diet goals with repeated, concrete implementation steps and adherence strategies that kept the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, co-created plans, invited input, and tailored wording to the patient's preferences, demonstrating strong collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely offered options, framed actions as choices, and accepted the patient's skepticism, supporting his ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some doable options and built on past success, but tended to move quickly into offering tools instead of drawing out the patient's internal motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with patient-selected preferences and tweaks, but frequent clinician-generated plans and scripts slightly shifted the balance toward an expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete commitments and reinforced change talk through planning, yet relied more on proposing solutions than on eliciting the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk via normalizing and reframing and offered Plan B options, but used few double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to explicitly capture ambivalence.
