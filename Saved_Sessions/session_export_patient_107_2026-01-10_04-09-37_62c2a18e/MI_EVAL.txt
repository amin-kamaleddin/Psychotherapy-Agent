# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence through validating language and nonjudgmental framing, supporting a strong rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflections and summaries that capture both sides of the client’s ambivalence with specific details, showing close listening and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on the target behavior (coffee) and consistently steers toward a specific, measurable, low‑stakes experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the client as a partner throughout planning, invites input and choices, and co‑constructs a practical plan that fits the client’s routine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice and control by offering options, framing the trial as low‑stakes, and asking which steps feel most doable to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivations and sensory indicators of benefit with targeted questions, but could probe deeper values and confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert, shares power in decision‑making, and invites collaborative planning that centers the client’s preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces tentative change talk with evocative questions and reflections, yet misses some opportunities to amplify and convert statements into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges and reframes sustain talk without arguing, using double‑sided reflections to reduce threat, though it relies more on reflection than on strategic tipping toward change.
