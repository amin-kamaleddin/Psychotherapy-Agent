# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through validation, normalizing, and nonjudgmental language, reframing misses as information rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, supportive reflections and acknowledges ambivalence, though there were missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of underlying values or emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity and guides the patient toward a specific, measurable, and time-bound plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine joint decision-making, offers ideas while inviting patient input and integrates patient tweaks into the plan.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
