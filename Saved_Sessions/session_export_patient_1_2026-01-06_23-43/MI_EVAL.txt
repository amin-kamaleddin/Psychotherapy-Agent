# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone, offered choices, and validated Ahmed’s perspective and reasons for mini‑cigar use, creating a warm, accepting atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate perspective‑taking were used throughout, with summaries and reflections that showed understanding of both practical and emotional elements; only occasional deeper naming of underlying feelings was missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction stayed clearly focused on mini‑cigar use, moving from assessment to specific goal setting and implementation planning with concrete, behavior‑directed tasks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician co‑designed the plan with Ahmed, invited his input on barriers and solutions, used joint rehearsal, and employed 'we' language to sustain a genuine collaborative problem‑solving stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Permission‑seeking language and option offering were used repeatedly, honoring Ahmed’s choices and pacing and reinforcing his ownership of decisions without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicited Ahmed’s own reasons and motivations (importance/confidence ratings, barriers, supports) and built client‑driven strategies, drawing motivation from within rather than imposing it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed the work as a shared enterprise, deferred to Ahmed’s expertise about his life, offered choices, and directly incorporated Ahmed’s ideas into the plan, demonstrating strong power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation of actionable change talk through scaling questions and planning led to concrete steps, though there were missed micro‑opportunities to amplify and elaborate Ahmed’s emerging change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled skillfully with reflective responses, neutrality, and permissioned information, avoiding argumentation and gently redirecting toward small, practical changes.
