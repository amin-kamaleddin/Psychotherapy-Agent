# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:**  
- Clinician consistently conveys respect and nonjudgmental acceptance; multiple validating statements.  
- Examples from session: “I’m here to listen and support you,” “That sounds really painful — no wonder you’re avoiding school,” and “You don’t have to prove anything — protect your safety and dignity.”

Checklist (marking only criteria explicitly met)

- [x] Validated patient feelings and experience

- [x] Maintained respectful tone throughout

### Empathy

Checklist

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:**  
- Secured concrete behavioral commitments (send text, role‑play, breathing, go to office, tell mom).  
- Did not systematically elicit intrinsic motivations, desire, reasons, or confidence language to strengthen internal commitment.

Checklist
- [x] Elicited behavioral commitment/intention (patient agreed to actions)
- [ ] Actively elicited and amplified intrinsic change talk (desire/reasons/need)
- [ ] Used scaling/confidence or values-based questions to deepen commitment

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:**  
- Clinician avoided amplifying sustain talk and validated avoidance as understandable; redirected toward action and safety.  
- Missed opportunities for strategic double-sided reflections or exploring costs/benefits of avoidance vs. approach to weaken sustain talk.

Checklist
- [x] Avoided reinforcing sustain talk/blaming language
- [x] Redirected conversation toward safety and concrete action
- [ ] Used double-sided reflections or explored pros/cons to strategically soften sustain talk
