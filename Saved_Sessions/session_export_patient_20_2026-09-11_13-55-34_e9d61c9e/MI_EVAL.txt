# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence with warm, nonjudgmental language that affirms the patient's competence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and perspective-taking around ambivalence and ritual concerns, though reflections generally remain at a surface level without deeper affective exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing weekday drinking and repeatedly guides the conversation toward concrete, measurable first steps and contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is genuinely collaborative—preferences are solicited and the plan emerges from the patient's ideas with clinician facilitation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes the patient's choice and expertise by offering options rather than directives and inviting decisions that reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out patient-generated options and commitments resulting in concrete change language, but could deepen evocation by exploring personal values and amplifying benefits.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as a partnership and defers to the patient's expertise, though frequent multiple-choice prompts sometimes constrain spontaneous contributions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits specific change talk and actionable steps (routine, day/time/place), but would benefit from stronger selective reflections and summaries to build momentum toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and does not reinforce sustain talk, yet misses opportunities to use double-sided reflections or summaries to actively soften sustain talk and help resolve ambivalence.
