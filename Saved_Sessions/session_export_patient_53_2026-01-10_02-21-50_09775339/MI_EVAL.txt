# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through validations and normalization of ambivalence, with no evidence of confrontation or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured both content and feeling and mirrored the client's language to validate ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on the target behavior (engaging in community activities) and consistently steered toward concrete, incremental steps and actionable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as equal partners co-creating a plan, building on the client's suggestions and negotiating steps rather than prescribing them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used invitational, noncoercive phrasing and explicitly acknowledged the client's choice and right to decline, reinforcing agency and ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited client motives, past successes, and doable steps, but could probe strength/importance/confidence more and more actively amplify emerging change talk toward commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed the work as joint and invitational, asked preference-based questions, and co-constructed the plan while avoiding an expert-driven stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific preparatory change talk and linked past success to capability, but did not consistently amplify or convert these statements into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk using double-sided reflections and avoided arguing for change, but often mirrored sustain language rather than selectively reframing to reduce its pull.
