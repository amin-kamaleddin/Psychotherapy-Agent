# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and explicitly endorsing the patient’s pace and imperfections.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides frequent, accurate reflections that capture content and some feeling, but misses deeper exploration of emotions and the personal meaning of being “not thrilled.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on specific target behaviors and moves logically to a single, concrete rule with supports and implementation details without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is a genuine partnership: the clinician invites the patient’s preferences, offers options, and jointly constructs the plan rather than prescribing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the patient’s freedom to choose, avoids pressuring language, and checks that the plan fits the patient’s limits and preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits pragmatic ideas and a concrete plan, but could more strongly draw out intrinsic motivations, values, and confidence to deepen internalized change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates shared problem-solving and power‑sharing through language like “we can brainstorm” and by incorporating the patient’s concrete preferences into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and develops concrete change language and commitments, but could better amplify personal reasons, benefits, and confidence to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently neutralizes and reframes sustain talk, validating the patient’s concerns and integrating them into feasible planning rather than confronting or correcting.
