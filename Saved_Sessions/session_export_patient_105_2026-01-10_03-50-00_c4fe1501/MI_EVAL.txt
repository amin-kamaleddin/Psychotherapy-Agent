# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician used warm, nonjudgmental language and repeatedly validated Naomi's experience, honoring her hesitation and avoiding blame or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections and summaries demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and mirrored Naomi's emotions and physical sensations, helping her feel understood before moving to strategies.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on managing anxiety during photography jobs with concrete, behavior-linked goals and successive summaries that maintained momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician and Naomi rehearsed and iterated together, continuously soliciting her preferences so decisions were co-constructed and Naomi's ideas shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently offered choices, asked permission before practices, normalized uncertainty, and emphasized Naomi's ownership of the plan without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited willingness and noticed readiness shifts using open questions and scaling, but could have drawn out deeper values, reasons, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Overall collaborative stance was evident through choices and co-creation, but at times the clinician became more prescriptive (specific breath counts and scripted wording) which shifted some control away from Naomi.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was effectively evoked and reinforced via rehearsal and scaling and progress was highlighted, yet the clinician could have probed more for underlying reasons and explicit commitment steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The therapist acknowledged Naomi's doubts but did limited active work to reduce sustain talk, relying more on strategy offers than reframing or eliciting past successes to soften resistance.
