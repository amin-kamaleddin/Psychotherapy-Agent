# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and individualized validation, normalizing the patient's feelings and avoiding judgment to create a safe, nonjudgmental atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used repeated, accurate reflections and paraphrasing of the patient's experience (e.g., overwhelm, grogginess), mirroring affect and helping the patient feel heard and disclose a small success.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on morning glucose checking with concrete, measurable experiments and follow-up, repeatedly steering the conversation back to a specific behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in genuine team-work language, solicited the patient's preferences, co-constructed the experiment and troubleshooting options, and planned follow-up together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed the patient's choice, framed tasks as low-pressure experiments and information (not pass/fail), and invited the patient to select and tweak micro-steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivation and a specific trial, but leaned toward offering solutions rather than using open, values-oriented questions to draw out the patient's internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance by offering options, asking which micro-step felt least awful, and inviting the patient to co-tweak the plan, sharing power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited small, actionable change talk and commitments (e.g., meter-in-hand/alarm), but missed opportunities to deepen and amplify personal reasons and success language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently avoided reinforcing sustain talk, validated resistance, reframed tasks as manageable experiments, and used micro-goals to reduce defensiveness and facilitate action.
