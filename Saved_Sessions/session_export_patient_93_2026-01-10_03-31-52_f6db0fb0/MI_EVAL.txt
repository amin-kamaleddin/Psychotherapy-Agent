# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and normalization of the client's stance, offering support rather than blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good understanding and reflective listening but missed opportunities for deeper, emotionally nuanced or double-sided reflections to capture ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use and harm, anchoring the session to concrete, behaviorally specific goals and follow-up plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as collaborative problem-solving—inviting choices, co-designing a feasible plan, and adapting to the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported client choice by offering options, seeking permission, honoring preferences, and avoiding coercion in planning and implementation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation effectively through questions and a tracking trial, but could deepen intrinsic motivation with more open-ended, client-elicited exploration of values and reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invitational, though at times clinician-provided strategies felt somewhat prescriptive rather than eliciting the client's own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and small commitments, but could better amplify and follow up on the client's change statements to deepen desire, reasons, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and pivoted to harm-reduction options effectively, though more strategic double-sided reflections could further diffuse sustain talk and highlight discrepancy.
