# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accepting and nonjudgmental, validating Ethan's experience and allowing him to set the agenda while normalizing his desire to change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear empathic understanding with reflective phrasing and validation, but reflections were often surface-level and missed deeper ambivalence and emotional drivers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on weekend drinking and harm-reduction throughout, tying each intervention to concrete steps to reduce harm.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered collaboration through permission and role-play, yet at times relied more on clinician-provided solutions instead of co-creating strategies with Ethan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking preferences, and encouraging doable options while avoiding pressure or mandates.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Ethan's own solutions and commitments (choice of strategies, role-play) but could have further linked change to personal values and used scaling to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as collaborative and invited input, but often offered expert-driven prescriptions rather than eliciting and refining Ethan's own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged and reinforced change talk and secured concrete commitments, though could have more frequently reflected and amplified change statements and probed for ability and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward concrete steps, but used few double-sided reflections and sometimes shifted quickly into advice instead of rolling with ambivalence.
