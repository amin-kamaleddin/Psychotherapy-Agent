# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating throughout the session, explicitly honoring Marcus's attachment to his dog and avoiding blame; demonstrates warm, unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows solid reflective listening and acknowledges overwhelm and financial strain, but reflections are brief and the clinician moves quickly to problem‑solving, missing opportunities for deeper emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on improving asthma management with realistic, low‑cost steps and repeatedly returns the conversation to concrete, goal‑directed tasks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a collaborative tone (role‑plays, scripts, rehearsals) and invites choice, but much of the content is clinician‑provided rather than co‑generated from Marcus's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Marcus's sense of choice and control, respects his decision about the dog, offers options, and asks permission before proceeding.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change language but relies more on offering solutions than drawing out Marcus's internal motivations; limited open, evocative questions or scaling to deepen intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance and asks permission for help, yet retains an expert role with concrete directives; could better elicit Marcus's own ideas and priorities to co‑create the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces several concrete change statements and feasible small steps that led to commitments, though could more often amplify Marcus's own change language and explore personal reasons/values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and offers pet‑friendly alternatives that acknowledge sustain talk, but missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections to both validate reluctance and gently steer toward change.
