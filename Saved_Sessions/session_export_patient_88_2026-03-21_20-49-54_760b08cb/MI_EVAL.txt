### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment through warm, permission-based language and frequent affirmations; normalizes ambivalence and respects Marcus’s choices.

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on weekend drinking and harm-reduction, guiding the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong collaboration by inviting Marcus’s preferences, co-constructing the plan, and framing the interaction as joint problem-solving rather than clinician-led instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces choice through permission language, offers multiple low-effort options, and validates partial commitments to support Marcus’s sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Marcus’s own motivations, reasons, and strategies (change talk, confidence rating, concrete plans) and draws out resources rather than supplying arguments for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treats Marcus as the expert on his life by asking which options feel easiest, seeking consent, and sharing power in deciding strategies and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces several change statements and commitment-relevant language (plans, confidence ruler), but would benefit from more active amplification and summarizing of change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
