# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard and normalization of fear throughout (e.g., “That nervousness is normal…”, “I accept the fear and respect the courage in trying”). Multiple validating reflections preserved dignity and reduced shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective‑taking and frequent affective reflections (e.g., repeated reflections of ambivalence, linking past success to current plan). Reflections integrated content and feeling, deepening understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear focus on the targeted behavior (starting study session). Clinician guided agenda-setting and used concrete planning (time, breathing routine, friend check‑in, 0–10 confidence ruler) without imposing decisions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance (“Let’s treat you as the expert…”). Decisions and specifics were co‑constructed (friend wording, timing, alarm, voice note), preserving client ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeated invitations for patient choice and preference (which idea feels most realistic, what friend should say, which support would nudge confidence). Clinician avoided directives and emphasized Hana’s expertise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful elicitation of Hana’s motivations and resources (past success with breaking tasks). Used targeted questions to draw out change talk and concrete supports (voice note, alarm, notes by couch).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power shared throughout; clinician solicited Hana’s solutions and refined them collaboratively (timing, exact wording for friend, safety plan), reinforcing ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently amplified and reinforced Hana’s change language (desire to prove follow‑through, reclaim “lightness”). Used the 0–10 confidence question and asked what would raise it — classic evocation moves.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed into manageable steps (safety plan, small blocks). Minor drift toward mild persuasion (“I’ll bet that even attempting it will feel like progress”)—effective here but slightly less pure MI; otherwise appropriately softened.
