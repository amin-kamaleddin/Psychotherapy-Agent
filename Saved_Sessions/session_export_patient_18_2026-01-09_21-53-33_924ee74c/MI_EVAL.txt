# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing statements and supportive affirmations, avoiding judgment or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and meaning without imposing interpretation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on managing occasional drinking and peer pressure and progressively moves the conversation to a concrete, measurable practice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner by offering to co-create phrases, asking permission to work on strategies, and letting the patient select the practice to try.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly reinforces the patient's sense of choice with no pressure, offering options and asking which strategy she prefers to pursue.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivations, reasons, and strategies through open questions and reflections, leading to a self-directed practice plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invites joint problem-solving by soliciting preferences and framing interventions as collaborative brainstorming rather than directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, reflecting and exploring the patient's reasons and strengths and guiding toward specific, doable actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence, though could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge social pulls while steering toward change.
