# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Rosa’s feelings, avoiding judgment and creating a safe tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses solid empathic statements and some reflections that acknowledge fears and shakiness, but often moves quickly to information rather than exploring deeper emotional nuance or using complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on smoking cessation and ties each intervention (medication options, coping strategies, role-play) directly to the quit goal while pacing suggestions appropriately.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as a joint enterprise by inviting preferences, offering role-play and scripts, and repeatedly asking Rosa to practice and choose options, demonstrating consistent power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports Rosa’s control and pace by offering choices, avoiding pressure, and affirming her right to delay a quit date while providing resources and options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some preferences and willingness to practice but misses repeated evocative open questions to draw out Rosa’s intrinsic motivations, values, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites input and shares decision-making through scripting and practice, but at times shifts to clinician-led teaching instead of co-constructing solutions from Rosa’s own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to prompt change talk and offers concrete steps, but misses opportunities to elicit, amplify, and summarize Rosa’s own reasons, desires, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and provides practical strategies, but relies mainly on informational responses rather than using double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to gently reframe sustain talk.
