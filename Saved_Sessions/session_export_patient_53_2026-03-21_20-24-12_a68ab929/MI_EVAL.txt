# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validates the client's experience, and praises concrete efforts using nonjudgmental language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and summaries that capture ambivalence and the client's emotional perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and purposefully guides the session toward concrete next steps while staying client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructs plans, invites the client's preferences at each decision point, and engages in practical shared tasks, showing strong two-way partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly emphasizes choice and control, offers options rather than directives, and respects pacing to reinforce client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits preparatory change talk and uses scaling effectively but could probe more for deeper, personally meaningful reasons for change to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance throughout, asks permission for interventions, and privileges the client's expertise and decisions in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and affirms small steps and commitments with concrete suggestions and scaling, but missed opportunities to amplify values-driven motivations and follow up on fleeting change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change with reflections and reframing, though more strategic double-sided reflections and selective summaries could further tip ambivalence toward change.
