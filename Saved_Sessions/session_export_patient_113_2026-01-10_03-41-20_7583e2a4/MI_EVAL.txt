# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validates Rowan’s priorities, and normalizes reluctance without criticism, modeling strong acceptance throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician accurately reflects Rowan’s perspective and asks about his worries, showing clear understanding, though deeper, more complex reflections of ambivalence would strengthen empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on charging and carrying the device and consistently moves toward a specific, actionable plan (adhesive pouch + power bank for a week).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites Rowan’s preferences and co-creates a plan with offers to check in, but the interaction leans toward clinician-provided solutions rather than first eliciting Rowan’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician strongly supports choice by offering options, honoring the 'no promises' boundary, and leaving the final decision and pace to Rowan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicits a key worry that produces change-relevant language but moves quickly into problem-solving instead of systematically drawing out Rowan’s own motivations or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician frames the interaction as collaborative and invites input, but provides many concrete recommendations rather than first building on Rowan’s generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits and affirms tentative change talk and uses low-effort strategies to reinforce commitment, though opportunities remain to further amplify desire, ability, and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing, normalizes sustain talk, and reframes the plan as a low-pressure experiment, but does not consistently use double-sided reflections to explicitly juxtapose sustain and change talk.
