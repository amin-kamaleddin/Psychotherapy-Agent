### Direction

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with repeated invitations for Ryan's choices, but frequently offering specific strategies shifts some control to the clinician; eliciting Ryan's ideas earlier would strengthen partnership.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through values and confidence scaling, producing concrete change language; could more selectively reflect and magnify tentative change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
