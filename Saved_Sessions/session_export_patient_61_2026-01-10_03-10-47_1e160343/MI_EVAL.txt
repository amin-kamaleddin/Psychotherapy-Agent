# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Jonathan's experience with frequent affirmations and validations. No blaming or minimization was present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, sustained reflective listening that mirrored the patient's language and linked feelings to values, showing deep perspective-taking. Complex reflections tracked ambivalence and kept Jonathan feeling heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the concrete target behavior (one lunchtime glucose check) and systematically narrowed choices to a feasible plan. Kept the session goal-oriented without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session felt like joint problem-solving with the clinician and patient co-constructing a practical plan. Could strengthen collaboration by explicitly inviting the patient to set the agenda or summarize his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed options, validated reluctance, and invited the patient's choice, reinforcing his control over decisions. Language avoided pressure and supported pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out internal motivations and a relevant past success, linking change to values. Missed opportunities to transfer that past success into explicit confidence-building or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance and sought the patient's input throughout the session. At times offered specific suggestions rather than explicitly endorsing the patient's expertise, which slightly reduced partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, producing multiple patient change statements and practical ideas. Did not consistently amplify those statements into commitment language or elicit readiness/confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Consistently reflected sustain talk with empathic reflections but often neutralized rather than strategically redirected it. Limited use of double-sided reflections or reframes to soften resistance and shift toward change.
