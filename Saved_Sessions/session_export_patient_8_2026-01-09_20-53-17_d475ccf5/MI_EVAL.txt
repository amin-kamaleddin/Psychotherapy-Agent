# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrates strong unconditional positive regard through normalization and nonjudgmental language, consistently acknowledging the patient's feelings and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely reflections and double-sided summaries that deepen the patient's self-exploration and validate emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear focus on reducing problematic phone use and moves toward actionable, specific, time-limited steps (e.g., nightly muting, phone outside bedroom, one-week test).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician genuinely collaborates, soliciting the patient's preferences, co-constructing practical setup details, and negotiating decisions rather than issuing directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice by offering options, framing the plan as a voluntary test, and normalizing slips to reduce pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits values and past successes to draw out motivation, but could deepen intrinsic motive by using scaling and linking sleep/grades more directly to long-term personal goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert, asking which options feel doable and incorporating her prior strategies and preferences into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and elicits concrete tests, though there were missed opportunities to amplify commitment language and use scaling to strengthen resolve before planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation and redirects toward tradeoffs and tests, but uses few double-sided reflections that explicitly link sustain reasons to values before pivoting to change.
