# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing the patient's perspective and avoiding moralizing language, which conveyed unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate validating reflections that acknowledged Marcus's guardedness and viewpoint, but sometimes moved quickly into information-giving, limiting deeper reflective exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on adolescent alcohol use and harm reduction, returning to concrete behaviors and closing with a specific, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a joint project with negotiated advice, co-created plans, and mutual role-play, demonstrating genuine partnership in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized choice by offering options, accepting limits, and adapting recommendations to Marcus's preferences, supporting his sense of control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out Marcus's ideas and intentions and invited choices, but at times relied on prescriptive tip lists instead of evocative questions to deepen intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Marcus as the expert on his life, offered options, and co-created strategies while adapting language to his style, reflecting authentic power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced specific change language and asked for concrete steps, but missed chances to amplify ambivalence into stronger desire/reason/need/ability/confidence talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk, using harm-reduction rather than moralizing, but did not consistently use double-sided reflections or highlight discrepancies to gently shift sustain language.
