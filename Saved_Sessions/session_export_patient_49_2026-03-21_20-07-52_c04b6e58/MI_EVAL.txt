# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician opened with an explicit nonjudgmental stance and maintained a respectful, affirming tone throughout, normalizing Hana’s pace and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening that validated Hana’s experience, though reflections were mostly paraphrase and some phrasing (e.g., “Quick questions”) felt slightly rushed and missed deeper emotional naming.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on the behavioral goal of cutting down at work, with every question and suggestion linked to concrete, actionable steps (gum, alarm, stash, backups).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was clearly co-constructed with Hana, soliciting and integrating her preferences at each step, demonstrating strong two-way problem-solving and shared ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician honored Hana’s choice and pace by asking permission, offering multiple options, and repeatedly asking which approach felt most doable, reinforcing her control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician drew motivation and solutions from Hana with evocative questions and scaling, eliciting concrete commitment language and actionable change statements (alarm, gum choice, reward).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and respectful with permission before advice, though an extensive early menu of options slightly risked slipping into an expert role despite subsequently handing choice back to Hana.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited and reinforced change talk through open questions, scaling, and concrete commitments, but could have amplified Hana’s motivations and strengths more to deepen change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward manageable steps, but missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections or explicitly normalize ambivalence when subtle sustain talk arose.
