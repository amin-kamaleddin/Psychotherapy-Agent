# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opens nonjudgmentally, validates Dylan's feelings, and avoids shaming throughout the session. Acceptance feels somewhat procedural and scripted rather than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and reflective listening, and elicits Dylan's priorities (e.g., penalties). Reflections generally stayed at the situational level and missed opportunities to explore deeper affect like shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on preparing Dylan for the conduct hearing with concrete, actionable interventions. Every element—options, scripts, and role-plays—remains targeted to the specific behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Dylan to choose scenarios and practices with him, creating a team approach. Collaboration is reduced somewhat by frequent provision of exact phrasings rather than co-constructing responses from Dylan's language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks permission to practice, and honors Dylan's boundaries about admissions of guilt. The clinician refrains from pressuring and supports Dylan's control over responses.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies on clinician-generated scripts and solutions instead of drawing out Dylan's own motivations or values. Missed opportunities to pursue Dylan's statement about penalties to evoke change talk or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acts as a partner by offering options, practicing together, and soliciting preferences. The partnering effect is weakened at times by an expert/teacher stance and directive scripting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited a single change-relevant statement about penalties but did not amplify or develop it. The session prioritized scripted denials and rehearsal over asking questions that would elicit Dylan's reasons or commitment to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoided arguing but frequently reinforced sustain-talk through coaching defensive denials and rehearsed wording. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections, reframing, or highlighting discrepancy to soften sustain talk.
