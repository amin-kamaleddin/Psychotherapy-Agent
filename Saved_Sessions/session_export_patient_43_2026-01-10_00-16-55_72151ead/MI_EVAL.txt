# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing slips and affirming autonomy throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and perspective-taking validated the patient's experience and struggles, though many reflections stayed surface-level and could probe deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session remained clearly focused on smoking and progressed logically from exploration to a specific, practical behavior-change experiment with concrete supports and timelines.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed and patient preferences guided choices, but frequent clinician-provided options sometimes shifted the interaction toward clinician-led problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician strongly supported choice and permission, avoided pressuring pharmacotherapy, and reinforced the patient's ownership of a small, self-directed experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited (importance/confidence scales, pros/cons), but the clinician moved quickly to strategies and missed opportunities to explore deeper personal reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A collaborative stance was maintained with regular invitations for input and negotiated follow-up, although the clinician occasionally led the menu of options rather than eliciting the patient's own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicited modest change talk and used scaling questions, but did not consistently amplify or follow up on the patient's reasons for change to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and gently redirected sustain talk with low-effort experiments and double-focus statements, but could use more strategic double-sided reflections linking sustain talk to change reasons.
