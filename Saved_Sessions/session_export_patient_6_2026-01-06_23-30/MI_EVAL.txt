# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, using validation and normalization without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate reflections that acknowledge stress and worry; could use more complex, affect-focused reflections and summaries of ambivalence to deepen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol prevention and safety, guiding the session toward concrete, actionable steps and resisting drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as a team with the patient by soliciting input, co-creating the plan, and checking back frequently, showing equitable two-way engagement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly asks permission, offers options, and affirms the patient's choices; advice is presented after permission and reinforces the patient's control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation by linking strategies to the patient's priorities and inviting choices, but leans on structured planning; could use more open questions and reflections to draw out intrinsic reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters power-sharing through invitations to rehearse, choose strategies, and co-construct the plan, demonstrating respect for the adolescent's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encourages and amplifies change talk via permission, reinforcement, and rehearsal, strengthening commitment language; missed opportunities to elicit the patient's own verbalized reasons tied explicitly to values like soccer or scholarships.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and uses curious, nonjudgmental questions to reduce sustain talk, then redirects to planning; could strengthen by using double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while linking it to change reasons.
