# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and validating feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflections that capture both sides of ambivalence, name emotions and stakes, and link past experience to current motivation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol risk to soccer/scholarship and moves logically from values exploration to concrete planning without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative: the clinician repeatedly invites input and co-constructs the plan, including drafting the exact text with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking which feels doable, and affirming the patient’s decisions while avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient’s own reasons and motivations for change (e.g., protecting soccer future) and draws out self-generated plans and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert and shares power in decision-making through open invitations, options, and joint planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and secures concrete commitments, but could more explicitly amplify, summarize, or use scaling to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and respectfully reframes minimization, though more double-sided reflections or exploration of sustain-talk function would strengthen the approach.
