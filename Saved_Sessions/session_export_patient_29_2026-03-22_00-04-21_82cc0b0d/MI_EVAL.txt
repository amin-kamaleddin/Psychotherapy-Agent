# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validation, normalizing difficulty and avoiding shame or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with frequent reflections and summaries that capture both content and emotional tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking cessation with concrete, time-bound steps (medication interest, triggers, rehearsal, and a specific small test).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a genuine joint enterprise: asks permission, invites patient input, and follows the patient's lead in shaping the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices and affirms the patient's control over decisions, using non-directive language throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits concrete, patient-generated plans but misses opportunities to draw out deeper values, reasons, and scaled importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power with the patient by ceding decision-making about content and sequencing and co-creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces preparatory change talk with specific planning and rehearsals, but could probe reasons and confidence to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to coping strategies, though could more directly reflect ambivalence (e.g., double-sided reflections) to soften sustain talk further.
