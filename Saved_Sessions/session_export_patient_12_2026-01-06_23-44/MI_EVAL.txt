# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating (e.g., opening warmth, multiple affirmations, normalizing shame), conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured Amira’s overwhelm and exhaustion, though a few deeper, more complex reflections probing the core of helplessness would strengthen empathy to a top score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, goal-directed focus on reducing substance use and a concrete first step (scheduling the support-line call) while balancing emotion and planning without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong shared decision-making: invites input, negotiates specifics (call timing, check-in method), adapts to preferences, and co-constructs the plan with Amira.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces choice and control (asks permission, offers options, avoids pressure), supporting Amira’s autonomy in deciding next steps and check-in preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits important values and some reasons for change (protecting housing/job) but stops short of systematically deepening intrinsic motivation or using scaling and follow-up evocation questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative, treating Amira as the expert on her life, asking which steps feel least overwhelming and offering help only as requested, demonstrating strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk and small-step options that produced commitment to a call, but misses opportunities to amplify, reflect, and scale change language to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk without confrontation, but largely accepts it rather than using double-sided reflections, reframing, or selectively highlighting change-preparatory statements to reduce sustain talk's impact.
