# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician projected respect and nonjudgmental support (opening “I’m here to support you,” validations, explicit respect for the dog). Minor drift to evaluative praise (“I’m proud…”) that risks sounding paternalistic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and summarizing of stressors; acknowledged emotions and financial/appointment burdens. Missed opportunities for deeper affect reflection (e.g., exploring the dog’s emotional role).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear behavioral focus (med refills, breathing technique, ER safety). Produced a concrete, agreed-upon plan (call CVS with script, pursed‑lip breathing, ER if needed).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited patient input and preferences, offered choices, and framed problem‑solving as joint (asked which option felt doable; asked permission before drafting script).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported patient control (did not press dog issue, offered alternatives). Reinforced ownership of decisions with options rather than mandates.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of motivation (asked what felt urgent; patient voiced change talk), but clinician moved quickly to problem‑solving without deeper exploration of values, importance, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functioned as collaborator, offering scripts and troubleshooting. Slight drift toward clinician approval and instruction rather than continuously eliciting patient expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and converted some change talk into action (patient agreed to call), but missed opportunities to amplify change talk with reflective amplification, scaling, or follow‑up evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk (did not pressure on dog; offered nonthreatening alternatives). Could have used more double‑sided reflections or explicit reframing to resolve ambivalence verbally.
