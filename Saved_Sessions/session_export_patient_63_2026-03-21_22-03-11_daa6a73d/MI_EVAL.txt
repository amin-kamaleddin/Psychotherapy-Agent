# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated consistent unconditional positive regard and normalization, creating a respectful, nonjudgmental atmosphere throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking to capture ambivalence, though there were missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflections (shame, fear of losing friends).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on preventing shoplifting with concrete if-then planning and linked strategies to the court mandate to keep the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan with the client by offering options, asking preferences, and negotiating rehearsal and steps, reflecting a true collaborative approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed options, respected the client’s choices, and avoided pressure, reinforcing the client's ownership of decisions and alternatives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intentions and used a confidence ruler and open questions to draw out motivation, but could more directly probe personal values and reasons for change to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing by asking permission, offering choices, and centering the client's preferences when building the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and moved toward planning, but could further amplify and explore the client's stated reasons and desired outcomes for stopping.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk (peer pressure, desire for items) but tended to redirect quickly into solutions rather than using double-sided reflections or strategic techniques to soften sustain talk.
