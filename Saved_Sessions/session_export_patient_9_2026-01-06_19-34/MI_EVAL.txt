# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and frequent validation. No blaming or dismissiveness, meeting the highest standard for acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the client's words and checking meaning. Reflections capture ambivalence and emotional tone, showing strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the concrete target behavior (bedtime routine) and repeatedly returns to measurable outcomes. Narrows planning toward an immediate, doable experiment with strong directional consistency.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative, using inclusive language and soliciting the client's preferences at each step. Recommendations are offered within the client's values and constraints and the plan is co-created rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and control, offers options, and frames steps as within the client's control. Avoids pressure and supports the client's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivations and client-generated plans (love for son, need for breathing room), producing value-based change talk. Could deepen evocation by probing confidence/commitment, eliciting past successes, and exploring obstacles more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the session as a joint exploration, invites the client's expertise, and shares power in decision-making. Co-constructs the plan with the client and offers options rather than directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change statements and reinforces client-generated goals, producing specific, doable steps. Missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate change talk more explicitly (e.g., reflective summaries, scaling confidence/importance).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects attention to what the client can control, framing the plan as an experiment. Could further weaken sustain talk by using double-sided reflections, rolling with resistance, and exploring coping strategies for the father's non-cooperation.
