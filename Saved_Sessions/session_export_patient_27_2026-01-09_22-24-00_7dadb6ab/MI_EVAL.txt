# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth (e.g., opening line, normalizing reflections, supportive praise). No pathologizing or blaming language observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening that validated ambivalence and specific emotional concerns. Reflections and invitations showed sustained understanding of Daniela's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on disclosure and linked every intervention to that goal. Progressed logically toward rehearsed action and practical strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine joint enterprise: co-constructed scripts, negotiated wording, and iteratively refined role-plays with Daniela. Decisions and pacing were shared rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Daniela's choice and readiness, offered options, and respected her pace and decisions. Checked readiness before role-play and framed actions as her decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical motivations and some commitment language through planning and role-play. Could strengthen evocation by eliciting deeper values, asking why disclosure matters, and using scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly fostered client expertise and agency by asking Daniela to choose content and pace. At times adopted an advisory stance by offering specific scripts without first asking permission or exploring her own phrasing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change-oriented language and encouraged rehearsal and concrete planning. Missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic reasons for disclosure and to assess importance/confidence to amplify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward planning and coping, using role-plays to address fears. Rarely used double-sided reflections or explicitly explored costs of not sharing to deepen ambivalence resolution.
