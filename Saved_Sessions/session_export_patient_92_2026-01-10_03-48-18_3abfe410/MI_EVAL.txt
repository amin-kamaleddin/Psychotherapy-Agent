# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental regard through warm, validating language and normalization; there were no dismissive or shaming statements.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and timely reflections captured both the patient's content and affect, enhancing rapport and matching his request for straight facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on weekend drinking that moved systematically from assessment and AUDIT‑C feedback to concrete, small-step harm-reduction strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was a joint exploration with shared decision-making—asking permission, offering choices, inviting role‑play, and letting the patient shape the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly reinforced the patient's control by offering options, asking permission before information, and normalizing tentative testing rather than pressuring change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician drew out motivations and ideas (confidence, barriers, tactics) but at times shifted into information delivery rather than deeper open-ended questions linking change to values or long-term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative stance—co‑created strategies, offered choices, and treated the patient as an equal partner in deciding next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced tentative change language and asked about confidence and barriers, but missed some opportunities to amplify and probe for stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and used permissioned, factual feedback to counter minimization, though could have employed more double-sided reflections to strategically soften sustain talk.
