# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, warm acceptance via validations, normalizing relapse, and thanking the patient, avoiding blame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely empathic reflections and perspective-taking that mirrored the patient's affect and helped sustain disclosure and engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, goal-directed focus on substance use and immediate coping, progressing logically from safety to triggers to a concrete 15‑minute plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner, co-constructing the plan, offering options, and soliciting the patient's input and modifications throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, honoring refusals, and emphasizing voluntary engagement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited small commitments (breaths, delay, text) but did not sufficiently probe for intrinsic reasons, values, or past successes to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative stance: invited choices, shared power, and centered the patient's preferences when developing and refining coping steps and texts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created multiple opportunities and reinforced small commitments but missed follow-up evocative questions to deepen and amplify the patient's change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to coping strategies effectively, though rarely used double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly contrast sustain and change talk.
