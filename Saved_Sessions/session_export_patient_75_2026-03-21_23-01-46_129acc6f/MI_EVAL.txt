# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes Linda's experience, and uses warm, non‑shaming language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, complex reflections capture both content and emotion and appear to deepen rapport and trust with the patient.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (a cautious inhaler trial) and ties questions and reflections to actionable steps and outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is strongly collaborative—clinician solicits preferences, adapts to responses, and co‑designs the plan with the patient as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirms choice, offers multiple options rather than directives, asks permission to draft wording, and invites the patient to review and control decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicits Linda's own values and reasons for change (sleep, safety, fewer flare‑ups) and builds on those motivations to design a feasible trial.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician treats Linda as the expert on her life, co‑creates the plan, and invites detailed patient input (exact wording, side‑effect list) throughout the encounter.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits and reinforces several strands of change talk and offers concrete options, but misses opportunities to summarize and amplify the patient's own arguments into explicit commitment or confidence measures.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Psychiatrist reframes concerns into actionable safeguards and avoids reinforcing sustain talk, but could more strategically use double‑sided reflections and brief amplifications of change reasons to further reduce sustain talk's pull.
