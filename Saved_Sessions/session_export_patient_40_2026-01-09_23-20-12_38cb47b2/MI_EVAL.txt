# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and validation with multiple affirmations and normalization, avoiding blame or shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates a warm empathic tone and brief reflections that validate fear and shame and checks safety, but reflections are often short with missed opportunities for deeper emotional probing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on relapse risk and immediate safety throughout assessment, safety planning, and arranging follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative: co-creates the safety plan, drafts scripts together, repeatedly asks preferences, and stays aligned with the patient's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and honoring Elias's decisions about contacts and actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete steps and plans but does not sufficiently probe internal reasons, values, or importance/confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions the encounter as collaborative with shared decision-making and offers, though could more explicitly foreground Elias's expertise and reflections.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces protective actions and offers praise but does not systematically elicit or amplify Elias's desire, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to safety, but misses opportunities for double-sided reflections to gently surface and resolve ambivalence.
