# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—explicitly reassures no judgment, normalizes slips, and affirms realistic choices which reduced defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and validation of the patient’s perspective, but reflections remain mostly surface-level and could probe deeper emotions or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear behavioral focus on a specific, measurable target (swapping sugary drinks) and moves logically from goal selection to planning and check‑in without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative with joint problem‑solving, frequent solicitation of the patient’s input, and respect for the patient as a co‑expert in implementation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control through invitational language, explicit statements of autonomy, and honoring the patient’s preferences for check‑ins and reminders.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change reasons, options, and commitment plans but could more fully evoke intrinsic motives, values, and past successes to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power and co‑constructs the plan by offering options, asking which is doable, and negotiating contingencies and check‑in format with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk by offering specific options, asking for when/where and confidence ratings, and reinforcing the patient’s stated commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and skillfully pivots from sustain talk to manageable options, reframing ambivalence into a small experiment and reducing defensiveness.
