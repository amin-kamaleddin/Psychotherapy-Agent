# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through validation, normalizing ambivalence, nonjudgmental language, and affirming the client's priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Often used accurate reflections and perspective-taking to validate the client's concerns and summarize desires, but could deepen affective reflections to capture underlying emotions like anxiety or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on cutting back drinking to protect job, family, and sleep, progressing systematically to a specific, time-bound two-week experiment with concrete steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving: the clinician asked open questions, elicited client ideas, and integrated those ideas (buddy system, tracking) while checking feasibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supported the client's sense of choice and avoided coercion by validating preferences and soliciting decisions, though occasional directive language edged toward persuasion and could be softened with more explicit autonomy statements.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from the client by asking about past successes, eliciting concrete reasons and measurable benefits, and expanding the client's own change talk and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative and invited co-creation of solutions, using client-generated strategies to share power and shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, prompting for past successes and specifics, and guided the client toward articulating a concrete, measurable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reflected sustain talk while redirecting toward experimentation, but could more proactively use double-sided reflections and explicit discrepancy work to reduce sustain talk.
