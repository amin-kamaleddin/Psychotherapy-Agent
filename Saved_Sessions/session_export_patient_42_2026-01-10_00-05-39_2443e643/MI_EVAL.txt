# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validation, normalizing Elise’s experience and avoiding judgment to create a safe, nonshaming atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, acknowledging ambivalence and low confidence; could deepen connection with more complex reflections and emotional summaries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with interventions tied to the target behavior and a pragmatic, testable plan (e.g., Friday trial, gum, rehearsed lines).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with shared planning and role-play, though at times the clinician offered many specific recommendations rather than first eliciting Elise’s own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering options, using permissive language, asking which feels doable, and framing steps as low-stakes tests the client could choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation through the readiness ruler and concrete commitments, but could use more open-ended questions to draw out values, reasons, and confidence-building reflections.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance with invitations and co-created plans, yet occasionally shifted toward expert-driven content by listing options before fully inviting Elise’s ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (readiness ruler, past attempts, doable step), but could better amplify client change statements and probe what would increase readiness or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reframed sustain talk respectfully and offered alternatives to preserve connection, while more double-sided reflections and strategic discrepancy could further soften sustain talk.
