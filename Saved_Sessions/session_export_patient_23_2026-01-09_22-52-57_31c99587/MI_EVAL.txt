# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizing ambivalence and affirming worth without judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and feeling and checking for accuracy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol use, tying questions and reflections to actionable steps without pushing the client beyond readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a joint exploration—solicits the client's ideas, builds on them, and co-constructs a concrete, context-sensitive plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice, avoids prescriptive language, and repeatedly invites the client to select and shape the steps they prefer.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal resources and past successes to inform strategies, but could more systematically link those motivators to concrete commitments and amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as collaborative, invites the client's expertise, and leaves decisions to her, producing shared ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces specific change language with focused questions and options, though could better amplify, summarize, and seek confidence/commitment to deepen momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with validation but misses opportunities for strategic softening (e.g., double-sided reflections or immediate pivots toward change).
