# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the patient's constraints and priorities (routine, family).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that validates and engages the patient, though reflections focus more on facts/behavior than deeper emotions or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on managing smoking at work and advancing a one-week trial, moving logically from elicitation to plan and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the interaction as a joint enterprise by offering options, asking preferences, and integrating the patient's priorities into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes the patient's control and frames the experiment as low-pressure and reversible while offering selectable supports to choose from.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation and solutions from the patient by eliciting change-oriented ideas, labeling change talk, and asking for structure and success criteria.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Softening Sustain Talk
