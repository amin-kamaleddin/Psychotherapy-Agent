# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated nonjudgmental regard and validated Dylan’s perspective, setting clear boundaries against coercion. Warm, respectful reflections protected what mattered to Dylan throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and summaries that likely helped Dylan feel heard. Empathy was mainly cognitive and factual, with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions or affective nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on responding to the conduct process and protecting scholarships/transcript. Questions and reflections consistently tied back to the target goal and practical resolution.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled a true collaborative stance by co-constructing tasks and inviting Dylan’s expertise about what would work. Language and offers (e.g., drafting texts together) demonstrated mutual problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Dylan’s sense of choice by offering options and avoiding pressure. The clinician framed decisions as Dylan’s to make, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete plans and commitments but leaned on instrumental, external motivators (avoiding transcript/scholarships). Missed opportunities to draw out intrinsic values, importance, and confidence to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, asking Dylan to choose next steps and offering to shape materials together. Power-sharing and client-led decision-making were evident throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited specific change language and commitments (witnesses, class/community service, sending the text). Could more often amplify and probe importance/confidence to strengthen and consolidate change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk into pragmatic problem-solving, which prevented escalation. Would benefit from more strategic softening techniques (double-sided reflections, reframes) to explicitly reduce sustain language.
