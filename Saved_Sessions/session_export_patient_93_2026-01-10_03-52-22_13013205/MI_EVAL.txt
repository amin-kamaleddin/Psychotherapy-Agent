# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed Margaret’s self‑awareness while accepting her choice not to quit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captured social/contextual factors and ambivalence, but empathic work stayed mainly at the behavioral level with limited probing of deeper emotions or meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol-related risk and progressed logically from assessment to options to a concrete implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a genuine partnership: the clinician checked consent, invited preferences at every step, and co‑constructed the plan with Margaret.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission before advice, and following Margaret’s preferences about goals and materials.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons for and against change using scaling and prompts, but missed opportunities to deepen personally meaningful motivations and to amplify client language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative interaction with shared decision-making and consistent incorporation of Margaret’s input into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (rulers, planning, summaries) and helped form a concrete short-term plan, though could more strategically amplify and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce minimizations, redirecting to harm-reduction options, but could have used more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence.
