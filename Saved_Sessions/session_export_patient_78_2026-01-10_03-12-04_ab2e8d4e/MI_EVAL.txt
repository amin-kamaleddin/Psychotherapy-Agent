# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and normalizing, honoring the patient's 'no lectures' boundary and reducing confrontation. Language is somewhat repetitive/formulaic rather than offering more individualized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing both affect and content. Could use more complex reflections to explore deeper emotions and explicitly validate coping efforts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking and keeping the house smoke-free, moving stepwise from problem to concrete strategies. Session progresses logically to specify cues and observable signs of progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative, inviting patient preferences and co-constructing the plan (gum, keys, alarm). Clinician offers support options rather than directives and honors the patient's requests.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient's freedom to choose and avoids pressuring for a quit commitment. Supports small, self-chosen steps and respects the patient's pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently draws motivation and practical ideas out of the patient, eliciting concrete, client-generated strategies and signs of progress. Questions and reflections effectively elicit internal motivations like protecting her daughter.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions the work as collaborative and cedes expertise to the patient, asking how to best support her. Open invitations and shared decision-making let the patient lead choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits several pieces of change talk and helps articulate specific small steps and signs of progress. Did not consistently amplify, summarize, or use scaling to strengthen and consolidate change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids argumentation, rolls with sustain talk, and reframes toward protective actions like no smoking indoors. At times repeats sustain language verbatim without pairing it with double-sided reflections to pivot more strongly toward change.
