# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and validation, avoided judgment (e.g., normalizing nerves) and maintained a warm, respectful stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections of Marcus’s experience, though there were missed opportunities for deeper emotional probes about the dog’s meaning and intensity of overwhelm.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on asthma management with concrete, progressive steps (inhaler check, spacer, action plan) and repeatedly returned to a measurable first step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative and co‑constructed (joint decision‑making, script co-creation, role‑play), with advice tailored to Marcus’s constraints and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and control by offering multiple low‑burden options, asking permission before role‑play, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and tracked readiness (4→6) using readiness rulers and reflections, but could have probed deeper into intrinsic values and asked scaling follow‑ups to strengthen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by asking which options felt doable and offering to draft scripts, though some pragmatic scripting felt slightly directive and could be framed more as editable options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (readiness rises, concrete steps co-created) but could have amplified reasons and confidence by asking more about personal values and specific benefits of change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided argument and respected Marcus’s sustain talk about the dog while offering compromises, but did not employ advanced strategies (e.g., double‑sided reflections or exploring downsides of status quo) to weaken sustain talk.
