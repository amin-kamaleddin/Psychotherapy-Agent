# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician adopts a consistently nonjudgmental, supportive stance and validates the patient's experience, normalizing ambivalence and conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective listening by summarizing concerns and mirroring affect, though there were missed opportunities for deeper emotional exploration (e.g., loneliness) before moving to skills coaching.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on preventing another DUI and increasing safety, with every intervention directly tied to that behavioral goal and little drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works as a joint problem-solver by soliciting ideas and offering role-play, but several directive coaching moments placed more control with the clinician rather than fully sharing session content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Explicitly supports patient choice and asks which strategies feel doable, but frequent prescriptive scripting and detailed instructions sometimes reduced the patient's felt autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete patient strategies and motivations (e.g., rideshare, calling a ride) and reinforces them, yet could do more to draw out deeper intrinsic values and systematically evoke confidence and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites patient input and co-creates practical plans and role-plays, but occasional expert-like scripting and instructions shifted power back to the clinician at times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces specific change talk and actionable steps, though additional amplification of long-term reasons, values, and confidence statements would strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes sustain talk while redirecting to harm-reduction strategies, but could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence.
