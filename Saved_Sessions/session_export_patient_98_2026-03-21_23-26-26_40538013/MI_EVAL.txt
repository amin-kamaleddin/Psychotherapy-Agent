# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect through normalizing language and validation, with a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and high-quality reflective listening that captures both factual and emotional elements and checks understanding repeatedly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing safe physical activity and progressively narrows to concrete, incremental steps with pragmatic safety rules and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint effort, consistently solicits patient preferences, and co-constructs the plan so decision-making is shared.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the patient's sense of choice by offering options, avoiding pressure, and affirming the patient's expertise and control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's reasons and specific plans for change, though could further deepen internal motivation by exploring values and eliciting confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly adopts a collaborative stance and repeatedly invites the patient's input, demonstrating genuine partnership in planning and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and reinforces change talk with open questions and prompts for specifics, but would benefit from more consistent amplification and summarizing of the patient's change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to small, safe steps using double-sided reflections, though could use stronger strategic reflections or discrepancy work to further soften sustain talk.
