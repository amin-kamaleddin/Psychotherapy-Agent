# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, offering unconditional positive regard and normalizing Marcus's experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures ambivalence, but reflections stay more on content than deeper emotions like shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on adolescent alcohol use and harm reduction, repeatedly steering the conversation toward concrete, behavior-specific strategies and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as joint problem-solving: clinician invites Marcus to choose strategies, tailors lines, and co-constructs a plan based on his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes Marcus's choice and control, offering options and asking permission rather than pressuring for abstinence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Marcus's own motivations and resources through open questions, scaling, and prompting self-generated strategies and solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance with shared decision-making, asking permission, offering to script or role-play only if Marcus wants, and letting him guide the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively draws out and amplifies change talk using pros/cons questions, importance/confidence scaling, and asking Marcus to select and commit to doable harm-reduction steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and acknowledges sustain talk, but could more often use double-sided or selectively emphasized reflections to more strategically soften sustain statements.
