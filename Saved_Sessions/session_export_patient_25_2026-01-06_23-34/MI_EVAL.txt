### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming (e.g., praised quitting smoking and normalized noticing patterns), with no stigmatizing language.

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership
