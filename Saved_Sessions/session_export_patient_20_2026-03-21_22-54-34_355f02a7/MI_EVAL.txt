# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, creating a safe, nonjudgmental stance that reduced defensiveness and supported disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely, accurate summaries that expanded the client's thinking and feelings throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol use and consistently steers conversation toward practical, behaviorally specific strategies and measurable signs of success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducts a joint exploration by soliciting the client's ideas, checking feasibility, and letting the client's preferences shape the plan rather than imposing expert solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice and control, normalizes imperfect adherence, and avoids pressure, which preserves intrinsic motivation and respects the client's pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's own motivations and solutions and elicits a personal indicator of success, but could more directly probe commitment and confidence to solidify motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as collaborative and positions the client as the authority, using questions that invite the client to lead planning and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change talk and specific steps that the client can take, but could better amplify and summarize those statements to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and uses double-sided reflections to respect reasons for drinking, though selectively reframing sustain talk to contrast costs and benefits would further reduce resistance.
