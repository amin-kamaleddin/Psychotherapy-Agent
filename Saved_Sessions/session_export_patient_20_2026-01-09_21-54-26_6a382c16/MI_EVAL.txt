# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding criticism throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking with timely, tailored reflections that capture both content and feeling and deepen the client's expression.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on reducing weeknight drinking and repeatedly returns the conversation to a concrete, actionable plan and measurable indicators of success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner by offering options, inviting client choice, and co-constructing the implementation plan and problem-solving together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice and ownership by accepting limits (keeping weekends) and asking which options feel 'doable' rather than directing behavior.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the client's reasons and motivations and draws out multiple change statements, but stops short of systematically deepening or consolidating them into firmer commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert, invites input, and jointly develops supports and cues, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with prompts for small, doable steps and supports, but misses some opportunities to amplify and sequence change talk toward stronger commitment or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk without argumentation, yet uses few explicit strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or weighted summaries) to pivot sustain talk more strongly toward change.
