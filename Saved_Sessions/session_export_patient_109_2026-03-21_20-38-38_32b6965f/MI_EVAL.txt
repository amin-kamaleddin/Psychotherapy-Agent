# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with normalizing statements and affirmations that conveyed unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking were frequent, but reflections tended toward pragmatic paraphrase rather than deeper, affect-laden reflections of discouragement or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on returning to the gym with concrete, time-bound planning (text tonight, Saturday 9:00 AM, 20-minute workout) and backup options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was mutual problem-solving with frequent invitations for Rafael’s input and co-construction of the plan, treating him as an active partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeated permission language and multiple options were offered, consistently reinforcing Rafael’s sense of choice and control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited Rafael’s reasons and resources (health, past routines, buddy) but could more consistently follow change statements with probes linking them to personal values and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful of choices, though substantial clinician scripting and offers to send messages risked shifting some ownership away from the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with readiness/confidence rulers and small-step options, but missed opportunities to more frequently amplify change language and elicit explicit commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided challenging sustain talk and used double-sided reflections to redirect toward change, but could use more frequent reflections emphasizing costs of staying the same to further reduce sustain pull.
