# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, avoiding labeling or shaming. Multiple validations and nonjudgmental language reinforce acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with complex reflections and repeated paraphrasing that capture content and emotion. Clinician checks preferences, showing strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, tight focus on the vaccine decision and proximal steps (e.g., three verified 15–30s peer clips). Prompts consistently tie back to what would help the patient decide.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as an equal partner by checking preferences, offering options, and adapting the plan to his choices. The session is clearly co-constructed rather than expert-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the patient's choice and frames offers of help nonpressuringly, validating uncertainty. Multiple options and deference to his preference reinforce autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and practical preferences that produced change-oriented statements, but could more consistently deepen intrinsic motivation and link to values or confidence. More probing for importance/confidence and outcomes would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as collaborative, asking which approach feels most useful and following the patient's lead. Power is clearly distributed through choice and shared planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits several change-talk statements by focusing on what would help him decide, but could better amplify and summarize those statements to build momentum toward commitment. Follow‑ups to deepen desire, ability, reasons, and need would help.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses some double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk, redirecting to helpful steps. There were missed opportunities to more strategically soften sustain talk and then elicit change reasons.
