# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Nadine’s exhaustion, avoiding blame. Acceptance is reinforced throughout when offering advice and validating distress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and attention to both emotion and practical concerns are present (naming exhaustion, asking about feeding/help). More complex, elaborated reflections to capture underlying emotional burden (burnout/guilt) would deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on infant sleep/feed routines and Nadine’s rest with concrete, practical steps and measurable next actions. Frequent short action plans keep the conversation goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is presented as joint problem-solving, soliciting preferences and co-producing scripts/texts Nadine requested. The clinician integrates Nadine’s constraints into recommendations, reinforcing partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly affirms Nadine’s choice and offers multiple options (dream feed, pump/hand‑express, call pediatrician). Normalizing small formula top‑ups and short pumps supports Nadine’s control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are some invitations and prompts to try the plan, but the clinician relies more on expert advice than eliciting Nadine’s internal motivations, values, or confidence. Limited use of scaling or systematic change‑talk elicitation reduces evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with permission‑seeking and co‑creation of scripts, offering choices and invitations. Occasional directive moments occur when offering specific instructions without first eliciting Nadine’s ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and strengthens change talk with a concise plan, staged trial, and concrete scripts, prompting a commitment to try. Missed opportunities include amplifying spontaneous change language and asking change‑evoking questions to solidify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician validates skepticism and fatigue and offers realistic, small‑step reframes that reduce sustain talk (e.g., 5–10 minute pumps, small formula top‑ups). More use of double‑sided reflections naming ambivalence before pivoting to change would strengthen this area.
