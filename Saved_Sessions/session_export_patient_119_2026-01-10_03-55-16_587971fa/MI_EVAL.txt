# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming throughout the session, normalizing Selene's experience and highlighting strengths. No blaming or minimization was present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking that captures ambivalence and practical barriers. Could probe deeper emotional nuance (fear, guilt, stress) to reach a higher empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, sustained focus on medication adherence with concrete, actionable planning and specific next steps. Repeatedly returns to the target behavior and secures time-bound supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction models joint problem-solving with frequent solicitation of Selene's preferences and co-creation of strategies. Clinician provides options but follows Selene's lead and offers paste-ready tools when requested.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking permission before giving advice, and leaving decisions to Selene. Avoids pressure and validates flexible, low-burden steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Selene's own motivation with open questions, confidence scaling, and linking her value of staying healthy to supports. Draws out intrinsic reasons rather than imposing change rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters client expertise and shared decision-making by asking which options fit Selene's life and offering to help draft messages. Could further emphasize Selene's ideas by inviting her to brainstorm before listing clinician-generated options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change language, summarizes decisions, and helps craft implementation tools. Would benefit from more strategic amplification of Selene's own reasons and explicit linking of those reasons to the plan to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses neutral reflections of sustain talk but does not consistently employ strategies to soften it or elicit discrepancy. More selective reflections, cost exploration, and reframing toward change would reduce sustain talk more effectively.
