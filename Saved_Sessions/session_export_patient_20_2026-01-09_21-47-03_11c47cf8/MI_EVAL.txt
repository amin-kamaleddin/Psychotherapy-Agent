# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating the client's priorities and avoiding moralizing language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, specific reflective listening and perspective-taking by mirroring the patient's language and emotional priorities, which deepened the client's articulation of goals.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and a concrete, measurable change (two sober weeknights), guiding the conversation toward supports and implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicited the patient's ideas and co-constructed a practical experiment (seltzer+show, shifting drinking nights), showing genuine shared problem-solving and validation of patient priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly reinforced the patient's control and choice by normalizing testing rather than quitting and asking for preferences instead of imposing a plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified the patient's own motivations and values with evocative questions and reflections, drawing motivation from the patient rather than supplying external reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as an equal expert, offering options and inviting decisions that resulted in a clearly patient-led plan and shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited, reflected, and reinforced change talk using open questions and summaries, and invited forward planning to strengthen commitment to the experiment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing with sustain talk and redirected to a collaborative test, but made limited use of explicit double-sided reflections or reframing to further diminish sustain talk's pull.
