# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation (including a safety check), honoring the client's experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used empathic reflections and validated frustration and nervousness, but could deepen connection with more complex affect reflections before moving quickly to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on attending interviews and resolving childcare barriers with all interventions tightly linked to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine collaboration by co-constructing messages, sequencing tasks to the client's preference, and repeatedly soliciting the client's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed client choice, offered multiple options, and avoided pressuring language while emphasizing the client's control over pace and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some readiness and preferences but relied more on offering solutions than drawing out the client's underlying motivations, values, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a collaborative partner throughout—asking preferences, tailoring materials on request, and sharing decision-making power with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and supported change talk with invitations and concrete action planning, though missed opportunities to amplify and probe the client's strong self-statements for deeper motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated sustain talk and immediately shifted to practical alternatives to reduce its impact, but used fewer deliberate MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly work ambivalence.
