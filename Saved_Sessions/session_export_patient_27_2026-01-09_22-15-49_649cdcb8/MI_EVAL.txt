# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating ambivalence and praising steps. Created a caring tone throughout without minimizing concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening, normalized nervousness, and offered practical reassurance. Could deepen empathy by exploring the emotional meaning of secrecy and reflecting values more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on disclosure to peers with concrete, sequential steps and practice opportunities. Kept the session tightly aligned to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited input, checked preferences, and practiced with the patient, creating an overall collaborative feel. Collaboration was somewhat limited by offering many clinician-crafted scripts rather than co-creating language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Daniela's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission to practice, and avoiding pressuring language. Reinforced agency around what, when, and how much to share.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited limited internal motivation and relied more on providing scripts and tips than drawing out Daniela's own reasons, values, or confidence. Missed opportunities for evocative open questions and scaling/confidence queries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally partnered with the patient by inviting preferences and practicing, but the clinician often supplied solutions and wording, reducing equal power-sharing. Would benefit from more co-creation of plans and language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Encouraged small, concrete steps that elicited some change talk and choices, but did not systematically amplify or deepen Daniela's own motivations or confidence. More follow-up questions to expand reasons and readiness would strengthen evocation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and normalized fears while redirecting to small experiments and privacy lines. Did not employ stronger MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections, explicit cost/benefit exploration) to more fully resolve ambivalence.
