# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated respectful, nonjudgmental acceptance and normalized difficulty throughout the session. Affirmations and supportive language created safety for honest disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening with effective summaries that tracked ambivalence and linked to values. The clinician also checked safety and contextual realities, reinforcing emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing gambling and improving finances, tying specific actions to valued outcomes like job protection and travel. The session stayed action-oriented with concrete planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Work was clearly co-created—preferences were solicited at every step and tools were offered on request. Decisions and backups were shaped by the client's real-world constraints, reflecting equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options, asked permission, and affirmed that plans were the client's to accept or modify. This approach supported choice and reduced resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's reasons and practical strategies for change using scaling and elicitation prompts. Could strengthen by deeper exploration of intrinsic motivations and by eliciting more confidence and explicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the work as a collaboration, inviting the client's choices and co-constructing the plan. The clinician respected the client's expertise about her life and supported shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced planning and commitment language, converting vague intent into specific steps and timelines. There were missed opportunities to more frequently amplify desire/ability/need/confidence language to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward practical alternatives and supports, reducing its momentum. Could have more often used double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence while steering toward change.
