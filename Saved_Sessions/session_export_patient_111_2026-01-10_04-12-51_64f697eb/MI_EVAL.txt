# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, avoiding blame or lecturing to create safety for exploration.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking that mirrored emotions and motivations, helping the patient elaborate ambivalence without feeling challenged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking behavior and progressively guided the conversation toward a concrete, time-limited change plan without pressuring the patient.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like a joint project—open questions, invited patient solutions, and co-constructed concrete rules and check-ins that reflected the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly honored the patient's control and choices, affirming limits and avoiding coercive language which reduced reactance and encouraged voluntary steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out intrinsic motivation by eliciting values, strengths, and past successes, prompting the patient to generate reasons and strategies for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative stance—clinician invited, used, and prioritized the patient's ideas rather than prescribing solutions, conveying power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and amplified change talk (e.g., protecting his wife) and asked for concrete steps, but missed opportunities for scaling confidence/importance and further strengthening commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward planning, but relied mostly on neutral reflections rather than more strategic double-sided reflections or reframes to transform sustain talk more actively.
