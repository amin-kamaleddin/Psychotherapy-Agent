# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect for Seamus's choices, honoring limits like no phone reminders; validated his preference for a small, self-directed step throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate paraphrase and reflective listening that showed understanding and avoided lecturing, but missed opportunities to name and explore deeper emotions like frustration and weariness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction tied to oral health and moved from options to a specific, time-limited action (tea after meals) and coping plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted cooperative two-way problem solving by inviting Seamus's input at every step, offering options rather than prescriptions, and co-creating a practical plan adapted to his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Seamus's freedom to choose, solicited his own ideas, honored boundaries (no pills, no nagging), and avoided pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked evocative questions that produced self-generated change talk and a concrete commitment, but could have probed more into importance, confidence, and personal values to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Seamus as the expert on his life by asking for his 'best idea' and sharing decision-making power, leading to a jointly created, acceptable plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and shaped change talk with open questions and actionable prompts, yet could have strengthened momentum by amplifying affirmations and eliciting more reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk without confrontation and reframed toward manageable harm-reduction steps, though using more double-sided reflections could further soften resistance and invite exploration.
