### Empathy

**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing violent outbursts and alcohol use and moved toward concrete, testable behavioral experiments; avoided drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Effectively elicited Luka’s own reasons and plans for change, producing concrete change talk and commitments (rehearsals, wristband trial, nightly notes); drew on his internal resources to build the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
