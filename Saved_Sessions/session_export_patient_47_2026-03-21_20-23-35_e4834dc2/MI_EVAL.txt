# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, with an explicit opening that set an accepting tone and continued warm, respectful language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and attention to context, accurately capturing key themes; reflections are more content- and practical-focused and could include deeper emotional/complex reflections to deepen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction with concrete planning questions and tangible outputs (templates, tracking plan); guidance stayed on target and was appropriately structured.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session exemplified two-way co-construction of the plan, integrating the patient's preferences and constraints; choices about wording, formats, and reminders were brokered rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported client autonomy by asking permission, offering multiple options, and framing decisions as the client's choices; avoided pressure and affirmed ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation through open questions and confidence scaling, producing concrete change-related language; could strengthen evocation by drawing out values, past successes, and personally meaningful reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed a collaborative stance and deferred to the client's expertise, co-creating a practical, patient-centered tracking plan; repeated invitations to choose demonstrated strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and supported change talk with permission, goal-setting, and confidence questions that produced client intentions; could more actively amplify and summarize the client's change language to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and steered toward change options, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframing to explicitly name ambivalence; more direct techniques to soften minimizations would reduce sustain talk's pull.
