# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used a consistently nonjudgmental, supportive tone, acknowledged competing concerns, and normalized pragmatic harm-reduction steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening, summarizing concrete plans well, but missed opportunities for deeper emotion-focused reflections (e.g., worries or values).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, continuous focus on target behaviors (smoking, dust exposure, medication adherence) and moved the conversation toward a specific, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the session as joint problem-solving, offered options, and repeatedly invited Samir to choose and refine realistic plans, creating an equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Samir's sense of choice with invitational language and honored his readiness to cut down rather than pressuring him to quit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicited Samir's own reasons and concrete intentions, but could have probed deeper into personal values and asked for commitment/confidence language to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Samir as the decision-maker, used collaborative language, and co-constructed a realistic plan that fit his life and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk resulting in concrete steps, though could have amplified and reflected change language more and used rulers to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward achievable changes, but could have used double-sided reflections and explicit pros/cons exploration to better resolve ambivalence.
