# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect with affirmations and nonshaming safety checks, creating a warm supportive climate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides accurate reflections and empathic validations about hurt and determination, though could probe more deeply into fear of losing independence and the emotional meaning of that loss.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing drinking, safety tonight, and steps toward reunification with concrete, time‑limited goals and actionable plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co‑creates the relapse plan and practices calls with the client, but frequent directive checklists and scripts slightly reduce client‑originated problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and agency by offering options, accepting reluctance without pressure, and asking which strategies feel most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivations and links them to concrete actions (reunification, supports), but at times moves quickly to clinician solutions rather than deepening the client's own verbalization of motives and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with shared decision making and invitations to choose, yet several expert‑style recommendations limit the extent of client‑led problem solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces commitment language and doable steps, but could better amplify change talk with elaboration, importance/confidence scaling, and stronger commitment probes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accepts ambivalence and redirects to workable alternatives without arguing, though more double‑sided reflections and strategic summaries could further soften sustain talk.
