# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses a respectful, affirming, and normalizing tone that highlights Samuel's progress, but some acceptance felt a bit formulaic; more explicit unconditional regard and normalization of ambivalence would strengthen this area.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening throughout, paraphrasing patterns, validating reluctance, and matching Samuel's language and tone effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on smoking reduction and moves logically from assessment to a concrete, measurable action plan (gum trial, tracking, backups, confidence ruler).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is clearly collaborative—ideas are solicited and negotiated, the plan is co-designed, and the clinician frames the work as a team effort with shared troubleshooting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supports Samuel's choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and reinforcing that he retains ownership of the plan and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician draws out Samuel's own motivations and problem-solving (choice of gum, cues, confidence scaling) rather than imposing reasons for change, eliciting commitment language and practical solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician frames the session as a partnership, repeatedly inviting Samuel's input, deferring to his rehearsal preference, and co-constructing the concrete plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through praise and specific planning, but could more deliberately amplify and summarize Samuel's strongest change statements and probe for desire/ability/reason/need to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing and gently redirects toward planning with neutral reframes and alternatives, though using more double-sided reflections and reframing sustain talk into change-oriented reflections would further reduce sustain talk.
