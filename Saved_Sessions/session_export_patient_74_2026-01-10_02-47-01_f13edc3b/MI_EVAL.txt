# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone with affirmations and normalization, demonstrating unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provided accurate, timely reflections that captured both content and affect, helping the patient elaborate ambivalence and feel understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking-related behavior with concrete, actionable goals (smoke-free car/house, one-week plan) and consistently steered the session toward these steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co-constructed with frequent invitations for the patient's preferences, rehearsal, and shared troubleshooting, and plans were adjusted to fit her limits.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the patient's choice, asked permission before exploring, and framed small, voluntary options without pressure, supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out existing motivations (concern for children, role-modeling) using open questions and a confidence ruler, but could further deepen value linkage and amplify her own change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative and respectful of the patient's expertise, though occasionally offered multiple clinician-led suggestions; more explicit statements that the patient drives decisions would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk via options and follow-up questions (confidence ruler, doable steps), but could more deliberately amplify and summarize the patient's change language to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated the pleasurable, stress-relieving aspects of smoking while shifting toward risk-reduction strategies; could incorporate more double-sided reflections to more actively resolve ambivalence.
