# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing the patient’s experience and repeatedly affirming preferences (e.g., preserving the morning cup and offering slow options).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows some understanding of benefits and downsides but relies heavily on questions and directives with few reflective statements, missing deeper reflections of ambivalence and emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on caffeine use throughout the session and ties all suggestions to measurable steps and outcomes like tracking, jitter, and sleep.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—solicits tool preferences and offers follow-up—but sometimes supplies detailed plans and templates without first eliciting how much structure the patient wants.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering multiple options, conditional advice, and explicit permission to slow or repeat steps, while accepting the patient’s choice to keep the morning cup.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation (goal setting, willingness to track) but emphasizes information-giving and planning over asking evocative questions that would draw out personal reasons and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames work as collaborative with offers to tailor plans and review progress, yet occasionally defaults to expert-led solutions rather than co-creating the level of detail.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits tentative change talk (agreeing to track and try plans) but misses opportunities to amplify, explore, and deepen desire, reasons, ability, and commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids confrontation and softens sustain talk by validating benefits and offering harm-reduction strategies (preserving morning cup, small swaps), though could use more double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
