# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalizing the patient's struggle and avoiding blame. Warm acceptance is communicated repeatedly and supports rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing emotions and ambivalence. Reflections invite elaboration and the patient affirms feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on concrete target behaviors and moves the session logically from problem recognition to a specific, actionable plan. Questions repeatedly steer toward small, realistic steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative with the clinician inviting input and incorporating the patient's ideas into the plan. Patient-generated options shape the strategies used.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and avoids pressure, normalizing tentative plans and respecting the patient's limits on promises. Clinician emphasizes patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation, values, and past strengths and links them to behavior change. Could deepen exploration of intensity (confidence, importance) and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally frames the work as joint and invites the patient to set steps, conveying that their expertise matters. Falls short of full partnership at times by steering the agenda rather than explicitly negotiating options to transfer decision-making power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reliably elicits and amplifies change talk and asks action-oriented questions that invite commitment. Would be strengthened by more systematic amplification of desire/ability/reasons/need language and use of scaling or commitment probes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without overtly reinforcing it, but often mirrors sustain phrasing which can re-center doubt. Missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to reduce sustain talk's influence.
