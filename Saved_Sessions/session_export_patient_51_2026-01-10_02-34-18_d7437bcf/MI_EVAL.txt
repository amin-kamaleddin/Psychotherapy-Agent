# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, validating the patient's effort and competence without blame or corrective language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, nuanced reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing the patient's plans and constraints throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps clear focus on safe medication checking and moves the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps and realistic choices.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in collaborative problem-solving, building on the patient's ideas and offering options rather than imposing clinician-driven solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering choices, asking which option feels doable, and affirming the patient's capacity to implement the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivations and strengths, using past successes to draw out internal resources and practical solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the decision-maker and co-creates the plan, repeatedly soliciting preferences and validating choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with targeted open questions and reinforces the patient's concrete intentions and plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adequately minimizes limited sustain talk by reframing toward feasibility, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections or name ambivalence to further soften resistance.
