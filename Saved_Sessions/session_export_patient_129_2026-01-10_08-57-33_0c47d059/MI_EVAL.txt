# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used consistently nonjudgmental, warm language and normalized Tyler's perspective, validated feelings, and avoided moralizing throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking (labeling feelings and summarizing) but reflections were often surface-level and missed deeper emotions, values, and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on substance use and the immediate goal (planning the conversation with mom), offering practical options and keeping the session on task.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: invited Tyler to set the agenda, co-wrote and refined scripts, role-played on request, and repeatedly checked his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, respecting refusals, emphasizing Tyler's control, and avoiding pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to evoke motivation (asking about change and feelings) but did not consistently draw out internal reasons, values, or discrepancies to build motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative, power-sharing stance—asked permission, co-constructed plans, and invited rehearsal and feedback to position Tyler as the expert on his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made occasional invitations for change talk but did not systematically elicit or amplify Tyler's reasons, desires, or values; the session shifted to scripting rather than exploring latent concerns.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and accepted Tyler's stance while redirecting to productive tasks, though could have used more double-sided reflections or strategic reframes to surface ambivalence.
