# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalized struggle, and affirmed the client's experience without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking that captured the client's thoughts, feelings, and physical sensations throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing avoidance and building confidence with concrete, actionable steps and avoided drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a genuine joint effort by soliciting the client's input at each decision point, co-doing interventions, and adjusting the plan based on the client's cues.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission, and emphasized 'no pressure,' reinforcing the client's control over decisions and pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited small commitments and used rulers to draw out motivation, but did not consistently probe values or deeper, long-term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated shared expertise and deference to the client by co-creating the plan, asking permission, and following the client's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited actionable change talk and small commitments (e.g., timers, brief tasks), but missed opportunities to amplify intrinsic reasons and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing for the status quo and normalized ambivalence, but could have used more double-sided reflections and summaries to further soften sustain talk.
