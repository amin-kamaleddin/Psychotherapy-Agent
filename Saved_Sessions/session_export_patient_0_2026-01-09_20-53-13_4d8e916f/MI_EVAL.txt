# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, inviting the patient’s expertise. Warm, nonjudgmental language is used throughout to support rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows clear, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking around practical concerns. Misses deeper emotional material (shame/embarrassment/fear) that would strengthen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on harm-reduction goals and repeatedly brings the conversation back to concrete steps. Questions and reflections move the patient toward a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a co-created plan with tentative offers and menu-style options. The clinician adjusts to the patient’s priorities and follows the patient’s lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and control, offering options rather than directives and never pressuring the patient to quit. Consistently reinforces the patient’s freedom to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient, eliciting reasons and converting intentions into concrete actions. Moves (e.g., asking what feels doable) evoked a clear commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance that positions the patient as the expert and negotiates next steps together. Offers support tailored to the patient’s preferences (e.g., discreet pickup first).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk, asking for a specific, small commitment and turning it into a concrete plan. Reflections reinforce the patient’s desire for safety and produced a clear action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and redirects sustain talk toward safer options, maintaining rapport. However, at times the clinician repeats the patient’s sustain language verbatim, which can unintentionally reinforce it rather than strategically soften it.
