# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating throughout the session, normalizing doubt and offering supportive praise that maintained engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections of somatic distress and safety concerns, but missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of resignation and hopelessness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on reducing self-harm and ensuring safety with repeated checks, targeted interventions, and escalation instructions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—checked preferences, offered choices, and invited co-creation of the plan—though some structured, directive instructions were used given immediate safety needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client choice and control by offering options, asking permission before exercises, and respecting the client's decisions about whom to involve.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited factual readiness and behavior but did little to draw out intrinsic motivation, values, or ambivalence, and did not amplify emerging change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly adopted a collaborative stance—inviting input and offering to stay for urge-surfing—but sometimes shifted to more expert-led instructions appropriate to safety work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited safety planning and skill use which produced some willingness to try tools, but did not systematically elicit or deepen the client's own reasons, goals, or benefits for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to safety and alternatives, though could more often use strategic MI moves (e.g., double-sided or amplified reflections) to further soften sustain language.
