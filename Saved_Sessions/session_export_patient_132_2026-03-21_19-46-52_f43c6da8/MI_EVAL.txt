# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validates and normalizes Yasmin's concerns throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses reflective listening and perspective-taking and validates discouragement and worries, but often shifts to informational content without deeper, complex reflections of underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a steady, appropriate focus on transitioning to sustainable eating/exercise with concrete, behavior-focused strategies and relapse planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is cooperative and frequently invites tailoring, though several moments are clinician-driven and prescriptive rather than equally co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Yasmin's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and emphasizing flexible, client-led decisions while discouraging coercive responses.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to draw out motivation (offers plans, non-scale wins) but relies more on offering solutions than asking evocative questions to elicit Yasmin's intrinsic reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively, asks permission and checks preferences, yet at times adopts an expert stance with detailed recommendations that reduce equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites and reinforces some change talk and offers actionable options, but misses systematic elicitation of Yasmin's own desire, ability, reasons, need, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without escalating it, but underuses double-sided reflections and sometimes reassures about weight loss in ways that may unintentionally reinforce sustain talk.
