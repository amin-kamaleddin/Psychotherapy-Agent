### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Opened with an evocative question and linked skills to values, but then shifted into teaching and coaching. Missed opportunities to elicit deeper personal motivations, ambivalence, or confidence (e.g., follow-up evocative questions or scaling).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making with permission requests and choices of focus and practice. Partnership was limited by clinician-supplied scripts rather than co-creating responses together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change language and tied suggestions to her goals, but relied mainly on advice and scripted responses. Could have followed up with questions to deepen and amplify Anya’s own reasons for change.
