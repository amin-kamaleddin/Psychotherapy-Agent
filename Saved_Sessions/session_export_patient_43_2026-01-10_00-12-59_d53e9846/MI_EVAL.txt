# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental language with frequent affirmations and validation (e.g., thanked patient, normalized modest goals), demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and mirroring of the patient's perspective and fatigue, but missed some opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections that probe emotional drivers beneath rationalizations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on smoking with a concrete, modest behavioral goal and specific implementation strategies, without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative co‑construction of the plan with repeated invitations for the patient's input and decisions shaping timing, content, and supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by asking permission, offering options rather than directives, and explicitly honoring preferences (e.g., keeping wife out of it).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited modest, practical motivation and commitments (delay, confidence increase) but did not consistently explore deeper personal values or long‑term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine partnership by following the patient's lead, negotiating plan details, and positioning the work as joint problem‑solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used scaling, permission, and low‑effort options to evoke concrete change talk and commitments, though could have amplified and followed up more to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and pivoted to small, nonthreatening steps, but could further employ double‑sided reflections and explicit validation of ambivalence to soften sustain talk more strategically.
