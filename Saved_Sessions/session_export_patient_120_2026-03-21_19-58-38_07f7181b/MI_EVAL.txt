# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows clear respect and nonjudgmental validation that reduces shame and offers practical support. Could add more individualized affirmations of Sofia's strengths and explicitly normalize ambivalence to reach a top rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and some reflective listening, validating fear and guilt and checking safety. Reflections are mostly brief and factual rather than deep explorations of emotional conflict and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains strong focus on alcohol use and safety throughout, offering concrete, actionable steps and moving the session toward behavior change. The clinician reliably keeps the conversation on-topic and goal-focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction reads as mutual problem-solving, responding to requests and tailoring scripts while inviting input. However, the clinician often supplies ready-made solutions instead of eliciting Sofia's own ideas, limiting equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects Sofia's choices by offering options, permission-style offers, and reassurance that slips aren't catastrophic. At times the clinician uses prescriptive language (e.g., 'it’s safest to stop now and tell your OB'), which reduces autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits factual information and some willingness to change but makes limited efforts to draw out Sofia's values, motivations, or past successes. The clinician leans toward offering external strategies rather than asking evocative questions to mobilize intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance by asking permission, tailoring scripts, and offering choices that share power. Could strengthen partnership by inviting Sofia to generate more of the plan and framing decisions as co-created.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supports some change intent with planning and reinforcement, and Sofia expresses willingness to stop. The clinician frequently moves quickly to information and solutions, missing opportunities to elicit and amplify Sofia's own reasons, values, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges risks and safety concerns but does not consistently use MI strategies (double-sided reflections, rolling with resistance) to diffuse sustain talk. The reliance on advice and tactics risks strengthening the clinician's agenda instead of helping Sofia resolve ambivalence internally.
