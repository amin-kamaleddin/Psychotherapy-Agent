# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 5/5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect (e.g., validated fears about cancer and not being dismissed). Avoided blame or minimizing throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 5/5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and summaries captured emotional tone and specific details (food poisoning, symptom types, impact on work/family), deepening rapport.

### Direction
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 5/5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on diagnostic uncertainty and functional goals; consistently guided toward actionable outcomes (second opinion, targeted tests, drafting referral) without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 5/5
**Comments:** Framed work as partnership, solicited patient preferences, and co‑constructed the referral wording using the patient’s own language.

### Autonomy Support
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 5/5
**Comments:** Offered options rather than directives, asked which path the patient preferred, and supported patient choice (tests plus symptom management).

### Evocation
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 4/5
**Comments:** Elicited change talk (what would change if pain stopped, small doable step) and secured action, but missed opportunities to more deeply evoke values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 5/5
**Comments:** Demonstrated power‑sharing (invited exact wording, asked which sentence to include about work/family); clinician acted as collaborator rather than authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 4/5
**Comments:** Recognized and reinforced patient change talk and obtained a concrete action (ask for referral this week), but did not systematically amplify into stronger commitment language or confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### Score (1–5): ☑ 4/5
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double‑sided reflections to acknowledge concerns about psychological labels; could more actively reframe and normalize mind–body links or elicit small experiments to further reduce sustain talk.
