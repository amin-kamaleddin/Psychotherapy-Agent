# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with repeated validations and normalization of ambivalence; clinician affirms Maria's pain without shaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking are frequent, but reflections are often repetitive and miss opportunities to probe the deeper emotional texture behind 'nothing works.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on suicidal ideation, means restriction, and a specific, feasible behavioral step (saved draft + alarm), balancing validation with movement toward safety.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conversation feels joint with shared decision-making and co-creation of wording, though the clinician often leads with clinician-generated options rather than eliciting more client-generated ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking which feels most doable, and avoiding coercive language, reinforcing Maria's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of motivation (protecting sister) and practical commitment, but limited deeper elicitation of personal values, importance, or confidence to strengthen internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invites Maria's preferences, yet often presents clinician-originated alternatives instead of explicitly inviting Maria to generate solutions, limiting fuller power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively scaffolds small, concrete change steps aligned with Maria's care for her sister and elicits a specific plan, but could better amplify and elaborate fleeting change talk and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and pairs it with Maria's other side before redirecting to manageable actions, though the clinician often moves quickly to problem-solving rather than exploring sustain talk's emotional content.
