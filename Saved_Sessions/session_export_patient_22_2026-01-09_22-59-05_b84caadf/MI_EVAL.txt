### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Camila's feelings without judgment, repeatedly validating resilience and concerns. This creates a safe, nonshaming tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely empathic reflections of both content and feeling, mirroring ambivalence and concrete details. Reflections help Camila feel heard and understood throughout the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on reducing drinking and progressively refines a specific, time-limited plan for the weekend. Links desired outcomes (better mornings, more energy) to concrete actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner, consistently soliciting Camila's preferences and inviting her input at each step. Decisions and plans are co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by offering options, framing the change as Camila's test, and avoiding coercive language. Emphasizes her control over which elements to try.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Camila as the expert on her life and invites collaboration in planning, repeatedly asking her to choose doable options. Power is shared and the clinician supports client-led decision-making.

**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces multiple strands of change talk and guides toward a small, concrete experiment. Missed opportunities to amplify desire/ability/need statements and to strengthen commitment language with summaries or scaling.
