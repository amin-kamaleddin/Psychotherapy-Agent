# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, respected Lucía's boundaries, and normalized ambivalence; warm, nonjudgmental stance was maintained throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking validated Lucía's feelings and frustrations; several reflections were somewhat formulaic and could have probed deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking behavior and moved to a concrete, measurable experiment with a specific follow-up; the session remained action-oriented and goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited Lucía's input at every decision point, presented options, and co-created the plan; interaction reflected equal partnership rather than an expert stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasized choice and control, honored explicit limits, and avoided pressuring language; offering multiple support options reinforced Lucía's ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons for change and actionable ideas (health, daughter, delay first cigarette) but did not sufficiently probe deeper intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Lucía as the expert on her life and deferred to her preferences, confirming wording and scheduling together; clear power-sharing was evident.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and summarized the experiment to strengthen commitment; missed opportunities to deepen change talk by asking why goals matter or assessing confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly rolled with sustain talk, respected resistance, and reframed the plan as a client-controlled experiment; successfully redirected conversation away from argument or convincing.
