# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized difficulty, and avoided blame, creating a safe nonjudgmental space for honest disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflective listening that captured both content and affect, mirrored the patient's language, and validated her feelings, facilitating deeper disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking cessation and immediate, concrete steps, repeatedly steering the conversation back to actionable behavior-linked goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a co-created effort by soliciting the patient's options, inviting her choices, and translating her ideas into a shared plan without dominating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized patient choice and pace, used permission-oriented language, and framed suggestions as options rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation and strategies from the patient through evocative questions, eliciting client-generated reasons and concrete solutions for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored the patient's expertise about her life, invited joint planning, and shared decision-making to build genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified multiple change statements, linked values to specific actions, and used past successes to strengthen commitment and self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk sensitively and redirected to small steps, but missed opportunities for strategic double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of evening smoking functions.
