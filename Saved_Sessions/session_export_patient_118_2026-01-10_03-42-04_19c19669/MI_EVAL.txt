# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and honored the patient's request to avoid quitting-talk, validating priorities and avoiding judgment throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely understanding of the patient's feelings and perspective with reflections, calming suggestions, and normalization of repeated advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on dental treatment planning and actionable next steps while resisting drifting into unwanted smoking counseling.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked in a team-like, co-constructed manner by soliciting preferences, offering options and practical tools, and positioning the patient as an active partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice by honoring decisions, offering options, and inviting the patient to prioritize tasks rather than imposing directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did not actively elicit intrinsic motivation for smoking change and missed opportunities to probe values, importance, or use brief scaling or permission-prefaced questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned as a helper/co-worker by offering options, asking preferences, and sharing decision-making power rather than adopting an expert stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made minimal attempts to evoke or strengthen the patient's change language, largely avoiding exploration of ambivalence and missing chances to amplify existing change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and limited sustain-talk amplification through concise factual information, though could further soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections and strategic phrasing.
