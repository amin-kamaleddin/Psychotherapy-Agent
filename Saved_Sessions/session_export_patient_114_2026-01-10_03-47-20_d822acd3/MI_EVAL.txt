# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with validating statements and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing difficulty and affirming the client's efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening with complex reflections and probes that sensitively track ambivalence and the client's concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on risky drinking and concrete change goals throughout the session, moving from assessment to specific, actionable plans and commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is co-constructed: the clinician offers tools while repeatedly inviting the client to choose wording, buddies, and strategies, shaping the plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission before advising, and affirming the client's ownership of decisions and limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and resources, linking plans to values like scholarship and prompting internal reasons and confidence-building steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and client-led: asks permission, offers choices, and invites the client to pick strategies and commit, framing it as joint problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions, scaling, and repeated invitations to concrete planning that the client accepts and commits to.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and pivots to practical solutions, though could more often use double-sided reflections or explicit summaries to deepen discrepancy.
