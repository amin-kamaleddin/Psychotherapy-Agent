# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician clearly communicated unconditional positive regard and repeatedly validated Ryan's feelings, creating a safe, nonjudgmental tone for disclosure and planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and accurate reflections captured content and affect, but the clinician could have probed deeper into defensiveness, shame, and identity-related concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on risky drinking and immediate protective strategies, consistently moving from values to specific, actionable steps and logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was joint problem-solving throughout, with the clinician inviting Ryan's preferences, offering supports only with consent, and co-constructing the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician affirmed client choice, presented options rather than prescriptions, normalized ambivalence, and offered supports as optional to preserve Ryan's agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited motivation tied to Ryan's values and practical reasons for change, but did not consistently deepen intrinsic motivation or elicit confidence and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treated Ryan as the primary decision-maker, repeatedly asking him to choose among options and offering suggestions as optional supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicited and amplified change talk, converting vague interest into concrete proximal steps (e.g., sober ride, scripts, reminders) and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided argument and reframed sustain talk without judgment, but could have used more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to further diminish sustain talk's influence.
