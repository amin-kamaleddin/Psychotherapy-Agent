# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance from the opening, repeatedly affirmed the patient's choices and empowered them to lead the conversation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated disappointment and readiness; could deepen empathic responses by naming emotions more vividly and using amplified/nuanced reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on the behavioral goal (attending interviews) with concrete, measurable steps (scripts, timing, backups, rehearsals) that advanced action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by offering expertise (scripts, role‑play) while inviting the patient to choose options and co‑construct the plan, fostering shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and avoiding pressure while checking confidence and inviting ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's own motivations using open questions, scaling, and invitations to articulate steps and confidence, leveraging intrinsic reasons to build a plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint project, regularly invited the patient's input on choices and rehearsals, and honored the patient's expertise about her life in deciding next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with questions about small steps and confidence and supported commitment through planning; could more consistently explore underlying values and reasons to amplify commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and pivoted quickly to solution-focused brainstorming and double-sided reflections; a fuller normalization summary and more explicit diffusion of barriers could further reduce sustain talk.
