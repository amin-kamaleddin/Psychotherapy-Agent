# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental respect, normalizing and validating Liora's experience throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and picked up key details, but could deepen empathic connection with more complex reflections naming exhaustion, numbness, and somatic pain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing self-harm and developing concrete safety-plan steps, consistently tying interventions back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-constructed the plan, offered options, asked permission, and let the client lead decisions, demonstrating clear shared power in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and control, avoided pressuring language, and invited the client's preferences for each detail of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical solutions and small commitments well but focused more on logistics than exploring broader intrinsic reasons, values, or past successes to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance throughout, inviting the client's input and deferring to her expertise when selecting steps and details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and shaped change talk with specific, testable steps, though opportunities to amplify commitment language and assess confidence were sometimes missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it, but did not consistently use double-sided reflections or reframing to normalize and reduce the pull of sustain talk.
