### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating ambivalence and affirming Erik’s strengths throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, calibrated reflections and summaries that matched Erik’s affect and underlying values, demonstrating sustained perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, client-centered focus on exercise and back protection, moving logically from values to specific, doable steps (trainer, calendar, 10‑minute plan).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed, with the clinician soliciting Erik’s input, building on his language, and facilitating decisions together rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

#### **Score (1–5):** 5
