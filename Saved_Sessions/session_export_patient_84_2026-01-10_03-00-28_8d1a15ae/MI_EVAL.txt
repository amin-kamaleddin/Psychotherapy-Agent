# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained unconditional positive regard throughout, validating Malik's perspective and avoiding judgment while affirming his goals and concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking were used to capture competing pressures and motivations, though reflections could probe deeper emotional nuance (belonging, shame) and use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session kept a clear focus on cannabis use and related goals, linking exchanges to concrete, measurable steps (protected nights, homework deadline, scripts, month check-in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with joint problem-solving, shared decision-making about nights, scripts, and role-play, and Malik's preferences shaping the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supported Malik's choice and control by offering options, avoiding pressure, and emphasizing slips as opportunities to reset rather than failures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good use of open questions and confidence scaling to elicit Malik's reasons and solutions, though deeper probing of intrinsic motivations and stronger amplification of change language would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician fostered partnership by inviting input and co-creating the plan, with a slight drift toward offering clinician-suggested wording before eliciting Malik's own phrasing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was actively elicited and reinforced with concrete commitments (no use on school nights, homework deadline), but the clinician missed chances to amplify and consolidate commitment language and confidence drivers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was skillfully rolled with and not confronted, developing discrepancy through problem-solving, though more strategic double-sided reflections and gentle amplification of tentative change statements could further soften sustain talk.
