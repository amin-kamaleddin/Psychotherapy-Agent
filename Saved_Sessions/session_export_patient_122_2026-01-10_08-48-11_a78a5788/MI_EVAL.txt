# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard with normalization and affirmations (e.g., opening invitation, validating slips), avoiding blaming language and conveying warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries and acknowledged mixed feelings, but tended to move quickly into solutions rather than exploring emotional nuance more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on concrete target behaviors (10‑minute walk, if‑then plan) and repeatedly asked implementation questions to maintain goal-directed work.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—checked preferences and co‑constructed the plan—but frequent provision of ready‑made scripts and stepwise implementation shifted some decisions back to the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supported choice by offering alternatives and asking which step felt doable, though several prescriptive suggestions and exact wording could have been framed more as options or offered after permission.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced existing change talk and used choice questions, but missed deeper elicitation of personal values, long‑term reasons, and past successes to draw out intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and invited input, yet supplying specific scripts, trackers, and detailed implementation sometimes took an expert lead rather than fully partnering on specifics.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk, encouraged concrete commitments and contingency plans, but could have probed more to amplify desire, reasons, and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and offered fallback plans to minimize its impact, but could have used more double‑sided reflections and explicit reframes toward strengths.
