# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth with validating, nonjudgmental language, creating a safe climate for the patient to share.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, with timely reflections that capture both content and feeling and deepen self-exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, steady focus on follow-through and confidence, guiding the session from problem description to a specific implementation plan and troubleshooting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the interaction as a partnership by offering choices, inviting input and rehearsal, and letting the patient shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports the patient's sense of choice through options, readiness/confidence checks, and avoidance of pressuring language, reinforcing patient control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation using confidence rulers and exploring supports, but could more often evoke deeper personal values and selectively reinforce client-generated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Exhibits strong power-sharing by asking permission, offering options, and co-creating concrete steps, with the patient's choices driving next actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk via choices, confidence ratings, and a concrete plan, though would benefit from more selective amplification and elaboration of the patient's change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reduces sustain talk with double-sided reflections and tiny fallback plans without arguing, but could more strategically juxtapose sustain and change talk and elicit the patient's arguments against staying the same.
