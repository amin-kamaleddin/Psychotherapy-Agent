# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental from the opening line, normalized concerns and affirmed the patient's actions without shaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and paraphrase, repeatedly checked understanding, and tailored information to the patient’s specific worries, helping move them from confusion to reassurance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on understanding and safely initiating atorvastatin with each question and plan tied directly to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by eliciting specifics, checking interactions, and co‑creating the portal message and symptom checklist based on the patient's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported the patient's choice, offered options and reasonable advice while avoiding coercive language, and affirmed the patient’s ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation by asking what the patient wanted and offering choices, but relied mainly on information exchange rather than systematically drawing out deeper personal reasons or confidence for long‑term adherence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative throughout: repeatedly solicited preferences, let the patient shape content, and shared power in planning and decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several change statements and invited commitment (e.g., timing choice), but missed chances to amplify change talk by exploring why adherence matters or assessing confidence and implementation intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated and normalized skepticism and avoided arguing, then redirected to practical steps, though could have used more double‑sided reflections to explicitly soften ambivalence.
