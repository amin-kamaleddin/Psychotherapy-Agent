# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating language conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective summaries acknowledged emotional weight and ambivalence, helping Lars feel understood and opening space for planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on concrete target behaviors (avoiding risky weekends, vocational steps) and tied questions and reflections to actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly an equal partnership—clinician solicited preferences, followed Lars’ lead, and co-constructed plans and role-plays based on his requests.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed Lars’ choices and invited decisions, though offering scripts and blunt lines (even when helpful) risked feeling slightly prescriptive without explicit permission.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Lars’ own motivations and practical ideas using targeted questions and reflections, eliciting concrete implementation intentions and change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used inclusive language and shared decision-making throughout, repeatedly asking which options Lars preferred and engaging him in rehearsals and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced preparatory change talk and concrete plans, but could have further amplified and linked Lars’ change statements to core values to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double-sided reflections and alternatives, but moved relatively quickly to solutions without fully exploring sustain-talk themes like boredom and embarrassment.
