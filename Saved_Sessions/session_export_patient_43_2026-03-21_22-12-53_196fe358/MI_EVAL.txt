# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with permission‑giving and validating reflections, framing steps as optional and nonjudgmental.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and probed bodily sensations, which deepened disclosure, but could have explored underlying emotions and the personal impact of wife/doctor pressure more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking and risk reduction throughout and progressed logically to a concrete implementation intention without imposing goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co‑creative—checked preferences, offered options, and integrated the client's choices into the plan rather than dictating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's right to be 'not ready,' offered optional experiments, and used noncoercive language to preserve client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client motivations and resources (what smoking gives him, confidence ruler, experiments), but could have probed deeper intrinsic values and sustained change motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership by inviting choices, asking permission, and letting the client shape the specifics of the experiment and backup plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and translated mild change talk into a specific, actionable test, though could have more consistently amplified and explored personally meaningful reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and reframed sustain talk toward low‑stakes experimentation, but could have used more double‑sided reflections to explicitly highlight discrepancies and further soften sustain talk.
