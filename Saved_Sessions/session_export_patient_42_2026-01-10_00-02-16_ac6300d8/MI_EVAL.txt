# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and repeatedly validates Elise's social concerns. Lost points because responses are sometimes solution-focused and formulaic rather than individualized expressions of unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides several accurate reflections and normalizes cravings, showing understanding of Elise's ambivalence. Could deepen emotional reflection and linger more on feelings instead of quickly moving to practical advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on smoking cessation tied to social bonding and consistently links interventions to that target. Actions and role-plays are directly aimed at actionable next steps toward quitting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Often invites choice, mapping, and joint practice which supports a team approach. At times shifts to an instructional mode with long lists and scripted replies that reduce co-construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports Elise's control by offering options, asking permission, and asking her to pick tactics. Language and framing consistently reinforce client autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to elicit motivation through mapping and role-play, producing practical commitments. However, the clinician frequently supplies solutions instead of drawing out deeper personal reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes collaborative invitations and shared exercises but often adopts an expert stance with detailed plans and scripted language. More co-creation in Elise's own words would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change-oriented language via mapping, role-play, and asking her to pick tactics. Misses opportunities to use open questions and reflections to draw out Elise's own reasons, desire, and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and validates sustain talk without judgment and offers strategies to preserve social connection. Does not consistently use MI-specific softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) and often responds with practical fixes that may minimize sustain talk.
