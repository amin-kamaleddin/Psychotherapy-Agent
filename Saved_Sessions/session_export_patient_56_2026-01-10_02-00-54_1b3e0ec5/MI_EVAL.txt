# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating and normalizing the client's concerns to create psychological safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections captured both content and feeling (e.g., fatigue and tradeoffs), helping the client deepen articulation and feel understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on protecting family happiness via self-care/boundaries and moved logically from values clarification to concrete steps without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative with co-created solutions (multiple script drafts, timer, rehearsal) and clinician integrated the client's feedback throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered options and invitations, emphasized trialing and the client's ownership, and respected comfort levels without pressuring commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively linked change to the client's core value (family happiness) and elicited concrete experiments, but could have more deeply drawn out the client's own motivations, confidence, and commitment statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing and treated the client as the expert, inviting input and co-creating the plan with clear shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged specific, workable change language and small experiments and elicited preferences, though missed opportunities to ask for the client's own reasons for change and to assess commitment/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was minimal and the clinician skillfully acknowledged and reframed worries, offering low-risk experiments and normalization to minimize resistance.
