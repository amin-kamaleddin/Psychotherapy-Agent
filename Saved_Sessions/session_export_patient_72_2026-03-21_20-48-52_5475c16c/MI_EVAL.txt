# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support through invitations, affirmations, and reassurance, creating a warm, respectful stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, validating reflections and summaries of content and plans, but could deepen empathy by reflecting underlying emotions and using more complex affect-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on restarting sustainable exercise with concrete steps (YMCA visit, trial details, appointment, backups) and repeatedly returned to planning and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting the client's input at each step, co-creating the plan, and integrating her practical preferences into the strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by emphasizing no pressure, offering options, asking permission before tools or role-play, and framing suggestions as optional.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons, confidence, and action steps, but could further deepen intrinsic motivation by exploring personal values, past successes, and using summaries to consolidate change talk into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership through shared decision-making, offering choices about trial and tour details, and repeatedly asking for the client's preferences and consent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified the client's change language, invited concrete implementation intentions, and used permissioned tools and rehearsals to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers and redirected to practical coping strategies and contingencies, but could improve by using more double-sided reflections and deeper reflection of sustain concerns before moving to solutions.
