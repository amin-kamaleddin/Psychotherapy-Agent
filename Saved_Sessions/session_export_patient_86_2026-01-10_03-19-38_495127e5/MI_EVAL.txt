# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Marco's stance throughout, avoiding judgment or pressure even in the face of sustain talk.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections mirror Marco's language and feeling-tone (guarded, weary, protective of autonomy), building rapport and lowering resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on dental harm reduction and consistently narrows toward a specific, achievable behavior (rinsing/brushing) with purposeful questions toward action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is joint problem‑solving: clinician and Marco co‑generate and refine the 'stub, pump, swill' plan with the clinician soliciting and adapting to Marco's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Marco's right to choose, resists pressuring language, and offers options while asking which would be easiest rather than prescribing a course of action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits intrinsic reasons for change tied to valued outcomes (keeping teeth), but misses opportunities to deepen evocation by exploring confidence, past successes, and broader values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician treats Marco as the expert on his life, asking preferences and co‑designing the plan so Marco's choices shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk that leads to a concrete one‑week trial, though the clinician could further amplify and consolidate commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and rolls with sustain talk to reduce defensiveness, but sometimes restates sustain language and could better use double‑sided or change‑focused reflections to soften resistance.
