# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Linda's concerns and affirming her efforts without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured both content and affect, repeatedly checking preferences and showing an empathic stance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on inhaler adherence and asthma management, steering to practical strategies, safety information, and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, co-created question lists and scripts, used inclusive language, and invited Linda to lead choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, asked permission before giving information or role-playing, and reinforced Linda's right to choose what to try without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivations and action-oriented statements (choice of target, rehearsals) but leaned on information-giving rather than probing values, importance/confidence, or past successes to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly operated from a partnership stance by inviting input, tailoring wording to Linda, and sharing power in decision-making and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and supported change talk with concrete steps and scripting, but missed opportunities to amplify and probe for reasons, values, and confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed fears toward manageable concerns and actions, though sometimes moved quickly into information-giving instead of using double-sided reflections or strategic summaries.
