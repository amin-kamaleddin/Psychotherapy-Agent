# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized challenges, using supportive, nonjudgmental language throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking to show understanding of triggers and readiness, but missed opportunities for deeper affective reflection about stress and low confidence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on smoking cessation with concrete, stepwise planning (meds, starter regimen, scripts, and follow-up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged the patient as a partner by soliciting detailed input and tailoring recommendations, repeatedly incorporating patient priorities into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered meaningful choices, confirmed preferences and consent, and reinforced the patient’s control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited change reasons, readiness, and triggers and used them to shape the plan, but often shifted into prescriptive information without further deepening the patient’s own motivations or self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance, negotiated the plan together, and invited the patient’s input on logistics and prescriber communication, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (benefits, reasons, preferences) but could have paused more to amplify prior successes, bolster confidence, and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not reinforce sustain talk while offering alternatives and strategies, though more double-sided reflections and exploration of smoking’s function would further soften sustain talk.
