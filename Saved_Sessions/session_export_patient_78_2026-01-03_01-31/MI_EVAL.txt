# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental stance (e.g., “I hear you — no judgment,” “I won’t lecture”). Explicitly respected boundaries and normalized slips, preserving rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking (“that makes sense…pull both ways”) capturing ambivalence. Missed opportunities for deeper affective exploration (shame/anger about feeling judged).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking cessation, used importance/confidence rulers, elicited past attempts and moved to a concrete plan (nicotine gum + 30‑minute delay; discreet desk strategies).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed work as joint project and invited input (“What might feel doable?”). At times provided many options without pausing for the client to prioritize, limiting client-led decision making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Lucía’s right to choose, minimized pressure, normalized slips, and offered supports without coercion (“If you want more support later, call the quitline or come back”).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client reasons and motivations (importance/confidence, past attempts, daughter as motive) and facilitated concrete change talk. Could have probed more deeply into values and long‑term consequences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed partnership through joint planning and checking what felt doable. Slight drift to an expert/informational role when offering long lists of tips and brand recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk (e.g., “I want to be around for my daughter,” commitment to try gum + delay). Missed chances to amplify and chain change talk to stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and rolled with sustain talk, preserving rapport. Did not consistently use double‑sided reflections or strategic reframing to shift persistent sustain talk (client repeatedly: “Every time I go to any doctor…”).
