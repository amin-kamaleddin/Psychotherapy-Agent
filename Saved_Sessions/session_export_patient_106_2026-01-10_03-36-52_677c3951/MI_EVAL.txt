# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental, warm tone—validating worries, normalizing slips, and affirming efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and perspective-taking with emotion naming and brief reflections, but reflections were often pragmatic and could be deeper to capture loneliness and fatigue.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction and protecting the child with concrete harm-reduction steps, specific planning, and follow-up homework to keep momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative—soliciting the patient's ideas and practicing skills together—but at times instructions were instructional and clinician-led rather than fully co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the patient's choice and agency by offering options, asking what she wanted to try, normalizing choice, and avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited motivation with readiness/confidence questions and having the patient state plans aloud, but relied more on offering solutions than asking open questions to draw out deeper values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance through permission-seeking and shared planning, though some directive, prescriptive language slightly shifted power toward the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk using scales, affirmations, and specific plans, but could better amplify and reflect the patient's own change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and did not challenge minimizations, yet missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections or explore ambivalence before moving to solutions.
