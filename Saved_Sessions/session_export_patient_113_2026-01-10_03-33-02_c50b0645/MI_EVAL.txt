### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance from the opening through affirmations, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid empathic listening with accurate reflections that validate Rowan's experience, though some reflections are brief and the clinician sometimes shifts quickly to problem-solving rather than exploring deeper feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (charging and using the device) with concrete, goal-oriented steps, if-then plans, and timed check-backs.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed as a joint enterprise throughout, inviting Rowan's preferences, negotiating follow-up, and co-constructing action steps so decisions are made together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Rowan's choice and control by offering options, avoiding pressure, and explicitly emphasizing that decisions are up to him.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits some small commitments and ideas but focuses more on pragmatic implementation than on deeper personal reasons or values that could strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance, invites Rowan's expertise and choice, and co-creates plans while honoring his input and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through questions and affirming small steps, but could better amplify tentative statements to build stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and responds with validation and gentle reframing toward small steps, though more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries could further reduce sustain talk.
