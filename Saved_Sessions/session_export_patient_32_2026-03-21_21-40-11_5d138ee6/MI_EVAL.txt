### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, acknowledging losses and social context with warmth. The clinician avoided judgment and thanked the client for engaging, fostering a safe atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and reflective listening, accurately summarizing Derrick’s conflicts and emotions. Reflections matched his concerns and built rapport and trust without minimizing his experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on preventing a repeat DUI, linking each intervention to practical protective strategies. The session progressed logically from exploring concerns to a concrete one-week experiment and implementation intentions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly cooperative, with the clinician soliciting Derrick’s preferences and offering options rather than prescribing solutions. Decisions and specific steps came from Derrick, evidencing genuine teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Derrick’s sense of choice by offering multiple options and asking which he preferred to do. The clinician emphasized voluntary control and reinforced ownership of the plan throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete change language, plans, and confidence scaling, using strategies to move motivation forward. Could further deepen intrinsic motivation by more systematically linking plans to broader personal values or longer‑term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a collaborative exploration and deferred to Derrick’s expertise about his life. The clinician co-constructed specific steps and invited role-play and rehearsal, demonstrating shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified specific change talk (plans, confidence, small experiments) and linked past success to current steps. There were missed opportunities to more frequently reflect and explicitly amplify brief commitment statements to solidify momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and accepted minimizing views to prevent entrenching resistance, using reflective responses. Could more actively use double-sided reflections and immediately pair sustain reflections with change language to further reduce sustain pull.
