# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing and validating statements and a respectful, nonjudgmental tone. Jamal is repeatedly affirmed and never labeled or shamed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing both emotional and practical concerns. Could probe deeper into underlying feelings and use more nuanced reflections rather than occasional formulaic summaries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on punctuality and getting to school on time, tying every question and decision to concrete steps and measurable outcomes. There is no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is strongly collaborative: the clinician co-designs the experiment, repeatedly solicits Jamal’s ideas, and treats him as an equal partner in problem-solving. Power is shared throughout planning and measurement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports Jamal’s autonomy by offering choices, framing the plan as his experiment, and avoiding pressuring language. Repeated statements reinforce that the change is his to control and pursue at his own pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Jamal’s reasons, desires, and concrete visions of success, building intrinsic motivation. Would be strengthened by deeper, value-based elicitation and by pressing for commitment and confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and invites Jamal to choose strategies and measurements, centering his expertise about his routine. Occasional clinician self-statements of belief slightly shift the balance toward encouragement rather than pure client-led authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, generating clear desire/reason/need language and a concrete one-week experiment. Missed opportunities remain to deepen commitment and elicit confidence/importance scaling to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and ambivalence but does not consistently use stronger MI techniques to soften it, so external motivation stays prominent. Could use double-sided and strategic reflections to selectively emphasize change talk and shift framing toward intrinsic reasons.
