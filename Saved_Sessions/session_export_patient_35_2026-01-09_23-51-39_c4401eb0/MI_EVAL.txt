# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Elena’s efforts, acknowledging competing demands (work, single parenting) without blame. Repeated affirmations validate her priorities and caring stance toward her child.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates frequent, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both feelings and facts (exhaustion, ambivalence, smoking as coping). Follow-up open questions show deep understanding of Elena’s constraints and priorities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing child exposure and on-shift smoking reduction, moving from priorities to concrete steps and troubleshooting. Each question advances planning (options → small step → supports) while remaining client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reads as collaborative problem-solving: clinician solicits Elena’s ideas, builds on them, and integrates practical constraints like schedule and supports. The clinician reinforces and consolidates Elena’s plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly upholds choice and ownership by using open questions, normalizing struggle, and avoiding prescriptive language. Supports trial-and-adjust approach so Elena retains control over her plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits Elena’s own reasons and strategies through evocative questions (priorities, past successes, concrete signs of success). Elena generates a practical, self-authored plan (gum, alarm, leaving cigarettes at home).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as co-constructed, repeatedly inviting Elena’s expertise and choices about what will fit her life. Clinician helps organize and support her decisions rather than directing them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with open invitations and reinforcement of attainable options, then probes for a specific small step and concrete signs of success. Moves consistently draw out Elena’s own reasons and plans for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to pragmatic exposure-reduction strategies and small steps, using some double-sided framing. Could be stronger with more explicit targeted reflections that name and then reframe sustain statements to further defuse resistance.
