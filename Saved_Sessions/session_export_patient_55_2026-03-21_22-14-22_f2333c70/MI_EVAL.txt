# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, acknowledging constraints (IB, band) and conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, validating ambivalence and reflecting the client's schedule and feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on the client's chosen goals (stress/time management) and moves logically to a specific, measurable plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the client as an equal partner by soliciting preferences, co-creating the if-then plan, and negotiating check-in details and nudges together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, frames suggestions as optional, and respects the client's hesitancy, supporting the client's sense of control over choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client's reasons and uses a confidence ruler and options to draw out motivation, but could more strongly probe past successes, values, and amplify tentative change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine power-sharing through joint planning, asking which supports feel realistic, and incorporating the client's ideas into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits client language favoring small changes around time-management and stress reduction, but missed opportunities to amplify tentative commitments into stronger change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Does not reinforce sustain talk about alcohol and appropriately pivots to prioritized goals, though it did not use double-sided reflections or pros/cons exploration to further resolve ambivalence.
