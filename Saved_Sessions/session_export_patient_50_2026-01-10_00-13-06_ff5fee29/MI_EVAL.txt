# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Hana’s reactions with validating language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections captured both somatic and cognitive aspects of Hana’s anxiety and matched her affect and content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the specific behavioral goal (a short timed practice test) and guided concrete planning (time, duration, coping, reminders).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the plan with Hana by soliciting her wording, respecting edits, and following her lead on supports and pacing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission before offering help, and framed suggestions as optional to reinforce Hana’s control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited values-linked reasons and used a readiness ruler, but could have probed more deeply to amplify intrinsic motivation and confidence into commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative, though at times the clinician offered multiple concrete suggestions without always pausing to elicit Hana’s first preference.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced preparatory change talk and used readiness scaling, but missed opportunities to deepen and amplify change statements into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reframed barriers and shifted to coping strategies, but used few explicit techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to reduce the pull of sustain talk.
