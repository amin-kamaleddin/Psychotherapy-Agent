### Acceptance

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Session maintained a tight focus on a single, concrete target behavior and clinician repeatedly guided toward actionable elements without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited Ingrid’s reasons for change and concrete steps, but could have probed motivation strength (confidence/importance) and amplified Ingrid’s change statements more explicitly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently treated Ingrid as a co-creator of the plan, soliciting her rationale and preferences and privileging her lived expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and reinforced change language and a concrete trial plan, though they missed opportunities to amplify commitment language and use scaling to deepen resolve.
