# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing the patient’s routine and ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries capture the patient’s perspective, but reflections lean toward content and solutions rather than deeper emotional or complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on coffee use and related jitters/BP concerns, moving logically from exploration to concrete strategies, skill practice, and a measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as a team effort with shared decision-making; the clinician co-constructs reminders, agenda items, and the behavior experiment with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers multiple options, explicitly defers choices to the patient, and affirms small, self-directed steps, consistently fostering the patient’s sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some commitment to a trial but relies more on offering solutions than drawing out intrinsic motivations or exploring personal values and meanings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance—clinician repeatedly invites input, negotiates plans, and respects the patient’s preferences in shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits occasional pragmatic change talk and a week-long trial, but misses opportunities to deepen or amplify reasons/importance/confidence using scaling or follow-up reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward harm-reduction and small experiments effectively, though could more often use double-sided reflections to tip ambivalence toward change.
