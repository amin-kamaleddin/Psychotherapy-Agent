# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client’s experience, acknowledging shame without blame to create a warm, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides frequent, accurate reflections of content and affect that capture ambivalence and deepen the client's self-expression.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on gambling/credit behavior and uses concrete planning questions and strategies to keep the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Models a co-constructed process by inviting client choices, checking preferences, and letting the client's ideas drive the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice, offers options, solicits preferences, and avoids pressuring language so the client retains ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits intrinsic motivations (e.g., travel, pride, future parenting) and ties those values to concrete steps, drawing motivation from the client herself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative, asks what would help most, and defers to the client's expertise so the plan emerges from her language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, asks for specific small steps and supports, and amplifies commitment language to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to change, but could more strategically use double-sided reflections and discrepancy amplification to further soften sustain talk and strengthen commitment.
