# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing Daniela’s concerns. Supported her boundary requests (promise not to tell) and validated her feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and reflective listening, accurately capturing Daniela’s worries and desired reactions. Used focused empathic questions and validating language that showed emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (disclosure to Maya) and practical steps to achieve it. Kept the conversation goal-directed with specific actions like scripts, timing, and a pre-talk checklist.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the clinician as an ally by inviting Daniela to shape the plan and offering to role-play or rehearse. Encouraged joint decision-making about wording, timing, and supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Daniela’s choice and control, offering options and asking which approach felt more doable. Respected privacy preferences and avoided pressure, reinforcing her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited strengths and concrete steps that generated change statements, but missed opportunities to probe deeper values or long-term reasons for disclosure. Tended to offer options rather than systematically drawing out Daniela’s own arguments for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a genuine collaborative stance, asking permission and deferring to Daniela’s preferences while co-constructing the plan. Invited her input on rehearsal, timing, and how she wanted others to respond.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (doable steps, practice, scripting) leading to clearer commitment language. Could have deepened and amplified change talk by asking more open, values-oriented questions and reflecting Daniela’s change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully reframed sustain talk by acknowledging concerns and offering manageable, normalized options (practice with one friend, short lines). Redirected avoidance toward small actions without minimizing Daniela’s fears.
