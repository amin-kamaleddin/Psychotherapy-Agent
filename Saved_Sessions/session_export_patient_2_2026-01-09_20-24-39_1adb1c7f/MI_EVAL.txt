# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and affirming, acknowledges Aisha's burdens and efforts; would reach a 5 with more individualized, empathic normalization of ambivalence and occasional unconditional validation of her whole experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows perspective-taking and a supportive opening but reflective listening is limited and the clinician moves quickly to solutions; deeper, affect-focused reflections and checks for understanding are needed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on smoking reduction throughout the session and consistently steers content back to a concrete smoking plan; refrains from making alcohol the primary focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Frames some elements as collaborative (one-week experiment, follow-up) but primarily functions as the expert providing detailed instructions; Aisha's ideas and preferences are respected but not consistently co‑constructed into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirms choices, offers options, and respects Aisha's decision not to address drinking while inviting her to choose next steps; could more consistently emphasize ownership by asking permission before advice and using more open questions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits modest commitment via a one‑week experiment and acknowledges stated reasons for change, but does not systematically explore intrinsic motivators, values, or past successes; session leans toward information‑giving rather than deeper evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasionally invites input and follow‑up but the interaction is clinician-driven with many specifics presented rather than co-created; more elicitation of Aisha's ideas and prior successes would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces existing change talk and operationalizes a concrete plan, but lacks strategic evocation using open questions to deepen importance and confidence; change talk is acknowledged but not systematically elicited or amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and respects Aisha's stance on drinking, but including 'avoid alcohol' among tactics risks provoking resistance; few reflections or double‑sided statements were used to acknowledge sustain talk while leaning toward change.
