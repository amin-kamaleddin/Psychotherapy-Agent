# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validating Amina’s distress and affirming her motives throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing values and practical burdens, but reflections were more content-level than deep affective reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, behaviorally defined focus on establishing a bedtime routine and consistently steered the session toward concrete next steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine co-creation through role-play, joint message drafting, and shared problem-solving while leaving key decisions to Amina.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options, asked permission before interventions, and emphasized Amina’s control and choice without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out Amina’s motivations and a concrete plan, but could have more systematically elicited and amplified specific change-talk categories and confidence/importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful of Amina’s expertise, though at times moved into more directive solution-giving without explicit permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change language well, prompting a concrete plan, but missed opportunities to amplify desire/ability/commitment and elicit past successes or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered low-threat alternatives and scripts, but could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
