# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, using normalization and affirmations while honoring the patient’s limits. No blaming or shaming language, supporting a respectful and safe interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that matched Ahmed’s language and concerns, demonstrating strong perspective‑taking. The clinician picked up on pragmatic and family/work values and validated his experience throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on tobacco use and related health concerns while guiding the conversation toward concrete, testable actions. Progressed from open exploration to a specific plan with start date, confidence rating, barriers, and follow‑up arrangements.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co‑constructed, inviting Ahmed’s input at every decision point and using his language in reflections and summaries. The plan was shaped by Ahmed’s choices, indicating a fully collaborative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Ahmed’s control by asking permission, offering options, and honoring his decision not to quit for now. Language emphasized choice and reduced pressure, supporting ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focused on eliciting Ahmed’s own motivations through targeted questions (worries, confidence ruler, doable steps) that produced personal reasons and self‑generated strategies. Clinician drew out intrinsic motivations like protecting job and family.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently sought permission and preferences, offering options and framing decisions as Ahmed’s to make. Power‑sharing behaviors signaled a genuine partnership in planning and problem‑solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language using questions about worries, offering options, confidence scaling, and affirmations, which led to concrete commitment statements. Reflections and summaries strengthened Ahmed’s stated plan and intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not amplify sustain talk, redirecting to harm‑reduction steps, but missed some micro‑opportunities for deeper ambivalence work. Could improve by using more double‑sided reflections and explicitly exploring discrepancy to further soften sustain talk.
