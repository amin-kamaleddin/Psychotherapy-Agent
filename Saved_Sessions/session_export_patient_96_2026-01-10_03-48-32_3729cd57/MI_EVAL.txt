# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized the piercing decision, and avoided judgment or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, sustained empathic understanding through reflective listening and summaries that captured the patient's tone and priorities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged oral-health risks early but largely followed the patient's preference to focus on identity and feelings, missing repeated opportunities to tie exploration back to concrete monitoring or safety steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a joint exploration, solicited the patient's agenda, and co-created a concrete plan (the weekend experiment) while offering supports by permission.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offered options, asked permission before shifting focus, and reinforced that decisions about the piercing and next steps remain the patient's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out identity-based motivations and elicited a behavioral test, but did not consistently link those motivations to protective health actions or longer-term monitoring.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance, deferred to the patient's expertise about her values, and integrated her preferences into the plan and agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-oriented statements and a conditional plan (checking with piercer/dentist) but did not systematically reflect, amplify, or assess confidence to strengthen commitment to protective actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used validating reflections, though could have used more double-sided reflections or summaries to gently juxtapose sustain and change language toward safety planning.
