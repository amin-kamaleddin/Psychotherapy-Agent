# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes exhaustion, reducing shame and blame to support the client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate reflections of content and affect with timely perspective-taking; could occasionally name deeper emotions (e.g., frustration) more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing short walks and progressively narrows to a specific, actionable plan (calendar block, alarm, Sam nudge).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as genuine co‑creation by eliciting preferences, weighing options together, and avoiding an expert, prescriptive stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses invitational questions and affirmations that preserve choice and agency, avoiding pressuring language while supporting experimentation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out practical motives and past successes but explores intrinsic values and confidence less; could probe personal meaning and use brief scaling for commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving, follows the patient's lead, and co‑constructs the plan through repeated invites for input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with targeted prompts and follow-ups, producing concrete commitments (calendar block, exact SMS and time).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges and reframes sustain talk, normalizing self-doubt and redirecting toward small, doable strategies without arguing.
