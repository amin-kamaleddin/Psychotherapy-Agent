# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation throughout (e.g., “I’m so sorry—you’re in awful pain…”, “this is unbearable and it’s not stupid”). Avoided blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and affective naming matched patient affect and language; stayed with emotion and attunement facilitated disclosure of concrete suicidal ideation and supports.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate clinical focus on immediate safety (plan/means checks, options for contacting supports, contingency if Rosa doesn't arrive); guided toward concrete safety steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed work as joint (asked permission, offered choices), co-created safety plan, stayed on the line to support call and honored patient preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed agency and offered options rather than directives (e.g., “Which of those options feels most possible?”; asked what patient wanted if Rosa didn’t arrive).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflected the patient’s “small part” wanting relief and to be with her sister, which elicited some change talk; missed opportunities to follow with evocative questions to deepen and expand reasons for staying.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly acted as a partner (scripted call with patient, negotiated escalation plan, sought patient input) and shared power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Noted and reflected limited change talk (the “small part” twice) but did not systematically elicit DARN-C language, amplify reasons, or obtain explicit commitment statements or brief scaling queries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used neutral/double-sided reflections to acknowledge both despair and reasons to stay; could have used more consistent double-sided reflections and reframes to further reduce sustain talk.
