# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard, warm language, and nonjudgmental validation throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and checks for understanding that capture both emotion and practical concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on protecting a weekly downtime slot and skillfully moves from values to specific, actionable planning steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is co‑creative and team‑based, offering options and following the patient's preferences while offering concrete supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offers choices and frames supports as optional, affirming the patient's control over decisions and implementation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits the patient's ideas and plan well, but could more deliberately draw out deeper intrinsic motives and amplify change language (e.g., ask about meaning, importance, or confidence).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A clear partnership stance is maintained—power is shared, the patient is invited to lead, and the clinician offers resources without directing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective in eliciting concrete change statements and planning, yet missed opportunities to reflect/amplify the patient's own change language and use scaling questions to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was minimal and not reinforced; clinician shifted quickly to actionable options and avoided amplifying resistance.
