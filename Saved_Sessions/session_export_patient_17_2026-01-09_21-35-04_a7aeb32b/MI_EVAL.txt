# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrated consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing shame and reframing slips as non‑failures throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective‑taking were used repeatedly, accurately summarizing both content and affect and checking understanding with summaries and confirmations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on smoking cessation and moved logically from exploration to a concrete, time‑bound plan (start day, patch/gum rules, break strategies, check‑ins).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician worked as a teammate, co‑creating the plan with the patient, asking preferences, and offering to draft messages and reminder lines jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported through offering options, asking permission before intervening, and affirming choice while framing slips as not failures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited concrete reasons for change and used a confidence ruler, but could have probed deeper into intrinsic values and emotional consequences to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was consistently co‑constructed with frequent invitations for the patient's input and deference to her preferences in planning and wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited and reinforced (past successes, reasons, confidence ruler), though the clinician could have more systematically amplified and deepened specific change statements before planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Barriers and sustain talk were acknowledged and normalized with practical strategies offered, but there were missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections and deeper ambivalence resolution.
