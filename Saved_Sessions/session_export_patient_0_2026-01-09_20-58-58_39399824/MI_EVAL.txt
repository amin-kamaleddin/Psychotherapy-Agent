### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, and validated the patient's priorities without shaming or blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections captured both content and affect, fostering elaboration and validating the patient's perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on meth use harm-reduction throughout, with questions and steps that moved toward concrete, actionable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan with the patient, solicited ideas, and integrated suggestions to create genuine teamwork rather than an expert-driven approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized the patient's choice and expertise, avoided pressuring language, and respected the patient's hedges and limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons, benefits, and specific steps from the patient but could have probed readiness/confidence and elicited stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Expressed a collaborative stance, treated the patient as the expert, and invited the patient's preferences at each decision point.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out multiple pieces of change talk and plans, but missed chances to amplify and consolidate those statements into stronger preparatory or commitment talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected toward change, though more double-sided reflections or explicit discrepancy work could further soften sustain talk.
