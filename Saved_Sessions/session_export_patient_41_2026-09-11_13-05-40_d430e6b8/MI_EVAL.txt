# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized Elira’s experience, and validated her efforts without blame. The clinician treated hesitancy as understandable and legitimate throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflections that captured both content and emotional tone and frequently checked understanding. Reflections mirrored ambivalence and moved the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on a specific behavioral target (attending for ten minutes and sending a heads-up text) and tied each intervention back to that goal. The session progressed logically from problem identification to a concrete, time-bound plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by soliciting Elira’s input, offering suggestions tentatively, and co-creating the plan. Exchanges read as joint problem-solving rather than clinician-led instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking which felt best, and inviting consent before composing the message. This reinforced Elira’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out client-generated solutions and made the plan feel doable, eliciting specifics that supported action. Could deepen evocation by asking about personal reasons, values, or scaling confidence and prompting the client to voice her own arguments for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly asked for Elira’s preferences and invited co-creation (e.g., composing the message together), deferring decisions to her. The interaction reflected genuine power-sharing and respect for the client’s expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, prompting concrete options and commitments that led to specific action. Could more explicitly amplify and summarize the client’s motivations and confidence to consolidate change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and pivoted quickly to planning without reinforcing reasons for avoidance. To improve, the clinician could use double-sided reflections or reframes to further defuse sustain talk and help resolve ambivalence.
