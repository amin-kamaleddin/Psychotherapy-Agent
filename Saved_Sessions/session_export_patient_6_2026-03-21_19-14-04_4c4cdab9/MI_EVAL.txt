# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally respectful and nonjudgmental—normalizes disclosure and honors privacy—though explicit affirmations of the patient’s strengths are limited and some responses feel procedural rather than warmly individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows empathic moments (distress check, appreciation) and follows the patient’s lead, but reflective listening of emotions and ambivalence is limited and the clinician often moves quickly to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on adolescent alcohol use and its impact on soccer/scholarships, with concrete, actionable strategies tied to safety and eligibility.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is cooperative and materials are adapted to the patient’s preferences, but the clinician frequently supplies content rather than eliciting the patient’s own wording or solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices and asks permission, respecting the adolescent’s control, though occasional prescriptive lists reduce emphasis on the patient’s ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies mainly on clinician-provided advice and scripts with few open, evocative questions to draw out the patient’s internal motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites input and offers options, but often leads with scripted steps instead of co-creating plans, resulting in a mixed and uneven collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supports change indirectly by providing language tied to values and goals, but misses opportunities to ask open questions, reflect, and amplify the patient’s own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing minimization and redirects to protective strategies, yet misses chances for double-sided reflections and gentler validation to resolve ambivalence.
