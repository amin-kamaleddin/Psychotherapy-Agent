# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validates effort, normalizing difficulty without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, though could deepen emotional exploration of loneliness and identity-related feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on weight‑related behaviors and moves the conversation toward a concrete, actionable plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a joint exploration with invitations to weigh trade‑offs and co‑construct a feasible plan like the weekly jeans check.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers explicit choices repeatedly and affirms the client's selections, reinforcing ownership of decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws out the client's motivations and links steps to personal meaning, but could more often prompt for reasons, confidence, and past successes to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and invites preferences, but sometimes provides a limited menu or offers directive suggestions instead of eliciting the client's own options first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces several strands of change talk and links small actions to outcomes, yet could more actively amplify and consolidate desire/ability/reason language and elicit stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk without confrontation, preventing escalation, but often mirrors sustain talk verbatim rather than using double‑sided reflections or strategic pivots to blunt its influence.
