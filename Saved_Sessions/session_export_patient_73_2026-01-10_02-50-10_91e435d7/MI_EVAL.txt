# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm—normalizes worries, uses affirmations, and treats slips as information rather than failure, conveying unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic statements and validation of fears, but reflections are brief and the clinician often moves quickly to advice rather than offering deeper, complex reflections of ambivalence and shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with concrete, measurable steps (delay first cigarette, refusal lines, coping techniques) and sustained momentum toward actionable change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with roleplay and following Linda's requests, but the clinician sometimes directs by prescribing many specific scripts and tactics rather than eliciting Linda's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering multiple options, framing advice as optional, and validating that setbacks are informative rather than punitive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation is present (small goals, practice prompts), but the clinician does not sufficiently draw out Linda's intrinsic motivations, values, or past successes to deepen commitment to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames decisions as collaborative and follows patient preferences in practice, though an expert stance with numerous specific recommendations occasionally reduces equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits occasional change talk (specific small goal, encouragement), but does not systematically evoke or amplify Linda's reasons, values, or confidence to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers alternatives, yet tends to move quickly to strategy without using techniques like double-sided reflections or exploring identity/group-belonging aspects of sustain talk.
