# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing Jamal's perspective and avoiding blame to preserve rapport with a defensive adolescent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking by summarizing concerns and checking focus, but did not probe or reflect deeper affective tones like irritation or worry.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on concrete behavioral goals (probation compliance, keeping grades) and moved the interaction toward an actionable checklist and planning without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan with Jamal, asked which item to address first, provided tailored scripts, and invited ongoing debriefing—evidence of equal-partner problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly used permission-seeking language and offered clear options, framing suggestions as choices to reinforce Jamal's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked about how marijuana fits with priorities and elicited some externally framed change language, but did not sufficiently draw out intrinsic motivations or use scaling/confidence techniques.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Jamal's input and respected his preferences; the session reflected power-sharing and recognition of Jamal's expertise about what will work for him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited change talk tied to external goals and translated these into steps, but missed opportunities to deepen desire, ability, reasons, or need language to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided confronting minimizations and redirected toward concrete steps to protect priorities, though did not use double-sided reflections or summaries to more actively reduce sustain talk.
