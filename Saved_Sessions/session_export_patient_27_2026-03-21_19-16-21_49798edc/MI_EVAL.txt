# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental support with affirmations and normalization that set a warm, accepting tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows solid empathic listening and brief reflections that validate the patient’s worry, but reflections are often practical rather than exploring deeper emotions and nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior (disclosure) and uses practical, behavior-linked skills (scripts, role-play, emergency plan) to steer toward a concrete goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as a coaching partner—using role-play, asking the patient to choose options, and building on the patient’s choices to co-create the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors patient choice and control by offering options, inviting selection, and framing suggestions as voluntary rather than prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation and preferences and fosters practice, but tends to provide scripts early instead of first drawing out the patient’s own language and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as shared effort—frequently checks preferences, invites role-play choices, and adapts the plan to the patient’s comfort.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change-oriented language and preparatory steps (goal-setting, rehearsal), but could further amplify and elaborate the patient’s change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by validating worries and redirecting to action and manageable alternatives, though more double-sided reflections could better explore ambivalence.
