# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the patient’s choice, validating feelings without shaming or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections that matched the patient’s tone and emotions, creating clear understanding and psychological safety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on the piercing and relevant goals, steering the session toward concrete outputs (scripts, rehearsal, aftercare handout).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the visit by offering options, checking preferences, and adapting pace and content to the patient’s requests.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient’s right to choose, offered options rather than pressure, and honored requests (e.g., no lectures).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did not systematically draw out internal motivations or explore values; missed opportunities for targeted evocation to elicit change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as an equal partnership by inviting input, checking confidence, and incorporating the patient’s preferences into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made minimal attempts to elicit desire, reasons, or commitment for change, largely focusing on supporting the current decision rather than exploring ambivalence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward harm-reduction and boundaries, though could have used more double-sided reflections to gently surface ambivalence.
