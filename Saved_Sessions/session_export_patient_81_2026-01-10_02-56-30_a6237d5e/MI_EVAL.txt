# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalizing language and nonjudgmental reflections, and maintains a nonpressured stance throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening and accurate reflections and tracks emotional shifts, but reflections often stayed at paraphrase level rather than probing deeper affective meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on meth use and related concrete goals, repeatedly bringing the conversation back to measurable, time-bound actions and contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with co-created plans and rehearsal, though offering multiple ready-made scripts at times risked a slightly directive tone despite invitations to modify them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, inviting tailoring of scripts, and consistently returning decisions to Lukas to reinforce ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Lukas's motivations and resources (readiness ruler, concrete first step, trusted supports), but could push further to elicit stronger commitment language and link to values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership through options and joint rehearsal and seeks Lukas's input, with a minor limitation when ready-made materials were presented early but then adapted with his input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk and invites small concrete steps and future-oriented planning, though could more often reflect and deepen 'I need to' statements and confidence work.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids arguing, but uses few strategic softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframing identity-linked reasons) to gently tip the balance.
