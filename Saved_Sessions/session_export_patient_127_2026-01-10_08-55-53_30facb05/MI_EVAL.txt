# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalized urges and validated fear of failure, showing clear unconditional positive regard without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and some reflections, but missed opportunities for deeper emotional or identity-focused reflections beyond pragmatic validation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on relapse-prevention and job-stability goals, translating concerns into concrete, actionable steps and repeated goal-checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—asked preferences, iteratively adjusted wording and tools with the patient, and negotiated next steps rather than imposing them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, endorsing low-pressure plans, and honoring the patient's decisions (e.g., declining role-play).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and commitments but tended to move quickly to solutions instead of systematically probing values, reasons, or confidence to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fostered a collaborative partnership and invited input, though at times the clinician supplied plans and tools rather than co-constructing them from the patient's language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific intentions and small commitments, but could use more open, evocative questions and more frequent amplification of change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence and normalized sustain talk but used limited strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or exploring costs) to actively soften it, shifting instead to solutions.
