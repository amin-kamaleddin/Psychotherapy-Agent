# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, using warm validating language and avoiding blame throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and some reflections (validating fear and shame), but misses opportunities for deeper, complex reflections to fully explore ambivalence and pride.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on relapse risk and immediate safety, guiding a concrete safety plan and actionable steps without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—invites Elias's input at every step, co-constructs the safety plan, and offers suggestions while deferring to his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and framing actions as the patient's decisions without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits actionable intentions through practical planning and commitments but does not sufficiently draw out internal motives, values, or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making and positions Elias as an active collaborator, asking him to choose strategies and co-create scripts and plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical change-oriented commitments (strategies, contacts) but does not consistently probe or amplify intrinsic change talk such as desires, reasons, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to safety and coping, though missed chances to use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence while pivoting to change.
