# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with multiple explicit validations and thank-you statements, avoiding blame or shame and affirming the client's distress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates sustained, specific empathic listening and accurate reflections that mirror Maria's affect and keep her disclosing while asking sensitive safety questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on imminent suicide risk with explicit safety questions and repeated, specific requests for safety actions and information.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a shared process by staying, soliciting small safety steps, and co-creating plans, though the stance becomes more directive when safety requires it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently supports choice by asking permission and offering options, while appropriately imposing necessary limits when imminent risk reduces perceived choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation (sister memory, scaling, 'part of me wants help') but does not consistently follow up to deepen or develop that change talk into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally adopts a collaborative stance—asking permission before contacting others and offering to stay—though authority increases appropriately when immediate safety actions are needed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes intermittent attempts to elicit change talk (sister memory, readiness scale) but misses opportunities to amplify and explore those statements into stronger desire, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validates hopeless/sustain talk and prevents escalation, but relies more on directive safety measures than MI-consistent softening like double-sided reflections or ambivalence summaries.
