# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, acknowledging competing values early and throughout the session. Warmth and respect are maintained without conditional praise or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening that captures both content and ambivalence. Reflections helped the patient expand on thinking and concrete plans.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on reducing risky drinking and avoiding another incident. Skillfully narrowed broad ambivalence into a concrete, actionable goal (leave by 11 twice this week).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative problem-solving with inclusive language and co-constructed decisions. The clinician mirrors the patient's ideas and invites him to choose next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes the patient's choice and respects his honesty about limits, inviting options rather than prescriptions. This consistently supports ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient using evocative questions and building on his values (education, safety). Elicited practical, patient-generated strategies and a measurable short-term goal.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert on his life and preferences, avoiding authority and sharing power in planning. Questions repeatedly invite the patient's preference and choice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evokes and amplifies change talk through targeted open questions and reflections, prompting specific small-step language. Follow-ups deepen commitment to concrete actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and validates sustain talk, reframing changes as low-key to reduce identity threat. Could be more proactive with double-sided reflections or explicit discrepancy to further deflate sustain language.
