# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveyed consistent warmth and positive regard throughout, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the patient's efforts without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured ambivalence and stress triggers, though there were missed opportunities for deeper, more affective or complex reflections about belonging and anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on stopping vaping for the season with stepwise, measurable goals (start date, practice, supports) that advanced toward a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently solicited the patient's ideas, validated them, and jointly constructed the plan, allowing the patient's preferences to shape next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices and control were repeatedly honored through options and permission-seeking, reinforcing the adolescent's ownership of decisions and the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicited the patient's own reasons and resources for change (playing better, avoiding suspension) and built on intrinsic motivation rather than imposing advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction felt collaborative and egalitarian, with the clinician inviting brainstorming, offering options, and deferring to the patient's priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful elicitation and amplification of change talk using scaling questions, specific supports, and commitment details that strengthened the patient's arguments for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward ambivalence and planning, but could use more deliberate double-sided or reframing reflections to further defuse sustain talk.
