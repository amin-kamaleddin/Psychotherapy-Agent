### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect with validating language and a nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking through paraphrase and validation, though could probe deeper for underlying feelings with more affect-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on punctuality and moved the conversation toward concrete, sequential steps and small tests to support change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with frequent checks, options offered, and the client’s choices driving the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission, accepting refusals, and framing suggestions as voluntary rather than prescriptive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motives and plans but relied more on offering solutions than drawing out Jamal’s intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed power-sharing and co-construction of the plan, though offered concrete strategies fairly quickly instead of eliciting more of Jamal’s own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change language and concrete commitments, but could have amplified intrinsic motives and asked importance/confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected toward change with normalization and options, yet missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and exploring costs of staying the same.
