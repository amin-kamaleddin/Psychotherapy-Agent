# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation from the outset, normalizing feelings and avoiding blame or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening with accurate reflections of ambivalence and affect, but could use more deeper, emotion-focused reflections before moving to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on tobacco cessation throughout and translated motivation into a specific, actionable plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently worked jointly with the client, inviting input and co-creating the plan (reminder text, coping card, check-in) rather than imposing expert-driven advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported client choice and control by offering options, seeking permission before advice, and emphasizing the client's decision-making throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited importance and confidence and reinforced client-generated ideas, but could more consistently draw out values, past successes, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the client as an equal partner and expert on her life, asking which options felt doable and inviting edits to the plan and wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited desire, reasons, and preparatory planning and affirmed choices, though missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate change statements into stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and barriers without arguing and offered practical alternatives, but relied more on solutions than explicit MI strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to soften sustain talk.
