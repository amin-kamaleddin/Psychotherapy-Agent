# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through normalization and affirmation, avoiding blame or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the client's language and validating feelings which enabled elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking-related goals, moving logically from values to specific, actionable implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint exploration, soliciting input and building the plan from the client's ideas to foster genuine co-production.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and control, offering options and reinforcing the client's ownership of decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the client's own motivations and solutions with targeted questions, producing internally generated change language and a concrete plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited the client to co-create the plan, deferred to his expertise about his context, and repeatedly asked for preferences to share power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and concrete commitments, but missed some opportunities to amplify reasons and confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double-sided reflections, though relied more on neutral reflection than targeted techniques to tip ambivalence toward change.
