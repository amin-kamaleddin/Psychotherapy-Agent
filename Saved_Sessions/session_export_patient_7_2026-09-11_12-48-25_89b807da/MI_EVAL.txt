# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, repeatedly normalizing ambivalence and affirming Amina’s perspective without shaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate empathic listening with multiple complex reflections that captured both emotional concerns and pragmatic worries, mirroring the client's guardedness sensitively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the concrete target behavior (a short boundary statement and small practice steps), keeping the conversation on a realistic, stepwise plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly co-constructed: the clinician solicited Amina’s input at each decision point and adjusted suggestions to fit her preferences and safety concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly emphasized choice and control, framing steps as experiments the client could opt into and respecting her decisions about caseworker involvement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client’s own practical ideas and preferences (e.g., rehearsal, writing a line) but missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivation or link steps to personal values or benefits.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invited co-design of the plan and shared power by asking which options felt doable, reinforcing an equal, collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully generated preparatory change talk through very small, controllable options, though the clinician could have amplified and reinforced Amina’s tentative change statements more deliberately.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed sustain-talk into low-risk experiments, but did not sufficiently probe the client’s repeated 'I probably won't' statements to soften resistance or explore barriers.
