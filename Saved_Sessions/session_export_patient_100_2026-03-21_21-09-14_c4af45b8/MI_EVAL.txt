# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (e.g., opening support, normalizing pace, validating disclosure), creating a warm, safe tone throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently used reflections and perspective-taking and calibrated well to Maya's guarded style, but could deepen emotional reflections and use more complex reflections of ambivalence to reach a top rating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and a specific behavioral goal (leaving by 11) and linked discussion to consequences, though a brief summary/check of longer-term priorities would strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—decisions were co-created, language was tailored to Maya's voice, and the clinician honored preferences while inviting rehearsal and tailoring.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy with permission-seeking, offering a menu of options, accepting hesitancy, and emphasizing Maya's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation via open questions, scaling, and choice of strategies which produced a concrete plan, but could probe more for intrinsic motives and amplify spontaneous change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and repeatedly solicited Maya's voice (choices, tweaks, rehearsal), though at times providing many concrete suggestions slightly shifted the balance toward clinician expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk (importance/confidence scales, menu leading to an exit plan) but did not consistently follow up to deepen desire, reasons, or confidence or to reflect and summarize change statements to strengthen them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized ambivalence with permission-seeking and acceptance, but could more actively shift sustain talk using techniques like double-sided reflections and selective emphasis of change language.
