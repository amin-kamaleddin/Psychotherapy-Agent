# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently nonjudgmental and supportive (e.g., normalizes feelings, offers options). Suggestion noted to name specific strengths to further consolidate acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Accurate, affect-focused reflections and validation (e.g., “that sounds really scary and exhausting,” safety checks). Could deepen by linking feelings to values/consequences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on target behavior (scheduling intake); provided scripts, roleplay, and asked for a specific day/time. Consider closing with a brief negotiated plan specifying who will do what and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Routinely invited patient preference and input (voicemail vs. call-back, roleplay on request) and respected limits. Could make next steps more co-created (e.g., write wording together).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5/5
**Comments:** Explicitly preserved patient control with multiple options, framed tasks as small steps, and avoided pressure. Reinforce ownership of chosen actions when closing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and reasons for action; patient expressed desire and tentative commitment. Missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivation (explore values, use scaling).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and power-sharing, but occasionally more prescriptive (offered full scripts) without explicitly inviting personalization. Ask patient to rephrase scripts to enhance ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Reinforced change talk and reduced barriers with concrete options; produced preliminary commitment (Friday morning). Could strengthen by eliciting scaled importance/likelihood and specific implementation steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5
**Comments:** Validated barriers and reframed sustain talk into options (voicemail, sliding scale). Would benefit from more double-sided reflections and brief exploration of specific sustain fears to resolve ambivalence.
