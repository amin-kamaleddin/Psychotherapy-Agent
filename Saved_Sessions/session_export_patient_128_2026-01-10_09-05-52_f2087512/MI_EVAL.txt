### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries of concerns, though affect and underlying fears (e.g., belonging/reputation) could have been probed more deeply.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing alcohol-related risk with concrete goals, scaling questions, and actionable planning throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving—clinician invited choices, co-drafted texts, and incorporated Tyler’s input and language into plans.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and shared decisions, though offering many concrete options sometimes shifted the session slightly toward clinician-led problem solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change language with questions and scales but did not consistently amplify, reflect, or sequence that talk to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
