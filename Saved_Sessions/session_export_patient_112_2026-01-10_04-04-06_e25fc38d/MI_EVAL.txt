### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms the difficulty of the situation, supporting rapport and safe disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening with accurate reflections that capture ambivalence and emotions, though reflections are sometimes repetitive and a few opportunities to deepen emotional content were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking cessation and moves progressively to concrete, measurable actions, securing a specific, time-limited plan for the week.

### Collaboration

#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicits value-focused motivations and concrete supports that produced change talk, but could probe deeper into personal meaning, future imagery, and confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Cultivating Change Talk
