# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes difficulty, avoiding blame or shame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and summaries that name feelings and concerns, though empathy is sometimes formulaic and could deepen emotional exploration with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on restarting exercise with progressively concrete implementation steps and resists topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages the client in joint problem‑solving and integrates her ideas, but occasional phrasing leans mildly authoritative rather than purely invitational.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and control by offering options, honoring the client's selections, and avoiding prescriptive directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits specific motives and solutions (cues, backups, plan) but could further probe values, confidence, and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a genuinely collaborative stance, deferring to the client's expertise and co‑creating a practical plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and amplifies concrete change talk and steps, yet misses opportunities to explicitly elicit stronger commitment or use scaling/commitment questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and pivots to solutions effectively, though could strengthen use of double‑sided reflections or strategic reframing.
