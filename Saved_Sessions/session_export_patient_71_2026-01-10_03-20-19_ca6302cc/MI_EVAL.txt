# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys nonjudgmental acceptance and normalizes setbacks, using validating language and honoring the client's pace to reduce shame and support engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures content and affect, but reflections are often similar in form and could deepen emotional nuance or explore ambivalence more complexly to reach a top score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on values‑linked, time‑bound behavior (15‑minute morning movement, two‑week trial) and moves the session toward concrete planning and commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a genuine partnership: clinician invites preferences, builds on the client's ideas, and co‑constructs a feasible plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choice and frames suggestions as options, avoids pressuring language, and reinforces client ownership of the plan and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out client motivations and resources through evocative questions and links those client‑generated reasons to values like self‑love and growth.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters client expertise and shared decision‑making, though occasional belief statements and labeling of change talk introduce a mildly expert/directive voice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with focused questions, reflections, and summaries that build momentum toward specific, doable commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframes worry and avoids amplifying sustain talk, but could more often use double‑sided reflections or explicit summaries of ambivalence to resolve sustain talk strategically.
