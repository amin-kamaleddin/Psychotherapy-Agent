### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Samir's frustration, rolling with his guarded style and humor without criticism. Invited collaboration and acknowledged constraints rather than blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, synthesizing feelings and practical barriers (tiredness, brain fog, cost) throughout. Validated realism about not being perfect, showing nuanced understanding of Samir's stance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on medication adherence and practical problem-solving, progressing logically from barriers to concrete implementation planning. Kept the conversation goal-directed while staying patient-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited Samir's input at each decision point and offered help only if desired, negotiating solutions rather than imposing them. Language and choices reinforced shared problem-solving and equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Samir's choice and avoided pressure, asking permission to explore and offering options rather than directives. Honored his right to decline and deferred to his preferences on next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out some personal motivation (e.g., labeling the reminder 'For my family') and used the patient's language to support the plan. Could have more systematically deepened intrinsic motivation with importance/confidence scaling or further values exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructed decisions with Samir, repeatedly asking permission and deferring to his routine and preferences. Fostered power-sharing by letting his choices shape the plan (timing, label, backup).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and operationalized change talk leading to a measurable first step (phone reminder with specifics). Could have amplified and explored value-based language more to strengthen motivation and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk, reframing options into low-effort steps and offering troubleshooting. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections or explicit summaries contrasting pros and barriers to further soften sustain talk.
