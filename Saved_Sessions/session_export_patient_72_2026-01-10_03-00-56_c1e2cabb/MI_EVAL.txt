# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard—normalizes challenges and affirms strengths throughout the session. No judgment or minimization; praise and thanks reinforce a warm, supportive stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and summaries and asks about values, demonstrating solid understanding at the pragmatic/cognitive level. Could deepen empathic connection by adding a few more complex/emotional reflections about body confidence or feelings behind goals.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus (restarting regular activity) and links questions/reflections to concrete actions, timing, barriers, and coping plans. The session moves logically from values to specific commitments and implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a true collaboration: the psychiatrist and patient co-construct the plan, role-play, and adjust based on Leila’s preferences. Language and actions consistently reinforce teamwork and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Leila's choice and control by offering options and asking her to choose without pressure. Normalizes trialing options (e.g., trial pass) and emphasizes agency in all steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Leila’s own motivations and reasons with evocative questions and builds on intrinsic motives to strengthen commitment. Elicits coping strategies and uses scaling to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a collaborative partnership by offering options, inviting preferences, and sharing power in decisions. Integrates Leila’s knowledge and choices into the plan rather than taking an expert-only stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reflects, and amplifies change talk, asking for specifics, timing, and commitment. Reinforces change talk through rehearsal, planning, and scaling questions to translate motivation into action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers without amplifying sustain talk and avoids arguing; pivots promptly to problem-solving and rehearsed responses. Uses validation plus concrete coping plans to reduce resistance and maintain momentum.
