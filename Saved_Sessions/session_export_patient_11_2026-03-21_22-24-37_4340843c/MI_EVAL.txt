# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and affirmation, normalizing difficulty and highlighting effort. Warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases. Several reflections felt formulaic and could be varied to deepen emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, persistent focus on incremental physical activity and resists drifting to other topics. Every intervention ties back to concrete, behaviorally specific steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as joint problem-solving with frequent invitations for client input and co-design of the plan. Language and actions demonstrate mutual influence on session direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports client choice by presenting options, deferring to Amira's judgment, and avoiding pressure. Reinforces that the client controls pace and content of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations, benefits, and success markers, prompting rich change talk. Stopped short of systematically consolidating intrinsic motivations into a clear verbal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly positions the client as the expert and consistently invites Amira to choose supports and specifics. The conversation is clearly co-constructed and client-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces desire, reasons, ability, and some commitment language, and prompts specifics that strengthen change talk. Could more explicitly amplify and summarize key change statements to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids reinforcing it by focusing on doable steps and honoring boundaries. Would benefit from more targeted techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to further diffuse sustain talk.
