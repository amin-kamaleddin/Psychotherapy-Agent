# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently maintained unconditional positive regard and repeatedly validated Isabella's perspective, normalizing ambivalence without shaming or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and summaries that captured ambivalence and emotional weight (e.g., complex reflection and 'that's a lot to hold'), helping the patient feel heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use in pregnancy and guided the conversation toward concrete steps (importance/confidence scaling, 2-week trial, printable plan).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly solicited preferences, offered choices, and co-created a tailored plan and materials with the patient and partner, demonstrating joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly honored autonomy through permission, options, tentative trials, and affirmations of ownership over the decision-making process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and some self-motivational statements via pros/cons and scaling, but could have probed deeper values and imagined outcomes to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as an equal collaborator by checking preferences, co-constructing the plan, and tailoring materials and rehearsal to the patient's needs.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk and concrete options, yet missed chances to consolidate stronger commitment language and have the patient articulate her plan in her own words.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided colluding with sustain talk and offered choices and permission-based information, but could have used more double-sided reflections and discrepancy-focused techniques to further reduce sustain talk.
