# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys respect and nonjudgmental stance with validations and normalizing remarks, but some corrective information felt procedural; more individualized empathic reflections would strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows perspective-taking and links concerns to Alexei’s goals, yet reflections are mainly cognitive/logistical rather than deeper emotional reflections (e.g., fear about parents or peer tension).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and concrete goals (soccer, scholarships) throughout, using role-play and coaching to keep the session on target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a team-like rehearsal by cueing scenarios and soliciting feedback, but at times leads with ready-made lines and directive coaching instead of eliciting the client's own phrasing sooner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites consent and offers multiple options (role-play, scenes, comebacks), supporting client choice and ownership of strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation by connecting alcohol to valued goals and using role-play to generate commitment, but could use more open-ended questions to draw out the client's own reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance by asking permission and soliciting preferences, yet provides many clinician-scripted lines rather than co-creating language with the client more often.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively ties alcohol to Alexei’s goals and elicits change talk through rehearsal, but could further amplify the client's own change language and explicitly probe for desire/confidence/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Does not escalate minimization but also misses opportunities to soften sustain talk; normalization risked validating minimization instead of using double-sided reflections to shift emphasis.
