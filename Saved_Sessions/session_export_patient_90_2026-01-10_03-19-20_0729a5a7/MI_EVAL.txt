### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Direction

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified vivid change talk about fewer ER visits and better sleep, but missed some opportunities to deepen commitment and confidence with scaling or barrier planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
