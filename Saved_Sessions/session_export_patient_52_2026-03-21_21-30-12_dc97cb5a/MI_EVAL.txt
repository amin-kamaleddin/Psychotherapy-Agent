# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Irina’s burden, normalizing ambivalence and honoring limits. Warm, nonjudgmental language was used throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective summaries of stressors and the coping role of alcohol. Reflections validated feelings and reduced defensiveness while encouraging disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing evening alcohol use with a narrowly defined, actionable 7‑day plan. The clinician repeatedly oriented choices toward concrete next steps to preserve momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑created the plan, offered multiple tailored strategies, and rehearsed messages and replies together. Decision-making and problem‑solving were shared rather than clinician‑driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offered options and respected Irina’s limits, supporting self‑directed experimentation. Even corrective language preserved choice and ownership over change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client-generated motivations and used scaling to surface readiness, but missed opportunities to probe values and future imagery more deeply. Strong evocation that could be strengthened with more evocative follow‑ups.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Irina as the expert by inviting preferences and tailoring options, including drafting and revising texts together. The interaction felt genuinely collaborative and client-centered.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced multiple instances of change talk and used a confidence rating, but could have deepened and amplified change language with more evocative follow‑ups and exploration of past successes. Good elicitation that wasn’t fully exploited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged minimizations and reward language without confrontation and used small‑step framing and normalization of setbacks to defuse sustain talk. Rarely used explicit double‑sided reflections that could further resolve ambivalence.
