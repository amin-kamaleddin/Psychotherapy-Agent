# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens with explicit nonjudgmental language and repeatedly affirms and normalizes Amina's efforts, conveying warmth and respect with no blaming or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and validating reflections and matches affect, but reflections are mostly surface-level and could probe deeper emotions and meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on attending interviews, moving systematically from problem identification to concrete, actionable solutions and follow-up steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint effort and co-creates scripts and practice, though introducing polished scripts sometimes limits space for Amina to generate her own wording first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors Amina's choice and control by asking what she wants, seeking permission to brainstorm, and offering alternative supports without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation and concrete examples (confidence ratings, STAR details) and presses for specifics, but could more often draw out underlying values and why change matters personally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance with permission-seeking and choice, yet occasionally shifts to a more directive role by rapidly offering scripts and options that reduce client-generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change-oriented statements and confidence and offers supports to convert intent into action, though could more frequently reflect and deepen personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates barriers and quickly pivots to pragmatic solutions that minimize sustain talk, but could use more explicit double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence while steering toward change.
