# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental stance, naming both sides of ambivalence and validating proposed steps. No shaming or moralizing; affirmed client's dignity and practical solutions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated ambivalence and guided elaboration. Some reflections were repetitive and could vary to capture deeper emotional nuance (shame, fear, longing).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on gambling risk and concrete protective steps throughout the session. Questions and reflections consistently channeled toward actionable change while resisting tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner—solicited preferences, co-constructed plans, and offered help with permission (e.g., drafting the message). Tone and language framed the session as joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice and ownership by avoiding coercive language and offering options rather than prescriptions. Validated imperfection and framed suggestions as voluntary tools.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out concrete motives, goals, and solutions (Egypt travel, card-handling plan, supports). Could deepen elicitation of intrinsic motives and confidence with scaling or more exploration of personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—invited input, offered joint drafting and rehearsal, and shared decision-making about which step to start with. Demonstrated respect for the client's expertise about her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and reinforced client-generated change talk and concrete steps (handing cards, direct debits, enlisting supports). Missed some opportunities to press for firmer commitment language or a specific timeline.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged the pull of gambling but tended to move quickly into planning rather than exploring sustain talk in detail. More double-sided reflections or explicit exploration of what gambling provides would better resolve ambivalence.
