# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalized the client's priorities, and affirmed choices without judgment. Reinforced competence and validated feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate understanding and reflective listening by paraphrasing preferences and mirroring emotions and values. Reflections were timely and attuned to the client's perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the target behavior and linked choices to the client's values, moving efficiently from goal selection to concrete planning. Used summaries to orient and progress toward actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as a cooperative joint effort with repeated elicitation of the client's input and co-creation of the plan. Clinician deferred to what felt natural for the client and offered options rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering multiple options, checking preferences, and avoiding pressure. Reinforced the client's ownership of the plan and provided supports only with permission.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out motivation tied to the client's values and elicited preparatory and commitment language (confidence rating, outcome query). Could deepen evocation by using more open questions for the client's own reasons and amplifying their phrasing.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative, offering options and asking the client to choose, which shared power in the session. At times the clinician supplied ready-made scripts rather than first eliciting the client's own language, which slightly reduced partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with commitment details and confidence scaling, moving the client forward. Missed some opportunities to deepen and amplify change statements with more open invitations and reflective amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing any reluctance and honored ambivalence about tone while offering neutral pushback lines. Strategically prevented sustain talk from derailing momentum and kept focus on change.
