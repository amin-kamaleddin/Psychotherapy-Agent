# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently used nonjudgmental, respectful language and normalizing statements, validated the patient’s experience, and repeatedly offered choice rather than commands.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking captured core conflicts and feelings, but would benefit from more frequent complex reflections to probe deeper emotions like loneliness or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session remained clearly focused on smoking and parenting concerns, with targeted exploration of triggers and a concrete, measurable harm-reduction experiment plus arranged follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician invited preferences, co-designed the behavioral test, used inclusive language, and honored the patient’s requests about follow-up content and format.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported autonomy by offering menu-style options, avoiding prescriptive language, respecting limits about quitting, and framing the experiment as voluntary and adjustable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited internal motivations and values (concern for her son, desire to keep coping) but missed opportunities to deepen motivation with scaling, amplification, summaries, or recalling past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician acted as a genuine partner—deferring to the patient’s expertise, co-creating the plan, and sharing power in decision-making about strategies and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited and reinforced change talk and set a small experiment, but could have amplified and strengthened desire/commitment using targeted reflections or scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided arguing or reinforcing sustain talk and offered harm-reduction strategies, though more use of double-sided reflections or reframing could better soften sustain talk and highlight discrepancy.
