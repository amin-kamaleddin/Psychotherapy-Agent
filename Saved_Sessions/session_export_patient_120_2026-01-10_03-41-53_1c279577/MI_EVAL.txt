# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming throughout the session, normalizing Sofia's experience and reinforcing her positive steps without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid empathic listening with validations and brief reflections, but reflections are often surface-level and miss deeper emotional nuance or complex ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use during pregnancy and advances practical, behavior-linked strategies (scripts, exit lines, mocktails) toward a plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is largely collaborative—offers options, solicits preferences, and invites practice—but at times the clinician shifts to directive medical advice that reduces co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice by offering multiple options and asking permission, though occasionally frames medically appropriate recommendations in a more instructive than exploratory way.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some preferences and practice but relies on providing solutions rather than using open, evocative questions to draw out Sofia's underlying motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership through permission-seeking and co-shaping of scripts, yet sometimes adopts an expert stance when listing directives, reducing shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Affirms stopping and offers practical steps, but seldom uses open, evocative questions or reflective amplification to systematically elicit and strengthen Sofia's own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain-related worries and offers concrete tools, but misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or strategic summaries and can become prescriptive.
