# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through nonjudgmental summaries and affirmations, normalizing ambivalence and respecting boundaries.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases and attention to values, though reflections were sometimes repetitive and did not probe deeper emotions or meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on medication adherence and proximal behavioral goals, repeatedly steering the discussion toward concrete, actionable steps tied to the patient's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-constructed the plan by inviting the patient's ideas, offering choices, and checking preferences, positioning the clinician as a partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized patient choice by presenting options, following the patient's lead, and respecting his decision not to involve his wife.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's motivations and linked them to values, but did not sufficiently deepen or consolidate those motivations with scaling or commitment questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making throughout the session by asking which option felt realistic and co-creating a concrete, patient-led plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk and practical supports, yet missed opportunities to probe strength/confidence and elicit more specific commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk gently without confrontation and redirected toward values and steps, but could have used double-sided reflections or more explicit normalization to further soften ambivalence.
