# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently demonstrated respect, warmth, and non‑judgmental acceptance (e.g., opening “I’m glad you came,” numerous affirmations and validations). Multiple explicit affirmations and normalizing of ambivalence (≥8 instances) preserved dignity and supported rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective‑taking with accurate paraphrases and emotion naming (guilt, panic). Several missed opportunities to probe deeper affect (rarely explored meaning of “guilt” or friendship loss beyond restating).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session kept clearly focused on boundary‑setting and action planning (script, timeline, homework). Repeated directional prompts and concrete steps (give notice tonight; contacts by Wed; pack/viewings weekend; move Fri).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician largely partnered with client—asked permission to role‑play and offered options. Slight tilt toward clinician‑provided scripts/timing rather than eliciting more client‑generated wording first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent honoring of client choice (permission for role‑play, asking which supports would boost confidence). No coercive language; advice/offers given with consent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation of client reasons, confidence, and implementation intentions (0–10 ruler, supports needed). At times clinician moved quickly to offer polished scripts, reducing space for spontaneous client language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong teamwork tone—“I’ll listen and help you build an assertive plan,” co‑planning and rehearsal. Occasional clinician‑expert role when supplying ready‑made scripts slightly reduced full power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (asked for specific doable language, confidence steps, rehearsals). Multiple targeted elicitation and amplification moves (≥7) strengthened commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence and used double‑sided reflections to redirect toward planning. Some missed opportunities for more strategic softening (deeper linking of sustain talk to values and explicit discrepancy work).
