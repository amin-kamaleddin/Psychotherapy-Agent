# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone and acceptance of the patient's choice to cut down rather than quit; could increase individualized affirmations and normalize ambivalence more explicitly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows some perspective‑taking and tailors plans to the patient's schedule, but reflections of feeling and deeper paraphrase of ambivalence are limited and inconsistent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, pragmatic focus on smoking reduction with specific, actionable plans and repeated redirection to concrete behavior change and safety considerations.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adapts the plan to the patient's workday and offers joint problem‑solving moves, but the clinician mainly leads with structured instructions rather than fully co‑creating options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides choices and normalizes cutting down while emphasizing patient control, though the clinician sometimes gives directive medical advice without fully eliciting the patient's acceptability of options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Provides reasons and strategies but does little to draw out the patient's own motivations, values, or past successes; minimal use of questions to elicit change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some partnership elements (asking preferences, tailoring to breaks) but interaction is clinician‑driven with fewer open questions to let the patient shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Creates space for change with options and a plan but rarely elicits or amplifies the patient's own desire, ability, reasons, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation, offers harm‑reduction and normalizes slips which reduces sustain talk, but uses few strategic MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to further soften resistance.
