# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, acknowledging constraints and validating tension between work and health. Could add explicit normalizing language about ambivalence to further enhance safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, sustained perspective-taking using the patient's words and reflections that capture both content and feeling. Reflections foster rapport and keep the exchange collaborative.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused target on feasible, time-friendly actions and moves from exploration to a concrete, time-bound plan. Repeatedly brings the discussion back to actionable steps and follow-through.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Plan is clearly co-constructed with options offered and the patient choosing specifics (slot, alarms, reschedule rule). The clinician solicits the patient's assessment and uses it to shape implementation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently foregrounds choice by asking what 'feels doable' and accepting limited commitment language. Avoids pressure and reinforces the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies the patient's own motives and reasons for change through open questions and prompts. Focus stays on drawing motivation from within rather than supplying external reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on their life, inviting input and deferring to his judgment when selecting steps. Language and process demonstrate genuine shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits, reflects, and reinforces concrete change language, culminating in a specific commitment with calendar details and barriers addressed. Selective reflections and requests for specifics deepen and sustain change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses double-sided reflections and logistical problem-solving to reduce polarization. Could further weaken sustain talk by exploring costs of the status quo and selectively amplifying discrepant values beyond practical fixes.
