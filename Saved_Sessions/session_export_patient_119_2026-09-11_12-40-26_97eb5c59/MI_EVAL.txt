#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Selene's values and normalizing limits. Reframes outcomes as useful data rather than failure, reducing shame and supporting engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of ambivalence that help Selene expand on specifics. Could deepen emotional empathy by reflecting underlying feelings (anxiety, guilt, exhaustion) and the personal meaning of reliability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on medication adherence and small, concrete experiments, sequencing questions to move from brainstorming to action. No drift—prompts are purposefully linked to Selene's values and target behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative: the clinician invites, responds to, and integrates Selene's suggestions so her ideas shape the plan. Language and choices signal equal partnership and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, frames the plan as an experiment, and emphasizes choice and ownership without coercive language. Reinforces that Selene can adjust or decline, supporting her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Selene's practical motives and specific plans (e.g., alarm, pillbox, 3‑day test) to elicit motivation. Could more explicitly probe deeper intrinsic motives, prior successes, and push toward firmer commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Selene as the expert and co‑constructs the plan through frequent choice questions and deference to her judgment. Session decisions flow from her preferences, demonstrating authentic power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with open invitations and concrete planning that elicit desire and steps for change. Missed opportunities to more often reflect and strengthen change statements or use scaling to convert desire into commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizing slips and gently redirecting to workable experiments. Could employ more double‑sided reflections or selective reflections to further decrease sustain talk's intensity and invite reasons it may be less true.
