# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, using validating language and normalizing external stressors throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, empathic reflections that helped the patient open up, though could have probed more deeply into the emotional experience underlying 'escape' and the guarded-to-opened trajectory.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on heroin use within a harm-reduction framework and consistently steered the session toward concrete, realistic first steps and safety planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance—invited agenda-setting, used shared problem-solving, role-played with the patient, and adapted the session to the patient's preferences and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported patient choice from the outset, offered options and asked permission before sharing information, and avoided pressuring language about treatment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own motivations and plans through open questions and prompts, amplifying internal motivation and concrete commitments like telling the housemate and securing Narcan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership by treating the patient as the expert, inviting input, co-constructing scripts and contingencies, and sharing power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions and affirmations, but missed some opportunities to deepen commitment by eliciting specific timing, confidence ratings, or stronger implementation intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to safety, but used few targeted strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or strategic summaries) to actively resolve ambivalence before moving to planning.
