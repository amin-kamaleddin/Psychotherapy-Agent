# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, using warm, validating language and affirmations that normalize concerns and highlight strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and accurate understanding of the client's perspective, but could deepen emotional labeling and capture underlying affect and ambivalence with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on preventing alcohol use and links every question and reflection to concrete, goal-directed action steps tied to valued outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance—co-creates the plan, checks preferences, uses role play and options, and follows the client's lead in choosing strategies and wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking which wording feels natural, and framing the plan as the client's decision without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and past successes, using those self-derived reasons and strategies to build a concrete, internally motivated plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates in a genuine partnership—solicits the client's preferences, defers to their judgment, and jointly constructs the strategy and script.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits, amplifies, and builds on change talk by linking behavior to scholarships and soccer and reinforcing concrete, commitment-oriented steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects minimizing/sustain talk toward change through reframing and pivoting to goals, though could strengthen use of double-sided reflections or empathic summaries.
