# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized difficulties, and avoided judgment or pressure using warm, respectful language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening through paraphrases and summaries that kept the patient heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on restarting sustainable physical activity and progressed logically from options to a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner by inviting preferences, co-constructing the plan, and offering assistance only in ways the patient preferred.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice and control by offering options, emphasizing 'no pressure,' and deferring to her judgment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and concrete planning (strength, energy, supports) but could further deepen evocation by asking about personal meaning and linking values to specific commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and client-led, repeatedly inviting choices and leaving decisions and priorities to the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and supported change talk with open, action-focused questions and reflections, though could more often amplify and elaborate the patient's own change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers without reinforcing them and skillfully redirected toward alternatives and contingency plans, avoiding confrontation or repeated probing of sustain talk.
