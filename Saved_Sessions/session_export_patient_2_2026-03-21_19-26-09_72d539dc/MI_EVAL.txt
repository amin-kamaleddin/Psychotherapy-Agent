# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, honoring boundaries (e.g., not bringing up drinking) and validating practical goals while offering supports rather than moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Showed basic understanding of smoking patterns and motivators and provided useful info, but used few reflective statements naming feelings or exploring ambivalence and moved quickly into advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on evening smoking reduction with concrete stepwise plans and relevant resources while resisting digressions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solicited preferences and tailored recommendations to context and cost concerns, but tended to deliver expert-led plans rather than fully co-creating the strategy with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing decisions as hers, reinforcing her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's agenda and responded to volunteered change talk, but relied more on information and recommendations than on targeted evocative questions to draw out motivations and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operated in a collaborative stance—asking what the patient wanted and offering choices—yet often led the encounter with detailed plans instead of building them together step-by-step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced patient-initiated change talk and provided concrete plans, but missed systematic opportunities to evoke and amplify importance, reasons, and confidence toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoided reinforcing sustain talk by respecting boundaries and not pressuring cessation, but did not use double-sided reflections or reflective summaries to actively soften and redirect sustain talk.
