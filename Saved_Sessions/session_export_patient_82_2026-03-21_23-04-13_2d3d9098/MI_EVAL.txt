# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician was consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating Lukas's experience and strengths throughout the session. No shaming or labeling occurred, supporting a top score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflections captured both content and feeling and often mirrored Lukas's language and nuance. The clinician demonstrated strong perspective-taking and repeated empathic statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on peer-influenced drinking and protecting season/GPA and progressed logically from exploration to a concrete first step. The clinician guided narrowing to a feasible plan without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently co-created the plan with Lukas, eliciting his ideas and preferences and integrating them into concrete strategies. Decision-making was shared and patient suggestions shaped the outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were honored and the clinician offered options rather than directives, repeatedly checking which approach felt comfortable to Lukas. Limitations were accepted nonjudgmentally, reinforcing Lukas's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist effectively drew out Lukas's own motivations, past successes, and concrete ideas rather than imposing reasons to change. Questions and prompts strengthened Lukas's internal motivation and practical strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was framed as collaborative, privileging Lukas's expertise and inviting him to help design the plan. The clinician avoided taking over and used open questions to invite joint problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicited and amplified change language with targeted open questions and reinforced Lukas's emerging commitments. The session moved toward a specific, doable first step generated by Lukas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and quickly pivoted to options and solutions, reducing its impact. However, there were missed opportunities for more deliberate double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to further diminish sustain statements.
