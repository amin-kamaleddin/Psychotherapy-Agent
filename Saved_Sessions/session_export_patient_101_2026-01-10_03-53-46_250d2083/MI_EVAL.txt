# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently showed respect, normalization, and positive regard—validating Mei’s worries and affirming her values and choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate emotion labeling built rapport and understanding; more frequent complex or double-sided reflections could deepen empathic resonance and capture ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing risky drinking and improving contraceptive reliability, using targeted questions to generate concrete behavior-linked goals and implementation intentions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician acted as an equal partner—offering options, co-creating detailed plans, role-playing at Mei’s request, and repeatedly checking her preferences to support shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were frequently affirmed and Mei was invited to decide what felt right, though occasional nudges toward immediate action (e.g., prompting to set alarms now) could be tempered with brief permission checks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively drew out Mei’s own reasons for change (family history, avoiding pregnancy), elicited intrinsic motivators, and built on her language to generate commitment and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently privileged Mei’s expertise about her life, asked which options she preferred, and invited her to lead planning and rehearsal, demonstrating genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Change talk was actively elicited, reinforced, and amplified—clinician translated Mei’s statements into specific commitments (Lina check-in, pill alarm, condom) and probed confidence and next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward planning and safeguards, though using more double-sided reflections could better acknowledge ambivalence and highlight discrepancy with values.
