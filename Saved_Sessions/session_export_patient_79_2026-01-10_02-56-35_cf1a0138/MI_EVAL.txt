# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding blame while acknowledging the patient's priorities and efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking validated the patient's context, though deeper affective reflections (e.g., on frustration or sarcasm) were sometimes missing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician kept a clear, consistent focus on asthma self-management and adherence, moving from values elicitation to concrete, time-bound steps without getting sidetracked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was clearly collaborative with shared decision-making; clinician suggestions were offered as options and the patient’s preferences shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly affirmed choice and control by offering options, asking permission before interventions, and inviting the patient to decide follow-up methods.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from the patient's own values and reasons through open questions and scaling, and the clinician built on that intrinsic motivation to elicit commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The encounter was positioned as a joint project with co-created plans and clinician input framed as help rather than prescription.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicited, reinforced, and specified change talk, converting vague desires into concrete, actionable steps and checking confidence and timing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and skillfully redirected sustain talk toward practical options, though more double-sided or ambivalence-focused reflections could further soften resistance.
