# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with specific affirmations and validations, avoiding judgment and reinforcing the patient’s competence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring the patient’s wording and summarizing feelings to build rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on staying safe in social situations and consistently guided the session toward concrete, time-bound implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited patient input at each step and integrated patient-generated options, co-constructing the plan rather than dictating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered choices and avoided directives, consistently respecting the patient’s control and reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient’s motivations and barriers, focusing on internal drivers like privacy and enjoyment and prompting patient-identified enablers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance with repeated invitations to choose, ensuring the patient’s decisions shaped the content and sequencing of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used evocative, future-oriented questions that elicited concrete change talk and commitment (e.g., choosing strategies, drafting messages, backups).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and avoided reinforcing it by pivoting to planning, but missed opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections to more gently resolve ambivalence.
