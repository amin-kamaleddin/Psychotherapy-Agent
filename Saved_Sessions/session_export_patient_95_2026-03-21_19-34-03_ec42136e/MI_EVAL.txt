# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and explicit validation throughout the encounter, thanking and affirming Maria’s experience and avoiding judgmental language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections, honoring Maria’s request to be heard and repeatedly acknowledging the depth of her pain and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on imminent suicidal risk and immediate safety—assessing means, intent, history, and negotiating concrete safety behaviors throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint effort by asking permission, offering options, co-creating the safety steps, and checking Maria’s preferences frequently.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and agency by offering options, requesting permission for questions, and respecting Maria’s decisions while framing recommendations as voluntary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited internal motivation—acknowledged the 'tiny part' wanting help but missed multiple opportunities to draw out values, reasons for living, and deeper sources of hope.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly worked as an equal partner—stayed with Maria during safety behaviors, invited her input on messages and plans, and repeatedly checked in about her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change-oriented steps (e.g., securing pills) and small commitments, but could have more actively amplified and explored those statements and the underlying motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Appropriately avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward safety, though more strategic double-sided reflections and reframing could have better softened sustain statements.
