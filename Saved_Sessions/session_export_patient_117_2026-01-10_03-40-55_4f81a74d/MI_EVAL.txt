# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician communicated consistent, nonjudgmental support and validated Samuel's efforts (praising the journal and progress), demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician showed good understanding and validated urges and social concerns, but missed opportunities for deeper reflective listening and extended paraphrases that would have named Samuel's mixed pride and worry.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on smoking reduction with concrete, actionable elements (7-step workplace plan, scripts, role-play), maintaining momentum toward a specific behavior-change plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction felt clearly collaborative—strategies were chosen with Samuel, rehearsal was invited, and recommendations were tailored to his workplace concerns and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician offered multiple options, respected Samuel's preference against the patch, and repeatedly invited him to choose what felt doable, reinforcing his sense of control and choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and linked Samuel's progress to goals and choices, but relied more on offering strategies than systematically drawing out Samuel's intrinsic motives, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned Samuel as an equal partner by co-creating the step-by-step plan, offering choices, and asking for his input on wording and tactics throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively reinforced and amplified change talk (praising progress, asking which strategies to try), yet could have done more to elicit stronger commitment and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered neutralizing strategies and alternatives, though greater use of double-sided reflections or explicit normalization could further soften sustain talk.
