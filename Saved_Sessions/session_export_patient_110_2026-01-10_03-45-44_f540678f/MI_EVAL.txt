# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys respect and positive regard, normalizing ambivalence and validating the patient's values without judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and feeling and validating the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, sustained focus on cholesterol management and moves the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps with problem-solving and follow-through planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-creates the plan with the patient, repeatedly soliciting preferences, offering role-play, and treating the patient as an equal partner in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and control, offers options and permission, avoids pressure, and reinforces the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from the patient's own values and reasons, linking specific actions to those values and eliciting confidence and rehearsal to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative by offering choices, asking which options to try first, and inviting joint problem-solving and role-play.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, ties goals to values, amplifies intentions, and strengthens commitment through scaling and concrete planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by avoiding reinforcement and pivoting to values and action, but could more intentionally use double-sided reflections and explicitly name competing views.
