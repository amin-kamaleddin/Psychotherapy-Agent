# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys consistent unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing feelings and accepting refusals without judgment. Allows imperfection and avoids punitive language throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and normalization that help Rowan feel heard. Reflections are sometimes functional and brief; deeper or amplified reflections of emotion would strengthen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on charging and device-related safety, repeatedly tying interventions to the target behavior. Avoids drifting into unrelated topics and checks for acute symptoms when relevant.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative with co-created steps and frequent requests for preference. Occasional directive phrasing slightly reduces the equal-partner feel.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering multiple options, normalizing choice, and inviting small experiments without pressure. Reinforces the client's freedom to choose and accepts non‑perfection.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk and small commitments by asking targeted questions and offering options. Could deepen motivation by exploring personal reasons, values, and using scaling questions for importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership and shared decision-making, letting Rowan shape the plan and choose options. Some moments of more prescriptive language slightly reduce co-equal collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change language and reinforces commitments, resulting in actionable steps (e.g., plug in now, set reminder). Would benefit from more amplification and systematic probing of desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids fueling sustain talk by normalizing ambivalence and offering manageable options rather than confrontations. Could more often use double-sided or amplified reflections to explicitly acknowledge sustain talk while pivoting toward change.
