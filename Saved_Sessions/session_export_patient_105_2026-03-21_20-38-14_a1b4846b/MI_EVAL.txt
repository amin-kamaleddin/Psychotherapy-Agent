### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth—validating Naomi, thanking her honesty, and avoiding blaming language. Normalizing past struggles strengthened an accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good, accurate reflective listening and summaries showed understanding of symptoms and skepticism. Could deepen empathic reflections by explicitly naming emotional nuances like shame or self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on managing social/performance anxiety and moved logically to a concrete, time-bound plan for Friday. The session stayed action-oriented and goal-focused throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was a clear joint effort—clinician solicited Naomi’s ideas, operationalized them, and co-constructed solutions. Language and problem-solving consistently supported shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Naomi’s choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and inviting modifications. Confidence scaling and explicit invitations to accept or change the plan reinforced agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation and specific change ideas (small signs of progress, next steps) and used confidence scaling. Could further strengthen by probing values and selectively amplifying change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership through collaborative language, checking preferences, and co-creating the plan (sticker, rehearsals, alarms). Power-sharing was evident in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and supported change talk with concrete commitments and planning (rehearsals, cues, success indicators). Missed chances to amplify Naomi’s desire/hope and explore deeper reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without reinforcing it, using pragmatic problem-solving to reduce resistance. Could more often use double-sided reflections or reframing to actively pivot ambivalence toward change.
