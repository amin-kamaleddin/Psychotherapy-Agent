# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental tone, using validating reflections and honoring the patient's request for no lecture.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking with accurate, specific reflections that name the patient's emotions and align closely with her concerns and priorities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on the screening flag and reassurance, repeatedly steering to concrete next steps (explain practice, check policy, arrange follow-up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: the clinician co-constructs the plan, solicits Sofia's preferences at each decision point, and shares problem-solving rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering explicit options and letting the patient choose how to proceed, avoiding pressure or directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's practical motivations and needs (privacy, reassurance, follow-up) but stops short of deeper exploration of values, importance, or confidence and lacks scaling to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys shared decision-making and power-sharing by asking which option to do first and inviting the patient to lead choices about wording and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out practical change language and small commitments (choices, preview, check-in) but does not explicitly amplify or deepen commitment with scaling or a commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids eliciting or reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to solutions, though it misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or preemptive framing to soften subtle reluctance.
