# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful with repeated validation and normalization of ambivalence; safety framing and encouraging language reinforced unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and matched the client's language to capture ambivalence and embarrassment, though the session could have probed deeper affect (shame/stress) with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing risky drinking with practical, behavior-linked strategies and an actionable one-line plan tied to the client's concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative—steps, wording, and choices were co-created and the clinician repeatedly sought the client's input and approval.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed options rather than mandates, asked permission before offering help, and emphasized the client's ownership of the plan and ability to try small steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete change language and commitments (alternating drinks, alarms, buddy check) but could have asked more to draw out intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong power-sharing by offering menus of options, asking preferences, and co-creating messages and plans with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited and reinforced change talk with focused choice questions and summaries that led to specific commitments, though more open questions and reflections could have amplified reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without confrontation but used few strategic techniques (double-sided reflections or reframes), moving quickly to pragmatic solutions so sustain talk remained only partially redirected.
