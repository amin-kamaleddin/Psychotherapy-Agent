# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and avoids shaming; could deepen individualized affirmations beyond occasional formulaic praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows strong empathic listening with frequent, accurate reflections that mirror specifics and validate barriers; could occasionally use more complex, affect‑laden reflections to deepen emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps clear focus on smoking reduction with repeated goal‑directed questions and a concrete, measurable plan; balances exploration and planning effectively.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates equal‑partner problem solving by inviting Hana’s input and co‑creating the plan, checking preferences at every turn; session language and decisions reflect strong joint work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by asking permission, offering options, and honoring Hana’s readiness level without pressuring; reinforces Hana’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations using scaling and reasons for change, and lets Hana drive the plan; could use more evocative, open questions linking change to values and long‑term identity to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters partnership through inclusive language, shared decision‑making, and asking permission before offering options; co‑creates steps and follow‑up in a collaborative way.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits change talk with scaling, evocative prompts, and practical options, producing actionable commitments; sometimes moves quickly to solutions without pausing to amplify Hana’s own reasons and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects to small steps and strategies; tends to neutralize rather than use double‑sided reflections or explicit cost‑of‑continuing prompts to more actively soften sustain talk.
