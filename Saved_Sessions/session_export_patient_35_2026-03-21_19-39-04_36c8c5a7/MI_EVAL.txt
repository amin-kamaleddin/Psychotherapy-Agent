# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's struggle, avoids blame or judgement throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective statements, but reflections are mostly surface-level and could probe deeper emotional ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking-related goals with structured, actionable steps and follow-up planning that keep the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works jointly with the patient, eliciting preferences and shaping the plan, though the clinician often supplies solutions rather than prompting the patient to generate them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, and normalizing doubt, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some commitment to try strategies but largely focuses on pragmatic solutions and does not sufficiently draw out the patient's intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making and respects patient preferences, but the balance sometimes shifts toward clinician-directed problem solving with prescriptive lists.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Encourages limited change talk through options and small experiments, but misses opportunities to deepen or amplify the patient's own reasons and motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes minimizations into action, though could use more strategic double-sided reflections to highlight ambivalence.
