# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validated Marco’s experience, and framed slips nonjudgmentally throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used reflective listening and perspective-taking that matched Marco’s guarded stance, but reflections remained somewhat surface-level and did not fully explore deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking and harm-reduction, moving from exploration to concrete, measurable goals and a specific follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Solicited permission and preferences, used inclusive language, and co-created a specific plan with a mutually agreed check-in.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and ownership by offering options, avoiding pressure, and asking Marco to select steps and the follow-up time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited what smoking provides and what Marco disliked, producing some change talk, but then shifted to offering strategies rather than deepening internal motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered clear partnership and power-sharing by repeatedly asking for Marco’s input on agenda and strategies and positioning him as the expert in his choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked permission and reinforced small change statements, but missed opportunities to amplify past successes, use scaling or explore importance/confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively rolled with resistance and avoided argument, normalizing sustain talk and steering toward harm reduction, though could use more double-sided or strategic reflections to highlight ambivalence.
