# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent nonjudgmental, respectful regard and affirms Matteo's ideas, normalizing change as an experiment; no minimizing or labeling was observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and summaries of Matteo's plans and concerns, showing perspective-taking, but empathy stayed largely at the logistical level without exploring deeper affect or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking with every question and reflection tied to concrete, measurable steps and supports toward a single testable experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly joint—options are offered and Matteo selects what to try—co-constructing implementation without an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and frames strategies as optional experiments, leaving Matteo to pick the single step and supports that fit his life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits concrete motivations tied to Matteo's priorities and asks him to articulate signs of success, but misses deeper elicitation of intrinsic values, confidence, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative tone and invites Matteo's input repeatedly, though the clinician's structured prompts and agenda sometimes guide the interaction more than Matteo's spontaneous direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk by framing change as an experiment, prompting choice of a single step, and reinforcing Matteo's concrete intentions and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and acknowledges sustain-talk concerns while offering compromises and actionable options, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections or affirm the sustain talk before pivoting.
