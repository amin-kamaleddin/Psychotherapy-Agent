# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides consistent unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental, affirming language and normalizes mixed feelings, supporting the client's values without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate reflections of both affect and content that clarify the client's mixed emotions and practical concerns, demonstrating strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on the target behavior and moves the conversation step-by-step from goal to concrete implementation actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner by co-developing wording and steps with the client and letting the client's contributions meaningfully shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices, asks which option feels most doable, and refrains from pressuring, thereby reinforcing the client's sense of control and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's internal motivations and self-generated reasons for change by asking about authentic wording and the single step they will take now.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as collaborative, deferring to the client's expertise about what is doable and inviting their decisions at each turn.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicits, reinforces, and amplifies change talk, guiding the client to articulate desire, reasons, and concrete commitments that lead to immediate action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is minimal and not amplified, but the clinician missed a few opportunities to deepen softening strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or explicit elicitation of responses to worries).
