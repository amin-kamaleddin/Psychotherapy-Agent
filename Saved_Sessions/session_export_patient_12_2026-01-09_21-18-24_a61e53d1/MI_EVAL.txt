# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, affirming the patient's actions and normalizing doubt throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses reflective listening, validation, and safety checks effectively, but reflections are often brief and there are missed opportunities to probe deeper affect and history.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on immediate coping and concrete small steps, but does not frequently connect these stabilizing moves to longer‑term substance use change goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites patient input and stays with them through exercises, yet tends to present directive step sequences rather than fully co‑constructing plans with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing tasks as experiments without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some brief change language and tiny‑step choices, but does not reliably probe reasons, values, past successes, or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance with choices and permission‑seeking, though clinician‑led instructions at times reduce the sense of equal power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and affirms small change statements and offers concrete options, but could more often reflect, amplify, and follow up to deepen desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and skillfully redirects sustain talk toward manageable actions using normalization and reframes, but would benefit from more double‑sided and strategic reflections to further soften sustain talk.
