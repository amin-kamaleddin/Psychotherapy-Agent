# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing harm‑reduction and avoiding shaming while offering practical alternatives to counseling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows moments of understanding and adapts recommendations to the patient's preferences, but offers limited reflective listening of emotions like shame or privacy concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on safer snorting and harm reduction with actionable, relevant guidance throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the session as cooperative by asking the patient's preference and tailoring information, but the interaction becomes more clinician‑driven and could invite more patient ideas for implementation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient's choices and offers multiple discreet options, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions and respecting declines for counseling or testing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies heavily on information provision and safety instructions with few open, evocative questions to draw out the patient's internal motivations or values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites choice and shares power by offering options and resources, but could better co‑create steps and solicit the patient's specific plans before giving techniques.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates opportunities by asking focus and following the patient's lead, but rarely elicits the patient's own reasons, confidence, or commitment to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk and does not reinforce minimizations, yet uses few double‑sided reflections or reframing to gently resolve ambivalence before moving to instructions.
