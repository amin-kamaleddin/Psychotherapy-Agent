# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Maria's unbearable distress and honoring her request to be heard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, affect-focused reflections and summaries that match Maria's language and feelings, leaning into emotional experience rather than moving to premature problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on immediate safety and coping actions throughout the exchange, asking timely, specific questions about plan and means.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in co-construction of the plan, invites Maria's preferences, offers to co-write the message and stay with her, and shares expertise as an offer.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and framing actions as Maria's decisions without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits strands of internal motivation and doable steps but could deepen elicitation by probing values, reasons to live, and using scaling to strengthen intrinsic motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions Maria as the expert in her experience and uses shared decision-making, avoiding an authoritative stance throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces brief change talk and willingness to act (e.g., texting sister), but misses opportunities to amplify reasons, importance/confidence, and commit language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and neutralizes sustain talk without amplifying it, though it rarely uses strategic double-sided reflections or reframes to link sustain talk to values and reduce its force.
