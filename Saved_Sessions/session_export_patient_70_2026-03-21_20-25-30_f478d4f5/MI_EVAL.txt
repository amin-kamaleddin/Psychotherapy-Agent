# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, with warm opening lines and frequent affirmations that normalize Lars's feelings and avoid blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and reflective listening, summarizing ambivalence and practical confusion; could probe deeper into affect (guilt, shame, restlessness) with more emotional reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, actionable focus on completing the polytech application, breaking tasks into steps, offering to do them together, and scaffolding role-plays.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reads as a collaborative effort—checks readiness, invites input, and offers to work together—but sometimes leads content creation by providing preformatted wording rather than fully co-constructing phrasing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Lars's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure, which reinforces his ownership of next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motives and links actions to values (job, fatherhood, avoiding prison) and prompts concrete steps, but could use more evocative open questions to deepen DARN-C and assess confidence for high‑risk moments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership through invitations and joint problem-solving, though occasionally slips into an expert role by supplying exact scripts instead of inviting Lars to generate wording first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change language and concrete planning, and prompts rehearsal, but could further amplify aspirational statements to strengthen desire, ability, reasons, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk (boredom, loyalty to mates) and offers options, but does not consistently use strategic techniques (double-sided reflections, reframing, exploring downsides) to soften or redirect it.
