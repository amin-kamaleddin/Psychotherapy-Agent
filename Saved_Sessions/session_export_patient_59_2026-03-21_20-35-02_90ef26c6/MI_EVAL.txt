# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, conveyed unconditional positive regard (e.g., opening statement and frequent validations) and avoided shaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that built rapport, but missed opportunities to probe deeper emotions (frustration, embarrassment, fear) for greater empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on resolving the citation and minimizing school/probation consequences, repeatedly returning the conversation to concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a helpful partner by co-producing scripts and asking preferences, but at times provided unilateral legal information rather than inviting Jamal to propose or prioritize options first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice and agency by framing options, asking permission before offering tools, and affirming Jamal's preferences and control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited mostly pragmatic information and problem-solving; did not systematically draw out Jamal's intrinsic reasons for change or use scaling questions to assess importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and solicited priorities, but could increase partnering by more often asking Jamal to generate solutions and co-design actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced brief change-oriented remarks and provided practical steps, but did not systematically elicit desire, reasons, or commitment for longer-term change beyond procedural compliance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not confront minimization and tactfully redirected to legal realities and next steps, though could strengthen impact with more double-sided reflections highlighting discrepancy.
