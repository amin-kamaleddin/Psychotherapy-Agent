# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated respect and nonjudgmental support, opening warmly and validating Anya's concerns and tentative willingness. No blaming remarks were made and practical options were offered with unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening captured values and ambivalence, using accurate reflections and summaries. There were missed opportunities to linger on emotional nuance (humor, shame, nervousness) and deepen affective reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on gambling/financial stability and tied goals to Anya's values, repeatedly steering to concrete, actionable steps. Scaling and plan rehearsal kept momentum and prevented drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly co-constructed, with the clinician soliciting Anya's input at every decision point and adjusting content to her feedback. Reciprocal planning, scripting, and rehearsal demonstrated true two-way collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported choice by offering options, inviting tweaks, and framing suggestions as optional supports rather than mandates. This reinforced Anya's ownership of the plan and preferred language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evoked Anya's intrinsic motivations using open questions, scaling, and eliciting her own wording and ideas. The clinician scaffolded doable steps and repeatedly drew out change reasons tied to personal values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance through offering options and asking preferences, inviting role-play and edits to scripts. At times the clinician provided detailed instructions rather than co-generating every element with Anya.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk well using importance/confidence scaling and prompting specific steps, building on Anya's motivation. Could more consistently amplify and summarize her change statements to elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing for the status quo but did limited strategic work to soften sustain talk when Anya minimized the problem or expressed doubt. More use of double-sided reflections and explicit cost-benefit framing could better tip ambivalence toward change.
