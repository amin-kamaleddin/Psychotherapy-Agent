# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes slips, and affirms the patient's efforts throughout the session without blaming or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate, practical reflections that fit the patient's life, but relies mostly on content‑level reflections and misses deeper emotional or ambivalence exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on smoking, respectfully redirects away from alcohol, and moves efficiently to a specific, measurable plan with a two‑week follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly a joint endeavor—patient input is solicited and incorporated, suggestions are tailored to her routine, and the plan reflects her preferences and expertise about her life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking permission, accepting preferences without pressure, and framing restarts and slips as the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation is present (reflecting motives, eliciting readiness), but the clinician moves quickly into advice and solution provision rather than using open questions to draw out deeper motives and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative process by asking permission and offering options, though parts of the plan were more clinician‑driven than fully elicited step‑by‑step from the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and amplifies the patient's change talk and sets a small achievable goal, but misses opportunities to elicit stronger personal reasons and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects when resistance arises, but could better use double‑sided or complex reflections to resolve ambivalence.
