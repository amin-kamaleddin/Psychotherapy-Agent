# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, offering gentle troubleshooting and accepting patient limits without disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking (summaries, naming anxiety) but missed opportunities for deeper emotional exploration and more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing phone/social media use with tightly organized, measurable steps and outcome indicators throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving: co-created the plan, tested solutions together, and solicited permission before offering help, keeping the patient actively engaged.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and control by offering options, scaling readiness, framing actions as experiments, and accepting caveats without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and concrete steps (sleep, grades, baseline use) but tended to move to solution-building without further deepening intrinsic reasons or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with menu-style options and permission-seeking, though at times the clinician adopted a slightly more instructional/expert stance when giving technical guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited specifics and readiness and affirmed concrete change language, but could have amplified and consolidated the patient's own reasons for change more strategically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and offered practical substitutions and tech workarounds, yet used few MI-specific techniques (double-sided reflections, strategic summaries) to shift sustain talk toward change.
