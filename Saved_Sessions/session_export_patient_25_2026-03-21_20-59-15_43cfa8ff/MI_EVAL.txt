# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental stance with multiple affirmations and respectful language; no signs of labeling or minimizing the patient's experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated pride and worry, but missed some opportunities to deepen emotional reflections (e.g., exploring shame or fear of exclusion more explicitly).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking cessation and alcohol-related goals throughout the session and used concrete tools (confidence ruler, role‑play, small achievable tasks) to move toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem‑solving, solicited the patient's preferences, and co-created scripts and role‑plays with the patient guiding choices and content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission to role‑play, and framing suggestions as options rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own motivations and strategies (what they've tried, doable next steps, confidence ruler), drawing out pride and commitment language rather than imposing reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance from the start, repeatedly deferred to the patient's expertise, and invited the patient to choose strategies and lead the practice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk using open questions, reflections, a confidence ruler, and role‑play, which increased the patient's expressed confidence and intention.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed social pressure into concrete supports and scripts, though could further reduce sustain talk with double‑sided reflections and explicit validation of the pull to drink.
