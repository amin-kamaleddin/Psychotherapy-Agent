# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the client's experience, validating humiliation and avoidance without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, nuanced empathic reflections that captured both content and emotion and tracked shifts in the client's perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on school attendance/safety and consistently tied questions and reflections to concrete steps for getting to class.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative, using inclusive language, soliciting the client's input, and co-constructing the plan rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized client choice and control, offered options and respected the client's preferences without pressuring a single solution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's own motivations and reasons for change, eliciting self-generated plans and a measurable trial period.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a shared endeavor, privileged the client's expertise about what would work, and built the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk, reinforced small commitments, and asked for observable indicators of success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to practical options, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections, brief pros/cons, and stronger elicitation of confidence.
