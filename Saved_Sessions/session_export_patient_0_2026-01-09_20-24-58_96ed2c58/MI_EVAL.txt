# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, normalizing concerns and tailoring the approach to harm reduction rather than pushing abstinence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates a warm, attuned tone and some reflections (e.g., validating fear and frustration), but misses opportunities for deeper, more complex emotional reflections about shame or defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on meth use related to the infection and on concrete harm‑reduction actions, repeatedly bringing the conversation back to practical next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames interaction as joint problem‑solving by soliciting preferences, offering choices, and co‑constructing plans like scripts and clinic options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice and avoids pressure, offering options and asking permission before intervening or searching for resources.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation (concern about rehospitalization, openness to supplies) but relies more on information and logistics than on evocative questions to draw out the patient's own reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—asks permission, adapts language to the patient's preference, and invites the patient to shape decisions and next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits change‑oriented statements about safer use but provides mostly info and logistical help rather than amplifying or exploring the patient's own reasons, confidence, or ambivalence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and honors ambivalence by redirecting to harm‑reduction options, though could use more double‑sided reflections to gently highlight mixed feelings.
