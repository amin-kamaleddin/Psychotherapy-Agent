# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard with affirmations and normalization, avoiding blame and validating Lukas’s perspective throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent accurate reflections and tonal matching captured ambivalence and consequences well, but the clinician missed opportunities to probe deeper affective themes like shame, loneliness, or identity threats.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing disruptive meth use and protecting audition/rent money, consistently steering conversation toward concrete, actionable steps and plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with co-produced solutions (manager-hold plan, texts, rehearsals) and frequent invitations for Lukas to choose what to work on next.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by offering options, respecting Lukas’s choice not to quit, and using invitational language so decisions remained his to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Lukas’s motivations and past strategies and had him draft plans, but at times leaned toward offering concrete solutions rather than consistently amplifying his own reasons, values, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Lukas as the expert, invited his preferences repeatedly, and collaborated on drafting and refining plans, demonstrating strong power-sharing throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited specific first steps and reinforced change-oriented language, though could have more systematically amplified, summarized, and asked for importance/confidence or commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk while reframing toward practical steps, but used few double-sided reflections or explicit contrasts to more actively soften sustain talk.
