# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental opening and consistent validation of Erik's defensiveness conveyed strong unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician used timely reflections that reduced defensiveness, but empathic responses were brief and more cognitive than deeply explorative of underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session kept a clear focus on diabetes-related targets and concrete next steps, repeatedly steering toward practical goals and follow-up plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician invited input, revised the question list with Erik, and practiced lines together, though much content and structure was supplied by the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Erik's autonomy by offering options, asking permission to practice, and coaching him to request a time-limited lifestyle trial and written targets.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Limited elicitation of Erik's intrinsic motivation — the clinician translated desires into actions but did not probe personal reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction felt collaborative with co-creation and role-play, but the clinician's delivery of long checklists kept the balance somewhat expert-driven rather than fully privileging Erik's priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Erik showed emerging change talk and the clinician reinforced action, but missed opportunities to elicit and amplify Erik's own reasons, importance, and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician validated sustain talk and avoided confrontation, helping shift toward planning, though double-sided reflections could have more explicitly acknowledged ambivalence while leaning toward change.
