# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing small-step change and highlighting the patient’s priorities. No confrontational language; clinician warmly validates the patient's stance and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring both content and ambivalence. Multiple reflections and summaries clearly capture Ahmed’s feelings and motivations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (reducing smoking around the kids) and narrows to concrete implementation steps. Questions move the conversation from general concern to specific action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly two-way with the clinician soliciting and integrating Ahmed’s ideas and routines. The plan is co-designed around what fits his work and family life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly preserves and affirms Ahmed’s choice and control, accepting his statement that he’s not quitting yet. Frames changes as the patient’s experiment and asks which option he wants to try first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations and values (kids’ health, pride, spouse noticing) and links them to concrete steps. Builds the plan from the patient’s own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language and explicit partnership (e.g., designing an experiment together) so the patient’s solutions drive the plan. Clinician integrates patient-generated strategies rather than imposing expert solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and cultivates change talk, producing concrete commitments to reduce exposure (no smoking around kids, locked box, gum). Missed opportunities remain to amplify and consolidate stronger commitment language or use scaling to elicit confidence/importance.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward achievable changes, effectively minimizing reinforcement of the status quo. Could more strategically use double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence and further reduce sustain talk.
