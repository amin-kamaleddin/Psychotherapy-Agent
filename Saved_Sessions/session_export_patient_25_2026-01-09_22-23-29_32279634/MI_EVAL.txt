# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard—praises the quit, normalizes slips, and responds nonjudgmentally to concerns, fostering warmth and support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic acknowledgement and normalization but leans toward offering information and suggestions rather than using deeper, complex reflections to amplify feelings and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking reinforcement and alcohol-reduction strategies, progressing logically to concrete, actionable steps for events.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative stance—offers options, asks patient preferences, and integrates the patient’s choices (scripts vs. role-play) to co‑construct the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by presenting multiple approaches, asking which the patient prefers, and avoiding pressure to adopt any single solution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical preferences and choices but does little to draw out intrinsic motivations, values, or personally meaningful reasons for change beyond initial statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making through option-giving, tailored script choices, and role‑play offers that center the patient’s input and expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and builds on existing change talk with affirmations and concrete plans, but much content is clinician-provided rather than elicited from the patient’s own reasons or confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids amplifying sustain talk by offering flexible alternatives and gentler phrasing, though could use more double-sided reflections or elicited counterarguments to further soften sustain talk.
