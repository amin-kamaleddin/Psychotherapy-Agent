# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing Laura’s experience and using affirming language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed solid perspective-taking and reflective listening with validating statements, but often moved quickly into practical suggestions rather than deeper emotional exploration that could deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, goal-directed focus from values to a tiny behavior and implementation intentions, sustaining a coherent progression toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited patient preferences and integrated them into the plan, but frequent long lists of prescriptive tips at times shifted the balance toward clinician-driven problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking for preferences, avoiding pressure, and emphasizing that misses are data rather than failures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited doable steps, confidence ratings, and visualization to draw out motivation, but relied periodically on external strategies instead of amplifying Laura’s own reasons and self-generated solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly invited input, negotiated check-in details, and framed suggestions as options, fostering genuine power-sharing and client influence over session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with planning and a confidence ruler, yet at times shifted too rapidly to offering strategies instead of reflecting and amplifying Laura’s change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used normalization, fallback plans, and reframing to reduce its impact, though more MI-specific double-sided reflections could have further softened sustain talk.
