# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicated respect and support throughout and avoided judgment, but often shifted quickly to problem‑solving without deeper normalization or explicit unconditional positive regard tailored to Amina's emotions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered basic empathic statements and calming techniques and acknowledged safety concerns, yet provided few complex reflections that captured Amina's nuanced feelings of being stuck, nervous, and overwhelmed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on improving co‑parenting interactions and reducing child exposure to conflict; every intervention tied directly to that behavioral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative — iteratively drafted and revised scripts with Amina, soliciting feedback and honoring her preferences rather than imposing a single solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice by offering alternatives, asking which option would help most, and respecting Amina's wording preferences without coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some preferences and centered the child's wellbeing, but relied mainly on providing language and strategies rather than systematically drawing out Amina's intrinsic motivations, values, or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through options and co‑creation of texts, yet frequently led with solutions instead of turning questions back to Amina to fully co‑generate ideas and wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change language about caring for her son and co‑creating scripts, but missed opportunities to ask evocative questions that would deepen Amina's own reasons, confidence, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Responded to sustain talk with practical boundaries and scripts that prevent escalation, but did not strategically reflect or use double‑sided reflections to explore and reduce ambivalence.
