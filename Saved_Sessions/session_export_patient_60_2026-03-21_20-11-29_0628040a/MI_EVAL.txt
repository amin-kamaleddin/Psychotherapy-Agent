# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows nonjudgmental regard and respect, validating Jason's goals and avoiding blame while honoring his preferences throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases and check-ins. Reflections were somewhat task-focused and did not probe deeper emotions or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target task of preparing Jason for the conduct meeting with actionable steps (drafting, rehearsal, printing). There was no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving: the clinician offered options, invited choice, and accommodated Jason's requests (e.g., printed copies), positioning clinician and client as partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized and honored Jason's choices (Option A vs B, role-play vs written) and framed actions as his decision, supporting a strong sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited minimal internal motivation for broader behavioral change, focusing on pragmatic solutions to avoid immediate consequences rather than exploring values, future costs, or personal reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited Jason's input and deferred final decisions to him, creating a collaborative tone; however, the clinician provided templates and directives rather than fully eliciting Jason's own wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited very little client language in favor of behavior change beyond resolving the incident and did not amplify or follow up on brief change statements to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did not actively shift attention away from sustain talk and at times facilitated minimizations by helping craft a non-admitting statement instead of using double-sided reflections or gentle discrepancy strategies.
