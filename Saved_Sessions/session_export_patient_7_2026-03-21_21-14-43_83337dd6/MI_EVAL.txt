# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and accepting the client's caveat of 'not promising' without admonishment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening and paraphrase, acknowledging both emotion and context to maintain rapport with a guarded adolescent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing yelling with measurable goals, concrete small steps, safety checks, and rehearsal, moving logically from problem identification to a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co-constructed the plan by soliciting Amina's preferences, offering choices, rehearsing on request, and positioning the clinician as a helper rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by honoring privacy requests and limited commitments, offering multiple options and avoiding pressuring language to reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited Amina's own ideas and motivations (e.g., safety concerns, desire for autonomy) and used those to shape realistic, internally motivated strategies despite minimal verbalization.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered an egalitarian interaction by asking permission, inviting choices, and letting Amina direct key decisions like wording, location, and reminders.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced limited change talk (importance rating, small step), affirmed doable actions, but missed some opportunities for deeper evocative questions to expand desire/reasons/ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed small attempts as meaningful to soften sustain talk, though more use of double-sided reflections could have better acknowledged both sides of ambivalence.
