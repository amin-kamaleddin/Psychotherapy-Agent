### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental, warm, and validating, using normalization and balanced reflections to convey unconditional positive regard. Affirmations and respectful language reinforce support throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent accurate reflections and clear perspective-taking capture emotional nuance and ambivalence. Responses show sustained effort to understand Claudia's experience and feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on reducing alcohol with concrete, doable strategies and a measurable two‑week check‑in. Maintains goal orientation while respecting the client's pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative, using Elicit–Provide–Elicit to offer options and invite the client's choice. Plans and follow‑up are co‑constructed and mutually agreed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves choice and ownership by framing suggestions as experiments and avoiding pressuring language. Repeatedly asks which options feel doable, supporting client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
