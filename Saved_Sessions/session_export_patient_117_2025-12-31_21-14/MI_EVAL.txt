**Comments:** Notices and reinforces change talk (acknowledging progress, asking to try). Does not systematically elicit stronger DARN-C (desire, ability, reasons, need, commitment) language or amplify confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):**  
- [ ] 1  - [ ] 2  - [ ] 3  - [x] 4  - [ ] 5  
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk, normalizes doubts and validates slips (“slips are part of progress”), reducing defensiveness. Could improve by using more double-sided reflections to explicitly resolve ambivalence toward change.
