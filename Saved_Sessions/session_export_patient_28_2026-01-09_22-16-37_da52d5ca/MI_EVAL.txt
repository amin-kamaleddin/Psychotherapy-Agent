# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing limits and responding patiently to hesitations; never shamed or minimized Dante's barriers.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided several accurate reflections and validations of practical constraints and past success, but could have explored deeper feelings about letting routines slip rather than moving quickly to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on re-establishing exercise and moved logically from exploration to concrete micro-goals, a practical checklist, and a scheduled 2-week check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt clearly joint: the clinician solicited Dante's preferences, incorporated his feedback into materials, and invited shared planning for follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice with multiple options, tentative language, and encouragement of small experiments while avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited relevant change language and set structures to draw out motivation, but leaned more on offering solutions than on eliciting Dante's own reasons, confidence, or evocative summaries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach that treated Dante as the expert on his life, co-creating the checklist, routines, and follow-up plan based on his input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and built a concrete micro-plan with a check-in to support engagement, but could have more actively amplified Dante's reasons for change and probed confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed barriers into manageable alternatives while validating limits, though there were missed chances for double-sided reflections to explicitly surface ambivalence.
