# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, using validating reflections and normalizing ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking with timely, complex reflections and summaries that supported the client feeling understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol-related risk reduction, linking questions and planning directly to safety and preserving school/band functioning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—permission was sought, options were offered, and the client’s choices consistently shaped the co-created plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice, avoided pressuring language, normalized imperfection, and framed options to reinforce voluntary uptake of strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions and confidence scaling to draw out the client's motivations, but tended to move into solution-giving before fully amplifying intrinsic reasons or linking to values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine power-sharing and co-creation by asking permission, inviting selection of steps, and offering help only when the client agreed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change talk (open questions, scaling) but could have paused more to reflect and amplify the client's own desire/ability/need language before offering strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected skillfully, though there were missed opportunities for double-sided or reframing reflections before shifting to problem-solving.
