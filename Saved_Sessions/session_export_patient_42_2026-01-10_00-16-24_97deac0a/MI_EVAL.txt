# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used a warm, nonjudgmental, and supportive tone throughout, normalized ambivalence, and consistently affirmed Elise’s choices, creating unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captured ambivalence and perspective, though deeper, affect-laden or complex reflections exploring the emotional meaning of 'belonging' could have further deepened empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear focus on smoking cessation and a concrete, small-step plan (skipping one evening), with role-plays and rehearsals aligned to that target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician invited input, practiced in-session with Elise, and adapted lines and rehearsal difficulty to her preferences, reflecting genuine two-way teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirmed Elise’s autonomy, avoided pressure, offered multiple options, and asked about preferences to reinforce Elise’s ownership of decisions and the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician focused on eliciting Elise’s own reasons and resources (gains, confidence scaling, self-chosen steps) and built on her statements to strengthen internal motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician positioned Elise as the decision-maker and co-created the plan, asking which scripts felt like her and offering to practice only as desired, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and amplified change talk through targeted questions, scaling, eliciting a concrete small step, and reinforcing incremental successes that raised confidence and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged the social benefits of smoking without reinforcing sustain talk, validated concerns, and strategically redirected to alternatives and practical strategies to support change.
