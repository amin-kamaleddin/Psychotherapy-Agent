**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms patient control and choice (offers options, checks preference, states decision is hers). Frames guidance as optional and preserves ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ] 1  [ ] 2  [ ] 3  [x] 4  [ ] 5  
**Comments:** Elicits patient’s immediate motivations and plan (choosing handout, practicing script), and reinforces change talk. Could deepen intrinsic motives (meaning of parenting, hopes/fears, confidence).

**Comments:** Treats session as joint problem-solving (offers options, invites practice). At times defaults to providing answers/scripts and praise, which recenters clinician expertise instead of expanding patient problem-solving.

**Comments:** Notices and reinforces change talk (importance of healthy baby; specific steps to remove alcohol) and elicits commitment by practicing language. Missed opportunities to probe confidence, barriers, and past successes.
