# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, validating stance (e.g., “You’re right to feel angry and hurt — that sounds really lonely,” “You handled this well”). No blaming for skipping school; normalization of feelings present. Minor opportunity: more individualized affirmations of strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used reflective statements showing understanding of emotional state (“that sounds really lonely,” “I’m hearing you’ve been avoiding school…”); safety check conducted. Reflections were accurate but often high-level/repetitive; deeper probes of vulnerability/shame were limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept focus on concrete, behaviorally relevant goals and offered a clear four-step plan (documenting, talk with teacher, message friend, grounding). Slightly prescriptive early on; clinician did ask which options felt doable afterward.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered options, asked permission to help draft message, and invited role‑play (“do you want help drafting a message?”). At times detailed scripting and stepwise directives edged toward telling rather than co‑creating.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported patient choice throughout (“Which of those feels doable…”, “You don’t have to force staff contact if you’re not ready”). Repeatedly affirmed control over decisions and timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited short behavioral commitments (keeping a list, possibly sending a message) but did not systematically evoke deeper reasons, values, or long‑term motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered collaborative stance via invitations and checks (offered tweaks to wording, role‑play). Occasionally reverted to directing with final scripts before co‑authoring them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created opportunities for brief change talk (“I’ll maybe do #1”), provided concrete options, but did not amplify or deepen desire/ability/reasons/commitment (no confidence or commitment scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validated mistrust and reluctance (neutral stance), avoided reinforcing sustain talk, but used few double‑sided reflections or reframes to strategically soften sustain talk and link it to small acceptable steps.
