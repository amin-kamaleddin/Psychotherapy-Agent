# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Amira's choices and framing suggestions as options rather than directives.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking that tied interventions to Amira's values, but reflections were often surface-level and rarely named underlying emotions or nuanced feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on initiating brief morning activity and advanced concrete, actionable steps throughout the exchange while resisting tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was explicitly collaborative with the clinician and client co-designing the routine; the psychiatrist solicited preferences at each decision point and used inclusive, joint problem‑solving language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed suggestions as options, affirmed Amira's limits, and supported her ownership of the plan by asking her to choose specifics like anchor, days, and note wording.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out Amira's motives and linked small actions to personally meaningful outcomes (energy, strength, confidence), but could deepen evocation by eliciting broader values, past successes, or more internal motivators.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Amira as the expert on what will work, repeatedly asking her to choose among options and co‑creating the plan so decision-making was clearly shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and tied choices to stated benefits, though there were missed opportunities to explicitly amplify specific change statements or summarize cumulative change talk to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and respected Amira's limits, but could further soften sustain talk by using double‑sided reflections and briefly exploring barriers and past successes.
