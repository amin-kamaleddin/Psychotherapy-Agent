### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and helpful at the practical level and validated feelings, but the clinician missed opportunities to deepen emotional empathy by exploring anxiety, embarrassment, or the personal meaning of the diagnosis.

### Direction

**Comments:** Kept a clear, steady focus on blood glucose monitoring and moved the session toward a specific, measurable action with concrete logistical steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Marcus’s sense of choice and agency by offering options, checking readiness/confidence, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Marcus’s ideas and practical motivations, but could have probed more for personal values and reasons for change and invited him to voice his own arguments for checking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed shared decision-making and power-sharing, letting Marcus choose among options and shaping the plan based on his input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited change talk with scaling and concrete planning, yet could have amplified and summarized Marcus’s change language and probed for deeper reasons and stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected ambivalence toward practical solutions, though more use of double-sided reflections could further soften resistance.
