# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, validating and normalizing Erik’s experience and progress without judgment. Repeated affirmations and supportive tone reinforce acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic attunement with accurate reflections and linkage of details to Erik’s motivation and distress. Clinician notices affect and ambivalence and responds with understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (gym attendance and habits) and consistently returns to concrete, actionable steps. Uses scaling and targeted problem-solving to move the session toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as genuine team effort with shared decision-making; clinician solicits Erik’s ideas and follows his lead. Suggestions are offered as options and plans are co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports Erik’s sense of choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and checking willingness. Normalizes uncertainty without pressuring or imposing an agenda.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits reasons and resources for change and draws out practical motivators, but could probe deeper into personally held values or identity to strengthen internal motivation. More explicit linking of values to the plan would boost evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates clearly as a collaborator rather than an expert prescriber, asking permission and inviting choices about rehearsal and planning. The clinician centers Erik’s lived knowledge in shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk, highlights progress and elicits concrete plans and confidence ratings. Could more actively amplify and summarize change language to elicit firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and skillfully pivots to problem-solving and coping strategies to avoid reinforcing it. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections or explicit reframes to more directly soften sustain language.
