# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, affirming Samuel's progress and offering practical alternatives while normalizing difficulty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows solid empathic listening and validation (praise, tailoring options) but uses few complex reflections and misses some emotional depth behind ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on smoking reduction with clear, practical plans (lunch plan, NRT dosing, tracking) and consistent steering toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative—asks questions, offers choices, and co-constructs a plan—but long lists of expert recommendations sometimes dominate the exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, checking preferences, and encouraging adjustments, reinforcing Samuel's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some practical commitment-oriented speech but rarely draws out internal motivations, values, or confidence, relying more on advice than elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally adopts a collaborative stance with permission and choices, though at times provides directive detail without first checking Samuel's preference for that level of input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and builds on change talk with praise and concrete planning, but could more actively elicit and amplify the client's reasons and meaning to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers effective alternatives, yet misses opportunities for double-sided reflections that explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
