# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveyed warm, nonjudgmental acceptance throughout (validations, praise, normalizing ambivalence) and avoided blaming language while affirming Kevin's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good use of accurate reflections and summaries and open questions showed understanding, but the clinician missed opportunities to probe deeper into underlying emotions like shame and stress triggers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on drinking and harm‑reduction, progressing logically from exploration to specific action planning and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The exchange was highly collaborative—options were offered, choices deferred to Kevin, and plans and language were co‑created and rehearsed together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently emphasized choice, offered multiple options rather than directives, and respected Kevin's pacing and tentative commitments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation was drawn from Kevin via evocative questions and scaling, and the clinician amplified and scaffolded Kevin's own ideas into a concrete, actionable plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A genuine partner‑like stance was evident—permission was sought, preferences invited, and the clinician worked with Kevin to co‑construct practical strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited and strengthened change talk through evocative prompts, a confidence ruler, and encouragement of specific, small steps and rehearsals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and not argued with, using some double‑sided framing, but there were missed chances to more actively roll with resistance or selectively amplify faint change statements.
