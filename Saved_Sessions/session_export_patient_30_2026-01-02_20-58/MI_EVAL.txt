# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
- [x] Criterion met
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental acceptance (e.g., “I hear your worry — that’s very reasonable,” “I’ll respect wanting straight answers, not pressure”). Avoided labeling/shaming despite repeated resistance.

### Empathy
- [x] Criterion met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear empathic statements and accurate reflections of concerns (worry about muscle weakness, desire for straight answers). Mostly cognitive and brief; limited deeper exploration of emotional nuance or life barriers.

### Direction
- [x] Criterion met
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on cholesterol management and concrete options/monitoring throughout (ASCVD risk, diet-first plan, specific statin/monitoring recommendations).

### Collaboration
- [x] Criterion met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited patient input and offered options (asked permission to review labs, offered choices). Interaction shifted to information delivery at times, limiting deeper joint problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
- [x] Criterion met
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported patient choice and reinforced autonomy (validated preference to try diet, “Tell your doctor your preference,” avoided coercive language).

### Evocation
- [ ] Criterion not met
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of facts and preferences (labs, whether to calculate risk), but limited effort to draw out personal motivations, values, or reasons for change beyond stated concerns.

### Partnership
- [x] Criterion met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated patient as partner (asked permission, co‑created a diet‑first plan). However, clinician largely led the session and patient ideas did not strongly shape follow‑up steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
- [ ] Criterion not met
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Change talk was minimal and tentative; clinician did not systematically amplify or explore the patient’s tentative change language or elicit commitment/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
- [ ] Criterion not met
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and provided balanced information, but used few strategic MI techniques (double‑sided reflections, reframing) to soften repeated sustain talk and reduce resistance.
