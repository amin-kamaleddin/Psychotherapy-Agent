# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard and explicitly reassures the patient, normalizing ambivalence; the patient acknowledges feeling unjudged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, frequent reflections that capture content and feeling and mirrors the patient's language, deepening rapport and eliciting further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol-related fall risk and narrows to a specific, measurable experiment (one less glass), with a logical progression from ambivalence to action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly team-oriented: the clinician invites input, follows the patient's lead, and helps co-create a practical plan rather than prescribing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy by offering options, leaving choices to the patient, and framing the experiment as low-pressure and non‑committal.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motives and personally meaningful reasons for change, prompting values-based language about steadiness and independence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly solicits the patient's preferences and responds to her choices, framing decisions as hers and asking how she wants to measure outcomes.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits, reinforces, and develops change talk with focused, evocative follow-ups, producing concrete plans and a confidence rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and uses reflective, double-sided responses to acknowledge sustain talk and redirect toward a small, feasible experiment.
