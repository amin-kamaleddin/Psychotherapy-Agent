# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validates and normalizes the student and uses affirming language and praise without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening throughout, with paraphrases that capture ambivalence and match the student's affect and content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the target behavior (homework before hanging out) and moves the conversation to concrete, actionable steps (calendar, alarm, plan).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly co-constructs the plan, repeatedly solicits the student's preferences, and offers practical help (drafting texts, setting alarms) in a partnership stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the student's freedom to choose, offers multiple options, accepts imperfection, and avoids pressure while affirming ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits values, ideas, and past successes to draw out change talk, but could probe deeper into personal reasons and use scaling to strengthen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shares power throughout the interaction by deferring to the student's choices, asking which option they'd prefer, and inviting joint planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions and reflections and references past success, though missed opportunities to boost confidence/commitment (e.g., rulers).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk but does not consistently redirect or explore barriers with techniques like double-sided reflections or contingency planning to reduce its pull.
