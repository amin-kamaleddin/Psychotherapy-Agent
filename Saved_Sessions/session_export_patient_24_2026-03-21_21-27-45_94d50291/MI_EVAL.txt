# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed consistent nonjudgmental, respectful regard, normalizing ambivalence and validating the client's perspective which fostered strong rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured content and ambivalence and showed perspective-taking, but empathic work stayed at the surface level; deeper, more complex reflections exploring underlying feelings would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol use tied to the client's training goals and moved the session toward a specific, measurable test (Mon/Wed alcohol‑free, tracking, follow‑up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner by co‑creating the plan, offering suggestions tentatively, and inviting client input on scripts, tracking, and start dates.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice by offering options, asking preferences, and affirming decisions, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evoked motivation with targeted questions and scaling, eliciting client‑generated reasons for change, specific goals, and a concrete plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative with shared decision‑making; the clinician continually checked preferences and integrated the client's language into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and strengthened change talk using scaling, concrete options, and summaries, which produced clear commitments (one‑week experiment, if‑then plans, increased confidence).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and neutrally acknowledged skepticism while redirecting toward testing benefits, but could use more double‑sided reflections or explicit validations to further soften sustain talk.
