# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Mateo’s persistence and normalizing his struggle throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and paraphrases captured Mateo’s emotions and pain, helping him feel understood; could have probed slightly deeper emotional nuance but overall empathic stance was strong.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing brief walking and moved from exploration to a specific, measurable plan (5‑minute walk, day/time, warm‑up, stop rule, follow‑up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—clinician and Mateo jointly identified the goal and co‑designed the plan, negotiating timing, supports, stop rules, reminders, and check‑in content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Mateo’s choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and allowing him to set the stop rule and check‑in preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and barriers using scaling and supportive questions, but did not sufficiently probe underlying values or explicitly amplify Mateo’s personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Mateo as the expert in his life and co‑created the plan, consistently soliciting his preferences for timing, warm‑ups, cues, and monitoring.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and operationalized change talk into a concrete plan with scaling and planning questions, but missed opportunities to amplify reasons and elicit a verbal commitment before finalizing the plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledged sustain talk (pain, discouragement) without reinforcing it and pivoted to doable strategies and safeguards to reduce its influence and promote action.
