### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating throughout, normalizing feelings and offering supportive follow‑up which models unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic reflections and acknowledges emotions and values, but could use deeper, more complex reflections of ambivalence in a few moments.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on gambling-related goals with specific, measurable steps and timed commitments, keeping the session action-oriented and on track.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages the client as an active partner and tailors tools to her preferences, though an early clinician-generated list of strategies made the approach slightly expert-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, invites the client to choose steps, and frames suggestions as tools, preserving client choice and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and links them to behavior (e.g., travel goal) and uses scaling and commitment, but could draw out more intrinsic reasons before proposing solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—asks preferences, co-constructs plans, and seeks permission—but supplying many concrete suggestions early slightly reduced shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk, asks for concrete first steps and confidence ratings, yet could more strategically amplify spontaneous change language to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids joining sustain talk and redirects to planning, but rarely uses explicit MI softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or rolling with resistance) to acknowledge ambivalence.
