# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with repeated validations and explicit acceptance of her readiness not to decide; no shaming or moralizing occurred.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate summaries that demonstrated perspective-taking and understanding of her anxiety and concerns about hormones and weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on contraception and related planning while balancing coping needs, providing concise options and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used inclusive language, solicited preferences repeatedly, co-constructed scripts and role-played with the patient, reflecting genuine mutual planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered choices, asked permission before information, accepted her 'not ready' stance, and recommended reversible short trials to preserve patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited relevant fears and preferences and used them in planning, but leaned more on providing information than on systematically exploring deeper values or intrinsic motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as an active decision-maker, invited her input throughout, and shaped the session and plan around her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced some change talk and offered practical interim steps, but could have done more to amplify and deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Gently redirected and normalized ambivalence without arguing, though missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and explicit discrepancy-building to further soften sustain talk.
