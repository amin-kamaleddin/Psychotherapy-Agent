# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect through affirmations and nonjudgmental summaries, avoiding blame or lecturing. The clinician affirms the patient’s ideas and preferences throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective‑taking that validates priorities and concerns. Could deepen emotional exploration with more complex feeling reflections (e.g., fear about parental reaction) to reach the highest rating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, task‑focused direction on managing alcohol exposure and links everything back to soccer and scholarship goals. Progresses logically from exploration to specific planning and a concrete behavioral commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative with frequent check‑ins, invitations for input, and joint construction of the plan. The clinician builds on the patient’s ideas and uses inclusive language rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options and making steps optional (e.g., role‑play is optional). Questions invite the patient to set pace and decide on the most doable step.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient’s own motivations, values, and resources and ties them to concrete strategies. The clinician draws out change reasons from the patient rather than imposing them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters an equal, co‑constructed relationship by inviting choices and following the patient’s lead. The clinician positions the patient as an active collaborator in deciding steps to try.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reflects, and amplifies change talk, guiding toward a specific, doable behavior (rehearsing a refusal line). The patient articulates and deepens commitment to a concrete action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk and redirects conversation toward goals and strategies, reducing resistance. Could more explicitly use double‑sided or strategic reflections to acknowledge ambivalence and accelerate resolution.
