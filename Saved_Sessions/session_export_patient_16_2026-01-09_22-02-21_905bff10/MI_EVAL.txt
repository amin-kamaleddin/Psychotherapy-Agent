# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard and normalizes Anika's experience, using warm, nonjudgmental language and validating her pace. Blaming language is avoided and the tone remains supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking with multiple accurate, complex reflections and summaries that capture emotions, values, and function of vaping. Questions follow reflections to deepen understanding and validate Anika's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on vaping by linking it to Anika's values and steering toward a specific substitution (shootaround) and actionable steps. Each question narrows toward planning and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner with Anika, co-creating the plan through joint drafting and decision-making. The clinician repeatedly asks for her input and follows her choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Anika's right to choose and avoids pressuring language, offering options and eliciting preferences. Respect for her selected low-pressure approach (saving a draft) strengthens ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits personally meaningful reasons for change (better sleep, performance, social enjoyment) and draws motivation from Anika rather than telling her why to change. Converts internal reasons into concrete steps and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Anika as the expert in her life, offering choices and co-authoring the message and plan. Collaborative language and shared decision points demonstrate genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, amplifies, and reinforces change talk, reflecting and summarizing statements that favor alternatives to vaping. Uses targeted questions to move from desire to concrete, specific steps and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change-oriented strategies and alternatives. However, there is limited explicit reflection of earlier minimizations and fewer double-sided reflections to name both sides of ambivalence.
