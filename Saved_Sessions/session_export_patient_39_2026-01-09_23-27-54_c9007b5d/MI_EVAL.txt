# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental stance, repeatedly affirming Elias's efforts and validating his concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections consistently captured Elias's feelings and perspective (enjoyment vs discouragement), showing accurate, empathic understanding throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on increasing safe physical activity and concrete planning (time, moves, safety limits), progressing logically from values to a realistic plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician co-created the plan and materials with Elias, soliciting preferences and revising items (format, font, checkboxes) to suit his needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was reinforced through offering choices, asking permission before suggesting tools, and accepting Elias's right to try or miss days while maintaining his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician evoked Elias's own motives and resources by probing values, past supports, and confidence, and built the plan directly from his stated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction treated Elias as the expert on his life—power was shared via tentative suggestions, invitations to revise, and joint decision-making about the plan and materials.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections elicited and amplified Elias's change language, operationalizing it with specifics (time, movements, start days) and asking about confidence and supports.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician acknowledged barriers and redirected toward solutions without reinforcing sustain talk, though more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries could further help resolve ambivalence.
