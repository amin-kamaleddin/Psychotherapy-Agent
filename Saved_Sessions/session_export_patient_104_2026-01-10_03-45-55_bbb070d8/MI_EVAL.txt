# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and reduced shame through validating language and affirmations of Nadine's efforts. Avoided blaming language and repeatedly reassured her that asking for support was appropriate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely empathic reflections that named exhaustion, worry, and fear of judgment. Reflections captured both affect and content, showing strong perspective-taking throughout the interaction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on producing a practical one-page note and plan for obtaining reassurance. Minimal topic drift and each exchange moved the session toward the actionable goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as an equal partnership by soliciting input at every step and iteratively incorporating Nadine's exact phrasing. The final product was clearly co-constructed rather than clinician-imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Nadine's sense of choice by offering options (hand vs read, wording choices) and framing suggestions as choices rather than directives. Affirmed her preferences and respected her control over how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical concerns and preferences (what to include, how to hand the note) but focused more on information-gathering than on deeper intrinsic motivations. There were missed opportunities to probe values or confidence for follow-through.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively shared power by asking permission, offering choices, and co-authoring the note with Nadine's wording retained. Repeatedly checked for approval and adjustments, reinforcing collaborative decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited preparatory change talk and encouraged concrete actions (creating and handing the note), but rarely amplified or reflected change statements to strengthen commitment. Did not consistently ask about confidence or commitments to follow through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected with normalization and practical steps, moving toward actionable support. A higher score would require more strategic double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence while highlighting reasons for change.
