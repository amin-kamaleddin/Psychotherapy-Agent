# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, creating a warm, accepting tone; could be slightly strengthened by briefly normalizing common obstacles (e.g., withdrawal cues).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-quality, specific reflective listening that captured both content and feeling and helped elicit more change talk; occasional use of more complex reflections naming underlying emotions would deepen empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking and a concrete, testable experiment with measurable outcomes, keeping the session goal-directed and stepwise.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative with mutual planning and frequent solicitation of the client's input, producing a realistic plan driven by the patient's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed the client's freedom to choose, framed the experiment as voluntary and reversible, and avoided coercive language to support agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out intrinsic reasons by linking the experiment to personal values and eliciting commitment, but could deepen internal motivation with scaling questions, evocative 'why' prompts, and exploration of past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the client as the expert, handed decision-making to him, and co-created a plan that fit his work and priorities, reinforcing shared power in the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk through values-oriented questions and consequence-focused follow-ups, producing concrete commitment to a small experiment linked to priorities.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed ambivalence to move the client forward, though more targeted double-sided reflections and explicit exploration of long-term costs would further reduce sustain talk.
