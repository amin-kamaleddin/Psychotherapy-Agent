# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing fear and affirming the client's pace and limits throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, frequent reflections that capture both content and emotion, showing strong perspective-taking and prompting further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on the target behavior (making an informational clinic call) and uses a stepwise approach to keep the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a mutual problem-solving tone by inviting the client's input and checking summaries, though occasional offering of concrete solutions slightly shifts control toward the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and pace, framing offers as options and asking which supports the client prefers without using pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motives and past strengths that produce change talk, but could deepen intrinsic motivation by probing values and eliciting commitment or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative and invites the client's expertise, yet the clinician's quick provision of scripts and offers to stay on the line somewhat reduced shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies concrete change talk and small doable steps, but stops short of eliciting stronger commitment language or probing confidence/readiness.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and rephrases sustain talk and reframes the task into manageable steps, though more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries could further soften resistance.
