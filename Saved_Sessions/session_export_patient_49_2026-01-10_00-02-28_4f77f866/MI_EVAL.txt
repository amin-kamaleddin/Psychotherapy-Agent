# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing ambivalence and offering harm‑reduction without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective‑taking and occasional reflections, but uses few deep or affective reflections and misses some emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on smoking reduction/cessation with concrete next steps and repeated returns to doable actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites preferences and co‑designs a one‑week plan, though extended clinician‑driven information at times reduces the sense of equal exchange.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering a menu of options, proposing trials rather than mandates, and framing decisions as the patient's.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits commitment to a small change and choices, but rarely probes core values, intrinsic reasons, or importance/confidence to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters patient expertise and tailors tips collaboratively, but sometimes shifts into an expert information delivery without first checking permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk effectively and offers a simple trial plan, yet could ask more questions to deepen desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk and offers harm‑reduction reframes, but could use double‑sided or strategic reflections to more actively soften sustain talk.
