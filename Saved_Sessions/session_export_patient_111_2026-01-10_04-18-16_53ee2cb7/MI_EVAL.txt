# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's experience, validating feelings without shaming and reducing defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate understanding through reflective summaries and mirroring of the patient's language and emotions, which lowered resistance and deepened engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking-related behavior with practical, observable goals and moves the conversation from exploration to concrete planning and troubleshooting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the interaction as a genuine partnership by asking preferences, offering options, and co-constructing a plan that reflects the patient's input (e.g., porch-light signal).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms the patient's control and offers choices, avoiding coercion and reinforcing ownership of any decisions or changes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits relational motives and past successes that support change, but could probe more deeply into the patient's own values, importance/confidence, and intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly models shared decision-making and power sharing, inviting the patient to co-create and operationalize the plan while treating him as the expert on his routine.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out practical change talk and options tied to the relationship, yet misses some opportunities to amplify internally owned reasons and deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully rolls with resistance and redirects to doable steps without arguing, though could more often use double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while nudging toward change.
