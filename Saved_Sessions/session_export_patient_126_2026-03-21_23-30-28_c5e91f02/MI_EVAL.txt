# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys respect and positive regard through nonjudgmental reflections and validation, honoring the patient’s limits and preferences.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and feeling and showing high empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on relevant target behaviors (smoking, alcohol, health monitoring) and steadily moves the patient toward a specific, actionable next step (after-hours BP/cholesterol check).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint problem-solving, repeatedly invites the patient’s input, and offers concrete supports while letting the patient shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, avoids pressure or lecturing, and honors the patient’s preferences to support ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient’s own motivations, values, and concrete plans, scaffolding change talk into specific, feasible actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a collaborative, egalitarian exchange by relinquishing expert authority and co-constructing the plan with the patient’s priorities driving decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change language, invites options, and translates the patient’s ideas into a concrete plan and commitment steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects toward change but rarely uses explicit strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to soften sustain talk and acknowledge the pull of the status quo.
