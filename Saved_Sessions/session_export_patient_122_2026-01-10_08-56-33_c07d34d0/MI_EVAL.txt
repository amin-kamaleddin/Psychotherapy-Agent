# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validated the patient’s struggles throughout, creating a safe atmosphere for discussion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and reflections acknowledged frustration and ambivalence, but empathy was sometimes surface-level and often followed quickly by directive advice rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on increasing short bouts of movement and a breathing tool, progressing each turn toward concrete, measurable plans (when, where, backups, accountability).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist solicited preferences and adapted suggestions, framing work as joint problem-solving, but offered many clinician-generated tips instead of eliciting more patient-generated strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering options, checking willingness before drafting messages, and reinforcing that the patient makes the final decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used confidence rulers and questions about supports to draw out commitment language, but could strengthen evocation by asking more open, values-oriented questions to elicit the patient's own reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited input and co-constructed the plan, conveying that the patient’s preferences matter, though frequent specific clinician recommendations sometimes positioned the clinician as the primary solution source.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk through specific planning and confidence scaling, but could more consistently reflect and amplify the patient's own change statements to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and provided pragmatic miss-day plans to redirect, but could more actively soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections or explicit reframing of ambivalence.
