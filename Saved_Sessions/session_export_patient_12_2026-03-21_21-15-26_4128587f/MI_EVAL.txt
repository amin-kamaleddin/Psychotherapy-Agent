# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician communicates clear, unconditional positive regard, normalizes difficulty, and maintains a respectful, non-blaming tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections capture Amira's shame and helplessness, but the clinician misses a few opportunities to deepen emotional exploration and probe the meaning or history of her feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session focus is consistently on the concrete target (scheduling and attending an intake) with minimal drift and repeated steering toward executable steps and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is clearly joint problem-solving: the clinician invites role-play, repeatedly asks for preferences, and co-constructs scripts, checklists, and reminder plans with Amira.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician offers choices, seeks permission before advising, acknowledges limits (e.g., cannot send texts), and helps Amira create a plan that preserves her ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, evocative questions and a focus on a small first step elicit some change-related talk, but the clinician could more directly draw out Amira's values, reasons, and confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently invites Amira's input, shares decision-making, and positions themselves as an ally, co-creating the plan and asking for acceptance or modification.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician scaffolds and elicits modest change talk and helps specify concrete actions, but could better amplify and summarize Amira's statements to build momentum and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
