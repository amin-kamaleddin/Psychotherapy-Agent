# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated nonjudgmental, warm acceptance and validated Yasmin's values and concerns throughout the session. Tone and language conveyed unconditional positive regard with no shaming or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing ambivalence and affect clearly. Reflections and validations consistently mirrored Yasmin’s experience and normalized her fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on shifting from dieting to sustainable health behaviors and reducing body dissatisfaction. Session progressed logically from exploration to a concrete, collaboratively developed plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with joint problem-solving and incorporation of Yasmin’s modifications and preferences. Decisions about timing, message format, and check-ins were co-created rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission, and honoring Yasmin’s request to avoid weight unless she raises it. Invitational language reinforced Yasmin’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Yasmin’s values, reasons, and concrete steps for change, reinforcing internal motivation and self-efficacy. The clinician drew plans from Yasmin rather than supplying reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled strong partnership by repeatedly inviting input and incorporating Yasmin’s preferences into the plan. Suggestions were framed as options and decisions were negotiated collaboratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using values exploration and a confidence ruler, prompting concrete steps. Could further amplify and summarize Yasmin’s change statements to increase momentum toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and redirected focus to non-weight goals and coping strategies, avoiding reinforcement of dieting language. Could add more double-sided reflections to explicitly contain sustain talk while shifting toward change.
