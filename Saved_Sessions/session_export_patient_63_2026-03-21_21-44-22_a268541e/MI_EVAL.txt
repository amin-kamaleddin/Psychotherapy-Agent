### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Jordan's experience and affirming his honesty; avoids labeling and conveys unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained, accurate perspective-taking with reflective summaries that capture ambivalence and emotion (guilt, fear of losing friends); strong empathic listening that validates Jordan's feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, sustained focus on shoplifting and concrete behavior change, repeatedly steering conversation toward trigger mapping, refusal lines, exits, and near-term practice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly partner-based—clinician solicits Jordan's input at each step, adapts to his requests, and offers suggestions rather than orders, sharing decision-making and pacing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission for interventions and role-play, and reinforcing that Jordan decides what to try and practice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits values and some self-motivated statements (e.g., valuing freedom) and links them to action, but often supplies language and could further draw out Jordan's own reasons, past successes, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative—asks to work together, lets Jordan choose targets and rehearsal style, and co-constructs the plan in a power-sharing manner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and uses role-play to strengthen readiness, but misses deeper elicitation of commitment and importance/confidence scaling and sometimes offers language before Jordan generates it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects sustain talk with double-sided reflections and practical strategies, but could more strategically weaken sustain talk by exploring past successes resisting peers and amplifying consequences.
