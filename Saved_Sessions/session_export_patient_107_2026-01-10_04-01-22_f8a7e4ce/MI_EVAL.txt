# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental stance with frequent reflective validation, but offers relatively few explicit affirmations or normalizing statements of ambivalence to fully highlight strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate understanding with timely reflections that capture ambivalence and values, though could deepen exploration of emotional meaning and use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on coffee reduction and systematically guides the conversation toward a concrete, measurable one-week experiment without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co-constructs the plan by soliciting the client's ideas and following his lead, offering suggestions only after eliciting his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice by framing the plan as a voluntary, time-limited test and repeatedly inviting the client's decisions, preserving agency throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client's own reasons and goals for change and generates actionable ideas, but could probe deeper into intrinsic values and amplify change talk toward commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint exploration and clearly shares power by asking the client to choose strategies and define success criteria.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits specific change talk and practical steps (decaf, tracking), yet could better consolidate and amplify that language into explicit commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation, but seldom uses double-sided reflections or reframing to actively soften sustain talk or highlight longer-term costs.
