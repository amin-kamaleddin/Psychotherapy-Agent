# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeated, explicit nonjudgmental statements (“you’re accepted wherever you are,” “you’re accepted exactly where you are”) and empathic restatements after repeated resistance reduced confrontation and conveyed unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective‑taking using the patient’s language (“reading pack,” “the only reason I’m here”), capturing both content and emotional tone (frustration, protective).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear focus on smoking and moved toward concrete behaviour steps (smoke outside, cut down, gum/vape, book check‑up). Early repetitive open questions delayed tighter focus on a single next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed as joint problem‑solving (“Let’s figure this out together”), invited Richard’s choices, and offered help rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly respected Richard’s agency and limits (“don’t try to push me”), used nonpressuring language and supported options Richard was willing to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Richard’s reasons and concrete ideas for change (relational motivation, harm‑reduction steps) but could probe more for intrinsic motives and confidence (importance/confidence scaling).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shared power and invited the patient’s expertise in planning (“which of those steps feels most doable… how would you like me to help?”), co‑constructing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created opportunities for preparatory change talk and elicited multiple change statements (calmer mornings, smoke outside) but did not consistently amplify or strategically reflect those statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and rolled with resistance, redirected toward acceptable small steps (harm‑reduction). Could further soften sustain talk with double‑sided reflections and selective summaries to shift momentum toward change.
