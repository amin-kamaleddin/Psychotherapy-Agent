### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** Strong empathic listening and accurate reflections of fear and exhaustion with safety checks and context, though could deepen reflections of affect, isolation, and meaning before moving to problem‑solving.

### Direction

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, honoring skepticism about therapy, asking patient preference, and avoiding pressure while offering help in requested ways.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicited practical commitments (email, log, role‑play) but did not sufficiently draw out the patient’s internal motivations, values, or meaning that would deepen change talk.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some action‑oriented movement occurred (agreement to bring materials, symptom log), yet the clinician largely led these steps instead of systematically eliciting and amplifying the patient’s own reasons and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
