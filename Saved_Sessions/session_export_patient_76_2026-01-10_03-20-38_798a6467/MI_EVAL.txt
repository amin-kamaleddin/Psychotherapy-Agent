# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing the client’s struggle with warm, validating language throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate empathic listening and perspective-taking, reflecting both emotional and somatic experience (aches, knot, tension) reliably.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on self-harm and immediate safety, guiding the session toward a specific, actionable plan (calling roommate to sit quietly).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the safety plan by soliciting preferences at every step and treating the client as the expert, demonstrating genuine collaborative work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and control, avoided coercive language, and affirmed that planning to reach out does not equal failure, supporting client agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client’s motives and desired outcomes (getting through class, not hiding wounds) but did not systematically deepen or amplify change talk into confidence or commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a shared effort, offered options and invited the client's input, which kept power and decision-making with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced small change talk and concrete steps, but missed opportunities to probe desire, ability, reasons, and to elicit a specific commitment or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided colluding with sustain talk and used double-sided reflections, though could more actively explore costs of cutting and evoke discrepancies to soften sustain talk further.
