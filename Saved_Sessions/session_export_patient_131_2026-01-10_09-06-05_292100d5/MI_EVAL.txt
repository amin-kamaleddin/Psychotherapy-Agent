# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (e.g., opening reflection, frequent affirmations, normalising nervousness), using strengths‑focused, respectful language that avoids blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and perspective‑taking with accurate summaries and validation, though reflections seldom probe deeper into the emotional texture (shame, fear, remorse) to increase empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on target behaviors and concrete steps (calling Aunty, drafting scripts/texts, rehearsal, barrier management), with each intervention moving toward implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative: co‑creates wording, rehearses role‑plays together, and develops shared contingency plans while inviting the client to shape language and decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms client choice and equips Wiremu to decide (call vs text, confidence/readiness ratings, scripts as suggestions), reinforcing ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation and links actions to values (getting kids back), using scaling and implementation intentions, but could more often deepen intrinsic motives and summarize change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership by asking preferences, co‑creating scripts, inviting practice, and sharing power rather than prescribing, which supports client autonomy and engagement.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk through scaling, planning, and praise and translates intentions into concrete steps, but would benefit from more frequent reflection and amplification of explicit change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalises and contains barriers, and offers coping strategies; could use more double‑sided reflections to gently pivot ambivalence toward change.
