# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, using affirming language and practical help throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately reflecting confusion and safety concerns; could deepen emotional reflection and probe underlying feelings or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on understanding and safely initiating the medication with concrete summaries and a practical safety plan and minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by soliciting Mai’s input, co-constructing next steps, offering role-play and phone support, and incorporating her needs into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient’s control and offered choices (wait vs start, call or role-play), reinforcing ownership of decisions throughout the encounter.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited preparatory reasons and planning (scripts, role-play) but did not deeply probe intrinsic motivators or elicit confidence/commitment language to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as a shared endeavor, invited Mai to shape the process, and repeatedly deferred to her preferences when planning next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created multiple opportunities for preparatory change talk and concrete planning, but missed chances to amplify and elicit stronger commitment or confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not reinforce sustain talk, offering options and information; could more often use double-sided reflections and summaries to gently reframe ambivalence.
