# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validates Sofia’s distress, normalizing protective behavior without blame. Tone is nonjudgmental and supportive, which keeps the teen engaged.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that mirror Sofia’s experience and feelings. Reflections capture both content and function of behavior and are followed by invitational questions that show understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on increasing school attendance and concrete small steps. Questions and interventions consistently steer toward actionable moves (texting a friend, choosing a bail line, first-period attendance).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as an equal partnership with joint problem-solving and option offering rather than directives. The clinician defers to Sofia’s judgment and repeatedly invites her to choose or tweak wording and plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and avoids pressure, framing actions as optional and reflecting Sofia’s hesitance without contradiction. Offers and plans reinforce Sofia’s control (e.g., 'not committing to forever,' picking text wording).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Sofia’s reasons, strengths, and small steps (hopes for reconnection, bail planning), drawing out internal motivation. Could deepen evocation by exploring values, consequences of avoidance, and using scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the work as collaborative and highlights Sofia’s expertise, inviting her preferences and co-constructing plans. Offers to craft messages together and asks which option feels most like her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces multiple pieces of preparatory change talk and encourages small, doable steps. Falls short of consistently amplifying or eliciting stronger commitment language or using confidence/importance scaling to develop ability and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids reinforcing it, using double-sided reflections to validate ambivalence. Misses some opportunities to actively pivot sustain talk toward change by summarizing costs of avoidance or eliciting counterarguments.
