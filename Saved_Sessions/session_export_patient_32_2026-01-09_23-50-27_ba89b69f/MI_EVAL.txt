# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, validated strengths, and avoided judgmental language. Tone was respectful and normalizing, supporting the client's perspective throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective summaries and understanding of practical concerns. Could deepen emotional reflections (e.g., explore loneliness) and vary phrasing to avoid sounding formulaic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on avoiding another DUI and guided the session to concrete, implementable steps. Each turn advanced a practical plan without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly joint, eliciting the client's ideas first and refining them together. Decisions were co-constructed and the client's preferences drove the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice by offering options and asking which felt most doable, preserving the client's sense of control. Suggestions were framed as optional supports rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical motivations and preparatory steps tied to protecting license and job. Could more directly evoke deeper intrinsic values and stronger commitment language (e.g., confidence/importance scaling or a verbal commitment).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited the client's input and framed problem-solving as a shared effort. The clinician refined the client's ideas collaboratively and respected his expertise about his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified specific change talk, producing concrete preparatory and commitment language (cash, app default, out-by-11 rule). The clinician shaped and reinforced these statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used reflective, double-sided framing to acknowledge sustain talk without reinforcing it. Could more actively explore the costs of sustain talk in the client's own words or use strategic reflections to pivot further toward change.
