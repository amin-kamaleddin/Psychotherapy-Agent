# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and explicitly states acceptance, creating a safe space for honest disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and tracks emotional tone throughout, which facilitates deeper disclosure and rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on reducing alcohol-related risk and moves logically from ambivalence to a specific, time-limited plan with useful summaries and checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—solicits the patient's ideas, uses his language, and co-constructs the plan while checking summaries and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient's choice, avoids pressure, and frames steps as experiments to respect and strengthen autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own values, reasons, and memories of past supports, producing concrete self-motivational statements and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert, invites choice, and co-develops realistic strategies drawn from the patient's own suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits desire, reasons, and ability statements and some commitment-like language, but could better amplify strong change statements and probe confidence/importance to build firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and skillfully redirects sustain talk toward practical alternatives, though more double-sided reflections and explicit naming of both sides would further soften sustain statements.
