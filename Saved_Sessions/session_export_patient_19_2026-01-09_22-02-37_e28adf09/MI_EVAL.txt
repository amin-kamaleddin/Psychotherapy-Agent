# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized shame and ambivalence, and affirmed the client's experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking captured ambivalence and key feelings, though deeper, more complex reflections of guarded humor and shame would further deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear behavioral focus on gambling risk-reduction, repeatedly brought conversation back to concrete target behaviors, and helped form a specific first-step plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interactions were framed as joint problem-solving with the clinician helping to operationalize strategies and inviting the client to co-design wording and scheduling without an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly affirmed choice, offered options for the client to pick from, checked preferences for support, and honored the client's requests, supporting ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively drew out motivations and prior successful tactics and linked them to steps, but could more deliberately evoke personal values and use importance/confidence scaling to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician shared power, solicited the client's expertise, and offered to help set up plans collaboratively, demonstrating equal partnership throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Evocative questions and reinforcement elicited concrete change language and plans, though the clinician could better amplify desire/importance/confidence and summarize change talk to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and acknowledged ambivalence but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections, reframes, and explicit elicitation of reasons against change to soften sustain talk.
