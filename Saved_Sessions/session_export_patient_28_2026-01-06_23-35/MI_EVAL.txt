# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Dante’s experience with a nonjudgmental tone, validating fatigue and skepticism throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and matched Dante’s ambivalence, but missed opportunities to deepen emotional nuance by exploring the felt impact and meaning of past exercise.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on re-establishing physical activity and moved the conversation from barriers to a concrete, measurable plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint project, asked permission, checked preferences, and co-constructed the plan while offering supportive follow-up and wording help.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and avoided coercion by offering options, asking permission, and framing the experiment as Dante’s decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited past success and preferences and drew some motivation into planning, but often supplied scripts rather than using more open questions to elicit values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—asked permission and offered choices—but sometimes shifted control back by providing prescriptive scripts instead of eliciting more of Dante’s own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and translated it into concrete plans, though there were missed chances to amplify Dante’s own reasons and strengths more strategically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk via normalization, low-intensity fallbacks, and reframing misses as data, but used few double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
