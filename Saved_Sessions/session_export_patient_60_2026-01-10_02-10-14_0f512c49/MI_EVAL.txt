# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, offering supportive options and affirming the client's priorities without blame. Tone and language demonstrate strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and accurately labels frustration and priorities. Could deepen exploration of underlying emotions and values rather than moving quickly into solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on preparing for the conduct meeting with concrete, behavior-linked strategies. Resists tangents and consistently guides toward relevant actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the client in co-constructing language and repeatedly checks preferences, modeling joint problem-solving. Offers multiple assists and adapts to the client's chosen approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices and respects the client's decisions, framing recommendations as options rather than directives. Follows the client's lead even when advising otherwise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some effort to elicit goals and preferred outcomes but then shifts into advising and scripting. Limited elicitation of internal motivations, values, or discrepancy to fuel change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly orients the work as a partnership from the outset, inviting input and adapting phrasing based on the client's wishes. Demonstrates strong power-sharing throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to draw out change-relevant language about housing, classes, and sanctions. However, the clinician relies more on advising and scripting than open questions to elicit the client's own reasons or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Often rolls with sustain talk and occasionally redirects, but rehearsal of deflecting language risks reinforcing minimization. Lacks consistent use of double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to reduce sustain talk.
