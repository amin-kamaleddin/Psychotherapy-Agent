# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming throughout the session, validating Wiremu’s efforts and normalizing difficulty; no blaming or minimizing language was used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and some reflective statements about priorities and ambivalence, but missed opportunities to name deeper emotions (shame, fear) and use more complex feeling reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on target behaviours (housing, avoiding Tristan, Stopping Violence) and repeatedly steered the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps, culminating in a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative: the clinician and Wiremu co-created the script, safety plan, and logistics while consistently seeking Wiremu’s input and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Wiremu’s sense of choice by offering options, trial approaches, and avoiding pressuring language, reinforcing that decisions were his to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Wiremu’s motivations and linked them to actions, but could have further amplified intrinsic reasons (e.g., asking for summaries, confidence/importance ratings) before moving to planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a genuine partnership stance—inviting choices, offering roleplay options, and jointly constructing details while respecting Wiremu’s expertise about his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited, reinforced, and amplified change talk and translated it into specific commitments (trial stay, scripted call, safety steps), fostering actionable planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and offered compromises (e.g., trial week) without confronting, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections to explicitly soften ambivalence further.
