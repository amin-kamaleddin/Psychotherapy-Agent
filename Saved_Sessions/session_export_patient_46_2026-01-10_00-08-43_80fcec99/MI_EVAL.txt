# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard from the opening through thank-yous, maintaining a nonjudgmental, supportive tone; adolescent-appropriate acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured frustration and social motives, but some reflections were surface-level and missed deeper feelings like defensiveness or belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on underage drinking and harm reduction, tying questions, suggestions, and role-plays to concrete, time-bound plans without losing client-centeredness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative with co-created plans, permission-seeking, role-play, and tailoring of scripts, positioning the clinician as a teammate rather than sole expert.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed options, used readiness/confidence rulers, and invited Ethan to choose what to try, avoiding coercion and reinforcing ownership of any change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited internal motivations and alternatives and used scaling to draw out a concrete experiment, but could further deepen values, identity, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was collaborative with permission and client choice and included role-play, though offering specific directives at times (e.g., a 3-drink limit) reduced client-generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and encouraged change talk via questions, planning, and rulers, but could more often reflect/amplify change statements and probe for desire, reasons, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk but missed opportunities to actively soften it using double-sided reflections, reframing, or selective emphasis of change talk before offering prescriptive steps.
