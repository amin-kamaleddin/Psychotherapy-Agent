# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect via validating reflections and affirmations, with a supportive, nonjudgmental tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent understanding through reflective listening and paraphrase, tracking affect and somatic cues without imposing interpretation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on imminent self-harm risk and safety planning, asking about intent/means/supports and keeping interventions tightly directed to harm reduction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as an equal partner by inviting input, tailoring options, and sharing tasks (e.g., scripting messages, offering to stay), supporting shared problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely supports autonomy with permission-seeking and multiple explicit choices, avoiding coercion and affirming the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some intrinsic motivation and action (scaling, coping choices, texting), but misses deeper exploration of values, hopes, and confidence to strengthen internal motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as collaborative, asking permission, offering options, and deferring to the patient's preferences while co-constructing the safety plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk and commitment behaviors (grounding, texting, removing items) but could more actively amplify reasons for change and explore values to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward safety with validations and small-step alternatives, though more double-sided reflections and cost exploration could further soften sustain talk.
