# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating throughout the session with frequent affirmations that reinforced acceptance rather than shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and acknowledges ambivalence and worry, but sometimes shifts quickly to problem‑solving, limiting deeper emotional reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on alcohol use and safety/risk reduction, repeatedly returning to concrete goals and implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co‑constructed with repeated invitations for input, tailoring of supports, and decisions driven by the patient’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice with options, consent checks, and normalization of slips, avoiding coercive language and preserving ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation tied to the fall and son’s concerns and draws out a specific plan, but could use more evocative open and scaling questions to deepen intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by offering choices, asking permission before moving forward, and co‑creating the plan with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and reinforces change talk (goal setting, diary, concrete steps), though could amplify and probe change statements and assess commitment strength.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk but does little active softening (e.g., double‑sided reflections or explicitly acknowledging both sides) to reduce minimizations.
