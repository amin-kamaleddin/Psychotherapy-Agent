# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and normalized Malik's experience while affirming his competence; no blaming or moralizing language was used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking captured ambivalence and validated Malik's feelings; could use more emotion-focused or complex reflections to deepen exploration of frustration, shame, and social identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on Malik's cannabis use and steered the conversation toward specific, achievable actions, resulting in a concrete behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with iterative check-ins and co-creation of tactics, and the clinician avoided a directive expert stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Malik's choice and control by framing decisions as his and validating partial change, fostering ownership without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Malik's motivations and resources (past success, supports) but could further elicit values, identity-based reasons, and commitment/confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician positioned Malik as the expert and repeatedly invited his input, co-constructing realistic steps and demonstrating strong power-sharing throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced concrete change talk and past successes, producing actionable ideas; missed explicit commitment language and confidence scaling to consolidate intentions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by using double-sided reflections and redirecting toward alternatives; could more gently explore costs of continued use to further balance sustain talk.
