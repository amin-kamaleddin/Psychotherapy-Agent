# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, validates the client’s dual concerns and offers options and role-play without blaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys understanding of ambivalence and contains emotions through coaching, but uses few deeper or complex reflections and moves quickly into problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on getting the flatmate to move out with deadline setting, a 5-step plan, scripting, and consistent refocusing to behavioral goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages the client in role-play and choice of tasks, creating a team-like rehearsal, but frequently supplies the script and steps which shifts power toward the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice by offering options, asking preferences (tone, deadline, check-in), and avoiding coercive language while helping refine the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete commitments and action (deadline, script, check-ins) but leans toward tactical direction and misses deeper elicitation of values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Contains collaborative moments and invitations, yet the session is largely clinician-led with provided plans and scripts, reducing true co-construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change-related commitments through deadline setting and practice, though it could better amplify the client’s own language about desire, ability, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively validates fear then pivots to concrete steps and refuses bargaining, but detailed legal information could raise anxiety and would benefit from more double-sided reflections.
