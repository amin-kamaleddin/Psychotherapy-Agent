# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validating goals and offering practical support without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, supportive reflections and acknowledged frustrations, but could deepen emotional exploration (e.g., the personal meaning of body confidence) for a higher empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on restarting activity with concrete action steps (scheduling, scripts, backups) and avoided unrelated tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan by soliciting and implementing the patient’s preferences, demonstrating genuine two-way teamwork rather than directive instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed offers as optional, sought consent before interventions, and respected choices (e.g., not signing up immediately), reinforcing patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values and converted them into steps but leaned more on pragmatic planning than extended exploration of intrinsic motives and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance throughout, treated the patient as the expert, and shared decision-making on scripts, timing, and rehearsal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, translated desire into specific commitments (dates/times), and used rehearsal to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward solutions with alternatives and scripting, but could more explicitly use MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to further weaken sustain talk.
