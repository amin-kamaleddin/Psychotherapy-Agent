# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician displays consistent unconditional positive regard—normalizing, affirming limits, and avoiding judgment throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and acknowledgement of emotions/practical worries, but missed opportunities to probe deeper into the meaning of 'fitting in' and emotional ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on social alcohol use with interventions directly tied to concrete behaviors and a stepwise plan to change them.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance—invites the client to choose strategies, role-plays when requested, and jointly develops practical steps that reflect the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical reasons and solutions from the client, but could deepen motivation by linking to values, using scaling questions, and eliciting explicit commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed with the client; the clinician treats the client as the expert on her life and adapts plans to her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and offers behavioral options, but does not consistently amplify statements into confidence/commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to manageable strategies, though more double-sided reflections could further acknowledge competing desires while reinforcing change.
