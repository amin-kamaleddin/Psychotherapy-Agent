# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard with validations and nonjudgmental language, honoring Malik's reasons for attending and maintaining warmth throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that showed understanding of Malik's ambivalence, but missed some opportunities for deeper empathic exploration of adolescent identity and frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on cannabis use and risk-reduction goals, asking concrete implementation questions and keeping the conversation action-oriented without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in genuine collaborative problem-solving by offering options, co-creating language and plans, and adapting the plan to Malik's priorities and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly affirmed Malik's right to choose, repeatedly offered options rather than directives, and avoided pressuring him to commit beyond his comfort level.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motives and used scaling to draw out change signals, though could have probed more for intrinsic values and longer-term goals to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Malik as an expert in his own life, shared power by asking permission and following his preferences, and ensured decisions reflected his input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and strengthened some change talk with targeted questions, scaling, and rehearsals, but could have more consistently amplified and built momentum on Malik's change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double-sided reflections and harm-reduction strategies to redirect the conversation, though could more proactively elicit discrepancy to reduce sustain talk further.
