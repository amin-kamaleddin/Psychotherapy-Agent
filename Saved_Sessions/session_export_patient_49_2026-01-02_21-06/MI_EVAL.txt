# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed respect and positive regard (multiple affirmations: “That makes sense,” “well done,” “I’m proud of this step”). No judgment or minimizing; warm and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries of triggers, past attempts, and ambivalence; normalized uncertainty. Reflections stayed mostly content-level — could probe deeper emotional nuance (stress, social pressure, “not sure I’ll stick with it”).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on smoking reduction: agenda-setting, readiness ruler, selection of a concrete plan, and scheduled follow-up. Session moved logically without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited patient preference and permission, co-constructed the plan, and allowed the patient to choose the target behavior (no smoking at home).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Honored patient choice and ambivalence, offered options rather than directives, and framed actions as the patient’s decision; no coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited patient’s reasons, past strategies, and readiness (0–10 scale) and used them to form a plan. Could have further elicited values/reasons (e.g., “what would be better if…”) to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated patient as expert on her life; used collaborative language and shared decision-making (patient selected tactic, if‑then plan, follow-up).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created and reinforced opportunities for change talk (readiness ruler, past success, concrete plan) and affirmed the plan. Missed chances to amplify and deepen change language (importance/confidence elicitation, elaboration on why change matters).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, normalized ambivalence, and reframed options. Could improve use of double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to tip balance further toward change.
