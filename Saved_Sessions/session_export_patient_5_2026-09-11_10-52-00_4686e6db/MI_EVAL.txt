# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used affirming, nonjudgmental language and normalized uncertainty, validating the patient's efforts and concerns. Demonstrated unconditional positive regard across turns without labeling or dismissal.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, repeatedly summarizing the patient's priorities and emotions. Reflections tracked her cautious, thorough stance and practical needs effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on safe timing and management of the medication interaction and steered toward actionable next steps. Repeatedly negotiated immediate tasks that advanced the safety plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with frequent invitations for the patient to set priorities and choose formats. Co-created the plan and treated the patient as an equal partner in decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options and solicited the patient's preferences, reinforcing choice and control. Avoided directive language and encouraged confirmation with the pharmacist to support ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited priorities and practical commitments that leaned toward safety, pulling out actionable motivation. Could deepen evocation by probing underlying reasons, confidence, and past experiences more often.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative throughout and repeatedly deferred to the patient's expertise about what would be useful. Shared power by asking which tasks to draft and prioritize first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Facilitated concrete change talk and commitments through offers of small steps and choices that led to a safety plan. To reach top score, could use more open invitations to have the patient verbalize her reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing hesitation and redirected ambivalence toward safety planning, which limited sustain talk. Could more actively use double-sided reflections or strategic summarizing to explicitly soften sustain language.
