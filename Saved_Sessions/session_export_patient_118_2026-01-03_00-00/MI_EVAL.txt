# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates respect and avoids judgment (e.g., “I won’t push,” honoring no‑call/no family note). Creates a safe, non‑shaming atmosphere; language sometimes felt formulaic/repetitive rather than individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective‑taking (accurate summaries: teeth, embarrassment, meds/lecture fatigue). Reflections are timely and keep patient engaged; missed opportunities to probe deeper emotion or meaning behind resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept encounter focused on smoking relative to dental goal and steered toward small, practical options. Could have used more strategic evocation to deepen and consolidate change momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked with patient to identify a mutually acceptable, low‑burden step (brush after meals). Interaction felt cooperative; clinician occasionally reverted to repetitive phrasing rather than building on patient’s wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, explicit autonomy support throughout (offers choices, honors preferences, “you’re in charge of next moves”). Clear strength of the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some successful evocation (importance scaling, offering options) but inconsistent follow‑up on change language and limited amplification of the patient’s own reasons/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance maintained; clinician partnered on practical solutions and respected limits. Partnership could be strengthened by deeper, individualized reflections.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete, small change talk (3/10 importance; commitment to brushing after meals) but did not systematically explore or strengthen reasons, confidence, or implementation details.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce sustain talk; offered reversible, low‑pressure options and honored skepticism about meds. Could have used double‑sided reflections or selective reinforcement to more actively tip ambivalence toward change.
