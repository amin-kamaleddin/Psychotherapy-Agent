# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, frequently normalizing and affirming the patient's feelings and experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with effective double-sided reflections and checks after role-play that validated the patient's emotions and reduced escalation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing snapping and rehearsing specific coping strategies, continually tying the conversation back to the practical behavioral goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt genuinely collaborative—plans were co-created, role-play was negotiated, and the clinician used language that invited the patient's input and preferences at each step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported the patient's sense of choice by offering multiple options, accepting hesitancy, and avoiding pressure while asking which steps felt most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own motivations and solutions through targeted questions, use of past-success examples, confidence ratings, and prompting the patient to define concrete steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the patient as the expert on their experience and choices, sharing power by asking which options and role-plays to pursue.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and shaped change talk with questions about desired change, past successes, specific scripts, and confidence ratings, and reinforced the patient's stated plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward low-risk solutions, but could have used stronger double-sided reflections and deeper validation of system-level grievances to further soften sustain talk.
