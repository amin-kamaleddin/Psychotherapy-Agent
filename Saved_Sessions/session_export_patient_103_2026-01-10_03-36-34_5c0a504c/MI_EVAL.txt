# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful—validated the patient's frustration, apologized, and offered practical options without labeling or dismissing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid, accurate empathic listening with brief reflections and check-ins, but missed deeper, complex reflections exploring the intensity or meaning of the anger.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, practical focus on immediate de-escalation and resolving the wait-time issue, but did not connect this work to broader long-term goals for anger management.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—solicited preferences, followed the patient's lead, and co-constructed messages and next steps in a joint problem-solving manner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice and control by offering multiple options, asking preferences, and empowering the patient to decide and act without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited immediate procedural choices and feelings but did not probe intrinsic motivations, values, or past successes to draw out internal reasons for longer-term change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt clearly client-led with the clinician acting as a collaborative partner—offering choices, acknowledging limits, and co-creating language and plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made few efforts to elicit or amplify change talk; interventions were pragmatic and immediate rather than asking about desired outcomes, confidence, or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided amplifying sustain talk and redirected toward coping and problem-solving, though could have used double-sided reflections to further shift focus toward change.
