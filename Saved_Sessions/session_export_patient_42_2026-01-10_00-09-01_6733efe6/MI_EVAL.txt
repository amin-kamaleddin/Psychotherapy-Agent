# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizing Elise’s feelings and avoiding blame. Warm, patient-centered language and repeated validations likely reduced defensiveness and supported engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing relational loss and ambivalence. Could be strengthened by deeper emotional reflections or affect-labeling to explore the meaning of belonging more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on quitting in social situations with behavior-specific questions and planning. Interventions repeatedly return to actionable steps and a time-bound plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session feels cooperative with invitations to rehearse and tailored options for context. At times the clinician supplies many ready-made lines and step-by-step tips, which slightly shifts the balance toward expert-driven guidance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering options, asking permission, and emphasizing Elise’s decisions. Advice is offered nonpressuring and optional, reinforcing her control over change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation and a self-chosen, concrete plan with rehearsal and a commitment. Some elicitation relied on offering options rather than drawing out more of Elise’s own alternatives, reasons, and confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance by offering choices and checking preferences, communicating that Elise’s judgment matters. Occasional dominance of clinician-generated ideas (many scripts) slightly undermines full co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and cultivates concrete change talk and commitment, producing a specific plan and schedule. Missed opportunities to amplify Elise’s own change statements and to probe confidence and underlying reasons more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and the loss of social connection but moves quickly to offering solutions and scripts. Would benefit from strategic MI techniques (double-sided reflections, selective reinforcement) to more subtly soften sustain talk.
