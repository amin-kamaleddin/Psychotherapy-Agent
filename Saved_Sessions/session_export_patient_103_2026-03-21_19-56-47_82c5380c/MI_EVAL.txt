### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on addressing the appointment issue with concrete action steps (emails, checklist, next steps), though balancing emotion‑regulation work versus escalation could have been stronger.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative with co‑construction of messages and frequent checks for approval, but the clinician sometimes took the lead in composing rather than eliciting more of Nabil's own wording first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before drafting, and clearly reinforcing that sending/action remained the client's decision.

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a collaborator—asking tone preferences and inviting review—but at times led the wording and could have invited more client‑led problem‑solving before drafting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Offered options early but did not elicit desire, ability, reasons, or need for changing emotional reactions; emphasis stayed on external escalation rather than eliciting personal change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
