# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes difficulties, and avoids blame, creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate reflections and perspective‑taking that capture Dante's feelings and barriers, which the patient repeatedly affirms.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity and moves the conversation toward a specific, measurable step (one 20‑minute lunch walk).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as a joint exploration by inviting brainstorming, soliciting Dante's input, and co‑constructing realistic options rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirms Dante's control and choice, offers options instead of directives, and emphasizes his freedom to decide which strategies to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Dante's reasons and ideas for change (energy, kids, short walks) but could probe deeper into internal motivators and elicit stronger self‑motivational statements or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power by asking which ideas feel realistic and inviting Dante to choose among options, demonstrating strong collaborative decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Systematically elicits and reinforces change talk and concrete steps, but would benefit from stronger amplification of commitment/confidence (e.g., scaling questions, asking for a personal summary).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and validates sustain talk without judgment, yet often mirrors resistance rather than using double‑sided reflections or reframes to more forcefully tip toward change.
