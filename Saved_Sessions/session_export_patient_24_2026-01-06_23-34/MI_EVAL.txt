# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard, normalizes slips, and maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries, but could deepen emotional reflections (e.g., embarrassment, frustration) to better validate feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol use linked to marathon training and produces a specific, measurable first-step plan with a follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session feels joint and incorporates patient choices, though the clinician sometimes offered detailed, clinician-driven solutions before eliciting the patient's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, framing suggestions as optional, and reinforcing the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation (readiness ruler, choices) but often shifts to advice instead of using evocative questions to draw out values and strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and co-creative (patient-selected tracking and alcohol-free days), yet at times the clinician assumed an expert role with ready-made scripts and app recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited change talk (surprise at drinking, willingness to track, alcohol-free days) but did not consistently amplify or explore reasons, desire, ability, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and pragmatically redirected sustain talk with harm-reduction and normalization, but could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly surface ambivalence.
