# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental opening and repeated affirmations conveyed respect and practical support, but the stance was often task-focused and lacked explicit normalization of ambivalence or fuller unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and paraphrasing kept rapport and summarized Jason's practical aims, but the clinician missed opportunities to probe deeper emotions (frustration, fear, embarrassment) behind his defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, appropriate focus on clearing the conduct record, but it did not connect that target to longer-term behavior change or academic/safety goals to promote reduced risky drinking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: the clinician co-created scripts, timelines, and emails, asked permission to fill in details, and repeatedly checked Jason's preferences, operating as a helper rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, honored Jason's preferences, and set ethical boundaries (refusing to craft misleading statements) while providing alternative, acceptable options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effective evocation of procedural motivations to avoid a record, but minimal elicitation of intrinsic motives, values, or commitment related to changing drinking behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong invitations for input and shared decision-making in drafting materials, though at times clinician-provided templates shifted some power toward the clinician's tools rather than explicitly foregrounding Jason's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to elicit desire, ability, reasons, need, or commitment to change drinking; the conversation focused on procedural solutions without developing change talk or exploring discrepancy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The clinician largely accommodated sustain talk by concentrating on avoiding sanctions and drafting defenses, with only one boundary against misleading statements and few attempts to reframe or gently contrast sustain talk.
