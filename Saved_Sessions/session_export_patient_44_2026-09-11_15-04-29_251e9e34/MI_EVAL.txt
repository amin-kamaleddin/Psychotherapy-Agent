# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and affirms strengths and progress without judgment, treating setbacks as understandable and supportive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking about gains and ambivalence, but could deepen emotional exploration and use more complex reflections to capture underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on exercising/consistency and repeatedly guides the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps and micro-behaviors.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in true two-way problem-solving, integrates the patient's past successes and preferences, and lets the client's ideas shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking the patient to pick what feels doable, and framing steps as suggestions rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the patient's own motivations, resources, and past successes, building motivation from within rather than imposing reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert, shares decision-making, and co-constructs plans with repeated invitations for the patient to choose the preferred steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reflects, and amplifies change talk, converting tentative language into specific commitments and feasible plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk without confrontation and offers practical alternatives, but would benefit from more strategic softening (e.g., double-sided reflections or selective amplification of change talk).
