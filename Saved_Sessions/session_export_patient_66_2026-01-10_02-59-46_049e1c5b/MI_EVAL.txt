# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth with validating reflections and normalizing language. Could add an explicit normalization of relapse/struggle to make acceptance even more explicit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used timely, accurate reflections and perspective-taking that helped Kareem feel understood and validated. Missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional drivers or link smoking to values/role (e.g., family, identity).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on cutting down/quitting and progressed from assessment to concrete, measurable action planning. Questions and suggestions stayed tightly linked to the target behavior and a one-week test.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a true collaborative problem-solving partnership, soliciting Kareem's ideas and jointly refining the plan (alarm wording, Mark script, one-pager). The clinician rarely adopted an expert-only stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by framing efforts as experiments, asking permission before advice, and offering options rather than directives. Language reinforced Kareem's control over what he would try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited prior successes, readiness/confidence scales, and practical reasons to try, which evoked self-efficacy and concrete ideas. Could strengthen by exploring deeper intrinsic motivators (values, family, health) with more 'why' questioning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Kareem's input, co-created the plan, and checked permission for wording/resources, demonstrating strong power-sharing. This fostered clear client influence over decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk using readiness rulers and a small, testable plan, converting ideas into specific steps. Could further elicit reasons for change and stronger commitment language tied to personal values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and skillfully redirected sustain talk toward manageable experimentation and neutral reframing of slips. More explicit early double-sided reflections could have softened sustain talk and highlighted discrepancy sooner.
