# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and avoids judgment throughout the session, supporting the client's reasons and preferences. Maintains a warm, noncritical stance while offering support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and accurate paraphrasing of content and affect, capturing key concerns and ideas. Could deepen emotional empathy by probing the intensity/meaning of fears about social loss and reflecting underlying needs for belonging more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, specific focus on smoking cessation via a one-hour smoke-free hangout and consistently steers toward actionable steps. Moves discussion from options to a concrete plan with momentum toward implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative with joint problem-solving, asking permission, and continuously soliciting the client's wording and preferences. Suggestions are offered as optional supports and Elise's ideas drive the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering multiple options, checking what the client wants to work on, and avoiding pressuring language. Reinforces the client's ownership of decisions and tailors wording to fit her voice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivation and concrete change statements (e.g., pride, readiness, plans) and asks evocative questions about what would make the experiment feel connecting. Misses some opportunities to probe deeper values, past successes, or use scaling to strengthen motivation and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters an equal, collaborative stance with language that invites co-creation and defers to the client's expertise about her social world. Offers choices and checks preferences repeatedly to shape the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and reinforces change talk, obtains concrete plans and willingness language, and amplifies incremental readiness. Could more explicitly elicit importance/confidence/commitment and summarize change statements to convert them into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and skillfully redirects toward small experiments and alternative bonding activities, using implicit double-sided reflections and reframes. Would benefit from more explicit softening techniques (clear double-sided reflections, reframes, and normalization) to acknowledge sustain talk while pivoting to change.
