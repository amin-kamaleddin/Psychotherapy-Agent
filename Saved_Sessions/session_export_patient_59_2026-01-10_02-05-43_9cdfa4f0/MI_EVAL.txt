# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, using affirmations and avoiding blame. Offers help in a warm, respectful way that validates Jamal's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing Jamal's worries and priorities. Could deepen connection with more complex feeling reflections to probe emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on target behaviors (legal requirements, PO/mom communication, staying in school) and practical next steps. The session stays on track and repeatedly returns to actionable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language, invites Jamal to choose scripts, and rehearses with him, supporting a shared working alliance. At times a structured list of steps makes the clinician more of an information-provider than an equal co-explorer.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports Jamal's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing help rather than commands. Reinforces Jamal's ownership of decisions and scripted statements.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Begins by eliciting concerns and values but then shifts to procedural solutions and rehearsing statements rather than systematically drawing out intrinsic motivations. Would benefit from more targeted questions to elicit DARN-C language and amplify pro-change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely positions the work as collaborative, inviting input and practicing together, which supports alliance. Occasional expert-style information delivery limits fuller co-creation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits limited change talk focused on short-term compliance (finishing requirements, keeping it simple) and supports pragmatic commitments. Does not consistently explore or amplify Jamal's intrinsic reasons for longer-term behavior change or ask importance/confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids arguing with minimizations, normalizes concerns, and redirects toward risk reduction and options. Could further strengthen by using more double-sided reflections to highlight discrepancy between values and sustain talk.
