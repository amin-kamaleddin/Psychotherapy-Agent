# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with normalization, reflective summaries, and affirmations; avoided shaming and responded to concerns with practical options.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate, timely summaries; reflections tended to remain surface-level and missed deeper emotional meaning (e.g., feelings about the dentist's comment).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking reduction throughout, moving logically from context and readiness to barriers, options, and a concrete implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed—offered multiple options, invited the patient's choices, co-developed scripts, and rehearsed preferred lines with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's autonomy by offering options, asking permission before intervening, and emphasizing choice and the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation using open questions and a readiness ruler and secured a concrete trial, but could more explicitly link reasons to values and elicit confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership with shared power and patient-guided decisions; clinician invited preferences at each step and collaborated on the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (readiness ruler, probing, praise for a chosen step) but could better amplify spontaneous change language and draw out ability/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and responded pragmatically to doubts about gum and cost, minimizing sustain talk; missed an opportunity for double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
