# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently expressed nonjudgmental support and affirmed Laura's values and efforts, normalizing ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate understanding and reflective summaries of Laura's perspective and worries, but missed opportunities for deeper, emotion-focused reflections exploring the meaning of her values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, specific behavioral focus (morning yoga) and tied every intervention to concrete planning and implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed with mutual problem-solving; clinician drafted texts and reminders while inviting Laura's edits and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice, offered options, and avoided pressuring language, reinforcing Laura's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation and confidence with open questions and scaling, though could have probed more for intrinsic motivators tied to Laura's core values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Laura as the expert on her life, offering choices and inviting her final wording and decisions, demonstrating strong power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified concrete change talk and commitment through questions and reinforcement, but could have more explicitly linked her values to reasons and plans for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk while offering fallback plans to reduce its pull, though additional double-sided reflections and exploration of lapse triggers would strengthen this further.
