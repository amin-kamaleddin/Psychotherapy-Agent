PSYCHIATRIST: Thank you — that’s an important question. Citalopram is an SSRI used for depression and some anxiety disorders. Common side effects: nausea, headache, sleep changes, dry mouth, sweating, and sexual side effects; high doses can prolong the QT interval. Citalopram and phenelzine (an MAOI) should NOT be used together — the combo risks life‑threatening serotonin syndrome and other problems. You need a washout period (typically ≥14 days) after stopping an MAOI before starting citalopram. Call your prescriber or pharmacist now; seek urgent care if you have fever, rapid heartbeat, severe agitation, stiffness, or vomiting.
