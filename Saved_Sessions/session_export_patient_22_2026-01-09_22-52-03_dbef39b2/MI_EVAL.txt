# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveyed warm, nonjudgmental positive regard with frequent affirmations and normalizing language that validated Camila’s strengths and reduced shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured ambivalence and practical concerns, but the clinician could have deepened empathic attunement by naming feelings like embarrassment or social anxiety more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, sustained focus on reducing drinking and progressed logically from values and consequences to concrete options and an actionable experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician co‑constructed the plan with Camila, offered tentative suggestions, and left final decisions and steps to her, reflecting genuine teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multiple options were offered and Camila was repeatedly asked which felt doable, preserving her sense of choice and ownership over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and targeted prompts drew out Camila’s own reasons and commitment, and the clinician helped translate those motives into specific implementation intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed the work as collaborative, invited Camila to lead decisions, and shared power in choosing a feasible experiment and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interviewer actively elicited and reinforced change talk through open questions and reflections, then amplified commitment by summarizing and narrowing to a specific experiment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged with double‑sided reflections and alternative strategies, though there were missed opportunities to more directly pivot sustain statements into change talk or explore underlying fears of exclusion.
