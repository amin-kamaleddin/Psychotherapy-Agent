# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful—validates Sofia's experience and normalizes coping in a way that preserves dignity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and reflective statements and offers grounding strategies, but misses some opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of vulnerability and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors with concrete next steps (message drafting, rehearsals, safety/exit planning) and consistently brings the conversation back to action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with options and joint planning, though the clinician still tends to lead the process with multiple specific scripts and instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently offers choices, checks preferences, and avoids pressuring language, consistently reinforcing Sofia's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some intentions and preferences but relies on supplying solutions rather than drawing out Sofia's personal reasons, values, or past successes to build intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers choices and asks permission, but interaction often shifts to clinician-led scripting rather than truly co-constructing from Sofia's language and ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Prompts occasional change talk through suggestions and rehearsals, yet primarily supplies solutions instead of eliciting, amplifying, or exploring Sofia's own reasons and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain/avoidant behavior without escalation, but tends to move quickly into problem-solving instead of using double-sided or complex reflections to soften sustain talk and explore ambivalence.
