# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing Daniel's perspective and acknowledging constraints. Frequent affirmations and acceptance of his preferences supported strong rapport throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking with useful summaries of ambivalence. Reflections were mostly content-focused and could more explicitly capture deeper emotions like defensiveness or surprise.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use in relation to marathon training and recovery. Produced a concrete, time-bound one-week tracking plan with specific follow-up and outcome targets.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a joint problem-solving process, with frequent requests for Daniel's input and integration of his preferences. Decisions (tracking method, alarm wording, backups, follow-up) were co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Daniel's choice and offered multiple voluntary options rather than directives. Framed the experiment as data-gathering and consistently deferred final decisions to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Daniel's own reasons and motivators using discrepancy questions and a readiness scale. Could have probed values, past successes, and confidence more and used amplified reflections to strengthen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and client-centered, positioning Daniel as the expert on what will work. The clinician adapted to his suggestions and negotiated plans together, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions, a menu of experiments, and readiness scaling, drawing out motivators tied to sleep and performance. Missed opportunities to amplify Daniel's change statements and to more deeply explore values to multiply change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and gently redirected toward small, testable changes while normalizing barriers. Could increase use of double-sided reflections and reframes to more explicitly highlight discrepancy.
