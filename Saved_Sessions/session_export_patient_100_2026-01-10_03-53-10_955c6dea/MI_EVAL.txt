# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, offering affirmations and normalizing ambivalence throughout the session without blaming or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and accurate paraphrase that validate the client's experience; could probe emotional nuance more and pause less quickly for action.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and its academic consequences, leading to a concrete one-week, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the session as a team effort by inviting client input at every decision point and adapting language and tasks to the client's voice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the client's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and affirming that the decision to try the plan was the client's.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's reasons for change (GPA/college) and readiness, but could deepen evocation of values, identity, and past successes to boost commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and client-centered, sharing power in planning, wording texts, and selecting which strategies to try.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk using scaling and targeted questions and amplified client statements, though more sustained elicitation of ability/confidence and past successes would strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reflected competing motives (relaxation vs. grades) without reinforcing sustain talk; could use double-sided reflections and further explore peer pressure to soften sustain talk more effectively.
