**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and explicitly affirms the patient’s pace and values (e.g., “I accept that pace and it honors who you are”). No judgmental or corrective language; clear validation throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ⬜4 ☑5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflective listening that captures content and feeling (e.g., “That pull sounds important and tiring”). Reflections are timely and fit the patient’s brief responses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ⬜4 ☑5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on protecting two email‑free weeknights and building a guarded end‑of‑day hour. Questions progressively move from values to concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ⬜4 ☑5
**Comments:** Clinician frames the work as joint exploration, elicits patient preferences and past strategies, and avoids imposing solutions (e.g., “Which of those, or something else, feels doable to try first?”).

---

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ⬜4 ☑5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces patient ownership and choice, honors pace, and avoids pressuring for large or immediate change (e.g., “I accept your pace… I believe you can try this gently”).

---

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ⬜4 ☑5
**Comments:** Uses evocative questions to draw out the patient’s motives, values, and resources (e.g., “What matters most right now…?”, “What past strength…?”), eliciting sustained change talk.

---

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ⬜4 ☑5
**Comments:** Clinician acts as an equal partner, defers to the patient’s expertise, checks feasibility, and co‑creates the plan (reflects patient language like “guarded hour”).

---

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ☑4 ⬜5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reflects change talk and links plans to values, but misses some opportunities to amplify commitment and confidence (no confidence ruler, no explicit commitment statement).

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ⬜1 ⬜2 ⬜3 ☑4 ⬜5
**Comments:** Sustain talk is minimal and clinician validates ambivalence, but could more actively soften remaining doubts with double‑sided reflections, reframes, or strategic pivoting toward action.
