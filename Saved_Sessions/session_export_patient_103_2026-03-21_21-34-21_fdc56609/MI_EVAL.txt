# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with frequent validations and affirmations that reduced threat for an angry, defensive client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections of emotion and ambivalence, though reflections occasionally could probe deeper emotional nuance before moving to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on managing anger around appointment delays and every intervention was tied to that target behavior and plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction felt joint with role-plays and shared decision-making, but at times the clinician led content more than eliciting the patient's own solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and ownership by offering options, asking permission, and affirming the patient's right to test or decline the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out reasons and a concrete plan with confidence scaling, but could have elicited more personally meaningful motivators and amplified change talk further.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with invitations for preference and co-creation of the plan, though some scripting shifted expertise toward the clinician rather than being fully patient-generated.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited a specific, low-risk experiment and confidence rating, but could better amplify tentative change language and explore past successes to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged doubt without confrontation, but used few double-sided reflections or reframes and moved to solutions rather than exploring sustain talk more deliberately.
