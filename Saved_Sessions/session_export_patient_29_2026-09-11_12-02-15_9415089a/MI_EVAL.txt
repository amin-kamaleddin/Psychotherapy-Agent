# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful—normalizes ambivalence and validates practical concerns while offering suggestions as collaborative options.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, echoing concrete patient language and following up with tailored support (e.g., red flags for patch fogginess).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and treatment choices; every question and reflection advances a concrete, actionable objective.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—solicits and incorporates patient preferences repeatedly and co-constructs a practical trial checklist with shared decision-making language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces choice and patient control, offering options and honoring the patient's selections without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits pragmatic commitment and planning (trial, supports) but focuses mainly on logistics rather than deeper intrinsic motivations or values; could probe importance/confidence more.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the conversation as co-owned and honors the patient's expertise, inviting him to set trial parameters and removal cues throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with choice questions and invitations to name doable options, leading to concrete patient commitment and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with neutral, double-sided reflections and pivots to small tests, but misses some opportunities to reframe sustain talk or link it to values to reduce resistance further.
