# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, affirming Samuel's accomplishments and normalizing difficulties throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening but missed opportunities to deepen emotional reflection and more fully capture the emotional texture of Samuel's ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking reduction with concrete, goal-directed planning (if–then plans, five-day trial, and a scheduled check-in) throughout the interaction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with co-created strategies, negotiated wording, and consistent invitation of Samuel's preferences, showing joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and confirming Samuel's decisions, reinforcing his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and reasons for change using scaling and specific planning, but could have probed values and past successes more to amplify commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally conveyed that expertise resides with Samuel and co-created the plan, though could more explicitly solicit his past strategies and use earlier teach-back to increase ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (praise, reflections, confidence scaling) but could more frequently amplify strong change statements and explicitly elicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected toward change, but used few double-sided or strategic reframing reflections that would further lessen sustain talk's influence.
