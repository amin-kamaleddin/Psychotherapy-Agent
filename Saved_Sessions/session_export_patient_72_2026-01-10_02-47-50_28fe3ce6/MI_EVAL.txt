# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, affirming the client's plan and confidence while naming and normalizing barriers and offering concrete solutions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and acknowledgement of feelings (e.g., nervousness) and motivations, but missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of affect and meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on restarting exercise with concrete, behaviorally specific goals (YMCA questions, walk time, routine) and avoided drifting off task.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed—clinician solicited preferences, incorporated patient additions, offered choices, and invited follow-up troubleshooting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently supported choice and ownership by offering options, asking permission, and framing suggestions rather than directives, endorsing the client's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client's motivations and plans and translated them into action steps, but could have used more evocative questions to draw out values and personal meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative in tone and process—asked permission, co-created materials, and repeatedly checked and deferred to the client's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific change talk and commitments (YMCA visit, walk), though could deepen by eliciting more personal reasons, values, and stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and mitigated barriers with practical strategies and role-play, but relied more on neutral reflections and problem-solving than strategic MI moves to pivot sustain talk toward change.
