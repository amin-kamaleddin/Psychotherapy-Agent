### Evocation

**Comments:** Strong basic evocation (ruler, elicitation of options) that led to selecting a tool; could have probed one brief follow-up to elicit more intrinsic motives or personal meaning of change.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Clinician successfully amplified very small change talk present, but could have asked one brief probe (e.g., “What was different when you felt a little calmer?”) to deepen and extend change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing (“I hear you — that feels intrusive… not ready”)
- [x] Redirected toward small, nonthreatening action rather than debating the status quo
- [ ] Use of double-sided reflections to gently shift sustain talk (limited/absent)

**Comments:** Effective validation and redirection minimized resistance; adding double-sided reflections could more gently highlight movement toward change while honoring ambivalence.
