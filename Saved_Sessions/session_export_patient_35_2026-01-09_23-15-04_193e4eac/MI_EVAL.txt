# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes difficulty, and respects the patient's boundary about not wanting a lecture.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, compassionate reflections and sensitive safety checks that clearly mirror the patient's stress and concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a steady focus on smoking/harm‑reduction throughout the session with concrete, actionable steps and a planned follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the encounter as joint problem‑solving, solicits patient preferences, and tailors recommendations to her constraints and goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice, offers options rather than prescriptions, and asks permission before offering help, reinforcing patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own plans and preferences effectively but could probe deeper into intrinsic motivations, values, and confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear power‑sharing by inviting input, negotiating next steps, and co‑creating a realistic harm‑reduction plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change statements and small goals, though could further amplify internal reasons and long‑term values to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and gently redirects sustain talk toward harm‑reduction options, with a missed opportunity for more double‑sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
