# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, warmth, and nonjudgmental support while validating decisions and respecting time constraints.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Matched the patient's time pressure and provided accurate reflections and summaries, though reflections were mostly informational with limited deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the drug interaction and safe switching, avoided sidetracks, and guided the encounter to a concrete outcome (drafting the prescriber message).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the message and negotiated details with the patient, incorporating her preferences and demonstrating balanced two-way problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered options, asked permission frequently, respected the patient's choices, and affirmed her plan, supporting clear patient control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete actions and invitations for steps but did not probe the patient's internal motivations, concerns, or confidence about switching medications.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership through offers, drafting, soliciting edits, and filling in details at the patient's request, sharing power throughout the encounter.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created space for and reinforced the patient's commitment language and concrete follow-through, though missed opportunities to explicitly elicit confidence or contingency planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing any reluctance and effectively redirected to safety and actionable steps; sustain talk was minimal and did not develop further.
