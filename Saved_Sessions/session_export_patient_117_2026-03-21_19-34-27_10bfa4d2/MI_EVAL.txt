# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed respect and positive regard, affirming Samuel's efforts and progress with nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good understanding and supportive reflections (praised progress, asked open questions), but missed opportunities for deeper reflective listening of Samuel's ambivalence and emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, action-focused agenda on cutting down/cessation with interventions and concrete next steps tied directly to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by co-creating the break-by-break plan, inviting input, offering role-play, and tailoring suggestions to Samuel's context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking which action Samuel preferred, and leaving final decisions in his hands.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and concrete change intentions (confidence ruler, mapping, asking preferences), but at times supplied many solutions early instead of eliciting more client-generated reasons and ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance throughout, repeatedly asking permission and inviting Samuel to tailor plans so he remained the primary agent of change.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (praise, planning prompts, confidence ruler) and prompted concrete goals, though could have amplified and reflected change statements more to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing for smoking but moved quickly into advice and planning without using double-sided or validating reflections to soften sustain talk and resolve ambivalence.
