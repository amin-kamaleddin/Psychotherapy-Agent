# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect (apologized for wait, validated frustration) and used pragmatic, nonjudgmental language throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening (accurate summary of time barrier and symptoms), but reflections were mostly content-focused and could probe emotional subtext more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on target behaviors (micro-movements, breathing, snacks, tracking) and tied suggestions and safety advice directly to the behavioral goals and a 4-week follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative: co-created the one-page tracker, invited iterative tweaks, offered choices, and honored patient preferences for scheduling and reminders.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by offering a menu of options, asking which felt doable, and respecting boundaries (minimal tracking, no extra reminders) without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some actionable commitment (choice of micro-movements and 4-week trial) but did little to draw out deeper intrinsic motivation, values, or importance/confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shared power with the patient—asked preferences, co-created materials, and adjusted plans per Andrei’s requests, reflecting genuine patient influence on decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created opportunities for change talk and reinforced small commitments (menu of brief options, tracking plan) but missed chances to amplify importance and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk about time barriers and smoothly redirected to feasible options, though double-sided reflections could have further reduced sustain talk.
