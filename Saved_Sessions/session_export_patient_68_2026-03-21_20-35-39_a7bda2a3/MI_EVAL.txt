# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance from the start, using normalizing statements and affirming the patient's agency throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures ambivalence and embarrassment, though reflections are mostly summary-level and could go deeper into affective experience (shame, stress).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol-related risk, efficiently narrowing from ambivalence to concrete harm-reduction steps, an if‑then plan, and troubleshooting strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in a genuine joint venture by soliciting input at each step, co-constructing plans and wording, rehearsing with the patient, and offering alternatives rather than dictating a plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms the patient's choice and ownership by offering options, asking which strategies feel doable, and inviting the patient to choose exact wording and timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk using open questions and scaling, but could strengthen evocation by probing values, broader reasons for change, and past successes to boost internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions the patient as the expert and uses invitational language, asks permission before offering ideas, and follows the patient's lead in selecting and refining plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits concrete change talk (rulers, specific steps, if‑then plan) but would benefit from more frequent reflections and amplification of the patient's change statements and probing for reasons and past success.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and offers respectful harm-reduction options without reinforcing it, though it rarely employs double-sided reflections or explicit reframing to resolve ambivalence.
