# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, repeatedly affirming Wiremu's efforts and normalizing his tension. The warm, respectful tone maintained the client's dignity throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, echoing the client's language and validating ambivalence. Reflections were somewhat formulaic and missed opportunities to probe deeper into emotions like shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on target behaviors with concrete, incremental steps (trial stay plan, counsellor selection, booking text). Consistently steered toward specific, feasible next actions while preserving client autonomy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative, team-like stance by offering help while keeping Wiremu in the lead and inviting his preferences at each step. Co-constructed the plan so Wiremu's ideas materially shaped the outcomes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Continuously emphasized choice and enabled Wiremu to retain control (picking the counsellor, making first contact, choosing the booking text). Language avoided pressure and reinforced ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked motivation by linking actions to Wiremu's core value (tamariki) and eliciting concrete ideas for action. Could more systematically draw out different forms of change talk (desire, ability, reason, commitment) and probe confidence and barriers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Wiremu as the expert on his life, offering options and deferring decisions to him to share power. Invited choice and tailored support to his preferences, exemplifying strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk and linked values to behaviour, highlighting concrete change actions. Could do more selective reflections and targeted questions to build momentum for commitment and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without arguing, and explored compromises to reduce resistance (trial stay, own room, paying board). Could further reduce sustain talk using strategic devices like double-sided reflections or eliciting pros/cons that tip toward change.
