# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Tyler’s mixed goals without shaming; language was warm, respectful, and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing ambivalence, but tended to move quickly into problem-solving instead of using deeper, more complex reflections to explore emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on risky drinking and prevention of future incidents, guiding the session toward a specific, time-bound, client-centered plan without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session read as a genuine collaboration—plan was co-constructed, clinician invited rehearsal and checked Tyler’s comfort, honoring his expertise about his social context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Tyler’s sense of choice by offering options, normalizing partial change, and eliciting his preferences rather than dictating actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Tyler by eliciting his values (education, avoiding trouble) and translating them into actionable steps that amplified his own change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used inclusive language and built the plan from Tyler’s ideas, sharing power and weighing his preferences heavily in decision-making and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk through open questions and concrete follow-ups, resulting in multiple client-generated change statements and a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and used some double-sided framing to avoid reinforcing it, but moved fairly quickly into strategies instead of lingering to elicit Tyler’s own arguments against heavy drinking.
