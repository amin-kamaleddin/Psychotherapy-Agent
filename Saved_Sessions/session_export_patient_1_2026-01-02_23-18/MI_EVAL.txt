# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental ("I hear you," "That's understandable," "I'm here to support you"). Normalized doubt and allowed limited commitment without pressure; strong, warm acceptance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of beliefs/barriers (use pattern, dust, crew pressure). Could deepen by reflecting underlying emotion/meaning (e.g., smoking as relaxation/connection) and using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on mini‑cigar use and oral irritation. Steered to a concrete, measurable two‑week trial with specific ratings and follow‑up; asked targeted questions (concern/confidence/supports).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited Ahmed's input and choices (permission to share, options offered, negotiated nights/replacements). Plan was co‑constructed and responsive to patient preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Ahmed's right to choose, framed the plan as optional and under his control, avoided coercive language and respected limited commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited views, concern/confidence scales, barriers, and some change language (willingness to cut back). Tended to move to solutions quickly rather than pausing to amplify Ahmed’s own reasons, values, or motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Ahmed as an equal collaborator (asked which supports to set up, checked potential difficulties). Shared decision‑making and power effectively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced tentative change talk (agreement to trial, selection of nights/gum), used affirmations and planning to support self‑efficacy. Missed opportunities to deepen and amplify desire/reason/need and to elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argumentation and validated sustain concerns (mini‑cigar minimization, dust explanation). Pivoted to a low‑effort test to reduce resistance; could further use double‑sided reflections to make discrepancy more explicit.
