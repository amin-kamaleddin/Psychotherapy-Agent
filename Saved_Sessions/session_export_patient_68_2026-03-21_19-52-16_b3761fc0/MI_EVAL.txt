# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental, warm, and validating language throughout the session, normalizing Kevin's feelings and avoiding blame to demonstrate clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and some reflective statements, picking up practical concerns and ambivalence; could deepen and make reflections more consistent rather than moving quickly to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on alcohol-related harm reduction with concrete behavioral goals and minimal drift, guiding toward an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—repeatedly asks Kevin's preferences, co-creates messages and refusal lines, and shapes decisions around his input rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by asking permission, offering multiple options, normalizing choice, and reinforcing Kevin's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation with choices, a confidence ruler, and planning prompts, but could use more open-ended elicitation and deeper exploration of values and personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly fosters partnership and power-sharing by inviting Kevin to choose strategies, checking supports, and co-constructing the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and reinforces change language and translates intention into concrete steps and commitments, though could more consistently reflect and amplify change talk and explore underlying reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to planning, but underuses double-sided reflections and strategic summaries that would better acknowledge the appeal of drinking while guiding toward change.
