# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and avoided lecturing, explicitly honoring Marco’s request not to be lectured throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that captured Marco’s history, emotions, and the function of smoking, demonstrating strong perspective‑taking and reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking cessation, consistently tying the conversation to triggers, concrete strategies, and a specific short‑term plan with a check‑in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Created a genuine team approach by soliciting Marco’s input at decision points, offering choices, and co‑constructing a tailored action plan and monitoring arrangement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Marco’s freedom to choose, offered options without pressure, and reinforced support regardless of his decisions, preserving client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivations and a concrete plan, but much content was clinician‑provided; could increase open questions to draw out Marco’s own reasons, values, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the encounter as collaborative by asking what to focus on, inviting choice among options, and negotiating the timing and tone of the check‑in.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced preparatory and commitment language and asked for a check‑in, but missed opportunities to first elicit Marco’s own ideas and to amplify his brief change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
