# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing ambivalence and validating feelings; however the clinician moves quickly to scripting and problem‑solving and misses deeper, individualized affirmations of values and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and containment through calm tone and validations; could deepen emotional reflections and use more complex reflections rather than shifting to directive interventions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, unwavering focus on the target behavior (getting the flatmate to leave) and ties all interventions to the deadline, message wording, and safety planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—clinician supplies expertise while deferring key decisions to the client, negotiating drafts, timing, and co‑creating the escalation plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering options, asking permission before role‑play or drafting, and affirming the client's right to choose how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of readiness and invitations to act are present, but the clinician leans toward offering solutions and scripts instead of drawing out the client's own motives, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership throughout—session is co‑constructed with the client choosing drafts, timelines, and practice, showing effective power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and creates opportunities for change talk by offering tools and invitations to act, but often supplies language rather than eliciting and amplifying the client's own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirects sustain talk toward action with validation, scripts, and rehearsals; missed opportunities to use double‑sided or deeper reflective strategies to resolve ambivalence more thoroughly.
