# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing, nonjudgmental language and acceptance of resistance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate empathic listening—reflections capture both content and affect and responses are well attuned to the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on diabetes self-management and steers toward concrete, behaviorally specific targets and safety instructions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving, solicits the patient's input and preferences, and tailors suggestions to the patient's constraints so the plan emerges collaboratively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and noncoercion, offers alternatives when the patient declines, and frames follow-up as optional to reinforce control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits minimal pragmatic motivation (agreement to a tiny experiment) but does little to draw out deeper intrinsic reasons or values linked to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making by offering options, inviting preferences, and treating the patient as the expert in selecting feasible steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces small commitments and experiments but misses opportunities to deepen desire/reason/need language and intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully validates resistance and redirects to low-pressure, manageable options, avoiding argument and reducing the power of sustain talk.
