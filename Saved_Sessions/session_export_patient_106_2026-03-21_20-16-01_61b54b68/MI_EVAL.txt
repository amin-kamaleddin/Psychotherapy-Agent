# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, using validating language and affirming harm‑reduction choices. Avoids labeling or shaming throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides accurate reflections and paraphrases that capture the core tension between coping and parenting concerns. Could probe deeper into emotional nuance (fears, guilt, sources of stress) to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking reduction and links conversation to concrete, specific goals (no smoking inside, delay first cigarette, buy gum, check‑ins). Effectively narrows ambivalence into actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co‑constructed with frequent invitations for patient input and joint problem‑solving. Clinician offers to draft messages and then invites the patient to choose and tweak wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors patient choice, normalizes cutting back versus quitting, and offers options rather than directives. Asks permission before advice and defers to the patient's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's own motivations, reasons, and implementation intentions using rulers and follow‑up questions. Helps the patient articulate concrete steps and internal motivators for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and shares power, inviting the patient to lead decision‑making and choose supports. Co‑creates the plan (texts, reminders, gum use) with the patient driving choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through open questions, importance/confidence rulers, reflections, and reinforcement of stated intentions. Strengthens commitment by eliciting specific, doable next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Manages sustain talk well by not confronting and reframing toward harm‑reduction and small steps. Could more strategically use double‑sided reflections or summaries to explicitly tilt toward change talk.
