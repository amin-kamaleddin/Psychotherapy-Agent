# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (opened with safety check, normalized slips, avoided shaming and offered options).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a good empathic stance and some reflective listening but missed deeper emotional reflections and rarely reflected the patient's guardedness or surprise.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and its relation to marathon training, producing a time-limited, measurable tracking plan with concrete follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the tracking template and honored patient preferences throughout, positioning the clinician as a resource rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-relevant information and linked drinking to valued activity but relied more on offering strategies than drawing out the patient's own motives or using scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invited input and shared decisions (e.g., phone note vs app, weekly totals) demonstrating strong power‑sharing and collaborative work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited change‑relevant statements (surprise, desire to see effects) but did not sufficiently amplify or follow up to strengthen commitment or use scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
