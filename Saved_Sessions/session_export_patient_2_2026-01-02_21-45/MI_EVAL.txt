# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard and normalization (multiple affirmations, “Slips are normal…”, “You’re doing this for your health and your son”). No labeling or criticism; clinician validates priorities and difficulty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective‑taking (good paraphrases of context and affect). Missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional drivers (fatigue/escape function of evening smoking).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on smoking reduction; guided client to a concrete, stepwise plan (specific strategy, implementation intentions, and scheduled check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction framed as joint problem‑solving; patient selects strategies and co‑creates plan. Occasional clinician praise (“I’m proud…”) slightly shifts tone away from equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports client choice and control, accepts reduction as valid goal, avoids pressure and honors limits (no push on alcohol topic).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits client’s motivations and ideas (open questions, scaling). Could have probed more systematically for additional intrinsic motives and explored discrepancy more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance and invitations for input; plan was co‑created. Slight clinician‑centered language at times that could be reframed as patient agency.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk (planning language, reported reduction to four). Could amplify and elicit stronger commitment language beyond “I’ll try.”

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not confronted and was effectively redirected toward change. Could further use double‑sided reflections to acknowledge both sides explicitly.
