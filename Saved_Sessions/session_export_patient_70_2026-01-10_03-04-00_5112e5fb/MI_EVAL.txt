# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing effort and avoiding judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking but missed opportunities to deepen emotional exploration of shame, guilt, and the need for 'action.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on target behaviors and moved efficiently from values and barriers to a concrete, time-bound plan with logistics and contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative interaction with co-created plans, practical supports, and regular checks about Lars's preferences and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Lars's sense of choice, offered options, and followed his lead without using pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation with open questions and scaling but could have probed deeper for personally resonant reasons and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered meaningful power-sharing and joint problem-solving, though some directive phrasing risked reintroducing clinician authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified change talk into a concrete action plan, but there were missed chances to further amplify Lars's own motivational language and consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain-talk without challenging it but did little to strategically reframe or explore alternatives to boredom, mates, and alcohol to tip ambivalence toward change.
