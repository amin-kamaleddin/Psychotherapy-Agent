# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly conveyed nonjudgmental regard and validated Ahmed's perspective and priorities (work/family), avoiding shame or labeling throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and summaries captured both content and underlying concerns, with regular checks of Ahmed's feelings and preferences before moving forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stayed focused on mini-cigars and concrete next steps (one-page facts, photos, dentist/ENT), with questions and reflections consistently steering toward evidence and feasible actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed the interaction as a partnership, invited Ahmed's choices, and co-created the plan (facts first, photos, dentist note), making decisions jointly rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were offered throughout and the psychiatrist affirmed that the decision rested with Ahmed, supporting his preference for information and gradual change without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician drew out Ahmed's own reasons and criteria for change by asking what would convince him and exploring intrinsic motivations like staying able to work and support family.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly sought Ahmed's input on next steps, offered to draft materials together, and deferred to Ahmed's expertise about what felt doable, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist successfully elicited multiple change statements and reinforced them, but missed opportunities to elicit commitment or confidence (e.g., scaling likelihood or a specific first-step/timeframe).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided confrontation and validated Ahmed's minimizations while gently redirecting to options and evidence, though could have used more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to further reduce sustain talk.
