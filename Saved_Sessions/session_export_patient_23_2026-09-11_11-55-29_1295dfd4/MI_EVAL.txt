### Collaboration

#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from Claudia by eliciting her own reasons, past strategies, and specific plans rather than lecturing. Successfully converts internal motivation into concrete, client‑generated actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and amplifies change talk through open questions, reflections, and reminders of past successes. Repeated prompts help translate general desire to change into concrete commitment language.
