# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth, affirming honesty and realism while avoiding criticism throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing situation and worries, but missed some opportunities to probe deeper emotions like discouragement or family dynamics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus and steered the conversation toward concrete, time-bound steps using scaling and iterative problem-solving without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, repeatedly invited the client’s input, and co-constructed the plan with offers to role-play or draft texts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, respecting caveats, and asking what felt most doable without using pressure or coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations, past successes, and resources using scaling and prompts, but could more deeply evoke intrinsic reasons and amplify strengths to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the client as the expert, offering options and asking permission while inviting collaboration so the client’s preferences shaped the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and built on change talk with concrete planning and scaling, though could do more to amplify and summarize the client’s change language to consolidate motivation and elicit commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward solutions, but relied more on neutral reflections rather than double-sided reflections or deeper exploration of what sustains the ambivalence.
