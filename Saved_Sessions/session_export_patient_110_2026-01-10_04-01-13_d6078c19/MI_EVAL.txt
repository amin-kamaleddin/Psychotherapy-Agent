# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and avoids judgment; repeatedly affirms the patient’s priorities and feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and affect in ways the patient endorses.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on cholesterol management and consistently ties discussion back to the central goal while moving from values to specific actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative—solicits the patient’s input, checks feasibility, and co-designs strategies so decisions emerge from the patient’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the patient’s choice and control, offers options without pressure, and elicits voluntary commitments that reinforce ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient by linking change to personal values and eliciting the patient’s own reasons, plans, and confidence strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the conversation as a joint endeavor, invites the patient’s expertise, and co-creates the plan in a non-expert stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions and reflections, producing concrete change statements and commitments from the patient.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and often pivots to planning, but misses some opportunities for deeper exploration of the function of sustain talk and use of double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
