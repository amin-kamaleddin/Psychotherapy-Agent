# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalized struggles, and honored the patient’s preferences when offering options and tools.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking about practical barriers, but did not consistently deepen emotional content or use more complex reflections to explore ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol in relation to marathon training and moved efficiently to a specific, actionable tracking plan with a start date and check‑in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the plan with the patient, solicited format/timing preferences, and problem‑solved practical barriers together, reflecting genuine teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, asking permission before tools, and framing the plan as the patient’s decision while normalizing imperfection.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some of the patient’s motivations (marathon concerns, surprise at 18/week) but could have more directly drawn out intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power‑sharing through agenda‑setting, repeated permission, and collaborative drafting of templates and reminders, centering the patient in decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and affirmed change talk and secured a concrete commitment to tracking, but missed opportunities to probe importance, desire, and confidence to amplify motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used neutral/redirection and double‑sided reflections, but could have more strategically reflected sustain talk then pivoted to strengthen change language.
