# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing the patient's stance and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that captured both sustain and change sides; could deepen affective and complex reflections to explore underlying emotions more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking and readiness to change, guiding the session toward actionable outcomes like scripts, monitoring, and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a team approach by offering choices, checking preferences, and co‑constructing plans (scripts, chart wording, 3‑month follow‑up).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient autonomy by giving control of pace and focus, offering options, and honoring his decision not to quit while planning monitoring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Employed rulers and focus questions to elicit some client statements but relied more on information and scripting than on drawing out intrinsic motivations or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance with invitations to choose and role‑play; occasional movement into an expert role slightly reduced pure client‑led exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change language (importance/confidence scores, willingness to monitor) but missed probes to amplify or deepen reasons and strengths that supported change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used a useful double‑sided reflection early, but made limited strategic attempts to reframe sustain talk or highlight discrepancy toward change.
