# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming with frequent normalization of struggle and positive regard that avoids blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with compassionate acknowledgments; could use deeper affective or complex reflections to name unspoken emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing community engagement and progressively narrows to a specific, concrete action plan and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as joint problem-solving with frequent invitations to preferences and co-creation of the plan, treating the client as an equal contributor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure while accepting disclosures of non‑action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses evocative open questions and elicits a concrete plan, but change talk remains tentative and could be deepened by drawing out personal reasons and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and invites input, though at times supplies scripts and phrasing rather than eliciting the client's own wording to increase ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with planning and affirmations, but could more often reflect and amplify tentative 'yes' statements and assess confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects to small steps and normalization; could further reduce sustain talk using more double-sided reflections to highlight ambivalence.
