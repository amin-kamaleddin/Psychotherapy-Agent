**Comments:** Frequently invited client preference and choice (asking which step/script, offering options, role‑play). Generally co‑constructed plan, but sometimes prescriptive with provided scripts rather than eliciting client phrasing first.

**Comments:** Affirmed control and choices (“you don’t have to cut friends completely,” asked which option felt doable). Acknowledged slips as learning. Occasional directive language and praise (“I’m proud of your effort”) shifted slightly toward advising.

**Comments:** Drew out client reasons for change (travel, job security) and elicited concrete steps and confidence ratings. Missed opportunities to deepen and amplify change talk (explore why Egypt matters emotionally, elaborate consequences).

**Comments:** Positioned work as joint (role‑play offer, asking preferences), solicited confidence ratings. At times clinician supplied scripts/recommendations rather than inviting co‑creation of language first.

**Comments:** Elicited desire/reason language (“I really do wanna travel”), preparatory/commitment steps (giving card, travel jar, scripts) and reflected/summarized them. Could more actively amplify and elicit stronger verbal commitment in client’s own words.
