# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician opened with an explicit nonjudgmental stance, named Lukas’s roles, and consistently communicated respect and normalization throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and summarizing captured Lukas’s concerns, values, and ambivalence well, though deeper exploration of emotional nuance (guilt, peer pressure anxiety) was limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on early alcohol use and relevant consequences, offering targeted strategies and rehearsals that kept the work action-oriented and on-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a joint enterprise with shared decision-making, role-play, and clinician adjustments based on Lukas’s feedback, demonstrating strong collaborative practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly emphasized choice, asked permission before offering advice, and provided multiple options to support Lukas’s sense of control and agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited Lukas’s preferences and some motivations, but missed opportunities for deeper, systematic elicitation of personal reasons, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A collaborative stance was fostered through choices and rehearsal, but the clinician offered several specific scripts fairly early rather than eliciting more of Lukas’s own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited and supported change-oriented language and used rehearsal effectively, yet did not consistently amplify or systematically elicit importance, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist avoided arguing and offered low-threat strategies that redirected sustain talk, but could have used more double-sided reflections and explicit normalization to resolve ambivalence.
