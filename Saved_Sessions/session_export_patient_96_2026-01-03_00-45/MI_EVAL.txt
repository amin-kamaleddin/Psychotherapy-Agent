- [x] Used open questions to elicit patient perspective
- [ ] Sustained probes to draw out motivations or concerns

**Comments:** Clinician opened with evocative questions but moved quickly to practical information once minimal worry was expressed; limited follow-up to deepen motivation or explore latent concerns.

**Comments:** Clinician behaved as a true partner—offering options, checking preferences, co-authoring concise aftercare text the patient requested.

- [x] Elicited brief/limited change talk (minimal)

**Comments:** Only minimal change talk appeared (“Maybe I’ll worry if it gets messed up”), which was not pursued or amplified; missed opportunities to expand desire/reason/need for change or secure a small commitment.
