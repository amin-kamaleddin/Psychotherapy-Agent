# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful tone and normalized the patient's choices, creating a warm, accepting atmosphere. Blaming language was avoided and the clinician acknowledged barriers without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking with reflective statements and validation of barriers and skepticism. Could deepen empathy by using more complex emotional reflections and checking understanding with confirmatory reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on blood pressure management and tied recommendations back to BP throughout the session. The clinician provided a concrete four-week plan and steered conversation back to feasible solutions when barriers arose.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was framed as a joint effort with co-constructed plans, specific follow-up, and patient input shaping decisions. The clinician repeatedly invited preferences and tailored recommendations to the patient's context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering options and honoring preferences without pressuring language. The clinician emphasized the patient's control over decisions and offered support for whatever choices the patient made.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to draw out motivation (e.g., willingness to try an alcohol-free day) but evocation was limited and often supplanted by offering solutions. The clinician did not routinely use scaling questions or explore deeper personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by asking for preferences and building a realistic plan together, including agreed check-ins. Could strengthen partnership by eliciting the patient's solutions before offering options to increase power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change talk (logging, walks, one alcohol-free day) but did not consistently amplify or deepen it. Missed opportunities for open, evocative questions, rulers, or eliciting personal goals to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and responded neutrally to minimizations, redirecting sustain talk toward evidence (tracking BP) and offering compromises. Could improve by using more double-sided and complex reflections to surface and resolve ambivalence.
