# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Elena’s worries, avoiding criticism or correction throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, empathic reflections captured Elena’s perspective and feelings and the clinician adjusted responses to her emotional state and requests.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on the child’s vaccination decision and concrete preparation (sources, script, role‑play) without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician engaged in genuine two‑way problem solving, solicited preferences, integrated Elena’s input, and co‑constructed the brief script and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly affirmed Elena’s right to choose, offered options and framed suggestions as offers rather than directives, avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited practical steps and preparatory motivation but did not consistently draw out deeper intrinsic motives, values, or use importance/confidence scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction demonstrated strong partnership—decisions were made collaboratively, the clinician respected Elena’s expertise about her concerns, and offered to practice together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician created and reinforced tentative change talk (resources, script, rehearsal) but missed opportunities to deepen or amplify reasons for protecting the child and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled gently with validation and balanced options rather than argument, though the clinician rarely used double‑sided reflections to explicitly name ambivalence and pivot to change language.
