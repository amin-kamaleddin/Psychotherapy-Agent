# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Sofia's feelings and avoiding blame or moralizing throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring Sofia's language and summarizing her emotions and motivations to reinforce rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on school attendance/safety and moved the conversation from problem identification to concrete, near-term action planning (text, meeting, backups).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, co-created text options and backups, and repeatedly invited Sofia's preferences so decisions felt mutual.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Sofia's sense of choice, avoided pressure, and reinforced her ownership of the plan by offering options and inviting input rather than directing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client-generated reasons and practical strategies (what helped before, what matters tomorrow) but could have probed deeper into values or used scaling to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fostered client-led planning using Sofia's wording, open questions, and shared decision-making, keeping expertise and control with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several instances of change talk and a specific doable plan, but did not systematically assess commitment or confidence (no scaling or explicit commitment request).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk, but missed opportunities to strategically downweight it (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframing to pivot toward change).
