# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes disclosure, and honors privacy requests without judgment. Reassurances and supportive language reduce shame and promote safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, validating emotions and concerns. Repeatedly checks safety and emotional state, showing clear understanding of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on overdose risk and harm reduction throughout the session. Moves logically from exploration to concrete skills training and action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint effort, inviting patient input and co-creating scripts and rehearsals. Practices skills together and shares power in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices and respects the patient's decisions, avoiding coercive language. Reinforces ownership by framing actions as the patient's choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and specific change steps (e.g., Narcan, role‑play) but could probe deeper into underlying values and reasons for the patient's strong readiness. Further questions about what makes it a '10' would strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positions the session as collaborative and defers to the patient's preferences and expertise. Offers options and honors the patient's plan for disclosure and practice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change-oriented language and concrete harm-reduction steps, supporting rehearsal and commitments. Missed opportunities to amplify the patient's strong change language and explore confidence and barriers in more depth.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges reasons for continued use neutrally and redirects toward practical risk-reduction strategies, limiting sustain talk. Could strengthen approach with double-sided reflections and eliciting the patient's own statements about costs of continued use.
