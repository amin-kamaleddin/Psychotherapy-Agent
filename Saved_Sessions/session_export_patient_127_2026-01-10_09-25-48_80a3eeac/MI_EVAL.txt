# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, praised strengths (e.g., 22 days) and normalized difficulty without judgment. These behaviors conveyed respect and validation throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-quality perspective-taking and reflective listening captured both content and emotion (ambivalence, identity concerns, pride). Reflections and summaries were used effectively to check understanding and elicit more from Timur.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on reducing recidivism and building a pro-social life through targeted questions and reflections. The conversation progressed logically from values/ambivalence to concrete steps and a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session modeled an authentic partnership by co-creating messages/scripts, negotiating the check-in time, and aligning support with Timur’s preferences. The clinician invited Timur’s input and adapted plans accordingly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported via choices (draft A vs B, whether to send, which task to prioritize) and offers of help framed as options. The clinician affirmed Timur’s control over decisions and respected his pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician drew motivation from Timur’s own values and reasons for change, using open questions to elicit internal motivations. Reflections amplified those motivations and translated them into actionable steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician genuinely shared power by asking what would help, offering options, and co-planning concrete actions. Timur shaped the plan and the clinician supported implementation rather than dictating solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk by highlighting positives (22 days, wanting Fiona) and asking what felt doable this week. Change language was operationalized into specific, realistic tasks and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used neutral and double-sided reflections to validate ambivalence without reinforcing sustain talk, and generally redirected toward change. There were missed opportunities to more explicitly challenge rationalizations or amplify the long-term costs of returning to crime and to probe past successes/confidence.
