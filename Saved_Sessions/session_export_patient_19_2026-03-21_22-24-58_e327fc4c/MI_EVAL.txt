# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalization, validation, and affirmations without judgment or punitive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and complex reflections that capture ambivalence and the patient's perspective throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on gambling/financial stability and repeatedly ties discussion to concrete, time‑bound plans and desired outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaves as an equal partner with co‑constructed planning; the patient's ideas consistently drive action and the clinician offers support rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking preferences about wording/timing, and explicitly avoiding pressure or lectures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out values and motivations (travel, future responsibility, past successes) but could probe further to elicit stronger, specific commitment language and scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a co‑equal stance by delegating authority, checking wording/timing, and negotiating accountability in a collaborative manner.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with open questions and reflections leading to concrete plans, though could more actively amplify importance/confidence and consolidate commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses double‑sided reflections, but could more strategically reframe sustain statements and elicit the costs of continuing the behavior.
