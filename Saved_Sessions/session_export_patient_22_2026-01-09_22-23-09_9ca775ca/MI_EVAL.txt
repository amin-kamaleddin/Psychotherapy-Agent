### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician provides clear, nonjudgmental support and normalizes doubt throughout the session; could strengthen by naming Camila's specific strengths and more individualized empathic affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol use, social anxiety, and sleep with concrete goals and strategies; consistently returns to specific, actionable targets and safety considerations.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Interaction reads as cooperative with invitations for input and follow-up, but the clinician leans toward directive coaching and scripted solutions instead of eliciting Camila's own ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and personal control by offering multiple options, asking permission, and affirming that quitting is not required, fostering ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some open questions and invitations to choose a change, but focuses more on clinician-generated solutions rather than drawing out Camila's internal motivations, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses permission and invitations but often shifts into an expert/instructor role with prescriptive strategies; would benefit from co-creating the plan and eliciting Camila's preferred options first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk and sets a choice, yet moves quickly to offering plans and strategies instead of lingering to amplify Camila's reasons, desires, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
