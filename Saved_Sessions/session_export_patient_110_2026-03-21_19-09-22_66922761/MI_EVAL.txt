# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (open invitation, normalizing, validating) and repeatedly affirms the patient’s priorities like retirement and staying active.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and links emotions to values, helping the patient feel understood, but reflections are sometimes brief and followed quickly by information rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on starting statin therapy and safe monitoring, with every intervention tied to actionable steps for adherence and preparation for the appointment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance—solicits preferences, co-creates scripts and logs, and iteratively refines tools with the patient, framing next steps as joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering help with permission, using nonpressuring language, and affirming that decisions and use of tools are the patient's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivational material and ties recommendations to the patient’s values (retirement, activity) but relies more on information-giving than systematic elicitation of internal motivations, confidence, or scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Takes a collaborative stance and invites co-creation of plans and language, but frequently supplies ready-made scripts and instructions rather than eliciting the patient's own formulations first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk by connecting treatment to values and offering concrete scripts, though could deepen change talk with more open questions about importance and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing minimization and reframes concerns with monitoring, low-dose starts, and symptom logs, but could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly resolve ambivalence.
