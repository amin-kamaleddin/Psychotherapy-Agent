# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard through nonjudgmental summaries, validation, and normalization of needing more time; avoided blame or prescriptive language throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking using Elena's affect-laden phrases, but many reflections were close paraphrases and sometimes repetitive rather than deepening emotional exploration of fears or avoidance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on moving toward reliable contraception and feasible next steps, with each exchange returning to actionable planning and a specific booking plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician invited Elena's input, offered a menu of supports, and asked her to choose preferences, making the plan a joint problem-solving process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored Elena's choices and control by offering options, asking permission, and leaving decisions in her hands, thereby supporting her sense of agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited Elena's own motivations and priorities with open questions, producing specific change talk and a self-generated plan (online booking, condoms) rather than imposing reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly positioned Elena as the expert on her life, co-created the plan, and invited her to decide how the clinician could help, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced concrete change language and doable steps, but missed opportunities to strengthen commitment and confidence (no scaling/confidence ruler or summary to consolidate reasons for change).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledged ambivalence and avoided amplifying sustain talk, reframing reluctance into a plan, though could more deliberately use double-sided reflections or summaries to tip the balance toward change.
