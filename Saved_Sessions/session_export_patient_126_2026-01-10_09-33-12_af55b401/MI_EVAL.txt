# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes competing priorities, and consistently avoids judgment or directive language while acknowledging the patient's limits.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and summaries capture the patient's values and ambivalence (e.g., 'wiped out', 'no fuss'), deepening rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol to increase energy for the child and repeatedly steers toward concrete, achievable steps tied to the patient's values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is framed as a joint exploration with frequent invites for input, options offered, and the plan built from the patient's responses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice, presents alternatives, accepts ambivalence, and supports the patient's ownership of the change process without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits motivation from the patient's own values (being present for his child) and draws out specific internal reasons and implementation intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert, invites collaboration, and co-creates a realistic, patient-led plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with targeted open questions and reflections, leading to concrete intentions and planning (e.g., NA beer, stashing it).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects toward change, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections and explore costs of staying the same to further soften sustain talk.
