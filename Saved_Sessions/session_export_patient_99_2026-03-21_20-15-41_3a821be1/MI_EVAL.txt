### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician opened with clear unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, validated Matteo’s priorities, and consistently affirmed his choices and constraints.

### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
