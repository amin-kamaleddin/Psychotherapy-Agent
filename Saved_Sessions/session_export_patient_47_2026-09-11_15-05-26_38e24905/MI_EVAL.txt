# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, using supportive language and affirmations of the client's pace; no judgment or shaming was evident.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate content reflections and validation; more complex, affect-focused reflections probing emotions behind minimization or defensiveness would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on reducing drinking and progressed stepwise from goal to specific supports and measures of success; avoided topic drift throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative, with the clinician soliciting the client's ideas and shaping them together rather than prescribing; mutual problem-solving and shared decision-making were evident.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the client's choice and control by framing options as the client's decisions and leaving room for slips and self-defined success; avoided directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out the client's intrinsic motivations (health, clear-headed mornings, pride, son's relief) and used those reasons to build and expand a concrete plan; evocation informed the change strategy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co-constructed with repeated invitations for the client's expertise and choices; the clinician followed the client's lead and reinforced shared work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk by asking for realistic steps and specifics, which deepened commitment and planning; reflections and prompts reinforced desire, ability, and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and effectively redirected minimizing language toward achievable steps, preventing sustain talk from dominating; could more often use double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to explicitly hold both sustain and change talk.
