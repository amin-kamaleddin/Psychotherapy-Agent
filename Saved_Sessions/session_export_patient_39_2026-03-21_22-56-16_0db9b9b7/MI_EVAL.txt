# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with validating language and normalizing statements; the patient explicitly said they appreciated being understood, indicating a nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking by accurately mirroring the patient's words and naming emotions, which the patient accepted and elaborated on.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear agenda focused on increasing activity within mobility limits and steered the conversation through problem exploration to a specific, measurable one-week plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structured the interaction as a true partnership by soliciting the patient's ideas, offering options, and allowing the patient to choose the reminders and plan, resulting in active engagement and plan adoption.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by offering choices, honoring boundaries, and repeatedly asking what felt doable while giving permission to stop if tired, preserving the patient's decision-making control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out personal reasons and values that supported change and elicited a self-generated plan, though deeper elicitation (confidence/importance rulers, past successes, stronger summaries) could further consolidate motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the patient as the expert and co-created the plan by asking preferences and deferring to the patient's choices, demonstrated by the patient's selection of the chime.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and shaped change talk through questions about doable steps and values, leading to a concrete plan, but could more deliberately amplify and summarize change language to strengthen commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully validated barriers and reframed sustain talk toward a gentle approach with permission-to-stop, though use of double-sided reflections or eliciting counterarguments to sustain talk could further tip the balance toward change.
