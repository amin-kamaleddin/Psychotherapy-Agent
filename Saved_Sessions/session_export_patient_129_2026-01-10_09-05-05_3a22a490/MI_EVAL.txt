# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through normalizing statements and permission phrases that reduced threat for a resistant adolescent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking with timely paraphrases and invitations to set the pace, reinforcing rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on adolescent substance use and guided the session toward concrete, behavior-focused strategies and a tangible plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged Tyler as an equal partner by soliciting preferences at decision points, adapting options, and co-developing a realistic plan he helped shape.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Tyler’s sense of choice and control with permission statements, offering options and reassurance about imperfect attempts to reduce pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited personal strategies and a recent pressure episode but missed deeper exploration of values, long-term goals, and the meaning of feeling 'crappy' to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the session as collaborative and deferred to Tyler’s expertise, letting his choices meaningfully shape the plan and next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced concrete change language and strategies, but could more consistently amplify and summarize change talk and explicitly elicit readiness or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected resistance with acceptance and normalization, though more double-sided reflections could better acknowledge ambivalence while highlighting discrepancy.
