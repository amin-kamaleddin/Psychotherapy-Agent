# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental—opens warmly, normalizes nervousness, and affirms the patient’s plan and readiness throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and some reflective listening (checks safety, acknowledges nervousness), but falls short of deeper emotional or complex reflections of core concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on increasing activity and moves from exploration to a concrete, measurable 10‑minute daily plan with tracking and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as collaborative problem‑solving—solicits preferences, adjusts the plan per patient requests, and co-constructs tools and follow‑up steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently upholds patient choice by offering options, designing a co‑created fallback rule, and inviting the patient to set timing and reporting preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits intrinsic motivations and secures commitment (e.g., check‑in), but misses opportunities to probe deeper reasons, values, and confidence to intensify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong power sharing—repeatedly asks for the patient’s input, negotiates decisions, and shares responsibility for planning and adjustments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reinforces and extends change talk with a concrete 7‑day plan and commitment, but could more often elicit the patient’s own reasons and selectively summarize motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk and uses normalization and redirection (focus on activity, forgiving fallback rule), but uses fewer explicit techniques (double‑sided reflections/reframes) to actively shift sustain language.
