### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—repeatedly asks preferences, incorporates Kenji’s choices, and frames the work as joint problem‑solving. Co‑creates reminders and coping strategies while offering to support troubleshooting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, normalizing ambivalence, and avoiding pressuring language. Explicitly acknowledges Kenji's right to decide pace and participation in follow‑up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change language and a commitment to a small experiment but leans toward offering solutions. Would benefit from more evocative questions to surface values, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and invites input, incorporating Kenji’s selections into the plan. At times provides long lists of tips that shift control back to the clinician; offering fewer options and eliciting Kenji’s ideas would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and planning, reinforcing commitments to a small step. Missed opportunities to deepen and amplify motivations with follow‑up questions and targeted reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk, normalizes it, and reframes slips as information rather than failure. Could more often use MI techniques like double‑sided reflections to explicitly highlight ambivalence and reduce sustain talk's persistence.
