# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Jordan's experience, reducing shame and avoiding blame. Acceptance was evident across multiple empathic statements that validated the client's context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician used accurate, frequent reflections that captured both content and feeling and tracked emotional shifts across the session. Nuanced double-sided reflections showed strong perspective-taking and continuity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on securing steadier income and reducing panic, moving from problem description to concrete, achievable steps. Questions and reflections repeatedly returned to actionable planning and contingency strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician solicited Jordan's ideas and integrated his past experiences into a co-constructed plan, making the client an equal partner. Decisions and supports were shaped by Jordan's preferences and practical input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician explicitly reinforced Jordan's choice and control, repeatedly offering options and inviting the client to choose which steps felt realistic. Language avoided pressure and supported ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited Jordan's internal motivation and past strengths by asking about prior persistence and resources. Questions focused on the client's own reasons and durable supports for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was framed as collaborative, with the clinician deferring to Jordan's expertise and co-creating the plan. Power-sharing was evident through repeated invitations for Jordan's input and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced change talk, prompting concrete steps and benefits of change, and affirmed strengths to amplify motivation. There were missed opportunities to more frequently amplify change statements into firmer commitment language and use scaling to deepen confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician used strategies like double-sided reflections and pivoted to practical strategies to avoid reinforcing sustain talk. However, sustain talk recurred and the clinician could have used more selective reflection and summarizing to highlight change language and tip the balance further toward commitment.
