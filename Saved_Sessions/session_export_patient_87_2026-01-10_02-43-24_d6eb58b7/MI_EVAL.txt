### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on evening snacking throughout the session, moving from trigger identification to a specific, time-bound action plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership

**Comments:** Fosters partnership by offering options, practicing aloud, and scheduling follow-up, yet at times privileges clinician expertise through directive scripting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some commitments and ratings but does not consistently deepen or amplify Marco’s own reasons for change, leaning toward offering solutions instead of evoking more change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
