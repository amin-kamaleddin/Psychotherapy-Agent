# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with safety check, normalization, validation, and tailored offers of help demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows clear perspective-taking and accurate responses to concerns, but stops short of deeper emotional reflection or summarizing ambivalence to deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on asthma management and inhaler adherence throughout, offering concrete, actionable steps and explicit follow-up plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative—invites input, tailors recommendations, and co-creates routines and tools—but could more often elicit the patient’s own solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice by offering options and asking preferences, though could strengthen explicit affirmations of patient control and jointly negotiate next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some preferences and tentative commitments but relies heavily on giving information rather than drawing out the patient’s own motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance with repeated checks and co-construction of plans, but occasionally shifts to expert-delivery without explicit negotiation of the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Prompts some change statements and offers practical tools, yet misses opportunities to reflect, amplify, and explore motivation, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and provides useful factual alternatives that reduce sustain talk, but rarely uses double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to pivot toward change.
