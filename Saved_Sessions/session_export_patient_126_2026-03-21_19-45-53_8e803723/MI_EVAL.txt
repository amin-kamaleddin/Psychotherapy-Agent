# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing experimentation and validating the patient's perspective throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking with timely reflections that mirror both feeling and content, creating strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol use and converts discussion into a time-bound, measurable action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True collaborative stance—offers options, invites patient choices (strategy, nights, check-ins), and tailors supports to the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, emphasizing reversibility, and avoiding judgment for imperfect adherence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits a concrete experiment from the patient, but could deepen evocation with more open questions about values, reasons, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is co-created with shared power—clinician proposes options and the patient selects and shapes the plan, demonstrating strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits specific change talk and a concrete plan using low-effort options, though it could strengthen by eliciting more desire/reasons and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and gently reframes sustain talk into low-cost experiments and facts, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence more actively.
