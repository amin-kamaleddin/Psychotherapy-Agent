# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through validating reflections and normalization, avoiding labels or criticism. Repeatedly affirms the client's sensible caution and warmth throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality, accurate empathic understanding with reflections that capture both content and feeling. Mirrors the client's language, signaling close listening and perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on disclosure to peers and repeatedly brings the conversation back to concrete steps. Keeps the interaction goal-directed without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: solicits the client's input, invites joint problem-solving, and frames decisions as shared. Treats the client as an equal partner whose preferences shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and pacing by offering options, asking permission, and validating hesitation. Reinforces self-determination and avoids pressuring the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the client with open invitations, eliciting concrete reasons and strategies and asking about past successes. Resists supplying reasons and pulls motivation from within the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the client as the expert and co-creates the plan through open questions and choice. Language consistently invites the client's preferences and makes the plan clearly hers.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicits and amplifies change talk, asking targeted follow-ups about wording, timing, and steps. Builds on a concrete, doable plan to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and redirects it toward action, reducing reinforcement of resistance. Missed some strategic opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframing sustain talk into preparatory change language.
