# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental support with multiple normalizing statements and explicit praise that validate the patient's experience without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and validation but uses deeper empathic reflections only occasionally, missing chances to explore underlying ambivalence and emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on making health/yoga more consistent, with each intervention tied to the target behavior and goal-directed supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-created the 7-day plan and texts, incorporated patient edits, and iteratively tailored materials to the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by offering options, asking permission before drafting content, and emphasizing the patient's ownership of decisions and restarts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivations and invites planning but often moves quickly to solutions rather than using more evocative questions or scaling to deepen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively and invites input, though at times becomes somewhat directive by offering specific plans quickly instead of eliciting more patient-generated options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk through invitations and validation, but could better amplify and summarize the patient's change language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes lapses, and reframes slips nonjudgmentally while pivoting to practical planning and coping strategies.
