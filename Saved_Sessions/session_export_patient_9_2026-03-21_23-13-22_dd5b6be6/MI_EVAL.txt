# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing distress and affirming efforts. Tone is warm and nonjudgmental throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, emotionally attuned reflections that capture both feeling and content, helping the client feel understood. Reflective listening is consistent and keeps the client engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on increasing the child's bedtime routine throughout the session. Keeps returning to practical, behaviorally specific steps and a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in true two‑way problem solving by soliciting the client's ideas and building on her past successes. Co‑constructs scripts and practices with permission rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly offers choices, checks permission, and affirms acceptable outcomes without pressure. Reinforces that the client controls the steps she will try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client's motivations, values, and past strengths to generate change ideas. Could deepen intrinsic motivation by using scaling questions and prompting the client to summarize reasons or state a commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong power‑sharing language and co‑creation of solutions, inviting input on scripts and follow‑ups. Decisions are guided by the client's preferences and expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and small, doable steps, offering practice and scripts. Missed opportunities include not asking importance/confidence scales or explicitly eliciting a commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responds neutrally to doubts, normalizes concerns, and plans brief, non‑blaming follow‑ups to minimize escalation. Could more actively pivot from sustain talk using double‑sided reflections and selective amplification of change language.
