# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, offering choices and validating the client's priorities. Affirmations and normalizing language supported Nathaniel without shaming or dismissing his perspective.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening that captures both sides of ambivalence and acknowledges barriers like coworker offers and late meetings. Could deepen empathic accuracy by exploring the personal meaning of the morning routine and the client's curiosity about whether change will reduce jitters.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on coffee consumption and moves from exploration to a concrete, measurable plan with follow-up. The clinician keeps direction without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is framed as joint problem-solving with repeated solicitation of the client's preferences and integration of his choices into the plan. Decisions are co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirms the client's choice, offers multiple options, and checks what feels doable while normalizing slip-ups. Language and structure consistently reinforce client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation through open questions, confidence scaling, and choice of supports, drawing out practical reasons to test change. Could strengthen evocation by inviting the client to generate more of his own strategies and amplifying brief self-motivating statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a genuine partnership by offering menus of options, asking which supports to choose, and preparing materials for follow-up as requested. The clinician positions expertise as shared with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and shapes change talk with low-effort experiments, confidence ratings, and barrier/support planning. Missed some opportunities to amplify the client's brief expressions of curiosity or desire to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by proposing plans that preserve the morning ritual and normalizing experiments and slips. Could further soften sustain talk with more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to highlight discrepancy.
