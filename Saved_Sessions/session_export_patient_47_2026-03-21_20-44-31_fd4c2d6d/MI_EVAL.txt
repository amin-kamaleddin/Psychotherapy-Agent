# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, validating ambivalence and asking permission before offering advice.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used strong reflective listening and accurate summaries that matched affect and content, which helped the patient elaborate practical plans.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session clearly focused on alcohol use and fall risk with a logical sequence of goal → options → plan → barriers, maintaining excellent directional control.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the checklist and plan, iterated wording per the patient's requests, and used inclusive language that fostered teamwork and shared problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced autonomy by avoiding coercive language, offering multiple options, asking permission, and letting the patient choose start date and edits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited personal reasons and strategies (independence, son’s worry) but moved fairly quickly into planning; could deepen motivation by exploring values and linking actions to long‑term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear partnership stance—frequently asked for the patient's input, negotiated roles (son’s involvement), and shared expertise in decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked multiple change statements and concrete steps, but could do more to amplify and consolidate change talk into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used neutral redirection and harm‑reduction options, though relied on summaries rather than targeted double‑sided reflections or reframes to more actively soften sustain talk.
