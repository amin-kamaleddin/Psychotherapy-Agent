# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing ambivalence and reducing shame. Tone sometimes feels formulaic with repeated 'I hear you' and would benefit from more varied, specific affirmations of strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening that sustains rapport. Could deepen empathic attunement with more complex, evocative reflections and less repetitive phrasing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on asserting boundaries and getting the flatmate to move out, tying each intervention to concrete steps. Effectively narrows toward action and contingency planning without getting sidetracked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Models an equal partnership by inviting the client's preferences and co‑planning the approach. Suggestions are offered as options and the client's ideas clearly shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and ownership by repeatedly asking what would feel doable and offering options rather than directives. Honors the client's agency and minimizes pressure throughout the interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations and future‑oriented reasons for change, producing clear change talk and commitment language. Could probe deeper values, past successes, and confidence to strengthen durable motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as a joint exploration and privileges the client's expertise, co‑constructing the plan. Regularly checks back and invites the client to choose next steps, supporting true power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk, leading to a specific, implementable plan. Missed opportunities to strategically amplify commitment language and elicit confidence/ability talk to boost implementation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence in ways that prevent escalation. Relies mainly on neutral reflections and normalization rather than targeted double‑sided reflections or eliciting arguments against sustain talk.
