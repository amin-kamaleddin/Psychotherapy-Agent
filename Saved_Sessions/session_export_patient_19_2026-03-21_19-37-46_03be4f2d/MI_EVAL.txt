# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard, normalizes difficulty, thanks the patient, and respects her limits without judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic attunement by acknowledging distress and practical concerns, but misses opportunities for deeper reflective listening about Anya's anxiety, defensiveness, and wistful ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on gambling and financial management and repeatedly advances the session toward concrete behavioral steps and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a cooperative dynamic by inviting choices, role-play, and co-constructing scripts and budgets, though occasionally leans into an expert role when supplying detailed content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by offering multiple options, asking what feels doable, accepting declines (e.g., GA), and offering assistance only by invitation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some actionable change language but relies heavily on offering solutions rather than drawing out Anya's own values, reasons, and confidence; missed chances for scaling questions and amplifying spontaneous change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and sharing control through questions and invitations, though at times the clinician shifts toward providing expert solutions rather than purely co-creating them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits concrete change talk and supports steps with role-play and scripts, but could better amplify intrinsic motivations and elicit confidence and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids debating sustain talk by moving to harm-reduction, but does little active rolling with resistance or use of double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence before planning.
