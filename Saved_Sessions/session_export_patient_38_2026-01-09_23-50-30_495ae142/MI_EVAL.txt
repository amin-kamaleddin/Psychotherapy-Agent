# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Elena's worry, explicitly accepting her pace and avoiding blame; warmth and supportive language were consistent throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking mirrored Elena's language and emotions, tying feelings to values and behavior to show strong attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on contraception and immediate, actionable steps, consistently linking questions to the target behavior and Elena's stated values without being pushy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician worked as an equal partner, inviting Elena's expertise and preferences and co-creating a feasible small experiment together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Options were offered rather than directives, pacing was acknowledged, and the clinician reinforced Elena's ownership of decisions and choice of strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited Elena's own motivations and strategies through open questions and prior-success exploration, prompting self-generated, value-consistent steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was framed as joint problem-solving with repeated invitations for input and shared decision-making, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and translated values into a small-step plan, though could have more strongly amplified change language and probed confidence/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and the clinician pivoted to tiny, manageable actions, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to resolve ambivalence more directly.
