# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Jamal's feelings and avoiding blame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and attunement to Jamal's annoyance and desire to finish, mirroring his brief statements rather than minimizing them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on immediate procedural safety and actionable steps, but did not progress toward addressing longer-term marijuana use.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as a joint effort by offering options, rehearsing lines together, and repeatedly checking Jamal's preferences, creating a clear sense of teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by presenting choices, noting limits (no legal advice), and emphasizing Jamal's control over decisions without using pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Rarely attempted to elicit Jamal's internal motivations or change talk; few open questions or strategic prompts were used to draw out reasons or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through choice and rehearsal and invited input about immediate needs, though could have more explicitly co-created a broader agenda or follow-up plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made minimal effort to elicit intrinsic reasons for changing marijuana use; small change statements were acknowledged practically but not deepened with follow-up questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with minimizations and prevented escalation, but missed opportunities to strategically soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections or eliciting ambivalence.
