# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Isabella’s experience with affirmations and nonjudgmental language, reinforcing strengths and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries demonstrated strong understanding of Isabella’s perspective and emotions, picking up on worry, feeling 'stuck,' and linking these to her strengths.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained clear focus on community engagement and guided the conversation toward concrete, very small action steps (look up events, set a reminder, ask someone to text).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was co-constructed—choices were offered, the clinician solicited Isabella’s input at each decision point, and offered to help draft a one-line message together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician honored Isabella’s choices throughout, offering options rather than directives and affirming that small steps are the client’s to choose, supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited some internal motivation and past supports (friend reminder) and asked about benefits, but could have deepened and amplified Isabella’s tentative change talk and probed confidence/values more.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician acted as a collaborator, repeatedly asking which option felt realistic, deferring to Isabella’s preferences, and jointly planning practical steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively asked for tiny, doable steps and elicited concrete change-language, though there were missed opportunities to amplify tentative statements and elicit stronger commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician validated sustain talk appropriately but at times mirrored it verbatim in ways that may have maintained focus on avoidance; more double-sided reflections or reframing could better tip the balance toward change.
