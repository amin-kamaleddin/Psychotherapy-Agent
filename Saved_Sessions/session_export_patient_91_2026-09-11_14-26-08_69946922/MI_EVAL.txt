# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—normalizes, validates limitations, and affirms small steps without judgment throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking with specific reflections that mirror the patient's language and foster trust; a brief deeper probe into underlying anxiety could strengthen this further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on initiating blood sugar self-monitoring, repeatedly narrowing ambivalence into concrete, actionable steps and specifics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine two-way problem solving by offering practical options, eliciting preferences, and refining the plan based on the patient's responses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking which feels most doable, and preserving agency in enlisting support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivation and solutions, amplifying internal resources and drawing out commitment behaviors rather than supplying reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert and co-creates the plan by inviting choices and checking comfort, resulting in the patient leading decisions and commitments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits, reinforces, and amplifies change talk through open questions, reflective statements, and conversion of intent into specific support-backed actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects to testing, but could use more double-sided reflections and explicit containment of status-quo pull to further weaken resistance.
