# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental—affirmed protective behavior and praised small successes while accepting the patient's pace and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, validating stress and tailoring guided exercises to the patient's context and experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on smoking-related goals and linked stress-relief interventions directly to managing cravings and harm reduction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created a stepped plan, solicited the patient's wording for scripts, and repeatedly adapted interventions to the patient's preferences and context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed choice and control by offering options, asking permission before guiding, and respecting the patient's decisions about treatment timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation (e.g., 'I've thought about quitting,' successful practice lowering urge) but could more systematically draw out personal reasons, values, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated true partnership by inviting preferences, following the patient's lead to focus on stress relief, and shaping interventions based on the patient's input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generated and reinforced change language and a small plan, but missed opportunities to deepen change talk with importance/confidence scaling or elaboration of earlier statements about quitting.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not strengthen sustain talk, offering harm-reduction instead, but could have used double-sided reflections and gentle reframing to pivot sustain talk toward change.
