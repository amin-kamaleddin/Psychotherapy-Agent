# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintains a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful stance and normalizes ambivalence, accepting Milan's disclaimers without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening are evident through paraphrases that capture both content and feeling and by mirroring the patient's language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on reducing drinking at social gatherings and repeatedly narrows the conversation toward a single, actionable next-weekend plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is a genuine collaborative problem-solving exchange, with the clinician soliciting Milan's ideas and co-constructing a realistic plan tailored to his context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently affirms Milan's right to choose and avoids pressure, explicitly framing the plan as something Milan can shape and accept or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicits Milan's own reasons, options, and concrete experiment (soda, line, Mark), leading to client-generated solutions rather than imposed advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician fosters a power-sharing stance by inviting Milan to pick the most doable step and by checking preferences rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits plentiful, concrete change talk and asks for a single doable step, but misses opportunities to explore deeper motivations, values, and confidence levels.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk into low-stakes experiments, though they could more explicitly use double-sided reflections and probe the perceived benefits of drinking.
