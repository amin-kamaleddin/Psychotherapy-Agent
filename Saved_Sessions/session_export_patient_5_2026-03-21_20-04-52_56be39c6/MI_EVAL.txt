# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened with explicit acceptance and nonjudgment, affirmed the patient's caution, and maintained respectful, noncritical language throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used validating responses and reflections about safety concerns and worries, but offered relatively few deeper or complex reflections exploring underlying emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on medication safety (washout timing, serotonin syndrome, QT/ECG) and reinforced concrete next steps like scripts and role‑play.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑created multiple script versions, adapted to patient requests, asked clarifying questions, and offered role‑play, demonstrating genuine shared problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's autonomy by offering options, avoiding pressure to change meds, and advising confirmation with prescriber/pharmacist.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited engagement through practical prompts (scripts, role‑play) and factual questions but did not draw out the patient's own motivations, values, or readiness to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and tailored to the patient's input, but the clinician did not explicitly highlight the patient's expertise or invite her to set priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provided actionable supports and invites to act (scripts, role‑play) but did not systematically elicit or amplify the patient's own change talk about reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by redirecting to safety‑focused options and practical guidance, though used fewer strategic MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to gently reframe ambivalence.
