# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental, respectful language, normalizing slips and offering low-pressure options. Supported the patient's choice to only track and framed suggestions as optional.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and accurate paraphrase of the patient's situation and plans. Could deepen emotional resonance by exploring underlying feelings and ambivalence rather than focusing mainly on logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on coffee consumption and monitoring, setting a concrete 7-day tracking plan and specific review targets. The session stayed on-task without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with options offered and decisions co-constructed; clinician provided technical help only after the patient requested it. Balanced clinician expertise with the patient's preferences throughout planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported patient choice by offering options, respecting the decision to track only, and using a confidence scaling question to elicit self-directed solutions. Emphasized nonjudgment and control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation (confidence rating, behavioral experiment) but focused mostly on logistics and feasibility rather than drawing out personally meaningful reasons for change. Could use more open, values‑focused questions to mobilize intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine power-sharing by inviting choices and following the patient's lead (e.g., offering but waiting to provide a shortcut walkthrough). Positioned the patient as the expert of his own life and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced specific commitment to a 7-day tracking experiment and concrete implementation plans. Missed opportunities to amplify change talk about reducing consumption or to link concerns (e.g., BP) to personal values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce sustain talk, offering low-burden experiments and normalizing lapses. Could more actively pivot sustain talk toward change with double-sided or evocative reflections.
