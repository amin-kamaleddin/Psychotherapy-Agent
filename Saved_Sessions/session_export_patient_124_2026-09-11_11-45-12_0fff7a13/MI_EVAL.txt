# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and avoids judgment or lecturing while affirming the patient's autonomy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows accurate reflective listening and perspective‑taking but rarely names underlying emotions or offers deeper affective reflections to validate the difficulty of change.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with concrete, incremental steps and consistently redirects to actionable planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner by eliciting the patient's ideas, co‑constructing the plan, and avoiding unsolicited directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice, offers options, and frames change as a low‑pressure experiment to reinforce the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own solutions and plans but does not probe broader values, confidence/importance scaling, or link plans to longer‑term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustains a collaborative stance by asking for preferences, incorporating patient solutions, and framing expertise as residing with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and deepens tentative change talk into specific plans, but misses opportunities to elicit confidence/importance ratings or tie motivation to values for stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes goals as a pause, but could more actively use double‑sided reflections and selectively amplify doubts to build discrepancy.
