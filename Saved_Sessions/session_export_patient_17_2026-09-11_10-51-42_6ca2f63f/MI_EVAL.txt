# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating the patient's experience, offering options rather than directives and normalizing caution after slips.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and acknowledged ambivalence and shame, though could have deepened affect-focused reflections to explore the patient's intense feelings of shame more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on smoking cessation and moved progressively to concrete planning (one-week NRT trial, morning texts, brief check-ins, slip reset).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the interaction as a joint enterprise, co-creating the plan, offering to prepare materials while leaving final decisions to the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and preference, offered multiple options, respected the patient's wish not to involve her daughter, and volunteered to be the calm contact.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the patient by eliciting her own solutions and operationalizing them into a concrete plan, repeatedly asking her to choose and refine preferences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert, invited her choices, and emphasized power-sharing through co-creation of texts, calls, and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and summarized the patient's plan, but could more explicitly consolidate commitment language (e.g., confidence/importance ruler or a brief verbal commitment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validated sustain talk and redirected toward safe, manageable steps (one-week trial, non-shaming slip plan) without arguing or reinforcing reasons to stay the same.
