# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and affirming with frequent normalizing statements and no blaming language. Could add explicit normalization of ambivalence to be even stronger.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid empathic reflections and attends to emotional tone (fear, shaking, guilt). Missed some opportunities for deeper, feeling‑labeled or double‑sided reflections when sustain‑talk revealed identity/friendship concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy with concrete, behavior‑specific interventions and agenda‑setting questions. Role‑play and action steps reinforced the target without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tone is collaborative and the clinician co‑constructs a plan with Sofia and role‑plays per her request. However, the clinician often supplies scripts and options rather than eliciting Sofia’s own ideas first, which limits deeper co‑creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, and framing options nonpressuringly throughout the session. Clinician consistently preserves Sofia’s agency even when providing concrete suggestions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Sofia’s motivations and resources (rulers, doable plan elements, role‑play) and builds plans around her stated reasons for change. Practices and prompts cultivate Sofia’s own voice rather than imposing clinician reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance with invitations to choose and practice, sharing control in many moments. Some prescriptive moves (detailed scripts/plans) slightly shifted expertise back to the clinician instead of fully co‑creating wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk well (0–10 rulers, asking what feels doable, prompting concrete steps), producing clear statements of intent. Could amplify and summarize change talk more and ask follow‑ups to move from desire/reasons to ability and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects it into workable strategies that preserve social ties (mocktails, alternative plans). Tends to move quickly into planning rather than first exploring the emotional meaning of sustain reasons—double‑sided or feeling‑labeled reflections could soften ambivalence more strategically.
