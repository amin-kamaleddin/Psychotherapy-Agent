### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Mei’s experience with nonjudgmental language. Repeatedly acknowledges doubts without criticism, supporting a top rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and mirrors the client's language and emotions, summarizing both content and affect. Reflections validate ambivalence and help Mei feel heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing risky drinking and improving contraception, steering conversation toward specific, actionable steps. Regularly checks feasibility and keeps momentum toward behavior change.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Conducts the session as a true collaboration, co-creating the plan and deferring to Mei’s preferences. Language and offers (drafting text, mapping plan) emphasize joint work and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options and asking permission before acting. Avoids pressuring language and affirms that partial adherence is acceptable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
