# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard—validated Andrei's time pressure and honored his requests (no voicemail, one-line email). These behaviors conveyed respect and acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking by naming pressures and practical concerns. Some reflections were a bit formulaic and missed deeper affective exploration of meaning or emotional burden.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on specific target behaviors with a measurable 1-week plan and scheduled check-in. The clinician avoided drifting into unrelated topics, supporting effective brief MI.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True collaboration: the clinician solicited Andrei's ideas and co-constructed the plan and logistics. Decisions were negotiated and tailored to the patient's preferences rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice by offering options, asking which felt doable, and accepting limits (micro-moves, no alarms). Reinforced that the plan was the patient's decision, strengthening personal control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used evocation techniques (readiness ruler, asking what felt doable) to elicit actionable change talk and a self-generated plan. Could have probed more for intrinsic motives, values, and confidence to deepen internal motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative with repeated invitations for input and adjustments to the plan (e.g., 'Pause' cue, no voicemail). Power was clearly shared between clinician and patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk via readiness assessment, feasibility checks, and a tiny, actionable plan. To reach a top score, the clinician could have further explored reasons, benefits, and linked change to values to amplify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Appropriately avoided arguing with sustain talk, acknowledged barriers, and reframed missed days as data rather than failure. Missed opportunities remain for explicit double-sided reflections or eliciting the patient's own counterarguments to sustain talk.
