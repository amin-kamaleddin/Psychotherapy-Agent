# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—praises effort, normalizes difficulty, and links strengths to values without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries, timely reflections, and open questions that deepen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use with behaviorally specific goals and stepwise moves toward a concrete, implementable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as joint problem-solving—clinician treats the patient as an active contributor, adapts suggestions, and invites practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking what feels doable, and avoiding pressuring language while acknowledging limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient by linking change to values and past successes and using scaling with a follow-up to elicit internal reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—asks permission, offers options, and co-constructs the plan with frequent checks to ensure patient ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk through choices, tightening the plan, and using a confidence scale with a follow-up to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change, but missed opportunities for more explicit double-sided or amplified reflections to soften the sustain side.
