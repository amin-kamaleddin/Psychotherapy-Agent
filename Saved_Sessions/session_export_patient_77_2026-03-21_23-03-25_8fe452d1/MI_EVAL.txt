# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Liyana’s experience, reducing shame. Warmth and validation are persistent and specific to the client’s situation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately naming emotions and ambivalence. Reflections invite gentle exploration and convey understanding throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on a concrete target behavior and repeatedly steers toward achievable steps. Avoids topic drift and links planning to the client’s goals and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently solicits the client’s input and co-creates the plan, drafting text and offering options together. Language and actions demonstrate genuine shared problem-solving and mutual influence.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports choice and minimizes coercion by offering multiple options and asking which feels most doable. Respects the client’s pace and hesitations while affirming her control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits desired outcomes and past successes but stops short of deepening intrinsic motivation. Missed opportunities to explore underlying values, scale importance/confidence, and consolidate commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as an equal partnership with collaborative language and repeated invitations for the client to choose. The clinician shapes the plan from the client’s ideas, demonstrating power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces several instances of change talk and concrete planning (text, timer, reward). Could have amplified and consolidated stronger commitment language via scaling or asking for a verbal commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk empathetically and redirects toward change without arguing, offering alternatives and reframes. Would benefit from more double-sided or summary reflections and explicit reframe of plateaus to reduce self-blame.
