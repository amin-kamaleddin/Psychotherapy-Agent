# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard, normalized Elena’s conflict, and affirmed protective behaviors in a warm, nonjudgmental way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking were used throughout, with complex reflections that matched Elena’s language and helped her elaborate her concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on parental smoking and exposure reduction and progressed logically to a concrete, time-limited behavioral test (nicotine gum) with practical planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently co-created the plan, solicited Elena’s input, and used inclusive language to integrate her ideas into a mutually developed, realistic strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly reinforced by framing the gum trial as Elena’s controlled, no‑pressure test and by validating her choice to stop or not commit to quitting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited Elena’s own motivations and ideas (e.g., prior success with gum and reasons to protect her child) but could deepen and consolidate motivation into stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The exchange was collaborative and deferential to Elena’s expertise, with repeated invitations to plan together and multiple checks about what felt realistic for her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was successfully elicited (concrete plans to test gum and past success), though the clinician could have amplified and reflected Elena’s change language more forcefully to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed as legitimate coping needs without argument, but more explicit pivoting (stronger double-sided reflections or summaries highlighting change language) could further weaken sustain arguments.
