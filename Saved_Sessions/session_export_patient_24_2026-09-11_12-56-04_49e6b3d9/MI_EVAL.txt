### Empathy

**Comments:** Shows accurate reflective listening and pragmatic perspective-taking but relies more on cognitive than affective reflections and misses some emotional nuance (e.g., surprise or defensiveness).

### Direction

### Collaboration

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
