# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, supportive regard and affirmed the patient's efforts, avoiding labeling or shaming while normalizing mixed feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and validation of situational constraints were evident, but the clinician missed opportunities for deeper affective reflections on social anxiety, loneliness, and breakup-related vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the clear target of reducing weekday drinking and progressively narrowed to concrete steps (specific nights, reminders, wording) without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative, with the clinician co-constructing the plan, offering to draft language together, and inviting rehearsals so decisions emerged mutually.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offered choices and asked which option felt most doable, reinforcing the patient's ownership and refraining from prescribing outcomes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively drew out intrinsic motivations and concrete strategies, but could have probed importance/confidence more explicitly (e.g., scaling questions) to strengthen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language and actions demonstrated power-sharing and respect for the patient's expertise, allowing her ideas to drive the plan and fostering a true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Open questions and reflections elicited repeated, specific change talk that the clinician amplified into actionable commitments and concrete implementation steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician redirected sustain talk toward practical supports without confronting it, but could have used more double-sided reflections and explicit normalization of ambivalence.
