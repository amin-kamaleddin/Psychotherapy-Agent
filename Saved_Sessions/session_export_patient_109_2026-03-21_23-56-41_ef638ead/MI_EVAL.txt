# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalizing statements and affirmations, validating constraints without blame in a warm, nonjudgmental tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking with precise paraphrases and summaries that track ambivalence and invite exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on returning to exercise with each question and reflection linked to concrete next steps (scheduling, reminders, one-week experiment).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-constructs the plan by inviting Rafael's input at decision points, checking acceptance, and framing solutions as jointly explored.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces choice and control by offering options, framing the plan as a low-pressure experiment, and avoiding pressure or expert-driven directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits personal values and practical change ideas but could further probe past successes, confidence/importance ratings, and use more evocative reflections to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as an equal collaborator by offering choices, asking which option feels realistic, and deferring to his preferences and pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces several pieces of change talk and plans, yet misses opportunities to amplify and summarize change talk to build stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Neutralizes sustain talk with normalization and low-pressure framing effectively, but could better use double-sided reflections and strategic redirection to shift ambivalence toward change.
