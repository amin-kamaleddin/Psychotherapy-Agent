# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone (open invitation, repeated reassurance). Avoids labeling or criticizing and normalizes ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking, mirroring Jordan’s concerns about side effects, work, and protecting grandparents. Could deepen rapport with more complex reflections that probe emotional content behind brief answers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the vaccination goal and steadily moves the conversation toward concrete planning (scheduling, tips, confidence rating). Guides without coercion and repeatedly brings discussion back to actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as a true collaboration: clinician invites Jordan’s input, co-creates tools (checklist, text), and responds to his preferences. Decisions are negotiated rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly affirms Jordan’s right to choose with statements like 'No pressure' and offers options about information and timing. Supports ownership of the plan by letting Jordan choose wording and appointment time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits intrinsic motivation by linking vaccination to protecting grandparents and asking what would increase confidence. Could strengthen evocation by prompting more of Jordan’s own language about personal meaning and asking him to summarize his reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine partnership: asks what to focus on, requests permission before giving information, and co-creates practical solutions. Power is shared and Jordan’s expertise about his life is respected.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evokes and reinforces change talk around protecting others and concrete planning, using a confidence scale and eliciting a specific appointment. To reach higher, the clinician could have amplified and reflected Jordan’s change statements more explicitly to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and provides targeted information to reduce concerns, but relies more on information provision than strategic MI techniques. Could use double-sided reflections or reframes to explicitly integrate ambivalence and nudge toward resolution.
