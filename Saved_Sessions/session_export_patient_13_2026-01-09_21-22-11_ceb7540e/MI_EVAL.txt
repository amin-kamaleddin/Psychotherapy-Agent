### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, validating the patient's fear and normalizing distress while checking safety respectfully, creating a safe atmosphere for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and reflective listening with accurate reflections and pacing, including offering grounding and one-line-at-a-time role play to match the patient's distress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on getting a medical assessment, guiding the patient through concrete steps (scripts, role-play, triage transfer) toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partnership by providing expertise while centering the patient's preferences, co-creating wording, and pausing for patient input during practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the interaction as collaborative, asking permission to role-play, offering choices, and co-practicing language with the patient to share power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
