### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, acknowledged concerns and affirmed goals (e.g., normalizing ambivalence and accepting his choice about mom). Conveyed unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and validations that tracked Alexei’s feelings and perspective (e.g., paraphrasing the event and labeling ambivalence). These summaries helped him feel heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on adolescent alcohol use and linked interventions directly to soccer and scholarship goals. Guided toward concrete, actionable strategies (refusal scripts, safe-ride, first step selection) without losing client-centeredness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a cooperative partner, co-creating plans and inviting Alexei’s input (role-plays, wording tweaks, Plan B). Adapted suggestions to his preferences and prioritized his expertise about his social context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, asked permission before advising, and invited Alexei to choose strategies, respecting his wish to avoid involving his mom. Reinforced ownership by asking which option felt most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited Alexei’s own reasons for change and used his goals to fuel motivation (open questions about desired differences, confidence ruler). Leveraged personal motivations to build a client-driven plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance with power-sharing practices (asking which refusal lines felt like him, inviting role-play). Encouraged joint problem-solving and honored his choices throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language, translated reasons into action (practicing refusals, Plan B), and highlighted goal-consistent motivations tied to scholarships and soccer. Strengthened commitment by asking for concrete first steps and confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected toward change without reinforcing it, but sometimes moved quickly to solutions. Could increase use of double-sided reflections and explicitly preserve client language when exploring minimizations.
