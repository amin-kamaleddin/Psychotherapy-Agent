# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized pacing, repeatedly affirming the client's choices and validating goals in a warm, nonjudgmental way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that captured ambivalence and patterns, with timely checks that demonstrated strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on cutting back (not abstinence) throughout the session, using rulers and gathering specifics to end with a concrete, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner—co‑created strategies, role‑played, and continually solicited the client's preferences and tweaks to plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supported the client's sense of choice by asking permission, offering multiple realistic options, and framing strategies as testable experiments under the client's control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation with rulers and doable commitments, but sometimes led with a menu of options rather than first drawing out the client's own reasons and values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert by inviting input, asking permission before advising, and jointly developing scripts and contingency plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change language and used rulers, yet often shifted to offering strategies instead of strategically amplifying and summarizing the client's own change statements to strengthen them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it, but did limited active work to soften or reframe those arguments (few double‑sided reflections or pivots toward costs).
