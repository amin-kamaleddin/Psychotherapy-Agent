# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms progress without judging or shaming the patient.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately naming emotions and contextual factors that drive the patient's drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in social settings and links discussion to broader health/family goals while moving logically from exploration to concrete strategy and a slip‑plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—plans and refusal lines are co-created and the clinician actively solicits and incorporates the patient's input at each step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports the patient's sense of choice and control by asking permission, offering multiple options, and emphasizing that the patient chooses what to lock in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's own reasons for change using open questions, scaling, and invitations to generate and refine personal strategies and commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the session as a joint effort, deferring to the patient's expertise and co-constructing a realistic plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, reinforces past successes to boost self-efficacy, uses scaling questions, and invites specific strategy selection and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and skillfully redirects to change, though there were modest missed opportunities to use explicit double-sided reflections to deepen discrepancy.
