# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard—avoids admonishment, honors boundaries, and normalizes slips. Repeated affirmations convey respect and acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, empathic listening with content reflections that show understanding of Marco's situation. Missed opportunities for deeper, multilayered reflections of resignation, fatigue, and guarded humor.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on a specific smoking behavior with measurable targets, start date, barriers, and contingency plans. Persistent but low-pressure steering toward actionable change was well executed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaboration—invites Marco’s input at every step, defers to his preferences, and integrates his choices into the plan. Language and decision-making are co-creative and mutually negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by emphasizing choice, asking permission, and respecting explicit limits (no lectures, no partner involvement). Reinforces that the plan belongs to Marco and normalizes slips.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses open questions and a confidence ruler to elicit reasons and commitments, producing concrete change talk. Could more systematically draw out intrinsic motivators and amplify tentative change language before shifting to planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats Marco as a collaborator and expert on his life, offering options and asking which fits best. Co-constructs a specific, patient-selected plan that Marco drives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk effectively (permission, options, confidence scaling) leading to concrete commitments. Would benefit from pausing to amplify and deepen Marco’s reasons and imagined outcomes before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by rolling with resistance, avoiding argument, and honoring past failures and limits. Missed chances to use double-sided or shifting reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and pivot toward change.
