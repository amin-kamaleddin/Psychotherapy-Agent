# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental tone, validates the client's burden, and affirms efforts without blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good reflective listening and perspective-taking and accommodates the client's nerves, but misses opportunities for deeper emotional reflection of vivid shame/stigma language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking cessation with concrete steps, timelines, and follow-up actions, keeping the session consistently goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a true partnership by co-designing the plan, role-playing, soliciting preferences, and adjusting the plan to fit the client's life and schedule.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, checking permission before planning, and repeatedly inviting the client to select next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation using reasons and readiness/confidence scaling, but could more systematically draw out deeper intrinsic motives and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares expertise with the client, asks permission, and defers to the client's preferences while jointly developing and rehearsing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change-oriented language and translates it into concrete steps, though it could more actively elicit and strengthen explicit commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers and validates constraints but uses few strategic MI techniques (double-sided reflections, reframes) to intentionally soften sustain talk before moving to planning.
