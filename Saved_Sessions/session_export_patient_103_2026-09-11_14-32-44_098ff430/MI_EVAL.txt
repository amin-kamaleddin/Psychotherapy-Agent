# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing the client's priorities and avoiding judgment. Validated time pressures and treated choices as reasonable.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used strong perspective-taking and concise reflections that likely calmed the client, but missed opportunities for deeper affective reflections of the client's anger and its intensity. More complex reflections could have strengthened emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on managing reactions to delays with practical, actionable solutions. Avoided tangents and efficiently moved toward a specific plan and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as joint problem-solving with options offered and decisions co-constructed. The clinician and client worked together to refine wording, rehearsal, and contingency plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and avoided coercion, asking which options fit the client's routine and offering help only if wanted. Validated the client's decisions while preserving ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete motivations and commitments but relied largely on presented options rather than open-ended exploration of values, prior successes, or confidence. Could increase elicitation of internal reasons and self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the client as the decision-maker by asking which wording and strategies felt most doable. Power-sharing was evident in invitations to choose and rehearse.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and strengthened change talk through choice questions, rehearsal prompts, and affirmations, leading to concrete commitments (10-minute line, car rehearsal, reschedule plan). The sequence moved from idea to specific action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Did not join system-blame or argue; acknowledged frustrations and reframed the interaction toward practical strategies that reduced rumination. Redirected sustain talk into actionable steps without dismissing concerns.
