**Comments:** Initial collaboration was good (eliciting week and allowing choice of step) but the clinician shifted to an expert/advice role, prescribing scripts and strategies rather than co‑constructing them.

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Demonstrated respect for patient choice and self‑directed experimentation; somewhat undermined by repeated directive instructions without explicit invitation to adapt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
- [x] Elicited factual information (typical week) and a chosen step
- [x] Asked confidence (evoked self-assessment)
- [ ] Did not sufficiently draw out patient’s own reasons, values, or past successes

**Comments:** Some evocation present (assessment and confidence rating) but limited exploration of intrinsic motivation and reasons for change before moving to problem‑solving.

---

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
- [x] Positioned some tasks as joint (mapping, picking a step)
- [ ] Predominantly shifted to an expert/prescriber role (offered scripts/instructions)
- [ ] Did not consistently solicit patient wording or rehearsal

**Comments:** Occasional partnership moves but overall leaned toward directing and scripting rather than sustained collaborative co‑construction.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
- [x] Elicited some change talk (regret about injury; commitment to try Wednesday)
- [ ] Did not amplify or strategically elicit additional change talk
- [ ] Missed follow‑up to strengthen commitment and confidence after elicitation

**Comments:** Produced initial change talk and a commitment, but clinician moved quickly into logistics instead of eliciting and amplifying the patient’s own motivations and strengths.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
- [x] Avoided arguing with sustain talk (did not confront “drinking helps me relax”)
- [x] Acknowledged ambivalence
- [ ] Did not use double‑sided reflections or decisional balance to actively soften sustain talk

**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and not challenged, but the clinician did not strategically explore both sides to reduce resistance; session progressed to tactical planning rather than resolving ambivalence.
