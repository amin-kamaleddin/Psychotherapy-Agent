# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, including supportive safety checks and affirmations; warmth and respect were evident throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and reflective listening with summaries and checks, but missed opportunities for deeper, affect-focused reflections about shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol use in team settings with targeted, behaviorally relevant strategies (scripts, rehearsals, sober-buddy and exit planning) that moved the session toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled genuine teamwork through role-play, joint problem-solving, and negotiated planning, creating a cooperative, inclusive interaction rather than a top-down approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered choices, asked permission, and affirmed decisions, but occasionally provided scripted advice without first eliciting the patient's own wording, which could reduce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited change talk via rehearsal and alignment with team values, but could increase evocation by asking more open questions, using scaling, and prompting the patient to summarize personal reasons and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively by inviting practice and preferences and responding as a teammate, though supplying many ready-made lines rather than eliciting the patient's own formulations slightly limited partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change language through rehearsal and affirmation, but would strengthen impact by amplifying motivations and eliciting explicit commitment or confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to practical change strategies and rehearsals, though more double-sided reflections could better acknowledge ambivalence while guiding toward change.
