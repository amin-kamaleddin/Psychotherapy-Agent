# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard and affirms autonomy without judgment, explicitly stating acceptance of Hana's choices. Acknowledges values (independence, social life) and frames suggestions respectfully.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with clear summaries of patterns and concerns. Reflections are somewhat repetitive and could probe deeper emotional content or underlying values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, steady focus on smoking during shifts and moves logically from options to a concrete implementation plan. Keeps the session action-oriented with specific supports identified.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with inclusive language and frequent invitations for Hana's input. Longer clinician summaries and reframes sometimes create a slightly clinician-led rhythm.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and avoids pressuring language, repeatedly asking Hana which small step feels doable. Reinforces self-direction by having Hana select supports and the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Hana's ideas, past success, and concrete planning, drawing on internal motivation. Misses opportunities for deeper evocation such as importance/confidence rulers or an explicit commitment question.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co-constructs the plan and invites Hana's expertise, reinforcing a joint approach. Occasional clinician-assumptive endorsements shift the voice slightly toward the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and strengthens change talk by asking for options, specifics, and past successes, and by supporting incremental steps. Hana articulates concrete change language and a chosen small step as a result.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes patterns to reduce defensiveness. Could further soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections or selective amplification when sustain language appears.
