# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and validation, honoring the patient's biomedical priorities without blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used empathic reflections and validated the patient's fear and functional impact, but did not consistently deepen exploration of affect or emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, patient-centered focus on obtaining timely diagnostic evaluation and provided concrete, actionable steps and materials to pursue testing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-produced messages, scripts, and a tracker while repeatedly inviting choices, working as a partner rather than an authoritarian expert.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's right to choose order and content of care, asking permission and offering options without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited little of the patient's internal motivation or personal reasons for change, relying mainly on logistical supports rather than exploring values or goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited input and shared power in decision-making, deferring to the patient's priorities and co-creating practical solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Missed opportunities to follow up and amplify tentative change statements or explore desire, ability, reasons, and need for behavioral changes beyond tracking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and escalation of sustain talk but did not strategically use double-sided reflections or reframing to shift sustain talk toward change.
