# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard, normalized struggle, and validated Aisha’s feelings while acknowledging constraints and safety; warm, nonjudgmental language throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and validation with pragmatic reflections that addressed emotions and concerns; could use more complex/deeper reflections to explore shame, social avoidance, and underlying meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on initiating movement with coherent, concrete action planning (tiny goal, scheduling, if‑then scripts, pain rules, and check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Felt like joint problem‑solving: clinician solicited input, adapted recommendations to knee soreness, and invited Aisha to set alarms and scripts so decisions were co‑constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently supported choice by offering alternatives, emphasizing patient responsibility, and avoiding coercive language; reinforced Aisha’s sense of control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited specific commitments and framed the plan as an experiment, but relied heavily on clinician‑provided strategies and missed chances to draw out values, past successes, and deeper intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership by asking preferences and inviting co‑creation, but often presented clinician‑generated plans rather than prompting Aisha to generate more of her own options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change‑oriented language and converted desires into concrete behaviors and plans; could further elicit reasons, importance, and confidence language to amplify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively minimized sustain talk by normalizing doubt and reframing barriers into actionable experiments, though occasional prescriptive elements risked reintroducing subtle resistance.
