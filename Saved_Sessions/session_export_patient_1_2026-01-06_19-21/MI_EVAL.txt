# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys clear unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validates the patient's views, and repeatedly affirms readiness without shaming or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate understanding with timely reflections and normalization, listens well and tailors information to the patient's stated preference for facts over lectures.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on mini-cigar use and oral irritation, progresses logically to a concrete short-term harm-reduction plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—solicits preferences, co-creates a simple checklist and reminder, offers options rather than mandates, and invites a week‑end check-in.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice throughout, asks permission before giving information, respects the patient's decisions, and avoids pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivations and a behavioral commitment but does not deeply explore internal values, discrepancies, or use scaling to probe importance and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters collaboration and mutual problem‑solving but at times moves into clinician-led advice instead of eliciting more of the patient's own ideas and strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk and offers options, yet could more consistently probe for the patient's reasons, importance/confidence, and amplify change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes sustain talk while redirecting toward change, but could more often use double-sided reflections and emphasize even small change statements to tip ambivalence.
