### Empathy

### Direction

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors choice and offers multiple low-pressure options, avoids pressuring language, and accepts declinations while proposing alternatives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses some evocation tools (what appeals, scales, tiny steps) but does not consistently draw out Isabella's internal motivations, values, or personal reasons for change before shifting to planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently asks for preferences and co-creates the plan, checking comfort and edits. At times the clinician is somewhat expert-driven with many templates, leaving room to further elevate client ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates opportunities for change talk and offers concrete steps, but rarely follows up to elicit and amplify reasons, values, or stronger commitment language before moving to logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledges and reframes sustain talk, normalizing anxiety and proposing stepped, lower-pressure options. Could increase use of double-sided reflections to highlight ambivalence and tilt toward change.
