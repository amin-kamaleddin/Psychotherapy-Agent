### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation for the narrow communication change and small commitments, but did not deeply explore intrinsic reasons, values, or broader goals to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified small change talk and co-created a concrete behavioral experiment, though could have probed deeper reasons for change and used scaling/confidence questions to bolster commitment.
