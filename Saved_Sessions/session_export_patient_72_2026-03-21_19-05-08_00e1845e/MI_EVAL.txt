# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating the patient's choices and treating preferences and limits as legitimate throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows solid empathic understanding with accurate reflections and validations, but uses few complex or affect-focused reflections to deepen emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on restarting sustainable physical activity, tying every intervention to concrete, behaviorally specific steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as a joint effort by soliciting preferences, using role-play, and inviting feedback, creating a true two-way interaction shaped by the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers options, asks which interventions the patient prefers, and avoids pressuring language, consistently reinforcing the patient's right to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits concrete motivation and commitment (scheduling, calls, routines) but could use more evocative questions and reflections to draw out deeper personal motives and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and invites input and role-play, though at times offers multiple specific actions without always prefacing them with permission or eliciting the patient's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and commitments through planning and prompts, but could amplify expressed values and use follow-ups to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes barriers into manageable options, though would benefit from more double-sided reflections or explicit normalization of ambivalence.
