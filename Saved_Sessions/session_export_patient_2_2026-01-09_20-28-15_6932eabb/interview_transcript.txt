PSYCHIATRIST: Nicotine replacement — quick brochure

Patch
- Provides steady nicotine. Common strengths: 21/14/7 mg (varies by country).
- Apply each morning to clean, dry, hairless skin; rotate sites. Follow product timing.

Gum/Lozenges
- Gum: 2 mg or 4 mg. Chew slowly until tingling, “park” between gum and cheek, repeat 30 min.
- Lozenges: dissolve slowly; avoid eating/drinking 15 min before/after.

Combo
- Patch + gum/lozenge for strong cravings.

Side effects
- Patch: skin irritation, sleep changes. Gum: jaw ache, indigestion. Lozenges: throat irritation.

Precautions
- Ask GP/pharmacist about dose, pregnancy/breastfeeding, recent heart problems, meds. NRT safer than smoking but get advice.
