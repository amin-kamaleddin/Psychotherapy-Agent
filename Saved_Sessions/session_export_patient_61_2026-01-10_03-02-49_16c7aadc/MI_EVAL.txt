# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance with frequent normalizations and affirmations of small steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, mirroring the patient's overwhelm and constraints with concise paraphrases and validations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a tight focus on performing blood sugar checks at work and advanced toward a concrete, measurable one-week plan (time, alarms, kit, backup).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, solicited patient input at each decision point, and co-constructed the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the patient's choice and avoided coercion by offering options, accepting reluctance, and asking which approach felt most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical commitments and used a confidence ruler, but focused mainly on logistics and could more directly draw out personal values and deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear collaborative stance, invited preferences, and shared decision-making in selecting specific times, backups, and strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change statements (alarms, kit, times) but missed opportunities to deepen and amplify intrinsic motivation or summarize the patient's reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and offered practical solutions, though could further weaken resistance by exploring underlying values or reframing sustaining arguments.
