# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating the patient's experience and normalizing concerns to create safety and respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate brief reflections that acknowledged frustration and goals, but missed opportunities for deeper affective reflections about emotional meaning and social anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on adolescent drinking and immediate harm reduction with concrete, actionable steps and a closing checklist, maintaining strong directional focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like genuine teamwork: the clinician invited choices, rehearsed lines together, and co-wrote and adapted the sober-buddy text to the patient's wording preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice by offering options, using readiness scaling, and emphasizing the patient's control over plans and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited meaningful change talk and concrete goals but relied more on offering strategies than systematically drawing out the patient's own reasons, values, and confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance by offering choices and co-creating plans, though at times the clinician moved into a more instructive mode by providing long lists before eliciting the patient's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change-oriented statements (readiness ruler, choices, rehearsals, drafting text), but could further amplify spontaneous change talk with more open questions and reflective summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and softened sustain talk with normalization and neutral strategies, though used few double-sided or complex reflections to explicitly bind sustain and change talk.
