### Empathy

**Comments:** Accurate, emotionally attuned reflections capture both Amina’s values and worries, demonstrating skilled perspective‑taking and emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on a concrete behavioral goal (a short, safe handover and related messages), with each intervention tied to that target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
