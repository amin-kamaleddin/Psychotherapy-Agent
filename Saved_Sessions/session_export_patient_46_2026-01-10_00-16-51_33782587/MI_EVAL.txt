# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, respectful regard and normalized ambivalence throughout the session, with no labeling or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries that helped Ethan feel understood, but reflections largely stayed at the content/behavior level with missed opportunities to name deeper emotions or internal conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear, appropriate focus on adolescent drinking and safety, with questions and interventions tightly linked to harm-reduction and a concrete implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician and Ethan co-constructed the plan, rehearsed lines, and negotiated contingencies, positioning the clinician as a teammate and resource rather than an authority figure.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently asked permission, offered multiple options, and confirmed Ethan's control over the plan, fostering a strong sense of choice and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician drew out Ethan's motivations and solutions using scaling questions and planning, but could probe personal values and feelings more to strengthen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician treated Ethan as the expert on his life, invited his input, and shared power in decision-making and planning, demonstrating strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced clear change talk and specific steps, including rehearsals, but could have amplified and consolidated Ethan's own change language more frequently to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk, but did not consistently deepen exploration or strategically reframe minimizations to further soften sustain talk.
