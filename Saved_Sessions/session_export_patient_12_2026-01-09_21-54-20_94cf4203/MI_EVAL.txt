### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits some reasons for change and imagined consequences of small steps, prompting change-oriented language. Could further deepen evocation by exploring confidence, past successes, and values to move toward commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveys partnership by sharing control, offering choices, and inviting co-creation of plans (e.g., drafting a text together). The clinician treats Amira as the expert on what feels tolerable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Recognizes and elicits small-step change talk but misses opportunities to systematically amplify it with importance/confidence follow-ups and to build toward commitment language. More strategic follow-up questions would strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk respectfully and avoids arguing, but often mirrors sustain statements without using double-sided reflections, discrepancy, or reframing to weaken sustain talk's hold. More active softening strategies are needed.
