# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client's situation with a supportive, nonjudgmental tone that helps the patient feel heard and respected.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, ongoing reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring ambivalence and specific details of the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking reduction and protecting the child, steering the conversation toward a concrete, time-limited one‑week plan with practical steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-creates the plan with the client by soliciting her ideas and preferences and refining them rather than prescribing solutions, demonstrating genuine partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors the client's choice and agency, avoids pressure, and uses questions that support the client's ownership of decisions and pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the client's own motivations and solutions, probes anticipated benefits, and surfaces intrinsic reasons for change rather than supplying reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the interaction as a joint effort, shares power in decision-making, and repeatedly invites the client's input on which steps feel most doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and develops change talk (choosing concrete strategies and outcomes), but could better amplify and summarize key change statements and use readiness/confidence rulers to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and neutrally acknowledges rationalizations, yet relies mainly on reflections rather than more strategic moves (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframing) to actively reduce sustain talk.
