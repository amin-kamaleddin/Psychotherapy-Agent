# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, affirming small steps and conveying warmth and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, paraphrasing the patient's language and checking understanding with open invitations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on the ten-minute trial and specific actionable elements, keeping the conversation goal-directed and practical.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving with balanced contributions, co-constructing the plan and integrating the patient's preferences into decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices and avoids pressure, framing options and asking what feels most doable to support the patient's ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and ideas using evocative questions, guiding her to generate concrete reasons and a plan for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions the patient as the expert and invites collaboration, using language that shares power and solicits her input on next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, highlights, and amplifies change talk with targeted questions and reinforcement of small, concrete steps toward the goal.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk and uses double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence, but occasionally repeats sustain statements across multiple turns before pivoting to solutions.
