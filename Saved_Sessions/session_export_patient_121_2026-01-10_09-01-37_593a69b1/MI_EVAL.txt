# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone with repeated validations and caring checks (including a suicide-screening question). Acceptance was evident throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking, summarizing both content and affect and validating Sofia's guardedness and ambivalence. Reflective moves were timely and showed clear understanding of her internal state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on managing peer conflict and safety, set an agenda, and co-constructed a concrete, actionable plan (window table, 12:30, 1:15 check-in). The session stayed tightly tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the session as cooperative and repeatedly invited input, but frequently proposed concrete steps and scripted language rather than eliciting Sofia-generated alternatives. Collaboration was strong but somewhat clinician-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Sofia's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and adapting to her preferences (e.g., declining counselor involvement). Moves reinforced that decisions and plans belonged to Sofia.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation for immediate behavioral steps (confidence ruler, specific intentions) but often supplied strategies instead of more deeply exploring Sofia's intrinsic values and reasons for change. Evocation worked well for practical steps but less so for underlying motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited preferences, yet several moments of directive scripting and multiple offered solutions reduced the sense that Sofia generated the plan. Partnership was present but could be more equal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (questions about priorities, past strategies, and confidence), but moved relatively quickly to suggestions instead of deepening Sofia's own reasons and imagined benefits of change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected toward change, but did not consistently use double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and soften sustain talk more strongly.
