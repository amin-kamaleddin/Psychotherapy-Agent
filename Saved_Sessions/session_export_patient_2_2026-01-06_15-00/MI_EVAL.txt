# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard through validation, normalization of ambivalence, and a warm nonjudgmental tone; honors the patient’s limits and goals.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and paraphrase of content and feelings that reinforces understanding; a bit formulaic at times with missed opportunities for deeper, more complex emotional reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused and goal-oriented by steering toward a concrete first step (childcare night and drafting a text); could more explicitly link the plan to smoking-relapse strategies or obstacle planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—uses inclusive language, asks preferences, co-creates wording and supports, and follows the patient’s lead in shaping the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces patient choice by offering options, normalizing possible refusal, and leaving decision-making with the patient, supporting ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient’s own motives and reasons for change with open questions that surface intrinsic goals; could deepen sequencing toward commitment and elicit confidence or past successes more often.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as an expert and genuinely co-constructs the plan, offering practical, collaborative supports rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and prompts concrete planning that produced a commitment; could further amplify and selectively reflect the strongest change statements and ask for confidence or commitment scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to actionable supports and small steps; would benefit from occasional double-sided reflections to artfully acknowledge sustain talk while nudging toward change.
