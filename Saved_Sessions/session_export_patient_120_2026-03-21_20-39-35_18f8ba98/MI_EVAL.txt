# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, opening with explicit support and using normalizing/validating language. No shaming or blaming occurred and the clinician praised caring motives.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate naming of emotions and ambivalence. Could deepen empathy with more frequent, complex emotional reflections around guilt and anxiety instead of moving quickly to skills.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on weekend alcohol use throughout the session and steered toward concrete, behaviorally specific goals. No drift to unrelated topics occurred.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction with repeated invitations for client input, choice, and joint problem-solving including role-play and rehearsal. The clinician engaged Sofia as an equal partner in creating strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported client autonomy by inviting Sofia to set pace, offering options, and asking permission before rehearsals. The clinician emphasized Sofia's ownership of choices and encouraged selecting what felt natural.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used effective evocation tools (importance/confidence scaling, asking for a small step, exploring how to raise confidence). Could further draw out Sofia's own reasons and amplify values-language and explicit commitment statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making and client expertise through options and invitations to choose wording, with collaborative role-play. A few moments were somewhat directive (providing many specific lines) without first eliciting Sofia’s preferred phrasing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change language using scaling, praise, and rehearsal while highlighting client-generated steps. Missed opportunities to strategically amplify stronger change statements and elicit longer-term reasons or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided argument and did not reinforce sustain talk, but mainly shifted to planning rather than explicitly reflecting sustain talk. Limited use of double-sided or strategic reflections to acknowledge competing desires before pivoting to change.
