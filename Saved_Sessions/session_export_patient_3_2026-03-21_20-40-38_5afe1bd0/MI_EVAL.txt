# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, normalizing feelings and praising commitment. Avoided labeling or minimizing the client's experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, validating reflections and acknowledged emotions and barriers. Could deepen validation with more complex, emotion-focused reflections using the client's vivid language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on starting an exercise/walk routine and moved purposefully from elicitation to planning and troubleshooting. Summarized decisions and asked for next steps appropriately.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative with frequent checks about what felt doable and co‑construction of the plan. Offered suggestions while deferring to the client's preferences and expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported client choice throughout, offering options and small experiments without pressure. Language consistently reinforced that decisions belong to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations, past successes, and strategies and used confidence scaling. Could further probe personal meaning, values, and identity to deepen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as a partnership, seeking input and sharing power in decision-making. Checks for feasibility and collaborative drafting of messages reinforced partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and built on change talk with reflections, specific steps, and confidence questions, strengthening commitment language. Missed opportunities to more explicitly amplify the client's own change statements and ask more evocative DARN questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and promptly pivoted to brainstorming manageable backups and reminders. Could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence while guiding toward change.
