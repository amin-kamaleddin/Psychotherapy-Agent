### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence with warm, validating language; the patient explicitly expressed appreciation, indicating acceptance was experienced.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate, complex reflections that name emotions and practical realities, reinforcing understanding and trust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction/cessation and moves logically from problem identification to a concrete, time-bound one-week plan with specific actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a true partnership: the clinician repeatedly invites the patient's input, offers options after getting buy-in, and shapes the plan around the patient's preferences and strengths.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by avoiding coercive language, offering multiple options, and emphasizing the patient's agency in selecting and implementing the plan.

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructs the plan with the patient, asks for the patient's expertise about what will work, and consistently frames decisions as the patient's to make.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk through strength-focused questions and a concrete week-long plan, though missed opportunities remain to amplify and consolidate commitment language (e.g., confidence scaling, selective reinforcement).

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
