# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated respect and nonjudgmental understanding (e.g., validated worries about muscle side effects, framed the two‑week trial as sensible). Avoided labeling/shaming and normalized caution. Opportunity: individualize affirmations (highlight specific strengths like willingness to try a small test).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective‑taking (accurate paraphrases of barriers and worries). Reflections tracked guarded tone and practical constraints. Missed opportunities for deeper empathic exploration (limited complex reflections about feelings or personal meaning of health).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on target behavior (meds vs. lifestyle) and a concrete objective (two‑week trial). Each exchange advanced a structured plan (steps, barriers/supports, if‑then plan, monitoring, follow‑up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance: clinician offered options and invited David to choose, shared power in decision‑making, and offered tools only with permission. Co‑construction of the plan evident throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly honored David’s choice and avoided pressuring for meds, framed the trial as his decision, and reinforced calling the doctor for safety concerns. Opportunity: add explicit ownership statements to further bolster self‑efficacy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical reasons for change and barriers/supports; used confidence scaling and framing the trial to draw out willingness. Focused mainly on tactics rather than eliciting deeper personal values or long‑term motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reflected mutual expertise and shared problem solving (clinician provided safety guidance; patient provided preferences). Permission‑seeking before offering suggestions reinforced partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked questions to elicit ability and commitment (doable step, confidence scales), producing specific but tentative change talk. Missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate limited change talk or press toward stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not confront sustain talk; validated concerns and redirected to a neutral experiment (two‑week trial) and safety planning. Could use more double‑sided and selective reflections to tip ambivalence more toward change.
