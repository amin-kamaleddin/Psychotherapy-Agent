# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard—validated Marisol, normalized concerns, and avoided judgment while honoring her decision to keep the piercing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and invited emotional disclosure, acknowledging Marisol's feelings and demonstrating nuanced perspective-taking throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the conversation clearly focused on the lip piercing and oral health, repeatedly returning to monitoring signs and specific next actions with no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited Marisol's input, co-created and polished her script, and offered choices of supports rather than prescribing a single course of action.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, explicit support for autonomy with multiple statements of choice and noncoercive offers rather than directives, reinforcing Marisol's ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited effort to elicit internal motivations or change talk—few scaling or pros/cons questions and reliance on information-giving rather than strategic evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fostered partnership by asking permission and offering options, though an early information-heavy, clinician-led segment reduced opportunities for co-discovery.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered supports that created some space for change talk but did not probe for reasons, desire, ability, need, or commitment, missing opportunities to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and affirmed autonomy to prevent escalation of sustain talk, but could have used more double-sided reflections and selective reflections to pivot toward concerns.
