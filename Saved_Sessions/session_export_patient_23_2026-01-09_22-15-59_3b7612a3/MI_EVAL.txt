# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with explicit affirmations and a clear safety check; validated efforts and framed slips as learning rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good understanding and tailored suggestions to the patient's context and reluctances, but offered relatively few deep reflections of emotional experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use and guided the session toward concrete, actionable steps and summaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated the session as joint problem-solving and adapted recommendations to the patient, though often presented multiple ready-made options rather than eliciting ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, asking permission, and respecting refusals while normalizing slips and emphasizing the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Included some open questions that led to planning, but largely shifted to giving advice rather than systematically eliciting deeper motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited co-creation of the plan and incorporated patient feedback, though at times the clinician took a more directive stance with many specific suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced and prompted some change talk through praise and planning, but missed opportunities to elicit and amplify the patient's own reasons, importance, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and respected ambivalence, but did not actively use MI strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explore or soften sustain talk for ambivalence resolution.
