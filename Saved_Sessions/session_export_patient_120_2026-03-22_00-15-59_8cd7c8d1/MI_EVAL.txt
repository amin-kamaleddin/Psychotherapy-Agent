# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizing and validating the patient's experience without blame or shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses frequent, often complex reflections that capture ambivalence and emotional weight, though some reflections are repetitive and could probe affect (guilt/shame/fear) more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on stopping alcohol during pregnancy, tying each intervention (support person, text, rehearsal) back to that goal without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem‑solving, invites Sofia's input at every step, co‑constructs the plan, and accepts her choices, demonstrating genuine collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers options, asks which feels doable, and reinforces that decisions are Sofia's, with no evidence of pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, ideas, and barriers and turns them into actionable steps, but could probe deeper into values and elicit confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a true partner by sharing power, offering role‑play, and letting the patient direct which strategies to try first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk and follows with planning, but could more consistently reflect spontaneous change statements and directly elicit commitment or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk but does not systematically use double‑sided reflections or reframing, often pivoting quickly to solutions rather than reducing sustain talk's pull.
