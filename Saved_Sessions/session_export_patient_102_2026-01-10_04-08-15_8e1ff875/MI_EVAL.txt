# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with explicit acceptance statements, reflective language, and a nonjudgmental tone, creating a safe space for the client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections captured both content and emotion, demonstrating strong perspective-taking and attunement to the client's ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on risky weekend drinking while honoring the client's values and moved logically from values to specific, feasible steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co‑constructed: the clinician solicited the client's input at each step and jointly named strategies rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician reinforced the client's choice and control by acknowledging he was not ready to quit, avoiding pressure, and asking him to choose supports that felt doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used evocative questions and reflections to draw motivation and concrete reasons from the client, converting desire into client-generated action steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and centered the client's expertise, inviting him to prioritize and plan strategies together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions and prompts that produced specific, actionable intentions and plans from the client.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double-sided reflections to redirect sustain talk, though could have more explicitly normalized the sustain side and used additional strategic reflections to further defuse resistance.
