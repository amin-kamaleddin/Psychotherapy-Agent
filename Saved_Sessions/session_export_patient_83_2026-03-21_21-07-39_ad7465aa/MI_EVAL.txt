# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician offered explicit unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language throughout, normalizing and validating the patient's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections demonstrated clear perspective-taking and accurately captured worries, but deeper affective exploration of frustration or anxiety was sometimes lacking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on the medication goal—identifying the statin, interactions, dosing, monitoring, and concrete next steps—without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction resembled a team effort: the clinician solicited the patient's goals, co‑created scripts/texts, and made joint decisions about next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently affirmed patient choice, offered options, respected preferences, and supported self‑direction while ensuring safety planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited the patient's motivations, plans, and confidence estimates, but could have probed more for personal values and discrepancy to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed the encounter as collaborative, asked permission before providing information, and iteratively refined tools with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Several change‑oriented statements were elicited and reinforced and concrete plans were made, though the clinician could more explicitly amplify and consolidate commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and was redirected toward practical safety steps, but more double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries could further acknowledge ambivalence.
