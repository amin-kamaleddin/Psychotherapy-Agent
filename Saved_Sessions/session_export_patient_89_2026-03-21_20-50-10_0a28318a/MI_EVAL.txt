# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician opens nonjudgmentally, validates frustration, and maintains respectful, praise-based language throughout, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and tracking of emotional shifts, though deeper affect-focused reflections about discouragement and past relapses would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays tightly focused on increasing physical activity and moves logically from values and barriers to a concrete action plan and a one-week follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as equal partnership with joint problem-solving, regular checks for preferences, and acceptance of Marcus’s solutions and plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently offers choices, asks for preferences, uses scaling to invite self-assessment, and avoids pressuring language to support client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and strategies are drawn from Marcus’s own values and ideas (family, enjoyment, sleep/stress benefits), with effective elicitation of intrinsic reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist co-constructs the plan, invites Marcus to decide specifics, and shares power in planning, reflecting strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician elicits and amplifies multiple change statements, uses specific questions and reflections to strengthen commitment and concrete next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and redirected toward practical solutions without arguing, but would benefit from more double-sided reflections or explicit ambivalence summaries to further weaken resistance.
