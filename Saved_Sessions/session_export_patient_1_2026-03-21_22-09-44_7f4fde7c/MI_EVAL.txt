# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, validating Ahmed’s perspective and normalizing a stepwise approach throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and perspective-taking were used repeatedly, capturing both content and affect and summarizing Ahmed’s concerns and plan with high attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on mini-cigar use and moved logically from assessment to options to a concrete implementation plan without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician and patient co-created the plan (tracker, alarm, buddy text), invited preferences at each step, and adapted wording to Ahmed’s style, demonstrating true egalitarian collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently reinforced Ahmed’s choice and control, offering options, permission to receive information, and letting Ahmed select strategies to increase buy-in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Several strong evoking moves drew out intrinsic motivations (health/family) and concrete change ideas, though deeper probing of values and stronger reinforcement of desire/reasons could have increased commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language and behaviors (e.g., 'If you want, I can…', asking which supports to set up) positioned Ahmed as the expert and produced a jointly owned, practical plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited specific change language and actionable steps, but missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate Ahmed’s change talk into firmer commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and was respectfully reframed toward a palatable test, though double-sided reflections explicitly addressing beliefs about 'harmless' mini-cigars could have reduced sustain talk further.
