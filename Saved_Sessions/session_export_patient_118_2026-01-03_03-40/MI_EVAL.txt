# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Communicated respect and nonjudgmental acceptance
- [x] Validated patient's perspective and frustration
- [x] Honored patient's request for "no fuss" and followed his lead

**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental (multiple validations: “I hear your frustration…”, “I’ll follow your lead”), honored preferences and avoided labeling or shaming.

---

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Used accurate reflective listening (content and some affect)
- [ ] Deepened emotional exploration or probed underlying feelings/values

**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflections that captured frustration and embarrassment; reflections remained mostly at the surface and did not consistently deepen emotional exploration.

---

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Maintained clear focus on dental/oral health target behavior
- [x] Redirected to concrete harm‑reduction options
- [x] Asked goal‑directed questions to elicit doable steps

**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on dental outcomes and practical options (cleanings, gum, rinse); repeatedly asked what would work now to reduce harm.

---

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Framed interaction as joint decision-making
- [x] Offered options rather than imposed plans
- [x] Deferred to patient preferences (e.g., no demo, samples by chair)

**Comments:** Repeatedly invited patient choice (“Which of these sounds doable…?”, “What feels easiest?”) and honored his selections, demonstrating strong partnership.

---

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Emphasized patient choice and control
- [x] Checked preferences between alternatives
- [x] Accepted patient's decisions without pressure

**Comments:** Multiple offers of options and explicit statements of following the patient's lead reinforced autonomy throughout the encounter.

---

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Elicited patient's ideas and practical solutions (gum, rinse, cleaning)
- [ ] Probed past successes, confidence, or deeper motivators

**Comments:** Frequently invited the patient's input and obtained practical commitments, but did not probe deeper motivational drivers or confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

---

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Shared power and decision-making
- [x] Avoided expert‑authority lecturing stance
- [x] Followed patient's lead on plans and delivery

**Comments:** Clinician acted as an equal collaborator, offering choices and repeatedly checking what “feels easiest,” effectively sharing power.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Elicited and reinforced change‑oriented statements (agreement to gum, rinse; desire for improved appearance and reduced pain)
- [ ] Did not sufficiently amplify or consolidate commitment/confidence language

**Comments:** Good elicitation and reflection of change talk (small harm‑reduction steps agreed), but missed brief moves to convert that into stronger commitment or confidence ratings.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Avoided argumentation or confrontation
- [x] Validated concerns and redirected toward acceptable alternatives
- [x] Reduced resistance by honoring preferences

**Comments:** Consistently softened sustain talk by validating (“I hear your frustration”) and offering feasible non‑drug options, successfully avoiding pushback.
