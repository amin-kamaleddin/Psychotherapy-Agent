### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence with warm, nonjudgmental language. Reassured and validated Jordan repeatedly without shaming or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing trade-offs between side effects and protecting grandparents. Could deepen empathic responding by probing and reflecting more of Jordan’s felt emotion rather than mainly cognitive summaries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on getting the flu vaccine and related practicalities throughout. Questions, reflections, and offers of help all advanced momentum toward concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-created decisions by asking Jordan’s preference at multiple points and following his lead on which supports to prepare. Support was clearly shared rather than clinician-directed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Jordan’s control (e.g., 'your call'), offered options, and avoided pressure while providing information when invited. This consistently reinforced his sense of choice and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation by exploring values (protecting grandparents) and concrete criteria that would tip the decision. Could strengthen evocation with more evocative questions or scaling to deepen and quantify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Jordan as the expert on his life, offered choices, and negotiated next steps collaboratively. Power was shared and the clinician deferred to Jordan’s preferences and constraints.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and translated ambivalence into actionable supports (summary, questions, clinic slots). To reach top score, the clinician could more strongly amplify and consolidate Jordan’s own change statements into explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double-sided reflections to acknowledge both sides of the ambivalence without reinforcing sustain talk. Could more strategically emphasize the pro-change side first and use reframes or summaries to shift momentum further away from sustain arguments.
