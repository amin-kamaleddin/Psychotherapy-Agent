# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, affirming small wins and priorities; could add a few more explicit strength-based affirmations to maximize acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures ambivalence and context, but could deepen empathic reflections by probing underlying emotions (shame, fear) and their meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on shoplifting/recidivism and consistently ties questions and reflections to specific coping strategies and a concrete weekly action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with solicitation of the client's ideas, but the clinician often leads the structure and could invite more joint agenda-setting and corrective summaries.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly frames choices and asks the client to select options, avoids coercion, and validates ambivalence—supporting client ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's own motivations and solutions with open questions and elicits concrete, self-generated implementation details and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates respect for client expertise and invites input, but could strengthen power-sharing by explicitly naming the client as the expert and co-creating contingency plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and narrows toward specific, doable steps and commitments; adding a confidence/readiness scaling could further solidify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without judgment and redirects toward change with double-sided reflections, though more explicit exploration of the costs of continued behavior and strategic summaries would further soften sustain talk.
