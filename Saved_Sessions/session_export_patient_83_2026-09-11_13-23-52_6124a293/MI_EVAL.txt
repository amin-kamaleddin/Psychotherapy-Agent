# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the patient's mixed feelings, affirming her chosen precautions and treating her perspective with respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate empathic listening and reflections, but reflections were sometimes repetitive and could be deepened to explore the emotional nuance behind the patient's uncertainty.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on medication safety and adherence, consistently redirecting to concrete observable actions like pharmacist questions, SMS checklist, and a 7–14 day watch period.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—offers of role formats, co‑authoring the script and SMS, and asking the patient's preferences supported joint problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the patient's autonomy by offering multiple options, asking which support she wanted, and avoiding pressure so she could choose the plan that felt right.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified the patient's own motivations and commitment language with open, choice-oriented questions and rehearsal that solidified a self‑driven plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the patient as a collaborator—asked permission, negotiated role‑play style, and co‑created practical tools, demonstrating genuine power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively drew out concrete change talk and planning by asking what felt doable, helping craft exact language for the pharmacist, and setting clear monitoring steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and redirected to practical steps, but could have briefly explored the specific source of doubt or used a double‑sided reflection to further soften ambivalence.
