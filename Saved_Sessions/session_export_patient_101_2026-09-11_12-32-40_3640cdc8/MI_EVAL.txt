# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates the patient's experience, normalizing ambivalence; could add more explicit affirmations of strengths and normalization of setbacks to deepen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing ambivalence and practical plans; could use more amplified or affect‑focused reflections to deepen emotional resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing alcohol use and improving contraceptive reliability, progressing from ambivalence to concrete, time‑bound implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: elicits the patient's ideas, checks back, and refines options together rather than prescribing, with shared problem‑solving throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the patient's choice by inviting preferences and avoiding coercive language, consistently emphasizing that the patient selects what feels doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation and solutions from the patient through open questions, drawing out patient‑generated strategies and a concrete trial plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites the patient to lead and co‑creates the plan, asking for preferences and deferring to the patient's expertise about what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and nurtures change talk with open questions that produce concrete planning, but seldom amplifies or summarizes change talk or explicitly elicits commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to change strategies, but rarely explores sustain talk empathically or uses double‑sided reflections to soften it.
