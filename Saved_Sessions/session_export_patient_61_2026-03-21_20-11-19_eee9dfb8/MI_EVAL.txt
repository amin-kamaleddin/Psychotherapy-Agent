# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, using validations and normalizing language that communicated unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking with targeted empathic questions and repeated validations, showing clear emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on one specific behavior (one quick glucose check tied to morning coffee) and developed a simple, measurable plan without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan with the patient, consistently solicited preferences and choices, and framed the work as joint problem-solving with negotiated options and do-over rules.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the patient's sense of choice by offering options, avoiding pressure, and emphasizing that the patient decides what to try and how.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions, scaling, and small-option offers to draw out reasons and confidence, but did not sufficiently probe deeper values or amplify personal motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the interaction as a shared enterprise, offering tentative suggestions and deferring to the patient's decisions to foster power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited modest change talk via scaling and concrete small steps that produced agreement to try, but missed opportunities to explore intrinsic motives or secure a stronger commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and accepted barriers and avoided arguing, which softened resistance, but did not employ stronger MI moves (e.g., double-sided reflections or strategic ambivalence summaries) to shift sustain talk.
