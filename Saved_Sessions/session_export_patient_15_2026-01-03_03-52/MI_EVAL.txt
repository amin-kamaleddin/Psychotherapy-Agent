# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful (validations, normalizing small steps). Multiple supportive reflections and summaries; no shaming or directive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of the patient's stance and priorities. Some reflections were repetitive/formulaic and the clinician missed opportunities to probe deeper feelings or the patient's minimizing language about stress/drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session kept tightly focused on BP‑related behavior. Clinician repeatedly steered toward a single, concrete, achievable plan and progressed toward planning and commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Patient perspective solicited at each step; plan co‑constructed (walk, spouse prompt, indoor backup). Clinician avoided an expert‑only stance and checked feasibility with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multiple options offered, choices emphasized, and patient's low‑commitment stance accepted. Language preserved patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons and past strategies (smoking cessation → routine, reminders). Drew out practical motivations (avoid meds, time with spouse) but could have probed more emotionally salient motivators and consequences of inaction.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Collaborative, partnership stance throughout—shared control, invited patient choice, and allowed patient contributions to direct the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reflected clear change talk (willingness to try walk, reasons such as avoiding meds and spending time with spouse). Could have amplified change talk and elicited a stronger commitment (scaling/confidence question).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not confront sustain talk and effectively redirected to feasible action. Missed opportunities to explore/minimize sustain talk more explicitly (e.g., probe the alcohol minimization, use double‑sided reflections to surface discrepancy).
