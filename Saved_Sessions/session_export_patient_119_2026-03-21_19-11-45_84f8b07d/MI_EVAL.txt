# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, accepting tone and validated the patient's perspective, offering practical help rather than criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid perspective-taking and occasional paraphrase of constraints, but empathic responses were pragmatic and could have more deeply reflected underlying anxiety or guardedness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on medication adherence with goal-linked interventions and moved from options to a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as a true collaborative problem-solving partnership, using inclusive language, soliciting the patient's input at every decision point, and adapting recommendations to her concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking permission, and framing advice as choices while respecting the patient's request for simplicity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's motivations and preferences and helped craft personalized plans, but relied more on offering solutions than systematically exploring deeper reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited preferences and co-created the plan with the patient, sharing power and letting her select doable options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged and reinforced change talk through choice-focused questions and positive feedback, but did not probe importance, confidence, or the personal meaning of 'staying healthy' to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and effectively redirected sustain talk toward low-friction strategies, though could have used more double-sided reflections or explicit normalization of ambivalence.
