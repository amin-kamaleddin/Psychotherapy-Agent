# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician spoke respectfully, normalized Hana's fear, and affirmed her efforts, but acceptance sometimes felt procedural and tied to moving quickly into a prescriptive plan rather than fully warm, individualized positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrated accurate understanding with reflections and normalization and linked to Hana's past success, but did not consistently explore emotional nuance or the deeper feelings behind Hana's shame and relief.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on preparing for the exam with concrete, goal-linked strategies (test date, Pomodoros, practice tests, coping tools) and progressive action steps toward follow-through.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician checked preferences, adapted the plan (25→15 minutes), and invited Hana's input on tasks and the accountability message, though strategy design leaned slightly clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices and permission were offered (timing, guided breathing, message wording), supporting autonomy, but multiple directive recommendations reduced the degree of explicit autonomy reinforcement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation occurred (confidence ruler, commitment prompt, highlighting past success), but the clinician more often provided solutions than systematically drawing out Hana's own reasons, values, and motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were partnership moves (asking permission, checking readiness, co-creating some steps), yet the interaction remained largely expert-driven with the clinician setting the agenda and methods.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced occasional change talk (ruler, praise, commitment), but used few open questions or reflections to amplify Hana's expressed hopes and reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician validated fear and redirected sustain talk toward manageable experiments and coping strategies, avoiding debate, though double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence were limited.
