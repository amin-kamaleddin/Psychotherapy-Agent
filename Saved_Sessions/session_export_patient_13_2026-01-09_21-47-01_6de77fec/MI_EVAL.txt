# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizing Anara's fear and affirming her motives. No judgmental or minimizing language, which reduced shame and supported openness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, reflecting both content and affect. Reflections mirrored Anara's hesitant, vulnerable tone and encouraged further elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on seeking medical assessment and support throughout the session. Interventions kept returning to concrete next steps (info call, script, role-play) and avoided drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative, framed as joint problem-solving rather than expert instruction. The clinician co-constructed the script, offered role-play options, and repeatedly asked how best to support Anara.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Anara's sense of choice and control by offering options and soliciting preferences. Offers to help were conditional on her readiness and pacing, with no pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out Anara's motivations and concrete small steps (care for child, calling clinic), but underused deeper evocation techniques. Scaling importance/confidence, exploring past successes, and more evocative questions could have strengthened commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Anara as the expert in her life and shared control of the agenda. Frequent invitations to choose options and co-create the script demonstrated true power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk (values and a doable step), and affirmed concrete planning like the script and practice. Missed opportunities to elicit past successes, use importance/confidence rulers, and probe for a time-bound commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reframed sustain talk, validated protective intentions, and offered practical small-step options. Could have used more double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to further tip the balance toward change.
