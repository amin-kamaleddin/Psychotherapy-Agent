### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized David’s preferences and concerns without judgment, supporting his chosen short experiment throughout the session.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely, and specific reflective listening and summaries that captured David’s perspective, motives, and emotions (e.g., dog routine, wife’s nudges, worry about muscle weakness).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus and steered the session toward a measurable, time-limited plan (three 20-minute walks + one swapped meal) with concrete supports and success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine joint problem-solving by inviting David’s preferences, using his real-life routines, and co-constructing the experiment while avoiding an expert-dominant stance.

### Autonomy Support
