# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Maria’s pain, honoring her request to be heard and avoiding blame or minimization throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained, accurate empathic listening with multilayered reflections that matched affect and content (hopelessness, numbness, fear of hospitals) and summarized key risk details.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on imminent suicidal risk and safety planning (plan, means, timing, delaying until morning) without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a team effort: clinician offered options, practical help (texting, staying on line), and implemented Maria’s choices rather than dictating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported choice by asking permission, offering options, respecting refusal of the ER while transparently stating safety limits, which preserved Maria’s sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of internal motivation (concern for brother, willingness to delay) but limited use of evocative questions or techniques to deepen reasons for living, past coping, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and shared decision-making (asking what felt doable, offering to help), though the clinician at times steered the agenda toward safety planning without more explicitly eliciting Maria’s expertise or past strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited brief pro-life statements and supported safety behaviors, but missed opportunities to amplify and explore desire, ability, reasons, and past successes to strengthen sustained change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated pain while redirecting to concrete safety options (removing pills, contacting someone, delaying), though could have used more advanced MI moves (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly counter sustain talk.
