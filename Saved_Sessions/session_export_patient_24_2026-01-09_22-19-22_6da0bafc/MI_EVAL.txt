# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating from the opening line through closing, using warm, respectful language and normalizing ambivalence; demonstrates strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening that captures ambivalence and surprise, but reflections remain mostly surface-level and could probe deeper emotions, values, or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use in the context of marathon training and consistently moves toward a specific, measurable behavior-change plan with concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a joint enterprise: the clinician repeatedly asks for input, negotiates options, and tailors the plan to the patient's preferences, reinforcing mutual problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by offering multiple low-pressure options, affirming his decisions, and providing a noncoercive safety net if problems arise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation (importance ruler, goal choice) but the clinician moves quickly to offering options and advice rather than deeply drawing out the patient's own reasons, values, and intrinsic motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance with repeated invitations to choose what feels doable and co-creation of the plan, demonstrating respect for the patient's expertise about his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through questions, scaling, and affirmations, but could better amplify and deepen DARN language linking change to the patient's goals and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and redirects sustain talk toward manageable experiments and tracking, though could more often use double-sided or strategic reflections to explicitly soften sustain talk.
