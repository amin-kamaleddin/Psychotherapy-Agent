### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence with validating statements. This nonjudgmental stance supported safety and rapport throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking with frequent summaries capturing both feelings and behavior. Reflections reliably mirrored Mei's core concerns and motivations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing drinking and improving contraceptive reliability, progressively narrowing options to specific, testable steps. Each turn moved the conversation toward actionable commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed with repeated invitations for Mei's preferences and joint decision-making. The clinician offered to draft wording together and framed next steps as mutually chosen.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored choice by offering options, framing the plan as an experiment, and accepting uncertainty without pressure. This approach reinforced the client's sense of control over the plan.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Drew motivation and ideas from Mei using open questions and elicited her own reasons for change rather than supplying rationale. The clinician used these client-generated motivations to shape the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the work as shared and foregrounded Mei's expertise, asking permission to brainstorm and inviting her to choose steps. Language consistently transferred agency to the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk through targeted questions and requests for specific commitments, producing concrete client change language. The clinician reinforced expected benefits and elicited commitment details.
