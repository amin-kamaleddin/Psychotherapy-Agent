# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with explicit validation (e.g., 'resting counts, too') and repeated affirmations of the patient's choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Multiple empathic reflections and supportive language showed good understanding of the patient's experience, though deeper affect-focused reflections (e.g., frustration about inconsistency) could have strengthened resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate behavioral focus on increasing physical activity with a tight agenda and forward-moving steps (micro-goals, start date, follow-up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction felt cooperative and patient-centered with co-created steps and accommodations for energy limits, but the clinician sometimes led with specific solutions rather than eliciting the patient's own brainstorms first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed choice and offered options, yet occasionally provided prescriptive wording and a detailed plan early on which may have subtly narrowed perceived choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out the patient's motivations and elicited concrete commitments (start date, check-in), but could use more open, evocative questions and reflections to amplify internal motivation and confidence/importance language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally framed the work as collaborative and sought input on preferences, though the clinician sometimes shifted initiative by proposing a concrete plan before fully exploring the patient's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and concrete commitments, but missed some opportunities to summarize, amplify, and deepen desire/ability/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided strengthening sustain talk by normalizing rest and offering low-cost alternatives, though more double-sided reflections could have explicitly surfaced ambivalence while guiding toward change.
