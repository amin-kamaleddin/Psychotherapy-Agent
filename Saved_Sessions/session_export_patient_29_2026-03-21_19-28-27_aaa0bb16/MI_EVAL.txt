# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician opens and maintains a supportive, nonjudgmental tone, normalizes disclosure, and avoids blame or labeling throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides timely empathic reflections and links coping to the patient's stressors, but reflective work is economical and could probe the emotional impact of work and stress more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and next actionable steps (med options, quit timing, logistics) throughout the encounter.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: elicits preferences, co-produces and iteratively edits the prescriber message, and co-constructs decisions with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering multiple options, asking preferences, and respecting timing and backup plans, reinforcing patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits basic readiness (quit now vs cut down) but focuses on information and logistics rather than eliciting deeper personal motivations, values, confidence, or commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine partnership through shared decision-making and collaborative drafting of the message, centering the patient's expertise and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and supports some change talk (readiness and medication candidacy) but misses opportunities to deepen or amplify the patient's own reasons, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain-related factors (stress, social triggers) and avoids arguing for the status quo, but uses few explicit softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or strategic reframing) to resolve ambivalence.
