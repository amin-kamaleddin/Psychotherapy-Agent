# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizes slips and invites patient preferences; any directive problem-solving is offered with permission and affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and some reflective listening, acknowledging social context and preferences, but offers few deep reflections of underlying emotions or explicit reflections of minimization.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, sustained focus on reducing alcohol-related risk and safety throughout, consistently bringing the session back to actionable steps and goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-creates plan details, elicits preferred phrasing, and practices role-play while repeatedly checking for agreement and preference.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports choice by offering options, seeking permission, and framing slips non-punitively to preserve self-efficacy and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own commitments and practical strategies but could more deeply evoke intrinsic motives, values, and confidence/importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative with shared decision-making and permission-seeking, though the clinician sometimes offers many suggestions before soliciting the patient's solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and amplifies change talk into concrete commitments (drink-free days, tracking), but could probe importance and confidence more explicitly to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and focuses on harm-reduction, but misses opportunities for double-sided reflections and strategic evocation to soften ambivalence.
