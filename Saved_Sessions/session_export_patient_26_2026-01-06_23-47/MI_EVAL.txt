### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through validating, normalizing, and affirming statements while avoiding judgmental language. Multiple explicit affirmations reinforced Daniela's efforts and strengths throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with timely, layered reflections of both practical stressors and emotional vulnerability. The clinician checked in on feelings and reactions (e.g., after the role-play), signaling high-quality reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused agenda on securing reliable childcare and moved the conversation systematically from problem identification to concrete actions. Each exchange advanced toward specific, time-bound steps (ask neighbor, confirmation text, Head Start call).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was strongly collaborative: scripts and plans were co-created, the clinician offered role-play and followed Daniela's preferences, and decisions emerged from mutual work rather than clinician prescription. Language and actions supported joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Daniela's choice and control by offering options, asking permission to role-play, and using scaling to elicit self-assessment. The clinician avoided pressure and framed suggestions as optional, supporting client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Daniela's own motivations and solutions using scaling, eliciting specific next steps, and amplifying her successes. The clinician encouraged internal reasons for change and strengthened commitment through reflective reinforcement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Daniela as the expert and repeatedly invited her preferences, following her lead in choosing the trade and practicing wording. Power-sharing was evident in option-giving and co-constructed decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced multiple concrete change statements with choice questions, specific planning, and repeated confidence scaling. The clinician reflected and amplified momentum, strengthening Daniela's commitment to action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized barriers and worries without reinforcing sustain talk and offered practical reframes and backups. There were fewer explicit double-sided reflections that name both pull toward change and reasons for hesitancy, which could further shift ambivalence into change talk.
