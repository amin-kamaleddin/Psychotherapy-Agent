### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and accurate reflections, including useful summaries, but misses some opportunities for deeper affective or complex reflections of Aisha's raw emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on restarting exercise and moves the conversation from motivation to concrete planning without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation through questions about past successes, values, and confidence scaling, prompting multiple self‑motivating statements and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the client's expertise, repeatedly asking which options feel most doable and incorporating her choices into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly elicits and amplifies change talk using small‑step questions, the confidence ruler, reflections, and reinforcement of specific commitments and self‑efficacy.
