# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing Elena’s feelings and offering support without pressure. Clinician validates ambivalence and avoids blaming or minimizing language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate reflections, acknowledging fear and relief and checking on supports. Could deepen emotional reflections and provide longer summaries to capture the intensity and impact of Elena’s feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on emergency and regular contraception, asking timing and offering concrete next steps. Balances client-led pacing with purposeful guidance and avoids getting sidetracked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses collaborative language, co-creates practical materials (script, clinic options, reminder), and follows Elena’s lead when she declines firm action. Invites participation and shares decision-making power throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and control, offers options and delayed supports without pressure, and honors Elena’s repeated statements of not being ready. Reinforces Elena’s authority over timing and decisions consistently.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits values and some self-motivating statements and frames options to align with those values. Would benefit from more targeted evocative questions and amplification of change talk to deepen desire, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clearly collaborative stance by asking permission, checking preferences, and inviting Elena to co-decide next steps. Distributes expertise and fosters shared decision-making effectively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits change-relevant language and aligns information with Elena’s priorities, offering concrete next steps and supports. Could more actively reflect and amplify change talk and ask evocative questions about desire, ability, reasons, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence, but rarely uses double-sided reflections or reframing to shift sustain statements toward change. More strategic reflections and selective summarizing of change talk could help soften sustain talk.
