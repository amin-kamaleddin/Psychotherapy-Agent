# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently showed respect and positive regard (warm tone, normalized referral, reassured confidentiality) without judgment or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective‑taking captured both content and feelings (stress, sleep, strengths), deepening rapport rather than merely restating facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed goal‑directed on the client‑chosen target (balancing IB and band) with clear progression from agenda setting to concrete planning and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned themselves as a partner, using inclusive language and integrating Isabelle’s ideas (alarms, phone placement, friend ping) into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were explicitly offered and the clinician asked permission to revisit drinking, reinforcing Isabelle’s ownership of decisions and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strongly evoked Isabelle’s reasons and plans for study changes (scaling, supports), but did little to elicit internal motivation regarding the flagged alcohol concern.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach with repeated invitations for preference-setting and co‑creation of the plan, showing shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened change talk about study/sleep habits (concrete plan, confidence scaling) but minimally pursued change talk about alcohol.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and normalized sustain‑talk about drinking, which softened resistance, but missed opportunities for more strategic moves (double‑sided reflections or discrepancy).
