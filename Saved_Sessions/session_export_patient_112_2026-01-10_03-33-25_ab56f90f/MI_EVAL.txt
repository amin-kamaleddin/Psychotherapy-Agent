### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking cessation with concrete, behaviorally relevant steps (med options, quit plan, script, rehearsal) and no drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Demonstrates an equal‑partner stance by co-creating materials, rehearsing lines together, and repeatedly soliciting Rosa’s preferences, positioning her as an active co‑creator.

#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
