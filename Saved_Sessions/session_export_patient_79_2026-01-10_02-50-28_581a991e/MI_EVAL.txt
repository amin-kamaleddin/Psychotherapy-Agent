# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, validating frustration and normalizing the burden of self-care throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening with accurate, affect-matching reflections of fatigue and frustration, but reflections were mostly surface-level and did not probe deeper emotions or the meaning behind sarcasm/defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, narrow focus on improving inhaler accessibility/adherence, offered pragmatic low-burden strategies, secured a specific action and follow-up, and set appropriate safety boundaries.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the exchange as joint problem-solving, invited the patient's preferences, made decisions together, and planned follow-up adjustments, conveying equality and shared ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's choice and control by offering options, respecting limits, and avoiding pressure, supporting a strong sense of autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited a practical commitment (one-week trial) but did not sufficiently draw out the patient's internal motivations, values, or past successes to deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by offering options and asking the patient to choose what’s doable, though the many concrete suggestions were somewhat directive and could have amplified the patient's expertise more.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific change language and secured behavioral commitment with a concrete plan, but missed opportunities to probe internal reasons, values, or use scaling to broaden change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided engaging or arguing with sustain talk by validating and reframing skepticism, but could have used double-sided reflections or other strategies to further highlight ambivalence.
