# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, normalizing struggles and naming strengths throughout the session. Celebrates small wins and affirms Marcus's honesty and effort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and accurate reflections, validating feelings and practical constraints. Could deepen exploration of underlying emotions (e.g., fear or shame) rather than moving quickly into planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity with agenda setting, concrete plans, tracking, and a scheduled follow-up. Keeps the conversation goal-directed and moves toward specific, measurable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: co-constructs the plan, adapts wording to Marcus's preferences, and sets a mutually agreed check-in. Solicits input at multiple points and adjusts based on his responses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Marcus's choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and avoiding pressuring language. Reinforces flexibility and Marcus's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Marcus's motivations and choices (time windows, supports, check-in structure). Would benefit from more open, evocative questions to elicit intrinsic reasons and confidence before offering detailed solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely client-centered and collaborative, asking permission and soliciting availability. The clinician offered a fairly prescriptive plan early, which slightly reduced opportunities to elicit Marcus's own solutions first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk, amplifying preferences and prompting commitment and a check-in. Could deepen change talk with more evocative open questions to strengthen desire, ability, reasons, and need language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers nonjudgmentally and reframes with micro-moves and normalization rather than arguing. The session moves to concrete planning relatively quickly instead of using double-sided or complex reflections to further soften sustain talk.
