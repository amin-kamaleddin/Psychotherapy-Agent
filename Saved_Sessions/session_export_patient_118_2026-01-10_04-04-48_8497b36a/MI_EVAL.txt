# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, repeatedly validates Seamus and accepts his reluctance to quit while working within his priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries capture content and context, though the clinician missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional weariness or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on dental repair and oral-risk reduction, using specific planning questions to guide the visit toward concrete outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—patient input is solicited and integrated at every step, and decisions are negotiated rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Seamus's right to choose, avoids pressure, and offers options while honoring his stated boundary about quitting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical solutions and patient-generated plans for the dental visit, but stops short of drawing out broader intrinsic motivations for changing smoking behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership stance—clinician shares power, defers to the patient's expertise, and co-constructs the treatment plan with Seamus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some concrete change talk around oral-care behaviors but does not consistently follow up to amplify desire, ability, reasons, or commitment regarding smoking cessation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes toward manageable harm-reduction steps while honoring resistance; could add double-sided reflections to more gently highlight discrepancy.
