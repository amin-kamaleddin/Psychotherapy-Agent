### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicates nonjudgmental, supportive regard and affirms the patient's feelings and plan, conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking capture both content and meaning, validating emotions and mirroring ambivalence in a nuanced way.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on vaping and the specific goal (no vaping before practice/on school nights), guiding the conversation toward concrete implementation intentions and an immediate action step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in mutual problem-solving by eliciting the patient's knowledge and preferences and integrating their choices into the plan, reflecting genuine shared ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and control by offering options, checking comfort, and framing suggestions as adaptable choices without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from the patient's own values and desired outcomes with forward-looking questions that surface intrinsic motivators and concrete signs of success.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as collaborative and shares decision-making power, inviting the patient's ideas and checking preferences before proceeding.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits, highlights, and amplifies change talk using broad evoking questions and concrete follow-ups that deepen and concretize commitment and plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Effectively redirects away from sustain talk without confronting or reinforcing it, but misses opportunities for more strategic double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence more directly.
