# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and normalized Elena's difficulty with a nonjudgmental tone, which kept her open and cooperative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, sustained perspective-taking with reflective summaries that matched Elena's stress, guilt, and craving statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking reduction and child exposure, steering toward concrete, achievable behavior changes and a specific first-step plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan with repeated invitations for Elena's input and refined her ideas rather than prescribing solutions, showing strong teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, validated Elena's need to cope, and emphasized choice through harm-reduction alternatives rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motives (care for son, reducing guilt) and past successes, but missed opportunities to amplify change language and elicit commitment/confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed Elena as the expert and shared power in decision-making by offering choices and asking which supports she would use.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited desire, reasons, and concrete steps (gum, rules, jar), yet did not consistently probe commitment strength or use scaling to bolster resolve.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and used double-sided reflections to reduce defensiveness, though more selective reinforcement of change talk and exploration of costs of staying the same could further weaken sustain talk.
