# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, validates feelings, and uses nonjudgmental language while normalizing ambivalence. No criticism or minimization was observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening that mirrors both content and emotion and follows reflections with evocative questions. Shows strong perspective-taking and attunement to the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and specific behavioral steps (delay first cigarette, gum, reminders). Keeps the session goal-directed while remaining client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partnership by proposing ideas tentatively, asking permission, and modifying suggestions based on the client's responses. Frequent invitations to choose demonstrate genuine co‑creation of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's sense of choice and control, framing options and inviting preferences while avoiding pressuring language. Acknowledges role limits and reinforces client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and values, asking questions that draw out personal meaning and amplifying change-related responses. Could have probed confidence or past successes a bit more, but overall pulls motivation from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-creates the plan with the client, invites input, and offers help without taking over, clearly sharing power. Decisions are left to the client and the clinician follows the client's lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with change-focused questions and reflections, and elicits concrete steps (gum, reminders). Reflects and reinforces desire, reasons, and commitment to support movement toward action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and often redirects toward change, using acceptance and pragmatic problem-solving. Missed opportunities for more strategic MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or eliciting counterarguments) to further soften sustain talk.
