# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard—validated, normalized, and avoided blame while offering to move at the client's pace, creating a warm, respectful stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used multiple accurate reflections and timely summaries that tracked Amira's themes (helplessness, isolation, skepticism) and deepened understanding rather than just repeating facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing drug use by identifying triggers, offering concrete coping options, setting a specific night plan, and arranging a follow‑up check‑in to prevent drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem‑solving—repeatedly asked for Amira's views, offered menus, and co‑created the night plan and check‑in wording so session decisions were client‑driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices (plans, timing, method), inviting pace‑setting, and affirming any decision without pressure, reinforcing Amira's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change steps and small commitments (night plan, crisis line, check‑in) but could have probed more for intrinsic reasons, values, and used scaling or confidence queries to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed that expertise resides with Amira—asked permission before offering solutions, presented options, and adjusted recommendations to her stated preferences, reflecting genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and translated desire into specific, doable steps, though missed opportunities to probe personal reasons for change and to elicit confidence/commitment ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated sustain talk while redirecting toward small supports and plans, but did not consistently use strategic double‑sided reflections or queries about exceptions to more actively reduce sustain talk.
