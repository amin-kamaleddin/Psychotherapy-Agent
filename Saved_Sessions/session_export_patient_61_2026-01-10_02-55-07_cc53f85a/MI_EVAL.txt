# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, with frequent affirmations and normalization of Jonathan's experience. The clinician maintains unconditional positive regard throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures content and affect, with summaries to check understanding. Validates feelings and consistently echoes hesitancy without judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays focused on starting manageable blood glucose checks and moves from problem identification to a concrete plan and follow-up. The clinician narrows scope and minimizes topic drift while preserving choice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functions as joint problem-solving, seeking Jonathan's input and tailoring suggestions to his routine. The plan and follow-up are co-constructed, showing clear partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, and respecting limits. Language and approach foster ownership of the plan without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses rulers and elicits small, concrete steps but stays focused on logistics rather than exploring intrinsic motives or values. Could ask more value- or future-oriented questions to surface internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician adopts a collaborative stance, invites the patient's expertise, and defers to preferences when selecting options. Decisions are framed as mutual and agreed upon together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces tentative change talk (rulers, one-week trial) and amplifies practical commitments. Would benefit from deeper elicitation of personal reasons and values to strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes avoidance, and reframes barriers into manageable options. Relies mainly on practical problem-solving rather than double-sided reflections or explicit exploration of costs of staying the same.
