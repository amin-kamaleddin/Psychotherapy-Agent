# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard—affirmed efforts, normalized difficulty, and avoided blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective‑taking and timely reflective listening that mirrored the client's language and emotional concerns, facilitating disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on a specific target (calling the Jobcentre) and guided the session toward concrete, measurable steps and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative stance—negotiated the agenda, co‑created scripts, and divided tasks so the plan reflected the client's preferences and lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserved choice by offering options, checking what the client wanted, and framing suggestions as supports rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values and reasons for change (Fiona, ‘tea on the porch’) and asked confidence, but at times moved quickly to solutions instead of deepening the client's own arguments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert and repeatedly invited his input on choices and next steps, reinforcing an equal‑partner dynamic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with specific planning and rehearsal, though could have lingered more to deepen spontaneous commitment language before offering solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Addressed barriers and brainstormed coping strategies but did not consistently use advanced softening techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) and sometimes shifted to logistics or direct accountability.
