# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through nonjudgmental language and supportive praise, avoiding lecturing or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring frustration and summarizing barriers so the patient felt understood and safe to share.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking with actionable, measurable experiments and a planned follow-up while avoiding a directive stance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting patient choices, co-designing the experiment, and incorporating her input on reminder wording and check-in content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy with choice-focused language, offering options rather than prescriptions and honoring the patient's decisions about the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intrinsic motivation and ideas and secured a specific experiment, but could have probed deeper into values (e.g., daughter’s health) or used scaling to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reflected clear partnership and power-sharing, repeatedly asking preferences and letting the patient shape the plan and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change language and low-pressure experiments, but missed opportunities to amplify, summarize, or elicit confidence/importance ratings to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected without confrontation, though more double-sided reflections or explicit reframes could further pivot toward change reasons.
