# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing ambivalence and validating the client's pace. No blaming language and clear respect for Ryan's priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking that captured the push–pull of Ryan's feelings. Reflections were sometimes repetitive and the clinician occasionally moved quickly to logistics instead of exploring deeper affect like embarrassment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on risky drinking and avoiding another incident, progressively steering toward concrete, actionable steps. Effectively narrowed options to a single prioritized, doable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in joint problem-solving by inviting Ryan's ideas, building on them, and checking his preferences. Language and questions preserved an equal partnership and ensured Ryan's plan shaped the outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently endorsed Ryan's choice and control by offering options and avoiding pressuring language. Allowed Ryan to select the strategy and affirmed his ability to act at his own pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified client-generated motivations and solutions rather than prescribing them. Used evocative questions that led Ryan to articulate commitment and concrete steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative with repeated invitations to co-create the plan and deference to Ryan's judgment. Encouraged shared decision-making and honored the client's expertise about what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited, reflected, and reinforced multiple change statements, prompting solution-generation and commitment. Skillfully operationalized client language into specific actions he agreed to prioritize.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and effectively redirected to strategy-building, reducing reinforcement of reasons to maintain drinking. Missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections that would explicitly hold ambivalence and deepen discrepancy.
