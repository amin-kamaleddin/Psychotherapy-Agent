# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Marek's priorities without shaming or minimization. Language and questions were neutral and respectful, supporting acceptance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and summaries that captured ambivalence and built rapport. Missed opportunities to probe deeper feelings or values behind the social drinking to reach a fuller empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing weekend drinking and steadily narrowed from broad options to a specific action plan. Questions and suggestions all advanced actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with iterative clinician–client refinement of options and supports. The plan emerged from Marek's choices rather than clinician prescription.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Marek's freedom to choose, offered options, and avoided pressuring language. Clinician validated hesitancy and built a plan that fit Marek's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical reasons and implementation intentions that moved Marek toward change, drawing motivations from him rather than supplying them. However, the clinician focused more on logistics than deeper intrinsic motives and did not use scaling or summaries to amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Marek as the expert and co‑created the plan with repeated invitations to choose which options felt doable. Language emphasized genuine power‑sharing throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced pragmatic change talk, helping Marek progress from ambivalence to concrete commitments. Could have more explicitly probed desire/importance/confidence and elicited a stronger time‑bound commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk and repeatedly pivoted to solution‑focused exploration. At times reflections mirrored sustain‑talk language without explicitly framing ambivalence or using double‑sided reflections to more actively soften it.
