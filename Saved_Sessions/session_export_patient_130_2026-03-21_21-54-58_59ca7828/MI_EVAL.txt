# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing ambivalence and offering explicit support. The clinician avoided labeling and maintained unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated both sides of Viktor’s experience and elicited concrete examples. Could deepen empathic exploration of underlying emotions (shame, fear) and use more complex/amplified reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on reducing weeknight use with repeated returns to concrete steps and a one-week trial. Goal-directed questions linked the plan to Viktor’s stated value of more peace at home.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session read as genuine joint problem-solving with Viktor choosing goals and shaping scripts and exit strategies. The clinician offered options and role-play while letting Viktor set the level of practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Viktor’s sense of choice by offering options, asking permission to role-play, and inviting him to decide which strategies to try. Avoided coercion and reinforced ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully drew out Viktor’s reasons, confidence, barriers, and strategies using open questions and scaling. Focused on Viktor’s resources and past successes to elicit concrete change talk and planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine power-sharing by asking Viktor to pick doable changes and co-creating specific responses and contingency plans. The clinician checked in about Viktor’s preferences and readiness throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk with scaling, specific planning, and commitment language that produced concrete in-session commitments. Used questions to boost confidence and translate ambivalence into a practical one-week trial.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently redirected sustain talk toward manageable steps and limits. Missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to more fully neutralize resistance.
