# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's caution, avoiding blame or minimization. Treats the patient's safety boundary with respect rather than challenge.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing both content and affect throughout the session. Mirrors the patient's priorities and worry without minimizing them.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on safe initiation of citalopram and progressively narrows to concrete next steps. Questions and offers are tightly tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly two-way, with inclusive language and repeated invitations for the patient's input on wording, contact method, and callback. Offers to draft materials but asks for patient approval, demonstrating shared shaping of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly preserves choice and honors the patient's boundary about not starting without written confirmation. Offers options and asks what would feel most helpful, reinforcing patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivations and preferences tied to safety and logistics, drawing out actionable reasons to proceed after confirmation. Less exploration of deeper intrinsic motivations, values, or scaling of readiness/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Cedes expertise and decision-making power to the patient and invites preferences about format and process. Explicitly asks to approve wording, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encourages movement toward concrete planning (one-page, callback, confirmation) which supports change-oriented steps. Missed opportunities to elicit explicit desire/ability/need/confidence language or a commitment statement about starting after confirmation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes the patient's sustain talk without arguing, redirecting toward safety-focused solutions (draft, portal confirmation, callback). Offers concrete options that reduce barriers rather than minimizing concerns.
