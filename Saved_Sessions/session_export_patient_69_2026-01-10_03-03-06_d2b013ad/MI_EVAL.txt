# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Kieran and honoring his limits. The clinician avoided criticism and affirmed honest statements throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used clear reflective listening and summaries that validated Kieran’s perspective and reduced pressure. Empathy was more cognitive/behavioral than affective, with limited deeper emotional reflection (appropriately calibrated to the client's wishes).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on improving compliance and completing paperwork with concrete, actionable steps. The clinician avoided tangents and repeatedly returned the conversation to practical logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative with joint planning of tasks, timing, and document handling; clinician solicited preferences and adjusted accordingly. The clinician directed the logistics (appropriate here) but could invite more client-generated ideas about broader follow-up steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Kieran’s choice by offering options, checking permission, and explicitly honoring boundaries (e.g., no calls to Diaz, no feelings work). Language consistently supported client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited internal motivation focused on reducing oversight and showing up, but primarily pursued logistical solutions. Missed opportunities to draw out intrinsic reasons, values, confidence, or imagined benefits of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership by offering choices and co-creating the plan, repeatedly checking Kieran’s preferences. The clinician led much of the logistical planning, so partnership was strong but not fully equal in generating goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced concrete commitments (keeping appointments, bringing documents) and translated them into actions like a checklist and cover note. However, there were missed opportunities to deepen or broaden change talk using scaling, eliciting reasons, or amplifying small change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing or amplifying sustain talk and validated Kieran while pivoting to workable, low-threat options. Could have used double-sided reflections more to gently surface ambivalence and highlight discrepancy.
