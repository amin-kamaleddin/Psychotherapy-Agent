**Comments:** Offered choices and invited preferences (e.g., ask to name a word, choose breathing vs grounding, “Which would you like to try now?”). Clinician acted as collaborator.

**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced patient control (“Totally okay not to share details,” “If you want another check‑in later, reach out”). Avoided pressuring language.

**Comments:** Limited elicitation of patient’s own reasons for change; mostly preference/fact questions and intensity rating. Few attempts to draw out values or DARN-C language. (Evaluator originally rated 2/5; adjusted here to reflect minimal evocation)

**Comments:** Generally partnered tone and shared decision-making, but relied on clinician-initiated structure and repeated scripted closings rather than co-constructing next steps.

**Comments:** Very little client-generated change talk beyond an ambiguous “maybe I’ll go for a walk.” Missed opportunities to amplify or elicit DARN-C language after breathing success.
