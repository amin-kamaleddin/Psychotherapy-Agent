# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, inviting the student's perspective. Avoided blame and treated Anya as competent and cooperative throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening with concise reflections and normalization of feelings. Reflections were often brief and the clinician moved quickly to solutions rather than exploring deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on adolescent alcohol exposure, refusal skills, safety planning, and behavioral rehearsal. The session remained goal-oriented and progressed logically toward practical practice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engaged in a collaborative coaching exchange and co-constructed practice scripts with Anya, inviting her preferences. At times the clinician supplied multiple specific strategies up front, which reduced some client control.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission for role-play, and normalizing any decision. Repeatedly emphasized that Anya chose what to practice, reinforcing ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts to draw out concerns and planning but the session was largely clinician-driven with ready-made solutions. Missed opportunities to elicit Anya's personal reasons, values, or deeper motivations for avoiding alcohol.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by offering options and inviting choice, and used inclusive language during practice. Occasionally adopted a more expert/teaching role by offering scripts before fully eliciting Anya's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some concrete change-oriented statements and practical planning but relied heavily on offering strategies and scripts. Rarely reflected or amplified Anya's pro-change statements or explored importance/confidence in depth.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There was little sustain talk and the clinician avoided strengthening status quo, normalizing lower-risk status and focusing on practical supports. Could add more explicit techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) if sustain talk emerges.
