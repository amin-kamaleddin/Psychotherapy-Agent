### Collaboration

**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation and actionable reasons (avoid another infection, bring extras, test small amounts) and built on them. Could use more evocative questions, scaling (importance/confidence), and attempts to elicit commitment or implementation intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—repeatedly asked Adrian's preferences and shared decision-making power (e.g., offering options, role-play, discrete resources). The clinician clearly treated Adrian as the expert on his own life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with options and concrete, doable steps, producing client statements in favor of safer use. To improve, the clinician could amplify change talk with reflective reinforcement, evoke importance/confidence, and pursue commitment language.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated sustain talk while redirecting to harm reduction, using appropriate softening strategies. Could employ more double-sided reflections to subtly highlight discrepancy and more actively reframe sustain talk toward consequences and values.
