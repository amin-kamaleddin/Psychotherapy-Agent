# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—normalized the patient's experience, avoided judgment, and validated constraints like family and work.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective‑taking by accurately capturing emotions and context and mirroring the patient's language throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on resuming short walks with concrete planning (frequency, timing, cues, backups) and did not drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a true joint effort—offered options, solicited the patient's preferences, and built the plan from the patient's selections rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices and asked the patient to choose what fits her life, supporting ownership and avoiding pressure to comply.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's reasons for change and used them in planning but could deepen evocation by probing values, importance/confidence, and drawing out commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative and deferred to the patient's expertise, co‑constructing the plan through open questions and shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and built an implementation plan, though missed some opportunities to amplify desire/ability/importance with scaling or elaboration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered low‑friction alternatives and contingency plans, but could more explicitly use double‑sided reflections or evoke counter‑arguments to further diminish sustain talk.
