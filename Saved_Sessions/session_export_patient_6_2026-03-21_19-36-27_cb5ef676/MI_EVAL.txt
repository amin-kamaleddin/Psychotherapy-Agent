# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used nonjudgmental, supportive language, normalized distress, checked safety, and affirmed Alexei’s concerns, conveying strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There are clear empathic acknowledgements and brief reflections of stress and fears, but the clinician often shifted quickly to solutions and missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on adolescent alcohol use and its relevance to soccer and scholarships, with interventions consistently tied to preventing future drinking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tone and activities (choice of role-plays, practicing together) were collaborative, though the clinician frequently supplied ready-made scripts rather than co-constructing responses with Alexei.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician respected Alexei’s agency by asking permission, offering options, and inviting his preferences, avoiding coercive language and supporting choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some linking to Alexei’s values elicited change language, but the clinician relied heavily on giving solutions instead of systematically drawing out internal motivations with open, evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invited input and shared decisions (role-play choices, who to speak with), yet delivered many prescriptive lines instead of frequently generating language together with Alexei.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician created openings and tied content to goals, and Alexei voiced change language, but the clinician did not consistently elicit, reflect, or amplify those statements with evocative questions or selective reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and gently reframed without arguing, effectively redirecting to strategies, though more double-sided reflections could have better balanced validation and movement toward change.
