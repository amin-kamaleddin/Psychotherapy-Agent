# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing the patient's struggle and praising small successes; there were no instances of shaming or blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections captured both content and emotion and the clinician mirrored the patient's language and checked in about experiences, fostering a strong empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on avoidance and building follow-through with concrete behavioral experiments and sequenced micro-tasks, consistently guiding toward actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort by staying with the patient during exercises, soliciting input, and adapting to preferences, treating the patient as an equal partner throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control through offering options, seeking permission, and accepting the patient's pace and decisions, reinforcing agency without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited confidence ratings and micro-commitments that produced change talk, but relied more on clinician-suggested strategies than drawing out the patient's deeper reasons, values, or long-term motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered collaboration with permission and joint problem-solving, but at times offered detailed, prescriptive plans rather than eliciting more patient-generated solutions to strengthen shared planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk via questions, affirmations, and confidence ratings that led to concrete actions, yet missed opportunities to amplify and summarize the patient's change language and commitment reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully limited sustain talk by redirecting to small achievable steps, but used few explicit MI strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or ambivalence summaries) to more directly soften sustain arguments.
