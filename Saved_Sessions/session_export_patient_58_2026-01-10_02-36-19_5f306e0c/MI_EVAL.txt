# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language (e.g., calling Jamal the expert and normalizing small steps), conveying respect throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking that capture motivations and ambivalence, though reflections are sometimes repetitive and miss deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on getting to school on time with concrete steps and measurable tests, progressing logically from goal to plan to observable outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed—clinician solicits Jamal's preferences, past strategies, and jointly decides on the test, framing the work as shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Jamal's choice and control (e.g., 'you're the expert,' asking which step feels doable), avoids pressuring language and reinforces ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, strengths, and concrete change language, but could probe more to shift external motivations toward deeper intrinsic values and ask for scaled commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine partnership by inviting Jamal's expertise, offering options, and sharing decision-making rather than lecturing or prescribing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with targeted questions and reflections, producing concrete commitment language and a clear, testable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk but often mirrors externally framed reasons (e.g., avoiding nagging) and misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or exploring the costs of the status quo.
