# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance, validated ambivalence repeatedly, and followed Elise’s lead, demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections and perspective-taking were accurate and timely, capturing both sides of the ambivalence and helping Elise expand on feelings and specifics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on smoking cessation and its social context, moving logically from exploration to a concrete, rehearsed work-break plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was co-produced: the clinician checked preferences, solicited input, adjusted the plan to Elise’s suggestions, and rehearsed language together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supported choice by offering options, asking which to pursue, accepting Elise’s pacing, and reinforcing her control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicited Elise’s own motivations and resources through values work, future-self imagery, examples, and scaling rather than supplying external reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed the session as collaborative, shared power in decision-making, and integrated Elise’s wording and tweaks into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was elicited via values questions and scaling, but the clinician missed some opportunities to explicitly reflect and amplify statements (e.g., wanting to be present for family, the 4/10 fit) to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician rolled with sustain talk and redirected to ambivalence and coping, but relied mostly on neutral reflections rather than strategic double-sided reflections or summaries to diminish sustain talk.
