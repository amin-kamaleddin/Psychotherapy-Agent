# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, normalized frustration, and honored the patient’s priorities without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained, accurate reflections captured the patient’s perspective and emotional weight, helping to calm defensiveness and build rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on a mutually agreed task—preparing the patient for dental care while continuing to smoke—and moved toward a concrete outcome (printed handout).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted genuine two-way problem solving by inviting the patient to set the agenda and iteratively co-constructing the handout with their input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed the patient’s right to choose, offered multiple options, and deferred to the patient’s preferences at each decision point.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did little to elicit intrinsic motivation for smoking cessation, avoiding brief invitations to explore desires, reasons, or hopes for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used inclusive language and shared decision-making, allowing the patient substantial influence over phrasing, format, and delivery of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Missed opportunities to evoke or amplify any change talk; respected avoidance of quit discussion but did not use brief, curiosity-based prompts to explore change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided reinforcing sustain talk by honoring resistance and redirecting to a neutral, patient-prioritized task, preserving rapport.
