# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent acknowledgments of the patient’s choices and plans, demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some empathic acknowledgments (safety, practical concerns) but limited reflective listening and few reflections of the patient’s minimization or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, focused agenda on home BP monitoring and alcohol reduction with concrete protocols, thresholds, and a specific follow‑up timeline.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and supportive with invitations to choose, but the clinician provided heavy informational content and could have elicited more patient expertise and joint problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by asking permission, offering options, and framing recommendations as choices without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited minimal internal motivation—relying mostly on factual questions and information provision rather than drawing out personal reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through permission and choices and built on the patient’s plan, though more explicit co‑creation and patient-led planning would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supported some change talk and affirmed the patient’s plan, but missed opportunities to deepen or amplify change talk with reflective summaries and evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered neutral information, but used few strategic techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to soften minimization and sustain talk.
