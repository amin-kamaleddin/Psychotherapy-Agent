Quick step-by-step cues
- Wall push-up: hands shoulder-width at chest height; step back so body is a straight plank from head to heels; hinge at elbows, lower nose/chest toward wall, elbows ~45°, press back.
- Incline (counter) push-up: hands on stable surface, body straight, lower chest to edge, keep core tight.
- Knee push-up: hands under shoulders, knees down, hips neutral, lower chest to floor then press.
- Seated press (bottles/dumbbells): sit tall, core braced, press straight up without arching; keep neck long.
- Band press: anchor at chest height, step forward, press forward while bracing core.
