# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Sofia's feelings and normalizing coping. Avoided blaming or minimizing even when the patient was defensive.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening with accurate reflections of affect and perspective and checked safety in a caring way. Offered immediate soothing options when distress was apparent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on peer conflict, social reconnection, and coping with concrete, measurable steps (DM drafts, timing, follow-up window). Guided the session toward actionable plans linked to Sofia's concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-created messages, repeatedly solicited Sofia's preferences, and refined wording together. Sought the patient's input before proceeding and shared decision-making throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking which she preferred, and honoring declines without pressure. Framed suggestions as choices and reinforced Sofia's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intentions and small preparatory steps effectively (choice of message, timing), but did not sufficiently draw out deeper motivations or values. Could use more evocative open questions and scaling to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a genuine partnership by deferring to Sofia's expertise, offering choices, and jointly constructing the plan. Power was shared through collaborative drafting and preference-driven decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change-oriented language and supported small achievable steps (message choice, timing), but missed opportunities to amplify and summarize change talk or use scaling questions to strengthen commitment. More explicit reinforcement of expressed hopes would increase impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to practical options with validation, which is appropriate MI practice. Could increase use of targeted strategies (double-sided reflections, explicit reframing) to further soften sustain talk.
