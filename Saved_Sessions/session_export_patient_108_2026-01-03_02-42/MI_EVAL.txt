### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Oliver’s feelings (e.g., “you’ve been generous and kind… wanting boundaries now is reasonable,” “That’s courageous and kind”). No judgment; repeated validating language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeated, accurate reflections of Oliver’s perspective and ambivalence (payoffs and costs). Timely complex reflections that deepen exploration rather than shifting to unsolicited advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays focused on target behavior (asking flatmate to move). Clinician repeatedly elicits concrete next steps (draft → rehearse → text tonight; two‑week deadline; offer contacts), keeping clear momentum toward action.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Interaction framed as a joint project; clinician invites Oliver’s expertise and preferences (“What do you think would be the most doable first step…?”, offers help only as needed). Plan is co‑constructed.

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Clinician emphasizes Oliver’s choice and agency, asks what he wants and when he will act, and offers optional assistance (e.g., polishing wording) without pressuring.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Clinician draws out Oliver’s motivations, past successes, and strategies. Elicits and expands change talk (reasons, desire, ability, plans) across the session.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Tone and language position clinician as an empathic partner (“I hear you,” collaborative questions). Oliver’s ideas drive the plan and clinician builds on them.

**Comments:** Multiple instances of change talk elicited and reinforced (desire for space, intention to draft/text, concrete strategies). Clinician reflects and amplifies commitment language throughout.
