# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and warmth, validating and normalizing Sofia's experience to create a safe, nonjudgmental space. A brief normalization of common setbacks (e.g., many people struggle after a move) could further reinforce acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections of content and feeling helped Sofia feel understood and encouraged elaboration. Occasional deeper affect-focused reflections (naming disappointment or grief) might access emotional drivers more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior (sustainable increases in movement) and moved logically from values to a concrete plan. The session stayed appropriately goal-directed without imposing solutions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as co-created by inviting input at each step and incorporating Sofia's preferences into the plan. The clinician checked back frequently and shared decision-making throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Sofia's sense of choice by offering options, avoiding pressure, and asking what felt best for her. This reinforced ownership and internal motivation for the chosen plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Sofia's own reasons, strategies, and strengths using her language, which amplified intrinsic motivation. A brief confidence/importance ruler could have further strengthened commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Sofia as the expert and the clinician as a collaborator, using invitational language and co-constructing the plan. This approach promoted shared power and engagement in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited, amplified, and reinforced concrete change talk, helping Sofia specify actionable steps (10-minute walks, alarm, shoes by the door, fallback). Recaps and affirmations sustained and deepened her change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk neutrally and redirected toward planning, avoiding argumentation and validating concerns about stress derailing plans. The clinician could more systematically use double-sided reflections or explicit rolling-with-resistance language to further soften sustain talk.
