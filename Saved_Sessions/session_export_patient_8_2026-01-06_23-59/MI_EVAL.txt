# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence without blame, using warm, nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate summaries, but responses became somewhat formulaic and could deepen exploration of emotional texture (guilt, restlessness).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on phone/social media use and related outcomes, guiding the conversation toward a concrete, testable experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative and the plan emerged from the client, though the clinician sometimes led with menus and sequencing rather than fully equal mutual agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and used permissive phrasing, encouraged experimentation over pressure, and reinforced that the client decides what and how to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicited motivation and solutions from the client; most strategies and the concrete plan were generated by the client after invitational questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited the client's input, but occasionally used directive options and could more explicitly invite the client to set the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled at eliciting and amplifying change talk with targeted open questions and reflections, helping the client articulate a specific, measurable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk with useful double-sided reflections, but did not consistently use strategic pivots to actively reduce sustain talk or amplify discrepancy.
