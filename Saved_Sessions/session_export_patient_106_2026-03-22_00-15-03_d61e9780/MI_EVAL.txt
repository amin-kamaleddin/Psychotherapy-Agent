# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's struggle, creating a warm nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely complex reflections and perspective-taking that paraphrase ambivalence and deepen the patient's expression, which the patient confirms and expands on.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction/cessation and moves the conversation from values to concrete planning (supports, speed-dial/code word, backups, 10-minute marker).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages in joint exploration and co-constructs a practical plan, but could strengthen collaboration by explicitly co-weighing options and probing potential obstacles before finalizing the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the patient's choice, avoids pressure or judgment, and offers selectable options so the patient retains control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's own motivations and solutions through values- and strengths-focused questions, resulting in patient-generated, actionable strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as collaborative and solicits the patient's expertise, though occasional expert-style reassurance slightly reduces purely elicitive partnering.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and elicits specifics of change talk, leading the patient to articulate concrete, testable steps (e.g., speed dial, code word, tea).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk and uses neutral reframing, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections, exploring the costs of sustain talk, and more strategic softening to tip ambivalence.
