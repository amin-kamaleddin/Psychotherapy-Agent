# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating—normalizes struggle and offers frequent affirmations. Demonstrates genuine positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing barriers and readiness. Reflections are often plan-focused with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions like shame or discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on building small daily habits and reducing home temptations. Repeatedly brings discussion back to concrete next steps and avoids drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine two-way problem-solving, soliciting Marco’s ideas and building on them to co-create plans. Offers brief role-play and shared decision-making rather than top-down instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely offers options, checks preferences, and frames suggestions as choices to preserve client control. Even scripted lines are presented as optional and collaborative.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits pragmatic motivations and uses scaling to draw out change talk, increasing specificity and commitment. Could deepen evocation by exploring personal values or why the change matters at a deeper level.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters power-sharing and positions Marco as the expert, asking which options feel most doable and inviting his choices. Co-constructs the plan with repeated invitations to select and shape actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk using scaling, targeted questions, and reflections which led to more specific commitment and a confidence increase. Reinforces small wins and elicits concrete if‑then plans and scheduling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects toward practical coping strategies and backups, reducing its influence. Would be strengthened by more double-sided reflections and selective reinforcement of ambivalence language.
