PSYCHIATRIST: Two quick latch fixes (dim light):
1) Ticklish top lip: stroke her upper lip until she opens wide, aim nipple to the roof of her mouth, then bring her whole body to you (not breast to baby).  
2) Asymmetric latch: aim more areola under her top lip so her chin and lower jaw take most of the breast.

Breast compressions (without waking her):
- Wait until sucking slows but she’s still sucking.  
- Support breast in a C (fingers under, thumb on top behind areola).  
- Gently compress toward the chest wall and hold until you see/sense swallowing, then release slowly.  
- Repeat during active suck cycles; keep movements small and steady.
