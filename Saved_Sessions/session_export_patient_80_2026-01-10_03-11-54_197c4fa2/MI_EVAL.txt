# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment, using warm validating language and affirmations that preserve Luka’s dignity. There is no evidence of labeling or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring Luka’s language and checking meaning with targeted questions. Reflections are timely and deepen the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol use and preventing outbursts, moving logically from values to concrete, actionable steps and rehearsals. Questions and reflections consistently tie back to agreed behavioral goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly egalitarian with routine invitations for Luka’s input and co-design of the plan, positioning the clinician as a partner rather than an authority. Luka’s ideas meaningfully shape the plan and practice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly normalizes ambivalence, emphasizes Luka’s control and pace, and asks permission-style questions while respecting his limits. The plan is co-designed to fit Luka’s readiness without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits internal motivations, priorities, and strengths leading to concrete planning, but misses deeper exploration of core values and long-term motives. Opportunities remain to consolidate evoke statements into stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician frames the encounter as collaborative, inviting Luka to choose priorities and design steps, and rehearses solutions together. Power is shared throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicits and amplifies change talk and practical steps, but stops short of systematically strengthening and sequencing change talk into a firm commitment or using scaling tools. More targeted elicitation of reasons and confidence would deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician empathically acknowledges sustain talk and avoids confrontation, redirecting toward small achievable steps, but often repeats sustain-leaning phrases without using double-sided reflections or reframing to reduce sustain strength. There is limited explicit work exploring costs of staying the same.
