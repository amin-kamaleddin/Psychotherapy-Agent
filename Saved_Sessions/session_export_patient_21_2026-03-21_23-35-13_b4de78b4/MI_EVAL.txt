# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Camila’s struggle, using affirmations and praise for small steps without judgment. Warm, validating language reinforces competence and avoids labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections capture both content and emotion and the clinician checks understanding with open questions. Summaries and perspective-taking demonstrate strong empathic listening throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity and moves logically from values to actionable steps (walks, fallback, alarm). Repeatedly brings conversation back to concrete behavior and measurable plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with client input driving the plan, but the clinician occasionally seeds ideas which edges toward problem-solving. More frequent elicitation of client proposals before offering suggestions would strengthen collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves client choice by offering options, asking preferences, and framing interventions as suggestions rather than commands. Emphasizes the client’s control in co-creating the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out client motivations and reasons for change through evocative questions, eliciting energy and presence goals. Could deepen evocation by amplifying ability and commitment language (e.g., confidence scaling, elaborating past successes).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative tone and invites co-creation, centering the client’s preferences. Occasionally shifts toward offering the clinician’s belief or suggestion; asking the client to propose options first would enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk by asking about values, past helpful strategies, and a one-week trial, and reflects client confidence in small steps. Could more systematically amplify and summarize specific change statements and use scaling to solidify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk with normalization and low-pressure fallbacks, avoiding argumentation. Increasing use of double-sided reflections or brief pros/cons exploration would further soften ambivalence.
