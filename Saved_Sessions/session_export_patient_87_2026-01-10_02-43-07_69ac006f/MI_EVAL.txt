# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes setbacks, avoiding blame; reframes slips as useful data which supports a nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening with accurate, validating reflections of feelings and physical sensations, but reflections are often pragmatic rather than deeply exploratory of underlying emotions or self-view.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on evening snacking with concrete, behaviorally specific goals and purposeful sequencing from assessment to plan and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction reads as cooperative problem‑solving with invitations to plan and follow up, though frequent clinician-provided lists and scripts sometimes steer the session more than co‑construction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, asks what the patient wants to try, and emphasizes choice and self‑compassion, reinforcing Marco's control over decisions and adjustments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits useful commitments and self‑observations (confidence scaling, specific tonight plan), but could more fully draw out values, past successes, and broader intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by asking the patient to choose strategies and offering follow‑up, yet repeatedly supplying specific strategies and scripts reduces some of the shared construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and strengthens change talk (confidence scaling, securing concrete commitments) but misses opportunities to amplify the patient's own reasons and past successes before offering strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk skillfully by normalizing difficulty, reframing slips as learning, and redirecting toward small experiments and plan adjustments without arguing or confronting.
