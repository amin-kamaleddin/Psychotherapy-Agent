# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalization and explicit validation, reducing shame and maintaining a warm, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, repeated reflective listening that captures both content and feeling and invites further elaboration, showing strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on protecting the smoking quit and reducing risky drinking, guiding the session toward concrete, patient-chosen actions without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed: the clinician elicits the patient’s preferences and co-designs the plan so decisions are jointly made rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers options, asks which feels doable, and defers to the patient’s choice of first step, reinforcing the patient’s sense of control and choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient’s own motivations and past successful strategies, building on internal reasons and resources rather than supplying external rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as shared problem-solving, invites the patient to prioritize steps, and explicitly shares power in planning, reflecting a strong partnership orientation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and links past successes to current plans, but could more explicitly consolidate commitment and confidence (e.g., scaling or a verbal summary).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without judgment and uses double-sided reflections and practical strategies, though more selective reinforcement of emerging change language or discrepancy reflections would strengthen this area.
