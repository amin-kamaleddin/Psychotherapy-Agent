### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's reasons and resources (GPA, sleep, past Pomodoro success) and converts them into a small experiment. Could deepen evocation by probing intrinsic values, using scaling (importance/confidence), and amplifying commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently invites the patient's input and treats her as the source of solutions, supporting power-sharing in planning. At times multiple rapid suggestions subtly steer the conversation and there is limited explicit negotiation of the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk, highlights strengths and past successes, leading to concrete commitments (25-minute Pomodoro). Missed opportunities include more amplification, summarizing of change language, and probing for importance/confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids arguing, which prevents escalation, but primarily uses reflective repetition of sustain-language without strategic reframing. More double-sided reflections or reframing of scrolling's function and eliciting alternatives would better reduce sustain talk's weight.
