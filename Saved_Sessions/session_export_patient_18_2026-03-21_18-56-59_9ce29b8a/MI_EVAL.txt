# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, validating, and nonjudgmental throughout the session, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard and encouragement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and accurate reflections of content, but misses some opportunities for deeper affective reflections and emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on underage drinking, refusal skills, and safety planning, moving the session toward concrete action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—invites role-play and preferences and jointly builds a plan—but occasionally leads with solutions rather than first eliciting the client's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, normalizing imperfection, and avoiding coercive language, preserving Anya's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits reasons for change and elicits concrete planning that produced change talk, but could further probe intrinsic motivations and use scaling to consolidate commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance with invitations and shared tasks, yet sometimes shifts toward an expert/advisor role by providing long lists before soliciting the client's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and concrete steps through practice and planning, but could amplify and summarize motivations and ask confidence/importance questions to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence while redirecting to harm-reduction and safety, though an early didactic comment was slightly more directive than optimal.
