# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and affirming small steps to reduce shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that matches the patient's language and validates feelings, fostering rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing drinking and consistently guides the conversation toward concrete, measurable steps without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a joint exploration with frequent invitations for the patient's input and tailoring of the plan to his preferences and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and control by offering options, asking what feels doable, and respecting the patient's preferences for follow-up and strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation and past attempts but leans toward offering strategies; would benefit from deeper open-ended elicitation of personal values and importance/confidence scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the patient as a collaborator, asking permission, inviting choice, and co-constructing a specific, patient-selected plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces tentative change talk and small goals but missed opportunities to probe deeper into the patient's personal reasons, values, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects toward change, though could strengthen by using more double-sided reflections to explicitly name both sides of ambivalence.
