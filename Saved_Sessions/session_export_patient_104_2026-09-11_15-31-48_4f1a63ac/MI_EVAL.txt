# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Nadine's feelings without judgment, validating limits and emotions throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflective listening captured Nadine’s exhaustion and nervousness and appeared to lower resistance and encourage disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on arranging rest via childcare, guiding the conversation toward a concrete, time-specific plan without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a partnership: the clinician solicited preferences, revised drafts per Nadine’s requests, and co-created the final actionable messages.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Nadine’s sense of choice by offering options, asking permission before drafting or sending messages, and suggesting alternatives without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Nadine’s own motivation and a concrete commitment to act, but missed chances to deepen intrinsic motivation by exploring reasons for rest, confidence, or what success would feel like.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Nadine as the expert on her life, inviting her input at each step and sharing power in decision-making about who, when, and how to ask for help.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited specific, doable change talk and reinforced the plan, yet did not explicitly amplify desire/ability/commitment language or assess strength of commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and quickly redirected to solution-focused options to avoid amplifying it, though could have used double-sided reflections to more strategically integrate sustain talk with reasons for change.
