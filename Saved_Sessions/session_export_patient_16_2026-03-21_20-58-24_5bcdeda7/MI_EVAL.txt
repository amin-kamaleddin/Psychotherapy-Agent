# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating the teen’s perspective and normalizing ambivalence through affirmations and supportive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking with accurate reflections and summaries; could deepen emotional reflections to capture underlying stress and identity-related feeling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on quitting for the season and ties every prompt to concrete steps and contingency planning without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as a teammate—solicits the teen’s preferences and voice, co-creates texts and responses, and adapts interventions to the teen’s style.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks permission, and honors the teen’s decisions, reinforcing ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the teen’s own motivations, values, and resources and helps translate them into actionable supports and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the teen as an expert and collaborator by offering options, checking preferences, and co-constructing the plan while following her pace.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reflects, and amplifies change talk, probes confidence, and turns desire and reasons into specific, doable steps and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to change, but missed chances for explicit double-sided reflections or reframing when the teen minimized harm.
