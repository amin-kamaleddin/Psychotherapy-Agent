# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and strengths-focused validation through supportive reflections; there is no shaming or minimization of the client's struggle.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, active reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the client's language and linking feelings to her roles and constraints.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing evening alcohol use with concrete, measurable goals (one drink, specific nights, signs of success) and progression toward a week-long experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is genuinely collaborative: the clinician elicits the client's judgments, builds on her ideas, and offers suggestions only after the client identifies options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's choice and control by offering options, inviting preferences, and framing the plan as a test the client can accept or modify.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's intrinsic motivations (mothering values, pride, better sleep) with questions that elicit personally meaningful reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the client as the expert on her life, soliciting input at each step and co-constructing a practical plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk by asking for specific steps, doable nights, signs of success, and celebrations, then reflecting and reinforcing the client's commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids strengthening sustain talk and redirects toward change with small experiments, but missed opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections or naming the appeal of drinking to more actively soften sustain talk.
