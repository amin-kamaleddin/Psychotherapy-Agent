# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard—normalized ambivalence, affirmed efforts, and avoided judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, noticing somatic cues and responding empathetically to Oliver's nervousness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on boundary-setting with interventions tightly linked to the target behavior and a concrete, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited choices and coached rather than taking over, but frequent ready-made scripts and step-by-step instructions shifted some work away from the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options, asked permission before interventions, and left key decisions to Oliver, reinforcing his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited commitment through questions, date selection, and practice, but could have more fully drawn out Oliver's personal reasons and values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative feel and co-constructed the plan, yet relied on directive scripting instead of eliciting Oliver's own language more often.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change-oriented language and commitments, though at times supplied scripts rather than amplifying Oliver's own wording.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk and redirected toward change, but could have used more double-sided reflections to soften resistance before offering solutions.
