# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, validating the patient’s choices and avoiding shaming throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening captured emotional and contextual concerns (shift work, cost, skin irritation), demonstrating strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on daytime NRT (product, dosing, measuring success) and provided practical, time-limited recommendations to move toward a measurable behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co‑constructed the plan with the patient, soliciting preferences and tailoring options to his work context while using role-play and scripting to support shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered choices, checked preferences, and framed the two‑week trial as optional while explicitly allowing continuation of the after‑work cigarette, supporting patient agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited personal reasons for change and observable indicators of success and encouraged problem-solving, but could have probed deeper values or used scaling to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a strong partnership stance by inviting input, respecting the patient’s expertise about his life, and sharing control over specifics like start date and pharmacist scripting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (reduced shakiness, two‑week trial) but missed opportunities to amplify and convert tentative reasons into firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and pragmatically incorporated it into the plan, though could have used double‑sided reflections or summaries to more actively shift sustain talk toward change.
