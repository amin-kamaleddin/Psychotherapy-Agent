# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, supportive stance, validated feelings, and normalized options, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There were multiple accurate reflections and acknowledgment of Ryan's concrete concerns, but reflections remained mostly surface-level and missed deeper affective ambivalence or values exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed tightly focused on preparing for the conduct hearing and academic consequences, with interventions and questions consistently linked to the immediate goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician solicited preferences, co-created rehearsal scripts, and offered role-play, but relied on a prescriptive checklist and ready-made scripts that made some elements more clinician-driven than ideal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Choices were repeatedly offered (grounding vs. planning, short vs. fuller statement, rehearsing, legal consult), though directive checklist language occasionally reduced the sense of pure autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited around academics and consequences, but much of the momentum came from clinician-provided templates rather than probes into Ryan's own values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was generally collaborative with invitations to choose wording and rehearse, yet partnership was limited by offering templates before asking Ryan to generate his own language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicited some change talk and framed tasks toward academics, but did not consistently use evocative questions or reflections to amplify and explore Ryan's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or discrepancy statements, moving quickly to pragmatic planning instead of softening ambivalence.
