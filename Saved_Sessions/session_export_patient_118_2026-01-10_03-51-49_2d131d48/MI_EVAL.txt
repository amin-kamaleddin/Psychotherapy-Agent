# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and honored Seamus’s boundaries, avoiding lecturing or challenging. Explicitly agreed not to raise smoking unless he asked, reinforcing nonjudgmental acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking, validating Seamus’s priorities and fatigue with repeated advice. Paraphrased his ratings and side-effect experiences, demonstrating strong empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on the patient’s immediate goal of dental care while briefly addressing smoking and then reorienting to a concrete plan. Could be strengthened by later re-linking dental risks to motivation in a nonconfrontational way.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑designed the one-page checklist, checked formatting and copies, and accepted Seamus’s preferences. The interaction reflected joint problem‑solving rather than clinician-driven instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported choice by asking permission before offering information and honoring his decision to defer quit-related discussion. Reinforced patient control with offers to hold options for later and to avoid raising smoking unless asked.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempted to elicit motivation with importance/confidence questions and past attempts but did not follow up to deepen or draw out internal reasons. Missed brief, nonpressuring prompts that could have amplified internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine partnership by inviting preferences, co-creating checklist content, and checking details like heading and anonymity. Deferred to Seamus’s choices throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited brief change language (e.g., 4/10 importance, 'got to really want to') and offered non-pill options, but did not systematically follow up to amplify or explore those statements. Opportunities to deepen reasons and confidence were not pursued.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not amplify sustain talk, reframing the agenda to match Seamus’s priorities and holding options for later. Could increase impact with more strategic double‑sided reflections to gently highlight ambivalence.
