# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, affirmed values and praised concrete steps, and used warm, nonjudgmental language that normalized difficulty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening that captured both content and underlying meaning, guiding helpful follow-up questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, specific focus on alcohol-related behavior and strategies, avoiding drift and steering toward a small, achievable next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner with the patient, co-constructing the plan and repeatedly seeking the patient's input and priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported the patient's sense of choice by inviting preferences and avoiding coercive language, promoting ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's internal motivations and resources, linking change to what matters to him and building a self-authored plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the patient as the expert on his life, using questions that invited him to lead planning and craft wording for accountability.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk with open questions and reflections, converting ideas into concrete, actionable commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence and avoided colluding with sustain talk, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and reframing to more directly pivot sustain language toward change.
