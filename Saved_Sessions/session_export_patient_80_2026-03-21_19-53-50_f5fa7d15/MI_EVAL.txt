# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support through affirmations and acceptance of imperfect follow-through. Normalized ambivalence and performed an explicit early safety check.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries and clarifying questions. Relied more on informational/summary reflections and missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflections (fear, shame, relief).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing violence risk and managing alcohol-related triggers throughout the session. Interventions (safety plan, breathing, scripts) were tightly linked to concrete prevention goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal collaborator in joint problem-solving, presenting interventions as editable and seeking Luka's preferences. Offered help only when invited and co-constructed the safety plan and strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used noncoercive, permission-seeking language and offered multiple options to preserve choice. Honored Luka's caveats about follow-through without shaming.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical solutions and supports but did limited work to draw out internal motivation, values, or confidence. Missed opportunities for open elicitation (scaling, values questions) to deepen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed with frequent invitations for input and permission. Safety plan and strategies were negotiated and tailored to Luka's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created multiple openings and reinforced existing change language (reduced drinking, using supports) and prompted concrete actions. Did not systematically elicit or amplify importance, confidence, or commitment language with scaling or strategic reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward manageable, actionable steps while accepting ambivalence. Could have used double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to further tip the balance toward change.
