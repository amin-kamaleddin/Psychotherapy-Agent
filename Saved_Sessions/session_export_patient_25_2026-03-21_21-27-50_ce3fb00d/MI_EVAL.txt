# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and affirmed Daniel’s efforts, normalizing success and respecting his pace. Responses avoided judgment and validated ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrated accurate perspective‑taking with frequent, well‑targeted reflections and paraphrases. They linked Daniel’s concerns to his values, showing a strong understanding of his experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a clear focus on alcohol-related behavior change and progressed from values to specific, behaviourally‑specific strategies. They resisted topic drift and moved toward concrete implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was a genuine joint effort, with the clinician inviting Daniel to shape scripts, mocktail choices, and support plans. Language and rehearsal fostered mutual problem‑solving rather than directive instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently framed suggestions as options and sought Daniel’s choices, reinforcing his sense of control. Information and scripted options were offered without coercion to support patient ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician drew motivation from Daniel by eliciting values, using scaling questions, and asking what would move the numbers up. This generated patient‑derived strategies and built commitment through planning and rehearsal.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician treated Daniel as the expert on his life, co‑constructing the plan and repeatedly asking which options he preferred. Power and decision‑making remained with the patient throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician actively elicited and reinforced change talk via specific questions, scaling, and invitations to elaborate on strategies. They affirmed choices and used confidence ratings to deepen and consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician validated uncertainty without arguing and gently redirected toward planning and rehearsal to minimize sustain talk. Problem‑solving options and a neutral, collaborative tone reduced reasons for maintaining the status quo.
