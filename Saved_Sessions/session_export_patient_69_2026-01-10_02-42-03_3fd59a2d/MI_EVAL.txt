# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating exhaustion and allowing the patient to set limits throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely, accurate reflections that matched the patient's tone and internal state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on engagement and brief experiments, progressing logically from assessment to a concrete, measurable action step without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was consistently collaborative: the clinician invited choices, integrated the patient's preferences, and co-constructed the implementation details and follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized choice and framed the plan as an experiment, avoided pressure, and reinforced the patient's control over timing and limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited a concrete, low-effort commitment and used readiness scaling, but did not sufficiently explore deeper intrinsic motivations or values to expand reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance throughout, treated expertise as residing with the patient, and invited the patient to guide decisions about the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evoked and reinforced small change (options, readiness scale, a specific experiment) but missed opportunities to deepen and amplify the patient's tentative change talk into stronger desire/reason/ability/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently redirected toward experimentation using validation, though could have used double-sided reflections or gentler discrepancy techniques to more actively soften sustain talk.
