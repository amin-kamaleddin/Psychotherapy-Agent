# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, affirmed efforts, and avoided labeling or shaming language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections that captured both emotional and factual content and were accepted by the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior, narrowing options and co-creating a specific micro-plan with time, backup, and confidence rating.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a true partner by soliciting preferences, accepting client tweaks, and jointly constructing the plan and reminders.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed client choice and control, offered multiple options, and respected limits while providing support and editable resources.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations and past successes to surface intrinsic reasons for change, but missed some opportunities to deepen motivation with values or vivid future-oriented questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert, invited input on wording and reminders, and co-created steps so decisions were made jointly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change talk and commitments (e.g., rehearsal plan, alarms, reward) and used a confidence ruler, but could have amplified brief change talk and probed importance/desire more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, normalized concerns, and offered small-step options, though it lacked more explicit double-sided reflections to highlight and resolve ambivalence.
