# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent validating and normalizing statements that conveyed warmth and positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective‑taking, but missed opportunities to deepen emotional content or use complex reflections about feelings like loneliness or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, goal‑directed focus on increasing physical activity with a concrete micro‑plan and a scheduled one‑week check‑in, repeatedly returning to implementation and troubleshooting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was largely joint—eliciting preferences and negotiating follow‑up—but frequent ready‑made instructions and checklist elements limited deeper co‑construction of routine details.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, adaptations, and invitations to change the plan or message midweek, reinforcing Amira's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation (readiness rating, benefits) and set monitoring tasks, but could have used more open questions to draw out values and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative tone with shared planning and invitations for support, though the prescriptive micro‑plan and checklist slightly shifted power back toward the clinician rather than fully co‑creating details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (readiness, benefits) and affirmed statements, but could have amplified and probed further to deepen commitment beyond scheduling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and skillfully redirected reluctance into flexible, practical options and pacing, respecting Amira's preference to focus on activity first.
