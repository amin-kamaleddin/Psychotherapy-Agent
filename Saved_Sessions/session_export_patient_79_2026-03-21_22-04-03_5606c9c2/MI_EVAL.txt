# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes frustration, and explicitly honors the client's boundaries and limits throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good reflective listening and echoes the client's perspective, but could deepen empathy by exploring the emotional subtext behind sarcasm and resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on one specific target behavior (routine inhaler use) with concrete implementation details and an appropriate brief safety check.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine co‑construction of the plan by offering options, checking preferences, and repeatedly adjusting based on the client's input rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice by offering options, asking permission, respecting stated boundaries, and inviting the client to change the plan if desired.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits change talk, concrete plans, and confidence levels, but could better evoke intrinsic motivators by linking the plan to personal values or longer‑term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fosters shared decision‑making, negotiates limits (no lectures/no surprise contacts), and repeatedly checks that the plan fits the client's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively shapes change talk into a small, specific experiment and asks about indicators of success, yet could amplify and reflect change statements more to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes low readiness while redirecting to manageable steps, but could more directly soften sustain talk with double‑sided reflections or strategic reframing.
