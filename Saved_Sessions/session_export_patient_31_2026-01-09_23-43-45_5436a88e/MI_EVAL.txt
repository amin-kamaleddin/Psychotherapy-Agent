# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's priorities with nonpunitive, validating language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with repeated paraphrases and summaries that capture context and affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on reducing daytime shakiness with every question and reflection linked to concrete actions (patch, gum, tracking).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaves as an equal partner by inviting the patient's input at each decision point and co-creating the plan rather than prescribing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and control, offering options and affirming the patient's right to keep evening smoking while supporting a self‑directed experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation tied to the patient's priorities and asks evocative questions, but could probe deeper into intrinsic motives and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by repeatedly asking for the patient's choices and tailoring support to his preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits change talk and reinforces a clear, doable plan, yet misses opportunities to amplify and sequence change talk toward explicit commitment or use scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectfully avoids arguing and normalizes sustain talk while reframing the plan as an experiment, though could use double-sided reflections and stronger pivots toward change.
