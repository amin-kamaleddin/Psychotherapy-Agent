# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, warmth, and normalization of concerns without blame, repeatedly affirming the patient's efforts and bravery.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and emotional labeling, mirrors affect, and checks understanding, demonstrating consistent perspective-taking and attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on safe inhaler management and a supervised step‑down plan, consistently returning to monitoring criteria and red‑flag signs with little drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a cooperative enterprise—invites patient input, drafts and refines talking points, and treats the patient as an active partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports choice by asking permission, offering options (including non‑med supports), and endorsing second opinions, avoiding pressure and reinforcing ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits patient's values and concerns and uses an Elicit‑Provide‑Elicit pattern, but could more systematically draw out and amplify intrinsic motivators, DARN statements, and confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—asks priorities and invites role‑play—but at times shifts into an expert role by offering a clinician‑ready plan rather than fully co‑creating each element with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change talk and supplies useful language, but often moves quickly to information/planning instead of reflecting and amplifying the patient's own desire, reasons, ability, need, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers harm‑reduction strategies, but underuses strategic softening techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections, reframes) to pivot doubts toward experimentation.
