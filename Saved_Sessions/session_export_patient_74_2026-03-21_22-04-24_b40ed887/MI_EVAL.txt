# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Linda’s attendance and efforts throughout the session, avoiding blame or shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections of Linda’s ambivalence, but could have used more complex or affect‑laden reflections to explore deeper emotions like fear or guilt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on parental smoking and the child’s exposure, ending with a concrete, time‑bound behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in collaborative problem‑solving by inviting Linda’s input at each step and integrating her solutions while framing offers of help as optional supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Linda’s sense of choice and agency by asking permission before suggesting and explicitly accepting her decision not to quit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic reasons for change (protecting her daughter) and self‑generated strategies, but could have probed values and discrepancies more to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative atmosphere and co‑created the plan, though offering clinician‑crafted wording and step‑by‑step instructions slightly shifted solution‑generation away from Linda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk about protecting the child and concrete planning, yet could have amplified role‑modeling language and strengthened desire/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reflected ambivalence while pivoting to harm‑reduction, though some detailed problem‑solving risked normalizing the habit; a double‑sided reflection could further soften sustain talk.
