# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing the client's feelings and avoiding criticism or coercion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening that captures content, affect, and somatic experience and is well-matched to the client's terse, resistant style.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains an appropriate focus on disengagement and required activities and resists drifting, though could more actively guide toward a brief, client-chosen next step when readiness signals appear.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint exploration, solicits preferences, and consistently honors the client's requests, keeping an egalitarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure or persuasion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits descriptive material about the client's experience but underuses subtle evocation strategies to probe the meaning of behavior or nascent motivations and to amplify tentative change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power and authority about change with the client, repeatedly asking permission and inviting collaboration about the session's focus.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes limited, relatively weak efforts to evoke change talk; honors the client's request to avoid fixes but misses openings to explore or amplify statements like 'I keep showing up.'

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reflects and normalizes sustain talk without arguing, though tends to remain purely reflective rather than using double-sided reflections or subtle pivots toward change language.
