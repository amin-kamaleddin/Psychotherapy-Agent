# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, validating strengths and normalizing struggle throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and often complex, demonstrating good perspective-taking, but the clinician could have probed or validated the emotional (affective) side of test anxiety more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, appropriate focus on reducing test stress through a concrete study strategy and iteratively refined a simple, doable plan with the client.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with the clinician inviting Sofia's input at every step and co-constructing the final plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly emphasized Sofia's choice and control, asked permission before suggestions, and accepted hedging without pressure, reinforcing client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician drew out Sofia's ideas and preferences for strategies, but did not deeply explore underlying values or assess importance/confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treated Sofia as the expert on her life, offering options and routinely deferring to her preferences to co-create the plan and share decision-making power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and amplified tentative change talk and concrete steps, yet could have more directly elicited desire, reasons, confidence, and stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and normalized ambivalence, softening resistance with no-pressure options, but could have used more double-sided reflections or cost/benefit exploration to shift sustain talk.
