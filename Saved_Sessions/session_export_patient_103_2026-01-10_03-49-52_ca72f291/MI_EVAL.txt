# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect with repeated validations and nonjudgmental language, creating a warm, accepting stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, specific reflective listening and perspective-taking (e.g., linking bodily response, thoughts, and values), which helped the patient feel deeply heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on managing responses to appointment delays and progressed logically from exploration to concrete action planning and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was joint problem-solving: the clinician offered expertise while continuously soliciting the patient's input and adjusting interventions to his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently presented multiple options, invited the patient's choice, and respected reluctance, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific intentions and commitments (buffer, script, rehearsal) but could have probed deeper into personal motivations, importance, and confidence to strengthen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treated the patient as the expert on his experience, co-creating plans and repeatedly asking which steps he preferred to try or rehearse.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked actionable change talk and reinforced small commitments (script, buffer), though did not fully explore internal motivations or elicit confidence/strength language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk without reinforcing it, but mainly pivoted to problem-solving and missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or summarizing ambivalence to reduce resistance.
