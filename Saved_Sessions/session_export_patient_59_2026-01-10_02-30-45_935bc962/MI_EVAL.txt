# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed clear, nonjudgmental acceptance and validation throughout, normalizing the mistake and avoiding blame. This fostered rapport with a defensive adolescent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking accurately mirrored Jamal’s concerns and tracked shifts in his language. Reflections reduced friction and supported alliance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly task-focused on producing a concrete deliverable (one-page checklist/timeline) with minimal drift. The clinician repeatedly steered toward operational choices and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly a joint effort, with the clinician inviting Jamal’s input at each decision point and checking for alignment. Options were offered and Jamal shaped the final plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician offered choices, asked preferences, and affirmed Jamal’s control over the format and priorities without coercion. This centered Jamal’s decision-making and respected his autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited pragmatic reasons for change (stop drama, focus on school) and past strengths, but did not deeply explore values, long-term goals, or confidence/commitment. More probes could have deepened intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed with concrete options and repeated preference checks, demonstrating strong power-sharing. Clinician synthesized Jamal’s responses into actionable steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and reinforced pragmatic change talk about avoiding drama and focusing on school, and drew out strengths for follow-through. However, intrinsic desire, confidence, and commitment language were not strongly amplified.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided confrontation and pivoted to practical planning rather than reinforcing sustain talk, which reduced resistance. There is room to more strategically use double-sided reflections or selectively explore ambivalence to shift sustain language.
