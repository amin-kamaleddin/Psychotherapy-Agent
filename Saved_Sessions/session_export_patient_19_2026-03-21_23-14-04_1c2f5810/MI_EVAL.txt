# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, avoiding labeling while validating Anya's feelings. The clinician affirmed strengths and insight throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate empathic listening with multiple reflections and summaries that captured content and feeling. Mirrored ambivalence and used client language to make Anya feel heard and safe to disclose.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on gambling/credit use and continually steered the session toward practical risk-reduction strategies. Repeatedly moved conversation to concrete, time-bound experiments and planning details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative in tone and content, inviting Anya's ideas and co-constructing implementation details. The clinician checked preferences and shaped the plan around her social context and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized choice and framed plans as experiments rather than mandates, avoiding pressuring language. Offered options and deferred to Anya to decide manageable first steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic motivations (travel, independence) and linked them to behavior change, drawing useful imagined outcomes. Could have probed deeper for more emotionally vivid reasons and stronger commitment/confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly conveyed a collaborative, power-sharing stance and treated Anya as the expert in designing the plan. Repeated invitations for her preferences fostered genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and strengthened substantial change talk with concrete steps (e.g., $20 nights, travel jar), and asked forward-looking questions. Missed opportunities to amplify and chain those statements into firmer commitment language or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk without reinforcing it. Could have used more selective reflections and strategic emphasis on change reasons to undercut minimizations like 'just a bit of cash.'
