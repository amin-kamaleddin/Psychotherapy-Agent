# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, used affirmations and normalization, and prioritized safety without shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and pragmatic reflections, but missed some opportunities for deeper, complex reflection on underlying emotions and stressors.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, specific focus on reducing risky alcohol use with concrete harm-reduction options and a time-bound action plan, avoiding drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative—preferences were solicited, patient suggestions were integrated, and the plan was co-constructed with shared responsibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the patient's choices by offering options, inviting selection of strategies, and emphasizing voluntary engagement and no-judgment responses to slips.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation and confidence with open questions and rulers and encouraged self-monitoring, but could probe more for intrinsic, value-based reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a partnership stance by inviting input, letting the patient choose strategies, and working together to create a realistic plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (rulers, planning, debrief) and raised readiness/confidence, though personal values and reasons for change could be explored further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward change-oriented strategies with normalization of slips, but could use more double-sided reflections to strategically acknowledge ambivalence.
