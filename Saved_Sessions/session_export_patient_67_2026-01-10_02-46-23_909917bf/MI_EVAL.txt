### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance with affirmations and normalization; avoided labeling or moralizing throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated perspective-taking and reflective listening and acknowledged Kenji's experience, but reflections stayed summary/practical rather than exploring the deeper emotional texture of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative and co-constructed, repeatedly inviting Kenji's preferences (reminder wording, tracking method, reward) and using them to shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Kenji's sense of choice by offering options, permission not to be perfect, and language that reinforced his ownership of the process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation (noticed impacts, agreed to track) but relied more on practical strategies than on drawing out Kenji's intrinsic reasons or using evocative questions and scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Kenji as an equal partner, asking which steps felt most helpful and letting his choices (timing, wording, reward) guide the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and affirmed some change talk and small steps, but missed opportunities to amplify or deepen those statements with follow-up questions and scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
