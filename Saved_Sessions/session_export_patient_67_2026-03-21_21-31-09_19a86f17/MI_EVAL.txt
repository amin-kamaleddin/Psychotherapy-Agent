# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental tone, normalizing worries and removing blame. Missed entries were framed as acceptable and the clinician affirmed honesty and choices throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and accurate summaries of triggers and concerns, validating feelings and worries. Could deepen emotional exploration (e.g., loneliness, shame) with more affect-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and moved from assessment to a concrete, time-limited 7‑day tracking plan linked to Kenji's goals. Kept momentum with specific follow-up and support arrangements.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving and integrated Kenji's suggestions (alarms, voice note, wording, timing). Used inclusive language and built the follow-up plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options, asked permission, and emphasized choice, framing tracking as neutral data rather than a test. Honored Kenji's preferences about reminders and the midweek check-in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions and scaling to draw out motivations and barriers and connected tracking to personal goals. Could more consistently elicit deeper intrinsic reasons and use scaling follow-ups to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Kenji as the expert in his life, inviting input and co-creating the plan with explicit choices. Power was shared through collaborative decisions on methods and messaging.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, operationalizing intention into a concrete experiment and affirming goals. Could further amplify spontaneous change language with targeted follow-ups about values and consequences.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and did not reinforce sustain talk, using normalization and pivoting to low-risk options like tracking. Could more often employ double-sided reflections and strategic reframing to further weaken sustain talk.
