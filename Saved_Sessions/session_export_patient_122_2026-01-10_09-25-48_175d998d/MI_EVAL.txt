# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through normalization and validating language; no judgment or shaming, which modeled true acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured exhaustion and caution; could deepen emotional inference and linger more on feelings like loneliness or shame instead of moving quickly to planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with concrete planning and measurable indicators (10–15 minute walk, frequency); minimal drift from the change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort and co‑constructed the plan, inviting the client's experience and preferences to shape decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the client's sense of choice by offering options, asking which felt most doable, and avoiding coercive language, reinforcing ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from within the client by eliciting personal reasons, concrete signs of success, and past helpful strategies with targeted evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly invited the client's input, framed decisions as hers, and solicited preferences (e.g., wording, tracking) demonstrating genuine power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk using open questions and reinforcement, focusing on concrete, client‑selected actions and deepening reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and normalized caution while redirecting to small, manageable steps and supports; could use more double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to further reduce sustain talk.
