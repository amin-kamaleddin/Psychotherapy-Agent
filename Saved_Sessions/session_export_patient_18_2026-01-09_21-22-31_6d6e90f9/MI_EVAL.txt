# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental language, normalization, and frequent affirmations. Feedback and suggestions were offered respectfully with no shaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good empathic statements, brief reflections, and checked distress/confidence, showing attention to Anya's internal state. However, empathy was sometimes short and moved quickly into advice rather than deeper exploration of feelings or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on alcohol exposure, peer pressure, and FOMO with actionable strategies tied to the target behavior. Session tasks and interventions stayed directly connected to the agreed target throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session moves were negotiated and co-developed with the client through roleplays and choices, demonstrating equal partnership. The clinician repeatedly sought Anya's input before proceeding with interventions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, using permissive language, and leaving decisions to Anya without pressure. Advice was given after permission and the clinician reinforced Anya's control (e.g., selecting scripts, roleplay options).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation (confidence ruler, statements about wanting help) but primarily offered solutions and scripts rather than drawing out Anya's personal reasons, values, or goals. Missed opportunities to amplify and deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance with shared decision-making, active participation in roleplays, and client-guided choices. Advice and teaching followed checks for comfort and preference, keeping power shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created opportunities for change talk (asked about strategies, used a confidence ruler) that produced some client change language. Relied more on offering scripted lines than eliciting Anya's own reasons or reflecting/amplifying her change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was minimal and the clinician avoided reinforcing it, teaching effective refusal strategies and redirecting toward Anya's preference not to drink. Could have used more double-sided reflections when brief sustain-like language appeared.
