# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized David’s concerns, validating his preference to protect energy for family/work in a warm, nonjudgmental way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections that captured content and feeling, though some reflections were repetitive and there were missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on cholesterol management and specific, actionable behaviors, but did not secure a time‑bound follow‑up, confidence rating, or explicit commitment to tighten direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine partner by soliciting input, offering options, and co‑constructing the plan rather than prescribing changes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced David’s control and choice, framing steps as optional experiments and repeatedly inviting his preferences on supports.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited David’s own motivations and concrete change ideas, but could have probed deeper into values, future goals, and confidence to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and invited choices frequently, though at times the clinician’s suggestions slightly nudged the agenda rather than letting David fully lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced specific change talk and practical steps, but did not consistently amplify strong change statements or follow up with confidence/commitment questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and avoided confronting sustain talk but mainly restated concerns instead of using double‑sided reflections or eliciting discrepancy to gently shift sustain talk toward change.
