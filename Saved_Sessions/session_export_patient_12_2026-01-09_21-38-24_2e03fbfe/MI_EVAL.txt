# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing difficulty and using affirmations that created a warm, supportive stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, timely reflections that matched the patient's tone and helped sustain rapport and engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on reducing drug use and engaging treatment, using concrete planning (script, appointment, reminders) to keep the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the action plan with the client, repeatedly framed tasks as shared work, and let the client's preferences shape next steps and supports.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced client choice with permission-based offers and alternatives, checking preferences and avoiding pressure while inviting the client to select options and times.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the client's own motives and resources through targeted questions about strengths and supports, then used those responses to build concrete commitments and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Displayed a highly collaborative, power-sharing approach by asking permission, offering options, and partnering on tasks like finding clinics and role-playing calls.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk via evocative questions, reflections of capability, and converting intention into a concrete commitment (booking the appointment and planning supports).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and avoided confrontation, often pivoting toward change, but missed some opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections or summary statements to resolve ambivalence.
