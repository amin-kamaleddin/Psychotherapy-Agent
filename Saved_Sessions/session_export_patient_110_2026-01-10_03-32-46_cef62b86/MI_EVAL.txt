# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validates and normalizes the patient's worries and avoids judgment. Repeatedly affirms the patient's goals and decisions, fostering a respectful stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective listening, accurately mirroring the patient's concerns and values. Reflections are timely and convey clear understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on cholesterol management and adherence with concrete next steps. The conversation progresses logically from concerns to actionable plans (questions, trial, monitoring).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-created the one-page printout and repeatedly invited the patient to lead. Solicited preferences and adapted the session content to the patient's priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice and ownership by offering options and asking permission before providing tools. Recommendations are framed as negotiable questions, avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively links treatment to the patient's values and elicits concrete actions, but does not consistently use deeper evocative techniques (e.g., scaling, 'why' questions). Could probe more for intrinsic motives and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fosters partnership and power-sharing through choice offerings, permission-seeking, and co-creation of materials. Patient input clearly shaped the plan and next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk and converts tentative statements into actionable steps (printout, 3-month trial). Missed opportunities to deepen change language with more open, evocative, or scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes minimizations into monitoring and trial options. Could strengthen use of double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to further reduce ambivalence.
