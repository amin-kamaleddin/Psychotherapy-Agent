# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes difficulty, and avoids blame. Repeated affirmations and supportive language create a warm, respectful stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates highly accurate empathic listening and reflections that capture affect and practical constraints. Regularly checks understanding and mirrors feelings, keeping Elena heard and reducing defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking and protecting the child and moves toward a specific, doable plan. Could be more strategic in summarizing, securing an explicit behavioral commitment, and planning for high‑risk moments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Models an equal partnership by soliciting preferences, tailoring suggestions, and co‑constructing concrete if‑then plans. Avoids an authoritarian stance and lets Elena's ideas shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Elena's freedom to choose, asks permission before offering options, and respects hesitancy about quitline/NRT without pressure. Multiple choices and check‑ins support her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation and concrete intentions (including a 0–10 confidence rating) but misses opportunities to probe deeper into personal reasons/values and past successes. More reflective amplification of spontaneous change statements would strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the interaction as collaborative, repeatedly invites Elena's input, and co‑creates the plan. This gives Elena clear control and ownership of the steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and shapes some change talk and asks for doable options and confidence ratings. Could more actively amplify and deepen change language by reflecting, exploring reasons/values, and eliciting past successes to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully softens sustain talk with validating, double‑sided reflections and reframing tradeoffs into manageable steps. Avoids confrontation or arguing and pivots productively to client‑chosen options.
