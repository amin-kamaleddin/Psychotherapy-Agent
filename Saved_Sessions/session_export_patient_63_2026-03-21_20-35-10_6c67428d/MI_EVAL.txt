# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating and normalizing Jordan’s struggle. These behaviors created a safe, respectful atmosphere throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Showed accurate perspective-taking and strong reflective listening, capturing both cognitive and emotional aspects of Jordan’s ambivalence. Reflections and summaries demonstrated high-fidelity empathic MI skills.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on stopping shoplifting and progressively moved toward concrete change strategies and situational planning. Balanced client-centered exploration with practical, behavior-focused steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort, continually inviting Jordan’s input and co-constructing plans (drafting texts, role-play). Inclusive language and shared problem-solving reinforced equality and teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fostered choice and control by offering multiple options, asking permission, and affirming Jordan’s ownership of decisions. Questions and phrasing emphasized his agency in selecting and refining the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focused on eliciting Jordan’s own motivations and resources using open questions, scaling, and eliciting past successes. Consistently drew out client-generated reasons and solutions rather than supplying them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently acted as a collaborator rather than an expert, inviting Jordan to choose what felt doable and co-create steps. Language and offers (e.g., role-play only with permission) positioned Jordan as the decision-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced change statements and moved toward specific planning (cash, sister check-in, scaling). Missed some opportunities to amplify and consolidate Jordan’s change language into stronger commitment talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and redirected attention toward alternatives and supports, including useful double-sided reflections. Could more actively reframe and explore sustain talk (and underlying social drivers) to further weaken its pull.
