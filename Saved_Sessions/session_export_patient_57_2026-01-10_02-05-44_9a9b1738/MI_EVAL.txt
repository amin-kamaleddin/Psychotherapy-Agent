**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalizing Jamal's values and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear focus on alcohol-related behavior and practical strategies, though at times shifts into offering a menu of solutions rather than negotiating a single concrete next step.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-creates the session by inviting choices, practicing lines, and checking preferences so Jamal shapes the work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Jamal's practical ideas and preferences well, but misses opportunities to deepen motivation by exploring underlying values, reasons, and confidence more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance with invitations to choose and practice, though occasionally gives directive lists that reduce perceived power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk and options but does not consistently reflect, amplify, or deepen Jamal's own language of desire, reasons, or confidence before offering strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain-leaning rationales but rarely uses MI-specific tactics (e.g., double-sided reflections or selectively reflecting the change side) to reduce sustain talk's pull.
