# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalizing and validating Hana’s fear and willingness to try.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking were used (complex reflections and frequent echoes of Hana’s feelings), showing clear understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on preparing and scheduling study time with interventions aimed at concrete action steps and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative, co-creating the plan and incorporating Hana’s ideas while offering tentative, client-centered help.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Hana’s sense of choice by offering options, asking preferences, and avoiding pressure while emphasizing she controls pace and method.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Hana’s ideas, prior strategies, and practical motivations well, but explored less of deeper intrinsic values or used scaling to strengthen commitment and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly invited Hana’s expertise and preferences, framing the plan as co-created and deferring to her choices throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk about short blocks and accountability, but missed chances to deepen commitment and confidence with scaling or follow-up prompts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to practical steps while acknowledging fears, though could have used double-sided reflections or reframing to further soften ambivalence.
