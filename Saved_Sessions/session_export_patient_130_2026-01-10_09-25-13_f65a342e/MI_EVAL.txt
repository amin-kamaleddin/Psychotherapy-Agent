# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Viktor's ambivalence with a warm, nonjudgmental tone. No language undermines acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, nuanced reflective listening that captures both sides of ambivalence and the emotional weight, validating Viktor and reducing defensiveness. Reflections are repeatedly accepted and expanded by Viktor.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing relationship harm and steadily moves toward concrete, achievable steps. Guidance is purposeful without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is joint problem-solving with frequent invitations for Viktor's input and co-constructed decisions. The clinician offers options and rehearses wording together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Viktor's choice and agency, offering options rather than directives and respecting boundaries. This consistent affirmation reduces pressure and supports internal motivation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Viktor's own ideas and a concrete plan (text wording, timing, reminders) but focuses more on logistics than deeper values, reasons, or confidence. More exploration of underlying motivation would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative, power-sharing stance with language like 'Let's shape it together' and by centering Viktor's preferences in plan development. Viktor's contributions clearly shape the outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, moving Viktor toward specific, doable options and a plan. Could further amplify reasons, probe confidence and past successes, and elicit a firmer commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively redirects sustain talk toward manageable, low-drama change options but sometimes accepts minimizations without explicitly reflecting sustain language. More frequent double-sided reflections would acknowledge sustain talk while guiding toward change.
