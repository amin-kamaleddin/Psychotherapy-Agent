### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing ambivalence and validating concerns while offering options. Warmth and nonjudgmental language are evident throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent perspective-taking with reflections that capture both content and affect. The clinician tracks shifts in readiness and responds with understanding rather than correction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on the target behavior (securing a trial shift/call) and repeatedly links prompts back to concrete action. Momentum is maintained toward a realistic first step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly a joint effort with frequent requests for Timur’s input and choices, adapting interventions to his preferences. Decisions are co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports Timur’s sense of choice by offering options, checking preferences, and avoiding pressure. This approach fosters ownership of the plan and next steps.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Positions Timur as the expert and follows his lead, offering collaborative supports (scripts, role-plays) and asking how to proceed. Power is clearly shared in planning and practice.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
