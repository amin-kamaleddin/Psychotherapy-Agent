### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Nadine’s feelings, repeatedly affirming strengths with nonjudgmental, validating language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured both content and affect (exhaustion, fear of burdening partner) and were timed to elicit more detail, demonstrating a strong empathic stance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on improving sleep/support via partner involvement and tracking, moving logically from values to specific, achievable actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-created: clinician invited Nadine’s priorities, choices, and worked alongside her to draft and refine the text rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly reinforced Nadine’s freedom to choose, used nonpressuring language, and validated her pacing and preferences, supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew on Nadine’s own motivations and past successes (e.g., texting her sister) to elicit internal reasons and practical strategies for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing by asking Nadine to select the gentlest next step and co-constructing the plan, treating her as the expert on her situation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and produced a concrete plan, but could have more deliberately amplified and consolidated commitment language (e.g., confidence/importance ratings).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and reframed concerns to reduce defensiveness, though more double-sided reflections would better contrast sustain and change talk.
