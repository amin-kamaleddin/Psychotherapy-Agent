# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician communicates respect and validates ambivalence consistently, but acceptance sometimes feels formulaic and repetitive rather than deeply individualized or explicitly normalizing specific emotions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking that encouraged elaboration, but missed opportunities to explore emotional tone more deeply and use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on concrete target behaviors and progressively narrowed to a specific, actionable plan, effectively translating ambivalence into steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely joint problem-solving with rehearsals and scripting, though the clinician sometimes framed choices rather than eliciting fully open patient-generated ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice and control, avoided pressuring language, and repeatedly asked which option felt most likely to work for the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation from the patient's own statements and practical motivators, but stopped short of deeper exploration of intrinsic motives, values, and confidence/commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session read as a collaborative effort with invitations for input and preference expression, but offering many preframed options slightly reduced opportunities for the patient's agenda to lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced several change statements and concrete planning, though could better amplify, sequence change talk, and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without confrontation, but mostly used logistical problem‑solving and neutral reflections instead of strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or highlighting past success) to actively soften sustain talk.
