# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes concerns, and affirms the client without judgment. Uses supportive language and timely affirmations to validate values and limits.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and emotion. Regularly checks in about safety and what feels doable, with no major missed empathic opportunities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on a clear, actionable target (securing short-term support) and moves from exploration to a concrete plan. Maintains forward momentum toward behavior change rather than drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the client in genuine co-creation of wording and next steps, offering choices and following the client's lead. Balances clinician expertise with solicitation of the client's preferences to make joint decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's sense of choice and agency by offering options and avoiding pressuring language. Frequently asks which approach feels safest and defers to the client's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits and builds on the client's own motivations and values, linking the chosen step to her desire to be calmer for her son. Encourages internal motivation and prompts the client to generate specific solutions and rehearse them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a collaborative partnership, asking permission to help craft language and inviting the client's input. Shares power substantively by co-creating the plan and adapting to the client's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk, asking for doable steps and securing a behavioral commitment to send the text. Supports rehearsal and contingency planning to strengthen follow-through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and avoids reinforcing sustain talk about boundary risks, respecting the client's ambivalence. Missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to more actively resolve ambivalence.
