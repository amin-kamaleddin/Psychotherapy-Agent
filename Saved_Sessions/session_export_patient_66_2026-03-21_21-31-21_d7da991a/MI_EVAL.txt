# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used respectful, nonjudgmental language and affirmed Kareem’s perspective, normalizing a stepwise approach without shaming. Tone and offers of options supported the patient’s dignity and choices throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurately captured Kareem’s mixed feelings and key concerns (enjoyment, social exclusion, fatherhood). Could probe affect more deeply (e.g., feelings of isolation or the emotional meaning of being a father).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking with concrete, time‑bound steps (2 mg gum, start Monday, tracking, Wednesday check‑in). Balanced client-centeredness with consistent movement toward behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Read as a joint effort with repeated invitations for Kareem to shape the plan and practical assistance offered only in response to his needs. Clinician checked preferences for stores, scripts, and what to report at follow‑up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission for experiments, and respecting Kareem’s chosen pace and decisions. No coercive or directive language; assistance was optional and client‑directed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intrinsic motives (not wanting to be a bad example) and asked what matters, inviting Kareem’s own reasons for change. Could have probed values and used scaling or evocative questions to deepen motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear partnership and power‑sharing: the plan was co‑constructed and the clinician repeatedly solicited Kareem’s input on options and logistics. Language treated Kareem as the expert in his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk (permission for an experiment, affirming the gum plan, asking about check‑in goals). Could more consistently amplify change statements and probe for reasons, confidence, and explicit commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and skillfully redirected sustain talk toward achievable steps like managing cravings and cutting down. Would be stronger with more explicit double‑sided reflections to balance ambivalence and gently pivot to change talk.
