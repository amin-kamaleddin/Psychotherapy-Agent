### Acceptance

### Direction

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

**Comments:** Some efforts to elicit motivation are present, but the clinician relies more on offering tools than systematically drawing out Kieran's own reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as a partnership by asking permission, sharing control, and letting Kieran's preferences determine the agenda and next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates openings for change and offers practical options, but does not consistently reflect, amplify, or follow up on tentative change language to deepen desire, ability, reasons, or need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
