# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing mixed feelings and conveying warm, unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, sustained reflections and summaries matched the patient's concerns and underlying motivations, helping him feel understood and prompting disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol use and preventing return to smoking, steering the conversation toward specific, small, and actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving with the patient's ideas driving the plan; clinician offered reflections and questions rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's choice, avoided pressuring language, and presented suggestions as options for the patient to choose from.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own motivations, strengths, and past successes to build self-efficacy rather than providing reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as the expert on his life, inviting input and choice so that the patient's preferences shaped the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with evocative questions and reflections, leading to clear, concrete change statements and plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and pivoted to small-change options, but could have used more double-sided reflections or strategic summarizing to resolve remaining ambivalence.
