# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validating Elena’s choices and normalizing her priorities. Tone was nonjudgmental and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and summaries captured content and motivation well. Could be strengthened by adding occasional deeper emotional or complex reflections about the internal meaning of self‑respect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and linked each turn back to values and the boundary goal. Session progressed logically from values to concrete planning and commitment without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a true partnership, with the clinician inviting input, integrating Elena’s language, and deferring final choices to her. Many session elements were chosen by the client, demonstrating co‑creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly reinforced Elena’s choice and control by offering options rather than directives and asking for her preferences. Language consistently communicated that the client retained ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation using scaling questions and linking actions to values, increasing change talk. Opportunities remained to deepen intrinsic motivation by exploring personal meaning and eliciting a client‑voiced commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the work as collaborative and acknowledged Elena’s expertise about her life. The clinician offered suggestions but allowed the client to drive choices like timing, wording, and backup plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk through importance/confidence scaling, asking for small steps, and specifying implementation details. Summaries and reflections amplified the client’s change statements into concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was minimal and the clinician avoided reinforcing reasons to maintain the status quo. Potential barriers were acknowledged and reframed into workable contingencies and backups.
