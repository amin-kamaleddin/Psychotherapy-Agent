# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation (e.g., "I'm here to listen", normalizing ambivalence), supporting a warm, accepting stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections and summaries of Rafael's feelings and constraints, checking preferences and listening closely to his experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on re-engaging in physical activity and guided toward a specific, time-bound behavioral experiment (Wednesday 12:30, 10-minute walk) without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan with Rafael by offering options, soliciting his input on reminders and timing, and adapting supports (alarm text, calendar block, backup stretches) in partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and framing missed attempts as information rather than failure, reinforcing Rafael's decision-making authority.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation and past success but did not sufficiently draw out personal reasons, values, or use scaling questions to deepen Rafael's own motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled an equal partnership by inviting Rafael's preferences, asking which options felt doable, and checking how he wanted reminders and follow-up to shape the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited tentative change talk and a small commitment (willing to try the 10-minute walk) but could have amplified desire/ability/reason/confidence language and used scaling or summaries to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and pivoted to small, specific options and backups, but sustain talk remained prominent and more active softening (double-sided reflections, amplification of change reasons) was missed.
