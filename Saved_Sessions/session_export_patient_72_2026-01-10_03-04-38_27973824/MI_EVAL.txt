# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—validated choices, avoided shaming, and offered help with permission throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and reflective summaries, but reflections were more pragmatic/task-focused and could probe deeper emotions or meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on restarting physical activity with concrete, actionable steps and no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used inclusive language and co‑created the plan with the patient, shared responsibilities, and used role‑play and follow‑up to support joint problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and respecting the patient’s preferences and timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons and concrete commitments (YMCA visit, walk), but could use more targeted questions to deepen intrinsic motivation and values-based reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a partnership by inviting agenda-setting and input, though at times offered clinician-generated solutions rather than first eliciting the patient’s alternatives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged and amplified change talk and helped specify goals, but missed opportunities for targeted evocation and selective amplification to build stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used pragmatic planning to address barriers, but could have employed more double-sided reflections and other MI softening techniques.
