# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed consistent warmth, respect, and positive regard with affirmations and nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of content, but reflections were more pragmatic than deeply affective; could probe the emotional meaning of goals and fatigue more.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused target of increasing physical activity, tying questions and suggestions to concrete days, times, and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint effort with mutual planning, offering help (draft texts/calendar notes) while leaving final decisions to Camila.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Camila's sense of choice by offering options, checking preferences, and inviting buy-in without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evoked Camila's internal motivations with open questions, scaling, and amplification of her reasons rather than providing external rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and client-centered, though frequent concrete suggestions sometimes nudged the process toward clinician-driven problem solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk through open questions, a confidence ruler, and requests for specific, client-generated plans and actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and offered practical fallbacks to reduce its impact, but could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly balance ambivalence and shift momentum.
