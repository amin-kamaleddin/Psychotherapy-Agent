# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental regard (“I’m glad you came in… That sounds stressful”), normalized ambivalence, and used behavior‑focused affirmations. Suggestion: favor descriptive affirmations over evaluative praise when eliciting internal motivation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate paraphrase of ambivalence and barriers (“I hear you’re torn…”; readiness ruler). Missed some opportunities for deeper affective reflections (shame/loyalty/fear) — include occasional complex reflections to deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol-related behavior and moved session logically from goals to concrete steps, rehearsal, and follow‑up (bring sober drink, ask Malik, coach check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed plan with Jamal, solicited his preferences (which line, who to ask, role‑play consent), and adapted recommendations to his limits — strong partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respected client choice and offered options (lines, coach involvement). Slight drift toward evaluative praise (“I’m proud”) which can lessen autonomy—use more ownership‑focused language and descriptive affirmations.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Jamal’s motivations and barriers (specific change talk, 0–10 ruler, what would move him higher) and used his reasons to shape a practical plan and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Jamal as expert on his life; decisions (Malik, line, limits of commitment) were client‑driven and clinician supported choices collaboratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced multiple change statements (sober practice, sober drink, ask Malik, use line) and consolidated commitments with specific, actionable steps.
