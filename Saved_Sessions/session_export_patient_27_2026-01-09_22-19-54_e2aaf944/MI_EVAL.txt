# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, validates feelings, and avoids judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic understanding with validations and brief reflections and asks permission, but misses some deeper emotional reflections that would capture adolescent shame and social vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on disclosure to friends with practical, goal-directed interventions like scripts, role‑play, and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—co‑creates messages, offers multiple options, and checks back so the client's preferences shape content and process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and normalizing any pace without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits reasons and desired outcomes for disclosure and uses them to shape scripts, but could more actively draw out values, confidence, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites input, builds on the client's language when drafting texts, and demonstrates power‑sharing in planning and practice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change‑oriented language through questions, scripts, and role‑play, but seldom amplifies change talk or secures specific confidence/commitment statements or timelines.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates and normalizes sustain talk and redirects to coping strategies and responses, though more use of double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries could further soften ambivalence.
