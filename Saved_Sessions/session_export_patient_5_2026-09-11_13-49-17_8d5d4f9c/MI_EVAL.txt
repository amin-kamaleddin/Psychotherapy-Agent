# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming (opening reflection and repeated praise) that normalizes the patient's concerns and conveys respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing the patient's worries clearly, but could deepen affective reflections to capture underlying fears and emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on producing the one‑pager (content, format, citations, script) with no drift and purposeful steering toward a concrete outcome.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Models an equal partnership by soliciting preferences at each decision point and positioning the clinician as a resource and co‑creator rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers clear options (checklist types, file formats, wording) and uses noncoercive language that empowers the patient to direct decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's practical priorities and motivations for action, but could probe more for internal motives, confidence, and a commitment timeline.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and client-centered—the clinician defers to the patient's choices and invites her to select formats, wording, and next steps throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete, action-oriented language and plans (checklist, citations, bringing to prescriber) but misses opportunities to amplify commitment and ask for timing or confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing reasons against change and redirects to practical solutions, though could use more explicit double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence while shifting toward action.
