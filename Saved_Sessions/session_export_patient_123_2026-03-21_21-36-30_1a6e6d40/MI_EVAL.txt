# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance through a warm opening, affirmations, and normalization of ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, summarizing Sofia's stressors and feelings to deepen mutual understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, client-chosen focus on study planning with concrete steps and commitments, but did not address the original alcohol screening concern or tie the discussion back to that referral target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-created the plan with Sofia by offering options, asking permission, and scaffolding steps while treating her as the expert on her schedule and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering multiple options, framing actions as voluntary experiments, and avoiding directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client-generated reasons, plans, and confidence ratings that produced concrete intentions, but could deepen evocation by linking plans to values and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a partnership, deferring to Sofia's preferences, asking which options felt doable, and sharing power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and reinforced change-oriented language and implementation intentions, though missed opportunities to amplify and summarize Sofia's change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively minimized sustain talk by normalizing doubts and redirecting to small experiments and backups, but did not use double-sided reflections or deeply explore underlying barriers.
