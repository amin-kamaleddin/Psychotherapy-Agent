# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing the patient's experience and affirming her efforts and constraints.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses timely, accurate reflections and perspective-taking that convey understanding, but could add more complex, feeling-focused reflections to probe deeper emotions like shame or discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on starting to exercise, linking values to concrete planning (length, timing, cues, fallback) and guiding follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a genuine team effort—clinician solicits and follows the patient's ideas, co-constructing the plan and taking a supportive facilitative role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers options, asks the patient's preferences, and confirms wording for check-ins, reinforcing the patient's choice and control without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's reasons and a patient-generated plan, though could deepen evocation by probing values, importance/confidence, and eliciting stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites the patient's decisions and preferences, shares power in planning, and checks exact wording and timing to respect the patient's expertise about her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and supports change talk and converts desire into a concrete action, but missed chances to amplify strong language and elicit explicit commitment or confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids reinforcing sustain talk by acknowledging concerns, normalizing limits, and reframing worries into realistic, doable planning without debating or minimizing.
