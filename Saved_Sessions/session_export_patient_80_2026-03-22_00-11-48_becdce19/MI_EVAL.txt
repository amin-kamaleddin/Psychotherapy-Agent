# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys clear unconditional positive regard throughout and avoids blaming language. Normalizes imperfection and consistently affirms the patient's strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective listening and perspective taking, reflecting both content and feeling and checking meaning with open questions. Captures ambivalence effectively and fosters strong rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing drinking and protecting the relationship, tying each turn to concrete behavioral goals. Progresses from values to concrete implementation without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner by soliciting the patient's ideas and co-constructing a plan. Frames the work as joint problem solving and respects the patient's expertise.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's right to choose, offering options rather than directives and avoiding pressuring language. Reinforces ownership by asking which options feel most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motives and concrete indicators of change with questions about what matters and what would need to happen. Could deepen evocation by probing broader long-term values and eliciting verbal commitment and confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites the patient's input and uses collaborative language to share power, avoiding unilateral prescriptions. Builds on the patient's preferences to create the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and amplifies change talk, producing concrete change language and implementation intentions. Could strengthen by more systematic amplification, summarizing, and eliciting stronger commitment or scaling statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and pivots toward strategies and strengths, using normalizing language. To improve, incorporate more double sided reflections that explicitly name both pulls of ambivalence while guiding toward change.
