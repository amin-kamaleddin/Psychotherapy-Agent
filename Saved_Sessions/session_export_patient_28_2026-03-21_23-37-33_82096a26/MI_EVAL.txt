# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Dante's struggles, creating a safe, nonjudgmental tone; a repeated validating phrase felt slightly scripted at times.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening that captures both content and affect and invites elaboration; could occasionally probe deeper into underlying values or what ‘‘feeling like yourself’’ means emotionally.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on re-establishing a morning exercise routine and consistently steers conversation toward practical planning and specific implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a co-constructed exploration—clinician solicits Dante's ideas, offers options, and refines plans together without taking an authoritative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses invitational, noncoercive language and repeatedly reinforces Dante's choice and control by offering options and encouraging experiments rather than prescribing actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits desire, reasons, and past successes and generates implementation ideas, but could more actively deepen intrinsic motives and consolidate change talk into confidence and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treats Dante as the expert and invites him to choose among options, co-creating a feasible plan and sharing power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits multiple change statements and reinforces them with reflections and summaries, but missed opportunities to selectively amplify DARN-C language and to elicit explicit confidence or commitment ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk to avoid confrontation and redirects to small supports, yet seldom used more active softening techniques like double-sided reflections or explicit pivots to resolve ambivalence.
