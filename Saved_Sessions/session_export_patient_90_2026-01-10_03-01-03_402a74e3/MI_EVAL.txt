### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard, warmth, and validation while avoiding judgment about the dog or finances; uses multiple affirmations and normalizing language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of Marcus's experience, but moves relatively quickly to options without deeper, more evocative exploration of underlying emotions like fear or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on improving asthma management with concrete, testable steps and repeatedly brings the conversation back to actionable behaviors and a 24-hour next step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—invites Marcus's input on choices, wording, and timing, follows his pace, and co-constructs the plan so decisions are shared and negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Marcus's own motivations and reasons for change (confidence ratings, preferred wording, chosen steps) and draws out intrinsic commitment language rather than imposing solutions.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Fosters power-sharing and treats Marcus as the expert on his life—asking which option he prefers, co-creating specific steps, and seeking his exact wording for the ask to his wife.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk through choices, confidence rulers, and prompting small, concrete commitments; affirms and strengthens Marcus's doable plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
