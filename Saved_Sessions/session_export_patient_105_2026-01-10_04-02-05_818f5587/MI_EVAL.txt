# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, respectful regard and normalized Naomi's experience, validating her distress and avoiding blame, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening accurately captured Naomi's physical and emotional experience and likely made her feel heard, but reflections became repetitive and missed deeper affective exploration like embarrassment and strengths.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and moved logically toward a single, concrete, testable plan (the 20-second grounding cue) without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative: the clinician co-created the plan, solicited Naomi's preferences, and incorporated her solution (ribbon/rubber band), supporting equal team-based problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirmed choice, framed the exercise as a practical experiment rather than a demand, and avoided pressuring Naomi to commit beyond what she was willing to try, protecting autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Naomi's ideas and internal motivation, drawing out concrete change talk and success signs, but could have probed deeper for underlying values and used scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance and invited Naomi's input, though offering several specific options early on sometimes subtly steered the agenda; overall power-sharing was strong but not maximal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and strengthened several strands of change talk and a very small, testable behavior, but missed opportunities to probe importance/confidence and to amplify past successes to bolster self-efficacy.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently validated doubts and reframed the plan as a manageable experiment to reduce sustain talk, though using double-sided reflections could better acknowledge ambivalence while tipping toward change.
