# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and respecting the patient's pace.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate reflections and mirrors the patient's language to clarify emotions and practical barriers, deepening understanding of fear and overwhelm.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on seeking information/clinical evaluation and consistently returns to pragmatic, specific next steps like a one-line script and timing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates an equal, collaborative stance by soliciting preferences, co-constructing the script, and treating the patient as partner and expert in what will work.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the patient's choice and control by offering multiple options, affirming pace, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits doable steps and recalls past successes to draw out internal resources, but could further probe importance, confidence, and invite a verbal commitment to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as co-created, invites the patient's judgment, and shares power in planning and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk around small, concrete steps, yet misses opportunities to amplify and consolidate those statements into stronger commitment language (e.g., scaling or specific timing).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Neutralizes sustain talk by validating concerns and gently redirecting toward manageable alternatives and supports, avoiding arguments or pressure.
