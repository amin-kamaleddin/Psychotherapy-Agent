# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validation, normalizing concerns and avoiding shaming. Maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used high-quality empathic listening with accurate reflections and language matching to capture feelings and practical concerns. Repeated summaries demonstrated nuanced perspective-taking and tracking of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, behaviorally specific focus on improving daily controller inhaler adherence with a concrete, testable two-week plan. Consistently narrowed scope to actionable strategies and follow-up steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in genuine teamwork by soliciting the patient's ideas and co-creating the plan. Regularly invited input and offered options rather than prescribing a solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and reversibility, emphasizing permission to stop and letting the patient select tracking and reassurance methods. Avoided coercive language and preserved patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited meaningful change talk about sleep, activity, and safety but could probe deeper into core values and longer-term personal reasons for change. More systematic elicitation of commitment language would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear collaborative stance and deferred to the patient's preferences, asking which options felt best. Co-constructed the plan with shared decision-making and mutual respect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and labeled change talk and offered small, reversible steps, but opportunities were missed to deepen motivations into explicit commitment statements. Could have more strongly summarized motivations linked to values to sustain movement toward action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized and reframed concerns and avoided arguing, using reversible trials to reduce resistance. Missed some chances for double-sided reflections that explicitly name both sides of ambivalence to further shift sustain talk.
