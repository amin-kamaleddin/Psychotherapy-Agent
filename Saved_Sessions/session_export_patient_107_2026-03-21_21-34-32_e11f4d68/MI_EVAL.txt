# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance using validating language and praise. Normalized ambivalence and avoided labeling or coercive language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate, timely reflections that mirrored the client's ambivalence about alertness versus jitters/BP. Reflections were specific and fostered rapport while inviting the patient's view on feasibility.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused agenda on modifying caffeine timing/amount with concrete steps, tracking, and a scheduled follow-up. Problem-solving stayed tightly linked to the behavior-change goal without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in genuine co-creation of the plan, repeatedly seeking the client's preferences and deferring to his judgment about feasibility. Decisions (drink swap, start date, reminders) were made jointly and reflected shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed suggestions as options and invited choice, emphasizing that slips are data rather than failures. Reinforced client ownership and avoided assuming he wanted to quit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used evocative questions (readiness scale, what would move you) that elicited tentative change plans and reasons. Could strengthen by eliciting deeper intrinsic motives/values and amplifying change-talk to draw out commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed an equal partnership by inviting collaboration, asking preferences, and letting the client shape the plan. The clinician avoided an expert-only stance and co-constructed concrete steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited change talk with readiness scaling and proposing small experiments, producing tentative willingness to try stopping after 3pm. Could more consistently amplify and reflect client change statements and ask targeted importance/confidence questions to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk using double-sided reflections and by preserving the ritual with decaf swaps, steering toward testing rather than debate. Could further soften sustain talk by explicitly acknowledging sustain reasons while increasing discrepancy with longer-term goals.
