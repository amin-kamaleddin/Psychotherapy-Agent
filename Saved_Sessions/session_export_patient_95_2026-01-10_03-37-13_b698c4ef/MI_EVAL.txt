# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance with repeated validations and affirmations, creating a warm, respectful tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections of both content and affect, repeatedly validating the patient's intense distress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a clear, appropriate focus on suicidal ideation and means restriction throughout the session, keeping safety the central priority.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction generally felt joint with invitations to choose options and stay involved, though necessary directive language about emergency steps slightly reduced equal partnership at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician often offered choices and asked permission, supporting autonomy, while clinically necessary limits (e.g., contacting services if imminent risk) modestly constrained perceived control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to elicit motivation (mentioning the brother, delay agreement) were present, but the clinician missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic reasons and amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician fostered a collaborative stance and offered options while staying with the patient, though occasional directive safety language slightly shifted power back to the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were limited, opportunistic efforts to elicit change talk (noting the brother, inviting small steps), but the clinician did not sufficiently evoke or amplify the patient's faint reasons for living.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk, validating despair while redirecting to concrete protective steps, though could better use double-sided reflections to gently shift ambivalence.
