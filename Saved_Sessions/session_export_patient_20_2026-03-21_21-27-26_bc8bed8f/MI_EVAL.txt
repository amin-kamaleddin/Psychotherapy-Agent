# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, affirming the client's honesty and limits. Supports client choices with warmth and minimal pressure throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing concerns about stress, sleep, and drinking. Reflections are mostly content-level and would be strengthened by more complex emotional naming (e.g., anxiety, shame, relief).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol use as it relates to sleep and stress and progresses logically to a specific, measurable plan. Elicits concrete details (which nights, replacements, confidence, backups) tied to clinical outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine co‑construction of the plan by offering options and asking the client to choose and shape strategies. Checks agreement and incorporates client requests about wording, timing, and limits (e.g., no family talk).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice, offers options rather than directives, and respects the client's limits about topics and pace. Reinforces the client's ownership of decisions without pressuring for change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivation through doable steps, confidence scaling, and planning that produced client-generated strategies. Could deepen evocation by exploring underlying values and personal reasons why less tension/better sleep matters to the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the client's expertise about what will work, allowing the client to select specifics. The plan reflects genuine power-sharing and mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with specific steps, choice options, and confidence rulers, and affirms the client's planning. Misses some opportunities to ask strategic open questions linking change to intrinsic motives and to amplify the client's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing and accepts the role of drinking for unwinding, avoiding confrontation. Could more often use double-sided reflections to highlight ambivalence and gently tip language toward change.
