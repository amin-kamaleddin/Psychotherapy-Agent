# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone through supportive reflections and normalization; there was no shaming or blaming around relapse or juice consumption.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening that matched the patient's affect and content, validating both pride and frustration and deepening rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing orange juice intake, linking education, substitution strategies, rehearsal, and a one-week trial to an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine team with the patient by inviting choices, co-creating scripts, role-playing responses, and shaping the plan around the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the patient's control by offering options, asking which felt doable, avoiding pressure, and reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's own motivations, plans, and confidence, producing patient-generated commitment and concrete steps to try for a week.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making and invited input, but at times offered many specific scripts and tactics without first asking permission, which slightly shifted toward expert-driven guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk that led to concrete commitments, though could have probed more for deeper values and longer-term reasons to amplify intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk nonconfrontationally and moved to harm-reduction options, but missed chances for double-sided reflections and exploring the emotional/functional role of the juice.
