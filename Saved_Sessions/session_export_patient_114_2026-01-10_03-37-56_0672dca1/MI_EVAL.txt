# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental stance, validating feelings and honoring autonomy throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and reflective listening with frequent summaries, but reflections rarely probe deeper emotions like shame or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on alcohol-related harm reduction and moved each exchange toward a concrete, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session reads as joint problem-solving with strong solicitation of input, though frequent clinician-driven suggestions occasionally shifted power toward the expert role.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the client's choices, offered options, and reinforced that the plan and decisions belong to the client without applying pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and used confidence rulers and commitments effectively, but relied at times on strategy rather than deeper evocative questions to draw out intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with permission-seeking and co-creation of plans, yet multiple scripted recommendations sometimes edged into a more directive stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk and increased confidence, but missed some opportunities to amplify and deepen the client's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward harm-reduction, though could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence.
