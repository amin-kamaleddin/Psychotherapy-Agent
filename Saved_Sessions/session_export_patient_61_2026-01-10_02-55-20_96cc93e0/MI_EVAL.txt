# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with normalizing and appreciative statements; some warmth felt formulaic and lacked more individualized normalization tied to Jonathan's specific situation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening, accurate paraphrases, and validation that reduced defensiveness; missed opportunities to probe deeper emotions or use complex reflections to deepen affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on morning blood‑glucose checking and used scaling and summaries to build toward a concrete, time‑bound plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was collaborative with inclusive language and joint planning; however, the clinician sometimes led with multiple solutions before eliciting more of Jonathan’s own problem‑solving ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Jonathan’s choice, offered options, and framed steps as his decision, reinforcing client ownership without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempted to elicit motivation with scaling and questions about change, but largely focused on logistics and did not sufficiently explore intrinsic motives, values, or broader consequences.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited input and co‑constructed the plan, checking preferences repeatedly; could strengthen partnership by eliciting more patient‑generated options before offering solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited a small commitment (morning check) using scaling questions, but did not sufficiently amplify or deepen change talk or explore past successes and personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing, validated sustain talk, and reframed into small manageable steps that reduced resistance; relied mostly on logistical redirection rather than strategic double‑sided reflections to surface ambivalence.
