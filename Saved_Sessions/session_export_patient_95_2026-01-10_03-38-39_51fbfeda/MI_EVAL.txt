# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance with repeated validations and warm language; honors the patient's distress before moving to safety planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective listening with multiple empathic reflections and safety naming; reflections are sometimes repetitive and miss opportunities to deepen or nuance the patient's emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, necessary focus on immediate safety throughout the interaction with direct questions about plan/access and concrete protective actions; does not drift from the safety target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative in tone—invites input, offers to stay, and affirms small steps—but the clinician retains decision-making lead due to safety responsibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices and asks permission (e.g., options for safety planning, asking to contact others) while balancing necessary directive prompts given imminent risk.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional attempts to draw out motivation (noting sister worry, praising steps) but lacks systematic elicitation and amplification of reasons for living or other internal motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites participation and offers options, showing collaborative intent, yet frequently drives the agenda with repeated safety prompts which limits the patient's role in shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some efforts to reinforce change-oriented language and praise small steps, but misses opportunities to elicit and expand emerging change talk (e.g., exploring the sister concern or meaning of staying alive).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses empathic reflections to contain hopeless statements, but does not employ advanced MI techniques (double-sided reflections, reframing) to actively redirect sustain talk.
