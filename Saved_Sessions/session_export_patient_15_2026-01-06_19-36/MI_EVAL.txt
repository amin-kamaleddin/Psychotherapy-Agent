# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and respects the patient's choices and boundaries throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking to validate the patient's experience, but stays at a surface level and does not explore deeper emotions or reasons for minimizing stress and drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, coherent focus on initiating home blood pressure monitoring and moves through practical steps, but misses an opportunity to briefly address the alcohol–BP link and the second target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint exploration, solicits the patient's ideas and preferences, and co-constructs the plan rather than imposing recommendations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice and control, affirms his right to decline change, and emphasizes that the plan is his to implement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effectively evokes intrinsic motivators and commitment language around monitoring (pride, self-efficacy) but does not attempt to draw out motivation or discrepancy regarding alcohol use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership through invitation-style questions and power-sharing, integrating the patient's solutions (alarms, monitor placement, handling reminders) into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies concrete change talk for home monitoring with implementation intentions, but fails to cultivate any change talk about alcohol use.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and neutrally validates the patient's sustain talk about drinking, yet misses chances to gently soften sustain talk with double-sided reflections or pros/cons exploration.
