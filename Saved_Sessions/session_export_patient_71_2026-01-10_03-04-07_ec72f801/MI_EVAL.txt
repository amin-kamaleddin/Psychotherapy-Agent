# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed nonjudgmental respect and positive regard with warm, personalized reflections and acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, but could deepen emotional reflections to better capture the nuance of ambivalence and feelings like frustration or embarrassment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on the target behavior (brief morning yoga/stretch) with concrete, measurable goals and repeated return to action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engaged in joint problem‑solving and co‑creation of plans and texts, though occasional directive technical guidance (automations, alarm labels) nudged the tone toward expert instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered choices and checked preferences frequently, supporting agency, but some technical steps (toggling automation settings) risked reducing the client's control without explicit consent.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from the client using open questions, scaling, and prompts to elicit supports and personal reasons for change rather than imposing rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited the patient's input on decisions, with minor drift into an expert role when providing detailed technical instructions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited, reflected, and amplified change talk via values-based questions, confidence scaling, and concrete planning, strengthening commitment language throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and redirected sustain talk effectively but used few double‑sided reflections or reframes to explicitly normalize ambivalence and soften sustain statements.
