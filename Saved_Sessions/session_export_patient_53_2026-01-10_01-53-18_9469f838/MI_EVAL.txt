# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with warm normalization and affirmations throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, consistent reflections and validations that named and mirrored the patient's anxiety and hesitation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on attending the meetup, using concrete proximal steps (drafts, timers, 15‑minute plan) that tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint project, offering options, checking preferences, and co‑constructing the plan with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and avoided pressure by offering multiple options and emphasizing that not sending would still be okay.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited small, actionable motivations and choices but did not sufficiently draw out deeper personal reasons or amplify the patient's change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely shared control, followed the patient's selections, and adapted language to match her voice, showing collaborative decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced small change talk with low‑effort options, but could have more actively reflected and amplified the patient's change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected to incremental action, though more double‑sided reflections could better resolve ambivalence.
