# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence with nonjudgmental language, creating a safe atmosphere; no shaming or lecturing was evident.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate summaries helped Sofia feel understood, though reflections were mainly content-focused and could have probed deeper emotional nuance (e.g., anxiety, fear of disappointing family).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on marijuana use and moved logically from values to a specific, measurable plan (no smoking on school nights), using strategic questions to protect the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction exemplified teamwork—clinician invited Sofia's ideas, used them to shape the plan, and checked what felt doable without adopting an expert-dominant stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Sofia's choice and control by avoiding coercive language, asking open questions, and inviting her to pick her own smallest step and supports.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal reasons for change through values-focused questions and strengths, producing meaningful motivation; could further probe desire/ability/confidence and amplify change talk into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Sofia as the source of ideas and choices and co-created concrete steps and supports, demonstrating strong shared decision-making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced multiple strands of change talk and concrete steps, but could have amplified and consolidated those statements into stronger commitment language (e.g., follow-ups on confidence).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and validated the pull of both sides, reducing defensiveness; could employ more active softening techniques (double-sided reflections, reframing) to shift momentum toward change.
