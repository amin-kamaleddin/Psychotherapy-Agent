# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's perspective, avoiding shame or blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurately reflects and summarizes the patient's views and ambivalence with timely reflections that capture both sustain and change language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and a concrete goal (one week of tracking), consistently steering toward practical steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the patient as an equal partner by offering choices, checking preferences, and co-constructing the tracking plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice with permission language and multiple options, avoiding pressure to commit to cutting back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits a behavioral commitment to track but does limited work to draw out intrinsic motivations, values, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites the patient to guide decisions (tool choice, start date) and frames the interaction as joint problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates opportunities and secures a low-effort commitment (tracking) but does not consistently amplify or deepen the patient's sparse change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes defenses nonconfrontationally, though could use more double-sided reflections to juxtapose costs and benefits.
