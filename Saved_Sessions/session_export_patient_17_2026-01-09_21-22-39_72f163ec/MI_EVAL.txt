# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing struggle and slips while affirming the patient's feelings and efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate reflections of distress and shame, but occasionally shifts too quickly into advice rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on smoking cessation with concrete goal-setting, sequencing, and stepwise planning throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with role‑plays and support planning, though frequent ready-made templates are offered before fully eliciting the patient's own solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking preferences, and emphasizing restarting after slips, reinforcing patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effort to elicit motives and past attempts, but the session is often advice-driven with missed opportunities to draw out deeper internal motivation and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displays a collaborative stance and invites input, but leans toward presenting expert templates rather than co-creating plans from the patient's own phrasing and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk and returns to motives, yet often moves into prescriptive planning without fully reflecting and amplifying the patient's own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses normalizing, nonconfrontational responses and compromise to respect ambivalence, but could more frequently employ double-sided reflections and value-focused redirection.
