# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent nonjudgmental regard, validates feelings, and normalizes Tyler's stance, but occasionally shifts into directive problem-solving without deeper individualized affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and attention to affect with tone-matching and brief validations, though empathy could be deepened by exploring the meaning behind Tyler's relationships and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on immediate safety, de-escalation, and harm-reduction, but could more explicitly sequence toward longer-term behavioral goals when readiness emerges.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally joint problem-solving with choices and invitations to select options, but much of the content is clinician-generated rather than co-created using Tyler's own language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors Tyler's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure to quit, effectively sustaining autonomy throughout the interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes modest attempts to ask what would help, but largely provides solutions and information instead of eliciting Tyler's intrinsic motivations, values, or reasons for use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance with permission-seeking and role-play offers, yet sometimes slips into expert/advice-giving rather than fully sharing power and co-constructing language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are brief prompts and some small change statements, but the clinician does not systematically evoke or amplify desire, ability, reasons, or need for change and often moves to advice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids confrontation and pivots to harm-reduction, reducing sustain talk, though could use more explicit double-sided or complex reflections to gently tip ambivalence toward change.
