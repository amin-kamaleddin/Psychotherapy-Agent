# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, avoiding blame. Affirmed the patient's priorities and treated minimizing statements respectfully.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, using summaries and mirrored language. Reflections captured the patient's feelings and priorities clearly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking and reducing child exposure and moved the conversation toward actionable steps. Progressed logically from ambivalence to a specific experiment (porch at 6pm, reminders).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine joint exploration by soliciting the patient's ideas and preferences and integrating them into the plan. Co-constructed realistic implementation details that fit the patient's routine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by framing changes as an 'experiment you control' and offering options without pressure. Emphasized patient choice and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicited the patient's values and intrinsic reasons (protecting kids, staying calm) and linked them to options. Could deepen motivation by probing emotional meaning and using scaling questions (importance/confidence).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative and treated the patient as the decision-maker. Asked preference and setup questions that shared power and respected the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened concrete change talk that led to a doable plan and implementation steps. Could further amplify and summarize change statements and elicit a stronger commitment or confidence rating.
