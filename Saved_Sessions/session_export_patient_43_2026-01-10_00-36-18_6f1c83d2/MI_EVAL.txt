# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—normalizes ambivalence and reflects choices without shaming (e.g., accepting the after-shift cigarette as a safety net). Tone is respectful and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately paraphrasing goals and worries; could deepen probes into emotional nuance beneath guarded responses (fear of 'falling apart').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking reduction with a concrete, measurable behavioral experiment (skip morning cigarette for a week) and guides toward specific next steps without imposing solutions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as an equal partnership with joint problem-solving and tailored strategies (phone tally, gum, leaving pack in truck), consistently deferring to the patient's lived experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice and control, honors the decision to keep an after-shift cigarette as a safety net, and solicits the patient's preferences for monitoring and planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical reasons for change and resourcefulness, but could more deliberately draw out intrinsic values and confidence/commitment language (e.g., confidence ruler, commitment statement).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership by inviting the patient's ideas at each step and co-creating a pragmatic plan, framing decisions as the patient's to make.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, leading to a concrete small step (skipping the morning cigarette) and amplifying benefits and practical plans to support further change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids argumentation and normalizes sustain talk while redirecting to a small experiment; could more strategically use double-sided reflections or succinct summaries to more explicitly tip the balance toward change.
