# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance with validating language and affirmations; no shaming or confrontational moments were present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured both content and emotion, with checks that invited deeper reflection and patient elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on reducing alcohol to lower fall risk with concrete, outcome-focused questions and a specific time-limited behavioral experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner throughout, co-constructing the plan, soliciting Margaret's input on specifics, and offering suggestions only after asking her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and control by offering options, affirming that the plan was the patient's decision, and avoiding pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew on Margaret's values (independence, social life) and elicited concrete change talk and a behavioral test, but could more consistently deepen intrinsic motivation and elicit commitment or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint exploration with clear power-sharing, offering choices and letting the patient's ideas drive the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and a small doable step, but could more explicitly amplify and summarize the patient's reasons and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and skilfully reframed sustain talk toward manageable change, though greater use of double-sided or selectively down-weighting reflections would further weaken sustain language.
