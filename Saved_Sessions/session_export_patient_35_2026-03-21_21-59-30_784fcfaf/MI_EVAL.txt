# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validates the patient’s efforts and constraints, normalizing difficulty rather than blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and summaries capture the patient’s perspective and concrete barriers, showing clear understanding of her experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking reduction/cessation and moves the conversation toward a specific, measurable one‑week trial and practical strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invites the patient’s input at each decision point and tailors suggestions to her preferences, creating an equal, co‑constructed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks permission before offering options, and avoids pressuring language, supporting the patient’s sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some motivations (protecting her son, past week quit) but could use more open, value‑based questions to deepen intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the patient’s expertise, letting her select strategies and shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and secures a small commitment, but misses opportunities to amplify personal meaning and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids argument and validates minimization, yet could more actively use double‑sided reflections or explore costs of staying the same to reduce sustain talk.
