# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established and maintained unconditional positive regard with validating language and no blame; normalized ambivalence and respected the patient's choices throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking (including complex reflections) that tracked ambivalence and helped the patient elaborate her feelings and concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on smoking from start to finish, moving logically from values and ratings to a specific, time-bound behavior-change plan while remaining patient-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a joint exploration—asked permission, solicited preferences, incorporated patient suggestions, and co-constructed a feasible plan emphasizing teamwork and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored the patient's right to choose by offering options, framing suggestions as choices, and accepting limited commitment without pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited meaningful motivations and barriers (e.g., role-model concern, willingness to experiment) but moved fairly quickly to solutions and could deepen DARN-C exploration before planning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative, deferred to the patient's expertise, and used inclusive language to co-create the tiny plan that reflected the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened change talk via scaling and probing and encouraged small steps, though could more explicitly amplify and summarize the patient's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double-sided reflections and redirection toward experiments to minimize sustain talk, but could more strategically reframe sustain-talk content to further reduce its influence.
