# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing the patient's perspective and avoiding shaming language. Repeated affirmations and supportive openings maintained a welcoming stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking with summaries and supportive reflections. Missed a few opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections to explore underlying emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on teen alcohol use and concrete harm-reduction goals from start to finish. Kept the session centered on actionable strategies and avoided tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was strongly collaborative with joint problem-solving, frequent solicitation of preferences, and co-creation of the plan. Concrete actions (drafting texts, choosing backups, scheduling follow-up) reflected equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Robustly supported the patient's autonomy by offering choices, permission-based suggestions, and accepting his decision not to quit while focusing on safety. Repeatedly reinforced patient ownership of decisions and actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked actionable motivation for harm-reduction (commitment to a pickup plan and reminders). Could further elicit intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fostered partnership and shared decision-making, inviting input and honoring the patient's direction. Language and behavior promoted power-sharing and patient influence over the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited specific, concrete change-oriented behavior (safe ride plan) but did not systematically draw out broader change talk about desire, reasons, or confidence. Missed opportunities to probe importance and commitment beyond logistics.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and shifted to pragmatic harm-reduction options, which softened resistance. Could strengthen by using more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence.
