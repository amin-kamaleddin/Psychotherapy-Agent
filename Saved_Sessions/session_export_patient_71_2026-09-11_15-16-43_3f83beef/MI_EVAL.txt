# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful—validates lapses and affirms the plan, conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and paraphrase that mirror the client's ambivalence and practical setup, but misses a few opportunities to probe deeper emotional meaning behind the ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on establishing a short morning yoga practice and steadily guides the conversation toward a specific, measurable action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed—clinician checks preferences, offers options, and refines the plan based on the client's choices, reflecting an equal-partner dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice and offers options rather than directives, emphasizing the client's ownership of the plan and pacing without exerting pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits values-consistent reasons and change language, but could more explicitly link the behavior to core values and probe strength of motivation/confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative approach that invites the client's expertise and preferences, avoids imposing solutions, and co-creates a realistic plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully draws out desire, reasons, and commitment with concrete planning and summaries, though could more often reflect and amplify change statements or use confidence scaling to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalizes lapses and redirects to achievable strategies without reinforcing sustain talk, but could incorporate more double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while pivoting to change.
