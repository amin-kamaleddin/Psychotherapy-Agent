# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed nonjudgmental support and respect, normalizing ambivalence and affirming Lukas's effort. Warm, validating stance was sustained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring Lukas's language and emotions. Reflections and validations deepened understanding and rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on peer-influenced alcohol use and its impact on grades and eligibility. Questions and reflections consistently guided toward actionable steps tied to Lukas's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was a joint problem-solving effort, with the clinician inviting input and building on Lukas's ideas. Decisions and rehearsal were co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Lukas's choice and control by offering options and seeking permission to practice. Avoided coercive language and affirmed his right to decide how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited values, practical reasons, and strategies, and encouraged rehearsal of plans. Could deepen evocation by probing core motivations, asking importance/confidence questions, and amplifying personal reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Lukas as the expert on his life, inviting his preferences and co-creating the plan. Strong power-sharing language and collaborative practice supported partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk with values-focused questions and rehearsal, producing concrete plans. Missed opportunities to strengthen commitment and confidence (e.g., rulers, eliciting 'want/need/commit' language) and to use strategic summaries.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and redirected toward workable strategies, using reflections and planning to reduce resistance. Could more often use double-sided reflections and selective summaries to further diminish sustain talk and amplify change talk.
