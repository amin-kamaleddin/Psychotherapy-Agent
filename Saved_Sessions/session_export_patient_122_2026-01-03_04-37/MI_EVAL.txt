# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalization (e.g., “That mixture of hope and overwhelm makes perfect sense…”, “that realism is a strength”). Hedged statements from the patient (“I’m not promising I won’t bail”) were accepted without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Multiple accurate, complex reflections capturing content and feeling (e.g., “moving to Arizona has left you excited but also tired…”, “tired and anxious but willing to try a tiny step”). Demonstrated sustained perspective‑taking across the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Maintained clear, persistent focus on a single target behavior (post‑lunch brief walk). Frequent narrowing to supports/backups and concrete planning with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited patient input and offered options (“Which of these supports would you choose…?”, “Which feels most doable…?”). Decisions were co‑constructed and patient‑led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Emphasized choice and respected limits (allowed hedging without pressure). Offered a very small initial commitment option and framed starting small as acceptable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Elicited patient’s reasons, resources, and anticipated benefits (asked about differences if behavior occurred; queried past strengths). Missed some opportunities to deepen motives (no scaling questions or deeper values exploration).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Fostered genuine collaborative stance; clinician and patient worked as partners to build the plan and drew on patient’s past strategy (breaking tasks into small steps).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reflected change talk (desire/reason/commitment language around alarm and short walk). Could have amplified and summarized cumulative change talk and used scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
[✔] Criteria clearly met
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk; normalized and reframed concerns and offered practical backups. Could increase use of double‑sided reflections and selective amplification of change talk to further weaken sustain talk.
