# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and explicit affirmations, validating the patient's choices and feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, frequent reflections and perspective-taking that summarized both facts and feelings, and asked responsive questions aligned with the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and drinking goals, purposefully moving from exploration to specific, actionable planning with targeted questions and steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint effort, inviting patient input at each step, checking preferences, and co-creating the plan rather than prescribing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by accepting the patient's goals, offering options with permission language, and avoiding pressuring or directive statements.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's own motivations and strengths, linking past successes to new goals and eliciting intrinsic reasons and rising confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly deferred to the patient's expertise, invited collaboration on strategy selection, rehearsal, reminders, and scheduling, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk through open questions, scaling, linking past successes, and building a concrete plan, with documented increases in commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged the patient's preference for reduction without arguing, honored sustain talk, and offered harm-reduction options that redirected toward workable change.
