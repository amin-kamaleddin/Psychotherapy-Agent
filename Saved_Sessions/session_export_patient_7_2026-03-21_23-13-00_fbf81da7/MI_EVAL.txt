# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys respect and positive regard, using reflections that validate both wanting change and protecting herself. Language is nonjudgmental and explicitly honors the client's choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, repeated reflections mirror Amina’s feelings and underlying motivations, demonstrating strong perspective-taking. The clinician validates the client's logic and prior coping even when she is minimally verbal.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays focused on a concrete target (protecting personal space) with repeated returns to actionable steps and measurable signs of success. The clinician avoids drifting and keeps the conversation goal-directed and safety-oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Therapist behaves as an equal partner by soliciting Amina’s ideas, co-constructing the plan, and inviting iterative tweaks. Decisions and the experimental approach are framed collaboratively rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is emphasized throughout, with explicit statements honoring agency and invitations to voluntary experimentation. The clinician accepts refusals and frames the plan as the client's choice to foster ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician elicits the client's internal motivation and concrete solutions using open invitations and prompts about past strategies. Amina’s values (autonomy, breathing room) are leveraged to fuel the change conversation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly frames the work as joint and defers to Amina’s expertise, incorporating her suggestions into the plan. This power-sharing approach allows the client’s contributions to shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits concrete, doable steps and follows up to deepen change language, leading to specific strategies to try. Missed opportunities include more explicit amplification, summaries of commitment language, and brief confidence/probing to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and not argued with, and the clinician pivots effectively to solution-focused experimentation. Could further soften sustain talk by using more double-sided reflections to highlight both resistance and the client’s interest in change.
