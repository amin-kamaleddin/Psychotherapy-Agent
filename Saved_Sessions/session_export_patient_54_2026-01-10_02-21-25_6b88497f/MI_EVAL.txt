### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing both content and emotional tone and checking understanding with open questions.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained focus on alcohol use in pregnancy and moved the session toward a concrete behavioral product (one-page brochure and partner actions), keeping questions tied to the central issue.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
