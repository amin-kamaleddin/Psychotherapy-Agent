# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing and affirming Sofia's experience and efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking (e.g., reflecting panic, sleep/anxiety issues) but missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of ambivalence and emotional tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on marijuana use and related goals, translating the session into specific, actionable steps (taper options, sleep strategies, scripts, coping card).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicited Sofia's input and co-created plans, adapting recommendations to her fears and priorities and sharing decision-making throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission, honoring pace, and framing slips as data rather than failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some personal motivations (cost, sleep, doubts) and built on them, but could use more evocative questions and scaling to strengthen client‑owned reasons and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative, equal interaction by checking preferences before drafting materials and jointly creating the daily plan and coping card.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created and reinforced opportunities for change talk and affirmed small commitments, though tended to move to solutions quickly instead of lingering to elicit more spontaneous change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and often reframed toward change-friendly options, but could improve use of double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to acknowledge both sides.
