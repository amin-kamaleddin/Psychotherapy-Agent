# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental regard that normalizes Anya's situation and affirms her strengths. No labeling, moralizing, or punitive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and accurate paraphrase that tie behavior to priorities like sports and college. Could deepen empathic work with more complex reflections of feelings and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, steady focus on early alcohol exposure and refusal skills, moving logically from assessment to skill-building and rehearsal. Interventions consistently tie back to protecting school, sports, and college goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is framed as a joint effort with the clinician asking preferences and adapting to Anya's choices. Feedback is consultative and Anya’s responses shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly supported through offering choices, asking what Anya wants, and avoiding coercive language. The clinician invites Anya to select lines to rehearse and does not push a single solution.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits actionable motivation by linking refusal language to Anya’s priorities and using role-play to reinforce goals. Could increase open, evocative questions to draw out deeper internal motives and commitment statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership with shared control of the agenda and regular invitations to choose next steps. The clinician acts as a teammate (e.g., playing the friend) and co-constructs the plan with Anya.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change-oriented language through practical tips and rehearsal. Missed opportunities to systematically deepen desire, ability, reasons, need, and commitment with targeted open questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses neutral redirection and practical strategies. Could be more active in softening sustain talk using techniques like double-sided reflections and explicit acknowledgment of both sides.
