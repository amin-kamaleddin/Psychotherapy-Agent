# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard, normalizing Ethan's context and affirming strengths; tone is nonjudgmental and reassuring throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking validate Ethan's ambivalence and help him elaborate his thoughts; clinician paraphrases goals and acknowledges limits effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conversation stays clearly focused on alcohol use at social events and moves repeatedly toward concrete harm-reduction planning and actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a joint exploration with frequent invitations for Ethan's input and co-construction of the plan, avoiding an expert-knows-best posture.

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly emphasizes choice and frames the plan as an experiment Ethan controls, respecting his agency without pressuring for perfection.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Clinician elicits several self-generated ideas and past success stories, but could deepen evocation by exploring underlying values and using scaling to mobilize commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with open questions and shared decision-making; clinician honors Ethan's expertise about his social context and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective elicitation of concrete change language and practical steps, though the clinician could better amplify, summarize, and elicit a specific near-term commitment or confidence rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoids arguing and skillfully pivots to values and alternatives to reduce sustain talk, but could use more double-sided reflections and explicit summaries to fully acknowledge both sides of ambivalence.
