# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard with normalizing statements, reflections, and affirmations; no shaming or moralizing language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that reduces defensiveness. Could deepen emotional exploration by probing feelings like loneliness or embarrassment with complex reflections or brief empathy statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on social drinking and concrete short-term strategies, repeatedly returning to behavior-specific plans and experiments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed—strategies and rehearsal are developed together and the clinician invites the patient's evaluation and tweaks.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering options, asking the patient to choose, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's ideas, strengths, and past tactics but leans toward practical problem-solving; could further evoke deeper intrinsic motivations and use scaling for importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Models partnership and shared expertise with inclusive language, joint rehearsal, and deferring to the patient's judgment about wording and tone.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk, helps generate concrete steps and small experiments, but could more strategically amplify commitment and probe confidence/why to strengthen resolve.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain concerns and gently redirects to solutions without dismissing them; could use more double-sided reflections and explicit cost/benefit exploration to resolve ambivalence.
