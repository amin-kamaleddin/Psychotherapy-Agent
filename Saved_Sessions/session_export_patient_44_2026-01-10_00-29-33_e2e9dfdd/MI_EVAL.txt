# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through affirmations and nonjudgmental language, normalizing progress and framing choices as sensible and doable.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and validation of concerns, though responses are somewhat formulaic and could probe deeper into emotions like shame or discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on target behaviors and progressively narrows to a specific, actionable plan tied to gym attendance, strength goals, posture, and tracking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-constructs the plan by proposing options, inviting patient choices, and iteratively building specifics together rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking what feels doable, and emphasizing the patient's preferences in shaping the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motives and future imagery (e.g., 'surprised then proud') but could deepen evocation with confidence scaling and eliciting a clearer commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the source of solutions throughout, repeatedly inviting selection and prioritization to foster client ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk by highlighting gains and asking for specific, doable goals, though could more consistently amplify and summarize to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk nonjudgmentally and redirects to actionable steps, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to diminish sustain talk's influence.
