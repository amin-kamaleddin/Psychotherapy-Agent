**Comments:** Provides clear, consistent unconditional positive regard with validating language and no shaming; corrective feedback remains warm and nonjudgmental.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, capturing ambivalence and repeatedly checking understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on weekend/social drinking and advances to a concrete, measurable plan (start day, specific strategies, confidence rating, follow-up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co-creates the plan and invites client input at every step, positioning Marek as an equal partner in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and framing suggestions as voluntary experiments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, worries, and past attempts through open questions and scaling, but occasionally moves to offering solutions before fully amplifying the client's own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Marek as the expert on his life, negotiates options and logistics, and consistently invites him to shape the plan and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits change talk via pros/cons, scaling, and small-experiment offers, but could more often reflect and amplify change statements and secure explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk using neutral reflections and normalization, yet could more strategically use double-sided reflections and evoke the client's own arguments for change.
