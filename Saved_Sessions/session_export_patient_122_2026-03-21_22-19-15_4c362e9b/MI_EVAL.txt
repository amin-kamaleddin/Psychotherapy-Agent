# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently expressed unconditional positive regard, validated Sofia’s experience, and affirmed strengths (e.g., park visits), avoiding judgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and timely reflective listening captured Sofia’s affect and ambivalence (e.g., "That sounds really tiring and frustrating"), deepening rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on increasing short walks and coping with emotional eating, repeatedly steering to specific, measurable actions and follow‑up without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician co‑constructed plans with Sofia (if‑then plan, reminder wording, accountability text), checked preferences, and shared decision‑making in a genuinely collaborative way.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician honored Sofia’s autonomy by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and inviting Sofia to choose and tailor the plan rather than directing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good elicitation of motivation via doable options and confidence scaling that led to concrete commitments, though deeper probing of values or longer‑term reasons would strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sofia was positioned as the expert in her life; the clinician sought permission, offered choices, and repeatedly invited Sofia to decide which options fit her routine.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively evoked and operationalized change talk (e.g., 10‑minute/5‑minute walk, reminders, texting Ana) but could have more frequently reflected and amplified spontaneous change statements to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed into practical if‑then coping plans and shorter steps, though the clinician relied more on neutral validation than on MI techniques like double‑sided reflections or deeper exploration of sustain concerns.
