# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—normalizing Kieran's frustration, affirming boundaries (e.g., pay as a dealbreaker), and avoiding judgment throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate empathic listening and reflections captured both content and feeling (e.g., 'feeling boxed in') and probed for concrete details in a manner that matched the client's terse, practical style.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear behavioral focus on obtaining concrete employment leads/placements and the clinician repeatedly narrowed the discussion to actionable specifics and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction modeled a cooperative team effort with the clinician offering to act only in ways aligned with Kieran's preferences and repeatedly soliciting his input on which options to pursue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician explicitly supported choice and control—offering options, asking which to try first, and honoring Kieran's right to decline or set non‑negotiables like pay.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively drew out Kieran's internal motivation and criteria for engagement, eliciting specific conditions (named contact, $18/hr, confirmation) and a past success to link to future behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned Kieran as the expert on his needs, asking for his ideas and choices and tailoring offerings to his terms (e.g., day‑before text, 10‑minute call).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited concrete change talk (e.g., 'If it’s real, I’ll show up' and a $/hour threshold) and a past success, but could have amplified and consolidated those statements more to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician validated sustain talk and redirected toward actionable alternatives without confrontation, though more double‑sided reflections or explicit reframes could have further softened ambivalence.
