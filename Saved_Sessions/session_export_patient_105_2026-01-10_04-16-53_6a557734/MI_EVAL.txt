# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with normalizing, nonjudgmental statements and validation, avoiding blame or criticism throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured Naomi's physical sensations and emotional impact, but many reflections were repetitive and missed opportunities for deeper, more complex empathic reflections about shame or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on managing anxiety during photo shoots and tied every clinician turn to concrete, job-specific strategies and outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a genuine collaborative partner by co-creating the plan, offering options, and inviting Naomi's input and choices at every step.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Naomi's choice and control, offered options and a low-stakes experiment, and respected her decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited client-generated reasons and steps and drew out motivation, but could have evoked values and strength of motivation earlier and used scaling to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint exploration, explicitly sought Naomi's preferences, and shaped the plan around her selections, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change-oriented language and translated it into a concrete experiment, but could more deliberately elicit and amplify DARN elements and confidence/commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected toward problem-solving and low-stakes testing, though more strategic moves (double-sided or selectively reducing reflections) would further soften resistance.
