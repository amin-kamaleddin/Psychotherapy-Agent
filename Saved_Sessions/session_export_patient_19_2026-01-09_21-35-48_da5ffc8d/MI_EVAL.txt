# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing ambivalence and offering practical help without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displayed strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and attention to emotion, though several reflections stayed content-focused rather than probing deeper shame or hopes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, focused orientation on gambling and financial behavior change with concrete steps and a scheduled follow-up, moving the session toward a doable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functioned largely as a collaborative partnership with role‑play offers and co‑created plans, but at times the clinician moved into clinician-driven problem solving rather than equal co‑generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by emphasizing choice, offering options, and normalizing decisions, reinforcing the client's sense of control over actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivations using scaling and values linkage, but shifted to providing solutions instead of further deepening the client's own reasons, confidence, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative, client-centered stance and invited choice, though occasionally offered many concrete suggestions before eliciting the client's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk with a readiness ruler and targeted reflections, but could further prompt desire/ability/reasons/commitment in the client's own words.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and acknowledged ambivalence, but used few targeted MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections or reframing) to reduce sustain language.
