# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with validating summaries and explicit normalization; repeatedly honors the patient's wish not to focus on stress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and paraphrasing of worries, symptom patterns, and feelings of being dismissed; would be stronger with deeper emotional reflections (fear, shame, loss of identity).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear clinical focus on diagnostic clarity and referral, consistently steering toward actionable steps like tests, letters, and escalation options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑creates the GP letter and scripts with the patient and follows her priorities and choices throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking which option is most helpful, and respecting her decision not to discuss stress now.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation of practical goals (tests, return to work) but limited elicitation of deeper motives, values, or confidence and missed opportunities for scaling or meaning exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership demonstrated through shared decision‑making, permission‑seeking, and jointly developing concrete plans driven by the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change‑related language and offers options, but does not systematically reflect, amplify, or follow up to deepen desire, reasons, ability, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and accepts sustain talk but provides little active softening (e.g., double‑sided reflections or reframes) to gently reduce sustain talk's influence.
