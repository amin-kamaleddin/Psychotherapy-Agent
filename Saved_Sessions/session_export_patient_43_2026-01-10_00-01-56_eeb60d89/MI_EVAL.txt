### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, validated Eric's feelings and respected his readiness level throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, though reflections were sometimes more summary- or strategy-focused rather than probing deeper feelings or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on smoking and related interpersonal issues with concrete, behaviorally-specific goals and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as co-author of the plan by inviting Eric's preferences, using joint problem-solving, and engaging him actively in role-play and decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Eric's choice and control by offering multiple options, asking what he preferred to focus on, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation and a small commitment, but leaned toward planning and problem-solving rather than using evocative questions to draw out deeper intrinsic reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and co-created the compromise, though at times supplied scripting without first inviting client-generated wording to strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively encouraged and shaped concrete change talk and a specific plan, but missed opportunities to deepen desire, importance, and confidence language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and accepted sustain talk while pivoting to manageable compromises, though sometimes moved quickly to directive problem-solving rather than using nuanced reflective strategies.
