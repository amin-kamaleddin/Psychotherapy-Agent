# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental language that validated Selene's context and values, avoiding blame and conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic attunement with accurate reflections and perspective-taking, though there were fewer deeper reflections of underlying emotions (fear, guilt, responsibility) to reach the top score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on medication adherence with concrete, measurable goals, contingency plans, and a planned follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as joint problem-solving: clinician invited preferences, incorporated Selene's responses into the plan, and kept control shared while respecting boundaries.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported Selene's choice and control by asking permission, offering options rather than prescriptions, and framing the plan as her decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal reasons and values (staying healthy for family) and used scaling, but could have more consistently deepened and amplified Selene's own motivations and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance and invited input on specifics, but could have strengthened partnership by eliciting Selene-generated options before offering clinician suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with summaries and scaling and asked for specifics, yet missed some opportunities to amplify brief change statements and convert them into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk but tended to move quickly to problem-solving rather than using double-sided reflections, strategic summaries, or explicit contrasts to shift sustain talk toward change.
