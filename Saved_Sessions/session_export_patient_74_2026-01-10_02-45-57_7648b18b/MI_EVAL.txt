# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing Linda's feelings and avoiding blame or shame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening with accurate content-level reflections and validation, but missed opportunities for deeper affective reflections around guilt and role-model identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking and a concrete, measurable 10-minute experiment with minimal drift and repeated tie-backs to planning and evaluation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative—solicited Linda's input, let her choose the focus and concrete steps, and framed the work as joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored Linda's autonomy by offering options, framing the intervention as an experiment, and using nonpressuring language that kept control with the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operated from the assumption that motivation resides within Linda and used questions to draw it out, but could have used more evocative questions and scaling to amplify intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Linda as the expert and co-created the plan, offering choices and asking which options felt most helpful, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change language and converted ambivalence into a specific, low-pressure experiment, though more probing and amplification of Linda's own reasons and importance/confidence scaling would strengthen this domain.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided arguing and normalized enjoyment while offering harm-reduction alternatives, but could have used more double-sided reflections and subtle reframing to further soften sustain talk.
