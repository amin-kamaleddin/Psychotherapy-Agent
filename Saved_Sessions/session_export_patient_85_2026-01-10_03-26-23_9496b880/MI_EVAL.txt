# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard, normalizes feelings, and avoids blame throughout the session. Warm, nonjudgmental language made the patient feel respected and supported.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured both content and affect. Reflections deepened understanding and consistently attuned to the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior (reducing OJ) and advances actionable planning at each step. Questions and prompts stayed tied to realistic next steps rather than drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in true joint problem-solving by soliciting and integrating the patient's ideas at multiple decision points. Language consistently supported shared planning rather than prescriptive advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options and asking for preferences, reinforcing agency. Avoids directive language and respects the patient’s selected approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, values, and concrete plans but could probe deeper into the meaning behind values and sources of stated confidence. Missed opportunities to elicit a stronger explicit commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance that privileges the patient's expertise and choices. Repeatedly checks in and co-constructs the plan with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with targeted, forward-looking questions and affirmations supporting specific plans. Could more consistently amplify, expand, and summarize change language to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward workable solutions while validating the patient's attachment to OJ. Could strengthen use of double-sided reflections and explicit ambivalence exploration to further diminish sustain talk.
