# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard, normalizing language, and nonjudgmental acknowledgement of Elena's constraints and efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate empathic listening with reflections capturing both content and feeling and open questions that invited emotional elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking reduction/cessation, moved from exploration to specific, actionable planning (patch, reminders, two‑week test).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a joint problem‑solving exchange with the patient’s expertise respected and the plan co‑created rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Elena's choice by offering options, honoring reluctance to quit cold turkey, and framing the trial as her decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intrinsic reasons and practical motivation (son's health, guilt, two‑week trial) but could further deepen values linking and strengthen voiced commitment and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Elena's ideas and preferences, framed the clinician role as helper, and meaningfully shared power in co‑constructing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete change statements and a short‑term plan but missed opportunities to amplify and summarize change language or elicit stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double‑sided reflections to redirect ambivalence, though more targeted techniques could have further reduced persistent sustain talk.
