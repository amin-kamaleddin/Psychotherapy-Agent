# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing difficulty and validating Daniela’s stress without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear understanding and reflective language mirror practical constraints and overwhelm, though the clinician emphasizes logistics more than probing deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintains a focused, organized approach on agreed targets (childcare and reducing smoking/drinking) with concrete, actionable steps and checklists.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician frames the work as joint—asking priorities and tailoring messages—but often supplies ready-made solutions rather than eliciting Daniela’s co-generated ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supports choice with permissive language, offers options, and honors Daniela’s preferences, reinforcing her control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some self-motivated language is elicited, but the approach is largely informational and problem-solving, with missed opportunities for evocative questions about values and motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative elements like asking which task to tackle, yet much of the interaction is clinician-driven with directive instructions rather than full co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician affirms small successes and helps generate steps, but uses few open questions or reflections specifically designed to evoke and amplify Daniela’s own motivators.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain-talk is acknowledged and practical solutions are offered, but MI strategies to soften ambivalence (e.g., double-sided reflections, rolling with resistance) are used inconsistently.
