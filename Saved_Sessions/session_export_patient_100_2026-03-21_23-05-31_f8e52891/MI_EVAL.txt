### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with normalizing, nonjudgmental language and validation of Maya's concerns throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated highly accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, repeatedly summarizing content and ambivalence and mirroring Maya's emotions and tensions.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol to protect academics/college plans and moved the conversation toward concrete, feasible actions and a specific plan.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Interaction was consistently collaborative and co‑creative, with the clinician asking for Maya's preferences and negotiating plans rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership
