# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates respect and nonjudgmental acceptance with validations and normalization, but at times language feels formulaic and could include more individualized affirmations of strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking with accurate, well‑timed reflections that capture both emotional tone and concrete worries, and frequently mirrors the patient’s language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on the specific target behavior (protecting time/asking for a text/ETA) and consistently ties questions and reflections to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructs the plan by soliciting the patient’s input at each decision point, summarizing and inviting refinement, and maintaining a non-authoritative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking what feels doable, and respecting the patient’s limits without pressuring or coercing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical reasons for change and a concrete plan, but could deepen intrinsic motivation by linking the change to broader values and strengthening commitment questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert, defers to their judgment, and narrows options only after eliciting the patient’s preferences, demonstrating strong power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports specific, doable steps and returns to the chosen action, but misses opportunities to amplify and consolidate commitment and confidence (e.g., scaling or eliciting stronger commitment language).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids argument and validates skepticism, yet relies mainly on mirroring and problem‑solving rather than using stronger MI techniques (double‑sided reflections, reframing, or selectively reinforcing faint change language) to reduce sustain talk.
