# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental. Validated Camila’s mixed goals, affirmed practical steps, and repeatedly assured non‑shaming follow‑up; preserved client autonomy around not wanting to quit completely.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries (loss of study time, weekday drinking, ambivalence). Missed deeper affective exploration of social‑anxiety drivers and emotional meaning of drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on reducing weekday drinking. Offered options, elicited choice and confidence ratings, moved logically to a concrete plan (drink‑free Wednesday) and arranged follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely co‑constructed the plan (client chose line, supports, and steps). Occasionally offered strategies/advice (mocktails, leave if needed) without explicitly asking permission.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Camila’s control over decisions, honored preference to cut back not abstain, and used questions that left final choices to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client’s reasons, resources, and confidence (6→7) and encouraged practical supports. Could have probed deeper into intrinsic motives and values (what "in control" means).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine power‑sharing: practiced client’s chosen lines together, negotiated which supports to use, and arranged a mutually agreed check‑in.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited desire, reasons, and plans (choosing Wednesday, texting Luisa, mocktails) and reinforced efforts. Missed opportunities to more strongly amplify and summarize change talk into commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated sustain talk and avoided arguing for abstinence; used double‑sided reflections. Could more actively reframe or link sustain talk’s costs to Camila’s goals to further weaken it.
