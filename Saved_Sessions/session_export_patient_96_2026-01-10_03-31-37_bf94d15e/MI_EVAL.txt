# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating and respecting the patient's choices throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good empathic listening with brief reflective language and offers of support, but reflections stayed pragmatic and did not probe deeper feelings or meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the lip piercing and related oral‑health concerns, tailoring all interventions to that target without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative—co‑creating scripts, offering options, and inviting rehearsal—but could better elicit the patient's agenda and more consistently use her language in summaries.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly affirmed the patient's right to decide, avoided pressure, and offered harm‑reduction options while honoring her decision to keep the piercing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did little to draw out the patient's own motivations or reasons for change, relying mainly on information and scripted responses instead of evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated the session as a joint enterprise by asking what the patient wanted and co‑developing wording, though occasionally defaulted to offering medical recommendations without explicit permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal attempts to elicit or amplify change talk; missed opportunities to ask open questions that would surface desire, ability, reasons, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce sustain talk, but did not actively pivot it toward change using double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to highlight discrepancy.
