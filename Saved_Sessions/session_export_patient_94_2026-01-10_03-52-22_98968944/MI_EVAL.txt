# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validated Maria's time constraints and concerns about inhalers, never dismissing her worries.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with effective reflections and summaries, capturing both practical and emotional barriers and tailoring proposals to her preferences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on inhaler use/adherence throughout, moving from exploring barriers to co-creating concrete, actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged Maria as an equal partner at each decision point, co-creating the one-page product and negotiating content, format, and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, asked permission, and respected Maria's conditions for trying medication, supporting her sense of control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited conditional willingness and translated it into a practical plan, but did not sufficiently probe deeper personal values or use scaling/commitment questions to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a collaborator throughout, repeatedly inviting Maria's preferences and positioning her as the expert on her life and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified limited change talk and offered concrete options, but missed opportunities to elicit broader values, importance/confidence ratings, or explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argument and validated concerns while offering reframed choices, though could have used double-sided reflections and selective reflection of sustain talk to better tilt toward change.
