# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating language (e.g., normalizing and explicitly validating painful memories) conveyed strong unconditional positive regard throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and paraphrases showed strong understanding of Elira’s experience, but the clinician sometimes moved quickly to solutions and missed opportunities for deeper affect-focused reflections (e.g., naming shame/child felt experience).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on avoidance driven by anxiety and the inner critic, with each question and intervention linked back to follow-through and behavioral options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was a joint exploration with inclusive language, repeated invitations for Elira’s preferences, and co-construction of the evidence-mapping exercise rather than an expert monologue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offered explicit choices and asked which path felt more useful, consistently reinforcing Elira’s agency and avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions elicited memories, critic content, and counterexamples well, but the clinician could have probed values or identity and asked Elira to elaborate on what small wins mean for her future to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician positioned Elira as the expert, followed her lead, and scaffolded rather than prescribing, with multiple choice points that demonstrated strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicited change-relevant language and used scaling, but could have amplified and reflected Elira’s successes more aggressively to chain change talk toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided amplifying sustain talk and reframed the critic’s protective function while steering to disconfirming evidence, though more double-sided reflections would have softened sustain talk more actively.
