# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding labeling or moralizing about missed doses.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and validated the patient’s exhaustion and ambivalence, de-escalating defensiveness; could have probed deeper into emotional impact and reflected the patient’s sarcastic tone for greater understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on daily preventive inhaler use and produced a concrete, measurable one-week trial with specific implementation steps and safety criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a true collaboration—clinician regularly asked for input, adapted suggestions to preferences, and co-designed the monitoring plan rather than assuming expert authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and control by offering options, using language that emphasized the patient's authority, and honoring boundaries about appointments and follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited personally meaningful outcomes and change talk that led to a plan, but could have further probed values, past successes, and used scaling questions to strengthen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed a collaborative stance and ceded expertise to the patient, inviting tweaks and honoring preferences throughout the plan development.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked and reinforced change talk resulting in a realistic trial and practical supports, but could have amplified reasons for change and elicited stronger commitment language or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence and avoided arguing, which reduced escalation, yet missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and directly reframing sarcastic minimizations to further soften sustain talk.
