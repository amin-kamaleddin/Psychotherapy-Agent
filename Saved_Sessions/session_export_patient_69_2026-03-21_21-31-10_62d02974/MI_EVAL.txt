# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—normalized Kieran's experience, avoided criticism, and respected his flat affect (offering to sit quietly). These behaviors maintained warmth and acceptance throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and validation of Kieran's tiredness and resignation. Could have probed deeper emotional nuances (e.g., frustration, shame) beyond surface paraphrase to reach a 5.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on target behaviors and translated ambivalence into clear, actionable steps (paperwork now; 30-minute CV task). Used specific planning questions to maintain behavioral focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the session as a joint enterprise—asked preferences, co-constructed the CV line, and negotiated decisions rather than imposing them. Tone and language positioned the clinician as a partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, checked Kieran's preferences, and affirmed his control (e.g., 'I’ll back whatever you choose'). Avoided pressure and reframed suggestions as options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical commitments and a concrete plan but did not explore intrinsic motivations, values, or ask importance/confidence questions. Limited effort to draw out personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaboration with repeated invitations for input and co-creation of solutions (backup plans, CV wording). Clinician consistently deferred to Kieran's expertise and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced small change talk, converting compliance into a specific, measurable commitment with coping strategies. Could have amplified reasons for change and probed importance/confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and pivoted toward planning, which avoided reinforcing resistance. Could have used double-sided reflections or selective reframing to further soften sustain language while preserving rapport.
