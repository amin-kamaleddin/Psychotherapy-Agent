# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently displayed unconditional positive regard, normalized setbacks, and used supportive, nonjudgmental language while praising small successes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good understanding with brief reflections and summaries, but empathy was sometimes more transactional and could probe emotional nuance and complex feelings more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing brief walks with concrete, measurable action steps and if‑then plans throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Felt like genuine collaboration—client input shaped choices, plans were co-constructed, and follow‑up was requested to maintain partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options, avoided coercive language, and reinforced Sofia's ownership of the plan while normalizing acceptable fallbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivations and short-term commitment but relied more on offering strategies than drawing out Sofia's deeper values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with invitations to choose and co-create plans, though at times the clinician shifted toward more directive recommendations rather than fully affirming Sofia as the expert.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change language and commitment to a first step, but missed opportunities to use more open, evocative questions to deepen desire, reasons, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and offered scaled fallbacks to reduce its potency, but did not frequently use strategic MI moves (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly resolve ambivalence.
