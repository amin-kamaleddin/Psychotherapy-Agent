# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental stance, normalized the patient's resistance, and honored boundaries (e.g., refusing to push appointments).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and an appropriate safety check showing care, but sometimes moved quickly to practical solutions instead of exploring underlying sarcasm, defensiveness, and overwhelm more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on improving day-to-day asthma self-management with a clear, measurable small-action plan and follow-up targets.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as an equal two-way exchange by soliciting the patient's preferences and repeatedly adapting the plan to fit the patient's constraints and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed patient choice, asked permission before advising, offered multiple optional strategies, and framed suggestions as choices rather than prescriptions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited minimal motivation and a short commitment but relied on advice-with-permission rather than drawing out the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-created the plan with the patient (alarm time/label, reminder type, weekend exclusions) and repeatedly checked in to honor the patient's preferences and limits.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced some commitments and co-constructed doable steps, but did not systematically evoke or amplify DARN-C language (desire, reasons, confidence, etc.).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively accepted and accommodated sustain talk to reduce defensiveness and offer low-burden options, though missed opportunities for double-sided or amplified reflections to pivot ambivalence more strategically.
