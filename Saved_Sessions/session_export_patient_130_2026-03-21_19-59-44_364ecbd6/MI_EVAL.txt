# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and avoided judgment (e.g., opening ‘I’m here to listen’ and multiple empathic acknowledgements), creating a warm, respectful tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking and asked permission before giving advice, but missed opportunities for deeper affective or complex reflections about Viktor’s feelings and relationship impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the target behavior and safety concerns, and guided the conversation toward a specific, measurable plan (one-month safer-limits agreement and check-in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong co-creation of the script and safety plan with iterative edits and offers to role-play, adapting wording to Viktor’s preferences and sharing authority over outcomes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Viktor’s choice by asking permission, offering options, accepting his boundary about not quitting, and framing decisions as his to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used evocative questions that elicited commitment to a one-month trial and safety measures, but could have probed more for personal values, reasons, and confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered collaboration by inviting Viktor’s input and choices, though the clinician sometimes assumed the expert role by drafting wording rather than eliciting Viktor’s language first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made appropriate attempts to elicit change talk and generated some commitment (e.g., trying a month), but did not consistently amplify or explore Viktor’s own reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontational responses and redirected to harm-reduction options, yet missed strategic moves (double-sided reflections or summaries) that would more actively soften sustain talk.
