# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Maria's exhaustion and honoring her request not to be fixed immediately. Maintains a warm, supportive stance and offers presence rather than pressure to change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses strong, accurate empathic reflections that often mirror Maria's exact language and checks understanding. Invites how Maria wants to be heard, demonstrating careful perspective-taking and responsiveness to emotional intensity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps an appropriate focus on emotional pain and immediate safety and offers options for support, but the session stays mainly in validation and reflective listening. Could more explicitly move toward a concrete safety plan or coping strategy as readiness emerges.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as an equal partnership: solicits Maria's preferences, follows her lead, and co-constructs the moment-to-moment plan (presence first, safety planning later). Language and behavior consistently frame the work as joint.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices (how to be present, whether to repeat reflections) and checking what would be most helpful. Consistently refrains from imposing fixes and respects Maria's pacing and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some attempts to elicit internal motivation but relies primarily on reflecting sustain talk and a single cue about Maria's mom. Does not systematically draw out or amplify reasons, values, past successes, or confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and power-sharing; repeatedly asks how Maria wants to proceed and follows her stated preferences. Demonstrates clear deference to Maria's expertise about her needs in the moment.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits a small hint of change talk (the faint part for her mom) but does not deepen or systematically expand it with targeted open questions. Misses opportunities to amplify brief change-language or elicit hopes, reasons, or past moments of relief.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk and validates the reality that 'nothing works,' which prevents reinforcing reasons for staying the same. Could more strategically use double-sided reflections and summaries to highlight ambivalence and tilt toward change.
