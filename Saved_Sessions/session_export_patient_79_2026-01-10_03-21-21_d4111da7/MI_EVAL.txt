# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated the patient's pragmatism and limits, avoiding judgment even when the patient was sarcastic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, nuanced reflective listening and perspective-taking by paraphrasing ambivalence and the patient's priorities, which lowered resistance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on preventer adherence and moved the conversation toward specific, actionable steps tied to the patient's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged the patient as a partner throughout, using his ideas (alarm, nightstand, spare) to shape the plan and keeping decisions driven by his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control with invitational questions, honored the patient's limits (no extra appointments), and avoided pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified the patient's intrinsic motives (better sleep, work functioning) and encouraged patient-owned reasons and plans for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as the expert, shared power in planning, and repeatedly asked for and deferred to his ideas about what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk with targeted questions and reflections, producing concrete plans and expressed commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward change, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections and explicit ambivalence summaries.
