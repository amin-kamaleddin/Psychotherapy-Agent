# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming—normalizes Rosa's concerns, praises strengths, and respects her pace throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate content reflections, but would benefit from more affect-focused and complex reflections to deepen emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with concrete planning (medication options, timelines, pocket scripts, slip plan) and actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed—offers choices, rehearses lines with Rosa, and repeatedly checks preferences to tailor the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by asking permission, offering options rather than directives, and repeatedly emphasizing Rosa's choice and ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits motivations, barriers, and past experiences (scaling, triggers), but could more systematically draw out personal values and amplify Rosa's own reasons for quitting.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative and invites Rosa's input, though the clinician could hand more agenda-setting to Rosa earlier and explicitly leverage her past successes in shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and shapes change talk via readiness scaling and 'what would move you' questions, but could better amplify change statements and elicit stronger importance, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk but often moves quickly to information and problem‑solving instead of using double-sided reflections, reframing, or strategic redirection to weaken sustain talk.
