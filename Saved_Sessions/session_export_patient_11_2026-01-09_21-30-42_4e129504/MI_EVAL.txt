# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized Amira’s experience, and affirmed her choices without judgment. The clinician maintained a warm, respectful stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, validating emotions like low energy and loneliness. Reflections mirrored Amira’s priorities and functional goals, showing clear understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a focused, appropriate direction on increasing physical activity with a concrete, measurable goal (20 min, 3x/week). The clinician repeatedly returned to the target behavior and ended with a clear implementation plan and check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the plan with Amira, invited her input on wording and reminders, and tailored the checklist and check‑in to her preferences. Language positioned clinician and client as partners sharing decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple choices and framed suggestions as options, repeatedly checking Amira’s preference and honoring her decision to focus on activity over nutrition. This preserved her sense of control and avoided pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited Amira’s motivations and resources with evocative questions (desired differences, readiness, barriers) and used her responses to build the plan. The clinician drew motivation from Amira rather than imposing reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited Amira’s input, refined plans based on her responses, and deferred to her choices about check‑ins and praise wording. The interaction clearly reflected shared power and mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk using scaling questions and by affirming concrete goals, producing strong commitment language (8/10 readiness). The clinician built on Amira’s statements to secure a specific plan and check‑in.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk and redirected ambivalence into problem-solving with fallback plans and cues. Could improve by using more double-sided reflections and strategically amplifying brief change statements to further reduce sustain talk.
