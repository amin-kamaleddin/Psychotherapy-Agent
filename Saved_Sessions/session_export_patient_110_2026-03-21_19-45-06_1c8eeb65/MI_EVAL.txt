# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating the patient's concerns and normalizing worries so the patient felt safe raising hesitations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking with accurate reflections and pragmatic validation, but missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of anxiety and wavering affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on starting statin therapy and managing side effects, moving the session toward concrete steps like a written monitoring/action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly a joint effort: the clinician invited input, integrated patient suggestions, and role‑played together so decisions were co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by asking permission, offering options, and framing plans as decisions the patient would make with his doctor.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some value‑driven statements but relied heavily on information, scripts, and plans rather than systematically drawing out the patient's own motivations and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance and co-created the plan with the patient, though frequent expert information slightly shifted the balance toward instruction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some reinforcement of change talk through question lists and rehearsal, but lacked open questions and strategic reflections to amplify personal reasons and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized concerns, but did not consistently use double-sided or amplified reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and soften sustain talk.
