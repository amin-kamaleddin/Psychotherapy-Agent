### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and understanding of Amira's perspective and barriers, though some reflections were brief and missed opportunities to deepen emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity, repeatedly steering the session toward concrete planning tied to Amira's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and co-creative, with the clinician continuously eliciting Amira's choices and tailoring recommendations to her context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported Amira's sense of choice by offering multiple options, asking preferences, and affirming fallback plans without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intrinsic motivators and readiness effectively but shifted to fairly specific prescriptions early, limiting opportunities for Amira to generate her own options and deeper change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making and treated Amira as the expert in her life, co-constructing the plan through questions and checks for consent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced desire, reasons, and commitment through targeted questions and planning, but could more frequently amplify and reflect Amira's change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
