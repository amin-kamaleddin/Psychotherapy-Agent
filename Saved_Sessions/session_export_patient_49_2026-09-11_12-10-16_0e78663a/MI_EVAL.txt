# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, validating constraints and affirming the patient's competence and choices without blaming or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking with accurate reflections of context and concerns, but could deepen empathic reflection of affect and ambivalence using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on cutting back smoking and moves logically from options to a concrete, testable one-week experiment with specified supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative approach—clinician invites input at every decision point and tailors the plan to the patient's preferences and lived experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, checks consent for the experiment, avoids pressure, and honors the patient's wish to try on her own, reinforcing autonomy throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and practical solutions, but could probe deeper values and elicit stronger commitment or confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed with shared power; the patient directs choices and the clinician repeatedly invites joint planning and prioritization.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits change talk via questions about differences and past successes and by offering doable options, though could more often amplify and summarize change language to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes toward manageable swaps, but would benefit from more double-sided reflections that acknowledge sustain talk while highlighting change.
