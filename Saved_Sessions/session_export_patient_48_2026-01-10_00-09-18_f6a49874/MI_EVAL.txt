# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—opened with clear positive regard, normalized uncertainty, and reframed self‑criticism to reduce shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Multiple validating statements and normalizations showed good empathy, but deeper reflective listening and complex reflections of emotion/ambivalence were limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on increasing brief physical activity with concrete, measurable steps and no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong co‑creation—invited input, let the client choose wording and options, and modified the plan to fit the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering multiple options, asking permission before intervening, and normalizing slipping to preserve client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of doable options and small commitments occurred, but the clinician relied heavily on giving solutions and missed opportunities to draw out deeper motives, values, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance and invited selection, yet much of the plan was clinician‑generated rather than elicited from the client's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited preparatory change talk and intentions with choice questions and affirmations, but did not frequently probe underlying reasons, values, or amplify stronger desire/commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframed self‑criticism, normalized slipping, and avoided confrontation, though there were missed chances to further explore resignation or use double‑sided reflections to pivot sustain talk toward change.
