# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect for the patient's boundaries with repeated nonjudgmental reassurances and options rather than pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses reflective language that tracks the patient's desire for privacy, but reflections are somewhat repetitive and surface-level rather than varied or deeper.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on initial engagement and rapport-building, centering the session on a safe, nonpressured space and linking safety checks to that aim.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a partnership—offers choices, asks permission before prompts, and honors the patient's preferences, including no follow-up contact.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the patient's sense of choice and control through repeated 'no pressure' statements, offering options, and explicitly honoring boundaries.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes minimal effort to elicit the patient's internal motivations or reasons for change and misses simple, low-risk evocation opportunities (e.g., scaling or open curiosity questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently defers to the patient's expertise, offers a menu of session formats, and follows the patient's lead, demonstrating shared power and collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does not actively elicit change talk; after offering options the clinician fails to follow up with evoking questions to draw out desire, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids confrontation and neutralizes resistance by restating boundaries and refusals, though misses opportunities for double-sided reflections to gently invite exploration.
