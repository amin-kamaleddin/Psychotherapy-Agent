# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician communicated clear unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support through validating statements and affirmations, avoiding criticism and allowing Jamal to state his needs.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and normalization and summarized practical barriers, showing good perspective-taking; reflections stayed at a surface level and did not probe deeper emotions like shame or autonomy concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on getting to school on time and translated discussion into concrete behavioral strategies and a specific action plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative problem-solving: clinician proposed options but invited Jamal’s input and final selections, with the plan materially shaped by Jamal’s choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently affirmed Jamal’s choice and control by offering options, checking preferences for reminders and messaging, and avoiding pressure while encouraging ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical reasons and some commitment language but did not sufficiently draw out intrinsic motivation, values, confidence, or discrepancy to deepen self-generated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently worked in partnership by offering multiple options, asking which steps felt doable, and inviting Jamal to shape follow-up and troubleshooting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific change talk and helped Jamal commit to a concrete first step, though there was limited probing of deeper internal reasons beyond problem-solving.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it but did not use techniques like double-sided reflections or explore discrepancy to reframe externally driven motives into intrinsic reasons.
