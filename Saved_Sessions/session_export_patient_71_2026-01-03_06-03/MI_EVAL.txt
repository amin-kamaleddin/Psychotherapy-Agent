### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Multiple instances of change talk were elicited and reflected (vision, commitment to 10 minutes ×4, possible addition of self‑care night). The clinician reflected and reinforced these but could have more actively amplified and summarized change talk into explicit commitment language (e.g., scaling or a firm “I will…” statement).

Observed behaviors:
- [x] Elicited and reinforced change talk (desire, ability, commitment language)
- [ ] Explicitly amplified/summarized change talk into a stronger commitment statement
- [ ] Used scaling or direct commitment elicitation

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and normalized without reinforcing it; clinician used validation and moved to contingency planning (3‑minute backup, buddy text). Could further benefit from double‑sided reflections or selective summarizing to reduce sustain talk more explicitly.

Observed behaviors:
- [x] Validated and normalized sustain talk (exhaustion, late nights)
- [x] Pivoted to contingency planning to address barriers
- [ ] Used double‑sided reflections or selective summaries to resolve ambivalence
