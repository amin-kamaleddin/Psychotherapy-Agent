# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalization and supportive language, avoiding judgment or moralizing. Affirms the client's goals and pragmatic efforts without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates excellent reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing both content and underlying ambivalence. Reflections are timely and deepen understanding rather than merely repeating facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on limiting drinking and progressively narrows conversation toward concrete steps like a two-drink limit and buddy strategies. Each question advances planning and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as genuine joint problem-solving with the clinician eliciting preferences and co-constructing the plan. The clinician invites the client's input at every step, ensuring the plan fits the client's context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, deferring to the client's expertise, and avoiding directives. Actions are framed as the client's choices to try rather than clinician-imposed rules.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from the client through open questions that elicit reasons, past successes, and strategy ideas. Reflections amplify the client's own change language and capability.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasizes shared decision-making and the client's expertise, using language that distributes power and invites collaboration. The clinician repeatedly asks what fits the client's life and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk by asking for a small doable change and encouraging specific planning (e.g., two-drink limit, buddy system). Reflections and labeling strengthen the client's expressed intentions and strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing or confronting, reflecting ambivalence and normalizing imperfection. Gently redirects toward harm-reduction planning without amplifying resistance.
