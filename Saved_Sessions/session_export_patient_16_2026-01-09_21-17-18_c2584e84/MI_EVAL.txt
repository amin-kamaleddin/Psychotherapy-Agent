# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with frequent affirmations and acceptance of Anika's choices (e.g., not forcing device disposal). The clinician conveys strong unconditional positive regard throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and some reflective listening, summarizing priorities and triggers. Deeper emotional reflection on peer pressure, stress, and ambivalence is limited and could be expanded.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on vaping and its impact on basketball eligibility, returning to concrete goals and action steps. The session moves purposefully toward a start date, coping plans, and role-play.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as an equal partner with frequent invitations to participate, co-constructing scripts and practice opportunities. The clinician tailors recommendations to Anika's preferences and follows her lead on what she will try.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces Anika's right to choose and invites decision-making (e.g., picking start dates, accepting not to throw the pen away). Slips are normalized and framed as learning, supporting client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivation by asking readiness, triggers, and linking change to valued goals like basketball, and secures a concrete start date. The clinician, however, moves to problem-solving relatively quickly instead of further drawing out intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invites input, rehearsing lines and planning together. At times the clinician shifts to delivering detailed advice and steps without explicit permission, creating a mild expert stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and helps articulate a specific goal and strategies. Could more consistently reflect and amplify client change statements to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is tolerated and not punished, but the clinician does not actively soften it with techniques like double-sided reflections or exploring vaping's function. The session often moves quickly to advice, leaving sustain talk less addressed.
