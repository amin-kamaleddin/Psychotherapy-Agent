# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently displayed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, validating the client’s preferences and limits. This created a safe space for the client to state needs without pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections captured content and basic emotional tone, supporting rapport and understanding. Could deepen by probing emotional nuance and using more complex or amplified reflections at key moments.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with questions and reflections tied to concrete action. The session progressed logically from values to specific planning and contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative, with co-design language and client-driven decision-making throughout. Suggestions were offered as options and the plan integrated the client’s routines and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirmed the client’s right to choose and avoided controlling language, offering multiple options and asking which felt most likely to fit. This supported self-directed decision-making throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client’s own motivations and linked them to concrete behavior change, eliciting robust change talk about energy and confidence. Could push further to deepen intrinsic motivation and consolidate commitment (e.g., ask for a brief summary or scaled importance/confidence).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician adopted a genuine co-creative stance, positioning the client as the expert and inviting preferences at every decision point. Power-sharing language like 'let’s co-design it' enabled shared planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively evoked and reinforced change language, reflecting and amplifying goals and concrete steps (e.g., three sessions, calendar blocks). To advance, more deliberate amplification and summary of change talk could convert momentum into explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledged sustain talk (e.g., postponing nutrition) without judgment and redirected to strengths and workable plans. Could further use double-sided reflections or brief ambivalence exploration to help the client resolve sustain talk organically.
