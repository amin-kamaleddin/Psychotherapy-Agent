# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating ambivalence and affirming priorities without shaming. Reflections and supportive language normalize Anya’s experience throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained, accurate reflective listening captures feelings, trade-offs, and values, allowing Anya to elaborate and feel heard. Summaries and perspective-taking show high empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol-related risk to protect grades, sports, scholarships and trust, moving from values to concrete planning. Questions and reflections consistently tie back to that target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as mutual problem-solving with the clinician inviting Anya’s input and co-constructing plans (movie night, refusal lines, texts). Tone and language foster shared responsibility and client-led choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician preserves Anya’s sense of choice by offering options, asking which strategies feel easiest, and checking preferences before scripting. Suggested lines are offered in a way that maintains agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits values and reasons for change and connects them to options, drawing out internal motivation. Could more deliberately deepen and amplify change statements (confidence ratings, past successes, or explicit commitment).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative with active power-sharing; the clinician resists an expert-only stance and lets Anya steer specifics of the plan. Frequent prompts for her preferences demonstrate strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk through open questions and reflections, naming concrete strategies and benefits. Misses opportunities to systematically amplify strength/confidence or elicit explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk using double-sided reflections and redirects toward practical strategies, minimizing debate. Could more actively address the specific fear of teasing (e.g., explore examples, rehearse responses) to deepen resolution.
