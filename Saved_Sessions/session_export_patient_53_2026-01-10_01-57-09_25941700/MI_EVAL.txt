# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating feelings and normalizing setbacks throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good empathic statements and reflective checks that made the patient feel heard, but tended to move quickly to problem-solving rather than using frequent complex reflections to deepen emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on increasing brief community engagement and moved the conversation toward a realistic, measurable action plan with specific steps and timing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with invitations for preferences and rehearsal, though frequent clinician-provided scripts and detailed options slightly reduced equal sharing of power.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the patient's right to choose, emphasized permission to bail, and checked preferences for reminders and wording, strongly supporting autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some elements of the patient's plan (choice and timing) but relied heavily on clinician-generated strategies rather than drawing out intrinsic motivations, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited input and offered choices, but the session was often clinician-driven with detailed plans and scripts rather than co-constructed from the patient's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and praised some change talk (choice of a walk), but did not consistently probe importance, confidence, or amplify tentative statements to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk, validated avoidance as understandable, and offered manageable alternatives, though could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly resolve ambivalence.
