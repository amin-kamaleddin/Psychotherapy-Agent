# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, affirming Maya’s values and strengths throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, complex reflections captured ambivalence and linked feelings to behavior, helping Maya feel understood; a bit more strategic silence could occasionally deepen impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use affecting school and moved logically to a concrete, actionable goal (finish homework before going out) with practical implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was co-constructed with Maya—clinician elicited ideas, offered options, and allowed her to select and refine steps, demonstrating genuine shared problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently emphasized choice and ownership by offering options and asking which step felt most doable, avoiding pressure or authoritative directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective evocation linked behavior to values and elicited personal reasons for change, but could be strengthened by asking confidence/importance ratings and eliciting a client-stated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was framed as a collaborative exploration, with repeated invitations for Maya’s input and respectful power-sharing in generating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicited change talk and moved toward a concrete plan, but should amplify and reflect change statements more forcefully and probe for stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and double-sided reflections reduced defensiveness, yet social-pressure sustain talk remained prominent and could be redirected more selectively toward change.
