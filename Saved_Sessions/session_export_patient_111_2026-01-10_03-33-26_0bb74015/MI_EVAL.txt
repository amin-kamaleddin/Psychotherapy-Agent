# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with validating language and normalization, but occasionally becomes directive and solution-heavy instead of maintaining purely unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking (e.g., early reflections and tone in role-plays), though there are missed opportunities to deepen emotional reflections around anger, shame, and the function of smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on smoking and a concrete two-week trial with clear steps, agendas, and check-ins, avoiding drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—decisions are co-constructed, the clinician adapts to patient requests, invites role-play, and integrates the patient’s routines and preferences into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, emphasizing the trial is voluntary, framing slip/reset non-punitively, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some relational motivation and readiness but relies more on offering scripts and options than on drawing out the patient's intrinsic motives, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by asking the patient's preference and inviting participation, but occasionally slips into giving multiple specific recommendations that feel expert-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk (readiness ruler, relational reasons, concrete plan) but misses opportunities to deepen or amplify desire, ability, reasons, and need language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and offers compromises and autonomy, yet often focuses on logistics and accepts sustain language without consistently using double-sided reflections or reframing to reduce resistance.
