# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, opening warmly and validating the client's coping and workload.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking to capture practical pressures and doubt, but reflections are more cognitive/behavioral and could probe deeper emotional nuance or use occasional complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, behaviorally-focused direction toward reducing late nights/completing the essay earlier and consistently steers the session to concrete planning and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a true collaborative partnership by soliciting client input on specifics (assignment, times, wording) and co-creating the intervention without imposing choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and reinforcing ownership of the plan rather than using directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client's motivations and planning with questions and a confidence scale, but could more actively draw out values, past successes, and amplify the client's own change language to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters genuine partnership and power-sharing from the start by inviting agenda-setting and letting the client's preferences shape the plan and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits change talk through goal-setting, confidence rating, and concrete steps, but missed some opportunities to amplify change language and explore importance or past successes to increase commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes doubts constructively and quickly shifts to problem-solving, which limits sustain talk; could further reduce ambivalence using double-sided reflections and summaries that highlight reasons for change.
