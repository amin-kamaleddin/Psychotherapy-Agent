# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, validates motivation and builds from the teen's values (soccer, scholarships). Avoids blame or shame and maintains unconditional positive regard throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear understanding and links to Alexei's goals, with safety checks and attention to his preferences. Could use more frequent reflective paraphrases of ambivalence and feelings to deepen empathic responding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a tight focus on alcohol risk and its relevance to soccer/scholarships, steering toward a concrete, time-bound plan. Role-plays and contingency plans further maintain behavioral focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solicits choices, co-creates the plan, and practices together, showing good teamwork. However, the clinician often leads with a written plan and directive coaching, which frames them as the primary expert.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges choice, offers options, and invites input (e.g., check-in time, reward), avoiding coercive language. Still, prescriptive recommendations and exact phrasing reduce the patient's felt ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation tied to values and elicits commitment language through planning and practice. Would benefit from more open-ended questions and reflective listening to draw out the patient's own arguments for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively by inviting planning and role-play and incorporating the patient's choices. Frequent prescriptive instructions and coaching cues at times shift authority back to the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with specific plans, commitments, and rehearsals. Missed opportunities remain for deeper open questions and reflections to amplify internal reasons and desire for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and redirects minimization toward valued goals, reducing sustain talk's intensity. Lacks more explicit double-sided reflections and normalization that would acknowledge minimization while gently contrasting it with goals.
