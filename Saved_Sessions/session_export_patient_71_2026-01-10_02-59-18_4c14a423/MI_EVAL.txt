# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, respectful regard with normalization, praise, and offers of support; there was no labeling or criticism throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking helped Laura feel heard, though some reflections were pragmatic and could probe deeper into the emotional meaning of values like feeling 'grounded.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and guided the session logically from values to a specific, actionable experiment with planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician co‑constructed the plan, asked preferences and permission, and deferred decisions to Laura, making the interaction clearly collaborative rather than directive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options, invited Laura to choose, and avoided pressuring language, consistently supporting her sense of choice and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation from Laura's values and used scaling questions, but sometimes moved quickly to clinician-provided solutions which limited further client-generated elaboration of change reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal collaborator through permission-seeking, shared planning, and deference to Laura's preferences, reinforcing a partnership stance throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with specific questions, confidence scaling, and summaries, though opportunities were sometimes shortened when the clinician shifted to offering solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized ambivalence while offering constructive fallbacks, but could further soften sustain talk using more double-sided reflections to highlight both sides of ambivalence.
