# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through validations and normalizing statements, repeatedly recognizing the client’s strengths without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides strong, accurate reflections of ambivalence and priorities, though several reflections repeat content rather than deepening into emotional nuance or bodily experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on boundary-setting and concrete action steps, with each question and reflection linked to the target behavior and plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently works as an equal partner, asking for the client’s preferences, responding to choices, and co-creating the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports autonomy by offering choices, asking what feels most useful, and framing decisions as the client’s to make, avoiding pressure while structuring options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client’s own reasons, strengths, and concrete motivators, building on the client’s language to draw out internal resources for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the client’s expertise, inviting input at each step and offering help only when invited.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and strengthens actionable change talk and planning, but could more intentionally amplify commitment (e.g., scaling questions or eliciting a verbal commitment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it and uses double-sided reflections, but could more strategically diminish sustain talk by juxtaposing amplified change talk or exploring consequences.
