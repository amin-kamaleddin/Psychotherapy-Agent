# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with validating, nonjudgmental language and normalizing statements; tone remained warm and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, nuanced reflective listening that captured both content and emotion (shame, burden, difficulty) and responded to fears with acceptance and practical support.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking cessation and steadily moved toward a specific, measurable plan (gum at 5:30, alarms, check‑in) without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the plan with the client, solicited her ideas and wording preferences, and adjusted the plan based on her input, reflecting genuine partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options, invited preferences, avoided pressuring language, and respected the client's pacing and choices, reinforcing her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons to quit and a confidence rating but relied somewhat on offering solutions rather than deeper probes to draw out stronger, value‑linked self‑motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the encounter as collaborative from the start, repeatedly invited input, and shared decision‑making power in selecting steps and check‑in details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (daughter, health, shame) and affirmed chosen steps, but missed opportunities to amplify and deepen those motivations with evocative follow‑ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk without confrontation, normalized slips and offered alternatives, but used few double‑sided reflections or deliberate reframes linking sustain talk to change reasons.
