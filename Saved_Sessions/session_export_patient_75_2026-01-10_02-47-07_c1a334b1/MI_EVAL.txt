# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's concerns; warm opening and validating language are maintained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and validation (acknowledging anxiety and desire to be heard) but sometimes stays at a surface level and could use deeper reflective summaries of the patient's inner conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on asthma medication adherence and communication with the prescriber, with all interventions tightly linked to improving adherence and safety.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—co-creating lists, scripts, and rehearsing together—but at times leans toward clinician coaching by providing multiple ready-made phrasings rather than eliciting the patient's own wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by offering multiple options, asking which feels most helpful, and affirming the patient's control over decisions and pace of action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to draw out motivation and choices lead to some commitment language, but evocation is mostly clinician-driven and misses deeper elicitation of the patient's values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a partnership through shared decision-making and practice, though several moments feel clinician-directed and could better incorporate the patient's own phrasing and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Produces some tentative change talk via options and scripting, but does not systematically elicit the patient's desires, reasons, or confidence to strengthen and deepen change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and corrects misinformation, yet relies more on problem-solving and scripts than on strategic reflections or double-sided reflections to soften resistance.
