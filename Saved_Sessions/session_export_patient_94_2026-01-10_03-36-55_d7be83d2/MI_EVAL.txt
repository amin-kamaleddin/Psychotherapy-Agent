# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating the patient's stance and avoiding blame despite sustain talk and time pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic understanding with validations and practical help, but missed opportunities for deeper reflective statements about the patient's frustration and research‑based skepticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on inhaler adherence and feasible next steps, keeping the conversation centered on a specific behavior and safety thresholds.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—co‑constructed the plan, repeatedly invited patient edits to the PCP message, and adjusted recommendations to fit the patient's constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient autonomy by offering options, respecting refusals, and affirming the patient's control over decisions and follow‑up choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited intrinsic motivation; focused on logistics and safety rather than drawing out the patient's values, reasons, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated active partnership through shared decision‑making, frequent checking, and deferring to the patient's preferences and edits.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created opportunities for change talk (trial offer, drafting PCP message) but did not systematically elicit or amplify the patient's own reasons, desires, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing and normalized concerns while reframing monitoring options, though at times relied on corrective information rather than double‑sided reflections to address ambivalence.
