# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, acknowledges the patient's upset and mirrors his priority of protecting his job without blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate empathic reflections and picks up ambivalence, but misses opportunities to explore deeper emotional intensity and links to broader values or identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, goal-oriented focus on addressing the waiting incident and protecting the patient's job, providing concrete steps and resisting topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acts largely as an equal partner by asking preferences and offering roleplay, but frequently supplies exact wording and delivery instructions instead of co-creating messages.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control, offering options, respecting refusals, and providing tools for the patient's chosen course without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits actionable motivation and preferences that lead to concrete plans, but does not sufficiently probe underlying values, past successes, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the patient's input and adapts to his preferences, foregrounding his expertise, though clinician-led scripting slightly shifts the balance of power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and strengthens concrete change language and rehearses specific behaviors, but does not elicit broader reasons for change or confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing, but does not use MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections, pros/cons) to soften it and at times facilitates escalation with firm scripts.
