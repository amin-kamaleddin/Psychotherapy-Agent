# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Amira’s experience, validating priorities without judgment. No instances of labeling or admonishment were observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided accurate, rapport-building reflections of content and some feeling, using perspective-taking language. Reflections were sometimes surface-level and formulaic, and could be deepened with more affect‑focused or amplified reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on increasing physical activity and moved the session from exploration to concrete planning and commitment. Questions and reflections consistently narrowed toward actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in joint problem-solving and repeatedly invited Amira to shape the plan, checking preferences and supports. Language and offers made the exchange a cooperative, co‑created process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options and asking which the client preferred, respecting declines (e.g., nutrition/socializing). Offers of help were framed as optional, reinforcing client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited Amira’s own reasons, functional goals, and concrete motivation, and drew out a client-originated commitment. Questions encouraged articulation of benefits and practical steps rather than imposing external rationale.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a collaborative partner by inviting preferences, checking readiness, and co‑constructing supports like calendar blocks and backups. The clinician maintained shared decision-making and client expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk through specific, change‑oriented questions and reflections, culminating in a concrete commitment. Reinforced desire, reasons, and commitment language throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not escalate sustain talk and respectfully honored Amira’s limits (e.g., delaying nutrition/social plans). Could more actively use strategic softening techniques (double‑sided reflections, selective emphasis on change language) to resolve ambivalence.
