# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently expresses respect and positive regard, normalizes feelings, and affirms the client's strengths without judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections capture both behaviors and internal states, showing strong perspective-taking and emotional attunement with the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays clearly focused on a concrete behavioral experiment (attending the dance meeting and related proximal steps) and moves steadily toward actionable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a joint exploration: the clinician solicits the client's input at each step and incorporates her ideas into a shared, practical plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking which feels most achievable, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good use of evocative questions to draw out desires and reasons for change, though deeper probing of underlying values and stronger elicitation of commitment would strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician fosters shared power and co-creates the plan by asking permission, offering choices, and treating the trial as a collaborative experiment.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits specific change-oriented steps and frames an experiment, but could do more to amplify, summarize, and solidify the client's own reasons and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and normalized without argument, yet the clinician often reflects sustain talk rather than using double-sided reflections or selective amplification to more strongly pivot toward change.
