### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Used scaling and asked for specific doable steps that generated change talk

**Comments:** Scaling and planning elicited concrete change language (alcohol-free nights, swaps). Could more deliberately elicit and amplify reasons for change (e.g., “What makes that a 5 and not a 2?”) to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
- [x] Avoided confrontation / did not argue
- [ ] Used double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk
- [ ] Explicitly summarized sustain talk before moving to planning

**Comments:** Clinician avoided confrontation but tended to move to planning without explicitly softening sustain talk (e.g., double-sided reflections). Some sustain talk (downplaying need to change) remained unaddressed and could have been more effectively mitigated.
