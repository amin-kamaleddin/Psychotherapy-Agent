# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with multiple normalizing and validating statements and avoids blaming or shaming language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing both content and affect (shame, ambivalence, triggers).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and moves logically from assessment to concrete goal-setting and action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the patient as a partner by co-constructing the plan, offering choices, and drafting materials to support the patient's agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Affirms the patient's freedom to choose, offers options rather than directives, and checks preferences for follow-up and interventions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivation (daughter, readiness/confidence scaling) and asks what would increase motivation or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making throughout, asking which strategies feel most doable and inviting the patient to shape the plan and check-ins.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces multiple change statements and sets a small goal, but could do more to amplify commitment language and elicit the patient's own verbalized reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects to coping strategies and planning, though would benefit from more double-sided reflections and explicit exploration of sustain arguments.
