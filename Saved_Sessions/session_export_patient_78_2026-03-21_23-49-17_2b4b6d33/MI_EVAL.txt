### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows strong perspective‑taking with frequent, accurate reflections that validate Lucía’s frustration and practical priorities. Reflections reframe the gum trial as manageable and make Lucía feel understood.

**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused target on smoking reduction with practical steps (nicotine gum trial, swap one smoke per break, measurable success criteria). Questions and reflections repeatedly tie back to the agreed experiment without pressuring for dramatic change.

**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by framing the gum trial as a time‑limited experiment and soliciting preferences for check‑ins and tips. Respect for boundaries (no lectures) and multiple invitations for input reinforce ownership and self‑determination.

**Comments:** Elicits Lucía’s motivations (being there for her daughter) and obtains a concrete experiment, but misses opportunities to deepen internal motivation with importance/confidence scaling or exploration of past successes. Strong evocation overall but could probe deeper to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk, getting a specific time‑limited commitment and linking behavior to values, but could more actively amplify and summarize change statements and probe strength/confidence. Doing so would help convert initial desire into firmer commitment.

**Comments:** Skillfully rolls with sustain talk and normalizes resistance, explicitly accepting Lucía’s boundary about lectures and redirecting to pragmatic options. This reduces resistance and keeps focus on workable, patient‑chosen steps.
