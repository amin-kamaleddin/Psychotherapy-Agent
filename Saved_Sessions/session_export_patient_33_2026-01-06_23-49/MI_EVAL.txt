### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validated Diego's experience and choices throughout the session and normalized ambivalence while offering practical support.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that helped Diego open up, but could deepen empathy with more complex, feeling-laden reflections exploring underlying stressors.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on heroin-related harm reduction and steered conversation toward specific, actionable safety goals without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Diego as an expert in his life and co-created plans, repeatedly asking his preference and collaborating on scripts and resource options.
