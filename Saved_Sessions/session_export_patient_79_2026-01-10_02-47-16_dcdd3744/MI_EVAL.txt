# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, honoring the patient's requests and normalizing small steps; avoided lecturing or admonishment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and validation of the patient's frustration and limits; could deepen empathy with more complex reflections that name underlying feelings or motives.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on inhaler availability and a low‑effort routine while providing appropriate safety guidance; the session stayed on task.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was cooperative problem‑solving—clinician solicited preferences, incorporated patient suggestions, and co‑created practical, concrete solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the patient's right to choose, offered options, and explicitly honored the request for no scheduled follow‑ups, avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some client‑generated ideas and a readiness rating but did not sufficiently explore personal values, deeper reasons for change, or confidence in the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and power‑sharing—options were offered, wording was co‑crafted with the patient, and the plan reflected the patient's input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced actionable change talk with concrete options and if‑then plans; could further amplify the patient's own reasons and confidence to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used acceptance to defuse resistance, redirecting toward small acceptable changes; missed some opportunities for double‑sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
