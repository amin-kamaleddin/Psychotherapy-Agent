# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalized ambivalence, and used frequent affirmations to validate Richard without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and accurate understanding of concerns and values; reflections were mostly pragmatic and could deepen by naming underlying emotions (e.g., anxiety about surprises, hopes for retirement).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the conversation clearly focused on adherence and lifestyle change using scaling, barrier identification, and stepwise concrete planning to maintain momentum toward the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a genuine collaborative partner by co-creating plans, inviting preferences, rehearsing language, and building realistic backup strategies with Richard.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Richard's freedom to choose, offered multiple options, checked permission, and supported self-efficacy with small, doable steps without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations with scaling and linking change to valued goals, producing commitment language; could strengthen evocation with deeper, open follow-ups and scaling probes to amplify intrinsic reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by asking which steps felt helpful and sharing decisions, though occasionally listed multiple specific suggestions in a slightly more directive way without explicit permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk through importance/confidence questions, choice of small steps, and value-linking, but could further amplify personal reasons and explore discrepancy to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without reinforcing it, but did little to strategically soften or redirect it; using double-sided reflections and exploring costs of staying the same would strengthen this area.
