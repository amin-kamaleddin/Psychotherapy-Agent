# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Sofia’s feelings with nonjudgmental, individualized validation and praise. Warmth and acceptance are sustained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, summarizing feelings and realistic goals while validating caution and worry. Reflections are specific and consistently responsive to Sofia’s experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on the peer conflict and immediate coping/action, quickly moving to concrete, actionable steps and follow-up plans. The session remains goal-directed without overriding Sofia’s preferences.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a collaborative exploration, soliciting Sofia’s input at each step and co-creating the plan (message wording, timing, contingencies). Clinician offers help only when requested and builds solutions together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Sofia’s sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and emphasizing her right to decide when and how to act. Language avoids pressure and reinforces control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, desired outcomes, and practical reasons for change, but offers several directive scripts that somewhat reduce motivation arising solely from Sofia. Could deepen evocation with more open questions, confidence/importance scaling, and past-success exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Sofia as the expert on her life and invites co-creation of the plan, repeatedly offering choices and permission-based support. Power-sharing is clear and consistently practiced.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with open questions and a small concrete step, and asks for a commitment to try once. Could more systematically reflect and amplify Sofia’s change statements and explore importance and confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward low-risk, manageable steps while normalizing caution and offering contingency plans. Could more strategically use double-sided reflections or explicit summaries of sustain talk to honor ambivalence while guiding toward change.
