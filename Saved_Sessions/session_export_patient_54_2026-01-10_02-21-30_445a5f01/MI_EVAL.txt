# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizing tensions and affirming values. Maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with strong reflective listening and synthesis. Uses open questions to check understanding and invite disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use during pregnancy and moves the conversation toward concrete, time-bound actions. Progresses logically from values to specific supports and steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving, soliciting the patient's input on specifics and offering to practice rather than impose solutions. Language positions clinician and patient as teammates.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes patient choice and control, offering options without pressure. Elicits preferred approaches and reinforces ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and practical resources tied to the patient's values but focuses more on planning than exploring deeper intrinsic reasons. Could probe strength of motivation and confidence more systematically.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co-constructed with collaborative language and shared decision-making. Solicits permission and adapts to the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and affirms concrete change intentions and steps but stops short of maximizing change talk. Could amplify desire/ability/need/confidence language and elicit explicit commitment or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk and acknowledges autonomy, but does not actively use double-sided reflections or gentle reframing to reduce doubt. Largely pivots to planning instead of exploring or softening sustain-oriented statements.
