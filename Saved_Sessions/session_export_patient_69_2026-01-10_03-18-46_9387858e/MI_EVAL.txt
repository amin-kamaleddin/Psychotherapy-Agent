# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing tension and creating a safe stance for a defensive client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries, but reflections are often content-focused and sometimes formulaic with limited deeper affective exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on improving engagement with support services and consistently moves the conversation toward a specific, actionable plan (secure an interview, practice a one-liner).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving, solicits the client's input at each step, and uses his answers to co-create the plan rather than directing unilaterally.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices, asks for the client's preferences, and affirms that decisions rest with the client, reinforcing ownership of next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the client's own reasons and motivations, highlights conditional commitment, and draws on the client's strengths to build motivation from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power with the client by repeatedly asking preference, inviting co-planning, and letting the client's agenda shape the specifics of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk, guiding the client from general desire to concrete, actionable commitment language and measurable outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and avoids reinforcing sustain talk and pivots to change, but could more often use double-sided reflections, strategic reframing, and explicit validation of reasons for not engaging.
