# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, offering choices and accommodating the patient's preferences throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used empathic listening and reflective statements that validated concerns, but frequently shifted quickly to factual content with limited deeper emotional probing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on medication-safety: assessed status, explained risks, and produced concrete communication tools and next-step plans without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and co-constructed the materials, iteratively editing drafts to incorporate the patient's requested lines and offering ongoing assistance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by offering options, framing recommendations as choices, and preserving the patient's control while providing necessary safety guidance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical needs and details but did little to draw out internal motivations, values, or confidence; could have asked readiness or elicited the patient's own reasons for action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner by inviting input, implementing edits, and sharing decision-making throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged action by offering concrete steps and co-creating messages that facilitate contacting providers, though missed opportunities to explicitly elicit and amplify the patient's own change language or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce uncertainty and redirected toward safe actions with factual reframing, though could have used more MI-consistent double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence.
