# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a warm, nonjudgmental stance, validated the patient's choices, and normalized ambivalence throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries and echoed the patient's language, but reflections stayed more summary-level and did not deeply explore underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on vaping and the concrete change goal, with each intervention tied to the target behavior and a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting input, negotiating strategies, and confirming the patient's choices rather than imposing a plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice by offering options, acknowledging limits, and framing suggestions as choices the patient could accept or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and intentions and reinforced intrinsic motives linked to outcomes, but relied more on practical solutions than deeper evocative questioning about values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership by co-constructing the plan, asking permission before offering help, and deferring to the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Highlighted and reinforced the patient's change language and invited commitment steps, though could have further amplified the patient's own reasons and values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and did not strengthen sustain talk, but missed opportunities to elicit or strategically diminish sustain talk (e.g., double-sided reflections) before moving to planning.
