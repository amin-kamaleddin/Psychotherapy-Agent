### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed consistent unconditional positive regard and normalized Yasmin’s struggle, validating her values and praising sensible choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking matched Yasmin’s ambivalence, though deeper emotion-focused reflections about body dissatisfaction (shame/fear) could strengthen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on shifting from crash dieting to sustainable habits and produced concrete, time-bound steps (Sunday 3:00pm roast, reminders, portioning).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative—clinician routinely invited Yasmin’s input, co‑constructed the plan, and responded to her requests (grocery list, text) rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently respected Yasmin’s choice, offered options instead of prescriptions, and checked what felt doable to reinforce ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Yasmin’s own reasons and solutions using open questions and scaling, prompting her to generate supports like batch-cooking, reminders, and accountability.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Yasmin as the expert on her life, asked for her preferences, and shared decision-making in creating a practical, acceptable plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and reinforced change language with targeted questions and reflections, used a confidence ruler, and amplified Yasmin’s planning and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward change with practical strategies, but used few double-sided reflections and did not deeply explore reasons for the persistent desire to lose weight.
