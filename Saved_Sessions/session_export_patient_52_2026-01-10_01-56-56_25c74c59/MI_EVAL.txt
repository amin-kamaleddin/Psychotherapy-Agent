# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard, normalized the patient’s experience, and avoided shaming while praising effort and validating distress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and timely validations captured exhaustion and guilt well, though there was limited deeper exploration of shame and underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on alcohol as a coping behavior and tied all interventions (tracker, backup plan, safety instructions) back to that target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner—asking permission, co-creating and tailoring tools, and following the patient’s preferences throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options and invitations rather than directives, emphasized small/experimental steps, and checked the patient’s comfort with tasks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change statements and used the patient’s language in tools, but could more systematically draw out reasons, values, and confidence (e.g., scaling questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reflected a genuine partnership stance—regularly asked permission, invited choice, and adapted plans to the patient’s requests.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk and translated it into concrete small steps, but missed opportunities to deepen commitment with follow-up open questions and values-based prompts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used neutral/double-sided reflections to defuse sustain talk, though could more actively elicit the patient’s pros/cons and amplify parenting concerns to counter sustain statements.
