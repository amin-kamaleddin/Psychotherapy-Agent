# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validates the client’s burden while honoring her priorities and plans without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective listening focused on practical concerns, but could use more affective reflections to capture underlying emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on a specific, actionable target (one short calm text and bedtime) and moves the conversation from problem identification to concrete implementation without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑constructs wording, boundaries, and rehearsal options, treats the client as an equal partner, and adapts to her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices, follows the client's lead, and affirms her control over the plan with no directive pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical motivation tied to the client's values and generates preparatory commitment, but could probe deeper meaning, confidence, and past successes to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine power-sharing by asking the client which options feel best and building the plan around her expertise about what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces specific change language (script, timing, backup) and consolidates commitment, though it could more directly assess importance/confidence and elicit past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects toward change-focused actions, normalizing concerns, but could use more explicit double-sided reflections and strategic pivots when sustain talk appears.
