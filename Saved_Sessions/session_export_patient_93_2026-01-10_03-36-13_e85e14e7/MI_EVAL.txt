# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming (e.g., opening support statement and repeated praise for choices), avoiding shaming when the patient minimized drinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captured ambivalence and values, but missed deeper exploration of emotional nuance (e.g., retirement loneliness or feelings about the fall) with more probing reflections or open questions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use with concrete goals and actionable steps, keeping the session tied to the behavior-change target throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance — options were offered, permission asked, and the patient’s choices shaped the plan, demonstrating joint problem-solving and co-construction of goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and ownership (offering multiple options, asking which felt doable, normalizing trial periods), supporting the patient’s agency throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective at eliciting the patient’s motivations and a concrete plan (e.g., asking which strategies felt doable), but could use more open-ended prompts and scaling/confidence questions to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful, though several clinician-generated suggestions were offered in succession; eliciting the patient’s own ideas before providing many options would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk and specific commitments (weekly limit, alcohol-free days, tracking), but could have amplified and summarized change language more and asked about confidence and outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected toward change in response to sustain talk, yet missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and explicitly affirming sustain talk while highlighting discrepancy.
