### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows clear unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental language and multiple affirmations; could add more explicit validation of underlying emotions (e.g., embarrassment or fear) to deepen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and accurate, complex reflections that capture both sustain and change sides and keep the client engaged; occasional deeper reflection of underlying emotion would further strengthen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol reduction and fall risk, steering toward specific behavioral goals and implementation plans with minimal drift; balances exploration with purposeful goal-directed planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as genuine joint problem-solving with co-developed tactics and language that elicits the client's preferences; no signs of an authoritarian stance or unilateral decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice, offers options rather than directives, and frames suggestions as choices to consider, reinforcing the client's ownership of the plan; excellent support for self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation by drawing on values, past success, and desired outcomes and converts them into a concrete plan; could more explicitly link articulated values to the chosen behaviors in a closing summary to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites Evelyn's input, shares power in decision-making, and builds the plan from her prior successes, demonstrating she is treated as the expert on her life; partnership is strong and consistent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions and linking to past successes, converting desire into concrete plans and confidence scaling; could selectively reflect the strongest change statements verbatim to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk respectfully with double-sided reflections and neutral validation, avoiding reinforcement; there were missed opportunities for more strategic softening (e.g., amplified reflection or pointed discrepancy) to accelerate movement toward change.
