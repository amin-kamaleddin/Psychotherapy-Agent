### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance throughout, normalizing hesitancy and offering manageable steps and reassurances.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent empathy with reflective listening and naming emotions, and anticipated freezing by offering a calming strategy and a one‑sentence line to use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on seeking medical evaluation, breaking the task into actionable micro‑steps and steering the session toward a specific next step chosen by the patient.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in joint problem‑solving by co‑creating scripts, offering role‑play, and soliciting the patient's preferences for contact and follow‑up, adjusting plans to her comfort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice and control (e.g., 'no pressure to book,' which step feels most doable), offering options rather than directives and validating the patient's decision-making.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicited actionable motivation for pragmatic micro‑steps but did not sufficiently draw out deeper intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence to strengthen longer‑term commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treated the patient as a partner in decisions, sharing control over wording, mode of contact, and the plan while inviting her input at each step.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited change talk for small commitments and used supportive techniques, but missed opportunities to amplify reasons for change and to assess importance/confidence.
