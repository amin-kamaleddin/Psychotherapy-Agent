# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and validates the patient's priorities and concerns throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, concise reflective listening and perspective-taking that tracks the patient's emotional and pragmatic concerns across multiple turns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on medication hesitancy and advances the conversation toward a concrete, measurable one-month experiment and follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates clearly as a partner with the patient, inviting priorities and co-designing supports so the patient's ideas shape the plan rather than relying on top-down advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces choice and reversibility, elicits what the patient is willing to try, and affirms the patient's right to pause or stop the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and practical strategies tied to values, but could probe deeper into personally meaningful reasons and more strongly elicit commitment/confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames decisions as joint work, asks what help the patient wants, and shares power in planning, demonstrating strong collaborative decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits and reinforces change talk and prompts actionable steps, but would benefit from amplifying change statements and explicitly eliciting commitment/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain talk and pivots toward change without confrontation, though more use of double-sided reflections and targeted reframing could further soften sustain talk.
