# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through affirmations, normalizing ambivalence, and avoiding labels throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and accurate summaries of stress and triggers, but missed some opportunities for deeper affective or complex reflections to surface meaning and emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use from assessment to a concrete, testable harm‑reduction plan and relapse strategies, guiding the session toward behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—solicited Brandon’s input on specifics, co‑constructed the plan, and tailored recommendations to his preferences and feasibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and avoided coercion by offering options, asking permission, framing experiments as voluntary, and normalizing slips as learning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations, resources, and practical reasons for change (confidence rating, coping ideas), but could have probed values and personal importance more to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Brandon as the expert and shared decision‑making power, inviting him to choose goals and plan details rather than prescribing actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out and reinforced change talk with questions, confidence ruler, and a concrete plan, yet could more often amplify and chain change statements toward stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed sustain talk toward harm reduction, but missed some opportunities for explicit double‑sided reflections to highlight ambivalence and reduce sustain talk's pull.
