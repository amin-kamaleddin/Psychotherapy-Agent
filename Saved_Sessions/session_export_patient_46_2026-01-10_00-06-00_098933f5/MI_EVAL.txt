# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, avoiding labels or shaming; validated Ethan and asked which steps felt most doable.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that likely helped Ethan feel understood, though there were missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional content and defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on adolescent alcohol use and moved efficiently to practical, specific harm-reduction strategies and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative and co-constructed—choices, scripts, and plans were tailored to Ethan's language and social context with frequent solicitation of his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Ethan's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure, reinforcing that decisions were his to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited desire and reasons for change using targeted questions and a readiness ruler, but could have further explored intrinsic motives, confidence, and barriers to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed a genuine partnership by seeking permission, sharing options, and letting Ethan shape the plan, reflecting equal-power problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited concrete change talk with questions and the readiness ruler, though reflections and amplification of specific change statements could have been used more to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk with harm-reduction options, but did not consistently use double-sided reflections or explicit summaries to more actively pivot toward change.
