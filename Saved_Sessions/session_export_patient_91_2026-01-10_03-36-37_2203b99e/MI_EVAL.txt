### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through normalization, reassurance, and compassionate responses to missed attempts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate empathic listening and reflection, naming Marcus’s anxiety and overwhelm and validating his concerns about monitoring and readings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on blood sugar monitoring and ties all suggestions and tools directly to the 2-week monitoring goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint problem-solving by soliciting preferences, asking permission, and co-creating the plan (texts, start day, tracker).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, framing the plan as a 2-week experiment, and encouraging self-compassion for missed days.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation and plans but shifts quickly to offering strategies rather than deeper open questions to draw out Marcus’s personal reasons, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally invites Marcus’s input and preferences, but frequently offers concrete scripts and recommendations that sometimes position the clinician as the primary problem-solver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits change language (plan, start day) unevenly and tends to provide solutions instead of amplifying Marcus’s own desire, ability, reasons, or need for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes ambivalence effectively but uses few strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided or selective reflections) to diminish sustain talk and pivot toward change.
