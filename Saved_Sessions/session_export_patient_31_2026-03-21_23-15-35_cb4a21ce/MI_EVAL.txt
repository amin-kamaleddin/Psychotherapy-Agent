**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing ambivalence and honoring David's preference to keep an evening cigarette without shaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and implied emotions and using reflections to check understanding rather than direct the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration

#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
