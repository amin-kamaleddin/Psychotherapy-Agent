# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician is supportive, nonjudgmental, normalizes fear, and affirms Sofia’s steps; occasional directive medical instructions early in the session felt more prescriptive than warmly accepting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Therapist offers accurate, validating reflections of Sofia’s fear and distress and supports her through role-play; missed opportunities to reflect deeper feelings about social loss and identity tension.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear focus on alcohol use in pregnancy and moves logically from risk information to concrete, actionable steps (scripts, exit plan, check-in text).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is joint—role-play and co-created plans show collaboration—but moments of unilateral medical instruction reduced the sense of fully equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offers choices and frames strategies as options, supporting autonomy; however, some directive medical recommendations were given without asking permission, slightly reducing choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation through planning and rehearsal, but underused questions that would draw out Sofia’s internal motives, values, strengths, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites Sofia’s preferences and co-constructs plans demonstrating partnership, though initial prescriptive directives briefly shifted into an expert role.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits and reinforces change language, converts responsibility into rehearsed statements and concrete plans, but could more explicitly prompt and amplify desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk toward practical strategies that preserve social connection while supporting abstinence; could add double-sided reflections to better acknowledge sustain talk.
