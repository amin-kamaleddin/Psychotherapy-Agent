# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental normalization of slips, with repeated safety checks reflecting caring, respectful concern throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic stance with accurate reflections and validation of ambivalence and consequences, though the clinician could probe deeper into emotional meaning and use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on weekend drinking and moved the session logically from assessment to concrete, measurable harm-reduction planning without losing a client-centered tone.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co-constructed the plan with the patient, invited input, and shaped practical strategies around the patient's ideas and constraints, modeling an equal collaborative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, and honoring the patient's readiness level while encouraging ownership of selected strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some of the patient's motivations and commitments and encouraged self-monitoring, but relied more on offering strategies than on deeper elicitation of values, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned the patient as the expert and invited him to choose strategies, using inclusive language and shared decision-making to build the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change-related language and helped select doable steps, yet missed opportunities to amplify change talk with reflective emphasis and scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk with normalization and harm-reduction options, though could have used more double-sided reflections and deliberate elicitation of counterarguments.
