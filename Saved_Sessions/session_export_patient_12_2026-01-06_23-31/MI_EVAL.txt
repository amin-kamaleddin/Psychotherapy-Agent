# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing feelings after relapse and affirming effort throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, compassionate reflections and safety checks, but misses some opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of the patient's hopelessness and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear harm-reduction focus with concrete, short-term goals (24‑hour target, timers, coping steps) and consistently brings the conversation back to actionable plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames interaction as joint problem-solving and checks which options feel doable, but supplies many specific steps which shifts the balance toward clinician-driven content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently offers choices and emphasizes patient control, though occasional wording around commitment (e.g., asking for a 24‑hour commitment) could be experienced as slightly pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete behavior commitments and actions but does not sufficiently draw out the patient's internal values, reasons for change, or use scaling to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and supportive (stays with patient, offers to check back), but at times shifts into an expert/teacher mode with long prescriptive lists instead of co‑constructing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to elicit and shape change talk with small-step prompts and praise, but rarely explores or amplifies the patient's brief change statements or uses scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and validates sustain talk, yet misses opportunities for strategic techniques (double-sided reflections, exploring exceptions) to pivot sustain talk toward change.
