# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard from the opening line, normalizes ambivalence, and maintains a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently accurate perspective-taking with complex reflections and paraphrases that validate the patient’s worries about focus, side effects, and competing priorities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a sustained focus on smoking cessation and concrete implementation steps (patch vs prescription, breathing tool, monitoring), with minimal topic drift and progressive planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaves as an equal partner by co-designing the plan, asking procedural preferences, and consulting the patient at every decision point rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices and affirms the patient’s control (e.g., asking which option feels most doable) and checks preferences for follow-up and involvement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out motivations and patient-centered reasons/solutions but could probe deeper for confidence and a stronger verbal commitment (e.g., scaling questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Overtly collaborative with power-sharing evident: the patient’s preferences drive the plan and the clinician invites spouse/doctor involvement as desired.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change language leading to concrete statements (patch + breathing tool), but could better amplify and consolidate change talk and elicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk (stress, social cues, concentration fears) and pivots to solutions, though at times sustain talk is restated rather than strategically reframed to reduce its influence.
