# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, validated the client's perspective, and honored boundaries without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, specific reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured both content and feeling and helped the client elaborate.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, tight focus on a single, concrete target behavior and used questions and reflections to narrow toward practical action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session decisions were negotiated and the client’s input repeatedly shaped the plan, creating an equal, cooperative process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice, offered options, and avoided pressure, emphasizing the client's control over participation and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited pragmatic motivations and past success factors but did not probe deeply into intrinsic values or confidence to strengthen internalized motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert by inviting preferences and delegating decision-making, enabling co-design of the session plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk, moving from abstract desire to concrete, testable commitments and specific action steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently redirected sustain talk by validating resignation, though could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence more explicitly.
