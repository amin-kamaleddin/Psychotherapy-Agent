# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect through validating, normalizing language and avoided judgment. The clinician affirmed Nadine's feelings and struggles, creating a warm accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, often complex reflections that captured both concrete details and emotional tone, repeatedly validating Nadine's internal experience. Empathy was strong and consistent throughout the exchange.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on improving Nadine's rest via partner support and a nap, repeatedly steering toward actionable, time‑limited steps. This kept the session goal-directed and productive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like an equal partnership with ideas co‑generated and clinician inviting Nadine's input at multiple junctures. Decisions were co‑constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Nadine's control by offering choices, framing the plan as optional and reversible, and asking which step felt most doable. The clinician avoided pressure and supported autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out Nadine's reasons and resources (desire for rest, past success) and elicited doable steps, but could have probed deeper into values and asked for confidence/commitment ratings. More linking to broader values would strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative, client‑centered stance by inviting choices, offering options, and framing the plan as a joint experiment. The clinician deferred to Nadine's expertise about her life throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited change talk and turned ideas into a concrete plan, with Nadine offering several doable statements. Missed opportunities remain to amplify willingness and elicit stronger commitment language or a confidence/commitment rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and used strategic reframing and small‑experiment language to reduce resistance. Could increase use of double‑sided reflections and explore concerns about downsides more explicitly.
