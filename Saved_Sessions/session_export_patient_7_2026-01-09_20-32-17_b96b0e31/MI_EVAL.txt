# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respected Amina's boundaries, honored silence, thanked her for setting limits, and used no judgmental or pressuring language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and some reflective language and normalization, but missed opportunities for deeper reflections naming underlying feelings like mistrust or frustration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear session focus on initial engagement and safety throughout and avoided drifting into unrelated topics or pressuring for details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Built the interaction as a partnership by offering options, asking the client's preference, and making decisions contingent on Amina's wishes throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by repeatedly reinforcing client control, offering clear choices, and honoring explicit requests without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made only minimal attempts to draw out intrinsic motivation; after the opening invitation the clinician did not pursue gentle curiosity-based prompts to elicit desire, reasons, or need for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative, allowing Amina's preferences to shape the session and demonstrating shared control and teamwork.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicitation of change talk was rare; the clinician appropriately avoided pressuring but also did not use brief strategic prompts (e.g., scaling, curiosity about hopes) to invite even small change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by validating boundaries and redirecting control to the client, though could have further reduced sustain talk with double-sided reflections or brief invitations to consider small options.
