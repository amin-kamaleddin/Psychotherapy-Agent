# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard—validates frustration, normalizes low readiness, and supports small steps without admonishment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses solid reflective listening and perspective-taking and attends to safety, but does not probe deeper emotions (e.g., irritation with authority or worry about her daughter) with more complex reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on smoking urges and organizes the session around specific, testable behavioral changes ending with a concrete homework plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly joint—solicits preferences, co-creates the plan, checks in repeatedly, and incorporates the patient's chosen wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice and control, offers multiple options instead of directives, normalizes partial engagement, and avoids pressuring the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits small commitments and some client-generated language but rarely probes deeper motivations, values, or asks evocative open questions to draw out DARN-C.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters power-sharing by offering choices, asking preferences, and respecting short responses while co-constructing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits brief change talk and behavioral experiments but relies more on offering strategies than systematically evoking or amplifying the client's own reasons, desire, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids confrontation—validates exhaustion and reframes work as small and voluntary—but could further use double-sided reflections or explicitly acknowledge sustain reasons before pivoting.
