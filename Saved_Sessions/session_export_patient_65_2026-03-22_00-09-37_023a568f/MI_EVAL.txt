# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client’s concerns (e.g., accepting the “no promises” stance). Tone is supportive and nonjudgmental with no blaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening that captures both content and affect and tracks the client’s evolving thinking. Reflections validate stress and worry and deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on completing court-ordered community service and systematically moves toward concrete steps. Questions and reflections tie back to logistics and a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames tasks as joint problem-solving and repeatedly solicits the client’s input and consent before proceeding. Offers to assist (script, alarm, practice) are contingent on the client’s willingness.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, asks what feels doable, and explicitly accepts ambivalence, supporting the client’s sense of choice and control. Avoids pressure despite the external legal mandate.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete, client-generated logistical solutions and plans but does not sufficiently explore broader values, importance, or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation. Missed opportunities to draw out desire/need language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the client as the primary decision-maker through collaborative language and preference checks. Co-constructs the plan rather than imposing directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits actionable change talk and planning (script, alarm, scheduling) but focuses mainly on logistics rather than amplifying intrinsic reasons or strengthening commitment. Opportunities to elicit firmer commitment and confidence were underused.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes avoidance as understandable, redirecting toward small steps which reduces defensiveness. Relies mostly on neutral reflections and logistical redirection rather than advanced strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or costs/benefits) to fully dissipate sustain talk.
