# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing ambivalence and using affirmations throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and validation, but reflections were often brief and pragmatic rather than deeper, feeling-focused explorations of motivations like 'unwind' or social connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking and moved conversation toward specific, measurable behavior-change steps while keeping it patient-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the encounter as a joint enterprise by repeatedly soliciting patient preferences, co-creating the plan, and setting the check-in method/time to the patient's choice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, avoided coercive language, normalized slips, and emphasized voluntary small steps, supporting the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and resources with readiness rulers and choices, but could more deeply explore intrinsic reasons, values, and past successes to strengthen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: asked permission, tailored alternatives to the patient's preferences, and co-created realistic goals and follow-up plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and translated readiness into concrete actions, though opportunities to amplify and deepen change reasons were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected toward change-oriented options, but used few double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge sustain while pivoting to change.
