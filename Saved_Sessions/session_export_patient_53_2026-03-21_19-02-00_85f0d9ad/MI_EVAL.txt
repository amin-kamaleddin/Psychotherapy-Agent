# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, normalizing avoidance and reinforcing small steps which demonstrates strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses an empathic tone and some reflective listening and safety checks, but misses opportunities to deepen reflections that name the patient's ambivalence or fear more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing community engagement through concrete, measurable micro-steps and links these to coping strategies and follow-up actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operates as a partner by offering options and asking permission, but sometimes shifts to clinician-led instruction instead of jointly generating the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice and control, offering multiple options, emphasizing the ability to stop anytime, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some client-generated details (confidence, step choice) but largely offers solutions and does not sufficiently draw out the patient's personal reasons, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates a collaborative stance by soliciting choices and confidence ratings, yet often presents a detailed clinician-led plan rather than co-creating steps with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates opportunities for change talk and small commitments, but does not consistently elicit or amplify the patient's own reasons, benefits, or intrinsic motivation for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes hesitation, but tends to redirect quickly to planning without using double-sided or strategic reflections to soften sustain talk.
