# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone with explicit supportive language and validation throughout, praising effort and avoiding shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, but many reflections stayed surface-level and missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of ambivalence and emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on school punctuality and moved the session toward a concrete, time‑bound plan with commitment and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used inclusive language, invited input, and planned follow-up, but frequently offered multiple prescriptive tips that shifted the process toward clinician-driven problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and emphasizing that plans could be adjusted based on Jamal's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Employed evocation tools (confidence ruler, option selection, visualization) but did not sufficiently elicit Jamal's internal motives, values, or past successes beyond external reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and invited choices, yet the clinician's many concrete suggestions reduced opportunities for Jamal to generate his own solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change talk and used scaling and planning, but largely supplied solutions instead of prompting Jamal to articulate intrinsic reasons and personal motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and gently reframed toward benefits, but did not use double-sided or selective reflections and often moved quickly to problem-solving, leaving sustain talk underexplored.
