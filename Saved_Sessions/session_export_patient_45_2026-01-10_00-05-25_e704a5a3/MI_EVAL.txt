# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating skepticism and avoiding admonishment; explicitly promised no lectures after slips.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and perspective-taking with validations and repeated safety checks; lacked deeper, complex reflections exploring underlying feelings or fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on drinking/eating and moved to a concrete, time-limited action (7-day log, water-first rule) with a start date and check-ins; could better link week-one plans to longer-term diabetes goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative co-creation of the plan—patient choice of focus, joint design of the log and check-ins, and adaptation to patient preferences throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by offering options, respecting the patient's choices (including no lectures), and affirming that the decision is the patient's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation via choice and a confidence ruler, but did not sufficiently draw out the patient's intrinsic reasons, values, or personal goals for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through power-sharing and asking the patient to choose priorities and check-in preferences, though occasional expert-style information-giving appeared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and a concrete commitment to a small experiment, but did not systematically amplify or elicit deeper personal reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided argument and reframed sustain talk, normalizing skepticism and treating slips as data; could use more double-sided reflections to shift ambivalence toward change.
