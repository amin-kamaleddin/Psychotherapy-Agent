# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, honored the patient's choices, and affirmed her efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, but could deepen exploration of underlying emotions (fear, mistrust) to move empathy from good to exceptional.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the vaccine decision and translated concerns into practical next steps (printable questions, nurse lines, scheduling considerations).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as joint problem-solving, co-created materials, adapted to the patient's preferences, and solicited input at multiple points.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy with explicit statements of choice, multiple options for information delivery, and tools to help the patient assert boundaries at the clinic.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some spontaneous change talk and questions, but relied more on information provision than systematically drawing out the patient's own motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong power-sharing and partnership by offering choices, inviting rehearsal, and treating the patient as an equal collaborator in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generated some change talk through questions and evidence, but frequently returned to didactic information rather than reflecting, amplifying, or consolidating the patient's emerging reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and corrected misinformation nonconfrontationally, but used few MI-specific strategies (double-sided reflections, emphasis of discrepancy) to soften sustain talk and explore tradeoffs.
