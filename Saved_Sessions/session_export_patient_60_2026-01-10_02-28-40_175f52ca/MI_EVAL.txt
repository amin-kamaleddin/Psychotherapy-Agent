# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Jason's feelings in a warm, nonjudgmental tone. Could be enhanced by naming a specific client strength to deepen affirmation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and mirrors both content and affect, showing good perspective-taking. Empathy often stays at a surface paraphrase and would benefit from more complex or amplified reflections to probe deeper feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on the target behavior with strategic sequencing from priorities to concrete steps and rehearsal. Interventions consistently tie back to avoiding a mark and preparing for the meeting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—clinician frames the work as joint, repeatedly asks for Jason's preferences, and co-creates the plan. The clinician avoids an authoritative stance and uses Jason's input to shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice by offering options, framing suggestions as optional, and inviting Jason to select what to practice. Consistently avoids pressuring or directing, reinforcing Jason's control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits tactical motivations and procedural plans (e.g., talking to roommate, pulling report). Would be stronger by drawing out broader intrinsic motivations and linking actions to values or future goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys that expertise lies with Jason and solicits his input, offering options and co‑construction. Occasional clinician endorsements shift voice slightly away from the client; more consistent elicitation of Jason's ideas before proposing options would help.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces preparatory change talk and concrete steps, strengthening responsibility and planning. Misses opportunities to deepen change talk by exploring longer-term reasons and securing explicit commitment timing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and skillfully rolls with sustain talk, redirecting toward control and practical steps. Could further reduce sustain talk using double-sided reflections and more explicit softening language to surface ambivalence.
