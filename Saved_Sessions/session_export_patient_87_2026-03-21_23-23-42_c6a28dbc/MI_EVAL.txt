# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized struggle, and acknowledged effort without judgment, creating warmth and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, sustained reflective listening that captured ambivalence and affect, inviting elaboration and eliciting richer patient disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, behaviorally focused target (three 20‑minute walks) and repeatedly steered toward concrete planning and implementation intentions with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked jointly with the patient to co‑construct solutions, routinely checked preferences, and kept the plan driven by the patient's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasized choice and ownership, offered options rather than directives, used permissive language, and accepted modest, patient‑led commitments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited motivation from the patient's values and past successes with evocative questions, drawing out intrinsic reasons and workable strategies for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a joint endeavor with collaborative language and invitations to choose supports, reinforcing shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk using evocative questions, reflections, scaling, and past‑success probes, producing specific, actionable change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated and acknowledged sustain talk and redirected toward problem‑solving, but tended to repeatedly reflect the barrier and missed some opportunities for double‑sided reflections or reframes.
