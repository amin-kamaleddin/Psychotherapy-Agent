# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing stress and affirming the client while respecting her humor and minimizing language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing ambivalence and concerns; reflections tended to be summary-level and could have more explicitly addressed shame, nervous laughter, and deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on gambling/financial risk and efficiently moved to concrete, value-linked action steps while checking for barriers and contingency plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—co-constructed solutions, drafted messages together, rehearsed language, and consistently sought the client's input and agreement on plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported the client's choice and control by offering neutral options, asking which felt doable, and avoiding pressure or shaming while reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic motivations and tied them to behavior (travel/family goals) using scaling and targeted questions, but could have deepened evocation with more probing follow-ups and confidence/past-success exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear power-sharing and teamwork throughout, inviting the client's preferences and jointly drafting and rehearsing steps with mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk (scaling, translating intentions into a concrete plan) and affirmed steps taken, though could amplify the client's own change language and probe commitment/confidence further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk but did little active work to soften it; missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or exploring gambling's functions to resolve ambivalence more fully.
