# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard (e.g., “I’m here to listen without judgment”), validated Milan’s perspective, and maintained a nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking captured both content and feeling (e.g., pulled into the meeting, social ease), demonstrating high empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session kept a clear focus on alcohol use at social gatherings and moved toward specific, measurable steps (not drinking before school, cutoff time, supportive friend) while staying client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was framed as a joint enterprise: clinician asked permission, offered options rather than directives, invited Milan’s ideas, and positioned themselves as a partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly reinforced choice and avoided pressured language, honoring Milan’s ambivalence and explicitly supporting his right to decide (e.g., asking permission, accepting caveats).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited reasons and capacity for change (preferences, concerns, practical strategies) but could have probed deeper for personally meaningful values to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and invited Milan’s input repeatedly, though could have more explicitly emphasized his expertise and asked him to summarize or co-create the plan earlier to deepen ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited multiple change statements and used a confidence scale, yet missed opportunities to systematically amplify and selectively reflect change talk to convert tentative plans into firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used double-sided moves to acknowledge enjoyment and risks, but could have more explicitly reflected sustain-language to help Milan hear his own minimizations.
