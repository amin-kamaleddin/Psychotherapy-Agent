# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and respect, validating the patient's frustration and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections of symptoms and emotions, validating the patient's fear and exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on obtaining investigations and concrete next steps, using pragmatic planning (timelines, letters, scripts).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively by co‑authoring letters and scripts, soliciting the patient's wording and priorities, and checking which task to do next.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices and respected the patient's preferences, clarifying limits while providing actionable alternatives to preserve control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited elicitation of the patient's internal motivations or values; missed opportunities to explore exceptions and draw out reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a partnership, inviting the patient's input on focus, options, and co‑created materials like drafts and role‑plays.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made minimal efforts to elicit or amplify change talk, largely following the biomedical agenda and not probing evocative moments about improvement or coping.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk but did not actively use double‑sided reflections or strategic reframes to pivot toward exploring coping options.
