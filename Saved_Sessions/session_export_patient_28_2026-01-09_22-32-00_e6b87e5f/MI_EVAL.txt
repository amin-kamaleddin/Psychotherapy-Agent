# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, using affirmations and normalizing language that convey warmth and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, summarizing Dante's situation and validating barriers in a way that matches his ambivalence and emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on increasing physical activity and steadily moves the conversation toward a specific, measurable plan (8:30 Wed walk, backups, reminders, follow-up).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a true co-construction: the clinician elicits Dante's ideas, offers options when appropriate, and repeatedly asks which choice feels best to him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses invitational language, acknowledges Dante's right not to promise, and solicits preferences, reinforcing that the change is his choice and responsibility.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from within by eliciting past success, personal values, and using scaling questions to explore internal reasons and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters a collaborative stance and checks preferences, but at times offers multiple concrete suggestions that shift some planning responsibility toward the clinician rather than first inviting Dante to generate solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reflects, and reinforces change talk with evocative questions and summaries, strengthening and consolidating Dante's emerging commitments and plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges sustain talk without arguing and pivots to practical problem-solving and backups, normalizing ambivalence and reducing barriers to change.
