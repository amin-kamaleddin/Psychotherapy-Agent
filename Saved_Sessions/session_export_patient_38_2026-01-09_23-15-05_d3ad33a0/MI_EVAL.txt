# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, offering unconditional positive regard and normalizing Elena's feelings throughout. No examples of labeling or blame and clinician respects her reluctance while offering support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections of relief and anxiety and useful open questions. Missed opportunities for deeper, more textured empathic exploration of avoidance and the emotional meaning of the scare.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on contraception and immediate harm-reduction steps across the visit. Keeps conversation centered on practical, patient-centered options and avoids tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction feels clearly collaborative, framing suggestions as choices and asking how much help the patient wants. Offers to do tasks only with permission and uses shared problem-solving language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Elena's right to choose, presents multiple voluntary options, and avoids pressure. Provides practical privacy and control strategies that empower her decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation and draws out a concrete, doable first step (keeping EC on hand) and reflects her ambivalence. Could more systematically explore internal reasons, values, and confidence (e.g., scaling or values questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making by inviting preferences and offering choices while providing expertise on request. Could more explicitly highlight Elena's expertise and co-create a clearer follow-up plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change-oriented language and asks an evocative question about doable steps. Would be strengthened by more systematic elicitation of desire/ability/reason/need and targeted commitment questioning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, uses low-pressure alternatives and supportive redirection when Elena resists commitment. Could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence and pivot toward change.
