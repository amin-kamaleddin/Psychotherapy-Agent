# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed clear unconditional positive regard and a consistent nonjudgmental, reassuring tone from the opening line through the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate paraphrases addressing the client's logistical concerns, but missed opportunities to probe deeper emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a tight focus on the target behavior (completing and documenting community service) and advanced practical, actionable steps throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative: the clinician framed options, sought preferences (ZIP, schedule), asked permission, and co-constructed tools with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserved client choice by offering multiple options and asking which tasks to do first, supporting the client's sense of control without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to elicit internal motivation or values—no open questions, scaling, or exploration of what completing hours might mean to the client beyond avoiding trouble.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician invited input and choices, creating a collaborative alliance, but tended to supply solutions rather than explicitly eliciting the client's own problem-solving or ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal evocation of change talk; the session stayed logistics-focused and did not follow up on or amplify the client's sparse change-related statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively neutralized sustain talk by acknowledging it and immediately offering concrete steps, though it lacked more explicit double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
