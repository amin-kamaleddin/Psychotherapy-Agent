### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained clear unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental tone, affirming Sofia’s plan and ambivalence which encouraged disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and clarifying questions demonstrating good perspective-taking, but missed opportunities for deeper emotional or values-based reflections and more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a consistent focus on marijuana use and cutting back throughout the session, leading to specific planning (Wed check-in, friend responses, slip strategies).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like joint problem-solving: clinician solicited preferences, adapted materials to Sofia’s requests, and asked permission before offering suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options, respected Sofia’s choices and limits, and avoided pressuring language, reinforcing client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Sofia’s motives, past successes, and concrete reasons for change, but could have more systematically drawn out intrinsic motives and linked them to long-term values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative and co-creative in planning (Sofia chose the experiment), though the clinician provided many ready-made responses instead of eliciting more of Sofia’s own wording earlier.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited change talk (importance/confidence scaling, concrete experiment) but did not consistently amplify or summarize Sofia’s change statements to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reflected sustain talk without arguing and redirected toward possibilities, yet could use more double-sided reflections and explicit normalization of ambivalence.
