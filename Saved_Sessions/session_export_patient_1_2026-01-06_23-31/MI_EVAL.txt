# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validated the patient's stance multiple times, but warmth sometimes felt formulaic and more clinical than deeply individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently made empathic acknowledgements and brief summaries, but reflections were short and missed opportunities for deeper, affect-focused exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on mini-cigar use, oral/throat risks, and concrete next steps without drifting from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—asked what would help, offered to draft questions and role-play—but the clinician retained substantial directive control with many specific instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported patient choice, offered options and exact language to decline biopsy, and avoided coercion while respecting the patient's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made few efforts to draw out the patient's own motives or values and relied largely on information-giving rather than asking open, evocative questions to elicit change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used inclusive language and co-created some tools, but the interaction remained clinician-driven with repeated medical recommendations rather than centering the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered harm-reduction options and elicited some change language, but rarely used open questions or reflections specifically designed to evoke and amplify the patient's motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged the patient's stance while offering harm-reduction, though repeated risk/statistic delivery risked re-intensifying sustain responses.
