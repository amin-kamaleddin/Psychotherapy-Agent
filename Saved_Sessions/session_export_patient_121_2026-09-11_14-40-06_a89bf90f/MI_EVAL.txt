# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with neutral, nonjudgmental language, normalized Sofia’s experience, and offered options rather than directives.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and recognition of emotional nuance throughout. The clinician mirrored Sofia’s hurt and doubt, which helped her articulate concrete steps.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused agenda on school attendance and coping, with each question advancing concrete planning and avoiding unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session consistently read as a collaboration with repeated invitations to co‑plan and in‑session practice, positioning the clinician as a partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options and explicitly asked Sofia to choose, honored her preferences (e.g., not telling teachers), and reinforced ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation and solutions from Sofia rather than supplying them, eliciting and scaffolding her ideas into a concrete, patient‑owned plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Sofia’s preferences, let her choose the plan, and tailored planning to her responses, demonstrating clear power sharing and mutual decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, asked for implementation details and commitments, and reinforced planning (e.g., timing, cue, number of breaths) to strengthen movement toward action.

**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce sustain talk, but missed some opportunities for double‑sided reflections or brief ambivalence summaries before pivoting to action planning.
