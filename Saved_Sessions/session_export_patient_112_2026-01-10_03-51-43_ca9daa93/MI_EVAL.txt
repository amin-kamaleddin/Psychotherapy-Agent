# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding blame. Validations and open invitations (e.g., asking what Rosa would like to do first) reinforced a nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring Rosa's language about withdrawal, weight, and social connection. Affective matching and checking in on confidence/importance made Rosa feel heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on smoking cessation throughout the session with targeted scaling and concrete implementation planning. The clinician prevented topic drift and linked questions and suggestions directly to the smoking goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner by eliciting Rosa's preferences, offering options, and co-creating the week plan and follow-up. Offers to draft doctor scripts and role-play positioned the clinician as a supportive collaborator rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Rosa's choice and self-determination by offering options, asking permission, and validating concerns rather than pressuring. The clinician honored autonomy while providing structured help and checking readiness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used effective evocation techniques (importance/confidence scales, change-evoking questions) that produced concrete change talk and a plan. Could have spent more time exploring internal motives and values (e.g., what 'rethink everything' meant) before shifting to solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making with repeated invitations for Rosa's input and choice of first steps, co-constructing the plan together. Language and offers (e.g., 'Which would you like to do first?') reinforced collaborative working.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and strengthened change talk using scales and evocative questions, producing concrete commitments (patch, start day, swaps). Missed some opportunities to amplify and deepen Rosa's potent change statements with follow-up probes to build stronger intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively contained and focused sustain talk by asking which worry was most blocking change and validating fears rather than arguing. Would be improved by more double-sided reflections that name both sustain and change sides and then pivot to change to help resolve ambivalence.
