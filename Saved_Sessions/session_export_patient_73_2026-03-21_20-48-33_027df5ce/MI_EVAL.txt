# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalization of ambivalence, avoided blaming language, and framed slips as information rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that showed understanding of Linda’s perspective, but could include more complex affective reflections to capture frustration and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation throughout and repeatedly tied questions and suggestions to concrete, time-limited steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative by inviting preferences, co-creating the plan, offering rehearsal, and agreeing on mutual check-ins and commitments.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering multiple options, checking client preferences, and avoiding pressuring language while affirming the client's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons for change, importance/confidence ratings, and supports, but could probe deeper into core values and translate confidence gains into a concrete micro-plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited input and shared decision-making and generally offered options rather than mandates, though occasionally provided more directive recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Deliberately evoked and reinforced change talk with rulers and affirmations and invited concrete steps, but missed opportunities to amplify specific change statements and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized ambivalence, yet could use more strategic double-sided reflections to explicitly contrast sustain talk with change motives.
