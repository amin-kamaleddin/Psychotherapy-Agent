# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

- [x] Acceptance  
### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5  
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys nonjudgmental acceptance and positive regard (e.g., “You’re not being judged here,” “I’m not judging”), normalizing the patient’s priorities and defensiveness throughout the session.

---

- [x] Empathy  
### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5  
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking; reflections capture content and underlying emotion (e.g., “you’re protecting your place in the group,” “fitting in comes first”), and patient responses indicate feeling understood.

---

- [x] Direction  
### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4  
**Comments:** Maintains focus on alcohol use/safety and elicits protective strategies (alternate drinks, buddy system, not driving). Session could progress more toward a concrete, consolidated plan or commitment.

---

- [x] Collaboration  
### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5  
**Comments:** Interaction framed as joint exploration; clinician repeatedly invites the patient’s input and choice and avoids prescriptive language, allowing patient-generated solutions to drive the conversation.

---

- [x] Autonomy Support  
### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5  
**Comments:** Clinician explicitly honors autonomy (e.g., “You’re not promising…,” “What matters most to you?”), avoids pressuring change, and explores protective options consistent with patient preference.

---

- [x] Evocation  
### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4  
**Comments:** Frequently elicits patient’s reasons and past helpful strategies (desire to avoid trouble, buddy system, bringing drink). Elicitation remains largely preparatory; could more actively amplify change talk toward commitment.

---

- [x] Partnership  
### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5  
**Comments:** Clinician positions patient as expert on his life and repeatedly asks for his perspective and solutions, demonstrating genuine shared decision-making.

---

- [x] Cultivating Change Talk  
### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4  
**Comments:** Elicits preparatory change talk (willingness to “try it sometimes,” strategies that work) and reflects it, but does not consistently follow-up to strengthen commitment or assess confidence for a specific next step.

---

- [x] Softening Sustain Talk  
### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4  
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes it toward safer alternatives; could use more double-sided reflections and strategic amplification of faint change talk to further reduce sustain talk.
