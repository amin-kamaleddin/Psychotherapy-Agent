### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with strengths-based summaries and validation; no shaming or labeling and the clinician accepts Thomas's perspective while offering help.

### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Elicited Thomas's reasons and ideas (family motivation, past success, confidence rating) but at times moved quickly to offer solutions instead of drawing out deeper internal motivations and strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Thomas as a collaborator and expert on his life, co-constructing the plan and inviting tweaks rather than prescribing a unilateral approach.
