# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with multiple affirmations and normalization of slips. Tone is warm and validating with no shaming or confrontational language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good understanding through reflective summaries and acknowledgement of ambivalence and barriers. Could deepen empathic connection with more complex reflections probing emotional nuance about the fall and independence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use, moving from exploration to concrete goals and a one-week trial. Balances open questions with purposeful planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the patient as a partner using inclusive language, integrates her suggestions into the plan, and negotiates practical compromises. Decisions are co-constructed rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly asks permission, offers multiple options, and emphasizes patient choice and ownership. Normalizes slips and frames returning to the plan as the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's ideas and uses importance/confidence scaling to draw out motivation. Could strengthen evocation by exploring intrinsic motivators and linking change to valued goals like independence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a true partnership with frequent permission-seeking, solicitation of preferences, and collaborative plan-making. Power is shared and the patient's ideas clearly shape the outcome.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with scales, specific steps, and commitment to a one-week trial. Missed opportunities include more frequent amplification of change language and probing for stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and gently explores discrepancy while framing suggestions as options, which prevents escalation. Could more often use double-sided reflections or other targeted techniques to directly soften sustain talk.
