# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Jordan’s feelings with multiple validations. The tone remained warm and nonjudgmental throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking that named Jordan’s ambivalence and emotional state. Reflections deepened understanding rather than minimizing concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on getting the flu vaccine and related preparatory actions. Repeated summaries and concrete action items maintained strong directional focus toward vaccination.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by soliciting preferences and co-constructing the plan, script, and checklist with Jordan. Decisions were jointly made rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Jordan’s sense of choice by offering options and inviting his preference for what would be most helpful. Statements respected his agency and avoided pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Jordan’s core motivation (protecting grandparents) and built on it, but then often moved into providing solutions and instructions. Missed some opportunities to draw out additional client-generated reasons, confidence ratings, or a verbal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the exchange as collaborative and invited Jordan to steer the session, including permission to rehearse and co-creating the clinic script. Power and decision-making were shared throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk by translating ambivalence into concrete preparatory behaviors and rehearsing the one-sentence script. Provided actionable steps that built on Jordan’s expressed desire to protect his grandparents.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and frequently redirected toward planning and protections, but used limited MI-specific moves to soften sustain talk. More double-sided reflections or eliciting consequences of maintaining the status quo would strengthen this area.
