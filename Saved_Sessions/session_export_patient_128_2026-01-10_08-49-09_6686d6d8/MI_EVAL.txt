### Acceptance

**Comments:** Conveys respect and nonjudgment throughout, offers choices and honors Tyler's preferences; slightly procedural at times and could use warmer, more individualized affirmations to fully normalize ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and perspective-taking at key moments, but often shifts quickly to practical solutions without deeper reflections of Tyler's mixed emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on the conduct incident and mitigation planning from start to finish, progressing logically through drafting, preparation, and risk-reduction steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages Tyler in shared problem-solving by soliciting preferences and integrating his wording into drafts and rehearsal, creating a strong sense of joint decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Tyler's choice by offering options, checking comfort with wording and actions, and inviting consent rather than imposing directives.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Respects Tyler's perspective and solicits input, but the interaction is often clinician-led and expert-driven rather than co-creating solutions from Tyler's own statements.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and supports some change-oriented actions (course enrollment, counseling) but misses opportunities to amplify Tyler's own motivations and explore his reasons for change more deeply.
