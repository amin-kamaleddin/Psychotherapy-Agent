# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, warm, and supportive stance using normalizing and validating language and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening by summarizing the patient's experience, though some responses were more didactic and missed deeper emotional/sensory reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on task avoidance and building confidence with concrete 5–10 minute behavioral experiments and a logical session flow.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Read as joint problem-solving with repeated preference checks and co-created experiments, and decisions driven by the patient's selections.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Softening Sustain Talk
