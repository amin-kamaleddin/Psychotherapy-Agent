# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating ambivalence and competing needs throughout the session; normalizes the patient's experience without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening with brief reflections of ambivalence and tiredness, but reflections are pragmatic and miss deeper emotional nuance (e.g., FOMO, anxiety).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on social media/phone use, progressing from recognition to concrete experiments and measurable outcomes, and narrows toward an implementable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative with options and solicitation of preferences, though frequent clinician-led suggestions create a slightly more directive rhythm than a fully equal exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's choice by offering voluntary options, avoiding pressure, and reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations, values, and indicators of progress, centering internal reasons for change rather than supplying them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the patient's input and builds the plan from her responses, but repeated similar prompts and many suggested tactics slightly reduce the sense of a fully client-driven agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change talk and specific wins (e.g., three blocks, less tired), but could more consistently amplify, summarize, and probe confidence to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and accommodates sustain talk to reduce defensiveness and preserves valued functions of the apps, but would benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections to highlight ambivalence.
