# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates respect and positive regard with validating language and no judgment about setbacks. Could further normalize slips explicitly to deepen unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases and summaries. Could add more complex emotional reflections or explicit emotion labels to capture mixed feelings more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors and moves the conversation toward concrete implementation and short-term measurable outcomes. Keeps momentum on actionable change rather than drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a team effort using inclusive language and co-designing decisions with the patient. Reflects patient ideas and preferences, creating genuine back-and-forth rather than prescribing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by asking what feels realistic and offering options without pressure. Affirms the patient's plans and respects their chosen accountability and reward methods.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation out with evocative open questions and then reflects and summarizes to strengthen the patient's own reasons for change. Uses patient values and past successes to elicit sustained change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the decision-maker and expert, inviting ownership of the plan and offering options to be chosen by the patient. Turn-taking and language convey genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and reinforces specific, commitment-oriented change talk through open questions and affirmations. Summaries and downstream evocative questions amplify the patient's reasons and ability statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to actionable planning rather than arguing for change. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections that explicitly validate enjoyment of juice while acknowledging costs.
