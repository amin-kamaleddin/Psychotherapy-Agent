# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and reframing slips as information. The clinician's warm, respectful stance contains no shaming or blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening with accurate reflections, summaries, and validation of awkwardness and priorities. Could deepen connection by using more complex, feeling‑laden reflections to capture emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on smoking reduction and moves logically from assessment to a concrete, measurable one‑week plan. Questions and interventions consistently orient to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructs the plan with the patient, invites ratings and preferences, and offers tools with permission while checking comfort. Decision‑making is shared and tailored to Hana's practical concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports patient choice by offering multiple options, asking which she prefers, and normalizing ambivalence. Emphasizes that slips are information and leaves decisions about meds and strategies to Hana.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and preferences through readiness and pros/cons ratings and by prompting elaboration. At times the clinician shifts to offering multiple solutions fairly quickly instead of further amplifying the patient's own change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a genuine collaborative process, asks permission before listing options, and invites the patient to choose which strategies to try. Recommendations are tailored and power is shared throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with open questions about triggers, past attempts, values, and readiness, and affirms the patient's chosen specific goal. Repeated invitations to rate and choose strategies reinforced commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges and names the benefits of smoking when mapping pros/cons and then pivots to workable alternatives without arguing. This approach normalizes sustain talk and redirects focus toward achievable change.
