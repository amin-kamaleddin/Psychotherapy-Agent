# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, names the patient's priorities, and normalizes caution without judgment. Could occasionally acknowledge the difficulty of balancing safety and urgency to deepen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately reflecting concerns and tailoring responses. Could add occasional deeper feeling reflections to capture emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on securing provider confirmation and drafting a portal message, with each turn driving toward that target. Conversation stays on task and yields a concrete patient-ready product.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent co-construction of the portal message with iterative patient input, revisions, and checks for preference. The patient’s ideas substantially shape the final output.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices, asks preferences, and avoids pressuring the patient, reinforcing ownership of decisions and wording. Provides options for formatting and sending to support the patient's control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's intent to seek a second opinion and practical next steps, eliciting specifics and commitment to send a message. Could further evoke internal motives, values, and confidence to strengthen follow-through.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership orientation—asks for preferences, invites edits, and co-authors the message with the patient at each step. Positions the patient as an equal collaborator in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete, action-oriented language and a clear plan to obtain provider confirmation, including drafting pasteable text. Could more systematically amplify change talk by asking about importance, confidence, and explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing ambivalence and redirects toward safety-focused actions like drafting questions and including dose details. Would be strengthened by more double-sided reflections that highlight both caution and readiness to act.
