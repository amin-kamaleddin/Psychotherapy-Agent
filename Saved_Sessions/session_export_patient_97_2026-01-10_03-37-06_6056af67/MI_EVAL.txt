# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive with multiple affirmations and normalization, conveying warm, unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic statements and some reflections, but rarely offers deeper or complex reflections to unpack Mateo’s emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on relapse prevention with concrete, immediate, testable actions and resists topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint effort and invites input and role-play, but frequently supplies detailed instructions rather than eliciting the patient’s own wording or preferences first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports choice by offering options and asking permission to role-play, but occasionally leans toward directive scripts and checklists without always prefacing advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivation and agreement to try tactics but misses opportunities to draw out intrinsic, value-based reasons for change or explore discrepancies with wanting 'a normal life.'

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with co‑constructed plans and invitations for input, though at times the clinician shifts into a more prescriptive stance that reduces shared power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Prompts some ability/commitment language via small steps and tasks, but does not sufficiently evoke or amplify Mateo’s underlying desire, reasons, need, or values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes ambivalence to soften sustain talk and redirects to actionable plans, yet could use more double‑sided reflections or reframing to further shift sustain talk toward change.
