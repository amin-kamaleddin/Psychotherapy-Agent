# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validates frustration, praises small successes, and uses warm, nonpunitive language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring feelings and behaviors and using reflections/summaries to check understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on post-workout crashes, overeating, and social support; questions and reflections consistently drive toward a concrete behavioral experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session reads as a joint enterprise with permission-seeking and inclusive language, but numerous clinician-supplied scripts and tips tilt the work toward clinician-led problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the client’s choice and control by offering options, acknowledging skepticism, and letting the client select the plan and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and highlights past change to build a plan, but does less to deepen intrinsic values, importance/confidence scaling, or personal meaning of goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and invites the client to choose, yet providing many specific suggestions and scripts shifts some control away from co-construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk by highlighting prior successes, asking choice/timing questions, and guiding the client to a specific commitment and plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk constructively and avoids arguing, but misses opportunities for stronger double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of ambivalence.
