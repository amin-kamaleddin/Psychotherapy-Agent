# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard with affirmations, normalizing ambivalence and using nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking, but missed opportunities to deepen emotional exploration around shame and guardedness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on meth-use risk and concrete harm-reduction steps, balancing elicitation with pragmatic guidance toward a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as joint problem-solving, negotiated choices, and co-created a plan based on the patient's preferences and needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the patient's sense of choice, offered options rather than pressure, and validated pacing and personal decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own motivations and practical reasons for safer use, but could probe values and longer-term consequences to draw out stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert in his life, shared power in decision-making, and invited input on next steps and level of contact.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change-oriented language using scaling and options, though could amplify and summarize change talk more to elicit explicit commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and offered harm-reduction alternatives instead of confronting sustain talk, but could use more double-sided reflections to gently highlight discrepancy.
