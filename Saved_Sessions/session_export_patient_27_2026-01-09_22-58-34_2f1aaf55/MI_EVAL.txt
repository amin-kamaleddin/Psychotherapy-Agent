# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence with a warm, nonjudgmental tone; could deepen personalization of affirmations to avoid sounding slightly scripted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking that tracked emotional nuance throughout the session; occasionally could push reflections deeper to explore underlying emotions more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, goal-directed focus on disclosure and safety, moving logically from values to a concrete plan (choose Maya, script, rehearsal); balanced exploration with purposeful steering.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—invited patient preferences, co-constructed the plan, and offered support like role-play while asking before proceeding; demonstrated true joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and provided multiple options without pressure, supporting the patient's right to decide and offering assistance only if wanted.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's motivations and practical reasons for change and helped generate a plan; could more consistently draw out internal motivators and use scaling or commitment questions to deepen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as a respectful partner who deferred to the patient's expertise and preferences, inviting co-creation of steps and offering collaborative supports like rehearsal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change-oriented statements and concrete planning (practicing and a one-on-one), but could amplify and frequency-stack change talk and probe confidence/commitment more strategically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and routinely normalized caution, which softened resistance, but relied mostly on neutral validation rather than more active strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to pivot toward change.
