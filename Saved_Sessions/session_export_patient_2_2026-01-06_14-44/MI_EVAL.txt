# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental throughout the encounter, with frequent validation and explicit honoring of the patient's boundary about alcohol.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening about life stressors and motivations, but empathy was often pragmatic and there were missed opportunities for deeper affective reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with concrete strategies, measurable follow-up, and avoided derailment by the patient's reluctance to discuss alcohol.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was collaborative with co-created checklist and plans and invitations for choice, though the clinician sometimes offered detailed solutions rather than eliciting more of the patient's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by presenting options, recommending GP consultation rather than dictating treatment, and normalizing lapses to reinforce patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Highlighted meaningful reasons for change and reinforced the patient's plan, but did not systematically elicit or deepen the patient's own motivational statements or explore ambivalence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally worked alongside the patient, inviting preferences and integrating her contributions, but at times the interaction became clinician-led through specific recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced existing change talk and tied plans to personally important reasons, yet missed opportunities to use open, strategic questions to amplify desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well by not confronting and by respecting limits about alcohol while redirecting to smoking, though could have used more double-sided reflections and summarizing to soften ambivalence explicitly.
