# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Kieran's feelings throughout. Explicitly honored limits (e.g., respecting his request not to receive more materials).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening that mirrored both affect and content, capturing ambivalence and resignation. Responses validated Kieran's experience without challenging or minimizing it.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, pragmatic focus on turning mandated compliance into job‑focused actions. Routinely steered the session toward concrete steps (search phrases, messages, resume bullets).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem‑solving and repeatedly invited Kieran's preferences. Adjusted the plan to exactly what he asked for, avoiding an expert‑prescriptive tone.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and offered explicit options while honoring his limits. Clearly reinforced client control (e.g., asking which format he preferred and accepting 'don’t send more').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical, instrumental reasons for action and produced actionable responses (job types, agreement to skim/apply). Missed opportunities to probe deeper internal values or use scaling questions to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑created the plan and deferred to Kieran's choices throughout the session. Invited input at each decision point and offered to act only with his consent.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully evoked concrete change language and small commitments (picking target jobs, agreeing to skim/apply). Could have amplified and explored those statements more (e.g., asking what would make a job appealing or affirming his willingness to act).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce dismissive statements, instead offering neutral reflections and low‑effort options. Could strengthen use of double‑sided reflections to gently highlight discrepancy and shift sustain talk toward change.
