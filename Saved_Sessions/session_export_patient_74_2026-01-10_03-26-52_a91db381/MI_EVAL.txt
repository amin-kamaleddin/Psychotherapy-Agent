### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and avoids judgment, using warm, validating language and praising concrete efforts which supports Linda's comfort in exploring ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases and value recognition, though reflections are sometimes repetitive and could deepen emotional access (guilt, fear, relief).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction feels like joint problem-solving with frequent solicitation of Linda's input, building on her examples, and co-constructing plans rather than lecturing or directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Linda's right to choose, offers options rather than pressure, and preserves her control over decisions and timing throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from Linda by eliciting past successes, asking her to choose manageable steps, and inviting her own implementation strategies and indicators of success.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance treating Linda as the expert on her life, repeatedly asking which options feel truest and co-creating the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, links past small successes to current possibilities, and converts motivation into a concrete, measurable two-week experiment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and uses reflective statements to acknowledge sustain talk, but sometimes repeats sustain language verbatim and could use more double-sided reflections to highlight discrepancy.
