# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard and normalizes Amina's experience, offering frequent validation and warmth. No blaming or judgmental language; the supportive tone builds safety from the start.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic reflections and validations help Amina feel heard, including naming overwhelm and anxiety. Some reflections remain surface-level and the clinician moves quickly to solutions instead of exploring deeper affective content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session keeps a clear behavioral focus on sending a bedtime-plan text and rehearsing responses, with each turn advancing concrete action steps. The clinician consistently steers back to planning and contingency strategies without getting sidetracked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative, with the clinician inviting co-construction of messages, offering options, and following Amina's lead. The clinician positions themself as a teammate and makes decisions jointly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is strongly supported through repeated choices, respect for Amina's preferences, and non-pressuring language. The clinician affirms her control and encourages ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective use of importance/confidence scales and eliciting a first step drew out motivation and raised confidence. Evocation could be deeper by asking more open questions to elicit Amina's own wording, values, and intrinsic reasons before offering scripts.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently frames the work as collaborative, asking what Amina wants to focus on and co-creating plans and rehearsals. Power-sharing and repeated preference checks demonstrate strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicits and amplifies change talk with scales and planning, noting increases in confidence and highlighting progress. There were missed opportunities to deepen spontaneous change talk and elicit stronger commitment language tied to values and long-term reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician skillfully redirects potential sustain talk into problem-solving and reinforces non-engagement strategies. More use of double-sided reflections and explicit acknowledgment of ambivalence (both sides) or exploring the father's perspective would further soften sustain talk.
