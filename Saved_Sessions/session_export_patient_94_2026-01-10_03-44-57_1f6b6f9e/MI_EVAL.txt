# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized Maria’s worries, and respected her boundaries without blaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking to show understanding of practical impacts, but missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflection on distrust and anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on inhaler use and translated the conversation into specific, actionable next steps (portal message, reminder, trial options).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by offering concrete tools, co‑constructing wording and timing, and presenting suggestions as modifiable options for Maria.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice by offering options, asking permission before drafting text, and reinforcing Maria’s right to decline daily medication.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some reasons for change (sleep, exercise, family calm) but leaned toward pragmatic problem‑solving and did not consistently probe importance, confidence, or deeper motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Maria as the decision-maker, asked which options sounded better, and offered to paste lines for her to copy rather than taking control.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited brief change statements and benefits but rarely amplified or followed up (no scaling or systematic elicitation of desire, ability, reasons, needs, or commitment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed sustain talk into manageable steps, though could have used more double‑sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both concerns and hints of willingness.
