# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with normalizing statements and nonjudgmental language, validating ambivalence and avoiding labels or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking that capture both sides of ambivalence, checks understanding, and invites elaboration so the patient feels heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking behavior and moves the conversation toward a concrete, time-limited experiment tied to oral health outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—solicits the patient's ideas, validates them, and refines a joint plan framed as a shared experiment rather than an expert-only solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces patient choice and control by framing the plan as an experiment the patient controls, avoiding pressure and honoring tentative commitments.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits patient-generated reasons for change and a doable plan, but could further deepen intrinsic motivation and elicit stronger commitment/confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear power-sharing—asks open questions, invites the patient's choice of steps, and explicitly frames the interaction as working together as equals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk and a specific experiment with some commitment, but misses opportunities to deepen desire/ability/confidence and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reflects sustain talk using double-sided reflections to prevent escalation, though could more proactively shift sustain talk toward change by probing costs and immediately amplifying change statements.
