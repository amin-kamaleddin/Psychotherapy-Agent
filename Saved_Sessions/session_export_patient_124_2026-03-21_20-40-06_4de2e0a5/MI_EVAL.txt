# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard from the first line and maintains a nonjudgmental, validating tone throughout, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding shaming or interruption.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate perspective-taking with timely, complex reflections and summaries that capture ambivalence and affect, deepening mutual understanding and validating the client's concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on marijuana use and moves the conversation from ambivalence to specific, measurable actions and logistics while preserving a client-centered approach.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the session as a joint exploration, co-creating plans (texts, one-liners, supports) and offering help as options rather than directives, preserving an equal, teamwork dynamic.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirms the client's freedom to choose, offers options instead of directives, and supports ownership of the plan by inviting the client's preferences and tweaks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and solutions using open invitations, confidence rulers, and prompts that draw out personally meaningful reasons and supports for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the client's expertise about what will work, inviting input at each step and jointly shaping concrete strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change language through open questions and reflections, then converts that language into concrete steps and supports (e.g., gym plan, texts, coping scripts).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully uses double-sided reflections and avoids reinforcing sustain talk, but misses some opportunities to more strategically pivot to discrepancies or explore longer-term costs to further weaken sustain arguments.
