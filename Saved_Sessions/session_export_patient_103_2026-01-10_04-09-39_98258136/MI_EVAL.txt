# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard through normalization and validation, accepts the patient's stated preferences, and avoids judgmental language throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate paraphrases, emotion labeling, and probing questions that show deep understanding of the patient's concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on managing anger and securing respectful communication, repeatedly steering the conversation toward concrete, achievable actions and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is explicitly collaborative—invites input, co-constructs a script, and checks confidence—treating the patient as a partner in problem-solving rather than as a passive recipient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice and control by offering options, accepting preferences, and avoiding pressuring language, reinforcing patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the patient's own motivations and values with questions about desired outcomes and signs of success, avoiding supplying reasons for change and strengthening intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with invitations to choose and practice, but occasional clinician-led affirmations and directive scaffolding slightly shift the balance away from full patient ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through goal-oriented questions, offering options, and prompting commitment and rehearsal, which produces a clear plan and verbal commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reflects sustain talk without confronting it, using double-sided language to hold ambivalence, but makes limited efforts to reframe doubts or elicit exceptions to weaken sustain talk.
