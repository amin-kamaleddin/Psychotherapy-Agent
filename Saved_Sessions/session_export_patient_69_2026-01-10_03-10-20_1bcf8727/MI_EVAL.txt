### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and normalized Kieran’s feelings, avoiding shaming language and pressure. Validated limited engagement and frustration throughout the session.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflections that captured both content and affect and mirrored the client’s flat, skeptical tone. Reflections encouraged disclosure and conveyed strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on change-relevant behaviors (sleep, phone placement, alarm, buddy check-in) and steadily moved the conversation toward concrete steps. Questions and prompts consistently advanced a practical plan.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly respected Kieran’s choices and skepticism, offering options rather than directives and affirming voluntary, small steps. Reinforced the client’s sense of control over pacing and decisions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the interaction as collaborative, deferring to Kieran’s preferences and jointly drafting plans when invited. Power-sharing was evident in choice of supports and wording.

**Comments:** Elicited concrete change talk with well-placed open questions that produced specific steps (bedtime, phone placement, job follow-up). Missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate that change talk into stronger commitment language.
