### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections of Rosa’s concerns, though could deepen emotional nuance around identity and loss language.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking cessation with concrete, actionable next steps (first-week goal, weekly calls, NRT and reduction plan).

### Collaboration

### Evocation

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (importance/confidence probe, strengths), yet missed opportunities to more frequently reflect and amplify explicit change and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
