# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Samir's fears and efforts. Avoided blaming language and maintained a warm, respectful stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and accurate reflections, acknowledging the frightening cough and practical barriers. At times moved quickly to solutions without deeper exploration of emotional ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on asthma-related targets and tied each turn to concrete next steps (wipes, inhaler pouch, gum, tracking). Balanced safety warnings with pragmatic change options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally used inclusive language and solicited preferences, creating a collaborative feel. However, the clinician often shifted into an instructive mode with multi-item lists instead of co-constructing solutions from Samir's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and agency by offering options, normalizing gradual change, and asking permission before giving information. Never pressured Samir to quit and accepted his decision to try harm reduction first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Samir with evocative questions and requests for commitment, which produced specific, self-directed plans and experiments to try.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a partnership and invited Samir's choices, fostering shared decision-making. Frequent clinician-driven advice and detailed recommendations slightly reduced the sense that Samir's expertise was primary.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively and repeatedly elicited and reinforced change talk, asking for small realistic steps and immediate commitments. This produced concrete commitment language and a follow-up tracking plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and accepted partial change, offering harm-reduction and pragmatic options that respected Samir's stance. These approaches effectively minimized sustain talk and shifted focus to safer behaviors.
