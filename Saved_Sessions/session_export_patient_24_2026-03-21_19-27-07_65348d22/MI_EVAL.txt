# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician used a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone and explicit positive regard (offered choices, normalized concerns and slip-ups) and affirmed the patient's tentative commitments throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and summarizing acknowledged ambivalence and linked alcohol to recovery/sleep, but more complex emotional reflections (e.g., exploring guardedness or deeper values) would strengthen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in relation to marathon training, leading to a behaviorally specific plan and scheduled follow-up with minimal topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—decisions and planning were co-constructed, the clinician solicited the patient's input at every stage, and accepted the patient's suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supported autonomy by offering options, asking which approach the patient preferred, and affirming that decisions and pacing were the patient's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Several evocative strategies elicited the patient's own reasons for change and a concrete test plan, but deeper open questions, scaling, and more frequent reflections could have further consolidated internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned the session as a partnership—offering options, co-creating the two-week test, and inviting patient choice about start day and check-in time.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced multiple change statements through review, pros/cons, and goal-setting, but could have amplified change talk with follow-up questions about importance/confidence and personal values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was handled without reinforcement via neutral pros/cons and compromise strategies, though more strategic techniques (double-sided reflections, selective emphasis) could further reduce sustain talk's pull.
