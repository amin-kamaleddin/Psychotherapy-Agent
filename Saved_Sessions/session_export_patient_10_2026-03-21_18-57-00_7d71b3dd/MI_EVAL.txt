# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, affirming strengths and efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and reflective listening captured both frustration and motivation, with clarifying questions and responses that validated the patient's concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on attending/rescheduling interviews and the childcare barrier, guiding the conversation toward concrete, actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was team-oriented and responsive to the patient's requests, though it could invite the patient to generate more alternatives and jointly summarize the plan for co-ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options and repeatedly asked which the patient preferred, preserving choice and presenting tools as resources the patient could adopt or modify.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reflected existing motivation but relied more on offering solutions than eliciting the patient's own reasons, values, or deeper commitments to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership by inviting choices and refining materials with the patient, but occasionally shifted to expert delivery without explicitly checking permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced the patient's expressed confidence and desire for the job, but did not sufficiently prompt the patient to elaborate her own reasons, values, or specific motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to practical solutions, but could further normalize ambivalence using more double-sided reflections.
