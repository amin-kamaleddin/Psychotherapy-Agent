# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with validation and warmth throughout the session, creating a clearly accepting atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening and accurate reflections, mirroring the patient's language and capturing both cognitive ambivalence and emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on disclosure and practical next steps, steering the conversation toward a concrete, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works jointly with the patient to co-construct the plan, invites input on role-play and responses, and reinforces equal partnership in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and pace by offering options and repeatedly confirming the patient's control over what and when to share.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's internal motivations and past strengths (storytelling, humor) and translates them into actionable steps for disclosure.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert in her life and genuinely shares power by offering options and asking her to choose how to proceed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and a specific plan, but missed opportunities to deepen commitment/confidence (e.g., confidence ruler or explicit commitment statement).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence without reinforcing it, but relies more on reassurance and problem-solving than strategic double-sided reflections or exploring reasons for secrecy.
