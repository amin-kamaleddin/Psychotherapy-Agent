# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—validated, normalized, and reassured the patient throughout the encounter, avoiding labels or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and perspective-taking but missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflection of the patient's fear and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on asthma management and inhaler use, repeatedly steering the session toward actionable next steps and preparation for the appointment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-created the question list, offered options, and engaged in back-and-forth role-play that framed the work as mutual and jointly decided.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by offering options, cautioning against unilateral stopping for safety, and letting the patient select scripts and whether to practice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited useful patient language and practice statements but relied more on providing solutions than on open, evocative questions to draw out personal reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated a shared, client-centered approach—invited the patient to shape content, choose phrasing, and practice collaboratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited concrete change talk via question lists and rehearsal, but could use more open questions to deepen the patient's own reasons and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed concerns into practical harm-reduction steps, though used few double-sided reflections and sometimes moved quickly to information provision.
