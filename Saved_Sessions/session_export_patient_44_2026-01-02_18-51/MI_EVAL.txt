# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5  (4/5)  
**Comments:** Consistent respect and positive regard (multiple affirmations, praise for progress). At times praise felt formulaic and normalization of setbacks could be more exploratory.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☒3 ☐4 ☐5  (3/5)  
**Comments:** Acknowledges frustration, comparisons, and benefits (reduced pain) but reflections are brief and often immediately followed by advice rather than deeper feeling-focused reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☒5  (5/5)  
**Comments:** Strong, consistent focus on behavioral targets with concrete, specific directives (nonnegotiable gym days, packing bag, alarm, 8:00 check-in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5  (4/5)  
**Comments:** Involves patient in choosing elements (time, snack, text vs call). Clinician often takes lead by offering multiple specific recommendations, which slightly reduces equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5  (4/5)  
**Comments:** Acknowledges choices and offers alternatives (walk vs gym, snack options), asks preferences. Some prescriptive language (“Commit here”) could undermine autonomy if overused.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☒3 ☐4 ☐5  (3/5)  
**Comments:** Elicits triggers and a doable goal but relies heavily on clinician-generated solutions; limited elicitation of Erik’s deeper reasons, values, or motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5  (4/5)  
**Comments:** Frames work as joint (offers reminders, check-ins, adapts to preferences). Partnership reduced somewhat by clinician organizing the plan and logistics repeatedly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☒3 ☐4 ☐5  (3/5)  
**Comments:** Reinforces existing change talk (acknowledges improvements, praises planning) and sets accountability, but does not sufficiently amplify or elicit further desire/ability/importance/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☒3 ☐4 ☐5  (3/5)  
**Comments:** Normalizes sustain-talk (frustration, comparisons) but frequently moves quickly to reframing/solutions instead of using double-sided reflections or exploring ambivalence more fully.
