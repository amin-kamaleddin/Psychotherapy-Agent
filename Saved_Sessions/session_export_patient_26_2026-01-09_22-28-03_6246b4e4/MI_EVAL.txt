### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Daniela’s experience, avoiding blame or minimization. Praise and validation are balanced and keep the client feeling respected throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening capture practical constraints and distress, with accurate summaries of load and emotions. Reflections tend to stay at the surface, so deeper emotional mirroring (fear, exhaustion, trust issues) could be strengthened.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintains a clear focus on stabilizing childcare and concrete next steps, narrowing options and linking suggestions to Daniela’s goals. Conversation stays action-oriented and time-limited toward achievable tasks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session is a clear partnership with co-produced artifacts (texts, replies, scripts) and iterative edits based on Daniela’s input. The clinician balances expertise with client choice and incorporates her preferences throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly supported by offering choices, asking what feels most helpful, and inviting customization of scripts. The clinician offers help only when requested, reinforcing client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits Daniela’s motivations and preferences, drawing out commitment-oriented responses about childcare and next steps. At times the clinician moves quickly to solutions instead of pausing to elicit deeper internal motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction exemplifies shared planning and decision-making, with the clinician deferring to Daniela’s choices and co-creating practical supports. Power is balanced and solutions are shaped by the client’s ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicits and reinforces Daniela’s change language about childcare and cutting back on smoking/drinking, and offers concrete supports. Opportunities remain to amplify change talk (ask about past successes, use scaling to deepen commitment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician validates sustain talk around cost and trust but does limited strategic work to soften it (few double-sided reflections or reframes). Proposing solutions quickly sometimes bypasses opportunities to resolve ambivalence more thoroughly.
