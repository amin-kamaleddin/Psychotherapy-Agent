# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, using affirmations and normalizing language and treating slips as data rather than failures.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries of events and context, but could deepen emotional reflections to capture frustration, guardedness, or the meaning of sluggishness for training.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and related goals throughout the session, moving logically from assessment to options to concrete planning and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was cooperative problem‑solving with frequent invitations for input, responsive tailoring of recommendations, and shared decision‑making on methods and goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing slips as information, reinforcing the client’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some client motivations and plans (surprise at weekly total, willingness to track), but could use more open, evocative questions and scaling to draw out deeper reasons and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership and power‑sharing by asking preferences, letting the client choose methods and goals, and co‑creating the plan and check‑in.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created multiple opportunities and reinforced change language with concrete strategies and goal-setting, though could more strategically elicit and amplify specific desire/importance/confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized skepticism while focusing on data collection, but occasionally shifted to normative guidance and could better use double‑sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk.
