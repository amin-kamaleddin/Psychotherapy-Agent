### Partnership

**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions, choices, and summaries that led to concrete intentions and plans. Could more deliberately amplify Naomi's reasons and confidence (e.g., ask 'why' and use confidence rulers) to deepen change talk.
