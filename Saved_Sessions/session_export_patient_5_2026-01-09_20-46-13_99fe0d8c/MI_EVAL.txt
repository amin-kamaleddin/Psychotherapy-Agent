# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, repeatedly validates the patient's concerns and priorities, and accommodates requests (written plan, format preferences).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening with validations, but reflections are mostly informational and there are few affective reflections exploring the emotional weight of the decision.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, sustained focus on safely switching from phenelzine to citalopram with concrete washout timing, dosing, monitoring, and next steps; conversation stays on target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is framed as a team effort—creates shareable materials, adapts to patient requests (fields, format), and coordinates roles for psychiatrist/PCP/pharmacist as allies in the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors patient choice about oversight, format, and timing, refrains from pressuring, and explicitly supports the patient's decision to wait for a signed plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivations, concerns, and practical needs effectively but focuses more on expert information and logistics than on deepening the patient's internal motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—asks preferences and offers to co-create materials—but at times provides expert direction without fully inviting the patient to shape clinical recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change-oriented language with concrete, manageable steps and a draft plan, but misses opportunities to amplify emerging change talk or use scaling and commitment questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids reinforcing reasons not to change, honors the patient's caution, and provides requested supports (draft plan, monitoring) that diffuse sustain talk without pressure.
