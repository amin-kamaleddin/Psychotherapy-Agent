# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental respect. Validated ambivalence and supported the patient's low-stakes experiment without pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, matching the patient's language and emotional stance. Normalized ambivalence and acknowledged the patient's skepticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and moved the session toward a measurable 1-week experiment. Kept conversation oriented to practical, trackable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan by soliciting preferences and adapting recommendations to the patient's concerns. Jointly decided gum strength, start day, and the logging format.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options and explicitly invited the patient to choose, framing the plan as low-stakes. Reinforced patient control over decisions and follow-up.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited a concrete commitment to try nicotine gum and tracking, drawing some motivation from workplace concerns. Could have probed more for personal reasons, values, and used scaling questions to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as shared problem-solving and honored the patient's input throughout. Repeatedly invited collaboration and checked preferences before finalizing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change-oriented language, resulting in a clear plan to try gum. Missed opportunities to amplify tentative change statements and to explicitly elicit desire/reason/need/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk neutrally and avoided arguing or reinforcing reasons to keep smoking. Could have used more double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to pull ambivalence toward change.
