# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveyed unconditional positive regard from the start and used validating, nonjudgmental language throughout, avoiding blame or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and reflective listening were evident through accurate paraphrases and summaries that showed clear understanding of Laura's feelings and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on the target behavior (short morning routine) with repeated returns to concrete steps like scheduling, alarms, backups, and accountability.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly co‑constructed: the clinician offered options and consistently invited Laura to choose, tweak, and prioritize supports and steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly affirmed Laura's ownership and choices, avoided pressure, and offered options while checking her preferences (e.g., timing of check‑in).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and reasons were elicited from Laura (values, expected benefits, confidence scaling) and used to shape a concrete, self‑generated plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a collaborative stance, inviting Laura's input on specific steps and supports and negotiating follow‑up as joint work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited desire and reasons and secured specific commitments, though could more often amplify and stack Laura's change statements to deepen and consolidate commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and normalized without reinforcement, then skillfully redirected to planning, backups, and compassionate fallback language.
