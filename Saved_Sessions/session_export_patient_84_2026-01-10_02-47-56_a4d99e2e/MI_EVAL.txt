# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental, validated Malik’s feelings and normalized harm-reduction; avoided blaming language and conveyed unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking that helped Malik feel heard, but many reflections remained surface-level with limited exploration of deeper emotions or adolescent values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on cannabis use, safety, and behavior-change strategies, using role-play and a concrete small commitment to keep the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a true collaboration—co-created language and plans with Malik, invited his choices, and let his responses guide pace and content rather than adopting an authoritative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Malik’s sense of choice by offering options, avoiding pressure, and framing the plan as a small voluntary experiment he controlled.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of Malik’s perspective and minimal self-motivated language occurred, but the clinician often moved quickly to tools instead of using open, curiosity-driven questions to draw out intrinsic reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Malik as a collaborator by asking preferences, inviting options, co-writing lines, and asking permission to role-play, sharing power throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created opportunities for change talk and helped convert brief statements into a specific plan (try a line once this week), though could have more directly amplified Malik’s own concerns and probed importance/confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and rolled with resistance, offering neutral harm-reduction language, but could have used more strategic double-sided reflections to gently highlight discrepancy and pivot sustain talk toward change.
