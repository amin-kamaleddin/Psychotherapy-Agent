# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm with frequent validating reflections that normalize ambivalence and support the patient's pace.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate paraphrase of concerns, but reflections are often surface-level or repetitive and could probe deeper feelings and meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on smoking and protecting the child, linking questions and reflections to concrete, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicits the patient's ideas and integrates them into the plan, using inclusive language and shared decision-making throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, avoids pressure, and repeatedly affirms the patient's right to choose pace and strategy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivations and patient-generated solutions, but misses opportunities to deepen and sequence those reasons toward commitment (e.g., scaling or summarized strengths).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert and frames the work as collaborative, co-creating implementation details together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reflects clear change talk and benefits, yet does not consistently amplify or summarize change language to build toward specific commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and skillfully redirects sustain talk toward protective steps, though could use double-sided reflections or explicit summaries to further reduce sustain talk.
