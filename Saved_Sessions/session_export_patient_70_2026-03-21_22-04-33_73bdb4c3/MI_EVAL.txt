# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect through normalization, affirmations, and nonjudgmental language, preserving the client’s dignity and safety to disclose.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and timely, specific reflections that captured both content and affect, validating the client's emotions and experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the behavioral goals tied to re-entry risk and steered the session toward concrete, time‑bound actions (forms, rehearsal, alarm, text) with no drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a genuine team effort using inclusive language, shared decision‑making, and frequent invitations to the client to choose and shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offered options and asked the client's preference to preserve choice, though occasional phrasing (e.g., 'I’ll push you') could be reframed as an offered option to fully support autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from the client with questions about values and preferred steps, eliciting self‑endorsed reasons and concrete change plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively partnered with the client by asking permission, offering options, and co‑constructing the plan, demonstrating genuine power‑sharing throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited meaningful change talk and concrete commitments (bringing forms, rehearsing, alarm), but could more often amplify and probe for stronger, explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain themes and redirected to practical solutions, yet used fewer techniques like double‑sided reflections or reframing and occasionally risked mild pressure when offering to 'push.'
