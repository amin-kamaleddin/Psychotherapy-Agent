# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing Julian's situation and accepting his brief, guarded responses. The warm, practical tone supported a strong sense of acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking captured content and some affect (relief, nervousness). Could probe more for emotional nuance and ambivalence beneath Julian's guardedness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on completing court-ordered community service with specific, time-bound actions. Each turn reinforced the target behavior without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with joint brainstorming and co-constructed decisions, adjusting to Julian's preferences. The clinician avoided an authoritarian stance and shared power in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized Julian's choice and asked which options felt doable, offering help only after asking his preference. Supportive language affirmed autonomy without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Julian's motivations and supports (relief, deadlines, reminders) and asked targeted questions to draw out reasons. Could have probed deeper into personally meaningful reasons and translated them into stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Julian as an equal collaborator, offering options and inviting his input at each decision point. Power-sharing was evident in drafting scripts and selecting the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, reinforced relief and practical steps, and shaped a concrete first action (call parks office tonight). Missed opportunities to elicit stronger commitment or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk but offered limited active strategies to soften it, mainly redirecting to logistics. Could use double-sided reflections and gentle reframing to explore and diminish Julian's doubts.
