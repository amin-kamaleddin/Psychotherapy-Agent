# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with multiple validations and nonjudgmental language, affirming the client's dignity and efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflective listening and perspective-taking are strong and capture ambivalence, but reflections are sometimes repetitive and the clinician missed a few opportunities to deepen emotional exploration (shame, fear, pride).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, behavioral focus on reducing drinking and a time-limited trial living arrangement, using purposeful questions that move toward concrete, measurable steps and documentation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly a joint enterprise: the clinician invites co-creation, checks preferences, and solicits the client's exact wording, preserving client decision-making while offering professional support.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes the client's choice and control (e.g., 48-hour exit clause, asking which clauses to prioritize), resisting pressure and facilitating client ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively links behavior to core values and elicits self-generated indicators of progress, but could use more evocative questions and commitment/confidence probes to surface deeper intrinsic motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership by sharing power, asking for the client's preferences, and inviting joint drafting of the agreement, fostering co-creation and mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and translates values into measurable items, though the clinician could more consistently amplify and summarize change statements and probe confidence/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with double-sided reflections and redirects to trial solutions without arguing, but would benefit from more strategic softening techniques (amplified reflections, exploring costs of status quo).
