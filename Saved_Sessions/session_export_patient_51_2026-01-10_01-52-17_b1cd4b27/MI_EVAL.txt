# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates respect and a nonjudgmental stance, validates the patient's plan, and uses polite, supportive language throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some supportive language and appropriate safety checking are present, but reflective listening and emotional exploration are limited and the patient's hurried affect is rarely validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on medication safety with accurate guidance on contraindication, washout timing, symptom monitoring, and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint problem-solving and responds to the patient's request, but largely supplies information rather than co-creating a stepwise plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient autonomy by advising to consult the prescriber, offering options, and phrasing suggestions as choices to preserve decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Primarily provides expert information and safety instructions with minimal elicitation of the patient's motivations, reasons, values, or confidence to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance (asks focus, offers rehearsal) and invites follow-up, though the exchange remains somewhat clinician-led with limited elicitation of the patient's views.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces the patient's change-oriented statements and provides rehearsal, but rarely asks questions or uses reflections to elicit or amplify the patient's own motivations or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing reasons to delay and redirects toward safety and action, which prevents sustain talk from taking hold, though explicit strategies like double-sided reflections are limited.
