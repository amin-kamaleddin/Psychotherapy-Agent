# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows clear, nonjudgmental acceptance throughout, normalizing Maya's perspective and acknowledging pressures without shaming. Positive regard and respect for her pace support engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking capture ambivalence and concern about college and grades. Could deepen empathy with more emotional and rolling reflections (e.g., awkwardness, worry about exclusion).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays focused on alcohol use and its impact and moves logically from exploration to a concrete plan with options and role-play. The clinician consistently guides toward specific, doable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction feels collaborative with invitations to choose and rehearse strategies, and adjustments based on Maya’s responses. At times the clinician supplies many scripted suggestions instead of eliciting Maya’s own wording first, which reduces mutual shaping.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission for brainstorming/role-plays, and framing steps as small experiments. This reinforces Maya’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicits Maya’s motivations and ideas (weeknight rule, concerns about friends) and reinforces them. Increasing use of evocative and scaling questions would further deepen and mobilize internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician frequently invites collaboration and respects Maya’s expertise about what will work. However, offering many ready-made lines and tactics sometimes shifts toward expert direction rather than co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits and affirms concrete change language (weeknight rule, limits) and amplifies it through affirmation and brainstorming. Further exploration of reasons, confidence, and envisioned outcomes would lengthen and deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician acknowledges the positives of drinking and redirects to alternatives and experiments without confronting sustain talk. More use of double-sided reflections explicitly balancing the pros and cons would better soften sustain talk.
