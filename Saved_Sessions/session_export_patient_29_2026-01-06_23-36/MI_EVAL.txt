# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates the patient's request, offering options and normalizing help-seeking; lost points because affirmations feel clinical rather than warm and individualized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges stressors and mood history and makes validating statements, but empathic listening is brief and often immediately followed by information-giving rather than deeper reflections of feelings or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and medication strategy, steering the session toward concrete tasks like quit planning, dosing, and mood monitoring.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites patient input (quit plan/scripts) and offers coordination with PCP/spouse and tailored recommendations, yet frequent expert-driven technical details limit step-by-step co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Presents multiple reasonable options and affirms patient choice, reducing pressure; could improve with more explicit statements reinforcing the patient's right to decide and more permission-seeking before advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Relies mostly on providing information and responding to requests rather than eliciting the patient's internal motivations, values, or confidence to generate change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Displays collaborative moves like offering choices and involving supports, but frequently defaults to an expert/informational stance without consistently eliciting the patient's perspective before recommending a plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change behaviors and responds supportively to readiness, but does not actively draw out, amplify, or deepen the patient's own desire, reasons, ability, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers and offers practical strategies that prevent reinforcement of sustain talk, but mostly counters with advice and information rather than using MI techniques like double-sided reflections or reflective ambivalence work.
