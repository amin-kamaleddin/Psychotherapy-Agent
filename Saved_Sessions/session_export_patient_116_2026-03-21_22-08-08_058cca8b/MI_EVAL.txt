# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, affirms the patient's strengths, normalizes ambivalence, and honors boundaries throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with useful summaries, but missed opportunities to probe deeper emotion behind 'not myself' and to use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the conversation tightly focused on medication adherence and moves toward a clear, concrete plan with specific logistics and timeline.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-creates the monitoring plan, repeatedly solicits the patient's preferences, and uses invitational language to share decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by offering options, deferring action until patient consent, and explicitly reinforcing the patient's control over involvement and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's reasons and criteria for change and shapes the plan around those values, but could probe deeper for intrinsic motivators and use confidence/importance rulers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates equal partnership through shared planning, honoring requests about communications, and inviting the patient to guide implementation details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits and amplifies tentative change language into a concrete two-week trial and tracking plan, but did not explicitly elicit strength, confidence, or a commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and skillfully reframes sustain talk, offering neutral reflections and practical options that defuse resistance while preserving autonomy.
