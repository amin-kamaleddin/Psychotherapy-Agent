# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through reflections, affirmations, and explicit belief in the patient's ability; no minimizing or critical language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and attitude to foster rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused direction on reducing alcohol to impact blood pressure, with questions and reflections tied to the target behavior and measurable outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—inviting patient input, summarizing choices, and building the plan together rather than delivering clinician-led instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the patient's sense of choice and control by offering options, honoring preferences, and framing the trial as the patient's experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out concrete, practical motivations and measurable commitment (two dry nights, BP readings), but could have probed deeper into broader personal values or confidence/importance ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert in his life, offered choices, and co-created decisions (days, substitutes, BP readings), demonstrating genuine power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk by asking for specific steps and benefits, reflecting and affirming the patient's plan and willingness to experiment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated skepticism without reinforcing it, then skillfully redirected to low-burden experiments and concrete actions to reduce sustain talk's influence.
