# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect and positive regard with warm, validating language and normalizing the patient's "not perfect" stance. Repeated affirmations supported an unconditional positive regard stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking by accurately mirroring the patient's language and summarizing values and concerns. Empathic checks and reflections showed high attunement to the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing drinking on school nights and progressively guided the conversation toward a concrete two-week experiment. Questions and reflections consistently moved the plan-building forward with minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with the clinician inviting input and co-constructing the plan rather than prescribing solutions. Open questions, reflections, and inclusive language reinforced mutual contribution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the patient's choice and emphasized voluntariness by framing options and inviting the patient's selection. The clinician accepted the patient's "not perfect" caveat and reinforced ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicited the patient's own motivations, values, and past strategies (planner, routines) rather than supplying reasons for change. Focused questions drew out envisioned benefits and concrete internal motivators.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative interaction using inclusive language and invitations to choose among options, sharing power in decision-making. The clinician elicited the patient's expertise about feasible supports and implementation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk with targeted prompts about desired outcomes and indicators of success. Reflections and reinforcement helped the patient articulate practical steps and reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected to change-oriented language, validating the patient's realism. Could improve by using more explicit double-sided reflections when the patient minimizes change to acknowledge ambivalence while leaning toward change.
