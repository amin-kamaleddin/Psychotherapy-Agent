# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating; normalizes ambivalence and affirms the patient's priorities and practical thinking.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that helps the patient feel heard; could deepen affective nuance by explicitly reflecting anxiety, embarrassment, or fear of rejection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral target and keeps the session focused on actionable next steps; moves effectively from exploration to concrete planning and rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as a true partnership with co-constructed decisions, options offered rather than directives, and frequent checks of the patient's preferences and lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options and respecting the patient's preferences; affirms permission to bail and avoids pressuring compliance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits doable steps, implementation intentions, and some commitment language; could further probe personal values, confidence, and past successes to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—asks for the patient's preference, co-creates the plan, and invites rehearsal—demonstrating shared power and respect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with specific, measurable plans and commitment steps; missed opportunities to amplify and summarize the patient's change language and link it to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and reframes concerns into practical experiments and safety nets; would benefit from more double-sided reflections and explicit normalizing of peer pressure to resolve ambivalence.
