# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with a nonjudgmental tone, validating the client's priorities and offering explicit praise.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and feelings like being drained and protective of team/grades.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on vaping and the client's concrete goal, guiding the conversation toward specific, actionable steps without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters an equal partnership by soliciting the client's ideas, checking feasibility, and integrating the client's language into co‑constructed plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms the client's choice, offers options rather than directives, and elicits preferences to strengthen ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's internal motivations and strengths, linking prior successes and values (team/grades) to current change plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as collaborative, inviting the client's expertise and letting the client choose which steps to start with.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with open questions, reflections, and follow‑ups that deepen desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses double‑sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk, but misses some opportunities to explore minimizations and deepen discrepancy.
