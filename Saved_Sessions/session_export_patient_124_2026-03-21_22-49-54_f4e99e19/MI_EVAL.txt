# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, avoiding judgment or moralizing language throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections and summaries that synthesize the patient's emotional and practical concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on marijuana use and concrete change options, keeping momentum toward a small, testable behavioral step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving by soliciting the patient's preferences and co-constructing a feasible plan that is shaped by the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the patient's sense of choice and frames steps as experiments, explicitly accepting ambivalence and avoiding pressure to commit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motives, values, and past successes but could more actively deepen and amplify the patient's change talk and elicit confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance that invites the patient's input and choices, demonstrating power-sharing rather than directive advice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and builds change talk through past success and values-oriented questions, but misses opportunities to cluster, amplify, and elicit stronger commitment language or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk with acceptance and redirection toward strategies, though could further soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections and strategic reframing to increase shift toward change.
