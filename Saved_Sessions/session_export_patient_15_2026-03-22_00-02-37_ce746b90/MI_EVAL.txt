# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the patient's experience with a nonjudgmental, supportive tone that created psychological safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, validating the patient's values (family-first) and feelings throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, practical focus and moved to a specific walking plan, but did not address the other stated target (alcohol) or link it to blood pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in true collaborative problem-solving by soliciting input, reflecting back the patient's ideas, and co-creating a realistic action plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice by offering options, avoiding pressure, and emphasizing that the plan should fit his life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out intrinsic motives and past-success strategies for walking but did not elicit motivation or ambivalence about alcohol or probe confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly framed the interaction as collaborative, deferred to the patient's expertise, and repeatedly invited preferences to share decision-making power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified change talk about physical activity and values effectively, but missed opportunities to cultivate change talk regarding alcohol use.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used neutral reframing, yet missed chances to gently explore and soften minimization around alcohol specifically.
