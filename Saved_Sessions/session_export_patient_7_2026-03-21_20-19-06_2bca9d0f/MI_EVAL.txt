# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, honoring the client's privacy and limits throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflections and attunement to both content and affect, validating the client's guardedness and relief from music.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on engagement and a brief coping strategy, though progressive agenda-setting toward future steps could have been slightly stronger.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Actively co‑constructed the session with the client by offering choices, following the client's lead, and summarizing a jointly created plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and honored the client's decisions, offering options and avoiding coercive or prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as a shared endeavor, affirming the client's expertise and leaving decisions to the client throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively amplified small pieces of change talk and secured a one‑sentence plan, though additional exploration of personal reasons or values could deepen motivation.
