# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a consistently respectful, nonjudgmental, and validating tone, normalizing setbacks and affirming Camila without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and validations were accurate and frequent, but the clinician could have probed more deeply into the emotional meaning and context of Camila's low energy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with a specific, measurable plan, cues, contingencies, and a concrete follow-up check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session read as a shared enterprise with negotiated reminders and check-ins, but frequent ready-made scripts reduced opportunities for Camila to generate solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Camila's choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and emphasizing that she can stop or tweak the plan as needed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk was elicited and a commitment requested, but the clinician relied on structured strategies and missed chances to draw out Camila's deeper reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative with joint goal-setting and negotiation, though the clinician's provision of templates and scripts made the process somewhat clinician-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced concrete commitment language and planning, but often offered solutions instead of eliciting Camila's own motives and phrasing.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged, normalized, and reframed with flexible alternatives, but the clinician moved quickly to problem-solving rather than using more double-sided reflections to explore ambivalence.
