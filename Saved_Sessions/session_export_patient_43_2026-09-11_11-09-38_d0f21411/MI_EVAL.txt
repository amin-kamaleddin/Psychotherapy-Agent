# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental—uses normalizing reflections and affirms the patient’s pace and choices, demonstrating unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and accurate reflections that mirror the patient’s stance, though many reflections are summary-level and could probe deeper emotions or meanings behind statements like “I feel fine.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking and a narrowly defined, testable change; questions and planning consistently tie back to the experiment and measurable outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session functions as team problem-solving with the patient shaping much of the plan, but the clinician frequently offers structured options that subtly steer the interaction rather than inviting broader patient-generated agenda-setting.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors patient choice and pace, emphasizes voluntariness, offers options rather than directives, and affirms that small tests are the patient’s to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical, behavior-linked motivations and criteria for success, but tends to focus on logistics rather than probing deeper values, importance, or confidence to bolster intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative with frequent invitations for the patient to choose plan elements, yet the clinician’s frequent structured prompts and options limit more explicit power-sharing and patient-led agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies concrete change talk and invites small experiments, but could further deepen emotionally resonant reasons and stronger commitment language beyond logistical planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and pivots to manageable experiments, reducing resistance; could strengthen this by using more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to resolve ambivalence.
