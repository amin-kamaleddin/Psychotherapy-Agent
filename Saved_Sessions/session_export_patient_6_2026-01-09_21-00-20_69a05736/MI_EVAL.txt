# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing ambivalence and acknowledging values; language felt somewhat formulaic and repetitive at times, with missed chances to name strengths more specifically.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and accurate reflections that helped Alexei open up; reflections were often summary-level and could deepen emotional meaning with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and its link to soccer/scholarship, moving from values to concrete action steps and readiness assessment; questions progressively narrowed toward specific, achievable targets.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was genuinely collaborative with frequent invitations for Alexei's input and joint refinement of plans (text, ally, role-play); clinician positioned suggestions as options rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Alexei's choice and offered options, asking what felt most likely and respecting his control over decisions; consistent emphasis on ownership fostered autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Alexei by linking alcohol to his own goals and eliciting self-generated reasons and commitments, resulting in a concrete plan and readiness rating; clinician avoided supplying reasons or advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Alexei as the expert and co-created the plan through open invitations and power-sharing, with the session evolving from his responses; clear mutual decision-making evident throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk, asking for specifics that led to concrete commitments (ally, text, not drinking before tryouts); clinician built on and strengthened Alexei's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and redirected toward protective goals, linking minimization to concrete consequences; could further soften sustain talk using strategic double-sided reflections or explicit contrast to elicit more discrepancy.
