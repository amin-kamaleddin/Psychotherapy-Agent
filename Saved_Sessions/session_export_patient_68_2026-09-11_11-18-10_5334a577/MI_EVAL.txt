**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint exploration and defers to Kevin's expertise, asking which options feel doable. Frequent invitations to choose and shared decision-making demonstrate strong power-sharing.

**Comments:** Elicits and strengthens change talk with small-step options and repeated encouragement of a one-night experiment. Could more strongly amplify and reflect change language and use rulers to deepen desire, ability, and commitment.
