### Acceptance

- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language; repeatedly asked permission and honored autonomy (e.g., “Would it be okay if we talked…?”, “Would you prefer a printed copy or an emailed PDF?”). ### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking (e.g., “It sounds like wine with meals connects you to family…yet you also really want a healthy baby.”). Validated both sustain- and change-talk but did not probe or reflect deeper underlying emotions (anxiety/guilt) — missed opportunity for a more complex reflection.

### Direction

- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol in pregnancy and concrete next steps (one‑page sheet, remove alcohol, partner involvement, one‑week check‑in). Presented options and steered toward actionable choices without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Positioned exchange as joint exploration: asked permission to share, offered format/timing/partner choices, and incorporated Isabella’s preferences into the plan (sheet content, partner on call, trial period).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Explicitly reinforced autonomy throughout (“It’s your call,” “I respect your choice…”), avoided pressuring language, and left final decisions with the patient while offering support and options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Elicited Isabella’s reasons and plans (asked what drinking means to her; “Which option feels most doable?”), producing change-talk (willingness to trial). Could have further evoked and sequenced change talk (e.g., ask “What makes that important?” or confidence scaling).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Treated patient as expert on her life and co‑created the plan (content of sheet, partner role). Decisions were negotiated and reflected Isabella’s preferences (printed sheet, reminders, who joins call).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced explicit change language (importance of a healthy baby; agreement to try a trial and remove wine). Did not consistently amplify or build momentum into an explicit commitment or confidence rating — missed opportunity for scaling commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Criterion observed
**Comments:** Avoided arguing or reinforcing sustain-talk (acknowledged tradition, skepticism) and provided neutral factual information (“no one has proven a safe amount”). Could have used double-sided reflections to more actively tip toward change.
