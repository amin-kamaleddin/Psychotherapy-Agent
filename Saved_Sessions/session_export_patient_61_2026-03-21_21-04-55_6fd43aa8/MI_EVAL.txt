# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through validations and normalization from the opening onward, avoiding judgment and affirming the patient's effort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking that captures concrete barriers and the patient's values, though deeper reflection of emotion and the burden would further strengthen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on diabetes self-management with a specific, measurable goal and repeatedly brings the conversation back to doable behavior change and concrete steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as a joint exploration, co-constructing decisions (nights, scripts, backups) and consistently soliciting the patient's input and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices, asks permission before advice, and emphasizes low-pressure experimentation, reinforcing the patient's right to accept or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own reasons and motivations (family-focused value and importance/confidence ratings) but could better amplify those reasons and draw on past successes to deepen discrepancy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a strong collaborative stance by asking permission, brainstorming together, and offering to draft scripts, which positions patient and clinician as partners.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk using choice and scaling, and generates small achievable steps, though there were missed opportunities to amplify and expand the patient's change statements and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by validating ambivalence and offering low-pressure alternatives and backups, but could more often use double-sided reflections to further soften sustain talk.
