# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalized relapse, and affirmed the patient’s efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good empathic listening with accurate reflections and matched affect, but reflections were often pragmatic and missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of helplessness and shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on relapse and immediate safety with concrete harm‑reduction steps, though the plan emphasized short‑term tasks without a stronger move toward treatment engagement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a team‑oriented approach by inviting preferences, offering to stay and coach, and co‑constructing immediate steps rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking permission, emphasizing choice, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some tentative motivation via small‑step questions and reinforcement, but did not systematically use open, evocative prompts to draw out personal reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and solicited the patient's input repeatedly, though the session remained slightly clinician‑structured with limited co‑creation of longer‑term plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change talk (e.g., pill removal, breathing) but did not consistently amplify or explore desire, reasons, past successes, or elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and redirected to normalization and achievable risk‑reduction steps effectively, but missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections to further soften ambivalence.
