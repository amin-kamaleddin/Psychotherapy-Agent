# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating with multiple normalizing statements; affirms concerns and boundaries throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening, mirrors language, and validates anxiety, but reflections are pragmatic and miss deeper affective exploration of underlying feelings or personal meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the decision about cholesterol medication with concrete goals (risk/NNT, tests, time‑limited trial, follow‑up) and stays on task.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine teamwork: co‑creates scripts and checklists, invites rehearsal, integrates the patient's blunt style, and checks which supports would help most.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by offering options, proposing a time‑limited trial, avoiding coercive language, and honoring the patient's right to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits actionable commitments (one‑liner, bringing the paper, conditional trial) but underuses questions to draw out personal values, reasons, and confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative and power‑sharing: solicits preferences, co‑authors phrasing, and follows the patient's lead in planning and problem‑solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates conditions for small change steps and elicits concrete language, but does not consistently probe, amplify, or explore the patient's own reasons or readiness for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to actionable options (trial, tests, checklist), though could more often use double‑sided reflections to explicitly tip ambivalence toward change.
