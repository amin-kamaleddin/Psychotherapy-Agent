# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard with respectful, validating language and repeated affirmations that normalize Linda's experience and honor her goals.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate paraphrase and reflective listening that captures Linda's perspective and history, but often stays at content level rather than reflecting deeper affect (discouragement, shame, hope).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with concrete agenda items and next steps (quit date, meds, weekly check‑ins, craving toolbox) while avoiding coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as an equal partnership—clinician repeatedly asks Linda's views, invites choices, and co‑constructs the plan and supports based on her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Linda's control and choice by offering options, asking permission, and explicitly honoring her decisions without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits reasons, importance/confidence, and some commitment language, but could do more to deepen intrinsic motivations and amplify change statements in the moment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions work as co‑created with repeated invitations to choose topics, draft prescriber questions, and shape the quit plan, reflecting genuine power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and translates it into a concrete plan, though it could more often amplify client language and seek stronger commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids confrontation but uses few strategic double‑sided reflections or reframes to explicitly weaken sustain talk and resolve ambivalence.
