# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—normalized burden, avoided judgmental language, and affirmed the patient's feelings of shame while respecting her perspective.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate, timely reflections and summaries that invited correction and deepened rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking cessation, steered toward a concrete, time-limited plan (one-week test, start date tomorrow) and set specific follow-up arrangements.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—solicited the patient's wording and preferences, adapted the plan to her requests, and co-constructed decisions about strategy and messaging.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported patient choice by offering options with permission, honoring requests (no lecturing), and repeatedly inviting the patient's decisions about the plan and messages.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation (readiness scale, concerns, small test) but could have used more evocative questions and amplifying reflections to draw out deeper intrinsic reasons and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled equal partnership and joint problem-solving, repeatedly checked preferences, and used the patient's input to shape the SMS plan and follow-up structure.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk well (asked about important concerns, solicited a realistic first step) but missed some opportunities to deepen desire, reasons, and commitment with targeted follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed slips constructively, though could have more systematically used double-sided reflections or summaries to shift balance further toward change.
