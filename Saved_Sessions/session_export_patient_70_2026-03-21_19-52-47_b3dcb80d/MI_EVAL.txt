# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—validated fears, affirmed strengths, normalized small steps, and offered practical help without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening and validated Lars’ nervousness and supports, but missed deeper reflections on emotions like shame, boredom, and more complex ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on target behaviors and change goals, steering to concrete actions (forms, calls, rehearsals) and linking steps to Lars’ values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative—co‑created scripts, rehearsed role‑play, and invited Lars into problem solving rather than delivering unilateral advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering options, asking which step felt doable, and avoiding coercive language while emphasizing Lars’ control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked motivations by linking actions to values and eliciting specific steps, but could use more open questions and confidence/commitment elicitation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative with choice and rehearsal, though occasionally felt slightly prescriptive when offering exact scripts; could invite Lars to craft more of the wording himself.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and converted intentions into plans, but would benefit from more systematic elicitation of ability, confidence, and long‑term goal language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used alternative strategies and concrete coping plans to reduce sustain talk, yet could better employ double‑sided reflections to explicitly surface and resolve ambivalence.
