# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard with normalizing language, affirmations, and nonjudgmental validation of Elena’s feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections, matched affect, slowed the pace, and provided grounding and stay-with-me support, showing strong perspective-taking and emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on immediate safety, testing, and stabilization, but gave only light emphasis to the longer-term contraception target rather than briefly evoking it while respecting readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a companion and resource by inviting preferences, checking what would help most, and co-constructing concrete steps like reminders and scheduling.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Elena’s choice, offered options, asked permission before interventions, and refrained from pressuring decisions, reinforcing her ownership of choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited fear and relief but did not systematically draw out or expand Elena’s internal motivations about contraception or use scaling/values questions to deepen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Elena as a collaborator and decision-maker by offering choices, asking permission, and letting her preferences shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change-relevant language was elicited (avoid another scare) but the clinician missed opportunities to amplify and link that language to concrete reasons, ability, or commitment around contraception.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and honored hesitation, redirecting to productive tasks and safety, though there were missed chances to use double-sided reflections to gently highlight ambivalence.
