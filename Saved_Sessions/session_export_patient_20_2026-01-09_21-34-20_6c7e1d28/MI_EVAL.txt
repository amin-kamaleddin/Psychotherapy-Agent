# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, validating ambivalence and avoiding labels; sustained unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening and summaries that captured both pull toward drinking and its consequences; strong perspective-taking across the interaction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the conversation tightly focused on alcohol use with concrete goals (prevent hangovers, protect work) and moved toward specific, practical behavior change actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed—invited input at every step, co-created implementation plan, and used role-play to practice skills; clinician acted as a facilitative partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and control (asked permission, framed changes as tests), respecting client autonomy and reducing pressure to commit to quitting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Brandon's own motivations and solutions (protecting work, avoiding hangovers), used scaling and prompted specific implementation intentions to elicit internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative process with options and permission, but could have more explicitly emphasized Brandon's expertise and solicited his ideas earlier for creative alternatives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and converted desires into actionable steps, but did not consistently amplify or summarize change talk to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and reframed toward harm reduction without arguing, yet used few double-sided reflections or explicit validations that would more actively soften sustain talk.
