# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sets a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validates Alexei’s concerns, but occasionally uses mildly corrective/educational language rather than consistently unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and links substance use to Alexei’s goals, though reflections are fairly surface-level with few complex or feeling-level reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and its impact on soccer/scholarship goals, offering concrete, stepwise options and moving toward rehearsal and a plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly framed as joint problem-solving—options are offered, Alexei chooses strategies, rehearsals are invited, and the clinician adapts to his social concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice by offering multiple options, asking preferences, and inviting Alexei to select strategies, supporting his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws motivation from Alexei’s values and elicits concrete steps, but uses few evocative open questions and does not systematically probe reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with invitations to choose and rehearse, though the clinician occasionally provides a structured plan rather than co-creating every element with Alexei.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk by offering options and asking him to pick actions, but could more frequently amplify spontaneous change language and probe DARN (desire/ability/reason/need).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Gently redirects minimization toward risks and goals and avoids reinforcing sustain talk, but misses opportunities for double-sided reflections and explicit normalization to further soften sustain talk.
