# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence throughout the session, avoiding blame or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking but reflections were often summary-level and could have probed deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and moved the conversation toward specific, feasible actions and implementation plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked jointly with the patient to co-create a practical experiment and scaffolded rather than directed the plan, demonstrating strong partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offered options and invited the patient's choices, respecting agency and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own reasons and values for change but could have amplified internal motivation with scaling or a personal commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and solicited preferences, though could have asked the patient to generate more ideas before offering options or negotiated the agenda earlier.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and helped form a concrete plan, but could more consistently summarize and convert change talk into explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and shifted toward problem solving, yet missed opportunities for double-sided reflections to more directly soften sustain talk.
