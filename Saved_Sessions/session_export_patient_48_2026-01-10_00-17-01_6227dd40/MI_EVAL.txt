# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, validates Farid’s experience, and normalizes ambivalence without criticism. Language is warm, nonjudgmental, and respectful throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections that help Farid open up. Missed opportunities to deepen emotional reflection on shame and self-doubt, focusing more on logistics than affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing short walks and ties every question and suggestion back to that behavioral goal. Effectively resists drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as an equal partnership with frequent invitations for input and integration of Farid’s preferences. Clinician co-constructs the plan and checks preferred follow-up methods.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking permission, and emphasizing adjustability if plans are missed. Repeatedly reinforces that the patient retains control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivation with questions about values, concrete steps, and confidence scaling that draw out change talk. Could probe more for intrinsic motivators, past successes, and more frequent amplification of change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites preferences and integrates Farid’s input into the plan, demonstrating strong power-sharing. At times the clinician offered ready-made wording and plans rather than eliciting more of Farid’s own language first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk using open questions, affirmations, and confidence scaling while co-creating concrete actions. Could more intentionally amplify, reflect, and summarize emerging change talk to strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes uncertainty, redirecting to problem-solving which softens sustain talk. Would benefit from deeper exploration of sustain-talk emotions and use of techniques like double-sided reflections.
