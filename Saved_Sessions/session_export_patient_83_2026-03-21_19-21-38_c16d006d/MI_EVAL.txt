# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard and respectful, nonjudgmental language, frequently validating the patient's concerns and affirming their competence and plan.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening and normalizes worry, but sometimes shifts quickly to education instead of deepening emotional reflection on the anxiety's intensity or impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on safely starting simvastatin throughout the encounter, repeatedly bringing the conversation back to dosing, interactions, monitoring, and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acts as an equal partner by eliciting the patient's routine and co-designing tools (call script, symptom log), though at times the clinician supplies solutions rather than inviting the patient to propose alternatives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports patient choice and offers options without coercion, but could more consistently ask permission before giving advice and elicit the patient's preferred approach among options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation and commitment language, yet the interaction is information-heavy and clinician-driven, missing opportunities to draw out the patient's own reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites shared decision-making and checks readiness, co-creating the plan with the patient, though some recommendations are somewhat directive rather than explicitly permission-seeking.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and concrete commitments and supplies practical tools that support action, but could further explore the patient's personal values and long-term reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes doubts without arguing, but relies mostly on factual clarification and directives rather than using double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to pivot sustain talk toward change.
