# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating the patient's experience and offering choices without minimizing or criticizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows strong perspective-taking with timely, accurate reflections of ambivalence and emotional pain, and checks how to make the practice feel safe and validating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on suicidality/emotion regulation and the one-minute breathing experiment, repeatedly returning to planning and safety while staying goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine two-way partnership by soliciting the patient's input at each step, implementing preferences, and framing follow-up as shared data-gathering.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice and control—invites decisions about technique, timing, and wording, grants permission to stop, and frames the trial as the patient's test.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's reasons (e.g., 'for my brother') and elicits safety needs and a commitment, but could amplify reasons for living and assess confidence/readiness more directly.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and co-creative—offers options, defers to the patient's expertise, and shares power so the patient's choices shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with a concrete, small experiment and a specific commitment, but could more explicitly amplify and summarize the patient's change language and probe confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and acknowledges sustain talk while redirecting toward a testable behavior and safety planning, though more frequent double-sided reflections earlier would better soften sustain talk.
