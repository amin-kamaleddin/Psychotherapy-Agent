# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with multiple reflective statements and affirmations. Could show more individualized warmth and explicit normalizing of ambivalence to reach a 5.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, surface-level reflections of Dylan’s emotions and priorities. Missed opportunities to explore deeper feelings (e.g., shame, worries about a record) and their meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, pragmatic focus on clearing the charge and minimizing sanctions. Did not pursue broader clinical targets like reducing risky drinking or preventing future incidents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem‑solving and treated Dylan as co-author of the plan, with his ideas driving action. Moved quickly into offering concrete wording and tactics without explicitly negotiating alternative shared goals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Dylan’s choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and leaving decisions to him. Reinforced autonomy throughout the interaction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited Dylan’s immediate intentions and preferences but focused narrowly on tactics to contest the charge. Missed probing deeper motivations, values, or ambivalence about future behavior.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly invited Dylan’s input and shared decision‑making was evident as he shaped the plan. Could deepen partnership by explicitly eliciting Dylan’s expertise about his social network and co‑developing value‑aligned contingencies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to elicit or amplify change talk about alcohol use, responsibility, or harm reduction; emphasis was on contesting sanctions. Needs probes to draw out Dylan’s own reasons for behavior change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Frequently mirrored and operationalized sustain talk (e.g., crafting witness lines) rather than gently redirecting or using double‑sided reflections. Few attempts were made to explore consequences or evoke discrepancy to soften sustain talk.
