# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing Camila's concerns and affirming strengths. No signs of shaming or dismissal; acceptance is clear throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and some reflective listening, validating enjoyment of walks and energy concerns. Reflections are often brief and quickly move to solutions, so deeper reflection of ambivalence would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on increasing physical activity and moves clearly toward small, actionable steps. Guides transitions from brainstorming to a concrete starter plan and schedules a timely check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction reads as collaborative with co‑created reminders and tailored options. However, the clinician often offered many ready-made solutions before eliciting Camila's own ideas, tilting the balance toward clinician-driven suggestions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and flexibility with multiple low‑effort options, a no‑pressure rule, and allow‑snooze defaults. Preserves Camila's control by inviting wording/scheduling choices and offering gentle backups.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effort to draw out preferences and doable options, but much of the session relies on clinician-generated solutions rather than eliciting Camila's deeper values or reasons. Greater use of open questions and scaling for importance/confidence would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Mostly collaborative and co-constructive, asking which options feel doable and shaping reminders with the patient. Early provision of structured lists and starter plans reduced opportunities to elicit Camila's ideas first, limiting equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and translates it into a concrete plan with commitments and a check‑in. Missed opportunities remain to deepen autonomous reasons and ask for confidence/importance ratings or patient‑stated motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers many gentler alternatives, decision rules, and a no‑pressure approach to reduce ambivalence. Could further soften sustain talk with double‑sided reflections and eliciting Camila's own counterarguments for change.
