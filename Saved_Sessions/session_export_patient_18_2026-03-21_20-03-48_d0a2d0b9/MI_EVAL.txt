# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through a neutral, nonjudgmental tone and invitations to share; normalized curiosity rather than labeling the client.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated concerns about school and risky decisions; could deepen connection with more complex or affect-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol risk reduction and progressed toward concrete behavioral strategies and planning throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created solutions with the client by offering options, soliciting preferences, and using role-play feedback in a non-authoritarian, cooperative manner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice by asking permission before offering information, providing multiple options, and inviting the client to select what to practice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client's own reasons and practical commitments (protecting grades, doable strategies) but could more consistently probe values, importance/confidence, and amplify change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled genuine power-sharing by inviting input, asking which strategies felt authentic, and offering collaborative rehearsal options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made reasonable attempts to evoke change talk (context, worries, doable steps) but did not systematically deepen or amplify those statements toward stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided strengthening sustain talk by normalizing and redirecting attention to concerns and options; could add double-sided reflections or gentle reframes when sustain talk appears.
