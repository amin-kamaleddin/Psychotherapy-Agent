# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Camila’s experience while calmly addressing safety concerns. The clinician avoided judgment and validated difficulty throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate understanding of Camila’s situation and emotions. Responses sometimes moved quickly into solution-offering rather than lingering to explore deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on reducing problematic drinking and linked interventions directly to that goal. The clinician steered toward concrete, behaviorally specific strategies and included safety checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with inclusive language, mutual decision-making, and co-created role-play. The clinician solicited preferences and offered brief feedback rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supported Camila’s autonomy by offering choices, checking what she wanted, and avoiding prescriptive language. Invitations to choose and practice reinforced her control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and concrete reasons for change and drew out planning details. Could more systematically probe intrinsic motivators, values, and use scaling to deepen internal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Camila as a collaborator and expert on her life, offering options and inviting refinement of the plan. Power was shared throughout the session, with the clinician following her lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and strengthened change talk with concrete planning questions and affirmations, helping Camila commit to specific steps. Missed some opportunities to amplify and reflect her change language to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and provided compromise options and scripts to redirect it toward workable strategies. Could increase use of explicit double-sided reflections to artfully reduce sustain talk while validating ambivalence.
