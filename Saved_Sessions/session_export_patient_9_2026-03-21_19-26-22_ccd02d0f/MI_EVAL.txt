# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing the client's burden and affirming her intentions while offering practical safety and backup plans.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, attuned reflections of both affect and content, and tailored responses that show perspective-taking and emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on bedtime/screen routines and co‑parenting consistency, with every intervention tied to the target behavior and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrably collaborative—invites choice, co‑works on mapping and wording, role‑plays with the client, and adapts language to her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice and control by offering options, asking permission, accepting readiness levels, and preserving agency in planning.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits goals, readiness, and a small experiment, but could strengthen evocation with more open, evocative questions to draw out reasons, strengths, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and invites client input, though occasionally shifts to providing full scripts rather than co‑creating wording first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates many opportunities for change talk and translates desires into concrete steps, but could amplify and reflect the client's own change statements more often to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers practical de‑escalation strategies, yet could further soften resistance using double‑sided reflections and selective reflections of sustain language.
