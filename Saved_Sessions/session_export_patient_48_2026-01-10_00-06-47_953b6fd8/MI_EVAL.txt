# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated a nonjudgmental, respectful stance (opening line, validations, safety prompt) and normalized Farid’s experience throughout, avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and normalization demonstrated good empathic understanding, but reflections were often brief and quickly followed by solutions rather than deeper exploration of emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing brief physical activity with repeated, concrete steps (time windows, reminders, contingency plans) and actionable follow‑ups.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative—choices were solicited and incorporated—but it leaned toward an expert/coach role due to many clinician-provided scripts and step‑by‑step instructions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician preserved choice by offering alternatives and normalizing missed attempts, though at times moved quickly into detailed plans without first checking whether Farid wanted that level of help.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was drawn (choices, commitments, check‑in) but the clinician relied more on offering solutions than systematically eliciting Farid’s reasons, values, strengths, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were moments of shared planning (asking which windows felt doable), but the heavy offering of specific recommendations and scripts reduced the sense that Farid’s expertise was leading the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited and reinforced specific change talk and commitments (selected times, one‑week trial), though missed opportunities remained to amplify desire, confidence, and past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was softened through reframing and normalization and by shifting to small experiments, but could be strengthened with more double‑sided reflections explicitly acknowledging ambivalence.
