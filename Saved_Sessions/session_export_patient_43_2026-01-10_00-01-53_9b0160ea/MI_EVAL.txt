# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalizing setbacks and using nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking to validate the patient, but missed deeper exploration of emotional subtext (e.g., feelings about wife's pressure or identity tied to smoking).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and moved to a specific, measurable experiment with follow-up, keeping the session goal-directed without imposing solutions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting the patient's input, co-creating the plan, and honoring the patient's choices about goals and timing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy with permission language, offering options rather than directives and emphasizing that change is the patient's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own reasons and commitments through options and an if-then plan, but could further evoke personal values and importance/confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated true partnership by asking permission, offering options, and letting the patient determine the plan and follow-up timing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk leading to concrete steps and reported reductions in urge, though more strategic amplification and summarization of the patient's change language would strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed toward small experiments to reduce defensiveness, but could use more double-sided reflections and selective summaries to further soften sustain talk.
