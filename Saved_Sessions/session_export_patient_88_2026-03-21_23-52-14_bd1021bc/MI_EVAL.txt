### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing Marcus's priorities and accepting his qualifiers (e.g., 'not promising'). The clinician maintains a supportive tone and avoids shaming throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring Marcus's language and summarizing his ambivalence and strategies. Could have briefly deepened emotional reflection, but empathy is strong and lowers defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on alcohol harm-reduction and narrows from options to a concrete, trialable plan. Questions and reflections consistently tie back to actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True side-by-side approach: asks permission, solicits preferences, and co-creates the plan without taking an expert stance. Marcus's voice drives the content and decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and ownership, offering options and help without pressure and making clear Marcus will send the text himself. Language preserves autonomy throughout the session.

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits Marcus's own strategies and motivations and generates self-directed solutions like pacing, a bail plan, and a drafted text. Could be strengthened by exploring personal values, long-term consequences, confidence, and past successes to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as shared and client-led, inviting Marcus's input at each step and deferring final actions to him. The plan emerges from mutual collaboration with Marcus in the lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits multiple strands of change talk and turns vague ideas into concrete, trialable steps (pacing, bail plan, text). Missed opportunities include less exploration of importance and confidence and fewer moves to amplify and reinforce change language.
