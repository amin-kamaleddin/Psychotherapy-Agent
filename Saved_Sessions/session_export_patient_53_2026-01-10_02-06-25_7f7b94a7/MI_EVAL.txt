# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Isabella's hesitation without shaming, creating a warm, accepting stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening with accurate, timely reflections and recognition of subtle cues; could deepen empathy with more complex, feeling-focused or bodily reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on increasing small community activities, with questions and interventions consistently linked to a concrete first step and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician solicited preferences, co‑constructed the plan, and invited Isabella to choose reminders and backup options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly supported choice by offering options, asking permission, and validating backups, reinforcing that the plan belonged to Isabella.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician used evocative questions and confidence scaling to draw out motivation but relied more on pragmatic scaffolding than eliciting intrinsic reasons, values, or DARN language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership demonstrated by power‑sharing, frequent checking of preferences, and inviting Isabella to shape specifics like alarm wording and reminder source.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited and reinforced tentative change talk and concrete steps, but could more actively mirror, amplify, and elicit personal reasons to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to manageable steps effectively; could enhance impact with double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to resolve ambivalence.
