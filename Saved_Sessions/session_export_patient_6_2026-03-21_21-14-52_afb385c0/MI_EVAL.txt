# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing Alexei’s concerns and affirming his values (soccer, scholarships) without shaming or blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective listening that captured factual and emotional content, though the clinician sometimes shifted quickly to action and could use more complex, evocative reflections to deepen emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on alcohol use tied to Alexei’s goals and progressed logically from eliciting importance to selecting strategies and rehearsing concrete steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative: the clinician offered options and scripting while Alexei supplied preferences and co-constructed plans (refusal lines, code word, backups).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission (e.g., for role-play), and inviting Alexei to select what felt most helpful, reinforcing self-directed decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician effectively drew out intrinsic motivation by linking behavior to valued goals, using open questions and eliciting specific change talk that Alexei then translated into commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician treated Alexei as a collaborator throughout, inviting his input on strategies, role-plays, and who to name as backups, reinforcing joint problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong elicitation and reinforcement of change talk (importance ruler, linking to soccer/scholarships), and translating that language into actionable plans and commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided confrontation and redirected minimization toward goals, but used few explicit double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence before pivoting to solutions.
