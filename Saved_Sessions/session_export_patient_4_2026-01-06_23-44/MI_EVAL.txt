# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—validated and normalized distress, rejected dismissive framing, and honored patient requests (drafting the letter, offering role‑play).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective‑taking and reflective listening—captured both factual and emotional content and invited further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on the GI symptoms and concrete next steps—prioritized investigations, referral, and short‑term coping and advocacy tasks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine two‑way collaborator—co‑constructed the referral letter, offered role‑play and scripts, and repeatedly checked the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering multiple options, asking which she preferred, and respecting her decision to prioritize tests first.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some goals and motivators (what she wanted, times symptoms eased) and offered invitations to act, but could more systematically draw out values, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power‑sharing and treated the patient as an active collaborator—language and actions centered the patient's needs and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts (noting stress‑safety pattern, suggesting coping options) but did not consistently elicit or amplify the patient's own reasons, confidence, or specific goals for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated the desire for tests which prevented escalation, but used few double‑sided reflections or reframes to actively shift sustain talk toward change.
