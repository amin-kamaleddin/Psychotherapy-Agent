# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing nervousness and affirming the patient without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic engagement and safety checks, but missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of the patient’s ambivalence and emotional conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on harm-reduction goals throughout the session, steering toward concrete safety steps and next actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a genuine partnership with co-created plans, options offered, and joint rehearsal rather than prescriptive advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and leaving timing and decisions to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited readiness for specific harm-reduction steps but did not sufficiently probe deeper personal motives or values behind the change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong collaborative stance, treating the patient as the expert and inviting choices about scripts and resources.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced actionable change talk and concrete plans, though could have used more open questions and reflections to amplify internal motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and gently redirected to safety and harm reduction, but could have used more double-sided reflections to validate ambivalence while steering change.
