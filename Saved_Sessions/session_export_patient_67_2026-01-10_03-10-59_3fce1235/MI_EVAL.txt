# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicates nonjudgmental acceptance and normalizes ambivalence, but statements sometimes feel formulaic and would benefit from more individualized affirmations of specific strengths or efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections reliably mirror content and help Kenji elaborate, yet they are often close paraphrases rather than deeper, affect-focused or amplified reflections that name underlying emotions and tie themes together.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use and the session logically moves from eliciting concerns to brainstorming options and selecting a concrete first step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is a genuine joint exploration—open questions elicit Kenji's ideas and the clinician uses those ideas to co-create a practical plan without adopting a prescriptive stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly supported through invitations to choose, normalization of ambivalence, and checks that plans feel manageable, fostering client ownership rather than coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician successfully elicits Kenji's reasons and concrete options and evokes internal resources, but could deepen evocation by amplifying change statements, using scaling, and linking change to values or identity.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session demonstrates strong partnership: the clinician solicits Kenji's expertise, shares power in decision-making, and invites him to lead planning and check‑in preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk is elicited and Kenji generates specific steps, yet the clinician rarely amplifies or strategically reinforces change language or elicits confidence/commitment ratings to strengthen resolve.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged without confrontation and reframed neutrally, but opportunities are missed for double-sided or strategic reflections that would more actively shift emphasis toward change.
