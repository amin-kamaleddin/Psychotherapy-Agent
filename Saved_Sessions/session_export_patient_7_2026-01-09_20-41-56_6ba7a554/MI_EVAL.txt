# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, honoring boundaries and requests for silence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and validations, matching the client's guarded stance and preference for privacy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on engagement and immediate safety while respecting boundaries, offering relevant topic options and a coherent plan to follow the client's lead.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the encounter as a joint process by consistently offering choices, checking permission, and adjusting actions (no timer, wait for nod) based on the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively and repeatedly affirms the client's control over topics and timing, clearly stating limits while emphasizing choice and respecting decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some invitations to choose topics and offers of coping strategies are present, but the clinician rarely uses targeted evocation techniques (scaling, values, or reasons) to draw out motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as collaborative, using shared decision language and following the client's lead with joint planning and negotiation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Attempts are limited to offering topic options and strategies, with missed opportunities for gentle, evocative prompts or selective reinforcement to elicit even brief change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids confrontation and honors refusals, reframing boundaries and offering alternatives, though could use more strategic double-sided reflections to further soften sustain talk.
