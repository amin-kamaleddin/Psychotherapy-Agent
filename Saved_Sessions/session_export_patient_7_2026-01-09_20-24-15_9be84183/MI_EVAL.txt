# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and positive regard (apology, honoring boundaries, framing the session as the patient's space), normalizing the patient's stance and avoiding judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good perspective-taking and validating statements but missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflections or summarizing the client's likely feelings to build greater connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on teaching and rehearsing a concrete skill (setting limits) and kept the session purposefully on task for initial engagement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort, routinely invited the patient's preferences and participation, and co-constructed the script and role-play effectively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, honoring refusals, and repeatedly emphasizing that it was the patient's space and no pressure to commit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited small bits of motivation and tentative commitment through practical prompts and rehearsal, but could use more open, evocative questions about values, reasons, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fostered collaboration by offering choices and inviting co-creation, though at times shifted toward directive teaching when presenting multiple tools rather than eliciting the patient's wording first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited rehearsal and tentative behavioral commitment from a minimally responsive client, but could amplify and explore past successes or confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated doubt while redirecting to practical strategies, though could further soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections and strategic pivots toward change language.
