# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language, validating reflections, and encouragement throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and reflective listening, accurately naming feelings and constraints; could use more complex reflections to deepen emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on physical activity/weight goals with concrete behavioral targets, a 2-week plan, and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as an equal partnership with frequent invitations for input, co-creation of materials, and negotiated next steps rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, and emphasizing the patient's control and ability to adapt the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and builds on the patient's change talk and commitment (check-in), but could deepen internal motivation with more open questions, scaling, and amplifying reflections.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: offers options, solicits preferences, and co-creates the checklist and reminders with the patient guiding decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change statements and creates specific plans and accountability; would benefit from more selective reflections and scaling to amplify change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, reframes with small alternatives and normalizes setbacks; could add double-sided reflections to acknowledge both sustain and change considerations more explicitly.
