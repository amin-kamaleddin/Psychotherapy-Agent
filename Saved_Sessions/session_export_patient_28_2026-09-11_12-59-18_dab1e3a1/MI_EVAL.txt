# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing statements and strength‑based language, avoiding labels or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, sustained reflective listening—naming emotions, summarizing ambivalence, and following the patient's perspective rather than redirecting the agenda.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on restarting physical activity and logically moves from values/past success to a single, actionable plan without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine co‑construction of the plan by soliciting the patient’s ideas and preferences and refining them together rather than dominating or remaining passive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly reinforces choice and ownership—asks what feels realistic, invites tweaks, and avoids directives so the patient retains control of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits reasons, past supports, and small steps, but could deepen motivation by linking to values, using scaling, and amplifying spontaneous change language more consistently.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on his life, asking for his preference and choices and sharing power in decision‑making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (past success, small steps, concrete plans) but could more selectively amplify and summarize statements to strengthen desire/ability/reasons and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and pivots to planning, yet at times repeats the patient’s skepticism verbatim instead of using more double‑sided or evocative reflections to downweight sustain talk.
