# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with validating reflections and supportive language throughout the session, without shaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate summaries and targeted questions that aided disclosure, but relied more on logistical paraphrase than deeper emotional or autonomy-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on school punctuality with concrete, actionable strategies, readiness/confidence checks, and a specific plan without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged Jamal as an active partner by inviting preferences, co-creating scripts, and seeking his input on wording and choices throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, and checking which strategies Jamal found most doable, preserving his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons and pragmatic motives for change and checked confidence, but did limited work to draw out deeper intrinsic values or personal meaning of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful of Jamal's input, though frequent clinician-provided scripts risked tilting toward an expert-driven stance rather than eliciting his own wording first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using choice questions, a 0–10 confidence scale, and amplified commitments leading to concrete steps and a plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not amplify sustain talk and frequently redirected to change options, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and explicit acknowledgement of Jamal's legitimate reasons.
