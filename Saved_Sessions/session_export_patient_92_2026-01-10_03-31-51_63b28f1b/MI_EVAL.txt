# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validating Marek and avoiding blame. Safety concerns are emphasized without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good basic empathy and some reflective listening that acknowledges ambivalence between enjoyment and risk. Reflections are mostly brief and cognitive with limited deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays clearly focused on weekend drinking and risk reduction with a concrete 2‑week tracking plan and specific check‑in content. The clinician repeatedly restates goals and next steps to maintain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—clinician solicits preferences, tailors materials (one-line checklist), offers practical supports, and defers to Marek's agenda. Plans are co‑created and inclusive.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly supported through offering options, asking permission, and affirming Marek's control over change. The clinician avoids pressure and frames tracking as Marek's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation is present (inviting tracking and reflection) but in-session elicitation of Marek's motivations is limited. The clinician gives information without consistently using evocative questions or amplifying tentative change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership and shared decision‑making with multiple choice offers and co‑created plans. Clinician positions themselves as a helper and repeatedly asks what would be most useful.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are targeted attempts to elicit change talk (tracking experiment, positive reinforcement) but few open, evocative questions to draw out desire, reasons, or commitment. Change talk is encouraged mainly via behavioral tasks rather than amplified exploration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoids arguing and neutralizes sustain talk by normalizing risks and offering small experiments. Missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections that explicitly highlight ambivalence would further soften sustain talk.
