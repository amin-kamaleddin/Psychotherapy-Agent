# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing difficulty and validating the client's efforts with warm, supportive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both content and affect to deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on attending interviews and practical steps to address childcare barriers, repeatedly returning to actionable implementation plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructs decisions by soliciting the client's ideas, integrating them into summaries and plans, and using inclusive, non-expert language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers options and asks the client to choose, preserving the client's sense of control and ownership over decisions and actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the client by eliciting internal reasons and strategies, translating values into concrete behavioral intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the conversation as a shared endeavor, invites the client's expertise, and collaboratively builds a realistic plan with her input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicits and amplifies change talk and confidence through evocative questions and highlighting momentum, leading to concrete commitments and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects to solutions but misses opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections, normalization of competing priorities, and eliciting contingency coping plans.
