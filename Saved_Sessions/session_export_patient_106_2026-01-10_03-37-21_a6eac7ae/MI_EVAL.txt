# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with normalizing statements and gentle reframing of slips throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good empathic statements and reflected practical barriers and stress, but missed deeper affective reflections about guilt, mothering worries, and the tone of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction and protecting the child with specific, measurable steps and a concrete two‑week follow‑up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem‑solving, solicited the patient's input, tailored recommendations to her context, and arranged a collaborative follow‑up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission, honoring reluctance about meds, and encouraging tweaking of the plan as needed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete steps and some motivations (logs, fixed times) but could have done more to draw out deeper intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence/importance ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative relationship and asked permission before recommending, though at times provided many concrete suggestions rather than co‑constructing solutions from the patient's language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change‑oriented statements and offered a clear reduction plan, but missed multiple opportunities to amplify the patient's own reasons and desire for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and accepted sustain talk while offering harm‑reduction, but rarely used double‑sided reflections or strategic reflections to resolve ambivalence.
