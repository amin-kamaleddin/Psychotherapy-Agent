# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, praising efforts and normalizing ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening by naming mixed emotions and validating the difficulty of change.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on gambling/financial control and linked discussion to the patient's values, keeping the session centered on concrete behavioral steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way partnership—clinician invited patient input at each decision point and integrated responses into a jointly built plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supported autonomy with permission and invitational questions, but supplying many specific options at times risked reducing the patient's experienced choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently drew motivation from the patient's own goals and strategies, amplifying change talk and converting reasons into specific plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered collaboration and invited choices, though at times leaned toward offering expert solutions rather than exclusively following the patient's lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited, reinforced, and built on the patient's change language using targeted questions that produced concrete commitment and action steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to change planning, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and deeper ambivalence exploration.
