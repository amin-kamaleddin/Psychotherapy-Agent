# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened with a supportive, nonjudgmental stance and repeatedly validated Jordan's concerns, normalizing his tension without shaming. Warmth and positive regard were maintained throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with timely reflections that matched Jordan's affect and expanded his thinking. Clinician paraphrased emotional drivers (fear of needles, concern for grandparents) effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the agenda focused on flu vaccination and related behaviors, linking options back to the target behavior and guiding toward concrete next steps. Direction was maintained without pressuring Jordan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Authentically co-constructed the plan, invited Jordan's edits, and offered support only when requested, showing true partnership rather than a top-down approach. Language encouraged shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering multiple low-commitment options and affirming Jordan's control over decisions. Avoided coercive language and reinforced ownership of any next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intrinsic motives like care for grandparents and drew concrete change talk, but sometimes provided information or scripts before fully eliciting Jordan's own formulations. Could deepen evocation with more strategic prompts (e.g., scaling or strength-based questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative, invited Jordan into decision-making, and accepted his wording and edits for messages and plans. Demonstrated strong power-sharing throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk through pros/cons and concrete options that produced actionable intent, but at times offered unsolicited advice and scripts without first asking permission. More strategic reflections to amplify expressed reasons and commitment would strengthen this area.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledge­d sustain talk without arguing and shifted to harm-reduction strategies that reduced barriers (scheduling, distraction, numbing cream). Reframed concerns into practical steps that made change more doable.
