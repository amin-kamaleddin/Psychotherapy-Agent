# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows respect and normalizes slip‑ups, reducing shame; warmth sometimes feels procedural rather than deeply individualized, with fewer explicit personalized affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective‑taking and accurate reflections of ambivalence; missed opportunities to explore emotional nuance and reflect inner feelings more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and sleep/concentration throughout the session and moves purposefully from assessment to a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—elicits client input at every step, adapts to preferences, and co‑constructs reminders and plans rather than imposing them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking permission to help, and normalizing missed days, reinforcing that change is the client's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client reasons using scaling and exploratory questions and links them to steps; could probe deeper into values, past successes, and confidence to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the client as an equal collaborator with frequent invitations to choose and shape the plan, demonstrating genuine power‑sharing in decisions and follow‑up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk through scaling and targeted questions, and achieves a concrete plan; could amplify and summarize client change language more to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without confrontation and redirects to manageable options, but would benefit from more double‑sided reflections and explicit linking of maintenance reasons to costs.
