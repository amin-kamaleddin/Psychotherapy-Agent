# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, using normalizing statements and validating Oliver's conflict and guilt. Warmth and unconditional positive regard are maintained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid empathic understanding with some reflective phrasing, but often shifts quickly into advice and scripts. Could deepen reflective exploration of emotion and ambivalence to better mirror Oliver's feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on the target behavior (setting a move-out boundary) and keeps the conversation goal-oriented. Suggestions, scripts, and rehearsal consistently support follow-through.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites role-play and adjusts wording when requested, showing a collaborative posture. However, the interaction sometimes tilts toward clinician-led scripting rather than letting the client generate content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respects agency by offering options and affirming the client's choice, and asks permission to rehearse. Some prescriptive, ready-made recommendations slightly undermine ownership and could be framed more as choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation indirectly through planning and rehearsal but relies more on didactic scripting than open, evocative questions. Greater use of questions that draw out Oliver's values and reasons would strengthen motivation from within.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows some collaborative moves (rehearsal offers, wording adjustments) but often adopts an expert, directive stance with many specific scripts. Stronger co-construction—inviting Oliver to generate language first—would enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively fosters and reinforces change language through invitations to rehearse, concrete scripts, and coaching practice. Could increase use of open questions that elicit Oliver's own reasons, values, and confidence to further amplify change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects skillfully toward boundary-setting, offering strategies to respond to pleading. Relies more on directive redirection than double-sided reflections or summaries that explicitly validate both sides of ambivalence.
