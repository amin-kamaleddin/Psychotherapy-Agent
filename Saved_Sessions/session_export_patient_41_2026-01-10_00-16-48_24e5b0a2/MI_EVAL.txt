# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with validating reflections and frequent affirmations, avoiding blame. Normalizes difficulty and treats setbacks as information rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening, capturing behavior and ambivalence. Could deepen affect-focused reflections to explore the emotional meaning and daily impact of anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on a single concrete behavioral experiment (5 minutes) and returns to actionable details throughout. The session progresses logically from goal selection to rehearsal and debrief planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving, invites patient preferences, and co-constructs the plan (script, supports, rehearsal). Follows the patient's lead and honors reluctance rather than prescribing a solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes patient choice and frames experiments as voluntary, avoiding pressuring language. Offers options and explicit permission to leave, reinforcing control and self-determination.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and barriers using open questions, scaling, and requests for specifics, bringing client reasons into the talk. Missed deeper probes into personal values, past successes, and underlying reasons for wanting confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the process as collaborative and honors choices by offering options and asking which supports to add. At times offers multiple clinician-generated strategies quickly, which slightly risks an expert-driven feel instead of deeper elicitation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk through open choices, scaling, and concrete planning which produced intentions and steps. Could amplify and reflect client's change statements more strongly and explore personal importance to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk, validates concerns, and reframes leaving as data while offering low-pressure experiments to reduce defensiveness. Could further use double-sided reflections to more actively resolve ambivalence.
