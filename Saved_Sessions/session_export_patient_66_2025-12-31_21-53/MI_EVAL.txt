# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent respect and positive regard; multiple normalizing, nonjudgmental statements (“That’s a sensible place to be,” “I’ll support whatever you decide”); validated patient preferences (no patch) and testing changes rather than pressuring to quit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflective listening and summaries; echoed patient ratings (5/10 importance, 6/10 confidence) and paraphrased ambivalence to match tone and content throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking at work/cutting down; offered concrete, behaviorally specific options (cap, walk, gum/lozenge), a short script, and scheduled a Friday check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly invited patient preference (“Which feels more useful?”), co-created the plan, and adjusted recommendations to Kareem’s choices (no patch; gum/lozenge options).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed autonomy (“Not promising to quit is okay,” “I’ll support whatever you decide”), avoided pressure, and normalized slips to preserve patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivational material (importance/confidence, preferred options, barriers) and asked evocative questions, but missed deeper probes into personal values or long-term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked jointly to set realistic goals (40% cap, walk, gum/lozenge), solicited feasibility and obstacles, and negotiated follow-up—decisions clearly made together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change-oriented statements and arranged a follow-up; however, opportunities to amplify and chain change talk into stronger commitment language were not fully utilized.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and used double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence; could have more selectively reflected and amplified change statements to further reduce sustain talk.
