# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, normalizing and validating Maya’s experience. Honors limits (e.g., refusing parental involvement) and uses supportive, nonlabeling language throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and captures core tensions between relief and consequence. Could deepen by probing more nuanced affect (e.g., deeper exploration of fear/worry behind 'freaks me out').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on harm reduction (no drinking on school nights) and consistently builds toward concrete, measurable steps. Links strategies—reminders, walks, check‑ins—directly to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction feels like an equal partnership: the clinician co-creates the plan, solicits preferences, and responds to them rather than imposing solutions. Offers choices about support format and timing to fit Maya’s needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Maya’s choice and control, offering support only with permission and respecting her decisions. Reinforces ownership of the plan and allows pacing without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits personal strengths, past strategies, and concrete indicators of success to draw out motivation. Could be strengthened by linking change to deeper values or long-term goals to further anchor intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance that invites Maya’s input and shares expertise lightly while ceding decision-making to her. Co-constructs specifics (alarm wording, check-in timing) to center the client’s preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits, reinforces, and amplifies change talk by asking about doable options and what success would look like. Uses reflections and affirmations to strengthen Maya’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk nonjudgmentally and normalizes ambivalence, avoiding argument or pressure. Reframes toward practical harm-reduction options rather than all-or-nothing demands.
