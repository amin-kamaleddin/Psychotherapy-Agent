# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized uncertainty, and reframed slips as learning opportunities; no shaming or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening with brief reflections and tailored support for triggers; could use deeper, more complex reflections exploring underlying emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction/cessation and moved the session toward concrete behavioral steps without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as genuine partners by soliciting the patient's input at multiple decision points, co-planning strategies, and using role-play rather than directing unilaterally.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, and reassuring that slips are not failures, preserving the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation with scaling and preference questions and achieved concrete commitments, but relied at times on prescriptive language instead of more evocative open questions and amplifications.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: repeatedly asked permission, offered options, and invited the patient to direct the plan, demonstrating shared power and mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened change talk through scaling, offering options, and co-creating a 2-week plan; could deepen change talk with more evocative questions and strategic reflections.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided reinforcing sustain talk by honoring concerns and offering alternatives and harm-reduction strategies; could further reduce sustain talk using double-sided reflections that contrast sustain and change.
