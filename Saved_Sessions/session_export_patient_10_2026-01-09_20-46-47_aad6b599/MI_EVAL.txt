# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Amina’s efforts without judgment, normalizing her struggles. No critical or minimizing language observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, naming emotions and matching Amina’s statements. Minor opportunities to further deepen the emotional texture were noted but overall highly empathic.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on overcoming childcare barriers to attend interviews and moved the session toward specific next actions. Every turn returned to actionable steps tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively and solicited input, adjusting materials to Amina’s preferences, but often generated specific text and stepwise plans. More invitations for Amina to draft solutions first would strengthen shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Amina’s choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and framing plans as her decisions. Avoided directive language and reinforced ownership of actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation effectively with importance/confidence scaling and preference questions, but frequently supplied solutions rather than using more evocative open questions to deepen reasons and values. More prompts like 'What makes that a 10?' would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative tone and invited preferences, yet provided multiple ready-made drafts and plans that shifted some problem-solving to the clinician. Increasing client-originated ideas would enhance true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk via scaling and affirmations and asked about doable steps, but missed opportunities to deepen and amplify commitment and reasons. Targeted probes to explore meaning of a '10' would have strengthened change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and neutralized sustain talk, reflecting worries and promptly moving to problem-solving and options. Could further soften sustain talk with more double-sided reflections and strategies to elicit the client’s own resolution.
