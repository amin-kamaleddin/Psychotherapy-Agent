PSYCHIATRIST: Good—here’s a short, practical rundown so you can weigh varenicline.

Varenicline (Chantix)
- Most effective single med for quitting; reduces cravings and reward from smoking.
- Common side effects: nausea (most common), vivid/dreamy sleep, insomnia, constipation, headache.
- Typical start: low dose, titrate, often begun 1 week before quit date.
- Rare/serious: mood changes, agitation, suicidal thoughts reported in some cases—large trials/meta-analyses haven’t shown a clear increase, but monitoring is advised.
- Dose adjust if you have kidney problems; discuss pregnancy or other meds with your clinician.

Alternatives
- Bupropion SR: helps if low mood, but can cause insomnia, dry mouth, and lowers seizure threshold.
- Nicotine replacement (patch/gum/lozenge): safe, can be combined (patch + gum/lozenge) for better control of cravings; side effects usually local (skin, mouth) or sleep disturbance.

Best approach
- Medication + behavioral support (counseling, quitline, apps) = highest success.
