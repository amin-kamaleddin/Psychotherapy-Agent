# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard through normalizing, nonjudgmental reflections and affirmations, validating wins and struggles without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate reflections including double-sided formulations; some reflections felt formulaic and could probe deeper into affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on targeted behaviors and effectively moves the conversation from motivation to concrete implementation (scheduling, reminders, coping cues).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co‑constructed—clinician solicits and validates the patient's ideas, offers options, and repeatedly asks for the patient's preference.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, inviting the patient to pick steps, and avoiding pressuring language, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's motivations and commitments but occasionally shifts toward clinician-suggested solutions rather than maximizing patient-generated strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership tone—uses inclusive language, invites input, and co‑creates a practical plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits and amplifies change talk, turning general ideas into specific commitments and implementation steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and contains sustain talk and pivots to planning, though at times repeats sustain content verbatim instead of using more strategic double‑sided reflections or brief cost exploration.
