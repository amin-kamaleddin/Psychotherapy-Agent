# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Mateo's experience, using warm, nonjudgmental language that preserves dignity. No stigmatizing or minimizing language was used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the patient's phrasing and emotional tone. Reflections and validations kept Mateo engaged and felt understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing safe activity/household independence and moves logically from barriers to a concrete, testable plan. The session produced a measurable goal with defined success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly egalitarian and co-constructive, with the clinician soliciting Mateo's input at every decision point. Decisions about goals, timing, and supports were made together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and control by offering options, asking for Mateo's ideas, and emphasizing his ownership of the plan. Clinician reinforced the voluntary nature of the experiment and allowed stopping if it hurt.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivation and past success factors, drawing out reasons like helping his wife and seeing progress. Missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivation with values questions or scaling confidence/importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint problem-solving and defers to Mateo's lived expertise, inviting his preferences throughout. Co-creation of the plan reflects genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk and turns motivation into a concrete experiment, but did not explicitly probe confidence or use scaling to strengthen commitment. Could have explored additional personal reasons or values to deepen resolve.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects doubts toward low-risk experiments, reducing focus on reasons not to change. Could more actively use double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to further neutralize sustain talk and explore past flare-up management.
