### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently expressed nonjudgmental support and positive regard (thanking, normalizing, reminders to be kind) with no shaming or scolding.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate understanding and timely reflective listening, paraphrasing meaning and emotion to guide the conversation empathetically.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on mini‑cigar use, offered targeted strategies, and moved the session toward a specific, measurable plan with follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session read as joint problem‑solving: checked preferences, co‑created the implementation intention, and incorporated the patient's ideas into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced choice and avoided pressure by offering options and asking which felt doable, supporting patient ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own solutions and a small commitment, but could have probed more for personal reasons, values, and confidence to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance: invited decision‑making, asked about follow‑up preferences, and positioned the patient as the expert on what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and a concrete behavioral commitment, yet missed opportunities to deepen reasons for change and to explicitly elicit confidence or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently reframed risk while validating minimization, though more double‑sided reflections could better acknowledge benefits and costs to resolve ambivalence.
