### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on self-harm and safety, moving the session pragmatically from problem exploration to a concrete safety/test plan without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative and co-constructed; the clinician solicited Liora's ideas and iteratively built a plan driven by the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered multiple options, avoided pressuring language, and left decisions and next steps with Liora, reinforcing her sense of choice and control.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively drew out client-generated safety strategies and immediate solutions but could further evoke deeper intrinsic motives, values, and longer-term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the work as a partnership, deferring to the client's expertise and asking which strategies felt most doable to test.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical change language and a small testable step, but could better amplify and summarize that change talk and use targeted probes or scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
