# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Jamal’s conflicted feelings, affirming his priorities and choices without blame. Acceptance was clear throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, complex reflections that captured Jamal’s feelings and pragmatic concerns, deepening engagement and trust. Empathy guided the session and honored his desire for efficiency.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (protecting Jamal’s record) and moved logically from values to concrete planning. Each intervention tied back to risk reduction and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a true partnership—clinician repeatedly solicited Jamal’s input and worked with him to craft practical tools. Jamal’s ideas shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered options rather than directives and respected Jamal’s choices, amplifying his sense of control over the plan. No coercion or pressure was evident.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Jamal’s own motivations and solutions through open questions, leading to concrete change language and actionable steps. Motivation came from Jamal rather than being imposed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed planning as joint problem‑solving and repeatedly checked for what sounded real to Jamal, avoiding an expert-imposing stance. Collaboration was evident in drafting texts and exit lines together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, prompting Jamal to generate specific strategies and a first-step plan (e.g., going straight home, drafting a text). The clinician helped operationalize intentions into concrete actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing minimizations and redirected toward risk reduction, which prevented escalation. However, the clinician missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and explicit exploration of ambivalence or confidence to further soften sustain talk.
