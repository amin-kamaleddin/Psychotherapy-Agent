# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validates David's context and normalizes difficulty, conveying clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and timely, accurate reflections of triggers and goals, but could use deeper emotion-focused reflections to strengthen attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with concrete next steps (quit date, meds, safety checks, logistics) and avoids drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint problem-solving, invites the patient's input, and iteratively incorporates his requests into the PCP message and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by offering multiple options, asking preferences, and repeatedly framing actions as the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits importance and links it to concrete steps, but could more systematically draw out the patient's own reasons, values, and confidence to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance and solicits preferences, though clinician-driven drafting of messages sometimes shifted task ownership away from the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change language (importance rating, quit date, treatment choices) but missed opportunities to probe reasons for change and elicit confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, but seldom uses double-sided reflections, reframing, or exploration of costs to actively soften sustain talk.
