**Comments:** Consistently conveys nonjudgmental respect and positive regard, using validations and normalizing ambivalence. Warm affirmations and supportive language promote a respectful stance throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and accurate summaries of barriers and supports. Could deepen affective exploration and use more complex reflections to uncover underlying emotions or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on re-engaging in exercise and moves from exploration to a concrete, actionable plan. Elicits barriers, options, specific days/times, and coping strategies to drive goal-directed change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly joint, using inclusive language and integrating Rafael's preferences into next steps. Co-creates the reminder text and adapts the plan to his requests, avoiding an expert-only stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering multiple options, asking permission before advising, and validating the client's right to be kind to himself. Avoids pressure and affirms Rafael's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits past strategies, supports, and implementation intentions to draw out motivation. Could probe more on internal motives, values, and personal reasons for change to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with scaling questions and small-step options, and presses for specific commitments. Missed opportunities include more frequent reflection and amplification of Rafael's change statements to solidify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and quickly pivots to manageable steps, but often echoes sustain-talk formulations rather than using double-sided or reframing reflections. Would benefit from highlighting past successes and using shifts that reduce the power of sustain talk.
