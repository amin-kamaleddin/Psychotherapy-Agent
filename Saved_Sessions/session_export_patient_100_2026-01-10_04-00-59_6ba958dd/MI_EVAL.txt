# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language throughout, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the patient's priorities and efforts without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides accurate, frequent reflections that capture ambivalence and feeling, but the style is somewhat formulaic and could probe deeper emotional meaning at times.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a steady focus on alcohol-related peer pressure and protecting grades, narrowing choices and guiding the patient toward a concrete, doable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative and co-creative, repeatedly inviting the patient's ideas and leveraging their knowledge (e.g., Sofia) to shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient autonomy with open questions, options rather than directives, and language that preserves choice and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivation and past success (AP Chem) and invites personally meaningful steps, but could further deepen and amplify intrinsic motivations and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as joint problem-solving, defers to the patient's expertise, and co-constructs the plan in a way that shares power and responsibility.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits concrete change talk and doable steps, generating hopeful language from the patient, but would benefit from more strategic reinforcement, amplification, and elicitation of commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses double-sided reflections to acknowledge doubts, yet often reflects sustain talk neutrally and misses opportunities to more actively reframe or emphasize change-focused discrepancy.
