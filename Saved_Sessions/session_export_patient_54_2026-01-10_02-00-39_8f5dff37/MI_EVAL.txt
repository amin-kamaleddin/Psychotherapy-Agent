# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, offering unconditional positive regard and normalizing concerns (e.g., thanking and supporting the patient).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid empathic listening and accurate reflections of the patient's values, but could include deeper emotional reflections and more open invitations to explore feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a strong, appropriate focus on alcohol use during pregnancy and moves the session toward clear, concrete change-oriented tasks and a practical quit plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-creates scripts and plans, tailors language to the patient's preferences, and uses inclusive consultative language throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding coercive directives, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation (asking about help and making a plan) but interaction is largely information- and solution-focused with missed opportunities to elicit deeper intrinsic motivations and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making by asking permission, offering multiple options, and co-constructing the plan with the patient based on her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and amplifies existing change talk and translates it into concrete steps, but could more strategically elicit the patient's own reasons, confidence, and envisioned outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Counters sustain talk mainly with authoritative information rather than MI techniques; would benefit from double-sided reflections, rolling with resistance, and acknowledging doubts before offering facts.
