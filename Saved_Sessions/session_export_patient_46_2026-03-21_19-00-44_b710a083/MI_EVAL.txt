# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validates Ethan and normalizes ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and helpful reflective summaries, but misses some opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of underlying concerns and sustain talk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use with a logical progression from eliciting concerns to options, rehearsal, and a tiny plan.

### Collaboration

#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out practical reasons for change and elicits change talk, but leans toward offering strategies rather than eliciting deeper personal values, scaling, or Ethan's own summaries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invites input, though the clinician sometimes shifts into an expert/problem‑solving mode by supplying many scripts instead of eliciting Ethan's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with praise, rehearsals, and concrete plans, but could use more evocative questions and scaling to deepen desire, ability, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and accepts Ethan's minimizations, but does little to strategically soften or redirect sustain talk (e.g., missed double‑sided reflections or explicit ambivalence work).
