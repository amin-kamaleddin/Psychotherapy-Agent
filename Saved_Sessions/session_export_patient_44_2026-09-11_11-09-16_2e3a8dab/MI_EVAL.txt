### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm—validates progress, normalizes setbacks, and avoids blame throughout the session.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and timely, specific reflections that capture both Erik’s behaviors and emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on increasing gym attendance and protecting momentum, repeatedly narrowing to concrete, actionable steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
