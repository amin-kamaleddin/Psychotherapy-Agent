# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with frequent validations and normalization, avoiding blame or moralizing language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, frequent reflections that matched the patient's affect and content, keeping the patient engaged and feeling heard and safe.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on substance use, safety, and immediate coping with concrete, actionable steps and appropriate safety checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session read as a genuine joint effort—invited the patient's preferences, co-created scripts and plans, and repeatedly checked in about next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before advice, and framing suggestions to preserve the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some behavioral commitments but relied more on offering solutions than on open, evocative questions to draw out the patient's intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the clinician and patient as collaborators through option-giving, permission asks, and offers to stay with the patient during steps, reinforcing shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforced and encouraged small change behaviors (e.g., sending a text, calling a helpline) but missed opportunities to elicit and amplify the patient's own change language and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk without escalating it and offered alternatives, though could more often use double-sided or strategic reflections to explicitly shift ambivalence.
