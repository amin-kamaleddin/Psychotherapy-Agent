**Comments:** Affirms choice and reduces pressure (fresh‑start framing, asks permission). Some directive/evaluative language (e.g., “I’m proud you tried”) slightly reduces pure autonomy support.

**Comments:** Asks which steps feel doable and elicits brief commitments, but spends more time offering solutions than drawing out Dante’s reasons, values, or past facilitators.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 1 [ ] 2 [ ] 3 [ ] 4 [x] 5 [ ]
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful; uses “we” language and solicits preferences. Partnership is weakened when clinician provides scripted wording/rituals rather than inviting patient generation.

**Comments:** Elicits small change talk (agreement to try, reporting an ~8‑minute walk) but does not consistently evoke, reflect, or amplify statements like “I used to work out pretty good.”
