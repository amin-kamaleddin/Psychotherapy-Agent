# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental (reminds of client’s values, frames plan as an "experiment"/"short trial"). Normalizes imperfection and demonstrates steady unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Several accurate reflections and summaries that capture content and practical concerns (time, energy). Missed opportunities to deepen emotional texture (e.g., lifelong commitment, “cautious optimism,” desire for connection that feels loving rather than a chore).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on evening connection behavior with logical sequencing (options → choice → script → barrier planning → readiness → scheduling → homework). Strong goal-directed planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invites client input, co‑constructs the plan, defers to client wording, checks back and asks what client needs from clinician. Genuine partnership in decision‑making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicit support for choice and voluntary participation (frames as experiment, asks permission). Uses backup/rescheduling options to preserve client control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client motivations, preferences, concrete implementation details, and signs of success. Uses readiness ruler and explores what would increase commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision‑making and seeks client language, but occasionally supplies full clinician scripts rather than first eliciting the client’s own phrasing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits desire/reason and preparatory commitment language (specific days, alarm, backup). Could more consistently amplify and link change talk to values (double‑sided reflections, brief explicit commitment statement).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validates reluctance (not another task), reframes with simplification and contingency plans, avoids arguing and successfully redirects to manageable solutions.
