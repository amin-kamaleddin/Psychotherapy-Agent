# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful stance and honored Richard's choices throughout the session. Unconditional positive regard was clear in affirmations and in avoiding blame or confrontation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that often mirrored Richard's language and relational concerns. Some reflections felt formulaic and the clinician could probe deeper into underlying emotions like fear or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on smoking behavior and moved the conversation toward a concrete, testable plan. The clinician effectively elicited specific steps and supports to operationalize change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with frequent solicitation of Richard's input and co-construction of the plan. The clinician avoided an expert-driven stance and respected the client's preferences in decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly reinforced—choice was emphasized and boundaries with the wife were honored. The clinician offered support options while leaving decision-making authority with Richard.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivations such as concern for the wife and prior success, and used these to form a small experiment. Could strengthen evocation by drawing out more personally owned reasons, values, confidence, and longer-term motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative and positioned Richard as the decision-maker, co-creating specifics like timing and follow‑up. Power was shared and the client's expertise guided the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively highlighted and reinforced change language and elicited a concrete plan and small steps. Missed opportunities include further amplifying intrinsic motives and eliciting stronger commitment or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and redirected to strengths and possibilities, accepting Richard's stance while pivoting to choice. Could use more double-sided reflections and varied softening tactics instead of some repeated neutral phrasing.
