# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validation, normalizing the patient's distress and offering non-pressuring, small options. Warm, supportive stance was maintained throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, naming emotions and summarizing fears to build rapport. Could deepen empathy by probing the emotional texture (shame/fear of judgment) and using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on moving from avoidance toward small, concrete actions and repeatedly redirected to doable steps. Interventions were tailored to the patient's expressed readiness and did not drift off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a true joint enterprise: clinician invited input, listened, co-constructed scripts, and practiced with the patient instead of dictating. The plan was adjusted based on the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by presenting multiple options, inviting the patient to choose, and giving permission to stop if overwhelmed. Language and offers reinforced the patient's sense of control and agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's motivations and barriers through open invitations and co-creation of a message, drawing on patient-generated content. Could strengthen evocation by eliciting personal reasons for change, importance/confidence ratings, and steps toward commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveyed a collaborative stance and shared power, asking which option felt most doable and following the patient's lead. Co-writing and rehearsing the message exemplified strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change-oriented language via small options, rehearsals, and practice, helping convert ambivalence into manageable steps. Could further amplify change talk and ask about reasons for acting now to deepen motivation and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by breaking worries into specific, manageable steps and offering alternatives. Sustain talk remained present, and using more MI-specific strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections, summarizing ambivalence) could further soften it.
