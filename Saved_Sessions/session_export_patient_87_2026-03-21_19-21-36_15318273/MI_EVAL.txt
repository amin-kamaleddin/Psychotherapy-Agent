### Acceptance

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behaviors with concrete implementation questions and action‑oriented planning, keeping the session on task and goal‑directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine teamwork by inviting Marco to co‑author the plan, checking preferences before offering solutions, and using inclusive language to negotiate decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserved Marco's agency by offering options, asking permission, and prompting him to choose and adapt plans while normalizing slips and restarts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some intrinsic motivation through goals and confidence ratings, but relied heavily on clinician‑provided strategies and did not sufficiently explore Marco's personal reasons, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally shared power and invited Marco's input on specifics, though at times offered multiple prescriptive solutions that risked leaning toward an expert role rather than explicitly framing them as options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and scaffolded some change talk and commitments with scaling and concrete actions, but missed opportunities to amplify, deepen, and explore why change matters to Marco to strengthen desire and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward strategies, though could more often use double‑sided reflections or explicitly explore the legitimacy of sustain talk before pivoting to solutions.
