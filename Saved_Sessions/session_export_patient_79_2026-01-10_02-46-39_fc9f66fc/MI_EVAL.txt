# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validating the patient's preferences and affirming small efforts. No shaming or blaming occurred despite sarcasm and defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, acknowledging frustration and minimizing confrontation. Missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional tone (e.g., anger, overwhelm) behind the patient's sarcasm.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, sustained focus on the target behavior (inhaler use cue) and related safety contingencies without drifting. Summaries and plan iterations maintained the agenda toward measurable change and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a partner in joint problem-solving, using the patient's language to co-create practical strategies and inviting feedback. Framed suggestions as trials and adjustments rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, validated the patient's right to decline lectures, and avoided pressure or coercion. Emphasized patient ownership of the plan and decision to check in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited commitment language by asking which option felt doable and requesting a follow-up report, leading to a concrete plan. Did not sufficiently probe intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence underpinning the change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveyed shared expertise and power-sharing by offering options and asking the patient to choose a doable plan. Co-created actionable steps and invited the patient to report back for adjustments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked and reinforced concrete change talk around a specific behavior and amplified commitment into a plan. Focused mainly on action planning rather than deepening desire, reasons, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided argumentation and repeatedly pivoted away from sustain talk through validation and practical options. Rarely used double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence, which could further reduce sustain talk.
