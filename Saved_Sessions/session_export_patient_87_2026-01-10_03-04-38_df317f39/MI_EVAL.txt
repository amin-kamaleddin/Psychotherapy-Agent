# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming—repeatedly validates Marco's efforts and values, creating a safe space for disclosure.

### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Session is largely co‑constructed with checks about preferences and wording, though the clinician sometimes leads specifics (times, draft text) more than the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

### Partnership

**Comments:** Collaborative tone and invitations to shape the plan are present, yet clinician-driven structuring of details slightly reduces client-led generation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change talk (plans, rising confidence) and asks for specifics, but misses some opportunities to further amplify desire and reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers and plans for contingencies which redirects sustain talk toward solutions, though double-sided reflections and explicit normalization could be used more often.
