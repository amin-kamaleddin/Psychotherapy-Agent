# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the patient's worth while praising past successes. Language is nonjudgmental and supportive, creating a safe environment for planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring ambivalence and specific fears. Could deepen reflections to capture emotional nuance (e.g., identity loss, shame, grief) for greater empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with a concrete two-week work-based trial and actionable steps. Questions and reflections consistently return to planning and sharpening the experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative, co-constructing the plan and brainstorming tactics together while deferring final choices to the patient. The clinician invites the patient's preferences at every step and offers support without taking over.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports the patient's sense of choice by offering options, using experimental language, and acknowledging the right to decide. This preserves autonomy and reduces pressure while encouraging voluntary engagement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's internal resources and past successes (soda rule) to draw motivation from within. Could more consistently use questions that probe personal reasons, importance, and confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint exploration, inviting input and co-creating a manageable experiment that builds on the patient's lived experience. The clinician fosters shared decision-making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, leading to a concrete plan and expressions of confidence. Would benefit from more frequent amplification and reflective summaries to convert intentions into stronger explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk respectfully and uses double-sided framing to validate ambivalence without judgment. Could more often pivot to strategic reflections that highlight discrepancies or costs of staying the same to further soften sustain talk.
