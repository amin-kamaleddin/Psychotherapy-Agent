# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth (e.g., “Whatever you noticed is useful — no judgment.”; repeated affirmations of progress). Praise was specific to Samuel’s actions (journal, reduced cigarettes).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking (paraphrased triggers, supports, and ambivalence). Reflections captured emotional stance (honest/cautious confidence) and offered support.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained focus on smoking reduction and the lunch-specific plan; questions and interventions advanced a concrete, testable action without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction framed as joint problem-solving: offered options, asked preferences (which line fits), and invited Samuel to choose and modify wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician honored choice and avoided pressure (nonjudgmental language, asked permission before role‑play/scripting). Elicited Samuel’s preferred approach rather than imposing solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited Samuel’s own motivations, reasons, and plans with open questions (triggers, small steps, confidence scale) and built on his self‑statements of progress.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered true partnership—co‑created scripts/options, offered to tweak language to fit Samuel’s style, and offered supports contingent on his choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively reinforced and amplified change talk (highlighted journal, reduction, lunch plan), asked for specific next steps (ask coworker, set reminder) to consolidate intent.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged concerns about “caving” without reinforcing them and redirected to problem‑solving (scripts, gum, headphones, rehearsal) to reduce sustain talk.
