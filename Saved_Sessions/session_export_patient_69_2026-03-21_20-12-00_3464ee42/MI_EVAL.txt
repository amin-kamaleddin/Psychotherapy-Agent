# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, using validations and appreciative statements without blame. This warmth fits the client's guarded stance and fosters safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses timely reflective listening and accurate summaries that help the client elaborate their experience. Could incorporate more complex/affective reflections to name deeper frustration or resentment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on increasing meaningful engagement tied to employment and moves the session toward a measurable behavioral experiment. Each step logically builds to the 20‑minute attendance test and unsigned message.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as joint problem-solving, co‑creating the message, logistics, and scripts with the patient. Solicits input at each decision point and adapts to the patient's blunt style.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and control, offers options, and respects the patient's wish for anonymity without pressure. These behaviors reliably support the client's sense of agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete ideas about what would make activities meaningful and secures a small commitment tied to the client's preference. Would strengthen evocation by probing intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence (e.g., scaling or 'why now' questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a consistently collaborative stance, inviting preferences and deferring to the patient on wording, timing, and follow‑up. The plan is co‑owned by clinician and patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits specific, behavioral change language and nets a testable commitment (20 minutes and unsigned message). Could more often amplify and reflect emergent change talk and explore reasons/confidence to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, acknowledges skepticism, and pivots to practical experiments that limit resistance. Could use more double‑sided reflections to link sustain talk with any flickers of desire for change.
