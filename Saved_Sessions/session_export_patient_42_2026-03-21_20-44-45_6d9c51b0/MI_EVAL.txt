# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, using validating statements and affirmations throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking around ambivalence and social connection, but reflections stay more pragmatic and could probe deeper emotional meaning (belonging, identity).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction/cessation with a concrete, time-bound action plan (one smoke-free break at 2:30 PM) and preparation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative—offers menus of options, asks permission to role-play, integrates Elise's choices, and treats her as an equal partner in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice and offers options rather than directives, checking Elise’s preferences and leaving decisions in her control to support ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivations (health goals, confidence rating) and invites reasons for change, but could deepen evocation by exploring intrinsic values and amplifying change language more often.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership evidenced by co-creating the plan, inviting input on lines and supports, and offering joint rehearsal and role-play to share power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and supports change talk through goal-setting and a behavioral experiment, but could more strongly reflect and amplify desire, reasons, and past successes before moving to solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolls with sustain talk and offers alternatives to protect connection, though occasionally validates continued social smoking and could use more double-sided or selectively reinforcing reflections to reduce sustain talk.
