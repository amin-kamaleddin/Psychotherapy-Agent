# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful: normalized concerns, validated feelings, and honored hesitancy while offering alternatives and accommodations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and targeted reflections about work-related fears and tailored suggestions; could use more extended reflections to explore the emotional/practical meaning of his ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on cholesterol management with concrete monitoring and follow-up plans (4‑week plan, CK/LFTs, 6–12 week recheck); minimal drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engaged the patient as a partner by soliciting preferences and co-creating the 4‑week plan and notes, though substantial clinician-provided medical recommendations occasionally shifted toward expert guidance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly affirmed patient choice and control, offered multiple alternatives (lifestyle first, alternate dosing, non‑statins) and tailored suggestions to his preference not to commit to meds.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Limited systematic elicitation of intrinsic motivations or values; relied more on providing information and problem-solving than on drawing out or amplifying the patient's personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned the encounter as collaborative by asking what the patient wanted and inviting agreement on plans, though clinician expertise sometimes dominated agenda-setting.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Created openings and obtained a concrete month trial, but did not consistently elicit or amplify the patient's own reasons for change, with many responses remaining informational rather than invitational.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing concerns, offering monitoring and alternatives, and reframing resistance; could further use double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence while nudging toward change.
