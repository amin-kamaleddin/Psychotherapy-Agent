# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes difficulty, tying affirmations to the patient’s context to convey genuine acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent empathic reflections that capture both content and feeling, validating the patient’s ambivalence and exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and medication strategy, steering conversations back to actionable solutions when barriers arise.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—solicits patient input at multiple decision points and integrates responses into a shared, practical plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports patient choice and control by offering alternatives, asking preferences, and inviting the patient to shape a realistic plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical motivations and a concrete plan but emphasizes logistics over deeper intrinsic reasons; could probe values, confidence, and past successes more.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as a partnership, checking back for alignment and distributing decision-making to the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified change talk that produced specific commitments (patch, gum, timing), but could deepen by exploring personal reasons, confidence, and prior successes to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects to collaborative problem-solving, which reduces its influence; could further soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections and exploring emotional content.
