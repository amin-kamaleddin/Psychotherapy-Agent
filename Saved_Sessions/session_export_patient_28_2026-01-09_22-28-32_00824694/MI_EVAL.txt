# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates respect and positive regard through empathic normalization and supportive language, never criticizing or minimizing. This warm, nonjudgmental stance is sustained across the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking capture goals and barriers accurately and timely. Could deepen emotional content and use more complex reflections to explore the meaning of values like “being around for them.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing short walks and moves from exploration to concrete, actionable planning. Each turn ties back to measurable steps without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative with co‑constructed plans, invitations, and offers to draft reminders or practice wording. The clinician follows Dante's pace and treats him as an equal partner throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressuring language. These moves reinforce Dante's sense of agency and ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Dante's own motivations and resources (energy for kids, past successes) through open questions and scaling. The clinician builds on client-generated reasons and invitations rather than supplying motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently acts as a collaborative partner by offering choices, checking what feels doable, and inviting co‑creation of plans. Language positions Dante as the expert in his life and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk well, using reflections, micro‑experiments, and confidence scaling to draw out reasons and abilities. Could more often ask for elaboration on strengths and explicitly amplify or summarize change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk non‑judgmentally by acknowledging barriers, normalizing setbacks, and offering contingency plans. Would benefit from more double‑sided reflections and strategic pivots that reframe sustain talk into manageable experiments.
