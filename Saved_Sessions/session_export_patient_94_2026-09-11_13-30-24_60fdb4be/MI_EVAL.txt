### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Maria’s experience, validated frustrations without judgment, and honored her limits (e.g., refusing daily steroids).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, specific reflective listening that captured Maria’s ambivalence and mirrored her concerns, lowering resistance and inviting collaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, concrete behavioral target (carrying/accessing the rescue inhaler) and moved stepwise toward implementation and follow‑up without drifting into lecture.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was co‑constructed: clinician elicited Maria’s preferences, checked acceptability, and adapted the plan to her limits, allowing her ideas to shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Maria’s choice and control, respected her refusal of daily steroids, and offered options that reinforced her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical, action‑oriented motivation (carrying inhaler, spares, text reminder) but missed opportunities to draw out deeper intrinsic motives or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated true partnership by asking what would work for Maria, offering options rather than directives, and treating her as the expert in her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified several pieces of change talk around concrete steps, but did not sufficiently explore underlying importance or bolster confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
