# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, validating Rafael's mixed feelings without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries of supports and worries, though reflections stayed more cognitive/behavioral than exploring deeper emotions like discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on re‑engaging in physical activity and skillfully narrowed ambivalence to a specific, measurable plan while preserving client choice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and co‑constructed the plan, but offering multiple solution options early sometimes subtly steered the client instead of first eliciting his ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Rafael's sense of choice and control by offering options and inviting him to choose and adapt a plan without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical ideas and past supports, but could more often elicit intrinsic motivators, use scaling, and seek stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Rafael as the expert and shared decision‑making, asking him to select specifics and integrating his preferences into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete options and follow‑ups, though more reflective amplification and scaling could strengthen and consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing doubts and redirecting to supports; using double‑sided reflections and selective reflections of sustain talk could further reduce its pull.
