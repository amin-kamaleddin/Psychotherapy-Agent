### Acceptance

**Comments:** Uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes needs while offering practical accommodations. Could add more individualized affirmations and explicitly normalize ambivalence to reach a 5.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused target (increase physical activity) and aligns each suggestion with that goal using SMART planning and specific routines. Avoids drifting and keeps the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Frames the work as cooperative, offers adjustments, and checks scheduling/medical issues, showing responsiveness. Collaboration leans clinician-led; stronger co-creation would come from eliciting Amira's ideas before presenting solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Aligns the plan with Amira's motivations but does little to draw out or deepen her own change language. Missed evocative questions to explore values, reasons, and stronger DARN statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks preferences and invites adjustments, indicating some partnership, but the interaction is largely clinician-led and prescriptive. Partnership would improve by soliciting Amira's expertise and ideas earlier and more often.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Reinforces change by setting a SMART goal and doable routine, supporting change-oriented behavior. However, there was limited use of questions or reflections to elicit and amplify Amira's own change talk (e.g., exploring why she's an 8/10).
