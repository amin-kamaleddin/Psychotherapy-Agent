# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys warm, nonjudgmental regard throughout with repeated affirmations, normalization, and respectful language that supports the patient’s choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with frequent reflections and summaries that validate ambivalence and make the patient feel understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing late‑night snacking and consistently guides conversation toward specific, measurable action steps and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works jointly with the patient to build the plan, solicits preferences, integrates the patient’s language, and offers options rather than prescriptions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reinforces patient choice by offering options, checking which feels doable, and framing plans as the patient’s own decisions without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and converts them into concrete plans, but could probe deeper into intrinsic values and more often amplify the patient’s stated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert, invites collaboration on strategy selection, and shares decision-making power throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with targeted questions and requests for specifics that yield concrete actions, but could more consistently amplify and highlight the patient’s change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and redirects toward strategies without arguing, though more use of double‑sided reflections could better juxtapose ambivalence toward change.
