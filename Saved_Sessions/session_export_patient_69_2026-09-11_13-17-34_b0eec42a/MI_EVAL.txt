# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, reflected skepticism without challenge, and honored the client's requests and boundaries throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provided accurate, timely empathic reflections and paraphrased the client's concrete fixes, demonstrating careful listening and tuning to the client's guarded affect and perspective.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the conversation tightly focused on a pragmatic target behavior and steered toward a concrete, testable two‑week text trial with clear next steps and logistics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a facilitator and co‑creator of the plan, repeatedly offering options and deferring to the client's preferences so the plan originated from the client's priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly reinforced choice and reversibility, accepted the client's decision to send the message and to 'stay out,' and reiterated willingness to act only if asked.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked pragmatic, behaviorally focused motivation and commitment language but did not sufficiently explore deeper values, longer‑term motives, or confidence levels.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established a collaborative stance by inviting the client's decisions at multiple junctures and consistently honoring his chosen role in implementing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced specific change talk, converting dissatisfaction into a clear, reversible plan and preserving the client's ownership of the next steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk and reframed it neutrally, but missed some opportunities for double‑sided reflections or explicit normalization of ambivalence to further diffuse resistance.
