# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizing ambivalence and validating the patient’s practical ideas without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking match the patient’s feelings and ambivalence, encouraging further disclosure and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear focus on reducing injury risk while preserving social life, repeatedly returning to concrete, harm-reduction strategies and a single implementable behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative, inviting the patient’s input, integrating their ideas into the plan, and co-constructing steps rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently offers choices, normalizes slips, and frames the patient as decision-maker, reinforcing the patient's right and ability to choose what feels doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicits the patient’s reasons and concrete indicators of success but could deepen evocation by using scaling questions and more amplification of the patient’s change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician fosters a strong partnership by seeking the patient’s ideas, inviting choice, and negotiating a shared, practical plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good use of open questions that elicit concrete change language and a specific commitment, though the clinician could better amplify and summarize change talk and ask confidence/commitment scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoids reinforcing sustain talk and pivots to harm reduction, but could more actively use double-sided reflections and explore the function and costs of drinking to further tip the balance.
