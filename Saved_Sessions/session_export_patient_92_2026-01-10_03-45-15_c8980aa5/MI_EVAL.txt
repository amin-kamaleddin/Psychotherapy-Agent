# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and uses respectful, nonjudgmental language to create a safe atmosphere for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and accurate paraphrase of Marek's position, but occasionally misses chances to probe deeper feelings or the emotional meaning of social drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on weekend/social alcohol use and consistently moves toward concrete, behaviorally specific change steps and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint endeavor, invites Marek's input at each step, offers options rather than prescriptions, and co-constructs the implementation plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports Marek's sense of choice—asks permission before advice, normalizes imperfection, and repeatedly lets him choose among strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Marek's own motivations and resources using scaling questions and targeted prompts, drawing out his reasons and concrete plans for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats Marek as the expert on his life, uses collaborative language, and invites him to select and tailor strategies to fit his context.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and shapes change talk and generates concrete plans, but could better amplify, summarize, and consolidate those statements into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and often redirects sustain talk toward change, though more double-sided reflections and strategic summaries could further soften sustain language.
