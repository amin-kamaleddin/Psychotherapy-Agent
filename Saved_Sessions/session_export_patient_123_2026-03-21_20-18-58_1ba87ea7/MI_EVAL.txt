# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizing the patient's reasons for attendance and avoiding labeling or criticism across the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing content and context (barriers, values) and responding to fatigue and time pressure with understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on making time for reading/outdoors, guiding the session logically from priority identification to a specific, actionable plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructs the plan with the patient throughout, using language that invites shared problem-solving and ensuring decisions about supports and follow-up are patient-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice by asking permission, offering options, and keeping responsibility for the plan with the patient without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations, abilities, reasons, and specific steps using open questions and scaling to surface internal motivation and resources.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as an equal collaborator, repeatedly seeking preferences and offering choices that demonstrate genuine power-sharing and respect for her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with open, change-oriented questions, eliciting concrete planning and commitment language (alarms, specific book/time).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk and reframes barriers with accessible options, but could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and further elicit change leaning.
