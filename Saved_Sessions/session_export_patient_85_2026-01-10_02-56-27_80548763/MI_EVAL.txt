# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—validated setbacks and framed the juice discovery as useful information rather than blame. Tone was supportive and nonjudgmental throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, acknowledging frustration and pride. Could deepen emotional attunement with more complex reflections naming mixed feelings (pride, frustration, attachment to OJ).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing orange juice intake with all questions and suggestions tied to that target behavior. Kept momentum toward a concrete action plan and scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as genuine joint problem-solving—clinician offered options and consistently solicited Marco's input. Decisions (phrasing, reminders, tracking) were co-constructed and driven by the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Marco's choice and control by presenting options and asking what felt doable. Avoided pressure and checked preferences for reminders and tracking support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled at eliciting Marco's own motivations and strategies through prompts (confidence scaling, phrasing for his wife, noticing signs of success). Drew out concrete reasons, plans, and commitments rather than imposing solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance with shared decision-making and frequent invitations for Marco's phrasing and choices. Clinician framed suggestions as offers and supported client ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk using specific prompts (small doable changes, confidence ruler, concrete wording) and reinforced commitment to steps like tracking and backups. Marco generated clear statements of desire and reasons to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and redirected toward practical strategies, but missed chances for double-sided reflections that explicitly validate enjoyment while highlighting costs. More rolling with sustain talk could further soften resistance.
