# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect through normalizing statements and affirmations, honoring Jordan’s limits and validating care for his grandparents.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective‑taking with accurate reflections and mirrored language, though deeper probing of emotional content and more complex emotional reflections would strengthen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained clearly focused on the flu vaccine and practical next steps, consistently guiding toward actionable, vaccine‑related options without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently treated the interaction as a joint effort—offering choices, co‑creating materials, and letting Jordan’s preferences shape the plan rather than instructing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supported choice and control by honoring the 'not promising' stance, offering options, asking permission, and framing steps as voluntary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively drew motivation from Jordan’s value of protecting his grandparents and elicited change reasons, but could have probed deeper into values and used scaling to amplify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear collaborative stance with shared decision‑making—asking which options felt doable, co‑creating the handout and text, and inviting Jordan to direct pace and format.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk around family protection and small steps, though reflections could have amplified specific change statements and checked importance/confidence to build strength.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed it into manageable steps, but missed opportunities to use double‑sided reflections to more explicitly summarize ambivalence and pivot toward change.
