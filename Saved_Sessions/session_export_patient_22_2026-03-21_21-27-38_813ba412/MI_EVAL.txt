# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed warm, nonjudgmental support and affirmed Camila's strengths and resilience; occasional explicit normalization of relapse or past attempts could further strengthen this stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, layered reflections and validation captured both concrete consequences and felt ambivalence, fostering safety and deeper disclosure throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing alcohol use and linked discussion to specific goals, moving the conversation toward actionable steps and rehearsed strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated as an equal partner—asking which options felt easiest, co-creating the plan, and offering to role-play—demonstrating genuine joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Camila's choice and control by offering multiple options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure, preserving client ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Camila's internal motivations and supports (readiness ruler, probing what would move her higher) and drew change reasons from her rather than imposing them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated shared decision-making, invited client solutions, and refined plans together, reflecting a collaborative, power-sharing stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and supported change talk through readiness scaling and specific planning, but could more often amplify and summarize Camila's change language to bolster commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responded empathically to barriers and offered alternatives that reduced sustain talk, yet could increase use of double-sided reflections and selective reflections of sustain followed by change amplification to further soften resistance.
