# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed nonjudgmental, respectful regard—validating ambivalence, honoring cultural values, and avoiding shaming language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries of competing values, though some reflections were brief and could probe deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy with questions, suggestions, and rehearsals tied to concrete behavior-change steps and a one-week experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative—work framed as joint problem-solving, with the patient’s input solicited and plans/scripts co-created and practiced together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before sharing information or role-playing, and reinforcing Isabella’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Isabella’s motivations and practical solutions well (NA wine, partner support), but could have probed more to deepen intrinsic motivators and amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power was effectively shared—clinician offered choices, invited preferences, and engaged in co-creating and rehearsing scripts, reinforcing an equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk and co-created a concrete experiment, yet missed some opportunities to amplify change statements and elicit explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and reframed sustain talk into options and experiments, but could strengthen technique with more double-sided reflections and strategic pivots to change talk.
