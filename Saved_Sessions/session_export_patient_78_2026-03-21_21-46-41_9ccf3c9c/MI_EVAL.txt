# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently shows unconditional positive regard, validates frustrations, and avoids judgment or lecturing; tone is warm and nonstigmatizing throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and paraphrase of triggers and context, but could deepen empathic reflections by exploring underlying emotions and meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, collaborative focus on smoking reduction with concrete, time-bound tests, measurable check-ins, and specific behavioral strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly invites the client's input and co-constructs the plan (timing, reminders, backups), demonstrating equal-partner teamwork and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly emphasizes choice and control, respects the client's preference for how to report back, and avoids pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client-generated steps and readiness with open questions and planning, but could more consistently probe values and longer-term motives to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions expertise alongside the client, asks permission and preferences, and defers to the client's choices to maintain a strong partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk through readiness scaling, offering options, and reinforcing the chosen plan, though it could further strengthen desire and importance by linking to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and rolls with resistance, validating limited readiness and offering low-pressure options, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections to reduce sustain talk further.
