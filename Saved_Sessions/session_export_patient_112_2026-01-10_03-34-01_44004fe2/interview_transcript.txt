PSYCHIATRIST: Good questions — practical and realistic.

Medications:
- Varenicline (Chantix) and bupropion (Zyban) blunt cravings; varenicline often gives the strongest reduction in urge, bupropion can curb weight gain while taking it. 
- NRT (patch, gum, lozenge, inhaler) eases withdrawal; combo patch + gum/lozenge is very effective.
- Side effects: varenicline (nausea, sleep/vivid dreams), bupropion (insomnia, dry mouth; seizure risk if predisposed), NRT (local irritation). Always review with your doctor.

Withdrawal:
- Starts within hours, peaks 2–3 days, acute symptoms often lessen in 1–3 weeks; cravings can recur for months but get easier.

Affordability:
- Generic meds, insurance coverage, state quitlines often provide free counseling/NRT, coupons, pharmacy savings.

Being around smokers:
- Set boundaries, have gum/lozenge or quick nicotine dosing, use a 10-minute “delay” + deep breaths, plan exits, and use short refusal lines (“I’m quitting—can we hang out smoke-free?”).

Weight/control tips:
- Higher-protein snacks, fiber, water, small frequent meals, and brief daily activity (even 10-minute walks, stairs, or 5–10 minute strength moves) protect metabolism without major time/cost.
