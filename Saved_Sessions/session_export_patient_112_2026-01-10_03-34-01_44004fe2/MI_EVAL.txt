### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental support with frequent normalizations and acknowledgement of practical barriers; never blames or minimizes Rosa’s concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections of Rosa’s feelings and fears show good perspective-taking, but the clinician often moves quickly into information and solutions rather than deeper, affect-focused exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and concrete next steps, repeatedly returning to a quit plan and practical barriers.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving and seeks Rosa’s input, but the interaction leans toward clinician-led expertise and does not deeply integrate her past strategies or co-create the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, avoiding pressure, and inviting Rosa to decide her preferred approach and quit date.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some preferences and affirmations but largely supplies information and solutions, missing repeated opportunities to draw out internal reasons, values, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites preferences and offers options so Rosa’s input matters, yet could strengthen true power-sharing by asking more about her ideas and past successes to co-construct the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Affirms intentions and elicits limited change talk, but does not use open, evocative questions to amplify Rosa’s own motivational language or link it to values and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes fears, but relies on informational responses rather than double-sided or complex reflections that would gently tip toward change.
