# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician established a nonjudgmental, validating stance from the start and consistently normalized Farid’s concerns, demonstrating unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking matched Farid's affect and ambivalence, with nuanced reflections that captured both desire and barriers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with concrete targets and repeatedly redirected conversation toward actionable planning and commitment steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treated the work as shared problem-solving, offering options, inviting choice, and using Farid's selections to co-create and refine a feasible plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and affirming that any steps taken were Farid's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician elicited Farid's own motivations and ideas using scaling and probing questions, amplifying internally generated reasons and practical low-friction strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A collaborative stance was maintained with frequent invitations for Farid's input and joint decision-making, honoring his preferences in selecting the next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicited and reinforced change talk by asking for specific steps, reflecting and amplifying plan language, and using confidence ratings to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and normalized without argument, using double-sided reflections and gentle redirection to contingency planning that reduced resistance.
