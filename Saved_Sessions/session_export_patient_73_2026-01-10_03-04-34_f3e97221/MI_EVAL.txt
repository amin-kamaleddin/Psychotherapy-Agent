# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Linda’s experience, avoiding blame and repeatedly affirming her strengths (e.g., past month off).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that fostered understanding, but sometimes moved quickly into information-dense advice rather than deeper affective reflection. More complex emotional reflections (e.g., exploring 'being a burden' or relapse frustration) would deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on tobacco cessation throughout, moving from exploration to concrete action (med choices, quit date, patch timing, and check-ins) without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was a joint problem‑solving effort, repeatedly inviting Linda’s input on meds, quit date, and role‑plays, and coordinating plans with her prescriber to co‑construct the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before giving information, and checking preferences for dosing and check‑in times, reinforcing Linda’s agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and built on Linda’s internal motivations (health, money, not being a burden), using a confidence ruler and past successes to draw out change reasons tied to specific actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Linda as an equal partner with collaborative language and frequent choice points, allowing her preferences to shape the plan and confirming that actions 'sound okay.'

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk through open questions, affirmations, a confidence ruler, and requests for concrete commitments (quit date, patch start, weekly check‑ins).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to coping and planning, but missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections or selectively reflecting sustain talk to more directly resolve ambivalence.
