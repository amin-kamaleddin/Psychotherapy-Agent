# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, validated frustration, and honored boundaries while supporting harm-reduction steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and helpful reflections and acknowledged the patient's preference for practical help; could probe deeper into emotional subtext and use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking and making visits useful, steering toward concrete, measurable steps without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative—co-created scripts, invited role-play, and provided a written plan per the patient's request.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, respecting 'not ready to quit,' and framing suggestions as optional while validating conditional commitment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited concrete behavioral commitments and logistical reasons but largely focused on problem-solving rather than drawing out intrinsic motivations, values, or importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established and maintained a strong partnership by asking what the patient wanted, offering choices, and treating her as the expert on what would work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific change and commitment language for harm-reduction steps but missed opportunities to amplify internal reasons and values behind those statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently avoided reinforcing sustain talk, rolled with resistance, validated frustration, and reframed the interaction toward practical options.
