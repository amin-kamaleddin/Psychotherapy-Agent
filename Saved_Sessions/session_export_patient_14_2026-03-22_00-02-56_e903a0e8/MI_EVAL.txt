# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes frustrations, and validates the client's constraints and priorities. Could slightly increase individualized praise (e.g., affirming insight or past successes).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and uses the patient's language to show understanding. Could deepen empathic responding with more complex reflections that probe underlying emotions and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on time‑efficient behavior changes and moves logically from values to specific, measurable actions. The session avoids drifting and keeps momentum toward concrete goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem‑solving and explicitly solicits the patient's input to co‑construct the plan. Offers suggestions but defers to the patient's choices, indicating true partnership rather than top‑down advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly invites choice, offers options instead of directives, and explicitly accepts the patient's preferred pace. Language consistently reinforces the patient's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's motivations and links them to concrete, feasible actions using targeted evoking questions. Draws out values and personal signs of success to shape endorsed behavior changes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative, power‑sharing stance by asking for the patient's preferences and leveraging their expertise about their schedule. The patient’s selections meaningfully shape the intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk through evoking questions and reflections, leading to clear commitment language and implementation intentions. Prompts and summaries amplify practical, doable plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes sustain talk into realistic, manageable planning, reducing resistance. Missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections and reframing sustain talk as strengths to resolve ambivalence more explicitly.
