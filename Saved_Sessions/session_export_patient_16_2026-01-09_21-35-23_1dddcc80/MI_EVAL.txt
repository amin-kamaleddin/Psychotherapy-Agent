# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating the client's perspective and avoiding blame. Warmth and normalization set a respectful tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening that captured both the social value of vaping and concrete concerns about team/school. Reflections and follow-ups showed nuanced perspective-taking and built rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on vaping and steered the session toward concrete behavior-change steps (triggers, strategies, start date, backups). The clinician repeatedly returned the conversation to the target behavior and goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a joint enterprise, invited client input on strategies and supports, and co-created the plan based on the client's preferences. The interaction read as client-centered rather than clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supported choice and control with permission requests, offering options instead of directives, and honoring the client's preferences about who to involve. Language avoided pressure and reinforced agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the client's own reasons, confidence rating, and a concrete start date through evocative questions and prompts. Pulled motivation and commitment from the client rather than supplying reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing by asking permission, offering choices, and checking agreement before documenting or sharing the plan. The clinician treated the client as the primary expert and integrated their ideas into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with targeted open questions and summaries, converting ambivalence into specific reasons, strategies, and a plan. Reinforcements and affirmations strengthened the client's commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward change using neutral reflections and planning, which prevented escalation. However, there were missed opportunities for stronger MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or selectively following sustain talk immediately with change language) to further tip the balance.
