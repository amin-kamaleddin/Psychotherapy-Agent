# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized the client’s urgency and frustration throughout the session, avoiding judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and summaries captured both content and affect, reducing distance; could have probed slightly deeper into underlying fears.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a tight, appropriate focus on completing community service hours and concrete logistical barriers, with every question and reflection tied to actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative joint problem‑solving using inclusive language and iterative refinement; the clinician actively invited the client to shape the script and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered multiple options and asked the client’s preference, avoiding pressure and reinforcing the client’s control and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out practical, instrumental reasons for action (reduced anxiety, avoiding court) but explored limited deeper personal values that could strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine power‑sharing by deferring to the client’s preferences and inviting choice about format and content, respecting the client’s expertise about what will feel doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified change talk around completing hours and reduced nervousness with evocative questions, but did not systematically shape stronger commitment language or use scaling/timing questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged avoidance without arguing and redirected to low‑barrier solutions (scripts, bullets), though missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections and explicit reframing to further soften reluctance.
