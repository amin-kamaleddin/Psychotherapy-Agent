# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental—used permission, validating language, and normalization throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking (e.g., reflecting mood and goals). Could use more complex reflections that capture ambivalence and invite elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on a concrete behavioral target and progressed logically from values to barriers to a specific, time-bound action with backups and a check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine collaborator—co-created the script, scheduling, and reminders, and adjusted to the client’s comfort and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by seeking permission, offering multiple options, accepting declines, and emphasizing that the plan and decisions belong to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivation with future-vision questions, readiness ruler, and eliciting barriers/steps; could further elicit personal reasons, confidence, and past successes and amplify spontaneous change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and partnership-oriented—invited the client's agenda, offered choices, and repeatedly asked which option the client preferred.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and shaped change talk via future-orientation, readiness ruler, and asking for a specific action; could more deliberately reflect/amplify change statements and probe for desire/ability/reasons and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing and offering manageable steps and backups; could strengthen use of double-sided reflections and explicit reflection of sustain talk before pivoting to change.
