# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through nonjudgmental language, explicit appreciation, and normalization of small steps, creating a warm, accepting space.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and accurate reflections of the patient's stance and context, but could use more complex emotional reflections exploring meaning and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on mini-cigar use throughout, tying questions and summaries to concrete reduction strategies and a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as a joint effort by co-creating the plan, asking preferences, and scheduling a mutually agreed follow-up, with the patient’s ideas shaping decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy by offering choices, respecting refusals (e.g., patches/quitlines), and reinforcing that the patient is in charge of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's motivation using open questions, a confidence ruler, and requests for specific commitments, which produced concrete change talk and goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership by inviting input and offering options, though at times offers many suggestions before eliciting the patient's own ideas and expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with strategy menus and confidence scaling that yielded concrete commitments, but could probe more for personal reasons and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk, provides factual information nonjudgmentally, and pivots to options, but could more often use double-sided reflections to acknowledge and shift sustain talk.
