# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician used respectful, nonjudgmental language and normalized Oliver’s ambivalence (e.g., “That’s understandable,” “That’s brave”). Supportive but sometimes formulaic and shifted quickly into coaching rather than prolonged unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective‑taking (e.g., “You’re torn…,” “I hear pleading worries you”), capturing sustain and change talk. Reflections were mostly surface‑level and often followed immediately by directive scripting rather than deeper exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (assertiveness/getting flatmate to move out). Repeatedly steered conversation to concrete actions (role‑play, script, move‑out date, documentation, landlord steps).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly invited patient choices (role‑play format, in‑person vs text, which reaction worries him). At times moved into expert mode by offering many ready‑made scripts rather than eliciting Oliver’s own wording first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice and control (asked permission for role‑play, offered options, asked readiness). Emphasized Oliver’s ownership of decisions and avoided coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited preferences, concerns, and concrete intentions (target date, backup text, next steps). Could have probed more deeply for personal reasons and values driving the change (e.g., meaning of reclaiming space).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated session as joint problem‑solving (coaching, rehearsal, asking preferences). Partnership reduced when clinician supplied many scripts rather than co‑constructing phrasing with Oliver.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and concrete commitments (timeline, “I need my flat back”) but did not consistently amplify reasons/ability or ask confidence/commitment scaling to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk (guilt, comfort) and redirected toward planning. Could use more MI‑specific techniques (double‑sided reflections) to further weaken sustain talk while reaffirming change.
