# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence with warm, nonjudgmental language, preserving rapport and encouraging cooperation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and summaries captured both content and feeling and matched the patient's language and stance, showing strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on protecting oral health while keeping the piercing and progressively narrowed toward specific, achievable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co‑constructed—clinician solicited input, followed the patient's lead, and shaped a plan that fit her values and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly validated the patient's right to choose, offered options, and asked which small step felt best, preserving and reinforcing a sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's values and solutions with open questions and reflections, then amplified self‑generated motivations and concrete steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and deferred to the patient's expertise, checking preferences and next steps to maintain equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk using open, forward‑looking questions and reflections, converting tentative language into concrete commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double‑sided reflections to prevent sustain talk from dominating, but could have used one or two targeted reflections or elicited the patient's own wording about downsides to further weaken sustain momentum.
