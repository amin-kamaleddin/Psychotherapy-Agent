# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating language throughout the session, with explicit positive regard and no blaming—strong unconditional support.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows some normalization and brief empathic statements but few complex reflections of emotion or ambivalence; the clinician often moves quickly to practical steps instead of deeper reflective listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent behavioral focus on shoplifting with interventions and plans repeatedly tied to reducing the target behavior and preventing recidivism.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is largely collaborative with choices, practice, and co-designed plans, but the clinician frequently supplies concrete tools and scripts rather than eliciting more of the client's own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly invites choice and avoids pressuring language while offering options and supports, though some interventions are somewhat prescriptive rather than fully client-led.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits behavioral commitment (one-week trial, practice, accountability) but does little to draw out intrinsic motivators, values, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Has collaborative elements but leans toward an expert/problem-solver stance with many specific tactics and scripts, reducing full client-led partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk by asking for a doable step and gaining commitments, but misses opportunities to probe desire, reasons, values, and strengths to amplify change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers trigger-targeted strategies, but uses few double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and reframe sustain statements.
