# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician communicated nonjudgmental, respectful acceptance from the opening line and repeatedly validated Viktor without shaming, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate empathic reflections and clarifying questions captured ambivalence and perspective, though reflections could have probed deeper emotions (hurt, fear, pride) to strengthen connection and elicit richer change talk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on substance use and its impact on the marriage and consistently steered the session toward a specific, concrete short-term behavioral plan and debriefing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative — the clinician co-wrote messages, solicited Viktor's input at each step, and integrated his edits, creating genuine joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respected Viktor's choices by asking permission before suggestions, offering options, and avoiding pressuring language, thereby preserving his sense of agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited values and reasons for protecting the marriage, but shifted relatively quickly into clinician-provided solutions, reducing opportunities to draw out Viktor's own motivations and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine partnership through shared decision-making, co-construction of plans and messages, and consistent invitations for Viktor's preferences and edits.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified some change language and concrete steps, but missed opportunities to probe DARN, confidence, and commitment before moving into planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and acknowledged both sides to neutralize sustain talk, though strategic techniques (double-sided or cost-focused reflections) were used infrequently to further reduce sustain talk.
