# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm affirmations and normalization, creating a safe, nonjudgmental context for disclosure and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate summaries, reflective listening, and notices strengths and emotions, though some reflections remain at a summary level and could probe deeper affective nuance like shame or feeling 'unaccepted.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, client-centered focus on reducing recidivism and rebuilding trust, using agenda-setting and linking micro-skills to concrete first steps without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative and egalitarian—clinician invites Timur's input, accepts edits, and co-constructs role-plays and plans as joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy by offering options, seeking permission, and framing tools as choices, reinforcing Timur's ownership of decisions and preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and amplifies change talk, but occasionally shifts to offering ready-made solutions and phrasing, which reduces opportunities for client-generated language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters strong partnership—asks permission before interventions, co-creates the message and role-plays, and centers decisions on Timur's priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, asks for doable specific steps, and links Timur's strengths (e.g., 22 days, routines) to concrete commitments and actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change, but seldom uses advanced softening strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or explicit exploration of the pull toward crime and self-labels) to resolve ambivalence.
