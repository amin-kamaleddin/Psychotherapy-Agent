# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with validating, nonjudgmental language and normalizing statements that affirmed the client's experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking with complex reflections that captured facts, feelings, and ambivalence to guide the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused target on attending interviews/getting steady work and kept questions and reflections tied to concrete, actionable steps toward that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in equal, joint problem-solving using inclusive language, soliciting the client's input at key decisions and co-constructing the plan (scripts, role-play, schedules).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice and agency by offering options, asking permission, and emphasizing the client as the expert in making decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the client's internal motivation by asking evocative questions about values and benefits and then leveraging the client's own language to build commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance, repeatedly deferring to the client's expertise and inviting preferences while offering to practice and support chosen strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with evocative questions and reflections, moving the client toward concrete intentions and a specific next step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and used some double-sided reflections but at times pivoted to solutions without more strategic reframing or explicit normalization of fears to further reduce resistance.
