### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with early validation and repeated affirmations, conveying unconditional positive regard for Samuel's efforts.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking but reflections were brief and the clinician missed opportunities for deeper exploration of Samuel's emotions and identity around smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction and progressed logically from assessment to strategy, planning, barriers, and backup plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a joint problem-solving effort, using inclusive language and co-constructing a concrete implementation plan with summaries and check-ins.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering multiple options, asking which felt doable, and proposing a low-pressure home trial rather than prescribing action.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Samuel's internal motivation and reasons for change by linking goals to values, using journal insights, and eliciting confidence and commitment scales.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance that honored Samuel's preferences, invited his input on strategies, and shared power in creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk through importance/confidence scaling, reflecting successes, and asking for concrete steps and a start date linked to values.

**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected to planning, though could more directly resolve ambivalence using double-sided reflections or explicit summaries of both sides.
