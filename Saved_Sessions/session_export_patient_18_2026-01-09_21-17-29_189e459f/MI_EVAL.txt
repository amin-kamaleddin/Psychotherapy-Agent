# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive (early safety check, repeated affirmations and normalization); reassured choices and safety throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathy via open questions and validations, but missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflections (e.g., embarrassment, tiredness, worry about college).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and safety with concrete harm-reduction steps and a specific follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was collaborative with joint planning and rehearsal, but the clinician often offered multiple expert-generated options without first eliciting Anya’s ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission before interventions, and repeatedly affirming that decisions are the patient’s to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation and commitment (agreement to limits and texting follow-up) but did not draw out deeper intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence with scaling or follow-ups.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally treated the session as a partnership with co-created plans and choice, though could strengthen power-sharing by eliciting her solutions before offering many strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited initial change language (willing to cut back) and reinforced the plan, but missed chances to amplify desire, reasons, ability, and commitment with targeted follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing and shifted to harm reduction when sustain talk appeared, though rarely used double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence while steering toward change.
