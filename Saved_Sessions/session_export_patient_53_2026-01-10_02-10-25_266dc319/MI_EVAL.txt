# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing responses and affirming small steps throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking and normalizes nervousness, though could probe deeper into underlying emotions and the impact of isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing community engagement with concrete, time‑bound steps and regular use of the confidence scale to monitor readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co‑constructs the plan and checks preferences frequently, but providing many structured tools and scripts slightly shifts some agency toward the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission before suggestions, avoiding pressure, and affirming the client's control over timing and actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client's motivations and readiness and reinforces modest change talk, but could deepen intrinsic motives and use reflective amplification to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and invites input, yet the frequent provision of specific suggestions and scripts modestly shifts balance toward clinician-driven solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with choice‑laden options and labeling progress, though missed chances to amplify examples and deepen the client's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk which partially softens it, but missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections and selective rolling with sustain to reduce its momentum.
