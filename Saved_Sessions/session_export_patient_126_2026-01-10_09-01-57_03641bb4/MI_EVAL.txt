# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating with repeated affirmations and normalization of slips, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking were evident, but reflections stayed at the content level and rarely probed underlying emotions or tension about fatherhood and risk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on a specific behavioral target (alcohol-free Mon/Wed) with measurable, actionable steps and a scheduled two-week check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative with inclusive language and co-creation of the plan, consistently eliciting and incorporating the patient's ideas and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice by offering options, asking permission, honoring preferences, and framing suggestions as offers rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation and capability via scaling and open questions leading to concrete planning, but could have probed deeper into values and discrepancy to strengthen intrinsic motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as an equal collaborator throughout, deferring to his expertise and jointly shaping decisions and the behavior-change plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and amplified change talk (scales, specific plan, confidence increase) and reinforced commitments, though missed some opportunities to deepen reasons/values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk but also did not use strategic MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or exploring minimizations) and pivoted quickly to planning, missing opportunities to reduce sustain talk intentionally.
