# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating language throughout the session, normalizing the patient's tension between coping and parenting and showing clear positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and attunement (safety checks, perspective-taking), but reflections are often brief and miss opportunities to explore deeper affect and emotional weight.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, behaviorally-focused agenda on smoking reduction and protection of the child, advancing concrete, stepwise goals and plans throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—regularly solicits the patient's preferences, rehearses scripts together, and adjusts suggestions based on the patient's responses, framing the work as a joint effort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking which to try, and avoiding pressure while reinforcing the patient's control over goals and plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits child-focused motivations and uses confidence scaling and rehearsals, but could more deeply probe values, past successes, and intrinsic reasons to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-constructs the plan with the patient, inviting decision-making and tailoring steps to the patient's context and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces actionable change talk (confidence ruler, small-step choices, rehearsals) but could more often amplify spontaneous change language and tie values to commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids argument and does not reinforce sustain talk, but uses few strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or summaries) to actively explore and reduce sustain talk.
