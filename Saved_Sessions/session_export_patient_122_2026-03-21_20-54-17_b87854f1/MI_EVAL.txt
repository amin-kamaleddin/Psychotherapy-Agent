# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental stance and frequent validation of Sofia's experience. Praised realistic planning and avoided shaming or minimizing her struggles.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, naming emotions and capturing specific stressors and supports. Reflections were timely and helped Sofia feel understood and elaborate.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on small, sustainable increases in movement and concrete planning throughout the session. Kept returning to specific behavioral steps and resisted drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative, offering drafts and explicitly inviting Sofia to choose and tweak wording. The clinician adjusted to her pace and preferences and co-created decisions rather than prescribing them.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Sofia's sense of choice by offering multiple options and checking which wording felt like her voice. Language emphasized ownership and permission without pressuring decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from Sofia by eliciting her own reasons, using scaling, and asking for signs of success. Used open questions and reflections to surface and amplify internal motives and practical strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Sofia as an expert on her life and fostered shared decision-making, inviting preferences and following them. Co-constructed the plan and repeatedly checked in about her choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk using questions about doable steps, a 0–10 confidence scale, and follow-ups to raise commitment. Reinforced and deepened Sofia's commitment language and concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk and redirected toward planning and contingencies, which shifted momentum toward change. Missed some opportunities for explicit MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or normalizing reframes) that would further defuse sustain talk.
