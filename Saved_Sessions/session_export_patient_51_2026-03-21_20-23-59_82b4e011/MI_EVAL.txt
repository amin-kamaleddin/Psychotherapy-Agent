# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing concerns and offering concrete safety planning that demonstrated unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and timely reflective listening that validated worries, though deeper, more complex reflections or a longer empathic summary of ambivalence were limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused target on safe switching (washout, risks, next steps) and kept the session tightly on task with actionable guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the encounter as a joint enterprise by co‑drafting materials, checking phrasing, and shaping actions based on the patient's responses.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy from the outset, offered choices, asked permission, and affirmed the patient's control over follow-up and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out preparatory motivations and concerns and invited patient-generated questions, but did not sufficiently elicit deeper intrinsic reasons, values, or commitment strength.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and invited input, offering to co‑draft and prioritize, but could more explicitly invite the patient to summarize next steps or co-create wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced preparatory/change-oriented planning and safety steps, yet could more actively amplify motivation and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected with pragmatic options, though more double-sided reflections explicitly acknowledging both sides of ambivalence would help soften sustain talk.
