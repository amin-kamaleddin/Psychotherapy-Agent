# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, respectful regard, normalized Dante’s feelings, and repeatedly offered support without blaming or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good, validating reflections that acknowledge ambivalence and worries, but reflections stayed mostly surface-level and did not deeply explore emotional meaning or loss of identity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus with concrete, actionable steps (type, exact times, duration, alarms, backups) that kept the conversation goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with repeated solicitation of Dante’s preferences and co-construction of the plan and follow-up arrangements.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and agency by offering options, inviting preferences, and framing suggestions as choices rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of plans and commitments occurred, but the clinician relied more on offering solutions than drawing out Dante’s intrinsic motivations or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician acted as a collaborator throughout, sharing decision-making and inviting input on multiple components of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk, prompted commitment and follow-up, though could further deepen and amplify Dante’s desire and past-success language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not amplify reasons for inaction and used normalization and low-pressure backups, but could strengthen softening with more double-sided reflections juxtaposing sustain and change talk.
