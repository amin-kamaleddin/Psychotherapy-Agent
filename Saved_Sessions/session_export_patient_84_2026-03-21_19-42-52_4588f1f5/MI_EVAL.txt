# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes Malik's experience, and avoids judgment throughout the session. Multiple validations and acknowledgements support a strong accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening with accurate reflections, validation, and checks for safety and specifics. Reflections are sometimes surface-level and could deepen exploration of Malik's feelings and experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors and actionable, proximal goals (e.g., 9 p.m. check-in, de-escalation lines). The clinician consistently steers conversation toward concrete steps and avoids drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invites Malik to choose, practice, and tweak language, fostering collaboration. However, frequent provision of ready-made scripts and directive suggestions reduces the degree of client-generated solution-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasizes choice and avoids pressuring language, offering options and letting Malik shape the wording. Repeated statements that he doesn't have to promise everything reinforce autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to draw out small commitments and choices but relies heavily on offering scripts and plans. There is little exploration of Malik's internal motivations, values, or confidence about change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters a collaborative dynamic by asking permission and inviting input on wording and practice. Partnership is weakened when the clinician supplies most of the content instead of co-creating it with Malik.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some concrete change language (selecting a doable step, agreeing to check in) but frequently supplies the content and scripts. The clinician misses opportunities to elicit and amplify Malik's own reasons, importance, and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation and notes Malik's minimizing stance. However, the clinician rarely uses double-sided reflections, reframing, or strategic summaries to highlight ambivalence or discrepancy.
