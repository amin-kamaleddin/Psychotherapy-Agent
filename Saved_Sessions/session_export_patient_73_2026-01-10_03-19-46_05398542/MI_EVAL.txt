# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalizing statements and affirmations, avoiding blame and validating mixed feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing ambivalence and emotional nuance with timely, specific reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on tobacco use with a concrete behavioral target and sequences questions from values to a specific, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is genuinely collaborative, using inclusive language, building on the patient's suggestions, and negotiating plans that fit her routines and strengths.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the patient's choice by offering options, normalizing doubt, and avoiding pressuring language, reinforcing voluntary engagement and ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations, values, and past successes, using her language to build intrinsic reasons for change rather than imposing external justifications.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert by asking permission-like, collaborative questions and leaving final decisions to her, demonstrating strong power sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, amplifies, and builds on change language, translating motivation into concrete steps and reinforcing commitment-relevant statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and normalizes doubt but misses opportunities for deeper exploration of smoking's function and use of double-sided or amplified reflections before moving to planning.
