# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and nonjudgmental regard, explicitly naming the patient's frustration and acknowledging time pressure while preserving dignity and moving to problem-solving.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, ongoing perspective-taking through reflections and paraphrases that matched the patient's language and emotional tone, keeping him engaged rather than defensive.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on brief physical activity to support blood pressure/weight goals, with each question purposefully advancing a concrete implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted true joint problem-solving by inviting the patient's input, incorporating his constraints, and co-constructing the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's choice and control by offering options and asking which he would try, reinforcing his ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own motivations and ideas with prompts that led to self-generated planning and specific commitment language (e.g., three calls, headset, calendar alert).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as the expert on what will work, frequently asking him to choose between options and collaboratively building the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk with focused questions that moved the patient to concrete commitments and actionable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and pivoted to doable options, but used few explicit ambivalence-resolution techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or confidence scaling) to further soften resistance.
