# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful (offers factual information, honors “text only” preference, normalizes feelings). No pressure or minimization; affirms choices and keeps interaction safe.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and validation throughout (“that sounds frightening,” “relief and lingering shock”). Empathic language is frequent but somewhat repetitive/formulaic; missed opportunities for deeper emotional exploration (impact on school/relationships).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear, appropriate focus on confirming pregnancy and arranging a test/clinic contact; repeatedly returns to concrete next steps and low‑pressure follow‑up. Intentionally cautious rather than pushing toward a final decision.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Work is clearly co‑created (asks what’s most important, offers choices, checks follow‑up method/time). Treats Elena as an equal partner and incorporates her preferences into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and pacing (“it’s okay not to make decisions now,” respects any reply). Provides safety information without coercion; preserves patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits initial concerns (what’s important, how support should look) but then shifts to offering solutions/logistics. Limited deeper elicitation of motivations, values, or ambivalence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates collaborative stance and shared decision‑making (permission to text, co‑created script). Could more actively invite patient ideas about barriers, resources, or next‑step preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Patient produced change talk (relief, intent to call clinic); clinician reflected this but did not consistently amplify or strategically elicit desire/reasons/ability/confidence beyond logistics. Supported a small behavioral commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates reluctance and honors delay, offering low‑pressure follow‑up which reduces sustain talk. Did not use double‑sided reflections or more strategic reframing to shift ambivalence toward change.
