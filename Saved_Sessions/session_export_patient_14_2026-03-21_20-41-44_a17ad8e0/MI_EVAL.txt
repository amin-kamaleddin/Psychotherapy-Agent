# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, validating the patient's frustration and honoring time constraints throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking focused on practical concerns, but could deepen reflections to capture the emotional weight of ongoing stress and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on a single, concrete target behavior (snack swap) and moved the conversation toward specific, measurable action steps and reminders.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine partner with the patient, soliciting input at each step and tailoring recommendations to his preferences so the plan was co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before advice, and framing actions as the patient's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivation, confidence ratings, and client-generated solutions, but could probe deeper intrinsic values or personal reasons to strengthen lasting commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited the patient's preferences, asked permission for tips, and co-created the plan (snack choice, timing, reminder wording, and prep routine) demonstrating strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and concrete commitments, though additional evocative follow-ups could have deepened reasons and strengthened commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed sustain talk into practical options and small steps, but could use more double-sided reflections to further highlight ambivalence and shift emphasis to change talk.
