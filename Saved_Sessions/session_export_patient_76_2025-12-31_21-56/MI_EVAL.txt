**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and summaries (references urges, chest tightness). Elicits sensory antecedents. Missed deeper, complex reflections of repeated hopeless refrain (“I don't even know why I'm doing this”).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ]1 [ ]2 [ ]3 [ ]4 [x]5
**Comments:** Maintains clear safety focus from start (direct safety questions, repeated urge checks) and co‑creates a concrete safety plan (warning signs, coping steps, contacts, access reduction).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ]1 [ ]2 [ ]3 [ ]4 [x]5
**Comments:** Asks permission, offers options, and checks what feels doable. Plans are co‑constructed (patient selects coping options and access‑reduction steps).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ]1 [ ]2 [ ]3 [ ]4 [x]5
**Comments:** Respects patient choices and pace (offers to send text vs save it), affirms doubt while supporting trying steps, avoids coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ]1 [ ]2 [ ]3 [x]4 [ ]5
**Comments:** Elicits antecedents and past coping strategies; prompts patient‑generated options (shower, ice, roommate text). Missed opportunities to draw out reasons/values for change and strengthen change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ]1 [ ]2 [ ]3 [x]4 [ ]5
**Comments:** Treats patient as expert on her experience and solicits preferences/timing. Occasional clinician‑centered phrasing (transcription slip) and could invite more agenda‑shaping by patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ]1 [ ]2 [x]3 [ ]4 [ ]5
**Comments:** Attempts to elicit willingness and small commitments (willing to try shower/ice, lock blades), but rarely amplifies or reflects tentative change talk to strengthen desire, ability, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** [ ]1 [ ]2 [x]3 [ ]4 [ ]5
**Comments:** Does not argue with sustain talk and accepts statements like "cutting is the only thing that helps." Limited use of double‑sided reflections, reframing, or highlighting exceptions to reduce sustain talk.
