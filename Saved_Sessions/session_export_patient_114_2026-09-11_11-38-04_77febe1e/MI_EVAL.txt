# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and affirming Ryan's values and competence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used solid reflective listening and perspective-taking that helped Ryan elaborate, but moved to planning relatively quickly and missed deeper exploration of embarrassment, defensiveness, and belonging.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol use and guided the session toward a concrete, client-generated plan with successive implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving and co-constructed the plan with Ryan, using inclusive language and inviting his solutions throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored Ryan's freedom to choose with multiple-choice invitations and acceptance of caveats, supporting ownership and self-efficacy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Ryan's solutions and motivations but could have deepened intrinsic motivation by eliciting personal reasons, importance/confidence ratings, or stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Ryan as the expert on his life, invited his ideas, and maintained power-sharing in decision-making and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk via open questions, reinforcement of past successes, and requests for specific steps, leading to concrete commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward values and concrete planning, though more use of double-sided reflections could have better acknowledged competing pulls and reduced resistance.
