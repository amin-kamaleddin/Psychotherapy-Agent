# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with validations and affirmations (e.g., normalizing frustration and praising progress), conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses clear empathic reflections and adapts suggestions to the patient's constraints, but tends toward practical problem-solving rather than deeper exploration of the patient's emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a focused, consistent agenda on weight-related eating and short movement bursts, narrowing to a specific, measurable plan and follow-up without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely co-constructed with negotiated goals and adapted plans, though the clinician occasionally leads with a preset list of suggestions which reduces equal contribution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by offering options, adapting when the patient objects, and co-creating a voluntary one-line check-in while emphasizing no judgment for lapses.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits behavioral commitments and brief intentions but does not sufficiently probe intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence (no scaling or deeper why/questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally treats the patient as a partner—asking what to discuss and inviting choices—however the initial long list of suggestions felt somewhat expert-driven rather than fully client-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and short trials, converting desire into commitments, but could deepen by exploring personal reasons and values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and validates barriers while redirecting to small, doable alternatives; could be improved with more double-sided reflections to link ambivalence to change.
