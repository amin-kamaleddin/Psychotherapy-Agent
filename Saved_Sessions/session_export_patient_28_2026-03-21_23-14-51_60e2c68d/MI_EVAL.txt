# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Dante’s struggle with warm, nonjudgmental language. Repeated affirmations and validating statements reinforce acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking with accurate paraphrase of content and feeling. Could deepen empathic reflections by exploring underlying emotions (e.g., frustration, shame, identity as a dad).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on re-establishing physical activity and ties every intervention to actionable steps. Keeps the conversation goal-directed without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co-constructed: options are proposed, Dante’s preferences are solicited, and decisions are built jointly. Clinician language consistently reinforces teamwork and joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports Dante’s sense of choice by offering options and emphasizing his expertise about his life. No pressuring or directive language; clinician honors constraints and empowers adaptation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical commitments, plans, and small signs of success from Dante’s own thinking. Could more explicitly draw out intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence/commitment language (e.g., scaling questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions Dante as the expert and invites his preferences to shape the plan. Frequent collaborative prompts and checks of feasibility create a strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with specific, small-step planning and reflections. Missed opportunities to amplify and sequence DARN-C (desire, reasons, ability, need, confidence/commitment) for stronger momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes doubts toward planning and solutions in a validating way. Could more strategically use double-sided reflections and summaries to explicitly link sustain and change talk and reduce ambivalence.
