# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, and validated the patient without shaming or minimizing, creating a respectful supportive tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely empathic reflections that mirrored the patient's language and deepened understanding, with frequent checks and summaries that kept the tone collaborative.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on managing alcohol at social events and consistently steered toward a concrete behavioral plan with clear next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-created the plan by inviting the patient's ideas, praising suggestions, and organizing them into feasible steps rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, checking willingness, and emphasizing the patient's control with small, self-directed steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own motivations, values, and strengths (linking quitting smoking to current goals) and drew out internal reasons for change rather than imposing them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a true partnership, repeatedly asking for the patient's preference and deferring decisions to the patient while helping implement chosen strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk by asking outcome-focused questions, reinforcing planning language, and co-creating a one-line plan the patient voiced aloud.
