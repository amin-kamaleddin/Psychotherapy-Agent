# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened and maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, supportive tone and validated concerns, creating a safe space for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated reflective listening and perspective-taking by summarizing ambivalence and responding to confidentiality concerns, but reflections stayed at content/practical level rather than exploring deeper affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on vaping and the concrete goal of no vaping during season, linking all interventions to that target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by co-creating scripts and plans, role-playing, and repeatedly checking the patient's preferences and choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently asked permission, offered multiple options, and emphasized the adolescent's control over decisions, respecting their wish not to involve parents.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used some open questions and discrepancy reflections but shifted quickly to clinician-provided solutions, missing opportunities to elicit deeper personal motivations and stronger self-generated commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as collaborative by offering choices, co-writing texts, and inviting the patient's input on which strategies to use.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforced and amplified patient change talk and invited concrete planning, but could have used more open questions to draw out additional reasons and strengthen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged the perceived benefits of vaping, yet rarely employed strategic softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframes) to gently shift sustain talk.
