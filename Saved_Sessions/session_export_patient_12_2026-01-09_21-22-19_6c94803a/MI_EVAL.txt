# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates unconditional positive regard throughout, normalizing relapse and honoring the patient's pacing without blame or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, timely reflections and safety checks that match the patient's affect and concerns, showing strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on relapse, safety, and immediate coping steps, repeatedly steering toward practical next actions while staying patient-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively co-constructs plans (call script, role-play) and solicits the patient's preferences, creating a clear mutual working relationship.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices and frames suggestions non‑pressuringly, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some practical commitments (script, role‑play) but does not consistently probe for personal reasons, values, or past strengths to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the clinician as collaborator, inviting the patient to choose focus and participate actively in planning and role‑play.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers options that produced some movement toward action, but misses opportunities to amplify change language or ask evocative follow-ups about reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validates and reframes sustain talk at times, yet uses few targeted techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to more directly diminish sustain talk and resolve ambivalence.
