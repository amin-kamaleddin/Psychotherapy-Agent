# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validation (e.g., thanking the patient, normalizing concerns) and avoided blame or labeling throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking and accurately summarized concerns, but could use deeper, more complex reflections to explore underlying emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use linked to soccer/scholarship goals and moved logically from assessment to concrete planning and role‑play to build momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered genuine collaboration by co-developing if‑then plans, soliciting preferences, and tailoring suggestions to the patient's choices throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission before intervening, and framing suggestions as invitations rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively linked behavior to personally relevant goals and elicited practical strategies and desire to change, but could further deepen elicitation of values and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively shared power and treated the patient as the expert, asking which supports were realistic and inviting input on planning and practice decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (importance/confidence, specific strategies, if‑then plans) but could more consistently amplify and elicit explicit commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Moved toward change by linking alcohol to goals but did not routinely use double‑sided reflections or explicit acknowledgment of minimization; more direct softening of sustain talk would help.
