# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens nonjudgmentally and maintains a respectful, validating tone throughout, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding labels or blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and perspective-taking that capture ambivalence and emotional weight, but could use more complex reflections to convey identity/loss nuances.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with concrete actionable steps (scripts, NRT info, 24‑hour plan) and resists drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates true co‑construction—drafting scripts and texts together, asking preferences, inviting role-play, and adapting materials to the patient's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice and avoids pressure by offering options, asking permission, and framing suggestions as offers to support the patient's agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical reasons and solutions and secures a concrete action, but could deepen values exploration and use scaling or discrepancy reflections to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works collaboratively and client‑centeredly, repeatedly asking what the patient wants and sharing power in planning and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and offers supports, yet missed opportunities to reflect and amplify explicit change statements to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and contains sustain talk with double‑sided reflections and practical reframes, but could use more strategic reflections to tip the balance toward change.
