# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong unconditional positive regard and normalizes Sofia's reactions, reducing shame and creating a safe, nonjudgmental space for a defensive teen.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking—validating pain, exhaustion, and guardedness—consistently conveying understanding of Sofia's inner state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on the target behavior (reconnecting safely) and practical safety/coping steps, helping translate ambivalence into concrete low-risk action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—invites rehearsal and choices—but frequently supplies scripted language and stepwise recommendations that somewhat reduce client-led idea generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, emphasizing limits and choice, asking permission to role-play, and avoiding pressuring language throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Attempts to draw out Sofia's motivation and elicit replies, producing commitment language, but relies on ready-made scripts rather than probing values, reasons, or confidence with open questions or scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance and invites input, yet often provides exact wording and solutions instead of first eliciting Sofia’s own phrasing, slightly tipping toward expert provision.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits tentative change talk and offers low-risk steps that produce willingness, but could deepen and amplify change talk by using more open questions and reflections about meaning and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates avoidance and normalizes sustain talk which helps defuse resistance, but could better use double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly resolve ambivalence rather than shifting back to scripted tactics.
