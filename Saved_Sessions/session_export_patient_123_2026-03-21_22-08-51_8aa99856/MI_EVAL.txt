# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, validated feelings, and honored the client's wish to leave the screening aside, creating a nonjudgmental atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing concerns well, but missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional content (e.g., anxiety about college apps or surprise at the screening).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on task planning and study behavior and moved systematically toward a concrete plan, though did not explicitly link the chosen focus back to the original alcohol screening referral.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a true collaborator by building on Sofia's own suggestions, co-creating the checklist, and offering options rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, following the client's lead, and emphasizing Sofia's control over which steps to take and when to revisit topics.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivations and planning (deadlines, mini-goal, reward) but could have drawn out intrinsic motives, values, and confidence/importance to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session demonstrated clear power-sharing and partnership, inviting Sofia's preferences and co-constructing plans that reflected her input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced concrete change language and planning, yet could have amplified 'I’ll try' statements, asked about past successes, and assessed commitment/confidence more explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, normalized ambivalence, and offered practical supports and reframing, but could have used double-sided reflections and explored specific barriers more explicitly.
