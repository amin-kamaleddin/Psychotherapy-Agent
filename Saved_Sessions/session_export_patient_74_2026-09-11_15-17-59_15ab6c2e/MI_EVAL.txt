# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and avoids judgment; reflections convey understanding and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate summaries that capture ambivalence, but reflections remain largely content-level; could deepen empathy by exploring the underlying emotions (guilt, fear, pleasure) more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking and advances the session toward concrete, behaviorally specific steps (one-week trial, start date, 8:15 pm reminder, confidence rating).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Operates as a collaborative problem-solving effort, inviting client choice and buy-in for supports, but occasionally offers specific solutions without first checking whether suggestions are wanted.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors Linda's autonomy by accepting her decision not to quit and using choice-oriented language; supports a client-selected harm-reduction plan rather than pressuring for cessation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations (concern for children) and commitment language, translating this into a practical experiment; could further probe personal reasons and elicit more desire/ability/reason/need language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint exploration, invites Linda to choose options and supports, but could more consistently co-create success criteria and ask permission before offering suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change-related language and converts values into a specific experiment (stepping outside); could amplify and systematically evoke more change talk to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and uses double-sided reflections and harm-reduction pivots to soften sustain talk; could more explicitly explore the costs of smoking and gently shift the balance away from sustain talk while maintaining respect.
