# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Ryan’s perspective and avoiding blame. The clinician normalized his choices and showed clear respect with no instances of labeling or confrontation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking that helped Ryan feel understood through paraphrases and naming concerns. Reflections tended toward pragmatic summaries rather than probing deeper emotions (e.g., embarrassment or fear), which could be explored more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol-related risk and progressively narrowed to a feasible first step (11pm end time + pacing). Summaries and consolidation of the plan kept the session on task without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, co-creating language and inviting role-play while deferring to Ryan’s social knowledge. The clinician repeatedly invited Ryan’s input and tailored options collaboratively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Ryan’s sense of choice by offering multiple options and normalizing his chosen low-key approach. The clinician repeatedly checked what Ryan wanted to try, preserving his ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively linked behavior to values (school) and elicited concrete steps leading to self-motivational statements. Could more systematically elicit and amplify internal motives (ask why changes matter, use scaling for importance/confidence) to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated clearly as a collaborative partner, inviting Ryan’s input and co-constructing the plan rather than dictating actions. Offers to role-play and tweak language reinforced shared power and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited concrete change talk and helped convert it into a specific plan (end time + pacing). Could further strengthen by reflecting and amplifying change talk, and probing importance and confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and strategically redirected toward practical solutions like planning rides and pacing. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections or briefly naming sustain talk before pivoting to disarm resistance and strengthen change.
