# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's fear throughout; respects limits and offers alternatives instead of pressuring.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and supportive language; stays with the patient in moments of distress.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on obtaining a medical evaluation; interventions are tightly linked to scheduling and concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Creates a collaborative process by co-creating scripts, practicing together, and shaping the session tempo to the patient's needs; invites partnership repeatedly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice and agency by offering options, asking permission, and providing outs; respects the patient's decisions and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits action-oriented steps but gives limited attention to internal motivations, values, or commitment; could use more evocative questions about importance and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the interaction as a true partnership by soliciting input, offering options, and collaborating on wording and practice; power-sharing is evident.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generates and strengthens behavioral change talk through concrete supports and practice, but misses opportunities to deepen intrinsic reasons and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk, normalizes avoidance, and reframes goals as small steps; could add strategic double-sided or amplified reflections to further soften sustain talk.
