# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- Checked criteria (only items explicitly/clearly met):
  - [x] Nonjudgmental language used (e.g., “I’m here to listen”)
  - [x] Respectful tone / positive regard (affirmations: “That’s honest and a good start”)
  - [x] Honor patient preferences (accepted “text only”, “no lectures”)
  - [ ] Intensive warmth/individualized positive regard beyond respectful tone

**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect and avoided shaming; affirmed the patient and honored preferences. Tone was generally supportive though somewhat formulaic at times.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
- Checked criteria (only items explicitly/clearly met):
  - [x] Basic reflective listening / acknowledgement (e.g., “Thanks — that makes sense”)
  - [x] Safety check up front
  - [ ] Deep, complex empathic reflections (not clearly present)

**Comments:** Demonstrated basic reflections and validations and checked safety. Missed opportunities for deeper empathic reflections about ambivalence, embarrassment, and the emotional meaning of drinking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
- Checked criteria (only items explicitly/clearly met):
  - [x] Maintained clear focus on target behavior (cutting back alcohol)
  - [x] Collected quantitative baseline (drinks per night/week)
  - [x] Set specific, concrete goals (Mon/Wed sober; 2–3 drinks other nights)
  - [x] Offered harm-reduction tactics and follow-up plan (phone reminders, two‑week text check)
  
**Comments:** Session stayed strongly goal‑directed with clear, pragmatic steps and a scheduled follow‑up. Direction was consistent and effective.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- Checked criteria (only items explicitly/clearly met):
  - [x] Invited patient input on doable strategies (“Which of those feels most doable?”)
  - [x] Asked patient to choose alcohol-free days and readiness rating
  - [x] Accepted patient edits to friend text

**Comments:** Collaborative overall—invited choices and accepted edits—but clinician offered an early, detailed plan that reduced some co‑creation.

### Autonomy Support

- Checked criteria (only items explicitly/clearly met):
  - [x] Framed options rather than directives (“If you want to cut back (not quit) …”)

  - [x] Honored communication preference (text only follow‑up)

**Comments:** Strong support for autonomy throughout; repeatedly reinforced patient ownership of decisions and respected preferences.
