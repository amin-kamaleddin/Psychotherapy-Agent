# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms the client's pace and preferences. There is no minimizing or judgment throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures both content and affect. Reflections consistently mirror the client's concerns about calmness, skepticism, and stress triggers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (brief post-work walks) and guides the conversation toward concrete, measurable planning. Questions and reflections progressively specify when, how, and why the client will act.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters an equal, co-creative process with frequent invitations for the client's choices and joint selection of supports and backups. The client's ideas directly shape the plan and the clinician remains invitational.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's sense of choice by offering options and avoiding prescriptive language. The clinician affirms the client's right to decide and respects her pacing and skepticism.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's reasons, values, and personal resources (e.g., calmness, mom's phrase) but could better amplify and deepen those motivations. More systematic summarizing of change talk and exploration of confidence would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly shares power and frames the interaction as collaborative, using language that centers the client as the expert. Frequent check-ins and joint planning reinforce a strong partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits specific change language and a realistic plan (10–15 minute walks) and reflects it back, but misses some opportunities to selectively amplify desire, ability, reasons, and commitment. Probing for confidence or a commitment statement would increase impact.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and neutralizes sustain talk by validating skepticism and co-constructing tiny backups, reducing its power. Could be more strategic with double-sided reflections, reframes, or explicit contrasts to further pivot sustain talk toward change.
