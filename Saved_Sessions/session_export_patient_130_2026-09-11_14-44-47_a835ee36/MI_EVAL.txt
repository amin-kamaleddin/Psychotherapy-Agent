**Comments:** Elicits and shapes multiple pieces of change talk through choice-oriented questions and concrete planning. Would benefit from more active amplification, summary of change talk, and follow-up to convert intentions into commitment language.

**Comments:** Avoids arguing and acknowledges sustain talk but often leaves statements like 'I got this' unchallenged. Lacks consistent use of strategies (double-sided reflections, reframing, strategic summaries) to soften sustain talk and resolve ambivalence.
