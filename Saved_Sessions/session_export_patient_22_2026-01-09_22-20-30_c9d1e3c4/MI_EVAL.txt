# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, with warm language and normalization of ambivalence that conveys unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and perspective-taking with reflective statements and safety checks, but misses some opportunities to explore deeper emotional subtleties (e.g., social anxiety around dating).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use, gathers specific baseline data, and sets a concrete two-week plan with explicit behavioral tasks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages the patient in choosing days and limits and tailors scripts to her requests, though much of the session is clinician-provided content that can feel expert-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice and control, endorsing a non-all-or-nothing approach and offering multiple options rather than mandates.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits factual information and agreement to steps but does not sufficiently draw out or amplify the patient's internal motivations, values, or strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance—asks permission and offers choices—but often shifts to delivering solutions rather than co-generating ideas with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some patient change language is acknowledged and followed with practical steps, but the clinician does not consistently elicit or amplify the patient's own reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes slips, yet does not actively employ MI-specific strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to reframe or soften sustain statements.
