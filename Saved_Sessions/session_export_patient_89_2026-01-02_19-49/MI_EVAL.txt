# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently nonjudgmental and respectful (opening statement, normalizing language). Multiple affirmations of strengths (walking, dance, family support); no signs of labeling or disapproval.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate, frequent reflections of barriers and frustration (e.g., “time and responsibilities make change hard,” “work running late…”). Good perspective-taking but limited deeper emotional exploration of discouragement tied to repeated weight regain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, sustained focus on target behavior (increase activity). Kept conversation goal-oriented with concrete, time-bound plans and follow-up questions (confidence scale, commitment).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed as joint problem-solving — offered options, solicited preferences, invited Marcus to choose and refine the plan (if-then planning).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Honored Marcus’s control and choice (offered multiple options, asked readiness/confidence, requested a Yes/No commitment). Avoided coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited multiple instances of change talk and commitments (plans, confidence ratings). Could have probed more for intrinsic motives and links to values (family role, meaning).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Active power-sharing throughout (negotiated plans, joint troubleshooting, deferred to Marcus’s choices). Demonstrated genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively prompted and reinforced change talk (commitments, follow-through). Could further amplify by selectively reflecting and summarizing positive change statements to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized barriers; offered realistic scaled alternatives and contingencies (10‑minute walks, 5‑minute dance). Could use more double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of sustain talk emotions.
