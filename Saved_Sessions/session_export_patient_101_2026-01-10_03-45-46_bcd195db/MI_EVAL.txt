# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys consistent, nonjudgmental positive regard through validation and specific praise, avoiding blame or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate paraphrasing and reflective listening to acknowledge worries and ambivalence, but could probe emotional nuance more and use complex reflections to name underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on reducing alcohol use and improving contraceptive reliability, linking actions to outcomes and eliciting specific, time‑bound steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively partners with the client by inviting choices, asking permission before offering help, role‑playing, and co‑constructing concrete plans together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice, offers options rather than directives, and checks preferences to reinforce Mei's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivations, strengths, and confidence‑building ideas, but sometimes offers many solutions instead of prompting more spontaneous client-generated ideas and richer motivational detail.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invites Mei's input and co-creates the plan, though there is some drift toward offering multiple expert suggestions before eliciting more of her own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk with scaling questions and celebration of steps, but could further deepen and amplify spontaneous change talk and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to strategies, but misses opportunities to empathize with ambivalence or use double-sided/complex reflections to resolve sustain talk.
