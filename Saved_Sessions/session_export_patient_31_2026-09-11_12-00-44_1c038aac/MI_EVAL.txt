# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalizing ambivalence and validating the patient's experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries that made the patient feel heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking/NRT and progressively narrows the conversation toward specific, actionable steps and a one-week experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True collaborative style—solicits the patient's preferences at each step and builds the plan around his practical knowledge and routines.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and control by framing the plan as a self-directed experiment and avoiding pressure or prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits ideas and doable steps from the patient but could probe more into intrinsic reasons, importance/confidence, and past successes to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as an equal partner, co-constructing decisions about cues, reminders, and the initial plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk about small steps, though could amplify reasons and confidence to convert intent into stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and uses double-sided reflections to soften sustain talk, but missed opportunities to explore costs of continuing or more forcefully evoke discrepancy.
