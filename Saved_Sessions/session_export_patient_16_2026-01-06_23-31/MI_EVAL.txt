# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, using affirmations and praise to validate the client's strengths and planning.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician provided accurate reflections and perspective-taking but could have deepened emotional connection by exploring the feelings behind vaping and stress more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on vaping and purposefully guided the session toward concrete steps (quit date, device removal, if‑then coping plans).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner throughout—eliciting preferences, co-constructing texts and scripts, and inviting rehearsal to refine the plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely supported client choice by asking permission, offering options, and framing actions as the client's decisions to foster ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's own motivations and solutions (confidence scaling, alternatives, reward) and relied on client-generated strategies and language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the interaction as collaborative—inviting input, sharing decision-making, and incorporating the client's ideas into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk by asking what would move confidence, reflecting commitment language, and converting intent into a locked quit date.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk but did not explicitly soften or explore minimizations; missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or ambivalence summaries.
