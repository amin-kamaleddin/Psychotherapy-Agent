# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful—opened with safety check, validated concerns, and normalized worries which helped shift the patient from defensive to cooperative.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and invited the patient's experience, but could deepen empathic connection by naming underlying emotions and using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on medication, alcohol, and lifestyle goals and consistently moved toward concrete, realistic next steps and plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician solicited preferences, tailored recommendations to shift work, and co-constructed decisions with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking permission before help, and emphasizing safety and the patient's choice throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited intentions and practical change language aligned with concerns, but could have probed deeper for intrinsic reasons, values, and confidence to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fostered partnership through asking preferences and offering options, though at times shifted to an instructional tone without explicitly co-creating the level of detail.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete suggestions and elicited commitments, but missed chances to amplify, summarize, and ask scaling/commitment questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk while redirecting to manageable steps, but could more actively use double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
