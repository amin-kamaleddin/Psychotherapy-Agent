# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, avoided judgment, and affirmed Andrei’s practical constraints and priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician demonstrated strong empathic listening and reflected time pressure and desire for control, but missed an explicit, early reflection of Andrei’s initial irritation about the 45‑minute wait.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on small behavior changes for weight/BP, with each question tied to actionable steps and progression from ideas to a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned themselves as a partner by soliciting Andrei’s expertise, offering choices, and co‑constructing a plan that was clearly shaped by the patient’s input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was strongly supported through repeated choices, respect for preferences (e.g., no external check‑ins), and language that reinforced Andrei’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively drew out Andrei’s own motivations and resources using open questions, a confidence ruler, and invitations to commit to specific next steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly framed the work as collaborative ('I want to figure this out with you'), invited decision‑making, and built the plan together with Andrei rather than prescribing it.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician successfully elicited and amplified concrete change talk, reflected commitment language, and used planning and confidence scaling to solidify intentions (e.g., two lunch walks).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided amplifying sustain talk and redirected toward feasible options that respected billable hours, though could have used more double‑sided reflections and brief exploration of costs of the status quo.
