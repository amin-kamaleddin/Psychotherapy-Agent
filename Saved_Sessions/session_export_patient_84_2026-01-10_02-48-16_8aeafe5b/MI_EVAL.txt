# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm, with repeated validations and normalization that created safety for a guarded adolescent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic stance with accurate reflections and invitations to elaborate, though the clinician sometimes shifted quickly to problem-solving instead of deeper reflective follow-up.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on cannabis use and parent conflict and steered toward actionable steps, but emphasized relational/harm‑reduction goals more than long‑term substance‑use consequences or early agenda prioritization.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and egalitarian—asked Malik’s preferences, incorporated his language, and jointly refined the plan and scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported Malik’s sense of choice and ownership by offering options, preserving identity with a low‑admit approach, and checking before role‑playing or imposing scripts.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Partially evoked motivation around reducing parental conflict but did not sufficiently elicit Malik’s own values, concerns, or intrinsic reasons regarding his cannabis use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Excellent power‑sharing and co‑creation—clinician consistently invited choices, summarized Malik’s tweaks, and made the plan client‑owned.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change language (a two‑week trial) but underused opportunities to amplify Malik’s desire, reasons, ability, or commitment to change his substance use.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided confrontation and used harm‑reduction strategies to soften sustain talk, though more double‑sided reflections could have further eroded sustain language.
