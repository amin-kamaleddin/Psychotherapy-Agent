### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Criteria met:**
- [x] Created opportunities for change talk (open question, confidence ruler)
- [x] Client produced change talk (acknowledged need to complete service, agreed to send messages, said nudge would raise confidence)
- [ ] Actively amplified or reflected change talk to deepen commitment
- [ ] Elicited stronger commitment language or asked explicit commitment/if‑then planning

**Comments:** Change talk occurred but was not actively amplified or deepened into clear commitment. Therapist could have reflected and amplified client statements (e.g., "You already see this would make a difference") and asked explicit commitment questions or if‑then planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Criteria met:**
- [x] Did not argue with sustain talk; acknowledged doubts neutrally
- [x] Redirected to practical solutions to reduce avoidance
- [ ] Used double‑sided reflections to explicitly name and resolve ambivalence

**Comments:** Clinician successfully avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward solutions, but could improve by naming ambivalence more explicitly (double‑sided reflections) to further resolve sustain talk.
