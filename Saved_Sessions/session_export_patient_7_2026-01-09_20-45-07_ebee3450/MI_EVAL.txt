# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing the patient's boundaries and repeatedly affirming safety and limits.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, validating both the patient's emotional and somatic experience (privacy concerns, loosened chest).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps the session focused on a specific, achievable coping behavior and repeatedly steers toward a single plan; could close with a succinct summary and follow-up plan to strengthen direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative with invitational language and shared decision-making, allowing the patient's ideas to shape the session and plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy through repeated choice-based language, explicit checks of preference, and framing the plan as the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits implementation intentions and uses scaling to explore readiness, but could further probe the patient's own reasons or values to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a true partnership by inviting input, co-designing the coping plan, and sharing power throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete, low-effort change talk and commitment (breathing plan, confidence rating) and uses scaling to deepen commitment, though amplification of the patient's willingness could strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes lapses while redirecting to options without escalation; could employ more double-sided reflections to gently resolve ambivalence.
