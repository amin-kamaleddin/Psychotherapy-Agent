# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized Aisha's experience, and repeatedly honored her preferences and choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used clear empathic listening and accurate reflections that validated context and feelings, but could deepen emotional content with more complex reflections of intense statements like “I feel gross.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on restarting exercise to improve body comfort, with each intervention tied to that goal and a logical progression to a concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by co-constructing the plan, soliciting scheduling constraints, and adapting details based on Aisha's input in a reciprocal manner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, checking preferences, and avoiding directive language, reinforcing Aisha's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and change talk but at times moved to offering concrete strategies before fully drawing out Aisha's own problem-solving ideas, limiting deeper intrinsic reasoning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance, asked permission before suggesting strategies, and allowed Aisha to shape the plan, sharing power throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk using options, a confidence rating, and affirmations, helping Aisha articulate motivation and specific plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, normalized setbacks, and offered nonjudgmental fallback strategies that reduced self-criticism and minimized resistance.
