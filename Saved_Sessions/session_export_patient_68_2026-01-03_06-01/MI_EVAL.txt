**Comments:** Several instances of change talk captured (“I’ll chuck a pair in the boot,” “I’ll set the 6pm alarm,” “I’ll try — no promises”). Recommend deliberate amplification and consolidation (ask confidence/importance scaling, stack and summarize change statements) to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Effective avoidance of confrontation and reasonable redirection (“you enjoy nights out AND don’t want to injure yourself”). Could more often end double-sided reflections on the change side or explicitly summarize sustain talk then pivot to discrepancy to further soften sustain talk.
