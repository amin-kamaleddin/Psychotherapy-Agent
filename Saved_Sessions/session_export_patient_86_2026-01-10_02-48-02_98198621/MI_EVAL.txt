# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating the patient's stance and accommodating preferences without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used reflective, empathic statements and acknowledged the patient's feelings, but missed opportunities for deeper complex reflections about resignation and the emotional meaning of smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking and harm reduction with practical strategies, a time-limited plan, and a scheduled follow-up to sustain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative—decisions were co‑constructed, patient preferences shaped the plan, and the clinician offered tools and invited joint tweaking.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by offering choices, honoring refusals, normalizing slips, and framing the plan as the patient's decision throughout the encounter.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation occurred (asked what smoking gives and elicited a week-long trial) but the clinician relied more on offering options and missed deeper elicitation of intrinsic motivations and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a joint enterprise, asked permission, offered mutually exclusive options, and co-created a simple plan that honored shared power and the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited concrete commitment and measurable steps (10‑minute delay, tally, 4 mg gum, 1‑week trial), but could have amplified and explored emerging change talk (desire/reason/confidence) more.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing and lecturing and offered small, nonthreatening options that softened resistance; could further reduce sustain talk with more double-sided reflections or reframing.
