# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through validation and normalization, creating warmth and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that reduced defensiveness, though some reflections remained surface-level and could probe deeper emotions and history.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on disclosure planning with actionable steps, role-play, and frequent redirection to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like joint problem-solving with invitations to choose, tweak, and practice lines and scenarios, allowing the client to shape the work collaboratively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and emphasizing pacing and consent for role-play.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivation and tentative commitments through open questions and rehearsal, but missed deeper elicitation of intrinsic values and scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with shared decision-making and co-creation of scripts, though frequent provision of ready-made lines sometimes shifted toward expert-led guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and commitment language through questions and affirmations, but could have deepened reasons for change and asked importance/confidence questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and often redirected toward change with normalization and pragmatic steps, but could further use double-sided reflections and strategic acknowledgment of ambivalence.
