# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized reactions, with multiple affirmations and no judgment or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic presence and some reflections, but missed opportunities for deeper or more complex reflections of ambivalence and affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol, gathered relevant information, developed a specific measurable plan, and set a concrete follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner throughout—co‑constructed and negotiated the plan, incorporated patient tweaks, and agreed on monitoring and check‑ins.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported patient choice by asking permission, offering options rather than directives, and accepting the patient’s adjustments to the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and prior successes, but at times moved quickly to offering strategies instead of pausing to amplify the patient's own reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—solicited preferences, invited tweaks, and shared power in decision‑making, reinforcing that the patient’s expertise matters.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change language using rulers and a specific plan, though could have paused more to deepen spontaneous change talk before listing strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized ambivalence, but missed chances for targeted double‑sided or reflective reframing to further soften sustain talk.
