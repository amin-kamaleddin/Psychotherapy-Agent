# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect, nonjudgmental regard and normalizing language (e.g., “You clearly care deeply…”, “that feels harder and I’m not ready to pick that fight alone” accepted without judgment). Multiple affirmations and supportive framing throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective‑taking and reflective listening across turns (summary of choices, reflections of ambivalence and emotion, validating meaning). Reflections captured both practical and emotional content (fatigue, guilt, desire for connection).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on a clear, achievable goal (calmer routine → 10‑minute prep) and progressed stepwise from values to a concrete implementation plan (reminders, backups, habit link).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician invited choice, offered options, and elicited the patient’s preferred strategies rather than prescribing (asked which backup felt easiest; refined patient’s plan together).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Power/choice remained with the patient throughout (questions like “which matters most… and why?”). Clinician honored hesitancy about co‑parenting and did not impose directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillful elicitation of reasons for change and progressive deepening from values to concrete steps. Questions consistently drew out patient’s own motivations and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative stance — clinician co‑constructed the plan, validated the patient’s expertise and preferences, and supported ownership of the chosen behaviors.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician elicited and amplified sustained change talk (e.g., “the calm hug would motivate me most,” specific implementation language). Built on change talk to develop a feasible trial.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward solutions (reminders, habit‑linking). Missed some opportunities for double‑sided reflections and a brief exploration of co‑parenting as a barrier that could have further neutralized sustain talk.
