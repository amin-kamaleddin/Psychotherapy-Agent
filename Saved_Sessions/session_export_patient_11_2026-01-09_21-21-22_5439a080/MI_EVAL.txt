# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently used warm, nonjudgmental language and validated the patient’s choices, conveying respect and positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic statements and brief reflections that normalize worries and tailor recommendations, but reflections were often solution-focused and missed deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with logical progression from assessment to planning and concrete implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative in asking preferences and offering options, but the clinician supplied most of the specific program content rather than co-constructing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided multiple choices and respected the patient’s preferences (timing, makeup rules), though some detailed prescriptive language reduced the sense of full autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Occasional elicitation of preferences occurred, but the clinician did little to draw out or amplify the patient’s internal reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were collaborative elements (scheduling, checklist choices), yet the clinician often assumed an expert role by delivering detailed prescriptive plans instead of jointly generating them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced existing change talk and praised readiness, but missed opportunities to elicit and amplify the patient’s own desire/ability/reasons with open, evocative questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk constructively by normalizing concerns and offering pragmatic adjustments, though more strategic reflections (e.g., double-sided) could further soften resistance.
