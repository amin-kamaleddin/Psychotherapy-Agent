# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized Jordan's distress, and honored his limits without judgment. Warm validations and options preserved safety and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent reflective listening and curiosity about emotional/contextual detail showed strong perspective-taking. Reflections captured both content and feeling without minimizing Jordan's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a clear, sustained focus on employment and concrete next steps, moving logically from patterns to tools and a specific timeline. Questions and suggestions were consistently tied back to the work-focused change goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was genuinely team-based, with joint problem-solving, frequent solicitation of Jordan's input, and supports offered contingent on his choices. Decision-making was shared and co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supported Jordan's choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and refusing to take over tasks. This preserved ownership while providing scaffolded support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Motivation and ideas were drawn from Jordan through evocative questions and tailoring to his preferences and barriers. The clinician used Jordan's answers to shape realistic, acceptable steps and tools.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed interactions as collaborative, invited Jordan to choose priorities and formats, and co-created the plan rather than prescribing. Jordan's perspective consistently guided the process.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced change language and secured a specific plan and timeline, but missed chances to amplify and shape Jordan's change statements (e.g., intensifying desire/ability/commitment or asking confidence/importance).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and reframed sustain talk into concrete alternatives, which softened defeatist statements, but could have used more targeted techniques (double-sided reflections, explicit cost/benefit framing) to further lessen sustain talk's potency.
