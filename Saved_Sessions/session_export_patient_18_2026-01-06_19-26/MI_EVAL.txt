# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental—normalized the referral and validated Anya’s preferences and refusal lines. No labeling or moralizing; affirmed her choices and feelings throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking with paraphrase and normalization and tailored responses to her goals. Reflections stayed practical and surface-level, with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions or meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, focused agenda on underage drinking and related goals (safety, sports, college) and repeatedly steered to concrete skills and information. Session maintained tight focus on the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting choices, role-playing at Anya’s request, and tailoring feedback to her reactions. Modeled joint problem-solving and shared control over agenda and methods.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission before information or practice, and emphasizing that decisions are hers. Avoided pressuring language and validated her authority to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited preferences, some reasons, and practice choices but relied more on providing scripts and facts than drawing out intrinsic motivations or confidence. Missed chances to use evocative questions to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance with shared decision-making and invited input on wording and practice. At times leaned toward offering prescriptive scripts rather than co-constructing phrasing from Anya’s own language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Prompted some change-oriented statements (safety, sports/college concerns) and practice willingness but did not consistently amplify or deepen change talk. Could have used more open, evocative questions and reflective amplification to build desire and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and modeled neutral redirects and alternatives during role-play. Could have more actively used double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence and soften sustain talk further.
