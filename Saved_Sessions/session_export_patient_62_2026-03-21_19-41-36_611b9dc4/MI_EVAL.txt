# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician was consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, offering sympathy and normalizing Jordan's feelings. Affirmations of effort and willingness to help reinforced unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were timely and accurate, naming exhaustion, skepticism, and effort which kept Jordan engaged. The clinician showed strong perspective-taking and emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on finding work and reducing financial pressure with actionable, time-bound steps. The clinician repeatedly steered conversation to concrete tasks, templates, and a follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decisions and plans were co-constructed, with frequent invitations for Jordan's input and negotiated follow-up timing. The clinician offered to role-play and co-write messages, reinforcing a joint effort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently preserved choice by offering multiple options, asking permission, and framing steps as a low-cost experiment. Emphasis on Jordan's control of timing and decisions supported autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to draw out motivation occurred, but the clinician relied more on offering solutions than eliciting Jordan's internal reasons or strengths. Missed opportunities included asking what success would mean or using scaling questions to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A generally collaborative stance was evident through options and negotiated follow-up, but the clinician at times shifted into directive problem-solving. More elicitation of Jordan's own ideas before offering solutions would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited concrete, small commitments and secured a specific experiment and timeline. However, they could have probed more for personal reasons, values, or amplified change statements to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged, normalized, and reframed into a low-risk experiment without arguing. The clinician rarely used double-sided reflections or explicitly explored the helplessness narrative to help resolve ambivalence.
