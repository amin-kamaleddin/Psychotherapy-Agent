# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician explicitly stated nonjudgmental intent and consistently used validating, normalizing language, creating a safe space for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Multiple accurate reflections and empathic statements acknowledged Elena's feelings, though the clinician moved fairly quickly to information/planning instead of deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session remained tightly focused on contraception and immediate risk-reduction steps, consistently steering toward concrete, relevant actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician invited preferences, offered options, co-created scripts and summaries, and adjusted to the patient's requests, demonstrating genuine teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were presented neutrally and the clinician repeatedly affirmed Elena's right to delay decisions, reinforcing her sense of control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and Elicit–Provide–Elicit drew out reasons and small steps, but the clinician could have probed more for personal values, confidence, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Decisions were co-created (script wording, timing, role-play) and the clinician checked back frequently, reflecting strong power-sharing and mutual respect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced concrete change talk and helped operationalize plans, but could better amplify reasons for change and explicitly elicit commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk was met without confrontation using acceptance and double-sided reflections, while still directing toward small, manageable steps.
