# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validated priorities, and normalized effort while avoiding judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately captured key elements and specifics but were often pragmatic; could deepen emotional attunement with more complex reflections of feeling (e.g., worry or frustration).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and steadily moved from values to a concrete, measurable action and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—suggestions were offered then refined by the client, with shared decision-making and power‑sharing throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice, offered options and permission, and left final decisions with the client, reinforcing autonomy throughout the exchange.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited the client's own motivations, values, and concrete solutions through open questions, producing client-led planning and ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the client as the expert and co‑created the plan, inviting input and avoiding an expert‑directed stance to foster ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened change talk with decisional and planning questions and confidence scaling, but could have amplified and summarized change language more to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing reluctance and focused on solutions when concerns arose, though using more explicit double‑sided reflections could further tip ambivalence toward change.
