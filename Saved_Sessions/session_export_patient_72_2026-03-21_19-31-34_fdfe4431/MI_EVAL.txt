# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and unconditional positive regard (opening support, repeated affirmations) with no judgmental language; clinician maintained a non-dismissive tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and validation (acknowledging concerns and offering calming strategies) but did not consistently deepen emotional reflections or explicitly name the patient’s feelings and lived experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on restarting sustainable exercise with concrete behavioral steps (YMCA visit day/time, walk plan, triggers, contingency strategies) and avoided drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged the patient as an equal partner by soliciting preferences, co-creating the plan, and adjusting support to the patient’s requests (script, role-play, checklist).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly reinforced choice and control, offering optional supports and asking permission before advising, which respected and strengthened the patient’s autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient’s own motives and resources, using the patient’s language to build plans and encouraging rehearsal of personally meaningful reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a genuinely collaborative stance by repeatedly asking which option the patient preferred and allowing her preferences to shape the plan, promoting true power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk and confidence (asked for concrete steps, confidence ratings, rehearsals, and post-visit rewards), turning abstract desire into commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered practical responses to barriers (scripts, contingencies), but relied more on rapid problem-solving than MI-specific techniques like double-sided reflections to fully soften ambivalence.
