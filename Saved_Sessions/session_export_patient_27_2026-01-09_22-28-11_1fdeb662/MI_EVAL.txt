# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with repeated validation and normalization; no shaming or minimizing, which supported a warm, accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing emotions and ambivalence and deepening the patient’s sense of being heard.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on disclosure to peers, moving from ambivalence to a concrete plan (choice of line, role‑play, timing, and check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with role‑play and shared planning, but frequent provision of exact scripts and step‑by‑step instructions reduced opportunities for the patient to generate content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering permission, choices, and affirming the patient’s selections while avoiding pressure and respecting pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient’s own motivations and resources (e.g., storytelling strength) and used practice and choice to draw out intrinsic reasons and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with inclusive language and joint planning, but could have elicited client‑generated wording earlier before offering clinician scripts to increase ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and strengthened change talk through open questions and planning, but missed chances to deepen commitment and confidence (e.g., scaling confidence, exploring barriers).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and redirected to manageable steps, though more explicit double‑sided reflections or summarizing sustain talk could have further reduced its influence.
