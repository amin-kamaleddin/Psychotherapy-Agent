# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth (affirmations, invitations to proceed at the patient's pace), validating ambivalence and treating the patient with respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking that captured both the patient's reasoning and values, and asked open, empathic questions to invite elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the target behavior (getting the flu vaccine) and steered the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps like scheduling, texting grandparents, and reminders.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a team effort and co‑constructed the plan, offering tailored supports (drafting texts, scripts, backup plans) rather than adopting an expert-only stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's freedom to choose, offered options and asked permission before giving information, preserving the patient's sense of ownership over the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own motivations and reasons (confidence ruler, 'what would help' questions), producing internal motivators and small, practical next steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as collaborative and balanced power by asking which supports the patient wanted and tailoring plans together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several strands of change talk and affirmed progress, but missed opportunities to amplify and consolidate emergent change talk into firmer commitment (e.g., booking the appointment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used neutral facts and reflective summaries to not bolster sustain talk, though more strategic double‑sided reflections could have more actively softened sustain language.
