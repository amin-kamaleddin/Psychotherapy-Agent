# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language (e.g., opening support, validating worry, affirming progress). Warm acceptance was sustained throughout and slips were framed as information rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections (checked inhaler use, reflected frustration and family concerns). Could deepen empathy by exploring the emotional weight of repeated flare‑ups, long work hours, and resignation with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on asthma‑relevant behaviors and moved logically from assessment to a concrete behavior change plan. The session stayed goal‑oriented with actionable steps and a follow‑up check.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner throughout, co‑creating alarm scripts, substitutes, and check‑ins with Samir. The clinician repeatedly invited and incorporated Samir’s input and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported Samir’s choice by offering options, asking which plan felt doable, and acknowledging he was not ready to quit completely. The clinician avoided coercion and reinforced patient ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and resources (medication use, cigarettes/drinks, observed breathing improvements) and drew out implementation intentions. Some moments were solution‑heavy without permission, and the clinician could have probed deeper for intrinsic reasons, values linkage, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed shared expertise and co‑construction by asking permission, offering choices, and inviting Samir to pick and shape the plan. Power was clearly shared and Samir’s ideas determined many plan details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (baseline questions, offering options, affirming progress and linking behavior to breathing). Could amplify change talk further with more evocative follow‑ups to deepen meaning and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and supported a reduction approach, offering modest, concrete steps that softened sustain talk. Using more double‑sided reflections and emphasizing discrepancy with values would further reduce sustain talk.
