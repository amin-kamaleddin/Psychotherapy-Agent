# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental—normalized Sofia's feelings and acknowledged difficulty (e.g., noted shaking and guilt), communicating unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking (validated ambivalence and physical symptoms), but missed deeper exploration of identity conflict and guilt statements like losing friends or not having another chance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear and sustained focus on reducing alcohol use during pregnancy, with each intervention tied back to the target behavior and no notable drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally team-oriented with reciprocal role-plays and acceptance of patient edits, though the clinician sometimes supplied many scripts/options which shifted some control toward the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by asking permission, offering multiple options, and inviting Sofia to choose and tweak scripts that felt authentic to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions, a readiness scale, and practice to elicit change talk, but could have probed more about values, past successes, and confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance evidenced by co-creating language and practicing together, although the clinician at times led with suggested phrasing instead of eliciting Sofia’s own first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk via the importance scale and rehearsal of scripts, but could further amplify Sofia’s intrinsic motives and reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some softening moves (validating ambivalence, practical strategies, role-play) but did not explicitly reflect or double-sidedly address core sustain concerns like fear of losing friends.
