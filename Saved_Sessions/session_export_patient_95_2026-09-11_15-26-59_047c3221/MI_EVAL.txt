# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validates/normalizes Maria’s distress, honoring her request to be heard and avoiding blame or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking with repeated reflections, compassionate risk questions, and acknowledgment of the relief of being believed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on imminent suicide risk and means restriction, progressing logically from assessment to concrete safety steps and contingency planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Behaves largely as an equal partner—inviting input, offering options, and staying with the patient—though a few clinician-led moments limit patient-generated planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choices, asks permission, and respects Maria’s boundaries, framing actions as collaborative decisions that reinforce her agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces limited internal motivation (safety-focused actions) but misses opportunities to draw out values, hopes, or broader reasons for living.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and checks consent, offering choices, but often relies on clinician-generated options instead of inviting Maria to propose her own steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Notices and reinforces immediate change talk around safety behaviors (e.g., moving pills, brief check-in) but does not sufficiently elicit broader motivations or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates hopeless statements and avoids argument, using empathic reflections to reduce sustain talk; could more consistently employ double-sided reflections and summarizing to shift balance toward change.
