# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental respect, validating the patient's stance and normalizing fatigue with being lectured.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, specific reflective listening by restating key lines and emotions, which kept the patient engaged and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking reduction/cessation and consistently guided the session toward manageable, time-limited experiments and concrete steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan by soliciting the patient's ideas and preferences and used inclusive, nonprescriptive language so the plan was shaped by the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by offering low-pressure experiments, choices, and accepting the patient's right to decline or not promise anything.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own reasons, ideas, and motivators with evocative questions and drew out internal strategies rather than supplying reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative ('with you, not for you'), invited the patient's input, and respected his expertise about his life when deciding next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced specific change talk and small experiments, but could have better amplified and consolidated emerging motivation (e.g., strategic summaries or confidence/importance scaling).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently acknowledged and reframed sustain talk with empathic reflections and offered low-pressure options, avoiding argumentation and reducing resistance.
