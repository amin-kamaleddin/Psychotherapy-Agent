### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validating, respectful, and nonjudgmental throughout the session. No labeling or shaming language is used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate, concise reflections and perspective-taking that capture Tyler's ambivalence. Reflections are often brief and followed quickly by solutions, limiting deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing risky drinking with every turn tied to actionable steps. Session stays goal-oriented with specific planning and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solicits preferences and co-plans practical steps, showing collaborative moments. Frequent multi-item lists and scripted suggestions occasionally shift the balance toward clinician-led problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and control by asking permission, inviting selections, and respecting contingencies. Offers noncoercive options and tweaks rather than pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivations, worries, and reasons for change and uses confidence scaling to target support. Tends to supply solutions rather than consistently drawing out Tyler's own ideas and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance with invitations to choose and rehearse, allowing Tyler to shape the plan. At times moves into an expert/advising role with extended lists and instructions instead of co-construction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk via evocative questions, options, and confidence ratings, leading to concrete commitments. Could more consistently reflect and summarize Tyler's change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Validates sustain motives and redirects toward low-awkward strategies that preserve social connection. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections and summarizing of sustain talk to gently tip the balance toward change.
