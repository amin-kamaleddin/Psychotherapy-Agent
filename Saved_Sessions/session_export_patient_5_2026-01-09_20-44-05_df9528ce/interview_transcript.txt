PSYCHIATRIST: Alessia — brief facts: Citalopram is an SSRI used for depression and anxiety. Common side effects: nausea, sleep changes, headache, sexual side effects, sweating; rare but important: hyponatremia and QT prolongation (max 40 mg/day; 20 mg/day if >60 or liver impairment).
