# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating—normalizes ambivalence and honors the client's ritual while avoiding prescriptive or critical language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening and summaries that capture both content and underlying meaning, which the client affirms.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing afternoon caffeine and repeatedly orients the conversation toward specific, measurable actions (decaf, 2 p.m. reminder, tracking).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is joint problem-solving throughout—clinician solicits the client's input, reflects it back, and builds the plan from the client's ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly reinforces client choice and pace, offers options and normalizes setbacks so the client retains ownership of decisions and the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the client's own motivations, preferred strategies, and expected payoffs, resulting in a client-generated plan and commitment to a midweek check-in.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as collaborative and positions the client as the expert by repeatedly asking for preferences and doable next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and strengthens change talk by converting ambivalence into concrete steps, but misses opportunities to more explicitly amplify importance/confidence and elicit a clear commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids arguing and honors the ritual while redirecting toward experiments, though could more often use double-sided reflections or strategic discrepancy to further soften sustain talk.
