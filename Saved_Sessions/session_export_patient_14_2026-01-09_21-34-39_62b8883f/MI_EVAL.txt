# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating frustration and affirming choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately reflecting irritation and priorities, but reflections stayed fairly surface-level and did not consistently probe deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on fitting healthier habits into a busy schedule and guided the session toward a specific, measurable action with implementation planning and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative and framed as joint problem-solving with frequent checks for comfort, but the clinician often offered multiple specific suggestions rather than eliciting more of the client's spontaneous ideas early on.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by emphasizing choice, offering options, honoring preferences for mode and brevity of follow-up, and explicitly stating no unwanted follow-ups.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation through scaling and questions about what would nudge confidence and signs of success, but could have explored deeper personal values and discrepancy to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed that expertise resides with the client and co-created the plan with invitations for choice, though more elicitation of the client's own problem-solving earlier would enhance partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using scaling and concrete options and amplified commitment language, but missed chances to deepen exploration of personal reasons and elicit stronger ability/commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk and offered smaller alternatives, but used few strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframing) to systematically soften sustain language and resolve ambivalence.
