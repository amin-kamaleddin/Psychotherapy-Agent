# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizing ambivalence and affirming Anya's priorities without judgment. Honors her choices and maintains a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, nuanced reflective listening that mirrors both content and feeling, validating Anya's concerns and curiosity. Reflections are timely and complex, helping Anya elaborate on motivations and worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on early alcohol exposure and protective behaviors tied to Anya's values. Questions are ordered to move from values to options to a specific, testable step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative, with the clinician soliciting Anya's input and co-constructing the plan (soda, practice lines, friend, exit plan). Uses inclusive language and repeatedly asks Anya to choose among options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports Anya's autonomy with statements that respect her choices and by inviting her selection of doable steps. Avoids pressure or directives, reinforcing her ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic reasons and practical ideas that align with Anya's goals, but could more explicitly ask for confidence/importance ratings and deeper value linkage. Strategic summaries and scaling could better chain change statements into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as joint and elicits Anya's expertise about what will work, demonstrating genuine power-sharing. Frequently invites her choices and checks what would help her follow through.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with questions about values and specific steps, producing clear change language. Could better amplify and summarize existing change talk and use scaling to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing ambivalence and using double-sided reflections that acknowledge curiosity while pivoting to protective strategies. Does not confront or argue, effectively minimizing sustain talk focus.
