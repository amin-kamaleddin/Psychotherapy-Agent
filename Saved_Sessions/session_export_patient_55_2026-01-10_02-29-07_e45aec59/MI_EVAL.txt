### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence (e.g., "you’re not defined by this"), creating a nonjudgmental atmosphere throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking (e.g., summarizing values and conflict), showing nuanced understanding of both practical and emotional concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol-related risk with purposeful guidance toward a specific, proximal plan (no drinking on school nights with concrete supports) and a one-week trial.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with repeated invitations for patient input and plans built directly from the patient's ideas rather than imposed by the clinician.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking what felt most doable, and normalizing possible lapses, reinforcing the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic motivators by linking change to the patient's values (grades, band) and drawing out personal reasons and supports for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative with power-sharing language and repeated questions that privileged the patient's expertise and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
