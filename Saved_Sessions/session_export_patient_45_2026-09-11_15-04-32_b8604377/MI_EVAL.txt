# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing ambivalence and treating tentative commitments as legitimate; acceptance is explicit and continuous throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that helped soften resistance, but could deepen affective reflections to name emotions like defensiveness, worry, or frustration to strengthen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on alcohol reduction within diabetes care and progressively narrows to a clear, actionable first step with supports and tracking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly framed as joint problem-solving—offers ideas but repeatedly asks Erik to choose and builds the plan from his suggestions, demonstrating balanced mutual input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes Erik's choice and control, offers options, and normalizes realistic limits rather than using directive or coercive language.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively elicits Erik's reasons, strategies, and success indicators, but could probe deeper into personal values and use scaling or commitment prompts to amplify intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Erik as the primary agent of change, soliciting his expertise and co-constructing the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly elicits and reinforces change talk and obtains concrete change statements, but could more actively amplify and consolidate change talk and ask for a clearer commitment or confidence rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Handles sustain talk without confrontation using two-sided reflections and practical steps, yet could further soften sustain talk by exploring underlying fears and linking sustain and change sides more explicitly.
