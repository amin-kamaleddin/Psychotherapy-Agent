# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, creating safety for a guarded adolescent. No labeling, shaming, or moralizing observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, timely reflections that capture content and affect and legitimizes Tyler's feelings. Empathic stance is steady and facilitates rapport without confrontation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on the mutually chosen goal of de‑escalation and moves toward concrete actions. Offers specific low‑effort strategies and guides selection and refinement of a plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates true partnership by co‑creating wording options, offering role‑play, and adapting suggestions to Tyler’s voice. Consistently solicits preferences and shares power in decision‑making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly endorses Tyler's choice and control with invitations, options, and noncoercive language despite the mandate. Normalizes ambivalence and avoids directives that would provoke reactance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits short‑term, pragmatic motivations and concrete intent for de‑escalation but does not probe deeper values or use importance/confidence rulers. Could strengthen sustainable motivation by drawing out intrinsic reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative, equal‑power stance through frequent invitations, options, and co‑construction of the plan. Clinician centers Tyler’s perspective and expertise throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some change and safety talk and moves to low‑effort actions that support enactment. Missed opportunities to deepen and amplify desire/reason/need and to assess importance and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and skillfully redirects toward de‑escalation and safety, focusing on doable steps. Could better use double‑sided reflections and explicit discrepancy to reframe sustain language into change talk.
