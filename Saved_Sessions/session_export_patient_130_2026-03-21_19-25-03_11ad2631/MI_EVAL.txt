# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, validated Viktor's perspective, and normalized ambivalence, demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries showed good perspective-taking and elicited disclosure, though there were missed opportunities for deeper emotional or complex reflections (e.g., shame, irritation).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on drug use and safety concerns with concrete goals, homework, and a stepwise plan toward behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with Viktor invited to choose texts, nights, and who holds keys, and the plan was co-constructed using his practical knowledge.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician offered options and respected Viktor's agency while avoiding coercion, though a few moments of gentle pressure could have been framed more explicitly as Viktor's choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited (readiness ruler, small-change invitations), but most reasons remained external (for his wife) and the clinician did not sufficiently draw out Viktor's personal motives or use stronger evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician worked alongside Viktor, asking permission and soliciting input, but at times offered many concrete suggestions without fully eliciting Viktor's priorities first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician prompted some change talk and commitments to safety, yet did not consistently deepen intrinsic reasons or explore discrepancy behind Viktor's ambivalence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided argument and redirected sustain talk toward safety and manageable steps effectively, though more strategic double-sided reflections could further soften sustain talk.
