# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the patient's values.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and perspective-taking were accurate and helped the patient feel heard, but there were missed opportunities to probe more deeply into emotions and the meaning of family traditions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use with concrete steps, role-plays, and summaries that kept the session action-oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a team with the patient by co-creating materials, adapting wording to preferences, and following the patient's lead in role-plays.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported the patient's sense of choice by asking permission, offering options, normalizing indecision, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's motivations and practical plans but could have further deepened change talk using more open, evocative questions and scaling.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and treated the patient as expert, though slightly more explicit power-sharing when choosing next steps would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced several change statements and small goals, but could have asked more open questions to elicit stronger desire, ability, reasons, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and normalized doubts, but often responded with authoritative information rather than using MI-specific strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections or eliciting the patient's own counterarguments).
