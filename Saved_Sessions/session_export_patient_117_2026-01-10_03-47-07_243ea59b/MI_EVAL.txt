# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with multiple affirmations and validating language; no judgmental or shaming remarks, signaling warmth and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and accurate content reflections that linked observations to core values; could strengthen by using more complex/empathic reflections to capture emotional nuances of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent behavioral focus on reducing lunch smoking/increasing walks and consistently steered conversation toward concrete, measurable steps and implementation strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed with frequent solicitation of the patient's input, rehearsal of plans, and joint problem-solving, positioning the clinician as a partner rather than an instructor.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's choice and offered multiple options instead of directives, inviting ownership and respecting the patient's autonomy in selecting strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicited the patient's own reasons and self-generated solutions, anchored plans in the patient's values, and used confidence ratings and rehearsal to draw out intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative language and option-offering allowed the patient to shape the plan, demonstrating strong power-sharing and mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with pattern-reporting, specific experiment planning, confidence scaling, and reinforcement of the patient's change language into actionable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and provided practical backups to address obstacles, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections or pivoting summaries to name sustain talk before shifting to change.
