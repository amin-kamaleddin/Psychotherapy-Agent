# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent nonjudgmental, respectful regard—validating concerns, normalizing the test approach, and affirming protective behaviors like stepping out to the porch.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and summaries captured Linda's ambivalence and feelings, validating both emotion and behavior and inviting further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and protecting the children throughout, moving logically from exploration to a specific, measurable harm‑reduction plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained an equal‑partner stance by inviting input, letting Linda choose options, and co‑creating supports and concrete implementation steps with her.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported Linda's choice by asking permission, framing changes as a voluntary test, offering options, and normalizing slips to preserve client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal motivation tied to Linda's role‑model value and used scaling, but missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic motives and amplify change talk for stronger commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative: clinician asked permission before offering options, invited Linda to pick what felt doable, and shared decision‑making power throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete change talk and an if–then plan (yard‑only smoking, gum/tea) but could have amplified and summarized change language more to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk and worked around Linda's enjoyment, yet could have used more double‑sided and amplified reflections to link sustain talk to reasons for change.
