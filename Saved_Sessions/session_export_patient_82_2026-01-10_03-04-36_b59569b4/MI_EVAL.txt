# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental regard, normalizing Lukas’s concerns and validating his conflict without shaming or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections were accurate and timely, capturing both content and emotional tone which encouraged further disclosure and signaled strong attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on alcohol’s impact on school and team goals, offering a concise menu of targeted strategies and checking which option felt doable.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician behaved as an equal partner, inviting Lukas to shape the plan, offering options rather than prescriptions, and responding to practical needs like formatting and rehearsal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported through permission‑seeking, offering choices, and repeatedly affirming Lukas’s right to decide how to proceed and disclose information.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Some of Lukas’s motivations were drawn out and led to actionable steps, but the clinician missed chances to deepen intrinsic motivation using scaling or more targeted why/how questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reflected genuine power‑sharing with co‑constructed plans and repeated invitations for Lukas to choose and tailor strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced change‑oriented language and priorities, yet could have amplified and deepened change talk with targeted elicitations of desire, ability, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected to practical options, though double‑sided reflections could have more explicitly acknowledged ambivalence and guided toward change.
