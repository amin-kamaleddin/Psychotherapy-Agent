# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, validating the client's pace without judgment. No shaming or labeling and reluctance was accepted throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and perspective-taking were used to capture the client's concerns and hedged language. The clinician mirrored emotional stance and summarized key worries effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed focused on the target behavior with concrete planning (if‑then plan, alarm, reminder text). Questions and interventions repeatedly returned to incremental, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as collaborative problem-solving with the client choosing wording, timing, and steps. The clinician positioned themself as a helper and accepted the client's hesitancy without overriding it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were repeatedly reinforced and suggestions were framed as nonbinding, preserving client control. Permission was asked before offering ideas and multiple options were provided.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good use of evocative questions elicited intrinsic motivators like reduced worry and small relief. Could strengthen by amplifying values and linking tentative reasons to more personal outcomes or commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician fostered shared decision-making and invited client input at every turn, co-constructing the plan. Power was clearly shared and the client's preferences drove the specifics.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effective prompts produced several instances of change talk and concrete intentions (reminder, charger, if‑then plan). Missed opportunities include more consistent amplification and consolidation of emerging change language into firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was not reinforced and was skillfully redirected toward low-effort options and normalization. Could further benefit from more double-sided reflections to explicitly validate both sides while tipping toward change.
