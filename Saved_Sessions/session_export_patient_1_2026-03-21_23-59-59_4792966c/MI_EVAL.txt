# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing, nonjudgmental language and validation; never criticizes and works from the patient's perspective.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, ongoing reflective listening including complex reflections that capture both sides and mirror the patient's constraints, fostering engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on protecting throat and gums and sequentially moves from options to selection and implementation planning without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently solicits the patient's ideas and integrates them into the plan (truck cue, dash kit, packets), treating the patient as an equal partner in decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice throughout by offering options, accepting the decision not to quit, and asking what feels doable to reinforce the patient's control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skilled at drawing out practical motivations and eliciting implementation intentions, but could probe more for personal values or use a confidence ruler to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the conversation as a joint exploration, defers to the patient's expertise about his routine, and co-constructs decisions collaboratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits concrete change talk and action steps, though could more explicitly amplify and summarize the patient's change statements or elicit a commitment rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk with double-sided reflections and neutral summaries, avoiding argument and gently steering toward harm-reduction options.
