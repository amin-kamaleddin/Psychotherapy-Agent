### Acceptance

**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (bedtime routine/lights-out at 8pm) and moves the conversation toward concrete steps, planning, rehearsal, and troubleshooting without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-develops language and plans with Amina, invites her preferences about sequencing, and role-plays collaboratively rather than prescribing solutions, supporting a genuine interaction of equals.

#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation by exploring values, using scaling, and prompting small steps, but at times offers scripts more directly rather than using explicit Elicit–Provide–Elicit and could more strongly amplify value statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Amina as the expert on her life, repeatedly inviting her input and choices about action, pacing, and rehearsal, which shares power and fosters a collaborative approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits change talk through future imagining, scaling, and asking for small steps and commitments, with room to more frequently amplify and reflect change-talk language to deepen commitment.
