### Empathy

### Collaboration

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership evident through shared decision-making, repeated solicitation of preferences, and client-led session content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using invitational questions, scaling, and requests for specific steps, successfully converting interest into a concrete plan.
