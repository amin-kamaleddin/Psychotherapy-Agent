# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client’s experience, affirming strengths and treating the plan as a trial rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the client’s language and emotions to deepen rapport and elicit elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on vaping and performance, guiding the conversation from values to a concrete, actionable plan with progressive steps toward implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-creates solutions with the client, soliciting and building on her ideas and positioning the clinician as a facilitator rather than an expert director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses choice-respecting language and invites the client to select strategies, framing the plan as the client’s decision and supporting ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from the client’s own values and past successes, eliciting internally generated reasons for change and having the client craft concrete reminders.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint project, regularly asking for the client’s input and preferences and sharing decision-making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through open, action-focused questions, reflections, and reinforcement of concrete commitments and supports.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation and pivots to planning, but could more often use strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections, selective amplification) and deeper exploration of sustain functions.
