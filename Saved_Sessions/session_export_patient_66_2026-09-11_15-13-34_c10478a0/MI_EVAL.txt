# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating the patient's experience and normalizing a gradual approach; avoided shaming or pressuring language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing ambivalence and contextual pressures; could have probed more into underlying emotions and reinforced empathic statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking cessation tied to the workplace rule and consistently guided the patient toward a specific, time-limited first step and concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner with the patient, soliciting input on wording, timing, and supports and co-constructing the plan rather than imposing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the patient's freedom to choose, presented options neutrally, and let the patient determine the plan without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own motivations and practical reasons for change and secured actionable responses; could further probe intrinsic motives and elicit stronger commitment or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invited the patient's agenda and choices, offered options, and co-created concrete supports and implementation details with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk leading to a concrete plan, but would benefit from more frequent amplification, summarization, and elicitation of stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without confrontation using double-sided reflections and steered toward manageable options; could more strategically reflect and amplify change talk while minimizing sustain statements.
