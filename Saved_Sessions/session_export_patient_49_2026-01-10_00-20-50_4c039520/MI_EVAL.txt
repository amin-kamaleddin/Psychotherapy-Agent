# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician was consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating, using frequent affirmations and normalization; this supportive stance reinforced Hana's efforts and concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and perspective-taking were accurate and helped Hana elaborate, but many reflections stayed content-focused rather than using more complex reflections to explore deeper emotions or identity connections to smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on smoking reduction with concrete behavioral steps and progressive movement from exploration to actionable planning (gum strength, alarms, role‑play).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was joint problem-solving throughout—asking what would fit, co‑creating the pocket/prep system, and adapting suggestions to Hana's preferences and requests.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently offered choices, asked permission, and reinforced Hana's ownership of the plan, avoiding coercive language and supporting her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open questions and the confidence ruler elicited Hana's motives and practical ideas, but there were missed opportunities to link these motives to personal values and to amplify her expressions of desire and importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed the session as collaborative, eliciting Hana's expertise and preferences and sharing power in decision-making and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was actively elicited and reinforced with specific planning and confidence assessment, though the clinician could more strategically summarize and amplify Hana's own change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing, normalized ambivalence, and shifted quickly to small options, but could do more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both pulls and gently reduce sustain talk momentum.
