# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validated frustrations without blaming, creating a nonjudgmental tone that reduced defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflections capturing both logistical burdens and emotional stance, linking concerns to the patient's priorities (work/family).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on improving day-to-day asthma management and efficiently moved to a single, measurable action with implementation details and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—solicited the patient's ideas, integrated preferences into the plan, and invited joint troubleshooting rather than issuing directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, emphasizing voluntariness and experimentation, and asking how the patient preferred to check back in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Focused on drawing out the patient's own motivations and confidence using open questions and a confidence ruler, building on self-motivated statements rather than supplying reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner—asked permission before interventions, offered options, and co-created the plan with the patient guiding choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk by inviting a low-burden experiment, reinforcing specifics, and helping the patient articulate concrete success criteria.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoided arguing and redirected toward a pragmatic trial, but missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of sarcastic defensiveness.
