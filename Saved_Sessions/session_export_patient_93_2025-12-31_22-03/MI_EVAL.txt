# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated respect and nonjudgmental tone; used affirmations that conveyed positive regard. Occasional evaluative praise ("I’m proud of this step," "Well done") could be experienced as mild pressure rather than pure unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurate paraphrase and reflective summaries acknowledged ambivalence and values (health, independence, social life). Missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of underlying feelings (fear of losing independence, loneliness/boredom).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, consistent focus on reducing drinking with clear progression (assessment → options → specific rule → confidence scaling → follow‑up). Effectively guided toward actionable change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed plan; clinician invited preferences and choices (selected rule, alarm, text, check‑in). Maintained partnership stance and shared decision‑making throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Emphasized choice and respected Margaret’s pacing (cutting down vs quitting). Framed suggestions as options and reinforced patient ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited change talk (plans, concrete steps, confidence ruler) and reasons for change. Could have amplified and deepened intrinsic motivation and explored values/discrepancy more deliberately.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative, shared control of agenda and timing (agreeing on check‑in). Slight shifts into evaluative/directive phrasing at times reduced the fully equal partnership stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Multiple instances of change and commitment language elicited and summarized (alternate drinks, call son, set alarm). Would benefit from more strategic amplification and targeted follow‑ups to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and validated social importance; redirected sustain talk toward doable reductions. Could use more double‑sided reflections and explicit normalizing of ambivalence to further soften sustain talk.
