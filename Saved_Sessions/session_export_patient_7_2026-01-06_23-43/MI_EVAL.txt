# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, respects privacy, and offers options without blaming or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective listening (e.g., validates privacy and desire for quiet) and checks safety gently and nonintrusively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, clinically appropriate focus on engagement and safety, using repeated safety checks and a simple plan (nonverbal signal) to preserve focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the encounter as a joint effort by offering options, asking what would feel safe, and co-constructing boundaries (agreeing to silence and a hand-squeeze signal).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice throughout, explicitly honors requests (no calls, no questions) and negotiates a safety cue that preserves the client’s control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation attempted (initial open invitation) but limited thereafter; there were missed low-intensity, permission-based prompts that could lightly draw out internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the client as the expert and shares power (repeatedly stating 'you’re in charge' and following the client's lead).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Efforts to elicit change talk were minimal; after the client declined, the clinician prioritized silence and safety instead of trying one or two gentle prompts to evoke any small desire or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing resistance by reflecting and accommodating the client’s stance, offering alternatives, and providing a clear conditional safety exception.
