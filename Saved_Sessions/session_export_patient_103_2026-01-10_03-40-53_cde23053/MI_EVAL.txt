# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing and legitimizing the patient's frustration and concerns throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used frequent, accurate reflections and summaries that attended to both affect and concrete consequences, with clarifying questions that deepened understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on appointment delays, documentation, and job impact, steering the interaction toward concrete, actionable solutions (scripts, emails, escalation steps).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed messages and plans with the patient, solicited input, and adapted wording per the patient's requests, modeling an equal, iterative partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission before drafting materials, and framed recommendations as optional, preserving the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete commitments and preferences but could have probed more for the patient's internal reasons and values to amplify intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Privileged the patient's preferences with repeated invitations to choose options and approve wording, demonstrating genuine shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively scaffolded action and reinforced commitments (scripts, sending emails/texts) but missed opportunities to deepen change talk by exploring long‑term motives and desired outcomes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected to problem‑solving, but relied more on practical solutions than targeted MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to soften sustain talk.
