# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens with explicit nonjudgmental support and maintains a respectful, validating tone throughout, using statements that convey warmth and unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with multiple reflections that match the patient’s affect and meaning, inviting further exploration of emotions and motivations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on adolescent alcohol use and its impact on soccer/scholarship goals, consistently steering toward concrete prevention strategies and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in collaborative problem-solving by offering options, rehearsing scripts with the patient, and jointly developing plans rather than directing or lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports patient choice from the outset, offers alternatives, asks permission for role-play, and frames suggestions as options to preserve the patient’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient’s own reasons and strategies for change through open questions, scaling, and role-play, connecting change to personally important goals to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on his life, co-constructs solutions (scripts, signals, backup plans), and consistently defers to the patient’s judgment about what feels right.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with importance/confidence scaling, options selection, praise for change-oriented statements, and translation of goals into actionable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing minimizing sustain talk and redirects it toward consequences and goals, though could more strategically use double-sided reflections and summaries to soften ambivalence further.
