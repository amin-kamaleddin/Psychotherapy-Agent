**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating constraints and offering encouragement throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing activity with every intervention tied to concrete steps, progressing from choice to a specific implementation plan.

### Collaboration

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and guided Farid to a specific, doable plan, yet missed opportunities to selectively amplify and reflect change talk to strengthen commitment.
