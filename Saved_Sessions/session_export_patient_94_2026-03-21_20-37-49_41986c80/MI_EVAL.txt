# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, validated concerns, and respected the patient's time and boundaries, avoiding judgment or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening with repeated paraphrases and emotional mirroring, validating the patient's priorities and worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on asthma management decisions, guiding the session from concerns to options to concrete monitoring rules and a timed follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Built a cooperative plan by soliciting input at every decision point and tailoring recommendations to the patient's constraints and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and confirming the patient's preferences without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited preferences and some reasons for change through open questions and role-play but could probe deeper into values or life impacts to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shared power and co-created the plan, incorporating the patient's choices (e.g., skip meter, 7 pm call) and repeatedly inviting collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and nurtured change talk by asking open questions and offering doable options, but missed chances to amplify commitment with scaling or evocative follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward alternatives and problem-solving, though could use more double-sided reflections and exploration of consequences to further soften resistance.
