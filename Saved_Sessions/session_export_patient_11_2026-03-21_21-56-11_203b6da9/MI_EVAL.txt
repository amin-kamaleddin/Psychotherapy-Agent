# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with frequent affirmations and validations, accepting client limits and avoiding judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking that helped the client feel heard and scaffold planning, though deeper emotional reflections (e.g., discouragement or body-confidence concerns) would strengthen empathic resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with concrete strategies, scheduling, fallbacks, and a measurable plan without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by offering options, soliciting preferences, and adapting offerings so decisions were co-created and shaped by the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering multiple options, honoring preferences (e.g., deferring nutrition), normalizing setbacks, and avoiding directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from the client with evocative questions, elicited concrete change talk, and amplified the client's own reasons into specific commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative, asked permission before offering suggestions, and invited client-led choices that clearly shaped the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified desire, reasons, ability, and commitment language (including an 8/10 readiness) and used that material to build a concrete plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected to achievable activity steps, but could add more strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or selective evocation) to resolve ambivalence.
