# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (e.g., 'I’m here to listen and support you'), validated the patient's choices, and avoided confrontation when the patient declined role-play.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections and validations that recognized ambivalence and workplace concerns, showing good perspective-taking; reflections were more pragmatic than affective, so deeper emotional or complex reflections could strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on the concrete target behavior (adapting to a smoke-free workplace) and consistently steered the discussion toward actionable solutions and a clear follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative: strategies and manager language were co-created, permission was sought before suggestions, and the patient's input shaped the plan throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering options, emphasizing choice, and respecting the patient's decisions (e.g., accepting no role-play) while framing suggestions as optional tools.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some practical commitments (off-site breaks, mints, tracking) but did not systematically draw out the patient's deeper reasons, values, or confidence for change beyond workplace compliance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited the patient's preferences and co-created plans (e.g., tweaking manager lines, choosing monitoring level), demonstrating strong power-sharing and mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited preparatory and some commitment language about specific behaviors, but did not consistently amplify intrinsic motives or use scaling/confidence questions to strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and skillfully redirected to pragmatic problem-solving and options, though could add double-sided reflections or strategic summaries to further neutralize sustain talk.
