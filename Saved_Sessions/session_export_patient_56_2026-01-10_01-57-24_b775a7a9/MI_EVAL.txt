# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through warm, nonjudgmental language and normalization of difficult feelings, validating the client's concerns without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking, staying attuned to the client's feelings and concerns and acknowledging the sting of pushback.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a tight, practical focus on a specific target behavior (30-minute weekly recharge) and repeatedly returned to concrete steps and follow-up actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong two-way partnership by jointly drafting and revising scripts, role-playing on request, and negotiating feasible responses and plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice with multiple options, nonpressuring language, and respect for the client's preferences (e.g., avoiding the term 'boundaries').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively linked the request to the client's core value (family happiness) and elicited commitment behavior, but could probe importance and confidence more deeply to expand intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert and co-created the plan, inviting edits and honoring the client's decisions throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete steps, scripts, and a short trial, though could more systematically amplify the client's own language about reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used softening responses, trade-offs, and stepped-down asks to defuse resistance, but could employ more double-sided reflections to acknowledge both sides.
