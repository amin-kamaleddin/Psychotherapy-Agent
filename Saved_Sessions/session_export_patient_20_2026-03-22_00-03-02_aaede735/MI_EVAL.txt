# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalizing, nonjudgmental language and explicit affirmations of the patient’s limits and honesty. Avoids labeling or confrontation and treats guardedness with warmth throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and mirrors the patient’s language to deepen rapport. Summaries and perspective-taking helped Brandon elaborate feelings and practical concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a focused agenda tied to the concrete target of two alcohol-free weeknights and links questions and reflections to actionable strategies and measurable outcomes. Resists drifting and progressively narrows toward a realistic plan and commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way partnership with joint problem-solving and invitations to draft the plan together. Clinician elicits preferences and integrates the patient’s choices into the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and ownership by affirming limits (keeping weekends) and offering options instead of directives. Normalizes imperfection and preserves agency while helping the patient endorse the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient by prompting personally meaningful reasons and converting them into concrete steps. Questions elicit internal motivators (better mornings, confidence) and reinforce the patient’s own arguments for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session conveys power-sharing through repeated requests for the patient’s preference and co-creation of the plan. Offers to troubleshoot and jointly draft messages reinforced equality and shared responsibility.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and amplifies change language by asking for small realistic steps, soliciting strategies, and reflecting commitment statements. These moves produced multiple explicit change and commitment expressions from the patient.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids strengthening sustain talk and normalizes ambivalence, using double-sided summaries, but uses fewer advanced softening moves. Could more strategically deploy explicit double-sided reflections or discord-reduction to tip ambivalence further toward change.
