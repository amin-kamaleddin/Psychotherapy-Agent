# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard, warmth, and nonjudgmental validation throughout the session, including routine safety checks. This supportive stance normalized setbacks and fostered a respectful environment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections, validation of emotion, and targeted empathic questions demonstrated strong attunement to Luka’s experience. The clinician picked up on cues about triggers and skepticism and responded with tailored suggestions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The conversation stayed focused on key behavioral targets (reducing alcohol and managing anger) with a concrete one‑week plan and scheduled follow‑up. The clinician repeatedly brought discussion back to specific, achievable steps to create momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session was a clear joint problem‑solving effort with frequent checks for feasibility and agreement, ensuring Luka’s input shaped the plan. Advice was offered as options and the clinician co‑constructed strategies with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices and options were consistently offered and decisions were left to Luka, framing suggestions as experiments and emphasizing self‑direction. The clinician avoided coercion and reinforced ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited some of Luka’s motivations and resources (what helped cut down, partner support) but often offered practical strategies rather than systematically drawing out intrinsic reasons and long‑term values. More scaling or values linking would deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician fostered power‑sharing by inviting choices, asking which options Luka preferred, and checking logistics together. The menu approach and collaborative tone respected Luka’s expertise about his life and constraints.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited and reinforced change talk, praised progress, and summarized a concrete plan which encouraged commitment. Missed opportunities included deeper exploration of personal motives and eliciting confidence (e.g., scaling questions).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and normalized ambivalence while redirecting toward change with small, achievable options. However, there was limited use of MI techniques like double‑sided reflections or strategic discrepancy to more actively soften sustain talk.
