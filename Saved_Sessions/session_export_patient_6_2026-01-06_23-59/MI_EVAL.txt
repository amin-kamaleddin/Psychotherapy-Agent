# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and framing the conversation as collaborative to reduce defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent perspective-taking with complex reflections that capture both content and feeling and paraphrases the patient's goals and worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on alcohol use and its connection to soccer performance and scholarships, moving purposefully from ambivalence toward concrete planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—invites the patient's agenda, validates ideas, and co-constructs a realistic plan while repeatedly asking for the patient's input on wording and approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and personal responsibility by offering options and asking which strategy the patient prefers, preserving the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivations and self-generated strategies linked to core values, but could deepen evocation by probing importance/confidence and eliciting a clearer commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as shared problem-solving and defers to the patient's expertise, repeatedly inviting the patient to choose and shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and follows with concrete planning, though there were missed opportunities to amplify change statements and elicit explicit commitment or confidence ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and mirrors sustain talk without arguing and redirects to values, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections or early selective amplification to soften sustain talk further.
