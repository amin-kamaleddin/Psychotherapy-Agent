# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental tone and affirmed realistic steps; virtually no blaming or shaming language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that built rapport; could deepen emotional nuance with more complex reflections exploring frustration, fear, or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and concrete next steps (CO/spirometry, scripts, follow-up) while avoiding coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated an equal partnership by inviting choices, checking language, and co-constructing plans with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice and ownership by offering options, asking preferences, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient’s motivations and used them to form a plan, but could ask more open, evocative questions to draw out values, confidence, and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance throughout, asking permission and deferring to the patient’s preferences to share power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively invited and reinforced change talk (scaling, pros/cons) and translated it into action steps, yet missed opportunities to amplify client-generated change language and explore values more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation but did not consistently use double-sided reflections or evocative questions to soften sustain talk, often moving quickly to solutions rather than exploring ambivalence.
