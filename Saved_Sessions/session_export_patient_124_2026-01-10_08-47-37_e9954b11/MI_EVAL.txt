# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental tone, normalizing struggles and repeatedly respecting Sofia’s stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking validated Sofia’s feelings and ambivalence, though there were a few missed opportunities to deepen emotional content (e.g., family expectations/social-belonging).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on marijuana use with concrete questions, a tracking plan, and follow-up arrangements that tied discussion to actionable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—Sofia was invited to choose options, co-designed a simple tracker, and the clinician framed next steps as joint work with review planned together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by repeatedly offering choices, respecting Sofia’s decisions and limits, and avoiding pressure or coercion throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation via open choice questions, but tended to offer information and tools early rather than drawing out Sofia’s own reasons, values, or discrepancy to elicit intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a partnership by checking preferences and inviting input, though at times the clinician introduced specific plans/tools early instead of co-constructing them step-by-step with Sofia.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited tentative change language and affirmed tracking, but did not consistently follow up to amplify or explore change statements (e.g., importance/confidence scaling or elaboration on 'clearer thinking').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and avoided judgment, yet primarily moved to practical suggestions rather than using strategic softening techniques (double-sided reflections, reframing) to diminish sustain talk.
