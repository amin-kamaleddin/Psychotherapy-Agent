# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and warmth through normalizing reflections and explicit reassurance, avoiding blame or lecturing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely empathic understanding—reflections capture both history and emotions and deepen the patient's expressed ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction, progressing logically from eliciting ideas to refining a concrete plan to delay the first cigarette by 30 minutes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative and equal, with the clinician soliciting preferences and co-designing supports and check‑ins that fit the patient's life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering options, asking which supports the patient prefers, and framing suggestions as invitations rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motives and reasons through evocative questions and reflections, moving her from concern to concrete personal goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Recurrently positions the patient as the expert and invites collaboration, asking permission and deferring to her knowledge when co‑creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with focused questions and reflective summaries, helping the patient articulate specific, actionable steps and wins.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects to actionable planning without arguing, but could more often use double‑sided reflections and stronger amplification of change statements to further weaken sustain talk.
