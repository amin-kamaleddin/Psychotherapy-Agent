# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating the client's struggles and creating a safe, respectful tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing ambivalence and concerns, but missed deeper exploration of underlying emotions like discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on returning to morning exercise with tightly linked questions and progressively narrowed to a feasible, concrete plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co-constructed with joint problem-solving, frequent invitations for preferences, and decisions driven by the client's choices and buy-in.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the client's autonomy by offering multiple options, asking which felt doable, and respecting Rafael's selections without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited multiple concrete reasons and plans for change and had the client generate solutions, but did not sufficiently probe personal values or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative, deferring to the client's expertise and repeatedly asking Rafael to choose strategies that fit him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and developed specific change language and small steps, though could better amplify and selectively reflect stronger change statements to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk using double-sided reflections and redirection to supports, but missed some opportunities to selectively reinforce pro-change statements or explore costs of staying the same.
