# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard and respect, validated Ingrid's time pressure and initiative, and maintained a supportive, nonjudgmental tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective‑taking with accurate reflections of being rushed and preference for concise formats, but could use more complex reflections naming underlying feelings or burdens.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, specific focus on medication safety maintained throughout, with targeted clinical information and a practical follow‑up (one‑page checklist/email).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction treated as joint problem‑solving—asked preferences and tailored the document—but it stopped short of inviting Ingrid to co‑design checklist content beyond contact details.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Ingrid's choices and offered options rather than directives, and respected her decision to leave without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivation (safety, desire for a quick solution) but did not probe values, confidence, or use Elicit‑Provide‑Elicit to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Language was collaborative and consultative with power‑sharing around format and details, though it did not fully engage Ingrid in shaping the checklist content.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change‑oriented language and commitment to receive the PDF/email were elicited, but opportunities to follow up, amplify reasons, or assess confidence were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by accommodating time constraints with concise options and respect, though a double‑sided reflection could have further softened sustain talk.
