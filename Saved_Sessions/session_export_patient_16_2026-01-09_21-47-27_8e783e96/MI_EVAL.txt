# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validated the client's values and strengths, using warm, nonjudgmental language and supportive summaries throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries captured both content and implied emotion (care for team, peer pressure), sustaining empathic listening across multiple turns to guide the conversation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on vaping and proximal behavior change, moving logically from goal-setting to specific strategies and rehearsals without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative, inviting the client's input at every step and co-designing plans and role-plays so the client’s preferences shaped the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's choice and offered options rather than directives, letting the client choose which parts to troubleshoot and how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motivation tied to team performance and confidence but could have probed deeper values, longer-term reasons, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the session as a joint enterprise, asking which part to prioritize and deferring to the client to select role‑plays and refusal lines.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk with targeted questions and reflections, producing concrete plans (one‑month goal, 7/10 confidence) and operationalized strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward planning, but missed opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections that acknowledge enjoyable aspects while linking to the client’s goals.
