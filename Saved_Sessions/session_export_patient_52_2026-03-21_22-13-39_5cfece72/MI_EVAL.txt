### Acceptance

**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental, warm regard with frequent validation and normalization, praising realistic efforts without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries captured Irina’s ambivalence and concrete details, demonstrating sustained perspective-taking and emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on reducing alcohol to one drink with measurable, time-bound planning and repeated returns to concrete steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was highly collaborative, inviting Irina’s input at each step, co-creating plans, and offering editable drafts and options rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently affirmed choice and agency by offering options, asking preferences for supports and check-ins, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician drew out Irina’s own motivations, supports, and concrete ideas (importance/confidence, triggers, alternatives) and converted them into client-generated strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Language and behavior emphasized joint decision-making and equal partnership, treating Irina as the expert and inviting co-planning throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician actively elicited and reinforced change talk using scaling questions, follow-up probes, and positive reinforcement to amplify Irina’s commitment and plan.
