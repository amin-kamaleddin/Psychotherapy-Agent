# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently expresses nonjudgmental support, normalizes concerns, and affirms the patient’s goals, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and frequent reflections tying recommendations to the patient’s values, though reflections were often brief and missed opportunities to explore deeper emotion behind ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear, appropriate focus on medication acceptance/adherence with concrete stepwise plans and consistent linkage to the patient’s goal of staying active in retirement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is framed as cooperative with negotiated decisions, invitations for input, offers to co-write scripts and role‑play, and positioning the clinician as a helper rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician supports choice and shared decision-making by offering options (time-limited trial, dose alternatives), asking preferences, and avoiding pressuring language to foster patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to draw out motivations (linking to retirement, asking about planning the doctor visit), but the session relied heavily on information-giving and missed systematic evocative questions to elicit intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and inviting of patient input with co-created plans, though at times the clinician shifted to an expert/informational role without first eliciting the patient’s perspective on options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician affirms and elicits some change statements and offers role‑play, but relies more on practical planning and information than on systematically evoking, amplifying, and summarizing change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and normalized and addressed with factual information, but MI-specific softening strategies (double-sided reflections or strategic summaries) were underused to resolve ambivalence.
