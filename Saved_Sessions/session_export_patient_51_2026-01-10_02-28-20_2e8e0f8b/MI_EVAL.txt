# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Communicates clear unconditional positive regard and validates the patient's experience and strengths throughout; could occasionally normalize missed doses explicitly to deepen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring content and affect; could occasionally use more complex reflections to deepen empathic resonance around ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a tight, appropriate focus on medication management and moves strategically from problem identification to a specific, measurable one-month experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a clear partnership, soliciting lived experience and jointly co-creating feasible strategies with shared decision-making and frequent preference checks.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice and frames the plan as an experiment the patient can tailor or stop, supporting ownership and avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations, past successes, and practical ideas, and builds on those resources rather than imposing solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as collaborative and defers to the patient's expertise, offering options while leaving decisions and details to the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits and amplifies change talk, drawing out concrete plans and indicators of success; a final explicit commitment prompt could strengthen follow-through.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally contains sustain talk by validating realism and redirecting to alternatives, but would benefit from more double-sided reflections and proactive elicitation of plans for days when barriers arise.
