# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, with normalizing statements and options that conveyed warmth and respect throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that matched ambivalence, but reflections were mostly summary/behavioral and missed deeper affective exploration of feelings like loneliness or fear of rejection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking and moved logically from ambivalence to concrete, behaviorally specific goals and implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a genuine partnership with frequent invitations for input, co-creative language, and negotiated decisions rather than prescriptions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Elise's control by offering options, asking what felt doable, and avoiding pressuring language, supporting ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation with open questions and a readiness ruler and drew out concrete steps, but missed opportunities to probe values and imagined outcomes to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently asked permission, offered choices, and co-created the plan so Elise's preferences and decisions shaped the session and outcomes.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and translated it into actionable steps, though there were missed chances to amplify and deepen commitment language with follow-up probes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed sustain talk into a doable trial, but could have used more double-sided reflections and strategic redirection of statements like 'instant friends' toward values or costs.
