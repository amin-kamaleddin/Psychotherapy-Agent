# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing concerns and praising progress without blame. Language is warm and validating throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring both content and feeling. Probes gently to understand triggers and the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction/cessation with all questions and suggestions tied to that goal. Balances exploration and action planning without drifting from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is generally collaborative, eliciting preferences and shared planning. Occasionally supplies concrete scripts and instructions rather than first inviting the patient to generate wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respects Hana's autonomy by offering options, asking permission, and framing suggestions as choices. Encourages ownership of decisions and preserves patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient by eliciting her reasons and using scaling to surface motivation and confidence. Leads Hana to articulate concrete motivations and commit to specific actions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Overall collaborative and client-centered, soliciting preferences and involving Hana in planning. At times shifts toward an expert role by providing scripts without first inviting Hana to produce her own wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk using open questions, scaling, and prompts to identify small first steps. Moves the patient from contemplation to a concrete, realistic plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects ambivalence toward planning and coping strategies. Could use more explicit double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while leaning toward change.
