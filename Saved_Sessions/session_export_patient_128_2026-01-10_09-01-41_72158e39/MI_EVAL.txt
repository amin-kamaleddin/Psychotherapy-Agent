### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental positive regard with normalizing reflections and explicit support; no shaming and clear affirmation of Tyler's values and efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening that validated ambivalence and tied together Tyler's motives and consequences effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol-related harm reduction with concrete, stepwise planning and role-play to keep momentum toward behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Autonomy Support

**Comments:** Elicited internal reasons and asked about importance and feasibility, but sometimes shifted quickly to offering language and strategies rather than deepening Tyler's own elaboration and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong power-sharing by inviting Tyler to lead decisions, offering role-plays, and testing feasibility together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reinforced Tyler's change language and commitments (e.g., leaving earlier) but could pause more to elicit expanded, spontaneous change talk before offering solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward practical tactics, though relied more on replacing sustain talk with strategies than on double-sided or amplify-change reflections.
