# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (validated concerns, normalized slips, respected agenda and constraints).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good validation of fears and barriers and offered parenting-specific strategies, but missed deeper emotional reflections and summaries of ambivalence. 

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking throughout, obtained baseline, set a concrete 1‑week goal and scheduled a specific follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative—patient shaped the agenda and checklist—but clinician sometimes offered multiple expert-driven suggestions instead of eliciting the patient's ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by asking permission, honoring limits (declining to discuss alcohol), and offering options without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some reasons and a readiness rating, but did not systematically draw out values, discrepancies, or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and co‑created the plan (checklist tailored to patient/country), though at times the clinician led with recommendations rather than inviting choice among options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and translated it into a concrete plan, but did not strategically reflect or amplify pro‑change statements or explore confidence and commitment further.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoided arguing and redirected to change, but relied on neutral/pragmatic moves instead of MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to actively soften sustain talk.
