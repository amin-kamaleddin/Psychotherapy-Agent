# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—affirms progress, normalizes struggles, and avoids judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking, validating feelings like discouragement, but could deepen emotional exploration of statements such as “feeling useless.”

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (gym, back-friendly routine, reminders) and consistently guides the conversation toward concrete planning and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine joint problem‑solving by repeatedly checking preferences, offering options, and co-creating the schedule, routines, and reminders with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and agency by offering options, asking permission, and honoring the client’s decisions without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits values and reasons (keeping up with kids, less pain) and uses scaling, but could more fully draw out and amplify the client’s own motivational statements and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a true partnership—shares decision-making, invites input, and co-constructs the plan in ways that center the client’s preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (progress, concrete steps, confidence scaling) yet could more often reflect and amplify client-originated change statements to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and redirects to coping strategies without arguing, but could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly name sustain vs. change and defuse helplessness.
