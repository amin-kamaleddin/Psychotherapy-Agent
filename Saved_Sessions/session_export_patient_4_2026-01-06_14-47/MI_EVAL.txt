# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validated the patient's distress, and honored requests (helped draft letters/scripts), demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate empathic reflections and safety checks and offered grounding, but reflections were somewhat surface-level and often shifted quickly to problem-solving rather than deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on urgent investigations, expedited GI referral, and safety, repeatedly returning to concrete goals, test lists, and rehearsal lines.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked alongside the patient to create the letter, scripts, and rehearsal and invited input, though multiple clinician-authored drafts and directive advice made parts of the interaction more clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respected choices, asked permission, and offered options (breathing, private referral), but sometimes pushed specific tests and exact wording, limiting patient ownership over priorities.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some immediate intentions to act (bringing letter, desire for tests, rehearse) but did not systematically draw out the patient's own motivations, values, barriers, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Included collaborative moves (asking permission, co-creating materials) but frequently adopted an expert stance by specifying detailed tests and wording without fully inviting the patient's prioritization.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced actionable steps the patient wanted (lists, scripts, rehearsal) but did little to elicit or amplify intrinsic change talk about coping, longer-term goals, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and honored sustain talk, but repeatedly accommodated requests to exclude stress attribution and relied on biomedical recommendations rather than MI strategies like double-sided reflections or gentle reframing.
