# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Jordan's feelings and normalizing difficulty without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that tracked emotions and guilt; could deepen empathy by exploring the affect behind statements and the meaning of 'friends' for Jordan.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on preventing shoplifting with concrete, action-oriented risk-reduction steps and linkage to legal urgency and follow-up care.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with co-created plans and role-play, but at times the clinician moved into a more instructive role with detailed scripts rather than eliciting alternatives from Jordan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, affirming decisions, and avoiding coercive language to reinforce ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change statements and commitments, but relied more on clinician-offered strategies than systematically drawing out Jordan's own values, reasons, and motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited input, yet occasionally assumed an expert problem-solver role with prescriptive phrasing instead of eliciting Jordan's own language and solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete commitment questions and planning; could further amplify spontaneous change language and use scaling/confidence queries to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and avoided confrontation but often shifted quickly to problem-solving without using double-sided reflections or exploring ambivalence to soften sustain talk.
