# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental stance (e.g., normalizing slips, offering choices). Avoided criticism or confrontation and maintained a low‑pressure approach throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used frequent, accurate reflective listening that captured the patient’s perspective and validated feelings. Could deepen affective reflection and use more complex reflections to explore the emotional meaning behind statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on smoking and produced a concrete, measurable experiment with specific next steps. Repeatedly returned to the plan and follow‑up without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was a clear collaboration between equals with the patient’s input guiding decisions and plan details. The clinician honored the patient’s preferences for follow‑up and adapted to his wishes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, emphasizing reversibility, and checking the patient’s preferences. Reinforced control over decisions and accepted the patient’s pacing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some self‑generated motivation and practical resources (options, confidence scaling, planning). Missed opportunities to draw out deeper intrinsic reasons, values, and past successes to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed a collaborative stance and co‑constructed the experiment, asking which options felt realistic. Power was shared and the patient influenced session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened concrete change talk through options, a confidence rating, and planning (delay, alarm, if/then). Could have amplified and elaborated change talk more to deepen desire, reasons, and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing, acknowledged sustain talk, and normalized ambivalence while pivoting to small experiments. Could use more double‑sided reflections to gently highlight discrepancy between sustain and change sides.
