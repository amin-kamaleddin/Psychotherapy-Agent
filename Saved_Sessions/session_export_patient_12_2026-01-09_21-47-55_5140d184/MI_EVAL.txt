# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and validation, normalizing ambivalence and affirming strengths. Tone is supportive and nonjudgmental throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, sustained perspective-taking with frequent reflections and attention to emotional nuance. Tracks repeating themes like helplessness and shame effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (one-day trial and breathing practice) and builds progressive momentum toward a time-bound plan. Questions and reflections repeatedly tie back to the concrete goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session reads as joint problem-solving with frequent invitations for the client's input and co-construction of the plan. Occasional directive suggestions (e.g., proposing two alarms) slightly reduce the sense of fully equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports client choice by offering options, eliciting preferences, and respecting pace without pressure. Client ownership of decisions is consistently reinforced.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own reasons and resources using evocative questions and by highlighting the client's language. Rarely relies on external motives, instead drawing out personally meaningful change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally treats the client as the expert and invites decisions about specifics, fostering collaboration. Occasional recommendation-style phrasing without explicit permission slightly tilts toward clinician authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, amplifies, and reinforces change talk, using labels and evocative questions to draw out desire, ability, and reasons for change. Prompts concrete, doable steps that strengthen change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and validates sustain talk respectfully and sometimes pivots toward change, but often mirrors sustain statements rather than using stronger double-sided reflections or selectively emphasizing the change side. There are missed opportunities to more strategically weaken sustain talk.
