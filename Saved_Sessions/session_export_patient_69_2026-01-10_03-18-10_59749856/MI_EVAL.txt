### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, high-fidelity reflections that capture both affect and content and echoes the client's language. Demonstrates clear perspective-taking and understanding throughout the exchange.

### Direction

**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by inviting input (e.g., tweaking wording, offering help setting alarms) and treating the client as an active participant. At times the clinician steers with detailed proposals instead of eliciting more client-generated language.
