# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and praises concrete actions (e.g., values-based reflections and affirmations), creating a nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and accurate reflections, validating ambivalence and nervousness. Could deepen empathic depth by more frequently exploring emotions like remorse, shame, and fear rather than moving quickly to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (reducing alcohol, living arrangements, safety) and consistently returns to concrete, actionable steps and timelines (e.g., booze-free days, role‑play scheduling).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly a shared endeavor: the clinician negotiates rehearsal order, asks for Wiremu’s preferences, and co-constructs scripts and coping plans rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by offering options, inviting Wiremu’s own wording and steps, and avoiding pressure when suggestions are declined, reinforcing his sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk and concrete plans, but could increase intrinsic motivation by asking more discrepancy and confidence probes (e.g., 'Why an 8 and not a 4?') and exploring barriers before offering solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Wiremu as collaborator and expert on his life, repeatedly checking what he wants to practice and co-creating next steps, which sustains shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change language and converts values into specific, doable steps (e.g., choosing Wednesdays and Sundays, committing to role‑play and coping strategies).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change with trial solutions, but would benefit from more double-sided reflections and explicit reframing to name both sides of ambivalence.
