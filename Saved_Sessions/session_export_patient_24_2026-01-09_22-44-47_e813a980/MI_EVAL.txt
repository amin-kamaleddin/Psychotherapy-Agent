# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and uses validating language that honors the patient’s pace and choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with timely, tailored complex reflections that capture both pride in training and concern about drinking, reducing defensiveness and keeping the patient engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on alcohol as it relates to marathon performance, repeatedly ties discussion to measurable outcomes, and secures a concrete two-week trial.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in joint problem-solving by soliciting the patient’s preferences for tracking, co-creating a simple template, and shaping the plan together in an egalitarian manner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms choice and avoids coercion by offering options, framing suggestions rather than prescriptions, and emphasizing a low-key, patient-led approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient’s internal reasons for change by linking the experiment to valued goals (marathon performance) and drawing out client-generated motivations and intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert, checks preferences, offers choices, and lets the patient drive decisions, demonstrating genuine power-sharing throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk by asking about realistic first steps and meaningful outcomes, leading to specific commitments (two-week tracking, swapping weeknight beers).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and acknowledges sustain talk, but misses some opportunities for double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of minimizations/skepticism that could further reduce resistance.
