**Comments:** Demonstrates consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing Maria's pain and honoring her request to be heard while thanking her for protective actions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent empathic listening with frequent reflective paraphrases and mirroring of affect and content that validates the intensity and ambivalence of Maria's suffering.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Makes some attempts to elicit and reinforce change talk and acknowledges protective actions, but misses opportunities to deepen and amplify desire/ability/need statements for sustained motivation.
