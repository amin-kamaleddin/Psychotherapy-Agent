# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with warm openings, multiple affirmations, and validating reflections; avoided blaming or moralizing language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening with accurate, timely reflections of content and somatic experience; could deepen emotional reflections and use more complex reflections to capture underlying hopelessness and resignation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, safety-focused direction by assessing plan/intent/means, monitoring urge intensity, implementing concrete safety strategies, and providing contingency instructions without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a clear collaborative partner by repeatedly soliciting Liora's preferences, offering options, co-drafting messages, and adjusting interventions to her comfort level.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by asking permission before interventions, presenting noncoercive options, and honoring Liora's choices while negotiating doable steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts to draw out internal motivation (urges, doable options), but overall evocation was superficial and missed opportunities to elicit reasons, values, past successes, or amplify change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fostered a partnership stance—inviting input, offering choices, and positioning Liora as the decision-maker in safety planning and next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempted to elicit change language with options and questions, but did not consistently amplify or follow up on explicit change statements (e.g., 'I don’t want to keep doing it').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered alternatives, but missed systematic strategies (double-sided reflections, reframes) to diminish sustain talk and elicit reasons for change.
