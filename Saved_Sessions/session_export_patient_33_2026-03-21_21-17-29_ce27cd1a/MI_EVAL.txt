### Empathy

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Reflected and amplified change talk (desire for less danger, readiness) and helped convert intentions into steps; missed opportunities to probe the reasons behind his 10/10 readiness and to explore confidence and barriers more deeply.
