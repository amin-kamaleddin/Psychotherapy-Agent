# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used nonjudgmental, respectful language, normalized concerns, and conducted a safety check, conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening and permission-seeking but missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections to explore the patient's feelings and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on mini-cigar use and concrete behavior-change steps, summarizing and refining a specific one-week plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged the patient as an equal partner, co-creating the plan, offering options, and repeatedly checking preferences and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly asked permission, offered choices, respected the patient's readiness to decline a quit plan, and supported a patient-led short trial without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation (frequency, confidence ruler, one-week trial) but the session relied heavily on clinician-provided strategies and did not deeply explore the patient's personal motives or values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and negotiated the plan, though could more explicitly frame the patient as the expert and solicit his ideas and past successes.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced concrete commitments and used a confidence ruler, but could have used more open questions and amplification to draw out the patient's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and responded with neutral information and permission-based questions, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections to gently highlight discrepancy.
