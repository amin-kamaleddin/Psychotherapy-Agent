# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support throughout, with warm, respectful language and validation of progress. Avoided blame and reframed slips as information rather than failure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening, validating feelings and summarizing ambivalence effectively. Reflections could be deeper and more complex to explore bodily sensations and richer emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, sustained focus on the target behavior (smoke-free lunch) and anchored the session to specific, actionable goals and plans. Direction was precise and consistently reinforced.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the exchange as a joint enterprise, soliciting Samuel's input and integrating his ideas into the plan (e.g., rain backup, second alarm). The interaction read as a genuine partnership with shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Samuel's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and respecting his pace and preferences. Avoided pressuring language and reinforced that slips are not moral failures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation through confidence rulers, self-reported progress, and co-created implementation intentions that drew ownership from Samuel. Techniques consistently amplified his change language and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative tone and invited Samuel to choose options and co-create plans. At times the clinician offered many specific suggestions, which leaned toward directive coaching rather than leaving more space for client-generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, praised reductions, used confidence rulers, and helped convert vague desire into concrete commitments and plans. Encouraged specific steps and a clear check-in plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively minimized sustain talk by pivoting to strategies and planning when doubts arose. Missed opportunities for more strategic softening (e.g., double-sided reflections or selective reflections that reduce the pull of sustain language).
