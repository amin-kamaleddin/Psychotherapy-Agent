# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing concerns and reinforcing the patient's self-advocacy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated perspective-taking and useful reflections mirroring concrete concerns, but reflections were generally brief and missed deeper emotional or tonal nuances.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on cholesterol management and medication hesitancy, translating ambivalence into concrete next steps (labs, lifestyle, second opinion, 3-month recheck).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively by offering options, co-drafting the second-opinion script, and incorporating the patient's preferences into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice and ownership, avoided pressure, and reinforced decision authority while offering information and options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some behavioral commitments through offered options, but did not sufficiently draw out the patient's intrinsic motivators, values, or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely acted as a partner by asking preferences and co-constructing plans, though at times shifted into an informational doctor voice without first eliciting permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Used choice and options to generate some change talk and commitments, but did not systematically probe importance/confidence or use scaling/open questions to amplify motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not reinforce sustain talk, offering alternatives and neutral redirections; could strengthen use of double-sided reflections and summaries to resolve ambivalence.
