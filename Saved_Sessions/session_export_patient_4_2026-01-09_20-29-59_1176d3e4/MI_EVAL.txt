# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating the patient’s fears and acknowledging symptom severity; avoids blaming or minimizing language throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening and acknowledges exhaustion and fear, with practical supports offered; missed opportunities for deeper reflective statements capturing ambivalence and emotional subtext.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a very clear, sustained focus on obtaining investigations and a GI referral, repeatedly returning to specific tests, scripts, and timing to meet the patient's goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely cooperative with invitations to choose options and rehearse responses, though clinician-led medical guidance sometimes dominates the dialogue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirms patient choice and offers multiple escalation options and role‑play only if desired, while occasionally giving firm, prescriptive recommendations that slightly reduce flexibility.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Largely informational and coaching with few attempts to elicit the patient's own reasons, values, or confidence for change; limited reflection and amplification of change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows collaborative elements by asking preferences and offering rehearsal, but often assumes the expert role with prescriptive tests and timelines instead of co‑constructing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal elicitation or deepening of the patient's change language; the session is mainly directive and advice‑giving rather than strategically drawing out motivations or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and provides rebuttal scripts to counter dismissal, but uses few MI strategies (e.g., double‑sided reflections or rolling with resistance) to soften sustain talk internally.
