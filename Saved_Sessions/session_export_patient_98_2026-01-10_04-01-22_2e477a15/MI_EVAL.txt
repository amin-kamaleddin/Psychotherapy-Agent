# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Mateo’s pace, avoiding pressure or judgment; acceptance is explicit, genuine, and well‑timed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate summaries of both feeling and behavior; many reflections are near‑verbatim so using more complex reflections to capture deeper emotion would deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on rehab behavior and builds a specific, measurable plan with follow‑up; questions and summaries continually tie back to observable outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicits Mateo’s input on concrete details (anchor, tick box, text content/time) and co‑creates the plan, showing mutual problem‑solving and shared responsibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms choice and provides explicit permission to stop or modify activities, reinforcing Mateo’s ownership of the plan and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Mateo’s own reasons and practical anchors for change (coffee routine, criteria for success); could deepen evocation by exploring values and confidence and amplifying change statements into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the interaction as co‑constructed—invites preferences, asks for input, and positions Mateo as an equal contributor to planning and follow‑up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change talk and builds on past success to produce a doable plan; would benefit from more amplification of change statements and explicit measures of confidence/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation but often mirrors sustain language in ways that may reinforce it; missed opportunities for double‑sided or strategic reflections to pivot toward change.
