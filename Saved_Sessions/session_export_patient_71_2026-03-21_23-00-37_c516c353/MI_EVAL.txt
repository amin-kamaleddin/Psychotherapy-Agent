# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, normalizing setbacks and affirming caring. No shaming or minimizing present.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent perspective-taking and reflective listening, mirroring Laura's language and emotional tone. Reflections capture mixed feelings and subtleties throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on aligning actions with values and progressively narrows to a concrete, actionable plan (calendar block, alarm, 30‑sec breath). Questions consistently move the conversation toward feasibility and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a joint problem-solving effort with the clinician building on Laura's ideas and refining options together. Language and actions position the clinician as a helper, not a director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice, offers options rather than directives, and invites Laura to select what feels doable. Reflections reinforce Laura's agency and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Laura's motivations and reasons for change (groundedness, showing up better), but could further deepen intrinsic motivation with more vivid future-oriented questions and scaling. Evocation is strong but not maximally amplified.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats Laura as the expert on her life, asking collaborative questions and inviting choice among options. The clinician co-constructs the plan with Laura and shares power in decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and reinforces change talk and planning language (calendar block, alarm, habit cues), yet misses opportunities to amplify commitment and confidence (e.g., scaling or explicit commitment statements).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk and normalizes lapses without confrontation, steering toward manageable alternatives. Could be more strategic with double-sided reflections and eliciting costs of staying the same.
