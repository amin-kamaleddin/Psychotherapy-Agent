# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and respect, repeatedly validates progress and normalizes setbacks. No judgmental language and frequent affirmations of Samuel's efforts and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and basic reflective listening by acknowledging feelings and normalizing cravings. Would benefit from deeper exploration of ambivalence and underlying fears or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with interventions tied to measurable goals and monitoring. Regularly brings the conversation back to concrete actions like the if-then plan and journaling.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructs plans and tools with Samuel, adapts wording on request, and sets up joint review, positioning the clinician as a helper rather than an authority. Invitations for input are frequent and genuine.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers multiple options, asks which the client prefers, and frames rules as testable suggestions rather than mandates. Reinforces Samuel's decision-making and control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws out practical motivation and planning but is somewhat clinician-driven with many concrete suggestions. Fewer questions were used to elicit deeper internal motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative, client-led approach by asking permission, co-creating the if-then plan and journal, and inviting Samuel's input. Language and actions consistently convey shared problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and amplifies change talk through praise and planning, and asks preference-based questions to elicit steps. Missed opportunities to deepen and amplify Samuel's own motivational statements and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently avoids reinforcing sustain talk, reframes slips as data rather than failure, and redirects focus to small, testable strategies and contingency planning. Provides backup options rather than arguing with sustain reasons.
