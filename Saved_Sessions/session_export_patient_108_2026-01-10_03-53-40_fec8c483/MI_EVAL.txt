# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard—normalizing feelings and praising effort without blame. Warmth is genuine and maintained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate perspective-taking with timely reflections that mirror emotion and meaning. Minor opportunities existed to probe deeper nuances (e.g., shame or fear of loss).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on concrete behavioral goals (Jan 24, text, backups, rehearsals) and repeatedly steers the session toward actionable steps. Directional leadership is strong while remaining client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed as a joint effort with frequent solicitation of Oliver's input; suggestions are tailored to his preferences and co-created rather than imposed. Role-plays and brief feedback reinforced equality and shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers options and asks which approach Oliver prefers, avoiding pressure and reinforcing his control over timing and choices. Clinician gives tips rather than commands to support ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited substantial motivation and translated it into a concrete plan but sometimes supplied language/tools instead of fully drawing out Oliver's own reasons. Could use more strategic open questions and scaling to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and shared decision-making by offering options, checking preferences, and inviting Oliver to lead rehearsals. The plan is co-constructed and aligned with the client's goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through open questions, date selection, and affirmations, and elicits specific steps. Could pause more after change statements to amplify commitment and use confidence/importance ratings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward action while normalizing ambivalence. Would be strengthened by more double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to gently emphasize costs of the status quo.
