# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard—praised choices, normalized experimentation, and explicitly honored the client's ritual and preferences throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used strong reflective listening and perspective-taking (notably an early complex reflection), but reflections were sometimes brief and solution-focused rather than exploring deeper emotions or summarizing internal conflict.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on coffee reduction and systematically moved to a specific, time-bound SMART-style plan with start date, tracking method, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co‑created the plan with the patient, invited input at each step, and adapted recommendations to the patient's stated preferences and rituals.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's freedom to choose, offered multiple options framed as experiments, and avoided pressuring language to preserve patient ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation via scaling and confidence questions, but relied heavily on clinician-generated solutions and did not sufficiently draw out the patient's personal values, reasons, or amplify fleeting change statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly framed the interaction as joint exploration—asked preferences, negotiated wording and logistics, and ensured the patient's choices directly shaped the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete change talk and commitment using a menu and scaling ruler, converting interest into a specific test, though at times the clinician moved quickly to offer strategies rather than deepening client-generated reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and honored coffee's benefits while offering reversible tests, but could increase use of double-sided reflections and strategic reframing to more actively pivot sustain talk toward change.
