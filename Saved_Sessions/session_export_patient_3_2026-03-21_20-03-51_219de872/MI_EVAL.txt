# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, using validating language and normalizing statements throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and captured practical barriers, but missed some opportunities to probe and reflect deeper emotional distress (e.g., shame, “I feel gross,” loss of confidence).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on resuming exercise and moved efficiently from goals to concrete actions (schedule, backups, accountability).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—solicited the patient’s input at each step and co-developed tools (messages, reminders, role‑play) with the patient as an equal partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before intervening, and normalizing the patient’s right to accept or decline suggestions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out intrinsic motivators and practical enablers with evocative questions, amplifying the patient’s own reasons for change and translating them into action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a genuine partnership stance—checked preferences, invited choices, and shared power in creating the plan and messages.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with targeted questions and reflections, and helped convert expressed motivations into a concrete, doable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and provided backups/normalizing, but could have used more explicit softening techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections or immediate amplification of change language).
