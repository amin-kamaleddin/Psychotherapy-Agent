# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful with normalizing statements and supportive remarks, demonstrating clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent empathic validation and some reflections acknowledge stress and discomfort, but deeper, more complex reflections exploring emotional nuance and ambivalence are limited.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on marijuana use and related concerns throughout the session, from assessment through strategy selection and follow-up planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner with shared decision-making, permission-based advice, and regular invitations for Sofia’s input, creating a collaborative tone.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, checks preferences, and frames plans as Sofia’s decisions while normalizing slips, strongly supporting client autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation (readiness ruler, asking what she wants) produced limited commitment, but the clinician missed opportunities to probe motivations, values, and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and negotiates plan together, though frequent provision of specific strategies shifts slightly toward expert problem-solving rather than co-creating solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk (readiness rating, choice of strategies) but does not consistently amplify, reflect, or probe for reasons, confidence, or elaboration of change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes difficulties which reduces pressure, but uses few double-sided reflections or strategic reframes to actively soften sustain talk.
