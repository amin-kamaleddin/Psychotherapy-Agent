# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance using validating, normalizing language and a warm, respectful tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking; reflections captured both content and affect and helped Amira expand on specifics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (a 10–15 minute morning walk) and guided the conversation toward concrete implementation without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative and invitational, repeatedly asking for Amira's preferences and co-constructing a manageable routine together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited choice, respected Amira's boundaries, and encouraged her ownership of the plan with no pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete reasons, supports, and plans and reinforced them, but missed opportunities to more deeply evoke underlying values and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered partnership and shared decision-making by offering options and positioning Amira as the expert on what will work for her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk through focused questions and reflections about planning, though could have probed for deeper reasons and an explicit commitment (e.g., 'What makes you an 8?').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk while pivoting to problem-solving, but could further weaken sustain language using double-sided reflections or more strategic reframing.
