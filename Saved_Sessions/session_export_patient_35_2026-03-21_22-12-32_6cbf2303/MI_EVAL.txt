# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—named and normalized Elena's situation, affirmed protective actions, and used nonpunitive language. No shaming or dismissive statements; offered support rather than directives.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely, specific reflections that validated constraints and intentions. Open, empathic questions invited Elena's perspective rather than imposing a view.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on parental smoking and reducing the child's exposure, steadily narrowing to a single, actionable target (car rule). Followed through with concrete planning and implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like teamwork—clinician invited Elena's choices, co-created texts/alarms, and adjusted recommendations to fit her style. Mutual influence shaped the plan throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and avoided coercion by offering options and honoring Elena's preferences. Validated limits and reinforced ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivations and intentions (e.g., Saturday start, car rule) but relied more on planning than probing deeper values or emotional reasons. Could have amplified core personal motives to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively partnered with Elena—invited input, implemented her preferences, and shared power in decision-making. The clinician honored her expertise about what would be realistic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced several strands of change talk and concrete intentions, leading to specific plans. Missed some opportunities to deepen and amplify change talk and elicit stronger commitment/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated minimizations while shifting to protective options, which prevented entrenchment. Could have used more double-sided reflections and reframing of rationalizations to further soften sustain talk.
