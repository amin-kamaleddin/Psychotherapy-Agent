# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validated frustrations while affirming strengths, maintaining a sustained nonjudgmental tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and tied values to practical steps, but largely remained at a pragmatic level with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions like worry or feeling 'chaotic'.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a focused, behaviorally specific agenda and guided the conversation to a clear, concrete action plan with backup strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as joint problem‑solving, integrated the patient's routine expertise, and co‑constructed decisions rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered choices, respected the patient's limits, and consistently reinforced the patient's control over pacing and next steps without applying pressure.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations and linked values to action, but could deepen evocation by probing intrinsic motives, confidence, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance, positioned the patient as the expert, and repeatedly invited the patient's choice in selecting feasible options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk, using the patient's own language to strengthen commitment to a specific, manageable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
