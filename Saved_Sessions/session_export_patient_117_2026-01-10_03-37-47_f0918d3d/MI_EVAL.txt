# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated progress, normalizing setbacks and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and perspective-taking that acknowledged pride and doubt, but emphasized pragmatic solutions over deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking reduction with concrete goals, action plans, tracking, and follow-up tied to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked jointly to co-create plans and invited patient choices, though frequently offered ready-made solutions instead of eliciting patient brainstorming first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirmed choice and framed strategies as experiments with multiple options, but several prescriptive suggestions and drafted materials reduced opportunities for patient-generated plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited intentions and concrete plans but relied more on supplying strategies than drawing out the patient’s intrinsic motivations, values, or change narrative.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with invitations for the patient to choose approaches, yet the clinician sometimes shifted toward an expert role by providing many specific recommendations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforced and amplified change talk and elicited concrete commitments (e.g., gum + walk), but missed deeper probing of personal reasons and explicit commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized doubts and redirected toward change-focused experiments and backups, though used fewer double-sided reflections that explicitly acknowledged both sides before pivoting.
