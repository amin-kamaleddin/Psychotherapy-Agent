**Comments:** Elicits triggers, readiness, and offers choice between options, but does not sufficiently draw out client values, reasons for change, or past successes to build intrinsic motivation.

**Comments:** Client expresses brief change statements (“I can’t keep doing it,” “I have to stop”) and clinician affirms attempts, but clinician does not consistently elicit, amplify, or summarize change talk to build momentum.
