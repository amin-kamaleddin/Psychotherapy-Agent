# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental stance with explicit invitations to share and crisis check; thanks the client and avoids blaming language, creating a safe, accepting tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and affective validation that captured ambivalence early, but missed deeper probing of emotions and personal values before moving into planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on weekend drinking and related consequences, consistently steering toward concrete, behaviorally specific goals and strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framing and language invite joint problem-solving and choice, but frequent provision of ready-made lines and tactics reduces equal co-creation at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports client choice with option-giving, respectful responses when the client declines suggestions, and repeated prompts to choose what feels doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits drinking patterns and consequences and invites strategy selection, but relies heavily on supplying solutions rather than drawing out values, importance, or intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the client's input and checks what feels helpful, fostering partnership, though many clinician-provided scripts shift some expertise back to the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asks about frequency and consequences and elicits some change statements, but rarely amplifies or strategically elicits the client's own motivations, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges consequences which counters minimization, but uses few double-sided reflections or explicit strategies to resolve ambivalence and soften sustain talk.
