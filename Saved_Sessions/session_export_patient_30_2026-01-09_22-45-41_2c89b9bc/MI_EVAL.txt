# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient’s lifestyle-first choice; no evidence of blame or minimization throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflective summaries that make the patient feel understood and elicit concrete plans, but reflections are sometimes surface-level, repetitive, and miss deeper emotional probes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on cholesterol management and adherence decisions, guiding the conversation toward a specific, measurable behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner by inviting the patient’s input and co-creating the plan (after-dinner walks, alarm, checklist) that fits the patient’s life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by normalizing options, avoiding pressure, and explicitly asking what feels realistic for the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits personally meaningful reasons for change (energy for family) and probes progress, but could further amplify the patient’s own arguments and link deeper values to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses partnership language and shared decision-making throughout, inviting the patient’s judgment and preferences when shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reflects concrete change talk (e.g., 'I’ll give it a shot' and a specific walking plan), but could more actively amplify reasons, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk about medication side effects consistently but mostly echoes concerns rather than using double-sided reflections or reframes to gently reduce sustain talk.
