# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed warm, nonjudgmental acceptance, acknowledged Isolde’s core value of family happiness, and affirmed her concerns throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking mirrored Isolde’s language and feelings, validating her experience and reducing defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained clear focus on values-aligned, practical steps and moved efficiently to a concrete plan (when, wording, rehearsal, fallback, start day).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was a genuine joint exploration with iterative tailoring, permission-seeking, rehearsal invites, and co-construction of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Options were offered and choices respected throughout, with language reinforcing that the plan belonged to Isolde and checking which wording felt like her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited motivations and commitment (choice of ritual, rehearsal, start date) but could have used more evocative questions and asked for Isolde’s own reasons to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A collaborative stance was present, but the clinician often supplied multiple scripted phrasings rather than first eliciting Isolde’s own wording, reducing opportunities for co-generation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician actively elicited and reinforced change talk by offering doable options, asking Isolde to choose and rehearse, and positively reinforcing her practice and steady tone.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustain talk about appearing to withdraw was met with empathic rephrasing, reframing for family benefit, reassuring language, and planning responses to likely objections.
