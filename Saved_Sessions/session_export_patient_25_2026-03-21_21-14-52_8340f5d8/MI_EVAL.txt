# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and affirmation, normalized ambivalence, avoided judgmental language, and asked permission before offering help.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective understanding by linking behavior to the patient’s values and tailoring suggestions to the patient’s experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and alcohol goals, moved from exploration to concrete planning, and guided rehearsal of specific next-step strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by co-constructing the plan, drafting texts together, inviting rehearsal, and refining options based on the patient’s input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and ownership by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding pressure or prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient’s own motivations and resources through values-based questions, linking to past success, and asking about confidence and what would raise it.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a partnership stance throughout—asking permission, inviting patient preference, and shaping decisions collaboratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions, reflections, affirmations, and use of a 0–10 confidence scale to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward change with practical strategies, but missed some opportunities for double-sided or amplified reflections to further soften sustain talk.
