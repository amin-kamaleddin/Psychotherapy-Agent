# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation (e.g., supportive openings, normalizing ambivalence) while avoiding moralizing or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured ambivalence and salient cues, but reflections stayed practical and could have probed deeper emotions or meanings (e.g., feelings about the fall or son's concern).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, focused attention on alcohol use with screening, harm-reduction options, and a concrete one-week goal, while including appropriate safety guidance without losing focus.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the conversation as a joint enterprise by offering tools and options and explicitly asking for the patient's input on choices, feasibility, and follow-up, reinforcing shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy through permission-seeking, offering choices, accepting limited commitment, and consistently framing decisions as the patient's without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation via past attempts, small goals, and factual discrepancy, but could have more deeply drawn out values, importance/confidence, or used scaling to strengthen intrinsic reasons and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled a collaborative stance by co-creating the plan, offering options for implementation, and checking the patient's preferences (e.g., which nights, tracking method, check-in timing).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and secured a concrete plan (tracking + two alcohol-free nights), but missed chances to amplify change statements and probe importance/confidence to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided strengthening sustain talk by using neutral language, objective feedback, and harm-reduction options, though could have employed double-sided reflections to more strategically highlight discrepancy while honoring sustain talk.
