# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard—named and normalized struggles and affirmed persistence. Tone was nonjudgmental and transparent about limits.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Displayed accurate perspective-taking with timely, relevant reflections that mirrored Amina’s feelings and readiness. Validated both emotional burden and practical concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on getting Amina into feasible job interviews and addressing barriers. Questions and suggestions were tightly linked to actionable steps (platforms, pay, hours).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interactional tone and structure were strongly collaborative—invited Amina to shape the agenda and offered help after asking her preference. Co-constructed tools and deliverables on her terms.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offered choices and invited Amina to pick priorities, while being transparent about limitations. This sustained her sense of control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motivations, skills, and preferences (pay, hours, experience) but could probe deeper for intrinsic reasons and elicit an earlier commitment statement. Strong on practical motivation, weaker on values-based exploration.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative and invited choices, but much of the session included clinician-driven plans and templates. Would benefit from more explicit co-creation of implementation ideas and next-step language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified action-oriented change talk and confidence, asking concrete questions about pay and steps. Missed some opportunities to deepen change language by exploring why change matters and eliciting commitment or consequences.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and quickly redirected to problem-solving and options. Could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to explicitly validate ambivalence while guiding toward change.
