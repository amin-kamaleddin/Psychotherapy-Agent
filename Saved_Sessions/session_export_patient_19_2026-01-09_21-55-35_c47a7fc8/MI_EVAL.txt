# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—normalizes shame and practical stress and reassures the client—though occasional explicit affirmations of strengths could individualize acceptance further.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, attuned reflections capture mixed feelings and mirror the client's language, creating strong empathic connection; a brief probe into the depth of shame or loss of parental support could deepen this further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on gambling and debt and ties them to values, using concrete behavioral goals and an immediate timeframe to move from ambivalence toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is genuine joint problem-solving with the client leading choices; the clinician shapes options without dictating and co-constructs practical steps and accountability.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and pacing, framing suggestions as optional and client-driven and consistently reinforcing the client's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and links them to small actionable steps while drawing on past successes to build efficacy; could add a direct confidence or commitment query.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance by inviting priorities and doable steps, sharing power and supporting ownership of the plan throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled at eliciting and amplifying change talk and operationalizing it into concrete actions and momentum toward values; occasional summarizing of DARN elements could further potentiate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes ambivalence using double-sided reflections and redirection, but could be more strategic in exploring the functions of gambling and summarizing pros/cons to weaken sustain talk.
