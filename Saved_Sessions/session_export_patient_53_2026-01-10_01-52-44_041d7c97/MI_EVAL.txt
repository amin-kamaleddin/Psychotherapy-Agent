# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed warm, nonjudgmental acceptance—validating and normalizing hesitation and praising effort without blaming, which reduced shame and supported disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, attuning to both emotional and somatic experience and matching the patient’s affect and brevity.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the target behavior with specific, measurable proximal steps (doorknob, open door, breathing) and consistently tied micro-steps to the larger goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructed the plan with the patient, asked permission before interventions, offered to draft reminders, and invited patient input at each step, reflecting genuine partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and provided multiple options and tentative suggestions, reinforcing the patient's control over whether and how to act.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited moment-to-moment motivational content and highlighted relief as progress, but missed deeper elicitation of personal values and long-term reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative, honored the patient's limits, and offered concrete tools the patient could accept, conveying respect for the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully evoked and reinforced small-step change talk and affirmed attempts, but could have amplified change talk by asking follow-ups about reasons, values, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized reluctance while shifting to manageable alternatives, though double-sided reflections were underused to explicitly soften sustain talk and highlight ambivalence.
