# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validating and normalizing the patient's experience while avoiding criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections, perspective-taking, and mirroring to capture ambivalence and acknowledge barriers, which encouraged patient elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking and drinking throughout, moving logically from exploration to specific planning and scheduling a follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Truly co-constructed solutions by inviting the patient's practical suggestions, endorsing them, and jointly agreeing on supports and a check-in time.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission before giving information, and framing decisions as the patient's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and commitment with open questions and scaling, but time spent on permissioned information and suggested steps reduced opportunities to deepen personal reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership and power-sharing by inviting input, offering options, and allowing the patient to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened some change talk through questions, scaling, and planning, but could have amplified and consolidated the patient's commitments more after providing facts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk gently without confrontation and reframed ambivalence, though missed some chances for double-sided reflections and clearer discrepancy to further weaken sustain language.
