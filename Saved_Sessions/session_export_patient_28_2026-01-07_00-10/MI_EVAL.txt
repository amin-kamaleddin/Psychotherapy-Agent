# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Dante’s struggle with nonjudgmental, affirming language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking by accurately paraphrasing Dante’s language and emotions, validating ambivalence and keeping him engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on restarting exercise and successfully guides the conversation toward concrete, time-bound steps and a testable action plan without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a genuine partnership: the clinician repeatedly invites Dante’s input, checks which options feel doable, and co-constructs the plan with him.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly honors choice by framing the plan as an experiment, respecting hesitation, and offering options rather than directives to reduce pressure and support self-direction.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits personal reasons and meaningful outcomes (modeling for kids, less guilt) but could probe more deeply into core values and use scaling to assess importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Establishes a collaborative stance throughout, avoids an expert-driven approach, and positions Dante as the primary source of knowledge about his life and routines.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk (small-step goals, past success) and asks for specific plans, but could deepen desire/importance and confidence language and amplify stronger statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes the plan as an experiment, yet uses few explicit double-sided reflections or other strategic techniques to further resolve ambivalence.
