# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (e.g., welcoming, normalizing, validating) and avoided blame or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely empathic reflections and perspective-taking that mirrored Rowan’s ambivalence and barriers, sustaining a calm, understanding tone across the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on charging and device use with questions and interventions that tied directly to barriers, concrete strategies, and measurable follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session read as a true partnership—clinician invited Rowan’s input at each decision point, affirmed choices, and co-constructed the low-effort plan rather than directing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Rowan’s sense of choice by offering options, asking permission for experiments, honoring his request for no nagging, and framing the plan as his decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited pragmatic motivations and barriers with targeted questions that produced client-generated change language, but could have probed deeper intrinsic reasons or values to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative stance—invited co-creation of the plan, sought Rowan’s wording and preferences, and shared power over decisions and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced concrete change talk by offering low-effort options and asking for commitments, though the clinician more often offered solutions instead of first eliciting Rowan’s own ideas.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk nonconfrontationally with reflections and normalization and shifted toward low-effort strategies, but could use more double-sided or amplified reflections to soften sustain talk further.
