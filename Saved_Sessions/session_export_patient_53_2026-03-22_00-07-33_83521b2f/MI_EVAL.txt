# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized hesitation, repeatedly validating pacing and safety throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking that captured behavior and underlying feelings, facilitating the client's modest elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on the behavioral target (tiny steps toward social engagement), returning to concrete next steps throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint exploration by offering options and co-constructing the plan, shifting decisions based on the client's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and respected pacing, offering multiple low-pressure options and supporting client ownership of the next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited what would feel meaningful and asked about supports, successfully drawing out motivation but could probe past successes or use scaling to deepen intrinsic motives and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited the client to choose among options and actively co-created the plan (e.g., drafting and selecting who would read it), demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited change talk with small-step prompts and pride language, but missed opportunities to amplify or summarize the client's change statements to strengthen and extend them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk using empathic redirection and double-sided reflections, though relied mainly on neutral acceptance and small-step options rather than stronger strategic moves to tip ambivalence.
