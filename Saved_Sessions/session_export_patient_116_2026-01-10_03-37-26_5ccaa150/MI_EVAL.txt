# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (validating statements, reassurance about not digging in now). Avoided criticism and normalized reluctance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate empathic reflections, perspective-taking, and safety checks that validated Samir and his guardedness. Could deepen emotional reflections and link resistance to broader life/context to invite more disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus by shifting to feasible micro-behaviors and concrete implementation plans that matched readiness. Would be strengthened by explicitly linking micro-tasks back to medication/health goals or scheduling a revisit of medication.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked collaboratively by asking Samir to choose focus, co-designing the micro-plan, and tailoring supports to his limits. Used inclusive language and sought permission before offering exercises or scripts.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice and control with phrases like 'no pressure' and offered multiple options rather than directives. Repeatedly framed steps as suggestions Samir could accept or decline, supporting client ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation for small behavioral changes but did not systematically draw out internal motivations or reasons related to medication adherence. More open, evocative questions and scaling or values exploration could deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely worked in partnership by co-constructing plans and honoring Samir's choices. At times adopted an instructive stance (detailed device scripts) without checking his preference for that level of direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced practical change talk around small steps and implementation intentions, but did not systematically elicit desire, reasons, confidence, or commitment language. Missed opportunities to deepen or amplify Samir's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully softened sustain talk by accepting hedges, normalizing reluctance, reframing missed attempts as learning, and offering acceptable alternatives. Avoided arguing and redirected toward achievable micro-changes.
