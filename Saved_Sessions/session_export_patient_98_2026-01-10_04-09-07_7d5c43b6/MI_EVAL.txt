### Acceptance

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate, often complex reflections that capture content and affect, mirroring Mateo’s tone and worries. Could deepen emotional exploration (e.g., the meaning of 'being a burden') and use more evocative validation at times.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the behavioral target and repeatedly narrows to one small, actionable step. Steers purposefully without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducts the session as a genuine collaborative exploration, soliciting preferences and co-constructing the plan. Decisions are clearly shaped by Mateo’s responses, showing shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice and control by offering options and asking which the client wants to start with. Avoids pressuring and reinforces Mateo’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out practical ideas and commitments using open questions and invitations to choose. Could further evoke personal values and use targeted prompts (e.g., confidence scaling) to deepen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Mateo as the expert on his experience and invites his input to co-create the plan. Could strengthen partnership by explicitly inviting teach-back or clarifying preferred clinician involvement.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces concrete change statements, reflecting and amplifying practical steps. Would benefit from more frequent selective reinforcement, summarizing of change language, and probing for confidence/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, which prevents escalation, but relies mostly on neutral reflections. Could use more double-sided reflections, strategic reframes, and explicit pairing of sustain and change talk to resolve ambivalence.
