# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard and repeated nonjudgmental validation, normalizing shame and relief to maintain respect throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing both content and affect to show strong understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on reducing outbursts and drinking with concrete, actionable steps (cue, timer, rehearsal) and purposeful narrowing toward behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a joint planning process: the clinician solicits the patient's input at every step and co-constructs the rehearsal plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and pace, respects the patient's readiness, and frames plans as the patient's decisions to foster ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits values, desired signs of change, and self-generated strategies (cue, script, safe spot), reinforcing internal motivation and practical capabilities.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as the expert and invites collaboration through repeated choices and preference-seeking, enabling shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits and strengthens change talk by asking about priorities, concrete signs of improvement, and inviting commitment to specific steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk and responds nonjudgmentally, but could use more strategic techniques (double-sided reflections or highlighting costs of status quo) to pivot toward stronger commitment language.
