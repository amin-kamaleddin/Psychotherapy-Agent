# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support (warm welcome, normalization, honoring shame), and repeatedly deferred to the patient’s preferences when offering options and wording.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured key feelings and conflicts, but reflections were more summarizing than deeply empathic and could name emotional undercurrents more often.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, steady focus on smoking cessation with agenda-setting, targeted options, and a concrete one-step plan tied to the patient’s values and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a true two-way partnership by inviting the patient to shape reminders, wording, timing, and the plan, and by following her choices exactly.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used invitational language, asked permission before advising, framed options rather than directives, and reinforced that decisions and pacing were the patient’s to control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited important motivations and reasons for change (daughter, stigma, readiness) but could more systematically deepen intrinsic motives, probe confidence, and draw out past strengths and commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative throughout—co-created the plan, solicited wording tweaks, and incorporated the patient’s input at each step, demonstrating equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and offered a doable plan, though the clinician could have amplified and reflected key change statements more and probed for commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized slips while offering reframe and strategies, but could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and shift language toward change.
