# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates respectful, nonjudgmental regard with validating statements and normalizing ambivalence. At times moves quickly into a structured plan, which slightly reduces the sense of fully unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and reflective listening, acknowledging stress, burden, and protective motives. Could deepen emotional reflections and use more complex reflections to name underlying feelings like guilt or exhaustion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with concrete, actionable goals and progressively narrows to a single target behavior. Structures a practical plan with specific replacements, tracking, and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently solicits the client's input, tailors strategies to her constraints, and co-constructs the plan rather than imposing it. Repeatedly checks preferences for timing, format, and specific choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Overtly supports autonomy by offering options, asking which path feels doable, and inviting choices about specific cigarettes and check-ins. Reinforces agency and normalizes slips to reduce shame and pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits concrete commitments and specifics that led to behavioral steps (home/car smoke-free, cutting a cigarette, replacements). Missed opportunities to probe deeper intrinsic motivations and to elicit confidence/readiness ratings to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the encounter as collaborative, shares control of the agenda, and repeatedly invites Elena's expertise and preferences. Decisions and the plan are clearly co-created and responsive to her needs.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits change talk by asking for specifics and doable options, which produced client statements in favor of change. Does not consistently amplify or reflect those change statements to strengthen desire, ability, reasons, and need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing, normalizes difficulty, and redirects sustain talk into usable options while offering multiple pathways. Could increase use of double-sided reflections and be more cautious proposing a near quit date to further reduce resistance.
