# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance with validating and normalizing language; could add a few more specific affirmations of the patient's strengths or efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections and empathic questions; could use more complex reflections to name deeper emotions (e.g., shame, fear) and probe emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session clearly focused on initiating glucose monitoring (and sensor options), using scaling and targeted questions to maintain focus while remaining responsive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as an equal, cooperative conversation—solicited preferences, offered options, and co-created a practical aid (the one‑sentence question) consistent with teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and control with 'no pressure' language and multiple options, reinforcing the patient's ownership of decisions and avoiding coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited meaningful internal motives using values and scaling questions (protecting work/family, avoiding complications); could probe values more deeply and amplify change-oriented statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and regularly solicited the patient's input, though could more explicitly endorse the patient's expertise and co-create a concrete, client-led plan with follow-up steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk about preventing complications and interest in sensors but did not consistently reflect, amplify, or develop commitment language or elicit past successes to build confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized and reframed sustain talk using double-sided reflections and non-pressured exploration to reduce defensiveness; could employ longer strategic reflections to steer sustain talk toward change.
