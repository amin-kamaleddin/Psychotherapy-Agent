# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive throughout (e.g., “I’m glad you’re here,” “That sounds really exhausting,” “You’re doing great asking for help”); normalized Nadine’s experience and avoided blaming (≥5 clear affirmations). Minor improvement: occasional empathic summaries could deepen warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and concise reflections (“That sounds really exhausting,” “You’re understandably worn out”), plus safety checks. Missed deeper, complex reflections of underlying emotion (fear/guilt/overwhelm) — reflections were often surface-level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, appropriate focus on infant sleep/feeding and parental coping with repeated, goal-linked guidance (dream-feed, pumping, redistributing care, LC referral). Asked which options felt doable and kept forward momentum without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Nadine as a partner: invited choices, offered multiple options, co-constructed a plan (scripts, timing, LC). Frequent collaborative language and invitations to participate (≥4 clear instances).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered alternatives rather than directives, emphasized choice (“Which of these feels doable tonight?”), and preserved agency with normalizing, nonpressured language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Nadine’s ideas and choices (selection of steps, request for scripts) but leaned toward providing information/how‑to rather than drawing out Nadine’s own reasons, values, or confidence; missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership via invitations and shared planning (texting partner, pump timing, LC), but sometimes moved quickly to didactic instruction without always checking permission, slightly shifting from mutual exploration to expert delivery.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete intentions and planning (agreeing to text partner, try dream-feed, pump, book LC) and provided scripts to facilitate action. Could more actively amplify change talk (importance/confidence queries, reflective summaries, elicited verbal commitment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively acknowledged and reframed skepticism and concerns (e.g., fears about pumping, partner refusal) with normalization, reassurance (“one low pump won’t ruin supply”), and pragmatic alternatives — consistently redirected sustain talk toward options and hope.
