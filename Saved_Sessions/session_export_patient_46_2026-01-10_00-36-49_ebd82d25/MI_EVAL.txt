# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, creating a warm nonjudgmental stance; occasional repetition of 'You're not judged here' risked sounding formulaic.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflections that captured both content and feeling and summaries that validated Ethan's experience, facilitating deeper disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on reducing alcohol-related harms while preserving social life, consistently steering toward concrete, actionable harm-reduction steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as an equal, collaborative problem-solving process with repeated invitations for input and shared decision-making, fostering client ownership of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Ethan's right to choose, offered options, and invited him to select what felt realistic, supporting autonomy and likely increasing buy-in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out client-generated strategies and motivations but missed opportunities to probe strength of motives, link them more explicitly to values, or elicit a commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned the work as a joint enterprise, honored Ethan's expertise, and used power-sharing language that promoted co-creation and ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several change statements and practical strategies, but did not consistently amplify change talk into stronger commitment or assess importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided directly reinforcing sustain talk but did not strategically soften it—more use of double-sided reflections and pivoting to discrepancy would help reduce sustain talk.
