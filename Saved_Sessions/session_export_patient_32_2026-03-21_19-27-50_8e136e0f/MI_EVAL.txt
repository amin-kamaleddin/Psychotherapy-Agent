# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard from the opening line through the session, normalizing the patient's concerns and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated feelings and reflected social context early on, but empathic work stayed somewhat surface-level with missed chances to explore deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on avoiding another DUI, getting the license reinstated, and managing peer pressure with every intervention tied to those goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created concrete items (refusal lines, texts, checklist) and incorporated the patient's wording and preferences, demonstrating equal partnership and shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options and asked permission, accepted patient tweaks, and reinforced that the patient was in control of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out the patient's own ideas and commitment language and bolstered those plans, but could have probed more for intrinsic motives and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invited input, though occasional directive instructions slightly reduced the sense that expertise resided entirely with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and helped operationalize concrete steps, but could use more open questions to deepen desire/importance/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided directly reinforcing sustain talk and moved to planning, but did not explicitly reflect or explore ambivalence (e.g., double-sided reflections) to reduce resistance.
