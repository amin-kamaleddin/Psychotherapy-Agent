### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
Checklist:
- [x] Avoided reinforcing sustain talk or becoming confrontational

**Comments:** Clinician effectively softened resistance with validation and a low-risk experiment; caution that some suggested options (lighter in pocket, preserving chair) risked validating the smoking ritual rather than shifting momentum toward change.
