# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed Sofia's choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured ambivalence and risks, but missed deeper exploration of underlying emotions (e.g., stress, family expectations) and more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on marijuana use and progressed logically from exploration to a concrete harm-reduction plan with tracking and safety measures.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with shared decision-making and role-play, but the clinician often introduced many specific options early, making parts of the exchange clinician-led.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Sofia's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing actions as her decision without coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited doable steps and encouraged experimentation, yet did not sufficiently evoke values, future goals, or use scaling to deepen intrinsic motivation and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance inviting Sofia to choose steps and tweak scripts, but offering many concrete recommendations somewhat reduced the sense that solutions originated with Sofia.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some tentative change talk and reinforced Sofia's choices, but missed opportunities to systematically probe desire, reasons, ability, and use scaling to amplify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged benefits of use, but used limited double-sided reflections or reframing to redirect sustain talk toward discrepancy and consequences.
