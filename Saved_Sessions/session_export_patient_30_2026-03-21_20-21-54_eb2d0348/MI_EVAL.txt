# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful; validated the patient's concerns and avoided labeling or pressure throughout the visit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections captured both content and feeling and matched the patient's guarded tone, which kept him engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the decision about statin use and steered the conversation toward concrete action steps (lifestyle plan, labs, possible low‑dose trial).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as collaborative problem solving with joint decision‑making, inclusive language, and follow‑through on the patient's requests (note formatting, lab scheduling).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored patient choice by offering options, asking permission before giving information, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific change language and confidence ratings but could deepen evocation by drawing out personal values, motives, and past successes to strengthen internal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established a genuine partnership—co‑created plans, let the patient's preferences guide decisions, and used inclusive, power‑sharing language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively invited and reinforced change talk (praise, specific behavior commitments, confidence ruler) but missed opportunities to amplify and convert change language into stronger commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and provided risk data and contingency plans to reduce threat, though double‑sided reflections and reframing toward small steps could have been used more often.
