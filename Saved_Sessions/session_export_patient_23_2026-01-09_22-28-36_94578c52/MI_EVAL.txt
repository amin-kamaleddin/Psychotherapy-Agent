# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing concerns and avoiding blame throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, acknowledging nuanced emotions, but missed opportunities to deepen reflection on depression and guilt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on reducing alcohol with a specific, measurable plan and a clear follow-up, balancing exploration with action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by soliciting Claudia's ideas, implementing her exact wording, and offering options rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control (asked permission, offered options, deferred to Claudia's preferences) and emphasized ownership of the plan and slips.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Claudia's reasons, plans, and supports (confidence rating and concrete strategies) but could probe deeper into values and amplify change language linking drinking to depression.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed the work as collaborative, co-created the plan and reminders, and checked Claudia's preferences, demonstrating strong power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and a concrete goal (two measured glasses), though could have amplified and consolidated stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided strengthening sustain talk and redirected ambivalence toward small changes, but could use more double-sided reflections and strategic pivots to further soften sustain talk.
