# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates a respectful, nonjudgmental stance and normalizes Tyler's feelings; could add more individualized affirmations and warmth to reach the highest rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid empathic listening and clarifying questions, acknowledging stress and reluctance; reflections are mostly surface-level and could deepen emotional ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on the conduct referral with concrete next steps and drafts; spends less time linking administrative problem-solving to longer-term behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly solicits input and incorporates Tyler's preferences into plans and messages; at times offers ready-made scripts that shift power back toward the clinician's agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently presents options, checks preferences, and respects boundaries (e.g., removing promises about documentation); strong support for client choice throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Does little to draw out Tyler's own motivations for changing drinking behavior and rarely uses evoking questions; missed opportunities to probe values, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Functions as a collaborative team, asking which tasks to address and honoring choices like tone and timeline; could invite co-generation of ideas earlier instead of offering solutions first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and supports practical steps to avoid academic/legal consequences but does not systematically elicit or amplify change talk about drinking itself; missed chances to prompt desire, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers alternative strategies, but rarely uses double-sided or reframing reflections to shift sustain talk and instead moves quickly into problem-solving.
