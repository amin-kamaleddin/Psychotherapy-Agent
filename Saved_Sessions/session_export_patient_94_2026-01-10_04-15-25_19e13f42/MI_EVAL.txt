# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the patient’s limits, using multiple validations and explicit acceptance statements that created a nonjudgmental climate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking but reflections were somewhat formulaic and repetitive, with missed opportunities to probe emotional nuance more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on inhaler access/use and PCP contact, moving logically from options to a specific, measurable plan with confidence assessment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative—checking preferences and co-creating wording—but initially presented clinician-generated solutions rather than first eliciting the patient’s ideas, creating mild asymmetry.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly affirmed choice and avoided coercion, repeatedly offering options and accepting the patient’s limits while letting her decide which steps to try.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete motivators and used tools like a confidence ruler, but relied at times on offering solutions instead of deeply exploring the patient’s underlying values for staying healthy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance by offering choices and inviting input, though the clinician’s initial list of solutions made the opening somewhat clinician-driven rather than fully patient-led.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited specific change talk and commitments (carry inhaler, PCP text) and used a confidence ruler, but could have amplified and summarized the patient’s reasons for change more explicitly to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed it into manageable options, yet used limited advanced MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or amplifying subtle change signals) to further soften sustain talk.
