# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized the patient's pace, avoiding judgment. Affirmed boundaries and reduced shame by framing the plan as low-pressure and respectful.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, specific reflections that captured both practical concerns and emotional context (stress, ambivalence). Frequent summarizing demonstrated strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on a pragmatic daytime nicotine-replacement trial and tied every question and reflection back to that goal. Avoided drifting into unrelated topics and kept the plan actionable.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicited the patient's preferences and co-created the plan (form, timing, reminders, tracking). Regularly checked feasibility and accommodated the patient's request for minimal follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced choice and control by framing the plan as an optional trial and normalizing stopping if it caused problems. Avoided prescriptive language and confirmed the patient could revise or stop at any time.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own motivations and strategies through evocative open questions and by asking the patient to define success criteria. Drew out change ideas rather than supplying reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint exploration and deferred to the patient's expertise about what fits his life. Used collaborative language to ensure equal-partner decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Asked targeted open questions and prompted specific, small commitments (one-week trial, low-dose gum, meaningful outcome). Reflected and amplified the patient's change language to strengthen its salience.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validated reluctance without reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward a pragmatic, nonthreatening experiment. Softened resistance by accepting the patient's stance and focusing on doable steps.
