# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Dylan's feelings with reflective, nonjudgmental statements that reduced defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent reflections accurately captured emotions and priorities, but the style was somewhat formulaic and missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on avoiding future problematic drinking and protecting Dylan's record, moving from identification to concrete implementation steps without drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative: the clinician invited Dylan's ideas, elicited strengths, and co‑constructed an experiment rather than delivering top‑down advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Dylan's choice by offering options and framing the plan as a self‑directed experiment he could accept or modify.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical motivations and resources (alarms, roommate, desire to protect record) but could have probed broader values or longer‑term reasons to strengthen internal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the exchange as joint problem‑solving and deferred to Dylan's expertise, asking him to choose and refine the strategies throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change language, converting intentions into a specific, actionable plan (11pm alarm, pump‑up song, roommate nudge, telling friends).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk and used reflections and double‑sided statements to pivot toward change, though more exploration of the pull of sustain talk would deepen discrepancy.
