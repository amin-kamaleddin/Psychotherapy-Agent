# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with affirmations and validation throughout the session. Frames suggestions as options and avoids shaming language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and accurate summaries of Jamal's concerns and priorities. Could deepen exploration of underlying emotions or the social/ emotional meaning of use.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on avoiding possession and legal/family trouble with concrete steps and commitment questions. Keeps choices and plans tightly linked to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites Jamal's input, asks preferences, and co-constructs plans including role-play practice. Some clinician-driven content (detailed instructions/wording) reduced opportunities for Jamal-generated solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, respects boundaries, and checks permission, supporting Jamal's control over decisions. Repeatedly asks which approach he prefers rather than imposing directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens with an evocative question but quickly shifts to giving advice and harm-reduction strategies. Misses sustained probing of Jamal's internal motivations or building discrepancy with his values/goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conducts the session as a collaborative effort, asking Jamal to choose focus and select steps to practice. Partnership is strong but somewhat reduced by offering a clinician-generated list before eliciting Jamal's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits limited change talk and reinforces responsibility at times, but moves quickly to practical advice instead of amplifying or probing change-oriented statements. Missed opportunities to deepen desire, ability, or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing or confronting sustain talk and redirects toward pragmatic harm-reduction steps. Could more consistently use strategic MI tools (e.g., double-sided reflections) to draw out discrepancy.
