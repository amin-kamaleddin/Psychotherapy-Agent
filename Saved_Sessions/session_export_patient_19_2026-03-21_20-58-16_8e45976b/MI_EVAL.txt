# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys respect and nonjudgmental support (opening line, affirmations of insight and steps) and avoids blame despite the referral context.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures ambivalence and barriers, but missed some opportunities to probe deeper emotional tone behind humor and self‑criticism with more affect-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on gambling, debt, and risk-reduction strategies, moving logically from exploration to concrete planning and safeguards.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co‑constructed—brainstorming options together, co-creating scripts, and roleplaying while inviting the patient to select and refine plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly supported through choice-preserving language, offering options, and asking permission before offering help or drafting materials.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations, values, and past successes and links those client-generated reasons to concrete, actionable steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters genuine power-sharing by asking which options feel most doable, offering choices, and inviting the patient to lead decisions about rehearsal and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and develops change talk (open questions, confidence ruler) and translates it into specific plans and rehearsals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk with double-sided reflections, but could more explicitly name and validate the pull of immediate pleasure and linger to further defuse sustain talk emotionally.
