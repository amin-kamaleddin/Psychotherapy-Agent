# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizes the patient's experience, and repeatedly affirms efforts without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening by naming the patient's experience, validating feelings, and linking barriers to past successes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on reducing problematic drinking with concrete action steps and follow-up planning, avoiding drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine two-way problem solving by soliciting the patient's preferences, offering tentative options, and tailoring plans to the patient's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering multiple options, asking which feels doable, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and resources, links past successes to current goals, and draws out specific, self-generated plans and confidence boosters.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance throughout, sharing power and co-creating a detailed plan that reflects the patient's preferences and wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk by inviting small, specific harm-reduction steps, reinforcing capability, and eliciting commitment and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk nonjudgmentally and redirects toward harm-reduction, but could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly pair pros and cons and further neutralize ambivalence.
