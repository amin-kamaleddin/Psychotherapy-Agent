# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with validating, nonjudgmental language and normalizing statements, praising the client's strengths and openness throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, sustained reflective listening and perspective-taking by summarizing competing pulls and paraphrasing the client's concerns to deepen understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking cessation and purposefully moves the conversation from values to concrete, time-bound steps without overriding client autonomy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as a joint endeavor, asks permission to co-create plans and scripts, and repeatedly seeks the client's input to build feasible solutions together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly affirms choice and offers multiple options, respects the client's concerns, and reinforces that final decisions rest with the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and values using open questions, past successes, and future imagery, allowing change reasons to come from the client.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the client as an active collaborator by offering choices, checking what fits her life, and sharing power in shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change language with focused questions, reflections, and summaries that build momentum toward concrete supports and goals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk well by avoiding reinforcement and using some double-sided reflections, but missed opportunities to more strategically pivot and immediately amplify small change statements.
