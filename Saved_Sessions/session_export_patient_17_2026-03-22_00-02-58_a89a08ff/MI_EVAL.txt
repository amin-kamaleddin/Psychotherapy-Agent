# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with normalizing and affirming statements and avoids blame or moralizing throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate empathy using reflective listening and occasional verbatim language to capture shame, exhaustion, and meaning, which builds rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking cessation and steadily moves from values to concrete, actionable steps (no-smoking-at-home, gum, walks, reminders).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine shared problem-solving by inviting preferences, checking back, and integrating the patient's choices into the plan rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering options, affirming pace, and asking which strategies fit her week, preserving agency while structuring feasible steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's own motivations and values with evocative questions and elicits concrete reasons and commitments from the patient herself.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopts a genuinely collaborative stance, repeatedly framing decisions as co-created and deferring to the patient's judgment about what will fit her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk via values-oriented questions, prompts for small doable steps, and queries about outcomes that deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids reinforcing it but misses some opportunities for more strategic softening (e.g., double-sided reflections, explicit exploration of the pull of smoking).
