# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validated progress and normalized setbacks, and checked safety calmly and respectfully.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, but sometimes moved quickly to problem-solving and missed opportunities to deepen the emotional content of the patient's statements.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on target behaviors and safety, progressing from assessment to concrete, actionable goals and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the interaction as a joint enterprise by inviting input, tailoring guidance to the patient's concerns, and seeking buy-in for plans and check-ins.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Regularly offered options and asked permission, but at times used more directive language and firm templates rather than explicitly framing elements as adaptable to the patient's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and supported internal motivation (amplifying progress and securing commitments) but often shifted to offering solutions instead of systematically drawing out the patient's own reasons and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—co-created plans and asked the patient to choose check-ins—but frequently provided detailed plans without first soliciting the patient's solutions or preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reinforced and elicited change talk and invitations to commit, though there were missed chances to reflect and amplify the patient's own desire, ability, reasons, or confidence before offering solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used normalization and reframing to reduce sustain talk, but occasionally supplied many prescriptive strategies that could inadvertently validate doubts; more double-sided reflections would help.
