# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys respect and a nonjudgmental stance with practical affirmations and choice; could be more richly personalized and explicitly normalize ambivalence to deepen rapport.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and restates the patient’s priorities well; tends to remain at a surface/functional level without exploring deeper emotions or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on preparing the patient for the doctor appointment and consistently guides toward concrete, actionable outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is co‑constructed—clinician offers expertise while repeatedly soliciting and following the patient’s input, so the patient shapes the session throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, asks permission, and respects the patient’s choices, supporting ownership of the plan without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits immediate priorities and a concrete commitment to raise the issue with the doctor but does not probe for intrinsic reasons, values, or broader motivation for long‑term change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance with frequent offers of choice and invitations to edit wording; power‑sharing is evident and the patient helps direct the work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces practical, action‑oriented steps (talking points, card) but does not systematically elicit desire, ability, reasons, or confidence for broader behavior change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids amplifying sustain talk and redirects to practical steps, but misses opportunities for strategic double‑sided reflections or explicit reframing of minimizations.
