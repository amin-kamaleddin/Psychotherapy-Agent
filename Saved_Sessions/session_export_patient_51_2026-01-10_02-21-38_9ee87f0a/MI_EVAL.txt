### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing Ingrid's situation and affirming strengths. No labeling, minimizing, or disapproving language was used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses high-quality, accurate empathic reflections that capture both content and meaning (e.g., ‘I hear you,’ being ‘stretched’). These reflections help Ingrid feel understood and validated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on medication safety and immediate actionable steps throughout the exchange. Questions and reflections consistently move the conversation toward a specific, practical plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reflects genuine teamwork with co-creation of the checklist and one‑liner and a dialogic flow of question → choice → reflection/action. The clinician defers to Ingrid's decisions and priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking preferences, and inviting ownership of the plan. Language emphasizes what feels doable for Ingrid rather than prescribing a single course of action.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation tied to values (protecting ability to care for work/family) and probes reasons and confidence. Could strengthen by probing deeper about personal meaning and eliciting a brief verbal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as collaborative, asking what matters most and prioritizing Ingrid's choices. Power is shared and the patient shapes the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies concrete change talk (photo, reminders, calendar entry, confidence rating) and reinforces these statements. Could more explicitly elicit a final verbal commitment (who/when) to consolidate follow-through.

**Comments:** Acknowledges time constraints without reinforcing them and offers brief, doable actions to reduce hesitation. A few double-sided reflections could further soften the limited sustain talk about time.
