# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, normalizing ambivalence and offering harm‑reduction without shaming, creating a safe tone for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and accurate reflections, validating stress and uncertainty; could deepen empathy with more complex reflections of ambivalence and affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol risks, conducts AUDIT‑C and converts findings into concrete harm‑reduction steps; at times moves quickly to solutions without first eliciting Ryan's priorities and values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively by checking preferences, offering choices, and co‑constructing a small plan; slightly clinician‑led at times and could ask Ryan to generate more of the specific steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Ryan's sense of choice—asks permission, offers options, and honors his reluctance to commit—avoiding pressured language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation and consequence awareness but relies more on providing information and options than drawing out Ryan's intrinsic reasons, importance, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision‑making by inviting preferences and offering draft/role‑play options; could enhance co‑construction by prompting Ryan to propose and shape specific actions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits limited change language and establishes an accepted harm‑reduction plan, but does not systematically evoke or amplify Ryan's own reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confronting sustain talk and provides harm‑reduction options, but misses opportunities for double‑sided reflections or gently exploring costs of maintaining the status quo to soften sustain talk.
