# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, validated Daniela’s feelings, and normalized stress without judgment, fostering safety and acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking (including safety checks) showed care and understanding; deeper reflections that capture emotional nuance (fear of job loss, shame about asking for help) would increase empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the primary target—securing reliable childcare—and kept the session on concrete, actionable next steps like calling Head Start and scheduling intake.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative with offers to co-work (role-play, screening), but the clinician could more often elicit Daniela’s ideas before proposing solutions to better shape the plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission for role-play, and framing help as offers rather than directives, affirming Daniela’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations and small-step plans, but sometimes shifted quickly to providing solutions; more open questions about values, past successes, and confidence would deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fostered a collaborative stance with invitations to co-create the plan, though at times the clinician adopted an expert/instructive role by offering detailed scripts without first explicitly eliciting Daniela’s input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk well through choices and concrete steps, and used role-play to build confidence; could more consistently reflect and amplify Daniela’s own reasons and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers and immediately offered concrete options and backups to prevent sustain talk from dominating, but could use more double-sided reflections linking concerns to Daniela’s goals to transform sustain talk into change talk.
