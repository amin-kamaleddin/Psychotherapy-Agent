# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened with clear unconditional positive regard and maintained a nonjudgmental, validating stance throughout, with multiple affirmations and no signs of blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening (including double-sided reflections and empathic summaries), checking understanding and capturing both feelings and content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, sustained focus on phone use and related goals, guiding the session toward a concrete, time-limited experiment with specific implementation details and outcome measures.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine partner in joint problem-solving, soliciting the patient's preferences, incorporating her modifications, and co-constructing a doable plan and follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by asking permission, offering options, framing the change as a test, and emphasizing the patient's right to stop or modify the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own reasons and resources for change (scales, what would move confidence, anticipated outcomes) and drew out intrinsic motivation rather than supplying reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance by asking permission before offering ideas, presenting choices, and inviting the patient to decide which strategy felt most doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk (importance/confidence scaling, selecting a specific test), but at times moved quickly into planning without further deepening or sequencing the patient's underlying values and reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and offered respectful compromises, but could have used more strategic double-sided or amplified reflections and exploration of the emotional costs to more sharply pivot sustain talk toward change.
