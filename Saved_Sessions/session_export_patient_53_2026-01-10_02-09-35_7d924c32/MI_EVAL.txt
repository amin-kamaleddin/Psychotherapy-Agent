# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, validating safety concerns and normalizing nervousness while affirming small efforts, demonstrating warmth and unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening and repeated summaries of core concerns, matching the patient's hesitant style so she felt understood rather than interrogated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on attending a brief community activity and steered the conversation to concrete, time-bound steps without being pressuring.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, co-created invite wording and plans, solicited feedback, and adjusted wording per the patient's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Isabella's right to choose, offered multiple options, respected her hesitancy, and left decisions clearly in her control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation by asking what matters and what felt manageable, but often moved to solution-offering instead of probing deeper for intrinsic reasons or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Isabella as a collaborator and expert on her experience, inviting choice and input and tailoring solutions to her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged small-step change talk and reinforced tentative movement toward action, though could have used more evocative, confidence-building questions to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it and redirected toward supports and planning, but used few double-sided reflections to strategically resolve ambivalence.
