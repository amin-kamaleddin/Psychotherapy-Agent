# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized uncertainty, and avoided judgment while honoring Timur's preferences (e.g., Fiona not present). Tone was strength-focused and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, empathic listening and good content reflections but leaned toward content over affect. There were missed opportunities for deeper, affect‑focused reflections naming shame, hurt, or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (meeting the probation coach) and linked each intervention to concrete action steps. Balanced exploration with practical, goal-directed planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner to co-produce the CV, script, and plan, using inclusive language and checking preferences. The clinician consistently invited Timur to shape decisions and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice by offering options, asking permission, and respecting Timur's decisions (e.g., about Fiona and how to proceed). Framed help as collaborative rather than directive.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited reasons for change (Fiona, mornings, 22 days clean) and some commitment actions, but could more systematically draw out values and have the client verbalize a concise commitment. Deeper linking of values to specific steps would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shared power and invited co-creation of the plan, offering choices and checking preferences for next steps and supports. Partnership was evident in drafting materials and role‑playing together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change talk using importance/confidence scaling, highlighting strengths (22 days), and prompting specific, doable steps. Moves encouraged commitment language and concrete planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reframed self‑critical sustain talk and avoided argumentation, redirecting to actionable steps. Could further use double-sided reflections and more aggressive amplification of change talk to diminish sustain language.
