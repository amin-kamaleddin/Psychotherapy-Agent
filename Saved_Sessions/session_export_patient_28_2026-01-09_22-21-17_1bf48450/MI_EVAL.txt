# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive with normalization and repeated affirmations; could be strengthened by more specific praise of the patient's past strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides accurate, timely reflections that make the patient feel heard, but reflections remain largely pragmatic rather than exploring deeper emotions or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the conversation tightly focused on re-establishing physical activity and repeatedly moves to concrete, measurable steps (time, cue, days, fallback).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally co-creates the plan and solicits preferences, though the clinician sometimes shifts to scripted suggestions without explicitly inviting joint ideation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers choices, asks what feels doable, and avoids pressure while framing missed attempts non-punitively, supporting the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk and choices but relies more on clinician-generated strategies; missed opportunities to draw out deeper values, motives, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters client expertise and shared decision-making by asking the patient to choose options, yet occasionally provides clinician-originated solutions without asking permission.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces client change talk and specific commitments, but could further amplify the patient's reasons and values to deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk and reframes barriers into manageable options and fallbacks, though more double-sided reflections could better capture ambivalence.
