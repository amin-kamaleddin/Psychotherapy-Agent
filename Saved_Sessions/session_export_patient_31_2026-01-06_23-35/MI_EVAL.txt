# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental stance and harm‑reduction options are offered consistently, but deeper individualized normalization or empathic reflection of ambivalence is limited.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some basic listening and tailoring to sleep/wiring concerns is present, yet reflective listening and nuanced paraphrase of the patient's perspective are infrequent.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on cutting back/safer nicotine use with practical, stepwise harm‑reduction advice and a logical trial plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Patient preferences are acknowledged and a script is offered, but the clinician often assumes an expert role with dosing specifics rather than engaging equal, co‑constructed problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice by offering options and a trial plan, though occasionally uses directive language when presenting dosing recommendations without fully eliciting the patient's preference.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited elicitation of internal motivation or personal reasons for change; the clinician supplies information more than drawing out the patient's values, confidence, or change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are offers of collaboration (choices, permission to help), but much of the interaction remains clinician‑driven rather than jointly co‑creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Misses opportunities to evoke and amplify the patient's own change language; few questions probe desire, reasons, ability, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Sustain talk is accepted and the clinician moves to practical advice instead of using reflections or double‑sided summaries to diminish resistance and explore ambivalence.
