# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence with supportive, nonjudgmental language, helping the patient feel accepted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with timely, accurate reflections that deepen the patient's self-awareness and encourage disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and ankle protection, guiding the patient from ambivalence to specific, concrete steps without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem-solving, solicits the patient's ideas, and refines plans together, producing a co‑constructed, patient-led plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors the patient's freedom to choose by offering options, asking which feels doable, and affirming the patient as the expert in decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own values and motivations and amplifies self-authored reasons and strategies into actionable steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a genuine partnership by inviting the patient's input, sharing power, and offering help only after the patient selects a direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and builds on change talk by asking about priorities, possible strategies, and eliciting specific commitments the patient is willing to try.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward planning, but rarely uses explicit double-sided reflections or brief exploration of pros/cons to acknowledge the pull of not changing.
