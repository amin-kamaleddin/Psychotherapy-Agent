# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental tone and validates ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking (naming somatic ache and ambivalence); could deepen affective reflections to capture exhaustion, shame, or deeper emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing cutting by orienting every question and reflection toward concrete, actionable experiments (rubber band, ten‑minute wait, roommate check).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Approaches the work as a joint effort—brainstorms together, offers low‑pressure options, and lets the patient’s ideas shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes choice, elicits preferences, and frames offers of help as options rather than directives, respecting the patient's skepticism.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out past strengths and links them to the plan, helping the patient imagine alternatives; could more systematically elicit desire/ability/reasons/need (e.g., scaling) and amplify willingness to build commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance with shared power—repeatedly invites co‑creation and asks how the clinician can assist while honoring the patient’s expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits preparatory change talk and a specific experiment (band, reminder, roommate); could more consistently amplify and summarize change language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk alongside change talk without judgment, which avoids reinforcing reasons to stay the same; could add double‑sided reflections and more amplification of emerging reasons for change.
