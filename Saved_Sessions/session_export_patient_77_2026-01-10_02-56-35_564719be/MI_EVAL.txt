# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, normalizing feelings and affirming effort throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures both cognitive and emotional content and encourages disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on food-logging/photos and privacy strategies, proposing a concrete three-day experiment and stepwise in-session practice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine joint problem-solving, offers options and supports rather than directives, and follows the patient's tempo with decisions originating from the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves the patient's choice with permission-seeking language, accepts reluctance without pressure, and adjusts expectations to support ownership of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation using open questions, a low-pressure experiment, and a confidence ruler, but often shifts into technical coaching instead of deeper elicitation of personal motives or values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters partnership and shared decision-making by offering options, asking permission to guide practice, and co-creating the plan with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits change talk and concrete planning, but misses several opportunities to amplify and deepen the patient's change statements and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids debating sustain talk and reframes the task as low-pressure while redirecting toward experiments, though could use more double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
