# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalization and validation, avoiding moralizing or shaming language. The clinician maintained warmth and nonjudgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with useful paraphrases and emotion naming that help Jason feel heard. Could use a few deeper or more varied reflections to capture nuance and avoid formulaic repeats.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps clear focus on the target behavior and relevant outcomes, tying interventions to concrete actions (attend meeting, script, exit plan). The clinician maintained momentum toward pragmatic, achievable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly cooperative, offering expertise while deferring to Jason's preferences and lived experience. Decisions were made jointly and Jason's wording materially shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports Jason's sense of choice by presenting options, asking preferences, and avoiding pressure. The clinician affirmed his right to keep socializing while offering risk-reduction strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Jason's own motivations and reasons for reducing risk with evocative questions that drew out personal goals. To improve, the clinician could deepen evocation by eliciting values, using scaling, and highlighting strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a genuine partnership by asking for preferences, offering options, and co-creating the script and rehearsal plan. The clinician positioned Jason as an active decision-maker throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with targeted questions and reinforcement of client choices, producing concrete change language. Could more often amplify client change statements verbatim and explicitly elicit commitment/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully rolls with and reframes sustain talk, using neutral-to-positive reframing and double-sided summaries to shift focus. Could further blunt sustain talk by more explicitly naming its downsides and using strategic double-sided reflections.
