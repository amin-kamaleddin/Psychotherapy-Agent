# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating the patient's perspective and respecting his goal to cut back rather than quit.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening and summaries that matched Brandon's ambivalence, and checked in on his feelings about practicing lines.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on cutting back alcohol with concrete, actionable steps (specific dry days, alternatives, refusal lines) and no drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by offering options, soliciting the patient's input, and following his requests to tweak lines and role-play, so the patient shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's choice and control, offered options rather than directives, and avoided pressure while checking confidence and preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation (readiness ruler, reasons, confidence change) but relied more on offering strategies and could deepen evocation by exploring core values and self-generated motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-created decisions (which days, strategies, wording) and treated the patient as the expert on what will work, fostering genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively evoked and reinforced change talk with concrete questions, invitations to specify actions, rehearsal of refusal lines, and prompts about confidence and plan details.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged sustain talk while pivoting to alternatives, but missed some opportunities for strategic techniques (double-sided reflections, selective amplification) to further reduce sustain talk.
