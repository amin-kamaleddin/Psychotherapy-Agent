# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated nonjudgmental regard and normalized Sofia's struggle, which likely helped her feel seen; however the repeated stock phrase felt formulaic and would benefit from more personalized affirmations woven into reflections.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated Sofia's ambivalence and worries; missed opportunities for deeper emotion-focused reflections and open empathic prompts to further deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, consistent focus on avoiding weekend drinking during pregnancy with each question and reflection tied to concrete strategies, moving clearly from ambivalence to an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a genuine co-creation: the clinician checked Sofia's preferences, invited her ideas, and built the plan from Sofia's solutions rather than imposing advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported through choice-oriented questions and options rather than directives, fostering Sofia's ownership of the plan and respect for her decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values-based reasons for change and grounded planning in Sofia's motivations; could deepen evocation by eliciting past successes, confidence ratings, and more consistently amplifying spontaneous change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Partnership was a clear strength with repeated language of working together, invitations to choose strategies, and genuine power-sharing that allowed Sofia's preferences to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician asked focused, evocative questions that drew out multiple change statements and summarized a concrete plan; to improve, more frequent amplification of strong change language and elicitation of commitment/confidence would help.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and quickly redirected to problem-solving and planning without reinforcing rationalizations; more use of double-sided reflections could have explicitly tipped ambivalence toward change.
