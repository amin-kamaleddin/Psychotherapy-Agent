# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened with explicit nonjudgmental language and repeatedly validated the patient’s perspective, avoiding labels and affirming the choice to delay medication.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that captured the patient’s ambivalence and practical concerns, though could have probed deeper into emotional and identity-related consequences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the decision about starting cholesterol medication and steered the conversation toward concrete, actionable next steps (questions, stats, monitoring plan).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked in an egalitarian way by co-creating tools (script and stats), asking preferences, and letting the patient shape the session outcome.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered options rather than directives, honored the patient’s preferences, and explicitly reinforced that the decision to delay was reasonable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited pros/cons, scaling, and criteria for readiness which produced concrete motivators, but could have more deeply explored personal values to strengthen and increase change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned choices as collaborative, invited the patient to choose next steps, and built resources together, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asked for pros/cons and used scaling to draw out change-relevant language and actionable thresholds, but missed opportunities to amplify brief change statements into stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized sustain talk while reframing ambivalence into safety-seeking steps, though could use more double-sided reflections and discrepancy work to reduce sustain talk further.
