# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing the patient’s fears and caregiving burdens to create a safe tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses high-quality, accurate empathic listening and complex reflections that capture both affect and practical context throughout the interaction.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (making a brief call/phone triage) and ties every question and reflection to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in true co‑creation by soliciting the patient’s preferences, offering help as requested, and jointly developing a concrete action plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice and control by offering options, checking preferences, and avoiding directives to preserve the patient’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient’s intrinsic motivations (caring for child, peace of mind) and links those values to practical, small steps toward action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently acts as a collaborator, sharing decision‑making and inviting the patient to choose wording and strategies that feel doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with strategic questions and reflections, producing concrete intentions; could strengthen by eliciting more explicit commitment language (specific time or confidence).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk while offering smaller, manageable options to reduce avoidance; would benefit from more explicit double‑sided reflections to surface ambivalence.
