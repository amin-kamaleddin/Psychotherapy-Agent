### Empathy

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

**Comments:** Avoided confronting sustain talk but did not use double-sided reflections or gentle evocation to explore minimizations about stress and alcohol, missing opportunities to shift ambivalence.
