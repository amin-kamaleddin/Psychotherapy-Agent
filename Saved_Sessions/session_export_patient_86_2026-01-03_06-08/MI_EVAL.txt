### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician conveys explicit unconditional positive regard (“You’re worthy and I accept where you are.”). Consistently nonjudgmental language and normalization of ambivalence across multiple turns; avoids lecturing or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☑5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking from the outset (detailed opening reflection). Repeated, accurate reflections of tone, concerns, and values (autonomy, dislike of lectures).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☑5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on smoking; progressively narrows to a single, concrete behavioral test (delay first cigarette 30 minutes) and measurable success criteria.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☑5
**Comments:** Work framed as joint (“Let’s figure this out together”), clinician offers options and explicitly defers final choices to the patient; patient-selected supports and follow‑up integrated into plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☑5
**Comments:** Multiple explicit affirmations of patient control (“keeps you in charge,” “no lectures”). Avoids pressuring language and frames the small test as the patient’s experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☑5
**Comments:** Elicits patient reasons and motives (care about smile, keeping his word); uses evocative questions repeatedly and draws actionable change statements from the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☑5
**Comments:** Treats patient as the expert on his life; shared decision‑making evident (patient chooses supports, tracking, and follow‑up). Language fosters collaboration throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☑5
**Comments:** Effectively amplifies and consolidates change talk into a concrete commitment (specific plan, calendar Xs, self‑reward). Reflections and questions deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☑4 ☐5
**Comments:** Successfully rolls with resistance and avoids arguing; sustain talk accepted and redirected toward a low‑pressure test. Opportunity to strengthen use of double‑sided reflections or explicit cost/benefit exploration to further dilute sustain talk.
