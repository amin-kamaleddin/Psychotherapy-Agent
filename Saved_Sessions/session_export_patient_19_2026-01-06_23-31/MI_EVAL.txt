# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, nonjudgmental tone that validates Anya’s honesty and insight and avoids blaming or labeling. This warm stance supports trust and a sense of being accepted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and validation, reflecting key content and responding to affect. Would benefit from deeper affective reflections (e.g., naming shame or fear) and more complex reflections to increase emotional depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on gambling and concrete behavior-specific actions, repeatedly returning to practical steps and eliciting an immediate commitment. This clear, action-oriented focus supports momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages Anya as an active partner by co-constructing scripts, tailoring resources, and seeking consent for role-play and next steps. Decisions are negotiated and Anya’s ideas meaningfully shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding coercive language while framing suggestions as choices. This approach strengthens Anya’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation by linking actions to Anya’s travel goal and prompting commitments, creating opportunities for change talk. However, reliance on external solutions limits deeper elicitation of her own reasons, confidence, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative with invitations to co-create scripts and tailor resources, and by negotiating decisions like role-play. At times the clinician provides many recommendations and could improve by eliciting Anya’s priorities before presenting options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk, prompts for concrete steps, and offers role-play to strengthen commitment. Missed opportunities remain to amplify spontaneous change statements and to elicit confidence/commitment scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing minimizing statements and redirects toward practical risk-reduction strategies, reducing sustain talk’s influence. Could more consistently use MI-specific techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to gently resolve ambivalence.
