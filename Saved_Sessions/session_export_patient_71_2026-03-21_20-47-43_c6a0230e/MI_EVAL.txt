# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful, warm, and nonjudgmental, with frequent validation and affirmations of effort. The clinician maintains unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that accurately summarizes the patient's struggles. Could deepen affective reflections (e.g., exploring underlying emotions like guilt or shame) to reach a higher empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on the target behavior and moves clearly from values to specific, measurable steps. The clinician consistently elicits triggers, micro-strategies, and confidence/success criteria to maintain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is an equal, collaborative process with the patient actively shaping choices and strategies. The clinician solicits input, builds on patient ideas, and negotiates plans together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Honors patient choice by offering options, alternatives, and inviting the patient's decisions without pressure. The clinician fosters ownership and avoids prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly draws motivation from the patient's own values and reasons, using questions and rulers to elicit internal motivation. The session centers patient-generated motivations and practical ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative stance with clear power-sharing and permission-seeking before offering ideas. Decisions are co-constructed and the patient leads selection of strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions and concrete planning that produces change statements. Could more often use selective reflections and summaries to reinforce desire/reason/ability/commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes lapses, and offers compassionate resets and fallback plans. Missed opportunities exist for more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and further soften sustain language.
