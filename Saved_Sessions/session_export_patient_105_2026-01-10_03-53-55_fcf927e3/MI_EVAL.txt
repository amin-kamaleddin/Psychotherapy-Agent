# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing Naomi's experience and affirming small successes throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, mirroring Naomi's language and linking symptoms to her values and work impact.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on managing anxiety during shoots with practical, measurable goals (identify wave timing, micro-exposure, rehearsals) and monitored readiness throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative: decisions were co-created, Naomi's suggestions were integrated, and the clinician treated the plan as a joint experiment rather than didactic advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Naomi's sense of choice by asking permission, offering options, and reinforcing her ownership of the plan and cues.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Naomi's ideas, confidence ratings, and a client-generated experiment, but offering multiple concrete strategies early reduced time spent drawing out deeper personal motivations and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly treated Naomi as the expert on her experience, shared power in decision-making, and invited her preferences at multiple points to co-construct the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk (importance/confidence, small steps) but could have amplified desire/ability/reasons more before moving into strategy suggestions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and often redirected to experiments, though more double-sided reflections and explicit contrast with Naomi's change statements could further resolve ambivalence.
