# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental understanding (e.g., “I hear how exhausted and discouraged you are,” “I’m sorry — that’s exhausting”). Normalized setbacks and affirmed small progress (“You’ve planned a clear, doable step — that’s progress”).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent accurate reflections and empathic statements that matched affect and content (“that canceled job is a painful setback,” “that doubt is understandable”). Missed some opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections naming the emotional core (e.g., drained of hope).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on a single, concrete target (accessing emergency financial/food help). Provided specific tools (2-1-1 script, upload list, small action plan) and repeatedly returned to the goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited client input and co‑constructed the plan (offered choices, asked which felt doable, asked permission to role‑play, stepped back after coaching). Avoided an authoritarian stance and respected client limits.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supported client choice and control (offered options, respected limit of a single step, affirmed right to try one small action). Language reinforced ownership and minimized pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited reasons/willingness (asked which option felt doable, invited role‑play, requested noticing a small win). Could have used more open, evocative questions to deepen reasons and connect to values or longer‑term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned interaction as a joint effort (offered to draft script, role‑played operator, offered to review responses). Consistently power‑shared and used client expertise about feasibility.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some change talk (client: “I’ll try that call,” “I’ll spend 10–15 minutes tonight…”). Missed opportunities to amplify and deepen change talk (no confidence ruler, few amplified or strategic reflections).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and used normalization/reframing (“If the call doesn’t solve everything, it still moves you toward options”). Could use more double‑sided reflections and selective reinforcement of change talk to further dilute sustain talk.
