# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and double-sided reflections that normalize ambivalence and validate Tyler’s position. Repeated acceptance of Tyler’s choices reduces shame and supports a safe atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows accurate, sustained perspective-taking with specific reflections that capture both content and underlying meaning (values, conflict, realistic self-appraisal). Reflections are regularly used to check understanding and deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on risky drinking and protecting Tyler’s semester, with every question and reflection tied to behaviorally specific goals. The session progresses logically from values to concrete steps without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is an equitable partnership: the clinician solicits Tyler’s ideas at each decision point and revises the plan based on his preferences. Tyler’s suggestions drive the practical strategy, showing strong co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Tyler’s choice and control by asking what feels acceptable and accepting imperfections rather than imposing a plan. Language supports self-determination and ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Tyler’s reasons, abilities, and plans for change with evocative questions and follow-ups, leading to concrete strategies. Could further evoke deeper intrinsic values and strengthen commitment language (e.g., scaling questions).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructed interaction with active power-sharing—clinician asks preferences, invites choices, and integrates Tyler’s ideas into the plan. Decisions are made collaboratively and respect Tyler’s expertise about what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk through targeted open questions and reflective listening, moving Tyler toward doable steps (e.g., not drinking before classes). Missed opportunities include amplifying tentative change statements and probing importance/confidence or stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk using double-sided reflections that validate social motives while redirecting to priorities, which reduces sustain talk’s momentum. Could more actively elicit the downsides of continued heavy drinking and draw sharper discrepancy to shift language toward change.
