# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard—praised insight, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed the patient's concerns without judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used strong reflective listening and validations that acknowledged feelings and perspective, though a few more complex/amplified reflections could have deepened emotional resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on phone/social media use and linked outcomes (sleep, coursework), progressing purposefully to specific micro-goals, an if-then plan, and a scheduled check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-created the experiment, reminder wording, and recovery plan by soliciting patient input at each step, demonstrating shared problem-solving and joint ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported choice by offering options, asking preferences, and respecting limits, which preserved the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own motivations, past attempts, and resources, resulting in a concrete, patient-led 3-day experiment and recovery plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative—asked permission before offering strategies, offered choices, and invited patient decisions that shaped the plan multiple times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with goal questions, readiness/confidence scaling, and micro-goals, but could have more consistently amplified and summarized the patient's change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized ambivalence while offering respectful strategies, though more double-sided reflections or strategic redirection would further tip momentum toward change.
