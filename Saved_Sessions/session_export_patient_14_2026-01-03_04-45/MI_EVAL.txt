**Comments:** Strong, accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening (e.g., “constantly juggling tasks and working long hours,” “not promising perfection”). Reflections capture content and affect and deepen understanding.

**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative: clinician offers menus/options and invites patient choice (e.g., “Which single step…would you choose?”). Decisions are co‑constructed and patient-driven.

**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces patient choice and normalizes imperfect follow‑through (“Not promising miracles,” “if it’s not realistic we’ll try something else”). Avoids directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1 [ ] 2 [ ] 3 [ ] 4 [ ] 5 [x]
**Comments:** Elicits the patient’s own motivations and solutions with open, evocative questions (“What matters most…?”, “What’s one small, realistic change…?”). Change talk is elicited and expanded.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 1 [ ] 2 [ ] 3 [ ] 4 [ ] 5 [x]
**Comments:** Conversation reflects true partnership: clinician frames options, defers to patient judgment, and asks patient to select and shape the plan (calendar slot, headset, walking during calls).

**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk and turns it into implementation intentions (asks what would make the walk doable, which step to try first, backup plans), strengthening commitment language.
