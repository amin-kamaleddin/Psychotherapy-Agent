# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Communicates respectful, nonjudgmental regard—warm opening, affirmations, and normalization of anxiety. Some responses are formulaic and task-focused, lacking deeper individualized emotional validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective-taking and reflective listening, validating fears and support. Empathy is not consistently deepened—few complex reflections of shame or appearing weak before shifting to directive teaching.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on reducing drinking, managing triggers, and preventing impulsive reactions with concrete strategies. Every intervention clearly ties back to behavioral goals and next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages in collaborative problem-solving with trigger input, tailored scripts, rehearsal, and feasibility checks. At times the clinician provides prepackaged solutions that feel expert-led rather than fully co-constructed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers multiple options and occasionally asks which feels doable, supporting choice. Many responses remain prescriptive with long lists and fewer explicit affirmations of Luka’s control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some internal content (triggers, feasibility) but is heavier on supplying strategies than drawing out motives, values, or past successes. Missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes collaborative moments and invitations to choose, but often adopts an expert stance with ready-made scripts and step-by-step plans. Power-sharing is present but uneven, limiting co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk and affirms progress while asking about triggers to plan. Rarely draws out or amplifies Luka’s own motivations or asks questions to deepen desire, reasons, or commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids strengthening sustain talk, supports partial change, and provides harm-reduction strategies to maintain progress. Could be stronger with double-sided reflections that explicitly acknowledge social pulls while redirecting to goals.
