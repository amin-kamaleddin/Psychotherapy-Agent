# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard—opening with support and safety checks, normalizing the patient's experience, and offering permission to rest or choose smaller steps without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections and tailored normalizing statements that matched the patient's tone and reduced shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on increasing small amounts of movement and relapse-prevention, using concrete incremental goals and targeted troubleshooting rather than drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine collaborative stance—solicited the patient's input, offered alternatives, and helped translate her ideas into practical tools like alarm labels and if-then plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking permission to help with wording/planning, and explicitly permitting smaller or no activity on bad days.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some intrinsic motivation and personal reasons but missed opportunities to deepen evocation (e.g., asking about values, personal meaning of more energy, or using scaling for importance/confidence).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited collaboration and deferred to the patient's choices, co-constructing plans and refining them based on her preferences and expertise about what feels doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced concrete change talk and commitments (e.g., 10-minute walk, alarm label), but could have amplified desire/ability/reason/need language and probed confidence or importance more deeply.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively softened sustain talk by reframing misses as data, lowering the bar with micro-options, and providing compassionate scripts and permission to reduce self-critical responses.
