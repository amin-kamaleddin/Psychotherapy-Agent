# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, warmth, and specific affirmations while normalizing Camila's experience, supporting a top rating.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing practical impacts and ambivalence; could probe more for deeper feelings (fear of losing friends, loneliness) to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking and repeatedly steered toward concrete, actionable steps without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with shared decision-making, inclusive language, and frequent invitations for Camila's input and choice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and resources with open questions and planning, but could more consistently amplify the client's change statements and link them to values or longer-term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Camila as the expert on her life and co-constructed the plan, repeatedly seeking her preferences and allowing her to shape the approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk through scaling and specific planning, though could more strategically amplify client change language and elicit explicit commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
