# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently demonstrated nonjudgmental acceptance and respect throughout the exchange, normalizing ambivalence and validating the patient's stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking captured ambivalence and practical concerns, but reflections were sometimes repetitive and did not probe deeper emotional pain or fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on cannabis use, peer dynamics, and safety planning, effectively steering the session toward a concrete harm-reduction plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative with co-creation of the plan, role-play offers, and joint problem-solving that positioned clinician and patient as partners.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently honored the patient's agency, offered choices, avoided coercive language, and invited the patient to select what felt doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited multiple client-generated harm-reduction strategies and reasons for change, but missed opportunities to probe deeper values, longer-term goals, or intrinsic motives for broader behavior change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and inviting of input, though the clinician occasionally led with options rather than prompting the patient to generate ideas first to fully shift expertise to him.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited concrete change talk around safety and harm reduction, but less successful at deepening desire, reasons, and confidence for broader reduction or cessation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged both sides, redirecting to safety planning, but could further weaken sustain talk using double-sided or strategic reflections to explore underlying maintenance factors.
