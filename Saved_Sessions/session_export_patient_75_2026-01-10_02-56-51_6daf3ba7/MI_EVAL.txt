# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard—validated concerns, normalized feelings, and avoided judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Showed accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, paraphrasing values and anxieties to convey strong understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on inhaler adherence and the monitored-trial plan, with each turn tied back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fully collaborative—co‑created the one‑page plan, solicited and incorporated patient edits, and adjusted the product to reflect Linda's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission before information, and leaving decisions about dose/stop criteria to the patient and PCP.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations with evocative questions and reflections but could have elicited more personal reasons and used confidence/importance scaling to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as an equal partnership—regularly invited input and edits and positioned Linda as a co-author of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and co‑created a concrete trial plan, though missed opportunities to amplify change language and elicit commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and redirected to safety-focused strategies, but could have used more double-sided reflections or strategic rolling with sustain talk.
