# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally warm, nonjudgmental, and affirming with normalizing statements, but an exaggerated, alarmed role‑play response risked conveying judgment and reduced unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and perspective‑taking that made the teen feel heard, though a dramatized reaction and some missed chances to explore deeper emotions weakened empathic consistency.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on the behavioral target (disclosure) with concrete next steps, practical planning, and resistance to topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was highly collaborative—offered options, asked permission, and routinely invited the patient's input to co‑create scripts and plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering multiple options, avoiding pressure, and explicitly asking what the patient preferred.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited desired outcomes and motivations but often supplied scripts instead of eliciting client‑generated language and missed systematic questions about importance and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a genuine partnership by soliciting preferences, offering to role‑play or model, and letting the adolescent shape the plan and wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and rehearsal, but could do more to deepen and amplify client‑generated reasons, confidence, and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated and normalized sustain talk and redirected it toward manageable steps, though stronger use of double‑sided reflections and less reactive role‑play would better resolve ambivalence.
