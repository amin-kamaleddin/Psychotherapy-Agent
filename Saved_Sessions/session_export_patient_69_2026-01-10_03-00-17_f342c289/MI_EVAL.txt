# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized the client's guardedness, avoiding blame or pressure. Responded respectfully to dismissive replies throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and accurate understanding with frequent summaries and perspective-taking. Could include more complex, affect‑focused reflections to deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on engagement with required activities and actionable goals. Questions and offers stayed tightly linked to concrete steps toward work.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaved as an equal partner by soliciting preferences, offering contingent supports, and co‑constructing the plan. Tone and decision-making conveyed teamwork rather than expert-driven instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and honoring limits without pressuring. Consistently reinforced the client's autonomy (e.g., 'Your call', not pushing commitments).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client's own motivations and intentions, producing concrete change language and a small test. Could probe importance and confidence and explore personal reasons/values more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint project and checked in before acting, which fostered client influence over the plan. Could more explicitly highlight the client's expertise and tie their values/limits into the summary.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited specific change talk and narrowed it to a doable experiment (look up one listing). Could strengthen by amplifying and reflecting change talk and eliciting confidence/importance language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and acknowledged doubt but used few strategic techniques to soften it. Would benefit from double-sided/complex reflections and questions like 'What makes you say maybe?' to reduce sustain talk.
