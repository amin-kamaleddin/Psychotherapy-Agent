### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, with repeated validations and normalization of Dylan's concerns; no shaming or moralizing throughout the session.

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the conversation tightly focused on the incident and concrete remedial steps (course, mitigation email, rehearsals) and moves the session purposefully toward action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly joint problem‑solving: the clinician invites input, co‑writes the email, and uses role‑play with Dylan directing key choices, demonstrating shared work rather than authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits pragmatic, externally‑motivated reasons (protecting academics, avoiding sanctions) but does not systematically draw out deeper intrinsic values, importance/confidence, or discrepancy.

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete change behaviors (agreeing to a course and drafting/polishing an email) but misses opportunities to amplify change language and explore personal reasons, importance, or confidence for change.
