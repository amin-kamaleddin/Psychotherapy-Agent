# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Yasmin’s experience (e.g., “That makes total sense and doesn’t mean you’ve failed”). Avoided blaming/labeling and validated frustration with prior diets throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking; reflections accurately captured ambivalence and values. Missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections probing emotional weight (e.g., shame, identity tied to weight).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on sustainable behavior change. Each turn advanced toward concrete steps (values → small habit → backups → experiment). Questions consistently steered toward actionable goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Work framed as joint: frequent invitational questions and patient-selected plans. Clinician avoided expert-first prescriptions and co‑created the experiment/backups with Yasmin.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong use of invitational language and normalization of ambivalence. Clinician preserved Yasmin’s choice and control throughout (e.g., “What would you be willing to try?”).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Yasmin’s own reasons, values, and criteria for success (steady energy, non‑scale wins). Prompts drew out motivation and shaped it into specific, personally meaningful experiments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power‑sharing; Yasmin’s ideas drove decisions (choice of habit, backup). Reflections and summaries reinforced the collaborative relationship.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicited and reinforced change talk, built on strengths (routine, accountability), and translated it into concrete commitments (two gym sessions, snack, check‑ins).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and respected (e.g., ambivalence about weight loss vs. sustainability). Less use of strategic techniques to diminish sustain talk (double‑sided reflections, discrepancy questions) before redirecting to change.
