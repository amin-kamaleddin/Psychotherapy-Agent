# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing concerns and repeatedly reassuring the patient.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated reflective listening and perspective-taking but missed opportunities for deeper emotional exploration and more complex reflections of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on blood glucose monitoring with a simple, actionable plan and concrete thresholds.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—solicited preferences, co-created the checklist and practiced scripts—though partnership was occasionally diluted by teaching moments.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed the patient's choice and control, offered options, and framed missed checks as low-stakes to reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some willingness to try but relied heavily on providing solutions and education rather than drawing out the patient's own motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited input and co-constructed plans (short two-check plan, alarms, scripts) but occasionally shifted back into expert-driven instruction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited limited change talk (agreement to try) but did not systematically evoke or amplify reasons, importance, or confidence with targeted questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized reluctance, though could strengthen impact with more double-sided reflections to shift sustain talk toward change.
