**Comments:** Emphasizes choice and frames plan as a low‑pressure experiment rather than a rule; accepts hedged commitments and invites the patient to choose backups (alarm, phone placement, telling friends).

**Comments:** Clinician elicits the patient’s own reasons and concrete solutions repeatedly (doing homework after school, timers, phone in another room, specific days), drawing out motivation and plans from the patient.

**Comments:** Power is shared; clinician invites patient expertise and adjusts recommendations to her choices (“Which backup sounds best…?”, co‑constructs the experiment). Interaction is collaborative throughout.
