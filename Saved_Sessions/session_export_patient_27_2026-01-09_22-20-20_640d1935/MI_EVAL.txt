# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating Daniela and avoiding blame. Warmth and reassurance were present throughout, supporting her vulnerability.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, supportive reflections and normalized anxiety, and summarized mood and readiness early. Could deepen emotional reflections to capture fear of rejection and underlying feelings more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, focused goal on disclosure and consistently oriented interventions (scripts, breathing, practice) toward that target. Avoided drifting and redirected to concrete next steps effectively.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited input and choices and built on Daniela's responses, offering ongoing support for replies. Tended to supply ready-made scripts rather than co-creating initial language, which slightly reduced equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, checked readiness, and framed suggestions as optional, respecting Daniela's pace and self-direction. There was no pressuring language and autonomy was strongly supported.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some small commitments and offered practice, but emphasized options and scripts over drawing out Daniela's own reasons, values, and motivations. More open questions and strategic reflections would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited Daniela's perspective and asked how to support her next steps, sharing control of the process. Offering many clinician-provided scripts early reduced opportunities for co-construction of her voice.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced existing change talk and prompted action with concrete tools, helping movement toward sending the message. Relied more on providing scripts than eliciting Daniela's own motivations, values, or confidence to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and offered small steps to reduce barriers, preventing escalation. Did not consistently use double-sided reflections, pros/cons exploration, or gentle reframing to resolve ambivalence more strategically.
