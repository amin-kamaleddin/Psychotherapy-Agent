# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with multiple explicit validations and restatements of respect, never challenging the client's desire for privacy.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections accurately acknowledge the client's feelings and perspective, but they become somewhat formulaic and repetitive rather than deepening emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains appropriate focus on initial engagement and safety, repeatedly follows the client's lead; could be strengthened by briefly clarifying a follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint process by offering options and repeatedly deferring to the client's preferences, creating an equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy with repeated statements of choice, avoids pressuring language, and backs off when the client declines interventions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Shows limited evocation of the client's internal motivations beyond an initial open question and does not use brief probes or scaling to draw out reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invites the client's preferences repeatedly and defers to her lead, though the interaction at times feels clinician-scripted rather than co-creating next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to elicit change talk after the client declines to engage; the clinician largely moved into containment rather than trying gentle evocation strategies.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively softens sustain talk by avoiding argument, validating resistance, offering choices, and preserving engagement throughout the encounter.
