# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently displayed unconditional positive regard, normalized frustration, and validated the patient's low‑effort preferences without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective‑taking, but reflections leaned toward practical/behavioral content with missed opportunities to name deeper emotions like irritation or resignation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on quitting/reducing smoking and moved logically from exploration to concrete implementation planning without being directive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in genuine teamwork by soliciting preferences, integrating the patient's suggestions, and jointly adapting the plan to her context and schedule.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed choice, offered options, accepted reservations, and supported the patient's control over whether and how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's practical ideas and solutions but did not routinely probe values, meaning, or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a shared process, co‑constructing the strategy and emphasizing the patient's expertise about her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced multiple statements favoring change and concrete steps, but missed opportunities to amplify change language, scale confidence/importance, or elicit a commitment statement.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully rolled with resistance, avoided arguing or confronting sustain talk, and redirected to small, autonomy‑preserving options to keep momentum.
