# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveys unconditional positive regard from the start and uses affirmations and normalizing language throughout, creating a nonjudgmental, supportive tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and paraphrases capture the patient's stress, low energy, and emotional eating, with validation that shows strong emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (10‑minute after‑work walks) and repeatedly steers the conversation toward concrete implementation steps and contingency plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine two‑way problem‑solving by offering options, asking the patient to choose among them, and checking feasibility, resulting in a jointly constructed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, respecting the patient's decision to decline friend check‑ins, and inviting the patient to select and refine strategies without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses open questions and confidence scaling to draw out the patient's own motivations, ideas, and fallback solutions, strengthening internal problem‑solving and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance with frequent invitations for the patient's input and shared decision‑making, ensuring the plan reflects the patient's preferences and context.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk through targeted prompts, scaling questions, and asking for specific implementation steps, which generated concrete commitments from the patient.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges the patient's concerns about lapses without arguing and redirects to pragmatic if‑then plans and implementation strategies that validate barriers while promoting action.
