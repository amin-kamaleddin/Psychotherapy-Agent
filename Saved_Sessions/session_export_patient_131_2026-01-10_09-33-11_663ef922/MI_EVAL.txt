# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and warmth, using affirmations and nonjudgmental language while avoiding labels or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides frequent, accurate reflections that capture both practical concerns and affective states, mirroring the client's language and emotional tone throughout.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing alcohol, suitable living arrangements, and engagement with Stopping Violence, guiding the session from ambivalence to concrete planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as shared decision‑making, offers options (drafting pact, role‑play), and consistently follows the client's lead without an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoids pressure and repeatedly asks for the client's preferences about priorities and next steps, reinforcing the client's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits substantial motivational material and detailed plans but could more often use strategies (scaling, summary of reasons, direct commitment prompts) to deepen and solidify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the client as an active partner, co‑constructing decisions and using collaborative language to share expertise and power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with affirmations and open, change‑focused questions, and supports concrete steps for implementation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates ambivalence and uses double‑sided reflections to avoid reinforcing sustain talk, though could more strategically briefly reflect sustain talk then immediately amplify change talk or reframe.
