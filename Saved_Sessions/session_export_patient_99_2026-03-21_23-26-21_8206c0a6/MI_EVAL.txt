**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Matteo’s ambivalence while affirming his priorities; no blaming or minimization, which reduces shame and supports disclosure.

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in the context of ankle recovery and links discussion to functional goals; conversation progresses logically to concrete, actionable steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces Matteo’s sense of choice, accepts partial commitment, and asks which steps feel doable; avoids directives and preserves client autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Matteo’s own reasons and prior strategies for change, drawing motivation from him rather than imposing it; could be strengthened by eliciting confidence ratings and amplifying expressed motivation to build commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters power-sharing and positions Matteo as the expert by inviting him to co-direct the plan and choose supports; regularly defers to his judgment about what’s realistic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and strengthens change talk with concrete, change-focused questions that yield specific plans; should more frequently amplify and summarize change language and elicit explicit commitment/confidence.
