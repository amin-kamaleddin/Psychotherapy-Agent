# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with affirmations and normalization of slips.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate summaries but reflections were sometimes formulaic and could probe deeper into underlying emotions and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on smoking reduction, returning to concrete behavioral options and producing actionable, measurable next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by offering options, checking feasibility, and co-constructing the plan based on the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice and control, avoided pressuring language, and reinforced ownership of the plan while validating imperfections.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's own motivations and past successes and used the patient's examples to build internally driven reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited input and shaped the plan together, consistently sharing power and decision-making with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and linked to past success, but could more deliberately amplify and selectively reflect stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and normalized ambivalence, though at times sustain talk was restated rather than reframed with double-sided or tilt-toward-change reflections.
