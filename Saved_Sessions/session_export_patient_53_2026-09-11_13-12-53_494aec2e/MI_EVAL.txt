### Acceptance

**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates nonjudgmental, supportive regard—validating pacing and normalizing ambivalence while framing steps as small and reversible. No shaming or dismissive language is present.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking that mirrors Isabella's ambivalence, but reflections are sometimes formulaic. Could deepen empathy with more complex, affect‑focused reflections and exploration of hesitancy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on increasing community participation and progressively narrows to a concrete, tiny reversible trial. No drifting to unrelated topics and keeps momentum toward actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages Isabella as an equal partner by co-creating reminder wording, offering to practice a script, and soliciting her input before moving forward. The plan is constructed so Isabella's choices determine next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes Isabella's pace and offers multiple options rather than directives, framing steps as reversible and nonpressuring. Questions and language consistently bolster client choice and ownership.

#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits pragmatic problem‑solving and concrete plans but rarely draws out intrinsic motives, values, or confidence/importance ratings. Misses opportunities for evocative open questions to deepen internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses collaborative pronouns and co-creation language to center Isabella's preferences and expertise, inviting her to shape the plan. This conveys genuine power-sharing and respect for her choices.
