# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validated the patient’s experience (e.g., "That anger makes sense"), avoiding blame or minimization throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displayed strong, accurate empathic reflections matching physical sensations and consequences (jaw clenching, pacing, spillover into work/home), though could probe deeper into underlying emotional meaning and use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused agenda on anger and practical strategies, moving efficiently from exploration to two concrete options and a succinct pre/post plan with reusable templates.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by soliciting and incorporating the patient’s wording preferences (blunt single-line scripts), offering technical help only after asking, and co-creating the final tools.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered clear choices (in-the-moment vs pre-appointment plan), invited edits, and encouraged a low-pressure small-experiment approach that preserved the patient’s control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited pragmatic motivations (protecting job, avoiding spillover) and co-created feasible strategies, but could further draw out deeper personal values and probe confidence/barriers.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong power-sharing by treating the patient as the expert on wording and needs, iteratively adjusting templates based on the patient’s input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively evoked and amplified change talk by offering options and eliciting concrete goals and commitments (copying templates, trying once), yet missed explicit scaling of commitment/confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled skepticism nonconfrontationally and reframed toward small experiments and tweaks, though could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and steer toward change language.
