# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistent, nonjudgmental positive regard throughout: validating language, normalization, and affirmation (e.g., calling the plan realistic and a win) conveyed unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking about practical stresses and tiredness, but the clinician stayed at surface-level concerns and could probe deeper emotions or meaning to reach a top score.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, appropriate focus on a specific target behavior (study/time-management) with stepwise guidance from options to an explicit one-week Pomodoro plan and troubleshooting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt genuinely collaborative with co-construction of the plan, frequent invitations to the patient's input, and teamwork language rather than an expert-driven approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering choices, emphasizing the patient is in charge, allowing modification or decline, and asking how she wanted to report back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's preferences and implementation ideas (choosing Pomodoro, barriers), but could further evoke personal reasons, values, prior successes, or use scaling to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly framed the session as client-led and repeatedly invited the patient's input, demonstrating genuine power-sharing and co-creation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (selecting Pomodoro, concrete plan), but missed opportunities to amplify commitment/confidence (e.g., confidence scaling or summarizing commitment language).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk skillfully by normalizing, rolling with resistance, reframing small efforts as meaningful, and offering pragmatic troubleshooting without confrontation.
