# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, validating efforts and normalizing constraints with a warm, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and mirrored the client's language, but reflections tended to be paraphrastic and could more deeply name emotions like shame or discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus on reconnecting with morning walks and moved systematically from motivation to a concrete, measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal collaborator—asking, inviting suggestions, and co-constructing the plan so decisions were driven by the patient's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored choice and agency, offered options rather than directives, and accepted hedging without pressure to support ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited reasons and past successes to surface internal motivation, but could deepen evocation by eliciting importance/confidence ratings and amplifying commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Ceded expertise to the client and consistently invited her preferences, enabling a client-led planning process and strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk (past success, desired outcomes), though more frequent amplification, summarizing of DARN-C, and probing for commitment/confidence would strengthen momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing barriers and reframed realism as acceptable, but could more actively soften sustain talk with double-sided reflections and explicit contingency planning for missed mornings.
