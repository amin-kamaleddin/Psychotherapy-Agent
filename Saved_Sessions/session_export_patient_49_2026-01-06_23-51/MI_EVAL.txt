# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, warmth, and nonjudgmental support through validating statements and praise. No signs of blaming or minimizing were observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking with accurate reflections of stressors and practical concerns, but reflections tended to be surface-level. Deeper emotional reflections (e.g., frustration, identity/social meaning) would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking reduction/cessation and guided the session toward a concrete, patient-chosen plan with specific steps and start date. The session stayed goal-directed without losing a patient-centered tone.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a true collaboration with the patient invited to shape the plan at every step, including wording and role-play. Clinician consistently positioned Hana as co-designer rather than a passive recipient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy was explicitly supported through affirmation of choice, offering multiple options, and repeatedly asking for Hana’s preferences. Clinician respected Hana’s control and invited her selection throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values and used scaling to draw out motivation, producing incremental increases in readiness and confidence. At times the clinician moved quickly to concrete solutions and could further amplify Hana’s own reasons and elicit a clear verbal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the interaction as a partnership, asking Hana to choose strategies and co-constructing the plan. Power-sharing was evident in offering options and seeking her input before suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited change talk with rulers, options, and planning prompts that raised readiness and confidence. Missed some opportunities to immediately reflect, amplify, and summarize Hana’s change language to consolidate commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk, normalized slips, and gently redirected to changeable steps with concrete alternatives. Could more often use double-sided reflections and selectively downplay sustain talk while amplifying change talk.
