# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental respect, normalizing sarcasm and validating the patient’s protective stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, nuanced understanding with specific content-and-feeling reflections, tying past successes to the current plan and paraphrasing boundaries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on consistent preventer use with a concrete two-week experiment and repeatedly redirects to practical, doable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-creates the plan by soliciting the patient’s past strategies and preferences and negotiating decisions rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by framing the plan as an experiment the patient controls and honoring the no-check-ins boundary.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic reasons and links to past success, but could probe importance/confidence more and elicit a clear verbal commitment or coping plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong power-sharing: the plan originates from the patient’s ideas and the clinician repeatedly defers choices to the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces several change statements and uses evocative questions, yet stops short of systematically amplifying or consolidating commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk, avoids arguing or pressuring, and effectively minimizes escalation while preserving engagement.
