# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently used nonjudgmental language, normalizing and validating Lars' feelings and offering support, creating a warm, shame-reducing atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and perspective-taking with supportive reflections of anxiety and embarrassment, though a few deeper reflective summaries of ambivalence were missing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on concrete target behaviors (employment/education, avoiding reoffending, managing alcohol/peers) with actionable next steps throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—inviting preferences and co-working on tasks—but sometimes provided ready-made scripts instead of first co-creating wording with Lars.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before acting, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation by inviting tasks and reinforcing choices, but relied heavily on offering solutions rather than asking evocative questions to draw out Lars' own reasons and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through choices and offers to help, yet at times shifted toward an expert role by delivering detailed scripts instead of jointly constructing them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and affirmed some change statements and commitments, but frequently moved quickly to solutions rather than reflecting and amplifying Lars' own arguments for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided endorsing sustain talk and provided practical alternatives, but did not explicitly use double-sided reflections or summaries to acknowledge the pull of mates while guiding toward change.
