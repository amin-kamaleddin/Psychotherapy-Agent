# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with validating reflections and affirmations, keeping a warm nonjudgmental tone throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking were evident via accurate paraphrases, summaries, and attention to emotional/contextual details beyond facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and safety, moving logically from problem recognition to specific, client-centered behavioral plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co-constructed—clinician invited choices, offered help only after assent, and allowed Evelyn’s input to shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, and framing suggestions as possibilities that Evelyn could accept or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from Evelyn with open questions, elicitation of personal reasons, confidence rulers, and prompting concrete self-generated steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated shared power through frequent solicitation of Evelyn’s preferences and by asking her to choose which strategies felt most doable.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk using evocation, summarizing intentions, and exploring feasibility and supports to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and used double-sided reflection early, but could have been more strategic in systematically naming both sides and immediately amplifying brief change talk after sustain statements.
