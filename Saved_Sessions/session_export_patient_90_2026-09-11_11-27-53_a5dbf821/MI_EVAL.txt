# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and honored Marcus's values and constraints (wife, dog, finances). Built a plan that avoided pressuring him to give up the dog, demonstrating strong acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening with accurate, timely reflections of practical and emotional concerns (swamped, money tight, sleep/cough). Could deepen affective or complex reflections to capture overwhelm and attachment more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused agenda on improving asthma control and moved the conversation toward concrete next steps (12–1 callback, editable checklist, trial home change). Balanced client-centered language with behavioral direction effectively.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged Marcus as an equal partner by soliciting preferences for timing, wording, and format and co-creating the checklist and plan. Decisions were clearly co-constructed and responsive to his input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered multiple options, asked which felt most doable, and avoided directive language, reinforcing Marcus's control over decisions. Provided editable materials and invited his exact phrasing to support autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Marcus's reasons for change (better sleep, more energy, less worry) and elicited concrete goals. Could more systematically elicit confidence/importance ratings, past successes, or a commitment statement to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned Marcus as the decision-maker, invited his input on options and phrasing, and followed his lead throughout the session. Demonstrated clear power-sharing and respect for his expertise about his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited multiple instances of change talk tied to concrete benefits and steps (sleeping through night, more energy, med review). Could have amplified and summarized the most potent change language and elicited ability/confidence and commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk about dog attachment, time, and cost without challenging and offered low-burden alternatives to defuse resistance. Could incorporate more strategic double-sided reflections or reframing to further resolve ambivalence.
