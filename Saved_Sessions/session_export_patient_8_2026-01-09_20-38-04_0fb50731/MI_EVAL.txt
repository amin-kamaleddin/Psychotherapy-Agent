# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—warm, affirming language and nonjudgmental normalization of setbacks throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking with accurate reflections and summaries of both change and sustain talk, though the clinician often moved quickly to suggestions instead of exploring the emotional texture behind sustain talk and anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on improving sleep with concrete steps tied to the target behavior, keeping the conversation goal-directed without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving—solutions were co‑constructed, the clinician asked for input and offered editable drafts, and decisions reflected the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong support for autonomy with choices offered, feasibility/confidence checks, and framing actions as the patient's decision while normalizing slips and encouraging self‑curiosity.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation through importance/confidence questions and preparatory commitment, but could more often use evocative questions and summaries to deepen intrinsic reasons and strengthen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance—clinician invited preferences, shared power, and repeatedly deferred to the patient's expertise when shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and translated it into specific actions, though could more frequently reflect and amplify the patient's own change statements to deepen desire/need/ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and avoided confrontation, but tended to shift quickly to practical solutions instead of using MI strategies like double‑sided reflections or explicit linking of costs and benefits to reduce sustain talk's hold.
