# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm and nonjudgmental—normalizes ambivalence and explicitly affirms Camila’s efforts (e.g., appreciating referral, validating the plan). No minimization or criticism, demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking with frequent, concise reflections and mirroring of Camila’s language; checks understanding with open questions. These behaviors reinforced that Camila felt heard and understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on the specific target behavior (short after-dinner walks) and consistently guides toward concrete, observable steps and measurable outcomes. Minimal drift with purposeful questions advancing planning and problem-solving.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session framed as a joint enterprise using inclusive questions and frequent solicitation of Camila’s choices; summaries incorporate her wording and invite next steps. The clinician co-constructs the plan rather than prescribing it.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports choice and control—offers options, normalizes permission to stop at 10 minutes, and avoids pressuring language. Reinforces Camila’s ownership of the plan and tailoring to her energy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical motivations and desirable outcomes but could better draw out deeper values or personal reasons for change. Missed opportunities to use scaling questions (importance/confidence) to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Camila as the expert on what will work, using collaborative phrasing and deferring to her preferences (e.g., choosing supports, how to involve her husband). Strong power-sharing and mutual decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with concrete planning and outcome queries, producing commitment-related language. Could deepen and convert desire/intent into firmer commitment by probing 'why' the change matters and eliciting strength/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reflects sustain talk without arguing, reframing barriers into solvable supports and integrating protections (e.g., 10-minute stop). This reduces the salience of reasons not to change and promotes movement toward action.
