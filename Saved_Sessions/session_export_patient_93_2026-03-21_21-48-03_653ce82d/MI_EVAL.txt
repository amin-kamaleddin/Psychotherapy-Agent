# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental regard, explicitly acknowledged Margaret's values and repeatedly affirmed her choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and validations of Margaret's perspective, but missed opportunities to probe and reflect deeper emotions (e.g., feelings about the fall or her son's concerns).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on reducing alcohol with concrete goals, specific tactics, confidence ratings, and a planned follow-up check-in to sustain forward movement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a genuine partner by offering options, asking which strategies sounded doable, and tailoring the plan to Margaret's preferences and wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed Margaret's choice and control, presented options rather than directives, and framed slips as informational rather than failures.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motivations and client-generated plans, but could have further explored deeper values and emotional meaning to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the work as collaborative by asking permission, co-constructing the plan, and inviting Margaret to choose strategies and wording for family conversations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with open questions, concrete first-step options, summaries, and use of a confidence ruler to deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently reframed sustain talk to support reduction, though more strategic techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) could further soften sustain talk and resolve ambivalence.
