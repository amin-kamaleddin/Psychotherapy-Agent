# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through affirmations, validation, and normalizing cautiousness. The clinician honored the patient's pace and limits throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and reflective listening about practical constraints but stayed at a pragmatic level. Missed opportunities to explicitly name or explore underlying fear, frustration, or emotional nuance about side-effect concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on inhaler trial and addressing side-effect worries, consistently returning to concrete goals and action steps. Skillfully moved from exploration to specific implementation details while preserving MI spirit.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative, soliciting the patient's input at every decision point and offering choices for wording and supports. The clinician followed the patient's lead and co-created practical supports she found doable.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly reinforced autonomy by offering options, deferring sending the message, and emphasizing the patient's control and stop rules. Avoided pressuring language and honored the patient's pace and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the patient's ideas, preferences, and internal motivation, leading to a concrete, patient-generated trial plan. The clinician elicited solutions rather than imposing them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as an expert on her life and co-designed the plan, offering options and accepting her wording. Power-sharing moves allowed the patient to shape the intervention.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and reinforced incremental change talk and stepwise commitments, including evocative questions and reflections. Could more strategically amplify spontaneous change statements and consolidate commitment language (e.g., confidence rulers or explicit commitment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged sustain concerns while redirecting toward small experiments and information-seeking. Could strengthen use of explicit double-sided reflections to further soften resistance and increase discrepancy.
