### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear unconditional positive regard and reassurance of client control, consistently normalizing and affirming Lukas without judgment. Warmth and respect are evident throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and open questions that show perspective-taking and understanding of Lukas' experience. Could deepen empathic engagement by probing emotional nuance (anxiety, identity tension, isolation) more consistently.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on peer-influenced drinking and its impact on athletics/academics and steers toward concrete, actionable steps. Uses role-play and planning to maintain an action-oriented session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner—offers options, invites feedback, and co-constructs plans based on Lukas' preferences. Adjusts approach responsively (e.g., tougher push, choice of lines).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy from the start, seeks permission before offering strategies, and provides multiple alternatives rather than directives. Reinforces Lukas' agency by asking which language feels most like him and encouraging self-selected actions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation through importance/confidence scaling and invites Lukas to choose doable strategies, which increased his confidence in-session. Could more consistently draw out spontaneous change talk by linking change to values and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as joint exploration, accepts Lukas' suggestions, and builds on them during practice and planning. Acts as coach and collaborator rather than an expert authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change language using scaling, options, and role-play, and amplifies brief wins (confidence bump). Would benefit from more systematic deepening of change talk (values linkage, past resistance strategies).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids legitimizing sustain talk and redirects toward practical change strategies, using role-play to prevent reinforcement of pro-drinking arguments. Could add more double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence while tipping toward change.
