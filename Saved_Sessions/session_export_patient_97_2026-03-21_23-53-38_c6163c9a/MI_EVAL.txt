# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, using validating reflections and normalization. Could occasionally name hard emotions more explicitly to deepen empathic validation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong empathic listening and accurate reflections that mirror the client's language, which enhances understanding. Could deepen empathy with more complex, affect-focused reflections and avoid repetitive summaries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing recidivism via concrete steps (temp job → fill one app) and progressively narrows to an actionable task. Direction is client-centered and goal-oriented throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as genuine collaboration with co-constructed plans, frequent invitations for input, and shared decision-making about logistics and priorities. Clinician reliably defers to the client's expertise and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports choice by offering options, asking permission, and acknowledging the client's right to decline. Avoids coercion and preserves client decision-making while offering assistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits the client's own motivations, values, and past successes using open questions and prompts to generate solutions. The clinician consistently draws on intrinsic motivation rather than imposing reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance with inclusive language and joint problem-solving, honoring the client's preferences and involving him in planning. This fosters shared ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits desire, reasons, and examples of success that support change and self-efficacy. Could more actively amplify and summarize change talk and elicit explicit commitment and confidence language to strengthen momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Responds adaptively to sustain talk with neutral acknowledgments and double-sided reflections that avoid confrontation. Could more strategically pivot sustain talk into change talk by emphasizing and expanding change-oriented statements.
