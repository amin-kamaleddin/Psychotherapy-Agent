### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Ethan's competing priorities without judgment. Repeated affirmations and nonlabeling language model a warm, accepting stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, sustained empathic understanding with complex reflections and summaries that mirror Ethan's feelings. The empathic stance helps Ethan expand on concrete plans rather than withdraw.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on risky drinking and progressively narrows toward a concrete, doable plan. Moves from values and ambivalence to specific strategies and a one-week behavioral experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as genuine teamwork: the clinician solicits Ethan's ideas, checks feasibility, and jointly troubleshoots options. Planning and rehearsal are co-created rather than expert-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirms Ethan's choice and frames steps as his decision, avoiding coercion. Offers structure while preserving self-direction and small-step experimentation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled at drawing out Ethan's own reasons for change and preferred strategies through open questions and reflections. Summaries and prompts amplify commitment language and concrete implementation steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions the work as joint problem-solving and invites Ethan to choose and operationalize options. Power-sharing is evident in repeated invitations and respect for Ethan's contextual knowledge.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with targeted questions and reflections, converting ambivalence into specific intentions. Repeatedly reinforces Ethan's stated plans (e.g., soda alternation, buddy support).

**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward workable change, but misses opportunities for more explicit double-sided reflections. Could more directly validate the pull of peer belonging while highlighting discrepancy to further reduce sustain talk.
