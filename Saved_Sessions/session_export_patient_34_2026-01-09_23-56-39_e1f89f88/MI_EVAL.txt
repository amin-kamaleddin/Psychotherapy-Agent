# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing Dylan's experience and validating his priorities throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used multiple accurate reflections and empathic summaries that paraphrased Dylan's feelings and priorities, which strengthened rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on the conduct referral and concrete next steps, repeatedly steering the conversation to actionable plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—inviting preferences, co-creating message wording, and following Dylan's lead on tone and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Dylan's sense of choice by offering options, asking which felt doable, and requesting permission before offering help or role-play.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited practical, defensive motivations (protecting room/scholarship) but did not sufficiently probe deeper intrinsic reasons or attitudes about drinking and long-term goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered shared decision-making and invited Dylan's input, though at times the clinician supplied ready-made solutions rather than eliciting his own ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made limited attempts to evoke change talk and focused on defense; did not explore Dylan's thoughts about drinking, risks, or willingness to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation but frequently validated rationalizations and helped craft denials, with little strategic redirection to explore costs or create discrepancy.
