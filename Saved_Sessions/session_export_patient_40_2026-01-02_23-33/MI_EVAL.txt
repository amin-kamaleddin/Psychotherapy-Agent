**Comments:** Clinician consistently nonjudgmental and respectful; normalizes shame and affirms values (e.g., “You’re carrying a lot of shame…”, references to his kids). Supportive tone maintained throughout.

### Direction

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Change language present (“I don’t want to feel that way again,” “They’re better, yeah”) but not consistently amplified or elaborated to build stronger commitment and confidence.
