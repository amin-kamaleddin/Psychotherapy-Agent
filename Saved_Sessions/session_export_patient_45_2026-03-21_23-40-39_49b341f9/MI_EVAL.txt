# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with warm, nonshaming language and normalization of the patient's perspective. Could add even more explicit normalizing of common barriers (e.g., reliance on alcohol after late shifts).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, reflecting both content and feeling which promoted engagement. Only a few minor missed opportunities remain.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction and glucose monitoring, tying concrete behaviors to measurable outcomes and medication goals. This focus moved the patient toward a specific, achievable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as a genuine joint exploration with the clinician inviting the patient's input at every decision point and integrating his choices into the plan. Tone and questions communicated teamwork effectively.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes patient choice and frames suggestions as options compatible with his life, avoiding coercive language. Medical information is presented conditionally, preserving the patient's sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the patient by eliciting values, strengths, and realistic first steps, producing concrete change talk about reducing meds. Could be strengthened with more targeted scaling/confidence questions to consolidate commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and respects the patient's expertise, offering options and eliciting preferences that inform the plan. Would improve by more consistently asking permission before offering advice and explicitly co-creating follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces meaningful change talk that produced concrete commitments and plans. Could more actively amplify and consolidate commitment using scaling, targeted summaries, and requests for specific commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids arguing, but underuses advanced softening strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections, amplified reflections, and strategic summaries) to defuse resistance. More targeted softening would better reduce sustain talk's influence.
