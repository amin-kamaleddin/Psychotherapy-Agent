# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating throughout the session, normalizing struggles and avoiding blame; strong acceptance reflected in repeated affirmations.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that helped Marco feel heard, though deeper, more complex reflections of underlying emotions or ambivalence would strengthen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on resisting home-based snacks and concrete coping strategies, repeatedly steering the conversation toward a specific, testable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Tone and exchanges felt collaborative with shared choices (alarm text, snack list), but the clinician supplied most ideas instead of eliciting more from Marco.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offered multiple options and asked which felt doable to support choice, although pressing for specific commitments at times edged toward a more directive stance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some efforts to draw out motivation (confidence rating, commitment question) but the session relied heavily on clinician-provided strategies rather than eliciting values, reasons, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited input and joint problem-solving, but clinician-led planning and many prescriptive suggestions limited Marco's role as the primary source of solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited modest change talk and brief commitments, yet missed opportunities for open, evocative questions and reflective amplification to deepen Marco's own reasons and desire for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not confront sustain talk, redirecting to pragmatic alternatives which de-escalated resistance; could improve by using double-sided or strategic reflections to further resolve ambivalence.
