# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, with frequent affirmations and validations that created a warm, nonjudgmental tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, timely reflections captured both content and affect and accurately mirrored ambivalence, facilitating elaboration and reducing defensiveness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on reducing gambling/financial harm tied to personally meaningful goals and consistently steered toward concrete, behaviorally specific steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with the clinician asking for the client's input at every step and following the client's priorities in designing the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honored the client's choice and ownership by offering options, eliciting preferences, and inviting the client to decide pace and strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations and past successes and elicited specific strategies, though could deepen evocation by linking values to identity and amplifying commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine co-construction was evident in inclusive language, shared decision-making, and invitations to design the plan together, fostering client ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced concrete change talk and doable steps, but missed some opportunities to amplify and convert motivations into a clear verbal commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and neutrally reflected sustain talk, but could more actively soften it with double-sided reflections and gentle reframing of rationalizations.
