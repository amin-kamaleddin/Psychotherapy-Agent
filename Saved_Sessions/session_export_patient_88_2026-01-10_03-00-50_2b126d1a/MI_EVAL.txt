# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed nonjudgmental acceptance, validated Marcus's perspective, and asked permission before offering information, avoiding labeling or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong use of reflective listening and accurate summaries captured Marcus's ambivalence and concerns, demonstrating clear perspective-taking throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on teen alcohol use and safety, moving logically from assessment to options to a concrete safety plan and skill rehearsal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with the clinician inviting Marcus to choose options, co-construct plans, and practice lines, positioning Marcus as the expert on his life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supported autonomy by asking permission, offering choices rather than directives, and framing decisions as Marcus's to make without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited Marcus's own motivations and strategies with open questions and a readiness ruler, though there were missed opportunities to probe deeper values and further amplify self-generated reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician worked as an equal partner—offering ideas, role-playing, and inviting Marcus to select and shape the plan—demonstrating strong shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good evocation of change talk through open questions, a readiness ruler, and option menus that produced concrete steps, but the clinician could have better amplified and sequenced these into firmer commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician avoided confronting or reinforcing sustain talk, using balanced reflections and gentle redirection to honor Marcus's perspective while moving toward safety-focused options.
