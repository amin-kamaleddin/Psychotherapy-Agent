# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard with validating reflections and no blame, creating a safe, nonjudgmental space for the adolescent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening that captured frustration, loneliness, and worry, but many responses were formulaic; more complex, affect-focused reflections would deepen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and progressed logically from goals to concrete implementation details, yielding an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative—invited the client's input, co-created the checklist/if‑then plan, and tested options together as a team.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, respecting the client's preferences (e.g., to buy snacks herself), and affirming ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited the client's motivations and concrete reasons for change, but sometimes offered many options that risked steering; could probe deeper into personal values and longer-term meaning.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the client as the expert on what fits her life, repeatedly soliciting preferences and co-constructing the plan rather than imposing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk through open questions and prompts that converted intent into specific, testable actions while reinforcing the client's agency.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and barriers and redirected to planning, but relied on neutral validations and missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or strategic ambivalence summaries.
